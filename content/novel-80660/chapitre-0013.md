# Chapitre 13 — The Birth of Sasuke, An's Planning!

Time passed in the blink of an eye.

In the blink of an eye, the day of Mikoto’s delivery arrived.

Still inside the hospital room at Konoha Hospital, Mikoto was giving birth.

Outside the room, the three father and sons were waiting anxiously.

Although Itachi had already eaten the Op-Op Fruit, unfortunately, with his current medical skills, he could not help at all.

« Waa ! Waa ! »

Hearing the sounds from inside the room, An knew that his younger brother had been born.

« Stupid little brother, you can call your brother 'stupid little brother' from now on. »

Itachi looked helplessly at his elder brother and twitched the corner of his mouth.

The three of them quickly rushed into the room, only to see Mikoto smiling weakly on the hospital bed, with an infant crying at the head of the bed.

Upon seeing this, Fugaku immediately stepped forward and took Mikoto’s hand.

« Thank you for your hard work, Mikoto. »

An and Itachi leaned over to the bedside to look at the crying infant.

« Itachi, don’t you think our little brother looks very ugly ? »

« Mm-hmm ! »

« You were just as ugly when you were born. »

Hearing the brothers' conversation, Fugaku and Mikoto both laughed.

« Let’s give the child a name ! »

Fugaku pondered for a moment and said, « Sasuke, Uchiha Sasuke. »

Itachi looked at Sasuke at the head of the bed and muttered to himself, « Uchiha Sasuke, huh ? So I have a younger brother now too. »

An leaned over the hospital bed and reached out to poke Sasuke, causing Sasuke’s crying to become even louder immediately.

« Then I have one more stupid little brother. »

Afterward, the father and his three sons accompanied Mikoto in the hospital during her postpartum recovery every day. The whole family poured a lot of effort into this new life, Sasuke.

Seven days later.

The family returned home from the hospital.

An and Itachi still did not continue their training at Training Ground 3, but instead stayed home every day to take care of the newborn Sasuke.

« Stupid little brother ! »

As An’s voice fell, both Itachi and Sasuke turned their heads to look at him.

After An’s long and continuous persistence, both Itachi and Sasuke had developed a conditioned reflex to this nickname.

« Stop teasing your brother, An. »

Mikoto said as she wiped the drool from Sasuke’s mouth. By now, Sasuke was already starting to look a bit like 'Two-Column'.

However, An also knew that since Sasuke had been born, Naruto should be born soon as well. He needed to be careful during this period.

« Mother, aren’t you quite familiar with Aunt Kushina ? Could you let me go see her ? »

In reality, Kushina and Mikoto were not that close ; the two of them only greeted each other when they occasionally bumped into one another. Mikoto really didn’t know why An would have such a request.

« If you want to see Kushina, just go find her ! You know where the Minato family lives anyway. »

An certainly knew where Minato lived, but as a small child, he would not be taken seriously if he went to discuss matters.

Perhaps seeing the doubt in An’s heart, Mikoto spoke again, « An, feel free to go ! You certainly won’t be disappointed. »

« Alright ! Then I’ll head out for a bit. »

After An finished speaking, he left the house, while Itachi continued to take care of Sasuke.

After leaving home, An walked towards the Minato family’s residence, familiar with the route. Upon arriving at the destination, An noticed that the atmosphere was already a bit tense.

Nine months of pregnancy, childbirth in one day.

Kushina was currently eight months pregnant, and children are generally born around nine months.

If any unexpected accident were to cause Kushina to go into premature labor, the Nine-Tailed would inevitably cause massive destruction to Konoha. Therefore, it was likely that Minato Namikaze had made some arrangements to protect Kushina.

Knock, knock, knock !

« Please come in ! »

Hearing a clear female voice, An pushed the door open and entered.

« Teacher Minato, why are you at home ? »

An did not expect that Minato, as the Fourth Hokage, would also be at home.

However, Minato Namikaze’s expression was a bit unpleasant, « This is my house, is it not normal for me to be here ? »

Seeing this, An could only helplessly let out an awkward laugh to ease the tension. Kushina laughed heartily at seeing Minato get deflated.

« I thought Teacher Minato would definitely be busy at the Hokage Building, but I didn’t expect you to be at home accompanying your wife. »

Minato Namikaze looked at Kushina and dotingly pulled her into his arms.

« Naruto will be born soon, and I can’t rest easy leaving Kushina alone at home, so I stay home to accompany her whenever I have time. »

An didn’t expect to be fed a dose of dog food like this. Kushina pulled An over and gently stroked his cheek.

« If only Naruto could be as well-behaved as An when he grows up. »

An felt a bit embarrassed by Kushina, as his mind was that of an adult, after all.

Minato Namikaze poured a glass of water and handed it to An, then sat down beside Kushina.

« What’s the matter, An ? Did you come to find me because you have questions about your training ? »

« I came to see my teacher’s wife to protect her as she is about to give birth. »

Minato Namikaze and Kushina smiled at each other, and Kushina gently stroked her belly.

« Naruto, your big brother An has come to protect you. »

Minato Namikaze : « An, I have already made arrangements for your teacher’s wife’s safety... »

An shook his head directly and said in a firm tone.

« Teacher Minato’s arrangements are Teacher Minato’s intentions, and me coming to protect your wife is my intention. »

« Besides, with my strength, are you worried that I won’t be able to protect her well, Teacher Minato ? »

An felt that Minato Namikaze’s arrangements were unreliable. After all, in the original timeline, Minato and Kushina died, and Hiruzen Sarutobi’s wife, Biwako Sarutobi, also died, causing heavy losses to Konoha. An felt that he had to rely on himself to deal with the impending crisis.

Looking at the stubborn An, Minato Namikaze and Kushina didn’t know how to refuse. However, Minato Namikaze was aware of An’s strength, and with An’s protection, Kushina would be even safer.

« Alright ! An, then I’ll leave Kushina to you. »

« I’ll go find a room to tidy up. » As she said this, Kushina was about to get up.

Seeing this, Minato Namikaze immediately stopped her. Could they really let a pregnant woman do the work while two grown men were standing there ?

Thus, Minato Namikaze pulled An along to tidy up a small room.

« An, your goal shouldn’t just be to protect Kushina, right ! »

« Teacher Minato, I am worried that an accident might occur when the teacher’s wife is giving birth. If the Nine-Tailed were released by someone with ulterior motives, Konoha would inevitably suffer heavy losses, and other ninja villages might even try to take advantage of the situation. »

Hearing An’s analysis, Minato took note of it privately. After everything was tidied up, An was considered settled in at Minato’s house.

Afterward, An took over the chores of laundry and cooking that originally belonged to Minato, serving Kushina every day just as he had served Mikoto.

Under An’s care, the smiles on Kushina’s face grew more frequent, and sometimes Minato even felt that he was the redundant one.

As the due date drew closer, Minato spent more and more time at home. This also allowed Minato to carve out more time to guide An’s training.
