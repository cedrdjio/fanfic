# Chapitre 63 — Setting Out!

The night passed without incident.

Through Uchiha An’s tireless efforts, he successfully persuaded Uchiha Fugaku and Uchiha Shisui. The group finally reached a consensus and decided to follow Uchiha An’s arrangements.

As for the three elders and other members of the Uchiha Clan, Uchiha An believed they would follow his arrangements.

After all, Uchiha An had distributed quite a few Devil Fruits, which had also created a positive impact within the Uchiha Clan.

Moreover, when the other members of the Uchiha Clan saw that Uchiha Fugaku and the elders were willing to accept Uchiha An’s decision, they would inevitably show their support.

Uchiha Itachi was very cooperative in this matter, following Uchiha An’s arrangements completely without raising any objections.

Under Uchiha An’s leadership, the Uchiha Clan began to exhibit an atmosphere of active preparation for war.

...

The underground tunnels not far from the Hokage Building.

As the leader of Root, Uchiha An was also accustomed to appearing here ; it seemed the leaders of Root always liked dark places.

Over these past few years, Uchiha An had been supporting the development of Root, but his management of Root had always been in a hands-off state.

Uchiha An let Yamato, Aburame Torune, and Fu Yamanaka discuss most matters.

« How many people in Root are willing to listen to me now ? »

« Everyone ! »

Aburame Torune’s tone revealed a hint of firmness and determination, looking unquestionable.

It was no joke ; a leader who could bestow Bloodline Limits upon his subordinates would inevitably earn their loyalty.

« Yes, Lord Uchiha An, please believe in our loyalty. We, who are always in the darkest corners of Konoha, are absolutely loyal. »

Fu Yamanaka also actively expressed his stance at this moment. Although his big boss didn’t handle affairs, the Yamanaka Clan owed him a favor.

Yamato just nodded coldly, not saying any grand words, but his attitude was already very clear.

« Good. Within three days, recall all Root members from everywhere. We are going to blitz the Hidden Mist Village and unify the Ninja World ! »

Hearing Uchiha An’s words, Fu Yamanaka and Aburame Torune trembled involuntarily. Just thinking about unifying the Ninja World was exciting.

Yamato remained very calm, maintaining his cold demeanor.

However, the Seven-Tails Chomei inside Yamato curled its lip in disdain.

« Unify the Ninja World ? That is a feat that so many powerhouses have failed to achieve. Do you believe this teenager in front of us can unify the Ninja World ? This is truly ridiculous. »

« I believe in him, just as he believes that I can get along well with you and become a perfect Jinchuriki. »

Immediately, Seven-Tails Chomei said no more ; after all, Chomei was now one of their own.

« Yes ! » ×3

Then the entire Root began to mobilize, whether they were spies in various countries or members responsible for patrolling the Land of Fire.

When everyone in Root knew it was Uchiha An who was calling them, they immediately returned with 100% enthusiasm.

...

However, after arranging matters for Root, Uchiha An left Root.

After all, relying solely on the Uchiha Clan and Root could not achieve the conquest of the entire Land of Wind.

Now, Uchiha An had come to the Shimura Clan’s compound to ask for Danzo’s help.

Along the way, Uchiha An noticed that the members of the Shimura Clan still didn’t like him very much ; after all, the conflict between the Shimura Clan and the Uchiha Clan was long-standing.

« Uchiha An, what made you think of coming to see an old man like me ? »

Danzo Shimura’s tone carried a hint of loneliness. After all, he had previously been a powerful figure in Konoha, and now, even though he was idle, he couldn’t accept it.

Seeing that Danzo felt like a hero in his twilight years, Uchiha An spoke bluntly.

« Lord Danzo, the conflict between the Uchiha Clan and the Shimura Clan is long-standing. Now is the time to resolve the conflict between the two clans. »

« Speak ! What idea do you have this time ? You should know that I am very satisfied with you as my successor. As long as it is within my power, you can ask for anything. »

Uchiha An felt a mix of emotions inside. Whether it was the Darkness of the Ninja World or the scapegoat Danzo.

At the very least, the person in front of him had not mistreated him and had even been quite good to him. It could be said that Uchiha An already regarded Danzo as one of his own.

« Lord Danzo, I want to use the strength of the Shimura Clan to help me defeat the Hidden Sand Village, and even directly execute the Daimyo of the Land of Wind to control the entire Land of Wind. »

« Hahaha ! Truly terrifying. I am becoming more and more reassured about Root being in your hands. » As he spoke, Danzo Shimura’s expression immediately became serious.

« Leave the Shimura Clan to me ! My words as clan head still carry some weight. »

Hearing this, Uchiha An immediately nodded in agreement. Even though Danzo was no longer the leader of Root, as the head of the Shimura Clan, he governed the Shimura Clan with an iron fist.

Moreover, with Danzo’s strength, certain guys in the Shimura Clan couldn’t stir up any trouble.

Next, Uchiha An began to lobby the Ninja clans he was more familiar with.

Like the Ino–Shika–Chō trio and the Inuzuka Clan, Uchiha An hadn’t skimped on gifting Devil Fruits ; it could be said that all four clans owed Uchiha An quite a few favors.

Of course, the Hyuga Clan had them too, but Uchiha An didn’t want to use the Hyuga Clan’s favor for the time being.

Although Uchiha An had already poached Hinata, the eldest daughter of the Hyuga Clan, living off a woman was ultimately not the right path.

With Uchiha An’s planning and actions, half of Konoha’s strength began to mobilize, and Ninja clans were constantly recalling their own Ninjas.

As for the Ninja clans that noticed, and Anbu members like Minato Namikaze, they didn’t react to this.

Even though they were on guard, only the core members of each Ninja clan knew Uchiha An’s purpose, and the information had not leaked out.

...

Three days later.

On a plain outside Konoha Village.

The Ino–Shika–Chō trio, the Inuzuka Clan, the Uchiha Clan, the Shimura Clan, and the members of Root, totaling three large formations, stood together.

This operation could be said to have brought out nearly half of Konoha’s strength, with five thousand Ninjas gathered together, waiting for Uchiha An’s command.

The heads of each clan stood with Uchiha An, and the atmosphere on the scene was serious and tense.

« Ahem ! »

With a simple cough, the attention of the entire crowd was focused on Uchiha An.

« Ninjas of Konoha Village, today I, Uchiha An, stand here to speak to you, welcoming a war that is imminent, or rather, inevitable. »

« The enemies we face are the Hidden Sand Village, the Land of Wind, and even the entire Ninja World. The enemies we face next will be powerful and cruel ; they are full of ambition and malice toward our land and our families. »

« We cannot be intimidated, nor can we yield ! We must unite as one and use our flesh and blood to defend our homes and our loved ones ! »

« Everyone must commit themselves to this war. Regardless of your surname, age, or personal strength, today we must all fight for ourselves ! »

After Uchiha An finished his last sentence, the atmosphere of the entire scene was ignited.

Then, Uchiha An waved his hand, and the five-thousand-person army set off simultaneously.
