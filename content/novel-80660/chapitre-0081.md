# Chapitre 81 — Uchiha Madara!

As Zetsu’s voice faded, the White Zetsu on the ground was wrapped in a layer of black mud, as if it were being completely devoured.

Then, the mud began to crack, and a figure slowly emerged.

A figure with long black hair covering his right eye, deep eye bags, and wearing red samurai armor appeared.

His eyes were red, with three black markings—the highest state of the Sharingan—the Mangekyo Sharingan.

Once Zetsu’s technique concluded, the man’s consciousness returned.

He slowly opened his eyes, looked at his own body, then looked at Zetsu and Obito, a cold sneer curling the corners of his mouth.

« It seems the plan is proceeding very smoothly. Now, resurrect me ! It’s been a long time since I’ve stretched my limbs. »

Uchiha Madara’s tone was relaxed and comfortable ; even as an Edo Tensei, Uchiha Madara remained absolutely confident in his own strength.

Uchiha Madara performed a symbolic hand seal, then slammed his palms onto the ground.

« Edo Tensei ! Release ! »

Uchiha Madara’s voice was deep and majestic. As his voice fell, the control from Zetsu was instantly lifted.

Uchiha Madara then slowly stood up, sensing his Edo Tensei body ; it seemed there was no discomfort.

However, Zetsu and Obito looked awkward and uneasy ; neither dared to meet Uchiha Madara’s gaze.

« Master, in truth, there are some reasons why we have resurrected you. »

« That is to deal with Uchiha An, that heinous fellow. »

« He is an unparalleled genius of the Uchiha Clan, arguably the most gifted member of the Uchiha Clan. »

Zetsu began to stutter as it spoke, clearly still traumatized by Uchiha An’s terrifying strength.

Uchiha Madara smiled faintly, not taking the so-called genius in Zetsu’s words seriously.

He had once been invincible during the Sengoku era, having faced all kinds of geniuses ; those who could rival him and Senju Hashirama were few and far between.

« A genius, you say ? I wonder if he can entertain me enough, now that I’ve just been resurrected. »

Upon hearing Uchiha Madara’s words, Obito said nothing more.

After all, at this moment, Obito had no attachment left to the world or his own life ; Obito had long decided to trade his life for Uchiha Madara’s resurrection, determined to use his own sacrifice to realize his and Uchiha Madara’s ideals.

Then, Obito slowly closed his eyes and began to weave hand seals rapidly.

Immediately, a powerful and mysterious aura began to radiate from Obito’s body, and his thin lips softly uttered a sentence.

« Rinne Tensei no Jutsu ! »

As Obito’s voice faded, a white light shot from his palms, piercing through Uchiha Madara’s body and enveloping him.

This was a power that transcended life and death, a power capable of reversing fate, a power capable of creating miracles.

Uchiha Madara’s body began to change ; his blood began to flow, and his heart began to beat.

« Lord Uchiha Madara, I hope you will not forget our agreement. »

After saying this, Obito did not speak again.

Soon, Obito’s body became extremely weak, his hair turned snow-white, and his aura began to fade.

Obito felt his life slipping away ; as his vision gradually blurred, a trace of peace filled his heart.

Dying, Obito thought of the girl he had once loved—her smile, her gentleness, her sacrifice.

He hoped that he would be able to see her after death, hoped that he would be able to embrace her after death.

Uchiha Madara, however, completely failed to notice Obito’s emotions, only excitedly feeling his own resurrection.

This was a brand-new vitality, an existence that surpassed Edo Tensei.

He was no longer a bound corpse, no longer a forgotten legend.

The current Uchiha Madara was a truly living person, a man determined to change the world.

« Master ! »

Zetsu pointed in horror behind Uchiha Madara, its voice trembling as it spoke.

« Master, do you see ? That is Uchiha An. He is the Uchiha Clan’s most powerful enemy. Now, only you can use your supreme power to subdue him. »

Uchiha Madara turned his head away disdainfully, feeling that Zetsu was truly embarrassing itself, lacking even an ounce of the aura of a powerhouse.

But when Uchiha Madara fully turned around and saw An’s state clearly, Uchiha Madara’s heart couldn’t help but be shocked, though it was quickly filled with excitement.

The newly resurrected Uchiha Madara stared at An’s figure in the distance, his body trembling involuntarily.

Of course, this was because Uchiha Madara was too excited.

« I originally thought that after my resurrection, I would easily rule the Ninja World. I didn’t expect there to be such an unexpected surprise waiting for me. »

« Is he Uchiha An ? An existence capable of rivaling Hashirama ? »

« However, with such a massive stature, is he really that powerful ? Can he really compare to my old rival, Hashirama ? »

After saying this, Uchiha Madara ignored the panicked Zetsu beside him and instead looked at Obito on the ground.

« Rest assured, Obito, our plan will definitely be realized ! »

Uchiha Madara said softly, a flash of brightness in his eyes. Then Uchiha Madara slowly bent down, extended a finger, and said :

« Your eyes... it is time to return them to me. »

Suddenly, Uchiha Madara forcefully jammed his fingers into Obito’s eye sockets and ripped out that pair of precious eyes.

Obito’s already withered corpse twitched violently, and blood gushed out from his empty eye sockets, staining the surrounding snow red.

Uchiha Madara did not hesitate at all, aimed Obito’s Rinnegan at his own eye sockets, and shoved them in, one on the left and one on the right.

Inserting and removing them at will, Uchiha Madara did not feel the slightest discomfort, only feeling a powerful force surging within his body.

Feeling the unparalleled aura radiating from his body, a satisfied and confident smile appeared on Uchiha Madara’s face.

« Is this the Rinnegan ? Truly a power worth reminiscing about... » Uchiha Madara praised in a low voice. « Then let me use these eyes to take a good look at this world ! »

Uchiha Madara turned his head and saw Zetsu beside him.

Seeing this, Zetsu immediately said : « Lord Madara, please be sure to realize your ideal ! Please be sure to make this world peaceful ! »

Uchiha Madara did not answer Zetsu, only glanced at it indifferently, then turned and left.

Next, he would take action, to create a perfect dream, a world without pain or sorrow.

Then, Uchiha Madara dashed rapidly in the direction of An, leaving behind a blood-red afterimage along the way.
