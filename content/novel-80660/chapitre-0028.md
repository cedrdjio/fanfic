# Chapitre 28 — Inquiring About Ninjutsu!

Once the assessment concluded and they returned home, Uchiha Fugaku sent Uchiha Itachi to take care of Uchiha Sasuke, who hadn’t appeared for several chapters.

As for Uchiha An, he was unceremoniously dragged into the study by his old man.

« What were you thinking ? Why would you want to join Danzo Shimura’s Root ? »

« The Shimura Clan and our Uchiha Clan have always been at odds. Joining Danzo Shimura’s Root so recklessly is extremely dangerous. »

« Everyone who joins Root is branded by Danzo Shimura with the Cursed Seal of Hatred. Once that happens, you’ll be at his mercy ! »

« Uchiha An, I’ve always been confident in your performance, and you’ve always handled everything well. But this time, you’ve truly disappointed me. I’m going to go see Danzo Shimura in a moment and have you withdraw from Root ! »

Uchiha Fugaku stared at Uchiha An with a look of frustrated disappointment, while Uchiha An appeared completely indifferent.

Seeing this, Uchiha Fugaku felt his anger rising, and he even silently picked up a bamboo pole.

« Don’t worry, Father ! I’m confident I can protect myself in Root, and I can handle everything ! »

In reality, Uchiha An didn’t care about the so-called Cursed Seal of Hatred at all ; after all, killing Danzo Shimura would break it.

What Uchiha An wanted was the power of Root—a force hidden in the shadows that was not inferior to any other faction in Konoha.

Unfortunately, after hearing Uchiha An’s explanation, Uchiha Fugaku remained furious, his chest heaving as if it might explode at any moment.

« You’ll handle everything ? Are you underestimating Root and Danzo Shimura too much ! »

« He is a disciple of the Second Hokage, and he built Root from nothing into what it is today. »

« In short, Danzo Shimura is not someone to be trifled with. You’d better withdraw from Root ! »

Upon hearing this, Uchiha An firmly shook his head in refusal. Not to mention that he didn’t want to leave, there was no way Danzo Shimura would let him go so easily.

Even if his old man could convince Danzo Shimura to let him leave, it would likely come at a significant cost.

« Don’t worry ! With my strength, Danzo Shimura can’t do anything to me. At most, his Cursed Seal of Hatred might have a slight effect on me. »

Uchiha Fugaku looked at Uchiha An, knowing that this was no longer something he could persuade him out of.

« How about I use the Wash-Wash Fruit to see if I can wash away the mark of the Cursed Seal of Hatred you mentioned ! »

As he spoke, Uchiha Fugaku couldn’t suppress his curiosity and reached out to pry open Uchiha An’s mouth to experiment.

Seeing this, Uchiha An immediately swatted Uchiha Fugaku’s hand away. « I haven’t even been branded by Danzo Shimura yet ! You should save your Wash-Wash Fruit for Mom to do the laundry ! »

After saying this, Uchiha An ignored Uchiha Fugaku’s livid expression and ran out of the study.

Uchiha Fugaku, meanwhile, began to contemplate his authority as a father and whether he should use the Wash-Wash Fruit in combat.

« Dear, there’s a bit too much laundry. Help me wash the clothes. »

« Eh ! Alright. »

...

The night passed without incident.

At nine o’clock the next morning, Uchiha An and Uchiha Shisui arrived at the Hokage Office.

It was a pity that Uchiha Itachi hadn’t continued fighting at the time ; otherwise, he could have come to extort a high-level Ninjutsu as well.

« You’re here so early ! Can’t wait anymore ? Have you decided which Ninjutsu you want ? »

Minato Namikaze appeared even more excited than Uchiha An and Uchiha Shisui, which left the Hokage’s aide, Shikaku Nara, speechless.

« Hokage-sama, let’s hear what Uchiha An and Uchiha Shisui have in mind first ! »

Seeing this, Uchiha Shisui stepped forward two paces and began to recite the lines Uchiha An had taught him.

« The Second Hokage, Lord Senju Tobirama, was a legendary master of forbidden Ninjutsu. I am very curious about the forbidden Ninjutsu created by Lord Senju Tobirama, so I hope to choose one of them. »

« Forbidden Ninjutsu ? Have you thought this through ? You should know that every forbidden Ninjutsu of Lord Senju Tobirama carries a certain degree of danger and could very well harm you. »

Clearly, Minato Namikaze’s advice to Uchiha Shisui was useless, and Uchiha Shisui would not change his mind.

In reality, Uchiha Shisui was not qualified to access forbidden Ninjutsu, but geniuses always received special treatment, so it was acceptable to let Uchiha Shisui choose some less critical ones.

« I hope Hokage-sama will allow me to choose a Ninjutsu created by Lord Senju Tobirama. »

Seeing Uchiha Shisui so persistent, neither Minato Namikaze nor Shikaku Nara could advise him further.

Then, Shikaku Nara rose and left, seemingly to retrieve the scroll of sealing.

At this moment, Minato Namikaze’s attention shifted to Uchiha An.

« You aren’t thinking of learning some forbidden Ninjutsu too, are you ! I’m telling you, that’s impossible ! »

Uchiha An immediately waved his hands. « How could that be ? Minato-sensei, I’m not interested in forbidden Ninjutsu at all. »

After saying this, Uchiha An smiled sheepishly. Seeing that smile, Minato Namikaze felt a subconscious sense of panic ; he feared this little guy was about to stir up trouble.

Suddenly, both Minato Namikaze and Uchiha An fell silent, leaving only Uchiha Shisui standing there feeling a bit awkward.

Fortunately, Uchiha Shisui’s awkwardness didn’t last long.

Soon, Shikaku Nara pushed the door open, carrying the massive scroll of sealing.

« Go ahead and choose the Ninjutsu you want ! »

At this moment, Uchiha Shisui could no longer suppress his curiosity ; he stepped forward to carefully examine and ponder the Ninjutsu within the scroll of sealing.

Uchiha An looked at Minato Namikaze and Shikaku Nara and also put forward his own request.

In reality, Uchiha An basically had no weaknesses. If he had to name one, it was his own physical constitution, which simply couldn’t withstand the extreme speed of lightning.

If eating the Rumble-Rumble Fruit meant one could immediately reach the speed of lightning, then kindergarten principal Kizaru would have long since slaughtered his way through the pirate world ; after all, no one could withstand a hit at the speed of light.

Therefore, in order to be a little bit faster, Uchiha An still wanted a Ninjutsu that could train his body.

« Um... I feel my body is a bit weak. I want a Lightning Style Ninjutsu that has a body-strengthening effect. It would be best if it were similar to the Lightning Release Chakra Mode of the Hidden Cloud Village. »

Immediately, both Minato Namikaze and Shikaku Nara were covered in black lines.

Konoha’s Lightning Release Ninjutsu were already scarce, and those with body-strengthening effects were even fewer. As for a Ninjutsu that could rival the Hidden Cloud Village’s Lightning Release Chakra Mode, it basically didn’t exist.

« What you’re saying is too unrealistic. If we had such a Ninjutsu in Konoha, its reputation would have already spread across the Ninja World. »

Regarding this, Uchiha An felt somewhat helpless ; after all, in his memory, Konoha’s Lightning Release Ninjutsu were indeed scarce, and those suitable for him were even fewer.

Just then, Shikaku Nara suddenly spoke up. « Actually, our Nara Clan has such a Lightning Release Ninjutsu. »

Uchiha An hadn’t expected things to take such a turn. Even Minato Namikaze looked at Shikaku Nara beside him with curiosity.

It seemed that even Minato Namikaze, as Hokage, didn’t know that the Nara Clan possessed such a powerful Lightning Release Ninjutsu.

One could only say that there was a reason why the various ninja clans had been able to pass down their legacies for so long.
