# Chapitre 15 — Obito's Action Fails!

« He is a member of our Uchiha Clan, but it seems our Uchiha Clan doesn’t have such a strong fighter. »

Uchiha An explained to Minato while quietly shifting his position.

Minato, who had been mentoring Uchiha An for a long time, crouched slightly.

« Attack, Minato-sensei ! »

A flash of lightning crackled in the air, and in an instant, he arrived in front of Obito.

Minato did not hesitate to use the Flying Thunder God Technique, appearing behind Obito in the blink of an eye.

Uchiha An and his teacher Minato coordinated with great synergy, their attacks striking at Obito from front and back.

« You want to kill me ! »

In an instant, Obito hid in another space, and the Kunai in Uchiha An’s hand passed right by the Rasengan in Minato’s palm.

« An, what kind of Ninjutsu is this ? ? »

Minato felt rather helpless regarding Obito’s situation ; even after just one simple exchange, Minato had already realized that the masked man’s Ninjutsu might be a Space-Time Ninjutsu.

Minato naturally knew how difficult this type of Space-Time Ninjutsu was to deal with, and now only Uchiha An, who had been fighting for a long time, had more information.

« Minato-sensei, this is Space-Time Ninjutsu ! As long as we keep attacking, we can defeat this guy ! »

Uchiha An continued to attack Obito at extreme speed.

Hearing Uchiha An’s words, Obito felt a sudden panic ; he hadn’t expected the weakness of his technique to be discovered after just a few short exchanges.

Minato nodded heavily. After all, the unknown was the most terrifying thing, and now that he knew the information about this technique, he just needed to keep attacking.

The three of them moved and fought constantly in the narrow space, leaving Kushina, who was present, unable to keep up with the action.

In reality, what was most striking now was Uchiha An’s performance. For a five-year-old to possess such powerful strength and be able to participate in such high-intensity combat—it had to be said that Uchiha An’s brilliance overshadowed the geniuses of his generation.

Boom !

Finally, the room could no longer withstand the fierce battle of the three, and it burst apart as rubble flew everywhere.

Minato immediately pulled away to carry Kushina and Naruto out, while Uchiha An flew out from the collapsing house.

« Protect the Hokage-sama ! »

« We cannot let this intruder escape from Konoha ! »

At this moment, the Anbu also reacted, and Ninjas kept appearing to surround Obito.

Beneath the mask, Obito’s face was full of anger ; it was clear that his operation this time had failed completely.

« Damn it ! »

This time, Obito did not hesitate and shot out towards the outside of the encirclement.

« Pursue ! »

Seeing the masked man trying to escape, the Anbu members immediately set off in pursuit.

Minato’s figure swayed slightly ; he also wanted to chase the fleeing masked man, but he stopped.

Seeing this, Kushina immediately knew what Minato was struggling with.

« Go ! Minato, I am safe now, » Kushina said, rubbing Uchiha An’s head, « Besides, Uchiha An will protect me. »

Minato gave an apologetic smile, then immediately pulled away and gave chase.

« Mistress is truly gentle, but this is also Minato-sensei’s duty. »

Uchiha An knew that this operation would inevitably end in vain ; a Ninja who knew Space-Time Ninjutsu was not that easy to catch.

« Uchiha An is also a gentle boy. I wonder how many girls he will charm in the future. »

« No, I still prefer gentle, beautiful, and cute big sisters like you, Mistress. »

« Haha ! But gentle and beautiful big sisters like me are hard to find. »

The night passed without incident.

Uchiha An took care of Kushina for another day before returning home. After all, Naruto had already been born, and the Nine-Tails Jinchuriki was no longer so weak, so naturally, she didn’t need Uchiha An to take care of her anymore.

As for Minato, he did not return all night, but unfortunately, he still gained nothing. Konoha could only strengthen its guard to prevent the masked man from coming to launch another sneak attack.

Of course, none of this had anything to do with five-year-old Uchiha An.

« I’m going out to play ! »

« Uchiha An, you don’t even know how to help take care of Sasuke. »

« With my stupid little brother here, I’m not needed. »

Uchiha An said as he ran out of the house ; after all, it was better to leave taking care of one-year-old Sasuke to Itachi.

Mikoto watched Uchiha An run out and shook her head slightly. Although five-year-old Uchiha An had begun to show his own opinions, as a mother, she also felt a lot of pressure.

However, when she saw Itachi and Sasuke playing together, the expression on her face involuntarily relaxed, and she stepped forward to take the two brothers into her arms.

« Your older brother is always so mysterious. »

Uchiha Mikoto and Uchiha Fugaku both noticed the unusual nature of Uchiha An and Itachi, but the two did not pay much attention to it ; after all, there were always some geniuses in this world that ordinary people found difficult to understand.

After jogging all the way out of the Uchiha Clan compound, Uchiha An went straight to wander around Konoha.

Konoha, having just ended the war, seemed unaffected ; the streets were still bustling with people enjoying the rare peaceful time in the Ninja World.

However, Uchiha An knew that undercurrents were still surging in the Ninja World.

If it weren’t for him, Minato and his wife would have died in battle, the Nine-Tails Incident would have erupted in Konoha, and the Hidden Cloud Village would have gone to war with Konoha again. I’m afraid few people would be able to enjoy peace at this time.

« I don’t know if it’s right or wrong ? »

After muttering a word, Uchiha An touched his own fair, tender little face.

He had saved Minato and Kushina, so the future plot would probably be of no use to him.

However, he would cross that bridge when he came to it ; with the Rumble-Rumble Fruit, he basically didn’t fear anyone.

Thinking of this, Uchiha An walked with cheerful steps towards the distant Inuzuka Clan compound.

The Inuzuka Clan was a rather famous Ninja clan in Konoha. The Ninjas of the Inuzuka Clan were much stronger than ordinary people in terms of smell, hearing, and vision, and even their intuition was higher than that of ordinary people.

They were usually assigned to serve as guards and law enforcers for Konoha Village.

And every member of the Inuzuka Clan would have a Ninja dog in their childhood, so one could imagine how many dogs were in the Inuzuka Clan compound.

« Woof woof ! »

« Woof woof woof woof ! »

One bark after another broke the originally quiet environment of the Inuzuka Clan.

Inuzuka Tsume hurried out with several clan members to inspect what was happening.

Their faces were full of worry and unease, and it was inevitable that they felt some panic in their hearts.

After all, the Inuzuka Clan had basically never encountered this situation. As combat partners, the Ninja dogs were constantly trained ; for all the Ninja dogs to suddenly become restless, something must have happened.

When Inuzuka Tsume arrived at the scene, she saw a large group of Ninja dogs baring their fangs at Uchiha An, their vicious eyes making people shudder as if they were ready to devour someone at any moment.

However, their trembling bodies and bristling fur revealed the fear and anxiety of the many Ninja dogs.

As the culprit behind this incident, Uchiha An had not expected such a good effect.

A year ago, during the Third Ninja World War, Uchiha An had tried his best to save Itachi, and at that time, Uchiha An seemed to realize that he had awakened a special power—a power that did not belong to this world.
