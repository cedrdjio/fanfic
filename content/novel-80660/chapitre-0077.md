# Chapitre 77 — Instant Kill!

Within the Shinobi Allied Forces of fifty thousand, Ninja after Ninja fell one after another.

It could be said that the majority of the Ninja could not withstand the immense pressure brought about by Uchiha An’s Haoshoku Haki ; their minds and wills were crushed by Uchiha An’s Haoshoku Haki, causing them to collapse to the ground one by one.

Only a few of the strongest were able to barely resist Uchiha An’s Haoshoku Haki, yet they too were pale-faced and drenched in sweat.

Uchiha An’s single use of Haoshoku Haki was astonishingly powerful, instantly taking out the majority of the Shinobi Allied Forces.

Only a small number of powerful Ninja managed to survive, but they were all intimidated by Uchiha An’s terrifying strength, not daring to make any rash moves.

Seeing this scene, the people from Konoha felt immensely joyful and proud. No one had expected Uchiha An to be capable of such a feat ; Uchiha An’s strength even surpassed everyone’s understanding of what a Ninja could be.

« Is this the power of Uchiha An ? It’s truly astonishing. I fear even Shodai(1st)-sama could not have so easily suppressed fifty thousand people in an instant, » Hiruzen Sarutobi said with emotion.

« Sigh ! I never expected Uchiha An to be so outstanding. How can I, as a father, even compare ? »

Although Uchiha Fugaku said this, a smile of relief and pride appeared at the corners of his mouth.

« Hmph ! That technique of his can only affect the weak. It is we who will truly decide the outcome of this battle. »

Although Deva Path Pain’s words were a bit forced, they still steadied some of the panicked Ninja.

Ōnoki and Raikage Ai, the two leaders, felt Uchiha An’s pressure and knew this was a battle of life and death, where failure was not an option.

« Go all out ! Testing him at this moment is completely meaningless ! »

« Hahaha ! I never thought that at my age, I would still be taught a lesson by a youngster. »

Although Ōnoki’s tone was relaxed and easygoing, his eyes were incredibly sharp ; he did not dare to be the slightest bit careless.

Uchiha An’s performance reminded Ōnoki of the enemy who had once struck fear into his heart—the rival of the God of Shinobi, Uchiha Madara.

As the voices of the three leaders died down, all the Ninja present chose to go all out. The four Jinchuriki, in particular, immediately covered themselves in a layer of Tailed Beast shroud, appearing exceptionally serious.

As for Raikage Ai, he directly activated his Chakra Mode, while Nagato summoned the six Pains. The other Ninja also frantically gathered their Chakra, choosing to unleash their strongest techniques immediately.

Because everyone was going all out, a fierce wind blew across the battlefield, kicking up snowflakes, and the entire sky even began to darken.

Seeing this, Uchiha An continued to speak as he walked toward the Shinobi Allied Forces.

« In truth, ever since I began my training, I have rarely had the opportunity to go all out. »

« And now, I don’t even know just how powerful I have become. »

« If I accidentally kill anyone in the coming battle, I hope you all won’t mind. »

Uchiha An’s words revealed a sense of indifference and confidence, sending a chill through the members of the Shinobi Allied Forces.

Everyone did not know if Uchiha An was exaggerating or telling the truth, but they all understood that this battle would be a difficult one.

« Begin ! »

Uchiha An’s voice resounded across the sky like thunder. Then, leaving only an afterimage on the snow, Uchiha An vanished in an instant.

It could be said that Uchiha An’s speed had already surpassed the limits of humanity ; even Yugito, the Two-Tails Jinchuriki, could not track Uchiha An’s movements.

Then, amidst the astonished gazes of the crowd, Uchiha An appeared directly beside Yugito, his right hand tightly clutching a massive Rasengan that emitted a blue light.

« I’m not the type to kill beautiful women, especially those who have been so generous. »

Uchiha An said, his eyes revealing a hint of playfulness and provocation.

Upon hearing Uchiha An’s words, Yugito was extremely shocked. She had not expected Uchiha An to be able to break through her perception and defenses, appearing directly before her.

Yugito immediately tried to react, but unfortunately, it was already too late.

Uchiha An’s Rasengan struck her without mercy, erupting with an intense shockwave that sent her flying backward like a meteor, crashing heavily into the distant snow and losing the ability to fight.

However, in the time it took for Uchiha An to say that, the other Ninja had already reacted and launched attacks at Uchiha An one after another.

Various Ninjutsu intertwined in the air, finally slamming into the spot where Uchiha An had been standing, exploding with a roar and kicking up sheets of snow.

But when the smoke and dust cleared, everyone discovered that there was only an empty crater where Uchiha An had been standing ; as for Uchiha An, he was nowhere to be found.

Uchiha An had utilized his Imitation-Imitation Fruit ability, transforming into an ordinary Ninja within the Shinobi Allied Forces, blending into the crowd while pretending to search for himself.

Uchiha An’s target was Ōnoki, that guy with the Dust Release ability. After all, Dust Release could break an opponent down to the atomic level, and Uchiha An did not believe he could withstand that.

Thus, Uchiha An quietly approached Ōnoki, and while he was distracted searching for him, suddenly attacked.

« Lightning Release : 100 Million Volts : Thunder Dragon ! »

Uchiha An’s voice was deafening, and a massive thunder dragon formed in his right hand, flashing with white light.

The thunder dragon rushed toward Ōnoki at high speed, emitting a roaring sound.

Ōnoki sensed the danger and hurriedly tried to dodge Uchiha An’s attack, but it was too late.

Uchiha An’s thunder dragon was faster and more ferocious than he had imagined, striking Ōnoki directly and erupting with a blinding light and a sky-shaking roar.

Under the effect of the 100 million volts of electricity, Ōnoki was scorched black all over, with smoke rising from his body.

Having taken this blow head-on, Ōnoki lost consciousness and fell to the ground, his fate unknown.

As for whether he still had a sliver of life left, that would depend on the old man’s luck.

« Watch out, this guy Uchiha An can turn into anyone present at any time ! »

Tsuchikage Kurotsuchi had been standing not far from Ōnoki just a moment ago and had witnessed the entire process of Uchiha An’s attack.

It could be said that Kurotsuchi had completely seen an Iwagakure Ninja transform directly into Uchiha An, and had seen how Uchiha An used Lightning Release to defeat Ōnoki.

At this moment, Kurotsuchi’s heart was filled with terror and shock toward Uchiha An.

At Kurotsuchi’s warning, everyone immediately became vigilant, looking at the people around them, not daring to relax in the slightest.

Even brothers and fathers who had gone into battle together silently put some distance between themselves, fearing that Uchiha An might be hiding among them.

But what was even more terrifying was Uchiha An’s strength ; in just a few short minutes, he had taken care of a Jinchuriki and the Kage of a Village. Such strength exceeded everyone’s imagination.

Seeing this, Uchiha An revealed his true form before everyone, his eyes flashing with a red light, a look of arrogance and disdain hanging on the corners of his mouth.

« Defeating you all like this is truly too boring. I’d rather fight you all head-on ! » Uchiha An said, looking provocatively at the Shinobi Allied Forces.
