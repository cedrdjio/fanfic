# Chapitre 53 — The Condition of the Egao Clan!

Newgate saw this and retreated unhurriedly behind the others.

Lightning gathered continuously in his hands, and as he walked slowly, he accumulated energy, preparing to unleash a shocking lightning attack.

Beside Newgate, the lightning intertwined into a flashing spectacle, revealing his immense power.

« Lightning Release : Lightning Dharma King ! »

The ultimate power of lightning roared out, and Newgate struck at the crowd with speed too fast to cover ears, not at the Three-Tails.

Meanwhile, the attacked Three-Tails no longer maintained its gentle posture, roaring angrily at the sky, while Chakra gathered rapidly in its mouth.

In an instant, the people present were struck by the lightning released by Newgate, their bodies stiff and unable to move.

However, the clever Three-Tails seized the opportunity, spitting water bullets from its mouth and slamming its tail into the Ninjas who were besieging it.

The battlefield underwent a dramatic change in an instant ; the Three-Tails' attack completely altered the situation.

The Ninjas struck by the Three-Tails fell immediately, their lives fading like a splash of ink, sinking into the silent surface of the lake.

Meanwhile, those hit by the water bullets fired by the Three-Tails were severely injured and could no longer continue to participate in the battle.

It could be said that the originally dominant Hidden Mist Village group immediately fell into a disadvantage, and the plan to capture the Three-Tails became even more difficult.

« Newgate, you damn bastard, you did that on purpose, didn’t you ! »

Egao Hekong roared fiercely at Newgate, a trace of sinister intent revealing itself in his eyes.

Egao Jianfang, however, had a normal expression, his tone remaining very calm.

« Newgate, if the mission fails, you will have to take responsibility when you go back. You don’t want to disappoint that woman, Mei Terumī, do you ? »

Newgate waved his hand dismissively. « It’s just a mere Three-Tails ; I’ll handle it easily. »

As Newgate’s voice fell, lightning danced wildly in the sky, breaking the tranquility of the lake.

It could be said that Newgate’s Rumble-Rumble Fruit had been developed to an incredible level ; even if this move had no attack power, it directly maxed out Newgate’s aura.

The Three-Tails, seeing this, continued to condense Chakra, emitting a suffocating, wild energy.

« Newgate, you don’t have much time left. You must act quickly ; otherwise, after this blow, it is very likely that the Three-Tails will submerge back into the water. »

Newgate glanced at Egao Jianfang ; he feared this guy was plotting something.

However, Newgate didn’t care about these details ; he just activated the ability of the Rumble-Rumble Fruit, gathering the power of lightning in his hands.

Lightning arcs crisscrossed, and the light illuminated the entire lake.

« Lightning Release : Lightning Ball Technique ! »

In an instant, the huge lightning ball struck the Three-Tails directly, emitting a violent explosion.

The lake surface split as if it had been cut open, and a huge water column churned up, forming a barrier like a city wall.

Seeing this scene, the Ninjas who had retreated to rest opened their mouths wide, astonished by the power of Newgate’s strike.

« What a powerful guy ! »

« Are we going to be enemies with him next ? »

Newgate naturally didn’t know their thoughts, but the attacks in his hands did not stop, continuously releasing lightning energy to attack the Three-Tails.

« Roar ! »

Facing Newgate, who kept bombarding it, the Three-Tails let out a deafening howl, then used its massive body and Water Release Ninjutsu to continuously attack Newgate, pouring out Chakra as if it were worthless.

But Newgate relied on the ability of the Rumble-Rumble Fruit to change his position rapidly, skillfully dodging every attack from the Three-Tails.

As time passed, the frequency of the Three-Tails' attacks became slower and slower, and Newgate used the instant bursts of the Rumble-Rumble Fruit to successfully create a series of consecutive strikes.

« Go to hell ! Watch my Thunder Breathing, Seventh Form : Fire Thunder God ! »

Of course, this was just Newgate shouting in a chuunibyou manner ; in reality, this move was a strike where the lightning generated high temperatures.

In an instant, Newgate’s whole body radiated dazzling lightning brilliance, condensing into a huge lightning blade.

In the astonished eyes of everyone, the huge lightning blade cut through the sky, followed by a loud noise. A brilliant light erupted from the Three-Tails, and then explosions sounded one after another, stirring up a water curtain dozens of feet high.

Accompanying the water curtain slowly descending, the Three-Tails' figure gradually revealed itself before everyone’s eyes.

However, at this moment, the Three-Tails had completely lost self-awareness, its charred body rolling powerlessly, floating quietly on the surface of the water.

This scene actually made everyone present feel an inexplicable sense of pity.

The Three-Tails, which had been majestic just a moment ago, had now become so fragile and helpless, as if it had lost all its strength.

« Quick, go seal it ! The power of the Three-Tails will be mine ! »

Egao Hekong’s high-pitched clamor woke everyone from their state of shock, forcing them back to reality.

Immediately after, the sealing squad began to nervously prepare for the operation to seal the Three-Tails.

However, Egao Jianfang’s heart was filled with regret.

From the beginning, his plan was to launch a sneak attack during the final clash between Newgate and the Three-Tails, hoping to take the opportunity to make a fatal strike and kill Newgate directly.

However, he had been too deeply immersed while watching the battle, even forgetting his original plan, which made Egao Jianfang feel extremely guilty toward himself, inwardly condemning his own carelessness.

For this mistake, Egao Jianfang even wanted to give himself a few slaps.

This also made Egao Jianfang realize that this negligence had almost pushed the entire plan to the edge of failure ; now, the difficulty of killing Newgate was too great.

However, Newgate was directly angered to laughter by the two from the Egao Clan, and he went up and slapped Egao Hekong in the face.

« Damn it, you’ve been watching the show on the sidelines, and you still want to take the Three-Tails from me ? You Egao Clan people have really thick faces ! »

Newgate’s voice echoed in the field, and a heavy pressure caused the atmosphere to freeze instantly.

Egao Hekong stood there, his eyes flickering, struggling with what to do now.

And Egao Jianfang glared fiercely at his son, feeling only that he was mud that couldn’t be helped onto the wall.

So Egao Jianfang’s voice sounded in the silence : « Newgate, you have consumed too much power in the fierce battle with the Three-Tails just now, and you have no extra strength to take on the task of becoming the Three-Tails Jinchuriki. Rather than letting you be overwhelmed and even face life-threatening danger, why not let the Three-Tails go to Hekong. This can be considered the Egao Clan owing you a favor. If you have any intention of becoming the Mizukage, we are willing to provide help. »

It must be said that Egao Jianfang was much more capable than Egao Hekong, but Newgate would not indulge this guy.

« Thanks for the suggestion, Clan Leader Egao, but I just want to say, are you worthy ? »

Newgate’s voice was firm and decisive, making the atmosphere at the scene even colder.

Then Newgate looked at Egao Hekong and smiled : « As his son, do you dare to fight your old man for the Three-Tails ? »
