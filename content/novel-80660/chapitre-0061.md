# Chapitre 61 — Orochimaru!

Two years later. Land of Rice Fields.

As a country bordering the Land of Fire, the living environment was actually quite good.

At this moment, An was walking along a small path with Kisame Hoshigaki, heading toward Otogakure, which was not far away.

« Halt ! » In an instant, four figures appeared in front of An and Kisame Hoshigaki.

An had already known that someone was lying in ambush ahead, but he hadn’t expected the Sound Five to appear.

Of course, that weakling Kimimaro wasn’t here, so the guys who appeared were just four and a half people.

« Looking for Orochimaru. Lead the way ! »

After two years of training, the honest Kisame Hoshigaki had accepted all of An’s information and was now fully fulfilling his role as a lackey.

The hot-headed Sakon immediately spoke up, « Who do you think you are, daring to address Lord Orochimaru like that ! »

In an instant, Kisame Hoshigaki transformed into his hybrid form of the Ancient Zoan Shark-Shark Fruit : Megalodon model, and then threw a fierce punch at Sakon and Ukon.

« Boom ! » Sakon and Ukon were pressed directly into the ground by Kisame Hoshigaki’s massive hand, their heads exploding in the same spot, which looked extremely bloody.

It could be said that the current Kisame Hoshigaki was a Tailed Beast with a personality and self-awareness ; of course, this Tailed Beast only took orders from An.

Kisame Hoshigaki’s decisive strike also effectively intimidated the other three, making them afraid to move. After all, with the strongest of the four, Sakon and Ukon, being taken out in one move, the others had enough self-awareness to know their place.

« Take me to find Orochimaru ! » At this moment, the three didn’t dare to speak, so Tayuya took the initiative to step forward. « Please follow me, my lord ! »

An nodded coldly. In reality, An actually quite liked this sharp-tongued Tayuya.

While traveling toward Otogakure surrounded by the three of them, An had already grown accustomed to this kind of situation.

Upon arriving in the Village, An realized that what was said on the internet in his previous life was quite reliable ; this guy Orochimaru was a pure human trafficker. Otogakure was full of minors ; there were hardly any adults.

Before long, they arrived in the center of the Village. In An’s Observation Haki perception, there was a massive base underground.

No wonder the animal representing Orochimaru was a snake ; this living environment was not much different from a snake’s.

Soon, An and Kisame Hoshigaki were brought by Tayuya to an office on the third underground level, where Orochimaru was sitting in a chair, wearing a white lab coat.

« Oh ! Sashiburidana ! Kisame, is this your new partner ? »

« Orochimaru, this is my master ! »

In reality, An did not want Kisame Hoshigaki to address him like that, but Kisame Hoshigaki insisted on doing so.

Orochimaru was immediately startled when he heard Kisame Hoshigaki’s words.

He hadn’t expected that this Hidden Mist Village missing-nin had also subdued Kisame Hoshigaki, another Hidden Mist Village missing-nin. It could be said that Orochimaru was also very covetous of Kisame Hoshigaki’s strength.

« Oh ! Hidden Mist Village missing-nin, Three-Tails Jinchuriki, known as the world’s strongest man—you certainly have many titles ! »

An sensed that Orochimaru looked down on him a bit ; after all, those with high intelligence generally didn’t think much of Jinchuriki.

If a Jinchuriki’s IQ were too high, they probably wouldn’t get along well with their Tailed Beast.

« Lightning Body Flicker ! » As An’s voice fell, his figure appeared before Orochimaru’s eyes and unleashed a punch.

« So fast ! » After exclaiming this, Orochimaru immediately raised his arms to block An’s attack, but unfortunately, it was all in vain.

« Bang ! Bang ! » Two shattering sounds rang out ; one was the sound of Orochimaru’s chair breaking, and the other was the sound of Orochimaru crashing into the wall.

Then, An removed his disguise and returned to his original appearance.

« Orochimaru, the research on the Calm-Calm Fruit hasn’t yielded any results, has it ? »

In an instant, Orochimaru knew who the figure before him was.

« I didn’t expect that the Hidden Mist Village Three-Tails Jinchuriki would actually be the leader of Konoha’s Root, known as the Lightning God of Konoha, Uchiha An. »

Tayuya was immediately shocked. She hadn’t expected the identity of the person in front of her to be so complicated, and now that she had heard this secret, she was likely in danger.

Just then, An turned his head and glared at Tayuya, which gave her quite a scare.

After the horseplay, An looked at Orochimaru with a playful expression. « In the competition for the position of Hokage, you lost to Minato-sensei, and plus the Third Hokage Hiruzen Sarutobi wouldn’t let you research forbidden jutsu ; I’m afraid you harbor quite a bit of hatred toward Konoha ! »

« How about it ? Do you want to destroy Konoha and carry out a Konoha Crush plan ? »

An’s tone was very low, like a creepy uncle trying to lure a child.

The reason An said this was that he felt Orochimaru definitely didn’t have a Konoha Crush plan like in the original story right now.

After all, Minato Namikaze and Kushina were still alive and well. Relying on just a rampaging Ichibi Jinchuriki and Orochimaru himself, there was no way they had the strength to crush Konoha.

However, hearing this, Orochimaru felt a stir in his heart, a feeling of wanting to take revenge on Konoha.

« Then, Lord An, what do you think I should do ? I wonder what role you would play in this ? »

Regarding Orochimaru’s question, An just smiled and said nothing.

Then, An pointed his finger at Tayuya, a smile appearing on his face. « I want her. »

« No problem. »

Tayuya hadn’t expected that she would be sold off so easily, without even a chance to voice her own opinion.

Having obtained what he wanted, An continued, « The Chunin Exams are imminent. Konoha will be inviting Sunagakure to participate, and the Ichibi Jinchuriki and the Fourth Kazekage will be there. »

« It’s just that the relationship between the Sunagakure Kazekage and the Jinchuriki is poor. We will intercept and kill the Kazekage, and then you, Orochimaru, will impersonate the Kazekage and enter Konoha. »

« When the Ichibi goes on a rampage, we will make our move. Kisame and I will hold off Minato Namikaze and Kushina. I can even order the Uchiha Clan and Root not to intervene. The rest will be up to you. »

An had simply added a little something extra to the Konoha Crush plan from the original story, but to Orochimaru, it felt like he had found a kindred spirit ; the Jinchuriki in front of him was not a low-IQ individual.

« Fine. On the last day of the Chunin Exams, we will begin our Konoha Crush plan. »

In reality, it wasn’t that Orochimaru was gullible ; it was that he was confident he could retreat unscathed under any circumstances.

Just like that, An completed his scheme and left Otogakure with Kisame Hoshigaki and Tayuya.

Along the way, Tayuya remained silent, clearly worried about her future.

« Um... » Tayuya just wanted to say something, but she was immediately interrupted by An.

« Just be a good little lackey for me ; leave everything else to me. »

Thus, An decided on Tayuya’s arrangement, and at the same time, he gained a little lackey.

Next, An left Otogakure and returned to Konoha ; his plan was about to begin.
