# Chapitre 43 — Byakugan, Killer, Ao!

The portico of the main hall was carved with the meticulously crafted emblem of the Hidden Mist Village, symbolizing the ownership of this place.

Next, Takayama Yume led Newgate inside, where only a few figures could be seen working within the main hall.

Upon seeing one person, Takayama Yume became visibly excited and whispered to Newgate.

« Lord Newgate, your luck is quite good this time ; Lord Ao, the bodyguard of the Fifth Mizukage, has come for an inspection. »

« If you can catch his eye, you will be a trusted aide to the Mizukage directly. »

In reality, Newgate had also been carefully observing Ao in the crowd.

As a representative Jonin of the Hidden Mist Village, Ao’s most striking feature was his unique Byakugan, while his other, more powerful abilities remained unrevealed.

Of course, the current Ao was not the seemingly aged figure from his introduction, but instead radiated the vitality and charm of his prime.

Looking several years younger, Ao stood tall with a resolute expression, exuding an aura of maturity and composure.

« Takayama Yume ! Is there something going on ? »

The one who spoke was Bi, Ao’s close partner and also the person in charge of this base.

In fact, Bi had long noticed Newgate standing to the side ; after all, the Newgate that Newgate was portraying was nearly three meters tall, and such a formidable height was hard to ignore in the Hokage world.

Of course, in the One Piece world, you would be too embarrassed to call yourself a powerhouse if you weren’t at least two and a half meters tall.

Bi’s meticulous observation sparked a keen interest in him ; such height and muscle mass were things that couldn’t be faked.

« Newgate, are you the one who wants to join the Hidden Mist Village ? »

Newgate smiled and said : « If the Hidden Mist Village is worth me joining, then naturally I will not refuse. »

Both Ao and Bi could hear the implication in Newgate’s words, realizing that the person before them likely had his own agenda.

However, considering the Hidden Mist Village’s urgent need for fresh blood, they decided to keep an open attitude toward recruiting talent and naturally would not refuse the addition of a powerhouse.

However, the name Newgate was indeed uncommon, which caused Ao and Bi to have some confusion and doubts about Newgate’s identity.

« Then let’s spar, and let me see if you have the qualifications to join the Hidden Mist Village ! »

After speaking, Ao did not give anyone else a chance to talk and walked straight outside.

Bi immediately realized the situation was off and quickly explained.

« Ao’s attitude might be a bit impulsive, but he just wants to spar with you once to understand your strength. This will also allow us to get to know each other better. »

Bi’s tone was calm and friendly, hoping to ease the atmosphere with his explanation.

After all, Ao’s straightforwardness might have left a bad impression, so he decided to play the role of a mediator, showing a side completely different from Ao.

Newgate did not care about Bi’s explanation and followed Ao straight outside.

Seeing this, the others immediately followed, eager to witness the fierce battle that was about to unfold.

When Newgate followed Ao to the training ground, a large crowd had already gathered there, creating a bustling scene.

However, when everyone saw Newgate’s physique, they were all taken aback ; after all, hearing about and seeing Newgate’s stature and presence were two completely different things, and everyone felt surprised, unable to help but show expressions of astonishment.

When Newgate saw the large crowd gathered around, he decided to stir up trouble directly.

« Be my son ! »

When they heard Newgate’s words, everyone was shocked beyond measure.

Is the Ninja World like this now ? Inviting someone to be your son just upon meeting ?

The onlookers showed expressions of disbelief, looking at this strange guy Newgate with surprised eyes.

And at the center of the storm, Ao, upon hearing Newgate’s words, had veins bulging on his forehead, was stunned for a moment, and then couldn’t help but twitch the corner of his mouth.

« If you get beaten to death, don’t blame me ! »

At this moment, Ao was also mentally breaking down ; he originally thought he had successfully recruited a powerful Ninja, but he didn’t expect the other party to be a lunatic !

Wanting to be his father right off the bat was simply inconceivable.

Ao tried hard to control the anger in his heart ; he took a deep breath, trying to remain calm, and stared sharply at Newgate, a flash of disdain in his eyes.

« Water Release : Water Piercing Gun ! »

Since Ao had determined that Newgate was a lunatic, he didn’t intend to be polite to him.

Thus, Ao launched the first attack without any sportsmanship, the water droplets in the air shooting towards Newgate like real bullets.

Newgate sensed the danger and reacted quickly, symbolically forming hand seals.

« Earth Release : Earth Wall ! »

In an instant, an earth wall one foot thick appeared in front of Newgate, like a solid barrier.

The water droplets slammed into the earth wall, emitting a booming sound of explosion, and the splashed chunks of earth flew everywhere.

Seeing this, Ao didn’t say much and used the Clone Technique without hesitation.

Several figures crisscrossed like phantoms, launching frantic attacks around Newgate, with Water Release Ninjutsu being unleashed continuously, as if his Chakra was inexhaustible.

Newgate had already activated Observation Haki, his own sensory ability greatly enhanced, capturing Ao’s figure at every moment and blocking every one of Ao’s attacks with Earth Release Ninjutsu one by one.

The onlookers witnessed this scene and were amazed. Everyone hadn’t expected Newgate to have such excellent defense, making such rapid reactions in a short time and successfully blocking Ao’s attacks.

« Newgate, not bad at all, you are much stronger than the average Ninja. »

Ao looked at Newgate with an expression full of appreciation, feeling very satisfied with Newgate’s performance ; with Newgate’s strength, it was fully sufficient for him to become a member of the Hidden Mist Village.

However, Ao also knew that he could not easily be defeated by Newgate, otherwise, not only would he be looked down upon, but it might even affect the reputation and dignity of the entire Hidden Mist Village.

So Ao silently activated his Byakugan, clearly seeing the flow of Chakra within Newgate and Newgate’s strong figure.

« Water Release : Water Shockwave ! »

Turbulent Chakra energy gathered in Ao’s throat, waiting for the right moment to strike.

Suddenly, Ao sprayed a stream of water like a waterfall directly from his mouth, violently charging towards Newgate.

Seeing this scene, the onlookers retreated, fearing being caught in the aftermath of Ao’s powerful Ninjutsu.

However, as Ao’s partner, Bi knew that this was precisely Ao’s display of going all out ; I’m afraid this guy named Newgate had pushed Ao to the limit.

Even facing such an extensive Water Release Ninjutsu attack from Ao, Newgate remained calm, his hand seals executed without the slightest fluster.

« Earth Release : Earth Pillar Technique ! »

In an instant, a huge stone pillar seemed to appear out of thin air under Newgate’s feet, presenting a perfect circular platform shape.

Then, the rock pillar grew rapidly, quickly lifting Newgate off the ground.

The massive water flow continuously washed over the rock pillar under Newgate’s feet, sweeping away some gravel, while Newgate’s figure stood firmly at the top of the rock pillar, unshakeable.

The washing of the water flow was more like a gentle breeze brushing against Newgate’s body, having no effect on him at all.
