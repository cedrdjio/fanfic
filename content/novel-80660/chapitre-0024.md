# Chapitre 24 — Graduation Exam!

Six months later.

« What ? All three of you want to apply for early graduation ? »

Shisui, Uchiha Itachi, and Uchiha An arrived at the Hokage Office early in the morning to apply for early graduation ; after all, the Ninja Academy curriculum was truly meaningless.

Minato Namikaze looked at the three brats in front of him with a helpless expression. Were young people these days really this competitive ? They wanted to graduate at seven years old.

Uchiha An : « Minato-sensei, you should know our strength. Continuing to study is just a waste of time. »

Shisui and Uchiha Itachi nodded in agreement. Who would want to waste time in the Ninja Academy when they could be out performing missions as a Ninja ?

« Shisui is only two years away from graduation, so he can apply for early graduation. » After saying this, Minato Namikaze looked at Uchiha An and Uchiha Itachi standing to the side. « You two brothers have only been in the Ninja Academy for a year, and you want to apply for graduation now ? »

« For geniuses like me and Uchiha Itachi, it’s only reasonable to have some privileges, right ? Mi—na—to—sen—sei ! »

After saying this, Uchiha An winked at Minato Namikaze, hoping to get by with some cuteness.

Minato Namikaze tugged at his yellow hair in distress, truly not knowing how to handle the three of them.

If he allowed the three to graduate early, Uchiha An and Uchiha Itachi were simply too young. But if he refused directly, Minato Namikaze felt embarrassed to say it, as the Ninja Academy did allow early graduation.

However, Minato Namikaze quickly thought of a good idea.

« Since the three of you want to graduate early, and the Ninja Academy happens to allow early graduation, then I will naturally allow it. »

« However, early graduation requires an examination. If you don’t pass the exam, you cannot graduate early. »

The expected look of frustration did not appear, and Minato Namikaze rubbed his chin sheepishly.

« Since the three of you have no objections, let’s hold the exam tomorrow morning at Training Ground 21 ! »

« Yes, Hokage-sama ! » × 3

After saying this, the three quickly retreated from the Hokage Office, and Minato Namikaze quickly made arrangements regarding their early graduation.

After leaving the Hokage Building, the three finally completely relaxed.

« Uchiha An, what kind of exam do you think the Fourth Hokage will arrange for us ? »

Uchiha An picked his nose while turning his head to look at Shisui and Uchiha Itachi.

« What could Minato-sensei arrange ? At most, he’ll just find a few people to fight us. »

« I don’t think it will be that simple. »

After saying this, Uchiha Itachi had a contemplative expression ; it was clear he was thinking about the methods the Fourth Hokage might use.

Seeing this, Uchiha An stepped forward, wrapped his arm around Uchiha Itachi’s neck, and pulled him to his side.

« Don’t worry, with our strength, we can easily handle whatever moves Minato-sensei throws at us. »

Meanwhile. Inside the Hokage Building.

The arrangements for the three of them applying for early graduation at the same time were made, and Danzo, the leader of Root, quickly received this information.

Danzo had some theories about the three graduating early, but he didn’t expect it to be this soon.

« Do you know about this information ? »

Hearing this, Shimura Kazama shook his head with a bitter smile. Although he worked at the Ninja Academy, how could he constantly pay attention to three students who were basically never at school ?

« Since that’s the case, go and contact the three of them to see if they are willing to join Root. At the very least, you must recruit one person into Root. »

« Yes, Clan Leader ! »

Shimura Kazama dared not refute Danzo’s arrangement, but he knew that even getting one person wouldn’t be easy, as all three were named Uchiha.

If he had to recruit someone, Shimura Kazama hoped that person would be Uchiha An.

Following Danzo’s order, Shimura Kazama directly took a large group of Root members to guard the perimeter of the Uchiha Clan.

The three, who knew nothing of all this, were wandering around Konoha.

Especially halfway through their walk, Uchiha An suddenly got interested and took Shisui and Uchiha Itachi to have a barbecue on the Hokage Rock.

Just like that, Shimura Kazama, who had rushed all the way to the Uchiha Clan grounds, successfully missed Uchiha An and the others.

… The next day.

Following Minato Namikaze’s request, Uchiha An, Uchiha Itachi, and Shisui arrived at Training Ground 21 before nine o’clock.

However, what caught their attention was why so many people had come for what was supposed to be a simple graduation exam.

Sandaime Hiruzen Sarutobi, Fourth Hokage Minato Namikaze, Hokage Assistant Shikaku, and of course, Danzo and Shimura Kazama.

However, Danzo and Shimura Kazama both looked unpleasant ; after all, they had been stood up by the three yesterday.

Minato Namikaze looked at the three youths in front of him with a smile.

« Although the difficulty of this exam is a bit high, I still hope you can pass it. »

Minato Namikaze recognized their strength, but with Danzo here this time, he always felt that something unexpected might happen.

« Hurry up ! Minato-sensei, I can’t wait to graduate. »

Hearing Uchiha An’s childish words, everyone’s faces showed a hint of a smile.

Then Minato Namikaze clapped his hands, and three figures behind him instantly landed in front of Uchiha An and the others.

« These three are my personal guard, and they are also Konoha’s Special Jonin. »

« The three of you pick an opponent each. As long as you can hold out for three minutes, your graduation exam will be considered passed. »

In reality, the early graduation exam for ordinary students would at most involve fighting a Chunin, but Minato Namikaze saw that Uchiha An, Uchiha Itachi, and Shisui were simply too talented, so he came up with this method.

Raido Namiashi, Aoba Yamashiro, and Shiranui Genma looked at each other, and then the corners of their mouths curled up slightly.

Two seven-year-olds and one eleven-year-old brat actually required them to make a move ; it could be said that the three of them were somewhat annoyed by Minato Namikaze’s arrangement.

« Minato-sensei, what if we win ? »

Although Uchiha An’s voice wasn’t loud, everyone present was stunned by his words.

Before Minato Namikaze could say anything more, the anger of Shiranui Genma and the others had already been ignited by Uchiha An.

Raido Namiashi : « Uchiha brat, do you know what you are saying ? »

Aoba Yamashiro : « Very well, you have completely angered me. »

Shiranui Genma : « You three will definitely fail this graduation exam. »

Watching the tense atmosphere in the field, Hiruzen Sarutobi and Shikaku wanted to intervene, but Minato Namikaze raised a hand, and the two stopped.

Minato Namikaze smiled brightly. The tense, sword-drawn atmosphere did not affect his mood to watch the show at all ; clearly, he was very much looking forward to the upcoming battle.

« If you three win, I can teach you some rare Ninjutsu, very rare ! Of course, if you three don’t hold out for three minutes, then go back to school ! »
