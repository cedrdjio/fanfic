# Chapitre 80 — Internal Strife!

A loud boom echoed as the thunderclouds in the sky churned, as if about to tear the entire world apart.

From that massive sphere, a beam of white light suddenly shot out, like a divine judgment, illuminating all the Ninja on the battlefield.

« Lightning Release : Hundredfold Thunder God ! »

Uchiha An’s soul-stirring voice announced endless destruction.

Under the impact of the light beam, the giant sphere burst into countless fragments, scattering into the air.

And in the center of the fragments, a Lightning Giant woven from lightning slowly emerged.

The Lightning Giant had a human form but exuded a majesty that transcended humanity.

As time passed, the figure of the Thunder God continued to expand and grow, as if it wanted to occupy the entire sky, making everyone fear its existence.

One hundred meters.

Two hundred meters.

Five hundred meters.

Finally, the Thunder God controlled by Uchiha An reached a height of nearly eight hundred meters, almost connecting with the clouds.

Just then, Uchiha An also stopped the transformation and maintained his form.

But the Lightning Giant controlled by Uchiha An did not stop releasing lightning ; every action of Uchiha An was accompanied by thunder and lightning, making people terrified.

Just as the figure of the Thunder God became larger and more majestic, Nagato, who was hidden in the shadows far from the battlefield, also encountered his own crisis.

Nagato, hidden in a cave, only felt a powerful killing intent approaching, and then saw two familiar yet strange faces appear before him.

« What is it ? Obito ! Zetsu ! »

At this final moment of the war, Nagato no longer held anything back.

In reality, Nagato had never trusted the so-called Uchiha Madara, and even regarding Zetsu, who had always been a comic relief character, Nagato, despite possessing the Rinnegan, admitted he had not seen through him.

However, Nagato believed that shout from Uchiha An on the battlefield, and he called out « Obito » with a hint of doubt.

« Leader, I don’t want to hide it from you anymore. I am Uchiha Obito, and the reason I joined the Akatsuki Organization was to resurrect Lord Uchiha Madara. »

Obito revealed a mysterious smile, and as he spoke, he pointed to Zetsu beside him.

« Zetsu is the manifestation of Lord Uchiha Madara’s will. Our goal is to resurrect Lord Uchiha Madara. »

Zetsu did not hide his identity either ; several figures were seen emerging from the dirt inside the cave—these were the White Zetsu hidden in the shadows.

« You... why are you doing this ? »

Nagato felt a wave of helplessness. Even though he didn’t have absolute trust in the two, he still treated them as comrades.

It turned out that the two, who had always been mysterious, did indeed have ulterior motives for joining the Akatsuki Organization, which greatly disappointed Nagato.

« Then your goal must be my Rinnegan ! You want to cast the Samsara Heavenly Life Technique to resurrect Uchiha Madara. »

Nagato quickly understood their true target : his own pair of Rinnegan.

The Rinnegan can cast the heaven-defying Ninjutsu known as the Samsara Heavenly Life Technique. The effect of this technique is to resurrect the dead, but the cost is the user’s own life.

« However, the Samsara Heavenly Life Technique can only resurrect those who have died recently. Otherwise, how could I have watched Yahiko... »

As Nagato said this, his voice suddenly choked with emotion.

Nagato remembered what Yahiko had said to him before he died, remembered Yahiko’s expectations for him, and remembered Yahiko’s dream.

Then, before Nagato could finish speaking, he was directly interrupted by Zetsu.

« Rest assured ! Leader, we naturally have our own ways to resurrect our master. »

Zetsu said coldly, his eyes devoid of any emotion, seemingly filled only with loyalty and adoration for Uchiha Madara.

« And Leader, you have also seen the situation on the battlefield now. If we don’t defeat Uchiha An, I’m afraid our plan will never be realized. »

Zetsu pointed to the Thunder God in the distant sky, seemingly to demonstrate Uchiha An’s power.

« Now is the time for us to be honest with each other and realize our common ideal. »

Hearing this, Nagato hesitated ; after all, his own goals were completely different from the ideas of these two guys in front of him.

Seeing the hesitation on Nagato’s face, Obito and Zetsu exchanged glances.

« If you are unwilling, Leader, then we will do it ourselves. »

After Zetsu finished speaking, he and Obito launched an attack on Nagato.

The battle ahead was extremely intense, and the two did not want to waste time. They only wanted to seize Nagato’s Rinnegan as soon as possible and then use the Samsara Heavenly Life Technique to resurrect Uchiha Madara.

Nagato was forced into a corner by their attacks, feeling only a sense of powerlessness and despair.

...

Three minutes later, Obito and Zetsu walked out of the cave, greeted by a gloomy sky and Uchiha An’s unrivaled, massive body.

Obito’s eyes had undergone a stunning change ; his original Sharingan had disappeared, replaced by the eyes of the Six Paths Sage—the Rinnegan—exuding a mysterious and powerful aura.

« Start, Obito ! »

Zetsu did not hesitate at all. He rushed directly toward the nearest White Zetsu, grabbed its neck, and pinned it firmly to the ground, as if doing something very mundane.

Obito, however, stood still, a complex expression on his face.

Seeing that Obito did not move, Zetsu immediately sensed Obito’s hesitation.

So Zetsu frowned and said in a cold and unquestionable tone : « You don’t think that just because you have the Rinnegan, you can defeat that guy Uchiha An, do you ? You have seen his strength with your own eyes ; he is an existence that transcends common sense. Even with the Rinnegan, you are simply no match for him. »

« For now, hurry up and resurrect our master. Only the master can defeat Uchiha An and complete our plan. Do not forget why we are doing these things ; do not forget the ideal we are pursuing. »

After listening to Zetsu’s advice, the conflict in Obito’s heart subsided slightly.

Then, Obito took a deep breath, looked at Zetsu with a firm gaze, and nodded heavily.

At this moment, Obito knew he could no longer hesitate ; he had to complete his mission—for Uchiha Madara, for his own plan, and also for her.

Thus, the two assumed their respective roles and began the final step of resurrecting Uchiha Madara.

Zetsu showed no mercy, directly beating the White Zetsu in his hand until it was severely injured, throwing it onto the ground, and then skillfully and quickly formed hand seals.

A trace of fanaticism and excitement flashed in Zetsu’s eyes. He had waited too long for this moment ; to resurrect his mother, Zetsu did not even know how long he had been plotting.

Although there had been variables in his plotting, as the plan proceeded step by step to this point, Zetsu felt that success was not far away.

« Edo Tensei ! »

With a loud shout from Zetsu, the sound echoed continuously in the gloomy sky, as if telling this world.
