# Chapitre 35 — Target, Seven-Tails Chomei!

One week later. On the streets of Konoha Village.

An, who was strolling with Inuzuka Hana, was suddenly blocked by a figure.

« Danzo-sama has a new order ! »

« An, who is this guy ? »

Inuzuka Hana immediately stood in front of An, pointing her Kunai directly at the person.

And Inuzuka Hana’s Ninja dog, Snow Cloud, also bared its teeth and glared vigilantly at the guy who had suddenly jumped out.

« Torune, it’s one thing that you don’t have a partner, but you also lack any sense of tact. »

« Can’t you see I’m on a date with my girlfriend ? You really came at a bad time. »

Hearing An’s words, Inuzuka Hana immediately blushed and shyly lowered her head.

The expression under Aburame Torune’s mask turned ugly. Two eight or nine-year-old brats dating and walking the streets ? Do young people these days play so fast and loose ?

Helpless, Aburame Torune could only say seriously.

« Danzo-sama needs you for an important operation, otherwise I wouldn’t disturb your rare vacation. »

« Fine ! »

After saying goodbye to Inuzuka Hana, An followed Aburame Torune’s pace.

Along the way, An and Aburame Torune jumped across the rooftops of Konoha Village. This made An feel a bit awkward, so he casually put on his Peppa Pig mask.

« Why are we heading toward the center of Konoha Village ? Is Danzo-sama waiting for us at the Hokage Building ? »

« Just keep up. »

Looking at the unyielding Aburame Torune, An could only follow in silence.

Soon, the two saw the towering Hokage Building not far ahead.

Suddenly, Aburame Torune’s figure dropped directly, jumping straight into the sewer below.

« What the ! »

An didn’t expect this trick. This meant he was probably going to the real headquarters of Root, the sewer where Danzo often appeared in the anime.

So, An had to hold his nose and jump down, diving into the sewer.

After jumping down, An discovered that it was completely different from the scene of garbage-strewn, foul-smelling filth he had imagined. Even though there were some pungent odors, it was overall clean and tidy.

« Keep up ! »

An and Aburame Torune turned left and right through the sewers until they reached the main hub.

In the center of the sewer was a huge circular platform, with four high bridges connecting to the surrounding four directions, suspended in the air.

There were already three figures on the circular platform.

Danzo Shimura was rarely seen leaning on his cane, his expression looking very serious.

Through Observation Haki, An perceived that one of the other two figures was Shimura Kazama, but he felt no familiarity with the other one.

« You’re here, An. Your performance over the past year has been good. »

« It is all thanks to Danzo-sama’s guidance. »

Hearing this, a trace of a smile appeared on Danzo Shimura’s face ; he was clearly quite satisfied with An’s answer.

The others were astonished to see this ; was this really that cold, stern leader of Root ?

Seeing that everyone had arrived, Danzo Shimura tapped the ground with his cane.

« Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi of Takigakure was seriously injured during the war with the Cloud Ninja. Takigakure is likely looking for a new Jinchuriki to control the Seven-Tailed. »

« Therefore, your mission is to sneak into Takigakure and do your best to capture the Seven-Tailed. »

« This mission will be led by Yamato as captain. You must complete this mission. »

As Danzo Shimura’s voice fell, everyone understood Danzo’s plan.

Currently, the Nine-Tails Jinchuriki is still Kushina, and Kushina’s relationship with Danzo is not very good.

As the leader of Root, Danzo Shimura naturally would not miss the opportunity to obtain a Tailed Beast, even if it might start a war.

An muttered to himself, « Yamato ? »

Yamato was the code name for Yamato in Root. Currently, Yamato had not yet gone to steal Kakashi’s Sharingan and was still an outstanding Wood Release Ninja of Root.

After giving the order, Danzo Shimura left the place.

« Gather at the main gate of Konoha Village at nine tomorrow morning ! »

After saying this, Yamato turned and left, leaving everyone with a cold, aloof back.

An, Aburame Torune, and Shimura Kazama took off their masks ; after all, they were all acquainted with each other.

« A year has passed in a flash. Who would have thought that An would have made such a big name for himself ? Danzo-sama has certainly praised you quite a bit ! »

Shimura Kazama’s tone was very helpless ; An was no longer someone he could manipulate.

However, the conflict between the two still existed, and this operation was a good opportunity.

« If there’s nothing else, I’ll take my leave. »

An didn’t want to bicker with Shimura Kazama here. They were leaving tomorrow, and he had things to do.

After speaking, An nodded at Aburame Torune and then flashed away.

Seeing that An was unwilling to pay attention to him, Shimura Kazama secretly hated him in his heart, firming his resolve to kill An.

...

After An crawled out of the sewer, he didn’t go home ; instead, he turned and went to find Kushina.

When An appeared at Minato Namikaze’s home, Naruto was playing at the door.

« Big Brother An, are you here to see me ? »

« Yo ! Naruto, playing by yourself ? I’m here to see your teacher’s wife. »

After Naruto said this, he immediately trotted back into the house.

Looking at the chubby little Naruto, An felt a sense of accomplishment.

With Minato Namikaze and Kushina present, the current Naruto didn’t have to drink expired milk or eat junk food. The current Naruto was the true Prince of Konoha.

« An, you’re here. Naruto said you were looking for me ? »

As Kushina spoke, she wanted to step forward and skillfully pick An up, but An didn’t want to experience that again, so he immediately flashed away to dodge.

« Teacher’s wife, I have to leave again tomorrow. I came to ask you for a favor. »

« It’s rare for a genius like you to ask me for something. Come in ! »

After saying this, Kushina led An into the house and thoughtfully poured him a glass of water.

Having run around for the better part of the day, An didn’t stand on ceremony and picked up the cup, drinking it in one gulp.

« Teacher’s wife is a member of the Uzumaki Clan and has the Nine-Tailed inside her, so your sealing technique must be very powerful. »

« My mission this time involves a Tailed Beast, so I wanted to come and learn sealing techniques from you. »

Knowing An’s purpose, a look of hesitation appeared on Kushina’s face ; she didn’t know if she should let An go on the mission.

After all, every operation involving a Tailed Beast was full of risks.

« Don’t worry ! Teacher’s wife, with my strength, it’s just a mere Tailed Beast. »

Kushina looked at the eight-year-old boy in front of her and couldn’t help but cover her mouth and chuckle.

As for the Nine-Tailed inside Kushina, it rolled its eyes in disdain, clearly unimpressed by An’s arrogant words.

« Big Brother An, what is a Tailed Beast ? »

« Naruto, a Tailed Beast is a very powerful monster. You’ll know when you go to school. »

After coaxing Naruto, Kushina looked at An.

« I can teach you the Eight Trigrams Sealing, but I have conditions. »

Hearing that Kushina was willing to teach him the Eight Trigrams Sealing, An nodded immediately.
