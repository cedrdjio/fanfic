# Chapitre 76 — The Fourth Ninja World War!

« But what about the casualties of ordinary Ninja after a great war ? They also have their own families waiting for them to return home, and they should also enjoy the hard-won peace that is about to come. »

Uchiha An’s words were like a sharp blade, directly piercing through the defenses of everyone present ; after all, this was an objective fact.

All Ninja fought for the sake of future peace, but everyone was flesh and blood, and no one wanted to sacrifice themselves in vain.

Suddenly, the head of the Inuzuka Clan, Inuzuka Tsume, stood up and said in an intense tone, « Then tell me, what should so many of us do ? Should we just surrender ? Do you think we are afraid of death ? I... »

Seeing this, Uchiha An shook his head again and smiled, « This time, let me end this war by myself ! »

Uchiha An’s voice was very calm and firm, without a trace of hesitation or fear.

A look of contemplation flashed through Nara Shikaku’s eyes, and his face immediately turned into an expression of shock.

« Uchiha An, you aren’t planning to face the Shinobi Allied Forces by yourself, are you ! You must know that Iwagakure, Kirigakure, the Akatsuki Organization, and various small Ninja Villages have united, possessing a Shinobi Allied Forces of nearly 50,000 people. This is not something that the power of one person can contend with. You are being too reckless ! »

Uchiha An did not expect Nara Shikaku to guess his intention immediately, so he nodded in approval.

« I know this is very dangerous, but I have no other choice. I don’t want more people to sacrifice themselves, nor do I want the Ninja World to suffer from the flames of war in the future. I believe my power can change all of this. »

Seeing this, the others stood up in shock, all staring at the calm-faced Uchiha An.

Everyone knew that Uchiha An was extremely powerful, but they also felt that he was being too desperate.

« Impossible. I will not allow you to take such a risk, especially since you are not only the leader of Konoha’s Root, the young clan head of the Uchiha Clan, but also my son. As your father, I will not allow you to take risks alone. » Uchiha Fugaku said in a heavy and sorrowful tone, endless worry and love flashing in his eyes.

Minato Namikaze also spoke up to persuade him : « Since this is the final war, some casualties are inevitable. Don’t force yourself, Uchiha An. You should trust your companions and friends. We will all fight alongside you and win this war together. »

The others also echoed him repeatedly, all hoping to persuade Uchiha An to give up this crazy plan.

However, Mei Terumī remained silent, looking at Uchiha An with a pitiful expression.

As Uchiha An’s lover, Mei Terumī did not want to lose him, but she also did not want to stop him from realizing his ideals.

Therefore, Mei Terumī could only pray silently, hoping that Uchiha An would return safely.

Looking at everyone who cared about him, a warm current naturally flowed through Uchiha An’s heart. He knew they were doing it for his own good and did not want him to face everything alone.

But Uchiha An was also clear about his responsibility and mission : to try his best not to let more innocent people be harmed by the war.

Therefore, Uchiha An could only relax his demands and come up with a compromise plan.

« Then let the Jinchuriki and the Jonin and Kage in the Village join me in the battle. After all, facing the Rinnegan and Tailed Beast, such a battlefield is still too cruel for ordinary Ninja, » Uchiha An said, his tone revealing a hint of helplessness and determination.

Seeing Uchiha An relent, everyone immediately agreed. After all, this was a world where strength was respected, and without strength, one didn’t even have the qualifications to go to the battlefield.

Even if Uchiha An encountered danger then, everyone would have the opportunity to step in and save him.

...

Three days later.

On a vast snowy field, the two sides, totaling 50,000 troops, stood opposite each other, all full of imposing momentum and murderous intent.

In the end, only nearly a thousand people followed Uchiha An to the battlefield, while the Shinobi Allied Forces had more than 50,000 people.

Such a disparity made people feel horrified, but Uchiha An and the people behind him showed no trace of fear on their faces ; instead, they held their heads high and their gazes were firm.

At the head of the Shinobi Allied Forces were Tsuchikage Ōnoki, Raikage Ai, and Deva Path Pain, while the others followed behind the three of them.

Several of them were strong figures in the Ninja World and leaders of the Shinobi Allied Forces, and at the same time, they all harbored deep hatred and hostility toward Uchiha An.

« Uchiha An, you despicable and shameless guy ! It was a waste of me to trust you so much, yet you betrayed my trust. »

Deva Path Pain started ranting as soon as he came up, directly lashing out at Uchiha An.

Nagato’s voice was cold and angry, and his eyes were filled with killing intent.

However, Uchiha An was not intimidated, coming alone before the 50,000-strong Shinobi Allied Forces.

Seeing this scene, everyone only felt that Uchiha An’s figure was lonely and proud, and his aura was powerful and steady.

Uchiha An was not swayed by Pain’s words ; instead, he looked at Pain and sneered, « Trust me ? Ridiculous. I’m afraid there aren’t many people in the entire Akatsuki Organization that you can absolutely trust ! »

Tsuchikage Ōnoki and Raikage Ai stepped forward upon seeing this, both looking at Uchiha An with unfriendly expressions.

Both fought to protect their own Villages and to maintain peace in the Ninja World. They both believed that Uchiha An was the culprit of the war and a public enemy that the Ninja World must eliminate.

« Hmph ! You war criminal, what qualifications do you have to talk here after instigating the Fourth Ninja World War ? » Raikage Ai said in a sharp and disdainful tone, a flash of disgust and contempt in his eyes.

Ōnoki echoed, « Guys who don’t know what war is always easily start wars, and in the end, it still requires us old guys to clean up the mess. »

Ōnoki and Ai both acted full of themselves, and their words did not put Uchiha An in their eyes.

However, Uchiha An didn’t care about this at all ; instead, he looked at Zetsu, who was in the second echelon.

Then, a hint of playfulness appeared in Uchiha An’s eyes, and his mouth curled into a strange smile.

« Get ready, your plan might be about to be realized. »

Uchiha An’s tone was full of sarcasm and provocation. It was unknown what Zetsu would choose to do in the face of this war.

Zetsu, seeing Uchiha An’s strange smile, immediately felt a panic in his heart. However, now that the arrow was on the bowstring, it had to be fired ; Zetsu could not afford to let his imagination run wild.

Therefore, Zetsu could only pretend to be calm and said in an indifferent and arrogant tone, « Just wait and see, you will regret starting this war. »

Uchiha An’s expression remained unchanged, and he walked toward the Shinobi Allied Forces without a word.

Uchiha An’s footsteps were firm and steady, crunching on the snow, and his gaze was cold and ruthless.

Everyone watched this strange scene, not knowing what Uchiha An, who was completely defenseless, was going to do by walking over. However, everyone felt the powerful aura emanating from Uchiha An.

« Let me show you all what the ruler’s Haoshoku Haki is ! »

As Uchiha An’s voice fell, an overwhelmingly strong aura covered the entire field, and the Haoshoku Haki, like that of an Asura, was unleashed with full force, instantly covering the battlefield where everyone was located.
