# Chapitre 59 — Fierce Battle with Pain!

Meanwhile, on a tree in the distance, An stood on a branch, unharmed, watching the scene.

« The power of the Six Paths Sage ! »

Three-Tails Isobu was also extremely surprised at this moment, not expecting to encounter the power belonging to the Six Paths Sage here.

Especially when Deva Path Pain used Tengai Shinsei, Isobu truly believed this was the eyes of the Six Paths Sage.

An chuckled when he heard this : « It can’t compare to the Six Paths Sage, but it’s already far stronger than ordinary Bloodline Limits. »

As he spoke, a smug expression appeared on An’s face.

« Of course, it still can’t compare to me ! »

Watching the arrogant An, Three-Tails Isobu let out a disdainful snort.

« Hmph ! »

However, Three-Tails Isobu also knew that his Jinchuriki was truly ridiculously powerful, an existence completely beyond his scope of understanding.

Of course, Three-Tails Isobu also wanted to see the power of this Rinnegan user, so he began trying to provoke An.

« Are you not planning to make a move ? He doesn’t seem weaker than you. »

An gave Three-Tails Isobu several punches inside the Tailed Beast space.

« I have already made a move ! »

As An’s voice gradually faded, Three-Tails Isobu stared intently at the battlefield.

In the sky in the distance, a thick bolt of lightning gathered together, striking straight toward the ruins on the ground like an arrow.

This scene alerted Nagato, who immediately summoned the Preta Path to fly into the air, attempting to block the lightning attack in order to absorb the Ninjutsu released by An.

Konan quickly dodged away from the range of the lightning, clearly skeptical about whether the Preta Path could block An’s attack.

At this moment, the tense atmosphere on the battlefield reached its peak.

« Boom ! »

The lightning flashed like a surging tide, instantly swallowing the figures of Deva Path Pain and the Preta Path, both of whom seemed to take the hit without even having time to react in the air.

When the lightning gradually dissipated, the ruins before them had turned into a bottomless crater, and the figures of Deva Path Pain and the Preta Path had disappeared without a trace.

However, seeing the four Pains not far away still standing in place, Konan breathed a sigh of relief.

Just then, An flashed directly to Konan’s side.

« See that ? This is the power of a god. I named this move Divine Punishment—a small punishment from a god. »

Konan was directly shocked by An’s words ; she hadn’t expected this guy to be so arrogant.

« Shinra Tensei ! »

Deva Path Pain’s voice suddenly came from the deep crater, and the original crater was seen expanding by several meters.

Deva Path Pain’s figure slowly floated out from the deep crater.

« Konan ! »

Konan naturally knew what Nagato meant and left the battlefield in a few flashes.

Seeing Konan retreat, An didn’t make any moves, but instead, the corners of his mouth lifted slightly.

« Do you also know that the following battle is not something she can participate in ? Even a god has people they want to protect but cannot, don’t they ? »

An’s words made Nagato think directly of Yahiko, especially the moment Yahiko died, which made Nagato feel true Pain.

« You have thoroughly angered me ! »

Hearing Nagato no longer refer to himself as a god, An knew that Nagato was truly thoroughly angered by him.

Perhaps it was Yahiko’s death that gave Nagato his current ideology : make the world feel Pain !

« Feel failure ! »

An was rarely being chuunibyou for once, roaring as he jumped toward Nagato in the air.

« Bansho Ten’in… Shinra Tensei ! »

In an instant, a huge repulsive force came, pushing An flying backward.

Actually, Nagato had originally wanted to pull An over to punch him a few times, but he hesitated the moment he saw An’s massive form.

« Boom ! »

An’s figure was directly shrouded in the dust raised by the impact.

After the dust settled, An was seen to have undergone Tailed Beast transformation, with three tails supporting his massive form.

« Not bad power ! »

Gravity is an attractive force, while repulsion is a repulsive force ; both have important influences, whether macroscopically or microscopically.

Someone who can master one of them is already a powerhouse, and a guy who masters both gravity and repulsion is indeed a god-like existence, controlling these two laws.

However, An was not afraid of Deva Path Pain’s ability ; after all, his own electricity-generated magnetism and electricity-generated heat, plus the lightning he controlled, were also very powerful.

« Magnet Release : Super Scientific Electromagnetic Cannon ! »

As he spoke, An directly took out a special Kunai and threw it, and the Kunai, carrying flashing lightning, blasted toward Deva Path Pain.

Just then, a figure appeared, fiercely blocking in front of Deva Path Pain.

« Resurrected the Preta Path so quickly ? »

After An’s voice fell, the Kunai carrying lightning directly pierced into the chest of the Preta Path, causing a violent explosion, and the newly resurrected Preta Path was blown to pieces again.

At this moment, Nagato, hiding in the dark, also looked at this scene in confusion.

« Why can’t the Preta Path absorb your Ninjutsu ? I could understand the first absorption failure as your attack power being too great, exceeding the Preta Path’s tolerance range. »

« But why did the Preta Path fail to absorb the second attack as well ? I cannot perceive the existence of Chakra at all. »

An naturally wouldn’t tell Nagato that this was his Devil Fruit ability.

So, An pretended to be mysterious and said : « Because I am the true god ! »

Nagato knew that An didn’t want to say more, and manipulated Deva Path Pain, Shurado, and the Animal Path—the two Pains capable of fighting—to attack together.

« Summoning Jutsu ! »

In an instant, three huge figures appeared on the field and rushed directly at An.

« Monster Arm Rocket ! »

Shurado and Ningendo attacked simultaneously, pinching An from the left and right.

An activated his More-More Fruit ability and instantly appeared behind Ningendo, the little girl.

« Lightning Release : Lightning Metallurgy ! »

In an instant, extreme high temperature spread from behind Ningendo, and in just a breath’s time, Ningendo turned into fly ash.

Then An performed the Body Flicker Technique again, appearing behind Shurado, and with the same strike, turned Shurado into dust.

« Damn it ! »

At this moment, Nagato was also helpless in the face of the strong An. Since awakening the Six Paths of Pain, this was the first time Nagato had faced such a failure.

The more powerful of the Six Paths of Pain had all died, and as for Jigokudo, it could resurrect the other six, but it had no impact on the battle situation.

And he was manipulating Yahiko’s corpse ; Nagato didn’t want to ruin his partner’s corpse for a battle.

« You don’t want to fight anymore, do you ? »

An’s words directly broke Nagato’s train of thought.

Deva Path Pain nodded stiffly, clearly not wanting to answer An’s question to avoid losing his cool.

Then An put on a look of deep grievance.

« Actually, destroying so many of your puppets—these are all your faults. You should know that you were the ones who started it first ; I was just eating ramen. »
