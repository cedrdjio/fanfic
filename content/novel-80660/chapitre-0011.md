# Chapitre 11 — Op-Op Fruit!

Uchiha An bowed in return, and only then did the crowd straighten up.

« You killed five Chunin at the age of four, and with the help of Sarutobi Kazekaze, you killed the Jonin Toroi from the Hidden Cloud Village. Now, you are our Young Clan Leader ! »

Gaining the support of the Uchiha Clan had always been part of Uchiha An’s plan, but he had not expected this day to come so quickly.

Of course, more importantly, both the Clan Leader and Uchiha An had performed exceptionally well in this war, which was why they had gained the recognition of the majority.

Even though quite a few Uchiha clansmen were unconvinced, no one would show it at this moment.

« Three grandfathers, my younger brother and I will take our leave now. »

« Go ahead ! »

The Great Elder finished speaking and waved his hand to signal that Uchiha An and Uchiha Itachi could leave. Once he saw their figures completely disappear, the Great Elder immediately pulled the Second Elder and Third Elder to his side.

« Luyan, is what you wrote in the letter true ? Have we been a bit too hasty ? »

Regarding the Second Elder’s description in the letter that Uchiha An’s intelligence was almost monstrous, both the Great Elder and the Third Elder were a bit skeptical.

Seeing this, the Second Elder Luyan simply pushed them both away.

« I only believe what I hear and what I see. Even if the Young Clan Leader does not have such wise insights, he is still qualified to be our Young Clan Leader based on his strength alone. »

After saying this, the Second Elder Luyan left immediately.

The Great Elder Hehuo and the Third Elder Hongguang huddled together again.

« Hongguang, what is your take on this matter ? »

« Uchiha An is still young. We should just quietly watch how things develop. If any problems arise, since we were the ones who pushed Uchiha An to the position of Young Clan Leader, we can naturally depose him as well. »

The Uchiha Clan did not practice a strict hereditary system ; they could deliberate on deposing the Clan Leader, and they could also deliberate on deposing the Young Clan Leader.

After setting the tone, the Great Elder and the Third Elder went their separate ways to attend to their own affairs. It could be said that everyone had their own hidden agenda.

Meanwhile.

Uchiha An and Uchiha Itachi had already returned home, and Uchiha Mikoto was already waiting at the door.

When Uchiha Mikoto saw her two sons in the distance, she felt that all her long-standing longing had been worth it.

« Uchiha An ! Uchiha Itachi ! »

Uchiha Mikoto walked over quickly, her eyes filled with tears.

Uchiha An and Uchiha Itachi immediately trotted forward, and the two brothers dove into Uchiha Mikoto’s embrace.

Feeling his mother’s warm embrace, Uchiha An also shed tears unconsciously.

« Mom, my younger brother and I are back. »

Uchiha Mikoto kissed Uchiha An and Uchiha Itachi on the forehead and said with a choked voice : « It’s good that you’re back, good boys ! Good boys ! »

The mother and her two sons hugged each other tightly, and time seemed to have stopped at this moment.

« Alright, don’t stay outside anymore. Mom has prepared delicious food for you. »

Then Uchiha Mikoto picked up Uchiha An and Uchiha Itachi and placed them on the dining table inside the house.

« Wait for Dad for a while. »

After Uchiha Mikoto finished speaking, she returned to the kitchen alone to busy herself, looking very happy.

Uchiha An silently moved to Uchiha Itachi’s side.

« Foolish little brother, tonight, your older brother has a gift for you too. »

« What ? »

Uchiha An reached out and rubbed Uchiha Itachi’s head. « It’s a secret ! »

Evening.

Uchiha Fugaku, whose expression was slightly grim, finally returned home to his warm house.

Uchiha Mikoto, Uchiha An, and Uchiha Itachi had long been waiting for the return of the head of the family, and a sumptuous dinner was already set on the table.

« Father, hurry up and wash your hands so we can eat. »

Uchiha Fugaku smiled and patted Uchiha An’s head, then walked over to Uchiha Mikoto, and the two embraced tightly.

As their eyes met, the couple tacitly conveyed their deep love for each other.

This made Uchiha An and Uchiha Itachi both feel like they were an accident, as if the two brothers were the ones who were superfluous.

« You’re back. Sit down and eat quickly, » Uchiha Mikoto said softly.

Then the family sat around the table, saying nothing about the war, and only talking about interesting things.

The family’s atmosphere gradually became relaxed and pleasant, and Uchiha An also felt the warmth and happiness of the family.

After dinner, Uchiha Fugaku called Uchiha An into the study alone.

« Having experienced the Third Great Ninja War, this time we only won a pyrrhic victory. The intermediate power is almost broken, while a new generation of Ninjas is emerging. If another great war breaks out, I’m afraid the entire Konoha will be destroyed. »

« And there have always been radicals in the Uchiha Clan who oppose our governance philosophy. »

« Now, your sudden emergence has given me a glimmer of a turning point. So, son, do you understand what I mean ? »

After Uchiha Fugaku finished speaking, he looked at Uchiha An with full expectation, as if waiting for Uchiha An’s answer.

It could be said that at this moment, Uchiha Fugaku was treating Uchiha An completely as his military advisor.

Uchiha An, however, was under immense pressure. Was his father overestimating him too much ?

« Another great war is impossible. From the First Great Ninja War to the Third Great Ninja War that just ended, there has been a war about every twenty years, as if to deliberately control the number of Ninjas. In any case, there will be no more great wars in the near future. »

« As for the radicals of the Uchiha Clan, you can handle them, Father. This can also be considered a gift from me to you ! »

As Uchiha An said this, he placed the Paramecia-type Wash-Wash Fruit on the table and handed the prepared note to Uchiha Fugaku, which contained all his hypotheses regarding the development of the Wash-Wash Fruit.

Of course, the drawbacks of the Devil Fruit were also written on the paper by Uchiha An.

Uchiha Fugaku picked it up and read it word by word.

After reading everything, he stood up, picked up Uchiha An, and kissed him.

« What a truly miraculous fruit. Even if it cannot completely influence the radicals within the clan, it can still change a lot. »

Unable to bear Uchiha Fugaku’s eager gaze, Uchiha An pushed his father away and immediately took two steps back.

« I understand Father’s intention ; you want me to become the leader of Konoha’s new generation of Ninjas and further expand the Uchiha Clan’s influence. »

« I understand all these principles, and I have the same intention, so there is no need for Father’s guidance. »

After Uchiha An finished speaking, he made a funny face and trotted back to his room.

Uchiha Fugaku looked at the running Uchiha An and shook his head with a wry smile. Being able to have two such children was his good fortune.

Of course, he could also try harder and strive to give birth to another child as smart as Uchiha An and Uchiha Itachi.

Returning to his room, Uchiha An flopped directly onto the bed, and Uchiha Itachi’s hairstyle was ravaged once again.

« Foolish little brother, this is your older brother’s gift ! »

Uchiha An placed the Op-Op Fruit on the bed, which looked particularly bizarre under the light.

Uchiha Itachi curiously examined this strange fruit.

« Older brother, what is this ? Is it a rare, special fruit ? »

« It can be considered a special fruit ; you’ll know once you eat it. »

Uchiha Itachi picked up the fruit and took a bite, his expression twisting, looking as if he were enduring extreme pain.

« One bite is enough ! »

« Cough, cough... »

Uchiha Itachi, who managed to swallow the Devil Fruit, gasped for air. Clearly, the shit-like taste of the Devil Fruit just now would surely make Uchiha Itachi remember it for the rest of his life.
