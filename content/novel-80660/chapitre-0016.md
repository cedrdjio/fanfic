# Chapitre 16 — Excited Hana!

That was the Haoshoku Haki, which only appears in one out of a million people in the world of One Piece.

As a standard feature for the strong, An had determined it only after many furtive attempts, and it came in handy right at this moment, instantly scaring the ninja dogs of the Inuzuka Clan until they trembled.

« An, are you here to play with Hana ? »

Facing so many rabid dogs, An’s face remained calm and indifferent, watching them without the slightest nervousness.

An exuded an indescribable confidence and imposing aura, sending chills down people’s spines.

Inuzuka Tsume watched this scene, and couldn’t help but pay more attention to An, whose status in her heart kept rising.

Inuzuka Tsume could see that An not only possessed powerful strength but also had an unattainable mindset and wisdom.

Combined with the fact that An was the young clan leader of the Uchiha Clan, Inuzuka Tsume approved of An even more in her heart.

Therefore, Inuzuka Tsume said to An in a slightly gentler voice : « An, Hana is waiting for you. »

Several experts of the Inuzuka Clan did not know this ; they were just wondering when their clan leader had developed such a good temper, being so gentle to a child.

An smiled slightly without revealing too much emotion.

Maintaining a calm attitude, An replied : « I came to find you, Auntie. »

Inuzuka Tsume stepped forward, her expression unchanged, and picked up An before walking inside.

Clearly, Inuzuka Tsume did not think An had any serious business looking for her ; it was nothing more than trying to woo her daughter.

« I came to give you a gift, and you will definitely be very satisfied with this gift... »

Just as An was speaking, Inuzuka Tsume carried An directly into the room and tossed him to Inuzuka Hana, who was reading a book.

« Keep an eye on this kid. »

After saying this, Inuzuka Tsume left without looking back, seemingly busy with something.

« An, did you come to see your big sister ? »

Inuzuka Hana immediately reached out, picked An up, and started kneading him, quickly turning An’s small face bright red.

Only after Inuzuka Hana had had her fill did she reluctantly remove her devilish hands from An’s face.

« Hmph ! You haven’t come to play with me for so long, you really are a heartless guy. »

Suddenly, sweat dripped down An’s forehead.

He knew that children in the Naruto world were precocious, but he didn’t expect them to be this precocious !

Inuzuka Hana was only six years old ; how could she understand these things ?

However, he had to coax his own child bride himself ; after all, Hana-jie was quite lovely when she grew up.

« Haven’t I been training with the Fourth Hokage Minato Namikaze ? I came to see you immediately after finishing ; this is my true devotion ! »

Looking at An’s exaggerated movements, Inuzuka Hana wrinkled her nose unconsciously, clearly not believing what An was saying.

« Since that’s what you think, then the gift I originally prepared for you is gone. »

« Gift ? What gift ? »

Little girls' attention comes and goes quickly ; she was immediately attracted by the gift An mentioned.

Inuzuka Hana’s eyes were like two bright gems, shining with curiosity and anticipation. She completely forgot the previous topic, her full attention focused on the mysterious gift An mentioned.

« Ta-da ! »

A strange-looking fruit instantly appeared in An’s empty hands. Its unique shape and texture aroused great curiosity in Inuzuka Hana.

Therefore, Inuzuka Hana stepped forward and picked up the Devil Fruit from An’s hand to examine it carefully.

« This fruit looks okay ; I’m barely satisfied with it as a gift. »

Inuzuka Hana clearly did not know the significance of the Devil Fruit and just treated it as an ordinary fruit.

An did not explain too much ; anyway, once Inuzuka Hana ate the Whisper-Whisper Fruit, she would know its effects, and she would definitely thank him like crazy then.

« This is a legendary treasure, a rare myth-level fruit. »

As An said this, he grabbed the Devil Fruit and stuffed it into Inuzuka Hana’s mouth.

In an instant, Inuzuka Hana’s expression became incredibly distorted ; clearly, the taste of the Devil Fruit was not good, feeling like eating dung.

Inuzuka Hana, having barely swallowed a bite, gritted her teeth and glared fiercely at An with her big eyes.

« What kind of strange fruit is this ? It tastes terrible ! »

As she spoke, Inuzuka Hana lunged at An.

In an instant, An dodged and caught Inuzuka Hana in his arms.

« Don’t worry ! This fruit has a very special effect ; I guarantee you won’t regret it. »

Then An explained it to Inuzuka Hana. After learning the truth about the Whisper-Whisper Fruit, Inuzuka Hana consciously gave him a kiss.

It was a pity that Inuzuka Hana was only a six-year-old girl now, preventing An from extending his beastly claws for the time being.

« Thank you, An ! »

Feeling Inuzuka Hana, who was like a little beast in his arms, An’s heart was extremely calm at this moment.

« Waaah ! Waaah ! »

Suddenly, a baby’s crying broke the rippling atmosphere between the two.

Inuzuka Hana immediately broke free from An’s embrace and walked into the room next door.

Before long, Inuzuka Hana walked out holding a baby.

« This is my younger brother, Kiba. He doesn’t seem much older than your younger brother, Sasuke. »

« Yeah ! I hope he can become good friends with Sasuke. »

An knew how unreliable his words were. As one of the Konoha 12, the handsome guy who used Fang Over Fang from the plot until the end of the Fourth Great Ninja War, An naturally knew Kiba very well.

It could be said that Kiba’s personality still had some conflicts with Sasuke’s personality.

However, what was rare was that An looked at the current Kiba and found him somewhat cute, hoping that his future personality would be a bit softer compared to the original plot.

Of course, An knew this wasn’t very reliable either ; after all, with his existence, the plot had already become confusing and unpredictable.

« An, it won’t be long before you enroll, right ! »

An nodded listlessly, « The studies at the Ninja Academy are meaningless to me now ; I might apply for early graduation in a year. »

An was still confident in his own strength ; I’m afraid it won’t be long before he can go out and roam freely.

Even if it weren’t for his young age, An might be able to go out and roam freely right now.

« Okay, I’ll graduate early with you ; after all, practicing the Three Body Technique is something I’ve already mastered. »

An neither nodded in agreement nor directly refused Inuzuka Hana’s words ; after all, who can say for sure what will happen in the future ?

After chatting briefly with Inuzuka Hana and teasing the still-young Kiba, An left the territory of the Inuzuka Clan.

Although An had mastered a little bit of the use of Haoshoku Haki, he was still quite confused about the training of Observation Haki and Armament Haki.

Actually, An planned to spend time researching the Six Powers from the world of One Piece. If he succeeded, wouldn’t that be a huge win ? If he could pass down the Six Powers, An believed his status would definitely not be much lower than the Six Paths Sage.

A taijutsu expert who had eaten the Rumble-Rumble Fruit slaughtering indiscriminately in the Naruto world—wouldn’t that be a very interesting scene ?
