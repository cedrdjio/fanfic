# Chapitre 62 — Persuasion!

Konoha Village.

It had been many years since Uchiha An last entered Konoha through the main gate.

« It’s been a long time, Kakashi ! »

« It’s been a long time, Uchiha An. I didn’t expect the leader of Root to walk through the main gate every day ! »

Uchiha An had just returned when he happened to run into Kakashi ; perhaps Kakashi had been waiting for him.

« Hokage-sama wants you to preside over this year’s Chunin Exams. »

« I am the leader of Root, hidden in the shadows. How can I preside over the public Chunin Exams ? It would be better to leave that to Ibiki. »

Uchiha An did not want to change the original plot ; he had important things to do.

Hearing this, Kakashi couldn’t help but roll his eyes. As a Root leader who was always on the front lines, it was safe to say that the entire Ninja World now knew that the leader of Root was the Konoha Thunder God.

After rejecting Kakashi, Uchiha An offered a reminder.

« Kakashi, read less adult literature and take care of your health, lest it affect your Chakra levels ! »

« Hmph ! »

Kakashi let out an impatient huff, continued staring at the Icha Icha Paradise in his hands, and then vanished with a few flashes.

Seeing this, Uchiha An shook his head with a bitter smile ; it was clear he couldn’t be persuaded !

« Is that the Copy Ninja Kakashi ? The man who has copied thousands of Ninjutsu ? »

Beside him, Tayuya looked shocked. She hadn’t expected to see such a famous figure in the Ninja World immediately upon arriving at Konoha.

« Just a closet pervert ! »

As he spoke, Uchiha An continued walking toward the Uchiha Clan compound. Now, Uchiha An needed to utilize the power of the Uchiha Clan.

As for Tayuya, she let out a cold huff, clearly disagreeing with Uchiha An’s assessment.

Just as Uchiha An was passing by the Hyuga Clan compound, two figures—one large and one small—suddenly rushed out and threw themselves directly into Uchiha An’s arms.

« Bastard, you didn’t even come to see me for so long. »

« An-gege, Hinata missed you ! »

Feeling the two lovely ladies in his arms, Uchiha An only wanted to say that his happy life was not far off.

Uchiha An pinched Inuzuka Hana’s cheek and smiled. « It’s been a long time, Hana-jie, you are still as sexy as ever. »

Then Uchiha An patted little Hinata’s head. « It’s been a long time, Hinata, you’ve become even cuter. »

As Uchiha An’s voice fell, Hinata transformed into a 'steam girl' and collapsed directly into Uchiha An’s arms.

Seeing this, Inuzuka Hana poked Uchiha An’s forehead hard.

« You know Hinata is shy, yet you still tease her on purpose. »

« How could that be ? I’m telling the truth. »

« Mm-hmm ! The truth. »

After saying this, Inuzuka Hana also leaned into Uchiha An’s arms, completely unconcerned about the gazes of those around them. The three of them enjoyed this moment of peace.

Only Tayuya felt a bit awkward, not knowing if she should be standing there.

After a long while.

Having calmed down, Hinata left Uchiha An’s embrace, while Inuzuka Hana chuckled and pushed Uchiha An away.

« An-gege, are you leaving again soon ? I’m participating in the Chunin Exams this time. Can you come and cheer for me ? »

« Of course, no problem. But Hinata, you must ensure your own safety when fighting. »

Although Hinata now possessed a Devil Fruit, over the past few years, all the Devil Fruits Uchiha An had obtained had been given away.

It could be said that the current Konoha 12 were all much stronger than they originally were.

Even the most outstanding ones, Sasuke and Naruto, could solo Ichibi if they ran into Gaara.

« Alright, I’ll spend time with you all once I’m done with this busy period. »

The 'gentle land' is a hero’s grave ; with Inuzuka Hana, Hinata, Mei Terumī, and the sharp-tongued Tayuya by his side, Uchiha An was already very satisfied.

Before long, Uchiha An led Tayuya back to the Uchiha Clan compound. Along the way, Uchiha clansmen kept greeting Uchiha An.

The Uchiha clansmen now respected and feared Uchiha An, their young clan leader, and had absolutely no other ulterior motives.

« Nii-san ! »

« Foolish little brother ! »

In the courtyard, Uchiha Itachi and Sasuke were practicing their Shurikenjutsu.

Uchiha An felt a wave of annoyance inside ; with their strength, did they really need to practice such things ? It was clearly just the brothers playing around.

« Big brother ! » × 2

At this moment, Uchiha An was finally noticed by the brothers. They both stopped their actions and looked curiously at Tayuya, who was standing to the side.

« My little follower, you two don’t need to worry about her. » As he spoke, Uchiha An instantly flashed in front of Sasuke and ruffled the aloof hairstyle of the 'Second Pillar.'

« If you can’t pass the Chunin Exams, then get ready for me to spar with you. »

Feeling Uchiha An’s deep voice, cold sweat almost broke out on Sasuke’s forehead.

What kind of sparring was that ? That was clearly him getting beaten up.

« Don’t worry ! Big brother ! »

After resolving the issue with Sasuke, Uchiha An looked at Uchiha Itachi.

« I have something to discuss with you and Father tonight. Call Uchiha Shisui over as well. »

« Mm ! »

Uchiha Itachi nodded to indicate he understood, then immediately ran into the house.

« Mother, Big brother brought another woman back ! »

The expression on Uchiha An’s face instantly collapsed. He hadn’t expected his aloof little brother to turn out like this.

As for Tayuya, she blushed, likely embarrassed by Uchiha Itachi’s comment.

« You brat, the issues with the Inuzuka Clan and the Hyuga Clan are already hard enough to deal with, and now you’re causing me this kind of trouble... » (10,000 words omitted here)

Finally, Uchiha An escaped his mother’s nagging and managed to have a reunion dinner with his family.

At night, Uchiha Fugaku, Uchiha An, and Uchiha Itachi—the father and two sons—sat together, along with the only outsider, Uchiha Shisui.

« Am I a bit redundant ? »

« A little, but I called you here because there is something important to discuss. »

Now, Uchiha An had to utilize every force he could ; he wouldn’t let anyone off the hook.

« The Ichibi Jinchuriki and the Fourth Kazekage of Sunagakure are participating in the Chunin Exams. Right now, Sunagakure and even the Land of Wind are very empty. This is our chance to act and annex the Land of Wind. »

Instantly, three figures stood up, all looking in shock at the calm Uchiha An.

« Uchiha An, you know this will trigger a war again ! »

Uchiha Fugaku’s tone was heavy. Having experienced multiple Ninja World Wars, Uchiha Fugaku clearly did not want to start another war.

« Right, I also don’t think we should attack Sunagakure like this. »

Uchiha Shisui’s expression was not very good ; he hadn’t expected Uchiha An to be so bold.

Only Uchiha Itachi looked indifferent as he spoke to everyone present.

« When I was four, Big brother explained to me what war is and why it exists—it’s all because of the differing interests between various countries. »

« I now agree with Big brother’s point of view. We must actively initiate the war and unify the entire Ninja World. This is the only true way to end war ! »

Upon hearing Uchiha Itachi’s words, Uchiha Fugaku and Uchiha Shisui remained silent, looking as if they had been persuaded.

Uchiha An continued to persuade them until both men reluctantly accepted his idea.
