# Chapitre 48 — Picking a Fight!

Early the next morning, An slowly woke up from his comfortable three-meter-wide bed.

After stretching, An felt his body gradually regaining its vitality. Upon getting up, he quickly dressed, combed his hair, and prepared to go to the kitchen to make breakfast.

Although time was tight, An still felt a sense of peace and joy, enjoying the process of cooking, cleverly combining fresh ingredients to create a delicious breakfast.

After finishing breakfast, An hurriedly cleaned up the tableware, tidied the kitchen, and set off for work.

Ten minutes later, An rushed all the way to the Mizukage’s office, but the scene there took him by surprise.

In the spacious and bright office, several members of the Ninja clans, led by Egao Jianfang, sat quietly without saying a word.

Inside, An saw a familiar figure : Hozuki Lanyue, who had ambushed Konoha at the end of the Third Ninja World War.

Mei Terumī's face revealed a nervous expression, while Ao seemed completely indifferent, letting out a yawn.

When An pushed the door open and walked in, everyone’s gaze immediately turned toward him, letting An know that they had come with ill intentions.

« Morning ! Have you all eaten ? Would you like another massage, Mizukage ? »

Mei Terumī was immediately anxious and angry ; why did this guy have to bring up the massage in front of so many people ?

Taking a deep breath, Mei Terumī tried hard to control her emotions and understood Newgate’s behavior. After all, Newgate didn’t know that today’s grand display was related to him.

Upon seeing An arrive, Egao Jianfang immediately smiled and said : « The main character has finally arrived. I heard that you are the man known as the strongest in the world—Edward Newgate ! That name is truly strange. »

Egao Jianfang paused for a moment, a flash of disdain appearing in his eyes : « However, our Hidden Mist Village is not a place where just anyone can join. As for that battle between you and Ao, it really doesn’t amount to much ; after all, that guy’s strength is only average. »

« From now on, you will only be qualified to become a member of the Hidden Mist Village if you compete against several of our most powerful Ninjas. »

Egao Jianfang’s tone was full of confidence and arrogance, clearly skeptical of An’s strength.

« Sure ! Let’s have a fight then ! It’s best if it’s a life-or-death battle ; I won’t be responsible if you get killed. »

An didn’t want to keep bickering with these guys. Letting them witness his strength so they wouldn’t dare to act like a mantis trying to stop a chariot was the only way to solve the problem once and for all.

Hearing An’s arrogant words, the group became furious and, disregarding An’s three-meter height, immediately began to berate him.

« Kid, aren’t you afraid of biting your tongue ? Don’t end up kneeling and begging for mercy when you get beaten. »

« But if you do kneel and beg for mercy, we might actually spare your life. »

Listening to them chattering on, An impatiently curled his lip and suddenly unleashed his aura of absolute dominance.

In an instant, the expressions of the group, who had been ranting wildly, froze, and even Mei Terumī and Ao were stunned for a moment.

« I want to fight ten of you ! »

At this moment, An’s tone was exceptionally arrogant, yet this time, no one dared to argue with him, as those guys had already been intimidated by An’s overwhelming presence.

« Hmph ! If you have the guts, follow us. »

Egao Jianfang said, leaning on his cane as he walked out the door, and the other guys immediately followed in his footsteps.

Seeing them leave, Mei Terumī and Ao immediately walked up to An.

« Newgate, your actions were too impulsive. Let me handle this matter. »

« Don’t worry, Mizukage. Based on Newgate’s strength, he will definitely not be at a disadvantage. »

Ao’s tone was incredibly firm ; clearly, he still remembered the battle between himself and Newgate, during which he was constantly at a disadvantage.

Mei Terumī wanted to speak again, but An glanced at her dominantly.

« Fighting is the best way to resolve this matter. »

An said straightforwardly, leaving Mei Terumī unable to say anything more.

Soon, the group arrived at a desolate and mysterious island, surrounded by thick fog, making the place appear exceptionally remote.

When everyone stepped onto the island, they saw two figures already waiting there for An.

One of them was Hozuki Lanyue, a figure An was very familiar with.

The other person was completely unfamiliar to An, but this guy had a tall and imposing figure, standing there with a relaxed stride, inexplicably exuding the aura of a total show-off.

Looking at the scene before them, everyone couldn’t help but feel that the situation had clearly become unfavorable ; it was a two-on-one scenario. Mei Terumī immediately felt nervous, and Ao also began to lose confidence in An.

Mei Terumī did not hide her inner confusion and anger, « What the hell are you doing ? Why is Egao Hekong here too ! »

Facing this sudden situation, Mei Terumī also felt anger and pressure.

Egao Jianfang sneered, unafraid of Mei Terumī's questioning.

« That insignificant fool Newgate asked to fight ten, I only sent two people ; I have already shown mercy. »

Egao Jianfang, his tone filled with mockery and arrogance, clearly did not take the Mizukage, Mei Terumī, seriously.

This conversation made everyone realize that things were not simple, and the appearance of Egao Hekong triggered concern in both Mei Terumī and Ao.

Just as they were talking, the battle had already begun on the field.

« Water Release : Hidden Mist Jutsu ! »

As Egao Hekong’s voice fell, the field, which was already covered in light mist, instantly turned into thick fog, leaving everything a vast blur.

Seeing this, Hozuki Lanyue retreated outside the thick fog, clearly not believing that An could be a match for Egao Hekong.

« What despicable and shameless guys. »

Mei Terumī was full of resentment, feeling angry at Egao Hekong’s despicable and shameless behavior.

Because the Hidden Mist Village was originally hidden in fog, coupled with the effect of the Hidden Mist Jutsu, Newgate directly lost his vision, which filled Mei Terumī's heart with anger.

Egao Jianfang laughed with a trace of mockery and said mercilessly : « Ninjas can use any means to complete a mission ; how can you call it despicable ? »

« Jianfang-sama is right ; I’m afraid Newgate has already been defeated by Master Hekong ! »

« Not necessarily, maybe he’s kneeling and begging for mercy ! »

The heads of the major Ninja clans outside the field echoed each other, all expressing doubt about Newgate’s strength, and heavy pressure weighed on the hearts of Mei Terumī and Ao.

However, the two had no time to refute any accusations, focusing intently on the thick fog before them.

Meanwhile.

On the battlefield, Egao Hekong held two sharp Kunai, slowly approaching An.

The cautious Egao Hekong moved his feet carefully, avoiding leaving any sound in the thick fog.

An’s three-meter height had now become a disadvantage, appearing very obvious in the vast, thick fog.

Egao Hekong knew that this was the best time to launch an attack.
