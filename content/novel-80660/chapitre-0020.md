# Chapitre 20 — The Bold Inuzuka Hana!

When An returned home, his old father Fugaku was already sitting on the porch waiting for him.

« Are you trying to ask why Shimura Kazama targeted you today ! »

« I don’t want to. »

Fugaku immediately had an expression of being choked up.

An wasn’t going to spoil this old father, otherwise, the two could chat all day.

« Well, I have to tell you anyway, you’ll know sooner or later. »

After saying this, Fugaku directly pulled An to his side.

« From the fact that his surname is Shimura, you should know that the Shimura Clan and our Uchiha Clan have never gotten along. The two clans have had deep conflicts since the Sengoku period. »

« And the last Ninja World War, didn’t you have a big battle with the Cloud Ninja ? When Konoha Chunin Yama Nara died, you appeared, so Shimura Kazama thinks you arrived late on purpose and didn’t want to save anyone. »

« And the reason Shimura Kazama thinks this way is because Yama Nara was a student Shimura Kazama thought highly of, and was also a Ninja that Root wanted to cultivate. »

After Fugaku finished speaking, An twitched his lips speechlessly. This guy was truly unreasonable.

Of course, this is the Ninja World, and such situations are too normal in a Ninja World where masters are occasionally killed.

But regardless, An was not planning to let Shimura Kazama off the hook.

« Where is Itachi ? Why didn’t he come back with you ? »

The big-hearted Fugaku finally noticed that he was missing a son. Again, fatherly love is like a mountain !

« Itachi went on a date with Izumi. I was redundant there, so I came back too. »

« Then you could have gone to find the little girl from the Inuzuka Clan ! »

An didn’t expect his old father to be such an old lecher. He was only six years old and already teaching his son to snatch someone else’s daughter. That was a bit inappropriate, wasn’t it !

« Where is my stupid little brother ? »

« He’s sleeping. Wait for Itachi to come back for dinner ! »

Then the father and son began to sit in silence. After all, most fathers and sons are like this ; they run out of things to say after a few sentences.

« How is the relationship between the clan and the Village now ? »

Hearing An’s question, Fugaku laughed directly, and there was a hint of complacency on his face.

« Many members of the Uchiha Clan can integrate well into various departments. As for some opposition factions, they have been influenced by me using the Wash-Wash Fruit and are unwilling to go to war with the Village. Only a very small number of hardliners still want to launch a coup and seize the Konoha government. »

« Then just kill them ! »

« Hmph ! You don’t need to remind your old man about that. »

After saying this, Fugaku got up and walked back into the house. An stood on the spot and scratched his nose embarrassedly.

Being able to survive the Ninja World War and still be the patriarch of the Uchiha Clan, this old father of his was no pushover. It seems he was overthinking things.

Finally, with a rippling expression on Itachi’s face, everyone entered the lunch session.

Night falls.

An, who had been practicing all afternoon, lay quietly on the bed and started the system lottery.

It was still the familiar process, the familiar taste.

Soon, the pointer stopped on a Devil Fruit that An was quite familiar with.

[Ding ! Congratulations to the host for obtaining the Paramecia Calm-Calm Fruit !]

The Paramecia Calm-Calm Fruit can be said to be a rather pit-digging existence in the One Piece world. Of course, this is due to the user.

However, An, who had gone through nine years of compulsory education, knew how powerful the Calm-Calm Fruit was.

The Calm-Calm Fruit can isolate all external sounds. People inside the soundproof wall cannot hear any external sounds, and the outside cannot hear the sounds inside. You can even make your own movements completely silent.

Of course, those who understand physics regarding sound know that if developed well, the Calm-Calm Fruit is also a very powerful Devil Fruit.

What’s more, the Ninja World is full of assassinations and traps. The performance of the Calm-Calm Fruit in the Ninja World will inevitably be much better than in the One Piece world.

And the performance of the Calm-Calm Fruit reminded An of Ge Xiaolun’s ability to make people shut up. It must be said that the two have similarities.

« Then who should I give this Devil Fruit to ? »

An, lying on the bed, inexplicably began to struggle. After all, he didn’t know many people, and it wasn’t safe to give away such a magical thing casually.

Unable to figure it out, An decided not to think about it anymore and fell into a deep sleep lying on the bed.

...

The next day.

An and Itachi walked into the campus together, and the guards and teachers behind them looked at the backs of the two and exclaimed repeatedly.

It can be said that the high-level officials of Konoha at this time knew that two genius Ninjas had appeared in the Uchiha Clan. It can be said that the performance of An and Itachi amazed everyone.

« Brother, it seems that we will inevitably receive more attention in the future. »

Regarding Itachi’s worries, An didn’t care at all, just flicked his bangs dashingly.

« Don’t worry, if there are beautiful little girls pestering you, you can push them to me as much as you want. »

« Cough, cough... »

Itachi coughed awkwardly twice, then turned his head away and pretended not to know An.

The two brothers walked through the long corridor of the teaching area and came to the designated classroom.

The moment he pushed open the door, An was a little surprised because the room was basically full of children from various Ninja clans, and there were basically no children of commoners.

« I’m afraid the third assessment is prepared for the final class assignment. Many people in the classroom basically didn’t take many hits during the third assessment. »

« Stupid little brother, you are talking a bit too much today. This doesn’t fit your cold personality. »

Itachi didn’t expect that his kind reminder would also be shot. He could only say that he deserved to be his old brother.

And just as the two were talking, the people in the classroom were attracted by the sound of the two pushing the door.

However, when they saw An’s figure, most people dared not look directly at An. The fierce reputation left behind when An was being assessed was too great.

Almost castrated an invigilator, targeted by the chief invigilator, these are not things an ordinary student can do.

« An, you finally came to school ! »

Suddenly, a beautiful figure rushed in and took An into her arms.

« Sister Hana, there are so many people here ! Give me some face, give me some mystery. »

Inuzuka Hana curled her lips in disdain, then lightly pecked An on the face, and then ran away immediately, as if declaring sovereignty.

Other girls in the classroom who were interested in An gave up on An and turned to look at Itachi on the side. After all, the charm of a cold male god is still very great.

Seeing this, Izumi stepped forward and took Itachi’s hand, also as if declaring sovereignty, but not as bold as Inuzuka Hana.

In this way, An and Itachi started their Ninja Academy career, but for the two brothers, one a genius and one a fake genius, such courses were still too boring.

So An began to take Itachi to skip class with Shadow Clones, while their real bodies came to the waterfall behind the Uchiha Clan’s compound to practice. Of course, An was also constantly guiding Itachi on how to develop the Op-Op Fruit.
