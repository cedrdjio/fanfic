# Chapitre 21 — Uchiha Shisui!

One week later, at the waterfall behind the Uchiha Clan compound.

« Shuriken Throwing Technique ! »

With a low shout, Itachi’s figure darted through the woods, Kunai and shuriken flying from his hands as if he were determined to skewer his own brother.

Beside An, three Kunai floated in the air, blocking Itachi’s attacks one by one, the sound of metal clashing ringing continuously through the forest.

Both brothers maintained their composure ; clearly, this kind of battle had happened more than once.

« Room ! »

In an instant, Itachi swapped positions with a Kunai near An, then threw out a handful of explosive tags.

« Boom ! » A massive explosion rang out, leaving the spot charred black.

Itachi didn’t think this would be enough to take down his brother, so he leaned against a tree, guarding himself.

An, hidden in the shadows, attacked without hesitation.

« Lightning Release : Lightning Bolt ! »

A straight bolt of lightning, carrying an astonishing aura, pierced through Itachi’s chest.

Then, Itachi’s body suddenly turned into a puff of white smoke.

« Foolish little brother, quite a good Clone Technique ! »

« It’s not over yet ! »

The usually aloof Itachi was no longer cold ; instead, his battle-hungry lineage was triggered. Even though he had never won once, every time he fought his brother, Itachi felt his blood boiling.

Itachi produced a short blade from who knows where and charged at An again.

« Thousand Cuts ! »

Itachi continued to choose suppressive fire, constantly throwing the remaining Kunai as if he wanted to win in one strike.

Then, Itachi lunged forward, the silver-white short blade in his hand slashing out with immense, violent momentum.

Seeing this, An controlled his Kunai to knock away Itachi’s Kunai, and as for Itachi’s short blade, An simply dodged to the side.

Just when An thought Itachi’s tricks were over, the Kunai that had been knocked away suddenly changed direction. Itachi was continuously using his Fruit ability to shift positions, though the short blade in his hand never stopped slashing.

« Foolish little brother ! A very good idea, using your own ability to make it seem like the Kunai were knocked away by me, and then launching close-quarters slashes when I was slightly careless. You could say an ordinary Ninja would have already died to this move. »

« Unfortunately, for your big brother, it’s still too green. »

« Wahahahaha ! Who told you your big brother is a genius ? Actually, I don’t even want to be this outstanding ! »

As An spoke, he suddenly threw several Kunai into the forest, then unleashed another Lightning Release attack.

Seeing this, Itachi gripped his short blade tightly, watching the spot his brother attacked with caution. Itachi had unconditional trust in his brother ; he feared something might be happening in the forest.

A young man with short hair and a slightly wicked, charming face walked out of the forest.

« Sorry, I don’t mean any harm, I was just curious about your battle. »

« You two must be the Clan Leader’s sons ! What a battle between strong individuals ; you truly live up to the title of geniuses of our Uchiha Clan. »

Looking at the person in front of them, who seemed a bit too familiar despite his cold appearance, An and Itachi both had strange expressions ; they wanted to laugh but felt embarrassed to do so.

An stepped forward and asked, « You’ve said so much, but you haven’t introduced yourself yet. »

« Uchiha Shisui, I suppose I’m your senior ! »

Neither An nor Itachi doubted Shisui’s words ; after all, this was the Uchiha Clan’s compound, and outsiders couldn’t easily enter.

Furthermore, Shisui was already somewhat famous ; once he graduated and started carrying out missions, he would likely earn the title of 'Shisui of the Body Flicker'.

« Senior, would you like to spar ? »

In reality, An was quite curious about Shisui’s current strength and didn’t know if he had already awakened the Sharingan.

Shisui did not refuse An’s request, simply drawing the katana at his waist.

Seeing this, Itachi also became interested, gripping his short blade and stepping forward to stand in front of An.

« Let me handle this. »

Itachi had a confident expression ; it seemed the previous battle hadn’t been enough to satisfy him.

However, in An’s view, this was likely Itachi wanting to bully a newbie and taste the flavor of victory.

Shisui started with the Uchiha Clan’s specialty, Shurikenjutsu, sending a dozen Kunai piercing toward Itachi’s body in rapid succession.

« Clang ! Clang ! Clang ! »

After the battle with An, Itachi no longer had any Kunai or shuriken on him and could only rely on the short blade in his hand to block Shisui’s attacks one by one.

Soon, the ground at Itachi’s feet was covered in a layer of Kunai and shuriken, yet Itachi hadn’t moved more than a few steps.

Seeing this, Shisui didn’t waste words, leaping into close range and slashing fiercely at Itachi with his blade.

« Clang ! »

Itachi’s short blade collided with Shisui’s katana, sending sparks flying in the air.

However, Shisui was extremely surprised. The boy in front of him was three years younger than him, and he had jumped and slashed down.

Yet, he was easily blocked by Itachi. Thinking of this, Shisui finally brought out his true strength.

Both Itachi and Shisui chose to abandon Ninjutsu and began a constant close-quarters struggle.

Their figures constantly collided and separated in the forest, appearing only as blurs ; it could be said that both were pitting their agile speeds against each other.

Unfortunately, under the influence of their attacks, the surrounding trees became riddled with holes.

« Clang ! »

After another clash, Shisui and Itachi landed on the ground simultaneously.

« Such fast speed ! »

Shisui hadn’t expected a boy who had just entered the academy to be able to push him to this extent ; even though he was three years older, he hadn’t gained any advantage.

Itachi silently wiped the sweat from his forehead ; he had thought it would be a simple match to bully a novice, but he hadn’t expected the battle to last this long.

« Hmph ! »

With a cold snort, Itachi’s eyes began to change. His originally black and white eyes turned dark red, with a One-Tomoe Sharingan swirling within.

« Have you already awakened the Sharingan ? »

Shisui knew he had to bring out his real skills for this battle.

At the same moment, Shisui’s eyes also began to change, likewise a One-Tomoe Sharingan.

Seeing this, An was almost going crazy with jealousy. There was no hope for his own Sharingan right now ; could it be because he was too laid back ?

Otherwise, it should have been his turn by now !

Shisui and Itachi naturally didn’t know An’s thoughts, and the two soon collided again.

Unfortunately, Itachi was at a disadvantage in this battle ; after all, in terms of Sharingan development and physical stamina, the six-year-old Itachi was no match for the nine-year-old Shisui.

But how could An watch his younger brother get bullied ?

So, An whistled, and Itachi instantly understood his brother’s meaning. It seemed he was to activate the Op-Op Fruit’s ability to quickly end the battle.

« Click ! »

The short blade, covered by the Op-Op Fruit, sliced Shisui’s katana in half as if it were tofu.

This scene gave Shisui quite a scare ; after all, at only nine years old, this was the first time Shisui had encountered such a situation.
