# Chapitre 7 — Full Harvest!

As the captain, Fugaku scooped up Uchiha An in one arm and Uchiha Itachi in the other, rushing the two brothers straight to headquarters.

...

Several days later.

Inside the medical room.

The pungent smell of medicine made Uchiha An feel very uncomfortable.

Feeling the pain in his body, Uchiha An knew he had been saved.

« He has woken up ; his body will be able to recover in a few days. »

Hearing a stranger’s voice, Uchiha An struggled to open his eyes.

The only word to describe what he saw was big—the biggest in the Ninja World.

« Uchiha An, Lady Tsunade saved you. »

Inuzuka Hana looked at the waking Uchiha An, then suddenly threw herself onto the hospital bed and began to cry.

« Your body seems to have been under immense pressure, so there wasn’t a single intact part of you, but after my treatment, you will be able to recover in three days. »

« I didn’t expect a four-year-old brat like you to be able to kill five Chunin from the Hidden Cloud Village, and even help kill a Jonin alongside the heavily injured Sarutobi Kazekaze. You are truly a terrifying little guy ; I am becoming more and more curious about you. »

After speaking, Tsunade turned around to leave, but looked back at Uchiha An before departing.

« During the days you were unconscious, she spent quite a lot of time by your side. »

As Tsunade left, Inuzuka Hana, alone in the room with him, felt very embarrassed.

She then began to serve tea and water to Uchiha An, fussing over him with concern.

Before long, Uchiha Itachi burst in, holding a bouquet of flowers which he placed by the bedside.

« Brother, how are you feeling ? Father went to carry out a mission. When he returns and finds out that you’ve woken up, he will definitely be very happy. »

« Also, thank you for protecting me back then, otherwise... »

At this moment, Uchiha Itachi was chattering away like a chatterbox, and his behavior was similar to that of a normal child.

Uchiha An looked at the downcast Uchiha Itachi and reached out to rub his hair.

« Isn’t it only natural for an older brother to protect his younger brother ? »

Uchiha Itachi nodded heavily ; he must train hard so that in the future, he could also protect his brother.

« Brother, I think I have awakened the Uchiha Clan’s Bloodline Limit, the Sharingan. »

He saw the One-Tomoe Sharingan swirling in Uchiha Itachi’s eyes, the scarlet pupils filled with an eerie feeling.

Seeing this, Inuzuka Hana felt a bit envious. It was one thing that the older brother was so strong, but the younger brother was also a rare genius ; it really left no path for normal people like her.

However, Uchiha An knew what the Uchiha Clan had to experience every time they awakened their eyes ; he could only say it really felt like a curse.

« Then train hard and adapt to these eyes as soon as possible. »

In short, seeing Uchiha Itachi’s growth, Uchiha An was still very gratified.

It was a pity he hadn’t awakened his eyes ; otherwise, with the speed of the Rumble-Rumble Fruit, Uchiha An would have had absolute certainty of instantly killing Jonin Toroi.

After the three chatted for a while longer, Uchiha Itachi and Inuzuka Hana left the ward, letting Uchiha An rest properly for a while.

Once the two had completely left, Uchiha An began to check the system that had been silent for four years.

Three lottery chances—two standard draws and one super guaranteed draw—could be considered quite good.

« System, perform two standard draws first ! »

A massive roulette wheel appeared before Uchiha An’s eyes, with familiar Devil Fruits displayed on it.

As for the Logia-type Rumble-Rumble Fruit that Uchiha An had already consumed, it had turned completely gray.

Then, the huge pointer on the wheel began to spin, passing over one Devil Fruit after another, before stopping on a flat-shaped Devil Fruit.

[Ding ! Congratulations to the host for obtaining the Paramecia-type Wash-Wash Fruit.]

« Paramecia-type Wash-Wash Fruit ? »

Uchiha An was quite satisfied with the result of this draw. The Wash-Wash Fruit was not weak in combat performance ; upon making contact, it could turn people and objects into laundry, washing and hanging them out to dry.

But more importantly, the Wash-Wash Fruit had an effect on the soul.

If objects were washed, they would lose their form, while living beings would lose their strength. It could also slightly wash away the evil deep inside a person’s heart, cleansing their soul.

One could only say that any Devil Fruit capable of affecting the soul would not be too weak.

« Continue ! »

As Uchiha An’s voice fell, the huge pointer started again, finally stopping on a Devil Fruit Uchiha An did not recognize at all.

[Ding ! Congratulations to the host for obtaining the Paramecia-type Whisper-Whisper Fruit.]

The Paramecia-type Whisper-Whisper Fruit was a Devil Fruit that was relatively useless in the One Piece world, and even more useless in the Ninja World.

If an animal was willing, one could communicate with it ; if the animal was unwilling, it could simply ignore the ability user.

In the One Piece world, animals generally couldn’t speak, so the Whisper-Whisper Fruit had some use.

But in the Ninja World, even slightly powerful Tailed Beasts and Summoned Beasts could speak, making it appear even more useless.

« Quick, System, for my final super guaranteed draw, I must obtain a powerful Devil Fruit ! »

Uchiha An began to pray frantically.

The huge pointer began to spin once again.

He didn’t know if it was his imagination, but it felt like the pointer spun for a long time during this draw.

[Ding ! Congratulations to the host for obtaining the Paramecia-type Op-Op Fruit.]

He didn’t know what was going on today ; it seemed like he had stumbled into a nest of Paramecia-type Devil Fruits.

Ranking the three Devil Fruits by value, the Op-Op Fruit was naturally the strongest, followed by the Wash-Wash Fruit. As for the Whisper-Whisper Fruit, it was truly trash.

After finishing the lottery, Uchiha An happily fell asleep on the hospital bed ; after all, his body had not yet recovered.

...

Three days later.

With his body much better, Uchiha An began his training, as did the now cold and aloof Uchiha Itachi.

The two brothers began their Taijutsu and Ninjutsu training in the Uchiha Clan camp.

The previous battle had made Fugaku very worried, so he allowed the brothers to learn any Ninjutsu they wanted.

Suddenly, a young man with yellow hair appeared in the Uchiha Clan camp and suddenly grabbed toward Uchiha An.

« Fire Release : Great Fireball Technique ! »

Upon seeing this, Uchiha Itachi immediately used the only technique he knew, the Great Fireball Technique, to protect his brother, but the yellow-haired man’s figure simply vanished.

« As expected of a young genius from the Uchiha Clan, to have mastered a C-rank Ninjutsu at such a young age. »

« The Yellow Flash, Lord Minato Namikaze. »

The person who had arrived was none other than the Little Sun, the Yellow Flash, Minato Namikaze.

« You must be Uchiha Itachi ! Your performance just now was good, and your move was very decisive. »

Minato Namikaze reached out and ruffled Uchiha Itachi’s hair. Uchiha Itachi looked at Minato Namikaze with a hint of admiration ; after all, during this Ninja World War, Minato Namikaze had made a tremendous name for himself.

Uchiha An felt a bit jealous seeing this and stepped forward, blocking in front of Uchiha Itachi.

« You must be Uchiha An ! I am aware of your performance, too ! I came here this time to reward you. »

Minato Namikaze took a handful of money directly from his wallet and stuffed it into Uchiha An’s hand.

« You made a significant contribution in the previous battle, killing five Chunin from the Hidden Cloud Village and helping to kill a Jonin, preventing Konoha’s intelligence from leaking. This is equivalent to completing a B-rank mission ; this money is your reward for completing the mission. »

« I didn’t expect a four-year-old child who hasn’t even become a Ninja yet to have completed a B-rank mission. Not bad. »
