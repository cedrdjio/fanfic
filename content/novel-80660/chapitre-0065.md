# Chapitre 65 — Defeating the Enemy with One Move!

When An noticed the guards on the perimeter of the Hidden Sand Village falling, he realized that the phosphorus powder had begun to take effect.

After waiting quietly for a moment, An finally gave the order to attack.

« Follow me and kill the enemy ! Do not be soft-hearted at this moment, show no mercy ! »

After An’s voice fell, the members of the Shimura Clan and the Uchiha Clan roared and charged toward the Hidden Sand Village. A faint atmosphere of competition radiated between the two clans, but it was a healthy competition, and An did not want to interfere too much.

As the two sides made contact, the combined Ninjutsu that An had cast quickly displayed its powerful influence.

The Konoha Ninja dealt with the Hidden Sand Village’s Ninja as easily as if they were toying with children ; they could even cut them down in an instant with a simple slash.

Even when Ninja wearing gas masks appeared occasionally, everyone showed no mercy in surrounding and killing them.

Soon, led by An and numerous experts, the group advanced effortlessly and arrived directly at the core area of the Hidden Sand Village.

« Is this the Hidden Sand Village ? It looks much poorer than our Konoha. »

« Our Konoha is the wealthiest Village in the Ninja World ; this is something no other Ninja Village can compare to. »

« I never expected to be able to fight into the core area of the Hidden Sand Village in this lifetime ; Lord An is truly too great ! »

« Victory ! »

« Victory ! »

Just as the spirits of the Konoha group were high, dozens of figures suddenly appeared to block their path. These were all the Jonin left behind in the Hidden Sand Village.

Then, countless figures sprang out, directly surrounding the leaders of the group.

« Hmph ! You have fallen into our trap, you Konoha fellows. This shall be your burial ground ! »

Ebizo’s tone was very firm, as if he felt he had everything under control.

If it weren’t for the fact that An’s Observation Haki had not detected anything unusual, he might have actually believed this old man’s words.

« Oh ! We broke through all the way here quite easily. I wonder what kind of ambush the Hidden Sand Village has ? »

An appeared carefree, instantly calming the restless morale of his troops.

« Hmph ! Despicable and shameless fellows, actually using poison to launch a sneak attack on us. Then, this old woman will teach you a lesson with poison as well ! »

Chiyo’s voice was grinding with teeth ; it was obvious she was extremely furious about this sneak attack.

In an instant, a burst of green smoke was spewed from Chiyo’s mouth, aimed directly at the people from Konoha Village.

« We were already guarding against you ! You stinking old hag ! »

At this moment, Danzo had the feeling of an old urchin ; he had truly let himself go, and then decisively put on a gas mask.

Seeing this, the other Konoha Ninja also hurriedly put on gas masks. This left Chiyo, Ebizo, and the others somewhat disappointed ; it seemed they could not count on winning with poison.

« Don’t let them stall for time, attack immediately ! »

Following a loud shout from An, the two sides immediately engaged in battle.

However, this time the Konoha Ninja were no longer fighting so easily ; instead, a battle of blood and fire had begun.

Nevertheless, Konoha still held a huge overall advantage in this battle. Not only were there more high-quality and numerous experts, but with the output from the Devil Fruit users, they were still beating the members of the Hidden Sand Village until they did not know which way was up.

Meanwhile, the most capable members of the Hidden Sand Village, Chiyo and Ebizo, relied on the advantage of their Puppet Technique to wreak havoc on the battlefield, causing significant casualties among the Konoha group.

« Leave these two old fogies to me ! »

An’s loud shout immediately drew everyone’s attention on the battlefield. As for Chiyo and Ebizo, they instantly withdrew and stood back-to-back, both watching An with full vigilance.

« Oh ! Are you two old fogies, famous for so long, afraid of a fledgling like me ? »

« You are not some fledgling brat, but a powerhouse who has been famous for a long time. »

After speaking, Chiyo and Ebizo immediately used their strongest methods, and eighteen puppets instantly blocked the space in front of them.

« Our unique Puppet Technique of the Hidden Sand Village is the nemesis of Lightning Style Ninjutsu. I’m afraid you, the Konoha Thunder God, will die in the Hidden Sand Village today ! »

Ebizo’s tone was very firm, as if he already had the certainty of victory.

An did not refute this ; after all, the puppets of puppeteers were mostly made of wood and iron, and wood indeed had very poor electrical conductivity.

Unless An used Water Release Ninjutsu to wet the wood, it would indeed be difficult to fight.

However, An did not care about this ; with his abilities, breaking the Puppet Technique was still easy.

« Then let’s give it a try ! »

As An’s voice fell, Ebizo and Chiyo made the first move, and eighteen puppets directly surrounded An.

An was calm and decisive amidst the encirclement of the puppets, taking a deep breath and feeling the scent of blood that filled the air.

« Die ! »

In an instant, the eighteen puppets attacked simultaneously, constantly striking at An with both ranged and melee attacks.

However, An’s body moved suddenly, flexibly dodging the puppets' frantic attacks, while the ferocious puppets left behind a trail of shattered figures in An’s wake.

« Lightning Release : 100 Million Volts : Thunder Dragon ! ! »

Lightning rapidly condensed in An’s palm, forming a massive surge of energy that was released frantically toward the surrounding puppets.

The wooden and iron bodies of the puppets made creaking sounds, clearly unable to withstand the power of the lightning.

« Kid, you cannot defeat us by relying on Lightning Style Ninjutsu ! »

As Chiyo spoke, she took out a Ninjutsu scroll, and instantly, ten more puppets appeared on the field.

Ebizo saw this and followed suit, likewise taking out eight more puppets.

« That was just playing around ; prepare to face defeat from now on ! »

As An spoke, he once again condensed the power of his Rumble-Rumble Fruit ; the ultimate power of lightning appeared exceptionally powerful.

« Lightning Release : Thunder Metallurgy ! »

As An’s voice fell, the high-temperature flames erupting from within the lightning enveloped the puppets, forming a raging sea of fire.

The temperature of the flames caused the air to distort, and the whistling sound seemed to be the whispers of the raging fire.

The puppets emitted wailing sounds like living people, and like scorched metal fragments, they turned to ashes in the high heat.

A moment later, as the flames gradually subsided, only a scene of ruin and scattered dark debris remained.

An stood amidst the ruins of the puppets ; the surroundings were silent, with only faint embers rising slowly.

« Are there any more puppets ? If there are, I’ll just use a few more moves. »

In an instant, the entire battlefield was shaken. Seeing this, the Konoha Ninja cheered one after another, praising An’s power.

As for the Hidden Sand Village Ninja, they were devastated. Their two strongest trump cards had already failed, and their already unfavorable situation was made even worse, causing the members of the Hidden Sand Village to lose their will to fight completely.

« Surrender and you still have a way to live. If you are unwilling, not only will you die, but the entire Hidden Sand Village will be destroyed ! »

Danzo Shimura’s words were like the final straw that broke the camel’s back ; after exchanging glances, the higher-ups of the Hidden Sand Village laid down the weapons in their hands.
