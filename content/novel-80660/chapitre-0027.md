# Chapitre 27 — Joining Root!

Just like that, Uchiha An and Shiranui Genma began to chase each other around the arena, one attacking and the other defending.

However, it didn’t take long for Shiranui Genma to lose his patience. After all, with the three-minute time limit, Shiranui Genma didn’t dare to keep playing hide-and-seek with An.

« Are you only going to keep dodging ? You evil Uchiha ! »

« I originally wanted to save you some face, but since you want to lose so quickly, I won’t hold back anymore ! »

Originally, An had planned to tangle with Shiranui Genma a bit longer, but thinking about the gift Minato had already prepared for him, An became even more impatient.

« Lightning Release : Five Million Volts : Lightning Snake ! »

A silver-white bolt of lightning, shaped like a small snake, struck swiftly toward Shiranui Genma.

An’s first strike was timed with extreme precision.

Facing the extreme speed of the lightning, it could be said that Shiranui Genma had neither the time nor the ability to dodge ; he could only watch helplessly as An’s attack landed on him.

Instantly electrocuted, Shiranui Genma lost consciousness immediately, lying on the ground charred and smoking.

Of course, if a normal human were shocked by five million volts, their body would basically be cold already. Unfortunately for him, Shiranui Genma is a two-dimensional character, so this blow only caused him to lose consciousness.

In One Piece, Enel starts off with tens of millions of volts, yet he barely kills anyone. One has to say that the humans in the One Piece world are truly tanky.

However, Ninjas in the Naruto world are high-attack, low-defense beings, and their physical strength is not as high as humans in the One Piece world. Even though An’s attack was only five million volts, it still shocked Shiranui Genma quite severely.

« How are you, Genma ? »

« Not good, hurry up and save him ! »

Raido Namiashi and Aoba Yamashiro immediately rushed forward and reached out to help Shiranui Genma up.

The two examined him carefully and found that Shiranui Genma was not seriously injured ; he had just been knocked unconscious by the electricity.

However, An’s performance had shocked everyone present.

A seven-year-old child instantly defeating a Special Jonin—are you sure this isn’t fiction ?

[Congratulations to the host for defeating a Special Jonin. Reward : One super guaranteed lottery chance.]

An did not expect this to yield a super guaranteed lottery chance ; it was truly a pleasant surprise.

Minato Namikaze, Hiruzen Sarutobi, and Shikaku Nara all smiled at this, clearly very satisfied with An’s performance. This was, after all, a peerless genius of Konoha.

As for Danzo Shimura, his gaze toward An was filled with intensity, which made An feel very uncomfortable. An even began to have a few doubts about Danzo Shimura’s sexual orientation.

Beside him, Shimura Kazama’s expression was incredibly ugly, looking as uncomfortable as if he had eaten a dead fly.

Anyone targeted by such a peerless genius would feel very nervous.

[Congratulations to the host for graduating early. Reward : One super guaranteed lottery chance.]

An did not expect this damn system to be so generous this time, giving him two super guaranteed lottery chances.

« Uchiha An has passed the assessment and is permitted to graduate early ! »

« Of course, my previous promise will still be fulfilled. You can come to the Hokage Office to find me and choose the high-level Ninjutsu you want ! »

After Minato Namikaze finished speaking, the group prepared to leave. Originally, things should have ended here.

However, right at this moment, Danzo Shimura, the « Pot Shadow » of Konoha, jumped out.

« Since we are all here, let’s arrange things for these three geniuses today. I wonder what kind of arrangements you have in mind for the three of them, Minato ? »

Hearing this, Minato Namikaze frowned.

Before he became the Fourth Hokage, it was fine for Danzo Shimura to call him Minato, as he was a contemporary of Teacher Hiruzen.

But after becoming the Fourth Hokage, Danzo Shimura still called him Minato, which made Minato Namikaze feel it was difficult to establish his authority.

Because Danzo Shimura was an Advisor elder, Minato had long wanted to push him out.

« I plan to have the three of them form a squad to carry out missions, with Kurenai Yuhi as their team leader Jonin. »

Hearing Minato Namikaze’s arrangement, everyone nodded in satisfaction.

Letting Kurenai Yuhi, an expert in genjutsu, teach the Uchiha was, it could be said, very appropriate.

Danzo Shimura and Shimura Kazama’s expressions were somewhat gloomy ; if things were arranged this way, then their Root wouldn’t get anything.

« Minato, I think your arrangement is too reckless. »

« If an accident happens to all three of these peerless geniuses at the same time, it wouldn’t just be the Uchiha Clan’s business, it would be a huge loss for our entire Konoha ! »

« So I think it would be better to arrange them separately ! »

Danzo Shimura’s words had a hint of reason, which made the others hesitate a bit.

Shikaku Nara pondered for a moment and walked up to the three of them.

« So, what do you three think ? »

In an instant, everyone’s attention was focused on the three of them.

After all, if the three of them were unwilling, no matter how they assigned them, it would be useless.

An looked at Shisui and Itachi beside him. After the three exchanged glances, An finally stepped forward as their representative and spoke.

« The three of us are willing to be separated. Shisui and Itachi can join Teacher Minato’s Anbu or follow a Jonin to carry out missions. »

« As for me, I am willing to join Root and serve as a subordinate to Lord Danzo ! »

Hearing An’s words, everyone’s faces showed strange expressions. The Uchiha Clan and the Shimura Clan were not on good terms, and Uchiha An and Shimura Kazama had some conflicts.

Clearly, no one expected that An would actually be willing to join Root, with which he had a bad relationship.

Danzo Shimura and Shimura Kazama both smiled very brightly, the skin on their faces wrinkling up like blooming chrysanthemums.

Neither of them expected that An, the most talented of the three, would voluntarily join Root ; this was truly rare.

Shimura Kazama was even secretly delighted ; as long as Uchiha An came to Root, he could deal with him as he pleased and eliminate this future trouble at any time.

« In that case, let Uchiha Itachi join the Anbu, and let Uchiha Shisui join a Jonin’s squad ! »

As Minato Namikaze’s voice fell, the distribution of the three was basically settled, though everyone present had their own thoughts.

In fact, this was a decision An made out of consideration for the future of Itachi and Shisui.

According to the original plot, both of them would experience various kinds of pain and awaken the Mangekyo Sharingan at a very young age.

However, An was afraid that because of his own interference, the plot had already become somewhat unrecognizable, and it might instead affect their growth.

If he separated from Shisui and Itachi, letting them face all this on their own, perhaps their Mangekyo Sharingan would awaken even earlier.
