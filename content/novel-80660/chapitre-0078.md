# Chapitre 78 — A Painless Death!

An decided to stop using the Imitation-Imitation Fruit’s ability ; after all, that was too boring.

An had played enough. Now, An was going to use his true strength to confront the Shinobi Allied Forces and let them know what despair truly was.

And at the very moment An revealed his form, two figures attacked simultaneously.

The two were none other than the powerhouses of the Shinobi Allied Forces : Raikage Ai and the Eight-Tails Jinchuriki, Killer Bee.

« Double Lariat ! »

Raikage Ai, in his Chakra Mode, and Killer Bee, covered in his Tailed Beast cloak, struck at the same time, executing this combination taijutsu with perfect coordination.

It could be said that they dared not be the slightest bit careless, as An’s terrifying nature had already made everyone wary.

In this war, An was an unpredictable variable that had to be eliminated as soon as possible.

To everyone’s astonishment, An’s head was cleanly severed by the attack and fell to the ground, instantly filling the air with the scent of death.

However, what struck everyone as strange was that not a single drop of blood flowed from the dead An.

Furthermore, An’s body did not fall ; it simply stood quietly in place.

Just then, the head on the ground spoke : « Scary ! How terrifying ! »

Then, amidst the astonished gazes of the crowd, a flash of lightning occurred, and An returned to his body.

In the world of One Piece, An’s display was merely the application of a Logia-type Devil Fruit.

But this was the Naruto world ; this scene frightened everyone present, leaving them frozen in place.

Everyone felt as if they had just seen a ghost, unable to believe what was happening before their eyes.

« Hehehe... Did you see that ? This is my true strength ! You cannot kill me at all ! You can only let me toy with you ! » An said with a wild laugh, a hint of evil, charming amusement flickering in his eyes.

Next, An was going to use a move he had modified to crush Raikage Ai and Killer Bee in one fell swoop.

« Lightning Release : Flash Bomb – Flying Thunder God – Slash ! »

Of course, this was a slash executed by An at extreme speed, not the technique of Senju Tobirama.

This was a technique An created by fusing the light and speed attributes of the Rumble-Rumble Fruit, resulting in such a terrifying method of attack.

This technique of An’s did not require any markers or scrolls ; it only required him to be the center, emitting light that was highly irritating to human eyes, while An himself, relying on Observation Haki, was like a fish in water amidst the intense glare.

Relying on his own speed, An could flash to any location within nearly a hundred meters, and while moving, he could rapidly unleash his slashes, forming a blade of light that was impossible to defend against.

In an instant, the dazzling light illuminated the dim sky as if it were broad daylight, and everyone instinctively closed their eyes.

Feeling a powerful and dangerous aura, everyone’s hearts sank.

« Ah ! »

« Ah ! »

Immediately, two screams drew everyone’s attention, and they all became alert, secretly wondering who An had attacked.

When the light dimmed and eventually disappeared, Raikage Ai and the Eight-Tails Jinchuriki, Killer Bee, were seen lying in pools of blood, with two wounds deep enough to see bone on their bodies.

« Lord Raikage... Killer Bee... »

The other members of the Shinobi Allied Forces, seeing this, all wore expressions of sorrow and despair.

An’s attacks were like the scythe of the Shinigami, mercilessly reaping the lives of the Shinobi Allied Forces.

In a short time, the Shinobi Allied Forces had lost half of their combat strength, and both Iwagakure and Kumogakure had lost their leaders and Jinchuriki.

It could be said that An’s display had dealt a massive blow to the Shinobi Allied Forces, leaving those present who were still conscious with absolutely no courage to fight An.

Looking at An’s calm, smiling face, everyone felt unprecedented fear and helplessness, not knowing what to do or if they would survive.

« Do you still want to continue ? Do you still want to resist ? Do you see ? This is your final outcome ! You have no hope left ! ! »

At this moment, An had secured an absolute advantage ; the Shinobi Allied Forces were in a desperate situation.

The soldiers of the Shinobi Allied Forces looked at An before them, their hearts filled with fear and despair.

It could be said that they no longer had any will to resist ; they only wanted to escape this terrifying battlefield.

However, just then, a black shadow darted out from the void, thrusting towards An’s throat.

It was a sharp Kunai, radiating an endless cold light.

« Ding ! »

A crisp metallic clashing sound rang out, breaking the dead silence of the scene.

Everyone looked in astonishment at the attacked An, only to see his neck wrapped in a layer of black substance, as if he were wearing armor.

This was the defense An had formed using Armament Haki, blocking the attack from the void.

« Obito ? »

An looked at the Kunai in surprise, then immediately turned to its owner.

Seeing this, Obito did not linger. Having failed to land the hit, Obito immediately knew that he was no match for An.

Thus, Obito immediately retreated back into the Kamui dimension, disappearing from everyone’s sight.

However, Obito’s attack greatly restored the morale of the Shinobi Allied Forces ; after all, even in this desperate situation, there was someone who dared to challenge the invincible An.

« For the Village, everyone, attack together ! »

« I don’t believe this guy is invincible ; his Chakra might already be exhausted. »

« Attack together. »

The Special Jonin and Jonin who still maintained some strength unleashed their most powerful Ninjutsu and weapons, attempting to inflict some damage on An.

Some used Fire Release, Water Release, Earth Release, Wind Release, and Lightning Release, the five basic nature transformations, to attack, while others used Kunai, shuriken, darts, and other hidden weapons.

Everyone surrounded An tightly, as if they were not prepared to give him any chance to catch his breath.

However, An did not care at all about the Shinobi Allied Forces' attacks ; he just looked at them coldly, a trace of disgust and anger flashing in his eyes.

Suddenly, An leaped up and flew directly into the air, moving away from the battle zone.

« Lightning Release : 100x Speed – 100x Power – World-Ending Thunder Welcome ! »

As An shouted the name of this Ninjutsu, he formed hand seals with both hands and then pointed towards the sky.

Instantly, a massive black thunder ball appeared in the sky, emitting terrifying electric currents.

That was the world-ending power An had created using his own Chakra and the Rumble-Rumble Fruit ; it possessed a hundred times the speed and power of an ordinary Lightning Release Ninjutsu.

Then, An waved his hand gently, and that black thunder ball smashed towards the ground with lightning speed.

« Boom ! »

A heaven-shaking and earth-shattering explosion rang out, and the entire area was engulfed by An’s thunder ball.

Intense lightning raged across the snowfield, illuminating the entire sky.

Those Ninjas who had just been attacking An had absolutely no time to react before they were swallowed by An’s thunder ball.

The members of the Shinobi Allied Forces didn’t even have time to scream before they were turned into ashes.
