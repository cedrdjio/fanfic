# Chapitre 38 — Fierce Battle with Takigakure Ninja!

« Hahaha ! You all must die ! »

Outside the smoke, Shimura Kazama laughed wildly, clearly believing he had victory in his grasp.

However, Uchiha An, who had been tracking Shimura Kazama’s presence using Observation Haki, curled his lip in disdain. He pulled a Kunai from his leg holster with his right hand and threw it.

Shimura Kazama, who was in the middle of laughing, instantly fell silent as blood flowed uncontrollably from his neck.

The laughable Shimura Kazama was thus easily slain by Uchiha An.

As the dust settled, the Takigakure Ninja revealed their figures.

It was clear that half of the original sixty-plus Ninja were dead or injured, and the survivors were all essentially wounded. As for Leader Shechuan of Takigakure and the Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi, they were covered in charred marks.

It seemed likely that to protect the Takigakure Ninja, the two of them had blocked much of the damage during the explosion.

Watching the Takigakure Ninja on the riverbank not far away, a rare smile appeared on Yamato’s face.

« Although Shimura Kazama was a traitor to Konoha, this turn of events has significantly lowered the difficulty of our mission. »

After speaking, Yamato took the lead and charged toward the riverbank, launching an assault against more than thirty people alone.

Aburame Torune and Uchiha An saw this and immediately followed, both trailing behind Yamato.

« Hmph ! Isolated and dying men, just struggling in vain now. »

In an instant, the Takigakure Ninja stood in a row, performing hand seals in unison while Chakra gathered in their mouths.

« Water Release : Raging Sea Waves ! »

In an instant, the water from the river gathered together, forming a water column over ten meters high that directly covered the entire area.

Seeing this, Yamato knew he had to block this attack, so he also quickly performed hand seals.

« Uchiha An, help me ! »

Receiving the order, Uchiha An understood Yamato’s intent and instantly flashed over to his side.

« Wood Release : Wood Barrier ! »

An equilateral triangular wooden barrier blocked the path directly in front of the three, and Uchiha An immediately stepped forward to activate his fruit ability.

« Fiftyfold Volume Enhancement ! »

« Fiftyfold Weight Enhancement ! »

Yamato instantly felt the barrier he was controlling sink, and he struggled to maintain the Ninjutsu that had been enhanced fiftyfold.

« Bang ! »

The massive water column slammed into the barrier, delivering continuous impacts.

Unfortunately, it was blocked by the combined Ninjutsu of Uchiha An and Yamato.

By the time the attack completely ended, the distance between the two sides had closed significantly.

Many Takigakure Ninja leaped into the air, pouncing on the three like ghosts from every corner.

Kunai, shuriken, and sharp daggers whistled through the air, and some even sneakily threw Kunai attached with explosive tags.

Uchiha An, Aburame Torune, and Yamato were no weaklings, and they all took up their weapons to defend themselves.

The sounds of explosions, the whistling of air, and the clashing of metal intertwined.

Soon, Uchiha An, Aburame Torune, and Yamato were separated by the Takigakure Ninja during the battle, but this also allowed Uchiha An to attack without any reservations.

« Lightning Release : Thousand Thunderbolts - Fiftyfold Enhancement ! »

In an instant, rolling thunderclouds suddenly appeared above Uchiha An’s head, accompanied by flashes of lightning.

As if the sky’s fury had been ignited, countless bolts of lightning poured down from the clouds, dancing wantonly throughout the entire area.

The erratic lightning struck down without restraint, each strike bringing violent tremors and deafening roars.

The ground trembled under the impact of the lightning, plants swayed unsteadily in the aftermath of the thunderstorm, and Ninja lives were worthless at this moment.

Uchiha An stood at the very center of this lightning storm, his body covered in lightning light, with every current spiraling and dancing around him, emitting a shocking and powerful energy.

Under the illumination of the lightning, the entire area was instantly lit up, resembling the apocalypse.

« This guy is a madman ; he’ll strike himself down ! »

« The Konoha Thunder God ! Is this truly the power of a god ? »

Uchiha An’s attack directly took out most of the Takigakure Ninja surrounding him ; only a very few managed to escape.

Leader Shechuan and Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi felt their hearts bleeding at this sight. They had brought half of Takigakure’s power out to ambush the Konoha Ninja, but now it was almost completely lost.

The guy they originally thought would be the easiest to deal with now appeared to be the most troublesome one.

Instantly, Leader Shechuan and Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi both gave up on besieging Aburame Torune and Yamato, simultaneously lunging directly at Uchiha An.

« You all besiege those two ; the Konoha Thunder God will be handled by me and Leader Shechuan. »

As he spoke, Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi directly activated his Tailed Beast Cloak. Given Uchiha An’s performance just now, Zezhi dared not be the slightest bit careless.

« Seven-Colored Illusion ! »

The phosphorous powder from Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi’s mouth quickly covered Uchiha An, while simultaneously unleashing a high-level Genjutsu.

Hearing Zezhi’s voice, Uchiha An activated his One-Tomoe Sharingan. Coupled with his own super-strong Observation Haki, Zezhi’s Genjutsu had absolutely no effect on him.

« Lightning Body Flicker ! »

Uchiha An’s figure instantly appeared at Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi’s lower abdomen.

Seeing this, Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi seized the opportunity, and all seven tails lashed out violently at Uchiha An.

Unfortunately, within the perception of Observation Haki, Zezhi’s attacks were all useless ; Uchiha An dodged every one of Zezhi’s tails with agile footwork.

« Rasengan : Fiftyfold Volume Enhancement ! »

In an instant, the originally palm-sized Rasengan grew to the size of a house, then slammed heavily into Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi.

« Boom ! »

With just one hit, Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi’s figure was sent flying backward, breaking several large trees in succession.

Already severely injured, Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi violently spat out a mouthful of blood, his face gaining a hint of ruddiness.

Aburame Torune and Yamato were extremely surprised at the sight and resisted even more vigorously.

It could be said that if Uchiha An were not here, the two of them would likely not have had a good outcome today.

Seeing this, Leader Shechuan immediately helped Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi up, looking at Uchiha An with a face full of hatred.

« Leader, I’m afraid the Seven-Tails will no longer belong to our Takigakure. You take the remaining people and leave first. »

« But... »

Leader Shechuan wanted to say something more, but was directly interrupted by Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi.

« There are no 'buts' at a time like this. The Ninja World is about to change. »

At this moment, Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi thought of many things. Konoha, which was already the richest and most powerful of the Five Great Ninja Villages, combined with the Seven-Tails and an unparalleled genius like Uchiha An, would likely lead to constant war in the Ninja World.

Hearing Zezhi’s words, Leader Shechuan sighed helplessly. If they continued to fight, he feared that half of Takigakure’s power and he, the leader, would all die here.

Helpless, Leader Shechuan handed a small bottle to Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi, then roared at the sky.

« Retreat ! »

After giving the order, Leader Shechuan was the first to pull away and run into the distance. The rest of the Takigakure Ninja glanced at Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi and followed in Leader Shechuan’s footsteps.

Seeing this scene, Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi laughed loudly, but the tears at the corners of his eyes betrayed his reluctance to leave the Village at this moment.

« Chomei, my lifetime is very short to you, but as I’m dying, can you, the last friend to be by my side, help me one last time ? »
