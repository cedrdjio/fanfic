# Chapitre 79 — The Great Battle Continues!

Following that strike from An, countless Ninja lost their lives in an instant.

If the battle had not constantly shifted, I fear few of the fifty thousand Ninja present would have survived.

Their corpses and blood stained the snowy plains red, becoming witnesses to this tragedy.

The people from Konoha, upon seeing this, were stunned, as if terrified by an attack that resembled a divine punishment.

After all, they had witnessed the world-destroying beam of light that An had unleashed. Even though An was just standing there quietly, the unparalleled pressure radiating from him made it almost impossible for them to breathe.

« To see the power of a god once again ! »

Konan, having narrowly escaped with her life, muttered to herself. If she had not been on the outskirts of the battlefield, she would likely have been among those who had just perished.

Konan looked at An’s cold, emotionless face, and a trace of awe and terror rose in her heart.

She knew that An was no longer the domineering yet kind Newgate she had once known ; he had become an existence that transcended humanity.

Deva Path Pain, or rather Nagato, who was controlling Pain, was extremely exasperated. After all, that last move had destroyed five of the Pain bodies, and for the upcoming battle, he would have to use all of his power.

Feeling his body growing increasingly weak, a sense of unease and anger rose within Nagato.

It could be said that at this moment, Nagato was extremely unwilling to accept this. He did not believe it—how could An possess power greater than his own ? How could he possibly stop him from realizing his ideal of peace ?

Yet at this moment, An stood at the edge of the battlefield with a look of compassion for the world. Looking at the devastation and deathly silence before him, a trace of sorrow and helplessness arose in his heart.

An had no choice at this moment ; he could only do this. He could only use his power to end this meaningless war and let the world return to peace and beauty.

An’s actions were like those of the First Emperor ; only through tragic sacrifice could the unification of the world be achieved.

Only by sparing no effort to forge an order of his own with blood and fire could a peace belonging to the future be maintained.

Furthermore, this was a unification that was not understood by the masses ; naturally, there was no lack of slanderers and detractors.

They would inevitably be filled with dissatisfaction and resistance toward An’s actions, and they would be full of doubt and mockery regarding An’s ideals.

« I intended to offer my heart to the bright moon, yet the moon shines upon the ditch ! »

Hearing An’s words, the surviving Ninja immediately split into two groups. One group, filled with righteous indignation, cursed An angrily.

« You devil ! »

« You cold-blooded, heartless wretch, sooner or later you will die a miserable death ! »

The surviving Ninja were filled with hatred and resentment toward An, believing only that An was a cold-blooded tyrant and a madman who sought to destroy the world.

As for the remaining Ninja, they had been scared out of their wits by An and fled the battlefield without saying a word.

They were filled with fear and despair toward An, believing that An was an irresistible god and an inescapable nightmare.

At this moment, the only one who could barely trade a few blows with An was Nagato, who possessed the Rinnegan, or rather, Deva Path Pain.

« Konan, do your best ! »

Deva Path Pain’s voice was somewhat hoarse, and a trace of helplessness and sorrow was revealed in his eyes.

Nagato knew that An’s strength had already surpassed the imagination of him and the others ; even his Rinnegan could not contend with it.

Konan nodded calmly. It could be said that Konan knew full well that her abilities were limited ; after the next move, the battle would no longer be something she could participate in.

She could only pray that Nagato would be able to create a miracle and defeat this guy in front of them who was like a god.

« Oh ! Nagato, actually, I know all your intelligence, but unfortunately, it is of no use against me. »

An’s expression remained very calm, but his eyes flashed with coldness and mockery.

By now, An had already wiped out more than half of the Shinobi Allied Forces. It could be said that his only remaining opponents were Nagato and Obito, who was hiding in the shadows.

It was a pity that neither of them had any way to deal with him, and they could not even cause him the slightest bit of harm.

In an instant, Konan’s figure suddenly transformed into countless sheets of paper in the air, drifting toward An as if blown by a gentle breeze.

A trace of disdain flashed in An’s eyes. Isn’t it just six hundred billion explosive tags ? This could not pose any threat to him at all.

In a flash, one sheet of paper after another drifted down near An, and then they began to explode continuously.

Every single sheet was an explosive tag that Konan had exhausted all her strength to create and collect ; the power of the explosion was enough to split mountains and crack stones.

In an instant, the explosion blasted a deep pit directly around An, with fire soaring into the sky and smoke rolling in waves.

Konan did not stop the attack. She gritted her teeth, kept increasing the output of explosive tags, and continued to bombard the place where An was.

Konan knew this was the only thing she could do ; she had to buy a chance for Nagato.

One minute. Two minutes. Five minutes. Ten minutes.

Just as the explosion was about to end, a figure suddenly dashed into the explosion ; it was Deva Path Pain, being controlled by Nagato.

At this moment, Nagato had abandoned everything, determined to use Yahiko’s corpse to gamble on his future dream.

Konan, seeing this, had tears in her eyes. She clearly felt Nagato’s determination and helplessness. Konan also knew that after this battle, she might not even be able to preserve Yahiko’s corpse.

Konan, known as the Angel, could only pray silently, hoping that Nagato would succeed and that Yahiko could finally rest in peace.

« I have never tried this move either, I don’t know what the effect will be ! » As Deva Path Pain spoke, he pushed the black hole in his right hand straight out, directly at the position where An was standing.

That was one of Nagato’s most powerful secret techniques, the strongest method capable of devouring all matter.

In an instant, the violent explosion was absorbed by the black hole, and the surrounding soil and snowflakes were also continuously absorbed by the black hole in the air.

Even An’s body, at the edge of the black hole, underwent a certain degree of distortion. This strange experience made An feel somewhat intrigued.

And there was no need to mention the trees and giant rocks that kept crashing over ; they were all ruthlessly devoured by the black hole.

Soon, a giant sphere of one hundred meters appeared in everyone’s line of sight ; it was formed after the black hole had devoured all the matter.

As for An’s figure, it had been completely swallowed, leaving no trace behind.

« This time, did we succeed ? »

Konan, watching the battle from afar, muttered to herself, her bewildered eyes filled with anticipation and worry.

Konan did not know if Nagato’s secret technique could completely destroy An, nor did she know if there was any possibility of preserving Yahiko’s corpse.

And Deva Path Pain, unable to bear his own power, had his body slowly dissipate, and in the end, everything returned to calm.

Just at this moment, the hundred-meter giant sphere suddenly shook violently, and the surrounding snowy plains also trembled continuously.
