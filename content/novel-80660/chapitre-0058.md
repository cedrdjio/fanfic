# Chapitre 58 — Nagato and Konan!

Observing the fanatical crowd around him, An had to admit that Konan and Nagato’s method of rule might not be flawless.

However, in this era filled with war and famine, their leadership could be considered quite excellent.

Although there might be some issues with their way of ruling, they had tried their best under such difficult circumstances.

« Let’s go outside and talk ! I’m sure you wouldn’t want to fight me inside the Hidden Rain Village. »

Konan glanced at An, nodded slightly, and briefly stepped aside.

However, when Konan noticed An’s massive figure, she quickly flashed to the side, giving way without hesitation.

An rubbed his nose, looking slightly embarrassed as he walked out.

Soon, amidst the surprised gazes of the crowd, the figures of An and Konan disappeared.

« Who is that guy ? »

« Is Angel-sama going to punish him ? »

« The Three-Tails Jinchuriki who defected from the Hidden Mist Village, known as the strongest man in the world—only he has this physique. »

There were naturally Ninjas from the Hidden Mist Village present, who could also judge An’s identity from his build.

« If it’s him, it’s actually possible he might join our Hidden Mist Village. After all, Angel-sama and God are just too powerful ! »

Suddenly, a smile appeared on the faces of the ordinary civilians present. After all, the stronger the Village, the more peacefully everyone could live ; this was the common interest of all.

But back to the story.

In the rainy night, An closely followed Konan’s figure. As they ran together, it made An involuntarily think of a person and a line.

« I recall the running under the sunset that day ; that was my lost youth. »

Just as An was lost in thought, Konan suddenly stopped in her tracks ahead.

Six strange-looking figures appeared on the distant hillside, perhaps fearing that fighting him would destroy the Hidden Rain Village !

An didn’t expect that he would actually be able to make all of the Six Paths of Pain take action.

This was Jiraiya’s treatment, though unfortunately, Jiraiya ended up dead in the end.

« Three-Tails Jinchuriki, God needs your power ! »

It was Deva Path Pain, acting as Nagato’s representative, who spoke.

Upon hearing this, An rested his chin on his right hand, making a check mark gesture, wearing an expression of deep thought.

« You say you are God ? Is it because of your Rinnegan ? Or because of your powerful strength ? »

Konan snorted coldly and spoke with an unfriendly tone.

« God is naturally omnipotent ! »

Nagato also said : « While these eyes bring me Pain, they also make me omnipotent ! »

An nodded in agreement. With the blessing of the Rinnegan, Deva Path Pain’s abilities included manipulating gravity and repulsion ; Asura Path’s abilities included powerful Taijutsu ; Human Path’s abilities included reading souls ; Animal Path’s abilities included the Summoning Jutsu ; Preta Path’s abilities included absorbing Ninjutsu and Chakra ; and Naraka Path’s abilities included life resurrection.

It could be said that in this world, Nagato’s abilities were indeed similar to an omniscient and omnipotent God to ordinary Ninjas.

Thus, An continued to ask : « Then, can God create a stone that even he himself cannot lift ? »

« If God can create a stone that even he himself cannot lift, then God is not omnipotent. »

« Because there is still a stone that he cannot lift. »

« If God cannot create a stone that even he himself cannot lift, then God is also not omnipotent. »

« Because there is still a stone that he cannot create. »

An directly brought up the famous stone-creation paradox from his previous life to strike at Nagato and Konan, the two who constantly preached and firmly believed that the power of the Rinnegan was God.

Upon hearing An’s words, the CPUs of both Konan and Nagato were burning rapidly ; clearly, they were stumped by this question from An.

Then, Nagato stepped forward and said in a stern tone.

« Then let you witness the power of God. »

While speaking, Deva Path Pain spread his arms and slowly floated up, floating directly in the air in a very scientific manner.

Konan, who was very close to An, directly activated her Bloodline Limit, Paper Ninjutsu, slowly disappearing from An’s side and appearing beside the Six Paths of Pain a moment later.

Only An remained on the plain, watching this pretentious scene.

« Shinra Tensei... Chibaku Tensei ! »

Deva Path Pain directly created a black sphere full of gravity, silently attracting everything around it.

As time passed, the black sphere gradually grew, attracting everything that touched its range. Whether it was the giant rocks on the ground or the trees, all were bound by this invisible force.

When the stone sphere attracted surrounding objects, the energy suppressed within it would constantly accumulate, forming intense pressure.

With the continuous accumulation of energy, the mass of the stone sphere would also become increasingly massive.

Before long, a hundred-meter-large boulder appeared before An’s eyes. An had not expected Nagato to be so reckless, unleashing his ultimate move without even exchanging two blows.

Perhaps he was pushed into a corner by his stone-creation paradox, An thought.

As for Deva Path Pain, he was blindly confident in this move, directly letting out wanton laughter.

« This is the power of God, this is the stone created by God ! »

While speaking, the huge stone sphere fell from the sky, smashing fiercely toward An.

Seeing this, An stood motionless on the spot, as if he were scared silly.

« Hmph ! A mortal who does not know what Pain is, this is the true power belonging to God ! »

Deva Path Pain looked down at An, muttering to himself.

Konan, however, looked worried. « If the Three-Tails Jinchuriki is killed like this, the Three-Tails might leak out and disperse into Chakra, requiring a long time to condense into a new Three-Tails. This will slow down our speed of capturing Tailed Beasts. »

« Rest assured, I have my own measures ! »

As Konan and Nagato’s voices faded, the stone sphere finally smashed into the ground, enveloping An’s figure.

When the stone sphere made contact with the ground and erupted, the resulting shockwave burst forth with heart-shaking power, leveling everything in the surroundings to the ground.

The massive destructive force raged in the air, destroying the hillside and tearing the earth apart.

This scene was shocking, as if the apocalypse had truly descended upon the world.

Even within the distant Hidden Mist Village, the tremors could be clearly felt, which shows just how great the power of this move was.

Then, Deva Path Pain and Konan floated together above the ruins.

Looking at the ruins below, Konan asked suspiciously : « What should we do next ? »

« Use your Paper Clone to find a suitable Ninja in the Hidden Mist Village and let him receive the Three-Tails to become the Three-Tails Jinchuriki. When the Tailed Beast capture plan begins, we won’t need to worry about Three-Tails Isobu anymore. »
