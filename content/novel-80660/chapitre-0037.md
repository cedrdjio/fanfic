# Chapitre 37 — Intelligence Leaked, the Surprising Shimura Kazama!

Yamato even found Uchiha An a little more agreeable, though only just a little.

« Wood Release : Wood Clone ! »

As Yamato’s voice fell, a piece of wood shot out from the ground and began to coil together. Before long, a person identical to Yamato appeared.

« Get a good night’s rest tonight. Once we reach Takigakure, there won’t be any time for rest. »

After Yamato finished speaking, he walked toward the wooden hut, and the others immediately followed suit upon seeing this.

The night passed without incident.

At five o’clock the next morning, Yamato woke everyone up. It must be said that Yamato did a good job in this regard.

After eating a simple meal, the four of them resumed their journey in silence. Relying on their speed, they soon arrived at the outskirts of Takigakure.

The waterfall on the outskirts of Takigakure cascaded down like a silver ribbon, with water droplets splashing and shimmering in seven colors under the sunlight.

The high rock walls were covered in green vines, which coiled around the stones to form a natural barrier, separating Takigakure from the outside world.

« Infiltrate quietly. Do not engage until we have located the Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi ! »

Yamato then stepped back a few paces and used Wood Release again.

In an instant, a flight of stairs quickly emerged from the ground.

At first, the stairs looked unremarkable, but as time passed, they began to grow rapidly, extending upward at high speed.

Soon, the length of the stairs gradually approached the height of the waterfall, eventually hanging flush beside it.

It must be said that Danzo’s arrangements were quite reasonable ; Yamato’s Wood Release could not only suppress Tailed Beasts but also made it easier for the four of them to infiltrate Takigakure.

« Move out ! »

Upon Yamato’s command, the four of them immediately used the stairs to gain momentum and quickly landed above the waterfall.

The moment Uchiha An landed on the ground, his Observation Haki sensed the presence hidden in the shadows.

At the same time, Aburame Torune also sensed the current situation using the Aburame Clan’s secret techniques.

« Intelligence leaked. We are surrounded. »

As Aburame Torune’s voice fell, dozens of figures instantly leaped out from the surrounding forest and landed on the waterfall, surrounding Uchiha An and his group.

Then, a middle-aged man with slightly graying temples and a somewhat weathered look stepped out.

« I didn’t expect that there would really be Konoha Ninja trying to infiltrate Takigakure to seize the Seven-Tails. It seems the intelligence was true. »

As he spoke, the middle-aged man threw out a mask.

« The spies from your Konoha Root organization have also been exposed. That old dog Danzo’s scheme has failed. It would be better for you all to surrender early. »

Hearing the person in front of him also call Danzo an old dog, Uchiha An couldn’t help but laugh out loud.

Uchiha An’s laughter instantly eased the tense atmosphere, and both sides' attention was immediately drawn to him.

« Kid, what are you laughing at ? Being sent out on a mission at such a young age, you’re probably just cannon fodder. »

Uchiha An felt quite helpless about others calling him a kid ; who told his eight-year-old physique to be so deceptive.

However, Uchiha An did not answer his question, but instead drew the short sword from behind his back.

Yamato, Aburame Torune, and Shimura Kazama all assumed combat stances.

Suddenly, another middle-aged man walked out. His complexion was a pale shade, almost devoid of blood, as if he had lost all vitality.

« Leader Shechuan, he must be Konoha’s Thunder God Uchiha An. Leave him to me ! »

Upon hearing this, Leader Shechuan’s expression immediately became tense, and he looked at the figure beside him with concern.

« Zezhi, your injuries... »

« Let me contribute my strength to Takigakure one last time ! »

Leader Shechuan looked at this Seven-Tails Jinchuriki who had dedicated his life to Takigakure and nodded helplessly.

The atmosphere on the field instantly became tense again.

« Break out ! »

As Yamato’s voice fell, a Kunai suddenly stabbed directly at Uchiha An.

« Watch out ! »

Aburame Torune could only helplessly warn Uchiha An, as his body couldn’t react in time.

Everyone on the field was stunned by this scene ; they had not expected the Konoha Ninja to start infighting among themselves first.

« Clang ! »

Uchiha An reached his right hand behind his back and, without even turning his head, used the short blade in his hand to block Shimura Kazama’s Kunai.

« It was you who leaked our intelligence, wasn’t it ! »

Seeing that his long-prepared attack was easily blocked by Uchiha An, a ferocious look appeared on Shimura Kazama’s face.

« Hmph ! That old dog Danzo actually wanted to pass Root on to you, even though I am clearly his clansman brother. »

« With my strength, I should have been a Jonin already, but unfortunately, that old dog kept suppressing me. If you were to become the leader of Root, I would have no good days ahead of me. »

« As long as I kill you here today, Root will still be mine, and the head of the Shimura Clan will also be mine. »

At this moment, Shimura Kazama was like a complete madman, constantly venting his dissatisfaction.

Aburame Torune said angrily : « Is this your reason for betraying Konoha ? »

« As long as you all die here, I will still be a Special Jonin of Konoha when I return. »

Looking at the crazed Shimura Kazama before them, everyone felt nothing but disdain.

Unless you have the ability to kill everyone here, no Village will let a traitor go.

« Then I will clean up the house for Lord Danzo. »

Yamato delivered a fierce flying kick, and Shimura Kazama raised his arms to block it.

Taking advantage of the force of Yamato’s kick, Shimura Kazama’s figure soared upward.

« Did you think I would act without being sure of myself ? Now, I will make sure you all die without a burial place ! »

While roaring in the air, Shimura Kazama quickly formed hand seals.

« Multiple Mutually Multiplying Explosive Tags ! »

Everyone immediately became tense and began to use their own methods to defend.

« Wood Release : Wood Locking Wall ! »

Yamato instantly cast Wood Release to enclose Uchiha An and Aburame Torune within it.

« Boom ! Boom ! Boom ! »

Intense explosions rang out one after another. Inside the Wood Locking Wall, Uchiha An felt the surroundings shaking constantly, and the wood on the outside was crumbling.

Sensing the danger of the Wood Locking Wall collapsing, Uchiha An placed his right hand on the wall.

« Tenfold Weight Enhancement ! »

In an instant, the crumbling Wood Locking Wall stabilized. However, it made Yamato feel extremely strained ; after all, the Wood Locking Wall he had cast was on the water, and having its weight increased tenfold was quite uncomfortable.

When Yamato felt that the explosions had passed, he retracted his Wood Locking Wall.

Even though the explosions were over, the water on the river surface was still churning, constantly spreading outward.

Thick smoke spread in the air, permeating the entire scene.

Black smoke rose from the point of the explosion, merging with the sky and obscuring the sunlight.

Charred corpses were scattered on the river surface ; it seemed Takigakure had suffered significant losses.

The once clear and bright water surface had become murky, the flow of water disrupted by the massive impact of the explosion.

The vegetation on the riverbank was also affected by the impact ; some trees had fallen, and the soil had cracked, revealing scorched earth and rock fragments.
