# Chapitre 56 — Four Years Later!

In the blink of an eye, four years of time slipped quietly through the fingertips.

During this time, An experienced two completely different identities, sometimes as Uchiha An, and other times as Edward Newgate, busily shuttling through every corner of the Ninja World.

Under the arrangements of Danzo Shimura and Hiruzen Sarutobi, An quickly became the leader of Root and completely abolished the Cursed Seal of Hatred that Danzo Shimura had maintained.

In order to attract more talent, An continuously used low-level Devil Fruits to win people over, rapidly strengthening Root and recruiting many members.

At the same time, over these four years, An and Mei Terumī worked together to suppress the Ninja clans within the Hidden Mist Village.

They upheld an open and inclusive attitude, dedicating themselves to changing the status quo of the Hidden Mist Village.

Of course, An could not avoid conflicts with other Ninja Villages, especially his confrontation with the Three-Tails Jinchuriki Newgate, where he displayed astonishing strength and prestige.

Four years of time allowed An to constantly grow, playing a crucial role.

It was precisely An’s dual identity that enabled him to freely shuttle through every corner of the Ninja World, constantly striving for his goals.

During this period, An won over many supporters and also built a huge reputation for himself.

Four years passed like an arrow, and An’s footprints covered every corner of the Ninja World.

...

Inside the Mizukage’s office.

Having finished enjoying a massage, Mei Terumī leaned back in her chair in an extremely alluring and relaxed posture.

« Newgate, what do you think of the Hidden Mist Village now ? »

« Not bad at all ! »

Of course, nowadays, under An’s guidance, the development momentum of the Hidden Mist Village was exceptionally rapid.

Such a terrifying and grotesque environment naturally attracted a large number of tourists, so An developed some related entertainment activities, which added quite a bit of income to the Hidden Mist Village.

It could be said that in the past four years, An’s prestige in the Hidden Mist Village was no less than that of the Mizukage, Mei Terumī.

Having such good results made An’s significant investment of time and energy worthwhile ; he had spent most of the four years in the Hidden Mist Village, ensuring its prosperous development.

« I have seen everything you have done ; the Hidden Mist Village’s development today is inseparable from your efforts, but that is not what I want to talk about today. »

« Then just say it ! Is there any secret between us ? »

An’s tone was full of temptation, and Mei Terumī felt a little embarrassed upon hearing this. After gathering her thoughts, Mei Terumī spoke up.

« The Second Hokage of Konoha Village, Senju Tobirama, established various institutions and departments such as the Ninja Academy and Anbu, providing Konoha with a viable organizational structure. »

« Subsequently, major Ninja Villages vied to imitate this system, and it has continued to this day. »

« So, I want to be like Konoha and establish the position of Mizukage Assistant, and I hope you, Newgate, can take on this responsibility. »

An did not expect that before he had even reached « three years and another three years, » he would almost be the leader of the Hidden Mist Village’s organization after only four years.

However, his current work was already similar to that of a Mizukage Assistant, as Mei Terumī already handed many government affairs over to him to handle.

« The development of the Hidden Mist Village is already on the right track ; whether I take this position of Mizukage Assistant or not doesn’t make much of a difference. »

« Newgate, this is the first time you’ve refused me ! »

Mei Terumī's expression looked a bit unpleasant, clearly dissatisfied with An’s response.

As for An, seeing this, he smiled bitterly and shook his head ; he feared that the person in front of him had misunderstood his intention.

« Nowadays, an organization called Akatsuki in the Ninja World has gradually grown and developed, having a profound impact on the Ninja World. As a mercenary organization, they use inciting small-scale wars as a means, monopolizing war contracts in small countries at extremely low prices, and gradually expanding the scale of wars. Even worse, they are attempting to weaken the power of the Ninja systems of the great nations ; this series of actions is truly worrying for the entire Ninja World. »

« These guys even had operations to capture Tailed Beasts before. I am worried that they will pose a huge threat to the Ninja World, so for the coming time, I want to infiltrate the Akatsuki Organization and obtain their intelligence. »

An’s voice was impassioned and generous, unreservedly expressing his determination to protect the Ninja World.

An’s words stirred deep emotions within Mei Terumī, and her eyes gradually moistened.

This man in front of her bore too many responsibilities and missions for her and the entire Village, which made Mei Terumī feel incomparably heartbroken.

Of course, An did not know that Mei Terumī would overthink so much ; he could only say, woman, your imagination is too rich.

In reality, An was just too bored staying in the Hidden Mist Village, so he wanted to go out and have some fun, and incidentally, plan a thing or two for the future.

« Can’t someone else go ? You know the Hidden Mist Village cannot do without you, and I also cannot do without you anymore. »

Although Mei Terumī's voice was barely audible by the end of her sentence, only An and Mei Terumī were in the room, and An was certainly not deaf at this moment.

« You know I have a very special secret technique called Bone Shrinking Technique ; when the time comes, I can turn into someone else’s appearance to infiltrate the Akatsuki Organization, which is something others cannot do. » As he spoke, An’s tone directly became gentle.

« After solving the Akatsuki Organization, you just wait for me to come back and get mar... »

Mei Terumī immediately covered An’s mouth, « Mm ! I will wait for you to come back. »

Looking at the beautiful woman in front of him, An did not want to set any bad flags ; after all, whoever sets such a flag dies !

Next, An began to make arrangements and preparations before leaving, because infiltrating the Akatsuki Organization was definitely not a simple task that could be completed in a short time.

First, he arranged for Ao to continue to be responsible for the protection of the Hidden Mist Village, especially to closely monitor the movements of various Ninja clans ; he had to ensure the safety of the Village and prevent any internal or external threats.

Next, An directly arranged for his lackey Takayama Yume to temporarily take over all his affairs. He believed that Takayama Yume, strengthened by the Devil Fruit, had enough ability and wisdom to ensure that the Hidden Mist Village could still operate well after he left.

Finally, An decided to bid a final farewell to Mei Terumī. An knew this was the moment of their parting, but at least before leaving, he had to settle their relationship.

By the time all this was finished, a week had already passed.

During this week, it rained incessantly, adding a touch of melancholy to the whole farewell.

Also in this drizzly weather, An chose to leave the Hidden Mist Village.

Only Mei Terumī came to see him off ; no one else knew that An’s departure was a secret, a secret that only a few people in the Hidden Mist Village knew.

Of course, An knew that when he returned again, he would inevitably return to the Hidden Mist Village with a different identity and status ; he just didn’t know what choices the people of today would make when that moment arrived.
