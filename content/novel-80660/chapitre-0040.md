# Chapitre 40 — Refusing the Seven-Tails!

« Then let’s talk about it in a few years ! »

Before long, Yamato finished the recovery work of the Seven-Tails, and then the three of them brought back the corpses of Shimura Kazama and Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi, rushing back to Konoha Village without stopping.

The twilight gradually faded in the sky, and the three exhausted figures sprinted with all their might, finally returning to Konoha Village before nightfall.

« I will hand over the corpses of Shimura Kazama and Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi to the Intelligence Department first. You two go find Lord Danzo and report on this mission ! »

As soon as Aburame Torune finished speaking, his figure disappeared from An’s field of vision after a few rapid leaps.

An and Yamato had to drag their exhausted bodies and hurry towards the Root headquarters. After all, the matter of the Seven-Tails still needed to be dealt with, and An knew well that the sooner this was resolved, the better.

When An and Yamato hurried to the headquarters, Danzo Shimura was already waiting for them there quietly.

At this moment, An realized that all the information about this mission might have already been conveyed to Danzo by Yamato or Aburame Torune.

« Lord Danzo, mission accomplished ! »

Yamato said, then silently handed the scroll in his hand to Danzo Shimura.

Danzo Shimura took it without even glancing at it and immediately handed it to Fu Yamanaka, who was standing to the side.

In the silence, Danzo’s eyes revealed a hint of contemplation and guilt.

« Shimura Kazama’s betrayal caught me by surprise. This was my negligence. »

Listening to Danzo Shimura’s tone, it seemed to reveal a trace of self-reproach, which made An feel extremely surprised.

« However, you completed this mission well, especially An, who turned the tide. »

« The reward for completing the mission will be delivered to you tomorrow. Now, let’s discuss the ownership of the Seven-Tails ! »

Fu Yamanaka and Yamato were secretly surprised in their hearts. Since when was Lord Danzo so easy to talk to, discussing things with them ?

An was also a bit confused. Was he going to take over Danzo Shimura’s position and become the leader of Root and the darkness of the Ninja World ?

If this were true, it wouldn’t be unacceptable to An. After all, there was no conflict between him and Danzo.

Perhaps if An became the leader of Root, he could ease the conflict between the Uchiha Clan and the Shimura Clan, allowing An to better control the power of Konoha.

« Everything is subject to Lord Danzo’s arrangements. »

Fu Yamanaka and Yamato kicked the ball back, clearly not wanting to express their own opinions.

But An was secretly pondering. The arrangement for Seven-Tails Chomei was indeed a problem for Danzo Shimura.

This mission was carried out behind the back of the Fourth Hokage and other Ninja clans in Konoha. If they found out that Root had captured the Seven-Tails without telling anyone, Danzo Shimura would have a hard time explaining it.

Moreover, it was difficult to choose a Jinchuriki for the Seven-Tails. First, it couldn’t be someone who often appeared in public, otherwise the existence of the Seven-Tails would be exposed.

Secondly, the Seven-Tails Jinchuriki needed to have sufficient strength ; otherwise, it would be ridiculous if they were easily kidnapped and the Seven-Tails was taken away.

Finally, the Seven-Tails Jinchuriki needed to be able to communicate with the Tailed Beast ; otherwise, they couldn’t unleash the full power of the Tailed Beast.

« Lord Danzo, I think the most suitable candidate for the Seven-Tails Jinchuriki is Yamato. »

The sudden proposal made everyone present fall into a brief state of astonishment. They didn’t expect An to actually hope that Yamato would master the power of Seven-Tails Chomei.

Even Yamato, who was standing on the side, felt surprised by An’s decision, a flash of doubt in his eyes.

Facing this unexpected suggestion, Danzo Shimura frowned slightly, watching An with suspicion.

« An, why can’t the Seven-Tails Jinchuriki be you ? If you had the power of the Seven-Tails, I’m afraid no one would be your match. »

Hearing Danzo Shimura’s tempting words, An shook his head with a wry smile.

« The power of the Tailed Beast is indeed incomparably powerful. They have their own specialized attacks and possess staggering combat capabilities. »

« I am also quite curious about this powerful force of the Tailed Beast, but the power of the Seven-Tails Chomei is too weak ; I don’t really think much of it. »

A rare smile appeared on Danzo Shimura’s face. It was unknown what he was thinking about An’s answer.

« Hmm ! You all may leave ! »

Everyone knew that Danzo Shimura had his own arrangements, so they left the headquarters one after another.

Returning home, An lay on the bed without disturbing anyone. After all, it was a rare opportunity to get a super-guaranteed draw.

« System, start the draw ! »

After the familiar process was completed, the indicator stopped again.

[Ding ! Congratulations to the host for obtaining the Logia Ice-Ice Fruit !]

An didn’t expect that he had almost collected the Devil Fruits of the three Navy Admirals. He had previously obtained a super-guaranteed draw opportunity while performing a mission, and as a result, he obtained the Logia Magma-Magma Fruit, and this time he drew the Logia Ice-Ice Fruit again.

Originally, An was prepared to give the Magma-Magma Fruit to his stupid little brother. Now it seems that giving the Ice-Ice Fruit to Naruto is also quite suitable.

Let Naruto and Sasuke, Indra and Ashura continue to love and kill each other !

After some rambling thoughts, An fell into a deep sleep on his bed.

...

The next day.

Goose-feather-like heavy snow fell in the sky. Konoha Village had been covered by white snow, like a silver-clad fairyland.

An woke up from a sweet dream and strolled on the streets of Konoha in a pleasant mood, admiring the magnificent scenery after the snow.

The villagers on the street were busy clearing the snow, and the shops were filled with colorful goods, emitting an alluring aroma.

The children were excitedly engaged in their games...

Well ! An had misunderstood. It was a group of naughty brats bullying a little girl, mischievously throwing snowballs and cornering her.

Seeing this scene, a surge of anger rose in An’s heart.

So, An rushed over without hesitation and stood in front of the little girl, casting his figure into a protective shadow.

An stared at those brats, a ruthless light flashing in his eyes.

« Punks ! »

Then An kicked the brats to the ground one by one.

« Damn it ! My brother is a Ninja, how dare you hit me ! »

« Waaah ! I’m still just a child ! How can you be so heartless ! »

For this group of brats, An didn’t want to say a word more and directly grabbed them and threw them away.

Realizing that An was not someone to be trifled with, the few brats immediately ran away on their short legs.

An reached out his hand, gently stroking the little girl’s hair, and looked at her with a smile.

« Hinata, are you okay ? As the eldest lady of the Hyuga Clan, you must be strong ! »

« Big brother, thank you for helping me. Do you know me ? Are you from the Uchiha Clan ? »

Hinata gently wiped the tears from the corners of her eyes, gazing at the handsome big brother in front of her in a daze.

Looking at the tall and upright figure in front of her, Hinata felt an inexplicable warmth and sense of security.
