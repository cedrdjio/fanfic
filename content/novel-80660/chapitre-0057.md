# Chapitre 57 — Arrival at Hidden Rain Village!

After leaving the Hidden Mist Village, An reverted to his true appearance and sped off.

Over the next four years, for various reasons, An could only rely on his Multiple Shadow Clone Jutsu clones to train. Although his progress was not slow, it was still unsatisfactory for him.

If it weren’t for the massive Chakra support provided by the Three-Tails Isobu, An felt he wouldn’t have been able to use the Multiple Shadow Clone Jutsu so frequently.

When An completely left the territory of the Land of Water, he transformed into Newgate again, ready to continue his journey.

Meanwhile, the Ninja World was abuzz with a piece of news.

It turned out that the Three-Tails Jinchuriki of the Hidden Mist Village had defected, fighting his way out from within the Land of Water and passing through numerous small nations like the Land of Hot Springs and the Land of Fields.

Along the way, many Ninjas interested in the Three-Tails chose to attack, but unfortunately, these greedy fellows were all easily dealt with by An.

Especially Takigakure, who had lost their Seven-Tails, hungered to regain the power of a Tailed Beast, and thus they gathered most of the Village’s strength to launch a sneak attack on An.

However, the outcome was unexpected by everyone ; An once again counter-killed the people from Takigakure, slaughtering them one by one.

During this period, An’s strength had improved significantly. Even without using his fruit abilities, An was qualified to be called a true powerhouse.

An’s strength also attracted the attention of the entire Ninja World. After the news spread, many Ninjas developed a keen interest in him.

People speculated about just how strong An was, and some even wanted to challenge him.

An didn’t care about any of this, because by now, he had arrived at the Hidden Rain Village.

...

The Hidden Rain Village seemed to be in an eternal rainy season, with rain flowing ceaselessly into the inland lake surrounding the Village.

Furthermore, because the Village was located at the intersection of the Land of Earth, Konoha, and the Land of Wind, it was an area where wars frequently occurred. This unique geographical location allowed the Ninjas of the Hidden Rain Village to train their abilities to the maximum extent.

Therefore, the Hidden Rain Village was famous for its excellent assassination techniques, often accepting commissions from other countries to carry out various types of assassination missions.

The Village’s architecture was primarily made of steel, with a considerable portion also using stone. Many steel pipes of various sizes were used to construct a unique landscape, which was closely related to the rainfall in the Hidden Rain Village.

Huge pipes and sewers covered the inside and outside of the Village, providing a necessary drainage system. These characteristic buildings made of steel and stone complemented the unique climate of the Hidden Rain Village.

The moment An’s figure stepped into the Hidden Rain Village, the sky was still drizzling.

When An entered the Hidden Rain Village, the moment he got wet was the moment he was exposed.

I’m afraid that by now, Konan already knew the news of his arrival in the Hidden Rain Village, or perhaps she was already discussing with Nagato how to deal with him.

Meanwhile.

Above the high tower in the center of the Hidden Rain Village.

« Pain, a strange guy has come here. »

Konan’s voice carried a cold clarity. She stood aloof on the high tower, overlooking the Hidden Rain Village like a true angel.

As for the Deva Path Pain, he stood expressionless to the side, also overlooking the rainy Hidden Rain Village.

« Is it another spy from another Ninja Village ? Then I’ll leave it to you to handle ! Konan. »

« This time, it’s not a character I can easily deal with. I’m afraid you’ll need to take action. »

Konan’s tone remained very cold, but it carried a hint of solemnity.

Of course, this was also the awkward situation facing the Akatsuki Organization. Although there were many strong individuals, they were split into teams of two, which dispersed their power, and most of the time, the organization members were not together.

Now, faced with the arrival of a strong enemy, they had no choice but to helplessly deploy the Six Paths of Pain.

« Who is it ? »

At this moment, Nagato also felt a hint of curiosity. After all, a character who could make Konan feel troubled must be a famous powerhouse in the Ninja World.

« A rogue Ninja from the Hidden Mist Village, the Three-Tails Jinchuriki Edward Newgate, an ignorant person who claims to be the world’s strongest man. »

As Konan’s voice fell, the Deva Path Pain’s expression changed, albeit rarely.

« The world’s strongest ? How does that compare to a god ? »

« Mortals cannot be mentioned in the same breath as gods. »

In an instant, the figures of Konan and the Deva Path Pain disappeared directly, and the next second they appeared on the roof of the building below.

Meanwhile, An did not know about the neurotic conversation between Konan and Nagato ; instead, he was sitting in an ordinary ramen shop eating.

However, An’s three-meter-tall figure was simply too conspicuous, and a vacuum zone formed directly around him.

« Customer, your special bamboo shoot tonkotsu ramen is ready. Please let me know if you need any other service. »

The boss felt a sense of familiarity when he saw An’s massive build, because the boss was also a burly man who was 2.3 meters tall.

An, however, felt that this bowl of bamboo shoot tonkotsu ramen was quite extraordinary, as one could immediately smell the rich aroma.

First, what caught the eye were the fresh, tender bamboo shoots, bright green in color, sliced thin, and floating in the soup, giving one a refreshing feeling.

The ramen broth was based on delicate pork bones, which had been boiled for a long time, slowly releasing a rich flavor.

The ramen noodles were firm and chewy, with a springy sensation when bitten. Each noodle had been steamed by the heat, sliding into the mouth with the soup, making one feel incomparably satisfied.

« Boss, with such great culinary skills, why not make the shop bigger ? You could earn more money that way. »

« Sigh ! Under the protection of the Angel and God, our Hidden Rain Village has basically stayed away from war, but the climate and environment of the Hidden Rain Village don’t attract many tourists, so business can only be like this. »

Speaking of this, the boss’s tone lowered. After all, in the Hidden Rain Village, a land of constant war, living in peace was already quite an achievement ; how much money one earned was secondary.

An moved his chair closer to the boss and said in a low voice.

« Then, boss, have you considered moving out of the Hidden Rain Village ? I heard that the Hidden Mist Village is developing very well now and is relatively friendly to outsiders. » As he spoke, An’s tone became even deeper, « I feel that the atmosphere in the Hidden Rain Village is very oppressive ; I’m afraid you all are living quite painfully ! »

In an instant, the boss recoiled and looked at An as if he were a madman.

Suddenly, a fragrant breeze blew by, and a beautiful figure appeared in the scene.

« Rogue Ninja of the Hidden Mist Village, Newgate, are you questioning the policies of the Hidden Rain Village ? »

Konan, with a fierce tone, rarely had a hint of anger on her face, which added a touch of cuteness.

However, Konan’s appearance excited the people of the Hidden Rain Village, and they all knelt on the ground one after another.

« Greetings, Lord Angel ! »

« I didn’t expect Lord Angel to actually appear before us. »

« Lord Angel ! Thank you ! »
