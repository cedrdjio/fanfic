# Chapitre 5 — Cloud Ninja Attack!

An and Itachi followed Sarutobi Kazekaze through many twists and turns. Just as An was about to lose patience, the three finally arrived at a cave.

Dozens of children were already waiting inside ; it could be said that this place held the core members of every ninja clan.

No wonder Hiruzen Sarutobi had sent members of the Sarutobi Clan to protect An and the others. It seemed there was a lot of planning involved ; those who knew, knew.

« For your safety, Master Jiraiya has dispatched me and a team of Chunin to protect you all. You will stay here for the time being ! »

Itachi, seeing this, asked suspiciously : « This is a cave. If enemies come, how are we children supposed to retreat ? Is there an exit on the other side ? »

Sarutobi Kazekaze looked at Itachi approvingly.

As expected of the Uchiha Clan leader’s son, he was very sharp and had noticed the flaw.

But when Sarutobi Kazekaze saw An picking his nose, he felt nothing but disdain for him deep down.

« Something like that ! If there is any emergency, we will arrange everything. »

After An and Itachi entered the cave, they discovered it was quite spacious inside, with small, mini tents laid out one after another. It seemed this was where the brothers would be living.

« Big Sister Hana ! Long time no see ! »

An said, sticking right up to her. Inuzuka Hana saw this and naturally hugged An.

Itachi, watching his older brother take advantage of Inuzuka Hana again, felt embarrassed and silently retreated to find the tent belonging to the two brothers.

An lived in the cave from then on, and he could only learn bits and pieces of news about the outside world from Sarutobi Kazekaze.

The Third Raikage had fallen in battle.

Iwagakure of the Land of Earth suffered heavy casualties.

The Seven Ninja Swordsmen of the Hidden Mist Village were kicked into a sorry state by Maito Duy.

An knew that the war would probably end soon.

In the days that followed, An got closer to the many other kids. It could be said that once these people grew up, they would be a force to be reckoned with.

Unfortunately, An was too young, a fact that was often teased by the others.

After all, only the Uchiha Clan would send two four-year-old brats to the battlefield ; everyone else was more or less older than An and Itachi.

However, when everyone learned that Itachi had already mastered the Three Basic Techniques and An had mastered the Clone Technique and Transformation Technique, no one dared to look down on the brothers.

One week later.

An, Itachi, and the group were cooking. There was no one specifically assigned to cook for the children at this time.

Suddenly, a figure flashed in quickly, wearing a Konoha forehead protector, but covered in bloodstains.

« Lord Yama Nara, why are you here ? »

The person who arrived was Chunin Yama Nara, one of Sarutobi Kazekaze’s subordinates.

« A group of Cloud Ninja seem to have discovered this place. I will now use Ninjutsu to open the back door of the cave. You all must leave immediately and return to headquarters to notify Master Jiraiya. »

With that, Chunin Yama Nara began to form hand seals.

« Earth Release : Earth Turning Technique ! »

Instantly, the ground inside the cave rolled like waves, and a two-foot-square passage appeared in the depths of the cave.

After doing this, Chunin Yama Nara turned and left, presumably to support Sarutobi Kazekaze.

« What should we do ? »

Four-year-old Itachi was a bit panicked ; after all, this was Itachi’s first close encounter with war.

An exerted force with his right hand, pulling Itachi to his side in one go.

« Itachi, you take them and run toward the headquarters. Keep your guard up along the way. »

« Brother, what about you ? »

Seeing this, An gave Itachi a reassuring look.

« Remember, I have plenty of special tricks up my sleeve. You take care of yourself too. »

Soon, under the command of An and Itachi, the many brats quickly evacuated the cave, leaving An alone in the end.

An immediately chased out of the cave, following Chunin Yama Nara’s blood trail toward the battlefield.

At this moment, An finally went all out, using his greatest speed without reservation.

Lightning flashed in the forest, and a small figure instantly appeared tens of meters away, moving through the forest at extreme speed.

Meanwhile.

On the distant battlefield.

Sarutobi Kazekaze clutched the Kunai in his hand tightly and wiped the blood from his face.

Of the three Chunin under his command, besides Chunin Yama Nara who had already left, he was the only one left alive. Moreover, Sarutobi Kazekaze knew he couldn’t hold on for long ; the injuries on his body and his nearly depleted Chakra were telling Sarutobi Kazekaze that he couldn’t keep fighting.

« I didn’t expect to run into a Konoha Jonin like Sarutobi Kazekaze on the way to retreat, and with four of you guarding this place, there must be something important here. »

Sarutobi Kazekaze did not reply upon hearing this ; he couldn’t reveal his position at this time.

The attacking Cloud Ninja was Jonin Toroi, famous for his Magnet Release. His two subordinates had both died at this guy’s hands. All Sarutobi Kazekaze could do now was stall for time.

Toroi, seeing that Sarutobi Kazekaze did not respond to him, turned his head to look at his companions behind him.

« You guys scout the surrounding area, try to find this Sarutobi Kazekaze, or figure out what Konoha sent people here to do ! »

« Yes ! »

The six Chunin who received the order immediately scattered.

In an instant, a Kunai with an explosive tag stuck directly into a Cloud Ninja Village Chunin, and the others scattered upon seeing this.

Boom !

After the explosion, the Cloud Ninja Village Chunin fell directly to the ground, his aura slowly fading.

Chunin Yama Nara saw that his attack was effective ; killing an enemy Chunin was already beyond his expectations.

Just as Chunin Yama Nara relaxed, a huge square Kunai pierced through his chest.

Bang !

Watching Chunin Yama Nara’s corpse fall from the tree, Jonin Toroi curled his lips in disdain.

« No one can dodge my Magnet Release. » After finishing, Jonin Toroi looked angrily at the five remaining Chunin behind him. « I don’t want to see any more casualties. Kill Sarutobi Kazekaze and retreat immediately. »

Then, the six from Cloud Ninja Village began to search the surroundings. As the six people’s search range grew wider, Sarutobi Kazekaze knew that being discovered was only a matter of time.

At this moment, Sarutobi Kazekaze only hoped that the children of the various ninja clans would return to headquarters safely as soon as possible ; only then would he have a glimmer of hope for survival.

Suddenly, a short figure appeared in Sarutobi Kazekaze’s eyes, making the already desperate Sarutobi Kazekaze even more desperate.

« Long time no see ! Lord Kazekaze ! »

An greeted Sarutobi Kazekaze with a face full of smiles, while Sarutobi Kazekaze felt like choking An to death right then and there.

« You bastard kid, why are you here ! Get out of here quickly ! »

« I came to support you, have a better attitude. »

Sarutobi Kazekaze felt as uncomfortable as if he had eaten a fly. Are you, a four-year-old brat, sure you aren’t here to feed the enemy a kill ?

Just as An was talking to Sarutobi Kazekaze, dozens of shuriken covered the sky and came down.

Sarutobi Kazekaze immediately hugged An and flashed to a tree on the side.
