# Chapitre 71 — Returning to the Hidden Mist Village!

Tayuya saw this scene and just shook her head and sighed helplessly ; another guy had been conned.

As for Kisame Hoshigaki, he had a sense of déjà vu ; this was just like his former self !

« It was my shallowness, it was my recklessness. Thank you for the reminder, Lord Newgate, for letting me know who the true enemy of the Village is. »

« Since Lord Newgate already has his own plans, I will listen to everything Lord Newgate says. I only hope that you will lead us to defeat Konoha when the time comes. »

Watching Utakata, who had been conned by him, Newgate wore an expression of « he is a promising student. »

Thus, for the rest of the journey, the group of three became four, and Newgate also had a rare experience of Utakata’s Bubble Jutsu.

The four of them sat in the bubbles like this, heading towards the Hidden Mist Village in an extremely bizarre posture.

...

When the four of them arrived at the Hidden Mist Village, the entire Village was already shrouded in a tense atmosphere.

Newgate stood on the hillside, overlooking the Hidden Mist Village. His eyes flashed with desire. After all, what man wouldn’t want to achieve unification like the First Emperor ? And what he was doing now was precisely such a great achievement.

Of course, part of the desire was also for Mei Terumī, as the two had not seen each other for a long time.

Kisame Hoshigaki and Tayuya stood firmly behind Newgate, both silently watching ahead, without showing any hesitation or fear.

Utakata, on the other hand, had a look of longing, firmly believing that under Newgate’s command, the Hidden Mist Village would defeat any invading enemy and would certainly achieve final victory.

« Let’s go ! »

With that, Newgate walked towards the Hidden Mist Village, and Kisame Hoshigaki and Tayuya immediately followed.

Utakata was a bit hesitant when he saw this, as his identity as a rogue Ninja still existed. However, after glancing at Newgate striding forward ahead, Utakata regained his confidence.

Just as Newgate’s group entered the Hidden Mist Village, they were greeted by waves of cheers.

« Lord Newgate is back ! »

« Has Lord Newgate finished his mission ? I knew you wouldn’t betray the Village ! »

« Welcome home, Lord Newgate ! »

It could be said that the reputation Newgate had accumulated over the years reached its peak at this moment ; the grand occasion was even more enthusiastic than when Mei Terumī succeeded as Mizukage.

Of course, Utakata, who was mixed in the group of four, was also discovered, but what greeted Utakata was not cheers.

« Hey ! Isn’t that the guy who killed his master ? What right does he have to walk with Lord Newgate ! »

« That’s right, scum like this should get out of the Hidden Mist Village ! »

« We firmly oppose such a Ninja as Utakata returning to the Village ! »

The public sentiment of the Hidden Mist Village was ignited at this moment, and everyone condemned Utakata.

Seeing this, Utakata was somewhat helpless and could only silently endure it, enduring the verbal abuse from the people he wanted to protect.

In an instant, an incredibly powerful aura covered the entire scene, and the whole street fell silent. If it weren’t for Newgate fearing it would be too shocking, he probably would have unleashed his full Haoshoku Haki, and ninety percent of the people on this street would have fainted.

After a while, everyone came back to their senses, and only then did Newgate begin to explain.

« Dear villagers, in fact, this matter is not the fault of the Six-Tails Jinchuriki, Utakata ; the fault lies with everyone. »

« Because in order to protect everyone, Utakata was chosen to become the Jinchuriki, and it is a very arduous and painful thing for a Jinchuriki to suppress a Tailed Beast. »

« And Utakata’s master wanted to extract the Six-Tails from Utakata’s body, but unfortunately, he was accidentally killed by the suffering Utakata. »

« In fact, this is our fault. It was us who made a teenager who should have been happy become a Jinchuriki, causing this teenager to betray the Village because of a misunderstanding and wander in the Ninja World. »

As Newgate’s voice fell, the whole scene fell silent again.

The more intense the crowd’s words were just now, the more embarrassed they were now.

« Well said, and correctly said. As the Fifth Mizukage, I apologize to Utakata first ; this is also my dereliction of duty ! »

Mei Terumī's figure appeared on the scene in real-time, and she took the lead in apologizing to Utakata.

Seeing this, the other villagers immediately echoed, and whether they were sincere or not, everyone was apologizing to Utakata at this moment.

Watching this scene, Utakata’s eyes welled up with tears. It was this man in front of him, whom he had only known for a few days, who changed him and allowed him to return to the Village.

As for Kisame Hoshigaki, no one cared that he was a rogue Ninja. After all, this guy just killed his leader, and furthermore, he was the one who was betrayed by his leader first.

...

After a round of cheers, Newgate’s group arrived at the conference room of the Mizukage building.

However, there were no representatives from the various Ninja clans at this meeting. After all, in these few years, Newgate alone had suppressed the Ninja clans of the Hidden Mist Village so much that they couldn’t lift their heads.

So this meeting was only a small meeting of six people : Newgate, Kisame Hoshigaki, Utakata, Mei Terumī, Ao, and Hozuki Lanyue.

« You’re finally back. You don’t know how busy I’ve been during this time. »

« I’m sorry, I came back late and left you to face so much alone. »

Just when Newgate and Mei Terumī were about to say something more, Ao finally couldn’t watch anymore.

« Ahem... We are having a meeting here ; let’s talk about the important things first ! »

Seeing this, Hozuki Lanyue immediately echoed : « Yes, yes, yes, let’s talk about the important intelligence first ! »

Seeing the situation, Newgate couldn’t say anything more and had to continue his « conning technique. »

« This time I went undercover in the Akatsuki Organization and learned important intelligence. The existence of the Akatsuki Organization is to seize the nine Tailed Beasts, and then use the nine Tailed Beasts to destroy the world, letting the world feel pain, so that everyone will cherish the hard-won peace. »

After hearing Newgate’s words, everyone except Kisame Hoshigaki had a look of surprise.

Destroying the world through Tailed Beast weapons, and then letting the survivors feel pain and cherish peace—this idea was truly wonderful.

However, Ao couldn’t care about so much for the time being ; the information about Konoha was more important right now.

« This information is secondary. The important thing is that Konoha has taken the initiative to start a war and wiped out the Land of Wind and Sunagakure in one fell swoop. I’m afraid Konoha will make a move against us next. »

Ao sighed as he spoke. After all, Konoha, which could easily destroy Sunagakure, must have powerful strength. Even if the Hidden Mist Village could defeat Konoha, it might be annexed by other Ninja Villages.

However, the return of the two Jinchuriki now gave Ao enough sense of security.

« Regardless of whether Konoha’s next target is us, the Hidden Mist Village must prepare countermeasures. »

« Of course, we cannot relax our defense against other Ninja Villages, especially the Hidden Cloud Village, which is close to us. »

Mei Terumī's words could be said to have directly set the tone for the content of this meeting, and even provided guidance for the next actions.

The Hidden Mist Village must not only prevent Konoha’s sneak attacks but also guard against the Hidden Cloud Village in the north.
