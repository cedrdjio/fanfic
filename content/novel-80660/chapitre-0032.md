# Chapitre 32 — A Crazy Explosion, Shisui Eats a Devil Fruit!

After eating, An immediately bathed and burned incense, then returned to his room and lay on the bed.

With two super guaranteed draw chances and one ordinary draw chance, An was really looking forward to what would happen next.

« System, give me one ordinary draw. »

He would use one ordinary draw to test the waters, and if the Devil Fruit he pulled was decent, An would use the two super guaranteed chances as well.

It was still the familiar environment and familiar feeling, and the large pointer soon stopped on a Devil Fruit.

[Ding ! Congratulations to the host for obtaining the Paramecia-type Imitation-Imitation Fruit.]

An’s expression immediately improved significantly ; the user of the Paramecia-type Imitation-Imitation Fruit was the real man, Bon-chan.

Although the fruit’s ability did not improve combat power, the ability itself was extremely powerful.

Activating the fruit’s ability could turn the user’s appearance into anyone from their memory ; the right hand was used to change the appearance, and the left hand was used to restore the original form.

Even though the Transformation Technique existed in the Ninja World, unfortunately, for the strong, the Transformation Technique could be seen through at a glance.

In addition, the Imitation-Imitation Fruit also had the function of storing memories ; as long as it was an object that had been imitated in the past, it could be imitated again, which could be said to be a very tricky ability, something that the Transformation Technique could not compare to.

« System, can I eat a second Devil Fruit ? »

In One Piece, Blackbeard was a dual or triple fruit ability user, and An believed that since he had a system, he would definitely not be worse than Blackbeard.

[Ding ! In this world, only the host is qualified to consume multiple Devil Fruits.]

Hearing the system’s explanation, An felt a bit regretful but also relieved.

He could eat multiple Devil Fruits, but unfortunately, others could still only eat one.

Composing his mindset, An began the super guaranteed draw.

As the pointer kept spinning, An’s heart became more and more nervous ; after all, even a super guaranteed draw could yield a trashy Devil Fruit.

The moment the pointer stopped, An’s heart was completely put at ease.

[Ding ! Congratulations to the host for obtaining the Paramecia-type More-More Fruit !]

The user of the More-More Fruit in One Piece was Byrnndi World, but unfortunately, this guy was a bit lackluster and didn’t deserve such a powerful fruit.

The More-More Fruit’s ability allowed the speed, power, volume, and weight of objects he touched to be magnified by multiples, up to 100 times.

Unfortunately, it could not be applied to living creatures other than himself, which was a bit imperfect.

An decided to eat the Imitation-Imitation Fruit and the More-More Fruit ; once he had developed all three Devil Fruits, that would be when he would be invincible in the Ninja World.

Unable to suppress his excitement, An began the super guaranteed draw again.

[Ding ! Congratulations to the host for obtaining the Zoan-type Mythical Zoan Bat-Bat Fruit, Vampire Model !]

« System, you’re incredible ! »

The user of the Mythical Zoan Bat-Bat Fruit, Vampire Model was Patrick Redfield ; this guy was a one-man army, and it could be said he was ridiculously strong.

The Mythical Zoan Bat-Bat Fruit, Vampire Model first provided an all-around improvement to physical fitness, and through the bat’s abilities, it enhanced his Observation Haki.

More importantly, the Bat-Bat Fruit, Vampire Model could accelerate the aging of opponents by consuming their blood, while at the same time restoring his own youth ; in some respects, this was already immortality.

The system materialized the Imitation-Imitation Fruit and the More-More Fruit, and An couldn’t wait to eat them both.

Of course, the most fun one was still the Imitation-Imitation Fruit, which could imitate everything, even body shape and such, with 100% accuracy.

This reminded An of the teachers he watched in his previous life : Teacher Asuka, Teacher Yoshizawa, Teacher Fukada...

Nothing happened overnight.

The next morning, An climbed out of bed, exhausted.

After a simple wash, An brought Itachi to the place where the three of them trained, and saw that Shisui was already waiting there.

« It’s rare to see you two late together. »

An threw the Devil Fruit in his hand directly over.

Shisui curiously took it and examined it, but after looking for a long time, he only thought it was unremarkable.

« This is a gift from Brother to you ; it is the kind of power that we wield. »

Listening to Itachi’s explanation, Shisui also knew what the fruit in his hand represented.

The three of them had been training together for a year, and Shisui knew about the special nature of Itachi’s power and An’s power.

However, Shisui hadn’t asked about all this, so he only knew a general idea.

« The fruit you ate is the legendary Devil Fruit. My Devil Fruit is a Logia-type, Itachi’s Devil Fruit is a Paramecia-type, and as for the Devil Fruit in your hand, it is a Mythical Zoan Bat-Bat Fruit. »

« After eating the Devil Fruit, your physical fitness will be improved in all aspects, and your Observation Haki will also improve, but your appearance after transforming will be a bit strange. »

« Of course, if you mind, you can also choose not to eat it. »

An’s words were deafening ; Shisui had never thought that there was such a magical Devil Fruit in the world, and he had never heard of it.

Then Shisui looked at An and Itachi firmly and made a gesture to take a bite.

« You must eat it all to gain the full power ! »

Itachi said, the corners of his mouth lifting involuntarily ; it was obvious he was looking forward to what would happen next.

An didn’t expect his foolish little brother to play a prank, but An didn’t expose him ; after all, everyone had to go through this.

So Shisui bit down without hesitation. In an instant, Shisui’s complexion sank, and his handsome and cool demeanor instantly vanished.

However, Shisui still endured the unswallowable taste in his mouth and swallowed the entire Devil Fruit in a few bites.

« How do you feel ? »

Following Itachi’s question, Shisui’s figure immediately began to change.

Seeing this, two furry ears grew directly on Shisui’s head, his body suddenly grew to two meters tall, and a pair of leathery wings grew from Shisui’s back, looking very evil and terrifying.

« Click ! »

The sound of an instant photo caught everyone’s attention ; of course, the only one who could do such a thing was An.

« An, how do I change back ? »

Although Shisui wanted to gain powerful strength, if he had to keep this appearance forever, Shisui would rather die !

« Zoan-type Devil Fruits grant people the power of animals, so Zoan-type Devil Fruit users have three forms : Human form, Beast form, and Hybrid form. »

« Human form, this is the most basic form. It is also your usual form, which belongs to the Human form. Although it is the Human form, it has the powerful physical ability granted by the Zoan-type. »

« Hybrid form, this is a form that combines the flexibility of the Human form and the wildness of the Beast form. It can be said to be the strongest form of a Zoan-type Devil Fruit. »

« Beast form is your current form, possessing all the abilities of the beast ! Of course, if you want to change back to Human form, then master it yourself ! »

After speaking, An and Itachi began their own training, while Shisui pondered his own abilities.
