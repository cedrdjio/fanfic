# Chapitre 39 — The Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Defeated!

« Hmph ! Even if you die, I will still be captured by them, and I will still have no freedom. Since that’s the case, I’ll go crazy with you one last time. »

Seven-Tails Chomei chose to directly unleash its power, supporting its Jinchuriki, Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi, with everything it had.

As Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi accepted the power of the Seven-Tails, the Tailed Beast Cloak on his body solidified even further.

« Let me handle this ! »

In an instant, Yamato flashed to stand in front of An, his hands moving rapidly to weave signs.

« Wood Release : Tree Bind Eternal Burial ! »

Suddenly, a towering tree erupted from the ground, rapidly wrapping its massive body around Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi, tightly binding him within.

Aburame Torune seized this opportunity and acted decisively, finding the perfect moment to strike.

« Secret Technique : Insect Bite ! »

In an instant, a swarm of insects gathered, forming a massive cluster that rapidly enveloped Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi like a cloud of smoke.

« Argh ! »

Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi let out a heaven-shaking roar, deciding to activate his Tailed Beast transformation immediately.

His body instantly transformed into the massive image of the Seven-Tails, fierce and majestic.

This behemoth easily broke through Yamato’s restraints, sweeping past like a storm, unstoppable.

Aburame Torune’s attack seemed like a mere ripple, completely ineffective against Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi’s solid defense.

« An, it’s all up to you now. »

At this moment, Aburame Torune and Yamato felt the massive drain on their Chakra from the previous battle, having exhausted the strength needed to contend with Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi.

Their Chakra was depleted, causing their Ninjutsu to weaken to the point where they could no longer unleash any effective attacks.

« Leave it to me ! »

An agreed without changing his tone, standing solemnly in the center of the battlefield. His body radiated intense electrical currents, and his eyes stared resolutely and sharply at the massive Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi.

Fully transformed, Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi was covered from head to toe in hard plating, looking incredibly fierce. His seven tails swung like sharp blades, carrying destructive power.

Suddenly, An struck, letting out a loud shout.

« Lightning Release : 100 Million Volts - Dragon God - 50x Amplification ! »

A massive bolt of lightning erupted from An’s body, and a world-destroying evil dragon appeared before everyone’s eyes, rushing toward Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi at a speed invisible to the naked eye.

Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi did not show weakness either ; after a roar, Chakra continuously gathered in his mouth.

« Tailed Beast Ball ! »

In an instant, the lightning and the sphere collided, emitting a piercing explosion. The battlefield was filled with violent energy and intense light.

A massive wave of energy surged across the battlefield, and an atmosphere of despair spread out, like a ruthless black hole sweeping through everything in its path.

The massive energy wave spread at an astonishing speed, quickly enveloping the entire battlefield.

The surrounding trees and boulders were completely pulverized into dust, and even the nearby river was severed. Under the ravages of the energy wave, it fell silent for a while before the water finally filled the riverbed again.

« Today, I’ll test the power of the Lightning Dragon technique. »

After saying this, An rushed forward, with Observation Haki and Armament Haki fully activated. A real man chooses to use physical strength to solo the Seven-Tails.

Seeing this, Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi was so angry he could explode ; this guy was looking down on the power of a Tailed Beast too much.

« Arrogant ! »

Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi also chose to shoot forward. The two powerhouses launched an intense duel on the barren clearing, moving, dodging, and attacking rapidly.

Lightning and flames intertwined wildly in the space, the explosions sounding like a continuous roar, and the entire battlefield was once again shrouded in thick smoke and energy fluctuations.

« Bang ! »

Suddenly, a figure was sent flying backward, slamming heavily into the ground.

Aburame Torune and Yamato’s hearts tightened instantly, but they relaxed after seeing clearly who the figure on the ground was.

« You were already heavily injured, and now your body is at its limit. Don’t make any more meaningless resistance. »

« Hmph ! Even if I die, I will eliminate you as a threat to Takigakure. »

Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi silently took out a small bottle, which Leader Shechuan had once left for him.

Then, Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi opened the cap and drank the liquid inside without hesitation.

Perhaps sensing Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi’s determination, Seven-Tails Chomei immediately supported him with all its might, continuously injecting its Chakra into his body.

At this moment, Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi and Seven-Tails Chomei had both exhausted all their strength, using up every bit of power within their bodies. In particular, Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi directly stimulated the deepest Chakra within him, releasing it all at once.

Man and beast staked all their Chakra on this final attack, with all the energy gathering in their mouths.

« 10x Super Tailed Beast Ball ! »

In an instant, a Tailed Beast Ball ten times larger than the previous one appeared before An.

As for Aburame Torune and Yamato, they dared not watch the battle any longer and immediately fled into the distance. Now, the battle depended solely on An, this eight-year-old boy.

An also struck without hesitation, immediately releasing all his power, going all out to face this decisive battle.

« Lightning Release : Judgment of God - 100x Amplification ! »

An’s voice resounded through heaven and earth, and a massive pillar of lightning instantly rose up. The mountain-high pillar of light emitted a blinding radiance.

At the same time, Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi also felt An’s power. The two tacitly struck at the same time, and the Tailed Beast Ball and the pillar of lightning collided with a roar.

For a moment, the intense sound of the collision and the splashing sparks of light made the entire world seem as if it had been set ablaze. The blinding light illuminated every corner of the surroundings, making it impossible to open one’s eyes.

When the smoke gradually cleared, a figure fell from high in the sky and finally slammed heavily onto the ground.

This person was none other than Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi, whose clothes had become tattered, and he himself had lost all signs of life.

[Ding ! Congratulations to the Host for defeating the Seven-Tails Jinchuriki. You have received one Super Guaranteed Draw opportunity.]

« It’s a pity that you failed in the end. »

Just then, Aburame Torune and Yamato landed right beside An.

Facing the massive destruction caused by the battle, the two were dumbfounded, their mouths agape, unable to find suitable words to describe the scene before them.

Staring at the destroyed trees, the scorched earth, and the floating dust, this desolate scene made the two feel heavy and helpless.

In their hearts, An was now an existence even more terrifying than the Nine-Tailed.

« Leave the rest to me ! »

After saying this, Yamato walked forward and began to control the Seven-Tails Chomei within Seven-Tails Jinchuriki Zezhi.

Aburame Torune slowly strolled over to An’s side.

« If Lord Danzo intends to pass his position to you, Fu and I will fully support you. »

« However, this will likely take a few years. You are still too young right now, and you basically cannot command respect yet. »

An didn’t expect Aburame Torune to suddenly declare his stance. It could only be said that this is a world where strength is respected ; only with strength can one have a voice in this world.
