# Chapitre 72 — Scheming!

« Since that’s the case, we must prepare for both scenarios and guard against both Konoha and Kumogakure simultaneously. If the two Villages collaborate, the consequences will be unimaginable, » An said in a deep voice, his brows furrowed with deep concern.

« But if we do that, our forces will be spread too thin. If both Villages unite and attack us at the same time, it could lead to a total collapse of our lines ! »

Hozuki Lanyue’s tone became heavy, expressing his worry about the potential situation.

As the Mizukage, Mei Terumī remained silent for the moment, though she knew she had to make a decision, as this would determine the future arrangements for the Hidden Mist Village.

An looked at the silent conference room and realized that just arguing like this wasn’t enough. He had to drop the bombshell he had prepared long ago, placing a crumpled piece of paper in front of everyone.

An said, « On my way back, I encountered a squad of Konoha Ninja. This is the intelligence that was revealed after I killed them. »

Utakata immediately grabbed the paper from the table and began to read. After a moment, his expression turned grim.

Afterwards, everyone began to pass the paper around. Upon reading it, each person wore an expression of undisguisable disappointment.

Mei Terumī sighed, « I didn’t expect Konoha to actually choose to cooperate with Kumogakure. It seems this war will be much more difficult than we imagined. »

However, this intelligence was actually false information that An had deliberately spread.

With Konoha’s momentum currently so strong, how could they possibly choose to involve Kumogakure, a Village that could betray them at any moment, and increase the difficulty of the war ?

« Perhaps we can borrow the power of the Akatsuki Organization. They have always been interested in collecting Tailed Beasts. Now is the perfect time to let them take action and capture the Jinchuriki of Konoha. »

Hozuki Lanyue said this with a smug smile, clearly very satisfied with his own strategy.

An hadn’t expected Hozuki Lanyue’s thoughts to align with his own, but unfortunately, it was already too late to implement this plan.

« Then Edward Newgate and I might face such a situation. Perhaps the Akatsuki Organization is already plotting to capture Edward Newgate and me. »

Utakata’s words made the already dire situation worse, and the atmosphere in the room turned icy.

Just then, Kisame Hoshigaki drew the giant blade from his back and slammed it down onto the conference table.

The sturdy solid wood table was instantly split into splinters, and the entire conference room fell into silence.

« Anything said now is superfluous. What matters is that we must take action and prepare for the coming war ! » Kisame Hoshigaki’s voice rang through the entire conference room, his dead, shark-like eyes staring intensely at everyone present.

« Edward Newgate and I will lead a portion of the Hidden Mist Village’s Ninja to the western part of the Land of Water to guard against Konoha. Utakata and Ao will lead another portion of the Ninja to the northern part of the Land of Water to guard against Kumogakure. »

« As for the Mizukage, she will lead the major Ninja clans to defend the Hidden Mist Village. At the same time, I will dispatch the Feudal Lord to send troops to assist us in our defense. After all, the success or failure of this war concerns us all ! »

Kisame Hoshigaki’s voice was authoritative and firm, and everyone in the conference room looked at him in shock.

They hadn’t expected that the usually taciturn, honest, and simple-minded Kisame Hoshigaki would make such arrangements, which surprised everyone present.

It could be said that Kisame Hoshigaki’s actions this time completely overturned the impression everyone had of him, and at the same time, it revitalized the atmosphere in the room.

Seeing this, An nodded with great satisfaction. The time he had spent teaching Kisame Hoshigaki had not been in vain ; Kisame’s performance was indeed outstanding.

Mei Terumī looked at everyone excitedly and began to assign tasks to each person according to Kisame Hoshigaki’s instructions.

Soon, after two hours of discussion, everyone reached an agreement on an iron defense plan that was considered very reliable.

After the meeting ended, everyone left, and even Kisame was sent away by An.

Shortly after, An returned to his residence in the Hidden Mist Village.

He saw a person wearing a black battle robe sitting alone on a chair.

« Long time no see ! »

An’s voice rang out, and upon hearing it, the figure in black naturally knelt on the ground instantly.

« Lord Newgate ! »

Takayama Yume’s voice was choked with sobs, and there were even rare tears on Takayama Yume’s face.

Seeing this scene, An couldn’t help but feel a chill ; this guy was being way too melodramatic !

« Alright, don’t make it so flashy. Keep it simple. »

Takayama Yume shook his head repeatedly to refuse, « Lord Newgate is our leader ; the treatment a leader deserves must never be lacking at any time. »

Watching Takayama Yume’s performance, An was already starting to regret brainwashing this guy.

If he had known earlier, he wouldn’t have given so many demagogic speeches. Now he felt like he was turning into the Führer.

« Let’s not talk about that. How did the task I assigned to you go ? »

Takayama Yume immediately became interested, looking at An with an expression of worship.

« With the God-given fruit from Lord Newgate, everyone in the Hidden Mist Village is willing to serve Lord Newgate. »

« Of course, everyone also highly approves of your ideology ; only a unified Ninja World has a future. »

Hearing Takayama Yume’s words, An nodded repeatedly. Although this guy wasn’t very reliable as a person, he was quite reliable when it came to getting things done.

If this scheme succeeded, An had absolute confidence that he could take over the Hidden Mist Village with minimal cost.

« Very good. Next, you just need to set out with me, and we will surely succeed by then ! »

After arranging everything, it was already very late. After some simple cultivation, An lay down on the bed and fell into a deep sleep.

...

The next day.

Having just returned, An took Kisame Hoshigaki and Tayuya and left the Hidden Mist Village again, but this time An brought nearly six thousand Ninja with him.

The samurai of the Land of Water’s Feudal Lord were responsible for transporting war supplies. After all, finishing the Fourth Great Ninja War in four days, as in the original work, was impossible.

Even if Ninja were highly efficient, it was impossible to prepare for a large-scale war in four days.

As for An’s next task, it was to set up a defensive line in the western part of the Land of Water to guard against himself.

I am afraid that even though he had arranged things quite well, he might still encounter quite a few troubles when the time comes.

However, Takayama Yume’s existence was meant to clear these troubles, making this operation simpler and more direct.

« Lord Newgate, everything has been arranged. »

« Mhm ! Be extra careful. Assign everyone a task, and make sure to use people we trust. »

After carefully giving Takayama Yume a few instructions, An let him leave. After all, it was wartime now, and being careful at any time was never a mistake.

« Mas... Master, what did you arrange for him ? »

Tayuya curiously leaned in, her eyes full of curiosity.

« You’ll know when the time comes. »

An said this and left with a laugh, leaving a bewildered Tayuya behind.
