# Chapitre 12 — Training the Rasengan!

An handed another piece of paper to Uchiha Itachi, believing that with Uchiha Itachi’s intelligence, he would certainly be able to develop the Op-Op Fruit well.

Perhaps with the addition of the Op-Op Fruit and his own abilities, Uchiha Itachi would become a medical Ninja without equal, past or future.

« Brother ! »

Uchiha Itachi knew this was his older brother looking out for him, and he couldn’t help but feel moved.

An didn’t want to get all sentimental, so he immediately changed the subject.

« Get some sleep early ! Tomorrow, Jiraiya-sama is going to teach us an A-rank Ninjutsu. »

« Mm-hmm ! »

Giving the Devil Fruit to his father and younger brother had settled his concerns.

In Fugaku’s hands, the Wash-Wash Fruit could effectively prevent the Uchiha Clan from splitting apart ; of course, this would also require An to plan accordingly.

And the Op-Op Fruit, with its superpower-like existence, would certainly allow Uchiha Itachi to no longer be affected by terminal illness.

An had also already decided who to give the remaining Whisper-Whisper Fruit to.

...

The next day.

An and Uchiha Itachi got up early to eat, while Fugaku had already rushed off to work at the Konoha Police Force.

« Hurry up and wash your hands for breakfast, don’t keep Jiraiya-sama waiting. »

Looking at her two sleepy sons, Mikoto enjoyed the warmth of home at this moment.

The news that Jiraiya was going to teach An and Uchiha Itachi had been revealed by Fugaku at the dinner table yesterday.

« Yes ! Mother ! »

After eating, An and Uchiha Itachi left the house alone ; after all, Training Ground 3 wasn’t far from their home.

Along the way, many members of the Uchiha Clan still treated An with great respect, which further solidified An’s determination to change the Uchiha Clan’s fate of being wiped out.

When An and Uchiha Itachi arrived at Training Ground 3, Jiraiya did not appear, but instead, they saw another familiar figure waiting for them.

« Minato-sama ! Why are you here ? »

« Hello there ! An, Uchiha Itachi. »

Minato Namikaze instantly flashed in front of the two.

« Jiraiya-sensei had something to take care of, so he asked me to come and teach you the Rasengan. »

Uchiha Itachi still looked at Minato Namikaze with a hint of admiration.

An, however, curled his lip in disdain ; he was sure that perverted sage Jiraiya must have gone peeping again !

It had to be said that if Jiraiya could overcome that flaw, he could be called a perfect Ninja.

« Next, we will conduct Taijutsu training. First, run twenty laps around Training Ground 3. Given that you are still young, let’s skip the weighted training for now. »

Minato Namikaze’s words were like the devil’s whisper.

Training Ground 3 was so large that one lap was probably a kilometer.

And yet, Minato Namikaze was asking his four-year-old self to run twenty laps, nearly twenty kilometers.

« Minato-sensei, aren’t we supposed to be training Ninjutsu ? »

« With your current Chakra, it’s impossible to cast an A-rank Ninjutsu. After two years of honing your bodies, you might have enough Chakra to cast an A-rank Ninjutsu once. »

An and Uchiha Itachi’s expressions involuntarily slumped ; Minato Namikaze’s words left the brothers with no room to argue.

Especially since Uchiha Itachi had eaten the Op-Op Fruit, which required him to swap positions at will within the sphere created by the fruit’s ability, as well as cut and splice things, which required immense physical stamina as support.

However, this also allowed An to pursue physical training without reservation, as Uchiha Itachi, having eaten the Op-Op Fruit, would certainly be able to heal any hidden injuries in his body.

An could even start experimenting with the Lightning Body Tempering method he had always wanted to try.

Of course, the most pressing matter was the twenty laps in front of them.

« Charge ! You stupid little brother ! »

With a roar, An immediately took the lead and rushed out. Uchiha Itachi didn’t know why his brother was acting up, but he immediately accelerated to follow.

The two brothers began running lap after lap around Training Ground 3, starting their arduous Taijutsu training.

Days passed, and An and Uchiha Itachi went back and forth between home and Training Ground 3. Minato Namikaze occasionally guided An and Uchiha Itachi in their training.

Even without Minato Namikaze’s supervision, An and Uchiha Itachi remained steadfast, not slacking off for a single day.

Soon, half a year flashed by.

An and Uchiha Itachi had both grown quite a bit in height, and it probably wouldn’t be long before they had a younger brother.

When Minato Namikaze appeared in front of An and Uchiha Itachi again, he was wearing a different outfit.

He wore the Hokage robe with « Fourth Hokage » written on it, along with the exclusive Hokage hat.

« Greetings, Fourth Hokage-sama ! » X2

« You two make me feel a bit uncomfortable like that. I didn’t expect to become the Fourth Hokage myself. »

An, however, knew that once Minato Namikaze became Hokage, the Night of the Nine-Tailed would break out before long, and he would need to take action then.

« Minato-sensei, I feel like I can start training the Rasengan now. »

After saying this, An gave Uchiha Itachi a nudge.

The sensible Uchiha Itachi immediately formed hand seals, and Chakra began to condense in his mouth.

« Fire Release : Great Dragon Fire Technique ! »

Instantly, flames several meters long erupted from Uchiha Itachi’s mouth.

Minato Namikaze and An both retreated, both feeling a wave of heat wash over them.

At this moment, Minato Namikaze was extremely shocked. To be able to cast such a large-scale Ninjutsu at five years old, the talent of the two brothers was truly incomparable.

« You two are truly geniuses of the Uchiha Clan. Now it seems it’s time to teach you the Rasengan. »

Next, Minato Namikaze’s training method was the same as the one Jiraiya used to teach Naruto, having An and Uchiha Itachi begin the preliminary water balloon training.

After more than half an hour.

An and Uchiha Itachi had mastered the basic training method. Seeing that they could practice on their own, Minato Namikaze left.

Having become the Fourth Hokage, Minato Namikaze was much busier and didn’t have much time to guide An and Uchiha Itachi anymore.

After another busy day of training, An and Uchiha Itachi returned home.

They saw Fugaku and Mikoto already waiting for the two brothers.

« You’re back. Hurry up and eat ! »

Mikoto immediately prepared to get up and serve rice for An and Uchiha Itachi, but was pressed back down by Fugaku.

An and Uchiha Itachi obediently sat in their chairs to serve themselves rice.

« You two should know that Minato is already the Fourth Hokage. This couldn’t have happened without the support of our Uchiha Clan. Once he fully controls Konoha, our situation will be much better. »

« Your mother is going to give birth to a little brother for you soon. Don’t go out for training during this period ; stay at home and take good care of your mother. »

As Fugaku spoke, he didn’t look at An and Uchiha Itachi, but instead looked at Mikoto with doting eyes.

An and Uchiha Itachi stood up simultaneously and replied : « We will definitely take good care of Mother. »

Mikoto rubbed An and Uchiha Itachi’s heads. « Then I’ll leave myself and your younger brother in your care for this period. »

Thus, for the remaining two months, An and Uchiha Itachi took on the role of babysitters at home, cooking, doing laundry, and cleaning every day, which also brought a rare sense of domestic warmth to the house.

In short, under the feeding of An and Uchiha Itachi, Mikoto had gained quite a bit of weight ; perhaps the future Sasuke would be a chubby little boy.

And in their spare time, An and Uchiha Itachi worked hard to develop their Devil Fruits, and both made significant progress.
