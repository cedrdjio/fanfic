# Chapitre 42 — Entering the Hidden Mist Village!

Two years later.

Konoha Gate.

« An, going out on a mission again ! »

« This time, I’m going to travel the Ninja World. You two better guard the Konoha gate well ! »

« Don’t worry, no one has ever breached the Konoha gate ! »

Izumo and Kotetsu spoke with firm conviction, evidently extremely confident in their own strength.

An waved goodbye to the two and walked off into the distance.

Watching An’s back as he left Konoha, Izumo and Kotetsu knew that when he returned from his travels, he would undoubtedly be famous throughout the entire Ninja World.

An’s choice to travel the Ninja World was not just out of boredom ; more importantly, it was his own decision.

For a long time, life in Konoha had been too monotonous. He could only spar with people he was familiar with occasionally, which made An gradually feel a sense of weariness.

In short, this trip to travel the Ninja World gave An an opportunity—a chance to scheme for the entire Ninja World.

After leaving the sphere of influence of Konoha, An headed towards the eastern part of the Land of Fire.

Since the main purpose of this trip was to travel the Ninja World, An was in no rush.

Along the way, An relied purely on his own two legs, and through this, he gained a deeper understanding of the current state of the Land of Fire.

In a world with televisions, computers, and electric lights, it was clear that the level of productivity was not low. Coupled with the fact that Ninjas themselves were representatives of high productivity, the common people’s lives were still miserable.

The loose management system of the entire country, along with nobles, officials, bandits, and wandering Ninjas, posed a huge threat to the survival of ordinary people.

Furthermore, the constant wars between large and small Ninja Villages further exacerbated the hardships of the people’s lives.

It could be said that war orphans like Nagato, Yahiko, and Haku could be seen everywhere.

After traveling for a while, An set a small goal for himself : to unify the Ninja World.

...

Half a month later.

An barely managed to cross the vast ocean and finally set foot on the land of the Hidden Mist Village.

However, unfortunately, An could not adapt to traveling by boat at all. Before long, he felt dizzy and couldn’t help but start vomiting.

This short voyage felt like a torture of darkness and chaos to An.

« I didn’t expect to get seasick in my past life, and it’s the same in this life. »

Suddenly, several figures sprang out and quickly surrounded An.

However, when they saw An’s massive physique, they were all stunned for a moment.

In reality, An had used the Imitation-Imitation Fruit to perfectly imitate the world’s strongest man, Edward Newgate. His nearly three-meter-tall frame was extremely oppressive.

In any case, for the next period, An would travel the Ninja World with this new identity.

« We are the Land of Water Hidden Mist Village security force. You... who are you ? What is your purpose in coming to the Land of Water ? »

An never expected that he would be caught so quickly just after infiltrating.

He couldn’t help but marvel at the benefits brought by the small land area of the Land of Water ; at the very least, it allowed them to concentrate their defense forces.

An expanded his Observation Haki to perceive everything within several hundred meters around him.

However, An was surprised to find that there were only these few guys around.

This made An feel a bit relieved ; it seemed his whereabouts had not been exposed.

« Gurarara ! I am the world’s strongest man, Edward Newgate. I came here merely to travel the Ninja World ! »

An’s words caused the hanging hearts of the group to gradually relax, as his over-three-meter-tall frame was truly too oppressive. A look of relief finally appeared on their faces.

Just then, one of the guys who looked like the leader stepped out.

« Lord Newgate, I am Takayama Yume, captain of the 64th squad of the Hidden Mist Village security force. May I ask if you are a wandering Ninja ? Would you like to join our Hidden Mist Village ? »

« Of course, I am not qualified to accept any of your conditions, but if you are willing to join the Hidden Mist Village, I can recommend you. »

In reality, it wasn’t that Takayama Yume was desperate to recruit people, but mainly because the Hidden Mist Village had just ended its reputation as the 'Village of the Bloody Mist,' and the Fifth Mizukage, Mei Terumī, had just taken office.

Therefore, the current Hidden Mist Village was severely lacking in both high-level and low-level combat power.

Even if An was a powerhouse whose appearance was quite deceptive, Takayama Yume did not want to give up.

An, on the other hand, hadn’t expected to have so many unexpected gains after being stopped. His goal was the Three-Tails Isobu ; if he could infiltrate the Hidden Mist Village and obtain information about the Three-Tails Isobu, then his actions would be much simpler.

This was better than searching blindly in various lakes by himself without knowing any information.

« I am indeed a wandering Ninja. If the Hidden Mist Village suits me, I am willing to stay. »

Hearing An’s words, Takayama Yume and the others relaxed significantly.

In an instant, the tense atmosphere between them eased significantly, especially as Takayama Yume and the others silently withdrew their hands from their weapon pouches.

Although their tense vigilance remained, along the way, Takayama Yume and the others took turns surrounding An and occasionally scanned his body.

Despite this, An did not mind this guarded attitude.

If they had no vigilance, they probably wouldn’t have been sent to guard the borders of the Hidden Mist Village.

An hour later.

Under the guidance of Takayama Yume, An followed him into the mountains.

« This is one of our assembly points in the Land of Water. »

An nodded in acknowledgment and continued to follow Takayama Yume deep into the mountains.

Before long, they arrived in a remote and hidden valley, where a Village composed of Ninjas stood amidst the dense forest.

The Village was surrounded by towering peaks, hidden away from the public eye like a paradise.

Only those insiders who knew the secret passage could find this place deep in the mountains.

As he walked, An expanded his Observation Haki to perceive the surroundings. It could be said that the entire Village was filled with meticulously designed traps and ambush points, forming one line of defense after another hidden in the shadows.

The path wound upwards, shrouded by dark tree shade. An followed the figures of Takayama Yume and the others, moving rapidly through the forest with agility, dodging traps with nimble movements.

« Lord Newgate, please follow our movements closely. You must not take a single wrong step. »

An nodded calmly, his movements light and agile, each step accurate and flawless.

When An entered the Village, he discovered that it was a world of its own. The Village buildings maintained a traditional style, blending wood and stone structures.

The houses were cleverly hidden within the rock walls, making them difficult to detect. Some rooftops were even covered with quiet gardens and training grounds, and every corner exuded a strong martial atmosphere.

It could be said that the entire Village was filled with a serious and focused atmosphere. Many Ninjas were undergoing strict training, practicing various martial arts techniques and the use of hidden weapons.

Under the leadership of Takayama Yume, they soon arrived at the central square of the Village, where a solemn and majestic hall stood before them.
