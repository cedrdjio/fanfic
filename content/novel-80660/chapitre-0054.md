# Chapitre 54 — Becoming the Three-Tails Jinchuriki!

« Newgate, I hope you can see the situation clearly before you and voluntarily choose to give up the Three-Tails. »

Egao Jianfang’s voice echoed across the battlefield, and all the Ninjas gathered around Newgate.

Even the Ninjas who had been seriously injured previously struggled to stand up, assuming a posture ready to deal with Newgate.

Egao Jianfang watched this scene and said with a smile, « After a fierce battle with the Three-Tails, do you still have the ability to defeat so many people ? Voluntarily giving up the Three-Tails will be the best outcome for you. »

Newgate remained silent, calmly assessing the situation around him.

Then, Newgate slowly said, « You are right, I did have a fierce battle with the Three-Tails, but that does not mean I will submit to you. If I were to submit to power, what kind of man would I be ! »

Egao Jianfang smiled slightly, a trace of appreciation appearing in his eyes as he looked at Newgate.

« Since you are so determined, then Newgate will die for capturing the Three-Tails. I will take this message back to the Village. »

As Egao Jianfang’s voice faded, the battlefield fell into chaos, and both sides immediately engaged in fierce combat.

Newgate took the initiative without a hint of fear, facing the remaining living Ninjas.

« Spring Flash ! »

Relying on the speed of the Rumble-Rumble Fruit and the enhancement of the More-More Fruit, Newgate nimbly dodged one Ninjutsu after another, then accurately unleashed lightning attacks.

With every instant movement and release of Ninjutsu, Newgate controlled the rhythm of the battle, using his own speed and agility to quickly close in on every enemy and easily slaughter them.

The Ninjas who had originally surrounded Newgate fell to the ground one after another, completely unable to withstand any of his attacks.

Newgate’s eyes revealed his determination as he constantly dodged attacks, searched for the enemies' weaknesses, and then delivered a fatal blow when they least expected it.

« Can Newgate not be killed ? ! »

« Attack quickly ! »

The battlefield was in chaos. Egao Jianfang stood in the distance, gazing at the chaotic battle.

He hadn’t expected that after a great battle with the Three-Tails, Newgate’s strength could still gradually suppress the Ninjas present, which made Egao Jianfang click his tongue in secret.

Newgate fought tirelessly, his figure swiftly weaving through the crowd.

Every one of Newgate’s attacks was accurate and fatal, defeating the enemies one by one.

As time passed second by second, the battlefield became increasingly messy, with Ninjas falling one by one at Newgate’s feet.

Everyone began to feel despair ; Newgate’s strength exceeded their imagination.

Egao Jianfang watched this scene thoughtfully, feeling surprised by Newgate’s strength in his heart. Newgate’s power exceeded everyone’s expectations.

« Everyone, attack together ! »

Egao Hekong, seeing this, was not afraid of the pressure and chose to take the initiative. The Wind-style Chakra in the cane in his hand seemed to turn into a sharp blade, and his fierce offensive looked aggressive.

With the two of them joining, the pressure on the battlefield eased slightly, but this did not change the situation of Newgate slaughtering everyone.

As one Ninja after another fell, the mentality of the living Ninjas present began to collapse.

« This guy is too strong ! Is this the power of the world’s strongest man ? »

« His Chakra seems endless ; he is the real Tailed Beast ! »

« I’m not fighting anymore, Patriarch Egao. I’ve done my best to help here. »

While speaking, several guys retreated, directly leaving the battlefield.

Egao Jianfang and Egao Hekong secretly hated this. What was the use of running now ? They would still die in the end.

« Oh ! You want to attack me whenever you feel like it ? Do you think I’m easy to bully ? »

Newgate’s principle was : If people don’t offend me, I don’t offend them ; if people offend me, I will definitely kill them.

When the fighting started, these guys all stood out ; now it was too late to run.

« Lightning Body Flicker ! »

In an instant, several of Newgate’s figures appeared in front of the guys who were preparing to flee, and then he used the heart-piercing technique that White Zetsu was good at, directly killing these guys.

Seeing this scene, the others were even more stunned by Newgate and stood there feeling at a loss.

« Newgate, can we just let this matter go ? You can propose any conditions ! »

Suddenly, Egao Jianfang lowered his posture extremely, and there was even a hint of fawning in his tone.

« As long as you, Newgate, spare us, my Egao Clan is willing to offer two hundred million taels. Additionally, Egao Hekong can acknowledge you as his foster father, and at that time, our Egao Clan will assist you in becoming the Mizukage of the Hidden Mist Village. »

Before Newgate could respond to Egao Jianfang’s words, Egao Hekong jumped out directly.

« Father, maybe this guy is just holding on. Is it necessary for you to offer such good conditions ? You even want me to acknowledge him as my foster father ! »

Obviously, Egao Hekong was unwilling to accept this proposal ; otherwise, he might really end up with an extra father.

Of course, Newgate would not accept the generous conditions proposed by Egao Jianfang ; after all, Newgate was truly not interested in the position of Mizukage.

« What you said is worth my consideration... »

As soon as Newgate finished speaking, he directly, and without any sense of martial virtue, stepped forward to sneak-attack Egao Jianfang. The lightning spear in his hand pierced through Egao Jianfang’s chest in the astonished eyes of the crowd.

« Father... »

Egao Hekong, seeing this, immediately lunged straight at Newgate, as if he wanted to perish together with him.

How could Newgate make such a simple mistake ? In an instant, a large sphere appeared in Newgate’s hand, and then it was pressed onto Egao Hekong’s face.

In an instant, the situation on the field changed suddenly. Both members of the Egao Clan died in succession, and as for the other guys who wanted to escape, they were also easily slaughtered by Newgate.

At this moment, only the figure of the Three-Tails Isobu remained on the field, floating lonely on the surface of the lake.

Seeing this, Newgate walked forward without ceremony, choosing the simplest and crudest way to absorb the Three-Tails, using the power of the Eight Trigrams Sealing to directly seal the Three-Tails into his own body.

« Hey ! Kid, I admit your strength, but my power won’t be lent to you so easily ! »

At this moment, the Three-Tails inside Newgate also regained its consciousness and let out an angry roar.

Looking at the Three-Tails Isobu inside the Eight Trigrams Sealing, Newgate disdainfully picked his nose.

« Isobu, do you think I’m going to spoil you ? »

Inside the Tailed Beast space, Newgate used all his strength and began a righteous beating on the Three-Tails Isobu.

The Three-Tails, which was already suppressed by the Eight Trigrams Sealing, did not have much strength to begin with. Coupled with Newgate’s all-out, merciless attack, the Three-Tails Isobu was soon beaten black and blue, and its entire turtle body had swelled up quite a bit.

After Newgate had completely vented his frustration, he retreated from the Tailed Beast space.

Seeing this, the Three-Tails thought that Newgate only had these tricks, so it roared angrily.

« Hmph ! Betting on the dignity of a Tailed Beast, I will absolutely not submit. »

« Hmph ! Then prepare to get beaten up every day ! »

Newgate was not prepared to spoil this guy. If it didn’t submit, he would just beat it until it did. If it still didn’t submit, he would keep beating it until it did.
