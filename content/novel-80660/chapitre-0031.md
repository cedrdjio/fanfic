# Chapitre 31 — Aburame Torune's Sense of Aesthetics!

After An followed Aburame Torune out, he kept trailing behind him.

Suddenly, Aburame Torune stopped right in front of a stone building.

Then, Aburame Torune pushed the door open and walked in, allowing An to see the full view of the room.

The space inside this room was large, filled with various ninja tools, masks, and clothing.

Of course, these were all masks and uniforms bearing the Root insignia.

« Lord Danzo has not made any special arrangements for you for the time being, so you should first collect our Root equipment. Only after Lord Danzo has arranged your matters will you be considered a formal member of Root. »

As he spoke, Aburame Torune began to pick out equipment suitable for An in the room.

An stood to the side, thinking about Danzo Shimura. He wondered what kind of task this old fox Danzo would assign him ; it would be hilarious if he were tasked with monitoring the Uchiha Clan.

An had only been standing there thinking for a moment when Aburame Torune brought a large pile of equipment he had selected and tossed it in front of An.

Kunai, shuriken, smoke bombs, a katana, and various other miscellaneous items were piled together. Unfortunately, An was not very interested in these things.

Aburame Torune did not notice An’s expression and continued to talk incessantly.

« Because you joined Root at seven years old, we don’t have clothing and equipment this small, so I just took the smallest sizes. If you feel they don’t fit, you can alter them yourself ! »

« Then put on this mask, and make sure all your equipment is ready. You are now a member of Root. »

An picked up the clothes from the table and put them on. Unfortunately, An was simply too young ; the clothes were more than a size too big for his frame, making him look like he was wearing a sack.

As for the mask, Aburame Torune chose a red pig-face mask that looked just like Peppa Pig.

Seeing this, the expression on An’s face immediately collapsed. With his looks, he should at least get a fox-mask or a cat-mask !

Only that would suit the handsome appearance of his Uchiha Clan.

It had to be said that Aburame Torune’s aesthetic sense was a bit off ; he simply couldn’t appreciate his peerless beauty.

« Then, I don’t know who will contact me in the future ? Or should I come here to find Lord Danzo ? »

Aburame Torune looked at An solemnly, a hint of questioning in his tone.

« You are a peerless genius specially recruited by Lord Danzo, so I will be the one contacting you individually. If Lord Danzo has any arrangements for you, I will also be the one to notify you. Also, my surname is Aburame ; I am a member of the Aburame Clan. »

An nodded to signal he understood, then began to helplessly load various equipment onto himself, and he could only wear the oversized clothes for now.

By the time An put on the oversized clothes and the Peppa Pig mask, he looked quite comical, as if he were doing some special cosplay.

Seeing that An had finished getting ready, Aburame Torune said directly.

« Follow me ! »

After speaking, Aburame Torune ignored the uncomfortable An and, once again without a word, led An through the winding paths of the Root underground base.

Under Aburame Torune’s guidance, the two soon arrived at a sterile white room.

Aburame Torune didn’t even knock on the door ; he just walked straight in with An.

The room was filled with glassware and experimental specimens ; it looked comparable to a laboratory at a science and engineering university from An’s previous life.

« To become a member of Root, you must accept the Cursed Seal of Hatred controlled by Lord Danzo ! »

« The name alone tells me it’s not a good Ninjutsu. I don’t want to accept it, is that okay ? »

« No ! »

The impatient Aburame Torune directly pressed the helpless An down onto a chair, then moved to cover An’s eyes.

Before long, An felt his tongue heat up and knew he had been hit by Danzo’s Cursed Seal of Hatred !

In the time that followed, An followed Aburame Torune running around. By the time they finished all of this, it was already three hours later.

Of course, after An came out, he just felt that Root’s efficiency was about the same as the administrative efficiency of his previous life.

[Ding ! Congratulations to the host for joining Root, you have obtained one lottery chance.]

An didn’t expect this to grant him a lottery chance directly, but that would have to wait until he got back.

Looking back at the antique building behind him, An knew this was definitely not the Root headquarters ; at most, it was just one of the strongholds Danzo had arranged.

After all, as the darkness of the Ninja World and the « scapegoat » of Konoha, Danzo Shimura understood the principle of a cunning rabbit having three burrows.

...

Leaving Root and returning home, the setting sun dyed the Uchiha Clan’s compound red.

Along the way, An politely responded to the Uchiha Clan members who greeted him. Now, An was gaining recognition from more and more people in the Uchiha Clan due to his strength.

Especially under the planning of Fugaku and An, the relationship between the Uchiha Clan and Konoha was becoming increasingly close.

When An returned home, he saw that the meal was already set on the table, as if the whole family was waiting for him.

However, Itachi was nowhere to be seen ; he was probably training with Shisui.

It was rare for An to go up and actively pick up the two-year-old Sasuke, then he started to bury his head in eating.

« Nii-san, I want to eat this. »

Although the two-year-old Sasuke could speak, his voice was still very immature.

« Alright, alright ! You foolish little brother ! »

An picked up some food for Sasuke, and the two brothers just went on like that : one feeding, one eating.

This made Fugaku and Mikoto, who were on the side, feel a bit conflicted. After the two exchanged glances, Fugaku was the first to speak.

« An, how was it ? That old dog Danzo Shimura didn’t give you any trouble, did he ? »

Hearing Fugaku’s words, the rice ball in An’s mouth sprayed out directly.

His old father had always maintained a relatively gentle image ; he didn’t expect him to be so merciless when it came to Danzo Shimura !

« For a peerless genius like me, Danzo Shimura was practically begging me to join Root. »

« Besides, with your son’s strength, could Danzo Shimura possibly fool me ? »

Hearing this, the corners of Fugaku’s mouth raised slightly ; it was obvious he was satisfied with An’s answer.

His son had taken down a Special Jonin at seven years old and possessed extraordinary intelligence ; this was comparable to himself in his youth.

« Good, if Danzo Shimura gives you any trouble, tell me anytime. »

An understood Fugaku’s intention ; his old father was just afraid he would be taken advantage of !

However, An didn’t think Danzo Shimura could threaten him. With his current abilities, as long as it wasn’t one of those overpowered fighters from the late Fourth Shinobi World War, An was confident he could traverse the Ninja World.

« Alright, why talk so much ! An has been busy all day, hurry up and eat. »

At this moment, Mikoto’s status as the head of the household was reflected ; even Sasuke, the two-year-old, sped up his eating.
