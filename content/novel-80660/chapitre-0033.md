# Chapitre 33 — One Year Later, Hiruzen Sarutobi's Inquiry!

One year later.

The border of the Land of Fire.

« Lightning Release : 30 Million Volts : Tenfold Thunder Bird ! »

Instantly, the Thunder Bird’s form expanded tenfold, and its speed increased tenfold as well. The entire area was filled with lightning, looking like the end of the world !

« He is the Thunder God of Konoha ! »

« Mission failed ! Retreat ! »

Facing Uchiha An’s attack, the Rock Ninja watched this scene and scrambled to retreat to the perimeter with all their might. Unfortunately, some unlucky ones weren’t fast enough and were instantly electrocuted into charred remains.

As for the surviving Rock Ninja, they scattered and fled in an instant. Seeing this, Aburame Torune’s face was filled with a smile, his eyes full of awe as he watched Uchiha An displaying such might.

« Uchiha An, your moniker as the Thunder God of Konoha is now on par with the Fourth Hokage’s title of Yellow Flash. »

« It’s alright ! »

After a year of tempering and bloodshed, Uchiha An had not only grown significantly in height but had also matured in mind.

Because he had killed Special Jonin Shiranui Genma in a single strike, Danzo had started assigning Uchiha An higher-difficulty missions from the very beginning.

At the same time, it allowed Uchiha An to witness what the Ninja World was truly like ; friendship and peace were extremely rare. All that existed were conflicts, assassinations, and unscrupulous means.

A year of time also made Uchiha An realize that Konoha was not just righteous, but also evil.

Likewise, Root was not entirely under Danzo’s control, but the power Danzo wielded was simply too great.

« In one year, you have terrified the four great Ninja villages, and the smaller villages don’t even dare to harbor any thoughts against Konoha. You are now the most famous guy in Root. »

Hearing Aburame Torune’s teasing, Uchiha An smiled bitterly and shook his head.

The louder the reputation, the more intelligence exposed, and such people died the fastest.

« The Rock Ninja who attempted to infiltrate the Land of Fire have been beaten back. What mission does Lord Danzo have next ? »

Hearing that Uchiha An wanted to carry out another mission, Aburame Torune immediately became nervous.

« If we assign you any more missions, it might spark the Fourth Great Ninja War, so both the Fourth Hokage and Lord Danzo hope you can return to Konoha for a rest. »

« Hmm ! »

Uchiha An knew that it was indeed not the right time to provoke other Ninja villages. If a Great Ninja War were to be sparked, it would be his fault.

Moreover, Konoha was not prepared for war right now, so even the staunchly hawkish Danzo Shimura hoped that Uchiha An could return to Konoha to rest.

Two days later.

Uchiha An and Aburame Torune arrived back, exhausted from their journey.

After entering the gates of Konoha, Uchiha An parted ways with Aburame Torune. Aburame Torune needed to report to Danzo Shimura, while Uchiha An headed towards his home.

Suddenly, two figures flashed past on the rooftop next to Uchiha An.

« Rare ! »

Seeing this, Uchiha An couldn’t suppress his competitive spirit. Relying on his own physical fitness, he leaped up onto the roof and chased after the two.

The three figures moved rapidly across the rooftops. There was a small gap between the two in front, while Uchiha An followed closely behind.

The three leaped over one balcony after another, their speed so fast that the ordinary Konoha villagers on the ground could hardly keep up.

At this moment, Uchiha An was chasing Kakashi and Might Guy with all his might, constantly closing the distance between them.

Although Uchiha An was several years younger than the two, after his deliberate training and the enhancement of his Lightning Dragon technique, his own physical fitness had improved rapidly.

Now, the speed Uchiha An displayed was even slightly faster than that of Kakashi and Might Guy.

Perhaps Kakashi and Might Guy sensed Uchiha An’s approaching aura, and a pressure they couldn’t ignore welled up in their hearts.

The two increased their speed again, and the three of them began to run circles around Konoha frantically.

The three raced side by side like this. As time passed, more and more people noticed the figures on the rooftops.

« Stop ! »

In an instant, an aged figure blocked their path. Seeing this, Uchiha An and Kakashi immediately dodged, but Might Guy, unable to stop in time, crashed straight into him.

« Bang ! »

« Ouch ! »

The three stopped immediately. Seeing this, Kakashi immediately stepped forward to help the person who had been hit up.

Might Guy didn’t expect to run into Hiruzen Sarutobi. He immediately became a bit nervous.

« I’m sorry, Lord Third. »

After glaring at Might Guy, Hiruzen Sarutobi waved his hand dismissively.

« It’s fine for you two to compete in Konoha, but you absolutely must not affect everyone’s normal lives. »

Kakashi and Might Guy immediately nodded like chickens pecking at rice, tacitly realizing that the situation before them had nothing to do with them.

After all, neither of them was blind, and they immediately understood that Hiruzen Sarutobi’s purpose was to find Uchiha An. Thus, the two didn’t say a word and quickly left, leaving behind a quiet atmosphere.

Hiruzen Sarutobi snorted coldly in the direction the two had left, but his gaze immediately softened when it turned to Uchiha An.

« You’ve done well. Because of your presence this past year, we haven’t lost many regional battles on Konoha’s borders. You are truly the guardian deity of Konoha ! »

« I heard that now, when Ninjas from other countries encounter you, they retreat directly, just like Minato did back then. »

Hearing Hiruzen Sarutobi rambling without getting to the point, Uchiha An helplessly nodded in agreement.

Perhaps realizing that Uchiha An had just returned and that it wasn’t appropriate to keep him talking here, Hiruzen Sarutobi immediately steered the conversation to the main point.

« Back when the Second Hokage died, I succeeded him as the Third Hokage. Because Konoha was in a precarious situation at the time, for the sake of Konoha’s stability, Danzo and I established Root. »

« Now that Minato has become the Fourth Hokage, yet Danzo still continues to hold control over Root. As his friend, I still hope that Danzo can step back to the second line and not be so overworked. »

Hearing Hiruzen Sarutobi’s statement, Uchiha An was stunned for a moment. He had just returned from the border of the Land of Fire ; why did Hiruzen Sarutobi come to him to say these things ?

« Uchiha An, I think you should understand what I mean. »

After saying this, Hiruzen Sarutobi turned and jumped onto the road, greeting the Konoha villagers in a friendly manner.

Uchiha An remained standing on the rooftop, deep in thought. It seemed that Hiruzen Sarutobi wanted him to take over Danzo Shimura’s position so that he could better cooperate with the Fourth Hokage.

It was just that Uchiha An couldn’t guess whether this was Hiruzen Sarutobi’s intention or the Fourth Hokage Minato’s intention.

However, regardless, Uchiha An would definitely vie for Danzo Shimura’s position.

After such a small episode, Uchiha An no longer had the mood to stroll around the streets. Speeding all the way, Uchiha An soon returned to the Uchiha Clan’s compound.

« Greetings, Young Clan Leader ! »

« The Young Clan Leader is finally back ! »

« The Thunder God, Uchiha An—the Young Clan Leader is truly too powerful. »

Along the way, facing Uchiha An’s return, everyone showed great enthusiasm. After all, Uchiha An’s achievements were earned through hard fighting on the battlefield, so he could naturally gain the respect of others.

Especially since Uchiha An had made such a great name for himself in the Ninja World, it made everyone in the Uchiha Clan feel honored.
