# Chapitre 44 — I Want to Provide for You in Your Old Age!

Although his attacks had no effect, Ao was not discouraged ; instead, a faint smile appeared on his face.

« Water Release : Hydrification Technique ! »

As Ao finished speaking, his entire body instantly merged into the surrounding massive water flow, disappearing without a trace like a river flowing into the ocean.

The onlookers were stunned by this scene ; from their perspective, they could no longer see Ao’s figure at all, as if he had vanished into thin air.

However, An’s Observation Haki had been tightly locked onto Ao’s position, while Ao skillfully used Ninjutsu to hide himself within the water flow.

An decided to take advantage of this situation, deliberately pretending not to know Ao’s exact location, and lured Ao to gradually approach the rock pillar where he stood.

As the water level gradually receded, Ao got closer and closer to the rock pillar where An was located.

At the critical moment, Ao seized the opportunity, leaping out from the water, rushing straight toward the high rock pillar, and pounced fiercely at An.

Ao’s right hand had its four fingers pressed together, with Chakra gathering in his hand, forming a straight sword.

Seeing An standing there unshaken, as if he hadn’t sensed any danger, Ao couldn’t help but laugh triumphantly.

« As long as I land this strike, your Chakra meridians will be sealed, and once you lose your Chakra, you will undoubtedly be defeated ! »

As he spoke, Ao’s right hand thrust fiercely toward An. In the astonished gaze of the onlookers, Ao’s attack brushed past An’s figure, missing by only a hair’s breadth.

Ao was startled, but he was not discouraged, because as long as he could land a successful hit, victory would be his.

With this thought in mind, Ao quickly attacked with both hands simultaneously, stabbing continuously at An.

However, relying on his keen Observation Haki, An dodged every one of Ao’s attacks in succession, causing every strike of Ao’s to return without success.

In the eyes of the onlookers, An’s three-meter-tall body was exceptionally agile, always able to avoid Ao’s attacks in unexpected ways and angles.

As time passed, Ao became increasingly impatient, and his attacks with both hands became even more rapid.

« Bastard, if you have the guts, stop dodging ! »

Despite his continuous attacks, Ao had not achieved any results, and the expression on his face looked increasingly frantic.

However, An didn’t want to drag this out any longer ; after all, fighting back and forth with the same two moves was pointless.

Thus, An deliberately exposed a flaw to lure Ao into the trap.

The impatient Ao fell for the trick, just as An had expected, and thrust straight at An’s ribs.

Since he dared to expose this flaw, An was confident in his ability to protect himself, using his maximum capability to activate Armament Haki for defense.

« Crack ! »

The sound of bone cracking was barely audible, but An clearly perceived this tiny sound and the change in Ao’s facial expression ; that hit must have been incredibly painful for Ao.

In the middle of the fight, Ao suddenly put both his hands behind his back, looking like an expert.

« Bastard, if you have the skill, then stop hiding ! »

Ao continued to attack incessantly, but he still gained nothing, and the expression on his face became increasingly frantic.

However, An was already tired of this boring entanglement ; after all, this guy Ao only had these two tricks, and there was no point in continuing to fight.

Thus, An deliberately exposed a flaw to lure Ao into the trap.

Indeed, the impatient Ao fell for the trick, just as An had expected, and struck fiercely at An’s ribs.

An was already prepared, and at the moment he was attacked, he quickly used Armament Haki to defend, doing his best to protect himself.

« Crack ! »

A barely audible sound of bone cracking rang out. An keenly perceived this tiny sound and the change in Ao’s facial expression ; Ao must have been in great pain at that moment.

Suddenly, Ao withdrew and retreated, placing his hands behind his back and striking a pose like an expert.

« Your strength is not bad ; I can recommend you to join the Hidden Mist Village ! »

An curled his lip in disdain ; the opponent was clearly not up to the task yet kept putting on a brave front.

However, An didn’t pay attention to that, instead, he switched to a more sincere tone and said, « Become my son ! Rely on my name to gallop across the Ninja World ! »

[Ding ! Congratulations to the host for defeating Jonin Ao. You have received one lottery chance.]

Now, when facing ordinary lottery draws, An could remain completely calm ; only the super guaranteed lottery draws could make An feel even a trace of excitement.

Originally, Ao should have felt angry at such words, but after learning of An’s strength, Ao didn’t say much about it.

After all, what strong person didn’t have some strange quirks ? It’s just that the quirks of this strong person, Newgate, were a bit too weird.

At present, the newly appointed Mizukage, Mei Terumī, urgently needed to integrate the various forces and resources of the Hidden Mist Village to cope with potential future challenges.

It could be said that this was a time when Mei Terumī felt immense pressure, and Newgate’s arrival was like a timely gift, which would inevitably provide Mei Terumī with great help and support.

« Tomorrow, I will take you to meet the Mizukage. »

After speaking, Ao turned and left the training ground directly, fearing that An might say something else surprising.

Bi saw this and immediately followed Ao’s footsteps. The two walked out while whispering to each other, and the onlookers didn’t know what they were talking about.

The onlookers, who had finished watching the spectacle, immediately gathered around, led by Takayama Yume.

However, when the crowd saw An’s three-meter-plus tall figure, they still dared not approach easily.

Everyone stared fixedly at An, feeling the powerful aura emanating from him. This presence made people involuntarily step back, as if facing an insurmountable mountain.

« Alright, Lord Newgate has had a hard battle, so everyone should stop bothering Lord Newgate. »

Under Takayama Yume’s persuasion, the crowd quickly dispersed, leaving only An and Takayama Yume standing on the training ground.

Realizing that the surroundings were completely empty, Takayama Yume quickly approached An’s side.

« Lord Newgate, if you attract the attention of the Mizukage in the Hidden Mist Village, then I will naturally and directly become your man. I hope Lord Newgate will cultivate me more at that time, » Takayama Yume said full of expectation.

An did not expect this guy to actually possess a certain potential for being an official ; he seemed to have already become a qualified lackey.

« Hmm. »

An responded with a sound, indicating affirmation, and a smile immediately bloomed on Takayama Yume’s face.

In fact, An had his own considerations ; being a newcomer, he couldn’t rely entirely on himself, and perhaps Takayama Yume could come in handy in certain situations later on.

Just at this moment, Takayama Yume suddenly knelt on the ground and kowtowed to An.

« Lord Newgate, you haven’t adopted a son yet, and my parents died early. If you don’t mind, I would like to provide for you in your old age. »

Seeing this scene, An just wanted to take a frozen hairtail and smack this guy ; he was just imitating Whitebeard, not actually wanting to adopt a son.

« My sons can only be strong people. »

After saying this, An turned and left ; next, it would naturally be good food and drink to be served !

Takayama Yume was instantly excited ; since he wasn’t rejected, he still had a chance.
