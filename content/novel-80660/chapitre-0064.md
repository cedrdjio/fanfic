# Chapitre 64 — Combined Ninjutsu Shows Its Might!

With Uchiha An’s constant corrections, the coordination of over five thousand Ninjas became increasingly tacit, and they all marched forward in unison, their movements flexible and precise.

Their footsteps were balanced and coordinated, like a precision machine, without the slightest disorder.

As they continued to advance, the wind caught the black cloaks on their bodies, resembling a rolling dark cloud.

« Very good, Uchiha An, you fellow are truly a natural-born leader ! »

« Mm ! »

The relationship between Danzo Shimura and Uchiha Fugaku remained poor.

However, seeing Uchiha An mold a force of five thousand people into this shape in just a few short days, both men were extremely satisfied with him.

« It’s nothing special. »

Uchiha An knew this was just a small trick ; what was more important was the upcoming battle.

« Uchiha An, what do you say we should do next ? You must know that after the war, relying on five thousand people won’t be enough to hold the long border of the Land of Wind. »

Inuzuka Tsume wore a look of worry ; after all, sending five thousand people into a large country made it impossible to establish stable rule.

Upon hearing this, Uchiha An chuckled and said to Inuzuka Tsume, « Rest assured, Mother-in-law, I have already made all the arrangements. »

« Once we enter the Land of Wind, I will lead the Shimura Clan and the Uchiha Clan in a joint, fierce attack, with the primary target being Hidden Sand Village. »

« At the same time, Father and the Nara Clan Head will lead the others to attack the Daimyo’s Palace ; we must capture the Daimyo of the Land of Wind alive. »

« Of course, we still need to consider the issue of the long and difficult-to-defend border of the Land of Wind, but I have already made the corresponding preparations for these. »

Uchiha An explained his plan to everyone concisely and guaranteed that he had already considered all possible situations.

Uchiha An’s firm and confident tone revealed his determination and confidence in this operation.

After hearing Uchiha An’s words, everyone showed a smile of relief.

Even if no one knew what Uchiha An’s specific plan was, everyone believed that he would definitely be able to lead them to final victory.

In reality, Uchiha An had only requested that the Daimyo of the Land of Fire train a unit of firearm troops ; after all, in the original manga, firearms existed in the world of Hokage.

Uchiha An’s goal was to have the firearm troops be responsible for occupying the border of the Land of Wind, and as for the Ninjas on the border, Uchiha An had Tayuya step forward to hire the Akatsuki Organization to take action, and of course, Kisame Hoshigaki was among them.

It could be said that Uchiha An had already begun his planning for the Land of Wind long ago.

...

Three days later. Outside Hidden Sand Village.

Uchiha An, Fu Yamanaka, Danzo Shimura, and Yamato were hiding beneath the sand, while the others were trailing far behind.

« Lord Uchiha An, what are your arrangements ? You don’t intend to rely on the four of us to attack Hidden Sand Village, do you ! »

Fu Yamanaka’s tone trembled slightly ; he seemed a bit nervous.

However, Uchiha An could understand ; after all, the distance was a bit too close, and Uchiha An could even clearly see the faces of the guards in the distance.

« I called you here naturally because there is an opportunity for you to act. When the time comes, you will first trigger a massive sandstorm to block the vision of the Hidden Sand Village guards. »

« Yamato will manipulate Seven-Tails Chomei to exhale paralyzing phosphorous powder, and as for me, I will amplify the power of the phosphorous powder. »

« Finally, Lord Danzo will use Wind Release Ninjutsu to blow the phosphorous powder into Hidden Sand Village. »

Upon hearing Uchiha An’s arrangements, everyone nodded in approval ; this plan could be said to be quite perfect.

The reason Uchiha An could make such an arrangement was because that fellow Fu Yamanaka had eaten the Logia-type Sand-Sand Fruit, and thus directly became Uchiha An’s die-hard loyalist.

However, just at this moment, Danzo Shimura raised a question.

« There is an old woman in Hidden Sand Village named Chiyo. Her medical skills are extremely superb, and it is possible she might detect the phosphorous powder in the air. »

« Even if Chiyo detects it, it won’t affect us ; after all, by that time, many people in Hidden Sand Village will have already collapsed. »

After hearing Uchiha An’s words, everyone stopped saying anything else and began to make their preparations.

« Sand Release : Desert Grand Tornado ! »

In an instant, a massive storm suddenly formed in the sky not far from them, with rolling dark clouds and fierce winds, as if it were about to swallow Hidden Sand Village.

The guards of Hidden Sand Village felt a bit strange, but they did not slack off. They immediately deployed anti-sand measures, using sandbags to build temporary defensive walls, placing them firmly at important locations around the Village to prevent the sandstorm from harming the villagers and buildings.

« Secret Technique : Phosphorous Powder Technique ! »

As Yamato’s voice fell, he directly summoned a cloud of yellow phosphorous powder and mixed it with the sand particles in the air, making the phosphorous powder almost undetectable.

Seeing this, Uchiha An immediately decided to utilize his More-More Fruit ability, manipulating the power and range of the phosphorous powder to expand it a hundredfold.

« Wind Release : Vacuum Palm ! »

Then, Danzo Shimura formed hand seals with one hand, continuously condensing his own Chakra to form a powerful hurricane.

He used the hurricane to push the air carrying the phosphorous powder and sand, rapidly enveloping the entire Hidden Sand Village.

This hurricane not only pushed the sandstorm but also mixed the phosphorous powder and sand into it, making its power even stronger and even harder to detect.

« I believe it will take effect very soon. »

As Uchiha An’s whispered voice fell, the phosphorous powder quickly arrived at Hidden Sand Village.

The speed at which the phosphorous powder spread caused Hidden Sand Village to be directly shrouded in a dim mist, and it soon exerted a powerful paralyzing effect.

The first to be affected were the guards at the high points of Hidden Sand Village. The guards, who were usually highly vigilant, discovered the abnormality, but they were unable to move and immediately collapsed to the ground, unable to make any resistance at all.

As they fell silently, unable to send out any warning signals, it was as if they had been stripped of all their strength.

Next, the effect of the phosphorous powder spread to the exterior of the buildings within Hidden Sand Village. The affected Ninjas fell to the ground one after another, all in the same posture and state, as if quickly suppressed by this invisible force.

However, this scene alerted other Ninjas inside the buildings, and a violent and shrill alarm immediately resounded throughout Hidden Sand Village.

The Ninjas were instantly awakened by the alarm. Everyone felt an aura of danger, potentially facing a perilous situation that threatened the safety of the entire Hidden Sand Village.

« Prepare for poison defense, go out and investigate which Ninja Village has attacked our Hidden Sand Village. »

At this moment, Ebizo, as the retired leadership, immediately responded and arranged for Ninjas to go out and investigate.

As for Ebizo’s sister, Chiyo, she did not appear ; instead, she began to prepare an antidote by herself.

The Ninjas who received the order held their breath and focused, like invisible leopards, quickly spreading throughout Hidden Sand Village to search for flaws and traces of the enemy.

In this environment shrouded in the phosphorous powder mist, everyone moved cautiously, not daring to be careless.

The atmosphere of the entire Hidden Sand Village was tense and strained ; urged by the alarm, the high-level officials of Hidden Sand Village quickly formulated a countermeasure.
