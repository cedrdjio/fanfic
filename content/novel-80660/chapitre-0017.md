# Chapitre 17 — Entrance Exam!

The journey was silent.

When An returned home, he saw that his family was already waiting for him at the table, and Itachi was holding one-year-old Sasuke and feeding him ; the two of them looked very harmonious.

« You’re back ! An, hurry over and eat ! »

Mikoto naturally picked up a bowl to serve An rice, then pulled An into her embrace.

Ever since Itachi started holding Sasuke while eating, Mikoto had extended her clutches toward five-year-old An so she would have a toy in her arms too. An was powerless to resist and could only silently endure it.

Thus, only Fugaku looked a bit displeased, silently rubbing his nose.

« The Fourth Hokage has extended his reach into the Police Force. There are quite a few Anbu Ninjas inside the Police Force now. »

Fugaku’s expression turned gloomy as he spoke. He seemed dissatisfied with Minato Namikaze’s arrangements, believing this was an encroachment on the Uchiha Clan’s authority to manage the Police Force.

An knew his plans were beginning to take effect ; this was one step toward saving the Uchiha Clan.

« Then, did Teacher Minato allow people from the Uchiha Clan to enter the Anbu or other departments ? »

Fugaku nodded. « There are indeed such arrangements... »

As he spoke, Fugaku looked at An, who was being forced to eat, a hint of confusion appearing on his face.

« An, how did you know about this ? »

« Father, this is my older brother’s arrangement. »

Apparently, five-year-old Itachi also sensed the unusual atmosphere. He suspected this must have been done by his mysterious older brother.

« This is just a little plan of mine. By letting Teacher Minato’s forces enter the Konoha Police Force, while having members of the Uchiha Clan enter other departments in Konoha, Konoha can better accept the Uchiha. »

The Konoha Police Force had always been managed by the Uchiha Clan, handling trivial matters between villagers, which was inherently unpopular.

An had arranged for the Hokage’s forces to enter the Police Force while simultaneously having the Uchiha Clan members enter other departments.

An believed that with the Uchiha Clan’s tsundere nature, they would integrate well into Konoha, avoiding the alienation from the Konoha higher-ups that occurred in the original plot.

« Since it is your arrangement, I will not ask any more questions. »

As an old father, Fugaku felt quite helpless regarding his overly intelligent son, but he still trusted An’s arrangements.

Who could imagine that the leader of Konoha’s number one noble clan would trust his five-year-old son so much ?

Time passed like a galloping horse.

One year later.

Soon, it was the day for Ninja Academy enrollment.

In front of the Ninja Academy, there was already a dense crowd of children enrolling and their parents. It could be said that the desire for one’s children to succeed was timeless.

An and Itachi were dragged to the Ninja Academy gate by Fugaku early in the morning. The two brothers—one cool and handsome, the other smiling brightly—attracted quite a lot of attention.

Naturally, as members of Konoha’s number one noble clan, the clan crest on their backs was also a focal point for the crowd.

« Stupid little brother, don’t be so cool. Haven’t you noticed that everyone is paying attention to you and ignoring me ? »

Itachi impatiently glanced at his older brother beside him, looking as if he didn’t know this person.

His older brother was sometimes shockingly intelligent, and other times as foolish as a simpleton ; it was truly speechless.

« Greetings, Clan Head, Young Clan Head, and Itachi ! »

« Hello ! Little sister-in-law ! »

An’s blunt words caused the young girl, Izumi, to blush deeply. She didn’t say a word and just ran away.

Fugaku shook his head at this, while Itachi looked at his older brother with an angry expression.

In reality, An and Izumi hadn’t met many times, and An had only learned about Izumi from his old father.

It was just that An went straight for the big move upon meeting, which left both Itachi and Izumi feeling helpless.

Next was the mutual greeting between the various Ninja clans.

However, the Uchiha Clan was still not welcomed ; few people came to greet Fugaku, though they naturally couldn’t help but praise the brothers An and Itachi.

In short, most of the students who came were clan Ninjas from various families, while the children of commoners were basically marginalized.

« Parents, please hand your children over to us teachers. The assessment will take place inside the school. Parents who are worried can also wait here. »

Fugaku nodded at the two brothers, and Itachi and An walked into the school quite consciously.

However, even though it was their first day of school, none of the hundreds of children were crying ; they all seemed quite mature.

Led by an extra teacher, several hundred mischievous children stood in a large square formation. An glanced over and estimated there were over three hundred children participating in the assessment.

Suddenly, a man with a decent appearance and a slightly aged look walked in front of everyone.

« I am Konoha Jonin Shimura Kazama. I will be responsible for your assessment from here on. »

Hearing this, the corners of An’s mouth rose involuntarily. He wasn’t wearing a Jonin vest, so he must be a Special Jonin. This guy was a bit narcissistic !

And his name was Kazama—this was Naruto, a Ninja brawl, not Kasukabe. This guy must have walked onto the wrong set !

« The first assessment is the extraction of Chakra. Only children who can extract Chakra are qualified to participate in the next assessment. »

Next, led by a group of passerby Chunin, the three hundred children quickly stood in orderly lines.

Thirty pitch-black stones were placed on tables, with a teacher standing in front of each table.

« These are Chakra Sensing Stones. They are used to gauge the amount of Chakra your body can extract. The greater the amount of Chakra, the lighter the black color of the stone becomes ; some geniuses can even turn the black Chakra Sensing Stone completely white. »

Immediately, the many brats below began to exclaim, as if this were some incredible gadget.

But in An’s view, this thing was just average. After all, ordinary people were like Kakashi, lacking mana ; only those with bloodline cheats were qualified to cast various Ninjutsu without worrying about running out of Chakra, so this thing was basically useless.

« Next, those whose names are called, step forward and try to inject your Chakra into the Chakra Sensing Stone. »

« First, Sakata Gintoki... »

« Second, Kurosaki Ichigo... »

...

Soon, one child after another passed the assessment ; basically, no one was eliminated in this round.

After all, physical powerhouses in this world also have Chakra ; reaching the pinnacle solely through physical strength is basically impossible.

Soon, one child after another completed the Chakra sensing, and it was immediately the turn of the brothers An and Itachi.

« Stupid little brother ! Older brother will leave this opportunity to you. »

As he said this, An put on an expression as if he had suffered a huge loss, as if he had missed out on something.

Itachi curled his lip in disdain. His older brother was just letting him take the lead as a setup for his own entrance, after which his older brother, with his even more monstrous talent, would dominate the entire scene.
