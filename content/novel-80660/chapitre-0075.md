# Chapitre 75 — Preparing for Battle!

One month later, the war between the Hidden Mist Village and Konoha had not broken out, and no irreparable rift had been created between the two Villages.

However, Konoha’s move to unify the Ninja World triggered vigilance and resistance from other Ninja Villages.

The Kumogakure of the Land of Lightning and the Iwagakure of the Land of Earth, as two powerful major Ninja Villages, naturally joined forces to resist Konoha’s aggression.

Some smaller Ninja Villages unwilling to submit to Konoha also joined their camp, forming a massive Shinobi Allied Forces.

After An’s true identity was exposed, the one who regretted it most was Nagato. He regretted listening to An’s words back then, helping Konoha conquer other Ninja Villages, and paving the way for Konoha to unify the Ninja World.

And now, facing An, who could stand on equal footing with him, Nagato also lost confidence in capturing all nine Tailed Beasts on his own.

Therefore, Nagato decided to lead the Akatsuki Organization to join the Shinobi Allied Forces, gathering everyone’s strength to jointly oppose the immensely powerful Konoha.

As the Hokage, Minato Namikaze chose to take action personally this time, and directly issued a challenge, setting the battlefield in the neutral Land of Iron.

As for Iwagakure and Kumogakure, they also dispatched their Villages' war weapons : the Two-Tails Matatabi Jinchuriki Yugito, the Four-Tails Son Goku Jinchuriki Roshi, the Five-Tails Kokuo Jinchuriki Han, and the Eight-Tails Gyuki Jinchuriki Killer Bee.

It could be said that this battle gathered all the power of the Ninja World, and the great war in the Land of Iron would surely determine the future of the Ninja World.

Of course, the happiest among them was Nagato. As long as he defeated Konoha, he could use his own strength to collect all the Tailed Beasts in an instant, and then he would be able to realize his plan.

At this moment, in a valley in the Land of Iron, Konoha had set up a temporary command post.

This was the headquarters of the Allied Forces and the center for strategic decision-making for the three Villages.

Inside a huge tent, the high-ranking officials of the Allied Forces were meeting to discuss the upcoming battle plan.

As the Hokage and Commander-in-Chief of the Allied Forces, Minato Namikaze, wearing his Hokage robe and hat, sat in the main seat with a calm and determined gaze.

On the left and right sides of Minato Namikaze sat the two Kage, Mei Terumī and Gaara.

Mei Terumī was the Mizukage of the Hidden Mist Village, and Gaara was the Kazekage of the Hidden Sand Village, although the Land of Wind and the Land of Water no longer existed.

Opposite the three of them sat An, the greatest contributor to the unification of the Ninja World.

Although An was only the leader of the Root organization in the Village, An possessed astonishing strength and prestige.

In fact, the great achievements An had made in this war to unify the Ninja World were enough for An to sit in the main seat.

But An did not do so, instead humbly sitting in the fourth position.

Regarding this, no one else had any objections because everyone knew that An was not a person who coveted fame and fortune ; An only wanted to fight to establish a unified nation and for world peace.

Moreover, now was not the time to distribute credit ; the most important thing now was how to win this war.

Minato Namikaze, as the Hokage, his eyes flashing with a determined light, stood before the conference table and said in a loud and powerful voice : « This Ninja World War will surely be the last one. Once we unify the Ninja World, we will definitely end wars completely ! We must fight bravely for all the sacrificed Ninjas, for all the suffering civilians, and for all the descendants who yearn for peace ! »

Minato Namikaze’s words were like a pebble rippling across the surface of the water, resonating with everyone present.

Everyone present had experienced previous Ninja World Wars ; it could be said that everyone understood the pain of war. They had all been through countless wars and hardships, and everyone was longing to build a world without bloodshed and violence.

« This time we must win, so that our descendants will no longer endure the pain of war. »

« Yes, by that time, everyone will live peacefully in one nation. »

Just then, Gaara clapped his hands, drawing the attention of everyone present. « We are now facing a huge war, and this war will inevitably bring about huge casualties. Let’s discuss how to fight first ! »

Mei Terumī chimed in : « This time, Iwagakure and Kumogakure have joined forces, along with the Akatsuki Organization and various small Ninja Villages ; this force cannot be underestimated. They have four Jinchuriki and nearly forty thousand powerful Ninjas gathered together. We must absolutely not underestimate the enemy in this battle. »

As the Mizukage of the Hidden Mist Village, combined with her experience in multiple wars, Mei Terumī could be said to have a clear and objective understanding of the enemy’s strength.

« Especially Raikage Ai of Kumogakure and the two Jinchuriki—the Eight-Tails Gyuki and the Two-Tails Matatabi—as well as the Tsuchikage Ōnoki of Iwagakure and the two Jinchuriki—the Four-Tails Son Goku and the Five-Tails Kokuo. You must know that the reason Tailed Beasts are called war weapons is that the power of a Tailed Beast is enough to influence the entire battlefield ; this is not something that can be shaken by numbers alone. »

As Mei Terumī's voice faded, the slight pride the group felt before the war was shattered to pieces.

The four Jinchuriki alone could sway the tide of war. Even if Konoha’s strength was not weak at all, the casualties would be too great for those present to bear.

Suddenly, everyone fell silent ; after all, no one had any good solutions.

Then everyone looked at An, who was sitting in a chair. After all, it was An who had been planning everything that led to this situation.

Now everyone hoped that An could step forward with an idea.

Seeing this, An stood up and walked before the group, his face full of calmness, looking exceptionally indifferent.

Seeing this, the others calmed down, waiting for An to speak.

« Actually, there is an extremely powerful force we have overlooked, and that is the existence of the Akatsuki Organization. »

« The leader of the Akatsuki Organization, Nagato, is a powerhouse who surpasses the Kage of every Village. He possesses eyes like the Six Paths Sage, and it can be said that he is a very difficult guy to deal with. »

« And the main members of the Akatsuki Organization are composed of powerful S-rank rogue Ninjas from various Villages. It can be said that each of them possesses strength not inferior to that of a Tailed Beast. »

After An finished speaking, everyone’s expression sank. No one had expected a mercenary organization to have such powerful strength.

Just as the morale was getting lower and lower, Takayama Yume suddenly stood up.

« Lord An, if what you say is true, then I won’t be intimidated in the slightest. With the power you bestowed upon me, I believe I can hold off a Jinchuriki. »

« Yes, and I am confident that I can kill the Tsuchikage ! »

« Then I will kill the Raikage ! »

Uchiha Itachi and Shisui also stood up, clearly having absolute confidence in their own strength.

It could be said that the two of them now possessed the strength from the original plot, and with the addition of the Devil Fruit, what they said might really be possible to achieve.
