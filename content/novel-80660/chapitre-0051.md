# Chapitre 51 — Delivered to the Door!

Half a month later.

Inside Mizukage Mei Terumī's office.

An was skillfully massaging Mei Terumī, his gentle technique making her feel incomparably relaxed.

A faint fragrance filled the room, and the warm light cast soft spots on the walls, creating a tranquil and comfortable atmosphere.

It could be said that the two of them had now grown accustomed to the daily massage sessions, which were also an effective way for Mei Terumī to relax.

« Bang, bang, bang ! »

Just then, a series of urgent knocks on the door broke the pleasant atmosphere between An and Mei Terumī.

The two were slightly startled, and after exchanging a glance, they couldn’t help but feel a hint of awkwardness.

However, after the knocking stopped, Ao pushed the door open with a nervous look on his face.

Seeing Ao’s tense expression without him saying a word, Mei Terumī knew that Ao must have something to say ; otherwise, given Ao’s personality, he would have spoken directly.

Thus, Mei Terumī said to An in a slightly apologetic tone, « Newgate, let’s stop here for today ! You should go down and rest as well. »

Mei Terumī's words revealed a unique strength. An was slightly stunned, but he still obediently left the room.

However, as he left, An placed a listening device in his spot, preparing to listen to whether the following conversation between the two had anything to do with him.

After the door was gently closed by An, Ao exhaled in relief. He stepped into the room and sat opposite Mei Terumī.

At this moment, Mei Terumī looked at Ao with both curiosity and anticipation, waiting for him to speak his mind.

Seeing that An had completely left, Ao spoke urgently, « Mizukage-sama, there is news about Three-Tails Isobu. »

A flash of surprise crossed Mei Terumī's eyes, and she said with a hint of pleasant surprise, « Are you sure ? We have been searching for Three-Tails Isobu for half a month, and this matter has tied up too much of our Hidden Mist Village’s strength. »

Upon hearing this, Ao’s expression immediately became grave and serious : « Based on the last battle with Yagura-sama, we have investigated everything around that center, and finally found Three-Tails Isobu in a lake. It doesn’t belong to anyone right now, which is the perfect opportunity for us to recover Three-Tails Isobu. »

An, who was eavesdropping in the restroom, was somewhat exasperated. That guy Ao only mentioned a lake without giving a specific location ; how was An supposed to find it !

Mei Terumī took a deep breath, and she gazed at Ao with a firm look : « Ao, what do you think of Newgate ? »

Upon hearing Mei Terumī's words, Ao was stunned for a moment.

According to the previous agreement, all information about Three-Tails Isobu should be kept from Newgate.

Therefore, Ao didn’t understand why Mei Terumī was bringing up Newgate now, and a flash of confusion crossed Ao’s bewildered eyes.

Mei Terumī knew that Ao didn’t understand her intentions, so she explained, « I want to make Newgate the Three-Tails Jinchuriki. I don’t know if this idea is right or wrong. »

After listening to Mei Terumī's explanation, Ao quickly thought about the situation.

After a while, Ao firmly shook his head to refuse.

« Newgate has only joined the Hidden Mist Village for a little over half a month. It’s not that I don’t trust him, but he is not suitable to become the Three-Tails Jinchuriki. »

At this moment, An, who was listening in the restroom, also didn’t expect that after knowing each other for only a dozen days, Mei Terumī would dare to entrust Three-Tails Isobu to him, an outsider whose background was unknown.

It had to be said that this required immense courage and trust, which actually moved An a little.

Mei Terumī countered, « Then tell me, besides the powerful Newgate, who else is more suitable than him to serve as the Three-Tails Jinchuriki ? »

Instantly, Mei Terumī's question plunged Ao into silence.

Ao began to carefully consider the candidates around the Mizukage who could be trusted and were powerful, only to find that there were actually very few.

After all, among the guys who could be trusted, basically no one could suppress the Three-Tails with their strength.

And Ao also knew deeply that if the Three-Tails were in the hands of other Ninja clans, the Mizukage’s power would be even more restricted.

This thought made Ao feel increasingly troubled, because he was helpless about it.

Seeing Ao fall into confusion, Mei Terumī knew he was wavering, so she immediately said, « Newgate’s strength is very powerful ; he is careful and cautious, and he handles political affairs very well. His existence is far more important than a Three-Tails that could cause destruction at any time. »

Mei Terumī knew that doing this was a bit risky, but if one didn’t even dare to take risks, what qualifications did one have to hold the position of Mizukage ?

« Newgate has shown surprising talent and ability in a short time, but the Egao Clan is responsible for hunting the Three-Tails. I’m afraid Newgate will be besieged by them. »

« And even if Newgate can seize the Three-Tails, can he control it ? »

« I believe in him ! »

Mei Terumī understood that this decision had some risks, but she firmly believed that Newgate could handle this task and become an excellent Jinchuriki.

« Arrange for Newgate to meet with the Egao Clan tomorrow morning. You can lead trusted Anbu members to accompany him. If necessary, I allow you to take action at any time. »

« Yes ! Mizukage-sama ! »

Ao understood that this matter could no longer be changed, so he stopped arguing and could only do his best to ensure Newgate’s safety when the time came.

After the two finished speaking, An, who was listening in the restroom, quickly stood up, gently rubbed his numb legs, and then walked out, leaning against the wall.

At this moment, An deeply felt that the decision to infiltrate behind enemy lines was correct. He had been worrying about how to obtain information about Three-Tails Isobu, and he hadn’t expected that the information would be delivered to his doorstep.

When An walked back, he happened to meet Ao, who was coming out.

« We have a secret mission tomorrow. Please follow my instructions at that time ! »

An nodded to signal that he understood, and didn’t say anything more.

When An returned to the office and wanted to continue his massage business, Mei Terumī politely declined An’s request.

« There is an important mission you need to participate in this time. Pay attention to your safety and protect yourself. I will wait for you to come back and help me handle political affairs. »

As Mei Terumī spoke, her tone softened, clearly worried about An participating in the Three-Tails capture mission.

An nodded heavily, his eyes revealing a firm and resolute determination.

Even though An had absolute confidence in protecting himself, since the atmosphere was like this, it was better to cooperate a little.

Seeing that Mei Terumī was still a bit worried, An stepped forward and directly grasped Mei Terumī's hand tightly.

At that moment, Mei Terumī could feel the warmth from An’s hand, as if this simple and pure contact was conveying an indescribable strength.

The distance between the two was very close, and an ambiguous and tense atmosphere permeated the air.

An only felt that he had fallen deeply into this woman’s hands ; perhaps even if he obtained Three-Tails Isobu when the time came, he wouldn’t leave easily !
