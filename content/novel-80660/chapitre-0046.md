# Chapitre 46 — "Mizukage, my targets are the Three-Tails Jinchuriki and the Six-Tails Jinchuriki of the Hidden Mist Village."

« I heard they are invincible existences. Let either one of them fight me ; as long as they can defeat me, I will join the Hidden Mist Village. »

An punched his fists together, displaying a simple and valiant aura.

Mei Terumī and Ao looked at each other in embarrassment ; after all, no one in the current Hidden Mist Village possessed the power of a Tailed Beast.

Utakata, as the Six-Tails Jinchuriki, had long since left the Village, betraying the Hidden Mist Village to become a rogue Ninja. Furthermore, not long ago, the Three-Tails Jinchuriki Yagura had unfortunately passed away, and the whereabouts of the Three-Tails had become a mystery.

It could be said that there were no Tailed Beasts within the Hidden Mist Village at all, and too many people felt a sense of loss and emptiness.

In the past, Tailed Beasts symbolized power and status and were the Village’s precious weapons of war, but now, these precious existences had all disappeared.

« Ah ! What a pity ! Newgate, the Six-Tails Jinchuriki of the Hidden Mist Village, Utakata, has already defected. Only the Three-Tails Jinchuriki, Lord Yagura, remains in the Village, » Ao explained helplessly.

« However, Lord Yagura is currently executing a mission outside the Village, so he is not in the Hidden Mist Village for the time being. »

To make An stay, Ao could only think of one way : to hide the news of Yagura’s death and create an information gap.

Ao knew full well that this lie was not honorable, but for the future of the Hidden Mist Village, he had no other choice.

Seeing that Ao had already said this, Mei Terumī immediately chimed in, « That’s right, what Ao said is correct. Newgate, wait for Yagura to return and rest a bit, and then we will fight you. »

Although Mei Terumī felt embarrassed, she could only tacitly approve of this fabricated fact, because she understood that for the future of the Hidden Mist Village, one sometimes had to lie.

If the situation took a turn for the worse, the Hidden Mist Village would dispatch more Ninjas to search for the location of the Three-Tails Isobu and do their utmost to find a replacement candidate to ensure the power of the Three-Tails could be recovered.

This could be seen as part of the Tailed Beast recovery plan, where they could find a suitable candidate as soon as possible to let the Hidden Mist Village possess the protective power of a Tailed Beast once again.

« Fine, then I will wait for the Three-Tails Jinchuriki to return ! »

An’s tone was somewhat low, allowing Ao and Mei Terumī to intuitively feel An’s low spirits.

Although An knew that Yagura’s corpse was likely already cold and that the Three-Tails was not in the Hidden Mist Village.

The reason An said this was because he hoped to use the power of the Hidden Mist Village to find the whereabouts of the Three-Tails, so that he could swoop in and snatch it for himself later ; both sides were simply using each other.

« Then what do I need to do for the time being ? »

The expressions of joy on Ao and Mei Terumī's faces immediately froze ; they had only thought about how to fool An, but hadn’t thought about how to arrange for him.

However, An didn’t want to actually work himself to death for the Hidden Mist Village ; it was better to find a way out for himself.

« I think this place is nice. I’ll just stay in this office and protect the Mizukage ! Of course, the deserved compensation cannot be lacking. »

Hearing An’s suggestion, a flash of realization crossed the faces of Ao and Mei Terumī ; in this world, any problem that could be solved with money was not a problem at all.

The two looked at each other, conveying their thoughts with tacit understanding ; naturally, the sooner this matter was settled, the better.

Meanwhile, An was also very satisfied ; he had found a suitable job where he could admire the beautiful Mei Terumī while also earning compensation.

In this job, he could feast his eyes and achieve financial gain ; it could be described as the best of both worlds.

In short, both sides thought they had gotten the better end of the deal, which could be considered a true win-win situation.

« Hmph ! Mei Terumī, why did you strip our Egao Clan of our rights in the Ministry of Education, and why can only you appoint officials to the Ministry of Education ? »

« Could it be that you want to establish a dictatorship just after becoming the Mizukage ? Don’t forget that it was with our help that you were able to sit in the position of the Fifth Mizukage. »

An old man strode in directly and began sternly questioning Mei Terumī, his tone devoid of even the slightest respect for her.

Seeing this scene, An was a bit stunned and began to carefully observe this guy who had not appeared in the original plot.

This old man leaned on a primitive wooden cane, which was carved with patterns left by the passage of time.

And on his face was a deep scar, extending from his left eyebrow all the way to his right cheek. Although his youth had passed, that pair of sharp eyes still revealed an aura of ferocity and tenacity.

It could be said that this guy looked like someone not to be trifled with at a glance, and he likely possessed immense influence in the Hidden Mist Village.

« Clan Leader Jianfang, this is the Mizukage’s arrangement ; you have no right to question it ! »

Ao’s tone was very stern, and he had not a shred of respect for this Ninja clan leader named Jianfang ; it seemed that such situations happened quite often.

« Hmph ! Ao, when I was risking my life for the Hidden Mist Village, you weren’t even a Ninja yet ! Now I am speaking to the Mizukage ; you should just stick to your job as a bodyguard on the side. » After speaking, Jianfang looked at Mei Terumī behind the desk. « Mizukage, can you give me an explanation for this arrangement ! »

Mei Terumī observed the Jianfang before her, her brows slightly furrowed ; she was clearly quite impatient with this guy.

« Hey ! Old man, watch your attitude. You are talking to the Kage of a Village ! »

An’s sudden interjection directly broke the tense atmosphere in the office and simultaneously touched a nerve in Jianfang.

When Jianfang turned his head and saw An’s massive figure, a glimmer of astonishment and surprise flashed in his eyes.

An was a full three meters tall, causing Jianfang to feel an incomparable sense of oppression for a moment, while his heart was filled with questions and speculations about An’s identity.

However, Jianfang quickly recovered from his surprise, and the flames of anger began to burn in his heart.

For a long time, no one had dared to speak to him like this, leaving Jianfang feeling so disoriented, and his gaze was filled with rage and disdain.

« Who are you ? How dare you offend me like this ! »

Jianfang’s voice was full of majesty and oppression, deafening, and he stared fiercely at An with sharp eyes, attempting to suppress An’s aura.

« Since you ask so earnestly, I will answer you with great mercy. »

« To prevent the world from being destroyed, to protect the peace of the world, to carry out the evil of love and truth, the cute and charming righteous character, Crayon Shin-chan from the M78 Nebula. »

Listening to An’s words, and looking at An’s massive figure again, everyone couldn’t help but feel nauseated.

However, Mei Terumī did not want Jianfang to become interested in An, otherwise, wouldn’t her previous efforts have been in vain ?

« I will transfer the rights lost by the Egao Clan in the Ministry of Education to the Anbu ; the rights you deserve will not be lacking one bit. »

Seeing that Mei Terumī had given her promise, Egao Jianfang snorted coldly and left directly ; this matter could be considered to have come to an end.
