# Chapitre 73 — Success!

Ever since the team led by Uchiha An arrived at the border of the Land of Water, they had set up a temporary camp on an open grassland and began a peaceful and laid-back life.

Every morning, Uchiha An would personally lead a group of people to patrol, checking the surroundings for traces of enemies, while also training everyone’s physical fitness and combat skills.

After returning from patrol, they would enjoy a hearty breakfast prepared by Takayama Yume, then rest for a while.

At noon and in the evening, Uchiha An would organize training activities, such as combat drills and tactical research, to improve everyone’s combat effectiveness and coordination.

After training finished, it was time for dinner and sleep.

Every day, Takayama Yume would take a few people to the nearby seaside or forest to find various delicious seafood and fruits, improving everyone’s quality of life.

Because of this, Takayama Yume became increasingly popular within the team, and some people even scrambled to go with him to gather food.

The troops under Uchiha An’s command spent a long time on the border of the Land of Water like this, without encountering any danger or trouble, nor anything fresh or exciting.

It was another noon like any other, except this time, Takayama Yume was quite exhausted.

Takayama Yume could be seen cutting various fruits like grapes and pineapples into small pieces while arranging seafood platters on large plates, feeling secretly proud of himself.

Having been educated by Uchiha An, Takayama Yume naturally knew that eating these fruits and seafood together would cause indigestion or even diarrhea.

And this was exactly the method Takayama Yume intended to use to deal with his comrades, letting them experience the pain of stomach trouble while enjoying the food.

Then, Uchiha An led his subordinates to place these plates on a table in the center of the camp, covering them with a large cloth, pretending it was a prepared lunch.

Then, Uchiha An quietly left the camp with Kisame Hoshigaki, Tayuya, Takayama Yume, and a few others, hiding in the nearby woods to observe.

Before long, the Hidden Mist Ninja who had finished their training appeared in the camp.

Having just gone through grueling training, the group spotted the food on the table, and their eyes instantly lit up, their mouths watering. Without thinking twice, they picked up knives and forks and began to wolf down the food.

Watching them devour the food, Uchiha An chuckled inwardly. Very soon, these guys would learn the meaning of the distance between heaven and hell.

In the distance, Uchiha An, Kisame Hoshigaki, Tayuya, Takayama Yume, and a few others only ate some fruit, avoiding the rest, and continued to observe the movements of the Hidden Mist Ninja, waiting for things to ferment.

Soon, a chain reaction occurred.

Once the first guy went to the toilet with diarrhea, a second and a third followed immediately.

All the Hidden Mist Ninja on the scene began to feel their stomachs hurting as if they were about to explode, and they scrambled toward the toilets or the bushes.

Some couldn’t even make it in time and defecated right on the ground.

The entire camp instantly turned into a foul-smelling, polluted zone.

« Let’s go ! I’m afraid this place won’t be suitable for us to stay for a long time, » Kisame Hoshigaki said, wrinkling his nose.

Kisame Hoshigaki already had a very keen sense of smell, and this stench was truly unbearable for him ; he felt as if he were about to pass out from the smell.

Upon hearing this, Tayuya nodded repeatedly ; this scene was truly too distressing for her.

Especially the smell in the air ; it made Tayuya feel like she was about to vomit from disgust.

Uchiha An smiled and patted their shoulders. « Don’t worry, we’ve already completed the mission. These guys have no combat power left. We just need to wait for Hyuga Hizashi and Uchiha Shisui to bring their men to deal with them. »

With that, he led everyone away from this scenic spot, waiting for the Konoha team to take over.

...

In the afternoon.

When Hyuga Hizashi and Uchiha Shisui arrived with their team, everyone couldn’t help but vomit.

The once scenic and pleasant territory was now covered in a foul stench.

As for the camp, indescribable liquids and figures collapsed from diarrhea were visible everywhere ; this sight thoroughly disgusted everyone.

« Shisui, was this Uchiha An’s idea ? It seems the effect is quite good, it’s just that... »

Hyuga Hizashi finished speaking and ran to a tree to vomit, and this act triggered a chain reaction, with everyone else unable to help but vomit as well.

By the time Hyuga Hizashi regained his composure, he realized his own team had also lost most of their combat effectiveness.

It could be said that this move was a double-edged sword, damaging the enemy by a thousand while hurting oneself by eight hundred—not something to be used often !

« The methods of the Uchiha Clan are truly indescribable, » Hyuga Hizashi lamented.

Although Hyuga Hizashi admired Uchiha An’s intelligence and courage, he also felt that such methods were too sinister and cruel.

Uchiha Shisui didn’t know how Uchiha An had come up with this idea, nor did he know how Uchiha An had pulled it off.

Uchiha Shisui only knew that if this incident spread, it wouldn’t be good for the reputation of the Uchiha Clan.

« Uchiha An’s actions this time can only represent him personally ; in this matter, he does not represent the Uchiha Clan. »

Thus, Uchiha Shisui instantly decided to throw Uchiha An under the bus. Although the method had achieved stunning results, it sounded terrible when spread. He was doing this to preserve the reputation of the Uchiha Clan, and he trusted that Uchiha An would understand.

After comforting himself, Uchiha Shisui and the others began to clear the battlefield, rounding up the Hidden Mist Ninja who were now unable to resist.

It could be said that Uchiha An’s plan was highly successful. By simply using a mixture of fruits and seafood, he had directly incapacitated the Hidden Mist Ninja, causing the Hidden Mist Village and even the Land of Water to lose their defensive forces in most of the western territory.

After the task was completed, Uchiha An left without the slightest hesitation. Now, Uchiha An only thought about how to provide more intelligence for the Konoha army, allowing Konoha to conquer the Land of Water faster and more easily.

In the subsequent war, Uchiha An used his communicator to keep in touch with the Konoha command center, constantly reporting the movements, positions, troop strengths, and other intelligence of the Hidden Mist Ninja.

Uchiha An even secretly sabotaged some important facilities of the Hidden Mist Ninja, such as warehouses and water sources.

With Uchiha An’s intelligence sharing, Konoha swept through the Land of Water, and even the Land of Water’s Daimyo’s residence, which had weakened defenses, was easily captured by the Konoha forces.

Under these circumstances, the Konoha Ninja who participated in the battle were filled with respect and gratitude for Uchiha An, believing that Uchiha An was the most outstanding and reliable leader of Konoha.

It could be said that in the Land of Water, apart from the team led by Ao and Hozuki Lanyue at the northern border, only the Hidden Mist Village remained uncaptured by Konoha.

Everyone representing Konoha unanimously decided to concentrate all their efforts on attacking the Hidden Mist Village, striving to end this war as soon as possible.

Uchiha An had also prepared his final plan—a plan fraught with extreme risk, yet one that could potentially subdue the Hidden Mist Village with minimal cost.
