# Chapitre 41 — Little Hinata!

« I am Uchiha An, a member of the Uchiha Clan. »

« Hinata, as the eldest young lady of the Hyuga Clan, did your clan not send anyone to protect you ? »

Hearing An’s question, Hinata lowered her head, looking slightly embarrassed, and poked her index fingers together, feeling at a loss.

« Actually, I snuck out. No one in my family knows I left. »

An hadn’t expected young Hinata to be so bold as to dare sneak out of the house at just four years old.

However, since they had met by chance, An couldn’t bear to leave four-year-old Hinata here.

Looking up at the sky, An estimated it was nearly noon.

« Let’s go, Hinata ! I’ll treat you to a meal. »

« Mm-hmm ! Thank you, Brother An. » Hinata replied happily.

Then, An took Hinata’s small hand, and the two arrived at a nearby restaurant. Thanks to An’s 'money power,' the table was soon filled with all kinds of delicacies.

Hinata happily ordered a sumptuous sushi platter and some fragrant dango, her eyes sparkling with excitement.

When all the food was served, Hinata didn’t hesitate to start tasting it.

She chewed each piece of sushi in small bites, her brow slightly furrowed as if savoring the flavor.

She finished every dango cleanly, her facial expressions shifting constantly, looking as cute as a little hamster.

Watching Hinata eat, An couldn’t help but feel a surge of tenderness, finding it a happy and warm thing to be protecting such an innocent and lively little girl.

Of course, this was the 'old father' mentality An had possessed since he was eight ; mostly, it was just that young Hinata was too cute !

Finally, the delicacies on the table were eaten clean by Hinata and An—though mainly by Hinata, while An was just enjoying the view.

Suddenly, Hinata let out an embarrassed burp. She pursed her lips and looked at An, saying with a hint of apology.

« Brother An, did I eat too much ? »

An smiled and shook his head, replying gently, « Not at all, not at all. How can you grow properly if you don’t eat enough ! »

However, An kept one thought to himself : If you don’t eat enough, how will you be able to wear a qipao in the future ?

When An and Hinata walked out of the restaurant, the sun was already high in the sky.

Hinata yawned, looking like a tired little kitten.

« Now I need to take you back. Don’t sneak out to play by yourself again in the future. »

Hinata nodded repeatedly to show her agreement, knowing well that if this big brother hadn’t appeared, she might have truly fallen into danger.

An and Hinata walked silently toward the Hyuga Clan grounds, the two figures accompanying each other, chatting about various topics.

Their pace gradually became lighter, and Hinata’s small figure followed by An’s side, listening to him tell his stories.

Along the way, the two shared their joys and sorrows, and their relationship progressed rapidly.

« Brother An, can I come find you to play often in the future ? »

There was a hint of longing in Hinata’s voice, which made An feel unable to refuse.

An gently reached out and patted Hinata’s little head, revealing a touch of kindness and comfort.

« Of course you can, Hinata. You can come find me anytime. »

These words brought a bright smile to Hinata’s face, her eyes filled with joy and anticipation.

Yet, a thought emerged in An’s mind—he still had a Devil Fruit suitable for Hinata stored in his system space.

« Hinata, Brother An wants to give you a very important gift, but I only want your father to know about it, okay ? »

An had originally thought Hinata would gladly accept his gift, but she unexpectedly shook her head, indicating a refusal.

« Brother An saved me before, and now you want to give me such an important gift ; Hinata doesn’t think she can accept it. »

« Then, next time we meet, can you give me a gift that you find very important ? Let’s just treat it as a little secret between us ! » An proposed gently.

In an instant, a conflicted look appeared on Hinata’s face, and after thinking for a good while, she nodded in agreement.

Seeing that Hinata had agreed, a Paramecia-type Weapon Fruit instantly appeared in An’s hand.

In truth, An also wanted to see what kind of sparks would fly when a Barrett sniper rifle combined with the Hyuga Clan’s Byakugan.

Hinata gazed curiously at the fruit that had suddenly appeared in An’s hand, her eyes revealing an interest in exploring.

« Brother An, what kind of fruit is this ? Hinata has never seen it before. » Hinata asked.

An crouched down and subconsciously rubbed Hinata’s tender, smooth little face.

« Eat one bite of this fruit, and you will gain a unique power. But remember, just one bite is enough. » An reminded her gently.

An didn’t want Hinata to eat the whole Devil Fruit like Shisui and Uchiha Itachi did, otherwise, it would just be suffering.

Hearing An’s words, although Hinata was full of confusion, she did not refuse his kindness.

Next, An began to explain the development of the Weapon Fruit to Hinata, and time passed without them realizing it.

Before long, the two arrived at the territory of the Hyuga Clan, and a tall middle-aged man suddenly ran out.

« Eldest young lady, please don’t sneak out by yourself again, okay ? »

« Yes, Uncle Hizashi. »

Hyuga Hizashi looked suspiciously at the fruit in Hinata’s hand.

Seeing this, Hinata immediately explained : « This is a gift from Brother An. »

« An ? »

Hyuga Hizashi only now noticed the figure beside her.

An, however, knew that Hyuga Hizashi was still alive thanks to his own efforts ; because the Nine-Tailed incident hadn’t occurred, the Cloud Ninja naturally wouldn’t come to Konoha to sign any armistice agreement, so the subsequent events hadn’t happened.

« Thank you for bringing the eldest young lady back, An. »

« Mm ! Since Hinata is back, I shall take my leave. »

After An finished speaking, he turned around, prepared to leave.

Hinata watched An’s retreating figure, her eyes filled with reluctance. A surge of courage welled up in her heart, and she shouted loudly.

« Brother An, wait for a while ! Hinata will come to find you to play ! »

Hinata’s voice rang out in the air, her tone filled with determination and resolve.

Hyuga Hizashi stared in astonishment at the figure beside him, never having expected that the usually timid and shy eldest young lady would have such a bold side.

« Alright, then I’ll wait for you ! »

In reality, An didn’t give the Paramecia-type Weapon Fruit to Hinata because she possessed a series of virtues like gentleness, cuteness, and beauty.

Rather, it was because An hoped that the Hyuga Clan would owe him a favor, which he would inevitably be able to make use of when the time came.

In the time that followed, An stayed in Konoha, focusing on cultivating his various methods.

If he had nothing to do, he would spend time with his family or get familiar with the major ninja clans.

If he really couldn’t decline, he would occasionally leave the village to complete a mission ; one could say that An’s semi-retired life was spent very comfortably.
