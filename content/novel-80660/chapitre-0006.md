# Chapitre 6 — Fierce Battle and Rescue!

Jonin Toroi and his five subordinates, who were Chunin, appeared in Uchiha An’s sight.

Just as Uchiha An was sizing up the group of Kumogakure Ninjas, they were also sizing him up ; no one expected a young child to suddenly appear on the battlefield.

« The Uchiha Clan crest, a child appearing on the battlefield—your status within the Uchiha Clan must not be simple ! »

Jonin Toroi’s analysis was flawless ; he was basically able to deduce Uchiha An’s identity.

Uchiha An was slightly surprised internally. Who said the intelligence of the natives must be lower than that of transmigrators ? Jonin Toroi’s performance didn’t show any signs of being feeble-minded.

However, Uchiha An was very uncomfortable being held by Sarutobi Kazekaze and instantly broke free from his restraint.

« What is the strength of this group of Kumogakure Ninjas ? »

Uchiha An would not fight an unprepared battle, so he asked for intelligence on the Kumogakure Ninjas.

« The one with purple hair is Jonin Toroi from Kumogakure, and the other five are Chunin from Kumogakure. These are not existences you can fight ; I will cover your retreat immediately. »

« Mere Kumogakure Ninjas, that is all. »

Uchiha An leaped down from the tree and landed steadily on the ground.

He picked up a katana from the ground—he didn’t know whose it was—struck Zenitsu Agatsuma’s classic pose, and began to crouch and gather power.

This scene made the people from Kumogakure roar with laughter.

« Uchiha Clan brat, are you trying to kill us with that sword ? Be careful not to cut yourself. »

« I thought it would be some big fish, but it turns out to be a fool. Could the Uchiha Clan have thrown you onto the battlefield to die on purpose ! »

Several Kumogakure Ninjas laughed at Uchiha An without restraint. After all, a four or five-year-old child holding a katana as long as his own height and saying he wanted to kill someone was truly hilarious.

As for Uchiha An, who was being mocked, he didn’t care about the ridicule and instead silently observed the positions of the few.

« Thunder Breathing, First Form : Thunderclap and Flash, Sixfold ! »

Of course, Uchiha An naturally didn’t know any Thunder Breathing ; it was just cool to shout it out.

In an instant, Uchiha An’s figure vanished from where he stood.

After a flash of lightning, one Chunin clutched his neck and fell to the ground, dying shortly after from excessive blood loss.

[Ding ! Congratulations to the host for completing the first kill. Reward : one lottery chance.]

[Ding ! Congratulations to the host for slaying a Chunin. Reward : one lottery chance.]

Uchiha An didn’t expect that the system, which had been silent for four years, would finally pop up, and it even gave him two lottery chances directly.

The most important thing right now was the battle, so Uchiha An immediately focused his mind.

The other Kumogakure Ninjas were shocked to see this ; who could have imagined that a four-year-old child could easily slay a Chunin.

And in the moment they were stunned, the battle continued.

Uchiha An, with extreme speed, swung his blade again.

Bang ! Bang ! Bang ! Bang !

The sound of bodies hitting the ground rang out continuously ; the other four Chunin from Kumogakure fell to the ground in the same posture as the first Chunin who died.

Finally, using his absolute speed, Uchiha An immediately swung his blade at the last person, who was also the Kumogakure Jonin, Jonin Toroi.

Clang !

After a crisp sound, the katana in Uchiha An’s hand was snapped directly by Jonin Toroi using a Kunai. Uchiha An was sent flying backward, a trace of blood spilling from the corner of his mouth.

« Fortunately, I could see your movements ; otherwise, I would have died easily at your hands just like them. » Then, Jonin Toroi changed the subject, « To possess such a powerful Lightning Release at four years old—killing you would make the Uchiha Clan, or perhaps all of Konoha, feel the pain ! »

Uchiha An’s performance just now had thoroughly frightened Jonin Toroi, which only strengthened his resolve to kill Uchiha An.

Uchiha An was getting anxious now. The only advantage he could rely on was speed ; in terms of stamina, Ninjutsu, and Taijutsu, the gap between him and Jonin Toroi was too large.

Moreover, Uchiha An also knew that he currently hadn’t realized the potential of the Rumble-Rumble Fruit. Otherwise, with the speed of the Rumble-Rumble Fruit, how could a mere Jonin block him ?

« Wind Release : Wind Cutting Slash ! »

In an instant, dozens of giant wind blades, resembling long swords, slashed vertically toward Jonin Toroi.

Seeing this, Jonin Toroi immediately formed hand seals to perform the Substitution Jutsu.

In an instant, dozens of wind blades shredded Jonin Toroi’s body.

Poof !

A piece of broken wood was shredded into pieces.

« The Uchiha Clan has produced another peerless genius. I will help you from here on ! However, I can’t cast many more Ninjutsu, so let’s end this quickly ! »

Uchiha An nodded and immediately stood side-by-side with Sarutobi Kazekaze.

« Distract him ! »

Uchiha An’s loud shout signaled the start of the battle, and Sarutobi Kazekaze understood Uchiha An’s intention.

Sarutobi Kazekaze lowered his body, quickly formed hand seals, and Chakra continuously gathered in his mouth.

Uchiha An moved continuously around Jonin Toroi, attracting his attention.

« Wind Release : Vacuum Wave ! »

Kamaitachi-like wind blades slashed at Jonin Toroi from all directions, cutting gullies dozens of centimeters deep into the ground.

« Magnet Release : Manipulated Windmill Shuriken ! »

Jonin Toroi instantly threw two giant Windmill Shuriken to protect himself, then used Magnet Release to control the two Windmill Shuriken to slash at the Kamaitachi-like wind blades.

Boom ! Boom ! Boom !

The huge roars resounded through the sky, kicking up clouds of dust.

As the dust gradually dissipated, Jonin Toroi’s figure slowly appeared.

Seeing this, Sarutobi Kazekaze immediately performed the Body Flicker Jutsu and flew forward.

Clang !

Unfortunately, Sarutobi Kazekaze’s Kunai was directly blocked by Jonin Toroi.

« Trying to sneak attack me... »

Jonin Toroi had just finished speaking when he felt a pain in his abdomen.

Uchiha An’s figure appeared directly behind Jonin Toroi ; he had taken advantage of the cover of the dust to get here and decisively swung his blade.

« Die ! »

After saying this, Uchiha An violently pulled out the blade, bringing up a large spray of blood that splashed all over his face.

[Ding ! Congratulations to the host for slaying a Jonin. Reward : one super guaranteed lottery chance.]

Feeling his life slipping away, Jonin Toroi let out a roar.

« Do you think you’ve won ? »

The Windmill Shuriken that had been on the ground hovered in the air again, then flew toward Uchiha An’s right side at extreme speed.

A small figure appeared in everyone’s eyes.

« Uchiha Itachi ! »

Uchiha Itachi, looking at this scene, stood frozen in place, as if he had completely forgotten he knew the Substitution Jutsu.

Seeing this, Uchiha An immediately rushed toward Uchiha Itachi at full speed. A tearing sensation made Uchiha An feel excruciating pain ; he was afraid his body was already severely injured.

After all, Uchiha An was only four years old and did not have a strong enough physique to withstand the extreme speed of the Rumble-Rumble Fruit.

Bang !

Finally, at the last moment, Uchiha An’s right hand grabbed the giant Windmill Shuriken, and blood dripped down the blade of the Windmill Shuriken onto Uchiha Itachi’s face.

Then, Uchiha An lost consciousness. Just as he was about to fall to the ground, he felt a hint of warmth.

A rough, large hand picked Uchiha An up.

Uchiha Fugaku watched the blood slowly seep out of Uchiha An’s body, and tears flowed from his eyes involuntarily.

« Clean up the battlefield ! »

Following Uchiha Fugaku’s order, the brought Konoha Ninja began to handle the bodies of their comrades and collect intelligence on the battle.
