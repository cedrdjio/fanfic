# Chapitre 3 — Participating in the Third Ninja World War!

Fugaku’s old face instantly flushed red.

« What I just demonstrated was a very basic Kunai throwing technique. Next, I will show you the throwing arts of our Uchiha Clan. »

Then, Fugaku picked up nine Kunai, leapt into the air, and threw the Kunai in his hands with incredible speed.

Bang ! Bang ! Bang !

Seven Kunai struck the center of the target directly.

« Ding ! » The sound of metal clashing rang out. The eighth Kunai violently struck the ninth, altering the trajectory of the ninth Kunai and causing it to embed itself in the tree behind the target.

« Father is so amazing ! » Itachi watched this scene with great interest, his tender little face filled with admiration for his father.

It seemed that Fugaku’s performance just now had a great impact on the four-year-old Itachi.

Noticing his son’s reaction, Fugaku was very satisfied, and the corners of his calm mouth curled up slightly.

« Itachi, after a period of training, you will be able to do this too. » Then, Fugaku’s expression shifted abruptly, and he looked angrily at An, who was digging for boogers on the side. « An, why are you indifferent to this ? Is it because I didn’t explain it well enough ? »

An remained calm as he flicked the booger off his pinky finger.

In the Ninja World, there is a principle that Kunai don’t kill people ; rarely have any powerhouses died at the hands of a Kunai.

« Dad, that was cool, but have you ever killed any powerhouses using this move ? »

Fugaku was just about to say something when his expression suddenly turned ugly.

Thinking back carefully, it seemed that no powerhouses had died from his Kunai throwing technique. Of course, this move was still useful.

« Your father has killed quite a few Genin and Chunin with this move. »

« So that means you haven’t killed a single Special Jonin or Jonin ? »

Hearing his own son expose his shortcomings, Fugaku became even angrier.

« You just need to follow along and train properly ! Now, both of you, practice with me. »

Since he couldn’t disobey his father, An had to start basic Kunai throwing training with Fugaku.

However, Itachi seemed very happy to have his father’s company.

Just like that, An and Itachi began refining Chakra and practicing basic Taijutsu every day.

In reality, most people in the Ninja World don’t value Taijutsu, but basically all Ninjas perform some Taijutsu training.

Time passed day by day as An and Itachi trained, and Fugaku started coming home later and later.

Half a month later.

Fugaku sat at the dining table with a heavy expression, and Mikoto’s complexion didn’t look too good either.

Perhaps sensing that the atmosphere at the scene was not good, An didn’t dare to mess around with Itachi anymore.

« Father ! Mother ! Has something happened ? »

The precocious Itachi naturally saw that something was wrong, so he asked directly.

« The Sandaime Kazekage of Sunagakure has gone missing. Sunagakure suspects it was done by the other four great Ninja villages. Coupled with the increasingly tense relations between the Five Great Ninja Villages, war is coming. »

« Konoha is already making preparations in all aspects, and I need to lead the Uchiha Clan onto the battlefield. »

« Mikoto, take good care of An and Itachi at home. Try not to go out for a while. »

The atmosphere at the dining table instantly became heavy.

War means sacrifice ; I fear that this time, many families in the Uchiha Clan will be hurt again.

« Then let me and my foolish little brother go to the battlefield to see for ourselves. »

An’s statement stunned the three of them ; Mikoto and Fugaku stared at their son in shock.

Itachi also looked at his older brother in great surprise. Even though he had that intention himself, Itachi knew that his father and mother would not easily let him go to the battlefield.

« An, you’re only four years old ! What battlefield ? I absolutely do not allow it. »

« Your mother is right. You don’t have the strength to protect yourself on the battlefield. You’d better stay at home and keep your mother company ! »

Clearly, neither Fugaku nor Mikoto thought it was possible to let two four-year-old children go to the battlefield.

Upon hearing this, Itachi’s expression drooped ; he was likely disappointed that he couldn’t go to the battlefield.

Seeing this, An hurriedly put food into his father’s and mother’s bowls. « Everything is negotiable ! As long as Itachi and I have enough strength, then we can go to the battlefield to see, right ? »

Hearing An’s words, a look of hesitation appeared on Fugaku’s face.

Refusing directly seemed like it would hurt the two little ones, but he also wasn’t at ease taking them along.

« Three days from now, I will lead the Uchiha Clan to the battlefield. If you two brothers can master any one of the Three Body Techniques before then, I will take you both to the battlefield to see. »

As E-rank Ninjutsu, the Three Body Techniques are not difficult, but for two four-year-old children, it is enough for them to learn for a while.

Of course, if An and Itachi could master any one of the Three Body Techniques within three days, they would have a sliver of self-preservation ability on the battlefield, and he would be able to better protect the two brothers.

An and Itachi looked at each other and smiled upon hearing this. With the two brothers' talent and their persistent refining of Chakra, mastering one of the Three Body Techniques was a piece of cake.

So, An and Itachi quickly finished dinner and immediately returned to their small room to start training.

« My foolish little brother, which Ninjutsu are you planning to learn first ? »

Itachi held the Three Body Techniques scroll and began to struggle with the decision. Mastering one in three days would be enough ; at the very least, he should become proficient in one of the techniques.

« I want to practice the Transformation Technique first ! »

An decisively reached out again to pinch Itachi’s aloof little face.

« Then I’ll practice the Transformation Technique too. If there’s anything you don’t know, you can ask your older brother ! »

After a brief discussion, An and Itachi began their training.

The reason An chose to practice the Transformation Technique, one of the Three Body Techniques, was because it was the most useful to him.

Having eaten the Rumble-Rumble Fruit, he could elementalize, making the Substitution Technique basically useless for him.

As for the Clone Technique, it wasn’t very useful, so An was more interested in the Transformation Technique, which allowed him to take on the appearance of others.

... Three days later.

The two brothers, having gone through strenuous training, sat at the dining table.

« How is it ? How did you two brothers do with your practice ? If you haven’t learned any Ninjutsu, you cannot follow your father to the battlefield. »

An and Itachi looked at each other and smiled, then simultaneously released the Transformation Technique.

It was clear that Itachi had transformed into An, and An had transformed into Itachi.

« Our sons are truly geniuses ! They mastered Ninjutsu at only four years old. »

Mikoto gently stroked An and Itachi’s heads, very satisfied with the brothers' performance.

Seeing this, Fugaku couldn’t say anything more ; after all, both sons had met his requirements.

« Since you two have mastered the Transformation Technique so well, you are qualified to come with me to the battlefield to see. »

As he spoke, Fugaku changed the subject. « However, you must not stray more than ten steps away from me, so that I can better protect you both. »

« Yes, thank you, Father ! » (X2)
