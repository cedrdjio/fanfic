# Chapitre 45 — Arriving at Hidden Mist Village, Mizukage Mei Terumī!

The next day.

Early in the morning, Ao led An to set off for the Hidden Mist Village.

Shortly after leaving the base, Ao began to accelerate, quickly leaving An behind.

An felt somewhat speechless about this ; after all, this guy was much older than him yet acted so childishly.

However, An did not want to lose to Ao easily, so he immediately accelerated to catch up.

The two leaped over one tall tree after another, moving so fast that the animals in the forest couldn’t even react in time.

Unfortunately, An’s three-meter-tall physique became a hindrance at this moment, occasionally snapping branches and slowing him down.

Seeing this, Ao chuckled more smugly, secretly thinking that his strategy was correct.

After all, with such a massive build, Newgate would inevitably lack speed, and he could certainly win back points on this front.

The two continued to run through the dense jungle, both fully immersed in the contest, sprinting forward as they nimbly wove through the lush woods, their speed increasing ever more.

Although An had won a round before, he didn’t want to lose to this guy this time.

« Secret Technique : Bone Shrinking ! »

An gave a symbolic shout and activated the ability of the Imitation-Imitation Fruit, directly transforming into Super Vegeta.

Next, An unleashed his full strength without reservation, closing in on Ao’s figure step by step.

In this way, An was actually able to fully utilize his height advantage, striding forward to catch up.

Ao sensed An’s approaching presence ; although curious about An’s methods, he didn’t want to lose easily either.

Thus, Ao also quickened his pace, trying hard to maintain his lead.

However, the competition of speed did not depend solely on physical stature.

An skillfully adjusted his movements, nimbly dodging the branches that appeared at any moment, and kept closing in on Ao.

Leveraging his stamina and speed advantage, he gritted his teeth, suddenly accelerated in a sprint, and left Ao directly behind him.

« Lord Ao, I’ll be heading ahead then, waiting for you up front. »

Hearing An’s voice, Ao felt secretly resentful, but unfortunately, he had no ability to accelerate again.

After gaining a bit of distance, An quietly activated the ability of the Rumble-Rumble Fruit, dashing away and leaving everyone in the dust.

...

A quarter of an hour later.

An had successfully gotten lost and could only wait helplessly for Ao’s arrival.

When Ao caught up with An, he glanced at him and continued walking forward.

Because the two had been racing before, they had already covered a long distance ; afterward, An and Ao traveled at a normal pace and finally arrived at the hidden Hidden Mist Village at nightfall.

Upon entering, the Hidden Mist Village was filled with mist like a loom, and at night, the entire Village was shrouded in a gray veil.

The walls of the Hidden Mist Village flickered in and out of sight, like the body of a giant beast, with a gloomy atmosphere permeating the area, maximizing the sense of horror.

Below the walls, the moat was deep and heavy, seemingly hiding endless darkness and deathly silence, with pale moonlight spilling onto the winding water surface, further revealing a terrifying mystery.

Coupled with the swaying shadows of trees in the distance, it was as if ghosts were floating about, emitting a chilling aura.

It could be said that if the Hidden Mist Village were developed into a ghost town, it would surely make a fortune.

« Let’s go in ! »

Ao strode towards the direction of the Hidden Mist Village, with An following closely behind him ; throughout the way, not a single person greeted them.

Clearly, the Hidden Mist Village was still deeply affected by the Bloody Mist policy, which caused the entire Village to be shrouded in a dull atmosphere.

However, everyone who passed by couldn’t help but take an extra look at An, because in the Ninja World, a three-meter-tall figure was truly rare, enough to be eye-catching.

And An also heard the residents by the roadside whispering, some even giving a thumbs up, secretly admiring An’s might and strength.

However, as the two went deeper into the interior of the Hidden Mist Village, An could no longer hear any noisy sounds.

Under Ao’s guidance, An soon arrived at a towering building, where a huge « Water » character sign hung in front of the building.

Gradually, some Ninjas who knew Ao took the initiative to greet him, and even curious guys kept asking who the An behind Ao was.

These Ninjas of the Hidden Mist Village showed strong interest and curiosity about An’s appearance.

« This is where the Mizukage works ; if you have any conditions, you can talk directly to the Mizukage. »

Ao said this and led An directly inside, but when An entered the door, he had to lower his head.

After stepping into the office, An discovered the unusual design, and the scene before him made An feel very surprised.

The design here was exceptionally modern, with busy Ninjas everywhere, shuttling between their respective posts, some reviewing files, others communicating.

The entire space looked as if it had been carefully planned ; under the bright lights, it gave the room a sense of vitality and energy.

Large screens were embedded in the walls, displaying various real-time information and mission instructions.

It could be said that it was not much different from the office environment when An was a corporate slave in his previous life, giving An a feeling of having lived a lifetime ago.

« The one inside is the Mizukage. »

An also noticed the deepest part of this floor, which should be the office of the Mizukage, Mei Terumī.

Because in front of the office door stood two Ninjas wearing the Hidden Mist Village forehead protectors, standing guard to symbolically protect the safety of Mei Terumī.

Pushing open the office door, An and Ao entered a spacious and luxurious space directly, where the Mizukage, Mei Terumī, sat in a high-backed chair wearing the Mizukage attire, with a look on her face that seemed solemn yet dignified.

« Ao, you’re back. How is the border ? There shouldn’t have been any problems, right ! »

Mei Terumī's voice was soft and pleasant, like a spring breeze brushing over swaying willow branches.

Her voice contained a kind of warmth and soothing power, capable of calming people’s souls.

However, behind this beautiful voice, An could clearly feel a hint of exhaustion and vicissitudes.

At the moment when the Bloody Mist policy had just ended, everything revolved tightly around the Mizukage, Mei Terumī, which was why it was like this.

« Mizukage-sama, this is Newgate, whom I encountered by chance while inspecting. He is a strong fighter who can fight on par with me. »

« Under my persuasion, Newgate is also willing to join our Hidden Mist Village. »

A rare smile appeared on Mei Terumī's face ; now, having one more combatant was a huge help to her.

Especially An’s three-meter-tall figure, which inexplicably gave Mei Terumī a sense of security.

« Newgate, I am the Mizukage of the Hidden Mist Village, Mei Terumī. Welcome to the Hidden Mist Village. If you have any conditions, you can bring them up at any time. As long as it does not violate my principles, all your conditions can be met. »

An almost blurted out, « I need you, » but fortunately, he restrained himself, otherwise this would inevitably be a major social suicide scene for him.
