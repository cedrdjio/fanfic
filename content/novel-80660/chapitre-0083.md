# Chapitre 83 — An Ending That Isn't the End!

Uchiha An’s voice resonated across the sky like a judgment, chilling to the bone.

As Uchiha An finished speaking, the form of the Lightning Giant gradually dimmed, as if it were about to dissipate into thin air.

But just then, a shocking flash of lightning erupted from the belly of the Lightning Giant, condensing into a giant lightning hand far more solid than the giant itself, radiating a power capable of destroying everything.

« Break ! »

Uchiha An shouted coldly. The giant lightning hand immediately slammed forward, shattering the defense of the Susanoo formed by Uchiha Madara and easily crushing that Complete Body Susanoo.

Uchiha Madara watched in shock as his Susanoo was destroyed, powerless to stop the giant lightning hand from clutching him in its palm, feeling unprecedented pain and fear.

The next moment, an intensely dazzling lightning flash bloomed in the sky like a supernova, completely swallowing Uchiha Madara’s figure.

Covered by the lightning, Uchiha Madara let out a shrill scream, his body charred black and mangled, as if he had been burned into a lump of coal.

Then, Uchiha Madara’s figure plummeted from the sky like a meteor, crashing heavily into the snow and sending up a spray of blood-stained snow.

« Is the war over ? There won’t be any more Ninja World Wars, right ? »

« Unifying the Ninja World and ending the war—he is an existence that surpasses the God of Shinobi. »

« Long live Uchiha An ! »

This is a passage describing the aftermath of the battle between Uchiha An and Uchiha Madara. I tried to polish and expand it with more detailed language, and I hope you like it.

« Long live Uchiha An ! »

The crowd present, witnessing Uchiha An’s astonishing blow, couldn’t help but cheer, celebrating his victory.

But Uchiha An had no time to mind the clamor ; his figure slowly descended from the sky, landing on the snow and leaving behind a deep footprint.

Unlike the excitement and relaxation of everyone else present, Uchiha An remained cautious, sensing the figure below, his eyes revealing a hint of vigilance and unease.

Uchiha An’s Observation Haki remained locked onto Uchiha Madara’s life force, and he could clearly feel that Uchiha Madara on the ground was repairing his damaged body at an extremely fast speed ; his flesh, bones, and organs were constantly regenerating.

Uchiha An knew this battle was not over yet ; he had to finish him off as soon as possible.

« Konoha Ultimate Secret Technique : Thousand Years of Death ! »

« Bastard ! »

A roar erupted from the charred corpse on the ground, and immediately after, the body suddenly sprang up like a coil, leaping several meters away and dodging Uchiha An’s lightning attack.

« Hehehe ! Uchiha An, I underestimated you before, but this time you cannot escape. Now, let me show you the power of the Rinnegan. »

« Limbo ! »

Uchiha Madara said, closing in on Uchiha An with extreme speed to strike.

The four invisible clones controlled by Uchiha Madara also attacked simultaneously, striking at Uchiha An from four different directions. Each clone possessed the same power and skills as Uchiha Madara, and they were near-unbeatable existences created by Uchiha Madara using the Rinnegan.

The five figures attacked at once, directly sealing off all directions Uchiha An could dodge, forming an inescapable cage.

« Hahaha ! Madara ! Let me show you my power. »

Uchiha Madara laughed wildly, the light of red Sharingan flashing in his eyes.

The red Sharingan swirled within Uchiha An’s eyes, making him look incredibly wicked and charming.

« Mangekyo Sharingan ? Do you think this can defeat me ? »

Uchiha Madara didn’t care about Uchiha An’s methods. Regarding the development of the Sharingan, Uchiha Madara considered himself to have no rival. He even felt that Uchiha An was just stalling for time, trying to find an opportunity to escape.

Uchiha An, upon hearing this, sneered and watched Uchiha Madara silently.

Just as Uchiha Madara thought he had locked onto Uchiha An, Uchiha An suddenly dodged the attacks from five directions at an odd angle, causing a flash of disbelief in Uchiha Madara’s eyes.

Immediately after, Uchiha An threw a right punch covered in Armament Haki, a black fist shadow smashing toward Uchiha Madara’s face like a meteor, leaving Uchiha Madara dazed.

This punch was so fast that it pierced directly through the defense of Uchiha Madara’s Rinnegan, making Uchiha Madara feel unprecedented pain and shock.

Uchiha Madara’s nose bridge was broken, blood gushed from his nostrils, and his entire body couldn’t stop trembling.

Seeing this, how could Uchiha An let such a good opportunity to finish him off go to waste ?

« Lightning Release : Hundredfold High Temperature ! »

Uchiha An’s left hand slammed fiercely toward Uchiha Madara’s head, and a powerful bolt of lightning erupted from his palm, completely enveloping Uchiha Madara’s head. The dazed Uchiha Madara took the hit squarely.

In an instant, extreme heat appeared on the snowy plains, and the surrounding snow and blood vanished into nothingness.

This technique of Uchiha An’s raised the temperature of the lightning to the extreme, capable of instantly melting all matter ; even the air was burned.

Under such high heat, Uchiha Madara’s head was immediately scorched beyond recognition ; his hair, skin, eyes, and brain were all incinerated to ash.

It could be said that under such extreme physical temperatures, the Sharingan and Rinnegan were nothing but trash.

« The war... is finally... over ! »

As Uchiha An’s voice faded, the crowd present cheered one after another ; whether they were people who knew each other or strangers, they all embraced.

However, Uchiha An quietly turned around, his right hand pulling out a crafted high-strength silver needle.

« Go ! »

The silver needle shot out at a speed invisible to the naked eye.

In the air, the silver needle was like a silver thread, violently piercing through the figure of Zetsu in the distance. Zetsu’s head exploded with a boom, adding another soul to this battlefield where ten thousand had died.

Then, everyone began to skillfully clean up the battlefield, treating the Ninjas who hadn’t died.

After a brief rest, the group set out to conquer the various small hidden villages and the Land of Lightning and the Land of Earth, which had not yet been incorporated into the territory of the Land of Fire.

Unfortunately, having lost the massive power of the Shinobi Allied Forces, the others had no room to resist and surrendered one after another upon hearing the news.

Six years later.

A small lake on the outskirts of the Hidden Mist Village.

A modern villa sat on the riverbank.

« The spring night is short and the sun rises high ; from now on, the monarch will not hold court early. I never expected that I would have such a decadent day. »

Uchiha An woke up on the five-meter-wide bed and was just about to get up when he was pulled back down by a slender arm.

Mei Terumī and Inuzuka Hana naturally held Uchiha An in their arms, letting him feel the softness against his chest.

As for the still-young Hinata, she shyly burrowed into Uchiha An’s arms, like a cute little kitten.

Thus, the retired Uchiha An fell into a deep sleep, enjoying the rest of his life. (End of the book)
