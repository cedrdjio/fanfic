# Chapitre 22 — Uchiha An Targeted!

It must be said that the Op-Op Fruit is powerful ; it can grant immortality while also having such comprehensive effects.

« Damn it ! »

Shisui could only let out a low growl to express his dissatisfaction. He hadn’t expected his katana to be snapped directly, and he hadn’t even been able to use his specialty, the Body Flicker Technique.

Seeing this, Uchiha An stepped forward and patted Shisui on the shoulder.

« Hahaha ! Although Uchiha Itachi can’t beat me, and you were defeated by a six-year-old Uchiha Itachi, at the age of nine, Shisui is still very strong. »

Listening to Uchiha An’s seemingly boastful comfort, Shisui felt even more bitter inside.

In the following days, Shisui would run over here to spar with Uchiha An and Uchiha Itachi whenever he was free.

Later, Shisui also began to train with the two. Fugaku, knowing this, did not object. As a descendant of Uchiha Kagami, Fugaku felt it was better to take care of him when he could.

...

Two and a half months later.

Uchiha An and Uchiha Itachi spent their days slacking off at the Ninja Academy and training at the clan grounds.

However, because of their extremely high attractiveness and talent, Uchiha An and Uchiha Itachi remained famous figures at the Ninja Academy.

The reason the two brothers appeared at school today was because of the upcoming mock assessment, also known as practical combat training.

« This afternoon, we will conduct practical combat training. We will draw names for two-person matches, and the outcome will affect your final grades. »

« The drawing process is random, but you can also choose to challenge someone. »

Ebisu said, glancing at Uchiha An and Uchiha Itachi. Ebisu had mixed feelings about the two.

He loved that they were both geniuses ; as a teacher, there was no way he wouldn’t like geniuses. He hated that the two brothers were slacking off more than the other, having barely attended class in three months.

I’m afraid the two brothers are going to be targeted in this assessment. After all, their Shadow Clones have been attracting the attention of those little girls ; how could that not attract hatred ?

Neither Uchiha An nor Uchiha Itachi cared about the practical combat training Ebisu mentioned. Uchiha An even covered his mouth and yawned.

Compared to the sparring between the two brothers, the practical training arranged by the Ninja Academy was like playing house.

However, the change in the curriculum was quite good for Uchiha An. The memories brought back by the Shadow Clones made Uchiha An have no affection for the courses arranged by the Ninja Academy.

Soon, the boring morning classes ended, and the scorching sun had already climbed above everyone’s heads.

Uchiha An and Uchiha Itachi followed Ebisu out of the classroom, and everyone stood under the blazing sun.

Ebisu took out a list and stood in front of everyone, his pen twirling in his hand—perhaps he was making some notes !

After putting down the pen, Ebisu’s expression took on a rare touch of seriousness.

« After three months of study, you are still far from becoming Ninjas, but tests at each stage are necessary, which is why the school has arranged practical combat training for you. »

« You must follow my instructions for the upcoming assessment. Of course, if any students want to spar with each other, that is also allowed. »

Ebisu’s words instantly ignited everyone in the class. After all, what kid didn’t want to show off in front of everyone ? Except for Uchiha An and Uchiha Itachi.

Watching the rowdy kids, Ebisu coughed twice to regain control.

Suddenly, a dozen kids ran out and surrounded Uchiha An and Uchiha Itachi in the crowd.

« Teacher Ebisu ! I want to spar with student Uchiha An. »

« Teacher Ebisu ! I want to spar with student Uchiha Itachi. »

« Teacher Ebisu ! I want to spar with both students Uchiha Itachi and Uchiha An. »

Looking at the loser kids in front of him, Uchiha An helplessly rubbed his forehead. What kind of strength did they have to dare challenge him ? It seemed his deterrent power was far less than it was during the entrance exam.

As for Uchiha Itachi, he remained as cold and aloof as ever. Only when facing Uchiha An would Uchiha Itachi reveal his true personality.

After the two brothers looked at each other, Uchiha Itachi slowly stepped back, while Uchiha An stepped forward to start some trouble.

« I only duel with the strong. I will never fight trash. »

Saying this, Uchiha An looked at the burly guy in the crowd, naturally accompanied by a mocking gaze.

The little fatty who was despised by Uchiha An instantly burned with rage.

« Why are you looking at me ? Do you think I’m trash ? ! »

Uchiha An didn’t care about the guy’s reaction, instead waving his hands at the circle of people surrounding him.

Then, Uchiha An said in a very snarky tone : « Hey, no, no, no ! Don’t misunderstand. I’m not targeting you. I’m saying that everyone here is trash ! »

Ebisu watched this scene helplessly, cold sweat dripping down his cheeks.

It could be said that no matter what, the two sides had to fight now, but Ebisu didn’t think a dozen peers could be a match for Uchiha An.

At this moment, in the office building not far away, two figures were watching this scene in the field.

« Shimura Kazama, you called me over just to watch this ? Six-year-olds fighting ? »

Danzo Shimura said, looking at Shimura Kazama beside him with dissatisfaction. If it weren’t for the fact that this clansman still had some ability, Danzo Shimura would have wanted to finish him off himself.

Perhaps feeling the coldness in Danzo Shimura’s eyes, Shimura Kazama swallowed nervously.

« Don’t worry ! Clan leader, this time will definitely give you a surprise. »

Meanwhile.

Uchiha An in the playground moved. He suddenly lunged forward and threw a punch, and the guy closest to Uchiha An was knocked unconscious by a punch to the chin.

Seeing this, the other kids retreated in fear, though there were naturally those who weren’t afraid.

« Attack together ! There are a dozen of us ; he can’t knock us all down at the same time ! »

Immediately, the fear they had felt was cast aside, and they all attacked Uchiha An.

« Lightning Release : Full Body Paralysis ! »

Uchiha An slapped the ground with one hand, and lightning spread within a few meters around him.

As for the group of kids who actively attacked Uchiha An, they were directly electrocuted to the ground, foaming at the mouth.

Watching the sudden situation on the field, Ebisu already regretted not stopping this fight earlier.

To make up for his mistake, Ebisu immediately stepped forward to check the condition of the children on the ground.

After checking the situation, Ebisu felt relieved ; fortunately, there were no major issues.

In the office building in the distance.

A rare smile appeared on Danzo Shimura’s old face.

« I didn’t believe Uchiha An’s performance in the Third Ninja World War before, thinking it was just that old guy Uchiha Fugaku playing tricks. I didn’t expect it ! I didn’t expect it ! The Uchiha Clan has produced another genius. He belongs to our Root. »

After speaking, Danzo Shimura left the Ninja Academy ; after all, there was nothing left to see.

And after Danzo Shimura left, Shimura Kazama began to grin grimly in the office.

As long as Uchiha An entered the Root, he could easily manipulate this guy, and then he could do whatever he wanted.
