# Chapitre 50 — Mentality Shattered!

Then, An leaned close to Hozuki Lanyue’s ear and whispered, « Uchiha An wants to ask, when do you intend to kill him ? Otherwise, he is very afraid that you will die at someone else’s hands. »

These words were like a pebble thrown into a lake, stirring up ripples and making Hozuki Lanyue’s state of mind even more chaotic.

An’s deliberately provocative gaze and smile made the entire scene even more bizarre and enigmatic.

Hearing what An said, Hozuki Lanyue was instantly consumed by rage.

A surge of intense hatred welled up in his heart, and he couldn’t help but recall the scenes from the Third Ninja World War.

It was the appearance of that brat that caused his mission to fail, which only made Hozuki Lanyue angrier.

« I’ll kill him sooner or later ! »

With eyes full of fury, Hozuki Lanyue didn’t hesitate to draw two gleaming Kunai and launched a fierce close-quarters attack on An in front of him.

However, An was not going to be intimidated by Hozuki Lanyue’s fierce offensive.

Relying on his keen Observation Haki, An easily perceived every one of Hozuki Lanyue’s movements, as if it were a slow-motion scene.

Then, An utilized Armament Haki, blocking Hozuki Lanyue’s Kunai attack with a solid defensive stance, standing perfectly still without moving an inch.

After a prolonged Taijutsu exchange, Hozuki Lanyue’s arms were left numb from the astonishing strength of An, and he couldn’t help but pant involuntarily.

« Water Release : Water Gun Technique ! »

Hozuki Lanyue did not hesitate to display the secret Ninjutsu passed down through generations of the Hozuki clan, showing his deep hatred for An.

However, compared to the secret techniques of other ninja clans, the Hozuki clan’s secret technique seemed somewhat inferior.

Hozuki Lanyue made a gesture resembling a handgun, shooting liquid like bullets from the tips of his two index fingers.

« Fire Release : Fire Gun Technique ! »

An quietly activated the power of his Rumble-Rumble Fruit while using the observational power of his Observation Haki to track the trajectory of the water spheres.

The high-temperature small fireballs collided violently with Hozuki Lanyue’s water spheres, instantly evaporating and disappearing in mid-air.

« You are a shameless plagiarist ! This is an extremely clumsy imitation ! »

Hozuki Lanyue never expected that this guy would not only directly imitate his clan’s secret Ninjutsu but also unceremoniously rename it « Fire Gun Technique » and perform it right in front of him.

This was simply disgusting and completely intolerable.

« Hey ! I warn you, don’t talk nonsense. I’ll sue you for slander, you know ? I’ll sue you for slander ! »

« This is a secret technique I developed myself, which will be passed down as a secret technique of our Edward Clan to my sons. »

« Oh, right ! Are you willing to be my son ? Call me Oyaji, and you can roam the Ninja World under my name ! »

The crowd watching from afar felt a chill upon hearing An’s words ; he was disgusting others while simultaneously wanting them to call him « Daddy. »

Even Mei Terumī and Ao on his side felt a bit embarrassed, especially Ao, who recalled some unpleasant memories.

Mei Terumī : « Why does Newgate have this habit ? It seems he wanted to adopt you as his son back then too. »

Hearing Mei Terumī's words, Ao’s cheeks flushed slightly, and he could only nod silently in acknowledgment.

Back to the scene on the field.

The enraged Hozuki Lanyue formed hand seals, and suddenly, water vapor filled the air, forming a massive water curtain.

« Water Release : Swarm of Biting Snakes ! »

Water flowed continuously from the water curtain, winding and moving as if it were a living creature.

As time passed, the water flow gradually coalesced into individual water snakes, which shot rapidly and fiercely toward An.

Seeing this, An frowned slightly and immediately formed hand seals with one hand, with Chakra continuously gathering in his right arm.

« Fire Release : Flame-Splitting Wave-Devouring Blade ! »

An raised his arm and pushed his palm toward the sky, instantly forming a blade of flame.

« That is clearly a sword ! »

An sneered, « I’m willing to call it that ! »

In an instant, the fire blade whistled down, slashing across Hozuki Lanyue’s several water snakes. Upon collision, the water snakes were directly severed by the fire blade.

The collision between the water snakes and the fire blade produced a burst of intense steam that quickly dispersed.

Hozuki Lanyue used the water curtain to dodge quickly, and in an instant, he gathered another stream of water, forming a water column that rushed toward An.

Sensing Hozuki Lanyue’s attack, An dodged by shifting his body.

Then, Hozuki Lanyue and An engaged in a fierce battle on the field, their figures constantly darting across the battlefield with speeds too fast to capture.

The two of them, one using Water Release and the other Fire Release, fought endlessly, their elements countering each other.

Time passed second by second, and the water vapor and flames on the field intertwined into a spectacular scene.

Both were giving their all, with no intention of backing down in the slightest.

« Hmph ! Newgate, you bastard, you should be out of Chakra by now ! »

Hozuki Lanyue’s tone was filled with certainty. Newgate had already expended energy during his previous battle with Egao Hekong, so his remaining Chakra levels likely wouldn’t be high.

« Hahaha ! Don’t worry ! Your Oyaji still has enough Chakra to finish you off. » Saying this, An stopped moving. « I forgot to tell you, your Oyaji is best at Lightning Style Ninjutsu. Next, I will defeat you using Lightning Style Ninjutsu. »

An stood in the center of the field, his hands rapidly condensing extreme lightning.

Before long, the lightning displayed a dazzling blue radiance, flickering with powerful electrical currents.

Hozuki Lanyue sensed the crisis, but his subconscious reaction was to try and dodge, but unfortunately, it was already too late.

The speed of An’s attack was so fast that Hozuki Lanyue couldn’t react effectively in time.

The lightning struck like a silver sash, impacting Hozuki Lanyue fiercely.

A huge explosion resounded across the entire battlefield, and the field was immediately filled with smoke and dust.

In an instant, Hozuki Lanyue was directly struck by An’s purple lightning, and his entire body was sent flying, crashing heavily onto the ground.

Hozuki Lanyue’s body was covered in charred marks, clearly having suffered a serious blow. The heavily injured Hozuki Lanyue struggled painfully to crawl up, but it was clearly beyond his strength, and he collapsed back to the ground.

Ao said in disbelief, « He won ? »

He didn’t expect that Newgate had held back when fighting him earlier. Now that he could defeat two Jonin, he must be fighting with his full strength !

« Yes, Newgate won. He really is a reliable man. »

As she spoke, Mei Terumī licked her lips, looking entirely enchanting.

As for Egao Jianfang’s group, they looked devastated, carrying Hozuki Lanyue and the already bandaged Egao Hekong away from the scene.

They didn’t even greet Mei Terumī when they left, clearly not even wanting to go through the motions of courtesy.

« Newgate, you are now a member of our Hidden Mist Village. »

Just like that, An gained Mei Terumī's recognition and settled down in the Hidden Mist Village.

Occasionally, An would look at beautiful women, help handle the administrative affairs of the Hidden Mist Village, and if there was anything up, he would target the Egao Clan, adding some fun to his life.
