# Chapitre 66 — Post-War Arrangements!

Of course, there were still those who were obstinate and chose to resist, but unfortunately, they were directly executed under An’s formidable strength.

« The victors belong to Konoha ! »

In an instant, the Konoha side became excited, but they still began to clean up the battlefield strictly.

It could be said that the battlefield after the victory appeared quite chaotic, with corpses and debris scattered all around.

On the smoke-filled battlefield, the scent of blood and death permeated the air. Although everyone’s faces bore traces of fatigue and battle, their eyes revealed a hint of pride and the joy of victory.

Even though they wore heavy combat gear and some had wounds, no one had the slightest complaint ; there were only firm steps and decisive actions.

As one corpse after another was buried, the people of Konoha Village gradually took over the defenses of the Hidden Sand Village.

...

Hidden Sand Village. Inside a huge office.

An and Danzo Shimura sat opposite Ebizo and Chiyo. However, both Ebizo and Chiyo were wearing Chakra suppressors, which was the standard treatment for all captives in the Hidden Sand Village who refused to submit to discipline.

« Hmph, I didn’t expect Konoha to have a day where it would incite war. Danzo Shimura, you are truly a sinister old dog ! »

Chiyo’s words were extremely intense, and as for Ebizo beside her, he nodded repeatedly, adding salt to the wound.

Seeing this, An chuckled inwardly ; the character of his Lord Danzo was truly known to everyone.

As for Danzo Shimura, who was taking the blame, his expression was somewhat unsightly, but he did not offer much explanation.

« Konoha is the victor now. If you two stinky old man and stinky old woman keep saying such meaningless things, then you two can go meet the Third Kazekage. »

An also added, « I should say the Fourth Kazekage. He has already been killed by Orochimaru, and even the Ichibi Shukaku should have been captured by us by now. »

Since he had made a move against the Hidden Sand Village, An would naturally plan everything out and nip any threat in the bud.

« Our Hidden Sand Village is willing to pay any price, we only hope to get the Ichibi back ! »

Chiyo said these words in a pleading tone, her face full of entreaty.

However, An was amused to the point of laughter by this shameless old woman.

« The entire Hidden Sand Village is mine now. What price can you pay ? Wearing a swimsuit ? »

Initially, upon hearing An’s words, Chiyo’s old face fell, but upon hearing the latter part, there was a hint of willingness.

Seeing this, An was quite startled ; he was just joking, surely this old woman wouldn’t take it seriously !

Therefore, An quickly added, « Actually, I am more interested in the Hidden Sand Village’s Puppet Technique. If you can teach the Puppet Technique you have mastered to Konoha without reservation, I can also consider letting go of some residents of the Hidden Sand Village who are unwilling to be ruled by Konoha. »

Listening to An’s words, a ripple of change clearly appeared on Ebizo’s face.

« Agree to him ! Sister ! »

« Shut up ! »

After reprimanding Ebizo, Chiyo continued to think about all this very calmly.

After a long while, Chiyo said, « My brother and I can hand over the Puppet Technique to Konoha without reservation, but don’t you think your terms are too poor ? »

« We hand over the Puppet Technique to Konoha, and then take away the Hidden Sand Village Ninjas who threaten Konoha’s rule—all the benefits go to you. »

Danzo Shimura slammed the table, stood up, and stared at the two with great momentum.

« Hmph ! We have already obtained quite a bit of information on the Puppet Technique, and in time, we will be able to develop a unique Puppet Technique belonging to Konoha. »

« You two old fogies are just an afterthought ; don’t lose sight of your own position ! »

Seeing Danzo Shimura fly into a rage, Chiyo, who had no strength left, stood up fearlessly.

« Isn’t it because of the high-level Puppet Technique that Ebizo and I have mastered ? Otherwise, who knows how much time your Konoha would have to spend on research. Now that we have something you covet, you’d better think about the terms before you speak ! »

Seeing that the two were about to start arguing, An was also a bit impatient at this moment.

Quibbling over such a small matter was truly a waste of time.

As expected, those who play politics are all like this, constantly quibbling, making concessions, and compromising.

« If you teach the Puppet Technique to Konoha without reservation, I will let you lead the members of the Hidden Sand Village who refuse to submit to discipline away from the Land of Wind, and I can even help you conquer a small Ninja village so you can recuperate. »

« Impossible... »

Seeing that Danzo Shimura wanted to refuse, An immediately shook his head to signal Danzo Shimura to remain silent.

Seeing this, Chiyo and Ebizo immediately agreed without hesitation.

Soon, subordinates implemented this matter, but Danzo Shimura called An out again.

The two stood opposite each other on the rooftop at the highest point of the Hidden Sand Village.

« An, don’t you think your terms are too generous ? Especially helping them conquer a small Ninja village—we really don’t have that much military force. »

Danzo Shimura earnestly tried to persuade An, which could be considered well-intentioned.

An, however, looked at the Hidden Sand Village below without saying a word, and only spoke after a long time.

« Lord Danzo, do you know why the Daimyo of the Land of Fire was willing to send troops to annex the Land of Wind ? It was all for the sake of profit. »

« As long as we let the Konoha Ninja who come to the Hidden Sand Village obtain sufficient benefits, even Minato-sensei wouldn’t be able to stop me from launching a war at that time ! »

Suddenly, Danzo Shimura felt he had nothing to say. Binding Konoha together with interests would turn Konoha into a huge war machine, and the leader of this war machine was the young man in front of him.

« Then are we really going to help them conquer and occupy a small Ninja village ? »

An showed an expression of taking it for granted, then nodded heavily.

« Of course we must help them capture a small Ninja village to recuperate, it’s just that they won’t want it. »

« After we have captured all the small Ninja villages between the Land of Fire and the Land of Wind, this group of people will have lost their value. »

Naturally, there is no need to be so explicit when smart people talk ; Danzo Shimura already understood An’s meaning.

It was just using an excuse to occupy the surrounding small countries and connect the territories between the two great powers.

« I increasingly feel that handing Root over to you was a correct decision. »

« I think so too. »

Next, under the auspices of An and Danzo Shimura, the Hidden Sand Village slowly moved onto the right track, and some people were already able to accept Konoha’s rule.

And during this period, good news also kept coming.

Whether it was the battle against the Daimyo’s mansion of the Land of Wind or the battle to occupy the border of the Land of Wind, everything went so smoothly.

Soon, news also came from Konoha : the Ichibi Jinchuriki Gaara had been defeated by the Nine-Tails Jinchuriki Kushina, and Konoha had successfully recovered the Ichibi.

In short, as the Land of Fire defeated and occupied the Land of Wind, the entire Ninja World fell into chaos, while Konoha quietly absorbed the fruits of victory, waiting for the right moment to arrive.
