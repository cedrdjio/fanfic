# Chapitre 19 — Successfully Enrolled!

Naturally, this made it even more unbearable for Shimura Kazama, to the point where he was biting his teeth so hard they were about to bleed.

Soon, the group of brats finished the second assessment. Unfortunately, this second assessment alone blocked half the people ; it could be said that the elimination rate was quite high.

At this moment, many boys and girls ran out wiping away tears ; it seemed they were going to seek comfort from their parents.

Those who remained breathed a long sigh of relief ; after all, an elimination rate of fifty percent was quite frightening.

Seeing that everyone’s emotions had stabilized, Shimura Kazama glared fiercely at An before walking to the front of the group.

« Congratulations on passing the second assessment. Of course, I won’t waste any more words ; the third assessment will begin immediately. »

« The third assessment is very simple : it’s running. Run around the school playground until you don’t want to run anymore ! »

Soon, over a hundred children dashed out like wild horses released from their reins, clearly not taking Shimura Kazama’s words to heart. After all, what could be difficult about running ?

An and Uchiha Itachi exchanged a glance, a hint of wariness rising in them, before joining the wave of children running.

« Older brother, this is unusual. Look, all the teachers are evenly distributed around the playground. I’m afraid there will be further actions. »

Hearing Uchiha Itachi’s analysis, An also noticed this point. It was likely that Shimura Kazama, that sly fox, would target him.

However, An didn’t care. After all, when assessing a group of six-year-old brats, what kind of trouble could they possibly stir up ?

« Don’t worry, my foolish little brother. With our strength, this kind of assessment is just too simple. »

Uchiha Itachi nodded to show his agreement with An’s words, then silently distanced himself from An and ran in front of Izumi.

He didn’t know if the aerodynamics his brother spoke of were reliable or not, but Izumi was a girl and really needed him to look after her.

Just like that, Uchiha Itachi abandoned his older brother and contentedly took on the role of windbreaker for Izumi.

Soon, over a hundred children finished the first lap, and the many teachers still showed no reaction.

After the second lap was finished, the group of teachers still didn’t react, but most of the children were already panting heavily. Clearly, the distance of one kilometer had exhausted most of them.

When they reached the third lap, the teachers around the playground immediately began to move.

An, who had been leading the pack, was immediately targeted as the third lap began. The teachers who had been standing still suddenly threw several small red balls with their right hands, aiming directly at the few people in the lead.

Seeing this, An dodged to the side, but the others were caught in a flurry by the sudden attack and were all hit.

At this moment, Shimura Kazama’s annoying voice rang out across the entire field.

« Everyone, try your best to dodge the teachers' attacks. This is also part of the assessment. »

After speaking, Shimura Kazama grinned maliciously at An, who was running over, his eyes filled with ferocity.

Immediately, everyone became hyper-alert ; anything related to the assessment could not be taken lightly.

And when An saw Shimura Kazama not far away, he silently spat at the guy in his heart, while also keeping a constant watch on Shimura Kazama’s movements.

Just as the distance between the two was closing, Shimura Kazama suddenly moved, and several small balls in his hand were violently thrown at An.

Seeing this, An knew that with his current physical fitness, it would be difficult to dodge. He immediately used his Devil Fruit ability to speed up, narrowly dodging Shimura Kazama’s attack.

Unfortunately, the few guys running behind An were out of luck.

« Ouch ! »

« It hurts so much ! »

The small red balls hit the few of them, and those who were struck immediately wailed.

Seeing this, even the dullest person could tell that An was being targeted by Shimura Kazama. Consequently, a large vacuum zone immediately formed around An, and no one dared to follow behind him anymore.

Uchiha Itachi silently glanced at Shimura Kazama. He could easily handle such petty tricks himself ; to think he would try to make things difficult for his brother.

Following this, An began to pull far ahead, running lap after lap under the targeting of all the teachers.

However, the others didn’t have such good stamina. Soon, one after another, they collapsed. The few guys who had previously looked at An and Uchiha Itachi with displeasure had now given up, not daring to look at the two brothers again.

As the laps continued, more and more people couldn’t hold on. Slowly, only the two brothers, An and Uchiha Itachi, were left on the playground.

« Alright, Uchiha Itachi, if we keep running, Father will get impatient waiting. »

« Mm-hmm ! »

The two brothers stopped at the same time. Some teachers secretly breathed a sigh of relief ; the two little ancestors had been running continuously, and they themselves felt tired from trying to make things difficult for them—just thinking about it made them feel a bit ashamed.

As for Shimura Kazama, he had already given up. After all, the two were geniuses of the Uchiha Clan, and he couldn’t go too far.

Just as Shimura Kazama decided to let An off the hook, he didn’t know that An had already started holding a grudge against him.

« Alright, congratulations on passing the Ninja Academy assessment. You can officially enroll tomorrow. »

After saying this, Shimura Kazama turned and left, and the other teachers also nodded to the crowd and left.

Immediately, the hundred people who had been standing neatly scattered like birds and beasts.

However, Izumi quietly followed behind Uchiha Itachi, and Uchiha Itachi didn’t say much about it.

[Ding ! Congratulations to the host for taking first place in the entrance exam. Reward : one lottery chance.]

An was instantly startled by his own system.

This damn system hadn’t popped up in so long ; suddenly making a sound like that scared the life out of him.

As for the lottery chance, An didn’t really care. Unless it was a guaranteed super-tier lottery, it wouldn’t yield any powerful Devil Fruit.

After the three of them came out, they discovered that the leader of the Uchiha Clan had already disappeared.

« This old man is really confident leaving the two of us behind. Isn’t he afraid that someone might come to Konoha to kidnap the young leaders of the Uchiha Clan ? »

Fugaku was naturally confident in the strength of the two brothers, and since he was bored waiting here, he decisively slipped away.

It could only be said that his fatherly love was as heavy as a mountain, so heavy that it left An and Uchiha Itachi feeling a bit at a loss.

Seeing this, Izumi revealed a smile ; this gave her just the right opportunity.

« Since the Clan Leader has already gone back, let me treat you both to some three-color dango ! »

Of course, the words were spoken to An, the older brother, but Izumi’s gaze kept shifting toward Uchiha Itachi.

Uchiha Itachi naturally wouldn’t refuse Izumi’s invitation.

Plus, Uchiha Itachi liked eating that stuff anyway, so the two of them reached an agreement directly.

As for whether An wanted to go or not, no one cared, since Uchiha Itachi and Izumi had already set off.

« You two go ahead ! I won’t be a third wheel. »

After saying this, An didn’t care about the two of them and walked straight toward home. After all, there was still the matter of Shimura Kazama, and An still wanted to ask his old father about it.

He didn’t expect that after going around in circles, he would still need to go to school, and to primary school at that.

He only hoped that the Ninja Academy could give An a different experience ; otherwise, An really didn’t want to endure six years of time here.
