# Chapitre 18 — Assessment Accident!

Of course, Uchiha Itachi didn’t care about these things ; it could be said that he didn’t care at all about such opportunities to show off in front of others.

Therefore, Uchiha Itachi stepped forward two paces, placed his right hand on the Chakra sensing stone, and the Chakra within his body began to continuously surge into it.

Very quickly, the pitch-black Chakra sensing stone began to change ; the black traces receded like a tide, and in no time, the Chakra sensing stone turned white.

« Is this the legendary genius ? »

« Look at the clan crest on his back. A genius of the Uchiha Clan. »

« As expected of the greatest noble clan in Konoha, to possess such a gifted youth. »

...

At this moment, whether it was the teachers or the students, all eyes were focused on Uchiha Itachi. It could only be said that he had thoroughly stolen the spotlight this time.

Only a few individuals showed disdain, obviously unconvinced by Uchiha Itachi’s performance.

Uchiha Itachi didn’t pay them any mind, turning away coldly and leaving the area in front of the table to make room for his brother’s performance.

However, this cold demeanor of his was even more captivating, immediately attracting a large group of six-year-old female fans.

« So handsome ! This guy, I want to marry him. »

« Right, he’s so dashing. I’m going to be his girlfriend. »

Hearing the crowd becoming increasingly explicit, Izumi felt as though her Uchiha Itachi had already been snatched away by others, so she immediately shouted out.

« What relationship do you have with Uchiha Itachi ? We are the childhood sweethearts ! »

The atmosphere in the venue suddenly quieted down, and Izumi felt embarrassed at that moment.

Sensing Izumi’s awkward situation, Uchiha Itachi walked to her side without a word. The two of them looked like a perfect match.

Uchiha An hadn’t expected that before he could even make his grand entrance, he would be force-fed a massive wave of public displays of affection by these two six-year-olds.

« I guess I’ll have to rely on myself from here on out. »

After whispering to himself, Uchiha An stepped forward a few paces and then raised both hands high.

« Everyone, eyes on me ! »

Then, ignoring the astonished gazes of the crowd, Uchiha An placed his hand directly on the Chakra sensing stone.

In an instant, the black on the Chakra sensing stone receded, and a milky-white halo began to flow continuously across it, adding a mysterious quality to the otherwise ordinary stone.

This scene completely shocked everyone present ; both teachers and students stared blankly at what was happening.

After a long while, it was Shimura Kazama who first reacted.

« Alright, continue the assessment ! »

Following Shimura Kazama’s command, the entire line began to move.

However, Uchiha An’s performance just now had still frightened everyone, and many people looked at him with eyes full of awe.

Time ticked by second by second, and soon, over three hundred children had completed the test.

Out of more than three hundred people, only four were eliminated. It had to be said that this was already the best possible result, as the elimination rate was only one percent.

Of course, there were many young boys and girls from various Ninja clans who performed well, but unfortunately, none were as dazzling as the Uchiha An and Uchiha Itachi brothers.

« Those who passed the assessment, do not become arrogant. We will now begin the second assessment. »

Under Shimura Kazama’s arrangement, the numerous candidates lined up into several long queues again.

The teachers present placed the targets ten meters away from everyone.

« The second assessment is shuriken throwing. Each person gets ten shurikens. Hitting the target with six is passing ; those who cannot do so will be directly eliminated. »

Instantly, the group of brats began to discuss it. After all, getting a shuriken to stick into the target from ten meters away required significant strength, and many commoner Ninjas lacked exactly that.

You have to realize that in this era, people couldn’t even get enough to eat ; children from commoner families found it very difficult to surpass children with clan legacies in terms of strength.

However, no one cared about this, and the shuriken throwing assessment soon began.

As one child after another stepped forward to throw the shurikens in their hands, one child after another saw their expressions fall.

Ten meters was still a bit far for six-year-old children ; even if they could hit the target, it didn’t necessarily mean the shuriken would stick.

« Brother, testing these things is so boring ! »

At this moment, the mentally mature Uchiha Itachi also felt a bit impatient ; after all, this stuff really held no difficulty for him.

« Just bear with it a little longer ! A lot of people should be eliminated this time. »

One person after another finished, and in no time, it was Uchiha An and Uchiha Itachi’s turn.

It was another incredibly eye-catching performance. Uchiha Itachi stepped forward and quickly threw the shurikens in his hand. All ten shurikens hit the target quickly, forming a circle.

This scene once again shocked the onlookers ; even the teachers turned to look at Uchiha Itachi’s target, wearing expressions of admiration.

Uchiha An knew it was his turn to act, so he stepped forward two paces with the shurikens. The ability of the Logia-type Rumble-Rumble Fruit instantly activated, and the shurikens in Uchiha An’s hands were covered in lightning.

« Bang ! Bang ! »

In an instant, Uchiha An’s shurikens pierced straight through the target and were nailed fiercely into the tree behind it.

The Chunin teacher who had been supervising Uchiha An felt a chill between his legs, as if something strange had flown past, and then realized he had almost been turned into a eunuch by a child.

« There are no shurikens on Uchiha An’s target. First throw failed ! »

As he said this, Shimura Kazama looked at Uchiha An ; it was clear that he couldn’t stand Uchiha An’s performance, which was why he did this.

Uchiha An frowned. Damn it, this was just like Stephen Chow’s movie ; clearly, passing through the target was the strongest feat, yet it didn’t count for points. Even So Chan wasn’t as wronged as he was.

Alright ! Actually, they were both wronged.

Unfortunately, Uchiha An didn’t know why Shimura Kazama was targeting him, but Uchiha An wasn’t prepared to let this guy off so easily.

Therefore, Uchiha An took out another shuriken, which was again covered in lightning. Of course, this time Uchiha An was still aiming at the target.

However, with electricity creating magnetism and magnetism creating electricity, Uchiha An himself didn’t know where his shuriken would deflect to.

In an instant, Uchiha An’s shuriken pierced through the target again, but after passing through, the shuriken inexplicably made a turn and flew directly toward the nearby Shimura Kazama.

« Clang ! »

As a Special Jonin, Shimura Kazama still had some skill ; he directly blocked Uchiha An’s attack with a Kunai, staring at Uchiha An with eyes full of fury.

If he hadn’t been paying attention to the situation, he might have ended up a eunuch himself.

Seeing this, Uchiha An put on an innocent expression and said to Shimura Kazama in a soft, childish voice : « Sorry, teacher, I didn’t know this would happen. This was unintentional ! Of course, I also knew that as a Jonin, you would definitely be able to dodge it. I believe in your strength. »

Listening to Uchiha An’s words, Shimura Kazama became more and more furious, but unfortunately, he couldn’t do anything in front of everyone.

Therefore, Shimura Kazama could only glare at Uchiha An with fury, as if he could stare him to death alive.

Uchiha An didn’t care at all ; while continuing the assessment, he silently gave Shimura Kazama an international gesture of friendship.
