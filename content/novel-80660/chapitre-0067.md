# Chapitre 67 — A Heavily Modified Battle, Uniting Konoha!

One week later, the arena in Konoha Village was still bustling with activity.

On the podium, An sat quietly in a chair, intently watching the duel between Hinata and Neji on the stage.

This match had been influenced by An’s heavily modified plot. Hinata had eaten the Paramecia-type Weapon-Weapon Fruit, constantly transforming into various firearms to attack Neji ; meanwhile, Neji had eaten the Paramecia-type Glare-Glare Fruit, his eyes revealing an elusive, sharp glint as he keenly dodged every one of Hinata’s attacks.

The two engaged in an exceptionally intense contest, their speed so fast that the audience could barely keep up with their movements.

The two zipped through the arena like lightning, each attack sudden and precise.

Prediction, reaction, prediction, plus a little bit of luck, became the key to their attacks against each other.

However, Minato Namikaze on the podium was not focusing all his attention on the match.

Although his eyes were watching the spectacular performance of the two young Ninjas on the stage, his mind was wandering.

« An, you really shouldn’t have provoked a war between the Hidden Sand Village and Konoha, especially under these circumstances. It’s highly likely to trigger the Fourth Ninja World War. »

Minato Namikaze said earnestly to An. As someone who had personally experienced the Ninja World War, especially having made a name for himself during the Third Ninja World War, Minato knew full well the immense harm and sacrifice that would ensue once a war broke out.

An, sitting on the podium, understood Minato’s concerns and knew that he was shouldering a huge risk ; the possibility of being disgraced or even killed could not be ignored.

As the initiator of the war, victory might bring glory and benefits to Konoha, but defeat meant destruction.

« Minato-sensei, it wasn’t me who started the war ; rather, the war between Konoha and the Hidden Sand Village has already ended, and the final victor is our Konoha. » An responded firmly.

However, there was something else in An’s heart that remained unsaid : the war with other Ninja Villages was also about to begin.

« Minato, I feel that An has done an excellent job. Especially after defeating the Hidden Sand Village, he has brought so much tax revenue and population to the Land of Fire. »

The Daimyo of the Land of Fire, Narusawa, looked at An with an approving expression.

As the Daimyo of the Land of Fire, he was naturally a beneficiary of this war.

The Daimyo’s wife was even more excited, saying to An with exhilaration : « If it weren’t for the fact that An already has several confidantes, I would have wanted to marry my daughter off to him. »

An instantly broke into a cold sweat, secretly feeling fortunate that he was already taken ; otherwise, the daughter of the Land of Fire’s Daimyo in the original plot was quite a sturdy woman, and his small frame really couldn’t handle that.

Seeing that both the Daimyo and his wife approved of An’s plans, Minato had nothing more to say for the moment.

After taking a deep breath, Minato understood that as a teacher and Hokage, he had to try his best to protect Konoha and the young Ninjas.

At this critical moment, supporting An seemed to be his only choice.

« Since that’s the case, as your teacher, I can only support you. After all, a united Konoha is at its strongest. » After saying this, Minato changed the subject and spoke to An in a serious tone.

« However, if there are any future actions, you must let me know. After all, as Hokage, I still need a certain right to know. »

Hearing this, An immediately nodded, feeling grateful for Minato’s concern.

An understood that his current actions had already exceeded the scope of what a Root leader should do and even involved the fate of the entire Village and the Ninja World.

Despite this, his teacher still trusted him deeply, which moved An.

« Of course, Minato-sensei ! As Hokage, you possess important duties and authority, and I will ensure you receive the necessary information and right to know. » An replied solemnly.

Minato smiled slightly. Watching the student he had once trained become so mature and responsible, he couldn’t help but feel gratified.

Then Minato took a deep breath and said, « No matter what actions are taken next, you must let me know. Only a united Konoha is the strongest. »

An nodded in agreement ; after all, in the original plot, the Fourth Ninja World War was essentially a civil war within Konoha, which shows how powerful Konoha’s strength was to other Ninja Villages.

On the podium, Hyuga Hiashi, Minato, and many other important figures of Konoha Village nodded in tacit agreement. Everyone would work together to fight for the prosperity and peace of Konoha Village.

Just at this moment, the battle in the arena also came to an end.

After an intense contest, Neji, the user of the Glare-Glare Fruit, finally seized an opportunity to get close and used Gentle Fist to knock Hinata down.

An observed the entire process of the match and keenly noticed signs that Hinata had held back.

After all, in recent years, under An’s inspiration and help, Hinata had conducted in-depth research and development on her own Weapon-Weapon Fruit and had mastered more powerful weapons.

However, An could sense that Hinata had held back when attacking, perhaps worried that using her stronger fruit abilities would cause serious harm to Neji.

This made An feel gratified by Hinata’s intelligence and kindness.

« I’m sorry, An-gege, I lost. » Little Hinata apologized to An with her head lowered.

An looked at little Hinata, whose height only reached his waist, and a wave of tenderness and understanding welled up in his heart.

However, An forcibly restrained his sinful hands ; after all, her old father was still watching right here.

Hyuga Hiashi, sitting on the podium, was somewhat dissatisfied upon seeing this. Especially seeing his daughter greet the guy next to him first instead of immediately responding to his gaze, Hyuga Hiashi was even more displeased.

An also sensed Hyuga Hiashi’s dissatisfaction, but An understood that as Hinata’s father, the Hyuga Clan’s requirements and expectations for Hinata were far greater than for others.

« It’s okay, Hinata fought very well. Your father is surely proud of you. » Hyuga Hiashi replied calmly, trying to ease the atmosphere.

An could also understand the Hyuga Clan’s emphasis on family honor and strength.

Then, Hyuga Hiashi looked at An with a serious expression and said, « An, our Hyuga Clan is also a major clan in Konoha. I won’t say much more about your relationship with our Hyuga Clan, but next time if something happens, you must not forget our Hyuga Clan. »

An hurriedly nodded in agreement. An was very interested in the Hyuga Clan’s Byakugan ; after all, the Hyuga Clan was one of Konoha’s two great noble clans, and their internal strength would not be weak.

Seeing An’s reaction, Hyuga Hiashi nodded with satisfaction.

The following time naturally belonged to An and Hinata, of course, with the addition of Inuzuka Hana who had cut in, and the little follower Tayuya. The group started strolling around Konoha.

It could be said that An also enjoyed a rare moment of peace, though he certainly hadn’t forgotten to accumulate strength in preparation for the next war.
