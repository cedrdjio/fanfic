# Chapitre 23 — Teaching Shisui and Itachi!

An naturally did not know about the schemes Danzo Shimura and Shimura Kazama had against him, and of course, even if he did, he would not take them to heart.

However, after the scene An caused, combat training could not continue.

Under Ebisu’s arrangement, those who needed to go to the hospital went, while the rest returned to the classroom to continue the dull and tedious lessons.

An took the opportunity to cast the Shadow Clone technique and slipped away.

Unfortunately, Itachi did not grasp the essence and blankly followed everyone to class.

Walking alone on the streets of Konoha, looking at the peaceful and harmonious scene around him, An’s heart became increasingly tranquil.

Six years had passed since he transmigrated here.

In these six years, he had avoided the Uchiha Clan’s extermination and prevented the Night of the Nine-Tailed. It could be said that under Minato’s leadership, Konoha was showing signs of prosperity.

Thinking of this, An felt a hint of accomplishment ; after all, this was all changed by his arrival.

Just as An was lost in thought, a strong arm suddenly scooped him up.

« An ! Why are you here ? It’s class time now. Be careful, or I’ll tell Mikoto to spank your bottom. »

« Teacher’s wife, surely you wouldn’t do that ! »

The person who picked up An was none other than Kushina, while Kakashi beside her rolled his dead-fish eyes, holding little Naruto with a look of utter despair.

« So cute ! »

After speaking, Kushina began to rub An’s little face, leaving An with a look of utter despair.

One-year-old Naruto stared at An with big eyes. It had to be said that Naruto was cutest when he was little, though, of course, Naruto in Sage Mode was the coolest.

« Teacher’s wife, isn’t Kakashi a member of the Anbu ? Why is he out shopping with you ? »

Kakashi rolled his eyes and said, « Previously, Kumogakure had an operation targeting Teacher’s wife. Although it didn’t affect her, Teacher Minato was still worried, so he sent me to protect her safety. »

Listening to Kakashi’s account, Kushina’s wine-red hair stood on end.

The residents on the street scattered immediately upon seeing this scene.

An was a bit confused ; wasn’t it normal for the various Ninja Villages to target each other’s Jinchuriki ? Why was his Teacher’s wife reacting so strongly ?

Perhaps seeing An’s confusion, Kakashi explained again.

« The Kumogakure operation at that time almost hurt Naruto. »

Women are naturally weak, but mothers are strong. Kumogakure had touched Kushina’s reverse scale.

« Don’t worry ! Teacher’s wife, when I grow up, I will definitely destroy the Land of Lightning and Kumogakure. »

« Good, then Teacher’s wife will wait for you to avenge me. »

Kushina naturally took An’s words seriously ; otherwise, the Five Great Ninja Villages wouldn’t have been fighting each other for so many years.

However, An’s display was worth encouraging, so Kushina picked An up and kissed him, which made An feel embarrassed. After all, his mind was that of an adult !

« Teacher’s wife, I have something to do, I’ll be going first. »

After saying this, An broke free from Kushina’s embrace and ran off into the distance.

However, having walked only a few steps, An turned back around.

« Teacher’s wife, don’t let Kakashi watch the baby. He’s a secret pervert ; he’ll teach Naruto bad habits. »

After speaking, An didn’t care about the murderous look Kakashi was giving him from behind and ran quickly to the intersection, disappearing around the corner.

Kakashi didn’t expect to suffer this unprovoked disaster. How did An know he was a pervert ? He just liked reading erotica ; how could he be called a pervert ?

Could it be that Might Guy, who knew him well, told An ? When did those two meet ?

At this moment, Kakashi was already trapped in a brainstorm.

Kushina covered her mouth and chuckled at this ; that little guy An was truly too interesting.

After bidding farewell to Kushina and Kakashi, An had lost interest in shopping and turned back home.

The next day.

An and Itachi’s Shadow Clones walked into the school, and of course, there was Shisui’s Shadow Clone too.

The three of them skipped class together and arrived at their usual training spot.

The three, who should have been training, sat cross-legged together instead.

« Chakra is the product of the mixture of spiritual energy and physical energy within the human body ; it can be said to be the body’s own power. »

« But I have discovered that there is not only Chakra within the human body ; there is another mysterious power that we have yet to discover and utilize. »

Shisui and Itachi looked at An in confusion simultaneously. Shisui even reached out to touch An’s forehead to see if An had a fever.

An naturally understood what the two meant, but facts speak louder than words ; it was better to let them experience it intuitively.

In an instant, an overwhelming aura covered the entire area. An released his youthful Haoshoku Haki with full force, and the surrounding trees swayed outwards from An as the center, as if blown by a strong wind.

Shisui and Itachi’s expressions froze instantly, then their eyes regained clarity. Both of them looked at An simultaneously, the look of shock in their eyes impossible to erase.

« Six Paths Sage ! »

Shisui muttered to himself. The Six Paths Sage founded the Ninja Sect and popularized Ninjutsu.

If An’s training method was universally applicable, then An’s achievements would be enough to rival the Six Paths Sage, creating a training system belonging to An.

As for Itachi, although he was also quite shocked, once he thought about his brother’s mysterious nature, he was not as nervous.

« I have named this power Haki, and what I just displayed is only one type of Haki. There are higher-level applications of Haki, but I have not mastered them yet. »

An claimed the discovery and application of Haki without blushing or skipping a beat ; after all, no one in this world knew it, so he was the discoverer of this power.

After An finished explaining, Shisui and Itachi both looked at An with anticipation, clearly wanting to master this power as well.

« Alright, since I’ve told you, it’s because I want you two to master this power just like me. »

« However, the Haki I just displayed is called Haoshoku Haki. This can only be innate ; it is impossible to master acquired, so we must try our best to master other types of Haki. »

Hearing this, Shisui and Itachi nodded heavily.

Unknowingly, the three who had been training together had started to silently revolve around An, a fact that Shisui, Itachi, and An had not noticed.

« Next, I will start training Observation Haki, and you two will be my assistants. »

As he spoke, An took out a black strip of cloth and blindfolded himself.

« You two take turns attacking me one by one. When I can dodge both of your attacks, the training for Observation Haki will be considered complete. »

Shisui and Itachi naturally would not refuse such a perverted request from An, and they readily agreed.

Then the three began their training in Observation Haki, and piercing screams continuously echoed from the forest where they were training.
