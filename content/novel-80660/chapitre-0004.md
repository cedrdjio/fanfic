# Chapitre 4 — Inuzuka Hana!

After leaving home, An and Itachi followed Fugaku like little tails.

Soon, the father and his two sons arrived at a large open space.

One by one, Uchiha Clan members with the clan’s fan symbol on their backs stood together.

As everyone noticed Fugaku’s arrival, the atmosphere immediately became serious.

The status of the clan leader was evident at this moment.

Three elderly figures immediately stepped forward and bowed to Fugaku.

« Greetings, Clan Leader ! »

These three were the three elders of the Uchiha Clan, who usually helped Fugaku manage the Uchiha Clan’s affairs.

The Great Elder Uchiha Tsuruhi, the Second Elder Uchiha Roen, and the Third Elder Uchiha Hiromitsu.

At the same time, the three were also rare possessors of the Three-Tomoe Sharingan within the Uchiha Clan.

« Three elders, for this war, I am taking An and Itachi out to gain some experience. »

An and Itachi sensibly stepped forward, « Greetings, three grandfathers. »

« Good, good, good ! »

After a simple greeting, the Uchiha Clan headed toward the training ground ; after all, the Hokage Building could not hold the three thousand Ninja about to set out.

In reality, Konoha had more than just this many Ninja, but since every part of the Land of Fire needed Ninja for defense, most Konoha Ninja were scattered throughout the Land of Fire.

By the time the Uchiha Clan arrived, the vast majority of the Ninja clan members had already gathered there.

Looking at the familiar figures and clan emblems present, An felt that this was the real world.

« Hokage-sama has arrived ! »

The Third Hokage Hiruzen Sarutobi appeared on the high platform, followed by Jiraiya, Orochimaru, and Minato Namikaze.

Tsunade, who had developed hemophobia during the Second Ninja World War, did not appear before the crowd ; it seemed she was not planning to appear on the front lines.

Then, the Third Hokage Hiruzen Sarutobi began a cliché pre-war mobilization speech, which left An feeling drowsy.

Next, the Ninja army departed in batches toward the various borders of the Land of Fire.

An and Itachi set out with the army in Fugaku’s arms.

After a day of marching, the Konoha Ninja responsible for support arrived at the border of the Land of Fire to set up camp, guarding against potential attacks from various directions.

Inside the main tent, the leaders and Jonin of the major Ninja clans participating in the war gathered together.

Sitting in the main seat was the Third Hokage’s disciple, Jiraiya.

Of course, all of this had nothing to do with An, who was outside the tent.

At this moment, An was strolling around with his stupid little brother, and the figures of the two little boys attracted the attention of quite a few young ladies.

An noticed a cute little girl sitting alone on a rock.

« Oh ! Pretty little sister, are you a member of the Inuzuka Clan ? »

An knew the iconic crest of the Inuzuka Clan, which was relatively mysterious in the Naruto series.

« Are you the twin brothers of the Uchiha Clan, Uchiha An and Uchiha Itachi ? You look so cute. » Saying this, the little girl reached out and pinched Itachi’s face.

The cool and detached Itachi was instantly broken ; he didn’t expect someone to have the same habit as his older brother, and a slightly frightened Itachi took a few steps back.

« My name is Inuzuka Hana. I came here to gain some experience on the battlefield with my father. Also, I am one year older than you twins, so you should call me big sister. »

A strange feeling arose in An’s heart ; the title 'Big Sister Hana' reminded him of a Japanese actress.

Inuzuka Hana reached out her devilish claws toward An, but An proactively leaned his face in to let Inuzuka Hana pinch it.

Thinking about Inuzuka Hana’s beauty when she grew up, An felt that it was better to start cultivating a relationship with a beauty early.

Itachi looked at his brother, who was acting like a sycophant, and felt goosebumps cover his body, unable to stop himself from taking a few more steps back.

« Come, let big sister give you a hug. »

Ridiculous, how could An refuse ? He dove headfirst into Inuzuka Hana’s embrace.

It was a pity that Inuzuka Hana was only five years old and still too flat ; there was no roundness of her fully grown self, but considering that female Ninja in the Ninja World could develop quite a bit by the age of twelve, it wouldn’t be many years to wait.

Just as An was quietly enjoying the warmth, an abrupt voice sounded.

« You brat from the Uchiha family, what are you doing in Hana’s arms ? »

Seeing the person who had arrived, Inuzuka Hana’s expression immediately collapsed.

« Mother, why are you here ! »

Seeing this, An naturally knew what had happened, so he immediately pretended to look uncomfortable.

A smile appeared on the corner of Itachi’s mouth ; he seemed very happy to see his brother get snubbed here.

« Thank you, Big Sister Hana, I’m feeling much better. »

An reluctantly got up from Inuzuka Hana’s embrace and leaned shakily against Itachi.

Inuzuka Hana’s mother, Inuzuka Tsume, curled her lip in disdain.

« Our Hana is a veterinarian ; I don’t know why you little brat came to find her ? »

At this moment, An could only pretend he didn’t know anything, and pulled Itachi away from there.

« Hana, you need to be careful about your relationship with them ; you must keep your distance. »

Inuzuka Tsume said as she left, and the smile on Inuzuka Hana’s face faded.

Inuzuka Tsume had her own considerations regarding everything that had just happened.

The Uchiha Clan had always harbored some anti-Konoha sentiments ; if An were to stand on the opposite side of Konoha one day, the only one who would suffer would be her daughter.

An and Itachi left and returned to the Uchiha Clan’s encampment.

Unfortunately, everyone was busy walking back and forth, and no one paid any attention to the two little brats.

Bored, An and Itachi began practicing the Clone Technique.

Day after day passed.

The scale of the war became larger and larger.

Originally, it was just a dispute caused by the disappearance of the Third Kazekage.

Now, every Ninja village had joined the chaotic battle.

An and Itachi did not know to what extent the fighting outside had progressed ; they only knew that more and more wounded were being sent back.

Neither An nor Itachi knew medical Ninjutsu, so they could only do what they could to help the wounded, which instead earned the two little boys quite a bit of popularity.

One week later.

Fugaku called An and Itachi together again.

« The war is getting closer and closer to us now, and even here is starting to be raided. In the coming time, you two must follow me closely so that I can protect you. »

« Yes, Father ! » X2

An and Itachi agreed readily, but as for what they would actually do, that would depend on the situation.

However, as the scale of the war continued to expand, Fugaku became busier and busier, sometimes even coming to see An and Itachi without having time to wash the blood off his body.

Five days later.

An and Itachi were both practicing in Fugaku’s tent.

Suddenly, a middle-aged man with the Sarutobi Clan emblem walked in.

« Uchiha An and Uchiha Itachi of the Uchiha Clan, I am the Jonin of the Sarutobi Clan, Sarutobi Kazekaze. Now, you two come with me. »

An and Itachi exchanged a look, and secretly guarded as they followed this man from the Sarutobi Clan.
