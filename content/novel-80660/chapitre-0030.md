# Chapitre 30 — Presenting the Calm-Calm Fruit!

Along the way, Uchiha An and Shimura Kazama walked one after the other, both remaining silent.

Guided by Shimura Kazama, the path they took became increasingly remote, and the number of pedestrians they encountered steadily decreased.

Finally, after walking for half an hour.

Shimura Kazama led Uchiha An to a stop in front of an antiquated, secluded courtyard.

It was quiet here, with only a few withered, yellow leaves dancing in the air, gently scattering across the ground.

Shimura Kazama then stepped forward and knocked on the door : three short knocks followed by two long ones.

The door opened immediately, and a guy wearing a rat mask poked his head out.

« Snowflakes dancing ! »

« Cherry blossoms falling ! »

Just like that, after the two exchanged the secret code, the Root member wearing the rat mask opened the wooden door.

Watching the two of them meet, Uchiha An couldn’t help but chuckle ; this was exactly like a secret agent rendezvous.

Seeing Uchiha An’s amused expression, Shimura Kazama glared at him with dissatisfaction.

However, Shimura Kazama didn’t say much at this moment and led Uchiha An straight inside.

Once Uchiha An caught up, he discovered that there was more than met the eye inside.

It was still an antiquated wooden building, but the interior was clean and tidy.

What caught his attention were the traces of Kunai and shuriken practice visible everywhere ; this was likely a former training ground for Root, currently abandoned for various reasons.

« Do not look around when you see Lord Danzo later, and pay attention to your tone and attitude when you speak. »

Shimura Kazama’s demeanor reminded Uchiha An of a personal eunuch serving an emperor ; he had to admit, this guy had real potential as a sycophant.

Although Uchiha An felt disdainful inside, he still nodded to indicate he had heard.

Uchiha An followed Shimura Kazama as they wandered around the courtyard, until suddenly, Shimura Kazama entered one of the rooms.

Seeing this, Uchiha An immediately followed, only to discover upon entering that the room was designed like a labyrinth, filled with corners and mirrors everywhere.

Helplessly, Uchiha An followed Shimura Kazama through the twists and turns ; based on his perception, they had already descended fifteen meters underground.

In reality, through his Observation Haki, Uchiha An had already preliminarily determined everything about this place.

Finally, at the end of a gloomy corridor, there was a stone room, and Shimura Kazama led Uchiha An straight toward it.

Then, Shimura Kazama pushed the door open and entered naturally, and Uchiha An immediately followed suit.

The furnishings inside were exceptionally simple, with only a table and two chairs, outlining the minimalist feel of the entire space.

On the table sat several books and a pile of files, while the bookshelf next to it was filled with various scrolls and important documents.

It could be said that Danzo Shimura’s office was not much different from the Hokage Office.

Danzo Shimura, as the leader of Root, sat in the middle, appearing to have been waiting for Uchiha An for some time.

Behind Danzo stood a guy wearing a fox-faced mask, though it was unclear whether it was Fu Yamanaka or Aburame Torune.

« Lord Danzo, I have brought Uchiha An. »

After saying this, Shimura Kazama consciously retreated ; it was clear that this guy’s status in Root wasn’t particularly high.

The moment Shimura Kazama retreated, the atmosphere in the room instantly became tense.

« Uchiha An, welcome to Root. »

Danzo Shimura’s tone was gloomy and dull, as if all signs of life had completely vanished.

Danzo Shimura’s single eye revealed a sharp and icy light, and combined with the tight bandages on his body, he looked just like the terrifying Bandaged Man from the Detective Conan series.

It could be said that Danzo Shimura’s appearance, coupled with the surrounding environment, maximized the terrifying atmosphere, even making Uchiha An feel a hint of chill.

« Lord Danzo ! »

After offering a greeting, Uchiha An fell silent ; without a clear understanding of the situation, it was better to speak less.

« An, you should know that there has always been conflict between the Uchiha Clan and the Shimura Clan, so why are you willing to join Root under my command ? »

Uchiha An pretended to lower his head in thought for a moment, then immediately looked up, staring directly into Danzo Shimura’s single eye, and began his sycophantic mode.

« Our Root is situated in the shadows of Konoha Village, just like tree Roots deeply embedded in the depths of darkness. »

« The primary goal of Root’s mission is to eliminate all obstacles that threaten the village’s prosperity. To achieve this important mission, they execute tasks without hesitation, employing various means, showing no mercy ! »

« Lord Danzo even holds the title of the Darkness of the Ninja World. I believe such a Root is truly magnificent, so I am willing to join Root and am willing to assist Lord Danzo in becoming the Hokage ! »

After listening to Uchiha An’s long-winded speech, a smile unconsciously appeared on Danzo’s face.

He hadn’t expected that the person who understood him best would be a seven-year-old child from the Uchiha Clan, which made Danzo somewhat excited.

Of course, more importantly, Uchiha An’s last sentence reignited Danzo’s desire to become the Hokage.

Originally, after Minato Namikaze became the Fourth Hokage, Danzo had thought Minato was too young and had high prestige, so he had nearly abandoned the idea of becoming the Hokage.

But faced with the promise from the son of the Uchiha Clan head, Danzo Shimura instantly felt he could do it again.

However, Danzo Shimura would not trust Uchiha An based on just these few words ; otherwise, Danzo wouldn’t have been able to become the leader of Root.

« An, isn’t the Fourth Hokage your teacher ? Why are you willing to assist me in becoming the Hokage ? »

« Because I am not used to Minato’s style of doing things. I believe that only under Lord Danzo’s leadership can Konoha have a better footing in the Ninja World ! »

Danzo did not care about Uchiha An’s answer ; at this point, he wouldn’t be able to get anything else out of him.

« Torune, take him down to get processed. »

« Wait a moment, I have a gift for Lord Danzo ! »

Aburame Torune was stunned on the spot upon hearing Uchiha An’s words ; having been in Root for so long, this was the first time he had seen someone give a gift to Lord Danzo right after joining.

Suddenly, Danzo became curious ; the youth in front of him always had some unexpected actions.

To cover up his system, Uchiha An symbolically took out a Ninjutsu scroll.

« Release ! »

A bizarre-looking fruit appeared in the eyes of Danzo Shimura and Aburame Torune.

Uchiha An wrote down some basic information about the Calm-Calm Fruit on a piece of paper ; as for what Danzo Shimura could develop it into, that would be up to him.

Danzo Shimura took the note and examined it, and after reading all the content, he looked at Uchiha An with deep meaning.

« Take him down ! »

Seeing that Danzo Shimura didn’t eat it, Uchiha An was a bit disappointed ; he wondered what expression Danzo Shimura would have if he ate a bite of a foul-tasting Devil Fruit.

Helplessly, Uchiha An could only follow Aburame Torune out, leaving Danzo Shimura alone in the office.

« To think the Ninja World actually has such a special fruit ; why have I never heard of it before ? »

« And where did this brat from the Uchiha Clan get it ? It seems I still need to let Orochimaru take a look. »
