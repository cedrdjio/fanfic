# Chapitre 74 — Unification!

Uchiha An led the Konoha Ninja to break through the Hidden Mist Village’s defense line and arrived directly in front of the Village gates.

Then, Uchiha An directly deactivated the Imitation-Imitation Fruit’s ability, reverting to his original identity in front of everyone in the Hidden Mist Village—Uchiha An of the Uchiha Clan from Konoha.

In particular, the red glow of the Sharingan flashing in Uchiha An’s eyes made all the Hidden Mist Ninja feel fear and shock.

« I am Uchiha An of Konoha ! I am also the former Mizukage Aide of the Hidden Mist Village. Now, I have come to end this meaningless war and let the Ninja World truly return to unity ! »

Hearing Uchiha An reveal everything, the Ninja on the walls of the Hidden Mist Village cursed angrily.

« You are nothing but a damn traitor ! »

« You were once my idol, the savior of the Hidden Mist Village, but now you are just a lackey for Konoha ! »

« Damn Newgate, I will definitely kill you. »

It could be said that Uchiha An’s appearance greatly heightened the fighting spirit of the people in the Hidden Mist Village ; it could be said that for many, admiration had turned into hatred.

Only the various Ninja Clans in the Hidden Mist Village that had been targeted by Uchiha An had an expression of having known it all along upon seeing this scene.

« I... »

Before Uchiha An could finish speaking, he heard an angry shout.

« Uchiha An ! Newgate ! You treacherous bastard ! How could you do this to me ? To us ? »

A woman wearing a blue tube-top dress with a white belt tied around her waist rushed out from the crowd ; it was none other than the Mizukage of the Hidden Mist Village, Mei Terumī.

Tears streamed down Mei Terumī's cheeks, while she gripped a sharp Kunai in her hand. At this moment, Mei Terumī only wanted to pierce Uchiha An’s heart with the blade in her hand and make him pay the price for everything he had done.

« Mei Terumī... »

Uchiha An looked at Mei Terumī, whose face was covered in tear stains, his heart filled with complex emotions.

He loved this woman deeply, and the two of them had once worked together to build an open and prosperous Hidden Mist Village, which they had achieved through day-to-day contact.

But in the end, the two of them were not on the same path ; one was the leader of Konoha’s Root and the young patriarch of the Uchiha Clan, while the other was the Mizukage, the leader of a hostile Village.

The two could be said to be natural opposites ; there was no turning back for them, only battle and death.

« Mei Terumī, I know you hate me, and you have the right to hate me. I am also very sorry to you, sorry for the memories we once shared. But I cannot change my choice, and I cannot abandon my ideals. I want to make this world submit to my will, and I want to use my ideology to create a new order. You cannot stop me, nor can you defeat me. You only have one choice, and that is to surrender. »

As he spoke, Uchiha An’s figure stepped out from the Konoha ranks, continuously approaching the people of the Hidden Mist Village.

« Even though my original intention for coming to the Hidden Mist Village was not pure, I truly fell in love with this place. Over these years, I have always exhausted all my efforts to contribute to the development of the Hidden Mist Village, and I have seen the Village grow increasingly prosperous day by day. »

« My feelings for this Village are like those for my own child ; I hope it can become even better. And you all are the most precious harvest of my time here ; you are my most trusted partners, and Mei Terumī, you are the person I love deeply. »

« If you truly cannot forgive my past, then please end my life without hesitation. Being able to die at your hands would also be my final comfort. »

Mei Terumī felt despair and powerlessness upon hearing Uchiha An’s words. She looked at Uchiha An’s face, which seemed both strange and familiar.

Mei Terumī felt that she had lost everything—lost love, lost ideals, and lost dignity.

« Uchiha An... » Mei Terumī called out to him softly, « Are you really sure you want to do this ? Do you really want to sacrifice yourself ? Do you really want to destroy us ? »

Uchiha An heard Mei Terumī's words and pulled a Kunai from his waist, pressing it directly against his own chest.

« Yes, Mei Terumī. I have prepared myself. This is my final choice, and it is also my final monologue. I love you, Mei Terumī. I will always love you. »

As he spoke, Uchiha An slowly pushed the Kunai into his chest, making him understand what bone-deep pain truly was !

« No ! Uchiha An ! »

Mei Terumī screamed in terror. At this moment, she forgot everything—forgot the war, forgot the hatred, and forgot her identity as the Mizukage.

Now, Mei Terumī only wanted to save Uchiha An, only wanted to embrace him, and only wanted to save this familiar yet strange man before her.

Mei Terumī ran quickly toward Uchiha An, but it was already too late.

Uchiha An’s chest was pierced by the Kunai, and blood gushed out like a fountain, quickly staining Uchiha An’s clothes and the ground red.

Uchiha An’s eyes gradually dimmed, his body lost its balance, and he collapsed heavily into the dust.

The Konoha Ninja and Hidden Mist Ninja, who had been in a tense standoff, were all stunned. No one had expected that Uchiha An would use his own life to end this war.

Everyone instantly realized that Uchiha An was the bridge between Konoha and the Hidden Mist, and it was he who had led the two Villages from hostility toward peace.

At this moment, both sides disregarded their hostility and rushed toward Uchiha An’s side, attempting to save his life.

Both sides dispatched their best medical Ninja, using the most advanced medical techniques to treat Uchiha An.

At this moment, there was no longer any meaning to the war between Konoha and the Hidden Mist ; everyone only wanted to save one person’s life.

...

After being in a coma for several days, Uchiha An finally regained consciousness.

When Uchiha An slowly opened his eyes, he only felt a wave of intense headache and weakness throughout his body.

Just as Uchiha An wanted to speak, he discovered his throat was dry and could only emit a weak sound.

« You’re awake ? Uchiha An ! »

Mei Terumī saw with pleasant surprise that Uchiha An had opened his eyes. She immediately walked to his bedside, gently stroking his cheek, her eyes filled with concern and love.

Then, Mei Terumī carefully placed a pillow under Uchiha An, picked up a cup of water, and fed it to Uchiha An with a spoon.

« You need to drink more water ; your body is still very weak right now. »

Mei Terumī said gently while observing Uchiha An’s expression.

Uchiha An looked at Mei Terumī's smile, his heart filled with happiness. He reached out and tightly grasped Mei Terumī's hand, feeling the familiar warmth.

« How is the Village now ? There hasn’t been any more fighting, right ! »

Uchiha An asked concernedly ; in reality, he was very worried that his sacrifice had not brought about peace.

Mei Terumī smiled and shook her head. She said in a gentle tone : « Don’t worry ! Since you were willing to sacrifice your own life for the peace of the two Villages, there will never be war between the Hidden Mist and Konoha again. »

« The Villages have now merged into one big family. You can rest assured and recover ; when you have recovered, let’s go see the new Hidden Mist Village together. »

After Mei Terumī finished speaking, she gently kissed Uchiha An’s forehead, then hugged him, and the two nestled together in the hospital room.
