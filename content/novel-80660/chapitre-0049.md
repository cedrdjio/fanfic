# Chapitre 49 — Fierce Battle with Two!

« Die ! »

As Egao Hekong’s voice fell, he swooped down rapidly, two Kunai in his hands thrusting straight at An’s chest.

It could be said that this strike was delivered with the resolve to kill An, which in turn made An determined to finish this guy off.

« Armament Haki : Hardening ! »

After An’s voice fell, a layer of black Armament Haki covered his hands.

An then extended his right arm backward and slammed a punch into Egao Hekong’s face.

Instantly, Egao Hekong’s entire face began to deform.

Under An’s full-force blow, Egao Hekong’s eyes bulged, and blood sprayed everywhere—like a silk shop had been overturned, with reds, blacks, and crimsons all spilling out.

An did not let this tactical opportunity slip by ; he put force into his right leg and kicked out fiercely.

« Ah ! »

With just one kick, Egao Hekong was sent flying over a dozen meters, screaming as he tumbled backward.

An hadn’t expected this guy to yell before attacking ; was he afraid the enemy wouldn’t know his position ?

« Hmph ! Trash ! »

Egao Hekong wiped the tears, blood, and saliva from his face and stood back up with determination.

This time, Egao Hekong had learned his lesson and dared not act rashly by rushing straight at An again.

« Clone Technique ! »

Simultaneously, Egao Hekong cast the Clone Technique, with both the original and the clone launching an attack at An together, still mysteriously confident that An could not distinguish his true position.

An frowned slightly, sensing something amiss ; his Observation Haki still couldn’t fully distinguish the difference between the clones.

However, An already had a countermeasure.

« Fire Release : Great Fire Annihilation ! »

An shouted, and in an instant, a massive fireball expanded twenty times its size, slamming rapidly toward the figure An had sensed.

In reality, An had not mastered the Great Fire Annihilation Ninjutsu ; rather, relying on the power of the More-More Fruit, he had magnified his own Fireball Jutsu several times over.

The massive fireball emitted a suffocating heat, burning everything it touched as it relentlessly closed in on Egao Hekong, unstoppable.

Instantly, under the influence of the high temperature, the surrounding mist evaporated rapidly, revealing the true state of the battlefield.

Seeing this, Egao Hekong retreated rapidly while simultaneously performing hand seals.

« Body Replacement Technique ! »

In an instant, the flames engulfed Egao Hekong’s figure, but Egao Hekong himself appeared on a giant rock not far away.

Egao Hekong had quickly swapped his position in the moment of danger, thereby avoiding An’s direct attack.

« You are strong, especially your Fire Release Ninjutsu. » Egao Hekong changed his tone, « However, compared to mine, you are still a bit weak. »

As he spoke, Egao Hekong performed hand seals again, with Chakra gathering continuously in his mouth.

« Water Release : Water Swamp Technique ! »

As Egao Hekong’s voice fell, the surrounding land immediately became moist, and the ground where An stood, in particular, sank by ten centimeters.

« Lightning Release : Earth Flash ! »

In an instant, the entire battlefield was filled with the lightning released by Egao Hekong ; arcs of electricity coiled in the air like snakes, emitting a harsh buzzing sound.

Facing the man-eating lightning, An stood his ground without flinching, not wavering in the slightest.

However, Mei Terumī and Ao sensed the tense situation on the battlefield and began to worry.

Meanwhile, Egao Jianfang and Hozuki Lanyue wore smiles on their faces. The two felt proud of Egao Hekong for unleashing this power of lightning, believing that An could no longer escape the bombardment.

« Hahaha ! Have you been scared silly ? It seems you are no match for me at all. »

Of course, if Egao Hekong hadn’t said this while beaten black and blue, perhaps the statement would have been more intimidating.

Amidst the surprised gazes of the crowd, the lightning released by Egao Hekong instantly concentrated on An, completely enveloping him.

Egao Hekong confidently believed that his attack would thoroughly defeat An, a smug smile appearing on his face.

However, regrettably, after Egao Hekong’s attack ceased, An’s expression had not changed in the slightest.

Would a user of the Rumble-Rumble Fruit fear lightning ? How laughable this attack looked !

« Trash ! »

An’s words were like rubbing salt into Egao Hekong’s wounds, causing him to completely lose his composure.

« Lightning Release : Earth Flash ! »

« Lightning Release : Earth Flash ! »

…Egao Hekong was like a raving lunatic, continuously releasing his Lightning Release Ninjutsu, bombarding An again and again.

However, no matter how fierce the attacks were, they could not shake An’s expression ; he still looked at Egao Hekong with indifference, completely unconcerned by Egao Hekong’s attacks.

It was precisely because An’s face never changed that he exuded an unfathomable aura.

This scene left the onlookers dumbfounded ; the extraordinary abilities An displayed forced the people of the Hidden Mist Village to re-evaluate his strength.

« Mysterious guy ! »

Hozuki Lanyue muttered to himself before joining the battlefield, quickly flashing in front of Egao Hekong and launching an attack without hesitation, knocking Egao Hekong unconscious.

Because Hozuki Lanyue knew deep down that if Egao Hekong continued to release Chakra so recklessly, it was highly likely he would exhaust his own Chakra and die.

« Wait, this guy struck me with lightning so many times, and I haven’t struck him even once ; isn’t that a bit unfair ! »

Upon hearing An’s words, Hozuki Lanyue immediately became vigilant, preparing to defend strictly. He stared at An’s figure, fully focused on preventing An from suddenly attacking.

However, in the blink of an eye, An’s figure suddenly vanished, and the next moment he appeared directly in front of Egao Hekong.

Such physique and such extreme movement speed left Hozuki Lanyue and the others feeling surprised and shocked.

« Lightning Release : Palm Lightning ! »

An suddenly had a tiny, shining ball of lightning in his hand, which then struck Egao Hekong’s face like a bolt of lightning.

In an instant, a shrill scream filled the entire island, and Egao Hekong passed out on the ground from the pain.

Witnessing this scene, the cane in Egao Jianfang’s hand was nearly crushed ; the rage in his heart seemed to take physical form. He could not tolerate An’s ruthless attack method at all.

In reality, although An already harbored murderous intent, he knew that if he truly killed Egao Hekong in front of everyone, it would make things difficult for Mei Terumī and Ao.

Therefore, An decided to disfigure Egao Hekong first, temporarily settling for collecting a little interest.

« Are you looking down on me ? »

Hozuki Lanyue also felt anger in his heart ; watching An inflict such a heavy blow on Egao Hekong right in front of him was not only attacking Egao Hekong but also humiliating him.

Looking at Hozuki Lanyue’s twisted expression, a playful impulse surged in An’s heart.

An slowly walked toward Hozuki Lanyue, a smile appearing at the corners of his mouth.
