# Chapitre 60 — Join the Akatsuki Organization and Destroy the Ninja World!

When Nagato heard such shameless remarks from Newgate, even Nagato, who was known for being gentle, could not help but feel waves of anger rising in his heart.

After all, his Six Paths clones had all been destroyed by this guy, and now Newgate was nonchalantly shifting the responsibility onto him.

This was simply shameless to the extreme !

Nagato could not help but feel his temper rising, but he also tried to calm down ; he could not afford to start a war so easily now.

« Newgate, what is your purpose in coming to the Hidden Rain Village ? And what was your reason for defecting from the Hidden Cloud Village all the way here ? »

An quietly put on an expression of deep thought, as if pondering for a long time.

After a while, An spoke in a low voice.

« To make the world feel pain ! »

At this moment, Nagato, feeling played, could no longer remain calm and unhesitatingly manipulated Deva Path Pain to issue an angry condemnation.

« Newgate, regardless of why you defected from the Hidden Mist Village and came to the Hidden Rain Village, you must leave the Hidden Rain Village immediately, or even leave the Land of Rain. Otherwise, I am determined to fight you to the death ! »

Hearing this, An did not expect Nagato to react so strongly ; it seemed he had truly misunderstood him.

Thus, An immediately stepped back a few paces and lowered his guard.

« My goal is to make the world feel pain. I want to collect all the Tailed Beasts, let all our Tailed Beast Jinchuriki unite, control the entire world, and let the world run according to my will ! »

An’s words fully exposed his ambition, even making Nagato feel a trace of shock.

However, this also sparked a thought in Nagato’s mind : to utilize this guy with extraordinary combat power.

Anyway, everyone’s goal was to collect Tailed Beasts ; this point was still consistent.

Then Nagato manipulated Deva Path Pain to land, simultaneously indicating his stance.

Instantly, the tense atmosphere of battle between An and Nagato disappeared, though both were still secretly guarding against each other, their wary gazes meeting.

« Hahaha ! Newgate, it seems we are pursuing the same goal, which is to collect the Tailed Beasts. »

« Why not consider joining our Akatsuki Organization ? This way, we can share intelligence and even use the organization’s power to better capture the Tailed Beasts. »

« Imagine, if we join forces, it will be such a powerful force ; we will be like gods. »

« We can utilize the resources and technology of the Akatsuki Organization to conduct research and pursuit, thereby achieving our common goal. »

« Moreover, as a member of the Akatsuki Organization, you will gain more support, protection, and opportunities, saving you time to collect information on the Tailed Beasts. »

« Newgate, your joining will bring new strength to the Akatsuki Organization and will also bring you new opportunities and achievements. Think about it, join the Akatsuki Organization and pursue the Jinchuriki world with us ! »

Nagato let out three awkward laughs ; it was rare for him to say so much to get someone to join the Akatsuki Organization.

As for An, he looked at Nagato with a bit of surprise ; he did not expect this guy to have the potential to be a pyramid scheme salesman.

However, it just so happened that An’s goal was to join the Akatsuki Organization, and as for collecting Tailed Beast Jinchuriki, that was just An’s strategy.

« Join the Akatsuki Organization ? Then what are my benefits ? The salary won’t be less than when I was in the Hidden Mist Village, right ! »

Hearing this, Nagato just sighed at how jumpy the other person’s thinking was, but being able to get Newgate to take action with money, and even further control the Three-Tails, Nagato felt there was no problem at all.

At this moment, Kakuzu, who was out on a mission, did not know that he would have to earn the salary for one more person again.

« Any condition is fine, after all, we have the same goal ! »

Next, Nagato and An had a long talk, the two acting as if they were old friends who had known each other for many years.

When Konan arrived with a large group of Akatsuki Organization Ninjas, they saw their god sticking intimately with a three-meter-tall strong man.

It was just an instantaneous feeling ; Konan even felt her status was threatened.

« Our Akatsuki Organization usually operates in pairs. Newgate, your teammate is Kisame, who has always operated alone ; he is also a rogue Ninja from your Hidden Mist Village. »

« No problem, I will obey the organization’s arrangements ! »

Seeing An so compliant, both Nagato and Konan nodded in approval, happy to have absorbed another powerful member into the Akatsuki Organization.

Thus, in the days that followed, An temporarily lived in the Hidden Rain Village, put on the red cloud and black robe cloak, and once Kisame Hoshigaki returned, An would be considered a member of the Akatsuki Organization.

...

Three days later.

By the lake on the outskirts of the Hidden Mist Village.

This was also the scene where Kisame Hoshigaki first met Itachi in the original plot, only this time, it was Uchiha An, the older brother, who was present.

« Are you Newgate, the Three-Tails Jinchuriki who defected from the Hidden Mist Village ? »

« Oh ! Are you the honest guy, Kisame ? »

Regarding the original plot, Kisame Hoshigaki’s life truly lived up to the title of 'honest guy'.

And Kisame Hoshigaki also looked at the guy in front of him with curiosity ; it was rare to have such a tall and strong partner.

« From now on, we are partners, Mr. Newgate ! » Kisame Hoshigaki said, changing the subject. « I heard that the Village is developing quite well under the leadership of the Fifth Mizukage. I don’t know why Mr. Newgate wanted to betray the Village ? »

An did not expect Kisame Hoshigaki to be so good at chatting, asking so directly.

And An already had a way to deal with it, so he replied smoothly.

« Because of those damn Ninja clans in the Village, they did not agree to my marriage with the Fifth Mizukage, Lady Mei Terumī ! »

Kisame Hoshigaki was stunned for a moment ; were the reasons for defecting from a Village this sufficient now ?

Looking at Kisame Hoshigaki’s stunned expression, An knew that saying anything to this guy was superfluous ; after all, Kisame Hoshigaki was a guy who could kill a beautiful woman who asked him out for a meal.

« Kisame, why is your appearance... ? »

Before An finished his question, a look of reminiscence appeared directly on Kisame Hoshigaki’s face.

« Back when Yagura was in power in the Hidden Mist Village, I was a freak born at that time. I was used for Ninjutsu experiments since I was a child because the Village wanted to create an artificial Tailed Beast, so I became a half-human, half-fish creature. »

In Kisame Hoshigaki’s short sentence, An knew how much he had endured.

It could be said that Kisame Hoshigaki was similar to Yamato ; both were products created by Ninjas violating human nature.

It is just that Yamato’s ending was much better than Kisame’s ; this was indisputable.

« Since we are partners, let’s cooperate well in the future ! »

« Yes, Mr. Newgate. »

Just like that, An and Kisame Hoshigaki formed a team and began to take on missions for the Akatsuki Organization, participating in wars in various countries.

It is just that An was not just a mercenary ; when he was free, he would also kill high-level officials of certain countries, causing the ruling classes of various countries to slowly begin to become unstable.
