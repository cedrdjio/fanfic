# Chapitre 2 — Chakra Training!

Just like that, Uchiha An began a pig-like life, eating and drinking in his mother’s arms every day.

As for Uchiha Itachi, it was the same ; his chubby little face was very cute, completely lacking the aloofness he would have when he grew up.

...

Four years later.

By this time, An and Itachi were already three years old, but they looked more like four or five-year-old youths. One has to say that people in the Hokage world generally seem to be taller, even the children.

Unfortunately, for these four years, Uchiha An’s system was like it was dead ; it hadn’t reacted at all. If Uchiha An hadn’t actually eaten the Rumble-Rumble Fruit, he might have really doubted whether the system actually existed.

« Time to eat, An, Itachi ! »

Mikoto said, carrying An and Itachi to the dining table.

« Mm-hmm ! Thanks, Mom ! »

« Thank you, Mother ! »

Mikoto looked at the two adorable, porcelain-doll-like boys with great satisfaction, especially seeing An serving rice to Itachi, which made her feel that the brothers were very harmonious.

However, the four-year-old Itachi had already turned into a cool little boy, just shoveling rice into his mouth bite after bite, which made him look even cuter.

« Itachi is so cute ! »

An looked at Itachi chewing in front of him, feeling that the little boy Itachi was as cute as a hamster, and couldn’t help but feel an impulse.

Unable to hold back, An gently reached out his finger, preparing to pinch the aloof Itachi.

However, Itachi seemed to have sensed something, so he quickly turned his head, trying to escape An’s clutches.

Unfortunately, the four-year-old Itachi’s speed and reaction were no match for An.

An took the opportunity to extend his finger, gently pressing it against Itachi’s little face.

Itachi’s little face was soon pinched red, his eyes filled with anger and helplessness, feeling that his older brother was very childish.

« Mother, Brother is bothering me while I eat again ! »

Itachi knew that scolding An at this time would be useless, so he could only summon his mother.

An looked at Itachi’s angry expression, secretly amused, thinking that his foolish little brother was truly too cute.

So, while softly coaxing Itachi, An kept pinching Itachi’s little face, seemingly completely unaware that his behavior was too mischievous.

« An, eat properly, stop teasing your brother. »

Mikoto said, picking up Itachi and feeding him spoonful by spoonful.

Feeling An’s teasing gaze, Itachi’s face looked even more flushed.

After the meal, Mikoto called the two children in the house together.

« An, Itachi, you two are already four years old ; it is time to extract Chakra. »

« Your father is quite busy, so let me guide you two this time ! »

Saying this, Uchiha Mikoto took out a scroll and handed it to An, and the two brothers, An and Itachi, huddled together to read it.

« This is the best Chakra guidance technique of our Uchiha Clan ; you two must practice it well. »

An and Itachi nodded frantically like little chicks ; after all, this was the Chakra training method that both brothers had been looking forward to, so how could they not be excited ?

Of course, starting Chakra training at four years old is the advantage of being a Ninja from a Ninja clan. As for civilian Ninjas, they probably have to wait until they are six to begin Chakra training at the Ninja Academy, and they would only be practicing those run-of-the-mill Chakra guidance techniques.

Next, Mikoto taught An and Itachi how to refine Chakra. Unfortunately, even though both were geniuses, they didn’t feel the existence of Chakra after working on it all day.

Night fell.

Because An and Itachi were still quite young, the brothers were arranged to be in the same room.

And the two of them lay on the same bed, sleeping under the same covers.

« Alright, my foolish little brother, go to sleep first ! We’ll continue training tomorrow. »

An said, looking at Itachi, who was still refining Chakra on the bed.

Itachi looked helplessly at An, who was picking his nose, his heart filled with helplessness towards his brother.

« Brother, this world is determined by power. I want to master power as much as possible, so Chakra training must never be neglected. »

Itachi’s tone was very firm ; it was hard to imagine these words coming from a four-year-old child.

Of course, for Itachi, who had the mindset of a Hokage at seven, An felt that Itachi’s words just now were normal.

« Power, huh ? Don’t worry, as your older brother, I will definitely protect my foolish little brother. »

To An’s words, Itachi didn’t say much.

Itachi always took his older brother’s words with a grain of salt, as he was usually unreliable ; if he believed everything, he would be the fool.

Feeling that Itachi didn’t quite believe him.

An knew he had to establish his authority as the older brother ; otherwise, if he couldn’t even keep his own little brother in check as a transmigrator, then he might as well give up !

In an instant, the ability of the Rumble-Rumble Fruit was activated, and several arcs of electricity flickered on An’s hands.

Itachi looked at this scene with a blank expression, not expecting his usually childish and silly brother to have such a method.

Of course, this was just Itachi’s inner thought ; if An knew, he would definitely make Itachi enjoy some electrotherapy.

« How about that ? Your older brother can naturally protect you, my foolish little brother. »

« Then let’s go to sleep ! »

Seeing this, Itachi also gave up on Chakra training. After all, Itachi knew the principle that too much is as bad as too little, and his four-year-old body couldn’t handle prolonged training.

The two brothers lay together like this, but before sleeping, An still skillfully pinched Itachi’s little butt ; he was afraid that once Itachi grew up, he wouldn’t have the chance.

As for Itachi, he habitually twisted his body to express his protest ; after all, he was powerless to resist his childish older brother.

Feeling that Itachi had fallen completely asleep, An lay on the bed and pondered silently.

His system was like it was dead, but if there were any other Devil Fruits in the future, An decided he would give them to his foolish little brother to eat.

The Uchiha Clan’s Sharingan and the Logia-type Rumble-Rumble Fruit were already enough for him to develop for a long time ; trying to do too much would only compromise his combat power.

As long as he could obtain a second Devil Fruit in the future, and it was suitable for Itachi, An would give it to his younger brother.

The night passed without incident.

The next day.

Itachi removed the leg that was resting on him ; he could only indulge his sleepy older brother.

So, the early-rising Itachi practiced amidst An’s snoring, and only stopped when Mikoto came to call the two brothers for breakfast.

After breakfast, Fugaku called the two brothers to the training ground outside the house.

Targets could be seen hanging everywhere, along with quite a few shuriken and weapons.

« Yesterday, your mother already taught you Chakra training, so next, I will teach you the throwing techniques of the Uchiha Clan. You two must practice well ! »

After Fugaku finished speaking, he picked up a nearby Kunai, swung his right hand, and it pierced the target twenty meters away, hitting the bullseye.

Originally, Fugaku thought he would win the cheers of his two sons, and incidentally establish his authority as their father.

As a result, the expressions on An and Itachi’s faces were very calm ; they didn’t care about their father’s performance just now.
