# Chapitre 29 — Choosing Multiple Shadow Clone Jutsu!

« I didn’t expect the Nara Clan to have such deep foundations, but why haven’t I seen it before ? »

As the Hokage, Minato Namikaze had never seen the Nara Clan’s Lightning Style Ninjutsu.

An was also very curious about this. Having watched so many episodes of Naruto, he didn’t remember the Nara Clan having such a powerful Lightning Style Ninjutsu !

Upon hearing this, Shikaku’s expression became somewhat unpleasant.

« The name of this Ninjutsu is the Lightning Dragon Jutsu. It is a Ninjutsu that the Nara Clan has possessed since the Sengoku period. It is said that the Ninja who masters it will see a significant increase in speed, strength, stamina, and even the power of their Lightning Style Ninjutsu. »

« It is said ? »

Familiar with various tropes, An knew that the content following the words « it is said » was mostly unreliable.

Seeing An’s doubt, Shikaku explained helplessly.

« A Ninja practicing the Lightning Dragon Jutsu must first undergo physical training to strengthen their muscles and resilience, enabling them to withstand a portion of the lightning’s power. »

« Next, the Ninja needs to gather lightning into their body to temper their muscles and nerves, allowing their Chakra to deeply fuse with their body’s cells to adapt to the power of lightning. »

« This process requires the Ninja to have extremely high mastery of Lightning Style Ninjutsu and great patience. After all, the slightest carelessness will cause irreversible damage to the practitioner. »

After hearing Shikaku’s words, there was a moment of silence in the Hokage Office.

If muscles were injured, they could recover, but if nerves were damaged, recovery would be very difficult, not to mention that this was a long-term practice process.

« Then, has anyone in the Nara Clan successfully practiced it ? »

« Since the Nara Clan obtained this Ninjutsu, no one has ever successfully practiced it. After all, there are too few Ninjas in the Nara Clan who are proficient in Lightning Style Ninjutsu, and the difficulty of practicing this technique is very high. Some clansmen who forced themselves to practice it eventually lost their qualifications as Ninjas. »

After speaking, Shikaku sighed deeply. Possessing such a powerful Ninjutsu, it was a pity that no clansman could master it ; this could be considered a dereliction of duty on his part as the clan head.

Minato Namikaze looked at An with a worried expression, clearly not wanting An to practice this Ninjutsu.

« An, the difficulty and danger of practicing the Lightning Dragon Jutsu are beyond what ordinary forbidden jutsu can compare to. »

« Don’t worry ! Minato-sensei, I believe I can do it. » After saying that, An looked at Shikaku again. « Clan Head Nara, I want to practice the Lightning Dragon Jutsu. I wonder what I need to pay ? »

Shikaku nodded slightly ; the address of « Clan Head Nara » had already explained a lot. Besides, there was nothing more valuable than a favor.

« If you want to practice it, then take it ! In any case, the Lightning Dragon Jutsu is just a decoration in our Nara Clan. »

After speaking, Shikaku took a scroll directly from his bosom. It was obvious that Shikaku carried the Lightning Dragon Jutsu scroll with him, likely having anticipated this scene.

After all, as the Hokage’s aide and the brains of the Ino–Shika–Chō, Shikaku could definitely deduce An’s needs from An’s powerful Lightning Style Ninjutsu.

An carefully took it and nodded solemnly towards Shikaku.

In reality, An was not worried about the danger of practicing the Lightning Dragon Jutsu. His foolish little brother not only developed the Op-Op Fruit for combat but also did not neglect its development in the medical field. Itachi had not fallen behind in that regard either.

An believed that as long as he was careful, relying on the Rumble-Rumble Fruit and Observation Haki, he could complete the practice of the Lightning Dragon Jutsu.

« Hokage-sama, I have already made my choice. I want to practice the Multiple Shadow Clone Jutsu. »

Upon hearing this, Shikaku opened the scroll of sealing, and Shisui stepped forward to examine the training method for the Multiple Shadow Clone Jutsu.

An was also a bit surprised by this result. He had told Shisui a part of the contents of the scroll of sealing, but he hadn’t expected Shisui to actually choose the Multiple Shadow Clone Jutsu.

Perhaps it was because he understood the Multiple Shadow Clone Jutsu the best and had spoken more about it, so Shisui also wanted this cheat.

« The number of Shadow Clones created by the Multiple Shadow Clone Jutsu should not be too high ; otherwise, it will drain your Chakra in an instant. »

« Thank you, Hokage-sama. »

After Minato Namikaze finished explaining the Multiple Shadow Clone Jutsu, An and Shisui prepared to leave.

Just then, Shikaku called out to An again. « If you have any questions, you can come to the Nara Clan to find me at any time. »

« Yes, thank you, Clan Head Nara. »

An was very satisfied with the harvest this time, especially the existence of the Lightning Dragon Jutsu, which could be considered to have made up for An’s shortcomings.

Next, An and Shisui arrived at the waterfall behind the Uchiha Clan, and the two began practicing the Multiple Shadow Clone Jutsu.

As long as he mastered the Multiple Shadow Clone Jutsu, An believed that the development of his Rumble-Rumble Fruit would become even more handy.

As for Itachi, he was still at home taking care of Sasuke, but such a cheat-like tool, An would make sure Itachi practiced it no matter what.

...

Afternoon.

An estimated that the Ninja Academy had already let out, so he arrived here.

An’s main purpose was to find Shimura Kazama.

After all, An himself did not know where the location of Root was, nor did he know where that guy Danzo was.

Passing through the familiar corridor, An soon knocked on the door of Shimura Kazama’s office.

« Please come in ! »

When Shimura Kazama saw that it was An who had entered, a bright smile immediately appeared on his face, and he enthusiastically poured a cup of tea for An.

« You’ve finally arrived, An. Congratulations on joining Root ! »

« Then, thank you in advance for your care, Shimura Kazama-sensei. »

It could be said that even though both wanted to kill each other, at this moment, these two people, each harboring their own ulterior motives, began to exchange pleasantries.

One big fox and one small fox, their faces were full of hypocritical expressions.

« Since you have come to Root, how about working under me ? I will definitely take good care of you. »

« You have to know that in Root, having someone to look after you and not having someone to look after you, the results are naturally different. »

« Especially since I have some connections with Danzo-sama, a genius like you will definitely not be buried under my command. »

Regarding Shimura Kazama’s nauseating words, An curled his lips in disdain.

Even if he thought with his butt, he could guess what Shimura Kazama was planning. If he really worked under Shimura Kazama, he would inevitably be targeted by this guy.

However, An also knew that he had a grudge against Shimura Kazama. In short, he must find an opportunity to kill him.

As for now, it was better to keep a distance from Shimura Kazama.

« I just graduated from the Ninja Academy and do not understand the situation in Root at all. Everything will be according to Danzo-sama’s arrangements ! »

Shimura Kazama heard the meaning of rejection in An’s words and frowned slightly, but he didn’t say anything more.

As long as Uchiha An was in Root, he would have plenty of opportunities in the future to deal with this evil Uchiha.

After the Ninja Academy let out, Shimura Kazama then took An and walked quickly away from the Ninja Academy.
