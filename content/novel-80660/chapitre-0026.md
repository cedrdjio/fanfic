# Chapitre 26 — Uchiha Shisui Battles Aoba Yamashiro!

« Fire Release : Great Fireball Technique ! »

« Fire Release : Great Fireball Technique ! »

Uchiha Shisui and Aoba Yamashiro both chose the Great Fireball Technique to start. The two unstoppable fireballs collided, erupting with dazzling light and incomparable heat.

The grass on the ground was scorched black under this powerful impact.

« Not bad power ! »

Aoba Yamashiro dashed at full speed, two Kunai in his hands stabbing toward Uchiha Shisui from both the upper and lower paths.

Uchiha Shisui drew his katana and took the initiative to close in on Aoba Yamashiro, his Two-Tomoe Sharingan and Observation Haki fully tracking Aoba Yamashiro’s movements.

Just as they crossed paths, Uchiha Shisui suddenly crouched and delivered a side slash toward Aoba Yamashiro.

Seeing this, Aoba Yamashiro dared not be careless ; he immediately brought his two Kunai back to defend, joining them together in front of his chest.

« Clang ! »

The two collided and immediately separated, looking evenly matched.

In reality, it was Aoba Yamashiro who lost, because Uchiha Shisui was much younger than Aoba Yamashiro.

Immediately after, Uchiha Shisui and Aoba Yamashiro engaged in another fierce battle, their strategy for gaining an advantage being to pursue the ultimate speed.

In an instant, the entire arena seemed to become like nothingness. Their figures appeared out of thin air in the blink of an eye, exchanged blows, and vanished again, making them truly hard to track.

Their speed was beyond imagination ; only the crisp sound of the katana clashing with the Kunai could be heard echoing all around, accompanied by the splashing of sparks, like brilliant fireworks blooming in the air.

It could be said that both of their moves were fluid and natural, a perfect combination of strength and speed, full of destructive power and agility. They unleashed countless attacks in a short time, truly creating a sense of overwhelming pressure.

Shiranui Genma : « Why doesn’t Aoba use that move ? »

Raido Namiashi shook his head and explained : « Aoba’s Scattering Thousand Crows Technique isn’t very useful in front of the Sharingan. Furthermore, Aoba treats his crows as partners ; how could he let his partners appear in this kind of assessment ? It wouldn’t be good if they got hurt. »

At this moment, a slight change suddenly occurred in the arena.

Aoba Yamashiro threw away the two Kunai he was using and took out two more in the blink of an eye.

« Secret Technique : Stone Needles ! »

The Kunai with two black strings attached lunged fiercely toward Uchiha Shisui.

Uchiha Shisui, who had been on guard, immediately used the Body Flicker Technique to retreat several meters, dodging out of Aoba Yamashiro’s attack range. He also took the opportunity to cast a grateful glance at An.

In fact, An had long ago warned Uchiha Shisui and Uchiha Itachi that if they ever heard terms like 'Secret Technique' or 'Secret Skill,' they had to run away immediately and hide as far as possible.

After all, in Bleach, Naruto, and One Piece, everyone shouts out their ultimate move names as if they’re afraid others won’t know them ; that is inadvisable.

This time, it was precisely because of An’s warning that Uchiha Shisui managed to escape this calamity.

However, Uchiha Shisui, having nearly been hit, was also thoroughly enraged by Aoba Yamashiro’s move.

« Flash Shadow Clone ! »

Then Uchiha Shisui shouted, and suddenly six figures identical to Uchiha Shisui appeared in the arena.

Then, the six figures rushed toward Aoba Yamashiro at extreme speed.

Seeing this, Aoba Yamashiro was indeed a bit bewildered. With his strength as a Special Jonin, he had no idea which one was Uchiha Shisui’s real body, so Aoba Yamashiro could only try his best to defend.

In reality, this was Uchiha Shisui’s combo technique, combining the Body Flicker Technique with the Clone Technique to make it even harder for the enemy to determine his true position.

In the battlefield, Uchiha Shisui’s six indistinguishable figures attacked Aoba Yamashiro one after another.

Every attack from the six figures felt like the real thing to Aoba Yamashiro, leaving him overwhelmed and unable to distinguish which was a clone and which was Uchiha Shisui’s real body.

« Clang ! »

Another crisp sound of weapons clashing rang out ; Aoba Yamashiro temporarily blocked Uchiha Shisui’s attack.

Then, Uchiha Shisui’s katana began to swing continuously.

As time passed, Uchiha Shisui’s speed in swinging and slashing increased, and Aoba Yamashiro became more and more flustered.

Suddenly feeling exhausted, Aoba Yamashiro had a moment of negligence, and then the Jonin vest on his back was slashed open, with a few trickles of bright red blood flowing from Aoba Yamashiro’s back.

As for Uchiha Shisui, his offensive did not diminish, and his sword momentum and speed continued to stack.

Minato Namikaze saw this and knew he couldn’t let the two continue fighting, otherwise it would be hard to stop.

« Three minutes are up. Uchiha Shisui passes the assessment and is allowed to graduate early. »

Thus, under Minato Namikaze’s intervention, the battle between Uchiha Shisui and Aoba Yamashiro ended anticlimactically.

However, everyone in the arena understood that the winner of this battle was Uchiha Shisui ; if they had continued fighting, Aoba Yamashiro would not have fared well.

Just as Minato Namikaze was preparing to announce the start of the third assessment, An leaped up and jumped into the arena.

« Let’s start quickly ; I can’t wait anymore ! »

« Don’t worry, Minato-sensei, I already know which high-level Ninjutsu I want, the one you promised. »

Hearing An’s words, Shiranui Genma’s expression immediately darkened.

Apparently, this guy didn’t take him seriously at all.

Then, Shiranui Genma walked slowly into the arena.

« Kid, don’t you think you’re too arrogant ? »

« Hahaha, a youth who isn’t arrogant is wasted ; as the fastest man in the Ninja World, Minato-sensei’s student must also be fast in battle ! »

Hearing An’s words, a few black lines instantly appeared on Minato Namikaze’s forehead ; he was clearly quite annoyed by An.

What did he mean by the fastest man in the Ninja World ? A real man must absolutely not be fast.

Hiruzen Sarutobi and Shikaku, who heard this, wanted to laugh but didn’t dare to, so they had to keep holding it in.

Shiranui Genma sneered, though this was indeed directed at An.

« Then let me teach you a lesson on behalf of the Fourth Hokage. »

After speaking, Shiranui Genma drew a Kunai from his waist and charged quickly toward An.

However, Shiranui Genma stopped halfway.

« Why don’t you activate your Sharingan ? Are you looking down on me ? »

Regarding Shiranui Genma’s question, An could only offer a bitter smile.

Now, Uchiha Itachi already had the One-Tomoe, and Uchiha Shisui already had the Two-Tomoe ; as for An, there had been no sign of awakening the Sharingan.

It had to be said that this didn’t fit his identity as a transmigrator ; he didn’t feel like a cheater at all.

Regarding this Sharingan thing, An had no good solution ; he could only let things take their course and see.

Or maybe find a Genjutsu master and let himself experience the Uchiha Clan Downfall Night ; perhaps he would also be able to awaken the One-Tomoe Sharingan.

Shiranui Genma did not give An time to think or respond, and lunged forward, stabbing straight at An’s chest.

An took the opportunity to dodge to the side, directly avoiding the Kunai in Shiranui Genma’s hand.
