# Chapitre 82 — Fierce Battle with Uchiha Madara!

Uchiha An knew that Obito and Zetsu would never let Nagato go ; the two of them must be wanting to take advantage of the window while the Six Paths of Pain were being destroyed by him to launch a fatal blow against Nagato.

It could be said that the two would inevitably resurrect Uchiha Madara, and Uchiha Madara would inevitably seize the Rinnegan.

Uchiha An’s long-range Observation Haki was able to perceive every movement in the surroundings, and suddenly, he detected a powerful aura rushing towards him.

Following this, the Lightning Giant turned its head, and saw a black-haired, black-eyed man approaching him at an astonishing speed.

« Uchiha Madara ! »

Uchiha An was not surprised by the appearance of Uchiha Madara ; he had long anticipated this possibility.

Right now, Uchiha An happened to want to see exactly what Uchiha Madara’s strength was, and whether the current Uchiha Madara, who possessed the Rinnegan, could fight him.

« Hahaha ! Uchiha An, the peerless genius of the Uchiha Clan ! If you had been born in the Sengoku period, I fear that I would not be the most talented person in the Uchiha Clan. »

Uchiha Madara’s words resounded through the heavens and earth like thunder, instantly shocking everyone on the battlefield.

As for Uchiha Madara’s eyes, they flickered with admiration and respect for Uchiha An, as well as a hint of indescribable emotion and regret.

However, what Uchiha Madara said was also the truth ; after all, with an eight-hundred-meter stature standing here, it was not something that an ordinary Susanoo could compare to.

« Then let us fight ! »

Uchiha An shouted, manipulating the Lightning Giant’s massive form to charge towards Uchiha Madara.

Uchiha An no longer wanted to listen to the other party’s nonsense ; he only wanted to use his strength to have a good contest with Uchiha Madara.

Seeing Uchiha An so aggressive, Uchiha Madara did not show weakness either ; he leaped up, jumping into the air with high fighting spirit, preparing to launch an attack.

« Whoosh ! »

Uchiha An violently swung his right fist, which was dozens of meters in size, like a mountain peak, smashing it directly and fiercely towards Uchiha Madara in the air.

Seeing this, Uchiha Madara leaped and stepped onto Uchiha An’s arm, wanting to use this to dodge the fist’s attack, but upon sensing the lightning on Uchiha An’s body, he immediately used the force to jump again.

If it were Uchiha Madara in his Edo Tensei body, he naturally would not fear lightning, but unfortunately, the current Uchiha Madara, with a mortal body, could not withstand the powerful electric current on Uchiha An’s body and could only try his best to avoid it.

« Fire Release : Majestic Destroyer Flame ! »

Uchiha Madara in the air clapped his hands together, Chakra condensed in his mouth, and then he suddenly spat it out ; a hundred-meter-sized fireball smashed fiercely towards Uchiha An, wanting to turn him into ashes.

However, Uchiha An, with his eight-hundred-meter stature, simply took a deep breath and then exhaled directly.

« Hoo ! »

In an instant, the hundred-meter fireball was blown away by Uchiha An’s breath, disappearing without a trace like a candle in the wind.

Uchiha An’s aura swept through the surroundings like a hurricane, making Uchiha Madara feel immense pressure.

« Use your full strength ! Otherwise, fighting like this has no meaning ! »

Uchiha Madara knew that the eight-hundred-meter-tall Uchiha An was not easy to deal with, so he no longer held back.

« Susanoo ! »

Following Uchiha Madara’s low shout, the Complete Body Susanoo appeared before everyone’s eyes.

Uchiha Madara’s Susanoo was nearly a thousand meters tall, entirely blue, covered in a layer of thick armor, and with wings on its back and dual blades in its hands, it looked imposing and menacing.

Uchiha Fugaku, Uchiha Itachi, and Uchiha Shisui, who were watching the battle from afar, were shocked in their hearts.

« Itachi, can your Susanoo get this big ? »

« My Susanoo can reach seventy-six meters. »

Uchiha Shisui and Uchiha Itachi looked at each other, and both sighed deeply at the same time.

Seeing this, Uchiha Fugaku spoke up : « My Susanoo can reach one hundred and twenty meters, but I still do not have the qualifications to intervene in the battlefield. »

« Uchiha Madara is truly worthy of being the man who could contend with the First Hokage ; this power is simply too immense. »

The Ninjas present were inwardly shocked ; a Susanoo reaching tens of meters in size was a realm that ordinary Ninjas could not attain.

Could the Bloodline Limit of the Uchiha Clan really be this powerful ? Thinking of this, many Ninja clans were moved by the idea of intermarriage and looked towards Uchiha Itachi and Uchiha Shisui in the crowd.

In an instant, both of them felt a chill wash over them, shooting straight from the soles of their feet to the tops of their heads.

« They’re fighting ! »

Instantly, everyone’s gaze returned to the battlefield to watch the battle between the two giants.

Uchiha An and Uchiha Madara’s Susanoos collided with each other, triggering a massive roar and shockwaves.

Uchiha An used the ultimate power of lightning to cause great damage to Uchiha Madara, but Uchiha Madara was not to be outdone, constantly hacking and slashing at Uchiha An with the dual blades in his hands.

« Super Saiyan Full Power ! »

As Uchiha An’s voice fell, blue lightning flickered on the Lightning Giant’s body, and every attack carried a powerful electric current, causing Uchiha Madara’s Susanoo to tremble constantly.

Uchiha An’s fists were like thunder ; every strike could blast out huge craters, causing cracks to rise up in the ground.

Uchiha Madara’s dual blades were like wheels of fire ; every swing could slash out sharp blade energy, causing the air to burst with sparks.

The two giants clashed on the battlefield, as if the God of Thunder and the God of Fire were fighting.

Both of their strength and destructive power exceeded the imagination of ordinary people, leaving everyone shocked.

In the eyes of the crowd, neither of their offense nor defense had the slightest flaw, making it impossible for anyone to see who would win.

Meanwhile.

On the battlefield, Uchiha An was also finding it a bit unbearable ; maintaining an eight-hundred-meter stature while engaging in high-intensity combat consumed far too much of his stamina.

Therefore, Uchiha An decided to end this entanglement with Uchiha Madara and change his strategy ; otherwise, he would be the one to fail first.

« I am the embodiment of lightning ! »

The chuunibyou voice resounded through the clouds, and Uchiha An rushed forward, desperately grabbing Uchiha Madara’s Susanoo.

« You seek death ! »

Uchiha Madara roared, and the dual blades in his hands slashed fiercely at Uchiha An.

The dual blades easily sliced through Uchiha An’s arms like cutting tofu, directly severing both arms of the Lightning Giant.

However, Uchiha An had achieved his goal ; his massive body crashed directly into Uchiha Madara’s embrace.

Uchiha An did not give up, directly using his remaining two legs to tightly wrap around Uchiha Madara’s Susanoo.

Just like that, the two were locked in an embrace in an extremely strange posture, a scene that left everyone dumbfounded.

« Brat, what are you trying to do ? »

Uchiha An ignored the shouting Uchiha Madara and instead glared at him, who was wrapped inside the Susanoo.

« What do I want to do ? I want to kill you ! »

Then, Uchiha An began to mobilize his power, releasing all of his lightning power.

Staggering arcs of electricity erupted from Uchiha An’s massive body, dragging Uchiha Madara’s Susanoo into it as well.

In an instant, Uchiha Madara felt his whole body become paralyzed, and his entire body couldn’t stop trembling.

« One Hundred Percent Heart-Piercing Dragon Claw Hand ! »
