# Chapitre 14 — The Attack of Uchiha Obito!

« Hiruzen-sensei, Mistress, Kakashi, why are you all here ? »

Hearing Minato’s voice, Uchiha An immediately looked outside.

He saw that Hiruzen Sarutobi, Biwako Sarutobi, and Kakashi had all arrived.

« Minato, Kushina is about to give birth. This is when a Jinchuriki is at their weakest. I want to take her to another location, with Biwako taking care of her and Kakashi providing protection. »

As he spoke, Hiruzen Sarutobi entered the room and scooped Uchiha An up.

« Of course, the genius of the Uchiha Clan will also come along to look after Kushina. »

Uchiha An hadn’t expected the old man to seek him out, which was perfect as it saved him the trouble of finding an excuse.

« Don’t worry ! Sandaime-jiji, I will definitely protect the Mistress. »

Then Uchiha An struggled to jump down from Hiruzen Sarutobi’s arms and walked straight up to Kakashi.

It had to be said that Kakashi wasn’t as cheerful and optimistic as he would be later ; he still had an aloof, « don’t come near me » expression, looking as cold as Uchiha Itachi.

« You must be Senior Brother Kakashi ! I’m An, Uchiha An. I suppose that makes me your junior. »

« Oh. »

Kakashi’s tone was very cold, and he didn’t say much to Uchiha An.

Uchiha An didn’t mind the cold shoulder, as he knew Kakashi was just a closet pervert hiding behind a cold exterior.

Of course, if given the chance, Uchiha An still wanted to remove Kakashi’s mask to see his true face.

However, seeing this, Kushina pulled Kakashi in front of Uchiha An.

« Kakashi, greet An properly ! »

Hearing the Mistress’s request, Kakashi still had that dead-fish-eye expression. « Kakashi. Kakashi Hatake. »

After a brief greeting, everyone began to get busy. After all, relocating a pregnant woman about to give birth was quite troublesome, especially since they had to bring all the necessary supplies from the room.

By the time everyone had packed everything, it was already evening.

Under the cover of night, Kushina was quickly relocated to the outskirts of Konoha, where the surroundings looked quite desolate.

Naturally, the necessary doctors and guards were essential.

In particular, Uchiha An and Kakashi were arranged to stay right next to Kushina’s room.

Because Uchiha An and Kakashi were both quite young, they were in the same room.

In the following time, Uchiha An and Kakashi took turns guarding Kushina, while Biwako accompanied her twenty-four hours a day.

Unfortunately, because Minato had spent time accompanying Kushina previously, he was now extremely busy.

Finally, the day of Kushina’s delivery arrived.

Everyone was prepared to deal with any emergencies.

« An, I trust in your strength. No matter what unexpected situation arises, you must stay here and protect the Mistress. »

After Kakashi finished speaking, he handed a Flying Thunder God Kunai to Uchiha An.

« Master Minato asked me to give this to you. It might come in handy at a critical moment. »

« Don’t worry ! Leave this place to me. »

Uchiha An toyed with the Flying Thunder God Kunai in his hand, while internally, he became vigilant.

The masked man, Uchiha Obito, was bound to attack tonight.

And the most important thing was for him to protect Kushina and Naruto.

Listening to the chaotic sounds inside the delivery room, Uchiha An’s mind became even calmer.

Suddenly, a faint scent of blood drifted into Uchiha An’s nose, and there was the sound of metal clashing outside.

Uchiha An knew that Uchiha Obito had already made his move. The Anbu guards were likely no match for Uchiha Obito ; after all, Kamui was simply too unsolvable and not something an ordinary Ninja could block.

It was a pity he didn’t know what kind of sparks would fly if Kakashi were to encounter Uchiha Obito.

However, all of that had nothing to do with Uchiha An. Even though he was feeling a bit anxious, as long as he guarded the room, it would be enough.

« Waa ! Waa ! »

It seemed Naruto had been born, but Uchiha An was on high alert, as Uchiha Obito was likely about to make his move.

Just as Uchiha An was momentarily distracted, a scream suddenly erupted from inside the delivery room.

Uchiha An couldn’t care about anything else at this moment and slammed the door open with a kick.

Boom !

As wood chips flew, Uchiha An also noticed an unfamiliar figure in the room.

Uchiha Obito, wearing a mask and a black robe.

Hiruzen Sarutobi’s wife, Biwako Sarutobi, and the doctors were already lying in a pool of blood.

« An, protect Naruto. »

Kushina said, struggling to get up and shield Naruto.

However, Uchiha An instantly flashed in front of Kushina, his Kunai pointing directly at Uchiha Obito.

« Mistress, leave this guy to me ! You take care of Naruto. »

Looking at the five-year-old Uchiha An, who looked fierce despite his youth, standing in front of her, Kushina felt a complex mix of emotions.

But now, she and Naruto could only rely on the small Uchiha An before them.

Uchiha Obito, however, did not take the five-year-old Uchiha An seriously.

« Die ! »

Uchiha Obito rushed toward Kushina at extreme speed, while the Kunai in his hand stabbed straight at Uchiha An’s neck.

Uchiha An parried the Kunai away, and his right hand, glowing with intense lightning, pointed at Uchiha Obito.

« Lightning Release : Lightning Bolt ! »

Under Uchiha An’s control, the lightning blasted toward Uchiha Obito at extreme speed.

The speed of the lightning was not something Uchiha Obito could easily dodge, so he decisively activated Kamui, hiding his body in another dimension.

Boom !

Uchiha An’s attack blew a wall to pieces ; the power directly shocked Uchiha Obito.

« Troublesome brat. »

After muttering to himself, Uchiha Obito lunged forward again.

But Uchiha An was prepared to go all out.

With the extreme speed of the Rumble-Rumble Fruit, Uchiha An constantly maneuvered and attacked within the room, firing Kunai and Shuriken continuously.

If it weren’t for the need to protect Kushina and Naruto, Uchiha An would have long since used the heat and magnetic force he had developed to enhance his Kunai to blast Uchiha Obito.

Even so, although Uchiha Obito had Kamui to dodge Uchiha An’s moves, he was still overwhelmed by Uchiha An.

Let alone killing Kushina and Naruto or releasing the Nine-Tailed to destroy Konoha.

If Uchiha An had dragged it out any longer, Uchiha Obito might not have even kept his life.

Seeing the state of the battle, Kushina felt relieved and had the leisure to comfort Naruto in her arms.

« Naruto, you must be as strong as your big brother An in the future and protect Mommy well. »

Seeing this, Uchiha Obito was burning with rage ; his operation had completely failed because of the brat in front of him.

This operation had not met his expectations at all.

« Brat, you who blocked my actions, I will definitely kill you ! »

Just as Uchiha Obito finished his threat, a figure suddenly appeared beside Kushina.

« Kushina, are you okay ! »

« You’re a bit late, Minato. An protected Naruto and me very well. »

Minato Namikaze felt relieved, but after looking at the situation on the field, he felt that even if he hadn’t come, it wouldn’t have made a difference.

Seeing that the situation on the field was unfavorable for him, Uchiha Obito already had thoughts of retreating.

But running away right after making a threat would be too embarrassing.

« Sharingan ? »

The sharp-witted Minato once again discovered a blind spot ; the red Sharingan in Uchiha Obito’s eyes could not be hidden no matter what.
