# Chapitre 55 — Danzo's Change!

Next, An began to clean up the battlefield, commonly known as looting the corpses.

In reality, there was nothing on these guys that could catch An’s attention. After all, An himself was a wealthy tycoon and didn’t lack these trifles.

Just as An finished cleaning up the battlefield, Ao arrived late with a group of people, much like the police who appear in movies from An’s previous life only after the protagonist has resolved everything.

« How is it ? Newgate, are you okay ! »

« If you’re fine, go eat a plum. »

« What ? »

Ao looked at the somewhat unserious Newgate before him, only feeling that this guy must have hit his head while fighting someone !

Of course, Newgate himself was a bit of a lunatic, so this performance was just a little strange.

« Where are the Egao Clan leader and your teammates ? Where are they ? »

Upon hearing Ao’s words, An instantly channeled his inner actor. His eyes turned red and swollen, tears welled up endlessly, and he gripped his chest tightly as if trying to alleviate the pain within.

Seeing this scene directly shocked the others, and they looked at An’s performance with confusion.

« What’s wrong ? Newgate ! »

« The Egao Clan leader and the other team members resolutely faced the Three-Tails in a fierce battle. They ultimately succeeded in defeating the Three-Tails and, in an act of selflessness, sealed the Three-Tails into my body at the very last moment. »

« In this life-and-death struggle, the team members led by the Egao Clan leader unfortunately died in battle. Their lives were taken during this fierce fight. »

« It hurts ! It really hurts too much. Although I obtained the Three-Tails and became the Three-Tails Jinchuriki, I am heartbroken over the deaths of the Egao Clan leader and the many team members ! »

While speaking, An squeezed a few more tears from his eyes, straightforwardly expressing his sorrowful feelings.

The team members who followed Ao looked at An’s performance and felt that An was a person who valued relationships and loyalty, a partner worth entrusting one’s back to.

Only Ao had black lines on his forehead. Regarding Newgate’s performance, Ao only wanted to say one thing : it was truly damn hypocritical.

It was likely that the Egao Clan had attacked first and were countered, which was why only Newgate remained on the scene.

« Regardless, you have completed the mission of recovering the Three-Tails. Let’s head back to the Village directly ! Leave this place to me. »

Ao didn’t want to hear An talk about how sad he was anymore ; it was better to chase this guy back quickly.

Hearing Ao’s words, An cried with snot and tears as he quickly ran away.

Once his Observation Haki could no longer perceive the presence of Ao and his group, An stopped his movements.

« Isobu, would you be willing if I let you go back to the Hidden Mist Village ? »

An was currently struggling with whether or not to return to the Hidden Mist Village. His goal of capturing the Tailed Beast had been achieved, and returning to the Hidden Mist Village held no meaning.

« Hmph ! Foolish human, would you like a Village that has kept you imprisoned all this time ? »

Isobu’s tone was extremely contemptuous—not just towards An, but more so a complaint and dissatisfaction about being imprisoned in the past.

Hearing this, An nodded in agreement ; after all, in every Ninja World Village, the relationship between Tailed Beasts and villagers had never been good.

« Since you don’t want to go back to the Hidden Mist Village either, then I must go back. »

An said, changing his direction and heading straight towards the Hidden Mist Village.

Inside the Tailed Beast space, the Three-Tails Isobu suddenly became furious.

« Bastard, did you do that on purpose ! Was it worth it just to disgust me ? »

« How could it be just to disgust you ? Disgusting you is just a side benefit ! »

In reality, An just had a plan for the Hidden Mist Village, so he sped back all the way and soon stepped into the familiar Hidden Mist Village.

An walked hurriedly through the Village gate, heading straight for the Mizukage Office.

A day had already passed ; he wondered if Lord Mei Terumī had started missing his massage techniques.

...

Meanwhile, inside the Konoha Village Hokage Office.

The Third Hokage Hiruzen Sarutobi and the leader of Konoha’s Root, Danzo Shimura, had rarely come to Minato Namikaze’s office together.

Seeing this, Shikaku Nara knew that the three of them had important matters to discuss, so he consciously retreated.

« Hiruzen, Elder Danzo, why have you two come together ? »

Minato Namikaze was also somewhat surprised by their arrival ; after all, this was a rare scene.

Hiruzen Sarutobi revealed a faint smile upon hearing this, while Danzo Shimura remained as gloomy as ever.

« I came to tell you that Danzo is prepared to relinquish control over Root and let Uchiha An become the leader of Root. »

Danzo Shimura’s expression instantly became even gloomier, and he spoke in a stiff and cold tone.

« Uchiha An is the only Uchiha clansman I have ever thought highly of. I can rest easy handing Root over to him. »

« However, Root will remain an existence independent of the Anbu. An must absolutely not fully obey your orders, Minato Namikaze ; he needs the right to make his own decisions. »

« Of course, as the leader of Root, An will still be the Root of Konoha, the darkness of Konoha, silently guarding Konoha. »

It had to be said that Danzo Shimura’s arrangement was quite good ; it considered the power of Minato Namikaze as Hokage while also arranging a good future for An.

However, upon hearing this, a look of hesitation appeared on Minato Namikaze’s face. After simple consideration, Minato Namikaze spoke.

« But given An’s personality, can he become a qualified Root of Konoha ? Can he accept this dark side ? »

In Minato Namikaze’s heart, An was just a somewhat mischievous teenager, and becoming the leader of Root meant having to face the cold, ruthless Ninja World without moral bottom lines. This was not something An could face at his age.

Hearing Minato Namikaze’s words, Danzo Shimura angrily struck the ground with his cane.

« I am the leader of Konoha’s Root, and An has also been executing missions for our Root for so long. I am the one who understands An the best. He is absolutely qualified to be the leader of Root, and will absolutely be a qualified leader. »

« But An’s age... »

Danzo Shimura said angrily : « What about An’s age ? Konoha has never lacked young geniuses. Isn’t your disciple Kakashi one ? »

Minato Namikaze was naturally very satisfied with Kakashi’s performance, so at this moment, Minato Namikaze could not say anything more.

« In any case, when An returns from his travels in the Ninja World, he will be the leader of Konoha’s Root ! »

After saying this, Danzo Shimura did not care about the others and turned to push the door open and leave.

As for the appearing Hiruzen Sarutobi, he smiled and left after exchanging a glance with Minato Namikaze, a complete display of slacking off.

Of course, An did not know that Konoha was arguing over his position. At this moment, An was still continuing his massage career.

« Newgate, as the Jinchuriki of the Three-Tails, you will be the trump card of the Village in the future. Whether it’s the villagers or me, we will be counting on you. »

Facing Mei Terumī's words, An hurriedly nodded in agreement. It would be a bit stupid to refuse at this time.
