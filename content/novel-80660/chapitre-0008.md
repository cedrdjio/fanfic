# Chapitre 8 — The War Ends! Everyone Relaxes!

Uchiha An naturally would not refuse money delivered right to his doorstep.

Naturally, the more pocket money he had, the better.

« Minato-sama, could you let me see your Flying Thunder God Technique ? »

Since he had encountered the Yellow Flash, Uchiha An naturally did not want to miss the opportunity to witness the Flying Thunder God Technique.

Minato Namikaze showed a thoughtful look upon hearing this, but ultimately did not refuse Uchiha An’s request.

In an instant, several Kunai marked with the Flying Thunder God Mark were thrown in all directions by Minato Namikaze, and then his figure appeared continuously in various places, leaving behind trails of afterimages around Uchiha An and Uchiha Itachi.

« Too strong ! Minato-sama ! »

Uchiha Itachi’s admiration for Minato Namikaze grew even deeper.

Uchiha An, on the other hand, watched Minato Namikaze’s performance quietly, gaining a glimpse of the fastest speed in the Ninja World.

His current speed was certainly no match for the speed of the Flying Thunder God Technique ; after all, the Flying Thunder God Technique was a space-time Ninjutsu, which in some aspects could not be compared to the Rumble-Rumble Fruit.

However, Uchiha An was confident that as he continued to grow stronger, he would inevitably become the fastest man in the Ninja World—though certainly not in that way.

As long as Uchiha An developed the Rumble-Rumble Fruit well, he would inevitably be the strongest existence in the Ninja World. After all, everyone knows just how fast electricity is.

« You two should train hard. I look forward to your future performance ! »

Watching Minato Namikaze’s departing figure, Uchiha An felt he was full of charisma ; it was no wonder he was one of the most popular characters in Naruto.

« Brother, it seems Third Hokage Hiruzen Sarutobi-sama is preparing to abdicate, and Minato Namikaze-sama has the highest support to become the Fourth Hokage. »

« The other person who might become the Fourth Hokage is Hiruzen Sarutobi-sama’s disciple, Orochimaru-sama, but I approve more of Minato Namikaze-sama as the Fourth Hokage. »

« I just don’t know what Father-sama, or rather the Uchiha Clan, will choose. »

Uchiha Itachi’s behavior reminded Uchiha An of one person : Zhang Ye.

This kid is only four years old !

It could be said that Uchiha Itachi’s performance in this chuunibyou world was truly intellectually superior.

Of course, Uchiha An had to demonstrate his own superior intelligence to maintain his dignity as the older brother.

« The Fourth Hokage will be Minato Namikaze. As for Orochimaru, he will not become Hokage. Even though both have a mentor-disciple relationship with Third Hokage Hiruzen Sarutobi, only Minato Namikaze can become Hokage. This is determined by their personalities. »

« Minato Namikaze is powerful, decisive, and calm, and his personality is also relatively kind and gentle. »

« Orochimaru is also decisive and calm, but his personality is rather dark, and he is somewhat unscrupulous in his actions. Such a person will not gain the approval of the Konoha ninja clans. The only one who can become Hokage is Minato Namikaze. »

After speaking, Uchiha An switched to a cold expression, his whole person exuding a mysterious aura.

Uchiha An’s outstanding performance also earned him an admiring look from his little fanboy, Uchiha Itachi.

« An is right, and Itachi, you performed very well too. You two brothers are truly geniuses. As your father, I am very gratified. »

Uchiha Fugaku said this while directly pulling Uchiha An and Uchiha Itachi into his arms, his face full of smiles, looking to be in a very good mood.

« Now that the war has basically ended, you will be able to go home and see your mother soon. »

Uchiha An and Uchiha Itachi were immediately all smiles ; after all, no one likes war, and home is a special existence for everyone.

Once the news that the war had basically ended spread, the Uchiha Clan began to pack up the camp, ready to break camp and set off tomorrow.

At nightfall.

The members of the Uchiha Clan sat together and began a banquet.

Even though the members of the Uchiha Clan were all tsunderes on average, everyone relaxed at this moment.

After all, the Uchiha Clan at this time was not as severely divided as it would later become ; even if there were some internal conflicts, they did not yet affect the bonds within the clan.

However, Uchiha An and Uchiha Itachi’s situation at this moment was not very good.

« Come, you’re four years old, that’s the age to start drinking ! »

The Third Elder of the Uchiha Clan, acting unlike an elder, poured a full cup of sake for Uchiha An and Uchiha Itachi.

As for the other two elders, they were on the Western Front under Orochimaru, so they did not appear at the banquet.

Picking up what was in the cup, Uchiha Itachi took a small sip, and his expression immediately collapsed.

Uchiha An, on the other hand, picked up the small sake cup and downed it in one gulp, then pretended to stagger away from the dining table.

This was Uchiha An’s « Sake Escape »—pretending his alcohol tolerance was poor, then staggering away after one drink to make an escape.

« Sigh ! Why are you drinking at only four years old ! »

Bored, Uchiha An began to wander around the camp like this.

Along the way, all the ninja clans were celebrating, and everyone was all smiles.

Even in the infirmary where there were wounded soldiers everywhere, those who had survived were also celebrating.

However, when Uchiha An walked toward the outside of the main camp, he discovered two unexpected people.

« Lady Tsunade, Jiraiya-sama ! »

He saw that both Tsunade and Jiraiya were flushed, looking like they had drunk quite a bit.

« Oh ! A brat from the Uchiha Clan ! »

Tsunade instantly pulled Uchiha An into her arms, instantly making Uchiha An feel what a surging ocean was like.

« Drink ! »

In an instant, Tsunade poured a cup of sake into Uchiha An, causing him to choke and cough repeatedly.

Seeing this, Jiraiya snatched Uchiha An away.

« You must be Uchiha An ! Truly a peerless genius of the Uchiha Clan. Your contribution in this battle was not small. Do you want any reward ? »

Jiraiya spoke in a tone as if coaxing a child.

He completely treated Uchiha An like a four-year-old kid.

« Then, Jiraiya-sama, teach me a few S-rank Ninjutsu as a reward ! »

« Ahem... »

Jiraiya looked sideways at the brat, then waved his hand to refuse.

Seeing Jiraiya get snubbed, Tsunade laughed wildly in a wild posture, which was a feast for Uchiha An’s eyes.

« Brat, S-rank Ninjutsu is impossible, but if it’s a lower-ranked Ninjutsu, maybe he can teach you. »

Hearing Tsunade’s suggestion, Uchiha An became conflicted.

Mount Myōboku Senjutsu and Summoning Technique were unrealistic ; Jiraiya probably wouldn’t be willing to teach them to him.

As for ordinary Ninjutsu, Uchiha An didn’t really care for them ; after all, the Uchiha Clan wasn’t lacking in Ninjutsu.

« Doesn’t Minato Namikaze-sama have an A-rank Ninjutsu called the Rasengan ? Jiraiya-sama, just teach me that. »

For a moment, Jiraiya’s expression froze, not knowing how to reply to Uchiha An.

Teaching the Ninjutsu created by his own disciple directly to the brat in front of him—he didn’t know if his disciple would mind.

If he refused again, he would lose face.

« Fine, wait until I ask that kid Minato. »

Uchiha An nodded frantically like a chick pecking at rice ; he couldn’t easily refuse an A-rank Ninjutsu, and getting it for free was obviously even better !

« Then have another drink ! »

Tsunade pulled Uchiha An over again, pouring another cup of sake into him.

After chatting a few more sentences with Tsunade and Jiraiya, Uchiha An found an excuse to leave. If he didn’t run away quickly, he was really afraid of getting drunk and making a scene here.
