# Chapitre 36 — A transmigrator who can't cook isn't a qualified transmigrator!

No matter the conditions, Uchiha An was confident he could complete it.

Seeing Uchiha An agree, Uzumaki Kushina scooped up the little listener, Uzumaki Naruto, into her arms.

« Wait until Uzumaki Naruto graduates from the Ninja Academy, and you can be his teacher ! »

Looking at the little Uzumaki Naruto in her arms, Uchiha An felt a bit at a loss.

Even without the Nine-Tailed, relying on Uzumaki Naruto’s own Uzumaki bloodline, he would inevitably be a powerful Ninja in the future.

« What about Kakashi Hatake ? Isn’t he also quite suitable to be Uzumaki Naruto’s teacher ? »

Faced with Uchiha An’s question, the smile on Uzumaki Kushina’s face deepened.

« I asked Kakashi Hatake about this too, and he gave the same answer as you. When the time comes, you two can have a match, and whoever wins will be Uzumaki Naruto’s teacher. »

Hearing Uzumaki Kushina’s words, Uchiha An smiled bitterly and nodded in agreement ; his master’s wife really knew how to play games.

« Fine, I agree, Master’s wife, but please don’t tell Minato Namikaze about me learning the Eight Trigrams Sealing. »

« Mm-hmm ! I promise you that too. »

Because Uchiha An’s time was tight, he immediately began practicing the Eight Trigrams Sealing.

With the support of his One-Tomoe Sharingan, Uchiha An focused intently on learning the Eight Trigrams Sealing, not leaving until he had initially mastered it.

Upon returning home, there was another round of goodbyes ; after all, every time Uchiha An went out, he would be gone for a long time.

The night passed without incident.

The next day, at the Konoha gates.

As the gatekeepers of Konoha, Izumo Kamizuki and Kotetsu Hagane stared at the Konoha gates, bored, watching team after team of Ninja heading out on missions.

Kotetsu Hagane : « Yo ! Is our Konoha’s Thunder God, Lord Uchiha An, also heading out on a mission ? »

Izumo Kamizuki : « We two have heard plenty of legends about you while guarding here ; I’m afraid you’re off on another special mission this time ! »

Because Uchiha An was so young, and combined with his attire characteristic of Root, his identity was not much of a secret in Konoha.

Plus, Uchiha An had saved many Konoha Ninja over the past year, which gave him immense popularity in Konoha, so Izumo Kamizuki and Kotetsu Hagane recognized Uchiha An at a glance.

« With you two here, no matter how powerful the enemy is, they wouldn’t dare use the main gate of Konoha ! »

After joking with the two of them, Uchiha An quickened his pace toward the outside of the gate ; he was the only one missing from the four-man team.

When Yamato saw Uchiha An appear, he turned and left without saying a word, looking like a cool, aloof god.

Only Uchiha An knew that the later Yamato was also a closet weirdo.

Seeing Yamato set off first, Shimura Kazama, Uchiha An, and Aburame Torune immediately followed.

In the lush, green forest, the four people traveled in silence.

Sunlight filtered down through the gaps in the leaves, but unfortunately, the atmosphere among the four made Uchiha An very uncomfortable.

They rushed along like this for three hours before the four of them stopped to take a short break.

« Eat your military rations ; we don’t have time to waste here. »

Hearing the captain’s order, Shimura Kazama took out military rations and ate two pills directly.

Only Uchiha An made a bitter face, not wanting to eat this stuff ; after all, military rations in the Ninja world were about the same as compressed biscuits, and Uchiha An really had no desire to eat them.

Aburame Torune saw this and looked at Uchiha An with anticipation, as if hoping Uchiha An would do something.

So, Uchiha An took out a scroll he had prepared long ago.

« Release ! »

In an instant, fresh ingredients fell right at Uchiha An’s feet, along with various utensils, of course.

Seeing this scene, Yamato couldn’t help but speak up to stop him.

« We don’t have time for you to cook. We need to set off again after a half-hour rest. »

« Don’t worry, it won’t delay you for long. »

After saying this, Uchiha An ignored the sour-faced Yamato and began preparing the ingredients himself.

« Fire Release : Great Fireball Technique ! »

In an instant, Uchiha An ignited the firewood on the ground, the flames burning fiercely, continuously heating the wok to its limit.

Next, Uchiha An took out a cutting board, held a bright, large kitchen knife, and skillfully chopped the green onions, ginger, and garlic ; the crisp sound echoed through the forest.

Then Uchiha An took out a chicken that had been preliminarily processed and proceeded to chop it into pieces.

Sparks flew from the wok as the chicken pieces went in, making a crackling sound.

The hot oil in the pot gave off waves of aroma, filling the air with a mouth-watering scent.

« With such a strong smell and smoke, aren’t you afraid this will attract enemies ? »

« Not afraid ! »

Yamato’s expression stiffened immediately ; it seemed he was completely disregarded.

Sensing the awkward atmosphere between the two, Aburame Torune quickly stepped in to smooth things over.

« Previously, cooking at the border would attract Ninja attacks, but after Uchiha An arrived at the border a year ago, he killed Ninja from other Ninja villages until they would detour whenever they saw smoke. »

Hearing Aburame Torune’s words, Yamato felt embarrassed to say anything more, snorted to express his dissatisfaction, and then sat down to rest.

As for Uchiha An, he didn’t care about any of that, continuing to wield the spatula, stir-frying quickly in the wok, the chicken pieces bursting with golden light.

Smoke rose from the wok, and the aroma of the ingredients continued to spread.

Seeing that the heat was just right, Uchiha An carefully added various seasonings, and the unique spices drifted into the air, stirring the appetites of everyone present despite their resistance.

The heat was controlled perfectly ; the chicken became crispy on the outside and tender on the inside, and the juices exuded an enticing aroma.

Finally, Uchiha An sprinkled a handful of chopped green onions and green pepper strips, gave it a few quick stirs, and the chicken integrated the fresh fragrance of the vegetables, forming a stir-fried chicken dish that looked and smelled excellent.

Yamato, Shimura Kazama, and Aburame Torune all couldn’t help but sniff ; Aburame Torune, in particular, skillfully served himself a bowl first.

As for Yamato and Shimura Kazama, because they had eaten the military rations, they were unable to eat this portion of stir-fried chicken, and furthermore, they were too embarrassed to do so.

And so, the entire portion of fragrant, colorful, and delicious stir-fried chicken ended up in the mouths of Uchiha An and Aburame Torune.

Half an hour later.

« Let’s continue on our way ! »

After Yamato said this, he rushed out again, while Shimura Kazama glared at Uchiha An and followed.

Uchiha An and Aburame Torune simply tidied up and immediately followed.

Because of the little incident at noon, there was even less communication among the four, and they just kept their heads down, rushing madly on their way.

With the strength of the four, even the slowest, Shimura Kazama, was a Special Jonin, and the four entered the territory of the Land of Waterfalls before nightfall.

« We’ll rest here for the night and officially infiltrate Takigakure tomorrow. »

After Yamato finished speaking, he ignored the others and slammed his hands onto the ground.

Suddenly, the surrounding area for several meters shook, emitting a rumbling sound.

One could see long, green wooden strips rushing out from deep within the earth, intertwining like dragons.

In the blink of an eye, these wooden strips rapidly assembled on an empty house foundation, constructing a spacious and standard three-bedroom, two-living-room house.

Uchiha An looked at the house Yamato had built using Wood Release and immediately shouted in cooperation.

« Wood Release : Three-Bedroom, Two-Living-Room, One-Bath Technique ! »

Hearing this, a rare smile appeared on Yamato’s face ; after all, the name was quite apt and very suitable for his Ninjutsu.
