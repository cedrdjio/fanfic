# Chapitre 1 — Itachi's Big Brother!

Konoha Village. Konoha Hospital.

As the patriarch of the Uchiha Clan, Uchiha Fugaku paced anxiously outside the hospital room, staring blankly at the tightly closed door, his heart filled with worry and unease.

Uchiha Fugaku knew that the person lying in the room before him was his wife, his most beloved person—Uchiha Mikoto.

At this moment, she was experiencing the pain and discomfort of her first childbirth, and the fact that he could not go in to accompany her, a helplessness bound by circumstances, made Uchiha Fugaku even more anxious.

« Shh— » Suddenly, the sound of the midwife came from the doorway of the hospital room.

Uchiha Fugaku’s heart skipped a beat, but he immediately composed himself, waiting for what was happening inside.

Through the efforts of the many doctors at Konoha Hospital, Uchiha Mikoto finally gave birth to her babies.

« Waa ! Waa ! » When he heard the babies' cries, it was as if a bolt of lightning had struck Fugaku’s heart, and the corners of his eyes began to moisten slightly.

After a long wait, the door finally opened.

Uchiha Fugaku hurried inside, and seeing the exhaustion and satisfaction on Mikoto’s face, he breathed a deep sigh of relief.

« That’s wonderful, both children are healthy, and the mother is safe. » The nurse said to them with a smile.

Fugaku’s eyes were filled with gratitude and joy, and he reached out to gently take Mikoto’s hand.

« Thank you, Mikoto, I will protect you and the two children. »

Uchiha Mikoto nodded gently, looking extremely exhausted.

« Let’s give the two children names ! »

Looking at the two infants lying beside Uchiha Mikoto, Fugaku revealed a rare, doting smile.

« The older brother shall be called Uchiha An, and the younger brother shall be called Uchiha Itachi ! »

At this moment, Uchiha An on the hospital bed widened his eyes, looking at this scene in disbelief.

Having originally been a native of Blue Star, he had actually become Uchiha Itachi’s older brother. Was he supposed to call Uchiha Itachi a foolish little brother first ? That actually didn’t seem so bad !

However, Uchiha An immediately began to worry about his own talent. His twin brother had incredible talent, and the next brother was the reincarnation of the son of the Sage of Six Paths. This put a lot of pressure on Uchiha An as the older brother !

« System ? Cheat ? Old grandfather ? » Uchiha An could only recite silently in his heart, calling for his cheat to arrive.

[Ding ! Host is in position]

[Ding ! System is loading...]

[Ding ! Congratulations to the Host for obtaining the Devil Fruit System]

[Ding ! Congratulations to the Host for obtaining a Newbie Gift Pack. Would you like to open it ?]

Hearing the voice of his cheat, Uchiha An felt that this was like One Piece crashing into Naruto ; the Devil Fruit System definitely had potential.

Of course, Uchiha An was also very much looking forward to his Newbie Gift Pack.

« System, open the Newbie Gift Pack ! »

[Ding ! Congratulations to the Host for obtaining one super-guaranteed Devil Fruit draw. Would you like to start the draw ?]

An did not expect that he would have to obtain a Devil Fruit through a lottery format, but what did this « super-guaranteed » mean ?

Did it mean a guaranteed powerful Devil Fruit ?

« Start the draw ! »

In an instant, a huge roulette wheel appeared before An’s eyes, with the names of various Devil Fruits displayed on it.

As An’s voice fell, the indicator on the roulette began to spin, passing over one Devil Fruit after another, circle after circle.

As for Uchiha An, he watched this scene nervously ; after all, this was closely related to his own strength.

The idea that there is no difference in strength between Devil Fruits is all nonsense.

A Logia-type Flame-Flame Fruit versus a Paramecia-type Jacket-Jacket Fruit ; even a fool would know which one to choose.

Furthermore, the Naruto world was one where strength was respected, so a powerful Devil Fruit was extremely important to An.

As time passed, the indicator on the roulette slowly came to a halt, settling on a dark brown Devil Fruit with uneven barbs.

[Ding ! Congratulations to the Host for obtaining the Logia-type Rumble-Rumble Fruit]

« Not bad, it’s alright ! »

Uchiha An’s wrinkled face was filled with smiles ; in reality, he was very satisfied with his Newbie Gift Pack.

The Rumble-Rumble Fruit could be said to be the strongest Logia-type Devil Fruit ; one could say that eating it would surpass 99% of the powerhouses in the Naruto world.

Of course, the newborn Uchiha An didn’t count, as activating a Devil Fruit also consumed stamina.

« Fugaku, look, An is so cute ! »

Looking at Uchiha An in her arms, Mikoto only wanted to be a good mother in the future and let the two children grow up quickly.

Fugaku’s expression suddenly froze, as if he had remembered something.

« Mikoto, let’s go home after you recover for a few days ! »

« Alright, alright, I know. You still have matters with the Police Force to attend to, you don’t need to spend time accompanying me. »

« How could that be ? I am going to spend the whole day with you today. »

Uchiha An on the hospital bed could only watch his father and mother from this life feed him dog food, and soon, Uchiha An’s newborn body couldn’t handle it and he fell fast asleep.

Just like that, Uchiha An returned to his university life from his previous life, eating and sleeping every day.

It was a pity that there were no mobile phones in this world, otherwise Uchiha An would have been willing to be a good-for-nothing for the rest of his life.

A week later.

Fugaku carried Itachi, and Mikoto carried An as they walked down the streets of Konoha Village.

Uchiha An looked at everything around him with curiosity, feeling that it was both very familiar and yet strange.

The Ninja Academy, a landmark river of Konoha, and many Ninja clan compounds.

Along the way, many Konoha villagers and Ninjas greeted Fugaku, but An ignored the random passersby.

Half an hour later.

An, in his mother’s arms, finally arrived at the Hyuga Clan compound ; after all, the Uchiha Clan and the Hyuga Clan were adjacent to each other.

Looking at the clansmen of the Hyuga Clan entering and leaving, it was no wonder there was the meme, « Since Naruto entered the Hyuga Clan’s door, he has received nothing but the Byakugan. »

As the two largest noble clans in Konoha, the Uchiha Clan and the Hyuga Clan had significantly more members than other Ninja clans.

Finally, the family of four arrived at the Uchiha Clan compound, where Uchiha clansmen could be seen everywhere greeting Fugaku, the patriarch.

Some curious folks pinched An and Itachi’s little cheeks, but such people were a minority ; after all, the entire Uchiha Clan was full of tsunderes.

« Finally home. »

Upon returning to his home in this life and seeing the familiar large black television in the room, Uchiha An could only lament that the technology tree of the Naruto world was truly warped !

Radios and cameras had been created, yet all the Ninjas were still engaging in the most primitive forms of combat.

Even firearms appeared in the original manga, but unfortunately, they didn’t have much screen time, which was also a pity.

In short, the technology of the Naruto world was quite warped, which was about the same as in One Piece.
