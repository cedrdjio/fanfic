# Chapitre 34 — Refusing to Participate in the Chunin Exams!

After finishing with the clansmen who greeted him, An dragged his exhausted body back home.

« An, you have finally returned. »

Mikoto said, pulling An into her arms and weeping bitterly. As the wife of the Uchiha Clan head, Mikoto naturally knew that carving out such a great reputation in the Ninja World was inevitably accompanied by blood and slaughter.

And An was only an eight-year-old child ; what eight-year-old child should have to suffer such hardships ?

Suddenly, a small figure rushed out from inside the house and hugged An’s leg directly.

« Nii-san, welcome home ! »

It must be said that even if Sasuke would become a « second pillar » when he grew up, the children of the Uchiha Clan were all raised on cuteness, and they all appeared extremely adorable when they were little.

« You foolish little brother ! Have you been practicing diligently with your brother at home ? »

Baby-faced Sasuke immediately nodded, speaking as if asking for credit.

« Yeah ! I have been practicing with Nii-san the whole time. »

« Good, when you start school, Nii-san will give you a gift ! »

« Thank you, Nii-san ! »

After chatting at the door for a while, the three of them returned inside the house.

Mikoto was busy running around preparing snacks and fruits for An and tidying up An’s room.

Sasuke followed behind An like a little tail, asking questions about everything, inquiring about the situation regarding An’s missions on the border of the Land of Fire over the past year.

An naturally picked out some of the good things to tell Sasuke, enjoying the peaceful atmosphere of his family.

Before long, Fugaku, who worked in the Konoha Military Police Force, returned home. The moment he saw An’s figure, his eyes involuntarily moistened.

« You brat, you still remembered to come back ? You only sent a few messages in a whole year ! Couldn’t you have come home to take a look ! »

Hearing Fugaku reprimanding her son, Mikoto was immediately displeased and stepped directly in front of An.

« An has finally managed to come back ; can’t you say something nice ? Don’t mind your unreliable father. When Itachi comes back, we will all have a meal together. »

After Mikoto finished speaking, she pulled An and Sasuke to the dining table, leaving Fugaku standing there, feeling a bit awkward.

« Brother, you’re back ? »

« Yeah ! How about you ? How have you been ? I was in such a rush when I went on my mission that I didn’t even know about your and Shisui’s arrangements. »

Itachi scratched his head embarrassedly and said.

« Shisui-nii has been assigned to the Anbu, and he is currently out on a mission and hasn’t returned yet. »

« And you ? »

« I was assigned to Haku-Jonin’s squad, and I have two very good teammates ! »

An did not expect that Itachi would still be assigned to Haku’s squad just like in the original plot ; he just didn’t know if Obito would take action from the shadows.

An felt it would be better to remind Itachi.

« The Ninja World is currently teeming with undercurrents, especially with many guys in the shadows who have not given up on Konoha. Be careful when you and your teacher are out executing missions. »

Having given his own hint, An didn’t know if he could change the plot.

In any case, he had already done what he could ; he would do his best and leave the rest to fate !

« Alright, you two hurry up and come eat ; Sasuke can’t wait any longer. »

« Yes ! »

The family enjoyed this reunion dinner harmoniously, though only Fugaku felt that his dignity as a father had once again been challenged.

After the meal, Fugaku called Itachi and An into the study.

« The Chunin Exams are in a week ; do you two want to participate ? »

An took the initiative to step forward and pour a cup of tea for Fugaku, then shook his head to refuse directly.

In Konoha, Jonin fight with pots and pans, while Genin fight and destroy the world ; it was better for him to just quietly remain a Genin.

Then Fugaku looked up at Itachi, who was on the side, as if hoping Itachi would participate in the Chunin Exams ; after all, that would give him some face.

« My teacher also doesn’t allow me to participate in the Chunin Exams, hoping that I can remain a Genin and settle down for a few years. »

In reality, it was not wartime now, with a severe lack of combat power ; otherwise, an eight-year-old Chunin would be too rare.

« Sigh ! Then forget it ! »

Seeing the attitude of the two brothers, the helpless Fugaku could only give up, abandoning this opportunity to brag.

Night fell.

An lay quietly on the bed, thinking about the future. He had never used his Imitation-Imitation Fruit in front of anyone ; it could be said that no one knew he possessed the ability to completely turn into others.

His status in Konoha was already very high, and at this age, it was impossible for him to hold a high position.

Therefore, An decided that he still needed to leave Konoha for the time being and take the opportunity to venture out into the world.

After letting his thoughts run wild for a while, An fell into a deep sleep.

The next day.

Upon waking up in the morning, An did not choose to rest at home ; instead, he dragged Itachi to the waterfall where he had previously trained.

Looking around, An confirmed there was no one else before taking out a scroll.

« Brother, why are you so nervous ? There is basically no one here. »

« You don’t understand, you foolish little brother. »

After speaking, An didn’t explain to Itachi, instead slamming the scroll onto the ground.

« Release ! »

In an instant, dozens of scrolls scattered all over the ground.

These were all the spoils of war that An had captured on the battlefield over the past year.

Because he had been focusing on developing his Devil Fruit and Haki, An hadn’t trained in many serious Ninjutsu ; only the Rasengan could be considered presentable.

Now that it was technically An’s vacation, he decided to practice some of the lower-difficulty Ninjutsu first.

« What are all these ? »

« Ninjutsu I captured from Ninjas of other countries during battles. Take a look and see if you can learn them ; try to do it as quickly as possible. »

Regarding his older brother’s arrangements, Itachi felt extremely touched ; even while out executing missions, he was always thinking of him.

Thus, an excited Itachi began to pick up one scroll after another to learn, secretly vowing in his heart that he would definitely not let down his brother’s expectations.

« Multiple Shadow Clone Jutsu ! »

To speed up his training progress, Itachi directly summoned four Shadow Clones to help him practice.

Watching Itachi, who seemed to have been injected with adrenaline, An didn’t know what had happened.

He just wanted the smart Itachi to learn the Ninjutsu and then copy it with his Sharingan ; he didn’t know why this little guy was so excited.

Just like that, the two brothers spent their time, one diligently learning and mastering various Ninjutsu, and the other leaning against a tree, leisurely dozing off.

Time quickly arrived at the afternoon, and just as An was about to fall fast asleep, Itachi’s voice came from under the tree.

« Brother, I have mastered all of them. Some of the Ninjutsu in here were of a slightly higher grade, which is why it took so long. »

An jumped down directly from the tree, landing in front of Itachi, with his One-Tomoe Sharingan constantly swirling in his eyes.

« Hmm ! Not bad. Next, you can just start demonstrating them in front of me. »

Looking at his brother’s Sharingan, Itachi was also very happy.

After all, as the young clan head of the Uchiha Clan, it didn’t look quite right for An to not have awakened his Sharingan, even if he was powerful.

Next, the two of them continued like this, one demonstrating and the other learning, with An constantly mastering various Ninjutsu.
