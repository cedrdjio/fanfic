# Chapitre 25 — Battle Against the Special Jonin!

Having received the promise from Minato-sensei, An nodded heavily.

A hint of anticipation also appeared in the eyes of Itachi and Shisui ; after all, this was a high-level Ninjutsu mentioned by Hokage-sama, so its effects would naturally not be bad.

« Which one of you will go first ? »

Raido Namiashi said this while taking out a Kunai, spinning it on his finger, appearing very relaxed and at ease.

Shiranui Genma and Aoba Yamashiro looked at each other and smiled, not minding that Raido Namiashi was the one to attack first.

It could be said that none of the three took this matter to heart. As guards of the Hokage, even if they won against these brats, it wouldn’t be very glorious if word got out.

« Ahem ! »

Itachi stepped forward and drew the short blade from his back, his One-Tomoe Sharingan constantly spinning in his eyes.

Clearly, Itachi was bringing out his full skill ; he would not be careless against a Special Jonin.

« What is the meaning of this ? Why isn’t it that other brat ! »

Raido Namiashi felt slighted. The one who came out first was not the oldest guy, which clearly meant he was being looked down upon.

« Because I can easily deal with you ! »

« Bastard ! »

Enraged, Raido Namiashi gripped the black-bladed katana in his hand and rushed towards Itachi.

« Be careful, my black-bladed katana is highly toxic ! »

Receiving the warning, Itachi’s heart tightened, and he slashed out with his short blade.

« Clang ! »

After a crisp sound, their weapons collided.

Unfortunately, Itachi was still too young ; with just one blow, he was sent flying backward.

Seeing this opportunity, Raido Namiashi immediately leaped forward, swinging his black-bladed katana again in the same posture.

It could be said that Raido Namiashi was full of confidence in this strike, as Itachi could not gain leverage in mid-air.

However, Itachi’s expression remained normal, and he even had time to glance at the charging Raido Namiashi.

« Poof ! »

The Itachi that was instantly struck turned into a severed log.

« Was that a one-handed seal ? I underestimated you. I won’t hold back anymore. »

Raido Namiashi steadied his mindset and accelerated again, charging towards Itachi, still using the same move and force to strike at Itachi.

Seeing Raido Namiashi’s speed increase again, a drop of cold sweat rolled down Itachi’s forehead.

It seemed he had underestimated a Special Jonin ; with his One-Tomoe Sharingan activated, he could barely keep up with Raido Namiashi’s speed.

Facing Raido Namiashi, who had increased his speed again, Itachi knew he couldn’t win without using other means.

Itachi’s eyes instantly became sharper, and he directly activated his Observation Haki.

« Stone Manipulation Jutsu ! »

The fighting spirit in Itachi’s heart was ignited at this moment, and he took the initiative to charge towards Raido Namiashi. Lacking an advantage in speed, Itachi ultimately chose to engage Raido Namiashi in close combat.

At the same time, a giant rock on the left slammed violently towards Raido Namiashi.

« Strange technique ! »

Raido Namiashi knew he had to respond ; otherwise, if his offensive was dismantled, he would only be able to defend.

Thus, Raido Namiashi immediately performed hand seals, and Chakra continuously gathered in his mouth.

« Wind Release : Chaos Cross Slash ! »

In an instant, four cross-shaped wind blades cut the giant rock into small pieces.

Unfortunately, Raido Namiashi’s rhythm was disrupted by Itachi, but Raido Namiashi still chose to attack without reducing his momentum.

The two figures instantly crossed paths, and the sound of fabric ripping echoed throughout the scene, drawing everyone’s attention.

It was Raido Namiashi’s green Jonin vest that now had a tear in it.

« Hey ! Don’t embarrass us ! »

« Right, right, right ! »

Shiranui Genma and Aoba Yamashiro put away their carelessness after seeing Itachi’s performance, but they were merciless with their words towards Raido Namiashi.

« Hmph ! Don’t end up capsizing in the gutter yourselves. »

Clearly, Raido Namiashi was also a bit angry ; fighting a seven-year-old child for so long was very embarrassing for him.

Hiruzen Sarutobi and Nara Shikaku nodded with smiles upon seeing Itachi’s performance ; this was a genius of Konoha and a guarantee of Konoha’s strength.

As for An, he didn’t say much. If Itachi had to go all out to defeat Raido Namiashi, it would expose all of Itachi’s true strength. A draw like this was quite good, and it wouldn’t cause anyone to lose face.

Watching from afar, Danzo Shimura and Shimura Kazama both wore doting smiles. Such talent must be brought into the Root ; only the Root could allow such a genius to shine.

« Three minutes are up. Uchiha Itachi has passed the assessment and is allowed to graduate early. »

After speaking, Minato Namikaze glanced at Raido Namiashi. Understanding Minato Namikaze’s meaning, Raido Namiashi nodded and then retreated behind the Fourth Hokage.

Raido Namiashi knew this was for his own sake. Setting aside the uncertainty of the outcome if the fight continued, even if he won, spending so long to defeat a seven-year-old child would make it impossible for him to continue serving as a Hokage guard if word got out.

If he were actually defeated, Raido Namiashi knew he would definitely not hear the end of it.

« Next, the second round begins ! »

As Minato Namikaze’s voice faded, Uchiha Shisui and Aoba Yamashiro both leaped out simultaneously and landed in front of everyone.

« Oh ! It seems you take me quite seriously. Since that’s the case, I’ll go a bit easier during the assessment. I hope you can last three minutes ! »

« I hope you won’t be as pathetic as the last guy ! »

In an instant, the smell of gunpowder between the two rose directly. Both glared at their opponent, while Raido Namiashi caught strays just by lying there.

Just like Itachi, Shisui also directly activated his Sharingan, and two tomoe could be seen spinning in Shisui’s eyes.

« Two-Tomoe Sharingan ! »

Immediately, Shisui’s Two-Tomoe Sharingan attracted everyone’s attention. After all, being able to activate a Two-Tomoe Sharingan at this age made him a rare genius within the Uchiha Clan.

It could be said that if Itachi’s performance earlier made Danzo Shimura interested, then Shisui’s performance made Danzo Shimura tempted.

Hiruzen Sarutobi muttered to himself : « More and more geniuses are emerging from the Uchiha Clan. I just don’t know if this situation is good or bad for Konoha. »

« Isn’t the Uchiha Clan constantly integrating into Konoha now ? Sandaime-sama, there is no need to worry too much. Besides, there is also Fourth Hokage Minato-sama ; I believe he will definitely handle all of this well. »

Regarding Hiruzen Sarutobi’s words, Nara Shikaku could only try his best to persuade him.

Because as the Uchiha Clan slowly integrated into Konoha, once all their power was consolidated, Konoha would not fear any ninja village at that time.

On the field, Aoba Yamashiro’s expression was a bit ugly, and then he performed hand seals.

« Release ! »

In an instant, the Genjutsu Shisui cast was easily broken by Aoba Yamashiro.

« A powerful Bloodline Limit. Using Genjutsu right from the start, I have to say your combat awareness is not bad. »

Hearing Aoba Yamashiro’s praise, Shisui’s expression remained normal, clearly not thinking that a single Genjutsu could easily take care of a Special Jonin.
