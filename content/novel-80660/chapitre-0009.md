# Chapitre 9 — Hidden Mist Attack! Another Achievement!

With that, Uchiha An continued walking toward the outskirts of the camp. Having drunk quite a bit of alcohol earlier, he needed to find a dark, empty spot to relieve himself.

Before long, Uchiha An successfully « fertilized » a large tree.

As he turned back, he discovered that a faint mist had begun to shroud him.

« Would there really be fog in this weather ? »

Fog usually only forms at night when humidity and temperature conditions are met. Given how dry the weather was, he feared this mist was not simple.

Uchiha An looked around, staying alert. Thanks to his height, he could easily hide in the bushes, confident that even if someone were there, he wouldn’t be easily discovered.

Before long, just as Uchiha An was losing patience and about to head back, figures suddenly appeared in his line of sight. He saw them approaching the camp cautiously, each of them wearing a Hidden Mist Village forehead protector.

Uchiha An knew he couldn’t just sit and wait ; he had to act and rush back to sound the alarm.

Instantly, Uchiha An pushed the speed of his Rumble-Rumble Fruit to the limit, his lightning-like figure flashing through the mist-filled forest.

This scene was naturally spotted by the invading Hidden Mist Village Ninjas.

As the Jonin leading this assassination mission, Hozuki Lanyue naturally couldn’t just watch Uchiha An escape back to the camp to warn them.

« Gika, Ang, Enke, the three of you, attack with me. »

Hozuki Lanyue saw at a glance that Uchiha An’s speed was something an ordinary Chunin couldn’t keep up with, so he directly dispatched one-sixth of his top forces to hunt him down.

Instantly, four figures shot out from the group, targeting the fleeing Uchiha An.

As for the hundreds of other Chunin, led by the remaining Jonin and Special Jonin, they picked up speed and headed toward the Konoha camp.

Unfortunately, Uchiha An couldn’t identify the direction well in the dense fog, and having run so far away just to take a leak, it became very inconvenient to deliver the message.

Soon, the four figures were tailing Uchiha An closely.

When the four of them clearly saw Uchiha An’s figure, their eyes were filled with astonishment.

« So you are the Uchiha Clan’s peerless genius, Uchiha An. Your name has been circulating quite a bit these past few days. It seems killing you on this mission will be worth it. »

Hozuki Lanyue made up his mind to nip this genius in the bud.

« Water Release : Water Gun Technique ! »

As a Jonin, Hozuki Lanyue had incredibly rich combat experience. He launched his attack by predicting where Uchiha An would land, firing a liquid projectile like a bullet from his right hand, which instantly pierced through a large tree.

Seeing this, the other three Jonin were not to be outdone and attacked Uchiha An with their own Ninjutsu.

« Water Release : Water Spear Technique ! »

« Water Release : Water Tornado Technique ! »

In an instant, ten water spears thrust fiercely at Uchiha An’s back, while the water flow shaped like a tornado beneath where he landed seemed ready to devour him.

The Hidden Mist Ninjas' attacks were not over yet !

« Earth Release : Earth Wall ! »

A wall of earth several meters high stood directly in front of Uchiha An, immediately blocking his path.

« Do you think you’ve got me cornered ? »

Facing the joint attack of the three Jonin, Uchiha An had no intention of dodging.

Several water spears pierced straight through Uchiha An’s body, but he leaped up and landed on the earth wall.

Seeing this, both Hozuki Lanyue and the three Jonin under his command wore relaxed expressions.

Then, the water spears turned into water, drenching Uchiha An. It instantly gave him a « wet look » sensation, yet there wasn’t a single scratch on his body, nor was there a drop of blood.

« Foolish humans, do you know what elementalization is ? »

Elementalization from a Logia Devil Fruit was a major trump card ; how could Uchiha An not be proficient in it ?

Moreover, Devil Fruits feared seawater, not the fresh water here. If he were fighting by the sea, Uchiha An would definitely not tank a Water Release Ninja’s Ninjutsu.

Meanwhile.

Another figure, identical to Uchiha An, had already rushed back to the Konoha camp.

When he first discovered the intruders, Uchiha An had already used the Clone Technique to send a clone back to report the news.

Once the clone arrived, it went straight to find Jiraiya and Tsunade, who were still drinking.

« Ninjas are coming to ambush us, Lord Jiraiya, please hurry and arrange... »

Before he could finish his sentence, the clone vanished with a « poof » before their eyes.

However, even though the clone only delivered one sentence, Jiraiya, one of the Sannin, understood what Uchiha An meant.

« Tsunade, hurry back and look after the wounded. »

« Understood. »

Soon, under the arrangements of Jiraiya and Tsunade, the Konoha camp began to return to its usual state of discipline.

Minato Namikaze, who was originally in charge of guard duty, immediately took his subordinates to support Uchiha An.

At this moment, Uchiha An was not in as much danger as everyone imagined ; he was even feeling a bit relaxed.

« Damn Uchiha Clan brat, if you have the guts, stop dodging ! »

« You four Jonin are telling a child like me not to dodge ? Isn’t that a bit too bullying ? »

Relying on his speed, Uchiha An kept circling the four of them. Even when he couldn’t dodge some attacks, he still managed to persist until now thanks to elementalization.

Unfortunately, his body was still too weak, and he didn’t have much Chakra or stamina left to sustain the fight.

Hozuki Lanyue and the others were also feeling helpless. Who knew this four-year-old brat couldn’t do anything else, but was frustratingly fast ?

Of course, the occasional Body Replacement Technique also left the four of them in awe, further strengthening their resolve to kill Uchiha An.

« Don’t worry about Chakra, finish this quickly ! Killing a peerless genius of Konoha on this mission is worth it ! »

Hearing Hozuki Lanyue’s order, the three of them stopped worrying about their Chakra and unleashed various powerful Ninjutsu wildly.

In an instant, it was as if a rainstorm had fallen over the entire area, and small puddles had even formed under the trees.

Uchiha An retreated helplessly, panting heavily as he leaned against a tree.

The barrage of attacks just now had left Uchiha An unable to keep up. If reinforcements didn’t arrive soon, he would have no choice but to flee again, regardless of the toll on his body.

« Out of tricks, right ? Brat, for us four Jonin to take this long to kill a four-year-old child, you’ve already done enough to make a name for yourself in the Ninja World. »

Hozuki Lanyue leaped forward, charging directly at the recovering Uchiha An.

Clang !

After a crisp sound, Hozuki Lanyue’s Kunai was parried directly.

« Lord Minato ! »

Minato Namikaze exerted force suddenly, shoving Hozuki Lanyue back.

« Another achievement, young man. »

In an instant, dozens of figures followed Minato Namikaze and stood in front of Uchiha An.

Hozuki Lanyue and the three Jonin under his command looked extremely grim ; it was clear that this mission had completely failed.

« Signal for retreat ! »

« Lord Lanyue ! »

The Jonin Ang let out a low growl of opposition, but after Hozuki Lanyue glared at him, he lowered his head and remained silent.

Seeing this, Enke immediately sent the signal, and a red flare exploded in the pitch-black sky.
