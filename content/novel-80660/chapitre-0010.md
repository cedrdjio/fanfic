# Chapitre 10 — Enlightening the Thinking Itachi!

« Yellow Flash Minato Namikaze, you brats of the Uchiha Clan, I, Hozuki Lanyue, will definitely kill you ! »

After finishing his sentence, Hozuki Lanyue led the three people back, then leaped up and disappeared into the darkness.

Neither Minato Namikaze nor Uchiha An took this guy’s words to heart ; after all, it was normal to spout threats before running away.

« Lord Minato, are Itachi and my father safe ? »

« Fortunately, you notified us in time. We were well-prepared, and after one fierce assault, the Hidden Mist Ninjas retreated. »

With the sneak attack turning into a direct assault, An estimated that the losses for the Hidden Mist Village must have been significant.

Following Hozuki Lanyue’s defeat, An and his group immediately retreated toward the Konoha camp. After all, if they were to be caught in a counterattack at this moment, it would be Konoha that suffered heavy losses.

On the way back, Minato Namikaze looked at An several times.

« Lord Minato, is something wrong ? »

« Master Jiraiya has already told me about teaching you the Rasengan. The Rasengan is an A-rank Ninjutsu that I created. If there is time, I can also guide your training. I hope you can master this technique soon. »

Coming from a commoner background, Minato Namikaze naturally wouldn’t keep his techniques to himself, and Minato was also very much looking forward to An being able to learn the Rasengan.

A quarter of an hour later.

When An returned to the Uchiha Clan’s camp, he saw dozens of corpses lying on the ground, being dragged away for burial.

Fugaku was standing with Itachi, and both of them were silently watching this scene.

Itachi : « Father, why is there war ? »

Fugaku looked at Itachi with a heavy expression, not knowing what to say for a moment.

« I don’t know either. It’s just that wars started happening between nations without anyone realizing it, and everyone is killing without knowing what for. »

« Then how can we end the war ? »

Facing this question, Uchiha Fugaku, as the clan leader, couldn’t answer a single word at that moment.

Because Fugaku knew that trying to coax Itachi with empty words would be ineffective, so he remained silent on this issue.

« Why is there war ? It is simply because of interests. »

« The Five Great Nations and a dozen small nations in the Ninja World are all fighting for interests. For population, resources, water sources, and everything else—it is only because of disputes over interests that war exists. »

« Why is it that in every Ninja World war, Konoha is basically the one being invaded, while Sunagakure is rarely invaded ? That is because Konoha has rich land, a large population, and a beautiful living environment. As for Sunagakure, all it has is the Gobi and the desert. Without interests, who would go to invade Sunagakure ? »

Of course, this is under the premise that Sunagakure doesn’t have oil. If this world had oil as its primary resource, I fear Sunagakure would have long ceased to exist.

An’s answer caught the attention of Itachi and Fugaku, especially Itachi, whose eyes shone with an inexplicable brilliance.

« Then, brother, how can we end the wars in the Ninja World ? »

An hadn’t expected to dig a hole for himself with that ; who could guarantee an end to war forever ?

However, he had to see through the act he started, otherwise, it would be too embarrassing.

Thus, An had to activate his « Talk-no-Jutsu » again.

« Where there are disputes over interests, there will be war. Wanting to end war is a very difficult thing. »

« First, we must defeat the four great Ninja villages and the many small nations of the Ninja World to establish a unified Ninja World. »

« After that, establish a ruling system that aligns with the majority of people or the majority of classes. »

« Finally, reasonably allocate resources everywhere and use time to eliminate the estrangement between nations, between Ninja villages, and between Ninjas. Only in this way can war be fundamentally eliminated. »

Itachi suddenly threw himself into An’s arms and began to cry uncontrollably.

No matter what, Itachi was still just a four-year-old child ; he was carrying too much right now.

Fugaku recovered from his shock and looked at An on the side with gratification. With An, the future of the Uchiha Clan would not be too bad.

Similarly, not far away, the Second Elder, Uchiha Luyan, looked at An’s figure in shock, pondering the words An had spoken before silently walking away.

At this moment, An felt heartache for the boy in his arms and also reached out to hold Itachi tightly.

In his previous life, Itachi, who loved sweets, had suffered his whole life. An made up his mind that this time, he would definitely protect everything his brother had.

After a long while, having released his emotions, Itachi embarrassedly broke free from An’s embrace, a faint blush still hanging on his face.

« Alright, get some rest early ! We are going home tomorrow. »

Fugaku’s words eased Itachi’s embarrassment, and then he stepped forward to pull An and Itachi back into the tent.

The next day.

The Uchiha Clan began to move away from here, but An noticed a different feeling, as if many people in the clan had gained a bit more respect for him.

This situation left An a bit confused.

However, better than before, on the return journey, An was qualified to ride in a carriage.

Just then, the Second Elder approached An with a face full of smiles.

« An, your performance this time was good. There is a surprise waiting for you when we return. »

Saying this, Uchiha Luyan walked away laughing loudly.

An looked at Itachi, not understanding the meaning, « My foolish little brother, do you know what this old guy means ? »

Itachi shook his head, « I don’t know, but since it’s a surprise, it should be a good thing ! »

Now, Itachi didn’t mind An’s way of addressing him ; even An’s usual « low-class » behavior was seen by Itachi as a sign of great wisdom hidden behind clumsiness.

After a day of traveling, everyone finally returned to Konoha.

Looking at the gates so close at hand, many people had eyes brimming with tears.

« Welcome back ! »

« It is our Konoha that has won ! »

Regardless, the public showed great enthusiasm for everyone’s return.

Third Hokage Hiruzen Sarutobi was also full of smiles seeing this. In any case, whether it was the Second Ninja World War or the Third Ninja World War, they had been won during his administration.

His achievements would definitely not be erased, and next, he should step down and yield to a worthy successor.

Unfortunately, Hiruzen Sarutobi was conflicted again about who would succeed him as Fourth Hokage.

« You two go back with Grandpa Luyan first ! I need to report the battle situation to the Hokage. »

« An, wait for me at Training Ground 3 tomorrow. You can bring your brother along too. »

Jiraiya led the clan leaders who had participated in the battle and followed the Sandaime toward the Hokage Building. As for the other Ninja clans, they returned to their respective homes.

A quarter of an hour later.

An and Itachi followed the Second Elder, Uchiha Luyan, back to the Uchiha Clan compound.

Because the battle on the Western Front involved the Third Raikage fighting against ten thousand Rock Ninjas, both sides quickly ceased fighting and made peace, so the war ended much earlier than on the Eastern Front.

He saw that the First Elder, Uchiha Hehuo, and the Third Elder, Uchiha Hongguang, were already there to welcome everyone.

When An appeared, the Uchiha Clan, led by the three elders, bowed to An.

« Greetings, Young Clan Leader ! »

The scene before him left An somewhat at a loss ; it seemed this was the surprise the Second Elder had spoken of.
