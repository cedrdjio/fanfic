# Chapitre 68 — Akatsuki Organization Video Conference!

One week later.

Behind the Uchiha Clan compound.

Uchiha An and Kisame Hoshigaki sat on a rock, while Tayuya served as their temporary guard.

The reason for this was that Uchiha An had received a video call from Nagato, known as the Astral Projection Technique.

« Everyone, long time no see ! »

Uchiha An greeted them very familiarly, but unfortunately, almost no one present responded.

However, Zetsu looked at Uchiha An quite naturally, a wicked smile on his face.

« Long time no see, Newgate. »

At this time, the Akatsuki Organization had not yet started the Tailed Beast Capture Plan, so almost all members were present.

« Hey ! If you have something to say, say it quickly ! This is really delaying me from making money. »

« Kakuzu-san is still as impatient as ever. »

The conversation between the immortal duo was still very bizarre, and the others were accustomed to it.

« Yeah, I don’t like waiting for people either ! »

And although Sasori now knew the current situation of the Hidden Sand Village, he had no intention of avenging the Village.

« I have summoned everyone this time because there has been a change in the plan. Because Konoha has launched a war against the Hidden Sand Village, we must change our actions ! »

Nagato’s figure spoke up, and the others immediately fell silent.

In fact, for the members of the Akatsuki Organization, few people cared about the organization’s goals ; they were just rogue Ninjas gathered together to act.

So, facing Nagato’s words, the others remained silent.

« Then what does the leader think ? Now that Konoha has collected the One-Tails, Seven-Tails, and Nine-Tailed, if we also want to collect the Tailed Beasts, we will directly clash with the thriving Konoha, which will very likely cause unnecessary casualties. »

Zetsu’s tone was a bit low, which was also the helplessness of everyone.

If the Akatsuki Organization wanted to collect the Tailed Beasts, they had to start with the One-Tails, Shukaku, and take the Tailed Beasts in order, otherwise, everything would be in vain.

But if they were to collect the One-Tails, they would face Konoha head-on, which was an unsolvable problem.

« Hmph ! Such a simple problem can be easily solved by Mr. Newgate. »

« Oh my ! Kisame, I have always taught you to be a bit more low-key. »

« But they are really too stupid. »

Uchiha An and Kisame Hoshigaki traded barbs, dismissing everyone else as worthless, and Kisame Hoshigaki even cast a contemptuous look at everyone present.

It can be said that under Uchiha An’s influence, that honest man, Kisame Hoshigaki, no longer existed.

« You’d better be able to come up with a very reliable method, otherwise I will let you two see what true art is ! »

Deidara, the art youth, was already impatient, so he was not polite when speaking to the two of them.

As for the others, they watched coldly, quietly waiting to see what Uchiha An and Kisame Hoshigaki could say.

« Didn’t that peerless powerhouse from Konoha, the Konoha Thunder God Uchiha An, pay us Akatsuki to help him hunt Ninjas on the border of the Hidden Sand Village ? »

« Then the Konoha Thunder God Uchiha An will inevitably take action against other Ninja Villages, and at that time, we will join in the same way. »

« When Konoha truly unifies the Ninja World, they will inevitably have collected all nine Tailed Beasts. When the time is ripe, we can collect all the Tailed Beasts in one fell swoop. »

Uchiha An’s tone was very firm and confident, directly stunning everyone present.

Only Kisame Hoshigaki suppressed his laughter, quietly watching Uchiha An perform from the side.

After hearing Uchiha An’s idea, it took everyone a long time to react.

« The plan is very good, but what assurance do you have that you can defeat Konoha, which has completed the unification of the Ninja World and already possesses nine Tailed Beasts ! »

Sasori’s voice was very cold, and it could be said that it had no human emotion at all.

Hearing this, Kakuzu, Hidan, and Deidara nodded repeatedly, while the others frowned.

Even though everyone’s individual strength was very powerful, they did not have the confidence to defeat a country that had unified the Ninja World, and they might even be drowned in the sea of tactics of low-level Ninjas.

« What Newgate said is correct ; it is a feasible method. » After speaking, Nagato changed the subject and frowned, « However, the difficulty of implementing this method is too great. »

Even Nagato, who possessed the Rinnegan, felt it was quite troublesome. After all, if Konoha completed the unification, fighting them would mean going against the entire Ninja World.

But Uchiha An waved his hand nonchalantly, his expression relaxed and easy.

« Don’t forget that I exist ! I am the Three-Tails Jinchuriki ! »

« As long as I join Konoha, I’m afraid Konoha won’t refuse, and then we will have the opportunity to plot against other Jinchuriki. »

Instantly, everyone showed a smile on their faces ; after all, destruction from within is often more serious than destruction from the outside.

« Newgate is right. Next, we will help Konoha attack other Ninja Villages, and Newgate will infiltrate Konoha and wait for instructions. »

As the leader, Nagato set the tone for this meeting and then canceled the Astral Projection Technique.

Konan, who was on the side, had a face full of worry, looking at Nagato nervously and saying.

« Do you think Newgate can be trusted ? I think he has his own unique goals and thoughts. »

« Hmph ! Who in the Akatsuki Organization doesn’t have their own thoughts ? But as long as it doesn’t affect our final plan, it will be fine. »

« If Newgate has any bad intentions, then I will take action without any mercy. I just hope Newgate can grasp his own heart. »

...

Konoha.

Behind the Uchiha Clan compound.

Uchiha An, who had canceled the Astral Projection Technique, stretched symbolically, and Tayuya immediately stepped forward to give a professional massage.

Of course, this was all reasonable training by Uchiha An, not that Tayuya was particularly observant.

« Kisame, how are the arrangements going ? Have you made contact with the Hidden Mist Village ? »

Kisame Hoshigaki smiled bitterly and shook his head, « No, it seems you cannot plot against the Hidden Mist Village through me. »

Hearing this, Uchiha An sighed deeply. He originally wanted to use Kisame’s channels to incite some Ninjas in the Hidden Mist Village to defect, but who knew this guy really had no friends !

The main thing was that the Land of Water was located in a place surrounded by the sea, which made Uchiha An, who was preparing for war, feel a bit difficult to attack.

Of course, war was only a secondary means. If he could directly persuade the Hidden Mist Village to surrender, it would be even better.

« Then we can only follow Itachi’s idea. I hope there won’t be any accidents then. »

Hearing Uchiha An mention Itachi, a satisfied smile appeared on Kisame’s face.

« Your brother Itachi is truly a genius. »

« A genius ? Then let’s act quickly ! »

Soon, under Uchiha An’s organization, the military power of Konoha Village began to operate.

This time, except for the Ninjas guarding the Hidden Sand Village, most of Konoha’s combat power could join this war.

In short, this time, whether they were participating in the battle or not, everyone was full of confidence in the result of this battle.
