# Chapitre 70 — One-Shot Kill, the Terrified Six-Tails!

Seeing the critical situation, Uchiha An immediately retreated a few steps with composure and quickly formed hand seals.

« Fire Release : Super Majestic Destroyer Flame ! »

In an instant, a massive ball of fire spread out, directly covering the entire battlefield, shrouding everything within a hundred meters in a sea of flames.

The high-temperature flames collided violently with the highly acidic liquid, producing a sizzling sound.

In an instant, a foul stench permeated the battlefield, causing everyone to choke and retreat repeatedly.

The one who felt the most miserable was undoubtedly Kisame Hoshigaki, who deeply regretted having such a highly developed sense of smell.

« I never expected us to be able to collaborate on such a joint Ninjutsu. »

In reality, Uchiha An was quite satisfied with the effect of this move ; if used on the battlefield, it would be a proper biological weapon !

Seeing this, Utakata’s expression was not pleasant. He waved his hand in front of his nose in disgust before speaking.

« This kind of disgusting attack doesn’t suit me. I’d rather not have created this joint Ninjutsu. »

Uchiha An hadn’t expected this guy to be a bit tsundere, which sparked a hint of irritation in his heart.

Then, Uchiha An deactivated his Tailed Beast transformation, revealing his true form.

« I originally wanted to play with you for a while, but now, I don’t feel like playing anymore. »

After saying this, Uchiha An’s figure vanished rapidly from Utakata’s sight. Moments later, Utakata felt a sharp pain in his waist, and his entire body was sent flying uncontrollably.

Utakata instantly realized the massive gap between them. This blow made him deeply understand that the difference in strength between himself and Uchiha An was insurmountable.

« Come again ! »

« I’ll satisfy your request ! »

Uchiha An no longer held back. With his extreme speed and strength, he pummeled both the Six-Tails Saiken and Utakata into wailing continuously, relying solely on his physical prowess.

« Hmph ! I hate people who look better than me the most. Although there aren’t many such people, looking at your handsome face still makes me unhappy. »

As he spoke, Uchiha An landed several more punches on Utakata’s head, while his mouth didn’t stop moving either ; one could say he was practically launching a personal attack against Utakata.

Utakata was too busy dealing with Uchiha An’s fierce onslaught to pay attention to what Uchiha An was saying.

Finally, the moment he charged at Utakata again, Uchiha An instantly unleashed his extreme speed and power, aiming directly for Utakata’s waist.

« Liver-Exploding Fist ! »

Utakata’s pupils constricted and his body stiffened, but it was already too late. Uchiha An’s fist had already slammed mercilessly into Utakata’s waist.

In an instant, Utakata felt as if his liver were about to shatter. As for whether his kidneys were still intact, Utakata didn’t even want to think about it anymore !

« Take another hit of my Lightning Strike Beam ! »

In a flash, Uchiha An punched Utakata in the face again, and Utakata was struck directly by Uchiha An’s lightning, instantly falling to the ground unable to get up.

The Six-Tails Saiken retreated fearfully back into Utakata’s body. It was likely that for a long time to come, Uchiha An would be a shadow in the heart of the Six-Tails Saiken.

Then, Uchiha An slowly stroked his right fist as if wiping a priceless treasure, his gaze deep yet filled with indifference.

« Hmph ! Weakling ! »

Uchiha An glanced disdainfully at the fallen Utakata and turned to leave.

Utakata’s defeat was so rapid that Uchiha An had almost effortlessly completed a one-shot kill.

It could be said that Uchiha An only displayed his extreme speed and strength, yet his combat power had already surpassed Utakata’s imagination.

« Hey ! Are you okay ! »

The helpless Kisame Hoshigaki could only step forward and drag the unconscious Utakata. After all, this guy was now a trophy for their group, even if it was just a living old geezer.

« Let’s go ! »

Uchiha An said, setting off immediately to continue toward the Hidden Mist Village, and Kisame Hoshigaki followed closely behind.

Only Tayuya felt burning with anger, directly blocking the path of the two.

« We haven’t even rested yet ! Why don’t we wake this guy up first and interrogate him before moving on ? »

As she spoke, Tayuya walked up to Kisame Hoshigaki and slapped Utakata’s handsome face twice, pap-pap. Instantly, Utakata, who was already swollen beyond recognition, really did end up looking like Zhu Bajie.

Having received two heavy slaps, Utakata slowly regained consciousness, spitting out a mouthful of blood before finally coming to his senses.

Seeing Utakata wake up, Tayuya sat directly on the ground without much regard for her image, while Uchiha An and Kisame Hoshigaki sat helplessly on rocks. As for Utakata, he was tossed casually on the ground.

« Rest assured, I am a man of my word. For this year, I will listen to you, as long as you don’t make me do things that violate my principles. »

« Alright, start by cross-dressing ! »

Uchiha An said this in an extremely flat tone, but Utakata was completely broken by it.

« Impossible, absolutely impossible. That violates my principles. »

« Then aren’t you a rogue ninja from the Hidden Mist Village ? Why return to the Land of Water ? »

Utakata fell silent upon hearing this, but when he looked at Uchiha An, he spoke slowly.

« I returned for those who couldn’t return ! »

« Speak plainly ! »

Seeing the impatient expression on Uchiha An’s face, Utakata immediately straightened his attitude.

« Lord Newgate, I know your thoughts are the same as mine. Although we betrayed the Village, when the Village is in danger, we will still choose to help the Village and resist Konoha’s attack. »

« Lord Newgate, in this regard we are the same ; we both returned for the sake of the Village. »

« As long as the two of us kill that ambitious man from Konoha named Uchiha An, then we will have protected the Village, and we will be heroes of the Hidden Mist Village. »

Uchiha An really hadn’t expected a situation where he would be asked to kill himself, or rather, he hadn’t expected this guy in front of him to have such great hatred for a stranger.

So, Uchiha An casually delivered two more heavy slaps, hitting without mercy. Even Kisame Hoshigaki and Tayuya, who weren’t being hit, felt their own faces ache.

« Cough, cough... »

Then, the two chose to turn their heads away, not wanting to watch this tragic scene unfold.

« Idiot ! »

Utakata, who had just been hit twice, was furious, but when he was called an idiot by the person in front of him, he froze.

Seeing this, Uchiha An immediately said : « We are the Jinchuriki of the Village ; we are important military assets of the Village. When the Village needs me, I can die for the Village at any time. »

« However, I will not make meaningless sacrifices, especially deaths without purpose. That is not what I want. »

« Kill one Uchiha An, and thousands more Uchiha Ans will stand up. What we need to do is defeat Konoha, which covets our Village, not kill one or two insignificant small fries ! »

Listening to Uchiha An’s long-winded speech, Utakata was completely convinced, wearing an expression of sudden enlightenment as he nodded repeatedly in the direction where Uchiha An was standing.
