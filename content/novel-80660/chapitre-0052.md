# Chapitre 52 — Rounding Up Three-Tails Isobu!

The next day.

On the bridge outside the Hidden Mist Village, Egao Jianfang waited anxiously with a group of his clansmen.

Although there were many Ninja figures in the crowd, Egao Hekong, wrapped in bandages, was particularly eye-catching. Half a month had passed, and it seemed that Egao Hekong’s injuries had healed quite well.

In this world dominated by Ninja, the level of development in medical Ninjutsu was truly astonishing.

« Father, that bastard Ao made us wait for someone. Did he go back on his word ? Does he not intend to let our Egao Clan capture the Three-Tails anymore ? »

Egao Hekong complained irritably, his dissatisfaction plain to see.

At this moment, Egao Jianfang frowned deeply. The future of the Egao Clan was closely tied to this mission. If the Egao Clan obtained the Three-Tails, it would certainly elevate the Egao Clan’s status significantly.

It was just unexpected that Mei Terumī, as a woman, had such capabilities. After being pushed up by numerous Ninja clans, she was still able to secure her position as Mizukage through her own abilities.

« Hekong, don’t be anxious. Most of the Ninja in this operation are from our Egao Clan, and the Three-Tails is already in our pocket. Once you become the Three-Tails Jinchuriki, won’t you be able to easily take revenge on that bastard Newgate ? »

Hearing his father’s plan, Egao Hekong nodded in approval. Once they were in a secluded spot, Egao Hekong let out a low roar.

« I must kill that detestable guy Newgate. »

Egao Hekong’s expression was extremely ferocious, and paired with his terrifying appearance, he looked like a demon crawled out of hell.

Just then, Newgate flashed to Egao Hekong’s side.

« Oh ! You want to kill me ! Then I won’t be participating in this operation. »

Originally, upon hearing that Newgate was going to participate in this activity, Egao Hekong had been very excited ; his chance for revenge had arrived.

But when he heard the second half, Egao Hekong felt very distressed. If this guy didn’t participate in the operation, the difficulty of his revenge would increase significantly.

« How could that be ? Newgate, you misheard. How could I possibly want to kill Lord Newgate ? »

« Besides, this is a mission for the Village, and you cannot easily refuse it, Newgate. »

Egao Hekong’s tone was very firm, looking as if he had Newgate all figured out.

« Hmph ! Unless you call me 'Pops', I’m going to go find the Mizukage and cancel the mission. »

After saying this, Newgate turned around and prepared to leave without the slightest hesitation.

Seeing this, Egao Hekong panicked and hurried to catch up behind Newgate.

« Pops ! »

Although this « Pops » was barely audible, Newgate still heard it and nodded in approval.

« Since that’s the case, then as your 'Pops', I’ll accompany you on this trip. »

Hearing this, Egao Hekong felt relieved, and after signaling to Egao Jianfang, they set off.

And after Newgate’s party set off, Ao led another group of Ninja out of the gates of the Hidden Mist Village.

...

After leaving the Hidden Mist Village, Newgate followed directly behind Egao Jianfang and Egao Hekong.

The group dashed along the way, their speed impressive.

Everyone displayed good strength ; it could be said that there was not a single weakling in this operation.

However, as time passed, Newgate felt once again that he was surrounded by the others. It seemed that this group of guys all had some connection to the Egao Clan.

Despite this, Newgate was accustomed to this situation ; after all, it wasn’t the first time he had been treated this way.

However, Newgate did not act rashly, continuing to maintain his speed and silently following behind the group.

« We’re here ! »

Before everyone appeared a vast lake, like a boundless mirror, surrounded by lush forests.

The environment here was picturesque, full of elegance and magic, as if it were a tranquil sanctuary protected by nature, exuding a peaceful and harmonious atmosphere that made one reluctant to leave.

Newgate felt that this was a good place suitable for seclusion ; perhaps in the future, he could live in seclusion here.

« Newgate, your strength is the greatest here, so why don’t you be the one to lure the Three-Tails out ! »

Egao Jianfang dug a hole for Newgate right from the start ; it was very obvious that he wanted Newgate to act as bait, and perhaps even attack Newgate when he encountered danger.

But was Newgate someone easy to manipulate ? Would he fall for such a brainless trap ?

« I’ve only just joined the big family of the Hidden Mist Village, so I don’t know as much about the Three-Tails as Clan Leader Egao. As the leader of the largest Ninja clan in the Hidden Mist Village, wouldn’t dealing with a Three-Tails be easy for you ? »

Hearing Newgate’s words, a flash of hesitation and dissatisfaction crossed Egao Jianfang’s face, but Egao Jianfang also understood that now was not the time to argue with Newgate.

If he were to force Newgate away, everything would become difficult. Only when the situation became chaotic would it be the right time for him to make a move.

Egao Jianfang nodded helplessly, his expression softening slightly : « The Three-Tails is hidden in the large lake beneath our feet. What it’s best at is Water Release, and what’s harder to deal with is the Three-Tails' massive size. »

« We need someone with great strength to lure the Three-Tails out. Once the Three-Tails surfaces, we will first bombard it with Ninjutsu, and then use sealing techniques to seal the Three-Tails ! »

Then, Egao Jianfang pointed his finger at some unlucky guy, telling him to go down and lure the Three-Tails out.

Seeing this, the guy splashed into the water, and then his whole person disappeared.

Time ticked away, and the people on the water who had been on guard slowly began to lose patience.

« Tatsumi couldn’t have died down there, right ! »

« With no movement for so long, it’s very likely he was killed by the Three-Tails ! »

Just as everyone was discussing it heatedly, the surface of the water suddenly began to ripple.

A figure soared into the air from the water like lightning, with a huge wave vortex unfolding around his body, causing violent tremors for everyone on the water, making it difficult for everyone to steady themselves.

« The Three-Tails is about to appear ! Everyone, get ready ! »

In an instant, the figure of the Three-Tails suddenly emerged above the water, its massive physique drawing gasps of awe from the crowd.

Subsequently, everyone quickly formed hand seals, preparing to unleash their most powerful Ninjutsu to bombard the Three-Tails.

Newgate retreated unhurriedly to the back of the group, lightning constantly gathering in his hands.

In Newgate’s hands, the electric light intertwined into a shimmering spectacle, revealing an incomparably powerful force.

Everyone’s gaze was focused on the Three-Tails, the atmosphere heavy and tense.

Everyone strove to raise the power of their Ninjutsu to the extreme, pushing their strength to the limit.

« Attack together ! »

In an instant, everyone present simultaneously unleashed various powerful Ninjutsu, and the gathered power bombarded the massive Three-Tails violently.

A violent explosion sounded on the lake, water splashed everywhere, and the entire lake surface seemed to be torn apart by the intense impact.

Undoubtedly, facing this fatal blow, Egao Jianfang was convinced that their Ninjutsu would definitely be able to heavily injure the Three-Tails, and they would be able to take the opportunity to seal the Three-Tails.
