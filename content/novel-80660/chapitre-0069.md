# Chapitre 69 — Six-Tails Jinchuriki Utakata!

Just as Konoha was actively preparing for war, ready to attack the Hidden Mist Village, An, acting as the commander, decided to take action early and set off in advance with Kisame Hoshigaki and Tayuya.

The three of them departed from Konoha in the Land of Fire two weeks ahead of schedule, taking a boat eastward in the direction of the Hidden Mist Village.

The journey by sea was not calm ; the rough, surging ocean made the boat rock unsteadily.

However, facing the gale and raging waves, An’s eyes revealed a firm determination, for An knew that this operation was crucial to the future of the Ninja World.

Finally, just as An was about to reach the limit of endurance for the tossing of the sea voyage, they stepped onto the land of the Land of Water.

However, this time no one came to intercept them ; it seemed that the Hidden Mist Village was also consolidating its forces.

As they sped along the way, An and Kisame felt fine, but Tayuya was struggling to keep up.

« There is an inn ahead ; it looks like we can take a break. »

Tayuya looked helplessly at the two men beside her ; these two guys had monster-like stamina, but she certainly wasn’t that much of a beast.

« Alright, let’s take a break. »

The three stopped and walked towards the inn nearby, but as they approached, An caught the scent of blood ; it seemed something had happened inside.

Kisame Hoshigaki maintained a calm expression, his muscles tensed, radiating a powerful aura, ready to strike at any moment.

Tayuya stood silently beside the two, her eyes revealing coldness and determination, as if she had reverted to her former cold-blooded and sharp-tongued personality at this moment.

Just then, a lazy and handsome figure walked out from inside, pausing for a split second when seeing An and Kisame Hoshigaki.

« Long time no see ! Kisame, some nasty guys inside have already been taken care of by me. »

Seeing the figure that emerged, Kisame Hoshigaki breathed a sigh of relief.

An recognized the guy in front of him : the rogue ninja from the Hidden Mist Village, the Six-Tails Jinchuriki, Utakata.

« Utakata-san, I’ve always wanted to spar with you ; I didn’t expect to meet you today. »

As he spoke, Kisame Hoshigaki drew the great sword Samehada from his back, striking a pose ready to fight.

As for Utakata, he maintained his lazy demeanor, which, combined with his handsome face, was undeniably quite charming.

« Wanna fight ? Actually, I’m not that interested in you, but I am quite interested in the guy beside you. »

« The Hidden Mist Village rogue ninja, the Three-Tails Jinchuriki Newgate. I wonder if you are qualified to fight me ! »

An didn’t expect that this guy Utakata had some potential to be a show-off ; he certainly looked quite intimidating.

« Oh ! A Hidden Mist Village rogue ninja, the Jinchuriki of the Six-Tails Saiken. That sounds like a big reputation. » As he spoke, An waved his hand dismissively, his tone becoming quite contemptuous. « I, Newgate, don’t fight battles without benefits, especially against strong opponents like you—there’s absolutely no benefit in it. »

« Then what benefits do you want ? »

Utakata’s tone remained very calm ; he didn’t care at all about the benefits An proposed. Having traveled the Ninja World for many years, he had managed to save up quite a few things.

Kisame Hoshigaki, seeing this, revealed a strange smile on his face.

« Newgate-san loves to adopt strong people as his sons. If you lose, just call him 'Oyaji' ! »

Having transformed from an honest man into a trickster, Kisame Hoshigaki naturally set Utakata up ; it was just a matter of how Utakata would choose.

After thinking about it briefly, Utakata shook his head to refuse.

« Change the condition ! It’s impossible for me to agree to such a strange request. »

As the Jinchuriki of the Six-Tails Saiken, Utakata did not believe he had a one-hundred-percent chance of defeating another Jinchuriki ; if he were to fail, he would suffer a great loss.

« We are both strong ; if there isn’t enough benefit, I don’t want to go through all that trouble. »

« Then, without violating one’s principles, follow the other party’s orders for one year. »

« Alright ! »

Just like that, the duel between An and Utakata was settled, and the two chose an open space to stand facing each other.

Above the sea of trees, a strong wind whistled past, causing the forest to surge like waves.

An and Utakata stood on opposite sides, both radiating a powerful aura, staring at each other ; a tense atmosphere of battle permeated the air.

An’s white cloak fluttered in the wind, his gaze cold and sharp. His right hand tightly gripped a fake Murakumogiri he had forged, a counterfeit Supreme Grade sword, not hiding the blatant murderous intent on his face.

Utakata wore blue robes, his long hair dancing in the wind, exuding a mysterious aura. His eyes were full of confidence and mockery, as if he didn’t care about An’s strength at all.

« Start ! »

At Tayuya’s command, the battle began immediately. An rushed forward like lightning, and with a flash of the blade, he slashed fiercely and peerlessly toward Utakata.

Utakata smiled lightly, dodging An’s attack with a sidestep, then used his quick movement technique to flank An and launch a counterattack.

However, An’s attack exceeded Utakata’s imagination ; the Murakumogiri in An’s hand suddenly grew several times larger, and the attack he unleashed was accurate and fatal.

Every slash could easily pierce through Utakata’s defenses, as if he had predicted every move, perfectly meeting and countering them.

Utakata’s expression gradually became serious. He dealt with An’s attacks with all his might, and with the Six-Tails Saiken occasionally intervening, Utakata barely managed to avoid every fatal strike.

It could be said that not long after the battle began, Utakata knew he might not be the opponent of the person in front of him, but he wouldn’t give up easily either.

In an instant, Utakata’s body was covered with a layer of Tailed Beast cloak, clearly borrowing the power of the Six-Tails.

« Hiruma ! »

A highly corrosive liquid secreted directly from Utakata’s Tailed Beast cloak, dripping onto the Murakumogiri and instantly corroding An’s pirated Murakumogiri.

Seeing this, An’s eyes revealed a cold bloodlust, and he also chose to cover himself in a Tailed Beast cloak.

« Water Release : Water Explosion Wave ! »

In an instant, An spat out countless water spheres from his mouth, which then exploded around Utakata at an extremely high speed.

Seeing this, Utakata chose to pull back and dodge, constantly leaping backward.

Suddenly, An’s figure rushed out from the water curtain, his right hand transformed into the massive arm of the Three-Tails Isobu, striking Utakata with a punch.

« Boom ! »

Having taken just one hit from An, Utakata was knocked back, spitting blood, crashing through several large trees before falling heavily to the ground.

Unwilling to admit defeat, Utakata struggled to stand up and chose to fully transform into the Tailed Beast ; the form of the Six-Tails Saiken now lay on the ground like a small hill.

« Shiranui ! »

In an instant, a large amount of acidic liquid spewed from the mouth of the Six-Tails Saiken, directly covering the battlefield where the two were located.

The fallen trees and stones on the ground were immediately corroded by the acidic mucus of the Six-Tails Saiken ; this move looked incredibly formidable.
