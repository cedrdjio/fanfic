# Chapitre 26 — Returning to the Village

« What, you ! ! »

The Rogue Ninja’s crafty, savage grin suddenly froze.

The next instant, a tremendous force erupted from the front, snapping his arm directly.

Before he could react, Matsumi slashed out with his blade again.

Pfft—

On the rippling surface of the lake, a mist of blood exploded in mid-air.

The Rogue Ninja, who had originally planned an ambush, had not expected that Matsumi had never agreed to his proposal from the very beginning.

He had slaughtered an entire Village, showing no regard for the lives of others.

Yet, when faced with a life-threatening crisis himself, he fantasized about being spared.

Where does such a good thing exist ?

With a single slash, head and body were separated, and the Chakra condensed at the Rogue Ninja’s feet dissipated in an instant.

Accompanied by the sound of a heavy object sinking into the water, everything returned to calm once again.

[C-rank mission complete : Hunt down the Rogue Ninja who committed evil acts. Reward : 50 points.]

Reaching out to catch the heavy parcel, Matsumi turned and walked toward the lakeshore.

« It looks like all the valuable belongings from that Village are in this parcel, » Senju Matsuaki said.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi said with some worry : « How should we handle this situation next ? The Village that commissioned the mission has been destroyed, and I don’t know how it would be best to settle the child named Hanako. »

« As for the disposal of these belongings, let’s leave that to Uncle Hashirama, » Matsumi said with a wry smile.

He was also going out on a mission for the first time, and he hadn’t expected to encounter such an awkward situation.

To say they had completed the mission to wipe out the Rogue Ninja was true ; the prompt from the Golden Finger just now was one example.

But they had arrived a step late after all ; the Village that had commissioned Konoha to wipe out the Rogue Ninja had already been destroyed, and he felt ashamed to accept this money.

« As for the arrangements for Hanako, I do know a little about that, » Matsumi said.

« In the current situation, Hanako will likely be sent to the nearest Village or town, where she will either be adopted or placed in an orphanage in the Land of Fire. »

« As for Konoha’s orphanage, it currently mainly takes in orphans of Ninjas who sacrificed themselves or civilians who died accidentally in the Village ; it does not easily accept orphans from other Villages. »

These systems had only been gradually perfected by Konoha in recent years, and not easily accepting orphans from other Villages was out of precaution against enemy spies.

« I hope there will be a good arrangement in the end, » Hyuga Tsukuyomi said softly.

With this matter concluded, the two followed the path they had come by back to the destroyed Village.

During the time the two were wiping out the Rogue Ninja, Senju Hashirama’s Wood Clone had not been idle here either.

The burned buildings had been partially cleared, and the corpses scattered everywhere had been uniformly buried at a suitable location at the foot of the mountain.

If ordinary people were to do these things, it would probably take ten days or more even with all their might.

However, for a Ninja, it could be easily accomplished with the assistance of Ninjutsu.

Seeing the two return, Hashirama smiled and said : « It looks like your mission has been completed. »

Hashirama was holding Hanako in his arms ; at this moment, the child, only two or three years old, had fallen asleep from exhaustion.

After Matsumi and Tsukuyomi exchanged a look, they first roughly described the situation just now, and then raised their confusion.

« You encountered such confusion as well, huh. »

Senju Hashirama smiled with some emotion.

« When Tobirama and I were a little older than you are now, we encountered a similar situation. »

« At that time, my father told us that the mission commission is only related to the content of the mission ; if the mission objective is achieved, this commission is deserved. »

After saying this, he looked at the two and said : « This mission was to wipe out the Rogue Ninja, not to protect this Village. It might sound cold, but that is the logic. »

« To use Tobirama’s words, a Ninja should adhere to their own rules. »

« Whether it’s claiming a reward without completing a mission or refusing a reward after completing a mission, both are acts that break the rules. »

« We have been taught, Teacher Hashirama. »

Hearing this, the confusion in the hearts of Senju Matsuaki and Hyuga Tsukuyomi was resolved to a certain extent.

In the final analysis, in this era, a Ninja is an occupation similar to a mercenary, and the relationship with the employer is merely a contractual one.

If they were to accept less or no payment from time to time due to compassion, then in the end, not only would a financial crisis arise, but they would also lose their credibility.

Seeing the two silent, Senju Hashirama smiled again : « However, outside of the mission, we can still do things within our power ; this is also a manifestation of the kindness in our hearts. »

« It’s getting late. You two should settle the mission reward, and leave the remaining belongings to this child. »

In the following days, they settled Hanako in a nearby town, with local official institutions responsible for finding a family to adopt her.

Then, the group set off on the road back to Konoha.

The journey there and back, plus the time spent executing the mission, took about half a month from the time they left Konoha to returning to Konoha.

When they were about to reach the entrance of Konoha, Senju Hashirama dispelled the Wood Clone, which turned into a tree rooted in the forest outside Konoha.

Seeing this, Senju Matsuaki couldn’t help but sigh at Uncle Hashirama’s thriftiness.

Even when dispelling a Wood Clone, he still had to contribute to the Village’s greening.

After saying goodbye to Hashirama’s Wood Clone, Senju Matsuaki walked toward the Village together with Hyuga Tsukuyomi.

At this time, another Ninja squad seemed to have finished their mission and happened to be on the same path as the two.

These people looked different, but their common point was quite obvious.

On the back of their clothes, the Uchiha Clan’s crest—a fan—was embroidered.

Before meeting Senju Matsuaki and his companion at the intersection, this group of Uchiha Clan Ninjas had been chatting and laughing.

However, after meeting Matsumi and Tsukuyomi, the group’s conversation suddenly ceased. After a glance, they acted as if they hadn’t seen them and continued walking toward the Village.

Seeing this, Senju Matsuaki didn’t find it strange.

If it were other Ninjas, perhaps the Uchiha Clan Ninjas wouldn’t be so repulsive.

But Matsumi’s clothing also had the Senju Clan’s symbol embroidered on it, and Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s pair of Byakugan were already the most obvious sign.

If one were to list a ranking of « Ninja Clans the Uchiha Clan Hates, » the Senju Clan and the Hyuga Clan would definitely take the top two spots.

Therefore, neither of them felt very surprised by this scene.

Moreover, the other party’s attitude was just stiff and cold ; they didn’t come up to find fault or provoke them, so it had no impact on them.

However, Senju Matsuaki felt vaguely at this moment.

Among this group, there was a young Uchiha Clan Ninja who seemed to hold a more direct and concrete hostility toward him.

He could even feel that in the gaze cast by the other party inadvertently, there seemed to be a trace of resentment.

« When did I offend this person from the Uchiha Clan ? »

Such a doubt couldn’t help but arise in Matsumi’s heart.
