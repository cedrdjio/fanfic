# Chapitre 61 — The First Stage of the Assessment Begins!

Inside the conference hall.

The image on the crystal ball switched between different exam halls in turn.

But upon seeing the pineapple-headed Proctor in this exam hall, the representatives of the various Ninja Villages present turned their gazes toward Nara Kano from Konoha in unison.

As it happened, he had stayed up late the previous night handling clan affairs and hadn’t slept much, so he let out a yawn at that moment.

« This is my cousin. »

Seeing the strange looks from everyone, Nara Kano glanced at the image on the crystal ball, and his mouth twitched.

Inside the exam hall, the Proctor began to read out the rules for the entire Chunin Exams.

« This Joint Chunin Exams will test your comprehensive qualities, » the Proctor said sternly.

« According to the exam rules, you can treat this exam as an official mission, and we, the Proctors of each stage, are your superiors. »

« From the moment I walk into the exam hall and inform you of the upcoming arrangements, your mission will officially begin ! »

Seeing that all the Genin from various Villages in the exam hall were listening attentively, the Proctor smiled with satisfaction and continued.

« First of all, the first stage is a written test. After you finish the written test in the morning, we will grade it in the afternoon, and those who fail will be eliminated directly. »

« However, you can rest assured. As long as you aren’t some kind of trash with absolutely no Ninja common sense, you won’t fail even the written test. »

« What you need to be careful about is not to let us catch you cheating. »

After speaking, the Proctor stroked his chin, lost in thought for a moment, and then continued.

« If I recall correctly, there are about three hundred Genin participating in this Chunin Exams : seventy from Konoha, seventy from Kumogakure, sixty from Iwagakure, fifty from Hidden Mist, and fifty from Sunagakure. »

He only remembered an approximate number regarding the specific number of candidates from each Village.

« And then in the second stage of the Chunin Exams... ah, what a drag. The specific rules will be explained to you in detail by the Proctor of the second stage. However, in the end, only about one-third of you will remain. »

« Wait a second, are you mistaken ? Isn’t there a total of three stages ? Why is the elimination rate for the second stage so high ! ? »

Before the Proctor from the Nara Clan could continue, a Kumogakure Ninja in the exam hall suddenly stood up and questioned him.

His strength was only at a mediocre level among his peers, and if only half of the people were eliminated, he might still have had a hope of entering the third stage of the exam.

But if two-thirds of the people were to be eliminated, there was absolutely no way he could pass the second stage of the assessment.

Becoming a Chunin meant enjoying better resources in the Ninja Clan and being able to accept higher-level missions to earn more rewards.

Therefore, upon hearing this terrifying elimination rate, he immediately voiced his doubt.

« Huh ? Do you think just anyone can become a Chunin ? » The Proctor raised his eyelids slightly and said speechlessly.

« The assessment method was formulated by synthesizing the Chunin Exams modes of various Ninja Villages and has also been reviewed by the Kage of each Ninja Village. If you have any opinions, you can go ask your Raikage. »

« I don’t know about the Chunin Exams in your Kumogakure, but if you feel that the Joint Chunin Exams are too difficult, you can wait to participate in your own Village’s Chunin Exams next time. Perhaps the standards there are looser ? »

« Damn it... »

The Kumogakure Ninja who had just questioned him frowned, wanting to sit back down, feeling somewhat unwilling.

Unexpectedly, this Proctor from the Nara Clan stopped him : « Wait, don’t sit down. »

« Why ? »

The Kumogakure Ninja froze, maintaining a posture with his knees half-bent, about to sit but not yet sitting.

« Because you have already been eliminated, » the Proctor said indifferently.

« I should have mentioned it, right ? This Chunin Exams is equivalent to a mission for you, and I am your superior. »

« Disobeying a superior without cause during a mission can, in special circumstances, even lead to the destruction of the entire squad. Therefore, I believe you do not possess the qualifications to become a Chunin. »

Looking at the other party’s incredulous and indignant expression, the Proctor continued : « I already said, I am your superior, and I can deal with my subordinates within the scope of my authority. »

« Now, get out of here ! »

Seeing the other party still lingering, unwilling to accept this cruel reality, the Proctor’s gaze sharpened, and a surge of killing intent was about to burst forth.

Facing that icy gaze, the Kumogakure Genin felt as if his blood had frozen, and he hurriedly left his seat and fled the exam hall.

A Jonin is an elite who has killed their way out of a sea of corpses, while a Genin is a novice just starting out ; the gap between the two is like a chasm.

« Finally, for the third stage of the exam, we have prepared two contingency plans, and the assessment method will be chosen based on the number of people remaining at the end of the second stage. »

« Alright, that is all I have to explain. Now, patiently wait for the assessment to begin. »

Inside the conference hall, Hong, who had been paying attention to the situation here, turned dark in the face.

Looking at the mocking expressions of the representatives from other Villages around him, he felt he couldn’t hold his head high.

Just now, he only felt that the Genin of his own Ninja Village were embarrassing themselves, and he wouldn’t blame Konoha.

« No wonder you used underhanded means to let Jonin participate in the Chunin Exams. So it turns out your Village’s Chunin Exams are watery, and you’re afraid of losing face in front of other Villages. »

Hōzuki Gengetsu, who had long been dissatisfied, did not spare the other party, mocking them with sarcasm.

Upon hearing these words, a flash of anger appeared on Hong’s face, but then, as if thinking of something, a cold sneer appeared at the corners of his mouth.

You call us in Kumogakure underhanded ? Just wait until you are beaten by Konoha’s candidates until your own mothers wouldn’t recognize you, then you’ll understand.

However, it was not only Kumogakure that had a dark face after hearing these words from Hōzuki Gengetsu, but also the representative of Sunagakure.

Although he remained silent at this moment, Shamon’s complexion was a few shades darker than before.

He wasn’t sure if the Chunin Exams of Kumogakure were watery, but the Chunin Exams of Sunagakure were indeed watery.

After all, Sunagakure lacked resources and was not a place of outstanding people ; over so many years, not many outstanding geniuses had appeared.

If the standards for the Chunin Exams were not relaxed, I am afraid that after a few more years, the number of Chunin and Jonin would not be enough to support the operation of a major Ninja Village.

« Stop arguing, the exam has started, » Wu said.

I saw that on the exam hall floor, a neat test paper had already been placed on the desk in front of every candidate.

A total of ten questions were distributed sequentially on the front and back of the test paper.

With the reflection of the Telescope Technique, the representatives of various Villages in the conference hall could also clearly see the exam questions.

« Wait, these questions aren’t right ! What do you mean by 'When you are on a mission and encounter a black-skinned Ninja from a certain country snatching a woman, how do you choose between morality and the mission' ? I protest ! This is smearing the image of our Kumogakure ! ! »

Hong from Kumogakure was furious and protested.

Senju Tobirama said without changing his expression : « The exam question scenario is purely fictional. Any resemblance is purely coincidental. Kumogakure, please do not project yourselves onto it. »

« Hahaha, this question set by Konoha is truly vivid and realistic ! »

Hōzuki Gengetsu, the representative of Hidden Mist, laughed and clapped in approval.
