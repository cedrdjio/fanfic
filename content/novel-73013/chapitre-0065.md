# Chapitre 65 — New Missions! Huge Points Are Beckoning!

If it didn’t truly bring benefits to the Senju Clan, Senju Tobirama wouldn’t be advertising in such a public setting.

The wooden chess pieces in his hand at this moment, including the wooden products he mentioned earlier, belonged to the Senju Clan’s industry.

As early as the Sengoku period, the Senju Clan discovered that their clan’s Chakra had certain healing effects on ordinary people.

Back then, the Senju Clan would carve wooden plaques for newborn children in the clan to carry with them, blessing the children as they grew.

After Senju Hashirama awakened Wood Release, the Senju Clan discovered that the wood created by Wood Release could become a better carrier for the Senju Clan’s Chakra.

After Konoha Village was built, some members of the Senju Clan who left the Ninja profession developed this industry into one of the Senju Clan’s sources of income.

Looking at the target audience, these products were not actually meant for Ninjas, but for nobles, dignitaries, and the wealthy of various nations.

« Then what if the chess piece is destroyed by someone else ? »

A questioning voice suddenly came from the crowd.

It was unclear whether they were worried about their own chess piece being destroyed by others, or worried about accidents occurring when they destroyed someone else’s chess piece.

Senju Tobirama said in a flat tone : « A Ninja who cannot even protect their mission objective certainly does not qualify, and they will likewise be eliminated from the second stage of the exam. »

Seeing that the time was almost up, he did not intend to continue wasting words with the examinees.

« Next, the Proctors will lead you to your respective starting points. For the sake of fairness, regardless of the starting position, the relative distance to the finish line is the same. »

After Senju Tobirama finished speaking, he raised his right hand and made a gesture ; the many Proctors who had been waiting on the side immediately stepped forward.

« Dou, Yan... step out, you come with me ! »

A Proctor held the list in their hand and read out ten names.

Those whose names were called consciously stepped out of the crowd and followed the Proctor as they left quickly.

The remaining twenty-plus Proctors repeated this process in turn, leading the ten examinees they were responsible for away.

Soon, Senju Matsuaki’s name also appeared on the list read by one of the Proctors.

« Chiyo, Senju Matsuaki, Ebizo... step out, follow me ! »

Hearing the ten names, including his own, Senju Matsuaki left his original position and gathered by the Proctor’s side, while slightly frowning.

The grouping for the second stage of the exam was also random. Unfortunately, among these ten people, there was no sign of his teammate, Hyuga Tsukuyomi, with whom he had fought side-by-side for a long time.

According to the exam rules just mentioned, these ten people would start from the same starting point.

Perhaps at the very beginning of the exam, a conflict would break out among these people over the competition for chess pieces.

There were several other Konoha Ninjas in the same group as Senju Matsuaki, but they had no interaction in daily life, so they could only be described as familiar faces.

Instead, the other two Genin from the Hidden Sand Village in the same group left a deeper impression on him.

The girl named Chiyo had eye-catching shoulder-length purple hair, and her figure was relatively voluptuous among her peers.

In the previous first stage of the exam, the two happened to be in the same testing venue, so Senju Matsuaki had some impression of her.

Hearing the Proctor call out her name, he finally realized why he had felt a sense of déjà vu earlier.

Chiyo, the future Advisor of the Hidden Sand Village, was a top-tier puppeteer. Furthermore, she had outstanding attainments in Medical Ninjutsu, Taijutsu, and Sealing Jutsu, making her an all-around Ninja.

She was a Ninja from the same era as Hiruzen Sarutobi and others, and in the future, she would have a grandson named Sasori.

The other purple-haired boy named Ebizo was Chiyo’s younger brother.

In the future, he would also be a high-level Advisor for the Hidden Sand Village, making outstanding contributions to intelligence and espionage, and would serve as the Hidden Sand Village’s mastermind.

At this time, the two were still young. Although they were not as dazzling as the future Third Kazekage Yan in the Hidden Sand Village, they were still geniuses with outstanding talent.

On the way to the primeval forest outside the village, everyone was silently observing their companions, and no one took the initiative to speak.

For the sake of safety in numbers, the ten people were vaguely divided into five groups based on the Ninja villages they belonged to.

Because of the special occasion of the Chunin Exams, the group left Konoha without obstruction and soon arrived at the testing site.

Ahead was an endlessly dense primeval forest, and behind them was Konoha Village, which could barely be seen as a small dot.

« Here are the chess pieces for the ten of you. The exam will officially begin after I leave. »

The Proctor flicked their right palm, and ten wooden Shogi pieces flew out, shooting towards the ten people respectively.

Some Genin were quick-witted and caught the pieces, but others were flustered and failed to react in time, only bending down to pick up the pieces after they had fallen to the ground.

Through just this one detail, the gap in quality among the Genin was vividly revealed.

The silent pair of siblings from the Hidden Sand Village saw the front of the chess piece revealed by one of the Iwagakure examinees in their panic, and a sharp glint flashed in both their eyes.

Senju Matsuaki quickly glanced at the piece he had caught from an angle only he could see ; it was blank.

Immediately, he put the piece into his Ninja tool pouch without changing his expression.

It seemed that because the exam time had not yet arrived, the Proctor did not leave, but stood there with their arms crossed, waiting for the signal.

During this wait, two long-awaited notification sounds rang in Senju Matsuaki’s mind.

« B-Rank Mission Released ! Capture  »Jade«  Shogi pieces during the second stage of the Joint Chunin Exams. Reward : 100 Points per piece ! »

« B-Rank Mission Released ! Defeat other examinees during the second stage of the Joint Chunin Exams. Reward : 50 Points per person ! »

Hearing these two notifications, Senju Matsuaki felt relieved.

He wasn’t afraid of having too many missions ; he was only afraid of missions not coming.

During this time, the only mission he had was an S-Rank mission triggered by chance, which was far beyond his current capabilities.

Now that two missions had appeared at once, he finally had a relatively stable source of points.

Judging from the mission content, the duration covered the entire second stage of the Chunin Exams.

Both missions had clear objectives, pointing directly at the other examinees.

The first mission required seizing « Jade » pieces, and the target undoubtedly pointed to those Genin who were lucky enough to receive « Jade » pieces directly, as well as those Genin who had the strength to seize « Jade » pieces from others.

As for the second mission, it didn’t even let the remaining Genin off the hook.

Defeating one examinee would earn fifty points ; although it wasn’t as rewarding as seizing « Jade » pieces, it was still quite substantial.

There were a total of 270 examinees in the second stage, with 90 « Jade » pieces among them.

If he could seize all ninety « Jade » pieces, the final reward would be 9,000 points.

Of course, this assumption was a bit too idealistic.

Not to mention that there were some strong opponents in this exam, the time constraint alone would not allow him to achieve this extent.

But even so, the points he could potentially obtain in the end would still be very generous.

Even if he only seized twenty pieces, he could still earn 2,000 points in the end !

It could be said that at this moment, Matsuaki could already faintly see the huge amount of points beckoning to him.
