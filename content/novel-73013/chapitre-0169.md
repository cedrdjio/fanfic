# Chapitre 169 — All Things in the Universe, Turn to Ashes! Ryujin Jakka!!

« Fire Release : Great Fire Annihilation ! »

Uchiha Hikari performed hand Seals, exhaling a raging sea of flames from her mouth that collided head-on with the lava pillar.

With a deafening roar, flames and magma splashed in all directions, staining half the sky orange-red.

The Four-Tails had its attention drawn by this sudden attack, and its massive head turned toward Uchiha Hikari’s direction.

« Got you. »

Uchiha Hikari’s Sharingan spun violently, the three tomoe instantly merging into a strange pattern.

Mangekyo Sharingan !

The Four-Tails' movements paused slightly as an invisible mental shock invaded its consciousness.

This was another major reason why Senju Hashirama felt at ease letting the three of them capture the Tailed Beast.

The Mangekyo Sharingan naturally possessed extremely powerful control over Tailed Beasts, even capable of forcibly controlling them through ocular power.

This point had already been demonstrated when Uchiha Madara manipulated the Nine-Tailed during the attack on the village.

Of course, Uchiha Hikari did not completely control the Four-Tails with her Mangekyo Sharingan directly at this moment ; she merely planted a suggestion in its heart to lure it away from the vast expanse of lava.

It was not that Uchiha Hikari was incapable of direct control, but because of Senju Matsuaki’s instructions.

The Four-Tails was one of the few whetstones suitable for him in the current Ninja World, and he did not want to waste this opportunity.

Furthermore, this was also related to the new mission released by the Golden Finger platform not long ago.

[S-Rank mission released : Defeat and Seal the Tailed Beast ! Reward : 10,000 points each !]

On the eve of setting out to capture the One-Tailed, the mission prompt appeared suddenly, undoubtedly adding another layer of motivation for Senju Matsuaki’s actions.

Returning the perspective to the present.

Provoked once again by Uchiha Hikari’s attack, coupled with the suggestion planted by the Mangekyo Sharingan, the Four-Tails fell into a frenzy, rushing away from the large lava field and charging toward the location of the trio.

Just at this moment.

« Wood Release : Deep Forest Emergence ! »

Senju Matsuaki slammed his hands onto the ground.

The earth shook violently as countless thick vines and trees burst from the ground, crazily entangling the Four-Tails.

The Four-Tails came to its senses and struggled angrily, but those trees were exceptionally tough, winding layer upon layer around its limbs and tail.

Meanwhile, the Mangekyo in Uchiha Hikari’s eyes spun rapidly, and blood slid down the corners of her eyes.

« Susanoo ! »

Crimson Chakra soared into the sky, rapidly condensing into a huge skeleton, which was then covered in flesh and armor, finally transforming into a crimson giant holding a long sword.

The Susanoo did not launch an attack but stood guard on the side like a loyal protector.

« What is this ! ? »

The Four-Tails' body trembled, and the brain previously occupied by anger quickly calmed down.

At this moment, it felt like it had seen a ghost.

Why, while staying peacefully at home, did Indra’s Susanoo and Ashura’s Wood Release suddenly appear in front of it ?

Back in the day, either of those two alone could easily suppress it.

Let alone the simultaneous appearance of Susanoo and Wood Release.

However, the Four-Tails soon noticed something was wrong.

First, Ashura and Indra did not have the near-infinite lifespans of Tailed Beasts ; they had long since passed away.

Second, the Susanoo and Wood Release were not exactly the same as theirs.

The Susanoo’s color was crimson, not the ghostly purple of Indra’s.

And while that Wood Release was equally heart-stopping, it was still lacking something compared to Ashura’s.

Thinking of this, the Four-Tails calmed down.

If Ashura and Indra themselves were here, it would have surrendered without a second thought.

But if they were just their descendants, then it, the Four-Tails Son Goku, would not be captured without a fight !

The next second, the Four-Tails let out a roar of unwillingness, and violent Lava Release Chakra erupted from its body, attempting to melt the Wood Dragon.

White smoke began to rise from the surface of the Wood Dragon, emitting a hissing sound.

« Not enough ! »

Senju Matsuaki gritted his teeth and increased his Chakra output. « Wood Release : Wood Dragon Technique ! »

A huge wooden dragon rose from the ground, circling and wrapping around the Four-Tails' body, opening its large mouth to bite the back of the Four-Tails' neck, pinning it firmly to the ground.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s Byakugan was observing the surroundings at all times, and after noticing other movements in the distance, she said loudly.

« Matsuaki, someone in the distance has noticed the commotion here and is approaching ! »

« It seems to be people from Iwagakure, still keeping an eye on this place. »

Senju Matsuaki took a deep breath, and a smile tugged at the corner of his mouth.

They had previously observed that the Four-Tails' state was wrong, as if it had just experienced a battle not long ago.

It seems that it was indeed people from Iwagakure who found the Four-Tails before them.

Unfortunately, subduing a Tailed Beast relies not only on intelligence and speed, but strength is equally important !

« Next, let’s finish this quickly. »

Senju Matsuaki reached out to grasp the hilt of the katana at his waist, his eyes becoming incomparably sharp.

He was here to capture the Four-Tails, not to have a direct conflict with Iwagakure.

Although he was not afraid of the people from Iwagakure, there was no need to cause unnecessary trouble.

« This sword... it’s time to show its true power. »

He slowly unsheathed the sword, reciting softly.

« All things in the universe, turn to ashes— »

« Ryujin Jakka ! »

The moment the blade left the scabbard, a terrifying heatwave swept across the entire area.

The surrounding air seemed to be ignited, and the temperature rose sharply.

The already scorching lava zone became even hotter, and even the rocks in the distance began to melt.

The Four-Tails, still struggling to break free from the Wood Release’s restraint, suddenly froze, and a chill rose from its heart.

It locked its gaze on the source of this terrifying aura.

It was a katana...

Just by holding the sword in his hand, the rocks under Senju Matsuaki’s feet began to melt, forming a constantly expanding lava pit.

« Four-Tails, watch closely ! This is the first time this sword has shown its edge in the Ninja World ! »

Senju Matsuaki locked his gaze on the Four-Tails.

The Four-Tails instinctively felt a fatal threat ; it struggled frantically, desperate to break free from the restraints.

It did not know what this sword was.

But even when facing the Susanoo and Wood Release just now, it had not brought such Soul-deep fear !

« Hikari, Tsukuyomi, back away ! »

Senju Matsuaki held the sword with both hands, raised it high, and his eyes suddenly became sharp.

That’s right, this was the Golden Finger he had collected from the Golden Finger platform that day.

Zanpakuto · Ryujin Jakka !

This sword came from the world of Bleach ; it was the strongest and oldest of all flame-type Zanpakuto, known for possessing the strongest attack power in the Soul Society !

« Matsuaki ! ! »

He slashed down.

The world lost its color.

A flame tornado spanning the sky tore through the air, and wherever it passed, the air was ripped apart, and space seemed to be burning through.

That extremely scorching flame, with the momentum to burn everything, swept straight toward the Four-Tails.

The Four-Tails let out a desperate roar, opening its mouth to spew out its strongest Lava Release attack in an attempt to resist.

But in front of Ryujin Jakka, that lava pillar was like paper, easily broken through.

The scorching flames slashed through the Four-Tails' body without any obstruction.

Time seemed to stand still at this moment.

The next second—

Boom ! ! !

A heaven-shaking explosion rang out, and a huge pillar of fire shot into the sky, scattering the clouds for hundreds of meters.

The Four-Tails' massive body collapsed to the ground, covered in scorched wounds, its Chakra almost exhausted.
