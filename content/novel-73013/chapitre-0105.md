# Chapitre 105 — Wood Release: Deep Forest Emergence!!!

The injuries on Senju Matsuaki’s body could indeed be considered severe for a Ninja with a normal Physique.

Just a moment ago, the situation had been critical ; he had only managed to protect vital areas like his head, neck, and heart, leaving him no time to care for parts like his arms.

If a Ninja suffers an injury to their arm during combat, the consequences are self-evident.

If they were on the battlefield, without emergency treatment from a medical Ninja, death would be the only path awaiting the wounded.

Unfortunately, Senju Matsuaki’s Physique had long since surpassed that of an ordinary Ninja.

The naturally gifted Physique of the Senju Clan inherently provided extraordinary regenerative abilities.

And the presence of Asura Chakra only further allowed Senju Matsuaki’s regenerative abilities to gradually align with those of Senju Hashirama.

Looking back through the history of the Ninja World, any Ninja possessing Asura Chakra was top-tier in terms of vitality !

Thus, three pairs of scarlet Sharingan witnessed that while the wounds across Senju Matsuaki’s body appeared severe, they were not bleeding, and the gruesome lacerations were healing at a speed visible to the naked eye.

The smile on Uchiha Setsuna’s face vanished in an instant ; he widened his eyes in disbelief as his two tomoe spun rapidly.

« What is going on ? Could this be a genjutsu... No, that’s impossible ! ! »

Had they not been firmly convinced that it was impossible for the opponent to trap them in a genjutsu while their Sharingan were active, all three Uchiha would have assumed they were under the influence of one.

Facing their stunned expressions, Senju Matsuaki merely offered a calm smile.

« It has been a long time since I was injured. It seems my regenerative abilities have grown quite a bit stronger compared to a few months ago. »

He raised his right hand before his eyes ; the bloody hole pierced by the shuriken earlier had already scabbed over, and the scratches on other parts of his body were reduced to barely noticeable white marks.

The last time he had suffered an injury that truly demonstrated his healing power was during his battle against the Cloud Ninja, Dou.

Come to think of it, the strength of Dou at that time was probably on par with the two Uchiha Jonin currently in front of Senju Matsuaki.

The difference was that back then, Senju Matsuaki did not possess such a massive amount of Chakra, nor had he awakened Wood Release.

Therefore, he had struggled quite a bit during that fight and was ultimately defeated by Dou.

However, today is different from the past.

The comprehensive improvement to his physical fitness from the Breathing Technique, the all-encompassing, gradual qualitative change brought to Senju Matsuaki by Asura Chakra, and the Wood Release—a Bloodline Limit with an extremely high potential and threshold.

The combination of these factors allowed Senju Matsuaki’s strength to undergo several qualitative leaps in just a few short months !

« Well then, I shall get serious now. Let’s finish this quickly. »

A cold glint flickered in his crimson eyes as Senju Matsuaki sheathed his short blade, under the wary gazes of the three members of the Uchiha Clan.

Seeing this, the three of them couldn’t help but be stunned.

He said he wanted a quick battle, yet he put his sword away. Was he planning to run away ?

However, Senju Matsuaki’s next actions dispelled this unrealistic notion.

By now, the setting sun had long since descended, and the moonlight was intense.

Moonlight spilled from the night sky onto the ground, casting a faint glow over Senju Matsuaki’s silver short hair.

Senju Matsuaki’s hands moved rapidly to form seals, moving so fast in the flickering night that they left afterimages.

At the same time, a massive amount of Chakra, brimming with vitality, erupted from his body ; its overwhelming Aura caused the surrounding trees to tremble slightly.

« What kind of Ninjutsu is this ? How come I have never seen these hand seals before ? »

The tomoe in Uchiha Setsuna’s Sharingan spun wildly, capturing every single hand seal Senju Matsuaki made.

Yet, even after running through every Ninjutsu he had ever seen, he could not recall what this was.

« I haven’t seen this Ninjutsu either, but striking first is never wrong. »

The Uchiha Jonin in his twenties snorted coldly ; unable to discern what Ninjutsu Senju Matsuaki was using, he simply stopped being curious.

Although he didn’t know what Ninjutsu Senju Matsuaki was preparing to unleash, the mere Aura alone was already quite terrifying.

This presence even made him, a Jonin of the Uchiha Clan, faintly feel threatened and alarmed !

Thus, he also formed hand seals, intending to seize the initiative with his specialty, Fire Release Ninjutsu.

However, beside him, the older Jonin in his thirties was staring at the scene in a daze.

As if recalling something, the three tomoe that were spinning at a steady pace gradually slowed down.

Suddenly, his scarlet eyes widened violently, and a bone-chilling cold flowed from his spine throughout his entire body.

This familiar Aura—he finally knew where this sense of déjà vu came from !

« Run ! ! This is... »

He reached out, wanting to grab the arms of his two clansmen ; in his desperation, he even wanted to forcibly pull them away from the spot.

The other Jonin, who was in the middle of forming seals to cast a Ninjutsu, hadn’t expected his companion to suddenly pull on him ; his seals were interrupted, and he turned his head with an annoyed expression.

What met his eyes were the pale faces of his two companions, who were looking forward in terror.

« It is... too late... » the older Uchiha Jonin murmured.

His eyes—the three-tomoe Sharingan he had always prided himself on—were now filled with intense fear !

« Wood Release : Deep Forest Emergence ! ! ! »

Senju Matsuaki let out a low shout, and countless thick trees burst from the ground, filling the rare open space in the forest.

Not only that, but the trees that had originally existed in the forest were also manipulated by Senju Matsuaki, attacking the location where the three Uchiha Clan Ninjas were.

The thick branches seemed to transform into steel spears and iron whips at this moment, tearing through the air with a series of sonic booms !

The power of a Ninjutsu is always bolstered by the environment and terrain.

Lakes and oceans act as abundant ammunition depots for Water Release Ninjutsu, Magnet Release is greatly enhanced in areas rich in metal deposits, and the Qilin released by Uchiha Sasuke with the help of storm clouds is known as the pinnacle of Lightning Release Ninjutsu...

And for Wood Release, the most suitable terrain is this very dense forest where Senju Matsuaki currently stands !

« This is... Wood Release ! ? »

Even Uchiha Setsuna and the others, who hadn’t understood Senju Matsuaki’s hand seals just a moment ago, now understood what this technique was.

Wood Release, the signature Ninjutsu of the First Hokage, Senju Hashirama, which pacified the chaotic world and brought peace to the Land of Fire.

The more important point of memory was that Senju Hashirama could use Wood Release to suppress their Uchiha Clan’s former leader, Uchiha Madara !

This Bloodline Limit, Wood Release, could be called the psychological shadow of their Uchiha Clan.

The only thing they were thankful for was that only Senju Hashirama in the Senju Clan had mastered Wood Release, unlike the Sharingan, which was common within the Uchiha Clan.

Even the Mangekyo Sharingan, which was above the three-tomoe Sharingan, had periods where multiple pairs existed simultaneously within the Uchiha Clan.

However, since Senju Hashirama awakened Wood Release, not a single Ninja in the Senju Clan had awakened it for decades.

Regarding this, many in the Uchiha Clan were secretly delighted.

Uchiha Setsuna’s grandfather, Uchiha Hosai, who currently served as one of the Elders of the Uchiha Clan, had stubbornly believed that the loss of Wood Release would be the beginning of the Senju Clan’s decline.

And that, would be the time when the dawn of the Uchiha Clan’s resurgence would arrive !

But at this moment, their dream was shattered.

Thinking of this, Uchiha Setsuna’s expression instantly turned ferocious again ; his two-tomoe Sharingan spun rapidly, and a third tomoe suddenly appeared !

« We must kill him ! ! ! We absolutely cannot let the Senju Clan have a second Wood Release Ninja ! ! ! » he roared loudly.
