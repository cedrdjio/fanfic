# Chapitre 90 — Senju Tobirama's Insights on Water Release

After Biwako and Hiruzen Sarutobi left one after another, Senju Matsuaki closed the gate and returned to the courtyard.

Although having friends visit was nice, he was nearing the exams and still needed a quiet environment to cultivate.

Ever since his Golden Finger was activated, he had maintained a certain amount of time for cultivation every day, not skipping it even during these days in the primeval forest.

After all, besides completing tasks, the only source of points needed for the Golden Finger was the rewards for daily cultivation.

However, Senju Matsuaki’s home seemed livelier than usual today.

Before long, someone knocked on the gate outside again.

Senju Matsuaki opened the gate again, but outside stood an unexpected figure.

Senju Tobirama was wearing a black Ninja combat suit, standing outside the gate with his arms crossed, his expression somewhat strange.

« Second Uncle, why are you here ? »

Seeing who it was, Senju Matsuaki felt a bit confused.

Recently, Konoha’s higher-ups had been busy with the Chunin Exams ; even Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama were working together using their Wood Clones and Shadow Clones.

These days, Senju Matsuaki occasionally went to his Uncle Hashirama’s house for a meal, but each time, the two of them either didn’t return, or if they did, they left in a hurry after the meal.

« Matsuaki, you... »

Senju Tobirama’s expression was complex. Just as he was about to speak, he noticed members of the Senju Clan passing by on the road behind him, so he hurriedly closed his mouth.

« Let’s go inside and talk. »

After Senju Matsuaki and Senju Tobirama stepped into the courtyard, they walked under the shade of a tree and stood still.

« This tree is indeed Wood Release. »

Senju Tobirama raised his right hand and pressed it against the trunk of this thick tree, unable to help but reveal a hint of a smile at the corner of his mouth.

He was very familiar with Wood Release.

Although Senju Tobirama himself had not awakened this Bloodline Limit, he had been fighting alongside his eldest brother, Senju Hashirama, for decades.

If Senju Hashirama was the person most familiar with Wood Release, then he, Senju Tobirama, was certainly the second most familiar.

Getting close to this tree, Senju Tobirama clearly felt the vibrant, vital Chakra filling it.

At the same time, the overall material of the tree was much stronger, to a degree that naturally grown trees could not reach.

« You brat, when did you awaken Wood Release ? Why didn’t you tell your Uncle Hashirama or me ? »

There was both joy and reproach in Senju Tobirama’s eyes, though joy was clearly predominant.

Hearing this, Senju Matsuaki scratched his head in embarrassment and said, « Isn’t it because you and Uncle Hashirama have been too busy lately ? »

« Second Uncle, how did you know about this ? »

Upon second thought, Senju Matsuaki became curious.

Since he hadn’t taken the initiative to mention it, how did Senju Tobirama know he had awakened Wood Release ?

« Could it be that you set up a special office, Second Uncle, and used the Telescope Technique developed by Brother Hiruzen to arrange for a group of Ninjas to monitor the exam site twenty-four hours a day ? » Senju Matsuaki guessed.

« Ahem... »

This kid guessed too accurately !

Senju Tobirama coughed awkwardly twice, then patted Senju Matsuaki on the shoulder as if to change the subject.

His actions were unknown even to his eldest brother, Senju Hashirama, who still thought they could only manage short-term monitoring and rescue of the exam site.

However, in fact, as the chief Proctor for the second stage of the Joint Chunin Exams, he had long prepared several contingency plans.

The results also left him quite satisfied.

Konoha’s examinees received extra protection ; apart from one person who died after being attacked by a brown bear in the primeval forest and could not be rescued in time, no other examinees died.

As for the other Ninja villages, some Ninjas with Bloodline Limits or secret techniques were directly attacked and killed by examinees from other villages.

Especially Dou from Kumogakure and Yan from Sunagakure ; these two would decisively kill talented examinees from other villages during the exam.

Little did they know, they had become the sharp blades for Senju Tobirama to kill with a borrowed knife.

What surprised Senju Tobirama the most was that Senju Matsuaki had guessed the truth directly just by knowing that he was aware of his Wood Release Awakening.

A flexible mind combined with Wood Release—isn’t this the combination of my and my eldest brother’s strengths ?

Thinking of this, Senju Tobirama nodded thoughtfully.

However, seeing Senju Tobirama’s reaction, Senju Matsuaki’s expression also became somewhat strange.

Judging from this reaction, it was clear that Senju Tobirama really had carried out large-scale monitoring of the exam site.

« I have already instructed those in the know to keep this matter confidential ; no one else will know until you use Wood Release in the third stage of the exam, » Senju Tobirama said.

« I’m really curious what reaction my eldest brother will have when he finds out about this. »

Thinking of the shocked expression his eldest brother, Senju Hashirama, might have when he learned of this, Senju Tobirama felt a faint sense of anticipation.

When Senju Tobirama first learned through the Telescope Technique that Senju Matsuaki had awakened Wood Release, he was so surprised that it took him a long time to recover.

« By the way, there is one more thing today. »

Senju Tobirama had indeed been very busy lately, so he naturally wouldn’t have come solely because of the Wood Release matter.

He took out a light blue scroll from behind him and handed it forward to Senju Matsuaki.

« Second Uncle, what is this ? »

Senju Matsuaki took the scroll, and its heavy weight made him feel surprised.

This scroll was about as long as a forearm, light blue in appearance, and adorned with the Senju Clan’s emblem.

Senju Tobirama said, « These are my records and some insights regarding Water Release Ninjutsu over the years. Given your current Chakra volume and Ninjutsu accumulation, it should be of great help to you. »

« Although Wood Release is enough to make you reach the Kage level, your attainments in other Ninjutsu can make your abilities more comprehensive. »

« Water Release Ninjutsu is more flexible and varied compared to other Ninjutsu. My eldest brother also used Water Release most frequently before he awakened Wood Release. Perhaps it will also provide some inspiration for your future cultivation of Wood Release. »

« Thank you, Second Uncle ! » Senju Matsuaki’s eyes lit up as he thanked him.

Senju Tobirama’s attainments in Water Release needed no elaboration.

If this scroll recording his Ninjutsu and insights were known to a village skilled in Water Release like Hidden Mist, they would likely go to any lengths to seize it.

« We are family ; why say such things ? »

Senju Tobirama looked around the courtyard, which felt both familiar and somewhat strange, and sighed.

Back then, he and Senju Matsuaki’s father were also very good brothers and comrades-in-arms ; their appearances were even more similar than his and his own brother Senju Hashirama’s, so much so that outsiders would mistake them for biological brothers.

If the two families hadn’t been close, they wouldn’t have built their residences in adjacent locations when the Senju Clan moved to Konoha back then.

Unfortunately, having survived the Sengoku era, he sacrificed himself when Konoha was first established and other Ninja forces within the Land of Fire were launching a frantic counterattack, falling in the final darkness before the dawn.

Being busy with official business, Senju Tobirama gave a few more instructions before saying goodbye and leaving.
