# Chapitre 35 — Hidden Cloud Movement

The young Ninja from Kumogakure, who had wandered to the Ninja Academy at some unknown point, wore a rough expression that now seemed deeply contemplative, his eyes scanning his surroundings.

« Hmph, Konoha certainly has a lot of Ninja clans ! »

Hyuga, Akimichi, Inuzuka, Aburame, Uchiha...

Watching children with clan emblems embroidered on their clothes or distinct physical features walk by from time to time, the young Ninja from Kumogakure let out an annoyed huff.

He was purely acting out of sour grapes.

Whether it was Bloodline Limit or secret techniques, Kumogakure was far inferior to Konoha.

The people leaving the Ninja Academy at this time were all students ; as for the teachers, they still had other official business to attend to and wouldn’t be leaving so quickly.

Soon, he noticed Uchiha Kai lagging behind the crowd.

Compared to others who walked in groups, this seemingly arrogant brat was walking alone, and because he was lost in thought, he had fallen far behind the crowd in front.

« The fan pattern... that’s right, it’s a brat from the Uchiha Clan. »

After confirming this fact, a glint flashed in the young Ninja’s eyes, and he rubbed his hands together, wanting to step forward.

However, just as his hulking frame took two steps forward, he suddenly stopped.

His previously joyful expression changed, replaced by a deep frown.

« Uchiha Kai, just finished class ? »

Senju Matsuaki had appeared at the entrance of the Ninja Academy at some point, blocking Uchiha Kai’s path and waving at him with a smile.

Hearing this, Uchiha Kai, who had been fantasizing about punching Senju and kicking Hyuga in the future, subconsciously stopped in his tracks.

Looking up at the silver-haired, red-eyed figure that had suddenly appeared in his line of sight, his expression froze instantly.

Once he realized what Senju Matsuaki had just said, the string of reason in his mind snapped.

« You damn guy, did you come here specifically to mock me ? ! »

After shouting, Uchiha Kai didn’t hesitate to stomp on the ground and sprinted towards Senju Matsuaki in front.

Senju Matsuaki smiled, turned his head, and ran towards the location of the Uchiha Clan compound in his memory.

In the shadows not far away, watching the two figures chase each other away, the young Ninja from Kumogakure furrowed his brows and clenched his fists with reluctance.

« Tch. »

He loosened his clenched fists, observed for a moment, and finding no other suitable targets, he finally gave up and turned to leave.

On the other side.

Senju Matsuaki looked calm, maintaining a steady pace, neither too fast nor too slow, leading Uchiha Kai who was chasing behind.

Before graduating from the Ninja Academy, the other party was already no match for him.

It had been over half a year since graduation ; Matsumi was already a fully-fledged Ninja tempered by hardship, while Uchiha Kai remained an apprentice at the Ninja Academy.

The gap between the two had actually grown wider, yet the other party still fantasized that the gap was constantly shrinking.

Soon, after turning a corner, Matsumi directly dispelled his Shadow Clone.

Poof—

Accompanied by a puff of smoke, the Senju Matsuaki who had been chased by Uchiha Kai for the entire street seemed to vanish into thin air, as if he had never appeared.

That’s right, the Senju Matsuaki who had deliberately come out to provoke the other party and then ran away was not the real body, but still a Shadow Clone.

After all, Matsumi’s mission was to monitor the Kumogakure delegation ; how could he ignore that young Ninja from Kumogakure ?

The reason he sent a Shadow Clone to Uchiha Kai’s side just now was merely to make the young Ninja from Kumogakure, who seemed to want to do something, hesitate out of fear.

He saw Uchiha Kai alone and wanted to do something secretly.

But if other people were also present, he would likely worry that the commotion would be too great, leading to the exposure of his actions.

Not long after, Uchiha Kai, who had also turned the corner, looked at the empty space in front and stopped in his tracks, dumbfounded.

« Aaaah ! »

Bang—

Uchiha Kai punched the wall hard, suppressing the anger in his heart, and walked towards the nearby Uchiha Clan compound, gritting his teeth.

...

Ninja Academy entrance.

Shortly after the young Ninja from Kumogakure left, three figures leaped several times from nearby buildings, finally landing by the gate of the Ninja Academy.

« Matsumi, where is your real body ? »

Hiruzen Sarutobi’s expression was slightly anxious as he asked the Shadow Clone of Senju Matsuaki beside him.

Just now, he and Danzo Shimura were pushing a small cart, selling oden ; on the surface, they were doing business, but in reality, they were working undercover.

Because the Kumogakure delegation had already walked far away, they were also pushing the small cart, naturally following in the same direction.

But unexpectedly, just as they were immersed in their roleplay, the figure of Senju Matsuaki suddenly appeared next to their small cart.

Hiruzen Sarutobi, who had been in character, was startled and shivered, subconsciously wanting to ask the customer what they wanted to order.

To his surprise, Matsumi brought them even more explosive news, saying that the young Ninja from Kumogakure who had just bought oden from them had actually wandered near the Ninja Academy.

Not far away, Matsumi’s figure suddenly appeared from a corner, arriving in front of Hiruzen Sarutobi and Danzo Shimura in a few strides.

« I am also a Shadow Clone ; my real body is still monitoring the target. »

« Just now, the target seemed to be planning to attack someone from the Uchiha Clan in the Ninja Academy, but my real body used a Shadow Clone to ruin the opportunity. »

« Whether the true purpose was to gather intelligence or to carry out a kidnapping cannot be confirmed yet, but I think it is highly likely the former. »

Before the others could speak, Matsumi’s Shadow Clone finished explaining the situation in a few words.

Hearing that nothing irreversible had happened, Hiruzen Sarutobi’s anxious expression relaxed, and he reached out to scratch his head.

« It’s good that nothing happened, but your Chakra reserves are truly immense ; how many Shadow Clones is that now ? »

« Then Hiruzen and I will go report this situation to the Hokage-sama ; take care of yourself. »

Danzo Shimura’s expression also softened, but his expression wasn’t as expressive as Hiruzen’s, and it was hard to notice without looking closely.

« Then I’ll leave it to you. » Matsumi’s Shadow Clone said with a smile at the same time.

Poof—

Accompanied by two puffs of smoke, the Shadow Clone that brought the two here and the Shadow Clone that remained at the Ninja Academy to inform the two both vanished on the spot.

After a Shadow Clone disappears, the memories from its duration return to the real body.

Therefore, after the three Shadow Clones disappeared one after another and three sets of memories appeared simultaneously, Matsumi’s mind inevitably drifted for a second or two.

« I’m really not used to this feeling of things being suddenly shoved into my brain. »

Shaking his head and casting these thoughts aside, Matsumi’s sharp red eyes stared at the back of the Ninja from Kumogakure, watching him step by step enter the inn arranged by Konoha for the Kumogakure delegation.

As time passed, the daylight was swallowed by the night.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi, who had previously split up with Matsumi, met up with him outside the inn ; she still maintained her Byakugan, her pure white eyes appearing somewhat chilling under the moonlight.

However, when looking at Matsumi, there would always be an imperceptible smile on the corners of her mouth, making her temperament appear softer.

The Kumogakure delegation could rest, but they were burdened with a surveillance mission ; they couldn’t just turn around and go sleep on the streets now.

According to the duties and ethics of a Ninja, from now until Kumogakure leaves Konoha, they need to monitor them at all times.
