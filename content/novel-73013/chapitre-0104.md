# Chapitre 104 — Fierce Battle! Injured!

« Use Fire Release together to end this fight quickly ! ! » Uchiha Setsuna said in a deep voice.

As the grandson of Uchiha Hosai, Uchiha Setsuna held a higher status within the Uchiha Clan than ordinary clansmen, which had also fostered his arrogant personality.

Although the two Uchiha Clan Jonin did not like this commanding tone, they followed Uchiha Setsuna’s instructions in order to complete the mission.

The three Uchiha performed hand seals simultaneously, and a scorching Aura rose up from where they stood.

« Fire Release : Dragon Fire Technique ! ! »

« Fire Release : Great Dragon Fire Technique ! ! »

« Fire Release : Fire Dragon Flame Bullet ! ! »

Several thick fire dragons soared into the air, their flames instantly illuminating the pitch-black forest.

Sensing the commotion, the birds and beasts that had been living peacefully in the vicinity were instantly alarmed and scattered, fleeing far away from the area.

The Uchiha Clan Jonin’s mastery of Fire Release was indeed something a Chunin could not compare to.

If Senju Matsuaki had been confident in his ability to slice through the Great Fireball Technique released by Uchiha Setsuna with a single strike just moments ago, he now had no choice but to counter the Fire Release released by the three of them simultaneously with his own Ninjutsu.

« Water Release : Exploding Water Colliding Wave ! ! »

Senju Matsuaki performed hand seals, mobilizing a large amount of Chakra within his body, which made his breathing a bit heavier.

Finally, the Chakra gathered at Senju Matsuaki’s throat.

He opened his mouth, and a surging torrent of water erupted from it, with enough volume to form a lake, rushing aggressively toward the several incoming fire dragons.

The Exploding Water Colliding Wave was an upgraded version of the Water Colliding Wave, which was a step up in terms of Chakra volume and technique execution, and its power was also enhanced.

Senju Matsuaki had learned this Ninjutsu during the few days between the end of the second stage of the exam and the beginning of the third stage.

After mastering it, this was his first time using the Exploding Water Colliding Wave in actual combat.

The effect was good, but not entirely so.

As an upgraded version of the Water Colliding Wave, this Ninjutsu naturally had a higher baseline, and its power was more than a notch higher than the Water Colliding Wave.

Saying the effect was not good was because, judging solely by the execution, it was clear that Senju Matsuaki was still some distance away from mastering this Ninjutsu proficiently.

Senju Matsuaki could be seen with Chakra gathered at the soles of his feet, his body rising along with the surging water.

The scorching fire dragons collided with the surging water, raising a burst of steam at the point of contact, which blocked Senju Matsuaki’s line of sight.

« Not good ! ! »

A sense of foreboding suddenly rose in Senju Matsuaki’s heart, and he quickly held his short blade horizontally in front of him.

Within the water mist, a few flashes of light were vaguely visible, reaching Senju Matsuaki in the blink of an eye.

At close range, Senju Matsuaki was finally able to see clearly what was flying toward him.

More than a dozen sharp shuriken were flying in the air, each one coated in scorching flames, descending toward Senju Matsuaki like scattered Phoenix Sage flowers falling from the sky.

This is... a combination of shuriken and Fire Style : Phoenix Sage Fire Jutsu ?

Senju Matsuaki’s pupils constricted, and he slammed his hands onto the ground, causing the surging water to roll upwards in an attempt to block these flame-coated shuriken.

The water mist that had just risen provided cover for the incoming shuriken, causing Senju Matsuaki’s reaction to be not timely enough.

The water rolled upwards, only blocking half of the shuriken.

On the other side of the water mist, the three Uchiha were staring with their red Sharingan, trying their best to observe the situation behind the water screen.

« Phoenix Sage Flower Nail Crimson, you are becoming increasingly proficient with this Ninjutsu. »

The Uchiha Jonin, who was around thirty years old, patted the shoulder of the other Uchiha Jonin beside him and sighed with emotion.

Fire Style : Phoenix Sage Fire Jutsu is a C-rank Ninjutsu, while Phoenix Sage Flower Nail Crimson, which combines Fire Style : Phoenix Sage Fire Jutsu with shuriken, is a B-rank Ninjutsu.

Phoenix Sage Flower Nail Crimson requires not only precise control over Fire Style : Phoenix Sage Fire Jutsu but also precision in shuriken throwing ; it is an excellent combination of Ninjutsu and shuriken.

« This brat from the Senju Clan uses Water Release Ninjutsu well, but it’s a pity he lost because of his eyes. » Uchiha Setsuna said coldly.

Upon hearing this, a trace of arrogance flashed in the scarlet eyes of the two Uchiha Jonin.

There were different factions and viewpoints within the Uchiha Clan, but almost everyone was proud of their clan’s Bloodline Limit.

But on second thought, Uchiha Setsuna hesitated : « However, I cannot see clearly if the shuriken hit him ; what if… »

The two Uchiha Jonin looked at the two tomoe in Uchiha Setsuna’s eyes simultaneously, then moved their gazes away without a sound.

« I can see that his Water Release Ninjutsu blocked half of the shuriken, and as for the other half, he could not react in time. »

« Once hit by the scorching shuriken, his combat effectiveness will be greatly reduced, and then it will be our turn to reap the harvest ! ! »

The Uchiha Jonin who had just used Phoenix Sage Flower Nail Crimson narrowed his eyes slightly and said confidently, with composure.

The next instant, the massive water flow created by the Exploding Water Colliding Wave rolled forward violently, leaving the surrounding trees and soil in chaos.

After the water dissipated, what appeared in the three pairs of Sharingan was Senju Matsuaki’s already injured body.

« It seems I was a bit careless… »

Senju Matsuaki frowned slightly, endured the pain, and pulled out a shuriken that was embedded in his arm.

The only Uchiha Clan member he had ever fought before was Uchiha Kai, whom he encountered during his graduation exam, and that opponent had not yet activated the Sharingan.

Now, this was his first time fighting against Uchiha Clan members who had activated their Sharingan, and there were three of them at once.

Although Senju Matsuaki had been guarding against the genjutsu released by the Sharingan during the battle, he had neglected the Sharingan’s inherently excellent vision.

Even though the Sharingan did not have the penetrating ability of the Byakugan, its insight was terrifying compared to that of ordinary people.

Under the cover of the water mist just now, Senju Matsuaki could not see where the three opponents were, but they could vaguely see where Senju Matsuaki was.

« It seems you have reached the end of your rope ! ! »

« Just go to your death obediently ! ! »

Seeing Senju Matsuaki’s wounded appearance, Uchiha Setsuna, who had been slightly uneasy, finally felt relieved, and a cruel smile appeared on the corner of his mouth.

Not to mention the wound caused by the shuriken Senju Matsuaki had pulled from his arm.

His shoulders, abdomen, right hand, and other areas had already been burned and slashed in several places by the Phoenix Sage Flower Nail Crimson just now.

Although such injuries would not cause instant death, he was nothing more than a lamb waiting to be slaughtered.

« I was indeed a bit careless ; the Uchiha Clan’s Sharingan truly lives up to its reputation. »

Senju Matsuaki first earnestly affirmed the uniqueness of the Uchiha Clan’s Sharingan.

Then, he raised his head, his pair of crimson eyes flashing with a brilliance that none of the three pairs of scarlet Sharingan possessed.

« However, if you think you have already won just because of this, you are being too arrogant ! ! »
