# Chapitre 144 — Mangekyo Extinguished

« Whose child are you ? How did you get captured too ? »

Sensing that the young Uchiha who had escorted Senju Matsuaki over had already left, a middle-aged Ninja from the Senju Clan in the neighboring cell spoke up and asked.

The lighting was dim, so he could only see that the person captured was a child from the Senju Clan, but their specific features were hard to make out.

« My name is Senju Matsuaki. »

After pondering for a moment, Senju Matsuaki only stated his name.

If only members of the Senju Clan were held here, he would certainly be happy to discuss escape methods with his fellow clansmen.

But unfortunately, there were also several criminals from the Uchiha Clan imprisoned here.

Once information about his ability to escape the dungeon leaked out, these people would likely view it as a great opportunity to redeem their crimes.

« Senju Matsuaki... Never heard of you. Sigh, poor child... »

After a sigh, the Senju clansman in the next cell said no more ; only the sound of heavy, pained, and suppressed breathing could be faintly heard.

After a moment of silence, Senju Matsuaki shifted his attention to the girl in the opposite cell.

Given her age and appearance, the girl’s identity was beyond doubt.

The main goal of his trip, Uchiha Hikari.

However, looking at the girl’s dull expression and hollow eyes, it was clear she was in no state to communicate with anyone.

Uchiha Hikari, or rather Uchiha Wuming, seemed to have only been imprisoned by her clansmen as a weapon for a short time.

The sudden passing of her parents, the awakening of her Mangekyo Sharingan, and her imprisonment by her clansmen—the series of events had happened so quickly that she hadn’t even had time to react.

Of course, the former two only caused her grief and shock ; what she truly found incomprehensible was her clansmen’s imprisonment of her.

They were all fellow clansmen of the same Family, so why...

« Um, can you hear me ? » Senju Matsuaki tentatively tried to communicate with Uchiha Hikari.

The girl’s expression remained flat, as if she hadn’t noticed Senju Matsuaki’s presence at all.

After thinking for a moment, Senju Matsuaki spoke again : « You are from the Uchiha Clan, how did you get locked up here too ? »

A ripple of emotion appeared on the girl’s face.

That’s right, I am from the Uchiha Clan. I haven’t violated any clan rules, nor have I done anything wrong. Why am I being locked up here like an enemy or a criminal ! ?

« Why ! ! »

Tears streaming from her eyes, the girl roared with the indignation and grievance in her heart.

Senju Matsuaki’s words were like the final straw that broke the camel’s back, igniting the rage in the girl’s heart.

In the dim cell, her pitch-black eyes faintly flashed with red light, and a strange pattern, different from the Three-Tomoe Sharingan, flickered within them.

But soon, that Mangekyo Sharingan, which was on the verge of activating, faded back to its original black color, as if it were running out of power.

Before imprisoning her, the members of the Uchiha Clan certainly hadn’t forgotten to place a Seal on her.

With insufficient Chakra, even the Mangekyo Sharingan was powerless to turn the tide ; after flickering a few times, it announced its own extinguishment.

« Damn it ! ! »

The girl pounded the wall with all her might until she was exhausted, then curled up her body like a fragile young beast.

These eyes were obtained at the cost of her parents' lives, so why were they so useless now ?

Uchiha Hikari wept silently.

« The Uchiha Clan are not even human ; to think they would lock up their own clan’s child like a criminal. »

In another cell, a curse came from a Senju clansman.

Although he didn’t like Uchiha Clan whelps either, he judged based on the individual rather than the group, and such behavior from the Uchiha Clan truly made him despise them.

« What do you know, you Senju Clan idiot ! »

In another cell in the dungeon, a certain Uchiha clansman who had been imprisoned for committing a crime during the war spoke up and scolded.

« It serves Wuming right to be locked up here ! »

« Possessing powerful strength and being able to contribute to the Family is this brat’s honor, yet she refuses to obey the Family’s arrangements and obediently become our weapon. »

« If this brat had been more obedient, perhaps I wouldn’t need to be locked up here ! »

This imprisoned Uchiha clansman had been detained for abandoning his teammates and fleeing on the battlefield, commonly known as a deserter.

But in his view, a large part of the reason was that Uchiha Wuming refused to submit to the Family’s arrangements and become a weapon of war.

Otherwise, they would have long since defeated the Senju Clan, and he wouldn’t have been imprisoned by the Family for being a deserter !

Hearing the Uchiha clansman’s scolding, the Senju clansman who had just spoken up for justice retorted, refusing to be outdone : « Uchiha scum ! What powerful strength could a child possibly have ? Don’t make excuses for your own incompetence ! »

After all, most Ninjas at Uchiha Hikari’s age were at most Chunin, and could only be considered cannon fodder on the battlefield.

As for awakening the Mangekyo Sharingan at ten years old, it was simply unbelievable and not within the realm of normal consideration.

« You ! You... »

The Uchiha clansman opened his mouth, but in the end, he didn’t reply and remained silent in his cell.

Uchiha Wuming’s situation was still a secret of their Uchiha Clan ; leaking it recklessly was purely seeking death because he was tired of staying in the dungeon.

The dungeon, which had been noisy for a while, soon returned to silence.

Most of the prisoners lay in their cells, resting their eyes, or passed the time by watching the flickering lights outside the cells.

Senju Matsuaki could see Uchiha Hikari in the opposite cell from within his own.

The girl had now returned to the same hollow, dazed look as when he first saw her ; tear tracks remained on her cheeks, and she sat hugging her knees, staring blankly.

« Completely unable to communicate... » Senju Matsuaki felt a bit of a headache.

Judging from Uchiha Hikari’s behavior, she should have only been locked in the dungeon by the Uchiha Clan for a short while.

Combined with the fact that other Senju Clan members in the dungeon didn’t recognize Uchiha Hikari, it was evident that she had not yet been fully domesticated as a weapon by the Uchiha Clan.

During this period, the Uchiha Clan’s main purpose for imprisoning her should also be to break her will.

After all, a weapon with its own thoughts was not a good weapon.

At the same time, the game’s original plot stated that the Uchiha Clan during the Sengoku period transplanted ocular jutsus like Amaterasu and Tsukuyomi into Uchiha Hikari, which should also be happening during this period.

The already powerful Eight Thousand Spears, combined with the other Mangekyo ocular jutsus transplanted by the Uchiha Clan, was the ultimate killing weapon the Uchiha Clan had envisioned.

Upon closer inspection, this setting was also quite outrageous.

It wasn’t transplanting eyes, but transplanting « ocular jutsus » ; he had never heard of this concept outside of the game’s original plot.

However, since he was able to return to the Sengoku period through the space-time gate opened by the Ōtsutsuki power, and had indeed met the game plot’s original character Uchiha Hikari.

Even if there were some more outrageous settings, perhaps it wasn’t something he couldn’t accept.

After all, the Uchiha Clan had many outrageous secret techniques ; Izanagi, Izanami—which one didn’t have effects more bizarre than transplanting ocular jutsus ?

Moreover, the Uchiha Clan in the future was indeed not as prosperous as in the Sengoku period, so it was not impossible that some secret techniques had truly been lost.

After a day of tossing and turning, Senju Matsuaki was also a bit sleepy.

There was big work tomorrow, so he naturally had to get some good rest today.

Leaning against the uneven wall, he sat down, and after adjusting a few times, he finally found a relatively comfortable position and closed his eyes to rest.
