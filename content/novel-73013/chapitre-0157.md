# Chapitre 157 — Limited-Time Character Summon! Kirby!

The Valley of the End.

Senju Matsuaki’s gaze was locked tightly onto the two figures below, who appeared as gods in the eyes of the world.

He took a deep breath and turned to Uchiha Hikari beside him.

« Hikari, lower our altitude and get closer to the battlefield. »

Uchiha Hikari nodded and controlled the Susanoo to descend slowly.

The crimson giant flapped its wings, passing through the sky filled with flying wood chips and debris, as it approached the torn valley.

The closer they got, the more intense the pressure became.

The air was filled with the dense, intertwined aura of Senjutsu Chakra and Tailed Beast Chakra, making even breathing feel heavy.

Senju Matsuaki clenched his fists.

He knew that this was the crucial moment that would determine their fate.

« Uncle ! »

He stood on top of the Susanoo and shouted loudly towards the man standing atop the statue of the Thousand-Armed Kannon below.

Senju Hashirama glanced to the side slightly, a flash of surprise crossing those eyes marked with black eyeshadow.

Over the years, he had seen Uchiha Hikari’s Susanoo, so he wasn’t particularly surprised.

On the contrary, Uchiha Madara’s pupils constricted, his gaze locking onto the young girl on the forehead of that crimson Susanoo.

A look of interest curled the corners of his mouth : « To think there is still someone who has awakened the Mangekyo ? »

Uchiha Madara had assumed that, drowned in the false peace of Konoha, it would be difficult to see another pair of Mangekyo within the Uchiha Clan.

To some extent, his thinking wasn’t wrong.

Uchiha Hikari was originally from the Warring States period, living in an era even before Uchiha Madara.

And for the Uchiha Clan in Konoha, it wasn’t until the future, when they were on the brink of destruction, that the Mangekyo Sharingan appeared.

« Matsuaki ? Why are you here ? ! It’s dangerous here, retreat immediately ! »

Senju Hashirama frowned and shouted loudly.

Senju Matsuaki did not retreat ; instead, he and Uchiha Hikari and the others landed on the giant wooden figure, arriving beside Senju Hashirama.

He said in a deep voice : « Uncle, this is not a battle for you alone ! »

« This battle concerns not only the grudge between you and Uchiha Madara but also the survival of the entire Konoha Village ! »

Senju Hashirama’s brows furrowed, and just as he was about to speak, he was interrupted by Senju Matsuaki.

« I know there is a deep bond between you and Madara, and I also know you wish to end all of this with your own hands. »

Senju Matsuaki looked directly at Senju Hashirama : « But do you really think you can take down Uchiha Madara without a scratch ? »

Upon hearing this, Senju Hashirama was momentarily speechless.

He believed his strength was superior to Uchiha Madara’s, but to say he could win easily was impossible.

Based on past duels, after a fierce battle, both would end up injured, and by the time he claimed victory, he would also have sustained significant injuries.

If he were to fight Uchiha Madara to the death...

In the end, Uchiha Madara might die, but he would likely suffer heavy injuries that would be difficult to recover from !

« Uncle, do you really think this is just a personal dispute of pride between you and Uchiha Madara ? »

Seeing Senju Hashirama waver, Senju Matsuaki pressed his advantage.

« Have you ever thought about what would happen to Konoha if you were injured here ? »

« The other major Ninja Villages dare not start a war due to your might, but they are already eyeing us like tigers. If you fall, what will happen to those in the Village who trust and follow you ? »

« And Aunt Mito, Brother Hejian, and little Tsunade—how sad would they be ? »

Senju Hashirama’s eyes trembled slightly.

At this moment, Senju Tobirama once again activated the Flying Thunder God Technique to arrive at Senju Hashirama’s side.

Senju Matsuaki’s words naturally resonated with him, and he also persuaded him.

« Brother, Matsuaki is right ! »

« Uchiha Madara wants to destroy the Village, so everyone in the Village has a reason to fight against him ! »

« Heh. »

Opposite them, Uchiha Madara heard these words and sneered.

« Well said. Hashirama, your nephew is much more clear-headed than you. »

Senju Hashirama was silent for a moment, then finally sighed : « Matsuaki, you are right, but... »

He raised his head, his gaze sweeping over Senju Matsuaki, Uchiha Hikari, and Hyuga Tsukuyomi, his tone filled with a hint of helplessness.

« Your power is not enough to intervene in this battle, for now... »

He couldn’t possibly be unaware of Senju Matsuaki’s strength ; without a doubt, he had already stepped into the Kage level.

But such strength was fine for dealing with that orange Nine-Tailed fox, but facing Uchiha Madara was undoubtedly suicidal.

« I know. »

Senju Matsuaki said in a deep voice.

He was not a blindly confident person ; he knew exactly what he was capable of.

Although he had stepped into the Kage level, that was merely the Peak of ordinary Ninja, a powerhouse within the standard specifications.

But Senju Hashirama and Uchiha Madara were monsters completely outside those specifications !

However, this did not mean he lacked the ability to participate in the battle.

« Uncle, I also have my trump card ! »

He took out a card glowing with a pale golden light from his pocket.

« Your trump card is actually a card ? »

Senju Hashirama was slightly stunned, then couldn’t help but retort.

In the distance, Uchiha Madara also narrowed his eyes.

He was also somewhat curious now ; what confidence did this nephew of Hashirama have to participate in the battle between him and Hashirama.

If it was just a bluff...

Then he wouldn’t mind letting Senju Hashirama experience the pain of losing a loved one !

Senju Matsuaki’s gaze also fell onto the card in his hand.

This was the trump card he had prepared years ago for today’s battle at the Valley of the End !

[Limited-Time Character Summon Card : Can randomly summon a character from another world, time limit of one day.]

In the center of the golden card, some phantom shadows could be vaguely seen flickering and changing.

Such peculiarity did indeed seem extraordinary.

« What is this ? »

Senju Hashirama asked with confusion.

Beside him, Senju Tobirama, Hyuga Tsukuyomi, Uchiha Hikari, and even Uchiha Madara, acting as the enemy, all cast curious glances simultaneously.

Senju Matsuaki did not answer, but simply pressed his fingertips hard, easily crushing the card.

Golden light burst out from the card, forming a huge vortex in the sky.

The oppressive feeling vaguely emanating from the vortex made Senju Hashirama pause slightly.

This oppressive feeling...

In the distance, Uchiha Madara, who was observing the movement, frowned, and a sudden ominous premonition arose in his heart.

However, the next second.

A pink, round creature fell from the light, landing steadily at Senju Matsuaki’s feet.

It was only about twenty centimeters tall, not even reaching the height of an average person’s knee.

Its round body looked like a pink balloon, its small arms waving, and its big eyes full of curiosity.

« Hiya ! ! »

The unidentified pink creature looked around and let out a cheerful cry.

Seeing this, the corners of Senju Matsuaki’s mouth twitched.

This random character summon card—why did it summon this little ancestor ?

The whole scene fell into an eerie silence.

Senju Hashirama widened his eyes.

Senju Tobirama and Uchiha Madara’s mouths twitched.

Even Uchiha Hikari and Hyuga Tsukuyomi showed expressions of disbelief.

« ...Is this the trump card you were talking about ? »

Senju Hashirama asked with difficulty.

Uchiha Madara didn’t even hide his mockery : « Brat, you actually summoned a doll-like thing to come here and die ? »

Upon hearing this, a strange smile spread across Senju Matsuaki’s face.

He was only surprised by the identity of the summoned character, but he never doubted the strength of the pink creature before him !

To some extent, this pink creature was indeed a bug-level existence !

The reason he was surprised was precisely because he hadn’t expected to summon this pink creature.

« Kirby. »

Senju Matsuaki crouched down, gently shook hands with the unidentified pink creature, and at the same time, spoke its name.

« Hiya~ »

The pink creature named Kirby greeted Senju Matsuaki in a friendly manner.
