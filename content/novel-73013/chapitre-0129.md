# Chapitre 129 — The Next Era Is Named Senju Matsuaki?

The information fed back by the System left Senju Matsuaki unable to remain calm for a moment.

The 500 points were understandable ; that was the reward he obtained after defeating Sunagakure’s Yan.

But these 5,000 points...

Senju Matsuaki’s expression suddenly turned strange.

He had already realized the origin of these 5,000 points.

Just now, First Kazekage Retto was fine, but the future Second Kazekage, Shamon, had suffered quite a bit under the power of the Air Quake.

Kneeling on the ground in a sorry state, his prized Puppet shattered, and his arms covered in bloody wounds.

By the System’s judgment, Shamon had also become one of his defeated opponents in this Chunin Exams.

And although Shamon had not yet ascended to the position of Kazekage, it was only because the First Kazekage was still going strong, not because Shamon’s strength was insufficient.

In terms of strength, Shamon had already stepped into the threshold of Kage-level.

Even if there was a difference compared to some veteran Kage-level powerhouses, Kage-level was still Kage-level, representing a qualitative difference compared to Jonin.

« This... is truly good luck. » Senju Matsuaki laughed helplessly.

He knew his own limitations.

Although the destructive power of the Tremor-Tremor Fruit was immense, his control was limited, and he could not yet wield this power as easily as an extension of his own arm.

Kage-level destructive power did not equate to possessing Kage-level strength himself.

If Shamon hadn’t chosen to take the hit head-on to protect Yan behind him, he probably wouldn’t have been « defeated » like this.

With 5,500 points suddenly credited to his account, the points that had plummeted due to the Golden Fishing Hook not long ago immediately became abundant again.

One had to know that even obtaining the final victory in the Chunin Exams only awarded 3,000 points.

It was evident what a huge sum these 5,000 points were.

Just as Senju Matsuaki was overjoyed, the atmosphere in the medical room not far away was bleak.

The medical room was located right next to the examinees' lounge, and the door was tightly shut at this moment.

Retto, Shamon, and Yan—the three Kazekages of Sunagakure—were gathered here.

Retto stood leaning against the wall, his complexion slightly pale.

Shamon sat on the edge of the bed covered with white sheets in the medical room, arms stretched forward, while Yan helped him bandage them.

Seeing his respected elder, Shamon, injured and humiliated while saving him, a feeling of anger rose in Yan’s heart.

Combined with his pale complexion, he looked somewhat ferocious.

« Damn Konoha brat, when I have a chance in the future, I will definitely... »

As anger surged in his heart, the injuries on Yan’s body seemed to be aggravated, and a trace of blood seeped from the corner of his mouth.

This was the first time he had suffered such a heavy blow since he started his career.

« No. »

Before Yan could finish speaking, Retto interrupted the rest of his words with a stern tone.

« Even in the future, do not go looking for trouble with him again. »

« Not only that, if you encounter him while on a mission, you must run away immediately ! »

The resolute words not only stunned Yan, but even Shamon, who hadn’t frowned while being bandaged earlier, slightly furrowed his brows.

Facing their puzzled gazes, Retto did not explain, but slowly extended his hand, which had been hidden in his robe sleeve.

The moment they saw it clearly, both Shamon and Yan’s pupils shrank.

That large hand, covered in calluses and scars from years of battle, was now also a bloody mess, with drops of blood dripping onto the floor of the medical room.

« This... how is this possible ! ? »

Yan spoke in disbelief, even suspecting that he had fallen under a genjutsu.

In the eyes of the young people of Sunagakure, Retto was like a myth of the Land of Wind.

Even though the Ninja World rumored how powerful Senju Hashirama and Uchiha Madara were, they, the people of Sunagakure, did not think Retto was inferior by much.

It was just that their Kazekage was busy with the construction of their country and village and did not go to compete for those empty titles.

That brat from the Senju Clan, that terrifying blow had indeed defeated him ; Yan would not deny this by deceiving himself.

And Shamon had taken the hit head-on to protect him, so his serious injury was also unavoidable.

But why.

Why was Lord Kazekage also...

« But, what about Chiyo and Ebizo last time, and this time, are we just going to swallow these grudges ? » Shamon asked, equally puzzled.

« Face reality, Shamon, Yan. » Retto said in a deep voice.

« Senju Matsuaki, his name is already qualified to be remembered by everyone in the Ninja World. »

« The power just now, which seemed capable of shattering everything, erupted before Shamon’s Puppet could deploy its protective shield. »

« I relied on the power of the Sandstorm and Divine Wind to neutralize that power as much as possible, but the hurricane formed by my jutsu was shattered the moment it touched that white ball of light. »

Retto’s expression was serious, but his tone inevitably carried a hint of incredulity.

He sighed deeply : « Undeniably, I also underestimated the power of a ten-year-old boy. »

« If Shamon hadn’t blocked in front in time, I’m afraid as Kazekage, I would have become the laughingstock of the entire Ninja World. »

Hearing this, Shamon couldn’t help but widen his eyes : « In other words, what I blocked was merely the vibration weakened by your Wind Release ? »

« Correct. » Retto nodded slightly.

« The destructive power of this force is inconceivable. »

« If I didn’t use defensive measures, my body would be shattered instantly. Even though I used Wind Release to offload most of the power, if I were hit head-on, I would still suffer heavy injuries. »

This was not because Retto’s body was weak, but because it was a common problem for most Ninjas in this world—high attack, low defense.

Furthermore, one had to understand what they were facing.

For a Ninja’s flesh and blood to face the power of the Tremor-Tremor Fruit directly—if it weren’t for the fact that Senju Matsuaki had only recently obtained the Tremor-Tremor Fruit, I’m afraid there would be three cripples lying in this medical room right now.

« You must both remember. »

Retto’s expression was serious as he looked at Shamon and Yan one after the other, ensuring his words were taken to heart.

« If possible, I would spare no cost to kill him, but that is an impossible task. »

« Unless Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama lose their minds, it is impossible to allow such a Genius youth destined to reach the pinnacle to be assassinated. »

« This era is called Senju Hashirama, it is called Uchiha Madara, and the next era, barring any accidents, will be called Senju Matsuaki ! »

« Lying dormant when weak is not cowardice ; it is the way of survival in the Ninja World. »

Retto’s voice exploded in Shamon and Yan’s minds like a great bell.

The two were stunned, unable to imagine that their Kazekage held Senju Matsuaki in such high regard.

The next era will be called Senju Matsuaki ?

In other words, in the Kazekage’s view, he would be the next God of Shinobi ?

Seeing the two unable to accept it for a moment, Retto sighed inwardly.

What he feared was not the current Senju Matsuaki.

Even though the other party’s performance just now was extremely brilliant, there was an uncrossable chasm between the Jonin level and the Kage level.

What he truly feared was the current Senju Hashirama and the future Senju Matsuaki.

The current Senju Hashirama possessed overwhelming strength in the Ninja World and would inevitably escort Senju Matsuaki’s growth.

And the future Senju Matsuaki would take over this baton and continue to suppress this Ninja World.

For them, Sunagakure, this was an unsolvable problem.

As for assassinating Senju Matsuaki, Retto would never even entertain the thought.

It’s one thing to pull small tricks occasionally, but to directly kill someone’s nephew and then argue to Senju Hashirama that your Konoha can’t take revenge without evidence ?

Do you really think that because Senju Hashirama has been kind to others these past few days, he is just a spineless coward with no temper ?

The medical room fell into silence for a moment.

Just as Shamon and Yan were still digesting the information from Retto’s words, Senju Tobirama’s voice suddenly resounded over the exam venue.

« Kidney deficiency, sometimes it’s after excessive fatigue ! Try Ino–Shika–Chō brand health drink to replenish your overdrawn kidneys ! He’s good, and you’re good too ! »

« Ahem, the finals of this Joint Chunin Exams are about to begin. Will examinee Senju Matsuaki and examinee Dou please take their positions ! »
