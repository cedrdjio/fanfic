# Chapitre 22 — First Mission!

Early morning.

In front of Konoha’s gate.

Senju Matsuaki and Hyuga Tsukuyomi arrived here twenty minutes early.

Senju Hashirama also came to meet up before the agreed time.

Senju Matsuaki carried a small bag on his back, containing some essential items.

Although it was his first mission and also his first time leaving the Village to execute a mission,

the matters that needed attention were actually mentioned in the Ninja Academy courses.

As long as he paid attention in class and had a good memory, he wouldn’t forget these important pieces of knowledge.

« It seems you’re both ready. Let’s set off. »

Seeing that the two were fully prepared, Hashirama felt relieved.

In contrast, when Tobirama first led Hiruzen, Danzo, and Kagami on a mission, there were quite a few blunders.

Especially when Hiruzen carried a large bag, and Senju Tobirama was curious about what was inside, but Hiruzen was evasive.

In the end, it was discovered that not only did he bring an excessive amount of every essential item, but he also stuffed some adult magazines at the very bottom of his backpack...

He was severely scolded by Senju Tobirama on the spot.

From this perspective, his two students were much more reliable.

« Uncle, what mission are we undertaking today ? »

Senju Matsuaki quickened his pace to keep up with Hashirama and asked curiously.

« Today’s mission is commissioned by a Village in the Land of Fire to eliminate a group of Rogue Ninjas who have wandered into the Land of Fire. »

« Based on the commission details, the clothing and accents of these Rogue Ninjas suggest they are not from the Land of Fire. »

« If not eliminated in time, they will soon plunder the surrounding Villages, causing many innocent deaths. »

Senju Hashirama’s expression turned serious.

After the establishment of Konoha, conflicts large and small within the Land of Fire significantly decreased.

However, it is impossible to completely eliminate such incidents.

He originally believed that establishing Konoha could end the Sengoku era’s conflicts and achieve peace and stability.

But in recent years, he has realized that establishing Konoha might only be the first step toward peace.

He has also been pondering why Madara insisted on leaving back then ; perhaps he believed that establishing Konoha was not the peace he envisioned.

This was also the point of divergence between them.

Hashirama believed that even if establishing Konoha couldn’t bring immediate peace, it was at least the first step toward peace.

Madara, on the other hand, might have thought that establishing Konoha couldn’t truly bring peace and that an alternative path should be sought.

Thinking of this, Senju Hashirama couldn’t help but feel a bit down.

« Teacher Hashirama, are these Rogue Ninjas from other countries ? » Tsukuyomi asked curiously.

« Probably. Some may be defectors from other Ninja Villages, but most are bandits who have learned some Ninjutsu. » Hashirama said.

Senju Matsuaki suddenly understood : « No wonder they are unwilling to join a Ninja Village and prefer to be Rogue Ninjas. If they were previously wanted bandits, they would have records with the authorities. »

The three didn’t just chat idly ; their pace was not slow.

Over the past six or seven years since childhood, Senju Matsuaki and Hyuga Tsukuyomi had hardly ever left the Village.

For most people of this era, this was the norm.

Except for professions like Ninjas and merchants, the vast majority of commoners might never leave the Village they live in throughout their lives.

Along the way, both couldn’t help but feel curious.

Konoha is a Ninja Village, primarily inhabited by Ninjas, unique throughout the Land of Fire.

What were the other Villages or towns without Ninjas like in this era ?

Were the people, culture, and scenery outside Konoha different ?

However, the landscape of this era left Senju Matsuaki somewhat disappointed.

At first, he indeed saw many unfamiliar sceneries.

In the dense, undeveloped forests, there were various flowers, plants, and trees of different shapes.

Occasionally, wild animals would flash by in the jungle.

On the roads, they would sometimes pass by merchants' caravans.

Along the way, they even encountered a caravan that had once been escorted by Konoha Ninjas, who specifically came up to express gratitude to Hashirama.

However, as the group moved farther from Konoha, what they saw gradually changed.

Konoha was located in a relatively prosperous area of the Land of Fire.

The regions where Rogue Ninjas could run rampant were generally the less developed areas within a country.

As the journey neared its end, scenes of desolation, ruin, and poverty cast a gloomy shadow over the once colorful world.

People killed by bandits, people bitten to death by wild animals, or people who starved due to lack of food.

Along the way, Senju Matsuaki personally witnessed more corpses than he had seen in both his lives combined.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi, too, had left Konoha for the first time to travel such a distance and had never seen such brutal scenes.

Some of the sights encountered were so horrifying that both turned pale.

The group eventually arrived at the Village that had issued the commission as planned.

But what they saw made Senju Matsuaki and the others' faces darken.

Amidst the broken walls and ruins, charred wooden beams were stuck diagonally into the ground.

Pools of blood had congealed on the dry mud, and lingering smoke and dust mixed with the smell of blood permeated the dead air.

Scattered farm tools and broken pottery lay beside uncollected corpses, hastily covered with rubble.

« This is too much, » Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s eyes shimmered with tears, « all... »

« Tsukuyomi. » Senju Matsuaki said seriously, gently placing his hand on the girl’s shoulder.

« Use your Byakugan to scan the surroundings and see if there are any survivors. »

« Okay. »

Hearing this, Hyuga Tsukuyomi suppressed her emotions and activated her Byakugan.

After looking around, she was about to shake her head but suddenly fixed her gaze.

« Wait, there seems to be a survivor at the bottom of a well 200 meters to the right front. »

« Judging by the size, it seems to be a child. »

This is why the Hyuga Clan’s Ninjas are more popular in Konoha than the Uchiha Clan.

At the very least, within the visual range of the Byakugan, everything and everyone is exposed.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi swiftly ran toward the location her Byakugan had spotted.

Senju Matsuaki and Hashirama followed closely behind.

Arriving at the target, they saw a narrow well.

At the edge of the well, there was a thick rope, one end tied outside the well and the other descending into the dark opening.

Senju Matsuaki approached the well, grabbed the rope with both hands, and pulled hard.

From the weight transmitted through his hands, he could feel that there was indeed a child’s weight at the other end of the rope.

Seemingly frightened by the pulling of the rope, Matsuaki clearly felt the person at the other end struggling and shaking.

« Please don’t be afraid. We are Ninjas from Konoha ! » Matsuaki shouted loudly toward the bottom of the well.

These words quickly took effect ; the frequency of shaking at the other end gradually decreased.

Afraid of further startling the person hiding at the bottom, Matsuaki slowed his movements considerably.

Soon, the person at the bottom was pulled up.

They saw a wooden bucket tied to the other end of the rope, and inside the bucket curled up a thin little girl.

She was sallow and emaciated due to long-term malnutrition, and she looked at the three with fearful eyes.
