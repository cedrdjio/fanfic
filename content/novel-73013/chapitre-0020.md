# Chapitre 20 — Current Shortcomings

« It’s a bit inconvenient here. Let’s go somewhere else. »

After looking around, Senju Hashirama felt that the rooftop of the Ninja Academy building was a bit too cramped.

Consequently, the three of them quickly went downstairs and walked straight out of the Ninja Academy gate.

Upon arriving at the Konoha training ground.

Senju Hashirama took out a piece of Chakra paper.

Chakra paper can be used to test one’s Chakra nature ; it reacts differently depending on the nature of the Chakra.

He first looked at Matsumi but did not hand over the Chakra paper in his hand.

« I remember you tested your Chakra nature before, Matsumi, and the result was Earth and Water, so we won’t repeat the test today. »

Hearing this, Senju Matsumi nodded.

Within the Senju Clan, these two are likely the most common innate Chakra natures.

Some people have both, while others have only one.

However, that doesn’t mean one cannot learn Ninjutsu outside of their innate Chakra natures.

Chakra natures can also be acquired through later training.

For example, Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama did not possess all Chakra natures at the beginning.

Now, Senju Hashirama’s Chakra natures are Fire, Wind, Lightning, Earth, Water, and Yang.

Senju Tobirama is even more comprehensive, possessing Fire, Wind, Lightning, Earth, Water, Yin, and Yang.

For the two Genin, Senju Matsumi and Hyuga Tsukuyomi, their innate Chakra natures only determine where they start in their Ninjutsu training.

Because he innately possesses both Earth and Water natures, Matsumi’s training in Ninjutsu began with Earth Release and Water Release.

Then, Senju Hashirama looked at Hyuga Tsukuyomi.

« Tsukuyomi, have you tested your Chakra nature ? »

« Hokage-sama, I have not. »

Previously in the clan, she mainly focused on practicing the Gentle Fist, and only learned basic Ninjutsu like the Clone Technique.

« That’s perfect ; we can test your Chakra nature now. »

Senju Hashirama handed the Chakra paper to Hyuga Tsukuyomi.

« Also, just call me Teacher Hashirama from now on. »

« Yes, Teacher Hashirama ! »

Hyuga Tsukuyomi received the Chakra paper with both hands and then channeled her Chakra into it.

Senju Matsumi, standing nearby, also leaned in curiously, wanting to see the result.

Upon channeling her Chakra, the paper underwent a corresponding change.

With the middle as the dividing line, the left half quickly wrinkled, while the right half burst into flames.

« The left half of the paper wrinkling indicates Lightning nature, and the right half catching fire indicates Fire nature. »

Seeing Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s puzzled expression, Senju Matsumi explained.

The Hyuga Clan does not place much importance on learning Ninjutsu of other natures, believing that the Gentle Fist and the Byakugan are the foundation of the clan.

Therefore, in the education of their younger generation, they do not deliberately emphasize this knowledge.

« Not bad, two natures as well. And since your Chakra natures don’t overlap, you can perfectly complement each other, » Senju Hashirama said with a smile.

Then, he turned serious : « Although the Hyuga Clan’s Byakugan and Gentle Fist are very powerful, Ninjutsu can also have miraculous effects at times. »

« So, even if your future training focuses on the Gentle Fist, it is best to also have some reserve knowledge of Ninjutsu of various natures. »

« I was also present at yesterday’s graduation exam. Now, I will point out some of the shortcomings you currently have. »

Hearing this, both Senju Matsumi and Hyuga Tsukuyomi pricked up their ears to listen carefully.

Senju Hashirama’s strength was far greater than theirs, and he was a veteran of countless battles with incomparably rich experience.

The problems and flaws he identified might just be the direction for their future training.

This time, Senju Hashirama looked at Hyuga Tsukuyomi first.

« Tsukuyomi, you awakened your Byakugan early, and your coordination with the Gentle Fist is good ; that is commendable. Seeing your battle yesterday, you already have some of your older brother’s style. »

« However, the shortcoming is that the transition between your Gentle Fist and the Taijutsu learned at the Ninja Academy is quite rigid. »

« And as I mentioned earlier, you have a weakness in Ninjutsu, which will be a breakthrough point for enemies. »

« I have learned much, Teacher Hashirama. » Hyuga Tsukuyomi humbly accepted Hashirama’s advice.

Then, Senju Hashirama looked at Senju Matsumi.

« Matsumi, your performance yesterday surprised both me and your second uncle. I didn’t expect you to have improved so much. »

« However, your use of Monster Strength is not yet proficient enough, or rather, your control over Chakra is insufficient, which led to quite a bit of wasted Chakra. »

« Compared to the Monkey, your Ninjutsu repertoire is insufficient, but after all, there is a five or six-year age gap between you, so there is no need to be impatient in this regard. »

« Finally, your practical combat experience is insufficient. Compared to other Ninjas who have experienced real life-and-death battles, the gap is very obvious. »

« I understand, Uncle. »

Senju Matsumi also had a realization in his heart.

His proficiency in Monster Strength was indeed not deep ; he felt that Chakra was consumed very quickly when using the Monster Strength Slash, and it turned out this was one of the reasons.

As for practical combat experience, he had never experienced a real life-and-death struggle.

Previous battles were limited to the instructional combat with Tsugikuni Yoriichi and sparring with Hyuga Tsukuyomi.

« Tomorrow we will undertake the first mission for Team 11. After you go back today, make your preparations and gather at the Village gate at eight in the morning. »

« As for now, to celebrate you becoming Genin, I’ll treat you to barbecue ! »

Some are happy, some are worried.

In a large mansion within the Uchiha Clan compound.

A Uchiha youth of about fifteen years old, with a Ninja sword strapped to his back, stood there with a gloomy expression.

In front of him was Uchiha Kai, who had his head lowered with a fearful expression.

This Uchiha youth was Uchiha Kai’s older brother, Uchiha Setsuna.

He was a hawk within the Uchiha Clan, fanatically believing that the Uchiha Clan was the greatest clan.

The hawks of the Uchiha Clan believed that Konoha was jointly founded by Senju Hashirama and Uchiha Madara, and that after Senju Hashirama, Konoha should be led by the Uchiha Clan.

Originally, when Uchiha Madara was still in Konoha, they were somewhat well-behaved, believing that the position of Konoha’s Second Hokage should rightfully belong to the Uchiha.

However, now that Uchiha Madara had left the Village, it was clear that the person most likely to become the future Hokage was Senju Tobirama.

Therefore, the hawks of the Uchiha Clan became restless.

While Senju Hashirama was still around, they dared not openly make a move.

But once Senju Tobirama ascended to power in the future, they would likely be unable to restrain themselves any longer.

And even now, although the hawks of the Uchiha Clan dared not act blatantly, they still had some small movements in private.

« You trash ! Not only are you worse than the brat from the Senju Clan, but you are even worse than the brat from the Hyuga Clan ! »

Uchiha Setsuna scolded, even wanting to reach out and slap his younger brother.

But seeing that Uchiha Kai’s hand was still wrapped in bandages, he withdrew the thought.
