# Chapitre 153 — Returning to Konoha

It seems Uchiha Hikari went to great lengths to ensure everything was foolproof.

Though, the process was somewhat amusing.

For hundreds of years, many white-haired members of the Senju Clan must have felt puzzled by this inexplicable rule.

Noticing the boy’s gaze, Uchiha Hikari smiled and blinked.

« Matsuaki, by the way, who is this girl ? »

Senju Hashirama asked curiously.

« Her name is Uchiha Hikari, she is my friend. »

The air went quiet for a moment.

« Uchiha ? »

Senju Hashirama blinked and scratched his head.

« Hmm... Is she from the Village ? How come I haven’t seen her before ? »

Senju Tobirama’s gaze, however, suddenly sharpened.

He lowered his gaze, let it rest on Uchiha Hikari for a few seconds, and spoke slowly.

« Uchiha Hikari... Is this related to that scroll you discovered back then ? »

Senju Matsuaki felt a shiver in his heart.

As expected, he couldn’t hide it from his second uncle.

Senju Tobirama stood up, walked over to Uchiha Hikari, and looked down at her.

Uchiha Hikari met his gaze without flinching, though her right hand subconsciously gripped Senju Matsuaki’s sleeve.

Senju Matsuaki patted her hand, about to speak.

Before he could speak, Senju Tobirama had already looked away, and the stagnant atmosphere eased along with it.

Senju Tobirama looked at Senju Matsuaki and said softly, « Matsuaki, you have always had your own judgment. As long as you ensure she doesn’t harm the Village, Big Brother and I won’t ask any more questions. »

He already had some guesses about the girl’s identity.

That scroll passed down through the Senju Clan might be closely related to the girl.

And that scroll had been passed down since the Sengoku era...

How could a girl so closely related to it be a simple person ?

But just as he said.

He didn’t trust this unknown Uchiha girl, but he trusted his eldest nephew.

« Uh... can’t we ask ? »

Senju Hashirama hung his head in disappointment.

Senju Tobirama might not want to ask, but he still had some curiosity and gossip in him.

Senju Hashirama thought for a moment and said.

« By the way, if she is a lost member of the Uchiha Clan from outside the Village, do we need to notify the Uchiha Clan ? »

« Also, according to the Uchiha Clan’s rules, there would be housing allocation for lost clan members whose identities are confirmed. »

For ninja clans, having clan members lost outside is a very normal thing.

The order of the ninja Village hadn’t been established for many years.

During the years of war, many families had clan members lost outside.

If it were other families, it might be difficult to distinguish.

But for families with Bloodline Limits like the Uchiha and Hyuga, verifying a clan member’s identity couldn’t be simpler.

However...

Uchiha Hikari probably didn’t feel any sense of belonging to the Uchiha Clan.

In that Family, apart from her parents, there weren’t many fond memories for her.

Even in this era, those detestable clan members from back then had all turned to dust, but this bad impression couldn’t be easily erased.

Senju Matsuaki took a deep breath and was about to explain, but Uchiha Hikari spoke first.

« I don’t want to go back to the Uchiha. »

Her voice was soft, yet clear.

Senju Tobirama was slightly stunned.

Senju Hashirama looked at Senju Matsuaki, then at Uchiha Hikari, and suddenly burst into laughter.

« So that’s it ! You want to be with this kid, Matsuaki ! ? »

Senju Hashirama’s loud voice made the whole office shake.

This time, he had accidentally hit the truth.

Uchiha Hikari’s cheeks turned slightly red, but she didn’t deny it, only gripping Senju Matsuaki’s sleeve tighter.

Senju Tobirama glanced at his elder brother expressionlessly and said flatly, « Big Brother, can you be a little more restrained when you speak ? »

Uchiha Hikari answered calmly, « I just want to be with him. »

Senju Matsuaki also spoke up, « Uncle, Second Uncle, Hikari just arrived in the Village and probably isn’t used to it yet. »

« It just so happens that my house is quite large, and there are a few rooms that have been empty. »

« This... »

Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama looked at each other.

Senju Hashirama patted his eldest nephew on the shoulder.

« Matsuaki, don’t make little Tsukuyomi sad. »

« Tsukuyomi ? »

Senju Matsuaki was slightly taken aback, not understanding why she was suddenly mentioned at this moment.

But speaking of Tsukuyomi, he had been out of the Village for several days during this period, and he really hadn’t seen her for many days.

Her injuries should have recovered by now, and he could go visit her in a few days.

Seeing that Senju Matsuaki didn’t seem to understand what he meant, Senju Hashirama was about to continue, but was stopped by Senju Tobirama.

« Big Brother, why do you care so much about the children’s business ! »

Hearing this, Senju Hashirama immediately fell back into a low-pressure state.

Senju Matsuaki said goodbye to the two with a wry smile, then turned and left, taking Uchiha Hikari with him.

Walking out of the Hokage Building, the afterglow of the sunset spilled onto the two, casting two long shadows.

The sky was dyed orange-red, and the clouds caught their attention.

Uchiha Hikari looked up, her pitch-black eyes reflecting the orange-red light and shadow.

She said softly, « The sky here... seems a bit different. »

Senju Matsuaki turned his head to look at her, « Do you like it ? »

Uchiha Hikari thought for a moment, the corners of her mouth curling up slightly, « Not bad. »

Senju Matsuaki was stunned for a moment, then smiled and ruffled her hair.

« Let’s go, I’ll take you to see our home. »

« Mm. »

Uchiha Hikari walked lightly beside Senju Matsuaki.

« By the way... who is Tsukuyomi ? »

Uchiha Hikari asked softly, seemingly casually.

« She is my partner and teammate. »

Senju Matsuaki answered subconsciously.

Sensing something off in the girl’s words, he frowned slightly and asked, « Hikari, have you been in the Seal for too long ? Are you feeling unwell ? »

« No, I’m not. »

Uchiha Hikari looked at his somewhat slow reaction and smiled helplessly.

« That’s good. »

Senju Matsuaki felt relieved.

« Tsukuyomi is also a very kind girl ; perhaps you might unexpectedly get along well. »

Senju Matsuaki said with a smile.

Sometimes, friends are not people with the same personality, but rather complementary ones.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi and Uchiha Hikari’s personalities were somewhat different, but in his view, they were both kind-hearted people.

« Hmph, we’ll see. »

Uchiha Hikari turned her gaze away, leaving him only the back of her head.

Senju Matsuaki was a bit confused but didn’t think much of it, assuming she was just tired from traveling these past few days.

The two walked along the streets of Konoha, and when they passed a dango shop by the street, a steaming sweet fragrance wafted out.

Uchiha Hikari’s steps paused slightly, her gaze lingering for a moment on the skewers of three-colored dango in the customers' hands inside the shop.

Senju Matsuaki noticed and stopped.

« Want to try some ? »

Uchiha Hikari hesitated for a few seconds before nodding gently.

Senju Matsuaki smiled, walked straight to the shop, bought a portion, and handed one of the skewers to her.

Uchiha Hikari took the dango and took a bite.

The unique soft and chewy texture of the sticky rice and the sweetness of the red bean paste melted on her tongue.

Her eyes brightened slightly.

« How is it ? »

Senju Matsuaki asked with a chuckle.

« ...It’s okay. »

While she said it was just okay, she didn’t stop, taking another bite.

Senju Matsuaki watched her rare display of childishness, the corners of his mouth curling up involuntarily.

The setting sun merged their shadows together, and the evening breeze blew past, causing the wooden hairpin in her hair to sway gently.
