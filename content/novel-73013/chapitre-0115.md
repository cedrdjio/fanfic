# Chapitre 115 — Matsuaki: I Just Went Fishing

It had been about an hour since Senju Matsuaki left and returned, just as the Chunin Exams were in full swing.

Seeing him return, Hyuga Tsukuyomi asked with concern, « Matsuaki, did something happen ? »

Generally speaking, unless there was an urgent matter, examinees usually stayed in the waiting room to prepare for their exams and would not leave easily.

Even those who had already passed this round of selection would remain in the waiting room to rest or watch the battles of other examinees.

For Ninjas, intelligence was also an important factor in determining the outcome of a battle.

If one could fully grasp the opponent’s strengths in ninjutsu, taijutsu, genjutsu, or their combat style before the fight, they were already halfway to victory.

This was especially true for Hyuga Tsukuyomi, who possessed the Byakugan.

On the spectator stand outside the waiting room, she would occasionally use her Byakugan to observe opponents on the field that she felt were worth noting, collecting quite a bit of intelligence.

Moreover, when Senju Matsuaki returned just now, she observed that his sleeves were slightly damp, and a few water droplets were still clinging to his short silver hair.

« Nothing happened, I just went fishing, » Senju Matsuaki said, scratching his head and telling the truth.

« Eh, fishing ? » Hyuga Tsukuyomi was surprised by this unexpected answer, her small mouth slightly agape.

Going fishing in the middle of the Chunin Exams, that was just too relaxed.

However, since he did so, there must be a reason for it ; she figured he probably wanted to release some pressure as it had been quite high recently.

Thinking of this, the look of concern in Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s eyes deepened.

Next, the two stopped chatting and focused on watching the others' battles from the stands.

For Senju Matsuaki, there were still two opponents to watch out for in this Chunin Exam.

Yan from Sunagakure and Dou from Kumogakure were both Ninjas with Jonin-level strength who possessed Bloodline Limits or secret techniques.

Whether it was Magnet Release or Lightning Release Chakra Mode, both were quite troublesome.

Especially Dou from Kumogakure ; Senju Matsuaki could feel that the other had seemingly made significant progress in just these few months, and his strength might no longer be inferior to those Jonin of the Uchiha Clan he encountered yesterday.

The second round of selection for the third stage of the Joint Chunin Exams proceeded steadily and soon came to an end.

In the third-to-last battle, Hyuga Tsukuyomi fought against a Ninja from Kirigakure and easily secured victory after a few rounds.

In the final battle, Dou from Kumogakure fought against a Genin from Konoha, ending the fight swiftly and cleanly.

With the formal conclusion of the second round of selection, only fourteen of the original twenty-eight people remained to participate in the third round.

It was nearly noon, and under the guidance of the exam staff, the audience exited in an orderly fashion to go to Konoha for lunch and rest, returning to the spectator seats in the afternoon.

As for the examinees in the waiting room, some left with their companions, while others simply found a chair to lie down and rest.

There were also some stalls in the waiting room selling food. Ninjas didn’t have high demands for food, so having lunch right there was perfectly acceptable.

Inside the waiting room.

Dou, who had just won, was picking his nose with his left hand. Feeling hunger in his abdomen, he subconsciously reached out with his thick right hand to rub it.

His nose twitched, and he keenly caught the familiar aroma of food wafting from a nearby stall, which instantly triggered the gluttony that was already stirring within him.

« Although that Konoha opponent just now didn’t make me feel excited, the snacks here are actually quite decent. »

Dou’s eyes lit up, and he strode in the direction the scent was coming from.

Upon walking closer, he saw that it was actually three old acquaintances.

After half a day, the originally pale complexions of Hiruzen Sarutobi and Danzo Shimura had long since returned to normal, though their expressions still seemed somewhat weary.

The third person was Yan from Sunagakure, who didn’t seem to plan on leaving the waiting room at noon either.

After his previous battle ended, he had stayed alone and somewhat aloof on the stands, staring expressionlessly at the matches below.

But no matter how unsociable one was, one still had to eat, so he also came to the waiting room to find a suitable stall.

He didn’t have any particular preference for food, and upon seeing Hiruzen Sarutobi and Danzo Shimura, whom he had met once before, he chose this stall.

Yan had a rather deep impression of these two.

Although these two were born in Konoha, they had a profound understanding of the wind-and-sand spirit of Sunagakure. If not for fear of causing misunderstandings in Konoha, he would really like to invite the two to live and work in Sunagakure.

It was nearing noon, and there wasn’t much Oden left, just about twenty skewers soaking in the broth.

Seeing Yan carefully selecting, Hiruzen Sarutobi and Danzo Shimura exchanged a subtle, cunning glance.

Yan did not know that the moment they observed him walking towards them, Hiruzen Sarutobi and Danzo Shimura tacitly poured the « special ingredients » they had prepared into the broth base.

The bait had been cast, just waiting for the fish to bite~

And seeing Dou from afar also walking towards this side, Hiruzen Sarutobi and Danzo Shimura narrowed their eyes even further.

After standing behind Yan for a few seconds, Dou frowned impatiently and said, « Are you done choosing yet ? Stop dilly-dallying. »

« You savage, have a little patience. »

Yan retorted coolly, even deliberately slowing down his movements.

Two minutes later, he paid, took the paper cup in his hand, and slowly walked away, returning to the wooden chair where he had been resting earlier to eat.

After Hiruzen Sarutobi put the money away, he didn’t even glance at it and just casually set it aside.

Just kidding, did they go to all the trouble of mixing potions and setting up a stall for this meager income ?

They had a greater goal, okay !

« Ah, a regular customer. What would you like this time ? » Hiruzen Sarutobi looked at Dou with a just-right flattering smile and greeted him warmly.

« You don’t have much left here. »

Looking at the compartment of dark broth with only a dozen or so skewers left, Dou frowned.

Although this would be enough to fill him up given his appetite, as the big brother of the younger generation of Kumogakure, he didn’t intend to eat alone.

He had a good impression of this stall and intended to buy some for his companions who were also participating in the assessment and resting in the waiting room.

With more people, these dozen or so skewers seemed a bit insufficient.

It must be said that the previous strategy of Hiruzen Sarutobi and Danzo Shimura was effective.

After long planning, they finally became the preferred vendors for Yan and Dou, enabling them to catch both of them in one fell swoop at this moment !

« Valued guest, we still have a dozen or so skewers, is that not enough ? » Hiruzen Sarutobi scratched his head and asked, puzzled.

« I need to bring some for my companions too, they eat quite a lot. »

Hiruzen’s eyes lit up and he said, « That’s great, we can just cook some more. »

« Then thank you so much ! »

Dou’s eyes also lit up, and he thanked them with a smile.

This way, he could be full, and his little brothers could be full, solving the problem of food and clothing easily~

« No problem, I’ll give you some extra broth, eat it while it’s hot~ »

« Hahahaha~ »

Both sides looked at each other and laughed in a friendly manner, taking a big step forward in the harmonious relationship between the vendor and the consumer.

Fifteen minutes later, Dou left with two full paper cups in his hands, not yet knowing what awaited him.

And in the distance, Hiruzen Sarutobi and Danzo Shimura stared with subtle gazes at the back of the person leaving.

After waiting for him to walk far away, the two packed up their stall at lightning speed, pushed their cart, and left the waiting room through the exit.
