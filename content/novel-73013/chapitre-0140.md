# Chapitre 140 — Gate of Time and Space! The Sengoku era!

Thinking of this, Senju Matsuaki did not hesitate any longer and directly selected the Seal location in front of him as the target for his Check-in System.

[Check-in Location · Seal Location ! Obtained : Gate of Time and Space (Ultimate Storm Limited) !]

The System notification sounded.

Following it appeared a gray stone door, two people tall.

His previous guess was not wrong ; the Check-in System indeed provided a way to break the deadlock in front of him.

It was just that the result was truly unexpected.

Senju Matsuaki looked at the door that had appeared out of thin air in some astonishment, his brain stalling for a moment.

« Wait, what did you say this is ? »

« Gate of Time and Space, and it’s Ultimate Storm Limited ? »

« Where did this wave bring him to ? »

The information from the System notification was like the final puzzle piece, piecing together the scattered intelligence into a complete picture.

The joint Seal of the Uchiha Clan and the Sarutobi Clan.

The sealed object known as the « Ultimate Evil Weapon. »

The Gate of Time and Space that appeared due to this Check-in...

If his guess was correct, this should be the door leading to the past that Ōtsutsuki Toneri opened using the power of the Ōtsutsuki in the original game plot.

Then, the object sealed beneath this Seal location was no longer a mystery as it had been before.

Uchiha Hikari.

An original character from the game plot.

She was born in the Sengoku era, in the Uchiha Clan, and the era she lived in was even before Uchiha Madara and Uchiha Tajima.

Among the known, named figures of the Uchiha Clan, Uchiha Hikari was from the earliest period and had the highest seniority, which is why some people jokingly called her the « Uchiha Great-Grandmother. »

Her experience was a microcosm of the tragedies of the Sengoku era.

She awakened her Mangekyo Sharingan because her parents died in battle, was treated as a weapon by her clansmen due to the power of her ocular jutsu, and was later jointly sealed by the Senju Clan and the Sarutobi Clan because she was feared...

Setting aside these tragic experiences, her talent was truly absurd.

In the Uchiha Clan, it was not uncommon for close relatives to pass away due to war, but very few were able to be stimulated to awaken the Mangekyo Sharingan at such a young age.

This, in itself, was a symbol of talent.

Now, there were two choices in front of Senju Matsuaki.

One choice was to directly break the Seal and release Uchiha Hikari.

But after all, she was sealed by the Senju Clan, and she might not be grateful after figuring out Senju Matsuaki’s identity.

What’s more, after being unsealed, Uchiha Hikari was full of hatred for the entire Ninja World because of the imprisonment and abuse by her clansmen and the Seal from other Ninja clans.

Senju Matsuaki’s current strength was only at the Jonin level.

Once Uchiha Hikari, who had awakened the Mangekyo Sharingan, went on a rampage, he would likely be finished right here today.

As for the safety of the Ninja World, there was no need to worry too much about that.

Assuming Uchiha Hikari was really unsealed in this era and learned that her old enemies, the Uchiha Clan, the Senju Clan, and the Sarutobi Clan, were all in the same Village now...

She would likely go to Konoha for revenge immediately.

And then, she would be suppressed by Senju Hashirama in an instant.

And his second choice was, naturally, to return to the past through the Gate of Time and Space in front of him.

In the game plot, Uchiha Hikari sacrificed herself, and Boruto returned to the past to save her through the time-reversal power of Ōtsutsuki Toneri, passing through exactly this door in front of Matsuaki.

Although the time-reversal setting of this game plot was somewhat inconceivable.

But since this Gate of Time and Space was already placed in front of Senju Matsuaki, there was nothing to question.

As a reward obtained from the Check-in, Senju Matsuaki could naturally examine its information in detail.

After taking a step forward, his mind stirred, and the information about this Gate of Time and Space appeared in his mind.

[Gate of Time and Space (Ultimate Storm Limited) : A door linked to the past through the power of the Ōtsutsuki. Limited by the Laws of time and space, there are restrictions on both the range of activity and time.]

That made sense.

Seeing the introduction of this door, Senju Matsuaki breathed a sigh of relief instead.

If this Gate of Time and Space had no restrictions at all, he might not have dared to step into it easily.

The more restrictions there were, the less likely it was to go out of control.

And in the Sengoku era where Uchiha Hikari lived, top-tier powerhouses should only be at the Kage level ; existences surpassing the Kage level like Senju Hashirama and Uchiha Madara should not have been born yet.

With Senju Matsuaki’s strength as a Jonin, he wouldn’t be invincible in that era, but he would be the elite of the elite.

« Since that’s the case, let’s go back to the past once. »

After weighing the options, Senju Matsuaki made a decision and walked quickly to the Gate of Time and Space.

After sensing his arrival, the door opened automatically, revealing a scene inside as deep as the starry sky.

Taking a deep breath, he stepped forward, and his figure disappeared into the Gate of Time and Space.

...

The Sengoku era.

It was a chaotic and cruel era.

In this era, the hidden Villages of later generations did not exist. Ninjas often used families or organizations as bonds to accept commissions such as escorting and assassination.

And wars of various scales would break out between different Ninja families because they served different masters or competed for commissions.

Compared to wars between hidden Villages, the scale of wars between Ninja clans was smaller, but the degree of cruelty was about the same.

For example, at this moment.

The moment Senju Matsuaki regained the real sense of the surrounding space, a sound of breaking through the air rang out from behind him.

Whoosh—

Senju Matsuaki, who could be considered a veteran of many battles, didn’t even need to turn his head to judge that the sound came from a shuriken.

The short blade behind his back was unsheathed, and the exposed edge just happened to block and deflect the shuriken aiming at his neck.

Looking back, his sharp gaze instantly locked onto the enemy’s figure.

Several sounds of breaking through the air rang out.

It was not one person, but a group of people.

In a circle around Senju Matsuaki, six Ninjas had arrived one after another.

Some of them were standing on thick tree branches, staring down at Senju Matsuaki from a high position.

Others were standing on the ground, also holding Ninja blades, with murderous intent, blocking Senju Matsuaki’s retreat.

And on their clothing, there was a familiar pattern—a fan.

At this moment, the Golden Finger platform, which had been silent for a long time, suddenly made a sound.

[A-rank mission released : Save Uchiha Hikari. Reward : 5000 points !]

[B-rank mission released ! Defeat the enemy Ninjas in the Sengoku era. Reward : 50 points for Genin strength, 200 points for Chunin strength, 500 points for Jonin strength !]

A familiar double mission release.

The first mission, saving Uchiha Hikari, was the main quest.

The second mission was a side quest along the way, which was also good for grinding points.

When he heard the content of the mission clearly, the look in Senju Matsuaki’s eyes as he looked at the surrounding Uchiha suddenly became « kind. »

It’s about time.

The six Uchiha Ninjas who had already surrounded Senju Matsuaki clearly did not realize that something bad was happening.

On the contrary, they showed hideous smiles in unison.

« A lone brat from the Senju Clan, how unlucky. »

The leading Uchiha was a Ninja with Chunin strength, who identified his identity based on the Senju Clan crest on Senju Matsuaki’s clothes.

He was like a hyena eyeing prey, with a fierce look in his eyes, and said jokingly : « Today is your death day ! »

Then, he raised his voice and gave instructions to the other five Uchiha Clan Ninjas.

« Brothers, activate the Sharingan ! »

As soon as the words fell, countless scarlet gazes stared fixedly at Senju Matsuaki.
