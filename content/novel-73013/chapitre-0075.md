# Chapitre 75 — Meeting Chiyo and Ebizo Again!

« Scorched wood, is it from Fire Release Ninjutsu or a bonfire ? » Senju Matsuaki asked, his gaze narrowing.

For an ordinary person, it is difficult to distinguish the scent of trees burned by Ninjutsu from the continuous burning of a bonfire.

However, for the Inuzuka Clan, whose sense of smell is greatly enhanced, it is easy to tell them apart.

Inuzuka Rei twitched her nose and said, « This scent should be from a bonfire, and it’s vaguely mixed with the target’s scent... »

« Besides the target’s scent, I can also smell another scent... It seems to be the smell of a popular brand of shampoo in Konoha recently. It should also be one of our Konoha Ninja. »

« The recently popular shampoo, is it the one newly released by the Yamanaka Clan ? »

« That’s right. That floral scent is indeed unique, natural, and not pungent. It’s a pity it was sold out as soon as it hit the shelves, » Inuzuka Rei sighed.

With the Joint Chunin Exams currently underway, the various clans of Konoha have not missed this business opportunity, and the Yamanaka Clan took the chance to launch the new shampoo.

For the Inuzuka Clan, shampoo with a scent that is too pungent is nothing but trouble for them.

With their sense of smell amplified ten thousand times, the scent of some inferior shampoo’s artificial additives feels like it is directly crashing into their nasal cavities.

Suddenly, Inuzuka Rei’s expression changed slightly.

She stopped speaking and frowned, carefully sniffing the lingering scent in the air.

A moment later, she said gravely, « Something is wrong. I also smell a very strange scent. »

« It seems to be the smell of metal and minerals. I subconsciously ignored it just now, but logically speaking, this is not a scent that should naturally be here. »

Senju Matsuaki felt a bit bewildered by Inuzuka Rei’s description.

At this moment, Inuzuka Rei’s sense of smell was terrifyingly keen ; compared to him, it could even be said that she had an extra dimension to observe the world.

« Let’s go check the location of the bonfire. »

Senju Matsuaki quickly made a decision.

Since a situation had arisen that even Inuzuka Rei could not distinguish, it was better to continue following the scent to the scene.

Soon, the two tracked the scent to the bonfire.

Half a scorched branch remained on the ground, surrounded by ashes from the burning.

On the ground and tree trunks near the bonfire, there were obvious man-made traces such as footprints and Kunai scratches.

Looking at the scorched piece of wood, Senju Matsuaki stepped forward, extended his finger, and tapped it lightly.

« There is still residual heat. It seems they left here not long ago. »

Senju Matsuaki turned to look at Inuzuka Rei beside him and asked, « Can you track the direction they went next ? »

« No problem. The scent here is very strong ; it won’t take much effort. »

« Then I’ll leave it to you. »

« Wait, it seems there are two scents heading straight for us. »

After sniffing intently, Inuzuka Rei suddenly stopped with a strange expression.

...

« Damn it, that guy Yan actually abandoned us and ran off alone ! »

Chiyo was hurrying along with a puppet on her back, looking indignant.

« Sister, it is indeed more efficient for Yan to do this. If we can find another jade chess piece, there won’t be any problem with our second stage exam. »

Seeing that his sister was in a bad mood, Ebizo spoke up to console her.

After all, the three of them had formed a team to act together now, so getting along harmoniously was more conducive to achieving their goal.

« That’s true. »

Upon hearing this, Chiyo’s expression softened slightly.

Since forming a team with Yan, their efficiency had indeed improved significantly.

Originally, the two of them didn’t have a single jade chess piece, but after about half a day of searching, they had already seized one.

Once they found one more, the siblings would only need to head to the finish line to complete the exam.

« It’s all that Konoha brat’s fault. Otherwise, with our strength, we would have already headed straight to the finish line by now. »

Thinking of this, Chiyo gritted her teeth again.

Even though a day had passed, this lingering anger still weighed on her heart.

First, her puppet was broken, and then her chess piece was taken away.

She had spent most of the first day of the second stage exam repairing her puppet and searching for jade chess pieces.

« Uh, Sister, am I having hallucinations ? »

Ebizo was about to echo Chiyo’s words, but when he saw some familiar figures ahead, he suddenly stopped in his tracks and fell into deep thought on the spot.

« Hiss ! »

Ebizo, who was deep in thought, felt pain coming from his arm and gasped.

« Sister ! Why are you pinching me ! ? »

« If it hurts, it seems it’s not a hallucination... »

Chiyo said with a grave expression.

The two looked at each other and both saw the terror in the other’s eyes.

It’s over, this wave is coming for us !

Unfortunately, when Senju Matsuaki’s figure appeared in their field of vision, it also meant that their scent had long since appeared within Inuzuka Rei’s perception range.

Just as the two were planning to turn and run, they discovered a short blade flashing with cold light already lying across their path.

« Was it your companion who passed through here just now ? » Senju Matsuaki asked in a deep voice.

On the side, Inuzuka Rei and her Ninja Dog also assumed a posture ready to attack.

Once the two made any unusual movements, they would unleash their killing moves without reservation.

« We also just arrived here. Did something happen here just now ? »

Ebizo feigned ignorance without changing his expression, and Chiyo also wore a look of confusion.

« The two of them also have that scent of metal and minerals on them, » Inuzuka Rei said abruptly.

Upon hearing this, the feigned bewilderment on Ebizo and Chiyo’s faces instantly stiffened.

« Sunagakure, metal, minerals... Magnet Release ! ? »

Looking at the forehead protectors the two were wearing, a flash of inspiration struck Senju Matsuaki, and his expression instantly turned gloomy.

A pair of red eyes seemed to carry scarlet killing intent, making the Sunagakure siblings feel like they had fallen into an ice cellar.

« Wait ! If you kill us here, you won’t have time to save the person being hunted ! »

Both of them already had Chunin-level skills and were highly sensitive to killing intent.

They could feel that the other party was no longer hesitating about whether to kill them like when they met before, but had truly developed a firm intention to kill.

As a smart person, Chiyo did not say anything about Sunagakure retaliating if they were killed.

She knew clearly that even if they were killed, Sunagakure would likely have no evidence to accuse Konoha or find the murderer.

Just like how their Sunagakure’s Yan, when able to ensure no evidence was left behind, even dared to audaciously plot to seize the Byakugan.

In this relatively peaceful period, to hold another major Ninja village accountable, one had to provide evidence.

However, from Senju Matsuaki’s reaction, she roughly guessed that the person Yan was hunting was very important to Senju Matsuaki.

Thus, she used this as a breakthrough point, wanting to exploit the other party’s eagerness to save someone to spare their lives.

Upon hearing this, a cold glint flashed in Senju Matsuaki’s eyes, and he suddenly swung his blade towards the two.

The short blade carried a suddenly erupting monstrous strength, leaving the two with a vague sense of suffocation and despair.

They would die ! !

In her desperation, Chiyo quickly threw out the puppet on her back, while the Chakra threads connecting to the puppet in her hands moved.
