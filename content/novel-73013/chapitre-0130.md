# Chapitre 130 — The Finals Begin! Senju Matsuaki VS Dou!

« Second Uncle, you really have contributed a lot to the development of Konoha. »

Inside the waiting room, Senju Matsuaki stood up to stretch his muscles, his expression looking strange.

Listening to Senju Tobirama advertise the « Kidney Treasure » drink jointly produced by the Ino–Shika–Chō families, he truly felt an indescribable sense of dissonance.

However, he had also provided some inspiration for the design of this advertising slogan.

In a way, could it be considered that he had set up his Second Uncle ?

« Senju Matsuaki. » Dou’s calm voice came from the side, speaking his name.

Senju Matsuaki turned his head to see that Dou had walked to the exit of the examinee waiting room leading to the arena, though he didn’t know when.

Outside the exit, one could already hear the sounds of the audience shouting and anticipating their appearance.

However, due to the architectural design, these noisy sounds were well isolated outside the waiting room.

« Be careful. I’m not as easy to deal with as that guy from Sunagakure, » Dou said in a deep voice.

In his eyes, Senju Matsuaki saw a Battle Intent as violent as thunder.

It seemed that his performance in the semifinals just now had not made him shrink back, but instead further ignited his masculinity.

Indeed. How could someone with the spirit of a warrior who could hold off ten thousand men ever lose their fighting spirit so easily ?

« You should be careful too, » Senju Matsuaki said slowly.

« In my opinion, you aren’t much stronger than that guy from Sunagakure. »

Dou narrowed his eyes slightly and said coldly, « Arrogant ! »

The brief exchange ended there.

Facing the cheers of the audience, the two protagonists of the upcoming finals stepped out of the waiting room together.

Until they walked to the arena and stood still, Senju Matsuaki reviewed the intelligence on Dou.

Not only his memory of the future Third Raikage but also his observations of his combat process during the Chunin Exams.

Lightning Release Chakra Mode.

By wrapping lightning around his body to activate his flesh, he becomes as swift and violent as a thunderbolt.

With offense and defense integrated, he also possesses a speed that ordinary people cannot reach. He is, likewise, an almost unsolvable existence.

It is quite simple to restrain such speed.

Either be faster than him, just like when the future Fourth Raikage suffered a setback against the Golden Flash, Minato Namikaze.

Or use large-scale attacks to greatly reduce the advantages brought by his speed.

If the attack range is sufficient, no matter how fast he is, he will be a turtle in a jar, with nowhere to run !

But the problem to consider here is that attack intensity and range cannot usually be achieved simultaneously.

Ninjutsu uses Chakra as fuel, and ordinary Ninja are limited by their Chakra volume, so they need to make trade-offs between intensity and range.

Ordinary attacks seem meaningless against that « Lightning Release Armor. »

However, meeting the requirement of having both intensity and range doesn’t seem to be a problem for him.

Thinking of this, Senju Matsuaki already had a preliminary plan for the strategy in the upcoming battle.

He drew the short blade from his back and held it in front of him.

In his line of sight, the blade seemed to slice Dou’s body in front of him into left and right halves.

Dou was also mobilizing his muscles, puffing air out of his nostrils.

« Joint Chunin Exams, third stage assessment, finals, battle start ! ! »

When the two were ready for the upcoming battle, the voice announcing the start of the battle echoed over the arena.

As the voice fell, their bodies shot out like arrows from a bowstring.

In Senju Matsuaki’s hand, Chakra condensed on the blade, and the power of the Tremor-Tremor Fruit was also attached to it.

At the same time, he fully mobilized the strength of every part of his body through his Breathing Technique, instantly maxing out his state.

On the other side, Dou was not to be outdone.

Chakra and lightning intertwined and wrapped around his body, finally maintaining a perfect balance to form the so-called « Lightning Release Armor. »

« So fast ! ! » Senju Matsuaki’s pupils constricted.

Only when facing Dou from Kumogakure did he have a profound understanding of his speed.

Almost in the blink of an eye, the strong man wrapped in lightning scorched a charred mark on the ground of the arena.

The sizzling sound of rampaging electric arcs seemed to be ringing in Senju Matsuaki’s ears.

At this moment, Dou was like a wild bull riding lightning ; his terrifying speed and sturdy body brought a strong sense of oppression.

« Hmm ? » But just a second before colliding with Senju Matsuaki’s blade, Dou’s brows furrowed slightly, and a bad premonition arose in his heart.

The next instant, he decisively changed direction, shot off to the side, and once again distanced himself from Senju Matsuaki.

« This destructive power, it lives up to being the power that could beat Shamon into that state... »

After standing still, Dou raised his right arm.

The Lightning Release Armor wrapped around his knotted muscles had, at some point, developed a gap.

After all, the Lightning Release Armor was condensed from Chakra and lightning, and the lightning and Chakra that flooded over in the blink of an eye repaired this gap.

However, this scene had already caused Dou to furrow his brows deeply.

This meant that if he collided head-on with the power of vibration, the Lightning Release Armor around his body would probably only be able to last for three seconds before it shattered.

Taking a deep look at the vibration power condensed in Senju Matsuaki’s hand, Dou did not feel discouraged.

When watching the semifinals, he already had a preliminary understanding of this power of Senju Matsuaki.

What happened just now was merely a probe, which further confirmed his conjecture.

« This guy from Kumogakure, after discovering that there is no advantage in a head-on battle, the next step should be high-speed maneuvering to find my flaws. »

A flash of brilliance appeared in Senju Matsuaki’s eyes, and he instantly discerned his intention.

Playing to one’s strengths and avoiding weaknesses is almost an instinct for an experienced Ninja.

And Dou was not a reckless brute who only knew how to fight head-on, unlike his rough and savage appearance.

Just like his future child, that is, the future Fourth Raikage. He also possessed an overwhelming dominance in speed that surpassed Ninja of the same level.

Faced with such an enemy who has the advantage in speed, one must either control him or be faster than him !

The future Fourth Hokage, Minato Namikaze, had the Flying Thunder God Technique as a reliance when facing the Fourth Raikage, which allowed him to achieve the latter.

But as for now, Senju Matsuaki is really not particularly outstanding in terms of speed.

In this way, the relatively feasible approach is the former. That is, to control the opponent through some means.

Of course, the battle has just begun. At this stage, both sides of the battle are usually still probing each other, observing and looking for the opponent’s weaknesses.

If one throws a big move right from the start, it actually gives people a feeling of being at their wit’s end.

« Since that’s the case, let’s play for a bit ! »

Senju Matsuaki narrowed his eyes slightly. Since Dou from Kumogakure is trying to find the flaws in his Tremor-Tremor Fruit ability and then attack from the weak points...

Then it just so happens that this moment can become an opportunity to hone the power of the Tremor-Tremor Fruit !

Matsuaki held the blade in his right hand, while his left hand was left empty. Then, he clenched his left fist, sank his waist slightly, and set up the posture most suitable for exerting force.

In the eyes of Dou watching from afar, as well as the Five Kage, Daimyo, and the audience in the stands, Senju Matsuaki’s Aura had changed.

If he was like a drawn sword with its sharp edge exposed before, standing there with his blade.

Then at this moment, he seemed to have transformed into a savage Vicious Beast ready to devour someone !
