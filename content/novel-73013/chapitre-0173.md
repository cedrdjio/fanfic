# Chapitre 173 — Wood Release: True Several Thousand Hands Subdues the Six-Tails! The Eight-Tails' Trail Appears in the Land of Lightning!

A few days later, in a fog-shrouded marshland on the border of the Land of Water.

Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama stood on the branch of a giant tree, overlooking the wetland shrouded in miasma below.

« Big Brother, the Six-Tails is in the central area of this region. »

Senju Tobirama held a marked map in his hand, his eyes fixed firmly ahead. « The Hidden Mist Village patrol changes shifts every three hours. We have already bypassed their main警戒 lines. »

« However... »

He paused, his gaze narrowing slightly. « Once a battle breaks out, First Mizukage Byakuren should be able to detect the commotion here very quickly. »

Senju Hashirama grinned. « Isn’t that perfect ? Saves us the trouble of going to find him to say hello. »

As soon as he finished speaking, the thick fog ahead suddenly surged violently.

A low and intimidating roar came from the depths of the mist, and immediately after, the ground began to tremble.

« It’s here. »

Senju Hashirama leaped down from the branch, landing steadily on the wet, soft ground.

Senju Tobirama followed closely behind, crossing his arms and staring straight ahead, prepared for battle.

Within the thick fog, a massive dark shadow slowly emerged.

It was a giant creature the size of a small hill—the Six-Tails, Saiken.

Its body was translucent blue-white, covered in viscous liquid, and six tentacle-like tails danced behind it, each swing kicking up a cloud of acid mist.

The Six-Tails opened its giant mouth, and a burst of bubbles spewed out, sweeping toward the location where Senju Hashirama stood.

Wherever the bubbles passed, grass and trees withered instantly, and the surface of rocks was corroded into pits.

Senju Hashirama, however, remained motionless, not even intending to dodge.

« Big Brother ! »

Senju Tobirama shouted sharply, about to use the Flying Thunder God to take him away from the spot.

Just at this moment...

« Wood Release : Wood Human Technique ! »

A giant wooden shield carved with a demon’s face rose from the ground, blocking all those corrosive bubbles.

The bubbles hit the shield with a hissing sound but could not penetrate it in the slightest.

The Six-Tails seemed to be enraged ; its six tails whipped simultaneously, and countless acid liquids poured down like a rainstorm.

« Tobirama, you step back a bit. »

Senju Hashirama’s voice remained calm, even carrying a hint of a smile.

Senju Tobirama frowned slightly, but still retreated dozens of meters as instructed.

He understood his older brother’s personality ; when he spoke in this tone, it meant he was about to get serious.

Sure enough, Senju Hashirama put his hands together, and the massive, suffocating amount of Chakra within his body suddenly erupted.

« Sage Art : Wood Release : True Several Thousand Hands ! »

The earth shook violently, and a statue of a thousand-armed Buddha, many times larger than the Six-Tails, rose from the ground.

Behind the Buddha statue, thousands of wooden arms unfolded simultaneously, blocking out the sky and sun.

« ... »

The Six-Tails looked at the terrifyingly imposing Buddha statue, and its movements suddenly stalled.

That fierce Tailed Beast showed an emotion akin to fear for the first time, its six tails tightly curled in front of it, and its body involuntarily retreated backward.

However, at this moment, it realized that even its only path of retreat had been blocked by that white-haired Ninja using Water Release Ninjutsu at some unknown time.

Wait, just Seal me directly, don’t beat me up, is that okay ?

It swallowed a mouthful of acid, and such a thought emerged in its mind.

In the distance, the Hidden Mist Village Ninjas who noticed the commotion here were all stunned.

First Mizukage Byakuren stood on the top of a tall tree, looking at the Buddha statue that seemed to be about to pierce the sky with a livid face.

The few guards behind him were already drenched in cold sweat, their legs trembling slightly.

« This is... the true strength of the First Hokage... »

A Jonin muttered to himself, the Kunai in his hand almost impossible to hold steady.

Byakuren gritted his teeth, but did not issue any orders.

It wasn’t that he didn’t want to stop it, but...

He knew that in the face of absolute power, any strategy was meaningless.

If he went to stop them now, what if the opponent attacked them along with the Six-Tails ?

His own aging, broken body didn’t matter, but he couldn’t implicate the elite forces in the Village to die !

Senju Hashirama ignored the onlookers in the distance ; his gaze was locked on the Six-Tails.

« Sorry, Six-Tails. For the sake of peace in the Ninja World, please sleep for a while. »

« Top Transformed Buddha ! ! ! »

As the words fell, the arms of the Thousand-Armed Guanyin swung down in unison.

Countless fists smashed down like meteors, each punch carrying the power to collapse mountains and split the earth.

The Six-Tails tried to run, but its massive body appeared so insignificant within the coverage of the True Several Thousand Hands.

The first punch landed, and the Six-Tails' body was smashed into the depths of the marsh.

The second punch, the third punch... hundreds of punches followed one after another, and the ground was blasted into a bottomless giant pit, with mud and gravel shooting into the sky.

The Six-Tails didn’t even have time to let out a scream before it lost consciousness in the continuous attacks, its massive body lying limp at the bottom of the pit.

Senju Hashirama stopped his attack, dispersed the True Several Thousand Hands, and landed lightly on the ground.

Senju Tobirama appeared beside him at the right time, forming hand Seals to perform the Sealing Jutsu.

Complex sealing runes spread from Senju Tobirama’s palms, covering the Six-Tails' body, gradually compressing and shrinking it, and finally merging it into a specially made sealing scroll.

Senju Tobirama put away the scroll and nodded at Senju Hashirama.

« Big Brother, it’s done. »

Senju Hashirama patted the dust off his hands and turned to look at the dense forest in the distance.

There, Byakuren and the Hidden Mist Village Ninjas were still standing in place, motionless.

The two sides stared at each other ; the Hidden Mist Ninjas had complex expressions, while Hashirama was all smiles.

Senju Hashirama showed a friendly smile and waved at them.

« Brother Byakuren, we’ll be taking the Six-Tails ! Come to Konoha for tea when you have time ! »

Byakuren’s face turned from blue to white, finally turning into a helpless sigh.

He raised his hand, stopping his subordinates who wanted to rush out.

« ...Let them go. »

The guards looked at each other, and finally lowered their heads.

Just like that, Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama left calmly with the Six-Tails' sealing scroll under the watchful eyes of the Hidden Mist Village.

...

Meanwhile, in the Land of Lightning.

Senju Matsuaki, Uchiha Hikari, and Hyuga Tsukuyomi had already penetrated deep into the hinterland of the Land of Lightning.

The terrain of the Land of Lightning was completely different from that of the Land of Water ; everywhere there were steep mountains and precipitous cliffs, the sky was covered with dark clouds, and lightning struck from time to time.

« Traces of the Eight-Tails' activity appeared here. »

Hyuga Tsukuyomi activated her Byakugan, her white eyes scanning the surrounding valleys. « Five kilometers ahead, there is a large amount of residual Chakra, which should have been left by the Eight-Tails. »

Senju Matsuaki nodded, looking around vigilantly.

« Where are the people from Kumogakure ? »

« None found. » Hyuga Tsukuyomi shook her head. « At least within the visual range of my Byakugan, there are no traces of Kumogakure Ninjas. »

Uchiha Hikari narrowed her eyes : « Strange, according to the intelligence, Kumogakure should be determined to get the Eight-Tails. How could they let it roam outside ? »

« Either they have given up, or... »

Senju Matsuaki paused, his gaze becoming sharp. « It’s that they are waiting for us to appear, wanting to be the ones to reap the rewards in the end ! »
