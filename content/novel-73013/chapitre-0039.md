# Chapitre 39 — Asura Chakra!

Just now, I was busy making suggestions for the future of Konoha, so I temporarily put personal matters aside.

Now, with nothing to do, Matsumi began today’s check-in.

Still choosing his uncle as the check-in target, the notification of a successful check-in rang in Matsumi’s mind.

[Check-in Character · Senju Hashirama ! Obtained : Asura Chakra (Pure Chakra Version) !]

A hint of imperceptible joy appeared on the originally calm face of Senju Matsumi.

This time, a golden legend really popped out !

Asura was the younger son of the Six Paths Sage, Otsutsuki Hagoromo, and the common ancestor of the Senju Clan and the Uzumaki Clan.

After his death, Asura’s Chakra reincarnated through his descendants for generations, locked in an endless struggle with the reincarnated Chakra of Indra.

The future Uzumaki Naruto and Uchiha Sasuke are the reincarnations of Asura and Indra’s Chakra, respectively.

In this current era, the reincarnations of Asura and Indra’s Chakra are Senju Hashirama and Uchiha Madara, respectively.

In Matsumi’s view, the Asura Chakra did not bring a change in the quantity of Chakra, but a qualitative change in permission.

As descendants of the Six Paths Sage, both the Senju Clan and the Uchiha Clan have unique advantages.

However, the only ones who can truly reach the upper limit at present are Senju Hashirama and Uchiha Madara.

Being able to stand out from many clansmen, apart from their own outstanding talent, was also because their ancestors' Chakra raised their upper limits.

For Matsumi of the Senju Clan, Asura Chakra is like a key, allowing him to further develop the treasures of his Bloodline Limit.

And the « Pure Chakra Version » means that this rewarded Asura Chakra does not contain Asura’s will.

This proves that there is no possibility of being influenced by Asura’s soul after merging with this portion of Chakra, completely dispelling Matsumi’s only concern.

Just at the moment the reward was issued, Matsumi felt a warm force flowing from his Dantian to his limbs and bones.

Gradually, Matsumi felt as if every cell in his body was rejoicing.

The Breathing Technique that was maintained at all times was now capturing oxygen even faster, simultaneously causing his internal Qi and blood to become increasingly vigorous.

On the side, Senju Tobirama, who had been silent because the content of the discussion had little to do with the section he was responsible for, looked at Matsumi not far away with some confusion.

« How does this kid, Matsumi, suddenly make me feel more like big brother... »

Tobirama thought with some doubt in his heart.

However, thinking back to the scene where Matsumi was making suggestions in the Hokage Office just now without being humble or arrogant, he dispelled this illusion.

This kid is so smart, he is clearly more like me, how could he be like that silly big brother...

I don’t know if it will be possible in the future...

Tobirama smiled at Matsumi with satisfaction, and a thoughtful look appeared in his red eyes.

It seemed that his body had gradually become accustomed to the changes brought about by Asura Chakra, and the previous feeling of warmth and boiling blood gradually faded.

Seeing this, Matsumi did not feel disappointed.

The changes brought by Asura Chakra are subtle and cannot be achieved overnight.

He never fantasized that he could awaken Wood Release on the spot the moment he obtained Asura Chakra.

Time gradually approached the agreed meeting time.

The conversation in the Hokage Office gradually quieted down, and the clan leaders stood up to leave one after another.

« Matsumi, you wait and come with us. »

Just as Matsumi intended to leave with the crowd, Hashirama suddenly spoke up and kept him behind.

Inside the bright conference hall.

An oval conference table was placed in the center, surrounded by several simple white chairs.

The white-painted walls were hung with masterpieces by painters from the Land of Fire ; some were of mountains, rivers, and national landscapes, while others were of blades and swords, wind, fire, thunder, and lightning during Ninja battles.

The windows of the conference hall faced the entrance, and at this moment, a beam of sunlight fell through the window onto the conference table, forming a bright section.

Both Konoha and Kumogakure attendees were punctual and were seated before the agreed meeting time.

The representatives from Konoha participating in this meeting included Senju Hashirama, as well as the leaders of some Ninja clans and staff members from various departments.

As for Senju Tobirama, he stood expressionlessly behind Senju Hashirama’s chair, playing the role of a qualified bodyguard.

Even a shadow of a village is flesh and blood, and sometimes it is inevitable to be injured or killed due to enemy sneak attacks.

Although for Hashirama, perhaps he didn’t need guards very much, this practice had continued since the Sengoku period, and everyone was accustomed to it.

Even so, it was not enough to make the people of Kumogakure feel surprised.

What really surprised them was that beside Senju Tobirama, there stood another figure with silver hair and red eyes.

If not for the Konoha Ninja headband worn on his forehead, one might have mistaken him for an apprentice from the Ninja Academy.

However, looking closely, the Kumogakure Ninjas could clearly see the difference between the two.

Both had red eyes, but in Senju Tobirama’s eyes, it was a cold crimson, like a drop of fresh blood falling on white paper, which was chilling.

And in Senju Matsumi’s eyes, it was like a raging fire, symbolizing the vigor of a young man.

Just now in the Hokage Office, after the internal meeting of Konoha ended, Uncle Hashirama kept him behind.

Just when Matsumi was waiting for further instructions, Uncle Hashirama pulled him straight to the venue, letting him act as a bodyguard for this meeting just like Tobirama.

Participating in such a village-to-village meeting as a bodyguard for the first time, it would be impossible for Matsumi not to be curious.

Although he remained calm and focused on the surface, his eyes were already implicitly sizing up the Ninjas from Kumogakure opposite him.

On the Kumogakure side, at the head of the table was the Kumogakure Elder.

He held a high position in Kumogakure and came here this time representing the First Raikage to discuss matters related to the Joint Chunin Exams, making him the most important person in the delegation.

As for the other Kumogakure Ninjas in the seats, they were staff from various departments of Kumogakure, who accompanied them mainly to provide advice and answers on some professional matters.

Behind the Kumogakure Elder, there were also two guards, one big and one small.

They were the First Raikage’s guard, Hong, and Hong’s direct disciple, Dou.

Although named as guards, Hong was already a candidate for the next Raikage of Kumogakure, and his status in Kumogakure was equally important.

Because both sides had one large and one small guard standing behind their main personnel, the conference table appeared strangely symmetrical.

« It’s that brat ! »

Seeing the silver-haired, red-eyed kid behind Senju Hashirama, blue veins bulged on Dou’s forehead, and he only felt anger surging in his chest.

If it weren’t for this brat getting in the way yesterday, it is estimated that in another ten years or so, the Sharingan would have already spread its branches and leaves in Kumogakure.

However, he also knew that what they did yesterday could not be brought to the table, and now was not the time to make trouble.

Before the meeting started today, the Kumogakure Elder had repeatedly urged him to follow instructions and not to exert his subjective initiative at will.
