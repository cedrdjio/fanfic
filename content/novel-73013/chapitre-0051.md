# Chapitre 51 — Matsumi's Bounty

In the Land of Fire, on a tree-lined path.

A gentle breeze blew through the woods, carrying the fragrant scent of flowers and grass, making one feel refreshed and relaxed.

Senju Matsuaki and Hyuga Tsukuyomi walked side by side on the road. The passing merchant caravans and occasional farmers were unfazed by the pair’s Ninja attire.

« I still prefer being in the Land of Fire~ »

Matsumi narrowed his eyes slightly and remarked leisurely.

They had just returned from completing a mission in the Land of Rivers, which borders the Land of Fire, where they were responsible for clearing out a group of Rogue Ninja who had fled from the Land of Fire.

Geographically, the Land of Rivers is sandwiched between the Land of Fire and the Land of Wind, serving as a transitional zone between the prosperous and the barren.

After finishing the mission, they rested briefly before setting off back to the village, and by now, they had returned to the territory of the Land of Fire.

« This should be our last mission before the Chunin Exams. Who knows when we’ll be leaving the village again. »

Hyuga Tsukuyomi did a rough mental calculation and realized that the start of the Joint Chunin Exams was not far off.

The most obvious proof of this was that there were far more merchant caravans on the road than usual.

Some keen-nosed merchants, having heard from various sources that the Joint Chunin Exams would be held in Konoha, were eagerly making preparations in advance.

Just as the two were chatting.

« Fire Release : Great Fireball Technique ! ! »

On the road ahead, three figures wearing black tight-fitting combat suits appeared out of nowhere.

One of them performed hand seals and spat a Great Fireball towards where Matsumi was standing.

Seeing this, Matsumi frowned slightly.

« Coming for me ? »

The next instant, the Great Fireball, accompanied by a wave of heat, slammed into the ground, and the mass of flames turned into a sea of fire.

A moment later, the flames dissipated, leaving only scorched marks on the ground.

Seeing this, the three unknown Ninjas were stunned for a moment, then revealed expressions of relief.

« Big Brother’s Great Fireball Technique has improved again, he’s reduced the target to nothing but ash ! »

« They say the Great Fireball Technique can’t kill anyone ; I think that’s just because they haven’t met our Big Brother ! »

« Oh no, if there’s no corpse left, how are we going to collect the bounty ! ? »

Just as the three attackers were panicking about how to collect the bounty, two figures silently crept up behind them.

Matsumi drew the short blade from his back. Amidst the flashing of the blade, blood spurted from two of them, and the light in their eyes quickly faded.

The Ninja who had just used the Great Fireball Technique only then reacted, turning around to find his two underlings already lying on the ground.

He was about to avenge his underlings, but upon feeling the cold touch against his neck, he awkwardly lowered his hands.

« Do you want to avenge your accomplices ? »

A cold glint flashed in Matsumi’s eyes, and for a moment, killing intent permeated the air.

Before the opponent could respond, Matsumi threw a punch with his left hand while twisting his right wrist, turning the hilt upward and the blade downward, knocking the unknown Ninja to the ground and pinning one of his palms to the earth with the short blade.

At the same time, Matsumi gently raised a fist, aiming it at the opponent’s head.

If there was any movement, his Strength Enhancement would instantly erupt, smashing the Ninja’s head and the soil beneath into a pulp with a splat.

After all, some Ninjas might look subdued, but they could still have some underhanded tricks up their sleeves.

For example, his second uncle could use the Water Release : Heaven Weeping to shoot sharp water needles directly from his mouth ; even if his hands were controlled, it wouldn’t stop him from blowing an enemy’s head apart.

« Please spare my life, I won’t take revenge anymore ! ! »

As the intense pain traveled from his palm to his brain, the Ninja immediately begged for mercy in terror.

« Who sent you, and why attack me ? » Matsumi said coldly.

Only then did he carefully examine the appearance of the attacker.

All three were dressed as Ninjas, with somewhat worn-out Ninja tool pouches at their waists. Although they wore forehead protectors, there were no village insignias engraved on them.

Typical attire for Rogue Ninja, and not the kind that had defected from a hidden village.

« This... it was for the bounty. »

The Rogue Ninja hesitated for a moment, but upon meeting Matsumi’s chilling gaze, he shuddered and revealed the reason for the attack.

« Bounty ? » Matsumi frowned slightly.

« Is it the Underground Exchange ? » Hyuga Tsukuyomi, who was standing to the side, spoke up.

« I once happened to hear my brother mention that there is an intermediary organization in the Ninja World called the Underground Exchange, which accepts requests from government officials and wealthy people from various countries to issue bounties. »

Hearing Tsukuyomi say this, Matsumi gradually recalled some impressions, remembering what kind of place this was.

Before the era of hidden villages began, the Underground Exchange was quite large and was a force not to be underestimated.

However, after the major nations successively established hidden villages, the living space of the Exchange was constantly squeezed, and its scale became smaller and smaller.

However, there were always people with shady requests, such as officials wanting to assassinate superiors who were blocking their promotion, or the heirs of nobles and the wealthy assassinating their brothers to compete for inheritance rights.

In addition, some hidden villages would issue bounties on Ninjas from other villages.

What puzzled Matsumi was that Ninjas who were targeted for bounties were usually Chunin or Jonin, and he was merely a Genin ; who would issue a bounty on him ?

« Is it Kumogakure, or Uchiha ? »

He roughly recalled everyone who might have a motive and found that only two groups were the most suspicious.

One was Kumogakure ; during their last sparring session, he could vaguely feel that the future Third Raikage had murderous intent toward him, but being on away ground made it difficult to act.

The other was Uchiha ; based on the hint from Uchiha Kagami last time, there were also people within the Uchiha Clan who held hostility toward him.

« We saw the bounty for you, sir, at the Exchange. The bounty offered is very generous—five million ryo, almost comparable to the bounty on a Jonin. »

This Ninja seemed to notice that the killing intent in Matsumi’s eyes was gradually fading, so he continued to explain, fearing that if Matsumi were unhappy, he would behead him too.

« Five million ryo ? »

Senju Matsuaki and Hyuga Tsukuyomi exchanged a glance, both seeing the astonishment in each other’s eyes.

This was truly a heavy investment.

Since becoming Genin, they had performed quite a few missions, but even if their combined income were added together, it was still far from five million.

After all, Ninjas are basically in the service industry ; it’s not that easy to get rich overnight.

A bounty of five million ryo is equivalent to the price of Asuma Sarutobi minus one Chiriku.

In the original timeline, the bounty on Asuma Sarutobi, an elite Jonin, was 35 million ryo, but that was something that would happen decades later.

A five-million ryo bounty for a target who is just a Genin—it’s no wonder these Rogue Ninja were tempted.

However, the Ninja World is not like some « Great Pirate » world ; bounties are not a standard for measuring strength. The bounties on some government officials or nobles are several times higher than those on Jonin.

Thinking of this, Matsumi’s brow relaxed.

After ending the opponent’s life with one strike, he gently shook the blood off the short blade and then sheathed it.

Since they had made their move, they had to be prepared to be killed in return.
