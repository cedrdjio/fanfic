# Chapitre 168 — The Second Round Tailed Beast Capture Plan!

The Three-Tails instinctively closed its eyes, using its hardest forehead to take the blow head-on.

Seizing this opportunity, Senju Tobirama flashed into motion, appearing directly above the Three-Tails.

The speed of his hand Seals was so fast that they left afterimages.

« Water Release : Heaven Weeping ! »

Countless water needles, as fine as ox hair, descended from the sky, striking the Three-Tails densely.

Although their power was not great, their sheer number forced the Three-Tails to divert energy to defend itself.

Just then, a sharp light flashed in Senju Tobirama’s eyes.

« Now is the time ! »

He slammed his hands together.

« Water Release : Water Prison Jutsu ! »

A massive water prison formed instantly, trapping the Three-Tails inside.

The Three-Tails roared as it slammed against the walls of the water prison, but Senju Tobirama continued to output Chakra, maintaining the stability of the prison.

« Sealing Team, act now ! »

Senju Tobirama shouted, his voice carrying across the entire sea area via Chakra.

The members of the Sealing Team on the shore had long been prepared ; upon hearing the order, they immediately formed Seals.

« Sealing Jutsu : Four-Symbols Seal ! »

Ten members of the Sealing Team simultaneously pressed their palms to the ground. Complex sealing runes lit up, spreading along the pre-arranged Formation.

These runes extended along the coastline, finally submerging into the sea until they enveloped the Three-Tails.

« What ? ! »

The Three-Tails discovered with horror that its movements were becoming increasingly sluggish, and the Chakra within its body was being drained by the sealing Formation.

« Damn humans ! You plotted against me ! »

It struggled desperately, but at this moment, Senju Tobirama formed Seals once again.

« Water Release : Hard Whirlpool Water Blade ! ! »

His pride-and-joy Water Release Jutsu, combined with the earlier Water Prison Jutsu and the Sealing Jutsu performed by the Sealing Team, finally completely suppressed the Three-Tails.

« It’s over. »

Senju Tobirama narrowed his eyes slightly, inwardly letting out a sigh of relief.

The Three-Tails let out an unwilling roar, but eventually, its voice grew faint as it was sealed into the container prepared by the Sealing Team.

The sea surface returned to calm.

Senju Tobirama gathered Chakra under his feet and walked across the water back to the shore.

The captain of the Sealing Team stepped forward quickly and respectfully handed over the sealing scroll.

« My lord, the Three-Tails has been successfully sealed. »

Senju Tobirama took the scroll, checked it carefully, and only nodded after confirming there were no errors.

« Good work. »

He gazed at the misty sea in the distance, his eyes deep and thoughtful.

« Time is tight, return to the village immediately ! »

Not long after Senju Tobirama and the Sealing Team he led departed, several figures appeared on the shore.

The aftermath of the fierce battle just now still caused the waves to churn and surge.

Sensing a remnant of barbaric Chakra in the air, First Mizukage Byakuren frowned.

« Damn, did we still arrive one step too late... »

...

Konoha, Hokage Building.

Senju Hashirama put on a serious expression and spread out several intelligence scrolls on his desk, which detailed the recently collected movements of the Tailed Beasts.

« For now, the Ichibi and the Three-Tails have been successfully sealed. »

Senju Hashirama looked up at the few core members in front of him. « According to the latest report from the Intelligence Team, the trails of the Two-Tails, Four-Tails, and Five-Tails have all been locked onto. »

He tapped his finger on the three marked points on the map.

« The Two-Tails is currently active in the Ghost Mist Mountains in the southwest of the Land of Fire. The terrain there is complex, and the forest is dense, making it suitable for an ambush. »

Senju Hashirama looked at Senju Tobirama, who was sitting to the side. « Senju Tobirama, you lead the team responsible for the capture of the Two-Tails. »

« Let Hiruzen and the others accompany you, and assign two elite squads to assist. »

By now, Hiruzen Sarutobi, Danzo Shimura, and others had all successively promoted to Jonin. Although they had not yet reached the Kage level, they were already a force that could not be ignored.

« The Two-Tails ? »

Senju Tobirama nodded expressionlessly. « I will formulate the corresponding tactics. »

« The Four-Tails is located in the lava zone on the border of the Land of Earth. »

Senju Hashirama’s gaze shifted to the three young Ninjas on the other side. « Senju Matsuaki, Uchiha Hikari, Hyuga Tsukuyomi, you three form a squad and are responsible for the capture of the Four-Tails ! »

This matter was originally proposed by Senju Matsuaki, so he was naturally not surprised at this moment.

He nodded steadily and replied, « Understood, Uncle ! »

At present, Senju Matsuaki’s own strength had long stepped into the Kage level, and Uchiha Hikari, relying on the Mangekyo Sharingan, was also a combat force that could not be underestimated.

In addition, the former’s Wood Release and the latter’s Sharingan both had an innate suppression over the Tailed Beasts.

Plus Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s Byakugan to search for the traces of the Four-Tails...

It was hard to think of a reason for failure.

« Very good. »

Senju Hashirama looked at the three of them with satisfaction. « With the abilities of you three, as long as you cooperate tacitly, dealing with the Four-Tails should not be a problem. »

He finally pointed to a huge red circle in the center of the map.

« As for the Five-Tails... »

Senju Hashirama’s eyes became serious. « It is wandering in the plateau region in the north of the Land of Fire. The terrain there is open and the visibility is good, but that also means it is difficult to set an ambush. I will lead the team personally. »

He stood up, placed his hands on the desk, and his gaze swept over everyone present.

« Then, the second round of Tailed Beast capture operations officially begins ! »

...

Border of the Land of Earth, lava zone.

The scorching heat waves twisted the air, and the rocks underfoot cracked into a spiderweb pattern, with a dark red glow faintly visible in the fissures.

The terrain here was like the entrance to Hell, with solidified magma left behind by volcanic eruptions scattered everywhere.

« The Four-Tails is three kilometers ahead. »

Hyuga Tsukuyomi activated her Byakugan, veins bulging around her pupils. « Its Chakra is abnormally violent ; it seems to be in an angry state. »

« Angry ? »

Senju Matsuaki frowned. « It seems that before we arrived, someone else must have already come looking for it and angered it. »

« However, they ultimately failed to successfully subdue and Seal the Four-Tails. »

« In this situation, the Four-Tails will be even more ferocious, but it will also be easier to expose flaws. »

Uchiha Hikari opened her Three-Tomoe Sharingan, her scarlet eyes reflecting the churning firelight faintly visible in the distance.

« This is our chance. »

Senju Matsuaki said in a deep voice, « Proceed according to plan. »

The three looked at each other and set off at the same time.

The Four-Tails was currently letting out a deafening roar.

Its massive body was like a moving volcano, and its four thick tails whipped about, kicking up sparks that filled the sky.

The surrounding rocks had been melted into pools of magma by the high temperature, and the air was filled with a pungent smell of sulfur.

Not long ago, he had finished his rest as usual and intended to leave his magma-filled lair to go for a walk.

Unexpectedly, several humans wearing identical forehead protectors latched onto him like ghosts.

The old man leading them, although not tall, had Earth Release Jutsu that could be described as superb, easily causing the ground to shake.

There was also another man wrapped in bandages from head to toe, who was equally troublesome to deal with.

Fortunately, in the magma-filled terrain, he also had advantages that these Ninjas could not match.

In the end, he successfully drove the two of them away with his powerful Lava Release, but he also sustained some injuries.

He had wanted to take a leisurely walk, but inexplicably returned with injuries ; rage churned in his chest !

« Roar— ! »

The Four-Tails opened its mouth and spewed a scorching column of lava, shooting straight into the sky.

Just then, a crimson figure descended from the sky.
