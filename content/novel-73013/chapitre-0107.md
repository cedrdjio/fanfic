# Chapitre 107 — A New Function!

Seeing him arrive, a clear ripple of emotion appeared on Uchiha Hosai’s face, his expression slightly anxious.

« What is the situation ? Why didn’t Uchiha Setsuna and the others come back with you ? »

« Elder, Uchiha Setsuna and the others did not arrive at the designated rendezvous point on time. »

« What ! ? »

Uchiha Hosai stood up abruptly, the wrinkles on his old face appearing somewhat distorted due to the sudden movement.

« To deal with a mere brat with the strength of a Chunin, could the three of them really have run into some accident ? You returned much later than expected ; have you investigated the situation clearly ? »

« I was waiting for them to rendezvous on the outskirts of the forest when I saw firelight in the distance, accompanied by the sounds of Water Release Ninjutsu being cast, » the Ninja, kneeling on one knee, reported.

« Because the distance was too great, it was difficult to see the situation clearly. When I prepared to go and provide support, I heard a loud noise from afar, and after that, there was no more movement. »

« The situation over there also alerted other Ninjas. I noticed that the Ninjas on duty near the arena were rushing towards that direction, so I did not rashly approach, but instead returned to report to you, Elder. »

After speaking, the Ninja lowered his head and remained silent, not daring to look up and meet Uchiha Hosai’s gaze.

Although he believed he had done nothing wrong throughout the mission, the result was that the three people he was supposed to support had gone missing.

After biting the bullet and finishing his report, he dared not speak another word, lest he provoke the Elder.

« Fool ! You are a Jonin, after all ; just because others went there, you didn’t dare to go ! ? »

Sure enough, after hearing this, Uchiha Hosai was furious, his cold Sharingan filled with Killing Intent.

If it weren’t for the fact that the three missing people were all his trusted aides, and one of them was even his own grandson, he would not have lost his composure like this.

Uchiha Hosai suppressed his anger and said in a deep voice, « Go there now and investigate clearly. Do not reveal your tracks, then bring the three of them back to explain the situation to me. Why are they dawdling when dealing with a Chunin ! »

Even now, he had not considered that Senju Matsuaki could have killed the three Uchiha Ninjas he had dispatched.

In his view, even his own grandson, Uchiha Setsuna, could likely have killed Senju Matsuaki single-handedly.

If the operation didn’t require secrecy to ensure it was foolproof, he wouldn’t have dispatched his two trusted Jonin aides.

Two Jonin and one Chunin assassinating a Ninja with the strength of a Chunin was truly using a sledgehammer to crack a nut !

« Yes ! ! »

The Ninja kneeling on one knee inwardly complained ; just a moment ago, he was scolded for not investigating, and now he was being told not to reveal his tracks while investigating.

If it were really that simple, why don’t you go yourself ?

However, the authority Uchiha Hosai had built up over the years made him afraid to retort, and after acknowledging the order, he vanished from the spot using the Body Flicker Technique.

Uchiha Hosai turned around with a gloomy expression and stepped into the room, his crimson eyes appearing cold and sinister under the candlelight.

...

After cleaning up the scene, Senju Matsuaki did not return along the main road leading to Konoha. Instead, he found another direction to leave the forest first, then hurried back to Konoha via a small path.

After confirming Senju Matsuaki’s identity, the Konoha guards assumed he was an examinee who had been delayed outside and let him pass without asking too many questions.

After returning to Konoha and walking a few steps, he stepped into the brightly lit and decorated food street.

The tempting aroma of various delicacies combined with the colorful lights created a prosperous and bustling scene.

Seeing this, Senju Matsuaki’s brow smoothed slightly, and the tension that had remained in his heart after leaving the battle gradually dissipated.

The experience just now could be described as thrilling.

He had also carried out many missions ; as a Ninja, he had long since adapted to killing.

However, the situation just now was different.

The three enemies he faced were all Ninjas of the Uchiha Clan—two Jonin and one Chunin—which was almost the strongest lineup he had encountered so far.

At the same time, these three were nominally Konoha Ninjas. Even if his killing of them in retaliation was self-defense, if discovered by fellow villagers, it would ultimately be impossible to explain clearly.

Fortunately, Senju Matsuaki had cleared the scene after the quick battle, so even if the Uchiha Clan came to the scene and dug three feet deep, they would likely not be able to find the remains of Uchiha Setsuna and the others.

Even if the pulverized flesh and tissue of the three were found, there was no need to worry.

The only person in Konoha who could make the dead speak was Senju Tobirama, one of his own.

The situation just now was indeed a bit perilous, but it was all within Senju Matsuaki’s control.

The reason he pretended to fall into the trap after seeing through the Uchiha’s assassination plan was not blind confidence, but because he truly possessed a trump card to fall back on.

Just as one of the Uchiha Jonin had said, a large part of the power of Senju Matsuaki’s Ninjutsu relied on piling up a massive amount of Chakra, achieving the effect of brute force overcoming technique.

It was the same for Wood Release.

When it came to the attainments in Wood Release, Senju Matsuaki was still a long distance away from Senju Hashirama.

From the time he received Senju Hashirama’s Wood Release Ninjutsu scroll until now, he had only mastered a few of the commonly used Ninjutsu, which was far from being proficient.

But even so, Wood Release was still Wood Release.

Wood Release combined with Asura Chakra—the bricks flying under this immense, surging power were enough to kill Uchiha Ninjas whose strength was only in the mid-tier of Jonin !

Accompanying the danger was quite a rich harvest of spoils.

First of all, in a storage scroll inside Senju Matsuaki’s Ninja tool pouch, there were two pairs of Sharingan that had just been removed.

As for how to handle these two pairs of Sharingan, Senju Matsuaki was not quite sure.

Direct transplantation was definitely impossible, unless the Senju Clan really wanted to have a falling out with the Uchiha Clan.

Returning them to the Uchiha was also impossible ; that would be equivalent to turning himself in and saying that Ninjas from your clan were killed by me.

Keeping them in the scroll forever or disposing of them made him feel it was a waste.

After thinking about it, perhaps handing the Sharingan over to their Senju Clan’s number one scientist, Senju Tobirama, would be the best use of them.

[Exchangeable items detected, points exchange window is now open !]

The prompt sound suddenly rang out and then fell silent, leaving Senju Matsuaki, who was strolling on the bustling street, somewhat stunned.

Is it what I think it means ?

Holding feelings of doubt and anticipation, he opened the Golden Finger platform that was visible only to him.

After a brief browse, he discovered where the change was.

He saw that a new option had appeared in the upper left corner of the platform interface. After opening it, what caught his eye was a window with simple content.

On the left was « Submit, » and on the right was a question mark.

Seeing this, Senju Matsuaki was also full of question marks.

It was inconvenient to investigate this new function of the Golden Finger on the street, so after buying some food at the food street, he walked quickly through the crowd and returned home.

After closing and locking the mansion gate tightly, he leaned on a wooden chair inside the house, his mind immersed in the Golden Finger.

« It just said exchangeable items were detected, and there is this 'Submit' section. Could it mean... »

After hesitating for a moment, Senju Matsuaki still took out the storage scroll from his Ninja tool pouch and extracted the four fresh Sharingan that had just been removed.

The pupil power contained in the Three-Tomoe Sharingan had almost reached the limit that an ordinary Uchiha Ninja could achieve.
