# Chapitre 147 — Fierce Battle at the Camp!

« Let’s go, Uncle ! »

Senju Matsuaki shifted his gaze from Uchiha Hikari and landed it on the middle-aged man of the Senju Clan.

By now, the rest of the Senju Clan members in the dungeon had also finished resting.

Besides, there was no more time left for them to rest.

The dungeon door was gently pushed open by Senju Matsuaki.

The morning sunlight spilled onto the stone steps at the dungeon exit, illuminating the outline of the Uchiha Clan camp outside.

The commotion at the dungeon could not possibly be hidden from the Uchiha guarding the camp.

The patrolling Uchiha Ninja heard the noise and immediately looked over here vigilantly.

« Someone is breaking out of prison ! »

The roar instantly caused the entire camp to erupt into chaos.

Senju Matsuaki’s eyes narrowed, and he was the first to rush out.

Behind him, the middle-aged man and dozens of rescued Senju Clan members followed closely.

Uchiha Hikari hesitated for a moment, but ultimately followed at the very back of the group, her eyes flickering with complex emotions.

« Don’t get bogged down in fighting ! Break through to the east ! »

The middle-aged man shouted, pointing to the weak point of the camp.

After being captured, he hadn’t just sat idly by ; he had long since memorized the general layout of the camp.

However, the reaction speed of the Uchiha Clan far exceeded expectations.

Almost as soon as his voice fell, three figures sped over from different directions, blocking their path.

The leader was a thin Uchiha Jonin with a headband tied around his forehead ; the Three-Tomoe Sharingan in his eyes rotated slowly, and a sneer hung on the corner of his mouth.

« You stray dogs of the Senju Clan, you old, weak, sick, and disabled people—do you really think you can escape ? »

« If you had honestly stayed in the dungeon, you might have had a chance to live, but as for now... »

Before he finished speaking, he quickly formed hand Seals, and a huge fireball spewed from his mouth.

« Fire Release : Great Fireball Technique ! »

The scorching flames were like a giant beast opening its maw, swallowing toward Senju Matsuaki and the others.

The air was distorted by the high temperature, and the weeds on the ground instantly turned to ash.

Senju Matsuaki’s eyes narrowed, he pressed his palms together, and the massive Chakra within his body burst forth.

« Earth Release : Earth-Style Wall ! »

The thick ground suddenly bulged, forming a sturdy earth wall that blocked in front of everyone.

The Great Fireball slammed into the earth wall, erupting with a violent roar ; gravel flew, and flames scattered everywhere.

« Don’t let them run ! »

The thin Jonin shouted, and the two Uchiha Chunin behind him attacked simultaneously.

One of them threw several shuriken, which drew strange arcs in mid-air, blocking Senju Matsuaki’s path of retreat.

The other approached rapidly, a Kunai flickering with cold light in his hand, stabbing straight at Senju Matsuaki’s throat.

Senju Matsuaki dodged the shuriken, while simultaneously gathering Chakra and the power of the Tremor-Tremor Fruit in his right hand, punching toward the incoming Kunai.

With a muffled boom, the Kunai in the Uchiha Ninja’s hand shattered, and the person himself was knocked flying by the powerful impact, crashing heavily into the wooden house behind him, sending wood chips flying.

« This kid isn’t right ! »

The thin Jonin frowned deeply, a flash of gravity in his eyes. « His strength... »

« Don’t worry about that ; just stop them ! »

The other Uchiha Ninja shouted, « There aren’t many among them with combat capability, and they still have to be distracted by protecting the wounded ! »

Senju Matsuaki’s heart sank.

Indeed, the situation was as dire as this Uchiha Ninja had described.

Having been imprisoned in the dungeon for so long, although their Chakra had recovered after the Seals were lifted, recovering from their injuries would take time.

What they lacked most was precisely time.

« Uncle, take the clansmen and go first ! »

Senju Matsuaki shouted to the middle-aged man behind him, « I’ll hold them off ! »

« Kid, you alone... »

« There’s no time for nonsense ! »

Senju Matsuaki interrupted him, his figure flashing as he took the initiative to rush toward the three Uchiha Ninjas.

Among all the Senju Clan members present, only he and the middle-aged man could be considered significant combat strength.

Obviously, the middle-aged man was more suitable than him to lead these Senju Clan members to evacuate.

« ...Go ! ! »

The middle-aged man of the Senju Clan gritted his teeth and led the clansmen to leave first.

If he were alone, he would definitely stay to fight to the death.

But there were women, children, and disabled clansmen in the group now...

Senju Matsuaki’s speed was astonishing ; his feet cracked the ground as he stepped, and he slammed into the enemy formation like a cannonball.

He swung his right fist, carrying the Vibrational Power, and slammed it forward !

« Damn it ! »

The thin Jonin gritted his teeth, the Three-Tomoe Sharingan in his eyes spinning rapidly, attempting to capture Senju Matsuaki’s movements.

However, Senju Matsuaki’s Taijutsu relied more on strength than technique.

Every punch contained the Vibrational Power, which was by no means something that could be easily seen through by the Sharingan like ordinary Taijutsu !

In just a few breaths, the two Uchiha Chunin were disabled, and the thin Jonin was also forced back by Senju Matsuaki.

« Is this guy... really a brat around ten years old ? »

The thin Jonin clutched his chest, a trace of blood spilling from the corner of his mouth.

However, his expression was not dejected ; instead, a cruel smile gradually curled at the corner of his mouth.

It was almost time.

In the distance, footsteps were suddenly heard.

A burly Uchiha Ninja hurried over and blocked Senju Matsuaki’s path ; the Three-Tomoe Sharingan in his eyes exuded an oppressive aura.

« Senju Clan brat, you have quite some nerve. »

The burly Jonin spoke coldly, « Since you’ve broken out of prison, don’t even think about leaving alive. »

« Why waste words with him ? Just take him down. »

The thin Jonin from before said in a detached tone.

Senju Matsuaki looked around, his heart tightening.

The situation was not good.

Just now, he could handle one Jonin and several Chunin with ease.

But with another Jonin joining, the pressure on him was undoubtedly doubled.

He took a deep breath, and the Chakra within his body began to surge wildly.

Pressure was pressure, but it was not yet a desperate situation.

The power of the Tremor-Tremor Fruit was merely one of his trump cards.

Wood Release was also the key to his strength ranking among Jonin !

« Fire Style : Phoenix Sage Fire Jutsu ! »

The burly Jonin made the first move, his hands quickly forming Seals.

He spat out dozens of small fireballs, like petals dancing in the sky, attacking Senju Matsuaki from all directions.

Senju Matsuaki was about to cast Earth Release for defense, but he saw the thin Jonin also forming Seals at the same time.

« Fire Release : Great Fireball Technique ! »

A huge fireball followed closely, intertwining with the small flames of the Phoenix Sage Fire.

The Phoenix Sage Fire bypassed the frontal defense line, flanking from both sides, while the Great Fireball came crushing in from the front ; the two worked together seamlessly.

Senju Matsuaki’s expression remained unchanged, his hands flying through Seals.

« Water Release : Water Formation Wall ! »

Surging water gushed up from the ground, turning into an arc-shaped water curtain that protected him behind it.

The Great Fireball slammed into the water curtain head-on, kicking up a sky full of white steam ; the flames quickly dissipated under the suppression of the water.

Meanwhile, Senju Matsuaki tapped his toes lightly on the ground, his figure sliding half a step to the left, and his hands formed Seals.

« Water Release : Water Fang Bullet ! ! »

On the Water Formation Wall, the water flow condensed into sharp blades, precisely extinguishing the remaining fireballs of the Fire Style : Phoenix Sage Fire Jutsu !

A sharp look flashed in Senju Matsuaki’s eyes, and he was about to use Wood Release to end this battle.

But just at this moment, a slender figure suddenly walked out from a shadow behind him.

Uchiha Hikari.

Her steps were very light, as if treading on cotton, but those eyes were startlingly bright.

Her originally dim black eyes had now turned scarlet.

The dart pattern that appeared in her eyes rotated slowly within her pupils, exuding a heart-palpitating aura.

« Uchiha Mumei ? »

The thin Jonin was slightly stunned, and then a look of pleasant surprise appeared on his face.

« Mumei ! Quickly use your eyes to kill this Senju Clan brat ! »
