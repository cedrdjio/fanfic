# Chapitre 1 — Senju Matsumi

It was the seventh year of Konoha, inside the Ninja Academy.

It was evening, and the rays of the setting sun spilled through the window onto the podium, dazzling the children who were listening intently.

In a row near the back of the classroom, a little boy was staring fixedly at the blackboard.

He wore gray casual clothes, had fair skin, short silver hair, and rare red eyes that looked sharp and spirited.

Under the sunlight from the window, the boy seemed to be shrouded in a faint glow.

The Senju Clan Ninja on the podium, teaching tirelessly, couldn’t help but feel a moment of trance when glancing towards the boy, as if seeing another figure.

The boy’s name was Senju Matsumi, six years old this year, and he was from the same clan as the teacher on the podium—both were descendants of the Senju Clan in Konoha Village.

Unlike others in this Ninja World, Senju Matsumi’s soul came from another world. He had died in an accident in his previous life, and when he opened his eyes again, he had been born into the Senju Clan.

He was born just as Konoha was being established ; internal strife had just been quelled, but external threats remained unabated.

Because of this, shortly after Senju Matsumi was born, his parents sacrificed themselves while on a mission.

Fortunately, the Senju Clan had always been united, and since Senju Matsumi’s father was a cousin of Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama, he received quite a bit of care while growing up.

As a member of the Senju Clan, especially as a close relative of the God of Shinobi, Senju Matsumi’s talent as a Ninja went without saying, but that wasn’t the only reason he had the confidence to establish himself in the Ninja World.

In the world of Naruto, the later stages became increasingly exaggerated with cheats.

The Three-Tomoe Sharingan, which was full of prestige in the early stages, was nothing compared to the Mangekyo Sharingan.

And once the Rinnegan and Tenseigan appeared, the Mangekyo Sharingan seemed to fall short.

Compared to the reincarnations of Ashura and Indra’s Chakra, as well as the Ōtsutsuki Clan like the Six Paths Sage and Ōtsutsuki Kaguya, the bloodline of the Senju Clan really didn’t amount to much.

What really gave Senju Matsumi the confidence to stand in this world, where various secret techniques and Bloodline Limits emerged one after another, was the Golden Finger he had just awakened not long ago.

More accurately, it was « Golden Fingers. »

His Golden Finger was not a single system, item, or some old grandfather accompanying him to help him become stronger, but a platform.

This platform was named the « Golden Finger Requisition and Expropriation Platform, » and its content included the Golden Fingers of the Sons of Luck across the heavens and myriad worlds.

The method of use was also very simple : by consuming a certain amount of points, one could requisition or expropriate Golden Fingers from other planes on this platform.

Taking the same Golden Finger as an example, requisitioning was temporary and required fewer points, while expropriation meant permanently acquiring ownership of the Golden Finger, which naturally required consuming more points.

As for the source of the points, in addition to the points obtained from daily check-ins, daily training and missions could also yield varying amounts of points.

It had to be said that the points obtained from daily check-ins and training were as meager as mosquito meat, and since he was still attending the Ninja Academy, there were few opportunities to trigger missions.

However, little by little, after more than a month of effort, the points Senju Matsumi had accumulated were about to break the 100 mark ; the future was promising.

As school was nearing its end, the teacher on the podium stopped lecturing, and the attention of the students below became scattered.

Thump, thump !

The Senju Clan teacher reached out his right hand and gently tapped the podium, attempting to gather the students' attention once again.

After a pair of eyes looked back at him, the teacher cleared his throat.

« Everyone, pay attention. There is an important announcement. »

Sweeping his gaze over the young faces below, the teacher continued : « At the end of this month every year, it is time for the graduating class to conduct their graduation exams. According to custom, students in non-graduating years may also apply to take the exam and graduate early. »

After his voice fell, the students in the classroom showed different reactions.

Students born into Ninja clans were usually not surprised by this news, and their expressions could hardly hide their excitement and anticipation.

In contrast, some students from civilian backgrounds were stunned at first, and after reacting, they felt some hesitation and fear.

At this time, Konoha had only been established for a few years, and the Ninja Academy system was not yet perfect.

Coupled with the gaps in talent and foundation between Ninja clans and civilians, this made the model of applying for early graduation exams a necessity.

However, in relatively peaceful times, cases of early graduation to become a Genin were rare, whereas in times of war, there was a greater need to supplement the Village’s Ninja forces with fresh blood.

Taking in the students' reactions, the teacher sighed inwardly.

After becoming a Ninja, one had to truly face blood and cruelty ; having experienced the two periods before and after the establishment of Konoha, he knew this well.

Fortunately, the current Konoha was not engaged in foreign wars and was not lacking Genin, so the standards for the graduation exam were stricter ; even students born into Ninja clans might not necessarily pass the exam.

For these students, it was hard to know whether the tightening of the graduation exam standards was a good thing or a bad thing.

« Everyone, think carefully and ask for the opinions of your elders at home. Come to me to sign up before class tomorrow. Class dismissed. »

After speaking, the Senju Clan teacher turned and walked out of the classroom, and the previously quiet classroom became noisy as everyone chattered about this major news.

At his seat, Senju Matsumi yawned, stood up, and prepared to leave the classroom to go home.

As for whether to graduate early, he had no hesitation or worry ; he would just apply directly.

Although his Golden Finger hadn’t been open for long and hadn’t truly helped him yet, he hadn’t been doing nothing before this.

In fact, whether it was Senju Matsumi or other members of the Uchiha, Hyuga, and other families in this classroom, they had basically acquired the qualities of a Genin.

However, just as Matsumi was about to leave the classroom, he noticed a girl next to him with a worried and conflicted expression.

After thinking for a moment, he reached out his hand and waved it in front of the girl.

« Tsukuyomi, do you have any thoughts about applying for early graduation ? »

The boy’s action caused the distracted girl to snap back to reality. Her flawless white eyes looked over, and a faint blush, whether from nervousness or something else, appeared on her face.

« Matsumi-kun, I... »

The girl’s name was Hyuga Tsukuyomi ; she was a member of the Hyuga Clan, and her status was quite prominent—she was the youngest sister of the current Hyuga Clan head.

The Hyuga Clan had been very supportive of Konoha’s development in recent years and had a relatively close relationship with the Senju Clan.

Additionally, since the two were desk-mates, their relationship had always been quite friendly.

However, perhaps due to her introverted personality, Hyuga Tsukuyomi often appeared somewhat nervous and restrained when speaking with Senju Matsumi.

However, just at this moment, a discordant voice suddenly came from the side, and the surroundings instantly went quiet.

« If you don’t even dare to participate in the early assessment, you might as well pack up and leave early ; don’t stay here and make a fool of yourself ! »
