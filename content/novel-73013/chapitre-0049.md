# Chapitre 49 — Hashirama's Ninjutsu Scroll

« Aunt Mito. »

Matsumi arrived at Hashirama’s house and found only Uzumaki Mito leisurely tending to the flowers and plants in the courtyard.

Preparations for Senju Kazuma’s wedding were mostly complete, but due to the major event of the Joint Chunin Exams, the wedding had been postponed until after the new year.

Recently, official business had been busy, and neither Senju Hashirama nor Senju Tobirama could find the time ; even Senju Kazuma seemed to be away on some mission.

« Little Matsumi, are you here to find your Uncle Hashirama ? »

Uzumaki Mito smiled and stopped what she was doing ; a dignified smile rested on her young face, which looked no older than thirty.

In the original timeline, the first and second halves of her life could be described as two extremes.

Being able to marry the head of the Senju Clan, Senju Hashirama, naturally meant her status within the Uzumaki Clan was noble, as she was the daughter of the Uzumaki Clan head, Uzumaki Ashina.

As the daughter of the Uzumaki Clan head, she was able to grow up securely during the cruel Sengoku period.

After reaching adulthood, although she married someone she wasn’t familiar with, she discovered upon living together that he had a good character. He was a top hero in the Ninja World and would later become the God of Shinobi.

Viewed this way, the first half of her life was very fulfilling and happy.

However, as she approached middle age, first her husband, Senju Hashirama, passed away, and as the Nine-Tails Jinchuriki, she was left to remain in this world alone.

Not many years later, Uzumaki Mito’s hometown, Uzushiogakure, was tragically destroyed, and her relatives were wiped out.

All the beginnings of happiness from the first half of her life were cruelly taken away in the second half.

« Aunt, I’ve come to get Uncle Hashirama’s Ninjutsu scroll, » Matsumi said with a smile.

Ever since he graduated from the Ninja Academy, the room where Senju Hashirama kept his own Ninjutsu scrolls had been open to him, allowing him to take what he needed.

After all, Matsumi’s practice in Ninjutsu had long since reached the introductory level, and there was no need to pick out and teach him suitable Ninjutsu one by one as they had earlier.

Moreover, the Ninjutsu scrolls in Hashirama’s collection inherently contained some Ninjutsu and techniques passed down within the Senju Clan. These contents were already circulated within the Senju Clan, and every clansman could learn them.

« Then go ahead. Remember to be careful not to take your Second Uncle’s Ninjutsu by mistake ; some of those techniques are still too dangerous for you. »

Uzumaki Mito nodded with a smile, then, as if thinking of something, she urged him with a serious expression.

Although she didn’t know exactly which techniques they were, she did have some understanding of the danger level of certain Ninjutsu belonging to her brother-in-law.

It was undeniable that Senju Tobirama was a genius in Ninjutsu research, but some of his Ninjutsu were simply too dangerous—either dangerous to himself or to others.

Take the Flying Thunder God Technique, for example ; without talent in space-time, one might end up teleporting their upper body away while the lower body remained in place.

And the Edo Tensei was evil and dangerous to others ; sacrificing living people to summon the dead did not align at all with Konoha’s consistently upright image.

However, Uzumaki Mito was undoubtedly flustered by concern ; Senju Tobirama was not such a careless person. Forbidden techniques like the Edo Tensei would never be placed directly alongside other ordinary Ninjutsu.

« I understand, Aunt. »

After Matsumi acknowledged this, he hurried into a room in Uncle Hashirama’s house. After searching in front of the bookshelf for a while, he finally found a dusty bundle of scrolls.

After all, until yesterday, Senju Hashirama was the only Wood Release Ninja in the entire Senju Clan.

Even though Hashirama had recorded the Wood Release Ninjutsu he developed on scrolls, for the past ten years or so, no one had needed them.

After opening the scroll, he roughly browsed through the contents.

Besides some basic ones like Wood Clone, Wood Replacement, Wood Locking Wall, and Deep Forest Emergence, there were higher-level ones like Wood Spear and Wood Wall.

As the scroll unrolled, the difficulty and power of the Ninjutsu escalated sharply.

Things like Deep Forest Emergence, Deep Forest Bloom, and even Sage Art Wood Release : True Several Thousand Hands...

The content in this scroll was clearly not written in a single day, but recorded successively as Senju Hashirama explored and developed Wood Release Ninjutsu over the years.

Looking at the Ninjutsu recorded in the scroll, Matsumi seemed to see a young boy who had just awakened Wood Release, growing into the God of Shinobi who made the Ninja World tremble.

After closing the scroll, Matsumi took it and left the room, locking the door behind him.

In the courtyard, Uzumaki Mito watched Matsumi come out with a scroll ; she felt the scroll looked somewhat familiar but didn’t think much of it.

« Remember to come over for dinner tonight. »

« I know, Aunt Mito ! »

Noon.

The scorching sun baked the capital of the Land of Fire, causing pedestrians to frown.

Senju Kazuma wore a black tight-fitting suit and a bamboo hat, walking through the bustling streets.

Shops lined both sides of the Land of Fire’s capital, and the bustling market was the most prosperous in the entire Land of Fire.

However, Senju Kazuma did not stop there, but headed straight toward the Daimyo’s mansion.

Soon, the solemn Daimyo’s mansion appeared before his eyes. Two samurai, clad in armor and equipped with katanas, stood on the left and right at the entrance.

« Halt ! No unauthorized persons allowed inside the Daimyo’s mansion ! »

Seeing Senju Kazuma walking straight toward them, one of the samurai subconsciously drew his katana halfway, the cold light gleaming.

The other samurai was just about to step forward to question him as well, but after seeing the face under the bamboo hat, he shuddered and hurriedly pressed down on his companion’s hand that was drawing the sword.

« Idiot, this is Lord Kazuma, the Daimyo’s son-in-law ! »

« Uh, the Daimyo’s... »

« Sincere apologies, Lord Kazuma. This kid is new, » the samurai said with a sheepish smile.

« It’s fine. I’m going in, then ? »

Kazuma’s personality was basically like Hashirama’s—easygoing and unpretentious—and he didn’t take what just happened to heart at all.

« Please, go ahead. » The samurai stepped aside.

After entering the Daimyo’s mansion, Senju Kazuma asked for directions along the way and soon found where the Daimyo was.

In a pavilion by the lake, a middle-aged man with fair skin and a narrow face was playing Shogi with another man who looked restrained.

Seeing Senju Kazuma arrive, the Daimyo of the Land of Fire was somewhat surprised and waved his hand to dismiss the retainer who was playing Shogi with him.

« Kazuma, are you here to see me about something, instead of finding Mao ? » the Daimyo asked curiously.

Mao was his daughter and also Senju Kazuma’s fiancée.

« Father sent me to invite you to attend the Joint Chunin Exams, which Konoha is holding together with the other four great Ninja villages. Additionally, there are some other matters I need to discuss with you, » Senju Kazuma said respectfully.

This was the reason he had come to the Land of Fire’s capital this time : to extend invitations to the nobles of the Land of Fire to come to Konoha as spectators for the Chunin Exams.

It wasn’t just the Land of Fire ; Konoha had also sent invitations to dignitaries and wealthy people from other countries. Especially for the Daimyos of each country, Konoha had personally sent people to invite them.

« Oh, I saw him mention it in a letter from the Hokage before. I didn’t expect it to be starting so soon. » The Daimyo of the Land of Fire raised his brows, slightly surprised.

« This is the first time I’ve seen such an interesting event. I will go when the time comes. »
