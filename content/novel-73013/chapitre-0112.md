# Chapitre 112 — The Second Round of the Chunin Exams Begins!

Senju Matsuaki had a good night’s sleep, but some people could not sleep at all.

Within the Uchiha Clan, inside the residence of Elder Uchiha Hosai.

Elder Uchiha Hosai sat upright on a wooden chair in the main hall. His eyes were bloodshot, and his right hand gripped the edge of a side table tightly, veins bulging on the back of his hand.

There were two others in the hall besides him.

One was his grandson, Uchiha Kai. Knowing what was going to happen tonight, he naturally couldn’t sleep peacefully as usual.

Elder Uchiha Hosai had never kept him in the dark when discussing the assassination of Senju Matsuaki with Uchiha Setsuna.

If not for his lack of strength and fear of accidents, he would have even considered joining the operation.

Having heard his grandfather’s angry voice from his room, he didn’t know the situation and rushed to the main hall.

The other person in the hall was a Uchiha Jonin loyal to Elder Uchiha Hosai ; he was one of his trusted confidants.

In tonight’s operation to assassinate Senju Matsuaki, he was responsible for observing the movements of other Ninja outside the forest and was prepared to support Uchiha Setsuna and the other two at any time.

He had returned empty-handed earlier without meeting them, and after being scolded by Elder Uchiha Hosai, he went to investigate the scene again.

After a few more hours, he returned to Elder Uchiha Hosai’s home, his face pale.

He stood slightly hunched before the seated Elder Uchiha Hosai, cautiously relaying what he had discovered, cold sweat dripping from his forehead, neck, and back.

« Elder, I investigated the forest where Uchiha Setsuna and the others were supposed to strike. I found signs of a large-scale Earth Release Jutsu being used near the pre-planned ambush site, seemingly to cover up certain original traces. »

« Some trees remaining at the scene have extensive burn marks, which should have been caused by Uchiha Setsuna and the others' Fire Release, corresponding to the light I observed earlier. »

« Additionally, I noticed that the soil on the ground nearby was abnormally damp ; it seems someone had used a Water Release Jutsu. »

« Uchiha Setsuna and his team are not skilled in Earth Release or Water Release. Therefore, the one who used these Ninjutsu was either the target himself or... »

« Enough ! I don’t want to hear about who used what Ninjutsu ; I only want to know the result ! »

Elder Uchiha Hosai, his face already quite grim, interrupted his subordinate’s description, his brows furrowed, his eyes full of impatience.

The subordinate looked up at Elder Uchiha Hosai, and when his gaze met those scarlet eyes, he couldn’t help but shiver.

« Yes... »

After quickly responding, cold sweat dripped from his forehead onto the floor, and he hesitantly voiced the conclusion he had reached.

« Looking at the results... Senju Matsuaki returned to the Village unharmed, while Uchiha Setsuna and the other two are missing. I fear they have been killed ! »

« Elder, I am deeply sorry ! ! »

Having said this, the subordinate knelt on one knee, gritting his teeth and not daring to look directly at Elder Uchiha Hosai.

« What ! ! ? »

Elder Uchiha Hosai stood up abruptly, his Three-Tomoe Sharingan erupting with terrifying power. The intense Killing Intent made the weak Uchiha Kai tremble.

« Are you saying that Uchiha Setsuna and the other three—two Jonin and one Chunin—were killed by a brat who isn’t even a Chunin ! ! ? »

« No, it is possible that someone else did it. Judging from the traces at the scene, the person who used Earth Release and Water Release must have at least the strength of a Jonin ! »

« Above Jonin level, could it be... »

Elder Uchiha Hosai felt dizzy and collapsed back into the wooden chair, his withered palms covering most of his face.

It wasn’t that he couldn’t see the facts, but rather that he truly didn’t want to believe the truth.

Although he was usually very strict with his grandsons and would stop at nothing to achieve his goals, he held his Family in an extremely important place in his heart.

The hatred he felt for the Senju Clan in his heart now was largely because his relatives had been killed during the Sengoku era !

The emotions of the Uchiha Clan were extremely intense—both love and hate.

Even after decades, Elder Uchiha Hosai still couldn’t forget the way his parents and children looked before they died.

Every time he woke up from a nightmare filled with blood and corpses, his hatred for the Senju Clan deepened.

Now, because of his decision, he might have lost his grandson again.

How could he explain this to his deceased son ! ! !

« Where are... the bodies of Uchiha Setsuna and the others ? »

Elder Uchiha Hosai’s voice was no longer explosive as before, but instead filled with deep exhaustion.

« Elder, I didn’t find them at the scene. Perhaps they were collected by other Konoha Ninja who arrived first, or it is possible that... »

« Were there no remains left ! ? »

Elder Uchiha Hosai’s palm covering his face trembled slightly, and two tear tracks fell from the shadows of his cheeks.

« You may leave. Continue to closely monitor the subsequent impact of this matter. »

« Yes. »

The subordinate nodded, stood up, felt his legs go weak, and quickly left the hall with unsteady steps.

« Grandfather, has... my brother been killed ? »

Uchiha Kai walked quickly to Elder Uchiha Hosai’s side, looking up at him in disbelief.

Two trails of tears fell from his eyes. His eyes, filled with sorrow, had unknowingly turned scarlet, and a single black tomoe was rotating around the pupil in each eye.

Seeing that his other grandson had awakened his eyes due to the shock of this event, Elder Uchiha Hosai felt a mix of grief and joy.

In the dim hall, two pairs of scarlet eyes met, and the hatred seemed to solidify !

« Senju Tobirama, Senju Matsuaki... one day I will make sure you end up with no remains left as well ! ! ! »

The next morning, the Chunin Exams continued.

Spectators from all directions arrived one after another, some carrying steaming hot breakfast, sitting in the spectator stands and eating heartily.

« Hello, dear spectators. Yesterday, we concluded the first round of the third stage of the Chunin Exams. Today, we will officially begin the second round of selection exams ! »

Having been the host for a day, Senju Tobirama got straight into the zone.

At first, when Senju Hashirama arranged for him to serve as the chief Proctor and host, and also told him to be responsible for inserting advertisements, he was resistant.

It wasn’t that he resisted being a Proctor, but rather he resisted reading the advertisement scripts.

However, after realizing how much benefit proper promotion could bring to various industries in Konoha, his resistance didn’t seem so important anymore.

Senju Tobirama had always been a pragmatic person.

When Senju Hashirama first told him about bringing the Uchiha, Hyuga, Sarutobi, and other families together to build a Village, Senju Tobirama’s first reaction was also resistance.

But when Konoha was truly built, he ended up being a builder active on the front lines.

« In yesterday’s first round of selection, twenty-eight candidates stood out. Today, they will undergo even more brutal eliminations. »

« Today, the second, third, and fourth rounds of selection will be held, ultimately selecting four people to participate in tomorrow’s semi-finals and finals. »

« Next, as usual, the Daimyo and Kage of each nation will draw lots to determine the candidates for the assessment. Dear spectators, if you feel hungry or thirsty, you may orderly proceed to the shops on the first floor of the spectator stands to purchase snacks and drinks ! »
