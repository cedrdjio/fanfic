# Chapitre 36 — Seen Through!

Thinking back to what happened at the Ninja Academy this afternoon, Matsumi could not help but inwardly curse the despicableness of the Hidden Cloud Village.

Other Ninja Villages had those who spied for secrets, those who stole Ninjutsu and secret treasures, and those who stole Bloodline Limits.

But to dare to scheme against Konoha while Senju Hashirama was in command—one had to admit that these barbarians from Kumogakure were truly brave.

However, Senju Matsuaki knew clearly in his heart that Kumogakure did not have the guts to actually kidnap a Ninja with a Bloodline Limit from Konoha.

After all, secrets cannot be kept forever.

If the matter were exposed, every major Ninja clan in Konoha would push with all their might to launch a war against Kumogakure.

Konoha, with Senju Hashirama present, was an existence that Kumogakure could not contend with !

Therefore, the big guy from Kumogakure lingering near the Ninja Academy likely only aimed to understand Konoha’s educational model and the cultivation standards of the new generation.

Regarding the former, Konoha was also far ahead in the construction of the Ninja Academy.

And Kumogakure, which valued military force, was precisely lacking in the systematic training of Ninjas.

Regarding the latter, if a war were to break out between Ninja Villages in the future, Konoha’s new generation would be the main force of the war.

Kumogakure naturally wanted to scout intelligence on these fledglings in advance.

Of course, at least until it was confirmed that Senju Hashirama had passed away, this hypothetical war could not break out.

However, thinking of this, Matsumi could not help but feel a bit emotional.

Perhaps for Uchiha Kai, being kidnapped by the burly Kumogakure Ninjas would not necessarily be a bad thing.

A Bloodline Limit Ninja who was depressed and unappreciated in Konoha being taken away by a Kumogakure that valued talent, and receiving vigorous cultivation.

Then having their strength advance by leaps and bounds, rising all the way to become Raikage, and facing those who once looked down on them in Konoha as the Raikage, slapping their faces in pretense.

This trope, Matsumi had heard of many times in his past life.

And Uchiha Kai’s status and situation in Konoha precisely fit the « protagonist » of this trope.

Therefore, Matsumi could not help but sigh now ; fortunately, he had stopped the Kumogakure Ninja’s kidnapping plot in time.

He had almost let Uchiha Kai, this naturally evil Uchiha brat, live a good life.

However, the above was all just a power fantasy trope.

Not to mention whether Kumogakure had the guts to kidnap a member of a Bloodline Limit family from Konoha.

Even if they were truly kidnapped, the best outcome would be nothing more than becoming a breeding pig to propagate the Bloodline Limit.

Even, in order to prevent the matter from being exposed and thus angering Senju Hashirama, Kumogakure would completely keep Uchiha Kai and his descendants under house arrest.

As long as Senju Hashirama was not dead, these Uchiha born in Kumogakure would never see the light of day again.

As for being kidnapped by Kumogakure, establishing a new Uchiha Clan in Kumogakure that was not ostracized and was respected by everyone, and in the future sweeping across the Ninja World with the power of the Uchiha Clan...

Just dream about it in your sleep.

« Tsukuyomi, you look a bit tired, why don’t you take a rest for a while ? »

At this moment, Matsumi noticed a slight fatigue on his partner’s face, so he spoke up to suggest.

After speaking, not waiting for the other party to refute, Matsumi continued.

« Rest assured, I’m watching here. Even if someone leaves suddenly, I can use a Shadow Clone to follow them. »

« Then I’ll leave it to you. If there’s a situation, you can call me at any time. »

Hearing this, on one hand, she trusted Matsumi’s reliability, and on the other hand, she was indeed mentally exhausted, so Tsukuyomi did not decline.

Today could be said to be the day she had used the Byakugan most frequently in these past few months ; to say she wasn’t exhausted would be a lie.

Choosing a wall covered by shadows, Tsukuyomi leaned against it slowly, her body seemingly merging with the darkness.

Under the cold moonlight, only Matsumi remained, standing in the shadows with his arms crossed, his body as immobile as a sculpture in the wind.

...

« Elder, Teacher, I’m back. »

Inside the inn, the Kumogakure youth opened the door and walked in ; two people were already waiting quietly inside.

The inn Konoha had arranged for the Kumogakure delegation was the best one in the Village.

Warm-toned wooden floors, walls, and furniture with exquisitely carved patterns—these were things they had never seen in Kumogakure.

However, at this moment, the few people in the room had no mind to enjoy it ; they only looked towards the door with complex expressions.

Expectation, worry...

« Damn it, Konoha’s protection of the Ninja Academy is too thorough ; there’s simply no way to collect the needed intelligence... »

Facing the expectant gazes of the two, the youth sighed and shook his head, found a chair to sit down, and slapped his thick thigh fiercely.

He, who had pretended to pass by in order to collect intelligence, was stopped at the entrance of the Ninja Academy.

« Don’t be anxious, Dou. Tell us about today’s situation. »

The Kumogakure Elder’s face also held a look of disappointment, but he did not speak any words of blame, only saying gently.

Dou was the name of this Kumogakure youth.

In the original timeline, he would be the future Third Raikage, A.

However, « A » was not the real name of the Raikage, but a title passed down by every Raikage.

Before becoming Raikage, he also had his own name—Dou.

« Elder, it’s like this. » Organizing his thoughts, Dou began to recount today’s encounter.

« In the afternoon, I found an opportunity to sneak near Konoha’s Ninja Academy, following your previous instructions to scout Konoha’s educational model and intelligence on the new generation. »

« At the time, I was quite lucky ; I happened to encounter a Uchiha brat alone. »

Hearing this, the future Second Raikage was startled : « Uchiha ? »

« Hong, don’t be anxious, listen to Dou finish. » The Elder frowned, speaking with displeasure.

Just as the Third Raikage had his own name before becoming Raikage, the Second Raikage was the same before becoming Raikage.

The Kumogakure Elder was very senior, even being of the generation of the First Raikage’s uncle, so Hong did not dare to refute, instead nodding apologetically.

This Kumogakure Elder was mainly in charge of education in the Village, taking it as his responsibility to establish a perfect training model for Kumogakure Ninjas.

Having Dou collect intelligence on the Ninja Academy in Konoha was exactly at his instruction.

As for why they didn’t use spies, but instead adopted such a method to scout for intelligence...

Now was the early stage of the major nations establishing Ninja Villages ; planting spies would at least take a few years, wouldn’t it ?

Moreover, besides the high-level officials, the ones most familiar with Konoha’s educational model were the teachers at the Ninja Academy.

To ensure the safety of Konoha’s next generation, these teachers all had to undergo rigorous examinations ; it was impossible for spies to infiltrate them.

« At the time, I thought this would be a very good opportunity. If I used the Transformation Technique to turn into that Uchiha brat’s appearance, no one would be able to detect me immediately upon sneaking into the Ninja Academy. »

« With my speed, once I infiltrated the Ninja Academy where teachers were generally at the Chunin level, no one would be able to discover my tracks ! »

Dou was very confident in his speed.

Even without activating the Lightning Release Chakra Mode, his body had long been tempered to the limit for his age.

Generally speaking, Jonin-level combat power would not be wasted at the Ninja Academy ; those teachers were at most at the Chunin level.

For Chunin to discover him, a Jonin, unless they had a special Bloodline Limit, it would be as hard as ascending to heaven.

Once he succeeded in infiltrating and scouted out Konoha’s educational model, the plan to revitalize Kumogakure would take its first step.

Thinking of this, the breathing of the Kumogakure Elder and Hong couldn’t help but become a bit rapid.

« But I didn’t expect that at this moment, a silver-haired brat suddenly appeared, said something to the Uchiha brat, and the two ran away quickly. »

« The commotion created nearby was quite large, and I was afraid that infiltrating then would attract attention, so I temporarily shelved the plan. »

« I plan to wait until tomorrow to find another opportunity. By then, I will pay attention to the surrounding situation in advance and won’t fail like today again ! »

The story ended with a whimper like this.

The plan to revitalize Kumogakure also came to an abrupt end.

The Kumogakure Elder and Hong looked at each other, both seeing the look of disappointment in the other’s eyes.

However, as if thinking of something, the Kumogakure Elder frowned, stood up, and paced in the room, deep in thought.

« Wrong, something is wrong... »

A flash of inspiration seemed to pass through his mind ; the Kumogakure Elder suddenly widened his eyes, his tiger-like eyes shooting out like lightning, staring at Dou.

« Dou, do you still remember the appearance of that silver-haired brat ? Describe him to me in detail. »

« Elder, let me think... »

Under this sharp gaze, Dou felt somewhat restless, only feeling his scalp tingle.

Dou gently stroked his chin, which was already sprouting stubble, with his large palm, his brows tightly furrowed, recalling the scene from earlier in the afternoon.

« That brat had short silver hair, looked to be about seven or eight years old, and is likely still attending the Ninja Academy. »

« On his back, there was the crest of the Senju Clan embroidered ; he is a descendant of the Senju Clan. »

« Hiss, there is one point that is very strange ; he didn’t come out from the Ninja Academy, but came here from another direction. »

Speaking up to here, Dou felt as if he were about to grasp something, but that trace of inspiration was fleeting.

« Dou, it looks like you were followed. »

« And your actions should have already alerted the enemy ; next, Konoha will strengthen the protection of the Ninja Academy ! »

The Kumogakure Elder narrowed his eyes slightly, directly pointing out the truth.

« Previously, according to the intelligence sent back by spies, I roughly organized information on the next generation of several Ninja clans in Konoha... »

« That brat of the Senju Clan you saw is called Senju Matsuaki ; he has already been a Genin for several months. »

« So that’s how it is, what a damn brat ! »

Dou, whose mind had turned the corner, could not help but be furious, his thick fist smashing forcefully onto the tabletop beside him, causing the wooden table to split from the middle.
