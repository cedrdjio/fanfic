# Chapitre 106 — The Might of Wood Release!

Seeing this, the two Uchiha Jonin looked at him as if he were an idiot.

« Why don’t you go and kill him ? Is it that you don’t want to ? »

However, the two soon noticed the change in Uchiha Setsuna’s eyes and were instantly filled with wild joy.

From two-tomoe to three-tomoe, the Sharingan’s ocular power increases significantly.

With the Three-Tomoe Sharingan now supporting him, Uchiha Setsuna likely possessed the strength of a Jonin.

With three Jonin, they certainly had a chance of winning against the Wood Release of a Senju Clan brat.

Thinking of this, the two Uchiha Jonin felt at ease.

Their panic just now was merely due to the illustrious reputation of Wood Release.

But upon deeper thought, perhaps the power of Wood Release was only due to Senju Hashirama ; if it were a greenhorn, its power might be greatly diminished !

« Let’s go ! I don’t believe the Wood Release of such a brat can compare to Senju Hashirama’s ! ! » the older Jonin said in a deep voice.

This time, the three did not waste any more words and simultaneously formed hand Seals.

Especially Uchiha Setsuna, whose eyes had just evolved into the Three-Tomoe Sharingan ; he was ecstatic to discover the significant improvement in his vision and ocular power.

« Fire Release : Great Fire Annihilation ! ! ! »

Facing the surging sea of trees, the three Uchiha moved in unison to form hand Seals, with scorching heat gathering in their throats.

All three opened their mouths in unison, spitting out scorching flames that formed a raging sea of fire.

The scorching sea of fire resisted the countless thick branches, and for a moment, it actually slowed down the trees' assault.

Seeing that their combined Fire Release : Great Fire Annihilation had achieved some results, the three Uchiha felt their chances of winning were high.

Seeing this, Senju Matsuaki did not panic ; instead, he smiled confidently.

Fire can ignite wood—that is a principle even a child knows.

But if they truly thought they could suppress Wood Release with Fire Release, then Uchiha Madara, who excelled at Fire Release, wouldn’t have been suppressed and beaten by Senju Hashirama.

The effect of Deep Forest Emergence is to form an entire forest.

Within this forest, every tree was controlled by Senju Matsuaki ! !

The next instant, countless thick trees came from all directions, their roots converging and slamming down with immense force toward the spot where the three members of the Uchiha Clan were located !

Gazing at the sea of trees that occupied their entire field of vision, the three Uchiha Ninja felt regret.

Then, in order to seize that sliver of hope for survival, the three exhausted all their skills, their three pairs of Sharingan spinning rapidly, unleashing a final madness !

Boom— After a deafening roar that shook the heavens and earth, the long-noisy forest returned to silence.

Hiss— The previously arrogant, towering sea of fire was also ruthlessly extinguished, leaving behind only a cloud of gray mist.

« Phew~ » After exhaling a long breath, Senju Matsuaki did not lower his guard and walked forward with a cold expression.

Even though he believed in his heart that the three must be dead or severely injured under his all-out killing blow, Senju Matsuaki still remained vigilant.

The Sharingan can cast Izanagi, turning unfavorable events within a period of time into something that never happened ; it is a technique with Heaven-Defying effects.

Senju Matsuaki did not know if these three knew this Ninjutsu, nor could he be certain if anyone had used it.

Walking to the spot where the three were buried by the thick tree roots, he manipulated the sturdy, thick trees to move aside.

Seeing the situation clearly, Senju Matsuaki finally felt at ease.

In the deeply sunken crater, the body of that twenty-something-year-old Uchiha Jonin had already been crushed to pieces.

Before dying, he had instinctively used his hands to protect his head, which was the only reason it remained intact.

Not far from his side was the older Uchiha Clan Jonin.

His condition was even worse.

It seemed that in order to cover for the two juniors, he had stood at the very front when facing the bombardment of the sea of trees, attempting to block it for a moment using Earth Release Ninjutsu.

As a result, his injuries were the most severe, and he was almost beaten beyond recognition.

Flesh and blood were mixed into the mud—red, white, and yellow—a sight one would not want to look at for long.

Seeing this, Senju Matsuaki felt a bit of regret.

It was not a pity for the person, but a pity for his pair of Three-Tomoe Sharingan.

At the very edge of the crater, Uchiha Setsuna lay on the ground like a dead dog, unable to move.

What surprised Senju Matsuaki was that out of the three Uchiha Clan members present, it was this weakest one who still had a breath left.

After walking a few steps closer, Senju Matsuaki saw Uchiha Setsuna’s condition clearly.

Uchiha Setsuna’s body lay flat in the crater ; the left half of his body from the shoulder down was crushed into a bloody mess, while the other half of his body and his head remained intact.

Due to excessive blood loss, his breathing was becoming increasingly difficult to sustain.

Uchiha Setsuna’s eyes, filled with blood, saw Senju Matsuaki walking toward him, revealing bone-chilling hatred and fear.

« Don’t kill me ! My grandfather is Uchiha Hosai, and he won’t let you off ! ! ! »

Kid, you’re in danger.

Senju Matsuaki looked at Uchiha Setsuna with a strange expression on his face.

In the future, a certain Uchiha who was also crushed on one side of his body but still had breath left ended up turning the Ninja World upside down.

« Got it. Your grandfather is named Uchiha Hosai. Another day... I’ll send him to reunite with you. »

After kindly offering this reassurance, Senju Matsuaki struck with his blade, completely piercing Uchiha Setsuna’s heart.

Those crimson eyes filled with hatred gradually lost their luster, and his slightly heaving chest also returned to stillness.

With this matter settled, Senju Matsuaki did not linger any longer.

After extracting the two pairs of still-intact Sharingan and sealing them in a scroll, he first used Wood Release to thoroughly grind the three corpses into dust, then used Earth Release Ninjutsu to bury them deep underground.

...

Inside a residence of the Uchiha Clan, the aged-looking Uchiha Hosai sat with his eyes closed under the eaves.

The high walls of the courtyard blocked out the bustle outside the residence, and silence spread through the courtyard like ink seeping into clear water.

Uchiha Hosai opened his eyes, and his pitch-black pupils reflected the candlelight burning on the small stand in front of him.

As time passed, his heart flickered just like the candlelight.

The night grew late, the streets outside had long since returned to silence, and it was getting cold in the courtyard.

The attendant serving nearby saw this and stepped forward with some concern.

« Elder, the night is deep and cold. Why not go inside early to rest ? »

This voice, which suddenly broke the silence, caused a wave of irritation to well up in Uchiha Hosai’s heart.

He opened his pair of crimson Three-Tomoe Sharingan, causing the attendant to feel a chill from head to toe.

« No need. You may withdraw ; I will stay a while longer, » Uchiha Hosai said coldly.

« Yes. »

Upon hearing this, the attendant nodded and left as if granted an amnesty, leaving Uchiha Hosai alone in the courtyard.

Another thirty minutes passed.

A capable-looking Ninja leaped over the high wall and arrived in the courtyard, kneeling on one knee beside Uchiha Hosai.
