# Chapitre 21 — The Uchiha Clan's Conspiracy

Uchiha Kai, who was usually overbearing and arrogant at the Ninja Academy, now looked like a chick that had just spotted a hawk.

Seeing his brother’s angry expression, he shrank back continuously.

Yet, fearing that this would only further infuriate him, he dared not truly retreat.

Just then, the sliding door was pulled open from the outside.

An old man with a sinister face walked in, wearing a pair of wooden sandals.

After glancing at the situation in the room, he said nothing and instead walked over to a chair and sat down.

However, his equally gloomy expression revealed his intense dissatisfaction.

« Grandfather. »

Seeing the old man arrive, Uchiha Setsuna and Uchiha Kai both greeted him respectfully.

The old man was named Uchiha Huosai, an elder of the Uchiha Clan and the grandfather of the two.

Unlike the Hyuga Clan, the Uchiha Clan did not have a distinction between the Main Branch and the Branch Houses.

Apart from the clan leader, those who held the most power in the clan were these elders.

Uchiha Huosai was also one of the leaders of the Uchiha Clan’s hawk faction.

« It seems I still underestimated that brat from the Senju Clan ; I didn’t expect him to be able to trade blows with Hiruzen Sarutobi already, » Uchiha Huosai said in a deep voice.

« Grandfather, after I graduate next year, I will definitely... » Uchiha Kai was still somewhat unwilling to give up.

« Shut up. Did I tell you to speak ? »

Uchiha Huosai glared at him.

Then, as if thinking of something, his expression softened slightly.

« However, you did one thing well : provoking him to make him use his full strength during the graduation exam. »

« Although the result was not what we expected, at least we achieved our goal. »

Indeed, Uchiha Kai’s previous disparagement of Hyuga Tsukuyomi and his provocation of Senju Matsuaki were all secretly instigated by Uchiha Huosai.

His purpose was to test the strength of the younger generation of the Senju Clan in order to prepare.

If not for this, even with Uchiha Kai’s arrogant personality, he would not have been so aggressive and full of gunpowder at that time.

This was a sordid scheme secretly devised by Uchiha Huosai.

Since Hashirama and Tobirama were powerful, the Uchiha would avoid their sharp edge and strike at the Senju Clan’s juniors instead.

If the Senju Clan had no successors and no strong individuals capable of bearing the burden in the future, then the Uchiha Clan would naturally become the number one clan in Konoha.

« Grandfather, then I... »

Uchiha Setsuna’s expression turned sinister as he made a throat-slitting gesture.

This was also the ultimate goal of their test of Senju Matsuaki’s strength.

If Senju Matsuaki was just someone with mediocre talent, they wouldn’t risk being discovered to cause extra trouble.

Just like Hashirama’s son, Senju Kazuma, who had average talent and would soon leave Konoha to head to the capital of the Land of Fire.

Therefore, they had never regarded Senju Kazuma as a thorn in their side.

But Senju Matsuaki had outstanding talent, making it visibly clear that he was a major threat to the Uchiha Clan.

According to their original plan, they should now look for an opportunity to assassinate him.

« Hmm. »

After pondering for a while, Uchiha Huosai shook his head.

« It’s too conspicuous to act now ; let’s wait for a while and find another opportunity. »

« Moreover, your current strength hasn’t reached the level of a Jonin yet ; I’m afraid you can’t even compare to Hiruzen Sarutobi. »

« After some time, I will dispatch some manpower. By then, you can use some disguises before going to assassinate him. »

« Yes, Grandfather. »

Uchiha Setsuna replied, unwilling to let it go.

...

On the other side, in the Hyuga Clan’s compound.

Inside a residence in the most central area.

Two adult men were sitting on their knees, facing each other, with two steaming cups of tea placed on a low table in between.

One was a young man with a composed face, and the other was slightly older, with a few strands of white hair at his temples.

The similarity was that both of their eyes were an extremely distinct white—the Byakugan of the Hyuga Clan.

« Clan Leader Teruake, according to the rules of the Hyuga Clan, your sister Tsukuyomi must now be branded with the Caged Bird Seal. »

The old man’s words were respectful, but his tone seemed somewhat stiff.

« That’s right. Tsukuyomi graduated from the Ninja Academy this year and officially became a Ninja. According to the rules, it really cannot be delayed any longer. »

To his surprise, Hyuga Teruake, who had always pushed off and prevaricated on this matter, was now acting compliant.

Consequently, the old man’s expression softened quite a bit.

« If that is what the Clan Leader thinks, then that is good. »

According to the Hyuga Clan’s rules, when the eldest son of the Main Branch turns three, the Branch Houses should have the Caged Bird Seal branded on their foreheads.

In other words, when Hyuga Teruake was three, his other younger siblings, as members of the Branch Houses, should have been branded with the Caged Bird Seal.

Besides Hyuga Tsukuyomi, he also had several younger brothers.

When he was young, he had a very good relationship with his younger brothers, and in the time outside of training, he would often take them out to play.

However, after the Caged Bird Seal was branded, the bond between the brothers gradually grew distant.

The way they addressed him also changed from « Brother » to « Lord. »

At that time, he was still very young and powerless to change this situation.

Moreover, this was a system that had continued in the Hyuga Clan for many years and was already deeply rooted.

Once he revealed any thoughts of overturning this system, he would likely be deposed as Clan Leader by the other elders of the Main Branch in an instant.

Therefore, even though he had now inherited the position of Clan Leader, he could only use delaying tactics to postpone the fate of his sister being branded with the Caged Bird Seal.

« But... »

Hyuga Teruake changed the subject, causing the old man in front of him to frown, having a bad premonition.

« I heard from Tsukuyomi today that Hokage-sama is now her Jonin mentor. »

« After all, it is Hokage-sama ; this matter perhaps should be notified in advance, » Hyuga Teruake said with a smile.

« What does this have to do with Hashirama-sama ? » the old man asked reflexively.

Hearing this, Hyuga Teruake shook his head faintly.

« Hokage-sama is willing to personally instruct Tsukuyomi ; he must have taken a liking to her talent, knowing that she awakened the Byakugan early. »

« If we brand her with the Caged Bird Seal, causing flaws in Tsukuyomi’s Byakugan and lowering her talent, who will bear Hokage-sama’s wrath then ? »

« What ? Awakened the Byakugan ? ! » The old man caught the keyword and widened his eyes abruptly.

« How come I didn’t know Hyuga Tsukuyomi opened her eyes so early ? Teruake, were you hiding it from us ? ! »

The old man’s brows were tightly knitted, and he had uncontrollable anger in his heart.

The reason they had previously agreed to Teruake’s delay in branding Hyuga Tsukuyomi with the Caged Bird Seal was partly to give this newly appointed Clan Leader some face and not want to make things too awkward.

On the other hand, it was also because Hyuga Teruake had been hiding Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s talent, letting her keep a low profile in the Ninja Academy.

« You should know that the Caged Bird Seal is also to protect the Byakugan ; if there were no Caged Bird Seal... »

Hearing this, Hyuga Teruake’s expression sank : « Then please trouble Fourth Elder to report this matter to Hokage-sama. I will absolutely not interfere in this matter, and you will bear the consequences ! »

« You... Hmph ! »

The throat of the Fourth Elder of the Hyuga Clan’s Main Branch seemed to be gripped by someone, and he couldn’t say the second half of his sentence for a long time.

In the end, he just slammed the table hard and left, flicking his sleeves.
