# Chapitre 31 — Hashirama, the Wood Clone Substitute

This meant that the rewards obtained from checking in might be worthless or items he didn’t need.

But then, a thought struck him, and Matsumi slapped his thigh hard.

How foolish of me !

The strongest person in the world right now is right by my side. If I check in with Uncle Hashirama for the next twenty days, unless I’m incredibly unlucky, how could I not get anything good ?

Moreover, the original price of 500 points just goes to show how high the ceiling for this system is.

After all, the blue skill space key that only cost 100 points back then allowed him to learn the Breathing Technique, and he is still benefiting from it to this day.

And compared to the skill space key, the rewards obtained from the check-in system can be more directly converted into combat power.

At the same time, its scope is not limited to just « skills » ; it also includes talents, abilities, and items.

Having made up his mind, Matsumi gritted his teeth in pain and spent all 3,000 of his accumulated points to requisition the use of the check-in system for the next twenty days.

Time to go out ! Time to find Uncle Hashirama !

Strapping the short sword hanging on the wall to his back, Matsumi swiftly headed toward the Hokage Building.

There was still some time before evening, so it was still working hours, not to mention that Hashirama often worked overtime.

Sure enough, when Matsumi knocked and entered the Hokage Office, he was still sitting at his desk, processing paperwork with a bitter expression.

However, although he often heard his uncle complain about the heavy workload, Matsumi knew that he was generally enjoying it.

As for all this tedious paperwork, he just considered it paying the price for his childhood dream.

« Uh, Matsumi ? »

Looking up and seeing who had arrived, Hashirama was slightly taken aback.

« Why are you here again ? Is something the matter ? »

If he remembered correctly, the boy had only left the Hokage Building a little over an hour ago.

However, Senju Matsumi did not immediately answer Hashirama’s question ; instead, he silently chanted in his heart : « Check in ! »

[Check-in character : Senju Hashirama (Wood Clone) ! Obtained : Shadow Clone Technique !]

This result caused Senju Matsumi’s face, which had been full of anticipation, to darken.

Checking in with a Wood Clone and getting the Shadow Clone Technique was quite logical.

But the fact that the Hokage-sama currently processing paperwork in front of him was actually just a Wood Clone and not his real body, that was unreasonable.

The shadow of a village usually needs to oversee the Ninja village ; even if there is an important matter requiring them to go out, they must make thorough arrangements.

Therefore, even when leading Team 11 out on a mission, Hashirama would always send a Wood Clone to accompany them, while his real body remained in the Hokage Office.

And now, with no urgent matters at hand, Hashirama had left a Wood Clone in the office...

« Uncle, um... » Senju Matsumi forced a somewhat stiff smile.

« You’re a Wood Clone right now, right ? So where is your real body... »

However, before Matsumi could finish his sentence, Hashirama sprang up from his seat, looking panicked.

« How is that possible ? How could I be a Wood Clone ? ! »

« Ahem... »

Seemingly realizing that his reaction had been a bit too intense, Hashirama coughed twice and sat back down.

Realizing that his reaction had just exposed the fact that a Wood Clone was filling in for him, he couldn’t help but feel a bit awkward.

He picked up the document in his hand and put it down, then picked it up again, feeling generally restless.

« Um, don’t tell your Second Uncle about this. »

Hashirama glanced at the door of the Hokage Office and, after confirming that no one would be coming in for a while, whispered.

« Uncle, did your real body go to the casino ? »

« Ahahaha, I’ve been working overtime every day lately, and I’m just feeling a bit itchy to gamble, so... »

« ... »

Matsumi sighed helplessly. Seeing Uncle Hashirama like this, he was clearly a repeat offender when it came to using a Wood Clone to fill in for him at work.

However, this behavior itself wasn’t really a big deal ; Hashirama’s real body was still in Konoha, and he could return quickly if any urgent matters arose.

It was just that both Uzumaki Mito and Senju Tobirama were opposed to his gambling habit.

« Alright, Uncle, see you tomorrow. »

« Ah, see you. »

Inside the Hokage Office, only Senju Hashirama was left, scratching his head in confusion.

Come to think of it, what did that kid Matsumi come to find me for ?

...

Early the next morning, Senju Hashirama and Tobirama walked out of the residence side by side.

They were met face-to-face by Senju Matsumi, who happened to be passing by.

Their homes were originally close to each other, so Hashirama and Tobirama didn’t find the encounter strange.

« Uncle, Second Uncle. »

Matsumi smiled and greeted the two of them.

Of course, his appearance here at this moment was no coincidence ; it was just so that his check-in action wouldn’t seem so abrupt.

« Check in ! »

He targeted Senju Hashirama for the check-in again. After using today’s check-in opportunity, the notification appeared.

[Check-in character : Senju Hashirama ! Obtained : Chakra capacity increase (Large) !]

Noticing this notification and the change in his own Chakra capacity, Matsumi was stunned for a moment, then a satisfied smile appeared on his face.

« Uncle, Second Uncle, goodbye ! »

Having gained a lot, he left, very satisfied.

On the way to the training ground, Matsumi’s steps seemed a bit lighter.

This task reward might seem ordinary, but in reality, it was anything but.

First of all, for a Ninja, both physical energy and spiritual energy have limits, so the upper limit of Chakra is also fixed.

Generally speaking, by the age of thirty or forty, a Ninja’s Chakra capacity reaches its upper limit, and afterwards, as the body ages, the Chakra capacity will continue to decrease.

Even though the Senju Clan is gifted and has more Chakra than ordinary Ninjas, there is still an upper limit.

Moreover, reaching this upper limit is not easy ; even with daily training, the amount of improvement remains limited.

However, just after the successful check-in, Senju Matsumi’s Chakra capacity gained a considerable increase.

If his Chakra capacity was previously a small pond, it had now become a lake.

According to his original growth trajectory, he might not have possessed this level of Chakra capacity until he reached adulthood.

But the check-in just now allowed Matsumi to save ten years of time in terms of Chakra capacity.

However, thinking of this, Matsumi was even more amazed at Uncle Hashirama’s strength.

Just a single check-in could yield such a massive amount of Chakra ; he couldn’t imagine how vast Uncle Hashirama’s own Chakra capacity must be.

« This kid. » Senju Tobirama smiled and shook his head, then looked at Hashirama beside him. « Big Brother, where were we just now ? »

« Oh, right, we were just mentioning... »

After bidding farewell to Hashirama and Tobirama, Matsumi walked quickly toward the training ground.

As his and Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s strength grew, the training ground in Matsumi’s residence gradually became insufficient.

Sometimes he was even worried that his Ninjutsu would accidentally destroy the walls or buildings of the residence, which prevented them from unleashing their full power during sparring.

Therefore, after nearly setting fire to the building closest to the training ground one time, the two of them moved their sparring location to the Konoha training ground.
