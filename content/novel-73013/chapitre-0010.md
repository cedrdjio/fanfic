# Chapitre 10 — If I Let You Graduate Smoothly, It's My Loss!

Soon, the first and second rounds of the assessment were over.

Unlike the first round, those with the strength to participate in the second round were basically not pushovers.

Senju Matsuaki’s opponent in the second round was a boy from a civilian background.

He didn’t know any clan-specific Ninjutsu, nor did he have a Bloodline Limit, but he had mastered the basic Ninjutsu taught at the Ninja Academy very well.

Although he eventually lost to Senju Matsuaki, his performance clearly met the standards for graduation.

In addition, Hyuga Tsukuyomi, Uchiha Kai, and Sarutobi Go, whom Senju Tobirama and others had mentioned earlier, also successfully advanced to the third round.

After the first two rounds of matches, only six people remained who could continue fighting in the third round.

Besides Senju Matsuaki, Hyuga Tsukuyomi, Uchiha Kai, and Sarutobi Go.

There were also two other top students from the graduating year who had managed to hold on until this round.

After all, this was an assessment, not an official tournament.

It was impossible to pause and wait for everyone to recover before continuing.

Therefore, some who were injured or had exhausted their stamina and Chakra, even if they had won in the first two rounds, forfeited the subsequent matches.

Although the format of the graduation assessment was an elimination tournament, it wasn’t the case that only the person who won until the end could graduate.

The process was emphasized, not the result.

However, those who still had energy left by the third round couldn’t help but want to win until the end.

« I didn’t expect you guys to make it to the third round as well. »

Just as the six of them were preparing to line up to draw lots, Uchiha Kai, who had originally been standing further back, suddenly stepped forward forcefully.

After bumping into Hyuga Tsukuyomi who was standing in front, Uchiha Kai arrived at the front of the line as if it were a matter of course.

« But I guess you’re lucky you didn’t run into me in the previous rounds. »

As he spoke, he grabbed a paper ball from the drawing box and walked to the side to open it.

With a trace of an arrogant smile on his lips, Uchiha Kai directly unfolded the paper ball, facing the side with the number towards everyone.

He drew the number one.

In other words, the next person to draw the same number would be his opponent.

« Don’t talk big too early ; don’t regret it when you end up losing face later. »

Senju Matsuaki ignored the other party’s provocative expression and just responded indifferently.

He patted Hyuga Tsukuyomi on the shoulder, signaling her to continue drawing lots.

The process, which had stalled due to the small interlude just now, continued forward.

« It’s number two. »

After glancing at the number she drew, Hyuga Tsukuyomi said softly with some disappointment.

She had been hoping she could draw number one first, and then eliminate Uchiha Kai first.

That way, Matsumi wouldn’t have to face this annoying Uchiha.

« Don’t worry, Tsukuyomi. Leave him to me. »

Senju Matsuaki said with a smile, while also opening the paper ball in his hand, facing Uchiha Kai who was standing not far away with his arms crossed.

The number he drew was, surprisingly, also number one.

That meant the next first match would be between him and Uchiha Kai.

Although he also unfolded the paper ball towards his opponent, Senju Matsuaki’s expression lacked the arrogance and provocation that the other party had shown earlier.

However, aside from indifference, Matsumi’s face looked a bit more stern than usual.

Yet, even this expression made Uchiha Kai feel looked down upon.

Gritting his teeth fiercely, he raised his right hand to the height of his neck and made a throat-slitting gesture.

On the platform, many who saw this action frowned.

« Hmph. »

Senju Tobirama, who had always had a poor opinion of the Uchiha Clan, frowned even more and let out a cold snort.

It was no wonder he had always been hostile towards the Uchiha Clan.

It could be seen from the original work that the behavior of many Uchiha was quite extreme.

For example, Uchiha Obito, because the girl he had a crush on died, wanted to destroy the entire world.

In the Ninja World, most Ninjas had friends or family killed by others, and they all knew that every grievance had its perpetrator and every debt had its debtor.

However, some people in the Uchiha Clan, after being provoked, would act as if they had gone insane.

Senju Tobirama, who had fought against the Uchiha Clan on the battlefield for many years, naturally detested these unstable factors.

Furthermore, after the establishment of Konoha, Ninja clans, big and small, all behaved themselves.

Only the Uchiha Clan maintained an arrogant and insufferable attitude, refusing to integrate into Konoha.

Such behavior sowed the seeds of evil for the future of the Uchiha Clan.

By the time of Uchiha Fugaku, the situation of the Uchiha was far worse than it was now.

Even if the Uchiha Clan wanted to integrate into Konoha then, it would have been a thousand or ten thousand times more difficult than now.

Because trust between the two sides had long since been eroded.

These things aside for now.

Soon, everyone on the field had finished drawing lots.

Senju Matsuaki and Uchiha Kai, who drew the number one, were the opponents for the first match.

And Hyuga Tsukuyomi and Sarutobi Go drew the number two, making them opponents for the second match.

The remaining two drew number three.

« It seems your luck ends here, Senju Matsuaki. »

Uchiha Kai walked to the center of the field impatiently, while simultaneously drawing a Kunai from his Ninja tool pouch.

« You haven’t performed the Seal of Confrontation yet, put that Kunai away ! »

Just as Uchiha Kai was preparing to continue spouting threats, the Proctor on the side suddenly shouted loudly.

Hearing this, Uchiha Kai seemed to feel it was a bit embarrassing and didn’t comply immediately.

However, the Proctor’s icy gaze was as if to say :

If he didn’t comply, he would directly declare his assessment a failure.

« Pfft— »

Seeing the other party’s frustrated look, Senju Matsuaki laughed out loud without hiding it.

Since the other party was releasing hostility so undisguisedly, Matsumi naturally wouldn’t remain friendly towards him either.

After both sides performed the Seal of Confrontation, Uchiha Kai couldn’t hold back immediately.

With eyes full of rage, he drew his Kunai and charged towards Senju Matsuaki.

The Proctor on the side watched this scene and could only sigh helplessly.

He could tell that this brat from the Uchiha Clan hadn’t shown any restraint at all and was completely aiming to kill or maim.

If the situation were to get out of hand later, he would have to be prepared to prevent casualties.

Meanwhile, Senju Matsuaki wasn’t idle either.

After his Breathing Technique reached the state of Total Concentration : Constant, his body was filled with immense power at every moment.

At the same time, his physical fitness was also subtly improving under the effect of the Breathing Technique.

In the previous rounds of combat, his opponents were, after all, companions from the same Village.

To give his opponents a chance to show themselves, and fearing that he might directly break them, he had held back.

Otherwise, his battles in the first two rounds would have ended much faster.

But now, facing this person who kept spouting nonsense and wild words.

Matsumi wouldn’t treat him as a companion !

Therefore, there was no need to hold back at all.

If I let you graduate smoothly today, it’s my loss !
