# Chapitre 67 — "Sister, shall we just surrender?"

Beside her, Chiyo was the type to take action rather than speak. Her green-robed puppet, which she hadn’t put away yet, flew toward Senju Matsuaki, guided by the flickers of her fingertips.

Halfway through its flight, blades sprang out from the four pairs of hands and feet on the puppet’s sides—eight limbs in total.

« Konoha brat ! Taste the power of our Sunagakure Puppet Technique ! ! »

Under Chiyo’s precise control, the puppet spun rapidly in the air, its sharp blades turning it into a meat grinder.

The sound of the wind whipped up by the spinning blades made the surrounding Genin shudder with fear.

« Let’s go ! Don’t get yourself killed just watching the show ! »

Snapping out of it, the surrounding Genin hurriedly fled in different directions, their figures swallowed by the shadows of the dense forest in the distance.

« Is this Sunagakure’s Puppet Technique ? »

Watching the menacing puppet, a hint of curiosity appeared in Senju Matsuaki’s eyes.

In the Ninja World at this time, the Puppet Technique was considered a rather novel secret technique.

Controlling a puppet in combat was vastly different from the fighting style where ordinary Ninja fought in person.

At this point in time, the number of puppeteers in Sunagakure was not large, and even fewer had made a name for themselves in the Ninja World.

But judging by the power of the puppet before him, Chiyo of Sunagakure already possessed impressive strength at the Chunin level.

However, that was not enough !

The next second, the puppet’s whirling blades were already right in front of Senju Matsuaki.

Seeing this, a ruthless look appeared in Chiyo’s beautiful eyes.

« Die ! Konoha brat ! ! »

As a budding puppeteer, she was full of confidence in the puppet she had personally developed.

Although this puppet was only her first creation, it had received praise from her teacher, Shamon, after it was completed.

Her teacher, Shamon, as the pioneer of Sunagakure’s Puppet Technique, had even stated outright that she possessed unparalleled talent for the art.

Shamon even predicted that as long as Chiyo continued to study the way of puppetry, her future achievements would not be much lower than those of her senior, Yan.

She had only acted weak earlier because she didn’t know the depth of her opponent’s strength.

But now that the puppet was at close quarters, she did not believe Senju Matsuaki could counter it at such a short distance.

By the time Senju Matsuaki formed hand signs, he would likely have already been sliced into pieces.

Then, the next instant.

Senju Matsuaki raised his right fist, a massive amount of Chakra condensing within his clenched hand.

The moment his right fist was about to collide with the blades, the Chakra in his palm burst forth, crushing forward like a landslide.

Crack—

A crisp snapping sound rang out. The puppet hovering in the air cracked at the head, and the fissures quickly spread across its body, shattering it into a pile of broken parts on the ground.

Seeing this scene, both Chiyo and Ebizo felt a chill spread from the tops of their heads to the soles of their feet.

One had to know, while the puppet looked like it was made of wood, the internal parts and mechanisms that truly provided structural support were actually metal.

Even the wood on the exterior had been carefully selected and subjected to special processing methods, making it far harder than ordinary wood.

Yet just a moment ago, this puppet had been shattered by a single punch.

The ruthlessness of a sure-kill strike in Chiyo’s eyes had been completely replaced by shock and horror.

Beside her, Ebizo swallowed hard ; his earlier youthful arrogance had long since vanished.

« Sister, shall we just surrender ? »

Ebizo looked at his sister, Chiyo, with a pleading gaze. He knew by heart the principle that a wise person submits to circumstances.

A Ninja is one who can endure what ordinary people cannot.

Therefore, Ebizo decisively groveled.

After the siblings exchanged a look, Ebizo first took out the game piece he had been hiding and displayed it to Matsuaki.

The Shogi piece in his hand had no characters written on it ; it was blank.

Seeing this, Senju Matsuaki withdrew his gaze and turned to look at Chiyo, who was gritting her teeth nearby.

« Damn it... here ! »

Seeing that gaze filled with an implied threat, Chiyo, though unwilling, still reached her hand toward the fullness of her chest.

Her fingers moved within the cleavage, and the next second, a game piece was retrieved and tossed toward Senju Matsuaki.

Reaching out to catch the piece, the lingering body heat and faint fragrance upon it made Senju Matsuaki pause slightly.

Taking a look at the character « Jade » engraved on it, he put it away with satisfaction.

[Current Progress : 3 game pieces captured, 2 candidates defeated.]

Glancing at the current mission progress, Senju Matsuaki stroked his chin thoughtfully.

Two pieces belonging to the Iwagakure Genin and Hidden Cloud Village Genin from earlier, plus one originally belonging to Chiyo—that made three in total.

As for the two defeated candidates, it must have included Chiyo as well.

Perhaps because he had shattered the opponent’s puppet with a single punch, causing her most prominent ability to fail, it was judged as defeating her.

Thinking of this, Senju Matsuaki turned his gaze toward Ebizo, who was breaking out in a cold sweat, and rubbed his hands together.

« Since you’ve handed over the pieces, I’ll go easy on you. »

At different starting locations, such clashes were not isolated incidents.

In one such place.

Black Iron Sand condensed in the air into a black rectangular block, stalemated against a figure wreathed in violent lightning.

After several exchanges, the black Iron Sand splattered everywhere, and the riotous lightning spilled out into the air.

« As expected of a Sunagakure rising star, the Magnet Release Ninja’s reputation is well-deserved. »

After Dou and Yan stared at each other for a moment, Dou voluntarily withdrew the lightning armor wrapped around his body and said with a solemn expression.

« I have other things to do. If you don’t want to keep fighting, we’ll meet again later. »

Deep in Yan’s eyes was a look of wariness ; he also controlled his Chakra to withdraw the black Iron Sand.

After several probes, the two were temporarily evenly matched, but neither had revealed their true trump cards.

If they continued to fight, even if they could determine a winner, it would likely result in both sides suffering heavy losses.

The two originally had no conflict of interest and didn’t strictly need to decide a winner, so naturally, neither wanted to see that outcome.

« I hope to see you again in the third stage of the exam. »

Seeing Yan of Hidden Sand Village say this, Dou had no intention of stopping him ; he smiled and turned to leave.

The Ninja of Hidden Cloud Village were always united, and what’s more, he had been the leader of the pack among his peers since childhood, and was now the big brother of the younger generation.

He was unwilling to tangle with Yan for too long precisely because he wanted to regroup with other Hidden Cloud Village Ninja as soon as possible.

« Do you think there’s anyone else who could possibly eliminate me in the second stage ? »

Upon hearing this, Yan frowned and voiced his doubt.

Both of them understood their own and the other’s situation ; they already possessed the strength of a Jonin and were only participating in the exam to uphold their Village’s reputation.

In his view, this Ninja from Hidden Cloud Village named Dou was already the ceiling of strength among the candidates in this Chunin Exam.

Although he dared not say he was definitely stronger than the other, Yan considered himself not much weaker.

« Hahaha, who knows~ »

Dou turned around and waved at Yan behind him, as a farewell to this Magnet Release Ninja he had met once.

« Trying to be mysterious. »

After Yan snorted coldly, he inwardly concluded that the other party was just trying to affect his state of mind.

He lightly brushed the dust that had settled on his cloak during the battle, then turned and left in another direction.
