# Chapitre 167 — Ichibi, Three-Tails Seal!

« Ichibi, you are too dangerous ; we must Seal you away ! »

Hashirama shouted, and at the same time, the scroll on his back quickly unfurled ; the work of sealing Ichibi officially began !

Complex runes lit up with golden light, and chains rose from the ground, wrapping around Shukaku’s body and dragging it bit by bit into the sealing scroll.

When the last trace of the Tailed Beast Chakra was drawn into the scroll, the vanguard of the Hidden Sand Village had just arrived a hundred meters away.

Leading them was Retto, wearing the Kazekage hat. He looked at the sealing scroll in Hashirama’s hand, his face livid.

« Senju Hashirama... you Konoha are being far too disrespectful to us in the Hidden Sand. »

Hashirama strapped the scroll to his back and revealed a hearty smile.

« My apologies, Your Excellency Kazekage. The power of the Tailed Beast is too dangerous ; we are also doing this for the peace of the Ninja World. »

« You ! »

« Farewell. »

Hashirama waved his hand, took Senju Matsuaki, and with a flash, their figures disappeared into the sky filled with yellow sand.

Leaving only the Hidden Sand group standing there, gritting their teeth but helpless.

Strictly speaking, the Tailed Beast was indeed ownerless.

Just because Ichibi was temporarily residing in the Land of Wind did not mean it was the property of their Land of Wind or the Hidden Sand.

After all, sometimes Ichibi would just roll over and appear in the neighboring Land of Rivers.

...

Night fell, and Senju Hashirama and Senju Matsuaki temporarily set up camp to rest.

Beside the campfire, Hashirama sat cross-legged, holding the sealing scroll with a look of gratification on his face.

« Matsuaki, thanks to you today. »

Before he knew it, that Genin he had taken out of the village for his first mission had grown to an incredible level.

Matsuaki sat opposite him, adding a few pieces of firewood to the fire.

« Uncle, Ichibi is just the beginning ; there are seven more Tailed Beasts waiting for us. And... »

« Judging by today’s events, other Ninja Villages have also started moving ; we must get ahead of them ! »

« Indeed... »

Hashirama looked up at the night sky, a firm light flickering in his eyes, « To realize the vision of the Tailed Beast Council, we must complete this matter before they do. »

Matsuaki did not answer, just quietly watching the dancing flames.

The firelight reflected on his face, and deep within those calm eyes, there seemed to be some hidden thoughts that no one else could fathom.

The Tailed Beast Council was, of course, far better than the simple method of distributing Tailed Beasts as seen in the original work.

However, could it truly bring lasting peace as Senju Hashirama hoped ?

« By the way, I wonder if Tobirama has found the Three-Tails yet ? »

Senju Hashirama rubbed his chin, interrupting Senju Matsuaki’s train of thought.

« Uncle, splitting into two groups probably won’t be enough ; we need to split into three groups. »

Senju Matsuaki suggested in a deep voice, « You and Second Uncle can each lead a team for one route, and I, along with Tsukuyomi and Xiaoguang, will take charge of another. »

« Then let’s do that. »

Senju Hashirama pondered for a moment before nodding.

...

A few days later, in a certain sea area of the Land of Water.

Under a grey sky, the sea was rough, and thick fog filled the air.

On the shore, Senju Tobirama wore a black tight-fitting combat suit with a dark blue layered armored vest over it, his silver-white short hair fluttering slightly in the sea breeze.

Behind him stood ten members of the Konoha Sealing Team, their expressions solemn.

« Lord Tobirama, intelligence indicates that the Three-Tails has been appearing frequently in this sea area recently. »

A Ninja from the Sealing Team pointed to the sea ahead, « According to local fishermen, it seems to have built a nest on this seabed. »

Tobirama’s gaze was sharp, staring into the bottomless ocean.

« The Three-Tails is an aquatic Tailed Beast ; its combat power in water far exceeds that of Tailed Beasts on land. »

His tone was calm, « You stay on the shore and wait for orders ; I will go and lure it out first. »

« Lord, that’s too dangerous ! »

« Rest assured. »

Tobirama interrupted his subordinate and formed hand Seals.

« Water Release : Water Clone Technique ! »

Two water Clones appeared beside him, and the three of them exchanged a glance before leaping into the sea simultaneously.

The seawater was bone-chillingly cold, but Tobirama’s Chakra seemed to form a thin film on his body surface, insulating him from the seawater’s erosion.

He continued to dive, his gaze piercing through the dim seawater as he searched for traces of the Three-Tails.

As the depth increased, the surrounding light grew dimmer and dimmer, with only the occasional glowing fish bringing a faint light.

Suddenly, a powerful Chakra fluctuation surged from the depths of the seabed.

Tobirama’s pupils constricted, and he immediately stopped diving.

The next second, the seabed mud churned, and a huge black shadow slowly emerged.

It was a behemoth resembling a turtle, but its back was covered in sharp spikes, and its Three-Tails swayed slowly in the water, each movement causing undercurrents to surge.

The target of their trip, the Three-Tails !

It opened its eyes, its cold gaze instantly locking onto Tobirama.

« Human... daring to intrude into my territory... »

A dull voice came through the water, carrying a heavy sense of threat.

Tobirama did not waste words and quickly formed hand Seals.

« Water Release : Water Dragon Bullet Technique ! »

A huge water dragon condensed before him and roared towards the Three-Tails.

The Three-Tails roared disdainfully, its Three-Tails swinging simultaneously, creating a huge vortex that shredded the water dragon directly.

« Mere Water Release is not worth mentioning before me ! »

The Three-Tails opened its mouth wide, and a purple-black Chakra ball condensed within.

Tailed Beast Ball !

Senju Tobirama had seen this move before on the Nine-Tailed, so he was already mentally prepared.

Tobirama’s expression remained unchanged, and he formed hand Seals again.

« Water Release : Water Formation Wall ! »

A huge wall of water rose before him.

However, the Tailed Beast Ball crashed into the water wall, and after holding for only a second, it pierced through the wall.

But behind the water wall, Tobirama’s figure was already gone.

« Up there ! »

The Three-Tails looked up, only to see Tobirama had already leapt into mid-air at some point, holding a specially made Kunai in his hand.

« Flying Thunder God Technique ! »

Tobirama threw the Kunai, which instantly cut through the air like a streak of light, landing above the Three-Tails.

The next second, Tobirama’s figure also appeared there out of thin air, with an explosive tag already in his hand.

Boom !

The explosive tag detonated on the Three-Tails' back shell ; although it failed to cause substantial damage, it successfully enraged it.

« Damned human ! »

The Three-Tails was furious, its entire body rolling in the water, and countless water blades shot out in all directions.

Tobirama dodged flexibly, while continuously throwing Kunai marked with the Flying Thunder God Mark, shuttling throughout the battlefield.

For a time, the battle on the seabed raged on.

Water Release clashes, Taijutsu exchanges, Flying Thunder God surprise attacks... Tobirama pushed his combat skills to the limit.

But the Three-Tails was a Tailed Beast after all, and its amount of Chakra was far beyond that of a human.

As time passed, Tobirama’s breathing gradually became labored.

« As expected of the Three-Tails... it really is troublesome in the water. »

He thought to himself, while glancing in the direction of the sea surface.

The Sealing Team should be ready.

« Cannot drag this out any longer. »

A look of determination flashed in Tobirama’s eyes, and he formed hand Seals.

« Water Release : Hard Whirlpool Water Blade ! »

He created a high-speed rotating water vortex in his hand, like a sharp spear, and thrust it fiercely towards the Three-Tails' eye.
