# Chapitre 29 — Joint Chunin Exams?

Time flowed like water, and in the blink of an eye, several months had passed.

Konoha, Hokage Building.

Inside the Hokage Office, Senju Hashirama wore his Hokage hat, holding three scrolls in his hand.

He pushed the scrolls forward, and they rolled side-by-side to the front of Senju Matsuaki and Hyuga Tsukuyomi, who were standing on the other side of the desk.

« These three are the remaining missions that are most suitable for you. Lately, the Genin in the Village seem to be very active, and the number of C-rank missions is gradually failing to keep up with everyone’s demand. »

Senju Hashirama scratched his head and sighed.

Senju Matsuaki smiled and said, « After all, it’s almost time to apply for the Chunin Exams, so it’s normal for everyone to be so motivated. »

That’s right ; registration for Konoha’s annual Chunin Exams was opening soon.

Compared to the Chunin Exams, the Ninja Academy graduation assessment was as simple as children playing house.

And after becoming a Chunin, one could accept higher-level missions.

Some Ninja with expertise in certain areas could even be directly assigned to well-paying Ninja positions after becoming a Chunin.

Such as the Barrier Team, Sealing Team, or the Konoha Hospital, etc...

Going from a Genin to a Chunin was not just an increase in status, but also an increase in wealth.

« Speaking of the Chunin Exams, a special situation has arisen this year. »

Light seemed to shine in Hashirama’s eyes, and his face, slightly weary from work, became radiant.

Upon hearing this, Senju Matsuaki felt a bit curious.

What kind of special situation could make Senju Hashirama, currently so overworked, shed his fatigue from his desk work ?

However, it didn’t look like anything bad.

« You can take a look at the scroll in the middle. The matter I want to talk about is related to this mission. »

Senju Hashirama pointed to the middle of the three scrolls he had pushed in front of them earlier, then said nothing more, smiling as he waited for the two to finish reading the contents.

So Matsumi picked up the middle scroll and spread it out between himself and Hyuga Tsukuyomi.

« C-rank mission : During the period when a delegation from Kumogakure is sent to Konoha to discuss matters regarding the Chunin Exams, be responsible for surveillance and maintaining the Village’s security. »

Senju Matsuaki read the contents of the scroll aloud in a low voice, then exchanged glances with Hyuga Tsukuyomi, both seeing the surprise in each other’s eyes.

« Hashirama-sensei, why does our Village’s Chunin Exams need to be discussed with people from Kumogakure ? »

Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s white eyes held great confusion ; as a good student, she asked curiously.

The Ninja World had only entered the Hidden Village era a few years ago, and the joint exams that would be held many times in the future had no precedent in this era.

However, Matsumi knew that in the future, there would be many such Joint Chunin Exams held by multiple Villages, so his surprise quickly faded.

Senju Hashirama said : « Last week, I received a letter from the Raikage inviting us to host a Joint Chunin Exams. Through this model, we can deepen the friendly exchanges between the two Villages, and at the same time, allow the younger generation of the Villages to interact with each other. »

« Tobirama and I thought about it and felt that this new model was indeed feasible, so I wrote back to the Raikage. »

« They will send a delegation over to discuss specific matters. Based on the distance from there, they should arrive in Konoha in a few days. »

At least on the surface, this matter was indeed beneficial to peace in the Ninja World, so Senju Hashirama was very happy and very willing to support the holding of the Joint Chunin Exams.

However, Senju Matsuaki, who knew the nature of Kumogakure well, felt that things were not that simple.

Each environment shapes its people. The Land of Fire had fertile land and abundant resources, and coupled with Senju Hashirama’s gentle personality, Konoha’s image in the Ninja World was currently that of an honest, big brother.

They weren’t short on money, didn’t actively look for trouble, and when others provoked them, they would teach them a lesson, but would forgive them after hearing a few words of begging.

However, the difference was that the Land of Lightning was established on peaks that reached into the clouds, where mist dispersed and lightning was frequent ; its resources were far less abundant than the Land of Fire.

Kumogakure, established in such an environment, was a thorough martial faction.

If the First Raikage had the strength of Senju Hashirama, he probably would have gone to war everywhere long ago, wanting to unify the entire Ninja World.

And in the original timeline, Kumogakure was never a quiet neighbor.

Besides small moves like trying to kidnap Hyuga Hinata, there were many major acts of aggression, and it was the most active Village in starting wars during several Ninja World Wars.

« Could they have other motives ? »

« I have some understanding of Kumogakure’s style. I’ve heard they have always pursued martial power, and they have also caused some trouble on the borders of the Land of Fire from time to time. »

Senju Matsuaki said with some concern.

« And I also heard that a Genin squad that graduated in the same year as us encountered Ninja from Kumogakure during a mission. One of the Genin was severely injured and is still lying in the hospital. »

Hyuga Tsukuyomi said with concern.

Before Senju Hashirama could reply, the door to the Hokage Office opened from the outside.

Senju Tobirama, wearing blue layered armor, walked in from outside. It was evident that he bore traces of travel and combat, as if he had just returned from a mission.

« Rest assured, I have considered these aspects. »

Apparently, he had heard the conversation inside while standing outside the Hokage Office.

« Your uncle thinks that Kumogakure is truly coming for peace, but I don’t think so. »

Senju Tobirama snorted coldly.

« Even during the Sengoku era, the most restless ones were those barbarians from the Land of Lightning. If you say they are coming for peace, I would be the first to not believe it. »

Senju Tobirama noticed that Senju Hashirama seemed to want to speak up for Kumogakure, so he directly brought up examples from the Sengoku era to support his point of view.

Upon hearing this, Senju Hashirama could not find a reason to refute it and could only scratch his head and smile bitterly.

In the Sengoku era, apart from being hostile to local Ninja clans in the Land of Fire such as the Uchiha Clan and the Hagoromo Clan, the foreign Ninja they encountered the most were those from the Land of Lightning.

At that time, those dark-skinned, sturdy Ninja from the Land of Lightning had already set their sights on the richness of the Land of Fire.

If it weren’t for the local Ninja clans of the Land of Fire resisting them in unison, these Ninja from the Land of Lightning probably would have taken root in the Land of Fire decades ago.

« I have the same thought as Second Uncle. »

Senju Matsuaki said seriously.

« Ahahaha, actually, I didn’t completely believe them either. »

Seeing that these two family members, one big and one small, held the same view, Senju Hashirama did not stubbornly insist on his own opinion.

« Hey, don’t look at me with that distrustful expression ! »

Seeing the questioning expressions on their faces, Hashirama couldn’t help but get annoyed.

« Big Brother, if I hadn’t insisted, you probably wouldn’t have issued this mission, would you ? »

Senju Tobirama glanced at the scroll spread out on the table and shook his head helplessly.

« Monitor the delegation sent by Kumogakure, and at the same time, maintain the Village’s security. Tsukuyomi, what do you think ? »

Matsumi was inclined to accept this mission, but still wanted to ask for his teammate’s opinion.
