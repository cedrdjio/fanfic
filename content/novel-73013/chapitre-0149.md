# Chapitre 149 — Daily Life Living Together

Senju Matsuaki took Uchiha Hikari and headed east.

After crossing two forests and a river, they finally arrived at a small town far away from the war.

The town was not large, with about a hundred households, and a main street running north to south.

There were a few shops along the street selling groceries and grain, and occasionally, one could see farmers carrying hoes returning from the fields.

This place was far from the heart of the Land of Fire and far from the battlefield of the Uchiha and the Senju ; life here was peaceful and slow.

Senju Matsuaki found an open space near the town and used Wood Release to build a wooden house.

The house was not big, with one living room and two bedrooms, just enough for him and Uchiha Hikari to live in.

From now on, until the mission ended and he returned to his original era, he planned to spend this time here.

« You sleep in the inner room, and I’ll sleep in the outer one. »

Senju Matsuaki pushed open the wooden door ; the house built with Wood Release looked brand new, making him smile with satisfaction.

Then, he turned to Uchiha Hikari behind him and said.

Uchiha Hikari stood at the doorway, nodded gently, and did not speak.

She walked into the house silently and helped Senju Matsuaki tidy up.

The two of them bought some necessary daily supplies from the town and simply furnished the wooden house.

The sunset gradually faded.

It was dinner time, but it was too late to prepare any ingredients.

« It looks like we can only have porridge tonight. »

Senju Matsuaki scratched his head, feeling a bit embarrassed.

Uchiha Hikari squatted in front of the stove, clumsily adding firewood to the furnace, with a smear of ash on her face.

She looked up, and seeing Senju Matsuaki’s awkward appearance, the corners of her mouth actually curled up slightly.

« Is this going to be our home from now on ? »

Uchiha Hikari asked softly.

« Our ? »

Senju Matsuaki was slightly stunned, then shook his head and said, « No, I’ll be leaving after a while. »

« But this house is very sturdy ; you shouldn’t have any problems living here for a few decades. »

Since he had started helping, he would see it through to the end.

He built this wooden house with the intention of giving Uchiha Hikari a place to settle in the future.

As for what happened after he left, that was not something he could control.

Whether Uchiha Hikari lived a peaceful life or chose to live as a Ninja again,

it was a personal choice.

« Oh... »

Uchiha Hikari nodded gently and didn’t say anything more.

« Can you cook ? » she asked.

« ...Maybe a little bit. »

Senju Matsuaki answered with a guilty conscience.

Half an hour later.

The two of them looked at each other, facing the pot of burnt porridge.

In the end, they both tacitly took out Military Ration Pills.

« I’ll cook tomorrow. »

Uchiha Hikari put down her bowl and said seriously.

Senju Matsuaki looked at her in surprise : « You can ? »

« No. »

She paused, « But I can learn, at the very least... it won’t be worse than this. »

Senju Matsuaki : « ... »

Days went by like this.

For the first few days, the two of them were still a bit reserved and distant.

Uchiha Hikari spoke very little and most of the time just worked silently.

Washing clothes, sweeping the floor, and learning to start a fire and cook.

Senju Matsuaki was responsible for going out to buy supplies and hunting.

The money he had on him was limited, and since he wasn’t earning money as a Ninja, he had to play the role of a « hunter. »

Over the course of ten days or so, the two of them were like a pair of strangers who had just started living together, cautiously testing each other’s boundaries.

But time is the gentlest bonding agent.

Morning.

When Senju Matsuaki woke up, he found a bowl of steaming white porridge and a dish of pickled radishes on the table.

The porridge wasn’t burnt, and the radishes were sliced unevenly, but the taste was surprisingly good.

« You made this ? »

Senju Matsuaki was stunned for a moment.

Uchiha Hikari sat across the table, holding her own bowl of porridge.

She didn’t look up, just said softly.

« The pickled radishes were taught to me by the old lady next door. »

« I see... »

Senju Matsuaki picked up the bowl and took a sip ; the warm rice porridge slid down his throat into his stomach, and a sense of warmth spread.

And the pickled radishes were equally delicious.

He smacked his lips and praised sincerely : « Delicious. »

Uchiha Hikari’s hand holding the chopsticks paused slightly, her lowered eyelashes trembled, and she didn’t speak.

But Senju Matsuaki noticed that the corners of her mouth, which were always pursed, seemed to have relaxed a little.

Another morning, when Senju Matsuaki woke up, he heard the sound of a spatula clashing in the kitchen.

He put on his coat and walked out, seeing Uchiha Hikari standing on tiptoes trying to reach the salt jar on the top shelf of the cupboard.

The girl wasn’t tall enough ; after reaching a few times without success, her brows furrowed slightly.

Senju Matsuaki walked over, reached out to take down the salt jar for her, and casually ruffled her hair.

« You’ll be able to reach it once you grow taller. »

Uchiha Hikari stiffened, then glared at him angrily, but her cheeks quietly turned red.

« I can reach it without growing taller ! ! »

She snorted lightly, and a crimson skeletal arm of Susanoo manifested beside her.

The skeletal hand waved, grabbed another jar from the top of the cupboard, and gently handed it to her.

Seeing this, Senju Matsuaki’s mouth twitched.

After proving herself, Uchiha Hikari deactivated Susanoo and said, « Don’t touch my head. »

There was no anger in her voice, only the awkward, coquettish tone that a girl of this age would have.

Days flowed like a stream, plain yet clear.

They would go to the well to fetch water together in the morning and sit on the threshold to watch the sunset at dusk.

Senju Matsuaki taught Uchiha Hikari to identify wild vegetables, and Uchiha Hikari learned to mend holes in clothes.

The two of them would occasionally bicker over who should wash the dishes.

The people in town gradually came to know this young boy and girl living in the wooden house at the end of the village.

When someone asked if they were siblings, Senju Matsuaki smiled and nodded, saying yes.

Uchiha Hikari stood to the side without refuting it.

In the dead of night, Uchiha Hikari would sometimes wake up, listening to the steady breathing coming from the next room, and a sense of security she had never felt before welled up in her heart.

She thought of that dark and damp corner in the dungeon, and thought of those cold eyes and harsh words.

Then, she thought of that hand reaching out to her, and thought of the words, « Do you want to come with me ? »

She turned over, buried her face in the pillow, and closed her eyes.

Outside the window, the moonlight was like water, and the galaxy was brilliant.

In this small town far from the war, for the first time, she felt that being alive was not such a bad thing.

But...

Uchiha Hikari knew deep down that this beautiful time would be short-lived.

As time passed, she felt more and more of the urgency in Senju Matsuaki’s heart.

He taught her life skills, and at the same time, filled a storage cabinet in the house with the money he had saved from selling game during this time.

She noticed that the boy sometimes stared blankly at the full moon in the sky.

Uchiha Hikari vaguely felt that Senju Matsuaki did not seem to come from the Senju Clan’s ancestral lands.

But from somewhere further away ?

But every time she asked indirectly, he would always laugh it off.

« ...Don’t leave. »

Her lips moved slightly, her voice as soft as a whisper in a dream.

Her consciousness gradually blurred, and she fell into a deep sleep.
