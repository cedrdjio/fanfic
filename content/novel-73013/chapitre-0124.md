# Chapitre 124 — Vibration! Tsunami! The Water and Sky Reverse!

In the examinee lounge.

As the semi-finalists were determined, the atmosphere suddenly stagnated.

« If you are lucky enough to get a bye, you might still have a chance to reach the finals. »

« To think I’d meet you in the semi-finals, you’re really unlucky, kid of the Senju Clan. »

« This isn’t the forest anymore ; there isn’t such a huge area for you to run and hide ! »

Just before leaving the examinee lounge for the exam arena, Yan from Sunagakure turned his head and said to Senju Matsuaki.

In his view, Senju Matsuaki’s Water Release indeed had some level of attainment.

Caught off guard, even he had almost suffered a loss.

But unfortunately, a Ninja’s battle is a battle of information.

Since he already knew the level of the opponent’s Water Release and was prepared in advance, it was impossible to suffer a loss like before.

Hearing this, Senju Matsuaki was not angered. Instead, he raised an eyebrow and smiled, saying : « You were silent and cold before, but now you’re quite talkative. »

« Let me guess, did you get frustrated after suffering consecutive losses ? »

« You ! ! » Yan’s pupils constricted, and anger surged in his heart.

Whether he was frustrated before or not was unclear, but he was definitely frustrated now.

During the second stage assessment in the forest, his plan to seize the Byakugan had been blocked.

Even a lock of his hair had been cut off.

This matter was not glorious, and since he failed, he didn’t want to talk much about it.

But afterwards, he inexplicably fell for a bowl of oden.

Even if an enemy saw him entering the Chunin Exams with so many negative buffs, they should have let it go, right ?

According to his speculation, the person who could perform such petty tricks during the Chunin Exams was nine times out of ten someone from Konoha.

Thinking of this, he laughed in anger and said coldly : « I hope your strength is as formidable as your mouth. »

After speaking, Yan turned and jumped off the edge of the spectator stand, and black Iron Sand condensed into a platform convenient for standing.

Riding the Iron Sand platform, the figure shrouded in a cloak gradually descended and stood steadily on the arena.

« You dealt such a heavy hand to my companion ; it is time to collect the price that should be paid. »

The smile on Senju Matsuaki’s face faded, and a hint of coldness flashed across his eyes.

The opponent’s intention to seize the Byakugan was already trampling on the bottom line.

However, the opponent was truly cunning, knowing that if they weren’t caught red-handed, Konoha would have a hard time making a move.

But, it’s hard for Konoha to find trouble with you.

It wouldn’t be too much for me to beat you half to death in the exam arena, would it ?

...

On the exam arena, Senju Matsuaki and Yan stood opposite each other.

One had white hair and red eyes, with a sharp and high-spirited Aura, like a blade about to be unsheathed.

The other had blue hair and yellow eyes, with a cold and serious temperament, like a cruel and desolate desert.

The two had distinct temperaments, forming a strong contrast in the center of the arena.

Once truly on the arena, the two did not trade verbal jabs like before.

In the current situation, with countless eyes from all over the Ninja World watching, engaging in a trash-talking contest would be beneath them.

The only thing they could do was let their strength speak.

Under the gaze of everyone, the loser would be ground into dust.

« The battle, begin ! ! »

Following the signal to start, Senju Matsuaki and Yan simultaneously performed hand seals rapidly, and Chakra fluctuated violently.

« Water Release : Exploding Water Colliding Wave ! ! »

« Magnet Release : Wings of Iron Sand ! ! »

From Senju Matsuaki’s mouth, a surging giant wave gushed out, instantly submerging half of the arena.

His body stood atop the giant wave, climbing step by step with the surging tide.

His gaze was firm, as if he had already aimed for victory.

It was not just for the 500 points rewarded by the System after defeating a Jonin, or the 3,000 points rewarded for winning the third stage assessment.

It was even more because of the grudge he and Yan from Sunagakure had forged earlier.

At this moment, it was time for a reckoning !

« Petty tricks, I have long seen through this move ! »

Yan sneered. The effect of Magnet Release : Wings of Iron Sand was precisely the condensation of a pair of huge and flexible wings of Iron Sand on his back.

This technique not only possessed the ability to fly but also combined mobility, attack power, and defense.

The wings formed by Iron Sand could shoot out Iron Sand black feathers, tracking and piercing the enemy’s body at a speed difficult for the naked eye to capture.

When facing an enemy’s attack, the two huge wings could also transform into an iron curtain to resist all attacks.

The shield formed by Iron Sand was much harder than ordinary Earth Release Ninjutsu.

Riding on the giant wave, Senju Matsuaki’s eyes reflected Yan’s figure soaring in mid-air.

Indeed, flying in the sky avoided the impact of the Exploding Water Colliding Wave on the terrain to the greatest extent.

But if one thought his purpose in using Water Release was merely to change the terrain, it would be a bit arrogant.

Whether it was Exploding Water Colliding Wave or its advanced version, Great Exploding Water Colliding Wave, its true power did not lie in itself, but in its combination with other techniques.

Classic examples include Senju Tobirama using Great Exploding Water Colliding Wave combined with Mutually Multiplying Explosive Tags, and Kisame Hoshigaki using this technique to perform a Fusion form with Samehada.

Of course, Matsuaki didn’t know these two methods.

What he wanted to do was very simple : let this giant wave, which was enough to cover a mountain, become the best medium for conducting power.

In his memory, that maritime overlord nicknamed « Whitebeard » could use the power of the Tremor-Tremor Fruit to stir up a tsunami.

Now, Senju Matsuaki wanted to do the same thing.

The next instant, from Yan’s perspective, he only saw Senju Matsuaki’s body quickly merge into the giant wave, turning into a blurred black shadow.

« Playing tricks. »

While disdainful in his mouth, he felt a sense of vigilance in his heart.

The Magnet Release : Wings of Iron Sand on his back shook violently, and countless black feathers made of Iron Sand shot towards the giant wave.

In his view, the razor-sharp Iron Sand black feathers would cut through this seemingly voluminous but weak water surface like a blade cutting through tofu.

However, just as the Iron Sand black feathers were about to pierce the giant wave and skewer the body hidden in the water...

Something seemed to have changed.

In the eyes of Yan from Sunagakure and countless spectators.

In an instant, the water and sky reversed, and the giant waves surged to the heavens ! !

The water filling the exam arena trembled suddenly, and an invisible shockwave exploded !

The giant waves broke away from their original forward surging trend, suddenly rolling back and climbing towards the sky, with the water flow sweeping wildly upwards.

At the same time, the ground of the exam arena roared and cracked, especially the solid walls that marked the boundary between the exam arena and the spectator stands, which even had cracks climbing up them !

« This... »

On the spectator stand, Senju Tobirama opened his mouth, watching this scene in disbelief.

Exploding Water Colliding Wave was his signature Ninjutsu, and combined with his proficient Mutually Multiplying Explosive Tags, it was invincible and had defeated countless enemies.

But no matter how familiar he was with this technique, he couldn’t see how it could produce tsunami-like power.

No, using a tsunami to describe it seemed a bit mild.

This was clearly an apocalyptic scene unfolding in this space within the exam arena !

Although he was shocked and curious, he did not forget his official business.

« Reinforce the walls, don’t let the spectator stands be affected ! » Senju Tobirama commanded the Proctors waiting on the side.

As the words fell, many Proctors quickly took their positions, striving to minimize the impact of the commotion in the arena on the spectator stands.

On the ground.

When the water flow swept into the air, it naturally formed an open space on the ground.

At this time, Senju Matsuaki stood on the ground, his hands still maintaining the posture of stretching out to the sides.

On his fists, a faint white light was gradually fading, and the ripples of space vibration were still clearly visible around them.

Relying on the power of vibration spreading from his body as the center to the surroundings, the surrounding turbulent currents could not get close to him.

After obtaining the fruit’s ability, this was his first time using it with full power.

The specific feeling could only be summarized in one word...

Awesome !
