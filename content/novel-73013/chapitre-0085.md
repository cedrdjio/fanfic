# Chapitre 85 — The Finish Line, Ambush! A Powerful Breakthrough!

As afternoon approached, Senju Matsuaki and Hyuga Tsukuyomi finally neared the edge of the primeval forest.

Since leaving the cabin, they had encountered candidates from other Ninja villages as well as ferocious beasts native to the forest, resulting in numerous battles along the way.

« About four hundred meters ahead, there are two teams in ambush ; one group has seven people, the other has five ! »

Veins bulged near Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s temples. While maintaining her high-speed sprint, the rise and fall of her chest caused by her breathing remained steady.

Within the visual range of her Byakugan, she could see the efforts these competitors had made to conceal themselves in the distance.

They hid their bodies in the branches or grass, or used cloaks with colors similar to the trees and soil for cover.

These people were Ninjas who had undergone systematic training in major Ninja villages, so their abilities in this regard were unquestionable.

Unfortunately, before the insight of the Byakugan, they were riddled with flaws and had nowhere to hide, appearing just like a joke.

« Should we go around, or... »

« Don’t worry about them ; just keep moving along the original route. »

The division of labor between the two was clear : one focused on detection and sensing, while the other focused on combat.

The distance of about four hundred meters quickly shrank to one hundred, then fifty, at the speed of a Ninja...

Just as the two stepped into the attack range of these lurkers, the Ninjas hiding everywhere simultaneously revealed ferocious glares.

« Fire Release : Searing Migraine ! ! »

« Wind Release : Vacuum Bullets ! ! »

« Earth Release : Headhunter Jutsu ! ! »

Whoosh—

Ninjutsu, Kunai, and shuriken flew toward them, ruthlessly aiming for the vital points of Senju Matsuaki and Hyuga Tsukuyomi.

« Tsukuyomi, come closer to me. »

While performing hand seals, Senju Matsuaki did not forget to instruct Hyuga Tsukuyomi beside him.

The tacit understanding between teammates meant Hyuga Tsukuyomi did not hesitate ; her running body shifted slightly to the right, closing the distance between them.

At the same time, Senju Matsuaki finished his hand seals.

« Water Release : Water Colliding Wave ! ! »

Vast amounts of Chakra erupted after being guided, and the surrounding air seemed to become thicker as a result.

Rapid currents of water quickly condensed around Senju Matsuaki, transforming into a fierce water tornado that spiraled into the sky, shielding the two within it.

The fireballs, Vacuum Bullets, Kunai, shuriken, and other projectiles approaching the two had their arrogant momentum extinguished the moment they collided with the rising water tornado.

The Ninjutsu dissipated as if it had never existed, and even the smoke raised by the fireballs was instantly swallowed by the water tornado.

As for the Kunai and shuriken, they were carried away by the rising water tornado, turning into several tiny black dots within it.

« Run ! Run ! ! »

« Get out of here ! ! »

Seeing this unbelievable scene, the Ninjas who had been lying in ambush finally realized something was wrong.

The few Kirigakure Ninjas who had been waiting for their sneak attack to succeed so they could move in for the kill immediately turned and fled.

However, in the face of such a large-scale Ninjutsu, running on foot was already of no use.

In an instant, the water tornado spread toward these people, and the raging waves swept one figure after another into it, sending them all into the sky.

An Iwagakure Ninja who had just used the Headhunter Jutsu, even while hiding underground, was dragged out of the ground without any resistance and fell into the Water Colliding Wave along with the others.

« Seven Kirigakure Ninjas, five Iwagakure Ninjas—trying to ambush people a short distance before the finish line ? »

Located at the center of the water tornado, Senju Matsuaki remained completely dry, his red eyes reflecting the surging water currents.

After the distance closed, he also clearly saw the identities of the two groups lying in ambush.

Kirigakure Ninjas and Iwagakure Ninjas—he didn’t know if they had reached some agreement, or if they simply hadn’t discovered each other.

These two groups were each lying in ambush somewhere near the exit, simultaneously attacking the candidates who passed by.

No matter what route they took, they all needed to reach the same finish line in the end, so this was a path every candidate had to pass through.

Lying in ambush here could save a lot of time spent searching for targets ; it was indeed a good choice.

Unfortunately, they chose the wrong target.

« These guys really lack judgment. »

At the finish line, a candidate who could vaguely see what was happening in the distance sneered.

He was absolutely certain that after he and Sunagakure’s Yan reached the finish line, the strongest among the remaining candidates was Konoha’s Senju Matsuaki.

And this strength was on a completely different level.

Of all the people these ambushers could have chosen to target, they just had to pick such a tough nut to crack.

Beside him, Yan, who usually didn’t show much expression, couldn’t help but twitch the corner of his mouth slightly.

Having passed the exam himself, he had been guarding outside the finish line, monitoring the progress of the subsequent examination.

Just a moment ago, these two groups had arrived near the finish line almost simultaneously and had nearly broken into a fight.

However, it seemed that after weighing their options, they both concluded that the other side had too many people to easily take down, so both sides tacitly chose to lie in ambush there, waiting for the candidates who would arrive next.

If these two groups of Kirigakure and Iwagakure Ninjas had waited for their Sunagakure Ninjas, they probably would have succeeded by now, but unfortunately, they chose the wrong target.

Sure enough, choice is more important than effort.

Looking at the raging water tornado in the distance, Yan’s eyes narrowed.

« This Konoha brat has a terrifying amount of Chakra. »

Dou, standing beside him, saw the slightly grave expression on Yan’s face and asked curiously, « What, do you lack confidence ? »

The last time he visited Konoha with the Kumogakure delegation, he had fought Senju Matsuaki.

And in the primeval forest, because he and Yan were assigned to the same starting point, the two had clashed right at the beginning of the exam.

To be honest, he didn’t think Senju Matsuaki would be a match for Yan.

In Dou’s view, Senju Matsuaki had great talent, sure, but the time for that talent to manifest as actual strength was in the future.

Whereas Sunagakure’s Yan was already an up-and-coming Magnet Release Ninja ; his black Iron Sand could both attack and defend, and in their brief clash, he couldn’t help but feel some pressure.

« Heh, Ninjas aren’t judged solely by the amount of Chakra they possess. »

Hearing Dou’s questioning tone, Yan just sneered and then said no more.

However, deep down, he secretly felt a hint of vigilance toward this Kumogakure Ninja before him who seemed like a big oaf.

The question just now seemed like simple curiosity, but upon closer inspection, it might not have been without the intention of probing his situation.

These big oafs from Kumogakure look simple-minded, but they actually have plenty of schemes !

However, the two were relatively consistent in their views on Senju Matsuaki.

Although they acknowledged Senju Matsuaki’s talent and strength, they didn’t think he would be a match for them.

No matter how large the amount of Chakra, it was useless without corresponding jutsu to support it.

It was like the relationship between ammunition and firearms ; without ammunition, a firearm was probably no different from an iron rod.

But if one only had a few crates of ammunition without any advanced firearms to match them, the ammunition would only become decorations.

Dou had honed his strong body through countless arduous training sessions and endured the stinging pain of lightning to master the Lightning Release Chakra Mode.

Yan’s Magnet Release was also gradually mastered through day-after-day development and actual combat, not just by coasting on the gifts of his Bloodline Limit.

Strong individuals who could make a name for themselves in the Ninja World all had their own « special skills » to some extent.

Some Ninjas carried forward their Bloodline Limits or Family secret techniques, while others used certain types of Ninjutsu to perfection, possessing their own unique insights.

In the end, Ninjas rely on strength to speak.

To be looked at as an equal by them, one had to reveal trump cards that would make them wary.
