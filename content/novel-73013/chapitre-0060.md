# Chapitre 60 — Hidden Mist and Iwagakure Protest!

« This Ninjutsu developed by that monkey is quite useful. »

Senju Hashirama watched the clear image on the crystal ball and whispered his praise to Senju Tobirama beside him.

« You have to look at who trained that student~ »

Hearing Senju Hashirama’s words, a flash of pride crossed Senju Tobirama’s eyes, but he humbly replied, « That monkey still has a long way to go. »

This Telescope Technique was developed by Hiruzen, who had even consulted him when encountering problems during its development.

The Telescope Technique was exceptionally effective for Ninja reconnaissance, so Senju Tobirama had praised Hiruzen’s idea highly at the time.

However, when asked about the motivation for developing this Ninjutsu, the other party had been vague, which left him quite puzzled.

After understanding the principle of this Ninjutsu, Senju Tobirama was quickly able to use it with ease.

Although the Telescope Technique was clever, for Senju Tobirama, it was just a small gadget.

After all, Senju Tobirama’s research topics already involved space-time and resurrection ; they were on a completely different level from other Ninjas.

At this moment, Mu suddenly noticed something wrong and frowned as he looked at A from the Hidden Cloud Village side.

« Hey, Hidden Cloud Village, where is your little sidekick ? »

He had paid attention to the important personnel of the other Ninja villages and also understood the personnel composition of the Hidden Cloud Village.

Similar to the current Iwagakure, the First Kage was accompanied by Ninjas responsible for support and guard duties, being cultivated as the next generation of Kage.

The designated next-generation Kage were not idle either ; they were basically accompanied by their own direct disciples, who would undoubtedly become the future Sandaime of their respective villages.

Ōnoki, who was by Mu’s side, was his direct disciple and had been through many battles with him.

Mu remembered that in recent years, there had been a young man by the side of the Hidden Cloud Village’s A who was as sturdy as an ox despite his young age, and he was already a Jonin.

Logically, he should have been here watching the exams as well.

« Him ? He’s right here. »

A glanced at Mu and tilted his chin forward toward the crystal ball on the table.

Seeing this gesture, those present who were unaware were stunned, while those in the know all revealed strange expressions.

« What ! ? »

Hōzuki Gengetsu suddenly slammed the table and stood up abruptly, pointing his finger at the image reflected in the crystal ball.

The image provided by the Telescope Technique was very clear ; for them, it was basically like being on the scene.

Inside the crystal ball, a sturdy man with a build clearly larger than those around him stood in the crowd with his arms crossed, looking around.

« Organizers, I want to file a complaint ! I remember this guy is a Jonin, right ? Why has he become a candidate for the Chunin Exams now ! ? » Hōzuki Gengetsu rebuked angrily.

« That’s right, this doesn’t follow the rules, does it ? »

Beside him, Iwagakure’s Mu rarely opposed his arch-rival and also spoke up to question it.

After speaking, he looked at the group from Sunagakure who were maintaining an eerie silence, wanting to pull them onto his side.

« People of Sunagakure, don’t you think this situation is absurd ? »

« Uh, it’s alright, I guess. »

Shamon, not knowing if it was out of a guilty conscience or what, didn’t take his eyes off the crystal ball and replied indifferently.

« Teacher, look here... »

Just then, Ōnoki pulled on Mu’s sleeve while pointing his finger at the Sunagakure candidate in the crystal ball’s image.

Within the dozens of Sunagakure candidates, a cold-faced young man stood silently, while the candidates from his village looked at him with awe from time to time.

The genius youth of Sunagakure, the Magnet Release Ninja, Yan.

Since he began to stand out in the Ninja World with his Magnet Release Ninjutsu, the information on this Sunagakure rising star had been placed on the desks of the Kage of all major Ninja villages.

« Hey ! How come you guys too ! »

The target of Hōzuki Gengetsu and Mu’s condemnation instantly went from one to two.

Knowing they were in the wrong, the Hidden Cloud and Sunagakure didn’t say much and turned a deaf ear to the two’s condemnation.

« Alright, everyone be quiet ! »

Seeing that the other four major Ninja villages in the conference hall were about to start a dispute, Senju Tobirama quickly stood up to stop them.

« As the host of this Joint Chunin Exams, Konoha does not participate in nor advocate for this behavior. »

Senju Tobirama first stated Konoha’s position as the organizer, and also indicated to the two villages that Konoha had no similar behavior occurring.

Immediately, he changed the subject.

« Regarding the behavior of the Hidden Cloud and Sunagakure, we do not advocate it, but objectively speaking, this behavior did not violate the exam rules we formulated together at the time. »

The implication was that the exam rules were negotiated and formulated by our Five Great Ninja Villages together, so don’t blame us, the organizers, now that someone has found a loophole.

« Despicable, shameless ! »

Hōzuki Gengetsu and Mu could only sit back in their seats with expressions of indignation.

Mu even glanced at his direct disciple, Ōnoki, secretly hating in his heart that he didn’t have as many tricks up his sleeve as the Hidden Cloud and Sunagakure.

Otherwise, if he had let Ōnoki also demote himself to Genin to participate in the Chunin Exams, wouldn’t he be crushing everyone with his current strength ?

However, as Senju Tobirama said, the behavior of the Hidden Cloud and Sunagakure did not violate the exam rules ; it was at most just morally questionable.

...

On the other side, inside the Ninja Academy classroom.

The first stage of the Chunin Exams was a written test, so the exam venue naturally needed desks and chairs for writing.

In all of Konoha, only the Ninja Academy could accommodate so many people to participate in the written test.

Therefore, in order to hold the first stage of the Chunin Exams, Konoha specially gave the students of the Ninja Academy a few days off.

At this time, everyone was seated according to their assigned exam venues.

Senju Matsuaki sat in the familiar classroom, and he couldn’t help but feel some emotion.

This classroom was not the one he had been in when he attended the Ninja Academy, but the layout of each classroom was actually roughly the same.

The exam had not yet started, and the written test papers had not yet been distributed.

Looking around, he discovered that there were Genin from various Ninja villages present ; it seemed that the exam venue had randomly shuffled and distributed the candidates from the Five Great Ninja Villages.

In this exam venue, except for the Konoha candidates he had some slight impression of, he was seeing everyone else for the first time.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi was not assigned to the same exam venue as him ; instead, Hiruzen’s goddess, Biwako, was sitting near him.

It had been a few months since Hiruzen came to ask for advice on relationship problems, and he didn’t know how the situation was currently.

In addition, what also caught Senju Matsuaki’s attention was a graceful figure in front.

This figure had shoulder-length light purple hair and looked to be around the same age as Hiruzen and the others.

Although she was just a young girl, her figure was already not inferior to an adult’s, causing the surrounding young men to turn their heads and look from time to time.

Soon, the Proctor for this exam venue walked in from outside the room.

« Hello everyone, I am the Proctor in charge of your exam venue... »

He wore a pineapple-style hairstyle, and his face carried an expression as if he were forcing himself to stay awake after just waking up.

On the way into the exam venue, he couldn’t help but turn his head and covertly yawned.
