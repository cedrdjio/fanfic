# Chapitre 3 — The Senju Family

After Uchiha Kai left, the strange atmosphere in the classroom gradually dissipated.

For most, what just happened was merely a minor interlude.

Even though Uchiha Kai had just cast a wide net with his insults, everyone had more pressing matters to worry about ; who had the time to dwell on it ?

Thus, accompanied by the sound of chatter, the others left the classroom in small groups.

The once lively classroom suddenly appeared empty.

Standing beside Senju Matsuaki, Hyuga Tsukuyomi looked apologetic and whispered, « Matsuaki-kun, I’m sorry. »

She felt that what had just happened was her fault.

Because of her own cowardice, she had caused him to have to risk a confrontation.

Upon hearing this, Senju Matsuaki helplessly reached out and patted Hyuga Tsukuyomi on the head.

« You really just lack too much self-confidence. »

Hyuga Tsukuyomi wore her blue-black hair long, and combined with her somewhat introverted personality, she looked seven or eight parts similar to the future Hyuga Hinata, as if she were a miniature version of Hyuga Hinata with long hair.

However, this was quite reasonable.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s eldest brother was the newly appointed head of the Hyuga Clan, and this Hyuga Clan head would have two children in the future : Hyuga Hiashi and Hyuga Hizashi.

In terms of bloodline, Hyuga Tsukuyomi and Hyuga Hinata were already very close.

And there was naturally a reason why Senju Matsuaki said this.

He could tell that during the usual combat training at the Ninja Academy, Hyuga Tsukuyomi performed quite well ; the training from the Hyuga Clan had laid a good foundation for her.

It was just that in actual combat, because the teacher emphasized that they should stop at a certain point, Hyuga Tsukuyomi had obediently refrained from unleashing her true strength.

But the crux of the problem still lay in the fact that Hyuga Tsukuyomi lacked self-confidence.

Playing the pig to eat the tiger is fine, but one must not play the part until they actually believe they are a pig.

« But Matsuaki-kun, will it really be okay ? »

Hyuga Tsukuyomi lowered her head, feeling somewhat embarrassed, yet her face still showed worry.

« I heard from my eldest brother that Uchiha Kai is one of the most talented people in the Uchiha Clan in recent years. His grandfather is an elder of the Uchiha Clan, and his brother is also an elite of the Uchiha Clan, who has kept him training rigorously. »

Upon hearing this, Senju Matsuaki was not worried at all ; instead, he smiled and gave the girl a thumbs up, saying, « Don’t worry, I’m very strong too. »

Seemingly infected by his optimistic mood, the worry between Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s brows dissipated a little.

However, she still said with some unease, « If there is anything you need help with, Matsuaki-kun, you can come to me. »

It was getting late, and after chatting for a few moments, the two said their goodbyes and headed home.

Senju Matsuaki currently lived in the Senju Clan’s compound, located very close to the homes of Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama.

When he was younger, Senju Hashirama had adopted him, and he grew up under the care of clan members who lacked Ninja talent and worked as nannies.

By the time he was three or four and capable of taking care of himself, he moved back to the residence left behind by his parents.

However, he would occasionally go to Senju Hashirama’s house for a free meal, like today.

As he got closer to the Senju Clan’s territory, he encountered more and more Senju Clan members along the way, all of whom greeted Senju Matsuaki quite warmly.

After the establishment of Konoha, members of the Senju Clan spread into various industries.

For example, in the Ninja Academy, nearly half of the teachers were now members of the Senju Clan.

These people were all there to support Konoha’s educational endeavors, nurturing the next generation of talent for Konoha.

Soon, Senju Matsuaki arrived at Senju Hashirama’s residence.

This traditional residence featured a natural wood color scheme, and the wooden structure with low-hanging eaves exuded a gentle texture refined by the passage of time.

The dry landscape garden in the center of the courtyard was covered with moss and gravel, and branches of a maple tree by the house reached into the veranda.

Seeing Senju Matsuaki arrive, Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama, who were sitting inside the house, stopped their conversation.

In their daily lives, Hashirama and Tobirama did not wear their iconic red and blue battle armor, but rather more casual attire.

Besides Hashirama and Tobirama, there was also a dignified woman with red hair, and a sturdy-looking young man in his early twenties present.

The dignified woman was Senju Hashirama’s wife, Uzumaki Mito.

The young man was Senju Hashirama and Uzumaki Mito’s son, named Senju Kazuma.

« Uncle, Second Uncle, Aunt, Brother Kazuma ! » Senju Matsuaki greeted his relatives in the house with a smile.

« Ah, little Matsuaki is back. How was it at the Ninja Academy today ? » Senju Hashirama asked happily.

Most of the time, Senju Hashirama did not carry the dignity of a God of Shinobi or the Hokage ; he was very gentle and had no airs.

Sometimes, he even appeared a bit simple and honest.

When Senju Tobirama saw Senju Matsuaki, his usually stern face softened slightly.

The Senju Clan members' appearances were quite diverse ; back then, the four brothers Hashirama, Tobirama, Kawarama, and Itama all looked different.

Senju Matsuaki had inherited his father’s hair color and his mother’s eye color, so his silver hair and red eyes actually made him look quite similar to Senju Tobirama.

Because of this, Tobirama had always placed high hopes on his nephew.

Matsuaki found a seat and replied, « The teacher announced today that we could take the graduation exam early, and I plan to submit my graduation application tomorrow. »

Upon hearing this, Hashirama, Tobirama, and the others did not react much.

In their era, there was no such thing as a Ninja Academy.

After some systematic training within the clan, as long as there was a shortage of manpower on the battlefield, one had to put on armor and take up a Kunai to head to the battlefield.

Even though Konoha had now established a Ninja Academy, the older generation of Ninjas had not formed the notion that one must complete a certain number of years of schooling to graduate.

Although it was relatively peaceful now and the policy for early graduation had been tightened overall, they did not reject those who were truly talented from graduating early.

However, Uzumaki Mito was a bit worried : « Little Matsuaki, wouldn’t that be a bit too early ? After all, he has only just been enrolled for one year. »

« It’s fine, Mother, » Senju Kazuma, who was to the side, said with a smile. « Don’t underestimate Matsuaki ; he has already reached the level of a Genin. »

Hearing Senju Kazuma say this, Uzumaki Mito, knowing that Senju Kazuma occasionally guided Matsuaki’s training, finally felt relieved and nodded.

Thinking of a joyous event in the Senju Clan recently, Matsuaki looked at Senju Kazuma and said with a smile.

« By the way, I heard the news of Brother Kazuma’s engagement yesterday, and I haven’t had a chance to congratulate you yet. »

Senju Kazuma scratched his head somewhat embarrassedly, and a slightly shy smile appeared on his masculine face : « Ah haha, this news has already spread, huh. »

This happy event for the Senju Clan was Senju Kazuma’s engagement to the daughter of the Daimyo of the Land of Fire.

Senju Kazuma had met this princess of the Land of Fire during one of the Senju Clan’s visits to the Land of Fire’s Daimyo, and the two had fallen in love.

Since it was mutual affection, and it could also make the relationship between Konoha and the Land of Fire closer and more harmonious, both the Senju Clan and the Daimyo strongly supported this marriage.

Furthermore, Senju Kazuma himself did not like to fight, and his talent in the way of the Ninja was relatively mediocre, far inferior to his father.

Therefore, after the engagement, he would move to the Daimyo’s mansion to live with his wife.
