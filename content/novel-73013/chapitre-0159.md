# Chapitre 159 — Kirby's Susanoo!

The Eternal Mangekyo Sharingan pattern in Uchiha Madara’s eyes spun wildly.

The next instant, a massive amount of Chakra erupted from his body like a physical force, causing the surrounding space to begin to distort slightly.

« Susanoo ! »

Accompanied by a roar, deep blue Chakra shot into the sky like boiling flames, instantly condensing into a massive skeletal frame, followed by layers of flesh and armor covering it.

In the blink of an eye, a hundred-meter-tall giant with wings on its back stood in the air, wielding a massive katana and exuding a suffocating sense of oppression.

« That is... the Complete Body Susanoo. »

Senju Tobirama frowned slightly.

Eternal Mangekyo Sharingan—although this evil power was not something he was seeing for the first time, it still sent a shiver down his spine.

Beside him, Uchiha Hikari was about to activate her own Susanoo to meet the challenge, but was stopped by Senju Matsuaki, who patted her on the shoulder.

He revealed a faint smile at the corner of his mouth, signaling Uchiha Hikari not to be anxious.

« Hmph, you are nothing but a clumsy imitator. You know nothing of the power of these eyes ! »

Uchiha Madara stood at the center of Susanoo’s brow, looking down from above at the tiny Kirby below, his eyes filled with an arrogance that looked down upon the world.

« Let me see if you, monster, can block this strike ! »

As his voice fell, the massive katana, carrying a piercing shriek that tore through the air, slashed across the sky and came crashing down directly towards Kirby !

The power contained in this strike was enough to raze a small mountain to the ground !

Facing this heaven-destroying and earth-shattering attack, Kirby showed no intention of dodging.

Its round body crouched slightly, and its deep blue eyes stared straight at the approaching katana, as if evaluating something.

Just a moment before the giant sword was about to touch its head, Kirby suddenly moved.

It did not strike upwards to meet the attack, nor did it dodge backwards ; instead, it leaped upwards abruptly.

Its small body, draped in red layered armor, traced an arc in the air, and immediately after, it began to plummet rapidly.

During the descent, Kirby’s figure suddenly changed.

Its pink body rapidly hardened and changed color, and in the blink of an eye, it transformed into an unremarkable grey stone, slamming heavily into the ground.

Boom ! ! !

Uchiha Madara’s Susanoo giant sword slashed fiercely at the position where Kirby had just been.

The earth trembled violently, a terrifying fissure spread in all directions, and dust and smoke shot up into the sky.

A few seconds later, the dust and smoke slowly dissipated. The grey stone lay quietly in the center of the crater, its surface smooth, without even a single crack.

« What ? ! »

The arrogant smile on Uchiha Madara’s face froze.

He widened his eyes, looking in disbelief at the unharmed stone.

The strike he had delivered with his Susanoo at full power should have been able to easily cleave even a mountain in two.

Yet at this moment, it couldn’t even shatter a single stone ?

Under the astonished gaze of everyone, the stone began to sway slightly, and then, a soft light radiated from its surface.

After the light dissipated, Kirby reappeared before everyone’s eyes, completely unscathed, even patting off non-existent dust from itself, looking relaxed and comfortable.

« Hi-ya~ »

With an innocent smile on its face, Kirby waved its short pink arm towards Senju Matsuaki.

Senju Matsuaki also smiled and waved back.

« This... how is this possible ? »

Senju Hashirama muttered to himself, feeling his worldview receive an unprecedented shock.

That was a full-power strike from Susanoo. As an old rival, he knew best the power of this strike from Uchiha Madara.

Logically speaking, even a rock hardened by Earth Release should have been instantly shattered.

On the other side of the battlefield, Uchiha Madara’s expression had darkened to the extreme.

He stared fixedly at that unscathed pink creature, the shock and anger in his heart intertwined, almost snapping the strings of his sanity.

What exactly is this pink monster ? !

Uchiha Madara gritted his teeth, the Eternal Mangekyo Sharingan in his eyes spinning crazily.

« Since that’s the case, let me show you the true gap in power ! »

However, before he could launch another attack, there was movement on Kirby’s side.

Kirby’s deep blue eyes showed three pitch-black tomoe beginning to spin.

Under the stunned gaze of everyone, the speed of the tomoe’s rotation grew faster and faster, and the pattern kept changing.

Finally, it settled into the exact same Eternal Mangekyo Sharingan pattern as Uchiha Madara’s !

« It... it also activated the Eternal Mangekyo Sharingan ? ! »

Senju Hashirama exclaimed.

Immediately after, an even more shocking scene unfolded.

Deep blue Chakra surged into the sky from Kirby’s body, its Aura terrifying.

The Chakra condensed and took shape, rapidly constructing a strange, cartoon-style Susanoo silhouette behind Kirby.

Unlike Uchiha Madara’s mighty, domineering, and realistic-style Susanoo, Kirby’s Susanoo appeared particularly... distinct.

Its lines were round and cute, and its colors were bright and vivid, as if it had stepped out of an animation.

Perhaps influenced by Kirby’s own height, the height of its Susanoo only reached the knees of Uchiha Madara’s Susanoo.

The weapon it held was not the murderous katana of Uchiha Madara’s Susanoo, but a cartoon-style short sword, the whole thing exuding an inexplicable sense of hilarity.

« Pfft... »

Uchiha Hikari couldn’t help but let out a soft chuckle, but quickly realized the situation was inappropriate and hurriedly covered her mouth.

The usually serious Senju Tobirama also had his mouth corner twitch uncontrollably.

« You ugly thing ! »

Uchiha Madara was furious, feeling he had been greatly insulted.

Susanoo is the Uchiha’s third power, the power of gods !

And the thing this monster created was simply a blasphemy against Susanoo !

« A cartoon version of Susanoo... »

Senju Matsuaki stroked his chin, « Although the art style is unique, the Aura is quite sufficient. »

Senju Hashirama laughed out loud : « Madara ! Your Susanoo isn’t as cute as Kirby’s picture-book style Susanoo ! »

At this moment, Kirby’s cartoon Susanoo suddenly flapped its wings and flew forward.

The short sword in its hand was raised high, aimed in Uchiha Madara’s direction, and then swung down with force !

« Hi-ya ! »

A massive blue Sword Qi tore through the air, slashing towards Uchiha Madara’s Susanoo.

« Mere child’s play ! »

Uchiha Madara snorted coldly, controlling his own Susanoo to raise its blade to counter.

Boom— ! ! !

Two massive Sword Qi collided in the air, erupting with an earth-shattering roar.

The aftermath swept in all directions, blowing so hard that Senju Hashirama and the others in the distance couldn’t help but squint their eyes.

To everyone’s shock, Kirby’s Susanoo actually managed to block Uchiha Madara’s attack head-on !

The two forces reached a stalemate in the air, the two deep blue Sword Qi colliding and eroding each other, eventually annihilating together in the air.

Evenly matched !

« This... »

Senju Hashirama was completely speechless.

The Susanoo copied by that seemingly harmless pink creature actually possessed the power to go head-to-head with Uchiha Madara !

Uchiha Madara’s expression became incredibly ugly.

He had originally thought that the opponent’s Eternal Mangekyo Sharingan was just a fake, and even if it could copy abilities, it would never be able to reach the same level as his own.

But now it seemed that this monster had not only copied his abilities, but the power it unleashed was not even slightly inferior to his own !
