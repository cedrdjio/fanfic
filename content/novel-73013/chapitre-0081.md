# Chapitre 81 — Wood Release: Four-Pillar House!

By a stream in a primeval forest, a Cloud Ninja was clashing with a Rock Ninja in a battle of Ninjutsu.

Violent lightning collided with heavy rock fists, kicking up wisps of smoke.

The two were evenly matched, resulting in a stalemate.

Just then, a pillar of water suddenly erupted into the sky in the distance and crashed down toward their location.

Seeing this, the Cloud Ninja and the Rock Ninja turned back, their faces filled with confusion and terror.

The next instant, the two were like clothes caught in a spinning washing machine, their bodies losing balance in the vortex like ragdolls.

Eventually, the water dissipated, leaving only their battered, overlapping bodies collapsed on the ground.

Just as they struggled to get up, they realized a blade and a palm were held against their heads respectively.

Beside the Rock Ninja, Hyuga Tsukuyomi stood with her Byakugan filled with dignity, her Gentle Fist ready to strike.

In front of the Cloud Ninja, Senju Matsuaki pointed the tip of his blade at his throat, ready to kill at the slightest movement.

« Hand over your chess pieces, » Senju Matsuaki said coldly.

Hearing this, the Cloud Ninja and the Rock Ninja, who had been opponents just moments ago, exchanged a glance, both seeing the grievance and bitterness in the other’s eyes.

However, the situation left them no choice. To avoid having their heads cracked open by the Gentle Fist or their throats sliced by the short blade, they silently took out the chess pieces they had been defending with all their might.

The one from the Cloud Ninja had the character « Jade » written on it, while the one from the Rock Ninja was a blank chess piece.

Seeing this, Senju Matsuaki snatched the two chess pieces and looked at them with disappointment.

« I saw your Ninjutsu exchanges earlier, and they were decent enough, but you’re this pathetic when it comes to handing over the pieces ? »

« You both disappoint me. »

Senju Matsuaki sighed, sheathed his short blade, and massaged his right wrist with his left hand.

Several minutes later, the Cloud Ninja lay on the ground, face swollen and bruised, clutching his face, while the Rock Ninja beside him lay trembling on the ground, not daring to make a sound.

« Wait, why am I the only one getting beaten up ! ? » the Cloud Ninja cried out in grief and indignation.

« And if I recall correctly, the piece I gave you was more valuable, right ! ? »

Hearing this, Senju Matsuaki couldn’t help but feel a little embarrassed.

But thinking of the two Ninjutsu scrolls currently lying quietly on his bookshelf back in his room, his expression hardened again.

Take money, do the job.

Since he had accepted the Ninjutsu scrolls from Hiruzen Sarutobi and Danzo Shimura, if he didn’t beat up the Cloud and Sand Ninjas as requested, how could he show his face in the future ?

Beside him, Hyuga Tsukuyomi couldn’t help but cover her mouth and giggle.

She had been present at the scene of the « transaction » between Senju Matsuaki, Hiruzen Sarutobi, and Danzo Shimura, so she knew the whole story.

After putting away the jade chess piece, Senju Matsuaki casually tossed the blank piece toward the two fallen Cloud and Rock Ninjas.

[Current Progress : 21 chess pieces captured, 36 examinees defeated.]

This was the completion status of his two current tasks.

One jade chess piece was worth 100 points, and defeating an examinee was worth 50 points.

By this calculation, he had already earned nearly four thousand points in the second stage of the Chunin Exams.

It could be called an unprecedented harvest.

Seeing this, Senju Matsuaki smiled with satisfaction, his steps becoming a bit lighter.

He looked up at the sky and saw the red glow of sunset spreading across the horizon.

Seeing that it was getting late, Senju Matsuaki suggested, « It’s getting late. Let’s find a place to rest for the night, and tomorrow we’ll head straight for the finish line. »

Today was already the third day of the second stage of the exam.

Based on the average travel speed, most people who could pass the exam would arrive at the finish line around the fifth day.

Of course, one couldn’t rule out certain Ninjas with secret techniques or Bloodline Limits.

For example, Hikaru from Sunagakure could use Magnet Release to move quickly, shortening the five-day journey by several times.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi agreed with the proposal and activated her Byakugan to look for a suitable spot to camp.

They were currently near a stream, and it wouldn’t be difficult to find a suitable campsite by following the water.

« There’s a hidden clearing in the two o’clock direction ; it’s the most suitable spot nearby. »

« Then I’ll leave the leading to you, Tsukuyomi. »

The two passed through layers of ancient tree canopies, and the trees ahead suddenly thinned out, opening up their view.

A stream with abundant water, foaming with clear white spray, rushed down, crashing against the rock walls.

Beside the stream, moss covered the ground and the gnarled, exposed tree roots.

Several grey-black boulders, their edges smoothed by the stream water, were half-buried by the water’s edge, looking like natural benches for resting.

Both Senju Matsuaki and Hyuga Tsukuyomi were satisfied with the environment.

« Multiple Shadow Clone Jutsu ! ! »

Senju Matsuaki formed hand seals, and two Shadow Clones appeared beside him amidst a puff of smoke.

After the original and the Shadow Clones exchanged a look, the Shadow Clones immediately understood ; one ran toward the stream, while the other walked toward the forest away from the stream.

Matsuaki himself intended to set up the camp for the night right where he was.

« The area near this rock is suitable for resting, and the person on night watch can stay by that large tree ; if there’s any anomaly, they can spot it immediately. »

Relying on the Byakugan’s perspective function and over half a year of practical experience since becoming a Genin, Hyuga Tsukuyomi quickly figured out the layout.

For Ninjas, resting in the wild isn’t about comfort.

In the forest, one can rely on tree branches and leaves as a natural barrier. If the weather is clear, one only needs to sit against a tree and close their eyes to rest.

Rainy weather is much more troublesome ; one must either find a cave suitable for shelter from the rain or collect branches, vines, and even mud to simply construct a camp.

Out of vigilance, if on a solo mission, one would set traps around the resting area.

If on a multi-person mission, they would take turns keeping watch to ensure safety.

However, Senju Matsuaki said, « There is a new Ninjutsu I want to try. If the effect is good, we won’t need to specifically keep watch. »

« A new Ninjutsu ? » Hyuga Tsukuyomi looked over, curious.

At the same time, Senju Matsuaki had already formed hand seals.

Snake—Dog—Ox—Snake—Snake !

« Wood Release : Four-Pillar House ! ! »

After the five seals, a burst of Chakra filled with immense vitality erupted from his body, and the previously silent ground suddenly began to tremble slightly.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi, who was standing nearby, reacted quickly and focused Chakra into the soles of her feet, which allowed her to maintain her balance.

« This Chakra... » Feeling the vibrant aura of Chakra not far away, Hyuga Tsukuyomi was slightly stunned.

Back when Senju Matsuaki came to rescue her and Biwako, she had felt that his Chakra seemed a bit different from before.

But after Senju Matsuaki switched to using Earth Release Ninjutsu, this different aura disappeared.

Now, the feeling appeared again, giving her a sense of familiarity.

The next moment, under her shocked gaze, several thick wooden structures burst from the moss-covered ground, spiraling, crisscrossing, and combining upward.

In just a moment, a two-story wooden building, similar to the most common residential buildings in Konoha, stood before them.

It was as if the emptiness of the place just a moment ago had been an illusion.

« Th-this is Hashirama-sensei’s Wood Release ! ? »

Hyuga Tsukuyomi looked at this scene in shock, finally managing to close her small mouth that had subconsciously widened in surprise.

However, in the distance, someone’s reaction was even more exaggerated than hers.
