# Chapitre 178 — The Five Kage Summit Convenes!

Konoha, Hokage Office.

Senju Hashirama stood by the window, gazing at the busy Ninjas outside, a characteristic hearty smile on his face.

« Thank you all for your hard work. »

Senju Hashirama turned around, his gaze sweeping over the four people before him : Senju Tobirama, Senju Matsuaki, Uchiha Hikari, and Hyuga Tsukuyomi.

« The Six-Tails and Eight-Tails have been successfully brought back. Combined with the One-Tails to Five-Tails captured earlier, as well as the Nine-Tailed already sealed... »

He reached out and gently swept his hand over the map. « Aside from the Seven-Tails, the other eight Tailed Beasts are already under Konoha’s control. »

Senju Tobirama took over the conversation, his expression as calm as ever : « Big Brother, regarding the Seven-Tails, I have already sent an invitation to Takigakure according to our previous plan. »

« Although they possess the Seven-Tails, it is difficult for a small Village to maintain control over a Tailed Beast for a long time. »

« Moreover, they should be well aware that other Ninja Villages will covet the power of the Seven-Tails. »

« Therefore, they are generally supportive of the concept of the Tailed Beast Council. »

Senju Hashirama walked to the table, resting his hands on the surface, his eyes becoming sharp.

« Then the next task is to convene the Five Kage Summit, inviting the other four great Ninja Villages, as well as representatives from Takigakure, to jointly discuss the establishment of the 'Tailed Beast Council'. »

Senju Hashirama slapped the table, his spirit soaring. « I will personally draft the letters, inviting the other four Kage, as well as the leader of Takigakure, to hold the summit within the Land of Iron a month from now ! »

« The Land of Iron ? »

Senju Matsuaki was slightly taken aback. « Are we choosing that place ? »

« Yes, it is a neutral country, so everyone should be relatively accepting of it. »

Senju Hashirama explained.

A hint of a smile flashed in Senju Tobirama’s eyes.

If this step of the Tailed Beast Council succeeds, Konoha’s status will be further solidified.

The flames of war will likely burn across the entire continent ahead of schedule.

« Then, the next task is... »

Senju Hashirama scanned the crowd, his voice resounding.

« Prepare the materials needed for the summit, draft the terms of exchange, and simultaneously strengthen the guard over the Tailed Beasts. »

« Matsuaki, you, Hikari, and Tsukuyomi should rest for a few days first to recharge. This summit might require you to accompany me as guards. »

« Yes ! »

The three responded in unison.

...

Hidden Cloud Village, Raikage Building.

The letter was slammed heavily onto the desk, emitting a loud bang.

« The Eight-Tails was clearly in our territory. Now, not only did Konoha snatch it away, but they also want us to go to the Land of Iron and pay to 'redeem' it ? »

The First Raikage’s neck veins bulged, and faint arcs of lightning crackled around his body. « Is Senju Hashirama treating us in Hidden Cloud Village like fat sheep to be slaughtered ? »

The guard, Fu Yamanaka, said in a deep voice : « Tailed Beast Council ? This proposal from Konoha is truly unexpected. »

« Does Senju Hashirama actually want to drag the entire Ninja World into playing house with him ? »

The First Raikage sneered. « Go to the Land of Iron and see exactly what Konoha intends to do. »

...

Hidden Sand Village, Kazekage Office.

First Kazekage Retto finished reading the letter, his withered fingers slowly tracing the edge of the sand table, leaving trails behind.

« The One-Tails... it has fallen into Konoha’s hands after all. »

His voice was low, carrying a trace of unwillingness. « This 'Tailed Beast Council' is clearly meant to make us use our own family assets to trade back what should have belonged to us in the first place. »

Shamon frowned. « Konoha’s condition is 'equivalent exchange'. If we refuse, not only will we not get Shukaku back, but we will also be excluded from the new order. »

« The major Ninja Villages are eyeing the Tailed Beasts covetously. If other Villages obtain Tailed Beasts this time, and only Hidden Sand Village does not... »

« This situation must absolutely not happen ! »

A sharp glint flashed in Retto’s eyes : « Shamon, accompany me to the Land of Iron. No matter what, we must at least secure the One-Tails this time ! »

...

Iwagakure, Tsuchikage Building.

First Tsuchikage Ishikawa sat cross-legged on his chair. After listening to the report, he let out a cold laugh.

« Interesting. Konoha has captured seven Tailed Beasts and now wants to act as a middleman ? »

The Elder beside him was worried : « Lord Tsuchikage, this move by Konoha will likely have a huge impact on the situation in the Ninja World. »

« What is there to fear ! »

Ishikawa slapped his knee, causing the ground to tremble slightly. « Since it is a trade, it must be fair. »

« If the trading conditions proposed by Konoha are too outrageous, presumably the other Ninja Villages will not agree ! »

« In any case, Mu and I will go to the Land of Iron to take a look and see what this Tailed Beast Council is really about. »

...

Hidden Mist Village, Mizukage Office.

In the damp and cold air, First Mizukage Byakuren tapped his fingers lightly on the desk, making crisp sounds.

« The Three-Tails... has been captured by Konoha. »

His voice was aged, his expression gloomy. « And now they want us to bring things to trade for it ? What a joke. »

Nearby, Hōzuki Gengetsu whispered : « The Three-Tails is particularly skilled in naval warfare. If it falls into the hands of other Ninja Villages, it will be a threat we cannot ignore. »

« You are right. »

A trace of firmness flashed in Byakuren’s eyes. « That is why this summit must be attended. »

« Tailed Beasts are strategic weapons that cannot be ignored. Although Hidden Mist cannot compare to Konoha, at least we cannot be weaker than the other Ninja Villages ! »

...

Takigakure, Leader’s Residence.

Unlike the hardline or gloomy reactions of the other four great Ninja Villages, the leader of Takigakure let out a long sigh of relief after finishing the letter, wiping the cold sweat from his forehead with a towel.

« Fortunately... Konoha has no intention of forcibly seizing the Seven-Tails. »

He muttered, « Now that Konoha is convening the Five Kage Summit to establish a council, it actually gives us a better opportunity to maintain control over the power of the Seven-Tails. »

The Elder beside him nodded. « If we follow what the Hokage said and establish a Tailed Beast Council, Hidden Cloud Village, Iwagakure, and the others won’t dare to easily plot against the Seven-Tails in the future. »

« That is exactly the point. »

The leader stood up, his eyes becoming firm. « Prepare yourselves, and bring the activity records and characteristic analysis of the Seven-Tails. »

« We are not going to fight for the ownership of those seven Tailed Beasts ; we only seek the status of a 'Councilor' and Konoha’s protection. »

...

One month later.

The Kage of each Ninja Village, accompanied by their guards, set off one after another in the direction of the Land of Iron.

Amidst the snow and wind, Senju Matsuaki and the others, wearing cloaks, had already entered the territory of the Land of Iron.

Senju Hashirama held a stack of papers in his hand, looking down at them while walking, while also muttering something to himself.

Seeing this, Senju Matsuaki and the others just smiled at each other.

As the initiator of the Five Kage Summit, Konoha had many things to inform the other major Ninja Villages about.

Senju Hashirama was afraid of omissions, so he had written the draft in advance, recording the content of the meeting in great detail.

Worried about making mistakes, he familiarized himself with it multiple times before the meeting.

As for the core of this meeting, the Tailed Beasts...

Senju Matsuaki’s gaze fell on the scroll behind Senju Hashirama.

The scroll sealing the seven Tailed Beasts was currently being kept in the safest place in the entire Ninja World.
