# Chapitre 103 — Three Uchiha!

« We are all companions in the same Village now, and everyone is dutifully building the Village ; it is only you Uchiha Clan members who are constantly causing trouble. »

« Some of your Uchiha Clan Ninjas have had relatives and friends killed by the Senju Clan in the past, but don’t you think the relatives and friends of the Senju Clan Ninjas haven’t been killed by you ? »

« To be honest, many of the large and small Ninja clans in Konoha now were enemies with each other during the Sengoku period. »

« If we cannot let go of our hatred and get along, then we might as well all take off our Konoha forehead protectors and fight it out again ! »

« We Uchiha will take back the leadership of Konoha from the despicable Senju Clan. Konoha should be the Uchiha Clan’s Konoha ! ! »

Uchiha Setsuna closed and opened his eyes, his crimson Sharingan appearing ferocious and terrifying in the night.

The Uchiha Clan’s hawkish faction was not dissatisfied with Konoha, but rather dissatisfied that they, the Uchiha, were not the masters of Konoha.

If they were also dissatisfied with Konoha, they could have left the Village with Uchiha Madara back then.

With Uchiha Madara shielding them, Senju Hashirama and the others would not have made things too difficult, and they likely would have truly let the Uchiha Clan of Konoha leave.

At that time, under the leadership of Uchiha Madara, the No. 2 of the Ninja World, it would not have been impossible to directly replace a major Ninja Village.

However, these Uchiha Clan hawks did not make that choice.

Ultimately, they also felt that compared to the turmoil of the Sengoku period, life in Konoha was quite good.

« As long as we kill you here, then after Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama, there will be no other Senju who can rival us Uchiha ! ! »

At this moment, murderous intent was bared in Uchiha Setsuna’s crimson Sharingan, and he felt that the scene of the Uchiha Clan ruling Konoha and slaughtering and expelling the Senju Clan like stray dogs was right before his eyes !

« Do it ! »

Suppressing his excitement, Uchiha Setsuna did not want to waste any more breath on Senju Matsuaki.

Fearing that a long delay would lead to more trouble, he ignored whether Senju Matsuaki’s guess was correct or not, and instead directly issued the order for the two clansmen to attack together.

« Two Jonin and one Chunin want to take me down ? »

Seeing this, Senju Matsuaki also drew the short blade from his back, his crimson eyes igniting with crazy Battle Intent.

He had spent the whole day in the Chunin Exams lounge recharging his energy, but in the end, all he got was a mediocre Ninja from Iwagakure.

This was like sitting upright at a magnificent dining table in a high-end restaurant, waiting for a long time, only to be served a small dish of spoiled vegetables in the end.

Senju Matsuaki felt unsatisfied, sensing that his desire for battle had not been fully satisfied !

Ninjas who can become stronger step by step by lying flat and not liking to fight either exist in storybooks or have some kind of cheat that makes them stronger by lying flat.

However, any Ninja who has become a Jonin or even a Kage through their own efforts has, to some extent, a desire to become stronger and to fight.

Senju Matsuaki was also among them.

« We three can’t take down one of you ? Stop talking big ! ! »

Uchiha Setsuna’s eyes were filled with murderous intent, and upon hearing Senju Matsuaki’s questioning, he almost laughed out loud, his hand seals even faltering for a moment.

Being surrounded by two Jonin and one Chunin, perhaps only an elite among Jonin could possibly escape or counter-kill.

And in Uchiha Setsuna’s eyes, Senju Matsuaki’s strength was at best only that of a Chunin.

Even if it was at the top level of Chunin strength, it was still just a Chunin.

Having been a Chunin for many years and still unable to reach Jonin strength, Uchiha Setsuna knew the gap between a Chunin and a Jonin well.

For a Ninja with mediocre talent, the Bottleneck from Chunin to Jonin might trap them for a lifetime.

« Whether it’s big talk, you can come and try ! ! »

The short blade in Senju Matsuaki’s hand was covered in a layer of majestic Chakra, causing the surrounding air to slightly stagnate.

Stomping his feet hard on the ground, holding the blade with a cold glint, Senju Matsuaki seemed to turn into a stream of light as he charged towards Uchiha Setsuna.

« Fire Release : Great Fireball Technique ! ! »

Uchiha Setsuna finished the hand seals, Chakra gathered at the back of his throat, and he spat out a huge fireball, blocking in front of Senju Matsuaki.

Seeing this, Senju Matsuaki did not dodge or avoid it, but simply held the short blade carrying a large amount of Chakra horizontally in front of him.

« Monster Strength ! ! Break... for me ! ! »

The Senju Clan’s ancestral method of releasing Monster Strength involved bursting the Chakra on the blade forward, and the massive force caused a muffled sound in mid-air.

The fireball, burning with raging flames, was like fireworks exploding in the air the moment it was struck by the Monster Strength.

Tiny sparks drifted down from the air, falling to the ground and returning to silence.

This scene caused Uchiha Setsuna, who had originally been confident of victory, to have his pupils shrink sharply, his face showing fear.

And the two Uchiha Jonin, who were watching covetously, also noticed something wrong at this time.

Clearly, the information given to them by Uchiha Setsuna’s grandfather, Elder Uchiha Kasei, showed that Senju Matsuaki only had Chunin strength, but why did this faint sense of oppression now make even them feel alarmed ?

« Not good, be careful ! ! »

The thirty-year-old Uchiha Jonin’s pupils shrank sharply, and while using his Three-Tomoe Sharingan to capture Senju Matsuaki’s movements, he quickly drew his long sword to block in front of Uchiha Setsuna.

Seeing this, Senju Matsuaki simply changed his original target, and the blade in his hand slashed straight towards the Uchiha Jonin.

The Uchiha Clan also had the Uchiha Style Kenjutsu passed down from generation to generation. Although its popularity in the Ninja World was not as high as the Uchiha’s Sharingan and Fire Release, its actual power could not be underestimated.

The moment the blades collided, the Uchiha Jonin’s expression changed drastically, and a massive force instantly caused a strong feeling of distortion in his arm.

Experienced, he decisively let go of the sword and abandoned it, but the irresistible massive force still caused his body to retreat more than ten steps before stopping.

« This is the Senju Clan’s Monster Strength. This brat’s Monster Strength is already stronger than some of the Senju Clan’s Jonin ! ! »

The Uchiha Jonin’s hands trembled slightly, and he looked at Senju Matsuaki, who had stopped his movements, in disbelief, immediately notifying his companion of his discovery.

He had seen other Senju Clan Ninjas use Monster Strength before.

To reach the level where he had to abandon his sword and retreat in one encounter, it would at least have to be a Jonin of the Senju Clan.

« Don’t worry, he just has a larger amount of Chakra than the average Senju Clan member ; his combat experience has not yet reached the level of a Jonin ! »

Another Uchiha Clan Jonin stepped quickly to block behind Senju Matsuaki, his calmer personality allowing him to point out the truth in one sentence.

Senju Matsuaki also knew this point.

The reason he possessed Jonin-level strength now relied entirely on the brute force gained from the multiple enhancements of Breathing Technique, Chakra volume, Asura Chakra, and Bloodline Limit.

In addition, although he had attainments in Earth Release, Water Release, and Monster Strength that his peers could not match, he was still lacking compared to experienced Jonin in their prime.

And this was also the reason why Senju Matsuaki longed for a challenging battle !

Only in this way could he more quickly master the power that was becoming stronger day and night !

Of course, whether it was the so-called « one force overcomes ten techniques » or « deflecting a thousand pounds with four ounces, » both had their own logic ; it just depended on whether force or technique was superior.
