# Chapitre 47 — Wood Release!!

« But don’t you two just stand around idle ; this is what you’re responsible for today. »

Senju Tobirama took out two identical blueprints and handed them to the two of them. Seeing that they had taken the blueprints, he continued.

« It would be difficult for Big Brother and me to determine the locations accurately on our own, so we need your assistance. Once it’s done, it will count as a C-rank mission. »

« Understood, Second Uncle. »

Matsumi was not surprised.

Being called here together, it couldn’t possibly be just to watch and witness the majesty of Wood Release.

Whether it was his Shadow Clone or Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s Byakugan, both could be very useful at this time.

However, this C-rank mission did not trigger a mission for his cheat, presumably because it lacked sufficient challenge.

« Then I shall begin. »

Seeing that everyone was ready, Senju Hashirama rubbed his hands together and began to form hand seals.

In an instant, the massive amount of Chakra bursting from his body caused the three of them—Senju Matsumi, Hyuga Tsukuyomi, and the others—to pause, and even Senju Matsumi and Hyuga Tsukuyomi, who were closest, had to retreat a few steps.

Even for Senju Tobirama, a powerhouse who had long possessed Kage-level strength, his eyes narrowed slightly when facing this massive amount of Chakra.

In Konoha, Senju Hashirama was the Hokage ; in the Ninja World, he was the undisputed God of Ninja.

« Wood Release : Wood Golem Technique ! »

Senju Hashirama clapped his hands together, and whatever he called for appeared.

The moment Senju Hashirama pressed his hands together, a majestic torrent of Chakra surged into the ground. Accompanied by a deafening roar, the earth cracked and trembled.

Then, thick, gnarled tree roots surged from the ground, spiraling and intertwining upwards, eventually forming a giant god-like Wood Golem.

The Wood Golem’s body was covered in ancient tree patterns, with a mighty, thick Wood Dragon coiled around its arm. The Golem’s head towered into the clouds, and its divine eyes contained a fierce and powerful aura.

The moment the Wood Man broke through the earth, the people on the ground felt themselves enveloped by a shadow.

Matsumi looked up, trying to find Hashirama’s figure, and finally saw the relatively tiny figure of Senju Hashirama on the bridge of the Wood Man’s nose.

« Is this really something a Ninja can do ? »

Hyuga Tsukuyomi had activated her Byakugan at some point, but the sight of the massive Wood Golem filling her entire field of vision, along with the immense amount of Chakra, left her eyes filled with shock.

For Matsumi, who had some knowledge from his previous life, and for Tobirama, who had seen it countless times before, they were already mentally prepared.

However, this was Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s first time seeing such a huge and terrifying Ninjutsu ; this mountain-like Wood Golem was just like a deity.

But Matsumi knew in his heart that the giant Wood Man before him was not Senju Hashirama’s full power ; he still had Sage Mode as his trump card.

Sage Art combined with Wood Release allowed Senju Hashirama to truly achieve dimensionality reduction strikes in this era before the Ōtsutsuki Clan appeared.

After the shock, the group did not remain idle. They quickly went to their respective duties, scattering like arrows released from a bow.

« Multiple Shadow Clone Jutsu ! ! »

Senju Matsumi formed hand seals, and five Shadow Clones appeared at his side in one breath.

Not long after he obtained the Shadow Clone Technique through the check-in system, he asked Hashirama for the training method for the Multiple Shadow Clone Jutsu and quickly became proficient with it.

« Everyone is very motivated, so I must begin too ! »

On the bridge of the Wood Man’s nose, after seeing his younger brother and the students on the ground getting ready, Senju Hashirama also began today’s infrastructure mission.

With every gesture of the giant Wood Golem, the surrounding environment and terrain underwent massive changes.

It seemed he didn’t want to cause too much of a commotion and wanted to leave some time for the creatures living in the forest to escape, so Hashirama’s movements were neither large in range nor fast.

On the ground, Senju Matsumi, working in cooperation with his Shadow Clones, looked at the giant Wood Man and his eyes suddenly lit up.

Back then, when he checked in at the Hokage Office against Uncle Hashirama’s Wood Clone, he ended up obtaining the Shadow Clone Technique.

So, if he checked in now against Uncle Hashirama, who was using Wood Release, would there be any unexpected joy ?

It had been more than ten days since the Kumogakure delegation left Konoha, and today was the last day of the twenty-day requisition period for Matsumi’s check-in system.

Over these past ten-plus days, he hadn’t missed a single check-in. The rewards couldn’t be called rich, but they were decent enough.

Most of the check-in rewards weren’t very flashy, but they had practical uses, such as basic attributes like Chakra and physical constitution.

A few days, some Ninjutsu were dropped, but what Matsumi found somewhat regrettable was that the highest-grade one was only the A-rank Four Red Yang Formation.

This barrier technique had an extremely high upper limit ; it was an upgraded version of the Four Violet Flames Formation and could even block the Ten-Tails, but its flaw was that it required four top-tier Ninjas with Kage-level Chakra to cast it simultaneously.

« Check in ! »

As Matsumi silently chanted in his heart, the check-in system’s final usage count dropped to zero.

Although they were far apart, one in the sky and one on the ground, as long as Senju Hashirama was within Matsumi’s line of sight, there was no need to worry about being unable to lock on.

[Check-in Character : Senju Hashirama ! Obtained : Wood Release !]

Uncle Hashirama is really awesome today !

After hearing the prompt, Matsumi was stunned for a moment, and then an uncontrollable look of ecstasy appeared on his face.

Wood Release is undoubtedly the number one among the diverse Bloodline Limits in the Ninja World.

There are actually quite a few Bloodline Limits in the Ninja World. Besides ocular techniques like the Sharingan and Byakugan, and physical constitutions like Shikotsumyaku, there are also Magnet Release, Lava Release, Ice Release, Explosive Release, and so on.

But at least in the current Ninja World, these Bloodline Limits have to yield when facing Wood Release.

Although this is partly because Wood Release and Senju Hashirama mutually achieved each other, the upper limit of Wood Release itself is extremely high.

Moreover, the Senju Clan bloodline and Asura Chakra that Senju Hashirama possesses are now also possessed by Senju Matsumi, so there is no need to worry about his Wood Release becoming as weak as Yamato’s.

However, looking at the giant Wood Golem in the distance still focused on the infrastructure mission, Matsumi gradually restrained his joy.

There is still a long way to go from obtaining Wood Release to developing it to Hashirama’s level.

As time passed, the sun gradually set, and the construction progress of Hashirama’s construction team finally came to an end.

« Leave the rest for the construction team to finish. It’s been a long time since I’ve stretched my muscles like this. »

Senju Hashirama, who had already come down from the Wood Man, looked emotional. This feeling of manipulating the Wood Man to show off his power made him miss his best friend who had left the Village years ago without a trace.

« Big Brother, since things are settled here, let’s head back to the Village quickly. I still have an appointment with Luye tonight to discuss advertising placement. » Senju Tobirama urged.

Recently, it can be said to be the second busiest period since the founding of Konoha ; the last time it was this busy was during the initial startup period from zero to one.

Ever since Matsumi offered his advice in the Hokage Office last time, the Ino–Shika–Chō clans have been quite interested in the advertising placement for this Joint Chunin Exams, wanting to use advertising and investment promotion to take their clan industries to the next level.
