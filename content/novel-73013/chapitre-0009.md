# Chapitre 9 — The Assessment Begins

« Number 3. »

After Senju Matsumi picked up a paper ball from the box, he walked to the side and opened it.

The paper had the number three written on it, which was a relatively early number.

Looking around, he could see that the number of people participating in the graduation assessment was not large, only about fifty or so.

Furthermore, battles between Ninjas could often result in mutual injury, so many of them might choose to withdraw from subsequent rounds of the assessment even if they won the first.

Therefore, this elimination tournament only needed to last about three or four rounds.

« Look quickly, Hokage-sama is here ! »

Someone suddenly shouted, and everyone turned their heads, noticing a group of people who had arrived outside the field at some unknown moment.

Among them, Senju Hashirama walked in front wearing the Hokage hat, followed by Senju Tobirama and several other clan heads and Ninjas.

In the current Konoha, Senju Hashirama was the most beloved person.

If the older generation of Ninjas felt awe toward him, the younger generation simply worshipped him.

Seeing the next generation of Konoha greeting him, Senju Hashirama also smiled and waved at everyone.

« Hokage-sama is truly as popular as ever. »

Standing beside Senju Tobirama were several teenagers, one of whom looked like a monkey and remarked with emotion.

Among these youths were Hiruzen Sarutobi, Danzo Shimura, Uchiha Kagami, Utatane Koharu, Homura Mitokado, and Akimichi Torifu.

They were all the best among their peers, belonging to the first batch of graduates after the Ninja Academy was established in Konoha.

Since they happened to have no missions recently, they had come to the Ninja Academy with Senju Tobirama to watch the graduation assessment.

« Tobirama-sensei, are there any promising seeds in this batch ? » Hiruzen Sarutobi asked curiously.

Hearing this, the others nearby also looked over, pricking up their ears.

After thinking for a moment, Senju Tobirama said.

« In the graduating class, your cousin Sarutobi Go is relatively outstanding. »

Hearing this, the current head of the Sarutobi Clan, who was also Hiruzen Sarutobi’s father, Sarutobi Sasuke, took over the conversation.

« Logically speaking, he already had the strength of a Genin two years ago, but he was unwilling to graduate early. It really is... »

Sarutobi Sasuke shook his head in frustration.

As a veteran of Konoha, he had fought side by side with Senju Hashirama and the others for many years, so they were quite casual with each other.

Senju Hashirama said seriously : « Sasuke, one of the reasons we established Konoha was so we wouldn’t have to see children go to the battlefield so early. There is nothing wrong with that. »

Hearing this, Sarutobi Sasuke nodded in agreement and continued.

« From what I understand, if nothing unexpected happens, the strongest among this year’s graduates should be Go. »

The seemingly reserved Danzo Shimura suddenly asked : « Tobirama-sensei, what about those who graduated early ? »

He noticed that Senju Tobirama had only mentioned the graduating class, excluding those who graduated early.

However, by convention, true geniuses often chose to graduate early.

« Among those who graduated early, two have outstanding talent. »

« Senju Matsumi of the Senju Clan, and Uchiha Kai of the Uchiha Clan. »

When speaking of the former, Senju Tobirama’s expression softened slightly, but when mentioning the latter, his expression became somewhat cold.

Upon hearing the former’s name, Hiruzen Sarutobi and the others were stunned for a moment, then realized.

« It’s Matsumi. I didn’t expect it to be time for him to graduate already. »

« Time flies so fast. The last time I saw him at your house, Sensei, he was still just a child. »

As students of Senju Tobirama, they sometimes visited the Senju Clan compound and had met Senju Matsumi a few times.

« Uchiha Kai, do you know this person, Kagami ? »

Hiruzen Sarutobi looked at his good friend and asked.

Uchiha Kagami, looking somewhat awkward, reached out his right hand and scratched his cheek.

« I suppose I know him. He is the younger brother of Setsuna-senpai. »

At the same time, they also belonged to the hawk faction within the Uchiha Clan and had never liked clansmen like Uchiha Kagami who were close to Konoha and the Hokage.

Just then, Senju Hashirama patted Senju Tobirama on the shoulder.

« Look, it’s little Matsumi’s assessment. »

In the field, Senju Matsumi, carrying a short sword on his back, walked straight to the center position.

On the other side was a girl with short brown hair and a floral headband.

Although the girl’s appearance was not stunning, she was very pretty, and one could see resilience and calmness in her eyes.

« Senju Matsumi and Biwako, perform the Seal of Confrontation ! »

After both of them arrived on the field, the Proctor said loudly.

Hearing the opponent’s name, Senju Matsumi couldn’t help but be stunned.

Biwako was Hiruzen Sarutobi’s future wife.

When the Nine-Tails Jinchuriki, Uzumaki Kushina, was giving birth, she had accompanied her to the barrier far from the Village and acted as the midwife.

Unfortunately, she was killed by Uchiha Obito, who took advantage of the situation.

Biwako’s talent was clearly not as good as Hiruzen Sarutobi and the others, so she did not graduate early and was only participating in the graduation assessment this year.

Outside the field, Hiruzen Sarutobi watched the field blankly and suddenly muttered.

« I feel like I’ve fallen in love. »

Hearing this, Danzo Shimura frowned at him, hesitated for a moment, and still said.

« Hokage-sama and Tobirama-sensei probably won’t agree with this. »

« Danzo, what are you talking about ! It’s that girl ! »

After realizing, Hiruzen Sarutobi grabbed Danzo Shimura by the collar in exasperation.

And the other youths nearby burst into laughter.

...

« Both sides, perform the Seal of Reconciliation ! »

On the field, the battle ended in less than five minutes.

As expected, Senju Matsumi became the winner.

The battle could have ended even faster, but that would have lacked the process, making it difficult for the Proctor to grade.

As the winner, it was fine ; he still had chances to demonstrate his strength.

But for Biwako, as the loser, if she hadn’t performed well enough during the fight, she wouldn’t have been able to graduate smoothly.

And now, although she lost, she had at least demonstrated her level in Ninjutsu, Taijutsu, and other areas during the fight.

For the graduating class, as long as they reached the standard of a Genin, they generally wouldn’t be blocked from graduating.

After all, Senju Matsumi and Hiruzen Sarutobi had a pretty good relationship, so there was no need to make things difficult for the person who might become his future wife.

The assessment continued.

Some other candidates also had very impressive performances in the practical assessment.

Uchiha Kai was not as gentle as Senju Matsumi ; he decisively defeated opponents who were inferior to him.

Because they failed to demonstrate any Ninja qualities during the assessment, those eliminated by him would likely have to wait for the next assessment.

In addition, the performance of another person attracted the attention of the spectators outside the field.

« That little girl from the Hyuga Clan is performing quite well, » Senju Hashirama said with surprise.

After thinking for a moment, Senju Tobirama said : « She is Hyuga Tsukuyomi, the younger sister of Clan Head Hyuga Teruake. »

« It seems her talent hadn’t been revealed while she was at the Ninja Academy. »

As someone who also inherited a Dojutsu Bloodline Limit, Uchiha Kagami remarked : « To have awakened the Byakugan at 6 years old, she is indeed a rare genius. »
