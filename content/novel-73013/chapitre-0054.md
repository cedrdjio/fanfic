# Chapitre 54 — Hiruzen and Danzo's Request

On the streets of Konoha.

Matsumi and his companion, having returned to the village and headed to the Hokage Office to submit their mission, happened to run into a group of six people just coming out of the office.

Seeing the survivors' expressions on the group, Matsumi couldn’t help but feel a bit curious.

« Yo, Matsumi ! »

Encountering an acquaintance, Hiruzen Sarutobi forced himself to perk up and greeted him.

But then, as if realizing something, he quickly stepped forward to Matsumi’s side, his eyes flashing with a sharp light.

« You’re participating in this Chunin Exams, right ? »

« Yes... what about it ? »

Seeing the light in Hiruzen Sarutobi’s eyes grow even brighter after he finished speaking, Matsumi couldn’t help but take a step back.

Could it be that his pursuit of Biwako failed, and he changed his orientation ?

« This time, help me beat up the contestants from the Hidden Cloud Village and the Hidden Sand Village ! »

Hiruzen Sarutobi clasped his hands together in front of his chest and bowed slightly, making a pleading gesture.

« Please, this is the request of a lifetime ! »

« ...But wouldn’t that be detrimental to our friendly relations with the other Ninja villages ? »

Although he felt that Hiruzen Sarutobi’s words and actions were familiar, Matsumi still seriously considered the request.

He didn’t object to dealing a heavy blow to the Genin of the Hidden Cloud Village, considering they were suspected of having a bounty on his head.

However, he had no grudge against the Genin of the Hidden Sand Village, so targeting them didn’t seem right.

« This is a Ninjutsu I recently developed ; consider it my personal reward to you. »

Seeing Matsumi still hesitating, Hiruzen Sarutobi pulled a scroll from his robes and handed it over.

« Brother Hiruzen’s business is my business ! »

Matsumi reached out and took the scroll, and after opening it to take a look, a genuine smile appeared on his face.

« Wait, count me in too. »

Danzo Shimura suddenly stepped forward from behind, holding a scroll he carried with him.

Seeing Senju Matsuaki’s puzzled look, he briefly added : « My request is the same as Hiruzen’s ; teach them a lesson for me. »

After taking the scroll, Senju Matsuaki also pulled it open to glance at it, then looked at the two with a strange expression.

They had really gone all out this time.

What exactly did the Hidden Cloud Village and the Hidden Sand Village do to make these two so angry ?

« I understand, leave it to me. »

Taking money to do a job was the basic spirit of a contract, so after accepting the two scrolls, Matsumi gave them his assurance.

« Matsumi, I trust you. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi and Danzo Shimura both looked at their junior with satisfaction.

After chatting a few more words, the two groups said their goodbyes and left.

Arriving at the Hokage Office, after familiarly submitting the mission, the two said goodbye to Hashirama and Tobirama and left.

The two, tied up with official business, clearly had no intention of keeping them for a chat.

Returning home, Matsumi first took a shower to wash away the dust and fatigue of the days spent executing missions.

After resting a bit, he sat on the wooden chair in his room and took out the two rewards he had just received.

One was the scroll from Hiruzen Sarutobi, which roughly recorded the concept and casting method of a Ninjutsu.

This technique was called the Shuriken Shadow Clone Technique, a name that was not unfamiliar to him.

This was an A-rank Ninjutsu developed by Hiruzen Sarutobi himself, based on the principle of applying the Shadow Clone Technique to shuriken.

Combined with the Shuriken Shadow Clone Technique, a single shuriken could instantly split into countless solid entities, turning a basic shuriken attack into an area-of-effect skill.

This Ninjutsu seemed unremarkable, but it was a genuine high-level technique.

In the original timeline, during the Third Ninja World War, Hiruzen Sarutobi used this technique to suppress multiple enemy Jonin simultaneously.

Unexpectedly, this Ninjutsu had already been developed ; he was truly the direct disciple of Uncle Tobirama.

Putting down the first scroll, Matsumi picked up the one given to him by Danzo Shimura.

The content of this scroll had also surprised him just now, even more so than seeing the Shuriken Shadow Clone Technique.

What was recorded in the scroll was not an ordinary Ninjutsu, but a type of Cursed Seal.

The Cursed Seal of Self-Binding, which, when used, would cause patterns to appear all over the target’s body, could restrict the actions of the person affected by the seal.

Compared to the Shuriken Shadow Clone Technique, the Cursed Seal of Self-Binding was more subtle to cast, allowing it to catch the enemy off guard.

That was why Matsumi had been confused earlier about how the Hidden Cloud Village and the Hidden Sand Village had offended these two.

After all, both techniques were in the category of relatively precious ones, and mastering them could improve a Ninja’s strength.

After putting away the two scrolls, Matsumi got up and took out another somewhat old scroll from the bookshelf.

This was the Wood Release Ninjutsu scroll he had borrowed from Senju Hashirama’s collection last time.

After all, it was borrowed, so it wasn’t good to keep it for too long.

So, during his spare time recently, he had copied the Wood Release Ninjutsu recorded within it onto a newly prepared blank scroll.

Now that he was back from his mission and had some free time, he thought about returning the scroll to Uncle Hashirama’s collection.

After leaving the house, he walked a few steps to Senju Hashirama’s home, and the one who opened the door to greet him was Senju Kazuma, whom he hadn’t seen for a long time.

« Matsumi, long time no see. »

Senju Kazuma’s face revealed an honest smile, and he greeted Senju Matsuaki with a laugh.

« Brother Kazuma, you’re back from the capital ? »

Matsumi’s eyes lit up, and after stepping into Hashirama’s residence, he walked into the house with him.

The two brothers had a very good relationship ; although there was a significant age difference, Senju Kazuma’s personality was similar to Senju Hashirama’s, and he didn’t put on airs with the younger generation.

It was a bit regrettable that in recent years, Senju Matsuaki had just entered the Ninja profession, while Senju Kazuma had instead chosen to quit the Ninja profession to pursue happiness.

Everyone has their own aspirations, and each doing well is the best arrangement.

« Yeah, after all, I’m not officially married yet, so it’s not good to stay there for too long. »

Hearing this, Senju Kazuma scratched his head in embarrassment.

« By the way, is there anything you need, coming over at this time ? »

« Ah, I borrowed a scroll from Uncle Hashirama last time, and I had some free time recently, so I came to return it. »

Matsumi raised his right hand and shook the scroll in his hand.

« Then go ahead yourself. » Kazuma gave a wry smile, « Recently, Father and the others are busy with official business, and they dragged me in as labor ; I have to go on a long trip later. »

« But that scroll you’re holding, why does it look so familiar... »

Senju Kazuma looked at the somewhat familiar scroll in the other’s hand, frowning thoughtfully.

But because he was in a hurry, he didn’t think much of it.

After all, he had seen many of his father’s Ninjutsu scrolls, so it was natural to feel it was familiar.

« Then I’m off, remember to lock the door when you leave later. »

Before Senju Matsuaki could speak, Senju Kazuma waved his hand, then walked out the door and left.

Seeing the other’s retreating figure, Matsumi swallowed the words he was about to say.

He really hadn’t intended to hide the fact that he had awakened Wood Release.

It was just that everyone had been very busy recently, and with little time spent together, there hadn’t been an opportunity to bring up this matter.
