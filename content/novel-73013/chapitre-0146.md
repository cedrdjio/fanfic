# Chapitre 146 — Uchiha Hikari's Surprise!

After knocking out the Uchiha Clan member who had been shouting loudly to attract attention from outside the dungeon, the dungeon instantly became much quieter.

Senju Matsuaki’s sharp gaze swept over the other Uchiha Clan criminals imprisoned here, and those who had originally harbored similar intentions of snitching instantly dared not make a sound.

Seeing this, Senju Matsuaki nodded with satisfaction and began to rescue his clansmen one by one.

Breaking open a cell door didn’t require much effort ; he only needed to use his Vibrational Power to shatter the iron locks of the cells.

Before long, all the captives from the Senju Clan in the dungeon had been rescued by Senju Matsuaki.

« Those who still have strength, carry the severely injured ! Don’t leave anyone behind ! »

The middle-aged man who had been in the cell next to Senju Matsuaki was now standing beside him, instructing the clansmen, his words carrying a certain Aura of natural authority.

The Senju clansmen present were quite convinced by his command and hoisted their severely injured kin onto their backs.

« Mister, do you hold a high position in the clan ? » Senju Matsuaki asked curiously, raising an eyebrow.

« Kid, don’t you recognize me ? »

The middle-aged man looked at Senju Matsuaki with surprise ; had it not been for his distinct physical features, he would have suspected that the other party was a fake Senju clansman.

Realizing that saying too much could lead to trouble, Matsuaki silently closed his mouth.

Giving Senju Matsuaki a deep look, the middle-aged man clearly knew this wasn’t the time to dwell on such things and said in a deep voice :

« After we rush out of the dungeon, do not get entangled in battle. Our primary goal is to escape the Uchiha Clan’s camp. »

Most of the Senju clansmen present were injured, and some had already lost the ability to fight.

If they got tangled up with the Uchiha guards left behind in the camp for too long, these people would surely die.

« Before we leave here, there is one more person I need to take with me. »

As Senju Matsuaki said this, he stepped forward and walked toward the cell deep in the dungeon.

« Kid, that is a member of the Uchiha Clan… » the middle-aged man frowned subconsciously.

Although he sympathized with the young girl from the Uchiha Clan for what she was enduring within her own clan, an enemy was still an enemy.

He had never thought about rescuing her.

Senju Matsuaki said calmly : « The Uchiha Clan doesn’t consider her one of their own. »

Hearing this, the middle-aged man nodded thoughtfully. Although he didn’t fully agree in his heart, he made no move to stop him.

After all, they had all been rescued by Senju Matsuaki ; what right did he have to interfere with his actions ?

Sighing, he looked at the other clansmen : « I will help everyone undo their Seals now. Once we recover some energy, we must leave. Don’t let your guard down ! »

Click—

On the other side, Senju Matsuaki placed his palm over the lock of the cell where Uchiha Hikari was imprisoned, using his Vibrational Power for a precise detonation.

Accompanied by the screeching sound of the rusty iron door being pushed open, Senju Matsuaki walked step by step toward the black-haired girl curled up in the corner.

« You, don’t come over ! »

The sound of approaching footsteps made Uchiha Hikari feel like a startled cat. She backed away until her back hit the wall and spoke fiercely.

She had never even considered that the person in front of her was here to rescue her.

Although she was questioning her life after being imprisoned by her own clansmen, if she had to weigh it in her heart, the Uchiha were obviously still more trustworthy than the Senju.

Furthermore, her parents had died in the war between the Uchiha and the Senju.

She naturally harbored distrust and resentment toward people from the Senju Clan.

Moreover, she had heard the commotion earlier when Senju Matsuaki’s punch made the Uchiha clansman in the other cell go silent.

In her view, perhaps Senju Matsuaki intended to silence all the Uchiha in the dungeon, and now it was her turn.

However, it was clear that she, like all the prisoners in the dungeon whose Chakra was locked by a Sealing Jutsu, was like a tiger with its teeth pulled and claws clipped.

Other than baring her fangs and snarling, she had no ability to resist at all.

But to her surprise, the boy in front of her, who looked about the same age as her, stopped a meter away and then crouched down to look her in the eye.

In the dim dungeon, those red eyes caught Uchiha Hikari’s attention.

The color was brighter than their Uchiha Sharingan, like two rubies.

In those eyes, she saw sincerity, sympathy, and care, rather than the coldness, jealousy, and exploitation she saw in the eyes of her own clansmen.

Perhaps because she sensed that Senju Matsuaki had no intention of harming her, or perhaps because she knew she had no power to resist, Uchiha Hikari’s tense body slowly relaxed. She hugged her knees, resting her head on them.

Senju Matsuaki examined the girl’s fair face. Due to long periods without going outside, combined with the Uchiha Clan’s genes for attractive men and women, her skin was excessively pale.

He asked softly : « Do you want to come with me ? »

Uchiha Hikari’s eyes, which were narrowed from fatigue, widened slightly as she looked at Senju Matsuaki in surprise.

You, a member of the Senju Clan, want to rescue me, an Uchiha, from an Uchiha dungeon ?

Is something wrong with you, or is something wrong with me ?

« I am an Uchiha. »

Uchiha Hikari, who had intended to remain silent, couldn’t withstand that expectant gaze and squeezed a few words out.

The implication was : I’m not going.

However, Senju Matsuaki had already seen through the girl’s heart during their brief conversation and contact.

It wasn’t that she didn’t want to leave, but that she didn’t know where to go.

Especially since she didn’t know if leaving with a Ninja from the Senju Clan would be jumping from one fire pit into another.

So he chuckled : « Uchiha Mumei—who would give their own clansman such a name ? »

Ignoring the girl’s glare, Matsuaki reached out and pointed to the side.

It was a scene of clansmen supporting each other in adversity.

Clansmen who had had their Seals undone were helping those who hadn’t, and those still capable of moving were carrying their severely injured, immobile kin.

The remaining candlelight in the dungeon flickered, illuminating the brilliance of kin supporting each other and advancing together.

« How nice… »

Uchiha Hikari whispered to herself.

« Besides, I won’t force you to come with me. Once you leave this dungeon, your destination and destiny are for you to decide. »

As he spoke, Senju Matsuaki stepped forward and, under the girl’s angry, embarrassed gaze, pressed his palm against her abdomen.

« Release ! »

The Uchiha Clan’s Sealing Jutsu wasn’t bad, but it wasn’t particularly sophisticated either.

After breaking the Seal on her abdomen that was suppressing her Chakra, Senju Matsuaki stood up and stepped back, while Uchiha Hikari stared at him blankly.

Warm energy flowed from her Dantian to her limbs, and a long-lost sense of peace lingered in her heart.

The Seal, it was undone ?

Uchiha Hikari widened her eyes in disbelief.

This Ninja from the Senju Clan in front of her—instead of trying to kill her or kidnap her while she was at her most vulnerable, he actually undid the Seal for her ?
