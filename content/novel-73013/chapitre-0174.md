# Chapitre 174 — Single-handedly Fighting the Eight-Tails! Successfully Sealed!

As soon as the words fell, a deafening roar suddenly erupted from the valley ahead.

Immediately after, a behemoth charged out from the valley.

It was a giant ox with Eight-Tails resembling octopus tentacles—the Eight-Tails, Gyuki.

Its size was a circle larger than the Six-Tails, its entire body radiating violent Chakra. Its Eight-Tails thrashed wildly in the air, each lash leaving deep ravines in the ground.

« Get out ! ! »

The Eight-Tails stared fixedly at the three of them, emitting a low hiss from its maw.

« It seems it doesn’t welcome us. »

Uchiha Hikari chuckled softly, her pitch-black eyes suddenly turning scarlet as a Three-Tomoe Sharingan emerged.

« Be careful. »

Senju Matsuaki drew the archaic katana from his waist, the blade glinting in the light. « The Eight-Tails has high intelligence ; it knows we are here to capture it. »

Veins bulged on Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s forehead as her Byakugan locked tightly onto the Eight-Tails' movements.

« Matsuaki, Hikari, I will make sure the Eight-Tails doesn’t escape ! »

She was self-aware of her current strength.

At this moment, she was only of Jonin strength—already elite among Ninja, but still a long way from Kage-level.

Senju Matsuaki took a deep breath, and the massive amount of Chakra within him began to surge.

« Hikari, use your Sharingan to find its openings. Tsukuyomi, you are responsible for monitoring the Eight-Tails and preventing any sneak attacks from Kumogakure. »

« As for the Eight-Tails… I will hold it off head-on. »

As the words fell, Senju Matsuaki’s figure suddenly vanished from where he stood.

The next second, he appeared directly above the Eight-Tails. His right fist was enveloped in the power of the Tremor-Tremor Fruit, and a massive force of vibration erupted instantly !

The terrifying vibrational force slammed heavily onto the top of the Eight-Tails' head.

« Hum— ! ! »

The air vibrated violently, and a circular white shockwave spread out with his fist as the center.

The Eight-Tails' massive body was knocked back several steps, its octopus-like tails instinctively stabbing into the ground to steady itself, the earth beneath its feet cracking into a spiderweb pattern.

« Roar— ! ! »

In pain, the Eight-Tails let out an angry roar, and its remaining four tails swept toward Senju Matsuaki like steel whips.

Senju Matsuaki flipped in mid-air, using the tip of one of the tails to vault himself higher, dodging the follow-up attacks.

« Such tough skin. »

He steadied himself in the air, glanced at the pained Eight-Tails, and narrowed his eyes. « This Eight-Tails is indeed tougher than the Tailed Beasts I’ve encountered before. »

« However, if that’s all it has, it should just obediently be sealed. »

He muttered to himself, then formed hand Seals.

« Wood Release : Deep Forest Emergence ! ! »

Chakra was poured into the earth, causing it to shake violently. Countless thick vines and trees burst from the ground, winding toward the Eight-Tails as if they were living things.

The Eight-Tails roared, its Eight-Tails dancing frantically, tearing apart and smashing the approaching vines.

But the vines from Wood Release : Deep Forest Emergence were endless, surging forward wave after wave, gradually binding its limbs and torso.

Uchiha Hikari’s Sharingan spun rapidly, capturing every minute movement of the Eight-Tails : « It’s gathering Chakra ; it might be about to release a Tailed Beast Ball ! »

Sure enough, the next second...

From the Eight-Tails' maw, a purple-black Tailed Beast Ball condensed with terrifying power, then shot toward the location where Senju Matsuaki was.

« Wood Release : Wood Barrier ! ! »

Senju Matsuaki formed hand Seals, and a massive, demonic-looking wooden mask blocked before him.

Accompanied by a vibration that echoed across the heavens, the Tailed Beast Ball exploded, tearing through the wooden mask before dissipating.

Senju Matsuaki’s eyes hardened. He gripped the katana at his waist with his right hand but did not rush to draw it.

He clenched his right fist again, the white halo of the Tremor-Tremor Fruit’s power condensing on his fist.

« This punch… will make you go down ! ! »

Senju Matsuaki descended from the sky, his right fist slamming heavily into the center of the Eight-Tails' spine.

« Crack— ! ! »

The sound of shattering air echoed through the valley, and a massive white fissure spread out in the sky.

« Roar ! ! ! »

The Eight-Tails let out a pained hiss, its four legs supporting it on the ground buckling simultaneously. Its massive body was forced down, crashing heavily onto the ground.

The ground sank into a deep pit dozens of meters in diameter, with rubble and dust shooting into the sky.

But the Eight-Tails was the Eight-Tails after all ; even after being shaken to the point of disorientation, it still struggled to stand up.

Its Eight-Tails exerted force simultaneously, attempting to break free from the bindings of Wood Release : Deep Forest Emergence.

« This guy… is truly tenacious. »

Senju Matsuaki landed not far away, catching his breath slightly.

« However, it ends here. »

Senju Matsuaki finally placed his hand on the hilt of his katana.

A scorching aura faintly emanated from the archaic scabbard.

He slowly closed his eyes. The Zanpakuto vibrated and resonated, and a blazing aura rose from his body.

The surrounding temperature rose abruptly, the moisture on the ground evaporated into white mist, and the air began to distort.

« Tsukuyomi, let’s fall back ! ! »

Seeing this, Uchiha Hikari beside him quickly activated Susanoo, taking Hyuga Tsukuyomi with her as they moved away from the scorching aura at the center of the battlefield.

Senju Matsuaki opened his eyes.

His pupils seemed to reflect a sea of burning fire.

« All things in the universe, turn to ashes… »

He slowly drew his katana. The moment the blade left the sheath, crimson flames shot into the sky, dyeing half the heavens the color of a sunset.

« Ryūjin Jakka ! ! »

The moment the blade was fully unsheathed, a scorching storm of fire swept outward with Senju Matsuaki at its center.

The Eight-Tails' movements froze.

In those giant ox eyes, clear fear emerged for the first time.

It could feel that the power contained within that blade was enough to burn it to nothingness.

Just now, seeing that Uchiha girl’s Mangekyo Sharingan, it felt it wouldn’t necessarily lose.

Seeing that youth from the Senju Clan use Wood Release, it still felt it could struggle a bit.

But when those blazing flames ignited, it stood completely frozen in place, as if its feet were nailed to the ground.

Senju Matsuaki gripped the hilt tightly, the aura on the blade becoming increasingly intense. « Eight-Tails, my apologies. »

He swung his blade in a horizontal slash.

« Jōkaku Enjō ! ! »

A crimson flame, shaped like a giant palm, detached from the blade. Wherever it passed, the ground was scorched into charred glass, and the air emitted piercing explosive sounds.

In the Eight-Tails' terrified gaze, that irresistible flame swept over ! !

The Eight-Tails let out a weak wail, its massive body scorched black, crashing to the ground, no longer able to struggle.

Senju Matsuaki sheathed his sword, and the flames dissipated accordingly.

He walked up, placed one hand on top of the Eight-Tails' head, and cast a Sealing Jutsu.

Complex sealing runes spread from Senju Matsuaki’s palm, covering the Eight-Tails' entire body.

The Eight-Tails' body twisted into Tailed Beast Chakra, eventually gathering entirely into the sealing scroll.

Senju Matsuaki put away the scroll and let out a long sigh of relief.

Eight-Tails, sealing complete !
