# Chapitre 123 — The Semifinals, Open!

Just as Senju Matsuaki had resolved the previous battle cleanly and decisively.

The next two matches were equally devoid of suspense.

As the time for the semifinals and finals approached, the mindset of both the candidates and the spectators shifted.

Sunagakure’s Yan and Kumogakure’s Dou were both looking forward to the upcoming battles in the semifinals and finals.

Having been furious due to the earlier interlude, they had now suppressed their emotions, their expressions calm.

However, in contrast, when they faced their opponents in the fourth round of assessments, their offensives were even more crisp and decisive, resolving the battles almost instantaneously.

It was not that they were deliberately showing off their powerful strength to grab attention.

Rather, they had simply lost patience with these fragile opponents in front of them.

When all three matches of the fourth round concluded, Senju Matsuaki, Yan, and Dou all appeared in the candidates' lounge simultaneously.

The three were positioned quite far apart, yet they could still clearly see each other’s expressions.

Kumogakure’s Dou was the first to reveal a rampant grin.

His strong physique stood like an unshakeable boulder, recklessly radiating the Aura of a powerhouse.

Sunagakure’s Yan, on the other hand, had the corners of his mouth slightly curled, sneering with the confidence of someone holding a winning hand.

A cloak covered most of his body, but it failed to conceal his Aura.

These two, in the future, could be said to be among the few standing at the very pinnacle of the Ninja World.

Coincidentally, both were the future Third Kage of their respective villages.

The Third Raikage, the longest-serving Raikage in history, deterred the Ninja World with the « Strongest Spear » and the « Strongest Shield. »

Fighting ten thousand Ninjas alone, he persisted for three days and three nights before exhausting his strength and dying—a truly horrifying combat record.

The Third Kazekage was even explicitly certified as the « Strongest Kazekage. »

Although his record in the original work was somewhat lackluster, that was merely due to the limitations of the narrative perspective.

That Magnet Release Bloodline Limit was the kind of existence that countless Ninjas could only gaze up at, barely catching a glimpse of his back.

However, sitting quietly on a chair in the lounge, Senju Matsuaki felt no apprehension or fear toward these two formidable enemies.

On the contrary, as time passed, it was as if ripples were spreading through his heart.

This change actually caught Matsuaki himself slightly off guard.

« Am I... excited ? » he murmured.

So that was it.

Blindly suppressing his power could improve his control over the Tremor-Tremor Fruit, but it ignored its essence.

To shake this world !

This was the most fundamental power of the Tremor-Tremor Fruit !

When Senju Matsuaki stopped suppressing his power and instead used it to throw punches at the world recklessly, perhaps he could further grasp the essence of the Tremor-Tremor Fruit !

« So that’s how it is... » The corners of Senju Matsuaki’s mouth gradually curled upward.

This discovery certainly wasn’t too late.

After all, when it came down to it, he hadn’t possessed the power of the Tremor-Tremor Fruit for long.

As it happened, he had no intention of hiding himself anyway.

Sunagakure’s Yan and Kumogakure’s Dou were already beginning to stand out in this era.

In the original future, after the First and Second Kage of various villages went offline for various reasons, they would be the top powerhouses capable of swaying an entire era.

He, Senju Matsuaki, would not miss out on this wave of the era.

Furthermore, since he had arrived, the protagonist of the era should naturally change !

With this thought, a surging Aura, like a tidal wave, emanated from his body without any concealment.

Yan and Dou, who were nearby, both cast sidelong glances at him.

Yan snorted and said, « Arrogant brat. »

« He’s not simple, this Senju from Konoha. » Dou’s eyes narrowed slightly as he thought to himself.

On the other side, above the spectator stands.

The atmosphere at this moment was not as heated as before ; instead, it was strangely quiet.

Whether it was the spectators who had come to watch the match or the highly esteemed five Daimyo and five Kage, everyone seemed somewhat silent.

Of course, they were not feeling bored or unenthusiastic about the Chunin Exams before them.

On the contrary, they knew clearly that the upcoming battles were the main event of these Chunin Exams.

The quiet atmosphere at this moment was merely the calm before the storm.

« There are three candidates, one of whom will get a bye and advance directly to the finals, while the other two will compete in the semifinals. Does anyone have any objections to this arrangement ? »

Senju Hashirama, wearing the Hokage hat and looking much more reliable and composed than usual, asked.

The Daimyo and Kage present did not speak, clearly tacitly approving of this arrangement.

After all, with only three people left, this was the only relatively appropriate arrangement.

« Since that is the case, I will trouble the Daimyo to draw lots to determine who gets the bye. » Senju Hashirama smiled and handed the box in his hand to the Daimyo of the Land of Fire.

Seeing this, the others around did not say anything, merely waiting quietly for the result of the draw.

This should be the last draw for this Joint Chunin Exams, so it was reasonable and appropriate for the Daimyo of the Land of Fire to conduct it.

They were all people of status, so naturally, they would not do anything to overshadow the host on such an occasion.

The Daimyo of the Land of Fire smiled and nodded, reached into the box to fumble for a moment, pulled out a paper ball, and slowly unfolded it.

« So, the person who gets the bye is... Kumogakure’s Dou. »

The Daimyo of the Land of Fire blinked, his tone sounding somewhat regretful that he hadn’t drawn their Konoha candidate for the bye.

« You’re quite lucky, Land of Lightning. » The Daimyo of the Land of Wind said with a slightly sour tone.

At this moment, the First Raikage, who had been silent since just now, suddenly spoke up.

« Whether he gets a bye or not makes no difference to that child, Dou. »

« If anything, the bye is a pity for him. I heard him say that he really wants to fight the two candidates from Konoha and Sunagakure. »

The implication was that if Dou participated in the semifinals, the winner would undoubtedly be him.

The First Raikage stated these arrogant words in a calm tone, as if describing a fact.

The other First Kage nearby had different expressions, but they did not speak.

With their discernment, it was naturally not difficult to see from the previous battles that Dou’s strength was the greatest among the remaining three candidates.

Even the First Kazekage Retto had to admit that Dou’s strength was vaguely a cut above Yan’s.

« Hey, Raikage, the battle hasn’t even started yet, don’t jump to conclusions, » Senju Hashirama said sternly.

If someone said he was weak, he might just laugh it off.

But if they said his nephew was weak, he would have to argue about that.

Upon hearing this, Reto took a deep look at Senju Hashirama, and then said, « I spoke out of turn just now. »

« The break time is about over, and everyone must have been waiting for a long time. Let’s begin the semifinals. »

Seeing the atmosphere become slightly stagnant, the Daimyo of the Land of Fire smiled and smoothed things over.

Not long after his words fell, the announcement for the start of the semifinals echoed over the arena, spreading throughout the entire examination ground.

« Semifinals, Senju Matsuaki versus Yan. Will both candidates please take their positions ! ! »
