# Chapitre 45 — The Spar Ends!

« Little brat of the Senju Clan, I acknowledge you ! »

On the field, Dou stood tall with his arms crossed ; his thick, muscular arms had sustained many wounds during the recent battle.

In the next instant, the lightning circling his body flashed, and the high temperature from the electrical current caused the blood seeping from his wounds to evaporate.

At first, he had been secretly annoyed, thinking Matsumi was a sinister person who used Ninjutsu to cheat because he dared not face him in a head-on confrontation.

However, the subsequent battle dispelled that impression.

To be honest, even in the Hidden Cloud Village, he had never seen anyone of the same age with such combat prowess.

If this little brat of the Senju Clan had also reached the strength of a Jonin, I fear this Senju Clan Monster Strength would be enough to rival the Lightning Release Chakra Mode.

Thinking that the opponent was only seven years old this year, a hint of killing intent even flashed obscurely in the depths of Dou’s eyes.

If it weren’t for Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama watching from outside the field, he might have truly been unable to resist the urge to strike and nip Konoha’s future in the bud.

But immediately after, he discovered with a strange expression that the deep darkness all around was receding like a tide, and his eyes regained their sight.

This made Dou, who had grown accustomed to the darkness, narrow his eyes and sneer.

« What is it, little brat ? Is your Chakra running low ? »

However, the response he received was an even more massive surge of Chakra erupting from Matsumi’s body ; the sheer volume of Chakra, which seemed as vast as when the battle began, caused him to involuntarily freeze for a moment.

Where did this little brat get so much Chakra...

Maintaining Ninjutsu for a long time, as well as the constant release of Ninjutsu and Monster Strength during the battle, were all continuously consuming Chakra.

Yet, to think he could still have such a massive amount of Chakra in his body.

Thinking of this, Dou understood more and more why the Elder listed both the Senju Clan and the Uchiha Clan as the primary targets of their plan to plunder Kekkei Genkai.

« Next, one strike will decide the winner. »

With the Breathing Technique maintained at maximum power, coupled with the enhancement of the Asura Chakra on the Senju Clan’s body.

Senju Matsumi had never felt his body so full of power, to the point that his words became even more concise as he suppressed this force.

« I was just thinking the same ! »

A sharp glint flashed in Dou’s eyes ; he exhaled two visible streams of hot air from his nostrils and struck a fighting stance with his hands loosely clenched.

« Monster Strength Consecutive Slashes ! ! »

« Lightning Oppression Horizontal Thousand Generations Dance ! ! »

The two bodies seemed to transform into lightning, and after a few flashes, they collided fiercely.

In the eyes of those outside the field, they only saw countless blade glints and fist shadows, as well as Chakra and lightning bursting and scattering in all directions.

By the time the two figures pulled apart again, the battle on the field had already been decided.

« In this contest, I have lost. »

Matsumi sheathed his short blade with a slightly trembling right hand, calmly accepting his defeat.

The depths of his eyes remained calm ; he did not doubt himself because of this single setback.

Victory would come eventually, but not now.

« Little brat of the Senju Clan... »

Dou’s chest rose and fell with his breathing, and the lightning around his body instantly vanished.

At this moment, he looked at Senju Matsumi, who had voluntarily admitted defeat, with a complex expression ; there was not much joy of victory in his heart.

In a spot where others could not notice, the fingers of his right hand were bent unnaturally and were also trembling slightly.

Outside the field, seeing that the match had reached a result, everyone had different expressions.

« Truly impressive, Kumogakure has capable successors, » Hashirama said with a smile.

« Not at all, not at all, Konoha is truly full of talent, » the Cloud Village Elder said, also smiling, but there was a strange look in the depths of his eyes.

« Since the spar has ended, we will set off to return to Kumogakure. Thank you, Hokage-sama, for your hospitality these past few days. »

Seeing this, Senju Hashirama did not try to persuade them to stay further.

Then, the group saw the members of the Kumogakure delegation all the way from the Konoha training ground to the main gate, watching the backs of the Cloud Ninjas gradually disappear from view.

« Little Matsumi, you did well today ! » Hashirama said with a smile, patting Matsumi on the shoulder, his face full of satisfaction.

Although Matsumi had ultimately lost the match just now, neither the people from Kumogakure nor those from Konoha who were watching had developed any contempt because of it.

This was not a battlefield, but a spar ; the process was valued more than the result.

Letting the people from the Hidden Cloud Village see that an even younger Ninja from Konoha could fight back and forth with the strongest of the Cloud Ninjas' younger generation was enough.

Furthermore, in the previous youth group match, Senju Tobirama had taken the victory with ease.

One win and one loss, that is also diplomatic wisdom~

« Hiss~ Uncle, take it easy. »

Matsumi grimaced and dodged the hand Hashirama was patting his shoulder with ; his shoulder had been hit by Dou’s fist earlier, and being patted like this now made the pain feel like it was piercing deep into his bones.

However, looking up at Hashirama, Matsumi suddenly shook his head helplessly.

If he could have farmed some Wood Release or Sage Mode from Hashirama through his daily check-ins these past few days, then it would have been his turn to press the future Third Raikage and give him a beating.

Uncle Hashirama, you’re disappointing, sigh...

...

On the side of the Kumogakure delegation.

Ninja travel at high speeds, and in less than half an hour, they had arrived at another town within the Land of Fire to resupply.

Along the way, Dou had kept his head down without speaking ; seeing his tightly furrowed brows, neither the Cloud Village Elder nor Hong spoke to disturb him.

It was not until they arrived at a restaurant in the town and sat down that Dou came to his senses as if waking from a dream, and looked at the two elders with a solemn expression.

« Senju Matsumi, given time, will surely become another Senju Hashirama of Konoha. » He made his judgment concisely.

Upon hearing this, Hong almost spat out the wine in his mouth, his eyes filled with astonishment.

« Dou, do you know what you are saying ? »

Hong thought that Dou did not know what level Senju Hashirama’s strength was in the Ninja World, which was why he had made an incorrect judgment.

After all, after entering the era of Ninja Villages, there had been no formal confrontations between the major powers.

Therefore, except for the older generation of Cloud Ninjas who had entered the Land of Fire during the Sengoku era, the slightly younger Cloud Ninjas had never faced Senju Hashirama directly.

During the spar just now, they were all focused on watching the battle from outside the field.

Except for the initial Darkness Technique that made Hong somewhat worried, Dou had been in the upper hand throughout the subsequent battle.

In his view, among the younger generation, no one could surpass Dou, and when he grew up, it would be the time for the new peak of Kumogakure to arrive.

« Perhaps Dou is right. »

However, at this moment, the Cloud Village Elder’s eyes were dim, and he said words that were the exact opposite of what Hong expected.

Looking at Hong, the Elder added : « Hong, don’t forget the age of that Senju Clan boy. »

Hearing this, Hong froze, swallowing the rebuttal that had reached his lips, and a thoughtful look appeared on his face.

« If my memory serves me correctly, perhaps even Senju Hashirama back in the day could not compare to the current Senju Matsumi. »

Just as Hong and Dou were silent, the Cloud Village Elder once again said something shocking.
