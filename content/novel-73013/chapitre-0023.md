# Chapitre 23 — Tracking!

Senju Matsuaki lifted the wooden barrel and placed it steadily on the ground.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi used both hands to lift the little girl out of the barrel, then found a suitable spot for her to sit down.

The little girl looked like she was under three years old ; thanks to her small size, she was able to hide inside the barrel.

Besides the little girl, there were a few pieces of dry rations stuffed in the barrel.

It seemed that her family had stuffed her into the barrel before the disaster struck and then hid it in the well.

If not for Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s Byakugan, this little girl might have starved to death at the bottom of the well after some time.

But regardless, this outcome was still better than being discovered by the bandits.

Seeing that she was only a bit frightened and had not been injured, everyone breathed a sigh of relief.

« Little one, what is your name ? »

Although they were anxious and eager to learn about the Village’s plight and whether there were other survivors, Senju Matsuaki and Hyuga Tsukuyomi could both see that the little girl’s mental state was on the verge of collapse.

After all, she had been hiding at the bottom of the well just now, and that pitch-black environment alone was enough to terrify a child.

At the same time, she must have heard the slaughter and wailing coming from above.

« My name is... Hanako. »

The little girl said her name tremblingly, then stared blankly into the distance and suddenly pointed a finger.

« That is Papa. »

Following the direction of her finger, one could see a man’s corpse crudely discarded at the edge of a fence.

His arm was stuck between the wooden fence, and some of the blood from his neck had already seeped into the wood.

Upon hearing this, Senju Matsuaki and the others fell silent.

Communication technology in this era was not well-developed. For people in this Village to issue a request to Konoha, they either had to go to Konoha in person or entrust a passing merchant caravan to carry the request letter to Konoha.

By the time Konoha accepted the request and dispatched a Ninja, it might have already been a month or so.

Those who were unlucky, just like this Village, would encounter misfortune by the time the requested Ninja arrived.

« It looks like she doesn’t know anything, » Senju Hashirama sighed.

« You two continue to complete this mission ; I will be responsible for burying the people of this Village. »

Senju Matsuaki and Hyuga Tsukuyomi both nodded in agreement with this arrangement.

In fact, the person who had been going out on missions with them during this time had always been Senju Hashirama’s Wood Clone.

Hashirama’s real body should still be in Konoha handling official business at this moment.

It was precisely to test the two of them that, throughout the journey, apart from guiding their training during his spare time, Hashirama’s Wood Clone did not interfere with anything else.

And from the beginning, it was the two of them who were responsible for clearing out the Rogue Ninja.

[C-rank mission issued : Hunt down the Rogue Ninja who committed evil acts. Reward : 50 points.]

The mission notification appeared, but Matsuaki did not focus on it at this moment.

« Tsukuyomi, I’ll leave the tracking of this group of Rogue Ninja to you, » Matsuaki said.

He himself had learned how to search for traces of enemies at the Ninja Academy, but it was clearly not as effective as the Byakugan.

This group of Rogue Ninja clearly had not received systematic training ; while acting savagely, they also did not know how to eliminate their tracks.

Soon, the two locked onto a direction and rushed over.

...

On a small path, under the cover of the shade of trees, a group of about a dozen people hurried forward.

Seeming to be fatigued from the long journey, the last few people in the line gradually slowed their pace.

One short and thin man simply stopped.

« Boss, why aren’t we enjoying ourselves there ? Why are we in such a rush to leave ? »

His words were met with agreement from the others around him.

« Yeah, I haven’t had a taste of meat in almost two months. »

« It was finally a decent-looking woman just now, but you killed her with one slash, Boss... »

At this moment, the Rogue Ninja Leader walking at the front of the group turned his head back, his ruthless gaze making those who were complaining swallow their words.

« Do you think I didn’t want to ? ! »

Speaking of this, the Rogue Ninja Leader also looked full of resentment.

« Before that old man who was the Village head died, he said that Konoha’s Ninja would come to avenge them. If you want to stay here and die, that’s fine, but I’m not playing along ! »

Hearing this, the others looked at each other and dared not speak.

« Damn it, it’s so unlucky. Everyone said the Land of Fire was richer, so I thought about coming over, but it turned out to be just as poor, » the Rogue Ninja Leader cursed.

Having shifted the topic with this sentence, the group of Rogue Ninja began to chatter.

« I think the money here has all gone into the pockets of those nobles. The Land of Fire is even issuing wanted posters for us, even though they are the ones who look more like robbers. »

« Those stinking nobles are all the same. Back when I was in the Land of Grass, I couldn’t even afford to eat, yet I still had to pay taxes to support those nobles. I just quit. »

« Right, if they won’t let us live, then no one should live ! »

« Alright, alright, everyone be quiet ! » the Rogue Ninja Leader said.

« We are heading to the Land of Rivers now. They don’t have big Ninja Villages like Konoha there, unlike the Land of Fire. »

« When we get there, we’ll take over a mountain, rob a few surrounding Villages, and we can live a stable life for a good while. »

« But none of you should be lazy. Learn the few Ninjutsu I told you to learn, and don’t drag me down ! »

« Understood, Boss ! »

« Boss, let me help you carry your bag. I’ve almost learned the Clone Technique you gave me last time. Do you think next, I could... »

A skinny, sharp-mouthed, monkey-cheeked Rogue Ninja walked up to the Rogue Ninja Leader with a fawning look, trying to curry favor.

These Rogue Ninja had no affiliation with a Ninja Village, nor did they have a master, so opportunities to learn Ninjutsu were extremely rare.

The reason they acknowledged the Boss was, firstly, because the other party was relatively strong, and secondly, because the other party was generous and would actually teach them how to extract Chakra and learn Ninjutsu.

Having been at the bottom of society, after refining Chakra and learning some basic Ninjutsu, they were instead able to treat others as fish on the chopping block.

Saying this, the sharp-mouthed Rogue Ninja reached out to take the bag from the Boss’s back.

Seeing someone buttering him up, the Rogue Ninja Leader felt pleased and did not refuse.

However, just as the Rogue Ninja’s hand was about to touch the backpack, he suddenly froze.

« Hah, hah... »

A strange gasping sound came from his throat.

« Move faster, what are you... what are you... »

After a long time with no movement, the Rogue Ninja Leader turned his head back with some impatience.

But he discovered that the sharp-mouthed Ninja was clutching his throat with a painful expression, unable to speak.

One could see a thin, long needle precisely inserted into his throat at the spot he was clutching.

The others also noticed this terrifying scene, panicked, moved away, and looked around at the same time.

« Alert ! There are enemies ! » After reacting, the Rogue Ninja Leader shouted immediately.

At this moment, the Rogue Ninja who had just been pierced in the throat by the long needle had already fallen to the ground, having lost his breath.

His hands loosened powerlessly, revealing his purple-red neck.

« Be careful, the Senbon is poisoned ! » Someone noticed this scene and shouted a warning.
