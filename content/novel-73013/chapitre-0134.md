# Chapitre 134 — The Senju Clan's Celebration Banquet

Inside the Senju Clan’s hall, the lights were brilliant.

On the very night the Joint Chunin Exams were declared over, the Family gathered together to celebrate Senju Matsuaki’s success.

Of course, it wasn’t just Senju Hashirama, Senju Tobirama, Uzumaki Mito, and Senju Kazuma.

An unexpected figure had also appeared here.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi sat somewhat reservedly in the seat beside Senju Matsuaki, her beautiful, pure white eyes fixed on the tabletop in front of her, as if she were counting the wood grain on it.

In Senju Matsuaki’s previous life, he had imagined that seeing the Byakugan in reality would be somewhat frightening.

Setting aside the embellishments in manga and anime, and considering the flaws and details of real people, it would at least clash with general aesthetic standards.

However, seeing the Byakugan in reality truly shattered this notion of his.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s Byakugan did not look like the terrifying sight where the cornea and the whites of the eyes merged into one, indistinguishable from each other.

In fact, there was a clear boundary and color difference between the cornea and the whites of the eyes in the Byakugan.

At the same time, the colorful world reflected on the cornea of the Byakugan, casting a faint layer of color over the white, making it look like glazed glass.

These details were not captured in the manga and anime.

At this moment, Hyuga Tsukuyomi showed no obvious signs of injury on her appearance, a far cry from her appearance in the afternoon when she was still in a wheelchair.

The reason for this, naturally, was the person sitting opposite her.

« Tsukuyomi, don’t feel reserved, » Uzumaki Mito said softly.

Thanks to the Yin Seal, time had not left a single trace on her face.

In this era, Uzumaki Mito could be said to stand at the very pinnacle of the Ninja World in terms of both sealing jutsu and medical Ninjutsu.

With her help, Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s injuries had mostly recovered.

Seeing that the girl was somewhat reserved, she spoke up to reassure her : « You are Hashirama’s student, so naturally we are all one Family, and what’s more... »

As she spoke, Uzumaki Mito shifted her gaze and glanced at Senju Matsuaki.

« Why are you looking at me ? » Senju Matsuaki sensed Aunt Mito’s gaze and was quite puzzled inwardly.

However, he naturally couldn’t ask such a question aloud.

« Matsuaki, how come you didn’t tell us when you awakened Wood Release ? » Senju Kazuma asked with a tone of dissatisfaction.

This dissatisfied tone was, of course, just a joke.

Regarding Senju Matsuaki’s Awakening of Wood Release, he only felt envy, not jealousy.

He had now found his own life goal ; his ambitions did not lie in Konoha.

He was already engaged to the daughter of the Daimyo of the Land of Fire, and in the future, if there were no major upheavals, he would likely spend most of his time in the capital of the Land of Fire.

By then, he might enter politics in the Land of Fire, serving as a bridge between Konoha and the Land of Fire, which could also be considered making his own contribution.

Seeing that Senju Matsuaki had such talent, he felt happy instead.

To some extent, it could also be considered as making up for his regret of not being able to become a powerful Ninja and protect Konoha like his father and second uncle.

« I was wondering last time why the scroll you took from Father looked so familiar. »

« Now that I think about it, that must have been the scroll recording Wood Release Ninjutsu, » Senju Kazuma said with a chuckle.

At this moment, Senju Tobirama took over the conversation.

« Besides Big Brother, Wood Release Ninjas have also appeared in our Senju Clan throughout history ; these records are deeply hidden in our Senju Clan’s archives. »

« However, it is the first time that two Ninjas who have awakened Wood Release have appeared in the same era. »

As Senju Tobirama spoke, he fell into deep thought.

This was his scientific fanaticism appearing again.

Just as he said, ancestors who had awakened the Wood Release Bloodline Limit had appeared in the Senju Clan throughout history ; Senju Hashirama was not the first.

But as strong as Senju Hashirama was, he was the only one.

Those Wood Release Ninjas recorded in the Senju Clan’s archives were indeed stronger than other members of the Senju Clan.

But in the same era, there was almost always a Uchiha Clan Ninja who was evenly matched with them, or even slightly suppressing them.

A situation like Senju Hashirama steadily suppressing Uchiha Madara was unprecedented.

And now, another unprecedented situation in history had appeared.

In the Senju Clan, there were actually two clansmen who had awakened Wood Release at the same time ?

If it weren’t for the inappropriate occasion, Senju Tobirama would have wanted to suggest that Matsuaki squeeze out a few drops of blood for him to study.

« Hey, Tobirama, you knew Matsuaki had awakened Wood Release long ago, didn’t you ? » Senju Hashirama slapped his thigh with some dissatisfaction.

Judging from Senju Tobirama’s unsurprised attitude, it was clear he had known about this for a long time.

He could understand why Matsuaki didn’t mention it ; after all, he had been busy participating in the Chunin Exams recently.

But his own younger brother had been staying with him frequently recently due to work.

Not saying a word about it to him was a bit unjustifiable, right ?

« I was afraid that after I told you, Big Brother, the Kage of other Ninja Villages would all know about it within two days, » Senju Tobirama said coldly.

Of course, he indeed had the thought of keeping it quiet and then watching Senju Hashirama’s dumbfounded expression when he witnessed it with his own eyes.

But such a wicked hobby naturally wouldn’t be spoken aloud.

The reason for his rebuttal at this moment wasn’t him making things up either.

With Senju Hashirama’s personality, he might remain vigilant during wartime, but in daily life, he could be said to have no guard.

In this peaceful era, he probably really wouldn’t be on guard when talking to the Kage of other Villages.

Of course, this was also because Wood Release wasn’t considered much of a secret.

Senju Hashirama : « Uh, this... »

Listening to Tobirama’s blunt words, Senju Hashirama felt a bit dejected, and his spirits dampened.

However, on second thought, he felt something was wrong, and he looked up at Tobirama, asking : « But, Tobirama, come to think of it... how did you know Matsuaki awakened Wood Release ? »

« If I remember correctly, you’ve also been busy most of the time recently and haven’t really met with Matsuaki, right ? »

Senju Tobirama : « Uh, this... »

Now it was his turn to feel guilty.

However, compared to Senju Hashirama, Senju Tobirama didn’t show his emotions on his face, and he resolved this question with silence.

Listening to their conversation, Senju Matsuaki smiled and shook his head.

Why his second uncle Tobirama knew he had awakened Wood Release, he had also figured out afterwards.

Clearly, his previous guess was correct.

Senju Tobirama had indeed kept a backup plan from Senju Hashirama, secretly monitoring the entire second stage of the Chunin Exams.

However, as a junior, he naturally couldn’t expose such a thing.

« Alright, let’s talk about anything else later ; if we keep talking, the food will get cold, » Uzumaki Mito urged with a smile.

« Good, let us all congratulate Matsuaki and Tsukuyomi on becoming Chunin ! »

Senju Hashirama chuckled and raised his glass forward, and the others followed suit.

« Thank you, everyone ! »

Senju Matsuaki’s red eyes reflected the lively scene before him, and the corners of his mouth curled up uncontrollably.

After raising his glass and drinking the juice inside, he turned his head and happened to meet Tsukuyomi’s gaze.

In the girl’s eyes, besides shyness and joy, there was also a trace of imperceptible envy.

Compared to the happy and harmonious Senju Clan before her, that clan which divided into Main and Branch families seemed somewhat cold.

« Chunin Tsukuyomi, please take care of me in the future. »

Senju Matsuaki raised his refilled glass and said to the girl with a smile.

Tsukuyomi, who had been slightly distracted, came back to her senses and immediately raised her glass in a flustered manner.

Discovering that her glass was empty, she hurriedly refilled it with juice.

After raising her glass again, she once again met Senju Matsuaki’s gentle gaze.

« Chunin Matsuaki, please take care of me in the future~ »

After being stunned for a few seconds, she beamed, her pure eyes reflecting the bright lights.
