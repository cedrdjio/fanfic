# Chapitre 152 — Uchiha Hikari's Experience

Senju Matsuaki held Uchiha Hikari as he leaped out from the ruins, landing steadily on a patch of grass not far away.

Uchiha Hikari raised her head from his arms, her gaze sweeping over the unfamiliar scenery around them.

The small town that once stood here was long gone, reduced to a deserted ruin.

A trace of bewilderment flashed in her eyes, but she quickly calmed down.

« Is this... the future where you are ? »

She asked softly.

Senju Matsuaki nodded and set her down.

« Yeah, it’s been about... over a hundred years since the time we parted. »

Uchiha Hikari was silent for a moment, then gently squeezed his hand.

« Then I waited for a very long time. »

She spoke very softly, her tone devoid of any complaint, carrying instead a trace of satisfaction.

Senju Matsuaki looked at her calm face, his throat moving as he swallowed.

In the end, he only said : « Let’s go, I’ll take you home. »

It was a long way back to Konoha Village from here.

On the way, the two also talked about what happened back then.

« For you, it was just a blink of a few minutes, but for me, hundreds of years have passed. »

Uchiha Hikari sighed softly.

Hearing this, a look of apology appeared on Senju Matsuaki’s face.

« Sorry for making you wait so long. »

« Actually, it wasn’t too bad ; after entering the Seal, time stood still for me. »

A faint smile curled at the corners of Uchiha Hikari’s mouth.

The joy of reuniting after a long separation gradually washed away the melancholy.

« So, what happened later ? Why did you... »

Senju Matsuaki asked curiously about what happened after he left.

As he finished speaking, Uchiha Hikari’s lively eyes flickered slightly.

« You are my only Family, no matter what, I wanted to be by your side. That was what I thought at the time... »

« So, a month after you left, I also left that little cabin. »

« Before I left, I burned the house down. If I were the only one left behind, those memories would only cause more pain. »

Hearing this, Senju Matsuaki was silent for a moment.

Indeed, he had only considered helping the girl get used to the life of an ordinary person, never thinking that during this process, his own importance in her heart had also increased.

Compared to living a peaceful life.

Perhaps what she wanted more was to live a peaceful life with her Family.

Uchiha Hikari continued to recount her experiences.

« Later, I spent a year visiting many places, wanting to find miracles related to time and space. »

« The closest I came to my goal was when I reached a country called Loulan, where I heard the 'Dragon Vein' had the power to twist time and space. But unfortunately, I failed in the end. »

Senju Matsuaki nodded slightly and then asked : « In the end, you found the Senju Clan ? »

« That’s right. To be precise, I found the Uzumaki Clan first. »

A hint of reminiscence appeared in Uchiha Hikari’s eyes.

« After leaving Loulan, I heard that the Uzumaki Clan possessed a type of Sealing Jutsu that could freeze time and space. »

« I inquired about the location of the Uzumaki Clan and tracked them down. »

« At that time, the Uzumaki Clan was allied with the Senju Clan and the Sarutobi Clan. When I found them, the first person I saw was that uncle from the Senju Clan whom I had met in the dungeon back then. »

« He recognized me too and asked about your whereabouts. I didn’t answer the question. However, I always felt that he already knew something. »

« Later on, I entrusted the Senju Clan and the Uzumaki Clan to use that Sealing Jutsu to Seal me at the location of our 'home'. »

« Oh, by the way, the fee for the commission was the money you had saved up back then~ »

Uchiha Hikari winked playfully.

« Right, before entering the Seal, I handed the map recording the location of the Seal to that uncle from the Senju Clan. It seems he has kept it well all this time. »

With that, Uchiha Hikari finished recounting her experience.

Since then, she had slept quietly within the Seal, waiting for the reunion on some day in the future.

In her heart, she was actually unsure.

After all, the future is full of unknowns ; whether her existence would remain undiscovered, or if the two would miss each other forever, none of this could be determined.

Fortunately, time proved that her choice was correct.

In the distant future, she finally reunited with Senju Matsuaki.

...

A few days later, they returned to Konoha Village.

Konoha Village, Hokage Building.

Senju Hashirama was sprawled out on his office desk, with a stack of documents higher than him piled in front of him.

His head was buried in the pile of papers, emitting a slight snoring sound.

Sitting opposite him, Senju Tobirama was expressionlessly reviewing another document, and without lifting his head, he said.

« Big Brother, if you keep sleeping, I will use the Flying Thunder God to throw you and the desk together into the Naka River. »

« Mmm... »

Senju Hashirama lifted his head groggily, a trace of drool still hanging from the corner of his mouth, « Tobirama, I just dreamed that Matsuaki came back and brought a pretty girl... »

Senju Tobirama’s pen paused.

« You’d better not be sleep-talking. » He said indifferently.

Before his voice had even faded, the door to the Hokage Office was pushed open.

Senju Matsuaki stood at the doorway, with a black-haired girl standing beside him.

The girl wore a simple kimono, a wooden hairpin tucked into her hair, and a pair of pitch-black eyes quietly sized up the two people in the room.

Senju Hashirama’s sleepiness vanished instantly.

« Matsuaki ? ! »

He stood up abruptly, then vigorously patted Senju Tobirama’s shoulder with his right hand.

« Tobirama, my dream was right, look ! »

Senju Tobirama slapped Senju Hashirama’s hand away with a look of disgust.

Senju Matsuaki’s mouth twitched : « Uncle, what are you... »

On the side, Senju Tobirama stared at Uchiha Hikari, his brows slightly knitted.

There was no Uchiha crest pattern, and she had not activated her Sharingan.

But he could tell at a glance that this girl was an Uchiha !

Senju Hashirama’s gaze fell on the girl beside Senju Matsuaki, and he asked curiously.

« Who is this ? »

Senju Matsuaki did not answer immediately, but asked in a calm tone first.

« Uncle, Second Uncle, do you still remember what I went to do ? »

Senju Tobirama nodded slightly : « Of course. »

« You found a scroll in Big Brother’s storage room, which recorded a location. »

« You brought the scroll to ask Big Brother, and Big Brother said he vaguely remembered that Father had mentioned this to him back then. »

Hearing this, Senju Matsuaki was slightly stunned.

It seems that changes in the past had indeed affected the present.

Everything that was happening was already vastly different from his memories.

And because of the Door of Space-Time, he seemed to have retained his original memories.

He couldn’t help but ask curiously : « What did Grandfather Butsuma say back then ? »

« Father said... this scroll is a promise, and also a commission entrusted to the Senju Clan by a client. »

Senju Hashirama looked at Senju Matsuaki a bit strangely, but still repeated what had been said back then.

« The content of the commission was that every white-haired member of the clan must look at the contents of this scroll. Hahaha, what a strange commission. »

As the words fell, Senju Matsuaki looked at the girl beside him with a strange expression.

Uchiha Hikari hadn’t told him about this before.
