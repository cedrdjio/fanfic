# Chapitre 163 — Sealing the Nine-Tailed! The Tailed Beast Discussion!

« Hashirama, listen to me. »

Uzumaki Mito gently patted her husband’s hand.

« If the power of the Nine-Tailed is not controlled, it will sooner or later become a grave threat to Konoha. »

« Rather than letting it wander outside and be used by those with ulterior motives, it is better to Seal it within Konoha and have us supervise it ! »

Senju Tobirama nodded thoughtfully : « Sister-in-law makes sense. »

« The reason Uchiha Madara was able to control the Nine-Tailed to attack the Village this time is precisely because the Nine-Tailed was in an uncontrolled state. »

« If we can Seal the Nine-Tailed, we can at least ensure it won’t be used by enemies again. »

« But... »

Senju Hashirama remained hesitant.

« Uncle. »

Senju Matsuaki spoke up, « Aunt Mito’s Sealing Jutsu is second to none in the entire Ninja World. Since she is confident, we should trust her. »

The basis for his saying this naturally came from the original work.

In the original work, Uzumaki Mito indeed lived in peace with the Nine-Tailed throughout her life, and even lived to see the adolescence of Minato Namikaze and Uzumaki Kushina.

Because of her status as the wife of the First Hokage, although she was a Jinchuriki, she would not suffer bullying from the villagers of Konoha.

And the Physique and Sealing Jutsu of the Uzumaki Clan allowed her to almost completely ignore the influence of the Tailed Beast.

Senju Hashirama looked at his wife’s determined eyes, then at the expectant gazes of everyone around, and finally sighed.

« Alright. But if any abnormalities occur, abandon the Seal immediately. »

« The power of the Tailed Beast is very evil and powerful, but I can manage to suppress it ! »

Uzumaki Mito smiled and nodded : « Rest assured, I know my limits. »

...

The next morning, near the Valley of the End.

Uzumaki Mito sat cross-legged, her hands forming complex hand signs, her Chakra surging within her.

In front of her, the Nine-Tailed was still in a coma, its massive body firmly bound by Senju Hashirama’s Wood Release.

Senju Hashirama, Senju Tobirama, and Senju Matsuaki stood not far away, watching all this with solemn expressions.

« It begins. »

Uzumaki Mito let out a low shout, and the incantations of the Sealing Jutsu instantly formed on her abdomen !

« Four Symbols Seal ! »

Mysterious runes spread out from her abdomen, crawling toward the Nine-Tailed’s body like living things.

When those runes touched the Nine-Tailed’s skin, they emitted a blinding golden light.

The Nine-Tailed’s massive body began to tremble violently, as if feeling some kind of instinctive fear.

ROAR ! ! !

The Nine-Tailed suddenly opened its eyes and let out a deafening roar.

It struggled desperately, trying to break free from the Wood Release, but those runes had already branded themselves into its body like a Seal, beginning to drain its Chakra.

« Hashirama, help me hold it down ! »

Beads of sweat seeped from Uzumaki Mito’s forehead.

Senju Hashirama did not hesitate, putting his hands together : « Wood Release : Wood Dragon Jutsu ! »

A giant wooden dragon emerged from the ground, spiraling upward and tightly wrapping around the Nine-Tailed’s body, further suppressing its struggle.

Under the dual effects of the Wood Release and the Sealing Jutsu, the Nine-Tailed’s resistance gradually weakened, and its massive body slammed heavily onto the ground.

His hateful gaze glared fixedly at Senju Hashirama in the distance.

What is going on with these guys ! ?

He was hiding in an uninhabited place, sleeping soundly, when suddenly someone with eyes like Indra’s came over to kill him.

Before he could even react, he lost consciousness !

After waking up, it seemed he was tied up by someone using wood like Ashura’s, unable to move.

And now, they want to Seal him using a Sealing Jutsu ?

Why on earth ! ?

Seemingly sensing the Nine-Tailed’s hateful and dissatisfied gaze, Senju Hashirama said loudly.

« Nine-Tailed, your power is too great ; you must be sealed ! »

As the words fell, the thick wooden dragon swung its tail, completely knocking the Nine-Tailed to the ground.

Who exactly is the one who is too powerful ! ?

In the instant before being completely sealed, a deep sense of doubt surged in the Nine-Tailed’s heart.

Finally, accompanied by an unwilling roar, the Nine-Tailed’s Chakra turned into a golden torrent and was completely sucked into Uzumaki Mito’s body.

Uzumaki Mito let out a muffled grunt, and a mysterious Seal pattern appeared on her abdomen, then slowly faded away.

She opened her eyes and let out a long breath : « It’s done. »

Senju Hashirama hurriedly stepped forward to support her : « Mito, are you okay ? »

« I’m fine. »

Uzumaki Mito smiled, « I just need some time to adapt to the Nine-Tailed’s Chakra. »

Senju Matsuaki watched this scene and sighed inwardly.

The Sealing Jutsu of the Uzumaki Clan truly lives up to its reputation.

With this, the Nine-Tailed was officially sealed by Uzumaki Mito.

However, the matter regarding the Tailed Beast did not end there.

As time passed, news of the battle at the Valley of the End spread like wildfire, becoming a topic of conversation throughout the entire Ninja World.

And the term « Tailed Beast » began to gradually enter the view of the high-level officials of the major Ninja Villages !

...

After the matter of the Nine-Tailed came to an end, Senju Hashirama convened a meeting of the Village’s high-level officials.

In the conference room, Senju Hashirama sat at the head of the table, with Senju Tobirama, Senju Matsuaki, Uzumaki Mito, and the Clan Chiefs of the major ninja clans on either side.

« I called everyone here today to discuss the issue of the Tailed Beasts. »

Senju Hashirama got straight to the point.

Senju Tobirama took over : « According to the intelligence my big brother and I have gathered, the Nine-Tailed is not the only Tailed Beast in this world. »

« From the One-Tailed to the Nine-Tailed, there are nine monsters in existence that possess massive Chakra. »

A Clan Chief frowned and asked : « What does Lord Hashirama mean ? »

« Everyone has seen the power of the Nine-Tailed. » Senju Hashirama said in a deep voice.

« Just one Nine-Tailed is enough to destroy a Village. If the other eight Tailed Beasts are also controlled by people with ulterior motives, the consequences would be unimaginable. »

The conference room fell into silence.

Everyone present had witnessed the terror of the Nine-Tailed with their own eyes ; that power capable of destroying heaven and earth was truly chilling.

« So I propose. »

Senju Hashirama stood up, his gaze sweeping over everyone present, « That Konoha steps forward to capture and Seal all the Tailed Beasts. »

« What ! ? »

As soon as these words were spoken, the conference room immediately erupted in an uproar.

« Shodai(1st)-sama, this is too risky ! »

« Not to mention the strength of the other Tailed Beasts, just finding them is a massive undertaking ! »

« Besides, will the other great nations allow us to do this ? »

Senju Hashirama raised his hand to silence the discussion : « I know this decision is very risky. But precisely because of that, we must do it even more. »

He paused and continued : « The power of the Tailed Beasts is too great. Once it falls into the hands of ambitious people, it will become the fuse for war. »

« Rather than waiting for that day to come, we should strike first and make these powers controllable ! »

As the words fell, a few Clan Chiefs' eyes lit up.

« If Konoha can hold the power of the nine Tailed Beasts in its hands, this power might be enough for us to defeat the other four great Ninja Villages ! »

Hearing this unpeaceful thought, Senju Hashirama’s brows immediately furrowed.

« Capturing the Tailed Beasts is not for starting a war ! »

« My intention is to distribute the Tailed Beasts to the various great nations, using them as bargaining chips to balance the forces of all parties. »

« In this way, we can not only prevent the Tailed Beasts from being abused but also maintain peace in the Ninja World. »

As the words fell, Senju Tobirama’s expression stiffened, and he looked at his big brother Hashirama in astonishment.

Distributing Tailed Beasts to other great nations—how come he had never heard of this before ?
