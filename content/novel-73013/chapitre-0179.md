# Chapitre 179 — Tailed Beast "Auction"!

Land of Iron, Five Kage Summit venue.

The charcoal brazier crackled, dispelling some of the biting cold from outside.

On both sides of the long table, each Kage sat upright with their guards standing behind them ; the atmosphere was so tense it felt as if the air itself might freeze.

Senju Hashirama sat at the head of the table, still wearing his usual cheerful expression.

Yet, when his eyes swept over the group, the usual casualness was gone, replaced by an unquestionable majesty.

« Everyone, since everyone is here, let’s get straight to the point. »

With a wave of his large hand, Senju Tobirama immediately stepped forward and unfurled the pre-prepared scrolls onto the table.

« These are the seven Tailed Beasts currently held by Konoha. »

As he spoke, countless greedy, burning gazes instantly converged.

However, when they saw Senju Hashirama’s serious expression, those greedy thoughts receded like a tide.

The First Raikage was the first to snort coldly, pressing his fan-like hand onto the table.

« Hokage, speak ! What are your terms for returning the Eight-Tails ! »

« This time, it is not a return, but a trade. »

Senju Hashirama noticed the impropriety in the Raikage’s words and corrected him, « The Eight-Tails was never the property of the Hidden Cloud Village to begin with. »

Seeing that his older brother wasn’t acting foolishly, Senju Tobirama behind him breathed a sigh of relief.

If he had let the Raikage define it as a « return, » Konoha would have been at a moral disadvantage from the start.

Senju Hashirama swept his gaze around the room and got to the main topic.

« Next, in this Five Kage Summit, we at Konoha mainly want to discuss two things with everyone. »

« The first matter concerns the trade of these seven Tailed Beasts : Ichibi, Two-Tails, Three-Tails, Four-Tails, Five-Tails, Six-Tails, and Eight-Tails. »

« Since it is a trade, I will have to trouble everyone to bid and compete. »

As he finished speaking, there were no objections this time.

The other four Kage were certainly dissatisfied with Konoha’s method of auctioning the Tailed Beasts.

But they were even more afraid that if they raised an objection, Konoha would simply announce the cancellation of their eligibility to trade for Tailed Beasts.

At this moment, Senju Tobirama, standing behind Senju Hashirama, began to preside over the Tailed Beast auction.

« First, the Eight-Tails. »

Senju Tobirama raised his voice, « Each ninja village may bid ; secret techniques and supplies can also be used as currency. »

The Kage looked at each other, and in the end, they had no choice but to participate in the auction according to Konoha’s wishes.

Next, the auction proceeded in an orderly manner.

Because they were well-prepared, Senju Tobirama’s pacing was extremely seasoned.

The bidding for each Tailed Beast was controlled within a reasonable range, ensuring that all parties had a chance to participate without letting the prices spiral out of control.

After several rounds of bidding and negotiation, the final results were settled.

The results were consistent with the original work.

The Hidden Cloud Village won the bid for the Eight-Tails and the Two-Tails.

Although the First Raikage still looked displeased, after winning these two Tailed Beasts, a trace of relief flashed deep in his eyes.

The Eight-Tails was originally the target the Hidden Cloud Village was determined to obtain, and coupled with the stereotype in the Ninja World that more tails meant greater strength, the Eight-Tails fetched the highest price.

After winning the bid for the Eight-Tails, their budget was insufficient, so they ultimately added the fifteen-year mining rights to the southern mines of the Land of Lightning to barely manage to purchase the Two-Tails as well.

The Hidden Mist Village won the bid for the Six-Tails and the Three-Tails.

The First Mizukage Byakuren was quite satisfied with this result.

The Three-Tails and Six-Tails originally inhabited the Land of Water, and the Hidden Mist Village already had some understanding of their habits.

Additionally, the abilities of these two Tailed Beasts were quite compatible with the environment of the Hidden Mist Village ; if they could control their power, it would undoubtedly elevate the strength of the Hidden Mist Village to a new level.

It was just that this time, in order to win the two Tailed Beasts, the Hidden Mist Village had also bled heavily.

Not only money, but they also gave up a portion of the Hidden Mist Village’s secret techniques.

Iwagakure won the bid for the Four-Tails and the Five-Tails.

The First Tsuchikage Ishikawa stroked his beard, his eyes filled with a mix of satisfaction and pain.

The Four-Tails, that crimson Masira, was said to possess Lava Release and had astonishing power.

The Five-Tails, that white giant beast that looked like a cross between a horse and a dolphin, was known for its immense strength and Boil Release, which perfectly suited Iwagakure’s consistent, fierce combat style.

Ishikawa was very satisfied with these two Tailed Beasts, but he did not like the huge price he had paid.

The Hidden Sand Village won the bid for the Ichibi.

The First Kazekage took a deep breath ; his expression wasn’t great, but he ultimately accepted the result.

The financial resources of the Hidden Sand Village were relatively weak among the Five Great Ninja Villages, so it was expected that they couldn’t compete with the others.

If they were to buy another Tailed Beast, the Hidden Sand Village would probably be « bankrupt » by tomorrow.

However, the Ichibi, that raccoon dog, was skilled at using wind, sand, and Magnet Release, which was quite compatible with the environment of the Land of Wind.

In order to secure it, in addition to paying money, the Hidden Sand Village also paid a portion of their secret techniques as the price.

Thus, the seven Tailed Beasts each had a new owner.

Senju Tobirama closed the scroll in his hand, stepped back half a pace, and returned the floor to Senju Hashirama.

Senju Hashirama stood up, placed his hands on the table, and swept his gaze over the four Kage present.

« So, the Tailed Beast trade is hereby settled. »

« The time for the trade will be decided later ; money in one hand, Tailed Beast in the other. »

He paused, his tone becoming more solemn.

« Next, let’s discuss the second matter. »

« Regarding the establishment of the Tailed Beast Council. »

« The Tailed Beast Council ? »

The First Tsuchikage Ishikawa stroked his beard, a flash of sharpness appearing in his narrowed eyes, « Hokage, what do you mean by this ? »

Senju Hashirama smiled slightly, took out a pre-prepared scroll from his bosom, and unfurled it on the table.

The scroll was densely packed with clauses and annotations, clearly having been carefully prepared.

« Simply put, the Tailed Beast Council is a regulatory body jointly composed of the Five Great Ninja Villages and Takigakure. »

« The Council will be responsible for supervising the use and management of all Tailed Beasts, formulating unified standards for Sealing Jutsu, and coordinating Tailed Beast-related affairs between countries when necessary. »

He paused, his gaze becoming serious : « If any country, without the Council’s permission, unilaterally uses the power of a Tailed Beast to launch a war of aggression, the other four countries must join forces to impose sanctions. »

Once these words were spoken, the venue fell into a brief silence.

The First Mizukage Byakuren’s hand, holding a teacup, paused in mid-air, his eyes flickering.

The First Kazekage frowned deeply, his index finger tapping lightly on the table.

The First Tsuchikage Ishikawa continued to stroke his beard, the smile on his face meaningful.

And the First Raikage simply snorted coldly.

« Hokage, you speak nicely. »

The First Raikage’s voice carried obvious sarcasm, « What Council, what joint sanctions. In the end, isn’t it just wanting Konoha to ride on our heads and issue orders ? »

Senju Hashirama was not angry, just smiled and shook his head : « Raikage, you have misunderstood. »

« The seats on the Council are equal, each of the Five Kage has one vote, and any major decision requires a majority vote. Konoha has no special privileges, and I do not want any. »

« Easier said than done. »

The First Raikage snorted coldly, « Konoha now controls the most Tailed Beasts and the strongest Sealing Jutsu ; of course you can generously say you don’t want special privileges. »

« That is exactly why I proposed this Council. »

Senju Hashirama looked directly into the Raikage’s eyes, « It is precisely because Konoha possesses these that I need to use rules to constrain myself, and to constrain everyone else. »

« Only in this way can the Ninja World truly move towards peace. »

This guy, he’s actually serious...

The First Raikage opened his mouth, but couldn’t find any words to refute, so he had to turn his head away and say nothing more.

To tell the truth, he didn’t want to come into conflict with Konoha either, especially while Senju Hashirama was still around.

He even felt a faint sense of relief in his heart that Senju Hashirama, who possessed such immense power, was willing to play the game called « peace. »
