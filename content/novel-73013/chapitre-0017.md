# Chapitre 17 — Coming to a Close

Outside the arena, the Ninja Academy students who hadn’t left yet were all dumbfounded.

« This guy is just too strong, » Sarutobi Go muttered.

He had previously thought that his cousin, Hiruzen Sarutobi, was already a Ninja with top-tier talent.

Although only a year older than him, he had been able to successfully pass the early graduation exam and the Chunin exam.

While his own talent wasn’t bad, it could only be described as decent.

Among the mediocre, he was a genius, but among geniuses, he was merely mediocre.

Yet just moments ago, Senju Matsuaki, who was significantly younger than him, had been able to fight Hiruzen Sarutobi to a standstill.

« Matsuaki, please take this. »

After Senju Matsuaki left the combat arena, Hyuga Tsukuyomi trotted over to his side and handed him a pristine white handkerchief.

« Thank you so much, Tsukuyomi. »

After taking the handkerchief, Senju Matsuaki realized that he looked a bit disheveled at the moment.

During the battle just now, dirt, moisture, and his own sweat had covered his hair, cheeks, and clothes.

After a quick wipe, he put the handkerchief away, intending to wash it and return it later.

After a brief exchange, the two stayed where they were, waiting for the subsequent proceedings.

On the other side, Hiruzen Sarutobi also returned to his companions, looking somewhat disheveled.

He saw a cute little girl in the distance handing a handkerchief to Senju Matsuaki, so he cast an expectant gaze toward his own companions.

However, Danzo Shimura, Uchiha Kagami, Homura Mitokado, and the others just stood in the distance watching him.

They didn’t understand his expectant look.

« Damn it ! »

Hiruzen Sarutobi inexplicably felt as dejected as if he had lost a match.

But his companions were clearly focused on something else.

« So, Hiruzen, who lost ? » Akimichi Torifu asked curiously.

Although the battle had been stopped by Senju Tobirama and looked like a draw to bystanders, the outcome should have been clear to both parties.

Furthermore, they didn’t believe that Hiruzen Sarutobi, who had always been the best among them, would lose to a child who had just graduated.

Hearing this, Hiruzen Sarutobi’s expression turned serious, and he frowned in deep thought.

« The battle just now... »

After a while, he shook his head.

« If it was just the final clash, there was no winner or loser between us. »

« If Teacher Tobirama hadn’t stopped it, my Kunai might have pierced Matsuaki’s arm, while Matsuaki’s strike would have shattered my wrist bone. »

« However, I still had some Ninjutsu to counter with, while I fear Matsuaki was already exhausted. »

Speaking of this, Hiruzen Sarutobi didn’t feel very happy, but instead shook his head helplessly.

« I was only able to gain the upper hand because of the significant gap in our Ninjutsu reserves. »

Hearing this, Akimichi Torifu’s eyes widened, and he blurted out, « In other words, Matsuaki’s strength is already greater than most of us ? »

They would spar on ordinary days.

Overall, Hiruzen Sarutobi was the strongest, followed by Danzo Shimura and Uchiha Kagami, and then Akimichi Torifu, Homura Mitokado, and Koharu Utatane.

According to Hiruzen Sarutobi’s statement, at the very least, Akimichi Torifu, Homura Mitokado, and Koharu Utatane would not be a match for Senju Matsuaki.

« I feel that neither Danzo nor I could necessarily guarantee a win against him. »

Uchiha Kagami also admitted honestly.

Danzo, who was to the side, remained silent, noncommittal.

« Then it seems we will soon have a new comrade-in-arms, » Akimichi Torifu scratched his head and chuckled foolishly.

They were very close in their daily lives and often took on missions together.

If Senju Matsuaki graduated to become a Genin, given his strength and his relationship with Senju Tobirama, there would surely be plenty of interaction in the future.

Outside the arena, Senju Hashirama put on his Hokage hat and walked forward a few steps with a serious expression.

Seeing this, the Proctor in charge of the graduation exam very perceptively shouted, « Everyone quiet, Lord Hokage has something to say ! »

It was a tradition for the Ninja Academy graduation exam that Senju Hashirama, Senju Tobirama, and others would come here.

Besides watching the battle and seeing if there were any promising prospects.

Another purpose was to give a speech to encourage the younger generation and convey the philosophy of protecting the Village to them.

However, Senju Hashirama was clearly not a person who liked formalism.

He agreed with what Senju Tobirama said about conveying the philosophy of protecting the Village to the younger generation, but he was unwilling to give a long-winded speech.

Moreover, with Senju Hashirama’s current prestige, even just standing there, he was a spiritual pillar that no one could ignore.

In the end, after leaving a few words of encouragement, Senju Hashirama left with Senju Tobirama, Sarutobi Sasuke, and the others.

On the examination grounds, only the students who had participated in the exam that day remained.

« Everyone worked hard today. »

Not only the Proctors, but most of the Ninja Academy teachers were also on-site, having been responsible for some procedural work just now.

At this moment, the teacher from the Senju Clan who taught the class that Senju Matsuaki and Hyuga Tsukuyomi were in was beaming with joy.

In this graduation exam, the best performers were the students in his class.

Not to mention Uchiha Kai, who had been carried away after fainting, Hyuga Tsukuyomi had lasted until the second to last round in the elimination-style exam.

And Senju Matsuaki had won until the end and even had a very spectacular battle with Hiruzen Sarutobi.

As their teacher at the Ninja Academy, he also felt proud.

« The results of the graduation exam will be announced tomorrow, and at that time, everyone will also be notified of the arrangements after graduating to become Genin. »

« Students who did not pass the graduation exam should not be discouraged or give up ; continue to work hard, there will be opportunities later. »

With that, all matters regarding this graduation exam had concluded.

After saying goodbye to the people he knew, Senju Matsuaki left the examination site amidst the complicated gazes of others.

The only thing he wanted to do now was to hurry home, take a shower, and change his clothes.

Night.

Moonlight was like water, flowing over the blue-tiled, white-walled roofs, and spilling into the courtyard like crushed silver.

Senju Matsuaki, who had changed into a clean set of clothes, came to Senju Hashirama’s house to sponge a meal again.

Given his performance in the graduation exam today, passing the exam and becoming a Genin was a certainty.

Regarding this, Uzumaki Mito and the others were all very happy and planned to celebrate for Matsuaki at home.

Senju Hashirama and Tobirama also returned home after finishing their official business and praised Matsuaki’s performance during the day.

What warmed Senju Matsuaki’s heart was that his Uncle Hashirama and Second Uncle Tobirama did not pry into the secrets behind his improved strength.

The only thing they mentioned related to it was to urge him not to damage his body by overtraining.

Senju Kazuma was also very happy to see that his younger brother, whom he had watched grow up, possessed outstanding Ninja talent.

With an excellent next generation of Ninjas in the Senju Clan, he could also rest assured to go to the capital of the Land of Fire to pursue his own happiness.
