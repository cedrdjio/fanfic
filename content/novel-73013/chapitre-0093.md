# Chapitre 93 — Senju-Brand Happy Water

Konoha, Large Arena.

Since that day when Senju Hashirama rode the Wooden Golem to clear a plot of land, the construction team hired by Konoha had been working non-stop.

With the help of Konoha Ninja, a large arena rose from the ground half a month before the Joint Chunin Exams began.

This arena was generally circular, with a huge open space in the middle, and the ground was made of thick earth.

The surrounding area consisted of tiered spectator seats rising from low to high, allowing those sitting in them to have a clear, unobstructed view of the battle situation inside the arena.

At this moment, inside this large arena, many nobles and wealthy merchants had already entered early, chatting and laughing with each other.

Wealthy merchants with modest assets kept trying to get close to the major tycoons from various nations, hoping to become familiar faces and secure some opportunities for cooperation.

Meanwhile, the major tycoons sought opportunities to curry favor with nobles and even Daimyo, in order to find conveniences for their own businesses and industries.

If it weren’t for Konoha dispatching a large number of Ninja to maintain order at the scene, the stands would likely have already turned into a chaotic mess.

In the spectator stands, there were ten rather special seats at the very center.

These ten seats were divided into two rows of five, with the bottom row of five seats being one step lower than the top row.

The five Daimyo of the Five Great Nations sat in the five seats of the top row.

The five Daimyo had different appearances ; some were thin and tall with fair skin, some were sturdy with carefully groomed beards, and others had round, plate-like faces and were obese.

Behind the foreheads of the Daimyo from each nation, they wore crowns shaped like folding fans, with the fan faces painted with patterns symbolizing their respective nations : flames, water droplets, lightning, wind marks, and mountain valleys.

And in the five seats below the five Daimyo sat the Kage of the Five Great Ninja Villages, corresponding to each nation.

The First Kage were all powerhouses who had fought their way out of the mountains of corpses and seas of blood in their respective nations, representing the Peak of Ninja strength in the current era.

At this moment, the Kage of the major Ninja Villages were dressed in formal attire, wearing the conical hats that symbolized the name of Kage.

The area surrounding these ten seats was relatively empty, with a distance kept from the other spectator seats so as not to cause disturbance to the Daimyo and Kage of the various nations.

« Truly interesting. I never thought you, Hashirama, could come up with such a good idea. »

The Daimyo of the Land of Fire sat in his seat, chuckling happily. He glanced at the large battle arena below and then at the dense crowd of spectators around him, finding it quite interesting.

The other four Daimyo also nodded in deep agreement with the words of the Daimyo of the Land of Fire.

Entertainment in this era was truly scarce. Even as Daimyo who could enjoy the most resources in this world, their limits were still constrained by the development of the era.

Playing chess, tasting tea, and flirting with women were their most frequent forms of entertainment on ordinary days.

Now, sitting in the spectator stands of this arena, the Daimyo found it quite novel and interesting, adding some color to their dull and boring lives.

Watching the Daimyo of his own nation show such appreciation for the setup before them, the First Raikage could not help but have a livid expression.

It wasn’t just him ; the others, First Mizukage Byakuren, First Kazekage Reto, and First Tsuchikage Ishikawa, also had mixed expressions, feeling a bit jealous of the scene before them.

In the Sengoku era, they believed that strength was everything, and relying on powerful force, they became the founders of their respective Ninja Villages.

However, at the time the Villages were first established, they had realized something : for the development of a Ninja Village, powerful strength was important, but substantial financial resources were also important.

However, the Daimyo of the major nations, as the financiers, had no worries about food or clothing in their daily lives and lived the most extravagant lifestyles ; they were destined to be unable to empathize with these Kage.

« This idea wasn’t something I came up with. » Senju Hashirama laughed heartily.

During this period, watching the money pouring in continuously and Konoha’s funds becoming increasingly abundant, he was in a great mood.

However, he did have self-awareness and knew he couldn’t have come up with such an idea.

He was a dab hand at being a Ninja, and he was competent as Hokage in handling official business, but when it came to economics or business, he was clueless.

« Konoha is truly full of talent. I wonder who came up with such an idea ? »

The First Tsuchikage Ishikawa asked curiously, with a look of inquiry on his face.

At the same time, his mind became active.

Being able to come up with such a novel model, they must be an absolute talent in Village governance. If he could poach them to help build their Iwagakure, that would be a wonderful thing~

People in this era were not unintelligent, but due to the limitations of the era, their thinking was shackled.

For example, at the time, Senju Matsuaki had only proposed a rough idea, and Konoha’s top brass were quickly able to refine the specific plan and push it into implementation.

However, sometimes what was most lacking were such novel ideas.

« It’s my big nephew. » Senju Hashirama said with a happy laugh, sounding proud.

« Truly, heroes come from the youth. »

Hearing this, the First Tsuchikage sighed with regret in his heart, and the little idea that had just risen was immediately extinguished.

For a young person like this, who had a kinship or master-disciple relationship with the Kage of a Village and was also receiving key cultivation from the Village, they would have to be out of their mind to leave their own Village.

In the distance, among the group from Iwagakure sitting in the spectator stands, Ōnoki suddenly sneezed.

Seeming to feel his big nose was a bit itchy, he hurriedly rubbed it vigorously with his hand.

« Why are they dilly-dallying ? Why haven’t they started yet ? »

The Daimyo of the Land of Wind was a pot-bellied middle-aged man. Although some of the commoners under his rule suffered greatly from the sandstorms, he himself had fair skin and was oily with fat, clearly having taken very good care of himself.

At this moment, seeming to be a bit impatient from waiting, he spoke up to urge them.

« Daimyo, please wait a moment. There are some drinks here ; everyone can taste them first. »

Senju Hashirama waved his hand to the side to signal.

A girl from the Senju Clan, wearing clothing embroidered with the Senju Clan emblem, arrived carrying a large tray, on which were placed ten transparent glass bottles.

In the glass bottles, there was a dark brown liquid, and fine bubbles were constantly rising from the bottom of the cups.

« What is this thing ? »

The Daimyo of the Land of Wind had his attention drawn by this strange liquid.

He picked up a glass bottle from the tray and curiously held it before his eyes to examine it.

The constantly rising bubbles made him feel a bit hesitant.

Is this thing meant for people to drink ?

« This is also a type of drink invented by my nephew, called something like Happy Water. It tastes quite good. »

*Burp*—

While giving a simple introduction, Senju Hashirama did not forget to pick up a bottle of the drink himself, gulped down a few mouthfuls, and then let out a satisfying, loud burp.

« Is it that delicious ? »

Seeing Senju Hashirama’s look of enjoyment, the Daimyo of the Land of Wind hesitated for a moment before aiming the bottle opening at his lips.

The cold, sweet sugar water carried a slight fragrance and tartness of lemon, and the fine, soft bubbles brought a unique, stimulating sensation.

« Hiss~ »

The Daimyo of the Land of Wind widened his eyes in shock, instantly captivated by this novel drink.

This Happy Water is just too happy ! !

*Burp*—
