# Chapitre 148 — Amaterasu!

Senju Matsuaki frowned slightly, subconsciously looking at Uchiha Hikari.

The scene before him made him pause slightly.

Uchiha Hikari did not respond to the words of her clansman ; her cold eyes were not fixed on Senju Matsuaki, the enemy of the Uchiha.

Instead, her eyes were indifferent, calmly meeting the gaze of the Uchiha Ninja.

The burly Jonin was also stunned, his face immediately darkening as a bad premonition arose in his heart.

« Little girl, what do you think you are doing ? You are a member of the Uchiha Clan ! »

Uchiha Hikari did not answer.

She simply raised her head and looked at the two Jonin, her eyes devoid of any ripples.

That look was as if she were staring at two corpses.

The Mangekyo Sharingan in her eyes spun rapidly.

« Amaterasu. »

Black flames appeared out of thin air on the burly Jonin.

He didn’t even have time to let out a scream before the flames spread from his chest to his entire body.

His clothes, skin, and muscles turned to ash in the blink of an eye.

« What ? ! »

The lean Jonin was horrified and hurriedly jumped backward to dodge.

However, the flames of Amaterasu were like maggots on rotting bones, pursuing him relentlessly.

He changed direction three times in mid-air, but the black flames still clung tightly to his trail.

« There’s no other way ! »

The lean Jonin gritted his teeth, forcibly concentrating Chakra at the soles of his feet, and stepped on the camp fence to climb upward, attempting to create distance.

Yet, just at the moment he thought he was temporarily safe, the Mangekyo Sharingan in Uchiha Hikari’s eyes rotated once again.

Another cluster of black flames blossomed on his chest.

« Aaaaah— ! »

The shrill scream lasted only two seconds before coming to an abrupt end.

The lean Jonin’s body lost control, falling from mid-air and crashing heavily to the ground.

The flames completely engulfed his body, and soon, along with his bones, he turned to ash.

Two Jonin, in the span of just a few breaths, had turned into two piles of charred remains.

Senju Matsuaki, who was standing nearby, was also stunned for a few seconds.

This was the power of the Mangekyo Sharingan.

Amaterasu.

Perhaps Jiraiya and others from the original work could counter this ocular jutsu.

But for ordinary Jonin, it was nearly unsolvable.

Not to mention that these two Jonin had previously fought with Senju Matsuaki and were already injured.

However...

Does this count as kill-stealing ?

[Teammate Uchiha Hikari defeated 2 Jonin, 1000 points received !]

The prompt that rang out at just the right moment caused Senju Matsuaki to curl the corners of his lips, his previous concerns vanishing.

Perhaps because of the mission, Uchiha Hikari was actually classified as his teammate.

Uchiha Hikari’s scarlet eyes returned to black.

The ocular jutsu from just now had nearly exhausted the Chakra she had just recovered.

« Let’s go. »

Senju Matsuaki grabbed Uchiha Hikari’s wrist and dashed toward the eastern forest without looking back.

Uchiha Hikari took one last look at the charred corpse on the ground, a look of relief seemingly in her eyes.

Under the moonlight, her figure gradually disappeared into the depths of the dense forest.

The two traveled through the dense forest for about half an hour.

Following the tracks of his clansmen’s escape, Senju Matsuaki found the Senju clansmen who had evacuated first in front of a hidden cave.

« Kid, you’ve finally returned ! »

The middle-aged man stepped quickly out of the cave to greet them, and seeing Senju Matsuaki safe and sound, he let out a long sigh of relief.

His gaze then fell on Uchiha Hikari, who was following behind Senju Matsuaki, and he paused slightly but didn’t say anything more.

« This place is not suitable for a long stay ; the Uchiha will catch up soon. »

Senju Matsuaki let go of Uchiha Hikari’s wrist and said to the middle-aged man, « We must continue our journey ; we need to at least leave this mountainous area. »

The middle-aged man nodded and turned to shout into the cave.

« Everyone up, prepare to move out ! »

Although the clansmen were exhausted, they knew now was not the time to rest and stood up, supporting themselves.

Several people with more serious injuries also walked out, supported by their companions.

Senju Matsuaki counted the number of people and confirmed there were no stragglers, only then feeling slightly relieved.

He looked back at Uchiha Hikari, who was leaning against a tree trunk, head lowered, wondering what she was thinking.

« Can you still walk ? »

Senju Matsuaki walked up to her and asked.

Uchiha Hikari raised her head ; those scarlet Mangekyo Sharingan had already returned to their normal black.

Her lips moved, but in the end, she didn’t speak, only silently nodding.

« Then let’s go. »

Senju Matsuaki turned and led the way into the deeper night.

Before long, they finally put a relatively safe distance between themselves and the Uchiha camp.

« Kid, thank you. »

The middle-aged man walked to Senju Matsuaki’s side and patted his shoulder, « If it weren’t for you, we all would have died there. »

The other accompanying clansmen also cast grateful looks.

Senju Matsuaki shook his head : « It’s nothing ; we are all clansmen. »

The middle-aged man smiled and turned his gaze to Uchiha Hikari, who was sitting alone under a tree, lowering his voice.

« That little girl... do you really intend to take her with you ? »

« I said, her destination is for her to decide. »

Senju Matsuaki said, « I won’t force her to do anything. »

« But she is, after all, an Uchiha... »

« So what if she is Uchiha ? »

Senju Matsuaki interrupted him, « She was imprisoned by her own clansmen and nearly died in a cell. What is there to be attached to in an Uchiha like that ? »

« Besides, thanks to her killing those two Uchiha Jonin just now, we were able to escape smoothly. »

The middle-aged man was silent for a moment, then finally sighed : « You have a point. »

He turned to ask about another matter : « So you don’t intend to return to the clan together ? »

« I will settle her down first ; after all, I was the one who brought her out... »

Senju Matsuaki said softly, « After resolving this, I will return as soon as possible. »

This was a white lie.

To avoid affecting the future, he would return to his original time after resolving Uchiha Hikari’s affairs.

« Alright. »

The middle-aged man nodded slightly and took a deep look at Senju Matsuaki.

...

The middle-aged man left with the clansmen first.

Senju Matsuaki walked to Uchiha Hikari’s side and sat down beside her.

Moonlight spilled down through the gaps in the leaves, casting mottled shadows on the ground.

The girl hugged her knees, staring blankly at the distant mountains, not knowing what she was thinking.

« Thank you for your help just now. »

Senju Matsuaki said softly.

Uchiha Hikari did not respond, only slightly turning her head, her dark, lively eyes examining him.

« Why did you save me ? »

She finally asked, her voice carrying a trace of confusion, « I am Uchiha, your enemy. »

« Shouldn’t you have killed me in the dungeon ? »

« Enemy ? »

Senju Matsuaki smiled, « Do you think we are enemies ? »

« Isn’t that so ? »

« I don’t think so. »

Senju Matsuaki said earnestly, « In my eyes, you are just a poor person trapped in a cage, a microcosm of the pain of an era. »

« And I just happened to have the ability to help you open that door. »

Uchiha Hikari was silent.

For a long time, she lowered her head, her voice trembling slightly : « I don’t know where to go. »

« Then just follow me for now. »

Senju Matsuaki stood up and extended his hand to her, « When you’ve figured it out, you can leave at any time. »

Uchiha Hikari looked up at him, a complex expression flashing in her eyes.

In the end, she reached out and grasped that warm palm.

« Okay. »
