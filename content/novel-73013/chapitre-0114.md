# Chapitre 114 — Tremor-Tremor Fruit!!!

After weighing the pros and cons in his mind, Senju Matsuaki decisively placed the order.

Watching his points, which had exceeded five figures, rapidly decrease and return to four figures, he couldn’t help but feel a twinge of pain.

However, his attention quickly shifted from the platform interface before him to the palm of his hand.

At some unknown point, a small, exquisite golden fishing hook appeared in his palm, and the cold metallic sensation transmitted from the point where his palm touched the hook.

« It’s meant to be used with a fishing rod, which means I still have to go find a fishing rod to use this golden fishing hook. »

After looking around, Senju Matsuaki helplessly stood up, attracting the attention of Hyuga Tsukuyomi, who was quietly waiting on the sidelines.

« Tsukuyomi, I have something to take care of at home, I’ll be back in a bit. »

« Eh, alright, be careful on the way~ »

After greeting her, Senju Matsuaki turned and hurried away.

His second round of battles had already ended, and it was estimated that there were still several hours until the third round of selections.

The Chunin Exams did not restrict candidates from leaving the arena ; they only needed to be present when it was their turn to fight.

Therefore, even though he left through the exit of the lounge at this moment, no staff members stopped him.

At this time, Konoha appeared somewhat deserted ; there were few people on the streets, and the owners of some shops along the street were napping in their chairs.

After heading straight home, Senju Matsuaki opened the utility room that was usually kept locked, and after some searching, he took out an old fishing rod.

For a Ninja, a fishing rod was not an unfamiliar tool.

Besides being used for leisure, fishing rods could be used by Ninjas during missions to catch fish in the wild to satisfy hunger, and some even used fishing rods as a weapon.

After Senju Matsuaki wiped the layer of dust off it with a damp cloth, he ran to the edge of the pond in his backyard.

This fishing rod hadn’t been used for many years, but apart from being a bit old, it still had all its necessary functions.

After attaching the golden fishing hook, Senju Matsuaki, with slightly rusty movements, cast the fishing rod. The hook traced a golden arc in the air before falling into the water, creating ripples.

The pond was stocked with some ornamental fish, which had no interest in the baitless hook and, after idling nearby for a while, swam elsewhere.

« Is this not how it’s used ? »

Five minutes passed, and looking at the motionless hook, Senju Matsuaki couldn’t help but scratch his head.

According to the instructions given by the Golden Finger, it could be used with any fishing rod, and the method of use was naturally just like fishing.

However, this scene, which seemed like « willing victims taking the bait, » made him feel a bit hesitant.

Just as Senju Matsuaki was hesitating about whether to change methods, ripples suddenly appeared on the water surface near the previously dead-still golden fishing hook.

A golden light flashed, and a pitch-black hole suddenly opened at the bottom of the water beneath the golden fishing rod, as if possessing an attractive force, sucking the hook into it.

A moment later, a tugging sensation came from the fishing rod in his hand ; even someone like Senju Matsuaki, who wasn’t very good at fishing, understood that a « fish » had taken the bait.

Senju Matsuaki gripped the fishing rod tightly with one hand, overcoming the pulling force from the other end, while his other hand quickly reeled in the fishing line.

After some tugging, the golden fishing hook emerged again from the black hole it had just disappeared into, bringing out a blue, gourd-shaped fruit with a strange and eerie appearance, splashing out of the water.

Senju Matsuaki manipulated the fishing rod, letting the fruit land steadily in his hand.

This fruit was entirely blue, shaped like a gourd, and covered in a large number of twisted patterns, making it look unappetizing.

With this classic shape, Senju Matsuaki knew it was a Devil Fruit without even needing to look at the description.

The only thing that made him curious was what kind of ability this Devil Fruit possessed.

He had a slight impression of some fruits that appeared more frequently, such as the Mera Mera no Mi, the Rumble-Rumble Fruit, etc., which could be identified just by their flame-like or lightning-like shapes.

However, there were some fruits whose abilities could not be determined solely from their appearance, or whose forms had not been displayed.

Therefore, Senju Matsuaki opened the notification that appeared when he had just fished up this Devil Fruit.

[Tremor-Tremor Fruit (Perfect) : The power of vibration can shake space, thereby causing the sky, the earth, the atmosphere, and the ocean to vibrate. It can trigger intense earthquakes and tsunamis, as well as powerful shockwaves, possessing immense physical destructive power and the ability to destroy the world. It is hailed as the « Strongest Paramecia » Devil Fruit ! The curse of the sea has been removed ; it has reached perfection !]

! ! !

Three exclamation marks were the only way to describe Senju Matsuaki’s mood at this moment.

After seeing what kind of Devil Fruit this was, his pair of crimson eyes lit up, and his face revealed a look of surprise and joy !

The Tremor-Tremor Fruit, originally belonging to Whitebeard Edward Newgate, was hailed as the strongest Paramecia fruit.

Its ability was simple : the power of vibration, unadorned yet powerful !

Through the power of vibration, it could trigger tremors in the earth, sea, and atmosphere, possessing the power of a natural disaster.

When the ability was pushed to its limit, it could even cause space itself to shatter into cracks !

The only flaw of the original Tremor-Tremor Fruit was probably the curse of the sea, yet now that only flaw had also been removed.

This was truly worth it !

Holding the Tremor-Tremor Fruit in his hands like a precious treasure, Senju Matsuaki casually placed the fishing rod by the side of the pond, and the golden fishing hook, having completed its mission, turned into dust and dissipated bit by bit.

With the Tremor-Tremor Fruit in hand, the next thing that troubled Senju Matsuaki was how to consume it.

Another major characteristic of Devil Fruits was their extremely bizarre and terrible taste.

However, in the face of the terrifying power that the Tremor-Tremor Fruit could bring, this terrible taste was just a happy problem.

Senju Matsuaki did not hesitate ; he drew the short blade from his back and directly sliced the Tremor-Tremor Fruit open, revealing the strangely colored flesh inside.

Consuming a Devil Fruit did not require eating it all ; even eating just a small piece of the flesh would have the same effect.

After cutting off a piece of the flesh, Senju Matsuaki endured the strange taste, stuffed it directly into his mouth, and swallowed it in one gulp.

Just as the rumors said, the taste of the Devil Fruit could indeed be considered the worst thing he had eaten in all these years.

However, after having fully prepared himself mentally, this taste did not exceed Senju Matsuaki’s threshold of tolerance.

After swallowing the Tremor-Tremor Fruit, he clearly felt a power take root and sprout within his body, gradually filling his limbs and bones.

The power of vibration ! !

Senju Matsuaki stretched out his right hand and gripped it tightly ; the power of vibration erupted from his right fist as the center, causing a muffled sound in the air.

The trees not far away were affected by the power of vibration, their trunks instantly bursting and falling, kicking up a cloud of wood chips.

After all, he was in his own courtyard, so to avoid damaging the house and buildings, Senju Matsuaki only tried it out lightly.

Feeling the power of the Tremor-Tremor Fruit, Senju Matsuaki smiled with satisfaction.

The power of vibration and the Super Strength of their Senju Clan were somewhat similar in effect, but their principles were completely different.

Super Strength was essentially about manipulating Chakra to gather and burst at a specific point, thereby generating huge impact and vibration force in a certain part of the body ; it was a taijutsu technique.

Whereas the power of vibration was vibration itself ; once this power was released, it was as unstoppable as a landslide or tsunami.

The release of Super Strength required precise control, while the Tremor-Tremor Fruit was truly about grand, sweeping movements !

After disposing of the remaining fruit flesh, which had already lost its effect, Senju Matsuaki put away the fishing rod, shouldered his short blade, and hurried back to the Chunin Exams site.
