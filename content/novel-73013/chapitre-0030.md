# Chapitre 30 — Requisition · Sign-in System!

« Protecting the Village’s safety is also our responsibility, and my Byakugan happens to be suitable for surveillance, » Hyuga Tsukuyomi said earnestly.

Seeing this, the gazes of Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama softened. Looking at the two, they felt satisfied.

« You two are truly good children. Then, this mission is entrusted to you, » Senju Hashirama said.

After saying this, he took back the other two scrolls, leaving only the one in the middle, while simultaneously making the corresponding records.

« When the delegation from the Hidden Cloud Village enters Konoha, that is when your mission begins. »

« However, you can rest assured. Your main task will be surveillance. If any conflict occurs, other Chunin and Jonin on duty will come to support you. »

After recording the relevant information, Senju Hashirama added.

After chatting for a few more moments, Senju Matsuaki and Hyuga Tsukuyomi took their leave.

He was not overly worried about the Hidden Cloud Village delegation coming to Konoha or the future Joint Chunin Exams.

With Konoha’s current top-tier combat power, there was no room for the Hidden Cloud Village to launch any sort of « Konoha Crush Plan. »

The reason the Hidden Cloud Village wanted to hold the Joint Chunin Exams was at most to take the opportunity to understand Konoha’s development or steal some intelligence.

However, thinking of the Hidden Cloud Village’s future track record, it could not be ruled out that they might kidnap Konoha’s Kekkei Genkai Ninjas.

« But the current Konoha is still too different from the future. Logically speaking, such a mission should fall under the scope of the Anbu, » Senju Matsuaki thought to himself.

The current Konoha had not yet established the Anbu, leading to certain functions that would be handled by the Anbu in the future being issued to Ninjas in the form of missions.

It wasn’t just the lack of Anbu ; organizations like the Konoha Military Police Force hadn’t been established either, so public security work was currently also handled by issuing periodic missions.

« Maybe I can suggest it to Uncle and the Second Uncle later. The current organizational system of Konoha is indeed not yet perfect. »

« Matsumi, see you tomorrow~ »

« See you, Tsukuyomi. »

Soon, the two reached a fork in the road, the time to say goodbye.

It seemed that in order to catch up with Senju Matsuaki’s back, Hyuga Tsukuyomi had been training very hard.

Besides the rapid progress in Byakugan and Gentle Fist, she had also filled in the gaps in her Ninjutsu, and now she had the standard of a Chunin.

Therefore, when the registration for the Chunin Exams opened in a while, both of them would choose to submit their applications.

...

Returning home, Senju Matsuaki closed the sliding door and hung his short sword on the hook on the wall.

The technology of this era was still very backward, unlike the future with refrigerators, televisions, computers, and various home appliances.

But under his careful organization and cleaning, the interior of this room appeared quite cozy.

The room was surrounded by paper partitions on wooden frames, and soft light filtered through the partitions, creating a warm atmosphere.

In an alcove on one side hung a landscape painting with profound artistic conception, a collection piece of Matsumi’s father before he passed away.

And below the alcove was a vase with carefully pruned flowers, adding a touch of elegance to the room.

The vase for the flower arrangement had been placed there by the mother of this life of Matsumi.

When he was a little older, he moved back to his own home from his Uncle Hashirama’s house, and at that time, this bunch of flowers had long since withered.

Matsumi himself had no interest in flowers and plants, so this vase had been empty for about three or four years.

It wasn’t until Hyuga Tsukuyomi came to visit Matsumi’s home some time ago and noticed the vase that she kept it in mind and brought a bouquet of flowers to keep in the vase during her next visit.

Sitting on the chair in front of the coffee table, Matsumi browsed through today’s promotional options as usual.

The appearance of this promotional option had caused Matsumi to develop the habit of checking the refreshed promotional options every day.

This was similar to the daily check-ins and daily tasks of various mobile games in his previous life, attracting players to log in every day.

And the attractive part of the promotional options was that sometimes they could really burst out with suitable good things.

« Let me see what today’s promotional options are. »

Senju Matsuaki took a deep breath, his mind immersed in the goldfinger requisition platform.

The first item, the Mera Mera no Mi, original price ten thousand, now at 50% of the original price, only needing five thousand points.

After pondering for a few seconds, Matsumi decisively gave up on this option.

In the world of sailing, there were a bunch of people who could use fire without eating the Mera Mera no Mi, not to mention in this Ninja World where most Ninjas knew Fire Release Ninjutsu.

As a goldfinger, the Mera Mera no Mi could bring a decent boost, but unfortunately, the upper limit was not high.

Moreover, by now, devil fruit-type goldfingers had long developed into top-tier Logia types like the Pika Pika no Mi, Magu Magu no Mi, Goro Goro no Mi, or Mythical Zoan types like the Uo Uo no Mi, Model : Seiryu.

The Mera Mera no Mi had long lost its competitiveness among the goldfingers of the protagonists of the myriad worlds.

The second item, the third item, the fourth item...

Either the goldfinger itself was not suitable, or the price was too high.

Seeing that none of the promotional options refreshed today were suitable for selection, Matsumi did not feel too disappointed.

After several months of attempts, he had now discovered the pattern in this.

Most of the goldfingers that appeared as promotional options were either extremely expensive or chicken ribs.

Rather than spending points to requisition or commandeer unnecessary goldfingers for the sake of so-called discounts, it was better to get higher returns at the original price.

However, just as Matsumi had adjusted his mindset and intended to close the interface to continue his training for the day at the training ground.

His gaze suddenly fixed on the fifth option, and his calmed mood instantly became agitated.

« Requisition · Sign-in System, at 30% of the original price... »

Because the platform’s content was all-encompassing, even for goldfingers, there existed the possibility of having the same name but vastly different specific content.

Therefore, just looking at the name was not enough ; a more clear and convenient way was to view the specific content in detail.

[Requisition · Sign-in System : By checking in at specific characters or in specific locations, you can obtain random rewards related to the check-in object. You can check in once a day. Original price : 500 points per day. Promotional price : 150 points per day.]

The introduction in the detailed content was still concise and easy to understand.

This type of setting for a goldfinger was considered relatively common and cliché among the cheater army.

« If it requires points, the original price is 500 points to requisition for one day, and after the 30% discount, it only takes 150 points. »

Senju Matsuaki’s expression was uncertain, his mind calculating whether this transaction was cost-effective.

Over the past few months, he and Hyuga Tsukuyomi had performed C-rank missions many times, some of which also triggered his goldfinger, simultaneously issuing missions with relevant content.

Therefore, having accumulated over such a long time, his points had already exceeded three thousand.

Originally, he could only requisition this goldfinger for six days at five hundred per day, but now with the 30% discount, he could requisition it for twenty days.

Calculated this way, the cost-performance ratio was indeed very high, but the only concern was that the rewards obtained from checking in were random.
