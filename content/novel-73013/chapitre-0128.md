# Chapitre 128 — You Said How Much???

Inside the examinee lounge, as one of the participants in the finals, Dou from Kumogakure was feeling some pressure.

Cold sweat beaded on Dou’s forehead, and he subconsciously muttered, « This… that can’t be right… »

He certainly had self-awareness regarding his own strength.

He was a standout among the younger generation, but in the eyes of old-guard Ninjas like Shamon, he was probably nothing at all.

Looking at an entrance on the viewing platform.

The man pushing a wheelchair and the girl sitting in it stopped moving.

They both possessed a pair of pure white eyes ; it was the siblings, Hyuga Teruake and Hyuga Tsukuyomi.

Tsukuyomi had woken up from her coma earlier than the medical Ninjas had anticipated, and the first thing she wanted to do upon waking was to return to the examination hall.

She told her brother, Hyuga Teruake, that she wanted to witness Senju Matsuaki’s victory firsthand.

But, victory ?

Was that really possible ?

Hyuga Teruake thought his sister was just blindly optimistic.

In his view, three major disparities had formed a huge chasm between Senju Matsuaki and Yan from Sunagakure.

The age gap meant one was a fledgling rookie, and the other was a seasoned veteran.

The gap in strength—in a head-on confrontation where sneak attacks and poison were impossible, how could a Genin defeat a former Jonin ?

The gap in Bloodline Limit—how could Senju Matsuaki, who hadn’t awakened a Bloodline Limit, contend with Yan’s Magnet Release ?

Therefore, Hyuga Teruake never thought Senju Matsuaki could win.

As for the vow of certain victory the boy had made in the medical room not long ago, he hadn’t taken it to heart either.

But the facts told him that he was ridiculously wrong.

Watching the heroic-looking boy on the field, Hyuga Teruake subconsciously recalled the words he had once heard from his elders.

In front of ordinary Ninjas, the Hyuga Clan was a prestigious family in the Ninja World, just like the Senju Clan and the Uchiha Clan.

But when facing the true powerhouses of these two great Ninja clans, they were as weak as children.

The Ninja who said these words was none other than his and Tsukuyomi’s father, the previous Clan Chief of the Hyuga Clan, a Ninja from the same era as Senju Hashirama and Uchiha Madara.

On the wheelchair, Hyuga Tsukuyomi had a small blanket covering her legs.

Her face, which had appeared somewhat pale due to her injuries, was now flushed, and her pure white eyes were filled with a brilliant luster as she stared blankly at the boy standing on the water.

...

The scene on the field was deadlocked for a moment.

Personnel from outside the examination hall intervening in the examination was, by logic, against the exam rules.

But after all, the First Kazekage had a special status, so how to handle this still required asking the Hokage for instructions.

Senju Matsuaki knew clearly in his heart that the victory in this semi-final undoubtedly belonged to him.

The only pity was that he felt Yan’s injuries were not severe enough.

Most of the power of the Air Quake just now had been blocked by Retto and Shamon, and by the time it reached Yan, only some residual waves remained.

Of course, the residual waves of the Air Quake were by no means something that could be easily withstood by a Ninja’s physical constitution.

According to Senju Matsuaki’s observation, Yan’s internal organs had all suffered varying degrees of concussive damage.

Such injuries did not look serious on the surface, but the time required to recover would likely be longer than for external wounds.

But this was clearly not enough.

In his expectations, he needed to at least break the opponent’s arms and legs to count as avenging Tsukuyomi.

Senju Matsuaki’s gaze scanned the opponent’s arms and calves with ill intent.

However, Matsuaki also knew that with the First Kazekage and the future Second Kazekage both entering the field, there was no chance to deal with Yan now.

Still, there would be plenty of time in the future.

« This match ends here. The winner is Senju Matsuaki ! »

Senju Tobirama’s figure appeared between the two sides at some unknown time, staring at the First Kazekage Retto while simultaneously announcing the result of the battle.

Even though the First Kazekage Retto had suffered such a big loss, he unexpectedly showed no signs of anger or reproach.

After nodding slightly, the divine wind around him swept up Shamon and Yan, and flew towards the examinee lounge.

« Good lad. »

Senju Tobirama patted Matsuaki’s shoulder with satisfaction, his tone full of admiration.

The next second, Matsuaki felt an unusual Chakra conducting from Senju Tobirama’s palm, as if it were about to pull him out of this space.

Flying Thunder God Technique !

After realizing what it was, Matsuaki did not resist this Chakra.

Both of their figures disappeared from the spot simultaneously.

When the scene before his eyes became clear again, what greeted him was the familiar layout of the examinee lounge.

Crossing a distance of several hundred meters in the blink of an eye—this was the convenience of the Flying Thunder God Technique.

Not to mention the element of surprise in battle, just the aspect of traveling was already quite convenient.

Senju Matsuaki was also quite envious of this technique.

« The next match is the final, don’t be careless. »

« Remember to come home early for dinner tonight. »

After offering a few words of advice, Senju Tobirama activated the Flying Thunder God Technique again and left.

Although he came and went in a hurry, the care in his words still warmed Matsuaki’s heart.

At the moment, only one final match had yet to begin.

At this time, only Senju Matsuaki and Dou from Kumogakure remained in the examinee lounge.

Turning around and walking towards the bench in the examinee lounge, Matsuaki’s gaze met with Dou’s.

At this moment, Dou had already shaken off the shocked emotions he felt when seeing that space-shattering strike a short while ago.

The scene of space being shattered was indeed shocking.

In his heart, Dou couldn’t help but develop a sense of wariness towards Senju Matsuaki.

However, wariness did not mean fear, and it did not mean he had completely lost faith in the upcoming battle.

Having fought in the Ninja World until now, he had not failed to experience battles where the weak overcame the strong.

Among them were many powerful enemies who initially seemed impossible to defeat, but in the end, they still became dry bones.

In the less than ten minutes since the semi-final ended, Dou had been constantly simulating the scenario of his battle against Senju Matsuaki in his mind.

Compared to Yan from Sunagakure, he considered himself to have one advantage : information.

After exchanging a glance with Dou, Senju Matsuaki looked away.

Regarding Dou from Kumogakure, Senju Matsuaki had already gained quite a bit of understanding after a few contacts.

The future Third Raikage, who would hold his own against ten thousand, was already showing the rudiments of the strongest spear and strongest shield.

However, his personality was not that of a simple brute as his appearance suggested.

In Senju Matsuaki’s view, Dou from Kumogakure and Yan from Sunagakure were both vicious wolves lurking in the shadows, eyeing Konoha.

Using any means necessary for the benefit of one’s own Ninja village was nothing wrong in this world of Ninjas.

But now Konoha was his base, and his family and friends were all here.

If they wanted to plot against Konoha, they would have to get past him first.

Sitting down on the bench in the lounge, Senju Matsuaki began to pay attention to the System notifications he hadn’t had time to check while in the examination hall.

However, when he saw the specific content of the notification, his expression froze instantly.

[500 Points received !]

[5000 Points received !]

Wait, you said how much ? ? ?

Senju Matsuaki’s eyes widened sharply.
