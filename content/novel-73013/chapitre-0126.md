# Chapitre 126 — First Kazekage Retto Enters the Fray!

« Kazekage, don’t be in such a rush. Observe a little longer. »

The First Raikage stared intently at the arena, not wanting to be distracted by the First Kazekage’s movements.

At this moment, the Raikage felt quite grave.

He had originally thought that Dou’s strength was greater than Yan’s, and Yan’s strength was greater than Senju Matsuaki’s.

The result of this battle should have been Senju Matsuaki losing after trading blows with Yan for a few rounds.

Afterward, Dou would cleanly and decisively defeat Sunagakure’s Yan in the finals, establishing the reputation of their Kumogakure younger generation as being far ahead in the Ninja World.

In just these few minutes, his original thoughts had undergone an earth-shattering change.

That kid from Sunagakure can’t block it !

Not just the kid from Sunagakure, I’m afraid even their Kumogakure’s Dou would also...

Thinking of this, the First Raikage’s brows furrowed tightly, and a look of contemplation flowed in his eyes.

Hearing this, Retto almost rolled his eyes on the spot.

« Don’t be in a rush, » he thought. It’s not your Kumogakure Genius facing that terrifying might down there.

Yan’s talent was among the most powerful of any young person he had ever seen.

If he really got killed, Sunagakure would lose a Kage-level powerhouse in the future.

Thinking of this, Retto gritted his teeth, his body wrapped as if by a hurricane, and he flew down toward the arena.

Seeing this, Senju Hashirama and the others knew that the Kazekage just wanted to save someone and had no other intentions.

Everyone present who was a Kage could understand this feeling, so they did not move to stop him.

« What... is that thing ? »

Sunagakure’s Yan still maintained his forward speed.

In his original expectation, the Iron Sand giant needles would block all of Senju Matsuaki’s retreat paths, and then he would use Magnet Release to completely bury him.

However, at this moment, looking at the approaching blade not far away, his brain fell into a brief blankness.

Taijutsu ? It didn’t seem like it.

Ninjutsu ? He had never seen or heard of it either.

What exactly was that thing ? His rich Ninja experience could not give a judgment.

Only his intuition told him that he would really die !

What reacted faster than his consciousness were his intuition and instincts.

The pitch-black Iron Sand Wings suddenly flapped, the sharp edges of the wings slicing through the air, emitting a sound like the mournful cry of an injured bird.

Then, the Iron Sand Wings dispersed and reorganized.

In just a few breaths, they transformed from bird wings into a huge « tortoise shell » blocking in front of him.

« Like a mantis trying to stop a chariot ! »

Senju Matsuaki focused his mind on the blade, only smiling indifferently at Yan’s defense.

At the same time, seeing out of the corner of his eye the few Proctors rushing over from not far away, especially the tall man wearing a hat with the character « Wind » on it, the last bit of hesitation in Matsuaki’s heart dissipated.

If no one came to save him, he was really worried that he would accidentally kill Sunagakure’s Yan in one blow.

Although he didn’t care about Yan’s life or death, he had to be mindful of the occasion, didn’t he ?

This was the Chunin Exams, not a battlefield.

Yan would pay the price for his previous actions, but that price didn’t necessarily have to be getting shattered into minced meat here.

Now, someone was here to cover for him, especially with the Kazekage entering the fray himself.

Then he would no longer need to have so many scruples.

At this moment, in his eyes and heart, there was only the blade he was about to swing.

With his mind clear, Senju Matsuaki’s control over the Tremor-Tremor Fruit actually improved slightly at the moment he swung his blade.

The white orb of light condensing the power of vibration on the blade shone brilliantly, and cracks could be vaguely seen around it.

These cracks were not produced on any object, but were attached to the void out of thin air, turning the space that was originally elusive to ordinary people into a fragile mirror !

Then, the blade swung out !

First, the Iron Sand giant needles that had already closed in were shattered the moment they touched the power of vibration, falling toward the water surface below like ordinary sand.

In a breath, Senju Matsuaki had already arrived in front of Yan with his blade.

« Examinee Senju Matsuaki, please stop ! »

Several Konoha Proctors responsible for maintaining order in the arena approached the center of the battlefield a step before the Kazekage, attempting to stop his actions.

Although they also wished this Magnet Release Ninja from Sunagakure who had dealt ruthless blows to Konoha examinees would be killed here, if they failed to act as Proctors, they would likely be criticized by other Ninja villages.

Seeing this, Senju Matsuaki’s gaze quickly swept over these few Konoha Proctors.

They were all of Jonin strength, but not the elite among Jonin ; they probably couldn’t even compare to Yan’s strength.

« You can’t block it. Let the Kazekage come ! » Matsuaki said directly.

Hearing this, the few Proctors did not feel slighted ; instead, after exchanging glances, they retreated violently with a sense of relief.

They also had self-awareness.

That terrifying power made them feel chilled just by getting this close.

Moreover, the one facing this blade was an examinee from another Ninja village.

What was the point of risking their lives for an examinee from another Ninja village ?

Coming to dissuade him just now was only because their duty required them to step forward.

Now that the Kazekage had come, they naturally retired after completing their task.

« Senju brat, stop right there ! ! »

The next instant, the figure of the First Kazekage Retto swept over like a hurricane, stirring up surging whirlpools on the water surface.

In this era when Ninja villages had not long been established in various countries, he, as the strongest Ninja of the Land of Wind at this time, was at the Peak of his strength !

Retto did not have any Bloodline Limit like Magnet Release ; he reached the pinnacle of the Land of Wind by relying on his Wind Release, which had reached the Transcendent realm.

Circling around his body was a hurricane known as the « Divine Wind. »

For ordinary Ninjas, this hurricane was like a natural disaster sent down by the gods.

In the desert, he could easily stir up a massive sandstorm by relying on Wind Release.

As he swept over carrying the Divine Wind, Senju Matsuaki, who had already swung the short blade in his hand, also narrowed his eyes.

However, it was just right.

A mere future Third Kazekage would certainly not be able to block this slash of his right now.

Sand that shattered at a touch could not serve as a whetstone.

And the First Kazekage Retto, whose strength had been rooted at the Kage level for a long time, was just right for him to test the power of the Tremor-Tremor Fruit !

« Quake ! ! ! »

The short blade, condensing the power of vibration, swung forward with the force of a thousand jun.

The next instant, in the shocked eyes of countless people, space split open !

Crack—

The space, which in people’s cognition could never crack no matter what, was now like a shattered mirror.

The cracks spread outward from the point where the blade touched the air, as if forming a large, cracked net.

« Wind Release : Sandstorm ! ! »

Seeing this scene, Retto’s eyes widened in fury, his hands formed seals, and the Chakra in his body was driven crazily.

From that space that was shattered like a mirror surface, he even felt a breath of death.

« Lord Retto ! ! »

On the spectator stand, the future Second Kazekage Shamon, who had originally thought that there would be no problem with the First Kazekage Retto entering the fray personally, was suddenly panicked.

He saw his body shoot out from the spectator stand, and at the same time, he threw out a scroll.

The next second, the short blade carrying the power of vibration collided with the hurricane released by the First Kazekage Retto.

Crack—

Another crisp shattering sound rang out, like a mirror being smashed.

At the same time, terrifying turbulence swept across the arena in an instant, forming countless water tornadoes of various sizes, making it impossible for the outside world to see the situation.
