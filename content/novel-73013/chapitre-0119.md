# Chapitre 119 — Magnet Release! Eight Trigrams: Vacuum Palm! A Battle of Disparity!

« Lord Yan’s Magnet Release is not something that the mere Hyuga Clan can measure up to ! »

In the viewing stands, a Sand Ninja examinee sneered, his tone full of disdain for the Hyuga Clan.

They had only heard of the Hyuga Clan’s prowess through rumors, and even those were few and far between.

After all, in Konoha Village of the Land of Fire, the Senju Clan and the Uchiha Clan were more prominent, and the Hyuga Clan’s fame was still below the two.

As for Yan’s strength, these Sand Ninjas had seen it with their own eyes ; it was clear for all to see.

In their view, the Hyuga Clan’s Gentle Fist was nothing more than a pair of flesh palms ; when could that ever hope to rival the indestructible Iron Sand ?

And the actual situation was consistent with what most of the audience thought.

For Hyuga Tsukuyomi at this moment, there was only one technique capable of instantly defending against the sky-filling Iron Sand : Shigure.

« Eight Trigrams : Heavenly Spin ! ! »

Having practiced Eight Trigrams : Heavenly Spin many times and applied it in actual combat, she used it now with increasing ease.

The pale blue Chakra sphere spun at high speed, colliding with the falling Iron Sand : Shigure, causing the falling black needles to bounce off in all directions and fly away.

« An interesting technique, but it’s a pity you encountered me. »

The Hyuga Clan’s Eight Trigrams : Heavenly Spin caught the eye of Yan, who was also skilled in defensive Ninjutsu, but upon closer inspection, he could easily spot its flaws.

The defensive power of Eight Trigrams : Heavenly Spin was indeed formidable, but this defensive method combining taijutsu and Chakra was destined to consume a large amount of stamina and Chakra, making it unsustainable.

As a Ninjutsu-type Ninja skilled in long-range attacks, he was destined to win simply by continuously draining his opponent’s stamina.

Moreover, there was no such thing as an « absolute » defense ; as long as an attack exceeded the threshold that the defense could withstand, any defensive measure would be breached ! !

As Eight Trigrams : Heavenly Spin dissipated, Hyuga Tsukuyomi frowned slightly, her breathing becoming a bit heavier.

« This is bad ; the stamina consumption is greater than I imagined. I cannot just sit here and wait for defeat. »

The Eight Trigrams : Heavenly Spin just now was the longest she had used it since learning it, and both her stamina and Chakra were being depleted at a frightening rate.

In contrast, Yan on the opposite side, aside from not looking very well, appeared completely at ease.

Seeing this, Hyuga Tsukuyomi had no choice but to change her strategy and adopt an offensive approach.

After controlling her breathing frequency, the blood flow in Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s body accelerated, her heart beat more powerfully, and her vitality surged.

It had been over half a year since Senju Matsuaki taught her the Breathing Technique, and she was already able to enter the state with ease and maintain the Breathing Technique during combat for long periods.

If not for the fact that she had to divide her energy among Gentle Fist, Ninjutsu, and actual combat on a daily basis, she would likely have already reached the Realm of Total Concentration : Constant.

The next instant, she vanished from her spot at an astonishing speed ; dressed in white, she seemed to transform into a white shadow as she closed the distance with her opponent.

« Since you could defend against Iron Sand : Shigure, why not try this. »

The speed Hyuga Tsukuyomi displayed at this moment did indeed make Yan take a second look, but that was all it was—just a second look.

No matter how strong a Chunin was, to a Jonin, they were still just a Chunin.

He had already buried countless enemies with such speed using his pitch-black Iron Sand ! !

At this moment, he had lost his patience.

Just a moment ago, he had eaten the spiked oden all by himself, and even drank a few mouthfuls of the soup, still wanting more.

Even now, he felt his head spinning, his stomach churning as if the sea were overturning, not to mention the other negative effects...

Therefore, he also wanted to end the battle quickly and then go deal with his physical problems.

« Magnet Release : Iron Sand Waves ! ! »

The pitch-black Iron Sand rolled and surged from behind Yan, sweeping forward like waves.

This seemingly gentle black sea wave was actually a torrent of steel capable of easily crushing flesh.

Seeing the Iron Sand waves sweeping toward her, Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s Byakugan narrowed, but there was not a trace of fear on her face.

Just as that white-clad figure collided with the pitch-black darkness, she leaped into the air and assumed the Gentle Fist stance.

« Eight Trigrams : Heavenly Spin ! ! »

A spherical Chakra shield appeared for an instant, pushing aside the pitch-black Iron Sand while simultaneously tearing an opening in the dense curtain of Iron Sand.

Amidst the gasps of the entire audience, Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s body passed through the opening, and she formed hand seals.

« Fire Style : Phoenix Sage Fire Jutsu ! ! »

Over a dozen fireballs, like balsam seeds, scattered in all directions, approaching Yan from tricky angles.

« Insignificant tricks ! ! »

Seeing this, Yan just waved his hand, and the rolling Iron Sand surged up, swallowing the fireballs in an instant.

« If that is all you have, then accept your fate of defeat ! ! »

He placed his hands flat in front of him, then lifted them upward as if holding something invisible.

The giant waves that had been rolling previously stopped for an instant, then overcame their inertia to surge backward and upward, determined to engulf Hyuga Tsukuyomi.

Feeling the black waves rolling and chasing after her, a trace of helplessness welled up in Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s heart.

If it were purely a battle of taijutsu, she was confident that with the combination of her Gentle Fist and Breathing Technique, even the current Yan would have to expend some effort to defeat her.

However, the Magnet Release Ninjutsu, a Bloodline Limit that was both long-range and capable of both offense and defense, greatly reduced her advantage in taijutsu.

And the Ninjutsu she currently mastered did not possess the penetrating power required to break through the layers of Iron Sand.

In terms of Ninjutsu, she was mainly skilled in Fire Release and Lightning Release, but as a Ninja with the strength of a Chunin, it was almost impossible to break through a Jonin’s Magnet Release blockade with conventional Fire Release or Lightning Release Ninjutsu.

« The only thing useful right now, perhaps, is... »

Before being engulfed by the Iron Sand, Hyuga Tsukuyomi suddenly recalled a move she had seen her brother use while sparring with other clansmen.

Gathering highly condensed Chakra in her palm, then using the Byakugan’s insight to precisely lock onto the target, she released the high-density Chakra in her hand to form an invisible shockwave.

Its name is...

« Eight Trigrams : Vacuum Palm ! ! »

Hyuga Tsukuyomi thought silently to herself, pulling her right palm back, and Chakra instantly condensed in her palm.

The Chakra filling her palm, as she struck forward, stirred up a sound of breaking through the air and transformed into an invisible shockwave.

« What is she doing... »

Yan’s brows furrowed deeply ; he could see the opponent’s movements, but with the naked eye alone, he could not see the impact generated by the Eight Trigrams : Vacuum Palm.

However, a subtle premonition of danger fed back by his intuition caused alarm bells to ring in his heart.

« To think... »

In the viewing stands, a flash of astonishment crossed the eyes of the Hyuga Clan Chief, Hyuga Teruake.

Veins had appeared on his face near his temples at some point, taking in every move of both sides on the field.

He did not remember having taught Hyuga Tsukuyomi the Eight Trigrams : Vacuum Palm.

On one hand, he was busy with his own affairs ; on the other, he believed it was not yet the right time.

Now it seemed he had underestimated his sister’s talent and growth speed.

To be able to replicate Eight Trigrams : Vacuum Palm in actual combat just by having seen it once—such talent made even him, who was considered a Genius of the Hyuga Main Branch back in his day, feel amazed ! !
