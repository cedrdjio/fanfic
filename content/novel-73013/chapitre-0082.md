# Chapitre 82 — Holy Crap! Wood Release!!

« Holy crap ! ! »

A Ninja sitting in front of the crystal ball suddenly slammed the table and jumped to his feet, staring with his mouth agape at Senju Tobirama in the main seat.

« What are you shouting for ? You scared the... Holy crap ! ! »

Another Ninja nearby, startled by the sudden, strange cry, turned his head to complain.

His gaze happened to catch the crystal ball in front of the other man, just in time to see thick wooden materials bursting out of the ground.

And so, there were now two people in the office who had slammed the table, jumped up, and were standing there with their mouths agape, unable to speak.

« What is all this fuss about ? »

Meeting their shocked gazes, Senju Tobirama frowned and stood up, walking over.

Although the other Ninjas were curious about the situation, they were tied up with official business and couldn’t come over to join in.

« I remember telling you all before that when conducting intelligence work, you must first maintain composure under any circumstances. »

After approaching, Senju Tobirama looked at the immature and unstable behavior of his two subordinates and gave them a symbolic reprimand.

Then, he turned his gaze to the image on the crystal ball.

He was also curious what kind of situation could make the talents he had carefully selected show such a shocked expression.

At this moment, Senju Matsuaki’s Wood Release : Four Pillars House Technique was already in its final stages.

The two-story wooden structure looked as if it had been built by a carpenter with real wood ; the joints were tight and seamless, and the doors and windows were perfectly positioned.

One could even vaguely see the interior structure of the wooden house from the outside.

The structure of the first floor was relatively simple ; from the perspective of the Telescope Technique, one could only roughly see a ladder leading to the second floor.

The second floor’s structure was relatively complex, seemingly divided into two rooms, each equipped with a slightly crude tatami.

Seeing this, Senju Tobirama’s expression froze.

« I... *cough cough*. »

Senju Tobirama, who had almost cursed like his subordinates, stopped himself in time and coughed twice in embarrassment.

Suppressing his inner shock and surprise, he looked around the room and finally landed his gaze on the two subordinates who had first discovered this.

« Keep this matter confidential for now ; it must not be leaked until it is displayed in public. Do you understand ? »

« Yes ! Lord Tobirama ! »

The two subordinates nodded repeatedly like chickens pecking at rice.

« This kid Matsumi, no wonder his Chakra levels are exaggeratedly high now ; it turns out it’s because... »

After his shock slightly subsided, Senju Tobirama felt both regret and relief.

As a member of the Senju Clan, he had once imagined that one day he would awaken Wood Release like his older brother, Senju Hashirama, and then slaughter anyone—god or Buddha—on the battlefield.

It was just a pity that Wood Release was like math ; if you couldn’t do it, you couldn’t do it.

Although he had no obsession with Wood Release, he still felt some regret in his heart.

Now seeing that his older brother’s Wood Release finally had a successor, he felt very relieved.

On this side, after building the wooden house, Senju Matsuaki stepped into the house with Hyuga Tsukuyomi.

« So amazing ! Did Matsumi also awaken the same Bloodline Limit as Master Hashirama ? »

After her initial shock, Hyuga Tsukuyomi quickly accepted this new premise that Senju Matsuaki had awakened Wood Release.

After all, Senju Matsuaki and Senju Hashirama were both members of the Senju Clan, and the blood connection between cousins was relatively close.

Since Senju Hashirama could awaken Wood Release, there was no reason why Senju Matsuaki couldn’t.

« It just awakened suddenly a while ago. » Senju Matsuaki said with a smile.

As for the specific reasons, it was too complicated, and even if he explained, it would likely be difficult to make clear for the time being.

« Stairs, doors, windows, tatami, chairs—it has everything. Did Matsumi develop this Ninjutsu himself ? » Hyuga Tsukuyomi asked curiously.

Although she was no longer amazed by the fact that Senju Matsuaki had awakened Wood Release, it didn’t stop her from looking at him with admiration, her pure Byakugan seemingly dotted with little stars.

She remembered that Senju Hashirama did not know this Ninjutsu.

After all, during countless missions before, Senju Hashirama’s Wood Clone had always slept in the open with the two of them.

If there was such a good thing as the Four Pillars House, he should have brought it out long ago.

« This, I suppose it’s half-developed. » Senju Matsuaki scratched his head and said.

The origin of this technique was equally complicated.

The prototype was, of course, the Wood Release Ninjutsu of Yamato, the Wood Release Ninja from the future in the original timeline.

Of course, this couldn’t be called plagiarism ; after all, Yamato’s Four Pillars House Technique only provided a source of inspiration for Senju Matsuaki.

He didn’t know how to use this Ninjutsu specifically or what the principle of casting it was ; it was only by combining his learning experience of other Wood Release Ninjutsu during this time that he was able to replicate it.

Wood Release : Four Pillars House, a Wood Release Ninjutsu that lacked lethality but was excellent in functionality.

Relying on precise Chakra control, one could instantly raise a wooden building from the ground to serve as a temporary base.

As for the facilities inside this wooden house, they were built entirely based on the caster’s will and Chakra control ability.

And Senju Matsuaki was not limited to just replicating this Ninjutsu ; he had also made some improvements to it.

After all, his Wood Release was already equivalent to Senju Hashirama’s original version, whereas Yamato’s Wood Release was just a weakened version after transplanting Hashirama cells.

« Tonight, you sleep in the room on the inside, and I’ll sleep in the room on the outside. »

Senju Matsuaki quite gentlemanly assigned the safer, inner room to Hyuga Tsukuyomi.

« As for the issue of keeping watch, you don’t need to worry ; the outside of this wooden house has matching traps, and besides, if anyone approaches within a certain range, I can sense it immediately. »

« What a convenient Ninjutsu. »

« Before going to sleep at night, I will adjust the position and size of the doors and windows to a certain extent so that the outside world cannot discern the layout inside. »

Glancing at the two Shadow Clones that had returned to the distance one after another outside the window, Senju Matsuaki continued : « Then let’s go down and eat something simple now. »

The second stage of the Chunin Exams was essentially a survival exercise, which was the main reason why the exam site was chosen in a primeval forest.

For an ordinary person, they would encounter terrifying threats in the primeval forest such as venomous snakes, poisonous insects, wild beasts, and harsh environments.

If one chose to travel along the safest and most secure route, they would soon face a shortage of food and water, having to risk danger to obtain survival resources.

Taking the location where the two of them were currently as an example, the environment and scenery by the creek were quite good, but other wild beasts would also frequent this place.

For Ninjas, the environments encountered when executing missions were sometimes worse and more extreme than primeval forests.

If one were stopped by such a survival exercise, wanting to become a Chunin capable of handling things alone would undoubtedly be a pipe dream.

Outside the wooden house, Senju Matsuaki’s two Shadow Clones : one was holding two fish the size of an arm in his hand, and the other was holding dried wood that had been picked up in his arms.

After simple processing, they used Fire Release Ninjutsu to start a fire, and the charred aroma of the two grilled fish gradually spread in the air.
