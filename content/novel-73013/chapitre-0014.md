# Chapitre 14 — Monkey King · Enma!

Senju Matsuaki’s silver short hair was gently stirred by the breeze, his right hand reaching from his right shoulder to his back, gripping the hilt of his short blade.

His red eyes held no other emotions or distractions at this moment, looking intently at Hiruzen Sarutobi in front of him.

Just as Tsugikuni Yoriichi had taught him, he swung his blade with a focused mind.

« Have you entered the zone this quickly... »

Seeing that the face which had been gentle just a moment ago had become cold and stern, Hiruzen Sarutobi couldn’t help but mutter to himself.

The next second, the battle began, and both of them left their spots and charged at each other.

The instant the battle started, Senju Matsuaki drew the short blade from his back, ready to strike.

With his Breathing Technique running at full speed, a large amount of oxygen was inhaled into his abdomen.

His heart, blood, and muscles seemed to boil, filled with immense power.

After closing the distance, Senju Matsuaki swung his short blade, slashing toward Hiruzen Sarutobi.

Hiruzen Sarutobi’s expression did not change as he held a Kunai to block the short blade.

Hiss—

Feeling the web between his thumb and index finger on his right hand go numb from the impact, Hiruzen Sarutobi was secretly shocked.

Only during this clash did he realize just how great Senju Matsuaki’s strength truly was.

Meanwhile, Senju Matsuaki also wore a solemn expression, feeling a sense of admiration in his heart.

He was truly worthy of being the Ninja who would later wield a giant staff to go head-to-head with the Nine-Tailed ; even now, his strength was not to be underestimated.

However, with the physique of the Senju Clan combined with Total Concentration : Constant, Senju Matsuaki’s strength was still far more dominant.

Perhaps Hiruzen Sarutobi realized this, as he no longer clashed head-on with Matsuaki.

Using the recoil from the collision between the short blade and the Kunai, he nimbly pulled back to create distance and formed hand seals.

« Fire Release : Great Fireball Technique ! »

After completing the seals, Hiruzen Sarutobi spat out raging flames, which condensed into a huge fireball that rolled forward.

As a C-rank Ninjutsu, the Great Fireball Technique was basic but very practical.

It was difficult for some Genin to master, but for the current Hiruzen Sarutobi, it was nothing more than a piece of cake.

He did not go all out immediately, choosing instead to test him with the Great Fireball Technique first.

If it were a Genin who wasn’t skilled in Ninjutsu, their only option when facing the Great Fireball would be to dodge.

However, Senju Matsuaki had no intention of doing so.

Dragon - Ox - Rabbit !

« Water Release : Water Wave ! »

After forming the seals, Senju Matsuaki held his middle and index fingers upright, placing them about a fist’s distance in front of his cheek.

His Chakra flowed rapidly within him, and a torrent of water gushed from his mouth, forming a small pool that rushed forward.

Water Wave was also a basic Ninjutsu within Water Release, its power varying depending on the user’s Chakra reserves.

Therefore, the Water Wave that Senju Matsuaki unleashed this time was actually larger than Hiruzen Sarutobi’s Great Fireball.

« What a little monster. »

Seeing his Ninjutsu easily neutralized, Hiruzen Sarutobi said with a sweat-drop.

The collision between the Great Fireball and the Water Wave created steam, shrouding the arena in a layer of mist.

Using this as cover, Senju Matsuaki held his short blade and charged at Hiruzen Sarutobi once again.

This was the reason why he hadn’t dodged earlier, but had instead used Water Wave to counter the Great Fireball.

With the mist formed by the clashing Ninjutsu, his movements would not be immediately seen by Hiruzen Sarutobi.

This way, if the opponent wanted to reposition and cast Ninjutsu, he would have to take a few slashes from him first !

Clang—

Clang—

From within the mist came several crisp sounds of metal clashing, grating on the eardrums.

« Wind Release : Gale Palm ! »

Taking advantage of the gap in the clash, Hiruzen Sarutobi quickly formed seals, and a gust of wind pressure condensed in the palm of his left hand.

As his left hand struck toward Senju Matsuaki’s chest, a fierce gale was generated between them and released in Matsuaki’s direction.

Under the force of the gale, Matsuaki’s body was uncontrollably knocked backward, and he could only rely on the Chakra concentrated at the soles of his feet to try and maintain his balance.

During this time, Hiruzen Sarutobi did not remain idle.

« Wind Release : Great Breakthrough ! »

After forming seals again, he opened his mouth and spat out a whirlwind, dispersing the mist before charging straight toward Matsuaki.

Outside the arena, the examinees who had not yet left watched the battle on the field, unable to take their eyes off it.

Flashing blades, clashing Ninjutsu !

Compared to those immature battles from earlier, the battle on the field now matched their imagination of what a Ninja should be.

« Matsuaki’s swordsmanship and Taijutsu have improved greatly, but in terms of Ninjutsu reserves, he is still far behind the monkey. »

Senju Tobirama, who was watching the battle, commented aloud.

« The monkey is indeed very talented in Ninjutsu, but I think Matsuaki is no simpleton either. »

Sarutobi Sasuke said with a smile, not knowing if he truly felt that way or if it was just a polite remark.

After the mist dissipated, the distance between the two opened up again.

Both Senju Matsuaki and Hiruzen Sarutobi wore solemn expressions.

They could both feel that the other had not yet used their full strength.

From the beginning of the battle until now, neither of them had sustained even a minor injury.

The only change was that because of the previous Ninjutsu, both of their hair and clothes were dampened by the mist, and that was all.

« Matsuaki, I’m going to get serious now, otherwise Master Tobirama will probably lecture me later. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi’s expression relaxed first as he joked.

Subsequently, his expression turned serious, and after forming hand seals, he pressed his hands onto the ground.

« Summoning Technique ! »

Poof—

A cloud of smoke rose, and after it dissipated, a white-haired monkey with a height similar to Hiruzen Sarutobi appeared beside him.

The monkey looked like a Ninja, wearing a tight-fitting suit suitable for combat, with a Konoha forehead protector on its head.

« Hiruzen. »

The monkey spoke in human language, and after noticing that they didn’t seem to be in a life-or-death struggle, it greeted Hiruzen Sarutobi.

« Yo, Enma. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi smiled and patted Enma’s arm, greeting him before turning to look at Senju Matsuaki.

« Monkey King Enma is my Summoning beast, and he grew up with me since I was a child. From here on, he and I will fight side by side. »

« Come on, Brother Hiruzen. I will also give it my all from here on out. »

Senju Matsuaki just took a deep breath, his expression becoming even more focused, yet he didn’t feel anything was amiss.

The strength of a Summoning beast is naturally a part of a Ninja’s strength.

Even in the Chunin exams, using a Summoning beast is not against the rules.

It’s just that finding or raising a powerful Summoning beast is not easy, and the Sarutobi Clan happens to be the best among them.

They have been on good terms with the Monkey clan for generations ; once a Ninja of the Sarutobi Clan gains the recognition of the Monkey clan, they can sign a summoning contract with the monkeys.

« Enma ! Adamantine Staff ! »

Hiruzen Sarutobi gave a low shout, and Enma nodded tacitly, transforming into a staff that appeared in Hiruzen Sarutobi’s hand.

The Adamantine Staff spun in his hand with a series of stylish flourishes, its aura different from before.

« Watch out ! »

After the warning, Hiruzen Sarutobi wielded the Adamantine Staff and swung it forward.

During this process, the Adamantine Staff, which was originally just the size of a normal staff, suddenly elongated and grew larger, just like a wooden stake !
