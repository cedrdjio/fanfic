# Chapitre 11 — A One-Sided Battle!

Just as the distance between the two had closed by half since the start, Senju Matsuaki moved.

He didn’t even draw the short blade from his back ; he simply raised his fist, veins bulging.

In an instant, he appeared in front of Uchiha Kai, who was charging at him.

This speed not only made Uchiha Kai look as if he had seen a ghost, but it also caused many of the spectators to wear expressions of shock.

« That kid Senju Kazuma didn’t tell me that little Senju Matsuaki had already become this strong, » Senju Hashirama said, stunned.

He didn’t use Ninjutsu ; he achieved this speed purely through physical strength.

Even among Chunin, only those skilled in Taijutsu could accomplish this.

Furthermore, Senju Matsuaki was not even seven years old yet.

However, not even Senju Kazuma, who had instructed him for a period of time, knew about Senju Matsuaki’s changes.

After all, for the past month or so, Senju Kazuma had been a very busy man, preparing for his upcoming wedding.

And the leap in Senju Matsuaki’s strength had occurred precisely during this month of arduous training and « cheating. »

« Move, damn it ! ! »

Uchiha Kai, who had already begun to sense that something was wrong, tried his best to react.

He raised his right hand, tightly gripping a Kunai, held it across his chest, and swung it forward.

In response, a cold glint flashed in Senju Matsuaki’s red eyes.

The fist that had originally been aimed at his opponent’s abdomen suddenly changed direction and slammed heavily into Uchiha Kai’s wrist.

Snap—

Even from a distance, the sound of bones breaking could be clearly heard.

Uchiha Kai’s right hand lost all strength, and the Kunai, under such immense force, flew away and embedded itself into the ground.

Just as he was about to cry out in pain, Senju Matsuaki’s other fist slammed into his abdomen.

This punch wasn’t as forceful as the previous one.

After all, the target area was different ; if he had used the same force as before, Uchiha Kai’s internal organs might have been shattered.

Although he disliked his opponent, there was no need to be that ruthless.

The punch that broke the opponent’s wrist bone was just a lesson for Uchiha Kai, who had previously ruthlessly broken the bones of other examinees.

But even so, this punch still caused Uchiha Kai to double over like a shrimp.

Seeing this, Senju Matsuaki calmly withdrew his fist, stepped back a few paces, and looked at the Proctor outside the arena.

The Proctor, still surprised by the turn of the battle, snapped back to reality and began to announce the result.

« In this match, Senju Matsuaki is the winner ! »

Generally speaking, after a battle ends, the Reconciliation Seal should be performed to show friendliness.

But since one party was so heavily injured, it was more appropriate to seek treatment early.

Once the dust had settled, Senju Matsuaki prepared to turn and leave.

As for Uchiha Kai, if his injuries were not severe, he could leave the arena on his own.

If his injuries were serious, medical personnel would carry him off for treatment.

But clearly, Uchiha Kai was not reconciled to losing like this.

To him, having been knocked down by a single punch without even getting to showcase his Ninjutsu, Taijutsu, or Genjutsu was unacceptable.

Furthermore, the early graduation exam was already more stringent than the standard graduation exam.

In this way, even if he had performed well in previous battles, his final evaluation would likely be poor.

For someone who cared so much about his reputation, this was more painful than being killed !

Therefore, he struggled to stand up, stared fixedly at Senju Matsuaki’s back, raised his limp, powerless right hand, and intended to form hand seals.

He completely disregarded that the Proctor had already announced the end of the battle ; he was only thinking of washing away his humiliation using his most proficient Fire Style : Fireball Jutsu.

However, with his right hand severely injured, his hand seal movements were as slow as a turtle.

Senju Matsuaki could not possibly watch him launch a sneak attack without reacting.

He pushed off with his feet and turned around.

With a cold, angry expression on his face, Senju Matsuaki sprinted a few steps and arrived in front of Uchiha Kai.

He grabbed the opponent’s hair with his right hand and hoisted the person, who was about his own height, up into the air.

Then, he slammed him heavily onto the ground, kicking up a cloud of dust.

Uchiha Kai, who had planned to use Ninjutsu to launch a sneak attack, only felt a sharp pain in his head.

It felt as if his soul was about to be hammered out of his body.

« Super Strength ! »

A considerable amount of Chakra erupted from Senju Matsuaki’s body, concentrating in the center of his right fist.

Carrying immense power, it slammed heavily into the ground beside Uchiha Kai.

Accompanied by a loud crash, the ground cracked open directly.

Chakra is, by nature, the product of physical energy and spiritual energy.

Therefore, as his Breathing Technique became increasingly profound, his Chakra volume skyrocketed along with the enhancement of his physique.

Coupled with the results of his training during this period, Senju Matsuaki could now use Super Strength quite easily.

Under this punch, the originally level ground shattered.

Uchiha Kai lay in it, disheveled and barely conscious, as if he had been buried in a pit.

In a daze, he saw the silver-haired figure walking away in front of him.

In his eyes, there was no longer the unwillingness from before, but rather a great sense of fear.

Arrogance, competitiveness, and jealousy—all these emotions were shattered by this punch.

« Senju Matsuaki, that was amazing ! »

Hyuga Tsukuyomi trotted over to Senju Matsuaki’s side, and seeing him look so relaxed, she felt even more admiration.

During this time, she had often sparred with him, so she had the most intuitive sense of his progress.

From being evenly matched in the beginning to now, there was an insurmountable gap.

Therefore, she was not surprised by the outcome of this battle.

« Hokage-sama, this child of the Senju Clan is truly remarkable. »

Outside the arena, Sarutobi Sasuke watched this scene, somewhat dumbfounded, and exclaimed.

Originally, he had thought this would be a very exciting duel.

Furthermore, the identities of the two—the Senju Clan and the Uchiha Clan—made him feel a sense of destiny.

Since the Sengoku period, the two clans could be said to have been in constant conflict.

Those of them from the older generation of Ninjas had witnessed firsthand the many years of confrontation between Senju Hashirama and Uchiha Madara, and between Senju Tobirama and Uchiha Izuna.

But looking at it now, to hell with destiny.

Since when does a fated rival get beaten down in just a few punches and kicks ?

« This was also beyond our expectations. »

« The last time we saw little Senju Matsuaki use Super Strength, he couldn’t control his Chakra this precisely. »

Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama exchanged a glance, equally surprised and bewildered.

Although they were very busy on a daily basis, they still cared about the development of their younger kin.

However, the strength Senju Matsuaki displayed today had already exceeded their expectations.

With this kind of strength, let alone Genin, he was more than qualified to be a Chunin.

The only things missing were experience and seniority.

Furthermore, they noticed that Senju Matsuaki still had a short blade strapped to his back.

After several rounds of battles, this short blade had yet to be drawn.

However, seeing their own child be so powerful, they could only feel gratified.

After all, the younger generation of the Senju Clan didn’t have many outstanding Ninjas, which made them worry about a shortage of successors.

Nearby, Hiruzen Sarutobi, Danzo Shimura, and others also had grave expressions.

« Hiruzen, what do you think ? » Homura Mitokado asked in a low voice.

To be honest, watching the scene of the battle just now, he felt a bit uncertain himself.

Although he wouldn’t be beaten senseless like Uchiha Kai in a few moves, he probably wouldn’t be able to escape defeat either.

Hearing this, Hiruzen Sarutobi shook his head and said, « At his age, he is indeed ridiculously strong. »

« It’s hard to say in a few years, but for now, he probably isn’t my match yet. »

Soon, the medical personnel waiting outside the arena entered and carried away the unconscious Uchiha Kai.
