# Chapitre 132 — Wood Release: Deep Forest Emergence! Reactions from All Sides!

Within Senju Matsuaki’s body, the vibrant, life-giving essence of the Asura Chakra was now unmistakably manifest.

The surging vitality contained within that Chakra made the audience in the rows closest to the arena feel as refreshed as if they were bathed in a spring breeze.

After the System purged the will of Asura from it, the Asura Chakra became the purest form of nourishment for Senju Matsuaki to increase both the quantity and quality of his own Chakra.

Even a thousand years after death, it could still assist an outstanding descendant in their cultivation.

Surely, if the Old Ancestor Asura were watching from the heavens, he would feel quite gratified by this.

The vast majority of the audience was confused by Senju Matsuaki’s actions at this moment.

Not to mention the ordinary people, but most of the Ninjas from all over the world were also scratching their heads.

What exactly was this surging, life-filled Chakra that felt as vast as a boundless ocean ?

However, a small number of people did notice.

In a central section of the stands, a group of Ninjas wearing Konoha Ninja headbands stood up in unison, their eyes filled with surprise and shock, staring intently at the arena.

This group consisted of men and women, young and old.

Aside from some of the young people and children who looked at their elders with confusion, most of them maintained expressions of disbelief.

Their clothing and appearances varied, but there was one thing they all had in common.

On their garments, there was always a prominent spot embroidered with the same pattern.

That was the crest of the Senju Clan !

This group of Konoha Ninja were precisely the Ninjas of the Senju Clan.

Of course, because the core strength of the Senju Clan was currently on missions, those present in the stands were mostly the elderly, women, and children.

« That is... Clan Chief Hashirama’s... »

An elder of the Senju Clan murmured in a low voice.

Overcome with emotion, the elder’s latter half of the sentence could not be heard clearly.

However, these older generation Ninjas who had followed Senju Hashirama since the Sengoku era and lived to this day all understood what the rest of that sentence was.

Watching the new sprouts breaking through the soil in the arena, it was as if they were looking at their own faith.

...

In the arena.

It was only a fleeting moment from when Senju Matsuaki formed the hand seals to when his Chakra erupted with a roar.

After being stunned for a moment, a sudden flash of inspiration struck the mind of Dou from Kumogakure.

« This life force, the Senju Clan, Senju Hashirama... »

He was no fool.

Gathering and analyzing intelligence was a required course for Ninjas.

When everything he perceived before him connected with the intelligence stored in his mind, a thought that terrified him to the core arose in his heart.

« Are you kidding me ! ! »

Dou roared, his head tilted back, and lightning surged crazily around his body without regard for the cost.

It seemed that by doing this, he could dispel the unease in his heart.

Next, rather than sitting and waiting for death, he focused all his concentration on the tips of his right-hand fingers.

A massive amount of lightning was guided and concentrated into the four extended fingers of Dou’s right hand.

« Hell Stab ! ! ! Four-Finger Nukite ! ! ! »

His roar sounded like the bellowing of a wild bull, accompanied by the piercing whine of lightning tearing through the air.

Dou’s body turned into a lightning-shrouded afterimage, shooting out like an arrow released from a bowstring.

At this moment, the future « Strongest Spear » had already begun to take shape.

This scene naturally entered Senju Matsuaki’s eyes as well.

In his eyes, which were as clear as rubies when still, the approaching lightning was reflected.

However, it was all about to end.

The process of his technique was about to finish.

The final of this Joint Chunin Exams was also about to end.

« Wood Release : Deep Forest Emergence ! ! ! »

As his voice fell, the surging Chakra around Senju Matsuaki paused for a split second.

But immediately after, like a flood that had finally found an outlet to gush out, it poured frantically into the ground.

The ground began to tremble.

In the stands, some of the audience members in the front rows even felt the ground beneath their feet begin to shake, feeling panicked, and the order was in a momentary state of chaos.

Fortunately, Konoha had long since prepared a contingency plan for this sudden situation.

« Everyone, do not panic ! Grab the armrests on the side of your seats ! »

« The stands have been specially reinforced and will not collapse easily. Our Konoha Ninjas are currently further reinforcing them, so everyone can rest assured ! »

Senju Tobirama, who had long known that Senju Matsuaki had awakened Wood Release, naturally did not react with the same shock and loss of composure as the other Ninjas.

Noticing the panic among some of the audience members in the stands, Tobirama immediately provided reassurance and countermeasures.

At the same time, his eyes were fixed on the heaving ground of the arena, anticipating the new sprouts breaking through from underground.

On the platform, the First Kazekage, First Raikage, First Mizukage, and First Tsuchikage all watched this scene in silence.

Upon closer inspection, their expressions were as gloomy as if they had just received news of a tragedy at home.

Their hanging hands also subconsciously clenched into fists.

Right amidst the expectant, shocked, fearful, and wary gazes of countless people.

Countless trees, as thick and menacing as dragons, burst through the soil and shot straight into the sky !

The originally vast arena, the ground of which had been deliberately reinforced by Ninjas skilled in Earth Release after the semi-finals.

Was now once again torn to shreds by the countless giant trees bursting from the earth.

The empty ground was instantly covered by countless towering trees, as if it had transformed into a primeval forest.

« Wood... Wood Release. »

The First Kazekage’s pupils contracted sharply ; the scene before him caused his mind to go blank for a moment.

It was not just him ; the other three First Kage also subconsciously stood up and took a few steps forward, coming to the edge of the railing.

« Truly... the younger generation is to be feared. » The First Raikage said in a deep voice.

He dared not turn his head to look at Senju Hashirama.

Because he was afraid.

Afraid that the killing intent he couldn’t suppress on his face would enrage this God of Shinobi, who was currently at the Peak of his power.

« Konoha, has it been favored by the Six Paths Sage... »

The First Mizukage, Byakuren, murmured.

He was not as shocked and speechless as the Kazekage, nor did he harbor killing intent like the Raikage.

He merely felt extremely bewildered.

Among the Five Kage, he was the oldest, and his old injuries and illnesses flared up from time to time.

If one were to say which of the five First Kage would retire first, it would undoubtedly be him.

Byakuren had originally thought that since he had carefully cultivated the future Second Mizukage and even the Third Mizukage of the Hidden Mist, the future of the Hidden Mist would surely be even more glorious.

But now he was no longer certain.

Konoha, oh Konoha, with generation after generation of heaven-gifted geniuses, would their Hidden Mist ever have a chance to rise ?

The First Tsuchikage Ishikawa had an uncertain expression, his gaze shifting between the other Kage.

« Konoha will still dominate in the future. It seems that, in the future, I need to interact more with the other Ninja villages... » he thought to himself.

On the arena.

Senju Matsuaki’s body was lifted by a thick, giant tree, and the branches at the top of the giant tree, like loyal guards, wrapped around his body.

It seemed that the integration of the Asura Chakra had caused a qualitative change in his own Chakra, making it more compatible with Wood Release.

Senju Matsuaki felt increasingly adept at using Wood Release.

Compared to a few days ago, both the range and the level of control had improved.

At the same time, the countless giant trees seemed to have become his eyes and ears ; every move Dou made had no secrets under his perception.
