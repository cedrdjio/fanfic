# Chapitre 118 — Furious Yan! Tsukuyomi in Crisis!

The First Raikage also had a livid expression, and feeling the strange gazes of the several Feudal Lords and Kage beside him, he wished he could crawl into a crack in the ground.

The four Feudal Lords still present had clearly not yet recovered from the shocking scene just now.

Out of respect for their status, they had no intention of commenting on this unseemly scene, maintaining an eerie silence.

Finally, it was Senju Hashirama, as the leader of the host, who broke the silence, saying : « Then let us draw lots to decide the candidates for the next round of examinations. »

« Very well, then let the Feudal Lord of the Land of Wind and I draw the lots together. »

The Feudal Lord of the Land of Wind returned to his usual smiling expression, reached into the box, and pulled out another paper ball.

First Kazekage Reto followed closely behind, also pulling out a paper ball and opening it.

The Feudal Lord of the Land of Wind smiled and said : « On my side, it is Yan of Sunagakure. »

And the First Kazekage wore an innocent expression, displaying the paper ball in his hand forward.

« On my side, it is Hyuga Tsukuyomi of Konoha. »

Now, it was Senju Hashirama’s turn to feel his heart sink.

Yan of Sunagakure possessed genuine Jonin-level strength.

In the previous rounds of examinations, the Magnet Release Ninja Yan of Sunagakure was one of the few juniors who had impressed him.

It wasn’t that he hadn’t seen other Ninjas who used Magnet Release, but even with a Bloodline Limit, talent mattered.

Being able to hone Magnet Release Ninjutsu to this extent at just over ten years old, he was already a Ninja of superior talent.

With Yan’s strength, even peers like Hiruzen Sarutobi and Uchiha Kagami might not necessarily win, let alone Hyuga Tsukuyomi, who was currently only at the strength of a Chunin.

The hope of victory was practically slim.

« The two sides for the next examination are, Hyuga Tsukuyomi of Konoha and Yan of Sunagakure ! »

Senju Tobirama, upon receiving this match arrangement, also frowned for a moment before reading out the arrangement.

Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama did not have strict boundaries when teaching their students.

Just as Senju Hashirama would occasionally provide some guidance to Senju Tobirama’s students, Hiruzen Sarutobi and Uchiha Kagami, Senju Tobirama would also occasionally offer a pointer or two to Senju Matsuaki and Hyuga Tsukuyomi.

In fact, Senju Tobirama could be considered their half-teacher.

In the lounge, hearing this examination arrangement, the remaining dozen or so candidates all had different expressions.

Senju Matsuaki frowned slightly, looking at Hyuga Tsukuyomi with some worry.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi felt slightly nervous, her pair of fair, small hands subconsciously clenching into fists.

Not far away, the candidates from Hidden Mist, Iwagakure, and Sunagakure looked over here with schadenfreude.

They all knew in their hearts that Yan’s strength was one of the most terrifying among the candidates in this Chunin Exams.

His Magnet Release Ninjutsu, which could be used for both offense and defense and had unfathomable power, was difficult to guard against.

It seemed that the little girl from the Hyuga Clan was going to suffer greatly !

As for the Kumogakure candidates, they were currently all asleep on the chairs in the lounge, with no signs of waking up yet.

Of course, it wasn’t just Kumogakure ; the other candidate for the current examination, Yan of Sunagakure, was also in a deep sleep.

Compared to Kumogakure, Sunagakure was luckier.

Because of Dou’s willingness to share, all the Kumogakure candidates had been knocked out in one fell swoop.

While Yan of Sunagakure had been eating alone in a corner, and then fell into a deep sleep alone.

In daily life, Yan also maintained a « stay away from strangers » attitude in Sunagakure, so no candidates from his village dared to step forward to disturb him after he fainted earlier.

Now that it was Yan’s turn for the examination, the few remaining Sunagakure candidates, after pushing each other, finally pushed one person forward to shake Yan awake.

« Lord Yan, it is time for your examination. »

« I, this is... »

Yan opened his yellow eyes with a slightly dazed look ; his dizzy head made his breathing feel even heavier.

After his consciousness returned, he felt his physical condition, and a shadow cast over his cold face.

As a former Jonin, he quickly reacted to what he had just encountered.

A dizzy head, weak limbs, plus a dull ache in his abdomen and a strange reaction in his lower body—undoubtedly, he had been drugged just now.

Yan looked around and, upon discovering that the Kumogakure candidates, including Dou, were also passed out in a pile, he immediately understood.

His yellow eyes, filled with anger, shifted to find that the small cart stall selling oden had long been empty.

« Damn guys, I’ve fallen for your tricks ! ! »

Yan laughed out of extreme anger ; he had already roughly grasped the sequence of events.

However, with the examination imminent, he could not get away to take revenge on the two bastards who had set him up.

Getting up and brushing past the Sunagakure Ninja who had just woken him up, Yan jumped off the spectator stand with a livid face.

The jet-black Iron Sand formed a circular platform that just happened to accommodate his standing, carrying him down to the center of the Arena, where it then crumbled and gathered into grains of Iron Sand behind his back.

However, what was puzzling was that a black ribbon formed by Iron Sand was still floating at his lower body.

It was unclear whether this was for more swagger or to cover something up.

But undoubtedly, Yan, by taking the stage while levitating with Magnet Release, possessed an Aura that other candidates did not have from the very beginning.

Because even among Jonin and even Kage, very few possessed the ability to fly.

When he saw that his opponent was a familiar face, Yan narrowed his eyes slightly.

Back then, during the second stage of the examination’s survival drill, he had encountered her in the primeval forest.

That pair of Byakugan, which were not bound by the Caged Bird Seal, were naturally coveted by him.

It was just a pity that later, the other party threatened him with self-destruction of her eyes, and Senju Matsuaki arrived in time to rescue her, which eventually led to the bankruptcy of his plan.

However, from this, it could also be seen that this girl from the Hyuga Clan had a close relationship with Senju Matsuaki, who had heavily injured Chiyo and Ebizo !

Thinking of this, a chill appeared on his face, which looked like he was enduring something.

Sensing the strong pressure from the front, Hyuga Tsukuyomi also felt a tightness in her heart.

Having faced the opponent directly, she knew the gap between them.

For a Chunin to defeat a Jonin, perhaps there was a slim possibility through assassination, poisoning, or other underhanded tactics, but in a direct confrontation, there was almost no chance of winning !

Moreover, the opponent’s Magnet Release had a huge advantage in both offense and defense, which left almost no room for output for Gentle Fist, which relied mainly on close-quarters combat.

« The battle begins ! ! »

The moment the battle officially began, Hyuga Tsukuyomi activated her Byakugan, and Yan of Sunagakure did not hesitate to form hand seals.

Inside Yan’s body, Chakra was highly concentrated and converted into magnetic force, and the jet-black Iron Sand surged and rolled, quickly condensing into countless fine needles suspended behind him.

« Magnet Release · Iron Sand Shigure ! ! »

The Iron Sand needles trembled slightly, and then poured down toward the front like rain lines.

Such a large-scale and somewhat spectacular Ninjutsu immediately caused bursts of exclamations from the auditorium.

At the same time, everyone couldn’t help but break into a cold sweat for the girl on the opposite side who looked much more delicate.
