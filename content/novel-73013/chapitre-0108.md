# Chapitre 108 — Points Skyrocket!

Two pairs of freshly plucked Three-Tomoe Sharingan lay together, their cold, deep aura almost solidifying into reality.

« Submit. »

With a thought, the two pairs of Three-Tomoe Sharingan disappeared from the top of the unfolded storage scroll.

In their place, [Three-Tomoe Sharingan * 4] appeared on the left side of the point exchange window.

The question mark that was originally on the right side of the window flickered for a moment, and finally displayed the number « 2000 ».

After this operation, Senju Matsuaki finally grasped the function of this new feature.

In the past, his only channels for earning exchange points were fixed rewards after daily training and completing tasks issued by the platform.

The former could only be considered mosquito legs ; the bulk still came from the latter.

And now, a new channel for obtaining points had been opened : submitting items that the platform deemed to have a certain value in exchange for corresponding points.

Just now, Senju Matsuaki submitted the spoils he had obtained, and the four Three-Tomoe Sharingan were judged to be worth two thousand points.

To have a more intuitive comparison, Senju Matsuaki temporarily exited the exchange interface to check the points required to redeem Sharingan.

After looking it up, he found that redeeming a pair of One-Tomoe Sharingan required two hundred points, a pair of Two-Tomoe Sharingan required one thousand points, and a pair of Three-Tomoe Sharingan required two thousand points.

In other words, one One-Tomoe Sharingan was one hundred, one Two-Tomoe Sharingan was five hundred, and one Three-Tomoe Sharingan was one thousand.

Now, Senju Matsuaki submitted four Three-Tomoe Sharingan, and the points given by the platform were two thousand, which was half the amount of points required for redemption.

This halved ratio might seem a bit unscrupulous, but upon closer inspection, it was not the case.

After all, the Sharingan obtained through redemption were « natural eyes » without side effects, which was fundamentally different from these eyes that might not even be compatible after transplantation.

Calculated this way, the cost-effectiveness of exchanging these Sharingan for points was extremely high.

After weighing it in his heart, Senju Matsuaki directly pressed the confirm button ; the four Three-Tomoe Sharingan disappeared, and two thousand points were credited.

As early as after the end of the second stage of the assessment, the points accumulated by Senju Matsuaki had exceeded six thousand.

After these two thousand were credited, his accumulated points exceeded eight thousand.

It was so wealthy that it was hard to believe.

Of course, Senju Matsuaki’s gains tonight did not end there !

Just as he was excitedly reviewing the increased total points, he was astonished to find that the points were one thousand five hundred more than he had expected.

This obvious error almost made Senju Matsuaki wonder if he had developed amnesia.

Otherwise, how could there be an extra one thousand five hundred points suddenly, without any memory of it ?

After thinking about it, Senju Matsuaki’s pupils suddenly constricted, and he quickly opened the task issued by the platform today to investigate.

[B-rank task ! Defeat opponents during the third stage of the Joint Chunin Exams. Reward : Genin level 50 points, Chunin level 200 points, Jonin level 500 points !]

Jonin level 500 points ; one thousand five hundred was exactly the reward amount for three Jonin.

During the third stage...

Connecting these points together, Senju Matsuaki finally realized the source of these extra points, his pair of crimson eyes shining under the light.

It seems that the « third stage » mentioned in the task description does not refer to the battles within the Chunin Exams in a narrow sense.

During the third stage of the Chunin Exams, even battles occurring outside the exam venue count towards the task scope !

Just now, there were two Uchiha Clan Jonin, as well as the Chunin named Uchiha Setsuna.

In the end, in a desperate situation, Uchiha Setsuna’s Two-Tomoe Sharingan evolved into Three-Tomoe ; presumably, his strength also increased because of this, and he was judged to be a Jonin.

Thinking of this, the light in Senju Matsuaki’s eyes became brighter.

After finishing his bought dinner in a few bites and disposing of the trash, he quickly pushed the door open and left his mansion, heading straight for the neighbor’s house next door.

Knock, knock, knock —

Not long after knocking on the door, a tall, middle-aged man opened it. Seeing that it was Senju Matsuaki, the expression on his face became kind.

« It’s Matsuaki. I rarely see you since you became a Ninja. »

The man greeted Senju Matsuaki with a smile.

The man was also a Ninja of the Senju Clan, possessing Jonin strength. He was not only Senju Matsuaki’s neighbor but also had some blood relations.

He was slightly older than Senju Hashirama and the others, and was an elder of Senju Matsuaki’s generation.

However, after the establishment of Konoha, he, at the age of forty, no longer remained active on the front lines as he did during the Sengoku period, but instead actively applied to teach at Konoha’s Ninja Academy.

« Is there anything you need by coming to find me today ? » he asked curiously.

« Uncle, it’s like this. I’ve been participating in the Chunin Exams recently, and I was thinking not to waste this rest time in the evening, so I wanted to find someone to spar with. »

Senju Matsuaki scratched his head and stated his request for coming here.

Hearing this, the man’s eyes lit up, and his face showed admiration.

« Speaking of which, I was also in the audience during the day. I didn’t expect you to have such strength so soon after graduating. Our Senju Clan has successors. »

« Since a young person is working so hard, we old folks certainly can’t fall behind. Let’s go ! »

Seemingly infected by Senju Matsuaki’s diligence, the man also ignited his long-lost passion and pulled Senju Matsuaki toward the training ground in his own yard.

Twenty minutes or so later, Senju Matsuaki pushed open the gate of the neighbor’s house and walked out, waving goodbye to him.

The hairstyle that had been neatly combed before bed was messed up during the sparring, and it was still covered with dust from the use of Earth Release Ninjutsu.

« The younger generation is formidable. I didn’t expect that I haven’t seen you for a while, and you’ve improved to this extent. It makes me think of Lord Hashirama and the others when they were young. »

The neighbor patted the dust off his shoulders, a trace of recollection appearing in his eyes.

The sparring just now lasted only twenty minutes. At first, he held the mentality of guiding a junior and did not go all out.

However, he soon realized that his thinking was wildly wrong.

Having long since withdrawn from the front lines to teach at the Ninja Academy, his strength had declined significantly compared to his prime, and not long after the battle began, he actually fell into a disadvantage.

Even if he exerted his full strength afterward, he failed to reverse the decline.

Plus, this was just a friendly spar with a junior, and he had no desire to win or lose, so he eventually admitted defeat calmly.

After admitting defeat, he felt deep surprise in his heart.

He was a Jonin after all. Although his strength had declined, he wasn’t someone just anyone could mess with.

When he looked down at Senju Matsuaki in front of him, he suddenly realized something.

At this moment, Senju Matsuaki’s age seemed to be much younger than Senju Hashirama when he first began to make a name for himself in the Sengoku period decades ago.

After all, they had been neighbors for many years. If he remembered correctly, Senju Matsuaki was only about eight years old at this time.

Back in his day, although he had already stepped onto the battlefield at this age, the opponents he faced were only Genin, and even fighting against a Chunin was quite difficult.

If he had encountered a Jonin, his ashes would have been scattered in a single exchange.

« Thank you for your guidance. This is a small token of my appreciation ; I hope you can accept it. »

Looking at this elder’s slightly disheveled appearance, Senju Matsuaki also felt a bit sorry.

Thinking of the items the Family had sent to his home not long ago, his eyes lit up.
