# Chapitre 34 — The Hell, This Is Thirteen?

Upon hearing this, the Cloud Village Youth did not feel alarmed.

Since entering the Land of Fire, it wasn’t the first time he had been asked about his height and age, and besides, this wasn’t important information.

« Me ? I just turned thirteen this year. »

The Cloud Village Youth answered casually, then picked up a skewer of oden and stuffed it directly into his mouth.

The hell, this is thirteen ? !

In the distance, the expression of the Monkey Boy crumbled for a split second.

He had assumed that the youth before him, who was a head taller than himself, might have a youthful face, but his height surely suggested he was an adult.

Before leaving, the Cloud Village Youth looked at the two selling oden with some sympathy, especially the Monkey Boy.

« You look like you’re not even ten years old yet. Is the person next to you your brother ? I didn’t expect people in Konoha to have to start doing business at such a young age. »

After sighing with emotion, the Cloud Village Youth shook his head while munching on the oden and strolled off in another direction.

In the distance, the Monkey Boy rolled up his sleeves, his face filled with anger as he glared at the receding figure.

Meanwhile, the gloomy boy beside him grabbed his arm tightly, stopping him from rushing forward.

« Hiruzen, don’t be impulsive ! »

« Let go of me, Danzo ! I’m thirteen this year too, and he says I’m not even ten ? ! »

« But you... »

Although furious, the two continued their low-voiced exchange, ensuring the departing Cloud Village Youth could not hear them.

Not far away, Matsumi gave a wry smile and a thumbs-up to the two who were performing their undercover mission.

Although Hiruzen Sarutobi was quite pissed off, they had indeed managed to glean some information.

The Cloud Village Youth just now was about the same age as Hiruzen Sarutobi, yet his strength had already reached the level of a Jonin ; he was definitely a top-tier genius of the younger generation in the Hidden Cloud Village.

Coupled with his appearance and other comprehensive factors, he was likely the future Third Raikage of the Hidden Cloud Village.

Thinking of this, Senju Matsumi’s expression couldn’t help but turn solemn.

A Ninja village like the Hidden Cloud Village, which had many barbarians, was mostly composed of brutes, but the future Raikages were all people who possessed finesse amidst their roughness.

This Cloud Village Youth, who was suspected to be the future Third Raikage, had suddenly left his team, and it was unclear what his true purpose was.

After the nations of the Ninja World entered the era of one village per country, internal wars among Ninjas within nations had become increasingly rare, while friction between the Ninja villages of different countries had conversely increased.

The vast majority of people had not imagined the scenario of a large-scale war between the Ninjas of one entire country and those of another.

Therefore, they did not worry about the occurrence of such man-made disasters.

However, those with foresight who understood the situation were all worried about whether a war between countries was imminent.

...

« Where exactly is this guy going ? » Matsumi wondered to himself.

After leaving with Hiruzen and Danzo’s oden, the Cloud Village Youth seemed to be wandering around aimlessly.

It had been the afternoon when the Hidden Cloud Village delegation arrived, but now the sun was about to set.

However, good Ninja training allowed Matsumi to patiently follow behind him, maintaining a distance that was neither too far nor too close.

Just when Matsumi was wondering if his previous suspicions were superfluous, he noticed the burly, sturdy figure ahead suddenly change direction, heading towards a path completely different from before.

Seeing this, Matsumi hurried to keep up while constantly pondering.

Was there any important facility or object in this direction ?

Suddenly, a flash of inspiration struck his mind.

« That direction is... the Ninja Academy ? »

Senju Matsumi’s expression was uncertain, and a sharp glint flashed in his scarlet eyes.

If one were to ask what the most important places in Konoha were, the first should be the Hokage Building ; the vast majority of Konoha’s confidential documents were kept there.

And secondly, it should be the Ninja Academy, where the future of Konoha gathered.

If an accident were to happen at the Ninja Academy, Konoha would face a period of at least five or six years where the new generation would fail to replace the old.

Thinking of this, Matsumi quickened his pace while performing hand seals.

« Shadow Clone Technique ! »

A puff of smoke rose beside him, and a figure identical to Senju Matsumi appeared by his side.

After the original and the Shadow Clone exchanged a glance, the Shadow Clone turned and sprinted in another direction, while Matsumi’s original body continued to follow the Cloud Village Youth.

To be honest, Matsumi did not have absolute confidence that he could handle this youth suspected to be the future Third Raikage.

After all, it had been less than half a year since Matsumi graduated from the Ninja Academy ; although he had improved significantly compared to that time, it hadn’t been enough to bring him to the Jonin level.

However, according to the Byakugan reconnaissance of Hyuga Tsukuyomi and the results of Matsumi’s own observations while tracking him, this Cloud Ninja youth was clearly a genuine Jonin.

If it were twenty days later, after he had exhausted all his check-in system attempts and perhaps pulled some high-quality items during that time, it might be possible for him to cross swords with the Cloud Village Youth.

Since he couldn’t beat him, there was no need to force it ; just calling for backup was fine.

Uncle Hashirama had said before that during the surveillance, he could call for support from nearby on-duty Chunin or Jonin at any time.

And this morning, Hiruzen Sarutobi had also said that they would come to help if any unexpected situations arose.

« This guy’s target is actually the Ninja Academy. But what exactly is his purpose ? »

As time passed, Senju Matsumi frowned tightly ; the Ninja Academy had already appeared within his line of sight.

To say that the opponent’s goal was actually to attack Konoha’s Ninja Academy was unlikely.

After all, besides students, the Ninja Academy had many teachers ; most were Chunin, but some possessed the strength of a Jonin.

If this Cloud Ninja were to launch an attack just like that, he would likely be subdued by the Konoha Ninjas attacking in a swarm very quickly.

At the entrance of the Konoha Ninja Academy.

It was now the time for the Ninja Academy to let out for the afternoon.

Accompanied by bursts of noise, children who had finished their day of study and training walked out of the Ninja Academy gate in groups of three or five.

At the very back of the crowd, a solitary, arrogant figure did not walk with the others but headed forward on his own.

The child sported the iconic Uchiha Clan spiky hairstyle, with the Uchiha fan crest embroidered on the back of his short shirt, wearing an arrogant expression ; it was none other than Uchiha Kai.

« Just wait, Senju Matsumi... »

Uchiha Kai’s lips curled into a cold sneer as he thought to himself.

« My Fire Release Ninjutsu has improved by leaps and bounds during this period ; I will definitely pass the next graduation exam. Once I become a Genin, I will ask my grandfather for permission to participate in... »

However, as if he suddenly thought of something, his expression suddenly turned as ugly as if he had eaten shit.

« Damn it, how did that brat from the Hyuga Clan manage to awaken his eyes so early, and why can’t I do it yet ? ! »
