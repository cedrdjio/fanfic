# Chapitre 110 — Hiruzen and Danzo's Request

At this moment, Danzo Shimura was still wearing a dark gray coarse cloth outfit, with the scent of various seasonings mixed together emanating from his body.

Through the open door crack, Senju Matsuaki saw a small cart parked in the courtyard.

Hiruzen Sarutobi was busy bustling around this small cart, his hands filled with various bottles and jars ; it was clear he couldn’t get away for a while.

After listening to Senju Matsuaki state his purpose, Danzo Shimura looked troubled.

They were currently preparing to carry out their mission tomorrow.

Tomorrow, they would still disguise themselves as ordinary vendors to sell Oden in the lounge, while simultaneously monitoring the examinees present.

It wasn’t that they were obsessed with this Oden, but rather that the results had been quite good these past few times, and furthermore, their plan had received approval from Senju Tobirama.

« Sorry, you can see the situation here, we can’t spare a hand for a while. »

« Alright, but you should keep these two boxes of senju happy water ; you can quench your thirst after you’re done. »

Watching the two of them truly busy and fired up, Senju Matsuaki felt regretful, but he didn’t force the issue.

As he spoke, he handed the two boxes of senju happy water in his hands to Danzo Shimura.

« Then thank you, I wish you success in tomorrow’s exam. »

Taking the two heavy boxes of good intentions, a trace of a smile appeared on Danzo Shimura’s slightly gloomy face.

At this time, he hadn’t yet experienced the various events of the future, nor did he bear the darkness for Konoha, and his heart wasn’t twisted ; apart from his usually somewhat gloomy temperament, he was a fairly easy-to-get-along-with companion.

Just as Senju Matsuaki intended to bid farewell to Danzo and leave, Hiruzen Sarutobi, holding bottles and jars, finally stopped his movements and walked quickly toward the two.

« Sorry, I was afraid of messing up the order and dosage of the potions just now, so I didn’t dare to stop, otherwise we would have had to start all over again. »

« Understood, understood. »

Senju Matsuaki rubbed his nose, adhering to the principle of « better safe than sorry, » and didn’t ask what kind of potion they were mixing just now.

However, unexpectedly, Hiruzen Sarutobi’s eyes lit up, and he looked at him, saying : « Matsuaki, do you have time to help us with a favor now ? It will only take a little while. »

« No problem, if it’s only for a little while. »

Originally, Senju Matsuaki planned to find Homura Mitokado and Utatane Koharu after saying goodbye to the two, to ask if it was convenient for a spar.

At worst, he would go directly to the Konoha public training ground to see ; anyone who persisted in training late at night probably wouldn’t refuse an invitation for a spar.

But since Hiruzen Sarutobi needed him to stay and help with a small favor, it wouldn’t be too late to leave later.

« Good, then let’s go inside and talk slowly. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi and Danzo Shimura brought Senju Matsuaki into Hiruzen Sarutobi’s courtyard, and the three stopped in front of the small cart.

Looking at the colorful bottles and jars placed on the table, as well as the dark brown liquid bubbling up in a compartment in the small cart, Senju Matsuaki fell into silence for a moment.

The dark brown liquid was filled with an ominous aura, and the sound of the bubbling bubbles was like the whispers of a devil.

To create such a terrifying visual effect, this couldn’t possibly be a potion made by mixing tonics, laxatives, sedatives, and other terrible liquids together, right ?

« This is a potion we made by mixing tonics, laxatives, sedatives, and other terrible liquids together, » Hiruzen Sarutobi explained.

It really was that ? ?

Senju Matsuaki frowned and silently retreated two steps.

« We are still in the experimental stage now ; actually, this plan has also been approved by Teacher Tobirama, otherwise we wouldn’t be busy with this late at night. »

Seeing Senju Matsuaki show a resistant expression, Hiruzen Sarutobi quickly explained.

« Under Teacher Tobirama’s suggestion, we are not just selecting one type of potion, but mixing many together, striving for a one-hit kill ! »

As he spoke, Hiruzen Sarutobi walked quickly to the porch and lifted a wooden bucket, and the sharp squeaking sounds also echoed in the quiet courtyard.

After Hiruzen Sarutobi walked closer, Senju Matsuaki saw clearly that what was in the bucket were six or seven tied-up large gray rats.

« These are the rats that stole the grain stored in our Sarutobi family home ; they were supposed to be dealt with anyway, so now we’re just making use of them. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi bent down to place the bucket next to the cart, then straightened up to look at Senju Matsuaki and continued to explain.

« Danzo and I have already studied the general proportions, and now the problem is to control the specific dosage. »

« It can’t be too high a dose, which would directly poison the target or be easily detected, nor can it be insufficient, leading to it not taking effect or having a delayed effect. »

« It is best if it gradually takes effect in about five minutes ; the specific mixing proportions are easy for us to get mixed up if we rely only on the two of us, so we have to trouble you, Matsuaki, to help us record it. »

After about ten minutes, the potions with different configuration ratios had been tasted by the experimental rats, and the most suitable ratio was determined during the experiment.

The rats, which originally had full-throated squeaks, were now on the verge of death ; after excreting a foul-smelling pool of filth, their red eyes rolled back, and they fell into a coma.

Sigh, poor little rats...

After Senju Matsuaki sighed, he casually placed the two paper cups containing the brown liquid that had been mixed during the experiment onto the platform of the cart.

After mixing multiple potions, it was unlikely to make it colorless and tasteless.

Fortunately, Hiruzen Sarutobi and the others' ultimate goal was to mix these potions into the Oden broth, so a little color would be completely undetectable.

« Not bad, not bad, it finished earlier than expected ; after cleaning up for a while, we can rest early, and we have to open the stall early tomorrow morning. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi let out a hot breath and raised his right hand to wipe the sweat from his forehead.

Tomorrow and the day after were expected to be the last two days of their mission ; once this mission was over, their mission experience would be even richer.

Especially Hiruzen Sarutobi, who already possessed the strength of a Jonin a few months ago ; after working hard to pass the Jonin examination, he would be a Konoha Jonin.

And although Danzo Shimura lagged behind a bit in strength, after cultivating for a period of time to polish his most proficient Wind Release Ninjutsu, advancing to Jonin was a certainty.

« How was the Ninjutsu scroll I gave you last time ? If there is anything you don’t understand, you can come and ask me. » Hiruzen Sarutobi smiled and patted Senju Matsuaki on the shoulder.

Danzo Shimura was not to be outdone and also said : « It’s the same on my end ; the technique I gave you involves some knowledge of Seal techniques, and the learning threshold is higher than the technique Hiruzen gave you. »

Hearing this, although Hiruzen Sarutobi was a bit unhappy, he had to admit that this was the truth.

For a Ninja who had already learned the Shadow Clone Technique, the principles of the Shuriken Shadow Clone Technique had commonalities.
