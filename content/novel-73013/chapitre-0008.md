# Chapitre 8 — Extra Reward: Total Concentration: Constant!

During the day, he attended classes at the Ninja Academy, and after school, he sparred with Hyuga Tsukuyomi.

At night, he would enter the skill training space punctually to learn the Breathing Technique from Tsugikuni Yoriichi.

Days passed one by one in this disciplined routine, and half a month flew by in the blink of an eye.

Soon, the day before the graduation exam arrived.

And Senju Matsuaki’s study of the Breathing Technique came to a temporary pause.

In the pristine white space, the boy held a short sword in both hands, while the old man held a long sword.

Amidst the flash of blades, the crisp sound of swords clashing rang out.

« Do not remain tense at all times. »

« However, at the very moment you strike, your strength, technique, and spirit must converge. »

Tsugikuni Yoriichi nonchalantly parried each of Matsuaki’s strikes.

No matter what force Senju Matsuaki used to swing his sword, his opponent could intercept it with the exact same force.

In this process, Senju Matsuaki became increasingly proficient at applying the Breathing Technique in actual combat.

Strength, technique, and spirit.

He pondered these words of Tsugikuni Yoriichi in his heart.

The former two were objective things.

During this period, his strength and technique had grown steadily with his training.

However, the so-called spirit was something he always found difficult to grasp.

Just as Matsuaki became slightly distracted, a terrifying aura, like that of a rampaging beast, suddenly surged from in front of him.

When he looked closely, he saw that Tsugikuni Yoriichi, who had been calm just a moment ago, now wore a solemn expression.

In those clouded eyes, a sharp and piercing keenness was hidden.

He gripped his katana tightly with both hands and raised it slowly.

This movement was clearly identical to when he was teaching earlier, but now it seemed to carry a chilling killing intent.

If he failed to block this strike, he would be split down from the top of his head, like firewood chopped into two halves.

Once he realized this, Senju Matsuaki no longer had any distracting thoughts in his heart.

Is it to fight while bearing the expectations of others ?

Or is it to protect the people and things you love ?

Or perhaps, is it just to achieve one’s own goals, to establish oneself, and to achieve success and fame ?

However, at this moment, none of these thoughts were paramount.

Whether swinging a sword or throwing a punch, if these were still what one was thinking about, the movements would inevitably slow down.

The reason for swinging the sword is something to consider before swinging.

But when swinging the sword, the only thought in one’s heart should be the purpose that this strike is intended to achieve.

For now, the only thing to do was to block Tsugikuni Yoriichi’s sword !

On one side was a young boy, and on the other was an old man nearing the end of his life.

Yet at this moment, the force of their sword swings was enough to easily kill any robust adult.

Where their swords collided was also where their gazes met.

« You finally understand. »

Tsugikuni Yoriichi’s sharp gaze slowly returned to a peaceful state, his eyes filled with gratification.

« In the world where I lived, when the Demon Slayer Corps swordsmen fought against demons, any moment of negligence could lead to irreversible consequences. »

« I tried to tell them how to swing their swords, but the moment of true realization often comes between life and death. »

« Teacher Tsugikuni, you... »

Senju Matsuaki noticed that, at some point, a faint radiance had enveloped the old man’s body.

This tall, upright figure seemed to be fading away in this space.

« As for the Breathing Technique, I have nothing left to teach you. »

« It is a pity that due to the restrictions of this place, I cannot teach you any more. From now on, you must rely on yourself. »

Tsugikuni Yoriichi said with a smile.

« Compared to the world I lived in, this world is luckier, but also more unfortunate. I can feel that Chakra is a very magical thing. »

« Those who exhaust the path will arrive at the same destination by different routes. I hope you can find the 'breathing' that suits you best in the future. »

« I will, Teacher. » Senju Matsuaki said solemnly.

Although he knew that parting would come soon, when it was truly time to say goodbye, he couldn’t help but feel a bit reluctant.

After all, during these past few days, Tsugikuni Yoriichi had been very dutiful in his role as a master.

Not only the Breathing Technique, but his originally rusty swordsmanship had also improved by leaps and bounds because of this.

Watching Tsugikuni Yoriichi’s body gradually dissolve into particles of light, Senju Matsuaki respectfully performed the rites of a disciple to bid him farewell.

« Use your strength to protect everything you cherish ; do not end up like me... »

Tsugikuni Yoriichi’s voice gradually faded, and as he dissipated, a white light shone before Senju Matsuaki’s eyes once again.

It felt as if the world was spinning ; when he came to his senses, he had already returned to the courtyard of his own residence.

Just when Matsuaki was still feeling a bit lonely, a notification suddenly appeared.

[Skill training complete ! Based on the instructor’s evaluation and training results, this training session is rated excellent ! Extra reward : Total Concentration : Constant !]

Along with the end of the notification, Senju Matsuaki clearly felt the changes in his body.

When he realized what had happened, a feeling of joy welled up in his heart.

Total Concentration : Constant is a higher realm of the Breathing Technique ; even when entering a state of sleep, one can continue to stay focused and maintain the activation of the Breathing Technique.

In other words, the Breathing Technique had already become a constant state.

One must know that there is quite a long distance between learning the Breathing Technique and mastering Total Concentration : Constant.

However, after the extra reward was issued, this distance was directly bridged.

Thinking of this, Matsuaki couldn’t help but feel a bit emotional.

These days he had been relying on diligent training to improve his strength, and only now did he truly intuitively understand what it felt like to use a cheat.

It must be said that the feeling of using a cheat is indeed very satisfying.

But he would not forget that the reason he received the extra reward was precisely because of his hard training these past few days.

Tomorrow morning, he still had to go to the Ninja Academy to take the graduation exam.

Therefore, Matsuaki did not do anything else ; after washing up and tidying up, he returned to his room and slept soundly.

...

Early the next morning, after a simple breakfast, he arrived at the Ninja Academy.

Unlike usual, today there were many unfamiliar faces on campus.

The graduation exam of the Ninja Academy is a very important matter, at least in times of peace.

The Hokage and other high-ranking officials in the Village usually pay attention to the progress of the exam.

In addition, some Chunin and Jonin also come to the scene because of their own children or the juniors of their own clans.

The graduation exam is conducted at the Ninja Academy’s practical training ground, and the Proctor in charge of the exam is currently announcing the basic rules.

« The format of the graduation exam is a knockout tournament of practical combat. Everyone will draw lots, and those with the same number will be opponents. »

« During the practical combat, you will be graded on various aspects including your Taijutsu, Ninjutsu, Genjutsu, shurikenjutsu, combat awareness, and more. I hope everyone performs well ! »

After the words fell, the Proctor flashed to the edge of the field and tapped on a sealed cardboard box.

« Now, line up in an orderly fashion and draw lots one by one. »
