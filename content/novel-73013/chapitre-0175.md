# Chapitre 175 — Kumogakure Ambush! One-Punch Knockout of Dou!

« It’s over. »

Uchiha Hikari walked over, looking at him with a beaming smile : « Matsuaki, your strength is getting stronger and stronger ! »

« Except for the Seven-Tails, the other eight Tailed Beasts should all be sealed now. »

Senju Matsuaki smiled, then looked at Hyuga Tsukuyomi, « Tsukuyomi, what’s the situation around... »

Before he could finish, Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s expression changed abruptly.

« Someone is coming ! »

She turned her head sharply toward the northeast, her Byakugan fully activated, « There are many of them... at least thirty ! The one leading them is... »

Her pupils constricted slightly.

« The First Raikage, his guard Hong, and... that Dou who lost to you before. »

Senju Matsuaki and Uchiha Hikari exchanged a glance, their expressions turning serious at the same time.

« They came really fast. »

Senju Matsuaki tucked the sealing scroll into his robes and placed his right hand back on his sword hilt, « It seems they had planned to wait until we finished our capture to swoop in and snatch it. »

Uchiha Hikari’s Sharingan slowly rotated, the three tomoe merging into the pattern of her Mangekyo.

« Since they’re here, let’s let them know... »

A cold smile curled the corners of her lips.

« Some things can’t be stolen just by having more people ! »

In the distance, the figure of the First Raikage had already appeared at the edge of their vision ; he was wrapped in blue Lightning Release Chakra, speeding toward them like a bolt of lightning.

Behind him, dozens of elite Kumogakure Ninja followed closely, filled with Killing Intent.

The Raikage and his party approached aggressively, but the moment they saw the state of the battlefield, they all came to a sudden halt.

The expected chaotic ruins were indeed present ; the ground was cracked, trees were toppled, and charred scorch marks covered the valley.

But the three Konoha Ninja standing in the center of that chaos didn’t look exhausted as they had imagined at all.

Senju Matsuaki’s robes were neat, his breathing was steady, his right hand rested casually on the hilt of his sword at his waist, and his expression was calm.

Uchiha Hikari stood by his side, her Mangekyo Sharingan slowly rotating, a faint, ambiguous smirk playing on her lips.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s Byakugan remained active, vigilantly scanning the surroundings, without even a single bead of sweat on her forehead.

The First Raikage’s eyes darkened.

He had originally thought that these three young Konoha Ninja, after a fierce battle with the Eight-Tails, would at least be severely weakened, if not dead.

When he led his troops out, he would be able to take the Eight-Tails and bury these three Konoha geniuses in this valley without a sound.

With no proof left behind, Konoha would have nothing to say !

But the scene before him completely ruined his plans.

« Lord Raikage... »

The guard Hong lowered his voice, his gaze sweeping over the three of them with gravity, « They look... almost completely unexhausted. »

« Not 'almost'. »

The First Raikage’s voice was low and cold, « It’s completely ! »

Dou stood behind the two of them, fists clenched, his knuckles cracking.

His gaze was locked firmly on Senju Matsuaki, his eyes filled with unwillingness and Battle Intent.

Their last encounter was in the finals of the Chunin Exams, where he had ultimately lost to Senju Matsuaki, and he still deeply remembered that feeling of powerlessness.

Over the years, he had trained desperately, his strength advancing by leaps and bounds, to the point where he was only one step away from the Kage level.

He had worked so hard just to one day wash away the humiliation of that year !

« The Eight-Tails cannot be taken by them. »

The First Raikage took a step forward, his blue Lightning Release Chakra instantly covering his entire body, emitting a crackling sound of electricity, « Hong, Dou, come with me. »

« The rest of you, Seal off the perimeter ! Don’t let any of them escape ! »

« Yes ! »

Dozens of Kumogakure Ninja responded in unison and quickly dispersed, surrounding the entire valley.

Streaks of lightning jumped and connected between them, forming an airtight Lightning Release barrier, completely cutting off the escape route for Senju Matsuaki and the others.

Senju Matsuaki looked around, watching the flickering lightning, his face still as calm as water.

« It seems they are determined to keep us here. »

Uchiha Hikari chuckled, the pattern in her Mangekyo Sharingan slowly rotating : « Then let’s see whether their Lightning Release is tougher, or my Susanoo is tougher. »

Before her voice faded, a streak of blue lightning had already torn through the air, lunging straight for Senju Matsuaki’s face !

It was Dou.

He was wrapped in Lightning Release Chakra, his speed far faster than it had been back then, his right fist encased in lightning as he thrust forward.

« Senju Matsuaki ! ! »

His roar was mixed with complex emotions—unwillingness, anger, and a shred of awe he didn’t want to admit.

« Hell Stab : One-Finger Piercing ! ! ! »

Senju Matsuaki did not draw his sword.

He simply raised his right hand, clenched his fist, and then swung forward nonchalantly.

« Air Quake ! ! »

Hum—

A dull, vibrating sound echoed through the air.

White shockwaves exploded in front of his fist, and an invisible force slammed into Dou like a heavy hammer.

Dou’s pupils constricted violently.

In that instant, he felt as if he had been struck head-on by a great mountain.

The Lightning Release Armor covering his body shattered like paper in the face of the vibration force, and intense pain spread from his chest to his entire body.

He couldn’t even react ; he was sent flying backward like a stone thrown by a catapult, moving even faster than when he had approached, crashing hard into the mountain wall.

« Boom— ! »

The mountain wall cracked, and rubble tumbled down.

Dou was embedded in the rock wall, blood spilling from his mouth, his entire skeleton seemingly groaning.

He lifted his head with difficulty, looking at Senju Matsuaki, who stood where he was, not having moved a step.

How is it possible...

Clearly, a few years ago, they were evenly matched.

Clearly, back then, Senju Matsuaki did not have such terrifying strength.

He had chased after him with all his might, training day and night, thinking he was strong enough...

But why...

Has the gap only grown wider ?

A flash of light, intertwined with despair and unwillingness, crossed Dou’s eyes before everything went black, and he passed out.

The valley fell into a brief silence.

Those Kumogakure Ninja, who had been aggressive just a moment ago, couldn’t help but swallow their saliva.

They knew Dou’s strength all too well ; he was the pinnacle of the younger generation and the strongest in Kumogakure after the Raikage and Hong.

Yet, such a person couldn’t even take a single punch from the opponent ?

The First Raikage’s expression was so dark it was almost dripping water.

« Good... very good. »

He spoke slowly, his voice suppressing anger, « It seems that this generation of Konoha has indeed produced an incredible figure ! »

« Kid, your strength is much stronger than it was during the Chunin Exams ! I have never seen a Genius like you ! »

« But it’s a pity that you are from Konoha ! Hand over the Eight-Tails, and I will leave your corpse intact ! ! »

Blue Lightning Release Chakra surged on his body, his hair standing on end, and the electricity bursting from his body scorched the ground beneath his feet, leaving charred marks.

The guard Hong also released Lightning Release Chakra, standing side-by-side with the First Raikage.
