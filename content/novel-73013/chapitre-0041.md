# Chapitre 41 — Kumogakure Challenges!

« Hokage-sama, since both sides have reached an agreement, we shall return to the Village immediately to report to the Raikage-sama. »

After discussing some minor details, both sides felt there was still more to say, but due to the pressing time, the Cloud Village Elder proposed their departure.

« However, before leaving the Village, I have one presumptuous request, and I hope Hokage-sama will grant it. »

« Please, speak. »

« The two individuals behind me are the Raikage-sama’s bodyguard, Hong, and Hong’s student, Dou. Both of them are outstanding among their peers. »

The Cloud Village Elder wore a look of pride as he introduced the two men behind him to the Konoha Ninja.

In his view, although Kumogakure could not currently match Konoha in high-end combat power due to Senju Hashirama, they had great potential in surpassing Konoha in the next generation.

The Raikage’s bodyguard, Hong, had profound attainments in Taijutsu and Lightning Release Ninjutsu. His strength was sufficient to qualify as a Village Kage, and he was the only candidate for Raikage in Kumogakure.

Over the years, before becoming the Raikage’s bodyguard, he had a track record of independently completing S-rank missions multiple times.

It was only when the First Raikage felt he was getting on in years and that it was time to cultivate the next Raikage that he transferred him to be the Raikage’s bodyguard.

And Hong’s direct disciple, Dou, was the most talented Ninja the Cloud Village Elder had ever seen.

He was born with divine strength, as strong as an ox, and his talent in Taijutsu and Ninjutsu surpassed both the Raikage and Hong.

Although he was only thirteen years old this year, his body had been trained to be as hard as iron, and a terrifying aura emanated faintly from his body, like a fierce tiger ready to devour someone.

« They can no longer find any opponents among their peers in Kumogakure. Having heard that Konoha is full of experts, they specifically came with me this time to seek some pointers from all of you. »

« This... »

Upon hearing this, Senju Hashirama was somewhat surprised, but he didn’t say much.

After all, the impression of Kumogakure being martial and combative had already spread throughout the Ninja World, and many people considered the Cloud Ninja to be a group of rough, wild bulls.

« Since that is the case, it just so happens that the two of us will accept the challenge. »

Senju Tobirama’s scarlet eyes narrowed slightly, then returned to normal as he patted Senju Matsumi on the shoulder beside him.

Judging from their positions, the two of them were directly opposite Kumogakure’s Hong and Dou, and they had exchanged glances many times during the meeting just now.

« Me ? Fighting the Third Raikage ? »

Hearing his second uncle’s arrangement, Senju Matsumi subconsciously looked up at him, almost pointing his right index finger at himself.

In the original timeline, the future Third Raikage, A, fought ten thousand Iwagakure Ninja single-handedly, and only died of exhaustion after fighting for three days and three nights.

Because of this exaggerated battle record, many people speculated that he might be the strongest among the Third Kage of each Village.

Although he was not yet « A » but rather Dou, that terrifying combat power was clearly already beginning to show.

In Senju Matsumi’s view, the only person among his peers who might be able to go toe-to-toe with him was likely Hiruzen Sarutobi, and even that wasn’t a guaranteed win.

That was why he thought earlier that Second Uncle Tobirama would send someone to call Big Brother Hiruzen over to fight.

Unexpectedly, Second Uncle just dragged him along to do it.

However, things had reached this point ; saying discouraging words about backing down would make Konoha’s side appear weaker than the opponent, so Senju Matsumi had no choice but to bite the bullet and step up.

At the same time, the associated mission was issued.

[B-rank mission issued : Persist against the future Third Raikage in battle for ten minutes without being defeated. Reward : 500 points !]

This was the first time Senju Matsumi had received a B-rank mission from his cheat, and the reward was as generous as he had ever seen.

However, this mission was just like the one during his graduation exam against Hiruzen Sarutobi : it was to persist without being defeated.

Senju Matsumi felt that if more missions like this came along, he might just train himself to become the « Senju Toe-to-Toe » expert.

« I have long heard of the reputation of the Flying Thunder God, but I don’t know which is faster compared to our Kumogakure’s Ninja Taijutsu. »

Hong smiled and nodded at Senju Tobirama, but his tone carried a hint of provocation, which was slightly different from the friendly attitude he had when he first arrived in Konoha yesterday.

Kumogakure’s martial nature was not only reflected in their combativeness but also in their confidence in their own abilities.

Except for the Raikage, he could be said to be undefeated throughout Kumogakure, so naturally, he did not believe anyone in Konoha other than the Hokage could be his match.

Even if those in the know in the Ninja World spread rumors about Senju Tobirama’s Flying Thunder God Technique being godlike, one would have to clash with Kumogakure’s Ninja Taijutsu to know which was stronger or weaker.

« Then let’s see the real results in action. »

Senju Tobirama’s expression remained unchanged, but a glimmer of coldness flashed in the depths of his eyes.

Seeing him standing with his arms crossed, he gently raised a finger, and the aura that erupted from his body caused the people around him to freeze.

He had not forgotten what happened yesterday afternoon.

Although he had always disliked the Uchiha, Konoha’s Bloodline Limit was not something other Ninja Villages could covet.

« Wait, is there no one else in Konoha ? Why choose such a small child to fight me ? »

If Hong was satisfied with his opponent, then Dou was undoubtedly a bit reluctant.

Although he was indeed annoyed that his actions were stopped yesterday, and his heart was filled with rage making him want to beat up this white-haired kid from the Senju Clan.

But this was a semi-public match, and there were so many Ninja from Kumogakure present.

If word got back, and everyone knew he had sparred with a Konoha child who was only half his age, how would he be able to show his face when he returned ?

Seeing this, Senju Hashirama also felt the opponent had a point and immediately planned to send someone to call Tobirama’s direct disciple, Monkey, over.

After all, for fighting such a burly vanguard tiger, it would certainly be more appropriate to let Monkey come.

But to everyone’s surprise, the Cloud Village Elder frowned and looked at Dou, saying in a deep voice : « Dou, what did I say before we came ? »

« Sorry, Elder, I was impulsive. »

Upon hearing this, the tall and burly Dou paused his anger, took a few deep breaths to suppress his rage, his chest heaving up and down.

The reason he listened to the Cloud Village Elder so much was not only because the elder was highly respected in Kumogakure, but also because he was a close relative of Dou by blood.

« Don’t worry, kid. Senju Matsumi’s strength is not as simple as you think. If you aren’t careful, you will suffer. » Senju Tobirama sneered.

« Do you really think we in Konoha are like you barbarians in Kumogakure, judging strength by the size and quantity of muscles ? »

« Ahahaha, then let everyone move to Konoha’s training ground. »

Senju Hashirama heard the faint smell of gunpowder in his second brother’s tone, and realizing that he was dissatisfied with Kumogakure because of yesterday afternoon’s events, he quickly changed the subject.

On the way to the Konoha training ground.

The Cloud Village Elder spoke earnestly in a whisper to Dou beside him.

« Dou, don’t forget what our purpose is : to test the depth of Konoha, not to really let you fight to your heart’s content ! »

« The outside world thinks we in Kumogakure are a group of savage, brainless warriors, but if you see yourself that way too, you will never be fit to become the Village’s Kage ! »

« I have been taught, Elder. »

Dou knew he was in the wrong and lowered his head, humbly listening to the elder’s teachings.

« Hmm, since that kid from the Senju Clan can stand behind the Hokage today, he must be someone from whom much is expected in Konoha’s next generation. »

« Elder, then what should I do ? »

« Test his depth, but don’t strike too hard, so as not to anger the Hokage. After all, we are currently on their turf, so we should avoid their sharp edge. »
