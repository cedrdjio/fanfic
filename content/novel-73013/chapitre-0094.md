# Chapitre 94 — Conflict in the Lounge

« Your reaction is a bit exaggerated, isn’t it ? »

The Feudal Lord of the Land of Water held a folding fan, also painted with a water droplet pattern, to his mouth with his right hand, glancing with a hint of disdain at the Feudal Lord of the Land of Wind, who was burping without restraint.

Feeling a bit skeptical, he also reached out to pick up a bottle of Happy Water and took a tentative sip.

The cool sensation slowly smoothed out the furrowed brows of the Feudal Lord of the Land of Water.

Having just questioned how good this Happy Water could be, he suddenly fell silent.

Seeing this, the other Feudal Lords also picked up bottles of Happy Water one after another, and after taking a small sip, their eyes lit up instantly.

« Ahem, I’m old, so I won’t drink something with such high sugar content. »

The Tsuchikage, Ishikawa, was also tempted, but after smelling the sweet scent through the air, he politely declined.

« Hmph, we’ve been fighting since the Sengoku era, are we really afraid of a mere bottle of drink ? »

The Mizukage, Byakuren, who was also relatively old among the First Kage, refused to admit he was old. With a cold snort, he picked up a bottle of the drink and downed it in one go.

« Refreshing ! »

After letting out a loud burp, the Mizukage, Byakuren, placed the empty bottle back on the tray.

The Raikage, A, having learned from his past mistakes, keenly noticed the hidden business opportunity within.

He looked at Senju Hashirama and said, « What is the recipe for this drink called Happy Water ? I’ll pay to buy it. »

« This recipe is not for sale, but you can order Happy Water at our stores in Konoha. »

Senju Hashirama smiled and refused the Raikage’s request.

Although he didn’t know how to do business, he still knew the difference between being full for one meal and being full for every meal.

Moreover, the intellectual property rights for the Happy Water recipe belonged to Senju Matsuaki, and he had no right to dispose of it.

« Alright. »

Although he felt a bit regretful, the Raikage had only asked tentatively and didn’t hold much hope.

Therefore, after being refused, he just nodded indifferently and dismissed the thought.

Senju Hashirama continued, « Today, you are all free to drink as much Senju-brand Happy Water as you like. Just let us know if you want a refill. »

« Very well, then thank you, Hokage. »

Hearing Senju Hashirama say this, the Feudal Lords and Kage present no longer brought up the matter of buying the recipe.

In their view, no matter how good a drink tasted, it was just a drink. Spending effort managing such things was not as good as taking on more commissions.

However, at this moment, they could not foresee how this drink called Happy Water would sweep through the Ninja World in the future, bringing enormous profits.

« The candidates participating in the third stage of the exam should still be resting in the lounge. They will appear one after another only after the exam rules are read and the drawing of lots is completed. »

Noticing the impatient mood of the Feudal Lords present, Senju Hashirama roughly informed them of the upcoming arrangements.

The moment Senju Hashirama finished speaking, Senju Tobirama’s voice, amplified by a specific Ninjutsu, resounded throughout the arena.

...

Arena, inside the lounge.

Konoha had spent a lot of money to build the arena this time, so naturally, they did not skimp on making the lounge comfortable and well-equipped.

Beneath the numerous seats surrounding the arena, an angular space had been carved out.

The floor was made of smooth, white tiles, and against the wall stood a row of lockable wooden cabinets made from thick timber.

In the center, several wooden chairs with backrests were placed for the waiting candidates to rest.

In the front, there was an extended viewing platform with brand-new railings installed along the edge, making it convenient for candidates to watch the battles taking place in the arena.

After stepping into this viewing platform from the lounge and walking to the left or right, there were two long staircases leading down to the arena floor.

When it was a candidate’s turn to take the exam, they could walk quickly down these stairs and enter the central arena.

Of course, for a Ninja, this was definitely not the only path to the arena below.

Finally, it is worth mentioning that in the aisle between the candidates' resting seats and the viewing platform, there were mobile stalls selling various goods.

These stalls mostly sold food for satiety, thirst-quenching drinks, and emergency medical supplies.

« Let me see... »

Senju Matsuaki hadn’t eaten lunch, so he decided to find a stall here to buy something to fill his stomach.

As for Hyuga Tsukuyomi, because she had already eaten lunch within the Hyuga Clan before leaving, she remained in her seat and did not come along.

Wandering up to a stall, his pace slowed subconsciously until he stopped.

Looking at Hiruzen Sarutobi and Danzo Shimura, who were still disguised as ordinary residents selling Oden, Senju Matsuaki fell silent.

He didn’t know what kind of mission these two were actually executing.

Pushing a cart on the streets of Konoha earlier could still be justified as an undercover mission to monitor and guard against Ninjas from other villages.

But now, the two of them had actually set up shop in this lounge. It couldn’t possibly be just to drug the Kumogakure and Sunagakure Ninjas, could it ?

Seeing Hiruzen Sarutobi constantly signaling him with his eyes to leave, Senju Matsuaki’s mouth twitched slightly, and he moved away from the spot.

After passing a few stalls, he paid for a pancake and walked back to the wooden chair where he had been resting earlier.

However, when he was still a distance away from where he had been resting, Senju Matsuaki paused slightly.

He saw that Hyuga Tsukuyomi was still sitting in her original seat on the wooden chair where he had been resting, but sitting in his spot next to her was an Iwagakure Ninja with a sleazy face, wearing a reddish-brown combat uniform.

This Iwagakure Ninja, around fourteen years old, seemed to be hitting on her, talking to himself while his eyes kept staring at Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s beautiful face.

While making what he thought were humorous and witty remarks, he deliberately shifted his butt to close the distance between them.

On the other hand, Hyuga Tsukuyomi was sitting in her seat, looking a bit troubled, her expression slightly awkward and uncomfortable.

At the same time, her pure white eyes were distractedly looking in the direction where Senju Matsuaki had left earlier.

« Let me tell you, these so-called 'delicacies' of your Konoha are nothing but grandstanding. When it comes to real food, you have to look at our Iwagakure ! »

« If you have a chance later, how about I take you to our Land of Earth to look around and see some novel things ? »

The sleazy Iwagakure Ninja belittled Konoha while boasting about Iwagakure, and his body shifted a little closer to her.

He reached out his right hand greasily, his eyes filled with lewdness and smugness, looking as if he was about to place his hand on Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s shoulder.

However, at that moment, his outstretched lecherous hand was suddenly grabbed by a powerful hand by the wrist, unable to move an inch.

She is only eight years old, you beast !

Senju Matsuaki glanced at Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s troubled and uneasy expression, and a cold light flashed in his scarlet eyes.

Bang—

Senju Matsuaki exerted a little force with his right hand, pulling the Iwagakure Ninja up from his seat and casually slamming him heavily onto the tiled floor of the lounge.

The Iwagakure Ninja, who had been talking eloquently, was left dazed and seeing stars, and after reacting, he showed a look of anger.

« Konoha brat, do you want to die ! ? »

« What are you doing ? Do you want to die ! ? »

It wasn’t just him ; the other Iwagakure Ninjas around, seeing the conflict erupt here, also swarmed over, looking at Senju Matsuaki with ill intent.
