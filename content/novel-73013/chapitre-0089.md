# Chapitre 89 — Hiruzen Sarutobi: Watch Me Work!

« Please forgive me, these are special times. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi scratched his head in embarrassment, then curiously looked around the courtyard of Senju Matsuaki’s home.

It was generally similar to the estate of his Sarutobi Clan, featuring a training ground for practice, trees, flowers, grass, and a small pond.

The only difference was that the trees planted in Senju Matsuaki’s yard seemed exceptionally numerous and sturdy.

Why does this kid plant so many trees in his yard ?

Hiruzen Sarutobi noticed something a bit strange in the courtyard, but he didn’t think much of it, assuming it was just a personal hobby.

« Brother Hiruzen, aren’t you supposed to be on a mission ? »

After maintaining a suitable distance from Hiruzen Sarutobi, Senju Matsuaki could no longer smell the strange mixture of oden and medicinal herbs, and his nose felt much better.

Seeing that Hiruzen Sarutobi was clearly still dressed for his mission, Senju Matsuaki looked puzzled.

« I asked Danzo Shimura to cover for me on the mission for a while. It’s no big deal for me to slip away briefly ; I’ll just head back once I’m done, » Hiruzen Sarutobi explained.

As he spoke, he lifted the herbs he was carrying.

« I heard you saved Biwako’s life during the exams, and I’m here to thank you. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi patted Senju Matsuaki on the shoulder and said solemnly.

As he spoke, he handed a larger bag to Senju Matsuaki, and the fresh scent of medicinal herbs wafted towards him.

Without the scent of oden mixed in from Hiruzen Sarutobi, the fragrance of these herbs was not off-putting.

« I happened to need to go to the pharmacy to buy some medicine, and I thought since you’ve been participating in the Chunin Exams, these might come in handy. But it looks like you don’t need them after all. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi looked Senju Matsuaki up and down, secretly startled.

When he participated in the Chunin Exams back in the day, he was actually a few years older than Senju Matsuaki.

There was also a survival-type exam back then, but because the scale wasn’t as large as the Joint Chunin Exams, the venue was held in a smaller Forest of Death.

He still remembered feeling that he had passed the survival drill with ease, yet he had still inevitably sustained various injuries.

Some were caused by wild beast attacks during the survival drill, and others by fighting with other candidates.

You couldn’t avoid combat in the exams ; even if you ultimately won, you couldn’t avoid getting hurt.

This was the reason he had brought the herbs today to thank him ; he had assumed Senju Matsuaki was at home recovering from injuries.

He hadn’t expected that he had overthought it.

« Thank you, Brother Hiruzen, you’ve really been a huge help. » Senju Matsuaki thanked him with a smile.

He had taken a quick look just now ; the medicinal herbs inside were effective for recovering from minor injuries and replenishing Qi, and they weren’t cheap. Clearly, Hiruzen Sarutobi had put a lot of thought into expressing his gratitude.

Although he didn’t need them, he could keep them at home just in case he needed them in the future.

« As for the matter with Biwako, she is a Konoha Ninja, so it was only right to save her life when she encountered danger. »

As he spoke, Senju Matsuaki looked at the other bag that Hiruzen Sarutobi was still carrying.

Judging by the packaging, this was also medicine purchased from the pharmacy.

« Brother Hiruzen, what’s that bag in your hand ? »

« That brings me to the other reason I went to the pharmacy. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi narrowed his eyes slightly and gave a malicious grin, his smile looking somewhat lecherous.

« The Hidden Cloud Village and Hidden Sand Village went too far this time. They actually sent Jonin disguised as Genin to participate in the Chunin Exams. Danzo Shimura and I thought about it, and we can’t let them get their way so easily. »

Senju Matsuaki realized, « So, is this bag full of laxatives ? »

« Yes, and no. » Hiruzen Sarutobi nodded, then shook his head.

« Danzo Shimura suggested buying laxatives at the time, but I flatly vetoed it. It seems you guys are all the same, one level below me. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi sighed softly, suddenly feeling that he had traveled much further on the Ninja path than the two of them, and a sense of loneliness welled up in his heart.

If heaven had not given birth to me, Hiruzen, the Ninja World would be an eternal night !

Hiruzen Sarutobi’s eyes flashed with intensity as he held the bag of herbs high as if it were a precious treasure.

« This is a miraculous Yang-replenishing medicine from the Nara Clan’s pharmacy. After a normal man takes it, their blood and Qi will surge downwards, turning them into an invincible swordsman on the battlefield ! »

« Could it be that the medicine inside is… »

Senju Matsuaki was truly shocked. Brother Hiruzen didn’t look very honest on a daily basis, but he hadn’t expected him to come up with such a devious plan.

If someone took this medicine and it kicked in during the third stage of the exams, it would truly be humiliating in front of the entire Ninja World.

« That’s right. Don’t those guys from the Hidden Cloud and Hidden Sand want to show off ? I’ll make sure they... Matsuaki, what’s with that look ? »

Halfway through his sentence, Hiruzen Sarutobi noticed Senju Matsuaki constantly winking at him.

« Don’t worry, the Yang-replenishing medicine I bought dissolves in oden and has absolutely no strange smell, » Hiruzen Sarutobi boasted with a smile.

Well, he’s hopeless.

Senju Matsuaki slapped his forehead and gave Hiruzen Sarutobi a look of helplessness.

At that moment, the gate behind Hiruzen Sarutobi, which hadn’t been closed properly, was pulled open slightly, and a young girl appeared outside—it was Biwako.

« Excuse me, I’m here to thank you for your help last time. »

Seeing Senju Matsuaki look at her, Biwako spoke up to thank him and state her purpose.

In her hand, she held a small, exquisitely designed bag with the emblem of the Akimichi Clan, which appeared to be snacks or desserts purchased from a shop under the Akimichi Clan.

Among the young people in Konoha, this was a common type of gift.

« You’re too kind. Please, come in. » Senju Matsuaki welcomed Biwako with a smile.

As for Hiruzen Sarutobi, who was standing to the side, he was now frozen like a statue, completely motionless.

Some people are alive, but he is already dead…

Biwako cast a look of disgust at the herbs Hiruzen Sarutobi was holding, then shifted her gaze away without hesitation.

Arriving in front of Senju Matsuaki, she handed over the gift with both hands, not forgetting to say, « Thank you so very much ! »

Senju Matsuaki took the gift. The two weren’t originally familiar with each other ; their interactions had been limited to the graduation exam at the Ninja Academy and a brief conversation during the Chunin Exams.

Therefore, after chatting for a few sentences, Biwako took her leave, not saying a single word to Hiruzen Sarutobi the entire time.

« Brother Hiruzen, she’s gone. »

Senju Matsuaki patted Hiruzen Sarutobi’s shoulder helplessly, bringing him back to his senses.

« It’s over. Biwako might think I’m a lecherous person, and she might not want to talk to me in the future. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi held his head in his hands, a pained expression on his face.

Who could tell him how to explain being caught by his crush holding an aphrodisiac high in both hands ? ?

Senju Matsuaki kindly reminded him, « Brother Hiruzen, if you explain it to her properly later, it probably won’t be a big deal. But if you don’t return to your post to execute your mission, you’ll really be in trouble. »

« Hiss, I’m leaving then. Watch me work ! »

Hiruzen Sarutobi checked the time and saw it was indeed getting late. He took a deep breath and prepared to leave, not forgetting his and Danzo Shimura’s plan as he departed.
