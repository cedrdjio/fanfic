# Chapitre 142 — Infiltrating the Uchiha Clan Camp!

At night, the Uchiha Clan’s wartime camp.

Moonlight spilled down through the gaps in the clouds, only to be blocked by the branches and leaves of the lush forest.

The soil where flowers and grass once grew wild had been trampled into a path due to the recent war between the Uchiha Clan and the Senju Clan.

Tree trunks of suitable thickness had been artificially sharpened at the tips to form a perimeter fence around the Uchiha Clan’s camp.

At the main entrance of the camp, two young Ninjas from the Uchiha Clan stood guard.

Whenever any strange noise came from the surroundings, two pairs of eyes would simultaneously flash with an eerie red light in the darkness.

At this moment, hearing footsteps approaching from the distance, two scarlet gazes immediately locked onto the newcomer.

« Who is it ! ? » one of the Uchiha Ninjas asked vigilantly.

At the same time, both of their hands instinctively reached into the Ninja tool pouches at their waists, gripping the handles of their Kunai.

The footsteps continued to approach, but there was no response to the earlier question.

After their scarlet gazes met, they both assumed a ready stance, and a hint of killing intent emanated from the two Uchiha Ninjas.

The atmosphere instantly became oppressive.

However, just as a third scarlet gaze appeared, the gradually thickening atmosphere melted away like butter.

The two Uchiha Chunin were slightly stunned as they looked at the striking Three-Tomoe Sharingan in the darkness.

When the moonlight happened to pass through the gaps in the leaves and illuminate that pale face, both men breathed a sigh of relief, and their hands, which had been gripping the Kunai handles, slowly let go.

The two bowed together and shouted, « Senior Akita ! »

They recognized the person who had arrived ; he was a formidable Senior within the clan, and compared to young people like them who could only guard the gate, he was already a battle-hardened Ninja.

The atmosphere of worshipping the strong was very intense within the Uchiha Clan.

In the cruel Sengoku era, where the strong preyed on the weak, this atmosphere was even more pronounced.

To the respectful greetings of his two clansmen, Uchiha Akita did not give the slightest response, his footsteps unhurried as he walked into the camp.

As Uchiha Akita approached, the two Uchiha Ninjas finally saw clearly that there was another person following behind him.

He had silver-white hair and appeared to be about ten years old ; his clothing seemed somewhat different from that of most Ninjas, but it could still be identified as Ninja attire.

Most importantly, there was the clan emblem they detested, as well as his bound hands.

Thus, they simultaneously laughed viciously.

With a member of the Senju Clan becoming a prisoner of the Uchiha Clan, his future treatment would be spent in a dark, sunless dungeon.

Once the war with the Senju Clan ended, these prisoners would become bargaining chips for negotiations.

Of course, that was provided the prisoner could live until that time.

In this era, there was no such thing as treating prisoners well.

Harsh living conditions, food and water so simple they could even be described as « filthy, » and Genjutsu used during interrogations without restraint, which could destroy the brain if one wasn’t careful.

One had to endure all of this to have even a glimmer of hope for survival.

They did not notice.

Their respected Senior Uchiha Akita’s face was deathly pale today, and the whites of his eyes were unusually black, whether due to the influence of the dark environment or not.

« ... »

Senju Matsuaki followed behind Uchiha Akita without a word, turning a blind eye to the mocking gazes of the two Uchiha Ninjas.

It was unexpectedly smooth.

He had successfully entered the Uchiha Clan’s camp following Uchiha Akita.

Matsuaki’s gaze scanned the surroundings without leaving a trace.

Tents of all sizes, campfires used for lighting and warmth, and Uchiha clansmen walking back and forth with weary expressions.

The wars of the Sengoku era were just as tragic as in later generations ; inside a tent closest to him, Matsuaki heard suppressed wailing.

That was the sound of someone who had suffered heavy injuries and narrowly escaped, being bandaged and treated in the crudest way possible due to inadequate sanitary conditions.

Inside the camp, because they were exhausted from participating in the war during the day, most of the Uchiha were resting with gloomy faces, unwilling to pay attention to others.

Uchiha Akita stood in the middle of the road with a cold expression, and Senju Matsuaki, standing behind him, found himself in a dilemma.

The camp was filled everywhere with identical-looking tents, and apart from that, Uchiha clansmen were scattered everywhere.

Finding the location of the dungeon where prisoners and criminals were held did not seem simple.

At this moment, catching a glimpse of a young Uchiha clansman repeatedly casting glances in his direction, Matsuaki had a plan in mind.

« Hey, you damn Uchiha bastard ! Where are you taking me ! ? »

Senju Matsuaki’s loud shout attracted the attention of countless cold gazes.

Under the night sky, inside the camp, he was like prey targeted by a pack of wolves with murderous glares, ready to swarm up and tear him to shreds at any moment.

« You ignorant brat, you should be grateful that Senior Akita didn’t kill you ! ! »

The young man who had been looking this way earlier quickly approached, his One-Tomoe Sharingan already locking onto Senju Matsuaki.

If it weren’t for the fact that a Senior was nearby and it wouldn’t be appropriate to overstep, he probably would have already taught this Senju Clan prisoner a harsh lesson.

« Senior Akita, why don’t you go back and get some rest ? Shall I help you take the prisoner to the dungeon ? »

Then, the young man asked Uchiha Akita respectfully.

Uchiha Akita still did not speak ; he only nodded with a cold face and then walked away quickly in one direction.

The young Uchiha clansman noticed that he did not seem to be heading towards his living quarters, but assuming the other party might have other business to attend to, he did not call out to remind him.

« You damn brat, follow me obediently ! »

The young man snorted coldly, shoved Senju Matsuaki’s shoulder hard, and then walked in another direction without looking back.

Plan successful.

Senju Matsuaki breathed a sigh of relief and, without making any other moves, followed the young man step by step towards the dungeon.

Elsewhere.

Uchiha Akita, who had gradually distanced himself from Senju Matsuaki, passed through the tents, walked by the campfires, and finally bypassed the two camp guards from earlier, heading towards the outside of the camp.

The two Uchiha guards who noticed this scene looked at each other, their expressions full of confusion, but they were not in a position to ask.

Uchiha Akita’s figure disappeared into the shadows of the forest, his naturally dark clothing blending into the darkness.

He deactivated his Sharingan, and in his pitch-black eyes, the cold expression from earlier in the camp was gone, replaced by a dullness like that of a corpse.

Step by step...

Finally, in a remote place far from the battlefield, Uchiha Akita stopped.

Under a few rays of moonlight, his pale face suddenly began to twitch, and flakes of various sizes peeled off from his cheeks, body, and arms, then drifted towards the ground.

The standing Uchiha Akita collapsed within a few breaths ; after the debris scattered, what remained on the ground was a humanoid figure lying on the ground.

The moonlight passing through the gaps in the leaves inadvertently illuminated the corpse’s face, which was clearly completely different from the Uchiha Akita of a moment ago.
