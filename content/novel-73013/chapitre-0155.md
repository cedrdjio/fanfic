# Chapitre 155 — Senju Hashirama VS Uchiha Madara! The Battle of the Valley of the End!

Meanwhile.

Konoha Village, Hokage Office.

Senju Hashirama was sitting at his desk, focused on handling official business.

Suddenly, a Ninja burst through the door, looking terrified.

« Hokage-sama, Uchiha... Uchiha Madara ! ! »

« What ? »

Hearing the name of his close friend, which had rarely been mentioned in recent years, Senju Hashirama gave a start and looked up at the other person.

« Hokage-sama, Uchiha Madara has returned ! »

« Is Madara back ? »

Senju Hashirama chuckled, his lips curling upward uncontrollably.

He just knew that his close friend still cared about him !

Leaving the Village back then was just a minor spat ; that guy Madara was just a bit tsundere.

After being gone for a few years and thinking it over, he would realize that Konoha—with their shared ideals, with Senju Hashirama, and with the Uchiha Clan—was their true home !

Now that the two founders of Konoha were reunited, peace in the Ninja World would surely last even longer !

Senju Hashirama hurriedly asked : « Where is Madara ? »

The Ninja did not answer further, but instead pointed a pale hand toward the window of the Hokage Office.

Next to the desk, the figure of Senju Tobirama appeared abruptly, his face so gloomy it looked like it could drip water.

Senju Hashirama subconsciously followed the direction the Ninja was pointing and cast his gaze outside the window.

The monster with nine tails, roaring at the sky, immediately caused his expression to change drastically.

« Big Brother ! ! »

Without delay, Senju Tobirama placed his hand on Senju Hashirama’s shoulder with a solemn expression.

The next instant, the Flying Thunder God Technique was activated again, and both of their figures vanished from the spot.

...

The edge of Konoha.

The Nine-Tailed opened its massive maw, and a purple-black Tailed Beast Ball began to form within, exuding a devastating sense of pressure.

The next moment, the Tailed Beast Ball transformed into a beam of light and shot out with a boom, heading straight for the central area of Konoha.

At home, Senju Matsuaki didn’t have time to think ; he put on his shoes and dashed off.

« Matsuaki ! »

Uchiha Hikari called out, but without hesitation, she followed closely behind him.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi bit her lip gently and also activated her Byakugan to follow.

In front of the Tailed Beast Ball.

Senju Tobirama appeared with Senju Hashirama using the Flying Thunder God Technique.

« Wood Release : Wood Human Technique ! »

Senju Hashirama pressed his palms together, and the Chakra within his body surged and erupted !

In an instant, a massive wood human rose from the ground, glaring forward like an angry Vajra.

The wood human’s thick arm moved forward, forcibly blocking the Tailed Beast Ball.

The shockwave from the explosion swept in all directions, blowing off the roofs of surrounding houses, and rubble fell like rain.

With the crisis temporarily averted, Senju Hashirama spoke.

« Madara ! »

Senju Hashirama’s voice was filled with anger and sorrow, « Have you gone mad ? ! »

He looked at his former close friend in disbelief.

Senju Hashirama had originally thought that Uchiha Madara’s return meant he had thought things through and was willing to come back to help build the Village together.

He had even thought that having been in the Hokage position for over a decade, it had been long enough.

With Madara returning to the Village, it was the perfect time for him to become the Second Hokage.

Just as his granddaughter Tsunade was born, the coming days of enjoying family life and retirement would not be a bad way to spend his time.

But he hadn’t expected this.

Seeing Uchiha Madara again, he was met with a world-destroying strike !

Uchiha Madara stood atop the Nine-Tailed, looking down at Konoha Village below with indifferent eyes, devoid of any ripples.

« Hashirama, the grudge between us should be settled today. »

« Big Brother, he is an enemy of the Village now ! »

Senju Tobirama said coldly.

Uchiha Madara, just as he had initially thought, had brought destruction to the Village.

Only his big brother, that idiot, believed in him !

He waved his right hand, and several Flying Thunder God Kunai were shot toward the Nine-Tailed.

However, the Nine-Tailed, controlled by Uchiha Madara, suddenly swiped its claw, deflecting all the Flying Thunder God Kunai.

« Tobirama, don’t act rashly. »

Senju Hashirama said in a deep voice, « Move the battlefield first ! »

Senju Matsuaki landed on a nearby rooftop, looking up at that red figure.

He knew that this battle would be one of the most severe tests since the establishment of Konoha.

The future Nine-Tailed attack would truly be small fry compared to this « Uchiha Madara + Nine-Tailed » chaos.

Footsteps came from behind ; Uchiha Hikari had caught up and stood beside him.

She gazed at that giant demon fox, then looked at the red figure, fell silent for a moment, and suddenly asked : « Matsuaki, do you need my help ? »

Senju Matsuaki turned his head to look at her, seeing a hint of crimson faintly swirling in her pitch-black eyes.

At this moment, Hyuga Tsukuyomi, who had been half a step behind, also caught up.

She kept her Byakugan activated, her expression equally solemn.

...

Outside Konoha Village, in a desolate valley.

The light of the Flying Thunder God Technique flickered, and the figures of Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama appeared in the center of the valley.

Arriving at the same time were Uchiha Madara and the Nine-Tailed.

The Nine-Tailed’s massive body descended from the sky, its four paws slamming heavily onto the ground, causing the earth to crack.

Uchiha Madara stood steadily atop the Nine-Tailed, his red armor gleaming with a cold luster in the sunlight.

« Madara, you... »

Hashirama looked at that familiar figure, wanting to say something but stopping.

In his view, Madara’s willingness to move the battlefield outside Konoha Village clearly meant he still held feelings for the Village.

That’s right.

If Uchiha Madara hadn’t been willing, how could Senju Tobirama’s Flying Thunder God Technique have easily transferred him outside the Village ?

« Hashirama, you are still as naive as ever. »

Uchiha Madara said indifferently, the Sharingan in his eyes suddenly rotating, transforming into the pattern of the Eternal Mangekyo.

« Do you think I came here with you to avoid harming the innocent ? »

« I just didn’t want those ants to interfere with our decisive battle. »

Before his words had faded, the Tailed Beast Ball in the Nine-Tailed’s mouth began to condense again.

This time, it was even larger than before ; the purple-black Chakra sphere was over ten meters in diameter, radiating a suffocating sense of pressure.

« Madara, why did you attack the Village ! ? »

Senju Hashirama asked while forming hand signs.

« Wood Release : Deep Forest Emergence ! »

Senju Hashirama made the first move ; countless thick vines burst from the ground, frantically wrapping around the Nine-Tailed’s body.

The Nine-Tailed roared, and the Tailed Beast Ball was fired with a boom, but it was blocked and deflected by layers of Wood Release trees, eventually exploding on the side of the valley, kicking up clouds of dust.

« Big Brother ! »

Senju Tobirama furrowed his brows, found an opening, and thrust a Kunai directly at the Nine-Tailed’s hind leg joint.

However, just at the moment the Kunai was about to hit, Uchiha Madara formed hand signs with one hand.

« Fire Release : Great Fire Annihilation ! »

Towering flames came crashing down like a tsunami, instantly forcing Senju Tobirama to retreat.

Senju Tobirama was forced to retreat, his face becoming increasingly gloomy.

« Tobirama, this is a battle between me and Madara ! »

Senju Hashirama shouted, forming hand signs again, « Wood Release : Wood Dragon Technique ! »

A giant wood dragon shot up from the ground, circling and pouncing toward Uchiha Madara atop the Nine-Tailed.
