# Chapitre 154 — Three Years Later! Uchiha Madara Attacks the Village!

Time flew by, and in the blink of an eye, it was the 13th year of Konoha.

Several years had passed since Senju Matsuaki brought Uchiha Hikari back from the Seal.

Many changes had taken place in Konoha Village.

The trees that Senju Matsuaki had once planted along the streets were now lush and leafy, and the scale of the Village had expanded significantly since its founding.

The brothers Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama were still busy with Village affairs, and the new generation of Ninja was gradually growing up.

Over the past few years, apart from going out on missions, Senju Matsuaki’s life had been generally peaceful.

Uchiha Hikari had moved into his detached house.

At first, Senju Matsuaki had worried that she wouldn’t adapt to life in this era, but facts proved his worries were unnecessary.

Her own room, as well as the kitchen in the house, had completely become her territory to command.

Following the memory of what her 'home' looked like during the Sengoku period, she had roughly organized the room and the kitchen.

Every morning, when Senju Matsuaki woke up, breakfast was already set on the table.

Uchiha Hikari would sit opposite him, quietly reading books bought from the bookstore.

Books were also one of her ways of adapting to the new era.

In the evenings, if Senju Matsuaki didn’t have a mission, the two of them would go to the Village market together to buy groceries.

The relationship between the two, from friends to family, and then to their teenage years, seemed to have developed some indescribable feelings.

The days were plain, yet they made one feel at ease.

Additionally, Hyuga Tsukuyomi, for some reason, also frequently came to Senju Matsuaki’s house.

When she first met Uchiha Hikari, the atmosphere was somewhat subtle.

But from some point on, their relationship had gradually improved.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi had a gentle and introverted personality, doing things meticulously and thoughtfully.

Although Uchiha Hikari appeared cold on the outside, deep down she actually longed to interact with others.

Their personalities seemed distinct, yet they were unexpectedly complementary in some aspects.

Later on, Hyuga Tsukuyomi would come to the house two or three times a week, sometimes even staying the night.

The two girls would huddle under the same quilt chatting until late at night, with topics ranging from Ninjutsu insights to Village gossip.

The relationship between the three seemed to form a strange balance.

Just this year, there was another happy event.

Senju Hashirama’s son, Senju Kazuma, and his wife gave birth to a girl named Tsunade.

Due to being busy with official duties, Tsunade was sent to Konoha to be cared for by her grandmother, Uzumaki Mito.

Returning to Senju Matsuaki himself.

In terms of strength, Senju Matsuaki had not slacked off either.

He constantly honed and mastered Wood Release and the Tremor-Tremor Fruit in actual combat.

He had even thoroughly mastered the capabilities of both Wood Release and the Tremor-Tremor Fruit, developing various combination techniques.

After a sparring session, Senju Tobirama patted his shoulder and said : « Your comprehensive combat strength has already reached the Kage level. »

This statement, coming from the always strict Senju Tobirama, carried undeniable weight.

A twelve-year-old Kage might not be considered outstanding among the army of transmigrators, but it was enough to shock the world.

Last year, Senju Matsuaki was officially appointed as a Konoha Jonin.

After Uchiha Hikari also obtained her identity as a Konoha Ninja, Senju Matsuaki, Uchiha Hikari, and Hyuga Tsukuyomi began forming a team to execute missions.

The missions he led never failed, and his reputation in the Village grew higher and higher.

At the same time, the points accumulated on his Golden Finger platform were also steadily increasing.

He had completed dozens of missions, big and small, and the points had accumulated to a considerable amount.

But he had not used these points, saving them all up.

On one hand, he hadn’t encountered anything he urgently needed to exchange for, preferring to wait for the right item rather than settle for less.

On the other hand, he had a vague premonition that breaking through the Kage level to reach a higher Realm might depend on these points !

...

That afternoon, Senju Matsuaki returned home after finishing a mission.

Pushing open the door, the sound of a spatula stir-frying came from the kitchen, mixed with the voices of Hyuga Tsukuyomi and Uchiha Hikari talking.

« Hikari, you put in too much salt, didn’t you ? »

« Tsukuyomi, this is a kind of innovative method~ »

« But... »

« Oh my, why are you becoming more and more long-winded. »

Senju Matsuaki stood in the entryway, listening to the movement from inside the house, his lips curling into an arc unconsciously.

He changed into his slippers and walked toward the kitchen.

« I’m home. »

The sounds in the kitchen paused for a moment, and the voices of the two girls rang out together.

« Welcome back ! »

Senju Matsuaki finished changing into his house slippers and came to the kitchen, where the two girls brimming with youthful energy looked at him with smiles.

On the dining table, there was a portion of the meal that had just been served.

Senju Matsuaki was just about to open his mouth to respond, when the ground beneath his feet suddenly shook violently.

A deafening roar came from the outskirts of the Village, followed by screams one after another.

The bowls and chopsticks on the table rattled, and soup splashed out from the edges of the bowls.

The smiles of Uchiha Hikari and Hyuga Tsukuyomi froze simultaneously.

« What’s going on ? »

Hyuga Tsukuyomi reacted first, instantly activating her Byakugan, her gaze piercing through the wall to look outside the Village.

The next second, her face suddenly turned pale, « What... is that ? ! »

Senju Matsuaki’s pupils constricted, his figure already darting to the window.

In the direction of the distant Konoha wall, a huge orange demon fox was roaring with its head held high.

It was radiating violent Chakra from its whole body, its nine tails dancing wildly behind it, each swing creating a violent storm.

And on top of the Nine-Tailed’s head, a figure stood proudly.

Long black hair fluttered in the wind, and a set of red armor was particularly eye-catching in the sunset glow.

Uchiha Madara.

Senju Matsuaki’s heart sank sharply.

This day had finally come after all.

« Matsuaki. »

Uchiha Hikari’s voice came from behind, unable to hide the anger in her words, « Who is that ? »

The dish that had just been knocked over was one she had worked hard to research.

« Uchiha Madara. »

Senju Matsuaki said in a deep voice, « Uncle Hashirama’s former best friend, and one of the founders of Konoha. »

Uchiha Hikari narrowed her eyes slightly, her gaze falling on that red figure.

As a member of the Uchiha clan herself, she could feel the suffocatingly massive pupil power on the other person.

Was that the power of the Mangekyo Sharingan ?

No, that’s not right... It was definitely more than that !

That was the power of the Eternal Mangekyo Sharingan !

« What does he want to do ? »

Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s voice carried a hint of trembling.

« Destroy Konoha. »

Senju Matsuaki replied with a heavy tone, briefly.

Uchiha Madara...

At this moment, Senju Matsuaki was no longer the weak toddler of one or two years old.

But now that he had stepped into the Kage level, he still felt a heavy sense of oppression from Uchiha Madara.

He had only ever perceived this feeling from Uncle Hashirama.

Without a doubt, both Senju Hashirama and Uchiha Madara were Ninjas who transcended the Kage level !

In his previous life, there was often another term for powerhouses at the level of Senju Hashirama and Uchiha Madara.

Super Kage !
