# Chapitre 96 — The Assessment Begins!

Upon hearing the voice about to announce the examination rules, even the examinees who were about to clash immediately backed down, tossed out some harsh words, and found a place to sit.

It would be a huge loss if they were disqualified for accidentally violating the rules.

On a wooden chair, Senju Matsuaki stopped chatting with his teammates.

He nibbled on the pancake he had just bought while listening to the examination rules.

After a brief pause, Senju Tobirama’s voice rang out once again.

« There are a total of 56 examinees participating in the third stage of the assessment : 16 from Konoha, 12 from Kumogakure, 10 from Iwagakure, 10 from Hidden Mist, and 8 from Sunagakure. »

« Based on the number of examinees, this assessment is expected to be divided into six rounds until the final winner is decided. »

« The assessment will be conducted in a one-on-one combat format. There are no restrictions on means such as Bloodline Limits, secret techniques, Ninjutsu, Puppets, or Ninja tools. The match ends when one side is defeated or concedes. »

« The assessment criteria are not based solely on victory or defeat. »

« In the spirit of harmony, try your best to avoid maiming or killing. You must not continue to attack after the opponent has conceded. »

« For any examinee who maliciously harms an opponent, Konoha, as the organizer, will represent the Five Great Ninja Villages in issuing a warning or punishment ! »

Following this, Senju Tobirama briefly announced the other rules for the third stage of the assessment.

To save time for the subsequent assessment, he did not go into exhaustive detail, but rather focused on the important points.

Fifty-six examinees, six rounds...

This was the first time Senju Matsuaki had heard the total number of examinees entering the third stage.

With this kind of elimination format, even though the number of matches would decrease as the rounds progressed and the process would speed up, it was unlikely to finish in a single day.

The first round had twenty-eight battles ; calculating at twenty minutes per match, it would take roughly ten hours.

Of course, sometimes battles would fall into a stalemate, while other times one side would crush the opponent instantly ; the exact duration of a battle could not be measured.

« For the sake of fairness, the participants for each assessment will be determined by a lottery conducted by the Daimyo and the Kage ! »

After Senju Tobirama finished saying this, he fell silent.

Meanwhile, in the spectator stands, a Ninja wearing a Konoha forehead protector approached the location of the Daimyo and the Kage, carrying a square wooden box filled with paper balls.

« I didn’t expect such a segment ; how interesting. I’ll go first ! »

The Daimyo of the Land of Lightning, who was closest to the box, watched this scene with great interest and eagerly reached out to pull out a paper ball.

The other Daimyo nearby were disappointed that they hadn’t been the first to 'eat the crab,' but out of concern for their image in a public setting, they remained seated.

« Ai, this seems to be a name from your Kumogakure, right ? »

After glancing at the name on the slip, the Daimyo of the Land of Lightning turned it around to show the other surrounding Daimyo, finally pointing it toward the First Raikage.

Each country had some regional characteristics in their naming conventions, especially the Land of Lightning and Kumogakure, whose naming style differed from other countries.

« Indeed, it is a talented lad from our Kumogakure. » The First Raikage nodded.

« Then let me decide this lad’s opponent. »

The First Raikage, Ai, said this and reached into the box to pull out a paper ball.

After opening it, the slight smile on his Face stiffened, and his originally dark complexion seemed to darken even further.

« It’s an examinee from our Kumogakure, Dou. »

Under the gaze of everyone, the First Raikage had to accept his fate, struggling to maintain a smile as he showed the name on the paper ball to the Daimyo and other Kage.

« Pfft, hahaha, your Land of Lightning has really bad luck~ »

The Daimyo of the Land of Wind, who was closest to the Daimyo of the Land of Lightning, was amused by this scene and slapped his thigh, laughing.

In this way, the first match became an internal conflict for Kumogakure, and one of them would inevitably be eliminated.

However, what really tickled his funny bone was that the two people who drew the paper balls were the Daimyo of the Land of Lightning and the First Raikage themselves.

Hearing this laughter, the expressions of the Daimyo of the Land of Lightning and the Raikage were not very pleasant.

« You’d better worry more about Sunagakure ; we in Kumogakure have twelve examinees, while you in Sunagakure only have eight, right ? »

The Daimyo of the Land of Lightning was not a man of good temper, so he countered by pointing out the number of examinees.

This remark directly hit a sore spot for the Daimyo of the Land of Wind and the First Kazekage.

The Daimyo of the Land of Wind gave the First Kazekage, Reto, an unpleasant look, his eyes filled with a hint of blame.

It seemed as if he was saying, why is the quality of the Ninjas a notch lower than other Villages despite the generous funding ?

However, the First Kage were all supreme powerhouses who had risen to the top through their own strength, so even the Daimyo of the Land of Wind had to show some respect, and thus he did not voice his blame.

Soon, Senju Tobirama announced the names of the participants for the first battle.

In the lounge, Dou, who was resting on a wooden chair, was taken aback and looked in astonishment at his Kumogakure companion, who was also staring with wide eyes.

What is going on ? We are fighting our own people in the first match ?

Which bastard drew the lots ?

Dou of Kumogakure cursed inwardly, unaware that he had just cursed the leaders of his own country and Village.

Even though he was completely unwilling, he still walked down the steps from the sides of the stands outside the lounge to the arena with his companion.

In the spectator stands, the audience, who had been waiting for a long time, instantly perked up, their excitement rising.

« It’s starting ! It’s starting ! ! »

« Kumogakure, go ! Kumogakure, go ! ! »

« Are you still a person from our Land of Fire ? How could you cheer for someone from Kumogakure ! ! »

« Are you stupid ? I’m cheering for the weaker-looking Kumo-Ninja. If he wins, the opponent we Face from the Land of Fire will be weaker, right ! ? »

« Holy crap ! ! Brother, you are a genius ! ! »

In the arena, Dou of Kumogakure and the other Kumo-Ninja immediately engaged in battle after the command to start was given.

Dou knew in his heart that if he held back to save the other person’s Face and pretended to be evenly matched, it would actually damage the reputation of all of Kumogakure.

Instead of letting the audience and the Daimyo watch a pathetic display of incompetence, it would be better to give them a satisfying, crushing victory !

« Lightning Release : Explosive Bomb ! ! »

« Lightning Release : Lariat ! ! »

After cleanly resolving the battle, Dou and the Kumo-Ninja who was ruled as defeated left the arena with dark expressions.

There was only sparse applause from the surrounding audience, mostly from those from the Land of Lightning.

The battle, which had ended so hastily just now, did not satisfy most people.

After all, Dou could not really beat up a junior from his own Village.

If he were to provide an exciting match, the price would likely be pulverizing the Kumo-Ninja who was his opponent.

« Time is more precious than gold ; let’s hurry up and start the next match ! »

The Faces of the Daimyo of the Land of Lightning and the Raikage were both a bit embarrassed, and they hurriedly urged the start of the next round of matches to divert the audience’s attention.
