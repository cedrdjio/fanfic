# Chapitre 68 — Plan Adjustment!

Inside the conference hall, which had become the temporary remote proctoring center for representatives from each Village.

The assessment had just begun, and everyone present had only recently taken their seats.

Today, it was not Senju Tobirama casting the Telescope Technique, but Senju Hashirama.

He went through the same motions as Senju Tobirama had the day before ; a faint light shimmered on the crystal ball, but it ultimately faded back into darkness.

« Say, why isn’t there a picture on this thing yet ? »

Hōzuki Gengetsu rested his hands behind his head, looking boredly at the dark crystal ball on the table.

He had thought he could sit here, drink his beverage, and watch the live broadcast of the exam site just like yesterday—how pleasant that would have been.

Compared to the dull written exam, the second stage’s survival exercise was undoubtedly more interesting to watch.

« The Telescope Technique requires using the target’s Chakra as an anchor point. The reason we had a picture for so long yesterday was because we used the Proctors inside the exam site as our targets. »

« We haven’t identified the Chakra of every single candidate, so we can’t monitor all of them throughout the entire process. »

Seeing that everyone present had doubts and was dissatisfied, Senju Hashirama spoke up to explain.

Upon hearing this, the representatives from the various Ninja Villages felt no regret ; instead, they secretly breathed a sigh of relief.

They certainly did not want Konoha to actually identify the Chakra of their own Village’s Genin and be able to monitor them in real-time.

Otherwise, if they were to meet on the battlefield in the future, it would be a disaster for these candidates.

« However, there are a few situations where we can briefly see the feed, » Senju Hashirama continued.

Just then, the moment he finished speaking.

The crystal balls in front of the representatives from each Village suddenly lit up, and a clear image appeared, accompanied by ripples of Chakra.

In the image, towering trees could be seen in the surroundings, while the ground was covered with scattered rocks, weeds, and charred marks left by Fire Release Ninjutsu.

In the center of the image was a young man wearing a Hidden Mist Village headband ; his body was covered in wounds and bloodstains, looking utterly disheveled.

In his hand was a crushed blank jade chess piece, with traces of Chakra still lingering around it.

Seeing him crush the piece, the Iwagakure Ninja confronting him gave a mocking smile and then turned and left the area.

« Just like in the scene, after a candidate from your Village crushes the piece, a Chakra fluctuation lasting about five minutes will appear. During this time, the crystal ball will continue to broadcast the footage so that the Proctors can launch a rescue. »

Seeing the crystal ball’s sensitive response, Senju Hashirama nodded with satisfaction and continued to explain.

On the other side, Iwagakure’s Mū and Ōnoki also grinned, quite satisfied.

They weren’t just satisfied with the safety measures Konoha had arranged for the exam, but also with the performance of their own Village’s Genin in eliminating the Hidden Mist Genin.

« Little white-beard, why don’t you hurry up and thank Hokage-sama for the arrangement ? Otherwise, your Village’s Genin would have been dead just now. »

Mū sneered, not missing this opportunity to mock his nemesis.

Hearing this, Hōzuki Gengetsu did not retort, but instead stared at the crystal ball in front of him with a dark face.

A gentleman takes revenge, and ten years is not too late !

Soon, when the screen showed three Iwagakure Genin being defeated simultaneously, Hōzuki Gengetsu immediately clapped his hands with a smile.

« Iwagakure is truly united ; they even get eliminated together~ »

« Damn it ! Which guy did this ? ! »

This time, it was Iwagakure’s Mū and Ōnoki whose faces turned gloomy.

They wanted to see clearly in the crystal ball image who the culprit was that had eliminated their Village’s Genin, but limited by the scope of the screen, they couldn’t see clearly.

They only vaguely saw a silver-white flash pass by at the edge of the screen.

[Current progress : 5 jade chess pieces captured, 9 candidates defeated.]

After easily defeating the three Iwagakure Ninja who had initiated an attack upon seeing him alone, Senju Matsuaki quickly left the scene while checking his current progress.

« This speed is still too slow. »

Senju Matsuaki shook his head in disappointment, not very satisfied with his current progress.

After defeating Ebizo earlier, he had stopped paying attention to the Sunagakure sibling duo and instead headed straight into the depths of the primeval forest without delay.

Although he had indeed considered whether to eliminate a future major threat to Konoha right here, Senju Matsuaki ultimately set aside his killing intent.

It wasn’t out of a soft heart, but a trade-off based on the current situation.

An accidental death could only be attributed to bad luck, but if they were deliberately killed, it would certainly incite strong dissatisfaction from the deceased’s Village.

It might even directly become the fuse for the First Ninja World War, causing the war to break out years earlier.

Of course, there was another point : the location where he had fought the Chiyo siblings was the starting point of the assessment.

If a life were lost here, a deliberate investigation would quickly narrow down the general range of suspects.

Coupled with other candidates who started from the same point as witnesses, his identity would be locked down very quickly.

Therefore, all things considered, attacking the Sunagakure siblings was not a good idea.

Since that was the case, he would wait to see the true results when they met on the battlefield in the future.

Senju Matsuaki had absolute confidence in his own strength and the speed at which he was growing stronger.

If Chiyo and Ebizo were no match for him now, then they definitely wouldn’t be in the future either !

The reason he didn’t stifle the two in the cradle was that even if given another ten or twenty years, to Senju Matsuaki, they would still be in the cradle !

After entering the primeval forest, he had encountered other candidates as well.

For the Ninja he actively sought out, he simply defeated them.

If they had a jade chess piece, he would take it, but he didn’t completely eliminate them.

Although the jade chess piece in their hands was snatched away by Senju Matsuaki, because they hadn’t been eliminated, they could still snatch other people’s jade chess pieces to pass the assessment.

As for the Ninja who initiated attacks on him, he showed no mercy.

He not only beat them until they lost their combat effectiveness but also incidentally damaged their pieces, eliminating them on the spot.

However, Senju Matsuaki was not satisfied with the current situation.

In the vast primeval forest, encountering other candidates was entirely a matter of luck.

As the exam progressed, some candidates would be eliminated before Senju Matsuaki could encounter them, leading to the loss of those points.

Unfortunately, although the Senju Clan and the Uzumaki Clan were close relatives, the former could not master the latter’s exclusive Mind’s Eye of the Kagura.

Otherwise, if he could just activate the Mind’s Eye of the Kagura in this primeval forest, he would be able to experience the thrill of farming points.

« I need to adjust the plan for what comes next. »

After pondering for a moment, Senju Matsuaki made up his mind.

Although he didn’t have the ability to perceive over such a large range like the Mind’s Eye of the Kagura, the Byakugan of his good teammate, Hyuga Tsukuyomi, also had miraculous effects in this regard.

Unfortunately, the second stage of the Joint Chunin Exams shuffled the order of all candidates ; it was very rare for siblings like Chiyo and Ebizo to happen to be in the same group.

Senju Matsuaki and Hyuga Tsukuyomi hadn’t encountered such a coincidence and were arranged at different starting points.

Therefore, in Senju Matsuaki’s original plan, he also had the goal of meeting up with her in the primeval forest.

He knew Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s current strength ; if it were just a normal Chunin Exam, there would be no need to worry about safety issues or promotion issues.

However, this Joint Chunin Exams even had some Jonin from other Villages entering, and the level of danger far exceeded the scope of a normal Chunin Exam.

Even with the safety measures prepared by Konoha, it was impossible to guarantee absolute safety.
