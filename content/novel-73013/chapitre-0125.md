# Chapitre 125 — The Five Kage Change Color! The Power That Shakes the World!

In the spectator stands, whether they were ordinary people without Ninja talent or the Kage whose strength stood at the pinnacle of the Ninja World.

Upon witnessing the scene where the water and sky seemed to invert, they all collectively fell silent.

Laymen watch for the spectacle.

For the ordinary spectators and the five Daimyo, the scene in the exam arena was undoubtedly a superb performance.

Protected by the barrier surrounding the exam arena, the impact caused by Senju Matsuaki’s Tremor-Tremor Fruit was barely contained.

To these spectators, it was as if they were watching a surging tsunami through glass.

Experts watch for the technique.

There was no shortage of powerful Jonin in the stands, and among these Jonin, many were skilled in Water Release Ninjutsu, with quite a few having mastered the B-rank Exploding Water Colliding Wave.

But to summarize it in three words : they didn’t understand.

Even the Five Kage frowned in confusion when they saw the cracked ground and walls.

With their level of experience, they couldn’t discern where this tsunami-like power originated.

These cracks in the ground and walls didn’t look like they were formed by a Water Release impact ; instead, it was as if they had been shattered by some irresistible force.

Unfortunately, the turbulent currents in the arena had been churning, and they were seated high up in the stands, quite a distance away.

Therefore, they couldn’t see clearly what exactly had happened inside the arena.

« What terrifying Water Release, » First Kazekage Retto remarked, sighing at the prowess of the younger generation.

He still attributed the recent disturbance to the power of Water Release.

However, he then changed the subject : « But as far as Magnet Release is concerned, this is merely a minor wave. »

The other Kage did not refute this statement.

In the Ninja World, Magnet Release was also known for its formidable defensive capabilities.

In battles between those of the same level, trying to crush the defense of a Magnet Release Ninja head-on was almost impossible.

Soon, the changes on the field confirmed their viewpoint.

The lingering power of the Tremor-Tremor Fruit dissipated, and the torrential water fell like a downpour.

The exam arena had become like a small lake.

Senju Matsuaki concentrated Chakra under his feet, standing steady on the water’s surface, his eyes locked on the black sphere rising from beneath the water ahead.

Boom—

The black sphere burst out of the ground, splashing waves onto the surface.

Then, the sphere made of Iron Sand opened a slit in the middle, revealing Yan, who had been protected inside.

At this moment, although he was not seriously injured, he looked somewhat disheveled.

The cloak that had originally displayed a cool demeanor was now spread open, hanging shapelessly behind his back.

His deep blue hair was completely soaked, plastered against his scalp like a drowned rat.

« Damn it. »

He cursed under his breath, also concentrating Chakra on the soles of his feet to stand on the water.

He had truly been careless just now.

He had anticipated that Senju Matsuaki might use Water Release to change the terrain and environment, but he hadn’t expected such an unexpected follow-up move.

Hidden under his clothes, his arms, thighs, and other areas were bruised purple.

This was the damage sustained from the impact when he was hit by the giant wave earlier.

He only knew that the limitations of the exam arena restricted Senju Matsuaki, preventing him from avoiding his sharp edge as one might in a forest.

Yet he failed to realize that the space of the exam arena also restricted him.

When the Water Release flooded in, he was also like a turtle in a jar !

Senju Matsuaki looked at Yan’s disheveled appearance, smiled first, then turned his gaze toward the wall of the exam arena in the distance.

Then, Matsuaki chuckled and shifted his gaze back to Yan.

Yan followed Matsuaki’s gaze to the side, and his expression immediately darkened.

On the wall of the exam arena, besides the cracks formed by the vibrations, there was a more obvious circular crater.

Its size perfectly matched the Iron Sand sphere he had formed with his Magnet Release earlier.

Clearly, it was caused by being knocked into that spot by the giant wave when he used the Iron Sand to protect himself earlier.

This circular crater undoubtedly proved his disheveled state from just a moment ago.

« If that’s all you’ve got, then your defeat is next ! » Yan said in a deep voice.

In his yellow eyes, the belief in invincibility still remained.

In his view, although he had been a bit disheveled, ultimately, Senju Matsuaki had no way to break his defense.

No matter how surging the giant wave formed by Water Release was, if it couldn’t break his Magnet Release, then victory would ultimately belong to him !

Hearing this, Senju Matsuaki laughed directly.

I don’t know if your Magnet Release is hard, but your mouth is certainly tough.

However, Matsuaki knew in his heart that if it were the previous him, he might have found it truly difficult to deal with the opponent.

Powerful mobility, attack power, defensive power, combined with Jonin-level strength.

But just within these last few months.

Wood Release, Asura Chakra, Tremor-Tremor Fruit...

He had successively obtained new powers and digested and mastered them.

Raising his strength rapidly to a level unimaginable just a few months ago.

Not long ago, before he had obtained the Tremor-Tremor Fruit, he had already been able to bury those three Uchiha who attempted to assassinate him using Wood Release.

And now that he had obtained the Tremor-Tremor Fruit, his strength would rise another level.

After all, in the real world, there is no such thing as suppressing combat power.

With all his might, even Matsuaki himself didn’t know what kind of power he would unleash !

Red eyes met yellow eyes, and murderous intent suddenly appeared !

Senju Matsuaki stepped on the water in his Ninja boots, creating ripples.

Yan of Sunagakure suddenly flapped his wings, and the Iron Sand wings behind him slashed across the water, creating two furrows.

« Magnet Release : Iron Sand Giant Needle ! ! »

While speeding, Yan quickly formed hand signs, and the Iron Sand rapidly gathered and compressed behind him, eventually forming sharp spears.

This was a tactical choice he made after observing Senju Matsuaki’s fighting style.

He did not use the more agile and dense, but relatively fragile, Iron Sand Shigure, but instead switched to the thicker and harder-to-shake Iron Sand Giant Needle.

Under the control of Magnet Release, the giant needles formed by Iron Sand flew toward Senju Matsuaki.

« Senju Matsuaki ! ! » Yan shouted.

He seemed to suffer from the same common ailment as some Ninjas in this world.

When the battle got a bit heated, they would scream the opponent’s name at the top of their lungs.

To this, Senju Matsuaki’s response was...

« Who has a shonen manga rival setup with you ! »

« Super Strength ! ! »

First, the Chakra in his body surged, gathering on the short blade in his hand.

« Vibration ! ! »

Then, the power of vibration condensed into a white ball of light, also gathering on the blade of the short knife.

The two combined, achieving an effect where one plus one is far greater than two.

An incredibly terrifying power condensed on the blade in his hand ; at this moment, Senju Matsuaki felt as if he held the entire world.

A look of exhilaration and high spirits curled the corners of his mouth.

From this moment on, he could be said to have truly stepped onto the stage named « Ninja World » !

« Hey, are you kidding me ? ? ? »

« The space around that blade is trembling ! ! »

« That power is shaking this world ! ? »

In the stands, the Five Kage all changed color.

First Kazekage Retto, in particular, wore a look of shock, standing up from his seat suddenly without any regard for his image.

« Is... is this our Senju Super Strength ? »

Senju Hashirama looked as if he had a question mark floating over his head, his face showing a thoughtful expression.

If the power of Super Strength is fully unleashed, it indeed has the force to split mountains and crack rocks.

But to say that the power Senju Matsuaki was currently displaying, which seemed capable of shattering space itself, was Super Strength, was a bit beyond his understanding.
