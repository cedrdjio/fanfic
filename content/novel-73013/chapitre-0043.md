# Chapitre 43 — Matsumi VS Third Raikage (Youth Version)!

Both sides still had many trump cards they hadn’t revealed, but since it was just a sparring match, there was no need to go that far.

After fighting for so long, both sides had roughly figured out the extent of the other’s strength.

The current outcome wasn’t because Hong from Kumogakure was yielding, but because he understood that even if the fight continued, he would still be the one to lose in the end.

Seeing this, Senju Hashirama instinctively wanted to applaud his younger brother.

However, realizing what the occasion was, he hurriedly placed his half-raised hand over his mouth and gave a light cough.

« You two have worked hard. Now, what comes next is... »

Among the group from Kumogakure, Dou stepped forward into the arena, crossing his arms as he waited for Matsumi to take the stage.

« I can’t believe Master actually lost to that white-haired guy named Senju Tobirama... » Dou thought to himself.

Dou knew exactly how capable his master was.

Even though he often heard others praise him, saying his talent surpassed that of the Raikage and Hong in their youth, talent had yet to be fully realized.

As far as he knew, Hong’s strength wasn’t far off from the Third Raikage, and in a few more years, he might even surpass him.

He hadn’t expected that even such a powerful master would lose to Konoha’s Senju Tobirama.

This cast a shadow over his heart, as he had always believed that Kumogakure was not inferior to Konoha.

« Hey, kid, do you know that Flying Thunder God Technique thing ? »

Watching Senju Matsuaki walk onto the field, Dou couldn’t help but ask.

He had watched the previous battle very carefully, and he found Senju Tobirama’s elusive Flying Thunder God Technique simply unsolvable.

At least for him right now, he couldn’t think of a way to counter such an outrageous Ninjutsu.

They had learned about the relationships of these important Konoha figures beforehand, and he knew that this silver-haired, red-eyed kid in front of him was the nephew of the Senju Tobirama who had just fought.

If this kid also knew the Flying Thunder God Technique from earlier, then the coming match would likely be another bitter struggle.

« Flying Thunder God ? I don’t. » Matsumi shook his head indifferently.

If he could learn the Flying Thunder God, he wouldn’t have felt so unsure when he was asked to fight earlier.

The Lightning Release Chakra Mode was only a B-rank Ninjutsu, but the Flying Thunder God was a bona fide S-rank Ninjutsu.

The next golden-haired little sun to learn the Flying Thunder God Technique hadn’t even been born yet.

« Alright, I’ll go easy on you, so you can rest assured. »

Hearing Matsumi say he didn’t know the Flying Thunder God Technique, Dou felt relieved, but at the same time, he couldn’t help but feel a bit disappointed.

Although he felt the Flying Thunder God was hard to counter, he was still curious about what it felt like to face it head-on.

« Lightning Release Chakra Mode ! »

Dou was clearly already proficient in using the Lightning Release Chakra Mode ; just like when Hong activated the Chakra Mode earlier, lightning surrounded his body, forming an armor of crackling arcs.

The constitution of the Senju Clan was powerful precisely because their physical vitality was much higher than that of ordinary people.

And the Lightning Release Chakra Mode, through the stimulation of lightning, similarly enhanced the user’s speed and strength by increasing physical vitality.

At this moment, Matsumi performed hand seals, seizing the initiative before the distant Dou could get close.

« Demonic Illusion : Tree Bind Death ! »

In the distance, Dou, who had activated the Lightning Release Chakra Mode and was about to move forward to attack, was shocked to find a thick tree rising from the ground, binding his hands and feet completely.

« What ? ! Could this be the legendary Wood Release ? ! »

Dou’s eyes widened sharply, feeling the wood trapping his hands and feet tightening more and more. The trees that he would normally be able to break free from easily now felt as if they were cast from steel, unmoving.

Genjutsu was inherently related to the mind and the five senses ; since Dou subconsciously believed this was Wood Release, he naturally found it even harder to break free from the binding of the trees.

However, he was not some Genin who had just stepped onto the battlefield ; he quickly calmed down and discovered the clue.

« No, this is Genjutsu ! »

The lightning flickering on Dou’s body suddenly paused, then began to pulse at an even more frenzied frequency.

The principle of Genjutsu was basically to disrupt the enemy’s Chakra, thereby interfering with their normal perception ; therefore, those caught in a Genjutsu could also break free by disrupting their own Chakra flow.

Any qualified Ninja would not allow themselves to have a huge weakness ; even if they weren’t good at using Genjutsu, they had to be skilled at breaking it.

From Dou’s perspective, the trees that had been tightly binding his hands and feet vanished as if they had never existed.

And in front of him, Senju Matsuaki, holding a short blade, had already arrived before him, winding up to slash with all his might.

« Well done ! ! »

Dou laughed in anger ; his pent-up fury finally found an outlet, pouring entirely into his clenched right fist.

Whoever was suddenly hard-controlled by a Genjutsu while preparing to strike probably wouldn’t be in a very good mood.

Facing the blade now, Dou met it directly with his iron fists, yet he was not at a disadvantage.

Crackle—

Matsumi’s Enhanced Strength slash collided with Dou’s heavy, lightning-coated fist, and the already active electric arcs splattered everywhere as if stimulated.

« Such fast speed ! »

Matsumi’s pupils constricted sharply, his red eyes reflecting an increasingly intense and massive lightning glow, as he saw Dou’s other fist swinging toward him.

Reacting, Matsumi had no time to dodge, so he crossed his hands in front of him to take the blow head-on, his body sent flying backward.

As expected of the person regarded by the Cloud Village Elder as the future of Kumogakure, Matsumi was clearly at a disadvantage after just the first exchange of this battle.

After bracing himself against the ground to stand up, the pain from the heavy blow to his arms and the burning and numbing sensation caused by the lightning contact did not cause Matsumi to frown.

However, after feeling the pain and discomfort in the injured areas slowly disappearing, Matsumi was taken aback.

« Could it be the effect of the Asura Chakra ? »

The recovery ability of the Senju Clan was much stronger than that of ordinary people, but ordinary clansmen could not heal wounds visibly to the naked eye.

Even before, Matsumi’s recovery ability still belonged to the category of ordinary clansmen.

But now, he could clearly feel that his recovery ability was far stronger than it had been yesterday, before he obtained the Asura Chakra.

However, this was reasonable.

Uzumaki Naruto’s ability to take a beating was well-known, and Uncle Hashirama’s recovery speed was terrifyingly strong.

Now that he also possessed the Asura Chakra, there was no reason he wouldn’t get a potential boost in his recovery ability.

« The Elder mentioned yesterday that this is the Senju Clan’s Enhanced Strength ; it certainly has its merits. »

In the distance, Dou still maintained the Lightning Release Chakra Mode, thoughtfully recalling the Enhanced Strength slash Matsumi had just used.

However, Enhanced Strength didn’t have much reference value for him ; after all, the Lightning Release Chakra Mode emphasized ferocity and wide, sweeping movements, while Enhanced Strength required precision and control.

« If we were the same age, he might be a decent opponent, but right now, he is still too green. »

« Let’s end this battle ! ! »

However, just as Dou prepared to use Body Flicker to get close to Matsumi and then take him out with a combo of punches under the Lightning Release Chakra Mode...

...he suddenly discovered that Senju Matsuaki had already quickly sheathed his short blade and was performing hand seals rapidly with his hands held horizontally in front of him.

The originally bright sky and earth suddenly turned pitch black.
