# Chapitre 100 — Have You Already Fallen?

«  Hahahaha, looks like your luck isn’t very good, little sister~  »

Seeing Hyuga Tsukuyomi stand up and walk down the steps, the sleazy Iwagakure Ninja immediately let out a malicious laugh.

He was no match for the intimidating-looking Senju Matsuaki, but surely he couldn’t lose to this seemingly weak and fragile little girl, could he ?

Although the Hyuga Clan possessed the Byakugan, which could be called a heaven-sent gift, it still depended on age.

If they were of similar age, the sleazy Iwagakure Ninja would probably just go up, exchange a few moves, and surrender to avoid being taken out by a Gentle Fist combo.

But with an age gap of six or seven years, it was enough to erase the gap in talent.

Although I couldn’t beat a thirty-year-old Senju Hashirama, taking on ten one-year-old Senju Hashiramas wouldn’t be a problem !

Harboring such thoughts, the sleazy Iwagakure Ninja felt that he was capable again.

«  I originally wanted to get along with you as a caring big brother, but all I got in return was distance. I won’t pretend anymore ; I am the strongest among the Iwagakure candidates. I’m laying my cards on the table !  »

The sleazy Iwagakure Ninja tightened the gauntlets on his hands, threw out those harsh words, and waited expectantly for Hyuga Tsukuyomi to show fear and retreat.

In his eyes, Hyuga Tsukuyomi was just a weak Konoha Genin.

Setting aside the fact that someone was backing her up just now, there was no one to back her up in the arena.

However, Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s reaction stunned him.

Seeing Hyuga Tsukuyomi smile and say goodbye to Senju Matsuaki, she didn’t look back at the inexplicable commotion behind her, but instead walked quickly into the arena and stood her ground.

The sleazy Iwagakure Ninja’s previous words, aside from causing his Iwagakure companions to awkwardly distance themselves, caused no further ripples.

«  Will the other candidate please enter the arena quickly ? The battle is about to begin ! !  »

Senju Tobirama’s urging voice echoed over the arena, multiplying his embarrassment.

«  Damn it ! !  »

Cursing inwardly, the sleazy Iwagakure Ninja walked quickly down the steps and jogged all the way to stand opposite Hyuga Tsukuyomi.

«  The battle begins ! !  »

Seeing that both sides had arrived in the arena, Senju Tobirama shouted the command for the battle to begin without the slightest delay.

After all, Hyuga Tsukuyomi was his older brother’s student, so he had paid some attention to her and had some understanding of her strength.

In Senju Tobirama’s view, the battle would likely end quickly, so it was better to start it sooner to save time for battles that the audience would find more enjoyable later.

«  Witness the power of our Iwagakure ! !  »

The moment the battle started, the sleazy Iwagakure Ninja quickly formed hand seals.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi, who had already activated her Byakugan, could clearly see the Chakra inside the opponent’s body rapidly mobilizing towards his hands.

«  Earth Release : Rock Fist Technique ! !  »

Heavy Earth Release Chakra condensed on the sleazy Iwagakure Ninja’s right fist, and layers of hard rock tightly wrapped from his right fist to his forearm, forming a gauntlet made of rock.

During the second stage of the exam in the primeval forest, Senju Matsuaki had also encountered Iwagakure Ninjas using this Ninjutsu.

This was a Ninjutsu frequently used by Iwagakure Ninjas who excelled in Earth Release ; it had a low learning threshold but could be used for both offense and defense.

Judging solely from the execution process and effect of the Rock Fist Technique from this sleazy Iwagakure Ninja, he had indeed touched the threshold of a Chunin.

Unfortunately, he had met an opponent he could not match !

With his right-hand rock fist carrying heavy power, the sleazy Iwagakure Ninja wore a ferocious expression and rushed towards Hyuga Tsukuyomi.

Embarrassment, fear, anger, hatred... He wanted to vent all the negative emotions he had just felt into this one punch !

Seeing this, Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s activated Byakugan remained calm.

Her hands assumed the starting stance of the Gentle Fist at a leisurely pace, but if it were a Ninja familiar with the Gentle Fist, they might have noticed that her movements seemed somewhat different from the usual Gentle Fist.

«  What, this is ! ?  »

On the spectator stand, an elderly man with the Byakugan stood up abruptly, looking at the posture Hyuga Tsukuyomi had assumed in disbelief.

As if realizing something, he turned his head and looked at Hyuga Teruake, who was sitting upright to the side, with a blaming and questioning gaze.

The Byakugan-wielding elder narrowed his eyes slightly, his tone seemingly carrying a hint of chill : «  Clan Chief Teruake, shouldn’t you give me an explanation for this ?  »

Regarding this question, Hyuga Teruake did not answer directly, but only glanced at him indifferently before withdrawing his gaze.

«  Fourth Elder, you have lost your composure,  » Hyuga Teruake said in a deep voice, «  Sit back down quickly, don’t bring shame to the Hyuga Clan.  »

The spectator stand was indeed a public place, and the behavior of the Fourth Elder of the Hyuga Main Family suddenly standing up had already attracted dissatisfied gazes from some spectators in the back rows.

«  Hmph, after the Chunin Exams, this old man will visit again. I hope that by then, Clan Chief, you won’t be 'away from home' due to some 'important business' again.  »

The Fourth Elder sneered unceremoniously, then flicked his sleeves and sat back in his seat, watching Hyuga Tsukuyomi in the arena with a gloomy face.

On the contrary, the pair of Byakugan that Hyuga Teruake used to look into the arena grew brighter and brighter, full of anticipation and amazement.

Not bad, this is it.

How could such amazing talent be buried by the Caged Bird Seal ! ?

In the arena.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s hands waved rapidly, forming trails of afterimages.

At the same time, her feet also moved with a specific footwork and frequency, causing her petite body to spin like a top.

Seeing this scene, the sleazy Iwagakure Ninja, whose attack was already launched and could not be stopped, felt his pupils shrink violently, and for some reason, a trace of fear welled up in his heart.

However, it was too late.

«  Eight Trigrams Palms : Heavenly Spin ! !  »

Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s Chakra erupted from her body’s acupoints, and under the effect of the rotation, formed a Chakra hemisphere centered on her body.

The rapidly spinning Chakra hemisphere caused the surrounding air to whistle, clearly echoing over the arena.

Eight Trigrams Palms : Heavenly Spin was originally an Advanced Taijutsu secretly transmitted within the Hyuga Main Family, possessing a defensive power known as «  Absolute Defense.  »

In the Late Stage, when all sorts of overpowered characters were flying everywhere, Heavenly Spin was indeed just a defensive method that couldn’t even make the rankings.

But in the present, where the power levels had not yet completely collapsed, the name of Heavenly Spin’s absolute defense was not entirely inflated.

The sleazy Iwagakure Ninja’s Rock Fist collided with Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s Heavenly Spin.

The Rock Fist, which had appeared unstoppable in the eyes of the audience, suddenly came to a standstill.

In the next second, the sleazy Iwagakure Ninja’s body was sent flying outwards like a rag doll, and the thick rock fist on his hand had long since turned into bits of soil and scattered all over the ground.

By the time the audience reacted, they only saw him lying on the ground half-dead, his right fist, still coated with some mud, already a bloody mess.

Back at the original spot, Hyuga Tsukuyomi stopped the Heavenly Spin, took a deep breath, and intended to form hand seals to use Ninjutsu to press the attack.

However, when she saw the situation in front of her, her expression froze.

«  W...what, have you already fallen ?  » Hyuga Tsukuyomi murmured in surprise, her little mouth slightly agape.
