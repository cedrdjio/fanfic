# Chapitre 120 — Hyuga Tsukuyomi Unconscious!

« Some kind of invisible attack method ? »

As the impact of the Eight Trigrams Empty Palm drew closer, Yan reacted in time.

He was no stranger to this type of invisible attack ; most Wind Release Ninjutsu were like this, making their trajectories difficult to track with the naked eye.

Many people in the Hidden Sand Village were skilled in Wind Release Ninjutsu and secret techniques.

The reason he hadn’t reacted in time just now was simply because he didn’t know the Hyuga Clan’s Gentle Fist actually had long-range methods.

Drawing on his past experience dealing with Wind Release, he used Magnet Release to erect a shield of pitch-black Iron Sand in front of him, blocking his body.

However, in the next instant, something unexpected happened !

The impact generated by the Eight Trigrams Empty Palm collided with the pitch-black iron curtain, and the harsh sound of friction between the Iron Sand particles reached Yan’s ears.

Just as he thought he had blocked the impact, a hole was pierced through the sturdy iron curtain, and the impact surged straight toward his face.

A flash of shock crossed his yellow eyes. Yan tilted his body sharply to the side, trying to dodge, and in the end, his cheek narrowly grazed past the impact.

The pitch-black iron curtain dissipated, revealing his cold and extremely gloomy face.

The pain on his right cheek made him subconsciously reach out to touch it. When he looked at his fingers, a trace of blood remained on them.

« Such penetrating power... I underestimated you. »

Yan’s voice was very calm, but even the spectators who didn’t know his personality could feel the bone-chilling coldness hidden within it.

« However, your path ends here. Embrace your tragic fate ! ! »

He raised his blood-stained right hand, and the pitch-black Iron Sand gathered at his side as if forming a giant hand, sweeping toward the location where Hyuga Tsukuyomi was.

« Magnet Release : Iron Sand Coffin ! ! »

With Iron Sand covering the front and back, Hyuga Tsukuyomi had no room to dodge, and her petite figure in white was instantly engulfed by the pitch-black Iron Sand.

A trace of coldness and cruelty appeared in Yan’s yellow eyes as he made a grasping motion with his right hand.

« Magnet Release : Glass Crush ! ! »

A flash of satisfaction crossed Yan’s eyes.

Thinking back to the situation just now, he still felt lingering fear.

Although the power of the Eight Trigrams Empty Palm had been weakened by the blocking Iron Sand and couldn’t have caused him serious injury.

But if he hadn’t dodged, it was certain that one of his eyes and half of his face would have been injured.

And even though he had dodged in time, his right cheek had been grazed by the aftermath of the shockwave.

For a Jonin who had mastered the Magnet Release Bloodline Limit, being careless and getting his cheek scratched in a fight against a Chunin was such a huge humiliation ! ?

Because of this, the cruelty and brutality in his heart were ignited by anger, leading him to unleash what could be called a killing blow.

Glass Crush, by wrapping the enemy in Iron Sand and squeezing with full force, completely crushes the enemy into pulp ! !

As long as he clenched his right hand, Hyuga Tsukuyomi, who was already surrounded by the pitch-black Iron Sand, would instantly turn into a blood mist filling the sky !

« Not good ! ! »

On the spectator stand of the lounge, a murderous intent surged in Senju Matsuaki’s crimson eyes.

His right hand gripped the iron railing of the stand tightly. Under the influence of his anger and worry, his power leaked out, causing the sturdy iron railing to shatter inch by inch.

Senju Matsuaki exerted force with his feet, preparing to vault into the exam arena to save her.

On the field, just as Yan was about to clench his right hand, he suddenly felt a wave of dizziness in his brain, and his right hand couldn’t move at all.

His gaze shifted, and he discovered that the two Proctors who had been waiting at the edge of the exam arena had formed hand seals at some point.

The Yamanaka Clan Proctor released a mental shock, hindering his movements.

And the Nara Clan Proctor also used a Family secret technique, connecting the shadow at his feet to Yan’s shadow, controlling his body.

Seeing this, Senju Matsuaki breathed a sigh of relief and retracted the movement he was about to make to vault over the railing and intervene.

« The battle is over. Yan from the Hidden Sand Village wins ! ! »

« Additionally, due to attempted intentional murder of an opponent, a warning is issued to examinee Yan ! ! A second warning will result in disqualification from the exam ! ! »

Senju Tobirama’s voice came at the right moment, announcing the result of this assessment.

« Hmph ! ! »

Yan snorted coldly, but the last trace of killing intent in his heart dissipated along with it.

After his emotions calmed down, the churning feeling in his stomach became more intense, causing his face to turn pale and then green.

Constructing a circular platform of pitch-black Iron Sand under his feet, he flew straight toward the examinee lounge above the exam arena.

In the instant before flying past the stands and entering the lounge, his peripheral vision caught sight of Senju Matsuaki standing on the spectator stand.

Yellow eyes met crimson eyes, and both simultaneously revealed a trace of deep murderous intent !

...

In the medical room prepared specifically for those injured during the exam.

The medical Ninja was moving with steady and meticulous care, checking Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s physical condition.

The girl’s complexion was somewhat pale at this moment, and even her lips had lost their color.

Lying on the bed in the medical room, she looked like a delicate doll.

There were four other people standing around.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s older brother Hyuga Teruake, the two brothers Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama, who had sent a Wood Clone and a Shadow Clone respectively, and Senju Matsuaki.

At this moment, they were all frowning, anger suppressed in their eyes, yet they remained quiet for fear of disturbing the examination.

After a long while, the medical Ninja finished the examination and broke the silence.

« I have carefully examined the main injured areas, such as the limbs, shoulders, and back. In short, the patient is not in any serious condition, so you may rest assured, Lords, » she reassured them.

The examination just now was arguably the most nerve-wracking one she had ever participated in since she began medical work.

Konoha’s first-in-command, second-in-command, and the Clan Chief of the great Hyuga Clan.

Any one of these people alone was far more important than her current direct superior, let alone having all three gathered here together.

Pausing, she continued softly, « The patient only suffered contusions and stagnation of Qi and blood. Her internal organs and Meridians were not damaged, and there are no fractures or serious injuries. She will recover after recuperating for a period of time and will not have any sequelae. »

« Her current weakness and unconsciousness are likely due to excessive Chakra consumption from the high-intensity battle, causing her body to automatically enter a dormant state. She should wake up by tonight. »

Hearing the medical Ninja say this, the expressions of those present softened slightly.

Recalling the dangerous situation in the exam arena just now, Hyuga Teruake couldn’t help but say angrily in a low voice, « The Hidden Sand Village is truly bullying us too much, attacking Tsukuyomi with such heavy hands for no reason ! »

« Uh, this... »

Hearing this, the anger on Senju Matsuaki’s and Senju Tobirama’s faces paused, and after exchanging a glance, they both chose to remain silent.

It seemed, one couldn’t exactly say it was for no reason.

Senju Matsuaki and Senju Tobirama both understood why Yan from the Hidden Sand Village was so furious and had attacked so ruthlessly.

However, for Hyuga Tsukuyomi, this was indeed an undeserved disaster.

If the Proctors hadn’t intervened in time, Hyuga Tsukuyomi would likely have already been crushed into blood foam by the Magnet Release.

Even if Yan from the Hidden Sand Village might have pulled his punches at the last moment due to fear of Konoha, at the very least, a serious injury would have been unavoidable.

Thinking of this, a trace of cold murderous intent surged in Senju Matsuaki’s heart once again.

The Tremor-Tremor power he had recently acquired seemed to be stirred slightly by his angry emotions as well.
