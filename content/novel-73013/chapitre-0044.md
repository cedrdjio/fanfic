# Chapitre 44 — Genjutsu: Bringer of Darkness!

«  Genjutsu : Bringer of Darkness !  »

As Matsumi finished the hand seals, a massive amount of Chakra surged out from his body, causing everyone outside the arena to pause in shock.

The Cloud Ninjas, who had originally thought this would be a one-sided crushing victory for Dou, began to watch the match with serious expressions for the first time.

As for Dou on the stage, what he saw was a completely different picture compared to everyone else.

In his field of vision, it felt as if ink had been splashed into his eyes ; first, it started at the center of where he was looking, then it spread outwards at lightning speed until the entire world turned pitch black.

Dou quickly scanned his surroundings, only to find that both the distant buildings and the audience he could see just moments ago were submerged in darkness.

«  Another Genjutsu...  »

Realizing the situation quickly, Dou gritted his teeth and clenched his fists, the lightning surrounding his body suddenly darting outwards like snakes.

Upon discovering he had been caught in a Genjutsu again, he quickly repeated his previous tactic, attempting to dispel the Genjutsu by disrupting his own Chakra.

However, the pitch-black environment around him remained unchanged ; if anything, the darkness had deepened even further.

Genjutsu : Bringer of Darkness is an A-rank Genjutsu capable of stealing all light from the enemy’s vision, effectively sealing their sight.

Like the previous «  Demonic Illusion : Tree Binding Death  », this technique was created by the Senju Clan.

Aside from restraining enemies on the battlefield, Tree Binding Death could also serve to intimidate them, making them mistakenly believe they were facing the legendary Wood Release of Senju Hashirama.

Under such fear, the enemy would sink deeper into the Genjutsu until their Chakra was completely disrupted, making it impossible for them to break free.

The Bringer of Darkness technique, however, was developed specifically for the Uchiha Clan ; for most Uchiha Ninjas who rely on their eyes to cast Genjutsu, this technique is remarkably effective.

However, later on, when Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama faced the two pairs of Mangekyo from the Uchiha Clan, the effect of Bringer of Darkness was limited due to the Mangekyo’s ocular power.

But for ordinary Ninjas, Bringer of Darkness is not as easy to break as other common Genjutsu.

In the original timeline, Senju Hashirama, who had been reanimated by Orochimaru using Edo Tensei, used the Bringer of Darkness technique.

At that time, Hiruzen Sarutobi, the Third Hokage who was already hailed as the «  Professor of Ninjutsu  », was unable to break free from the Bringer of Darkness and could only passively defend and take hits.

For Senju Hashirama, Genjutsu was merely an auxiliary tool, and the most commonly used Genjutsu he mastered was the Bringer of Darkness.

Furthermore, since the Bringer of Darkness was created by the Senju Clan, it was suitable for Ninjas of the Senju Clan to learn and master.

Therefore, some time ago, after Matsumi mastered Tree Binding Death, Senju Hashirama had directly tossed him the scroll containing the training method for the Bringer of Darkness.

Matsumi did not disappoint Hashirama’s expectations ; after a period of hard training, he was already able to use this Genjutsu proficiently.

«  This technique...  »

Homura, the Raikage’s guard, furrowed his brows, watching Dou on the stage stumbling around like a headless chicken with a solemn expression.

The Bringer of Darkness technique was released only against Dou on the stage ; the vision of the audience was not affected.

He could tell that Dou was trapped in a Genjutsu at this moment, and even by disrupting his Chakra, he had failed to break free from it.

«  Big Brother, did you notice that Matsumi’s Chakra has become...  »

On the other side, Senju Tobirama also wore a look of surprise. However, what surprised him was not Matsumi’s Bringer of Darkness, but the immense Chakra he had just sensed.

In his view, the amount of Chakra Matsumi had just revealed was almost catching up to his own, which had been accumulated over decades.

«  Ah, perhaps Matsumi is just like me.  »

Senju Hashirama watched the match on the stage with great interest, not paying too much attention to what Tobirama had said.

«  I almost forgot about this.  » As if thinking of something, a flash of realization crossed Senju Tobirama’s eyes.

In the Senju Clan, every one or two generations, a gifted Ninja would appear, far surpassing the average level of the Senju Clan in both Chakra reserves and physical constitution.

This fact had always been recorded within the Senju Clan, and Senju Hashirama himself was the best example.

«  Don’t be impatient, Homura.  »

The Cloud Village Elder was not worried, but instead smiled and patted Homura on the shoulder.

He saw that Homura beside him was somewhat worried about the situation on the stage, fearing that Kumogakure would lose face in a foreign land if they lost two consecutive matches.

«  This Genjutsu is indeed strong, but thinking that Dou will lose just because of this is far from the truth.  »

After speaking, the Cloud Village Elder said no more, crossing his arms and watching the battle on the stage continue.

«  That’s true. Dou is a genius who has surpassed even Lord Raikage and me in terms of talent.  »

The last trace of worry in Homura’s heart dissipated with this thought.

«  Kid, don’t think you can leave me helpless just like this !  »

Dou was already a battle-hardened Ninja. After adapting to his blocked vision, he quickly found the right rhythm for battle.

Although he couldn’t see his opponent and would be nimbly dodged whenever he blindly charged in, hoping to catch him by luck.

If Matsumi wanted to launch an attack on him, whether it was Taijutsu or Ninjutsu, he would be able to react immediately and rush towards the direction of the caster.

«  A brute from Kumogakure, living up to his reputation...  »

About ten meters behind Dou, Matsumi stood still, holding a short blade, with cold light flickering in his scarlet eyes.

It seemed that the «  Strongest Shield  » of the future Third Raikage was already taking shape.

Just now, Matsumi had attempted to use long-range Ninjutsu, but Dou had directly smashed the Water Dragon Bullet Matsumi released without dodging or evading, while simultaneously locking onto his location. It was only after a few exchanges that Matsumi found an opportunity to break away.

In terms of raw strength, the gap between the two was simply too great.

It was impossible for a Chunin to defeat a Jonin, who was crushing him in every aspect, just by relying on a single Bringer of Darkness technique.

Using the Bringer of Darkness to forcibly prolong the battle was already a clever trick by Matsumi ; wanting to win this way was undoubtedly wishful thinking.

Soon, ten minutes had passed, and the notification of the completed mission appeared right on time.

[ B-rank mission completed : Persist for ten minutes without being defeated in the battle against the future Third Raikage. Reward : 500 points ! ]

Seeing this, Matsumi finally breathed a sigh of relief.

Within these ten minutes, he did not just keep dodging, but constantly looked for flaws and opportunities to launch attacks.

After all, he was currently representing Konoha in the battle against Kumogakure ; even if he were to lose due to being outmatched, it was better than dealing with it by constantly fleeing.

Every time they clashed, Dou was able to counterattack immediately after being struck.

After multiple exchanges, although Dou was also injured, Matsumi’s injuries were even more severe.
