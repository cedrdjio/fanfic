# Chapitre 102 — Ambush from the Uchiha!

For a Ninja, fighting in the dark is a basic operation.

Although the forest was pitch black at night, the impact on the two of them was not as great as it would be on ordinary people.

After running for about three minutes, the two had already moved away from the main road and deep into the forest.

In a clearing in the forest, Senju Matsuaki suddenly stopped.

His sharp red eyes reflected the last remnants of the sunset, while also harboring the first hint of darkness.

Noticing that the person behind him had stopped, the Konoha Chunin stopped as well, feeling somewhat puzzled.

« If I remember correctly, a few hundred meters ahead is where we encountered the beast. I don’t know if we’re already too late. »

The Chunin turned back and urged loudly, « Let’s go quickly. We don’t have time to stop and rest. Even if we can’t make it in time, we at least need to collect the remains of the victims and give their families some closure. »

However, Senju Matsuaki still did not respond, only looking at him quietly with a cold smile on his lips.

Seeing this, the Chunin slowly lowered his head. His previously pale complexion looked somewhat eerie under the shadow.

After three or four seconds of silence, he suddenly sighed softly.

« When did you start to notice ? »

The Chunin’s voice was no longer gentle, but carried a cold murderous intent, sounding even more eerie in the deep forest.

« From the beginning. »

Senju Matsuaki smiled and gave an answer that was completely unexpected to the other party.

« How is that possible ? »

« To be precise, from the moment we left the arena gate. »

Senju Matsuaki’s words made the Chunin’s heart sink.

If the « from the beginning » he just mentioned could be considered bluffing, specifying the location of the arena gate was certainly not.

In other words, their movements had been detected by Senju Matsuaki from the very start.

« Why do you look so confused ? Don’t underestimate a Senju’s sensitivity to Uchiha Chakra. »

Senju Matsuaki scratched his head, looking at the complex expression of puzzlement, confusion, and gravity on the other person’s face.

That’s right, although the other party appeared as the Konoha Chunin « Harada Takuya, » the scent of Chakra on him had allowed Senju Matsuaki to recognize his identity at first glance.

Not just him, but also the Ninja who was on duty at the arena gate earlier.

Although he had deliberately covered the clan emblem on his clothing while on duty, the unique Chakra scent belonging to the Uchiha Clan had mercilessly betrayed his identity.

« Nonsense ! How can you possibly confirm our identities just by so-called scent ! »

The Uchiha Clan Ninja did not believe Senju Matsuaki’s explanation at all.

Being able to recognize him as an Uchiha just through the scent of Chakra was simply a fantasy.

Did he really think his strength as a Jonin was just for show ?

If the Senju Clan could sense the Uchiha’s Chakra scent at a glance, then the Uchiha would have been wiped out long ago in the Sengoku era !

Seeing that the other party did not believe his explanation, Senju Matsuaki did not want to explain further.

In fact, most people indeed could not do this.

If an Uchiha Clan Ninja restrained their aura and hid it deliberately, even Senju Tobirama, who had fought the Uchiha Clan for decades, would probably be unable to distinguish between an Uchiha and an ordinary Ninja.

However, under the influence of Asura Chakra, Senju Matsuaki was indeed more sensitive to the Chakra of the Senju Clan and the Uchiha Clan.

In the Chakra aura of the Senju Clan, he could perceive vibrant vitality ; although not as strong as Senju Hashirama, it still far exceeded that of outsiders.

And in the Uchiha Clan’s Chakra, he felt a cold, repulsive yet blended aura, which was very contradictory.

« Transformation Technique, release ! »

Since his identity had been exposed, the Uchiha Ninja disguised as an ordinary Chunin simply stopped hiding.

After a puff of smoke dispersed, an adult man in Ninja combat gear with a cold expression appeared in his place.

He looked to be about thirty years old, wearing a Konoha forehead protector, and the Uchiha Clan’s fan emblem was painted on the arms and back of his combat gear.

« Let me reintroduce myself. I am a Jonin of the Uchiha Clan, and I am not like those few rookies you met in the Chunin Exams. »

« More importantly, my parents died at the hands of the Senju Clan before Konoha was established ! »

At this point, a trace of deep-seated hatred appeared in the Uchiha Jonin’s eyes, and his cold face became increasingly grim.

At this moment, another black-haired Ninja walked out from the shadows on the side ; it was the one who had been spying on Senju Matsuaki while on duty at the arena gate earlier.

The reason Senju Matsuaki was tracked down after leaving the arena was perhaps due to him tipping them off.

« Stop wasting words with him. Kill him directly, otherwise, it will arouse suspicion from others ! » the black-haired Uchiha urged.

However, that was not all.

From another direction, another figure appeared.

Compared to the other two Uchiha who were in their twenties and thirties, the newcomer was much younger, a boy of about fifteen.

The position where he appeared happened to be Senju Matsuaki’s only retreat, completely sealing off the possibility of Senju Matsuaki escaping.

« Setsuna, you arrived just in time. »

The boy blocking Senju Matsuaki’s retreat was none other than Uchiha Setsuna. Seeing him arrive, the black-haired Uchiha couldn’t help but breathe a sigh of relief.

Because Senju Matsuaki had discovered the ambush in advance, their current position deviated somewhat from the planned location.

Although they didn’t think Senju Matsuaki could escape from their two Jonin, this operation had to be foolproof !

Uchiha Setsuna’s strength was below theirs, but he was close to the Jonin level and was definitely a powerful asset.

The Uchiha Jonin who had lured Senju Matsuaki here gave a sinister smile and said, « Now, unless you also know that Flying Thunder God Technique, you won’t be able to escape even with wings ! »

« Oh, right, the person who killed my parents was named Senju Tobirama. You really look just like him ! »

« I heard you grew up in the residence of Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama. If you died, they would definitely be very sad, right ! ? »

Facing the several gazes filled with hatred and murderous intent, Senju Matsuaki was not afraid, but instead looked at the talkative Uchiha Jonin without changing his expression.

« Two Jonin, one Chunin. There aren’t many people in the Uchiha Clan who can mobilize this kind of manpower. Is the one who wants to kill me the Uchiha Clan Chief ? »

« No, that’s not right. »

Senju Matsuaki’s sharp red eyes looked at Uchiha Setsuna, who was ready to strike on the side, and he voiced his denial of his previous guess.

« I remember you. You are Uchiha Setsuna, the grandson of one of the Uchiha Clan’s Elders. That Elder doesn’t seem to be in the same faction as the Clan Chief. »

« Since you are here, it seems that the person who wants to take my life is indeed an Elder of the Uchiha Clan. »

At this point, Senju Matsuaki couldn’t help but feel a bit annoyed.
