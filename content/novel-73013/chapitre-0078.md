# Chapitre 78 — Massive Chakra! Tobirama: What Has This Kid Been Eating Lately?

« Such Water Release, he is worthy of being the guy who could defeat Chiyo and Ebizo... » Yan thought to himself.

If Senju Matsuaki knew what the other person was thinking, he would probably find it amusing.

Water Release : Water Severing Wave was Senju Tobirama’s signature Water Release Ninjutsu, and its original power was even more terrifying.

In the original timeline, during the future Fourth Ninja World War, Senju Tobirama used this technique to directly sever the roots of the God Tree.

At this moment, he could only be said to have just learned this Ninjutsu ; there was still much room for improvement in terms of power, and it was far from Senju Tobirama’s level.

« This appearance, it must be the future Third Kazekage. »

After roughly sizing up the other person’s blue hair and yellow eyes, Senju Matsuaki came to a conclusion.

The future Third Kazekage was skilled in using Magnet Release and was hailed as the « Strongest Kazekage » of Sunagakure.

« Wait, the direction he came from... »

Yan, who was about to leave, suddenly felt something was wrong. If he remembered correctly, Chiyo and Ebizo should have come from this direction.

However, the two were nowhere to be seen, and instead, Senju Matsuaki had arrived here early.

Thinking of this, he had a bad premonition.

« What did you do to Chiyo and Ebizo ! ? » Yan questioned loudly.

Beside him, dark Iron Sand surged to form long needles, poised to shoot out.

« They are probably dead. »

Hearing Yan’s question, Senju Matsuaki replied with a calm expression.

He really hadn’t paid attention to whether Chiyo and Ebizo were dead or alive.

At the time, he was in a hurry to rescue Hyuga Tsukuyomi, so he had casually unleashed a slash of super strength and continued on his way.

In his estimation, even if the two weren’t dead, they would at least be severely injured.

« You guy, you have already courted death ! ! »

Seeing this, Yan of Sunagakure was instantly enraged.

Although he usually didn’t like to interact with peers of his age and preferred to train and carry out missions alone to improve his strength.

But as Ebizo had said, Yan had a strong sense of belonging and honor toward Sunagakure.

Therefore, the casualties of his companions would make him feel angry and seek revenge for them.

« Foolish Konoha Ninja, let me show you the power of the Magnet Release I have honed from the wind and sand ! ! »

As Yan performed hand seals, a large amount of Iron Sand emerged from around his body, and the countless iron needles suspended behind him trembled violently, as if they were about to break free from their restraints and shoot out in the next instant.

The power of Magnet Release ?

Hearing this, Senju Matsuaki was not panicked at all.

The two most famous Magnet Release Ninjas in the future were Yan, the future Third Kazekage, who was standing in front of him.

However, he suddenly went missing in his prime, and was actually killed by Sasori of the Red Sand, with his body turned into a human puppet.

The other Magnet Release Ninja was Gaara’s father, the future Fourth Kazekage, Rasa.

He died under the assassination of Orochimaru, with help from Yakushi Kabuto and Kimimaro. He also died inexplicably, and his body was not discovered by Sunagakure until the Konoha Crush plan ended.

He died in his prime, causing Sunagakure to suffer another heavy blow.

Both of these Kazekages used Magnet Release as their specialty, but their performance was quite lackluster, causing the prestige of Magnet Release to plummet.

Although Magnet Release was not to be underestimated for ordinary Ninjas, it was clearly not enough for Senju Matsuaki at this moment.

Looking at the ferocious Magnet Release long needles, Senju Matsuaki crossed his hands in front of him, about to perform hand seals, and a massive amount of Chakra condensed within his body.

« Matsuaki, that Ninja from the Kumogakure delegation last time, he is lurking eight hundred meters away at the seven o’clock position ! »

Hyuga Tsukuyomi narrowed her eyes, detecting a familiar figure lurking within the range of her Byakugan.

It was Dou of Kumogakure.

They had paid attention to this guy when they were responsible for monitoring the Kumogakure delegation.

Unexpectedly, while Senju Matsuaki was confronting Yan, Dou had actually approached here silently.

Such behavior was clearly ill-intentioned.

« Is that guy trying to wait for an opportunity ? »

Hearing this, Senju Matsuaki frowned slightly. After glancing at Yan, who was already poised for action in the air ahead, the hand seals in his hands suddenly changed.

The Chakra, originally full of vitality, turned heavy, and the thick tree roots that were about to burst out of the ground fell silent.

« Earth Release : Earth-Style Wall ! ! »

After performing the hand seals, Senju Matsuaki quickly slammed his palms onto the ground.

The Chakra that suddenly erupted from his body made the two women beside him look sideways.

« Matsuaki’s Chakra seems to have increased a lot again. »

Hyuga Tsukuyomi was still keeping her Byakugan active, and she couldn’t help but feel shocked when she saw this massive amount of Chakra.

Earth-Style Wall was an defensive Earth Release Ninjutsu that Senju Matsuaki had recently learned.

Its principle was to trigger geological reorganization by injecting Chakra, causing the manipulated soil to crystallize and harden in the short term, eventually constructing a giant rock defensive wall.

On the battlefield, this technique could quickly build a tactical position or divide the battlefield.

As the amount of injected Chakra increased, the thickness of the wall would also increase.

Judging by Senju Matsuaki’s current amount of Chakra, he could easily shape a huge rock wall and change the terrain under his feet.

« This... this is too exaggerated ! ! »

Behind Senju Matsuaki, Biwako, who had regained some strength, looked at this scene, her eyes widened, and she was shocked.

This kind of Ninjutsu that could change the terrain in an instant was beyond her current vision.

« Pfft— »

In the office, Senju Tobirama, who had just picked up a water cup to take a sip, saw this, and the water in his mouth sprayed out uncontrollably.

« What has this kid been eating lately, why has his Chakra become so much ? ? »

After hurriedly wiping the table, Senju Tobirama couldn’t help but be surprised by what he saw through the Telescope Technique.

He also knew this Ninjutsu, but he didn’t usually use it.

During the Sengoku period, Ninjas of the Senju Clan would use this technique together to create a suitable position and gain a field advantage.

So Senju Tobirama could see exactly how much Chakra this Earth-Style Wall required.

If calculated based on the Chakra volume of a Senju Clan Jonin.

The scale of the Earth-Style Wall that Senju Matsuaki had just performed would probably drain the Chakra of two Senju Clan Jonin.

Don’t underestimate this number ; these were two Jonin of the Senju Clan, whose own Chakra volume was already far beyond that of ordinary Jonin.

After performing the Ninjutsu, Senju Matsuaki stood up without blushing or panting, and looked at Hyuga Tsukuyomi and Biwako behind him.

« Let’s go, let’s leave here first. »

If it were just Yan, Senju Matsuaki would be confident in taking him down at this moment.

Even if he was the future Third Kazekage, known as the strongest Kazekage.

However, the future Third Raikage was still lurking in the distance, eyeing them like a tiger.

If they were to fall into a three-way melee later, although Senju Matsuaki was not afraid, he was afraid that he would not have enough energy to protect his teammates.

A duel between Jonin, especially between two Jonin with Bloodline Limits, could be said to be a disaster for Chunin and Genin.

The opponent was a barefoot person who was not afraid of people wearing shoes, and could use Magnet Release and Lightning Release without any scruples, so he would undoubtedly be at a disadvantage.

Moreover, this was only the second stage of the assessment, and there was no need to collide with a strong enemy with all his might.
