# Chapitre 80 — Is my great-nephew feeling repressed?

« Including the jade chess piece in my hand, we have a total of eight. That means nearly one-tenth of all the jade chess pieces are with us. »

Hyuga Tsukuyomi thought about this surprising fact.

There were only ninety jade chess pieces in total for the second round of the exam, yet they were holding eight of them right now.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi looked at Senju Matsumi’s bulging Ninja tool pouch, wanting to say something but hesitating, and finally handed over the jade chess piece in her own hand.

« Since you like collecting these, just keep mine with yours too. »

« Uh, I’m not... »

« I heard other girls in the Ninja Academy say that boys more or less have a hobby of collecting something. »

Hyuga Tsukuyomi wore an understanding expression, smiled, and nodded at Senju Matsumi, signaling him to accept it.

At the same time, she urged him by extending her hand holding the chess piece a little further forward.

« Alright... »

Seeing this, Senju Matsumi had no choice but to take the jade chess piece.

[Current Progress : Captured 8 jade chess pieces, defeated 11 examinees.]

The current progress updated the moment he took the jade chess piece.

Senju Matsumi : So good~

However, looking at her eyes which fully understood his strange little hobby, Senju Matsumi felt that what he wanted to say next was hard to bring up.

« By the way, Hyuga Tsukuyomi, for the next few days, could you help me take these jade chess pieces from other examinees without affecting the exam ? »

« Eh ? »

Upon hearing this, Hyuga Tsukuyomi was slightly stunned, but quickly smiled, raised her hand, clenched her fist in front of her chest, and said : « Mm ! Leave it to me ! »

« She agreed so readily. »

This time it was Senju Matsumi’s turn to be surprised.

According to their goals, they had more than enough jade chess pieces now, and what they should do next was head straight for the finish line.

In fact, to achieve their goal, they should even actively avoid potential opponents they encountered along the way.

« After all, I’ve benefited a lot from your kindness, Senju Matsumi, so I’m happy to help with a small favor~ » Hyuga Tsukuyomi said with a smile.

Not to mention anything else, just the Breathing Technique that Senju Matsumi taught her earlier had benefited her a lot.

Although her physique was not as strong as the Senju Clan, and she didn’t have any cheats, her progress in practicing the Breathing Technique wasn’t as rapid as Senju Matsumi’s.

But after a few months, her physique had also been significantly enhanced.

As if thinking of something, she took out a bundle of small scrolls from behind her back and handed them to Senju Matsumi.

« Carrying too many jade chess pieces in your Ninja tool pouch will affect your ability to grab your tools, so it’s better to store them in this scroll~ »

...

Inside the office.

« You’ve worked hard, take a break. »

A Konoha Ninja walked in from outside, closed the door, walked behind a colleague, and gently patted his shoulder.

« Finally, it’s shift change. I’ll leave this to you. »

The colleague raised his left hand to rub his somewhat dry eyes, while his right hand reached for the water cup on the table.

After picking up the water cup, he stood up and left his seat.

The Ninja who came to take the shift sat in the empty seat, took over the duties of the person just now, and looked at the crystal ball displaying the scene in front of him.

When he caught a glimpse of the crystal ball in front of his colleague nearby, he was slightly stunned.

The two figures traveling together on the screen were exactly the same as those reflected in the crystal ball in front of him.

« Ah, you’re watching his feed too ? Then I’ll switch to another one. »

He recognized both people in the scene.

Senju Matsumi and Hyuga Tsukuyomi were both top-tier talents among Konoha Genin and were also their main subjects of observation.

The reason they formed a temporary office here was largely to protect these futures of Konoha.

However, observation is observation, and one-on-one is enough.

Having multiple Chunin or Jonin monitor one examinee together was a complete waste of manpower.

The Ninja formed hand seals, and his Chakra connected with the crystal ball in front of him.

The image on the crystal ball rippled like a water surface, gradually blurring until it disappeared completely.

What appeared immediately after was the same image as before.

Seeing this, the Ninja was slightly stunned and formed hand seals again.

The crystal ball changed as it did before, with ripples distorting the image.

What appeared was still the same image as before.

« Uh, is this broken ? »

The Ninja stood up, wanting to find the technician in the office who maintained the crystal ball.

He himself only knew how to perform the Telescope Technique at best ; what was wrong with the specially made crystal ball and how to repair it was another skill entirely.

« Don’t bother looking, it’s not a problem with the crystal ball. »

The colleague in the seat next to him looked at him nonplussed, and calmly pulled him back to his seat.

« Tobirama-sama’s good nephew currently has a total of twenty jade chess pieces, and since some of them are numbered sequentially, you’ll see different scenes if you switch a few more times. »

« Huh ! ? »

« Don’t be so surprised. Just like how I have a similar hobby—collecting shuriken from every Ninja I’ve killed—you’ll understand when you get to my age~ »

Suddenly, the office door was opened again.

Senju Tobirama walked in from outside without changing his expression and sat down in the center seat.

Seeing him arrive, the Ninjas present focused even more on their work, and the sound of the previous conversation vanished instantly.

A moment later, Senju Tobirama stood up, scanning his surroundings as if looking for something.

« Technician, this crystal ball seems to be broken ! »

« ... »

Upon hearing this, the surrounding Ninjas all cast strange looks, which made Senju Tobirama feel puzzled.

« Tobirama-sama, the situation is like this... »

After a Ninja beside him explained the ins and outs of the matter, Senju Tobirama’s expression also became a bit strange.

« Ahem, since that’s the case, we don’t need so many people here. You five go take care of other work. »

Senju Tobirama pointed, and although the five Ninjas were unwilling in their hearts after receiving the order, they quickly packed up their things.

Who would want to do field work if they could sit in the office ?

What is this kid doing, grabbing so many jade chess pieces...

Senju Tobirama sat back in his seat, looking at the image in front of him, feeling a bit of a headache.

The jade chess pieces are equivalent to signal receivers, allowing him to remotely monitor the situation nearby through the Telescope Technique.

But if the jade chess pieces are concentrated together, the effect will not be as expected.

Fortunately, for now, the jade chess pieces Senju Matsumi snatched all came from examinees from other villages and did not affect the protection of the Konoha examinees.

Could it be that my great-nephew has been too repressed lately, and is now releasing it ?

Thinking of this, Senju Tobirama felt a bit guilty.

Out of a protective instinct, he and his older brother Hashirama were quite conservative when arranging missions for Senju Matsumi and Hyuga Tsukuyomi.

The increase in mission difficulty seemed to be unable to keep up with the improvement in the two’s strength.

Now it seems that this protection is not only detrimental to the improvement of their strength, but also seems detrimental to the maintenance of their mental health...

Senju Tobirama thought to himself : « It seems I need to increase the difficulty of the missions later. »
