# Chapitre 64 — Rules of the Second Stage Examination

Early the next morning.

Within a training ground in Konoha, a dense crowd of people stood in formation.

If one were to replace the forehead protectors of these diverse-looking Genin with the « Ninja » forehead protectors, they might think the Fourth Ninja World War had broken out early.

At the very front of the formation, Senju Tobirama stood with his arms crossed, his expression impassive.

Today, Senju Tobirama was wearing his classic skin : blue layered armor, with a Konoha forehead protector wrapped around his cheeks.

« I am Senju Tobirama, the Chief Proctor for the second stage of this Joint Chunin Exams. »

Looking at the Genin before him who were still chatting, Senju Tobirama said coldly.

A famous name casts a long shadow.

After his voice fell, the previously noisy crowd instantly fell silent.

Awe, fear, hatred, confusion—various gazes intertwined and landed on Senju Tobirama’s face, yet his expression remained unchanged.

Having been in the Ninja World for so many years, his mental fortitude had long since been tempered to an extremely high level.

He also knew the source of these complex gazes.

Except for those from Konoha, he had encountered Ninjas from other Villages to some extent while carrying out missions.

When encountering situations where mission objectives conflicted, he would not hesitate to kill Ninjas from other Villages.

It could be said that, apart from the Konoha Genin present, some of the Genin from other Villages might have had elders who were souls under his blade.

No, that’s a hasty statement...

Senju Tobirama suddenly remembered that it seemed some of the elders of the Uchiha Clan Genin present had also been taken away by his Flying Thunder God Slash.

« First, those whose names I call are the ones who failed the written exam in the first stage ; step out of line and leave here, » Senju Tobirama said.

Upon hearing this, some of the Genin present were shocked.

They were not very confident about the first stage written exam, and originally thought that being gathered here was proof of having passed the first stage.

But they hadn’t expected it was just to gather everyone together to facilitate delivering the news.

Senju Tobirama held a list in his hand, and under the fearful gazes of some of the examinees, he quickly read out about twenty names.

Those whose names were called all looked devastated, their faces gray as they consciously left the area.

The remaining people rearranged their formation, waiting for Senju Tobirama’s next announcement.

« Next is the announcement regarding the second stage examination. »

Senju Tobirama put away the list in his hand and began to get to today’s main topic.

« The second stage examination will be conducted in the form of a survival exercise ; the examination venue is a virgin forest selected by us, the organizers. »

« Approximately thirty people were eliminated in the first stage for various reasons, leaving exactly two hundred and seventy people to participate in the second stage examination. »

« I believe you have already heard from the Proctor of the previous stage that you should treat this Chunin Exams as a formal mission. »

Speaking of this, Senju Tobirama took out a scroll from his bosom.

He opened the scroll, and after a puff of smoke rose, two Shogi pieces, with pointed tips and bell-shaped bodies, appeared in his hand.

After holding the two Shogi pieces in his hand to show the crowd in front, Senju Tobirama continued.

« These two types of Shogi pieces in my hand are the mission objectives you must protect ; one is engraved with the character 'Jade', and the other is blank. »

« Your mission in the second stage examination is to carry the piece with the 'Jade' character through the virgin forest and reach the finish line within a time limit of six days. »

« Based on the number of examinees participating in this stage, we will randomly distribute ninety 'Jade' pieces and one hundred and eighty blank pieces. »

« Except for the pieces and a map of the forest, we will not provide any other supplies. »

The implication was that at most ninety people would be able to pass the second stage successfully.

Because some powerful examinees could easily manage to possess multiple 'Jade' pieces and head to the finish line alone.

However, most of the examinees present had not yet realized this cruel fact.

Those who realized this already had worried expressions, while their eyes searched for companions to group up with for warmth.

In the crowd, Senju Matsumi was not surprised by this rule.

A one-third elimination rate ; he had already heard the Nara Clan Proctor mention it in the examination hall during the first stage yesterday.

For a Ninja, this elimination rate was not astonishing.

Those who could become Chunin were naturally the cream of the crop among Genin, and some were stuck in the bottleneck of promotion from Genin to Chunin their entire lives.

In the end, they either died accidentally during missions, remained Genin for their whole lives, or accepted the mediocrity of their Ninja talent and turned to other professions.

Although he was not surprised by the rules, he was surprised by something else.

Standing in a crowd of nearly three hundred people, it was not easy to notice a specific person unless they were prominent enough.

Thus, Senju Matsumi saw a Kumogakure Ninja to his front left who was significantly taller and more robust than the other Genin around him, standing out like a crane in a flock of chickens.

After seeing this person’s appearance clearly, Matsumi couldn’t help but have a strange expression.

After all, this was a formidable enemy he had faced directly before ; the other party’s identity and appearance were quite recognizable, so it was impossible not to recognize him.

He just didn’t expect that the future Third Raikage of the Hidden Cloud Village, the youth currently named « Dou », was actually participating in this Joint Chunin Exams as a Genin.

According to the intelligence he had gathered earlier, the other party was clearly already a Jonin of Kumogakure.

Therefore, even though he had met him on the streets of Konoha a few days ago, he had only assumed he was the team leader for Kumogakure, bringing Genin to participate in the examination.

He never thought he would see him in a group of Genin today.

Regarding this, although Senju Matsumi felt surprised, he did not feel any worry or fear.

Based on the original situation, there might be very few opponents in this Chunin Exams who would shine for him.

Dou, this former Jonin, « condescending » to participate in the Chunin Exams—such behavior, while shameless, made this Chunin Exams less monotonous.

Moreover, a few months ago, Dou was indeed a formidable enemy to him, but he was no longer the same person he used to be ; the outcome was still unknown.

« Finally, out of humanitarian concern, there is one more thing I need to remind you of. »

Senju Tobirama picked up the piece in his hand, applied force with his right-hand fingers, and crushed the piece directly.

The moment the piece shattered, a wave of vibrant Chakra dispersed and vanished into the air.

« If you encounter life-threatening danger, you can choose to destroy the piece in your hand ; we Proctors will immediately obtain the imagery of your location and remove you from the examination grounds. »

« Although this means elimination, it is better than losing your life in the exam. »

« There is one more thing... »

Speaking of this, Senju Tobirama’s expression was unnatural for a fleeting moment.

« These wooden pieces are specially crafted by our Senju Clan ; wearing them for a long time has the effect of calming the mind, enhancing intelligence, and delaying aging. In the near future, there will also be specialty stores in Konoha selling other wooden products with the same effects ; if you have free time, you may go and purchase them. »

After mechanically reading the above advertising copy, Senju Tobirama’s expression was slightly stiff.
