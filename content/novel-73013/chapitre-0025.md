# Chapitre 25 — A Ridiculous Hiding Spot

Whether it was the Phoenix Sage Fire Technique or the Explosive Tags, both were indiscriminate attacks.

Before they could hit Senju Matsuaki and Hyuga Tsukuyomi, the flames had already swallowed several unfortunate Ninja.

After deciding to use his subordinates as shields to cover his own escape, the Rogue Ninja Leader gave no thought to their lives.

« Shameless guy. »

Senju Matsuaki frowned, feeling contempt in his heart.

However, since these Rogue Ninja were all deranged individuals, he obviously wouldn’t care about their lives either.

« Earth Release : Earth Shore Return ! »

Senju Matsuaki formed hand seals, and a stone slab rose from the ground.

The power of the Phoenix Sage Fire Technique wasn’t strong, especially since the Rogue Ninja Leader had been in a hurry to escape and hadn’t aimed the Ninjutsu carefully.

What would actually pose a threat to the two of them were the Explosive Tags tied to the Kunai.

Explosive Tags were very expensive ; they had only seen them in the Ninja Academy and had never used them before.

At the same time, the power of an Explosive Tag was immense, making it a high-risk item for students who hadn’t yet become Genin.

After an Explosive Tag was detonated, it would produce a violent explosion after a short delay, which could be used to set traps.

It could also be used in conjunction with a Kunai, just like the Rogue Ninja had done just now.

When facing an Explosive Tag, one either had to escape the blast radius in time or use Ninjutsu to reduce the power of the explosion.

If one were to take the explosion head-on, they would likely be blown to pieces.

Consequently, after using the Ninjutsu, Senju Matsuaki scooped Hyuga Tsukuyomi up by her waist and retreated rapidly.

Only after putting some distance between them did he set Hyuga Tsukuyomi down.

The girl’s body was light, and it didn’t take much effort.

« Thank you. » Tsukuyomi’s small face turned slightly red, and she whispered her thanks.

Her speed wasn’t fast ; if she had retreated only after reacting, she wouldn’t have been severely injured, but she would still have been caught in the blast.

Boom—

In the distance, the roar from the exploding Explosive Tags jolted her out of her daze.

« Let’s continue the pursuit. Watch your step ; we can’t rule out the possibility that he set traps. » Senju Matsuaki said in a low voice.

Under the power of the Explosive Tags, those Rogue Ninja lost their lives without a shadow of a doubt.

Those near the center of the explosion were scattered everywhere.

Those further from the center of the explosion were also severely injured and on the verge of death.

Even if they didn’t finish them off, these people’s lives would come to an end before long.

After glancing at these people, Senju Matsuaki and Hyuga Tsukuyomi pursued in the direction the Rogue Ninja Leader had fled.

He was the one who posed the greatest threat.

If he were allowed to escape, he would likely recruit more subordinates in a few months and continue to commit evil deeds.

« He hasn’t escaped the range of the Byakugan yet ; he’s in that direction. »

Hyuga Tsukuyomi kept the Byakugan activated, and veins bulged near her temples.

After locating the fleeing Rogue Ninja, the two continued to sprint in his direction.

...

A few minutes later, the two stood by a lake with strange expressions.

Worried about alerting the enemy, the two continued to communicate only with simple hand gestures.

Looking in the direction Hyuga Tsukuyomi was pointing, Senju Matsuaki almost couldn’t help but laugh out loud.

Looking closely, one could indeed see a thin tube sticking out of the water on the surface of the lake.

This method of concealment perhaps aligned more with people’s perception of Ninja in his previous life.

However, objectively speaking, hiding this way was indeed effective.

If one were just passing by, one probably wouldn’t notice that someone was hiding in the lake.

However, this Rogue Ninja had calculated everything except for the existence of a Bloodline Limit like the Byakugan.

He clearly hadn’t received systematic Ninja training, nor had anyone explained to him what Bloodline Limits existed in the current Ninja World.

Perhaps when he saw her earlier, he might have been curious about why that little girl’s eyes were white.

But he absolutely couldn’t have guessed that the Byakugan possessed such terrifying insight.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi formed hand seals and then quickly pressed her palms onto the surface of the lake.

« Lightning Release : Ground Flash ! »

A beam of lightning shot out from her hands and spread along the surface of the lake.

This Ninjutsu could only release a single beam of lightning, but when combined with Water Release Ninjutsu or natural water currents, it could possess strong control capabilities, paralyzing the enemy’s body.

Seeing this, a smile appeared on the corner of Senju Matsuaki’s mouth.

It seemed that it wasn’t just the Byakugan and Gentle Fist ; Hyuga Tsukuyomi also had great talent for Ninjutsu.

The Hyuga Clan had always been famous for the Byakugan and Gentle Fist, but that didn’t mean they lacked talent for Ninjutsu.

The lightning spread through the lake water and soon reached the spot where the Rogue Ninja was hiding.

Ground Flash was not a Ninjutsu with high lethality ; its effect was more about paralyzing the enemy’s body with lightning, serving as a form of control.

Suddenly, ripples appeared on the previously calm lake surface, and violent splashes of water sprayed into the air.

A somewhat disheveled figure, soaked from head to toe, climbed out from beneath the water surface, with Chakra concentrated in his palms and soles.

This Rogue Ninja had the strength of a Chunin ; mastering such basic Chakra usage techniques wasn’t surprising.

Suddenly driven out of the water by the Lightning Release Ninjutsu, the Rogue Ninja inevitably choked on a few mouthfuls of water.

Looking at the two standing by the lake, he said with a gloomy expression : « Cough, cough... Can’t you just spare me once ? ! »

« Although I can’t compare to you Ninja from big hidden Villages, it won’t be that easy for you to take me down ! »

« Besides, the Village that hired you is gone now. If you want to get your commission, I can give it to you ! »

The Rogue Ninja carefully studied the expressions of the two.

But he discovered that the silver-haired, red-eyed little boy had a calm expression, and the little girl with a pair of white eyes was similarly frowning slightly.

« I’ll leave the Land of Fire immediately afterward and never come back for the rest of my life. You can just consider the mission complete, take this money back, and no one will know you let me go. » The Rogue Ninja continued.

« Sure. » Senju Matsuaki nodded indifferently.

At the same time, he concentrated Chakra under his feet, stepped steadily onto the lake surface, and walked toward him step by step.

Hearing this, a look of joy appeared on the Rogue Ninja’s face, and he said : « I’ll give you the money right now. Although it got soaked in the water just now, it can still be used after drying it out. »

After saying that, he took the bag he had been carrying on his back into his hands and also walked toward Matsuaki step by step.

Matsuaki’s voice was without waves as he said : « Count out the commission and hand it to me ; you don’t need to bring the whole bag. »

Hearing this, the Rogue Ninja smiled and held the bag in his hands forward.

« No, no, no, it’s better if I give you the whole bag, because that way you can be faster... »

« Go to hell ! »

The Rogue Ninja suddenly exerted force with both hands, throwing the bag toward Matsuaki, while his right hand quickly drew his Ninja sword. At the same time, he stomped heavily on the water surface and swung the Ninja sword forward with force.

However, faster than him was the short sword in Matsuaki’s hand, which had been drawn a step earlier.

« Super Strength... Slash ! »
