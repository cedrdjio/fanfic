# Chapitre 40 — Talks Begin!

« It seems they have already realized that what happened yesterday afternoon was no coincidence. »

Due to the seating arrangement, Matsumi was directly opposite Dou at the other end of the conference table, so he also caught the flash of anger that had just passed through the other man’s eyes.

« To our distinguished guests from Kumogakure, I wonder if you are getting used to staying in Konoha today ? If there is anything lacking, I hope you will forgive us. »

« Our commercial district in Konoha sells local specialties and delicacies from the Land of Fire. If you have time, you might want to try them. »

Facing the foreign guests at the conference table, Senju Hashirama began with a customary opening statement, trying to bridge the distance between the two sides.

Upon hearing this, the Kumogakure Elder was about to reply, but suddenly found his mind went blank.

They had only arrived in Konoha for a day, and most of their time had been spent planning things that could be brought to light and things that could not, so how could they have had time to experience the local customs of Konoha ?

Therefore, he had no choice but to reveal an awkward yet polite smile.

« Uh, Konoha is indeed very good. Hearing of it is not as good as seeing it, hahahaha... »

The Cloud Village Elder suddenly had a flash of inspiration, remembering that he seemed to have heard Dou say yesterday that life in Konoha was not easy, and that children of only eight or nine years old had to come out to set up stalls selling Oden, so he quickly said.

« Oh, that’s right, the Oden in Konoha is truly unique ! »

« Uh, is that so ? »

Senju Hashirama scratched his head in some confusion.

Oden ? Can this even be considered a specialty of our Konoha ?

The Kumogakure Elder suddenly straightened his expression and took a scroll from his bosom, handing it to Dou behind him.

After Dou received it with both hands, he quickly walked around to Senju Hashirama’s side and presented the scroll with both hands as well.

Even when passing by Matsumi, he did not cast a sideways glance, as if he had never seen him before, making one wonder if the anger seen just now was just an illusion.

« Hokage-sama, then let us get to today’s main topic. » The Kumogakure Elder said.

Upon hearing this, Senju Hashirama nodded and opened the scroll in his hand.

The scroll recorded a rough framework of Kumogakure’s preliminary concept for the Joint Chunin Exams.

The reason it was only a framework without specific details was because there were still many matters pending.

First, the list of Ninja villages willing to participate in this Joint Chunin Exams was not yet decided ; Kumogakure had sent more than one delegation, with other delegations going to other countries' Ninja villages for talks.

Secondly, the Chunin Exams models of various Ninja villages were not the same, and what model to choose for the joint assessment was also a problem.

Finally, there was the selection of the assessment venue, which was a prerequisite for the preparation of the entire assessment.

« For this Joint Chunin Exams, considering that some small countries are still in civil strife, we are only sending invitations to the Ninja villages of the Five Great Nations. » The Kumogakure Elder said.

« The four great Ninja villages, including your village, have all responded positively, and perhaps at this moment, just like us, they are discussing related matters. »

« I have roughly finished reading the proposal for this Joint Chunin Exams, and I have no objections... »

Senju Hashirama laid the scroll in his hand flat on the table and put it down, and just as he was about to state his demands, the other party spoke first.

« Hokage-sama, we in Kumogakure have another presumptuous request. I wonder if I should speak it or not ? »

The Kumogakure Elder looked troubled, first sighed heavily, and then looked at Hashirama across the conference table with expectant eyes.

« Elder, just say it. If there are difficulties, we in Konoha will also do our best to provide help. » Hashirama said seriously.

Having been Hokage for so many years, and also having been a clan leader for so many years before Konoha was established, he was certainly not truly naive.

Saying he would do his best to help here was just polite talk ; if the other party really posed some difficult problems, then whether to help and how much to help could be negotiated.

« It is like this : regarding the venue for this Joint Chunin Exams, our Kumogakure has the intention to take on the burden, but unfortunately, the geographical environment of the Land of Lightning is special, with steep and rugged mountain rocks scattered throughout the territory, and thunderstorms occurring from time to time. »

« It is not easy for us to find a suitable venue, and even if we could find one, I am afraid this special environment and climate would be unfair to Ninjas from other countries. »

« I see that the climate in the Land of Fire is suitable, and the distance to various countries is also roughly close, so why not... »

The Cloud Village Elder’s request could not be clearer ; it was nothing more than placing the venue for the Joint Chunin Exams in the Land of Fire.

Upon hearing this, Senju Hashirama’s eyes lit up, and he immediately wanted to nod in agreement.

« We also happen to... »

However, just as he was about to agree, Senju Tobirama behind him spoke first, while showing a troubled expression.

« Elder, we in Konoha do not yet have the experience of organizing such large-scale events, and recently Konoha is in a period of capital shortage and lack of manpower, this... »

« Sigh, that’s right... » Senju Hashirama, after his mind turned the corner, also timely echoed.

« We in Konoha have been renovating old building facilities during this period, and it is a huge expense every day. Unless... »

Couldn’t he find a better excuse...

The Cloud Village Elder secretly rolled his eyes.

Now that the major Ninja villages have just been established for less than ten years, where would you get old buildings to renovate ?

« Unless what ? Hokage-sama, if you have any difficulties, just say them, and we in Kumogakure will do our best to provide help. » The Cloud Village Elder also said seriously.

« This scroll says that the funds for hosting the Chunin Exams are jointly prepared by each Ninja village. »

Senju Hashirama picked up the scroll laid flat on the conference table and shook it in front of the Cloud Village Elder.

« First point, if Konoha is responsible for providing the venue and organizing the Joint Chunin Exams, I hope that the portion of funds Konoha is responsible for will be waived. »

« Indeed, we cannot let your village provide the land and personnel and still have to pay money ; waiving this part of the funds is also appropriate. » The Cloud Village Elder thought about it and felt it was reasonable, so he nodded and agreed.

Hashirama continued : « Second point, since Konoha provides the venue and organizes the exams, then I hope that apart from the various matters of the Chunin assessment which will be discussed by each village, other matters will be decided independently by Konoha. »

« After all, organizing a large-scale Chunin Exams takes a lot of time ; if everyone comes to point fingers, I guess we can only wait until the year after next to start. » Tobirama added indifferently.

These contents were all things they had discussed and prepared in advance before the talks began, so naturally, they were extremely fluent when speaking now.

« Uh, as for this... » The Cloud Village Elder was a little hesitant, « It should not be a big problem, but I need to discuss it with Raikage-sama. »

Although he felt that what the other party said was reasonable, after all, there were too many things involved, and as an elder, he did not have such great decision-making power.

« Last point, organizing the Chunin Exams requires a lot of manpower and material resources, and even if we waive the preparation funds, I am afraid that we will still lose a large sum of money in the end. »

« Therefore, all the proceeds from this Chunin Exams will belong to us, the organizer, Konoha. »

« Your village is shouldering such a great responsibility, so it is appropriate to recover some funds ; I can make the decision on this. »

The Cloud Village Elder clearly did not take this money seriously in his heart.

He had only seen spending a lot of money to host Chunin Exams, never seen making money from Chunin Exams.

Which time had their Kumogakure not spent a large sum of money to organize the Chunin Exams within the village.

As for the « proceeds » during the Chunin Exams, at most it would just be selling a few boxes of water.

Seeing that this major matter was settled, both sides were quite satisfied, and while talking and laughing, both felt that the other side was the sucker, and for a time, both host and guest were happy.
