# Chapitre 88 — The Visitor

Inside the conference hall.

« Everyone, the second stage of the exams is nearing its conclusion. As the host of these exams, we in Konoha have a few matters to notify you of. »

Senju Hashirama stood at the center table in the conference hall with a solemn expression, his hands pressed against the table, exuding an imposing Aura.

Out of respect for the strong, the representatives from the various villages present all turned their gaze toward him.

In fact, as the progress of the exams advanced, the images visible through the crystal ball in their conference hall had become fewer and fewer, and now, it was almost completely black.

The only reason they still came here on time every day was out of respect for Konoha.

After Senju Hashirama finished speaking, Senju Tobirama stepped forward and scanned the room with a stern expression.

« As the Chief Proctor for the second stage, I will now provide a brief summary of the situation regarding the second stage of the exams. »

« First, as of now, the second stage of the exams has been underway for five and a half days, with half a day remaining. Currently, fifty-two candidates have successfully passed the exam. »

« Fifty-two ? How could it be so few ? » Hōzuki Gengetsu couldn’t help but interrupt, asking curiously.

The time limit for the second stage was six days, but that was already a fairly generous amount of time.

Generally speaking, candidates capable of passing the exam usually arrived by around the fifth day.

Even if a few more people passed the exam in the end, the total number was estimated not to exceed sixty, which was far lower than the expectations of the village representatives present.

« Some candidates possessed more than one jade chess piece when they passed the exam, and at the same time, some jade chess pieces were damaged during the process. Therefore, the final exam results are far lower than the ideal figure of sixty people. »

If it were any other time, Senju Tobirama probably wouldn’t have bothered responding to Hōzuki Gengetsu so patiently.

However, as he was representing Konoha at this moment, he gave the explanation quite patiently.

Seeing that no one else had any further questions, he continued with his summary.

« Secondly, as of now in this exam, sixteen candidates have died, and thirty-eight candidates have been seriously injured. The injured are currently receiving treatment at Konoha Hospital. »

« As for the circumstances of the deaths, Konoha will do its best to investigate and will deliver reports to all of you afterward. »

Upon hearing this, the expressions of those present became complicated, with emotions such as grief, resentment, and dissatisfaction appearing one after another.

« This time, there have been casualties from all of the Five Great Ninja Villages. We all share the same grief, and we are also well aware that the Chunin Exams inherently carry a certain degree of risk. We hope for mutual understanding. »

« Konoha firmly opposes and condemns the cruel acts of deliberately murdering candidates from other villages. »

Glancing at the sour expressions of representatives from various villages like Hōzuki Gengetsu, Mū, Hō, and Shamon, Senju Tobirama continued.

What Senju Tobirama said was completely true.

Even though he had set up a dedicated office for round-the-clock monitoring, these preventive measures still had certain loopholes.

First, the scope of long-term monitoring was locked onto the shogi pieces inscribed with the character 'jade' (jade chess piece), while blank shogi pieces were not within the scope of monitoring.

After all, resources were limited, and it was impossible to mobilize such vast resources for surveillance just for the Chunin Exams.

Secondly, there was the lag in rescue efforts.

Accidents sometimes happen in just a split second, and by the time rescue personnel arrive, it might already be too late.

Therefore, in this second stage of the exams, although the number was significantly lower than in other Ninja villages, there were still deaths among Konoha’s candidates.

Hearing Senju Tobirama say this, the representatives of these Ninja villages felt more balanced, and their expressions actually improved.

It didn’t matter if everyone suffered together ; what they feared was being the only ones to suffer.

« The second stage of the exams has come to an end, so next, we will discuss matters related to the third stage of the exams, which is the final stage. »

After Senju Tobirama finished his summary of the second stage, Senju Hashirama transitioned the topic to the third stage of the exams.

The representatives of the various Ninja villages, who had long been paying close attention to this, silently pricked up their ears.

Regarding the third stage of the exams, the various Ninja villages had negotiated the basic rules, but the specific implementation was entirely handled by Konoha.

They were also curious about Konoha’s specific plans.

« Now, the Chief Proctor for the third stage of the exams will be responsible for the explanation. »

Senju Hashirama extended his right index finger, waving it back and forth in the air, indecisive.

The gazes of everyone present followed the direction of his finger as it moved back and forth.

Finally, Senju Hashirama’s finger pointed at the stiff-faced Senju Tobirama.

Damn it, that idiot big brother...

Realizing he had instantly become the center of attention, and facing all sorts of strange gazes, a vein popped on Senju Tobirama’s forehead.

For the sake of Konoha, he bit the bullet and forced out a stiff smile.

« I am the Chief Proctor responsible for the third stage of the exams, Senju Tobirama. Next, I will briefly introduce the specific rules and selection methods for the third stage. »

That’s right, not only for the second stage, but even for the third stage, the Chief Proctor was Senju Tobirama.

This was also a matter of necessity.

The participants of the Joint Chunin Exams included the Five Great Ninja Villages, so the status of the Chief Proctor for each stage naturally needed to carry a certain amount of weight.

In Konoha, aside from Senju Hashirama, Senju Tobirama was the most powerful and had the highest prestige.

As Hokage, it was naturally inconvenient for Senju Hashirama to preside over the work during each stage of the exams, so this heavy responsibility could only fall upon Senju Tobirama.

« Regarding the third stage of the exams... »

...

Passing the second stage of the exams was already a thing of the past.

Early the next morning, the door to Senju Matsuaki’s house was knocked upon.

Knock, knock-knock—

Inside the courtyard, Senju Matsuaki sheathed his short blade and looked curiously toward the main gate of his residence.

During these free days between the second stage and the third stage of the exams, he had no plans to accept missions, nor did he intend to go out and participate in any social activities.

As for Hyuga Tsukuyomi, her schedule was even tighter.

When they were eating barbecue together last night, he heard Hyuga Tsukuyomi say that her brother, Clan Chief Hyuga Teruake, was going to subject her to intensive special training over the next few days.

After all, the third stage of the exams would invite nobles, dignitaries, and wealthy individuals from various countries to watch, and Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s performance would, to a certain extent, affect the reputation of the Hyuga Clan.

Therefore, even the Elders of the main branch, who had always opposed the allocation of clan resources to the branch family, did not oppose this decision by Hyuga Teruake.

So, at this moment, he wondered who else would be coming to knock on his door looking for him.

Senju Matsuaki walked to the main gate and unlocked it cleanly and efficiently ; he pulled the heavy door open a crack with one hand.

A sneaky figure poked their head out from the other side of the door.

« Uh, Brother Hiruzen ? »

Upon seeing who it was, a few black lines appeared on Senju Matsuaki’s forehead.

The person who had come was none other than Hiruzen Sarutobi.

At this moment, he was dressed in a way that had nothing to do with being a Ninja, holding a few small bags in his right hand, and the scent of oden and medicinal herbs mixed on his body, which was quite pungent.

As this strange smell drifted into his nose, Senju Matsuaki couldn’t help but take two steps back, and Hiruzen Sarutobi took the opportunity to squeeze through the crack in the door into the courtyard.
