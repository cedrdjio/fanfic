# Chapitre 138 — A-Rank Mission! Check the Seal!

After listening carefully to Senju Matsuaki’s words, Senju Hashirama wore a thoughtful expression.

Senju Matsuaki and Senju Tobirama remained silent, waiting for Senju Hashirama to recall the relevant information.

After a long while, Senju Hashirama finally emerged from his deep thought and turned his head to look at Senju Tobirama.

With a sincere expression, he asked inquisitively, « Tobirama, do you have any impression of what Matsuaki mentioned ? »

« Big Brother, you are the Clan Chief ! »

Senju Tobirama’s mouth twitched, and an angry vein seemed to bulge on his forehead.

« According to Senju custom, this scroll should have been entrusted to you by Father at the time, right ? »

« As the Clan Chief, hurry up and responsibly recall it ! »

« But… » Senju Hashirama scratched his head in some distress.

« Did Grandfather Butsuma never mention this matter ? » Senju Matsuaki asked curiously.

Senju Butsuma was the father of the two brothers, Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama, and was also the previous Clan Chief of the Senju Clan.

And Senju Matsuaki’s grandfather in this life was the biological brother of Senju Butsuma.

Unfortunately, due to the extremely high mortality rate of Ninjas during the Sengoku period, they both died prematurely.

Speaking of this, Senju Hashirama’s expression became somewhat heavy, and he sighed, « At that time, Father passed away from severe injuries, and I hastily succeeded to the position of Clan Chief ; I hadn’t even processed these things before I had to return to the battlefield again. »

« Later, when the war subsided, I was busy building Konoha with Madara, which turned out to be even busier than during wartime. »

« Most of the things Father handed over to me were classics kept by previous Clan Chiefs, so after I stored them away, I didn’t pay them any more mind. »

« Big Brother, you… »

Listening to Senju Hashirama’s helpless words, Senju Tobirama didn’t know what to say.

After all, this situation really couldn’t be blamed on Hashirama.

Senju Tobirama knew best just how busy his Big Brother was before and after the founding of Konoha.

Seeing this, Senju Matsuaki couldn’t help but feel a bit helpless.

He had thought he could get some information from his uncle and second uncle, but he didn’t expect them to have absolutely no understanding of this.

However, this situation didn’t seem too hard to predict.

Although the scroll suggested that the descendants of the Senju Clan should go and check the Seal every six months.

But during the Sengoku period, there were constant conflicts large and small ; how could they have the leisure to check the Seal on time every time ?

Not to mention that Senju Hashirama didn’t know about this, I’m afraid even Senju Butsuma’s generation had already forgotten the existence of that Seal.

Hiss…

Senju Matsuaki gasped.

Doesn’t this mean that the Seal, which needs to be checked every six months, hasn’t been looked at for decades ?

« Since this matter is the entrustment of our predecessors, we have no reason to ignore it, » Senju Tobirama said thoughtfully.

« Since that’s the case, as the Clan Chief, I can only go there myself. »

Senju Hashirama said righteously, supporting himself on the desk with both hands as he slowly stood up.

He could tell which was more interesting : staying in the office to handle monotonous documents, or going out on an adventure like treasure hunting to find a Seal.

Along the way, he might even « coincidentally » pass by Tanzaku Town and take a break at the casino there.

Senju Tobirama indicated that the sound of his abacus beads was making his ears hurt.

Therefore, he reached out and pressed Senju Hashirama’s slowly rising body back down.

« Big Brother, some of the documents here can only be handled by you. »

Upon hearing this, Senju Hashirama, knowing he was in the wrong, couldn’t refute it ; he just lowered his head, and his aura dropped as well.

« Matsuaki, how about we leave this matter to you ? »

Senju Tobirama turned his head to look at his nephew, his eyes lighting up.

In terms of status, Matsuaki is also a member of the Senju Clan.

In terms of strength, Senju Matsuaki is the most talented existence among the younger generation, possessing the potential of his Big Brother, Hashirama.

Furthermore, this scroll recording the predecessors' entrustment was also discovered by Matsuaki by chance.

It couldn’t be more appropriate to leave this matter to him.

Hearing that his second uncle was entrusting this matter to him, Senju Matsuaki was stunned at first, but since he had this intention anyway, he naturally didn’t shirk it.

Nodding, he accepted the matter : « That is exactly my intention. »

Anyway, this trip had to be taken sooner or later.

In order to have more confidence to deal with future crises, this matter naturally had to be put on the agenda.

Senju Hashirama thought for a moment and felt that this arrangement was not improper.

He had always been a decisive person ; since a decision had been made, he immediately pulled open the drawer and took out an unused blank scroll.

Then, he held a pen and began to write on the blank space of the scroll.

At the same time, he said to Senju Matsuaki : « In that case, let this matter be your first mission after being promoted to Chunin. »

After hesitating for a moment, Hashirama continued : « The mission Rank is tentatively set as… A-Rank. »

Missions in the Ninja Village not only come from others' entrustments but also from assignments by the Hokage.

At this moment, Senju Hashirama wrote down the task of checking the Seal as the content of an A-Rank mission on the scroll.

« Alright, Uncle. »

Senju Matsuaki reached out and took the mission scroll.

There are strict regulations for mission grading.

B-Rank missions are generally missions that may require Ninjas to participate in combat during the execution process.

A-Rank missions are missions that concern the movements of the Ninja Village and the country.

It seems that because of the words « extremely evil weapon » mentioned in the scroll, Senju Hashirama set the mission as A-Rank.

Of course, Senju Matsuaki knew that the danger level of this mission might be even more than that.

Judging from the Golden Finger platform’s grading, this is definitely an S-Rank mission, and the danger level should be above an A-Rank mission.

However, that this mission could be set as A-Rank by Senju Hashirama was already beyond Senju Matsuaki’s expectations.

After all, according to the Ninja Village’s regulations, the highest level of mission a Chunin can generally accept is B-Rank ; A-Rank missions must be accepted by Jonin.

Perhaps it was because he saw that Senju Matsuaki’s strength was already at the Jonin level that there was some flexibility in the regulations.

Of course, this was obviously a good thing for Senju Matsuaki.

The reward for an A-Rank mission is two to five times that of a B-Rank mission, which is a quite generous sum.

At the same time, an A-Rank mission would also be an important entry on his resume.

After completing this A-Rank mission and doing some other tasks to fill the numbers, promotion to Jonin would be a matter of the next year or two.

« Since our Senju predecessors called it an 'extremely evil weapon,' there might be some unpredictable dangers ; you must not take this trip lightly. »

Although he knew of Senju Matsuaki’s strength and his relatively reliable performance in daily life, Senju Hashirama still spoke to remind him.

Then, after the few of them chatted for a while, Senju Matsuaki stopped bothering the two of them with their official duties.

He had always been a person of swift and decisive style ; since he had accepted this mission, he intended to go home and pack his luggage right now.

Instead of idling in the Village or being watched by some people because of his fame from winning the finals of the previous Chunin Exams, it would be better to complete this mission sooner.

Of course, he didn’t forget that the scroll recording the location of the Seal was still at Senju Hashirama’s house.

Before setting off, it would definitely be necessary to go there again to get this scroll.
