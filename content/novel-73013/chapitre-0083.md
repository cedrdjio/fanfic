# Chapitre 83 — Young People Really Do Have Good Sleep Quality

The night was already deep.

They still had to continue their journey to the destination the next day, so after dinner, the two just chatted for a while before returning to their respective rooms to rest.

Wood Release could create houses and beds, but it could not produce pillows or quilts.

However, as Ninja, they weren’t that particular, and Senju Matsuaki and Hyuga Tsukuyomi lay down on the tatami mats and soon drifted off to sleep.

As the night grew deeper, a few uninvited guests were moving along the stream toward the location of the cabin.

The newcomers were three Kirigakure, two men and one woman, all wearing Hidden Mist forehead protectors.

One of the men and the woman had generic appearances, and aside from their clothing which had a distinct Land of Water style, they had no other notable features.

However, the other male Ninja had a peculiar appearance, looking as if he had a shark face, with light blue skin, sharp serrated teeth, and a short sword commonly used by Ninja strapped to his back.

All three of them were covered in wounds, clearly having arrived here after a bitter battle during the day.

« Hold on a little longer, there should be a place suitable for resting up ahead soon. »

Seeing his teammates' pace gradually slowing down, the shark-faced youth wore a worried expression and turned back to encourage them in a low voice.

However, upon hearing his words, the two teammates did not speed up ; instead, they stopped right where they were.

The two Kirigakure stared blankly ahead, their gazes dull.

« I must be too tired and am hallucinating, » one Kirigakure muttered.

« There seems to be a wooden cabin ahead. Aren’t I participating in the Chunin Exams ? Where has this brought me ? » The other Kirigakure also doubted what he was seeing.

In this desolate, primitive forest, where aside from the examinees participating in the Chunin Exams, only wild beasts and venomous insects remained, how could someone have built such a wooden cabin ?

« No, it’s not a hallucination, I see it too ! »

The shark-faced youth turned his head to follow his teammates' gaze, his expression first one of astonishment, then filled with joy.

He smiled, revealing his sharp teeth, and said : « Perhaps it’s a cabin left behind by someone who lived here before. We can go there to rest for the night. It would be even better if any supplies were left behind ! »

Although he said that, he knew in his heart that the second half of his sentence wasn’t very realistic.

If they could really find useful supplies in a cabin encountered by chance, they would probably have to wonder if they were dreaming.

The reason he said this was just because he saw his teammates were exhausted and wanted to boost their morale.

However, the first half of his sentence was true.

Staying in a wooden cabin compared to resting directly on the ground definitely had a much higher safety factor.

Sure enough, with a clear goal, the group was no longer as listless while traveling as they had been before.

« It really feels uncomfortable in the Land of Fire ; the air is much drier than in the Land of Water~ »

Seemingly because her mood had relaxed at the prospect of being able to rest, the female Kirigakure voiced her complaint.

« If I had known, I would have been better off participating in the next Chunin Exams held by our own Village instead of coming here and suffering like this. »

« Well, we can’t let other Villages look down on us, can we ? »

« That may be true, but... »

Just as the three of them were chatting away, a crisis quietly arrived.

Crack—

A crisp sound, quite piercing in the silent night.

Hearing this sudden sound, the three Hidden Mist Ninja all straightened up, subconsciously becoming alert.

« Um... sorry, I accidentally stepped on a branch. »

The male Kirigakure suddenly raised his right hand to signal his companions, his tone somewhat embarrassed.

« What the, don’t scare us for no reason ! »

However, just as the other two breathed a sigh of relief, a slight tremor suddenly came from beneath the male Kirigakure’s feet.

The next instant, several thick branches burst out from the ground, coiling and winding like pythons, binding the male Kirigakure tightly.

« Ah ! ! »

The female Kirigakure screamed, terrified by this scene straight out of a horror story, and was just about to move away from the spot.

Unfortunately, her reaction was ultimately a beat too slow.

The moment the male Kirigakure was restrained, similar python-like branches also tightly coiled around this female Ninja.

« Damn it, what the hell is this ! ? »

The shark-faced Kirigakure reacted faster ; after jumping to avoid the incoming branches, he immediately drew the Ninja sword from his back and slashed at the branches coiling around his companions.

However, the counter-shock force transmitted through his hands, and the mere slight scratches visible on the branches, made him grit his teeth involuntarily.

He couldn’t cut through them with his sword in a short time, and he was afraid that using Ninjutsu would wipe out his teammates first.

After thinking it over, he decided to make a tactical retreat first to find the culprit manipulating these branches.

Giving his two teammates a look, the shark-faced Kirigakure prepared to turn and retreat.

What he didn’t notice was that the other two Kirigakure were staring behind him, their expressions becoming increasingly terrified.

« Watch out ! ! »

By the time the warning sound reached him, it was already too late.

Just as the shark-faced Kirigakure turned around, what occupied most of his field of vision was a huge red flower, which was strikingly red even in the dark night.

The flower bloomed on a thick vine, and its bright red petals trembled slightly as the vine swayed.

Whoosh—

The stamen area contracted and surged, and countless pale yellow pollen drifted toward where the three stood, covering them in an instant.

« Oh, no... »

By the time the shark-faced Kirigakure realized something was wrong, he could already feel the abnormality in his body.

Dizziness and weakness in his limbs, a fatigue just like not having rested properly for ten days and ten nights.

« As expected, it’s a hallucination... »

Before losing consciousness, this thought flashed through his mind.

He had lived for over a decade, and this was the first time he had seen such a huge flower.

Meanwhile, inside the cabin not far away, Senju Matsuaki smiled and withdrew the Chakra in his hands, then closed his eyes to continue resting.

Early the next morning, sunlight shone into the cabin through the open window on the wall.

Senju Matsuaki opened his eyes, the golden sunlight reflecting in his red pupils, his eyes slightly squinting.

After getting up and walking out of the room, he found that Hyuga Tsukuyomi from next door was just walking out of her room as well.

« Good morning~ »

« Good morning ! »

After greeting each other, the two walked side by side down the stairs to the first floor of the cabin.

Senju Matsuaki formed hand seals, and a door capable of letting two people pass through opened on the seamless wooden wall.

« Eh ? What’s that over there... »

The three Kirigakure, wrapped in branches and unable to move, appeared quite conspicuously before Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s eyes.

Senju Matsuaki briefly explained : « They were approaching here in the middle of the night yesterday, so I controlled them. »

After the two walked up to the three Kirigakure, Senju Matsuaki formed hand seals, and the branches wrapping around the three loosened slightly.

A dull thud of bodies hitting the ground followed, yet the three Kirigakure showed no signs of waking up.

As expected of young people, their sleep quality is really good.

Senju Matsuaki sighed inwardly.
