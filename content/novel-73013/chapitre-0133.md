# Chapitre 133 — Tree Bind Eternal Burial! Championship Won!

« What kind of... joke is this ! ! ! »

Accompanied by a violent surge of lightning, the expression of Kumogakure’s Dou became crazed, as his Four-Finger Nukite, carrying violent thunder, pierced through countless giant trees.

Wood Release.

This Bloodline Limit, which he had only heard of from his elders in Kumogakure, was almost just a legend to him.

And in the past, he had scoffed at it.

Wood was just wood ; trees struck by lightning would either snap at best, or turn to charcoal at worst.

Mere Wood Release ; he would crush it with Lightning Release.

Thus, he had even fantasized that in the future, when he was at his Peak, he would battle a not-yet-aged Senju Hashirama and snatch away the title of « God of Ninja. »

But now he knew that he had been wrong.

The Hell Stab was a Ninjutsu he had recently been developing and honing.

As the number of fingers used decreased, its power would skyrocket exponentially.

However, this technique also had some limitations, namely that the strain on the body was immense.

Currently, the only one he could fully master was the most basic Four-Finger Nukite, yet even so, its power was unparalleled in battles of the same level.

Yet at this moment, having merely broken through a few giant trees blocking his path, he already felt a distinct sense of obstruction.

« It’s just mere wood, don’t get too arrogant ! ! ! »

At this point, Dou could no longer care about so much.

With a crazed look on his face, he made a decision that went against the teachings of his ancestors.

The teachings previously given by his teachers and elders—not to further use this technique until his physical strength had increased—were completely thrown to the back of his mind.

« Hell Stab ! Three-Finger Nukite ! ! ! »

« Like a mantis trying to stop a chariot ! »

Senju Matsuaki let out a cold snort, and countless trees under his control surged with vitality, rushing toward Dou’s position one after another like countless giant pythons hunting for food.

After the Deep Forest Emergence, this forest was his territory, and he was the uncrowned king here !

Crack— Crack— One, two...

Giant tree after giant tree was snapped under the Hell Stab, with the electric current scorching charred marks where they broke.

If Senju Matsuaki’s Wood Release had only manifested a dozen or so giant trees, perhaps Dou really could have broken through with the powerful destructive force of the Three-Finger Nukite.

But unfortunately.

He had to face thousands of such hard, thick trees.

Standing on top of the giant tree beneath his feet, Senju Matsuaki took a deep breath, expressionless.

He might still have some shortcomings in other areas.

But as long as Wood Release was unleashed, he would have no match, at least among the Jonin level !

After countless victories, Dou, who prided himself on being invincible among his peers, was now suppressed without any ability to fight back.

However, the victory before him did not make Matsuaki arrogant.

He knew deeply that he was still an incredibly long distance from the Peak of the Ninja World.

Not to mention the Ōtsutsuki hidden behind the scenes and beyond the heavens.

Just the gap between him and the Peak powerhouses of this era was as vast as an abyss.

The road ahead is long and arduous.

« Wood Release : Tree Bind Eternal Burial ! ! »

Senju Matsuaki formed hand signs, his Chakra surging within him, connecting with the trees in the distance.

Dou, whose footsteps were gradually slowing down, was completely halted in a position far away from Senju Matsuaki, blocked by the endless giant trees.

His sturdy body appeared quite small against the backdrop of the giant trees.

In the final few seconds, through the gaps between the tree trunks and vines, he saw clearly the figure standing atop the giant tree, already possessing the initial air of invincibility.

« What a pathetic defeat. »

There was no unwillingness or resentment in his mind ; at the moment of defeat, Dou’s heart was strangely calm instead.

After doing everything he could and still failing to break the situation, he accepted his defeat.

[Defeated Kumogakure Jonin Dou, Reward : 500 Points !]

[B-Rank Mission Completed ! Won until the end in the third stage of the Joint Chunin Exams. Reward : 3000 Points !]

Two consecutive notification sounds happened to celebrate Senju Matsuaki’s victory at this very moment.

Of course, the sounds of celebration did not only come from the System.

In the spectator stands, after a few seconds of silence, a shout erupted, unprecedented since these Chunin Exams began.

« Senju Matsuaki ! Senju Matsuaki ! ! ! »

« Konoha ! Konoha ! ! ! »

The spectators who had watched the entire match were now cheering excitedly for Senju Matsuaki’s victory.

They came from all over the world, some even being citizens of the Land of Lightning, where the defeated party, Dou, came from.

But at this moment, they were all shouting the same name.

« Matsuaki really did it. »

On the viewing platform, Hyuga Tsukuyomi, still sitting in her wheelchair, smiled faintly as she gazed at the youth who had achieved victory.

She had never doubted that Matsuaki would lose, firmly believing that he would return in triumph.

Therefore, when it truly arrived, she did not feel any sudden, overwhelming joy, but rather a heartfelt, natural happiness.

Behind her, Hyuga Teruake looked at his sister with a somewhat complicated expression.

After wanting to speak several times but stopping, he remained silent.

On the other side, Senju Tobirama was equally stirred.

Of course, his change in mood was not just because of Senju Matsuaki’s victory, but also for other reasons.

« It seems that in the next few years, the number of mission requests for our Konoha will double, » he thought to himself.

In these Chunin Exams, Konoha had undoubtedly flexed its muscles in front of all the spectators.

Whether it was the ability to coordinate and maintain order during large-scale Chunin Exams, or the unrivaled combat power of its Ninja, both were plus points.

Once these spectators returned to their countries, Konoha’s reputation would spread throughout the Ninja World through repeated conversations.

The only trouble was that the number of Ninja in Konoha could not increase in a short time.

If the number of mission requests increased, the burden on each Ninja would become heavier.

But it didn’t matter ; for the sake of Konoha’s construction, let everyone suffer a little, and the bad reputation will be borne by me, Senju Tobirama... no, by my older brother, Senju Hashirama !

It was time to announce the results.

« The final winner of this Joint Chunin Exams final is, Senju Matsuaki ! ! ! »

« Next, the Five Kage will conduct a comprehensive evaluation of all candidates participating in this assessment to decide whether to promote them to Chunin ! »

« The assessment results will be distributed to all candidates one after another within this week ! »

« Konoha’s various entertainment facilities and the Food Street will remain open ; everyone is welcome to come and experience them ! »

« In addition, products such as senju happy water, Ino–Shika–Chō brand health drinks, etc., are continuously accepting pre-orders ; everyone is welcome to go to the unified ordering store to order the above products ! »

After finishing this series of announcements, Senju Tobirama, the Proctor and host of this competition, finally retired after successfully completing his task.

On the competition field, Senju Matsuaki, having won the final, also exited and left.

As for Dou, who was still buried under the tree vines, Konoha’s Proctors would naturally begin rescue operations for him.

Senju Matsuaki had not used lethal force ; otherwise, Dou would have already had his body twisted and deformed by the thick giant trees.

But he had not been too gentle either.

At the very least, he would not be able to recover without any lasting complications unless he lay in a hospital bed resting for a month or two.
