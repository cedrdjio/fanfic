# Chapitre 42 — Tobirama: A Slightly Soiled Hem

« Matsumi, are you wondering why I asked you to take on the challenge instead of letting the monkey do it ? »

Senju Tobirama walked behind Senju Hashirama, turning his head to look at Matsumi, whose expression was slightly grave.

« Could it be because Big Brother Hiruzen has other important missions and cannot get away ? » Matsumi asked, puzzled.

In terms of age, Dou was thirteen, and Hiruzen Sarutobi was also thirteen.

In terms of status, Dou was Hong’s disciple, and Hiruzen was also Senju Tobirama’s disciple.

In terms of future development, Dou would inherit the title of « A » to become the Third Raikage, while Hiruzen would be entrusted with the mission in a time of crisis to become the Third Hokage.

If Hiruzen Sarutobi were to take on the challenge, it would clearly be a perfect match, extremely appropriate.

Of course, Matsumi also knew in his heart that the wings of this Kochou had already stirred up a storm in this world, deviating from the established path, and he could no longer view the Ninja World based on his own memories.

« Hiruzen and the others do indeed have important matters to attend to today, but that is not the main reason. »

Senju Tobirama nodded first, then shook his head.

« Just now, the Kumogakure elder said they wanted to have a contest with us in Konoha, and the two people they brought out are both his bodyguards. »

« If we don’t accept on the spot, but instead take the time to gather our own candidates, we will likely lose a step in terms of momentum. »

« And most importantly, you don’t need to underestimate yourself. Although that kid looks aggressive, you might just have a chance of winning. »

A trace of an imperceptible smile played on the corner of Senju Tobirama’s mouth. His words were not only devoid of pessimism but actually showed considerable confidence in Matsumi’s upcoming match.

« Second Uncle, are you talking about that technique that First Uncle taught me last time ? »

Matsumi was a bit bewildered at first, but then, as if he had thought of something, he lowered his head in thought.

« Matsumi, just do your best. » Senju Hashirama did not turn back, but his gentle and generous voice came from the front.

...

The group soon arrived at the Konoha training ground.

The group, who had been walking one after another, tacitly spread out to the left and right upon arriving at the training ground, surrounding the edge of the training ground to watch the battle.

« Then let us old guys start first. »

Walking straight to the center of the training ground, Hong of Kumogakure rubbed his left wrist with his right hand, having already entered a state of combat readiness, and smiled as he looked at Senju Tobirama.

Although they were only in their thirties, compared to Matsumi and the others, they were indeed senior veterans.

At this moment, Hong only felt his fighting spirit boiling in his heart, as if a bull was charging and rampaging within his blood.

He knew this was because he hadn’t encountered an evenly matched opponent for too long, so his desire for battle had remained unsatisfied all these years.

After all, after becoming the Raikage’s bodyguard, he had left the village less and less to carry out missions, focusing more on guarding the Raikage and assisting the Raikage in handling daily official business.

Now in Konoha, he had encountered a Ninja who was in his prime just like him and renowned in the Ninja World as his opponent, so naturally, he was itching for a fight.

Hearing this, Senju Tobirama had no objections and calmly walked onto the field and stood still.

Since the other party wanted to witness his Flying Thunder God Technique, he would not hide anything.

He reached into his Ninja tool pouch, and a classic-style Kunai appeared in his hand.

Unlike a regular Kunai, the handle of this Kunai was marked with the Flying Thunder God pattern seal.

The Flying Thunder God Technique was one of the many Ninjutsu developed by Senju Tobirama. Usually, he only used Water Release as a conventional combat method, and would only take out the Flying Thunder God Kunai when encountering strong enemies.

When the Senju Clan and the Uchiha Clan were slaughtering each other in the Sengoku era, it was king against king, general against general.

His big brother Senju Hashirama fought fiercely with Uchiha Madara, while Senju Tobirama also fought to a draw with Madara’s younger brother, Uchiha Izuna.

It was not until Senju Tobirama created the Flying Thunder God Technique that he finally secured victory and cut down the evil Uchiha rival.

On the training ground, not only the people who had moved from the conference hall, but also some Konoha residents and Ninjas who were already nearby were attracted by the commotion here and gathered toward the outside of the field.

This was not an official match or assessment, so it would be somewhat ridiculous to have someone act as a referee to call the start of the match.

Moreover, the two people on the field were already battle-hardened Ninjas ; as soon as their eyes met, they knew when to start the battle.

« Lightning Release Chakra Mode ! »

On the field, the body of the Raikage’s bodyguard, Hong, erupted with branching, tiny electric arcs, which eventually formed lightning that wrapped around his entire body, with an aura full of destruction and violence rushing toward them.

Under the Lightning Release Chakra Mode, the user can wrap powerful lightning around their body for attack or defense, and it can also grant the user extremely fast movement speed and powerful damage reduction capabilities.

Although this was only a B-rank Ninjutsu, it depended on who the user was.

Hong, who had already tempered his body to be as hard as steel, instantly transformed into a violent lightning beast after activating the Lightning Release Chakra Mode.

Some civilians outside the field who had not stepped into the Ninja realm exclaimed at this sight and retreated backward to avoid being affected by the overflowing lightning.

« This is the Lightning Release Chakra Mode ; it truly lives up to its reputation. »

With dazzling lightning filling his vision, Senju Matsumi, who was watching from outside the field, couldn’t help but narrow his eyes slightly.

Then, his gaze subconsciously turned toward Dou, the future Third Raikage, who was also outside the field, warming up.

The practice of the Lightning Release Chakra Mode is not something that can be achieved overnight, but requires day-after-day tempering.

Therefore, thirteen-year-old Dou, although not yet worthy of the title of the future « Strongest Shield, » should already be able to proficiently use the Lightning Release Chakra Mode.

The speed of Hong, having activated the Lightning Release Chakra Mode, was like lightning itself ; however, Senju Tobirama’s Flying Thunder God Technique had long since exceeded the category of simple speed.

For a time, only two blue figures remained on the field, crisscrossing each other, with the dull thuds of fists and kicks colliding and the crisp clashing of Kunai being heard from time to time.

After all, having fought from the chaotic Sengoku era to the present, neither side was a simple person.

This high-speed battle lasted for more than ten minutes before the commotion gradually subsided, and the rhythm of the battle slowed down to a level that ordinary people could see clearly.

« Hoo~ The Flying Thunder God Technique truly lives up to its reputation. » Hong’s chest heaved violently as he said in a deep voice.

In the recent clash, he had fully experienced the elusive nature of the Flying Thunder God.

It was just like an extremely agile mosquito ; when you think you’ve clapped your hands to swat it to death, you open your palms only to find nothing.

He had originally thought that the Flying Thunder God Kunai would be the breakthrough point, and that he only needed to pay attention to the position of the Kunai to guard against the suddenly appearing Senju Tobirama.

But when he discovered that his arm had also been marked with the exact same Flying Thunder God seal as the one on the Kunai at some unknown point, he finally gave up.

« Thank you for the match. »

Senju Tobirama tossed the Kunai in his hand into the air, and it landed perfectly into his Ninja tool pouch when it fell.

The battle ended, the hem of his garment slightly soiled.
