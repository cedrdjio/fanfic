# Chapitre 171 — Ryūjin Jakka! Susanoo! Forcing back the Tsuchikage!

Ishikawa’s expression changed drastically.

He could clearly feel that the heat seemed capable of reducing him to ashes in an instant.

That was... a terrifying power capable of burning everything to nothingness !

« Kid, what exactly... is that sword of yours ? »

Ishikawa’s voice became low and grave ; he had never seen such a terrifying katana before.

He could not discern whether it was the power of the sword itself, or if the young man was channeling some other power through it.

Senju Matsuaki had a faint smile on his lips and did not answer his question.

« Tsuchikage, why don’t you try it yourself ? With the strength of the three of you, let’s see if you can take us down ! »

Senju Matsuaki smiled slightly, applied a little force with his fingers, and drew the blade an inch from its scabbard.

Just that one inch.

A blinding red light burst forth from the gap in the scabbard, accompanied by a low roar, and a violent wave of heat swept outwards as if it were a physical force !

Ishikawa subconsciously took a step back, and Mu and Ōnoki were forced to raise their hands to shield their faces.

The rocks beneath their feet began to melt, and viscous lava flowed slowly.

Senju Matsuaki gripped the hilt, his tone calm yet unquestionable.

« Tsuchikage-sama, I do not wish to be enemies with your village. But if you insist on attacking... »

He narrowed his eyes slightly, and the blade in his hand emitted an even more intense glow.

« Then you can just stay here today ! ! »

As the words fell, a terrifying heat wave surged, and in the eyes of the three, Senju Matsuaki, holding Ryūjin Jakka, seemed to transform into a demon god amidst the raging fire !

Ishikawa’s face turned livid, and beads of sweat seeped from his forehead.

He could feel the pressure radiating from that katana.

It was not simple Fire Release Chakra, but a far more pure and terrifying power.

Even as the Kage of a village, facing the katana in the young man’s hand, he could not help but feel a chill in his heart.

This feeling...

He subconsciously recalled the terror he felt when facing Uchiha Madara and Senju Hashirama in the past.

« Tsuchikage-sama... »

Mu lowered his voice, his eyes fixed intently on Senju Matsuaki, « That sword is not right. »

Ishikawa frowned and glanced at him.

Does this need to be said when it’s obvious at a glance ?

To the side, Ōnoki clenched his fists, and the figure of that man from many years ago involuntarily surfaced in his mind.

Although Senju Matsuaki was young, his calm and collected demeanor was surprisingly similar to that of Madara back then.

« Do you think you can threaten a Tsuchikage with just a sword ? »

Ishikawa’s voice carried anger, but he did not take another step forward.

Senju Matsuaki did not answer, merely drawing the blade out a little further.

In an instant, an even more intense white light bloomed ; the surrounding air twisted and distorted, and the rocky ground completely turned into crimson lava, bubbling and churning.

The three of them were forced to retreat again ; that heat wave had exceeded the limits of human endurance.

« Enough ! »

Ishikawa waved his hand abruptly, his face terrifyingly grim, « Put away your weapon. »

Senju Matsuaki smiled slightly and slowly sheathed the blade.

The suffocating heat wave dissipated, but the air remained scorching, silently telling of the terror that had just occurred.

« A wise choice, Tsuchikage-sama. »

Ishikawa took a deep breath, suppressing the anger and unwillingness in his heart.

As the Kage of a village, he knew well when to advance and when to retreat. The power displayed by this young man was too bizarre ; attacking rashly without knowing his background would only cause Iwagakure to pay an unnecessary price.

Ishikawa was silent for a long time before finally speaking slowly : « Kid, what exactly is your Konoha trying to achieve by capturing Tailed Beasts on such a large scale ? »

« With the power of Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama, and you as the successor in the future, Konoha shouldn’t need the power of these Tailed Beasts. »

« Hokage-sama’s goal is peace in the Ninja World. »

Senju Matsuaki answered calmly, « Collecting Tailed Beasts is merely to prevent them from causing chaos ; we have no intention of starting a war. »

« No intention of starting a war ? »

Ishikawa narrowed his eyes slightly, his expression shifting uncertainly.

« You may leave. »

Ishikawa said coldly, « But remember, although Senju Hashirama is strong, the other four great ninja villages are not to be trifled with ! »

« Understood. »

Senju Matsuaki nodded slightly, his gaze falling on the Uchiha, « Hikari, let’s go. »

Just as he finished speaking, Uchiha Hikari, who had been silent, suddenly raised her head, and a flash of scarlet crossed her pitch-black eyes.

Three tomoe spun and connected, transforming into a complex and gorgeous pattern.

Mangekyo Sharingan !

Ishikawa’s pupils contracted sharply.

Mu’s body jolted, and he blurted out, « That is... Mangekyo Sharingan ? ! »

Ōnoki’s face went deathly pale, as if he had seen something unbelievable.

That pattern, that Aura... how similar it was to the eyes of Uchiha Madara back then !

Uchiha Hikari remained expressionless, Chakra surging around her !

The next moment, a massive crimson Chakra skeleton appeared out of thin air, rapidly condensing into a giant, enveloping Senju Matsuaki, Hyuga Tsukuyomi, and herself within it.

The giant was clad in armor, with wings sprouting from its ribs, standing in mid-air, radiating a suffocating sense of pressure.

« Susanoo... »

Mu muttered to himself, his voice carrying undisguised gravity, « Except for the color, it is exactly the same as Uchiha Madara’s ! »

Ōnoki’s eyes widened, and the unforgettable memory in his mind rushed in like a tidal wave.

Once, he had encountered that man—Uchiha Madara—alongside Mu.

At that time, Madara had rendered him unable to move just by his Aura alone, and it remained the deepest nightmare in Ōnoki’s heart to this day.

And at this moment, the Susanoo of the young girl before him made him feel as if he had suddenly returned to that nightmarish battlefield.

« How is it possible... »

Ōnoki gritted his teeth, « Aside from Uchiha Madara, there is actually someone else who can awaken the Mangekyo Sharingan and use Susanoo... »

After Uchiha Madara left Konoha, there had been no word of a second pair of Mangekyo Sharingan appearing in the Ninja World.

But today, they had seen it.

Mu’s expression was equally ugly.

As a Kage-level powerhouse who had once faced Uchiha Madara, he knew very well how terrifying these eyes were.

Ishikawa watched this scene, his shock beyond measure.

He finally understood why Senju Matsuaki was so unafraid.

It was not just because of his own terrifying power, but also because he had such a companion beside him who possessed the Mangekyo Sharingan !

Both sides had two Kage-level powerhouses ; naturally, Senju Matsuaki had no reason to fear them.

« Let’s go. »

Uchiha Hikari said indifferently, her calm gaze sweeping over Ishikawa and the others.

Susanoo spread its massive Chakra wings and flapped them violently, whipping up a gale.

Ishikawa and the others subconsciously took several steps back, watching helplessly as the crimson giant soared into the sky, carrying the three of them off into the distance, soon disappearing at the edge of the horizon.

Only Ishikawa, Mu, and Ōnoki remained on the spot, along with the lava and fire on the ground that had yet to cool.

After a long silence, Ōnoki finally spoke with difficulty.

« Grandfather... could that Senju Matsuaki and the Uchiha Clan girl just now... »

« Your feeling is correct... »

Ishikawa nodded slightly, his expression grave : « Their power has undoubtedly reached the Kage level ! »

Mu looked at the sky and slowly expressed his feelings : « That boy named Senju Matsuaki, and the girl who possesses the Mangekyo Sharingan... their strength is likely far greater than what we have seen. »

« I can vaguely feel it. »

Ishikawa turned around, his gaze deep, « That boy from the Senju Clan, his power is already approaching the level of Senju Hashirama and Uchiha Madara ! »

As these words were spoken, the pupils of both Mu and Ōnoki contracted sharply.
