# Chapitre 95 — Uchiha Kagami Intervenes

« Your people from Iwagakure are bothering my companion, » Senju Matsuaki said coldly.

Judging solely by their appearance, these Iwagakure Ninjas were mostly teenagers, naturally having a more imposing presence in terms of height.

At this moment, these people were gathered together, attempting to create a sense of oppression.

« What bother ? I only saw you attack someone from our Iwagakure ! ! »

« Are you trying to pick a fight, damn it ! ? »

« Considering we’re before the exam, if you kneel down and apologize to us, we’ll forgive you ! ! »

Like attracts like.

The six Iwagakure Ninjas gathered before him all seemed to hold contempt and disdain for Konoha, and they themselves were not reasonable or polite people.

Every major Ninja village would, to some extent, promote its own strength while using half-truths to belittle other villages to strengthen the cohesion of their own Ninjas.

Truly powerful and clear-headed people would not become arrogant, but rather, it was these mediocre, narrow-minded people who were influenced by blind pride to become arrogant and overbearing.

Especially towards outsiders who they saw as inferior to themselves.

Listening to the increasingly noisy sounds around him, Senju Matsuaki’s brows furrowed slightly, and a terrifying Aura surged from within his body.

However, just as he was about to make his next move, a figure suddenly appeared in front of him.

« Enough. The exam rules will be announced soon ; there must be no shouting or arguing in the waiting room ! »

The person who arrived wore a Konoha headband, had a handsome face, black eyes, and slightly curled dark blue short hair.

He was wearing a black high-collared short-sleeved shirt, and the Uchiha fan crest on his back was quite conspicuous.

Uchiha Kagami ?

Looking at this figure he hadn’t seen in a long time, Senju Matsuaki was slightly stunned, and the Chakra that was about to burst forth returned to calm.

« Where did you pop out from, you guy ? Watch out, or we’ll take you on too... »

The sleazy Iwagakure Ninja, emboldened by his companions, was even more unscrupulous. Seeing Uchiha Kagami, who was about the same age as them, he didn’t take him seriously at all and spoke rudely as well.

However, in the next instant, cold Chakra surged from Uchiha Kagami’s eyes, and his originally black eyes turned scarlet.

In his scarlet eyes, three pitch-black tomoe slowly rotated.

Facing this pair of cold « Sharingan », these Iwagakure Ninjas felt as if someone had poured a bucket of ice water over them, and they shivered.

« Let’s go ! »

One Iwagakure Ninja pulled on his companion’s sleeve, wanting to retreat.

Some of them might not recognize the fan crest, but no one was unaware of the fame of the « Sharingan ».

The « Uchiha » Clan, possessing three tomoe, was definitely an existence that they, as Genin still participating in the « Chunin Exams », could not afford to provoke.

Seeing that his companions who had backed him up just a moment ago were backing down, the sleazy Iwagakure Ninja could only glare at Senju Matsuaki with hatred.

Before slinking away, seemingly to save some face, he didn’t forget to drop a harsh threat to Senju Matsuaki.

« Kid, count yourself lucky that we didn’t meet during a mission ! »

He belonged to that part of Iwagakure who could become Ninjas but had mediocre talent ; his future limit might just be stuck at « Chunin ».

Coupled with his sleazy appearance and terrible personality, girls of his age in Iwagakure didn’t want to pay attention to him in daily life.

This time, coming to « Konoha » and seeing a cute girl sitting alone on a chair, this sleazy Iwagakure Ninja felt an itch and went up to chat her up.

Unexpectedly, he was stopped before he could reach out his lecherous hand, losing face.

« This bunch of idiots... »

Seeing this group of gathered Iwagakure Ninjas leave, Uchiha Kagami couldn’t help but sigh helplessly while holding his forehead.

Looking at the appearance of these Iwagakure Ninjas, one might think it was his arrival that saved Senju Matsuaki.

In reality, the situation was completely the opposite.

The Aura that Senju Matsuaki had faintly emitted just a moment ago made even him secretly shocked.

You have to know, he was an « Uchiha » recognized by « Senju Tobirama ».

This was not only because of his personality but also because of his strength.

If he were a mediocre « Uchiha », no matter how good his personality was, « Senju Tobirama » probably wouldn’t pay too much attention to him.

Even within the « Uchiha » Clan, Uchiha Kagami’s talent was excellent, far surpassing « Uchiha Setsuna » and others.

Previously, his strength was slightly weaker than « Hiruzen Sarutobi » at the time.

And in a mission not long ago, after he awakened the three tomoe, his strength soared again, placing him in the middle tier among « Jonin ».

This was the greatest advantage of the « Uchiha » Clan.

From not having awakened eyes, to one tomoe, two tomoe, and three tomoe, each could bring a surge in strength to the « Uchiha ».

However, just now, he was able to feel a moment of terror from Senju Matsuaki.

Standing in front of Senju Matsuaki at that time, Uchiha Kagami only felt that there was an awakened lion behind him, wanting to tear the prey in front of it to pieces.

« Brother Kagami, are you on a mission ? » Senju Matsuaki greeted him with a smile.

Uchiha Kagami’s appearance could also be considered as getting him out of a tight spot.

After all, if they had really started fighting just now, whether it was these Iwagakure Ninjas or himself, it would have been a violation of the « Chunin Exams » rules.

« As for today, Homura Mitokado, Utatane Koharu, Akimichi Torifu, and I are responsible for maintaining order in the waiting room. As for Hiruzen Sarutobi and Danzo Shimura, they have other missions. »

Uchiha Kagami said this, glancing strangely at a stall not far away.

« That’s really a very hard mission, » Senju Matsuaki remarked.

Ninjas from various major villages gathered in the waiting room, and being at an age full of youthful vigor, conflicts were naturally inevitable.

« Yeah, I’ll be going now. »

Just as the two were still talking, another commotion came from a corner near the lockers not far away.

Looking closely, it turned out that a conflict had arisen between two groups from « Hidden Mist » and « Kumogakure ».

Seeing this, Uchiha Kagami gave a wry smile and then hurried over.

Before leaving, he gave Senju Matsuaki a complicated look.

« I didn’t expect such annoying flies to gather around just after leaving for a while. »

After Uchiha Kagami walked away, Senju Matsuaki sat back down in the seat beside « Hyuga Tsukuyomi ».

« Thank you, Matsuaki~ » « Hyuga Tsukuyomi »'s eyes curved into two crescents.

They didn’t take those noisy and annoying Iwagakure Ninjas to heart.

It wasn’t just Senju Matsuaki ; these people probably weren’t even qualified to be « Hyuga Tsukuyomi »'s opponents.

If it weren’t for the rules of the « Chunin Exams », making it inconvenient to fight in the waiting room, « Hyuga Tsukuyomi » wouldn’t have just endured it either.

Moreover, even without Senju Matsuaki, she wouldn’t have continued to endure it when that Iwagakure Ninja reached out his lecherous hand just now.

« It’s fine, later in the exam... »

Senju Matsuaki was just halfway through his sentence when he was interrupted by a voice that suddenly resounded throughout the arena.

« I am the head  »Proctor«  of the third stage of the exam,  »Senju Tobirama« . Next, I will briefly introduce the exam situation to all guests, spectators, and candidates, and read out the exam rules. »

« Senju Tobirama »'s steady and powerful voice was amplified by « Ninjutsu », echoing over the arena and clearly reaching the ears of everyone present.

Hearing that « Senju Tobirama » had become the head « Proctor » for the third stage again, Senju Matsuaki’s expression was slightly strange.

He’s really being used like a nuclear-powered donkey.

But on second thought, it was reasonable ; « Senju Tobirama » was now the person who could best represent « Konoha » under « Senju Hashirama », so more capable people should do more work.
