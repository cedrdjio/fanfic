# Chapitre 143 — Uchiha Hikari!

Inside the Uchiha Clan camp, Senju Matsuaki followed closely behind the young man in front.

That Uchiha Akita from just moments ago was the first to experience the Reanimation Jutsu he had obtained after checking in on Senju Tobirama.

While following the guidance of Senju Danzo to locate the Uchiha Clan camp, he happened to encounter two Ninjas from the Uchiha Clan.

A Jonin and a Chunin—they were already considered the backbone of their clan.

Senju Matsuaki’s age was somewhat deceptive, which meant they didn’t harbor any sense of vigilance.

At ten years old, there were indeed those with extraordinary talent who could reach the strength of a Jonin, but such geniuses were few and far between.

Even if the Senju Clan truly had such a Genius, as their old rivals, it was impossible for them not to have gathered the corresponding intelligence.

And this underestimation of their enemy opened the gates of death for them.

The moment they clashed, the erupting power of the Tremor-Tremor Fruit caused them to lose all ability to resist, leaving them completely defenseless.

Whether it was the more powerful Jonin or the weaker Chunin, there was no difference when their physical bodies bore the force of the Tremor-Tremor Fruit.

When the tremors dissipated, the two arrogant Uchiha Ninjas were already on the brink of death.

Senju Matsuaki used his Ninja blade to send the Jonin-level Uchiha to the Pure Land, then used the Chunin-level Uchiha as a sacrifice to test the new jutsu he had recently acquired.

Reanimation Jutsu.

It summoned the Souls of the dead from the Pure Land into the living world, under the control of the caster.

Judging by its effects, this jutsu truly deserved the title of « Forbidden Jutsu. » If other Ninja villages knew about this Ninjutsu developed by Senju Tobirama, they would likely condemn it as playing with the Souls of the dead.

But in this world where cruelty was the backdrop, being overly concerned about such things would be too naive.

Criticizing it verbally was one thing, but if given the chance to possess this Ninjutsu, all the Ninja villages would likely covet it with jealous eyes.

Furthermore, since the person being reanimated was an enemy who had intended to kill him, Senju Matsuaki had no psychological burden using the Reanimation Jutsu on him.

« Still, Second Uncle really is a Genius when it comes to developing Ninjutsu. »

Senju Matsuaki couldn’t help but sigh with emotion.

The Flying Thunder God Technique involving space, and the Reanimation Jutsu involving Souls and resurrection—if others wanted to achieve similar effects, they would have to rely on a Bloodline Limit or simply have the blood of the Ōtsutsuki Clan in their veins.

Yet Senju Tobirama was able to create these two jutsu simply through research, and they were even replicable and learnable by others.

Just as he was lowering his head, lost in silent thought, the young man from the Uchiha Clan had already brought him to the entrance of the dungeon.

After descending the uneven steps, his vision, which had previously been illuminated by campfires, instantly became dim.

The interior of the dungeon was a space excavated using Earth Release Jutsu. Since it was used for holding prisoners and criminals, it was naturally impossible for it to be repaired with much care.

Even though the lighting was dim and he couldn’t see the path clearly, Senju Matsuaki could still feel the unevenness of the ground beneath his feet.

Not far away, there was the sound of some creature scurrying about—it certainly wasn’t a human, but rather a mouse that had accidentally tunneled its way here.

Of course, this dungeon was not entirely dark and devoid of light.

Every few cells, there was a faint lamp hanging on the wall.

When the Uchiha young man brought Senju Matsuaki past them, the current of air stirred up would cause these faint flames to flicker.

By the dim candlelight, Senju Matsuaki’s gaze scanned through the cells.

As he took in the scenes inside each cell, his previously calm expression grew increasingly grim.

The veins on his arms, bound by ropes, bulged—a sign of suppressed fury reaching its limit.

At this moment, with the war between the Senju Clan and the Uchiha Clan ongoing, the majority of those imprisoned in the dungeon were naturally members of the Senju Clan.

In addition, there were a few of the Uchiha’s own clansmen.

Most of these Uchiha clansmen were imprisoned here as criminals because they had violated clan rules during the war, such as abandoning teammates or fleeing from battle.

But compared to the Senju clansmen also held here, the Uchiha’s own people imprisoned here were treated much better.

« The naturally evil Uchiha Clan... »

Senju Matsuaki’s expression was grim, and his Second Uncle Tobirama’s catchphrase, which he would occasionally blurt out, subconsciously flashed through his mind.

He took a rough look ; some of the Senju clansmen had serious injuries, clearly suffering from severe wounds after harsh interrogation, and it was only a matter of time before they died.

The other Senju clansmen also bore some injuries, leaning listlessly against the rough walls, their hands clutching their abdomens tightly to suppress their hunger.

The moment he saw these scenes, Senju Matsuaki almost couldn’t suppress his rage and explode.

Although he had never met these people before, they were, after all, his kin with whom he shared blood ties.

Before stepping into this dungeon, Senju Matsuaki had somewhat mentally prepared himself for these scenes.

After all, the Uchiha Clan’s dungeon couldn’t possibly only hold their own clansman, Uchiha Hikari ; more space would naturally be reserved for enemies.

But the scene before him still exceeded his imagination.

Having been born and raised in Konoha, Senju Matsuaki had experienced battles big and small, but this was his first time seeing war with his own eyes.

If the war between clans was already this cruel, then what would the wars between future villages and nations look like ?

Senju Matsuaki took a deep breath, his mood somewhat heavy.

His reason was not drowned out by rage, and he knew that now was definitely not the best time to burst out, kill, or save anyone.

In the camp at night, almost all of the Uchiha Clan’s elite were gathered here.

Once he released the prisoners in the dungeon, the commotion he caused would likely be noticed by the Uchiha clansmen in the camp immediately.

Facing one Jonin, he was confident he could take them out when they were off guard.

Facing two Jonin, if he used all his trump cards and relied on the power and uniqueness of his Wood Release and the Tremor-Tremor Fruit, it was not impossible for him to win.

But the number of Jonin in this camp, conservatively estimated, was over ten...

He wasn’t so delusional as to think he could take on ten Jonin at once.

« Get in and stay put ! »

Soon, the Uchiha young man stopped in front of an empty cell, opened the door, and shoved Senju Matsuaki inside roughly.

Pretending to stumble, Senju Matsuaki went along with it and sat down against the wall.

« Be glad you were only caught by Akita-senpai late at night. Enjoy tonight, because tomorrow won’t be this comfortable. »

After a cold snort, the Uchiha young man walked away with a sinister grin.

During wartime, many people had nowhere to vent their sorrow, anger, and stress, and these imprisoned Senju clansmen were the best targets for their abuse.

Akita-senpai... your Akita-senpai is already dead.

Senju Matsuaki watched the back of the person leaving and thought to himself.

Then, he exerted force with both hands, using the power of tremors to shatter the sturdy ropes binding his wrists.

After loosening his wrists, he stood up and walked to the cell bars, pressing his fingertips against the iron railings.

The smelting technology of this era was already similar to that of later generations ; the hardness could not be broken through by physical strength alone.

However, that Uchiha young man obviously couldn’t have imagined that Senju Matsuaki had bound his own hands to pretend to be a prisoner, and that his body had not been sealed like the other prisoners.

By the faint light of the dungeon, Senju Matsuaki looked toward the dim cell opposite him.

In the narrow space, a girl wearing a dark blue kimono with long, waist-length black hair sat hugging her knees. Her fair skin was still visible in the darkness, but her beautiful eyes were hollow and lifeless.

« What a coincidence. » Senju Matsuaki sighed.
