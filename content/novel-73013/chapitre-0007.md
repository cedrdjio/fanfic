# Chapitre 7 — Treating to Yakiniku!

This was the power of the Breathing Technique.

Even though Senju Matsumi was still a long way from the higher realm of Total Concentration Breathing known as « Total Concentration : Constant, » the effect was already quite significant.

After all, the Breathing Technique enhanced physical strength, and the Senju Clan had always been known for that.

Combining the two produced an effect where one plus one was greater than two.

Haa—

Matsumi could feel every cell in his body seemingly coming to life.

With each breath, his aura felt even more scorching than usual.

In the next instant, he had already vanished from where he stood.

Veins bulged on his right fist as he charged forward, carrying immense power.

Fast, accurate, and ruthless !

In the blink of an eye, the distance between the two closed rapidly.

« So fast ! »

Hyuga Tsukuyomi widened her eyes. Through the vision of her Byakugan, she could see his figure clearly.

However, her body’s reaction could not keep up.

Therefore, even though she subconsciously wanted to dodge, she couldn’t make the move.

Facing this powerful punch, she could only adopt a defensive stance, intending to endure it as best as she could.

As the distance closed, she could even feel the whistling sound of the air being displaced by his movement and strike.

However, the punch ultimately stopped right in front of her.

It was still about a palm’s width away from the arm she had held up to block it.

Her hair was blown back by the wind from his fist, and Hyuga Tsukuyomi stared blankly at Senju Matsumi before her.

After a long moment, she snapped out of it, her previously startled expression shifting into something akin to admiration.

« Matsumi-kun, that was amazing ! »

She could feel that the power of that punch was no less than the taijutsu of some Chunin in her clan.

« Ahaha, I’m still far from that level. »

Being looked at by such a cute girl with eyes full of brilliance made Senju Matsumi feel a bit embarrassed.

In reality, he still couldn’t maintain the Breathing Technique for long.

Especially in the heat of battle, the time he could sustain the Breathing Technique would be shorter than when he was stationary.

After all, he had only been practicing the Breathing Technique for a single day.

« Tsukuyomi, you’re the one who truly surprises me. »

« The coordination between your Byakugan and Gentle Fist is so harmonious ; I’m afraid that after graduation, you’ll only need a little bit of tempering to reach the standard of a Chunin. »

« Really... »

« Of course, you need to be more confident. I doubt there are many people in our class who are your match. »

Looking at the sky, it was getting late.

Senju Matsumi suggested, « Tsukuyomi, to thank you for practicing with me, let me treat you to dinner. »

« Eh ? »

Hyuga Tsukuyomi seemed tempted but appeared to have some concerns, her expression slightly conflicted.

However, after hesitating for a few seconds, she nodded and agreed, « Then I’ll go back first and tell my older brother. »

Although they looked similar, the lives of Hyuga Tsukuyomi and Hinata were completely different.

The former was not expected to inherit the Main Branch, and aside from following clan rules and learning etiquette, she was not subjected to much more restriction.

Hinata, on the other hand, had been raised as the heir to the Main Branch since childhood, held to high standards by her elders in every aspect.

Because she had several older brothers above her, especially since her eldest brother had already inherited the position of clan head, she, as the sister of the clan head, actually had a low profile.

By evening, the two met up again on one of the busiest streets in Konoha.

Choosing a yakiniku restaurant they frequented, Matsumi led the girl inside.

As soon as they stepped into the shop, the scent of grilling meat and spices drifted into their noses.

At the same time, one could see the oily, glistening grilled meat on the tables of other customers, its sheen and aroma enough to whet anyone’s appetite.

After sitting down and ordering, the two began to chat.

This rare atmosphere gave Matsumi a unique yet very relaxed feeling.

If one had to describe it, it was that friends who were originally limited to acquaintances at the Ninja Academy had further become friends in daily life.

The distance between the two closed significantly because of this, and the introverted Hyuga Tsukuyomi gradually began to talk more.

During their chat, Senju Matsumi also gained a better understanding of her and the Hyuga Clan.

In fact, the Hyuga Clan did not have only one Main Branch line belonging to the clan head.

If the clan head’s line was not cut off by an accident, then the heirs of other Main Branch lines would naturally become elders of the Hyuga Clan in the future.

If, due to some accident, the clan head’s line was left without successors, then other Main Branch lines would take over based on factors like bloodline and reputation.

This situation was much more realistic.

If, as many people assumed, the Hyuga Clan only had one Main Branch line, then many things would be unexplainable.

For example, how did Ao of the Hidden Mist Village break through the restrictions of the Caged Bird Seal to obtain a Byakugan ?

If this Byakugan belonged to the Main Branch, and there was only one Main Branch line, then the Main Branch of the Hyuga Clan would have been cut off right then and there.

Moreover, other members of the Hyuga Clan had indeed appeared in the animation, and there was no Caged Bird Seal mark on their foreheads.

The Main Branch having more than one line was a more reasonable explanation.

However, to prevent an unreasonable expansion in the number of Main Branch members, only one person from each line could inherit the Main Branch, while the other children had to be branded with the Caged Bird Seal and become part of the Branch House.

Otherwise, if one day the number of Main Branch members exceeded that of the Branch House, wouldn’t it become a joke ?

Was it the Branch House protecting the Main Branch, or the Main Branch protecting the Branch House ?

But although she was already attending the Ninja Academy, Hyuga Tsukuyomi had not been branded with the Caged Bird Seal.

As for the reason, she hadn’t brought it up herself, so naturally, he couldn’t very well ask.

After chatting, the two grew even hungrier.

Once the ordered meat arrived, they began to focus on enjoying their dinner.

Even though she appeared to be chewing slowly, Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s pace was not slow at all.

Originally worried that she wouldn’t be full, Matsumi had specifically ordered a three-person portion.

But now it seemed...

Looking at the imperceptible sense of wanting more on the girl’s face, as well as her unsatisfied appearance, a flash of inspiration hit Matsumi.

« Boss, add another three-person portion of meat here ! »

A couple of years ago, there was a period where his own appetite suddenly increased.

At first, he was worried that something was wrong with his body, until he asked Senju Tobirama and understood.

The special constitution of the Senju Clan was not like other Bloodline Limits that could be achieved overnight ; rather, it formed gradually.

At four or five years old, this process would accelerate, so the body naturally needed to replenish more energy.

The same applied to other Bloodline Limits ; during the process of awakening and evolution, more energy intake was required.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi had awakened her Byakugan early, showing that her bloodline purity was very high, so it was normal for her to need to consume more food.

« That was truly rude of me, Matsumi. »

After finishing, Hyuga Tsukuyomi lowered her head in embarrassment.

During their earlier chat, at Matsumi’s request, Hyuga Tsukuyomi had changed her original form of address and called him directly by his name.

However, she was still somewhat shy because she had eaten so much just now.

The reason she had hesitated about accepting the invitation earlier was also due to this concern.

Only after feeling that he really didn’t mind did she set her heart at ease.

However, even though she claimed to be full now, in reality, she was only about fifty or sixty percent full.

It was getting late, and after saying goodbye, the two went their separate ways home.
