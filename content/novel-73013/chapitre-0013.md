# Chapitre 13 — Friendship Battle! VS Hiruzen Sarutobi!

Because the winners of the other two battles forfeited one after another, Matsuaki was the only one left on the field with the strength to continue fighting.

Thus, he became the first place in this assessment.

When this result appeared, Matsuaki’s cheat also determined that two C-rank missions were completed.

« C-rank mission completed : Defeat Uchiha Kai during the Ninja Academy graduation assessment and let him understand who is the one that speaks with strength. Reward : 50 points. »

« C-rank mission completed : Pass the graduation assessment. Reward : 100 points. »

The previous mission rewarded a fixed 50 points.

The latter mission awarded 100 points based on Matsuaki’s comprehensive performance in this assessment.

Added to his accumulation over this period, his total points had already broken through the 200 mark.

Matsuaki was naturally very satisfied with this result.

However, the only slight shortcoming was that he felt he hadn’t had enough fun.

Matsuaki had originally thought that he would finally be able to fight an evenly matched opponent.

But only now did he realize that true genius Ninjas had already graduated within one or two years of entering the Ninja Academy.

And even Sarutobi Go, although he had long reached the standard of a Genin, could not compare to those top geniuses.

True geniuses like the future Kakashi Hatake, Uchiha Itachi, and others.

They graduated from the Ninja Academy at a very young age, and by the time their peers graduated, they had already reached the level of a Jonin.

Take Hiruzen Sarutobi at this time as an example.

He was only one year older than Sarutobi Go, but his strength was already not far from a Jonin.

Senju Matsuaki himself had extremely high talent, not inferior to the likes of young Kakashi.

Added to these cheats, it could be said that he had a growth rate far exceeding his peers.

It wasn’t just Senju Matsuaki ; others watching the battle from the sidelines also felt a bit unsatisfied.

Senju Hashirama didn’t know when he had taken off his Hokage hat and was holding it in his hand, fanning himself.

After looking left and right, his gaze finally locked onto Hiruzen Sarutobi beside Tobirama.

« Monkey, go and have another fight with little Matsuaki. » Senju Hashirama said with a smile.

« Huh ? Me ? »

Hiruzen Sarutobi pointed at himself with his right hand.

« That’s right, otherwise, it feels like it hasn’t completely ended yet. »

« Yes ! »

Originally, Hiruzen Sarutobi didn’t have much enthusiasm for fighting ; he was just obeying Hokage-sama’s orders.

But after seeing Biwako, who hadn’t left yet, his eyes lit up.

In the previous assessment, Senju Matsuaki had shown overwhelming dominance over the other candidates.

If he were to go and defeat him in the name of guidance now, wouldn’t he immediately attract Biwako’s attention ?

« This kid. »

Seeing Hiruzen Sarutobi go from reluctant to excited, Tobirama, who knew his disciple very well, also had black lines on his forehead.

Allowing people other than graduates and Proctors to participate in the graduation assessment was unprecedented.

However, he had no intention of stopping his older brother’s somewhat reckless arrangement.

After all, he also wanted to know what kind of strength his nephew had now.

If he could easily win against Genin-level peers, what would happen when he encountered Hiruzen, who was almost at the Jonin level ?

Hiruzen Sarutobi ran quickly to the side of the Proctor and whispered a few words.

The Proctor was slightly stunned at first, then looked to the other side of the field.

When he saw Hokage-sama nodding at him, he continued to announce.

« The final winner of this assessment is Senju Matsuaki ! »

« Next, an exhibition match will be held between the outstanding Ninja Academy graduate, Hiruzen Sarutobi, and student Matsuaki. The result of this battle will not affect the final graduation assessment results ! »

« A friendship battle, with brother Hiruzen ? »

Matsuaki, who had just been thinking that the battle wasn’t fun enough, was stunned.

Noticing the actions of Senju Hashirama and Hiruzen Sarutobi just now, he had thought there was some other matter to be notified.

Unexpectedly, it was to have him add a match with Hiruzen Sarutobi.

It seemed that not only him, but even Senju Hashirama and the others watching felt that he hadn’t shown his true strength.

But this was exactly what he wanted.

Matsuaki himself hadn’t fought enough, and at this moment, he felt a surge of energy pent up inside him.

Moreover, in Konoha, he was considered one of the biggest connections ; the Hokage was his uncle, and the next Hokage was his second uncle.

So he had no need to hide his skills at all.

Just at this moment, his cheat platform also thoughtfully released a corresponding mission.

« C-rank mission released : Fight Hiruzen Sarutobi without being defeated. Reward : 200 points ! »

Seeing this, Senju Matsuaki let out a long breath.

Even for missions of the same level, the richness of the rewards could vary greatly.

200 points, even exceeding the sum of the rewards from the previous two missions.

However, the mission reward was also directly related to the difficulty.

Although he didn’t know how strong Hiruzen Sarutobi was at this time, since he could be taken as a disciple by his second uncle, Senju Tobirama, and could become the Third Hokage in the original timeline, his strength was definitely not to be underestimated.

However, the requirements of the mission were obviously not that harsh.

It was just a friendship battle, not a life-and-death struggle. As a senior who had graduated for five or six years, Hiruzen Sarutobi obviously wouldn’t go all out.

And the requirement of the mission was just not to be defeated...

Regardless, he would only know after fighting !

Thinking of this, Matsuaki did not hesitate and walked slowly to the center of the field under the encouraging gazes of his companions.

Perhaps because of the change in opponent, his mentality was different from the previous few times.

His previous opponents had a large gap in strength compared to him.

But at this moment, the situation was the opposite ; he was the one with the weaker strength.

This made Senju Matsuaki feel pressure, and this was also motivation.

« Long time no see, Matsuaki. »

« Long time no see, brother Hiruzen. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi, standing in the center of the field, was just a twelve or thirteen-year-old boy at this time.

He was wearing tight-fitting clothes convenient for Ninja combat, with clean, short hair, full of the vitality of a young man.

At this moment, Hiruzen Sarutobi was not without pressure in his heart.

He had also watched the process of the previous match, and the strength Senju Matsuaki displayed was indeed impressive.

Although he felt that Senju Matsuaki was still not his opponent, there were no absolutes in Ninja battles.

If he accidentally lost to the other party by mistake, it would be too embarrassing.

And Hiruzen Sarutobi was now a battle-hardened Chunin with a certain level of discernment.

Facing Senju Matsuaki, he felt that he wasn’t facing a newly graduated Genin.

But a Ninja who could truly pose a threat to him.

« Both sides, form the Seal of Confrontation ! »

Seeing that both sides were in position, the Proctor said loudly and skillfully.

With the Seal of Confrontation formed, this friendship battle kicked off.

« Battle, begin ! »
