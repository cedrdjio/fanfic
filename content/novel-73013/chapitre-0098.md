# Chapitre 98 — Why Is Yours So Big!?

« Overestimating yourself ! ! When I mastered my first Earth Release Ninjutsu, I’m afraid you had barely learned how to hold a Kunai ! ? »

Seeing the hand seals Senju Matsuaki was forming, the Iwagakure Ninja was stunned for a moment, then couldn’t help but laugh out loud.

The seals were exactly the same as the ones he had just formed ; it was clearly the same Ninjutsu.

Earth-Style Split !

This was the main reason he couldn’t help but laugh.

Not everyone in Iwagakure is skilled at Earth Release, but overall, their proficiency in Earth Release Ninjutsu is definitely higher than other Ninja villages.

Part of this is due to the unique geographical environment of the Land of Earth, and part of it is because the proportion of people born in the Land of Earth who naturally possess Earth-attribute Chakra is higher than in other countries.

Plus the advantage in age.

This Iwagakure Ninja looked to be thirteen or fourteen years old, yet Senju Matsuaki looked like he hadn’t even reached ten.

How could he lose ?

At this moment, the Iwagakure Ninja could be said to feel like victory was already in his grasp ; he felt quite good about himself.

Among the spectators, there was no shortage of Ninjas from small Ninja villages or Rogue Ninjas who had come from afar to watch the competition.

Seeing Senju Matsuaki also form the seals for Earth-Style Split, they couldn’t help but exchange glances with their fellow Ninja companions and smile knowingly.

Other non-Ninja spectators who couldn’t understand the hand seals or see the situation on the field were completely confused.

However, with simple explanations from the Ninjas beside them, they roughly understood the situation on the field.

There were those who sympathized, and those who mocked.

« Young people really don’t know their own limits. »

« Heh, daring to use the same Earth Release Ninjutsu in front of Ninjas from our Land of Earth and Iwagakure... I think Konoha is getting a bit too arrogant ! »

« Sigh, I hope the Proctor saves this child in time ; after all, it’s still a young life. »

However, while those who didn’t understand or only half-understood laughed, those who truly understood were shocked.

In the center of the spectator stands, all four Kage, except for Senju Hashirama, wore expressions of shock.

« This amount of Chakra, are you kidding me ? »

Although it was only a fleeting moment, the immense Chakra on Senju Matsuaki still did not escape the perception of the four Kage.

This was merely a small fraction of the Chakra Senju Matsuaki was calling upon to use the Ninjutsu.

Kazekage Reto said in a deep voice : « His control over Chakra and proficiency in Ninjutsu are enough to rank him in the upper echelon of Chunin. Among all the examinees, I’m afraid there are only a handful who can surpass him. »

The « handful » he referred to were only Yan from Sunagakure and Dou from Kumogakure.

As for the outstanding ones among the others, they probably only reached the Chunin level within the last year and could not possibly possess such control and proficiency !

« To have such proficiency in Earth Release at this age, his talent is indeed rare. » Tsuchikage Ishikawa couldn’t help but feel a desire to cherish such talent.

Such talent, even his own beloved grandson Ōnoki couldn’t compare.

In the current younger generation of Iwagakure, there has been no other young person whose talent was excellent enough to make him marvel.

In Tsuchikage Ishikawa’s view, if such a junior could be fully cultivated in Iwagakure, he might have to struggle in the future over whether to let Ōnoki or this junior succeed as the Third Tsuchikage.

However, when he saw the clan emblem on the back of Senju Matsuaki’s clothes, his desire to cherish such talent immediately vanished, and he turned to look at the Hokage with envious eyes.

Someone from the Senju Clan, even if they really wanted to come to Iwagakure, he wouldn’t dare accept them.

« Sigh, it seems the boy from our village is going to lose. » The First Tsuchikage sighed.

He had really become an « honest man » in this Joint Chunin Exams, as those he sent to take the exam were genuine Genin.

It is expected that after another two or three rounds, their Iwagakure examinees will be wiped out.

Meanwhile, the First Raikage A looked thoughtfully at Senju Matsuaki in the arena.

« This is the kid that Hong and Dou mentioned. It seems they were right ; once this brat grows up, he will definitely be a major threat to Kumogakure ! » he thought to himself.

Back when the Cloud Village Elder returned to the village, he insisted on posting a bounty at the Bounty Station, and the target was a Genin from Konoha.

At first, the First Raikage didn’t quite understand this behavior, but out of respect for the Elder, he didn’t say much.

Now that he saw this, neither the Elder nor Hong and Dou had misjudged !

Senju Hashirama, Senju Tobirama... in a few more years, Senju Matsuaki will have grown up too. Will Konoha and the Senju Clan never end ! ?

In the arena.

« Earth Release : Earth-Style Split ! ! »

Senju Matsuaki pressed both hands to the ground, and a massive amount of Chakra surged into it.

Rumble—

The moment the technique was completed, there was a muffled thunderous sound from underground, as if a giant dragon lurking beneath the earth had let out a roar.

With the ground Senju Matsuaki had just touched as the center, a huge crack, like an overbearing and ferocious black dragon, spread forward with bared fangs and brandished claws.

The Earth-Style Split that the Iwagakure Ninja had cast was like a weak and powerless little snake, swallowed by the giant dragon’s maw and vanishing without a trace.

« Holy crap ! ! Why is yours so big ! ? »

Watching this scene that defied his understanding, the smile on the Iwagakure Ninja’s face instantly stiffened, and his complexion turned pale.

Someone tell him, what on earth is this Ninjutsu that is several times larger in scale than his Earth-Style Split ! ?

And in the spectator stands, there was complete silence at this moment.

The mockery, sympathy, worry... all such emotions and sounds had fallen into silence.

The non-Ninja spectators who had firmly believed the explanations of the Ninja spectators beside them all wore expressions of having been deceived.

Wasn’t it said that using the same Earth Release Ninjutsu in front of an Iwagakure Ninja was like showing off in front of an expert ?

Why is that Iwagakure Ninja now looking like he’s about to break down ?

Are you guys plants from Konoha, here to create contrast for the match, to suppress first and then raise it up later ? ?

In the arena, in the blink of an eye, the crack spread to the Iwagakure Ninja’s feet. At this moment, he was no longer thinking about winning or losing, but how to survive this large-scale Earth-Style Split.

Like most Ninjas, he also lacked the ability to fly.

And running to the sides to dodge was not feasible either.

Because Earth-Style Split doesn’t just attack the opponent through ground cracking, it can also cause the enemy to lose their balance through the shaking and trembling of the ground.

Once he ran to the sides to dodge, he would probably fall to the ground after a few steps, leaving him at the mercy of others.

In the midst of the crisis, he had no choice but to cast his gaze toward the Ninjas of the Nara Clan and Yamanaka Clan who were standing at the edge of the arena, ready to rescue at any moment.

After receiving this gaze, both Ninjas revealed awkward but polite smiles.

In fact, neither of them possessed the ability to fly.

Charging in to save him now would likely result in them being swallowed by the crack as well.

The secret techniques of the Nara Clan and the Yamanaka Clan were somewhat powerless in dealing with this situation.

However, besides them, there were other Proctors present who could handle this situation.
