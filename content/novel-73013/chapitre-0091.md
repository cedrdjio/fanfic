# Chapitre 91 — Convergence of All Parties!

Inside the training ground of the Hyuga Clan chief’s residence.

A ring of lush trees surrounded a plot of thick, black soil, where several wooden stakes of varying heights were driven deep into the ground.

Hyuga Clan Chief Hyuga Teruake wore a white training outfit, veins bulging at his temples and along his cheeks.

Before him, Hyuga Tsukuyomi also kept her Byakugan activated, assuming a Gentle Fist stance, ready for anything.

« Hyuga Teruake-sama, the Fourth Elder has come to visit. »

A beautiful woman, also possessing a pair of Byakugan, hurried to the edge of the training ground and called out softly.

She was Hyuga Teruake’s wife, and would also be the future mother of the two brothers, Hyuga Hiashi and Hyuga Hizashi.

« Tell him I am not here, and that I am currently accompanying Hokage-sama to receive the Daimyo who has arrived in Konoha, » Hyuga Teruake said after a moment of silence.

« Yes. »

After acknowledging, Hyuga Teruake’s wife retreated from the edge of the training ground.

On the training ground, only Hyuga Teruake and Hyuga Tsukuyomi remained.

Hyuga Teruake’s expression was complex, and in the end, he only let out a heavy sigh.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi, meanwhile, kept her head lowered, a hint of sadness in her expression.

Neither spoke, but both were well aware of the reason for the Hyuga Clan Main Branch Fourth Elder’s visit.

Caged Bird Seal.

According to the rules of the Hyuga Clan, members of the Branch House must be branded with the Caged Bird Seal when they reach a certain age.

In the past, the other Main Branch families of the Hyuga Clan had regarded Hyuga Tsukuyomi as an unremarkable, transparent figure with mediocre talent, turning a blind eye out of respect for the new Clan Chief, Hyuga Teruake.

However, now that Hyuga Tsukuyomi had displayed sufficient talent, these Main Branch members could no longer sit still.

A talented Branch House member who was not bound by the Caged Bird Seal could potentially become the culprit behind the destruction of the Hyuga Clan’s ancestral customs in the future !

« Brother... »

Hyuga Tsukuyomi was about to speak, but she was interrupted by Hyuga Teruake after only uttering the title.

« I will only demonstrate this technique once. » Hyuga Teruake’s expression returned to its initial calmness.

He assumed the starting stance of the Gentle Fist, and the Chakra within his body quickly became active.

Seeing this, Hyuga Tsukuyomi quickly cleared her mind and focused on observing the technique Hyuga Teruake was about to perform.

« Eight Trigrams Palms : Heavenly Spin ! ! »

Hyuga Teruake let out a low shout, and a massive amount of Chakra instantly erupted from his Chakra points, causing his clothes to flutter without any wind.

At the same time, his hands and feet moved in unison, driving his entire body to spin at high speed in place like a top.

The released Chakra and his high-speed spinning body encased Hyuga Teruake in a semi-circular protective shield formed by the Chakra.

Watching the execution of Heavenly Spin intently, Hyuga Tsukuyomi was lost in thought, occasionally experiencing moments of realization.

Soon, Hyuga Teruake stopped his movements, and the ground beneath his feet had formed a hemispherical crater.

« This is the secret technique of the Hyuga Clan, Eight Trigrams Palms : Heavenly Spin. It possesses a defensive power that can be called 'Absolute Defense'. »

Although he had just performed Heavenly Spin, Hyuga Teruake did not appear to have expended much effort ; clearly, this was child’s play for him.

As the Hyuga Clan Chief, while he was no peerless Genius, he possessed excellent talent and had long since reached the level of a top-tier Jonin.

« I will be meeting the Daimyo with Hashirama-sama this afternoon. If you have nothing else to do, you can stay here and train. »

After giving these instructions, Hyuga Teruake turned and left the training ground in the backyard of the residence.

In the distance, Hyuga Tsukuyomi recalled her brother’s movements and tentatively moved her hands and feet.

After completing ten rotations, her body became increasingly stable, and the deviation in her footing caused by the high-speed rotation diminished.

Next, she channeled her internal Chakra, following the scene she had just seen with her Byakugan, where the Chakra was released from the points.

For a member of the Hyuga Clan, releasing Chakra from the points was not difficult.

Another half hour passed.

A semi-sphere formed by Chakra began to take shape, stirring up currents of air within the quiet training ground.

Dozens of seconds later, the Chakra sphere dissipated.

Beneath Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s fair, sandal-clad feet, a spherical crater, similarly depressed, had appeared.

« Success. »

She lowered her raised hands and looked toward the branch of a tall tree in the distance.

A fledgling bird, seemingly startled by the airflow, chirped softly and flapped its wings, flying freely toward the distant sky.

After the conclusion of the second stage of the Joint Chunin Exams and before the start of the third stage, there was a gap of about one week.

This period was intended for the examinees to rest and recover from their injuries, as well as to allow sufficient preparation time for the spectators from all over the world.

In addition, the Kage from the major Ninja Villages also arrived in Konoha one after another over these few days.

As the Kage of a Village, they could not be away for long, so they had only sent their second-in-commands to the first two stages.

Out of respect for the Joint Chunin Exams, they would all attend during the third stage.

On the most prosperous street in Konoha.

A dark-skinned man wearing pale yellow attire walked along the side of the street.

Following behind him were Hong and Dou of Kumogakure.

Being able to have the future Second Raikage and Third Raikage follow him, the identity of this person was self-evident.

The First Raikage of Kumogakure, A !

The environment and facilities of the accommodation arranged by Konoha were very good, but unfortunately, entertainment was scarce in this era, and staying in the residence was quite boring.

Thus, the First Raikage dragged his guards, Hong and Dou, to wander around Konoha.

However, as they walked, he gradually frowned, his dark face filled with displeasure.

He said in a deep voice : « What exactly is the Hokage thinking, turning the Chunin Exams into such a spectacle for entertainment ? Is he treating Ninjas like Kabuki actors performing for money ? »

Along the way, besides seeing some nobles, he encountered more landlords and wealthy merchants from all over the world.

After all, as the leader of Kumogakure, he had encountered many wealthy people in his daily life.

For example, the construction company boss from the Land of Waves and the hot spring industry tycoon from the Land of Hot Springs were major clients for all the major Ninja Villages.

Yet now, these wealthy people, who were rarely seen on ordinary days, were gathered in Konoha.

As a Kage, A was greatly shocked by this.

If one day all these wealthy people gathered in Kumogakure, perhaps the Village’s economy would take off directly.

Curious about the arrival of these wealthy people, A inquired with a caravan leader he had been in contact with not long ago, and his expression instantly darkened.

His previous understanding of the Chunin Exams was limited to the fact that the Daimyo and nobles of various great nations would be invited to watch the competition.

Regarding this, although the First Raikage A felt it was a bit strange, he had no particular resistance to it.

After all, the funding for Kumogakure relied on the appropriations from the Daimyo of the Land of Lightning, so inviting the financial benefactors to inspect the Ninja Village’s military strength was not a bad thing.
