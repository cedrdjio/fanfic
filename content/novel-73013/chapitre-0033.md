# Chapitre 33 — The Monkey Youth and the Gloomy Youth

« I just used my Byakugan to roughly observe the Hidden Cloud Village delegation. »

Beside him, Hyuga Tsukuyomi closed her Byakugan ; after all, maintaining the Byakugan state also consumes Chakra.

« The two people at the lead should be the two strongest ones sent by Hidden Cloud Village this time. »

« The others in the group only have Chakra levels of a Chunin or Jonin, but there is one person who seems worth paying attention to. »

« Oh ? »

Upon hearing this, Senju Matsumi curiously came to Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s side and looked in the direction she was pointing.

« That sturdy-looking youth in the middle of the Hidden Cloud Village group looks to be about sixteen years old... »

« No, judging by the physical condition of these people from Kumogakure, he might be even younger. » Hyuga Tsukuyomi quickly overturned her own conjecture.

After all, she had discovered during her observation earlier that, perhaps due to environmental factors, adults in Kumogakure were generally taller than those in Konoha.

And what followed was the main point she wanted to make.

« Although he looks only about fifteen, » Hyuga Tsukuyomi chose a more moderate number, « his physical fitness and Chakra levels are already among the best in this group. »

« That person ? »

Following the direction Hyuga Tsukuyomi pointed, Senju Matsumi quickly found the target.

He saw a youth with an excessively well-developed physique looking around in the middle of the Hidden Cloud Village group.

Seeming to find listening to the conversation between the two high-ranking officials at the front boring, he even stuck a finger in his nose to pick it, then flicked a small, unidentified object onto the ground as if no one was watching.

« ... »

Watching this scene, Senju Matsumi was silent for a moment, but as he looked the youth up and down, he felt increasingly familiar with him.

His physical fitness and Chakra levels were among the best in this group ; could this person already have the strength of a Jonin ?

Just then, the Cloud Village Youth seemed to sense something and turned his head to look in the direction where Senju Matsumi was.

What met his eyes was an empty rooftop ; apart from some Konoha residents drying clothes there, there was nothing else.

Thinking it was just his imagination, the Cloud Village Youth turned his head back, pondered for a moment, and then started picking his other nostril.

On the side wall of the rooftop, the two had Chakra gathered at their feet, firmly clinging to the wall.

« I didn’t expect his intuition to be quite so sharp. »

Senju Matsumi shook his head, quickly stepped down the wall, and reached the ground.

The Kumogakure Elder and Senju Tobirama exchanged a few pleasantries, then followed the Konoha reception staff toward the inn arranged for their accommodation.

In order to continue executing the surveillance mission, Senju Matsumi also needed to come down from the rooftop and follow the group.

To avoid alerting the Kumogakure delegation, he didn’t have anything related to Ninjas on him today.

He had no Ninja tool pouch, short sword, or forehead protector on him, and his attire was no different from an ordinary resident.

Plus, Senju Matsumi wasn’t using Chakra, just walking at a distance behind the group with a calm expression ; even if seen by the Kumogakure Ninjas, it wouldn’t matter, as he would just be taken for an ordinary villager.

As for Hyuga Tsukuyomi, the Byakugan was, after all, too conspicuous a feature.

For a large Ninja Village like Kumogakure, the famous Bloodline Limits in the Ninja World were like treasures they knew by heart ; there was no chance of them failing to recognize the Byakugan like the Rogue Ninja from before.

Therefore, Hyuga Tsukuyomi remained hidden in the shadows, conducting surveillance from another perspective.

Just as the Kumogakure delegation was moving forward at a steady pace, the Cloud Village Youth who had been the focus of attention suddenly left the group and walked toward a street vendor.

Noticing the movement behind him, the future Second Raikage at the very front of the group turned his head, his brows furrowing.

« Hey, don’t run around, you brat ! Tsk... »

Seeing the other party walk away from the group as if he hadn’t heard him, he gritted his teeth, then turned to look at Senju Tobirama with a helpless expression.

« Sorry, the kid doesn’t listen ; let’s continue. He should know to return to the group on his own. »

« It’s only natural for him to be curious, visiting Konoha for the first time. »

Senju Tobirama nodded faintly, his gaze glancing subtly toward where Senju Matsumi was.

Seeing the other party receive his gaze and nod in agreement, Senju Tobirama felt relieved and continued talking with the Kumogakure Elder.

The group, which had stalled slightly due to the youth’s willfulness, continued moving forward.

Seeing this, Senju Matsumi exchanged a look with Hyuga Tsukuyomi, who was hidden in the shadows, and the two tacitly determined their next division of labor.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi left in the direction the Kumogakure group was moving, continuing the surveillance mission.

Meanwhile, Senju Matsumi pretended to also come to the surrounding vendors to browse for goods, while actually secretly paying attention to every move of the Cloud Village Youth.

Judging by the situation just now, this youth seemed to be attracted by the bustling world of Konoha and had left the group without the leader’s permission.

But no one could be sure if this was an arrangement discussed by Kumogakure themselves behind the scenes.

Using the opportunity just now to let this youth leave the group to complete some ulterior tasks.

However, watching the direction the other party was heading, Senju Matsumi couldn’t help but look strange.

He saw the Cloud Village Youth walking straight toward a mobile vendor’s cart parked on the side of the road.

On the other side of the cart stood two youths, and on the cart was steaming Oden, the aroma wafting out and making one quite appetizing.

Walking to the side of the cart, the Cloud Village Youth’s nose twitched, and a rumbling sound came from his abdomen.

From Kumogakure to Konoha, the journey had been dusty and arduous, and they had only been eating dry rations.

Now smelling such a fragrance, his stomach really couldn’t help but growl.

« This, this, and this, give me five skewers of each ! »

The Cloud Village Youth glanced at the youth standing by the cart who looked like a monkey ; after not feeling anything unusual, he reached out and pointed at the Oden on the cart.

« Alright, here you go, customer ! »

The monkey-like youth smiled and took the corresponding skewers out of the different iron grids, put them in a paper cup, and handed them to the Cloud Village Youth.

After taking the paper cup, the Cloud Village Youth did not leave immediately but looked at the other person with an inexplicable expression until the smile on the monkey-like youth’s face gradually became stiff.

Just then, the gloomy youth on the side, under the cover of the cart, forcefully stomped on the monkey-like youth’s foot.

« Oh ! Oh right, that will be two hundred yen ! »

After reacting, the monkey-like youth grimaced, enduring the pain on his foot, and randomly quoted a rough price.

« That’s actually quite cheap... Here you go. »

The Cloud Village Youth muttered a few words, took money out of his pocket, and handed it to the monkey-like youth.

Taking advantage of the time while receiving the money and the other person hadn’t left yet, the monkey-like youth asked casually.

« How old are you, big brother ? We rarely see someone as strong as you in Konoha. »

The Cloud Village Youth stood in front of the monkey-like youth, taller than the latter by a head ; it was completely the difference between an adult and a child.
