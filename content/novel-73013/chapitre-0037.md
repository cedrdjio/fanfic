# Chapitre 37 — The Path to Self-Destruction

« Stay calm. For now, the only option is to act as if we discovered nothing. Do not act rashly from here on out. »

Hong sighed, but quickly adjusted his mindset, while also soothing the agitated Dou.

The relationship between the two was that of master and disciple ; Dou was Hong’s direct apprentice, similar to the relationship between Senju Tobirama and Hiruzen Sarutobi.

This relationship was much closer than that between a Jonin instructor and a Genin.

Once Hong became the Second Raikage in the future, Dou would become his Raikage guard, just as he was currently the guard for the First Raikage.

Calling him a guard was more like calling him a Raikage-in-waiting, currently in a probationary and internship period.

« I have heard that Senju Hashirama is a generous man, but his brother, Senju Tobirama, is ruthless and decisive. »

« We have already made them suspicious. If there are any further unusual movements, I fear... »

Deep fear filled the eyes of the Kumogakure Elder, as if he were recalling some terrifying past event.

The Elder, whose physique was as robust as a tiger’s, now wore such a terrified expression at the mere mention of them, leaving both Hong and Dou quite confused.

« Elder, are those two brothers from the Senju Clan really that terrifying ? » Hong asked with a frown.

It was Senju Tobirama who had come to the Konoha gates to welcome them today, and they had conversed at length along the way.

For a Ninja, such a short period of time was sufficient to observe and collect a significant amount of information.

From his perspective, Senju Tobirama looked burly by Konoha standards, but in the Hidden Cloud Village, which was teeming with muscular men, he was simply not impressive.

« Hong, you do not understand. That scene, where they blotted out the sky, cannot be described in words. »

Upon hearing this, the Kumogakure Elder only shook his head with a bitter expression.

In his mind, a flash of a massive figure he had glimpsed from afar appeared, as terrifying as a natural disaster.

« Since ancient times, the Land of Fire has been rich in resources. If it hadn’t been for the Senju Clan, perhaps it would be us from the Hidden Cloud Village who had established a Ninja village in the Land of Fire. »

« And do not underestimate them. The people of the Senju Clan do not look strong, yet they possess special physiques, and their strength is no less than ours. »

« Is that so ? I didn’t get that impression... »

Dou muttered a few words ; judging by his expression, he was clearly unconvinced.

« Do not be discouraged ; there will be opportunities later. What is more important now is the upcoming meeting, » said the Kumogakure Elder.

The meeting between Konoha and the Hidden Cloud Village was scheduled for the afternoon of the second day after the Hidden Cloud delegation arrived in Konoha.

At noon, Matsumi and Tsukuyomi, who had been monitoring the Hidden Cloud delegation for a day and a night, successfully completed their mission and returned to the Hokage Office to report.

Throughout the day and night, the two had taken shifts, and since the Hidden Cloud Village had remained relatively well-behaved, neither of them was overly exhausted.

As for the monitoring tasks for the following days, other Ninjas would carry them out.

After all, the Hidden Cloud delegation might stay in Konoha for several days, and the C-rank mission that Matsumi and Tsukuyomi had accepted only covered the first twenty-four hours after the delegation’s arrival.

At this moment, the atmosphere inside the Hokage Office was a bit crowded.

In addition to Senju Hashirama sitting upright at the desk, Senju Tobirama was standing to the side with his arms crossed.

Furthermore, Senju Tobirama’s students, staff from various Konoha departments, and the heads of the various Ninja clans were all gathered there.

Among them, the one with the pineapple hairstyle and stubble on his chin, looking somewhat lazy and laid-back, was the current head of the Nara Clan.

A large, stout man with a big belly and a naive, charming smile, wearing armor with a huge character for « Food » on his chest, was the current head of the Akimichi Clan and the father of Akimichi Torifu.

In addition, the head of the Yamanaka Clan, the head of the Uchiha Clan, the head of the Hyuga Clan, Hyuga Teruake, and the head of the Sarutobi Clan, Sarutobi Sasuke, were basically all in the Hokage Office.

Under the gaze of so many people, Senju Matsumi calmly finished reporting on the monitoring mission.

After hearing Matsumi’s description, everyone present had different expressions.

The head of the Uchiha Clan, being directly involved in the « Ninja Academy Gate Incident » from yesterday, was so angry that his face turned livid.

« These damn Hidden Cloud savages, are they coming for me ? »

The head of the Uchiha Clan looked about the same age as the head of the Hyuga Clan, Hyuga Teruake—only in his twenties—appearing relatively young among the group of clan heads.

However, the Uchiha Clan Head glanced at the two true decision-makers present and had to swallow his anger.

« It seems the Hidden Cloud Village has no sincerity for this meeting. The so-called Joint Chunin Exams may just be a pretext. I hope Hokage-sama will reconsider. »

The Uchiha Clan Head said this in a deep voice, then stood in place with a cold face, saying nothing more.

Beside Hashirama, Senju Tobirama glanced at the Uchiha Clan Head, rarely agreeing with an Uchiha’s viewpoint.

A flash of killing intent crossed his crimson eyes, and a sharp, chilling aura erupted as he said coldly.

« Big Brother, the Hidden Cloud has already embarked on a path to self-destruction ! »

« Ugh, what a headache... »

Hashirama rested both hands on the desk, rubbed his temples, and looked at the Nara Clan Head nearby.

« Clan Head Kano, what is your opinion ? »

During the early days of Konoha, when everything was waiting to be rebuilt, the head of the Nara Clan, Nara Kano, had actively offered advice and suggestions, and many of his constructive proposals had been adopted.

Therefore, Hashirama now regarded him as a strategist, and whenever he encountered issues where everyone’s opinions differed, he would ask for his perspective.

Upon hearing this, Nara Kano knew he could not slack off and stand on the sidelines, so he touched the stubble on his chin with his right hand and said.

« I believe the Hidden Cloud Village’s intention to hold the Joint Chunin Exams is genuine. After all, they didn’t just invite us ; they invited other Ninja villages as well, » Nara Kano said seriously.

« As for the matter at the Ninja Academy... »

He glanced at the silent Uchiha Clan Head, hesitated for a moment, and then continued.

« Their goal was likely not the Uchiha ; it was just that the Uchiha child happened to be alone at the time, which gave them an opportunity. »

« In that case, their target must be Konoha’s bloodline clans, » Senju Tobirama said with a frown.

« No, our Konoha is currently stronger than the Hidden Cloud, and with Hokage-sama presiding, they shouldn’t have the guts, » Nara Kano said.

« According to our spies planted in the Hidden Cloud, in recent years, the Hidden Cloud has been imitating us by establishing a Ninja Academy and perfecting their education system. »

« However, after several years, they have not been able to achieve very good results. »

« Approaching the Ninja Academy this time was likely just to spy on our Konoha’s educational intelligence. »

« As for the Joint Chunin Exams, I believe we should still participate. »

Hearing this, Sarutobi Sasuke felt confused and asked : « Knowing full well that their intentions are not good, why... »

« For the Joint Chunin Exams, I fear the Hidden Cloud’s biggest goal is to understand the military strength of each Ninja village. Compared to war, this is a more moderate method. »

« Kano, do you mean that if the Hidden Cloud discovers during these Chunin Exams that other Ninja villages are far inferior to them, they might brew up a war ? And if they find the villages are evenly matched, they won’t act rashly ? »

Senju Hashirama realized.

« That is exactly what I mean. Therefore, I believe it is best for us to participate ; we can flex our muscles in this relatively peaceful way. »

« Furthermore, this is also an opportunity for us to understand the strength of other Ninja villages. »
