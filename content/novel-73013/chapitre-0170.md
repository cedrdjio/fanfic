# Chapitre 170 — Confrontation! Three Tsuchikage Gathered!

Senju Matsuaki sheathed his sword, fine beads of sweat seeping from his forehead.

« Hikari, Tsukuyomi, prepare to Seal the Four-Tails ! »

The two women’s eyes still reflected the blazing firelight ; while inwardly stunned, they quickly made preparations to Seal the Four-Tails, beginning to set up the Sealing Jutsu formation around the fallen beast.

Complex runes lit up on the ground, and layers of sealing chains coiled around the body of the Four-Tails.

« Who... exactly are you... »

The Four-Tails struggled weakly a few times, its gaze locked firmly onto Senju Matsuaki, filled with fear and confusion.

But the terrifying flames from just moments ago had already left it severely injured, and in the end, it helplessly closed its eyes, completely sealed.

« Finally, it’s done. »

Senju Matsuaki clapped his hands and breathed a sigh of relief.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi lowered her hands from the hand Seals and let out a long breath : « The Four-Tails' Chakra has been completely suppressed ; the sealing is a success. »

Uchiha Hikari held the container for sealing the Four-Tails—a sealing scroll—in her hands.

This was a container specially crafted by Uzumaki Mito and Senju Hashirama during this period for sealing Tailed Beasts, far superior to ordinary storage scrolls.

Senju Matsuaki gently patted the Zanpakuto at his waist, his gaze meeting the several figures rapidly flying in from the distance.

The three figures in the distance approached rapidly, landing on a huge rock nearby in the blink of an eye.

The leader was a short, elderly man wearing a Tsuchikage hat, with a resolute face and eyes revealing the steadiness and sharpness of one long accustomed to the battlefield.

Senju Matsuaki had caught a glimpse of this person from afar once before.

The First Tsuchikage, Ishikawa.

To his left stood a thin man, his entire body wrapped in white bandages, with only a pair of sharp eyes exposed, and a faint fluctuation of Chakra radiating from his body.

He was more familiar with this person : the future Second Tsuchikage, Mu.

To the right was a boy who looked to be only in his teens, with a round face, large eyes, and a nose unusually large compared to others.

This person was the future Third Tsuchikage, Ōnoki.

But at this moment, he was merely a Jonin in whom his elders placed high hopes, still a long way from the level of a Kage.

« Wood Release... and those eyes... »

Ishikawa’s gaze swept across the cracked ground beneath Senju Matsuaki’s feet, then passed over the Mangekyo Sharingan in Uchiha Hikari’s eyes.

Finally, it fixed on the katana that had just been sheathed, his pupils slightly contracting.

His gaze finally landed on the white-haired, red-eyed youth leading them.

Although years had passed, he still recognized him at a glance.

There was no other reason ; at the Chunin Exams, this boy had given him too great a shock !

« Senju Matsuaki, you people of Konoha dare to capture a Tailed Beast within the borders of the Land of Earth without authorization ! »

Ishikawa’s voice was low and dignified, his Chakra already surging within his body.

Senju Matsuaki met the First Tsuchikage Ishikawa’s gaze without backing down.

« First Tsuchikage, I didn’t expect you to still remember me. »

« The Four-Tails has always been a wild Tailed Beast roaming outside the major Ninja villages ; it is not your village’s private property. How can you speak of unauthorized action ? »

« Nonsense ! »

Before Ishikawa could speak, the bandaged man, Mu, had already shouted coldly from the side.

« The Four-Tails has long entrenched itself in the lava zone within our Land of Earth and has long been regarded by us in Iwagakure as a strategic resource of our nation ! »

« You Konoha Ninjas trespassing into our country to capture a Tailed Beast is a serious violation against the Land of Earth ! »

« Oh ? »

Senju Matsuaki raised an eyebrow, his tone remaining calm.

« Then, may I ask, since the Four-Tails is your country’s strategic resource, why did it already have battle scars when I arrived ? Presumably, your village has already attempted to capture it, right ? »

A trace of imperceptible embarrassment flashed in Mu’s eyes, but he still retorted coldly.

« So what ? Even if we haven’t succeeded yet, that doesn’t mean outsiders can interfere ! »

« Furthermore, your successful capture of the Four-Tails—isn’t that just picking up leftovers after we injured it ! ? »

« Heh. »

Senju Matsuaki chuckled, « Following that logic, as long as a rabbit runs into your yard, it belongs to you, and no one else can touch it ? »

The logic is simple ; if you want to say the Four-Tails belongs to Iwagakure, you must at least provide evidence to prove that Iwagakure actually controlled the Four-Tails.

If you haven’t even managed to subdue or Seal the Four-Tails, yet claim it is Iwagakure’s property, it’s a bit laughable.

As for picking up leftovers...

It seems Mu has somewhat overestimated the damage they inflicted on the Four-Tails and underestimated the Four-Tails' recovery ability.

The Four-Tails he fought just now was clearly not hindered by any injuries.

Mu’s chest heaved, and he shouted angrily : « You ! ! »

« Enough. »

First Tsuchikage Ishikawa raised his hand to stop Mu, stepped forward, and looked directly at Senju Matsuaki.

« Young man, sharp words cannot change the facts. »

« This is the Land of Earth. Hand over the Four-Tails, and I can pretend nothing happened. »

As these words fell, the Chakra on Ishikawa’s body erupted suddenly, and a heavy sense of oppression bore down like a giant boulder.

As one of the first Kage who pacified the chaotic world, Ishikawa’s strength was outstanding among the Kage, and his Aura was enough to make ordinary Ninjas' legs go weak.

Yet Senju Matsuaki remained motionless, not even his breathing disrupted in the slightest.

Uchiha Hikari and Hyuga Tsukuyomi behind him also remained unchanged, as if that oppression were merely a breeze.

« Tsuchikage. »

Senju Matsuaki’s smile slowly faded, and a hint of sharpness entered his eyes, « I think you might have misunderstood one thing. »

He reached out and pointed to the ground beneath his feet.

« This is indeed the Land of Earth, yes. However, the Tailed Beasts were born long before these countries were founded. »

« Grandpa, stop wasting words on him ! »

Ōnoki, who was on the side, finally couldn’t help but speak, pointing at Senju Matsuaki and shouting.

« They stole our Tailed Beast, so we’ll just take it back ! I don’t believe three people can beat our entire Iwagakure ! »

Senju Matsuaki looked down at this future Third Tsuchikage and couldn’t help but smile.

Compared to his future elderly state—white-haired and frequently throwing out his back—the current Ōnoki could be considered quite full of youthful vigor.

« It seems you have given up on using those untenable arguments to claim sovereignty over the Four-Tails and want to take it by force ? »

« That’s easier, actually ; it saves me the trouble of wasting my breath... »

Senju Matsuaki’s tone gradually turned cold.

« Kid, you... »

Ishikawa’s brows furrowed deeply.

His intuition told him that something seemed wrong.

This brat—wasn’t the reason he was able to subdue the Four-Tails because they had already injured it beforehand, and, combined with the wonders of Wood Release, he was able to pick up the leftovers ?

But what exactly was this vague feeling of unease ! ?

As he pondered, Senju Matsuaki’s right hand had already rested gently on the hilt of the sword at his waist.

Just this action.

Just the moment the hilt made contact with his palm.

An unspeakably terrifying high temperature radiated from the scabbard, the surrounding air visibly distorting, and the rocks beneath their feet began to make faint crackling sounds.
