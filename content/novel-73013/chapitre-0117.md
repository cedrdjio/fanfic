# Chapitre 117 — Shocking the Whole Audience!

After taking a quick look, he saw the situation inside the waiting room clearly.

Most of the examinees were awake, either chatting with their companions or leaning against the railing of the stands, waiting for the assessment to begin.

However, a small number of examinees seemed not to have rested well and were currently sleeping very deeply and soundly, with snoring sounds coming from them from time to time.

Looking at the Cloud Ninja examinees sleeping in a heap, the Proctor’s face darkened. He stepped forward, identified them, and then dragged two of them out separately.

He was very professional, waking only the two examinees for the current assessment without disturbing the sleep of the others.

After shaking the two awake with force, he felt his blood pressure rising.

The violent shaking caused these two Cloud Ninjas to open their eyes, looking at the grim-faced Proctor before them with sleepy eyes.

« You two, it is your turn to enter the field for the assessment. If you do not enter within five minutes, it will be considered a forfeiture ! »

« The two of us ? »

The two, just awakened, were clearly a bit bewildered. They looked back at Dou and the others who were still sleeping soundly, then looked at each other.

After urging the two to enter the field for the assessment, the Proctor quickly left the examinee waiting room.

Seeing this, the two Cloud Ninjas dared not delay and walked down the stairs of the waiting room stands towards the center of the Arena.

Inside the waiting room, Senju Matsuaki looked at their backs, a trace of sympathy appearing in his eyes.

According to the recipe of Hiruzen Sarutobi and Danzo Shimura, there were three types of potions in the oden they had eaten.

The first one took effect the fastest, which was the sleeping drug that had made them drowsy and weak just now.

Even though they were woken up, the effects of the drug still remained, which would leave their limbs weak and their minds groggy.

Of course, in Senju Matsuaki’s view, the most lethal ones were actually the last two potions...

One was a Yang-replenishing drug, which could make the two regain their vigor in public in the Arena.

The second was a laxative, which could turn the two into « jet warriors » in the Arena, unleashing a torrent.

After walking a few steps, the two Cloud Ninjas suddenly felt something strange.

A groggy mind could still be dismissed as just having woken up and not recovered yet, but the feeling of weak hands and feet at this moment was not normal.

And even more bizarrely, they simultaneously felt a hot current converging toward their lower bodies, and a feeling of blockage and pain in their abdomens as if their intestines were twisting.

Oh no, this seems to be...

Their pupils both shrank violently, yet at this moment, they already had one foot in the examination field ; the arrow was on the bowstring and had to be fired.

Inside the Arena, the two Cloud Ninjas were already in their respective positions.

But what puzzled the audience on the stands was that the movements of these two seemed a bit strange.

The two stood facing each other, their faces flushed red, both bending at the waist and clenching their legs, their entire bodies trembling slightly.

« Could it be, you too ! » x 2

The audience could see the abnormality of the two, and naturally, they also saw each other’s appearance clearly.

« I just said why it felt a bit wrong earlier ; there must be something wrong with what we ate at noon ! »

« I feel like I’m rock hard, and that stuff is about to overflow ! »

« Damn, why do you think Boss Dou gave us these things ? Coma, rock hard, and diarrhea... could it be that Boss is... »

As they said this, their expressions appeared somewhat bizarre and terrified.

However, time was limited, avoiding their conversation sliding into an irredeemable abyss.

« The assessment begins ! ! »

The cold voice of Senju Tobirama rang out, announcing the official start of the assessment.

Not only were they late, they were also performing performance art with strange postures in the exam field, and now they were whispering about who knows what, showing no regard for the Chunin Exams.

Hearing that the assessment had begun, the two Cloud Ninjas, whose conversation was cut short, had no choice but to fight.

They both knew in their hearts that with their current physical condition, they clearly could not sustain a normal fight.

In their plan, they would just go through the motions for a few rounds, and then one of them would deliberately get defeated to end the fight.

However, both were competitive by nature, and neither was willing to be the one to admit defeat.

By the time they finally realized they couldn’t drag it out any longer, the drug’s effect no longer allowed them to forfeit...

The two Cloud Ninjas only felt their consciousness gradually blurring, and the world in their eyes seemed to be covered with a faint pink layer.

The barrier that they had been keeping tight to avoid a massive release failed, and it gushed out.

At the same time, amidst the exchange of punches and kicks, they suddenly felt a tremendous attraction from the other’s dark muscles and rough palms.

« Brother, you smell so good~ » x 2

This was their last thought before losing consciousness.

Accompanied by a dull thud, the two Cloud Ninjas fell to the ground, hugging each other tightly as if intertwined, kicking up a cloud of dust.

In the front, they were performing a top-tier fencing match, while in the back, it was an uncontrollable landslide of mud flowing out.

« ... »

This shocking scene plunged the entire audience into an eerie silence.

Even the audience who had previously scolded the two for moving slowly as if they were fixing the match were now just gaping, unable to speak, staring blankly.

Undoubtedly, the scene before them was more shocking than any other battle in the past two days.

« I have traveled north and south for many years, what haven’t I seen ? » an experienced merchant sighed, « But this, I have truly never seen before ! »

On the stands of the examinee waiting room, Senju Matsuaki slowly closed his eyes, not forgetting to reach out and cover the eyes of Hyuga Tsukuyomi.

Brother Hiruzen, Brother Danzo, this prosperous age is as you wished...

He sighed with a moved expression.

*Clap* —

In an aisle near the spectator stands, Hiruzen Sarutobi and Danzo Shimura, who had changed back into their Ninja attire at some unknown time, looked at each other and high-fived in tacit understanding.

« No, you guys, this... »

Senju Tobirama was greatly shocked and spoke up, wanting to pull the derailed Chunin Exams back on track, but for a moment, he didn’t know how to start.

The monkey and the others only said that by combining different potions, they could weaken the combat power of the Cloud Ninja and Sand Ninja examinees.

They never said there would be such a shocking scene as this, right ? ?

He had witnessed countless battles from the Sengoku period to the present, but he had never seen such a situation.

« Ahem, both sides have lost their combat capability simultaneously, the battle is over ! Medical personnel, please take the two examinees away from the exam field for treatment ! Proctor, clean up the field ! »

Senju Tobirama quickly adjusted his state and made proper arrangements for this sudden incident.

Several medical personnel waiting outside the field entered reluctantly, their faces showing disgust as they placed the two on stretchers and carried them away.

Then, a Proctor entered the field and performed Earth Release Jutsu, covering up all the filth on the ground that made people frown.

« I am feeling a bit unwell, excuse me... »

The Daimyo of the Land of Lightning twitched at the corners of his mouth, greeted the other Daimyos and Kages with a stiff expression, and then waved to the servants waiting not far away.

Then, surrounded by his servants, he slowly exited.

He didn’t want to stay in this spectator stand for even one more second !
