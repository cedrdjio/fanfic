# Chapitre 136 — Frontier Tavern, That Man!

Limited-Time Character Summoning Card ?

Golden Finger abilities that summon characters are not uncommon, so it was easy for Senju Matsuaki to understand.

There were two points that needed consideration : price and effect.

After a forty percent discount, it cost three thousand points. For him at this moment, it wasn’t enough to break the bank, but it was by no means a small sum.

As for the effect, based on the conventions of the Requisition Golden Finger platform, it would at least be commensurate with the cost.

For example, the Golden Fishing Hook, which had an original price of ten thousand points and a promotional price of five thousand, was in a sense also a random-type item.

What it brought for Senju Matsuaki was the Tremor-Tremor Fruit, which was enough to cause a leap in strength !

And this one had an original price of five thousand points, and it was also time-limited.

The character summoned certainly wouldn’t be some powerless person from a mundane world.

At the very least, it shouldn’t be a problem for it to help him survive the crisis of Uchiha Madara attacking the village.

« Our Requisition Golden Finger platform has clearly marked prices, treats everyone fairly, and everyone who uses it praises it ! »

Of course, Senju Matsuaki did not expect the summoned character’s strength to be overly outrageous.

Fantasizing that the summoned object could shatter the Ninja World with a flick of a finger and deal with all the Ōtsutsuki hiding in the Ninja World or arriving from beyond the heavens was clearly unrealistic.

« I’ll take it ! »

Senju Matsuaki gritted his teeth and made the decision decisively.

As the point balance rapidly decreased, golden ripples appeared in the air before him.

Immediately after, a golden card, scaled up several times from what he had seen on the platform, emerged from the ripples and floated slowly in front of Senju Matsuaki.

He reached out his right hand and held the golden card between his index and middle fingers, feeling a warm and gentle sensation upon contact.

At the center of the card, some phantom images flickered and changed faintly.

Some of the phantoms could be vaguely identified as human, but there were also some strange-shaped phantoms that seemed to be non-human creatures.

After examining it for a moment, Senju Matsuaki put the card away.

This limited-time character summoning card would be one of his most important trump cards, and he had to keep it safe until the necessary moment arrived.

After requisitioning this limited-time character summoning card, Senju Matsuaki browsed through the other Golden Finger items.

The other Golden Finger items currently on sale were not within Senju Matsuaki’s selection range.

However, this did not mean he wouldn’t go to requisition other non-promotional Golden Fingers.

Although full-priced ones weren’t as appealing as promotional ones, he couldn’t just refuse to spend anything on other non-promotional Golden Fingers just to wait for the right sale.

In the future, there would be Uchiha Madara attacking the village, the First Shinobi World War...

Now, it was naturally time to seize every opportunity to improve his strength.

At this moment, Senju Matsuaki already had a preliminary requisition target.

This Golden Finger was not unfamiliar ; it was an old acquaintance.

[Requisition · Check-in System : By checking in at specific people or specific locations, you can obtain random rewards related to the check-in target ; you can check in once a day. Price : 500 points per day.]

The last time he requisitioned this Golden Finger, it brought Senju Matsuaki extremely considerable gains.

A massive amount of Chakra, Asura Chakra (pure Chakra version), and also Wood Release...

These rewards obtained from checking in allowed Senju Matsuaki’s strength to take a leap forward in a short period.

This was also the foundation that allowed him to compete on the same stage with the future Third Raikage and Third Kazekage, who were much older than him and had already begun to make a name for themselves in the Ninja World.

Although there was no promotional price now, this Check-in System was still a highly cost-effective Golden Finger.

Of course, to requisition this Golden Finger, one had to use it with a goal and a plan.

If one were to spend all their points at once to redeem it and then check in aimlessly, that would be pure gambling behavior.

Senju Matsuaki had already locked onto his target for checking in and had a preliminary plan.

Therefore, he did not hesitate.

With a thought and a wave of his hand, he directly requisitioned two days of the Check-in System, spending another one thousand points.

After closing the light screen of the Requisition Golden Finger platform before him, Senju Matsuaki jumped down, his feet landing steadily on the ground.

« I haven’t seen Second Uncle for several days ; it’s time to go maintain our relationship. »

Caring for the empty-nester Second Uncle, who else but him !

The border of the Land of Fire.

As the geographical location gradually moved away from the most central and prosperous areas of the Land of Fire, the forests became sparse, the rocks turned red, and the environment became increasingly desolate.

In a small town on the border, life was quiet, and the houses were low and scattered.

Travelers and merchants came and went in a hurry, stopping here for a break when they rested.

Most of these people would first walk into a restaurant with their arms around each other’s shoulders, and after filling their stomachs, they would swarm into the tavern next door.

Having a few drinks after a meal and chatting nonsense was one of the few entertainments they had on their journey.

However, since they still had to continue their journey afterward, a little drink was fine, but getting drunk meant waiting to be scolded by the team leader.

Inside the tavern, the topics gradually converged from all sorts of things to the major events that had recently occurred in the Ninja World.

« You all should have heard more or less about that major event that recently happened in the Land of Fire, right ? »

« Are you talking about that thing in Konoha ? »

« I heard a colleague mention it last time, but I didn’t hear it very clearly. What happened ? »

A large portion of these travelers and merchants were not from the Land of Fire, and their news was not very well-informed.

Regarding the major events that had recently occurred in the Land of Fire and Konoha, they had only heard a few fragments and didn’t know much.

Hearing someone bring up this topic, they were naturally curious and began to discuss it in a clamor.

In the corner of the tavern, a man wearing a dark blue kimono with long black hair was sitting at an empty table resting.

He was expressionless, his gaze distant, as if he were seeing far away through the walls of the tavern.

Because he exuded an Aura of « keep away » from his whole body, even though he hadn’t ordered anything, the shopkeeper didn’t dare to come up and ask.

When he heard « Land of Fire, » the man still had no reaction.

But when he caught the keyword « Konoha, » his brows suddenly furrowed, and a sharp light flashed in his cold eyes.

His attention had already returned from the distant elsewhere to this tavern, which was buzzing with lively discussion.

« Konoha recently held a large-scale Joint Chunin Exams, with the Daimyo and Kage of the Five Great Nations all in attendance. That scene... tsk tsk tsk. »

« You make it sound so amazing, but you haven’t been there yourself, have you ? Don’t tell me you’re just spouting nonsense to us ? »

« What kind of talk is that ! Although I haven’t been there, my uncle’s father-in-law’s sister’s neighbor has ! »

« Stop talking nonsense, hurry up and tell us what was there ! »

For ordinary people, what they paid more attention to was naturally the grand spectacle.

The huge Arena, the rich recreational facilities, the dazzling array of goods, the food street overflowing with fragrance...

« Boring... »

Hearing this, the man in the dark blue kimono felt bored and indifferent, stood up, and prepared to leave.

He thought it was some major event, but it was just a group of brats wanting to become Chunin playing house.

And, to think Hashirama was actually wasting time on a mere Chunin Exams, and even chatting and laughing with the Kage of other ninja villages ; he was truly becoming more and more negligent in his duties !

After a cold snort, the man walked toward the tavern door.

However, just as he was about to step out of the tavern, a voice made his footsteps falter.

« I heard there was also a Genin named Senju Matsuaki who won the final. I heard that, just like the Hokage, he awakened something... uh, Tree Release ? »

Tree Release ?

Wood Release !

The man’s pupils shrank slightly, his Aura leaked out a little, and the originally noisy tavern instantly became quiet.

Although they didn’t know what had happened, the people in the tavern instinctively shivered.

« Senju Matsuaki, Wood Release... interesting. »

The man instantly returned to his former indifferent, arrogant expression and strode away toward the door.
