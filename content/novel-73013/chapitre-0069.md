# Chapitre 69 — The Killing Intent of Sunagakure's Yan!

Somewhere in the primeval forest.

Chiyo and Ebizo crouched before a fallen tree trunk, beside which lay a puppet that had begun to take humanoid shape.

The puppet’s body was not covered by a cloak, revealing all its mechanisms and parts, which actually made it look less terrifying than when it was cloaked.

« Ebizo, hurry up. We still need to rendezvous with that guy, Yan. »

Chiyo held the metal tools for assembling the puppet, and seeing Ebizo’s slow movements, her anger flared.

« Sister, my right hand is still injured... »

Ebizo’s expression was bitter as he pointed with his left hand to the bandage wrapped around his right.

His talent for the Puppet Technique was originally not as good as his sister’s, and that was even more true now that his hand was injured.

For Chiyo, the Puppet Technique was her main lethal weapon, while for Ebizo, it was merely a secondary skill.

« By the way, didn’t you say you were reluctant to rendezvous with Yan earlier, Sister ? Why have you changed your mind now ? » Ebizo asked curiously.

« Have you already forgotten about that beating you just took ? »

Chiyo tapped away at the puppet with her tools and materials. She didn’t even look up when she heard Ebizo’s question, though her tone held a hint of coldness.

Rendezvousing with her senior brother Yan as soon as possible during the second stage of the exam was a requirement set by her teacher, Shamon.

Chiyo had not been very willing to fulfill this request at first.

Although they shared the same master, her relationship with Yan was not harmonious.

Because of his exceptional talent, Yan was cold and arrogant, and usually did not like to interact with his peers.

Chiyo herself was also a competitive person ; while she acknowledged Yan’s talent and strength, she disliked his insufferably arrogant attitude.

Therefore, although there hadn’t been any major conflicts between them, they couldn’t be considered friendly fellow disciples.

However, the situation had changed now.

The ability to easily suppress the two of them single-handedly was beyond her capabilities.

She carried some of the hopes of Sunagakure this time, and she could not act on personal likes or dislikes.

With a powerful enemy in the exam who could eliminate them at any time, sticking together was the only logical course of action.

« Of course I haven’t forgotten. »

Speaking of this, expressions of grief and indignation inevitably appeared in Ebizo’s eyes.

« We even gave him the chess piece, but he still beat me up. It would be one thing if he beat me, but he wasn’t even trying to eliminate me. He just beat me up and then turned and walked away. He’s a total psychopath ! ! »

Thinking of that beating just now, which caused little damage but was extremely insulting, Ebizo issued a strong complaint.

« Perhaps geniuses like that all have some eccentricities. »

Looking at the bruises on her younger brother Ebizo, Chiyo’s eyes held both anger and a hint of oddity.

The good news was that they hadn’t been eliminated by the opponent, nor had they suffered any serious injuries.

The bad news was that they had been beaten up, and the other party had walked away without a care.

However, thinking of Yan, the greatest genius in Sunagakure, who always had a « keep away » vibe, Chiyo attributed these strange points to the eccentricities of geniuses.

« Doesn’t that guy Yan think participating in the Chunin Exams is boring ? Then we’ll find him a challenging opponent. »

A sharp light flashed in Chiyo’s eyes, and her movements tinkering with the puppet did not slow down at all due to their conversation.

« Although Yan doesn’t usually think much of us, he has a strong sense of belonging and honor regarding the Village. I assume he will win back some face for us. »

Ebizo was a smart person himself, so he nodded thoughtfully upon hearing this.

However, remembering the scene from earlier, he said with some hesitation : « But Sister, I always feel that something is not right with that kid’s strength. What if Yan is no match for him ? »

Hearing this, Chiyo immediately looked at him with the eyes one would use for a fool.

« We were only Chunin before, but Yan was a Jonin. If that kid were stronger than Yan, how could he still be unknown ? »

« Hiss, that makes sense... »

Ebizo frowned and pondered Chiyo’s words, finding them very reasonable, so he had to suppress the unease in his heart.

Although the siblings were not as talented as Yan, they were considered outstanding among their peers and had become Chunin by passing the Sunagakure Chunin Exams two years ago.

The reason they were participating in the Joint Chunin Exams as Genin this time was the same tactic used by Yan.

However, they didn’t have much dissatisfaction with this.

They enjoyed the best resources provided by Sunagakure on a daily basis, so how could they shrink back when it was time to win face for the Village ?

« Did you encounter Ninjas from other Villages ? »

A voice rang out abruptly from beside the two, breaking the atmosphere that had just settled down.

« When ! ? »

Ebizo, who was crouching on the ground helping Chiyo handle puppet parts, was startled, thinking an enemy had snuck up beside them unnoticed.

It wasn’t until he turned back and saw who it was that he breathed a sigh of relief and put away the Kunai he had almost pulled out.

« It’s Yan... by the way, when did you arrive ? »

Ebizo first breathed a sigh of relief, but soon his heart tightened again.

He and his sister Chiyo had been squatting here, repairing the puppet while talking behind Yan’s back ; it would be too embarrassing if the other party had been listening from the side all along.

« Just now. »

Yan watched Ebizo’s expression change several times and was somewhat confused, but not wanting to meddle, he didn’t ask further.

At this moment, he was more concerned with another aspect.

Chiyo and Ebizo were previously Chunin ; logically, they shouldn’t have ended up in this state on the first day of the second stage of the exam.

Chiyo’s puppet was still lying on the ground, having just undergone major surgery, and it seemed it hadn’t been long since it was repaired.

And Ebizo looked like he had been beaten up, with bandages wrapped around his arm.

« I had a brief skirmish with a Kumogakure Ninja earlier. He was also a Jonin. It seems Kumogakure has someone with the same idea as Teacher Shamon. So, did you encounter such an opponent just now ? » Yan asked.

« No, we did not encounter a Jonin from another Village. »

Chiyo and Ebizo looked at each other, and finally, Ebizo stated the facts with a somewhat embarrassed expression.

« The opponent was a kid about seven or eight years old. If he were a Jonin at this age, it would be impossible for us not to have heard of him. »

« Oh ? » Yan began to get curious. « Then what are his features ? »

« Silver hair, red eyes... » Ebizo described the physical features he could remember.

« His clothes had the emblem of the Senju Clan ; he is a member of the Senju Clan, » Chiyo stated the key information she had noticed.

Upon hearing this, Yan lowered his head to think for a moment, then a look of realization appeared on his face.

« That kid is Senju Matsuaki of the Senju Clan. He is highly regarded by the Hokage, Senju Hashirama, who even personally became his team-leading Jonin. »

Being groomed as the future Third Kazekage, he was already able to access some of the Village’s intelligence.

Before coming to Konoha this time, he had also specifically learned about the Genin in Konoha worth noting.

« Once Sunagakure and Konoha become hostile in the future, such a genius will be our nightmare... »

Recalling how he had been beaten into having absolutely no power to fight back in an instant, Ebizo muttered.

Upon hearing this, a cold glint flashed in Yan’s yellow eyes, and his expression was cold and decisive.

« In which direction did he leave ? Take me to find him. I will kill him in this forest ! »

The killing intent in his words made both Chiyo and Ebizo look at him sideways.

« But... » Chiyo hesitated, « What if the Hokage investigates this matter afterward ? »

« It doesn’t matter. Given the gap in our strength, crushing him would be like crushing an ant ; I will not leave a single trace ! » Yan said coldly.
