# Chapitre 62 — Nothing Else, Just Skill

In the exam hall, the noise gradually subsided.

Apart from the sound of breathing, there was only the faint friction of the examinees' elbows against the wooden desks, and the swishing sound of pens scribbling away.

Inside the exam hall, there was one Proctor at the front and one at the back. Every so often, they would walk around to patrol near the examinees before returning to their original positions to stand.

Being in such an atmosphere, Senju Matsuaki felt a bit dazed for a moment.

The scene at this moment gave him the feeling that he had traveled back in time.

Just as the Proctor from the Nara Clan had said, these questions were of a decent difficulty, but for a qualified Ninja, they were by no means difficult.

Therefore, even the muscular, dark-skinned brute from Kumogakure didn’t have much hesitation with the pen in his hand.

However, just as most people reached the latter half of the test, their movements gradually slowed down.

The questions for this Chunin Exams written test were quite balanced. The first half focused more on questions with objective, fixed answers.

For example, the difference between the Clone Technique and the Water Clone Technique, as well as their respective advantages—this essay question could be instantly solved by an experienced Ninja.

The latter half, however, consisted of questions with more subjective answers. Some individual questions even rose to the level of belief and the Ninja Way.

A large portion of the Genin, upon seeing such questions, were immediately in a state of bewilderment, scratching their heads.

Most of them became Ninjas for simple reasons : either their elders were Ninjas, or they yearned for the power and status of a Ninja.

But when they intended to write this down, these people always felt that such an answer probably wouldn’t be well-received.

So, after hesitating for a moment, someone finally started to resort to small tricks.

« Konoha truly lives up to its reputation as the Ninja village with a leading level of education. The design of these questions is indeed ingenious, » Mū sighed in the conference hall as he watched the scene inside the crystal ball.

Relying on the vision provided by the Telescope Technique, they could see the actions of most examinees clearly.

It was easy to see that in almost every exam hall, there were already examinees using cheating methods.

Some had clever methods and were not caught by the Proctors, while others had clumsy methods and were expelled from the exam hall immediately after being caught.

However, this was precisely one of the items being tested in this Chunin Exams written test—intelligence gathering ability.

It wasn’t that cheating wasn’t allowed, but that cheating couldn’t be detected.

Just like that most classic Chunin Exams in the original timeline, this Chunin Exams also embodied a similar testing philosophy.

For a Ninja, completing a mission sometimes requires special means.

For a Ninja village, what is needed even more is a Ninja who can flexibly complete missions within the rules.

The representatives of the various Ninja villages present had long known about the testing philosophy and potential rules of this written test, and many had even disclosed this content to their own village’s examinees in advance.

This was also the reason why many Genin in the exam hall resorted to cheating without much hesitation.

And what made Mū of Iwagakure sigh was that the questions set by Konoha had almost completely led the examinees from various villages into this rhythm.

« Belief and the Ninja Way are indeed one of the most miraculous powers, but… »

Ōnoki, who was beside him, also showed a look of emotion, his gaze subconsciously looking at his own somewhat rough palms.

These questions reminded him of the « Will of Stone » mentioned by his grandfather, the First Tsuchikage, Ishikawa.

The « Will of Stone, » unyielding and immovable, was one of the most important pillars of support that had helped him walk to his current level after undergoing many trials.

But he also knew deeply that for the vast majority of Genin, understanding these things was still too difficult.

In the exam hall, when the surrounding examinees began to show off their unique skills, Senju Matsuaki also sensed it immediately.

Among all the examinees, he was one of the few whose answering speed had not slowed down in the slightest from beginning to end.

Nothing else, just practice.

These subjective questions in the second half were an indelible memory for him ; some answering templates were still branded in his memory.

The knowledge points were different, but there were many commonalities in answering techniques.

Because of this, he became the primary target for cheaters in the same exam hall.

Behind him, a flash of Chakra made Senju Matsuaki’s heart stir slightly.

After all, his teammate was a member of the Hyuga Clan, and he had long since memorized the fleeting Chakra fluctuations when activating the Byakugan.

Obviously, it was an examinee from the Hyuga Clan behind him who was activating the Byakugan to copy his answers.

The Byakugan had an unparalleled advantage in this occasion of testing « intelligence gathering ability. »

Feeling this peeping, the pen in Senju Matsuaki’s hand paused slightly, but then he continued to write as if nothing had happened.

After all, they were Ninjas from the same village, and the other party’s behavior didn’t interfere with him, so if they copied, they copied.

Five minutes later, a wisp of water vapor suddenly condensed in the air above Matsuaki’s right hand, slowly forming an ice mirror.

The ice mirror formed of water imaged clearly just like a real mirror, clearly reflecting the answering status of the test paper on Matsuaki’s desk.

Obviously, this was another new cheating method.

And the cheater was undoubtedly a Genin from the Ice Release clan of the Hidden Mist Village.

Ice Release is a Bloodline Limit combined from Water Release Chakra and Wind Release Chakra. In the original timeline, a well-known Ice Release Ninja was Haku.

As for the Ice Release Ninjutsu launched by the Ninjas of the Land of Snow with the help of the natural environment, it was not worth mentioning.

At this time, the Ice Release clan had not yet suffered a great change and was still a relatively strong Ninja clan in the Hidden Mist Village, with some members even holding high positions.

Senju Matsuaki didn’t want to be a meddler ; after glancing at this ice mirror, he withdrew his gaze.

However, the next second, the Proctor at the back of the exam hall was patrolling as usual and walked towards the middle of the exam hall.

A Hidden Mist boy with short, shoulder-length black hair, located to the right rear of Senju Matsuaki, gritted his teeth. Worried that his small trick would be discovered, he hurriedly withdrew the Ninjutsu he was performing.

The next instant, the ice mirror that had been floating in the air lost its connection to Chakra and fell downwards.

Noticing this change, Senju Matsuaki quickly moved the test paper on the desk away, but the area on his arm was inevitably wetted a little by the ice and ice water.

Seeing this, his brows furrowed, and a few blue veins couldn’t help but pop up on his forehead.

« Fine, fine, fine, that’s how you want to play, right ? »

If he hadn’t been quick-eyed and nimble-handed just now, the test paper would probably have been half-soaked by now.

And this wasn’t just ice water ; there were ice cubes too. If the test paper had been punctured or damaged, he really would have failed this first stage of the written test.

Senju Matsuaki slowly moved his left hand under the desk and, using the cover of the wooden desk, gently formed a hand seal.

To his right rear, several thin, tentacle-like branches extended from under the wooden chair of that Ice Release clan examinee.

The tentacle-like branches swayed slightly up and down, seemingly searching for a target.

But rest assured, the branches were not going to do anything that would offend someone’s dignity.

The next second, the branches aimed at the ground to the side and stabbed out like an arrow released from a bow.

The reaction force brought about by this action directly caused the examinee who had been sitting steadily on the wooden chair to lose his balance, and he fell to the left.
