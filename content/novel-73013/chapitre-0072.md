# Chapitre 72 — "Bad News" Arrives

« Senju Matsuaki. »

After stating his name, Senju Matsuaki held the three chess pieces he had just picked up in his hand.

[Current Progress : 6 jade chess pieces captured, 11 examinees defeated.]

This was the progress updated after he killed two Iwagakure Ninja just now.

After some thought, Senju Matsuaki handed the jade chess piece in his hand to Inuzuka Rei in front of him.

« Eh ? »

Inuzuka Rei blankly took the jade chess piece.

[Current Progress : 6 jade chess pieces captured, 11 examinees defeated.]

Senju Matsuaki glanced at the current progress ; even after giving the piece to her, there was no change.

Under Inuzuka Rei’s puzzled gaze, Senju Matsuaki took the jade chess piece back from her hand.

[Current Progress : 6 jade chess pieces captured, 11 examinees defeated.]

After checking the progress again, Senju Matsuaki understood.

It seemed that once the same jade chess piece was obtained by him, the count became fixed ; subsequently, losing or destroying the piece would have no effect.

Furthermore, as long as it was the same piece, even if it was taken by someone else and then retrieved, it would not be counted repeatedly.

This prevented him from exploiting loopholes to farm points.

« You keep this piece. »

Having verified his hypothesis, this extra piece was of no use to Senju Matsuaki.

Rather than keeping it and taking up space, it was better to give a Konoha Ninja a chance to advance.

« But, my piece is supposed to be this one. »

This time, Inuzuka Rei did not take the jade chess piece, but hesitated and pointed to another blank piece in Senju Matsuaki’s hand.

« You don’t want this jade chess piece ? »

Seeing this, Senju Matsuaki felt a little surprised but did not insist.

He handed the blank piece to her and put the jade chess piece away.

The remaining blank piece was useless and took up space, so he returned it to its owner and threw it beside the Iwagakure Ninja’s corpse.

« It and I have both sustained serious injuries, and the difficulty of this Chunin Exams is beyond our capabilities. Although it’s a pity, let’s just forget it... »

« Woo~ »

Inuzuka Rei squatted down and stroked the head of her partner, the Ninja Dog, which also lowered its head and let out a whimper.

« Knowing when to retreat is also a form of wisdom. »

Seeing Inuzuka Rei had made her decision, Senju Matsuaki did not resent her withdrawal but smiled and said.

« Then what are your plans next ? »

Looking at Inuzuka Rei and the Ninja Dog beside her, and recalling the intelligence regarding the Inuzuka Clan, Senju Matsuaki asked thoughtfully.

« Next, I’ll clean up these corpses, and then go to another place to wait for the Proctor’s rescue. »

After thinking for a moment, Inuzuka Rei glanced at the two Iwagakure Ninja corpses on the ground, her fingers caressing the blank piece she had regained.

« If possible, I’d like to ask you for a favor, and during this time, I will be responsible for protecting your safety, » Senju Matsuaki said sternly.

« Eh, this... no problem. »

...

Inside the conference hall where the representatives of each village were located.

The door was opened from the outside, and Nara Kano walked to the table where Senju Hashirama and other Konoha Ninja were sitting, his face as gloomy as water.

« Hokage-sama, Tobirama just informed me of a serious situation that I must report to you urgently ! »

Nara Kano’s voice was not loud, but in this quiet conference hall, it was enough to reach everyone’s ears.

Seeing this, the representatives of the surrounding villages all cast curious glances at him.

« What is the situation ? »

Seeing Nara Kano’s expression so grim, Senju Hashirama’s heart couldn’t help but tighten.

« Just now, a Proctor reported that a death of an examinee occurred in the exam venue ; this is a deeply painful loss, » Nara Kano said with a sorrowful expression.

« Is that so... » Senju Hashirama sighed and closed his eyes with a heavy expression.

Although safety measures had been prepared to protect the examinees, it was impossible to completely prevent casualties during the Chunin Exams.

Hearing the conversation here, the representatives of the other four Ninja villages all showed expressions of schadenfreude.

To them, Konoha was a huge threat and an imaginary enemy.

If they had the chance, they would not hesitate to come up and carve up Konoha.

Now that they saw their enemy’s young strength damaged, although they didn’t say it on the surface, they felt great joy in their hearts.

« It’s such a pity ; if only the safety measures could have been more comprehensive... » Senju Hashirama sighed deeply.

« Hokage-sama, please accept my condolences, but the Chunin Exams are not child’s play. In the Chunin Exams held by Iwagakure last year, one Genin died accidentally, and three Genin suffered serious injuries that were difficult to heal. »

Mū of Iwagakure shook his head, comforted him with a sentence, and then brought up the situation of the Chunin Exams previously held by Iwagakure.

This act was nothing more than wanting to clear his name.

Although he felt schadenfreude, Mū was also worried that the one who killed the Konoha Genin was an Iwagakure Ninja, and if Senju Hashirama got enraged, he might scatter their ashes on the spot.

By attributing everything to the accidental circumstances of the Chunin Exams, even if it was eventually discovered that an Iwagakure Ninja killed them, Konoha would have no reasonable grounds to blame them.

« That’s right. If you want absolute safety, I’m afraid the only way is for each of our villages to send Jonin to act as bodyguards for these examinees. »

The future Second Raikage, A, also agreed.

« Everyone has the opportunity to safely withdraw from the assessment. If they can still lose their lives, they are not qualified as Ninja. »

Hōzuki Gengetsu pinched his small mustache and said the same.

« Although it is regrettable, one should be prepared for this when becoming a Ninja, » Shamon said in a deep voice.

The Ninja of the four great Ninja villages were now standing on the same front.

Only in this way would they not be implicated by Konoha’s anger.

Moreover, they more or less knew the truth of the matter. Before setting off this time, they even specially instructed the outstanding candidates among their own village’s examinees.

If there is a chance to kill the geniuses of other Ninja villages, do not hesitate or delay.

« I didn’t expect everyone to see things so thoroughly. With this, I can rest assured. »

To the surprise of the future Second Kages of the various villages, after hearing their remarks, Nara Kano breathed a sigh of relief, and the look of sorrow vanished without a trace.

« What does that mean ? » Hōzuki Gengetsu asked with a frown.

Watching Nara Kano change his expression at the speed of light, he suddenly had a bad premonition in his heart.

« Originally, I was afraid that everyone would not be able to accept this regrettable news, but I didn’t expect that everyone was already fully mentally prepared. It is truly admirable. »

Nara Kano looked at the representatives of the Ninja villages present with a sincere gaze, his tone full of respect.

« What ! ? You mean ! ? »

At this moment, Mū, who was quick-witted, realized what was going on ; he stood up abruptly, his eyes wide as he looked at Nara Kano.

« Kano, what exactly is the situation ? »

Senju Hashirama, who had originally been grieving for the Konoha Genin who had died, was now also confused and hurriedly asked.
