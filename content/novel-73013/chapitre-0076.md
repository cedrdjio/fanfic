# Chapitre 76 — One Strike, Heavy Damage! A Total Crush!

« Puppet Technique : Shield of Light ! ! »

Chakra used the puppet as a medium to form a shield across the front of the two, facing the incoming blade directly.

However, at the very moment of contact, massive cracks spread across the shield.

Crack—Crack—

Two cracking sounds came in succession : first the Chakra shield, then the puppet that Chiyo had just repaired a short while ago.

The two hiding behind it had absolutely no power to resist this immense force and were sent flying backward like two ragdolls.

Seeing this, Senju Matsuaki had no intention of delivering a finishing blow.

Chiyo was not wrong just now ; if he wasted too much time here, Hyuga Tsukuyomi might truly run into trouble.

After bending down to pick up a jade chess piece that the two had dropped on the ground, Senju Matsuaki looked at Inuzuka Rei.

« The situation is urgent ; we must immediately catch up to that Magnet Release Ninja. »

From Chiyo’s panicked reaction just now, it was clear that she recognized the owner of that metallic mineral scent.

Metallic minerals combined with a Sunagakure background—it wasn’t hard to guess that it was a Magnet Release Ninja from Sunagakure.

Magnet Release is a fairly representative Bloodline Limit of Sunagakure ; it can attack and defend, making it very troublesome.

« Magnet Release, I understand, » Inuzuka Rei said, suddenly realizing.

Just moments ago, she was still confused as to why the scent of metallic minerals was strong in some places and faint in others.

At the same time, it was as if it could move at high speed ; she would smell it right here, only to find the target location completely empty upon arrival.

If it was the Bloodline Limit of Magnet Release, it made sense.

Immediately, the two stopped paying attention to the Sunagakure duo, whose fates were unknown, and quickly left in the direction of the scent.

Back at the scene, Chiyo struggled to crawl up and scrambled in a panic toward where Ebizo was.

At her chest, a light shield also made of Chakra shattered and dissipated into the air.

As a puppeteer, self-preservation is an important skill.

The essence of the Puppet Technique : Shield of Light is to use mechanisms to convert Chakra into a shield ; it can not only be equipped on puppets but can also be carried by the puppeteer themselves.

Just now, under the cover of the puppet, Chiyo activated the Shield of Light through another mechanism hidden on her person, saving her own life and Ebizo’s.

Otherwise, Senju Matsuaki’s strike just now would likely have pulverized both of their internal organs !

After struggling to crawl to Ebizo’s side, Chiyo’s eyes instantly turned bloodshot.

She saw that although Ebizo’s chest was still rising and falling, he was so heavily injured that he could barely speak.

What was even harder for her to accept was that Ebizo’s left arm, from the arm down, was a bloody mess ; flesh and blood mixed with dirt left a pool of crimson—this arm was clearly completely ruined.

« Damn it ! ! Konoha ! ! »

Chiyo glanced at her heavily injured younger brother, then looked at the puppet in the distance that had once again shattered into countless parts and debris ; her eyes were dark.

In the end, she took out a blank chess piece, and her slightly rough fingers squeezed it hard, crushing it.

A wave of Chakra spread to the surroundings, and the crystal ball in the distance was able to receive the signal from this location via the Telescope Technique.

« Chiyo, Ebizo ! ! »

Inside the conference hall, Shamon watched the image appearing on the crystal ball in disbelief, slamming the table and standing up abruptly.

Rage, astonishment—various emotions made the dragon tattoo on Shamon’s face and head look even more ferocious.

If it were an ordinary Genin who had been heavily injured, Shamon probably wouldn’t have been this angry.

It was truly because Sunagakure lacked talent, and those who had been heavily injured were precisely some of the few talents they had.

Chiyo and Ebizo, this pair of siblings, were geniuses in Sunagakure second only to Yan, and he had high hopes for them.

Chiyo had excellent talent in Puppet Technique, so he had taken her as his direct disciple.

And Ebizo was born with a meticulous mind and a flexible brain ; in the future, he was suitable to be cultivated as the head of Sunagakure’s intelligence and espionage department or an Advisor.

However, both of them had now been heavily injured, and Ebizo had even had an arm shattered.

For a Ninja, the importance of an arm goes without saying.

On the side, the representatives from various Villages who were curiously looking at Shamon also withdrew their gazes and remained silent.

Although they were not afraid of Shamon, they did not want to provoke him at this moment.

« Please rest assured, our Konoha rescue personnel will arrive at the scene very quickly, » Senju Hashirama comforted.

...

« I didn’t expect those two siblings to survive ; Sunagakure really has good luck. »

Just as Shamon was still lamenting his bad luck, the staff in Senju Tobirama’s office held the opposite view.

« It’s a pity that the little girl named Chiyo crushed the chess piece ; we can’t make a secret move even if we wanted to. »

« Although I admire her decisive and quick-witted reaction, if she grows up in the future, she will likely be a great enemy of ours. »

A female Ninja showed an expression of admiration, but thinking that such an outstanding young person was a Ninja of Sunagakure, she couldn’t help but feel some worry.

Hearing this, another Ninja said : « She currently has no power to fight back in front of a Konoha Ninja five or six years younger than her ; she surely won’t be able to make any waves in the future. »

« Lord Tobirama’s nephew is probably the most outstanding genius since the founding of our Konoha. »

Speaking of this, the staff present all subconsciously looked toward Senju Tobirama in the center, their expressions full of envy.

Some of them were from Ninja clans, and some were of civilian origin.

However, the performance of the younger generation in most of their families was actually inferior to when they were young ; there was a feeling that each generation was worse than the last.

This might also be because the environment after the founding of Konoha was relatively peaceful, lacking stimulation and motivation for young people.

Hearing the praise of his subordinates, Senju Tobirama felt secretly pleased, but he did not show it.

Those who could be arranged by him into this office were all geniuses in intelligence that he had carefully selected, and they were all Ninjas who carried the dark side of the Village.

Usually, what everyone talked about the most was how to spy on other Villages' intelligence, how to assassinate, poison, and sow discord.

The Ninjas here were all talented, and they spoke so well ; Senju Tobirama loved it here.

Working with these subordinates was like a fish in water ; it felt just like home.

« The Proctor sent to rescue is on standby at all times. Once the situation goes out of control, immediately take action to kill the Hidden Sand Village brats. The Byakugan is Konoha’s Bloodline Limit ; it cannot be lost ! ! » Senju Tobirama said in a deep voice, his words filled with intense killing intent.

Not « subdue, » but « kill. »

If Sunagakure wanted an explanation at that time, the charge of plotting to steal the Byakugan would be enough.

« Yes ! ! »

The Ninjas in the office responded one after another, then continued to perform their duties ; those who needed to convey orders conveyed orders, and those who needed to monitor the exam situation in the primeval forest stared at the crystal ball.

On Senju Tobirama’s side, he locked the image projected on the crystal ball by the Telescope Technique near Senju Matsuaki.

From his « God’s-eye view, » he could see that Senju Matsuaki was about to encounter Yan from the Hidden Sand Village.

As a top genius of the Hidden Sand Village, Yan undoubtedly had the strength of a Jonin.

Senju Tobirama really wanted to see if his good nephew would have a dazzling performance when facing Yan.
