# Chapitre 50 — I Became a Genin?

Land of Lightning, Hidden Cloud Village.

Above the steep cliffs lies a training ground for the Hidden Cloud Village.

At this moment, on the field, many sturdy youths and vigorous young women surrounded the burly Dou, yet everyone had a look of terror on their faces.

« Too weak ! Too weak ! Did you not eat your meals ! ? »

Accompanied by a roar like thunder, the surrounded Dou’s aura soared, as if he were the one surrounding the others.

Then, he saw him vanish from the spot in a flash, appearing before the nearest Cloud Ninja youth in the blink of an eye, his large, fan-like hand pressing down on the youth’s head.

Under the pale-faced gaze of everyone, the sturdy body of this Cloud Ninja was lifted up by Dou with one hand as if he were a chick.

« Boss Dou, I admit... »

Before the Cloud Ninja youth could finish saying he surrendered, he was thrown to the ground by Dou, sent spinning and seeing stars.

However, Dou did not look at him again, instead raising his head, his tiger-like gaze sweeping across the others.

« Boss Dou, I surrender ! »

Having learned from the previous person’s lesson, the others became smart, shouting their surrender before Dou could even make a move.

« I’m not listening ! I’m not listening ! I ! Am ! Not ! Listening ! »

However, Dou had no intention of stopping, instead stepping forward and swinging his iron fist to hammer the surrendering Cloud Ninja down.

Seeing this, everyone else on the field abandoned the lucky idea that surrendering would save them, and threw themselves into this one-versus-many sparring session with all their might.

« Lightning Release : Lightning Oppression Horizontal Dance ! »

« Lightning Release : Scatter-Hit Mudou ! »

« Fire Release : Great Fireball Technique ! »

« Forget it, who can you kill with Great Fireball Technique ! ? »

Means were used frequently on the field, yet the dull thuds of fists hitting flesh and screams rose one after another.

Five minutes later, Dou was the only one standing proudly on the battlefield with his arms crossed, shaking his head helplessly.

In just five minutes, he had hammered all of these people down with one or two punches each.

Even though there were many female Ninja present, his only act of chivalry was not hitting their faces ; they were hammered down just the same.

« With you like this, how can you become a Chunin ! ? » Dou said in a deep voice.

With the Joint Chunin Exams imminent, he did not want the Hidden Cloud Village to lose face in front of other Villages, so he specially gathered the younger brothers of the Village for a special pre-exam training.

Just then, a figure lying on the ground suddenly raised a hand weakly.

« Uh, Big Brother Dou, but I am already a Chunin. »

« Idiot ! »

Bang—

Hearing this, blue veins bulged on Dou’s forehead as he fiercely came to this person’s side and delivered another punch, and only then did all movement cease.

It was no wonder he was so worried ; in his view, these Genin could be knocked down by him in a few punches, so they would last at most one or two more punches against Senju Matsuaki.

Just as Dou was worrying about the Hidden Cloud Village’s performance at the Chunin Exams, the Cloud Village Elder who had previously led the delegation to Konoha walked over slowly.

« Dou, you are too impatient. »

« Elder, are you not worried ? This time at the Chunin Exams, Konoha has... »

The cohesion of the Cloud Ninja was very strong, and Dou was also a good youth with a strong sense of honor and a heart for the Hidden Cloud Village.

« Do not worry, this old man has his own methods. » The Cloud Village Elder stroked his beard and chuckled.

« Uh, you have a method ? » A glimmer of hope appeared in Dou’s eyes.

The Cloud Village Elder, who had previously looked gentle, suddenly turned serious.

He scanned the Cloud Ninja lying on the ground, his eyes revealing a hint of reproach.

« Dou, you actually attacked fellow Village Ninja indiscriminately ; this old man is very disappointed in you ! »

« Huh ? » Dou’s eyes widened suddenly. « Was it not you who told me to give them special training to deal with the upcoming Chunin Exams ? »

Hearing this, a trace of guilt inevitably surfaced in the Cloud Village Elder’s heart.

However, very quickly, this insignificant feeling of guilt was dispelled by him.

Forgive me, Dou.

A person who is not cunning enough cannot become Raikage ; I am doing this all to train you.

« Now I am punishing you by demoting you to Genin. I hope you will work hard in the future and not let down the Village’s expectations of you. »

After saying this, the Cloud Village Elder stiffened his old face and hurriedly turned to leave.

« Oh no, I became a Genin ? »

In place, only Dou was left with a face that alternated between green and white, along with Cloud Ninja everywhere who wanted to laugh but dared not make a sound.

...

Land of Wind, Sunagakure.

In the Kazekage’s office, the First Kazekage, Reto, sat in his seat with a worried face, sighing as he looked at a stack of registration forms.

Beside him was Shamon, who served as his bodyguard and would become the Second Kazekage of Sunagakure in the future.

Shamon had a black-clawed green dragon tattoo coiled around his head and right eye, and his appearance looked even more fierce and cold than the First Kazekage, Reto.

« Shamon, these Genin who signed up for the Chunin Exams this year—even in our own Sunagakure Chunin Exams, I’m afraid only a few would pass. If they go to Konoha... »

« What can be done about this ? Sigh... »

Hearing this, Shamon could only scratch his head, unable to offer any meaningful countermeasures.

If he had the ability to improve the overall quality of Sunagakure Genin, he would have done it long ago ; how could he have waited until now ?

It was impossible to just let Genin have the strength of a Chunin, right ?

Huh, wait...

Shamon suddenly seemed to have thought of something key, rubbing his chin thoughtfully.

Just then, the door to the Kazekage’s office was knocked, and after receiving Reto’s response, the door was opened from the outside.

« Lord Kazekage, Teacher Shamon ! »

A youth who looked no more than thirteen or fourteen years old walked in from outside the door and greeted the two.

The youth had short blue hair and yellow eyes, and his face was as sharp and serious as sandstorms, looking like someone who did not joke around.

« Yan, you’re here. » Seeing the youth arrive, the worry on the First Kazekage’s face slightly diminished.

This blue-haired, yellow-eyed youth was the most talented Ninja in Sunagakure in recent years, having already attained the strength of a Jonin at less than fifteen years of age.

Some time ago, he had even independently executed an A-rank mission and successfully promoted to Jonin.

And what satisfied Reto the most was that this youth named Yan had awakened a Bloodline Limit called Magnet Release, capable of manipulating Iron Sand for defense and offense.

Therefore, both the First Kazekage, Reto, and the future Second Kazekage, Shamon, had already been cultivating the youth as the future Third Kazekage in their hearts.

« Yan, the time has come for you to share the Village’s burdens ! »

Shamon looked the youth up and down and found that, among the rough crowd of Sunagakure, he could be considered delicate and fair-skinned, and judging purely by appearance, it was completely plausible to call him a Genin.

« Teacher Shamon, please give your orders. »

Looking at Shamon’s somewhat strange gaze, a bad premonition arose in Yan’s heart, but he still bit the bullet and said.

« Next, I ask you to participate in the Joint Chunin Exams with the identity of a Genin ! »

Shamon walked in front of Yan, pressed his hands on his shoulders, his gaze full of sincerity.

« What ! ? Teacher Shamon, I only just passed the assessment to become a Jonin last week... »

After realizing what he had heard, Yan felt as if he were falling apart, his originally composed young face full of shock.

« Yan ! The face of Sunagakure is entrusted to you ! »

Shamon’s broad hands patted Yan’s shoulders heavily, and after hesitating for a moment, the First Kazekage, Reto, did not oppose this proposal.
