# Chapitre 77 — Saving the Day! Water Release: Water Severing Wave!

« Awoo ! ! »

Just as the two were moving forward, Inuzuka Rei’s Ninja Dog suddenly shivered all over and lowered its head to bite the heel of Inuzuka Rei’s shoe.

« The guy ahead has an overwhelming presence ; this child is scared. »

Looking at Senju Matsuaki’s slightly puzzled expression, Inuzuka Rei explained in a low voice.

It wasn’t just the Ninja Dog ; even she felt a palpitation in her heart.

However, as a Ninja, she was better at suppressing biological instincts than her Ninja Dog.

« I understand. » Senju Matsuaki said knowingly.

It wasn’t just the Ninja Dog ; Inuzuka Rei’s expression was also tense, a change he naturally noticed.

« Thank you very much for your help along the way. I’ll go on alone from here. »

Senju Matsuaki first expressed his gratitude to the duo, then drew the short blade at his waist and said solemnly.

He had only asked Inuzuka Rei to help him find someone ; if he let her get involved in danger, it would only harm her.

« I wish you all the best ! »

Inuzuka Rei, who knew her own limits, didn’t try to be a hero and just softly expressed her well wishes.

In addition to their sense of smell, the Inuzuka Clan also possessed beast-like intuition.

Her intuition told her that if she continued forward, the enemy she faced might kill her in an instant.

If she insisted on following, she would not only lose her life but would likely become a burden to her companion.

Next, she planned to follow her previous decision : crush the chess piece and wait for the Proctor to take her out of the exam site.

The waters of this Joint Chunin Exams were too deep for her to handle.

Afterward, the two parted ways in opposite directions.

Senju Matsuaki continued to rush forward, while Inuzuka Rei looked for a suitable location to withdraw from the exam.

As the distance closed, he could increasingly sense the subtly different aura ahead.

« This momentum and killing intent are probably on par with that guy from Kumogakure. Who exactly did Hidden Sand Village send... » Senju Matsuaki thought to himself.

Sunagakure, Magnet Release, strength currently on par with the future Third Raikage...

Combining these characteristics, it was easy to think of a certain Suna Ninja.

...

On the other side, Hyuga Tsukuyomi, Biwako, and Yan were still in a stalemate.

Near Yan’s feet, black Iron Sand surged and circled him constantly.

Looking at his cold, gloomy expression, it was clear he had reached the limit of his patience.

« Damn it, why haven’t Chiyo and Ebizo arrived yet ? » Yan cursed inwardly.

According to his previous plan, he would detect the target via Magnet Release, then strike first, with Chiyo and Ebizo following right behind.

As a result, he had been attacking for so long that he was now stuck, yet he hadn’t even seen a shadow of them.

They were currently in a stalemate ; as long as Chiyo and Ebizo arrived, the scales would tip in Sunagakure’s favor.

Although Hyuga Tsukuyomi and Biwako combined were no match for Yan, he was wary of Hyuga Tsukuyomi destroying her Byakugan and dared not take further action.

And having reached this stalemate, he feared that even if he wanted to give up, the two from Konoha wouldn’t easily believe him.

« I don’t want the Byakugan anymore. Let’s treat today’s events as if they never happened. How about we both take a step back ? » Yan asked in a low voice.

« I don’t believe you ! » × 2

« ... »

The two’s unhesitating distrust made Yan’s face sink, and he had to continue holding Biwako with the black Iron Sand.

In fact, even he couldn’t guarantee that if he broke the stalemate, he wouldn’t continue to attack.

After all, for him, the temptation of a pair of Byakugan was simply too strong.

With the Byakugan plus Magnet Release, under the blessing of the dual Bloodline Limit, he was confident he could step into the Kage level sooner.

Of course, considering reality, at least while Konoha was in its prime, he dared not use this Byakugan openly.

But storing it in Sunagakure and using it later when Konoha declined was not impossible.

Even if he didn’t use it himself, handing it to a trusted subordinate in the future would be a rare trump card.

Just then, the Iron Sand deployed in the distance sent feedback : someone was approaching.

Just as Yan thought the lagging Chiyo and Ebizo had finally arrived, he turned around and his expression suddenly changed.

He saw a silver-haired Ninja in the distance, carrying a short blade and wearing a Konoha forehead protector, approaching rapidly.

« Damn it, it’s someone from Konoha... »

Yan’s expression fluctuated, but he finally made a decision to abandon the Byakugan and leave.

His premise for seizing the Byakugan was to be able to control the scene, kill the witnesses, and destroy the evidence after taking the eyes.

He had long memorized the rules of the second stage of the exam.

He knew that once someone crushed a chess piece, the scene here would be seen by the Proctors and representatives of the major Ninja villages.

In this way, his attempt to seize the Byakugan could be said to have both human and physical evidence.

He had self-awareness ; he knew that even if he added his teacher Shamon, they would likely be suppressed by the Konoha of this peak era in an instant.

At that time, Konoha would be in the right, and Sunagakure would be in the wrong and powerless, only able to suffer in vain.

However, just as he wanted to use Magnet Release to leave, Senju Matsuaki in the distance had already finished his hand seals.

« Water Release : Water Severing Wave ! ! »

A thin stream of water spurted from his mouth, forming a line of water that flew toward him.

Seeing this, there was no contempt in Yan’s eyes.

A black shield condensed from Iron Sand hovered in front of him, attempting to block the water line.

After the distance closed, he also recognized the person as the Senju Matsuaki that Chiyo and Ebizo had mentioned.

Being able to easily defeat Chiyo and Ebizo, although Yan didn’t think the opponent was stronger than him, he was still an opponent not to be underestimated.

However, before Yan’s astonished eyes, the black shield condensed from Iron Sand was easily cut open by the water stream.

The dense line of water severed a strand of his blue hair ; if Yan hadn’t dodged in time, he might have been decapitated.

« What kind of Ninjutsu is this ! ? »

Yan stood some distance away, looking rather disheveled, his eyes filled with shock and anger as he looked at Senju Matsuaki.

He thought he had paid enough attention, but he hadn’t expected to suffer a big loss in the very first exchange.

The power of that water line just now had exceeded his imagination regarding the attribute and substance of « water. »

He had performed missions for many years and encountered many Ninjas skilled in Water Release in other countries, yet no one had ever broken through the defense of his Magnet Release Iron Sand.

Yet just now, his proud Magnet Release was easily split in two by a line of water.

Because he lost control, Biwako, who had been restrained by the Iron Sand, fell to the ground without support.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi quickly stepped forward to help her up, then quickly supported her away from the distance, coming to Senju Matsuaki’s side.

« Sorry, I’m late. » Senju Matsuaki said.

He had seen that scene just now.

This Magnet Release Ninja from Sunagakure was holding Biwako hostage, while Hyuga Tsukuyomi was in the posture of about to destroy her own eyes.

The purpose of this Suna Ninja was self-evident : to seize Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s Byakugan.

« Matsuaki, you’re here. »

Seeing Senju Matsuaki blocking in front of her, Hyuga Tsukuyomi felt a warmth in her heart, and her nerves, which had been tense from the beginning, relaxed slightly.

« It’s that person from back then... »

And Biwako, looking at Senju Matsuaki, also had an impression.

She remembered that during the graduation exam at the Ninja Academy, they had even been opponents in one of the rounds.
