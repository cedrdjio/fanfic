# Chapitre 5 — Tsugikuni Yoriichi! Breathing Technique!

« I see. I have understood the situation. »

The old man looked at Senju Matsuaki with cloudy eyes, his voice gentle and steady.

So warm, just like the sun...

Senju Matsuaki, who had walked up to the old man, could feel his warm aura and energy.

Combined with the man’s appearance, he already had a guess as to his identity.

« I am Tsugikuni Yoriichi, here to teach you the secrets of the Breathing Technique. »

Tsugikuni Yoriichi rose from his kneeling position. His thin frame looked frail, yet it exuded a resilient aura.

Seeing this, Senju Matsuaki, who had been delighted, composed himself and bowed to Tsugikuni Yoriichi, saying, « Student Senju Matsuaki, please teach me. »

Watching the polite and sensible child in front of him, Tsugikuni Yoriichi smiled kindly and gently, nodding slightly in approval.

« This is a magical place. When you enter here, time in the outside world will stop. And when you have learned what I am going to teach you, this space will disappear. »

After sweeping his gaze over Senju Matsuaki, there was a fleeting moment of surprise on Tsugikuni Yoriichi’s face.

« Your physical fitness is quite good, even exceeding most people in the Demon Slayer Corps. This is a very good foundation. »

Hearing this, Senju Matsuaki was not surprised.

In the Ninja World, Chakra exists, and the physical fitness of a Ninja is inherently stronger than that of an average person in the Demon Slayer world.

Furthermore, he was a descendant of the Senju Clan, inheriting the Sage Body.

He was still young ; when he grew up, his physical fitness would become even more exaggerated.

Tsugikuni Yoriichi said, « Enough small talk, let’s begin the first lesson now. »

...

Before class at the Ninja Academy, the students were chatting and playing in small groups.

« Matsumi-kun, you seem a bit different today. » Hyuga Tsukuyomi, sitting next to Matsumi, said softly with a hint of curiosity on her small face.

« Is that so ? »

Matsumi was somewhat surprised by Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s keenness.

In fact, Hyuga Tsukuyomi was not wrong ; he had indeed undergone some subtle changes, which was mainly due to his practice of the Breathing Technique yesterday.

Whether for a swordsman or a Ninja, breathing is a very important matter.

At the Ninja Academy, there is also instruction on breathing methods.

How to keep one’s breathing steady during battle and how to minimize the sound of breathing while hiding is a discipline.

However, the Breathing Technique that Tsugikuni Yoriichi taught him was on a completely different level compared to these breathing methods.

The Breathing Technique is a skill created by Tsugikuni Yoriichi that can significantly enhance human physical capabilities.

It can strengthen cardiopulmonary function, allowing the blood to absorb a large amount of oxygen in a short time, instantly improving physical fitness, and even temporarily possessing power equivalent to that of a « Demon ».

What Tsugikuni Yoriichi taught Senju Matsuaki was not a specific Breathing Technique style like Sun Breathing or Moon Breathing, but merely the foundation of the Breathing Technique itself.

After all, you get what you pay for. For a cheat code that only cost 100 points, being able to draw the Breathing Technique was already great value.

Yesterday in the Skill Training Space, Tsugikuni Yoriichi explained the principles of the Breathing Technique and patiently guided Matsumi to get started.

Although the techniques of the Breathing Technique came from another world, Matsumi had already begun to grasp the basics.

Because of this, his posture and temperament had undergone subtle changes ; his bearing was more upright, and his aura was more refined.

« Also, I haven’t seen Matsumi-kun carry a short sword before. »

Hyuga Tsukuyomi said with some concern, pointing out another difference.

She worried that Senju Matsuaki was learning swordsmanship out of desperation because he was worried about the scheduled duel with Uchiha Kai.

Hearing this, Senju Matsuaki reached out and patted the scabbard hanging on his back, smiling as he said, « Actually, I had learned some basic swordsmanship from my elders before, and I just happened to have the opportunity to pick it up again recently. »

The most important reason was that, although the Skill Training Space restricted Tsugikuni Yoriichi to only teaching the Breathing Technique, such basic swordsmanship was not strictly restricted.

After all, Tsugikuni Yoriichi’s Breathing Technique had a certain connection with his swordsmanship.

Moreover, Tsugikuni Yoriichi, who had mastered the Transparent World, could see the structure of the opponent’s body and possessed top-tier swordsmanship, making him the best swordsmanship teacher.

In this way, not taking the opportunity to learn some swordsmanship from Tsugikuni Yoriichi would truly be a waste of a good opportunity.

« By the way, Matsumi-kun. » Hyuga Tsukuyomi looked hesitant and conflicted, but she still spoke up, « There is something I would like to ask for your consent on. »

Senju Matsuaki was slightly taken aback. He had known her for several years, and this was the first time he had heard her make a request.

« What is it, Tsukuyomi ? »

« It’s like this : there is only about half a month left until the graduation exam at the end of the month. If you are lacking a sparring partner, I wonder if... I could ? »

Seeming worried that he might refuse, the girl added in a low voice.

« And my Byakugan can clearly see human bones, meridians, and the flow of Chakra. Perhaps I can provide you with some advice during our sparring. »

Hearing the girl say this, Senju Matsuaki was truly surprised.

How old was Hyuga Tsukuyomi this year ? Only 6 years old, and she had already awakened her Byakugan ?

One must know that whether it is the Byakugan or the Sharingan, a certain trigger is needed to awaken them.

The Sharingan requires emotional stimulation, while the Byakugan requires continuous practice and training.

Of course, the speed of awakening also has a clear correlation with bloodline and talent.

Because Hyuga Tsukuyomi had never shown her Byakugan state, no one knew that she had awakened it at the age of 6.

One must know that Uchiha Kai, who is considered a genius among the younger generation of the Uchiha Clan, has not yet awakened the Sharingan at this time.

« That would be great, Tsukuyomi. Please take care of me for the next while. » Matsumi said with a smile.

The Byakugan and Tsugikuni Yoriichi’s Transparent World have similar effects ; both can see through the structure of the human body.

Having such a partner to spar with would indeed be twice the result with half the effort, so naturally, there was no reason to refuse.

« Yes ! I also want to help you in my own way. » Hyuga Tsukuyomi said softly.

However, Matsumi did not hear this sentence clearly, as the teacher had already walked into the classroom, holding a stack of papers in hand.

« Everyone, quiet down. » The teacher tapped on the podium.

« Now, students who wish to apply for the early graduation exam, come to me to get an application form, and hand it back to me after filling it out. »

After the voice fell, nearly half of the students in the classroom left their seats.

They consciously lined up in front of the podium, taking the application forms one by one.

There were also some people who, after hesitating repeatedly, still did not get up and remained in their seats.

If one looked closely, one could see that most of those applying for the early graduation exam were Ninjas from Ninja clans.

Except for Senju Matsuaki, Hyuga Tsukuyomi, and Uchiha Kai, almost everyone else in the class from the Hyuga, Uchiha, Sarutobi, and other clans had applied for early graduation.
