# Chapitre 38 — Have You Ever Heard Of...

« What does everyone think ? »

As a village leader who welcomed good advice and listened with an open mind, Senju Hashirama looked around at the high-ranking officials present and asked gently.

« I think the Nara Clan Head makes sense ! »

« I support Kano’s point of view ! »

« In that case, I have no other objections. »

The people present agreed one after another.

In the current Ninja World, Konoha was the one developing at high speed, and everyone present did not want to start a war.

And the one in the Ninja World that most wanted to start a war right now was the Hidden Cloud Village, which had strong military power and coveted the rich resources of the Land of Fire.

As for the likes of the Hidden Sand Village, although they were also drooling over Konoha’s land and forests, they did not have enough military strength to back it up.

Being able to tell the other side in such a gentle way that they were not to be trifled with and should not have any inappropriate ideas was naturally excellent.

« There is one more thing I would like to ask for everyone’s opinion on, » Senju Hashirama said seriously.

« Although we don’t know yet which Ninja villages will participate, the final location for the Joint Chunin Exams will inevitably only be chosen from one of them. »

« For such a grand event that unites multiple Ninja villages, I think it would be best if the first one could be held in Konoha. I wonder what everyone thinks ? »

After Hashirama finished speaking, everyone present looked thoughtful.

A moment later, Senju Tobirama spoke first : « Big brother, such a large-scale event is a first for Konoha. Both security and the economic burden will be a great pressure. »

« Moreover, we cannot rule out the possibility that other Ninja villages might take this opportunity to do things detrimental to Konoha. Therefore, I oppose setting the exam venue in Konoha. »

« Tobirama is right, » Sarutobi Sasuke echoed, « Since it was the Hidden Cloud Village’s proposal, they should be responsible for providing the venue. »

At that time, having not long entered the era of Ninja villages, everyone held great vigilance against Ninjas from other countries.

Furthermore, the Joint Chunin Exams meant that Ninjas from other Ninja villages would have to stay in Konoha for a relatively long period, which was an extremely unstable factor.

However, just at this moment, Matsumi, who had been standing quietly on the side listening to everyone’s discussion, suddenly spoke up.

« Seniors, I actually think it is more appropriate to host the Joint Chunin Exams in Konoha. »

« You... »

Hearing a strange and childish voice, the Uchiha Clan Head was about to retort.

After all, just yesterday, an Uchiha child was almost kidnapped, so he naturally did not want Ninjas from other Ninja villages frequently coming and going in Konoha.

However, seeing Matsumi’s familiar silver hair and red eyes, which were just like Senju Tobirama’s, he consciously withdrew his words.

'Yesterday, it was all thanks to him that our Uchiha child was not abducted by the Cloud Ninjas. I am not afraid of that guy Senju Tobirama...'

The Uchiha Clan Head comforted himself in his heart.

« Matsumi, tell us your thoughts. » Seeing that Matsumi supported his idea, Senju Hashirama’s eyes lit up, and he encouraged him.

After all, he was his nephew whom he had watched grow up, and he knew that he had had some novel ideas since he was a child, so perhaps he could really have some good suggestions right now.

Seeing this, others present did not have any contempt, waiting for Matsumi’s next words.

From the task report just now, they had already recognized Senju Matsumi as a qualified Ninja, possessing good qualities such as calmness, wisdom, and flexibility.

Since the other party had a different view, there was no harm in listening to it.

« Have you ever heard of international influence ? » Senju Matsumi smiled slightly.

« Just hearing the term, I can roughly guess what it means, but if I think about it carefully, I am still confused, » the Yamanaka Clan Head, who was standing right next to Matsumi, replied immediately.

« Then I will briefly explain to you seniors why I suggest holding the Joint Chunin Exams in Konoha. » Seeing that his uncle, second uncle, and the various clan heads present seemed a bit puzzled, Matsumi cleared his throat and explained it to them.

There followed words that were difficult for those present to understand, such as « advertising placement, » « stimulating service industry growth, » « driving the local economy, » and « voice in the Ninja World. »

It made everyone feel that it was awesome despite not understanding it, and they praised him in unison. For a time, the entire Hokage Office was filled with a cheerful atmosphere.

After listening to Matsumi’s simple explanation, Senju Tobirama’s eyes seemed to light up, and he nodded repeatedly.

« Well said ! These are things I hadn’t considered before. Now is exactly the time when Konoha needs money. If we can seize this opportunity of the Joint Chunin Exams, Konoha’s economy will surely be able to reach a higher level. »

Nara Kano also showed appreciation and said with a smile : « And as Matsumi just said, by holding the Joint Chunin Exams, our so-called voice in the Ninja World will also be stronger. »

« At that time, high-end clients such as politicians and wealthy people from various countries will surely prioritize hiring our Konoha Ninjas. »

« If Konoha shines this time, it should be easier to apply for funding from the Daimyo later, » Hashirama said with a smile, while his eyes were full of satisfaction as he looked at his nephew.

He previously just simply felt that the first Joint Chunin Exams had great commemorative significance, and it would be a pity not to hold it in Konoha, not having thought about so many hidden benefits behind it.

Hearing that the few smartest and most influential people in Konoha had already agreed, the rest of the people, even if they didn’t understand the words just now, did not stand up to refute.

After all, it was easy to voice opposition, but it would be difficult to explain the « why. »

Moreover, everyone present was within the interest community of Konoha. Even if they were not at ease with Matsumi’s proposal, they at least trusted the views of smart people like Nara Kano and Senju Tobirama.

Seeing that Hyuga Clan Head Hyuga Teruake and the Uchiha Clan Head still looked worried, Matsumi continued.

« Clan Heads, you don’t need to worry too much about security issues. When the time comes, we will invite nobles and dignitaries from various countries to attend, and these Ninja villages will not dare to act rashly. »

Upon hearing this, the Hyuga Clan Head and the Uchiha Clan Head, who were most worried about the leakage of their Bloodline Limits, also softened their expressions.

After all, besides mission rewards, the biggest source of income for Ninja villages was funding from the Daimyo of various countries.

With these important figures from various countries in Konoha, these Ninja villages would need to weigh things carefully if they wanted to cause trouble again.

Otherwise, if they accidentally hurt an important figure from any country and caused a boycott, the consequences would not be that simple.

In the original timeline, the Hidden Sand Village dared to cause trouble when Konoha hosted the Chunin Exams because the Kazekage had already been replaced by Orochimaru.

« Good, then in the afternoon’s meeting, I will try my best to fight for hosting the Joint Chunin Exams in Konoha, » Hashirama decided.

« After listening to Matsumi’s words just now, I have some new ideas... »

Next, taking advantage of the time before the scheduled afternoon meeting, Konoha’s high-level officials continued to discuss important matters in the Hokage Office.

Unlike just now, the matters everyone was discussing now involved the specific arrangements of Konoha’s various departments, as well as the detailed division of labor among the various Ninja clans in the Joint Chunin Exams.

Regarding these matters he did not understand, Matsumi did not interject, but silently opened the check-in system that was still in the requisition period.
