# Chapitre 19 — The Jonin Instructor Is...

« That’s great, » Hyuga Tsukuyomi looked at Matsuaki with a beaming smile, « We’ll be able to fight side by side from now on. »

Hearing this, Matsuaki also smiled and said, « That’s right, if it’s you, Tsukuyomi, I’ll be able to feel much more at ease. »

« Mm-hmm. »

Hearing these words, a slight flush appeared on Tsukuyomi’s face, and she only responded softly.

« I wonder which Jonin our instructor will be, » Senju Matsuaki said curiously.

The two were currently walking up the stairs toward the rooftop of the school building.

About to bid farewell to the Ninja Academy where they had spent a year, they both felt a little sentimental.

However, so as not to keep their instructor waiting, they both quickened their pace simultaneously.

After stepping onto the rooftop, their field of vision opened up.

Not only could they see most of the Ninja Academy’s scenery, but they could even catch glimpses of the passersby outside the school.

As for the rooftop itself, it was generally a place where Ninja Academy students chatted and ate during breaks.

It was currently class time, so there were no students on the rooftop, and it appeared quite empty.

And apart from the two who had just arrived, there was only one other person already there.

That figure had long black hair, wore a Konoha Ninja headband properly on his head, and was dressed in a black tight-fitting combat suit.

Over the black combat suit, he wore a set of red layered armor, which looked quite imposing.

Upon seeing this person, Hyuga Tsukuyomi covered her small mouth in surprise, and Senju Matsuaki was also stunned.

« Hokage-sama ! ? »

« Uncle Hashirama ? »

Responding to them was the boisterous laughter of Senju Hashirama.

« Hahaha, you’ve finally arrived. »

Since yesterday, he had been very curious about what reaction Senju Matsuaki and Hyuga Tsukuyomi would have when they met their Jonin instructor.

And at this moment, their reaction certainly did not disappoint Senju Hashirama.

Although Senju Matsuaki was surprised, because he was very familiar with Hashirama, he quickly reacted.

As for Hyuga Tsukuyomi, she had been so shocked by this news that she couldn’t speak.

In the eyes of Konoha Ninjas, Senju Hashirama was a god-like existence.

In their eyes, as long as Senju Hashirama was in Konoha, there was no need to worry about threats from Ninja Villages in other countries.

Senju Hashirama and Uchiha Madara were undisputedly the top two people in the entire Ninja World.

In the current understanding of Ninjas, Ninjas could generally be divided into Genin, Chunin, Jonin, Kage, and Senju Hashirama and Uchiha Madara.

In his memory, even the elders of the Hyuga Clan’s main family spoke with awe and wariness when mentioning Senju Hashirama.

« In other words, Uncle, you are our Jonin instructor ? »

Although he had already guessed the answer in his heart, Matsuaki still confirmed it.

« That’s right, » Hashirama nodded with a smile.

« Considering that Genin of similar ages have relatively similar strength and future growth rates, when forming teams, we first consider grouping people from the same grade together. »

« The number of people graduating from the graduating class and those graduating early from other grades happened to be a multiple of three, so only the two of you were left. »

Speaking of this, Hashirama continued to praise.

« But having said that, among the Genin who just graduated this year, you two are the youngest, yet your strength is ahead of the others ; this is the first time I’ve seen such a situation. »

Matsuaki didn’t feel there was anything wrong with having only one teammate in his team.

On the contrary, he was somewhat glad that he had instantly defeated Uchiha Kai during the graduation exam yesterday.

Although he wasn’t sure if the other person failed to graduate because of this, he thought the reason was probably much the same.

And if the other person had also successfully graduated, he reckoned he would have had to deal with another difficult teammate today.

He didn’t fear a weak teammate ; he only feared a sabotaging one.

One could anticipate and prepare for a teammate with poor strength, but a sabotaging teammate was truly impossible to guard against.

As for Senju Hashirama’s praise, Matsuaki agreed with it in his heart.

Even without cheating, his own talent was not weak ; regardless of whether he had the potential to become a Kage, becoming an Elite Jonin would be no problem.

Elite Jonin were already a rare existence in a Ninja Village.

And since Hyuga Tsukuyomi was able to awaken her eyes early, her talent in the Bloodline Limit was obviously not bad, possessing the potential to become a powerhouse.

« But Hokage-sama, you must be very busy usually ; if you divide your energy to instruct us, will it affect the Village ? »

Tsukuyomi looked nervous and said with concern.

Compared to the joy of having the Hokage as her instructor, Tsukuyomi was actually more worried about this point.

At this, Senju Hashirama scratched his head in embarrassment and said, « Don’t worry, most of the time what appears before you will just be my Wood Clone. »

Although it was just a clone, this was the Wood Clone of the God of Ninjas, Senju Hashirama.

It could be said to be more than enough to instruct and lead two Genin.

Seeming to fear that the two would misunderstand, he hurriedly explained further.

« The me right now is my real body, not a Wood Clone ; after all, this is the first meeting of our Team 11. »

« Team 11 ? » Matsuaki and Tsukuyomi looked at each other.

« That’s right, this is the designation for our squad, Team 11, composed of me and the two of you. »

As he spoke, Hashirama took out two headbands from behind him and handed them to the two.

The two took the headbands, both feeling unavoidably excited.

After thinking about it, Matsuaki tied the headband on his forehead and tied a knot at the back.

The purpose of the headband was in the name : to protect the vital area of a Ninja’s forehead.

Of course, some people preferred to tie the headband in other locations.

For example, right now, Hyuga Tsukuyomi tied the headband around her neck.

Having the headband protect the vulnerable area of the neck was also a good choice.

« But I remember, Uncle, you haven’t served as a Jonin instructor before, have you ? »

Thinking of this, Senju Matsuaki looked suspicious.

« Hey, little Matsuaki, » hearing this, Senju Hashirama hurriedly defended himself.

« When Tobirama served as the Jonin instructor for Monkey and the others before, I also contributed some instruction. »

« And just like when Tobirama led a team before, if I occasionally can’t make time, Tobirama will also temporarily take over my duties. »

« Second Uncle, then I’m relieved, » Matsuaki breathed a sigh of relief.

It wasn’t that he didn’t trust Senju Hashirama.

It was just that he knew his uncle’s tendency to act unreliable from time to time.

When it came to big things, Senju Hashirama was reliable, but for small things, well...

By comparison, Senju Tobirama, who was steady in his actions and decisive in killing, gave a more reliable feeling.

For instance, right now, hearing Matsuaki say this, Senju Hashirama’s expression instantly dropped.

It made people feel as if the surrounding air pressure had also dropped quite a bit.

Senju Matsuaki was already used to this scene.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi, however, was somewhat hesitant about whether to say something to comfort him.

She just felt that the Hokage-sama she was in close contact with was very different from the image described by the elders...
