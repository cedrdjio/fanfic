# Chapitre 87 — Assessment Ended! Mission Settlement!

Out of a total of ninety jade chess pieces, he had already secured thirty, pocketing one-third of the total quantity.

« Um... there is something I might need to remind you of. The second stage of the assessment only requires you to carry a jade chess piece to the finish line. There are no requirements regarding the quantity, nor is there a ranking list based on the number of pieces collected. »

Seeming to suspect that the two had misheard the assessment requirements at the beginning, the Proctor hesitated for a moment before speaking.

« I know. » Senju Matsuaki nodded, his expression unchanging.

The atmosphere became slightly awkward for a moment.

« Alright, then I will count them now. »

The Proctor crouched down, quickly finished counting the thirty pieces, and then stood up.

Then, he also took out a storage scroll from his bosom and placed the thirty jade chess pieces inside.

« The count is correct ; there are indeed thirty pieces, and they are all jade chess pieces. »

The Proctor looked at the two, revealing a smile of approval and affirmation.

Such a result was enough to prove that the two’s strength could not be underestimated.

If they were Ninjas from other Villages, the Proctor would likely have already felt a strong sense of wariness in his heart.

However, the fact was that they were the younger generation of Konoha, which only made him feel gratified.

Conversely, the surrounding Ninjas from other Villages were already furrowing their brows, with a hint of killing intent hidden in the depths of their eyes.

Even Dou and Yan had grave expressions, mentally elevating the importance of Senju Matsuaki to another level.

They simulated the scenario in their minds, but ultimately discovered a fact that was hard to accept.

That was, even with their strength, achieving such a feat would be anything but easy.

« Although the assessment has no requirements for the collection quantity, given the special circumstances, I will still make a note of it for you. »

The Proctor showed the paper and pen in his hand to Senju Matsuaki and Hyuga Tsukuyomi.

Regarding the two collecting thirty chess pieces, the Proctor had no objections ; on the contrary, he felt it had lightened his workload.

After all, the more candidates who passed the assessment, the greater the number of records they would have.

At the same time, in the subsequent third stage of the assessment, the workload of the Proctors would also rise accordingly.

Senju Matsuaki thought for a moment and said : « Senju Matsuaki and Hyuga Tsukuyomi. If you must record the quantity, then let’s make it fifteen for each. »

Upon hearing this, Hyuga Tsukuyomi was about to correct him, but after seeing Senju Matsuaki smiling and shaking his head at her, she swallowed her words.

She didn’t think the quantity should be split fifty-fifty, as Senju Matsuaki had contributed much more.

However, Senju Matsuaki held a different view on this.

Their contributions in this regard could not be accurately measured.

Although Senju Matsuaki contributed more in the process of seizing the pieces, in terms of searching for targets, it was almost entirely due to the efforts of Hyuga Tsukuyomi.

Without the help of her Byakugan, getting fifteen pieces in the end would likely have been an extraordinary performance.

« Alright, the recording is complete. You are free to move about. If you need medical attention, you can go to Konoha Hospital. The final assessment results will be announced at a unified time after the assessment concludes. »

After the Proctor finished instructing Senju Matsuaki and his companion, he turned and left to wait for the arrival of other candidates.

« Then let’s go back and get some good rest ; there’s not much point in staying here. »

Senju Matsuaki hesitated for a tenth of a second before making his choice.

Having stayed in the primeval forest for four or five days, even with a little cleaning every day, he felt uncomfortable all over after such a long time.

If he, a boy, felt this way, not to mention a girl with higher hygiene requirements.

Moreover, Senju Matsuaki was not like Yan from Sunagakure or Dou from Kumogakure.

These two were both previously Jonin of their respective Villages, and now, having been « demoted » to Genin by their Villages to participate in the Chunin Exams, they vaguely acted as leaders or persons in charge among the candidates.

Simply put, they were each invisible team leaders.

As for Senju Matsuaki, he was just one of many candidates from Konoha ; he didn’t need to be responsible for others, nor did he have the obligation to provide help to anyone else.

Theoretically, every candidate in the Chunin Exams is a competitor to one another.

However, because the participants of this Joint Chunin Exams were the Five Great Ninja Villages, they were naturally divided into different groups.

But even so, it did not rule out the possibility that some Genin might, based on their understanding of fellow Genin from their Village, turn against candidates from their own Village.

And in this second stage of the assessment, quite a few such situations did indeed occur.

Even when Senju Matsuaki was alone earlier, he had encountered a group of Konoha Genin who had formed a team spontaneously and showed hostility toward him.

It was just that, in the end, it seemed someone in the team recognized him, and out of wariness, they didn’t actually make a move.

Regarding this, Senju Matsuaki even felt a little regretful.

Although he personally did not want to snatch the chess pieces of Konoha candidates, if the other party made the first move, he would gladly accept them.

Unfortunately, they pulled back from the brink.

Before leaving, Senju Matsuaki looked at Yan and Dou once again, his red eyes filled with undisguised killing intent and Battle Intent.

And the two of them showed no intention of backing down either ; Yan’s gaze was cold and cruel, while Dou’s eyes were filled with Battle Intent.

The moment their gazes met, it seemed as if even the air solidified slightly.

They all knew in their hearts that the next time they met, it would likely be at the final third stage of the assessment.

At that time, there would be no other complex factors interfering ; it would be a fierce battle truly facing each other’s edge !

« Let’s go back, Tsukuyomi. » Senju Matsuaki suppressed the excitement in his heart and said gently to the girl beside him.

This excitement stemmed from the anticipation of strong enemies.

In the past few months, his strength had advanced by leaps and bounds, yet he had been unable to find a matching opponent.

Encountering Yan from Sunagakure a few days ago, he had thought he would have the opportunity to clash with the future Third Kazekage, but in the end, it was left unsettled due to concerns about Dou, who was eyeing them covetously from afar.

« Boring. »

After Senju Matsuaki left, Yan frowned slightly, wondering why that pair of cold, scarlet eyes appeared in his mind again.

Feeling inexplicably irritable, he snorted coldly, no longer wanting to stay there waiting for others, and turned, flicking his cloak, and strode away.

[B-rank mission complete ! Seize the jade chess piece in the second stage of the Joint Chunin Exams. Reward : 3000 points !]

[B-rank mission complete ! Defeat other candidates in the second stage of the Joint Chunin Exams. Reward : 2550 points !]

On the way back to the Village, the notifications for the rewards of the two completed missions sounded in Senju Matsuaki’s mind.

Adding the gains from the two missions, he ultimately obtained a total of 5550 points from the second stage of the assessment.

For a moment, Senju Matsuaki’s savings were far greater than they had ever been before.

Thinking of this, Senju Matsuaki couldn’t help but feel great.

« Tsukuyomi, go home and tidy up ; I’ll treat you to barbecue tonight. »

« Eh ? Okay~ »
