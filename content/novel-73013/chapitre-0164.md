# Chapitre 164 — Tailed Beast Council?

« Big Brother, have you lost your mind ? »

Senju Tobirama stood up abruptly, his eyes beneath his silver hair filled with rare disbelief.

« Distribute Tailed Beasts to other nations ? What terrifying strategic weapons those are ! »

« If we hand them over, and in the future they turn their guns on Konoha, how are we supposed to deal with that ? »

He tapped his finger heavily on the table, his tone cold and sharp.

« Furthermore, giving them away for free ? Big Brother, this isn’t charity ; this is gambling with the lives of every Ninja in the village ! »

« We, Konoha, spend so much manpower and effort capturing Tailed Beasts, only to end up working for free for other Ninja villages. I do not agree with this ! »

The murmurs in the conference room ceased abruptly, and the gazes of the Clan Chiefs shifted back and forth between the two brothers.

Uzumaki Mito frowned slightly but did not interject, simply watching the situation in the room quietly.

Senju Matsuaki, who had been silent all this while, gave a light cough.

« Second Uncle, your concerns are not without merit. »

He met Tobirama’s sharp gaze, his voice steady.

Others did not find his interjection inappropriate.

In the Ninja World, it has never been about seniority, but about respecting strength.

Possessing Kage-level strength at such a young age, Senju Matsuaki’s future was arguably even more brilliant than that of his two uncles.

Senju Matsuaki continued : « Big Uncle’s original intention is to maintain the balance of the Ninja World and avoid an arms race. »

« Matsuaki is right ! » Senju Hashirama laughed.

At this point, Senju Matsuaki changed his tone : « However, 'giving them away for free' is indeed too idealistic. »

« ... »

The smile on Senju Hashirama’s face froze, and he scratched his head in embarrassment.

Senju Matsuaki paused for a moment, tracing a circle on the tabletop with his fingertip as if outlining the contours of some contract.

« We don’t have to give them away for free ; we can 'trade' them. »

« Trade ? » Tobirama narrowed his eyes.

« Correct. »

Matsuaki nodded, « While distributing the Tailed Beasts to the Five Great Nations, we must sign a strict 'Tailed Beast Restriction Treaty'. »

« Each nation must provide equivalent rare resources in exchange, such as the secret techniques of each Ninja village, the forging techniques of the Hidden Mist’s Seven Ninja Swords, the Lightning Release of Kumogakure, or even vast amounts of economic resources. »

Senju Tobirama’s eyes lit up : « This plan is good. »

To be honest, he wasn’t all that enthusiastic about Tailed Beasts either.

Come to think of it, Tailed Beasts were not indispensable to Konoha.

As long as Senju Hashirama and Uzumaki Mito were in Konoha, subduing and sealing Tailed Beasts was easy ; what harm was there in giving them to other Ninja villages ?

If the two were not in Konoha, then hoarding a large number of Tailed Beasts in Konoha would undoubtedly be a bomb that could explode at any moment, and Konoha lacked the power to control them.

In this way, Tailed Beasts seemed somewhat like a burden to the current Konoha.

Senju Tobirama had never advocated for Konoha to control all the Tailed Beasts ; he simply didn’t want to strengthen other Ninja villages.

But the idea Senju Matsuaki proposed now provided a relatively reasonable solution.

If other Ninja villages wanted Tailed Beasts, they would have to exchange their own heritage for them !

For a moment, everyone present began to discuss in hushed tones.

When the noise gradually died down, Senju Matsuaki looked at Senju Hashirama and continued.

« Big Uncle, there is one more crucial point : the Ninja villages that obtain the Tailed Beasts may not use them to maintain peace as you hope. »

« It is highly likely that the Tailed Beasts will ultimately become weapons of war for the Ninja villages. »

« This... »

Senju Hashirama had to admit that Senju Matsuaki’s perspective was correct.

Especially the recent incident where Uchiha Madara controlled the Nine-Tailed and attacked Konoha, along with the rebellion of some members of the Uchiha Clan, had moved him.

Senju Matsuaki continued.

« More importantly, we must take the lead in establishing a 'Tailed Beast Council'. »

Senju Tobirama asked curiously : « Tailed Beast Council ? What is that ? »

Senju Matsuaki explained : « The Tailed Beast Council will be composed of Kage-level powerhouses from the Five Great Nations, jointly supervising all sealed Tailed Beasts and the sealing work. »

« If any nation uses the power of a Tailed Beast to launch an invasion without the Council’s permission, the other four nations must join forces to impose sanctions. »

« Council... »

Tobirama stroked his chin, his expression softening slightly, but his skepticism remained.

« How can we ensure the treaty isn’t torn up ? How can we supervise it ? »

« That is why the treaty must include the strictest breach clauses. »

Matsuaki looked at Mito, « In addition, based on Aunt Mito’s Sealing Jutsu, we can improve a monitoring mechanism to be implanted inside the Jinchuriki. »

« Once Tailed Beast Chakra is maliciously and excessively extracted, the Seal will instantly send a feedback signal to the Council, and it can even interfere with the caster under certain conditions. »

« I believe that with Second Uncle’s R&D capabilities and Aunt Mito’s attainments in Sealing Jutsu, achieving this will not be difficult. »

Upon hearing this, Senju Tobirama and Uzumaki Mito nodded slightly.

Finally, the corners of Senju Matsuaki’s mouth curled into a confident arc : « If there really is a Ninja village that tears up the agreement and uses a Tailed Beast to cause chaos, it doesn’t matter. »

« With Big Uncle here now, and me here in the future, mere Tailed Beasts cannot stir up any trouble. »

As his voice fell, Tobirama remained silent for a long time, his reason quickly weighing the pros and cons.

He had to admit that this plan was far more secure than Hashirama’s initial « free distribution » and more controllable than letting the Tailed Beasts run wild.

He finally snorted coldly and sat back down : « Matsuaki’s method is good. »

Listening to Senju Matsuaki’s analysis, Senju Hashirama’s eyes also revealed approval.

« Matsuaki is right ! This can not only reduce the risk of war but also allow nations to keep each other in check... »

« Good ! This is 'balancing power with force, and using binding power' ! »

Seeing that Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama had reached a consensus, the other Clan Chiefs also weighed the options and expressed their stances.

Trading potential threats for tangible resources and rule-based advantages didn’t seem like a loss.

« Then... »

Seeing that the time was ripe, Senju Hashirama slammed the table heavily, his voice booming like a bell.

« This matter is decided as such ; Tobirama will be responsible for drafting the treaty and developing the monitoring jutsu, and the intelligence department will begin searching for information and traces of the Tailed Beasts ! »

« Starting today, the Tailed Beast capture operation officially begins ! »

« Yes ! »

Everyone responded in unison, their voices shaking the roof tiles.

The meeting ended, and everyone dispersed.

Senju Tobirama walked to the window, his eyes deep and thoughtful.

He muttered to himself, with a trace of coldness : « The Tailed Beast Council, huh... I hope those guys can understand the price of following the rules, otherwise... »

Senju Matsuaki heard his Second Uncle’s muttering and knew that the other party actually still had some concerns.

« Second Uncle, there’s no need to worry too much about this. Even if other Ninja villages really tear up the agreement and use Tailed Beasts to start a war, it won’t matter ? »

« Oh ? »

Senju Tobirama cast an astonished look.

Under his gaze, the corners of Senju Matsuaki’s mouth curled into a smile filled with Killing Intent.

« If other Ninja villages are the first to act unjustly, then it will be just right for us to... reshape the order of the Ninja World ! »

« You brat... »

Senju Tobirama was slightly stunned, and then the smile on his face widened as well.

He patted Senju Matsuaki on the shoulder and then strode away, his inner worries having already vanished into thin air.

After the meeting, Senju Matsuaki walked out of the conference room and looked up at the sky.

In the sky, the clouds rolled and unfurled, a scene of peace.

But the landscape of the Ninja World was about to undergo a earth-shattering change.

And he, too, would play a crucial role in this transformation.
