# Chapitre 141 — Brothers, Activate the Sharingan!

Crack—

Trees snapped, the earth cracked, and space shattered like a mirror.

The six Uchiha Clan Ninjas who had just activated their Sharingan lay scattered on the ground.

Three Chunin and three Genin brought him 750 points.

Senju Matsuaki had not struck with lethal intent ; these people were heavily injured and lying on the ground, but he had spared their lives.

It was not because he was soft-hearted, but out of reverence for time.

If he were to accidentally kill an Uchiha here who happened to be the ancestor of some important future figure, then when he returned to the future, the Ninja World would likely be unrecognizable.

Judging from the game’s original plot, changing the past within this Gate of Time and Space would indeed affect the future.

Suppose he accidentally killed an ancestor several generations before Uchiha Madara ; then in the future, there would be no crisis of Uchiha Madara attacking Konoha, which could be considered a good thing to some extent.

But thinking about it from another perspective.

Indra’s Chakra would continue to reincarnate, and if it chose another Uchiha, who knows what kind of unknown, bizarre personality that might be.

By comparison, Uchiha Madara, whose intelligence was relatively transparent, would be better.

Furthermore, if Matsuaki’s journey to the past weakened the Uchiha Clan too severely, causing the Senju Clan to wipe them out before Hashirama was born...

Then whether Konoha would still exist in the future, or whether he would even exist, remained unknown.

Ignoring the Uchiha Clan Ninjas, Senju Matsuaki looked around and then chose a direction to leave.

He didn’t expect to extract any valuable intelligence from these Uchiha Clan Ninjas.

These professional Ninjas were well-trained, and ordinary methods wouldn’t pry a single word out of them.

While specialized interrogation methods might yield enough, Senju Matsuaki didn’t have the time.

The Gate of Time and Space limited the range of activity and time.

Although Matsuaki couldn’t see a countdown for his stay in the Sengoku period, he still needed to race against time.

For him, the best source of intelligence in the Sengoku era was naturally the Ninjas of the Senju Clan.

So, the next step was to use intelligence from the Senju Clan to lock onto the general location of the Uchiha Clan and rescue Uchiha Hikari !

As for the identity issue...

As a large Ninja clan, even the Clan Chief couldn’t claim to have met every single clansman.

Moreover, Senju Matsuaki’s appearance was very typical of the Senju Clan.

At least a quarter of the people in the Senju Clan had the same hair color as him.

« A lone Senju Clan brat, how unlucky~ »

A mocking voice came from the distance, causing Senju Matsuaki to pause slightly, his rapid pace through the trees gradually slowing down.

Why did that sentence sound so familiar ?

Soon, another voice came.

« Brothers, activate the Sharingan ! »

Senju Matsuaki’s mouth twitched : « ... »

Do you guys really love activating your Sharingan that much ?

Just hearing this content, he wondered if he had traveled back to the past again, returning to the moment he encountered those Uchiha Clan Ninjas dozens of minutes ago.

Regaining his focus, Senju Matsuaki quickened his pace and rushed towards the direction the voice came from.

This should be during the war between the Senju Clan and the Uchiha Clan.

Just as the voice from afar suggested, there should be a Senju Clan child surrounded by Uchiha.

In this era, the age at which Ninjas went to the battlefield was even earlier than in later generations.

With his peripheral vision, he could see the trees on both sides flying backward ; after a few breaths, several figures came into Senju Matsuaki’s view.

Three Uchiha Clan Ninjas were currently using a mix of Taijutsu, Ninjutsu, and Kenjutsu to gang up on a Senju Clan youth.

The Senju Clan youth had black and white hair, which was a hair color some Senju clansmen had.

He looked even younger than Senju Matsuaki, roughly about seven years old.

And the three Uchiha Ninjas surrounding him, though not very old, were teenagers in their teens.

Given the gap in age and strength, the Senju youth was gradually exhausting his energy and had sustained multiple injuries.

Dying at the hands of the Uchiha was only a matter of time.

« Water Release : Water Colliding Wave ! »

Just as this Senju youth was about to lose his life, Senju Matsuaki forcefully broke into the battlefield, formed hand seals, and Chakra surged within his body.

Those who would gang up on a youth were merely Chunin or Genin from the Uchiha Clan.

Ninjas at the Jonin level wouldn’t waste time doing such things.

Therefore, the moment the water surged over, the formation that had originally sealed off the Senju youth was instantly scattered.

« Let’s go ! »

Senju Matsuaki, his figure hidden within the water flow, directly pulled up the Senju youth and fled along the direction the water was moving.

The Senju youth, having gulped down a mouthful of water, was about to break free, but upon seeing the clan crest on Senju Matsuaki through the curtain of water, he gave up resisting.

...

In a clearing in the woods.

The Senju youth knelt halfway, supporting himself with his hands, and began to retch violently.

It had been too sudden just now ; he had swallowed several mouthfuls of water, which might have been mixed with some dirt and leaves.

Feeling slightly better, he turned around, slumped on the ground, and looked up at Senju Matsuaki, who was standing against a tree not far away.

« Hello, my name is Senju Danzo. Your Water Release is amazing ; how come I haven’t seen you in the Family before ? »

Senju Danzo thanked Senju Matsuaki with a chuckle.

Hearing his name, Senju Matsuaki raised his eyebrows in surprise.

Hiss, kid, that name of yours...

However, there were many people with the same name among Ninjas, and considering the long span of time, it was perfectly normal for some names to overlap.

« My name is Senju Matsuaki. I don’t like going out much except when executing missions, so you probably haven’t seen me. »

The more one says, the more mistakes one makes, so he chose to gloss over it in one sentence.

Then, before the curious Senju Danzo could continue asking, Senju Matsuaki blocked him with another question.

« Danzo, I am currently executing a mission to rescue captives, but I haven’t been to this area of the battlefield before. »

« Could you please tell me in which direction the Uchiha Clan’s camp is ? »

« Oh, oh, okay ! »

Hearing Senju Matsuaki’s request, Senju Danzo didn’t think much of it.

After all, the other party was asking about the Uchiha Clan’s camp, not any important intelligence about the Senju Clan, so there was no need to be vigilant.

Moreover, such a question actually reduced his suspicion about Senju Matsuaki’s identity.

After all, it was impossible for someone from the Uchiha Clan not to know where their own camp was.

After frowning and carefully identifying the location, Senju Danzo finally pointed in a direction and affirmed : « It’s in this direction ; just keep going straight through the forest and you’ll see it. »

After finishing, his eyes suddenly lit up, and he clapped his hands sharply.

« Um, Big Brother Matsuaki, could you let little brother Danzo also contribute to the rescue of the captives ? »

Senju Matsuaki chuckled and gave a short response : « No. »

During their brief interaction, he had somewhat figured it out.

This youth named Senju Danzo was just a fledgling Genin, and his personality was the typical hot-headed type found in some Senju clansmen.

Simply put, he couldn’t help and might even hold him back.

« I’m leaving now. You should train hard and contribute to the Family when you are stronger in the future. »

Having said that, Senju Matsuaki leaped and fled in the direction Senju Danzo had just pointed out.

Senju Danzo was annoyed for a moment and said loudly : « Damn it, you didn’t take me with you ! »

« Matsuaki, you will regret this ! »

« Danzo, I am the real Hokage ! »

Senju Matsuaki’s response could be faintly heard from afar ; this was a meme spanning time and space.

Back at the original spot, Senju Danzo scratched his head in confusion : « Hokage... what is that ? »
