# Chapitre 53 — "You Will Regret It" (Youth Version)!

Just then, the door to the Hokage Office was suddenly knocked upon, interrupting their thoughts.

« Come in ! »

Upon receiving a response from inside, the person outside pushed the door open. A group of six people walked in orderly, one after another ; it was the Konoha F6, Hiruzen Sarutobi’s peers.

« Hokage-sama, Teacher, we have come to submit our mission... »

Hiruzen Sarutobi, who was leading the group, stopped mid-sentence when he noticed his teacher, Senju Tobirama, looking at them with a strange gaze, causing his voice to gradually trail off.

It wasn’t just Hiruzen Sarutobi ; alarm bells rang in the hearts of Danzo Shimura and the others as well.

Having been students and subordinates for so many years was not for nothing ; whenever Senju Tobirama wore that expression, he was about to find new ways to torment them.

« Monkey, which foot did you step in with just now ? » Senju Tobirama asked suddenly.

« It should be... the left foot. » Hiruzen Sarutobi thought for a moment before saying, a bit uncertainly.

Upon hearing this, Senju Tobirama’s expression turned cold, and dissatisfaction flickered in his scarlet eyes.

« To think you entered with your left foot first ! Monkey, you have truly become slack ! I punish you by demoting you to Genin ! »

« What ! ? » Hiruzen Sarutobi’s eyes widened.

« Teacher, didn’t you say that after I completed a few more missions, I would be promoted to Jonin ! ? »

Facing his always-strict teacher, Hiruzen Sarutobi didn’t dare to get angry, so his face filled with an expression of infinite grievance as he stared at Senju Tobirama in disbelief.

However, Senju Tobirama ignored him and looked at Danzo Shimura, who was standing behind Hiruzen Sarutobi, terrified and silent.

« Danzo, which foot did you step in with just now ? » Senju Tobirama asked in a deep voice.

« I stepped in with my right foot first ! »

Having prepared himself mentally, Danzo Shimura held his head high and stated his answer decisively.

At the same time, he glanced at the dejected Hiruzen Sarutobi beside him and smiled triumphantly in his heart.

Hiruzen, I won this time !

It seems that among us, I am the one who will become a Jonin first.

Thinking of this, his somewhat gloomy face seemed to brighten up a bit.

Hearing Danzo’s decisive reply, Hiruzen Sarutobi turned his head back in disbelief to look at him, while Danzo responded directly with a smug look.

« Danzo, you will regret this ! »

« Hiruzen, I am the one who is the Jonin ! »

The two best friends completed a spiritual exchange through the meeting of their eyes.

However, Senju Tobirama did not praise his answer as Danzo Shimura had expected ; instead, his expression darkened as well.

« Right foot ? Danzo, what did I say before ? Do you understand team spirit ? Why did you step with your right foot when Monkey stepped with his left ? »

« Uh, I... »

Senju Tobirama’s questions left Danzo Shimura completely bewildered ; he opened his mouth but couldn’t say a word.

« Enough, say no more. You will also start over from Genin and reflect properly ! »

Without waiting for his reply, Senju Tobirama made his decision, cutting off whatever Danzo was about to say.

« Alright Tobirama, calm down, don’t make things difficult for the children. »

Beside them, Senju Hashirama watched this farce, feeling a headache coming on, and couldn’t help but reach out with both hands to rub his throbbing temples.

« Hokage-sama ! »

Uchiha Kagami, Homura Mitokado, Utatane Koharu, and Akimichi Torifu, who had not yet fallen victim to his hand, all looked at Senju Hashirama with expectant eyes.

Seeing these four gazes, Senju Hashirama couldn’t help but look away with a guilty conscience.

To be honest, he had actually considered whether he should let these talented youths, who were no longer Genin, fill in.

After all, other Villages were sending Jonin to participate in the Chunin Exams, so it wouldn’t be excessive for Konoha to send a few Chunin, right ?

This was also the reason why he hadn’t immediately spoken up to interrupt Senju Tobirama just now.

But later, he reconsidered ; the other Villages were doing this because they lacked Genin capable of holding their own, and they were worried about losing face at the Chunin Exams.

However, Konoha was uniquely blessed ; they didn’t need to resort to such desperate measures to have people who could hold the line.

Moreover, Konoha was, after all, the host of this Joint Chunin Exams, so they should be more mindful of their influence in this regard.

« But brother, Kumogakure and Sunagakure have both sent Jonin to participate in the Chunin Exams, so when the time comes... »

Senju Tobirama’s expression was inevitably anxious. If it were just an ordinary Chunin Exam, it wouldn’t matter, but this was an unprecedented five-Village joint exam.

If they performed too poorly this time, let alone using this opportunity to boost their influence in the Ninja World, the exam grounds they had worked so hard to build would likely become a stage for other ninja Villages.

By then, half of the layouts and cost investments Konoha had made for these Chunin Exams would likely go down the drain.

The Daimyo and nobles from various countries who were specially invited to watch in Konoha would all just be coming to watch Konoha make a fool of itself.

« Rest assured, Tobirama. This batch also has Matsumi and Tsukuyomi and the others. I believe in their strength. » Senju Hashirama smiled and comforted him.

« Them... »

Upon hearing this, Senju Tobirama’s expression softened. After hesitating for a moment, he withdrew the punishment he had just imposed on Hiruzen Sarutobi, Danzo Shimura, and the others.

However, he had already made up his mind.

If Konoha really performed poorly in the Chunin Exams, he wouldn’t be blamed for using some underhanded tactics himself.

« Phew~ » × 2

Hearing that Senju Tobirama had given up on the punishment that would have demoted them all the way down, Hiruzen Sarutobi and Danzo Shimura both breathed a sigh of relief.

At the same time, they also remembered what Senju Tobirama had said about the actions of the Hidden Cloud Village and the Hidden Sand Village.

Thinking that because of the despicable acts of the Hidden Cloud Village and the Hidden Sand Village, they had almost been reduced to Genin catching cats and walking dogs again, they couldn’t help but feel indignant.

We don’t dare to retaliate against Teacher Tobirama, but don’t we dare to retaliate against you ?

Kumogakure, Sunagakure, we have marked this grudge !

« You executed your mission very well during the last visit from Kumogakure. During these Chunin Exams, you will participate in security maintenance work as well. Do not give other ninja Villages any opportunity to take advantage, do you understand ? »

Senju Hashirama first praised the six for their performance last time, then his expression turned serious, and he pre-scheduled the six people’s subsequent mission assignments.

« Understood, Hokage-sama ! »

« Very good, very spirited ! »

Hashirama looked at the six of them, full of vigor, and showed a gratified look, nodding in praise.

These six were all good seedlings carefully selected by him and Senju Tobirama. Although their talents varied, they were all outstanding among their peers and would surely become the pillars of Konoha in the future.

Senju Hashirama said : « If there is nothing else, you may go back first. »

After Hiruzen Sarutobi and the other Konoha F6 members took their leave, Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama continued to browse the application forms from other Villages.

What made them breathe a slight sigh of relief was that neither Iwagakure nor the Hidden Mist seemed to be pulling such stunts again. The quality of the applicants varied, but they were all within the normal range.

However, at this moment, Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama were filled with sympathy for these two ninja Villages.

Iwagakure, Hidden Mist, honest people indeed...
