# Chapitre 58 — The Three Stages of the Chunin Exams

Just after Senju Matsuaki and the others left, Hiruzen Sarutobi and Danzo Shimura continued their business as usual.

Their business was lukewarm ; although residents or tourists occasionally patronized them, it did not hinder them from carrying out their mission under the guise of running a business.

During the course of their business, they also kept an eye on the passersby within their field of vision with their peripheral vision.

Among them, those Ninjas wearing headbands from other villages were particularly focused on.

Once these individuals displayed any suspicious behavior that they deemed potentially threatening, the two of them would decisively take action to subdue them.

Before long, another figure wandered up to their stall.

The newcomer had distinct physical features : blue hair, yellow eyes, and wore the unique hourglass-shaped headband of the Hidden Sand Village on his head.

Because he was from the Land of Wind, he habitually wore a cloak that covered most of his body.

Yan from Sunagakure, because he shouldered the hope and reputation of the entire Sunagakure, was demoted from a Jonin to a Genin.

He hadn’t even warmed his seat as a Jonin before being demoted to sit on the bench with the Genin ; his heart was as cold as the sandstorms at night in the Land of Wind.

Some of the Jonin leading the team this time had even carried out missions with him not long ago.

However, precisely because of this, the Jonin leading the team this time did not restrict him too much, allowing him to move around freely.

And he himself did not want to stay with a group of Genin, so he wandered around Konoha’s commercial street alone.

« This, this, and this. »

After quickly ordering, Yan stood in place with a slight frown, looking like a man of few words and ruthless deeds.

Seeing the conspicuous Sunagakure headband on this customer’s head, a flash of sharpness appeared in Danzo Shimura’s eyes.

« This customer, you are a Ninja from Sunagakure, right ? »

Changing his usual gloomy expression, Danzo said with a look of admiration.

Yan was taken aback, then nodded gently and said, « That’s right. »

« I have always admired the Ninjas of Sunagakure ; I didn’t expect to have the honor of meeting you today ! »

Upon hearing this, Danzo put down his busy hands, wiped them on the apron in front of his chest, and reached out to shake hands with the other party.

Looking at Danzo’s greasy hands, Yan subconsciously felt some disgust, but out of politeness, he endured the discomfort and shook hands with him.

At the same time, he asked curiously, « You are from Konoha, why do you admire us Ninjas from Sunagakure ? »

« Sunagakure Ninjas possess a resilient quality forged in the sandstorms ; this is what I admire most, » Danzo said with sincere eyes.

« Resilient quality forged in the sandstorms... That’s right, that is us. »

Yan pondered Danzo Shimura’s words thoughtfully, his somewhat tense expression gradually softening, revealing a look of agreement.

« May I ask for your honorable name, Ninja-sama ? » Hiruzen Sarutobi, who was on the side, spoke up at the right moment.

« Yan. »

His name was not a top secret that couldn’t be revealed, especially since Yan had come to participate in the Chunin Exams this time, and his personal information had long been placed on the organizers' desks.

So you’re the one named Yan, huh ! ?

Hiruzen Sarutobi and Danzo Shimura exchanged a glance, both seeing the deep, predatory malice in each other’s eyes.

« It’s rare to see a hero from Sunagakure ; this meal is on me ! »

Danzo Shimura quickly packed the few skewers Yan had just ordered into a paper cup and even added a few extra ones.

« Many thanks. I didn’t expect to find such a discerning person in the streets of Konoha. »

Seeing that Danzo Shimura’s hospitality was hard to refuse, Yan had to take the paper cup, his eyes full of being moved.

« You two, see you later. »

After bidding farewell to the two, Yan took the heavy cup of Oden and walked towards the inn where Sunagakure was staying.

Back at the spot, Hiruzen Sarutobi looked at Danzo Shimura with some confusion.

« Danzo, why didn’t you take his money, and why did you give him the skewers I prepared for dinner as well ? »

« Hiruzen, it seems I have the upper hand this time. » Danzo Shimura smiled contemptuously.

« Why do you say that ? »

« Aren’t we going to give them something big later ? If we aren’t attractive enough to him, how will he come to patronize us next time ? »

« Danzo, you rascal~ »

Konoha, inside the Hokage Office.

A Ninja appeared at the desk using Body Flicker, but Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama, who were handling official business, seemed to have expected it, and their hand movements did not pause.

« Hokage-sama, the candidate teams from Kumogakure, Sunagakure, Hidden Mist, and Iwagakure have all arrived in Konoha. »

« Understood, thank you for your hard work, » said Senju Hashirama.

Poof—

After delivering the message, the Ninja turned into smoke and dissipated on the spot ; it turned out to be just a Shadow Clone.

« Tobirama, where are the Daimyo and other nobles and dignitaries now ? » asked Senju Hashirama.

This aspect of matters was generally handled by Senju Tobirama, while Senju Hashirama was more responsible for the matters of the Chunin Exams themselves.

« Most have already set off, some earlier or later, but there is no problem with them arriving in Konoha before the third stage of the exams. »

« That’s good. »

Watching the first Joint Chunin Exams steadily advancing through his own efforts, a look of gratification could not help but appear in Senju Hashirama’s eyes.

This is a small step for Senju Hashirama, but a giant leap for peace in the Ninja World !

The Joint Chunin Exams combined the current assessment methods of the major Ninja villages in terms of assessment methods and were finally finalized into three stages.

The first stage is a written test, consisting of ten questions in total, used to test whether they possess the knowledge reserve and application ability sufficient to become a Chunin.

In the first stage of the written test, seventy points is the passing line ; candidates with scores lower than seventy are directly eliminated.

The second stage is a large-scale survival exercise. Because the scale of this exam is unprecedentedly large, the venue for the exercise was selected in a primitive forest outside Konoha.

In this stage of the assessment, every candidate will be issued a wooden chess piece.

One-third of these chess pieces are engraved with the character « 玉 » (Jade), and the other two-thirds are blank.

To pass the second stage of the assessment, one must carry a chess piece engraved with the character « 玉 » and cross the entire primitive forest to reach the finish line.

In other words, those who do not hold a « 玉 » piece must snatch the « 玉 » from others, while those who hold a « 玉 » need to protect their piece from being snatched by others.

According to the normal speed of a Ninja, it takes roughly five days to cross the entire forest ; too many things can happen during this period.

Finally, there is the third stage of the assessment.

The third stage of the assessment is not like the paper talk of the first stage, nor is it as fancy as the second stage.

The third stage of the Chunin Exams is to conduct battles in the large-scale examination venue that Senju Hashirama led them to open up at that time.

At that time, the invited Daimyo, nobles, dignitaries, and wealthy people from various countries will also watch the competition as spectators.

In this stage, the only thing candidates need to do is to defeat their opponents in battle as much as possible, while preserving their own strength.

Until the final winner is determined.

However, whether or not the final assessment standards are met to be rated as a Chunin does not absolutely depend on victory or defeat, but rather on the comprehensive performance.
