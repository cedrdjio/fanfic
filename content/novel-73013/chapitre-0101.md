# Chapitre 101 — Sudden Situation, Murderous Intent!

« What do you mean, you have already fallen ? ? »

Hearing this insult, the lecherous Iwagakure Ninja felt a surge of blood rush to his chest. Finally losing his last ounce of strength, his vision went black, and he fell unconscious.

« The match is over, Hyuga Tsukuyomi wins ! » Senju Tobirama announced the result with a smile.

Even he couldn’t help but feel surprised by Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s Rotation just now.

After all, this was an Advanced secret technique of the Hyuga Clan. Being able to learn and use it at such a young age was proof enough of her talent.

« Hokage-sama, you certainly know how to teach your students. »

In the spectator stands, First Tsuchikage Ishikawa said abruptly.

His expression was not good.

Having two candidates from his own Ninja village eliminated in succession by the students of Hokage Senju Hashirama was truly a loss of face for him.

Especially since the Daimyo of the Land of Earth, the wealthy benefactor who provided funding for their Iwagakure, was sitting in the row behind him. With the Iwagakure candidates performing so poorly, it was hard not to feel embarrassed.

At this moment, a remark from the Daimyo of the Land of Fire added fuel to the fire.

Seeing the Konoha candidates perform excellently twice in a row, the Daimyo of the Land of Fire laughed and slapped his thigh in great satisfaction.

« Hashirama, it seems Konoha is developing very well. The funding I gave you hasn’t gone to waste ! ! »

The Daimyo of the Land of Fire and Senju Hashirama were of similar age, and the two addressed each other as peers.

Furthermore, as the two sides were soon to become in-laws, their relationship was closer than that between the Kage and Daimyo of other Ninja villages.

Hearing the praise from the Daimyo of the Land of Fire, Senju Hashirama responded modestly, his mood also excellent.

However, the Daimyo of the Land of Earth, sitting next to the Daimyo of the Land of Fire, suddenly sensed something was wrong.

If your funding didn’t go to waste, doesn’t that mean my funding was essentially wasted ? ?

Thinking of this, he couldn’t help but look at First Tsuchikage Ishikawa with a strange gaze.

After Senju Matsuaki completed his first-round match, the progress of the Chunin Exams continued to move forward steadily.

The setting sun gradually sank, casting a shadow over the arena.

As a Hidden Mist candidate in the arena was bound hand and foot by pitch-black Iron Sand and fell to the ground, the final match came to an end.

« The first round of the third stage of the Chunin Exams has reached its Perfection. Please, candidates and spectators, leave the venue in an orderly fashion ! ! »

« The liveliest street near the hotels you are staying at is the Konoha Food Street. If you need to dine, you may head there yourselves to make purchases ! ! »

After saying these final words, Senju Tobirama vanished from the spot instantly.

The dense crowd merged into several black lines, leaving through the arena gates under the guidance of the staff and heading towards Konoha Village.

In the first round of selection, twenty-eight people were decided from the fifty-six candidates to participate in the next round.

Of the twenty-eight who were eliminated, some went to Konoha Hospital due to injuries, while others left the waiting room early because they were thin-skinned.

The thirty-plus candidates remaining in the waiting room left in groups with people they knew.

« Apologies, my elder brother is also here to watch the match today, and I need to go meet up with him now. »

« It’s fine. Get some good rest tonight and keep up the hard work tomorrow. »

After Hyuga Tsukuyomi apologetically explained the reason why she couldn’t accompany him, she bid farewell to Senju Matsuaki and left.

Looking at the waiting room where only a few people remained, Senju Matsuaki did not linger. After retrieving his stored items from the locker, he left through the exit of the waiting room.

Because there was limited open space within Konoha Village, the arena was located in the forest outside the village.

When Senju Hashirama was excavating this open space to build the arena, Senju Matsuaki was also present to assist.

It was about a ten-minute walk from the arena back to Konoha.

This path was originally just a small trail, most frequently used by workers from Konoha Village who needed to go into the forest to cut wood.

While the arena was being built, Konoha also simultaneously expanded this road, making it flatter and wider.

Senju Matsuaki soon reached the main gate of the arena ; the gray-white gate built of stone bricks looked grand under the setting sun.

The spectators had long since left, and the surroundings only held the staff clearing the area and a few other candidates who had not yet departed.

Near the gate, a black-haired Ninja wearing a Konoha headband was carrying out the task of maintaining order at the exit.

When Senju Matsuaki passed by to leave, the Ninja’s expression was slightly unusual. Watching Senju Matsuaki’s departing figure, his eyes narrowed slightly.

Sensing the strange gaze from behind, Senju Matsuaki frowned and turned his head to look back.

The Konoha Ninja near the gate stood in place with a normal expression, dutifully guarding the gate, while his eyes did not forget to scan the surroundings to ensure no spectators remained in the arena.

Seeing this, Senju Matsuaki suppressed the strange feeling in his heart, and his furrowed brows smoothed out.

« I’m a bit hungry. I should go back early and get something to eat~ »

With a faint, almost imperceptible smile at the corner of his mouth, he strode onto the path back to the village.

The road was slightly winding, so Senju Matsuaki could no longer see the figures of the crowd that had left earlier ahead of him.

After walking for about three or four minutes, there were no traces of spectators or candidates ahead or behind Senju Matsuaki.

The branches and leaves of the thick trees on both sides of the road swayed gently in the evening breeze, and the grass rustled occasionally due to some small animals passing through.

Just then, the sound of hasty footsteps rang out from the dark forest on the right side of the road.

Due to the dim light, Senju Matsuaki could not see clearly who the person was, only a blurry silhouette of an adult’s figure.

Seeing the other party heading straight for his position, Senju Matsuaki couldn’t help but show a look of vigilance.

The figure walked over quickly ; it was a brown-haired Ninja wearing a Konoha headband.

The brown-haired Ninja had a painful expression, clutching his right arm with his left hand. Blood could be faintly seen seeping out from the spot his left hand was covering.

Seeing the Konoha headband that Senju Matsuaki was also wearing on his head, the brown-haired Ninja’s eyes lit up, and a trace of joy appeared on his pained face.

« Great, I am the Konoha Chunin, Harada Takuya. Just now, while escorting the spectators away, a spectator left the group and ran into the forest to relieve himself. When I went to find him, I was attacked by a wild beast ! ! »

« What ! ? » Senju Matsuaki’s pupils constricted.

In the forest at night, nothing was strange.

Although it was not as dangerous as the primeval forest during the Chunin Exams, it was still full of crises for ordinary people who were defenseless.

Previously, Konoha had specifically driven away the wild beasts in the forest on both sides of the road, but there were inevitably oversights.

If a spectator really lost their life due to an attack by a wild beast, even if the cause was their own unauthorized departure from the group, it would still have a negative impact on Konoha.

« How is your condition ? Take me to find that lost spectator ! »

Senju Matsuaki looked at the Chunin’s bleeding arm and asked in a deep voice.

« My arm is injured, but it’s nothing serious. I’ll just bandage it simply. The urgent matter is to save the person quickly ! »

After saying this, the Chunin turned around and ran quickly towards the forest on the right side of the road, and Senju Matsuaki followed closely behind.
