# Chapitre 57 — The Big One Is Still to Come!

« If I’m not mistaken, those things displayed in the cabinet over there are storage scrolls, right ? »

Senju Matsuaki pointed to the colorful scrolls displayed in the cabinet on the other side of the shop and asked.

The Ninja Tool Shop Owner smiled and said, « The distinguished guest truly has a discerning eye. These are the latest storage scrolls I ordered. They not only have a much larger storage capacity, but their summoning speed is also significantly improved compared to the older models. »

After receiving a confirmed reply from the Ninja Tool Shop Owner, Senju Matsuaki continued.

« Although Ninjas are accustomed to taking out various Ninja tools, especially large ones, and holding them firmly before using them... »

« But if you operate precisely, perhaps you can skip the aiming and throwing steps and directly summon Ninja tools from the scrolls to overwhelm the enemy. »

« This... this must be such a dazzling offensive ! The distinguished guest is truly talented ! »

The Ninja Tool Shop Owner imagined such a scene in his mind based on Senju Matsuaki’s description and couldn’t help but clap in praise.

« I would like to call it the 'Hundred Weapons Chaos Technique' ! I must let my juniors learn this technique ! »

The Ninja Tool Shop Owner clenched his right fist and held it in front of his chest, his face filled with determination.

In his heart, he had secretly made up his mind to further expand the Ninja Tool Shop’s business to provide enough funds and Ninja tools for the next generation to use the Hundred Weapons Chaos Technique.

« Ah, this... »

Looking at the other’s attire, which resembled the clothing of the ancients from his previous life, coupled with this slightly familiar name, a sense of déjà vu could not help but arise in Matsumi’s heart.

It can’t be that much of a coincidence, right...

Coming back to his senses, the Ninja Tool Shop Owner looked at the two of them enthusiastically.

« They say the early bird catches the worm, and it’s true. I opened for business early this morning and actually met two distinguished guests ! »

« Take whatever Ninja tools you like ; it’s on the house today ! »

« Er, isn’t that a bit inappropriate ? »

« What’s inappropriate about it ? Take it, take it ! »

Five minutes later.

Senju Matsuaki and Hyuga Tsukuyomi were sent out by the Ninja Tool Shop Owner, both of them holding a storage scroll in their hands, feeling a mix of amusement and helplessness.

It seemed that seeing the two of them were embarrassed to accept the Ninja tools for free, the grateful owner simply stuffed some commonly used Ninja tools into the storage scrolls and shoved one into each of their arms.

« Sigh, why are so many people giving me scrolls lately ? »

Holding the heavy scroll in his hand, Matsumi felt the sincere emotions from the villagers of Konoha Village.

Counting Hiruzen Sarutobi and Danzo Shimura, who gave him Ninjutsu scrolls a few days ago, this was already the third scroll.

Holding the trust of the villagers in his hand and looking up at the blue sky, a feeling of emotion welled up in his heart.

« Could it be that, without realizing it, I have become a prominent figure in Konoha Village ? »

Otherwise, why is everyone giving me gifts ?

« Hehe, Matsumi really is an interesting person~ »

Hyuga Tsukuyomi on the side covered her mouth and chuckled.

Seeing this, Senju Matsuaki returned a warm smile.

The previous words were naturally just joking. He knew very well why the Ninja Tool Shop Owner was grateful to them.

For Ninjas without Bloodline Limits or secret technique inheritances in their families, there is an insurmountable gap between them and Ninja clans that pass down Bloodline Limits or secret techniques.

And for a civilian family starting from scratch to produce an excellent Ninja is even harder than ascending to heaven.

Unless one is lucky enough to find some excellent Ninjas to apprentice under when young, it is difficult to bridge this gap on one’s own.

For the Ninja Tool Shop Owner just now, he had no Ninjutsu or secret technique inheritance ; all he had were Ninja tools and money.

Don’t look at his shop as conventional and not very big ; the Ninja tool business is highly profitable.

Senju Matsuaki’s suggestion just now gave him the possibility of turning Ninja tools and capital into a Ninja’s foundation, so he was naturally grateful.

After leaving the Ninja tool shop, the two walked back.

It was already morning, and the pedestrians on the road were gradually increasing.

Not only that, one could even occasionally see Ninjas from other villages carrying big and small bags, strolling on the streets of Konoha Village.

In front of a street stall not far away, a familiar dark, rough figure was counting the bills in his wallet.

Seeing these three familiar figures, Senju Matsuaki’s footsteps paused.

The reason why they were called three familiar figures was because at this moment, Hiruzen Sarutobi and Danzo Shimura’s limited-time Oden cart was open for business again.

« Customer, here you go. That will be 500 Yen, please~ »

Seeing Hiruzen Sarutobi, who was disguised as a vendor, smiling as he handed the Oden in his hand to the future Third Raikage, Dou.

This time, his business was more proficient, and there were no omissions like forgetting to ask for money.

« The price went up ? »

Dou muttered, but didn’t say anything more ; instead, he decisively took out money to pay.

Not long ago, he was a Jonin and had quite a lot of savings, so he wouldn’t eat himself into poverty even if he ate freely.

« Still the familiar delicious taste~ »

After taking a bite, he found it was still the familiar taste. Dou gave Hiruzen Sarutobi and Danzo Shimura a look of praise, then turned and left.

Watching the other’s retreating back, Danzo Shimura frowned, turned around, and grabbed Hiruzen Sarutobi’s collar.

« Hiruzen, you really prepared a delicious Oden for him ? Didn’t you recognize who he was ? ? »

« Don’t be anxious, how could I forget him. »

A sharp light flashed in Hiruzen Sarutobi’s eyes as he sneered.

This despicable and shameless Cloud Ninja actually pretended to be a Genin in order to save face at the Chunin Exams.

That would be fine, but he also caused them to almost be demoted to Genin by Teacher Tobirama. Who could tolerate this ?

Although he had entrusted Senju Matsuaki to take revenge for him, this did not mean that he would not take action himself.

« Then why did you... »

Seeing that the anger on Hiruzen Sarutobi’s face did not seem fake, Danzo Shimura hesitantly let go of his hands.

« It would be too hasty to make a move now. Let him eat something good first ; the big one is still to come ! »

Hiruzen Sarutobi said with a smile, but the cold light in his eyes made Danzo Shimura’s heart tighten.

« Hiruzen, you’re a good one ! »

« Danzo, I am the real schemer ! »

In the distance, Senju Matsuaki took in the interaction between the two, and his expression couldn’t help but be a bit strange.

He was still curious before about who had offended them to the point of entrusting him to beat up Genin from the Hidden Cloud Village and Hidden Sand Village.

Now it seemed that the one who had provoked them was undoubtedly this future Third Raikage, Dou.

Although he didn’t know exactly what had happened, it was clear that the grudge was not small.

At this time, Senju Matsuaki still thought that this future Third Raikage had come as a team-leading Ninja.

He never thought that the other’s status in this Joint Chunin Exams would be a participating Genin.

The Hiruzen Sarutobi and Danzo Shimura in the distance were both carrying out missions, so it was naturally inconvenient to greet them.

The purpose of going out today had been over-fulfilled, so after Senju Matsuaki and Hyuga Tsukuyomi walked a distance together, they bid farewell to each other.
