# Chapitre 97 — Even in Earth Release, There Are Gaps!

The first round of the third stage of the Joint Chunin Exams continued to progress, while Senju Matsuaki sat in the lounge, closing his eyes to rest.

In the lounge, some Genin were also sitting in chairs, conserving their energy, while others ran to the outer stands to watch the battles in the arena.

At this moment, Senju Matsuaki was not paying attention to the exams, but instead had his mind completely immersed in his Golden Finger.

Just after the exams began, the Golden Finger platform had issued him a task after a long time.

[B-Rank Task Issued ! Win until the end of the third stage of the Joint Chunin Exams. Reward : 3000 points !]

[B-Rank Task Issued ! Defeat opponents during the third stage of the Joint Chunin Exams. Reward : 50 points for Genin strength, 200 points for Chunin strength, 500 points for Jonin strength !]

It was just like before ; either it wouldn’t issue tasks for a long time, or it would issue several at once.

The first task was simple to understand : achieve the final victory in the third stage of the exams.

For Senju Matsuaki, there were two hurdles in this process : Yan from Sunagakure and Dou from Kumogakure.

These two were likely the only candidates present besides Senju Matsuaki who possessed Jonin-level strength ; they were undoubtedly formidable enemies.

If these two fought each other as opponents before encountering Senju Matsuaki, then Senju Matsuaki would only need to face one opponent with Jonin-level strength.

If the two did not meet in advance, then Senju Matsuaki would need to fight them one after the other.

Fighting two Jonin in succession would be quite a lot of pressure for Senju Matsuaki.

However, at this moment, Senju Matsuaki actually hoped that Yan and Dou would not meet in advance.

Only in this way could he pocket the rewards for defeating Jonin in the second task !

The second task rewarded points based on the strength of the defeated opponent.

Judging from the current situation of the candidates, those with Chunin-level strength made up the majority, while those with Genin-level and Jonin-level strength were in the extreme minority.

After all, everyone who managed to last until the third stage of the exams, aside from a small number of Genin just making up the numbers, were Ninjas whose strength exceeded the threshold of a Chunin.

...

« Next match, Konoha’s Senju Matsuaki, against Iwagakure’s... »

Just as Senju Matsuaki was still lost in thought with his eyes closed, the voice of Senju Tobirama echoed from above the arena.

After catching the keywords, Senju Matsuaki opened his red eyes, and a cold glint flashed across the bottom of them.

In the distance, an Iwagakure Ninja also stood up as the voice appeared.

After noticing the movement on Senju Matsuaki’s side, the expression of this Iwagakure Ninja suddenly became cold.

Just a moment ago, this Iwagakure Ninja was one of the people backing that lecherous Iwagakure Ninja, so he could be considered to have a grudge against Senju Matsuaki.

« You brat from Konoha are finished ; you’ve drawn the strongest person among us Iwagakure Ninjas this time ! ! »

The lecherous Iwagakure Ninja who had tried to strike up a conversation earlier laughed and slapped his thigh, looking at Senju Matsuaki with a mocking expression.

« The strongest ? »

Upon hearing this, Senju Matsuaki was not angry but instead laughed, and his expression even became a bit kinder.

Since this lecherous Iwagakure Ninja said the contestant he drew was the strongest among the Iwagakure candidates, that should mean he has Chunin or even Jonin strength, right ?

The difference in reward points between these three levels was certainly not small.

At this moment in Senju Matsuaki’s eyes, his opponent, who was looking at him with an arrogant and provocative expression, was simply like glowing reward points.

However, Senju Matsuaki’s relaxed, even slightly smiling expression made the Iwagakure Ninja who was his opponent furious.

To laugh so easily and comfortably before the fight had even started !

After the battle, was he going to smile, brush off his clothes, and say, « My sleeve is slightly dirty » ? ?

« Overconfident brat, you’ll have plenty of time to cry later ! ! »

After dropping this harsh threat, the Iwagakure Ninja quickly walked out of the lounge and hurried toward the arena along the narrow staircase on the right side of the stands.

« Matsuaki, good luck~ »

Hyuga Tsukuyomi clasped her hands in front of her chest and whispered encouragement.

At the same time, Hiruzen Sarutobi, Danzo Shimura, Uchiha Kagami, and others who were also in the lounge, although they couldn’t speak aloud due to performing their duties, also nodded silently in acknowledgment.

« See you later. »

After responding to these words and looks of encouragement, Senju Matsuaki quickly walked down the steps and into the arena.

Arriving in the arena in person, he could observe some details he hadn’t noticed before.

For example, on a separate stand high up was the figure of Senju Tobirama, overseeing the whole situation.

On the edges of the arena stood two Ninjas from the Nara Clan and the Yamanaka Clan, responsible for maintaining order inside the exam venue and ensuring the safety of the candidates.

The secret techniques of the Nara Clan and the Yamanaka Clan were uniquely effective in stopping a target’s movements.

Once a candidate inadvertently or intentionally failed to hold back, the Ninjas of the Nara Clan and the Yamanaka Clan would instantly subdue them using their secret techniques.

Standing opposite each other in the arena, the Iwagakure Ninja’s face was still filled with contempt and coldness.

He didn’t mock or provoke him too much, yet judging from his expression, it was clear he didn’t take Senju Matsuaki seriously.

This phenomenon made Senju Matsuaki feel somewhat strange.

He had encountered many Iwagakure Ninjas in the second stage of the exams, and he hadn’t killed them all.

Logically speaking, these Iwagakure Ninjas he had easily defeated should have passed his information and intelligence to their comrades in the same village.

But in reality, this Iwagakure Ninja in front of him, including those he had conflicted with earlier, all seemed to have no knowledge of him.

This doubt of Senju Matsuaki’s was, however, insignificant before the impending battle.

After both sides stood firm, Senju Tobirama quickly announced the start of the battle.

« Battle start ! »

« Earth Release : Earth-Cracking Palm ! ! »

Although his expression was arrogant and contemptuous, the Iwagakure Ninja moved swiftly after the battle started, showing no mercy.

After quickly forming hand seals, he slammed his hands onto the ground, and Chakra poured from his palms into the ground.

The originally flat ground instantly cracked, starting from where his palms touched, and the crack, like a winding, thick black snake, bit fiercely toward Senju Matsuaki with the sound of the earth splitting.

This scene made many spectators and candidates in the stands brighten up.

Not rushing into battle when it started, but using Earth Release Ninjutsu to interfere with the enemy from a distance to break their balance was a common trick for Ninjas who excelled in Earth Release Ninjutsu.

If they could seize the opportunity, the scales of victory in the following battle would undoubtedly tilt toward the Iwagakure Ninja.

After all, not everyone had the ability to fly and could avoid the influence of the ground.

Even Senju Matsuaki at this moment did not have the ability to fly.

Although he couldn’t fly, Senju Matsuaki had full confidence in Earth Release !

If this Iwagakure Ninja’s Earth Release Ninjutsu met the standard of a Chunin, then Senju Matsuaki’s Earth Release Ninjutsu could be said to have already reached the threshold of a Jonin !

This Iwagakure Ninja should realize that even in Earth Release, there are gaps ! !

Just as the Iwagakure Ninja had a fierce look on his face, waiting for Senju Matsuaki to reveal a flaw under the Earth-Cracking Palm, Senju Matsuaki began to form hand seals.
