# Chapitre 151 — Reunion!

Uchiha Hikari stood alone in place, the morning breeze brushing past the tips of her hair, causing that wooden hairpin to sway gently.

She raised her hand to touch the small flower on the hairpin, her fingertips cold.

A tear fell silently, hitting the ground and soaking into a small, dark mark.

She bit her lip lightly, but another thought quietly rose in her heart.

In those black eyes that had been silent for a long time, a flash of crimson flickered once more.

One month later.

Uchiha Hikari stood in front of the wooden cabin, taking one last look at the small house where she had lived for two months.

She had taken good care of everything inside the house.

The tables and chairs were wiped clean, the blankets were folded neatly, and the jars and bottles in the kitchen were arranged in an orderly manner.

In that storage cabinet, the money left by Senju Matsuaki was packed into a sewn-up backpack.

She closed the door and turned to leave.

The black flames of Amaterasu burned the wooden cabin and everything inside to ashes.

She didn’t want others to occupy this place after she left.

Uchiha Hikari did not look back and walked away.

...

Senju Matsuaki opened his eyes.

The familiar shrine ruins ; the air was filled with a faint scent of dust.

He had returned to his own era.

He looked ahead.

The stone tablet originally engraved with the crests of the Senju Clan and the Sarutobi Clan stood there, but now it was completely blank.

The Seal beneath it, naturally, no longer existed.

« ... »

Senju Matsuaki was speechless for a moment.

Judging from the state of the sealed area, he had indeed changed the past.

Perhaps Uchiha Hikari’s fate was just like the one changed at the end of the game’s plot.

Freed from the shackles of her Family, she would spend the rest of her life as a free person, only occasionally reminiscing in her heart about the person who had saved her back then.

From a chronological perspective, in the year he saved Uchiha Hikari, his grandfather’s elder brother, Senju Butsuma, had only just been born.

By now, Uchiha Hikari would surely have already become a handful of yellow earth.

Everything felt like a dream.

He looked down at his hands ; he could almost still smell the scent of firewood and cooking smoke.

The girl’s voice seemed to still echo in his ears.

He shook his head to suppress these thoughts.

At this moment, a notification of task completion appeared.

[A-rank task completed : Save Uchiha Hikari. Reward : 5,000 points !]

[B-rank task completed ! Defeat enemy Ninjas during the Sengoku period. Final settlement reward : 3,000 points !]

Both tasks were issued shortly after he had passed through the Door of Space-Time to return to the past.

The first task provided a fixed reward of points.

The second task was settled based on the number of Genin, Chunin, and Jonin he defeated.

By the time he returned, the reward was exactly 3,000 points.

He suddenly froze.

He checked his Golden Finger platform.

In the column for incomplete tasks, a single item was strikingly listed !

[S-rank task issued : Unseal the Ultimate Evil Weapon. Reward : 10,000 points !]

Under his stunned gaze, this line of text changed !

[S-rank task issued : Unseal Uchiha Hikari. Reward : 10,000 points !]

It changed from « Unseal the Ultimate Evil Weapon » to « Unseal Uchiha Hikari. »

A change of just a few words caused Senju Matsuaki’s pupils to shrink suddenly.

This meant that Uchiha Hikari was still sealed.

Everything he experienced when he returned to the past—that wooden cabin, that bowl of hot porridge, that hairpin, that night she leaned on his shoulder—had none of it changed her ultimate fate ?

The task wasn’t completed ?

But he had clearly changed history ; the Sealing Ground within this shrine no longer existed.

Just then, as if he had remembered something, he quickly took out the scroll he had previously taken from Uncle Hashirama’s house.

On its front, a map was still drawn.

However, this map was completely different from the original map in his memory ; the location marked was not where he was currently standing.

Additionally, the warning originally written on the side to remind the descendants of the Senju Clan to maintain the Seal was also blank.

Instead, a passage of text had appeared there.

[I’ll be waiting for you at home.]

Senju Matsuaki stared at that line of text, his pupils shrinking violently.

He was all too familiar with these words.

During the two months they lived together in the wooden cabin, he had heard the girl say this every time he went out.

For him, who had just returned from traveling through space-time, this was a sentence he had heard not long ago.

If anyone else saw this sentence left on the scroll, they would likely be confused.

But Senju Matsuaki breathed a sigh of relief immediately.

This at least meant that Uchiha Hikari was no longer sealed because she was viewed as a sinister weapon, as originally intended.

But rather... it was her own choice ?

Suddenly, a detail he had overlooked surfaced in his mind.

On the eve of their parting, after Uchiha Hikari asked if he was from the future and received an affirmative answer, she had been lost in thought.

Senju Matsuaki snapped his eyes open.

He understood.

After he left, Uchiha Hikari didn’t stay in that small town either.

She knew he was from the future and that he would one day return to his own era.

She also knew she couldn’t go to the future with him.

So she made a decision.

Since she couldn’t reach the future in an instant, she would let time flow slowly.

And so, she sealed herself away until they could meet again in the future.

As for the method of the Seal...

Since this scroll from the Senju Clan still existed, the Seal must still be related to the Senju Clan ?

More doubts could only be cleared up by asking her in person.

« That idiot... »

Senju Matsuaki cursed softly, his eyes flickering slightly.

People of the Uchiha have intense emotions.

To put it negatively, they might become increasingly paranoid and stray onto the wrong path.

To put it positively, their emotions are fiery, and they do not give up easily.

He had underestimated Uchiha Hikari’s feelings for him.

He had rescued her from her cage and spent two warm months with her like Family.

In the girl’s heart, he had already left a deep impression.

Senju Matsuaki took a deep breath, suppressing his churning emotions, and his gaze became firm once more.

...

A few days later, he returned to the place where that wooden cabin had once stood.

The place Uchiha Hikari called « home. »

Times had changed, and the former small town was now nothing but ruins.

Senju Matsuaki drew his short sword, the blade covered with the power of the Tremor-Tremor Fruit, and began to dig.

Before long, traces of a Seal were revealed.

He formed hand Seals and released the Seal.

As the dust cleared, a transparent crystal was revealed beneath the site of the old wooden cabin.

Inside the crystal was a black-haired girl.

Her eyes were closed, her hands were crossed over her chest, and her expression was as peaceful as if she were fast asleep.

A wooden hairpin was tucked into her hair, with a small five-petaled flower carved on the tip ; having weathered the passage of time, it was still as good as new.

Senju Matsuaki stood before the crystal, looking down at her.

He saw the faint tear track at the corner of her eye.

It was left there at the final moment before she was sealed.

She was waiting for him.

Senju Matsuaki slowly crouched down, pressing his palm against the surface of the crystal ; a cold sensation traveled from his palm.

« I’ve come to get you, Hikari. »

He said softly.

As the Seal was released, cracks appeared on the surface of the crystal, spreading like a spider’s web.

Crack—

The first crack.

Crack, crack—

More and more cracks appeared, and the girl’s eyelashes inside the crystal flickered slightly.

The next second.

The crystal shattered with a bang, turning into countless shimmering fragments dancing in the air.

Uchiha Hikari’s eyelids trembled slightly, and that familiar figure appeared in her vision.

Her eyes seemed to be veiled in a layer of mist as her body leaped out from within the crystal.

Senju Matsuaki reached out and caught her.

Warm body heat radiated through the fabric, accompanied by a faint fragrance.

He looked down at her.

In Uchiha Hikari’s pitch-black eyes, his face was reflected.

She blinked, as if confirming whether the person in front of her was real.

Then, the corners of her mouth curled up slightly, revealing a faint, relieved smile.

« Long time no see. »

Her voice was a bit raspy, yet it carried an irrepressible joy.

Senju Matsuaki looked at her and smiled as well.

« Long time no see. »

He tightened his arms, holding her steadily in his embrace.
