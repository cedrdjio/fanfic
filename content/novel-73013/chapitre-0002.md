# Chapitre 2 — The Mission, Isn't It Here?

Hearing these words, Senju Matsuaki opened his eyes wide in surprise.

Beyond the arrogance and disdain contained in this remark, he was even more surprised by this stereotypical villainous statement.

« Are you the one using cheats, or am I ? Why are you so arrogant ? »

« If you keep acting like this, I’ll know exactly where my next mission is coming from. »

« The Hyuga Clan is nothing special. I really don’t know why anyone would compare the Uchiha Clan to the Hyuga Clan ! »

Following the voice, one could see that the person speaking was a boy with an arrogant expression.

He was wearing a dark blue short-sleeved shirt, and the biggest difference between him and his classmates was the Uchiha Clan crest—a fan pattern—printed on the back of his clothes.

Before Konoha was established, especially during the era of Hashirama and Uchiha Madara, the Senju and Uchiha were evenly matched, and other Ninja clans, including the Hyuga Clan, were not as strong as the two.

However, in the years since Konoha was established, the Byakugan of the Hyuga Clan has demonstrated excellent auxiliary capabilities, keeping their reputation in Konoha consistently good.

Conversely, the reputation of the Uchiha Clan has declined because some of its members are naturally arrogant and difficult to get along with.

The Byakugan and the Sharingan, the Hyuga and the Uchiha—as fellow dojutsu clans of Konoha, it is natural for them to be compared.

« Uchiha Kai, you... »

Hearing these words, even the usually gentle-tempered Hyuga Tsukuyomi flushed with anger, but her good upbringing left her momentarily unsure of how to retort.

Since Hyuga Tsukuyomi and he had always had a good relationship, Senju Matsuaki did not hesitate.

Walking to the girl’s side, his red eyes met those of Uchiha Kai, who had just spoken.

« As far as I know, among the students of the previous year, no one from the Uchiha Clan passed the graduation exam early, while one member of the Hyuga Clan did. »

Without using insults to strike back, Senju Matsuaki simply stated the fact calmly.

Upon hearing this, Uchiha Kai’s arrogant expression suddenly froze, and his Face turned somewhat gloomy.

This was indeed a fact, and it was hard to refute.

Even though all the Uchiha Clan students from the previous year applied for the graduation exam, the final number who passed was zero.

Among these people, besides some who were just making up the numbers, there were indeed some who had reached the standard of a Genin, but the flaw was that there was nothing outstanding about them.

Konoha is not currently in urgent need of Genin, and the policy for early graduation is tightening ; the Village, with the intention of protecting fledglings, has not been blindly letting people pass.

Instead, one student from the Hyuga Clan in the previous year passed the early graduation exam because they were quite outstanding in the use of the Byakugan and had initially demonstrated tactical awareness of team coordination.

Simply put, this is the difference between average talent and special talent.

However, Senju Matsuaki understood these intricacies, while these young Ninja Academy students were confused.

Therefore, regarding Senju Matsuaki’s words, Uchiha Kai was stunned for half a minute, unable to come up with a retort.

Seeing the other person stunned, Senju Matsuaki shook his head and continued.

« While pursuing power, you should also recognize your own limitations. Otherwise, not only will you harm yourself in the future, but you will also put your partners in danger. Weighing your own level before deciding whether to take the exam is a wise move ; there is nothing shameful about it. »

These words were not only spoken to Uchiha Kai ; in the pin-drop quiet classroom at that moment, they were clearly heard by everyone.

As a descendant of the Senju Clan, and having grown up under the influence of Hashirama, Tobirama, and others, he also felt a sense of responsibility toward this Village.

Of the classmates present, a small minority would become Jonin, while the rest would either remain Chunin or Genin for the rest of their lives, or leave the Ninja ranks to join other professions.

But no matter where they shine, they are ultimately the future of Konoha and are contributing to Konoha.

Therefore, Senju Matsuaki did not want anyone to suffer irreparable losses in the graduation exam and their subsequent Ninja career because they misjudged their own abilities.

Hearing these words, some who were originally struggling with whether to apply for the early graduation exam relaxed their brows, and their expressions softened significantly.

As if thinking of something, Uchiha Kai, who had been stiff-faced all along, suddenly smiled : « Talking so much nonsense, could it be that you also don’t dare to apply for the graduation exam, and are making excuses for your own cowardice ? »

Senju Matsuaki crossed his arms and looked at him, shaking his head : « No, I will apply to take the graduation exam. »

Hearing this, Uchiha Kai sneered : « That’s good. »

« Senju Matsuaki, don’t think that just because you’ve been first in the Ninja Academy for the past year, you’re actually something special. »

« To accommodate these commoners, the practical exams in the first year of the Ninja Academy are like playing house. However, the graduation exam is no longer like that. »

Uchiha Kai picked up a Kunai from his pouch, his black eyes and the sharp blade both pointed toward Senju Matsuaki, and said : « I will let you know that, as a Ninja, you ultimately rely on strength to do the talking. »

Just as his voice fell, two notifications suddenly appeared in Senju Matsuaki’s mind one after another.

[C-Rank mission issued : Defeat Uchiha Kai in the Ninja Academy graduation exam and make him understand who is the one that relies on strength to do the talking. Reward : 50 Points !]

[C-Rank mission issued : Pass the graduation exam. Reward : Reward points calculated based on the final grade !]

After realizing what happened, Senju Matsuaki raised an eyebrow, and his originally sharp eyes appeared even more spirited.

The long-awaited missions had finally appeared, and two of them at once.

The first mission alone could reward 50 points, and although the reward for the second mission was unknown, it was estimated that it would not be less than the first one.

The combined rewards of these two missions were already worth more than what he had accumulated over the past month.

For a moment, Senju Matsuaki’s gaze toward Uchiha Kai became much kinder.

When I beat you up during the graduation exam, I’ll go a little easier on you...

« Since that’s the case, I will accept the challenge. I hope you aren’t just all talk. » Senju Matsuaki’s gaze sharpened, and he said in a serious tone.

Looking directly into those red eyes staring at him, Uchiha Kai, who had always been proud of being a descendant of a dojutsu clan, felt a sense of trepidation for no reason.

Afraid of losing Face, he withdrew his hand holding the Kunai, averted his gaze, and instead looked around at the others in the classroom.

« The graduation exam is a one-on-one elimination screening ; these losers can’t stop me at all. I hope you don’t get eliminated halfway through. »

Uchiha Kai’s words clearly aroused public anger, causing other commoners and Ninja clan members to glare at him.

But helplessly, what he said was indeed the truth.

Although his personality was annoying, Uchiha Kai’s strength was indeed one of the best in this year.

Although the daily practical exams were always stopped at the point of contact, he often fought aggressively and injured his classmates.

« Hmph ! »

Glancing at Senju Matsuaki and Hyuga Tsukuyomi by his side, Uchiha Kai snorted coldly and then turned and left.
