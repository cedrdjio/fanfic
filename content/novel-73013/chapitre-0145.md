# Chapitre 145 — Jailbreak Time!

Early the next morning.

A faint ray of sunlight filtered through the dungeon entrance, signaling the arrival of a new day.

The passage of time within the dungeon offered no tangible sense of reality ; one could only discern the alternation between day and night by the sliver of light leaking from the entrance.

Senju Matsuaki opened his eyes and peered toward the cell opposite his.

Even though it was daytime, and even with a sliver of light from the outside world seeping in, the interior of the dungeon remained dim.

However, because his eyes had gradually grown accustomed to the dark environment, Senju Matsuaki could see more clearly.

Uchiha Hikari was curled up asleep in the corner of her cell, her limbs positioned in an instinctive defensive posture.

Enemies could not be trusted, and clan members could not be trusted ; in this cruel era, she had completely lost all support.

« Mister, when is the minimum number of people left to guard this camp ? »

Senju Matsuaki knocked on the earthen wall with his right hand and asked in a low voice.

« Hmm ? »

The Senju Clan member in the next cell seemed not to have expected anyone to ask him such a question, and he was stunned for a moment.

After realizing what was happening, he said, « How do you not even know this ? Well... looking at your age, this must be your first time on the battlefield in this war. Sigh... »

« There is no such thing as a minimum number of personnel guarding the camp ; the number of people is constantly changing dynamically. »

After thinking for a moment, he continued to reply in a low voice.

« If a sudden situation arises, even in the middle of the night, the personnel in the camp might all swarm out. »

« Conversely, due to tactical arrangements, sometimes during the day, everyone might turtle up inside the camp instead. In short, it’s unpredictable. »

« However... »

Suddenly, as if remembering something, the Senju Clan member’s tone became slightly hesitant.

« However, what ? » Senju Matsuaki’s eyes lit up, and he pressed for an answer.

The Senju Clan member pondered for a moment and said, « However, based on past situations, by now, some Uchiha bastard should have already brought the unpalatable dry rations and taken away the newly arrived prisoners for interrogation. »

« Although I have no way of judging the specific time, it should already be past the usual time point. »

« In other words, the Uchiha people might have left the camp in large numbers due to the war, resulting in a lack of extra manpower to do these things ? » Senju Matsuaki asked thoughtfully.

He recalled the Uchiha Clan member who brought him here yesterday, who had threatened him after shoving him into the cell.

He said that the next day, he wouldn’t be as comfortable as he was last night.

And now, although he was somewhat unaccustomed to the crude environment of the dungeon, he considered himself to be quite comfortable.

Since he was comfortable, then the Uchiha Clan must not be.

The battlefield changes in an instant ; no one can fully predict what will happen next, but at least the major trends can be forecasted.

Judging from the number of Senju Clan prisoners in the dungeon, as well as the number of wounded in the Uchiha camp and the weary, oppressive atmosphere he had glimpsed yesterday, this war between the two clans had reached its most intense stage.

Senju Matsuaki did not have a sufficient understanding of this period of history ; he did not know who won or lost, only that the Senju Clan and the Uchiha Clan still existed after their intense clashes.

However...

Senju Matsuaki glanced again at Uchiha Hikari in the opposite cell.

From the historical wariness the Senju Clan and the Sarutobi Clan held toward Uchiha Hikari, as well as his partial understanding of history, he could slightly infer some future events.

Next, the Senju Clan should pull in the Sarutobi Clan as allies ; after all, the relationship between the two clans had always been friendly, and they had cooperated many times in history.

Subsequently, the Uchiha Clan would be at a disadvantage when facing the two clans combined, and the turning point would fall upon their carefully cultivated weapon, « Uchiha Nameless. »

Uchiha Hikari, having been sent to the battlefield as a weapon, obviously caused the Senju Clan and the Sarutobi Clan heavy losses ; otherwise, the predecessors of the Senju Clan would not have expressed such wariness in the records.

But looking at the outcome, Uchiha Hikari was eventually sealed by the joint efforts of the two clans, and even after the war ended, she was not unsealed by the Uchiha Clan. It stands to reason that the Uchiha Clan was the losing party.

Thinking of this, Senju Matsuaki had a plan in mind.

Whether now was the best time to escape, he wasn’t entirely sure.

But if he missed this opportunity, he didn’t know how much longer he would have to wait.

His goal was not just Uchiha Hikari in the opposite cell, but also the other fellow clansmen trapped here.

After long-term imprisonment, some members of the Senju Clan were already hanging by a thread, and might even completely perish after suffering through hunger today.

Even if only for the sake of these clansmen, he could not wait any longer.

« Hey, kid, you aren’t planning on breaking out of jail, are you ? »

The Uchiha deserter from last night, who had expressed deep hatred for Uchiha Hikari’s disobedience to clan orders, suddenly sneered.

The dungeon had so little space in total ; hoping that lowering one’s voice would prevent others from hearing was truly self-deception.

He did not believe that Senju Matsuaki, who had only arrived here yesterday, could really succeed in breaking out of jail.

Every Senju Clan prisoner thought there was a possibility of escape, yet most of them were now lying in their cells like dead dogs.

Only he, a member of the Uchiha, knew that while the dungeon environment was crude and poorly ventilated, these iron bars were among the sturdiest in the Ninja World.

However, even knowing that the Senju Clan kid would only fall into despair after countless attempts, it didn’t stop him from treating this as a form of entertainment during his boring, monotonous time in prison.

« Correct. »

Senju Matsuaki replied casually, his tone devoid of any fluctuation.

Then, the power of vibration, which had been silent for the night, surged again on his right fist.

In the opposite cell, Uchiha Hikari, who had woken up at some unknown time, was the first to see this scene clearly.

Those hollow, lifeless eyes rarely carried a look of surprise and astonishment, as she watched the white light that suddenly appeared in the gloom.

The Awakening of the Mangekyo did not just bring her powerful ocular jutsu ; more fundamentally, it also enhanced her Mental Energy and sensory abilities.

She was the youngest among everyone present, but she was the one most capable of perceiving the danger level of that white sphere of light.

She could feel that if that power of vibration were to erupt beside her, it would likely shatter every bone in her body in an instant.

Instinctively feeling fear, she shrank her body toward the corner of the cell, but her pitch-black eyes could not hide the look of curiosity as she peered at the opposite cell.

In the next cell, the Uchiha Clan member, who could not see the movement here due to the angle, was still mocking, « If you can break out of jail, I’ll eat... »

Bang—

In the next instant, the sound of a mirror shattering rang out, and the originally indestructible iron bars fractured into countless pieces, flying out and embedding themselves into the rock wall.

The power of vibration smashed the walls around Matsuaki’s cell, kicking up a cloud of dust.

« Child, you... »

The Senju Clan member in the next cell opened his mouth, and before the look of astonishment could fade, a look of joy climbed onto his pale face.

Being able to break through one cell meant being able to break through the second, the third...

The clansmen are saved !

« Wait a moment, Mister ! »

After leaving behind these words, Senju Matsuaki first stepped out of the cell and walked straight toward a cell on the other side.

« Kid, you... someone come quickly ! Someone is breaking out of jail ! ! »

Seeing Senju Matsuaki actually come from another cell to the front of his own, the Uchiha Clan member wore an expression of terror, as if he had seen a ghost.

After reacting, he began to shout loudly for the clansmen outside the dungeon.

« You’re the one who talks the most ! »

Senju Matsuaki frowned slightly and swung his right fist.

The sound of space fracturing rang out, and the noisy sounds inside the dungeon immediately went silent.
