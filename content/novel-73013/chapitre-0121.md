# Chapitre 121 — Anger and Vows!

Mastering a Devil Fruit ability is not something that happens overnight, especially with the Tremor-Tremor Fruit, which is inherently better suited for wide-ranging, powerful attacks.

Upon noticing the leakage of power, Senju Matsuaki silently retracted his Devil Fruit ability.

This situation, on one hand, showed his lack of control over the fruit’s power, but on the other hand, it was not necessarily a bad thing.

It meant that this power from the Tremor-Tremor Fruit was gradually merging with him, becoming a part of his instincts.

Inside the medical room, the faint tremor just now did not attract the attention of the medical Ninja or Hyuga Teruake.

Even Senju Hashirama, the strongest person present, only tilted his head as if he had sensed something, but after finding nothing unusual, he dismissed it as an illusion.

Only Senju Tobirama, who mastered space-time Ninjutsu and was the most sensitive to spatial fluctuations, was slightly taken aback, a look of astonishment flashing in his eyes.

He certainly perceived that this spatial tremor had originated from near his good nephew.

It was just that he couldn’t figure out, no matter what, where this vibration force, which seemed to act directly on the surrounding space, had come from.

Even he, who considered himself extremely sensitive to spatial perception, couldn’t help but wonder if it was an illusion.

After all, the power of space was not something easily touched in this world.

Feeling his second uncle’s gaze, Senju Matsuaki remained composed.

He had no intention of hiding anything in front of his family.

At the very least, these abilities he had mastered were not things that needed to be hidden.

Of course, not hiding them did not mean he had to show them off immediately ; that required the right timing.

Right now, Yan, who had ignited the anger in his heart, would undoubtedly be his first choice to test his blade.

« Hyuga Tsukuyomi is my teammate, and I will make Yan of Sunagakure pay the price ! » Senju Matsuaki said in a deep voice.

These words drew a side-glance from Hyuga Teruake.

His gaze swept over Senju Matsuaki, and although he did not speak, a hint of skepticism was clearly visible in his eyes.

He had watched the entire third stage of the examination, and judging solely by his combat performance, Senju Matsuaki was indeed impressive.

But in his opinion, there was still a significant gap between Matsuaki and Yan of Sunagakure.

The former was merely an outstanding Genin of Konoha, while the latter had already begun to make a name for himself in the Ninja World.

A Jonin’s strength combined with the Magnet Release Bloodline Limit...

It was shameful to admit, but even Hyuga Teruake himself did not have absolute certainty that he could defeat Yan of Sunagakure.

Since that was the case, where did a Genin like him get the confidence to defeat the opponent ?

However, out of courtesy, Hyuga Teruake did not express his doubts, but instead offered words of encouragement.

« Then I will leave my sister’s revenge to you. »

After saying this, Hyuga Teruake sighed and turned his gaze to Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama standing nearby.

« Thank you both for your concern, but since my sister is not in serious condition, I won’t take up any more of your time. »

After speaking, Hyuga Teruake glanced at the unconscious Hyuga Tsukuyomi, then turned to leave.

Possessing the Byakugan, he could naturally roughly assess Tsukuyomi’s injuries, though he was ultimately not as skilled as a professional medical Ninja.

Upon learning that she was not in serious danger, his heart settled, and he intended to return to his duties.

Seeing this, the brothers Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama exchanged a glance and walked over to Senju Matsuaki’s side in unison.

Then, Matsuaki felt two broad, powerful palms pat his shoulders, one on the left and one on the right.

Poof—along with a puff of smoke, the two dispelled their Clones and left.

Standing there, Senju Matsuaki nodded in understanding, his handsome face filled with coldness.

Although his first and second uncles hadn’t spoken, he understood the meaning of that gesture instantly.

« Beat that Sunagakure brat to death for us ! ! »

The third round of the examination was originally intended to have 14 people compete to determine 7 winners.

However, in the previous assessment, the two Kumogakure Ninja who had taken the stage as opponents and performed shocking feats in public both gave up their opportunity to participate in the subsequent assessments.

With such an unexpected situation, the third round of the examination could only determine six candidates in the end.

Matsuaki did not encounter any noteworthy opponents in the third round.

In this Joint Chunin Exam, the only ones he could call opponents were Yan of Sunagakure and Dou of Kumogakure.

As for the other candidates, whether to Senju Matsuaki or to Yan and Dou, they were all cannon fodder.

Before the fourth round of the examination, the Daimyo and Kage on the viewing platform, as per custom, drew the opposing candidates for this round.

There were a total of six candidates in the fourth round, so they just needed to be divided into three pairs.

Therefore, after discussing among themselves for a few moments, the Daimyo simply decided to draw the opponents for all three matches at once.

« As the host, we of the Land of Fire will yield this opportunity to everyone else. »

The Daimyo of the Land of Fire covered the lower half of his face with a folding fan and said to the other Daimyo with a smile.

« In that case, I won’t be polite. »

The Daimyo of the Land of Lightning did not wait for the other Daimyo to react and was the first to reach into the box and pull out a paper ball.

After opening it, he held the slip of paper close to his eyes.

« Hmm... Senju Matsuaki. »

After seeing it clearly, he read out the name on the slip word by word.

At the same time, the First Raikage also proceeded to pull another slip of paper from the box.

Seeing this, the other Daimyo nearby became somewhat expectant.

The Daimyo of the Land of Water said with a smile : « If that kid from the Senju Clan, where the Hokage is from, runs into that kid from Sunagakure who plays with Iron Sand, or that kid from Kumogakure who is as strong as an ox, it would probably be very exciting~ »

Now he had figured it out ; it was unlikely that the Hidden Mist Ninja from his own country would have any spectacular performance from here on out.

Although he was indeed unhappy deep down, the Daimyo of the Land of Water quickly let it go and instead adopted the attitude of a spectator to watch the upcoming matches.

« Mine is... » the First Raikage read as he unfolded the slip, « Hidden Mist’s... »

Upon hearing this, the smile on the face of the Daimyo of the Land of Water froze.

Well, all hope had truly been extinguished now.

There were now six candidates remaining in the fourth round, two of whom were from Konoha, followed by one candidate from each of the other four Ninja villages.

In other words, Senju Matsuaki’s opponent this time was the last candidate from the Hidden Mist.

« It seems your luck is as bad as ever~ » the Daimyo of the Land of Fire teased with a smile.

Although the Daimyo present were not Ninja, they had a certain level of discernment themselves.

They might not have been experts, but they were certainly not ignorant.

Not to mention, among their personal bodyguards were some powerful Ninja.

Therefore, they could also tell that this Hidden Mist candidate was definitely no match for Senju Matsuaki.

« Hey, hey, the battle hasn’t even started yet, the final result is not certain ! » the Daimyo of the Land of Water insisted stubbornly.

« Then let our Land of Water draw for the next match ! »

Seemingly to change the subject, the Daimyo of the Land of Water immediately started the draw for the next match.

After unfolding the slip, he said : « I drew Dou of Kumogakure. »

« And here is, Iwagakure’s... »

The First Mizukage also proceeded to read out the other candidate he had drawn.

Then, the last pair of candidates naturally didn’t need to be drawn ; it was Yan of Sunagakure and the other candidate from Konoha.

« Oh, what a coincidence~ » the Daimyo of the Land of Earth said with a dubious smile.

The three seeded candidates, Senju Matsuaki, Dou, and Yan, had completely avoided each other before the semi-finals.

If he thought this was just a pure coincidence, then he would have wasted all his years sitting in the position of Daimyo.

Senju Hashirama clearly failed to grasp the hidden meaning in the words of the Daimyo of the Land of Earth, and just revealed a somewhat honest smile and said : « Yeah. »

He had completely left the matter of drawing lots to his good younger brother Tobirama.

Whether there was any manipulation involved, he really didn’t know.
