# Chapitre 12 — I'm First Already?

In his current state, Uchiha Kai was clearly unable to perform the Sign of Reconciliation.

After Uchiha Kai was carried away by medical staff, another teacher from the Ninja Academy stepped forward to repair the arena.

Generally speaking, during the graduation exam, most people were only at the Genin level and would not cause too much damage to the arena.

It was a rare occurrence for someone to smash a deep crater with a single punch, like Senju Matsuaki had just done.

After the arena was restored, it was time for the second match : the battle between Hyuga Tsukuyomi and Sarutobi Go.

Sarutobi Go was a twelve-year-old boy, so he looked significantly taller and bigger than Senju Matsuaki and the others.

Moreover, just like his name suggested, his appearance was sturdy and fierce.

If Hiruzen Sarutobi was like a monkey, then Sarutobi Go was like a gorilla.

Furthermore, Senju Matsuaki had also been paying attention to the previous battle.

It was evident that Sarutobi Go’s strength was not far from the Chunin level, and he had long since surpassed the standard of an average Genin.

As for why he hadn’t graduated early, perhaps it wasn’t that he couldn’t, but simply that he didn’t want to.

For an average person, encountering such an opponent in the graduation exam would mean very low odds of winning.

However, Hyuga Tsukuyomi was not an average person.

Having already awakened the Byakugan and being able to skillfully combine it with the Gentle Fist, she could not be measured by the standards of an average person.

There would be a tough fight between Hyuga Tsukuyomi and Sarutobi Go, but the outcome remained uncertain.

« Tsukuyomi. »

Senju Matsuaki didn’t say anything, just patted Hyuga Tsukuyomi on the shoulder and gave her an encouraging look.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi, who had been somewhat nervous, felt her expression relax quite a bit for some reason.

After responding to Senju Matsuaki with a faint smile, she turned and walked toward the center of the arena.

Seeing this, Sarutobi Go also walked to the center of the arena with a solemn expression.

During the previous rounds of battles, he had also noticed this girl who was much younger than him but possessed formidable strength.

A member of the Hyuga Clan who had awakened the Byakugan was indeed a troublesome opponent.

However, he did not believe he would lose.

In terms of age, he was five or six years older than Hyuga Tsukuyomi.

Although it might be shameful to say so, this was his greatest advantage.

« Both sides, perform the Sign of Confrontation ! »

After performing the Sign of Confrontation as per custom, the battle between the two began immediately.

At the very beginning of the fight, Sarutobi Go seemed to want to subdue Hyuga Tsukuyomi before she could activate her Byakugan.

However, for a Ninja of the Hyuga Clan who had mastered the Byakugan, the speed of activation was almost negligible.

After Sarutobi Go’s strategy fell through, the two began to trade blows back and forth.

Just as Senju Matsuaki had predicted, the two were evenly matched.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s Byakugan and Gentle Fist were able to cause some trouble for Sarutobi Go.

But Sarutobi Go was self-aware ; he knew that he couldn’t unblock the acupuncture points sealed by the Gentle Fist on his own.

Therefore, he tried his best to avoid the Gentle Fist, and when he couldn’t dodge, he tried to take the hits with less vital parts of his body.

At the same time, he also disrupted Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s offensive with shuriken, taijutsu, and Ninjutsu.

Coming from the Sarutobi Clan, Sarutobi Go had a much larger repertoire of Ninjutsu compared to his peers.

At the very least, Sarutobi Sasuke and the others outside the arena were satisfied with his current performance.

Although Sarutobi Go was not a top-tier genius, it was a certainty that he would become a Jonin in the future.

And whether for a Ninja clan or the Village, a Jonin was already a pillar of strength.

Soon, over ten minutes had passed.

Although both sides had tried their best to conserve energy, their Chakra levels were running low.

For a graduation exam, this fight had already lasted quite a long time.

It was estimated that the outcome would be decided within the next few minutes.

It was precisely because they knew this that both Hyuga Tsukuyomi and Sarutobi Go in the arena did not rush into attacking.

« I thought I had taken her seriously enough, but I didn’t expect to still underestimate her. The Byakugan of the Hyuga Clan truly lives up to its reputation, » Sarutobi Go lamented inwardly.

By now, many of his acupuncture points had been struck by the Gentle Fist.

The obstruction in his Chakra circulation made him somewhat uncomfortable.

And because of this, many Ninjutsu felt awkward to use.

« But if that’s all there is to it, then I will take this victory ! »

With this thought, Sarutobi Go reached out, pulled out three shuriken, and threw them toward Hyuga Tsukuyomi.

At the same time, he drew a Kunai and followed closely behind, hoping to catch Hyuga Tsukuyomi off guard with the cover of the shuriken.

Based on their previous exchanges, he could somewhat estimate her speed.

If she wanted to dodge the shuriken, Hyuga Tsukuyomi would have to face his Kunai head-on.

In such a rush, merely setting up a defensive stance would be considered a timely reaction, let alone using the Gentle Fist to counter.

If he weren’t facing the Gentle Fist, Sarutobi Go believed he wouldn’t lose.

However, the direction of a battle can sometimes be unexpected.

When both sides are giving their all, the side with more trump cards has a better chance of winning.

Previously, Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s only trump card was the Byakugan.

Relying on the Byakugan and Gentle Fist, she possessed an advantage over her peers.

As for whether she would soar in the future or fade into obscurity, that would depend on her personal opportunities and development.

But now, under the influence of Senju Matsuaki, her trump cards were not limited to just the Byakugan.

In the span of a single breath, Sarutobi Go felt his vision blur.

A white figure flashed past in front of him and instantly vanished from his field of vision.

« What ! ? »

After realizing what had happened, Sarutobi Go felt a chill run down his spine.

Turning around abruptly, what appeared in his line of sight was a pair of petite fists.

It’s over...

This was the last thought that appeared in Sarutobi Go’s mind.

What followed was the Gentle Fist, landing precisely on multiple acupuncture points on his body.

« I lost. »

Collapsing powerlessly onto the ground, Sarutobi Go smiled bitterly.

« Both sides, perform the Sign of Reconciliation ! » Seeing that the match had a result, the Proctor also announced loudly.

Outside the arena, Senju Matsuaki, who had been intently watching the match, also breathed a sigh of relief.

The battle between the two belonged to the category of the most watchable matches.

Both sides were evenly matched, trading blows back and forth, neither yielding to the other, creating an incredibly intense situation.

Fortunately, in the end, there was a good outcome for Hyuga Tsukuyomi.

After both sides left the arena, it was time for the two who had drawn the number three to step up and fight.

Compared to the second match, the third match ended much faster, with a result within five minutes.

Therefore, the ones who passed the third round of the elimination tournament were only Senju Matsuaki, Hyuga Tsukuyomi, and the winner of the third match.

« Sorry, I have no energy left to participate in the next round of the exam, »

Hyuga Tsukuyomi said, raising her hand while looking at the Proctor with some embarrassment.

In the just-concluded match, not only was her Chakra almost exhausted, but she had also sustained some minor injuries.

The reason she was able to suddenly burst forth and win in the end was thanks to the Breathing Technique that Senju Matsuaki had taught her during this period.

However, her progress in the Breathing Technique was not fast ; she could only maintain it for a very short time, and it placed a significant burden on her body.

Therefore, she had absolutely no energy left for what was to come.

At this point, the pressure fell onto the other person.

After glancing at the calm-looking Senju Matsuaki, this student from the graduation year also immediately raised his hand and said.

« Sorry, I was also injured just now, and I can’t continue with the following exams either. »

In fact, he still had some energy left.

It was just that after seeing Senju Matsuaki’s battle, he really felt intimidated.

Just a few punches, and he had hammered down an Uchiha who had performed quite well in the previous rounds.

Who could withstand that ?

Upon hearing this, Senju Matsuaki was slightly stunned.

I’m first already ?
