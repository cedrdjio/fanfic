# Chapitre 160 — Group Brawl! Madara Defeated!

« Damn it ! Nine-Tailed ! »

Uchiha Madara felt a strong sense of crisis rising in his heart ; he knew he could not let this entanglement continue.

He looked up sharply, his Eternal Mangekyo Sharingan spinning rapidly, regaining control of the Nine-Tailed.

A scarlet light flashed in the Nine-Tailed’s eyes, and it let out a roar to the sky, its massive body nimbly galloping toward Uchiha Madara’s direction.

Uchiha Madara leaped with a solemn expression, landing on the head of the Nine-Tailed.

Powerful Chakra surged into the sky, making the surrounding air become viscous.

« Uncle Hashirama, it seems we cannot just stand by anymore. »

Senju Matsuaki withdrew his gaze and looked at his uncle beside him.

Senju Hashirama took a deep breath, and the expression on his face also became serious : « Indeed, we cannot let them continue this chaos. »

The scene of Uchiha Madara attacking the Village with the Nine-Tailed had already been seen by many people.

At this moment, both he and Senju Tobirama had left the Village, so the hearts of the people within the Village must be in turmoil.

Senju Matsuaki smiled slightly, a hint of Battle Intent flashing in his eyes : « Then let me stretch my muscles a bit too. »

« Big Brother, let’s go ! » Senju Tobirama said in a deep voice, his gaze falling on Senju Hashirama.

As soon as the words fell, Senju Tobirama had already activated the Flying Thunder God Technique, and his figure instantly disappeared from the spot.

The next second, he had appeared near the Flying Thunder God marking left on the back of the Nine-Tailed.

« Water Release : Water Dragon Bullet Technique ! ! »

With hands forming seals, a huge water dragon rose from the ground out of thin air, crashing fiercely into the flank of the Nine-Tailed.

The Nine-Tailed was tilted by the impact of the water dragon, but Uchiha Madara stood motionless on its head.

His Eternal Mangekyo Sharingan turned slightly, and the skeletal frame of the Susanoo emerged, blocking all the aftermath of the water dragon.

« Senju Tobirama, you are still the same, only knowing how to sneak attack. » Uchiha Madara said coldly.

His expression was no longer as calm as it was at the beginning.

Senju Hashirama, Senju Tobirama, Senju Matsuaki, plus that strange pink creature...

Even with the Nine-Tailed’s assistance under his control, it was impossible to be their opponent.

Just then, Senju Hashirama’s hands were already clasped together, the markings of Sage Mode appearing on his forehead, and Chakra surged out like a tide.

« Wood Release : Deep Forest Emergence ! »

The earth shook violently, and countless thick vines and giant trees burst out of the ground, entangling the four limbs and body of the Nine-Tailed like living things.

The Nine-Tailed roared and struggled, but Wood Release had a natural suppression effect on Tailed Beasts. Those vines wrapped tighter and tighter, even starting to absorb the Nine-Tailed’s Chakra.

Uchiha Madara frowned slightly, about to urge the Susanoo to cut the vines, when he suddenly felt the body of the Nine-Tailed under his feet shake...

An even more powerful force was coming from underground !

Senju Matsuaki gathered Vibrational Power in his right hand and slammed it into the ground in front of him.

« Earthquake ! ! »

An invisible force of vibration spread from Matsuaki’s palm ; the ground shook and cracked, and even the space seemed to be slightly distorted.

The power of the Tremor-Tremor Fruit !

Over the past few years, he had already mastered the power of the Tremor-Tremor Fruit to a proficient degree.

Uchiha Madara’s pupils shrank. The body of the Nine-Tailed under his feet was actually shaken off balance by that vibrational force, and its massive body tilted to one side.

The Vibrational Power spread, and the shaking, vibrating ground finally caused the Nine-Tailed’s limbs to bend and fall.

The trees controlled by Senju Hashirama’s Wood Release completely bound the Nine-Tailed.

At the same time, the giant wooden figure’s right hand opened forward, and the huge « Suppress » character in the palm was eye-catching.

« Bringing the Taming of the Nine-Tails ! ! »

The Nine-Tailed, which was in a manic state and trying to break free from the Wood Release binding, suddenly stalled.

The next instant, its heavy head slammed into the ground, causing a cloud of dust.

Uchiha Madara snorted coldly and had to jump up to dodge, and just at the moment he was in the air.

« Air Quake ! ! »

A sharp look flashed in Senju Matsuaki’s eyes as he swung his fist violently toward the sky !

In the sky, a pattern of shattered mirror began to spread, and the Vibrational Power swept toward Uchiha Madara.

« Susanoo ! ! »

Uchiha Madara snorted coldly, the arm of the Susanoo swung out violently, and the katana was held horizontally in front of him.

« This power ! »

Uchiha Madara’s pupils shrank slightly.

After facing the unknown Vibrational Power head-on, he realized that he had underestimated this nephew of Senju Hashirama.

He had originally thought that the unknown giant force was the weird strength of the Senju Clan.

But upon direct contact, he discovered that the two seemed to be completely different powers !

The katana and armor of his Susanoo, which faced that giant force, even began to show signs of shattering after holding out for a moment.

The sword that was originally slashed down was also blocked because of this.

At this very moment, Senju Hashirama had already completed the second technique.

« Sage Art : Wood Release : True Several Thousand Hands ! »

A Thousand-Armed Buddha statue, even larger than the Nine-Tailed, rose from the ground, covering the sky and the sun.

Countless arms behind the Buddha statue opened at the same time, and each palm condensed majestic Sage Chakra.

« Second Uncle ! »

At the same time, Senju Matsuaki also exchanged a look with Senju Tobirama.

Senju Tobirama understood instantly, flashed to the side of Senju Matsuaki, and placed his palm on his shoulder.

At the same time, a stream of light cut through the sky, and a Flying Thunder God Kunai shot out through the air.

The next instant, the two figures disappeared from the spot and appeared in mid-air.

« Air Quake ! ! »

Aiming at the Susanoo standing in the air, Senju Matsuaki swung his fist again.

The air exploded, and a transparent shockwave headed straight for Uchiha Madara. Wherever it passed, fine cracks appeared in the space.

« Hashirama, you guys ! »

In front was the overwhelming Wood Release Buddha statue’s palms, and behind was the Vibrational Power capable of shattering the Susanoo. Uchiha Madara’s face finally changed.

He narrowed his eyes, about to dodge from the side.

On the left, Kirby’s deep blue cartoon Susanoo blocked the way.

Just at this time, another crimson stream of light struck from the right.

Uchiha Hikari’s Susanoo also held a katana, and the sharp blade light completely blocked Uchiha Madara’s path !

Uchiha Madara’s Susanoo was forced to draw its sword to meet the attack.

But at the moment the blades collided, Senju Hashirama’s True Several Thousand Hands had already launched an attack.

Countless giant palms fell like a rainstorm, each punch containing immense force, beating the Susanoo’s armor until it cracked inch by inch.

Uchiha Madara gritted his teeth to support, his Mangekyo eyes spinning rapidly.

However, without the help of the Nine-Tailed, Uchiha Madara was not Senju Hashirama’s opponent to begin with.

Moreover, at this time, Senju Hashirama also had the help of three Kage-level Ninjas : Senju Tobirama, Senju Matsuaki, and Uchiha Hikari.

Boom !

A shattering sound rang out, and the Susanoo finally collapsed.

Uchiha Madara’s figure was exposed to the air, and Senju Hashirama’s final punch was already heading toward him.

« Hashirama — »

Uchiha Madara roared, his Eternal Mangekyo Sharingan spinning crazily, hastily condensing the rib part of the Susanoo to block in front of him.

A dull sound rang out.

The next instant, the Buddha statue’s fist slammed Uchiha Madara into the ground.

The dust settled, and a bottomless crater was blasted into the ground.

The battle was over.

Uchiha Madara knelt halfway in the deep pit, his Eternal Mangekyo Sharingan slowly closing, and blood spilled from the corner of his mouth.
