# Chapitre 86 — Thirty Chess Pieces! Stunned Crowd!

The towering water tornado gradually stalled after reaching its limit in height, and the massive volume of water, carrying the people and objects that had been swept up in it, slammed heavily onto the ground.

At the center of the Water Release wave.

Senju Matsuaki and Hyuga Tsukuyomi were unaffected by this Ninjutsu, save for a few droplets of water that had dampened their upper garments.

Surrounding them, however, were twelve examinees who had been thrown into a state of disarray.

Having been swept into the sky by the violent water tornado and then slammed ruthlessly onto the ground, there was no doubt these twelve individuals had sustained serious injuries.

One of them was particularly unlucky ; while being swept into the air by the water tornado, they happened to be pierced in the abdomen by a Kunai mixed into the flow. At this moment, they were lying limp on the ground, barely breathing.

« Who would have thought that just as we were about to reach the finish line, we’d be gifted such a big bonus package. »

Senju Matsuaki tirelessly fished out the chess pieces held by these twelve people, and after counting them, revealed a satisfied smile.

[Current Progress : 30 chess pieces seized, 51 examinees defeated.]

He had thought his point-farming journey in the second stage of the exam was over, but he hadn’t expected such a surprise at the end.

These twelve examinees had a total of seven chess pieces inscribed with the character 'Jade'.

They had intended to ambush near the finish line to help their companions who hadn’t yet obtained chess pieces pass the exam.

Little did they know that their efforts all along would ultimately be for someone else’s benefit.

« Let’s head to the finish line ; the second stage of the exam is successfully concluded. »

After sealing the chess pieces into a storage scroll, Senju Matsuaki looked at Hyuga Tsukuyomi, who had just participated in the « looting of supplies. »

After exchanging a smile, the two did not linger and decisively headed toward the finish line ahead.

« Five people, come with me to go in and perform a rescue. »

Outside the finish line, a Konoha Proctor felt the gazes of the Iwagakure and Hidden Mist Ninja waiting there, making him feel like he was sitting on pins and needles.

Although he was extremely reluctant, he still organized several Proctors to go in together to carry out a rescue.

If not for the restrictions of the exam rules, these Iwagakure and Hidden Mist Ninja, who were straining their eyes at the finish line, would likely have rushed in to rescue them themselves.

A total of six Proctors were mobilized with astonishing speed, stepping into the now devastated battlefield.

« Well done ! »

As they brushed past Senju Matsuaki and Hyuga Tsukuyomi, who were heading to the finish line, a few Konoha Proctors even subtly gave them a thumbs up.

After dealing with the two previous waves of ambushers, the finish line was now close at hand.

In the distance, closest to the edge of the forest, a Kunai pinned a piece of white cloth to a thick tree, with the two characters for « Finish Line » written on it.

Near this conspicuous marker were several Proctors wearing Konoha headbands, each holding paper and pens for recording.

To ensure they could monitor the status of examinees arriving at the finish line immediately and prevent anyone from cutting corners, they were positioned at the very front.

Seeing the arrival of Senju Matsuaki, who was wearing a Konoha headband, some Proctors smiled and nodded, revealing friendly expressions.

A short distance behind the Proctors stood Ninjas wearing the headbands of their respective villages.

They were Chunin and Jonin from major Ninja villages, not participating in the Chunin Exams this time, but acting as support staff responsible for some auxiliary tasks.

At this moment, they were observing the results of the second stage of the exam near the finish line and reporting to their superiors in a timely manner.

Seeing the two arrive, most of these Chunin and Jonin from other villages were expressionless, while the Ninjas from Iwagakure and Hidden Mist cast hostile gazes with cold faces.

Upon receiving these unfriendly gazes, Senju Matsuaki lowered his head in thought.

A few seconds later, he reached a conclusion—he hadn’t beaten them enough !

Honestly, he had encountered more Ninjas from Kumogakure and Sunagakure in the exam, and they were the ones who had been beaten the most.

After all, he had previously received commissions from Hiruzen Sarutobi and Danzo Shimura, and one must be loyal to those who entrust them with tasks.

As for the examinees from Hidden Mist and Iwagakure, Senju Matsuaki wouldn’t let them off if he encountered them, but at least he wouldn’t beat them ruthlessly.

Senju Matsuaki noted that the Magnet Release Ninja from Sunagakure, as well as the Dou from Kumogakure, were also already at the finish line.

He wasn’t surprised by this.

Both of them already possessed strength at the Jonin level and were quite outstanding in terms of speed.

If the two hadn’t been distracted by other things in the primeval forest and had headed straight to the finish line after seizing the chess pieces, they probably could have completed the exam in less than a day.

« Please come over here to verify the exam results. »

A Proctor walked up to Senju Matsuaki and the other, signaling them to take out the chess pieces necessary to pass the exam for verification.

« Please wait a moment, the two of us have stored our chess pieces together. »

Senju Matsuaki said, taking out the storage scroll that Hyuga Tsukuyomi had given him earlier from his Ninja tool pouch.

Seeing this, the Proctor didn’t say much, just waited quietly for the verification results.

The format of the second stage of the exam mimicked the process of Ninjas seizing, guarding, or delivering intelligence or other mission objectives.

Two people forming a team to cooperate in delivering intelligence or mission objectives to the finish line was also recognized as passing the exam.

Seeing this, Dou and Yan not far away also cast attentive gazes.

Yan from Sunagakure sneered : « What a cautious guy, storing two people’s chess pieces in a storage scroll. »

Because of what happened a few days ago, he had a deep impression of these two.

One possessed the Byakugan, and it was a Byakugan that hadn’t been marked with the Caged Bird Seal—to him, it was an excellent prey.

Once he could seize a pair of Byakugan, it would be of great benefit to both him personally and Sunagakure.

However, the arrival of the other person had completely shattered his ambition.

Plus, Chiyo and Ebizo from Sunagakure were suspected to have been killed by Senju Matsuaki.

At the time, in his rage, Yan had even wanted to kill them both using Magnet Release.

Although he had now received the good news that Chiyo and Ebizo were not dead, he still harbored resentment toward the two in his heart.

« Release ! »

Senju Matsuaki spread out the scroll. On the center of the scroll was a drawing roughly in the shape of a circle, and around it were complex, snake-like winding incantations.

A puff of smoke rose from the center of the scroll and dissipated into the air.

Clatter—

What appeared before everyone present was a dense downpour of chess pieces.

The crowd stared blankly as the chess pieces gradually piled up on the ground like a small hill.

« This guy... » Yan’s sneer froze on his face.

« What a reckless brat. » Dou narrowed his eyes slightly, not knowing what he was thinking.

« Wait, wait a moment. »

This Proctor was used to seeing big scenes, but even he was stunned for a few seconds before reacting.

After he performed hand seals, all thirty chess pieces emitted a faint fluorescence.

« There are a total of thirty chess pieces here ; I’ll have to trouble you to count them. »

Senju Matsuaki rubbed his nose ; even with his thick skin, he felt somewhat embarrassed at this moment.
