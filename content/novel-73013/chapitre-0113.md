# Chapitre 113 — Golden Fishing Hook?

In the center of the spectator stands, the Daimyo of the Land of Fire smiled as he fished a paper ball out of the box, then looked expectantly toward Senju Hashirama.

Nearby, the other four Daimyo also held their breath, waiting for the results of the draw.

For them, the process of drawing lots had become one of the most interesting games of the past two days.

It was as if a simple set of rules had tacitly formed among the Daimyo of the Five Great Nations.

Once the draw determined that the two examinees were evenly matched, the Daimyo who drew would happily feel that they had good luck, having pulled a wonderful match.

If the draw resulted in a huge disparity in strength, with one side having an overwhelming advantage, then that Daimyo would be met with the dissatisfaction of the others.

Of course, if any Daimyo drew a match between two Genin from their own country and village, they would be mocked as a clown by the other Daimyo.

This little game, which carried a hint of gambling, provided these Daimyo with a long-lost sense of amusement.

« Konoha’s Senju Matsuaki. »

« Sunagakure’s... »

The Daimyo of the Land of Fire and Senju Hashirama simultaneously unfolded the papers in their hands to display them and read out the names written upon them.

Upon hearing the name from Sunagakure, the First Kazekage, Reto, his face darkened.

Originally, they had the fewest Sunagakure examinees entering the third stage, and now someone had encountered a formidable enemy.

He estimated that after this round or two, it would be visible to the naked eye that only Yue Xiaming would be left.

Now, he was only immensely glad that he had listened to Shamon’s suggestion at the time, allowing Yue Xiaming to participate in the Joint Chunin Exams with the status of a Genin.

Otherwise, if the exams reached the halfway point and it was discovered that the Sunagakure examinees had been completely wiped out, where would he put his old face ?

It was just a pity that Chiyo and Ebizo had encountered a formidable enemy during the second stage of the assessment, and Ebizo was even...

The First Kazekage, Reto, narrowed his eyes slightly, looking at Senju Matsuaki who had already entered the arena below, a faint cold glint flashing in his eyes.

...

The battle began.

The battle ended.

The huge disparity in strength shortened the battle time to the minimum.

Senju Matsuaki did not intend to waste time on an opponent far weaker than himself, and after symbolically exchanging one or two Ninjutsu, he ended the battle cleanly.

After all, he was also a part of the host, Konoha, and still needed to be slightly considerate of the audience’s feelings.

After returning to the examination area, Senju Matsuaki happened to pass by the stall of Hiruzen Sarutobi and Danzo Shimura.

The two were serving customers with practiced movements ; compared to their amateur performance at the beginning where they could even forget to collect money, they had improved too much.

Seemingly not yet recovered from last night’s accident, both of their faces were somewhat pale, looking like they had just recovered from a serious illness.

Seeing Senju Matsuaki pass by, both of them pulled a stiff smile as a greeting.

The two did not blame Senju Matsuaki in their hearts for what happened yesterday ; after all, they also knew that yesterday’s accident should be blamed more on their own negligence.

The reason their expressions were stiff was entirely because seeing Senju Matsuaki reminded them of yesterday’s rather unpleasant experience.

After Senju Matsuaki left, Sarutobi Sasuke, disgusted that they weren’t vomiting fast enough, had directly punched both of them in the abdomen.

After vomiting profusely, the two had avoided diarrhea, but had not completely avoided the effects of the sleeping drug.

Coupled with the excessive physical exertion, they had simply collapsed and slept in the restroom, and upon waking up early in the morning, they had rushed to set up their stall at the arena.

After greeting with their eyes, Senju Matsuaki walked back to his original seat to rest.

The Ninja World respected strength. When he defeated his opponents with a crushing advantage twice in the arena under the gaze of countless people, no one dared to look down on him because of his age anymore.

Among the five or six Iwa-nin who had previously looked for trouble with Senju Matsuaki, one or two had also advanced to the second round of selection, and thus were still in the lounge at this time.

When Senju Matsuaki passed by them, these Iwa-nin dared not sneer or clamor as before.

Instead, they all pretended to be very busy ; some lowered their heads, focusing on tidying the straps on their hand guards, while others held a small piece of griddle cake, gnawing on it for a long time without being able to finish it.

As for Senju Matsuaki, he had long ceased to pay attention to their existence.

After returning to his resting spot and sitting down, he exchanged greetings with Hyuga Tsukuyomi, who was waiting for her match, and then closed his eyes to rest.

At the same time, he opened the Golden Finger platform for requisition and appropriation, and a light screen unfolded before his eyes.

After farming points at the Senju Clan and Hiruzen Sarutobi’s house last night, the points he had accumulated already numbered in the tens of thousands, enough to requisition or appropriate some Golden Fingers with Heaven-Defying effects to strengthen himself.

Although his family assets were deep, Senju Matsuaki did not spend his points lavishly.

Only he himself knew the heartache of the points he had saved up.

Moreover, the rapid surge in points during this period was mainly because of the Chunin Exams ; once the Chunin Exams were over, it would not be so simple to accumulate points quickly.

Regarding the options in the platform, especially those useful to him, Senju Matsuaki had long since memorized them by heart after browsing them many times.

The only thing that changed every day was the top row of promotional options.

The promotional options refreshed today were of high quality. Among the five options, Senju Matsuaki even saw the Check-in System he had appropriated once before.

His Wood Release and Asura Chakra were both derived from this Golden Finger, and the massive amount of Chakra in his body now, which shocked ordinary people, also had an inseparable connection with this Golden Finger.

After being tempted for a moment, Senju Matsuaki ultimately gave up the thought of appropriating this Golden Finger again.

First, the discount this time was not as good as the last time ; last time it was 30% off, but this time it was 70% off.

If compared with last time, redeeming it now always gave a feeling of losing money.

Secondly, there were no suitable targets for checking in near Senju Matsuaki right now.

Senju Hashirama probably only had Sage Art that he needed right now ; otherwise, the ones with relatively high cost-performance were Senju Tobirama’s Flying Thunder God Technique and Uzumaki Mito’s Seal.

Of course, now that the Kage of the Five Great Villages were all gathered in Konoha, Senju Matsuaki could also take the Kage of the other four villages as check-in targets.

But thinking about it carefully, although these First Kage were powerful and had unique skills in certain aspects, they did not necessarily match Senju Matsuaki’s needs.

In the final analysis, there were no suitable, high cost-performance check-in targets.

If Uchiha Madara, Six Paths Sage, Ōtsutsuki Kaguya, or other Ōtsutsuki appeared in front of Senju Matsuaki now, he would probably appropriate this Check-in System without hesitation.

However, another promotional option caught Senju Matsuaki’s attention.

[Requisition · Golden Fishing Hook : Can fish for items of equivalent quality, can be used with any fishing rod. Original Price : 10,000 points. Promotional Price : 5,000 points.]

« Items of equivalent quality... »

Having long since figured out the functions of the Golden Finger platform, Senju Matsuaki naturally knew what this meant.

The so-called equivalent quality meant that the price of the fished items would fluctuate around the requisition price of the Golden Finger, and the range would not be too different.

The original price of the Golden Fishing Hook was 10,000 points, so the redemption points for items of equivalent quality would probably be in the range of a few thousand to tens of thousands.

A 50% discount was considered a relatively cost-effective tier.
