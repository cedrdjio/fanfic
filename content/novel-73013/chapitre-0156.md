# Chapitre 156 — S-Rank Mission! Repel Uchiha Madara!

« Tch ! »

Seeing this, Senju Tobirama had no choice but to retreat.

The battle before his eyes could easily reshape the terrain.

This scene of destruction was already far beyond the ordinary person’s definition of the word « Ninja » !

Additionally, he knew of his older brother Senju Hashirama’s fixation on Uchiha Madara and his unwillingness to let others intervene in their battle.

Although Senju Tobirama often said that his older brother Hashirama was unreliable, he still chose to respect his brother’s convictions at critical moments.

Of course, this was on the condition that Hashirama could win.

Senju Tobirama knew well that Hashirama’s strength had always been a cut above Uchiha Madara’s.

However, with the massive Nine-Tailed fox assisting Uchiha Madara, the outcome of this battle was undoubtedly thrown into uncertainty.

If Senju Hashirama showed signs of flagging, he would immediately step in to assist.

« Majestic Attire : Susanoo ! ! »

Uchiha Madara’s Eternal Mangekyo Sharingan spun at high speed, and the deep blue Susanoo armor covered the Nine-Tailed’s massive body.

Senju Hashirama was not to be outdone ; he also performed hand seals, and a giant wooden figure rose from the ground.

« Wood Release : Wood Human Technique ! ! »

The wooden figure engaged in close-quarters combat and brawled with the Susanoo-clad Nine-Tailed, sending shockwaves surging and shattering the mountain rocks.

...

Meanwhile, inside Konoha Village.

« Tsukuyomi, can you find Uncle Hashirama and the others ? » Senju Matsuaki asked in a deep voice.

Upon hearing this, Hyuga Tsukuyomi narrowed her Byakugan and nodded quickly, saying, « Found them ! »

Senju Matsuaki’s gaze fell on Uchiha Hikari, and he said, « Hikari, let’s go ! »

« Alright ! »

Uchiha Hikari’s eyes were tightly shut, and when she opened them again, a pair of crimson Sharingan were already lit.

Immediately after, her Mangekyo Sharingan activated, the shuriken pattern spinning rapidly as her ocular power climbed sharply !

« Susanoo ! ! »

A crimson skeleton appeared around her body, quickly solidifying into muscle and armor, finally transforming into a giant with wings on its back.

Senju Matsuaki, Uchiha Hikari, and Hyuga Tsukuyomi stood within the crystal atop the Susanoo, overlooking the scene below.

« Over there ! »

Hyuga Tsukuyomi had already activated her Byakugan, her vision piercing through layers of mountains to precisely capture the two figures fiercely battling in the valley.

« Go ! »

Uchiha Hikari controlled the Susanoo to spread its wings and flapped them violently, sending the Susanoo soaring into the air.

A gale howled past, and the Konoha Village below shrank rapidly.

Senju Matsuaki felt the wind rushing against his face, his gaze locked on the distant valley that had been turned upside down by the Chakra storm.

He could feel two terrifying forces of Chakra colliding fiercely there.

Those were the powers of Uncle Hashirama and Uchiha Madara.

That location would be known in the future as the « Valley of the End » because of today’s great battle.

After the war, Uchiha Madara would fake his death and go into hiding, allowing Black Zetsu’s thousand-year conspiracy to continue.

And perhaps Senju Hashirama would exhaust himself too much in this battle, passing away not long after.

In any case, Senju Matsuaki had to prevent Uncle Hashirama from meeting the same fate !

...

The valley outside Konoha.

The Nine-Tailed’s sharp claws, wrapped in the sharp edge of deep blue Chakra, slammed fiercely onto the wooden figure’s chest.

Wood chips flew, and the giant wooden figure staggered back several steps, each step leaving footprints several meters deep in the ground.

Senju Hashirama stood atop the wooden figure, his hands forming seals rapidly.

« Wood Release : Wood Hand Technique ! ! »

Several giant wooden palms burst from the ground, grabbing at the Nine-Tailed Susanoo’s body from all directions.

Uchiha Madara snorted coldly, the Nine-Tailed Susanoo’s four legs exerting force simultaneously, and its massive body nimbly leaped into mid-air, avoiding the encirclement of all the wooden palms.

Immediately after, the Nine-Tailed Susanoo turned in the air, its nine tails swinging in unison, and a large Tailed Beast Ball condensed in its mouth once again.

« Hashirama, how is this move ? »

Senju Hashirama’s pupils constricted slightly, and he slammed his hands together.

« Wood Release : Wood Defense Wall ! ! »

A wooden shield carved with a ferocious demon face rose from the ground, completely enveloping the wooden figure and Senju Hashirama.

The Tailed Beast Ball bombarded the shield repeatedly, erupting with a deafening roar.

Dust filled the air, and the earth trembled.

When the smoke cleared slightly, the surface of the wooden shield was covered in cracks, but it had not been completely shattered.

« Madara, your power has indeed grown stronger... » Senju Hashirama’s voice came from behind the shield, carrying a hint of complex emotion.

« But I cannot let you destroy Konoha ! »

The two leaped simultaneously with tacit understanding, shifting the confrontation from the wooden figure and Nine-Tailed to close-quarters combat between the two of them.

In an instant, it felt as if they had returned to the period before the founding of Konoha, those countless times of evenly matched exchanges.

« Hashirama ! ! ! »

« Madara ! ! ! »

« Madara, stop being so stubborn ! ! »

« Hashirama, you are too naive ! True peace is absolutely not like this ! ! »

The two separated again from close combat.

Uchiha Madara leaped high onto the Nine-Tailed’s head, and the Susanoo arm growing from the Nine-Tailed’s back held a katana, slashing down from above.

« Sage Art : Wood Release : True Several Thousand Hands ! »

Seeing that Uchiha Madara would not be swayed, Senju Hashirama finally gave up, his Aura changing abruptly as he unleashed one of his strongest techniques.

Senju Hashirama formed hand seals, the markings of Sage Mode spreading from his forehead, and black eye markings appeared beneath his eyes.

A thousand-armed Bodhisattva statue, dozens of times larger than the wooden figure, rose from the ground, blotting out the sun, with thousands of wooden arms densely arranged behind the statue.

Each palm condensed vast amounts of Sage Chakra, emitting a suffocating pressure.

Uchiha Madara’s expression finally changed.

« This power, have you finally brought out your true ability, Hashirama... »

...

In the air, Senju Matsuaki and the others finally arrived riding the Susanoo.

From thousands of meters away, they could sense those two terrifying Auras.

The heaven-blotting True Several Thousand Hands and the awe-inspiring Majestic Attire : Susanoo were in a standoff.

« These two guys, truly terrifying power... » Uchiha Hikari couldn’t help but say.

Having been in this era for a few years, she had gradually integrated, and in terms of knowledge, she was no different from the people of this era.

In the current Ninja World, the strongest in terms of strength were Senju Hashirama and Uchiha Madara.

Yet until today, she had only seen their power for the first time.

Moreover... if Senju Hashirama’s opponent were anyone else, she might have been able to offer assistance.

Unfortunately, Uchiha Madara’s Sharingan was the Eternal Mangekyo Sharingan, which surpassed her Mangekyo.

Uchiha Hikari’s Mangekyo Sharingan was already quite buggy in its settings.

The ocular technique « Yachihoko » could control others' spirits and absorb Chakra through markings !

However, « Yachihoko » happened to be unable to penetrate the Susanoo.

« In any case, we must defeat Uchiha Madara as soon as possible ! » Senju Matsuaki said in a deep voice.

Besides helping Senju Hashirama, there was another reason driving him.

Just now, a mission prompt had appeared.

[S-Rank mission released : Assist Senju Hashirama in repelling Uchiha Madara. Reward : 10,000 points !]
