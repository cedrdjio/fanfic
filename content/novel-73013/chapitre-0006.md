# Chapitre 6 — Sparring!

Signing up for the early assessment was merely a minor interlude.

In Senju Matsuaki’s mind, the graduation assessment was not his top priority.

Whether one is a Genin, Chunin, or Jonin, strength is the fundamental basis.

The urgent task at hand is to improve his own strength.

After all, the future Ninja World will not be peaceful.

Filling out a graduation assessment registration form doesn’t take much time ; after everyone handed them in, the teacher began the lecture.

Soon, half a day passed by in the Ninja Academy.

Perhaps considering that some students still needed to undergo training within their families, school generally ended quite early.

After the teacher left, Uchiha Kai was just about to jump out and provoke him with a few words.

However, he discovered that Senju Matsuaki had already grabbed Hyuga Tsukuyomi and hurried out of the classroom.

Seeing this, Uchiha Kai felt as if he had punched a pile of cotton ; his face turned various shades of green and white.

« Matsuaki-kun, please slow down a little. »

Hyuga Tsukuyomi looked at the hand Senju Matsuaki was using to pull her wrist, blushing slightly.

After leaving the classroom, Senju Matsuaki’s pace naturally slowed down.

The reason he left the classroom right on the teacher’s heels after class was, naturally, because he didn’t want the barking of certain people to affect the mood of his good sparring partner.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi already lacked confidence ; if her mindset were to be affected further, it wouldn’t be good.

However, he couldn’t exactly say these words directly to her.

« Sorry, Tsukuyomi. It’s rare to have a suitable opponent for live combat training, so I got a bit excited. »

This wasn’t a lie, either.

After all, in the Ninja Academy, live combat training for lower-grade students like them was always done with restraint.

A portion of their sparring partners had only just begun their Ninja training, causing most of the battles to be no different from children playing Ninja games.

But Hyuga Tsukuyomi had been undergoing training within the Hyuga Clan since she was young and had already awakened her Byakugan ; she was estimated to already have the strength of a Genin.

« Matsuaki-kun... »

Hearing this, Hyuga Tsukuyomi felt a warmth in her heart.

She naturally understood the intention behind him hurriedly pulling her out of the classroom.

It was nothing more than not wanting her to hear some provocative and belittling remarks.

Thinking of this, a firm expression appeared in her pure Byakugan eyes.

Someone is supporting and believing in her ; she couldn’t let him down !

There were public training grounds in Konoha ; although there were many people at this time, there were still empty spots available.

However, since there was a training ground in his own residence, he wouldn’t go and occupy public resources.

In the courtyard of Matsuaki’s home, there was an area specifically used for training.

Although it wasn’t large, it was sufficient for the two of them to spar.

On the trees planted at the edge of the grounds, there were targets used for practicing Shuriken and Kunai throwing, which bore quite a few marks.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi, visiting a friend’s house for the first time, was naturally a bit curious.

But out of politeness, she didn’t show it, only curiously looking around the courtyard.

Matsuaki-kun’s parents seem...

After discovering the desolation in the residence, Hyuga Tsukuyomi seemed to recall something, and a hint of sympathy appeared in her eyes.

« Tsukuyomi, if you’re ready, let’s begin. »

After loosening up his muscles, Senju Matsuaki looked at her.

« Yes, please guide me ! »

Because she had been distracted, the girl was a bit flustered for a moment, but she quickly adjusted her state.

Today was their first time sparring, so neither of them went all out from the start.

As it stood, Senju Matsuaki and Hyuga Tsukuyomi were evenly matched in strength.

Both of them had good talent, and neither had neglected the training arranged by their respective families for the younger generation since childhood.

And Matsuaki’s cheat had only just awakened, not yet enough to allow him to widen the gap between himself and his peers.

« Byakugan, activate ! »

After preliminarily testing his strength, Hyuga Tsukuyomi chose to activate her Byakugan, and the veins formed patterns that coiled around her temples.

With the assistance of the Byakugan, the Hyuga Clan’s Gentle Fist truly had a soul.

Senju Matsuaki could feel that her movements were more fluid, precise, and effortless than before.

After activating the Byakugan, Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s strength had increased by at least thirty to forty percent.

Of course, Senju Matsuaki was not much inferior.

If the Hyuga Clan’s inherited taijutsu, Gentle Fist, was characterized by its soft, silent ability to overcome hardness.

Then the taijutsu that the Senju Clan excelled in was characterized by its fierce, powerful, and overwhelming strength that breaks through finesse.

And that wasn’t all Senju Matsuaki knew ; he also had the monstrous strength techniques inherited by the Senju Clan.

However, he was still far from mastering them ; currently, they were difficult to control, and using them rashly might cause accidental injury.

On the training ground, both of them were completely immersed in the battle.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s palms, fair to the point of being almost transparent, moved left and right.

Carrying the unique force of the Gentle Fist, they struck like lightning toward the acupoints on the inner side of Senju Matsuaki’s right elbow and near his wrist pulse.

Matsuaki countered with rigid force, narrowly dodging and repelling her Gentle Fist time and time again.

However, even with the utmost care, it was occasionally unavoidable to be struck in an acupoint by the Gentle Fist.

This not only caused the flow of Chakra to be obstructed, but even the surrounding muscles were affected.

« So this is the Hyuga Clan’s Gentle Fist ; it truly lives up to its reputation. »

Senju Matsuaki exclaimed.

In the later stages of the original work, when Wood Release, Rinnegan, and Sage Mode were everywhere, Gentle Fist was certainly inconspicuous.

But don’t forget the pressure brought by the Gentle Fist during the early stages when combat power levels were still relatively normal.

Seeing through meridians, striking acupoints, obstructing Chakra, and even sealing the flow of Chakra.

For a Ninja whose foundation is Chakra, once they take a full set of Gentle Fist, they are at the mercy of their opponent.

However, on the other side, Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s eyes were shining with brilliance.

« Matsuaki-kun, you’re amazing. »

She actually wasn’t unaware that her strength was in the upper echelon among her peers.

It was just that she had always felt that her strength was only due to awakening her eyes earlier.

When her peers were just beginning to learn their family’s inherited secret techniques, or hadn’t yet awakened their family’s Bloodline Limit, she had already activated her Byakugan.

And such an advantage might only slowly disappear later on.

But even so, the current Senju Matsuaki could still fight back and forth with her.

Her hands even went numb from the shock when they clashed with his fists.

After another clash, the two pulled apart, a layer of fine sweat already coating their foreheads.

« Tsukuyomi, look out ! » Senju Matsuaki warned.

Afterward, his expression became serious as he adjusted his breathing according to the method taught by Tsugikuni Yoriichi.

With the massive intake of oxygen, his cardiopulmonary function was instantly stimulated, and the flow of blood accelerated as a result.

At the same time, he could clearly feel power surging into his body.

And in the perspective of Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s Byakugan, she could see that in just a few short seconds.

The flow of Chakra in Senju Matsuaki’s meridians had significantly accelerated, vaguely giving off a sense of threat.

Not understanding what had happened, Hyuga Tsukuyomi thought this was some kind of Ninjutsu of the Senju Clan.

After assuming the starting stance of the Gentle Fist, she prepared herself for his next offensive.
