# Chapitre 46 — Wood Release for Infrastructure

« Don’t look at me with such shock, old man isn’t senile yet, » the Cloud Village Elder snorted.

« When I was young, I dealt with the Senju Clan a few times, and I even crossed paths with Senju Hashirama on the battlefield. »

Speaking of this, the Cloud Village Elder’s expression was slightly unnatural, but he quickly hid it.

« At that time, the Ninja organization I belonged to coveted the prosperity of the Land of Fire and wanted to relocate the entire organization there, so naturally, we gathered intelligence on those Ninja clans in the Land of Fire. »

Before the era of hidden Villages, besides Ninja clans, there were many large and small Ninja organizations in the Ninja World, or perhaps it would be more appropriate to understand them as mercenary groups.

These organizations were just like the future Akatsuki Organization ; their maintenance relied on accepting and completing commissions.

In fact, even Ninja clans like the Senju Clan and Uchiha Clan were also a type of Ninja organization, the only difference being that their groups relied on blood ties to maintain themselves.

« Actually, although Senju Hashirama had excellent talent as a child, he was still within the normal range. »

« It wasn’t until later that I suddenly heard people say he used a Bloodline Limit that could manipulate trees on the battlefield that his strength gradually became something that we, the older generation, couldn’t fathom. »

« Elder, is Wood Release really that powerful ? » Dou asked, puzzled.

Over the years, he had encountered some Ninjas with Bloodline Limits in missions. In his view, Wood Release was no different from the Ice Release or Boil Release of the Hidden Mist Village.

« Senju Hashirama’s Wood Release is truly that powerful. »

The Cloud Village Elder was unwilling to talk more about this topic to avoid shattering their Dao hearts.

« However, we don’t need to worry too much. »

He looked at Dou and said sternly, « As far as I know, in the decades since Senju Hashirama was born, no other Ninja in the Senju Clan has been able to awaken Wood Release. »

« Senju Hashirama’s strength, which was unrivaled in the Ninja World, was mainly because of Wood Release. Without Wood Release, the Senju Clan would at most reach the level of Senju Tobirama. »

Hearing this, both Hong and Dou’s expressions eased.

Hong said, « Some Ninjas make rapid progress in the early stages but find it hard to improve later. Not everyone can achieve the strength of a Kage. »

However, recalling the other party’s performance during the sparring match just now, a shadow still hung over Dou’s heart.

« But Elder, leaving Wood Release aside, you must have seen that battle just now, » Dou said.

« Although it sounds like I’m boosting someone else’s morale, I’m afraid no one among the younger generation of Kumogakure would be his opponent except me, and he is six years younger than me. »

« In time, even if he cannot become a second Senju Hashirama, I fear he will be another Senju Tobirama. »

« I have also considered this issue. »

A hint of ruthlessness appeared on the Cloud Village Elder’s rugged face. He took a scroll from his arms and spread it on the table, revealing the content recorded on it.

Looking at the writing format and habits, the content recorded in this scroll was similar to the commission letters usually used to task the Village with missions.

However, a closer look revealed that although the format was similar, the content was not a commission, but a bounty.

« Since we are still discussing the Joint Chunin Exams, it’s not good for us in Kumogakure to act directly. Handing the bounty to the Underground Exchange and using a borrowed knife to kill is safer. »

Hearing this, neither Hong nor Dou objected.

After all, for Ninjas, things like assassination were commonplace. Since it concerned the future development of the Village, they wouldn’t mind using some underhanded means.

« It’s just that for the upcoming Joint Chunin Exams, I’m afraid no one from our Kumogakure will be able to overshadow that brat. »

Dou sighed with some depression, only feeling that his little brothers in Kumogakure had no future.

The Cloud Village Elder put away the scroll while showing a mysterious smile.

« That’s not necessarily true, Dou. »

...

After the talks between Konoha and Kumogakure ended, the delegations sent by Kumogakure to other Villages also returned one after another.

Unexpectedly, not only Konoha but also Iwagakure, Sunagakure, and the Hidden Mist Village all agreed to participate in the first Joint Chunin Exams of the Ninja World one after another.

The disagreement between the Villages mainly lay in the allocation of preparation funds.

Initially, Kumogakure’s idea was for everyone to share the cost equally, but Sunagakure did not agree with such an arrangement. After all, compared to other Villages, Sunagakure’s economic situation was already poor.

In the end, Kumogakure reached a compromise with the other Villages, agreeing to allocate funds based on a comprehensive consideration of each Village’s economic situation and the scale of personnel participating in the assessment.

After these basic matters were determined, the relevant departments in Konoha also began to operate.

During this period, Matsumi basically couldn’t see his uncle and second uncle at Hashirama’s house ; the two of them sometimes didn’t even have time to come back for meals.

Time passed day by day, and Konoha’s preparation plan for the Joint Chunin Exams moved forward steadily.

One day.

Senju Matsumi and Hyuga Tsukuyomi, who were in the gap between missions, were taken by Senju Hashirama to a primitive forest outside Konoha Village.

After arriving, Matsumi discovered that Second Uncle Tobirama seemed to have arrived long ago, walking around with a blueprint in his hand, stopping from time to time with a thoughtful look.

Seeing the group arrive, Tobirama came back to his senses, and his furrowed brows relaxed.

« Big Brother, I’ve confirmed everything on my end. You just need to follow this blueprint later. »

« This blueprint ? »

After Hashirama took the blueprint, he first looked at it intently, then raised his head, wanting to say something but stopping.

« It’s fine, Big Brother. Even if you make a mistake, you can just plant the trees back. Hurry up and start ; I have other official business to handle ! » Tobirama urged impatiently.

« Uh, alright. »

Hearing Tobirama say this, Hashirama, who was originally worried about messing up, felt relieved.

« This time we are going to build the competition venue needed for the Chunin Exams. After all, five Villages are participating together. Now Konoha doesn’t have a venue capable of supporting such a large-scale assessment, so we can only start from scratch. »

Hashirama showed Matsumi and Tsukuyomi the blueprint in his hand, which roughly planned the location, size, and relative distance of the exam venue from Konoha Village.

« Teacher, are we relying on just a few of us ? »

Hyuga Tsukuyomi looked at the huge circle on the map, then at the deep, boundless forest in front of her, and asked in confusion.

« You haven’t seen the true power of Big Brother’s Wood Release yet, have you ? » Hearing this, Senju Tobirama just smiled faintly.

« Wood Release ? »

When teaching them usually, Senju Hashirama’s Wood Clone sometimes used Wood Release, and both were deeply impressed by it.

However, it was just daily teaching after all ; the Wood Release Senju Hashirama displayed was only at the level of ordinary Ninjutsu.

He couldn’t possibly use the Sage Art Wood Release : True Several Thousand Hands on Matsumi and the others during training.

« Big Brother’s Wood Release is the top-tier weapon of war on the battlefield. The power displayed in front of you usually might not even be one percent. »

Senju Tobirama’s words made Matsumi and Tsukuyomi feel incomparably curious.
