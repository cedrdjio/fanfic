# Chapitre 27 — Hiruzen Sarutobi's Troubles

As soon as they returned to Konoha, Matsumi and his companion went to the Hokage Office to submit their mission report.

Only Hashirama was in the Hokage Office today ; Tobirama seemed to be away on a mission.

Seeing them arrive, a smile appeared on Hashirama’s face.

After the Wood Clone dissipated, he naturally acquired its memories and roughly understood what had happened during this time.

He felt a bit of pity for the Village’s plight, but he was also gratified by the growth the two had shown during this period.

« You’ve worked hard during this time. You can take on some missions within the Village next, and I’ll keep an eye out for any tasks suitable for you. »

« Then, Uncle Hashirama, we’ll head back first ! » Matsumi said.

They were still only Genin, so the missions they could accept were limited.

According to Konoha’s current system, missions were primarily assigned by the Village’s upper management.

Among them, Genin were assigned C and D-rank missions. C-rank missions still had some value for training, while D-rank missions were purely trivial matters.

In Matsumi’s view, D-rank missions were like odd jobs ; their only purpose was to subsidize household expenses for Genin who were temporarily unwilling or unable to leave the Village for missions.

After becoming Chunin, they would be assigned B and C-rank missions, and after becoming Jonin, they would be assigned A-rank missions.

Only a small elite group among the Jonin would be assigned S-rank missions with extreme difficulty.

Such an arrangement was not only to better complete the clients' missions but also to protect the safety of the Ninja.

However, neither of them was short on money, so there was no need to waste energy on trivial D-rank missions.

After saying goodbye to Hashirama, the two left the Hokage Building.

At a fork in the road, after parting ways with Tsukuyomi, Matsumi was preparing to go home and get some rest when he was suddenly blocked by someone, his heart immediately becoming vigilant.

After taking a closer look, Matsumi’s vigilant mood relaxed.

« It’s Big Brother Hiruzen, you scared me, » Matsumi said helplessly.

The one blocking his path was none other than Hiruzen Sarutobi.

At this moment, Hiruzen Sarutobi was not wearing the tight combat uniform used for missions, but rather everyday casual attire.

What was different from usual was that he was acting sneaky, as if afraid that what he was about to do would be discovered.

« Matsumi, do you have time now ? Come with me... »

Looking at Hiruzen Sarutobi’s somewhat anxious expression, perhaps there really was some urgent matter, so Matsumi did not refuse and followed him to a secluded corner.

« Big Brother Hiruzen, didn’t you go out of the Village on a mission with Second Uncle ? » Matsumi asked.

« Teacher Tobirama is carrying out an S-rank mission this time ; we aren’t qualified to participate in missions of this difficulty yet, » Hiruzen Sarutobi said, scratching his head.

Hiruzen Sarutobi suddenly put his hands together and said, « By the way, Matsumi, I have a very important matter I want to ask for your advice on today. »

« What is it ? Can’t anyone else help you with it ? » Matsumi asked curiously.

He and Hiruzen hadn’t interacted much before ; after all, one had been in the Ninja Academy while the other had been a Ninja for many years.

Although their relationship was quite friendly, they weren’t very close.

Judging by the closeness of their relationships, Hiruzen Sarutobi should have gone to find Danzo or Kagami for help first.

Hiruzen Sarutobi’s expression was a bit bashful, and he looked around like a thief before whispering, « It’s a problem regarding… romantic feelings... »

« ... »

Upon hearing this, Matsumi was silent for a moment, then turned and began to walk away.

« Goodbye. »

« Wait, » Hiruzen Sarutobi hurriedly grabbed him, « Listen to me finish first. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi’s face was bitter, looking quite helpless.

« Think about it, although Danzo and Kagami are much more handsome than me, they don’t have any thoughts of dating and have absolutely no experience in this area. »

« And we spend most of our time together carrying out missions ; I just feel it would be a bit awkward if they knew. »

« But I don’t have any experience in this area either, » Matsumi said, shrugging his hands.

To be honest, whether in his past life or the present, he was a novice when it came to romance.

And right now, all he was thinking about was getting stronger quickly to deal with future crises ; he had no thoughts in that direction at all.

Hiruzen Sarutobi scratched his head and said, « I see you and that young lady from the Hyuga Clan together all the time ; I feel like you’d have more experience than Danzo and the others. »

« We are just teammates and friends. »

« And Big Brother Hiruzen, you’re not even thirteen yet, right ? »

« What does that have to do with age ? Besides, some people have children by the time they are thirteen or fourteen. »

Hearing this, Senju Matsuaki didn’t know how to refute it for a moment.

He had almost forgotten that people in this world were generally precocious, and it was indeed common for them to be involved in romantic relationships at a young age.

And as Hiruzen Sarutobi said, some people were already married and having children by the age of thirteen or fourteen ; this situation was even more common among the ordinary population outside of the Ninja.

« Please, this is the request of my life ! » Hiruzen Sarutobi put his hands together.

« Then, Big Brother Hiruzen, what kind of romantic problem have you encountered ? » Matsumi asked curiously.

People are naturally curious, especially about the romantic problems of their friends ; it was inevitable to have some concern.

« It’s like this, I’ve been pursuing a girl lately. » Speaking of this, Hiruzen Sarutobi’s expression couldn’t help but be a bit awkward.

« Oh yes, you should have some impression of her. It’s that girl you met in the first round of the graduation assessment, Biwako. »

Hearing this, Senju Matsuaki was not surprised, but just signaled for Hiruzen to continue.

« During this time, I occasionally asked her to go to the training ground, but I found that after doing it a few times, she really seems to only view me as a kind-hearted senior. »

« When we spend time together, she is always very respectful and polite, but this makes me feel like there is too much distance. »

Matsumi interrupted, « Big Brother Hiruzen, you only go to the training ground when you meet ; have you never gone anywhere else ? »

« Uh... no. » Hiruzen Sarutobi bowed his head and thought for a moment before shaking his head.

« Because we both have to carry out missions, there isn’t much free time for either of us. »

« More importantly, if I were to invite her to go elsewhere, I always feel like I can’t bring myself to say it. »

« Big Brother Hiruzen, the lack of time is just your excuse ; the core reason is that you can’t bring yourself to speak up, right ? » Senju Matsuaki shook his head, feeling frustrated at his lack of progress.

« In my personal opinion, Miss Biwako’s impression of you is currently very positive, » Matsumi said.

Hearing this, a light appeared in Hiruzen Sarutobi’s eyes.

Matsumi continued, « However, if you don’t seek a change in the relationship in time, I’m afraid her perception of you will become fixed, and she will always treat you as a senior, not as a candidate for a partner. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi’s whole demeanor became gloomy again.

He seemed to see a future day where Biwako would hold a boy’s hand and come before him.

Then smile and say to him, « Senior Sarutobi, this is my chosen life partner ; please bless us~ »

Thinking of this, his eyes suddenly became firm, as if he had stepped onto the battlefield.

« I understand, Matsumi ! »

« If you have time next time, Big Brother Hiruzen, you might as well invite Miss Biwako to a meal and talk to her more about things other than missions and training. »

« Thanks, Matsumi ! I’ll thank you properly when I have time another day ! »

Watching Hiruzen Sarutobi’s back as he hurried away, Matsumi couldn’t help but shake his head and laugh.
