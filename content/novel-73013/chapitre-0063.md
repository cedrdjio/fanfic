# Chapitre 63 — Is the Hidden Mist weak?

Bang !

The massive sound was as distinct in the quiet exam hall as a pebble dropped into a calm pool of water.

Another examinee sitting to the left, seeing someone suddenly tumble to their feet, wore a bewildered expression and cast a look toward the Proctor for help.

« Hey ! What are you doing, kid ! ? »

The Proctor, who had just finished patrolling and was about to return to their spot, heard the noise, turned around, and shouted sternly.

Upon seeing who had fallen to the ground, the Proctor couldn’t help but make a strange face.

He did have some impression of this examinee from the Ice Release clan. After all, he was a genuine Jonin, and the cheating methods of these Genin were not sophisticated in his eyes.

However, he had his own set of standards ; for examinees with clumsy cheating methods, he would catch them directly and kick them out of the exam hall. For those whose cheating methods were relatively clever among the Genin, he would turn a blind eye.

After all, if he were to be truly strict, the Aburame Clan Genin with bugs flying all around them and the Hyuga Clan Genin with prominent veins bulging near their temples would be the first to be kicked out.

In mission scenarios, these traits were not conspicuous ; it was just that the exam environment amplified them.

The Genin from the Ice Release clan using ice mirrors to copy answers was, for now, considered a clever maneuver, but it was unlikely to fool a Jonin’s senses. The Proctor just felt the method was relatively sophisticated, so he pretended not to notice.

He hadn’t expected that not long after, this Ice Release clan examinee would cause such a huge commotion.

« It wasn’t me ! My chair moved on its own ! ! »

Seeing the Proctor walking quickly toward him, a trace of panic appeared in the examinee’s eyes as he pointed back at the wooden chair that had toppled to the floor.

However, the branch that had extended out just moments ago had already retracted, and the wooden chair was now back to its original state.

« Why don’t you say the chair grew legs instead ! ? »

Veins bulged on the Proctor’s forehead ; he felt his intelligence was being insulted. He had turned a blind eye to the examinee’s cheating, and the kid really thought he was a fool, right ?

« Wait ! I’ve been wronged ! »

As the Proctor escorted the Ice Release clan examinee out of the exam hall, the room returned to its previous calm. Although the other examinees were curious, they didn’t dare turn their heads to look, for fear that their inquisitive actions would be misjudged as cheating.

Some examinees who had been in the middle of cheating also hurriedly reined in their actions.

« All finished. » At his seat, Senju Matsuaki looked at his completed exam paper and smiled with satisfaction.

...

Inside the conference hall.

« Hey, did you guys see what happened just now ? » Hōzuki Gengetsu frowned as he looked at the toppled wooden chair on the ground, his face showing confusion.

He had some impression of that examinee ; he was a Genin from their Hidden Mist Village’s Ice Release clan, and his talent wasn’t bad. However, that scene had happened in an instant, and he hadn’t managed to see it clearly.

By the time he noticed the commotion in that exam hall, the examinee was already sprawled on the floor to the side.

The Telescope Technique could show a live feed but not a playback, so it was impossible for him to know what had just happened.

With the Ice Release Bloodline Limit and not-bad talent, this examinee originally might have been able to shine in the third stage of the assessment. Thinking of this, Hōzuki Gengetsu’s expression couldn’t help but turn even sourer.

« Are your Hidden Mist Ninjas weak ? Is that why he fell off his chair with wobbly legs ? »

Hearing Hōzuki Gengetsu’s question, he couldn’t help but sneer. As someone who had always been at odds with Hōzuki Gengetsu, he naturally wouldn’t miss this opportunity to mock him.

« Bandage man, do you want to die ! ? » Hōzuki Gengetsu was furious.

« Alright, alright, you two, stop arguing. » Senju Hashirama, representing Konoha as the host, spoke up to stop the two from their imminent quarrel.

Nara Kano, who was nearby, saw this and picked up a bottle of brown liquid that had been placed aside, then walked to Hōzuki Gengetsu’s side and placed it on the table.

« What is this ? » Hōzuki Gengetsu asked with a frown.

Nara Kano explained : « This is the Ino–Shika–Chō brand health drink, jointly developed by our Nara Clan, Akimichi Clan, and Yamanaka Clan. It contains precious medicinal herbs like velvet antler and Codonopsis pilosula. It has the effect of tonifying the kidneys and essence, warming the yang, and solidifying the foundation. It is significantly effective for problems like kidney deficiency, sore waist, and weak legs. »

« You— ! » Upon hearing this, Hōzuki Gengetsu was initially angry, but before his anger could erupt, his expression suddenly froze.

He saw him sheepishly extend his right hand, pick up the bottle of brown drink, and quickly stuff it into his pocket.

« Ahem, it’s not easy to come to Konoha, so I’m bringing some local specialties back. Everyone can understand that, right ? » Hōzuki Gengetsu gave a light cough and, seeing the strange looks from the others around him, explained with a guilty conscience.

« Understood, understood, it’s only human nature. » The male Ninjas in their thirties and forties present all showed expressions of sympathetic agreement, while simultaneously casting subtle glances toward Nara Kano.

Seeing that his silent advertisement had achieved its expected goal, a confident smile appeared on the corner of Nara Kano’s mouth.

« We will be opening sales channels soon. Friends who need to purchase, please pay close attention to relevant information~ »

...

« Stop what you are doing, sit in your original seats and wait for the papers to be collected ! » After the exam time ended, the Proctors in each exam hall decisively ordered the examinees to stop writing.

After such a written exam, the examinees from various Ninja villages gradually entered the zone, adopting the identity of « subordinates. » After receiving the instruction to stop writing, no one dared to disobey the order from the Proctor, their « superior. »

There were about fifty people in one exam hall ; two Proctors quickly collected the fifty or so exam papers, announced the dismissal, and then left the classroom without looking back.

After the Proctors announced the end of the session, the Genin left the exam hall one after another. The first stage of the exam was over ; the afternoon was time for the Proctors to grade the exam papers, and the second stage of the exam would formally begin tomorrow.

Walking on the way home, Senju Matsuaki sighed, feeling a lingering sense of unfinished business. In his past life, he loathed these kinds of exams, but just now in the exam hall, he felt a bit nostalgic for this long-lost scene.

Outside of the Chunin Exams, who would even treat him like a student anymore ?

For him, the first stage written exam was not a difficult hurdle ; the truly challenging parts would be the second and third stages of the exam later on.

It was already noon, and after finding a ramen shop outside to solve his lunch problem, Senju Matsuaki headed straight home.

Sitting under the eaves, he opened his Cheat platform interface, and the relatively meager points made him frown.

He hadn’t been out on many missions recently, and besides the points he gained from a certain amount of daily training, there were no other sources of points.

Coupled with the fact that the recent promotional options weren’t very good, it had been a while since he had requisitioned a suitable cheat.

Hopefully, tomorrow’s real-combat second stage exam will bring more gains.
