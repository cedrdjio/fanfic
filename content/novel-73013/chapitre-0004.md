# Chapitre 4 — First Time Requisitioning a Golden Finger

After dinner, the family sat together and chatted.

Senju Kazuma smiled and said, « Matsuaki, I heard that Uchiha Setsuna’s younger brother is in your class. Given their personality, he will surely apply for early graduation as well, so you must work hard. »

Although the Senju Clan and the Uchiha Clan established Konoha together, the relationship between the two sides could not be called harmonious.

Especially after Uchiha Madara left, the relationship between the two clans became vaguely subtle.

Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama also had differences of opinion on this matter.

Hashirama believed that since these people did not leave with Uchiha Madara, they were « their own people » and should be helped to better integrate into Konoha.

As for Tobirama’s attitude, it could be simply summarized in one sentence—the evil Uchiha !

But regardless of the attitude, the two clans were still competing secretly.

With the God of Ninja, Senju Hashirama, suppressing them, they couldn’t actually fight, so they compared whose descendants were more promising.

« I understand, Brother Kazuma, » Senju Matsuaki replied.

Hearing Senju Kazuma’s words, he was also a bit surprised ; he hadn’t expected Uchiha Kai to be Uchiha Setsuna’s younger brother.

In the original work, when Senju Tobirama served as the Second Hokage, Uchiha Setsuna was around twenty or thirty years old. Because he was dissatisfied with Senju Tobirama’s rule, he incited a rebellion and was eventually arrested and imprisoned.

But now, Konoha was still under the reign of the First Hokage, and Uchiha Setsuna was only a teenager, considered one of the geniuses of the younger generation of the Uchiha Clan.

Therefore, even Senju Matsuaki had heard of the other person’s name.

The only thing he hadn’t expected was that the arrogant Uchiha Kai had this connection with Uchiha Setsuna.

« According to tradition, my big brother and I will both be at the graduation exam to watch, » Senju Tobirama said with a smile, patting Senju Matsuaki on the shoulder.

After everyone chatted for a while, they each had things they needed to be busy with.

Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama were both busy dealing with the Village’s affairs.

And Uzumaki Mito and Senju Kazuma were busy with the matter of Senju Kazuma’s engagement.

Senju Matsuaki couldn’t help with either of these two matters right now.

So, after saying goodbye, he strolled back to his own residence.

Compared to the mansion of Senju Hashirama’s family, Matsuaki’s home seemed smaller, but the overall architectural style was consistent.

Moreover, this size was only relative to Senju Hashirama’s home ; in reality, it was already considered a relatively large residence in Konoha.

This was mainly because Senju Matsuaki’s late father did not have a low status within the Senju Clan.

If he were still alive, and if Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama had no successors, he might even have been elected as the next Clan Chief of the Senju Clan.

And now, only Senju Matsuaki lived in this residence, which was quite desolate.

Moonlight spilled onto the courtyard, and the sliding paper shoji doors brought the courtyard scenery into the room, while the mottled bamboo blinds filtered the fragmented light and shadows.

Leaning against the wooden steps of the veranda, Senju Matsuaki, just as he had done every day for the past month, activated his « Cheat. »

Golden Finger Requisition and Expropriation Platform.

The first thing that caught his eye was this line of text, followed by the dazzling array of exchange options and the points for each option that made him shrink back.

Whether it was something he could imagine or something he couldn’t, it could be searched for within this platform.

A certain Bloodline Limit from the Ninja World, a certain Devil Fruit from the navigation world, or even an entire systematic System possessed by some Son of Luck.

Everything could be found within this magical platform, and one could obtain temporary usage rights or permanent ownership after paying a certain number of points.

For example, on this platform, he could spend 10 points to temporarily requisition the usage rights of a One-Tomoe Sharingan, with a usage duration of 3 days.

At the same time, he could also spend 200 points to permanently requisition a One-Tomoe Sharingan, thereby owning it forever.

The Sharingan, which the Uchiha Clan took pride in, could be activated for only 200 points ; this was the difference between cheating and not.

But if he really did that, firstly, he couldn’t maximize his benefits, and secondly, it would be inconvenient to explain the source of the Sharingan.

Over the past month or so, while Senju Matsuaki had been constantly accumulating points, he had also been thinking about how to maximize the use of his points.

After some time of thought, he already had a long-term plan in mind.

And today, he had finally accumulated enough points to convert them into a shortcut to improve his strength.

Quickly scrolling past the rows of Golden Fingers that required a long string of zeros in points to requisition, Senju Matsuaki locked onto his target.

[Requisition · Blue Skill Space Key : With this key, one can randomly draw a skill of the corresponding level and form a skill training space ; the space will be destroyed after learning is completed. Price : 100 points.]

Besides these few brief introductions, there were more detailed descriptions that could be checked.

Previously, Matsuaki had already understood the content of this Golden Finger in detail.

If he was lucky, the 100 points spent could be worth more than the cost ; if the compatibility with himself was high, there could even be exponential gains.

But if he was unlucky, he might draw a skill that wouldn’t improve him much, wasting it in vain.

Regardless, at the current stage, this was indeed a relatively safe choice.

Therefore, Senju Matsuaki no longer hesitated and spent all the points he had accumulated to three digits.

Accompanied by a blue light appearing before his eyes, a blue key about half the size of his palm slowly landed in his palm.

Just as Senju Matsuaki was about to catch the key, the blue key scattered like fine sand, flowing over his palm.

Subsequently, the blue light grew fainter and fainter, gradually forming a light blue key-shaped pattern in the center of the back of his right hand.

Watching this pattern form, Senju Matsuaki held his breath, inevitably feeling a bit nervous and expectant.

« It’s been a long time since I experienced that feeling of drawing cards from my previous life. »

After thinking for a moment, he finally discovered where this familiar feeling in his heart came from.

Come on, a single draw creates a miracle !

With Senju Matsuaki’s use, his consciousness felt a bit dazed.

Before his eyes, it first went black, and then it brightened again.

The sound of the breeze brushing through the bamboo leaves in the courtyard instantly returned to silence, and what appeared before his eyes was a pure white space.

It seemed like the interior of a cube, but when looking closely into the distance, it seemed as if there was no boundary.

And in this space, besides Senju Matsuaki, there was only one figure kneeling in front.

This person looked old, with a thin figure and white hair.

He wore a high ponytail and was dressed in a samurai outfit with flame patterns, with red trim adorning the cuffs and lapels.

Although he looked old and decrepit, he still maintained the upright posture of a samurai, like a withered pine.

The most eye-catching thing was his wrinkled face, and on the position of his left forehead, there was a deep red flame-shaped mark.

At this moment, the old man’s face was calm, and his cloudy eyes were filled with compassion, watching Senju Matsuaki walking towards him step by step.
