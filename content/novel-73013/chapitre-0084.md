# Chapitre 84 — Heading to the Finish Line!

Among the three Kirigakure Ninja, the one that caught Senju Matsuaki’s attention was the shark-faced boy.

Such a distinct feature couldn’t help but make one wonder if he had some kinship with the future Kisame Hoshigaki.

For example, perhaps his grandfather or something similar.

After Senju Matsuaki and Hyuga Tsukuyomi exchanged a glance, they performed their respective duties according to the tacit understanding they had formed over the past two days.

Senju Matsuaki searched the shark-faced boy and the male Kirigakure Ninja and found two jade chess pieces, while Hyuga Tsukuyomi found another piece on the female Kirigakure Ninja.

« There are two, what a great harvest. »

Hyuga Tsukuyomi smiled and handed the jade chess piece in her hand to Senju Matsuaki.

Perhaps at first it was just to cooperate with Senju Matsuaki, but as they progressed, she also started to treat collecting jade chess pieces as a fun game.

Her bright smile made Senju Matsuaki daze for a few seconds.

After silently chanting « I am not a loli-con » in his heart a few times, he managed to restore his composure.

« This is indeed an unexpected harvest. »

After taking the pieces, Senju Matsuaki stored the two jade chess pieces into his scroll as usual, and tossed the blank pieces beside the Kirigakure Ninja.

« Then, after we tidy up a bit more, we should continue on our way. »

After putting the scroll away, Senju Matsuaki felt refreshed.

With the « package » delivered by these three Kirigakure Ninja in the middle of the night, the number of jade chess pieces he had seized reached 23.

The total points obtained from the second stage of this Joint Chunin Exams had also broken through the four-thousand mark.

After a simple cleanup, the two of them headed towards the finish line of the primeval forest without looking back.

As for the three Kirigakure Ninja lying on the ground, by the time they woke up and rushed to the finish line, they would likely be unable to complete the examination.

...

The exit of the primeval forest.

Several Proctors were guarding the boundary, chatting while waiting for the examinees to arrive at the finish line.

« There were 270 people when they went in, I wonder how many will pass the exam in the end ? »

« There are at most 90 spots for the third stage of the exam, so I guess there will be about 70 in the end. »

« That’s not necessarily true, I heard that some examinees have more than one jade chess piece in their hands. »

« Uh, wouldn’t that be too... »

« If your skills are inferior, what is there to say ? Cruel reality won’t listen to the weak reasoning. »

« That’s true. »

« Look, someone is coming. »

This Proctor’s voice shifted everyone’s attention to the exit.

In fact, there were not only Konoha Proctors present.

It seemed that for the sake of fairness in the exam, and to facilitate each Ninja village to know the status of their examinees at the first opportunity, each village had also sent several Ninja to wait at the finish line.

Seeing the figure carried by the black Iron Sand, the Sunagakure Ninja waiting there all showed proud smiles.

« That is our Sunagakure Ninja ! ! »

They completely forgot that just a few weeks ago, this « Genin » who made them feel proud was still a Sunagakure Jonin on equal footing with them.

As he was about to reach the finish line, the Iron Sand under Yan’s feet slowly dissipated, and his feet landed steadily on the ground.

After arriving at the finish line, he didn’t have any expression of joy, just a cold look mixed with a hint of anxiety.

« Where are Chiyo and Ebizo ? »

He looked at the Sunagakure Jonin closest to him and asked in a low voice.

After ending the standoff with Senju Matsuaki the other day, he began searching for the traces of Chiyo and Ebizo.

These two were also rare geniuses of Sunagakure ; once they grew up, they would be the backbone of Sunagakure and could not afford to be lost.

What worried him was that he couldn’t find them alive, and what kept him from being completely disappointed was that he couldn’t find their bodies.

Hearing this, the Sunagakure Ninja waiting here also darkened, with faint anger in his eyes.

« Chiyo and Ebizo are both seriously injured and are currently receiving treatment at the Konoha hospital. Fortunately, they did not lose their lives. »

« However, Ebizo, he... one of his hands was crushed ! »

« What ! ? »

Hearing the Sunagakure Ninja’s description of Chiyo and Ebizo’s situation, Yan first breathed a sigh of relief, then his face turned gloomy.

For a Ninja, what is the difference between losing a hand and being crippled ?

Current medical Ninjutsu has not yet developed to the point of regrowing severed limbs.

« Examinee, come and verify your exam results. »

Just as the two were whispering, a Konoha Proctor walked up to Yan and showed him the paper and pen in his hand.

Yan suppressed the anger in his heart, stiffly took out the three jade chess pieces from his arms, all of which had the character « Jade » written on them.

« Three ? »

When the Proctor first saw the scene, he couldn’t hide his surprise.

Just a moment ago, a group of Proctors was discussing that some examinees had seized multiple jade chess pieces.

But they didn’t expect that the first person to reach the finish line would have three.

Seeing the surprise on the Konoha Proctor’s face, Yan’s face darkened.

According to his original plan, he intended to search for Sunagakure examinees on his way to the finish line of the primeval forest and give away the extra jade chess pieces.

This would make Sunagakure’s exam data look as good as possible, so as not to lose face in front of other villages.

But he hadn’t expected that the other Sunagakure examinees were much slower than him, so he didn’t run into a single one of his own people along the way.

« Verification is correct. Congratulations on passing the second stage of the exam. What is your name ? »

After the Konoha Proctor performed hand signs, all three jade chess pieces emitted a faint green glow.

This was an anti-counterfeiting verification method specially set up by Konoha as the organizer of the Chunin Exams.

It involved some principles of Seal, and by using a specific method, the special Chakra inside the jade chess pieces could be made to react.

« Sunagakure, Yan. »

After coldly leaving his name, Yan frowned and stood to the side, looking at the exit.

Since he already knew the situation of Chiyo and Ebizo, he was not in a hurry to go to the Konoha hospital to visit them.

He was also curious about the final results of the second stage of the exam.

About twenty minutes later, six Kumogakure examinees arrived at the finish line together.

All six were wearing Kumogakure headbands ; some had darker skin, but two had fair skin.

Among them, five Kumogakure examinees surrounded the burly Dou in the middle, taking him as the core.

« Sunagakure Ninja who uses Magnet Release, I didn’t expect you to be one step faster. »

Dou grinned at Yan, not caring that his group had arrived at the finish line later.

He could have reached the finish line faster, but he was delayed because he spent time searching for other lone Kumogakure examinees.

However, he was much luckier than Yan.

He originally had a few extra jade chess pieces in his hand, which he happened to distribute to his partners from the same village, letting them experience the feeling of being carried by a big shot.

After the Proctor verified the exam results of these six people, the Kumogakure Ninja who had been waiting there for a long time came up to meet them.

« Dou. »

This Kumogakure Ninja was a Jonin from Kumogakure. At this moment, his gaze towards Dou was one of equality, as if treating a peer, and there was even a faint hint of looking up to him.

Dou was a Genius who was being focused on in Kumogakure, becoming a Jonin at a young age.

There are also differences between Jonin and Jonin.

Some people are only Jonin for their whole lives, while some may have the potential to become Kage.

« Take them to get their injuries treated, I’ll stay here for a while longer and watch. »

After saying that, Dou stood with his arms crossed, looking interested, occasionally looking towards the exit of the primeval forest, as if waiting for something.
