# Chapitre 18 — Graduation Results and Team Assignments

After dinner, Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama stayed at home for a while before rushing back to the Hokage Building.

At this hour, apart from the Ninjas on night duty, the Hokage Building was already completely empty.

However, as the Hokage, there was no strict concept of clocking out.

If there were urgent matters to handle, one had to sit honestly at the desk and work overtime, no matter how late it was.

And today, what they needed to handle urgently was not just the usual official business, but also the follow-up matters of the graduation examination.

Inside the Hokage Office, Senju Hashirama sat at the desk, while Tobirama stood to the side with his arms crossed.

« These children lack combat awareness, and they also have weaknesses in Ninjutsu, Taijutsu, or Genjutsu. Becoming Genin will only harm them. »

Looking at the reports compiled by the Proctors of the graduation examination, and combining them with what he had understood while watching the battles during the day, Senju Hashirama singled out the evaluation forms of several students.

These students were all in the graduating year, and even by normal standards, they still did not meet the requirements to become Genin.

Although Senju Hashirama had a gentle personality, he would not soften his heart at this moment.

After all, not becoming a Ninja didn’t mean one couldn’t survive. If they truly lacked the talent, they could still shine and contribute in other industries.

Konoha was currently in a state of recovery, and there were actually many job opportunities.

After confirming and approving the assessment results for the graduating year, the two looked over the evaluation forms of those who participated in the early graduation examination together.

The requirements for the early graduation examination were even more stringent.

Just now, they had picked out the files of those who had failed the examination ; now, they were picking out the files of those who had passed.

After picking through them, they had only selected fewer than twenty.

Among them were the evaluation forms for Senju Matsumi, Hyuga Tsukuyomi, and Uchiha Kai.

Of course, there were also students who were one or two grades ahead of them but not in the graduating year who had applied for early graduation this year.

Seeing this, Senju Tobirama frowned and picked up Uchiha Kai’s file separately.

On the evaluation form, his performance in the first few battles was recorded as excellent.

But in his final battle, which was the match against Senju Matsumi, it was noted as extremely poor performance.

After all, he had been taken out in a flash, showing nothing during the fight.

Having both excellent and extremely poor performance meant he was teetering between graduating and not graduating.

However, thinking of Uchiha Kai’s performance during the day, he still placed this evaluation form on the side of those who had failed.

« This Uchiha brat has too obvious character flaws ; he’d better settle down a bit more, » Senju Tobirama said.

Although he had never liked the Uchiha Clan, he wouldn’t make things difficult for a child.

The reason he did this was that he genuinely believed the other party did not meet the requirements for graduation.

Even if he was stronger than his peers now, after graduation, he wouldn’t be able to handle things alone as a Genin ; he would need to form a squad to participate in missions.

In Tobirama’s view, this Uchiha brat had zero sense of camaraderie, and becoming a Genin would likely jeopardize his teammates.

« That’s true ; it was my oversight. »

After thinking about it, Senju Hashirama agreed with Senju Tobirama’s view.

« Then finally, there is the matter of the Jonin instructors. »

Senju Tobirama organized the personal files of all the students who had passed the graduation examination and placed them on the table.

And Senju Hashirama pulled open a drawer and took out another stack of documents.

This was the list of people in the Village who were willing to become Jonin instructors this year to guide the newly graduated Genin.

Next, they would compare the two stacks of documents.

Then, they would select an instructor for each Genin that matched their individual circumstances.

Compared to the review for graduation just now, the current task was the one that was truly time-consuming.

Not only did they have to consider the strengths and weaknesses of the Jonin and Genin in Ninjutsu, Taijutsu, etc., but they also had to consider personality and team complementarity.

Especially for some Bloodline Limit clans or Secret Technique clans, which had very strong characteristics.

And since both of them were people who valued the growth of Konoha’s new generation, they were particularly cautious about this task.

It wasn’t until late at night that the two finished the assignments for this year’s newly graduated Genin.

« It’s finally done, » Senju Hashirama sighed with relief.

« Brother, with you arranging it this way, I imagine little Matsumi and the others will be very surprised tomorrow. »

« Hahaha, now that you mention it, I’m curious to see their expressions tomorrow too. »

The next day.

The atmosphere inside the Ninja Academy was different from usual.

In the classroom where Matsumi was.

Everyone who knew about yesterday’s examination results looked at Senju Matsumi from time to time with complicated gazes.

Admiration, worship, envy, jealousy...

And in a seat in the back row, Uchiha Kai looked quite disheveled at this moment.

His arm was wrapped in bandages and slung around his neck, and there were also obvious abrasions on his cheeks and arms.

He glared resentfully at Senju Matsumi, but seemingly afraid of being noticed by the other party, he lowered his head in a flustered and awkward manner.

And that wasn’t the only thing making him miserable.

The fact that he had won the first few rounds of the examination yesterday wasn’t publicized ; instead, the result of his final battle where he was taken out in a flash by Senju Matsumi was widely circulated.

At this moment, he could feel some of the people around him, whom he had previously looked down upon, staring at him with mocking eyes.

This feeling made him feel as if he were being pricked by needles ; he couldn’t sit still.

If it weren’t to find out the examination results, he wouldn’t have come here today.

In his heart, he still didn’t think he would fail to graduate.

Although he hadn’t performed well at the end, he considered his performance in the first few matches to have been excellent.

Soon, the teacher walked into the classroom with a smile on his face.

« The results of yesterday’s graduation examination have been announced, and two students in our class have passed. »

« Other students who didn’t pass the graduation examination, don’t be discouraged. This is only the first year ; there will be opportunities in the future. »

Wait, two ?

Hearing this, a bad premonition suddenly arose in Uchiha Kai’s heart.

After scanning the classroom, the teacher smiled and nodded at Senju Matsumi and Hyuga Tsukuyomi.

« Senju Matsumi and Hyuga Tsukuyomi, congratulations on passing the early graduation examination. »

« Your life of study at the Ninja Academy ends here. I hope you will become the backbone of the Village’s defense in the future. »

« Additionally, your Jonin instructor is waiting for you on the rooftop ; you two may head over now. »

Hearing this, Senju Matsumi and Hyuga Tsukuyomi looked at each other in surprise.

They weren’t surprised that they had passed the graduation examination, but rather that they had been assigned to the same team.

After collecting herself, Hyuga Tsukuyomi felt very happy, a faint smile appearing on the corners of her mouth.

And Senju Matsumi also thought this was good, as it saved the effort of breaking in new teammates.

However, he was a bit curious about which Jonin would be their instructor.

Watching the two pack up their things and walk out of the classroom, Uchiha Kai’s heart was filled with resentment.

He really wanted to slam the table and loudly demand why he hadn’t passed the graduation examination.

But for one thing, his right hand was currently wrapped in bandages.

Secondly, he didn’t really have the courage to do so.

He could at most act tough among his peers ; if he disrespected the teacher, he would be taught a lesson in a minute.

Thus, he could only look at the door at the front of the classroom in frustration, his eyes even faintly turning red from anger.
