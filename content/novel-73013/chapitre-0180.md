# Chapitre 180 — The Tailed Beast Council is Established!

At this moment, Senju Tobirama stepped forward and added, « Regarding the operational mechanism of the council, we have a preliminary plan. »

He took out another scroll, unfurled it, and laid it on the table, revealing a clear architectural diagram drawn on it.

« First, the council will have five permanent seats, to be held by the Kage of the Five Great Nations respectively. »

« Takigakure, as the controller of the Seven-Tails, will also receive an observer seat, allowing them to participate in discussions but without voting rights. »

« Secondly, all sealing work for Tailed Beasts must be conducted under the supervision of the council. »

« We will provide a unified Sealing Jutsu and dispatch technicians to assist each Village in perfecting their sealing systems. »

« Most importantly... »

Senju Tobirama’s gaze turned sharp. « All Villages that obtain Tailed Beasts must sign a 'Tailed Beast Restriction Treaty'. »

« The treaty clearly stipulates that Tailed Beasts can only be used for defensive purposes and must not be used proactively in wars of aggression. If violated, other signatory nations have the right to impose joint sanctions. »

The First Kazekage finally spoke, his voice low : « How will it be supervised ? How can we ensure the treaty is not torn up ? »

« A good question. »

Senju Hashirama nodded. « This is exactly the problem we need to solve next. »

« We plan to embed a monitoring mechanism into the sealing formula of every Jinchuriki. »

« This mechanism is improved upon the Sealing Jutsu of the Uzumaki Clan and is capable of sensing fluctuations in Tailed Beast Chakra in real-time. »

« Once Tailed Beast Chakra is maliciously over-extracted, the Seal will instantly send a feedback signal to the council. At that point, the council can react immediately. »

First Mizukage Byakuren set down his teacup and slowly spoke : « In other words, not only must we accept Konoha’s Sealing Jutsu, but we must also allow Konoha’s technique to be implanted into our Jinchuriki ? »

There was obvious wariness in his tone.

Senju Hashirama waved his hand : « Mizukage, you have misunderstood. »

« The principles and operational methods of the monitoring technique will be fully shared with all members of the council. »

« Each Village can dispatch its own Sealing Team to learn it, or even make improvements upon this foundation. As long as the monitoring mechanism is not removed, it will be fine. »

Byakuren was silent for a moment, his expression softening slightly, but he still did not commit.

First Tsuchikage Ishikawa suddenly laughed, breaking the slightly tense atmosphere.

« Hokage, your proposal is very interesting. However, I have a question. »

« If a Village truly violates the treaty, how will the joint sanctions be specifically enforced ? »

« If it is a first offense where the situation is not serious, warnings will be the primary response. »

Senju Hashirama replied without hesitation : « If the situation is serious enough, or if the council has issued repeated warnings without change, the council can organize a joint force to conduct military intervention. »

« Of course, how the sanctions are specifically carried out can be discussed and determined by everyone later. »

« What about the command of the joint force ? » Ishikawa pressed.

« It will be decided by a temporary vote of the council, case by case. »

Senju Hashirama answered frankly.

Ishikawa stroked his beard, nodded thoughtfully, and asked no further questions.

The meeting hall fell into silence again.

The four Kage were each weighing the pros and cons in their hearts.

In the room, perhaps only Senju Hashirama truly viewed the Tailed Beast Council as an ideal peace measure.

For the others, they were only concerned with whether the Tailed Beast Council could bring greater benefits, or if it would cause losses to their own Villages.

Konoha possessed the strongest power, the most perfect Sealing Jutsu, and the richest experience in Tailed Beast management ; in this system, Konoha naturally occupied a dominant position.

But on the other hand, not joining the council meant being excluded from the Tailed Beast management system.

By then, other Villages would be developing their Tailed Beast power in an orderly manner under the framework of the council, while their own Village would be left to fight alone.

No one was willing to bear such a disadvantage.

Moreover, Konoha had already sold the Tailed Beasts to them ; if they refused to join the council, would Senju Hashirama go back on his word ?

This thought flashed through the minds of the four Kage almost simultaneously.

Ultimately, it was First Mizukage Byakuren who broke the silence first.

« Hidden Mist can join. »

He set down his teacup, his tone flat. « But I have two conditions. »

« First, the core technology of the monitoring technique must be made public and jointly held by the council. »

« Second, the council headquarters cannot be located in Konoha ; it should be established in a neutral region. »

Senju Hashirama nodded : « Reasonable. I can accept these two conditions. »

First Tsuchikage Ishikawa was the second to state his position : « Iwagakure has no objections either. »

« However, this old man suggests that the initial meeting place for the council can be set in the Land of Iron. It is neutral and convenient for all parties. »

« Agreed. » Senju Hashirama replied decisively.

Seeing this, Senju Matsuaki, who was acting as a guard at the edge of the venue, nodded thoughtfully.

Hidden Mist and Iwagakure had one thing in common : the First Kage of these Villages were older than the First Kage of the other ninja Villages.

Ishikawa was already showing signs of old age, and Byakuren was even more decrepit.

For them, the most important thing right now was the stability of the situation and being able to successfully pass the position of Kage to their successors.

During this period, they did not want any major changes in the Ninja World.

Therefore, they were willing to support Hashirama’s proposal.

Driven by the First Tsuchikage and First Mizukage, the others also made their decisions.

The First Kazekage was silent for a while, but eventually nodded : « Sunagakure joins. »

Only the First Raikage remained.

Everyone’s eyes were focused on him.

The First Raikage’s expression was gloomy, his arms crossed, and he said nothing.

Honestly, he was certainly unwilling to do things according to Konoha’s rules.

But he was even more aware of what the consequences would be if Hidden Cloud Village refused to join.

If the other four Villages were developing under the framework of the council while Hidden Cloud Village was isolated, that would be a true disaster.

« ... Hidden Cloud Village can join. »

The First Raikage said this through gritted teeth. « But I also want to propose a condition. »

« Please speak. »

Senju Hashirama’s attitude was sincere.

« The delivery time for the Eight-Tails and Two-Tails must be prioritized. »

The First Raikage said coldly, « I want to ensure that Hidden Cloud Village can complete its sealing deployment as soon as possible. »

Senju Hashirama smiled : « No problem. The order of the transactions can be negotiated, and we have noted the request of Hidden Cloud Village. »

He looked around, and seeing that no one had any further objections, he slammed the table heavily.

« Good ! Then, the Tailed Beast Council is hereby established ! »

« Next is the agenda regarding the council’s charter and the 'Tailed Beast Restriction Treaty' ; let’s aim to set a draft first. If there are any revision suggestions later, we can discuss them through correspondence. »

« Three months from now, we will convene the first official council meeting in the Land of Iron, at which time we will sign the treaty and establish the permanent council institutions. »

The four Kage each nodded, indicating their approval.

Thus, the two agenda items of the Five Kage Summit were settled.
