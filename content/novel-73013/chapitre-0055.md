# Chapitre 55 — An Accidentally Triggered S-Rank Mission

The impression of the last time he retrieved a Ninjutsu scroll was still very vivid, so Senju Matsuaki quickly found the original location of the scroll.

After placing the scroll back on the wooden shelf, he intended to turn and leave.

However, at that moment, the somewhat old shelf swayed slightly due to his previous movement.

A scroll on the top layer of the shelf lost its balance and slid down like a rolling log.

This movement naturally did not escape Senju Matsuaki’s notice ; after reacting, he immediately reached out his right hand to catch the scroll, preventing it from falling to the ground.

« It’s about time Uncle Hashirama replaced these shelves. »

Matsumi brushed off the layer of dust covering the scroll in his hand, thinking helplessly.

If he remembered correctly, ever since he first came here a few years ago, these wooden storage shelves had already been in this dilapidated state.

Later, he heard from Senju Kazuma that these were moved over from the Senju Clan’s old clan grounds when Konoha was first founded.

This storage cabinet had been in use since his great-grandfather Senju Butsuma served as the head of the Senju Clan ; it was truly an antique.

However, just as Senju Matsuaki was about to return the scroll to its original place, his hand suddenly froze.

[S-Rank mission released : Unseal the Ultimate Evil Weapon. Reward : 10,000 points !]

« ... »

Hearing the prompt in his ears, the expression on Senju Matsuaki’s face instantly froze.

How much did you say ?

A reward of 10,000 points—if these points were credited, it would be enough to make his strength skyrocket like a rocket.

However, for an S-Rank mission, even if the reward was generous, he was afraid he wouldn’t live to spend it.

For some time now, he had already grasped the patterns of the missions released by his cheat.

The missions released by the cheat were similar to those released by Konoha Village in terms of rank classification, but there were some differences.

The previous spar with Kumogakure, which required him not to be defeated by the future Third Raikage for ten minutes, was classified as a B-Rank mission.

Judging by the opponent’s strength at that time, it was estimated that he was just an ordinary Jonin and had not yet reached the elite level.

Holding out for ten minutes without being defeated was somewhat difficult for a Chunin, but not impossible, which showed that the difficulty of B-Rank roughly corresponded to the strength of a Chunin or Jonin.

In this view, the difficulty of A-Rank missions roughly corresponded to the level of a Jonin or Elite Jonin.

And an S-Rank mission could only be attempted when he reached the level of an Elite Jonin or Kage in the future.

Therefore, without much deliberation, Senju Matsuaki decisively gave up on completing this mission now.

Although his strength had advanced by leaps and bounds in the last month or two, and he might have even reached the Jonin level.

But facing an S-Rank mission for the first time, he was afraid that going there would just be throwing his life away.

After all, there was no deadline requirement for the mission, so he didn’t need to worry about it expiring ; it wouldn’t be too late to complete it at a more suitable time in the future.

Although he decided not to take the risk for the time being, Senju Matsuaki was still curious about the contents of this scroll.

« What exactly is this 'Ultimate Evil Weapon' ? I’ve never heard Uncle Hashirama or the others mention it. »

He curiously opened the scroll in his hand, his gaze filled with inquiry.

As the scroll was spread out, a map with slightly faded and weathered ink also unfolded.

Looking at this map, Matsumi initially felt a bit bewildered.

He still had some impression of some of the place names marked on this map, as he hadn’t done few missions in the Land of Fire since becoming a Genin.

However, apart from a few familiar place names, the rest of the markings were vastly different from his impression.

But thinking that this might be a map left over from the Sengoku period, it wasn’t entirely incomprehensible.

And the most conspicuous thing on the map was a cross pattern surrounded by a circle ; he assumed this was the location of the so-called Ultimate Evil Weapon mentioned in the mission.

At the blank space at the end of the map, there was a single sentence written.

« Must check the seal every six months... »

The handwriting behind it was already a bit blurry, but the general meaning could already be discerned.

This was the predecessor of the Senju Clan who recorded this map, reminding the younger generations to check the seal at the marked location every six months.

« Then if I go to complete this mission, wouldn’t I be making a decision that goes against my ancestors ? »

Matsumi closed the scroll in his hand, his expression slightly complex.

The ancestors said to check the seal every six months, clearly not wanting this so-called Ultimate Evil Weapon to see the light of day again.

However, the mission just released by the cheat went in the opposite direction, asking him to unseal this weapon.

But it seemed that the people of the Senju Clan had probably long forgotten about this matter and had not performed regular checks according to the predecessors' requirements.

This was most likely Uncle Hashirama’s fault.

He was probably busy with the war against the Uchiha Clan, then founded Konoha, and exhausted himself for the construction of the Village.

After all these years of busyness, he had probably long forgotten this instruction from his predecessors.

« It seems I, his great nephew, have to share the burden for Uncle Hashirama. »

After Senju Matsuaki put the scroll back to its original place, he couldn’t help but sigh.

He didn’t know what this so-called Ultimate Evil Weapon was for the time being, and the scroll didn’t have a detailed introduction either.

But since the predecessors had specifically left a scroll as a reminder, and the cheat had also released a related mission, it wouldn’t hurt for him to check it if he happened to pass by that area later.

As for unsealing this Ultimate Evil Weapon, he had no such thoughts for the time being.

Who knew how terrifying the things inside were ; if he couldn’t handle it after unsealing it, it would be a disaster.

He didn’t want to become an unfilial descendant of the Senju Clan.

After finishing the matter of returning the scroll, Senju Matsuaki left Senju Hashirama’s house.

Before leaving, he didn’t forget to lock the door.

After returning to his own home, he routinely opened the cheat’s requisition and expropriation platform and browsed the discount options for today.

Since he last requisitioned the 20-day check-in system, his points were nearly cleared out, and he hadn’t spent much since then.

Recently, he had also completed quite a few missions released by the cheat ; although his points were not as abundant as before, he had at least accumulated some.

The Chunin Exams were imminent ; although it shouldn’t be much of a problem with his strength, it would be great if he could cram a bit before the exam.

There was about a week left until the Chunin Exams.

In the time leading up to the Chunin Exams, Senju Matsuaki didn’t plan to take on any more missions but would stay in Konoha to rest and recuperate.

After looking at today’s discount options, it was no surprise that they didn’t make him want to buy anything.

And among the other full-price options outside the discount ones, it seemed there were no cheats he urgently needed right now.

Since that was the case, it was better to hoard the points and save them for when there were more cost-effective options in the future.

Thinking of this, Senju Matsuaki closed the platform interface, strolled to the backyard, and sat down against a large tree to rest.

The shade of the tree blocked the sunlight, a breeze came from afar, gently shaking the leaves, and also brushing away the fatigue between the young man’s brows.
