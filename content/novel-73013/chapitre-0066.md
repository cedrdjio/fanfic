# Chapitre 66 — Intercepting the Sunagakure Brother-Sister Duo

As time ticked by, the examinees present were already growing impatient.

The Proctor who had brought them here suddenly used a Body Flicker Technique and vanished from the spot.

A lingering echo remained in the air : « The exam begins ! »

This calm voice, in this edge of the primeval forest, struck like a bolt of lightning from the blue !

The ten Genin, who had already been secretly wary, quickly put distance between themselves once they reacted.

Among the ten, one Genin from Kumogakure and one from Iwagakure shot off like arrows from a bow, bolting toward the depths of the primeval forest as if they had greased their feet.

Clearly, they were the lucky ones who had drawn the jade chess piece.

Seeing this, a sharp glint flashed in the eyes of Chiyo from Sunagakure, and she abruptly unfurled the scroll at her waist.

Poof—

A puff of smoke erupted and dissipated, and a humanoid figure wrapped in a green robe sped forward, instantly closing the distance with the Iwagakure Genin.

Sensing an abnormal sound behind him, the Iwagakure Genin turned his head, only to meet three cold eyeballs.

« What the hell is this ! ? »

Looking at the face that resembled a human’s yet was filled with jarring inconsistencies, the Iwagakure Genin felt his hair stand on end.

It nearly scared the wits out of him.

By the time he realized this was Sunagakure’s Puppet Technique, he had already been tightly bound by the four pairs of limbs extending from the green-robed puppet and collapsed to the ground in a sorry state.

On the other side, Senju Matsuaki was not idle either.

The instant the exam began, he locked onto the Kumogakure Genin who was intent on running deep into the forest.

Not all Kumogakure Ninja were dark-skinned, muscular men ; there were also some with fair skin, while those with a medium complexion were actually fewer.

Within a few breaths, Senju Matsuaki caught up to the Kumogakure Ninja who had run some distance away, moving at a speed that the surrounding Genin could barely track.

Since obtaining Asura Chakra, he felt his Senju Clan constitution growing stronger every day.

And his Breathing Technique, which had long since reached the Total Concentration : Constant stage, pushed his physical fitness up another level.

The Kumogakure Ninja, who was running at full speed to shake off the others, suddenly felt a hand on his shoulder.

This sensation instinctively reminded him of his childhood sweetheart back in the Hidden Cloud Village, who would pat his shoulder to cheer him on before he left.

However, he quickly realized something was wrong.

This was Konoha ; where would a childhood sweetheart come from ?

His pupils constricted sharply, and he strained his body to the right, attempting to break free from the hand on his shoulder.

But the next instant, a massive force erupted from behind him like a collapsing mountain, pressing him face-first into the ground.

His right cheek was pressed unceremoniously into the dirt, and his shoulder was visibly trembling unnaturally.

In just a single moment, he had gone pale and lost all ability to act, lying there at the mercy of another.

« Don’t be in such a rush to leave ; give me the chess piece. »

Senju Matsuaki crouched down, opened the Ninja tool pouch at the other’s waist, and took out the shogi piece with the character 'Jade' written on it.

After a moment’s thought, he proceeded to take a few shuriken from the pouch and stuffed them into his own.

Standing up, he saw the strange looks from the surrounding Genin and scratched his head.

« I’ve had some high expenses preparing for the Chunin Exams lately, so please understand. »

He felt no psychological burden regarding his actions just now.

If one viewed this Chunin Exam as a mission, then the Kumogakure Ninja he had just defeated was simply an enemy Ninja.

Everything seized after defeating the opponent was the spoils of war for the victor.

However, Senju Matsuaki seemed to have misunderstood.

The strange expressions on the surrounding Genin were not because he had confiscated the Kumogakure Genin’s shuriken, but because he had eliminated a Genin within less than a minute of the exam starting.

Such efficiency filled them with immense dread.

Over on the Sunagakure side.

Chiyo held her hands horizontally in front of her, her five fingers connected to threads formed by condensed Chakra.

As she curled her fingers, the puppet that had previously shot out retreated rapidly while binding the Iwagakure Genin, returning to her side within a few breaths.

« Ebizo ! » Chiyo hissed.

Ebizo, standing to the side, immediately understood and delivered a heavy hand strike to the Iwagakure Ninja’s neck, knocking him unconscious.

He then rummaged through the Ninja’s waist pouch and retrieved a jade chess piece.

Seeing this, Chiyo’s furrowed brow relaxed. She shot a wary glance at Senju Matsuaki not far away and prepared to leave with her brother.

She had just witnessed Senju Matsuaki’s strength.

It was strong.

But she couldn’t tell exactly how strong.

After all, the one-sided crush that couldn’t even be called a battle had ended in an instant, revealing very few details.

Now that Chiyo had obtained a jade chess piece, she only needed to ensure she reached the finish line and had no desire to invite unnecessary trouble.

Furthermore, she assumed she had no conflict of interest with him.

Yet unexpectedly, just as she planned to leave with her brother, Senju Matsuaki began walking slowly toward her.

« Hand over the chess piece. »

Looking at their faces, which seemed familiar yet quite different from the future, a sharp glint flashed in Senju Matsuaki’s eyes.

It was undeniable that in her later years, Chiyo provided significant help to Naruto and the others from Konoha, presenting a relatively positive image.

However, in her youth, she had caused quite a bit of trouble for Konoha.

Especially during the Second Shinobi World War ; if not for Tsunade, Konoha’s medical saint, countless Konoha Ninja would likely have been poisoned to death by Chiyo.

And although Ebizo did not have the renowned fame of Chiyo, he would later take charge of Sunagakure’s intelligence and espionage, becoming a viper hidden in the shadows.

For the sake of Konoha, intercepting the two of them here might be a good choice.

Having their path blocked, Chiyo and Ebizo both adopted defensive stances, wary of Matsuaki’s next move.

« Wait, you should already have a jade chess piece, why... »

Chiyo, who was about to question Senju Matsuaki, suddenly felt a cold killing intent wash over her, and her expression froze.

« Give him the chess piece... »

Gritting her teeth, Chiyo looked at her brother, Ebizo, beside her, and said unwillingly.

Although not particularly willing, Ebizo, who always listened to his sister’s advice, tossed the jade chess piece they had just captured toward Senju Matsuaki.

« If I’m not mistaken, you two have at least one more jade chess piece. »

Catching the tossed piece, Senju Matsuaki did not leave, but instead glanced at the two.

« Hey, you guy ! Isn’t this going a bit too far ! ? »

Upon hearing this, the young and brash Ebizo was immediately enraged.

We saw that you weren’t someone to mess with, so we let you have the piece we just grabbed, but now you want the piece that originally belonged to us too ! ?

Do you really think our Sunagakure genius brother-sister duo are pushovers ! ?
