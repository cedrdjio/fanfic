# Chapitre 109 — Farming, Farming, Farming!

He pulled a storage scroll from his Ninja tool pouch, unfurled it, and took out a small, heavy, and exquisitely designed crate of drinks.

« No need for that, why bring a gift when visiting... Wait, what is this ? »

Seeing that Senju Matsuaki seemed to be taking out a gift, the neighbor hurriedly waved his hand to stop him.

In his view, they had just had a friendly spar, and since he hadn’t provided any guidance or pointers, there was no reason to accept a gift.

However, upon seeing the crate of twelve bottles that Senju Matsuaki was holding, his resistant expression faltered.

If he wasn’t mistaken, could this crate be the senju happy water that had just launched today, becoming an instant hit and causing supply shortages due to the frantic pre-orders from nobles and dignitaries of various nations ?

He had watched the matches earlier that day, and after tasting the complimentary drink provided at the venue, he had wanted to buy more, only to be told they were sold out.

Although he was happy to see the drink produced by his own clan’s industry being so popular, he felt a bit sad that he couldn’t buy any himself.

Now, looking at the senju happy water he had desired but couldn’t obtain, he couldn’t bring himself to refuse.

« Good lad, how do you have this ? Weren’t you participating in the exams all day today ? »

He hesitated, then reached out to take the crate and hold it in his arms, asking in confusion.

« Uh, that’s a long story... »

Senju Matsuaki scratched his head, not knowing how to explain it for a moment.

Happy Water, also known as cola, certainly couldn’t just appear out of thin air in this world.

The truth was, a few months ago, after Senju Matsuaki made some suggestions to Senju Hashirama and others regarding the hosting of the Chunin Exams, he had written down the recipe for this carbonated drink based on his vague memories.

After handing the recipe to Senju Tobirama, the latter was initially skeptical about what could actually be made from it.

However, after the first attempt, the taste of the final product made even Senju Tobirama’s eyes light up.

Thus, after adjusting the recipe multiple times, the final version was confirmed and put into production. As for the name « senju happy water, » it was just something Senju Matsuaki had come up with on the spot.

Of course, a clan exists for its members.

After Senju Matsuaki contributed the recipe, the Senju Clan did not treat him unfairly, promising him a permanent thirty percent share of this new industry !

Today was the first day of the official promotion and sale of senju happy water. Early in the morning, before he had even left for the exam venue, Senju Tobirama had sent people to deliver over a dozen crates of Happy Water to Matsuaki’s residence.

This could be seen as a small privilege for him as the « Father of Happy Water, » and also a way for him to check the quality of the first batch of products.

Because he was in a rush in the morning, he had stored one crate in his storage scroll intending to drink it during a break, but he had completely forgotten about it after the assessment.

« The recipe for this Happy Water was something I gave to the clan earlier, so I received some of the product in advance. »

« Today is the first day of sales for senju happy water. The clan wasn’t sure about the production scale, and they also had to set aside a portion for free samples for the audience, so the remaining supply is limited. »

« The clan will expand production in the future, so it won’t be in such short supply like it is today. »

« Ah, I see. That’s good, that’s good... »

Hearing Senju Matsuaki’s explanation, the Senju Clan neighbor couldn’t help but be stunned.

So, not only is your strength as a Ninja improving rapidly, but you haven’t slacked off on the business side either ?

« Well, I’ll head to the next house then. I’ll visit again another day. » Senju Matsuaki waved goodbye.

« Alright, I look forward to your outstanding performance in the Chunin Exams tomorrow. »

After seeing Senju Matsuaki off, the neighbor closed and locked the door, pondering Senju Matsuaki’s last words with a hint of confusion.

What did he mean by « the next house » ?

After leaving the neighbor’s house, Senju Matsuaki continued to wander around the Senju Clan compound.

After winning the spar just now, the mission reward points were immediately credited, bringing a smile to his lips.

What is farming ? This is farming !

Since his Golden Finger didn’t restrict victories to only those occurring in the exam venue, Senju Matsuaki naturally couldn’t let this opportunity pass.

The Senju Clan was very united, and the relations between neighbors were also quite good.

Usually, Senju Matsuaki received plenty of care from these neighbors and elders, and out of gratitude, he would occasionally visit them with some gifts.

It wasn’t very late yet, so he figured the clansmen wouldn’t refuse a request for a spar.

Thinking of this, Senju Matsuaki walked quickly back home, packed up, put the remaining dozen or so crates of senju happy water into his storage scroll, and hurried out.

Thus, under the cover of night, the sounds of knocking and polite greetings could be heard everywhere within the Senju Clan.

« Uncle, are you free right now ? »

« Sister, your attainments in Earth Release are profound. I was wondering if you have time to give me some pointers ? »

« Hello, Auntie. Is my brother home ? I have a few questions about Ninjutsu that I’d like to ask him. »

« Brother Hejian, you’re still up ? Perfect, there’s something I’d like to ask of you. »

...

An hour or two passed quickly, and Senju Matsuaki walked out of a clansman’s house, feeling a bit unsatisfied.

After thinking it over, he realized he had visited almost everyone he could.

Although there were many Ninjas in the Senju Clan, not many were free at this time.

Some had missions to execute tomorrow and had likely gone to bed early ; he could see their lights were completely extinguished from outside their doors.

Senju Matsuaki did not disturb these clansmen.

In addition, some other clansmen who were not in the Ninja profession or whose strength was only at the Genin level were also excluded from his targets.

After all, time was limited, so he should prioritize Chunin and Jonin, who offered better efficiency.

Since there were no suitable targets within the clan, Senju Matsuaki set his sights outside the Family.

After walking quickly out of the Senju Clan compound, he headed straight for the Sarutobi Clan compound.

Among his peers, the ones Senju Matsuaki was familiar with were Hyuga Tsukuyomi, Hiruzen Sarutobi, Danzo Shimura, Uchiha Kagami, and others.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi was a good teammate with whom he had fought side-by-side countless times, while Hiruzen Sarutobi and the others, being students of Senju Tobirama, had frequent interactions with him, and over time, they had developed a deep friendship.

Although Senju Matsuaki had some friends from his time at the Ninja Academy, they weren’t even Genin yet, so they obviously couldn’t serve as mission targets.

After thinking it over, Hiruzen Sarutobi and the others were the best choices.

The Sarutobi Clan compound was much smaller in scale compared to the Senju Clan, though the architectural style was generally similar.

After walking for a few more minutes within the compound, Senju Matsuaki stood in front of a simple, brown, antique gate.

*Knock, knock-knock—*

After knocking on Hiruzen Sarutobi’s door, Senju Matsuaki stood in place, waiting for them to open it.

He first faintly heard the sound of conversation, followed immediately by hurried footsteps.

*Click—*

The door opened, but the figure that appeared was unexpected to Senju Matsuaki.

« Matsuaki, why are you here ? »

After clearly seeing who it was, Danzo Shimura’s expression was slightly surprised, and he didn’t forget to wipe the sweat from his face.
