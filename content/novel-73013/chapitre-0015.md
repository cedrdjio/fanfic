# Chapitre 15 — Possessing a body as hard as adamant, while also being able to freely extend and retract, this is the Transformation Technique of Enma.

The reason for this seems to be that Hiruzen Sarutobi believes it can bridge the gap in taijutsu between the two of them.

Because of his partner Enma, Hiruzen Sarutobi is also more proficient in staff techniques when it comes to taijutsu.

However, just as Hiruzen Sarutobi has not used his full strength.

Senju Matsuaki’s Breathing Technique is also far more than this.

In the world of Demon Slayer, ordinary people can fight against demons through Breathing Techniques and arduous training.

And the best among them, the Hashira, were merely mortal flesh and blood before practicing Breathing Techniques.

But here with Senju Matsuaki, where Breathing Technique meets Chakra, the miraculous reaction between the two brings about an effect where one plus one is greater than two.

« Brother Hiruzen, you must be careful too ! »

Giving as good as he gets, before going all out, Senju Matsuaki kindly warned him.

Subsequently, a massive amount of Chakra erupted from his body, finally concentrating on his hands and the short blade.

« Strength-Enhancement Slash ! »

Strength-Enhancement is a technique passed down within the Senju Clan, producing immense destructive power by concentrating and erupting Chakra.

Tsunade’s Heavenly Foot of Pain, Sakura Haruno’s Cherry Blossom Clash, are all forms of Strength-Enhancement.

And Matsuaki chooses to combine Strength-Enhancement with swordsmanship at this moment, releasing Chakra at the point where the blade makes contact with the opponent.

Then, Hiruzen Sarutobi was stunned to discover.

His staff strike, which he had been so confident in, was blocked by a short blade with a massive difference in size.

The whistling wind originally stirred up by the Adamantine Staff suddenly ceased at this moment.

Subsequently, he only felt a massive force coming from the opposite direction of the Adamantine Staff, causing his hands to be uncontrollably pulled behind him.

« Oh no ! »

Feeling his body lose some balance, Hiruzen Sarutobi reacted immediately, letting go and severing his connection with the Adamantine Staff.

After being thrown away, the Adamantine Staff transformed back into Enma in mid-air, who, after flipping to disperse the force, immediately ran to support Hiruzen Sarutobi.

« Enma ! Fire Release : Fire Dragon Bullet ! »

Hiruzen Sarutobi whispered while forming hand seals with both hands.

« Understood ! Wind Release : Great Breakthrough ! »

Enma, having fought side-by-side with Hiruzen Sarutobi countless times, immediately understood, stopped running, and also began forming hand seals.

This is also one of the advantages of the Enma clan.

Because their body structure is similar to humans, after training, they can also form hand seals and use Ninjutsu like a Ninja.

The Fire Dragon Bullet combined with the whirlwind created by the Great Breakthrough ; the fire borrowed the wind’s momentum, and the wind aided the fire’s power, turning into a fiery whirlwind that swept away.

« Water Release : Water Shockwave ! »

In response, Senju Matsuaki just quickly formed hand seals with both hands, then spat out a large amount of water from his mouth.

The water flow rose in a tornado shape, circling Senju Matsuaki’s body, and then spread downwards towards the direction of Hiruzen Sarutobi and Enma.

This is one of the Water Release techniques that Senju Tobirama excels at, and it is also the strongest Water Release Ninjutsu that Senju Matsuaki can currently master.

The difference is that Senju Tobirama’s attainments in Water Release are extremely deep, and he can summon massive amounts of water out of thin air even in a waterless environment.

While Senju Matsuaki, like other Ninja, can only spit out water flow from his mouth.

Therefore, there will be differences in both power and casting speed.

After the water dissipated, the originally dry ground became very damp ; an ordinary person walking on such ground would likely slip if they were not careful.

« It’s not over yet ! »

And at this moment, Senju Matsuaki moved again.

The previous Strength-Enhancement Slash was not his full strength.

« Strength-Enhancement Rapid Slashes ! »

Veins bulged on his arms, and the flow of Chakra within Senju Matsuaki accelerated again.

After closing the distance with Hiruzen Sarutobi at extreme speed, he swung the short blade in his hand ; those watching from the sidelines could only see streaks of afterimages.

« Hey, this is too reckless ! »

Seeing this, Hiruzen Sarutobi couldn’t help but feel a little apprehensive.

Compared to the previous Strength-Enhancement Slash, Senju Matsuaki’s Strength-Enhancement Rapid Slashes were slightly inferior in terms of force.

However, relying on the extreme speed, it was enough to compensate for this flaw.

Even though he already had psychological expectations and used his full strength when swinging the staff, Hiruzen Sarutobi still felt as if his entire arm was about to fall apart.

« Enma ! Adamantine Prison Wall ! »

Hiruzen Sarutobi whispered, and the Adamantine Staff in his hand suddenly flew out of his grasp.

The Adamantine Staff appeared to be thrown at Senju Matsuaki to disrupt his movements.

However, in mid-air, it split into dozens of staff segments, quickly forming a cubic cage, enveloping Senju Matsuaki within it.

Even so, Senju Matsuaki did not give up, and the movements in his hands did not stop.

Although he can keep the Breathing Technique active, his offensive at this moment is primarily based on Strength-Enhancement, consuming Chakra at every moment.

Each release of Strength-Enhancement causes him significant consumption.

If he cannot resolve the battle quickly, then without Hiruzen Sarutobi needing to make a move, he would have already been defeated by exhaustion.

« Break for me ! »

Strength-Enhancement Slashes continuously struck the Adamantine Prison, causing it to shake violently.

Even though it is claimed to have a body as hard as adamant, the Adamantine Staff that Enma transforms into still has certain limits.

If it were the Hiruzen Sarutobi and Enma of the future who had grown to their peak, perhaps Senju Matsuaki would really be trapped in the Adamantine Prison until he was exhausted.

But right now, they are only Chunin.

Trying to trap him like this is still far from enough !

« Hiruzen ! I can’t hold on much longer ! »

The Adamantine Prison shook violently, and Enma’s somewhat panicked voice came from within.

« I know ! Hold on a little longer ! »

After Hiruzen Sarutobi responded hurriedly, he formed hand seals with both hands again.

This time, the seals he formed were more complex than before.

Seeing this, Senju Tobirama outside the arena had a change in expression and was already prepared to enter the field to save them at any time.

And it was precisely because he knew that Hokage-sama and Master Tobirama could perform a rescue at any time that Hiruzen Sarutobi could confidently use this Ninjutsu.

« Break ! »

Accompanied by a low roar from Senju Matsuaki, the sturdy Adamantine Prison finally collapsed.

And Enma, who had been quite energetic just a moment ago, was already lying on the ground, shaking his head, feeling dizzy.

Matsuaki’s continuous slashes just now made him feel like his brains were about to be rattled loose.

Knowing he had no further power to fight, and that the battle on the field was approaching its end, Enma simply dispelled the Summoning Technique and vanished from the spot.

Just as Senju Matsuaki broke free and was preparing to launch an attack on Hiruzen Sarutobi.

Hiruzen Sarutobi also stopped the action of forming hand seals.

« Lightning-Water Dragon Bullet ! ! »

Hiruzen Sarutobi first spat out a stream of water from his mouth, forming a dragon-shaped water bullet beside him.

Subsequently, streaks of lightning were released from his palms towards the water dragon bullet, permeating into the water bullet, yet also circling outside the water bullet.

This is a composite Ninjutsu created by Hiruzen Sarutobi by combining Lightning Release and Water Release Ninjutsu.

Hiruzen Sarutobi had shown excellent talent in Ninjutsu since he was young, able to combine Ninjutsu to unleash even more powerful destructive force.

This is also the reason why Senju Tobirama accepted him as a personal disciple.

This kind of combination of Ninjutsu is not a Bloodline Limit, but it can also achieve an effect where one plus one is greater than two.
