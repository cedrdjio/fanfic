# Chapitre 73 — Sunagakure's Yan Attacks!

« Hokage-sama, it’s like this... »

« As the chief Proctor for the second stage of the Joint Chunin Exams, Tobirama-sama has appointed me to represent Konoha, the host of these exams, to convey to you all the current status regarding candidate casualties. »

After speaking, Nara Kano took a small slip of paper from his pocket and read the records on it.

« As of now, the corpse of one Iwagakure candidate has been found in Area 3, the corpses of two Hidden Mist candidates have been found in Area 7, and the corpses of one Iwagakure and one Sunagakure candidate have been found in Area 8. »

« What ! ? »

Upon hearing this, the representatives from Iwagakure, Hidden Mist, and Sunagakure all changed expressions.

Conversely, Kumogakure’s Hong breathed a sigh of relief.

« Hey, it’s only the first day, isn’t this mortality rate a bit high ? » Hōzuki Gengetsu asked with a dark face.

« While we feel regret for the misfortune of your village’s candidates, the Chunin Exams inherently carry risks and accidents. »

« The Proctors who discovered the bodies will issue investigation reports, which will be delivered to you. We uphold the principle of seeking the truth and use Konoha’s reputation to guarantee the authenticity of these reports. »

Facing Hōzuki Gengetsu’s questioning, Nara Kano was well-prepared ; with two sentences, he deflected the other man’s anger.

« Hmph. »

Hōzuki Gengetsu snorted coldly, his expression grim as he sat back in his seat.

He wanted to question the other side about the fact that two Hidden Mist candidates had died, but realized he had no solid grounds to stand on.

Mu, who was beside him, was the same ; as of now, Iwagakure had also lost two candidates, which was equivalent to the Hidden Mist’s losses.

He had just said that accidents and risks existed in the Chunin Exams, so now he couldn’t bring himself to ask any questions.

« I hope Konoha can give us a satisfactory result. »

In the end, Mu just threw out this one sentence, then stopped speaking, gazing distractedly at the crystal ball in front of him.

These quasi-second-generation Kage, who were gloating just a moment ago, were now mostly looking grim.

They had now realized that they had been played by this Konoha Ninja from the Nara Clan.

If they hadn’t mistakenly thought that the ones killed were Konoha Genin and said things to distance themselves from responsibility, they wouldn’t be unable to find a reason to question him now.

Otherwise, three Ninja villages joining forces to apply pressure because of Genin deaths would have been a significant burden even for Konoha at this time.

« Kano, you... »

Senju Hashirama looked at Nara Kano with a complicated expression, unable to speak for a long time, and finally just gave him a quiet thumbs-up.

People who use their brains like this really have a lot of schemes.

However, since it wasn’t Konoha’s Genin who died, Senju Hashirama didn’t feel as sad or regretful as before.

Seeing that the representatives from the other villages weren’t in the mood to talk, he also tried to remain serious and silent, watching the crystal ball on the table that only showed an image once in a long while.

While Senju Hashirama was watching short videos here, Senju Tobirama was hiding in his office watching a feature film.

« I haven’t paid attention for a while, but Matsumi has improved quite a bit. »

Seeing the image reflected in the crystal ball, Senju Tobirama smiled with satisfaction.

After seeing Senju Matsuaki and Inuzuka Rei quickly dispose of the two Iwagakure Ninja corpses, he stopped paying attention and switched to other images.

He only had Nara Kano report the ones where bodies were left behind to Senju Hashirama.

As for those whose bodies were disposed of without a trace, they would all be treated as missing persons after the assessment ended.

At night.

The burning campfire created a small patch of warmth and light in the impenetrable darkness.

Two girls sat by the campfire, their faces flickering in the firelight.

The younger girl looked about seven years old, and traces of a struggle were visible on her.

Although she looked a bit tired, her pure Byakugan were filled with determination.

The older girl looked to be thirteen or fourteen, with short brown hair and a Konoha headband on her forehead.

The two girls were Hyuga Tsukuyomi and Biwako.

Both being Konoha Genin, the two had teamed up after being assigned to the same starting point.

Although their start was unfavorable and neither was assigned a jade chess piece, after a day of effort, they had already seized one from other candidates.

Next, they only needed to seize one more jade chess piece, and they could lay low without conflicting with others and head straight to the finish line.

« Tsukuyomi, you’ve worked hard today, get some rest first, I’ll take the first half of the night. »

Biwako looked at the faint exhaustion on Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s face and spoke with concern.

« Then I’ll leave it to you, Biwako-neesan. »

Hyuga Tsukuyomi didn’t refuse Biwako’s suggestion.

It was true that she had contributed more today ; whether it was activating the Byakugan to search for targets or fighting with Gentle Fist and Ninjutsu, it was quite a drain.

Soon, she leaned against a thick tree and fell into a light sleep, her chest rising and falling steadily with her breathing.

Beside her, Biwako also dutifully performed her watch, standing against the tree, gripping a Kunai tightly in her hand.

Recalling the day’s experiences, her mood couldn’t help but sink.

She had originally thought that since becoming a Genin, she had experienced many missions and grown quite a bit compared to when she first graduated.

However, it was only during today’s second stage of the assessment that she realized.

When participating in C-rank missions before, either they didn’t encounter Ninjas from major villages, or even if they did, there were team-leading Jonin to cover for them as Genin.

Today, encountering Ninjas from other major villages, she suddenly realized she didn’t yet have the capacity to be a Chunin.

She wasn’t a top-tier genius, and she had been a Genin for less than a year ; perhaps she still needed more time to settle.

Although she had her own thoughts, making a decision required determination.

Soon, the first half of the night passed.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi, having regained some energy, woke up at the right time to switch shifts with Biwako.

In the second half of the night, Hyuga Tsukuyomi was responsible for the watch, while Biwako rested to recover her energy.

As time passed, the sky gradually turned from dark to bright, and sunlight spilled onto the ground through the gaps in the leaves.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi kept her Byakugan activated to monitor the surroundings.

At one moment, her expression suddenly changed.

After turning to kick out and extinguish the nearly burnt-out campfire, she gently shook the shoulder of the sleeping Biwako.

Biwako, who was already on alert, woke up quickly and was about to speak, but was stopped by the index finger Hyuga Tsukuyomi held up to her lips.

« There is a very terrifying person coming in our direction, we have to leave here immediately. »

Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s small face wore an unprecedented look of gravity as she whispered.

Seeing Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s serious expression, Biwako also realized the severity of the situation and quickly got up to follow her.

When a Ninja from the Hyuga Clan activates their Byakugan and tells you a strong enemy is coming, those who know already run three miles away.

« Is it a coincidence or intentional, to come straight for us... »

The Byakugan possesses a 360-degree field of vision, so even without looking back, Hyuga Tsukuyomi could see the scene behind her.

As time passed, her heart gradually sank to the bottom.

She discovered that the other party’s appearance didn’t seem to be a coincidence, but that they had truly found their location.

Moreover, as time went on, this unknown enemy’s speed was increasing at a terrifying rate.

« Not good ! Dodge ! ! »

Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s eyes widened, and after shouting a low warning to the running Biwako, she dodged to the right.

Biwako realized and dodged to the left.

Whoosh—

Several piercing sounds rang out.

Just in the path where the two had been running, several pitch-black long needles pierced into the hard ground at a forty-five-degree angle.

« You reacted quite quickly. »

Behind them, blue-haired and yellow-eyed, wearing a Sunagakure headband and a cape, Yan flew over on a circular platform made of black Iron Sand.

Seeing the two dodge his Iron Sand needles, he felt a bit surprised.

However, upon seeing Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s pair of Byakugan, he immediately understood.

The Byakugan, such a famous Bloodline Limit, can be said to be known to everyone in the Ninja World.
