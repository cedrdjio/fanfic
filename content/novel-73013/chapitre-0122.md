# Chapitre 122 — The Next Senju Tobirama?

The clamor of the surrounding crowd seemed irrelevant to the center of the examination arena.

Senju Matsuaki was dressed in lightweight combat attire, his short blade already drawn and held in his hand.

The mirror-like surface of the blade reflected the boy’s calm, red eyes.

Opposite him stood the last remaining Hidden Mist examinee, teeth clenched, staring fixedly at every move Senju Matsuaki made.

Seeing that Senju Matsuaki remained motionless, the Hidden Mist examinee felt conflicted.

If his opponent launched an attack first, he feared he wouldn’t be able to block it.

If he launched an attack first, he feared he had no chance of winning either.

Then what was the point of fighting !

Thinking of this, the Hidden Mist examinee’s mentality was already starting to give up.

He knew very well that the strength of Senju Matsuaki and the other two examinees was on a completely different level than that of real Ninjas who had just been promoted from Genin to Chunin.

How to lose with some dignity was the key to deciding whether he could be promoted to Chunin.

At this very moment, Senju Matsuaki was not thinking about how to strike next, or how to concentrate all his strength into the blade in his hand.

On the contrary, he was doing his best to restrain the Tremor-Tremor Fruit power within his body, preventing it from erupting during the upcoming battle.

He now understood, to some extent, that saying from Aizen.

« To step over an ant without crushing it, that amount of force is very difficult to control. »

Since he could not yet command the power of the Tremor-Tremor Fruit as easily as his own arm, if he did not deliberately restrain the fruit’s power, a casual slash would likely shatter the entire body of the Kirigakure Ninja opposite him.

He had no grudge against the Hidden Mist, and since this was an open examination arena, dealing such a heavy blow was certainly inappropriate.

However, the benefit was that Senju Matsuaki discovered that while constantly trying to restrain this power, his control over the fruit seemed to be gradually strengthening.

His body was constantly adapting to this Tremor-Tremor Fruit power, and this power was also constantly adapting to his body.

Finally, the Hidden Mist examinee opposite him seemed unable to bear the pressure of the standoff.

His black soft-soled Ninja boots left a mark on the ground as his figure shot forward like an arrow released from a bowstring.

Closer !

A little closer !

Not bad, at this distance... it is my victory ! !

« Lava Release : Lava Melting Technique ! ! »

After closing the distance to a certain point, the Hidden Mist examinee rapidly formed hand seals, while taking a deep breath, his chest swelling.

Then, he opened his mouth, and a pool of yellow, viscous liquid spewed out, like a large net spreading open, destined to seal off Senju Matsuaki’s retreat.

« A Bloodline Limit ? » Senju Matsuaki raised an eyebrow.

As expected, anyone who could make it all the way to the fourth round of the examination was definitely no simple character.

He had some impression of Lava Release.

The future Fifth Generation Mizukage, Mei Terumī, possessed two Bloodline Limits, one of which was Lava Release.

Lava Release was different from the other Lava Release ; it was not magma, but a highly corrosive acidic liquid.

This acidic liquid could not only corrode liquids, but it seemed it could even dissolve Chakra.

In the original work, Mei Terumī even successfully dissolved a Susanoo with her Lava Release, which could be considered one of her rare highlight moments.

However, compared to the future Mei Terumī, the Lava Release of this Kirigakure Ninja was somewhat lacking.

On the viewing platform, Sunagakure’s Yan and Kumogakure’s Dou, having undergone medical treatment, had their negative physical states largely recovered.

Except for being a bit pale, they appeared to be fine.

Seeing this scene on the field, a flash of brilliance appeared in their eyes.

As battle-hardened Ninjas, the two of them simulated at least three ways to counter it the moment they saw the Lava Release.

What they were curious about now was how Senju Matsuaki would deal with the Lava Release.

Would he dodge skillfully, try his best to defend, or launch an attack of his own to contend with it ?

The defensive method he chose would, to some extent, reflect his unique combat style.

To them, this was an important source of intelligence for understanding their opponent, Senju Matsuaki.

Under the gaze of the Daimyo, the Kage, and other spectators in the audience, Senju Matsuaki finally moved.

Ahead, the corrosive acidic liquid, accompanied by a pungent odor, swept forward with a roar, raising wisps of acid mist in the air.

Senju Matsuaki stomped on the ground, shattering it as he retreated rapidly, while simultaneously forming hand seals at high speed, his internal Chakra surging instantly.

« Water Release : Exploding Water Colliding Wave ! ! »

In an instant, the situation on the field underwent a drastic change.

« Hey, hey, this level is a bit exaggerated... »

The two Proctors responsible for maintaining order and safety at the edge of the examination arena exchanged glances, both seeing the look of astonishment in each other’s eyes.

They were closest to the two combatants, and even though Senju Matsuaki’s Exploding Water Colliding Wave was not yet complete, they had already felt the change in humidity in the air.

As Ninjas who often had to fight in the wild, they were quite sensitive to changes in air humidity.

The feeling the surrounding air gave them now was as if they had arrived at the edge of a lake or the ocean.

The next instant, Senju Matsuaki completed the technique.

One layer, two layers, three layers...

Layers of giant waves rose from the ground in an instant, lifting Senju Matsuaki into mid-air.

As for the corrosive acidic liquid previously released by the Hidden Mist examinee, it was swallowed, absorbed, and diluted the moment it came into contact with this massive flow of water.

Finally, it disappeared silently into the massive torrent.

Lava Release was indeed stronger than Water Release, but that was under the premise that the skill levels of the users were equal.

Facing the Exploding Water Colliding Wave unleashed by Senju Matsuaki using the massive Chakra within his body, the Hidden Mist examinee’s Lava Release crumbled like a house of cards.

Riding these layers of giant waves, Senju Matsuaki made no other superfluous movements.

He simply stood there with a normal expression, his red eyes reflecting the scene of his opponent being swept away by the giant waves.

« The winner, Senju Matsuaki ! »

On the viewing platform, Senju Tobirama announced the result of the match with an arrogant expression.

The other Kage’s eyes flickered, their gazes shifting between Senju Matsuaki on the arena and Senju Tobirama not far away.

With such Water Release, in time, this child might become the next Senju Tobirama...

Such a thought emerged in their minds simultaneously.

« Choosing to go head-to-head, skilled in Water Release Ninjutsu... »

On the other side of the platform, Sunagakure’s Yan had a calculating glint in his yellow eyes.

His brief encounter with Senju Matsuaki during the second stage of the examination, as well as observing the current battle, prompted him to reach this conclusion.

Subsequently, he gave a disdainful smile : « Using Exploding Water Colliding Wave to create a height difference, neutralizing the opponent’s attack while simultaneously forming an offensive that the average Ninja cannot resist... »

« Not a bad trick, but to me, it’s like a child’s game. »

His Magnet Release possessed flight capabilities and extremely high mobility, ranking among the best in the Ninja World.

With this reliance, he did not believe Senju Matsuaki could get the better of him.

Mere Senju Matsuaki, get lost !

« Interesting ! »

On the other side, Kumogakure’s Dou’s thoughts were relatively simpler.

He himself was also skilled in a broad and powerful style of combat, focusing on strength and speed.

Therefore, seeing this scene of the violent giant waves sweeping away the opponent, he showed an expression of appreciation.

Of course, deep down, he did not look highly upon Senju Matsuaki’s Water Release.

This level was fine for cleaning up Genin and Chunin fodder, but for Ninjas of their Jonin level, it could only serve to change the terrain.
