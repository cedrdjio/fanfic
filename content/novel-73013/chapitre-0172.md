# Chapitre 172 — Splitting Into Two Paths! Capturing the Eight-Tails!

Half a month later, Senju Matsuaki and the others gathered once again in the Hokage Office.

Senju Hashirama sat in the main seat, a detailed map of the Ninja World spread out before him.

Senju Tobirama, Senju Matsuaki, Uchiha Hikari, and Hyuga Tsukuyomi stood before the table, awaiting the Hokage’s instructions.

« You have worked hard. »

Senju Hashirama looked at everyone with a smile, « From the One-Tails to the Five-Tails, plus the Nine-Tailed... six of the nine Tailed Beasts have already been successfully captured. »

« But we cannot let our guard down yet. »

Senju Tobirama stood to the side, arms crossed, his expression serious.

« According to ancient records and intelligence, the remaining three Tailed Beasts—the Six-Tails, the Seven-Tails, and the Eight-Tails—are each not to be underestimated. »

Senju Hashirama nodded and pointed to several locations on the map.

« So, let us begin preparations for the third round of Tailed Beast capture operations. First, I will go over the intelligence compiled recently. »

« First is the Six-Tails. »

His finger tapped a swampy area near the Land of Water, « According to intelligence, the Six-Tails is currently residing in the swamps and cave systems near the Hidden Mist Village. »

« This Tailed Beast excels at using highly corrosive bubbles and acid. Its true form resembles a giant slug, and it moves extremely fast. »

« The Hidden Mist Village... »

Senju Matsuaki was thoughtful, « Regarding the Land of Water, what is the attitude of the Hidden Mist ? »

Senju Hashirama shook his head, « Naturally, they want to succeed in capturing the Six-Tails before we do, but the Six-Tails is just as slippery as it looks, and they haven’t succeeded yet. »

« However, if we want to get close to the Six-Tails, we must pass through the Hidden Mist Village’s perimeter. »

Senju Tobirama added, « The Hidden Mist occupies favorable terrain on their own turf. »

« Furthermore, the Six-Tails' acid attack has a massive range. If we get drawn into a protracted battle, it will be very troublesome. »

Senju Matsuaki silently noted this information.

« Next is the Seven-Tails. »

Senju Hashirama’s finger moved to the Land of Waterfalls on the map, « The Seven-Tails is currently controlled by the Hidden Waterfall Village and is sealed deep within their Village’s altar. »

Senju Tobirama remarked, « I never expected that such a small Village as the Hidden Waterfall Village would be able to capture the Seven-Tails on their own. »

Hearing this, Senju Matsuaki also nodded slightly.

He vaguely remembered that in the original story, the Hidden Waterfall Village was the first Village to capture a Tailed Beast on its own.

The Seven-Tails was also the only Tailed Beast that was not distributed to a ninja Village by Senju Hashirama, but rather captured by the Hidden Waterfall Village themselves.

« Since the Seven-Tails has already been captured by the Hidden Waterfall Village, let us cross it off our list of targets. »

Senju Hashirama sighed, « However, I am worried that the Hidden Waterfall Village cannot control the Tailed Beast’s power very well. »

His gaze fell on Senju Tobirama, « Tobirama, send word to the Hidden Waterfall Village later. If they are willing to join the Tailed Beast Council, Konoha is willing to provide more complete Sealing Jutsu. »

« Very well. » Senju Tobirama nodded, accepting the task.

« Finally, there is the Eight-Tails, Gyuki. »

Senju Hashirama’s expression became even more serious.

« The Eight-Tails is currently active within the Land of Lightning, and according to intelligence, Kumogakure has already carried out one capture operation. »

« However, it ultimately escaped Kumogakure’s encirclement by severing its tail to survive. »

« How is the Hidden Cloud Village’s strength ? »

Uchiha Hikari asked.

Senju Tobirama took over, « The First Raikage A, as well as his guard, Hong, are both Kage-level powerhouses. »

« Additionally, there is Dou, who was defeated by Matsuaki back then. His strength is now above that of an Elite Jonin, so he should also be noted. »

« Other than that, there are no other subjects that require special attention. »

Senju Hashirama pondered for a moment after hearing Tobirama’s report, his gaze sweeping over the four people present.

« In that case, I will make the overall deployment. »

He stood up, hands pressed on the table, his eyes sharp.

« Regarding the Eight-Tails, I intend to... »

Before Senju Hashirama could finish, Senju Matsuaki stepped forward.

« Uncle, leave the Eight-Tails to the three of us. »

His voice was calm yet firm.

The office went quiet for a moment.

Senju Hashirama raised an eyebrow at him, « Matsuaki, are you confident ? »

« The Eight-Tails' strength is not comparable to the previous Tailed Beasts, and those people from the Hidden Cloud are not easy to deal with either. »

« Precisely because the Hidden Cloud is also coveting the Eight-Tails, we need to resolve the battle quickly. »

Senju Matsuaki’s gaze was steady, « Both my and Hikari’s abilities can effectively suppress the Tailed Beast, and Tsukuyomi’s Byakugan can constantly monitor the movements of the Hidden Cloud and the Eight-Tails. »

Uchiha Hikari stood to the side, the corners of her mouth slightly raised, « Since Matsuaki says so, I have no objections. »

Hyuga Tsukuyomi also nodded slightly, « My Byakugan can assist in reconnaissance to ensure the Eight-Tails does not escape. »

Senju Tobirama frowned and pondered for a moment before speaking, « Matsuaki, I trust your strength. »

« But the fact that the Eight-Tails can sever its tail to survive shows it possesses extremely high intelligence and escape methods. The three of you must formulate a thorough strategy and must not underestimate the enemy. »

« Additionally, I will leave a Flying Thunder God formula on you so that after my brother and I succeed here, we can immediately come to support you. »

Neither he nor Hashirama opposed Senju Matsuaki’s proposal to capture the Eight-Tails.

The reason was simple...

Senju Tobirama’s gaze fell upon the archaic katana at Senju Matsuaki’s waist.

Not long ago, he had been curious about where his eldest nephew had scrounged up such an antique-looking katana.

To demonstrate the power of this archaic katana, Senju Matsuaki had gently drawn it from its scabbard.

In an instant, a heart-palpitating sensation had risen in his heart.

Only then did he realize that his good nephew’s strength had likely surpassed his own without him even noticing !

« Understood. »

Senju Matsuaki replied solemnly.

Seeing this, Senju Hashirama continued, « The Six-Tails will be handled by Tobirama and me. The two of us working together is enough to deal with the Hidden Mist’s interference and the threat of the Six-Tails itself. »

Senju Tobirama had no objections and simply nodded.

Seeing this, Senju Hashirama did not hesitate any longer and made the final decision.

« Good, then it is settled. Tobirama and I will head to the Land of Water to capture the Six-Tails, while Matsuaki, Hikari, and Tsukuyomi will head to the Land of Lightning to capture the Eight-Tails. »

He paused, then added, « After all the Tailed Beasts have been captured, we will return to the Village to prepare for the matters concerning the Tailed Beast Council ! »

« Yes ! »

The four of them replied in unison.

Senju Hashirama flashed a hearty smile, « Then, without further ado, prepare yourselves, and we depart tomorrow morning ! »

After the meeting ended, Senju Matsuaki walked out of the Hokage Building and looked up at the sky.

In that direction lay the Hidden Cloud Village in the Land of Lightning.

...

Early morning, outside the gates of Konoha.

Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama set off first, speeding toward the Land of Water.

Senju Matsuaki, Uchiha Hikari, and Hyuga Tsukuyomi formed a three-person team and headed toward the Land of Lightning.

The wind blew through the treetops, stirring up a few fallen leaves.

As Senju Matsuaki traveled, he mentally simulated the upcoming battle.

The Eight-Tails, Gyuki, was the Tailed Beast with performance second only to the Nine-Tailed, Kurama, in the original story.

Additionally, the First Raikage, and the future Second and Third Raikage, were all obstacles in this mission.
