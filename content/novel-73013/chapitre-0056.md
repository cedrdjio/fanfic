# Chapitre 56 — Is That Lord Hashirama!?

Early morning, on a street near the Senju Clan compound.

A male member of the Senju Clan, wearing a hard hat, looked with a headache at a piece of wood lying on the ground beside him that had split down the middle.

« Oh no, oh no. If I go to buy more wood now, I probably won’t make it in time. »

Besides Ninjas, the Senju Clan also had ordinary members who did not work in the Ninja profession, but instead managed the clan’s businesses.

For this upcoming Joint Chunin Exams, all the Ninja clans in Konoha, big and small, were making preparations, and their respective businesses were all taking action.

This particular member of the Senju Clan had intended to replace a plaque that hadn’t been changed in years.

But unexpectedly, the wood he needed had slipped from his grasp just a moment ago, crashing heavily onto the steps at the entrance and snapping in two.

Once the wood was broken, its length and other dimensions no longer met his requirements, so it was naturally useless.

Thinking of this, the Senju Clan man sighed : « Sigh, now I have no choice but to go and buy more wood. I hope it’s not too late. »

Buying in bulk would have been fine, but buying a small amount like this was actually quite troublesome.

« Sigh, if only Lord Hashirama were here. »

Standing beside the Senju Clan man was his wife, who was also a member of the Senju Clan.

She recalled the early days of the village’s founding, when the Senju Clan was building their compound, and the deeds of Clan Leader Senju Hashirama using Wood Release to create trees for the convenience of his clansmen.

« Lord Hashirama is extremely busy ; he wouldn’t have time to deal with small matters like ours. » The Senju Clan man frowned.

« It’s fine. At worst, I’ll just go ask the neighbors if they have any spare wood. »

Just as the two were looking distressed, a surge of Chakra full of vitality suddenly descended.

Snap—

They saw a small crack suddenly appear on the previously empty ground, and a sapling poked its head out from within.

In the blink of an eye, the palm-sized tree grew into a sturdy tree taller than an adult male.

« Is that Lord Hashirama ! ? »

Seeing this miraculous scene, the Senju Clan couple both beamed with joy and turned their heads to look for Senju Hashirama.

However, apart from seeing the back of a seven or eight-year-old child walking away in the distance, they did not see the figure they had imagined.

« Ah, did Lord Hashirama leave so quickly ? »

The two exchanged glances, feeling a bit disappointed.

That was undoubtedly Wood Release. In their view, Senju Hashirama must have just been passing by, solved their urgent problem, and then hurried away.

In the distance, Senju Matsuaki slowly lowered the hands that had just been gathering Wood Release Chakra, a faint smile on his lips, hiding his merit and fame.

After practicing Wood Release for some time, his mastery was gradually improving.

Today was a special day ; it was the day the examinees and team leaders from various Ninja villages participating in this Joint Chunin Exams were arriving.

Because the Chunin Exams were approaching, Senju Matsuaki had arranged with his teammate, Hyuga Tsukuyomi, to go to the Konoha Ninja Tool Shop together to purchase new Ninja tools.

After walking out of the Senju Clan compound and continuing for about two or three minutes, he saw the smiling girl standing at the intersection waiting for him.

« Matsuaki~ »

After the tempering she had undergone since becoming a Genin, Hyuga Tsukuyomi was much more confident than she had been back at the Ninja Academy.

Previously at the Ninja Academy, she hadn’t had the opportunity to use her full strength, and over time, her perception of her own abilities had become skewed.

Every mission since officially becoming a Ninja had been a real combat experience, gradually correcting her skewed perception.

The two went to the Ninja tool shop together. Since it was still early and the shop had just opened, there were no other customers.

« Welcome ! »

Seeing two customers dressed as Ninjas arrive, the shop owner immediately came up with a smile, not looking down on them because of their age.

The Ninja Tool Shop Owner was a middle-aged man whose attire was different from the local Konoha residents ; if one had to describe it, it was somewhat similar to the attire of ancient people from the Celestial Empire in his previous life.

« Good morning, you two. I wonder if the Kunai you bought last time worked well ? » the Ninja Tool Shop Owner said with a smile.

« You remember us ? »

Hearing this, Senju Matsuaki was slightly surprised.

Last month, his Kunai had been damaged while on a mission, so he had come to this Ninja tool shop to restock during his free time after the mission.

It was understandable for a customer to remember a shop owner, but he hadn’t expected this Ninja Tool Shop Owner to remember them, despite having only met once.

« Of course I remember. The two of you came to my shop last time and bought two Kunai, ten shuriken, and ten senbon. »

The Ninja Tool Shop Owner said, counting on his fingers with a smile, unhurriedly.

Seeing that the two seemed surprised by his memory, the owner waved his hand with a smile.

« You two became Ninjas at such a young age, so my impression of you is a bit deeper. »

After saying this, the smile on the Ninja Tool Shop Owner’s face disappeared, and he sighed softly.

« Back in the day, I also had a dream of becoming a Ninja, so when I heard that Konoha had been founded, I rushed over from afar. »

« It’s just a pity that I didn’t have any Ninja talent, and I was already in my twenties at the time, with no hope of achieving anything in this path. So, I chose to open a Ninja tool shop, which could be considered tangentially related to my former dream. »

Perhaps because there were no other customers and it was still early, the owner seemed to have opened up.

Before the two could offer any words of comfort, they saw the look of disappointment on the Ninja Tool Shop Owner’s face vanish.

Looking at Senju Matsuaki and Hyuga Tsukuyomi, flames seemed to ignite in the owner’s eyes, filled with burning fighting spirit.

« But I want Heaven to know that I do not admit defeat ! »

« If my next generation or the generation after has descendants with outstanding Ninja talent, I will definitely support them with all my might to become an excellent Ninja ! »

« Sigh, but ordinary people like me don’t have any Ninjutsu or secret techniques to pass on to our descendants. We are born behind at the starting line... »

Speaking of this, a look of frustration appeared on the Ninja Tool Shop Owner’s face again.

This emotional roller coaster in just a few short minutes left Senju Matsuaki and Hyuga Tsukuyomi, who were standing opposite him, not knowing how to offer comfort.

However, glancing at the dazzling array of Ninja tools in the shop, Matsuaki’s eyes suddenly lit up.

« Boss, who says you have nothing to pass on to your descendants ? Isn’t this whole shop full of Ninja tools exactly that ? »

« Valued customer, what do you mean ? » The Ninja Tool Shop Owner’s eyes showed a glimmer of hope.

« Although Ninja tools are only auxiliary equipment for a Ninja, if one can master their use, they can certainly become powerful weapons of mass destruction. »

As he spoke, Senju Matsuaki gestured for the owner to look behind him.

« Common ones, for example, are Explosive Tags, which can be even more terrifying in power than some Ninjutsu. And take that spiked iron ball hanging over there ; even a Jonin would be left with a few bloody holes if struck by it. »

« Please go on, valued customer ! Today, your purchase is on the house ! »

As Matsuaki explained, the Ninja Tool Shop Owner’s eyes gradually brightened, his rough palms rubbing back and forth unconsciously in front of him.
