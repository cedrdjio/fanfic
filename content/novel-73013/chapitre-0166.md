# Chapitre 166 — Capturing Ichibi!

The next day, at the gates of Konoha.

Senju Hashirama had a massive sealing scroll strapped to his back and was clad in red layered armor.

Beside him, Senju Matsuaki had also changed into his Ninja attire for the mission, a katana tied at his waist, his eyes as calm as still water.

« Matsuaki, this time it’s up to the two of us. »

Senju Hashirama clapped him on the shoulder, laughing heartily.

Senju Matsuaki nodded slightly and said softly, « Don’t worry, Uncle. Ichibi won’t be able to escape. »

The two of them flashed away, quickly disappearing along the forest path outside the gates of Konoha.

...

The border between the Land of Rivers and the Land of Wind was an endless sea of yellow sand.

The scorching sun hung high, heat waves distorting the air ; everywhere one looked, there were only rolling sand dunes.

« The intelligence says that Ichibi was last seen near an oasis thirty miles ahead. »

Senju Hashirama squinted into the distance, clutching a map in his hands.

The map marked the general activity range of Ichibi.

After all, Ichibi was a living creature ; while its range of movement was relatively fixed, it couldn’t just stay huddled in one spot forever.

Konoha’s intelligence department had analyzed several sighting locations to deduce this general area.

Senju Matsuaki and Senju Hashirama walked forward for another distance before stopping in unison.

« You sensed it too, right, Matsuaki ? »

Senju Hashirama laughed.

Hearing this, Senju Matsuaki nodded slightly and said, « There’s movement underground, very deep. »

For those at their level of strength, even minor anomalies were hard to escape their perception.

Especially the movement beneath the desert, which was definitely not some natural phenomenon.

Combined with the recent sighting locations of Ichibi, it was obvious who was responsible for this disturbance.

« It seems it’s hiding underground to rest. »

Senju Hashirama grinned, « In that case, let’s invite it out. »

He formed hand Seals and slammed his hands onto the ground : « Wood Release : Deep Forest Emergence ! »

Rumble—

The sandy ground shook violently as countless thick trees burst through the sand, their roots extending madly downward, turning the sandy layers for hundreds of meters upside down.

« Uncle, that’s really reckless. »

Senju Matsuaki’s mouth twitched, but his eyes narrowed.

The next second.

An angry roar came from underground.

Following that, the yellow sand exploded, and a massive creature shot into the sky.

What greeted their eyes was a gigantic raccoon dog.

Its entire body was covered in Violet-colored cursed Seal patterns. The massive raccoon dog body flipped in the air before crashing heavily onto the sand, kicking up clouds of dust.

A terrifying Aura instantly swept across the desert !

« As expected, Tailed Beasts are very dangerous powers. »

Senju Hashirama sighed, his eyes glinting with determination.

Seeing this, Senju Matsuaki’s eyes flickered slightly.

People always said that Senju Hashirama would talk about how dangerous Tailed Beast power was while effortlessly suppressing them with the back of his hand.

But in reality, Senju Hashirama said this entirely from the perspective of an ordinary Ninja.

For example, after Senju Hashirama passed away in the original timeline, the Nine-Tailed incident turned Konoha upside down, even necessitating the sacrifice of a Hokage.

And other Ninja villages had also paid many tragic prices to control their Tailed Beasts.

Senju Hashirama captured and sealed Tailed Beasts for the sake of future stability.

« Humans ! How dare you disturb my sleep ! »

Shukaku roared, its tail sweeping across to whip up a massive wave of sand that surged toward the two of them.

Senju Hashirama was about to act, but Senju Matsuaki had already stepped forward.

He clenched his right fist, and a faint white halo appeared on his arm ; it was the power of the Tremor-Tremor Fruit condensing.

« Air Quake ! ! »

He threw a punch.

The air seemed to shatter ; visible cracks appeared in mid-air. The wave of sand collided with an invisible wall and instantly disintegrated into clouds of fine sand.

Shukaku’s pupils constricted : « What ? ! »

It had lived for thousands of years, and this was the first time it had seen a human shatter space with a single punch.

In Shukaku’s heart, its anger gradually subsided, and it vaguely felt that something was wrong.

« It’s not over yet. »

The moment Senju Matsuaki landed, he formed hand Seals.

« Wood Release : Briar Killing Technique ! »

Dozens of thorny vines suddenly burst from beneath the sand, coiling around Shukaku’s body like venomous snakes, binding its limbs and tail tightly.

« Wait, what is this ? ! »

Seeing this Bloodline Limit it hadn’t seen in a long time, Shukaku was stunned, and then fear welled up in its eyes.

Shukaku roared in struggle, attempting to break free, but the vines tightened further, the thorns digging deep into its body and causing it to let out painful shrieks.

« Damn humans... don’t underestimate me ! »

Shukaku opened its mouth, and a black Tailed Beast Ball rapidly condensed. The terrifying Chakra fluctuations caused the surrounding air to tremble.

Senju Hashirama’s expression changed slightly ; as the elder, he immediately stepped forward, wanting to take over the fight.

« Don’t worry, Uncle ! »

Senju Matsuaki did not retreat.

He took a deep breath and clenched his right fist again.

This time, the white halo of the Tremor-Tremor Fruit was even more intense, and terrifying cracks even faintly appeared in the space around his fist.

« This punch will make you settle down. »

He leaped up, his fist carrying the power to tear through space, and smashed it hard against the Tailed Beast Ball that Shukaku had only half-condensed.

« Air Quake ! ! »

Crack—

The sound of space shattering rang out across the sky.

Cracks spread, and the terrifying Vibrational Power collided head-on with the purple-black Tailed Beast Ball.

The next instant, the Tailed Beast Ball was shattered, and a violent explosion erupted right in front of Shukaku !

Shukaku let out a shrill scream, its massive body toppling backward.

The moment Shukaku fell, several figures appeared on the sand dunes in the distance.

They wore the same forehead protectors, and a few of them wore masks to shield themselves from the wind and sand ; they were Ninjas from the Hidden Sand Village.

Just as Konoha was searching for Ichibi, so too was the Hidden Sand Village.

At this moment, they had clearly detected the disturbance caused by Ichibi and were rushing over at full speed.

This scene was clearly noticed by Senju Hashirama as well.

Senju Hashirama glanced at the black dots getting closer in the distance, then looked at the still-struggling Shukaku, and said loudly.

« Matsuaki, you’ve knocked down Ichibi ; leave the sealing to me ! »

« Understood. »

After landing, Senju Matsuaki quickly formed hand Seals, and the Chakra within him erupted surgeingly.

« Wood Release : Wood Dragon Technique ! »

A majestic wood dragon burst from the ground, tightly coiling around the body of Ichibi, Shukaku.

At the same time, the wood dragon rapidly absorbed Ichibi’s Chakra, causing it to feel a sense of powerlessness.

At this moment, Senju Hashirama also leaped up, the markings of Sage Mode appearing on his face, and then he formed hand Seals.

« Sage Art : Myojinmon ! ! »

Boom !

A massive red torii gate descended from the sky, precisely pressing down on Shukaku’s tail and pinning it firmly to the ground.

Following that, the second and third Myojinmon descended in succession, suppressing Shukaku’s head, torso, and forelimbs respectively.

Shukaku was completely unable to move, able only to let out unwilling low growls.
