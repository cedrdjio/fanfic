# Chapitre 127 — Kage-Level Destructive Power! The Entire Arena Stunned!

« Hey, this isn’t a joke, right ? »

First Mizukage Byakuren could be considered the oldest among the First Kage.

Also, due to injuries sustained during his early years of warfare, he was blind in his left eye, having completely lost vision in it.

As he watched the scene on the field, he couldn’t help but wonder if his remaining, functional right eye had also grown dim with age.

« Mizukage, you didn’t see wrong, » First Tsuchikage Ishikawa said.

« Although it’s unbelievable, the slash that kid unleashed has already surpassed the level of a Jonin... »

Ishikawa’s tone was quite grave, and at the same time, his gaze swept over Senju Hashirama without leaving a trace.

Are these guys from the Senju Clan all monsters ?

« Surpassing the level of a Jonin, indeed... »

The First Raikage felt as if a gloom had settled over his heart, his expression uncertain.

Surpassing a Jonin undoubtedly meant reaching the level of a Kage, where they themselves stood.

Although a full-power strike possessing Kage-level destructive power and possessing Kage-level strength oneself were two different things.

But this was still an incredible feat.

One had to know, the brat from the Senju Clan down below was likely only around ten years old, and in Konoha, his rank was that of a Genin.

Originally, he had thought that among the next generation, their Kumogakure’s Dou was the strongest.

He would surely lead Kumogakure to its Peak in the future.

But looking at it now, this conclusion would likely have to be questioned.

Konoha, the Senju Clan, truly blessed by heaven...

The First Raikage had previously thought that with Uchiha Madara’s departure, Konoha’s strength would be greatly diminished.

Yet, he hadn’t expected Senju Tobirama’s strength to be in no way inferior to some of the First Kage.

And Konoha, which he had expected to be in turmoil due to the departure of a founding elder, was still developing steadily.

And now, this new generation of the Senju Clan, only around ten years old, was also beginning to show his edge.

This strike that shattered space had also shattered their worldview.

Currently, it only caused Kage-level destructive power, but in the future, entering the level of a Kage was merely a matter of time.

Three Kage-level powerhouses in one clan, and even when the youngest grew up, the previous two would still be in their prime ; it was simply unheard of.

« Uh, this... why is everyone looking at me ? »

Noticing the gazes of the three First Kage around him, Senju Hashirama scratched his head in embarrassment.

He was also equally surprised by the strength Senju Matsuaki had displayed.

Right now, he could also see that the power capable of shattering space was definitely not the monstrous strength inherited by the Senju Clan.

As for what it was, he was also a bit curious and planned to ask about it later when he remembered.

However, this peculiar power was highly likely a Bloodline Limit-like ability and was not replicable.

He clearly had the foresight for that.

At this moment, watching the chaotic situation on the field, Senju Hashirama did not feel worried.

Just as First Kazekage Retto and Shamon entered the field one after another, Senju Tobirama on the viewing platform also entered.

The purpose was naturally to prevent Sunagakure from pulling any tricks.

As time passed, the commotion on the field gradually subsided.

The chaotic water tornadoes dissipated into droplets, pouring down.

The raging waves also gradually calmed down.

The audience seats fell into silence for a moment, and everyone opened their eyes as wide as possible, wanting to see the situation on the field as soon as possible.

The four Kage who had been conversing also fell into a tacit silence, waiting for the final result.

...

As the turmoil settled, the scene at the center of the exam field was exposed to people’s sight.

The shocking scene instantly made the pupils of the Ninjas on the viewing platform constrict.

Only to see Senju Matsuaki standing on the water with his blade in hand.

Opposite him, a Puppet as sturdy as an ox spread its arms, forming a huge shield with its metal structure to block in front of it.

The metal shield, refined from the hard minerals of the Land of Wind, now had only the core piece left ; the rest had shattered inch by inch and peeled away.

Behind it was Shamon, who had summoned the Puppet at the last moment to block in front of the First Kazekage.

He was seen half-kneeling on the water in a rather disheveled state, his arms stretched forward, with Chakra threads wrapped around his fingertips, connecting to the Puppet.

What made the onlookers gape in astonishment was that the clothing on Shamon’s arms had been completely shattered.

The exposed arms were a bloody mess, looking quite horrifying.

Behind him was First Kazekage Retto, who had faced the air tremor head-on from the start.

His expression tense, he stood in place, looking as if he had at least maintained the dignity of a Kage on the surface.

In the air to the side, the Kazekage hat that had been blown away at some point slowly spiraled down and sank into the water...

Further back was Yan, who had protected himself by forming a tortoise shell with Iron Sand.

After the turmoil subsided, he immediately dispelled the protective Iron Sand.

A streak of blood flowed down from the corner of his mouth.

In those originally arrogant and cruel eyes, the former luster was gone, replaced entirely by the terror and shock of having survived a disaster.

When he met Senju Matsuaki’s sharp red eyes, his body even involuntarily took half a step back.

With two Kage-level Ninjas blocking in front, the aftermath still left him injured.

His mentality had been completely shattered !

« First Kazekage, and the future Second Kazekage, Wind Release and Puppet Technique... »

Senju Matsuaki quickly saw the identity of the newcomers and had some guesses about the previous situation.

The First Kazekage and Second Kazekage only made brief appearances in the original work, belonging to the supporting characters with the least screen time.

Of course, in a certain game, the First Kazekage’s skill set consisted entirely of Wind Release Ninjutsu, showing that he established his dominance in the Land of Wind based on his attainments in Wind Release.

And the future Second Kazekage, Shamon, became famous as the founder of the Puppet Technique and Sand Manipulation Technique.

At the very last moment just now, it was he who controlled the Puppet to block in front of the First Kazekage and Yan.

This result couldn’t be said to be bad, but it was indeed somewhat unsatisfactory.

« It seems the power of the Tremor-Tremor Fruit still needs further development, » Senju Matsuaki thought to himself.

Only shattering a shield raised by a Puppet, cast from an unknown metal, was a result that left him less than satisfied.

However, the scene on the field left almost all the Ninjas on the viewing platform with blank minds.

One had to understand who was on the field.

That person half-kneeling on the ground with bloody, mangled arms was Shamon of Sunagakure, the almost certain future Second Kazekage !

In the current Ninja World, he was already a renowned Ninja.

And if Shamon was the second most powerful Ninja in the Land of Wind, then Retto, as the First Kazekage, was undoubtedly the first.

Yet at this moment, although he appeared unharmed, the Kazekage hat that had been blown away was like a slap to his face.

And at this moment, the two Ninjas who stood at the pinnacle of the Land of Wind were facing a young Ninja who was merely in a Chunin Exams, and who had been a Genin not long ago.

Was the world crazy, or were they the ones who were crazy ?
