# Chapitre 181 — Two Years! Tobirama Succeeds! Undercurrents Surge!

The news of the Tailed Beast Council’s formation was like a stone thrown into a lake, creating ripples throughout the Ninja World.

Less than a month after the Five Kage Summit ended, the major Ninja Villages began to receive the Tailed Beasts one after another in accordance with the agreement.

Under the personal leadership of Senju Tobirama, the Konoha Sealing Team traveled to each Village to teach them how to create monitoring techniques.

Everything was progressing in an orderly manner.

The draft of the « Tailed Beast Restriction Treaty » was negotiated through several rounds of correspondence and was finally officially signed at the first Council meeting.

The Kage of the Five Great Nations, along with the observer representative from Takigakure, solemnly signed their names on the treaty.

That day, the sky over the Land of Iron was exceptionally clear.

Senju Hashirama stood in the center of the Council hall in the Land of Iron, watching the Five Kage sign one by one, a heartfelt smile on his face.

The Ninja World had ushered in an unprecedented period of peace.

Small-scale conflicts still existed, and the grudges between Ninjas would not simply disappear into thin air.

But at least, the major Ninja Villages refrained from breaking out into large-scale wars, nor did they use the power of the Tailed Beasts to cause chaos.

Two years flowed by peacefully just like that.

During these two years, many changes occurred in the Ninja World.

The First Mizukage Byakuren of the Hidden Mist Village passed away suddenly due to his advanced age and the recurrence of old injuries.

Before he died, he passed his position to Hōzuki Gengetsu.

Due to a back injury, Ishikawa of Iwagakure also handed over the position of Tsuchikage to Mu a year later, retiring behind the scenes to spend his later years.

Over in Sunagakure, the First Kazekage Retto also gradually began to delegate power to Shamon ; although he had not yet abdicated, anyone with a discerning eye could see he already had the intention.

Only the First Raikage of Kumogakure still held onto power firmly, seemingly with no intention of abdicating.

And here in Konoha...

Senju Hashirama sat in the Hokage Office, looking through the window at the bustling streets of Konoha, a hint of relief in his eyes.

« Tobirama, I want to retire. »

Senju Tobirama, who was reviewing documents, looked up in astonishment : « Big Brother ? »

« Hey, hey, what’s with that expression ? »

Senju Hashirama shook his head with a wry smile, « I just feel that the Ninja World now no longer needs me to do anything. »

« Konoha is thriving, and the younger generation has grown up. Although there is friction between Villages, peace is generally maintained. »

« As Hokage, it seems I don’t have anything particularly important to do anymore. »

Senju Tobirama put down his pen and looked at his older brother seriously : « Big Brother, you have done very well. »

« Without you, the Village would not have developed to this point, and the Ninja World would not have this hard-won peace. »

Senju Hashirama scratched his head and smiled somewhat embarrassedly : « Haha, hearing you praise me like that actually makes me a bit uncomfortable. »

« But seriously, Tobirama, I think it’s time for you to take over for me. »

« You are more suitable to be this Hokage than I am. »

Senju Tobirama was silent for a moment, not declining.

Because he knew that what his big brother said was from the heart.

Senju Hashirama had extraordinary charisma and powerful strength, but he was not good at handling cumbersome administrative affairs.

Since the founding of Konoha, much of the administrative work had actually been handled by Senju Tobirama.

Furthermore, he also knew that as time went on and the situation in Konoha stabilized, Senju Hashirama gradually put more thought into his Family.

Unlike him, who was alone, Big Brother Hashirama had a wife and children, and his granddaughter, Tsunade, was already four years old.

In the past, Hashirama was busy with the clan and the Village, and did not have much time to accompany his Family.

On the other hand, Senju Tobirama himself had burning ambitions in his heart.

Although he was the second-in-command in Konoha in the past, it was inevitable that he couldn’t fully display his talents.

« Big Brother, if you have already decided, I have no problem with it. »

Senju Tobirama said calmly.

« Then it’s settled ! »

Senju Hashirama slapped his thigh, « I will announce this news tomorrow, and then start having your Hokage haori made. »

...

Half a month later.

Countless Konoha villagers and Ninjas gathered in the square in front of the Hokage Building.

Senju Hashirama stood on a high platform, wearing a kimono, with a bright smile on his face.

« Everyone ! I have gathered you all here today to announce an important matter ! »

The noise in the square gradually subsided, and everyone’s eyes were focused on Senju Hashirama.

« I have walked all the way from the Sengoku period, experienced countless battles, and witnessed the changes in the Ninja World. »

« Now, peace has finally arrived, and Konoha’s development is on the right track. »

« I feel that it is time for me to step down. »

As soon as these words were spoken, the square was in an uproar.

« Hokage-sama ! How can you abdicate ! »

« That’s right ! Konoha needs you ! »

« Please continue to serve as Hokage ! »

Senju Hashirama raised his hand, signaling for everyone to be quiet.

« I appreciate everyone’s good intentions. However, what Konoha needs is not a Hokage who sits in the position forever, but a Hokage who can lead Konoha into the future. »

« I believe that Senju Tobirama has the complete ability to do this. »

He turned his head to look at Senju Tobirama, who was standing to the side, his eyes full of trust.

« Tobirama is calmer than me, more rational than me, and knows better than me how to handle administrative affairs. »

« He will be a better Hokage than I am. »

Senju Tobirama stepped forward, facing the crowd in the square, his expression solemn.

« I will not let Big Brother down, nor will I let down your trust. »

« I will protect Konoha, and protect this hard-won peace. »

The square was silent for a moment, then erupted into thunderous applause and cheers.

In the ten-plus years since Konoha was established, Senju Tobirama had been conscientious and diligent, with constant military achievements, and already had a grassroots foundation.

« Second Hokage ! Second Hokage ! »

The shouts resounded throughout Konoha.

Down below, Senju Matsuaki, Uchiha Hikari, and Hyuga Tsukuyomi smiled at each other.

On the other side, Hiruzen Sarutobi and the others watched their teacher become the Second Hokage, and also cheered loudly.

The news of Senju Tobirama officially taking over the position of Hokage soon spread throughout the Ninja World.

The Kage of the major Ninja Villages sent letters one after another to express their congratulations.

In the Peace Pavilion in the Land of Iron, the Council Secretariat also sent a congratulatory letter.

Everything seemed so harmonious.

However, beneath this facade of peace, undercurrents had already begun to surge.

...

Kumogakure, Raikage Building.

The First Raikage looked at the intelligence in his hand, his eyes narrowed slightly.

« Senju Hashirama retires to recuperate, hardly leaving his front door... Senju Tobirama succeeds... »

He crumpled the intelligence into a ball and threw it into the brazier.

« Hmph, that guy Senju Tobirama has a much deeper mind than his big brother. »

« But that’s fine... at least in terms of strength, Senju Tobirama is not a monster like the First Hokage. »

« However... »

The First Raikage’s gaze swept over his guards, Hong and Dou, as well as several Elders of Kumogakure, a sharp glint flashing in his eyes.

« You all say, is there a possibility... that Senju Hashirama is also not doing well ? »

As soon as these words were spoken, everyone present constricted their pupils.

The First Raikage continued : « The First Mizukage passed away last year, and the First Tsuchikage’s old injuries recurred ; it looks like he won’t live for many more years either. »

« And Senju Hashirama, although he is about the same age as me, Uchiha Madara came to him for a great battle many years ago. »

« Although Uchiha Madara was defeated and retreated in the end, those two were evenly matched in the past ; how could Senju Hashirama be completely fine ? »

As his voice fell, the First Raikage felt that his speculation was very reasonable, and his gaze became increasingly fervent : « He abdicated to let a worthy successor take over ; I’m afraid it’s just like Ishikawa, his old injuries recurred, and he had no choice but to retire ! »

« There is such a possibility... »

A sharp glint also flashed in Hong’s eyes, « However, we cannot act rashly for the time being ; we still need to test him. »
