# Chapitre 116 — The Cloud Village's Nap Habit

The midday break ticked away, second by second.

Finding it too much of a hassle to return to Konoha, Senju Matsuaki and Hyuga Tsukuyomi stayed in the waiting room to rest.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi held a thin pancake in her hands, head bowed, eating it in small bites.

Senju Matsuaki, having already finished his lunch, leaned back in a wooden chair, his focus concentrated on his fingertips.

A white halo of light began to expand from Senju Matsuaki’s fingertips, condensing and compressing into a spherical shield.

This was one way to use the power of the Tremor-Tremor Fruit : concentrating and compressing the power of vibration into a spherical shield, which could be attached to weapons or fists to unleash an attack capable of shattering anything.

With nothing to do during the break, Senju Matsuaki began familiarizing himself with the power of the Tremor-Tremor Fruit.

For Senju Matsuaki, the power of a Devil Fruit was a strange new Domain ; it required constant practice and exploration to transform the potential of the Tremor-Tremor Fruit into his own strength.

Fortunately, his talent as a user seemed decent, and his control over the power of vibration was becoming increasingly handy.

Just as he was quietly familiarizing himself with the power of the Tremor-Tremor Fruit, a burst of conversation from afar caught him off guard, and the spherical shield at his fingertips nearly shattered.

« Boss, why do I feel so sleepy ? My head is all groggy. »

A Kumogakure examinee, still chewing on some « Sarutobi & Shimura » brand Oden, suddenly felt something was wrong as a wave of drowsiness inexplicably flooded his brain.

« Sleepy ? »

Dou frowned ; hearing the other person say this, he also seemed to feel a bit drowsy.

But soon, he found a reasonable explanation for this situation.

« Don’t make such a fuss. It’s normal to feel sleepy after lunch. »

As he spoke, Dou pointed to Yan in the distance, who had fallen asleep on a wooden chair like a dead log at some point.

Yan had purchased his meal earlier, so the effects of the drug appeared faster.

Being a loner, he had no companions to chat with to distract himself from the drowsiness, so he fell asleep the moment the drug took effect.

« Look at that Sunagakure guy ; he ate lunch a bit earlier than us, and now he’s already taking a nap ! »

After Dou finished speaking, he waited for agreement from his juniors from the same Village, but there was only silence for a long while.

He turned his head in confusion, only to discover that the three or four Kumogakure juniors who had shared the meal with him had already settled into positions and fallen asleep on the wooden chairs.

« How are you all… »

Seeing this, Dou also felt something was wrong, but it was already too late.

A wave of drowsiness surged up, and he only felt his vision go black before collapsing onto a companion and falling into a deep sleep.

This situation was naturally noticed by the other examinees present.

When they saw several burly, dark-skinned men sleeping in a heap together, the examinees from other Villages all wore strange expressions and averted their gazes with disgust.

« It seems the people from Kumogakure have a habit of taking afternoon naps, » Hyuga Tsukuyomi remarked softly.

There was no shortage of examinees resting their eyes during the midday break in the waiting room, but only the Kumogakure examinees were sleeping so neatly in a row.

« Maybe, hahaha… »

Senju Matsuaki gave a dry laugh, not intending to reveal the truth, lest he tarnish Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s pure soul.

Time ticked away, the temperature rose, and afternoon arrived.

The empty spectator stands were gradually filled by arriving audience members, and the five Daimyo and five Kage slowly arrived at their respective seats and sat down.

« Hokage-sama ! »

A Ninja with a grave expression hurried over to Senju Hashirama, leaned down to whisper a few words, and then stood straight, waiting for orders.

Upon hearing what he said, Senju Hashirama’s brows furrowed deeply for a moment before relaxing.

« In that case, you go down and rest first. We’ll discuss this matter after the Chunin Exams. »

« Yes ! »

The Ninja acknowledged the order and turned to leave, while Senju Hashirama, wearing a perplexed expression, rubbed his chin and fell into deep thought.

Last night, there were clear signs of Ninja combat in the forest near Konoha, but when Konoha’s Ninjas arrived at the scene, they only found a mess.

Upon learning of this, Senju Hashirama hurriedly sent people to investigate and eventually discovered that an Uchiha Clan Ninja responsible for security at the Arena had gone missing.

The Uchiha Clan’s Sharingan was coveted by others ; perhaps someone from an outside Village had killed him to steal his eyes.

Considering this, Senju Hashirama hurriedly sent someone to inquire with the Uchiha Clan.

The reply he received was that the missing Ninja had simply had an old injury flare up, and in his haste, he hadn’t had time to ask for leave and had gone straight home to recover.

Intuition told Senju Hashirama that the Uchiha family’s attitude was a bit abnormal, and there must be something fishy going on.

However, after the Uchiha Clan gave such an explanation, even as the Hokage, it was difficult for him to pursue the matter further.

Furthermore, he was truly busy lately and had no time to handle this matter personally.

Since the Uchiha Clan said their clansman hadn’t gone missing, that could be considered a good thing ; any subsequent issues could be discussed later.

« Don’t zone out, Hashirama. It’s your turn to draw lots. »

The urging voice of the Daimyo of the Land of Fire brought Senju Hashirama, who had been lost in thought, back to reality.

He smiled sheepishly, reached out, and pulled a paper ball from the box in front of him.

Senju Hashirama unfolded the paper ball and read the name written on it : « The one I drew is from Kumogakure… »

« Pfft, hahaha, you drew someone from Kumogakure too ! »

As soon as the words fell, the Daimyo of the Land of Fire beside him burst out laughing.

The Daimyo of the Land of Fire also unfolded the slip of paper in his hand and showed it to the other Daimyo and Kage ; it also bore the name of a Kumogakure examinee.

This situation naturally brought joy to some and sorrow to others.

« Senju Hashirama, you… »

The First Raikage opened his mouth, air snorting from his nostrils, nearly cursing out loud.

But his good upbringing led him to silently swallow his words, and he turned his head away with a dark face to watch the battle in the Arena.

Counting the internal battle between Dou and another Kumogakure Ninja during the first selection round, this was already their second internal battle for Kumogakure.

Although random drawing of lots couldn’t avoid this situation, and there was no shortage of examinees from the same Village fighting each other in other Villages, Kumogakure was the first to encounter this twice by the third round.

Although battles between examinees from the same Village would not affect the Proctors' evaluation of the examinees themselves, it would reduce the number of Kumogakure examinees advancing to subsequent rounds.

The negative impact was that Kumogakure lost face.

But with the Daimyo, nobles, and wealthy merchants all in the spectator stands, this negative impact would be constantly amplified.

As usual, Senju Tobirama read out the names of the two parties determined by the draw, his voice resounding over the Arena.

However, what puzzled the entire audience was that after waiting for a long time, no one came out of the waiting room for the exam.

« What is going on… »

Senju Tobirama frowned and gave a look to a Proctor nearby.

The other person nodded slightly and disappeared from the spot using a Body Flicker Technique.

Not long after, the Proctor hurried into the waiting room, instantly attracting everyone’s attention.
