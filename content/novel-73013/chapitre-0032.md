# Chapitre 32 — The Arrival of the Hidden Cloud Village Delegation!

« Matsumi, good morning~ »

Having received a proper upbringing in etiquette from a young age, Hyuga Tsukuyomi had been waiting for Matsumi at the Konoha training ground for ten minutes, and she smiled and greeted him upon seeing his arrival.

« Sorry to keep you waiting. »

After returning the greeting with a smile, Matsumi looked at Tsukuyomi’s bandaged right hand, his face showing concern.

« Is the injury you sustained during Ninjutsu practice last time feeling any better ? »

« Mm, it’s already fine. »

Tsukuyomi smiled as she raised her right hand and clenched her small fist.

Practicing Ninjutsu carries certain risks ; for instance, accidentally burning oneself when using Fire Release, or being injured by stray lightning when using Lightning Release.

A few days ago, while learning a new Lightning Release technique, her right hand had been accidentally paralyzed by stray lightning.

Fortunately, a Wood Clone of Senju Hashirama was nearby guiding her at the time, and after stopping it in time, he performed basic medical treatment.

After chatting for a few moments, the two assumed a combat stance.

« Byakugan, activate ! Huh ? ! »

For the Hyuga Clan, the first step in a battle is, as per custom, to activate the Byakugan.

However, just as the girl’s Byakugan tried to lock onto the opponent’s acupoints and Chakra pathways as it usually did during sparring, what she saw caused her heart to skip a beat.

Under the vision of the Byakugan, Matsumi’s bones and Chakra pathways were clearly visible ; starting from his dantian, Chakra flowed throughout his entire body.

What surprised her was that the amount of Chakra, which she already felt was massive when she last saw him, could now only be described as enormous.

« Matsumi, did you accidentally eat something just now ? »

Seeing this, Hyuga Tsukuyomi had no heart for battle ; instead, she stepped forward a few paces, her worried gaze lingering on Matsumi as she tried to use her Byakugan to find anything amiss.

In her impression, the only things that could cause such a sudden surge in Chakra levels were the secret medicines passed down within certain Ninja clans.

For example, the Akimichi Clan’s three-colored pills ; from green to yellow to red, the amplifying effects of the three secret medicines increase sequentially, as do their side effects.

These types of secret medicines can significantly boost Chakra levels, strength, and other attributes for a short time, but the side effects are generally extremely severe, potentially even leading to serious injury or death.

« Uh, eat something ? »

Looking at the girl’s worried expression, Matsumi was initially somewhat bewildered.

But upon immediately realizing the situation, he also understood the reason for Tsukuyomi’s anxiety.

Yet, with the words on the tip of his tongue, he did not know how to explain it.

He could not just say, « I acquired a check-in system yesterday, and this morning I checked in with Uncle Hashirama, which is why my Chakra levels have skyrocketed. »

« My Chakra levels... perhaps it’s because my body has been developing quite well recently, so the increase is more significant. »

Unable to state the real reason directly, Matsumi chose to tell a bald-faced lie.

« Eh, development or something... »

Tsukuyomi was about to say that her Byakugan had previously seen some Chunin and Jonin who had no room for further development, and they did not seem to have such massive Chakra levels.

However, before she could finish speaking, she noticed a figure approaching them rapidly from a distance.

« Brother Hiruzen ? »

The person who arrived was none other than Hiruzen Sarutobi, whom he had not seen in a long time. He was wearing a form-fitting Ninja combat suit, clearly in the middle of a mission.

Although he had been traveling, Hiruzen Sarutobi did not appear out of breath. He first greeted the two, then his expression turned serious.

« Hokage-sama asked me to come and inform you that the Hidden Cloud Village delegation will arrive in Konoha later this afternoon. »

« So soon ? » Matsumi was slightly taken aback.

He remembered that when he accepted this mission yesterday, Uncle Hashirama had said the Hidden Cloud Village delegation would arrive in Konoha in the next few days.

He had not expected them to arrive before even a full day had passed.

It was still morning, but it was only five or six hours until the afternoon.

« I understand. We will wait at the Village entrance later and be ready to start the mission at any time. » Matsumi and Tsukuyomi exchanged a glance and said seriously.

« Then I will return to report. This time, Kagami and the others and I are also responsible for the Village’s security work ; if there are any unexpected situations, we will come to help. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi clearly knew the contents of Matsumi and Tsukuyomi’s mission ; after all, the Village’s security work was a collective effort, and cooperation was essential.

After waving goodbye to the two, he left the area.

...

In the afternoon.

The Hidden Cloud Village delegation stepped into this Village, which had a style completely different from their own.

Even Ninjas have a sense of curiosity.

Especially living in Kumogakure, where military might is supreme and entertainment is monotonous, these Kumogakure Ninjas had not really seen the landscape of other Villages on a daily basis.

At the very front of the procession was a sturdy-looking old man. Although his hair and beard were white, his build was quite robust, and he did not give the impression of being aged.

Behind him stood an adult male with a long beard and his hair tied in a bun ; there was a white mark on the bridge of his nose, and it was unclear if it was a strip of paper or paint.

These two stood at the very front of the delegation, while the other members followed at a distance behind them.

It was not difficult to see that these two were the leaders of this Hidden Cloud Village delegation.

Not long after the group entered Konoha, Senju Tobirama also arrived at the Village entrance with a group to welcome them.

Today, as he was hosting distinguished guests from afar, Tobirama was not wearing his layered armor but was instead dressed in a kimono, appearing to attach great importance to the occasion.

Although he suspected in his heart that the other party had ulterior motives, his outward attitude remained quite polite.

When the two reached each other, Senju Tobirama shook hands with the old man from Kumogakure in greeting.

« Elder from Kumogakure, I have long admired your name. »

« Senju Tobirama, long admired, long admired. »

This robust old man leading the delegation to discuss the Joint Chunin Exams was one of the current elders of Kumogakure.

It was merely to discuss matters related to the Chunin Exams, not the official commencement of the Joint Chunin Exams, so there was no need for the Raikage to come in person.

On a building not far away, Senju Matsumi and Hyuga Tsukuyomi concealed their presence, observing the two groups meeting below.

Senju Tobirama was at the front of the group talking with the Kumogakure elder, with no time to attend to the others in the delegation.

And Matsumi and the others' mission was to hide in the vicinity and keep a constant watch on the movements of the entire delegation.

Matsumi had no impression of this Kumogakure elder, but the adult male standing behind him made him feel familiar.

Thinking carefully, this figure vaguely matched a figure in his memory.

The dark skin common in the Land of Lightning, the uniquely styled bun and long beard, and the conspicuous white strip on his nose.

This appearance was clearly that of the future Second Raikage of Kumogakure in his youth.

In the original timeline, when he was negotiating peace with the then-Second Hokage Senju Tobirama, he and Senju Tobirama were killed together by the rebelling Kinkaku and Ginkaku brothers.

At this time, he was still relatively young, the most powerful and prestigious among the youth of Kumogakure, serving as the bodyguard of the First Raikage.
