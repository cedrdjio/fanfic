# Chapitre 16 — Mission Complete!

The Water Dragon Bullet was coiled with violent lightning, truly like a dragon riding the lightning, immensely terrifying.

« Big Brother Hiruzen, you are truly worthy of being the genius recognized by the Second Uncle. »

Seeing this, Senju Matsuaki felt even heavier in his heart, and his grip on the short sword tightened.

If Tsugikuni Yoriichi were here, relying on Sun Breathing, he might be able to slice this lightning dragon apart effortlessly, remaining unscathed.

However, Tsugikuni Yoriichi’s swordsmanship had reached the pinnacle, something Matsumi could not reach.

But even so, he would not give up.

Greed leads to poor digestion, so before reaching the level of a Genin, Senju Matsuaki focused only on the practice of two types of elemental Ninjutsu.

One was Water Release, because Senju Tobirama was extremely proficient in Water Release and could provide him with a lot of guidance.

And the other was Earth Release, because Earth Release was better at defense and could complement other Ninjutsu.

After performing hand seals, a stone slab rose from the ground in front of Senju Matsuaki.

« Earth Release : Earth Shore Return ! »

This was a defensive Earth Release Ninjutsu, and the stone slab hid Senju Matsuaki firmly behind it.

But he had to do more than that.

He stomped his right foot forward violently, and his super strength erupted.

The stone slab standing on the ground shattered into several pieces, flying forward.

Unless mixed with certain special minerals, stones usually do not conduct electricity.

Using this as cover, Senju Matsuaki had no intention of avoiding it, charging forward directly with his sword.

At the same time, Chakra exploded throughout his body, causing the surrounding air to emit muffled sounds.

« What an enviable talent ! »

Outside the field, Sarutobi Sasuke turned his head to look at Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama standing beside him.

The amount of Chakra of the Senju Clan was also one of their unique advantages.

And even within the Senju Clan, everyone’s talent in physical fitness and Chakra volume differed.

Hearing this, Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama just looked at each other.

Both saw the surprise in each other’s eyes.

« At little Matsumi’s age, we didn’t have this much Chakra. »

« And in terms of physical fitness, he far exceeds what Big Brother and I had at the time. »

On the field, because Enma had already left, Hiruzen Sarutobi lost his handy Adamantine Staff.

He had to take out a Kunai again, while forming hand seals.

« Wind Release : Gale Palm ! »

Ninjutsu is not a rigid thing ; the same Gale Palm also has different methods of use.

Just now, Hiruzen Sarutobi used Gale Palm to knock Senju Matsuaki away to increase the distance between them.

And this time, he used the power of the gale to add power to the Kunai in his hand.

In his hand, that originally plain-looking Kunai was already covered with a layer of rapidly flowing gale.

Driven by the Kunai and the Ninjutsu, Hiruzen Sarutobi also faced Senju Matsuaki who was rushing towards him.

On Matsumi’s side, in the blink of an eye, he had reached the front of the Lightning Water Dragon Bullet.

Even with the obstruction of Earth Shore Return, the power of the Lightning Water Dragon Bullet was slightly reduced, but it was still not to be underestimated.

The aggressive water dragon collided with the short sword containing immense force, and the impact and current came together.

If he hadn’t offset the strong current by releasing his super strength, Matsumi’s body would probably have been charred black in an instant.

« Slash ! »

The sharp blade collided with the head of the Lightning Water Dragon Bullet, and the two paused briefly for a few seconds.

Subsequently, the Lightning Water Dragon Bullet ultimately lacked follow-up power, and its momentum slowed down.

Matsumi’s blade, like cutting tofu, sliced through the middle of the Lightning Water Dragon Bullet.

The aggressive Lightning Water Dragon Bullet turned into a pool of water and scattered from both sides, and the lightning was released into the air after losing its attachment, leaving no trace.

In the next instant, Senju Matsuaki’s super strength slash and Hiruzen Sarutobi’s Kunai attached with Gale Palm were about to collide head-on.

By this point in the battle, both of them were a bit carried away, with only their opponent in their eyes.

Outside the field, everyone subconsciously stopped talking, their hearts lifted, waiting for the result of this battle.

However, just at this moment, the heated battle on the field came to an abrupt end.

The whistling wind brought by the gale surrounding the Kunai, and the muffled sound of Chakra released by the super strength slash colliding with the air.

In an instant, everything returned to silence.

On the field, the tall figure of Senju Tobirama appeared between the two at some point.

His short silver hair was blown by the wind raised by the two’s Ninjutsu, his expression calm.

Senju Tobirama’s left hand grabbed Matsumi’s right wrist, forcing his action of swinging the short sword to stop.

His right hand pinched Hiruzen Sarutobi’s wrist, letting the gale wrapped around the Kunai gradually dissipate.

Both Senju Matsuaki and Hiruzen Sarutobi felt that their hands could not move after being grabbed by Senju Tobirama.

« Let’s end the battle here. » Senju Tobirama looked at the two.

« If we continue to fight, the consequences will be hard to control. »

Hearing this, Senju Matsuaki and Hiruzen Sarutobi had no other objections, nodded, and put away their short swords and Kunai respectively.

« Not bad. »

Senju Tobirama let go, his gaze swept across the two, rarely revealing a satisfied smile.

Whether it was Senju Matsuaki or Hiruzen Sarutobi, they both brought him surprises in the just-concluded battle, especially the former.

For Hiruzen Sarutobi, he was mainly surprised by the Lightning Water Dragon Bullet just now.

Although he knew that Hiruzen Sarutobi was talented in the combination of Ninjutsu, he didn’t expect that he could reach this level at this age.

And what surprised him even more was Senju Matsuaki.

To be honest, being able to graduate from the Ninja Academy and become a Genin at his age was already the best among his peers.

After all, in this relatively peaceful period, the requirements for early graduation were even more stringent.

However, Matsumi was not only able to do this, but also showed strength that was still impressive in the Chunin team during the battle with Hiruzen.

« Matsuaki, you have actually become so strong ! »

Hiruzen Sarutobi looked at Senju Matsuaki, who was a bit shorter than himself, with complex eyes.

He remembered that when he was at this age, he was still worrying about whether he could successfully pass the early graduation assessment.

At that time, the gap in strength between him, Danzo Shimura, and Uchiha Kagami was not big, and he encountered several bitter battles during the graduation assessment.

Unexpectedly, after many years, he returned to the scene of the graduation assessment as a senior, but he was still encountering a bitter battle.

However, he was also an open-minded person, quickly threw these emotions behind his head, and smiled while patting the other’s shoulder.

Hearing this, Matsumi just smiled bitterly and shook his head : « Big Brother Hiruzen, that was already my full strength just now. »

If it weren’t for the fact that there were many people watching outside the field, he would just want to sit on the ground now.

Whether it was physical strength or Chakra, he had consumed almost all of it.

His Chakra was much more than his peers, but it was not inexhaustible.

At this time, the notification of mission completion also appeared, and the reward points were also issued.

[C-level mission completed, fight against Hiruzen Sarutobi without being defeated. Reward : 200 points !]

Seeing this, even though his spirit and body were very tired, Matsumi still showed a satisfied smile.
