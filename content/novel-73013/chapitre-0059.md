# Chapitre 59 — Gathering of the Future Second Kage

Konoha, Ninja Academy.

Inside a conference room on the top floor of a teaching building.

Five groups of people naturally occupied different positions in the conference room, surrounding crystal balls placed at the center of their respective tables, their expressions varied.

These five groups were Ninjas from different Villages. Except for the central position led by Hokage Senju Hashirama, the leaders of the other Villages were basically the second-in-command of their respective Villages.

After all, the Joint Chunin Exams would last for quite a while, and the Kage of other Villages could not leave their Villages for long periods.

Therefore, just like the nobles and dignitaries from various countries who would be invited to Konoha later, they would only arrive at Konoha to watch the battle during the third stage of the assessment.

The central position belonged to Konoha, the host of this Joint Chunin Exams.

There were four people on Konoha’s side : Senju Hashirama, Senju Tobirama, Nara Kano, and the head of the Hyuga Clan, Hyuga Teruake.

Surrounding the center, there were four other round tables distributed in the conference room, with representatives from other Villages sitting around their respective round tables.

The team leader for the Hidden Cloud Village for this Chunin Exams was the future Second Raikage, A.

Beside him were several male Cloud Ninjas who were also sturdy and dark-skinned, as well as a female Ninja with fair skin and a slender, tall figure.

The team leader for Iwagakure was a man wrapped tightly in bandages.

He was the future Second Tsuchikage, Mu. At this time, his identity was the bodyguard or assistant to the First Tsuchikage, Ishikawa, but his status as the future Tsuchikage was almost set in stone.

Besides Mu, there was another young man in the Iwagakure group who was quite eye-catching.

His build was shorter than the others, but his appearance seemed to be that of an adult ; he was naturally short.

He had short hair, very thick eyebrows, and a nose that looked as if it were swollen.

The team leader for the Hidden Sand Village was the future Second Kazekage, Shamon.

At this moment, he was sitting on a chair with his arms crossed, expressionless. Sitting beside him were three Sand Ninjas wearing yellowish-brown clothes and Hidden Sand headbands.

The team leader for the Hidden Mist Village was a man with a very flamboyant appearance.

He had yellow hair styled in a flashy way, wore a high-collared trench coat, and his waist was cinched with a black silk ribbon.

He had a small mustache above his lips, but the area above his eyes was completely empty ; he had no eyebrows.

He was the future Second Mizukage, Hōzuki Gengetsu, who was already the second-in-command of the Hidden Mist.

The above were the main figures in the conference room.

These people were either the leaders or second-in-commands of their Villages, and their strength was basically at the top level of the current Ninja World.

With the team leaders of each Village, plus Konoha’s Senju Tobirama, it could be said that the future Second Kage of the Five Great Ninja Villages were all gathered here.

This was the first time these people had sat calmly in the same conference room without causing a dispute.

« Hey, what is this crystal ball placed in the middle for ? »

Hōzuki Gengetsu stroked the small mustache on his chin with his left hand, while pointing at the crystal ball on the table with his right hand, asking the question.

On the other side, Mu said in a deep voice : « Little guy with the white beard, can you be quiet ? You are the most noisy one here ! »

« What ! ? Bandage man, say that again if you dare ! ? »

Hōzuki Gengetsu was furious upon hearing this. He rolled up his sleeves and was about to go forward, but was held back by other Ninjas from his Village.

Subconsciously glancing at the First Hokage, Senju Hashirama, not far away, Hōzuki Gengetsu snorted coldly and sat back in his original position.

Most of the team leaders present were arrogant people. In their respective Villages, they were all reserve Kage, just waiting for the day when the previous Kage retired to immediately take office.

Although they were reserves in terms of status, they were not reserves in terms of strength.

Whether in the chaotic Sengoku era or the current era of Villages, their strength was among the top tier of Ninjas.

The reason they could sit obediently in a conference room as spectators was not because of anything else, but because of the deterrent power of Senju Hashirama.

Currently, large-scale wars had not yet broken out between the major powers, but small frictions occurred from time to time.

Regardless of before or after the era of Villages, there was no shortage of Ninjas from various countries who had fought against Senju Hashirama.

The strength between Ninjas was mostly spread by word of mouth and compared with each other.

Today I heard that A defeated B, and I feel that I am not as good as B, so I must not be as good as A.

But if I am stronger than B, then can I also go up against A ?

However, Ninjas from various Villages gradually discovered that it seemed they had never heard of Senju Hashirama losing to anyone.

And the previous Konoha was even more terrifying than it was now, because there was also Uchiha Madara, who had no other defeats except for losing to Senju Hashirama.

Of course, as Ninjas who had killed their way out of troubled times, the people present would not be as obedient as they appeared on the surface.

The other First Kage who could establish a nation in troubled times were not simple in terms of strength. Even if they were inferior to Hashirama and Madara, at least they had no rivals within their own country.

Coupled with the fact that Ninjas from various Villages often had a sense of worship for their own Village’s Kage, before witnessing the gap with their own eyes, they would not really think that their own Kage was inferior to Konoha’s.

« Everyone, please calm down. » Senju Hashirama said with a smile.

« This is a Ninjutsu developed by our Konoha Ninjas, called the Telescope Technique. »

Senju Hashirama picked up the crystal ball on the table, which emitted a faint glow under the light.

« This crystal ball is specially made to cooperate with this technique. It can project the situation of the examination venue into it, so we can see the situation of the examination venue without worrying about affecting the exam. »

Hearing this, everyone from the various Villages was stunned, and then they looked squarely at the crystal ball in front of them.

« Unimaginable... »

A from the Hidden Cloud Village frowned, trying to simulate the effect of the Ninjutsu Senju Hashirama just mentioned in his mind.

However, he was not good at developing Ninjutsu in the first place, and he simply could not understand how to perform such precise operations with Ninjutsu.

« Tobirama, let’s start. »

Seeing that the time was about right, Senju Hashirama looked at Senju Tobirama beside him and said.

Then, Senju Tobirama formed seals with his hands, and Chakra condensed in his palms.

The crystal ball on the table in front seemed to echo with it, and the light became increasingly dazzling.

As Tobirama finished forming the seals, the light on the crystal ball gradually dissipated, and some moving « impurities » appeared in it.

However, looking closely, one could find that this was not a flaw in the crystal ball, but that clear images were gradually appearing inside it.

Not only the crystal ball in front of Senju Hashirama and the others, but at the same time, images of the examination venue also appeared one after another on the crystal balls in front of the representatives of other Villages.

« That’s really incredible~ »

Hōzuki Gengetsu had a cynical smile on his face, but a flash of gravity flickered in the depths of his eyes.

Seeing the casting process of this Ninjutsu, he roughly understood the principle, but it was not that easy for him to replicate it.

Forming seals was almost just the most superficial part of this Ninjutsu.

In addition, how to use Chakra to connect the remote live scene with the crystal ball, and how to make this specially made crystal ball, were the real barriers of this Ninjutsu.

On the other side, the silent Mu felt even heavier in his heart.

Compared to others, he saw deeper and understood that there was another difficulty in this technique, which was the exquisite Chakra control involved.

He could see that the Chakra control required for this Ninjutsu was very precise, completely different from conventional combat Ninjutsu.

Among the people present, there were not a few who could reach this level, but there were very few who could develop this precise Ninjutsu from scratch.

However, this Ninjutsu was not even created by Senju Tobirama, who was rumored to be the best at developing Ninjutsu in Konoha.

Konoha really was hiding talented people...
