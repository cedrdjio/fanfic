# Chapitre 135 — Requisition · Limited-Time Character Summoning Card!

The morning light barely pierced through, and the shadows of the clouds gradually dispersed.

In Konoha, amidst the mountain forests far removed from the bustling city.

Senju Matsuaki lay leisurely on the branch of a large tree, enjoying the hard-won peace and quiet.

One of the benefits of Wood Release was that he could create a wooden chair at any time, anywhere, perfectly shaped to fit his sitting or lying posture.

Today marked one week since the end of the Chunin Exams.

The victory banquet with family and friends had been eaten.

The dividends from senju happy water had been collected.

The standardized flak jacket issued after his promotion to Chunin had been received.

Over this period, his name had become a household word in Konoha.

Winning the Joint Chunin Exams had brought him immense fame.

And Wood Release, that iconic Bloodline Limit of the First Hokage, had drawn countless gazes of attention toward him.

The status and reputation of the Senju Clan in Konoha made him a star in Konoha who could never « fall from grace. »

It was clearly visible that this reputation would continue to rise step by step as he achieved more victories in the Ninja World in the future.

However, having too much fame also had its downsides.

For the past week, his residence had been overcrowded, with countless people wanting to meet him.

Konoha residents and Genin who admired him, novelists wanting to use him as a prototype for their stories, and perhaps even spies from other Ninja Villages...

In short, he couldn’t find a moment of peace while staying at home.

Only by running to these rarely visited mountain forests could he find a moment of tranquility.

He was as leisurely now as he had been busy while participating in the Chunin Exams a while ago.

His good teammate, Hyuga Tsukuyomi, was still recovering from her injuries ; although she appeared to be fine on the outside, it was still inappropriate for her to go out on missions.

Furthermore, after this Chunin Exams, both he and Hyuga Tsukuyomi had been comprehensively evaluated as possessing the qualifications of a Chunin based on their performance and had been promoted to Chunin.

According to Konoha’s regulations, once a Ninja was promoted to Chunin, they possessed the ability to carry out missions independently.

Unless there were special circumstances, the original squad would have to be disbanded.

Of course, if the two agreed to carry out missions together, they wouldn’t be hindered as long as it didn’t contradict the Village’s mandatory regulations.

His uncle and second uncle were both busy with Village affairs.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi was still recovering from her injuries.

For a moment, Senju Matsuaki didn’t know what to do.

Leaning against the tree, he began to sort out the things he needed to do next.

First was the mission he had triggered by chance when he went to his uncle Senju Hashirama’s house to retrieve the Wood Release Ninjutsu scroll.

[S-Rank Mission : Unseal the Ultimate Evil Weapon. Reward : 10,000 points !]

The 10,000-point reward made him both covetous and wary.

One had to know that even when the System judged that he had defeated a Kage-level Ninja by chance during the exam, the reward was only 5,000 points.

Since this S-Rank mission could offer a reward of 10,000 points, the level of danger was self-evident.

But then again, this mission was about unsealing the Ultimate Evil Weapon.

If he didn’t unseal it and only observed whether the original Seal was still secure, there shouldn’t be any danger.

Otherwise, the predecessors of the Senju Clan should have given sufficient warnings in the scroll recording this matter ; they wouldn’t just let an inexplicable danger trap and kill their descendants.

Since it wasn’t recorded in the scroll, it was highly likely that he wouldn’t encounter any danger before removing the Seal.

If that was the case, then picking a time to scout the location and observe the situation wouldn’t be a problem.

If he could turn it into his own asset, he would be more confident in dealing with future crises.

Thinking this, Senju Matsuaki added this matter to his schedule.

Next was another matter that hung over his heart like a dark haze : the future development of the Ninja World.

Regarding the major events of the next few years, the timeline in the original work was somewhat blurry and not as detailed as during the main storyline.

However, combined with his understanding of the current situation in the Ninja World, he could slightly infer some future developments.

In the near future, that man whom he had only met a few times in his memories before leaving the Village, Uchiha Madara, would control the Nine-Tailed and attack Konoha.

Ultimately, Senju Hashirama and Uchiha Madara would fight a decisive battle at the Valley of the End, ending with the « death » of Uchiha Madara.

In terms of timing, it should be within the next year or two.

Taking this event as a turning point, the entire Ninja World would be like pressing an acceleration button.

First, the major Ninja Villages in the Ninja World seemed to take the strategic value of Tailed Beasts seriously because of the battle at the Valley of the End.

Senju Hashirama also realized the danger of Tailed Beasts, and to ensure balance, he would choose to distribute the Tailed Beasts at the first Five Kage Summit.

Senju Matsuaki actually did not agree with this approach in his heart.

Furthermore, Senju Hashirama seemed to have suffered hidden injuries in the battle at the Valley of the End, and after capturing the Tailed Beasts, he was completely burnt out, passing away shortly after the Five Kage Summit.

Then, the major Ninja Villages were dissatisfied with the distribution of interests in the Ninja World, and with the passing of Senju Hashirama, who had always kept them in check...

The First Ninja World War would sweep across the entire Ninja World !

The peace that had been briefly possessed over these years following the establishment of the one country, one Village System would vanish in an instant.

Of course, the First Ninja World War was still a matter for ten years from now, so it was too early to consider it.

The sword of Damocles hanging over his head at this moment, which could fall at any time, was the invasion by Uchiha Madara.

Because the battlefield would shift to the Valley of the End, the others in Konoha would not be in any major danger.

However, Senju Hashirama would irrecoverably lose a large amount of life force in this battle.

An elder who treated him like his own child would fall at his Peak just like that...

This point was something Senju Matsuaki simply could not accept.

« Uchiha Madara... »

Thinking of Uchiha Madara’s strength, Senju Matsuaki felt a sense of powerlessness in his heart again.

Let alone himself.

Other than Senju Hashirama, the other four original Kage would be nothing more than a steamroll in a fight against Uchiha Madara.

« Even five or six years would be fine, why only one or two... » Senju Matsuaki’s brows furrowed deeply.

In the future, he had the confidence to reach or even surpass Uchiha Madara.

Or rather, with the support of various Golden Fingers, this was almost an inevitability.

But unfortunately, time did not seem to allow him sufficient time to develop.

No, that’s not right !

Senju Matsuaki’s eyes suddenly lit up.

He suddenly realized that his previous thinking seemed to have some misconceptions.

He indeed had no way to reach Uchiha Madara’s level in a year or two, but that didn’t mean the Requisition Golden Finger platform couldn’t solve his predicament.

Improving his strength and dealing with Uchiha Madara were not necessarily equivalent to the same thing.

After figuring this out, Senju Matsuaki decisively opened the platform, and the light screen appeared before his eyes.

Promotional Items...

As always, Senju Matsuaki’s gaze first swept over the five promotional items refreshed today.

There were many useful Golden Fingers, but not many that could meet his needs.

After some screening, Senju Matsuaki locked onto a Golden Finger item that was 40% off.

It was a golden card, with a blurry image of a character flickering on it.

As Senju Matsuaki observed it, this blurry image was constantly changing, as if it wasn’t fixed.

[Requisition · Limited-Time Character Summoning Card : Can randomly summon a character from another world, time limit of one day. Original price : 5,000 points. Promotional price : 3,000 points.]
