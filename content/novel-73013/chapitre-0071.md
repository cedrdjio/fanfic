# Chapitre 71 — Rescue!

« Lord Tobirama, an Iwagakure Ninja in Zone 6 is attempting to kill one of our Genin. Should we initiate a rescue ? »

In the quiet surveillance room, the anxious voice of a Ninja suddenly rang out.

« Do not panic. Zone 6... »

Senju Tobirama’s heart tightened upon hearing this, but after glancing at the crystal ball in front of him, he let his suspended heart settle.

« No rescue is needed. Another Konoha Genin will arrive in the vicinity in half a minute ; he can handle it. »

« Yes ! »

...

Inuzuka Rei, a Genin from the Inuzuka Clan, had average Ninja talent and was still participating in the Chunin Exams at fourteen years old.

The Chunin Exams were held twice a year. This was her third time participating, and her second year taking the Chunin Exams.

She had originally thought that the Chunin Exams, jointly held by the Five Great Ninja Villages, would just be larger in scale compared to the previous Konoha Chunin Exams, and that the difficulty shouldn’t be much different.

However, only now, trapped in a crisis, did she realize that this thought was wrong—wrong to an absurd degree.

Although, with the exception of a very few candidates, the strength of candidates from other Villages was not much different from that of the Konoha candidates.

However, while Konoha candidates viewed each other as rivals, candidates from different Villages viewed each other as enemies !

In the previous Konoha Chunin Exams she had participated in, if you were eliminated, you conceded and left, and you would be ready to fight again for the next exam.

But now, trapped in a crisis, she didn’t even have the chance to concede.

The opponent was aiming to take her life !

At this moment, her cheeks, painted with red markings, bore a fierce, wild look, and the white dog following by her side was also baring its fangs.

The Inuzuka Clan grew up alongside their Ninja Dogs, mastering secret techniques to tame them and fight alongside them.

Because of these clan secret techniques, Inuzuka Clan Ninjas were all more or less imbued with the wildness of beasts.

At this moment, both the person and the dog had been driven into a desperate situation.

In front of this person and dog were two Iwagakure Ninjas traveling together.

One of them was burly, with massive fists crossed in front of his chest, wrapped in a thick layer of rock.

The other held a Kunai in his left hand, while his right hand was faintly guarding his front, wary of Inuzuka Rei’s desperate counterattack.

Seeing this, Inuzuka Rei and her Ninja Dog both displayed fierce glares.

A trapped beast will still fight ; this certain-death situation instead ignited their ferocity.

« Hand over the Inuzuka Clan’s methods and secret techniques for raising Ninja Dogs, and we will spare your life ! ! »

The Iwagakure Ninja with the rock fists curled his lips into a sinister grin, his gaze scanning Inuzuka Rei and the Ninja Dog greedily.

Killing in the Chunin Exams carried risks.

But when the benefits were great enough, these risks didn’t matter.

In recent years, Iwagakure had unified the Ninjas within the Village who possessed the Explosion Release Bloodline Limit, forming an Explosion Corps that had become Iwagakure’s great weapon.

Having tasted the benefits of this, Iwagakure placed increasing importance on the formation of special forces.

If the two of them could bring the Konoha Inuzuka Clan’s secret techniques back to Iwagakure and help Iwagakure form a Ninja Dog unit, they would definitely become great heroes of the Village.

Their talents were not weak ; as long as they received the allocation of Iwagakure’s resources, they would very likely enter the upper echelons of Iwagakure in the future.

At that time, not only they, but even their relatives and descendants would benefit infinitely.

« Do you think I’m stupid ? ! »

Hearing the Iwagakure Ninja’s words, Inuzuka Rei was furious, feeling as though her intelligence had been insulted.

Stealing secret techniques was an act that violated the bottom line of a Ninja Village ; once the matter was exposed, Iwagakure would face the wrath of Konoha.

Therefore, when the two revealed their intention to steal the secret techniques, the relationship between the two sides was already one of life and death.

« Four-Legs Technique ! ! »

Inuzuka Rei concentrated Chakra into her limbs, dropped to all fours like a beast, and prepared for a desperate fight.

« Awoo ! ! »

Feeling her master’s determination, the Ninja Dog also let out a loud roar, and its beastly eyes, filled with fierce light, also took on a touch of desperate madness.

« Hah, you ignorant fool ! Let’s go ! »

Seeing that his goal could not be achieved, the Iwagakure Ninja was annoyed and embarrassed. He raised his rock fist, intending to call his companion to launch an attack.

However, when he turned his head, he found his companion still standing there blankly.

Plop—

A light sound appeared quite noticeably in the ears of those present ; it was the Kunai from the Iwagakure Ninja’s hand falling onto the soil.

« Hey, you... what’s wrong with you ! ? »

The Iwagakure Ninja maintaining the Rock Fist Technique first frowned at his companion’s sluggishness, but then suddenly stepped back in terror.

He saw that his companion, who had been standing there blankly, suddenly split into two halves from the middle.

The blood spurting to the sides landed on the front of his shoes, and the warm blood sent a chill through his heart.

Then, the Iwagakure Ninja’s bisected corpse fell over, revealing the figure standing behind it who had appeared out of nowhere.

« It’s one of our own ! ! »

Seeing the Konoha forehead protector worn on the forehead beneath the newcomer’s silver hair, Inuzuka Rei’s eyes lit up, as if she had seen hope.

« Damn it ! ! You bastard, how dare you kill our Iwagakure people ! ! ! »

Seeing his companion killed, the Iwagakure Ninja reacted with a look of shock and anger, raising his pair of rock fists to charge forward.

« Watch out, his fists... »

Seeing this, Inuzuka Rei shouted a warning while also wanting to step forward to help.

The Rock Fist Technique was a representative Ninjutsu of Iwagakure, with a B-rank difficulty.

The user could cover their arm in a layer of hard rock, which could both protect the arm and give it extreme hardness.

In Iwagakure, basically any Ninja who was good at Earth Release Ninjutsu knew this move.

Inuzuka Rei had fought this Iwagakure Ninja earlier and knew that his Rock Fist Technique was quite troublesome.

However, what happened in the next instant caused her to stop her advance to assist in astonishment.

She saw that just as the Iwagakure Ninja launched an attack at the youth, the youth also met him head-on, holding a short blade.

With her dynamic vision, she could only barely make out the youth’s movement the moment he raised his blade.

The next moment, the youth seemed to transform into a streak of white light as he passed by the Iwagakure Ninja.

Another jet of blood sprayed out ; the Iwagakure Ninja’s rock fist crumbled from the middle to the sides, and his arm was severed from his body along with it.

The Iwagakure Ninja, whose life had already been cut short, collapsed with his burly body. His remaining consciousness made him want to say something, but after a few gurgling sounds, he finally breathed his last.

« Since you wanted to kill a Konoha Ninja, you must also be prepared to be killed yourself. »

Senju Matsuaki flicked the blood droplets off his blade, sheathed it, and walked over to the two corpses. After a quick search, he obtained three game pieces.

Two of them were blank, and the third had the character « King » written on it.

Seeing the crisis resolved, Inuzuka Rei sighed in relief, released her Four-Legs Technique, and brought her Ninja Dog with her to Senju Matsuaki’s side.

Seeing the youth’s youthful face, she couldn’t help but be surprised.

But when her gaze met his ruby-like eyes, she felt her heartbeat uncontrollably quicken.

« Thank you for saving me. My name is Inuzuka Rei. May I ask your name ? »
