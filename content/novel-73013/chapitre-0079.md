# Chapitre 79 — Senju Matsuaki's Collecting Habit?

Seeing this, the two women also recovered from the shock of the terrain changing in an instant, and followed Senju Matsuaki’s footsteps as they left the area.

Swish— Swish— Several sounds of something breaking through the air rang out, and pitch-black Iron Sand long needles pierced into the rock wall, embedding themselves firmly within.

« Damn Konoha Ninja ! ! »

In the sky, Yan’s figure crossed over the tall, thick rock wall, supported by the Iron Sand.

However, on the other side of the rock wall, the figures of the three had long since vanished.

Looking at the immensely massive rock wall beneath his feet, Yan could not help but feel shock and wariness in his eyes.

For a Ninja, sometimes the amount of Chakra can explain many things.

The Ninjutsu Earth Flow Wall was also known in the Hidden Sand Village, and Yan had seen seniors from his own Village perform this Ninjutsu.

But this was the first time he had seen one of such a massive scale.

If he were to consume such a massive amount of Chakra in an instant, he would likely have already collapsed and died.

Glaring resentfully in the direction the three had left, Yan did not pursue them, but instead flew towards the location where he remembered Chiyo and Ebizo were.

He needed to see them alive or see their corpses ; even if the two had really been killed, he had to find their bodies.

« They didn’t fight ? »

Hundreds of meters away, Dou’s burly and tall body slowly emerged from the bushes, his expression filled with regret.

If he could have taken them both out in one fell swoop while Yan from the Hidden Sand Village and Senju Matsuaki were both severely injured, then both the Hidden Sand Village and Konoha would lose a part of their future.

That was why he was willing to waste time lurking here, rather than continuing to advance toward the finish line.

With his naked eyes, Dou could only vaguely see the figures in the distance, unaware that his every move had already entered the range of the Byakugan.

Seeing that both sides had left the area, he suddenly felt bored and turned to leave as well.

Inside the office, Senju Tobirama switched the image on the crystal ball.

« The conflict in Area 12 has ceased. Transfer personnel to Area 14 ; there’s a brat from the Hidden Sand Village over there who’s about to be beaten to death. Go save him. »

After all, he wasn’t a devil ; it wouldn’t do to not rescue a Genin from another Village who was on the verge of death.

A few individual deaths could be called accidents, but if a large group died, it wouldn’t be conducive to unity.

Since the death quota was limited, he would let some Genin from other Villages who showed good talent in the intelligence reports have an « accident, » and let the other mediocre Genin live on in mediocrity.

In this way, the representatives from the other Ninja Villages wouldn’t react too excessively.

« Lord Tobirama, Chiyo and Ebizo from the Hidden Sand Village have already been sent to the Konoha Hospital for treatment. Should we... »

A Jonin entered from outside the door, and after reporting, he raised his right hand and made a throat-slitting gesture in front of his neck.

Senju Tobirama’s eyes flickered, but he shook his head and said : « Taking action in the Konoha Hospital is too obvious, and the Hidden Sand Village will likely send people to guard them soon. »

After thinking for a moment, he added : « Strengthen the security at the Konoha Hospital. Do not let the wounded be assassinated by Ninjas from other Villages. »

In the quiet forest, the sound of soles stepping on the soft soil was quite distinct.

After escaping the battle, Senju Matsuaki and the other two found a suitable place to sit and rest.

« Thank you very much for saving my life, and you too, Tsukuyomi. If it weren’t for you, I would have likely been killed by that Sand Ninja just now. »

Biwako thanked the two with a look of gratitude, still feeling lingering fear when recalling the scene from just a moment ago.

« It was nothing. » Senju Matsuaki shook his head.

They were all comrades from Konoha ; even if it had been someone he didn’t know, he would have saved them.

Furthermore, his original objective was to rescue Hyuga Tsukuyomi ; Biwako was just incidental.

Biwako took a deep breath, as if she had made a difficult decision.

« I wish you all smooth sailing from here on out. As for me... »

« Big Sister Biwako, you... »

« I’m sorry, Tsukuyomi. I’ve reached my limit. Even if I relied on your strength to pass the second stage of the assessment, it would be impossible for me to last long in the third stage. »

Biwako smiled bitterly and raised her hands, her fingers trembling slightly and uncontrollably.

Then she revealed her fair arms to the two of them.

It could be seen that the center of her arms was bruised, even turning purple.

When she was controlled by Yan’s Magnet Release just now, she even thought she would be crushed directly.

If not for the threat of Hyuga Tsukuyomi destroying her own Byakugan, which made Yan hesitant, she would likely be nothing more than flesh and blood scattered all over the ground by now.

Moreover, having persevered until now, she had also realized her current strength through the competition.

To become a Chunin, she still needed to settle down and refine herself.

Hearing this, Senju Matsuaki and Hyuga Tsukuyomi exchanged glances, and both respected Biwako’s choice, not attempting to persuade her further.

After resting for a while, Biwako crushed her blank chess piece.

After saying goodbye to the two, she was taken away from the examination venue by a Proctor who rushed over from afar, gradually disappearing from their sight.

« Thank you so much for today, Matsuaki. Otherwise... »

After Biwako left, Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s expression seemed a bit more relaxed.

Although her personality had gradually improved after becoming a Genin, she was still an introvert at heart.

Although she had fought side-by-side with Biwako for a day, she still felt somewhat reserved when interacting with her.

On the contrary, the tacit understanding that had been maintained from their time at the Ninja Academy to their time as Genin made her feel at ease after reuniting with Senju Matsuaki.

« There’s no need to be so formal between us, right ? » Senju Matsuaki said with a smile.

« Yeah, yeah… »

Hyuga Tsukuyomi lowered her head and, looking slightly flustered, took out a jade chess piece from her Ninja tool pouch, wanting to change the subject.

« By the way, Matsuaki, have you obtained this chess piece ? »

According to her Byakugan’s observation, there were fewer and fewer examinees still in the first half.

At the average pace, excluding the Genin who had been eliminated, most people would gather in the center of the primeval forest by tomorrow.

Therefore, if one had not yet obtained a jade chess piece, one had to keep pace with the majority and move forward.

« I’ve been collecting them for the past two days, but the progress hasn’t been ideal. »

Looking at the chess piece in Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s hand, Senju Matsuaki sighed with some regret.

For Ninjas with sensory abilities, such as Hyuga Tsukuyomi and Inuzuka Rei, it wasn’t difficult to find targets in the primeval forest.

But for him, he could only rely on luck along his route.

If he was lucky, he could run into two or three examinees in half a day ; if he was unlucky, he wouldn’t see a soul all day.

Hearing this, Hyuga Tsukuyomi was just about to offer some words of comfort.

However, she saw Senju Matsuaki open the Ninja tool pouch at his waist, revealing one, two, three...

Seeing this, the words she was about to say were swallowed back, and the expression on her small face appeared somewhat stunned.

« Se-seven ! ? »

Was this some kind of collecting habit ?

[Current Progress : Captured 7 chess pieces, defeated 11 examinees.]

While Senju Matsuaki was showing the Ninja tool pouch to Hyuga Tsukuyomi, he was also checking his current progress.

This progress was the real reason for his regret and dissatisfaction at the moment.

It wasn’t Senju Matsuaki humblebragging, but rather he was truly dissatisfied with this.

Now, for him, examinees carrying jade chess pieces were like walking points.

With points slipping away one by one, the feeling was like that of a merchant bearing massive losses every single moment.
