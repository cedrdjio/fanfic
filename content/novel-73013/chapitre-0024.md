# Chapitre 24 — Testing New Ninjutsu!

In the grass in the distance, the two of them kept the sound of their breathing and the magnitude of their movements to a minimum.

Seeing that one poison-tipped Senbon had killed a Rogue Ninja, Senju Matsuaki made a few hand gestures to Hyuga Tsukuyomi.

The general meaning was to praise Hyuga Tsukuyomi for doing a good job.

A Senbon is a tool similar to a flying needle ; it can be used to stimulate one’s own acupoints to stimulate potential, or it can be used to assassinate enemies.

Like Kunai and shuriken, Senbon are also essential items in a Ninja tool pouch.

Because their lethality is relatively low, poison is generally applied to them to increase their lethality.

Their advantage lies in the fact that they make little noise and are not easily discovered during flight.

Regarding Senbon, Senju Matsuaki could only be described as average in his usage ; he was better at throwing shuriken and Kunai.

However, Hyuga Tsukuyomi seemed to be more proficient in the use of Senbon.

Unfortunately, her original target was actually the Rogue Ninja Leader.

Judging from the tracking and observation just now, this person was the core of this group of Rogue Ninjas.

It was a pity that the sharp-mouthed, monkey-cheeked Ninja had been too close just now, blocking the body of the Rogue Ninja Leader.

Therefore, she had to change targets on the fly.

« Then next, it will be a frontal battle. » Senju Matsuaki took a deep breath.

The enemy was now alert, so it would be much more difficult to attempt an assassination.

The opponent’s strength was not very high, so it would be better to engage in a direct confrontation.

« We are outnumbered, so be careful. »

After giving a word of caution to Hyuga Tsukuyomi, who was beside him, Senju Matsuaki formed hand seals with both hands.

It was a rare opportunity to reach the actual combat stage, so he also wanted to test some of the new Ninjutsu he had learned during this time.

« Earth Release : Headhunter Jutsu ! »

After completing the hand seals, Senju Matsuaki’s figure dived underground.

Seeing this, Hyuga Tsukuyomi also used the cover of the grass to quickly close the distance to the enemy, making it easier to support Senju Matsuaki later.

On the other side, the Rogue Ninja Leader felt as if there was something strange underground.

« Watch out, it’s underground ! » He hurriedly gave a warning.

He then jumped up from his spot, instantly putting several meters of distance between himself and the ground.

The others clearly didn’t react as quickly as he did and stood blankly in place without moving.

The next moment, one of the Rogue Ninjas felt his body constantly sinking.

By the time he came to his senses, only his head was left exposed above the ground.

Earth Release : Headhunter Jutsu allows one to lurk underground, pull the target into the earth, and immobilize them.

Generally, there are only two outcomes for enemies who are hit by this technique : either they obediently divulge information and are then killed, or they are killed immediately.

At this moment, Senju Matsuaki did not want to extract any information from them.

Therefore, in the next second, a line of blood appeared on the Rogue Ninja’s neck.

After killing him, Senju Matsuaki lifted his red eyes and stared at the Rogue Ninja Leader in front with murderous intent.

« It’s a Konoha Ninja ! »

Someone noticed the forehead protector that Senju Matsuaki was wearing.

For them, who burned, killed, and looted within the Land of Fire, what they feared most was not the guards of nobles or the armies of the Daimyo, but the Ninjas of Konoha.

The symbol of Konoha had naturally become the sign they feared the most.

« What the hell are you afraid of ? It’s just two brats ! »

The Rogue Ninja Leader shouted angrily.

If an adult Ninja were here right now, he would definitely turn around and run.

But if it’s just a brat, it’s not impossible to kill them in return.

« There are about fifteen people left. »

In the blink of an eye, Senju Matsuaki roughly judged the number of enemies around him.

He did not have the 360-degree vision of the Byakugan, so within the short span of one or two breaths, he could only roughly judge the number of people on the battlefield.

In terms of numbers, the gap was incredibly vast.

But judging from the situation observed while tracking earlier, apart from this leader who was quite strong, very few of the others even reached the level of a Genin.

« Water Release : Water Dragon Bullet ! »

Senju Matsuaki formed hand seals with both hands, and a stream of water spewed out from his mouth, transforming into a ferocious dragon shape that flew forward.

The few Rogue Ninjas in front who couldn’t dodge in time were directly swept away by the Water Dragon Bullet, their bodies slamming into the trunks of the surrounding trees.

« Fire Release : Fireball Jutsu ! »

Hyuga Tsukuyomi was not idle either.

It was a rare opportunity to have opponents against whom they didn’t need to show mercy, so they both treated this as an excellent moment to practice Ninjutsu.

Because her Chakra nature was Fire and Lightning, she had started with the Fire Release : Fireball Jutsu during this period.

It could be seen that although she was still a bit rusty, the size of the Fireball was already quite impressive.

Compared to the Water Dragon Bullet, the lethality of the Fireball was more direct.

Several Rogue Ninjas who failed to dodge in time were engulfed by the flames, dying in severe agony from third-degree burns.

Seeing this scene, Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s expression couldn’t help but stiffen slightly.

Come to think of it, both Senju Matsuaki and Hyuga Tsukuyomi were on their first mission, and it was also their first time actually killing an enemy.

Just a moment ago, that one poison Senbon had killed a Rogue Ninja, but because the distance was too far, it wasn’t seen very clearly.

But at this moment, these Rogue Ninjas were not far in front of them, and the process of them losing their lives under the power of the Ninjutsu could be seen clearly.

Therefore, both of them felt a sense of strangeness and discomfort in their hearts.

However, since the enemy was still in front of them, they could only forcibly suppress this feeling.

« Damn it, five people are gone just like that... » The Rogue Ninja Leader cursed inwardly.

Subsequently, he drew a Ninja sword from his back.

« Everyone attack together, they are just Genin from Konoha, kill them ! »

After saying this, he also made a move as if to charge forward to fight.

Hearing these words, the others also suppressed the fear in their hearts.

That’s right, these two brats look like they just became Ninjas, what are we afraid of !

It wasn’t as if they hadn’t encountered Genin from other small Ninja villages on missions before.

Although they had suffered some sacrifices, in the end, the other side was always outnumbered.

« Fire Release : Phoenix Sage Fire Technique ! »

The Rogue Ninja Leader formed hand seals with both hands, and several fireballs were spewed from his mouth, each following a different flight trajectory.

Subsequently, he took an Explosive Tag from his bosom and tied it to a Kunai.

After throwing the Kunai toward the position where Senju Matsuaki was, he turned and ran away without looking back.

« A bunch of slow-moving trash, if it weren’t for you lot dragging your feet, I would have run away long ago ! »

The Rogue Ninja Leader abandoned his team members without hesitation, feeling a bit of heartache.

He didn’t feel heartache for these subordinates who followed him.

In fact, some of them had only been recruited by him for a short while, so there was no real affection to speak of.

What he felt heartache for was the Kunai and Explosive Tag he had just thrown away.

First, he lacked funds, and second, he lacked channels, so it was very difficult for him to buy an Explosive Tag.

The few he had were all for saving his life.

Behind him, the other Rogue Ninjas realized what had happened, and their eyes split in anger.

« That animal ! »

« To think I trusted you so much, only for you to abandon us and run ! »

They all felt anger and despair, losing the will to resist.

Only then did they wake up, realizing that the two Konoha Ninjas they were facing were strong enemies, even for their leader.
