# Chapitre 158 — Kirby vs. Uchiha Madara!

« Kirby ? » Senju Hashirama repeated the name, his expression still full of confusion.

This was a strange name he had never heard of before.

Moreover, he truly could not understand what kind of combat power this round, pink creature before him could possibly possess.

« Just wait and see, » Senju Matsuaki said with a smile.

Kirby, full name Kirby of the Stars, was considered a classic game character.

Some people also liked to call it the « Pink Demon » !

« End this farce ! » Uchiha Madara snorted coldly, preparing to weave hand Seals.

However, the next second, everyone’s expressions froze.

Seemingly wanting to prove itself after being looked down upon, Kirby opened its mouth, aimed at Uchiha Madara in the distance, and inhaled sharply !

Whoosh—

An invisible suction force erupted suddenly, forming a visible vortex of airflow in front of Kirby’s wide-open mouth.

Uchiha Madara’s pupils constricted ; he felt a chill in his heart as if he were being watched by a ferocious beast.

The next instant, he was uncontrollably dragged by that suction force, sliding toward Kirby !

« What ? ! »

He subconsciously jammed his fan into the gap of the Susanoo armor on the Nine-Tailed, attempting to stabilize himself.

However, the intensity of that suction was almost like a rule of nature, far exceeding Uchiha Madara’s imagination.

Uchiha Madara’s resistance was futile ; his body was abruptly torn away from the Nine-Tailed beneath him, flipping as he flew toward Senju Matsuaki and the others.

In mid-air, Uchiha Madara did not sit still.

Since the massive suction made it difficult to stabilize his body, he simply went with the flow of the suction and wove hand Seals.

« Fire Release : Dragon Flame Song Jutsu ! ! »

He finished the hand Seals and was about to open his mouth to spew four fire dragons.

Yet, to his astonishment, the Fire Release was like a flame being extinguished ; there was no reaction.

It was as if his Ninjutsu had been sealed.

Seeing this, Senju Matsuaki in the distance smiled slightly.

In game logic, once locked on by Kirby’s suction and the airflow took effect, there was almost no possibility of breaking free.

Unless the target was in a phase immune to inhalation or happened to hit a plot-mandated escape...

But Uchiha Madara clearly did not meet those conditions.

His only fate was to be sucked into Kirby’s stomach.

Uchiha Madara wanted to activate his Susanoo to resist, but the deep blue Chakra dissipated as soon as it condensed on his body surface due to Kirby’s suction.

« How is this possible ? ! »

Senju Tobirama’s eyes widened, his face full of disbelief.

Uchiha Hikari even gasped.

« It… it is devouring the Chakra of Uchiha Madara’s Susanoo ! »

That’s right, that suction was not just a physical pull ; it was devouring even the Chakra.

Only then did they realize that this seemingly fragile, cute creature might truly have the power to rival Uchiha Madara !

Uchiha Madara’s face was livid as he desperately channeled Chakra to resist.

However, that suction was like a bottomless pit ; no matter how hard he tried, he was powerless to resist.

Finally, in everyone’s astonished gaze, Uchiha Madara was sucked into Kirby’s mouth !

Gulp—

Kirby’s cheeks puffed out, and its eyes blinked innocently.

The battlefield fell into a dead silence.

Senju Hashirama opened his mouth wide, unable to say a word for a long time.

Senju Tobirama remained silent, wondering if he had fallen under some Genjutsu.

Uchiha Hikari and Hyuga Tsukuyomi looked at each other, speechless for a moment.

Only Senju Matsuaki, who had been mentally prepared from the beginning, had a slightly calmer expression, though a strange look flickered in the depths of his eyes.

That was Uchiha Madara !

One of the two strongest people in the current Ninja World, just like that… eaten ?

« Hi-ya ! »

Kirby patted its round belly, looking very satisfied.

Then, under the astonished gaze of the crowd, it opened its mouth and spat Uchiha Madara out.

Uchiha Madara spun at high speed, returning the way he came, as if transformed into a shooting star.

After colliding with the Nine-Tailed, Uchiha Madara revealed his original form, the dizziness causing him to stumble as he struggled to stand up.

« You’re courting death ! ! »

His scarlet eyes were filled with Killing Intent, but when he looked at the « Pink Demon » in the distance again, his expression changed.

The Kirby that had just been a simple pink ball began to change.

On its head, long black hair grew out, identical to Uchiha Madara’s.

A layer of red laminated armor draped over its pink body, and it gripped a flame fan in its hand.

Most striking were its eyes.

The originally deep blue eyes had now turned into a pair of scarlet Sharingan, with three tomoe slowly rotating within them !

« Th-this is… »

Senju Hashirama stammered, « It copied Madara’s abilities ? ! »

« This isn’t as simple as just copying abilities, » Senju Tobirama twitched the corner of his mouth. « It’s like it copied that guy Uchiha Madara entirely. »

To be honest, although he hated Uchiha Madara in his heart, seeing the series of events the other party had just suffered, even he, as an enemy, felt a bit relieved.

Senju Matsuaki nodded, a smile appearing at the corner of his mouth.

« Kirby can copy an opponent’s abilities by devouring them. It now possesses all of Uchiha Madara’s abilities. »

As soon as he finished speaking, Uchiha Madara stood up unsteadily. The red laminated armor on his body was damaged from the collision, and his face was terrifyingly gloomy.

He looked up at the pink demon, his eyes filled with Killing Intent.

« You damn monster… »

However, before he could finish speaking, Kirby moved.

Its speed was astonishing, leaving an afterimage in place as it appeared instantly before Uchiha Madara.

« Fire Release : Majestic Destroyer Flame ! »

Uchiha Madara wove hand Seals rapidly.

This time, it was not like before ; he finally successfully unleashed the Fire Release.

Surging flames spewed from his mouth, sweeping toward Kirby.

Facing the scorching flames in front, Kirby did not stop ; its small pink hands collided rapidly in front of its chest.

It was weaving hand Seals !

This thought crossed everyone’s mind.

The next second, huge flames did indeed spew from its mouth, likewise turning into a sea of fire, overwhelming Uchiha Madara.

« Majestic Destroyer Flame ? »

Uchiha Madara’s eye twitched, then he wove Seals : « Water Release : Water Formation Wall ! »

Water rose from beneath his feet, forming a water wall that blocked the onslaught of the flames.

Steam filled the air, obscuring vision.

Although he had blocked the flames, Uchiha Madara’s expression became increasingly gloomy.

That pink demon had not only copied his appearance and Sharingan, but even his Fire Release Ninjutsu could be replicated one-for-one.

Having walked the Ninja World for many years and climbed to the peak, this was the first time he had encountered such a bizarre thing !

Such an ability was far beyond the degree of what was called « copying » by the Sharingan.

« I don’t believe you can even copy the power of the Eternal Mangekyo ! ! »

A look of arrogance appeared in Uchiha Madara’s eyes.

He watched as the black three-tomoe in his eyes spun at high speed, deforming and transforming into the pattern of the Eternal Mangekyo !
