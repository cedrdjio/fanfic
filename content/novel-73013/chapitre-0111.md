# Chapitre 111 — Unexpectedly Knocked Out

« Thank you both very much, » Senju Matsuaki said gratefully.

However, the Curse Mark of the Tongue that Danzo Shimura had given him last time was an entirely new field for Senju Matsuaki.

Previously, the Ninjutsu he had learned were mostly Earth Release and Water Release, while his training outside of Ninjutsu focused on Kenjutsu and Taijutsu, occasionally involving some Genjutsu.

As for Sealing Jutsu, aside from the basic knowledge provided by the Ninja Academy courses, it was a complete blind spot for him.

« Not at all, you’ve helped us out quite a bit too, » Hiruzen Sarutobi said, then glanced at Danzo, « Danzo, don’t you agree ? »

Danzo Shimura nodded and said, « Hiruzen is right. Not just what happened earlier, you also did a great job with the task we entrusted to you last time. »

« You have a match tomorrow, so we won’t take up any more of your time. We’ll treat you to a meal after your assessment is over. »

As Hiruzen Sarutobi said this, he casually picked up a small paper cup placed on the cart.

It contained a brown liquid that was bubbling suspiciously.

Under normal circumstances, he might have suspected it was some kind of poison.

However, he had tasted the senju happy water that Konoha was giving away for free to spectators and staff during the day, and its unique flavor had left a deep impression on him.

Seeing that this liquid was quite similar to the happy water, except for being slightly lighter in color, Hiruzen Sarutobi drank it all in one gulp.

Seeing Hiruzen Sarutobi down the liquid, Danzo Shimura, who hadn’t felt thirsty, suddenly felt his mouth go dry and picked up another cup, drinking it in one gulp as well.

« Wait, this... »

Watching their actions, Senju Matsuaki was stunned for a moment. When he realized what was happening, his pupils constricted sharply, and he took a step forward, reaching out to stop them.

Unfortunately, it was one step too late.

Looking at the empty paper cups in their hands, he fell silent and slowly lowered his outstretched hand.

Hiruzen Sarutobi and Danzo Shimura exchanged glances, both smacking their lips with strange expressions.

After getting the taste, they realized something was wrong without needing Senju Matsuaki to remind them.

Hiruzen Sarutobi asked curiously, « Danzo, why does this senju happy water taste different from the one during the day ? Did you pour it just now ? »

Danzo Shimura frowned and said, « No, I thought you were thirsty and poured it out. »

« If it wasn’t you or me, then that means... »

The two of them looked simultaneously at Senju Matsuaki, who had a complicated expression on his face.

« I didn’t pour it either, » Senju Matsuaki sighed.

« The things I brought earlier are over there. »

He pointed to the side. On the corridor outside Hiruzen Sarutobi’s house were the two boxes of senju happy water that Danzo Shimura had taken from him and set down earlier.

Hearing this, Hiruzen Sarutobi and Danzo Shimura both felt a bad premonition.

« These paper cups are the ones we used for our experiment earlier. The liquid inside is... »

As Senju Matsuaki added this, their faces instantly turned the color of pig liver.

« Oh no, Hiruzen ! ! Spit it out quickly ! ! »

Danzo Shimura reacted quickly, grabbing Hiruzen Sarutobi’s arm and rushing towards the restroom.

A series of retching sounds came from afar, occasionally accompanied by the sound of objects and liquids falling into the basin.

Just as Senju Matsuaki was about to go forward and check, the sliding door of the Sarutobi house was suddenly pulled open from the inside.

Sarutobi Sasuke, looking sleepy and rubbing his eyes, walked out of the house with a confused expression.

« What the hell is that racket ? ? »

Sarutobi Sasuke had been a bit tired during the day, so he had gone to bed early, but the retching sounds had woken him up directly.

« Matsuaki, why are you here ? Are you here to see Hiruzen and the others ? »

After widening his eyes, Sarutobi Sasuke noticed a silver-haired, red-eyed boy standing in his yard and asked curiously.

Sarutobi Sasuke was Hiruzen Sarutobi’s father, a Ninja from the same era as Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama, and the head of the Sarutobi Clan.

During the Warring States period, the Sarutobi Clan was one of the major clans, second only to the Senju Clan and Uchiha Clan, and Sarutobi Sasuke had achieved great fame.

It was just that in Konoha, Senju Hashirama, Uchiha Madara, and Senju Tobirama were too dazzling, overshadowing him to a certain extent.

« Good evening, Uncle Sasuke. The thing is... »

Senju Matsuaki greeted him first, then described what had just happened briefly with a strange expression.

Now, the strange expression shifted from Senju Matsuaki’s face to Sarutobi Sasuke’s.

« You have the Chunin Exams tomorrow, so go back and rest first. I’ll look after those two. »

Sarutobi Sasuke scratched his head in distress, seemingly pondering how to deal with these two kids who had drunk a strange potion, vomited a mess, and would likely have other strange reactions.

However, out of hospitality, he still asked Senju Matsuaki to go back and rest first.

« But, this... »

Senju Matsuaki felt a bit guilty.

Although it was Hiruzen Sarutobi and Danzo Shimura’s carelessness that led them to drink the potion from the two paper cups, he still bore some responsibility.

« It’s fine, it’s fine, it’s just a small problem. They can handle it themselves, » Sarutobi Sasuke said with a smile.

For a Ninja, this was indeed not a big problem.

Firstly, it wasn’t a highly toxic substance, and secondly, it didn’t take effect immediately, so they should be able to minimize the effects after inducing vomiting.

Unable to refuse Sarutobi Sasuke’s kindness, Senju Matsuaki said goodbye and left Hiruzen Sarutobi’s house.

Senju Matsuaki estimated the time ; it was about time for most people to rest.

Since that was the case, he gave up the idea of going to spar with Homura Mitokado and the others, and headed directly towards the Senju Clan grounds.

He was at the age where he was still growing, so getting enough sleep was still very necessary.

Walking on the road, the lively crowds gradually dispersed, and the wide road seemed a bit deserted.

Moonlight spilled onto the ground, echoing the faint light from the street lamps, illuminating the path ahead.

After walking only a few steps, a notification sound suddenly rang in Senju Matsuaki’s mind.

He instinctively checked and found a notification that 700 points had been credited to his account.

500 points for a Jonin, 200 points for a Chunin, totaling exactly 700. It was clearly corresponding to Hiruzen Sarutobi and Danzo Shimura respectively.

« ... »

Senju Matsuaki was filled with complex emotions for a moment, not knowing what expression to make.

Deducing from this result, it seemed that the situation where Hiruzen Sarutobi and Danzo Shimura were knocked out by the potion he had concocted was also counted as him defeating them.

Although this was absurd, thinking about it carefully, it seemed to have some rationality.

After all, poisoning was one of the compulsory courses for Ninjas. Even if most Ninjas were not as proficient in this as Chiyo or Senju Tsunade, they still understood some methods in this regard.

It was just that this result made one feel a bit between laughter and tears.

It was getting late. After arriving home, Senju Matsuaki washed up and went to rest, his consciousness sinking into dreamland as he had a good sleep.
