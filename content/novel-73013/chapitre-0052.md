# Chapitre 52 — Unexpected Candidate List

Looking at the worried expressions of his teammates beside him, Matsumi smiled easily.

« Rest assured, we will be staying in Konoha for the next period, so these bounty hunters won’t have a chance to strike. »

Among the bounty hunters who take on bounty missions at the Underground Exchange, there are certainly some skilled individuals. The three weaklings from before, who couldn’t even trigger a system mission, were just bottom-tier fodder.

But as he said, he would not be leaving Konoha for some time.

No matter how bold these bounty hunters might be, they wouldn’t dare enter Konoha to attack Konoha Ninjas.

And by the time the Chunin Exams are over, Matsumi didn’t even know what level his own strength would have developed to, so there was even less to fear.

Those rough brutes from the Hidden Cloud Village are not being honest...

Matsumi had a preliminary guess in his mind about who issued the bounty.

Although both the Hidden Cloud Village and the Uchiha were suspects, the former was more suspicious.

Ever since Uchiha Kagami warned him that he might be in danger, there hadn’t been any movement.

However, not long after the Hidden Cloud Village visited Konoha last time, he faced an assassination attempt.

But Matsumi didn’t take this matter to heart ; there were more important things to do right now.

After returning to the Village to turn in this mission, the next thing would be the unprecedented Joint Chunin Exams.

As the strongest Genin among those from Konoha participating in the exam, he carried the hopes of the entire Village and had no time to play around with these bounty hunters.

...

Konoha, inside the Hokage Office.

Senju Hashirama sat at the desk, while Senju Tobirama also brought a chair and sat on the side, with stacks of documents on the table.

« It seems the efficiency of each Village is very high ; they’ve already compiled and sent over the registration forms so quickly. »

Senju Hashirama happily took a stack of registration forms, glanced at the thickness, weighed them, and then placed them back on the table.

« Not bad, it seems these Ninja Villages take this Chunin Exams very seriously. »

Senju Tobirama also expressed approval of the other Villages' high efficiency, a look of satisfaction appearing in his eyes.

The earlier these registration forms were sent to Konoha, the more time they would have for preparation and processing, and there would be no need for miserable overtime.

However, after flipping through them for only a short while, Senju Tobirama’s expression became a bit strange.

He held a registration form in both hands, his brows furrowed slightly, and after examining it again, he put the form down with a grim face.

« Big Brother, look at this registration form from Kumogakure. »

Senju Tobirama handed the registration form in his hand to Senju Hashirama with a sullen face, while signaling him to pay attention to the name and information on the form.

« Huh ? »

Taking a look at the name on the registration form, Senju Hashirama immediately noticed something wrong, and when he looked further down at the summary of the other party’s mission history, his expression became even weirder.

The registration form had a very short name written on it — « Dou ».

Senju Hashirama had an impression of this name ; it was the big young man who came with the Kumogakure delegation last time, who already possessed impressive strength at the same age as Monkey and Danzo.

At first, he thought it was just a name coincidence ; after all, the names in Kumogakure were not quite the same as those in Konoha.

But when he saw the mission history below, he went silent.

To avoid leaking secrets, this part of the mission history only required writing down the number of completed missions ; there was no need to describe any details.

However, those participating in the Chunin Exams were generally Genin, and everyone basically only competed on the number of C-rank and D-rank missions.

A few Genin had undertaken B-rank missions, but those were only special cases in rare situations.

Yet, on this registration form belonging to Dou that Senju Hashirama held, the number of completed B-rank missions was even higher than that of some Chunin.

What was even more outrageous was that the number of completed A-rank missions was actually quite high as well, with dozens of them.

With such a mission history, if you said you were here to participate in the Chunin Exams, someone who didn’t know better would think that a Jonin assessment was about to take place.

« There is a line of text here, hiss... »

Noticing that there seemed to be a remark below the mission history, Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama took a closer look and both of their faces darkened simultaneously.

Below Dou’s mission history, there was a line of small text, the gist of which was that he had been demoted to Genin for violating the Hidden Cloud Village’s Ninja management regulations.

Are they treating people from other Villages like fools ?

However, the two of them carefully recalled that it seemed the registration rules jointly formulated by everyone for this Joint Chunin Exams really didn’t prohibit such « Genin » from participating in the assessment.

Moreover, this move by the Hidden Cloud Village was also very rigorous.

Although this Chunin Exams didn’t prohibit Genin in such special situations from participating, it did stipulate that a Ninja’s mission history must not be concealed.

To avoid being reported by other Ninja Villages with this as an excuse and being disqualified, the mission history section on the registration form in front of them was filled out truthfully.

Those rough brutes from the Hidden Cloud Village are not being honest...

Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama looked at each other and both shook their heads and sighed.

However, since things had reached this point, it wouldn’t be good for them to change the rules on the fly to plug this loophole, lest they give others grounds for criticism.

The only comforting thing was that the Hidden Cloud Village seemed to only have this one case.

« The Hidden Cloud Village is indeed being underhanded by doing this, but the people of the Hidden Sand Village are simple and honest ; surely such a thing won’t happen there. »

Senju Hashirama quickly put Kumogakure’s cunning behind him and smilingly picked up the stack of registration forms from the Hidden Sand Village in the Land of Wind.

He had also met some Ninjas from the Land of Wind ; because material conditions there were poor, many Ninjas maintained a simple lifestyle and possessed resilient qualities.

Speaking of this, Senju Hashirama picked up the registration form on top.

This registration form belonged to a Genin from the Hidden Sand Village named « Yan ».

« Uh, why does this name sound a bit familiar too ? Wait... A-rank missions three times ! ? »

Senju Hashirama widened his eyes, having a feeling of being backstabbed by the guys from the Land of Wind.

Those people from the Hidden Cloud Village have dark hearts, so it’s one thing for them to do this, but you, Hidden Sand Village, with your thick eyebrows and big eyes, are doing this too ? Are you all acting like backstabbers ?

« Heh, I have an impression of this name too. »

Senju Tobirama, who was on the side, glanced at the name, thought for a few seconds, then showed a look of realization, followed by a cold sneer.

« Yan, a genius Ninja of the young generation of the Hidden Sand Village, possesses the Bloodline Limit Magnet Release, and is also Shamon’s direct disciple. »

« It seems he is also an incredible young man. »

Upon hearing this, Senju Hashirama also remembered this genius Ninja from the Hidden Sand Village, and his expression became somewhat grave.

He had some understanding of the important personnel of the Hidden Sand Village ; Shamon was the second-in-command of the Hidden Sand Village after Reto, almost certainly the next Kazekage.

And judging from Yan’s configuration, he was probably being cultivated with the goal of becoming the future Third Kazekage.

« Of course, back then I even wanted to send people... cough cough. »

Senju Tobirama, who almost let it slip, hurriedly coughed a few times, his expression becoming slightly unnatural.

Back then, when this Magnet Release Ninja from the Hidden Sand Village first emerged in the Ninja World, he had wanted to send people to assassinate him in advance while he hadn’t grown up yet, but unfortunately, the Hidden Sand Village protected him too well.

However, he didn’t dare to let his Big Brother know about this ; after all, Senju Hashirama had always favored peace, and such an assassination would count as actively provoking conflict.
