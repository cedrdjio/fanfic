# Chapitre 165 — Archaic Tachi! The Race of the Five Great Ninja Villages!

Konoha’s work on collecting intelligence about the Tailed Beasts was in full swing.

However, the pace of Senju Matsuaki’s life suddenly slowed down.

In 24 hours, Kirby, whom he had summoned not long ago, would return to its original world.

Before its departure, Senju Matsuaki treated it to plenty of food and fun within Konoha.

Finally, Kirby waved its short arms reluctantly, bidding farewell to Senju Matsuaki and the others.

Additionally...

Senju Matsuaki checked the notification that had popped up after he repelled Uchiha Madara not long ago.

[S-Rank mission completed : Assist Senju Hashirama in repelling Uchiha Madara. Reward : 10,000 points !]

Years of accumulation, combined with this massive influx of points, instantly pushed his stockpiled points to another peak.

At this moment, the Golden Finger platform gave him another huge surprise.

Among the top promotional items, a collection item currently on sale at a 50% discount caught Senju Matsuaki’s attention.

That archaic Tachi made his eyes burst with fiery light.

...

The matter of the Tailed Beasts soon stirred up visible waves among the Five Great Nations.

Although Uchiha Madara’s attempt to control the Nine-Tailed to attack Konoha ultimately failed, the terrifying momentum of that day still drew the attention of countless people.

The massive body, the terrifying power, the awe-inspiring Tailed Beast Ball...

Every Ninja immediately labeled the Tailed Beasts as « Weapons of War » !

When news of Konoha’s intent to capture the Tailed Beasts reached the major Ninja Villages through secret channels, no one was willing to fall behind in this gamble concerning the future of their Villages.

A silent race began.

Hidden Sand Village, Kazekage’s office.

The scorching sunlight filtered through the curtains, casting mottled shadows on the sand.

First Kazekage Retto frowned deeply.

« Konoha’s movements are faster than we imagined. »

He looked at his two successors beside him, « Shamon, Yan, what do you think ? »

Shamon said in a deep voice, « Spies have sent word that Konoha has already begun preparations for intelligence gathering and capturing the Tailed Beasts. »

« But we have yet to learn of Konoha’s true intent for capturing the Tailed Beasts... »

« The intention of collecting such weapons of war is self-evident ! »

Sitting below, the young future Third Kazekage Yan raised his eyes and said in a deep voice, « It is nothing more than wanting to use the power of these monsters to start a war ! »

Hearing this, Shamon also nodded in agreement, « Senju Hashirama calls for peace on the surface, but I did not expect him to have such ambitious actions ! »

Senju Hashirama did not know that he had been forced to have his « persona collapse » once, silently.

But whether it was First Kazekage Retto, Shamon, or Yan, they all undoubtedly believed that Senju Hashirama’s motives were malicious.

Otherwise, could it be that he caught them to give away to other Ninja Villages for free ?

« Kazekage-sama, I don’t think we can sit and wait for death. Why don’t we strike first ! »

Shamon said in a deep voice.

« Correct. »

A flash of light appeared in Retto’s eyes, « Ichibi is a scourge to our Sunagakure, but it should also become our shield ! »

« Yes ! » x2

...

Hidden Cloud Village, Raikage Building.

Thunder rumbled among the mountains, reflecting the heavy atmosphere in the room.

First Raikage A looked at the intelligence on the scroll in his hand, grinned, revealing a mouthful of white teeth, but there was no smile in his eyes.

« Eight-Tails... has finally appeared within our territory. »

Beside him, the burly Second Raikage said in a deep voice.

« Although we don’t know Konoha’s intent for capturing the Tailed Beasts, if all the Tailed Beasts fall into Konoha’s hands, it is definitely not a good thing. »

« Hmph, wanting to come into our Land of Lightning to capture the Tailed Beast, it’s not that easy ! »

First Raikage waved his hand, « We will strike first to capture the Eight-Tails ! With the Eight-Tails, plus our Kumogakure’s Lightning Release, who else could be our opponent ! »

« Also... »

A flash of light appeared in First Raikage’s eyes, « If we act quickly, the Tailed Beasts residing in other countries might also be ours for the taking ! »

...

Iwagakure, inside the library of the Tsuchikage Building.

Under the dim light, First Tsuchikage Ishikawa was lost in thought while facing a yellowed ancient book.

It recorded in ancient text a Four-Tails ape with a huge body, red fur, and a body burning with Chakra flames.

« Four-Tails... »

Ishikawa stroked his rock-like cheeks, making a dull sound, « History tells us that only the power grasped in one’s own hands is real. »

He turned his head and looked at his successor standing respectfully behind him, « From today on, Iwagakure will fully collect intelligence on the Tailed Beasts and begin preparations to capture the Four-Tails ! »

« Yes, Tsuchikage-sama ! However, about Konoha... »

« Hmph, Konoha ? »

Ishikawa snorted coldly, the rock floor under his hand trembling slightly, « Senju Hashirama is indeed strong, but the Tailed Beasts are ownerless objects. As long as we are one step faster, can they snatch the Tailed Beasts back from us ? »

« If they are truly that forceful, the remaining four Great Ninja Villages joining forces will not fear this God of Shinobi ! »

...

Hidden Mist Village, Mizukage Building.

The damp air was filled with a heavy atmosphere.

First Mizukage Byakuren looked at the intelligence report that had just been delivered to the table.

A rough portrait depicted a giant with three giant tails, carrying a huge turtle shell, stirring up huge waves in the sea.

« Finally found you... »

Byakuren’s voice was hoarse, like two wet rags rubbing against each other.

Behind him, several Hidden Mist Elders had gloomy expressions.

« Mizukage-sama, the Three-Tails is powerful, and ordinary Ninjas are probably unable to deal with it. »

« It doesn’t matter. »

Byakuren said without hesitation, « This time, I will personally take action. »

« The Three-Tails must be obtained. Hidden Mist Village stands on an island ; if the Three-Tails is controlled by other Ninja Villages, it will be a weapon of mass destruction against us ! »

He stood up, his pale fingers brushing over the sea area on the map.

« Although the Three-Tails is powerful, this old bone of mine is not afraid of it ! »

« Let me, make one last contribution to the Hidden Mist. »

...

Konoha Village, Hokage Building.

Senju Hashirama had two newly delivered intelligence scrolls spread out in front of him, his face showing a mix of excitement and gravity.

« The Ichibi is active in the desert area at the border between the Land of Rivers and the Land of Wind. »

He pointed to the map on the left, « The Three-Tails frequently appears in the deep water area of the northern sea of the Land of Water. »

Tobirama stood to the side with his arms crossed, « Big brother, the desert and the sea, these two places are not exactly close. »

« Furthermore, other Ninja Villages have also started racing with us. »

« I know, so we should speed up even more. »

Senju Hashirama nodded, his gaze firm, « The power of the Tailed Beasts is too dangerous. Leaving them alone will only make countries scramble to seize them, ultimately leading to greater wars. »

He looked up at the prosperous street scene of Konoha outside the window, « Instead of letting them become the fuse for war, it is better for us to control this power. »

« Then, how should the capture plan be arranged ? » Tobirama asked.

« Leave the Ichibi to me and Matsuaki. »

Senju Hashirama patted the table, « As for the Three-Tails, you lead the Sealing Team to the sea to capture it. »

He looked at Tobirama, « Although the Three-Tails is strong, your Flying Thunder God Technique combined with Water Release Ninjutsu should be able to complete the capture. »

Tobirama was silent for a moment, then finally nodded, « Understood. »

Senju Hashirama grinned, « Then it’s settled. In three days, we set off separately ! »
