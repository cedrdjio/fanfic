# Chapitre 131 — Tremor-Tremor Fruit VS Lightning Release Chakra Mode!

Limited by factors such as age, Senju Matsuaki’s current height is merely outstanding among his peers.

Compared to a hulking, gifted individual like Dou, he appears somewhat « slender. »

Yet, the moment Senju Matsuaki prepared to exert his strength,

in the eyes of everyone, it was as if Senju Matsuaki was the more imposing and towering one of the two.

His posture and Aura were like those of a demon god !

In comparison, Dou of Kumogakure actually seemed somewhat frail ?

« Air Quake ! ! »

Senju Matsuaki threw a punch that gathered all his strength and the power of the Tremor-Tremor Fruit, swinging it forward in the shocked gaze of Dou.

The white sphere of light held in Matsuaki’s left hand struck heavily against the air.

The next instant.

The earth and space cracked open.

Crackle—

Crackle—

The ground, which the Ninjas had specially reinforced with Earth Release, and the space that ordinary people could never grasp.

It was now shattering like a mirror.

Wisps of cracks carrying an Aura of destruction spread toward where Dou was located.

« What on earth is this ? »

Dou’s pupils constricted, a sense of palpitation arising spontaneously.

Unlike looking from afar on the stands, standing on this exam field and facing it directly, he was truly shocked by the overbearing and savage nature of this power.

However, Dou soon noticed something amiss.

Compared to the semifinals, although this punch from Senju Matsuaki was equally terrifying, it seemed to be inferior in momentum and destructive power.

« It seems that the power of this strength during the semifinals is not so easy to achieve. »

Dou narrowed his eyes, discerning the reason behind it.

That’s right, if a casual strike could have that kind of power, this kid from the Senju Clan would truly be an out-and-out monster.

While his thoughts raced, Dou didn’t just stand there.

Lightning blossomed all over his body, Lightning Release Chakra stimulating his knotted muscles, greatly increasing his cell activity.

Amidst the flashing lightning, his sturdy body vanished from its original spot, dashing away.

Lightning scorched a charred semi-circular arc into the ground ; Dou bypassed the vibration power that shattered everything and arrived behind Senju Matsuaki.

Senju Matsuaki twisted his wrist, swinging his short blade, which was condensed with monstrous strength and vibration power, toward his back.

However, the moment Senju Matsuaki swung the blade, Dou flashed to the side, then suddenly threw a punch toward Senju Matsuaki’s chest.

A punch carrying lightning, even if it did not yet have the momentum of the « Strongest Spear, » was not something that could be easily blocked.

Senju Matsuaki reacted just as quickly, clenching his left fist again ; the vibration power caused the air around his fist to emit low hums.

« Hmph ! »

Dou didn’t dodge or evade ; instead, his eyes seemed to ignite with high spirits as he suddenly threw out his lightning-covered fist.

The next instant, the fist wrapped in vibration power and the fist wrapped in lightning collided with a boom.

The power of the vibration caused both their hair to stand on end, the ground to sink, and the surging lightning made it impossible to open one’s eyes.

The burning and numbing sensation coming from his fist made Senju Matsuaki frown slightly.

Dou’s expression was not looking good either.

Perhaps it was because Senju Matsuaki’s previous performance in Ninjutsu and the power of vibration were too astonishing, causing him to overlook the opponent’s physical strength.

He was now astonished to discover that the immense strength he had painstakingly forged over many years was now on par with Senju Matsuaki’s.

At the same time, that vibration power also caused the lightning armor wrapped around his fist to shatter inch by inch, and even his internal organs were affected, with a faint feeling of shifting.

« Slash ! »

Senju Matsuaki did not sit and wait for death, exerting force with his right hand holding the blade, hacking toward Dou’s neck.

Dou, unwilling to show weakness, raised his other hand into a hand-blade shape, blocking Senju Matsuaki’s short blade.

For a moment, the two were deadlocked.

Persistence is for the sake of a Breakthrough ; if they remained in a stalemate, holding on stubbornly would be meaningless.

Therefore, after lasting for a few seconds, the two retreated backward simultaneously.

Senju Matsuaki glanced at his left hand without leaving a trace.

Just now, clashing fists with Dou had left traces burned by some overflowing lightning.

However, under his powerful regenerative ability, the charred skin gradually peeled off, revealing new, fair, and tender skin.

« Hahaha ! ! »

After escaping the attack range of the Tremor-Tremor Fruit, Dou couldn’t help but laugh three times.

After several probes, although he still held vigilance in his heart, the feeling of dread had subsided by seventy or eighty percent.

Dou said loudly : « Just as I expected, you probably haven’t learned this strange technique for long, right ? »

« Oh ? »

Senju Matsuaki raised an eyebrow, wanting to see what high opinion Dou had.

« Stiff, unrefined, having excess ferocity but insufficient agility ; I’m afraid you can only exert three parts of your ten parts of strength. » Dou commented unceremoniously.

« If you are like this, what else could it be but having just learned this technique ? »

Dou remembered that it was the same when he first learned the Lightning Release Chakra Mode back then.

At that time, barely maintaining the formation of Lightning Release Chakra was already difficult, and his control could be said to be disastrous.

When sprinting, he couldn’t brake and crashed into rocks ; when throwing a punch, excessive force caused his body to lose balance and fall to the ground...

These were all disastrous dark histories.

At this moment, Senju Matsuaki’s technique, which seemed capable of shattering everything, also gave him this feeling.

Therefore, he immediately understood that Senju Matsuaki had only just learned this technique recently.

« I am not that idiot from Sunagakure, Yan, who collided with you head-on. »

« If you truly mastered this technique, I’m afraid I would not be your opponent. »

« But now, in my opinion, you have too many flaws. »

As he spoke, Dou clenched his fists, and lightning rampaged around his body.

« Too many flaws ? »

Senju Matsuaki did not get angry upon hearing Dou’s belittling words.

Aside from the tone intentionally used to suppress the opponent’s spirit during battle, in a sense, what the other party said was also a fact.

He had just obtained the power of the Tremor-Tremor Fruit for less than a day.

Being able to achieve this level already showed extraordinary talent.

Being considered to have too many flaws was not unacceptable.

« This technique has too many flaws ? »

Senju Matsuaki was not affected by Dou’s words, but instead smiled as he put away his short blade.

But when Dou saw this smile on Senju Matsuaki’s face, he, who had been laughing heartily, could no longer laugh.

For some reason, a chill rose in Dou’s heart.

Senju Matsuaki said with a smile : « In that case, I have a more proficient technique that I learned a long time ago ; do you want to try it ? »

« Bluffing ! »

Dou snorted coldly, but for some reason, his body still tensed up subconsciously.

Intuition told him that things seemed to be developing in a direction he had not anticipated again.

Senju Matsuaki did not say anything more at this moment, forming hand seals in front of him.

At the same time, a surging Chakra rose from his Dantian, and an Aura like a vast ocean instantly swept outward from him as the center.

The fine stones on the ground were all blown away under this Aura.

On the stands.

Senju Hashirama, who was thirsty and drinking water, watched Senju Matsuaki’s hand-sealing movements on the field.

Those inexplicably familiar hand seals made him feel strangely familiar.

Pfft—

After realizing it, he widened his eyes instantly, and the water he had in his mouth sprayed out.

« Wait, this is ! ? »
