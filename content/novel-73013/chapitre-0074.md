# Chapitre 74 — Crisis!

« There is actually no curse mark on your forehead. Are you from the Hyuga Main Branch ? »

Yan glanced at Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s smooth forehead and made his judgment with a slightly uncertain tone.

The Hidden Sand Village had some intelligence on the Hyuga Clan, a major clan.

The Hyuga Clan was divided into the Main Branch and the Branch Family. Members of the Branch Family had the Caged Bird Seal cast on their foreheads, while members of the Main Branch did not.

The corpses of the Hyuga Clan’s Branch Family were meaningless to enemies because the moment they died, their Byakugan would be destroyed by the Caged Bird Seal.

As for the Hyuga Clan’s Main Branch, they had always been well-protected by the Branch Family, and instances of Byakugan being lost were extremely rare.

Upon hearing this, a trace of sorrow surfaced in the depths of Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s eyes.

Main Branch, Branch Family...

As she grew older, she gradually realized that this was a curse that the Branch Family of the Hyuga Clan could never escape.

However, the reason given by the Main Branch for the Caged Bird Seal seemed quite reasonable.

To prevent the Byakugan from being coveted by outsiders.

And at this moment, the undisguised greed in the eyes of Yan from the Hidden Sand Village served as proof of the reasonableness of this excuse.

That’s right ; at this moment, Yan had already set his sights on this pair of Byakugan.

In the primeval forest, even Konoha could not monitor everything.

If he took the eyes and destroyed the body, he wouldn’t leave any evidence, and no one would know that it was Sunagakure that had seized this pair of Byakugan.

Of course, after seizing this pair of Byakugan, Sunagakure would not use them openly right away, so as to avoid inviting retaliation from Konoha and Senju Hashirama.

When a day came in the future when Konoha lost Senju Hashirama, their pillar of strength, and the Ninja World was once again plunged into war.

This pair of Byakugan would become a secret weapon for Sunagakure.

« Tsukuyomi, hurry up and crush the piece ! ! »

Biwako’s face showed a look of struggle, but in the end, she made up her mind, stepped in front of Hyuga Tsukuyomi, and wore a look of determination.

« We absolutely cannot let the Byakugan fall into the hands of Sunagakure. As long as we crush the piece, the Village will notice the situation here, and he won’t be able to continue attacking. »

After saying this, she took out the blank piece from her bosom, intending to crush it.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi followed suit, taking her piece in her hand, ready to crush it the next moment.

The Chunin Exams could be taken again ; they could just wait another half a year.

But life only happens once ; once it’s gone, it’s gone.

And from a strategic perspective, the importance of the Byakugan was even greater than life itself.

Once Sunagakure obtained the Byakugan, the strategic significance of the Byakugan would be diminished whenever Konoha stood in opposition to them in the future.

« Naive and ridiculous. »

Seeing their actions, Yan was not panicked at all ; instead, he laughed mockingly.

He wore a cruel expression as he quickly raised his right hand in front of his chest.

A pool of black Iron Sand shot out, as if transforming into a giant black hand, directly gripping Biwako, who was blocking the front of Hyuga Tsukuyomi.

« If you crush that piece, I’ll crush her ! »

Yan’s expression was cold as he threatened them concisely.

« Don’t worry about me, hurry up and crush the piece ! ! »

Feeling the tightening pressure around her arms, Biwako gritted her teeth and shouted.

However, Hyuga Tsukuyomi did not crush the piece as Biwako had hoped, nor did she surrender and hand over the Byakugan as Yan had expected.

Instead, she raised her free hand in front of her eyes, her fair palm turning toward her own pair of pure Byakugan.

« If you continue to hurt my companion, I won’t give these eyes to you, even if I have to destroy them ! »

Hyuga Tsukuyomi looked up, meeting Yan’s cold gaze with a stubborn look.

Seeing the Chakra gathering in Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s hand and roughly gauging the speed of both sides, Yan’s expression became increasingly ugly.

He could judge that before he could stop her from self-destructing the Byakugan, the Chakra in her palm would be able to destroy the eyes.

In that case, he would fail to obtain the Byakugan and would still have to take the risk of offending the Hyuga Clan and Konoha, which would be completely counterproductive.

Thinking of this, he couldn’t help but control the tightening Iron Sand to slowly relax, giving Biwako a chance to breathe.

« Tsukuyomi... »

Biwako’s face showed a look of being moved, and for a moment, she couldn’t speak.

She had originally prepared herself mentally that Hyuga Tsukuyomi would choose to crush the piece to preserve herself and the Byakugan.

In that scenario, she might be crushed by the enraged enemy, but Konoha’s Bloodline Limit would not leak out.

But she hadn’t expected that the other party would choose to threaten self-destruction of the Byakugan for the sake of her life.

For a Bloodline Limit Ninja, the importance of the Bloodline Limit was perhaps no less than life itself.

The three of them fell into a stalemate for a moment.

Yan controlled Biwako but dared not take any further action.

Biwako’s brain kept thinking of various ways to escape, yet the huge gap in strength left her helpless.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi maintained the posture of wanting to self-destruct the Byakugan, while simultaneously staring fixedly at Yan.

The three of them were waiting for the right opportunity, waiting for one side to reveal a flaw first.

« The scent left by the target is getting stronger and stronger ; it seems they stayed nearby not long ago. »

Inuzuka Rei moved between branches on all fours, her movements amazingly synchronized with the Ninja Dog beside her.

With the enhancement of the Four Legs Technique, her speed was greatly increased, and her coordination with the Ninja Dog was even more tacit.

Beside the human and the dog, Senju Matsuaki traveled at a speed not inferior to theirs.

« You’ve really worked hard ; thank you so much. »

Watching the focused and serious appearance of Inuzuka Rei and the Ninja Dog, Senju Matsuaki thanked them sincerely.

« It’s nothing ; you saved our lives, and you also prevented our Inuzuka Clan’s secret techniques from being stolen by Iwagakure. » Inuzuka Rei couldn’t easily turn her head, so she just said it nonchalantly.

« Woof~ » The Ninja Dog on the side also let out a gentle bark in agreement.

Although Inuzuka Rei considered that she would rather die than leak the family’s secret techniques, it was hard to guarantee that the enemy didn’t have a secret technique to obtain intelligence from corpses.

Senju Matsuaki said seriously : « You are a part of Konoha ; this is something I should do in the first place. »

« How reliable~ »

Unable to resist glancing back at Senju Matsuaki, Inuzuka Rei hurriedly turned her head back, muttering to herself.

The skin beneath the red facial markings on her cheeks seemed to have turned a little redder as well.

« However, the scent on the shuriken you gave us is too faint ; otherwise, our search speed could be even faster. »

Seemingly to change the subject, Inuzuka Rei went back to the serious business at hand.

At this moment, she was exerting the last bit of her remaining energy in this Chunin Exams to help Senju Matsuaki find someone.

Once she found the target, she planned to directly crush the piece to leave the exam grounds and try again in the next Chunin Exams.

However, the biggest obstacle at present was that the only thing Senju Matsuaki could provide was a shuriken with a faint scent, which greatly delayed the search progress.

Fortunately, there were some intersections of paths during the process from various starting points to the finish line ; otherwise, searching would truly be like looking for a needle in a haystack.

Regarding this, Senju Matsuaki also felt helpless.

He and Hyuga Tsukuyomi were only teammates ; how could he possibly carry items with her scent on him ?

Wouldn’t that make him a creep ?

The reason why this shuriken had the other party’s scent on it was because it was something the two of them had picked out together at a Ninja tool shop not long ago.

Fortunately, after a period of searching, they had locked onto the approximate trajectory.

As long as they kept following the trajectory, they would eventually meet.

« There are traces of burnt wood over there ! »

Inuzuka Rei sniffed ; the Inuzuka Clan’s secret technique allowed her to concentrate Chakra on her nose, making her sense of smell ten thousand times stronger than an ordinary person’s.

Therefore, when passing by, this distinct scent immediately entered her nose, causing her to frown subconsciously.

She hurriedly withdrew the Chakra from her nose, which made her feel much more comfortable.
