# Chapitre 28 — Uchiha Kagami's Warning

Having resolved the issue with Hiruzen Sarutobi, Matsumi continued walking towards the Senju Clan’s compound.

However, just after turning the corner, Matsumi’s expression suddenly became somewhat subtle.

« What is this ? »

He saw that just at the corner a few steps ahead of where he had been talking to Hiruzen just moments ago, five people were leaning against the wall with varied expressions : Danzo Shimura, Uchiha Kagami, Utatane Koharu, Akimichi Torifu, and Homura Mitokado.

Judging by their expressions, it was clear that they had already been there when Hiruzen Sarutobi was asking about his relationship problems.

The five of them were already experienced Ninjas, and since Hiruzen Sarutobi hadn’t been actively sensing his surroundings, he hadn’t noticed their presence.

« I didn’t expect Hiruzen to be struggling with this ; what a waste of time... » Danzo Shimura shook his head.

« I have other matters to attend to, so I’ll be going first. See you next time. »

In his youth, Danzo Shimura was indeed quite handsome, though his temperament leaned towards being gloomy ; however, he was actually quite polite.

Perhaps he really did have urgent business, as he bid farewell to the others, including Matsumi, and left first.

Noticing the confused expression on Matsumi’s face, the kind-hearted Akimichi Torifu spoke up to explain.

« We’ve noticed that Hiruzen has been absent-minded these past two days, but every time we ask him, he finds excuses to brush us off. »

« Just now, after we finished our mission, we noticed him sneaking off alone. We were worried about him, so we followed him here. »

« I didn’t expect it to be for this reason. Honestly... » Utatane Koharu felt speechless.

At around twelve years old, she was a young girl with delicate features, her long hair tied back, and half of her bangs hanging down, making her look quite capable.

However, after saying this, her gaze quietly lingered on Uchiha Kagami, who was standing to the side, for a moment.

« But this is also quite good ; at least Hiruzen isn’t actually in any real trouble, » Homura Mitokado said with a smile.

Hearing this, Uchiha Kagami sighed and said, « I just feel that for Hiruzen, this is already quite a significant challenge. »

« As long as Brother Hiruzen doesn’t make any mistakes, there shouldn’t be any problems. »

Knowing the future, Senju Matsuaki did not feel as worried as the others ; instead, he was optimistic.

However, he noticed that within these few minutes, Uchiha Kagami had glanced at him discreetly several times, wanting to say something but hesitating.

It seemed he wanted to tell him something, but because his companions were present, it was inconvenient to speak openly.

« Then let’s head back too. We just finished a mission and need to rest for a few days, » Akimichi Torifu said, stretching.

« Indeed, it’s getting late. »

Uchiha Kagami looked at the sky and suddenly reached out to pat Matsumi on the shoulder : « If you have time later, you are welcome to visit my home. »

This action couldn’t help but make Matsumi pause for a moment.

« Be careful... »

Just as the group was saying their goodbyes and leaving, a barely perceptible voice reached Matsumi’s ears.

Upon hearing this, Senju Matsuaki’s pupils constricted, and his face, which had previously worn a friendly smile, turned solemn.

This was Uchiha Kagami’s voice ; he had deliberately lagged a few steps behind his companions to leave this whispered warning.

Watching the other’s back, Matsumi wanted to step forward and ask, but after hesitating for a moment, he remained where he was.

The other party’s hidden and vague warning was perhaps not because it was inconvenient to speak openly, but because the information he himself knew was also limited.

If Uchiha Kagami clearly knew who wanted to harm him, he would only need to tell Senju Hashirama or Senju Tobirama.

He didn’t do so, perhaps because he wasn’t sure if the danger definitely existed, and therefore didn’t want to escalate matters based on mere speculation.

Recalling the invitation Uchiha Kagami had made while patting his shoulder before they parted, Matsumi felt more and more that something was wrong.

Logically speaking, their relationship wasn’t good enough for this, and Uchiha Kagami’s behavior of inviting Matsumi to his home was obviously somewhat abrupt.

But combined with his final warning, perhaps this invitation had a deeper meaning.

Perhaps Kagami meant that the person or thing he needed to be careful of came from the Uchiha Clan ?

And that group of Uchiha he had just met at the Village entrance...

Connecting this to what he had seen at the Village entrance earlier, Senju Matsuaki felt more and more that his speculation was very likely correct.

« Do some members of the Uchiha Clan want to harm me ? » Matsumi frowned, pondering in his heart.

« Could it be because of the graduation exam earlier, so they want revenge ? »

« Or is it just out of hostility towards the Senju Clan, that’s why... »

Although he didn’t know who held hostility towards him, it was clear that they wouldn’t make a move within the Village.

If some members of the Uchiha Clan were to attack him, and the matter was exposed, no one could withstand the fury of Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama.

Therefore, the only time the other party could take action was during the period when he was outside the Village executing missions.

« I need to get stronger quickly. »

Senju Matsuaki looked back ; the distant Hokage Building, as a landmark of Konoha, gave people a sense of security.

The Uchiha people didn’t dare to attack Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama, so they came to pick on him as a soft target ?

Then let them see who is harder !

After choosing a restaurant on the street in Konoha to eat, Matsumi went straight back to the Senju Clan’s compound.

After greeting his clansmen along the way, he returned to his own residence.

He opened the Golden Finger Requisition Platform and browsed the options within.

Previous missions and his usual accumulation had allowed his point total to suddenly break through five hundred.

However, he did not intend to abuse it, so today was the first time he had browsed this interface in a while.

The other content was roughly the same as before ; the difference was that there was an extra row of content at the very top of the interface that hadn’t appeared before.

« Promotional Options ? »

Looking at this line of text, Senju Matsuaki was shocked.

After looking closely, he soon understood the changes to his Golden Finger.

He saw that in the original top position of the platform interface, there was an extra row of five options, each of which was discounted compared to its original price.

These promotional options would refresh when a new day arrived, and both the content and the discount intensity would change.

But after a rough browse, Matsumi was left between laughter and tears.

Among these five promotional options, the only one currently useful to him was the first one.

A check-in type system that could originally be permanently requisitioned for 200,000 points now only required 140,000 points at a 30% discount.

Compared to the original price, it was 60,000 points cheaper !

But the real problem was that, for him right now, it wasn’t these 60,000 points that he was missing.

It was just like in his past life : wanting to buy a car worth millions, the dealer says there’s a discount, and then sends a 5,000 yuan coupon.

Was it really this 5,000 yuan that he was missing ?

The road ahead is long and arduous...

Senju Matsuaki shook his head and closed the interface.
