# Chapitre 99 — Senju Hashirama: The Outcome Remains Unpredictable

Just as the Iwagakure Ninja was about to fall into the pitch-black fissure, a Kunai flashing with a cold glint flew down from the air.

When the Kunai reached the Iwagakure Ninja, a white light flashed, and Senju Tobirama’s figure appeared in mid-air.

Reaching out and grabbing the Iwagakure Ninja as if he were carrying a puppy, Senju Tobirama’s figure vanished from the spot once again.

The next instant, he appeared at the viewing platform where he had been standing just a moment ago and placed the shaken Iwagakure Ninja on the ground.

If not for the fact that he was in a public place with everyone watching, he probably would have just tossed the Ninja from another Village onto the ground like a dead dog.

« Senju Matsuaki wins this match ! »

After Senju Tobirama announced the result of the battle, he nodded at Senju Matsuaki in the arena, then waved his hand to signal the staff to take the Iwagakure Ninja away.

Looking at the badly cracked ground, Senju Tobirama couldn’t help but feel a headache.

Even relying on Earth Release Ninjutsu to repair the ground, it would likely take some time.

Fortunately, Konoha had already prepared a contingency plan for this.

« Staff, clear the arena. The exams will continue in five minutes. »

After Senju Tobirama finished speaking, several Ninjas wearing Konoha headbands hurried into the arena from the gate at the edge, casting Earth Release Ninjutsu on the cracked ground.

And this time spent repairing the arena was not wasted.

On the spectator stands, a Konoha kunoichi spoke softly, her melodious voice resonating throughout the entire venue thanks to the amplification of Ninjutsu.

« Kidney deficiency sometimes comes after excessive fatigue... Ino–Shika–Chō brand Kidney Treasure drink, treats kidney deficiency, sugar-free ! ! »

« Thirsty ? Pursue quality as well ! ! Delicious and refreshing ! ! Please drink Senju Happy Water ! ! »

Taking advantage of this opportunity, the personnel already arranged by Konoha began to recite the advertisements.

In the arena, Senju Matsuaki quickly left the spot and walked toward the lounge.

The Iwagakure Ninja from just now had the strength of a Chunin, providing Senju Matsuaki with a reward of 200 points.

This mission was different from the last ; rewards were not settled uniformly after the exams concluded completely, but were distributed directly after the battle.

While walking up the steps, Senju Matsuaki’s mind was still replaying the scene of Senju Tobirama using the Flying Thunder God Technique just now.

Whether in terms of visual effects or practicality, this Ninjutsu was undoubtedly top-tier.

The only pity was that the threshold was too high, requiring the user to have a certain amount of space-time talent.

In the original timeline, the only two people who truly learned this Ninjutsu were Senju Tobirama and Minato Namikaze.

Shiranui Genma, Raido Namiashi, and Aoba Yamashiro, who had served as Minato Namikaze’s guards, could only use the Flying Thunder Formation Technique together with their team of two Special Jonin and one Chunin.

Compared to the Flying Thunder God Technique, the Flying Thunder Formation Technique was inferior in every aspect.

As for his own space-time talent, Senju Matsuaki wasn’t quite sure, as he hadn’t tested or attempted it.

After all, he still had a lot of room for improvement in Earth Release, Water Release, Wood Release, and swordsmanship, so he shouldn’t be too greedy.

Walking along the steps to the viewing platform outside the lounge, the first thing that caught Senju Matsuaki’s eyes was Hyuga Tsukuyomi, who was watching the match in the arena.

After exchanging greetings, the two walked into the lounge together.

On the way, they inevitably encountered the few Iwagakure Ninjas who had caused the conflict earlier.

Seeing Senju Matsuaki’s performance just now, these Iwagakure Ninjas couldn’t help but reveal a hint of fear on their faces.

Those who bully the weak are often afraid of the strong.

Previously, they had thought that Senju Matsuaki and Hyuga Tsukuyomi were on the younger side among the examinees and were soft persimmons that could be easily manipulated, which was why they had been so arrogant.

However, at this moment, they realized that Senju Matsuaki was not as easy to provoke as they had thought.

If not for the rules prohibiting fighting in the lounge, they would have likely fled this place long ago.

Senju Matsuaki didn’t say a word, just walked straight ahead without changing his expression.

The few Iwagakure Ninjas gritted their teeth, and in the end, with expressions of humiliation and resentment, they stepped aside to make way.

« Damn it ! ! »

Watching how Senju Matsuaki didn’t even look at them, the sleazy Iwagakure Ninja who had tried to grope Hyuga Tsukuyomi earlier still felt somewhat unwilling.

Especially seeing the cute girl he had targeted earlier walking obediently by another boy’s side with a look of admiration, jealousy flared up in his heart.

However, he was by no means hopelessly stupid.

Knowing that his companion, who was stronger than him, was no match for Senju Matsuaki, he knew he had even less of a chance of winning.

Five minutes later, the arena was restored to a level surface under the effect of Earth Release Ninjutsu.

In the spectator stands, many audience members holding Senju Happy Water perked up, waiting excitedly for the upcoming matches.

For most of the audience, the Chunin Exams before them were a spectacular performance they had never seen before.

Although the ticket price wasn’t cheap, just the battles so far were enough to make it worth the price.

« Now, the exams continue ! ! » Senju Tobirama’s voice echoed over the arena.

« According to the lottery results, the two sides for the next battle are Konoha’s Hyuga Tsukuyomi and Iwagakure’s... »

Following the lottery results drawn by the Daimyos and Kages while the arena was being repaired, Senju Tobirama announced the contestants for the next battle.

Coincidentally, the match immediately following Senju Matsuaki’s was Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s turn to take the stage.

And even more coincidentally, Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s opponent turned out to be the very same sleazy Iwagakure Ninja who had started the trouble earlier.

« Good luck, let those Iwagakure Ninjas see your true strength. »

Senju Matsuaki patted Hyuga Tsukuyomi on the shoulder, giving her encouragement with a smile.

As for feeling worried, Senju Matsuaki didn’t have any at all.

Although she also possessed Chunin-level strength, he had seen Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s progress with his own eyes.

Even that so-called strongest Iwagakure Ninja among the examinees, whom he had just defeated, probably wouldn’t be a match for Hyuga Tsukuyomi right now.

Let alone this even lamer, sleazy Iwagakure Ninja.

In the spectator stands, First Tsuchikage Ishikawa looked at his old hands with some annoyance.

« Sigh, bad luck. Our Village’s examinee actually drew an opponent from the Hyuga Clan. »

Hearing this, Senju Hashirama kindly comforted him : « That child, Tsukuyomi, only became a Genin this year and is still quite young ; the outcome remains unpredictable. »

These polite words meant to comfort someone were, in fact, just words to be listened to.

As her supervising Jonin, he had always kept an eye on Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s progress.

To be honest, the speed of Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s progress made even him feel surprised.

It was just that Senju Hashirama saw that First Tsuchikage Ishikawa and the Daimyo of the Land of Earth seemed depressed due to the consecutive lottery results, so he spoke up to comfort them.

Unexpectedly, the polite words that were just meant to be heard were taken seriously by the Tsuchikage.

A gleam of hope appeared in First Tsuchikage Ishikawa’s eyes, and he said : « I didn’t expect the Hokage to be so well-informed about the growth of the Genin in our Village ; it is truly admirable. »

« Not at all, not at all. After all, she is also my student. »

After being praised, Senju Hashirama scratched his head in embarrassment and said with a smile.

« What ! ? »

First Tsuchikage Ishikawa’s eyes widened, and his expression, which had just eased up, instantly became stiff again.

My Village’s mediocre Iwagakure Ninja candidate is fighting your student, the honorable Hokage’s student, and then you tell me the outcome remains unpredictable ? ?

I’m pouring my heart out to you, brother, and you’re playing mind games with me ! !
