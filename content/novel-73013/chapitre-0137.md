# Chapitre 137 — Checking In with Tobirama, Reanimation Jutsu!

Inside the Hokage Office.

Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama sat side by side, working together on the mountain of documents piled up before them.

As time passed, Senju Hashirama gradually burned out, his gaze unable to stop drifting toward the distance outside the window.

In that direction lay one of the places in Konoha that he missed the most.

One could even say that, in his heart, that location was a Holy Land !

That’s right, it was the Konoha casino.

« Wind Release : Gale Palm ! »

A gust of wind suddenly struck from beside his ear, causing Senju Hashirama’s expression to stiffen.

Immediately following that, the gust swept across the window of the Hokage Office.

The direction was just right, the force moderate, and it perfectly pulled down the drawn curtains, obscuring the beautiful scenery outside the window.

« Hey ! That’s too cruel ! Tobirama ! » Senju Hashirama was instantly furious.

« You idiot older brother ! Hurry up and finish processing the documents in front of you ! » Senju Tobirama was also furious.

« Uh, this... »

Outside the office door, Senju Matsuaki, who was just about to knock, heard the commotion inside and held his hand mid-air.

Would it be bad to knock now ?

Just as he was hesitating, Senju Tobirama’s voice came through the door panel.

« Come in ! »

Hearing this, Senju Matsuaki didn’t hesitate any longer, pushed the door open, and walked in.

Inside the office, the two brothers who had just been shouting had resumed the dignity and kindness of elders, looking at Senju Matsuaki as he walked toward them.

« Yo, little Matsuaki, after resting for a few days, are you planning to take on a mission ? » Senju Hashirama asked with a smile.

After Senju Matsuaki and Hyuga Tsukuyomi in the original squad were both promoted to Chunin, their squad leader had announced his retirement.

From then on, the two could execute missions independently or team up to accept missions.

Seeing Senju Matsuaki come by, he naturally assumed the other party couldn’t sit still after resting for a few days and intended to come and accept a commission.

« Uncle, Second Uncle, I have something I would like to ask for your advice on. »

As Senju Matsuaki spoke, he unobtrusively opened the Check-in System he had just requisitioned today.

Before him were the two strongest people in Konoha at the moment.

This time, Senju Matsuaki’s target was not his uncle, Senju Hashirama.

After all, having targeted Senju Hashirama for check-ins many times before, he had already obtained Asura Chakra and Wood Release.

Except for Sage Mode, it could be said that he had already picked Hashirama clean.

Although the Sage Mode also made Senju Matsuaki drool, he didn’t think he could get it in one shot by checking in today.

Conversely, on Senju Tobirama’s side, there were quite a few rewards that made him drool.

The Water Release that was second to none in the Ninja World, the unfathomable Forbidden Jutsu...

Of course, Senju Matsuaki’s primary goal was still the Flying Thunder God Technique.

Ever since witnessing Senju Tobirama’s Flying Thunder God Technique, he had been drooling over it non-stop.

Not to mention the mobility of this technique in combat, just the convenience it brought in daily life was worth him consuming a check-in opportunity.

This Check-in System was requisitioned precisely to gain big returns from small investments !

However, before checking in...

Senju Matsuaki asked in advance : « Uncle, Second Uncle, by the way, you two are both your real bodies here, right ? Not some Wood Clone or Shadow Clone ? »

He had fallen into a trap last time.

Holding the newly requisitioned Check-in System, he had happily checked in on Senju Hashirama’s Wood Clone and joyfully obtained the Shadow Clone Technique.

He certainly wouldn’t make the same mistake again.

Hearing this, Senju Tobirama was slightly stunned but still answered patiently : « My big brother and I are both our real bodies here, why do you ask ? »

With this confirmation, Senju Matsuaki did not hesitate and immediately selected Senju Tobirama as the target for the check-in.

[Check-in Character · Senju Tobirama ! Obtained : Reanimation Jutsu (Improved Version) !]

Senju Matsuaki was stunned for a moment, then his pupils shook !

No, what did you say I obtained ! ?

Reanimation Jutsu ! ?

Senju Matsuaki’s expression became incredibly colorful.

This technique could be said to be worlds apart from his target, the Flying Thunder God Technique.

But its power and influence were even greater than the Flying Thunder God Technique.

The effects of this technique could be described as unimaginable.

When other Ninjas were still playing with fire and water, or even eyes and wood, Senju Tobirama was already pondering how to summon the dead to fight.

To have a necromancer appear among a group of Ninjas was simply a different art style.

Moreover, Senju Tobirama, who was quite talented in Forbidden Jutsu research, actually succeeded.

The Reanimation Jutsu could use the DNA of the deceased and sacrifice a living person to summon the Soul of the deceased from the Pure Land to the human world.

The summoned dead would gain an indestructible body and infinite Chakra, and although their strength might not be as good as when they were alive, for most Ninjas, their comprehensive strength was actually stronger than when they were alive.

Later, this technique was improved by another Ninja World scientist, Orochimaru, and thus further perfected.

In the original timeline’s Fourth Ninja World War, this technique was used extensively, causing the Allied Shinobi Forces to suffer greatly.

At this moment, what Senju Matsuaki obtained from the check-in seemed to be the more perfect version improved by Orochimaru in the future.

Feeling the casting method and related knowledge appearing in his mind, Senju Matsuaki didn’t know whether to laugh or cry.

It wasn’t that he disliked the reward for not being high-class enough.

After all, the Reanimation Jutsu was also an S-rank Ninjutsu, the same level as the Flying Thunder God Technique he originally wanted.

Although he failed to get the desired Flying Thunder God Technique from the check-in, this Reanimation Jutsu was definitely a jackpot too.

But the problem was, right now, even the developer of this technique, Senju Tobirama, probably wouldn’t dare to use it openly.

Otherwise, before the other Ninja villages in the Ninja World could denounce him, he would first be taught a lesson by his big brother Senju Hashirama’s fists.

As the saying goes—one punch wakes up the Forbidden Jutsu Soul, Big Brother is an honest man !

Even Tobirama only dared to hide and use this Forbidden Jutsu secretly after developing it, so naturally, he couldn’t use it either.

Of course, although he wasn’t a saint, he wouldn’t kill innocent people indiscriminately.

The use of this technique required sacrificing living people ; constrained by his conscience, he would only limit the casting Materials to those who were unforgivable or those he was in a life-or-death struggle with.

In any case, it wasn’t a loss.

After sorting out his thoughts, Senju Matsuaki said with a normal expression : « Uncle, Second Uncle, I found a scroll in the storage room where you keep your Ninjutsu scrolls last time. »

« The scroll looked a bit old, and inside was a map marking a location. »

« Then there was a line of text written on the side, reminding us descendants of the Senju Clan to check the Seal every six months. »

« I was a bit curious about the contents of this scroll, so I thought I’d come and ask. »

Matsuaki roughly described what he had seen in the scroll at that time.

Aside from the check-in, this was the second purpose of Senju Matsuaki’s visit.

Regarding that so-called « evil weapon, » it was impossible not to be curious.

Whether it was the reward points or the « weapon » itself, perhaps it could become his help in resisting future crises.

On the premise of acting cautiously, learning more information was not a bad thing.
