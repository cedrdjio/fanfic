# Chapitre 48 — Stand on the Shoulders of a Giant First

The next morning.

After today’s training concluded, Senju Hashirama’s Wood Clone gratifyingly handed two forms to Senju Matsuaki and his companion.

« These are the registration forms for the Joint Chunin Exams. I presume you have been looking forward to this for a long time. »

Based on previous years, the registration work for the Chunin Exams should have already started several weeks ago.

However, due to the variable of the Joint Chunin Exams, all tasks related to the Chunin Exams have been slightly delayed.

After receiving the registration form, Senju Matsuaki could not help but feel a bit excited.

Compared to Genin, Chunin are the ones who can truly stand on their own.

After becoming a Chunin, not only can one accept higher-level mission requests, but in times of war, one can also possess a certain degree of command authority in some situations.

For Senju Matsuaki, he valued the former more. Being able to accept higher-level B-rank missions meant more opportunities to obtain the points needed for his « cheat » system.

After becoming a Genin, Senju Matsuaki’s opportunities to obtain points increased greatly, ushering in a period of explosive growth in his strength.

Similarly, after becoming a Chunin, he would obtain even more points, and his strength would once again usher in explosive growth.

« This year’s Joint Chunin Exams are quite different from the Chunin Exams of previous years, but it shouldn’t be a problem for you two. After you go back, fill out these forms and hand them in to my office. »

After saying this, Hashirama’s Wood Clone waved goodbye to the two of them.

Senju Matsuaki looked down at the form in his hand and found that it was more or less the same as the registration form during the Ninja Academy graduation exam.

After all, the Ninja Village was similar to military management ; the Village had files on the vast majority of information for Genin, whether it was their mission history or basic information.

« The Chunin Exams… I feel like time has passed so quickly. » Hyuga Tsukuyomi looked down at the form in her hand, her pure Byakugan shimmering slightly as she murmured.

After becoming Genin, they spent most of their time executing missions and training, but they also had some time for rest and leisure.

Overall, the more than half a year since becoming Genin had been quite fulfilling.

Perhaps it was precisely because time was fully utilized that it felt like it passed very quickly, as if in the blink of an eye, they had gone from the Ninja Academy graduation exam to the Chunin Exams for promotion from Genin to Chunin.

« Don’t worry, didn’t Uncle also recognize your strength ? The mere Chunin Exams will be no problem for you. » Seeing that Hyuga Tsukuyomi seemed a bit down, Senju Matsuaki thought she lacked confidence in the Chunin Exams, so he smiled and comforted her.

Although the Hyuga Clan’s Byakugan did not have any eye-catching performances in the later stages, it was still a top-tier Bloodline Limit at the present time when combat power had not yet collapsed.

If a Ninja of the Hyuga Clan successfully awakened their eyes and their own talent was not bad, it was equivalent to boarding a direct train to becoming a Jonin. As long as they developed steadily, becoming a Jonin was no problem.

However, Senju Matsuaki clearly guessed the girl’s thoughts wrong.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi, who always appeared gentle, now had a rare look of resentment in her eyes, and she said quietly : « Senju Matsuaki, you really don’t understand anything~ »

Looking at the confused-looking Senju Matsuaki, she did not explain further, but lowered her head a bit dejectedly.

« After becoming Chunin, will we not be able to execute missions together anymore ? »

« Uh, no. » Hearing this question, Senju Matsuaki was slightly taken aback.

« Unless we join specific departments after becoming Chunin because of certain specialties, the mode of executing missions generally won’t change. »

The idea that one must disband the original Genin team after becoming a Chunin is actually a misunderstanding.

Just like the future Nara Shikamaru, after becoming a Chunin, he still executed missions with his Genin teammates from Team 10.

Kakashi Hatake, after becoming a Jonin at 12, was still in Team Minato, following Minato Namikaze and acting together with his two teammates.

However, speaking of this, Senju Matsuaki thought of the Four Red Yang Formation he had obtained from signing in a while ago.

While mastering this barrier Ninjutsu, he also learned a lot of knowledge related to barriers. If he had no ambition, he could completely just join the Barrier Team and slack off.

However, thinking of the future crises that would either destroy Konoha or destroy the entire Ninja World, Senju Matsuaki quickly dispelled the thought of slacking off.

« Eh, is that so ? » Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s eyes lit up, and her face returned to its usual gentle smile.

« Besides, for a reliable teammate like you, Tsukuyomi, I wouldn’t want to part with you either. » Senju Matsuaki said with a smile, his face showing encouragement.

I have to say, having a pair of Byakugan as a teammate is really much more convenient. Compared to teams without Byakugan, it’s practically like cheating.

No wonder the Hyuga Clan’s reputation in Konoha is so much better than the Uchiha Clan ; not only are they more qualified as teammates, but they also don’t have such big egos.

Although there are some problems within this clan, at least they don’t get in other people’s way.

« Ah, well, see you tomorrow~ »

Under Senju Matsuaki’s somewhat puzzled gaze, Hyuga Tsukuyomi said goodbye in a flustered manner, then quickly ran away with a blushing face.

Seeing this, Senju Matsuaki thought she had some urgent matter, so he also walked back toward his own residence.

After entering his mansion, Senju Matsuaki turned around and locked the door directly.

He did not go back into the house to rest, but walked straight to the training ground in the courtyard and continued training.

He did this not to secretly get ahead and outdo his teammates, but to practice his trump cards and killer moves.

After obtaining Wood Release from signing in yesterday, Senju Matsuaki felt that his body seemed to have undergone some magical changes again.

Earth Release Chakra and Water Release Chakra naturally began to fuse within his body, eventually forming another type of Chakra full of vitality and vigor.

« Wood Release : Wood Clone ! » Senju Matsuaki followed the impression of watching Senju Hashirama use Wood Clone in his memory, and began to form hand seals with both hands.

Wood Clone and Shadow Clone have commonalities in principle. Having mastered Shadow Clone and Multi Shadow Clone, he quickly found the feeling of Wood Clone.

Seeing the Wood Release Chakra on Senju Matsuaki’s body burst out, emitting a rich vitality and the aura of Wood Release.

Next, tree branches began to grow out from the right side of his body. After some surging of the wood, it formed a life-sized mannequin with a woody tone beside him.

In the next step, the same colors as Senju Matsuaki’s current clothing appeared on the mannequin, and the skin and hair color gradually became identical.

Finally, the Wood Clone’s eyes gradually gained spirit, a light appeared in the scarlet pupils, and the expression became increasingly vivid.

« Original, you succeeded. » Wood Clone Senju Matsuaki simply felt the power he possessed, then smiled and congratulated the original Senju Matsuaki.

It was his first time using Wood Clone, so he was somewhat unskilled, which is why the Wood Clone formed so slowly just now. Once he is proficient, he will be able to condense a Wood Clone in an instant.

After dispelling the Wood Clone, Senju Matsuaki pushed open his front door and walked toward the residence next door.

Now that his Wood Release Ninjutsu was severely lacking, he would first go and stand on the shoulders of a giant for a while.
