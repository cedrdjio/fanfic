# Chapitre 162 — Executing Elder Uchiha Hosai! The Rebellion Quelled!

However, at the very moment the Kunai was about to strike, Senju Matsuaki merely raised his right hand with an understated gesture.

« Air Quake ! ! »

A violent, vibrating force spread from his palm, and the air seemed to freeze for an instant.

Immediately after, Elder Uchiha Hosai felt a terrifying force strike him head-on.

His entire body felt as if it had been rammed by a massive mountain ; his bones seemed about to shatter, and he was sent flying backward, completely out of control.

Boom ! !

He slammed heavily into the wall behind him, causing it to crack and sending dust billowing everywhere.

« This... how is this possible... »

Elder Uchiha Hosai spat out blood, his eyes filled with disbelief.

He had once been a seasoned elite Jonin, and even though he was advanced in years, he still retained at least the strength of a Jonin.

This was the source of his confidence in attempting to storm the Hokage Building.

But now, why was he defeated by just a single blow ! ?

His internal organs seemed to have been completely shattered by that single punch !

The intense pain caused his vision to blur, and blood from the corner of his mouth stained his white beard.

Senju Matsuaki slowly retracted his fist, looking expressionlessly at the collapsed Elder Uchiha Hosai.

« Without someone like you, both the Village and the Uchiha Clan will be better off ! »

Elder Uchiha Hosai struggled to get up, but the excruciating pain in his chest made it impossible for him to move.

He felt as if his internal organs had been pulverized by that punch, and his life force was rapidly fading away.

« You... you actually... »

He struggled to raise his finger to point at Senju Matsuaki, his eyes filled with resentment and unwillingness to accept his fate.

But before he could finish his sentence, his hand fell limply.

The red light in those Sharingan eyes was extinguished forever.

A deathly silence descended upon the plaza.

All the Uchiha Clan members watched this scene, dumbfounded.

No one had imagined that an Elder of the dignified Uchiha Clan would be killed by a single punch from a young man.

Senju Hashirama frowned, but ultimately said nothing.

He knew that Senju Matsuaki was establishing authority.

At a time like this, it was necessary to use thunderous methods to deter those with malicious intent.

Those who betray the Village or act against its interests must pay a cruel price.

Senju Hashirama, who was usually a gentle soul, was more extreme than anyone else when it came to matters concerning the Village.

In the original story, he was even willing to kill his best friend during the battle at the Valley of the End to protect the Village.

« Who else wants to rebel ? »

Senju Matsuaki scanned his surroundings ; his voice was calm, yet carried an unquestionable majesty.

Those Uchiha Clan members who had been ready to stir up trouble all lowered their heads, not daring to meet his gaze.

Senju Tobirama stepped forward and scanned the Uchiha Clan members present : « Elder Uchiha Hosai intended to rebel and has been executed on the spot. »

« The remaining participants will be imprisoned in the Konoha dungeon to await interrogation. »

« If anyone wishes to escape punishment, feel free to try... »

He did not finish his sentence, but the threat was self-evident.

A storm that should have swept through the entire Village was thus stifled in its cradle.

When Senju Hashirama walked out of the Uchiha Clan district, the setting sun was already slanting westward, casting a long, weary shadow behind him.

His best friend had attacked the Village, and the Uchiha Clan members within the Village had also intended to rebel.

For a moment, he felt utterly exhausted.

« Uncle. »

Senju Matsuaki caught up. « Are you alright ? »

Senju Hashirama shook his head and offered a bitter smile. « I was just thinking that perhaps Madara was right. »

Senju Matsuaki was taken aback. « What ? »

« The world of Ninjas is forever trapped in a cycle of mutual harm. »

Senju Hashirama sighed. « Even though we established Konoha, and even though we want to protect peace, the conflicts between people will never cease. »

Senju Matsuaki was silent for a moment, then said earnestly, « But at least, we are trying. »

Senju Hashirama paused, then smiled. « Yes, at least we are trying. »

Senju Tobirama reminded him, « Big Brother, that Nine-Tailed monster hasn’t been dealt with yet. »

Senju Tobirama’s words made everyone present fall silent.

The Nine-Tailed.

That terrifying monster with nine tails was currently lying quietly in the forest near the Valley of the End.

After being subdued by the combined power of Senju Hashirama’s Wood Release and Senju Matsuaki’s Tremor-Tremor Fruit, the Nine-Tailed had been suppressed by Senju Hashirama’s technique and had fallen into a coma.

However, the suppression technique used on the Nine-Tailed was not permanent.

Relying on the suffocatingly massive amount of Chakra within its body, the Nine-Tailed would eventually break free from the restraints of the Wood Release.

« The Nine-Tailed is indeed a problem. »

Senju Hashirama rubbed his temples. « Its power is too great ; if left unchecked, it will sooner or later bring disaster to the Village again. »

Senju Matsuaki thought for a moment and asked, « Uncle, how do you intend to deal with it ? »

« Kill it ? »

Senju Tobirama suggested, « With Big Brother’s Wood Release power and Matsuaki’s power to shatter space, it might not be impossible. »

During the battle just now, he had also noticed the terrifying vitality of the Nine-Tailed.

But in Senju Tobirama’s view, even the most vigorous vitality eventually had a limit.

Senju Matsuaki shook his head. « Tailed Beasts are aggregates of Chakra ; even if you kill it, it will revive after several years. »

« At that point, we would lose control over it. »

Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama exchanged glances ; they had never heard this theory before.

They had long heard the legend of the Nine-Tailed demon fox, and there had been many records of sightings and battles throughout history.

But the problem was that whenever Ninjas had come into conflict with it, the greatest achievement was merely driving it back.

Killing it ? Never heard of it.

And in the last hundred years, the Nine-Tailed hadn’t really appeared in the Ninja World, so records about it had gradually become scarce.

If not for Uchiha Madara controlling the Nine-Tailed to cause chaos today, Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama might have long forgotten the legends they heard in their childhood.

Hearing Senju Matsuaki say this now, they did not doubt him.

They had been able to sense what the Nine-Tailed was made of during the battle just now.

It was not a creature of flesh and blood, but an aggregate of Chakra.

« Then let’s Seal it away. »

A gentle female voice came from behind them.

Senju Hashirama turned around, a hint of surprise in his eyes. « Mito, why are you here ? »

The person who had arrived was none other than Senju Hashirama’s wife, Uzumaki Mito.

She was wearing a simple, elegant white kimono, and her long red hair shimmered with a soft luster in the setting sun.

In the center of her forehead, that iconic diamond-shaped mark was faintly visible.

« With such a major incident occurring in the Village, how could I stay at home with peace of mind ? »

Uzumaki Mito smiled slightly and walked to Senju Hashirama’s side.

« Besides, I sensed the Nine-Tailed’s Chakra ; that power... it is truly astonishing. »

Senju Hashirama took his wife’s hand. « Do you have a way to Seal it ? »

« The Uzumaki Clan is most skilled at Sealing Jutsu. »

A trace of confidence flashed in Uzumaki Mito’s eyes. « Although the Nine-Tailed is strong, as long as it is in a weakened state, I am confident that I can Seal it within my own body. »

« What, within your body ! ? »

Upon hearing this, Senju Hashirama’s expression changed drastically. « No ! That is too dangerous ! »
