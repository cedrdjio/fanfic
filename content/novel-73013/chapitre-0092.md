# Chapitre 92 — The First Raikage Is Jealous

However, the First Raikage could not accept these wealthy individuals coming to watch the match.

In his view, the relationship between Ninjas, Ninja Villages, and these wealthy individuals was a simple, equal contractual one—while they paid him, he performed the work in return.

« You don’t provide funding to our Village directly like the Daimyo, so why should we report our Village’s military strength to you ? »

Furthermore, these wealthy individuals were even paying for tickets to enter.

« Isn’t this just performing for money ? »

Nearby, the future Second Raikage, Hong, looked visibly displeased upon hearing this news.

« No wonder Konoha accepted this proposal without much resistance ; it turns out they wanted to use this opportunity to make a fortune ! »

Hong’s perspective on the matter was completely different from that of the First Raikage.

As someone from the older generation, the First Raikage had more rigid thinking and placed great emphasis on etiquette.

The older Ninjas in Kumogakure often remarked that he possessed the 'legacy of the Sengoku era.'

Hong, however, was younger, and his views and perspectives were more flexible.

At the same time, Hong’s youth had witnessed the founding of Kumogakure from nothing, so he knew exactly what the Village lacked most for its development.

It boiled down to two things : first, talent, and second, money.

Therefore, he was equally furious upon hearing this news.

He wasn’t angry that the Chunin Exams had turned into a less-than-serious performance ; rather, he was furious that such substantial profits were not going into Kumogakure’s pockets !

Only now did he realize why Konoha hadn’t seemed so resistant to hosting the Joint Chunin Exams at the time.

It seemed even that initial resistance had been a facade ; Konoha had likely been eager to host the Chunin Exams themselves.

« What is it ? Can Konoha make a lot of money this time ? »

Hearing Hong say this, the First Raikage’s angry expression faltered, replaced by a hint of curiosity.

Dou, who currently lacked experience in managing the Village, also looked at his mentor, Hong, waiting for an answer.

« A lot. I’m afraid it’s more than we can imagine, » Hong said in a deep voice.

As he spoke, Hong extended his right hand and began counting on his fingers, as if it helped clarify his thoughts.

« First, there’s the ticket revenue for watching the matches. Based on the information I just gathered, the number of spectators this time is likely unprecedented. »

« Moreover, the ticket prices vary depending on the quality of the seating. The best seats are actually more expensive than the reward for an A-rank mission ! »

Hong extended a second finger and continued.

« Second, these tourists and wealthy individuals coming to Konoha are paying for their own accommodation, food, and entertainment while they stay here. This level of spending will stimulate Konoha’s economic development. »

Next, he extended a third finger.

« Third, I noticed along the way that businesses owned by Konoha’s major Ninja clans are selling goods—the Senju, Uchiha, Hyuga, Nara, Akimichi, Sarutobi… »

« These clans are the core of Konoha. With their businesses raking in profits, the clans will naturally have more resources to train Ninjas. »

« As the strength of these clans increases, Konoha’s overall strength will rise accordingly ! »

Finally, he extended a fourth finger.

« There’s one more point, though I’m not entirely sure what it signifies, so consider it my speculation. »

« The merchants and wealthy individuals I saw along the way, regardless of whether they have hired Ninjas before, have always had a distant and vague impression of Ninjas. »

« These Chunin Exams effectively bridge the gap between these people and Ninjas. Konoha, as the host, will likely carry more weight in their minds. »

Upon hearing this, the First Raikage’s expression shifted from initial anger to a trace of envy, and finally to extreme solemnity.

The First Raikage said in a deep voice, « In that case, perhaps in the future, when the convenience is comparable, these people might prioritize hiring Konoha Ninjas, leading to a loss of commissions for Kumogakure. »

After saying this, a cold sweat broke out on his back, and his brow furrowed tightly.

In his heart, he had always viewed the other major Ninja Villages, especially Konoha, as potential enemies.

The major Ninja Villages had been established for ten years, and the peace maintained until now was entirely due to the internal strife during their early founding years.

Those who understood the situation well could guess that a large-scale war between the major Ninja Villages was inevitable within the next decade.

Hearing that a potential enemy had such an opportunity, while his own Village had missed out, truly made the First Raikage anxious.

Simply put, the First Raikage was truly jealous and envious !

« Lord Raikage, we are powerless to change the first three points, but the last one is not necessarily irreversible. »

After pondering for a moment, Hong pulled Dou, who was also looking solemn, in front of him.

« If we in Kumogakure perform brilliantly in the upcoming third stage of the exams, and can even crush the candidates from other Villages, not only will it damage the reputation of those other Villages, but Konoha’s efforts to boost their own prestige will ultimately just be paving the way for our Kumogakure ! »

« I will definitely not fail the mission ! ! »

Seeing Lord Raikage and his mentor both casting expectant gazes at him, Dou felt a fiery fighting spirit burning in his chest.

Crushing the candidates from other Villages, especially Yan from Sunagakure and Senju Matsuaki from Konoha, was exactly what he had prepared to do !

...

Time flew by, and a week passed quickly.

The sun was high in the sky ; the day for the official start of the third stage of the Joint Chunin Exams had arrived.

« Where are the Daimyo now ? »

Inside the Hokage Office, Senju Hashirama was wearing his Hokage hat and adjusting his appearance in the mirror.

At the same time, he did not forget to ask a newly arrived Ninja about the situation with the Daimyo and the others.

Theoretically, the Kage of a Ninja Village controlled the nation’s largest military force, while the Daimyo represented the nation’s administrative power ; their relationship was one of political and military power-sharing and cooperation.

However, nominally, the Daimyo was the supreme ruler of the country, providing economic support to the Ninja Village and holding the power to appoint the Kage.

From Senju Matsuaki’s perspective, this situation was clearly somewhat inconceivable.

After all, for Ninjas who had reached the level of Kage, weak ordinary people like the Daimyo were practically like ants.

Even if the Daimyo recruited Ninjas and samurai as bodyguards, generally speaking, the strength of these bodyguards reached its limit at the Jonin level.

Yet, perhaps due to the lingering influence of history and culture, the core and sacred status of the Daimyo within a nation had indeed been passed down since ancient times.

Hearing this, the Konoha Ninja looked troubled and said, « The five Daimyo are currently having breakfast together at the hotel restaurant. I’m not sure if I should remind them to be mindful of the time ? »

It was laughable, really.

The Ninja Villages of the major nations were hostile to each other and would eventually be locked in life-and-death struggles.

Yet, the Daimyo of the major nations had become increasingly close due to complex and intimate kinship ties formed through intermarriage.

« I will go and notify the Daimyo myself in a while, » Senju Hashirama said.

Asking an ordinary Ninja to remind the five Daimyo, who stood at the pinnacle of power in the Five Great Nations, was asking too much of the young man.
