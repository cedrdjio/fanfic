# Chapitre 150 — Parting Across Time and Space

Another month passed.

Life in the small town had long since settled into a routine.

Every morning, Senju Matsuaki would head out to hunt, and when he returned in the afternoon, Uchiha Hikari would always be sitting on the steps in front of the door, waiting for him.

Sometimes she would be preparing vegetables, and other times she would be flipping through books borrowed from the town.

At other times, she would do nothing at all, simply resting her chin on her hand as she gazed down the path leading to the forest.

Seeing him approach from afar, she would stand up, dust off her skirt, and turn to go inside to heat up their meal.

Their days were as peaceful as a painting.

But Senju Matsuaki knew that this painting was coming to an end.

On this day, they were chatting on the steps outside the wooden cabin as they usually did, with the soft moonlight before them.

« Notification : The Door of Space-Time will open again in seven days ! Please return to your original era in time to avoid being permanently stranded ! »

Senju Matsuaki’s words, spoken halfway, came to a halt.

The countdown : seven days.

He had been waiting for this notification for a long time.

Yet, the moment it actually arrived, an indescribable feeling welled up in his heart.

He lowered his eyelids, staring at the clear moonlight on the ground, his expression unreadable.

« What’s wrong ? »

Uchiha Hikari, beside him, noticed his unusual behavior and turned her head to look at him.

A scroll was spread out on the girl’s knees.

It contained neither Ninjutsu nor any secrets, but rather recipes she had recently tried and decided to note down because they were good.

« It’s nothing. »

Senju Matsuaki shook his head, keeping his tone as normal as possible, « I was just thinking about where to go hunting tomorrow. »

This remark did not make Uchiha Hikari feel anything was amiss.

She did not press for details, simply saying « Oh, » and lowered her head to look at the recipes again.

The moonlight shone quietly upon them both, and the night breeze brushed against the bushes in the corner of the yard, making a soft rustling sound.

There was the chirping of insects in the grass, and occasionally the sound of dogs barking in the distance ; it was the most ordinary of nights for this small town.

But Senju Matsuaki knew that there were only seven such nights left.

For the next few days, he deliberately maintained his usual rhythm.

Heading out in the morning, returning in the afternoon.

Teaching Uchiha Hikari to identify more wild vegetables and mushrooms, accompanying her to town to buy needle, thread, and fabric, and listening to her complain about whether today’s soup was too salty or too bland.

The two of them talked about their pasts and then sat side by side on the roof in the twilight, watching the sky transition from orange-red to deep blue.

Uchiha Hikari seemed to have noticed nothing.

Or perhaps, she just didn’t say anything.

On the evening of the sixth day, Senju Matsuaki held something in his hand.

It was a small wooden hairpin.

He had made it using Wood Release ; the style was simple, with a small five-petaled flower carved at the top.

When he pushed open the door, Uchiha Hikari heard his footsteps and looked up, her gaze falling on the hairpin in his hand.

« For you. »

Senju Matsuaki handed the hairpin over and smiled, « Consider it... a token of thanks for this time. »

Uchiha Hikari was stunned for a moment, then reached out to take the hairpin and looked down at it for a long time.

She gently stroked the texture of the petals with her fingertips, saying nothing.

« Do you not like it ? »

Seeing her remain unresponsive for a long while, Senju Matsuaki asked somewhat nervously.

« No. »

Uchiha Hikari shook her head, her voice very soft, « I like it very much. »

She looked up at Senju Matsuaki.

Those eyes, usually fiercely sharp on the battlefield, were glistening in the setting sun, as if they held light within them.

« Help me put it on. »

Senju Matsuaki was taken aback, then stepped forward, took the hairpin, and carefully tucked it into her hair.

Her hair was very soft and carried an unknown, faint fragrance.

His fingers inadvertently brushed against her earlobe, and the warm sensation caused both him and the girl to stiffen at the same time.

« There. »

Senju Matsuaki withdrew his hand, stepped back half a pace, and examined her closely.

Uchiha Hikari raised her hand to touch the location of the hairpin, the corners of her mouth curving into a small arc.

The seventh night.

The moonlight was beautiful, with silver-white brilliance covering the entire courtyard.

Senju Matsuaki sat on the steps, and Uchiha Hikari sat beside him.

There was a half-fist distance between them, and neither spoke.

The distant town had already fallen into a deep sleep, with only an occasional lamp flickering in the night.

« Matsuaki, are you leaving tomorrow ? »

Uchiha Hikari suddenly spoke, her voice calm.

Senju Matsuaki was not surprised.

Uchiha Hikari was a sensitive child, and he knew she must have noticed.

He nodded and said, « Yes. »

Uchiha Hikari lowered her head, her hands clasped on her knees, her fingertips turning slightly white.

After a long while, she asked softly, « Then will you ever come back ? »

During their time together, she had actually come to regard him as family.

Now, this new family member seemed to be leaving her again.

However, it was precisely because of this newfound bond that she could not bring herself to forcefully ask him to stay.

Senju Matsuaki was silent for a long time.

He wanted to say « Yes, » but he couldn’t bring himself to say it.

If he used lies to make the girl wait a lifetime, it would be even crueler than telling the truth from the beginning.

He was from the future and had returned to this era by utilizing the requisitioned Check-in System, which allowed him to use Ōtsutsuki power to open the Door of Space-Time.

Returning to this era again was almost impossible.

« It is highly unlikely. »

In the end, he chose to tell the truth.

Uchiha Hikari’s shoulders trembled slightly, but she quickly returned to calm.

She took a deep breath, looked up at the moon in the sky, her voice dry.

« Then... can I go with you ? »

« This... »

Senju Matsuaki sighed.

Judging from the game’s plot, Boruto also returned alone after his time was up.

Uchiha Hikari was alone at that time too ; if it were possible, why not take her back with him ?

Whether the Door of Space-Time created by Ōtsutsuki power had limitations, or it was simply out of concern for the effects on time and space.

In short, he could not take Uchiha Hikari with him.

Senju Matsuaki turned his head to look at her.

Under the moonlight, the girl’s profile had soft lines, and her eyes were slightly red.

She tried hard to maintain a calm expression, as stubborn as when they first met.

« Then... I want to hear more about you. »

Uchiha Hikari bit her lip gently, « You come from the future, right ? »

The conversations over these past few days had allowed her to slowly confirm this guess.

Just like when talking about interesting stories from their childhoods.

The Senju and Uchiha were clearly in the same region, and their living standards and cultural backgrounds were not very different.

But many things Senju Matsuaki mentioned were unheard of to her.

Especially when mentioning Ninja in their conversations, she often referred to them in terms of « Ninja Clans. »

But he would often inadvertently bring up something called « Ninja Villages. »

It was as if they came from different times and spaces.

Hearing these words, Senju Matsuaki widened his eyes in surprise.

« ...That’s right. »

In the end, he sighed.

Having spent this much time together, how could he not have also come to view her as a companion and family ?

In the final moments, he did not want to lie.

« I knew it. Can you tell me some things about the future ? »

« Of course. »

Uchiha Hikari did not speak again.

She simply leaned her head gently on his shoulder and listened quietly.

Senju Matsuaki stiffened for a moment, but ultimately did not pull away.

The moonlight stretched their shadows very long, overlapping together as if they would never separate.

But the moment of parting would eventually come.

When the first ray of dawn pierced the sky, Senju Matsuaki felt the weight on his shoulder disappear.

He turned his head ; Uchiha Hikari had already stood up, stepped back two paces, and distanced herself from him.

There was not much sadness visible on her face, only her eyes were very red.

« Don’t forget me. »

She said, her voice a bit hoarse.

« Yes. »

Senju Matsuaki opened his mouth, wanting to say something, but in the end, he just nodded gently in response.

The next instant.

The « Door of Space-Time, » visible only to him, slowly opened behind him.

The white light coated his silhouette with a cold halo.

The light swallowed his figure, the « Door of Space-Time » slowly closed, and finally vanished.

The entrance to this wooden cabin, filled with traces of life, returned to silence once more.
