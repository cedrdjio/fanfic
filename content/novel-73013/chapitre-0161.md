# Chapitre 161 — The Battle of the Valley of the End Concludes! An Uchiha Clan Rebellion?

He wore a complex expression, looking up at the several people approaching him.

Senju Hashirama deactivated his Sage Mode, looking at his dear friend before him, his eyes filled with complexity : « Madara... enough. »

« Hmph ! » Uchiha Madara snorted coldly.

Inside the deep crater, Uchiha Madara knelt on one knee, the red light in his Eternal Mangekyo Sharingan gradually dimming.

Senju Hashirama walked slowly to the edge of the crater, looking down at his former dear friend, his eyes filled with complex emotions.

« Madara, stop. » Senju Hashirama’s voice was low, « You have already lost. »

Uchiha Madara raised his head, the blood at the corner of his mouth shocking to behold.

Yet his face still bore that defiant smile : « Lost ? Hashirama, when did you become so naive ? »

Before the words had even faded, the dim Eternal Mangekyo Sharingan of Uchiha Madara suddenly flashed with blinding red light once more.

The remaining deep blue Chakra surged out like a tidal wave.

« Susanoo ! »

The azure Chakra giant rose from the ground once again ; although it was not as bloodthirsty as before, its biting Aura was still heart-stopping.

While everyone was on guard, Uchiha Madara did not attack again, but instead spread his wings and soared into the sky.

Senju Tobirama’s expression changed : « Not good ! He’s trying to escape ! »

He immediately tried to intercept, but Uchiha Madara’s Susanoo had already spread its wings, whipping up a fierce gale.

« Hashirama, you have a good nephew this time ! » Uchiha Madara stood at the center of the Susanoo’s forehead, looking down at the people below, « But next time, I won’t give you such an opportunity again ! »

Senju Matsuaki clenched his fists, preparing to unleash the power of the Tremor-Tremor Fruit again, but was stopped by Senju Hashirama raising a hand.

« Let him go. » Senju Hashirama shook his head, his eyes full of exhaustion, « If Madara wants to escape, he cannot be stopped. »

Uchiha Madara took a deep look at Senju Hashirama, a flash of indescribable emotion crossing his Eternal Mangekyo Sharingan.

The next instant, the Susanoo shot into the sky, turning into a blue meteor and disappearing at the edge of the horizon.

Senju Hashirama stared at the receding light, remaining silent for a long time.

« Big brother... » Senju Tobirama walked to his side, wanting to say something but hesitating.

« I’m fine. » Senju Hashirama waved his hand and took a deep breath, « Let’s go back ; the Village needs us to calm things down. »

Senju Matsuaki looked at his uncle’s back and sighed.

This battle was far heavier for Senju Hashirama than it appeared on the surface.

The battle at the Valley of the End concluded, and news quickly spread back to Konoha Village.

When Senju Hashirama and his group returned to the Village, villagers flooded the streets, and cheers rose one after another.

« The Shodai(1st)-sama has returned ! » « Great ! We won ! » « Long live the Senju Clan ! »

However, there was not a trace of joy on Senju Hashirama’s face.

He simply walked silently through the crowd, heading toward the Hokage Building.

Just then, a Ninja rushed over and knelt on one knee : « Shodai(1st)-sama, there is an emergency ! »

Senju Hashirama frowned : « What is it ? »

The Ninja lowered his voice : « The Uchiha Clan... someone attempted to start a rebellion while you were battling with Madara-sama. »

Upon hearing this, everyone present changed their expressions.

« What ? ! » Senju Tobirama’s eyes flashed with cold light, « Who led it ? »

« Elder Uchiha Hosai, » the Ninja said in a low voice, « He gathered over thirty Uchiha clansmen, planning to seize the Hokage Building while the Village was undefended. »

« But upon learning of your safe return, they changed course and left ; they are currently assembling at their clan grounds. »

Senju Hashirama’s expression darkened.

He had not expected that while he and Madara were fighting to the death on the front lines, someone in the rear would dare to stab him in the back.

« Let’s go, to the Uchiha Clan grounds. » Senju Hashirama said in a low voice.

Senju Matsuaki followed behind his uncle, a cold glint flashing in his eyes.

Elder Uchiha Hosai ? He was not unfamiliar with that name.

He was a representative of the radical faction within the Uchiha Clan, who had always advocated that the Uchiha should control the Konoha government and was extremely dissatisfied with the Senju Clan holding the position of Hokage.

Now, taking advantage of the decisive battle between Uchiha Madara and Senju Hashirama, he thought he had seized a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity, never expecting Senju Hashirama to return so quickly.

When Senju Hashirama and his group arrived at the Uchiha Clan grounds, they saw dozens of Uchiha clansmen gathered in the plaza, the atmosphere tense and oppressive.

At the head was a white-haired, grim-faced old man ; it was Elder Uchiha Hosai.

Seeing Senju Hashirama walking over unharmed, a flash of panic crossed Elder Uchiha Hosai’s face, but he quickly forced himself to remain calm.

« Shodai(1st)-sama, you have returned. » Elder Uchiha Hosai squeezed out a smile, « We were just discussing how to support the front lines ; we didn’t expect you to have already... »

« Enough. » Senju Hashirama interrupted him, saying in a low voice, « I already know your plan. »

Elder Uchiha Hosai’s face stiffened, then he gritted his teeth : « Since Shodai(1st)-sama already knows, I won’t hide it from you anymore. »

« That’s right, I intended to take advantage of this opportunity to seize the position of Hokage. But I did this for the future of the Uchiha Clan ! »

« For the future of the Uchiha Clan ? » Senju Hashirama sneered, « Stabbing me in the back while I was in a decisive battle with Madara—is this what you call 'the future' ? »

Elder Uchiha Hosai’s face turned beet red : « Hashirama ! Don’t think that just because you defeated Madara, you can act so high and mighty ! »

« Our Uchiha Clan has never been inferior to you Senju ! Why should you be the ones to sit in the Hokage seat ? ! »

« Because you constantly have the clan on your lips, you are not fit to be Hokage. » Senju Tobirama said coldly.

This has nothing to do with strength ; it has to do with capacity.

To this day, both Senju Hashirama and he himself had long since transcended the shackles of their clans.

To be honest, if one day the Uchiha spoke only of the « Village » instead of the « Uchiha, » he would no longer hold such great hostility toward them.

« What did you say ? ! » The few young clansmen behind Elder Uchiha Hosai were instantly enraged, and they all activated their Sharingan.

Seeing that a conflict was imminent, Senju Matsuaki suddenly smiled.

He stepped forward slowly, his gaze landing on Elder Uchiha Hosai : « Elder Uchiha Hosai, you are truly laughably narrow-minded ! »

Elder Uchiha Hosai’s face grew uglier and uglier ; he gritted his teeth and stared intently at Senju Matsuaki : « Brat, what do you know ? ! »

Senju Matsuaki shrugged : « I only know one thing. »

« You, immersed in the era of the past, are dragging the Uchiha Clan into the abyss ! »

This sentence completely enraged Elder Uchiha Hosai.

With the matter exposed, he knew he couldn’t escape punishment from Konoha, and according to the rules, his life would certainly be forfeit.

Then before he died, he would at least avenge his grandson who died back then !

He would cut off the future of the Senju Clan ! !

A trace of ruthlessness flashed in his eyes, his figure suddenly exploded into motion, the Kunai in his hand thrusting straight for Senju Matsuaki’s throat !

« Brat of the Senju Clan, go to hell ! ! »
