# Chapitre 177 — Tobirama Arrives! Kumogakure Retreats!

Senju Matsuaki’s pupils constricted slightly, the power of the Tremor-Tremor Fruit vibrating before him, forming a transparent barrier.

Years had passed, and his application of the Tremor-Tremor Fruit was no longer limited to mere imitation.

Instead, he had innovated and gained his own insights.

« Quake : Wall ! »

Clang— !

The Lightning Release hand blade struck the vibrating barrier fiercely, emitting a deafening sound like metal clashing against metal.

The two stood deadlocked, the lightning and the force of the tremors contending against each other, neither willing to yield an inch.

Senju Matsuaki frowned slightly, his other hand quietly resting on the hilt of the sword at his waist.

If the First Raikage was unwilling to back down, then he would have no choice but to...

Once Ryūjin Jakka was unsheathed, he was even confident that he could keep the First Raikage and the future Second Raikage here permanently !

But if that happened, it would undoubtedly contradict the guidelines of their Konoha mission this time.

The Ninja World would likely be plunged into chaos in an instant !

But if the First Raikage remained obstinate, he would no longer show mercy.

Just as both sides were deadlocked...

A silver flash suddenly appeared beside Senju Matsuaki.

This scene made the corners of Senju Matsuaki’s mouth curl up slightly, while the First Raikage’s expression suddenly turned gloomy.

Senju Tobirama was covered in a few water stains, evidently having rushed all the way from the Land of Water.

« Matsuaki, are you okay ? »

Senju Tobirama’s gaze swept quickly across the battlefield, and his brow furrowed slightly when he saw the First Raikage locked in a stalemate with Senju Matsuaki.

« The Six-Tails has already been sealed. Big Brother asked me to come over first to check on your progress. »

Senju Tobirama said, turning his gaze to the First Raikage, his voice calm but carrying an unquestionable majesty, « Raikage, are you intending to wage a full-scale war against my Konoha ? »

The First Raikage withdrew his hand blade, stepped back twice, and looked coldly at Senju Tobirama.

« Senju Tobirama... I didn’t expect you to come as well. »

« Why, am I not allowed to come ? »

Senju Tobirama crossed his arms, his expression indifferent, « Or are you saying that you think Konoha’s Ninjas are objects you can bully at will ? »

The First Raikage’s mouth twitched.

If Senju Hashirama had arrived in person, he might have had some reservations.

But while Senju Tobirama was also a Kage-level powerhouse, he wasn’t enough to make him retreat.

But the problem was, since Senju Tobirama had already arrived, could Senju Hashirama be far behind ?

Furthermore, the strength displayed by this Senju Matsuaki before him had far exceeded his expectations.

Coupled with that Uchiha girl who possessed the Mangekyo Sharingan, and the Byakugan Ninja watching covetously from the side...

Facing at least three Kage-level powerhouses, although they still had dozens of Chunin and Jonin from Kumogakure standing by, it seemed it wasn’t quite enough.

Additionally, Senju Tobirama’s Flying Thunder God Technique allowed him to come and go freely ; he was not confident in being able to keep him here.

If this battle continued, Kumogakure might not necessarily gain any advantage.

The First Raikage’s thoughts raced, and finally, he let out a cold snort, pulling a stiff smile onto his face.

« Senju Tobirama, don’t misunderstand. I was just passing by and saw several young people from your village fighting the Eight-Tails, so I intended to lend a hand. »

As the Kage of a village, he appeared to be a brute but was actually extremely calculating.

Changing his attitude now was just standard procedure.

He knew that the killing intent he had just revealed could not be hidden, and this lie could easily be punctured.

But that was not important ; what was important was that he was using these words to signal a ceasefire.

« Oh ? »

Senju Tobirama raised an eyebrow, « Is that so ? Then I must thank the Raikage for your kindness. »

« No need. »

The First Raikage waved his hand, his gaze falling on Senju Matsuaki, and he said meaningfully.

« Young man, your strength is quite good. However, the Ninja World is vast, so don’t get too carried away. »

Senju Matsuaki smiled slightly : « Thank you for your advice, Raikage. This Junior will remember it. »

The First Raikage took a deep look at him, said nothing more, and turned to walk in the direction of the Kumogakure Ninjas.

« Retreat ! »

At his command, those Kumogakure Ninjas who had been on high alert breathed a sigh of relief, helped the unconscious Dou up, and quickly retreated following the Raikage.

Not until the figures of the Kumogakure group had completely disappeared at the end of the valley did Senju Tobirama withdraw his gaze and look at the three, including Senju Matsuaki.

« Are you okay ? »

« We’re fine. »

Senju Matsuaki shook his head and took the sealing scroll from his bosom, « The Eight-Tails has already been sealed. »

Senju Tobirama took the scroll, checked it carefully, and nodded with satisfaction.

His gaze fell upon Senju Matsuaki, Uchiha Hikari, and Hyuga Tsukuyomi, and he praised them : « You all did very well. »

Senju Matsuaki asked : « Second Uncle, are we returning to the village now ? »

« Yes. »

Senju Tobirama nodded, « Big Brother and the others should also be returning to Konoha soon. Let’s go, we’ll talk when we get back. »

With Senju Tobirama there, Senju Matsuaki and the others didn’t have to endure the arduous journey back this time ; instead, they experienced the convenience of the Flying Thunder God Technique.

With a flash of silver light, the four of them, carrying the scroll sealed with the Eight-Tails, disappeared from the spot.

...

In the valley, the First Raikage stood on a giant rock, watching the four Konoha Ninjas disappear, his expression terrifyingly gloomy.

His guard, Hong, stood behind him, silent.

For a long time, the First Raikage suddenly smashed his fist into the rock wall beside him.

« Boom— ! »

The entire giant rock shattered under his fist, sending stone fragments flying.

« That brat called Senju Matsuaki... »

The First Raikage said through gritted teeth, « He must not be left alive ! »

Hong was slightly stunned : « Lord Raikage, you mean... »

« Didn’t you see ? »

The First Raikage turned around, his eyes full of wariness, « That brat, how old is he this year ? And he already possesses this kind of strength ! »

« Wood Release, that strange vibration ability, and that sword at his waist... »

« Although that sword was not unsheathed, I could vaguely feel the terrifying power within it ! »

He paused, his voice becoming heavier, « And, he is also Senju Hashirama’s nephew ! »

« Senju Hashirama is already headache-inducing enough, if this brat is allowed to grow up... »

The First Raikage didn’t finish his sentence, but Hong already understood what he meant.

One Senju Hashirama had already made the other four great nations extremely wary, and that was even when Senju Hashirama was always inclined towards peace.

Because Senju Hashirama was inclined toward peace, the other major Ninja villages were willing to wait, waiting for the day when they could have a chance to get ahead after Senju Hashirama passed away in the future.

But if another powerhouse of the same level appeared, and one who was also Senju Hashirama’s successor...

Then Konoha’s future would remain a great mountain hanging over the heads of the other Ninja villages, one that could never be surpassed !

« Therefore, we must find a way to eliminate him. »

The First Raikage’s gaze became firm, « At all costs ! »

« Yes ! »

The future Second Raikage, Hong, responded in a deep voice, though his gaze subconsciously glanced at Dou, who was receiving emergency treatment on the side.

The future of Kumogakure, in whom they had placed high hopes, couldn’t even take a single punch from the opponent just now...

It was indeed as the First Raikage said ; if Senju Matsuaki was not eliminated, Kumogakure would likely be suppressed by Konoha forever !
