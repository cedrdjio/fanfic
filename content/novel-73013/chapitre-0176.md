# Chapitre 176 — Fierce Battle! First Raikage!

The First Raikage and the future Second Raikage locked their sharp, hawk-like gazes onto Senju Matsuaki.

« Hong, you and I will join forces, » the First Raikage said in a deep voice, « Do not hold back. »

« Understood. »

Their Lightning Release Chakra converged and resonated in the air, erupting with an even more terrifying might.

Blue electrical arcs ravaged the valley like wild snakes, illuminating even the sky with a ghastly pallor.

Senju Matsuaki slowly exhaled and gripped the hilt of his blade once more.

The great battle was on the verge of breaking out.

The First Raikage’s words from just a moment ago echoed through the valley, his killing intent undisguised.

Senju Matsuaki glanced at him indifferently, a faint smile even curling at the corner of his mouth.

« Leave my corpse intact ? Raikage-Senior, isn’t that a bit premature ? »

« Brat, you don’t know the immensity of heaven and earth ! »

Before the words had even faded, the First Raikage’s figure had vanished from where he stood.

Blue lightning tore through the air, carrying destructive power as it lunged straight at Senju Matsuaki’s face.

The Raikage’s right fist was wrapped in dense Lightning Release Chakra ; as he threw the punch, the air was filled with a scorched scent.

Senju Matsuaki’s eyes narrowed, and he met the blow with his own right fist.

« Quake ! »

The white ripple of vibration collided violently with the blue lightning in mid-air.

« Boom— ! »

The collision of the two forces erupted with a deafening roar, and the shockwave spread outwards, kicking up gravel and dust from the ground several feet high.

The First Raikage’s figure faltered slightly, a flash of astonishment crossing his eyes.

Although he hadn’t used his full strength in that punch, it was certainly not something an ordinary Ninja could withstand.

No wonder Dou had been sent flying by a single punch from this vibrating power earlier !

Even he felt his Lightning Armor being shattered by the impact.

Fortunately, the Lightning Armor was formed from Chakra and could recover rapidly !

« Interesting. »

The First Raikage snorted coldly, the Lightning Release Chakra on his body surging once more, « I want to see just how much of Senju Hashirama’s strength you possess ! »

He condensed lightning in both hands, forming two short spears made of electricity, and thrust them towards Senju Matsuaki like a violent storm.

Senju Matsuaki did not back down ; the power of the Tremor-Tremor Fruit condensed on his fists, and every punch he threw was accompanied by the sound of shattering air, clashing fiercely with the Raikage’s lightning spears.

The two engaged in a fierce close-quarters battle in the valley, each collision triggering small-scale explosions, causing the ground beneath their feet to continuously crack and collapse.

Meanwhile, the guard Hong’s gaze locked onto Uchiha Hikari and Hyuga Tsukuyomi.

He didn’t hesitate in the slightest, his body wreathed in Lightning Release Chakra as he charged towards the two like a bolt of lightning.

Hong’s fist was also wrapped in violent electricity as he punched towards Uchiha Hikari and Hyuga Tsukuyomi.

Uchiha Hikari sneered, her Mangekyo Sharingan spinning violently.

« Susanoo ! »

A crimson Chakra skeleton condensed and took shape around her, quickly transforming into a half-body skeleton that blocked the punch.

The collision between the lightning and the Susanoo emitted a harsh, metallic clashing sound, sending sparks flying everywhere.

« Gentle Fist ! »

Taking advantage of this opportunity, Hyuga Tsukuyomi rushed in, her palms wrapped in Chakra as she struck at Hong’s vital Acupoints.

Hong frowned, twisting his body at an uncanny angle to dodge Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s attack, while simultaneously throwing a backhand punch towards her neck.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi’s pupils constricted, and she hurriedly retreated to dodge the blow.

« Tsukuyomi, watch out ! »

Uchiha Hikari’s voice rang out, and immediately after, the Mangekyo pattern in her eyes shifted abruptly.

« Amaterasu ! »

Black flames appeared out of thin air on Hong’s Lightning Armor, spreading rapidly.

Hong’s expression changed, and without hesitation, he controlled his own Lightning Release Chakra to detach from the black flames, leaping several feet backward to distance himself from the two.

« Black flames... »

Hong’s gaze grew grave, « Is this the ability of the Uchiha Clan’s Mangekyo Sharingan ? »

« To think that after Uchiha Madara, another pair of such eyes has appeared in Konoha’s Uchiha Clan ! »

Uchiha Hikari did not answer, merely using another ocular technique to manipulate the black flames of Amaterasu.

« Kagutsuchi ! »

Under her control, the black flames changed shape, turning into several black fire arrows that shot towards Hong.

Hong dared not be careless, his hands quickly forming Seals.

« Lightning Release : Pillar Thunder Spear ! »

He pushed his hands forward, and a thick pillar of lightning surged from his palms, colliding with the black fire arrows.

« Sizzle— ! »

The lightning and black flames eroded each other, emitting a harsh, screeching sound.

Hyuga Tsukuyomi seized the opportunity to circle to the side, her Byakugan locked tightly onto the flow of Hong’s Chakra, searching for his flaw.

« His Lightning Armor defense is very strong, but there is a tiny gap in the Chakra flow on his left shoulder ! » Hyuga Tsukuyomi warned loudly.

Uchiha Hikari heard this, her Mangekyo spinning once more, and her Susanoo, wielding a crimson katana, slashed down from above.

Hong was forced to dodge repeatedly, his offensive momentum suddenly suppressed by the two.

On the other side, the battle between Senju Matsuaki and the First Raikage had entered a white-hot stage.

« Wood Release : Deep Forest Emergence ! »

Senju Matsuaki formed Seals with both hands, and countless thick vines burst from the ground, winding towards the First Raikage.

« This level of Wood Release is still far inferior to you, Senju Hashirama ! »

The First Raikage snorted coldly, his body surging with lightning, slicing through all the approaching vines.

But Senju Matsuaki had never expected Wood Release : Deep Forest Emergence to trap the Raikage ; he was just using it to buy time.

Just as the First Raikage said, his Wood Release was indeed still some distance away from Senju Hashirama’s.

But this gap was not in the Wood Release itself, but in Sage Art.

Senju Hashirama’s Wood Release had the augmentation of Sage Art, reaching another level compared to ordinary Wood Release.

Under the cover of the vines, Senju Matsuaki’s right fist had already condensed the power of the Tremor-Tremor Fruit, a white halo flickering on his fist.

« Quake : Shatter ! »

He threw a punch, the sound of shattering air resounding through the heavens, and a huge white crack spread towards the First Raikage.

The First Raikage’s expression changed slightly ; he could feel the terrifying power contained in this punch and dared not take it head-on, his body wrapped in Lightning Release Chakra as he turned into a bolt of lightning to dodge to the side.

The Vibrational Power grazed his shoulder, shattering a huge boulder behind him with a boom.

« Good kid... »

The First Raikage steadied himself, his gaze towards Senju Matsuaki filled with a touch of genuine respect, « It seems I truly underestimated you. »

Senju Matsuaki shook his slightly numb right hand, his expression unchanged.

« Raikage, continuing to fight will do no good for either of us. I have already sealed the Eight-Tails ; you cannot keep us here. »

« Cannot keep you ? »

The First Raikage sneered, lightning surging again, « Lariat ! »

His body surged with lightning, transforming into a bolt of blue electricity, charging towards Senju Matsuaki at a speed difficult for the naked eye to capture.

His right hand was shaped into a hand blade, condensing highly concentrated Lightning Release Chakra, stabbing straight at Senju Matsuaki’s heart.
