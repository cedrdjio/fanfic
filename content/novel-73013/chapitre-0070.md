# Chapitre 70 — Senju Tobirama's Surveillance Room

Somewhere in the virgin forest.

Violent thunder swirled around Dou, and he held his right hand up in front of his chest in the shape of a hand blade.

The surrounding trees had snapped in the middle and fallen in all directions, with charred marks visible near the broken stumps.

There were over a dozen craters of varying depths on the ground, seemingly caused by some kind of explosive.

In front of Dou lay an Iwagakure Ninja, clutching a bleeding arm.

« I once encountered an Iwagakure Ninja who used Explosion Release during a mission, but you are far inferior to him. »

« But come to think of it, you are only a Genin, so that is understandable. »

Dou stepped over the craters of various sizes on the ground, slowly closing in on the fallen Iwagakure Ninja.

Explosion Release is a Bloodline Limit from Iwagakure with immense explosive power.

« I don’t have what you need ; my piece is blank. »

The Iwagakure Ninja struggled to pull out a piece he had hidden on his body, showing the blank face of the piece to Dou.

However, Dou did not feel disappointed looking at the blank piece ; instead, his eyes were filled with killing intent.

« What I want is your life ! »

In the next instant, lightning flashed, and Dou’s figure vanished from his spot, reappearing beside the Iwagakure Ninja.

His hand blade, wreathed in violent thunder, slashed across the Iwagakure Ninja’s elbow, severing the arm as if it were tofu.

With the arm severed and the wound stimulated by electricity, the intense pain caused the Iwagakure Ninja to curl up like a shrimp.

However, the piece had already fallen into the dirt along with the severed arm, making it impossible for him to crush it to escape, costing him his last chance at survival.

« Are you not afraid of starting a war between Iwagakure and Kumogakure by doing this ! ? »

The Iwagakure Ninja wailed, his bloodshot eyes fixed on Dou with despair and hatred that reached deep into his marrow.

« Fool, if I kill you, who would know I did it ? »

Dou sneered, lifted his sturdy right leg, and stomped down hard with a foot crackling with thunder.

A clear cracking sound rang out, followed by crimson blood that began to spread across the ground.

Watching the corpse on the ground gradually grow cold, Dou lowered his head in silence for a moment, then turned and left without looking back.

The relationship between the various Ninja villages has always been one of competition ; peace is only fleeting, while conflict is the main theme.

Killing an undeveloped Explosion Release Ninja from Iwagakure might prevent dozens of Kumogakure Ninja from dying at the hands of an Explosion Release Ninja in the future.

He would not be soft-hearted about nipping an opponent’s future in the bud.

...

Konoha, Hokage Building, inside a dimly lit office.

Senju Tobirama sat calmly in the middle of the desk, with a dozen other Ninja wearing Konoha forehead protectors sitting beside him.

Besides a few files neatly stacked in the corner, a crystal ball was placed in front of each person on the desk.

If the situation here were known to the representatives of the other Ninja villages, they would likely be astonished.

Outside of the monitoring room where Senju Hashirama and the representatives of the other Ninja villages were currently located, there was actually another monitoring room containing over a dozen crystal balls !

And even more shockingly, the crystal balls in this monitoring room did not just show fleeting images, but provided a continuous view of the virgin forest.

The image on one of the crystal balls was exactly the scene of Dou killing the Explosion Release Ninja.

« Lord Tobirama, this situation... »

A Ninja, seeing this bloody scene, looked at Senju Tobirama with some hesitation.

« There is no need to intervene ; this does not affect us. » Senju Tobirama’s expression remained unchanged.

« Yes ! »

Looking at the crystal balls covering the desk in front of him, a flash of satisfaction crossed Senju Tobirama’s eyes.

As the host, it was impossible for Konoha not to have prepared any contingency plans for itself.

The inability to monitor the second stage of the exam in real-time was merely a smokescreen he had thrown to the other Ninja villages.

In fact, every piece engraved with the character « Jade » had been specially treated, and the Chakra within could be sensed by the Telescope Technique.

As long as Konoha was willing, they could monitor the situation around the ninety « Jade » pieces in real-time.

Through real-time monitoring, they could better understand the situation in the virgin forest and protect the safety of Konoha Ninja.

Another purpose for concealing the real-time monitoring was to eliminate potential Ninja from other villages.

Although Konoha had prepared a « safe exit » for this exam where one could withdraw by crushing the piece, a closer look revealed some loopholes.

For example, Ninja whose pieces were stolen by others, or Ninja who were killed before they could crush their pieces, could not receive timely rescue.

Senju Tobirama knew that Sunagakure and Kumogakure had both sent Ninja who were originally Jonin to infiltrate the Chunin Exams.

These people could not have failed to discover methods for killing during the exam, and they naturally had the motive to kill promising Genin from other Ninja villages.

Senju Tobirama had provided them with a « blind spot » where they could kill potential threats from other villages without being detected by the Telescope Technique.

Therefore, he didn’t need to do anything further ; Ninja like Dou and Yan would become the sharp blades he used to clear out geniuses from other villages.

This matter was too destructive to peace, so he hadn’t even told his older brother, Senju Hashirama.

The people assisting him in watching the crystal ball images in this office were all members of the intelligence department he personally led.

« These barbarians from Kumogakure are truly bloodthirsty ; this is already the third examinee to be killed. » A Ninja remarked with emotion.

« Lord Tobirama, if other Ninja villages apply pressure later... » another Ninja was concerned about the subsequent impact.

After all, Konoha was the host of this exam ; if any Ninja village were to vent their anger on Konoha due to the high number of Genin deaths, it could trigger a conflict.

« Just contact the Proctors responsible for the corresponding sectors in a timely manner and have them prepare investigation records. »

« When the time comes, if other Ninja villages want to demand an explanation from Konoha, just drag it out for a few days, then toss the investigation report at them. »

Speaking of this issue, Senju Tobirama was not overly worried ; clearly, he already had a contingency plan in mind.

Moreover, he viewed so-called war more realistically and thoroughly.

Since Konoha’s strength was currently superior to other Ninja villages, no matter how dissatisfied the others were, war would not start easily.

If Konoha were to become weak in the future, even if they hadn’t done anything to create conflict, other Ninja villages would manufacture reasons to start a war.

Since that was the case, it was better to nip these potential threats in the bud.

It was a pity that his older brother, Senju Hashirama, was stubborn and too idealistic and naive ; these logical points could not be explained to him.

Thinking of his family, Senju Tobirama switched the image on the crystal ball, wanting to see how his eldest nephew was doing.

He had already started monitoring the situation at each starting point when the exam first began.

Upon seeing Senju Matsumi let the Sunagakure siblings, Chiyo and Ebizo, go, Senju Tobirama felt a bit regretful.

After all, among the younger generation of Sunagakure, these two siblings were quite outstanding, even if not the absolute top.

However, he could understand Senju Matsumi’s actions.

It was not easy to cover up a killing if done right at the starting point.
