# Chapitre 139 — The Sealed Land! Returning Empty-Handed?

Several days later.

« According to the markings on the map, this should be the location, but... »

At the foot of the mountain, Senju Matsuaki held a yellowed, worn-out map in one hand while comparing it with the surrounding scenery.

One could not expect a map from that era to be very detailed ; some of the patterns could only be described as resembling the actual terrain.

However, for a Ninja, being able to read such maps was considered one of the required courses.

« This place should have indeed been a large river decades ago. Although it has dried up quite a bit now, it matches the map. »

Senju Matsuaki glanced at a small stream flowing listlessly not far away, then looked towards the mountain col in the distance.

« The mountain col nestled between a high and a low mountain—this also matches the markings on the map. »

When more convincing landmarks appeared, Senju Matsuaki felt assured.

After putting away the scroll containing the map, he took a few quick steps forward and then stopped before an open space.

« If the map is accurate, this should be... »

Matsuaki pressed his right hand onto the ground.

A white light containing the power of vibration gathered into a sphere in his palm.

This time, there was no earth-shattering vibration or destruction ; there were only cracks spreading in all directions, centered on the point where the vibrational power made contact with the ground.

Soon, the ground split open, revealing things that had been buried for decades.

This place had been a temple many years ago.

Due to years of disrepair, it had perhaps collapsed decades ago.

Under the excavation of the vibrational power, the outline from back then could be vaguely seen.

The sturdy stone pillars, apart from inevitably bearing the traces of time, still stood on the ground just as they had decades ago.

Some roof tiles were buried deep in the soil ; some had been shattered during the previous collapse, while others had just been shattered moments ago.

These tiles had no cultural relic value, so if they were broken, they were broken.

Along with the tiles, the timber that once made up this temple was also revealed.

Buried under the dust for a long time, these timbers already showed signs of partial decay or initial carbonization.

« This location was indeed a temple back then, so the location of the Sealed Land must be in this vicinity... »

Senju Matsuaki did not pay any more attention to these broken bricks and tiles.

The location of the Sealed Land would naturally not be inside this temple.

For one, that would be disrespectful to the faith, and for another, it would be disregarding the lives of the monks and pilgrims too much.

The purpose of this temple was also to serve as a landmark on the map, providing guidance for the descendants of the Senju Clan who came to find it.

The true location of the Sealed Land was in an uninhabited clearing behind the temple.

Senju Matsuaki walked quickly towards the target location and eventually stopped at a patch of overgrown flat ground.

A stone stele stood on the ground ; having been untended for decades, it was covered in a thick layer of dust.

He had no intention of brushing the dust away with his hands ; he gently vibrated the power of vibration to shake off the dust without damaging the stele.

He hadn’t been idle during this time ; as his control over the Tremor-Tremor Fruit improved daily, such delicate operations were no longer a problem.

After the dust fell, the original patterns and text on the stele became clearly visible.

The first thing that caught Matsuaki’s eyes was the clan crest of the Senju Clan, which was obviously within his expectations.

Then, the pattern that appeared to the side made him slightly startled.

« This is... the clan crest of the Sarutobi Clan ? »

Although the clan crest of the Sarutobi Clan did not appear in the original work, since he grew up in Konoha and knew members of the Sarutobi Clan like Sarutobi Sasuke and Hiruzen Sarutobi, he had naturally seen this pattern.

What was unexpected, however, was that the clan crest of the Sarutobi Clan had actually appeared here as well.

In other words, this Sealed Land was not sealed by the Senju Clan alone ; the Sarutobi Clan had also participated in it.

Thinking of this, Senju Matsuaki’s curiosity grew even stronger.

What exactly was it that made the Senju Clan and the Sarutobi Clan join forces to Seal it ?

One must know that the Sarutobi Clan during the Warring States period, while not as strong as the Senju Clan or the Uchiha Clan, was still one of the powerful Ninja clans.

However, on second thought, the corner of his mouth twitched.

That is to say, not only his Senju Clan, but even the Sarutobi Clan had completely forgotten about this Sealed Land for decades.

After taking a deep breath, Senju Matsuaki continued to browse the other content on the stele.

« The Ultimate Evil Weapon... »

On the stele was a description of the ferocity of this so-called « Ultimate Evil Weapon, » as well as a warning to future generations.

The degree of mystification was very high, but the amount of information was extremely low.

This left Senju Matsuaki, who had originally intended to obtain some information from the stele, feeling numb.

Our ancestors of the Senju Clan really like being riddle-mongers, don’t they ?

He walked around the stele to the back, drew the short blade from his back, and simply cleared away the weeds on the ground.

What was revealed was a Seal surrounded by a circle of black curse marks.

Senju Matsuaki patiently bent down and, based on his knowledge of sealing techniques, began to check the Seal.

Although he himself was not skilled at using sealing techniques, knowing how to check if a Seal was effective was one of the required courses for a Ninja.

« It seems that the Senju Clan and the Sarutobi Clan really attached great importance to the target of the Seal back then ; this Seal is still effective to this day... »

Senju Matsuaki stood up, staring thoughtfully at the Seal on the ground.

For a moment, he was also in a bit of a dilemma.

From the perspective of the mission, he had already completed the mission by reaching this point.

Next, he only needed to return to Konoha and report the situation here ; this A-rank mission would then be declared complete.

But if he did that, he would definitely not be able to get the reward points from the Golden Finger platform mission.

For him, this was the main purpose of this trip ; the A-rank commission from Konoha was actually just a side task.

The difficulty, however, lay in the fact that he currently knew absolutely nothing about the sealed object.

He was not a reckless brute ; he naturally knew not to act rashly when intelligence was almost blank.

« Oh well, since it is an S-rank mission, I might as well wait until my strength reaches the Kage level in the future before coming back to investigate. »

Senju Matsuaki hesitated for a moment, but still chose the relatively safe approach.

Previously, he had planned to complete the S-rank mission of breaking the Seal released by the platform during this trip, with the prerequisite being that he could learn more intelligence at the Sealed Land.

With insufficient intelligence, the plan to break the Seal was naturally suspended.

Thinking this way, Senju Matsuaki intended to turn around and leave.

Since he had found the location of the Sealed Land on this trip, searching for it again after being fully prepared in the future would naturally save the effort of finding the way.

However, just as Matsuaki took his first step to leave, he seemed to recall something, and this step was retracted.

« Wait, I almost forgot... »

A flash of inspiration struck Senju Matsuaki’s mind, and he turned back to look at the Sealed Land surrounded by black curse marks.

He suddenly remembered that he still had one method he hadn’t used yet.

The Check-in System he had requisitioned a few days ago had a total of two check-in opportunities, and the targets for check-in could be selected as characters or locations.

The first check-in was on the day he accepted the A-rank mission, when he checked in on Senju Tobirama and obtained Edo Tensei.

And at this moment, there was still a second check-in opportunity...
