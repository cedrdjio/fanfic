# Chapitre 5 — The Weight of Vitality

The 41st year of the Hokage era, late spring.

Shuichi sat under the plum tree in the backyard, holding a twig in his hand, and drew a simple table on the ground.

Lue squatted on his shoulder, tilting its head from time to time to look at the gibberish on the ground, occasionally pecking at Shuichi’s ear with its beak to indicate it didn’t understand.

The table listed all the [Income and Expenditure] since he awakened his ability.

Plunder : Flight Instinct (Sparrow, stripped and given to Lue), Jumping Instinct (Grasshopper × 2 stacked, stripped), Climbing Ability (Tree Frog, in body, digesting), Carapace Hardness (Beetle, stripped), Compound Eye Vision (Dragonfly, one-third, in mental slot).

Currently held : Climbing (digesting), Compound Eye Vision (1/3).

Shuichi used the twig to draw a circle next to the table.

This was the problem.

The Plunder ability itself worked normally, and the stripping mechanism had been verified many times.

But his Growth speed did not reach expectations.

He could Plunder Climbing Ability, Jumping Instinct, and Carapace Hardness, but these abilities were all limited by the body of a five-year-old child.

If the Tree Frog’s Climbing Ability were placed on an adult Ninja, it might be able to climb a rock wall ten stories high.

But installed in his five-year-old body, climbing a ten-meter beech tree was already the limit.

It wasn’t that the ability wasn’t strong enough.

It was that the container carrying the ability was too small.

More importantly, the [Space] occupied by each ability was different.

The Climbing Ability Plundered from a Tree Frog and the Carapace Hardness Plundered from a Beetle occupied significantly different [Volumes] in the constitution slot.

The former was light and small, and putting it into his body felt almost no pressure ; the latter was much heavier and thicker, and when stuffed in, the entire constitution slot was filled by nearly one-third.

The strength of an ability depended on the subject being Plundered.

A Beetle’s carapace was stronger than a Tree Frog’s climbing, because the Beetle’s own Carapace Hardness and body size were right there.

The stronger the ability, the larger the slot occupied.

If he Plundered a Taijutsu ability from an adult Ninja, one ability might fill his entire current constitution slot, and might even overflow.

Shuichi marked circles of different sizes next to [Tree Frog] and [Beetle] respectively.

So, what if he Plundered abilities from more powerful creatures ?

The effect would be stronger, but the occupation would also be larger.

This was a trade-off.

And if he could expand the total capacity of the constitution slot, the balance of this trade-off would tilt.

Shuichi raised his head, his gaze passing through the branches and leaves of the plum tree, casting into the distance.

He thought of something, the most basic thing that all living creatures possessed.

Not climbing, not jumping, not carapace.

But the underlying energy that supported all of this.

Vitality.

If he could directly Plunder Vitality to strengthen his own constitution base, would the total slot capacity expand accordingly ?

« Go to the Mixed Forest. »

Shuichi stood up, and Lue flapped its wings and flew off his shoulder.

In the Mixed Forest, Shuichi crouched by the bushes, reached his right hand into the grass, and his fingertips touched a bright green grasshopper lying on a blade of grass.

Perception unfolded.

Beneath the Jumping Instinct and chitinous carapace, there was a thin layer of Vitality that shimmered with warmth.

It was as thin as the dew on the grass in the early morning, which would dissipate with a gust of wind.

Shuichi concentrated his consciousness, and the suction of his right hand erupted precisely.

The layer of warm light inside the grasshopper was drawn out, flowing into his body along his palm.

The grasshopper’s body shriveled instantly ; although the shell remained, the inside was empty.

And Shuichi felt a tiny bit of warmth, which dissipated after traveling less than two inches on his right arm.

It was like pouring a cup of water into a river ; it was indeed poured, but the river surface didn’t even ripple.

He looked down at his hand.

There was no change.

« Too little. »

Shuichi frowned.

The Vitality of a grasshopper was probably less than one-fifth of that of a sparrow.

Plundering the Flight Instinct was a conceptual operation with high consumption ; but simply Plundering Vitality, a basic attribute shared by all living things, had much lower consumption.

However, the gain was also minimal.

For a creature of the grasshopper’s level, the total amount of Vitality was just that little.

Shuichi continued anyway.

He searched through the bushes and piles of fallen leaves for half an hour, Plundering Vitality from twelve grasshoppers, four beetles, and a green caterpillar as thick as a finger.

Each time he Plundered, that faint warmth stacked a layer in his body.

The feeling of twelve grasshoppers stacked together was roughly equivalent to soaking in warm water for five minutes : his body felt slightly warm, and there was a very subtle sense of fullness deep in his bones, but if he didn’t feel carefully, he would ignore it.

The capacity of the constitution slot had indeed increased.

But the increase was extremely small. If the original total constitution capacity was a small teacup, after Plundering the Vitality of twelve grasshoppers and four beetles, the water line had only risen by less than two millimeters.

« For insect-level creatures, the total Vitality is too low.

It took twelve times of stacking to barely perceive a change ; relying on this to double his constitution would require hundreds or thousands of them.

And the continuous mental consumption of Plundering adds up to quite a bit. »

Shuichi noted down silently in his heart.

He needed larger prey.

Shuichi’s gaze swept across the ground of the Mixed Forest.

This forest was too small ; the largest animal was a tree lizard.

He spent ten minutes finding one next to a rock warmed by the sun.

From snout to tail, it was about half the length of his arm, and its gray-green scales were well camouflaged among the dead leaves.

Shuichi slowly extended his right hand and placed it on the back of the tree lizard.

Perception unfolded.

The Vitality inside the tree lizard was more than an order of magnitude stronger than that of the grasshopper.

If the grasshopper was dew, the tree lizard was a bowl of water ; although it was still not much, at least it had a sense of volume.

At the same time, the tree lizard also had Climbing Ability and the instinct of tail regeneration.

Shuichi chose Vitality.

The suction of his right hand erupted in a deeper way.

The warm light inside the tree lizard was drawn out from every muscle, every bone, and every scale, flowing into Shuichi’s body along the channel of his palm.

This warm current was several times stronger than that of the grasshopper ; it wasn’t scalding, but a more lasting warmth.

It traveled up from his right arm to his chest, dissipated in his chest cavity, and then went down along his spine, permeating his legs and soles.

The tree lizard shriveled in his palm.

The scales remained, but the muscles and bones underneath were empty.

Shuichi let out a long breath and moved his limbs.

The change in the sense of strength was still not obvious.

After all, it was just a tree lizard.

But the constitution slot in his body indeed had a little more space.

It was about equivalent to one-tenth of a slot.

« The Vitality of a medium-sized tree lizard is approximately equal to ten to twelve grasshoppers. »

Shuichi calculated in his heart, « The mental consumption is about five times that of a grasshopper.

In terms of exchange ratio, the tree lizard is more cost-effective ; it exchanges less mental power for more Vitality. »

But even so, the increase was just a drop in the bucket.

He needed more and larger prey.

Shuichi sat down on the rock, and Lue circled twice from above and landed on his shoulder.

Its feathers shone with a grayish-brown luster in the afternoon sun, and its small, bright black eyes sized up its master.

« Not enough. »

Shuichi looked at the palm of his right hand, « The things in this forest are too small.

Insects, tree lizards—even if he turned the whole forest over and Plundered it once, the improvement wouldn’t have a qualitative leap. »

He needed to go to farther places.

The mountainous forest area north of the Training Ground, the dense forest upstream of the Naka River.

But those places were not for a five-year-old child to go to alone.

Lue.

Shuichi tilted his head and looked at the fledgling eagle on his shoulder that was preening its feathers.

Lue could already fly.

Although it couldn’t fly high enough or far enough yet, its flight ability was improving every day.

In a while, its wingspan would be wider, and its endurance would be longer.

By then, Lue could fly to places his feet couldn’t reach, and scout for him whether there was prey worth risking to go to in the depths of those mountains and forests.

« Let’s go. »

Shuichi stood up.

Lue flew off his shoulder, circled half a turn above the Mixed Forest, and then followed his footsteps toward the Uchiha Clan Grounds.

The sound of the Naka River gradually faded away behind him.

A faint pulsation came from the palm of his right hand.

Although the amount of Plunder today was small, the frequency was high enough, and his palm was temporarily satisfied.
