# Chapitre 83 — Assignment

The final class at the Ninja Academy.

The atmosphere in the classroom was different from usual. No one whispered, no one passed notes, and no one snuck glances out the window. Everyone sat in their seats, looking at the teacher on the podium as if listening attentively for the first time.

The teacher stood before the blackboard, wasting no time on idle chatter. He unfolded the mission assignment list in his hands, projected it onto the blackboard using a projection technique, and began reading off the names one by one.

« Uchiha Obito, » the teacher said. « Assigned to Minato Namikaze’s squad. »

« Rin Nohara, assigned to Minato Namikaze’s squad. »

Obito’s eyes lit up.

Both of them were in the same squad. Obito clenched his fists, his lips trembling slightly as if he wanted to say something but held back.

The teacher continued reading.

« Uchiha Tetsuya, assigned to the Intelligence Support Squad. »

Tetsuya stood up in silence and walked to the podium to collect his mission scroll. His complexion was still somewhat pale, but better than it had been a few days ago. He took the scroll, glanced down at it, then tucked it into his clothes without a word.

« Uchiha Shuichi, assigned to the Frontline Support Squad, Team 7. »

Shuichi stood up and walked to the podium. The teacher stamped the mission scroll and handed it to him. Shuichi took the scroll and glanced at its contents.

The Frontline Support Squad, Team 7, was responsible for supply transport and casualty evacuation on the northern border of the Land of Fire. The captain was a Chunin named Tanaka, and the five team members were all students who had graduated early this time.

Shuichi tucked the scroll away and returned to his seat.

The teacher continued reading names on the blackboard, one by one, until every student had been called. No one in the classroom spoke ; only the teacher’s voice echoed in the air.

After finishing, the teacher put down the mission assignment list.

« From today on, you are no longer students, » the teacher said. « You are Ninjas. You are Ninjas of Konoha. »

He looked at everyone in the classroom, his gaze sweeping over those young faces.

« War is cruel. You may get injured, you may lose teammates, you may face death. But you are Ninjas of Konoha. Konoha needs you. »

No one in the classroom spoke.

« Missions begin tomorrow, » the teacher said. « Go home and prepare today. Report to your respective mission assembly points at 7 :00 tomorrow morning. »

He paused, as if wanting to say something, but in the end, he just nodded.

« Class dismissed. »

---

At the classroom door, students filed out one by one.

Obito walked up to Shuichi and patted him on the shoulder.

« Shuichi, » Obito said, a light in his eyes that looked like excitement or anticipation. « See you on the battlefield. »

Shuichi looked at him and nodded. « See you on the battlefield. »

Obito smiled and turned to run toward the assembly point for Minato’s squad. His footsteps were light, hardly like someone about to go to war. He ran down the corridor, through the school gate, and along the street, like a child heading out for an excursion.

Shuichi watched his back disappear around the street corner.

Tetsuya walked over and stood beside Shuichi. His complexion was still very pale, but at least he wasn’t trembling like he had been a few days ago.

« I was assigned to the Intelligence Support Squad, » Tetsuya said, his voice low. « I don’t have to go to the front lines. »

Shuichi nodded. « I was assigned to a Frontline Support Squad. »

Tetsuya looked at him with worry in his eyes. « Shuichi, you... »

« I’m fine, » Shuichi said. « Don’t worry. »

Tetsuya was silent for a few seconds, then nodded. He turned and walked in the other direction, his pace much slower than Obito’s, as if he were deep in thought.

Shuichi walked back toward the Uchiha Clan Compound alone.

He passed the bulletin board, the commercial street, and the wounded soldiers leaning on crutches. His footsteps were steady, and his expression calm, like an ordinary Ninja student who had just received his mission scroll and was preparing to go home to pack.

No one noticed him.

Returning home, Shuichi entered his room and closed the door.

Lue flew down from the windowsill and landed on his shoulder. Its black feathers shimmered in the sunlight, and its crow appearance made it look like an ordinary pet.

« You were assigned to a Frontline Support Squad, » Lue’s voice was very soft. « Going to the battlefield ? »

Shuichi did not answer. He walked to the window and looked at the street outside.

« Leaving tomorrow morning, » he said. « Prepare first. »

Lue didn’t ask again.

Footsteps came from outside the door. Minako’s voice came from the kitchen : « Shuichi, you’re back ? »

Shuichi opened the door and walked out.

« I’m back, » he said.

Minako stood at the kitchen doorway, looking at him. There was a sense of worry in her eyes, as if she were confirming that he was still alive and still in front of her.

« Leaving tomorrow ? » she asked.

« Yes, » Shuichi said. « Assembly at 7 :00 in the morning. »

Minako lowered her head, as if wanting to say something, but in the end, she just nodded.

« I’ll go prepare your things, » she said, turning into the kitchen.

Shuichi watched her back without speaking.

He knew what she was worried about. His father had died in the war. Now it was his turn to go.

He walked into the kitchen and stood beside her.

« I’ll help you, » he said.

Minako looked up at him, then nodded.

The two of them prepared the items to take for tomorrow together. Ninja tools, soldier pills, first aid kits, and a change of clothes. Minako checked every item twice to ensure nothing was missing.

Shuichi stood to the side, watching her movements.

He knew she wanted to say something. But she didn’t.

Some words were too heavy to say.

---

The next morning, before dawn.

Shuichi stood in his room, hands forming signs.

Shadow Clone Technique.

Chakra surged, and a clone separated from his body. The clone looked exactly like Shuichi : an eleven-year-old face, black hair, wearing a Ninja combat uniform.

But this clone was different from a normal Shadow Clone.

Shuichi infused the clone with the concept of reality and the concept of recovery. The concept of reality turned the clone into a solid entity—not a mere Chakra phantom, but a truly existing body. The concept of recovery gave the clone the regenerative abilities of the Uzumaki Bloodline, allowing it to heal itself if injured.

The Chakra level was controlled at the Jonin level.

Too strong would attract attention.

The clone opened its eyes, revealing ordinary black pupils. It looked at Shuichi and nodded.

« I’m off, » the clone said.

Shuichi nodded.

The clone flew out the window and headed toward the assembly point. It was very fast, like a black shadow, disappearing into the morning mist in the blink of an eye.

The original body remained in the room.

Lue landed on the windowsill, watching the direction where the clone had vanished.

« You don’t want those Ninjas on the battlefield ? » Lue asked.

Shuichi did not answer. He looked at the sky gradually brightening outside the window, watching the figures starting to stir in the Village.

« There is nothing I need on the battlefield for the time being, » he said.

Lue didn’t ask again.

Shuichi turned and walked out of the room. Minako was already up, preparing breakfast in the kitchen. She paused when she saw Shuichi.

« You’re ready so early ? »

« Yes, » Shuichi said. « I’m leaving. »

Minako put down what she was holding and walked up to him. She looked at him, as if wanting to say something, but finally just reached out and straightened his collar.

« Take care, » she said, her voice very soft. « Come back alive. »

Shuichi looked at her and nodded.

« I will. »

He turned and walked toward the door, pushed it open, and stepped into the morning mist.

Lue followed him outside the window, while Haku lay in the courtyard watching his retreating figure. Fang’s pheromone web spread throughout the Village, sensing everything.

Shuichi walked down the street, looking at the few extra lines of casualty lists on the bulletin board, the wounded soldiers leaning on crutches, and the Ninjas hurrying along.

The war had only just begun.

He would survive this war, become stronger, and then wait for the real opportunity.
