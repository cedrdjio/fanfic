# Chapitre 59 — Returning Home

Late at night.

Konoha was shrouded in moonlight, and the streets were deserted.

A figure flashed past the edge of the Village, silently vaulted over the wall of the Uchiha Clan Grounds, and snuck into his home from the backyard.

Lue, in the form of a crow, landed on his shoulder.

« We’re home, » Lue whispered.

Shuichi said nothing and walked toward his room along the shadows of the backyard.

The night in the Uchiha Clan Grounds was very quiet, with only the occasional sound of dogs barking in the distance. Shuichi’s home was on the edge of the clan grounds, a simple wooden house with a plum tree planted in the yard.

He climbed through the window, landing almost soundlessly.

The room was dark, with only moonlight shining in through the window. Shuichi activated his Sharingan and scanned the room ; everything was exactly as he had left it—the desk, the bookshelf, the tatami mats, and the figure of his Shadow Clone sitting in the corner.

The Shadow Clone was sitting cross-legged on the tatami mat. Hearing the sound at the window, it immediately opened its eyes, the Three-Tomoe Sharingan activating automatically. Its body tensed, and its right hand already reached for the short blade at its waist.

Alert.

But the next instant, the Shadow Clone saw Shuichi’s face, and its body relaxed.

« You’re back, » the Shadow Clone said in a low voice, afraid of waking Minako in the next room.

« Yeah, » Shuichi said, walking into the room and closing the window.

The Shadow Clone stood up and stretched its stiff body. Four months of living as a substitute, although spent mostly in school and daily family life, had still taken a significant mental toll.

« Thanks for your hard work, » Shuichi said, patting the Shadow Clone on the shoulder.

The Shadow Clone shook its head and then dissipated into a puff of white smoke.

Four months of memories flooded back.

Daily life at school. The boredom in class. The roughhousing during breaks. Tetsuya’s complaints. Obito’s progress.

Minako’s changes. Recently, she would always cook an extra dish, then stare blankly at Shuichi’s bowl. Occasionally, she would ask Shuichi, « Are you too tired lately ? » and Shuichi would reply, « I’m fine. » She didn’t press him, but Shuichi knew she had sensed something.

Shuichi closed his eyes, slowly digesting these memories.

Four months.

The Shadow Clone had maintained his daily life in Konoha, while his real body had killed hundreds in the Land of Grass, awakened the Mangekyo Sharingan, and defeated Jiraiya, one of the Sannin.

With these two completely different lives overlapping, there was an indescribable sense of dislocation.

Shuichi opened his eyes and looked at his familiar room.

There were no enemies here, no fighting, no killing. Only the quiet Konoha Village outside the window and his mother sleeping in the next room.

He lay down on the tatami mat, looking at the ceiling.

He felt a strange sense of peace.

This was home.

Shuichi closed his eyes and quickly drifted off to sleep.

---

Early morning.

Sunlight shone in through the window, landing on Shuichi’s face.

He opened his eyes and felt the state of his body.

His mental energy had returned to its peak, and his Chakra had recovered to eighty percent. The fatigue from the Mangekyo Sharingan had completely faded after a night’s rest.

Shuichi stood up and walked out of the room.

Sounds of pots and pans came from the kitchen, along with the aroma of miso soup drifting over.

« Shuichi, are you up ? » Minako’s voice came from the kitchen.

« Yeah, » Shuichi said, rubbing his eyes as he walked toward the kitchen.

Shuichi walked into the kitchen and saw Minako busy at the stove. She was wearing an apron, her hair tied loosely behind her head, and her face held the gentle drowsiness of early morning.

« Breakfast will be ready soon. Go wash up, » Minako said without turning around.

« Okay. »

After washing up, Shuichi returned to the dining table, where Minako had already set out breakfast.

Shuichi sat down and picked up his chopsticks.

Minako sat opposite him, watching him eat.

« Did you sleep well last night ? » Minako asked, resting her chin in her hands.

« Yeah, very well, » Shuichi nodded.

« That’s good, » Minako smiled, picked up her chopsticks, and placed a piece of fried egg into Shuichi’s bowl. « You’ve been training too hard lately, eat more. »

Shuichi said « Yeah » and continued eating.

Minako watched him, something unspoken in her eyes, but she chose not to ask.

Morning.

Shuichi sat under the plum tree in the yard, basking in the sun.

It was the weekend, allowing Shuichi to get some proper rest.

« The time should be almost here, » Shuichi muttered to himself.

Then, Lue descended from high above, folded its wings, and landed on the courtyard wall.

It returned to its hawk form, its wingspan over two meters, looking majestic in the sunlight.

Minako walked out of the house carrying a basket of washed clothes, intending to hang them in the yard.

When she looked up and saw Lue on the wall, she froze.

« Lue ? » The basket in her hand nearly slipped.

She quickly placed the basket on the ground, hurried to the wall, and looked up at Lue.

« You’re back ? » Minako’s voice trembled slightly.

« Where did you go... I haven’t seen you for months... » She reached out to touch Lue, then pulled her hand back, as if afraid Lue would fly away.

« I thought you wouldn’t come back... »

Lue tilted its head, looking at Minako.

« I went off to train with my kin, and now that the training is over, I’ve returned. »

Minako’s hand froze in mid-air.

She stared blankly at Lue, her mouth slightly agape, unable to speak for a long time.

« You... you can talk ? » Minako’s eyes widened.

Lue tilted its head : « Yeah. »

Minako stared at Lue for a long while, as if confirming whether Lue was actually speaking.

Shuichi leaned under the plum tree, watching this scene without a word.

A smile appeared on Minako’s face.

« You... you’ve changed a lot, » Minako said softly, reaching out to stroke Lue’s feathers. « I haven’t seen you for months, you’ve changed a lot. »

« That’s good. It’s good that you’re back, » her voice choked up slightly.

Then she turned and walked back into the house, bringing out a plate of fish.

« You must be hungry, right ? Have something to eat, » Minako placed the plate on the wall.

Lue looked at the plate of fish, lowered its head, and began to eat bit by bit.

Minako stood by the wall, watching Lue eat the fish, the corners of her mouth slightly upturned.

« Training with your kin... » she murmured to herself, « Is that why you can talk now ? »

« Yeah, » Lue replied vaguely, still holding fish in its beak.

Shuichi leaned under the plum tree, watching this scene, the corners of his mouth also slightly upturned.

Clearly, having raised Lue for so long, Minako treated Lue as half a family member.

That was why its return made her so happy.

Afternoon.

Shuichi helped Minako clean the yard and prune the branches of the plum tree.

Minako watered the flowers nearby, occasionally chatting with Shuichi.

« Mom, how is the clan doing lately ? » Shuichi asked, turning his head.

« Everything is as usual, » Minako straightened her back and wiped the sweat from her forehead. « The Military Police Force has changed another batch of people. Your father’s former colleagues have come by a few times, asking if we need any help. »

« Did they say anything ? » Shuichi stopped his shears.

« Nothing special, » Minako shook her head. « Just asking if we have any difficulties in life. I told them everything is fine. »

Shuichi gave an « Mm » and continued pruning the branches.

After a while, Minako stopped what she was doing and looked at Shuichi.

« It’s just that there has been more talk about the Uchiha Clan in the Village lately than before, » her tone was somewhat heavy.

« What kind of talk ? » Shuichi looked up.

« They say we are too arrogant and don’t associate with the villagers. And some say the Military Police Force has too much power, » Minako sighed. « These words are harsh to hear. »

« Don’t pay attention to that, » Shuichi walked over and patted Minako on the shoulder.

« I know, » Minako smiled. « I’m just giving you a heads-up. »

Shuichi looked at Minako’s smile, his heart tightening slightly.

She was worried.

She didn’t say it, but she could feel the atmosphere in the Village changing. The rift between the Uchiha Clan and the villagers was deepening, and this rift would sooner or later cause problems.

Shuichi turned and continued pruning the plum tree.

The plum tree had been planted for many years and would bloom every winter. Minako loved this tree because it had been planted by Shuichi’s father.

Lue perched on the wall, occasionally interjecting with a brief sentence.

« Prune that branch shorter. »

« The one on the left is too dense. »

Hearing Lue’s words, Minako would still be slightly taken aback, then smile.

She was still getting used to the fact that Lue could talk.

Night.

Shuichi lay on the tatami mat in his room, looking at the ceiling.

The moonlight shone in through the window, casting a silvery glow on the ceiling.

After returning from this trip to the Land of Grass, his mindset had undergone a subtle change.

Before, home was just a place that needed protection. Leaving Konoha, getting stronger, killing—all of it was to protect himself on the night of the clan annihilation. At that time, home was just a burden.

But now, it was different.

With the Mangekyo Sharingan awakened and his strength surpassing the Sannin, Shuichi now possessed enough power to protect himself. Home was no longer a burden, but a bond.

Minako’s smile. Lue perched on the wall eating fish. The scent of the plum tree in the yard. The aroma of miso soup in the kitchen.

These things were more real than the hundreds he had killed, the Mangekyo he had awakened, or the Sannin he had defeated.

Shuichi closed his eyes.

He would continue to get stronger in the future. But the reason for getting stronger was no longer just for self-preservation.

It was also to protect these ordinary things.
