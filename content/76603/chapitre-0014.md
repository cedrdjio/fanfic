# Chapitre 14 — After School

Three days after the Chakra refinement class, Shuichi resumed his after-school hunting.

School ended at three in the afternoon, and it took about a quarter of an hour to walk from the school gate to the Uchiha Clan Grounds.

Shuichi usually parted ways with Tetsuya at the entrance of the clan grounds, because Tetsuya’s home was to the south, while Shuichi’s home was on the north side, towards the Naka River.

After parting ways, Shuichi wouldn’t go straight home ; instead, he would follow the path along the Naka River upstream, pass through the Training Ground, and enter the border area between the pine forest and the mixed forest.

Plunder was already waiting.

Every afternoon when the third period ended, Shuichi could see Plunder circling in the sky to the northwest from the classroom window ; its internal clock was more accurate than any timer.

When Shuichi walked into the pine forest, Plunder glided down silently from a high branch, its wings brushing against the pine needles with a faint rustling sound as it landed on Shuichi’s outstretched forearm.

« What do you have today ? » Shuichi stroked Plunder’s chest feathers.

Plunder’s feathers were denser than before he started school, its wingspan was nearing one point three meters, and the grip of its talons was strong enough to snap a branch as thick as a thumb.

Plunder let out a short cry and tilted its head towards the northeast.

Shuichi followed the direction indicated by Plunder through the pine forest.

The spring pine needles carpeting the ground were as soft as treading on cotton, and the air was filled with the mixed scent of pine resin and new leaves.

His footsteps were much lighter than before he started school ; this wasn’t due to his Climbing Ability, but Chakra.

By channeling a small amount of Chakra from his Dantian to attach to the soles of his feet, a very thin cushion of air formed between his shoe soles and the pine needles with every step, reducing the sound of his footsteps to almost zero.

After walking for about a quarter of an hour, the familiar border of the mixed forest appeared before his eyes.

The dried-up riverbed was the same as usual, and the carcass of the Red Fox on the riverbed had long since been decomposed.

Shuichi crouched by the edge of the riverbed, pressed his right hand onto the gravel ground, and sensed the direction Plunder was circling.

Plunder circled the riverbed twice, then dived towards the north-northwest direction.

Shuichi lowered his body and crossed the riverbed.

The Chakra attached to his soles allowed him to move across the gravel ground with almost no sound.

To the west of the riverbed was a denser oak forest ; the canopy was thicker than that of the mixed forest, and the patches of sunlight filtering through the leaves were pitifully scarce.

Plunder landed on a middle branch of a crooked oak tree.

Shuichi followed its gaze, and deep in the oak forest, a grey-yellow raccoon dog was crouching beside a fallen log, scratching at the rotting bark with its front paws.

Raccoon dog. Canine, omnivorous, and gentler than a fox.

It weighed about four to five jin, less than two-thirds that of a Red Fox.

With his current physical foundation roughly equivalent to that of a twelve-year-old child, plus the temporary strengthening of his muscles by Chakra, suppressing it would be no problem.

He approached from the side.

With Chakra attached to his soles to silence his movements, he moved across the pile of fallen leaves.

The raccoon dog was focused on scratching the bark and did not notice him at all.

Five steps. Three steps. Two steps.

Shuichi kicked off the ground and lunged.

At that same instant, Chakra shifted from his soles to his legs and lower back, and the speed at which his body launched into the air was nearly twice as fast as it would have been without Chakra.

His right hand accurately grasped the back of the raccoon dog’s neck, his left hand pressed down on its waist, and his entire body weight, combined with the downward pressure enhanced by Chakra, pressed down upon it.

Plunder.

The suction power erupted.

The raccoon dog thrashed violently under his palm ; although it was stronger than a hare, it was far inferior to a fox.

It kicked its hind legs a few times and opened its mouth wide, trying to turn back and bite, but Shuichi’s left hand firmly held its head down, giving it no chance to bite.

The Plunder lasted for about five seconds.

Longer than a hare, much shorter than a Red Fox.

The raccoon dog’s body went limp.

Shuichi rolled off it, knelt on one knee on the pile of fallen leaves, his breathing steady.

His mental energy consumption was about one-sixth, completely within the safe range.

The warm sensation of Vitality flowing in was comfortable and even.

« Under the enhancement of Chakra, the suppression power has more than doubled. »

Shuichi flexed his right fingers, « I can now suppress raccoon dog-level prey with one hand. »

« If another Red Fox comes along, it won’t be as messy as last time. »

He dragged the raccoon dog’s carcass under the fallen log and covered it with bark.

Plunder flew down from the oak branch and landed on the fallen log.

Afterwards, Shuichi hunted a few more small animals like pheasants before heading home.

On the way back, Shuichi continued to practice attaching Chakra to his soles, repeatedly fine-tuning the output to strive for the best effect with minimal consumption.

And the optimal state was to maintain a cushion of air thinner than paper.

He paused when passing the Training Ground.

In the target stake area on the east side, three Genin he didn’t recognize were practicing Earth Release ; the quality of the earth walls was average, but Shuichi memorized the heavier, duller sensation of the condensation of Earth Release Chakra.

When he returned home, Minako was washing rice.

Upon seeing Shuichi return, she asked, « What did the school teach today ? »

Shuichi almost stopped in his tracks, a hint of confusion appearing in his eyes.

Because Minako usually never asked about school matters.

Although confused, Shuichi answered truthfully.

« Today we learned about Chakra refinement. »

« The teacher asked us to try to make a leaf float. »

« Did you do it ? »

« Yes. I did. »

Minako nodded, the corners of her mouth lifting slightly, and she poured the rice-washing water into the sink.

Then she didn’t ask any further.

At dinner, Minako put an extra piece of grilled fish in Shuichi’s bowl.

After dinner, Shuichi walked to the plum tree in the backyard.

Plunder was already resting with its eyes closed on the plum branch.

Shuichi sat down against the plum tree and spread his right palm upward.

Chakra surged from his Dantian and gathered in his palm, a pale blue light glowing faintly in the night.

The Plunder ability and Chakra.

Two completely different powers, existing simultaneously in his right hand.

One was an innate special ability, and the other was a foundational power that every Ninja needed to master.

He clenched his right hand, and the light of the Chakra extinguished between his fingers.
