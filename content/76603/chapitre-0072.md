# Chapitre 72 — White Zetsu

Shuichi walked back along the mountain road.

His mental energy was nearly half depleted, his brain still felt hazy, and his temples throbbed faintly. The backlash from plundering the Edo Tensei body’s memories earlier was still lingering ; that feeling of mental energy being forcibly drained made him feel somewhat uncomfortable.

But the remaining mental energy was enough to support him back to Konoha.

After returning and resting well for a few days, his mental energy would recover.

He walked on the mountain path, his steps steady, his gaze scanning the road ahead.

Sunlight filtered through the gaps in the leaves, casting mottled light and shadows on the ground. On both sides of the mountain path was a dense forest, the canopy blocking the sky, and occasionally, one could hear the chirping of birds and the buzzing of insects.

Shuichi continued walking forward, his mind organizing the plans for what was next.

The path of Edo Tensei was currently impassable ; he needed to find other ways to obtain Wood Release.

Or, he needed to make the existing path feasible.

Increase his mental energy by another magnitude, or obtain the Rinnegan to pierce through the rules of life and death.

These were all long roads.

He repeatedly deduced various possibilities in his mind. Finding Madara directly was too risky, and Edo Tensei consumed too much. Was there a third path ?

Perhaps he could start with others who possessed Wood Release.

But Wood Release was a Bloodline Limit ; in the history of the entire Ninja World, those who possessed Wood Release could be counted on one’s fingers.

Senju Hashirama, Senju Tobirama’s grandson Danzo, Yamato, and also Madara.

The first three were all in Konoha, and their strength was far inferior to his. Only Madara was an exception.

But Madara was one of the strongest men in the Ninja World ; the risk of approaching him was too great.

Shuichi sighed.

Wood Release, what a troublesome thing.

Shuichi walked out of the forest and came to an open hillside.

The terrain here was relatively high, and one could see Konoha in the distance. The outline of the Village was clearly visible under the sunlight, and the four faces on the Hokage Rock overlooked the entire Village.

Ahead was the road down the mountain ; walking for another half hour would bring him back to the outskirts of Konoha.

He stepped forward.

Then he stopped.

Ten meters ahead, at the Roots of an old tree, something was moving.

Shuichi’s gaze fell there.

The soil layer was slightly bulging, as if something was pushing up from below.

The soil loosened, small particles of dirt rolled down, making a faint rustling sound.

Shuichi’s perception instantly extended over.

There was no Chakra fluctuation.

No, there was an extremely faint fluctuation, so faint it was almost imperceptible.

If he hadn’t been deliberately perceiving, he wouldn’t have discovered it at all.

The soil layer continued to bulge, then cracked open.

A white thing slowly floated up from the ground.

It was pure white all over, with only a humanoid outline, no facial features, no hair, and skin as smooth as wax.

It seeped out from the soil, its upper body exposed, its lower body still buried in the soil.

White Zetsu.

Shuichi’s right hand pressed onto the short blade at his waist, but he did not draw it.

His gaze was fixed tightly on White Zetsu, his body slightly tensed, ready to deal with any possible attack at any moment.

White Zetsu did not have an attacking stance.

It just stood there quietly, its hollow face facing Shuichi, as if observing him.

A few seconds of silence.

Then White Zetsu spoke.

« Uchiha Shuichi. »

The voice was light and airy, as if it came from a very far place, carrying a strange sense of echo.

« Lord Madara wants to see you. »

Shuichi did not speak.

His hand was still pressed on the hilt of the blade, his gaze calmly looking at White Zetsu, his mind racing.

White Zetsu continued : « Lord Madara knows about the matter in the Land of Grass. »

Its voice was still light and airy, with no emotion detectable.

« He appreciates you very much. »

Shuichi still did not speak.

He was thinking.

White Zetsu was a creation of the God Tree from the past, and now it was Madara’s eyes and ears and intelligence gathering tool.

White Zetsu could lurk underground, observing everything silently. Its Chakra fluctuation was extremely low, almost impossible to be perceived.

It could pass through any ground and appear anywhere.

If White Zetsu appeared here, it meant Madara had been monitoring him all along.

Shuichi’s brain was working at high speed, piecing all the information together.

White Zetsu.

Madara’s eyes and ears.

He had been ignoring this thing before.

He had killed hundreds of people in the Land of Grass, broken into Hozuki Castle, plundered the Box of Ultimate Bliss, and awakened the Mangekyo Sharingan. These things, if White Zetsu had been nearby, had all been seen.

Shuichi recalled every action he took in the Land of Grass.

If White Zetsu had been nearby all along, all of this had been seen by it.

And then it followed him all the way back to Konoha.

Shuichi’s back felt cold.

White Zetsu could find his location outside the Village, which meant White Zetsu had been following him all along.

Following him all the way from the Land of Grass back to Konoha, yet he hadn’t noticed at all.

What did this mean ?

It meant where his home was, who his mother was, his daily life trajectory, his weaknesses and soft spots.

Madara knew it all.

Shuichi thought of Minako.

His mother had been an ordinary person from beginning to end.

She had no Chakra, no combat ability, just an ordinary housewife. She went to work and came home every day, cooked and did laundry, living an ordinary life.

Xiaobai had some combat ability, but not much, and it was still in the digestion period, unable to exert its full strength.

Lue and Fang could provide some protection, but they also had their own tasks and could not guard his mother twenty-four hours a day.

Shuichi was not worried about his own safety.

What he was worried about was his mother’s safety.

If Madara used his mother to threaten him, he would be very passive.

What was Madara’s state now ?

Shuichi searched his mind for information about Madara.

According to his memories from his past life, Madara faked his death after the Battle of the Valley of the End and had been operating in the shadows ever since. The current Madara should be in an elderly state, half-dead, relying on the Gedo Statue to sustain his life.

In this state, Madara’s combat power was definitely not as good as his peak period.

Shuichi evaluated the strength comparison between the two sides.

He now had Kage-level strength, plus the Mangekyo Sharingan. The ability of Magatsuhi could make his body become non-existent, so no matter how strong Madara’s Ninjutsu was, it couldn’t hit him. As for defeating Madara, Shuichi didn’t have full confidence. But if he wanted to leave, Madara definitely couldn’t keep him.

The real problem was his mother.

If he used his mother to threaten...

Shuichi suppressed this thought.

He needed to ensure his mother’s safety first, and then consider other things.

Shuichi suppressed all thoughts.

His expression did not change, his gaze still calmly looking at White Zetsu. His heartbeat was steady, his breathing even, and no abnormality could be seen.

« Okay. »

His tone was steady, with no emotion detectable.

« But I need time. »

White Zetsu tilted its head, as if waiting for him to continue. That hollow face had no expression, but Shuichi could feel it observing him.

He looked at White Zetsu, his tone calm : « I need half a day. »

White Zetsu did not answer immediately, as if receiving some order.

It stood there quietly, half its body still buried in the soil, its hollow face facing Shuichi. That feeling of being observed became stronger and stronger, as if countless eyes were staring at him.

A few seconds later, White Zetsu nodded.

« Lord Madara is not in a hurry. I will come to pick you up tonight. »

Shuichi : « Half a day is enough. »

Subsequently, White Zetsu’s body began to sink.

It was like a piece of wax melting under high temperature, bit by bit retreating back underground.

The soil covered it up again, swallowing that white figure.

A few seconds later, it completely disappeared.

The ground returned to its original state, without any traces. No cracks, no bulges, just as if nothing had happened.

Shuichi stood in place, not moving.

His gaze fell on the place where White Zetsu disappeared, his mind racing.

Half a day.

In this half a day, he needed to arrange everything well.

His mother’s safety was the first priority. White Zetsu knew where his home was and who his mother was. If he left Konoha to meet Madara, his mother would be completely exposed under Madara’s eyes.

He could not let this happen.

Shuichi turned around and continued to walk in the direction of Konoha.

His steps suddenly quickened.
