# Chapitre 24 — Dining Table

Wednesday’s Chakra refinement practical class was held at the Second Training Ground.

It wasn’t the usual taijutsu field covered in fine sand, but the indoor dojo of the Second Training Ground, where the wooden floor had been polished bright by decades of Ninja stepping on it, and portraits of past Hokage hung on all four walls.

Teacher Mitomon asked the students to sit in a circle in the center of the dojo, with a palm-sized leaf placed in front of each person.

« You have been practicing the basics of Chakra refinement for two weeks. » Teacher Mitomon stood in the middle of the circle, pinching a leaf in his hand.

« What you need to do today is not to make the leaf move, but to make it float. Float it from your palm and maintain it for more than three seconds. »

Tetsuya sat to the right of Shuichi, frowning at the leaf in front of him.

His palm was spread open, the leaf resting on it, as he was already trying to channel Chakra.

The leaf shook twice and slid off his palm.

He picked it up and tried again, only for it to slide off again.

« Your body energy is too aggressive. » Shuichi lowered his voice. « Pull back a little. It’s not about pushing with brute force ; it’s about making it adhere to the leaf. »

Tetsuya took a deep breath and closed his eyes.

After a few seconds, the edge of the leaf on his palm curled up slightly, then the whole leaf flipped over and stuck to his palm, unmoving.

« It moved ! It moved ! » Tetsuya’s lowered voice was full of excitement.

Shuichi looked at the leaf on his own palm.

The last time he did this exercise was the first week of school ; back then, he was able to make the leaf float a full inch, and then immediately withdrew it, letting Teacher Mitomon see only a slight tremor.

Two weeks had passed, and his Chakra volume was continuously growing, because the physical improvement brought by Life Force Plunder made his body energy increasingly abundant, and his spiritual energy was also steadily increasing with the operation of his Sharingan’s pupil power.

He drew Chakra from his Dantian, transported it along the meridians of his arm to his palm, and then precisely controlled the output, allowing the Chakra to form an extremely thin cushion under the leaf.

The leaf floated less than half an inch above his palm, trembling slightly, like the level expected of a child who had just learned to levitate but was not yet stable enough.

Maintain for three seconds, then let the leaf fall back onto his palm.

Teacher Mitomon walked along the circle.

He paused in front of Genji ; Genji’s leaf was spinning wildly on his palm, as if wrapped in a small whirlwind.

Teacher Mitomon said, « Control, not explosive power, » and Genji’s leaf slapped onto the forehead of a big boy across from him.

A few children couldn’t hold back their laughter.

When Teacher Mitomon walked to Shuichi, Shuichi was making the leaf float for the second time.

Three seconds, just like the first time, then drop.

Teacher Mitomon nodded and walked past.

Hyuga Shimizu was sitting across the circle.

Shuichi glanced at his leaf, which was floating about an inch high above his palm, steady and almost without tremor.

It was maintained for about five seconds, then gently fell back onto his palm.

Teacher Mitomon stood in front of him for two seconds longer.

Shuichi made a mental note.

Hyuga Shimizu’s precision in Chakra control was, like his taijutsu, far beyond his peers.

The Gentle Fist style itself was a school that focused on Chakra meridian attacks, and children of the Hyuga Clan had to learn precise Chakra output control from a young age.

Hyuga Shimizu keeping the leaf at a height of one inch was likely also holding back.

After class, Teacher Mitomon announced the results.

Hyuga Shimizu was first, leaf floating one inch, five seconds.

Shuichi and three other classmates tied for meeting the standard.

Genji was punished to clean the dojo after class because his leaf flew out and hit someone.

Tetsuya did not meet the standard ; his leaf flipped over twice, but did not float up.

However, he didn’t look discouraged, instead, he kept muttering, « Practice again tomorrow, practice again tomorrow. »

At lunch, it started raining again.

Tetsuya and Shuichi sat under the classroom eaves eating their bento, the rainwater dripping from the tiles and making a row of small pits on the stone steps.

Tetsuya brought two bento boxes today, one was the taro rice balls he was eating, and the other was wrapped in blue cloth.

« My mom said this box is for Obito. » Tetsuya pushed the blue-cloth-wrapped bento to Shuichi. « The one we talked about making extra of last time. »

Shuichi took the bento ; the blue cloth was newly cut, and the edges were sewn very neatly.

Tetsuya’s mother probably sewed it next to the charcoal fire in the blacksmith shop—the cloth still retained a faint smell of charcoal.

« Help me thank your mom. »

« She said no need to thank ! She said you teaching Tetsuya taijutsu is more useful than four years of blacksmithing. » Tetsuya took a bite of a rice ball. « She saw me standing in a horse stance for a quarter of an hour yesterday and asked if I was punished by the teacher to stand. »

The corners of Shuichi’s mouth moved slightly.

The afternoon class was Konoha History.

When Teacher Mitomon talked about the history of the First Hokage, Senju Hashirama, and Uchiha Madara joining forces to establish the Hidden Leaf Village, the few Uchiha children in the classroom straightened their backs unconsciously.

Shuichi knew the second half of this history—Uchiha Madara’s defection, the Battle of the Valley of the End, and the Uchiha Clan gradually being marginalized.

The textbook talked about the first half, and the second half would only be covered in higher grades.

Hyuga Shimizu, sitting diagonally behind, also didn’t move.

Shuichi scanned his side profile with his peripheral vision ; Hyuga Shimizu was looking out the window, rainwater streaming down the glass, stretching his reflection into a blurry gray-blue shadow.

The rain stopped when school ended.

Shuichi walked out of the school gate, and Obito was already waiting by the stone pier.

He was drawing something on the stone pier with a twig, and seeing Shuichi come out, he immediately threw away the twig and stood up, his standing posture automatically switching to the standard stance from the day before yesterday.

« Big Brother Shuichi ! »

Tetsuya popped out from behind Shuichi and shoved the blue-cloth bento into Obito’s hands. « My mom made it ! For you ! »

Obito was stunned for a moment, looking down at the blue-cloth bundle in his hands.

He unwrapped a corner, saw the neatly arranged taro yokan inside, then quickly re-wrapped the cloth, raised his head to look at Tetsuya, his mouth opened and closed again.

« ... Thank you. » The voice was a notch lower than usual.

« No need to thank ! My mom said next time she’ll make red bean filling ! » Tetsuya patted Obito on the shoulder, then turned to Shuichi. « What are we learning today ? »

Shuichi looked at the two of them.

Tetsuya’s standing posture had become much more stable.

Obito’s standing posture hardly needed correction ; he had completely solidified what was taught three days ago into physical memory.

« Not teaching anything new today. » Shuichi said. « Review the forward step and backward step, then the two of you spar. »

« Sparring ! » Tetsuya’s eyes lit up.

When they returned to Shuichi’s courtyard, Minako was collecting the bedding that had been drying all day on the corridor.

She saw the three people come in, her gaze lingering on Obito for a moment.

« Obito, right ? » She asked.

Obito stood straight. « Yes ! Hello, Auntie ! »

Minako nodded. « Stay for dinner tonight. »

Obito was stunned.

He turned to look at Shuichi, whose expression gave no hint.

He turned back to look at Minako, but Minako had already turned and walked into the house with the bedding, giving him no room to refuse.

Obito stood in the courtyard, still holding the blue-cloth bento Tetsuya gave him, his lips pressed tightly.

Shuichi walked to the center of the courtyard and clapped his hands. « Sparring. Tetsuya stands here, Obito stands there. The rules are the same as at school, one hand on the shoulder, push the opponent out of the circle. The circle is the one drawn with chalk on the ground. »

Tetsuya and Obito stood facing each other.

Tetsuya was half a head taller than Obito, his arms a circle thicker, and his hand resting on Obito’s right shoulder was like an anvil pressing on a tree branch.

Obito’s hand rested on Tetsuya’s shoulder, his fingers barely hooking onto Tetsuya’s shoulder.

« Start. »

Tetsuya’s pushing force was like the bellows of a blacksmith shop, one after another, with a distinct interval between each.

The first push made Obito sway half an inch.

The second push made Obito’s knees bend a little, absorbing the force into his calves.

The third push caused Tetsuya himself to lose his center of gravity ; his power was not contained while pushing, and his body leaned forward with the force.

Obito felt it, and Shuichi saw it clearly ; at the moment Tetsuya’s center of gravity shifted, Obito added a lateral force with his right hand—not a push, but a pull.

Tetsuya’s right foot went out of the chalk circle.

« I lost ? ! » Tetsuya looked down at his foot stepping outside the circle, then looked up at Obito, a huge smile slowly spreading on his face. « How did you do that ! What was that move you did with your hand just now ! »

Obito scratched the back of his head. « It’s just... felt like I could push that way. »

« Felt like ? What do you mean felt like ! »

Shuichi stood on the corridor watching them.

The « felt like » Obito mentioned was his talent ; his body automatically sensed the shift in Tetsuya’s center of gravity during the confrontation, and then his hand instinctively added force in that direction.

It wasn’t learned, it wasn’t practiced, it was his body making the judgment itself.

This kind of judgment, like the dynamic capture of the Sharingan, was essentially the same thing : an intuitive perception of spatial relationships and the flow of power.

Tetsuya stood back into the circle. « Again ! »

Shuichi let them spar for five rounds.

Tetsuya won the second and fifth rounds ; the second round was him using brute force to push hard, and the fifth round was him finally learning to shift his center of gravity forward half an inch before pushing.

Obito won three rounds.

Every round Obito won in a different way ; in the second round, Tetsuya’s center of gravity was too stable for him to push, so in the third round, he changed his strategy, stepping back half a step the moment Tetsuya pushed, letting Tetsuya rush out on his own.

This wasn’t the backward step Shuichi taught, but something Obito thought of on the fly during the confrontation.

Shuichi updated his evaluation of Obito in his heart.

This child not only had good physical perception, but he also actively thought during combat.

Five years old, without any formal training, facing Tetsuya who was half a head taller, he used three different ways to win in three rounds.

« That’s enough. Sparring ends. » Shuichi walked to the center of the courtyard. « Tetsuya, your biggest problem is still your center of gravity. When pushing, the power is sent forward, and the center of gravity should follow, but after you send the power, your center of gravity shifts backward because you are afraid of falling. »

Tetsuya nodded, panting.

« Obito. It’s good that you used three different methods to win the three rounds against Tetsuya. But your strength is too weak. In the fourth round, once Tetsuya stabilized his center of gravity, you couldn’t push him. »

Obito also nodded.

« Tomorrow, continue practicing your stance. We’ll talk about sparring over the weekend. »

Tetsuya wiped the sweat from his forehead and bent down to pick up his schoolbag. « Then I’ll head back first ! My mom said I have to help rustproof the anvil today. » He ran to the courtyard gate, turned around, and waved at Obito. « Obito ! Tell me tomorrow if the taro yokan is delicious ! »

Obito raised the blue cloth bundle and waved it at him.

The courtyard fell silent. Lue flew down from the plum branch and landed on Shuichi’s shoulder, tilting its head to look at Obito. Obito looked at Lue’s spread wings, his eyes lighting up again with that pure curiosity.

« Brother Shuichi, what does Lue usually eat ? »

« He catches it himself. Mice, snakes, small birds. »

« Does it listen to you ? »

« Not really, » Shuichi said. « It just chooses to come back on its own. »

Obito thought for a moment. « Then it’s a friend. »

Shuichi paused. Obito was right. Lue was not a pet, nor a tool. It had been saved by Shuichi, who bestowed upon it Flight Instinct and Low-Light Vision, but every time it flew out to hunt and every time it landed on Shuichi’s shoulder, it wasn’t because of an order. It was because it chose to come back on its own.

Friend. In five seconds, the five-year-old Obito had summarized what had taken Shuichi more than half a year to realize.

The sound of Minako chopping vegetables came from the kitchen. Shuichi walked into the kitchen and saw three dishes already set on the stove : grilled fish, miso soup, cold spinach salad, and a small dish of pickled radishes. There were two more dishes than usual. Minako scooped the fourth dish out of the pot, Chikuzenni, which was chicken thighs stewed with burdock root, a portion large enough for three people.

« Is it enough ? » Shuichi asked.

« If it’s not enough, I’ll make more, » Minako said without looking up.

Shuichi walked to the porch and sat down. Obito was still standing in the courtyard, looking down at the circle drawn in chalk on the ground. He was tracing the edge of the circle with his feet, stepping exactly on the line with each step, heel-to-toe. Shuichi watched for a moment before realizing that Obito was practicing his balance on his own.

Dinner was eaten in the kitchen. Minako set the dining table next to the stove, and the three of them sat around it : Shuichi on the left, Obito on the right, and Minako opposite. Obito looked at the dishes on the table, holding his chopsticks without moving. Grilled fish, Chikuzenni, miso soup, and cold spinach salad ; the portion of rice in front of him was even slightly larger than Shuichi’s.

« Eat, » Minako said, placing the chopsticks in Obito’s hand. « If it’s not enough, there’s more in the pot. »

Obito took a bite of the Chikuzenni with his chopsticks. He chewed twice, and his hand suddenly stopped. Then he lowered his head and continued to eat, much faster than before—not wolfing it down, but a kind of speed where he was trying hard to control himself not to rush, but his body wouldn’t listen.

Minako didn’t look at him. She placed a piece of fish in the small dish beside Obito’s bowl, then continued eating her own meal, asking Shuichi about school as she ate—what he had learned today, whether the teacher had assigned homework, and how his balance beam practice was going. Her tone was exactly the same as usual.

While Minako was asking Shuichi questions, Obito sneakily took an extra piece of pickled radish. Shuichi saw it but didn’t say anything.

While clearing the bowls after dinner, Minako found a small, neatly folded piece of paper pressed under the bottom of Obito’s bowl. Opening it, she saw it was the taro yokan wrapped by Tetsuya’s mother. Obito had taken two pieces out of the blue cloth and placed them under his bowl. It wasn’t that he didn’t want to eat them, but rather that since he had eaten someone else’s food, he felt he should at least share what he had.

Minako placed the taro yokan on the stove and glanced at Obito in the courtyard, who was copying Shuichi by tying a Kunai to his calf. Then she lowered her head and continued washing the dishes, the sound of the water slightly gentler than usual.

By the time they saw Obito out, it was completely dark. The warm light of a few paper lanterns was reflected on the stone-paved roads of the Uchiha Clan Grounds, and the sound of the Naka River was clearer in the night than during the day. Obito took a few steps, then ran back.

« Brother Shuichi. Are you coming again tomorrow ? »

« Yes. »

« Then I’ll go back and practice what I learned today ten times. »

« Ten rounds. Not ten times. »

« Ten rounds ! » Obito finished speaking and ran off, his wooden forehead protector bouncing twice on the back of his head.

Shuichi stood at the courtyard gate, watching his retreating figure disappear into the alleys of the clan grounds. Then he returned to the courtyard and sat down on the porch. Lue flew down from the plum branch and landed on the railing beside him.

He spread open the palm of his right hand.

The wild boar’s Vitality had completely integrated. His physical foundation had stabilized at the level of a twelve-and-a-half-year-old. The next prey should still be Level 5, a wild boar or something of the same level. But there were larger things in the Forest of Death. There were rumors of bears in the upper reaches of the Naka River. A Level 6 prey was still too much for him to confront head-on with his current strength, but he could scout ahead.

The outdoor practical class would be held on the outer edge of the Training Ground in the upper reaches of the Naka River. At that time, the twenty-three children of the class and Teacher Mitomon would spend the entire day in that area. He couldn’t leave the group for too long, but he could use Lue for high-altitude scouting. At least he could map out the terrain of the upper reaches and the activity range of large prey.

Another matter was Chakra Paper. Before the school’s elemental affinity test next semester, he needed to know his own affinity first.

Shuichi thought of another plan. There should be an internal Ninja tool inventory within the Uchiha Clan Grounds, and the Military Police Force had its own equipment supply channels. If he could find a channel within the clan grounds, it would be much safer than outside. However, this required personal connections, and as a six-year-old child, he had no such connections.

Unless it was through his mother.

Minako was not a Ninja, but she was a widow of the Uchiha Clan. The clan had certain care policies for the families of fallen Ninja, including basic living allowances and some material rations. If she went to collect a piece of testing paper under the pretext of « helping Shuichi prepare for the Chakra Affinity Test in advance, » it shouldn’t attract too much attention.

Shuichi deduced the feasibility of the plan in his mind. Minako’s excuse for collecting the testing paper was natural enough : a mother concerned about her son’s studies, hearing about the affinity test next semester at school, wanting to help her son test in advance. It wasn’t unusual. The people at the Clan Administration Office wouldn’t ask too many questions. The only issue was Minako herself ; would she feel that Shuichi was being too impatient about his Chakra affinity ?

Not necessarily. After he placed second in the Taijutsu Class, Minako had become very accepting of his academic progress. The Chakra Affinity Test was a normal school exam component, and preparing in advance was a reasonable action.

Shuichi decided to bring this up with Minako over the weekend.

He stood up and walked under the plum tree. His One-Tomoe Sharingan automatically opened in the darkness, and the details in his vision instantly became clear. The tender buds on the plum branches trembled slightly in the night wind, the texture of every single bud scale perfectly clear. Lue was preening his breast feathers on the railing, the angle of each flight feather obvious at a glance.

He closed his Sharingan, and his vision returned to its normal dark state.

The concept of Growth operated quietly in his eyes. He was still far from the Two-Tomoe, but the One-Tomoe had already shown its value in actual combat. Anticipating attack trajectories, capturing muscle micro-adjustments, and judging the shifting direction of the opponent’s center of gravity in advance during a confrontation—these abilities were overwhelming advantages in Taijutsu combat.

However, the issue of continuous mental energy consumption still existed. With the Sharingan active, mental energy would be continuously extracted. During the battle with the wild boar, he had kept it open for about fifteen minutes, consuming about two-fifths of his mental energy. If the combat duration stretched longer, or if he active the Sharingan and performed Ninjutsu at the same time, the consumption would be even greater.

Mental energy was the fuel for his Plunder ability, as well as the fuel for his Sharingan. Both shared the same resource pool. This meant he had to precisely manage his mental energy usage in every battle ; plundering prey couldn’t consume too much, otherwise, his Sharingan wouldn’t hold up, and his Sharingan couldn’t be open for too long, otherwise, he wouldn’t have enough mental energy when plundering.

Life Force Plunder improves the physical foundation -> physical improvement expands the maximum limit of mental energy -> a higher maximum limit of mental energy supports more Sharingan usage and stronger conceptual Plunder. This was a closed loop. And the next key node of this closed loop was the Two-Tomoe.

However, he had not plundered any new mental-type abilities so far. What creatures in the Forest of Death had mental-type abilities worth plundering ? A bat’s echolocation ? A snake’s thermal sensing ? Or the scent tracking of certain insects ?

This matter required systematic investigation and research.

Shuichi stood up and patted the sand off his hands. Lue on the plum branch let out a short, low cry, a signal for night patrol. Shuichi looked up at it.

« Go ahead. »

Lue spread its wings and flew silently into the night. Its Low-Light Vision allowed it to see the details of the ground clearly even in the dark. Shuichi watched as its shadow merged into the night sky above the Uchiha Clan Grounds.

Then he walked into the house. Minako was sewing clothes under the lamp ; it was Obito’s shirt with the torn front. After dinner, she had asked Obito to take it off so she could mend it. The stitches were much neater than those sewn by Obito’s grandmother.

« Mom. »

« Hmm ? »

« I want to go to the Clan Administration Office this weekend. The school has a Chakra Affinity Test next semester, and I want to test my affinity in advance. »

Minako raised her head and glanced at him, her gaze free of doubt. She lowered her head and continued sewing. « Okay, go ask on Saturday. Go to bed early, you still have class tomorrow. »

Shuichi stood on the porch for a while.

« Good night. »

« Good night. »

He walked into his room, placed the Kunai under his pillow, lay down, and closed his eyes. The plum branches outside the window swayed gently in the night wind. Lue’s shadow swept across the high sky, silently circling above the Uchiha Clan Grounds.
