# Chapitre 10 — Minato's Gaze

Three days after meeting Obito at the square, Shuichi went to the mountain forest area to the north again.

The Red Fox had kicked him harder than he expected, and the bruise on his left rib had not yet fully faded. Over these three days, he had applied cold compresses at home twice. When Minako asked, he only said he had fallen while climbing a tree. Fortunately, his mother did not press further, simply silently adding two more grilled fish to dinner. Shuichi knew what that meant : if you are injured, you need to nourish your body, regardless of how you got injured.

This time, he did not head deep into the Mixed Forest. The lesson from the Red Fox was right there ; a seven or eight-pound fox could kick him and leave a bruise, and with his current physique, he simply could not suppress larger things in the Forest of Death. Plunder was not omnipotent ; before the prey fell, he had to pin it down first. And pinning something down relied on solid Taijutsu and strength, not the power of his right hand.

Today’s target was the wild chickens by the creek near the Training Ground. Lue was scouting from the air and noticed several large wild chickens moving in the bushes over there. Although the Vitality of a wild chicken could not compare to a wild rabbit, it had the advantage of a moderate size, about two pounds, so it would not be as difficult to control as the Red Fox when struggling. Moreover, they were concentrated in one area, allowing him to Plunder two or three at once, making the cumulative efficiency quite high.

Someone was practicing in the Training Ground.

Shuichi stopped behind the bushes near the Naka River Stone Bridge ; this was his usual observation spot. In the target post area on the east side of the Training Ground, a blonde teenager was standing about twenty meters away from the target posts, holding a Kunai in his right hand, practicing throwing. His movements were very slow, abnormally slow. It was not the slowness of someone unskilled, but a deliberate slowing down of every breakdown motion : the angle of the grip, the extent of the arm’s backward pull, and the micro-adjustment of the wrist at the final release point.

Minato Namikaze.

Shuichi narrowed his eyes. He had seen Minato practice Fire Release ; it was passionate and flamboyant, with the straight lines drawn by the flames steaming up a white mist over the Naka River. But today’s Kunai practice had a different temperament : quiet, precise, and almost obsessively correcting every micrometer of error. After each throw, Minato did not look at the target post ; he looked at the changes in his finger position on the handle, and the one-tenth of a second his wrist trembled during the release.

The Flying Thunder God Technique. Space-time Ninjutsu had much higher requirements for the accuracy of Kunai throwing than ordinary throwing ; a difference of a hair’s breadth could mean an error of several meters. Minato was not practicing with the Kunai ; he was practicing the starting stance for the Flying Thunder God.

« It has already begun, » Shuichi thought to himself. Early summer of the 41st year of the Hokage, Minato Namikaze was fourteen years old. A student of Jiraiya, Fugaku guided him in Fire Release shape transformation, and he had already begun to independently practice the basic movements of the Flying Thunder God Technique.

A crisp female voice came from the other side of the Training Ground.

« Minato ! It’s been over two hours, aren’t you tired ? »

Uzumaki Kushina jumped down from a large rock by the river, carrying a cloth bundle in her hand. She was wearing light green casual clothes today, with her red hair tied in a bundle behind her head.

« Almost done, » Minato did not stop his hand movements, his tone carrying his usual gentle smile, « My feel for it is good today. »

« You say that every time. » Kushina walked to his side, placed the cloth bundle on the ground, and opened it ; inside were a few rice balls and a small pot of water. « Eat something first. You practice with the Kunai even more desperately than you practice Fire Release. »

Minato finally put down the Kunai and sat down on a rock by the river. He took the rice ball, took a bite, and his gaze swept past the Training Ground’s target posts, looking toward the direction of the Naka River Stone Bridge.

Shuichi did not move. The bushes obscured his figure. But Minato’s gaze lingered in the direction where he was hiding for about the time of a single breath, then calmly withdrew, and continued eating the rice ball.

That glance was not vigilance. It was not the alert reaction of a Ninja discovering someone lurking. That glance was confirmation—as if Minato had long known someone was watching there, and only chose to confirm it today.

Shuichi felt a thin layer of sweat seep out on his back. Minato had known all along that someone was watching from behind the bushes.

« Hey, Minato. » Kushina swung her lower legs, suddenly changing the subject, « Are you still going to look for Big Brother Fugaku recently ? »

« Once a week. » Minato drank some water, his tone unchanged, « But Big Brother Fugaku seems very busy lately ; I didn’t see him last week. »

« It’s about the Military Police Force. I heard their budget got cut again. »

« Hmm. »

« The Sandaime is really something... » Kushina took a bite of the rice ball and said vaguely, « The Uchiha are, after all, one of the founding families of the village ; constantly targeting them like this isn’t very good, is it. »

Minato did not respond to this statement. He finished the rice ball and picked up the Kunai to continue practicing. But Shuichi noticed that Minato’s first throw after picking up the Kunai was no longer the slow, breakdown motion from before, but a full-speed, precise strike. The Kunai nailed into the center of the target post, embedding deeply into the wood.

Shuichi quietly retreated from behind the bushes, not continuing toward the creek. After his presence had been confirmed by Minato, he should not continue to be active here. He would leave the wild chickens for next time.

He walked back along the path by the Naka River. His mind was still turning over that glance from Minato ; it was not surveillance, not hostility, and not entirely curiosity. It was more like a tacit acquiescence. Minato knew someone was watching, more than once, but he did not drive them away, did not warn them, and did not even come over to ask.

Shuichi decided that in the future, he would no longer hide in the bushes to watch Minato practice. Minato had not exposed him, which was a kindness. And he should not take this kindness for granted.

When he returned to his own courtyard, Lue was squatting on the plum tree tearing at a small fish. Shuichi sat down under the plum tree and pressed on his left rib ; the area of the bruise had shrunk, but it still hurt when pressed. The Vitality of the Red Fox had completely merged into his body over the three days ; he could feel that the sense of fullness deep within his bones was a layer thicker than before he had used Plunder. But the bruise on his ribs was also reminding him : every life would resist when it was taken away, and the larger the prey, the more intense the resistance.

He used a branch to draw a line on the ground. To the east of the line were the Training Ground and Minato. To the west of the line was the Mixed Forest. To the north of the line were the pine forest, the dry riverbed, the carcass of the Red Fox, and the boundary of the Forest of Death.

At the northernmost end of the line, he drew an X. That was the spot where the Red Fox had almost bitten through his wrist.
