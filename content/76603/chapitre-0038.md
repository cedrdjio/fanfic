# Chapitre 38 — A New Thought

After school on Tuesday, Shuichi was digesting the Lightning Release Nature in the backyard.

He sat cross-legged under the plum tree, his right palm facing upward. The arc of electricity within the mental barrier had been flickering for three days. It was much quieter than Sakumo Hatake’s combat experience, like a small beast trapped in a cage, occasionally bumping against the inner wall of the barrier.

Shuichi took a deep breath and dispelled the barrier.

The nature transformation of the Lightning Release Chakra surged into his meridians.

It felt completely different from the Fire Release Nature. Fire Release was warm ; when the Chakra flowed through the meridians, it felt like a warm current, slow and steady. Lightning Release was prickly and numb ; the moment the Chakra flowed into his meridians, the hair on Shuichi’s entire right arm stood up, as if countless fine needles were pricking his skin simultaneously.

Shuichi endured the tingling sensation and guided the Lightning Release Chakra to circulate through his meridians.

On the first cycle, the tingling sensation spread from his right hand to his right shoulder, then diffused to his chest. Shuichi’s muscles were trembling involuntarily ; this was the instinctive reaction when an electric current passed through muscles.

On the second cycle, the tingling sensation weakened slightly. Shuichi’s body began to adapt to the flow of the Lightning Release Chakra. Unlike the Fire Release Nature, the Lightning Release Chakra flowed faster, but was less stable. Fire Release was like a river, while Lightning Release was like a bolt of lightning.

On the third cycle, Shuichi attempted to condense the Lightning Release Chakra in his palm. A dense tingling sensation came from his palm, followed by faint arcs of electricity. Shuichi looked down at his palm ; several tiny blue arcs were jumping between his fingers, like sparks just born.

From Plunder to condensing the first arc of electricity, it had taken less than an hour.

Shuichi recalled his training with the Fire Release Nature. He had sat in the backyard for a month before he could barely feel a noticeable rise in temperature from the Chakra in his palm, and it took another half month of practice before the edges of the leaves curled slightly. It took a month and a half just to get started with the Fire Release Nature.

This was the difference between Plunder and hard work. The Fire Release Nature was something he had trained step by step, exploring from scratch how to imbue Chakra with the nature of fire ; every slight improvement was built up with time. The Lightning Release Nature, however, was a template of nature transformation he had directly Plundered from the Cloud Ninja, and Shuichi only needed to integrate the template into his own Chakra system, skipping the entire exploration process.

Shuichi withdrew the Lightning Release Chakra and flexed his fingers. There was still a residual tingling sensation in his palm, like static electricity left on his hand after stroking a cat’s fur.

Lue crouched on the plum branch, watching him with its head tilted.

« Electricity, » Lue said.

« Yeah, Lightning Release Nature, » Shuichi replied.

« Fast. »

« Much faster than fire, » Shuichi looked at his palm. « Things that are Plundered are digested much faster. »

Lue thought for a moment and nodded.

---

Shuichi stood up, wanting to test the actual effect of the Lightning Release.

He walked to the wooden stake in the corner of the yard. This was the stake Shuichi usually used for practice—a piece of hardwood as thick as a bowl, nailed into the ground. Shuichi raised his right hand and performed hand seals.

Snake, Monkey, Boar.

Three seals, the most common initiation for Lightning Release. Shuichi’s fingers stumbled slightly while forming the seals ; this was his first time performing Lightning Release seals, and his muscle memory was not yet proficient. But Sakumo’s experience helped a great deal, allowing Shuichi to correct the mistake quickly, though the speed still needed practice.

Chakra compressed in his palm. The first time, the tingling sensation intensified. The second time, arcs of electricity jumped between his fingers. The third time—

« Lightning Release : Lightning Fang. »

A blue-white lightning strike shot from his palm, hitting the wooden stake.

« Crack ! »

The upper half of the wooden stake exploded directly. Wood chips and charred fragments splashed out, landing on the muddy ground of the yard. The cross-section of the stake was emitting smoke, and scorched marks penetrated deep into the wood, as if it had been pierced by a red-hot iron rod.

This piercing power was definitely at the Jonin level for a Lightning Release technique.

Shuichi looked at the cross-section with a satisfied expression.

But after he felt the consumption, his mood sank.

One-third.

A single Lightning Release : Lightning Fang, even with Sakumo’s experience used to release it with lower consumption, had still used up one-third of Shuichi’s Chakra. If it hadn’t been for Sakumo’s experience helping him compress the Chakra and avoid waste, the consumption for this Lightning Release might have been even higher.

Shuichi lowered his head and felt his remaining Chakra.

He made another attempt.

He took a Kunai from his Ninja tool pouch and attached the Lightning Release Nature Chakra to the blade. This was Sakumo’s most common combat style : Lightning Release attached to the white light, doubling the cutting power of the short blade and paralyzing the opponent’s muscles upon impact.

Blue electric arcs spread along the blade of the Kunai, making a faint « buzzing » sound. Shuichi gripped the Kunai and felt the rate of Chakra consumption.

It wasn’t fast. Attachment consumed much less than release ; Chakra flowed into the blade like a thin stream, maintaining the flickering of the electric arcs. Shuichi counted the seconds : one, two, three...

A minute passed. The Chakra consumption was about one-thirtieth of his total.

Five minutes. About one-sixth was consumed.

Ten minutes. About one-third was consumed. Added to the one-third from the previous Lightning Release : Lightning Fang, two-thirds of Shuichi’s Chakra had already been used.

Shuichi withdrew the Lightning Release attachment and put the Kunai back into his Ninja tool pouch.

The remaining Chakra was about one-third. If the Lightning Release Kunai attachment lasted for twenty minutes, his Chakra would be bottomed out. And this was without intense combat.

In actual combat, movement, dodging, taijutsu attacks, and Ninjutsu defense would all consume additional Chakra. In actual combat, the Lightning Release attachment lasting ten minutes would be considered decent.

Ten minutes.

Shuichi stood in the yard, looking at the blasted wooden stake, and pondered.

Ten minutes was enough for a one-on-one battle. With his current level of close-quarters combat, combined with the Lightning Release attachment and the Sharingan, killing an ordinary Jonin within ten minutes was not a problem. One-on-one battles focused on bursts of power, not endurance. Killing in one blow or resolving the battle in a short time was the way of the Ninja.

But the battlefield was different. The battlefield was not one-on-one ; it was a melee. Enemies would come in an endless stream ; after you killed the first, there was a second, and after the second, a third. Ten minutes of Chakra wouldn’t even last through one charge on the battlefield. Not to mention that on the battlefield, one needed to release large-scale Ninjutsu, protect comrades, and deal with emergencies ; the rate of Chakra consumption would be two or three times faster than in one-on-one combat.

Shuichi’s current strength was at the Jonin level ; his own explosive power was sufficient, but his endurance was not. If he could raise his Chakra volume to the Jonin level, the Lightning Release attachment could last for over an hour, and he could release Lightning Release : Lightning Fang over a dozen times ; then Shuichi would be able to fight freely on the battlefield. By that time, his strength would not just be Jonin, but Elite Jonin.

Chakra volume was the only bottleneck. Only by breaking through this bottleneck could Shuichi fully utilize the knowledge he had Plundered.

But how to increase Chakra volume ?

The normal path was long-term training. Chakra was formed by the fusion of physical energy and spiritual energy ; physical energy was improved through exercise, and spiritual energy was improved through meditation and actual combat. This was a slow process that required years of accumulation.

Shuichi had been training since he was five, and it took him three years to raise his Chakra volume from that of an ordinary person to barely the level of a Chunin. But this speed was not normal. Because Shuichi had Plundered three years of Vitality, his physique had been pushed from a five-year-old level to an adult level, and his physical energy far exceeded his peers. Combined with the strong spiritual power brought by being a transmigrator, his spiritual energy was also a large cut above his peers. These two prerequisites allowed Shuichi’s Chakra volume growth rate to far exceed that of ordinary Ninja.

Without the Plundered Vitality and the transmigrator’s spiritual power, relying solely on normal training, Shuichi’s Chakra volume in three years would likely have only reached the Genin level.

Even with these prerequisites, his Chakra volume had barely reached the Chunin level. A Chunin’s Chakra volume was roughly equivalent to Shuichi’s ; if he could Plunder a Chunin’s Chakra, Shuichi’s Chakra volume could directly double. A Jonin’s Chakra volume was about three to five times that of Shuichi’s ; by Plundering the Chakra of two or three Jonin, Shuichi’s Chakra volume could jump to the Jonin level.

To reach the Chakra volume of a Jonin from a Chunin, at the normal training speed, it would take at least four more years.

Four years. Still too slow.

---

Shuichi stood in the yard, looking in the direction of the Naka River.

He thought of himself three years ago.

Three years ago, when Shuichi had just awakened his memories of his past life, he had set a plan for himself : become stronger step by step, without haste or impatience, and use time to make up for the gap. At that time, he thought that the greatest advantage of a transmigrator was not a cheat code, but knowing the future. As long as he trained step by step and made the right choices at critical nodes, he could change his fate.

The Shuichi of three years ago felt that three years was a long time, enough for him to slowly become stronger.

The Shuichi of three years later stood in the yard, holding a Lightning Release that had just blasted a wooden stake, and realized that the Chakra volume he had accumulated through three years of hard training was only enough to release Ninjutsu three times.

Well... how should he put it ?

Plans couldn’t keep up with changes.

Two weeks ago, Shuichi still felt that training slowly at school was quite good, steady and solid, without haste or impatience. Within two weeks, he had Plundered Sakumo’s combat experience and the Cloud Ninja’s Lightning Release Nature, and his strength had jumped two levels. Then, he couldn’t stay calm anymore.

How to describe this feeling ? It’s like you’re working honestly for a monthly salary of three thousand, feeling that life is okay, but suddenly one day you win five million in the lottery. Can you still sit peacefully at your workstation the next day listening to your boss draw a pie ?

No.

Absolutely not.

This was Shuichi’s current mindset. Having tasted the flavor of quick money, if he were asked to go back to the rhythm of practicing basics every day and hunting bears every week, he couldn’t sit still.

But the problem was : in Konoha, even if you can’t sit still, you have to.

Konoha did not allow students at the Ninja Academy to carry out missions privately. Shuichi could not leave the Village to hunt other Ninja, and he could not Plunder their Chakra. He could only practice basics at school and hunt bears in the Forest of Death. Bears could give him Vitality, but they couldn’t give him Chakra volume, nor could they give him new attributes and combat experience.

There were no Ninja in the Forest of Death. There were Ninja in Konoha Village, but he couldn’t take action against Ninja of the same Village.

Shuichi stood in the yard, looking in the direction of the Naka River, with something heavy in his gaze.

If he could leave Konoha and go outside to hunt Ninja, Plunder their Chakra, attributes, and combat experience—

Shuichi didn’t continue thinking.

It wasn’t a determination, but a thought. A thought that had just emerged, not yet fully formed.
