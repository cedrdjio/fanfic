# Chapitre 13 — Chakra

The first week of Ninja Academy, Shuichi had only one feeling : slow.

It wasn’t that the teacher taught slowly. The homeroom teacher, Teacher Mitomon, was a Chunin in his forties with greying temples. His lectures were clear and logical, and occasionally he would tell a real battle case to keep everyone alert.

The slowness was in the course progress. The first three days were all Konoha history, starting from the First Hokage and Uchiha Madara throwing stones at the Naka River, then to the founding of the village, the One Village One Country system, and the First Ninja World War.

Shuichi sat by the window in the third row, having flipped through the entrance manual three times. He was so bored he started counting the number of circles the hawk was circling in the sky outside.

Sitting to his right was a fellow Uchiha named Uchiha Tetsuya. Six years old, round face, talkative. He struck up a conversation with Shuichi on the first day : « You’re an Uchiha too, right ? I’m Tetsuya ! My dad said our Class A has the most Uchiha, eight of them ! We’ll definitely win if we fight other classes ! »

Shuichi gave a non-committal response. Tetsuya interpreted his attitude as acquiescence and henceforth declared himself Shuichi’s best friend in class.

Sitting to the left of Shuichi by the window was a child from the Hyuga Clan. White eyes, silent, never left his seat during breaks, eyes always looking toward the playground outside. He had a dark blue cloth tied around his forehead, covering the center. Shuichi knew what was underneath. It was the Caged Bird Seal ; he was a child of the branch family.

Sitting in the center of the second row was a girl with blonde hair and blue eyes. Her hairstyle was strange, tied into two buns. Every time she raised her hand to answer a question, she kept her arm straight, and her voice was as crisp as a ringing bell. Teacher Mitomon called her « Student Yamanaka. » From the Yamanaka clan.

The fourth day was the Chakra refinement class.

This class was not held in the classroom. Teacher Mitomon took the thirty students of Class A to the Training Ground behind the school, the same one with the large banyan tree that Shuichi had visited on the day of the entrance ceremony. The spring morning sun warmed the ground slightly, and the target posts in the distance cast neat shadows in the light.

« Chakra. » Teacher Mitomon stood in front of the students, his right hand forming the Rat seal, palm facing up. « You have all heard this word. Your parents are Ninjas, your older brothers and sisters are Ninjas, and you have heard 'Chakra, Chakra, Chakra' since you were young. But before today, none of you have truly felt what Chakra is. »

He took a leaf from his Ninja tool pouch and placed it on his palm. The leaf trembled on his palm, then, as if supported by an invisible hand, slowly floated up. « Chakra is the power formed by the fusion of physical energy and spiritual energy within the body. By concentrating it on the surface of an object, you can make the object levitate or adhere to the body. This is the most basic application of Chakra. What you are going to learn today is exactly this. »

Shuichi stared at the leaf floating above the teacher’s palm. He didn’t think of the leaf, but of the Kunai that Minato had nailed to the center of the target post at the Training Ground. Minato concentrated Chakra at his fingertips to calibrate the release point, reaching micron-level precision. And what he had to learn now was just to float a leaf with Chakra.

The distance between the two was like the difference between crawling and flying. But he had to start from crawling.

Teacher Mitomon gave each student a fresh banyan leaf, then taught everyone the Rat seal—crossing their fingers, concentrating their minds to feel the inside of their bodies. According to the theory in the manual, physical energy exists in every cell of the body, and spiritual energy comes from the concentration of consciousness. Fusing the two within the body creates Chakra.

Shuichi sat cross-legged on the east side of the Training Ground near the large banyan tree, placing the leaf on his right palm. Three steps to his left was the child from the Hyuga branch family ; he already had a faint blue halo floating on his palm, and the leaf was trembling slightly but hadn’t floated up yet. Five steps to his right was Tetsuya, his face flushed red, veins popping out on his forehead, and the leaf in his hand remained motionless.

Shuichi closed his eyes.

Physical energy is stored in every cell. After more than half a year of accumulating Life Force Plunder, his physical constitution was at the level of an eleven or twelve-year-old child. Needless to say, his spiritual energy was even greater ; the fusion of two souls from his transmigration and rebirth, coupled with the mental strength repeatedly tempered by plundering dozens of lives, gave him a reserve far exceeding his peers.

Physical energy and spiritual energy, two great rivers. All he had to do now was lead them together.

Shuichi formed the Rat seal and followed the steps in the manual to guide the two energies to converge at his Dantian. Physical energy gathered from all over his body, warm and thick, like an underground river. Spiritual energy rose from the depths of his consciousness, cold and sharp, like the chill of an autumn night. The two distinct forces touched, rubbed, and then...

Fused.

A brand new energy surged from the point of fusion, flowing along his meridians to his limbs. It was not the warmth of physical energy, nor the coldness of spiritual energy, but a bright power with a slight tremor, as if a lamp had been lit inside his body. Wherever the Chakra flowed, muscle fibers tightened slightly, and a layer of almost invisible pale blue shimmer appeared on the surface of his skin.

Shuichi opened his eyes.

The banyan leaf on his right palm was slowly floating up. Not rocking, not trembling, but steadily, at a constant speed, rising to a position about three centimeters above his palm as if held up by an invisible hand.

Then he immediately realized that he had practiced too quickly.

He glanced around. The child from the Hyuga branch family had a faint blue light on his palm, the leaf trembling but not floating up yet. Tetsuya’s face was flushed red, and nothing had happened. The blonde-haired girl with buns was biting her lip, fine beads of sweat seeping from her forehead, and the leaf remained motionless. Of the thirty people in the class, only two or three children could see the faint light of Chakra so far, and no one had made the leaf float.

And his leaf had floated up three centimeters.

Shuichi calmly tilted his right hand slightly, and the leaf slipped from his palm and drifted onto the grass. He closed his eyes again, took two deep breaths, and then formed the Rat seal again. This time, he deliberately slowed the fusion speed, mobilized only a small portion of physical energy, and suppressed his spiritual energy to the minimum limit, letting the two energies rub slowly in his Dantian instead of crashing together. The generation speed of Chakra dropped to a fraction of what it was before. Then he guided this diluted Chakra to his right palm.

The leaf trembled. It just trembled.

Shuichi opened his eyes, putting on an expression of frowning and trying hard. The leaf shook a few times in his palm and then settled down.

« Not bad ! Student Uchiha Shuichi already has a Chakra reaction ! » Teacher Mitomon walked over to him, looked down at the leaf that had trembled slightly in his palm, and nodded approvingly. « The leaf trembling means that Chakra has been successfully refined and conducted to the palm. It’s just that the amount is not enough yet. Continue practicing. »

« Yes, teacher. » Shuichi nodded.

He did not let himself show any pride. The leaf trembling once was enough ; it wasn’t too outstanding, nor was it untalented. A performance that was just enough for the teacher to remember his name but not cause discussion in the faculty office. That was enough.

Without enough strength, Shuichi didn’t want to expose too much talent.

Tetsuya leaned over from the side, looking surprised : « Shuichi, you already have Chakra ? ! Why don’t I have anything ! I’m almost suffocating myself ! »

« Take it slow. » Shuichi said, « According to what the teacher said, first feel the energy in your body. Don’t force it, just feel it. »

Tetsuya nodded half-understandingly, closed his eyes again, and flushed his face even redder.

Shuichi watched his desperate appearance and thought of someone. Obito. After enrolling next year, Obito would probably be like Tetsuya, flushing his face red, unable to produce Chakra, and being mocked by the people around him as a dead last. But he would succeed in the end. Although Obito was a dead last in the original work, he eventually became a Ninja and awakened the Sharingan.

Shuichi stood up and put the leaf into his pocket. He walked along the edge of the Training Ground to the other side of the large banyan tree, where there was a row of wooden walls used for practicing climbing, about three meters high, which was infrastructure for senior students to practice Chakra adhesion.

Standing in front of the wooden wall, Shuichi placed his right hand on the rough wooden board. Chakra surged from his palm, not the diluted trace from just now, but the bright warm current from his first complete refinement today. Chakra adhered to the surface of the wooden board, and after the Climbing Ability at his fingertips was strengthened by Chakra, the adhesion force was at least three times stronger than when using Climbing Ability alone. He didn’t need any preparation ; placing one hand on the wall, his body hovered steadily.

But he didn’t climb up. Instead, he withdrew his hand. Chakra adhesion was usually taught to senior students in the Ninja Academy. Using it on the Training Ground now was equivalent to telling everyone he wasn’t a beginner.

Shuichi leaned in the shadow of the wooden wall, feeling the Chakra flowing quietly in his body. This was the first time he truly possessed Chakra, not Life Force, not Climbing Ability, not any foreign object obtained through Plunder, but his own power fused from his own body and spirit.

A warm pulse came from his right palm. The Plunder ability reacted to the Chakra, like some kind of confirmation. Shuichi made a note of a question in his heart, but didn’t think deeply about it. Now was not the time to test.

The bell rang. Shuichi walked out from behind the wooden wall, returned to the center of the Training Ground, and walked back to the classroom with Tetsuya.

« Where did you go just now ? » Tetsuya asked.

« Went over there and sat for a while. »

Tetsuya didn’t press. He was still complaining about how he had flushed his face red but still couldn’t produce Chakra.

« Shuichi, you have to teach me next time. » Tetsuya said, « How did you feel the energy in your body ? I can’t feel anything ; I just feel hungry. »

Shuichi glanced at Tetsuya’s stomach. « Eat your fill before practicing. »

« Really ? »

« Really. When there isn’t enough physical energy, refining Chakra will be even harder. »

Tetsuya nodded thoughtfully, then quickened his pace toward the cafeteria.

Shuichi didn’t follow him to the cafeteria. He returned to the classroom and sat by the window. Outside the window in the distance, the hawk was circling in the sky, its circling radius much larger than it was on the day of enrollment.
