# Chapitre 49 — Hozuki Castle

Early on the third morning, the sky began to brighten.

Shuichi stood on the lakeshore, looking at the island in the middle of the lake in the distance.

After leaving the inn, Shuichi had rushed toward Hozuki Castle without stopping. His powerful Vitality, having undergone a qualitative change due to the Uzumaki Bloodline, kept him full of energy even after three days of travel.

Hozuki Castle was built in the center of a large lake between the Land of Grass and the Land of Rain. The lake was vast, at least three hundred meters from the shore to the island. The lake water was a deep green, calm as a mirror, making it impossible to see its depth. The morning mist had not yet fully dispersed, spreading a thin veil of white gauze over the surface.

The entire island was a prison.

Stone walls were built along the contours of the shoreline, the gray-black walls exuding a cold, hard texture in the morning light.

The only connection to the shore was a wooden trestle bridge, about three meters wide, spanning three hundred meters of water, leading straight to the main gate on the island. A metal gate was set at the head of the bridge, with six guards standing at the entrance, one of whom was a Jonin.

Shuichi withdrew his gaze and began to think.

It was now the 45th year of the Hokage era. When he watched the Hozuki Castle movie in his previous life, Naruto had already mastered Senjutsu and was about seventeen years old. Naruto was born in the 51st year of the Hokage era, so there were still six years to go.

In other words, there were about twenty-three years left until the events of the movie.

So, is the warden still Mui ?

Shuichi rubbed his chin, his brows furrowed slightly.

He clearly remembered that the Heavenly Prison Technique appearing in the movie was a Fire Release forbidden technique created by Mui. This technique prevented the target from using Chakra ; if they tried to use it, they would be consumed by flames. The prisoners all had track-like black marks on their bodies, which were the markings of the Heavenly Prison Technique.

If the warden wasn’t Mui, or if the Heavenly Prison Technique hadn’t been invented yet, Shuichi would have to spend some effort on these prisoners after taking care of the warden. After all, without the suppression of the Heavenly Prison Technique, if a few tough characters with special abilities among the two hundred prisoners caused trouble, it would be very troublesome.

« Sigh ! »

Shuichi sighed.

He had only focused on killing in Kusagakure before and hadn’t managed to obtain any information about Hozuki Castle, leaving him completely in the dark about the situation there.

However, with Shuichi’s current strength, it shouldn’t be a big problem.

« Plunder, you go up to high altitude and overlook the layout of Hozuki Castle. Pay special attention to whether the prisoners have any strange tattoos. Come back as soon as you collect the information. »

« Understood ! »

Plunder flapped its wings and ascended, quickly shrinking into a black dot in the sky.

With the field of view elevated, Plunder returned to its comfort zone.

Its vision, which had been strengthened several times, coupled with further enhancement from Chakra, allowed Plunder to clearly see the details of Hozuki Castle below even from high altitude.

There was a watchtower every thirty steps along the wall, with two guards in each tower, taking turns watching inside and outside the wall. The number of guards was not high ; there were only about fifty guards on the entire island. But their task was not to fight, but to guard.

The buildings inside the walls were arranged in a circular layout.

The outermost perimeter was the prisoner cell area—there was a three-story stone building on the south, east, and west sides, with windows narrow enough to fit only a fist. The cell area formed an incomplete circle, with a gap on the north side leading to the middle ring.

The middle ring contained the guard barracks and the cafeteria, with several rows of neat wooden houses, and smoke rising from the cafeteria chimney. Further in were the Training Ground and work areas, a large, flat, rammed-earth ground where dozens of prisoners were working under the supervision of guards.

In the very center was a five-story Stone Tower, overlooking the entire island from a high vantage point. The base of the Stone Tower was square, with a side length of about twenty meters, getting narrower as it went up, with the top floor being only about ten meters square. The windows on the top floor were larger than those on other floors. Looking through the window, Plunder faintly saw a man standing in the building, looking up at the sky.

That position, that height, must be where the warden was.

Plunder did not linger over the Stone Tower. It glided down to the south cell area, flying low along the edge of the building, scanning with thermal perception and vision simultaneously.

There were about two hundred prisoners detained in the cells. Their Chakra activity was very low, making it impossible for Plunder to distinguish the strength of the Ninja through Chakra.

Plunder saw what Shuichi wanted it to find on the prisoners working in several work areas.

Their necks, forearms, and ankles were wrapped with track-like black marks. The marks spread along the veins, like living tattoos, slightly flickering with the pulse.

This must be the Heavenly Prison Technique.

The position and density of the marks varied from person to person. Some prisoners only had marks on their necks, while others spread to the entire arm. Plunder guessed that the density of the marks might be related to the prisoner’s strength.

Plunder memorized the position of the marks in its mind, then gained altitude and flew one last circle around the entire island.

After confirming there were no omissions, Plunder folded its wings and dove toward Shuichi’s direction.

—

Shuichi stood in place, watching Plunder descend from the sky and land on his shoulder.

« How was it ? »

« The prisoners have marks, » Plunder said. « On the back of the neck, arms, ankles. Black, wrapped around like tank treads. »

Shuichi nodded.

If there were marks, it meant the Heavenly Prison Technique already existed. Since the Heavenly Prison Technique was created by Mui, the current warden should be Mui.

« Do you have the warden’s location ? »

« The Stone Tower in the center of the island. I also saw a man standing by the window. »

« Number of guards ? »

« About fifty. Their strength is all Chunin. I didn’t see anyone else in the Stone Tower. »

Shuichi rubbed his chin and thought.

Fifty guards, scattered positions.

It was basically impossible to leave them all behind, but it didn’t matter. Shuichi didn’t intend to continue using this identity, so he was no longer afraid of exposure.

As for the warden, Mui, his strength in the movie was about that of an Elite Jonin. It was more than twenty years before the movie, so his strength should be inferior to that in the movie, but since he could become the warden and possessed the Heavenly Prison Technique, Shuichi planned to evaluate him as an Elite Jonin. With Shuichi’s current strength, as long as he wasn’t hit by the Heavenly Prison Technique, taking him down wouldn’t be a problem.

However, the problem to note was that he could not kill him directly.

The Heavenly Prison Technique was a continuous Sealing Technique. Once the caster died or lost consciousness, the technique should be lifted.

So the correct order was to take down Mui first, Plunder the Heavenly Prison Technique, and then continue the Heavenly Prison Technique himself to continue suppressing the prisoners. In this way, Hozuki Castle would fall into Shuichi’s hands.

As for after taking over Hozuki Castle...

Two hundred prisoners, from various Villages, each with their own abilities. This was a treasure trove. Shuichi could choose slowly and Plunder slowly.

« Let’s go, » Shuichi said. « While there is still mist on the lake, sneak into Hozuki Castle from the side and take down Mui first. »

Shuichi stepped on the lake surface, the morning mist covering his figure.

Plunder flew high in the sky, helping him keep an eye on the movements on the island.

Ten minutes later, Shuichi reached the foot of the stone wall.
