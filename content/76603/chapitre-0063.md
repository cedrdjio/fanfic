# Chapitre 63 — Mother Insect: Fang

The next day.

Shuichi did not choose to go directly to the Forest of Death, but instead headed towards the deep mountains on the north side of Konoha.

After walking for about an hour, Shuichi arrived at the edge of the deep mountains.

The trees here were dense, sunlight was blocked by layers of branches and leaves, and the light in the forest was dim.

« Lue, scan the area, » Shuichi said.

Lue flew up from his shoulder, ascended to a high altitude, and activated Infrared Sensing.

A few minutes later, Lue flew back and landed on his shoulder.

« Three kilometers to the northwest, there is a dense forest with several beehives inside, » Lue said. « The largest one should be a hornet’s nest, hanging on a tree branch about five meters above the ground. »

« Lead the way. »

Shuichi followed Lue northwest, passing through a thicket of bushes, and arrived at the dense forest.

Sure enough, a gray-brown beehive about the size of a basketball was hanging from the branch of a large tree. Several hornets were crawling on the surface of the hive, busy going in and out.

Shuichi stood under the tree and used Chakra to suppress the hornets.

Sensing an invisible pressure, the hornets' movements became sluggish.

He used a light body technique to leap onto the branch, approaching the hive.

The hornets inside the hive were all suppressed and motionless. Shuichi reached deep into the hive and found the queen bee.

The queen bee was several times larger than the worker bees, with a rounded abdomen and wings that shimmered with a metallic luster.

Shuichi placed his right hand on the queen bee and activated Plunder.

The symbiotic instinct of the colony, the queen bee’s pheromone coding instructions, the biological mechanism for the worker bees' unconditional obedience, and the entire colony’s pheromone communication network all flooded into Shuichi’s mind.

A few seconds later, the Plunder was complete.

The queen bee’s body quickly shriveled up and died.

Shuichi withdrew his hand and retreated from the hive.

The worker bees inside the hive lost the queen bee’s pheromone instructions and began to grow restless. But they were no match for Shuichi.

Eventually, the worker bees flew away in all directions, and the hive was empty.

Shuichi turned and walked deeper inside, preparing to collect a few more abilities.

After returning to the Uchiha Clan Compound, Shuichi circled around the small woods near his home.

He found a wasp nest nearby hanging in a bush, about the size of a fist.

Using the same method, Shuichi suppressed the worker bees with Chakra, reached in to catch the queen bee, and also took a few worker bees with him.

Shuichi brought the queen bee home, walked into his room, and closed the door.

He placed the queen bee on his desk and pressed his right hand onto it.

Granting began.

Colony symbiotic instinct. Crow’s speech ability. Concept of intelligence.

A small amount of Chakra, enough to power intelligence and speech.

The wasp’s body did not grow larger, but its compound eyes began to change. The scattered lenses now gradually focused and became clear.

A few seconds later, the granting was complete.

Shuichi withdrew his hand and looked at the wasp on the desk.

The wasp moved its wings, and its antennae trembled slightly. It turned its head, its compound eyes looking at Shuichi.

Then it spoke.

The voice was very soft, like paper rubbing together : « ...What am I ? »

« You are Fang, » Shuichi said. « The name I gave you. »

« Fang... » the wasp repeated, its voice still very soft.

« Your mission is to establish an intelligence network covering Konoha, » Shuichi said. « Monitor key locations and notify me directly of any anomalies. Tell me what abilities you need, and I will help you find a way. »

Fang was silent for a few seconds, as if trying to understand Shuichi’s meaning, then softly uttered an « Hmm. »

Shuichi looked at Fang, then at the few worker bees he had brought along.

He reached out and pressed his hand on one of the worker bees.

Granting—Sense of direction and identification ability.

Granting—A small amount of Chakra to strengthen wings and compound eyes.

Granting—Micro-memory, capable of remembering routes and key landmarks.

The worker bee’s body trembled slightly, the veins in its wings became clear, and the luster of its compound eyes brightened a bit. It flew up, circled around Shuichi, its movements much more agile than before, and then landed steadily on the windowsill.

Shuichi granted abilities to several more worker bees, each with strengthened bodies, compound eyes, and memory. The few worker bees crawled on the windowsill, much more agile than ordinary wasps, capable of executing simple reconnaissance orders.

« First, send the worker bees out to scout, » Shuichi said to Fang. « See what information they can bring back. »

Fang softly uttered an « Hmm, » its antennae trembling as it issued instructions to the worker bees.

The few worker bees flew out the window and scattered.

Half a day later.

The worker bees returned one after another, landing on the windowsill.

Fang received the information they brought back through pheromones, then relayed it to Shuichi.

« There are people on guard at the entrance of the Hokage Building, about four of them. »

« There are students active on the Ninja Academy playground, a large number. »

« There are many people in the commercial street, and the Chakra fluctuations are complex. »

Shuichi listened and frowned.

The information was too crude.

« Can you only identify this much ? » Shuichi asked.

Fang’s antennae trembled slightly, its voice very soft : « The worker bees' sensory abilities are limited. They can only identify rough information like 'someone present,' 'no one present,' or 'a lot of Chakra,' but lack details. »

« Then what should we do ? »

Fang was silent for a while, its antennae trembling slightly.

Shuichi thought for a moment and soon had an idea.

« How about this, » Shuichi said. « The worker bees' senses are limited, but they are numerous and inconspicuous, so they can be responsible for basic reconnaissance. Then I will grant intelligence to a few more insects, letting them be responsible for obtaining precise information. Ordinary insects will pass basic information to the smart insects, the smart insects will verify the details, summarize them for you, and you will relay them to me. »

Fang’s antennae trembled : « Division of labor ? »

« Correct, » Shuichi nodded. « Ordinary insects cast the net, smart insects focus on specific points. This is more efficient. Furthermore, the smart insects can be dispersed in different locations, each responsible for an area, so not all information needs to be passed to you ; you process the information and then pass it to me. »

« Understood, » Fang said.

« I will grant intelligence to a few more insects, » Shuichi said. « But before that, what else do you need ? »

« The pheromone coding of other insects, » Fang said. « Wasps can only control wasps. To control different types of insects, I need their language. The pheromone coding of each insect type is different ; I need to learn their coding to command them. »

« Is that enough for now ? » Shuichi asked.

« Yes, » Fang said. « Once I learn their coding, I can command these insects to work in coordination with the wasps. Different types of insects have different abilities, and only by combining them can we cover various environments. »

Shuichi stood up and walked to the window.

« Fine, have the worker bees go catch them, » Shuichi said. « Notify me when they are caught, and I will Plunder and transfer them to you. »

Fang softly uttered an « Hmm, » turned, and issued pheromone instructions to the worker bees on the windowsill.

Then it flew up with the worker bees and flew out the window.

Shuichi watched the direction Fang flew off in, thinking about the structure of the intelligence network.

Evening.

Shuichi walked out of the room and came into the courtyard.

Minako was watering flowers, and Haku was lying at her feet.

« Finished training ? » Minako asked.

« Yes, » Shuichi nodded.

« You must be hungry, dinner is ready, » Minako smiled. « Come in and eat. »

Shuichi followed Minako into the house.

During dinner, Minako talked about Haku’s situation.

Shuichi smiled : « It’s still young ; it will be powerful in the future. »

« You seem to think highly of it, » Minako was a bit surprised.

« Yes, » Shuichi nodded.

After all, its mission is to protect you, so how could it not be powerful ?
