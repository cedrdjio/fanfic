# Chapitre 30 — Minato

On Wednesday after school, Shuichi was at the Naka River bank.

Lue was practicing diving. Five days ago, after integrating the complete wisdom concept of the Monkey King, Lue began to proactively adjust its flight movements.

It took off from the old locust tree on the opposite bank, its two-meter wingspan carving an arc in the air, then folding, diving, coming to a sudden stop three feet above the water, turning, and gliding back to the treetop.

It couldn’t do this move well last week ; the timing of the sudden stop was always half a beat slow, and the turning angle was too wide. Today, Shuichi watched six rounds ; Lue’s timing for the sudden stop was about an instant faster than five days ago, and the turning angle had also narrowed.

It was learning. The Monkey King’s wisdom concept included a risk assessment module, and Lue was using it to evaluate the efficiency of its every move. Lue’s flight movements were speaking for themselves. The angle of every dive, the timing of every sudden stop, and the frequency of its wing beats during every turn—all were Lue using its body to tell Shuichi its current state.

Shuichi stood on the riverbank, watching Lue dive for the seventh time. The position of Lue’s sudden stop was two inches lower than before, and the splashing water wetted the feathers on its belly. Lue didn’t mind, turning and gliding back to the treetop, pausing on the branch for two seconds, tilting its head to look at the water, reviewing the move just now.

« Again, » said Shuichi.

Lue took off from the treetop for its eighth dive. This time, it didn’t jump from the top of the old locust tree, but leaped directly from a middle branch, saving the time needed to climb. Shuichi noticed this detail. Lue was optimizing its takeoff path.

The Naka River flowed downstream under the afternoon sun, and the pebbles on the riverbank were scorched hot. Shuichi sat down on a large rock by the riverbank, watching Lue practice over and over. The roofs of the distant Village were visible through the gaps in the treetops, and smoke rose from the chimneys of a few houses, only to be scattered by the wind. On Wednesday, after school, most children were still at school or had already gone home. This side of the riverbank was very quiet.

When Lue finished its twelfth round, Shuichi stood up. The sun had already shifted west and would soon fall behind the woods to the west of the Village.

« Time to go back, » said Shuichi.

Lue glided down from the air and landed on Shuichi’s shoulder. The two-meter wingspan, once folded, pressed down on Shuichi, causing him to tilt his head slightly. Its claws gripped the clothes on his shoulder, controlling their strength. Shuichi walked along the riverbank toward the stone bridge.

When Shuichi saw Minato on the Naka River Stone Bridge, Lue was still on his shoulder.

Minato was leaning against the stone bridge railing, his hands in his pants pockets, his gaze following the current downstream. The setting sun hit his golden hair, dyeing that tuft of hair tied into a small ponytail orange-red. He wore a dark blue sleeveless ninja outfit, with a Konoha forehead protector tied around his forehead. Seventeen years old, a Jonin, Jiraiya’s disciple. When Shuichi first saw him at the Training Ground two years ago, Minato was still practicing the starting stance for the Flying Thunder God. Now, the title of « Yellow Flash » had already spread throughout the Ninja World.

Shuichi walked onto the stone bridge. Hearing footsteps, Minato turned his head, his gaze lingering on Shuichi’s face for an instant before he smiled.

« Uchiha Shuichi. »

Minato called out his full name. Shuichi stopped in the middle of the stone bridge.

« Minato-senpai. »

Minato straightened up from the railing and walked two steps toward Shuichi. « I was resting here and saw you with a hawk. »

« Mm. »

« This hawk has a wingspan of over two meters ; it’s not a common breed, is it ? » Minato’s gaze landed on Lue. Lue crouched on Shuichi’s shoulder, its pupils slowly switching between dark-light mode and normal mode, also watching Minato.

« I’ve raised it for two years, » said Shuichi.

« Trained ? »

« Mm. »

Minato nodded without pressing further. « Do you come here often ? »

« I come after school. »

« Practicing Taijutsu ? »

« Sometimes, » Shuichi said. « Today I’m training it. »

« Training ? »

« Watching it fly, » Shuichi looked at Lue. « It’s learning new moves. »

Minato followed Shuichi’s gaze. Lue flew up from Shuichi’s shoulder, landed on the stone bridge railing, then flew up again, circling over the riverbank, performing a dive and sudden stop maneuver, with a tighter turning angle than before. Minato watched for two rounds.

« It’s adjusting itself, » Minato said.

« Mm. »

« Very smart. »

Shuichi didn’t respond. Lue landed on the railing, tilting its head to look at Minato. Its pupils contracted into thin slits, then dilated again, switching repeatedly. Minato looked into Lue’s eyes.

« It’s observing me, » Minato said.

« It does that with people it’s not familiar with. »

Lue tilted its head and let out a short, low chirp. Minato smiled.

« It’s responding to me, » Minato said. « Interesting. »

The setting sun had sunk below the Village’s roofline, and the light on the riverbank dimmed. Minato leaned his back against the stone bridge railing, his gaze looking upstream. The water of the Naka River shimmered with a grayish-blue light in the twilight, flowing out of the Village.

« I believe you’ve heard about Sakumo Hatake’s incident, right ? » Minato asked.

« Mm. »

« What do you think ? »

Shuichi looked into Minato’s eyes. Minato’s gaze was calm, without any hint of scrutiny ; he was asking a genuine question.

« He chose to save his comrades ; no one forced him, » Shuichi said. « Those who criticize him are criticizing his choice because it differs from their rules. But rules are dead, while people are alive. A person who chooses to save their comrades shouldn’t be criticized by everyone. »

Minato was silent for a long time. Lue changed its posture on the railing, its claws lightly gripping the stone, its wings flapping twice before folding again.

« You’re right, » Minato said.

His voice was very soft, but Shuichi could hear from his tone that Minato truly agreed with that statement.

Minato was silent for another instant. He looked at Shuichi, and there was an added layer in his gaze. Shuichi couldn’t define what it was, but the way Minato looked at him was different from before.

« I should go, » Minato straightened up from the railing and brushed the dust off his ninja outfit.

He turned and walked toward the Village, stopped after three steps, and turned his head.

« Don’t let anyone tell you what to ask. »

Shuichi watched Minato’s back disappear around the corner at the Village entrance. Lue jumped down from the railing and landed on Shuichi’s palm, its claws retracting their strength, not hurting him.

Lue raised its right claw and lightly touched Shuichi’s earlobe.

Shuichi was stunned for a beat. Lue had never done this before ; its interactions with Shuichi only consisted of crouching on his shoulder, chirping, and nodding. It had never actively touched Shuichi’s body with its claws.

Lue’s pupils switched between dark-light mode and normal mode once. It opened its beak, and a low, incoherent sound came from its throat.

« ...Goo. »

Clearer than the « chiu » from five days ago. The vocal organs were still a hawk’s throat and beak ; it was impossible to produce human syllables, but Lue was trying, trying to express itself with sound.

« Take your time. When the time comes, I’ll find a parrot and give you its vocal organs, » said Shuichi.

Lue tilted its head to look at him, then nodded.

Shuichi walked home along the Naka River. The sky had darkened, and the lights of the Village were lighting up one by one. The Uchiha Clan Grounds were in the direction of the upper reaches of the Naka River, and Shuichi’s house was on the north side of the Clan Grounds, near the river. He walked past the stone bridge, along the path by the riverbank toward the upper reaches. Lue crouched on his shoulder, wings folded, occasionally looking down at the road.

The White Fang incident was still fermenting. When Minato mentioned it today, there was no blame or defense in his tone ; he was just asking what Shuichi thought. Minato was a Jonin, Jiraiya’s disciple, and the « Yellow Flash. » His reputation in the Ninja World was rising. But regarding the White Fang incident, Minato didn’t have an answer either.

Shuichi remembered Obito running to find him during lunch break on Tuesday, with genuine confusion in his eyes. Obito asked, « Why would someone get criticized for saving people ? » Shuichi didn’t give him an answer. Obito needed to think for himself. In this world, there were some questions where the answers given by others would always belong to them ; only the answers one came up with oneself were truly one’s own.

The White Fang was consumed by the Village today. The Uchiha would be tomorrow.

Shuichi walked past the entrance to the Uchiha Clan Grounds, where the lights of the Military Police Force building were still on. A few adult Uchiha walked out of the building, whispering something, their tones filled with suppressed dissatisfaction. Shuichi didn’t stop, walking along the path on the north side of the Clan Grounds toward home.

Minako was cooking in the kitchen, and smoke rose from the chimney. Shuichi pushed open the courtyard gate, and Lue flew up from his shoulder, landing on a plum tree branch. The plum tree branch bent into an arc under Lue’s weight. Shuichi looked at it for a moment ; Lue’s weight was already more than twice that of a normal adult hawk, and this plum tree wouldn’t last much longer.

« You’re back ? » Minako’s voice came from the kitchen.

« Mm. »

« Wash your hands and eat. »

Shuichi walked into the house, placed his schoolbag on the tatami, and went to the water jar to wash his hands. Lue let out a low chirp in the courtyard. Shuichi turned his head to look ; Lue was tilting its head at him, and let out another « Goo. »

It was practicing vocalizing.

Shuichi finished washing his hands, walked into the courtyard, and reached out for Lue to jump up. Lue landed on his palm, its claws retracting their strength.

« Practice slowly, » said Shuichi. « No rush. »

Lue nodded.
