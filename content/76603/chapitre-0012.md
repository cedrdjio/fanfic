# Chapitre 12 — Enrollment

The 42nd year of the Hokage, spring.

The entrance ceremony for the Konoha Ninja Academy was held on the first sunny day of April. Red banners symbolizing the Will of Fire were planted on both sides of the school gate, and children from all corners of Konoha and their parents gathered in the open space at the entrance. The white-eyed faces of the Hyuga Clan were in groups of three or five, the Sarutobi children wore dark blue short shirts with their family crests, and children from commoner families huddled together, excited but reserved. The children of the Uchiha Clan stood in the front left of the crowd, the fan crests on their collars gleaming slightly in the spring sunlight.

Shuichi stood on the far right of the Uchiha children’s line, wearing a newly made dark blue casual outfit. The fan crest on his collar was hand-sewn by Minako, with stitches far neater than Obito’s grandmother’s work. Plunder was not on his shoulder, as it wasn’t suitable for the entrance ceremony today. So, Shuichi left it on the plum tree in the backyard, feeding it two extra small fish before leaving.

« Uchiha Shuichi. »

The registering teacher called his name. Shuichi walked forward and received a brand new enrollment handbook from the teacher.

Shuichi opened the handbook. The first page was the Ninja Academy’s motto—[Will of Fire : Where the leaves dance, the fire burns eternal]. The second page was the class schedule : Basic Taijutsu, Shurikenjutsu, Introduction to Chakra Cultivation, Konoha History, Ninja World Geography, Basic Survival Skills. The third page was the class assignment list.

He was assigned to Class 1-A.

Shuichi’s gaze swept over the class assignment list, finding several names he recognized. There were seven or eight Uchiha children in the same class, but Obito wasn’t among them, as Obito was a year younger than him and would enroll next year. Kurenai Yuhi was in Class B, Asuma Sarutobi was in Class C. The Sandaime’s son enrolled in the same year as him but was assigned to a different class, a common practice at the Ninja Academy to prevent children from prominent families from clustering together.

There were no formal classes on the first day of enrollment. The morning was the entrance ceremony, personally attended by the Sandaime Hokage, Hiruzen Sarutobi. Shuichi stood in the back, looking at the old man on the podium wearing the Hokage robe and straw hat, thinking of that spring morning when he first observed Minato on the Naka River Stone Bridge.

The Sandaime spoke at length. The Will of Fire, the history of Konoha, the past of the First Hokage and Uchiha Madara jointly founding the village. When he spoke of the First Hokage and Madara, Shuichi noticed the subtle change in expression on the faces of several Uchiha children in the classroom. It wasn’t disgust, but rather a complex emotion that was hard to describe. The Uchiha Clan’s way of recounting the village’s founding history was not entirely the same as the official Hokage version.

The afternoon was free activity time. Instead of going to the playground with the other children, Shuichi found a quiet spot by the Training Ground at the back of the school. There was a large banyan tree there, with several stones uplifted by its roots underneath, allowing one to sit and see the entire Training Ground.

Older students were practicing on the Training Ground. Most were second and third-year children, their Shurikenjutsu still very clumsy, with five or six out of ten Kunai missing the target. Shuichi watched for a while, then turned to the last page of the enrollment handbook, the Basic Chakra Cultivation tutorial. This was what he needed most. His Plunder ability provided him with physical constitution, climbing, and Vitality accumulation, but Chakra was a power of another dimension. According to the tutorial, Chakra is formed by the fusion of Physical Energy and Spiritual Energy within the body, and the cultivation process requires mobilizing both simultaneously.

Shuichi had plundered Vitality for several months, so he had no shortage of Physical Energy, and his physical foundation was roughly at the level of a ten-year-old child, so his Physical Energy was definitely more abundant than his peers. As for Spiritual Energy, with the fusion of two souls from transmigration and rebirth, his spiritual power far exceeded that of ordinary children, possibly even surpassing some Genin. If both these conditions were met, Chakra cultivation should be much easier for him than for his peers.

But Shuichi didn’t try it on the spot. The school Training Ground was crowded and had too many watchful eyes. Cultivating Chakra required hand seals and concentration, and Shuichi was afraid of being disturbed, so he needed to try it in a quiet and secluded place.

He closed the handbook and looked up at the sky. Plunder’s figure was clearly visible beneath the thin spring clouds, its wingspan already close to 1.2 meters, its circling posture exuding a calm and unhurried elegance.

At the end of the first day of enrollment, Shuichi met Obito on his way back to the Uchiha Clan Grounds. Obito was squatting on the stone steps by the plaza, in the exact same spot where Shuichi had squatted to draw symbols months ago. He held a new wooden forehead protector, better crafted than the previous one.

« Shuichi ! » Obito saw him, jumped up from the stone steps, and ran over. « Today’s your first day of school, right ? How is school ? Did anyone bully you ? My grandma said you’d definitely be assigned to Class A, you’re so amazing, you can hit shuriken with pebbles ! »

Shuichi looked down at Obito. The child who had been pushed down in the mud months ago, the scrapes on his face had healed, and the torn pants on his knees had been mended, but the light in his eyes hadn’t changed.

« School’s okay, » Shuichi said. « You’ll go next year too. »

« Yeah ! » Obito nodded vigorously, holding up the new forehead protector for him to see. « My grandma made me another one ! This time she didn’t carve the Fourth Hokage—she said the Sandaime-sama is still alive, and carving the Fourth Hokage is bad luck. »

Shuichi was silent for a moment.

« Do you want to be Hokage someday ? » he asked.

« Yeah ! »

« Why ? »

Obito tilted his head, thought for a few seconds, then broke into a big smile. « Because the Hokage is the strongest person in the whole village ! When you’re Hokage, you can protect everyone ! I want to protect Grandma, protect the people in the clan, and protect you too—you said you’d be my bodyguard ! »

Shuichi didn’t correct him. He reached out and patted Obito’s head, then continued walking.

« Shuichi ! » Obito called from behind him. « You have to teach me what you learn at school later ! »

Shuichi raised his right hand and waved it behind him. The pulse in his palm glowed faintly warm in the setting sun, as if responding to the distant child’s shout.

Back in his own yard, Plunder glided down from the plum tree and landed on his shoulder. Minako was cooking in the kitchen, and smoke rose from the chimney, blending with the twilight over the layered eaves of the Uchiha Clan Grounds.

Shuichi sat under the plum tree, opened the last page of the enrollment handbook, and looked at the illustrations for the Basic Chakra Cultivation tutorial. A continuous warmth emanated from his right palm—not hunger, but a quiet wakefulness.

April, 42nd year of the Hokage. He was six years old. First day of Ninja Academy.

The eighteen-year countdown entered a new phase today.
