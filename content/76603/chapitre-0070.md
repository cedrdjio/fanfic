# Chapitre 70 — Experimenting with Edo Tensei

A few days later.

Shuichi left Konoha Village through the east gate and walked along the mountain road towards the border of the Land of Fire.

For the past few days, he had been studying the Edo Tensei jutsu formula he had copied from the scroll of sealing.

He had scrutinized every detail—the sequence of hand seals, the method of forming them, the circulation path of Chakra, and the conditions for casting—until he had mastered it completely.

Thanks to the knowledge of various Ninja he had previously Plundered and absorbed, his understanding of Ninjutsu had reached a considerably deep level, which allowed him to learn this complex forbidden jutsu in just a few short days.

Now, he needed to test this jutsu.

More accurately, he needed to test whether Edo Tensei could become a new source for Plunder.

There were two paths for Wood Release ; the first was to go directly to Uchiha Madara, but the risk was too great.

The second path was to summon the deceased who possessed Wood Release via Edo Tensei and Plunder Wood Release from them.

If the second path proved viable, he would have an additional way to obtain Wood Release.

Shuichi walked along the mountain path, his gaze sweeping across the forests on both sides.

This area was a mountainous region on the border of the Land of Fire, sparsely populated and with rugged roads.

Many bandits would set ambushes here to rob passing merchants.

These bandits were exactly what he was looking for.

Edo Tensei required two conditions : first, a DNA medium of the deceased, and second, a living person as a sacrifice.

He would not use innocent people as sacrifices, but these highway bandits had blood on their hands and deserved to die.

Shuichi continued walking forward, his perception extending outward to cover a range of several hundred meters.

Two hours later.

Shuichi stopped at a bend in the mountain path.

Two hundred meters ahead, two people were crouching in the forest, observing the mountain path through gaps in the leaves.

Their breathing was steady, their heartbeats were normal, and there were no Chakra fluctuations.

They were ordinary people.

Shuichi lightened his footsteps and approached silently.

The two were still observing the path, completely unaware of someone approaching from behind.

« Why hasn’t anyone come today ? » one of them whispered.

« Wait a bit longer, plenty of merchants usually pass by this road, » the other replied. « The last haul was enough to feed us for a month. »

Shuichi stood five meters behind them, sizing the two up.

One was burly, with a short blade tucked into his waist. The other was smaller and thinner, carrying a burlap sack, likely for storing stolen goods.

Both had calluses on their hands from years of gripping blades. There were dark red stains on their clothes—blood that couldn’t be washed away.

How many people had these men killed ?

Shuichi had no interest in knowing.

He raised his hand, his movement too fast to see.

The burly bandit felt a chill at his throat.

He clutched his throat, wanting to speak, but only a gurgling sound emerged. Blood surged from between his fingers and sprayed onto the ground.

He turned his head and saw the youth behind him.

Those Sharingan glowed red in the shadows.

The bandit’s eyes widened, his mouth opened to scream, but blood rushed into his windpipe, choking off all sound.

He collapsed to the ground, his body convulsed a few times, then went still.

The other, smaller bandit froze.

He watched his companion fall, his mind blank, not yet realizing what had happened.

Then he saw the youth appear before him.

A palm struck the back of his head.

Darkness surged, the small bandit rolled his eyes back and collapsed, losing consciousness.

Shuichi looked at the two bodies on the ground.

One dead, one alive.

Perfect.

He crouched down, removed a piece of flesh from the dead bandit, wrapped it in a cloth he carried, and stored it in his Ninja pouch.

This was the DNA medium, the necessary material for summoning a soul.

Then he hoisted the unconscious bandit over his shoulder and walked deeper into the mountain path.

The dead bandit was left where he was. The beasts in the mountains would take care of the corpse ; no one would discover it.

Carrying the man, Shuichi walked for half an hour until he reached a wasteland at the end of the mountain path.

This place was far from villages and roads, surrounded by dense forest, where no one would pass by.

He placed the unconscious bandit on the ground and took out the piece of flesh from his Ninja pouch.

Looking at the bandit on the ground, Shuichi began to recall the Edo Tensei jutsu formula.

The sequence of seals, the method of forming them, the Chakra circulation path.

He had practiced it countless times in his mind ; now he only needed to put it into practice.

Shuichi took a deep breath and began to form hand seals.

Tiger.

His fingers shifted positions, and Chakra circulated within his body along a specific path.

It was a complex path, starting from the Dantian, passing through twelve meridians, and finally converging in his hands.

Snake.

His fingers continued to shift. The Chakra began to flow faster, surging through his meridians with a searing temperature.

Dog.

Chakra surged from his hands, injecting into the incantations on the ground.

Those incantations, which he had drawn with blood earlier, began to glow, the light growing brighter and brighter.

Dragon.

He pressed his hands together.

The jutsu activated.

Light erupted from the incantations, illuminating the entire wasteland.

Dust began to gather.

The dust seemed to come alive, rushing in from all directions, swirling in the air, and then descending to cover the unconscious bandit.

The bandit’s body began to change.

His skin turned grayish-white, losing its color. His bones were restructuring, emitting clicking sounds. His muscles writhed as if something were crawling beneath his skin.

The entire process lasted for over ten seconds.

Then the dust stopped gathering, and the light gradually dissipated.

The person on the ground opened his eyes.

They were hollow eyes, lacking focus and spirit. The pupils were dilated, like those of a dead man.

His face was identical to the dead bandit, but his skin was grayish-white, and the scent of dust drifted around his body.

Edo Tensei, success.

Shuichi looked at the Edo Tensei body before him and nodded slightly.

The jutsu formula had no issues.

Next, it was time to test the real thing.

Plunder.

Shuichi did not waste time.

He raised his right hand, aiming his palm at the top of the Edo Tensei body’s head, while simultaneously using Chakra to reinforce the suppression effect of the jutsu.

The consciousness of the Edo Tensei body was completely suppressed, unable to resist, and could only stand there blankly.

Shuichi pressed his right hand onto the top of its head.

A cold sensation came from his palm. This body had no temperature, no pulse, only fake flesh simulated by dust.

Plunder activated.

Shuichi closed his eyes and probed his consciousness into the Edo Tensei body.

He first tried to Plunder Vitality.

His right hand’s perception swept through the Edo Tensei body, from the top of the head to the soles of the feet, from skin to internal organs.

Blood vessels ? None.

Meridians ? None.

Chakra flow ? None.

There was nothing in this body. It was fake flesh simulated by dust, not a true body of flesh and blood.

The true body had long since disappeared when the corpse was cremated ; all that remained was the soul.

The only real thing about the Edo Tensei body was the soul.

Shuichi withdrew his perception and opened his eyes.

The first path was a dead end.

All attributes at the physical level—Vitality, Chakra capacity, physical strength—had vanished along with the cremation of the corpse. He could not Plunder anything from this fake flesh.

Then what about the soul ?

Shuichi closed his eyes again and probed his consciousness into the depths of the Edo Tensei body’s soul.

There, he found the experiences of the bandit during his lifetime.

He saw some images.

The nervousness of the bandit during his first robbery. That was three years ago ; he had been crouching by a mountain path, his hands shaking violently, nearly dropping his blade.

The fear of the bandit during his first kill. It was a merchant who had resisted ; he had accidentally stabbed the man, blood spraying onto his face, and he had been so terrified he fled overnight, not daring to sleep for three days.

The location of the cave where he hid his stolen goods. Three kilometers east of the mountain path, in a hidden cave, his loot from the past two years was piled up.

The names and appearances of his accomplices. He had a brother named A-Hu who worked on another mountain, and they would occasionally pull off big jobs together.

Shuichi locked onto these memories.

Plunder activated.
