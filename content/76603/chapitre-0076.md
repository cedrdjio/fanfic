# Chapitre 76 — After Uchiha Shuichi left, the cave returned to silence.

His footsteps gradually faded away, eventually disappearing into the depths of the cave. In the direction of the cave entrance, only moonlight remained, spilling in and illuminating a corner of the ground.

Uchiha Madara sat on the stone platform, his gaze calmly fixed on the direction of the entrance.

He did not move ; his body remained sitting quietly like a desiccated corpse. Only the Three Tomoe in his eyes rotated slowly, red light flickering within his pupils.

A few seconds later, the shadows on the ground began to flow.

Those shadows seemed alive, winding and gathering on the ground, then slowly manifesting into a black figure.

It was a humanoid existence, pitch black all over, with only its eyes being white. Its body seemed to be composed of some kind of liquid, flowing and shifting, without a fixed form. Its silhouette was slightly blurred under the moonlight, as if it could melt into the shadows at any moment.

Black Zetsu.

Uchiha Madara’s creation, Uchiha Madara’s will, Uchiha Madara’s shadow.

At least, that was what Uchiha Madara believed.

It emerged from the ground and stood beside Uchiha Madara, its gaze also fixed towards the cave entrance. Those white eyes shimmered in the darkness, carrying a sense of scrutiny.

« Uchiha Madara. »

Black Zetsu spoke, its voice carrying a strange texture, as if it were coming from a great distance away, yet also as if it were resounding directly inside the mind.

« Why didn’t you kill that brat’s mother earlier ? »

Uchiha Madara did not speak, simply looking ahead quietly.

His gaze was calm, but the Three Tomoe were rotating slowly, as if he were contemplating something.

Black Zetsu continued, « Or rather, control her. If we controlled his mother, he wouldn’t dare disobey Uchiha Madara’s orders. »

« In that case, he would have no choice but to obediently cooperate with the Eye of the Moon Plan. »

Uchiha Madara still did not speak.

Black Zetsu watched Uchiha Madara, waiting for an answer. Its body flowed slightly under the moonlight, as if adjusting its posture.

A few seconds later, Uchiha Madara spoke.

« You underestimate him too much. »

His voice was calm, carrying a faint rasp. Every word seemed to be squeezed from the depths of his throat, but his tone carried a warning.

« For someone like him, controlling his mother would, on the contrary, not make him obey your orders. »

Uchiha Madara’s gaze fell on Black Zetsu, the Three Tomoe rotating slowly.

« He would exhaust every means to save his mother from my hands. He would plan, he would wait, he would seek every opportunity. »

Uchiha Madara’s voice became deep.

« He would not submit ; he would only resist. »

Black Zetsu fell silent.

It looked at Uchiha Madara, waiting for more explanation.

Uchiha Madara continued, « This brat killed over a hundred people in the Land of Grass ; his methods were clean and efficient, without any hesitation. His strength is formidable, and his Growth rate is fast. More importantly, he is cunning and patient. »

« Such a person, once pushed to the brink, will unleash astonishing power. »

Uchiha Madara’s gaze became profound.

« Controlling his mother is equivalent to pushing him to the brink. He would not submit ; he would only try every means to resist. »

Black Zetsu listened to Uchiha Madara’s words without refuting them.

It knew Uchiha Madara was right.

Uchiha Madara continued, « If we simply killed his mother, he still wouldn’t obey our orders. »

« On the contrary, he would spare no cost to stop us. »

Uchiha Madara’s voice became cold.

« Such a person, once he has identified an enemy, will fight to the death. He has strength, intelligence, and patience. Making him an enemy is not a wise choice, at least not for now. »

Black Zetsu was silent for a moment, then spoke.

« Then why reach an agreement with him ? »

Uchiha Madara looked at Black Zetsu, his gaze calm.

« Because he is growing too fast. »

Uchiha Madara’s voice carried a faint sense of lament.

« I noticed him from the moment he stepped into the Land of Grass. »

« And from then until he awakened the Mangekyo Sharingan, only a few days had passed. »

Uchiha Madara’s gaze became profound.

« In just a few days, he grew from an ordinary Uchiha clansman to a level of strength sufficient to threaten me. »

Uchiha Madara’s voice was calm.

« Moreover, his Mangekyo ocular jutsu counters me too much right now. Even if I were to give up the remaining time I have left and choose to fight him, in the end, it would only be a solitary dance for me. »

Uchiha Madara’s gaze fell into the depths of the cave.

« If we are talking about defeating him, I have absolute certainty. But if we are talking about keeping him here, I cannot do that right now. »

« Furthermore, that method of his to absorb abilities is something even I don’t know what it is. »

Uchiha Madara’s voice carried a faint sense of doubt.

« Bloodline Limit ? It doesn’t look like it. Secret technique ? It doesn’t look like it either. Ninjutsu ? Even less so. »

« This Growth rate is something I didn’t expect. »

« And he seems to know a lot. »

Uchiha Madara’s voice continued to sound.

« He accurately found the location of the box of ultimate bliss. »

« Just now when he came in, he saw the Gedo Statue without showing any expression of surprise. »

Uchiha Madara’s gaze became profound.

« It was as if he already knew of the Gedo Statue’s existence. »

« When I proposed the Eye of the Moon Plan, he didn’t have much of a reaction either. »

« Ordinary people hearing about such a plan would either be shocked or suspicious. But he just listened calmly, without any reaction. »

Uchiha Madara’s voice carried a sense of doubt.

« It was as if he already knew about this plan. »

Black Zetsu listened to Uchiha Madara’s words and fell into deep thought.

« From the information obtained from White Zetsu’s perspective, he seems to have a natural sense of superiority over others. »

Uchiha Madara continued.

« When he was killing people in the Land of Grass, that composure, that indifference, it was as if he were dealing with some insignificant things. »

« He has no awe, no fear towards other Ninjas, only an attitude of condescension. »

Uchiha Madara’s gaze became profound.

« Such a person is either extremely confident, or... »

« He seems to know some things that we don’t. »

Black Zetsu was silent for a moment, then spoke.

« Then what do we do now ? »

Uchiha Madara looked at Black Zetsu, his gaze calm.

« Advance the plan. »

His voice carried a sense of decisiveness.

« Originally, I planned to find an Uchiha to cultivate slowly and let him become my proxy. But now the situation has changed. »

Uchiha Madara’s gaze became profound.

« Shift the focus of finding a proxy to targeting that brat in the Land of Rain. »

« Nagato. »

Black Zetsu’s gaze shifted slightly.

« Nagato ? »

« Yes. »

Uchiha Madara’s voice was calm.

« He possesses my eyes. Controlling him will greatly accelerate the progress of the plan. »

« Assist him greatly. »

Uchiha Madara continued.

« First, use some methods to make Nagato hate this world ; only through hatred will he have the motivation to change the world. »

« Then, have White Zetsu go and assist him, guide him, and let him walk the path we want. »

Uchiha Madara’s gaze became profound.

« Let him quickly master the power of the Rinnegan. »

« The plan to collect the Tailed Beast needs to be advanced. »

Black Zetsu listened to Uchiha Madara’s words and nodded.

« I understand, Uchiha Madara. »

« I will go and make arrangements in the Land of Rain. »

Uchiha Madara’s gaze fell on the Gedo Statue in the depths of the cave.

« There should have been enough time originally. But now with Uchiha Shuichi as a variable, we cannot proceed according to plan anymore. »

« Completing the plan ahead of schedule is the only way to avoid accidents. »

Black Zetsu was silent for a moment, then spoke.

« Then, regarding Uchiha Shuichi... »

« Do not worry. »

Uchiha Madara’s voice was calm.

« Before he possesses the power to completely ignore me, he will not act rashly. »

Uchiha Madara’s gaze became profound.

« As long as he doesn’t make a move, my plan can proceed smoothly. »

« As for five years from now... »

Uchiha Madara’s voice carried a faint smile.

« By then, my plan may have already been completed. »

The cave fell into silence once again.

Uchiha Madara sat on the stone platform, his gaze calmly fixed ahead.

His body was motionless, only his chest rising and falling slightly, his breathing so faint it was almost imperceptible.

Black Zetsu stood beside Uchiha Madara, also remaining silent.

Its body flowed slightly under the moonlight, as if it were thinking about something.

The Gedo Statue was in the depths of the cave, quietly bound by chains. Chakra flowed through pipes into Uchiha Madara’s body, sustaining his life. Those pipes shimmered with a faint light under the moonlight, like some kind of strange blood vessels.

Everything was very calm.

But Black Zetsu knew that beneath the calm, undercurrents were surging.

That brat named Uchiha Shuichi had even made Uchiha Madara feel a hint of vigilance.

And he was very likely to become an obstacle to it saving its mother.

Black Zetsu noted this name in its heart.

Uchiha Shuichi.

It must keep a close eye on him ; it absolutely must not let him interfere with Uchiha Madara’s plan.

In the future, if it encounters this brat, it must deal with him carefully.

Moonlight spilled in through the cave entrance, illuminating a corner of the cave.

Uchiha Madara closed his eyes and began to rest.

His body was very weak and needed a lot of time to recover its energy. Although the Gedo Statue’s Chakra could sustain his life, it could not restore him to his peak state.

He needed to wait.

Wait for the completion of the plan.

Wait for that new world.

Black Zetsu silently sank into the ground and disappeared.

Its body melted into the ground like liquid, leaving no traces behind.

Only Uchiha Madara remained in the cave, sitting quietly on the stone platform.

Meanwhile, outside the cave, Uchiha Shuichi was heading towards Konoha with White Zetsu.

Moonlight shone on the mountain path, illuminating the road ahead.

Uchiha Shuichi’s pace was steady, his gaze calm.

He did not know about the conversation that had taken place in the cave.

But he knew that Uchiha Madara was not a simple person.

That cunning and calculating man must be contemplating something.

The five-year agreement was just a beginning.

The real contest was yet to come.

Uchiha Shuichi looked up at the sky ; the moon was still hanging high.

Silver-white light shone on the mountain path, dyeing everything a faint white.

His gaze was calm, his steps firm.

Five years was enough.

He would become stronger, he would grow, he would accumulate power.

And then, go touch that higher level.

Uchiha Shuichi continued walking forward, his figure disappearing into the night.
