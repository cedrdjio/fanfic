# Chapitre 45 — Surging Strength

One and a half months later.

Shuichi walked into the Black Market hall and headed straight to the counter, as usual.

The people in the hall had changed. The bounty hunters who had been drinking in the corner a month and a half ago were gone, replaced by a few new faces.

Shuichi had come to the hall over a dozen times in these past six weeks, and each time the people in the hall were different ; only the middle-aged man behind the counter sat there as immovable as a mountain.

« Submitting a mission. » Shuichi placed a scroll on the counter.

The middle-aged man took the scroll and broke the seal. As the white smoke dissipated, a head appeared on the counter.

The middle-aged man lowered his head to check the register. After verifying the facial features, he took out the banknotes from the safe.

« A-014, 750,000 Ryo. »

Shuichi took the money but did not leave immediately.

« Are there any other Jonin-level targets nearby ? »

The middle-aged man glanced at him. Over the past month and a half, this wandering Ninja with a crow had submitted over a dozen missions, ranging from B-rank to A-rank.

Every time, the expression on the severed head was calm, and every time, the cross-section was perfectly neat. The middle-aged man had gotten used to it.

He opened the register, flipped to the A-rank page, and ran his finger down the list.

« Near Tanzaku Town... there are none left, » the middle-aged man said. « The closest Jonin-level target is at the border of the Land of Grass. There are a few A-rank missions, but the targets are not Ninjas. »

Shuichi shook his head and turned to leave.

Shuichi was not interested in non-Ninjas ; after all, money was more than enough for him now.

He walked out of the Black Market, crossed the woods, and returned to Tanzaku Town.

Tanzaku Town in the afternoon was a bit quieter than at night. Shuichi did not choose to return to the inn, but instead turned into a hot spring inn at the corner of the street.

He paid at the counter and requested a private bath.

Pushing open the door to the private bath, he found a small courtyard with a stone hot spring pool, steam rising from it.

Shuichi placed his short blade and coat on the wooden rack by the pool, took off his clothes, and stepped into the hot spring.

The hot water submerged his chest. Shuichi leaned against the pool wall and let out a long breath.

One and a half months.

He had stayed in Tanzaku Town for a month and a half, hunting over a dozen bounty targets through the Black Market.

Genin, Chunin, Jonin—he had killed them all.

For Genin, Shuichi focused on plundering Chakra nature transformations, while for Chunin and Jonin, Shuichi plundered Chakra volume.

Now, Shuichi’s five attributes were complete, and his Chakra volume was dozens of times greater than it had been a month and a half ago.

This was no exaggeration. A month and a half ago, his Chakra volume was barely at the Chunin level ; now, it was at the level of an Elite Jonin.

The Chakra of over a dozen Ninjas stacked together, and the quantitative change had triggered a qualitative change.

For the first time, Shuichi felt that when Chakra flowed through his meridians, it was no longer a trickle, but a wide river.

During this month and a half, he also endowed Lue with Chakra.

Although Lue’s current Chakra volume was very small, only about at the Genin level, it was enough for it to use basic Chakra adhesion and simple sensory techniques, which greatly improved Lue’s reconnaissance capabilities and flight speed.

And now, Shuichi’s Sharingan had successfully grown to three tomoe.

This was not something Shuichi did deliberately. The concept of Growth had been working slowly in his Sharingan, and going from two tomoe to three tomoe was just a matter of time.

The high-intensity combat over the past month and a half accelerated this process. Shuichi woke up the morning after a hunt and, when looking in the mirror, discovered that the three tomoe in both eyes had already formed.

With a three-tomoe Sharingan and Kage-level Chakra volume, Shuichi’s current strength already surpassed White Fang.

This was not arrogance. White Fang was a Kage-level powerhouse, and so was Shuichi.

But Shuichi had the pre-reading ability of the three-tomoe Sharingan, various sensory abilities plundered from others, and Sakumo’s combat experience.

Most of what White Fang knew, Shuichi knew, and Shuichi also possessed things White Fang did not. Overall, on paper, Shuichi’s strength had already surpassed White Fang in his prime.

Of course, his actual combat experience was still far behind.

White Fang’s intuition and judgment were honed by decades of licking blood from the blade, while Shuichi had only inherited his memories and had not yet truly been tempered repeatedly between life and death.

But at least in terms of raw strength, Shuichi no longer feared anyone.

Shuichi stood up from the hot spring, walked to the wooden rack by the pool, and took out a map from his clothes.

The map was bought from a general store in Tanzaku Town a few days ago, marking the main towns and routes in the Land of Fire and surrounding countries.

Shuichi spread the map on the wooden rack and moved his finger across it slowly.

There were still more than two months left before the Shadow Clone duration ended. No rush.

Shuichi’s finger paused on the map, and then he began to circle.

The first circle : Kusagakure.

The Land of Grass was a small country in the Ninja World, sandwiched between the Land of Fire and the Land of Earth, and its strength was not strong. But Kusagakure had two things worth Shuichi’s visit.

First, the Uzumaki Bloodline. In the original story, Karin’s mother should still be in Kusagakure.

The constitution and Chakra volume of the Uzumaki Clan were top-tier. If he could Plunder the Uzumaki Bloodline, Shuichi’s Chakra volume could reach another level.

Second, Hozuki Castle. Hozuki Castle was the prison of Kusagakure, holding Ninja prisoners from various countries.

These prisoners all possessed their own abilities and Chakra ; it was a mobile treasure trove.

The Land of Grass was not strong, and with Shuichi’s current Kage-level strength, he could completely walk sideways.

The second circle : Yugakure.

The Land of Hot Springs was also a small country, and Yugakure was famous for its hot springs. But Shuichi’s goal was not the hot springs, but the Jashin Cult.

The Jashin Cult had a stronghold in Yugakure, practicing some kind of immortality technique. If he could Plunder the core ability of the Jashin Cult—

Shuichi paused for a moment.

The returns were too unstable. Shuichi was not sure what the principle of the Jashin Cult’s immortality technique was.

Moreover, Hidan had not been born yet, and he didn’t know if ordinary believers had this ability.

If he went now, the cost and return would not be proportional. The strength of the Jashin Cult was unknown ; if the whole cult sacrificed themselves to take Shuichi down with them, he might not be able to escape unscathed.

The third circle : Land of Wind.

The ruins of the ancient city of Loulan deep in the desert of the Land of Wind. The Dragon Vein was under the ruins ; that was the ultimate resource for Sealing Technique and space-time jutsu.

Moreover, after the destruction of the Uzumaki Clan, there might be some inheritance left there.

But the Land of Wind was too far away. And the Third Kazekage was still alive. With his home-field advantage, Shuichi might not be able to defeat him.

The fourth circle : Land of Water.

Shikotsumyaku. Kimimaro’s bloodline ability, which could freely manipulate the Growth and density of bones.

This was an extremely powerful Bloodline Limit that combined offense and defense.

But the Land of Water was even further away, and the strength of the Hidden Mist Village could not be underestimated ; the difficulty of infiltrating was too great.

Shuichi looked at the four circles on the map and thought for a while.

Land of Grass. He was guaranteed to gain the Uzumaki Bloodline, and the Land of Grass was not strong, so the risk was controllable.

The Jashin Cult in the Land of Hot Springs had too unstable returns, and the Land of Wind and the Land of Water were too far.

Shuichi pressed his finger on the circle of Kusagakure.

« That’s it then. »

Shuichi folded the map, stuffed it back into his clothes, and went back into the hot spring to soak for a while.

Lue flew down from the courtyard wall, landed on the wooden rack by the pool, and tilted its head to look at Shuichi.

« Shu, » Lue said.

« Hmm ? »

« Where to next ? »

Shuichi had told Lue before that he would leave after finishing the bounty missions near Tanzaku Town.

« Land of Grass, » Shuichi said.

Lue nodded, without asking why. It had learned to trust Shuichi’s judgment.

Shuichi got out of the hot spring, dried his body, and put on his clothes.

He tucked his short blade into his waist, and Lue flew onto his shoulder.

Walking out of the inn, the evening in Tanzaku Town was still lively. Shuichi walked through the crowd, heading towards the direction of the inn. He would set off tomorrow morning for the Land of Grass.
