# Chapitre 61 — Raising a Dog

When he returned home, Minako was watering flowers in the courtyard.

« Back already ? » Minako straightened her back and wiped the sweat from her forehead.

« Yeah, I’m back. » Shuichi nodded and walked into the courtyard.

Lue flew up from his shoulder, landed on the plum tree, and quietly preened its feathers. It tilted its head to look at Shuichi, then at Minako, who was watering the flowers, as if waiting for something.

Shuichi stood in the courtyard, watching Lue, then looking at Minako.

Lue was a very good scout ; it could scout and also stand guard. But it usually followed him and wouldn’t stay home all the time.

What if someone did something to Minako when he wasn’t home ?

Shuichi frowned.

The matter at Hozuki Castle had ended, but that didn’t mean the danger had disappeared. There was never a shortage of undercurrents within Konoha : Root, Danzo, and other forces he didn’t know about. He couldn’t stay by Minako’s side every moment.

Even though Shuichi knew that it was highly likely nothing would happen to the Uchiha Clan Compound in the future, he didn’t dare to gamble.

He needed a guardian who could stay at home.

He hadn’t considered this before because no matter how much you trained an ordinary dog, it was just for guarding the house.

But now it was different. His spiritual power had increased significantly after his Mangekyo awakened, and combined with the Plunder ability of his right hand, it wouldn’t be difficult to raise a pet with a certain level of strength.

He could rely on Plunder to attach suitable abilities to the dog, making it grow quickly. He could even use his spiritual power to guide its Growth.

By then, it would not only be able to guard the house, but also protect Minako at critical moments.

Moreover, dogs were loyal enough.

Thinking of this, Shuichi spoke to his mother.

« Mom. »

Minako turned her head and put down the watering can : « What’s wrong ? »

« I want to get a dog. »

Minako was stunned for a moment, then smiled : « Why the sudden urge to get a dog ? »

« To guard the house, » Shuichi said. « I’m at school most of the time, and you’re home alone. It’ll be safer if there’s a dog. »

Minako walked up to Shuichi and reached out to stroke his head.

« Are you worried about me ? »

Shuichi didn’t speak, just nodded.

Minako’s eyes softened ; she crouched down and looked into Shuichi’s eyes.

« Mom is fine, don’t worry. But... » She smiled. « If you want to raise one, then let’s do it. The courtyard is a bit lonely anyway ; having a dog running around will be more lively. »

« I’ll go buy one after school tomorrow, » Shuichi said.

« Okay. » Minako stood up. « I’ll make dinner, you go rest first. »

Shuichi nodded and turned to walk into the house.

The next day.

After school, Shuichi didn’t leave with Tetsuya or Obito, but went straight to the commercial street.

« Shuichi, not eating ramen today ? » Obito chased after him to ask.

« I have something to do, » Shuichi said. « You guys go ahead. »

« Alright. » Obito scratched his head. « See you tomorrow then ! »

Shuichi nodded and turned to walk toward the commercial street.

Minako was already waiting at the street entrance. She had changed into a light blue kimono today, her hair tied behind her head, making her quite conspicuous in the crowd.

« Shuichi. » Minako waved.

Shuichi walked over, and the two walked into the commercial street together.

« Have you thought about what kind you want to buy ? » Minako asked.

« I’ll see when I get there. »

« Big or small ? Fierce or gentle ? »

Shuichi thought for a moment : « Small. Quiet. »

« Quiet ? » Minako was a bit surprised. « Shouldn’t you choose a fiercer one for guarding the house ? »

« Fierce doesn’t necessarily mean good, » Shuichi said. « Being quiet doesn’t mean it’s incapable. »

Minako smiled : « You certainly have a lot of ideas. »

The two arrived at the pet store they had passed yesterday. In the cage at the store entrance, several puppies were huddled together playing, barking. There were brown ones, black ones, spotted ones, chasing and playing with each other, very lively.

The shop owner was a middle-aged man, crouching next to the cage feeding them. Hearing footsteps, he looked up and wiped the dog food crumbs from his hands.

« Welcome, looking to buy a pet ? »

Shuichi didn’t answer, his gaze sweeping over the puppies in the cage.

Seeing someone arrive, the puppies flocked to the side of the cage, barking and wagging their tails, hoping to be chosen.

Only one was curled up in the corner of the cage.

It was pure white, squeezed out by the other puppies ; it didn’t bark or fight, just lay there quietly. Its light blue eyes looked outside the cage, its gaze calm, without anxiety or expectation.

« That one. » Shuichi pointed.

The owner took a look and smiled : « That one... its appearance isn’t great, it’s too quiet, doesn’t like to bark, nobody wants it. If you want it, I’ll sell it to you cheap. »

« How much ? »

The owner quoted a number, and Shuichi took the money out of his pocket and handed it over.

Minako stood by, looking at the white puppy in the cage, her expression a bit surprised.

« This one... » she said softly.

The owner opened the cage, picked up the white puppy, and handed it to Shuichi.

The puppy was very small, easily held in one hand. Its fur was pure white, its eyes light blue, and it lay quietly in Shuichi’s palm, not struggling or barking.

« This dog doesn’t like to bark, might not be great for guarding, » the owner shook his head. « But it’s fine to keep as a pet. »

« It’s fine. » Shuichi lowered his head to look at the puppy and reached out to stroke its head. « I like quiet ones. »

The puppy felt Shuichi’s touch and gently rubbed against his fingers.

Minako leaned in to look and reached out to stroke the puppy’s head.

« So cute. » She smiled. « The fur color is very pretty. »

The puppy felt Minako’s touch, gently rubbed against her fingers, and let out a slight whimper.

« It seems to like you very much, » Shuichi said.

« Is that so ? » Minako took the puppy and held it in her arms. « Have you thought of a name for it ? »

« Haku. »

« Haku ? » Minako smiled. « It matches its fur color quite well. »

She looked down at the puppy in her arms, her voice becoming gentle.

« Hungry, aren’t you ? Let’s go back and get you something to eat. »

Shuichi and Minako walked home together.

The sun was setting, and the people on the street were gradually thinning out. Golden-red light spilled onto the rooftops and treetops, stretching shadows long. Minako walked in front, holding Haku, humming softly. Shuichi followed behind, watching her back.

Lue circled in the air, then landed on Shuichi’s shoulder.

Shuichi looked down at Haku ; the puppy was lying in Minako’s arms, its light blue eyes half-open and half-closed, appearing a bit sleepy.

« It’s very quiet, » Minako said.

« Yeah. »

« Just like you when you were little. » Minako turned her head and smiled. « You didn’t like to cry when you were little, either ; you were always so quiet. »

Shuichi didn’t speak.

« Back then, your father would even say, 'Why is this child so well-behaved, is something wrong ?' » Minako smiled and shook her head. « In the end, the doctor finished the checkup and said everything was normal, it was just a quiet personality. »

Shuichi looked at Haku ; the puppy’s ears twitched, as if it had heard their conversation.

« Mom. »

« Hmm ? »

« Haku will be very powerful in the future, » Shuichi said.

Minako was stunned for a moment, then smiled : « Is that so ? Then I’ll leave it to you to train it well. »

At night.

Shuichi sat at his desk, flipping through a reference book about the various clans of Konoha.

This book was one he had borrowed from the school library, containing basic information about the major clans of Konoha. The chapter on the Aburame clan was only two short pages, describing the basic characteristics of Kikai bugs : they parasitized the host’s body, fed on Chakra, and in return provided the host with scouting and combat capabilities.

Shuichi closed the book and leaned back in his chair.

The information in the book was too little ; he needed to go see for himself.

Haku was lying by his bed, curled into a ball, already asleep. Its breathing was very light ; occasionally it would move its ears, then quiet down again.

Shuichi turned his head and looked at Haku.

He reached out and gently pressed his hand on top of Haku’s head.

He used his spiritual power to probe into Haku’s body, sensing its physical structure.

An ordinary puppy. No special bloodline, all physical indicators were within the normal range. The only advantage was that its spiritual power was slightly stronger than an ordinary puppy’s, which was probably why it was quieter and calmer than the other puppies.

Shuichi withdrew his spiritual power.

It could be cultivated.

He could Plunder some useful concepts and attach them to Haku, such as the scent-tracking ability of the Inuzuka clan’s ninja dogs, or the night vision of certain animals, or the reaction speed of wild beasts. Then he could use his spiritual power to guide its Growth, letting it gradually adapt to these concepts.

It didn’t need to be too strong ; being able to protect Minako was enough.

Shuichi turned to look out the window. Lue was perched on the plum tree, already asleep.

Since he was going to cultivate Haku, he could also help strengthen Lue at the same time.

Lue was currently his eyes and ears, responsible for scouting and surveillance. Its speed and perception were decent, but its combat ability was limited.

Shuichi remembered the Wind Release Chakra he had Plundered before. Lue was a bird, and its compatibility with Wind Release was excellent. If he could teach it some Wind Release Ninjutsu, like Wind Blade cutting or Wind Barrier, then it would at least have self-preservation ability when encountering danger, and could even cooperate with him in battle.

The Aburame clan’s intelligence network.

The Edo Tensei in the Scroll of Seals.

And the cultivation of Haku and Lue.

Shuichi stood up and walked to the window.

The moon was full, and silver-white light spilled into the courtyard. The shadow of the plum tree was cast on the ground ; the wind blew, and the shadow swayed gently.

Lue was perched on the plum tree, already asleep. Its feathers shone with a faint luster under the moonlight.
