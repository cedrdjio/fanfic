# Chapitre 43 — Black Market Intelligence

On the afternoon of the fourth day, the caravan arrived at a fork in the road.

The left path led to Takino Town, and the right path led to Tanzaku Town. Takino Town was north of Tanzaku Town, about half a day’s journey between them.

Tsunade and Shizune got off at the fork.

« Thanks for the ride, » Tsunade said casually to the guard of the carriage in front.

« You’re welcome, you’re welcome, » the guard said, waving his hands hurriedly. Although he didn’t recognize Tsunade, spending two days together had taught him that these two women were not to be trifled with.

Tsunade turned and walked toward Tanzaku Town. Shizune followed behind, carrying a small bundle on her back.

After walking a few steps, Shizune suddenly turned her head to look back.

She saw Shuichi.

The wandering samurai who had been on Yuanda’s carriage had also gotten off here. A crow stood on his shoulder, and he held a small cloth bag in his hand.

Shizune frowned slightly. Getting off at the exact same location from the same caravan was too much of a coincidence. She subconsciously took another look at Shuichi, but then she remembered that he had boarded the carriage earlier than them. He was already planning to go to Tanzaku Town ; she and Lady Tsunade were the ones who had hitched a ride later.

Shizune withdrew her gaze and quickly caught up with Tsunade.

« Mr. Shu, you’re getting off here too ? » Yuanda poked his head out of the carriage.

« Yes, » Shuichi said.

« Then... » Yuanda hesitated, pulled a small cloth bag from his coat, and handed it to Shuichi. « This is a token of gratitude for your help earlier. Thank you for stepping in. »

Shuichi glanced at Yuanda. Yuanda’s expression was sincere.

« Thanks, » Shuichi said, taking the cloth bag.

Yuanda grinned, revealing his chipped front tooth.

Shuichi stuffed the cloth bag into his coat, turned, and walked toward Tanzaku Town.

By evening, Shuichi arrived at Tanzaku Town.

It was livelier than he had expected. Two-story wooden buildings lined the streets, with red lanterns hanging from one end to the other, illuminating the entire street in a crimson glow. The air was thick with the scent of alcohol, cosmetics, and food. The streets were crowded with men and women in various outfits coming and going, their laughter, hawking, and the clattering of dice blending together.

Shuichi pulled up the collar of his dark green robe, which he used as a disguise, to cover his chin. Lue perched on his shoulder, his black crow eyes scanning the surrounding buildings.

Shuichi was in no hurry to enter.

He first looked for an inconspicuous inn.

The inn was located in a side alley of Tanzaku Town. It had a small storefront, and the writing on the sign had already faded. The owner, a thin, withered old man, glanced at Shuichi, his eyes lingering on the short sword at his waist. He then took the room fee and handed over a key.

« The room at the very end of the second floor, » the old man warned. « Don’t cause trouble. »

Shuichi nodded, took the key, and went upstairs.

The room was small, containing just a bed, a table, a chair, and a window. The window faced the alley, offering a view of the roofs of the buildings opposite. Shuichi locked the door, placed the cloth bag on the table, and sat down to wait quietly for nightfall.

The black market wouldn’t be marked on any map, nor would anyone shout about it on the streets. Information such as the black market’s entrance, secret codes, and operating hours only existed in the minds of a few. Casinos were the most lucrative business in Tanzaku Town, and there was almost certainly a connection between casinos and the black market. Finding a casino executive was equivalent to finding the key to the black market.

The casinos in Tanzaku Town operated twenty-four hours a day, but the people who actually ran things usually only appeared late at night. Since Shuichi needed a casino executive, he had to wait until dark.

The sky gradually darkened.

Shuichi sat by the window, watching the people come and go in the alley. The nightlife in Tanzaku Town had just begun, with drunk men and heavily made-up women passing through the alley from time to time.

By the early hours of the morning, the crowd on the street had thinned out.

Shuichi stood up and walked to the window. Lue flew onto the windowsill, tilting his head to look at him.

« Go find someone, » Shuichi said in a low voice. « Keep an eye on the back door of the casino and look for those who seem to be in charge. Follow him, and when he’s alone, come back and tell me. »

After digesting human intelligence, Lue was clearly much smarter and could understand Shuichi’s words.

It nodded, silently spread its black wings, and flew out of the window.

Shuichi sat back in his chair and closed his eyes to wait.

Lue’s night vision was far better than Shuichi’s naked eyes in the dark, and with his wide aerial view, Shuichi believed he would find a target soon.

About half an hour passed.

A soft sound of talons clicking on wood came from the windowsill. Shuichi opened his eyes, and Lue was perched on the windowsill, looking at him with his black eyes.

« Found someone, » Lue said. His voice was very soft, audible only to Shuichi.

« Speak. »

« A man came out of the casino’s back door. Fat, well-dressed. He took a woman into a building. »

Shuichi thought for a moment. Well-dressed, coming out of the casino’s back door, and bringing a woman back—there was a high probability he was a mid-level manager of the casino. Such a person should know quite a lot.

« Lead the way, » Shuichi said.

Shuichi climbed out of the window and made his way along the rooftops.

The buildings in Tanzaku Town were all two- or three-story wooden structures with little height difference between the roofs, allowing Shuichi to cross them in just a few steps. His footsteps were light, his soles making almost no sound as they touched down.

Lue flew above his head, occasionally dipping low to guide the way.

Three minutes later, Shuichi stopped on the roof of a two-story wooden building.

Below him was the house Lue had mentioned. Shuichi expanded his perception ; there were two heat sources in the house, both on the second floor. Their Chakra activity was very low ; they were ordinary people, not Ninjas.

Shuichi gently pushed open the window and slipped inside.

The room was very dark, with only the faint moonlight filtering in from the window. A fat man, about thirty or forty years old with a round belly, lay bare-chested on the bed, snoring loudly. Curled up beside him was a young woman in thin undergarments, her back turned to the man, her body trembling slightly.

Shuichi walked to the side of the bed.

Sensing something, the woman turned her head.

She saw Shuichi.

The moonlight shone on Shuichi’s face, making the scar at the corner of his eye stand out starkly in the shadows. He held a short sword in his hand, which gleamed coldly under the moonlight.

The woman opened her mouth, just about to scream.

Shuichi’s blade was already pressed against her neck.

« Don’t make a sound, » Shuichi said. His voice was quiet, but carried an unquestionable authority.

The woman’s mouth snapped shut, her eyes wide with terror, and her body curled into a ball.

Shuichi didn’t look at her. His gaze fell upon the fat man.

The short sword pierced the fat man’s medulla oblongata.

The movement was swift and precise. The fat man’s body convulsed once, his snoring stopped abruptly, and then he went completely still. From the thrust to his death, it took less than a second. No pain, no sound.

The woman witnessed the entire process. Her body began to tremble violently, her hands clamped over her mouth as tears flowed silently down her face.

Shuichi reached out with his right hand and pressed it against the fat man’s temple.

He expanded his perception, thrusting his mental energy into the hippocampus. A chaotic stream of information flooded Shuichi’s mind—casino ledgers, connections, transaction records, and daily trivia. Shuichi searched and filtered through the stream of information, like rummaging through a pile of clutter for a specific item.

Black market. Entrance. Code.

A few seconds later, he found it.

Image : A basement entrance behind the woods on the east side of Tanzaku Town. There was a stone slab with a triangle carved on it, containing a horizontal line.

Audio : « New Moon. » « Penumbra. »

Image : At the end of the basement corridor was an iron door. Behind the iron door was a hall, where a middle-aged man sat behind a table in the corner, a thick bounty mission booklet laid out in front of him.

Shuichi withdrew his right hand.

Memory Plunder completed.

He stood up and looked at the woman.

The woman was curled up in the corner of the bed, her hands over her mouth, her makeup smudged by her tears. She looked to be in her early twenties, with a petite build and some bruises on her skin.

« M-my lord... » the woman’s voice trembled, « I-I was forced... He-he brought me back from the casino, I didn’t want to... Please don’t kill me... »

Shuichi looked at her.

She was telling the truth. Shuichi’s perception could feel her terror. Her heart was beating rapidly, her body temperature was low, and her muscles were constantly spasming. These physiological reactions could not be faked.

However, based on the information obtained from the fat man’s memory, this woman was no saint either.

So Shuichi only hesitated for a single second.

Then, the short sword slit the woman’s throat.

Blood gushed from the wound. The woman’s eyes widened, her mouth opening and closing a couple of times without making a sound. Her body fell backward, leaning against the headboard before slowly sliding down.

Shuichi wiped the blood off his blade on the woman’s clothes and sheathed it.

He would leave no survivors. Even though he had several ways to make her lose her memory, a dead person was always the most secure option.

Shuichi climbed back out of the window and returned to the inn along the rooftops the same way he had come.
