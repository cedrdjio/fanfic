# Chapitre 9 — Obito

Early summer, the 41st year of the Hokage era.

Shuichi crouched on the stone steps at the edge of the Uchiha Clan Grounds plaza, clutching a twig in his hand. He was absentmindedly drawing symbols on the ground that only he could understand—symbols representing the sum of his efforts over the past two months.

His Climbing Ability had been fully digested ; his physical foundation was roughly equivalent to that of a ten-year-old child, gained from the accumulated Vitality of six wild rabbits, three tree lizards, one Red Fox, and over forty insects. The wingspan of his raptor had already exceeded the average of an adult falcon.

He was calculating the best route for his next trip to the edge of the Forest of Death when he suddenly heard a burst of rapid footsteps coming from the east side of the plaza.

The footsteps were distinct ; they lacked the steady rhythm of an adult and instead sounded like a child running for their life. The steps were chaotic, the breathing was rapid, and there was even a muffled groan mixed in from when the child tripped.

Shuichi looked up and saw a young Uchiha boy wearing goggles stumbling out of the alley on the east side of the plaza.

The boy was about four years old, dressed in the deep blue casual wear common to the Uchiha Clan Grounds. The Uchiha fan crest was embroidered on his collar, but it was crooked—likely sewn by someone in his family.

His knees were covered in mud, and there was a shallow scrape on his cheek. His eyes were big and round, welling up with tears that were on the verge of spilling over, yet he gritted his teeth and refused to cry.

Uchiha Obito.

Shuichi’s gaze locked onto the boy, the twig in his hand freezing in mid-air.

Three boys, all a year or two older than Obito, chased out from behind him. Judging by their attire, they were also children from the Uchiha clan.

The leader held a wooden shuriken in his hand and shouted something that Shuichi didn’t catch, but the mockery in his tone was palpable regardless.

The three children caught up to Obito, and the leader tapped the wooden shuriken against the back of Obito’s head. It wasn’t a heavy blow, but Obito still stumbled and fell to the ground.

« Loser ! You can’t even throw a basic shuriken straight ; how are you going to attend the Ninja Academy ? » The leader poked Obito’s back with the wooden shuriken. « Your grandmother wasted her money buying that shuriken for you. »

Obito scrambled up from the ground, his face covered in mud, and a hole worn into the knee of his pants. His lips trembled, but he still didn’t cry.

He bent down to pick something up—a cracked wooden model of a forehead protector. It was crudely made, with a crooked Uchiha fan crest carved into it.

« This was made for me by my grandmother... » Obito’s voice was small and nasal. « Give it back. »

« Come and get it yourself. » The leader held the wooden shuriken over his head.

Obito charged at him.

The outcome was predictable : a four-year-old child against three older children, with no weapon in hand.

Obito was pushed to the ground, only to climb back up, then pushed down again and back up again. Another scrape appeared on his face, and the other knee of his pants was torn as well.

Shuichi watched this scene, gripping the twig in his hand tightly.

He knew who Obito was. He knew who this four-year-old child, being shoved around in the mud with torn knees and a scraped face, would become in the future.

Uchiha Obito—the man wearing the orange swirl mask, the one who manipulated the Nine-Tailed fox to destroy Konoha, the mastermind who orchestrated everything from the shadows.

He would kill Minato Namikaze and Uzumaki Kushina, launch the Fourth Ninja World War, and leave the entire Ninja World drowning in blood.

But for now, he was just a four-year-old child whose forehead protector had been stolen.

Shuichi stood up from the stone steps. The twig in his hand gave a faint cracking sound.

He walked over at a measured pace, but each step was steady. When he reached Obito, he reached out and placed a hand on his shoulder.

Obito turned his head and saw a face he didn’t recognize. An older Uchiha boy, black hair and black eyes, expressionless, but without a hint of mockery in his gaze.

« Who are you ? » the leader of the opposing group asked, looking Shuichi up and down. « Don’t meddle in other people’s business. »

Shuichi didn’t answer. He looked at the wooden shuriken. From the perspective of his ten-year-old physique, snatching a wooden toy from a six-year-old child would take less than a second.

But he couldn’t reveal his abilities.

He picked up a small pebble from the ground, weighed it in his hand, aimed at the center of the wooden shuriken, and flicked it with his thumb. The pebble traced a fast, straight arc, striking the handle of the wooden shuriken with precision. The wooden shuriken flew out of the boy’s hand and fell to the ground.

Shuichi walked over, picked up the wooden shuriken, and handed it to Obito. At the same time, he bent down to pick up the cracked wooden forehead protector and placed it back into Obito’s hands as well.

The leader was stunned for two seconds. He looked down at his tingling fingers, then back at Shuichi, and wisely chose to retreat. « Let’s go, let’s go. » The three of them quickly disappeared into the alley on the east side of the plaza.

Obito clutched his forehead protector, staring blankly at Shuichi.

« Th-thank you... » His voice was still very small, but the tears finally fell. « My grandmother made this for me. She said that I would definitely become Hokage in the future, so I need to practice wearing a forehead protector in advance. »

Shuichi looked down at the cracked wooden forehead protector. The craftsmanship was very rough, the carvings of uneven depth, clearly the work of an elderly person. In the center of the forehead protector were three wobbly characters carved into it : [Fourth Hokage].

Shuichi’s breath hitched for a moment.

Fourth Hokage. Obito’s grandmother had carved [Fourth Hokage] for him. He didn’t know if this detail existed in the original work, but in this world, it was truly carved into that broken piece of wood.

And the true Fourth Hokage, Minato Namikaze, would die at Obito’s hands in the future.

« Hokage. » Shuichi repeated the two words, his tone devoid of mockery or encouragement. It was merely a statement.

« Yeah ! » Obito nodded vigorously, wiped away his tears, and revealed a smile that still had mud smudges on it. « I will definitely become Hokage ! Just you wait ! »

Shuichi looked at that face, covered in mud and scrapes yet smiling with such absolute determination, and remained silent for a long time. Then, he turned and walked in the direction of his own courtyard.

« What’s your name ? » Obito called out from behind him.

Shuichi paused for a step. He did not look back.

« Uchiha Xiu Yi. »

« Shuichi ! » Obito shouted loudly. « When I become Hokage, I’ll make you my bodyguard ! »

Shuichi did not answer. He continued walking forward, his right hand clenched tightly inside his sleeve.

He said a sentence in his heart, both to the future Obito and to the four-year-old child currently wiping away tears in the plaza.

You will not become Hokage. But perhaps you can become something else.
