# Chapitre 85 — Plunder

Northern border of the Land of Fire, Iwagakure frontline.

The war had been raging for three months, and this frontline had fallen into a stalemate.

Konoha and Iwagakure Ninja were constantly being depleted in small-scale skirmishes, and corpses and the wounded were being sent to the rear in a steady stream.

Shuichi’s Shadow Clone was part of one of the support squads, responsible for supply transport and the evacuation of the wounded.

The Shadow Clone’s performance was unremarkable.

It completed tasks according to the captain’s orders, didn’t talk much, was inconspicuous, and didn’t arouse suspicion.

Over the past three months, it had just been a slightly talented newcomer in the squad, and no one would notice it.

As for the main body, it was on the periphery of the battlefield.

Shuichi stood on the top of a hill, watching the smoke of war in the distance.

Lue circled above his head, its black feathers shimmering slightly in the sunlight, and its crow-like appearance made it look like an ordinary scavenger bird.

There were many corpses on the battlefield, and Lue’s reports came in one after another.

« Three kilometers to the east, Iwagakure squad, five people, no spatial characteristics. »

« Five kilometers to the south, Konoha outpost, three people, no spatial characteristics. »

« Eight kilometers to the west, Sunagakure intelligence operative, one person, precise barrier Ninjutsu structure, possibly has weak spatial affinity. »

Shuichi raised his head and looked to the west.

« Lock onto him, » he said.

Lue tightened its wings and flew towards the west.

---

In the river valley region of the Land of Rivers, the Sunagakure intelligence operative was walking alone on the mountain path.

His name was Sahara, a Chunin from the Hidden Sand Village, responsible for delivering intelligence between the Konoha and Iwagakure battlefields.

His attainments in barrier Ninjutsu were quite good, and he could set up a spatial sealing barrier in a short time to prevent enemies from pursuing.

This ability was very useful in intelligence work, allowing him to escape from danger many times.

But he didn’t know that he had already been targeted.

Shuichi stood on the rock wall above the river valley, watching Sahara walk away along the mountain path.

He wore a white, featureless mask, a black hood pulled down to his eyebrows, and a dark gray robe without any markings, no headband, and no identifiable features.

A short blade was hidden in his sleeve, and his Chakra was suppressed to the minimum.

Lue circled higher up, using Infrared Sensing to lock onto Sahara’s position.

Sahara walked to a fork in the road and stopped to observe his surroundings.

He was an intelligence operative with high vigilance.

But he didn’t find anything unusual and continued walking forward.

Shuichi followed behind him, maintaining a distance of two hundred meters.

He didn’t use the Body Flicker Technique, but instead moved between the rock walls using ordinary Taijutsu, his footsteps light and without making any sound.

Sahara walked for another half hour and came to an abandoned outpost.

He walked into the outpost and began to organize his equipment, preparing to rest.

Shuichi stood on the treetops outside the outpost, watching Sahara’s movements through the window.

Sahara took off his Ninja tool pouch and placed it on the table.

He took out a map and began to mark something.

Shuichi didn’t wait.

Body Flicker Technique.

He vaulted through the window, the short blade slid out of his sleeve, and he stabbed Sahara in the back of the heart with one strike.

Sahara’s body stiffened for a moment, then went limp.

Shuichi pulled out the short blade and pressed his right hand onto the back of Sahara’s head.

Plunder.

Spatial Perception.

A faint sensation was pulled out from Sahara’s Chakra.

It was very small, very light, like a wisp of smoke.

Shuichi could feel it enter his right hand and then merge into his own Chakra.

Shuichi withdrew his right hand and turned to leave the outpost.

Sahara’s corpse fell beside the table, blood oozing from the wound and staining the map red.

Shuichi vaulted out of the window and disappeared into the treetops.

Lue followed him in the air, and after flying for a while, it spoke. « The first one. »

« Hmm, » Shuichi said. « The next one. »

---

Three days later, on the border of the Land of Grass.

Shuichi stood on the roof of an abandoned outpost, watching the Kumogakure Chunin below.

This Kumogakure Chunin was named Heiyu, responsible for transporting supplies using a simplified variant of the Heavenly Transfer Technique.

The Heavenly Transfer Technique is a unique spatial transfer Ninjutsu of Kumogakure, requiring specific geographical markers and techniques to coordinate, but the user must possess a certain spatial affinity to withstand the pressure of the spatial tunnel.

Heiyu’s spatial affinity was not strong, so he could only use a simplified version to transfer small supplies over short distances.

But this was enough for Shuichi.

Heiyu was organizing supplies in the outpost, preparing for the next day’s transport mission.

He was not alone ; he had three teammates.

Kumogakure’s supply transport line was very long, and each transit point was responsible for by a squad to prevent it from being cut off by the enemy.

Shuichi jumped down from the roof and landed in the outpost’s courtyard.

Someone in the outpost noticed the movement and immediately shouted : « Who ? »

Shuichi did not answer.

He took a step forward, the door of the outpost was pushed open, and two Kumogakure Ninja rushed out, holding Kunai in their hands.

« Enemy attack ! » one of them shouted.

Shuichi’s figure moved.

He passed between the two Kumogakure Ninja, the short blade slid out of his sleeve, and two streaks of blood flashed simultaneously.

The two Ninja fell at the doorway, their throats slit.

The other two people in the outpost rushed out, and Heiyu was one of them.

He was holding an explosive tag in his hand, about to detonate it.

Shuichi had already reached him.

The short blade stabbed into Heiyu’s chest, and the explosive tag fell from his hand.

Shuichi caught the explosive tag with his left hand, pulled out the short blade with his right, and turned to slash across the other Kumogakure Ninja’s throat.

Four corpses fell in the courtyard.

Shuichi walked to Heiyu’s corpse and pressed his right hand onto the back of his head.

Plunder.

Spatial Perception was pulled out, clearer and more complete than the one from Sahara.

Shuichi could feel it merge into his own Chakra, making the originally blurry perception become slightly clearer.

Shuichi withdrew his right hand and stood in place, feeling it for a while.

Spatial Perception had indeed strengthened.

He could feel the trajectory of Chakra flow in the surrounding space, clearer than before.

But it was still very shallow, like looking at things through a layer of gauze.

He could perceive the existence of the Flying Thunder God mark, but the outline was still blurry, and he could not lock onto it precisely.

It was not enough.

Shuichi turned and left the outpost, Lue following him in the air.

« The second one, » Lue said. « Do you want to continue ? »

« Continue, » Shuichi said. « This bit of Spatial Perception is not enough. »

Lue did not ask again.

It tightened its wings and flew towards another direction of the battlefield.

Shuichi followed behind, his white mask reflecting no luster in the moonlight.

His figure passed through the forest, crossed the mountain ridge, and disappeared into the night.

There were many targets on the battlefield.

He would find them one by one.

---

Five more days passed.

Shuichi stood in a valley on the Iwagakure frontline, watching the Iwagakure camp in the distance.

Lue’s report was very detailed : there were three Ninja in the camp who exhibited spatial characteristics.

One was a Chunin whose Body Flicker Technique distance was nearly double that of a normal Ninja.

One was a Jonin whose Earth Release displacement coordinates had high precision.

Another was a Special Jonin whose barrier Ninjutsu spatial structure was more stable than that of an average person.

Shuichi listened to the report and thought about it.

Three targets, all in the same camp.

Dealing with them one by one was too slow and easily exposed him.

It was better to solve it all at once.

He took a white, featureless mask from his bosom and put it on his face.

The black hood was pulled down to his eyebrows, and a dark gray robe was draped over his body.

The short blade slid out from his sleeve.

Shuichi walked towards the camp.

---

The Iwagakure camp was not big, about thirty people.

Most were Chunin and Genin, with only three Jonin-level combatants.

There were sentries on the periphery of the camp and patrols inside ; the defense was not tight.

Shuichi vaulted in from the rock wall on the north side of the camp.

The first sentry stood in the corner on the north side of the camp, with his back to Shuichi.

Shuichi Body Flickered behind him, the short blade stabbed into his heart, and the sentry fell.

The sound of the sentry falling alerted the camp.

« Enemy attack ! »

The alarm sounded in the camp.

People rushed out of the tents, holding Ninja tools, and Chakra began to surge.

Shuichi did not stop.

The second sentry was on the east side, about to run towards the inside of the camp.

Shuichi Body Flickered behind him, the short blade slashed across his throat.

The third sentry was at the entrance of the camp, shouting to notify others.

Shuichi passed by him, the short blade stabbed into his neck.

All three sentries fell.

The people in the camp had already assembled.

The three Jonin-level Ninja stood at the very front, with more than twenty Chunin and Genin behind them.

They saw Shuichi wearing a white mask, the black hood pulled very low, and the dark gray robe without any markings.

« Who are you ? » the Jonin standing in the middle asked, his voice vigilant.

Shuichi did not answer.

He took a step forward.

The three Jonin moved simultaneously.

The Jonin in the middle formed hand seals, and Earth Release Chakra surged.

The Special Jonin on the left took out a sealing scroll from his Ninja tool pouch, preparing to set up a barrier.

The Chunin on the right activated the Body Flicker Technique and moved to Shuichi’s side.

Shuichi’s figure also moved.

He passed between the three Jonin, the short blade sliding out from his sleeve.

The first strike stabbed into the chest of the Jonin in the middle, interrupting the Earth Release Chakra hand seals.

The second strike slashed across the throat of the Special Jonin on the left, and the sealing scroll fell from his hand.

The third strike stabbed into the heart of the Chunin on the right, ending the Body Flicker Technique before it was finished.

Three corpses fell to the ground simultaneously.

Shuichi stood in place, blood staining the short blade.

The twenty-plus Chunin and Genin behind were stunned.

Three Jonin-level combatants, all dead in three seconds.

Some started to retreat.

Shuichi did not give them a chance.

He started from the edge of the crowd, and every swing of the short blade took a life.

Chunin, Genin, none of them were his opponents.

Some tried to counterattack, throwing Kunai and Ninjutsu at him, but his figure was too fast, passing through the gaps in the attacks every time.

Twenty corpses fell in the camp.

There were a few people left, and they had given up resistance and started running out of the camp.

Shuichi Body Flickered to catch up, stabbing in from behind with the short blade.

Five corpses fell at the camp entrance.

Thirty people, all dead.

Shuichi stood in the center of the camp, watching the corpses around him.

The smell of blood permeated the air, and the campfire was still burning, illuminating the bloodstains on the ground.

He walked to the corpses of the three targets and pressed his right hand onto the back of their heads one by one.

Plunder.

When the third fragment of Spatial Perception was pulled out, Shuichi could feel the depth of Spatial Perception being raised a notch.

He could perceive the outline of the Flying Thunder God mark more clearly, but still could not lock onto it precisely.

It was like looking at things through a layer of thin gauze, able to see the general shape but not the details.

Still a little bit short.

He withdrew the short blade, took off the mask, took off the robe, and stuffed everything into his bosom.

Thirty consecutive kills, three Plunders, the mental energy consumption was not small.

He needed to rest.

Shuichi turned and walked out of the camp.

Lue followed him in the air, and after flying for a while, it spoke. « Going back ? »

« Hmm, » Shuichi said. « Rest for a few days, then find the next target. »

After saying that, Shuichi’s figure disappeared into the night.
