# Chapitre 28 — Wind Whispers

News about Sakumo Hatake reached the school on Wednesday.

Shuichi heard a few upperclassmen discussing it in the school building corridor. A boy with a ponytail was leaning against the window, his voice low, but everyone around him was pricking up their ears to listen.

« My dad said the White Fang of Konoha abandoned his mission to save his teammates, causing the Village to lose an intelligence network. »

« Abandoned the mission ? Even a Jonin would abandon a mission ? »

« That is why it is a disgrace. The first rule of the Ninja code—mission priority. He saved his teammates, but the mission failed. Saving one person’s life cost the entire Village. »

« What about the person he saved ? »

« I do not know. It seems like it was a Ninja from his team. They probably feel terrible right now, after all, they made someone abandon a mission just to save them. »

Shuichi walked past them without stopping. He had met Sakumo Hatake once, last year on the edge of the Training Ground. From afar, he had seen a man with silver-white hair standing before the Memorial Stone, standing motionless for a very long time. At the time, he thought it was just an ordinary Jonin mourning his teammates. Only later did he realize it was the White Fang of Konoha. A legendary Jonin who made a name for himself as the « White Fang of Konoha » early in the Third Great Ninja War, with strength rumored to be on par with the Sannin.

After the lunch break bell rang, Shuichi went to the Training Ground. He was used to being alone during lunch breaks, and Tetsuya knew this, so he would not come to disturb him. Obito usually ate his bento with his classmates. The shade of the oak tree in the corner of the Training Ground was his regular spot, from where he could observe the entire Training Ground.

But today, he had not even been sitting for a quarter of an hour when Obito arrived.

Obito jogged across the sandy ground of the Training Ground, clutching half an unfinished rice ball in his hand. He came to a stop in front of Shuichi, caught his breath for a moment, and then plopped down onto the roots of the oak tree.

« Brother Shuichi, have you heard ? About the White Fang. »

« Hmm. »

« They say the White Fang abandoned the mission. » Obito took a bite of the rice ball and said indistinctly, « But did he not save his teammates ? Why is he still being scolded after saving someone ? »

Shuichi looked at Obito. The seven-year-old child’s eyes held genuine confusion, not a stance taught by adults, but something he could not reconcile after thinking about it himself. Obito had been in school for a year, and the first rule taught by the school was mission priority. But Obito’s kind nature told him that saving teammates was right. These two things were constantly fighting in his mind.

« What do you think ? » Shuichi asked back.

Obito’s chewing slowed down. « I think saving people is right. » He said, then added, « But the Ninja code says mission priority. I do not know which is more important. »

« No one knows, » Shuichi said.

Obito looked up at him.

« Even adults do not know. They are discussing it, arguing over which is more important. » Shuichi leaned against the oak tree trunk. « You do not have to decide right now. You have a lifetime to think about it. »

Obito was silent for a moment, then nodded vigorously. He stuffed the rest of the rice ball into his mouth, his cheeks bulging like a squirrel’s. Shuichi watched him, recalling the child who had crouched in the corner of the courtyard crying two years ago. In two years, Obito no longer asked « Brother Shuichi, what should I do ? », he started asking « Why are things this way ? ». This question had no answer, but being able to ask this question itself was Growth.

As the lunch break was about to end, Obito stood up and brushed the sand off his pants.

« Brother Shuichi, Rin said you did not eat much of the yokan last time. Do you not like the red bean flavor ? »

Shuichi paused. « No. I just was not very hungry that day. »

« Then I will tell her to make matcha flavor next time. » Obito ran off after saying that.

Shuichi sat under the shade of the oak tree, watching Obito’s back disappear at the entrance of the Training Ground. Obito was concerned about whether he had eaten the yokan, while the upperclassmen in the corridor were discussing the White Fang’s disgrace. There were five years between seven and twelve, and what separated them was far more than just time.

---

On the way home from school, Shuichi walked very slowly.

The road from school back to the Uchiha Clan Grounds passed through Konoha’s main street, with Ninja tool shops, herbal medicine shops, ramen shops, and wagashi stalls. Usually, at this time, the street was filled with children returning from school and Ninjas getting off work, noisy but peaceful. Today was different.

Two Chunin stood at the entrance of a Ninja tool shop. Shuichi recognized them ; they were from the Uchiha Military Police Force, whom he occasionally encountered at the clan affairs office. They were talking, their voices not loud, but Shuichi’s auditory perception had been enhanced, so he could clearly hear whispered conversations within fifteen steps.

« ...After the White Fang’s teammate was saved, not only were they not grateful, but they also wrote down the process of him abandoning the mission in the mission report. »

« The person who was saved wrote a report to expose him ? »

« Not exposing. It is reporting the facts. But everything written in the report is now known to the whole Village. If someone saved your life, and then everyone is scolding that person, what would you do ? »

« ...That is tough. »

« So he did not say anything. He did not defend the White Fang, nor did he join in the scolding. He just shut up. »

Shuichi walked past the entrance of the Ninja tool shop without changing his pace. In his mind, he pieced this information together with what he had heard earlier. The rescued teammate chose silence. It was not kicking someone while they were down, but there was no gratitude either. In Konoha’s system of rules, defending the White Fang would be equivalent to admitting that he also agreed that « missions can be abandoned, » which would end his Ninja career. So he chose to remain silent.

Shuichi did not think this person was hateful. He thought this person was pitiful, or rather, that a Ninja is ultimately just a tool, a tool for the strong.

Continuing forward, he heard another conversation when passing the ramen shop. Two villagers, not Ninjas, but ordinary merchants, were sitting on a bench outside the ramen shop waiting for their turn.

« Sakumo Hatake, I used to think he was quite impressive. Never expected this kind of thing would happen. »

« Well, for Ninjas, the mission is everything. If you cannot even complete a mission, what kind of Ninja are you ? »

« Isn’t his son still in school ? »

« Seems like he is still studying. What was his name... Kakashi ? »

« What a sin. »

Shuichi walked past the ramen shop, those two words lingering in his ears. A sin. An ordinary villager saying the White Fang’s son was a sin. Not because of anything the child had done, but because of the choice his father had made.

What Shuichi thought of was not the White Fang. He thought of the Uchiha.

The White Fang was a Jonin, and while the Hatake clan was not a prestigious family, it was a respectable Ninja family. The White Fang’s contributions to Konoha, his war merits early in the Second Great Ninja War, his legendary level of personal strength—all of it meant nothing in the face of public opinion. The Village would not forgive one « non-compliant » choice because of past merits. The White Fang was being devoured by the Village today, and the Uchiha would be devoured tomorrow. It was only a matter of time.

He stopped for a moment when he crossed the Naka River Stone Bridge. The water under the bridge flowed rapidly under the afternoon sun, the light spots on the river surface torn apart by the water ripples and then pieced back together. Lue flew over from the direction of the Clan Grounds, landed on the stone bridge railing, and tilted its head to look at him.

« What did you see ? » Shuichi asked.

Lue let out a short, low chirp. Shuichi perceived the information transmitted by Lue ; while circling over the Clan Grounds earlier, Lue noticed that the Military Police Force patrols were more frequent than usual. It was not because of security issues. It was because some people in the Military Police Force were discussing the White Fang’s affair, and when the discussion became heated, their voices were too loud, so the captain made them go out on patrol to cool off.

Even the Uchiha Military Police Force was discussing the White Fang. This matter had already fermented in every corner of Konoha.

Shuichi continued walking across the stone bridge, and Lue flew up from the railing and landed on his shoulder. It was still early when he walked into the Uchiha Clan Grounds. The streets in the Clan Grounds were much quieter than the main street because the Uchiha had never really cared much about outsiders' affairs, so the White Fang’s uproar made less noise inside the Clan Grounds than outside. But Shuichi knew this was just on the surface. The storm that the Uchiha themselves would face in the future would be even greater than the White Fang’s.

When he returned to the courtyard, Minako was folding clothes on the veranda. She looked up at Shuichi, and the movement of her hands paused for a beat.

« You do not look well. Something happen at school ? »

« No. »

Minako did not press further. She folded the last piece of clothing, put it into the basket, stood up, and brushed her knees. « There is yokan in the kitchen. Rin had Obito bring it to you. It is matcha flavor. »

Shuichi paused. Obito said during lunch break that she would make matcha flavor next time, but it was delivered that same afternoon, so it must have been prepared in advance.

He walked to the kitchen and took out the yokan. A note was attached to the paper wrapping, the handwriting very neat : « Shuichi-kun, this time it is matcha flavor, I hope you like it. Rin. »

« Uh. » Shuichi looked at the note and was a bit speechless. If he did not know the original work, he would have thought Rin was interested in him. But the items were delivered by Obito, and he probably saw the note too, so there would not be any misunderstanding, right ?

However, Shuichi thought about it, and given Obito’s current intelligence, he should not overthink it.

In the end, Shuichi stood in the kitchen and ate a piece.

'Matcha flavor is indeed better than red bean.'
