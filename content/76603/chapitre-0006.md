# Chapitre 6 — A week later, the Mixed Forest was empty.

It wasn’t that the trees were gone, or the shrubs were gone, but that everything Shuichi could Plunder was gone.

The last tree lizard he could find had turned into a shriveled husk three days ago ; even the centipedes hiding deep in rock crevices and the woodlice hiding under rotting tree roots had not been spared.

Over the course of a week, he had systematically swept through the insects and small vertebrates in the Mixed Forest, precisely Plundering only their Vitality from every single animal, without taking any other abilities.

The cumulative effect was real but slow.

The combined Vitality of about forty insects, six tree lizards, and one small snake he had accidentally encountered had raised Shuichi’s basic physique from that of an [Average Five-Year-Old Child] to that of an [Eight-Year-Old Child with Good Physical Fitness].

His grip strength had increased by about a third, he no longer panted after taking two steps while running, and the sense of fragility deep within his bones had been replaced by a thin layer of solidity.

But this was about all the Mixed Forest could provide.

The total biomass of this forest was only so large, and its regeneration rate could not keep up with his Plundering speed.

If he waited another week or two, perhaps new tree lizards would migrate here from elsewhere, but Shuichi did not want to wait.

What he needed more now were medium-sized prey—rabbits, foxes, badgers, or any creature weighing over a pound.

For animals of that size, the Vitality of a single one would be equivalent to ten tree lizards.

The problem was that medium-sized prey would not appear in the Mixed Forest at the edge of the Uchiha Clan Grounds.

They needed larger habitats, such as the mountain forest area north of the Training Ground, or the dense forest upstream of the Naka River.

Lue circled down from above the Mixed Forest and landed steadily on Shuichi’s outstretched forearm.

In one week, its wingspan had nearly doubled since it first fell from the nest ; its grayish-brown down feathers were being replaced by dark flight feathers, and the contours of its wing edges were becoming increasingly sharp.

The Flight Instinct had fully integrated within its body.

The talent Plundered from the sparrow had found a far more suitable host in the eagle’s body than its original owner.

« Go take a look to the north. »

Shuichi raised his arm, pointing towards the direction further north of the Training Ground cliff.

Behind that cliff was a stretch of rolling low mountains, with vegetation much denser than the Mixed Forest.

« Fly higher. See if there are any rabbits, foxes, or anything larger. Don’t fly too far, and come back if you see any people. »

Lue tilted its head and looked at him, as if confirming every detail of the instruction.

Then it flapped its wings and took off, the force of its wings beating against the air already carrying the crispness only an adult hawk possessed.

Shuichi leaned against a zelkova tree, closed his eyes, and felt the state of the Climbing Ability within his body.

Over the past week, he had repeatedly used Climbing—climbing trees, climbing rock walls, and climbing the walls within the Uchiha Clan Grounds.

As of yesterday, the Climbing Ability had completely transformed from an [External Ability] into [His Own Thing].

It was like the final stage of digestion : food was no longer food, but a part of the body.

It no longer occupied space in his physique slots.

The position previously occupied by it had cleared up, merging with the space left behind after the Carapace Hardness had been stripped away.

Now, his physique slots had a capacity of about five to six units ; a week ago, it was four to five.

After Plundering the Vitality of all the creatures in the Mixed Forest, it had expanded slightly.

After the Climbing Ability was digested, about one unit of space was recovered.

This meant he could accommodate another medium-intensity physique-type ability without squeezing his existing abilities.

Shuichi opened his eyes.

In the sky above, Lue had already become a black dot, circling high above the Training Ground cliff.

Looking from the Mixed Forest at his feet, Lue’s position had already passed over the top of the cliff and was moving towards the further mountain forest area to the north.

About five minutes later, Lue returned.

It swooped down from the northern sky, wings tucked into a sharp arc, and just one second before it was about to hit the zelkova canopy, it suddenly spread its wings to decelerate, landing precisely on Shuichi’s shoulder.

In its talons, it clutched a small tuft of grayish-brown fur.

Shuichi took the tuft of fur from Lue’s talons and examined it twice.

Rabbit fur.

It wasn’t long, and the color was grayish ; it was a wild rabbit, not a domestic one.

That Lue could catch rabbit fur meant it had flown far enough to the mountain forest area, because wild rabbits would not be active in places like the Training Ground where people came and went.

« How far ? » Shuichi asked.

Lue, of course, could not speak.

But it flew up from Shuichi’s shoulder, flew a distance toward the north, and then turned back, repeating this three times.

Shuichi estimated Lue’s flight speed and time ; it was likely at the foot of the mountain about one kilometer to the north.

One kilometer.

For a five-year-old child, it wasn’t close, but it wasn’t an impossible distance to walk.

Leaving from the north side of the Uchiha Clan Grounds, bypassing the Training Ground, and crossing a small shrub slope would allow him to reach that mountain foot.

It would take about an hour to go there and back.

The prerequisite was not to be noticed by anyone.

Shuichi planned the route in his mind.

He had walked the path on the north side of the Uchiha Clan Grounds before.

That was the path he took last time when he went to the Training Ground with Minako.

To the west of the Training Ground, there was an inconspicuous fork leading to the shrub slope, which he had observed last time he was at the Training Ground.

Beyond the shrub slope, the terrain began to undulate, which should be the area where Lue found the wild rabbit.

« Tomorrow. » Shuichi stroked Lue’s wings. « We’ll make a trip tomorrow morning. »

Lue let out a short cry and shook its feathers.

On the way back, Shuichi detoured to the main road of the Uchiha Clan Grounds.

He did not take the usual path along the Naka River, but deliberately passed through the central area of the Uchiha Clan Grounds.

Since awakening his memories, he had spent most of his time traveling between the Mixed Forest and his own backyard, rarely observing the daily appearance of the Uchiha Clan.

The center of the Uchiha Clan Grounds was a small plaza paved with gravel, surrounded by a few old shops : a blacksmith shop selling shuriken and Kunai, an herbal medicine shop, and a teahouse with faded curtains.

A few Uchiha clansmen were scattered around the plaza, walking about with the fan crest embroidered on their collars.

Their expressions were not cold, but they certainly could not be called enthusiastic.

Shuichi noticed a detail : at the entrance of the blacksmith shop on the east side of the plaza, two middle-aged Ninja were whispering to each other.

Their voices were kept very low, but Shuichi’s hearing, having been slightly enhanced by Vitality, was sharper than an ordinary person’s, and he faintly caught a few words.

« ...It’s the third cut. The equipment budget for the Military Police Force has been reduced by another fifteen percent this quarter. »

« Did Fugaku go to see the Sandaime ? »

« He did. It was useless. They said that post-war reconstruction needs to prioritize the civilians. »

« Civilians. » The speaker scoffed. « The reconstruction funds allocated by the Daimyo of the Land of Fire were for the Village, not specifically for the civilians. You and I both know whose hands that money ended up in. »

The other remained silent for a few seconds, then said in a low voice, « Don’t talk about it here. Let’s talk when we get back. »

The two turned and walked into the alley behind the blacksmith shop.

Shuichi withdrew his gaze and continued walking, his stride unchanged.

But inside, he was already churning.

Budget cuts for the Military Police Force.

This wasn’t the first time ; the last time he accidentally overheard the conversation of those two clansmen by the Naka River, they had mentioned the budget being cut.

But last time it was just a general complaint, and this time it was a specific number : fifteen percent.

And from the conversation, it could be inferred that this was already the third cut.

The forty-first year of the Hokage.

Only one year after the end of the Second Great Ninja War, the budget for the Uchiha Military Police Force had been slashed three times in a row.

The Village leadership no longer needed to hide their attitude towards the Uchiha—at least, they didn’t need to hide it in budget meetings.

Dissatisfaction within the clan was accumulating.

This was a trend Shuichi had known about from the original work for a long time, but seeing the trend fermenting around him with his own eyes was a completely different experience from reading it in a comic book.

The Uchiha in the comics were symbols on paper, while the Uchiha here were the middle-aged Ninja whispering at the blacksmith shop entrance, the woman drinking tea with her head bowed in the teahouse, and the exhausted members of the Military Police Force in the plaza.

They were living people.

They would die on the night of the clan’s extermination.

Shuichi clenched his right hand, feeling a warm pulse in his palm.

When he reached his own gate, Minako was sitting on the porch, mending clothes.

Seeing him enter, she looked up, her gaze lingering on him for a few seconds : from his hair covered in leaf fragments to his mud-stained trouser cuffs, and then to the increasingly large fledgling eagle on his shoulder.

« Went to the forest again ? »

« Yeah. »

« Your hair is full of leaves. » Minako put down her needle and thread and beckoned him over. « Come here. »

Shuichi walked over and sat down beside her.

Minako used her fingers to comb the leaf fragments out of his hair, her movements gentle and practiced, as if she were handling a daily chore.

« Shuichi, » she said while combing, her voice very calm, « Mom isn’t trying to stop you from playing outside. But in less than a year, you’ll be attending the Ninja Academy. At school, teachers will teach you how to refine Chakra, how to use Ninja tools, and how to cooperate with teammates. It’s fine for you to run and jump in the forest now, but when you get to school... »

« I will study hard, » Shuichi interrupted, « I won’t embarrass Dad. »

Minako’s hand paused for a moment.

Then she continued combing, her tone carrying a hint of something that was hard to tell whether it was relief or heartache.

« The way you’ve been speaking lately is becoming less and less like a five-year-old. »

Shuichi did not respond to this.

He didn’t know what to say.

Minako did not press him further either.

She picked out the last leaf fragment from his hair and gently patted Shuichi’s shoulder.

« Lue has been flying outside long enough today. Feed it something. »

« Yeah. »

Shuichi stood up and walked into the backyard.

Lue flew up from his shoulder, landed on a branch of the plum tree, and looked down at him.

Shuichi took a dried small fish from the kitchen, tore it into strips, and placed them in Lue’s designated wooden tray.

Lue glided down from the plum tree, landed on the edge of the wooden tray, and began tearing at the fish meat.

Shuichi squatted to the side, watching it eat, his mind still turning over the conversation he had just heard in the plaza.

Fifteen percent.

The third cut.

Fugaku’s protest was ineffective.

The Military Police Force was the most important institutional presence of the Uchiha in the Village.

It was the privilege the Nidaime Tobirama once gave to the Uchiha, and it was also a restriction.

But if the budget continued to be cut, the operation of the Military Police Force would be affected.

Old equipment, insufficient personnel, declining execution efficiency—and then the Village leadership could rightfully say : The Uchiha’s Military Police Force is no longer capable, it must be reorganized.

This was a slowly tightening noose.

Shuichi saw it very clearly now.

He stood up and looked towards the north.

The sun was sinking to the west, and the direction of the Training Ground cliff was shrouded in golden light.

Tomorrow morning, he would pass through the shadow of that cliff and walk into the mountain forest area further north.

There were wild rabbits there.

There was larger prey.

There was the Vitality he needed.

And eighteen years of time was slipping away, minute by minute.
