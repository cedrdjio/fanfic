# Chapitre 82 — Early Graduation

The 47th year of the Hokage, spring.

The Third Ninja World War broke out.

It was not triggered by any single event. It was a chain reaction, like dominoes ; one fell, and all the others followed.

The Third Kazekage went missing. Sunagakure was left leaderless and plunged into internal chaos. Kumogakure seized the opportunity, raiding the borders of the Land of Wind and swallowing up three Sunagakure border outposts in one go. Sunagakure was forced to withdraw troops from the Land of Fire to defend itself, but Kumogakure did not stop, declaring war on both the Land of Fire and the Land of Earth simultaneously.

Iwagakure immediately pressed its massive army against the borders of the Land of Fire.

The Seven Ninja Swordsmen of the Hidden Mist closed in on the coastal waters.

Konoha was forced to fight on four fronts.

The day the news reached Konoha, Hiruzen Sarutobi stood before the window of his office, looking out at the streets of the Village.

The sun was bright, children were running around on the streets, merchants were shouting their wares ; everything looked the same as usual.

« Kumogakure is raiding from the direction of the Land of Lightning, » an Anbu reported. « Iwagakure is attacking from the Land of Earth, with a force exceeding three thousand. The Seven Ninja Swordsmen of the Hidden Mist have reached the coastal waters, waiting for an opportunity to land. Although Sunagakure has withdrawn some troops, their shadow still looms near Kikyo Pass. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi did not speak.

Fighting on four fronts. Konoha had never faced so many enemies at once.

« What is the troop situation ? »

« The front lines need at least two hundred Jonin to hold the position, » the Anbu said. « But our current number of Jonin... is insufficient. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi turned around and looked at the map of Ninja distribution on the wall. The red markers represented the front lines, and the blue markers represented those stationed within the Village. There were too many red markers and too few blue ones.

« What about Minato ? »

« Minato Namikaze is already on his way, » the Anbu said. « He is responsible for the front line in the direction of the Land of Lightning. »

Hiruzen nodded. Minato was Konoha’s strongest Jonin ; with him there, at least one front line could be stabilized.

But there were still three others.

« What about Orochimaru ? »

« Orochimaru is in the Village, » the Anbu said.

Hiruzen was silent for a few seconds.

Orochimaru. One of his three students.

And the one who gave him the most headaches. Jiraiya had gone to the Land of Rain, and Tsunade had left the Village long ago ; only Orochimaru remained.

But Orochimaru’s relationship with the Village had always been delicate, and he was too close to Danzo.

But now was not the time to worry about that.

« Appoint Orochimaru as the supreme commander of the front lines, » Hiruzen said.

The Anbu was stunned. « Hokage-sama ? »

« Fighting on four fronts, I need someone to sit at the front lines and coordinate the overall situation, » Hiruzen’s voice was deep. « Minato is a battle commander, but he is not good at commanding large-scale battles. Orochimaru is different ; he has that ability. »

He paused, as if convincing himself.

« Besides, Orochimaru needs to be tied to the Village’s side. Give him command authority. »

The Anbu did not ask again.

« Send out all the Chunin that can be mobilized, » Hiruzen said. « The front lines need people. »

« What about the guards in the Village ? »

« Leave the bare minimum, » Hiruzen’s voice was deep. « In this war, we have no way out. »

---

Minato Namikaze used the Flying Thunder God Technique to hold back Kumogakure’s AB Combination on the side of the Land of Lightning.

The AB Combination was Kumogakure’s strongest duo (A and B). The two worked with tacit understanding ; the Lightning Release Armor combined with the Eight-Tails' Chakra made for an almost invincible combination. Kumogakure had sent them to the very front line, intending to break through Konoha’s defense line in one go.

But Minato stood before them.

The Flying Thunder God Technique allowed Minato to teleport across the battlefield, and he had marked every key position. A’s Lightning Release Armor could not hit him, and B’s Eight-Tails tentacles could not catch him.

But he could only hold them back ; he could not defeat this combination. Both sides were deadlocked at the border, neither able to do anything to the other.

Kumogakure’s breakthrough was stopped.

Intelligence from the front lines was sent back to the intelligence departments of various countries : A Konoha Ninja with golden hair was holding back the AB Combination.

Various countries began to take note of this name—Minato Namikaze.

But Minato could not save all the front lines by himself.

Iwagakure’s attack was even more fierce than Kumogakure’s. An army of three thousand pressed against the border of the Land of Fire, and Konoha’s defense line was beaten back step by step. The Seven Ninja Swordsmen of the Hidden Mist were cruising the coastal waters, ready to land at any moment. Although Sunagakure’s main force had withdrawn, their troops near Kikyo Pass were still waiting for an opportunity.

Konoha’s manpower was severely overdrawn.

There were more wounded soldiers on crutches on the streets.

They had retreated from the front lines ; some had lost legs, some were blind, some were wrapped in bandages, and some walked with a limp. They sat on benches by the street, watching the pedestrians passing by, their eyes hollow.

The supply warehouse began to issue Ninja tools and military rations according to quotas.

Previously, Ninjas could take shuriken, Kunai, and explosive tags at will. Now, everyone could only receive a fixed amount, not a single one more. Military rations were even scarcer, available only to those on front-line missions.

The list of those killed in action on the front lines was updated every day.

The first day, twenty-three names.

The second day, thirty-seven names.

The third day, forty-one names.

The list was getting longer and longer.

---

Shuichi walked down the street, passing by the bulletin board.

He saw the announcement and read it from start to finish.

His expression did not change.

He had long known it would be like this.

War was like this. Some people would die, some would be injured, some would return, and some would remain on the battlefield forever. This was the rule of the Ninja World ; he had known it from the moment he awakened his memories of his previous life.

He continued walking forward, his steps steady, his expression calm.

When he passed a wounded soldier, the soldier took a look at him. There was a strange light in the soldier’s eyes—like envy, like jealousy, like resentment.

Shuichi did not pay attention.

He walked to the entrance of the Ninja Academy and saw a new notice posted on the door.

A formal notice from the Ninja Academy teachers : All students of this year would graduate early.

Shuichi stood before the notice for a while.

He was eleven years old this year. He was supposed to graduate next year, but now that the war had broken out and Konoha needed people, it had been moved up.

---

In the classroom, the teacher stood on the podium, looking at the students below.

« The war has broken out, » the teacher said. « Konoha needs more Ninjas. Starting today, you are no longer students ; you are reserve Ninjas. »

No one spoke in the classroom.

« The graduation exam will be held early, » the teacher continued. « Those who pass will be assigned to different units. Some will go to the front lines, and some will stay in the Village to guard. »

A student stood up, his voice trembling : « Teacher, are we... are we going to war ? »

« Yes, » the teacher’s voice was calm, but there was a heaviness in his eyes. « This is the duty of a Ninja. »

The student sat down, his face pale.

Shuichi sat in the corner, watching all of this.

He saw some students start to cry, some students' faces turn pale, and some students holding each other’s hands, as if comforting each other. He saw Tetsuya sitting in the front row, his shoulders trembling slightly. He saw Obito sitting on the other side, his eyes wide open, as if he didn’t know how to react.

After school, Shuichi walked on the way home.

Tetsuya walked beside him, his face still very pale.

« Shuichi, » Tetsuya said, his voice trembling slightly. « Are you scared ? »

Shuichi looked at him. « Scared of what ? »

« Going to war, » Tetsuya said. « We are going to war. We might die. »

Shuichi did not speak.

He thought of his previous life. In his previous life, he was just an ordinary person ; he had not experienced war, nor had he experienced life and death. But in this life, he had been preparing since he was five years old. He had killed people, he had seen blood, and he knew what death looked like.

« Not scared, » Shuichi said.

Tetsuya looked at him, as if wanting to say something, but in the end, he didn’t.

They walked to a fork in the road, and Tetsuya walked to the other side. Shuichi continued to walk forward, passing the commercial street, passing the bulletin board, passing those wounded soldiers on crutches.

He returned home and pushed open the door.

Minako was cooking in the kitchen ; hearing the sound of the door opening, she turned to look at him.

« Shuichi, » her voice was a bit nervous. « Have you heard ? »

« I heard, » Shuichi walked into the kitchen and stood beside her. « Early graduation. »

Minako’s hand stopped in mid-air, as if not knowing how to continue. She looked at Shuichi, and there was a worry in her eyes, a fear, a terror that she didn’t want to voice.

« Will you go to the front lines ? » she asked, her voice very soft.

Shuichi looked at her, looking for a long time.

« I don’t know, » he said. « It depends on the assignment. »

Minako lowered her head and continued cooking. But the movements of her hands were much slower, as if she had lost her strength.

Shuichi stood by, watching her back.

He knew what she was worried about. His father had died in the war. Now it was his turn.

But he would not die.

He knew his own strength better than anyone.

But he did not tell Minako these things.

Some things, saying them out loud would only make her more worried.

At night, Shuichi sat in his room, looking at the moon outside the window.

Haku lay at his feet, sleeping quietly. The raptor kept watch outside the window, its black feathers shimmering in the moonlight.

The pheromone web of the spider spread throughout the Village, covering every corner.

Shuichi closed his eyes, sensing the information transmitted back by the spider.

The atmosphere in the Village had changed. Before, it was calm ; now, it was full of tension and fear. People on the street walked faster, spoke in lower voices, and smiled less.

War had changed all of this.

But Shuichi knew, this was just the beginning.

The war would last a long time. Three years, or even longer. Many people would die, many families would be broken, and many things would disappear forever.

He opened his eyes, his pitch-black pupils shimmering slightly in the darkness.
