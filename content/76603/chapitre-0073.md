# Chapitre 73 — Preparation

Returning to the outskirts of Konoha Village, Shuichi did not go straight home.

He first circled the Village, using the perception of him to scan the surroundings, confirming that no one was following him, and only then did he enter the Village through the East Gate.

The streets in the Village were still bustling.

Vendors were calling out the wares of them, Ninjas were patrolling, and ordinary people were shopping. Children were running and playing in the streets, while the elderly sat at the doorways of them chatting.

Everything was normal, no different from usual.

But Shuichi knew that this peace was fragile.

He crossed the street and headed in the direction of the home of him.

The sun had already slanted to the west, and the sky was gradually darkening. The streetlights on both sides of the street began to light up, casting dim, yellow light onto the ground.

Shuichi pushed open the front door of him and walked into the courtyard.

The plum tree in the courtyard was still blooming, and a faint floral scent drifted in the air.

Lue was crouching on the windowsill ; seeing him return, it opened the eyes of it for a glance before closing them again. The feathers of it shimmered with a faint luster under the sunset.

Fang was still in the nest of it, organizing the information brought back by the Worker Bees ; the antennae of it were twitching, emitting a slight buzzing sound.

The gaze of Shuichi swept across the courtyard before landing inside the house.

Through the window, he saw Minako.

The mother was busy in the kitchen, an apron tied around the waist, a spatula in the hand, stir-frying something. The movements of her were skilled, and the face of her wore a calm expression.

The rays of the setting sun filtered through the window, casting light upon her and dyeing the profile of her a warm orange.

Shuichi stood in the courtyard, watching that figure.

He let out a sigh of relief.

The mother was still here ; everything was well.

Shuichi did not go into the house immediately.

He stood in the center of the courtyard, the gaze of him still fixed on the direction of the kitchen.

White Zetsu knew where the home of him was. If he left Konoha to meet Uchiha Madara, and Uchiha Madara decided to target him, the mother of him would have no one to protect her and would be in danger at any moment.

He could not let that happen.

Shuichi suppressed the thoughts in the heart of him and turned to walk toward the corner of the courtyard.

Lue flew down from the windowsill and landed on the shoulder of him.

The claws of it gently gripped the clothes of Shuichi, the body of it leaning slightly forward, the gaze of it looking at him calmly.

Fang poked the head of it out from the nest and looked over. The compound eyes of it shimmered in the sunset, and the antennae of it were still twitching.

Shuichi did not speak.

The hands of him formed seals.

Shadow Clone.

Chakra surged out, condensing into shape beside him.

It was a massive amount of Chakra, starting from the Dantian of him, passing through the meridians of him, and finally pouring out from the hands of him. The Chakra swirled, condensed, and took form in the air.

A figure exactly identical to him appeared.

The features, build, and clothing were exactly the same. The only difference was the eyes ; the eyes of the clone were ordinary Three-Tomoe Sharingan, without the patterns of the Mangekyo.

Shuichi injected nearly half of the Chakra in the body of him into this clone.

The recovery concept and the toughness concept were all doubled.

The body of the clone began to change. The Chakra levels of it surged, the physical strength of it increased, and the recovery ability of it was enhanced.

Although it could not use Mangekyo Sharingan abilities, it was a genuine Kage-level combatant.

The Chakra of this clone was enough to last for over a year ; even if it fought at full power, it could last for several days.

The number of people in Konoha who could reach Kage-level was counted on one hand. The Third Hokage, Danzo, and a few Anbu captains. These people were either old and frail or had other missions, so it was impossible for them to keep watching a normal housewife.

Using this clone to guard Minako was enough.

The clone opened the eyes of it.

The gaze of it was calm, just like the gaze of Shuichi, carrying a sense of coolness and rationality. It looked at the hands of it, feeling the surging Chakra within, and then looked up at Shuichi.

Shuichi looked at the clone and said, « Guard her well. »

The clone nodded.

It walked to the window, jumped out, and disappeared into the darkness outside the courtyard.

From now on, it was another guardian of this home.

Adding Xiaobai, the insect swarm of Fang, and Lue, Shuichi had left all the protection he could.

Lue looked at Shuichi, the gaze of it calm.

It did not ask what had happened, nor did it ask why he was doing this. It just stood quietly on the shoulder of Shuichi, waiting.

Shuichi was silent for a while before speaking.

« I am leaving tonight. You all stay here. »

« ... »

Lue did not ask why.

It just nodded once and then turned the gaze of it toward the window.

The courtyard was very quiet, with only the rustling of leaves as the wind blew through. The afterglow of the sunset spilled onto the plum tree, dyeing the petals a pale gold.

The sound of light stir-frying came from the kitchen ; the mother of him was still busy.

Shuichi stood in the center of the courtyard, the gaze of him calm.

The appearance of White Zetsu had disrupted the plans of him, but it had also given him an opportunity.

This meant he could get close to Uchiha Madara, understand the status of Uchiha Madara, and understand the plans of Uchiha Madara.

He could observe the strength of Uchiha Madara and assess the gap between the two sides.

More importantly, he could find an opportunity to Plunder the Wood Release of Uchiha Madara.

The risk was great, but the rewards were also great.

He needed more information.

Shuichi turned and walked into the house.

Lue was still standing on the shoulder of him, not speaking. The body of it leaned slightly forward, as if it were thinking about something.

Fang poked the head of it out from the nest, looked at him, and then shrank back. The antennae of it were still twitching, continuing to organize the information brought back by the Worker Bees.

Everything was very calm.

But Shuichi knew that this was just the calm before the storm.

Tonight, he was going to leave Konoha to meet that legendary man.

Uchiha Madara.

One of the founders of Konoha, one of the strongest men in the Ninja World.

Shuichi walked into the room and closed the door.

He sat down at the table and began to deduce various possible scenarios in the mind of him.

If Uchiha Madara attacked him directly, he could use Magatsuhi to turn intangible and escape. By turning truth into falsehood, he could make the body of him become something that did not exist. No matter how strong the Ninjutsu of Uchiha Madara was, it could not hit something that did not exist.

If Uchiha Madara threatened him with the mother of him, the clone would protect the mother of him and evacuate.

The clone had Kage-level strength, and with the help of Xiaobai, Lue, and Fang, it would be enough to take the mother of him away from Konoha and hide in a safe place.

If Uchiha Madara wanted to recruit him, he could pretend to cooperate, find an opportunity to get close to Uchiha Madara, and Plunder the Wood Release.

For every scenario, he needed to prepare a response plan.

Shuichi deduced repeatedly in the mind of him, taking every detail into account.

He needed to organize all this information clearly.

Night gradually fell, and the time was getting closer.

Shuichi sat at the table, the gaze of him calm.

Outside the window, moonlight spilled onto the plum tree, and the shadows of the trees danced. The courtyard was very quiet, with only the occasional chirping of insects.

Half a day was enough for him to make all the preparations of him.

He stood up, walked to the window, and looked at the courtyard outside.

Under the moonlight, the petals of the plum tree shimmered with a faint white light. Lue was crouching on the windowsill, looking calmly into the distance.

Everything was very quiet.

But Shuichi knew that the real challenge had only just begun.

He was about to face one of the strongest men in the history of the Ninja World.

That would be a true test.
