# Chapitre 33 — Observation

Time entered late autumn of the 44th year of the Hokage era.

Shuichi’s training in Fire Release Chakra control had been going on for nearly a month.

The first step of Fire Release is to imbue Chakra with the nature of fire, the second step is shape transformation, and only the third step is forming flames.

Shuichi had already reached the end of the second step.

After school, Shuichi walked home along the Naka River.

Lue did not come to pick him up.

Previously, every day after school, Lue would circle above the riverbank waiting for him, and after seeing him, would fly down and land on his shoulder. But today, Lue was not there. Shuichi looked up at the sky, but Lue’s shadow was not there.

Shuichi did not wait. He continued walking home along the Naka River. When he reached the stone bridge, Tetsuya ran up from behind.

« Shuichi ! » Tetsuya ran to his side, panting. « Where is that eagle of yours ? I could see it every day before, but how come I didn’t see it flying in the sky when I left school today ? »

« It’s not feeling well and is resting, » Shuichi said.

« Oh. » Tetsuya scratched his head. « I’m used to seeing it flying in the sky, so it feels a bit strange not seeing it today. »

Shuichi did not explain further. Tetsuya did not press him either. The two walked along the Naka River for a while before parting ways at the fork in the road.

Shuichi crossed the stone bridge and walked home along the path by the river.

Lue did not come to pick him up today because Lue was executing the mission Shuichi had assigned yesterday : tracking and observing Sakumo Hatake.

From his memories of his past life, Shuichi knew the fate of the White Fang. He would commit suicide in the 45th year of the Hokage era. However, Shuichi had not interfered in any way, so this outcome was highly unlikely to change. What he needed to do was anticipate the timing, arrive at the scene as soon as possible after Sakumo committed suicide, and use his Plunder ability to Plunder Sakumo’s combat experience.

With the White Fang of Konoha’s combat experience, combined with his own body that had been strengthened multiple times, he would probably be able to contend with a Jonin.

Sakumo’s strength should have reached the Kage level ; his strength was definitely not below that of the Sannin. His combat experience, tactical judgment, and combat intuition were more precious than any animal’s ability. What was plundered from animals were only physical abilities, but what would be plundered from Sakumo was Kage-level combat experience, and it was primarily focused on Taijutsu. If successful, Shuichi’s practical combat ability would jump directly from theoretical Chunin to a level approaching Jonin.

But the timing had to be precise. After Sakumo committed suicide, the time when the body would be discovered was uncertain. If someone else discovered it first, Shuichi would have no chance. Therefore, Shuichi had to come into contact with Sakumo’s body before anyone else.

So he needed to know when Sakumo would commit suicide.

Sakumo’s state was continuously deteriorating. Shuichi had seen him from afar on the commercial street last week : silver-white hair, a gaunt face, and shoulders slightly slumped while walking, like a person who had had their bones removed.

Looking at Sakumo’s state, Shuichi judged that the time was probably near.

So, starting yesterday, he had Lue track Sakumo. At the same time, he specifically emphasized that Lue could only observe from a distance using Infrared Sensing and could not get close. Sakumo was a Kage-level powerhouse ; if Lue got too close, it was very likely that Sakumo would notice an abnormal eagle tracking him. Lue’s Infrared Sensing range was sufficiently long ; staying at a high altitude was enough to monitor Sakumo’s heat signature.

When Shuichi reached his doorstep, Lue flew down from the sky and landed on his shoulder.

« How is it ? » Shuichi asked in a low voice.

« He is at home, » Lue’s voice was very low. « He sat for the whole afternoon. He didn’t go out. »

« What about this morning ? »

« He went to the Memorial Stone in the morning, » Lue said. « He stood there for a long time. Then he went home. »

Shuichi nodded. Sakumo went to the Memorial Stone every day, stood there for a long time, and then went home. This pattern had continued for at least a week. Shuichi deduced in his mind : Sakumo went to the Memorial Stone every day, and the time he spent there was getting longer and longer. If he were to commit suicide, the Memorial Stone was the most likely place.

« Continue tracking tomorrow, » Shuichi said. « Keep your distance. »

« Mm. » Lue nodded.

On Wednesday morning, when Shuichi left the house, Lue had already flown away.

It had woken up earlier than Shuichi and was waiting in the high altitude near Sakumo’s house. Sakumo usually went out to the Memorial Stone in the early morning, so Lue needed to be in position before Sakumo left.

Shuichi walked to school alone along the Naka River. The morning mist had not yet dissipated, and the water vapor on the river was wrapped in a cold aura.

When he reached the commercial street, Shuichi noticed Tetsuya standing in front of a Japanese confectionery shop on the roadside, looking up at the sky twice.

« Shuichi ! » Tetsuya saw him and ran over. « That eagle of yours didn’t come again ? »

« Mm, » Shuichi said.

Shuichi did not explain further.

Shuichi made a mental note. Tetsuya had noticed Lue’s absence. It wasn’t just Tetsuya ; others in the clan’s territory who might have noticed Lue would also be suspicious. Lue usually circled above the Naka River with high frequency ; if it suddenly stopped flying, people would notice. Shuichi needed to arrange a reasonable excuse for Lue’s absence, or let Lue resume normal flight patterns when not tracking Sakumo.

But now was a critical period. Sakumo’s state was deteriorating, and Shuichi could not let Lue leave its surveillance post for too long.

Kakashi Hatake was throwing Kunai at the Training Ground in the early morning.

He had woken up half an hour earlier than usual today. Because when he returned home last night, he saw his father standing in the courtyard, looking up at the night sky. His father had stood there for a long time, so long that Kakashi had waited in his room for half an hour, and when he came out to look, his father was still standing there.

Kakashi did not call out to his father because he could not help him right now. He just stood behind the paper door, watching his father’s back, and then returned to his room to sleep.

When he left the house this morning, his father was no longer in the courtyard. The wooden chair was empty, with a thin layer of dew on the seat. His father had probably gone to the Memorial Stone again.

Kakashi did not follow him. He went to the Training Ground.

After throwing the Kunai, Kakashi walked over to pick them up and stood back on the throwing line. He had thrown the twentieth round, and every single Kunai landed in the center of the target post. But his mind was no longer thinking about Kunai.

He was thinking about how his father looked standing in the courtyard last night, staring at the night sky.

That back was very quiet. Not a peaceful quiet, but an empty quiet. Like a house where the furniture was still there, but the person had already moved out.

Kakashi gripped the Kunai tightly and continued throwing.

He didn’t need to think too much. He just needed to continue practicing, continue executing missions, and continue following the rules. If he was strong enough, excellent enough, perhaps the villagers would look at his father differently. Perhaps they would think, the White Fang’s son is a qualified Ninja, so perhaps the White Fang’s choice was not completely wrong.

This was Kakashi’s current goal. Not to become Hokage, not to become stronger, but to prove that his father was not wrong.

The Kunai flew from his hand and landed in the center of the target post.

After school, Shuichi walked home along the Naka River. Lue appeared in the sky, flew down, and landed on his shoulder.

« How was today ? » Shuichi asked in a low voice.

« He went to the Memorial Stone in the morning, » Lue said. « He stood there for even longer. »

« How long ? »

« Two hours. »

Shuichi was silent for a moment. Previously, Sakumo would stand at the Memorial Stone for about one hour ; today, he stood for two. The time was extending.

« And then ? »

« He went home. He sat in the courtyard. Looking at the sky. »

« How long did he watch ? »

« Until dark. »

Shuichi walked upstream along the Naka River, with Lue perched on his shoulder. He was deducing the changes in Sakumo’s state in his mind : the gauntness was worsening, the time spent at the Memorial Stone was extending, and he did nothing after returning home but look at the sky. These behaviors were accelerating. Sakumo was approaching a certain critical point.

Shuichi did not know exactly which day it was. In his memories of his past life, he only knew it was the 45th year of the Hokage era, with no more precise timing. But it was already late autumn of the 44th year of the Hokage era, and it was still a few months away from the 45th year. The speed of Sakumo’s deterioration was faster than Shuichi had anticipated.

Perhaps he didn’t need to wait until the 45th year of the Hokage era. Perhaps it would be within these few weeks.

« Continue tomorrow, » Shuichi said. « If he goes out, follow him. If he is at the Memorial Stone, watch from a distance. If he goes home, come back and tell me. »

« Mm. » Lue nodded.

Shuichi crossed the stone bridge and walked home along the path by the river. Lue was perched on his shoulder, occasionally looking down at the road surface.

« Lue, » Shuichi said.

« Hm ? »

« If he collapses at the Memorial Stone, you immediately signal me from high altitude. »

« What signal ? »

« Circle three times at high altitude. I’ll know when I see it. »

Lue thought for a moment, then nodded. « Understood. »
