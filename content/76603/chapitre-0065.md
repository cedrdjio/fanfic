# Chapitre 65 — Plundering the Byakugan

One week later.

Shuichi returned home from school and pushed open the door.

Lue was already waiting on the windowsill, and upon seeing him enter, its wings fluttered slightly.

« I found them, » Lue said.

Shuichi walked to the window and sat down next to Lue. « Tell me. »

« Two Chunin from the Hyuga Branch Family, » Lue said. « They often act together. Every day after dark, they leave from the back gate of the clan grounds, pass through an alley without patrols, and go to a tavern to drink. They don’t return until the early hours of the morning. »

« What about their route ? »

« Fixed, » Lue said. « They take the same alley every time. »

Lue paused, the light in its compound eyes flickering slightly.

« I also found out some other things. »

Shuichi looked at Lue.

« Earlier, when I was scouting outside, I heard some rumors, » Lue said. « They say that when these two are out on missions, the targets die in very miserable states. »

« How miserable ? »

« Dismemberment, burning, eye-gouging, » Lue said. « It doesn’t look like it was caused by normal combat ; it looks more like… torture and slaughter. »

Shuichi’s brows furrowed slightly.

« Also, » Lue continued, « they sometimes threaten their employers. After completing a mission, they demand extra payment, and if it isn’t given, they threaten to expose the employer’s privacy or threaten the safety of the employer’s family. »

« Have you confirmed this ? »

« I secretly followed them on a mission once, » Lue said. « Three days ago, they took a mission to escort a merchant caravan. After the mission ended, the caravan leader didn’t want to pay the final installment, using the excuse that the goods were damaged. »

Lue’s wings fluttered gently.

« They tied up the caravan leader and, in front of his subordinates, cut off the leader’s fingers one by one. Then they made the leader’s family kneel on the side and watch. »

« And in the end ? »

« The leader paid the money, and even paid double, » Lue said. « When they left, not a single person in the caravan dared to speak. »

Lue’s compound eyes stared straight at Shuichi.

« There was another time they took a mission to wipe out bandits. After the mission was finished, I went to check the scene. The bandits' corpses… didn’t look like they were killed in battle. Some had their limbs cut off, some had their eyes gouged out, and some had their stomachs cut open. »

Lue’s voice was calm, but Shuichi could hear the chill within it.

« They seem to enjoy the process, » Lue said. « Torturing targets, threatening employers—they find it amusing. »

Shuichi was silent for a while.

These two were indeed not good people.

But did their crimes warrant death ?

Shuichi thought about it. Torturing targets, threatening employers, cutting off fingers, making family members watch from the side… these actions were enough to justify the death penalty.

But that was in his past life ; this world now was the world of Hokage, where things like human experimentation could be conducted in the shadows.

Moreover, Shuichi had blood on his own hands. The people he had killed in the Forest of Death were not necessarily more deserving of death than these two.

He had no right to stand on the moral high ground and judge anyone.

However, that wasn’t the point.

The point was Lue’s judgment.

Lue had heard the rumors and then went to verify them. It didn’t just believe the rumors ; it secretly followed them on their missions and saw with its own eyes how they tortured targets and threatened employers.

Then, it made its choice.

This judgment process was very reasonable.

This wasn’t indiscriminate killing, nor was it just picking two random people to make up the numbers. Lue had its own standards, its own verification methods, and its own reasons.

As a first independent judgment, this was good enough.

Shuichi stood up, walked to the desk, opened the drawer, and took out a mask.

« Since the targets are confirmed, let’s take action tonight. »

Late at night.

Shuichi stood in the shadows of that alley.

He had changed into black clothes, wore a mask on his face, and completely concealed his presence.

On both sides of the alley were old residential buildings, with faint light shining through the second-floor windows. The entrance of the alley was empty, with only a few fallen leaves being swirled around on the ground by the wind.

Shuichi leaned against the corner of the wall and closed his eyes.

He could hear the noise coming from the tavern in the distance, with people singing and arguing. Further away, Konoha’s night patrol team was patrolling, their footsteps orderly and rhythmic.

He waited.

Half an hour later, the noise gradually subsided.

Two sets of footsteps came from the other end of the alley, accompanied by muffled voices.

« ...That last drink is on you, » one voice said, sounding a bit drunk.

« Why ? » the other voice said, dissatisfied. « You were the one who treated last time. »

« That’s different. You started drinking first last time. »

« Fine, fine, I’ll treat you next time. »

« Next time ? You said that last time too. »

Two figures swayed their shoulders as they walked into the alley, still arguing over the drink money.

Shuichi opened his eyes, the Three Tomoe Sharingan spinning in the darkness.

He could see the Chakra flow of both men, could see their muscle state, and could see their posture as they walked—loose, swaying, and completely devoid of vigilance.

Both men had been drinking, so their reactions would be slower than usual.

Shuichi stepped silently out of the shadows of the wall corner, following five meters behind the two men.

The two were completely unaware, continuing to walk forward while discussing which tavern had better alcohol.

Shuichi quickened his pace.

Three meters.

The two still didn’t notice.

Two meters.

Shuichi could smell the alcohol on them.

One meter.

Shuichi reached out with both hands simultaneously, each hand pressing onto the back of one of their heads.

Both their bodies tensed instantly, instinctively wanting to struggle. But Shuichi’s palms clamped onto their heads like iron pincers, and his Chakra invaded their nervous systems through his palms.

Forced Plunder.

The ability of the Byakugan was pulled out from their two pairs of eyes, surging into his body along Shuichi’s palms. The sensation was very clear—it was like two cold, thin threads extending from their eyes, drilling into Shuichi’s palms, and spreading upward along his arms.

The two convulsed violently in his hands.

Their mouths opened, wanting to scream, but Shuichi’s Chakra had already sealed their vocal cords. They couldn’t make a sound, only weak whimpers.

The Plunder lasted for two seconds.

But two seconds were enough to completely extract the Byakugan’s ability.

By the time the Plunder was complete, their brains had already been turned into mush by the violently invasive mental energy. Their eyeballs were still in their sockets, but their pupils had lost focus, becoming empty and cloudy.

Even if the Yamanaka Clan wanted to read memories from this kind of brain, they wouldn’t be able to read anything.

Shuichi released his hands, and the two corpses slumped to the ground.

He looked down at them.

Two Chunin from the Hyuga Branch Family died in an ordinary alley. Their faces still retained expressions of fear and pain, but no one would ever know what they had experienced.

Shuichi drew his short blade.

He added a slash to each of their throats, the cuts clean and efficient, severing their windpipes and arteries. Then, he left several stab wounds on their chests and backs to create the appearance of a close-quarters struggle.

Judging by the wounds, it looked like the work of a sword-wielding expert who killed with a single strike.

There were no traces of Ninjutsu at the scene, and no residual Chakra. Although the Byakugan’s ability was gone, the eyeballs remained intact in their sockets, and the Hyuga would not be able to detect anything unusual.

Shuichi sheathed his short blade and wiped his hands on the corpses.

Then he stood up, turned around, and left the alley.

The two corpses were left where they were ; they would be discovered tomorrow morning.

By then, the Hyuga Clan would investigate, but they would absolutely never be able to trace it back to him.
