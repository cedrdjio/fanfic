# Chapitre 2 — Probing and Boundaries

The day after awakening his memories, Shuichi snuck out of the house again.

The morning mist had not yet dispersed, and faint rustling sounds were already echoing from the Mixed Forest on the east side of the Uchiha Clan Grounds.

Shuichi crouched under an old beech tree, with his experimental equipment laid out before him : three small bamboo-woven cages, each containing a grasshopper, a Tree Frog, and a rhinoceros beetle.

« One by one, » he muttered to himself.

The burning sensation in his right palm was clearer than yesterday.

After plundering the sparrow, this hunger had not subsided ; instead, like gunpowder with a lit fuse, it continued to smolder in his palm.

Shuichi opened the first cage, grabbed the grasshopper by its hind legs, and pulled it out.

The grasshopper struggled desperately, its barbed hind legs scraping white marks onto his fingertips.

Shuichi did not hesitate ; he focused his consciousness into the palm of his right hand.

His perception expanded like a tidal wave—everything inside the grasshopper appeared in his mind once again : Jumping Instinct, the wide-angle vision of its compound eyes, the hardness of its chitinous carapace, and its faint Vitality.

He chose the Jumping Instinct.

The suction in his right hand erupted with precision.

The grasshopper stiffened violently, its hind legs twitched twice, and then its entire body went limp.

It was dead.

In Shuichi’s mind, a complete Jumping Ability had appeared—not the vague fragments from yesterday, but a complete, ready-to-use leg power generation pattern and aerial posture control.

He flexed his ankles, and a strange impulse surged from his legs.

He tried jumping in place.

The height of his jump clearly exceeded the normal range for a five-year-old child, and his knees instinctively bent to cushion the landing ; the movement was more than a grade smoother than before.

His current jumping ability was roughly equivalent to that of a ten-year-old child ; it wasn’t astounding, but gaining such an improvement in an instant was already terrifying.

[Complete Jumping Instinct.] Shuichi noted it down in his mental notebook.

He opened the second cage.

The Tree Frog clung to the bamboo strips, its green skin glistening with moisture.

Shuichi reached out and touched the Tree Frog’s back.

His perception expanded again : the Tree Frog’s Climbing Ability, skin respiration ability, and a keen sensitivity to humidity changes.

He hesitated briefly : Climbing Ability was a physical trait, skin respiration was also a physical trait, while humidity sensitivity leaned towards a mental trait.

What he needed to figure out now was how many abilities of the same type he could hold simultaneously.

He chose the Climbing Ability.

The suction erupted.

The Tree Frog’s body trembled slightly, then went still.

A slightly itchy warmth surged from Shuichi’s fingertips and soles simultaneously—the skin at his fingertips and the bottom of his toes seemed to be undergoing some subtle change, developing countless tiny, invisible wrinkles, like miniature versions of Tree Frog toe pads.

He walked over to the beech tree and pressed his right hand against the rough bark.

His fingers easily found an attachment point.

Without needing to exert conscious effort, his palm stuck to the bark as if glued by invisible adhesive.

He tried climbing up two steps, his body pressed against the tree trunk with no tendency to slip at all.

Then he stopped.

A subtle sense of discomfort welled up from deep within his body.

It wasn’t pain, but rather... crowding.

It was like stuffing another item into a backpack that already had a few clothes in it ; although it could still fit, it was starting to feel tight.

Shuichi jumped down from the tree and furrowed his brow.

[Two physical abilities. Jumping and Climbing.] He felt the subtle tightness in his body, [I can still stuff more in, but not much more.]

He estimated that with his current constitution as a five-year-old, holding both Jumping and Climbing physical abilities was already causing his body to feel [pressure].

A third physical ability could probably be forced in, but a fourth would most likely exceed his body’s capacity limit.

This was the capacity limit.

The limit for physical abilities was determined by the strength of his constitution.

His current constitution was just that of a five-year-old child ; even if he was stronger than his peers, it was extremely limited.

What about mental abilities then ?

Shuichi closed his eyes, sensing another completely different [space] in his mind.

Flight Instinct—a conceptual ability plundered from a sparrow—was quietly occupying a corner.

It did not occupy a physical slot but existed in another [container] on the consciousness level.

This container was currently quite empty ; the Flight Instinct occupied only a small part of it.

His soul from his previous life as a college student, combined with five years of life experience in this world, meant the fusion of the two souls made his mental power significantly higher than that of an average adult.

The capacity limit for mental abilities should be much more generous than for physical ones.

[Physical abilities can hold about three to four. Mental ones should be more.] Shuichi noted silently, [But this number will expand with the Growth of my body and spirit.]

He opened the third cage and pulled out the rhinoceros beetle.

The rhinoceros beetle crawled clumsily in his palm, its small head horn pushing against the web between his thumb and index finger.

Shuichi focused his consciousness to perceive : the most prominent traits of the rhinoceros beetle were its Carapace Hardness and the strength of its horns.

Both of these were physical traits.

He still had one or two physical slots left.

But compared to Carapace Hardness, he wanted to test another hypothesis more.

Shuichi focused his consciousness on the rhinoceros beetle while simultaneously trying to [push] the Jumping Instinct from his body back into his right palm.

Then he felt it.

The Jumping Instinct, that complete leg power generation pattern and aerial posture control, could be [touched] within his consciousness, just like an object that could be picked up or put down at any time.

He could keep it inside his body, or he could push it to his right palm.

And a new sensation came from his right palm.

It wasn’t hunger.

It was acceptance.

Shuichi’s pupils dilated slightly.

He did not Plunder anything from the rhinoceros beetle.

Instead, he stripped the Jumping Instinct from his body and pushed it into the rhinoceros beetle’s body through his right palm.

The rhinoceros beetle emitted a faint hiss, and its entire body arched violently.

Its six legs twitched simultaneously, and the muscles beneath its carapace spasmed violently.

After about ten seconds, it quieted down and lay motionless in Shuichi’s palm.

Then it jumped.

A rhinoceros beetle, a beetle that should only be able to crawl clumsily, jumped from Shuichi’s palm, catapulting nearly half a meter, hitting the beech trunk, and falling into a pile of fallen leaves.

It flipped over and jumped again.

Its movements were stiff, not as nimble as the grasshopper’s, but it was undoubtedly jumping.

Shuichi looked down at his hands.

The Jumping Ability had disappeared from his body.

He could feel that the physical slot had become empty, and the feeling of tightness had also eased significantly.

« It can be stripped away, » he murmured, [Abilities plundered can be transferred to other organisms. Once stripped, the occupation is lifted, effectively freeing up a slot.]

A new world opened up before him.

Not all abilities needed to remain within himself.

He could Plunder an ability from one creature and then strip it to another.

This meant he could cultivate his own combat partners.

Flight.

This word appeared in his mind almost instinctively.

The sparrow’s Flight Instinct still remained in his mental slot.

If he could find a suitable flying creature, strip the Flight Instinct to it, and then use his right hand to Plunder reconnaissance-related abilities from various places and continuously stack them onto it...

A high-altitude reconnaissance flying pet.

Shuichi closed his eyes, took three deep breaths, and forced himself to calm down.

This idea was too tempting, but now was not the time.

He needed to first figure out every boundary of his ability.

Over the next hour, Shuichi conducted systematic tests.

Test 1 : Can abilities of the same type be stacked ?

He caught two more grasshoppers.

He plundered the Jumping Instinct from the first one.

This time, the feeling from his right hand was completely different from before.

The Jumping Instinct did not occupy a new slot but instead merged directly into the position where the original Jumping Instinct was located.

Two streams of the same origin merged within his body, like two brooks flowing into the same riverbed.

Shuichi jumped in place.

The height increased by about another third compared to before.

He plundered the Jumping Instinct again from the second grasshopper.

The feeling of the second stacking was a bit weaker than the first—the enhancement magnitude dropped from one-third to about one-fifth.

But it was still enhancing.

[Abilities of the same type can be stacked, but there is a diminishing effect.] Shuichi noted silently, [After stacking three times, the increase is no longer very obvious. Perhaps the effect of continued stacking will become smaller and smaller, eventually approaching a limit.]

But regardless, the significance of this discovery was enormous.

If the Jumping Instinct could be stacked, then other abilities could be too.

As long as he found enough creatures of the same type to Plunder repeatedly, he could stack strength far exceeding normal levels in a single ability.

Test 2 : The coexistence and capacity of different abilities.

He used the last beetle to Plunder Carapace Hardness—a physical ability. After Carapace Hardness merged into his body, the tension in that physical ability slot immediately increased by a notch.

[Three physical abilities. Jumping (already stripped), Climbing Ability, Carapace Hardness. Now only Climbing Ability and Carapace Hardness are in my body—two, it feels okay.] He moved his body a bit, [but if I add another one, it should start to become strenuous.]

He Plundered a small part of Compound Eye Vision from a dragonfly that happened to fly by—this time it wasn’t a complete Plunder, but only took about one-third. The dragonfly didn’t die and flew away crookedly. After Compound Eye Vision entered the consciousness level, he clearly felt it fall into the mental ability slot, adjacent to Flight Instinct, without interfering with each other.

[Physical abilities and mental abilities occupy different slot systems.] Shuichi concluded.

[This means that reasonably arranging the ratio of the two types of abilities is important.]

Test 3 : Stripping to a creature that already possesses abilities.

He caught back the beetle that had its Jumping ability stripped—it was still jumping around in the pile of fallen leaves—and then tried to strip the dragonfly’s Compound Eye Vision to it. The beetle’s body convulsed violently again, then went completely stiff and died.

Failure.

Shuichi frowned. He caught a new beetle and this time tried to strip Carapace Hardness to it. Same convulsions, same death.

[Ordinary small creatures can only bear one Plundered ability. Exceeding one will cause them to collapse.] He noted this point heavily in his heart, [To stack multiple abilities on the same target, the target’s constitution or mental power must be strong enough.]

This means that choosing the ability carrier is crucial. Insect-level creatures can only bear one ability. Small animals might bear two to three. Large animals or humans, although not tested yet, should theoretically be able to bear more.

A flying pet. What he needs is not just any bird, but a flying creature with outstanding constitution and spirit. The size cannot be too small, and it is best to have a certain degree of intelligence.

Birds of prey are the most ideal choice. But birds of prey are not easy to obtain, and even harder to tame. An alternative might be corvids, crows or magpies ; they need to be smart enough and adaptable, and there are quite a few near the Uchiha Clan Grounds.

Shuichi stood up and brushed the dirt and fallen leaves off his knees. The sun had risen to the top of his head, and his mother would probably be back from the market in less than an hour.

Before going back, he had one more thing to do.

He walked to the bank of the Naka River at the edge of the Mixed Forest. There was no one by the river today, only the river water flowing murmuringly under the stone steps.

Shuichi squatted down by the river, looking at his reflection in the water.

Black hair, black eyes, a round face with baby fat that hadn’t faded yet. But the things in those eyes were already different from yesterday.

He opened his right hand, his palm slightly hot in the dappled light reflected on the water surface. After two days of experiments, he had preliminary control over this power—not taming, but understanding. He knew what it could do and where its boundaries were.

One Plunder per type. Can be stripped. Same type can be stacked. Capacity is determined by constitution and spirit.

These four rules combined to build a system far more complex than simple [Plunder]. He didn’t need to turn himself into a monster piled with abilities. He could use his right hand to Plunder abilities and then distribute them reasonably—keeping the core combat abilities for himself and stripping auxiliary abilities to loyal pets. Establishing an ability ecological network centered on him.

And the first step is to find a flying creature worth cultivating.

Shuichi stood up, took one last look at the surface of the Naka River, and then turned to walk towards the Clan Grounds.

When he walked out of the Mixed Forest, he heard a sound.

It wasn’t the low conversation of the two adult clansmen from yesterday. It was a boy’s voice, clear and powerful, coming from the direction of the upper reaches of the Naka River.

« Fire Release : Dragon Fire Technique ! »

Shuichi’s footsteps paused. He instinctively squatted down and approached the direction of the sound under the cover of the bushes. The Climbing Ability Plundered from the Tree Frog allowed him to climb up a crooked tree silently, looking towards the river surface through the gaps in the branches and leaves.

On the stone bridge in the upper reaches of the Naka River, a boy was practicing Fire Release Ninjutsu. Orange-red flames spewed from the boy’s mouth, pulling a straight line of fire above the river, steaming up a patch of white water mist. The boy was about fourteen years old, with golden hair shining with dazzling luster in the sunlight, and a serious yet gentle expression on his face.

Minato Namikaze.

Shuichi’s breathing stopped for a moment. The future Fourth Hokage, the user of the Flying Thunder God Technique, Uzumaki Naruto’s father. Now just a fourteen-year-old boy, practicing Fire Release at the Naka River by the Uchiha Clan Grounds.

No. Minato is not from the Uchiha Clan. What is he doing near the Uchiha Clan Grounds ?

Shuichi’s gaze shifted to another person beside Minato. A red-haired girl was sitting on the stone bridge railing, her legs dangling outside the bridge, chewing on a stalk of grass. She was wearing light green casual clothes, and her long red hair was fluttering slightly in the wind.

« Enough, enough, you’ve practiced twenty times already, Minato. » The red-haired girl spat out the grass stalk, « Fire Release isn’t your main area of focus anyway, that’s enough. »

« Five more times, Kushina. » Minato smiled, « The Uchiha senior said my Fire Release shape transformation is not stable enough. »

« Which Uchiha senior ? Big Brother Fugaku ? »

« Hmm. »

Shuichi squatted on the branch without moving, his pupils slightly constricted.

Minato Namikaze is learning Fire Release shape transformation from Uchiha Fugaku. This is a relationship not described in detail in the original work. There is some kind of friendly technical exchange between Minato and the Uchiha Clan—at least at this point in time, Fugaku is willing to guide Minato.

This is important. Very important.

Shuichi silently noted this information in his heart : The relationship between Minato and the Uchiha is positive, at least in the 41st year of the Hokage era. The rift between the Village and the Uchiha has not yet widened to an irreversible point. But the Nine-Tailed incident ten years later—that was the turning point that destroyed all trust.

He had ten years.

Shuichi silently retreated from the tree and walked away quickly along the path at the edge of the Mixed Forest.

When he returned home, Minako was already back, chopping vegetables in the kitchen. Seeing Shuichi enter, she poked her head out and smiled.

« You’re back ? Wash your hands, get ready to eat. »

« Hmm. »

Shuichi took off his clogs at the entrance and walked to the basin to wash his hands. A faint pulsation still came from his right palm in the water.

A flying pet. In the mental ability slot within his body, Flight Instinct was still quietly waiting for a host. In the physical ability slot, Climbing Ability and Carapace Hardness each occupied a seat, leaving one or two empty slots. These empty slots were precious resources ; before finding a flying pet, he should not fill them randomly.

Because once he finds a suitable flying pet, he needs to Plunder abilities from multiple places at the same time and then strip them to the pet. If the slots are all full, he needs to strip some away first to make space, which means waste.

Shuichi wiped his hands dry and sat down at the dining table. Minako served a bowl of hot miso soup and several rice balls.

« Mom. » Shuichi picked up a rice ball and asked, pretending to be casual, [Is it common for Ninjas to go practice in the upper reaches of the Naka River ?]

Minako blinked, seemingly surprised that her five-year-old son would ask such a question. But she still answered seriously : « There is a Training Ground there, and many Ninjas go there. Your father used to go there often too. »

« Do people from other clans go there too ? »

« Yes. Although the Uchiha Clan Grounds are on the edge of the Village, the Training Ground is open to all Konoha Ninja. » Minako smiled, « Why do you ask about this suddenly ? »

« Curiosity. » Shuichi took a bite of the rice ball.

Minako looked at him, her eyes filled with a trace of relief—since her husband passed away, this was the first time the child had shown interest in Ninja-related matters.

« You will be six years old next spring, and you can enroll in the Ninja Academy. » Minako said softly, « By then, you can also become an excellent Ninja like your father. »

« Hmm. » Shuichi nodded.

But what he was thinking about in his heart was not the Ninja Academy. He was thinking about the mountain forest in the upper reaches of the Naka River, near the Training Ground, where the trees were denser and there were more wild animals. There, the probability of finding a suitable bird of prey chick was far greater than in the Mixed Forest next to the Clan Grounds.

He needed a reason to leave the Village. Or someone who could take him out.
