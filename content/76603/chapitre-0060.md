# Chapitre 60 — Daily Life

The next day.

Shuichi returned to his daily routine at the Ninja Academy.

He had breakfast with Minako in the morning, then grabbed his schoolbag and headed out.

Passing by the dango shop at the street corner, Tetsuya was already waiting there.

« Shuichi ! » Tetsuya waved his hand and jogged over. « Why are you so slow today ? »

« I woke up late. » Shuichi slowed down to wait for him.

« You wake up late too ? » Tetsuya tilted his head. « Aren’t you usually the one waiting for me ? »

« Occasionally, yes. »

The two walked along the street towards the school, when Obito came running out from another alley, catching up breathlessly.

« Wait up ! » Obito held his knees, bent over, and panted. « I... I woke up late too... »

Tetsuya patted Obito on the back. « You wake up late every day. »

« No I don’t ! » Obito straightened his back and waved his hand indignantly. « Yesterday my alarm clock was broken, and the day before yesterday was... »

« What was it the day before yesterday ? » Tetsuya tilted his head, waiting for Obito’s answer.

Obito scratched the back of his head, his eyes drifting elsewhere.

« The day before yesterday... the day before yesterday the alarm didn’t go off. »

« That’s the same thing as being broken. » Tetsuya rolled his eyes.

« It’s not the same ! » Obito got anxious. « How can not ringing and being broken be the same ! »

Shuichi watched the two bicker, the corners of his mouth curling up slightly.

On the surface, everything was the same as before.

Tetsuya’s complaining, Obito’s lateness, the familiar shops on the street, the bulletin board at the school entrance.

Students walked into the school gate in twos and threes ; some greeted each other, while others ran past in a hurry.

Lunch break.

Shuichi sat on a bench outside the classroom, holding a rice ball in his hand and eating it slowly.

Tetsuya and Obito were chasing each other on the playground, laughter echoing from time to time.

Other students gathered in small groups, some chatting, some eating bento.

Shuichi took a bite of the rice ball, his gaze landing on the distant wall.

After awakening the Mangekyo Sharingan, he could feel the changes in his eyes.

Daily activation and occasional transformations didn’t consume much.

When he plundered the Uzumaki Bloodline, he had incidentally plundered some recovery concepts and attached them to his eyes, which could slowly recover light wear and tear.

But Shuichi knew this was only temporary.

Once he entered high-intensity combat in the future and used Magatsuhi, Musubi, or even Susanoo continuously, the rate of wear would far exceed the rate of recovery.

The result would be blindness.

In the original story, only Hashirama cells could create a special synergy with the Sharingan and truly repair the wear and tear of the Mangekyo.

Obito could use Kamui recklessly precisely because he had Hashirama cells in his body.

Shuichi needed Wood Release.

He had considered a few approaches.

The first approach : Find Madara directly.

In the plot of his past life, Madara obtained Hashirama cells after the Battle of the Valley of the End and awakened the Rinnegan in his later years.

But Shuichi thought of a key issue ; in his past life’s memories, Nagato’s Rinnegan was given to him by Madara.

If that was the case, then Madara might have already transplanted the Rinnegan out, leaving only an elderly body relying on the Gedo Statue to sustain his life.

Moreover, even a half-dead Madara had combat experience and Hashirama cells that Shuichi couldn’t easily handle right now.

Too unstable.

The second approach : Edo Tensei.

If he could learn Edo Tensei and summon Senju Hashirama, could he use his right hand to Plunder the Wood Release ability from this Edo Tensei body ?

Shuichi wasn’t sure.

An Edo Tensei body was essentially a product of souls and sacrifices ; whether the Plunder ability could work on such a thing would only be known after trying.

There were two ways to learn Edo Tensei : Orochimaru, or the Scroll of Seals.

Orochimaru couldn’t be touched right now.

It wasn’t because he couldn’t beat him ; with Shuichi’s current strength, defeating Orochimaru was more than enough.

But Orochimaru would become the Konoha commander-in-chief during the Third Shinobi World War, and his position would directly affect the direction of the war.

Touching him now would be like blowing up the timeline himself, and Shuichi wouldn’t be able to predict what would happen afterward.

So he had to go with the Scroll of Seals.

But the Scroll of Seals was in the Hokage Building, under tight guard.

Shuichi needed intelligence.

He realized that his biggest blind spot right now wasn’t strength, but information.

He currently had Kage-level strength, but he didn’t know what was happening in the village.

What areas Anbu were patrolling, and whether people from Root were getting close to the Uchiha Clan Compound.

He needed an intelligence network covering Konoha.

Only with intelligence could he protect his mother.

Shuichi finished the last bite of the rice ball and patted the crumbs off his hands.

The first thing he thought of was insects.

They were everywhere.

No one would notice a spider crawling on a wall, and no one would set up defenses against a wasp resting on a windowsill.

And the clan that understood insects best in Konoha was the Aburame clan.

They fed Kikaichu with Chakra, and the host could perceive the information collected by the insects.

Shuichi decided to find a time to visit the Aburame clan to understand the operational mechanism of the insect swarms.

« Shuichi ! »

Obito’s voice interrupted his thoughts.

Shuichi looked up and saw Obito and Tetsuya running over.

« What are you spacing out for ? » Tetsuya sat down next to Shuichi. « Finished your rice ball ? »

« Yeah. »

« My mom made me fried shrimp today. » Tetsuya proudly held up his bento box. « It’s super delicious ! »

« Your mom makes you delicious food every day. » Obito pouted. « My grandma only knows how to make rice balls. »

« Then you can come to my house and eat ! » Tetsuya patted his chest. « My mom would definitely welcome you ! »

Shuichi looked at the two and smiled.

« Obito, your rice ball is cold. »

« Ah ! » Obito hurriedly opened the rice ball and started eating it in big bites.

Shuichi leaned back on the bench, watching the students running on the playground and the white clouds drifting in the blue sky.

The calm on the surface couldn’t hide the undercurrents beneath.

---

After school.

« Shuichi, let’s go eat ramen together ! » Obito came running over, pulling Tetsuya along. « My treat ! »

« Where did you get the money ? » Tetsuya tilted his head.

« Saved it ! » Obito patted his pocket. « Today is allowance day ! »

Shuichi thought for a moment and nodded : « Okay. »

The three arrived at the ramen shop on the commercial street. Obito ordered a large bowl of miso ramen, Tetsuya ordered a medium bowl of soy sauce ramen, and Shuichi asked for a small bowl of salt-flavored ramen.

« Shuichi, why do you always order a small bowl ? » Obito slurped his noodles. « You should eat more while you’re growing ! »

« It’s enough. » Shuichi ate leisurely.

Tetsuya nodded : « Obito is right, you are indeed too thin. »

« This is a standard physique. » Shuichi said.

« No way ! » Obito swallowed the noodles in his mouth. « Look at me, I’m the one with the standard physique ! »

Tetsuya and Shuichi exchanged a look and simultaneously looked at Obito’s round belly.

« ...That’s because the rice balls my grandma makes are too delicious. » Obito covered his belly and muttered softly.

The three finished their ramen, Obito paid the bill, and patted his belly with satisfaction.

« I’m full ! » Obito stretched. « See you tomorrow ! »

« See you tomorrow. » Tetsuya waved his hand and walked down another path.

Obito also hopped and skipped away.

Shuichi walked home alone, his gaze sweeping over the shops on both sides as he passed the commercial street.

Ninja tool shop, pharmacy, bookstore, clothing store...

Lue crouched on his shoulder, tilting its head to look at a shop on the street.

Shuichi followed its gaze and saw it was a pet shop.

In the cage at the shop entrance, a few puppies were huddled together playing, barking.

He didn’t stop his steps and continued walking home.
