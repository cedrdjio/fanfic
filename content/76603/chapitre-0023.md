# Chapitre 23 — Respective Training

It started raining lightly on Tuesday morning.

Shuichi tied his Kunai on the veranda and looked at the plum tree in the courtyard, dampened by the rain. A bird was huddled high on the plum branches, wings tucked in tight, its black, glossy pupils reflecting the grey, overcast sky. The rain wasn’t heavy, but it was dense, creating a soft, rustling sound as it hit the tiled eaves.

Minako poked her head out from the kitchen. « The umbrella is by the door. »

« Mm. »

Shuichi picked up the old oil-paper umbrella, opened it, and walked out of the courtyard. The Uchiha Clan Grounds were quieter than usual in the rain, and the puddles on the stone path were covered in dense ripples from the raindrops. The sound of the Naka River was a bit louder than usual.

When he arrived at the classroom, Tetsuya had already arrived. He was sitting in the third row by the aisle, his pant legs wet up to his knees, and water droplets still hanging in his hair. Seeing Shuichi enter, he immediately dug a paper parcel out of his school bag and handed it over.

« Taro yokan made by my mom ! She told me to bring it to you ! »

Shuichi took the paper parcel and opened it to take a look. The squares were cut unevenly, with clear hesitation marks from the knife on the edges. Tetsuya’s mother probably didn’t make this often. He picked up a piece and took a bite ; the sweetness was faint, but the texture of the taro was quite solid.

« Not bad. »

Tetsuya grinned. « I told my mom you were teaching me Taijutsu, and she said she wanted to make something to thank you. »

« Did you mention that Obito is learning too ? »

« I did. My mom said she’d make an extra box next time. »

Shuichi folded the paper parcel and put it into his drawer. Tetsuya hadn’t just mentioned himself to his mother. This detail made a note in the back of Shuichi’s mind.

The first period was Basic Ninjutsu Theory. Teacher Mitomon hung a meridian chart on the podium and began to lecture on the basic concepts of Chakra nature transformation—the five major nature transformations : Fire, Wind, Lightning, Earth, and Water. Shuichi opened the Chakra textbook to Chapter 3, a chapter he had already finished reading last week.

« Everyone’s Chakra is naturally inclined towards a certain nature. » Teacher Mitomon wrote five large characters on the blackboard. « The Uchiha Clan is famous for Fire Release. »

A few faint snorts sounded from the back of the classroom. The matter of the Military Police Force was gossip in the mouths of adults, but it became a stereotype in the mouths of children. Shuichi didn’t turn around. Tetsuya did look back, but he clearly didn’t understand what those boys were snorting about.

« But this doesn’t mean that other natures aren’t important. » Teacher Mitomon continued, his voice drowning out the faint noises from the back row. « The Chakra nature test next semester will let every student understand their primary nature. Before that, basic meridian control and the stability of Chakra flow are the keys. »

Chakra nature test. Shuichi drew a circle on the desk with his right index finger. What was his own nature ? He didn’t know. He didn’t know his father’s nature, and his mother wasn’t a Ninja. The Uchiha Clan’s high aptitude for Fire Release was determined by bloodline, but he also possessed the Vitality he had plundered. He wasn’t sure if those life energies from different creatures would affect his Chakra nature inclination.

If there was a nature test next semester, he needed to test it privately in advance. He couldn’t buy the test paper issued by the school, but Ninja tool shops sold it, though it would be too conspicuous for a six-year-old child to go buy Chakra test paper alone. He had to find a way to deal with this.

The rain stopped during the lunch break. The sandy ground of the playground had been soaked by the rain into a dark brown, and stepping on it would leave a shallow footprint. Tetsuya pulled Shuichi to their usual spot in the corner of the playground to eat their bento.

« Shuichi, are you afraid of the field practice class ? »

« Afraid of what ? »

« The upper reaches of the Naka River ! There are wild beasts over there ! » Tetsuya’s voice was a mix of excitement and nervousness. « My dad said there are wild boars in the upper Naka River, and someone even saw bear tracks last year. »

Shuichi took a bite of his plum rice ball. He had just killed a wild boar last week. He hadn’t seen a bear, though. The bears in the upper Naka River were likely Asian black bears, larger than wild boars but not as big as brown bears. According to his hunting classification, they were likely Level 6 prey. With his current strength, Level 6 prey was not yet suitable for a direct confrontation.

« The field practice class follows the teacher, so we won’t go deep inside. » Shuichi said. « I’ve been to the outer edge of the Training Ground, there’s nothing big there. »

Tetsuya’s eyes widened. « You’ve been there ? »

« Just nearby. Taking a walk. »

« A walk ! Walking all the way to the upper Naka River ! Your family’s walking range is way too big ! »

Shuichi didn’t explain. Tetsuya muttered a few more times, « I want to go for that kind of walk too, » and then began counting on his fingers how many days were left until the field practice class. Next Friday, ten days away.

On the other side of the playground, Hyuga Shimizu was still sitting under that cedar tree. Today, his position was further out than yesterday, leaving a fist’s distance from the tree trunk.

Shuichi noticed this change. Someone accustomed to having support behind their back wouldn’t voluntarily move forward by that fist’s distance, unless he was deliberately training his sense of spatial security.

Hyuga Shimizu was also preparing for the field practice class.

Shuichi withdrew his gaze. What was the Hyuga Clan branch family child hiding ? Shuichi hid things because his right hand’s Plunder couldn’t be exposed ; Hyuga Shimizu hid things perhaps because a branch family child performing too well in public was inherently risky. The Caged Bird Seal didn’t just lock away the free use of the Byakugan, it also locked the upper limit of a branch family member’s performance in front of the main family.

Two people who were both hiding things had noticed each other in the same classroom. But neither pointed it out.

For the afternoon Taijutsu class, Teacher Mitomon arranged a new project—the balance beam.

There was a row of newly set up wooden posts by the side of Training Ground 3. The horizontal beam was about a foot and a half off the ground, a cedar log as thick as a bowl, and about ten steps long. Teacher Mitomon had the students line up and walk across the horizontal beam in turn. The rules were simple : walk from one end to the other without letting your feet touch the ground. If you fell, you went to the back of the line and started over.

« This is not a walk. » Teacher Mitomon stood next to the horizontal beam. « On tree branches, rooftops, and rock walls, Ninja battles don’t always happen on flat ground. The smaller the surface area you can step on, the higher the requirement for center of gravity control. This horizontal beam is the first step. »

Genji went up first. When the biggest boy in the class took the first step, the horizontal beam shook slightly, and he immediately spread his arms, balancing as if he were about to fly. When he reached the fifth step, his left foot slipped off the side, and he fell off the horizontal beam.

Teacher Mitomon made him get back in line.

When Tetsuya went up, Shuichi paid special attention to his center of gravity. Tetsuya’s soles stepped on the horizontal beam, with too much weight on his heels. He started wobbling after three steps, and missed his footing on the fourth. He climbed up from the ground, patted the sand off his pants, and made a face at Shuichi when he lined up at the back.

It was Hyuga Shimizu’s turn. Shuichi watched him step onto the horizontal beam. His footsteps were very light, with a level of control that an ordinary six-year-old child shouldn’t have. Every step he took was right in the center of the horizontal beam, and the contact surface between his sole and the beam was controlled with great precision. He walked seven steps ; when the beam wobbled, his knees bent slightly, with a very small range of motion, and he absorbed the force of the wobble into his calf muscles. He finished the ten steps without falling once.

Teacher Mitomon wrote something on the record board and then looked up at Hyuga Shimizu. The expression on Hyuga Shimizu’s face was calm as he walked back to the line, but Shuichi saw the corners of his mouth tighten slightly.

Shuichi was in the back. When he stepped onto the horizontal beam, he set a standard in his heart : complete the course without falling, but don’t strive for perfection. Being a bit steadier than Hyuga Shimizu was acceptable.

He took the first step. The surface of the horizontal beam was slightly damp from the moisture, and the friction was a bit higher than usual. He placed his center of gravity on the front part of his soles and, as usual, tested the wobble of the horizontal beam with his toes before stepping down with each step. When he reached the fifth step, the beam wobbled ; he let his body sway with it, then stabilized himself. He wobbled again on the ninth step. He finished the ten steps without falling.

Teacher Mitomon nodded. « Not bad. Excellent control of the center of gravity. »

When Shuichi walked back to the line, Hyuga Shimizu glanced at him. It wasn’t the passing gaze from before ; this time he looked at Shuichi for a full second before turning away. Shuichi made a note in his mind that Hyuga Shimizu was evaluating him. Just as he was evaluating Hyuga Shimizu.

After class, Teacher Mitomon read out the balance beam scores. Hyuga Shimizu was the first to complete the course and ranked first. Shuichi was second. Oshima was third. Genji fell twice and ranked seventh. Tetsuya fell three times and ranked twelfth.

« The top ten students can practice on their own after class for the next week. » Teacher Mitomon closed the notebook. « During the field practice class, you will discover the importance of a sense of balance. »

After school, Tetsuya was waiting for Shuichi at the school gate, with Obito standing beside him.

Obito wasn’t sleeping today ; he was standing by the stone pier at the school gate, holding the standard posture he had learned the day before yesterday, with weight evenly distributed on his soles and his knees slightly bent. Seeing Shuichi come out, the smile that blossomed on his face squashed half of his face.

« Big Brother Shuichi ! »

Shuichi looked at Obito. The scratch on his left cheek had scabbed over, turning into a small, pale red dot. The missing piece of fabric on the front of his shirt had been sewn back on, though the stitches were a bit crooked ; it was probably sewn by his grandmother.

« Why are you here ? »

« You said to call you directly next time. » Obito said. « So I just came directly. »

Shuichi was silent for a beat. He had indeed said that. He just hadn’t expected Obito to act on it the very next day.

« Then let’s go. » Shuichi said.

Tetsuya caught up from behind. « Obito, are you coming too ? What is Shuichi teaching today ? Yesterday was the forward step ; I practiced twenty rounds in the blacksmith shop’s backyard this morning, and my legs are sore. »

« Did you practice the standing posture ? »

« I did ! I stood for a quarter of an hour last night, and another quarter of an hour this morning ! »

Shuichi glanced at Tetsuya. There was indeed a faint bruise on his lower leg ; it wasn’t an injury, but an indentation left by muscles being tensed for a long time during the standing posture. Tetsuya was practicing Taijutsu in the style of a blacksmith : repeated hammering, using quantity to build quality.

When they returned to the Uchiha Clan Grounds, the light rain started falling again. Shuichi led the two of them into his courtyard. Minako glanced over from the kitchen as the three children took off their shoes on the veranda. She said nothing and continued chopping vegetables. She recognized Tetsuya, as he had visited before. This was her first time seeing Obito.

« Today we are learning the reverse of the forward step : the backward step. » Shuichi stood in the middle of the courtyard, where rainwater dripped from the eaves, creating a row of small pits in the sandy soil. « The forward step is for attacking, while the backward step is for retreating. But retreating is not running away ; retreating is for the sake of the next attack. »

He demonstrated it once. He pushed off with his right foot, stepped back with his left, and shifted his center of gravity smoothly backward. When landing, the ball of his foot touched the ground first, transitioning to the full foot. The speed was faster than the demonstration in Teacher Mitomon’s class, but the breakdown of the movements was very clear.

Tetsuya practiced first. His backward step was even worse than his forward step ; when retreating, his body instinctively leaned backward, and he nearly sat on the ground. Shuichi steadied his back, telling him to keep his center of gravity on his front foot.

« When you retreat, let your center of gravity follow the movement ; don’t shift your center of gravity before moving your foot. »

Tetsuya tried four times. On the fourth try, he finally got it right ; his center of gravity and his footsteps moved in sync, and his body didn’t lean backward. He was so excited that he jumped on the spot.

Shuichi watched Obito with more focus than he had with Tetsuya. Obito’s backward step was better than Tetsuya’s on the first try, and his body’s instinctive reaction was more correct. When retreating, his knees automatically bent a little deeper than usual, absorbing the backward momentum into his thigh muscles. This movement wasn’t taught by Shuichi ; it was a judgment made by Obito’s own body.

Shuichi recalled the conclusion he had reached the day before yesterday : Tetsuya relied on repetitive quantity to build progress, while Obito relied on bodily perception. The direction of their talents was completely different. This meant their teaching methods had to be different as well. Tetsuya needed precise corrections—telling him what was wrong and how to fix it, then he would use his strength to practice. Obito needed a vague direction ; show him once, let him try it himself, and his body would find the optimal solution on its own.

« Obito, you don’t need to practice too much, » Shuichi said. « Ten rounds a day at most. Too much will only make you confused. »

Obito was stunned. « Ten rounds is enough ? »

« Enough. »

« But I practiced the forward step for forty rounds yesterday. »

« That’s too much. » Shuichi interrupted him. « Your body learns things by feeling, not by quantity. »

Obito blinked and then nodded. He walked to the corner of the courtyard and began practicing the backward step quietly by himself, one round at a time, pausing for a few seconds between each round as if savoring the movement he had just performed.

Tetsuya was still practicing repeatedly in the middle of the courtyard. He had moved from the backward step back to the forward step, then from the forward step back to the backward step, stringing the two movements together. Sweat dripped from his forehead and mixed with the rain, and he couldn’t tell them apart himself. Shuichi watched from the side for a while, and when Tetsuya was connecting the footwork, he mentioned, « Bend your knees a little, » then said nothing more.

In the evening, the rain stopped. Tetsuya left first, saying he wanted to practice the standing posture for another quarter of an hour before dinner. Obito did not leave. He stood in the courtyard, hesitated for a moment, then walked over to the edge of the veranda and sat next to Shuichi.

« Big Brother Shuichi. »

« Hmm. »

« Is there someone from the Hyuga Clan at your school ? »

Shuichi turned his head to look at him. « How do you know ? »

« Tetsuya mentioned it while he was waiting for you to get out of school earlier. He said there’s a child from the Hyuga Clan in his class who wears a headband on his forehead, has very good Taijutsu, and took first place on the balance beam. »

« Hyuga Shimizu. »

« Is he very strong ? »

« His foundation is very solid, » Shuichi said. « His footwork has the basics of Gentle Fist. »

Obito was silent for a while. « Do people from the Hyuga Clan bully others like the people from the Uchiha Clan do ? »

Shuichi looked at Obito. His tone was very light when he asked this question, as if he were confirming the location of a dangerous item.

« The members of the Hyuga Branch House don’t bully others, » Shuichi said. « They are the ones being bullied. »

« Branch House ? »

Shuichi thought for a moment and decided to tell the truth. « The Hyuga Clan is divided into the Main House and the Branch House. People of the Branch House have a curse seal on their foreheads called the Caged Bird Seal. This seal prevents them from using their Byakugan freely and also limits their potential. People of the Branch House are born to be guards for the Main House. »

Obito’s expression changed from confusion to silence. When a five-year-old child was trying to understand the phrase « born to be guards, » the expression on his face wasn’t so much anger as it was a quiet confusion ; he didn’t understand why some people were born to be guards.

« Then... » Obito said, « Is he hiding his strength just like Big Brother Shuichi ? »

Shuichi paused. Obito’s words were too accurate. It wasn’t about being smart ; it was that his way of thinking habitually linked the words « being bullied » and « hiding strength. » Because he was doing the same thing within the clan grounds. When you don’t have enough power, you hide.

« Why do you think that ? » Shuichi asked.

« Because if he doesn’t hide, others will be unhappy, » Obito said very naturally. « If others are unhappy, they will bully him. So he has to hide too. »

Shuichi looked at the branches of the plum tree in the courtyard, soaked by the rain. At five years old, Obito already instinctively understood the logic of hiding—not because he was weak, but because being too strong was unsafe. It had taken Shuichi several years of being isolated in the clan grounds to learn this realization. Hyuga Shimizu was hiding in school, Obito was hiding in the clan grounds, and Shuichi was hiding in front of everyone.

Three people, each hiding in their own place.

« Obito, » Shuichi said. « You don’t need to hide all the time. »

Obito looked up at him.

« Your body learns things very quickly now. After you enroll, you don’t need to hide in Taijutsu class ; it’s normal to be good at Taijutsu. Just don’t mention the Sharingan. »

Obito’s eyes lit up. « Then can I win against others in Taijutsu class ? »

« Win as many as you can. »

Obito nodded vigorously. Then he stood up, walked into the courtyard, and performed two more rounds of the backward step. This time, it was more powerful than before.

At dinner, Minako set an extra pair of chopsticks and a bowl.

« Where is that child ? » she asked.

« He went back. »

« I know the one from Tetsuya’s family. Who is the other one ? »

« Uchiha Obito. From the clan grounds. Only his grandmother takes care of him. »

Minako was silent for a moment. She probably knew of Obito because there weren’t many orphans without parents in the Uchiha Clan, and Obito was one of them. She picked up a piece of fish for Shuichi and then said, « Ask him to stay for dinner next time. »

Shuichi looked up at his mother. Minako was wiping her hands on her apron with a calm expression. Her meaning was very clear : that child had no parents, and when he went home alone, only his grandmother was waiting for him. It was just an extra set of bowls and chopsticks.

« Hmm, » Shuichi said.

After dinner, Shuichi sat on the veranda with the third chapter of his Chakra textbook spread out in his hands. He had already memorized all the Chakra conduction paths on the meridian diagram, but the part about elemental transformation still required practical verification. The issue of the Chakra paper couldn’t be resolved for the time being.

He suddenly thought of Minato.

The blond young man who practiced the starting stance of the Flying Thunder God at the Training Ground. He hadn’t gone to the Training Ground to peek for more than half a year. Minato knew Fugaku and knew the location of the Uchiha Clan Grounds' Training Ground. If he used « curiosity about Chakra attributes » as an excuse to go to the Training Ground and run into Minato, he might be able to borrow a piece of paper. But this plan was too risky ; Minato was the future Fourth Hokage, and his insight was not something an ordinary person could compare to. If a six-year-old Uchiha child approached him proactively, he would definitely notice.

Shuichi closed the textbook and closed his eyes.

Deep within his eyes, the Tomoe was rotating quietly. The concept of Growth used his pupil power as nourishment, slowly growing at the ends of his optic nerves. He could feel the rhythm of that Growth. It was like the unripe plums on the plum tree that hadn’t grown out yet ; when the season arrived, they would naturally bear fruit.

But it still wasn’t enough. There were six years left until graduation, and the Third Ninja World War wasn’t far off either. He had to have enough capability by then, at least enough not to die silently in some corner.

Shuichi opened his eyes. The bird on the plum branch shook the water droplets from its wings in the night wind after the rain. In the distance, the lights of the Uchiha Clan Grounds lit up the night. The sound of the Naka River could be vaguely heard from the direction of the stone bridge.

Tomorrow is Wednesday. There is a practical class on Chakra refinement at school. Obito and Tetsuya will come over after school. In nine more days, there will be a field practice class.

Shuichi stood up, walked back to his room, and placed the Kunai under his pillow. Then he lay down and closed his eyes.
