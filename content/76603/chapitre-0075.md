# Chapitre 75 — Transaction

« Lord Madara. » Shuichi spoke, his tone respectful. « You summoned me. »

Madara looked at him with a calm gaze. His voice was very soft, carrying a hoarse quality, as if he hadn’t spoken in a long time. Every word seemed to be squeezed out from the depths of his throat.

« Uchiha Shuichi. » Madara’s gaze swept over Shuichi, as if observing him. That gaze carried a sense of oppression, as if wanting to see through to Shuichi’s heart. The Three Tomoe slowly rotated, red light flickering within his pupils.

« At your age, to have such strength, it is quite good. » Madara’s voice was calm. « Moreover, the way you would stop at nothing to achieve this level of strength makes me appreciate you. »

Shuichi bowed his head slightly : « Lord Madara is too kind. »

« I have a plan. » Madara’s gaze landed on Shuichi, the Three Tomoe slowly rotating. « A plan that can change this world. Are you willing to participate ? »

Shuichi raised his head and looked at Madara. His expression was calm, his gaze sincere. « I am willing. » He did not ask what the plan was, agreeing directly.

Madara looked at him, his gaze shifting slightly. « You don’t ask what the plan is ? »

Shuichi spoke, his tone respectful, with a hint of admiration in his eyes : « If it is Lord Madara’s plan, it must be a great plan. »

Madara looked at him, the corners of his mouth lifting slightly. « Heh. » His voice carried a faint sense of amusement. Then Madara began to narrate on his own accord.

Madara’s voice carried a strange, echoing quality, as if narrating an ancient prophecy. « This world is filled with war, hatred, and pain. Ninjas slaughter each other, and ordinary people live in fear. This situation has persisted for thousands of years. »

Madara’s gaze became deep, as if recalling the distant past. « I have seen too much death, too much pain. My brother died, my clansmen died, my friends died. This world is a hell, and everyone is suffering. »

« I want to change all of this. » Madara’s voice became firm. « As long as the Infinite Tsukuyomi is activated, everyone will be plunged into an illusion. In that world of illusion, there is no war, no hatred, no pain. Everyone can obtain the happiness they desire. » « This is the Eye of the Moon Plan. »

Shuichi listened to Madara’s words, his expression unchanged. Infinite Tsukuyomi. He knew of this plan from his past life. Trapping everyone in an illusion to create a fake world of peace. It sounded beautiful, but in essence, it was depriving everyone of their free will. That was not peace ; it was falsehood, self-deception.

However, Shuichi’s approach and thoughts were different. After listening to Madara’s narration, Shuichi spoke. His expression was calm, his tone sincere : « I also want to change this world. »

« This world is indeed filled with war and pain. If a peaceful world can be created, I am also willing to contribute to it. » His gaze landed on Madara, carrying a sense of seriousness and determination.

Madara looked at him with a calm gaze. « Wrong. » His voice carried a faint tone of negation. « Someone like you, who has not yet suffered setbacks in reality, who has not lost anything precious, will not have such thoughts. »

Madara’s gaze landed on Shuichi. « You have not experienced true pain, have not lost anything truly important. You will not understand my thoughts. »

Shuichi fell silent. His expression stiffened slightly, then returned to calm. Madara was right. He truly had not experienced that kind of pain. He had not lost a brother, not lost his clansmen, not lost his entire clan. His mother was still alive, and his life was relatively peaceful. He would not deprive everyone of their free will for the sake of so-called peace.

Madara did not agree to let him join. He continued to speak on his own. « All these years, I have acted for the sake of this plan. » Madara’s voice was calm. « Everything is for the Eye of the Moon Plan. »

His gaze landed on Shuichi. « I know that you have a method to extract abilities. You should also be interested in this body of mine. »

Shuichi’s gaze shifted slightly. Madara continued : « Therefore, I propose a transaction with you. »

« Within seven years, you must not interfere with the operations of the Ninja World, must not capture any Tailed Beast, and must not actively target the actions of my plan. » « In exchange, after I am about to die, I will hand over my body to you, for you to dispose of as you please. »

Shuichi looked at Madara with a calm gaze. Madara had proactively proposed this transaction. This was not quite what he had anticipated. Shuichi thought for a moment. Seven years. He quickly calculated the time in his mind.

According to the original timeline, seven years later would be roughly after the Nine-Tailed incident. At that time, the Uchiha Clan had not yet been exterminated, and Obito had just started acting. What could Madara do within seven years ? Why did Madara only propose seven years ? Perhaps it was because his physical condition could not hold out for much longer, or perhaps because the key node of the Eye of the Moon Plan was seven years away. Regardless of the reason, he could not let things go too smoothly for Madara.

Shuichi spoke, his tone firm. « Five years at most. » « Within five years, I will not interfere with the operations of the Ninja World, will not capture any Tailed Beast, and will not actively target Lord Madara’s plan. But after five years, I will no longer be bound by this agreement. »

Madara looked at him with a calm gaze. Madara fell silent for a moment, as if thinking about something. A few seconds later, he spoke. « Five years. » « Agreed. »

Shuichi breathed a sigh of relief. « There is one more thing. » He looked at Madara, his tone calm. « I want a few White Zetsu for research. »

Madara’s gaze shifted slightly. « White Zetsu ? » « Yes. » Shuichi nodded. « I am very interested in the abilities of the White Zetsu. » « I hope to take a few White Zetsu away to conduct research. »

Madara looked at Shuichi with a calm gaze. « You may. » He waved his hand. A White Zetsu floated up from the ground and stood before Shuichi. « This one is for you. » Madara’s voice was calm. « White Zetsu can split, one is enough for your research. »

Shuichi looked at the White Zetsu in front of him and nodded. « Thank you, Lord Madara. » The agreement was reached. Shuichi turned around, preparing to leave.

« Wait. » Madara’s voice came from behind him. Shuichi stopped in his tracks and turned back to look at Madara. Madara looked at him with a deep gaze. « You are quite interesting. » His voice carried a faint sense of amusement. « I have seen many people, but people like you are rare. You have strength, you have ambition, but you also know when to compromise. »

« Five years from now, I look forward to seeing what level of Growth you reach. » Shuichi looked at Madara with a calm gaze. « See you in five years. » He turned and walked toward the cave exit. The White Zetsu followed behind him, silent and soundless.

Walking out of the cave, Shuichi looked at the night scenery outside. Moonlight spilled onto the ground, illuminating the path ahead. As he walked, Shuichi organized the conversation that had just taken place. The agreement was reached. Five years. He calculated the time in his mind.

According to the original timeline, Madara died shortly after rescuing Obito, roughly around the year 49 or 50. In other words, Madara would not live to see five years later. But the current situation was completely different. Shuichi did not know what variables there might be. Perhaps Madara would live longer, or perhaps shorter. He could not afford to slack off at all during these five years.

Although the agreement stipulated that he could not interfere with major events in the Ninja World, running off to Plunder a few Bloodline Limits or understanding the general trend did not count as interference. Moreover, an agreement was just an agreement. If they truly fell out, Madara could not do much to him. Shuichi’s mindset was very peaceful.

He began to think about his next plan. First, learn the Ninjutsu from the scroll of sealing. Multi Shadow Clone, Flying Thunder God, Reaper Death Seal, Edo Tensei ; these were already memorized in his mind. Next, he needed to increase his strength as much as possible and master all these Ninjutsu.

Second, integrate the various Ninjutsu in his memory. He had Plundered the memories of many Ninjas, and the knowledge of those Ninjutsu was all in his mind. He needed to develop Ninjutsu suitable for himself and form his own combat system. Shuichi raised his head and looked toward the sky. The moon hung high, emitting a silvery-white light. He looked at the moon with a deep gaze.
