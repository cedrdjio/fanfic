# Chapitre 1 — Awakening

Konoha 41, Spring.

The morning in the Uchiha Clan Grounds was exceptionally quiet. Thin mist drifted over from the direction of the Naka River, wrapping the cool breath of the river water and gently covering the rows of tiled eaves.

In the distance, the stone faces of the three Hokage on the Hokage Rock were faintly visible in the morning light—the First, the Second, and the Sandaime, Hiruzen Sarutobi, who had been in power for twenty-one years—they silently overlooked this Ninja Village that was still in slumber.

Uchiha Shuichi opened his eyes from a long nightmare.

He lay on the tatami, staring at the wooden beams on the ceiling, his pupils slightly contracting.

It felt as if a whole river had been poured into his head ; countless images and sounds were surging through his consciousness, and two completely different lives were colliding violently in his mind.

One was a university student from the 21st century.

The dormitory, the Naruto anime on the computer screen, the all-nighters cramming before final exams, the unpalatable rice bowls in the cafeteria.

It finally froze on one scene : late at night, the blue light of the computer screen flickering, his chest suddenly tightened, and his vision plunged into boundless darkness.

The other was a child of the Uchiha Clan in the Land of Fire’s Konoha.

Five years old.

His father, Uchiha Chengren, had sacrificed himself in a mission a year ago ; that was the tail end of the Second Ninja World War, and he died in an unknown place.

His mother, Minako, had raised him alone.

He hadn’t started Ninja Academy yet, and his daily routine was playing with shuriken with a few other children from the clan in the open space of the clan grounds.

The two memories were tearing at, intertwining with, and merging into each other.

Shuichi sat up abruptly, gasping for air, his forehead covered in cold sweat.

« Shuichi ? What’s wrong ? »

His mother, Minako’s voice came from the next room, carrying the laziness of someone just awakened.

The paper door was pulled open a crack, revealing a gentle face—Uchiha Minako, with black hair scattered on her shoulders and fine lines at the corners of her eyes.

Those were the traces left from raising a child alone after losing her husband.

« I—I’m fine, Mom. » Shuichi’s voice was a bit hoarse. « I just had a dream. »

« Did you dream about Dad again ? » Minako’s eyes softened.

« ...Yeah. » Shuichi lied.

The paper door closed again.

Shuichi lowered his head and looked at his ridiculously small hands—these were the hands of a five-year-old, with fair, tender skin and short, stubby knuckles.

However, a strange, burning sensation faintly emanated from the palm of his right hand, as if something was writhing beneath the skin.

A tumultuous storm, known only to him, surged in his heart.

The Ninja World.

He had transmigrated into the world of Naruto.

And into the Uchiha Clan, at that.

The Uchiha Clan that was exterminated in the original story.

He closed his eyes, and memories of his past life flashed by like a revolving lantern : Uchiha Itachi standing with a blood-stained sword among the corpses of his clansmen, Sasuke’s young figure fleeing under the moonlight, and rows of Sharingan slowly rotating on Danzo’s bandage-wrapped right arm.

And deeper darkness : Uchiha Madara, Black Zetsu, and Ōtsutsuki Kaguya.

This was not a dream. It was not a hallucination. Inside this five-year-old body was the soul of an adult. A transmigrator who knew this world like the back of his hand.

No. It wasn’t just transmigration.

Shuichi looked down at his tender hands. This body still retained fragments of memories from another five-year-old child—practicing shuriken with his father in the yard, the sweet dumplings his mother made, the shock of seeing the Hokage Rock for the first time.

The original Uchiha Shuichi had also truly existed ; it was just that his consciousness had been overwritten and merged with the memories of his past life at some point.

Transmigration combined with rebirth. Two sets of memories, one soul.

Shuichi took a deep breath, forcing himself to calm down.

In his past life as an ordinary university student, he had never experienced a true life-or-death test.

But now it was different. This was the Ninja World, a place where one could lose their life at any moment. He had to adapt as quickly as possible.

He quickly sorted through the timeline in his mind.

It was now Konoha 41 ; the Second Ninja World War had just ended in the previous year, Konoha 40.

The name of the Konoha Sannin had already been made famous on the battlefield of the Land of Rain, Sakumo Hatake was still alive, and the future Fourth Hokage, Minato Namikaze, should still be a fourteen-year-old boy.

And the night of the Uchiha Clan’s extermination, according to the timeline of the original story, occurred around Konoha 59. He had about eighteen years.

Eighteen years sounded long, but in this world of Naruto, it was merely a snap of the fingers.

The Third Ninja World War was just a few years away, and the Night of the Nine-Tailed was only ten years away.

« Calm down, calm down, » he muttered to himself, using his childish voice to speak words that didn’t fit his age.

He spread his hands, his gaze landing on his right palm. The burning sensation was becoming more and more obvious, as if a beast that had been sleeping for many years had finally caught the scent of prey and was slowly waking up.

Shuichi frowned, staring at his right hand.

A five-year-old’s body didn’t have Chakra yet—or rather, Chakra was still in its most primitive, undeveloped state, which would only be systematically refined after entering the Ninja Academy.

But that heat flow in the palm of his right hand was clearly not Chakra.

It was something else.

He rolled off the tatami, put on his small wooden sandals, pulled open the paper door, and walked into the courtyard.

The morning in the Uchiha Clan Grounds was already bustling.

From the neighboring yard came the muffled sound of shuriken embedding into wooden stakes, and in the distance, someone was practicing the Fire Style : Fireball Jutsu.

The cracking sound of air being scorched by flames was clearly audible.

The Uchiha Clan valued martial arts ; even in the early morning, they would not slack off in their training.

Even though the Second War had just ended, the Ninjas still maintained a state of readiness to enter the next battle at any time.

Shuichi stood in his small courtyard, looking down at his right hand.

The burning sensation in his palm not only didn’t fade under the sunlight in the yard, but instead became even more intense.

It was a… sense of hunger. Like a black hole that could never be filled, it was silently calling out for something.

A sparrow landed on the plum tree by the courtyard wall.

Gray-brown feathers, a round body, it tilted its head and looked at the little boy in the yard with an eye like a black bean.

Shuichi’s gaze was drawn to the sparrow.

To be precise, it was his right hand that was drawn to the sparrow.

The burning sensation in his palm suddenly intensified the moment the sparrow appeared, as if an invisible thread had connected his right hand to the sparrow.

He walked toward the plum tree, as if possessed.

The sparrow didn’t fly away, because the sparrows in the Uchiha Clan Grounds had long been accustomed to the presence of humans and didn’t seem to fear this five-year-old child.

Shuichi reached out his right hand, his fingertips an inch away from the sparrow’s wing.

And then, he felt it.

He felt everything surging within the sparrow.

Not just life. Not just flesh and bone. He could clearly perceive every meridian, every wisp of breath, every inch of innate instinct within the sparrow.

It was the Flight Instinct, that talent that allowed wings to flap and the body to soar on the wind without needing to think.

It was a bird’s soul, tiny and pure, like a drop of dew concentrated in the morning light.

His right hand was craving it.

Craving to Plunder.

Shuichi’s pupils dilated slightly.

A bold thought flashed through his mind : if his right hand could really take something from this sparrow…

He tried to guide the burning sensation with his consciousness.

The next moment, an indescribable suction force erupted from the palm of his right hand.

The sparrow let out a weak chirp, its body suddenly stiffened, and then it fell from the branch of the plum tree like a kite with a cut string.

Shuichi reached out and caught it ; the cold touch of fading life transmitted to his palm.

The sparrow was dead.

But in Shuichi’s mind, there was something more—a subtle and strange instinct, as if he naturally knew how to adjust his body’s posture in the wind, how to perceive the direction of air currents, and how to make his body as light as a feather.

He gasped for air, his legs went weak, and he sat directly on the muddy ground of the courtyard.

Mental energy.

He clearly felt that a large portion of some kind of energy within him had been drained.

It wasn’t Chakra, but something more essential and primitive—the mental energy that supported the operation of consciousness and the soul.

That feeling of exhaustion was like being forced to run a thousand meters after staying up for three nights in a row.

His head was dizzy, and his temples were throbbing.

But the gain was real.

He had plundered the Flight Instinct from a sparrow.

Shuichi stared at his right hand, his heart pounding as if it were going to break his ribs.

He finally understood the essence of the burning sensation in his palm : it was a Plunder hand.

Greedy, thirsty, never satisfied.

It could Plunder anything from any living creature or any corpse that had just died.

Chakra, Bloodline Limit, Ninjutsu, Vitality, soul fragments, or even conceptual existences, as long as he was willing to pay enough mental energy as the price.

Plundering a sparrow’s Flight Instinct had consumed enough mental energy to make him almost unable to stand.

What if he plundered a Bloodline Limit ? A Sharingan ? The Tailed Beast’s Chakra ?

Shuichi revealed a deep smile that was completely inconsistent with a five-year-old child.

Mental energy would grow.

In his past life, he was an ordinary university student, and his mental energy was only slightly stronger than an average person’s.

But this life was different ; he was a transmigrator and a reincarnated person, and the soul of someone who had lived two lives was inherently heavier than that of an ordinary person.

Moreover, Chakra itself was formed by the fusion of physical energy and mental energy ; in the process of cultivating Chakra, mental energy would naturally grow along with it.

In other words, as he became stronger, the upper limit of this Plunder ability would also continue to rise.

« Shuichi ? What are you doing sitting on the ground ? You’ve dirtied your clothes ! »

Minako’s voice came from behind.

Shuichi hurriedly hid the sparrow’s corpse behind his back and turned around, revealing a bright, childlike smile.

« Mom, I’m just looking at the flowers. »

Minako walked over and ruffled his hair, her eyes filled with a hint of heartache.

Ever since her husband passed away, this child had become a bit different—much quieter, and sometimes he would stare blankly by himself.

She just assumed it was the trauma brought on by losing his father.

« Go wash your hands ; breakfast is ready. »

« Okay ! »

Shuichi stood up, and while his mother wasn’t looking, he gently placed the sparrow’s corpse into the soil at the Roots of the plum tree, then walked quickly into the house.

The palm of his right hand was still burning.

That sense of hunger was not satisfied by plundering a sparrow ; instead, it was as if it had been awakened, becoming increasingly greedy and craving more, stronger, and more essential prey.

Shuichi scrubbed his hands by the washbasin, watching his tender face reflected in the water.

Black hair, black eyes, the typical Uchiha look, with a bit of baby fat between his brows and eyes.

But the light flickering in those eyes definitely did not belong to a five-year-old child.

He calculated the future in his heart.

Konoha 41, he was five.

Following the normal trajectory, he would enter the Ninja Academy at six and graduate to become a Genin at twelve.

There was plenty of time to use for Growth and planning.

But the premise was that he had to be cautious enough.

The downfall of the Uchiha Clan stemmed from the conflict with the Village’s higher-ups.

Danzo and the suspicious high-level Advisors would not allow the Uchiha to continue growing.

But that was a political game ; for the current Shuichi, there was only one most important thing.

Becoming stronger.

Before the night of the extermination arrived, he had to grow to a level sufficient to change everything.

Moreover, he had an advantage that others didn’t : he knew all the important figures of the future.

Minato Namikaze was only fourteen now and still in the Growth stage.

Kakashi was a year younger than him and still a four-year-old child.

Obito, Rin Nohara, Might Guy—these characters who would stir up the Ninja World in the future were all just snot-nosed brats now.

Even Uchiha Itachi hadn’t been born yet.

Shuichi dried his hands and stood by the washbasin for a moment.

A faint pulsation came from the palm of his right hand, like a black hole that could never be filled, calling out silently.

Plunder.

Plunder more. Plunder stronger.

He clenched his right hand, his lips slightly upturned.

This world would not go according to the original script.

Possessing foresight and a hand that could Plunder all things, his starting point in this world was already higher than anyone else’s.
