# Chapitre 18 — Opening the Eyes

For a full week after merging the Growth concept, Shuichi tried every method he could think of.

He infused his right eye with Chakra, from trace amounts to moderate amounts, from continuous infusion to pulsed shocks. The sleeping warmth on his retina showed no reaction. He tried recalling every memory that could stir his emotions, like his father’s sacrifice. But his right eye remained quiet. He even tried the dumbest method : sitting still under the plum tree with his eyes closed, repeatedly circulating Chakra around his eyes.

A week passed, and nothing happened.

Sitting in the third row by the window in the classroom, Shuichi pressed his right index finger against his right eyelid. Outside the window, the older students on the playground were practicing shuriken duels. Teacher Mitomon was flipping through the lecture notes on Konoha history at the podium, speaking about the chapter where Tobirama founded the Military Police Force—Shuichi noticed the backs of several Uchiha children in the classroom stiffen slightly.

« Shuichi. » Tetsuya leaned over from his right, lowering his voice, « Why have you been touching your eye all day ? Is your eye uncomfortable ? »

« An eyelash fell in. »

« Oh. My grandmother says if your eye is uncomfortable, you should wash it with tea. »

Shuichi moved his finger away from his eyelid. He couldn’t let anyone notice anything unusual at school ; if an Uchiha child kept touching his eye, someone would eventually associate it with the Sharingan.

After school, Shuichi did not go to the pine forest. He walked along the main road of the Uchiha Clan Grounds to the far east side of the clan grounds. On the other side of the river were the duty station and the attached training dojo of the Uchiha Military Police Force. He stopped under a willow tree on the other side of the river, using the willow branches to hide as he looked toward the dojo.

Inside the dojo, two Uchiha Chunin were doing post-battle practice. Shuichi had seen one of them before ; it was the middle-aged Ninja who had complained about the budget being cut at the blacksmith shop entrance in the clan grounds square last time. The other one was younger, in his early twenties. Shuichi stood under the willow tree for a quarter of an hour and saw a detail : after a footwork mistake where the younger Chunin was almost grazed on the cheek by his companion’s Kunai, a faint red flash passed extremely quickly across the edge of his pupil. It wasn’t a full Sharingan awakening, just a momentary pale red halo on the outer edge of the pupil, which then quickly faded back to black. The whole process took less than half a second.

Shuichi saw it clearly. It wasn’t opening the eyes, but a natural fluctuation of pupil power produced in a stress state, a physiological response triggered by danger. This was because the Kunai had almost grazed his cheek. His body sensed the threat, and the pupil power was automatically mobilized.

On the way back, Shuichi repeatedly recalled that half-second red halo. Could he also try this method, making his eyes believe he was facing a threat ?

Back in his own courtyard, Shuichi raised his right hand, palm facing his right eye. Chakra was released from his palm, forming a thin energy film very close to the eyeball, then quickly pulled away, as if something had flashed past his eyes. His right pupil contracted violently. Something deep in his right eye jumped slightly. The Growth concept was stirred a little, then immediately sank back into silence.

Shuichi pondered ; the direction was correct. A threat response could trigger pupil power fluctuations. But a fake threat from Chakra wasn’t enough. It could make the pupil contract and make the pupil power jump slightly, but it wasn’t enough to fully open the Sharingan. He needed a real threat, one sufficient to activate the Uchiha bloodline instinct. A real opponent.

But he had no opponents. The Taijutsu opponents at school were all children of the same age ; their attacks had no threat, and his body knew this clearly.

Shuichi looked down at his right palm. Then he thought of the Forest of Death.

Early Saturday morning, Shuichi first went to the Mixed Forest. He found that nest of field mice under the same pile of rotten leaves—the six pups were still huddled in the nest chamber. The weakest one, whose Growth concept he had Plundered last week, was still a size smaller than its siblings, but it was still alive. Shuichi Plundered the Growth concept from another slightly larger pup for the second time, then filled the hole back up as it was.

When he returned home, Minako was hanging clothes in the courtyard. Shuichi walked under the plum tree, pressed his right hand against his left eyelid, built a Chakra barrier, pushed the Growth concept into his left eye retina, and suppressed the diffusion. The whole process was the same as with the right eye—the double-layered barrier was pushed slightly taut, and the Growth concept was firmly locked into the base of the retina.

Now, the same mass of gentle warmth was sleeping in both eyes.

He sat still under the plum tree with his eyes closed, letting his mental energy recover to near full. Then he stood up, summoned Predator from the plum branch, crossed the Naka River Stone Bridge, the Training Ground, the pine forest, and the dried riverbed, walking all the way to the edge of the Forest of Death.

Predator circled above his head. Its flight posture, with superimposed low-light vision and auditory positioning, was more precise than any wild raptor. It locked onto a position about fifty meters in front of Shuichi and made a short, indicative dive. Shuichi lowered his body and crossed the last row of oak trees.

The terrain of the Forest of Death was completely different from the outside. The canopy was dense and blocked the light, and the intertwined tree roots and thick layers of rotten leaves on the ground were like a piece of old, water-soaked carpet. The air was filled with the smell of damp soil and a deeper, fishy scent. Under an old oak tree that required three people to hug, next to a fallen log, a grey-black badger was digging in the soil.

Shuichi stopped thirty steps away. The badger was larger than he had imagined, nearly two feet from head to tail. The muscles of its forelimbs rolled under its grey-black fur, each dig bringing up a clump of humus larger than its head. A level four prey. The conclusion he had written in his mind late last summer was [Current physique does not recommend hunting alone].

Now, his physical foundation was close to the level of a twelve-year-old child, with Chakra-enhanced explosive power, all-terrain movement digested from Climbing Ability, and close-quarters suppression experience accumulated through multiple Plunders. He had already crossed the threshold of level four. His goal today was not hunting, but to fight a real close-quarters battle with a level four prey, using only Taijutsu and Chakra without using his right hand to Plunder.

Shuichi walked out from behind the oak tree. He didn’t sneak ; his feet made a slight crunching sound on the rotten leaves. The badger stopped digging and raised its head. It didn’t run. Badgers don’t run. Its instinctive reaction when sensing a threat was the exact opposite of a fox.

The badger turned around, its body low, front claws spread, and a low growl rumbled from deep in its chest.

Shuichi kept his right hand hanging down, his left hand half-raised in front of him. The Chakra in his Dantian split into two paths : one injected into his legs, the other built a double-layered barrier at the junction of his retinal and optic nerves in both eyes.

The badger charged over.

Its speed far exceeded Shuichi’s expectations. Its grey-black body was like a stone shot from a slingshot, and the rotten leaves kicked up by its four short legs splattered behind it. Shuichi kicked off the ground with his right foot, his body shifting horizontally to the left. The badger’s claws grazed the outside of his right arm, less than two inches away, and the sound of the air being torn was clearly audible.

Shuichi did not retreat. Before the badger landed and turned, he had already surged forward, his right elbow smashing into the side of the badger’s face, knocking it off course. The badger opened its mouth to bite back—Shuichi’s left hand was already waiting on the trajectory of its mouth, slapping the tip of the badger’s nose with one palm, knocking the biting route off. The badger’s teeth grazed the web of Shuichi’s left hand, leaving a shallow bloodstain.

Shuichi felt something move deep in both eyes simultaneously. It was an automatic response of the suppressed bloodline instinct upon identifying a body fighting with all its might.

The badger raised its front claws and swiped at Shuichi’s chest. Shuichi didn’t block it hard ; he infused Chakra into the sole of his right foot, performed a low-angle side slide, and slid to the badger’s side, pressing against its forelimbs. His left hand hooked the back of the badger’s neck, his right hand grabbed the badger’s waist, and he pressed his whole body onto it.

He could use his right hand to Plunder. But he didn’t. He used the strength of both his left and right hands to suppress the badger’s body. The badger rolled violently under his palm, its four legs kicking wildly, and the rotten leaves were kicked up everywhere. Its mouth twisted over, white teeth biting at Shuichi’s wrist ; Shuichi tilted his wrist to dodge, and the badger’s teeth bit into nothing.

A violent pulsation deep in his eyes. It wasn’t trembling ; it was something about to break through from the bottom of his eyeballs. The Chakra barrier was pushed outward by the pupil power surging from within ; the pupil power was waking up from its slumber, surging upward along the optic nerve.

The badger’s hind leg kicked Shuichi’s right ribs. Attacked, Shuichi felt a dull, diffusive, blunt pain. Shuichi let out a muffled grunt but didn’t let go. He flipped the badger over and pressed it onto the rotten leaves, his left knee pressing down on its hind legs, his right knee pressing down on its forelimbs, his entire body weight plus the downward pressure enhanced by Chakra all poured in. The badger’s struggle was suppressed to the minimum.

Then Shuichi raised his right hand.

It only raised for an instant. At that same instant, the badger made a desperate counterattack ; all its muscles contracted simultaneously, and its four legs exerted force together, attempting to flip out from under Shuichi’s knees. This was the most dangerous moment in the battle.

Something exploded deep in his eyes.

The heat flow surging from the bottom of his eyes poured into his brain along the optic nerve, and then poured back into his eyeballs from his brain along another channel. Shuichi’s field of vision was dyed pale red in an instant ; this was the energy fluctuation generated when the pupil power ignited the Growth concept sleeping on the base of his retina. The Growth concept woke up in both his eyes simultaneously, feeding on the pupil power, using the Sharingan’s blueprint as a template, and began to run in a self-Growth manner.

He opened his eyes. The badger in his field of vision was still maintaining its struggling posture, and he saw it clearly. The muscle fiber under its scapula that had been strained during the battle was twitching faintly ; the vibration precursor of the vocal cord deep in its throat, 0.1 seconds before emitting a low roar ; the tendon of its forepaw heel was exerting force, preparing for the next round of kicking. All information flooded into his brain in the same instant ; no analysis was needed, no judgment was needed.

A complete tomoe appeared on the edge of his iris. Black, with sharp contours, it sat steadily on the crimson iris outside the pupil.

One-tomoe Sharingan, eye awakened.

The badger twisted desperately under his knees, its mouth wide open. In the vision of his one-tomoe Sharingan, Shuichi saw the muscle gap at its neck that had been exposed after being knocked off by the previous elbow strike. Without needing to think, his left hand reached through from the side, backhanded and hooked the badger’s lower jaw. His right knee moved from the position suppressing its hind limbs to the badger’s chest, his body weight pressing down on its ribs. His left hand twisted hard, and the badger’s neck bone made a muffled sound under the grip strength infused with Chakra.

The struggle stopped.

Shuichi pressed his right hand onto the badger’s spine. Plunder. The suction force unfolded steadily, and the badger’s Vitality was extracted from its bones, muscles, and fur, pouring into his body along the channel in his palm. This time there was no struggle. His mental energy consumption was about one-fifth, similar to the previous Plunder of the raccoon dog, while the badger’s Vitality was nearly twice as dense as the raccoon dog’s. Without the extra burden caused by suppressing the struggle, there was no extra consumption. All mental energy was precisely used for the Plunder itself.

Shuichi rolled off the badger, sat on the fallen log, and felt the warmth of the Vitality slowly spreading in his body. His left ribs were still dull and aching. The badger’s kick would definitely be bruised tomorrow. The bloodstain on the web of his left hand had already dried. A small piece of fabric was missing from his pant leg. But deep in his eyes, the one-tomoe was operating steadily, and the Growth concept was quietly pushing the Sharingan to grow to the next stage with the pupil power as its target.

He sat on the fallen log for a quarter of an hour, letting his breathing stabilize and letting the Vitality complete the first stage of fusion. Then he summarized a new rule in his heart.

Fight first. Beat the prey until it loses its ability to resist, then Plunder. The benefits of doing this were two-way : the fighting process improved his practical experience in Taijutsu and Chakra usage, and when Plundering, the prey no longer struggled, reducing mental energy consumption to the minimum, so he didn’t have to spend nearly half of his mental energy on suppression like he did when Plundering the Red Fox. The Vitality of a badger was obtained with less than half the mental energy used for the Red Fox ; the return ratio had more than doubled.

The hunting classification table needed to be updated. Not updating the classification of prey, but updating the hunting method. In the past, he relied on sneaking, surprise attacks, and Plundering during struggles. It would be different from now on. With the Sharingan, with Chakra, and with more mature close-quarters Taijutsu, he could fight head-on. Beat the opponent until they had no ability to resist, then Plunder cleanly. This was the fighting style a Plunderer should have.

Predator glided down from the old oak tree, landed next to the badger’s carcass, looked down at the grey-black fur, and then looked up at Shuichi’s face. The black, bright pupils reflected the pair of crimson eyes.

« It will be like this from now on. » Shuichi said hoarsely, « Beat them crippled first, then Plunder. »

Predator tilted its head to look at him and let out a short cry.

Shuichi stood up, dragged the badger’s carcass under the fallen log, and covered it with rotten leaves. On the way back, he washed his hands and face in the stream by the pine forest. The stream reflected his face—his pupils had returned to the pure black of the Uchiha clan. One-tomoe was not a permanent state ; when not activated, it was no different from a normal Uchiha’s eyes.

When he returned home, the sun had just begun to slant westward. Minako was chopping vegetables in the kitchen ; she looked up at Shuichi, her gaze paused on the bloodstain on his left hand, and then she continued chopping vegetables.

« There is herbal tea in the kitchen. »

« Mm. »

Shuichi poured a cup of herbal tea and stood in the corridor drinking it. His right fingertips gently touched his right eyelid. A complete black tomoe lay quietly inside. The Growth concept was still running, and the pupil power was still growing.

And in his right palm, the badger’s Vitality was slowly permeating into the deepest parts of his bones and muscles.
