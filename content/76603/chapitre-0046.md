# Chapitre 46 — Land of Grass

After leaving Tanzaku Town, Shuichi traveled north, crossing the central plains of the Land of Fire, and arrived at the border between the Land of Fire and the Land of Grass in four days.

There was only one border checkpoint, consisting of two wooden huts, with three Ninja wearing Kusagakure headbands dozing off at the entrance. Shuichi bypassed it using a mountain path nearby. Given his current strength, a checkpoint of this level was effectively useless.

After entering the Land of Grass, Shuichi’s first impression was—poverty.

It wasn’t the kind of poverty where people were starving in the fields, but rather a drab, dull sort of poverty. The roads in the Land of Fire were packed dirt, lined with farmland and Villages, with trees neatly trimmed along the sides. The roads in the Land of Grass were a mix of gravel and mud, riddled with potholes, making carriages bounce violently as they passed. The farmland along the road was sparse, the crops were stunted, and the weeds on the ridges were taller than the crops themselves.

Villages were also scarce. Shuichi walked for most of the day and passed only two Villages, both consisting of a few dozen households with low, thatched-roof houses. Occasionally, one or two elderly people could be seen sitting at the Village entrance, staring blankly, not even looking up when they saw Shuichi pass by.

The Land of Grass was sandwiched between the Land of Fire and the Land of Earth, acting as a buffer zone for the power struggles between the major nations. It lacked strong military power, abundant resources, and even its agriculture suffered from low yields due to poor soil. Kusagakure was the only Ninja Village in the Land of Grass, and its strength was negligible in the Ninja World, not even on par with the Hidden Rain Village.

Shuichi walked along the main road for two days, arriving at Kusagakure on the evening of the third day.

Kusagakure was smaller than Shuichi had expected.

The Village was built against a mountain, with a low hill behind it and an open plain in front. The Village walls were made of wooden fences and rammed earth, less than two meters high—useless against Ninja, serving only to keep out wild beasts. Two Genin were on guard at the entrance, but one was yawning while the other was watching ants on the ground.

Shuichi did not enter directly.

He found a patch of woods outside the Village and sat down against a tree trunk. Lue flew up from his shoulder, circled twice in the air, and then flew toward Kusagakure.

« Go scout it out, » Shuichi said. « Focus on three things : how many Jonin there are, where the medical station is, and if there are any people being held captive. »

Lue nodded in the air, its black wings unfolding silently, blending into the evening sky.

Shuichi closed his eyes and waited.

About forty minutes later, Lue returned.

It landed on a branch next to Shuichi, tilting its head, its black eyes watching him.

« Four Jonin, » Lue said. « One Elite Jonin with very strong Chakra. The others are all Chunin and Genin. »

« Where is the medical station ? »

« On the east side of the Village, a separate building. There’s a green flag hanging at the entrance. »

« Any people being held captive ? »

Lue thought for a moment. « There is a dungeon in the Village, but I didn’t see anyone special. Inside the medical station... there is a person lying down. Their Chakra is very weak, but... very special. »

« How is it special ? »

« I can’t quite explain it, » Lue tilted its head. « It’s just... different from ordinary people. »

Shuichi nodded. Although Lue’s sensory abilities were strong, its descriptive skills were limited. However, with the description « Chakra is very weak but very special, » combined with Kusagakure’s penchant for collecting intelligence on Bloodline Limits, Shuichi already had a guess.

« We’ll make our move tomorrow, » Shuichi said. « We’ll go to the medical station first. »

Lue nodded.

The next morning, Shuichi walked into Kusagakure.

He did not use a disguise. Kusagakure was a Ninja Village, and the Transformation Technique was meaningless here—any Ninja above the Chunin level could see through an ordinary Transformation Technique. Shuichi chose to enter Kusagakure with his real face, but he hid his Sharingan and suppressed his Chakra to the level of a Chunin.

The streets of Kusagakure were narrow, and the houses on both sides were mostly single-story wooden structures, with an occasional two-story building. There weren’t many people on the street ; most were Ninja wearing Kusagakure headbands, though occasionally, a few civilians could be seen setting up stalls to sell vegetables along the road.

Shuichi headed straight for the medical station in the east of the Village.

The medical station was an independent wooden house, slightly larger than the surrounding buildings, with a green flag hanging at the entrance featuring a white cross. Shuichi pushed open the wooden door and walked in.

Inside was a small waiting area with a few wooden chairs placed against the wall and a teapot and cups on a long table. The air was filled with the scent of medicinal herbs.

Two Kusagakure Ninja were sitting at the long table chatting. One was wearing a Chunin-level tactical vest, and the other was in a standard Ninja uniform. The two were leaning back in their chairs, drinking tea, chatting about who had made a fool of themselves at the Training Ground yesterday, laughing uproariously.

When Shuichi pushed the door open and entered, the Chunin merely glanced at him casually without even stopping his conversation. Rogue Ninja frequently came and went in Kusagakure, and unfamiliar faces coming to the medical station for treatment were too common to be surprised by.

« You’re here for treatment, right ? » The Chunin pointed inside toward a cloth curtain, his tone as casual as if he were giving directions. « Go inside, just take a bite of that woman. »

Take a bite.

Shuichi didn’t speak ; he walked through the cloth curtain.

Behind the curtain was a small room, even smaller than the waiting area. A wooden bed was against the wall, and a person was lying on it.

A woman.

She was in her early twenties, with red hair spread across the pillow, a pale complexion, and cracked lips. She wore a greyish-white hospital gown that was loose at the collar, revealing several faint bruises below her collarbone.

Her condition was still passable, Shuichi judged inwardly. The Third Shinobi World War hadn’t started yet, so there weren’t many casualties in Kusagakure, and the exploitation of her wasn’t too severe yet. Once the Third War broke out and a large number of Ninja needed treatment, that would be the true torture.

But even so, the woman’s right arm was exposed from her sleeve, densely covered in bite marks. New ones overlapped the old ; some had scabbed over, some were still oozing blood, and others were white scars left after healing. The entire arm looked as if it had been gnawed on by thousands of ants.

Looking at that arm, a fire surged in Shuichi’s chest.

It wasn’t a burning rage, but a dull, stifling anger. It felt as if something was stuck there, unable to go up or down.

The laughter of those two Ninja in the waiting area still drifted in from behind the curtain, relaxed and casual, as if it were the most normal thing in the world.

Shuichi stood by the bed, his mind on other things.

White Fang saved his companion, the whole Village condemned him, and he eventually committed suicide. Kakashi was pelted with stones at seven years old. The Uchiha Clan was trapped by the Military Police Force, their budget cut year after year ; sooner or later, they would be consumed by this Village. After the night of the Nine-Tailed, Naruto would be ostracized by the whole Village and treated like a monster.

And this woman before him was treated as a living medical kit—just take a bite.

The person who said that didn’t even have any malice ; it was as natural as saying, « Go over there and have a drink of water. »

Shuichi suddenly felt that this world was deeply sick.

Although he wasn’t a good person in his past life, he still had basic moral bottom lines. Locking a person in a room and letting everyone bite her to drink her blood for healing—if such a thing happened in his past life, anyone would find it disgusting.

But here, it was normal. No one thought it was wrong ; no one thought it was cruel. « Just take a bite of that woman. »

This world was deeply sick.

The stifling anger in his chest didn’t dissipate ; instead, it burned brighter and brighter.

Shuichi withdrew his gaze and turned to leave.

He needed to go kill the Jonin first before coming back to deal with this woman.

But Shuichi stood in front of the curtain for a moment. He was dazed for a moment.

It was this moment that made the ordinary Ninja in the waiting area notice something was wrong.

The Ninja had been chatting with the Chunin, but Shuichi had been inside for too long. People who came for treatment would go in, take a bite, and come out ; they wouldn’t stay in there that long. Moreover, the man hadn’t made any sound after entering—no biting sounds, no talking, nothing.

The Ninja put down his teacup and stood up.

He walked to the curtain and lifted it to take a look.

Shuichi was standing by the bed, his back to him.

The Ninja scrutinized Shuichi carefully.

No headband.

Although rogue Ninja frequently came and went in Kusagakure, those who came to the medical station for treatment were either local Ninja wearing Kusagakure headbands or foreign Ninja wearing the headbands of other Villages. People without headbands weren’t non-existent, but they were rare.

And this man had no headband, carried a short blade at his waist, hadn’t registered when he entered, and after going in, had just stood there dazed without doing anything.

The Ninja’s gaze changed.

« Who are you ? » The Ninja’s tone shifted from casual to wary. « Which Village are you from ? »

Shuichi turned his head to look at him.

No answer.

The Ninja’s right hand quietly reached for the Kunai at his waist.

« I’m asking you a question, you are— »

Shuichi moved.

The short blade was drawn from its sheath, the edge flashing in the dim room. The Ninja didn’t even see how Shuichi had drawn his blade ; he only felt a chill at his throat, and then his vision began to tilt, the world spinning around him.

By the time his body hit the ground, his head had already been severed from his neck.

The Chunin’s voice came from outside the curtain : « Hey, what’s wrong— »

Shuichi lifted the curtain and walked out.

The Chunin saw the headless corpse on the floor, the short blade in Shuichi’s hand, and the blood that hadn’t been wiped clean from the blade.

He opened his mouth to scream.

Shuichi’s blade had already arrived.

From drawing the blade to sheathing it, it took less than a second. The Chunin’s throat was slit, blood gushing from the wound. He clutched his neck and fell to the ground, his legs kicking twice before he went still.

Shuichi stood in the waiting area of the medical station, looking at the two corpses.

He had changed his mind.

His original plan was to infiltrate, find the Uzumaki descendant, Plunder the Uzumaki Bloodline, kill the Kusagakure Jonin, and leave.

But now, he didn’t want to just kill the Jonin.

This world was deeply sick. White Fang was forced to his death, the Uchiha were ostracized, and the Uzumaki descendant was used as livestock. These things were not coincidental ; they were the root of the world’s sickness. And standing here in this medical station, looking at the woman with bite marks all over her arms behind the curtain, he felt that he could at least scrape away a layer of this festering sore.

Every single Ninja in Kusagakure deserved to die.

Shuichi turned and walked back into the room behind the curtain. The woman had heard the commotion outside, opened her eyes, and upon seeing the bloodstains on Shuichi, her body shrank back slightly.

« You... » the woman’s voice was hoarse.

Shuichi walked to the bedside and pressed his right hand onto the woman’s forehead.

He expanded his sensory perception. The woman’s Chakra unfolded in his mind—weak but special, completely different from that of an ordinary Ninja. The underlying structure of her Chakra was more than one level more complex than that of an ordinary Ninja, possessing a natural, almost infinite regenerative power. This was the essence of the Uzumaki Bloodline.

The Uzumaki Bloodline.

Shuichi withdrew his right hand and looked at the woman.

« Stay here and don’t move, » Shuichi said. « Wait for me to return. »

The woman looked into Shuichi’s eyes. Those eyes were calm, but they held something she had never seen before—not pity, not sympathy, but a very cold, very certain determination.

She didn’t know what this man intended to do, but she nodded.

Shuichi turned and walked out of the medical station.

The sunlight shone on him ; he squinted his eyes, and then walked toward the center of the Village.
