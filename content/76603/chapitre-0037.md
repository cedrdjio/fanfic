# Chapitre 37 — Cloud Ninja

Saturday. This was the fifth day since Shuichi began digesting Sakumo Hatake’s combat experience.

The burning sensation in his palms had mostly faded, leaving only a faint, lingering warmth.

Close-quarters combat and opponent analysis were basically integrated, and terrain utilization was gradually stabilizing through daily practice.

However, battlefield intuition remained sealed ; it required a stronger opponent in actual combat to trigger.

Shuichi walked along the upper reaches of the Naka River, turned north at the stone bridge, and passed through the pine forest at the edge of the Training Ground.

About two hundred steps into the pine forest, the hairs on the back of his neck stood up.

It wasn’t intuition. It was Sakumo’s combat experience at work. Shuichi’s hearing caught an abnormal sound about forty steps behind him.

It wasn’t the wind, nor the call of a bird ; it was the sound of pine needles being slightly compressed.

It was very faint—if not for the fact that Sakumo’s experience had tripled the alertness of Shuichi’s senses, he wouldn’t have heard it at all.

Someone was following him.

Shuichi did not turn around. His pace remained unchanged, his breathing steady, the angle of his shoulders fixed.

The biggest taboo when being followed is revealing that you have noticed.

Once the other party knows you’ve detected them, they will change tactics—either accelerate their attack or retreat. Shuichi wanted neither. He needed to lead them to a secluded place.

The Forest of Death.

Shuichi continued walking, his pace identical to usual.

He followed the path through the pine forest toward the northeast, passed through a thicket, and crossed a dry gully.

The follower maintained a distance of about forty steps, their movement rhythm very steady—not like a top-tier stealth expert, but not a rookie either. Likely at the Chunin level.

Shuichi calculated the route in his mind. It was about half a mile to the western entrance of the Forest of Death from his current position. He needed to maintain normalcy for this half-mile without letting the other party suspect anything.

He walked past the pine forest, across the grass at the edge of the Training Ground, and into the entrance of the Forest of Death.

The canopy closed overhead, and the light dimmed. Shuichi walked deeper along a route he was familiar with, passing through two pine groves, crossing a small stream, and arriving at the clearing surrounded by pine trees.

This was deep enough. There wouldn’t be anyone else within half a mile.

Shuichi stopped in the center of the clearing and turned around.

« Come out. »

His voice wasn’t loud, but it carried far in the quiet pine forest.

It was quiet behind the bushes for about three seconds. Then, a person walked out.

A male, about one meter eighty-five tall, with a sturdy build. Dark skin, white hair tied into short braids, thick lips, and a broad nose. He wore dark gray Ninja gear, with two Ninja tool pouches hanging at his waist. The forehead protector on his head bore the mark of Iwagakure.

When Shuichi saw that forehead protector, he understood seventy or eighty percent of the situation.

Dark skin, white hair, sturdy build—this was the typical appearance of a Ninja from Kumogakure. Kumogakure was in the Land of Lightning, where residents tended to have darker skin. Iwagakure was in the Land of Earth, where residents had lighter skin. A dark-skinned, white-haired person wearing an Iwagakure forehead protector was like a cat wearing a dog collar ; anyone could see it.

This wasn’t a disguise error. It was intentional.

Kumogakure wanted to spy on Konoha, but didn’t want Konoha to know it was Kumogakure doing the spying. So they sent a Ninja wearing an Iwagakure forehead protector. If caught, Konoha would think Iwagakure did it, and Kumogakure could clear its name. But this disguise was so crude that any experienced Ninja could see through it at a glance—why deliberately let people see through it ?

Because even if seen through, it didn’t matter.

Even if Konoha knew this person was a Kumogakure Ninja, there was no direct evidence. The forehead protector was Iwagakure’s ; Kumogakure could say « It has nothing to do with us, » and Iwagakure could say « We never sent this person. » Neither side would admit it, and Konoha couldn’t get any diplomatic leverage. Finding a plausible excuse—believe it or not, I said it anyway. You can’t do anything about it.

Very shameless. But very Kumogakure.

As for the objective, the news of Sakumo’s suicide had only spread three days ago. Other Ninja villages would be very sensitive to Konoha losing an Kage-level powerhouse. Kumogakure sending someone to confirm if Sakumo was truly dead was perfectly reasonable.

Shuichi didn’t voice these deductions. He just watched the cloud Ninja.

The cloud Ninja looked at Shuichi, the expression on his face not one of tension, but amusement.

« Yo, » the cloud Ninja said, his tone as relaxed as if he had met an acquaintance on the road. « You actually discovered me, kid ? »

Shuichi didn’t speak.

The cloud Ninja crossed his arms over his chest, looking down at Shuichi. At one meter eighty-five versus one meter thirty, he had to look down to meet Shuichi’s eyes. There was no vigilance in his gaze, only a condescending contempt.

« But it doesn’t matter, » the cloud Ninja said, the corners of his mouth curling up. « Soon, no one will know I was ever here. »

A Chunin saying this to an eight-year-old child wasn’t a threat ; it was a statement of fact. In his view, killing a student who hadn’t even graduated from the Ninja Academy was no different from stepping on an ant.

Shuichi watched him.

Center of gravity shifted back, defensive stance. Arms crossed, inclined toward taijutsu, no habit of drawing weapons. Ninja tool pouch on the right side of his waist, right-handed. Loose standing posture, threat assessment of Shuichi is zero.

« Who are you ? » Shuichi asked.

« That’s none of your business. » The cloud Ninja uncrossed his arms and reached his right hand toward the Ninja tool pouch at his waist. « I didn’t want to waste time, but since you’ve found me— »

He drew a Kunai and rushed toward Shuichi.

The speed wasn’t slow. A full-speed dash from a Chunin could cover ten steps in about two seconds. For an eight-year-old child, this speed was lethal enough.

Shuichi didn’t move.

He was waiting.

The cloud Ninja’s charge route was a straight line, no changes in direction. Kunai held in his right hand, blade facing down—this was a hacking posture, not a stabbing one. His center of gravity had shifted too far forward during the charge ; if he changed direction suddenly, he would lose his balance.

Two steps.

Shuichi stepped half a step to the left.

The cloud Ninja’s Kunai slashed down from the right, the blade only three inches from Shuichi’s shoulder. But Shuichi was no longer in his original position. As his left foot stepped out, his right hand drew a Kunai from his pocket, and the Kunai sliced diagonally into the outer side of the cloud Ninja’s right arm.

Entry angle thirty-seven degrees, from bottom to top.

The Kunai grazed the cloud Ninja’s right arm, slicing through his Ninja gear and skin. It wasn’t deep, but blood immediately welled up. The cloud Ninja’s right hand went numb, and he almost dropped the Kunai.

He paused for a moment.

It wasn’t the pain that made him pause. It was that he hadn’t seen clearly when Shuichi had drawn the Kunai.

The cloud Ninja retreated two steps, gripping the Kunai tightly with his right hand. Blood flowed from the wound on his arm, dripping down the sleeve of his Ninja gear. He looked down at the wound—it wasn’t deep, but the position was tricky, cutting right into the joint of the muscle ; using his right arm would hurt.

« You— »

Shuichi stepped forward.

The cloud Ninja raised his Kunai to block, but Shuichi’s Kunai didn’t slash head-on. The Kunai slashed diagonally from the side, cutting into the left side of the cloud Ninja’s waist. The cloud Ninja dodged half a step to the right, avoiding the cut, but as he dodged, Shuichi’s Kunai reversed direction—changing from a diagonal side cut to a horizontal swipe, the blade grazing the back of the cloud Ninja’s left hand.

Immediately following up with a change of move after the entry, no hesitation.

A bloodstain appeared on the back of the cloud Ninja’s left hand. He gritted his teeth and kicked his right foot toward Shuichi’s abdomen—a Chunin’s taijutsu counterattack, with no lack of power or speed.

Shuichi stepped back, avoiding the kick. As he retreated, he thrust the Kunai forward, the tip grazing the inner side of the cloud Ninja’s kicking right calf.

Retreating with the blade, retreating with steps ; advancing with the blade, advancing with steps.

There was an extra wound on the cloud Ninja’s calf. He stumbled and fell to one knee.

From the first cut until now, about five seconds. Three cuts. Right arm, back of the left hand, right calf. Every cut avoided fatal points, but every cut struck a position that affected combat ability. The wound on his right arm reduced his grip strength, the wound on the back of his left hand slowed his hand sign speed, and the wound on his calf halved his mobility.

The cloud Ninja knelt on the ground, looking up at Shuichi. For the first time, true fear appeared in his eyes.

It wasn’t fear of the wounds. It was fear of Shuichi’s swordsmanship. The angle, timing, and position of every cut from this eight-year-old child were as precise as if executing a preset program. No wasted movements, no hesitation, no panic. Every cut hit exactly the most effective spot.

This wasn’t a judgment a child could make. This was the precision only a battle-hardened Ninja could possess.

« You... what exactly are you ? » The cloud Ninja’s voice had changed, from the earlier contempt to trembling.

Shuichi stood before him, looking down at him.

One meter thirty versus one meter eighty-five. But at this moment, it was the cloud Ninja who was kneeling.

A flicker of the desire to survive flashed in the cloud Ninja’s eyes. Although he hadn’t planned on returning alive when he came to Konoha to spy, failing to gather intelligence and instead being defeated by a child created a massive psychological gap.

Shuichi did not give him a chance to beg for mercy.

The Kunai reversed after the cut, swiping horizontally across the cloud Ninja’s throat.

Blood sprayed from the throat wound. The cloud Ninja’s eyes widened, his mouth opened, but he couldn’t make a sound. His body swayed twice, then fell forward, crashing onto the pine needles.

Shuichi stood in place, looking at the cloud Ninja’s corpse.

His breathing was steady. No acceleration, no disorder.

Shuichi crouched down and pressed his right hand onto the back of the cloud Ninja’s head.

Perception expanded. The life signal in the cloud Ninja’s body hadn’t completely vanished, and neural pathways were still functioning. Shuichi didn’t Plunder combat experience ; a Chunin’s combat experience wasn’t of much value to him. He was plundering something else.

Lightning Release Nature Transformation.

The cloud Ninja was a Ninja from Kumogakure ; Lightning Release was his primary nature. His Chakra carried the complete Lightning Release Nature transformation—how to imbue Chakra with the nature of lightning, how to guide Lightning Release Chakra flow within the meridians, how to condense Lightning Release on the body surface.

Plunder.

A sensation different from before came from the palm of Shuichi’s right hand. It wasn’t the warm Vitality, nor the scorching combat experience ; it was a tingling sensation, as if a faint electric current was flowing from the cloud Ninja’s body into Shuichi’s right hand.

About five seconds. Shuichi withdrew his right hand, and a mass of something had appeared in his palm. It wasn’t fire, nor was it warm ; it was a sphere with a sense of electric arcs, pulsing slightly in his palm. Lightning Release Nature transformation.

Shuichi compressed it and locked it within the mental barrier in his right palm. Lightning Release Nature transformation was much simpler than combat experience ; the digestion cycle shouldn’t be too long.

Shuichi stood up and cleaned up the scene.

He searched the cloud Ninja for Ninja tool pouches, a forehead protector, and a cloth bag containing a small amount of dry rations. The tool pouch contained a few Kunai and Shuriken, the forehead protector bore the Iwagakure mark, and the cloth bag contained no valuable intelligence. Shuichi put away the forehead protector and tool pouch, then used a Kunai to dig a shallow pit in the ground, dragged the cloud Ninja’s corpse into it, and covered it with pine needles and dirt. It wasn’t a perfect disposal, but it was enough to last a few days. By the time the corpse decomposed, even if discovered, the identity wouldn’t be identifiable.

Shuichi covered the bloodstains at the scene with pine needles and checked the surrounding environment again. Aside from the traces of pine needles being disturbed, there were no other abnormalities on the ground.

He walked back along the route he had come, using his feet to nudge the pine needles he had stepped on back into place as he walked.

By the time he walked out of the Forest of Death, it was already getting dark. Shuichi passed through the pine forest, crossed the Training Ground, and walked home along the Naka River.

The tingling sensation in his palm remained. The Lightning Release Nature transformation pulsed quietly within the mental barrier, waiting to be digested.

He had gained two things today.

First, he had verified Sakumo’s combat experience in actual combat. Close-quarters combat, opponent analysis, timing of changing moves—all were effective. Furthermore, he confirmed that he could crush a Chunin without activating the Sharingan. The combination of Sakumo’s experience and Shuichi’s own strength gave him an overwhelming advantage against a Chunin in combat.

Second, the Lightning Release Nature transformation. An unexpected joy. Shuichi had originally planned to wait until the Third Great Ninja War to obtain the Lightning and Wind attributes through Plunder ; he hadn’t expected a cloud Ninja delivered to his door to solve the Lightning attribute. After digesting it, Shuichi would possess two mastered attributes—Fire and Lightning.
