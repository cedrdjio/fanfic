# Chapitre 68 — One Month Later

One month later.

The Hyuga Clan’s investigation into the two murder cases had lasted four weeks.

Hyuga Hiashi stood in the study of the clan head’s mansion, looking at the investigation reports piled on the desk.

« Still no leads ? » he asked.

« None. » The branch family member next to him lowered his head and reported, « There is no Chakra residue at the scene, no traces of Ninjutsu. The knife wound is the only clue, but the wound itself has no distinctive features—an ordinary short blade, ordinary sword technique. »

« What about witnesses ? »

« The residents on both sides of the alley were asleep that night ; no one saw the killer. The night patrol squad only discovered the bodies after smelling blood, at least three hours after the time of death. »

« What about intelligence from other Villages ? »

« The Anbu checked ; no suspicious persons entered Konoha that night. The intelligence network in the surrounding Villages also found no anomalies. »

Hyuga Hiashi was silent for a while.

The killer seemed to have vanished into thin air.

One strike to kill, leaving no traces, no witnesses, no Chakra residue.

This method did not seem like something an ordinary Ninja could achieve.

But the Hyuga Clan had already tried their best and still found nothing.

« Continue the investigation. » Hyuga Hiashi said, « But don’t waste too much manpower. »

« Clan Head, should we give up ? »

« It’s not giving up. » Hyuga Hiashi looked at the sky outside the window, « It’s waiting. »

The killer would not remain untraceable forever. One day, a clue would appear.

The face of the Hyuga Clan could not just be stepped on like this.

But he had already accepted the fact that the investigation had yielded no results.

---

Shuichi returned home after school and walked into the courtyard.

Xiaobai was lying at the kitchen door, ears pricked up. Upon hearing Shuichi’s footsteps, it immediately stood up, wagging its tail to greet him.

This month, Xiaobai had completely integrated into the family.

It now treated Minako as family.

When Shuichi was not home, Xiaobai would lie at the door keeping watch, or when Minako went out, it would follow wherever she went.

Shuichi walked into the kitchen and saw Minako chopping vegetables.

« Back ? » Minako looked up at him, « Dinner will take a while, go rest first. »

« Yeah. »

Shuichi walked out of the kitchen. Xiaobai followed him for a few steps, then stopped, looked back at Minako in the kitchen, hesitated for a moment, and finally turned back to lie down at the kitchen door.

Shuichi watched Xiaobai’s actions and had a judgment in his heart.

He had delayed for a month just to let Xiaobai and Minako build true trust. Now Xiaobai treated Minako as someone who needed protection, not just a regular feeder.

Shuichi walked into his room and closed the door.

He took a notebook out of the drawer and turned to the page recording abilities.

This month, he had successively Plundered some animal abilities in the Forest of Death, specially prepared for Xiaobai.

Bear’s resilience.

Snake’s thermal sensing.

Cat’s reaction speed.

Cheetah’s leg explosive power.

Badger’s bite force.

Five abilities, all designed around one scenario.

Someone breaks in, Xiaobai senses them before they appear, knocks Minako away, bites the intruder, and drags them until Shuichi returns.

And these were just the basics. After Xiaobai finished consuming them, Shuichi would also give it Chakra and teach it Ninjutsu.

Shuichi closed the notebook and walked out of the room.

He walked to the courtyard, squatted down, and reached out to pat Xiaobai’s head.

Xiaobai raised its head and looked at him.

Shuichi brought Xiaobai into his room and closed the door.

« Come here. »

Xiaobai walked to Shuichi and squatted down.

Shuichi extended his right hand, pressed it on Xiaobai’s head, and even stroked it.

Bestowal began.

The abilities were quickly input into Xiaobai’s body ; it only took a few seconds to complete.

Xiaobai’s body trembled violently for a few seconds.

Then it stood up, no longer wagging its tail, but stood in place, looking into Shuichi’s eyes.

Shuichi looked at Xiaobai’s gaze and knew it understood.

It understood who this person wanted it to protect, and why.

Shuichi extended his right hand again and pressed it on Xiaobai’s head once more.

Bestowal began.

Concept of Wisdom was injected into Xiaobai’s body.

This wisdom was slightly weaker than the one given to Lue initially, but it was enough for it to judge friend or foe, identify disguises, and make independent decisions when Shuichi was away. Shuichi’s mental power was now much stronger than when he had strengthened Lue back then ; this level of bestowal was no longer a problem.

Xiaobai’s body trembled again for a few seconds.

Bestowal complete.

Shuichi withdrew his hand.

Xiaobai stood in place, its gaze becoming clear. It looked at Shuichi, then lowered its head and nudged Shuichi’s knee with its nose.

Shuichi stroked Xiaobai’s head and said, « These are just basic abilities. Once you digest these, I’ll give you stronger ones later. »

Xiaobai raised its head and gave a soft « Hmm ».

It couldn’t speak yet, but it could already understand Shuichi’s meaning.

Although Xiaobai’s pedigree wasn’t great, its foundation wasn’t bad ; it was already smarter than an ordinary domestic dog. Now, with the Concept of Wisdom added, it could understand complex commands.

Shuichi judged that, according to Lue’s initial digestion process, Xiaobai would be able to speak in about two or three months.

« Go out. » Shuichi opened the door.

Xiaobai walked out of the room, returned to the kitchen door, and lay down, continuing to watch over Minako.

---

At night.

Shuichi sat in his room.

Fang was squatting in the corner of the windowsill, two worker bees had just flown back from outside, their antennae shaking at Fang for a while.

Fang raised its head, its voice very soft : « The target has started acting. »

Shuichi put down the notebook in his hand.

During this period, the insect swarm intelligence network had been completely laid out. The mixed insect swarm that Fang coordinated with pheromones covered the Hokage Building, Anbu dormitories, and archive passages.

A week ago, Lue had coordinated with the insect swarm to lock onto an Anbu codenamed « Crow ».

This Anbu patrolled the seal storage area alone every Wednesday late at night ; he was someone who knew the location of the Scroll of Seals.

« Where is he now ? » Shuichi asked.

Fang let the worker bees fly out to confirm.

Half a minute later, the worker bees flew back, their antennae shaking for a while.

Fang said : « Just left the Anbu dormitory, heading towards the Hokage Building. »

Shuichi stood up and walked to the window.

Lue flew down from the plum tree in the courtyard and landed on the windowsill.

« Should I follow ? » Lue asked.

« No need. » Shuichi said, « This time, I’ll go myself. »

Lue looked at Shuichi and didn’t ask further.

Shuichi changed into a black outfit, put on a mask, and completely concealed his presence.

He climbed out the window and walked towards the Hokage Building.

The night was very dark, and there was no one on the street.

Shuichi stopped 300 meters outside the Hokage Building and activated the Byakugan.

A pale blue light glowed deep in his pupils, his vision penetrating the walls of the Hokage Building.

He saw the Anbu codenamed « Crow », medium build, wearing a cat mask, walking through the corridor towards the underground area.

Shuichi deactivated the Byakugan and activated his Mangekyo left eye.

Magatsuhi rotated, and his figure began to turn intangible.

His body became something that didn’t exist. Footsteps disappeared, breathing disappeared, Chakra fluctuations disappeared. Not even the air would be disturbed by his movement.

Shuichi walked towards the Hokage Building.
