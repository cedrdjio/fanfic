# Chapitre 32 — Kakashi Hatake

On Saturday, Shuichi entered the Forest of Death as usual.

Lue scanned the forest from high above using Infrared Sensing, discovering a heat source moving on the east side of the pine forest. It was large and slow-moving, likely a bear. Shuichi walked in the direction indicated by Lue, activating his Sharingan, the two tomoe spinning within his pupils.

The bear was digging at the pine-needle-covered ground, likely searching for food. Shuichi approached from behind and plunged his Kunai through the gaps in its ribs, piercing its heart. The bear did not struggle as it collapsed ; its four-hundred-pound body slammed onto the pine needles, the dull thud echoing twice through the forest.

Shuichi Plundered the bear’s Vitality, keeping seventy percent for himself and sharing thirty percent with Lue. Lue flew down from the sky, landed on a rock, and received the Vitality from Shuichi’s right hand. Its body trembled slightly for three seconds, its wings half-spreading before folding back in.

« Full ? » Shuichi asked.

« Mm. » Lue uttered a clear syllable.

It was smoother than yesterday. Over the past two days, Lue had become able to steadily say a few simple words : « Mm, » « Shuichi, » « Full, » « Hungry, » « Come, » « Go. » The pronunciation was still not quite natural, sounding as if it were reciting characters, but every syllable could be heard clearly.

Shuichi covered the bear’s carcass with pine branches, then stood up and brushed the dirt off his hands. Lue took flight from the rock, circled half a turn above the pine forest, and landed back on Shuichi’s shoulder.

« Let’s finish early today and head back, » Shuichi said.

Lue nodded.

On Sunday, Shuichi practiced controlling his fire-attribute Chakra in the backyard.

He gathered a small sphere of Chakra in his palm, attempting to imbue it with the nature of fire. The Chakra rotated in his palm, feeling warm, but it had not yet reached the point of ignition. The foundation of Fire Release was to imbue Chakra with the nature of fire and then form flames through shape transformation. Shuichi was still on the first step : imbuing the Chakra with the nature of fire.

He sat under the plum tree for the entire morning, rotating the Chakra in his palm about fifty times, each time attempting to raise its temperature by just a little bit. By noon, he could feel a distinct heat from the Chakra in his palm, but it was still not enough to ignite a leaf.

Lue perched on a plum branch, watching him practice. Occasionally, it let out a low chirp, trying to understand what Shuichi was doing.

« Fire, » Lue said.

« Mm, fire attribute, » Shuichi said.

« Hard. »

« Practice slowly. »

Lue tilted its head, watching Shuichi’s palm, its pupils switching between night-vision mode and normal mode. It seemed to be observing the changes in the Chakra’s temperature.

Shuichi continued practicing throughout the afternoon. By evening, the heat of the Chakra in his palm was slightly higher than it had been at noon, but still not enough to ignite anything. Fire attribute was the Uchiha Clan’s natural affinity, but Shuichi’s Chakra reserves were not yet large enough, and the precision of attribute control required time to accumulate.

« Hot, » Lue said.

« Mm. » Shuichi retracted his Chakra, stood up, and stretched his limbs. « But it’s still far from enough. »

On Monday morning, when Shuichi left, Lue was still sleeping on the plum tree.

It had been practicing language for the past two days, which consumed more mental energy than usual. Shuichi did not wake it, walking alone toward the Ninja Academy along the Naka River. The morning mist had not yet dispersed, and the water vapor from the river carried a chilly air.

The affair regarding the White Fang was still fermenting.

As Shuichi walked past the commercial street, he heard people discussing something in front of the Ninja tool shop. Their tone was the same as it had been the past few days, filled with disdain and anger. Sakumo Hatake’s « disgrace » had become a staple topic of conversation for the villagers during their spare time.

Shuichi did not stop, walking right past them.

Kakashi Hatake stood at the edge of the Training Ground on the north side of the Village, holding a Kunai in his hand.

He was seven years old this year, having graduated from the Ninja Academy at five, and was currently a Genin. His hair was silver-white, and he wore a black mask over his face. The gaze in his exposed eye was filled with confusion, lacking the innocence a seven-year-old child should have.

There was no one else on the Training Ground. Kakashi was accustomed to practicing alone in the early morning to avoid the gaze of other Ninjas. Ever since the White Fang incident, his situation in the Village had changed. Previously, he had been « the son of the White Fang, » a genius, an elite. But now, he was « the son of a disgrace » ; he would be looked at sideways when walking on the street, and people would gossip about him when he went to the Ninja tool shop to buy equipment.

Kakashi did not pay attention to those discussions. He simply continued to practice.

The Kunai flew from his hand and landed on the target post twenty meters away. It was off by three inches. Kakashi frowned, walked over to retrieve the Kunai, returned to his original position, and threw it again. This time it was off by one inch. He retrieved it again and threw it once more. On the third attempt, the Kunai landed right in the center of the target post.

Kakashi was not satisfied. He continued to throw—the fourth, fifth, and sixth times, each landing in the center of the target post. But he did not stop, continuing to throw for the seventh time.

He was using repetitive practice to suppress the thoughts in his mind.

His father hadn’t left the house for days. Every morning when Kakashi returned home, he saw his father sitting on a wooden chair in the courtyard, his gaze fixed on the ground, motionless. His father’s face was also more gaunt than before, his cheekbones protruding and his eye sockets sunken. He didn’t speak, didn’t train, and didn’t do anything. He just sat there, staring blankly at the ground.

Kakashi did not know what his father was thinking. He did not ask. He simply continued to practice, continued to carry out missions, and continued to follow the Ninja Code.

Missions take priority. Companions come second. This is the first rule of the Ninja Code.

His father had violated this rule. His father chose to save his companions and abandon the mission. Then, his father was berated by the entire Village and called a « disgrace. » Kakashi was looked at sideways on the street and gossiped about at the Ninja tool shop.

But Kakashi did not hate his father.

He was wondering if his father saving his companions was right or wrong.

The school said missions take priority. The villagers said his father was a disgrace. But Kakashi remembered what his father had said before : « A Ninja is not a machine. A Ninja’s power exists to protect those who are important. »

His father saving his companions was protecting those who were important. This was consistent with what his father had said before.

But the mission had failed. The Village had lost a line of intelligence. The villagers cursed his father as a disgrace.

Kakashi did not know how to judge it. He just continued to practice, continued to follow the code. He was thinking that if he followed the code, completed missions, and proved himself to be a qualified Ninja, perhaps the villagers would view his father differently. Perhaps they would think that if the White Fang’s son was a qualified Ninja, then the White Fang’s choice might not have been entirely wrong.

Kakashi threw the eighth Kunai. It landed right in the center of the target post.

He continued to throw the ninth.

Shuichi rarely saw Kakashi ; he had only seen him once from afar on the commercial street—silver-white hair, a mask on his face, cold as a block of ice.

After school, Shuichi walked home along the Naka River. Lue circled above the riverbank, and upon seeing Shuichi, flew down and landed on his shoulder.

« What did you learn today ? » Lue asked.

Shuichi glanced at Lue. Lue was asking him using complete sentences. Its language ability had improved rapidly over the past two days.

« Chakra control, » Shuichi said.

« Is it hard ? »

« It’s okay. »

Lue tilted its head, watching him, its pupils switching between night-vision mode and normal mode. It was observing Shuichi’s expression, as if judging whether his answer was true.

« You are lying, » Lue said.

Shuichi did not speak. Lue’s concept of intelligence included an emotion recognition module ; it could judge a person’s true state by observing their expressions and tone. Shuichi was lying, and Lue could tell.

« I cannot show my true level at school, » Shuichi said.

« Why ? »

« I will be noticed. »

Lue thought for a moment, then nodded. It understood.

Shuichi walked upstream along the Naka River with Lue perched on his shoulder. Lue had been practicing language for the past two days and was improving rapidly. The digestion speed of the Monkey King’s intelligence concept was faster than Shuichi had anticipated ; it was possible that Lue’s physical foundation far exceeded that of a normal hawk, and its neural capacity was sufficient.

« Lue, » Shuichi said.

« Mm ? »

« You don’t need to speak usually. »

« Why ? »

« You will be noticed by people. »

Lue thought for a moment, then nodded. It understood.

When Shuichi returned home, Minako was cooking in the kitchen.

Smoke rose from the chimney, and the aroma of food wafted through the courtyard. Shuichi walked into the house, placed his school bag on the tatami, and went to the water vat to wash his hands. Lue flew off his shoulder and landed on a plum tree branch.

« It’s time to wash your hands and eat, » Minako’s voice came from the kitchen.

« Mm. »

Shuichi finished washing his hands and walked into the kitchen. Minako was busy in front of the stove, something was boiling in the pot, and steam was escaping from the edges of the lid.

« How was school today ? » Minako asked.

« It was okay. »

« What do you want to eat tomorrow ? »

« Anything is fine. »

Minako smiled and continued cooking. Shuichi stood at the kitchen doorway, watching her, a heavy weight in his gaze.
