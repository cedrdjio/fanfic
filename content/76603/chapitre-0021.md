# Chapitre 21 — Two Students

On Sunday, Shuichi did not go to the Forest of Death.

The boar’s Vitality was still slowly permeating his body. From yesterday afternoon until now, the buzzing warmth deep within his bones had not completely faded. The absorption process was much longer than that of the Red Fox. Shuichi estimated that it would take two to three days to fully absorb it ; during this period, his body was in a continuous state of assimilation and was not suitable for high-intensity hunting.

He was practicing shuriken in the backyard under the plum tree. The target was a fist-sized circle drawn in chalk on the trunk of the plum tree, and he threw three shuriken in turns. A six-year-old’s palm size made gripping the shuriken difficult, and there would always be deviations in the rotation axis of the throws. Shuichi threw twenty rounds ; sixty percent hit inside the circle, thirty percent hit the edge, and ten percent veered off onto the bark.

Red Fox crouched on a high branch of the plum tree. Whenever he veered onto the bark, it would let out a short, low chirp. This was the instinctive reaction of a hawk when it noticed a movement trajectory that deviated from expectations.

« You are quite strict. » Shuichi retrieved the shuriken and looked up at Red Fox.

Footsteps came from outside the courtyard wall. The steps were light and quick, stopping every few steps as if looking for someone. Shuichi put the shuriken in his hand back into his ninja tool pouch.

Obito poked his head out from the corner of the courtyard wall.

« Brother Shuichi ! »

When he ran into the courtyard, he tripped over the threshold with his left foot and staggered forward two steps before standing still. Five-year-old Obito was a little taller than last year, but he was still thin. A small tuft of hair on the left side was sticking up, and his hair was messier than the last time he saw him, as if he had trimmed it himself with scissors. He was still wearing that wooden headband, and the crooked 'Fourth Hokage' handwriting on it was a bit blurred by sweat.

« What’s wrong ? » Shuichi walked to the edge of the corridor and sat down, signaling for Obito to come over and sit as well.

Obito did not sit. He stood in front of Shuichi, both hands clenched into fists, his lips pressed tightly together as if organizing his words. Shuichi did not rush him. Obito’s face turned redder and redder, and then he suddenly lowered his head, his voice squeezed out from his throat.

« Brother Shuichi. Can you teach me ? »

« Teach you what ? »

« It’s just... » Obito raised his head, his eyes filled with a seriousness that a five-year-old child shouldn’t have, « Those things you learn at school. Taijutsu. How to stand firm, how to push people. »

Shuichi looked at him without answering immediately. Obito was not yet at the enrollment age. According to Konoha’s regulations, the minimum enrollment age for the Ninja Academy was six years old, and Obito could only apply next spring. He was asking this now, not because he wanted to preview the textbook in advance.

« Did you get bullied again ? » Shuichi’s voice was very calm.

Obito’s shoulders stiffened. Then he shook his head quickly.

« No ! I won ! » He said, then paused, his voice dropping, « But I only won against one. They had four. »

Shuichi pieced the scene together in his mind. The four Uchiha children were likely the same group that had bullied Obito in the square last time. This time, Obito had fought back, beaten one, and been beaten by the other three. Then he ran out and spent the whole morning looking for Shuichi in the Uchiha Clan Grounds.

« Sit. » Shuichi patted the floor of the corridor.

Obito sat down this time. He sat cross-legged opposite Shuichi, his hands on his knees, his eyes fixed on Shuichi.

« What do you want to learn ? » Shuichi asked.

« Everything. »

Shuichi shook his head slightly. « Start with the stance. »

He stood up and walked to the middle of the courtyard, his feet shoulder-width apart, his center of gravity lowered, and his knees slightly bent. This was the basic stance that Teacher Mitomon had taught in the Taijutsu class the day before yesterday. Shuichi did not add any details he had improved himself—no Chakra infusion, no subtle adjustments to the center of gravity ; it was just the most standard beginner version.

« Follow me. »

Obito jumped off the corridor and stood next to Shuichi. He spread his feet, then looked down to see the difference between his stance and Shuichi’s ; his feet were spread too wide, and his center of gravity was entirely on his heels. He pulled one foot back, and his center of gravity shifted to the other side. After adjusting back and forth three or four times, he finally found a roughly stable position.

« Bend your knees a bit more. Not too deep. »

Obito bent his knees. A five-year-old child’s leg muscles couldn’t last long, and his thighs began to tremble slightly. Shuichi stood beside him without correcting any more details. The first step in teaching a child is not to pursue perfection, but to let them find the feeling of their center of gravity first.

« Hold it. Three minutes. »

Obito bit his lip and persisted for two minutes. At the third minute, his left leg was clearly shaking, and his body was tilting to the right unconsciously. Shuichi reached out to support his left shoulder and pushed him back to the correct position.

« One more minute. »

Obito held on for three minutes. When Shuichi called stop, Obito squatted down directly, rubbing his thighs and sniffing.

« The stance is the most boring part. » Shuichi sat back down, « But when pushing people, whoever wobbles first loses. The day before yesterday, there was someone at school half a head taller than me, and he couldn’t push me over because he couldn’t stand firmly. »

Obito looked up. « Was it Genji ? »

Shuichi was stunned for a moment. « How do you know Genji ? »

« Genji is the cousin of Oshima from the blacksmith shop. » Obito said, « Oshima from the blacksmith shop is neighbors with Tetsuya, and Tetsuya is your classmate. »

« When did you find that out ? »

« Just... sometimes I go to the school gate to watch you finish school. » Obito’s voice grew quieter, « Every time Tetsuya comes out, he shouts 'Shuichi is second place'. »

Shuichi was silent.

Obito was isolated by his peers in the Uchiha Clan Grounds. His circle of peers was reduced to only Shuichi. But Shuichi was a year older than him and was already enrolled, so he was not active in the Uchiha Clan Grounds. So Obito ran to the school gate to watch him finish school, wanting to see if the Brother Shuichi who had once helped him out of a predicament was still there.

« How many times have you come ? »

« Three times. Once I saw Tetsuya pulling you to talk at the school gate, so I didn’t call you. »

Shuichi sighed in his heart. Obito was different from Tetsuya. Tetsuya was cheerful, had many friends, and admired Shuichi but did not rely on him. Obito had no other friends. He only had Shuichi in the Uchiha Clan Grounds, while Tetsuya had a group of classmates at school, so the attitudes of these two people towards Shuichi were on completely different levels.

« Call me directly next time. » Shuichi said.

Obito was stunned for a moment, then nodded vigorously.

Footsteps sounded outside the courtyard wall again. This time they were much heavier than Obito’s footsteps, and the pace was faster. Shuichi could almost judge who it was from the weight of the steps.

Tetsuya pushed open the courtyard gate and poked half his body in. « Shuichi, huh ? Who are you ? »

Obito stood up and leaned half a step towards Shuichi. « Uchiha Obito. »

« I’m Tetsuya ! Shuichi’s classmate ! » Tetsuya walked into the courtyard, his eyes scanning Obito, from the crooked 'Fourth Hokage' headband down to the gray marks on his knees from kneeling. « What’s wrong with your knees ? »

« Practicing the stance. »

« Stance ? You’re practicing the stance too ? » Tetsuya’s eyes lit up, « I practiced at my front door for a whole day ! I stood from yesterday morning until yesterday noon ! My dad said not to stand next to the anvil and block the way, so I went to the backyard to stand ! »

Shuichi looked at Tetsuya. He had indeed told Tetsuya to practice the stance yesterday morning, but he didn’t expect Tetsuya to really stand for a whole morning.

« How long can you stand now ? » Shuichi asked.

« I don’t know. I stood until my legs went numb and then I stopped. Probably... a long time ? »

« Standing until your legs go numb is not a good thing. » Shuichi said, « It means your center of gravity is in the wrong place, cutting off blood flow. Your center of gravity is too far back, and you’re putting all your weight on your heels. »

Tetsuya opened his mouth, then scratched his head. « Then why didn’t you say so earlier ? »

« You ran too fast yesterday. »

Obito couldn’t help but laugh from the side. Tetsuya turned to look at him, and Obito quickly held it in.

Shuichi stood up and walked to the middle of the courtyard. « Both of you, come and stand. Basic stance, let me see it one more time. »

Tetsuya and Obito stood side by side. Shuichi first looked at Tetsuya ; his stance was a bit better than the day before yesterday, the foot spacing was correct, but his center of gravity was still too far back, with too much force on his heels. He pushed lightly on Tetsuya’s right knee, and Tetsuya wobbled before steadying himself.

« Move your center of gravity forward an inch. Distribute the weight evenly on the soles of your feet. Right now you’re using your heels, and your legs will go numb after a while. »

Then it was Obito’s turn ; his foot spacing was still a bit wide, but his center of gravity was more accurate than Tetsuya’s. He pushed on Obito’s left knee, and Obito didn’t move at all.

Tetsuya’s eyes widened. « How long has he been practicing ? Why can’t I push him ? »

« His center of gravity is forward. » Shuichi said, « Yours is back. »

Tetsuya frowned and looked at Obito, his eyes showing no hostility, only pure confusion. He had been forging iron in the blacksmith shop for four years and was much stronger than Obito. How could he not push a five-year-old ?

« The position of the center of gravity has nothing to do with strength. » Shuichi said, « Pushing people isn’t about strength ; it’s about the center of gravity. »

« Then if I move my center of gravity forward, can I beat Genji ? »

Shuichi thought for a moment. « You can beat Oshima. Genji needs something else. »

Tetsuya stood back in place, this time carefully adjusting the weight-bearing points of his feet. Obito stood beside him, his left knee beginning to tremble slightly again, but this time no one rushed him. Tetsuya glanced at Obito’s trembling knee and didn’t make fun of him.

The afternoon sun spilled through the gaps in the plum tree, covering the courtyard in a layer of dappled gold. Red Fox flew down from the plum branch and landed on Shuichi’s shoulder, tilting its head to watch the two figures, one tall and one short, struggling in the middle of the courtyard.

Shuichi sat leaning against the corridor pillar, his right palm open facing upward. The boar’s Vitality was still flowing slowly through his body, like an invisible underground river deep in the earth. The pupil power deep in his eyes was operating quietly, and the concept of Growth was slowly developing.

Smoke curled from the Uchiha Clan Grounds in the distance, and the roof of the Military Police Force duty station shimmered with the light of gray tiles in the noon sun. Obito shouted in the courtyard, « Tetsuya, don’t wobble ! If you wobble, I’ll wobble too ! » Tetsuya replied, « I didn’t wobble ! You were the one who wobbled first ! »

Shuichi closed his eyes.
