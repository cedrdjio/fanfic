# Chapitre 48 — Harvest

« This is the last one. »

Shuichi withdrew his right hand from the corpse of a Chunin. His mental energy felt slightly bloated ; after plundering the Chakra of over thirty Ninjas, the drain on his mental energy was not small.

As satisfying as the killing had been earlier, finding the corpses one by one to Plunder them afterwards was just as troublesome.

Shuichi stood in front of the administration building, looking at the corpses strewn across the street. From the medical station to the administration building, a three-hundred-meter path, the corpses marked the trajectory of his path like signposts. Some were killed by his blade, with a clean cut across their throats ; some were pierced by Lightning Release, leaving charred electric arc marks on their bodies ; others were severed by wind blades, their cut surfaces as smooth as tofu sliced by a knife.

Shuichi sighed.

« Sigh, why couldn’t you all just gather together and let me kill you ? »

He stood up and stretched, his bones making a slight popping sound.

Shuichi felt the growth of his Chakra.

It had roughly doubled. Although it wasn’t as terrifying as the dozens-of-fold increase during that month and a half in Tanzaku Town, for Shuichi’s current Chakra volume, doubling in just a single day was already an incredible feat.

Of course, the gains were not limited to Chakra. There were also the Ninjutsu and combat experience of the Jonin he had killed earlier.

Jounin Kuroda’s Water Release and short-blade combat experience ; Elite Jounin Touji’s Earth Release and Taijutsu ; Jounin Koji’s Wind Release and high-speed combat techniques ; Jounin Mayumi’s Sealing Technique and Genjutsu basics ; and Kusano’s Chakra Chains technique.

Although a Jonin’s Chakra volume was several times that of a Chunin, Chunin were everywhere, while Jonin outside of the Five Great Ninja Villages were pitifully rare. Therefore, absorbing a Jonin’s knowledge was the most cost-effective choice.

Shuichi quickly organized his newly acquired abilities in his mind.

Jounin Kuroda’s Water Release : Water Bullet Technique was a standard B-rank Water Release, with the advantage of fast hand seals and low consumption. Elite Jounin Touji’s Earth Release : Earth-Style Rampart was a defensive Earth Release that Shuichi already possessed, but Touji’s version was more refined than the one he had plundered from his bounty target, with a much higher Chakra utilization rate. Jounin Koji’s Wind Release : Wind Cutter was the long-range attack method Shuichi had used today, but Koji’s version could release three wind blades simultaneously, which was much stronger than the single wind blade Shuichi had figured out on his own.

The most valuable was Jounin Mayumi’s Sealing Technique.

Shuichi’s previous understanding of Sealing Techniques was limited to sealing scroll operations, but the Sealing Technique Jounin Mayumi had learned from the Uzumaki Clan was completely different. It was a technique that used Chakra to construct formula arrays to suppress and lock down the target’s Chakra pathway system. Although Mayumi had only learned the basics, even those basics gave Shuichi a fundamental cognitive framework for Sealing Techniques.

Currently, Shuichi could be said to have a basic understanding of Ninjutsu of all elemental attributes. Although he couldn’t combine the five elemental attributes to unleash more powerful effects like the Third Hokage, it still allowed him to handle various situations better. After all, having many skills never hurts.

At this moment, Ryo flew down from high above and landed steadily on Shuichi’s shoulder.

« How is it ? Are there any survivors nearby ? » Shuichi asked.

« No, those civilians and Ninjas have all fled far from Kusagakure. I still remember the escape directions of two Chunin. Do you need me to track them ? » Ryo said.

« There’s no need to waste time chasing them. While the news hasn’t spread yet, we’ll head to Hozuki Castle after settling that woman. »

« Alright. »

---

Shuichi returned to the medical station and walked through the curtain.

The woman was still lying on the bed in the same posture as before, curled up. She had heard all the sounds outside—screams, explosions, the sound of wind blades slicing—but she didn’t know exactly what had happened. She only knew that the man had gone out, and then those sounds had vanished one by one.

Finally, everything fell into silence.

That silence made her more uneasy than any of the previous sounds. Because it meant it was over.

Shuichi stood by the bed, looking at her.

« Can you walk ? »

« Yes, » the woman said, struggling to get out of bed, only managing to stand steadily by supporting herself against the wall. Her legs were trembling, not from fear, but from muscle atrophy caused by being bedridden for so long. The Uzumaki Bloodline had kept her alive, but it hadn’t kept her healthy.

« There are crutches in the lobby ; you can use them. Do you have anywhere you want to go now ? »

« N-No... » the woman said after pondering for a moment.

She was an orphan herself and had been imprisoned in Kusagakure for several years. Let alone having a place she wanted to go, she didn’t even know what the situation was in the outside world right now. She didn’t even know what year it was, whether there was still a war going on outside, or if anyone she knew was still alive.

« Alright then, come with me. »

After saying that, Shuichi turned and left the medical station.

After a while, the woman limped out on crutches, wearing a coat. The coat was taken from the two dead Ninjas in the waiting area ; it was a bit too large, but it could cover the bite marks on her arms. She pulled the sleeves all the way down, wanting to minimize her presence as much as possible so as not to cause trouble for Shuichi.

Shuichi glanced at her but said nothing.

The two of them walked like this. Shuichi’s target was a large nearby village that had inns.

There weren’t many villages around Kusagakure, but when Shuichi came in earlier, he had noticed a fairly large village about ten miles to the south. It was a major transportation hub in the southern Land of Grass, with many passing merchant caravans, inns, and shops.

Although some escapees from Kusagakure might flee there, they had absolutely never seen Shuichi. Because Shuichi had killed everyone in Kusagakure who had laid eyes on him.

This thought made even Shuichi feel a bit cold-blooded, but such was the reality. With no survivors, there were no witnesses. No one would know who had come to Kusagakure today, and no one would know what he looked like. The destruction of Kusagakure would remain a mystery.

On the way, the woman walked very slowly. Her body was too weak, and she had to stop to catch her breath every few steps. Shuichi didn’t rush her, nor did he help her. He simply slowed his pace, maintaining a distance of three paces ahead of her.

Ryo flew in the air, occasionally landing to rest. Its round black eyes watched the woman struggling behind them, tilting its head, but it didn’t speak.

They walked for about four hours.

By evening, Shuichi and the woman successfully arrived at the village.

As Shuichi had expected, people from Kusagakure had already fled here. In the teahouse at the village entrance sat several Ninjas wearing Kusagakure headbands, their faces pale. Some were trembling, while others were whispering. They were surrounded by some villagers with terrified expressions.

News of the « homicidal demon » had already spread.

Shuichi walked right past them, and no one noticed him.

He took the woman to the village’s only inn.

The innkeeper was a chubby middle-aged man. When he saw Shuichi and the woman enter, his gaze lingered on the woman’s pale face for a moment, but he didn’t ask questions. In a place like this, asking no questions was the best rule.

Shuichi requested two rooms, one for himself and one for the woman.

Dinner was a mixed porridge and pickles provided by the inn. The woman sat at the table, eating the porridge with her head down. She ate very slowly, but took every bite seriously. Shuichi noticed that she scraped even the last bit of porridge from the bottom of the bowl clean.

In Kusagakure’s medical station, she probably hadn’t had a proper meal in a very long time.

After dinner, the woman’s eyelids began to droop. Having dragged her weak body to walk for four hours, it was thanks to her Uzumaki Bloodline ; anyone else would have collapsed on the road long ago.

« Go to sleep, » Shuichi said.

The woman nodded, stood up, and wobbled into her room. After the door closed, Shuichi heard the slight creaking of the bed—she had lain down.

Shuichi waited in his own room for a while.

About twenty minutes later, Shuichi’s perception confirmed that the woman’s breathing had become even and deep.

Her nerves, which had been tense for several years, finally relaxed. Kusagakure was gone, and she no longer had to be used as a tool. Fatigue rushed over her like a tide, completely overwhelming her.

Shuichi pushed open the door to the woman’s room and walked in.

There was only a single oil lamp in the room, casting a dim light. The woman was lying on her side on the bed, curled up, her breathing steady. She was still wearing her coat, and her crutches leaned against the bed.

Shuichi stood by the bed, looking at the sleeping woman, and slowly extended his right hand.

It was time to collect his reward.

The process went very smoothly. The woman had no guard up against Shuichi, or rather, she couldn’t. After all, with her strength, Shuichi could easily handle her however he pleased. Moreover, Shuichi had killed everyone in Kusagakure for her ; in a sense, she owed him.

Shuichi pressed his right hand onto the woman’s forehead, expanding his mental energy.

His perception pierced into the woman’s Chakra core.

The Chakra structure of the Uzumaki Bloodline was completely different from that of ordinary Ninjas. An ordinary Ninja’s Chakra was like a river, with a main stream and tributaries. The Uzumaki Clan’s Chakra was like a whirlpool—constantly rotating, compressing, and regenerating. This structure endowed the Uzumaki Clan with near-infinite Chakra recovery and Vitality, which was also the fundamental reason they could be used as living medical kits.

Shuichi carefully peeled away the concept of the Uzumaki Bloodline.

He did not choose to take it all, but only plundered two-thirds.

He needed to leave enough bloodline for the woman to protect herself. He stripped away two-thirds of the core structure of the Uzumaki Bloodline, leaving the outer one-third and the complete Chakra circulation pathways.

This way, the woman would still possess the Uzumaki Bloodline, but its intensity would be reduced to one-third of its original level. Her recovery rate and Vitality would drop significantly, but they would still far surpass those of ordinary Ninjas.

The Plunder was complete.

Shuichi withdrew his right hand, feeling the warm clump of Chakra in his palm. The concept of the Uzumaki Bloodline rotated in his hand like a miniature whirlpool.

Shuichi fused the plundered Uzumaki Bloodline into his own body.

The moment the bloodline fused, Shuichi’s body shuddered violently.

The Uzumaki Bloodline did not simply increase Vitality, but improved the quality of life itself. Shuichi’s Vitality was originally like a wide river ; now, the river remained the same width, but the flow speed had greatly increased.

This caused his Chakra recovery speed to increase tremendously. Previously, his Chakra needed rest to recover after being consumed ; now, while it was being consumed, it was frantically regenerating automatically. The regeneration speed couldn’t keep up with the consumption speed, but the gap was narrowing.

Shuichi closed his eyes, carefully feeling the changes in the Chakra within his body.

He tried releasing a small stream of Chakra to coat his finger, then let it go. As the Chakra dissipated, the Chakra at his lower dantian immediately began to replenish. The replenishment speed was nearly three times faster than before.

This was the effect of the Uzumaki Bloodline.

Shuichi opened his eyes.

The fusion of the Uzumaki Bloodline was complete. Although he had only taken two-thirds, the effect was already very significant. Leaving the remaining one-third for the woman was enough for her self-defense.

Then, Shuichi pressed his right hand back onto the woman’s forehead.

Expanding his mental energy, the bestowal began.

Shuichi extracted Chunin-level Taijutsu experience from his own combat experience and bestowed it upon the woman’s Chakra pathway system. The most basic evasion, blocking, and escape techniques, as well as judging attack trajectories, utilizing terrain, and disengaging from combat in unfavorable situations.

Along with them were the Body Replacement Technique and the Transformation Technique. The Body Replacement Technique was the most basic life-saving Ninjutsu, allowing one to instantly swap places with a nearby object when attacked. The Transformation Technique could disguise one’s identity.

This bestowal would not make the woman a powerhouse, but it at least gave her the most basic self-defense capabilities. She could run when encountering a Genin, and fight back when encountering ordinary people.

For her right now, this was probably the best gift.

Shuichi withdrew his right hand.

He looked at the woman’s sleeping face. Her complexion was slightly paler than before ; plundering the Uzumaki Bloodline had placed a significant burden on her body, but the remaining one-third combined with the Uzumaki Clan’s constitution ensured she would be fine. She might be weak for a few days after waking up, but she would recover quickly.

Shuichi pulled a stack of banknotes from his coat and placed them on the nightstand. It was about fifty thousand ryo, enough for her to live on for quite a long time.

Then he stood up.

Ryo flew up from the windowsill and landed on Shuichi’s shoulder.

« Shu, » Ryo said softly.

« Yeah. »

« What will happen to her in the future ? »

Shuichi thought for a moment. « She’ll figure something out once she wakes up. People of the Uzumaki Clan don’t die so easily. Actually, I look forward to her going to Konoha. I wonder how Uzumaki Kushina will react when she sees her clansman in this state ? »

Ryo didn’t ask further.

It noticed that Shuichi’s personality had also changed somewhat after becoming stronger, seeming more relaxed.

Shuichi pushed open the room door and walked out of the inn.

The night wind blew, carrying the chill of early spring. There was hardly anyone left on the village streets, with only a few lanterns swaying in the wind. In the distance came the barking of dogs and occasional whispers ; those Kusagakure Ninjas who had fled here were probably still discussing what had happened during the day.

Shuichi did not look back.

Kusagakure had been dealt with. The Uzumaki Bloodline was acquired, and the issue of Chakra recovery was basically resolved. Next was Hozuki Castle.

Kusagakure’s prison held Ninja prisoners from various countries. Those prisoners all possessed their own abilities and Chakra, making it a mobile treasure trove. Moreover, there should still be some Kusagakure guards at Hozuki Castle ; though they wouldn’t be too strong, it would be perfect to clean them up along the way.

Of course, there was also one most special thing : the box of ultimate bliss.

It was rumored to possess the power to grant people’s wishes. Although it wasn’t shown in the movie, it ultimately had its unique aspects. Shuichi was looking forward to what kind of ability he could Plunder from it.

Shuichi quickened his pace and disappeared into the night.
