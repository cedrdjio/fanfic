# Chapitre 67 — Investigation Request

The next morning.

Hokage Office.

The Third Hokage, Hiruzen Sarutobi, sat behind his desk, holding a pipe in his hand, looking at the view of Konoha Village outside the window.

A knock sounded at the door.

« Come in. »

The door pushed open, and Hiashi Hyuga walked in, followed by two members of the branch family.

« Hokage-sama. » Hiashi Hyuga bowed in salute.

« Hiashi, is this about last night ? » The Third Hokage put down his pipe.

« Yes, » Hiashi Hyuga said, « I would like to request Hokage-sama’s approval to allow the Hyuga Clan to participate in the investigation. »

The Third Hokage was silent for a moment, took a puff from his pipe, and exhaled smoke.

Killing someone inside Konoha Village, silently, with a single fatal blow, leaving no traces. This method did not seem like an ordinary vendetta.

He was well aware of the Hyuga Clan’s reputation. If a member of the branch family died, the main family would not care too much. But dying in the Village was another matter entirely. This was someone acting near the Hyuga territory, slapping the Hyuga in the face.

Hiashi Hyuga came to apply for investigative authority ; on the surface, it was to track down the killer, but in reality, it was to defend the family’s dignity.

« Very well. » The Third Hokage nodded, « The Hyuga Clan may participate in the investigation. But all investigation results must be reported to the Hokage Office. »

« Yes. » Hiashi Hyuga bowed, « Thank you, Hokage-sama. »

Hiashi Hyuga turned and left, with the two branch family members following behind him.

The door closed.

The Third Hokage picked up his pipe again and looked out the window.

A member of the Hyuga dying in the Village was, in itself, a signal.

The killer could kill two Chunin silently without leaving any traces, which meant their strength was not weak. Moreover, choosing to act within the Village indicated they were very familiar with the environment of Konoha.

An assassin from an outside Village ? Or someone from within Konoha ?

Although he had given the investigative authority to the Hyuga, he would not leave it unattended. The Hyuga Clan’s investigation might be biased, and he needed his own source of information.

« Come in. »

An Anbu wearing a mask appeared from the shadows.

« Hokage-sama. »

« Two people from the Hyuga Clan died last night. » The Third Hokage said, « You go and investigate, and see if you can find any clues. »

« Yes. »

The Anbu disappeared into the shadows.

The Third Hokage looked out the window, his gaze deep.

Within Konoha, there could be no more problems.

---

Forest of Death.

Shuichi stood in an open space and closed his eyes.

He activated the Byakugan.

A pale blue light emerged from the depths of his pupils, and his vision changed instantly. A 360-degree panoramic perception unfolded ; he could see everything within several hundred meters around him : trees, rocks, small animals hidden in the bushes, and underground water veins.

He deactivated the Byakugan and activated the Sharingan.

The Three Tomoe Sharingan rotated, and his vision returned to dynamic capture mode.

Then, he activated both abilities simultaneously.

This time, the two perceptions no longer conflicted. The Sharingan’s dynamic capture and the Byakugan’s panoramic perspective fused together to form a new field of vision. He could see a 360-degree panorama while being able to focus on any direction, capture dynamic details, and read movement trajectories in advance.

Shuichi opened his eyes.

He raised his hand, took out three shuriken from his Ninja tool pouch, and threw them casually in three different directions.

The three shuriken traced arcs in the air, flying towards three different targets.

Shuichi did not turn his head, but the trajectories of the three shuriken were all within his field of vision.

He raised his hand and flicked his fingertips lightly.

Three Kunai flew out, striking the three shuriken respectively, knocking them to the ground.

Throughout the whole process, Shuichi did not turn his head, did not move, and just stood in place.

The fusion of the Sharingan and the Byakugan had been preliminarily completed.

He deactivated the fused vision and felt his mental state.

The fusion with the Byakugan did not add any extra burden. The two abilities had merged into one, making them smoother to use than before.

Shuichi took a deep breath and reactivated the fused vision.

This time, he tried using the Byakugan’s clairvoyant ability to look further away.

Deep in the Forest of Death, several kilometers away, he could see several blurry figures. They were other Ninjas training, or a squad that had come to the Forest of Death to execute a mission.

He could see their Chakra meridians and the flow of Chakra within their bodies.

If he wanted to, he could lock onto them from several kilometers away and observe their every move.

Shuichi deactivated the fused vision.

The Byakugan’s ability was powerful, but continuous use would consume Chakra.

However, with Shuichi’s current amount of Chakra, keeping it open for days and nights would be no problem.

He turned around and looked at Plunder.

Plunder squatted on a tree branch, the wind currents at the edges of its wings trembling slightly.

« How is your Wind Release training going ? » Shuichi asked.

Plunder flew up, and the wind currents at the edges of its wings became sharp. It circled in the air once, then dove toward the ground.

The wind blades at the edges of its wings cut through the trunk of a small tree, leaving deep marks.

Plunder landed on Shuichi’s shoulder, its wings folding.

« The wind blades can be used now, » Plunder said, « but they are not sharp enough yet. »

« Keep practicing, » Shuichi said, « The power of Wind Release depends on the precision of Chakra control. Your current amount of Chakra is sufficient, but your control is not yet refined enough. »

Plunder nodded and flew up to continue practicing.

Shuichi watched Plunder circling in the air, the wind blades at the edges of its wings cutting through leaves and branches, leaving neat cuts.

Plunder’s progress was fast.

It had Chakra, it had Wind Release knowledge, and it had the Byakugan. Now what it needed was time to fuse these abilities into its own fighting style.

Shuichi leaned against a tree and closed his eyes.

He continued to organize the progress of the Byakugan’s fusion in his mind.

The fusion of the Sharingan and the Byakugan had been preliminarily completed, but it was not yet perfect. He needed more actual combat to familiarize himself with this new way of perception.

At that time, what would his eyes become ?

Shuichi opened his eyes and looked at his palm.

Mangekyo Sharingan, Byakugan, and the ability to Plunder everything.

His strength was growing stronger step by step.

And in the future, there were still Wood Release, Sage Body, and Rinnegan waiting for him to Plunder.

---

Noon.

Shuichi left the Forest of Death and walked in the direction of Konoha Village.

Plunder flew above his head, using the Byakugan to scan the surrounding area.

« There are two people ahead, » Plunder said, « The Chakra fluctuations are not strong ; they should be ordinary villagers. »

Shuichi didn’t pay attention and continued walking forward.

Plunder’s Byakugan field of vision covered a range of several kilometers ; no abnormality could escape its eyes.

Shuichi thought about the next plan.

The Byakugan was obtained, and the fusion was in progress.

Next was the perfection of the intelligence network and the acquisition of the Scroll of Sealing.

Fang’s insect swarm intelligence network had already taken initial shape, but it still needed time to expand.

Shuichi walked out of the Forest of Death and returned to Konoha Village.

He passed by the commercial street and saw several people from the Hyuga Clan wandering near the alley entrance, seemingly investigating something.

Shuichi did not stop and continued walking home.

The Hyuga Clan’s investigation had nothing to do with him.

He was just passing by.
