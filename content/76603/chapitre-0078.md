# Chapitre 78 — Land of Rain

The rain never stopped falling.

That fine, dense drizzle that never ceased tapped rhythmically against the tin roof.

The entire Land of Rain was soaked in a layer of gray mist ; there was no sun to be seen, no clouds, only the boundless, endless rain.

The water on the town’s streets rose above the ankles.

A few children chased each other and played in the puddles, splashing water that soaked their pant legs.

They were already used to the rain ; they didn’t care about getting wet and continued to run through the puddles just the same.

Women hung their laundry under the eaves.

The clothes were hung out and soaked by the rain, and they weren’t easy to dry even after a day, but they still hung them out day after day.

If they didn’t hang them, the clothes would get even wetter.

There weren’t many people in the vegetable market.

The price of rice was rising rapidly, and the grain merchant squatted on a wooden crate, looking at his half-empty rice bags, telling everyone who came to ask that the Land of Fire had stopped their supply.

The woman buying rice cursed a couple of times, but in the end, she still paid the money.

Life in the Land of Rain had always been like this.

When the power struggles of the great nations rolled over this land, they never gave any warning. The rain washed everything into a gray haze, but people still had to eat, still had to hang their laundry, and still had to survive in the rain.

In a wooden house on the edge of town, Jiraiya sat cross-legged on the tatami.

He had come to the Land of Rain this time to execute a mission, monitoring the movements of this land.

In the gaps between his mission, he stopped by to see these three students.

Yahiko, Nagato, Konan.

It had been years since he took them in.

Back then, they were still thin, small children, struggling to survive in the rain.

Now they were in their teens, their figures had grown taller, and the childishness had faded from their faces.

Yahiko sat in the position opposite Jiraiya, his eyes shining with light.

Nagato sat against the wall, the Rinnegan shimmering with a faint purple hue in the dim light.

Konan was organizing papers on a low table nearby, her fingers folding them nimbly.

« Teacher, how long can you stay this time ? » Yahiko asked.

« Probably only a few days, » Jiraiya replied. « I have to leave once the mission is finished. »

« Then you must teach us well during these few days, » Yahiko smiled. « We have encountered some problems recently. »

« What kind of problems ? »

« Tactical coordination, » Yahiko said. « I feel like the coordination between the three of us isn’t tacit enough ; it’s easy for us to get chaotic when we encounter strong enemies. »

Jiraiya nodded. « Then I will teach you that today. »

In the afternoon, the three of them followed Jiraiya to the woods outside the town.

The drizzle flew diagonally, tapping against the leaves with a rustling sound.

A thin layer of water had accumulated on the ground, and stepping on it would splash up mud.

« The three of you, attack together first, » Jiraiya said, leaning against a tree with his arms crossed. « Let me see your current level. »

Yahiko, Nagato, and Konan exchanged a glance and moved simultaneously.

Yahiko charged to the front, holding a Kunai in his hand, his figure weaving through the rain.

His speed was much faster than it had been a few years ago, his steps steady, with no wasted movements.

Paper wings unfurled behind Konan, and her body lifted into the air.

Pieces of paper scattered in the air, forming the shape of over a dozen shuriken, and flew toward Jiraiya.

Nagato stood in the back, the Rinnegan rotating.

His perception covered the entire battlefield, capturing every detail.

Yahiko’s Kunai approached Jiraiya’s front, Konan’s paper shuriken covered him from above, and Nagato was in the back, gathering strength to prepare a Ninjutsu.

A three-pronged attack.

Jiraiya dodged sideways, avoiding Yahiko’s Kunai, raised his hand to deflect Konan’s paper shuriken, and then pushed off with his feet, retreating three meters away.

« The force is not bad, » he said. « But there is a problem with the coordination. »

The three stopped their movements and looked at him.

« Yahiko, you charged too fast, and you were disconnected from Konan’s paper shuriken. »

« Konan, your coverage area is too large, wasting Chakra. »

« Nagato, you stayed in the back for too long ; if the enemy were faster, you wouldn’t have had a chance to attack at all. »

Yahiko frowned. « Then what should we do ? »

Jiraiya broke a branch from a tree and drew a simple formation diagram on the ground.

« Yahiko is responsible for frontal distraction, but don’t be in a rush to attack ; the focus is to attract the enemy’s attention. »

« Konan’s Paper Ninjutsu should flank from the side, restricting the enemy’s movement space. »

« Nagato should observe from the back, finding the enemy’s opening before attacking. »

He raised his head, looking at the three of them.

« Each of you now has the strength of a Chunin level ; there is no problem in one-on-one combat. »

« But if you encounter an enemy of Jonin level, one-on-one combat is just courting death. You must coordinate. »

Yahiko nodded. « Again ? »

« Come. »

The training continued until evening.

The rain was still falling, and the three of them were soaked to the bone ; it was impossible to tell if it was rain or sweat, but no one complained of fatigue.

They adjusted their coordination over and over again, correcting problems repeatedly, and their movements became increasingly fluid.

During the final drill, Yahiko’s frontal distraction and Konan’s flanking maneuver arrived almost simultaneously, Nagato’s Ninjutsu was released at the optimal moment, and the three forces converged, forming a complete offensive.

Jiraiya dodged sideways, but this time he had to respond seriously.

« That’s it, » he said, putting away his defensive stance. « It’s starting to look like something. »

Yahiko wiped the rain from his face and revealed a smile. Konan retracted her paper wings, and Nagato stood in the back, the light in his Rinnegan slowly fading.

« Let’s go back, » Jiraiya said, turning to walk toward the town. « I’ll treat you to a good meal. »

« Teacher is treating ? » Yahiko caught up. « Then we have to eat something good. »

Dinner was settled at a small restaurant in the town.

Yahiko ate the most, Nagato ate the slowest, and Konan ate the most quietly.

Jiraiya watched them, remembering the scene of seeing them for the first time many years ago.

Back then, they were still thin, small children, struggling to survive in the rain. Now they had grown up, had their own strength, and had the ability to protect themselves.

But this world was also changing.

Countries were all mobilizing their troops ; Kumogakure, Iwagakure, and Hidden Mist all had movements. The Third Shinobi World War was about to begin.

He didn’t know how long this war would last, nor did he know if these three students could survive.

« Teacher. » Yahiko’s voice interrupted his thoughts. « What are you thinking about ? »

Jiraiya snapped back to reality, looking at them.

« Nothing, » he said. « Eat more. »

The rain was still falling outside the window.

Jiraiya watched the curtain of rain outside the window, silent for a few seconds.

He had come to the Land of Rain this time, besides executing a mission, also because he wanted to see them again. After the mission ended, he would have to return to Konoha ; he didn’t know when they would meet again.

« I will teach you more things over the next few days, » he said. « Study hard. »

The three nodded.

The sound of the rain tapped rhythmically against the window, as if it were a countdown.
