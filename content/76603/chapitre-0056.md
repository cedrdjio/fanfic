# Chapitre 56 — Jiraiya

Shuichi sat cross-legged in the room of the Stone Tower, eyes closed, sensing the recovery of his mental energy.

The recovery ability of the Uzumaki Bloodline was also effective for mental energy, though the speed was much slower than that of Chakra.

After one hour, his mental energy had recovered to about fifty percent.

That was enough.

Shuichi opened his eyes and stood up.

He took out the prisoner information record sheet and quickly flipped through it.

Over two hundred prisoners, most of whom were Chunin and Genin, held no value for the current Shuichi.

Shuichi closed the booklet and walked out of the room.

The harvest had begun.

He first went to the East District prison cells.

East District, second floor, third cell.

A bald man sat cross-legged in the center of the cell, wearing tattered Ninja attire with a scorched mark on his chest.

His Chakra was suppressed by the Heavenly Prison Technique, but Shuichi could feel the residual Chakra fluctuations within his body.

An Iwagakure Rogue Ninja, skilled in Earth Release and Taijutsu.

Shuichi opened the cell door and walked inside.

The bald man looked up at Shuichi, his eyes guarded.

« Who are you ? »

Shuichi did not answer, pressing his right hand against the man’s temple.

Plunder.

A surge of Chakra fluctuations welled up, gathering in Shuichi’s palm.

This was Jonin-level Chakra, but for the current Shuichi, the knowledge from these ordinary Jonin was already useless.

A few seconds later, Shuichi withdrew his right hand.

The bald man collapsed limply to the ground, his eyes vacant.

Shuichi turned and left, heading toward the next target.

The second target, East District, third floor, Hidden Mist Rogue Ninja, skilled in Water Release.

The third, West District, second floor, Sunagakure Rogue Ninja, skilled in Wind Release and basic Puppet Technique.

The fourth, West District, third floor, Kusagakure Rogue Ninja, skilled in sensory perception and intelligence gathering.

Shuichi harvested them one by one.

Each Plunder took only a few seconds, harvesting either Chakra or special skills.

Time ticked by, second by second.

Shuichi had already harvested seven Jonin-level prisoners and was heading toward the eighth target when—

The sound of flapping wings came from outside the window.

Lue landed on the windowsill, folding its wings in its crow form.

« Shuichi. » Lue’s voice held a hint of tension, « A powerful energy is rushing here, moving very fast. »

Shuichi stopped and looked at Lue.

« What kind of energy ? »

« It’s very special. » Lue said, « It’s not like ordinary Chakra ; it’s... immense, and there’s a strange feeling to it that I can’t describe. »

Shuichi narrowed his eyes slightly.

« How far away is it ? »

« At its current speed, it will arrive at Hozuki Castle in about ten minutes. »

Shuichi was silent for a moment.

Ten minutes.

Given his current state, he had about forty percent of his mental energy left, and his Chakra had recovered to eighty percent.

Even if the visitor came with ill intentions, he had sufficient means to deal with them.

After all, with his current strength, the number of people in the entire Ninja World who could stop him could be counted on one hand.

Besides, he also wanted to test the abilities of the Mangekyo, and now he had a perfect target.

« Understood. » Shuichi said, « Keep watching, and tell me when he arrives. »

« Alright. » Lue spread its wings and flew back up into the sky.

Shuichi put away the prisoner information record sheet and stopped the harvesting.

The remaining Chunin and Genin offered negligible improvements for him.

So, he walked to the front of the Stone Tower and found a rock to sit on.

He was still using the Transformation Technique, appearing as a lean-faced man in his thirties with long hair tied behind his head.

Outside Hozuki Castle was a large lake, its surface shimmering with ripples under the sunlight.

The distant shoreline was faintly visible, and further away was the hazy sky.

Shuichi sat on the rock, waiting quietly.

He was not in a hurry.

Whoever the visitor was, he would know when they arrived.

Five minutes later.

Lue’s voice came.

« They’ve arrived ! Coming from the east, very fast ! »

Shuichi’s gaze shifted to the east.

A white figure was leaping across the rooftops of Hozuki Castle at an extremely fast speed.

Immense Chakra.

No, it was not just immense.

The structure of that Chakra was completely different from that of an ordinary Ninja ; it was natural energy fused with physical and mental energy, forming a denser, heavier type of Chakra.

This was Sage Mode.

The person arriving was a tall, white-haired man wearing a red haori, wooden sandals on his feet, and red, eyeshadow-like markings on his face.

His pupils were horizontal toad eyes, and there were distinct toad-like markings on both sides of his nose.

Jiraiya.

One of the Sannin.

And on his shoulders, two toads were perched.

Shuichi recognized the two toads : Sage Fukasaku and Sage Shima, the two great sages of Mount Myōboku.

Jiraiya’s Sage Mode was not perfect ; he needed the help of the two great sages to maintain it.

After seeing Jiraiya, Shuichi’s mind raced.

It was the end of year 45, and Jiraiya should have been in the Land of Rain teaching Yahiko, Nagato, and Konan.

Hozuki Castle was located between the Land of Rain and the Land of Grass ; from the Land of Rain to Hozuki Castle, at Jiraiya’s speed, it was at most a day’s journey.

After the news of Kusagakure’s destruction spread, Mui had sent a distress signal to the Five Great Nations.

And as one of the Konoha Sannin operating in the Land of Rain, it was perfectly logical for Jiraiya to come and investigate after receiving the message from Konoha.

Moreover, he had activated Sage Mode as soon as he arrived and brought the two great sages with him.

Clearly, he knew that the person who could wipe out Kusagakure was definitely not someone to be trifled with.

Although Jiraiya usually seemed carefree, when truly facing danger, he was more cautious than anyone.

He was not proficient in Sage Mode, so he had activated it in advance and brought the two great sages to assist.

This showed he had prepared for the worst.

However, since it was Jiraiya, he would have to play a part.

The current Jiraiya held no attraction for Shuichi.

The only useful Sage Jutsu was not what Shuichi wanted ; it was imperfect, and the toad-like appearance after using it was quite ugly.

Shuichi planned to go to the three great sacred places to absorb natural energy himself later, and then use his current knowledge to develop a Sage Mode of his own.

Of course, the most important point was that killing Jiraiya would have a huge impact on the entire timeline, which would not be worth the cost.

So, rather than killing Jiraiya, it was better to use him to establish an identity for his current persona.

Jiraiya stopped in front of the Stone Tower and looked up at Shuichi, who was directly in front of him.

Shuichi jumped down from the rock, a smile on his face.

There was no relaxed expression on Jiraiya’s face.

His Sage Mode sensory perception had already told him that the island was filled with the scent of death.

And the Chakra of the person in front of him...

Sage Fukasaku spoke from Jiraiya’s left shoulder : « Jiraiya-chan, this person’s Chakra is immense. »

Sage Shima nodded on his right shoulder : « Like a human-shaped Tailed Beast. »

Jiraiya frowned.

A man who looked about thirty years old, and his Chakra volume was greater than his own ?

And this person’s appearance... Jiraiya always felt something was off, but he couldn’t put his finger on it.

« Who are you ? » Jiraiya asked, his voice low.

« As for my name, you aren’t qualified to know it right now. Why don’t you tell me who you are ? Those on your shoulders are toads from Mount Myōboku, right ? » Shuichi smiled.

Jiraiya’s brain was racing.

What did he mean by not qualified to know ? And he knew about Mount Myōboku ?

Jiraiya immediately turned his head to look at the toad sages on his shoulders.

Sage Shima shook her head on his right shoulder : « I don’t know him ; I have no memory of him. »

Seeing Sage Shima’s response, Jiraiya speculated in his mind.

Bluffing ?

But there was no reason to, and as a powerhouse, how could he not know him ?

It was no wonder Jiraiya was so confident ; after being crowned with the title of Sannin, almost all Ninjas from every Village knew their names.

Now that a powerful person had suddenly appeared who completely didn’t know him, it was strange no matter how one thought about it.

However, it seemed there was still a possibility of communication, so he would try to extract as much information as possible now.

« My name is Jiraiya, from Konoha Village. Why did you carry out a massacre in Kusagakure, and even kill your way to Hozuki Castle ? » Jiraiya stared intently at Shuichi’s eyes, trying to find an answer within them.

« Konoha Village ? The Village founded by Madara ? I didn’t expect it to still exist ; I thought it would have been destroyed when he fought Senju Hashirama. As for the massacre you mentioned, I can only say I didn’t like the look of that Village, and as for Hozuki Castle, I just woke up and came here to feed. »

Hearing Shuichi’s reply, the massive amount of information nearly caused Jiraiya’s brain to crash.

He knew Uchiha Madara ? Judging by what he said, he seemed quite familiar with him. Could he be someone from the Sengoku period ? But he looked only about thirty. No, appearances could be deceiving. So, was he really from the Sengoku period, and was the « waking up to feed » the price for maintaining his youth ?

Quickly, Jiraiya’s brain automatically filled in the blanks regarding Shuichi’s identity.

No, what did his identity from the Sengoku period have to do with the massacre ?

Clearly, Jiraiya had already accepted that Shuichi was from the Sengoku period, and the focus of the question returned to the massacre.

« Just because you didn’t like it ? If someone offended you, you should have only targeted them, not involved the innocent. »

« Involving the innocent ? Hmm, you can just consider it me involving the innocent ; I just like involving the innocent. »

« Huh ? »

Jiraiya widened his eyes ; he had thought of several possibilities, but he never expected him to admit it directly.

Normally, powerhouses of his level cared a lot about their reputation, even if they used a fake reason to cover up what they had done.

But Shuichi’s frankness caused the « Talk-no-Jutsu » Jiraiya was preparing to misfire.

And Shuichi, watching Jiraiya, who looked like he was suffering from constipation, smiled even more.

How could he not know that future Naruto’s « Talk-no-Jutsu » skills were learned from Jiraiya ?

Therefore, choosing to admit it directly was better than continuing to bicker with Jiraiya.

Although Shuichi’s frankness caught Jiraiya off guard, he quickly reacted.

His expression began to turn serious.

Since the other party had already admitted it, there was no need for further conversation ; from here on out, they would let their strength do the talking.
