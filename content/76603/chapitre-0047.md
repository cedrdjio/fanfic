# Chapitre 47 — Slaughtering the Village

Shuichi walked out of the medical station and headed toward the center of the Village.

He did not hide his killing intent.

He stopped suppressing his Chakra, and his aura was released from his body, pushing forward like an invisible wall. Lue flew up from his shoulder, ascended into the air, and scanned the entire Kusagakure with its black eyes.

Shuichi walked north along the main street.

The Ninjas on the street had not yet realized what was happening. When Shuichi passed the first Genin, the Genin merely looked up at him once, then continued to lower his head and organize his goods.

Shuichi’s short blade slashed across his throat.

The Genin fell onto the pile of goods, blood splattering onto the wooden crates. Another Genin nearby heard the sound, turned his head, and saw his companion’s corpse and the blood-stained blade in Shuichi’s hand.

He opened his mouth.

Shuichi’s blade had already arrived.

Two corpses fell to the street. The nearby Ninjas finally reacted ; someone shouted, « Someone is killing people ! », and then the whole street erupted in chaos.

But Shuichi did not give them time to escape.

Lue circled in the air, and Shuichi’s perception expanded. With the two layers of perception overlapping, the location of every Ninja was clear.

A Chunin ran out of an alley on the left, gripping a short blade and charging toward Shuichi. Shuichi dodged to the side, countered with a slash, and the Chunin’s arm and blade flew off together. A second slash pierced his heart.

The door of a house on the right was pushed open, and two Genin rushed out, trying to run. Shuichi raised his right hand, and a white-blue arc of electricity passed through their bodies ; they both stiffened and collapsed simultaneously.

Fifty meters ahead, three Genin were desperately running north. Shuichi did not chase them ; he formed hand seals with his right hand. Wind Release : Wind Cutter. A sharp blade of wind shot from his palm, crossed the thirty-meter distance, and sliced the three Genin in half from behind.

Shuichi continued walking forward.

The Ninjas of Kusagakure began to panic.

They didn’t know what was happening, only that a Ninja was indiscriminately killing people in the Village. The speed of the killing was unimaginably fast ; some saw their companions' throats slit in one stroke, some saw people pierced by electric arcs, and some saw wind blades flying from afar to slice people apart.

Screams and the sound of running footsteps were everywhere.

A Chunin with two Genin tried to escape through a side alley. Just as they reached the entrance of the alley, Shuichi had already appeared before them. The Chunin’s face turned pale, and he turned to run back, but Shuichi’s blade had already arrived.

Three Ninjas, six seconds.

Shuichi walked north along the main street, and with every step he took, there were fewer living people on the street. Lue helped him lock onto every target attempting to escape or hide from the air, while Shuichi’s perception scanned the ground. With the two layers of perception overlapping, no one could escape.

Not a single survivor was left along the way.

Kusagakure Administrative Building, second floor.

The leader of Kusagakure was named Kusano. He was over fifty years old, small in stature, and had an old scar on his face extending from the corner of his eyebrow to his chin. He was wearing a dark green vest, with the Kusagakure forehead protector on his head, and was sitting behind his desk looking through a scroll.

The door was pushed open, and a Chunin rushed in.

« Leader ! Someone is killing people in the Village ! »

Kusano looked up and frowned. « Who ? »

« I don’t know ! No forehead protector, carrying a short blade, coming from the direction of the medical station ! They’ve already killed over twenty people ! »

Hearing this, Kusano put down the scroll and stood up.

« One person ? »

« One person ! But he... he is not human, he is— »

« Send Jounin Kuroda, » Kusano interrupted the Chunin. « Remember to remind him to focus on probing ; don’t get carried away. »

Jounin Kuroda was one of the four Jonin of Kusagakure. He excelled at Water Release and short-blade combat, and was the second-ranked Ninja in terms of combat power in Kusagakure.

The Chunin acknowledged the order and left.

Kusano walked to the window and looked south. The administrative building was the tallest structure in Kusagakure, and from the second floor, one could see most of the Village. There were no living people to be seen on the distant streets, only corpses lying scattered on the ground.

About two minutes passed.

Kusano felt a fluctuation of Chakra, very brief, like a needle pricking into water and being pulled out.

Then the fluctuation disappeared.

Kusano’s pupils constricted slightly.

Too fast. Jounin Kuroda’s Chakra fluctuation lasted for less than six seconds before disappearing, which meant he was either instantly killed or—no, there was no « or ». Jounin Kuroda was a Jonin ; it was impossible for him to voluntarily stop fighting within six seconds. Therefore, he had been killed.

A Jonin, unable to survive even six seconds, and that was with Kusano ordering Jounin Kuroda to focus on probing.

Kusano’s hand tightened around the window frame.

This is no ordinary rogue Ninja.

« Call Negishi here, » Kusano turned and said to another Chunin at the door. Negishi was his secretary and also the Chunin best at negotiation in Kusagakure.

Negishi arrived quickly. He was over forty, wore round-rimmed glasses, and looked more like a clerical worker than a Ninja.

« A powerful individual has come to the Village and is carrying out an indiscriminate slaughter, » Kusano’s voice was calm, but he spoke twice as fast as usual. « Jounin Kuroda has already been killed by him. Go and probe him, see if negotiation is possible. Ask him what he wants—money, information, people—everything is up for negotiation. »

Negishi pushed up his glasses, nodded, and turned to go downstairs.

By the time Shuichi reached the front of the administrative building, there were very few Ninjas left in Kusagakure.

From the medical station to the administrative building, a three-hundred-meter path, Shuichi had killed every Ninja he could find along the way.

Those who ran fast had escaped the Village before Shuichi reached their street. Those who were slow had all become corpses on the ground.

The administrative building was a three-story wooden structure, the largest building in Kusagakure. The two Chunin standing at the entrance saw Shuichi walking over, and both of their faces turned pale.

It was as if they saw the Shinigami walking toward them.

Shuichi’s body was not stained with any blood, but the blood on his short blade was still dripping from the tip. His pace was not fast, but every step was firm, as casual as a stroll.

The two Chunin exchanged a glance and drew their blades simultaneously.

Shuichi did not accelerate, did not dodge ; he simply raised his right hand, index and middle fingers pressed together.

Lightning Release : Lightning Fang.

A white-blue arc of electricity shot from Shuichi’s fingertip, passing through the bodies of the two Chunin. Both stiffened simultaneously, then collapsed like puppets with cut strings.

Shuichi lowered his hand and continued walking.

Just as he stepped onto the steps of the administrative building, a man walked out from the door.

A middle-aged man, wearing round-rimmed glasses and a Chunin uniform, had a polite smile on his face. But Shuichi could see sweat slightly forming on his forehead, and there was a barely perceptible tremor at the corners of his smiling mouth.

« Sir, » Negishi began, his voice steady, as if he had rehearsed it many times, « it seems you have encountered something unpleasant in our Village. If there is anything we have done that is inadequate, we can sit down and talk about it. After all, we have no grudges— »

Shuichi moved.

Body Flicker Technique.

Negishi’s pupils dilated suddenly. He didn’t even see how Shuichi disappeared ; he only felt a blur before his eyes, and then a sharp pain in his chest.

Shuichi stood behind Negishi, his short blade already plunged into Negishi’s back, piercing his heart, with the tip protruding from his chest.

Negishi looked down at the blade tip sticking out of his chest, his lips parted and closed twice, but no sound came out.

Shuichi withdrew the short blade, and Negishi’s body fell forward, crashing onto the steps of the administrative building.

There was no need for negotiation anymore.

Kusano stood in front of the second-floor window, watching Negishi’s corpse fall onto the steps.

His expression was grim.

Jounin Kuroda was dead, and Negishi was dead too. The opponent had no intention of negotiating ; he was here to wipe out the Village.

Kusano took a deep breath.

« Call everyone remaining to the administrative building, » Kusano turned and said to the Chunin beside him. « All Jonin, assemble. »

The three remaining Jonin of Kusagakure arrived quickly.

One was Elite Jounin Touji, an Elite Jonin and the strongest combat force of Kusagakure. In his thirties, with a burly build, he always wore an indifferent expression on his face. He excelled at Earth Release and Taijutsu, and was Kusano’s final trump card.

One was Jounin Mayumi, a female Jonin in her thirties, who excelled at Genjutsu and Sealing Technique. She had learned a set of Sealing Technique from the Uzumaki Clan that could suppress a target.

One was Jounin Koji, a Jonin who excelled at Wind Release and had the fastest speed in Kusagakure.

The three stood before Kusano with varying expressions. Elite Jounin Touji was expressionless, Jounin Mayumi frowned slightly, and Jounin Koji’s face was somewhat pale ; he had just seen the process of Shuichi killing Jounin Kuroda from a distance, and that speed made his back go cold.

« The opponent’s strength is formidable, » Kusano said. « Jounin Kuroda was killed instantly. And there is no room for negotiation. »

The three Jonin fell silent.

Killing a Jonin instantly. This information made the air heavy.

« However, » Kusano continued, « we have the Sealing Technique learned from the Uzumaki Clan. Jounin Mayumi, can your Sealing Technique suppress him ? »

Jounin Mayumi thought for a moment. « If the four of us coordinate, there is a chance. The key to my Sealing Technique is to render the opponent immobile during the casting ; Elite Jounin Touji and Jounin Koji will be responsible for containment, I will be responsible for casting the seal, and the Leader will be responsible for fixing it with Chakra chains. As long as the seal is successful, no matter how strong they are, they cannot break free. »

Kusano nodded. « Let’s do it. »

Shuichi walked up the steps of the administrative building and pushed open the main door.

The hall on the first floor was empty, tables and chairs had been pushed to the walls, and there were fresh footprints on the floor.

Shuichi’s perception expanded. Four strong heat sources were on the second floor, all with high Chakra activity. Three at the Jonin level, one Elite Jonin, matching the previous information.

But compared to Shuichi, they were far behind.

Shuichi walked up the stairs.

At the end of the second-floor corridor was a sliding door. Shuichi walked to the door and paused.

Behind the door, the breathing of four people was clearly audible. The heartbeats of three of them were accelerating—fear, tension, excitement, various emotions mixed together. Only one person’s heartbeat was steady, that Elite Jonin, Elite Jounin Touji.

Shuichi pushed open the door.

The four people stood in the room, distributed in a fan shape.

Elite Jounin Touji stood at the very front, fists clenched, his feet in the starting stance for Earth Release ; he had already spread a thin layer of Chakra on the floor, ready to activate Earth Release at any moment.

Jounin Koji stood on the left, his body slightly leaning forward, Wind-style Chakra rotating on his arms.

Jounin Mayumi stood on the right, her hands formed a half-seal, and the technique for the Chakra chains was already half-prepared.

Kusano stood at the very back, holding a sealing scroll in his hand.

The four of them looked at Shuichi simultaneously.

Shuichi stood at the doorway, his gaze sweeping across the four of them, finally landing on Jounin Mayumi.

« Sealing Technique ? » Shuichi said. « Learned from the Uzumaki Clan ? »

Jounin Mayumi’s expression changed. The opponent saw through her technique at a glance, which meant—

« Do it ! » Kusano shouted loudly.

Elite Jounin Touji was the first to move. He slammed his hands onto the ground, Earth Release : Earth Flow Wall, and a thick earthen wall rose from the ground, pressing toward Shuichi. At the same time, Jounin Koji rushed out from the left, Wind Release : Wind Cutter, and a sharp wind blade slashed toward Shuichi’s neck from the side.

The two coordinated well, one after the other, one slow and one fast.

Shuichi did not dodge.

He raised his right hand, Sharingan activated.

The three tomoe rotated in his pupils, the red light particularly glaring in the dim corridor.

The Sharingan saw through everything.

The Chakra distribution of the Earth Flow Wall, the trajectory of the wind blade, the Sealing Technique Jounin Mayumi was casting, the sealing scroll Kusano was unfolding—all actions were as clear as slow motion in Shuichi’s eyes.

At the same time, he also saw the flaws.

There was a 0.3-second gap between Elite Jounin Touji’s Earth Release and Jounin Koji’s Wind Release. Jounin Mayumi’s Sealing Technique required at least five seconds to complete, and Kusano’s sealing scroll needed Elite Jounin Touji’s earth wall to block Shuichi’s line of sight to be effective, but Shuichi’s Sharingan would not be blocked by the earth wall at all.

Shuichi passed through the gap between the earth wall and the wind blade.

It was not a dodge, it was a direct passage. His body walked through between the two attacks within the 0.3-second gap, as if he knew in advance where each attack would land.

Jounin Koji’s wind blade missed. Elite Jounin Touji’s earth wall was left behind Shuichi.

Shuichi’s short blade pierced into Elite Jounin Touji’s throat.

Elite Jounin Touji’s eyes widened. He was an Elite Jonin, the strongest Ninja in Kusagakure ; he thought he could at least withstand three of Shuichi’s moves. But he didn’t even withstand one.

Shuichi withdrew the short blade, and Elite Jounin Touji’s body fell backward.

« Elite Jounin Touji ! » Jounin Koji shouted in terror.

Shuichi turned to look at Jounin Koji.

Jounin Koji and Shuichi locked eyes for a second. The red light of the Sharingan was reflected in his pupils, and he saw the reflection of his own death.

Jounin Koji turned and ran.

Shuichi’s short blade pierced in from behind him, passing through his heart. Jounin Koji’s body hit the wall and slowly slid down.

Two Jonin, five seconds.

Jounin Mayumi’s hand seals were half-finished, and they stopped. She watched Shuichi walking toward her, his pace not fast, but every step felt like it was stepping on her heart.

Her Sealing Technique required five seconds, but Shuichi would not give her five seconds.

Jounin Mayumi gritted her teeth, forcibly accelerating her hand-sealing speed. Chakra chains shot from her hands, winding toward Shuichi.

Under the Sharingan, the trajectory of the chains was like slow-motion white lines. Shuichi dodged the first one, severed the second with his short blade, and then appeared in front of Jounin Mayumi.

« Wait— »

Shuichi’s blade slashed across her throat.

Jounin Mayumi fell, clutching her neck, blood gushing from between her fingers. Her eyes were still open, looking at the ceiling, her lips moving silently.

Kusano stood in the innermost part of the room, his back against the wall.

His sealing scroll was still in his hand, but it was meaningless. Without Elite Jounin Touji to contain, without Jounin Koji to coordinate, without Jounin Mayumi to cast the seal, he couldn’t do anything alone.

Kusano watched Shuichi walking toward him.

Shuichi’s footsteps were very slow, and the blood on the tip of his blade dripped onto the floor, drop by drop.

« Sharingan... you are of the Uchiha Clan... » Kusano’s voice was trembling, his pupils reflecting the red light of the three tomoe rotating in Shuichi’s eyes, « Why would someone from the Uchiha— »

Shuichi did not answer.

The short blade pierced into Kusano’s heart.

Kusano’s body leaned against the wall and slowly slid down. His eyes were still open, but they had lost focus.

Shuichi withdrew the short blade, wiped the bloodstains on Kusano’s clothes, and sheathed it.

Four Jonin, all killed.

The highest combat power of Kusagakure did not even last a minute before Shuichi.

Shuichi walked out of the administrative building, stood on the steps, and looked at the Kusagakure before him.

The Village was very quiet. Only corpses remained on the streets. Lue flew down from the air and landed on Shuichi’s shoulder.

Shuichi looked ahead : « Now is the harvest time. »
