# Chapitre 71 — Resistance from the Pure Land

Plunder activated.

Information began to flow toward the palm of his right hand.

Those memory fragments entered Shuichi’s brain bit by bit, flowing in like water along the Plunder channel.

The route map from when the bandit committed the robbery.

The specific location where the stolen goods were hidden.

The faces of the people he had killed.

The contact methods of his accomplices.

The memories unfolded in Shuichi’s mind, clear and real.

Then he felt that something was wrong.

His spiritual energy was draining away frantically.

It wasn’t a gradual consumption ; a massive force was pulling back along the Plunder channel.

Shuichi’s spiritual energy was being siphoned off, flowing ceaselessly toward that direction. That force was like a black hole, greedily devouring everything.

The Plunder channel was trembling.

Shuichi gritted his teeth and forcibly severed the connection.

His consciousness recoiled sharply, and his right hand sprang away from the top of the Edo Tensei body’s head.

Shuichi staggered back two steps, gasping for breath.

Cold sweat broke out on his forehead, and his temples throbbed with pain. His brain felt as if it had been stirred violently by something, and waves of dizziness washed over him.

The Edo Tensei body still stood in place, its hollow eyes staring forward, showing no reaction.

Shuichi leaned against the nearby stone wall, catching his breath for a moment.

The memory Plunder was partially successful.

Those fragments had already entered his brain ; although incomplete, he had indeed obtained them. The location of the cave where the stolen goods were hidden, the contact methods of his accomplices—all this information was there.

But the cost was too high.

More than half of his spiritual energy had been drained in just two seconds.

Shuichi leaned against the stone wall, closed his eyes, and regulated his breathing.

The loss of spiritual energy made him feel exhausted, and his brain felt somewhat groggy. He needed to rest for a while to recover.

Why was the consumption so great ?

This bandit was just an ordinary person, and the amount of memory was not large. Under normal circumstances, Plundering memories of this magnitude would not consume more than one-thousandth of his spiritual energy.

But this time, nearly more than half was consumed.

The problem did not lie with the target itself.

Shuichi recalled the feeling from just now.

That force pulling back did not come from the target’s resistance. The Edo Tensei body had been under his control from start to finish, without struggle, without resistance. It had no consciousness at all and could not actively resist.

That force came from another direction.

That thread deep within the soul.

During the Plunder, Shuichi had perceived the existence of that thread. It connected to the Edo Tensei body’s soul, extending far away, extending to a place that could not be reached.

What was that ?

Shuichi recalled his previous experience of Plundering the Red Fox.

When that fox struggled violently, the consumption had multiplied several times. But that was the fox’s own conscious resistance ; it was using willpower to resist the Plunder.

This time was different.

The Edo Tensei body did not resist, did not struggle, but that force still existed.

And that force did not come from the Edo Tensei body itself ; it came from the other end of that thread.

Shuichi closed his eyes, carefully recalling every detail from just now.

When the Plunder channel was established, his spiritual energy flowed toward the target, extracting information from the target’s soul. But during the flow, a force was pulling at him, wanting to suck away his spiritual energy.

That force came from deep within the soul.

Or rather, from the place the soul was connected to.

The Pure Land.

Shuichi opened his eyes.

He figured it out.

The souls brought back by Edo Tensei were, in essence, items deposited in the Pure Land.

The souls of the dead rested in the Pure Land, isolated from the worlds of the living and the dead. The Edo Tensei jutsu forcibly pulled the soul back to the living world, using dust to simulate a physical body, allowing it to reappear in the human world.

But there was still a connection between the soul and the Pure Land.

That thread connecting the worlds of the living and the dead had always existed. It was like a rope, tying the soul in the Pure Land ; even if the soul was pulled back to the living world, the other end of the rope was still in the hands of the Pure Land.

The life and death rules of the Pure Land were rejecting any attempt to forcibly extract things from deep within the soul.

Shuichi recalled his previous experience of Plundering the concept of Growth.

That time, he tried to Plunder power from the concept of Growth. Growth was one of the rules of the world, with the will of the world standing behind it. His forced Plunder was equivalent to challenging the rules.

As a result, he encountered resistance at the rule level, and his spiritual energy was frantically drained.

This time was the same.

Behind the Edo Tensei soul stood the rules of life and death.

His Plundering things from the soul was equivalent to challenging the rules of life and death.

And for opponents at the rule level, the toll was too expensive.

Shuichi stood on the wasteland.

The memories of an ordinary person had already drained nearly half of his spiritual energy.

What if the target was the soul of a Ninja ?

A Ninja’s memory volume was larger, their abilities stronger, and their souls deeper. Plundering a Ninja’s abilities or Bloodline Limit required delving deeper into the soul, reaching more core parts.

The consumption would be several or even dozens of times that of an ordinary person.

What if the target was a powerhouse with rule-level power ?

Shuichi thought of Uchiha Madara.

If that man’s soul were summoned by Edo Tensei, how strong would his power be ? At what level of the soul would he need to delve to Plunder his Wood Release ?

He might be directly drained by the backlash, turning into a hollow shell without consciousness.

The first path for Wood Release, going directly to Uchiha Madara, was too risky.

The second path for Wood Release, Plundering through Edo Tensei, consumed too much.

Both paths had hit a wall.

« Sigh. »

Shuichi let the Edo Tensei body disperse.

The ashes scattered on the ground, and the bandit who had been used as a sacrifice also dissipated, turning into nothingness.

He leaned against the stone wall, his gaze calm.

Edo Tensei could not be used as a source for Plunder for the time being.

In the future, when his spiritual energy reached another level, or when he obtained the Rinnegan to penetrate the rules of life and death, this path could be attempted again.

But not now.

Therefore, he must use the Mangekyo more sparingly in the future.

Every move he made was an advance on the future.

Shuichi organized the current situation in his mind.

The Byakugan had been fused, the Sharingan had evolved to Mangekyo, and the Plunder ability was continuously growing.

The intelligence network had been spread out, and the contents of the scroll of sealing had been memorized.

But he still needed stronger power.

Wood Release was still his biggest shortcoming.

Going directly to Uchiha Madara was too risky ; Edo Tensei consumed too much ; neither path was viable.

He needed a third path.

Or, he needed to make the existing paths viable.

Shuichi stood up straight and brushed the dust off his clothes.

Although more than half of his spiritual energy was consumed, what remained was enough to support his return to Konoha.

After returning and getting a good rest, his spiritual energy would recover.

He turned and walked in the direction of Konoha.

Behind him, the ashes were blown away by the wind, disappearing into the wasteland.

No one would know what had happened here.

Two bandits had gone missing, and no one would investigate.

Shuichi walked on the mountain path, his gaze calm.

Although this test had failed, it was not completely without gain.

At least he now knew the limitations of Edo Tensei.

Plundering the soul of an Edo Tensei body required facing the backlash of the rules of life and death. This was rule-level power, not something he could contend with at the moment.

In the future, when his spiritual energy was stronger, or when he had obtained the Rinnegan, this path could be tried again.

But for now, he needed to find other avenues.

Shuichi continued walking forward, his figure disappearing at the end of the mountain path.
