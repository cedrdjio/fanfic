# Chapitre 64 — Target: Byakugan

Two days later.

Shuichi sat in his room, watching Fang on the windowsill.

Fang was still crouching in the corner, with a few worker bees flying around it.

Over the past two days, the worker bees had caught a few ants and spiders one after another, and Shuichi had Plundered their pheromone codes and transferred them to Fang.

Fang was currently digesting these new codes.

Shuichi turned his head and looked at Lue.

Lue was crouching on the desk, tilting its head to look at him.

« Your abilities need an upgrade as well, » Shuichi said.

Lue’s wings fluttered slightly : « How do I upgrade ? »

« You already have Chakra, so you can learn Ninjutsu, » Shuichi said. « I plan to transfer some Ninjutsu knowledge to you. »

« What Ninjutsu ? »

« Wind Release. Combined with flight, it will significantly improve your speed and maneuverability, » Shuichi said. « You can also use Wind Blades to attack from a distance in the air, without relying on close-quarters combat. You can also use a Wind Barrier to protect yourself when encountering danger. »

Lue nodded : « Sounds good. »

« But Wind Release is just the foundation, » Shuichi leaned back in his chair. « I’m still thinking about what other abilities in Konoha might be suitable for you and me. »

Lue tilted its head and didn’t speak.

Shuichi’s gaze fell outside the window, looking towards the distant Hokage Building.

Konoha has many clans, and each clan has its own special abilities.

The Inuzuka Clan’s coordination with Ninja Dogs—Lue is a hawk, not a dog, so that doesn’t apply.

The Yamanaka Clan’s Mind Transfer Jutsu is not suitable for a reconnaissance role like Lue ; it’s better suited for an information-gathering role like Fang.

The Akimichi Clan’s Multi-Size Jutsu changes body size. Lue needs to be agile in the air, so becoming larger would be a burden.

However, I could get one for Haku when the time comes.

Finally, Shuichi thought of the Hyuga Clan.

Byakugan.

X-ray vision, long-range vision, and a 360-degree field of view.

If Lue could have the Byakugan’s X-ray vision, combined with its existing Infrared Sensing and low-light vision, its aerial reconnaissance could see directly through buildings and cover. Enemies hiding behind walls or underground would not be able to escape its eyes.

Furthermore, the Byakugan’s long-range vision would allow Lue to see details over a larger area from high altitude, and the 360-degree field of view means it could perceive all directions around it without having to turn its head.

This set of abilities is a perfect fit for Lue.

More importantly, Shuichi himself lacked this ability.

He currently possessed the Three-Tomoe Sharingan and Mangekyo ; his ability to predict movements and use Genjutsu were strong enough. However, he lacked the ability to see through walls. When enemies hid behind cover, he could only rely on sensing Chakra fluctuations to determine their position, which wasn’t precise enough.

If he Plundered a Byakugan and integrated the X-ray vision function into his own Sharingan, it would perfectly fill this gap.

Shuichi’s fingers tapped lightly on the desk.

The Byakugan is the lifeblood of the Hyuga ; they would investigate to the end if even one pair went missing. The Hyuga Clan protects the Byakugan extremely strictly. Because of the Caged Bird Seal, the Byakugan is sealed after a branch family member dies, making it impossible for outsiders to obtain.

But these were not problems for Shuichi.

He could Plunder their abilities before they died, though the cost would be higher.

The only problem was the bottom line he had always adhered to in Konoha : not to kill people from the same Village.

Shuichi leaned back in his chair and closed his eyes.

Why had he always adhered to this bottom line before ?

It seemed to be because of the risk.

Once he killed someone from the same Village, even if the probability of being found was small, Shuichi had previously been unwilling to gamble ; the risk was not proportional to the reward.

But now.

Shuichi opened his eyes and looked at his hands.

He now possessed the power to no longer fear the Village called Konoha.

He had absolute certainty that no one could discover him after he killed someone.

As for gratitude.

In this life, Konoha had protected him. As a child of the Village, this protection was real. His mother could live a stable life, and he could attend the Ninja Academy—these were all provided by Konoha.

But that debt had already been repaid when he let Jiraiya go.

Neither owed the other anything.

For him, Konoha was now just a place to live.

Because his mother lived here, he stayed here. Nothing else was that important.

And now, his strength was sufficient to protect his mother.

He no longer needed to rely on the Village, nor did he need to rely on anyone.

Shuichi stood up and walked to the window.

Then, that previous bottom line should no longer exist.

Shuichi looked out the window, his gaze calm.

He did not intend to kill indiscriminately.

He had a basic moral bottom line in his past life, and he retained it in this one.

But if some people within Konoha happened to have abilities he needed—Byakugan, special Ninjutsu, some kind of Kekkei Genkai—then he would just take them.

Leave no trace, harm no innocent, only take the abilities.

Shuichi turned his head and looked at Lue.

« You learn the basics of Wind Release first, » Shuichi said. « When I find a suitable opportunity, I will help you get the Byakugan. »

Lue nodded : « Okay. »

Shuichi walked back to the desk and placed his right hand on Lue.

« I will transfer the Wind Release knowledge directly to you. »

Granting started.

Wind Release nature transformation.

Shuichi had previously Plundered the memories of a Wind-style Ninja, which contained the basic knowledge and combat experience of Wind Release. Now, he stripped these pieces of knowledge out and transferred them to Lue.

A few seconds later, the granting was complete.

Shuichi withdrew his hand.

Lue’s wings fluttered slightly, and a faint current of wind formed at the edges of its wings, cutting through the air.

« How do you feel ? » Shuichi asked.

« It’s amazing, » Lue said. « I know what to do now. »

« Practice first, » Shuichi said. « You have the knowledge ; the rest is proficiency. »

Lue nodded and continued practicing.

Shuichi watched Lue practice for a while, then asked : « How is your Wind Release practice going ? »

At the edges of Lue’s wings, the wind current was much more stable than before, and cutting through the air produced a slight humming sound.

« A bit more proficient, » Lue said.

« Hmm, continue, » Shuichi nodded, then said, « Also, there is one thing. »

Lue stopped its movements and looked at him.

Shuichi’s gaze was calm, his tone flat : « Go and find two branch family members of the Hyuga as targets. »

Lue tilted its head.

« You want to take action against the Hyuga Clan ? » Lue asked.

« Yes. »

« Why two ? »

« One for you, one for me. The Byakugan’s X-ray vision, long-range vision, and 360-degree field of view are useful for both of us. Remember to just find branch family members ; there’s no need to make a big scene. »

« Who are the targets ? »

« That is for you to determine. »

Lue was silent for a moment.

It was thinking.

This was obviously not an ordinary reconnaissance mission.

Shuichi had always given Lue clear mission targets before.

But this time, he didn’t ; Shuichi only let it find the targets, meaning the choice was in its hands.

Lue looked at Shuichi, its gaze becoming serious.

It understood.

This was a test from Shuichi. It wasn’t about finding just any two people, but about learning to judge—judging who should be killed and who could be spared.

« Okay, » Lue said. « I will keep an eye on the activity in the Hyuga compound and tell you once I find suitable targets. »

Shuichi looked at Lue, the corners of his mouth slightly upturned.

« Hmm, » Shuichi nodded. « I will take action after you find the targets. »

Lue continued to practice Wind Release, and the wind current at the edges of its wings stabilized again.

Shuichi leaned back in his chair, looking out the window.

Lue understood his meaning.

But it didn’t ask why, didn’t question that the targets were from the same Village, and didn’t hesitate about whether it could be done.

It simply accepted the mission and began to think about how to choose.

Not just obeying orders, but also being able to understand his intentions and having the ability to judge for itself.

This was the kind of partner Shuichi needed.
