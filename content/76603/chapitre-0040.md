# Chapitre 40 — Half a Year of Preparation

Half a year later. Spring of the 45th year of the Hokage era.

Shuichi stood on the flat clearing in the pine forest deep within the Forest of Death, his right palm facing upward.

A cluster of something lay quietly in his palm. It was not fire, nor was it an electric arc ; it was a transparent, faintly glowing sphere. This object was the « Reality » concept that Shuichi had spent half a year accumulating, plundered from every single prey in the Forest of Death.

The « Reality » of an ant was so faint it was almost imperceptible ; the « Reality » of a wild rabbit was slightly stronger ; the « Reality » of a bear was so intense that it made Shuichi’s mental barrier tremble slightly. Over the past six months, Shuichi had plundered hundreds of « Reality » concepts from hundreds of creatures, compressing and accumulating them within the mental barrier in his right palm.

Enough.

Shuichi didn’t have one hundred percent certainty, but he felt it was about enough. The « Reality » concept sphere in his palm had expanded from the size of a grain of rice to the size of a chicken egg, and its internal density was becoming increasingly high. Shuichi tried probing the sphere’s capacity with his mental energy, feeling a sense of fullness and saturation, just like a cup about to overflow with water.

He took a deep breath and formed hand seals with both hands.

Cross seal.

Chakra split, and a Shadow Clone appeared before him.

Shuichi looked at the clone. It was exactly the same as the first time he created a Shadow Clone half a year ago—appearance, clothing, expression, all completely identical. But this time, Shuichi wasn’t here to test the stability of the clone.

He reached out his right hand and pressed it against the clone’s chest.

Perception expanded. The internal structure of the Shadow Clone unfolded in his mind : the Chakra pathways, the nodes maintaining its form, the mental energy channels. That « False » core point he couldn’t touch half a year ago was still there, emitting fluctuations on a conceptual level.

Shuichi did not touch that point.

He shifted his attention to the « Reality » concept sphere in his palm.

Grant.

Shuichi pushed the « Reality » concept sphere from his right hand into the Shadow Clone’s chest. The sphere passed through the clone’s skin, moving along the Chakra pathways toward the core. Shuichi used his mental energy to guide the sphere’s path, ensuring it wouldn’t dissipate along the way.

The sphere reached the clone’s core.

It collided with that « False » core point.

Shuichi felt a strange reaction. « Reality » and « Falsehood » met at the clone’s core, like two streams of water at different temperatures merging. It wasn’t a confrontation, nor was it repulsion ; rather, « Reality » was wrapping around « Falsehood, » covering « Falsehood » from the inside.

The clone’s body began to change.

Shuichi looked at the clone’s skin. A subtle luster appeared on the appearance that was originally indistinguishable from a real person, as if a layer of transparent film had been plated onto the skin’s surface. The luster lasted for about three seconds, then faded. The clone looked no different than before.

But Shuichi could feel the difference.

The clone’s Chakra consumption rate had slowed down. The amount of Chakra the Shadow Clone consumed per minute while existing had now dropped to one-tenth of its original amount. The clone no longer needed to consume a large amount of Chakra to maintain its « False » form—the « Reality » concept had covered the « Falsehood, » and the clone had become a true entity.

Shuichi took a Kunai from his Ninja tool pouch and stabbed it into the clone’s arm.

The Kunai pierced the skin, and blood flowed from the wound. Red, warm, real blood.

The clone did not dissipate.

Shuichi looked at the wound on the clone’s arm. The wound was bleeding, and the clone was frowning ; it could feel real pain. But the clone did not collapse, nor did it dissipate. It was injured and bleeding like a real person, but it did not disappear.

« Granting Reality » was a success.

Subsequently, Shuichi once again granted the clone two other abilities.

The abilities granted to a Shadow Clone needed to be carefully chosen. The clone’s Chakra reserves were limited, so he couldn’t grant abilities that consumed too much, nor could he grant abilities that required Chakra to drive. Shuichi referenced his own list of abilities and filtered out several concepts suitable for the clone to use.

The first was the « Recovery » concept, plundered from a mountain antelope. « Recovery » could accelerate self-healing, physical stamina recovery, and Chakra recovery ; for himself, it was a powerful sustain ability.

After granting it to the clone, the clone gained the ability to slowly heal wounds and recover Chakra. The recovery speed wasn’t fast ; a shallow scratch would take about half a day to fully heal, and the Chakra recovery speed was also much slower than the original body.

But for a clone whose Chakra consumption had already dropped to one-tenth, this recovery speed was enough to form a balance between daily consumption and recovery. A clone that could originally be maintained for one to two months could now last for three to four months after adding the « Recovery » concept.

The second was the « Toughness » concept, plundered from a bear. « Toughness » made his skin and muscle density far exceed that of an ordinary Ninja ; minor attacks were almost ineffective against him.

After granting it to the clone, the clone’s body also became more resistant to impacts.

Ordinary pushing and shoving wouldn’t cause damage, and minor scratches wouldn’t cause it to dissipate. Only when receiving relatively serious attacks, such as a Kunai stabbing or a shuriken hitting, would it be injured and bleed.

Shuichi looked at the wound on the clone’s arm. Under the effect of the « Recovery » concept, the bleeding speed of the wound was slowing down, and signs of slight healing were beginning to appear at the edges. At this rate, the wound would be completely healed in about half a day.

Shuichi looked at the Shadow Clone before him.

The « Reality » concept made the clone a physical entity that wouldn’t dissipate. The « Recovery » concept allowed the clone to slowly heal wounds and recover Chakra, extending its maintenance time from one to two months to three to four months. The « Toughness » concept made the clone harder to injure. Three concepts stacked, this Shadow Clone was no longer an ordinary Shadow Clone.

But the clone also had limitations. It couldn’t do things that consumed a lot of Chakra, couldn’t activate the Sharingan, couldn’t perform Ninjutsu, and couldn’t engage in intense combat. Although its Chakra consumption had dropped to one-tenth, its total amount was still limited.

Daily activities like going to school, walking, eating, and chatting consumed very little, so the clone could handle them easily. But once it involved large-scale Chakra consumption, the clone wouldn’t be able to hold on.

Therefore, the clone had to continue hiding its strength at school. It couldn’t fight with classmates, couldn’t perform too well in Taijutsu class, and couldn’t do anything that might consume Chakra. It was exactly the same as what Shuichi had been doing at school before.

Shuichi stood up and looked at the clone.

« From now on, you are Uchiha Xiu Yi, » Shuichi said.

« I know, » the clone said, its voice exactly the same as Shuichi’s.

« Go to school every day, come home after school, and eat with Minako. Chat with Obito, and practice footwork with Tetsuya. »

« I know. »

« Do not expose your strength. Do not activate the Sharingan. Do not use Ninjutsu. Do not do anything that consumes a lot of Chakra. »

« I know. »

Shuichi looked into the clone’s eyes. The clone’s gaze was as calm and composed as Shuichi’s own. It possessed Shuichi’s memories and personality and knew what to do.

Shuichi turned and walked toward the edge of the pine clearing.

---

In these six months, besides accumulating the « Reality » concept, Shuichi had also plundered some useful abilities for himself and Lie.

For example, he plundered Thermal Sensing from a snake. Now he could distinguish details of heat sources ; he could not only see the outline of a heat source but also the temperature distribution within it. This ability was very useful when hunting Ninjas, as he could judge the activity level of the opponent’s Chakra in advance and assess their strength level.

He plundered reaction ability from a cat, and combined with Sakumo’s experience, basically no one could assassinate him.

As for Lie, it had plundered night vision from a cat, significantly enhancing its nighttime mobility.

It also plundered Infrared Sensing from a snake. Like Shuichi, the precision of Lie’s Infrared Sensing had increased threefold. This was very useful for Lie when out scouting, as it could identify the Chakra activity level of targets at a greater distance.

It also plundered a faster diving speed from a falcon. Lie’s diving speed was already far superior to an ordinary eagle, and this time, another falcon’s diving speed was stacked onto it, allowing it to reach over sixty meters per second during high-speed dives.

Shuichi had digested these abilities one after another within half a year. Now, both Shuichi and Lie’s sensory capabilities and mobility were far stronger than they had been half a year ago.

---

Shuichi walked out of the pine forest and walked home along the Naka River.

He was thinking about tomorrow’s arrangements.

Tomorrow morning, the Shadow Clone would replace Shuichi to go to school. And Shuichi’s real body would leave Konoha.

The method of leaving was very simple : first leave Konoha, then use the Transformation Technique to change his appearance, and add a « Reality » concept to the transformed form, making the transformation not just an ordinary illusion, but a more stable disguise. As for what exactly to disguise as, Shuichi had an interesting idea.

Lie’s disguise was also ready. Shuichi had plundered physical characteristics from several different species of birds—the color of feathers, the size of the body, the shape of the wings. After granting them to Lie, Lie’s appearance would change into that of another bird, no longer that giant eagle with a wingspan of over two meters.

Shuichi had also decided on the direction after leaving the village.

The reason for Lie’s departure had been handled in advance.

Half a month ago, Shuichi had started making Lie disappear from the public eye.

Minako had also noticed Lie’s disappearance and asked Shuichi about it.

Shuichi replied : « Lie was lured away by an eagle ; I don’t know when it will be back. »

Minako’s expression was quite colorful when she heard Shuichi’s reason, but she accepted it quickly.

So when Shuichi and Lie left Konoha together, no one would notice that Lie was gone. Because Lie had already disappeared from the public eye for half a month.
