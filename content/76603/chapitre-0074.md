# Chapitre 74 — Uchiha Madara

Night fell.

Shuichi left Konoha Village and arrived at a forest outside the Village.

This was the place he and White Zetsu had agreed upon.

It was far from the Village, surrounded by an open grassy field. The moonlight spilled down, dyeing the grass a silvery white.

Shuichi leaned against a tree trunk, his gaze sweeping over the surrounding environment.

Everything was very quiet.

He waited for about ten minutes.

The soil began to rise.

Shuichi’s perception instantly extended over, probing the situation underground. But he sensed nothing, save for an extremely faint Chakra fluctuation, so weak it could almost be ignored.

White Zetsu emerged from the ground, its hollow face turned toward him.

It was a face without features, as smooth as wax. The moonlight shone upon it, reflecting a faint white light.

« Follow me. »

White Zetsu’s voice was airy, as if it were coming from a great distance.

Shuichi nodded and followed White Zetsu forward.

Shuichi followed White Zetsu, passing through the mountain forest and heading north.

They advanced along the mountain path at a fast pace. White Zetsu led the way in front, its body gliding over the ground as if soundless. Its footsteps made no noise at all ; it didn’t even disturb the leaves on the ground.

Shuichi followed behind, his steps light, making almost no sound.

The night was deep ; moonlight spilled through the gaps in the leaves, casting dappled light and shadows on the ground. The mountain forest was very quiet, with only the occasional sound of insects chirping and night birds calling.

As he walked, Shuichi observed the surrounding environment.

They were heading toward the border of the Land of Fire. If they continued north, they would reach the border between the Land of Fire and the Land of Grass.

Where was Madara’s hiding place ?

Shuichi searched his memories from his past life.

According to what he knew, Madara had faked his death after the battle at the Valley of the End and had been operating in the shadows ever since. The current Madara should be in an elderly state, half-dead, relying on the Gedo Statue to sustain his life.

Where was that Gedo Statue ?

Shuichi thought of Kannabi Bridge.

That was a bridge on the border between the Land of Fire and the Land of Grass.

Memories of that battle surfaced in Shuichi’s mind. The Battle of Kannabi Bridge.

Before this, Obito had been crushed by rocks and had given his Sharingan to Kakashi. Then, Obito was saved by Madara and taken to Madara’s base to recover.

Later, Obito witnessed with his own eyes Kakashi using Raikiri to kill Rin. At that moment, Obito’s world collapsed.

And that scene had taken place near Kannabi Bridge.

Obito had been brought here by Madara to be saved. Therefore, Madara’s hiding place was most likely in this area.

Shuichi suddenly felt some regret.

He should have thought of this when he came to the Land of Grass before. Kannabi Bridge was right on the border of the Land of Fire and the Land of Grass ; he hadn’t even thought to go and investigate when he passed by.

If he had thought of it then, perhaps he could have recalled Madara and thought of White Zetsu, and could have avoided some things.

They continued walking forward, passing through forest after forest.

Shuichi remained vigilant, ready at any moment to deal with potential danger.

His right hand rested on the short blade at his waist, and Chakra flowed slowly within his body. Although his Mangekyo Sharingan had not yet been awakened, he could activate it whenever he wished.

White Zetsu led the way in front, not speaking.

It just walked silently, its hollow face turned forward, like a puppet without a soul.

Shuichi looked at White Zetsu’s back, his mind contemplating various possibilities.

White Zetsu was Madara’s eyes and ears and a tool for intelligence gathering. Through White Zetsu, Madara monitored the Ninja World and obtained information.

White Zetsu had been tracking him for a long time, starting from the Land of Grass all the way until he returned to Konoha Village. This meant Madara knew his actions like the back of his hand.

Did Madara know he had the Plunder ability ?

He should know. White Zetsu had seen all his actions in the Land of Grass, including the process of him using Plunder to take abilities from enemies.

So, what was Madara’s purpose in having him come to meet ?

To recruit ? To use ? Or something else ?

Shuichi suppressed the questions in his heart and continued following White Zetsu forward.

They walked for about five hours.

The mountain path became increasingly remote, and the surrounding trees became increasingly sparse. They passed through a wasteland and arrived near a bridge.

Shuichi saw that bridge.

It was an ancient stone bridge spanning a river. The bridge was very long and narrow, with steep cliffs on both sides. The moonlight shone on the bridge deck, reflecting a faint white light.

Kannabi Bridge.

White Zetsu did not head toward the bridge but instead led Shuichi toward the mountainous area near the bridge.

After walking for about ten minutes, they arrived at the entrance of a hidden cave.

White Zetsu stopped.

« We’re here. »

It turned to look at Shuichi, its hollow face turned toward him.

« Lord Madara is waiting for you inside. »

Shuichi looked at the cave before him.

The entrance was covered by vines and shrubs ; if one didn’t look carefully, it would be hard to spot. Moonlight shone down from above, illuminating a small area near the entrance.

Shuichi walked into the cave.

His Sharingan rotated slightly, the Three Tomoe Sharingan glowing red in the darkness. Within the vision of the Sharingan, the darkness was no longer an obstacle ; he could clearly see the path ahead.

The inside of the cave was very deep, and the further in he went, the more spacious it became. Complex sealing formula patterns were carved into the ground, with Chakra flowing slowly through the patterns, emitting a faint light. That light had a light blue hue, as if the Chakra itself were flowing.

After walking about several hundred meters, a brighter light appeared ahead.

Shuichi stood there and took a deep breath.

Then he continued walking inward.

The interior of the cave was very spacious, with a height of over twenty meters and a width of over thirty meters.

The ground was covered with sealing formula patterns, dense and packed, like a giant web. Chakra flowed slowly through the patterns, emitting a faint light. Those lights flickered in the cave, illuminating the surrounding stone walls.

At the end of the cave, there was a huge stone platform.

On the stone platform sat an old man.

Shuichi’s gaze fell on that old man.

His hair was very long, gray-white, and draped over his shoulders. His face was covered in wrinkles, his skin so pale it lacked any color, like a dead person. His body was very thin, so thin that one could almost see the outline of his bones, with his ribs protruding one by one. His chest showed almost no rise and fall, and his breathing was so faint it was almost imperceptible.

He sat on the stone platform, his body motionless, like a mummified corpse.

But his eyes were very bright.

In those eyes, the patterns of the Three Tomoe Sharingan were slowly rotating. Red light flickered in his pupils, carrying a deep, profound feeling.

Uchiha Madara.

Shuichi stopped and looked at the old man on the stone platform.

This was one of the legendary strongest men in the Ninja World.

This was one of the founders of Konoha.

This was the man who had fought side-by-side with Senju Hashirama, and had also been his enemy.

Shuichi stood in place, observing Madara’s state.

Madara’s body was indeed in very poor condition.

His muscles had almost completely atrophied, his skin tightly clinging to his bones. His Chakra flow was very weak, as if it could break at any moment. His Vitality was ebbing away, barely maintained by some external force.

That external force was likely the Gedo Statue.

Shuichi’s gaze swept across the depths of the cave.

Behind the stone platform, a huge figure was bound by chains. It was a humanoid existence, but its skin was gray, its eyes were empty, and faint Chakra mist drifted around its body.

Gedo Statue.

It was bound by chains in the depths of the cave, and Chakra flowed through pipes into Madara’s body, sustaining his life.

This was Madara’s current state.

Half-dead, relying on the Gedo Statue to sustain his life.
