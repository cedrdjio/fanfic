# Chapitre 57 — Crushing

Jiraiya took a deep breath, and Sage Chakra surged within his body.

Since the other party had already admitted it, there was no need to say anything more.

« Elder Fukasaku, Elder Shima, I’ll have to trouble you. »

« Understood, » the two toads spoke simultaneously.

Jiraiya formed hand seals, and his Sage Chakra erupted.

« Sage Art : Goemon ! »

He took a deep breath and spat out a stream of scorching flames. Elder Fukasaku simultaneously spat out oil, and Elder Shima spat out a powerful gust of wind. The three combined in mid-air, forming a terrifying firestorm that swept towards Shuichi.

Shuichi stood in place, watching the firestorm.

He activated his Sharingan.

Within the scarlet pupils of the Mangekyo, black lines radiated outward from the edges of the pupils, like countless broken threads.

« Left Eye : Magatsuhi. »

« Turning truth into falsehood. »

The firestorm became transparent in front of Shuichi, as if it were an illusion. It continued to move forward, but the scorching heat vanished, and the color of the flames dimmed.

The firestorm passed through Shuichi’s body without causing any harm.

Then, behind Shuichi, the firestorm became real again and continued to spread, eventually striking the stone wall of Hozuki Castle, scorching it red.

Jiraiya’s pupils constricted.

« What ? ! »

Elder Fukasaku and Elder Shima were simultaneously shocked.

« The firestorm went right through him ? »

Jiraiya’s brow furrowed tightly.

What was that ability just now ?

The firestorm was clearly real, but it turned into a false illusion when it passed through that person’s body. Then, after passing through, it turned back into reality.

Turning a real attack into a false one briefly...

« Be careful, » Elder Fukasaku said, « There is something wrong with his eyes. That is a Mangekyo Sharingan, and its ability is stronger than a normal Sharingan. »

« Mangekyo ? » Jiraiya’s pupils shrank.

He had heard of the Mangekyo Sharingan but had never seen one. It was said that the Mangekyo Sharingan possessed unique abilities, and the ability of each Mangekyo was different.

And this person’s Mangekyo ability... could it render attacks ineffective ?

Jiraiya formed hand seals again, this time choosing to use a Ninjutsu with physical substance.

« Sage Art : Hair Needle Senbon ! »

His hair stood on end, transforming into countless needles that shot towards Shuichi. Each needle contained Sage Chakra and had immense penetrating power.

Shuichi stood in place without dodging.

The hair needles became transparent in front of Shuichi, like an illusion. They passed through Shuichi’s body without causing any harm, and then became real again behind him, shooting into the ground.

Jiraiya’s expression changed.

It happened again ?

Ninjutsu was ineffective against him.

« Little Jiraiya, this ability is very troublesome, » Elder Fukasaku said. « He can make your attacks briefly ineffective ; your Ninjutsu cannot hit him. »

« I know, » Jiraiya gritted his teeth.

Since Ninjutsu was ineffective, he would use close-quarters combat.

Jiraiya’s Chakra erupted beneath his feet, and he used the Body Flicker Technique to charge at Shuichi.

His right fist, imbued with Sage Chakra, slammed towards Shuichi’s face.

Shuichi watched him charge, his Mangekyo Sharingan still active.

But this time, instead of turning Jiraiya’s attack false, he made himself false.

Shuichi’s body became transparent, like an illusion.

Jiraiya’s fist passed through Shuichi’s face without any obstruction.

Jiraiya’s pupils constricted.

His body ?

Before Jiraiya could react, Shuichi’s right leg was already raised, and he kicked Jiraiya in the abdomen.

Bang !

Jiraiya was kicked away, slamming heavily into the stone wall behind him, and spat out a mouthful of blood.

He fell to the ground, gasping for air.

« Jiraiya ! » Elder Fukasaku and Elder Shima cried out in alarm.

Jiraiya gritted his teeth and stood up, wiping the blood from the corner of his mouth.

That person just now... turned himself into a false illusion briefly. That was why his fist passed through his body and couldn’t hit him.

And the moment his fist passed through, he countered.

Jiraiya’s mind raced.

Ninjutsu was ineffective, and close-quarters combat was also ineffective. That person could turn his own attacks into falsehoods, and could also turn himself into a falsehood.

So how could he fight him ?

The only way was to attack at a moment when he could not turn intangible.

But when was the moment he could not turn intangible ?

Jiraiya couldn’t figure it out.

Just as he was thinking, Shuichi’s attack arrived.

He used Genjutsu, and countless black spears appeared out of thin air around Jiraiya, pointing at him from all directions.

Front, back, left, right, up, down—there were over a dozen spears in every direction.

Dense and overlapping, like an ocean of spears.

Those spears looked very real, but Jiraiya could feel that they had no Chakra fluctuations.

An illusion ?

The enemy wouldn’t use such an obvious illusion.

So even though he perceived these spears as illusions, Jiraiya still reacted.

He formed hand seals, and Sage Chakra surged.

« Sage Art : Hair Needle Senbon ! »

His hair stood on end, transforming into countless needles that shot towards the surrounding spears.

The hair needles shot towards the spears.

They passed right through.

Without any obstruction, the hair needles passed directly through the spears and shot into the distance.

Jiraiya’s pupils constricted.

Was it really an illusion ?

No, that’s not right !

He used the Body Flicker Technique to leap backward, attempting to dodge.

But there was nowhere to hide.

Those spears were too dense and numerous, and every direction was sealed off.

Just as these illusions were about to hit Jiraiya,

Shuichi’s right eye turned slightly.

Those spears suddenly became real.

« Musubi. »

Over a dozen spears pierced towards Jiraiya from all directions, at an astonishing speed.

Jiraiya couldn’t dodge in time.

He desperately used the Body Flicker Technique to rush into a gap, but he was still struck by seven or eight spears.

The spears passed through his body, causing massive damage, but they all avoided his vital points.

Blood sprayed out.

Jiraiya fell to the ground, gasping for air. His body was pierced by seven or eight spears, the wounds deep enough to see bone.

« Jiraiya ! » Elder Fukasaku and Elder Shima cried out in alarm.

« Summoning Technique : Gamabunta ! »

Jiraiya gritted his teeth and used his last bit of strength to summon Gamabunta. The huge orange toad appeared, blocking in front of him.

Gamabunta looked at the wounds on Jiraiya’s body, his toad eyes filled with shock.

« Jiraiya, you... »

« Don’t speak, » Jiraiya gritted his teeth. « Block him. »

Shuichi stood in place, Mangekyo Sharingan still active, watching the heavily injured Jiraiya.

Jiraiya gritted his teeth and remained silent.

He had indeed dodged, but those spears were too numerous, and every direction had been sealed off ; there was simply nowhere to dodge.

Even though he felt no physical entity in his perception, they could avoid all attacks and hit him precisely.

This kind of ability was too terrifying.

« Little Jiraiya, your injuries are severe, » Elder Fukasaku said. « You must retreat immediately. »

« I know, » Jiraiya gritted his teeth.

He couldn’t stand up ; his body was pierced by spears, and blood kept flowing out.

Shuichi looked at the heavily injured Jiraiya, a smile appearing on his face.

He took a step forward.

Body Flicker Technique.

Shuichi’s figure disappeared from where he stood, appearing in front of Gamabunta the next instant.

Gamabunta opened his mouth, preparing to attack.

Shuichi looked at the huge toad.

He raised his right hand, his palm facing Gamabunta.

A Chakra shockwave shot from Shuichi’s palm, hitting Gamabunta in the face.

Bang !

Gamabunta was knocked back, his massive body sliding back a few meters and crashing into the stone wall.

This was an ability Shuichi had plundered, specifically targeting large summon beasts.

Shuichi withdrew his hand and walked towards Jiraiya.

He stood in front of Jiraiya, looking down at him.

« You lost. »

Jiraiya looked at the Mangekyo Sharingan in front of him, a sense of powerlessness welling up in his heart.

He had lost.

And he had lost badly.

« However, since you are from the Village founded by Uchiha Madara, and you have this kind of strength at your age, I will spare your life. »

Jiraiya’s pupils constricted violently.

Madara ?

Uchiha Madara ?

This person... did he really know Madara ?

« Who... exactly are you ? » Jiraiya asked with difficulty.

Shuichi did not answer.

He turned around, Mangekyo Sharingan still active.

« An old acquaintance. »

Then, he took a step and walked out of Hozuki Castle.

Lue descended from high above and landed on Shuichi’s shoulder.

« There is nothing valuable left in Hozuki Castle. Those Chunin and Genin are no longer of any use for my improvement. »

Shuichi said, « Let’s go. »

He walked out of Hozuki Castle, stepping on the surface of the lake, and walked into the distance.

His silhouette stretched long under the sunset, and the scarlet glow of the Mangekyo Sharingan gradually faded in the dusk.

Jiraiya lay behind Gamabunta, watching Shuichi’s departing figure, unable to move for a long time.

Elder Fukasaku said in a low voice, « Little Jiraiya, was what he said true ? Does he really know Uchiha Madara ? »

« I don’t know, » Jiraiya said with difficulty. « But the strength he displayed qualifies him to say such things. »

« And his eyes... » Elder Shima said. « That is the Mangekyo Sharingan. Being able to turn reality into falsehood, and illusion into reality... I have never heard of such an ability. »

Gamabunta spoke, « Jiraiya, your injuries are severe, you must be treated immediately. »

« I know, » Jiraiya gritted his teeth. « Let’s go back first... and report to the Hokage. »
