# Chapitre 34 — Sakumo Hatake

Saturday morning. Shuichi rarely chose to practice Taijutsu on the hillside north of the Memorial Stone.

Because this place is about two kilometers away from the Memorial Stone, separated by a dense forest. Shuichi chose this location because it was close enough, yet wouldn’t be seen by people in front of the Memorial Stone. He practiced footwork on a flat ground on the hillside—forward steps, backward steps, footwork transitions—just like he did at the Training Ground.

Just as the sky brightened, Lue sent a message.

Shuichi looked up. Lue circled twice in the sky in the distance, then flew toward the Memorial Stone.

Two circles. Sakumo had gone out, heading toward the Memorial Stone.

Shuichi slowed his footwork and continued practicing on the hillside. He didn’t need to go right now ; Sakumo going to the Memorial Stone didn’t mean he would act today. Sakumo went to the Memorial Stone every day, stood for a long time, and then went home. Today might be the same.

Shuichi waited on the hillside for about an hour.

Then Lue circled twice in the sky.

Shuichi stopped his footwork. Two circles. And another two circles. Two circles when Sakumo went out, and another two now meant Sakumo’s state was abnormal. Perhaps he had fallen, or perhaps he had done something unusual.

Shuichi did not hesitate. He ran quickly along the hillside toward the Memorial Stone. He passed through the dense forest, walked up a flight of stone steps, and turned a corner.

The Memorial Stone was in view.

Sakumo Hatake stood in front of the Memorial Stone, looking at a name on the stone.

Takuya Akiyama.

This name was carved in the upper left corner of the stone, the strokes deep. Takuya Akiyama, a Konoha Chunin, a teammate on Sakumo’s team. Ten years ago, during a mission, Sakumo chose to complete the mission and did not turn back to save Akiyama, who was surrounded by enemies. The mission was a success, but his teammate Akiyama died.

On the night the mission was completed, Sakumo stood beside Akiyama’s body without speaking. He had made a choice according to the Ninja Code. Mission first, comrades second. Intelligence was important, and Akiyama’s sacrifice was a necessary cost.

But no one mentioned Akiyama.

After the mission was completed, the Village only discussed the value of the intelligence. Akiyama’s name was written on the Memorial Stone and then forgotten. His wife raised their child alone, and no one visited her. His son was told at school, 'Your dad died because he wasn’t strong enough.' The mission report Sakumo submitted truthfully recorded Akiyama’s sacrifice, but the report was filed away and never read again.

Sakumo squatted down and reached out to touch the name on the stone. The surface of the stone was cool ; ten years of wind and frost had worn the strokes somewhat blurry.

« Takuya, » Sakumo said, his voice very soft, as if talking to Akiyama, « Ten years ago, I chose the mission, and you died. No one remembers you. Your wife is raising your child alone, and your son is mocked at school. I made the choice required by the Ninja Code, but your death had no meaning. »

The wind blew, and the withered flowers in front of the stone trembled.

« This time, I chose to save my comrade, » Sakumo said, « The mission failed. But at least the person I saved is still alive. At least someone will remember that he is alive. »

He stood up and looked at Akiyama’s name on the stone.

« Two choices. Ten years ago, I chose the mission, my teammate died, and no one cared. This time, I chose my comrade, the mission failed, and the whole Village cursed me. » Sakumo’s voice grew softer and softer, « Which one is right ? Or are they both wrong ? I don’t know anymore. »

He drew the short sword 'White Light' from his waist ; the blade emitted a cold white light in the morning sun. This sword had been with him for over twenty years, killing countless enemies and protecting countless comrades. There was a shallow notch on the blade, left from that mission ten years ago. At that time, he was fighting the enemy, and Akiyama was surrounded on the other side. He chose to complete the mission instead of saving Akiyama. The notch was chipped when his blade struck an enemy’s Kunai, and he had never repaired it.

Sakumo looked at the notch on the blade for a long time.

« I can’t find the answer, » he said.

He turned the short sword toward himself, the tip pointing at his left chest.

The blade pierced in.

When Shuichi walked to the Memorial Stone, Sakumo had already fallen.

His upper body lay on the ground in front of the stone, his right hand hanging by his side, fingers loose. The short sword 'White Light' was stuck in his left chest, the hilt exposed, and the blood on the blade flowed down along the hilt, pooling into a small dark red patch on the stone slab in front of the monument.

Shuichi stood ten steps away, looking at Sakumo’s back. Silver-white hair was scattered on the ground, and the lean body lay in front of the stone. The smell of blood permeated the damp air, and the scent, like rust, entered Shuichi’s nostrils.

Shuichi stepped closer, confirming that Sakumo was dead. The short sword had pierced the heart precisely ; it was a fatal blow. The time of death was about a few minutes ago.

Shuichi squatted down and placed his right hand on the back of Sakumo’s head.

Perception expanded. The life signals in Sakumo’s body had disappeared, but the neural pathways had not yet fully cooled. Combat experience was stored in the neural pathways, especially the tactical judgment area of the forebrain and the combat instinct area of the brainstem. Shuichi needed to complete the Plunder before these neural pathways cooled.

Plunder. Target : Combat experience.

The amount of spiritual energy extracted was larger than Shuichi expected. Sakumo’s combat experience was extremely rich, accumulated over decades of actual combat, from Genin to Kage-level Ninja, the complete combat journey, the neural memories of every life-and-death struggle, every decision-making loop of tactical judgment, all were stripped away. Shuichi felt as if he were trying to drain a lake with a straw ; the consumption rate of spiritual energy far exceeded his expectations.

The Sharingan activated automatically. Two tomoe rotated violently at the edge of the pupils, breaking down Sakumo’s neural signals into recognizable templates. Shuichi gritted his teeth to maintain the suction, ensuring that every combat experience template was completely stripped.

About fifteen seconds.

Shuichi withdrew his right hand, palm facing up. The thing in his palm was heavier than any previous time. It was not warm ; it was scorching. Sakumo’s combat experience was like a flame still burning, its internal structure so complex that Shuichi could not see the whole picture at once. Close-quarters combat, Ninjutsu combinations, tactical judgment, battlefield intuition, opponent analysis, terrain utilization, team cooperation—every neural pathway was engraved with Sakumo’s decades of blood and fire.

Shuichi took a deep breath. He couldn’t fuse it now. The amount of combat experience was too large ; fusion required time and energy, which was not something he could do in front of the Memorial Stone. He needed to stabilize this mass first, take it home, and then slowly digest it.

Shuichi compressed the combat experience in his palm and locked it in a spiritual energy barrier in his right palm. The compression process consumed a lot of spiritual energy, but it was finally stabilized. A continuous burning sensation came from his palm, like holding a wrapped flame.

Shuichi stood up and glanced at Sakumo’s body. The short sword 'White Light' was stuck in his chest, the white light on the hilt flickering slightly in the morning sun. Shuichi did not pull out the White Light ; that would cause unnecessary investigation.

He turned and walked back. Lue flew down from the sky and landed on his shoulder.

« Let’s go, » Shuichi said.

Lue nodded.

Shuichi walked down the hillside, his steps as steady as usual. He walked past the stone steps, through the dense forest, and returned to the flat ground on the hillside. The sky had brightened, the morning mist was dissipating, and the sounds of early risers in the Village could be heard in the distance.

Shuichi walked home along the Naka River. The burning sensation in his palm continued, like holding a fire that would never go out. Sakumo’s combat experience burned quietly in his spiritual energy barrier, waiting to be fused.

When Kakashi Hatake left home in the morning, his father was not in the courtyard, nor was he in the house.

He waited for an hour, but his father did not return. Kakashi walked to the door and looked at the empty courtyard. There was a thin layer of dew on the wooden chair, and the seat was cold. His father was not there.

Kakashi walked toward the Memorial Stone. He passed the Training Ground and walked up the stone steps on the hillside. The morning mist had not yet dispersed, and the dew on the stone steps soaked his sandals.

He walked up a flight of stone steps, turned a corner, and the Memorial Stone appeared in his view.

There was someone lying on the ground in front of the stone.

Silver-white hair was scattered on the ground, the lean body lay in front of the stone, the left hand hung by his side, and a short sword was stuck in his chest. The white light on the hilt flickered slightly in the morning sun.

Kakashi recognized that sword. It was his father’s sword, 'White Light.' He had seen that sword since he was a child ; his father would carry it every time he went out on a mission. There was a notch on the blade, which his father said was left a long time ago and had never been repaired.

Kakashi stood in place. His legs felt weak, but he did not fall. He walked to his father’s side and squatted down.

His father’s face was turned toward the stone, revealing his profile. His eyes were closed, and the expression on his face was very calm. No pain, no unwillingness. It was as if he had fallen asleep in front of the stone.

But the short sword in his chest and the blood on the ground were not sleeping.

Kakashi’s hand reached out and touched his father’s shoulder. The shoulder was cool. Not the coolness of the morning mist, but an icy cold.

Kakashi’s hand retracted. His lips trembled, and a very soft sound came from his throat, as if blocked by something. His eye sockets began to turn red, and tears welled up from the corners of his eyes, flowing down his cheeks and dripping onto his mask.

He just squatted there, looking at his father’s profile, tears falling drop by drop.

« Dad. »

The voice was very soft, barely audible. Kakashi’s throat trembled, his lips moved, but he couldn’t say more words. His hand clutched his father’s sleeve, his knuckles turning white, gripping it tightly as if to pull his father back.

But his father would not come back.

Kakashi squatted in front of the stone and cried for a long time. His tears ran dry, and the sound in his throat disappeared. He just squatted there, looking at his father’s profile, his fingers still clutching the sleeve.

Finally, he let go.

He stood up and looked at a name next to the position where his father was lying on the stone. Takuya Akiyama. He knew this name, but he knew his father came here every day just to look at this name.

Kakashi looked down at his father’s body. The short sword was stuck in his chest, and the white light on the hilt was still flickering.

He reached out and gently gathered his father’s silver-white hair scattered on the ground, so they wouldn’t be scattered on the muddy ground.

Then he picked up his father and turned to walk down the mountain.

When he reached home, there were no more tears on his face. His mask was wet, but most of it had been dried by the cold wind. His eyes were red, but his gaze was no longer empty. There was an extra layer of something in his gaze, heavy, as if a stone that would not move had been placed in his heart.

His father chose to save his comrade. The whole Village cursed his father as a disgrace. But was his father right or wrong ? Kakashi didn’t know. He only knew his father could no longer find the answer.

Kakashi wanted to find that answer for his father. Not for the Village, not for the villagers, but for his father.

Shuichi pushed open the courtyard gate and walked into the house. Lue flew up from his shoulder and landed on the plum branch. Minako was cooking in the kitchen, and smoke rose from the chimney.

« Back ? » Minako’s voice came from the kitchen, « You got up early today. »

« Yeah. » Shuichi walked into the kitchen, « I’m going to wash my hands. »

He walked to the water vat and scooped a ladle of water with his right hand. The burning sensation in his palm weakened slightly under the impact of the water. Shuichi looked at his right hand ; Sakumo’s combat experience was quietly waiting in the spiritual energy barrier.

He needed time to digest it. The amount of this stuff was too large ; fusion might take weeks or even months. But once the fusion was completed, Shuichi’s practical combat ability would undergo a qualitative change.

Shuichi finished washing his hands and walked into the kitchen. Minako was busy in front of the stove, something was boiling in the pot, and steam was coming from the edge of the lid.

« What do you want to eat today ? »

« Anything is fine. »

Minako smiled. Shuichi stood at the kitchen door, looking at her back.

A thought popped into his mind : Is there any need to stay in this Village ?

Shuichi asked himself in his heart. White Fang was forced to commit suicide by the Village today. A Kage-level Ninja, who gave up the mission to save his comrade, was cursed as a disgrace by the whole Village, and finally died in front of the Memorial Stone with a short sword pierced into his heart.

For the sake of so-called stability, for the sake of the so-called Village, constantly sacrificing others—this was not the Will of Fire that Shuichi had seen on TV before. They were more like a group of vampires, clinging to one strong person after another who didn’t know how to resist in the name of the Village, sucking blood. When their value was exhausted, or when they knew how to resist, they would swarm them.

Uchiha Xiu Yi took a deep breath to calm his mood.

There were people in the Uchiha clan he needed to protect. Minako was here. He didn’t have the ability to fully protect himself in the Ninja World yet.

He was only eight years old now, with Chunin-level strength and insufficient Chakra. Where could he go after leaving Konoha ? Other Ninja Villages would not accept a Uchiha child, and the risk of independent survival was too great.

So he still had to stay in the Village.

But his thinking had completely changed. Previously, Shuichi’s plan was to gain self-preservation ability before the Night of the Massacre and then deal with the crisis. Now, his plan was to leave the Village in advance. Before the Night of the Massacre, he would take Minako and those who were willing to go with him and leave Konoha.

The death of White Fang confirmed one thing to Shuichi : this Village was not worth trusting. Not because the villagers were vicious, but because the villagers were stupid and didn’t know it. They would forget everything Sakumo had done for the Village, just because he made a choice they didn’t approve of. They would forget everything the Uchiha had done for the Village, just because of a surname they didn’t trust.

Shuichi didn’t need to wait for the villagers to remember the good of the Uchiha. He only needed to take his people and leave before the Village made a move.

« Shuichi ? » Minako turned her head, « What are you dazed about ? »

« Nothing. » Shuichi walked into the kitchen, « Mom, let me help you serve the food. »

Minako looked at Shuichi who came over to help, smiled, and said : « Shuichi is more sensible today. »
