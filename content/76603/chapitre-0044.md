# Chapitre 44 — Black Market

The next day at noon, Shuichi walked out of the inn.

He walked east along Tanzaku Town, passed through several alleys, went around a few buildings, and arrived at the forest from his memory.

The forest was at the far east end of Tanzaku Town, in a remote location with no residents or shops nearby ; usually, no one came here.

Shuichi expanded his perception, confirmed there were no other heat sources around, and began looking for that stone slab.

Under the third pine tree, he found it.

The stone slab was half-buried in the soil, with a triangle carved on it, and a horizontal line drawn inside the triangle.

The marking was very shallow ; if one didn’t know the location, they wouldn’t notice it at all.

Beside the stone slab was an entrance partially hidden by dead leaves and weeds.

Shuichi brushed aside the dead leaves and walked in.

Below the entrance was a flight of stone steps leading underground.

The steps were not wide, moss grew on the stone walls on both sides, and the air was damp and cold.

Shuichi’s footsteps were very light, making almost no sound.

At the end of the stone steps was a corridor, about twenty meters long, with an iron door at the end.

There were no lights in the corridor, but Shuichi’s heat source perception told him there were seven or eight heat sources behind the iron door ; their Chakra activity varied, with the lowest being ordinary people and the highest being about Chunin level.

Shuichi walked to the iron door and knocked three times.

The small window on the iron door slid open, and a pair of eyes looked out from inside.

« New Moon. » The voice behind those eyes was low.

« Penumbra. » Shuichi said.

The small window closed, and the iron door opened.

Behind the door was a basement hall, about one hundred square meters, with a very low ceiling ; a few oil lamps hung on the walls, illuminating the hall dimly.

The air was filled with the smell of smoke and a certain indescribable fishy stench.

There were over a dozen people in the hall.

Some were standing by the wall whispering, some were sitting at a table in the corner reading scrolls, and some were talking to a middle-aged man in front of a counter.

In front of the middle-aged man lay a thick book, with a stack of scrolls next to it.

Shuichi walked into the hall and scanned the room.

No one looked at him.

People who came to the black market were accustomed to not paying attention to others ; one extra look could lead to trouble.

Shuichi walked directly to the counter.

The middle-aged man looked up and glanced at Shuichi.

His gaze paused for a moment on the short blade at his waist and the crow on his shoulder, then retracted.

« Taking a mission or submitting one ? » the middle-aged man asked.

« Taking a mission. » Shuichi said.

The middle-aged man pushed the thick book over.

« See for yourself. Bounties are arranged from low to high, D-rank to S-rank. Anyone can take them, just bring the proof to exchange for money. »

Shuichi opened the book.

The bounty missions in the book were arranged by rank, and each mission included the target’s name, estimated strength, last known location, and bounty amount.

The targets of D-rank missions were mostly ordinary wanted criminals, with bounties ranging from a few thousand to ten or twenty thousand.

The targets of C-rank missions were Genin-level rogue Ninja, with bounties of thirty thousand to one hundred thousand.

The targets of B-rank missions were Chunin-level, with bounties of one hundred thousand to five hundred thousand.

The targets of A-rank missions were Jonin-level, with bounties of five hundred thousand to two million.

The targets of S-rank missions were Kage-level, with bounties of over two million.

Shuichi chose a B-rank mission.

Target : Yejie. Iwagakure Rogue Ninja, Chunin level, skilled in Earth Release.

Last known location : Mountain road thirty miles south of Tanzaku Town.

Bounty : Three hundred and fifty thousand Ryo.

Shuichi closed the book and reported the mission number to the middle-aged man.

« B-027. » Shuichi said.

The middle-aged man glanced at the number, then looked at Shuichi again.

He took a scroll from beside the counter and handed it to Shuichi.

« The mission details are inside. After completing it, seal the target’s head into the scroll and bring it back ; I will pay the full amount after verifying it. »

Shuichi took the scroll and turned to leave the black market.

Two days later. Noon.

Shuichi walked into that remote forest again, brushed aside the dead leaves, and walked down the stone steps.

Shuichi walked into the hall.

This time there were more people in the hall than last time, about fifteen or sixteen.

A few men who looked like bounty hunters were drinking around a table in the corner, speaking loudly about their experiences hunting targets.

Two people were standing in front of the counter, negotiating with the middle-aged man.

Shuichi walked to the counter and waited.

The two people finished their business and left quickly.

The middle-aged man saw Shuichi and raised his eyebrows slightly ; this person had only come to take a B-rank mission two days ago ; he was back so soon ?

« Submitting a mission ? » the middle-aged man asked.

Shuichi placed the scroll on the counter.

No one in the hall paid attention to this side.

The bounty hunters in the corner were still drinking and bragging, and the people by the wall were still reading scrolls.

Shuichi reached out and undid the Sealing Technique formula on the scroll.

« Poof. »

After a burst of white smoke, a head appeared on the counter.

The expression on the head was very calm.

No fear, no pain, no signs of struggle.

The eyes were half-closed, and the corners of the mouth were even slightly drooping, as if it had been taken away in its sleep.

The cross-section of the neck was incredibly neat ; cut off with a single stroke, bone and flesh separated, the cut surface as smooth as a mirror.

The hall was silent for a moment.

The bounty hunters drinking in the corner stopped their glasses, their gazes all fixed on the counter.

The people reading scrolls by the wall looked up, staring at the head.

The air in the entire hall seemed to have been sucked away ; a dozen pairs of eyes fell on Shuichi at the same time.

A rogue Ninja with a crow, who had just come to take a B-rank mission two days ago, had returned with the target’s head two days later.

And the expression on that head was too calm.

The bounty hunters present had all seen dead people.

People who were killed, more or less, had fear or pain on their faces.

Those who were ambushed would be terrified, those fighting head-on would be gnashing their teeth, and those tortured would be twisted and distorted.

But this head had nothing ; the expression was as calm as if it were asleep.

What did this mean ? It meant the target didn’t even realize they were going to die before they died.

A single fatal blow, no resistance, no struggle, not even time to feel fear.

To kill an Iwagakure Rogue Ninja at the Chunin level without even giving the opponent time to feel fear.

Shuichi stood in front of the counter, his expression as calm as the one on the head.

He didn’t look at anyone ; his gaze fell only on the middle-aged man.

The middle-aged man was also shocked, but he quickly regained his composure.

He looked down at the head and opened the book to check against the information for the B-027 mission.

« Yejie, Iwagakure Rogue Ninja, Chunin level. » The middle-aged man muttered to himself, « Facial features match. »

The middle-aged man looked carefully at the cross-section of the head again, his gaze slightly fixed.

The cut was too clean, so clean that it didn’t look like it was cut off during a battle, but more like the target had stood still and been cut off with one strike.

He looked up at Shuichi, not saying anything.

« Three hundred and fifty thousand Ryo. »

The middle-aged man took a stack of banknotes from the safe under the counter, counted out three hundred and fifty thousand Ryo, and pushed it toward Shuichi.

Shuichi took the money and turned to leave.

The bounty hunters in the corner exchanged a few glances.

One tall, thin man held his glass, his gaze following Shuichi’s back the whole way.

Completed a B-rank mission in two days.

The head was cut off in one stroke, the target felt no fear before dying.

This rogue Ninja was definitely a Jonin, and an old-school Jonin at that.

Shuichi didn’t look back.

He walked to the iron door, pushed it open, and walked back to the surface along the corridor.

The iron door closed behind him, leaving only his own footsteps in the stone steps.

Shuichi walked out of the cave entrance and stood in the forest.

The afternoon sunlight spilled down through the gaps in the pine trees, shining on him.

Shuichi looked down at the money bag in his hand.

Three hundred and fifty thousand Ryo.

In the Ninja World, three hundred and fifty thousand Ryo was not a lot, but it wasn’t a small amount either.

More importantly, when he hunted Yejie two days ago, he had Plundered Chunin-level Chakra from him.

Because Chakra volume was his biggest weakness right now.

Shuichi stuffed the money bag into his chest and walked out of the forest.

Lue flew over from the edge of the forest and landed on his shoulder.

« Shuichi. » Lue said.

« Hmm ? »

« Next one ? »

Shuichi looked at Lue’s black round eyes, and the corners of his mouth moved slightly.

« No rush. Let’s go back and rest first. »

Lue nodded and retracted its neck.

Shuichi walked along the alley toward the inn.

The daytime in Tanzaku Town was not as lively as the night, but there were still many people on the street, and shops and restaurants were operating as usual.

Shuichi didn’t look back.

He had already gained a foothold in the black market.

Next, it was time to continuously take missions, hunt targets, and Plunder to become stronger.

He intended to keep this cycle going.
