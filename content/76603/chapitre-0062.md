# Chapitre 62 — Aburame Clan

Weekend.

Shuichi left with his backpack, telling Minako he was going to the library to read.

Minako was watering flowers in the courtyard, and Haku was lying at her feet, lazily basking in the sun.

« Come back early for dinner, » Minako said.

« Okay. » Shuichi nodded and turned to walk out of the courtyard gate.

Shuichi didn’t go to the library ; instead, he headed toward the Aburame Clan’s compound.

The Aburame Clan lived in the western part of Konoha Village, near the forest area.

Their compound was not large, but it was quite unique. The houses were surrounded by tall trees, and rows of wooden boxes hung from them—those were the nests for the parasitic insects.

Shuichi circled the perimeter of the compound and observed for a while.

The people of the Aburame Clan dressed very plainly, mostly wearing dark coats that covered their bodies completely.

Because the parasitic insects lived under their skin, exposing them would scare normal people.

Shuichi hid behind a tree and observed with his Sharingan for a while.

Then he activated the Transformation Technique.

Chakra circulated, and Shuichi’s body changed, transforming into the appearance of an ordinary Aburame Clan member : a male around thirty, black hair, an ordinary face, wearing a dark coat that covered his body completely.

At the same time, he utilized the ability he had previously Plundered to completely disguise his aura as that of the Aburame Clan.

Shuichi walked out from behind the tree and headed toward the entrance of the compound.

There were no guards at the entrance ; the Aburame Clan compound was not heavily guarded because the parasitic insects themselves were the best alarm system.

Shuichi walked into the compound and followed the path inside.

He didn’t know where the insect shed was, but that didn’t matter. He just needed to walk around the compound, observe the Aburame Clan’s insect-rearing methods, and see if there was anything worth learning from.

When passing by a few Aburame Clan members, they just glanced at Shuichi and didn’t pay any attention.

Shuichi quickly found the insect shed.

The insect shed was a two-story wooden building with a sign hanging at the entrance that read « Breeding Room ».

On both sides of the corridor were wooden shelves filled with glass jars. Each jar contained a different species of parasitic insect.

An Aburame Clan member was squatting in front of the shelves, explaining things to a few new disciples.

« The feeding method for parasitic insects is very simple, » the man said. « Use your own Chakra to catalyze the insect eggs, and the insects will develop directly from eggs into adults. The whole process only takes a few minutes. »

Shuichi stood to the side, listening quietly.

« There’s no larval stage ? » a new disciple asked.

« Because the development of parasitic insects depends entirely on Chakra, » the man explained. « Ordinary insects need to eat, molt, and grow slowly. But parasitic insects are different ; they feed on Chakra. If there is enough Chakra, their development speed will be dozens of times faster. »

The man pointed to his chest. « Parasitic insects live under the host’s skin and feed on the host’s Chakra. The host needs to feed them with Chakra regularly, or they will starve to death. »

The man paused and added, « The process of accepting parasitic insects involves making holes in your body and letting the insects parasitize inside. It will be very itchy at first, but you’ll get used to it later. »

Shuichi listened and silently noted it down.

After the explanation, the Aburame Clan member left. The few new disciples remained in the insect shed and began trying to catalyze the insect eggs with Chakra.

Shuichi didn’t participate ; instead, he walked to an empty corner of the insect shed and flipped through the insect-rearing manuscripts on the shelves.

The manuscripts were the accumulated experience of generations of the Aburame Clan. The content was very detailed, covering the classification of parasitic insects, feeding frequency, breeding methods, combat applications...

Shuichi flipped through the pages, his gaze landing on the chapter about breeding methods.

« There is no concept of a queen insect for parasitic insects, » the manuscript read. « Every parasitic insect is an independent individual. After mating and laying eggs, the host themselves acts as the mother—inputting their own Chakra to catalyze the eggs. The newly hatched insects continue to parasitize the host. The host is the mother ; the insects cannot reproduce if they leave the host. »

Shuichi frowned.

No queen insect ?

He continued reading.

« The autonomy of parasitic insects is extremely poor. They cannot survive without the supply of the host’s Chakra and require the host to actively transport Chakra to them. The Aburame Clan can use them this way because they spend their whole lives feeding these insects. »

Shuichi closed the manuscript and leaned back in the chair.

He understood.

The Aburame Clan’s insect swarm essentially turned the host into a « Chakra supply station. » The host constantly consumed Chakra to feed the insects, and in return, the insects provided reconnaissance and combat capabilities.

This system was fine for the Aburame Clan.

They started raising insects from a young age, and their bodies had adapted to this mode.

But Shuichi didn’t want to do that.

First, he didn’t want to make holes in his body and let insects parasitize under his skin. He couldn’t accept the idea of raising insects by cutting holes in himself.

Second, he didn’t want to divide his Chakra among thousands of insects. His Chakra should be used for fighting, for protecting his mother, not for feeding insects.

More importantly, although the parasitic insects had hiding abilities and their stealth was indeed good, that was only for ordinary Ninja.

The targets Shuichi needed to deal with were Kage-level powerhouses. Their reconnaissance abilities were extremely strong, and Chakra fluctuations were hard to hide. If the insect swarm relied on Chakra to function, this intelligence network would be exposed at a critical moment.

He thought for a long time.

What he wanted was an insect swarm that could operate independently. One that didn’t need Chakra support, didn’t need a host as a mother, and could reproduce, forage, and act on its own.

Were there such insect swarms in the natural world ?

Shuichi thought of ants, bees, and wasps.

They communicated via pheromones and didn’t need Chakra. Worker bees were naturally obedient to the queen bee ; the whole system ran on pure biological mechanisms.

The queen bee laid eggs, worker bees took care of the larvae, and the larvae grew into new worker bees. When the queen bee grew old, a new one took over. The insect swarm operated on its own without outside intervention.

Shuichi stood up and put the manuscript back on the shelf.

He walked out of the insect shed and headed toward the entrance of the compound.

After walking out of the compound, Shuichi dispelled the Transformation Technique and returned to his original appearance.

Shuichi looked at the forest in the distance, thinking about his next plan.

The Aburame Clan’s insect swarm didn’t suit him, so he would create his own.

He recalled some games he had played in his past life, which had settings like Zerg or Gu insects.

The Zerg had a queen, the queen commanded all the insects, and the insects communicated through a psychic link. Gu insects involved raising various poisonous insects together, letting them devour each other, and the last one to survive became the Gu King.

These settings gave Shuichi some inspiration.

He could create his own queen insect and give it the ability to command other insects. But unlike the settings in the games, he didn’t need to make the insects devour each other, nor did he need a psychic link.

He could just use the insects' own pheromones.

Because he didn’t need the insects to fight ; he only needed to gather information.

Moreover, the queen insect controlled the entire swarm through pheromones, and the swarm fed back information through pheromones. The entire system would be a pure biological mechanism, requiring no Chakra, and would not be discovered by sensory-type Ninja.

Through his Plunder ability, he wouldn’t be limited to a single species.

Ants, bees, wasps, spiders, moths... all kinds of insects would work. Different insects had different abilities, suitable for different tasks.

The queen would command all insects, with each type performing its own duties.

This would make collecting information more efficient.

When he returned home, Minako was cooking in the kitchen.

« You’re back ? » Minako poked her head out of the kitchen. « How was the reading ? »

« It was okay. » Shuichi walked into the house and put down his backpack.

Haku was lying in the corner of the living room. Seeing Shuichi come in, its ears twitched, but it didn’t stand up—it was still very quiet.

Shuichi walked over to Haku, squatted down, and patted its head.

Haku gently rubbed against his fingers.

« It went to play in the courtyard this afternoon, » Minako said, walking out of the kitchen. « It really likes chasing butterflies. »

Shuichi smiled. « Butterflies ? »

« It chased several, but didn’t catch any, » Minako said with a smile. « It runs quite fast, though. »

Shuichi looked at Haku, thinking about his cultivation plan.

Evening. Shuichi sat at his desk, flipping through a reference book about insects.

This book was borrowed from the school library ; it recorded the common insect species around Konoha.

Ants, bees, wasps, hornets, spiders, moths... many could be used.

Shuichi closed the book and leaned back in the chair.

Tomorrow, he would go to the forest to wander around and Plunder some useful abilities.

Then, he would find a suitable insect near his home to act as the queen and start cultivating it.

This was the first step for his intelligence network.
