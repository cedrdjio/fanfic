# Chapitre 77 — Wood Release

Moonlight spilled over the mountain path ; Shuichi’s footsteps were steady and quiet.

He crossed the mountain road, passed through the woods, and traversed the border of Konoha.

The guards at the gate yawned, completely failing to notice the two figures slipping past them.

The lights of the Uchiha Clan Compound had already been extinguished, with only the streetlights remaining lit.

Shuichi returned to his front door, pushed it open, and walked into the courtyard.

White Zetsu emerged from behind Shuichi, standing in the courtyard with its hollow face turned toward him, waiting.

Shuichi turned around, his gaze landing on it.

This was the test subject that Shuichi had specifically asked Uchiha Madara for to study.

He brought White Zetsu into his room.

His perception swept over White Zetsu’s body. There were indeed traces of Wood Release within it, but it didn’t feel quite right to Shuichi ; this power did not seem entirely the same as Senju Hashirama’s Wood Release.

Shuichi calmed his mind and perceived it carefully.

Senju Hashirama’s Wood Release was a metabolic byproduct of his own Yang-style Chakra, a life energy spontaneously generated by his meridians, like a complete circulatory system.

However, the Wood Release within White Zetsu was different ; it was more like a branch cut from a larger plant, lacking the ability to self-circulate and relying on absorbing external Chakra to maintain its vitality.

Essentially, its body originated from the Gedo Statue, which was the husk of the Ten-Tails, the Divine Tree itself.

Its underlying structure was that of the Divine Tree system, which was fundamentally different from the human meridian system.

Although Uchiha Madara had activated it using Senju Hashirama’s cells, its essence still leaned toward the Divine Tree.

However, there was an advantage.

White Zetsu’s Wood Release foundation was clean and its structure open ; it was more like a seed that could be re-cultivated, easily spurred to grow by external forces.

For Shuichi, who possessed the ability of Plunder, this non-native concept was better suited for being plundered, digested, and then re-processed.

Shuichi raised his right hand, his palm aiming at the top of White Zetsu’s head.

« Plunder. »

A suction force emanated from his right palm, and White Zetsu’s body convulsed violently.

The concept of Wood Release was extracted from its body, winding out like a green thread and coiling around Shuichi’s palm.

The logic of plant Growth, the woody veins of Yang-style Chakra, the flow of life energy at the cellular level.

All of it poured into Shuichi’s body.

White Zetsu’s body began to disintegrate. Its outline blurred, and black liquid seeped from its body, dripping onto the ground. A few seconds later, it completely transformed into a pool of black liquid and sank into the ground.

It was dead.

Shuichi withdrew his right hand, sensing the changes within his body.

The concept of Wood Release had taken root in his meridians, like a seed planted in soil. He could feel the flow of his Yang-style Chakra beginning to change, with a portion of the Chakra being guided into the woody fiber pathways, forming a new cycle.

But it was too weak.

This amount of Wood Release couldn’t even sprout a single twig.

However, it wasn’t a major problem ; as long as there was a starting point, the rest would be easy to handle.

Shuichi turned his gaze outside the window.

If the concept from one White Zetsu was weak, he could just get a few more and use the quantity to perfect the concept.

However, thinking of how disgusting White Zetsu’s Mayfly technique was, Shuichi dismissed that idea.

He then turned his thoughts to his previous experience of accelerating the maturity of his Sharingan : the concept of Growth.

The essence of Wood Release was Yang-style Chakra, which naturally aligned with the concept of Growth. If he could find a sufficiently strong concept of Growth to catalyze the Wood Release already within him, perhaps he could boost its vitality.

Early the next morning, Shuichi left Konoha.

He walked along the mountain path, passed through the woods, and arrived at a Mixed Forest.

This was a place he often visited when he had first awakened his abilities. At that time, he was only five years old, having just awakened his memories of a past life and the Plunder ability in his right hand, feeling curious and confused about everything.

He remembered there was a pond in this Mixed Forest.

There were frogs by the pond.

The Growth pattern of a frog changing from a tadpole into an adult was extremely concentrated ; the strength of its concept of Growth far exceeded that of other small animals.

It was here that he had first perceived the concept of Growth all those years ago.

Shuichi stood by the pond, his gaze sweeping across the surface of the water.

Lotus leaves floated on the water, their green blades swaying slightly in the morning light. The water rippled, and occasionally a fish would leap out of the water before sinking back to the bottom.

His perception extended outward, sweeping across the entire pond. A few seconds later, he locked onto his target.

A plump adult frog was squatting on a lotus leaf, its green skin shimmering in the sunlight. Its eyes bulged, staring at a flying insect above the water, its tongue ready to shoot out at any moment.

Shuichi walked over with light footsteps.

The frog did not notice him.

He raised his right hand, his palm aiming at the frog’s back.

« Plunder. »

The concept of Growth was extracted from the frog, looking like a pale green stream of air coiling around Shuichi’s palm.

Complete, without any bias.

Shuichi withdrew his right hand, his breathing steady.

The concept of Growth, which had once nearly drained his mental energy in a backlash, was now as easy to Plunder as picking up a leaf.

His mental energy was no longer what it used to be.

His perception swept across the pond again, and he soon found another frog.

Shuichi walked over and extended his right hand once more.

« Plunder. »

The second concept of Growth was obtained.

The two homologous concepts of frog Growth overlapped in his palm, like two streams of water merging together to form a complete version far stronger than the one from years ago.

Shuichi felt the power in his palm and nodded with satisfaction.

Returning to his room, Shuichi sat on the bed and closed his eyes.

He guided the concept of Growth into his body, allowing it to merge with the existing Wood Release pathways in his meridians.

The concept of Growth began to take effect.

It did not directly strengthen the Wood Release, but rather accelerated the Growth of the Wood Release itself.

It was like fertilizing a newly sprouted sapling.

The flow of Yang-style Chakra began to accelerate, and the woody fiber pathways slowly extended within his meridians. Shuichi could feel the Wood Release within his body growing stronger bit by bit—a deepening in quality rather than an explosive increase in quantity.

It was like a seed driving its roots deeper into the soil.

Shuichi opened his eyes, sensing the changes within his body.

It would take about another two months for the basic Wood Release ability to grow to a usable level.

During these two months, he could do other things.

The contents of the scroll of sealing appeared in his mind.

Flying Thunder God.

A Space-Time Ninjutsu created by the Second Hokage, Senju Tobirama, and brought to prominence by the Fourth Hokage, Minato Namikaze.

Marking, sensing, and teleportation.

The three core elements, none of which could be dispensed with.

Shuichi stood up from the bed, his gaze calm.

Two months was enough time to practice the Flying Thunder God to a level suitable for actual combat.

He walked to the window, pushed it open, and looked at the sky outside.

Sunlight spilled into the courtyard, illuminating that pool of black liquid—the remains left behind by White Zetsu.

Shuichi’s gaze was calm.

The five-year agreement was just the beginning.

He needed to become stronger, needed to accumulate power, and needed to possess enough influence before that plan was completed.

Wood Release, Flying Thunder God, Mangekyo Sharingan.

There were still more things waiting for him to acquire.

Shuichi turned around and began to prepare for his Flying Thunder God practice.
