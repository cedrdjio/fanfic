# Chapitre 69 — scroll of sealing

Shuichi moved in the shadows outside the Hokage Building, his form intangible.

His left Mangekyo rotated, and the power of Magatsuhi transformed his existence from reality into falsehood.

His body became something that did not exist. Footsteps vanished, breathing vanished, Chakra fluctuations vanished. Not even the air stirred as he moved. He was like a shadow that did not belong to this world, passing through the night, silent and traceless.

The Anbu codenamed « Crow » walked in the corridor ahead, wearing a cat mask, his steps nearly silent. His gait was steady, his rhythm even ; he was an experienced Anbu.

Shuichi followed behind him, maintaining a distance of ten meters.

In his intangible state, his perception remained normal. He could see « Crow’s » back, hear the other’s slight footsteps, and sense the steady flow of Chakra within the other’s body. But he himself did not exist at all.

The corridors of the Hokage Building were deep, with stone walls on both sides and dim lights every few meters.

The Anbu « Crow » passed through the corridor and stopped in front of a sealing door.

Complex seal patterns were carved on the door, with Chakra flowing slowly through them. This was the first sealing door, which only specific people could pass.

« Crow » reached out and pressed his hand onto the seal on the door, injecting Chakra. The seal flickered once, and the sealing door slowly opened.

He walked inside, and the door closed behind him.

Shuichi walked up to the sealing door, did not stop, and passed directly through it.

His intangible body passed through the sealing door, and the seal on the door did not react at all.

The sealing door detected the entrant’s Chakra and physical entity. Chakra fluctuations would trigger an alarm, and a physical entry would be recorded. But Shuichi currently had neither Chakra fluctuations nor a physical form.

He was something that did not exist.

The sealing door could not detect something that did not exist.

Shuichi passed through the door and continued to follow « Crow. »

They went down two flights of stairs ; the stairs were narrow and spiraled downward. The further down they went, the dimmer the light became.

The first basement level was the archive storage area, with dense filing cabinets on both sides, containing Konoha’s mission records and intelligence files from over the years.

The second basement level was the sealing material storage area, housing various materials and tools needed for Sealing Techniques.

« Crow » did not stop at these two levels and continued downward.

A third sealing door appeared before them.

This door was larger than the previous two, and the seal on it was more complex. The patterns were dense, like a precise web covering the entire door surface.

« Crow » injected Chakra again, and the door slowly opened.

He walked inside, and the door closed behind him.

Shuichi passed through intangibly, without triggering any alarms.

The third basement level was where the scroll of sealing was kept.

— —

« Crow » walked into the archive room at the deepest part of the basement.

Shuichi stopped outside the door, looking in through the intangible wall.

The archive room was not large, about twenty square meters, surrounded by thick stone walls, with no windows and only a faint light. In the center of the room was a stone pedestal, and on the pedestal sat a huge scroll.

The unfolded part of the scroll showed dense seal patterns, and the cover was written with four large characters : scroll of sealing.

Shuichi’s gaze fell on the area surrounding the stone pedestal.

Three layers of Sealing Techniques covered the stone pedestal.

The first layer was a sensory seal ; any Chakra fluctuation would be detected.

The second layer was a trigger seal ; anyone touching the area outside the stone pedestal would trigger an alarm.

The third layer was a recording seal, which would record the entrant’s Chakra characteristics and duration of stay.

The three layers of Sealing Techniques were superimposed to form a precise protective net. Once someone intruded, the Hokage Office would receive an alarm immediately, and the intruder’s information would be recorded.

« Crow » walked to the stone pedestal and checked the status of the seals according to the procedure.

He took a logbook from his waist, turned to today’s date, signed it, and recorded the inspection time and seal status.

Then he turned and left, his footsteps gradually disappearing at the end of the corridor.

Shuichi waited for a while, confirming that « Crow » had gone far away, before stepping out of the intangible state.

His form returned to solid, standing at the entrance of the archive room.

Shuichi pushed the door open and walked into the archive room.

The scroll of sealing on the stone pedestal was right in front of him.

He walked to the stone pedestal and looked at the three layers of Sealing Techniques before him.

If he were to force a break, he would need to dismantle all three techniques simultaneously without triggering any alarms. The difficulty was high, and the risk was great.

But Shuichi did not need to break them.

He activated his left Mangekyo.

Magatsuhi rotated, and his gaze fell on the three layers of Sealing Techniques.

Transform truth into falsehood.

The techniques began to change.

The Sealing Techniques that were originally real were transformed into falsehood on a conceptual level. They were still there, still visible, the patterns still flowing, the light still flickering. But they no longer existed.

They were false.

False techniques would not trigger alarms, would not record information, and would not sense Chakra fluctuations.

Shuichi reached out and touched the stone pedestal.

No alarm.

He walked to the side of the stone pedestal and opened the scroll of sealing.

The scroll unfolded, and dense seal patterns appeared before his eyes.

Shuichi’s Sharingan rotated, the Three Tomoe Sharingan spinning, engraving the content of every page into his memory.

The first page, Multiple Shadow Clone Jutsu.

This was a B-rank Ninjutsu but was listed as a forbidden technique. The reason was that the clones it created were physical entities, possessing the original’s Chakra and combat ability. The more clones created, the more Chakra was consumed. Ordinary people would die from Chakra exhaustion if they used it.

Shuichi scanned the patterns and hand seals of the technique, memorizing them.

This technique was useful to him. His Chakra reserves were large enough that creating dozens of physical clones was no problem. He could use clones to accelerate proficiency during training and use clones to confuse enemies during battle.

The second page, Flying Thunder God Technique.

A space-time Ninjutsu, a technique developed by the Second Hokage, Senju Tobirama. It involved leaving a mark on a target and then instantly teleporting to the marked location. There was no distance limit ; as long as there was a mark, he could reach it.

Shuichi looked at the structure of the technique, his brows slightly furrowed.

The hand seals for this technique were very complex, and the Chakra circulation route was also very precise. It required a lot of practice to master. But once mastered, his mobility would be elevated to another level.

The third page, Reaper Death Seal.

At the cost of the caster’s soul, it summoned the shadow of the Shinigami to seal the target’s soul. The sealed soul would be trapped in the Shinigami’s stomach forever, unable to escape.

Shuichi looked at this technique and remained silent for a while.

The cost of this technique was too high. Paying with one’s soul meant the caster would certainly die. Unless it was a last resort, he would not use it.

The fourth page, Edo Tensei.

Using a living person as a sacrifice, it summoned the soul of the deceased and bound it to the sacrifice’s body. The sacrifice’s body would be modified by the deceased’s Chakra, gaining the capabilities and memories of the deceased from when they were alive. The summoned person could not defy the caster’s orders.

Shuichi carefully observed every detail of this technique.

Hand seals, sealing methods, Chakra circulation routes, sacrifice requirements, and summoning conditions.

The core of the technique lay in two points : one was the sacrifice, requiring a living person as a vessel ; the other was the soul of the deceased, requiring an item or a part of the remains from when the deceased was alive as a medium.

The problem with this technique was the sacrifice.

He would not use innocent people as sacrifices. But people who deserved to die, such as enemies, criminals, or those hunting him, could be used as sacrifices.

Shuichi continued to flip forward.

There were several other forbidden techniques behind that he had not seen in his past life. Some were developed by the successive Hokage of Konoha, and some were seized from other Villages. Every technique was complex, with precise hand seals and clear Chakra circulation routes.

Shuichi memorized them all.

Regardless of whether he could use them now, he would memorize them first. There would always be an opportunity to use them later.

He finished flipping through to the end and closed the scroll.

The entire content of the scroll of sealing was now completely preserved in his memory.

Shuichi rolled up the scroll of sealing again and placed it back on the stone pedestal, with the position and angle exactly the same as before.

He deactivated the ability of his left Mangekyo.

The light of Magatsuhi vanished.

The ability to transform truth into falsehood was lifted.

The three layers of Sealing Techniques returned to reality, exactly the same as before.

No one would know that the scroll of sealing had been opened tonight.

Shuichi turned and left the archive room, returning the way he came.

He passed through the three sealing doors, went up two flights of stairs, and returned to the ground floor.

In the shadows outside the Hokage Building, he deactivated his intangibility, and his form returned to solid.

The entire process took less than ten minutes.

« Crow » was still walking on his patrol route, unaware that someone had just followed behind him through three sealing doors and opened the scroll of sealing.

Shuichi walked in the direction of his home, his figure disappearing into the night.

— —

Back home.

Shuichi climbed back into his room through the window and closed it.

The spider was still in the corner of the windowsill, with two worker bees flying around it, their antennae twitching to transmit information. It glanced at Shuichi, said nothing, and continued organizing the information brought back by the worker bees.

The predator was squatting on the plum tree in the courtyard, opened its eyes to look at Shuichi, and then closed them again.

Shuichi walked to the desk and sat down.

The seal patterns in his mind emerged bit by bit.

He first organized Edo Tensei.

Hand seals, sealing methods, Chakra circulation routes, and casting conditions.

Sacrifice requirements : A living person, preferably a Ninja with a large amount of Chakra.

Summoning medium : An item or a part of the remains from when the deceased was alive.

Control method : Implanting a curse seal into the sacrifice’s body allowed for forced control of the summoned person’s actions.

Shuichi carefully analyzed every step in his mind.

The sacrifice was a problem.

He would not use innocent people as sacrifices. But that group of people in the Land of Grass who deserved to die were already dead, leaving no usable sacrifices.

He had to think of an alternative plan.

Perhaps he could go to the Forest of Death to find a target ? Or wait for the next time someone came to hunt him down ?

Shuichi continued to organize other content in his mind.

The marking method of the Flying Thunder God Technique, the Chakra distribution technique of the Multiple Shadow Clone Jutsu, and the summoning conditions of the Reaper Death Seal.

He went through them one by one in his mind to deepen his memory.

These techniques all required a lot of practice to master. But at least the items were in his hands.

Shuichi finished organizing all the techniques and recalled them once more to make the memory more secure.

He leaned back in his chair and looked out the window.

The night was deep, and Konoha Village was quiet.

From the Mangekyo’s toll, to Wood Release, to Edo Tensei, to the scroll of sealing — this line had finally reached the practical stage.

Next, he needed to continue to grow stronger while digesting these newly acquired abilities.

Shuichi stood up and walked to the window.

In the distance, the lights of the Hokage Building were still on.

He looked in that direction, his gaze calm.

The scroll of sealing was in hand, the Byakugan had been fused, and the intelligence network had been laid out.

Everything was proceeding according to plan.
