# Chapitre 26 — Field Practice

On Sunday, Shuichi went to the Forest of Death.

He hunted a mountain antelope ; it was a speed-type, harder to chase than a wild boar, but its Vitality was absorbed faster.

During the hunt, he realized the weakness in his speed, but in the end, he managed to intercept it by relying on the prediction of his Sharingan.

By Monday, the mountain antelope’s Vitality had been fully absorbed, and his physique had taken a small step forward.

On Monday, on his way to school, Shuichi reported the information about the bear paw print to Teacher Mitomon, using the excuse of « hearing a patrol report from the Military Police Force while handling clan affairs. »

Teacher Mitomon reported it to the school that same day, and the safety perimeter for the practical class was pulled back one mile to the south.

The next few days passed quickly.

From Tuesday to Thursday, Shuichi repeated his daily routine between school and home : attending classes and watching Obito and Tetsuya spar after school.

Obito’s footwork transitions could already be done for ten cycles without stuttering, and Tetsuya finally achieved a smooth transition between stepping forward and stepping backward on Thursday, getting so excited that he did it three times in the courtyard.

Minako watched from the corridor for a while and remarked, « Tetsuya has made great progress. »

On Thursday night, Shuichi checked his ninja tools : Kunai, shuriken, bandages, and military ration pills.

Teacher Mitomon had said the school would provide uniform gear packs, but he prepared an extra set himself anyway.

Then he called Lue down and instructed it to stay on standby at high altitude during the practical class—no low flying, no chirping, only reconnaissance from high above.

Lue tilted its head to look at him, then flew into the night.

Friday, cloudy. The outer edge of the Training Ground upstream of the Naka River.

The twenty-three students of Class 1-A lined up in four rows on the river beach, their feet treading on the gravel and sand mixed riverbank.

The sound of the Naka River rushed in the morning breeze ; the pine forest on the opposite bank was shrouded in mist, and the air smelled of damp pine resin and river mud.

Teacher Mitomon stood in front of the formation, carrying a large gear pack on his back.

Beside him were a stack of maps and six canvas bags, which were the equipment for each group.

« The field practice class consists of three tasks, » Teacher Mitomon said, holding up three fingers.

« First, orientation. Each group will receive a map and a compass ; find the three designated checkpoints according to the map. »

« Second, wilderness survival. Each group must build a temporary shelter ; the standard is to be able to stay inside for half an hour without getting rained on. »

« Third, teamwork. The above two tasks must be completed together by all four members of the group ; no one is to be left alone. »

He unfolded a large map and drew a thick line with a charcoal pencil.

« South to the southern end of the river beach, north to one mile south of the river bend, and east and west not beyond the edge of the pine forest. »

« Anyone who crosses this line will have their score reset to zero and will be assigned to clean the dojo for a week upon returning to the village. »

Shuichi glanced at the map.

The safety line Teacher Mitomon drew was more conservative than he had expected ; there was nearly three miles between the north side and the bear paw print.

The caution of a Chunin in teaching was sometimes even stricter than a Jonin.

« Group leaders, come and collect the maps and gear packs. »

Shuichi stepped forward and took the map and canvas bag for the third group.

The map was a hand-drawn sketch, marking the locations of the river beach, the pine forest, the dry tributary, and the three checkpoints.

The three checkpoints were distributed in a triangle along the outer edge of the Training Ground.

The first was near a fallen tree at the southern end of the river beach, the second was under a rock wall at the edge of the pine forest, and the third was at the confluence of the dry tributary and the main river channel.

The route design was very basic ; no climbing, no traversing dense forests, the entire route was on open terrain.

Teacher Mitomon had a very accurate grasp of the stamina and attention span of six-year-old children.

The canvas bag contained four short wooden stakes, a roll of rope, a waterproof tarp, four military ration pills, and a basic first aid kit.

« The first checkpoint is at the southern end of the river beach, » Shuichi said, laying the map on a rock.

Koharu, Kenta, and Tetsuya gathered around.

« Follow the riverbank ; there’s no need to enter the forest. »

« Tetsuya, you go in front—watch your footing ; the stones on the river beach are slippery. »

« Koharu, you walk in the middle and hold the map ; Kenta and I will bring up the rear. »

He did not ask for their opinions.

There was no need to consult on the division of labor ; Tetsuya had the strength and was suitable for clearing the path, Koharu was careful and suitable for keeping the map, Kenta was tall and thin with long legs, suitable for bringing up the rear, and Shuichi walked at the very back to react immediately if anyone slipped.

The team walked south along the river beach.

The Naka River shimmered with a grayish-blue light in the morning sun, and the gravel on the riverbank had been washed round and smooth by the stream.

Tetsuya walked very fast at the front ; his footwork was much steadier than last week.

Although his center of gravity was still tilted back, he could at least adjust himself when stepping on stones.

Koharu started panting after less than a quarter of an hour.

She had the poorest stamina in the group ; Shuichi noted that her stance in school taijutsu class could only last five minutes.

Kenta walked slowly but steadily ; he had long legs and a large stride, and he made sure each step was firm before taking the next.

« Stop for a moment, » Shuichi said, noticing that Koharu’s breathing rhythm had started to become erratic.

« Drink some water. Don’t wait until you’re thirsty to drink. »

Koharu took her canteen out of the bag and took two sips.

Tetsuya stood in front waiting, having already drawn his Kunai—not because there was danger, but because holding it made him look the part.

Shuichi did not stop him.

Having a weapon in hand made a person calmer, even if there was nothing around.

Continuing south for about half an hour, they reached the fallen tree, an old oak tree struck by lightning that lay across the river beach ; the trunk was so thick that even on tiptoes, Tetsuya couldn’t reach the top.

The checkpoint marker was a red cloth strip tied to the fallen tree.

« First checkpoint. Tetsuya, carve a mark on the trunk next to the red cloth strip. »

Tetsuya used his Kunai to carve a crooked « Three » on the oak bark.

Shuichi glanced at it and didn’t correct him—it was ugly, but as long as it was recognizable, that was fine.

Setting off from the fallen tree toward the northwest, they passed through a small pine forest.

The ground of the pine forest was covered with a thick layer of pine needles ; it felt soft to walk on, more comfortable than the river beach, but one couldn’t walk too fast.

There were hidden pits under the pine needles, and stepping into one could sprain an ankle.

Shuichi had Kenta walk second and Tetsuya walk last ; the person clearing the path in the pine forest didn’t need strength, but observation skills.

Kenta’s high perspective could spot tree root tripwires and hidden pits covered by pine needles in advance.

Kenta discovered a set of footprints in the pine forest.

Flat-soled ninja shoe prints, about five or six people’s tracks, barely sinking into the pine needles.

Shuichi took a look and judged that they were left by Teacher Mitomon during his pre-scouting.

The Chunin had walked the route himself before the practical class—this detail raised his assessment of Teacher Mitomon by a notch.

After passing through the pine forest, the second checkpoint appeared under a low rock wall.

The rock wall was only two people high, its surface weathered and pitted ; the red cloth strip was tied to a piton in the middle of the rock wall.

Tetsuya carved another « Three »—this time it was even more crooked than the first, because the rock wall was harder than the bark, and his Kunai slipped on the stone.

Shuichi looked up at the sky.

The clouds were thicker than in the morning.

If it rained in the afternoon, the third checkpoint was at the confluence of the dry tributary and the main river channel ; the terrain there was low, and the riverbed might flood after the rain.

He had to make the third group speed up.

« The third checkpoint is to the east ; follow the edge of the pine forest. Tetsuya, continue clearing the path. Try to arrive before lunch. »

The third checkpoint was at the mouth of the dry tributary, a creek bed that only had water in the rainy season, now left with only gravel and dry mudflats.

The red cloth strip was tied to a willow tree at the confluence of the tributary and the Naka River.

When Tetsuya finished carving the last mark, Shuichi looked up at the sky.

The clouds had pressed down to the height of the pine forest treetops, and the humidity in the air had risen significantly.

Rain was coming soon.

« Build the shelter. Up above the tributary riverbed, not inside the riverbed. »

« Tetsuya, go drive the wooden stakes ; Kenta, pull the ropes ; Koharu, spread the waterproof tarp. »

The location he chose for the setup was on the bank of the tributary riverbed, between an old willow tree and a large rock, which naturally provided protection from the wind on two sides.

Tetsuya’s wooden stakes were driven in better than expected because he had been swinging a hammer at the blacksmith shop for four years ; although his technique for driving stakes was wrong, the force was sufficient, and the four stakes were driven firmly into the ground.

Kenta tied the ropes between the willow tree and the stakes ; the knots weren’t standard, but they were tight.

Koharu spread a layer of dry pine needles on the waterproof tarp ; this wasn’t something Shuichi had ordered, but something she had thought of herself.

With this extra layer of pine needles, the floor of the shelter was no longer wet mud, but a dry buffer layer.

« Done. »

Shuichi crawled in to test it ; it was just enough for four people, the wind protection was good, and the pine needle layer separated them from the ground’s dampness.

Koharu sat on the pine needles and let out a long breath.

Kenta leaned against his backpack, showing a relaxed expression for the first time.

Tetsuya stood outside the shelter, hands on his hips, looking at his wooden stakes with an expression on his face as if he were looking at a freshly quenched blade in the blacksmith shop.

The rain started to fall.

It was the kind of heavy rain that suddenly poured down from the clouds.

The raindrops made a dense, muffled sound as they hit the waterproof tarp, and the sound of the Naka River in the rain screen sounded several times louder than in the morning.

Shuichi sat at the entrance of the shelter and glanced south ; water was accumulating in the dry tributary riverbed, and the gravel beach was half submerged by the rain screen within minutes.

« Good thing we didn’t build it in the riverbed, » Kenta said, watching the stream that had started to flow outside, his voice suppressed under the sound of the rain.

Shuichi did not answer.

He was thinking about the other groups.

The fifth group, Hyuga Shimizu’s group, were all at the bottom in taijutsu, but Hyuga Shimizu himself was someone hiding his true strength.

If he was forced to reveal his true level during the practical class to help his teammates, then his cover would develop a crack.

For someone from the Hyuga Branch House, being noticed by the school for having « far exceeding expectations in taijutsu ability » was not better than being noticed by the Main House.

But this was not something Shuichi needed to worry about.

Hyuga Shimizu would weigh it himself.

Another thing : Lue.

With such heavy rain, Lue’s visibility at high altitude would be compressed by the rain, and the reconnaissance effect would be discounted.

But he had told Lue in advance to only stay on standby at high altitude today, not to fly low or chirp.

Lue could understand this instruction.

The rain stopped after about an hour.

The clouds split open, and sunlight poured down through the gap.

The gravel on the river beach was washed bright by the rain, and the scent of pine needles in the pine forest was several times stronger than on a sunny day.

Teacher Mitomon blew the assembly whistle.

The six groups returned to the river beach gathering point one after another.

Shuichi scanned around ; most people were soaked, a few physically weak girls were shivering, and Genji’s face was covered in a layer of mud, probably from a fall.

But no one was injured.

Hyuga Shimizu’s fifth group was the last to return.

Shuichi glanced at their equipment ; the waterproof tarp was set up very neatly, and the way the ropes were tied wasn’t the cross knot taught in school, but a more complex diamond cross.

Hyuga Shimizu’s teammates were basically dry.

A group of « all weaklings » staying completely dry in a heavy rainstorm—this alone was enough to make anyone who noticed it suspicious.

But Teacher Mitomon was busy collecting gear packs and taking roll, so he didn’t look closely.

Shuichi withdrew his gaze and helped Tetsuya roll up the third group’s waterproof tarp.

« The first place in the field practice class is, » Teacher Mitomon announced after counting all the checkpoint markers, « the third group. »

Tetsuya let out a shout and slammed his fist into his palm.

Koharu smiled—it was the first time Shuichi had seen her smile.

Kenta leaned against a rock, the corners of his mouth curling up.

Shuichi took the score sheet handed over by Teacher Mitomon and scanned it.

The third group had reached all three checkpoints within the specified time, and the shelter was structurally sound with a dry buffer layer on the ground—this bonus point was contributed by Koharu’s pine needle layer.

Hyuga Shimizu’s fifth group ranked fourth.

Not last, because Hyuga Shimizu’s personal performance pulled the whole group up by two spots.

But not in the top three either.

Shuichi glanced at Hyuga Shimizu ; he was standing behind his teammates, the cloth headband on his forehead soaked through by the rain, his expression as calm as usual.

It was already dark when they returned to the Uchiha Clan Grounds.

Shuichi pushed open the courtyard gate, and Minako was standing on the corridor ; her shoulders relaxed unconsciously when she saw him enter.

« Are you hurt ? »

« No. »

Shuichi walked to the corridor and sat down.

He was indeed not injured ; the field practice class was like a walk in the park for him.

But having been rained on all afternoon, maintaining his body temperature had consumed quite a bit of energy.

The mountain antelope’s Vitality was quietly permeating his body, repairing these minor expenditures.

Lue flew down from the plum branch and landed on his shoulder.

It had done a lap of low-altitude reconnaissance after the rain stopped.

The information Lue brought back : the bear paw print upstream of the Naka River was still there, but the position had moved about half a mile to the north.

Perhaps the mother bear had been startled by the sounds of various groups active in the south this morning and had retreated deeper with her cubs.

A move of half a mile meant the three-mile buffer zone had become three and a half miles.

Teacher Mitomon’s safety line was temporarily safe.

Shuichi stroked Lue’s chest feathers.

Water droplets from the plum branch dripped down in the night breeze, hitting the sandy ground.

Tomorrow was the weekend ; he could go to the Forest of Death again.

After the mountain antelope, he already had a rough idea of what the next prey should be.

But not today.

Today, he would dry his clothes first.
