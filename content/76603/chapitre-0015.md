# Chapitre 15 — Training Plunder

On the weekend, Shuichi did not go to the edge of the Forest of Death.

He sat under the plum tree in the backyard, with several items spread out on the ground in front of him : a pheasant flight feather picked up from the Mixed Forest, a Red Fox rib bone picked up from a dried-up riverbed, and a primary flight feather that Plunder had shed itself.

Plunder crouched on a plum branch, tilting its head as it watched its master lay out a row of inexplicable items in front of it, and let out a short, low chirp.

« Don’t be impatient. » Shuichi didn’t even raise his head. « No hunting today. Today is your training day. »

Plunder was already over a year old. When it fell from the cliff at the Training Ground last spring, it was just a ball of grey-brown fluff ; now, its wingspan was nearly 1.3 meters, its flight feathers were neat and sharp, and its soaring height could reach more than twice the height of the Training Ground cliff.

A one-year-old hawk is approaching adulthood, but Shuichi knew that Plunder was different from an ordinary hawk. An ordinary hawk’s Growth stops once it reaches adulthood, but Plunder’s did not.

Plunder’s body carried the Flight Instinct Plundered from a sparrow, as well as new abilities that Shuichi had been continuously stripping and transferring over. Its Growth potential was far beyond that of a normal hawk.

But currently, apart from the Flight Instinct, Plunder only possessed innate hawk senses. It could distinguish the movement of a field mouse in the grass from high in the sky, but when the light was poor at dusk, the details on the ground would blur into a mass of shadows.

It could hear the sound of a hare running in the bushes, but its directional positioning relied on tilting its head to capture the time difference of the sound—it was efficient, but not precise enough.

Shuichi wanted to change this. Plunder was the first node of his ability ecosystem, his eyes ; the strength of this node determined the upper limit of his entire reconnaissance network.

Start with vision.

Shuichi stood up, walked to the wooden frame next to the plum tree, and took down a small bamboo cage containing a grey collared dove.

He had caught it by hand that morning in the Mixed Forest by silently creeping up to the bushes where the dove was roosting, using Chakra to adhere to the soles of his feet.

The dove fluttered a couple of times in the cage, the fine patterns on its grey-brown feathers shimmering in the sunlight.

Shuichi reached his right hand into the cage and pressed his palm against the dove’s back. He expanded his perception. The visual system inside the dove unfolded layer by layer in his mind : color recognition, motion capture, long-distance focusing, and low-light perception.

He concentrated his consciousness, aiming the suction precisely at the low-light perception layer, taking only that layer and touching nothing else.

Plunder. What came from his palm was not the all-encompassing suction of a total extraction, but a more delicate operation, like using tweezers to pick out a single thread from a pile of tangled ends.

The mental energy consumption was greater than expected because the precision required was extremely high. The dove trembled in the cage but did not die ; its pupils' reaction to light became a bit sluggish, but it was not completely blinded.

Shuichi looked at his palm. A tiny trace of warmth remained in his palm—a small portion of low-light vision.

He pressed his right hand onto the top of Plunder’s head and pushed this low-light perception into Plunder’s body.

Strip. Plunder’s body trembled slightly, and its pupils dilated sharply before quickly contracting back.

It blinked, turned its head to look at the water vat in the shaded corner of the yard—a spot that usually looked like a blurry shadow to Plunder—but now it stared at it for several seconds, tilting its head left and right as if recalibrating its visual system.

Success. The compatibility of visual sub-items between bird species is very high.

Next was hearing. Shuichi took out a field mouse from the wooden compartment in the corner of the yard, which he had also caught beforehand. A field mouse’s auditory acuity far exceeds that of a hawk ; it can capture extremely faint infrasound, and its directional positioning accuracy is also higher.

Shuichi pressed his right hand on the field mouse’s back and precisely extracted the [Directional Positioning Accuracy] layer from its auditory system.

When stripping and transferring it to Plunder, Plunder’s reaction was more pronounced than before ; its feathers puffed up for an instant before settling down.

It turned its head towards the Naka River ; its head no longer needed to tilt thirty degrees to locate the sound source. Instead, it barely needed to tilt at all, as the directional information was directly parsed by its brain through the subtle time difference of the sound.

Shuichi let out a gentle breath. Low-light vision and directional hearing—two external abilities superimposed onto Plunder, both from birds and similar species, with sufficiently high compatibility.

Plunder’s reconnaissance efficiency had improved by about half a level. Ground reconnaissance at dusk no longer had blind spots, and the response speed for locating sound sources was a beat faster.

« That’s it for today. » Shuichi stroked Plunder’s chest feathers. « Superimposing two abilities at once, you need time to digest them. »

Plunder let out a low chirp, flew back to the plum branch, and tucked its head into its wing.

Shuichi returned to sit under the plum tree, closed his eyes, and organized in his mind all the rules he had accumulated over the past six months.

Life Force Plunder : Enhances physical constitution base, expands total slot capacity. Not subject to diminishing returns of the same type. Medium-sized or larger prey requires Taijutsu for suppression. Absorption takes time.

Ability Plunder : One Plunder per ability. Undigested abilities have diminishing returns ; once digested, there are no diminishing returns, but they still occupy a slot. Can be stripped and transferred to other organisms—compatibility is higher between similar species. Can precisely extract ability sub-items without harming the whole.

Plunder’s cultivation strategy : Plunder local sub-abilities from similar species and strip/transfer them to Plunder for superimposition. Each new ability allows Plunder’s reconnaissance network to cover a wider area.

Then there was another matter. A matter more fundamental than Plunder’s cultivation.

Sharingan.

Shuichi opened his eyes and looked at Plunder’s silhouette tucked under its wings on the plum tree. He was a member of the Uchiha Clan. The Sharingan was the Uchiha Clan’s strongest Bloodline Limit.

In the original work, the Sharingan requires extreme emotional stimulation to awaken : losing a loved one, witnessing death, or being betrayed by the person one trusts most. Uchiha Madara awakened his Sharingan on the battlefield, Obito jumped directly from two tomoe to Mangekyo after Rin Nohara died, and Uchiha Itachi awakened his Mangekyo after Uchiha Shisui died.

Every path was paved with corpses.

Shuichi didn’t know when his Sharingan would awaken. Perhaps on the night of the clan massacre, but that would be too late. Perhaps on the battlefield of the Third Great Ninja War, but that would be gambling with his life. He needed a path that didn’t require waiting for war, didn’t require waiting for a massacre, and didn’t require waiting for anyone to die.

Right now, he couldn’t directly Plunder a Sharingan ; not having enough strength or a target was one reason, and the fact that a Bloodline Limit belongs to the conceptual category—touching it with his current mental energy would be suicide—was the second reason. What if he could Plunder something that could make the Sharingan grow on its own ?

Growth. That’s it !

I could Plunder the concept of [Growth] from a creature that is rapidly growing—cell division, organ development, the underlying driving force of abilities growing naturally from nothing—and then fuse it into the Sharingan. The Sharingan would then grow on its own. From one tomoe to two tomoe to three tomoe, or even Mangekyo, without needing anyone to die, without needing any stimulation.

But as soon as this thought popped up, Shuichi stopped. He remembered the ten-second struggle when Plundering the Red Fox ; Plundering the life energy of a seven or eight-catty fox consumed nearly half of his mental energy.

And [Growth] is not a specific organ, not a layer of low-light perception, and not a type of directional positioning accuracy. [Growth] is an underlying law that permeates every cell, every gene, and every developmental stage. It is closer to the Root of a concept than the Flight Instinct, and even harder to grasp precisely than Plundering life force from a Red Fox.

Was his mental energy enough ?

Shuichi quickly estimated the data on Plunder consumption over the past half year in his mind. Red Fox life force : nearly half of his mental energy. Sparrow Flight Instinct : about one-third. Dove low-light vision sub-item : about one-sixth. And conceptual-level Plundering—the consumption of Flight Instinct was about two-thirds of the Red Fox’s life force. If the abstraction level of [Growth] was higher than Flight Instinct, its consumption could be two to three times that of the Red Fox.

With his current mental energy limit, he couldn’t sustain it at all. The result of forced Plundering wouldn’t be success, but rather his mental energy being drained, leading to immediate unconsciousness, or perhaps even never waking up.

Shuichi clenched his right hand.

No need to rush. He needed to first increase his mental energy limit. Life Force Plunder could expand his physical constitution base, but he wasn’t clear enough on the ways to increase mental energy. Perhaps he needed to Plunder mental-type abilities to expand his mental slots, or wait for his Chakra cultivation to improve, which would naturally drive the Growth of his mental energy.

There was another difficult problem : even if he had enough mental energy in the future and managed to Plunder the [Growth] concept, how could he precisely fuse it into the Sharingan and not his whole body ? If the concept of Growth spread to his whole body, his entire body would accelerate its development ; a six-year-old child’s bones growing at a speed visible to the naked eye would be a catastrophic anomaly in anyone’s eyes. He had to make the Growth act only on his eyes.

Precise Plundering. This was a technique he had just touched the threshold of while training Plunder—extracting only low-light vision from the dove without touching anything else, and extracting only directional positioning accuracy from the field mouse. Could the same principle be applied to conceptual fusion ? Guiding [Growth] only to the eyes, locking it within the cells and tissues of the eyes ?

Shuichi noted down two problems to be solved in his mind. First, ways to expand his mental energy limit. Second, methods for precisely fusing concepts into specific body parts.

On the plum tree, Plunder poked its head out from its wing and let out a low chirp. Its eyes reflected a brighter light than usual in the twilight, because the low-light vision had already begun to take effect.
