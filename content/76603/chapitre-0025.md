# Chapitre 25 — Test Paper

On Friday after school, Teacher Mitomon posted the group list for the field practice class on the classroom door.

Shuichi stood in front of the list and scanned it. Four people per group, six groups total. He was assigned to the third group. Besides Tetsuya, his group mates included two classmates he hadn’t spoken to much : a short-haired girl named Koharu and a tall, thin boy named Kenta. Neither of them ranked high in taijutsu class.

Tetsuya poked his head out from behind Shuichi’s shoulder. Upon seeing the list, he nearly jumped. « Shuichi ! We’re in the same group ! »

Shuichi continued reading down the list. Hyuga Shimizu was in the fifth group, paired with students who ranked at the bottom of taijutsu and balance beam classes. Teacher Mitomon’s grouping logic was obvious : break up the strong students and distribute them evenly. However, since he didn’t know Hyuga Shimizu was hiding his strength, the fifth group appeared to be the weak team in the teacher’s eyes.

Hyuga Shimizu stood two steps away from Shuichi, also looking at the list. His gaze rested on the fifth group for about three seconds—twice as long as he looked at the others. Then he turned and walked away, his footsteps as light as usual.

Below the group list was a note : Each group must select a leader responsible for collecting the map and equipment bag.

Tetsuya had already pulled Koharu and Kenta over and declared, « Shuichi will be the leader. » The tone wasn’t a suggestion ; it was a notification.

On Saturday morning, Shuichi and Minako went to the Uchiha Clan Office together.

The Clan Office was located in the center of the clan grounds, a two-story wooden building with gray tiles and gray walls, with a faded clan emblem hanging on the door. It opened early on Saturday mornings, but there weren’t many people there. Minako pushed open the wooden door, and a middle-aged Uchiha wearing glasses sat behind the counter.

« Minako. You handed in the forms the day before yesterday ; what is it today ? »

« Educational supply allocation. » Minako pulled the annual confirmation form from her file folder and pointed to the last item. « I haven’t collected this year’s yet. »

The middle-aged Uchiha took the form, retrieved a kraft paper bag from the archive shelf, and opened it to count the items : two Kunai, three shuriken, a roll of Ninja bandages, and a basic Ninja tool pouch.

« That’s all. »

« Does Chakra paper count as educational supplies ? » Shuichi asked directly.

The middle-aged Uchiha looked down at him over his glasses. « Chakra paper ? What do you need that for ? »

« The school has an affinity test next semester. I want to test it in advance. »

The middle-aged Uchiha was silent for two seconds. It was unusual for a six-year-old child to come and ask for the paper, but the Uchiha clan had always valued children’s Ninja aptitude, and testing for affinities early was considered « ambitious » rather than « suspicious. »

« It’s not included in the basic educational supplies. However, the clan has an extra quota for testing. » He stood up and walked into the storage room. A moment later, he returned with a palm-sized, pearlescent white piece of paper and placed it on the counter.

« Do you know how to use it ? »

« Just channel Chakra into it. »

The middle-aged Uchiha nodded and took the paper back, his fingertips igniting with a faint trace of Chakra as he touched each of the four corners. « Creases. Tear it up after use so others don’t pick it up and misuse it. »

Shuichi took the paper. « Thank you. »

As they walked out of the Clan Office, Minako glanced at him. « Are you going to test it back home or now ? »

« Back home. »

When they returned home, Obito was already waiting at the courtyard gate. Seeing Shuichi clutching something in his hand, he didn’t come over, just stood under the plum tree watching Shuichi walk to the veranda and sit down.

Shuichi spread out the paper, took a deep breath, channeled Chakra from his dantian, and touched his fingertips to the surface.

The change happened almost instantly.

The top left corner of the paper curled up as if ignited, the edges turning charred black. Fire affinity. A standard affinity for the Uchiha lineage ; no surprise there.

Immediately after, the right half of the paper began to dampen from the edges, its texture turning into a translucent, viscous substance. Water affinity. Shuichi’s brows twitched. Fire and water were mutually opposing affinities ; having both appear at the same time wasn’t very common, but it wasn’t rare either—Itachi had both Fire and Water affinities.

The changes weren’t over yet.

In the very center of the paper, at the junction of the fire and water marks, the paper wrinkled. The fibers became hard and rough under the influence of the Chakra, with a slight granular texture when touched. Earth affinity.

Three affinities.

Shuichi stared at the paper in his palm, now divided into three distinct textures, while his mind quickly flipped through memories regarding multiple affinities. The Sandaime Hokage, Hiruzen Sarutobi, was a master of five affinities and was known as the « Professor of Ninjutsu. » Kakashi Hatake had Lightning, Water, and Earth ; he became a Jonin at twelve. Multiple affinities weren’t uncommon among Ninjas ; Jonin generally possessed two or more. Testing for three at six years old just meant he had good talent ; it wasn’t worth making a fuss over.

What really made him think was where the Earth affinity came from.

He recalled the prey he had plundered over the past year. Tree lizards, wild rabbits, red foxes, badgers, wild boars—none of them had a clear Earth affinity on their own. But the life force of dozens of different creatures had been fusing within his body for a long time—insects, reptiles, small mammals—they lived in the leaf litter, rock crevices, and underground burrows. The tendencies carried by these life energies had permeated his Chakra system over time, eventually precipitating the third affinity on the paper.

It wasn’t luck. It was the inevitability of a year’s accumulation.

Shuichi tore up the paper and buried the fragments in the sandy soil under the plum tree. Lue flew down from the plum branch and landed on his shoulder, tilting its head as it watched him bury the items.

« Brother Shuichi, what is that ? »

« Chakra paper. For testing affinities. »

« What are your affinities ? »

« Fire, Water, and Earth. »

Obito blinked. « Three ? Is that really impressive ? »

« It’s not bad, » Shuichi stood up. « Ninjas with decent talent have two or three affinities. The Sandaime has five. »

He didn’t explain further. Three affinities meant a wider space for learning—Fire Release for offense, Water Release for support, Earth Release for defense. If the three nature transformations could be integrated, the possibilities for tactical combinations would be several times that of a single affinity. But this required time, and time was exactly what he lacked most right now.

« I’m not teaching anything new today. Review what we did the past few days, » Shuichi said.

Obito didn’t ask any more questions and walked to the corner of the courtyard to start practicing his footwork transitions. His rhythm was steadier than yesterday.

Shuichi sat on the veranda, rearranging the priorities in his mind. Sharingan Growth was the core ; the progression from one-tomoe to two-tomoe was automatically driven by the Growth concept and didn’t require extra time. Life Force Plunder leading to physical enhancement was the fuel, with continuous hunting maintaining the mental loop. Chakra affinity training was third ; he could wait until school started to learn Fire and Water nature transformations. He wouldn’t touch Earth for now ; trying to do too much would just make him ineffective.

But the result of the paper gave him additional information : Life Force Plunder didn’t just improve his physique ; it was also slowly enriching the underlying structure of his Chakra. The more diverse the prey he had plundered last year, the richer the diversity of his affinities might be. If this rule held true, the number of his affinities would continue to increase in the future. It wasn’t a bad thing, but it required a more strategic planning of his learning path.

After lunch, Shuichi walked out of the courtyard. Lue flew up from the plum branch and glided toward the Naka River.

He followed Lue upstream, walking past the stone bridge, the Training Ground, and the pine forest, and crossing the dry riverbed. On Wednesday night, during its night patrol, Lue had discovered bear paw prints in the upper reaches of the Naka River. Today, he wanted to confirm the location and freshness himself.

The outer edge of the Training Ground was an open river beach. The Naka River made a gentle bend here, with a transition zone of pine and mixed forests on both sides of the riverbank. It was reasonable for Teacher Mitomon to choose this as the destination for the field practice class ; the terrain was simple, the view was open, and it was hard to get lost.

But there was no such thing as a forest that didn’t let the wind through.

Lue circled twice over the river beach and then glided north. Shuichi lowered his body, moved through a pine forest, and, following Lue’s instructions, stopped at a river bend about two li from the outer edge of the Training Ground.

On the mudflat, the bear paw prints were clearly visible : five toes, a wide paw pad, and they had sunk about half an inch into the soil. The mud at the edges was still wet ; it was from either today or yesterday. Lue flew back from the opposite side of the river bend and gave a short, low chirp. A second set of prints was also on the opposite bank, one size smaller than the first. A mother bear with a cub.

Shuichi crouched behind a thicket and drew a new map in his mind. The straight-line distance from the Training Ground to the river bend was about two li, with two pine forests and a dry tributary in between. Teacher Mitomon’s field practice wouldn’t let the students run that far, but if someone got lost and wandered north, the possibility of them stumbling into the river bend existed.

A mother bear with a cub was the most dangerous creature in the forest. It wasn’t because it was stronger than a male bear, but because it would stop at nothing. If a six-year-old child even just saw the cub from afar and curiously walked a few steps closer, the mother bear might charge. Teacher Mitomon was a Chunin ; facing a mother bear protecting its cub, he wouldn’t necessarily be able to protect the whole class without injury.

Shuichi stood up and brushed the mud off his knees. When he went to school on Monday, he would tell Teacher Mitomon this news, using the excuse that « the clan’s Military Police Force discovered a bear in the upper reaches of the Naka River during a patrol. » This excuse was natural enough ; it didn’t involve Lue or his secrets. It didn’t matter if Teacher Mitomon believed him or not ; what mattered was that the safety perimeter would be redrawn.

On the way back, he walked along the river beach. The river breeze blew down from upstream, carrying the scent of pine resin and wet mud. Lue circled high in the sky, its wingspan casting a small, moving shadow in the afternoon sun.

There were six days left until the field practice class. Three things : report the bear on Monday, detail the reconnaissance plan, and enter the Forest of Death one more time before the practice class.

The wild boar’s life force had already been digested. It was time to put the next prey on the schedule.
