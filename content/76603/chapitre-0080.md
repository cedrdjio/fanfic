# Chapitre 80 — The Death of Yahiko

The rain in the Land of Rain never stopped.

Gray streaks of rain fell from the leaden sky, striking the muddy ground and forming countless tiny streams.

This land had been soaked by rain for so long that people had forgotten what sunlight looked like.

The air was filled with a damp, musty smell, and even breathing felt heavy with moisture.

A Konoha reconnaissance team moved through the curtain of rain.

Four Ninja, clad in dark combat gear, had their headbands covered with strips of cloth.

They had set out from the border of the Land of Fire and had been traveling for three days.

Their mission objective was to track the movements of Iwagakure in the Land of Rain and prepare for the coming war.

The Third Hokage had made it very clear in the mobilization order : every nation was increasing their troops, and this war would happen sooner or later ; reconnaissance was the first step.

The team was led by a Jonin named Fujiwara.

He was thirty-two years old, in his sixth year as a Jonin, and specialized in sensory perception and infiltration.

While he was packing his gear alone at the Konoha camp, something had burrowed into his shadow.

It was a rainy evening ; he was alone in his tent taking inventory of supplies, the candlelight flickering, causing his shadow to sway on the tent fabric.

He bent down to pick up a dropped Kunai, and at that moment, his shadow seemed to change.

He did not notice.

Black Zetsu had planted the seed of control deep within him.

It was not possession, but a deeper infiltration.

Fujiwara’s consciousness remained, but it was suppressed to the very bottom.

His voice, expressions, and movements showed no change.

The three Chunin following behind him did not notice anything unusual either.

The rain in the Land of Rain was too heavy, heavy enough to cover many traces.

« Captain, we are approaching the border of the Land of Rain, » a Chunin whispered.

Fujiwara nodded. « Continue forward. The objective is to cross the Land of Rain from south to north and record Iwagakure’s movements along the way. »

Fujiwara’s voice was calm. « Hanzou is still here, but his control is waning. Iwagakure, Sunagakure, and even our Konoha are infiltrating this place. »

They continued to advance through the rain, their footsteps masked by the sound of the downpour.

Fujiwara walked in the lead, his eyes fixed ahead.

In a wooden hut on the edge of a small town, Yahiko stood by the window, watching the rain outside.

Rainwater flowed down from the eaves, gathering into a thin stream on the windowsill.

He reached out to catch a drop of rain, the icy touch making him frown slightly.

Nagato sat cross-legged in the center of the room, hands forming seals.

Chakra flowed, resonating with the rain outside.

This was a Ninjutsu he had recently figured out, allowing him to perceive everything within the rain.

His perception spread out ; the range covered by the rain became his eyes.

A moment later, Nagato opened his eyes, the Rinnegan glowing with a faint purple light in the dimness.

« Someone is coming, » Nagato said. « Four people, south of the town. »

Yahiko turned around. « Konoha headbands ? »

« I can’t see clearly, » Nagato said. « But their formation is that of a reconnaissance team. »

Konan sat at the table folding paper, her fingers nimbly flipping the sheets as a paper butterfly took shape in her palm.

She looked up at Yahiko. « What are people from Konoha doing in the Land of Rain ? »

« It must be reconnaissance, » Yahiko said, his voice soft. « Every nation is increasing their troops ; this war will happen sooner or later. »

« I don’t know, » Yahiko turned to look at Nagato and Konan. « But the Land of Rain is now a target for Iwagakure. If Konoha sends people in as well, the situation will become even more complicated. »

Konan stopped folding, and the paper butterfly landed on the table. « What do you want to do ? »

« I’m going to talk to them, » Yahiko said.

Nagato frowned, the patterns of his Rinnegan shifting slightly. « Let’s go together. »

« No need, » Yahiko shook his head. « Too many people will make it look like we’re looking for a fight. I’ll go alone to show we have no hostility. »

« It’s too dangerous, » Nagato stood up, his voice urgent. « If they attack— »

« They are a reconnaissance team ; their mission is to gather intelligence, not to fight, » Yahiko interrupted, his tone calm but firm. « I’ll go explain our position and ask them to leave the Land of Rain. »

Konan looked into Yahiko’s eyes. « Are you sure ? »

« I’m sure, » Yahiko smiled—his usual smile, full of confidence. « Wait for my good news. »

He pushed the door open and stepped into the rain.

Nagato walked to the doorway, watching Yahiko’s back disappear into the curtain of rain.

The Rinnegan turned slightly, and a faint purple light flickered in the darkness.

« I should have gone with him, » Nagato said.

« He’s right, » Konan stood up and walked to Nagato’s side. « Too many people makes it look like a war. Yahiko knows what he’s doing. »

Nagato said nothing. He watched the rain outside, feeling an indescribable sense of unease.

The abandoned warehouse was on the east side of the town ; half the roof was missing, and rain fell through the holes, gathering into puddles on the ground.

The warehouse was piled with rotten wooden planks and rusty iron barrels, and the air was filled with a musty smell.

Yahiko stood in the center of the warehouse, waiting for the Konoha team.

He sensed them approaching. Four people, each with distinct Chakra fluctuations.

The leader’s Chakra was the strongest ; he must be a Jonin. The other three were at the Chunin level.

Fujiwara led the three Chunin into the warehouse, their footsteps echoing in the vast space.

Their gazes swept over Yahiko, and after confirming there was only one person, they relaxed their vigilance slightly.

« Who are you ? » Fujiwara asked.

« My name is Yahiko, » Yahiko said, his voice calm. « I live in this town. »

Fujiwara sized him up. A teenage boy, orange hair soaked by the rain and plastered to his forehead, no headband, no weapons, Chakra fluctuations at the Chunin level. He looked like an ordinary boy from the Land of Rain.

« What business do you have with us ? »

« I want to talk to you, » Yahiko said. « About the Land of Rain. »

Fujiwara said nothing, waiting for him to continue.

« The Land of Rain has suffered for too many years because of the disputes between great nations, » Yahiko looked into Fujiwara’s eyes with a serious gaze. « We don’t want to be dragged into any more wars. You are a Konoha reconnaissance team, and your mission is to gather intelligence on Iwagakure. I understand. But I want to ask you to leave the Land of Rain. »

Fujiwara’s teammates exchanged glances. It was not common for a reconnaissance team to be discovered and asked to leave by a local.

« You said 'we', » Fujiwara said. « Who are you ? »

« We are an organization, » Yahiko said. « Called the Akatsuki Organization. »

« Akatsuki Organization ? »

« Our goal is peace, » Yahiko said, his voice filled with eagerness. « The Land of Rain has already endured too much suffering. We don’t want to see Konoha, Iwagakure, or any other great nation fighting here. This land needs time to recover, not more war. »

Fujiwara listened to him, his expression calm.

Yahiko continued : « I know Konoha has no ill intent ; you just want to understand Iwagakure’s movements. But your presence here will trigger a chain reaction. Iwagakure will discover Konoha’s people, they will strengthen their guard, and they will take action. The Land of Rain will become a battlefield. »

He paused, his gaze sweeping over the three Chunin behind Fujiwara.

« So I want to ask you to leave. Not as enemies, but as residents of the Land of Rain. We don’t want the flames of war to reach this place. »

Fujiwara was silent for a few seconds.

Yahiko thought he was hesitating. What he didn’t know was that Fujiwara’s consciousness had long been suppressed to the very bottom.

Standing in front of Yahiko was Black Zetsu. Black Zetsu was observing Yahiko through Fujiwara’s sensory perception and judging the situation through Fujiwara’s memories.

This boy was a member of the Akatsuki Organization, just three people, Chunin level. No threat.

« Your idea is very interesting, » Black Zetsu said through Fujiwara’s mouth.

« Then are you willing to leave ? » Yahiko asked, a glimmer of hope flashing in his eyes.

Fujiwara’s hand pressed against the Kunai at his waist.

Yahiko noticed this movement, but he didn’t mind. The other party was a Konoha Jonin ; being alert was normal. Besides, he had already stated he had no hostility, so there was no reason for the other party to attack.

« I can consider it, » Fujiwara said. « But before that, I have a question. »

« What question ? »

« How many people are in your organization ? »

Yahiko hesitated. He didn’t want to lie, but he didn’t want to expose too much either. « Three people. »

« Three people, » Fujiwara repeated, his tone flat. « Then what makes you think you can stop the great nations from fighting here ? »

Yahiko’s voice was firm. « We just need to make the great nations understand that war benefits no one. Whether it’s a small nation or a great nation, everyone is human, and everyone wants peace. As long as we sit down and talk, there is no problem that cannot be solved. »

Fujiwara nodded.

« I understand. »

He drew his Kunai.

The movement was as fluid as practicing on targets at the Training Ground ; there was no warning, no killing intent, and it was so fast that Yahiko couldn’t react in time. That was Jonin-level speed, an instinct honed through countless battles.

The Kunai pierced into Yahiko’s chest.

Yahiko looked down at the Kunai in his chest, his eyes widening. Blood gushed from the wound, staining his orange hair and his clothes red. The pain only arrived half a second later, but by then, the Kunai had already pierced his heart.

He wanted to say something, but only a gurgling sound came from his throat.

Fujiwara let go, the Kunai still stuck in Yahiko’s chest. Yahiko fell to the ground, his eyes still open, watching the raindrops falling from the broken roof.

The three Chunin were stunned.

« Captain ? » one of the Chunin asked, his voice filled with shock. « Why— »

Fujiwara’s body suddenly trembled.

Black Zetsu withdrew from Fujiwara’s shadow and silently sank into the ground. The whole process took less than a second ; the three Chunin didn’t even see what it was.

Fujiwara’s eyes regained clarity. He looked at his hands, at Yahiko on the ground, and at the Kunai stuck in Yahiko’s chest. That Kunai was his own ; his name was even carved on the hilt.

« I... » Fujiwara’s voice trembled. « What have I done ? »

He didn’t know what had just happened. He only remembered walking into the warehouse, listening to the boy talk, and then—then he saw his own Kunai stuck in the boy’s chest.

A piece of his memory was missing.

« Captain ! » the three Chunin rushed over. « What’s going on ? »

Fujiwara knelt on the ground, his hands trembling as he tried to hold Yahiko’s wound. But the blood was gushing out too fast ; it couldn’t be stopped. Yahiko’s chest was no longer rising and falling, and the light in his eyes was fading.

« Captain, we have to go, » one of the Chunin whispered. « It’s not safe here. »

Fujiwara stood up. He took one last look at the boy on the ground ; the boy was still wide-eyed, looking at the sky.

« Let’s go, » Fujiwara’s voice was hoarse. « Hurry. »

Fujiwara turned and rushed out of the warehouse, and the three Chunin exchanged glances and followed. The sound of rain masked their footsteps.

The rain was still falling.

When Nagato arrived at the abandoned warehouse, the Konoha team was already gone.

He had sensed something wrong. Just minutes ago, he felt Yahiko’s Chakra suddenly vanish, like a lamp being blown out. Without any hesitation, he rushed out of the wooden hut and ran wildly through the rain.

He saw Yahiko lying alone on the ground with a Kunai stuck in his chest, blood pooling beneath him, mixing with the rain and flowing in all directions.

Nagato knelt in the blood, pressing both hands against Yahiko’s wound. His Rinnegan spun frantically, and a faint purple light flickered in the dimness. Chakra surged as he tried to find some way to sustain Yahiko’s life.

But the blood was gushing too fast ; it couldn’t be stopped. The heart had been pierced, and no medical Ninjutsu could save him.

« Yahiko, » Nagato’s voice trembled. « Yahiko, don’t sleep. »

Yahiko’s lips moved, but there was no sound. His eyes had lost focus ; he was looking in Nagato’s direction, yet it seemed as if he were looking at somewhere much further away.

Nagato could feel Yahiko’s Vitality slipping away, like sand sliding through his fingers ; no matter how hard he tried, he couldn’t hold onto it.

« Don’t die, » Nagato said, his voice hoarse. « Please, don’t die. »

The light in Yahiko’s eyes slowly faded.

Nagato’s hands were shaking, his Chakra was spiraling out of control, and the patterns of his Rinnegan were spinning madly. He felt as if something inside him had collapsed.

The boy who said he would bring peace. The boy who believed that dialogue could solve problems. The boy who always smiled and said, « Wait for my good news. »

The boy who had found him and Konan in the rain and given them food and hope.

Was dead.

Died under a Konoha Kunai. Died in the abandoned warehouse where he had gone alone to negotiate. Died in the conversation he thought could be resolved peacefully.

Nagato knelt in the blood, watching the light in Yahiko’s eyes slowly fade away.

The sound of the rain drowned out everything.

This was the scene Konan saw when she arrived.

She stood at the entrance of the warehouse, the paper in her hand falling to the ground. She saw Nagato kneeling in the blood, saw Yahiko lying on the ground, and saw the Kunai stuck in Yahiko’s chest.

She didn’t scream, didn’t cry. She just stood there, watching it all.

Rainwater fell from the broken roof, hitting Yahiko’s face, hitting Nagato’s back, hitting the paper at Konan’s feet.

Yahiko’s eyes were still open, watching the raindrops falling from the broken roof.

Nagato slowly reached out and closed Yahiko’s eyes.

« I will make them pay, » Nagato said, his voice very soft, so soft it was almost drowned out by the sound of the rain. « Everyone. »

His Rinnegan flickered with a purple light in the rain, as if something were burning within them.

Konan walked over and knelt on the other side of Yahiko. She said nothing, just reached out and took Yahiko’s cold hand.

The rain was still falling.

The rain in the Land of Rain never stopped.
