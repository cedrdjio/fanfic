# Chapitre 42 — Chance Encounter

On the third day after the caravan resumed its journey, Shuichi had already figured out the caravan’s routine.

They would set off at dawn every day, rest for two hours by the roadside at noon to feed the horses, and find a Village or post station to spend the night in the evening.

The three carriages traveled in a single file, with Yuanda’s dilapidated cart always at the very back.

Yuanda had hired another samurai in a Village they passed through, spending fifteen ryo.

The new guard was a taciturn middle-aged man with wrinkles on his face that looked like they had been carved by a knife ; one look was enough to tell he was a veteran who had traveled many trade routes.

He glanced at Shuichi but didn’t ask any questions.

After that incident, Yuanda’s attitude toward Shuichi had changed.

It was no longer filled with vigilance and calculation, but rather a cautious courtesy.

Occasionally, when his gaze swept over Shuichi, there was a sense of relief, as if thinking, « This person is very dangerous, but fortunately, he’s on my side. »

In the afternoon, Shuichi sensed something unusual.

About five hundred meters ahead, two heat sources were standing in the middle of the road.

It wasn’t an ambush ; there were no signs of concealment.

But the Chakra activity of one of them—

Shuichi’s body instantly tensed up.

Far exceeding a Chunin. Far exceeding a Jonin. It was even more intense than Sakumo’s Chakra.

This level of Chakra was something Shuichi had only felt from a very small number of people.

Kage-level.

Shuichi pushed his sensory perception and hearing to the maximum, ready to flee at any moment.

A Kage-level Ninja was not someone he could handle right now ; not to mention fighting, he might not even be able to escape.

However, the other party showed no hostility and was just standing in the middle of the road.

If they were coming for him, with a Kage-level Ninja’s sensory capabilities, he would have already been locked onto.

Shuichi forced himself to relax, but his muscles remained taut.

The carriage continued to move forward.

About two minutes later, the conversation of two women came from ahead.

Shuichi’s enhanced hearing delivered every word into his ears.

« Lady Tsunade, is it really okay for us to stop the carriage like this ? » The young girl’s voice was somewhat hesitant.

« What’s wrong with it ? It’s not often we come across a caravan ; what’s the harm in catching a ride ? » The woman’s voice was low and lazy, as if she were talking about something trivial.

« But won’t that scare them ? »

« Do you think I’m that scary ? »

« No, no, Lady Tsunade— »

« Then that settles it. »

Tsunade.

Shuichi confirmed it in his heart.

Shuichi slowly loosened his grip on his short blade.

They weren’t coming for him ; Tsunade just wanted to catch a ride.

The carriage came to a stop.

From the front came Yuanda’s voice, tinged with a hint of nervousness : « The two of you... is there something you need ? »

« Hmm, » Tsunade said casually, « Where are you all headed ? If it’s on the way, give us a ride. »

Yuanda replied, « We are planning to go to Takino Town. »

« That’s perfect then, just drop me off at Tanzaku Town. »

Shuichi’s gaze passed Yuanda and looked at the two people standing in the middle of the road.

Tsunade was wearing a light gray coat, a wide leather belt tied around her waist, and a huge scroll-shaped object on her back.

Her blonde hair shimmered with a pale golden luster under the afternoon sun, and the diamond-shaped mark on her forehead was faintly visible.

Standing behind her was a young girl, about eight or nine years old, with dark brown hair tied into two buns, wearing a light green top and a small scroll tucked at her waist.

Shizune.

Shuichi looked at Shizune’s face, his heart stirred slightly.

Two months ago, he had seen Shizune at the Ninja Academy.

Although he didn’t usually pay much attention, she was a character in the plot after all, so Shuichi had more or less some memory of her.

Shizune was born in the 36th year of the Hokage era ; she was the same age as Shuichi and also attended the Ninja Academy.

But two months ago she was still in Konoha, yet now she was by Tsunade’s side.

It was very likely that Tsunade had returned to Konoha within these past two months and then taken Shizune away.

By the way, what was Shizune to Tsunade ? Why was she being taken along by Tsunade ?

Yuanda clearly didn’t recognize Tsunade.

He was just an ordinary cloth merchant, and his understanding of the Ninja World was limited to some famous Ninjas ; although he might have heard of the Sannin, he certainly hadn’t seen them.

In his eyes, the people blocking the road were just two women, a big-breasted blonde woman and a little girl.

But Yuanda was a shrewd businessman.

Two women daring to stop a caravan, and looking so relaxed and comfortable, meant they were likely Ninjas.

So, upholding the principle that it’s better to avoid trouble, he agreed.

« So the two of you are going to Tanzaku Town, » Yuanda rubbed his hands together, « but my cart is small and can’t really fit... The carriage in front has space, how about you two sit there ? »

« Fine, » Tsunade said casually.

Yuanda breathed a sigh of relief.

His carriage already had Shuichi sitting in it.

Two women plus a wandering samurai of unknown origin—this combination was too prone to trouble.

Tsunade and Shizune walked toward the carriage in front.

When passing Yuanda’s carriage, Tsunade’s gaze swept across the carriage casually.

She saw a man leaning inside, in his early thirties, with a thin face, a ponytail, and his eyes closed as if dozing off.

A crow was perched on his shoulder.

Tsunade’s gaze rested on the man for less than a second.

She felt Chakra.

Chunin level. Not much, but not weak either.

Tsunade withdrew her gaze and continued walking forward.

Then the caravan resumed its journey.

That night, the caravan spent the night at a post station in a small town.

Yuanda and the driver started a fire in the clearing beside the post station and cooked a pot of stew.

The guards took turns by the carriages.

Tsunade and Shizune sat beside the carriage in front, having started their own fire.

Tsunade pulled a wine bottle from her bosom, took a sip from the mouth of the bottle, and let out a satisfied sigh.

Shuichi leaned against the underside of Yuanda’s carriage, his back against the wheel, eyes closed.

His hearing was continuously working.

After his hearing enhancement, it was now three or four times more sensitive than before.

On a quiet night, this distance was enough to hear conversations clearly from twenty meters away.

« Lady Tsunade, » Shizune’s voice came over, with a hint of hesitation, « Why... are we going to Tanzaku Town ? »

« Gambling, » Tsunade said.

The tone was matter-of-fact, as if she were saying « The weather is nice today. »

Shizune was silent for two seconds.

« Gam... Gambling ? »

« Yes. The casino in Tanzaku Town is the largest in the Land of Fire. I heard a new one opened recently, and the odds are good. »

Tsunade took another swig of wine, « I haven’t been to Tanzaku Town in a long time ; my hands are itching. »

« Lady Tsunade ! Shouldn’t we be going for training ? Didn’t you say— »

« What’s the rush ? Isn’t gambling a form of training too ? » Tsunade interrupted Shizune.

Shizune seemed to want to say something, but in the end, she just sighed.

A voice came from Tsunade’s side again.

« Oh, right, Shizune. »

« Hmm ? »

« How are those things I taught you coming along ? »

Shizune’s voice became a bit more serious : « I’ve practiced the extraction and neutralization of toxins until I’m proficient, and I can independently perform basic hemostasis and suturing for medical Ninjutsu. But the precise control of Chakra... is still lacking. »

Tsunade snorted. « Keep practicing. Precise Chakra control is the foundation of medical Ninjutsu ; if you can’t control it well, don’t even think about saving people. »

« Yes. » Shizune’s voice sounded a bit dejected.

« Don’t be so down, » Tsunade’s tone softened a bit, « How long have you been learning ? It’s much faster than I was back then. »

« Really ? »

« I’m lying to you. »

« Ah ! »

Shizune, knowing she had been teased again, sighed.

In the two months since she left with Tsunade, she had already been teased several times.

However, although Tsunade’s personality was a bit nasty, she was still a good teacher.

Knowledge that many teachers in school couldn’t explain clearly, Tsunade could make her understand in just a few times.

Now her medical Ninjutsu had made rapid progress.

Tsunade looked at Shizune’s aggrieved expression and took a sip of wine, « Go to sleep, we have to keep moving tomorrow. »

« Yes, Lady Tsunade. »

The conversation ended.

Shuichi leaned against the wheel, listening to the steady breathing coming from Tsunade’s side.

He couldn’t help but marvel that a Kage-level Ninja’s sleep quality was indeed good ; they could fall asleep just like that.

Shizune was still tossing and turning.

After a while, her breathing also gradually steadied.

Shuichi opened his eyes and looked at the night sky.

Tsunade. One of the Sannin. The heir to the Senju Clan. The legendary medical Ninja.

If he used Tsunade’s hemophobia now, could he take her down ?

But after thinking about it, Shuichi gave up on this dangerous idea.

He closed his eyes again and continued to rest.
