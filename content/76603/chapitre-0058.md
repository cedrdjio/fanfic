# Chapitre 58 — Report

Konoha Village, Hokage Office.

Hiruzen Sarutobi sat behind his desk, holding a pipe in his hand, and slowly exhaled a puff of smoke.

Outside the window was the peaceful Konoha Village, with sunlight spilling over the rooftops ; everything appeared calm and serene.

But this tranquility was soon broken.

« Hokage-sama ! »

An Anbu member appeared in the office, kneeling on one knee.

« Jiraiya-sama has returned, and he is severely injured. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi’s hand paused, and his pipe nearly slipped from his grasp.

« Severely injured ? »

« Yes. Jiraiya-sama has been pierced in multiple places across his body. His injuries are critical, and the medical team is currently performing emergency treatment. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi stood up immediately.

« Take me there. »

---

Konoha Hospital, Intensive Care Unit.

Jiraiya lay on the hospital bed, covered in bandages. His left shoulder, right leg, abdomen, and chest—every wound was carefully bandaged.

The medical Ninjas were using medical Ninjutsu to accelerate his recovery, but the wounds were too deep and the blood loss too great ; his recovery was very slow.

Hiruzen Sarutobi walked into the room, and seeing Jiraiya’s condition, his brows furrowed tightly.

« What is his condition ? »

« His injuries are very severe, » said the head of the medical team. « Multiple internal organs are damaged, and there was excessive blood loss. However, he has stabilized, though he will need at least a month of recuperation. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi nodded and walked to the bedside.

Jiraiya opened his eyes, and upon seeing Hiruzen Sarutobi, he spoke with difficulty.

« Hokage-sama… »

« Don’t speak, rest first, » Hiruzen Sarutobi said.

« No… I have important intelligence… I must report it… »

Jiraiya gritted his teeth, enduring the pain, and began to recount his experience at Hozuki Castle.

From receiving the news that Kusagakure had been destroyed, to activating Sage Mode and rushing to Hozuki Castle, and finally encountering that mysterious powerhouse.

« That person’s Chakra reserves were terrifying, » Jiraiya said. « And his eyes—they were the Mangekyo Sharingan. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi’s pupils contracted slightly.

« The Mangekyo Sharingan ? »

« Yes, » Jiraiya said. « His Mangekyo has two abilities. His left eye can turn real attacks into illusions, allowing Ninjutsu to pass through his body. His right eye can create illusions and then turn those illusions into real attacks. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi fell silent.

These two abilities combined offense and defense perfectly ; they were an unsolvable existence.

« What kind of person is he ? » Hiruzen Sarutobi asked.

« He appears to be around thirty years old, with a gaunt face, » Jiraiya said. « But his tone and way of speaking were like those of a very old man. »

Jiraiya paused for a moment, then continued.

« He said he knew Uchiha Madara. He also said that Konoha was a Village founded by Madara. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi’s brows knitted even tighter.

« He knew Madara ? »

« Yes, » Jiraiya said. « I speculate that he might be from the Sengoku era, a contemporary of Uchiha Madara. He must have used some kind of forbidden technique to remain young, which is why he only looks thirty. »

« The Sengoku era… » Hiruzen Sarutobi murmured.

« And he seems to have a favorable impression of Konoha, » Jiraiya said. « He said, 'Since you are from the Village founded by Madara, and you have this level of strength at your age, I will spare your life.' He could have killed me, but he didn’t. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi fell silent.

The Mangekyo Sharingan. A survivor from the Sengoku era. Acquainted with Uchiha Madara. Favorable towards Konoha.

These pieces of information swirled constantly in his mind.

But what worried him even more was another question.

What did the Mangekyo Sharingan signify ?

It was the Bloodline Limit of the Uchiha Clan.

This person possessed the Mangekyo Sharingan ; what was his relationship with the Uchiha Clan ?

Was he a member of the Uchiha Clan ?

If so, how many more secrets did the Uchiha Clan have that Konoha was unaware of ?

If not, where did he get his Mangekyo Sharingan ?

Hiruzen Sarutobi’s thoughts grew increasingly heavy.

« Where is he now ? » Hiruzen Sarutobi asked.

« He left Hozuki Castle, » Jiraiya said. « He said there was nothing of value in Hozuki Castle, and then he left. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi nodded and fell into deep thought.

---

The next day, Hokage Office.

Hiruzen Sarutobi summoned the high-level officials of Konoha—Danzo, Homura Mitokado, and Utatane Koharu.

« You have all read Jiraiya’s report, » Hiruzen Sarutobi said.

The three of them nodded.

« What are your thoughts ? »

Danzo spoke : « The Mangekyo Sharingan, a person from the Sengoku era. This individual is too dangerous. »

« And he has a connection to the Uchiha Clan, » Homura Mitokado said. « The Mangekyo Sharingan is the Bloodline Limit of the Uchiha Clan. The fact that he possesses it indicates he has some sort of link to the Uchiha Clan. »

« This is precisely what I am most worried about, » Hiruzen Sarutobi said.

« Do you think he is a member of the Uchiha Clan ? » Utatane Koharu asked.

« I’m not sure, » Hiruzen Sarutobi said. « But the appearance of the Mangekyo Sharingan means the Uchiha Clan might be hiding something. Perhaps the Uchiha Clan possesses power we are unaware of, or perhaps they have some connection to this person. »

Danzo’s eyes narrowed slightly.

« The Uchiha Clan… »

« Therefore, this matter must be kept confidential, » Hiruzen Sarutobi said. « We cannot let the Uchiha Clan know that we have obtained this intelligence. »

The three of them nodded.

« What about externally ? » Homura Mitokado asked. « What will other Villages think ? »

Hiruzen Sarutobi paused for a moment and said : « First, we will not disclose the person’s true identity to the outside world. We will only say he is a powerful rogue Ninja of unknown strength. »

« Why ? » Utatane Koharu asked.

« Because it is the Mangekyo Sharingan, » Hiruzen Sarutobi said. « If other Villages learn that the Uchiha Clan has such a powerhouse, they will turn their attention toward Konoha, toward the Uchiha Clan. Relations between Konoha and other Villages are becoming increasingly tense, and we cannot give them any excuses. »

« Furthermore, » Danzo added, « if other Villages learn that the Uchiha Clan has hidden such power, they will become even more wary of Konoha. They might even unite to deal with us. »

« Correct, » Hiruzen Sarutobi said. « Therefore, the matter of the Mangekyo Sharingan must absolutely not be leaked. »

« Second, we will misrepresent his strength, » Hiruzen Sarutobi continued.

« Misrepresent ? » Homura Mitokado asked, puzzled.

« Yes, » Hiruzen Sarutobi said. « When we disclose it to the outside world, we will state his strength to be even higher. »

« Why ? »

« To make other Villages believe he is an extremely powerful Ninja, » Hiruzen Sarutobi said. « This way, if other Villages encounter him, they will choose to either avoid him or go all out. If they choose to go all out, even if he can’t severely damage them, he can still inflict some losses upon them. »

Danzo nodded.

« Hokage-sama means to let that person become a threat to other Villages ? »

« Yes, » Hiruzen Sarutobi said. « He has a favorable impression of Konoha, or at least no hostility for now. We can utilize him to make other Villages wary of him. If other Villages come into conflict with him, it will be to our advantage. »

The three of them were silent for a moment, then nodded.

« This plan is feasible, » Homura Mitokado said.

« But we must also pay attention to monitoring the Uchiha Clan, » Utatane Koharu said. « If that person has a connection with the Uchiha Clan, we must be informed in time. »

« Of course, » Hiruzen Sarutobi said. « I will have the Anbu monitor the Uchiha Clan to see if there are any abnormalities. »

« What about other clans ? » Homura Mitokado asked. « Should we tell them ? »

Hiruzen Sarutobi was silent for a moment.

« We won’t tell them for now, » he said. « The fewer people who know about the Mangekyo Sharingan, the better. Moreover, we are not yet sure who might be colluding with the Uchiha Clan ; telling them might alert the Uchiha Clan prematurely. »

Danzo nodded.

« Then it is decided, » Hiruzen Sarutobi said. « Externally, we will only say he is a powerful rogue Ninja of unknown strength. Have both the Anbu and Root keep watch, and if there is any news about that person, report it immediately. At the same time, monitor the Uchiha Clan to see if there are any abnormalities. »

« Yes, » the three of them replied in unison.

---

After the meeting, Hiruzen Sarutobi sat alone in his office.

He looked out at Konoha Village from the window, his mind filled with complex thoughts.

The Mangekyo Sharingan. A survivor from the Sengoku era. The Uchiha Clan.

What exactly was the relationship between these three ?

Was that person truly a member of the Uchiha Clan ?

If so, how much power had the Uchiha Clan hidden ?

If not, where did his Mangekyo Sharingan come from ?

Hiruzen Sarutobi did not know.

But he knew that Konoha must handle this carefully.

Relations between Konoha and other Villages were becoming increasingly tense, and a Ninja World War could break out again at any time.

At such a time, the appearance of a powerhouse whose strength far exceeded the Sannin, and who might have a connection to the Uchiha Clan, was both an opportunity and a threat to Konoha.

If he could become an asset to Konoha, then Konoha would have an advantage in the Third Ninja World War.

If he became an enemy of Konoha, or an asset to the Uchiha Clan, then Konoha would face a tremendous crisis.

Hiruzen Sarutobi took a deep breath and lit his pipe.

Regardless, Konoha must be prepared.

Furthermore, he had to pay even closer attention to the Uchiha Clan.
