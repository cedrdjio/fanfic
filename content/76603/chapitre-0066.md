# Chapitre 66 — Fusing the Byakugan

Back home.

Shuichi walked into his room and closed the door.

He stood behind the door, closed his eyes, and replayed the night’s events in his mind.

After confirming there were no omissions, he walked to the bed, sat down, and began to digest the Byakugan abilities he had Plundered.

The sensory abilities of the two Byakugan flooded his mind. X-ray vision, telescopic vision, 360-degree vision—these abilities gradually integrated into his sensory system.

Shuichi’s brow furrowed slightly.

The Byakugan’s method of perception was completely different from the Sharingan. The Sharingan captured dynamic movement and predicted actions, with the focus concentrated on things directly in front. The Byakugan, however, was diffusive ; its field of view expanded in all directions, providing a 360-degree view without blind spots, while simultaneously penetrating obstacles to see behind walls, under floors, and the Chakra meridians inside a human body.

Shuichi tried to activate the Byakugan.

A pale blue light emerged in the depths of his pupils, and his vision changed instantly. Walls became transparent ; he could see the structure of the next room, could see Minako lying on the bed, and could see the Chakra meridians within her body.

He deactivated the Byakugan.

The pale blue light vanished, and his vision returned to normal.

He activated the Byakugan again, then activated the Sharingan.

With both abilities running simultaneously, his vision became chaotic. Dynamic capture and static X-ray vision interfered with each other ; the amount of information was too great, and his brain couldn’t process it all at once.

Shuichi deactivated both abilities and closed his eyes.

It needed to be fused slowly.

He reactivated the Byakugan, using only its perception.

He turned to look at the wall, his gaze penetrating the brick and stone. In the next room, Minako was already asleep, her breathing steady. He could see the Chakra meridians in her body. Chakra flowed slowly through the meridians, faint and steady.

He looked under the floor.

He could see the grain of the wooden planks and the stones of the foundation.

He looked out the window.

The entire Uchiha Clan Compound unfolded in his field of view. The internal structure of every house, every person in every room, the flow of every Chakra meridian.

The telescopic ability allowed him to see details hundreds of meters away clearly. He could see the expression on the face of an Uchiha clansman patrolling, the ninja tool pouch at the man’s waist, and the distribution of the man’s Chakra.

The 360-degree vision allowed him to perceive what was behind him without turning his head.

Shuichi sat on the edge of the bed, unmoving, but the information of the entire room, the entire courtyard, and the entire clan compound flowed through his mind.

This was the Byakugan.

He deactivated the Byakugan and reactivated the Sharingan.

The Three Tomoe Sharingan rotated, and his vision returned to the familiar dynamic capture mode.

Then, he tried to fuse the two abilities again.

This time, he didn’t let the two perceptions interfere with each other ; instead, he let them complement each other. The Sharingan was responsible for capturing dynamics and predicting actions, while the Byakugan handled X-ray and panoramic perception.

The two abilities gradually merged together.

His vision began to feel strange ; he could see a 360-degree panorama, while simultaneously focusing on any direction to capture dynamic details in that area.

He could penetrate walls to see enemies behind them, while simultaneously predicting the enemy’s trajectory.

After the fusion was complete, two types of light flickered in Shuichi’s pupils : the red patterns of the Three Tomoe Sharingan and the pale blue of the Byakugan.

He deactivated the fused vision, intending to Plunder the other Byakugan ability to give it to Lue.

Shuichi stood up and walked to the window.

Lue was crouching on the windowsill, watching him.

Shuichi reached out his right hand and pressed it onto Lue’s head.

« Are you ready ? »

Lue’s wings tightened slightly, its compound eyes watching Shuichi.

It didn’t speak, just nodded gently.

The bestowal began.

The sensory ability of the Byakugan was injected into Lue’s body.

Lue’s body trembled slightly.

It felt the sensation of that power surging in—cold and fine, like countless thin threads drilling into its eyes, spreading along its nerves to its entire head.

Its pupils began to change. The originally black compound eyes gradually glowed with a pale blue light, and it felt as if countless tiny blood vessels were pulsing in the depths of its pupils.

Lue did not struggle, just endured it quietly.

It knew this was a gift from Shuichi.

A few seconds later, the bestowal was complete.

Shuichi withdrew his hand.

Lue opened its eyes, the pale blue light in its pupils flickering slightly.

It turned its head to look at the wall.

« How does it feel ? »

Lue remained silent for a moment, its wings fluttering gently.

« My vision... has expanded, » it said. « Before, I could only see what was in front of me, but now I can perceive all directions without turning my head. And I can penetrate walls to see what’s inside. »

There was a complex emotion in Lue’s voice.

It had gained a new ability, but it also bore a new responsibility. Shuichi giving it the Byakugan was to make it a better scout, a better partner.

« Reconnaissance will be easier from now on, » Lue said. « I can see everything from high altitude without needing to get close. »

Shuichi nodded.

The Byakugan’s ability had already integrated into Lue’s sensory system. Combined with its original Infrared Sensing and low-light vision, its reconnaissance capability had now been elevated to a new level.

« Rest now, » Shuichi said. « We’ll continue practicing Wind Release tomorrow. »

Lue nodded and flew to the corner of the windowsill to crouch down.

Shuichi sat back on the edge of the bed, closed his eyes, and continued to familiarize himself with the newly fused sensory ability.

---

Three hours later.

The Konoha night patrol squad passed through the street next to the shopping district.

The team was led by a Chunin, followed by three Genin. They patrolled along a fixed route, circling Konoha every two hours.

A gust of wind blew by, bringing a faint smell of rust.

The Chunin stopped and frowned.

« What is that smell ? »

The Genin beside him sniffed. « It’s the smell of blood. »

The Chunin looked in the direction of the smell, pointing to an alleyway nearby.

« Let’s go inside and take a look. »

The four of them walked into the alley, their footsteps echoing in the narrow space.

Moonlight spilled down from the gap above the alley, illuminating the ground.

The Chunin saw two people lying on the ground and was stunned for a moment.

« Hey, are you guys okay ? »

He walked over and reached out to push one of them on the shoulder.

The man’s body rolled over ; there was a knife wound on his throat, and the blood had already dried.

The Chunin’s expression changed instantly.

« They’re dead ! »

He stepped back a few paces and turned to tell the three Genin : « Guard the entrance of the alley, don’t let anyone in. You, go notify the patrol headquarters and report that a body has been found. »

One Genin nodded and ran out of the alley toward the patrol headquarters.

The other two Genin guarded the alley entrance, stopping a few passersby who had been attracted by the smell of blood.

Soon, more patrol members arrived and cordoned off the alley.

---

Hyuga Clan Compound.

Late at night, the lights in the clan leader’s residence were still on.

Hyuga Hiashi sat in the study, reviewing the family’s recent mission records.

A clansman knocked on the door and entered in a hurry.

« Clan Leader, two bodies have been found in the alley next to the shopping district. They are Hyuga Tokutaro and Hyuga Kenji. »

Hyuga Hiashi put down the file in his hand and stood up.

« When were they found ? »

« Half an hour ago, by the night patrol squad. »

Hyuga Hiashi walked out of the clan leader’s residence, with a few branch family members following behind him.

They passed through the clan compound and headed toward the shopping district.

The night breeze was cool, and the streets were empty, with only patrol members guarding the perimeter of the cordon.

Hyuga Hiashi walked into the alley and saw the two bodies lying on the ground.

He crouched down to examine the bodies.

Both victims had been killed with a single strike ; the knife wounds on their throats were clean and precise. There were also a few stab wounds on their chests and backs, appearing to be from close-quarters combat.

Hyuga Hiashi checked the bodies' eyes.

The Byakugan were still in their sockets, intact and undamaged.

He stood up, his face grim.

« What about residual Chakra ? »

« None, » the clansman beside him reported. « There are no traces of Ninjutsu at the scene, no residual Chakra. The knife wounds are the only clue. »

Hyuga Hiashi was silent for a moment.

The lives of branch family members were not important.

The Hyuga Clan had dozens of branch family members ; the death of two Chunin would not significantly affect the clan’s strength.

But where they died was significant.

If they had died on the battlefield, it would be a normal sacrifice ; the Hyuga Clan would investigate the killer for the sake of their deaths, but wouldn’t care too much.

But dying in the Village held a completely different meaning.

This was a murder committed inside Konoha.

Konoha, the place where the Hyuga Clan lived, the territory of the Hyuga Clan.

Someone dared to take action here, dared to kill Hyuga people near the Hyuga Clan Compound.

This was a slap in the face to the Hyuga Clan.

Hyuga Hiashi looked at the bodies, his gaze cold and stern.

Even just to save face, the killer had to be found.

« Keep this quiet, » Hyuga Hiashi said. « Tomorrow morning, I will go see the Hokage and request that the Hyuga Clan be allowed to investigate this matter. »

« Clan Leader, will the Hokage agree ? »

« He will, » Hyuga Hiashi said. « Since Hyuga people died in Konoha, the Hyuga Clan has the right to know who did it. »

The clansman beside him nodded and turned to relay the order.

Hyuga Hiashi stood in the alley, looking at the two bodies.

Who was the killer ?

A master of swordsmanship, killing with a single strike, no traces of Ninjutsu, no residual Chakra.

This technique looked like that of a specially trained assassin.

Someone from another Village ? Or someone from within Konoha ?

Hyuga Hiashi’s brow furrowed tightly.

No matter who it was, he had to find them.

The face of the Hyuga Clan could not be trampled into the dirt like this.
