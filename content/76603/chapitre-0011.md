# Chapitre 11 — Mother's Intuition

The forty-first year of the Hokage, late summer.

When Lue flew back from the edge of the Forest of Death, it held a small tuft of silver-gray fur in its talons.

Shuichi took the tuft of fur from Lue’s talons and rolled it between his fingers ; it was coarser than rabbit fur, harder than fox fur, and carried a faint, fishy odor.

Badger.

Shuichi weighed this in his mind. A badger is slightly smaller than a fox, but its muscle density is extremely high. He had watched animal documentaries in his past life ; an adult badger could kill one of three attacking wolves and then escape unscathed.

Foxes are agile predators that rely mainly on kicking and biting to resist after being pinned down. Badgers are different ; they are head-on fighters. They won’t run ; they will fight face-to-face. Their front claws can dig through frozen earth, and their mouths can crush bones. If a Red Fox’s kick could leave a bruise on his ribs, a badger backed into a corner could bite off his fingers in seconds.

He touched his left ribs ; the bruise left by the Red Fox had faded, but the suffocating feeling of the prey fighting for its life remained in his memory. Plundering medium-sized or larger prey wasn’t just a matter of pinning it down with his right hand. During those ten seconds of Plunder, he had to suppress the prey’s struggle the entire time. And the taijutsu and strength required to suppress a badger, given his current physique which was roughly equivalent to a ten-year-old child, were far from enough.

He spread the badger fur flat on his knees and updated his hunting classification table in his mind.

Level 1 : Insects and small lizards — Instant Plunder, no resistance, can be handled in large quantities.

Level 2 : Wild rabbits and pheasants — Plunder for about two to three seconds, slight struggle, can be suppressed with one hand.

Level 3 : Red Fox — Plunder for about ten seconds, violent resistance, requires full-body suppression, potential for injury.

Level 4 : Red Fox and above — Not recommended for solo hunting with current physique. Requires a stronger taijutsu foundation or strategic coordination.

« You are more dangerous than a fox, » Shuichi whispered to the tuft of silver-gray fur on his knees, « Wait a little longer. »

He didn’t plan to go anywhere today. Not just because the badger was too dangerous, but also because he had noticed his mother’s gaze before he left the house.

Minako didn’t stop him. She hadn’t stopped him all summer. Every morning when Shuichi left, she would only say, « Come back early, » and in the evening when he returned, she would only glance at his mud-stained trouser cuffs before continuing with what she was doing. But her gaze was changing. It had turned from initial curiosity to observation, and from observation to something Shuichi couldn’t quite name. It wasn’t suspicion ; it felt more like waiting, waiting for Shuichi to speak up himself.

Shuichi sat under the plum tree in the backyard, with Lue perched on his shoulder, using its beak to preen the debris from its wings. Summer was nearly over, and Lue’s size was now close to that of an adult falcon, with a wingspan of nearly a meter, and its flight feathers had turned from grayish-brown to dark brown. Its posture while circling over the Forest of Death already carried a clear sense of territoriality ; once, Shuichi had seen it drive away a wild hawk that tried to approach.

« Lue, » Shuichi said softly, « Has Mom noticed ? »

Lue tilted its head and looked at him, emitting a short cry. It didn’t understand the question. But Shuichi knew he could no longer run out without a care in the world as he had at the beginning of summer. It wasn’t because his mother would stop him, but because she wouldn’t. She never stopped him, which made it harder for Shuichi to continue his silence.

At dinner, Minako made grilled fish. Shuichi sat opposite her at the dining table, eating quietly.

« Shuichi. »

« Hmm ? »

Minako put down her chopsticks and held the teacup with both hands. The steam from the tea rose slowly in front of her, blurring the fine lines at the corners of her eyes. She looked at Shuichi, silent for several seconds.

« Mom is not a Ninja, » she said in a soft voice, « But Mom married a Ninja, watched him grow up, watched him become a Chunin, and watched him go on missions. There are some things Mom can tell. »

Shuichi put down his chopsticks.

« You have been running out since spring. First the Mixed Forest, then the Training Ground, then places further north. Your body is changing. It’s not an ordinary change ; a five-year-old child wouldn’t have your current strength and speed. That hawk on your shoulder was able to fly in less than a month. Normal fledgling hawks don’t take that long. »

Shuichi remained silent.

Minako took a sip of tea and placed the cup back on the table. Her movements were steady. « Mom doesn’t know what happened. Perhaps it’s better not to know. But if what you are doing is dangerous, even just a little bit dangerous, you at least need to let Mom know when you are safe. »

Shuichi looked down at the palm of his right hand. There were no marks there. But over the past few months, this hand had Plundered dozens of lives, changed his physique, changed his abilities, and changed the trajectory of his entire life. The memory of fighting a Red Fox still lingered on his ribs ; that fox had nearly bitten through his wrist. And through all of this, his mother hadn’t asked a single word, until now.

« There is no danger, » Shuichi raised his head and looked into Minako’s eyes, « At least not regarding the things you are worried about. I can handle it myself. If I encounter something I cannot handle, I will tell Mom. »

Minako looked at him for a long time. Then she reached out and patted Shuichi’s head across the dining table.

« You are looking less and less like a five-year-old, » she said. Shuichi had heard this phrase several times before. But this time she didn’t wait for Shuichi to evade, but continued, « But regardless of whether you look like one or not, you are my child. »

Shuichi lowered his eyelids.

« You are starting school next spring, » Minako withdrew her hand, her tone returning to its usual lightness, « Once you go to the Ninja Academy, you will learn how to refine Chakra and how to get along with teammates. By then, you might find that you can do more than you can now. »

Shuichi understood the subtext of this sentence. Minako wasn’t asking where his abilities came from. She was telling him : No matter where your abilities come from, once you get to the Ninja Academy, you will at least learn how to use them safely.

« I will be careful, » Shuichi said.

« I know, » Minako nodded and picked up her chopsticks to continue eating.

Shuichi also picked up his chopsticks again. But his hand clenched tightly under the table for a moment.

He still had eighteen years. But some things were closer than eighteen years. Next spring was the Ninja Academy. Next spring, he would officially step over the threshold of this Ninja world. And before that, he needed to build a solid foundation—Lue needed to continue expanding its scouting range, and his physique needed to improve to a level sufficient to safely suppress medium-sized prey ; those prey larger and more dangerous than the badger would one day no longer be targets that needed to be avoided.

Outside the window, the branches and leaves of the plum tree swayed gently in the night breeze. Lue perched on a branch, tucked its head into its wings, and its black eyes glimmered in the darkness.
