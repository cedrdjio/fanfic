# Chapitre 40 — Heading to Kusagakure

« So, Kana-nee, will you go to Konoha with them first, or come with me to Kusagakure ? »

With Kakashi and Guy present, along with a joint escort of over a hundred elite Ninjas, the safety of Shikaku and the other wounded was not an issue.

After all, they were within the borders of the Land of Fire ; it was impossible for a large army to attack. Lone Kage-level powerhouses like Kakuzu were rare in the Ninja World ; one could only say Shikaku was incredibly unlucky.

« I want to go with you. Karin has never been away from me for this long. »

« If this mission hadn’t been so important, they wouldn’t have let me leave her side. »

Uzumaki Kana spoke firmly. She was worried about Karin and wanted to see the end of those demons with her own eyes. For either reason, she had to go.

Asakawa Shin nodded to her, then exchanged looks with Kakashi, Shikaku, and the others.

« Then it’s settled. You all head back to Konoha first. Kana-nee and I will be back soon. »

Nara Shikaku’s eyelid twitched, and the bad premonition in his heart grew stronger.

Although Shin said he knew what he was doing, the more he said that, the more ridiculous things tended to get, because his standards were completely different from those of ordinary people.

This kid... there’s something not right about him...

But since he couldn’t stop the brat, he decided not to care and just report it directly.

If the sky falls, the Hokage will be there to hold it up. Whatever comes, we’ll deal with it. It’s also good to let Hokage-sama know that being a teacher isn’t that easy.

After a brief rest, Asakawa Shin set off with Uzumaki Kana. Destination : Kusagakure, Land of Grass.

The Land of Grass is located in the center of the Ninja World, sandwiched between the Land of Fire and the Land of Earth. It serves as a natural war buffer zone. Much like its neighbor to the west, the Land of Rain, they are brothers in misfortune, becoming a battlefield in every Ninja World War.

Consequently, the Ninjas living here have long existed on the brink of war, even developing unique survival strategies, including quickly grasping situational trends and flexibly using diplomatic means.

Commonly known as 'fence-sitters,' which matches their country’s name rather well.

During the journey.

« Kana-nee, wait a moment. » Asakawa Shin suddenly braked on a tree branch and called out to Uzumaki Kana. Thinking there was an enemy, Kana immediately drew a Kunai and stood protectively in front of him.

« Kana-nee, no need to be nervous. I just think we’re traveling too slowly this way. »

« Ah ? I’m sorry, Lord Shin. It’s all because my strength is too weak. » Uzumaki Kana felt ashamed, thinking she was holding the little hero back.

« How about I carry you on my back ? That way we can be faster... »

Before Asakawa Shin could finish his sentence, he saw Kana’s two long legs, which were taller than he was. He looked down at his own short legs and couldn’t help but change his tune :

« I mean, why don’t I carry you in my arms, like I did before ? »

« Ah ? Lord Shin, but that posture is just too... » Embarrassing ? Kana was too embarrassed to say it out loud.

« Too what ? Kana-nee, don’t you want to see Karin sooner ? »

« I’m worried she might be bullied, and she must miss her mother very much right now. »

« Because if I were away from home for a long time, I’d miss my mom too. I wonder what that girl Hanako is doing right now ? »

Meeting Asakawa Shin’s innocent eyes, Kana cursed herself inwardly.

The child is only six years old. Why am I always thinking about such messy things ? I must have been tainted by that trash heap known as Kusagakure.

And is this child also thinking about his mother ? Thinking of this, Kana’s maternal instincts flooded out.

She gently pulled Asakawa Shin into her arms, softly stroking his hair, and whispered warmly in his ear : « Then I’ll leave it to you, Lord Shin. »

Asakawa Shin felt as if he had been injected with chicken blood, saying excitedly : « Oh ! Leave it to me ! »

With that, under Kana’s somewhat shy gaze, he picked her up in a princess carry.

« Let’s go, let’s go ! » After using Haki to form an armor to protect Kana, Asakawa Shin accelerated forward.

His speed was more than ten times faster than before. He could have gone even faster, but he was afraid Kana wouldn’t be able to handle it if he did.

He was truly afraid that some butterfly effect would cause Karin to disappear. Didn’t Shikaku almost die ?

Karin, a child alone in that nest of villains in Kusagakure, was in too much danger.

In Asakawa Shin’s arms, Kana watched the scenery receding at high speed in shock. How could it be this much faster ?

And she could hardly feel the wind, as if she were staying inside a metal box.

She asked Asakawa Shin and was told this was called the Airplane Technique.

Seeing Asakawa Shin’s relaxed and natural appearance, clearly not yet at full strength, it was no wonder the little hero thought the speed was slow.

She had judged the little hero’s heart with her own small woman’s mind. She really shouldn’t have, and she must never do so again...

'Karin, don’t be afraid. Mommy is coming by airplane.'

Asakawa Shin didn’t know he had gained an older sister who believed in him unconditionally ; he was just losing himself in the constant praise, burying his head in the journey.

Under his efforts, they entered the sphere of influence of Kusagakure in less than three hours.

Asakawa Shin also slowed down, easily avoiding the patrolling guards using Observation Haki to enter Kusagakure.

As for the Village Protection Barrier ? Because it was too crude, Asakawa Shin cracked it in a single second.

« We really got in. Lord Shin, you are simply too amazing. » Kana looked at Asakawa Shin with a face full of admiration.

Even though Asakawa Shin was beaming from the praise, he spoke with feigned restraint :

« This is nothing ! The Fuinjutsu scroll I gave you—if you practice diligently, you can easily do this too. »

Kana quickly nodded in agreement, but she laughed secretly in her heart : 'So Lord Shin is this type of person, quite tsundere.'

Under Kana’s guidance, Asakawa Shin quickly found the place where Karin was being held.

When they arrived, a Ninja was just walking out of the house.

There were also three Ninjas outside the door, seemingly waiting in line.

« Is it my turn ? I’ve been waiting for ages ! »

The speaker was a middle-aged Ninja in the queue. His arm had simple bandage marks with large bloodstains seeping through ; he seemed to have sustained some injuries.

The Anbu Ninja responsible for guarding Karin frowned and said : « Yours is just a common sword wound, barely skin-deep. Just go to the Medical Squad for some medicine and bandages, right ? »

The middle-aged Ninja was clearly dissatisfied and retorted loudly : « How much longer would that take to heal ? I’m in a hurry to go on a mission. »

« Stop your nagging. Why didn’t you say anything to those people before me ? You hypocritical piece of trash ! »

Due to the existence of Karin and her mother, the mission completion rate of Kusagakure had improved significantly.

The Ninjas performing missions were incredibly fierce in combat, not fearing injury at all ; therefore, there were many more wounded than before.

Because Kana had gone out on a secret mission, these wounded people turned their attention to Karin.

They collectively petitioned the leader, and the leader agreed without a second thought, just in time to test Karin’s healing effects.

Before the middle-aged Ninja, five or six people had already been treated, which led to the scene before them.

The rebuked Anbu Ninja remained silent. Hearing the cries coming from the house, he couldn’t help but think of his own daughter, which was why his heart had softened for a moment just now.

'Sigh ! Forget it, this is your fate. Who told you to have such a special constitution ?'

Seeing that the Anbu didn’t speak, the middle-aged Ninja snorted coldly and pushed the door open, in a hurry to heal his wounds.

Inside, the sobbing Karin was startled, her cries momentarily halting.

She shrank into a corner, looking at the newcomer with a face full of terror, her mouth puckering as she cried out again :

« Waaaah, Mama, where are you ? Karin is in so much pain and so scared... »

« Waaaah, I want Mama... »
