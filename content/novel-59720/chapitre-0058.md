# Chapitre 58 — Rivals for a Lifetime

'Hmm ? The power of this guy, Rock Lee, seems to have weakened !'

'It must be because of his previous injuries, making him unable to sustain the fight, right ?'

Uchiha Sasuke’s eyes flashed with a sharp light. As expected, his initial strategy of taking a defensive stance was correct.

After fighting for so long, Rock Lee was now running out of steam and could no longer bring the same powerful pressure as before.

Honestly, he never expected Rock Lee to be able to unleash such powerful strength, managing to hold on for so long despite his injuries.

But... Now it’s my turn !

Upon closer observation, Rock Lee’s breathing was indeed much more rapid than before...

His right leg was also trembling slightly, and during their exchanges, he was subconsciously avoiding impacts to his right arm, his movements already revealing a hint of strain.

Uchiha Sasuke’s eyelids twitched as he watched, and he couldn’t help but feel a sense of admiration :

'To be able to fight me for so long with these injuries without falling behind...

Rock Lee is truly talented in taijutsu, and his willpower is incredibly tenacious.

However...

I’m sorry, Rock Lee.

I have an opponent I must defeat, so I can only make you suffer for a little while...'

As these thoughts raced through his mind, Uchiha Sasuke decisively changed his strategy, switching from defense to offense, and actively launched a fierce attack on Rock Lee’s right side.

This sudden change caused the situation on the field to reverse instantly.

Rock Lee had injuries on both his right arm and right leg. Facing Sasuke’s swift and fierce offensive, he was clearly flustered and struggling to defend.

Soon, caught off guard, he made a fatal mistake...

Sasuke’s eyes lit up, and he seized the opportunity, flying up and kicking hard at Rock Lee’s injured right leg, causing him to instantly lose his balance and be knocked to the ground.

Sasuke looked at the fallen Rock Lee, seeing him clutching his right leg in agony. Even though he was the one who caused it, he couldn’t help but feel a twinge of reluctance in his heart.

Sasuke stepped forward and advised Rock Lee : « Rock Lee, that’s enough. If you continue, your body won’t be able to take it. How about we call this fight a draw ? »

A flash of struggle appeared in Rock Lee’s eyes, only to be immediately replaced by a firm gaze. He stood up and shook his head decisively :

« No, Sasuke. I promised you I would live up to your expectations, how can I give up halfway ? »

'Big brother, stop insisting ! You surrendering is my biggest expectation !' Sasuke complained inwardly.

Seeing that Rock Lee still refused to give up, he couldn’t help but feel a splitting headache. This guy was terrifyingly persistent.

If the fight continued, his own stamina reserves would likely hit the danger zone. How would he fight Naruto then...

Thinking about the possibility of losing to Naruto, under this intense stimulation, his little brain started running at an unprecedented speed...

Hum, humming~

Ding !

Suddenly, a flash of inspiration struck his mind. As if guided by divine insight, he turned to Might Guy and said solemnly :

« Referee, I choose to forfeit !

If this continues, Rock Lee’s injuries will worsen, and that is definitely not the result I want !

What I look forward to is a showdown with Rock Lee at his best, not watching him force himself through pain. If this fight continues, it will be meaningless !

Therefore, I request to forfeit ! Referee, please announce the result of the match ! »

Sasuke’s voice was clear and resonant, echoing across the arena, drowning out all other sounds...

The scene fell silent for a moment.

Might Guy, upon hearing this, felt deeply touched. His gaze fell on Uchiha Sasuke, as if he saw the young man glowing all over. That sense of responsibility and camaraderie moved him greatly.

'Who said the Uchiha were no good ? This Uchiha is simply too good ! It’s truly moving !

But it’s a pity for the young Uchiha to forfeit just like that, although that thick-browed boy’s body is indeed reaching its limit...

In that case...'

Just as he was about to speak, he heard Rock Lee shout suddenly :

« No ! Absolutely not !

I should be the one saying I forfeit !

Sasuke is only giving up the match because he cares about my body. The true winner of this fight should be him !

Referee, please judge me as the loser, I—I forfeit ! »

« Rock Lee... » Seeing Rock Lee take the initiative to forfeit, the stone hanging in Sasuke’s heart finally landed. He joyfully helped Rock Lee up, and the two agreed to have a showdown again after Rock Lee recovered.

Rock Lee also looked at him with burning eyes : « That’s right, Sasuke. Not just that one match, but also in the days to come.

From now on, we are rivals for a lifetime ! »

Sasuke didn’t realize the seriousness of the problem at all. He took Rock Lee’s hand and promised solemnly : « Alright, Rock Lee, let’s work hard together in the future ! »

Might Guy looked at this pair of mutually appreciative rivals with gratification, as if seeing himself and Kakashi.

He coughed lightly to compose himself and announced to the entire arena :

« Contestant Rock Lee forfeits, Uchiha Sasuke wins ! »

« Wow ! » The audience also applauded these two respectable contestants :

« They both forfeited at the same time ? This is the first time I’ve seen this situation. Honestly, I’m a bit moved, aren’t you ? »

« Is this what everyone calls friendship first, competition second ? Well done, they’ve truly displayed the will of fire of our Konoha ! »

« It’s really great. Rock Lee may have lost the match, but he has gained a lifelong friend and rival ! »

Naruto also rubbed his nose, somewhat unwillingly : « Sasuke is doing pretty well ! But I’m not any worse than him... »

Standing on the stage, Sasuke listened to the applause and discussion from below, feeling very proud internally :

'Big brother was right after all ! Sometimes words are more powerful than fists...

Then next...

There’s only one person left, Naruto !'

...

After Shin gave Rock Lee a thorough examination, he breathed a sigh of relief.

The injuries had indeed worsened slightly, but fortunately, the match was stopped in time, so there was no major harm. A few more days of rest would suffice.

After giving Rock Lee simple treatment, Shin continued to commentate on the match :

« This is truly an unexpected situation !

Two contestants actually chose to forfeit at the same time for the sake of the other. This spirit of putting friendship above competition is truly moving !

Let us offer our congratulations to Uchiha Sasuke. He has successfully advanced to the finals and is about to challenge for the position of team leader !

And the next match will be the ultimate showdown of the girls' group, which will determine the final owner of the team leader position.

Now, please welcome contestant No. 0, Ino Yamanaka, and contestant No. 5, Hyuga Hinata, to the stage. »

Ino and Hinata looked at each other deeply, and both stepped onto the arena without hesitation.

The difference was that Hinata still maintained that confident posture, her expression full of ease, without a trace of nervousness, as if she didn’t take Ino seriously at all.

Ino, on the other hand, was the exact opposite, her little face full of solemnity :

'Damn it, this glutton Hinata, how can I possibly defeat her ?

Urgent !'
