# Chapitre 202 — A Pat on the Head

The Nine-Tails' Tailed Beast Ball condensed with extreme speed ; now compressed to its limit, it seemed on the verge of erupting.

High in the air, a smirk suddenly tugged at the corner of Deidara’s mouth.

« I’m really being underestimated, aren’t I ? Let me show you my art as well. »

He mobilized the remaining Chakra in his body, frantically pouring it into the explosive clay in his hands.

« C3 : Number 18 ! »

With a roar, a doll-like clay bomb instantly took shape and was released as an air-drop.

The doll swelled rapidly upon leaving his hand, transforming into a colossal giant that plummeted straight down against the howling winds.

Below, the falling dark shadow was reflected in Six-Tailed Naruto’s vertical pupils, as he too seemed to sense the crisis.

He suddenly opened his massive maw, and the Tailed Beast Ball, which had long been gathering momentum, shot out, crashing directly toward the clay doll in the air.

« Tch, does he still retain some shred of reason ? That distance... it’s not at the center of the explosion ! What a pity. » Deidara curled his lip and formed a hand seal.

« Art is an explosion ! »

« Katsu ! »

With Deidara’s roar, the clay doll detonated violently, the destructive power it released proving to be on par with the Tailed Beast Ball.

The incoming Tailed Beast Ball was also instantly detonated, and the two terrifying explosions collided fiercely. In an instant, fire tore through the sky, and even the heavens and earth shook.

Immediately after, a violent shockwave rampaged outward, uprooting surrounding trees ; amidst the flying sand and stones, the land was left in utter ruin.

Gaara’s body bore the brunt of it, swept up by the rampaging winds and tumbling wretchedly through the air in a pathetic sight. Fortunately, the explosion occurred high up, otherwise, there might not have been a corpse left.

Deidara was prepared this time, flying high enough, and now he rode the gale, speeding away into the distance.

His condition had deteriorated to the extreme ; first, he had lost an arm while infiltrating the Hidden Sand Village to capture Gaara.

Then he participated in the extraction of the One-Tail, consuming a vast amount of Chakra.

Now, dragging his severed arm while engaged in a fierce battle with the Nine-Tails, he had reached his limit.

« I can’t go on, I must find Sasori-danna for support ! »

Deidara didn’t look back, heading straight for Sasori’s location, with a persistent tail still following closely behind.

Sasori was making similar calculations ; besieged by a group, he was finding it difficult to hold his ground, even forced to have the Fourth Raikage Human Puppet self-destruct.

Fortunately... he had still achieved some results.

The enemies now had no antidote left, making them inevitably hesitant and cautious in their movements.

Furthermore, the Third Kazekage’s Magnet Release countered puppet masters significantly, preventing Lady Chiyo from showing her true capabilities, leaving both sides in a temporary stalemate.

However, Team Guy was currently rushing to provide support, making time very unfavorable for Sasori.

At that moment, Deidara’s sudden appearance gave Sasori a boost. Was it finally his turn to help ?

Yet, witnessing the chaotic scene before him, Deidara couldn’t help but pause, his face full of confusion as he spoke, « Where is Sasori-danna ? He couldn’t have abandoned me and run away alone, could he ? »

As soon as these words were spoken, Sasori’s face instantly darkened, and he snapped irritably, « Shut up ! Am I not standing right here ? »

Deidara was shocked to hear this, staring in disbelief at the child-like puppet before him. « What ? You’re Sasori-danna ? Are you kidding me ? How could a wet-behind-the-ears brat possibly... »

Before he could finish, Naruto had already caught up like a shadow.

« Retreat quickly, Sasori-danna ! This guy’s Chakra is practically infinite, he’s tossing Tailed Beast Balls around like they’re free ! »

Scared out of his wits, Deidara didn’t say another word, controlling his clay bird to flee as fast as possible, leaving behind only a weak defense :

« It’s not that I can’t beat him, I’m just not in top form today ! You Nine-Tails brat, just you wait... »

In the blink of an eye, his figure vanished without a trace, leaving Sasori alone and bewildered in the wind.

Just as Sasori felt his future was bleak and thought he was about to meet his end, Naruto suddenly delivered a flying kick to Uchiha Sasuke, knocking him straight to the ground.

Sasuke clutched his lower back, his face contorted in pain, and roared, « Naruto, you bastard, how dare you sneak attack me ! »

Naruto said nothing, continuing his relentless assault.

For some reason, he felt that the guy in front of him was even more annoying than the one who could fly.

Seeing this, Sasori was overjoyed. He quickly controlled the Third Kazekage Human Puppet, using Iron Sand to lift himself into the air and hurriedly fled the scene.

The remaining people looked at each other and shook their heads one after another ; no one wanted to give chase.

Gaara was already dead ; pursuit was meaningless.

Thus, the battle ended in such an anticlimactic fashion.

Kakashi sighed, « We should find a way to seal Naruto first. »

Hearing this, the others felt a bit of a headache ; this guy was indeed a big trouble.

Just then, a figure abruptly appeared beside Naruto ; it was Asakawa Shin, who had rushed over.

He had been hesitating whether to disguise himself as Uchiha Madara to save Sasori, but he hadn’t expected the other party to escape on his own.

This actually made him breathe a secret sigh of relief. After all, he was an undercover high-ranking member of the Akatsuki ; both sides were his own people, and it would be tricky if either side suffered casualties.

Moreover, that guy Sasori still had some value to be utilized.

Asakawa Shin’s appearance startled everyone, but after seeing it was him, they all showed expressions of pleasant surprise.

The rampaging Naruto recognized no one, opening his mouth to let out a beast-like roar, but Asakawa Shin gave him a direct pat on the head.

« Kakuan Entering Society with Bliss-Bringing Hands ! »

Naruto felt as if he had been struck by lightning and instantly quieted down. The violent, boiling Nine-Tails Chakra on his body also subsided and vanished.

He only had time to weakly utter « Shin... » before his eyes rolled back and he fainted.

Not long after, Guy arrived in a flurry with Neji, Lee, and Tenten. Upon seeing Asakawa Shin, there was naturally a round of excited greetings.

After a long time, the crowd suddenly remembered Gaara.

When they hurriedly searched for him, Lady Chiyo was already standing there.

A trace of sorrow flickered in her eyes, but it was immediately replaced by determination. Then, under the astonished gaze of the crowd, she resolutely performed the « One’s Own Life Resurrection » technique, pulling Gaara back from the brink of death.

After the technique was completed, Lady Chiyo was completely exhausted, her breath as weak as a thread. Beside her, Sakura was already sobbing uncontrollably.

« Sakura, don’t cry. This was my own choice. Anyway, these old bones of mine don’t have much time left. It’s better to save Gaara’s life ; it’s a worthy death ! »

« Grandmother, please don’t say that, you’ll definitely be fine... » Sakura choked out an attempt at comfort.

However, Lady Chiyo just gently shook her head ; she knew deep down that this was the price of performing the forbidden jutsu.

She tremblingly took a scroll from her robes and handed it to Sakura, her voice weak but solemn : « This is the culmination of my life’s medical knowledge. I hope you will put it to good use. »

After everything was settled, she closed her eyes with a smile on her face and never woke up again.

A few days later, Gaara and Naruto also woke up one after another.

When Naruto saw that both Asakawa Shin and Gaara were alive and well, a look of wild joy instantly blossomed on his face.

However, when his gaze fell upon Gaara, who was wrapped in layers of bandages like a mummy, Naruto’s expression suddenly turned stern, and his brows furrowed tightly.

« Gaara, they actually injured you like this ? This is truly unforgivable ! »

Naruto gritted his teeth, a raging fire burning in his eyes. « Don’t worry, I will never let those guys get away with this ! »
