# Chapitre 36 — Kakuzu Doubting Life

Facing Kakuzu’s sneak attack, Asakawa Shin had already anticipated it.

'Are you kidding me ? You think my Observation Haki that predicts the future is fake ? Trying to sneak attack me ? Do you even know how to sneak attack properly ? Only I get to do that.'

« Flying Finger Pistol : Hundred Birds Facing the Phoenix ! »

Asakawa Shin’s hands almost blurred into afterimages, rubbing against the air to unleash countless waves of flame, and because they were fired from his fingers, the waves looked like birds spreading their wings and rushing forward at high speed, hence the name Hundred Birds Facing the Phoenix.

Although this move lacked the powerful piercing force and extreme Fire Release nature transformation of his Yang Flower Thrust, its attack range was much larger, with countless flying birds rushing toward the Fire Wind Turbulence one after another.

Both were products of Wind Release and Fire Release, and the moment they collided, high-temperature heat waves surged and spread in all directions.

« Damn it, the shockwave is coming, set up a defense quickly ! »

« Why does it feel like we are always casting defensive Ninjutsu ? »

« Stop complaining, if you don’t want to die, hurry up ! Earth Release : Earth Wall. »

« Got it, Water Release : Water Wall. »

Seeing Shikaku and the others hiding behind their crooked defensive formation, Asakawa Shin relaxed slightly ; knowing to run when the shockwave came was enough.

'Since my teammates are safe for now, I’ll play with you properly, Senior Kakuzu.'

The collision between the Fire Wind Turbulence and the Hundred Birds Facing the Phoenix didn’t take long to resolve ; the Fire Wind Turbulence clearly lacked follow-up power, but the fire birds continued to spread their wings and charge forward, finally breaking through the flame curtain formed by the Fire Wind Turbulence after holding on for a moment, and dozens of birds maintained their basic form as they bombed toward Kakuzu !

« What ? This instant-cast taijutsu has such power ? » Kakuzu was full of disbelief, this wasn’t a small scuffle with Shikaku, this combination Ninjutsu was Kakuzu’s all-out attack, and the last time it was broken was when he first met the leader Pain, crushed by the terrible repulsive force of the Rinnegan.

Returning to reality, the attack of the fire birds had already arrived, fortunately, it had been dissipated a lot by the Fire Wind Turbulence, and both its power and speed were reduced, so Kakuzu dodged flexibly, and as the birds hit the ground, dozens of large craters were smashed into the scene.

« Not bad, old man. » Asakawa Shin walked over slowly, with the composed posture of a powerhouse.

« Since you have fought the First Hokage, you must recognize this necklace, right ? It was passed down from the First Hokage to Tsunade, and then from Tsunade to me. »

« I once stood before this necklace and vowed to protect Konoha Village, to protect the companions in the Village, but today, they were beaten quite badly by you ! »

Speaking of this, Asakawa Shin was also a bit unhappy ; if he had arrived a step later, such an excellent workhorse like Uncle Shikaku would have been beaten to death. This wasn’t just slapping Shikaku in the face ; it was clearly slapping him on the butt, which made him very angry.

As Asakawa Shin’s emotions fluctuated, wisps of black and red lightning slowly appeared and wrapped around his body, and a strong sense of oppression instantly crushed toward Kakuzu.

« Old man, you are a Ninja who has fought the First Hokage and managed to escape unscathed, so it should be fine if I get a little serious, right ? »

« You guy... » Kakuzu looked at Asakawa Shin with full vigilance ; this sense of oppression reminded him of the leader’s Rinnegan, equally incomprehensible, and equally making one feel unmatched.

'No, I can’t sit and wait for death.'

Kakuzu decided to strike first after a short moment of thought, to test for more information and find a chance to win in adversity.

« Take this, Wind Release : Pressure Damage. »

The white mask on his shoulder opened its mouth wide, spitting out a vortex-like high-pressure storm that swept toward Asakawa Shin. Facing this attack, Asakawa Shin just jumped into the air and easily avoided it.

« Hahaha, the brat is still too young, no wonder you don’t even have a bounty. There is no leverage in the air, how will you dodge the next attack ? »

Kakuzu was overjoyed to see Asakawa Shin jump into the air, and he quickly formed hand seals, wanting to take advantage of this god-given opportunity to hit Asakawa Shin hard.

« Let the old senior teach you a lesson, brat ! »

« Fire Release : Searing Migraine ! »

A massive attack like a sea of fire instantly covered Asakawa Shin, even shrouding his landing point, so no matter where Asakawa Shin jumped to, he would be hit by the Fire Release, but...

« Strange, old man, do you actually not know the information about the Konoha Six Styles ? It’s widely circulated in Konoha, you know. Don’t just look at the bounty, pay more attention to the younger generation of the Ninja World. »

« Moon Walk : Ascent. »

Asakawa Shin stepped on the air repeatedly, his whole figure rising another hundred meters, easily avoiding the attack.

Kakuzu looked at Asakawa Shin with a numb face ; that avoided it ? You make me look stupid.

The enemies he had encountered before dealt with his Ninjutsu either by using defensive Ninjutsu, attacking him back, or the smarter ones would use the terrain and environment to outwit him.

But today, the style of facing Asakawa Shin was different. Facing that large-scale Wind Release Ninjutsu, he just did a big jump and avoided it by over a hundred meters.

And facing the Fire Release that locked onto all his dodging ranges and landing points in the air, he could actually do another big jump in the air to easily avoid it.

Is this right ? This is not right.

'This brat can actually fly, this is troublesome.' Kakuzu already had a very bad premonition in his heart. Fortunately, he had let the Lightning Release heart that hadn’t been exposed withdraw earlier, so even if all four hearts were destroyed now, there would be no life-threatening danger.

But the storage scroll containing the huge amount of funds was still here. If he was defeated... Kakuzu felt his heart twitch with Pain at the thought.

Just then, Asakawa Shin’s voice came from high above.

« Hey, old man, courtesy demands reciprocity. You should try this move of mine too. »

Asakawa Shin posed as if he was about to kick a football with great force, his body stretching to the fullest in the air like a spring, driving his right toe to draw a crescent moon.

« Tempest Kick : Flame Moon ! »

In an instant, a huge slashing wave hundreds of meters long flew toward the valley below. The slashing wave was crescent-shaped, with red-gold flames attached to it, and one could feel the high temperature that made one’s mouth dry before it even approached.

Shikaku widened his eyes below : « Is this kid getting more and more exaggerated ? The power of this move is too astonishing. »

Someone next to him echoed : « The power is definitely unquestionable, whoever touches it dies, but how does this direction feel a bit off ? Is it coming for us ? »

« Damn it, it seems like it really is ! »

« What should we do ? Lord Shikaku, should we still set up defensive Ninjutsu ? »

Shikaku took a deep breath and said calmly to everyone : « Everyone, don’t panic. There is no need to set up defensive Ninjutsu. We can’t block it anyway, just wait for death. »

« Haha, I’m joking. If I’m not mistaken, the target is not us. Stay in position and prepare to meet the impact. »

Indeed, as Shikaku said, the Flame Moon soon passed over their heads and smashed fiercely toward the spot hundreds of meters away, accurately finding the head of Kakuzu’s Water Release heart monster hiding in the stagnant water.
