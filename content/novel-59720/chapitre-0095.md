# Chapitre 95 — The Two-Tails Falls

« Pervert... me ?

Damn it, you dare insult a cat lover ! Even if you’re a cat, that’s not okay ! I’m really angry now. »

Asakawa Shin’s brow furrowed deeply. What was wrong with just wanting to huff a cat ? Kittens were born to be huffed by people.

This is no longer ordinary slander ; I must strike hard.

The Two-Tails turned a deaf ear to this, focusing all her attention on mobilizing and compressing the Chakra within her body. In an instant, a high-density Chakra ball was condensed into shape by her.

She narrowed her eyes slightly, Asakawa Shin’s lightning-fast speed from earlier flashing through her mind. Ordinary attack methods would likely be hard to hit, so...

Thinking this, she actually swallowed that Tailed Beast Ball into her stomach without hesitation.

The entire cat’s form began to twist and change violently, its belly swelling and bulging at an incredible speed. Even its neck disappeared, turning it into a round, giant blue cat.

Immediately after, she quickly adjusted her position, aimed her massive head at Asakawa Shin, and suddenly opened her mouth wide.

A surging torrent of Chakra gushed out like a pressurized shell, forcibly increasing the Tailed Beast Ball’s launch speed in this extreme manner.

Asakawa Shin had already foreseen this scene with Observation Haki, so he wasn’t surprised. He merely responded by quickly weaving hand signs :

'Sage Art : Water Release : Water Formation Wall !'

The surrounding water was instantly drained. Under the terrifying enhancement of Senjutsu, the water rapidly reshaped and reorganized, turning into a massive water wall hundreds of meters high, firmly blocking the Tailed Beast Ball’s path.

The Tailed Beast Ball arrived in an instant, slamming hard into the water wall and exploding.

In an instant, a terrifying shockwave swept in all directions, causing the Water Formation Wall to vibrate violently as if it might collapse at any moment.

Asakawa Shin’s eyes sharpened, and he quickly increased the output of Senjutsu Chakra while forming the Tiger Seal to make fine adjustments to the shape of the Water Formation Wall ahead.

Soon, the thick water wall was reshaped into something resembling the front of a high-speed train.

The effect of this change was immediate ; the originally violent shockwave was diverted to the sides along the smooth curved surface, and the pressure on the Water Formation Wall was greatly reduced.

Although the main body of the Water Formation Wall continued to evaporate and melt due to the high temperature, gradually shrinking, it ultimately managed to withstand the terrifying blow.

« How is this possible ? Even with the help of the watery environment, a mere Water Formation Wall... how could it block my Tailed Beast Ball ? »

The Two-Tails' pupils shrank sharply, her voice filled with disbelief as if her worldview had collapsed at that moment.

Asakawa Shin naturally couldn’t be bothered to explain to this bad kitty. Before his words even faded, he had already charged forward like an arrow released from a bow.

Seeing this, the Two-Tails quickly composed herself and spat out a ball of blazing fire, attempting to block Asakawa Shin’s offensive.

However, such an attack was far too slow in his eyes. With a slight flicker of his body, Asakawa Shin easily dodged it and continued to close in without slowing down.

A sharp glint flashed in the Two-Tails' eyes : 'A mere human body dares to engage me in close-quarters combat ? I’ll tear you apart !'

She lunged forward, raising a cat claw to strike fiercely at the top of Asakawa Shin’s head. Unexpectedly, Asakawa Shin accelerated and performed a smooth sliding tackle, easily evading the fatal blow.

That wasn’t all. Using his momentum, he slid across the water’s surface at high speed, instantly approaching the Two-Tails' vital abdominal area.

Asakawa Shin’s muscles suddenly tightened like a spring compressed to its limit. He abruptly exerted force and leaped into the air, turning his head into the hardest battering ram and slamming into the Two-Tails' soft belly with thunderous momentum.

« Bang ! »

The giant cat instantly doubled over in pain like a cooked shrimp, coughing up a mouthful of blood as the entire Tailed Beast flew backward uncontrollably.

'Haha, I didn’t expect Hinata’s move to have such a great effect in actual combat ?'

Seeing the outstanding effect of the attack, Asakawa Shin felt inwardly pleased, his lips curling into a smug arc. By applying what he had learned, he easily handled this arrogant big cat.

On the other side, the Two-Tails crashed miserably onto the water’s surface, kicking up a massive splash.

The intense pain made it impossible for her to get up for a while. She could only float helplessly in the water, bobbing with the waves, each breath accompanied by the smell of blood, looking exceptionally pathetic.

Looking at this, Asakawa Shin couldn’t help but show a hint of wariness. The Eight-Tails had tried to trick him like this earlier ; could this Two-Tails be using the same trick ? Did these Tailed Beasts take him for a fool ?

Thinking of this, he quickly wove hand signs. Regardless of whether she was faking it, he would give her a beating first :

« Cats are naturally afraid of water, and you’re fire-attribute, so aren’t you four times weak to water ? Jie jie jie !

Take this ! Water Release : Water Dragon Bullet Technique !

Gyarados, use Tackle ! »

Soon, a hundred-meter-long water dragon quickly condensed into shape, rushing toward the now-immobile Two-Tails with a momentum that could topple mountains.

Hearing the noise, the Two-Tails endured the intense pain and struggled to lift her head, only to see the terrifying water dragon already within reach. For a moment, she was scared out of her wits and cursed vehemently in her heart.

However, she was powerless to resist now and could only watch as she was struck hard by the violent water dragon, then swept into the surging current, torn and washed away as she sank to the bottom.

« Oh ? So she wasn’t faking it ? »

Asakawa Shin watched this, slightly surprised : « She’s too fragile ; she’s far behind compared to the Eight-Tails.

Forget it, forget it. Since you’re in this state, I’ll be magnanimous and forgive you. »

After all, he wasn’t a petty person. Since he had already taught her a lesson, he naturally wouldn’t pursue it further.

He wove hand signs with one hand, precisely controlling the water. Soon, Yugito, her eyes rolled back, slowly floated to the surface.

Asakawa Shin leaned in to sniff and nodded in satisfaction : « Nice, it’s this fragrance. Then I won’t be polite ! »

Before his words finished, he had already pressed his palm against her lower abdomen and began to absorb the Two-Tails' Chakra.

As soon as this pure energy surged into his body, it triggered a violent reaction. Every cell in his body seemed to come alive, scrambling to devour it frantically.

After a long time, Asakawa Shin couldn’t help but let out a loud burp, and he said in surprise :

« Hmm... this feeling, swallowing the Chakra of two Tailed Beasts in one go, I’m actually a bit stuffed ? But this feeling isn’t bad. »

Asakawa Shin casually slung Yugito over his shoulder and headed straight toward Killer Bee’s direction.

On the way, he habitually twitched his nose. That enticing fragrance had already dissipated ; it seemed the extraction of the Two-Tails' Chakra was complete.

Tossing the unconscious Yugito carelessly onto the ground and looking at these two Jinchuriki before him, Asakawa Shin couldn’t help but feel troubled :

« Seriously, what should I do with you two ? Should I kill you ? Hmm... that seems like a bit of a waste... »

Pondering this, he took out several syringes from a Sealing Scroll, first drawing a few test tubes of blood samples from each of them and carefully sealing them.

Then he pressed his hands over their hearts and operated with his eyes closed. An hour later, he finally let out a breath. Upon careful sensing, he indeed detected those unique spatial fluctuations within their bodies.

Indeed, just now, with the help of his superb Medical Ninjutsu, he had etched the Chakra marks of the Flying Thunder God Technique into their hearts.

After these days of dedicated research, he had already completed the prerequisite tasks for the Flying Thunder God Technique and could now etch his own Flying Thunder God Mark.

However, etching a Flying Thunder God Mark on human internal organs was a delicate operation he was doing for the first time, which was why it took so much time.

Asakawa Shin nodded slightly in satisfaction. The mission for this trip had been perfectly accomplished ; he only needed to do one more thing before he could return.

His hands flew as he quickly completed the seals : « Earth Release : Open Earth Rising Excavation ! »

The ground beneath his feet deformed with a roar, and a massive conical earthen mountain rose from the earth, spewing out a stealthy figure like a volcanic eruption—it was a White Zetsu.

Asakawa Shin’s eye twitched imperceptibly : 'This guy is probably responsible for scouting Kumogakure’s movements, right ? Running into me, consider yourself unlucky.'

He didn’t hesitate, quickly taking out a blank scroll and sealing the White Zetsu on the spot while it was still in a daze.

After repeatedly considering that no flaws were left behind, Asakawa Shin finally took a step and turned to start his journey back.

« Time to slip away... »
