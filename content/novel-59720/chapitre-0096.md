# Chapitre 96 — Be More Careful in Your Next Life

With Asakawa Shin traveling at full speed, it took him only a little over a day to return to the Land of Frost to reunite with Tsunade, arriving even a step ahead of the Kumogakure reinforcements.

After skillfully swapping places with his Shadow Clone, the clone headed out to find a secluded spot to dispel itself.

Asakawa Shin naturally walked up to Tsunade, but she looked him over strangely, asking in confusion, « Hmm ? Why do you feel a bit different ? Is it just my imagination ? »

Asakawa Shin laughed it off and waved his hand. « How could I be different ? Senior Sister, you must be joking. »

Tsunade stared at him suspiciously, then her eyes suddenly lit up as she pointed at the top of Asakawa Shin’s head. « You definitely are different ! Look, the seedling on your head has sprouted two new leaves, and the whole thing seems to have grown a bit larger. »

Asakawa Shin was shocked. He quickly asked Shizune for a small mirror to take a closer look. « Hiss... it really has grown a bit.

What is this thing going to turn into in the end ? A tree isn’t actually going to grow out of my head, is it ? That would be way too ugly... »

Tsunade couldn’t help but laugh. « Why is your constitution so strange ? I remember Grandfather wasn’t like this back then. You haven’t mutated, have you ? Hahaha ! »

Asakawa Shin complained with a troubled look, « Oh, I’m already annoyed enough as it is, and here you are making fun of me, Senior Sister. »

Unexpectedly, Tsunade naturally reached out her arm, pulled him into her embrace, and comforted him softly, « Alright, alright, it’s my fault. Can’t I just apologize to you ? »

Asakawa Shin was internally stunned : 'What’s going on ? It’s only been a few days, so why is Senior Sister being so affectionate ? Did the Shadow Clone do something behind my back again ?'

Soon, as a rush of new memories flooded his mind, he suddenly understood.

The Shadow Clone’s relationship with Tsunade had actually progressed by leaps and bounds over the past few days, and they had even made a promise to become lovers once he grew up. No wonder...

But this didn’t seem too bad, did it ? In the future, he could have several children with Tsunade to rebuild the Senju Clan, which would fulfill his promise to the Senju.

You actually did something good for once, Shadow Clone. It makes my exhausting business trip to Kumogakure these past few days worth it.

Asakawa Shin suddenly remembered something and asked curiously, « By the way, Senior Sister, has the Raikage paid the reward yet ? »

After all, he had just turned the Hidden Cloud Village upside down ; he hoped it wouldn’t affect his payment.

Upon hearing this, Tsunade’s face lit up with joy, and she said with a beaming smile :

« The money has been paid in full. As for the thunder-clap grass, they said they would organize personnel to harvest it as soon as they return.

However, the Hidden Cloud Village seems to have run into some trouble and won’t allow us to enter for the time being. They said they would send it directly using the 'Heavenly Transfer Technique'.

Whatever, it saves us the trouble anyway. It just means waiting here for a few more days. »

Asakawa Shin nodded in satisfaction. As long as there were no medical disputes, it was fine ; otherwise, he wouldn’t mind personally demonstrating what violent debt collection looked like.

« Huh ? »

« What’s wrong ? Did you find something ? » Tsunade asked curiously.

« Oh, it’s nothing. Five Ninjas have appeared outside the door. They’re likely the legendary Shinobi Hunters, the Xuanyuan Group. Senior Sister, wait a moment while I go out and deal with them. »

Having said that, Asakawa Shin was about to pull away from Tsunade’s embrace, but she held onto him tightly.

A cunning light flashed in Tsunade’s eyes as she chuckled, « Why are you in such a hurry to kill them ? I’m hoping they’ll cause a bit more trouble for the Hidden Cloud Village.

When the time comes... hehehe. »

Asakawa Shin gave her a look and said crossly, « What does that little bit of money matter ? You should know they are also Insect Users. The Kamizuru Clan of Iwagakure is already a headache.

Recently, they’ve been heavily favored by that old fossil Ōnoki to mass-produce imitations of our silk. Although their output is low and the quality is far inferior to ours, they have indeed made a significant impact on our downmarket segment.

Iwagakure is already enough of a problem. If Kumogakure also gains the secret insect arts of the Xuanyuan Group, I’m afraid we’ll have to fight a Silk War.

The Fourth Ninja World War might even break out because of it, so I have to take them out now before the main Kumogakure force arrives. »

Tsunade scratched her head in disbelief. « The Fourth Ninja World War ? Just because of silk ? Isn’t that too exaggerated ? »

« It can’t be helped. So-called business wars are often just that unadorned and boring. »

Asakawa Shin sighed, his tone tinged with helplessness. « Wealth moves the heart. We’re talking about tens of billions in annual profits, comparable to the total tax revenue of a small nation.

And that’s not even the limit ; that number is still growing rapidly.

Enough talk, I’m going to finish them off. Consider it their bad luck ; of all things to learn, they just had to learn insect techniques. »

As soon as he finished speaking, Asakawa Shin broke free from Tsunade’s charming embrace and strode away.

Meanwhile, outside, the Xuanyuan Group had not yet realized the gravity of the situation and were still discussing whether to abandon the mission.

Asakawa Shin walked straight toward their hidden position with a calm, unconcealed pace, instantly putting them on alert.

« Hmm ? Have we been discovered ? »

« No matter, it’s just a wet-behind-the-ears brat. Make it quick. Kill him and retreat immediately. »

Before the words even faded, a boom erupted. Accompanied by a blinding light, a massive swarm of Explosive Bugs swept toward Asakawa Shin, carried by the breeze from the explosion.

Asakawa Shin remained indifferent. Black lightning suddenly erupted around him as all the approaching Explosive Bugs were instantly crushed to death by his Haki. His hands blurred as he rapidly formed seals : « Fire Release : Phoenix Sage Fire Technique ! »

'Got him !'

The Xuanyuan Group had just finished that thought when they saw that Asakawa Shin was completely unaffected after performing the Ninjutsu. He spat out countless flames that looked like fluttering petals, clearing away the mass of Explosive Bug corpses while rapidly flying toward them.

« Nani ? How can this be ? »

They were greatly shocked and scrambled to set up a defense, barely managing to block the strike.

« Oh, were you wondering why your Explosive Bugs failed ? »

« It’s actually quite simple. The moment they got close to me, they were completely strangled by my technique.

If they’re just a pile of dead bugs, then what’s the difference between them and dust ? To be honest, they couldn’t even get past my nose hairs... »

Asakawa Shin continued to walk forward unhurriedly, his expression indifferent as if the people before him were mere ants. He even had the leisure to explain things to them.

In contrast, the Xuanyuan Group was now in complete disarray. Having their strongest survival method easily dismantled filled them with dread.

One of them spoke with a trembling voice, hesitantly suggesting, « Maybe... we should retreat. This mission is impossible to complete. »

Hearing this, the others looked at each other, seeing the desire to retreat in everyone’s eyes, and they all nodded in agreement.

However, just as they were about to act, Asakawa Shin’s slow voice, laced with bone-chilling cold, suddenly rang out :

« Want to leave ? Who gave you permission to leave ? Today, not a single one of you is leaving here alive.

Flying Finger Pistol : Five Consecutive ! »

In an instant, Asakawa Shin’s fingers tapped rapidly, his fingertips covered in ink-black Busoshoku Haki. At a speed that tore through the air, he fired five invisible air cannons.

The distance was too short and the speed too fast ; the Xuanyuan Group had no time to react before their chests were accurately struck by the lethal Flying Finger Pistols.

The violent impact instantly pierced through their bodies. Blood and shattered internal organs sprayed out behind them like exploding fireworks, cruelly and beautifully blooming into five crimson flowers.

« Wh... why... »

One of them, who had managed to dodge just enough to avoid his heart being hit, was now gasping for breath, yet he still spat out broken words : « We clearly... already decided to give up on the mission... »

A cold, mocking sneer curled onto Asakawa Shin’s lips, his tone devoid of any mercy. « Since you are Ninjas known as Shinobi Hunters, how can you still ask such naive questions ?

Besides, who told you to get in my way.

Be more careful in your next life. Don’t play with bugs ; you can’t handle them. »

« ? »
