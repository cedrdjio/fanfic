# Chapitre 68 — "Lord Shin, I understand. In short, you want to go to Shikkotsu Forest to train in Sage Arts, right?"

« Um, Katsuyu, just call me Shin. You don’t need to be so polite. »

« I understand, Lord Shin. »

« ... »

The little Katsuyu crawled onto Asakawa Shin’s shoulder, and upon sensing the vast, ocean-like amount of Chakra within that small body, she couldn’t help but be surprised :

« Lord Shin, your Chakra reserves are indeed sufficient to qualify for Sage Arts training, but there is one thing I must declare in advance !

The Sage Arts of Shikkotsu Forest are actually my own method of cultivation ; in essence, they are not suited for human bodies.

Even after constant improvements by generations of successors, there is still a risk of being eroded by Natural Energy and turning to stone during the training process !

Even so, do you still choose to train ? »

Asakawa Shin nodded solemnly. He knew all of this, of course, but where can one obtain power without risk ? Besides, Sage Arts were so important, so why should he hesitate ?

Seeing that Asakawa Shin had made up his mind, the little Katsuyu did not try to persuade him further : « In that case, I will take my leave.

In five minutes, I will perform the Reverse Summoning Jutsu and summon you to Shikkotsu Forest.

Then, Lord Shin, see you later. »

« Poof ! »

Asakawa Shin saw the worried look in Hiruzen Sarutobi’s eyes and returned it with a reassuring gaze : « Teacher, don’t worry. I will be extra careful when training in Sage Arts.

I don’t know how long this trip will take, so I will leave a Shadow Clone to go to school for me.

If there is anything urgent, you can have him notify me. »

Five minutes later, under the resentful gaze of the Shadow Clone, the original Asakawa Shin responded to the summons and vanished into a puff of smoke.

...

Shikkotsu Forest.

Asakawa Shin observed the surrounding environment, dumbstruck.

It was truly as the name suggested. What met his eyes were towering, pale white stone pillars that pierced straight into the ground like grim, bleached bones.

The surrounding environment was dark and damp, and acidic mist floated in the air, permeating a pungent odor.

The swamp was covered in a thick layer of humus, and countless slugs of all sizes were crawling slowly within it.

Seeing such harsh conditions, Asakawa Shin couldn’t help but curse inwardly ; this was truly a hellhole not even a dog would live in.

That idiot Shadow Clone must still think I’m here to enjoy myself, right ?

« Lord Shin, my humble abode is crude, and I apologize for the poor hospitality. Please forgive me.

Please follow me ; the main body wishes to see you. » A slug the size of a house stopped in front of him and carried him toward the depths of Shikkotsu Forest.

They hadn’t gone far before they could see the main body of Sage Katsuyu, because it was simply too large—from a distance, it looked like a small mountain.

Asakawa Shin roughly estimated that her body length was at least over a thousand meters ; this size was already comparable to the Ten-Tails.

She was worthy of being the most unique Sage of the three great sanctuaries, and with such a size, her strength was undoubtedly unfathomable.

« So big ! Katsuyu, your main body is just too huge ! That’s amazing !

But even being this big, you’re still so cute—ugly-cute, in a way~ »

Asakawa Shin was thrilled and did not skimp on his praise, completely failing to notice that the little slug beneath his feet stuttered for a moment, as if dropping frames.

In the distance, the main body of Sage Katsuyu, lying on the ground, also slowly raised her head and cast her gaze over :

'He’s probably complimenting me, right ? He must be... Tsk, suddenly I really want to spit a mouthful of acid and kill him. Never mind, never mind, children speak without thinking ; why should I bicker with a kid.'

A long while later, the little slug finally carried Asakawa Shin to the front of Sage Katsuyu’s main body ; viewing this massive size up close was incredibly overwhelming.

Sage Katsuyu had already adjusted her mindset by now and greeted Asakawa Shin gently :

« Little Shin, welcome to Shikkotsu Forest. I am very happy ; it has been a long time since we had any guests here, even though I quite like liveliness.

I remember the last one to come was little Tsunade, but that was over a decade ago, and she only came once and never returned. »

Asakawa Shin couldn’t help but scratch his head. It’s useless to like liveliness ; people without some special skills shouldn’t come to this hellhole.

« Sss ! »

« Hm ? What was that sound ? »

Asakawa Shin sensed something amiss and quickly looked down :

'Oh, so my skin has been corroded to the point of smoking, huh ? Tsk, it’s a good thing my self-healing ability is off the charts ; anyone else would be crying for their parents by now.'

But to be fair, the concentration of Natural Energy here was indeed extraordinary, at least dozens of times that of Konoha.

Especially here in the depths of Shikkotsu Forest, where the main body of Sage Katsuyu was located, the concentration of Natural Energy was over a hundred times that of the outside world.

It was worthy of being one of the three great sanctuaries. He wondered what the other two sanctuaries were like ; he could go see them if he had the chance in the future.

But for now...

« Sister Katsuyu, it’s not that they don’t want to come visit, but that the environment of Shikkotsu Forest is too unique ; ordinary living beings simply can’t get in. »

Hearing Asakawa Shin say this, Katsuyu became visibly dejected.

Shikkotsu Forest was a sanctuary, certainly, but she was the only living being that could adapt to the environment here ; how was this any different from being in prison ?

Yet, she required the Natural Energy here and could not leave for long periods.

Fortunately, she later came up with a method : splitting herself into countless individuals.

Although they shared a single consciousness, she could play role-playing games, which gave her life a little bit of flavor.

Seeing Sage Katsuyu so dejected, Asakawa Shin couldn’t help but feel a bit sympathetic. His eyes darted around, and he thought of a good idea :

« Sister Katsuyu, I have an idea ! Others might not be able to get in, but you can go out.

After I finish training in Sage Arts, when I go back, I will summon your clone and keep it with me. You can project your consciousness over then, and you can play outside all the time.

As for the Chakra supply, that’s not a problem at all. You can just use my Chakra to maintain your existence ; that little consumption is just a drop in the ocean, so don’t worry. »

« What ? You can do that ? »

Sage Katsuyu was deeply shocked. In the thousands of years, among so many successors of Shikkotsu Forest, no one had ever proposed this suggestion ; they had only treated her as a Summoning Beast to be driven.

Even those who were respectful to her had never truly cared about her feelings.

Thinking of this, Katsuyu suddenly felt a sense of being moved, and looking at this little child in front of her, she felt he was exceptionally pleasing to the eye.

« Why wouldn’t it work ? Because I was thinking, if it were me, and I had to stay in one place alone for a thousand years, I would definitely be driven crazy, right ?

But it doesn’t matter ; I’m here today, Sister Katsuyu. This is a fated encounter.

Since you like liveliness so much, let’s turn the Ninja World upside down together ! »

Under the perception of Asakawa Shin’s Observation Haki, which listens to all things, the big guy in front of him wasn’t as gentle and harmless as she appeared ; instead, she was filled with a kind of hysterical madness.

Although she was forcibly suppressing it herself, it was clearly captured by Asakawa Shin’s Observation Haki.

He looked at Katsuyu’s massive main body and suddenly thought of a wicked idea, then began to speak excitedly to Sage Katsuyu :

« Not only that, Sister Katsuyu. When I grow up a bit more and my Chakra is sufficient, maybe I can summon your entire main body.

When we encounter strong enemies then, we can fight side by side. »

« Strong enemies ? Little... Brother Shin, with my strength, what kind of enemy could be called a strong enemy ? The Nine-Tailed ? »

Sage Katsuyu’s large eyes were filled with great confusion.

Don’t be fooled by her appearance ; she was a terrifying existence known as one of the Three Great Sages, alongside the White Snake Sage of Ryūchi Cave and the Great Toad Sage of Mount Myōboku.

There were very few in the Ninja World who could make her look at them squarely.

« Hmm... Sister Katsuyu, what do you think of Manda ? »
