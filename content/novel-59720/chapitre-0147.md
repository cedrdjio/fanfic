# Chapitre 147 — Neji and the Stretcher

The seventh match : Uzumaki Naruto versus Inuzuka Kiba.

Naruto looked at Kiba, who was eager to start, and said with a look of disdain, « Kiba, you dead last, you might as well just surrender. After all, you’ve never beaten me in all these years. »

Kiba’s face flushed red, and a flash of annoyance momentarily crossed his eyes, but he quickly adjusted his emotions, a cold smile curling at the corner of his mouth.

« Naruto, don’t look down on people. In this match, I’ll have Akamaru fighting by my side. In this competition, using a dog doesn’t count as breaking the rules. »

Hearing this, Naruto couldn’t help but laugh heartily. « Using a dog ? I’m not an idiot like Sasuke who would let your dog get a successful sneak attack on me ! »

Kiba and Akamaru exchanged a look, their expressions turning solemn. Kiba gave a low shout : « Man-Beast Ninja Art : Beast Human Clone ! »

Accompanied by a puff of smoke, Akamaru, who had jumped onto his back, actually transformed into Kiba’s likeness. Two identical Inuzuka Kibas suddenly appeared on the field.

Both Kibas crouched on the ground at the same time, their eyes turning into beast-like vertical slits. They locked onto Naruto’s position, adopting a highly oppressive hunting stance.

However, Naruto showed no signs of panic upon seeing this ; he even sneered. « I see, so this is your trump card ? It’s true that adding a clone is equivalent to doubling your combat power... »

« But the number of clones is still too few ! Let me show you... »

Before he could finish his sentence, he formed hand seals and shouted coldly, « Ninja Art : Multiple Shadow Clone Jutsu ! »

After a burst of smoke, dozens of Naruto’s Shadow Clones appeared on the scene, surrounding Kiba and Akamaru like a rising tide.

Kiba was greatly shocked. 'This guy’s Chakra capacity is truly exaggerated ; he can actually produce so many Shadow Clones.'

« Akamaru, the moment to prove ourselves has arrived. Let’s go ! Use that move ! »

« Fang Over Fang ! »

He and Akamaru transformed into two high-speed rotating whirlwinds, roaring as they charged toward the surrounding Shadow Clones.

The whirlwinds carried a sharp impact force ; any Shadow Clone they touched instantly burst into smoke.

In an instant, several Shadow Clones had been completely defeated.

Seeing this, Naruto’s original body frowned slightly, then raised his arm and shouted, « Hmph ! Just a futile struggle. Get them ! »

The Shadow Clones exchanged glances and then surged forward like a tide.

For a time, the sound of fists and feet colliding crackled through the arena.

Although Naruto’s Shadow Clones were being dispersed one after another by the whirlwinds, the assault Kiba and Akamaru endured was even more violent.

Akamaru was the first to run out of stamina. With a shrill whimper, his body reverted to its original puppy form.

Kiba cried out in surprise, « Akamaru ! Are you okay ? »

Before he could finish, the remaining dozen or so Shadow Clones grinned and locked their eyes on him, launching a suicidal charge.

Two fists are no match for four hands ; under the dense siege, Kiba quickly fell and could only helplessly raise his hand to surrender.

The onlookers all shook their heads.

Naruto’s fighting had absolutely no technique ; it relied entirely on raw stats.

Yet his massive Chakra capacity and powerful constitution were able to support this mindless operation.

Except for a mere few, no one could really withstand this kind of numerical suppression.

But he himself had no such awareness. At this moment, he stood with hands on his hips, boasting triumphantly to the crowd below, « How about that ? My move is pretty impressive, right ? »

This caused everyone to roll their eyes. Sasuke even said bluntly, « Idiot. Isn’t that just a normal Shadow Clone ? What’s there to show off ? It’s completely lacking in artistry. »

Naruto was instantly exasperated, pointing at Sasuke’s nose to retort, « Are those red eyes of yours artistic ? You look like a rabbit. »

« Bastard, what did you say ? »

The two of them began to argue again as if no one else were there.

...

Shortly after, the drawing results for the next match were quickly revealed. It was actually Hyuga Neji versus Hyuga Hinata.

The onlookers' interest was immediately piqued. An internal battle within the Hyuga Clan ? A duel between Byakugan geniuses.

Hinata walked gracefully onto the stage. Hearing the surrounding discussions, she couldn’t help but sneer inwardly : 'Genius ? With a loser like Neji, how could he be worthy of being compared to me ?'

At this moment, Neji was already sweating profusely, cursing in his heart : 'How can my luck be this bad ? To actually draw a monster like Hinata.'

As a member of the Hyuga Clan, Neji knew all too well how terrifying this seemingly fragile sister really was.

Her appetite for a single meal was enough to feed a hundred people, and her progress in Taijutsu was even more staggering, having long since broken through the limits of what he could understand.

At this thought, the pressure on him was already at its limit.

With the referee’s command, the match officially began.

Neji activated his Byakugan without hesitation. Veins bulged around his pupils as his eyes stared fixedly at Hinata, not daring to relax for a single moment.

In contrast, Hinata was sizing up her cousin with great interest.

Seeing him activate the Byakugan and take the Gentle Fist starting stance, a trace of contempt appeared at the corner of her mouth.

'What era is it that people are still using Gentle Fist ? The current meta is Strong Fist Style.'

Thinking this, Hinata assumed the starting stance of the Strong Fist Style and gave Neji a faint warning :

« Brother Neji, you better keep your eyes wide open and not relax for a second. Otherwise, the victory between us will be decided in an instant. »

Neji’s mouth twitched. He was about to retort that he had the Byakugan, which came with X-ray vision and didn’t require him to stare so wide.

But before the words could leave his mouth, Hinata had already transformed into an afterimage and closed the distance.

« Brother Neji, you’re distracted. »

Accompanied by this whisper, a fist carrying a terrifyingly fierce wind smashed toward him.

Neji’s hair stood on end. He hastily used « Soru » to retreat rapidly.

In the next instant, Hinata’s fist heavily struck the spot where Neji had just been standing.

Accompanied by a deafening roar, a large crater several meters in diameter was actually smashed into the ground.

Seeing this, cold sweat soaked Neji’s forehead again as he shouted in horror, « Hinata, are you trying to kill me ? »

Hinata just shook her hand as if nothing had happened and said nonchalantly, « It was just a probe. Why are you reacting so strongly ? »

Watching from the side, Asakawa Shin frowned. He felt this move looked somewhat familiar and couldn’t help but blurt out, « This move... is it Diamond Crusher ? »

Hinata looked over in surprise. « What Diamond Crusher ? Shin, do you give a special name to your own heartbeat and breathing ? This was just a casual strike. »

Hearing this, Asakawa Shin felt his teeth ache with annoyance.

Hinata’s posturing was truly getting higher and higher. In the field of acting cool, he felt like he was falling behind. It seemed it was time to research it further...

Neji was currently terrified to the extreme, his mind rapidly weighing whether he should just surrender.

However, in the instant he hesitated, Hinata had already cast her gaze over.

In the next instant, Hinata transformed into an afterimage that even the Byakugan could hardly track, charging straight at him.

Neji’s pupils shrank. Without time to hesitate, he immediately used Rotation to deploy his defense.

A terrifying flow of Chakra erupted from the points all over his body. Accompanied by high-speed rotation, it formed a 360-degree defensive barrier with no blind spots.

This was precisely the secret technique of the Hyuga Main House, a Gentle Fist technique called « Rotation. »

Seeing this, Hinata did not slow down ; instead, she met it head-on.

She shouted coldly : « Finger Pistol : Tenketsu ! »

In the next moment, her hands turned into a sky full of afterimages. She didn’t dodge at all, directly attacking Neji’s high-speed rotating Rotation.

Under the horrified gaze of the crowd, the originally high-speed rotating Chakra sphere actually underwent violent deformation, being squeezed from a perfect circle into a distorted ellipse before crashing into collapse.

Neji was sent flying backward like a broken kite, slamming heavily onto the ground. His throat felt sweet as he coughed up a spray of blood.

« You actually broke through Rotation by force ? This is the Absolute Defense... how... how did you do it ? » Neji roared in disbelief.

Hinata sneered, looking down at him from a height. « Did you forget ? I have the Byakugan too. And I know the principles of Rotation inside out. Brother Neji, you can consider yourself unlucky to have drawn me. »

After saying that, she turned and walked away, leaving only an ethereal voice echoing around. « What a boring battle. Referee, you can announce the result. »

A bitter smile squeezed out of the corner of Neji’s mouth as he lay on the ground like a salted fish. « Quick... someone call a doctor for me. »

As Neji was carried away on a stretcher, the result of the match was as expected.

« Hyuga Neji is unable to continue. The winner is Hyuga Hinata ! »
