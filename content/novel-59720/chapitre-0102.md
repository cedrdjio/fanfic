# Chapitre 102 — Steam Ninja

« Damn it, it’s been a whole month ! Has this Han finally decided to move ? »

« What the hell ? He’s so lazy, making me wait for so long for nothing. »

« This grudge... I, Shin, will remember it ! »

Outside Iwagakure, Asakawa Shin indignantly sensed Han’s movements.

Although he was cursing under his breath, his feet didn’t stop for a moment as he chased after the direction Han had left.

This tailing lasted for an entire day, until Han reached a desolate mountain range, where Asakawa Shin finally revealed himself and blocked his path.

« Hmm ? Who are you ? Why are you blocking me ? »

Han’s gaze was cold, firmly locking onto the figure that had suddenly appeared before him.

Asakawa Shin appeared somewhat leisurely, even carrying a hint of playfulness. He shrugged and asked in return :

« What kind of person do I look like to you ? »

Han narrowed his eyes and lowered his voice :

« I see that you are hiding your identity and acting arrogantly ; you are definitely not a good person ! »

« Could it be that you’re here for a highway robbery ? »

« That’s right. I opened this road, I planted this tree. If you want to pass... »

Before Asakawa Shin could finish, the red armor on Han’s body suddenly erupted with several jets of scalding steam.

Accompanied by a piercing explosion, the powerful thrust enveloped him, turning him into a red shadow that flew forward with a thunderous flying kick.

« Boil Release : Unrivaled Strength ! »

Facing Han’s sneak attack, Asakawa Shin merely nonchalantly blocked with his palm in front of his face, actually intending to take the blow barehanded.

Seeing this, Han was overjoyed, thinking to himself that this enemy was simply a fool.

Seeing his calm demeanor just now, he had thought he was some formidable expert.

He didn’t expect him to be an ignorant fool who couldn’t even see the power of this move.

'Since you’re looking for death, I’ll grant it to you !'

With a thought, Han increased his Chakra output even further. His armor gushed out more violent steam, propelling him like a cannonball toward Shin.

« Die, you rat ! »

In a flash, Han had already arrived before him.

He delivered an aerial kick with undiminished force, aiming straight for Asakawa Shin’s face.

He intended to first shatter his palm, then blow right through the skull behind it.

« Boom ! »

With a loud bang, the two collided violently.

However, contrary to Han’s expectations, Asakawa Shin’s palm was not only unscathed but had steadily caught the blow.

It was only the massive impact that caused Asakawa Shin to involuntarily slide backward, plowing two drag marks over ten meters long into the ground.

But that was all.

What Han found even more unbelievable was that Asakawa Shin’s hand was like a pincer, firmly locking onto his ankle.

No matter how much he increased the steam output, he couldn’t break free at all.

Facing his horrified gaze, Asakawa Shin’s lips curled into a smirk as he mocked sarcastically :

« Tsk, is the so-called Steam Ninja only at this level ? How disappointing. »

« But your ability is quite useful. It’s more than enough for cooking rice or heating a boiler~ »

« Alright, you’ve played enough, haven’t you ? Next, it’s my turn. Take this ! »

A subtle excitement flashed in Asakawa Shin’s eyes. With a sudden burst of strength, he actually grabbed Han’s ankle with one hand and swung the Iwagakure Jonin like a ragdoll, slamming him hard onto the ground.

Accompanied by a dull thud, the hard rock instantly turned to powder, and a deep crater formed in the ground.

Han felt as if his internal organs had shifted. He nearly lost his breath, and his vision went black.

Before he could recover from the intense pain, a strong sense of weightlessness hit him again.

Asakawa Shin gave him no chance to breathe, pulling him up again with his arm and slamming him heavily onto the rocks on the other side.

This heavy blow caused Han to spit out a mouthful of blood, staining the crushed stones beneath him red.

However, this was only the beginning of the nightmare.

With a blank expression, Asakawa Shin swung his arm like a windmill, repeatedly slamming Han like a tattered sack.

The booming sounds of impact echoed continuously through the valley.

For a moment, dust filled the air, startling away countless birds.

After slamming him more than ten times, seeing that Han’s breath had become incredibly weak.

Asakawa Shin, worried he might accidentally kill him, finally stopped the violent assault.

Han was in a wretched state ; his specially-made hard armor had long since been shattered into pieces.

He lay limp on the ground, staring blankly at the sky, looking completely broken.

Seeing him not moving for a long time, Asakawa Shin thought he had beaten him senseless and scratched his head sheepishly. Unexpectedly, this single action jolted him awake.

He suddenly blocked his head with his hands and crawled backward, cowering in a defensive posture.

This left Asakawa Shin at a loss. Had he given the guy a stress response ?

Han then realized what he was doing, and his face couldn’t help but turn beet red.

Turning shame into anger, he glared fiercely at Shin and roared in his heart :

'You bastard, I must kill you today !'

At this thought, the Chakra around Han’s body suddenly boiled. In a few moments, he completed his Tailed Beast transformation, taking on the terrifying form of the Five-Tails, Kokuo.

A look of interest flashed in Asakawa Shin’s eyes. Was he another type who relied on his own will to forcibly control a Tailed Beast ?

However, how much of a Tailed Beast’s power could a Jinchuriki who couldn’t communicate heart-to-heart with it actually exert ?

Having transformed into the Five-Tails, Han saw that Asakawa Shin was still standing there, observing him with a look of leisure, and he immediately flew into a rage.

'It was one thing for you to be so relaxed before I transformed into the Five-Tails.'

'But now that I’ve become the Five-Tails, you’re still so relaxed. Doesn’t that make my transformation pointless ?'

'Die, you arrogant brat !'

He suddenly lifted his leg and stomped down with all his might, wanting to crush the eyesore next to him into meat paste.

As a result, the sensation from the stomp made him sense that something was wrong.

In his uncertainty, he couldn’t help but look sideways, and the scene he saw made his heart skip a beat.

Asakawa Shin had actually blocked his furious strike effortlessly with just one hand.

What horrified him even more was that Asakawa Shin’s fingers were like iron pincers, firmly gripping a corner of his hoof.

No matter how hard he pulled, he couldn’t retract his limb even half an inch.

This familiar scene made him tremble all over again.

Asakawa Shin keenly noticed the other’s gaze. A playful arc curled at the corner of his mouth as he mocked again :

« You really never learn, do you ? You actually dare to use your foot to kick me again ? »

« Well now, it’s my turn again ! »

Before he finished speaking, he suddenly exerted force, actually pulling the Five-Tails' massive hoof toward his chest with one hand.

This pull was so powerful that it actually shook the Five-Tails' massive body, causing it to lose its balance and stumble forward.

At the same time, Asakawa Shin wrapped both arms around the giant hoof, locking it tight, and let out a hearty laugh :

« Hehehe... Round two, begin. »

Then, Asakawa Shin suddenly exerted force, using a clean shoulder throw to swing the Five-Tails' massive body and slam it heavily onto the ground.

With a deafening boom, the rocks shattered, and a massive crater was directly smashed out.

« Muda muda muda ! »

Asakawa Shin didn’t stop. He quickly changed directions and repeated the trick, pulling the Five-Tails up like a ragdoll and slamming it hard to the other side.

For a time, the dignified Five-Tails was like a child’s toy, being repeatedly thrashed by Asakawa Shin without any power to fight back.

In just a short while, Asakawa Shin felt the hoof in his arms suddenly tremble and begin to shrink slowly.

He looked back and saw that the giant Five-Tails had almost disappeared, reverting back to Han’s form.

However, Han was now rolling his eyes, completely unconscious.

Asakawa Shin scratched his head in disappointment and couldn’t help but say in amazement :

« Is the top combat power of Iwagakure only at this level ? »

« It’s way too disappointing. This is it ? Really ? »

But since things have come to this, let’s eat first...

He skillfully walked over, placed his hand on the Tailed Beast seal, and directly began to absorb the Five-Tails' Chakra.

After a long time, he let out a long breath and slowly opened his eyes, though his expression held a hint of confusion :

« Something’s not right. Why is the speed of my body’s evolution so slow ? »

« It feels like that energy is just being stored in my body, not being quickly digested and absorbed like the previous times. »

« To use an analogy, if before it was devouring greedily, now it feels like chewing slowly... »

« What’s going on ? Why is the Five-Tails so special ? »

« Is there some difference between him and the Two-Tails, Eight-Tails, and Nine-Tailed ? »

He sniffed. The tempting aroma belonging to the Five-Tails had already vanished. It seemed the Chakra absorption was indeed complete.

Temporarily suppressing his confusion, he skillfully began to draw Han’s blood for storage.

Then, condensing Chakra in his palm, he pressed it directly onto Han’s chest.

This time it was much easier ; the Flying Thunder God Mark was instantly branded onto his heart.

A sharp light flickered in Asakawa Shin’s eyes as he pondered his next move :

« The Five-Tails is taken care of, so next it’s the Four-Tails' turn... »

« Speaking of which, I’ve been out of the village for so long... »

« I should use some tactics against the Four-Tails later to finish it quickly. »
