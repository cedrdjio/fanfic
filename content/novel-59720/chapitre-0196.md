# Chapitre 196 — Rinne Sharingan

Asakawa Shin pressed one hand firmly onto the giant tenseigan, while his other hand gripped Toneri’s throat like an iron pincer, his expression cold and abyssal.

This giant tenseigan was originally the crystallized aggregate of countless Byakugan from the Ōtsutsuki Clan, and the ocular power contained within was as vast as the sea.

Toneri, furthermore, was a chosen individual with extremely high bloodline purity, someone who had even drawn the attention of the main house of the Ōtsutsuki Clan.

At this moment, with these two things converging in his hands, the final piece of the puzzle was finally complete.

In an instant, a terrifying power of devouring erupted with a boom.

The massive ocular power within the giant tenseigan, along with the pure Chakra within Toneri’s body, transformed into two raging torrents, continuously being extracted, compressed, and poured entirely into Asakawa Shin’s body.

Eventually, they converged toward the budding flower of the Divine Tree atop his head.

« Hum— ! »

The giant tenseigan emitted a violent, death-throes tremor ; its originally dim pupil suddenly flashed with a blinding, eerie light, as if struggling in silence.

Immediately following, a shocking scene unfolded.

This giant eye, capable of easily destroying stars, began to crumble inch by inch, transforming into countless ordinary Byakugan that scattered across the sky like fireflies.

As the connection to the Tenseigan was forcibly severed, Toneri convulsed violently, let out a faint whine, and his life force was completely extinguished.

Asakawa Shin, however, let out a grunt of satisfaction.

The budding Divine Tree flower atop his head, the moment it devoured the giant tenseigan and all of Toneri’s life force, suddenly bloomed !

In the very center of the flower was not a normal stamen, but a bright red Rinne Sharingan, which was particularly eye-catching.

At the same time, a clear realization surged into Asakawa Shin’s mind like a tide.

He had awakened a dōjutsu of the Rinnegan, named—Amenotokotachi.

Judging solely from the literal meaning, this technique refers to « the constancy of heaven. »

Its specific ability was even more domineering and unparalleled.

The user of the dōjutsu can select their current state at a given point in time to « save » it.

As long as their ocular power is not exhausted, they can ignore injuries and death, reverting to that state an infinite number of times—it could be called an immortal body of a temporal nature.

Asakawa Shin frowned slightly ; this seemed to overlap with his original abilities...

But he immediately dismissed this thought. After all, this was a noble time-based ability ; how could it be so simple ? There must be many ways to develop it, right ?

I wonder if it can withstand Fuinjutsu ?

What about Transcription Seal ?

Countless thoughts exploded in his mind, making him almost unable to suppress the urge to try it immediately.

However, he took a deep breath, forcibly suppressing this restlessness and returning to calmness.

At this moment, he had grown into a true Divine Tree, capable of perfectly controlling his own form.

With a slight movement of his mind, the fully bloomed Divine Tree flower atop his head slowly descended, as if merging into the depths of his mind.

The next moment, a scarlet, leaf-shaped mark appeared on his forehead.

The mark was vertically elongated, resembling a closed eye ; it was his Ten-Tails humanoid form.

« I’ve planned for so long, yet I only awakened one Kekkei Mora, the Rinnegan ? »

Doubts filled his heart, and his thoughts surged.

« Why was Kaguya able to possess multiple Kekkei Mora powers ? Could it be related to her having consumed the Chakra Fruit ? »

« If I want to bear fruit, what should I do ? »

Questions surged like a tide, causing him a sudden, inexplicable headache.

Forget it, set it aside for now.

With a thought, he instantly entered the Six Paths Mode.

Nine pitch-black Truth-Seeking Orbs slowly floated up behind him.

The skin and hair all over his body turned into a holy, pure white.

The robes he had been wearing also changed, transforming into the Six Paths Sage Robe that symbolized the authority of a god.

Upon entering this state, he had mastered two powerful methods : Yin-Yang Release and the Truth-Seeking Orbs.

Yin-Yang Release is the collective term for Yin Release and Yang Release.

Yin Release is based on spiritual energy, creating form from nothingness.

Yang Release uses physical energy as its source and can bestow life upon forms.

When the two are combined, reaching a high level of mastery allows one to even create all things and bestow life upon them ; the nine Tailed Beasts were created in this way.

And the Truth-Seeking Orbs are the ultimate sublimation of Yin-Yang Release.

Using Yin-Yang Release as its foundation, it integrates the seven Chakra nature transformations—Water, Fire, Wind, Lightning, Earth, Yin, and Yang—condensing them into a high-density energy body.

Its power is enough to easily level an entire forest, and it can change shape at will, possessing impeccable offensive and defensive capabilities.

Most terrifying of all, it can completely nullify all Ninjutsu. Even an Edo Tensei body, once severely damaged, will vanish completely because it cannot reincarnate.

Asakawa Shin nodded slightly, quite satisfied with the results of this transformation, and voluntarily exited the Six Paths Mode.

'If someone were to seal me inside their body right now, I wonder if it would result in them becoming a Ten-Tails Jinchuriki ?'

He smiled, his gaze shifting slightly to look at Toneri’s corpse on the ground, and then he skillfully sealed it into a scroll.

The next instant, his figure flashed, appearing before the trembling Clan Head of the Main House, and he commanded in an indifferent tone :

« The rebellion of the Branch House has been quelled by me. Now, it is time for you to fulfill your promise. Go back and pack up ; I have a research project that requires your assistance. »

The Clan Head of the Main House trembled all over and asked with suspicion and uncertainty : « Research ? But... we know nothing about that. May I ask, my lord, what specific research is it ? »

« Being researched... »

These three words alone hit the Clan Head of the Main House like a bolt of lightning ; he swayed, on the verge of collapsing to the ground.

Seeing this, Asakawa Shin laughed heartily. After his mischievous urge was satisfied, he stopped scaring these people.

« Rest assured, it’s just drawing a few tubes of blood ; I won’t do anything to you. »

The Clan Head of the Main House finally breathed a sigh of relief.

At this point, they were the fish and he was the butcher’s knife ; they had no choice but to submit.

He secretly arranged for two young boy and girl to hide, then gathered the remaining people, who gathered before Asakawa Shin, trembling with fear.

Asakawa Shin knew it clearly but did not expose them, simply remaining silent as he took everyone and activated the Flying Thunder God Technique.

After a bout of dizziness, the large group of people arrived directly in Orochimaru’s laboratory.

The sudden appearance of a large group of people caught Orochimaru off guard. The test tube he was holding tightly jerked, and the crisp sound of shattering was particularly piercing in the silent laboratory.

However, Orochimaru paid no attention to the mess on the floor.

He blankly raised his head, his signature snake eyes staring fixedly at Asakawa Shin, his voice filled with disbelief : « Junior brother... you didn’t die ? »

Asakawa Shin was a bit confused by this nonsensical greeting and retorted : « I’m standing right here, perfectly fine. Why are you talking about whether I’m dead or not ? »

« Then where exactly have you been for these three years ? »

Orochimaru’s brows furrowed tightly, his tone carrying a hint of complaint, « You went missing for three whole years, and the Anbu of Konoha searched the Ninja World inside out without finding a trace of you. »

« Nani ? Three years ? »

Upon hearing this, Asakawa Shin was shocked, his pupils shrinking violently, « Are you sure it’s three years ? ! This is bad... »
