# Chapitre 43 — Shin Carries Rice

Seeing that negotiations were impossible, the white-bearded old man decisively ordered :

« We must kill this brat today, otherwise he will surely become a grave threat to our Kusagakure. Everyone, attack together !

And those two women are important medical resources for our Kusagakure. We must not let them escape ! »

The Ninjas of Kusagakure responded one after another, gathering their Chakra to release Ninjutsu and besieging Asakawa Shin.

« This brat is so arrogant, it’s simply outrageous ! Let’s go together and kill this stinking brat ! »

« We must take back those two women, otherwise, with those useless people in the Medical Squad, we won’t be able to meet our needs at all. »

« Brat, what boastful words ! You think you’ve got us ? Be careful not to break your teeth. »

For a moment, the light of various Ninjutsu and nature transformations flashed continuously, striking towards Asakawa Shin.

Facing this saturated Ninjutsu attack, Asakawa Shin calmly used Busoshoku Haki to reinforce the ground, guarding against a sneak attack from Earth Release.

Then he sheathed his sword, formed hand signs, and exerted his full strength to perform a Water Release defensive Ninjutsu in response.

« Water Release : Water Formation Wall. »

Instantly, water appeared out of thin air in all directions around Asakawa Shin’s group, tightly surrounding the three of them and forming a constantly rotating sphere of water.

Under Asakawa Shin’s massive Chakra supply, the size of the Water Formation Wall expanded rapidly, reaching a staggering height of over a hundred meters in the blink of an eye.

It not only blocked all the attacks from the surrounding Ninjas but also swallowed up some of the Ninjas who didn’t have time to retreat.

This move was exactly the Water Formation Wall improved by the Second Hokage, Senju Tobirama. A normal Water Formation Wall could only raise a single wall of water to defend against an attack from one direction.

However, the Water Formation Wall improved by Senju Tobirama could generate a sphere of water that rotated around the user, providing a defense with no blind spots, and even the number of hand signs was simplified and reduced.

Senju Tobirama’s mastery of Water Release was incredibly high ; even if he were to take the position of Mizukage on the spot, it wouldn’t feel out of place.

« Humph ! It’s not over yet, take another one of my moves ! »

Asakawa Shin’s hand signs changed again, completing four signs almost instantly. This was another Ninjutsu improved by Tobirama.

« Water Release : Water Dragon Bullet Technique ! »

As Asakawa Shin performed the technique, the giant sphere of the Water Formation Wall slowly stopped rotating, and its surface began to fluctuate and surge violently.

Under the despairing gazes of the surrounding Ninjas, the Water Formation Wall gradually transformed into a huge water dragon with distinct scales, which then surged towards them with fierce momentum.

« Don’t come over here ! »

« Waaah... »

The surging water dragon swept away the enemies and rampaged through Kusagakure, almost completely destroying the southern part of the Village. This single strike defeated hundreds of Ninjas.

The Ninjas who survived because they were not in the path of the water dragon’s attack turned and fled without looking back, no longer caring about the Village.

But how could Asakawa Shin let them go ? They wanted to run after bullying his sisters ? No such luck.

Four of Asakawa Shin’s Shadow Clones flew over their heads, reaching the four corners of the Village—East, West, South, and North—ahead of them, and formed hand signs simultaneously under their shocked gazes.

« Ninja Art : Four Red Yang Formation ! »

Four fiery red barriers rose at the same time, completely enveloping the entire Kusagakure, so that even a bird could not pass.

Several Ninjas who couldn’t slow down in time were bounced back the moment they touched the barrier, and were immediately ignited by the fierce flames on the barrier, burning into ashes. The surrounding Ninjas hurriedly moved away in fear.

The Ninjas tried to attack with combined Ninjutsu, but it was useless. With their attack power, they couldn’t break through the defense of the Four Red Yang Formation at all.

They couldn’t damage it, they couldn’t fight back, and they couldn’t escape.

Many Ninjas had already lost their will to fight, kneeling on the ground with despairing expressions, not even caring to pick up their weapons that had fallen.

« Why did it turn out like this ? Why is he so strong ? »

« What did we do wrong ? Why treat us like this ? »

« It’s all you bastards ! I told you long ago not to treat Kana and her daughter so cruelly, but you just wouldn’t listen ! »

« You damn executioner ! Do you know how many people you’ve killed ? »

« No... please, I don’t want to die yet. I was wrong, please spare me. »

« Why did we provoke him ? Why attack a Konoha Ninja ? Those higher-ups in the Village... »

Asakawa Shin heard the wails of these losers from a distance, and he couldn’t help but laugh.

It started as a smile, then a chuckle, then a loud laugh, and finally a wild laugh towards the sky.

The laughter even drowned out the chaotic voices of the Kusagakure Ninjas, leaving them dazed and terrified, until only this one sound remained in the entire area.

'What is this madman going to do now ?'

After a long time, Asakawa Shin finally finished laughing. His expression was cold and stern as he looked around at the Ninjas kneeling on the ground and said loudly :

« It’s not that you know you were wrong ; you just know that you’re going to die.

Survival of the fittest, the strong prey on the weak—isn’t this the truth you’ve always believed in ?

Because you were stronger than Kana, you could freely control their lives and trample on their dignity.

Now that I am stronger than you, are you ready to accept my judgment ? »

Without waiting for their answer, Asakawa Shin formed a hand sign with one hand. A huge stone throne slowly rose from the ground, lifting the three of them hundreds of meters into the air.

Asakawa Shin slowly walked to the edge of the throne and looked down at the entire Kusagakure. The Ninjas below looked up at him with various expressions—some terrified, some remorseful, and many with eyes full of hatred. Some unwilling Ninjas were still attacking him, but they were easily dealt with by his Shadow Clones.

The various aspects of the world were nothing more than this.

Asakawa Shin looked back at Karin and her mother. They returned a grateful look, their expressions free of fear or unease.

Asakawa Shin’s expression became firm. No longer hesitating, he controlled almost all the Haki in his body to concentrate on his right fist.

As the Haki gathered, streaks of black and red lightning flashed around him, and a light as bright as the stars condensed on his fist, illuminating the entire sky.

The youth’s clear voice then came from the sky, like a divine chant :

« Feel pain, contemplate pain, accept pain, know pain !

Those who do not know pain will never understand true peace.

From now on, let the world feel pain !

Fist Bone : Galaxy Impact ! ! ! »

The flashing light reached its peak in an instant, briefly stripping everyone of their vision. In a daze, they seemed to see the vast starry sky appearing before their eyes, and Asakawa Shin was the most brilliant star in that sky.

« What a beautiful strike... »

« The stars are falling ! »

Asakawa Shin’s full-power strike was finally unleashed, and the entire world seemed to pause for a moment.

The shockwave slammed into the ground from his charged right fist. At the moment of impact with the earth, the Haki erupted like a tsunami, surging in all directions.

Under this violent impact, buildings collapsed like fragile paper, and the Ninjas were swept away like ants, their fate unknown.

Smoke and fire churned, and the world fell into a dead silence.

Kusagakure, annihilated !

High in the sky, Asakawa Shin used Moon Walk to hover, holding a slender waist in each arm, preparing to find a place to land and set down the two women.

Suddenly, he felt a stream of heat on both sides of his body. Before he could react, he heard Karin say :

« Sorry, Master, I... I wet my pants. »

« I... me too... »

« ? »
