# Chapitre 213 — Three Generations of the Family

Iwagakure, the Tsuchikage’s Office.

Ōnoki slammed his palm heavily onto the desk in anger, making the thermos clatter. « Has that idiot Roshi still not been found ? »

« N-No ! »

The intelligence Ninja responsible for the report trembled as he saw the Tsuchikage’s increasingly grim expression, stuttering, « B-But... »

« Stop hesitating ! Speak up ! » Ōnoki barked.

« The traces of Lord Deidara you asked for have been found ! »

Just as Ōnoki showed a look of surprise, the intelligence Ninja’s next words were like a blow to the head, making his vision go dark, nearly causing him to faint.

« Sunagakure has sent a formal protest, identifying the culprit who attacked the Village and kidnapped the Kazekage as your disciple—Lord Deidara ! »

Ōnoki stammered, « You... you mean... that brat... not only joined Akatsuki but also participated in the mission to capture Tailed Beasts ? »

Just as the intelligence Ninja was about to speak, the heavy office door was suddenly kicked open with a bang.

A slender, long, beautiful leg first came into view before being retracted, followed by a valiant-looking young girl stepping inside.

Her voice arrived before she did.

« What did you say ? Big Brother Deidara joined that Akatsuki ? »

The girl stared intently at the intelligence Ninja. Seeing him nod with difficulty, an irrepressible light of excitement burst from her eyes.

« Akatsuki ? What if I want to go take a look ? I wonder how Big Brother Deidara has been doing these past few years. He hasn’t even come home to visit ! »

The moment Ōnoki saw his granddaughter, he felt his temples throb with a massive headache.

Hearing her openly clamoring to join the Akatsuki Organization, Ōnoki’s beard trembled with rage, and his temper flared uncontrollably.

In his fury, he actually jumped onto the desk and gave Kurotsuchi a sharp rap on the forehead from above.

Unexpectedly, he used too much force and overextended himself, resulting in a « crack » as he threw out his back.

« Ouch ! »

« It hurts so much ! »

The grandfather and granddaughter immediately huddled together, one clutching her head and the other his lower back, their wails rising and falling.

Everyone in the office looked at each other, black lines appearing on their foreheads ; the originally heavy and solemn atmosphere was instantly ruined.

In this awkward moment, a tall samurai clad in full armor spoke in a muffled voice, trying to bring the topic back on track.

« Lord Tsuchikage, I believe it’s very likely that Roshi has met with misfortune. News came a few days ago of a major disturbance in the Land of Water ; the Three-Tails has likely fallen into their hands. Calculating by this order... »

Before he could finish, everyone nodded with heavy expressions, clearly understanding the stakes.

Ōnoki rubbed his back and looked at the armored samurai, instructing in a deep voice, « Han, the next target is very likely you. For the coming period, you are not to step a single foot out of the Village ! We absolutely cannot let the enemy succeed ! »

« Yes ! »

Just as Han responded, a strange and flippant voice abruptly cut in : « That won’t do. That would be quite a headache for me. »

« Who’s there ? »

Everyone was instantly on high alert, their gazes shooting toward a corner of the office.

A figure had appeared there out of thin air at some point, wearing a black robe with red clouds, a ring with the character « Zhu, » and a Swirl mask—undoubtedly a member of the Akatsuki Organization.

Ōnoki’s pupils constricted, a flash of irrepressible sorrow crossing his eyes.

An Akatsuki member had infiltrated the Tsuchikage’s office ; it seemed Roshi had indeed met with misfortune, and his fate was self-evident.

His cloudy old eyes narrowed slightly, showing a hint of wariness. Could this be the Masked Man who attacked the Hidden Cloud Village and captured the Raikage ?

However, Kurotsuchi showed no fear ; instead, her eyes lit up with excitement. « Are you from Akatsuki ? How is Big Brother Deidara doing now ? »

The newcomer was Asakawa Shin. He shrugged upon hearing this. « To be honest, his situation isn’t great. I shaved his head bald a few years ago, and he lost an arm in the fight against the Kazekage a few days ago, though it was reattached later... »

« Pfft~ » Kurotsuchi covered her mouth and giggled, her eyes full of mischief. « Big Brother Deidara is so pathetic ! »

She smiled, her lively large eyes blinking. « I wonder if your Akatsuki Organization is still recruiting ? Would you consider a talent like me ? »

As she spoke, she put her hands behind her back and approached with feigned innocence.

Asakawa Shin stood with his arms crossed, watching her composedly. Was this person a performance personality ? So much drama ?

The moment the thought arose, Kurotsuchi’s face suddenly changed, and she launched a furious kick. « Your eyes are wrong, take this ! »

« That idiot ! » Ōnoki cursed inwardly, but he had no choice but to follow up.

His figure flashed as he instantly performed « Earth Release : Ultra-Light-Weight Rock Jutsu, » his body becoming as light as a feather as he took to the air.

Mid-flight, he quickly layered the « Rock Fist Jutsu, » his right fist instantly becoming covered in a thick layer of hard rock.

Following that, he formed another set of seals and shouted, « Earth Release : Ultra-Added-Weight Rock Jutsu ! »

The old man’s fist whistled through the air, carrying hundreds of tons of weight as it crashed down from above.

Han did not hesitate either, instantly activating his Boil Release steam. Though he was the furthest away, he was the first to flash to Asakawa Shin’s right, forming a pincer attack.

« Quite the coordinated teamwork, huh ? Pity. »

Asakawa Shin’s lips curled slightly as he slowly spread his arms. « Shinra Tensei ! »

In an instant, a terrifying 360-degree repulsive force erupted, meeting the incoming attacks from three sides like a raging tide.

The repulsive force was only slightly hindered for a moment before Asakawa Shin’s gaze turned cold, and his Chakra instantly surged.

The previously suppressed repulsive force suddenly intensified, turning into an insurmountable wall of sighs, forcefully pushing the three of them and their momentum back.

« Boom— ! »

The Tsuchikage Building collapsed at the sound, with debris and rubble flying in all directions like scattering flowers, and dust billowing into the sky.

Asakawa Shin shook his head, watching the movement at the edge of the ruins with interest.

Amidst the billowing dust and rubble, Kitsuchi steadily protected Ōnoki and Kurotsuchi, completely unharmed.

This Iwagakure general had just performed Earth Release in a flash, softening the building’s structure to act as a cushion, effectively neutralizing the impact of Shinra Tensei ; even the falling debris lost its lethality.

This calm mastery of Earth Release Chakra nature transformation was indeed impressive.

« I remember you’re called Kitsuchi, right ? » Asakawa Shin said, one hand in his pocket, his tone relaxed as if the exaggerated strike from moments ago had nothing to do with him.

Kitsuchi’s usually simple and honest face was now full of gravity. He carefully set his father and daughter down, his broad frame standing before them like a monolith.

As soon as Kurotsuchi steadied herself, she showed no fear. Instead, she gave Kitsuchi a thumbs-up. « Great job, Dad ! »

« Hmph ! » In mid-air, Ōnoki steadied himself, his hair and beard bristling with shame and anger.

He looked down at the ruined Tsuchikage Building and caught sight of the surrounding Ninjas who had rushed over with terrified expressions, then roared sternly :

« Everyone below Jonin level, evacuate immediately ! Do not take a single step forward ! »

The surrounding Genin and Chunin were momentarily stunned, their hearts trembling with shock.

To make the Lord Tsuchikage lose his composure like this, even to the point of dispersing everyone...

Could this be that terrifying guy who went on a rampage in the Hidden Cloud Village and captured the Raikage ?

Fear instantly spread through the crowd. No longer caring about appearances, everyone scrambled and crawled away in a panic, fearing they would be too slow.

Against that guy, a mere numerical advantage was meaningless.

On the other side.

Han rubbed his aching shoulder as he crawled out from the pile of rubble.

Looking at the three generations of the family standing side by side, a sense of resentment at being excluded welled up in his heart.

« So it turns out I’m the only outsider here ? Damn, I kind of miss Roshi... »
