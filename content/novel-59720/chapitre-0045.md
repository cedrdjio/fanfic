# Chapitre 45 — Encountering White Zetsu

Asakawa Shin brought the mother and daughter, Kana and Karin, to the capital of the Land of Grass. The bustling scene made Karin, who had never traveled far, marvel in wonder, finding everything new and exciting.

« Mama, what is that ? So many people are lining up ; it looks delicious. » Karin pointed at a snack shop and couldn’t move her feet.

Kana felt a bit awkward. As a medical tool of Kusagakure, her status was low, and she had no money at all. She couldn’t help but give Asakawa Shin a look for help.

« Oh ? Is there a branch of Three-Color Dango here too ? It just so happens I haven’t eaten it in a long time and have a bit of a craving. Let’s buy some and try it together. »

Asakawa Shin understood the hint and took out his wallet to buy six skewers of Three-Color Dango, exactly two skewers per person.

Karin had never eaten anything so sweet before. She was so happy that she quickly finished her two skewers.

Asakawa Shin and Kana each handed her another skewer, making her so happy her eyes crinkled into crescents.

Seeing her so happy, Kana’s heart also felt sweet.

« By the way, Sister Kana, this is the money from Kusagakure’s treasury. Keep it ; consider it the medical fees Kusagakure has owed you all these years. »

Asakawa Shin handed over a Sealing Scroll containing about forty million ryo, which had been seized by his Shadow Clone earlier.

For such a large Kusagakure, there was actually only this much money in the treasury. No wonder they were so fixated on Shikaku’s team.

Because this was Asakawa Shin’s war booty, Kana firmly refused to accept it.

She was Asakawa Shin’s maid ; it was natural to spend the master’s money, but taking the master’s money was considered rebellious.

Asakawa Shin had no choice but to say, « Fine, then you manage this money for me. What kind of master has to carry money himself when going out ? »

« From now on, our family’s expenses for food, clothing, and daily use will come from this money. There’s no need to be frugal ; everything should be the best. »

« After returning to Konoha, our family will have a large income, which you will also manage for me. »

Only then did Kana happily accept it.

The group then went to buy several sets of clothes and stayed at a very popular hot spring inn.

Although Asakawa Shin still had to go out at night to repair the rift, which would surely get him covered in dust and ash.

He still wanted to take a bath and change clothes first ; otherwise, he always felt a bit strange.

« Sister Kana, where is the hot spring bath ? I’m going to take a soak. »

« It’s just ahead. Our suite has a private hot spring, so we don’t have to squeeze in with others. »

Kana enthusiastically led the way for Asakawa Shin, bringing him to the entrance of the hot spring.

Asakawa Shin stepped inside, and Kana followed right behind him.

« Uh, Sister Kana, is there anything else ? » Asakawa Shin looked at Kana with some confusion.

Kana also looked at him strangely. « Hmm ? Master, aren’t you going to bathe ? I’ve come to serve you while you bathe. »

« Ah ? That won’t be necessary. I can wash myself. »

« Hmph, I’ve been washing Karin since she was little. You’re both so young, how would you know how to bathe properly ? You won’t get clean at all. »

« Besides, you can’t reach your back. You can’t possibly use a Shadow Clone to scrub your back for you. »

« Also, I am your maid. Serving you is my job. It’s just that Karin is still young and clumsy, otherwise... »

« But don’t worry, Master, I will train her well. »

« Then... I’ll leave it to you, Sister Kana. »

Asakawa Shin thought about the nature of his Shadow Clones and was easily persuaded.

He couldn’t count on his Shadow Clones at all ; it would be lucky if he didn’t have to scrub their backs instead.

Then he might as well try out what the life of a grand lord was like and critique it properly.

In the hot spring.

Asakawa Shin felt a bit distressed looking at the scars on Kana’s arms, calves, and shoulders.

« Sister Kana, show me your arm. Let me see if I can heal it for you. »

Hearing this, Kana shrunk back slightly but still extended her arm.

Asakawa Shin gently touched the scars to examine them. Some were old scars from several years ago.

Ordinary Medical Ninjutsu would be very troublesome to remove them, but...

« Sister Kana, they can be removed, but it will be a bit painful. Can you endure it for a while ? »

Kana was overjoyed. What woman doesn’t love beauty ?

« Master, can they really be healed ? Then I’ll leave it to you. A little pain is fine ; don’t pity me, the priority must be ensuring the scar removal effect. »

Asakawa Shin’s mouth twitched. As expected, when it comes to beauty, women know no fear. It’s the same in any world.

« Actually, I could knock you out first, so you’d wake up and the surgery would be over. »

Kana hesitated but still refused. « No need, I want to witness this moment with my own eyes. »

Asakawa Shin nodded. « Then bear with it, I’m starting. »

Asakawa Shin’s treatment method was actually very simple : use the precise technique of Haki’s internal destruction to completely destroy the scar tissue.

Then he would use his life-force-filled Chakra to perform the Mystical Palm Technique, which could easily heal the skin so the new skin would have no scars.

Of course, the pain was real. It was equivalent to peeling off the original skin to let it regrow.

However, Kana only cried out in pain at the beginning.

Once she saw the treatment was actually effective, she became as silent as if she had been anesthetized. Even though she was trembling all over from the pain, she didn’t call for a stop once.

By the end of the treatment, Kana was exhausted. She leaned against the hot spring stones, finding it difficult even to move.

But her spirit was very excited. The scars all over her body had been removed. She felt as if she had been reborn, and even the look in her eyes became more confident.

After settling Kana in, the sky had already darkened. Asakawa Shin left a Shadow Clone to be responsible for teaching Karin and protecting their safety.

Without any worries, Asakawa Shin used Moon Walk to fly towards the rift.

It was already late at night when he arrived, the perfect time for construction.

After observing the area, the rift was about ten meters wide but about a hundred meters deep, completely cutting off the Grass River. The riverbed downstream was already exposed, with only some small fish and shrimp splashing in the puddles.

Without delay, Asakawa Shin formed hand seals and performed an Earth Release Ninjutsu, [Earth Release : Earth Flow Rampart].

This Ninjutsu can use Chakra to form or transport the earth’s soil, raising a huge earthen wall beneath the user’s feet.

The shape of the earthen wall can be anything from flat to steep mountains, according to the user’s will.

It was most suitable for the current situation ; the only drawback was that it would consume quite a bit of Chakra and be rather tedious.

After all, it was a ten-kilometer-long, hundred-meter-deep rift. Destruction is easy, but creation is hard.

Thus, Asakawa Shin began his day’s work.

He prioritized restoring the riverbed of the Grass River, returning the downstream flow to normal, then repaired the rift outward from the riverbed and reinforced it.

Just as Asakawa Shin was about to perform the Earth Flow Rampart again at the bottom of the canyon, he saw a white figure out of the corner of his eye, lying at the bottom of the canyon, life or death unknown.

This gave him a start. In his Observation Haki perception, this figure felt just like a plant, with no special characteristics detectable at all.

If he hadn’t seen it with his naked eyes this time, he would have automatically ignored it.

Asakawa Shin cursed inwardly. Is this fucking White Zetsu ?
