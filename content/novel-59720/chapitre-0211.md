# Chapitre 211 — Strawberry Flavor

« Wuwuwu, that’s exactly how it happened, Senior Sister... »

Asakawa Shin’s face was swollen like a pig’s head as he crouched in the corner, weeping bitterly.

« I really wasn’t pestering Temari ; I was drugged ! I’m the complete and utter victim here ! »

Hearing this defense, Tsunade crossed her arms and thoughtfully stroked her chin, tossing out a remark seemingly nonchalantly.

« I remember your cell activity is extremely high, with a near-perverted resistance to toxins. Being drugged unconscious ? Who are you trying to fool ? »

Before she finished speaking, she slammed her palm onto the table, making the teacups rattle and causing Asakawa Shin to jump in fright.

« It must be that you failed to pick up the girl and decided to act like a hooligan ! Lewd ! Shameless ! Brazen ! »

Seeing Asakawa Shin speechless and unable to respond, the corner of Tsunade’s mouth curled into a smug arc, revealing a Sherlock Holmes-like wise smile.

« Don’t I know what’s going on in that head of yours ? You specifically target blonde beauties, don’t you ? »

As she spoke, she proudly puffed out her chest and began listing them off : « Ino Yamanaka, Temari, Nii Yugito, and then there’s me... Hahahaha, you certainly have sharp eyes. »

Beside them, Yugito’s eyes instantly lit up upon hearing this.

She hurriedly untied the long blonde braid on her chest, carefully combing it smooth, thinking to herself :

'I see ! In that case, I have a big advantage too ? No... wait... there are too many competitors in Konoha. I’m all alone ; my chances of winning might not be high.'

Her eyes narrowed slightly as she discreetly glanced at Tsunade’s magnificent chest, thinking to herself :

'This must be the legendary Main Wife, right ? Blonde, Mature Lady, big chest ! All the elements are there !'

With a plan in mind, she smiled slightly. 'The same configuration... I wonder if bringing Samui to Konoha as my helper would work ?'

While Yugito was weighing the feasibility of recruiting Samui, Asakawa Shin in the corner suddenly clapped his hands and stood up abruptly.

« I understand, Senior Sister ! »

Asakawa Shin looked enlightened and shouted indignantly, « What Temari gave me wasn’t poison at all, but a powerful tonic ! That’s why my cells couldn’t break it down. This is definitely a misunderstanding ; I’m innocent ! »

« Oh ? » Tsunade raised an eyebrow.

She paced back and forth in the office with her hands behind her back, muttering :

« Come to think of it, that seems possible ! That girl Temari usually looks carefree, but who knew she had such schemes. »

« Making use of your excessive cell activity is actually a brilliant idea... »

Unknowingly, a meaningful smirk appeared at the corner of her mouth.

Seeing this, Asakawa Shin’s expression changed instantly. He closed the distance, his face almost touching the tip of Tsunade’s nose, and pressed her with a burning gaze, « Senior Sister, what are you laughing at ? »

« Huh ? »

Tsunade was startled by the sudden question. She quickly retracted her smile and explained seriously :

« I was feeling gratified by a major pharmacological discovery ! You know I’m the top Medical Ninja in the Ninja World ; I can’t help but get excited when I think of this new type of pharmacology ! »

« You’d better really be doing academic research ! »

Asakawa Shin stared intently into her eyes. Seeing her expression was calm and flawless, he suspiciously backed away.

'It seems I was overthinking it.'

He shook his head secretly. 'Senior Sister Tsunade is the Hokage ; how could she do something like drugging me ? It’s too absurd. Truly, once bitten, twice shy.'

...

Because another new member was joining the household, Tsunade announced an early end to the workday.

Not only did she bustle about carefully arranging a room for Yugito, but she also uncharacteristically prepared to cook a welcome banquet personally.

Shizune frowned, sensing that things weren’t quite so simple.

Lady Tsunade taking the initiative to do housework ? This was even more unbelievable than the sun rising from the west.

Her intuition told her there was definitely a problem here !

So...

« What ? You’re going to drug Lord Shin ? How can you do that ! »

Tsunade jumped in fright and quickly reached out to cover Shizune’s mouth tightly, looking around guiltily. She only relaxed after confirming no one was watching.

She glared at Shizune and said crossly, « What choice do I have ? We’re too familiar ; it’s hard to make a move ! This is the only way... »

Shizune nodded understandingly. In the end, she didn’t expose Tsunade’s little scheme, only turning to leave with a heavy heart.

Tsunade stroked her chin with satisfaction.

It seemed she hadn’t pampered Shizune in vain all these years ; the girl was on her side after all.

She turned and poured all the special powder into the soup. After stirring for a moment with a spoon to ensure the powder was completely dissolved, she picked up the soup bowl and walked toward the dining room with a face full of smiles.

However, just after her figure disappeared at the end of the corridor, Asakawa Shin slowly emerged from the shadows on the ground with a grim expression.

He had felt Senior Sister Tsunade was acting strangely today and specifically kept an eye out. Sure enough, he had stumbled upon the truth.

With a complex expression, he reached out a finger, dabbed a bit of the white powder spilled on the counter, and after a moment’s hesitation, actually put it in his mouth to lick it.

« Damn, it’s actually strawberry-flavored ! »

He cursed inwardly and walked silently toward the dining room.

« Junior Brother, come, try this dish. I made it specifically for you. »

Asakawa Shin took a bite, chewed it carefully, and his eyes instantly lit up. « So delicious ! As expected of Senior Sister, I didn’t know you had such culinary skills. You were really hiding your light under a bushel. »

« Eat more if you like it. Try this one too. » Tsunade smiled brightly and pushed another plate toward him.

« Amazing ! Senior Sister, you’re really incredible ! » Asakawa Shin gave a thumbs up, praising her without reservation.

A glint flashed in Tsunade’s eyes, and she then brought over a bowl of steaming soup, her tone filled with expectation : « Junior Brother, try this last. »

« Alright ! » Asakawa Shin didn’t refuse, taking the bowl and drinking it all in one gulp.

Putting down the bowl, he smacked his lips and asked with surprise : « This soup is amazing ! It’s actually strawberry-flavored ? Senior Sister, how did you make seaweed soup taste like this ? It’s practically divine skill. »

Hearing this, a hint of awkwardness flashed across Tsunade’s face, and she quickly hid it with a dry laugh. « Haha, as long as it tastes good. Don’t worry about the details. »

« Senior Sister, I feel a bit dizzy ? »

« Relax, take deep breaths. Dizziness is normal. »

« Senior Sister, I seem a bit drunk. Could you please help me to the bedroom ? »

« Coming. Walk slowly. »

After watching the two leave supporting each other, Karin suspiciously approached the dining table, scratching her head as she stared at the empty soup bowl, looking puzzled. « Strange, can you get this drunk just from drinking soup ? »

Kana rolled her eyes at her speechlessly and shook her head.

Yūgao’s eyes sparkled as she suggested, « How about... I cook tomorrow ? »

The night was deep, and all was silent.

Except for...

« Junior Brother, how about... taking a break ? We’ve been at it all night ; you should take a breather... » Tsunade felt a bit dizzy and quickly took deep breaths.

« No need. »

Asakawa Shin shook his head, his tone firm and full of energy. « I’ve activated Life Return ; this level of intensity is easy for me. Senior Sister, don’t even think about slacking off. Hurry up and open the Yin Seal to its maximum ! »

« Junior Brother, I really... can’t take it anymore... please have mercy on me... » Tsunade’s voice became increasingly melodious, almost pleading.

« No way. » Asakawa Shin wouldn’t budge an inch, his eyes flashing with excitement. « The medicine hasn’t worn off yet. Senior Sister, your medicine is simply amazing. You must give me the formula tomorrow. »

« Muri... »

« Senior Sister, actually, I’ve had a dream since I was little... »

« Dame... »

« And that is to become the man of the Hokage ! Senior Sister, I did it today ! »
