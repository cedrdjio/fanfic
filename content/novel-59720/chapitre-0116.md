# Chapitre 116 — Team Assignment

« Today’s the day for team assignments. I’m so excited ! I wonder if I’ll be in the same class as you, Shin ? »

Naruto rested his hands behind his head, his face beaming with a bright smile.

« With Karin added, our three-person combo would be invincible ! Haha, as long as it’s not that guy Sasuke, it’s fine~ »

Hearing this, the corners of Asakawa Shin’s eyes curled slightly, a fleeting trace of amusement flashing by. « Well, who knows ? »

« Hmm ? What’s going on ? Why is the classroom so noisy ? »

Karin gave a faint glance and understood immediately. « It’s probably those girls getting jealous over Sasuke again, right ?

I really don’t get what’s so great about Sasuke that makes them so obsessed.

If it were Lord Shin, that would be more like it~ »

Hearing this, Naruto immediately flared up and shouted indignantly :

« Is that guy Sasuke stealing the spotlight again ? Just you watch, I’m going to go teach him a lesson ! »

After saying that, he strode toward Sasuke with a spirited and imposing air.

His actions immediately drew everyone’s attention.

Under the surprised gazes of the crowd, Naruto suddenly leaped up and crouched steadily on the desk in front of Sasuke, staring him down from above.

Seeing this, Sakura was about to explode with rage.

Over the years, this guy had been constantly pestering Sasuke, leaving her with no chance to get close, and her relationship with Sasuke hadn’t progressed at all.

Seeing them locking eyes again, she finally couldn’t help but complain loudly :

« Naruto, stop pestering Sasuke all the time when you have nothing better to do ! »

Seeing someone start the confrontation, the other girls quickly followed suit to voice their support :

« Yeah, stay away from Sasuke ! »

« Exactly, exactly ! »

These voices made Naruto even angrier. He stared intently at Sasuke, who returned the fierce gaze without backing down.

Their gazes met in mid-air, as if intense electric sparks were flying.

'It’s coming, it’s coming~'

Asakawa Shin pulled out a camera from somewhere, already prepared to capture the moment.

Just then, a boy sitting in the row in front of Sasuke was chatting excitedly with someone else. As he gestured wildly, he accidentally bumped into Naruto.

Caught off guard, Naruto’s body lurched forward. Without any buffer, he crashed into Sasuke, and their lips suddenly met.

« Chu~ »

Accompanied by that abrupt soft sound, the originally noisy classroom seemed to have its mute button pressed, falling into an instant, deathly silence.

Everyone widened their eyes, looking at the two people pressed tightly together in disbelief.

« No— ! »

After two seconds of dead silence, Sakura finally let out a desperate shriek, as if her soul were about to leave her body at that moment.

Her Inner Self roared hysterically : 'Bastard ! Sasuke’s first kiss should have belonged to me !'

« Blegh, my mouth... my mouth is going to rot ! »

Naruto was the first to snap out of it. He spun around, clutching his neck and dry-heaving while coughing violently.

Sasuke was frantically wiping his lips with a look of agony, looking as if he were suffocating, his voice even trembling slightly : « Naruto... you bastard, I’m definitely going to kill you ! »

...

« Hmm ? Killing intent ? »

The coughing Naruto suddenly felt a chill run from his spine to the top of his head.

He spun around, his heart instantly leaping into his throat.

He saw that behind him, a dense crowd of girls had already gathered.

They were all cracking their knuckles and staring at Naruto with unfriendly eyes, slowly closing in on him step by step.

« Wh-what are you trying to do ? That really was an accident just now ! »

Seeing that the girls were completely unmoved, Naruto broke into a cold sweat and hurriedly turned to Asakawa Shin for help : « Shin, help me ! Save me ! »

By this time, Asakawa Shin had already satisfiedly put away his camera and teased nonchalantly : « Shouldn’t you be happy ? Look how popular you are now~ »

« I don’t want this kind of 'popularity' ! Help me ! »

On the other side, Sasuke was gargling with milk when he suddenly felt a gentle tap on his shoulder and quickly turned around.

« Oh, it’s Hinata. What’s up ? Is something the matter ? »

Hinata smiled like a blooming flower, her cheeks slightly flushed : « Sasuke-kun, don’t leave in a hurry after the team assignments. I have something to talk to you about, it’s urgent ! »

...

After all the students in the class had arrived, Iruka finally walked in with the team assignment list.

By this time, Naruto had already been beaten into a pulp, lying on the desk with a face full of resentment.

Iruka coughed twice. Once the classroom quieted down, he spoke with a serious expression :

« First of all, congratulations to everyone on officially becoming Ninjas—specifically, Genin.

However, take note : the real test only begins today.

Next, you will be organized into three-person squads to carry out missions under the leadership of a Jonin teacher. »

As soon as Iruka finished speaking, the classroom erupted into a commotion, with students whispering and discussing among themselves.

« Three-person squads ? »

A light of anticipation sparkled in Karin’s eyes. She leaned close to Asakawa Shin with joy, her tone incredibly certain :

« Lord Shin, the two of us will definitely be in the same group, right ? After all, I am your handmaiden ; I can’t be separated from you no matter what. »

Ino obviously heard Karin’s words and immediately started complaining :

« Hey ! Karin, you’re being too sneaky ! I want to be in the same group as Shin-kun too ! »

Karin covered her mouth and laughed : « How is that possible ? Isn’t your 'Ino–Shika–Chō' known as the Iron Triangle ? The three of you have to be together. »

« Who cares about that ? »

Shikamaru smacked his lips and looked at his companion with a dejected face : « Tsk, I feel like our Ace Combination is breaking up before it even starts. Don’t you think so, Choji ? »

Choji didn’t pay him any attention at all, preoccupied with grabbing a handful of potato chips and munching on them loudly.

Sakura clenched her fists, desperately cheering herself on in her heart : 'I must be in the same class as Sasuke-kun !'

The other students were also lost in their own thoughts, and the classroom fell into a temporary silence.

Seeing this, Iruka continued : « To ensure a balance of strength in each class, the assignments were decided by the Instructors.

Now, I will announce the team results. »

« First, Team 7—Uzumaki Naruto, Sakura Haruno, and Uchiha Sasuke ! »

Before Iruka could finish, Naruto and Sasuke both exploded, their protests echoing through the classroom.

« What ? I have to be in a group with this idiot Naruto/Sasuke ! Are you kidding me ! »

« What did you just say ? » *2

Sakura wiped a bead of cold sweat from her forehead. How could these two be so in sync ? She suddenly felt a bit like a third wheel.

...

« Team 8, Hyuga Hinata, Inuzuka Kiba, and Shino Aburame ! »

...

« Team 10, Ino Yamanaka, Nara Shikamaru, Akimichi Choji ! »

As Iruka’s voice fell, the team results were soon fully revealed.

Naruto keenly noticed something was wrong and immediately raised his voice to ask : « Wait a minute, Iruka-sensei !

Shin and Karin haven’t been assigned yet ! Did you leave them out ? »

To his surprise, Iruka quickly shook his head, dismissing the guess : « The two of them will join an existing squad ; no new team will be formed separately for them.

Alright, that’s how it is. You’ll meet your respective Jonin teachers this afternoon. Class dismissed. »

« Great ! As expected, I’m assigned with Lord Shin ! »

Karin was in a great mood. Although she had a premonition, the moment it was confirmed still made her incredibly excited.

Once she calmed down, she couldn’t help but frown and asked in confusion :

« But what did Iruka-sensei mean by saying we would join someone else’s squad ?

Lord Shin, do you have any inside information ? »

Asakawa Shin looked at Karin with a mysterious expression : « This team-leading Jonin is no ordinary person. You’ll have to study seriously under them when the time comes. »

« Oh, Lord Shin, stop keeping me in suspense and just tell me~ »
