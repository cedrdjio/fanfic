# Chapitre 47 — Spreading bug medicine

« Teacher, I have one more favor to ask of you. »

« I’m so famous now, a genius known to everyone in Konoha, but why don’t I have any Anbu Ninja protecting me personally ? What if those Kumogakure barbarians come to kidnap me ? »

« I see Naruto always has several Anbu Ninja around him, I want some too ! »

Asakawa Shin thought for a moment ; he was about to move into a big mansion, but only three people were living there. It lacked life ; it’s more lively with more people.

He already had a maid, but he still lacked a personal guard.

« Protect who ? Protect you ? » Hiruzen Sarutobi was a bit confused. With Asakawa Shin’s combat power, did he really need protection ?

After receiving the urgent report from the intelligence personnel in the Land of Grass yesterday, he was shocked. Kusagakure had been quietly leveled to the ground, with not a single Ninja escaping.

And no one knew which major power did it, causing the entire Ninja World to be on edge. Everyone felt in danger, and the Daimyo of the Land of Grass even fled to the Capital of the Land of Fire overnight to seek asylum.

However, Hiruzen Sarutobi knew the inside story. The one who committed such a major crime was none other than his precious disciple, Asakawa Shin.

Did Asakawa Shin, who possessed the combat power to destroy a village, need protection ? At that point, it was uncertain who would be protecting whom !

The Kumogakure barbarians indeed had a history, but they would have to be incredibly unlucky to try and kidnap Asakawa Shin.

Despite his confusion, since it was his precious disciple’s request, he simply agreed.

« Ahem, the situation in the Ninja World has been a bit tense lately for certain reasons, so most Anbu Ninja are out on missions. »

« How about this, in a few days I’ll arrange a few for you... »

Asakawa Shin waved his hand and interrupted, « Teacher, I don’t need many people. As the saying goes, quality over quantity. I think that Yūgao Uzuki I saw last time is quite good. »

« She’s brave and fearless in combat, stays calm even when at a disadvantage, has a great figure, and has good taste. She’s a talent. »

« And she uses a sword, right ? I know a thing or two about swordsmanship. If I train her more when I’m free, she’ll definitely become someone great in the future ! »

« Just let her be my personal guard. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi gasped upon hearing this. He glanced at Kana and her daughter with a thoughtful expression.

« It couldn’t be what I’m imagining, could it ? Please, no ! »

His previous three disciples were known throughout the Ninja World for their vices—lust, gambling, and drugs—which had completely disgraced him in the educational world and made him a laughingstock. Now, he was counting on Asakawa Shin, his only hope, to redeem his reputation.

However, looking at Asakawa Shin’s righteous gaze and remembering he was only six years old, he breathed a sigh of relief. He was probably overthinking it.

« Yūgao ? Then I’ll ask for her opinion once she returns from her mission. Anything else ? Say it all at once. »

« Oh, there is indeed one more thing. »

« Since Karin has joined Konoha, at her age, she should naturally enter the Ninja Academy to study. »

« Could she join our class as a transfer student ? »

« After all, she’s new here, and I wouldn’t feel at ease with her in another class. If she’s near me, I can look after her. »

« Fine, that’s just a small matter. Anything else ? If not, go do what you need to do. I’m a bit tired. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi tapped the desk, feeling a bit weary. An Anbu Ninja immediately appeared to take the order and left.

« Alright, Teacher, then I’ll take my leave. Please get some rest. »

...

Asakawa Shin bid farewell to Hiruzen Sarutobi and took Kana and her daughter back to his home first.

After all, she would be his maid from now on, so he definitely had to introduce them to his family.

Asakawa Shin entered the house and saw Kenji and Hanako sitting on the sofa, snuggling together.

Hearing the door open, both of them shuddered and turned their heads toward the door with a guilty look.

« Holy crap ! Dad, what happened to you ? Why are you so thin ? »

« And those dark circles under your eyes are too much ! Your complexion is terrible too. Haven’t you slept for days ? »

It was no wonder Shin was surprised ; Asakawa Kenji’s current state was like a completely different person compared to when he first returned. He looked utterly exhausted, as if his lamp had run out of oil.

« Ahem, don’t worry, Shin. Dad is fine. I’ve just been doing a few too many missions these past few days. I’ll be fine after some rest, » Asakawa Kenji explained guiltily.

However, he wasn’t lying to Shin. Having intimate relations with Hanako was actually an urgent mission personally assigned by the Hokage. Each time counted as a B-rank mission’s pay, and if she got pregnant, it would be considered a completed S-rank mission.

The high reward and the wonderful process filled Kenji with fighting spirit. Even his will of fire burned brightly as he thought only of contributing to Konoha. Thus, he had been doing « missions » day and night for the past few days, which was why he looked so exhausted.

« Sigh, Dad, don’t overwork yourself. Your health is important ! »

« Besides, I’m making big money now. We’ll have financial freedom from now on, so you don’t need to worry about these trivial matters. »

After saying that, Asakawa Shin handed Hanako a scroll, which contained the four hundred million Ryo given to him by Hiruzen Sarutobi.

« Mom, there’s four hundred million Ryo in here. It’s the dividend from the Musi Group I mentioned before. »

« I plan to buy two luxury mansions in the center of the village later—one for you and one for me. Shall we move there together then ? »

Hanako looked at the scroll in her hand in shock. Had her son really earned four hundred million Ryo ? And he even wanted to buy them a house.

However, she quickly regained her composure and handed the scroll back to Shin, her face slightly flushed as she said :

« Shin, it’s enough that you have this filial heart, but there’s no need to buy us a house. »

« Although this house is small, it holds the most beautiful memories for your father and me. We can just continue living here. »

Asakawa Shin frowned slightly, but since it was Hanako’s decision, he was willing to comply. So he instructed Kenji and Hanako :

« Of course you can stay here, but you should also open the windows frequently to air it out ! »

« I’ve smelled a strange odor since I walked in ; it’s all over the house. »

As he sniffed around like a puppy, he announced the results of his investigation.

« Hmm... on the sofa, the coffee table, the TV stand, the floor, in the kitchen, on the sink, in the bathtub, on the toilet, on the balcony. »

« The smell is strongest in your bedroom. Eh ? It’s in my bedroom too. What is this smell ? What have you two been doing at home ? »

With every place Asakawa Shin mentioned, Hanako’s face grew redder, until finally, it was as red as a honey peach.

She buried her head deep in Kenji’s chest, while her pale hand gave Kenji’s waist a sharp pinch, as if saying, 'Hurry up and answer ! What is that smell !'

Wincing in pain, Kenji’s mind raced. In the blink of an eye, he answered Asakawa Shin with great pride :

« There have been nasty bugs in the house these past few days, and they’ve been bullying your mother. »

« So your mother and I have been spreading bug medicine in various places around the house, so she won’t be afraid of the nasty bugs anymore. »
