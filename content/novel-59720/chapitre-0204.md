# Chapitre 204 — The Two-Tailed Kitty

« As expected of Ninjas from the desert, they truly are as passionate as fire, » Asakawa Shin muttered, wiping the cold sweat from his forehead.

This « land of tenderness » was truly a hero’s grave ; it had actually caused him to completely forget his original plan.

If Nagato hadn’t urged him several times through the Magic Lantern Body Technique, he might still be wallowing in it, unwilling to wake up.

He slapped his forehead hard and growled with deep regret, « I’ve actually been led astray by wine and women to such a decadent state ! I’ve decided, from today on... I’m quitting drinking ! »

After finding the source of his decadence and resolving to fix it, he pulled himself together. With a flash, his figure instantly vanished into the vast desert.

In just the blink of an eye, he had already arrived at a hidden valley in the Land of Lightning.

Looking around and confirming no one was there, Asakawa Shin quickly formed hand signs and cast the Wood Release : Wood Transformation Technique, instantly transforming into the likeness of Uchiha Obito.

He then sped off toward the Hidden Cloud Village.

« Little kitty, here I come ! »

Inside the Raikage’s office, the atmosphere was solemn.

Yugito sat upright on the Raikage’s throne, her entire body exuding an awe-inspiring majesty as she looked down at the assembled high-ranking officials of the Hidden Cloud.

« Everyone, I’m sure you’ve all heard about the incident at the Hidden Sand Village. »

Yugito spoke in a low voice, « What are your thoughts on this matter ? »

A flurry of whispers immediately broke out in the conference room. After exchanging glances, everyone began to voice their opinions.

« The fact that the 'Akatsuki' organization could launch a frontal assault on the Hidden Sand and defeat and kidnap the Fifth Kazekage in broad daylight is truly appalling. Their strength definitely cannot be underestimated ! » one Ninja said with a grave expression.

« Indeed, » another added. « Though the Hidden Sand’s strength has declined in recent years, it is still one of the Five Great Hidden Villages. To be breached so easily proves the power of the Akatsuki Organization. »

« However, what is their true purpose ? As far as I know, they are a mercenary organization that takes on missions. Could they have been hired to incite war in the Ninja World ? »

« Hmph, no matter ! » a burly Hidden Cloud Jonin snorted, a flash of disdain in his eyes. « Our Hidden Cloud Village is not something a mere Hidden Sand Village can compare to. If they dare to invade, we’ll make sure they never return ! »

The Ninjas nodded in agreement.

« Is that so ? »

A playful voice suddenly drifted down from above, startling everyone. They hurriedly looked toward the source.

On the ceiling of the conference room, a figure wearing a black cloak with red clouds and an eerie swirl mask was suspended upside down, looking down at the crowd below.

« Who are you ? How dare you trespass in the Raikage’s office ! » Yugito shouted sharply, her anger flaring.

Hanging upside down from the ceiling, Asakawa Shin’s gaze behind the mask held a hint of cold mockery. He laughed chillingly, « Weren’t you all just boasting about making sure I never return ? What ? Have you forgotten in just the blink of an eye ? »

« You... you’re from the Akatsuki ? » The faces of everyone present changed instantly, as if facing a formidable foe.

They never expected that the Akatsuki, who usually had no interaction with them, would actually appear here.

A Sensory Ninja quickly scanned the area and reported, « Lord Raikage, there’s only one of him ! »

Before the words had even finished, everyone reacted, forming a battle formation and surrounding the intruder.

Yugito sprang from the Raikage’s chair and strode through the crowd to stand at the very front.

She flicked her wrist, her black hair tracing a graceful arc through the air like a waterfall. Then, with one hand on her hip, she glared arrogantly at Asakawa Shin.

« I don’t care how much they paid you to come cause trouble in my Hidden Cloud Village. »

She spoke proudly, her tone carrying an unquestionable confidence.

« If you stop now, leave, and tell me the name of your employer, my Hidden Cloud Village is willing to pay double. »

Asakawa Shin glanced at her with a hint of surprise ; he hadn’t expected her to be such a wealthy little lady.

Pity, the target this time was her.

At that thought, he couldn’t help but shake his head slightly, his voice indifferent : « You can’t afford what I want. »

Yugito’s brows furrowed slightly.

The reason she was talking so much was simply to stall for time.

On one hand, a distress signal had already been sent, and reinforcements were on their way.

On the other hand, she felt an eerie sense of pressure from the man before her, which made her hesitant to strike rashly.

However, at this point, there was clearly no room for negotiation.

Though she didn’t know his true objective, the fact that he dared to infiltrate the heart of the Hidden Cloud Village so brazenly meant he could not be allowed to escape.

After all, no one could be sure if the Hidden Cloud’s secret intelligence had already been leaked.

Below, a Ninja gave Yugito a subtle nod, indicating that the ambush was in place.

Seeing this, a sharp glint flashed in Yugito’s eyes, and she hesitated no longer.

She swung her hand down and shouted, « Attack ! Take him down ! »

Several elite Ninjas lunged forward, rushing from all directions. Before they even got close, several Kunai whistled through the air, aimed straight for Asakawa Shin’s face.

« It seems we’ll have to settle this with force after all, » Asakawa Shin sneered, his voice full of sarcasm.

He slowly spread his arms, his expression calm as he growled in a low voice, « Shinra Tensei ! »

In an instant, a terrifying repulsive force erupted with him as the center, sweeping out in all directions like a raging tide.

The incoming Kunai and shuriken seemed to hit an invisible wall ; they stopped instantly and were then violently repelled.

The Ninjas in mid-air turned pale and hurriedly brandished their weapons to block, but the violent force reached them in an instant, slamming heavily into them.

They were struck as if by lightning, spitting out blood and screaming as they were sent flying backward.

The violent repulsive force raged through, pushing everything in its path until the entire Raikage’s office was razed to the ground before it finally dissipated.

Hovering in mid-air, Asakawa Shin couldn’t help but click his tongue inwardly. « First time using this move ; I didn’t control the power well. The Chakra output seems a bit excessive... »

Looking around, the upper half of the once-grand Raikage Building was gone. Debris and broken walls flew out like meteors, riddling the surrounding buildings with holes and leaving a scene of total devastation.

Amidst the billowing dust, Asakawa Shin landed steadily on a massive broken beam. His eyes narrowed behind the swirl mask, his gaze piercing through the dust to lock onto the slender, graceful figure deep within the ruins.

« As expected of the Hidden Cloud’s Two-Tailed Kitty, her reactions are fast enough. »

Yugito was crouched nimbly among the rubble, her body enveloped in a dark red Tailed Beast Cloak. She had clearly activated her defensive stance the moment Shinra Tensei erupted.

« You bastard ! How dare you act so recklessly ! »

Yugito snapped her head up, her eyes burning with rage as she glared at Asakawa Shin atop the ruins.

« Oh ? Is the kitty’s fur standing on end ? »

Asakawa Shin casually shifted his posture. « Don’t be so angry. Didn’t I do this just to clear out a more spacious spot for us to chat ? The lighting in the Raikage Building was terrible ; being out in the open is much more comfortable. »

Yugito was so angry she laughed. The Chakra flames around her body surged several inches higher.

She slowly stood up, brushed the dust off her clothes, and her eyes shimmered with a dangerous light.

« Clearing a spot to chat ? Good, very good ! Then I’ll let you experience the Hidden Cloud Village’s hospitality ! »
