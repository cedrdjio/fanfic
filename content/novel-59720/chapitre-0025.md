# Chapitre 25 — Apprenticeship Ceremony

« It’s about time. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi waved his hand, signaling Asakawa Shin to follow : « The apprenticeship ceremony is about to begin ; let’s head over there. »

« Very well, teacher. »

...

The stadium was packed with a sea of people, the noise rising like a tide. Countless Ninja, villagers, and representatives from various clans had gathered, their eyes burning with anticipation as they looked toward the central platform, waiting for an apprenticeship ceremony destined to be recorded in the annals of Konoha.

« Look, Hokage-sama is here ! »

Someone exclaimed, and the entire stadium instantly fell silent. Thousands of gazes turned in unison toward the backstage entrance.

Sure enough, two figures slowly emerged from the depths of the passageway.

The one in front, wearing a conical hat and the Sandaime Hokage’s haori, was Hiruzen Sarutobi. His expression was solemn, his posture upright, and his steps steady. Between his brows, one could see the majesty and benevolence of a legendary Ninja hero.

The youth following close behind wore a dark gray Ninja outfit. He had a handsome face, but his eyes were calm and unruffled, showing exceptional composure. He was the protagonist of the day—Asakawa Shin.

He had no ostentatious aura, nor the halo of a prestigious lineage, but the moment he stepped onto the platform, the entire stadium fell inexplicably silent for a moment, as if the very air had frozen.

Hiruzen Sarutobi stopped, turned slowly, and gazed at the youth behind him with eyes like torches.

In those eyes, weathered by war and political maneuvering, there was a rare hint of warmth and expectation. He raised his hand and pressed it gently on Asakawa Shin’s shoulder, a slow but weighty gesture.

« Everyone. » The Hokage’s voice was not loud, yet it echoed through the entire stadium like a tolling bell. « Today, I, Hiruzen Sarutobi, in the name of the Sandaime Hokage, wish to formally accept Asakawa Shin as my personal disciple, to inherit the will of fire and strengthen our Konoha. »

« I declare the apprenticeship ceremony officially begun. »

« Whoa ! »

As soon as these words were spoken, the silence of the stadium was finally broken, and the crowd’s chatter erupted instantly.

« So this is Asakawa Shin ? Truly, a hero emerges from the youth ! »

« Indeed, indeed. When I was his age, I was still playing with mud. »

« Who’s to say otherwise ? My child is older than him, but can’t even do basic arithmetic. I almost died of anger when tutoring them. »

« What’s so great about him ? It’s just a bit of good luck ! »

« Good luck is enough to create the Konoha Six Styles ? Is it that hard to acknowledge someone else’s excellence ? »

« Of course it’s hard. Can’t you see he’s practically dying of jealousy ? »

« Lord Shin is so handsome ! »

...

On the platform, the apprenticeship ceremony officially kicked off.

At this moment, Hiruzen Sarutobi sat upright in a grand chair, his expression serious, but the smile reaching his eyes could not be hidden.

He looked at the youth below him and seemed to see his younger self—full of spirit and radiant.

Under the gaze of the masses, Asakawa Shin respectfully performed the apprenticeship rites for Hiruzen Sarutobi, his expression earnest and his demeanor impeccable.

First bow. « Student thanks teacher for your future diligent guidance. »

Second bow. « Student will surely work diligently and dare not slack off. »

Third bow. « Student is willing to join hands with teacher and move forward together to create a bright future ! »

After the apprenticeship rites were completed, an attendant walked up with a tray. Asakawa Shin took the tea placed on it, walked in front of Hiruzen Sarutobi, knelt on one knee, and offered the tea with both hands : « Teacher, please have some tea. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi had dreamed of this moment day and night. With slightly trembling hands, he took the tea and drank it in one gulp, moving so fast as if he were afraid someone would come to ruin the ceremony the next moment.

After he put down the teacup, his old face was smiling like a blooming flower. He held Asakawa Shin’s arms with both hands and slowly lifted him up from the ground.

« Good student, get up quickly. The ground is cold, hahaha. »

The Konoha high-ranking officials and the heads of the various Ninja clans nearby also rushed forward to offer their congratulations, bringing the atmosphere of the scene to its peak.

« Congratulations to Hokage-sama for gaining such an excellent student ! »

« Congratulations, Hokage-sama. »

« Let us rejoice together. Someone, set the banquet quickly ; let us have a drink together. »

This massive banquet lasted until nightfall, and the revelry of thousands only stopped then. Fortunately, using Ninjutsu to clean up was very effective, otherwise, the trash generated alone would have been a headache.

You ask about the cost of the banquet ? That, of course, came from public funds.

Hokage-sama’s apprenticeship ceremony greatly boosted Konoha’s morale, gave the villagers of Konoha full confidence in the future, and even narrowed the distance between the Hokage and the new Konoha leadership and the villagers, making everyone in Konoha united as one. Doesn’t this count as official business ?

Official business must be handled officially ; public and private matters cannot be confused.

...

« Hello, my name is Uchiha Shisui. »

At the banquet, Asakawa Shin curiously sized up the newcomer. The youth in front of him had a delicate face with a happy smile on his lips. Despite being an Uchiha, he gave people a very gentle feeling.

« I’ve heard so much about you. A genius of the Uchiha Clan, the renowned Body Flicker Shisui. Seeing you today, your reputation is well-deserved. » Asakawa Shin went straight into praise mode, which actually made Shisui a bit embarrassed.

« Haha, how dare I call myself a genius in front of you ? I came today to thank you in person. »

« Thank you for solving the problem that has plagued our clan for many years. Our Uchiha Clan owes you a big favor. » Shisui thanked Asakawa Shin sincerely.

« If there is anything you need from me in the future, please don’t hesitate to ask. Even if it costs me my life, I will definitely help. »

Shisui’s gratitude toward Asakawa Shin was beyond words ; if it weren’t for Asakawa Shin’s reform proposal, the Uchiha Clan might have been walking on the edge of the abyss.

The conflict between the Uchiha and the Village was long-standing. Back then, Uchiha Madara betrayed the Village and fought Senju Hashirama in the Valley of the End. Even though Senju Hashirama ostensibly won, he had overdrafted a massive amount of vitality, leading to his death a few years after the war. It wouldn’t be an exaggeration to say that Madara killed Hashirama.

Later, during the reign of the Nidaime Hokage, the Uchiha Clan had launched a rebellion. Although it was suppressed, it intensified the conflict between the Uchiha Clan and the Village.

Then there was the Nine-Tailed incident a few years ago. As long as you weren’t blind, you could see the huge Sharingan tomoe in the eyes of the Nine-Tailed Fox. Even if you said it wasn’t the Uchiha Clan’s doing, no one would believe it.

Of course, in fact, the Uchiha Clan wasn’t wronged, because the one who did it, Obito, was a native-born Uchiha.

That night, the Yondaime Hokage and his wife, who had just taken office not long ago, were killed, and countless civilians and Ninjas who went to the battlefield also died.

The Uchiha Clan’s situation in Konoha became even more difficult ; even their clan grounds were forced to relocate to the outskirts, far from the Village.

Even so, the Uchiha Clan wasn’t behaving. Those hawks were all shouting about an armed coup to seize power. They didn’t even understand the principle that « matters succeed through secrecy and fail through leakage » ; they made it known to everyone before they even started.

As for the three strongest Ninjas in the clan, two were traitors, and one offered his neck to be slaughtered without any resistance. Therefore, in the original story, the Uchiha Clan being wiped out wasn’t wrong at all ; there wasn’t a single normal person in the whole clan.

In short, it was quite abstract.
