# Chapitre 72 — Evolution?

[ Current Roger Template Fusion Rate : 79.1% ]

«  Phew... After a year of closed-door cultivation, am I finally going to complete this evolution before the fusion rate reaches 100% ?

I can clearly feel that my body’s absorption of that life force has reached an almost overflowing level ; it seems it’s about to end...

Hehe, I originally thought that if it still didn’t work, I would brave Hanako’s pressure and dye my hair a rebellious yellow to stop the fusion rate from continuing to increase. Fortunately... »

Asakawa Shin, with his long hair draped over his shoulders, sat cross-legged on the ground in a lazy posture, about to complete the final lingering effects of this evolution. By this time, he had been in closed-door cultivation in Shikkotsu Forest for over a year.

Even the Shadow Clone left in Konoha had exhausted its Chakra last month and dissipated into a puff of smoke right where it stood, amidst the shocked gazes of the entire class of teachers and students.

During this year, in order not to let the original body down, the Shadow Clone had been unusually self-disciplined, accumulating a massive amount of knowledge and memories through constant study.

At the moment of its dissipation, this knowledge flooded into the original body’s mind like a dam bursting...

It knocked Asakawa Shin to the ground in an instant ; he only had time to curse his Shadow Clone before rolling his eyes back and falling fast asleep.

Fortunately, Sage Katsuyu happened to be chatting with him nearby and immediately healed him when she saw what happened.

Otherwise, who knows how many days and nights Asakawa Shin would have slept.

As expected, people are never afraid of trouble when doing bad things, and the same goes for a Shadow Clone.

It had actually used great perseverance to thoroughly study all of Hiruzen Sarutobi’s book collection and the forbidden books of Konoha over the past year.

Thanks to it, it wouldn’t be an exaggeration for Asakawa Shin to call himself a Professor of Ninjutsu now.

Although Asakawa Shin’s original body reaped the rewards without putting in the work, every time he thought about it, he couldn’t help but want to grit his teeth and curse :

' This Shadow Clone is like it has some major illness...

Tobirama is even more of a useless piece of trash ! He couldn’t even fix such a serious bug in the Shadow Clone !

And he calls himself a master of forbidden jutsu. I, Shin, will remember this grudge !'

«  Hmph ! I won’t think about these unpleasant things anymore. Let’s finish this evolution first. »

Asakawa Shin held his breath, focused his mind, closed his eyes, and shut out all distractions, beginning the final breakthrough.

With a single thought, a massive amount of Natural Energy was siphoned over. Asakawa Shin’s body acted like the eye of a storm, indiscriminately devouring all of this Natural Energy.

In an instant, a hurricane seemed to sweep through Shikkotsu Forest, and even the clouds in the sky were drawn and gathered by this force, forming a rare vortex cloud.

«  Huh ? What is this ? »

Sage Katsuyu watched this scene with a solemn gaze ; she had never seen anyone absorb Natural Energy so violently.

Even the legendary Six Paths Sage could not do this when he was practicing Sage Arts, which unconsciously caused a trace of trepidation to arise in her heart.

' Little Brother Shin, what exactly are you...'

The phenomenon lasted for five minutes before slowly dissipating. Sage Katsuyu sensed Asakawa Shin’s current state with some confusion :

' Is this finished ? Why does it feel like there isn’t much of a change ?

With such a big commotion, did he only get a little bit stronger ?'

So, after seeing Asakawa Shin open his eyes, she immediately stepped forward and asked curiously : «  Little Brother Shin, how do you feel ? Did the training succeed ? »

Asakawa Shin’s expression was complex. He first nodded, then slowly shook his head. Just when Sage Katsuyu was getting anxious, she heard him sigh :

«  I can only say it’s half-successful. The energy required for the evolution is overflowing, but it seems to be missing some crucial catalysts...

I need them as catalysts to complete and sublimate my essence. »

Speaking of this, Asakawa Shin’s brows furrowed tightly. He could feel his body’s craving, but he didn’t know what it was craving.

However, after a while, his brows smoothed out again. Anyway, there were only a few things related to the Wood Release Sage Body ; he would just have to try them one by one to find out.

Time is on my side.

Asakawa Shin suddenly clenched his fist, and he actually crushed the air with his bare hand, emitting a piercing boom. Feeling the massive Chakra and vigorous life force within his body, he couldn’t help but smile confidently :

«  I should count as having reached the perfection of the Wood Release Sage Body now. Compared to me, I’m afraid even Shodai(1st)-sama would be slightly inferior.

And my Chakra volume is now not inferior to that half of the Nine-Tailed inside Naruto. What an exaggerated increase. »

Sage Katsuyu also nodded very cooperatively. A seven-year-old human cub possessing such a massive amount of Chakra was truly shocking.

One must know that the Nine-Tailed is the most special Tailed Beast, possessing almost endless Chakra, and has been regarded by all living beings in the Ninja World as the embodiment of natural disasters since ancient times.

«  Besides that, my affinity for Natural Energy has also risen to a new level. Now... »

Asakawa Shin clapped his hands together and began a storm-like inhalation of the surrounding Natural Energy, entering perfect Sage Mode in an instant.

Not only that, the amount of Sage Chakra in his body had increased dozens of times compared to when he first mastered Sage Mode a year ago.

This gave Asakawa Shin a significant boost in endurance, enough for him to go all out and continue fighting for several hours.

However, if he were in a place with thin Natural Energy, the effect would likely be greatly reduced.

Even so, it was already completely enough. If he combined it with the assistance of Sage Katsuyu, it would definitely make the enemy question their life.

More importantly, his body no longer desperately needed that life force from the system. Future increases in fusion rate could be more relaxed, without the sense of urgency he always felt in the past.

This closed-door cultivation was extremely rewarding. Even without fully evolving, he had already gained such a huge boost, which made Asakawa Shin full of anticipation for the subsequent completion and sublimation.

Just as Asakawa Shin was looking forward to the future, Sage Katsuyu’s body paused slightly, and she suddenly spoke, interrupting Asakawa Shin’s thoughts :

«  Little Brother Shin, Little Tsunade just asked me to pass a message to you.

The Uchiha Clan was attacked by an unknown enemy a few days ago, and their clansmen suffered heavy casualties.

Not only that, but your teacher, Hiruzen Sarutobi, has also fallen ill because of this. He asked you to return to Konoha as soon as possible if you have nothing else to do, to stabilize the situation... »

Asakawa Shin’s scalp tingled. At this point in time, the Uchiha Clan ? Could it be that the plot of the Uchiha Clan massacre still happened ? That’s impossible, right...

Thinking of this, Asakawa Shin became a little anxious. Whether the members of the Uchiha Clan died or not was none of his business...

But Shisui and Sasuke had clearly pledged allegiance to him ; they were his beloved Pals. They couldn’t be harmed !

Asakawa Shin bowed to Sage Katsuyu : «  Sister Katsuyu, it seems I have an urgent matter and must leave first.

I have really troubled you during this time. Once I return to Konoha, I will summon your clone and fulfill my promise to you.

So now...

See you~ »
