# Chapitre 113 — Completion

The border of the Land of Water.

Covered in wounds, the Six-Tails Jinchuriki, Utakata, lay powerlessly on the ground. His eyes were filled with anger and resentment as he stared fixedly at the man in red armor before him.

« How on earth did I offend you ? Why do you pursue me so relentlessly ? You don’t take my life, yet you never stop beating me ? »

« Han » replied expressionlessly with a cold voice, « Because you didn’t say thank you just now... »

« You’re... you’re a lunatic... »

Hearing this, Utakata’s head buzzed, and he felt the world spinning. His heart full of disbelief eventually turned into a free and easy smile.

Closing his eyes, he could no longer hold on and fainted.

Asakawa Shin nodded with satisfaction and stepped forward to operate skillfully. After a long while, he let out a long breath, and his mood became cheerful :

« Now the Chakra of the nine Tailed Beasts is all gathered. Looking back, it really was a hearty adventure~ »

After his sentiment, he tossed Utakata aside like trash and strode away at high speed.

...

Shikkotsu Forest.

After many years, Asakawa Shin returned to this place, preparing to undergo his final transformation in this sacred land rich in Natural Energy.

The result did not disappoint him. After spending a year absorbing all the Chakra of the Six-Tails Saiken, obvious changes occurred within his body, as if the missing puzzle piece had finally been filled. A new round of evolution was about to begin.

Soon, Asakawa Shin’s whole body shook. That ultimate pleasure of life’s essence sublimating surrounded him once more. This feeling was so familiar that he became intoxicated, unable to extricate himself.

'Hiss... this feeling ! I’m about to get high again~'

Looking inward at his internal world, he saw countless Natural Energy surging in like a tide, but in an instant, it was all devoured by Asakawa Shin’s cells, like a parched land drinking sweet rain.

As time passed, his flesh and bones quietly transformed. Fine wooden patterns actually emerged, extending layer by layer like plant fibers, weaving into a mysterious network of life reconstruction.

The phenomenon suddenly vanished. Just as Asakawa Shin’s mind experienced a violent roar and tremor, this bone-deep evolution finally came to a quiet end.

« Whew... whew... what was that just now ? I didn’t turn into a vegetable person, did I ? »

Asakawa Shin quickly composed himself and observed his body inside and out. Finding no abnormalities, he felt a bit relieved.

Carefully sensing the changes before and after, his physique had become a bit stronger. The magnitude wasn’t large, but the vitality of his cells had become exceptionally vigorous, more than ten times stronger than before.

Asakawa Shin formed a sword with his fingers and slashed at his left arm without hesitation, instantly carving a ten-centimeter long narrow wound.

But to his surprise, the wound healed in the blink of an eye. The previous scar seemed as if it had never existed, disappearing without a trace.

« Holy crap ! This exaggerated ? Can I still use the Summoning Jutsu then ? »

Upon further testing, Asakawa Shin found he could use Life Return to autonomously control the speed of wound healing. This put his mind at ease ; otherwise, the Sage Mode Power Bank plan would have been declared dead before it even started.

« As for Chakra, my current Chakra volume has completely surpassed the full Nine-Tails. What an exaggerated increase, and it’s even continuing to grow in 'Ka' units. »

Sensing the craving within his body, with a slight thought, the surrounding Natural Energy converged like hundreds of rivers returning to the sea, turning into a violent gale that he inhaled entirely into his body.

As soon as this Natural Energy entered his body, it didn’t even go through the step of Sage Art Chakra conversion before being swallowed whole by his cells, as if absorbing Natural Energy had already become his instinct.

Along with the influx of Natural Energy, an inexplicable throb rose in Asakawa Shin’s heart, as if some brand-new power was gestating and stirring deep within his body, about to break out of its cocoon.

« Since you can eat, then I’ll let you eat your fill ! »

Subsequently, Asakawa Shin controlled the Natural Energy to continuously gather and devour. This process lasted for three days and three nights, absorbing nearly half of Shikkotsu Forest’s Natural Energy before his body signaled it was full and fell into silence, seemingly gestating something silently.

At the same time, an irresistible weariness hit him like a tide. Asakawa Shin forced himself to stay awake and smiled bitterly at Sage Katsuyu :

« Sister Katsuyu, I’ll have to trouble you to look after me again. I need to take a nap first. »

Sage Katsuyu nodded slightly, a hint of curiosity appearing in her indifferent eyes as she thought to herself :

'You better not disappoint me, kid. After all, I’ve invested heavily ; the Natural Energy Shikkotsu Forest accumulated over many years has almost been drained dry by you.'

Even with her indifferent temperament, sensing that the Natural Energy in the sacred land had become significantly thinner, she couldn’t help but feel a twinge of heartache.

But事已至此, since he was her chosen successor, she could only pamper him through the tears.

Asakawa Shin’s sleep was exceptionally sweet. His whole body felt warm, as if he had returned to his mother’s embrace as a child, peaceful and cozy.

He stretched his body, gave a lazy yawn, and opened his eyes contentedly, feeling comfortable all over and refreshed.

As soon as he opened his eyes, he couldn’t wait to look down and examine his body. Seeing nothing unusual, he slowly let out a long breath and felt relieved.

« Hmm ? Something’s not right. My head feels so heavy ! »

Asakawa Shin was startled and quickly walked to the mirror in the bedroom to examine himself closely. The sight reflected in the mirror made him gasp.

The tender seedling on top of his head had vanished without a trace, replaced by a fist-sized light purple flower bud.

The flower bud was tightly wrapped in several emerald green sepals. Set against his exquisite and handsome face, it unexpectedly exuded a bit of strange beauty.

« Holy crap ! Am I going to transform into a Calabash Brother ? No...

Wait, I haven’t turned into the God Tree, have I ? Isn’t this the flower at the top of the God Tree ? »

Asakawa Shin’s pupils constricted. A flash of inspiration hit him, and he suddenly remembered something : « I remember there seemed to be a Nine-Tomoe Rinne Sharingan hidden in the stamen...

Could there be one of those in my flower too ? »

Asakawa Shin’s brow twitched. He quickly composed himself and sank his perception into the flower bud on his head.

A moment later, he indeed detected a trace of extremely pure Yin Release power being gestated deep within the flower’s center.

However, that power was still weak, only a sliver, clearly not yet fully developed.

« Yin born from ultimate Yang ? This is probably the highest level, Bloodline Encompassment, which surpasses Bloodline Limits. »

Asakawa Shin thought to himself : 'To fully gestate it into form, I’m afraid it will take some effort.

With my current physique, it wouldn’t be an exaggeration to call myself a God Tree seedling.

But if I follow the characteristics of the God Tree, how much Natural Energy will it take to satisfy this... it’s a bit tricky.

Forget it, I’ll take it one step at a time. I’ll finish the graduation exam and become a Genin first ; then I should be able to draw a new template. I’m really looking forward to it~'
