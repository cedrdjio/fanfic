# Chapitre 212 — O...bito?

« Strange... I’ve clearly given up alcohol, so why is my health still deteriorating ? »

Asakawa Shin frowned deeply, pacing back and forth in the room, lost in thought but unable to find an answer.

« Could it be excessive indulgence ? »

He immediately laughed and shook his head, « Impossible, I have the Sage Body ! »

After a long silence, he sighed helplessly, « Fine, Senior Sister Tsunade is known as the strongest Medical Ninja in the Ninja World. I’ll have to go ask her for help with some conditioning tonight. »

Before he finished speaking, his movements suddenly stopped, and he stared dissatisfied at the ring on his finger.

A disturbance was coming from the Akatsuki Organization ring.

Magic Lantern Body Technique ?

Could it be...

After a brief disguise, he injected Chakra into the ring.

Suddenly, the scene before him seemed to split in two, and a wisp of his consciousness arrived in a dark cave in a daze.

Several more figures appeared one after another. A moment later, all eight members of the Akatsuki Organization were present.

Asakawa Shin’s eye twitched. With Itachi and Hidan missing, this Akatsuki Organization felt off ; they couldn’t even count to ten.

And then there was that traitor, Orochimaru.

Once he successfully usurped power in the future, he would definitely re-enlist them.

Pain looked around, and seeing everyone was present, he spoke in a deep voice : « I have summoned everyone here today because Sasori and Deidara have captured the Three-Tails. Now that everyone is here, let the sealing ritual begin ! »

Asakawa Shin immediately clapped his hands, « Yo~ Blondie achieved another merit ? He didn’t even lose an arm this time, impressive, impressive ! »

Deidara gnashed his teeth in anger but dared not speak out.

Although he had improved significantly over the past few years, he was still not confident he could defeat Tobi.

News of the battle in the Hidden Cloud Village had already spread throughout the Ninja World. This man had defeated thousands of ninjas in a single move and captured the Raikage alive.

But it didn’t matter ; he was still young. In a few more years, he would surely defeat Tobi and settle all scores at once.

Seeing Deidara’s lack of reaction, Asakawa Shin felt bored and walked to his position to wait in silence.

Deidara breathed a secret sigh of relief. He was truly afraid of this lunatic ; he had just grown out his beautiful hair and didn’t want it to suffer again, so he would endure him for now.

The other members also took their places, waiting for the sealing to begin.

As several blue lights flashed, the nine-headed dragon reappeared, biting towards the Three-Tails.

Pain quickly noticed something was wrong and glanced inconspicuously at Madara, wondering why this fellow wasn’t putting in any effort.

To his surprise, Asakawa Shin noticed and immediately rolled his eyes, cursing in his heart : 'You still want me to do free labor ? You’re really asking for it.'

Thinking this, he quietly reduced his Chakra output further, prepared to drag Pain, the BOSS, into working overtime for free with him.

The veins on Nagato’s forehead bulged, but he was helpless.

After Konan heard the reason, she couldn’t help but roll her eyes as well.

It was this Madara again !

A few days ago, he had actually come to her door and taken a large sum of money, directly causing a major setback to her Six Hundred Billion Explosive Tags plan.

Although she could produce Explosive Tags at a low cost through her Paper Ninjutsu, purchasing raw materials still required a vast amount of money.

In that case, wouldn’t it be difficult to succeed when she plotted against Madara in the future ?

She harbored secret resentment : 'Damn it, that was money Kakuzu worked so hard to earn, yet Madara swindled it away so easily. He’s simply not human !'

Four days later, the sealing ritual finally came to a close.

Asakawa Shin stretched, cutting off the Chakra for the Magic Lantern Body Technique, and the strange sensation of multitasking dissipated.

In just one short month, the Akatsuki Organization had captured three Tailed Beasts. At this rate, he would be able to see that charming ancestor in less than half a year, right ?

His Chakra Fruit would then be within reach, wouldn’t it ?

Otherwise, what kind of God Tree would he be if he only bloomed but bore no fruit ?

For the God Tree to bear fruit, two conditions had to be met : sacrificing a qualified Ōtsutsuki and absorbing a massive amount of Chakra. That charming ancestor was simply the perfect candidate.

He would let Nagato work for him first, and when the time was ripe, he would take over personally.

He could fulfill his cheap third brother’s long-cherished wish while also enjoying the fruits of his labor—it was truly killing two birds with one stone.

But there was no rush. For now, the priority was to find Senior Sister Tsunade to condition his body.

Inside the Hokage Office,

Tsunade sat in the main seat looking radiant while handling official business. Seeing Asakawa Shin’s arrival, she beamed and gave up her seat : « Junior brother, come sit for a while. You’ve worked hard these past few days ! »

Asakawa Shin didn’t stand on ceremony and sat directly in the Hokage’s chair, saying with a worried face : « Senior Sister, your medical skills are superb. Please take a look at me. I’ve been feeling low on energy these past few days ; I’m afraid I might be sick. »

Tsunade’s expression froze slightly, looking a bit unnatural. « Perhaps Junior Brother is just too exhausted from being busy with official duties. I’ll prescribe some medicine to nourish you, and you’ll be fine. »

Asakawa Shin nodded in deep agreement, but secretly noted it down against Nagato.

Thinking back, these symptoms appeared shortly after sealing the Two-Tails. Was he really being over-exploited by Nagato ?

Working four days straight without sleep recently—that’s not something a human does. Even capitalists wouldn’t dare exploit someone like that !

...

Time flew like an arrow. Kisame and Kakuzu partnered up and quickly took the Four-Tails into their possession.

Because the Four-Tails Jinchuriki, Roshi, had always been at odds with the Tsuchikage Ōnoki and traveled abroad year-round, Kisame easily found his trail.

Kisame’s Water Release combined with Samehada perfectly countered Roshi. In the end, he defeated and captured the Four-Tails single-handedly.

After the sealing ritual ended, Pain took the initiative to contact Asakawa Shin, assigning him to capture the Five-Tails.

Perhaps intimidated by the Akatsuki Organization’s previous thunderous methods, the Five-Tails Jinchuriki—Han, known as the « Steam Ninja »—stayed inside Iwagakure like a startled bird, refusing to come out.

Looking at the entire Akatsuki Organization, the only ones who could storm Iwagakure and steadily take down the Five-Tails were the Six Paths of Pain and Asakawa Shin.

« In other words... you want me to work overtime again ? »

Sensing Asakawa Shin’s dissatisfaction, Nagato immediately adopted a more agreeable tone and suggested : « It’s just an exchange. The relatively simpler Seven-Tails can be handled by Kakuzu’s team instead. How about it ? »

Asakawa Shin refused instantly.

The Seven-Tails Jinchuriki, Fu, was a prey he had his eye on long ago. How could he hand her over to someone like Kakuzu, who had no sense of boundaries ?

« Fine, fine, leave it to me. This Akatsuki Organization would fall apart without me, » Asakawa Shin lamented.

Nagato breathed a secret sigh of relief. Sending Madara, whose information was already exposed, to carry out this mission was actually the most prudent choice at the moment.

If he were to act personally, the Akatsuki Organization’s deterrent power would surely rise exponentially.

By then, the entire Ninja World might scent the crisis and unite to resist the powerful enemy, which would instead lead to unnecessary complications.

« Then I’ll leave it to you. »

« Got it. »

Asakawa Shin impatiently cut off the Phantom Projection, and his consciousness instantly returned to his body.

He stood up unhappily, frowning slightly as he gazed into the distance.

« Iwagakure ? What a godforsaken place... »

He muttered in a low voice, his fingertips unconsciously stroking his chin. « But, it seems to be that Deidara fellow’s hometown. »

After a moment’s thought, a playful glint flashed in his eyes.

« What was that little fangirl of Deidara’s called ? Kurotsuchi, right ? Since I’m currently playing the role of Obito, it’s only reasonable to take her away while I’m at it. »
