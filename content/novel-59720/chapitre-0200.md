# Chapitre 200 — Hard to Kill

« Cough, cough, Granny, I’m fine ! »

Hearing that familiar voice, Chiyo’s hanging heart finally settled back into her chest.

She hurriedly bypassed that thick curtain wall of Iron Sand and rushed to support Sakura.

When she arrived, she was surprised to find that Kakashi and Sasuke had already appeared on the battlefield.

Without a doubt, it was they who had saved Sakura at the critical moment.

At this moment, Sakura couldn’t care less about the grim atmosphere of the battlefield ; her focus was entirely on Sasuke, her sparkling eyes filled with uncontrollable joy.

« Sasuke-kun, did you come specifically to save me ? As I thought... you still care about me, don’t you ? »

Sasuke merely raised a hand to wipe the cold sweat from his brow, his blunt tone shattering her fantasy.

« Don’t flatter yourself. Someone even more fierce showed up outside ; Kakashi-sensei and I couldn’t handle him, so we had to retreat here to take cover for a while. »

« Wha... what do you mean ? »

Beside them, Kakashi coughed lightly and began to explain.

It turned out that Naruto had been heavily provoked and had lost control, going on a rampage.

Three years ago, his good friend Asakawa Shin had vanished without a trace ; in the Ninja World, this was practically a death sentence.

And today, his dear friend Gaara had also been brutally killed.

That alone would have been bad enough, but the key was that Deidara fellow was acting despicably, repeatedly desecrating Gaara’s corpse.

The succession of blows completely shattered Naruto’s reason ; he could no longer suppress the resentment in his chest, and his Nine-Tailed Chakra instantly went berserk, attacking indiscriminately.

Unable to do anything about him for the moment, Kakashi and Sasuke had to leave a Shadow Clone behind to observe in secret, while their real bodies came to support the battlefield against Sasori, saving Sakura by chance.

« Is Naruto okay ? »

Sakura felt a bit worried upon hearing this ; the opponent was a tough character who had invaded the Hidden Sand Village and captured the Kazekage alive. Could Naruto handle him alone ?

« That guy Naruto is too fierce ; there’s no need to worry at all... »

Sasuke glanced coldly at Sakura, a hint of imperceptible irritation in his voice. « We should finish this quickly and find a way to seal Naruto as soon as possible. »

Chiyo also set aside her shock, her gaze becoming sharp once more. She looked at the three of them, who had solemn expressions, and said in a low voice :

« In that case, let’s not waste any time. The trouble in front of us is not any lighter than the one outside. »

Sasori stood high in the sky atop the Iron Sand, taking in their conversation. The mocking smile at the corners of his mouth grew even wider :

« What a touching display of classmate camaraderie. However, you seem to have misunderstood one thing. »

He suddenly spread his ten fingers, and the Human Puppets of the Third Kazekage and Fourth Raikage instantly took up attacking stances. The terrifying pressure of their Chakra made the air feel as if it had solidified.

« This is your burial ground, not a meeting room for discussing strategies. »

« Stop wasting words with this grandson ! Sakura, let’s go ! »

Chiyo shouted sharply, taking the lead by using Chakra Threads to control Sakura’s body and charge forward.

Since ordinary puppets were countered by Magnet Release, they would simply use a « Human Puppet » that could not be controlled by Magnet Release.

Sasuke and Kakashi exchanged a look, and with great tacit understanding, they split to the sides, flanking from the left and right.

« Chidori ! »

Sasuke formed a hand seal with one hand, and a dazzling lightning light lit up in his palm, pressing directly toward Sasori’s flank.

Kakashi pulled out a Kunai, his form vanishing like a ghost into the shadows of the Iron Sand.

« Overestimating yourselves. »

Sasori snorted coldly and crossed his hands in front of his chest with a sweep.

The Third Kazekage puppet behind him suddenly spat out a vortex of black Iron Sand, forming a shield in front of him that forcefully blocked Sasuke’s attack.

The Fourth Raikage Human Puppet rampaged about like a human-shaped dump truck, forcing everyone to scramble in defense.

The scene fell into a stalemate for a moment.

Sasori’s hands danced almost into afterimages, yet he still couldn’t take down these stubborn « ants, » causing his expression to grow increasingly dark.

Finally, he swung his left hand violently and shouted like thunder :

« Iron Sand World Order ! »

Before his voice could fade, the Third Kazekage puppet suddenly opened its mouth, and a massive torrent of pitch-black Iron Sand gushed out, freezing in mid-air before transforming into a sky full of sharp iron thorns that pierced down toward the ground !

Simultaneously, the Fourth Raikage Human Puppet’s left arm on the other side instantly deformed, a pitch-black cannon barrel extending out to spew a large amount of thick poison mist, blocking everyone’s retreat.

Kakashi and Chiyo, being further back, narrowly escaped the attack range, but Sasuke and Sakura, who were in the front, had nowhere to run !

Sasuke’s Sharingan spun rapidly, and his hands formed seals as fast as afterimages. In an instant, the dazzling electric light of Chidori covered his palm.

Under the effect of Lightning Release Chakra body activation, his speed soared to its limit. His figure flickered repeatedly, perilously weaving through the dense Iron Sand thorns.

For those attacks that were truly unavoidable, he simply swung his Chidori to meet them head-on, shattering the iron thorns.

On the other side, under Chiyo’s control, Sakura’s body twisted into bizarre poses like a marionette, miraculously avoiding the stabs of the iron thorns.

She had just struggled to her feet, her face still wearing the joy of survival, when her body suddenly went stiff.

A sudden sensation of numbness washed over her like ice water, instantly devouring her strength.

Looking down, she discovered that a small wound had been sliced into her shoulder at some unknown point.

As blood slowly seeped out, her vision went dark, and she collapsed to the ground.

« Finally got you ! You really are hard to kill. Didn’t expect that, did you ? My Iron Sand also contains poison ! »

Sasori’s gaze suddenly sharpened. With a slight movement of his fingertips, the Fourth Raikage Human Puppet turned around, and thick poison mist quickly enveloped Sakura.

Sakura cursed inwardly, 'Damn it, why does this redhead keep targeting me !'

She was resentful, but her hands were not slow at all.

Just before the poison mist could swallow her, she held her breath and quickly pulled out an antidote from her thigh, injecting it into her body.

Fortunately, while treating Kankuro earlier, she had successfully deciphered this toxin ; otherwise, she might have truly kicked the bucket right now.

In just an instant, the medicine took effect.

She suddenly stood up and charged toward the Fourth Raikage Human Puppet with lightning speed.

The thick poison mist obscured his vision, and Sasori was completely unaware, still immersed in his absolute confidence in his poison techniques.

He never dreamed that someone could decipher his toxin so quickly.

In a flash, a vigorous figure burst through the poison mist, carrying immense force, and a heavy punch imbued with « Enhanced Strength » slammed fiercely into the Fourth Raikage puppet’s left arm.

With a loud « boom, » the core device spewing the poison mist instantly shattered, and the heavy puppet body was sent flying by the terrifying strength, crashing straight toward Kakashi’s direction like a kite with a broken string.

« Well done, Sakura. »

Kakashi understood perfectly, his hands moving rapidly as he used Lightning Blade.

He transformed into a bolt of lightning, the Lightning Blade in his hand acting like a scalpel, precisely cutting the Chakra Threads linked to the Human Puppet.

The Human Puppet, having lost its source of power, instantly went limp as if its batteries had been removed, falling heavily to the ground without a sign of life.

Kakashi breathed a sigh of relief. « Great, we got it. »

« Is that so ? »
