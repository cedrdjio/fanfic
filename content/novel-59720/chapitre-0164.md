# Chapitre 164 — Izanagi

Danzo stared fixedly at Uchiha Itachi opposite him, a flash of greed crossing his eyes.

Fugaku’s Mangekyo Sharingan ability was not combat-oriented, but this wicked brat... might be a different story.

Seeing Itachi actually clash head-on with Susanoo, the corners of Danzo’s mouth curled into a cold, sinister smile.

Although he didn’t know the specific reason, his Mangekyo Sharingan ocular power was recovering automatically, so there was no need to worry about the risk of blindness.

Looking at Itachi opposite him, how much longer could he hold out ?

Thinking of this, his eyes gleamed with malice, and he intensified his offensive without any hesitation.

Uchiha Itachi’s expression was grim, and he was secretly shocked : « This old man’s strength is too exaggerated. He isn’t a member of the Uchiha clan, yet he can utilize the Mangekyo Sharingan to such an extent ; I truly underestimated him. »

Itachi felt a splitting pain in his eyes ; his Mangekyo Sharingan power was depleting rapidly, and his vision was beginning to blur. There was no time for him to think.

« Damn it, I must end this quickly. »

As his thoughts raced, his Susanoo underwent a sudden transformation, rapidly growing muscles and meridians, and its size expanded significantly.

It wasn’t over yet ; a set of archaic Tengu armor appeared out of thin air, fitting perfectly over the Susanoo giant.

The Susanoo became even more solid and massive, and a terrifying aura that shook the entire battlefield erupted, instantly drawing everyone’s attention.

Seeing this, Danzo was shocked and hurriedly activated his Mangekyo Sharingan.

However, his mastery of ocular jutsu was clearly inferior to Itachi’s. His Susanoo had only just grown muscles and meridians, advancing to the second stage.

A cold glint flashed in Itachi’s eyes as he launched a preemptive strike.

The Susanoo warrior took a massive gourd from its waist, coldly aiming it at Danzo.

Immediately after, under Danzo’s horrified gaze, a dazzling light shot out from the mouth of the gourd, instantly piercing through the Susanoo’s defense and punching a massive, bloody hole directly through Danzo’s chest.

That wasn’t all ; an eerie, unfathomable sealing power spread out instantly, drawing Danzo toward the solidified blade.

Danzo gasped in shock : « This effect... could it be the legendary Totsuka Blade ? »

Seeing he was in a desperate situation, there was not a hint of fear on Danzo’s face ; instead, his expression grew even greedier.

Itachi frowned slightly, feeling completely puzzled.

But seeing that Danzo had been sealed into the Totsuka Blade, he eased his mind slightly, suppressed his doubts, and retracted his Susanoo.

The next moment, his legs felt weak, his body swayed, and he nearly collapsed to the ground.

A piercing pain shot through his eyes, and warm blood-tears uncontrollably trickled down from the corners of his eyes.

He gasped for air, the corners of his mouth curling into a bitter, self-deprecating smile : « It seems activating the third stage of Susanoo is still a bit too much. »

Just then, Sasuke’s anxious roar suddenly rang out : « Brother, watch out behind you ! »

Itachi’s hair stood on end, and he instinctively activated the initial form of Susanoo again.

« Bang ! »

A massive Wind Release jutsu slammed violently against the skeletal frame ; the seemingly sturdy defense instantly cracked with countless fissures, nearly shattering.

Itachi turned his head in shock to see Danzo standing unscathed not far away, a hint of regret on his face.

He cast a cold glance at Sasuke in the distance, his tone chilling : « As expected of the naturally evil Uchiha brats. Don’t worry, once I finish off your brother, you’ll be next. »

Itachi furrowed his brow, his heart churning with shock : « How is this possible ? The Totsuka Blade’s seal actually failed ? How did you dodge it ? »

Before Danzo could answer, he had already discovered the truth : « So that’s it, it’s Izanagi... How dare you ! »

Danzo’s right arm was exposed, revealing ten Three-Tomoe Sharingan arranged on it, one of which had completely lost its luster, turning into a dead, gray-white color.

Itachi and Sasuke both felt a chill shoot straight to their scalps, the flames of rage instantly consuming their reason.

That bastard Danzo had actually used the clansmen’s Sharingan as expendable materials to be trampled upon ; such behavior was simply unforgivable !

On the other side of the battlefield, Uchiha Shisui and the Eight-Tails Jinchuriki Killer Bee, known as the « Killer Bee, » were also locked in battle.

Killer Bee brandished his seven ninja swords, his movements as fast as a ghost, and his attack trajectories were unpredictable. Even Shisui’s Mangekyo Sharingan struggled to fully track him, leading to several near-death situations.

What was even more troublesome was that his pride-and-joy genjutsu had little effect on Killer Bee, causing only brief delays that would be forcibly broken the next second by the Eight-Tails, with whom he shared a mental connection.

Fortunately, Shisui was proficient in the Body Flicker Technique, so he could barely hold his own against the opponent.

Killer Bee was likewise wary of Shisui’s Mangekyo Sharingan and did not dare to easily enter full Tailed Beast mode.

Compared to the intense clash over there, the battle between Tsunade and Orochimaru seemed somewhat strange.

Although Orochimaru was controlling the two Edo Tensei bodies of Senju Hashirama and Senju Tobirama, he was unable to take down Tsunade.

Obito, hiding in the shadows, furrowed his brow. Even with his low intelligence, he sensed that something was wrong at this moment.

The battlefield where Orochimaru, in whom the Allied Forces had placed high hopes, had failed to achieve a breakthrough for a long time.

This couldn’t help but make people wonder if Orochimaru had harbored ulterior motives all along, intending to betray his allies.

Raikage Ai was extremely annoyed. He should have been rampaging across the battlefield, but he was blocked by a weirdo in a green bodysuit and unable to break through.

Even though he had activated his Lightning Release Chakra Mode, the opponent could still contend with him.

Whether in speed or strength, this man named Might Guy was at a top-tier level. Relying on his astonishing taijutsu, he held him firmly in place, preventing him from breaking away to support other battlefields.

Meanwhile, Gaara was also feeling incredibly frustrated.

As the Konoha Crush plan unfolded, he had intended to unleash his full power and finish off Uchiha Sasuke himself.

However, the timely arrival of Jiraiya and Uzumaki Naruto completely disrupted his plans.

Seeing that his revenge was hopeless, he made a prompt decision to fully transform into the Tailed Beast.

In an instant, his body expanded rapidly, and sand filled the sky, erupting from within him.

The dust slowly cleared, and a towering sand beast appeared. It was entirely forged from sand, with strange black sealing runes wrapped around its surface.

Accompanied by a heaven-shaking roar, the One-Tail Shukaku finally reappeared in the world !

As the first Jinchuriki to fully transform into a Tailed Beast on the battlefield, the terrifying pressure it released instantly swept across the surroundings.

Jiraiya shook his head helplessly ; the opponent assigned this time was truly exceptionally troublesome.

The next moment, he turned to glance at Naruto and mocked him mercilessly : « Naruto, look closely ! This is the correct way to use the Summoning Jutsu, not like the embarrassing display you made during the match. »

Naruto turned beet red with anger, staring daggers at Jiraiya as if he wanted to burn a hole through his teacher, hoping only that he would make a complete fool of himself.

Jiraiya snorted coldly, decisively bit his fingertip, smeared the blood on his arm, and then formed hand seals at lightning speed, his hands moving like blurs.

« Ninja Art : Summoning Jutsu ! »

With a loud « bang, » thick white smoke instantly burst open.

Naruto and Jiraiya’s figures suddenly rose high, and beneath their feet, a massive toad was summoned.

The toad had a pipe hanging from the corner of its mouth and a giant blade tucked aggressively at its waist ; it was none other than Jiraiya’s signature summon—Gamabunta.

Gamabunta narrowed his eyes, caught a glimpse of Shukaku opposite him, and couldn’t help but sigh.

« Hey, Jiraiya... why is it another one of these troublesome guys ? »
