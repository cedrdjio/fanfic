# Chapitre 221 — The Six Paths of Pain

« Big Sis ! »

Kurotsuchi’s eyes widened in disbelief.

Just a moment ago, Konan had the absolute upper hand, looking down on the entire field. How could she have been defeated in the blink of an eye ?

She hadn’t expected this weird uncle’s strength to be so unfathomable !

She instinctively wanted to rush forward to help, but was pulled back by Asakawa Shin.

« What are you going up there for ? To die ? »

« Big Brother, hurry and save Big Sis ! She’s about to be taken away ! »

Asakawa Shin looked over at the sound and saw that Konan had already been tied up like a zongzi by Jiraiya, with sealing talismans stuck to her body. Currently, that fellow was carrying her on his shoulder and sprinting toward the outskirts of the village.

Asakawa Shin remained unmoved by this, but someone else couldn’t sit still.

A figure slowly drifted down from high in the sky, blocking the path Jiraiya had to take to escape.

Jiraiya looked at the newcomer with uncertainty and shock. « You’re Yahiko ? Why do you have the Rinnegan ? Where is Nagato ? »

Hearing this, a flash of grief crossed the eyes of Konan, who was being carried on his shoulder. This subtle change in expression was keenly captured by Jiraiya.

His voice trembled as he asked, « Nagato... is he dead ? Did he entrust his eyes to you before he died ? »

However, the « Yahiko » before him was expressionless, those Rinnegan eyes hollow and devoid of any emotional ripples.

Jiraiya stared at him blankly.

He truly couldn’t connect this puppet-like figure before him with the high-spirited, cheerful, and confident youth Yahiko from back then.

Just what kind of despair had he experienced to turn a person into this ?

Jiraiya took a deep breath, forcibly suppressing the stormy waves in his heart.

The successive appearance of Akatsuki Organization members had already confirmed that the Hidden Rain Village was their home base.

No matter what, this intelligence must be sent out today !

« Yahiko, move aside ! »

The Deva Path in front finally spoke hoarsely, « Jiraiya-sensei, do not make any more futile struggles. Please... lose your life here today. »

Jiraiya’s expression was grave to the extreme.

Yahiko was intercepting him in front, and he didn’t know how many more Akatsuki Organization members were rushing over from behind.

And looking to the side, Asakawa Shin was floating in mid-air with Kurotsuchi, leisurely watching the show.

Jiraiya cursed inwardly : 'How come all four of these Akatsuki Organization members can fly ? Is this right ? This isn’t right !'

As his thoughts raced, Deva Path Yahiko had already closed in.

Facing Jiraiya who was carrying Konan on his back, he was inevitably somewhat constrained in his attacks, only using basic Taijutsu for feints and entanglement.

But his true intention was to stall for time, waiting for the rest of the Six Paths puppets to arrive as reinforcements.

Jiraiya saw through the situation at a glance, a smile curling at the corner of his mouth.

With a leap backward, he quickly shifted Konan’s position, tying her firmly to his back.

Then, he bit his fingertip, his hands forming seals like phantoms, and slammed them hard against the ground.

« Ninjutsu : Summoning Jutsu ! »

« Poof ! »

As the white smoke cleared, two small toads appeared in the center of the battlefield. They were the Sage couple who had cultivated on Mount Myōboku for eight hundred years—Fukasaku and Shima Sage.

As soon as Shima appeared, she complained dissatisfiedly, « Little Jiraiya, I was just about to make dinner. Why did you call us here in such a hurry ? »

Fukasaku suddenly poked her with his elbow and lowered his voice, « Old woman, something big has happened ! Look across ! »

« Oh ? That’s... the Rinnegan ? The Dojutsu of the legendary Six Paths Sage ! Looking closely, this person... isn’t he Little Jiraiya’s disciple ? »

Jiraiya nodded solemnly and explained quickly, « They are the Akatsuki Organization that has been running rampant in the Ninja World recently. The situation is urgent, there’s no time to explain in detail. Please help me enter Sage Mode quickly, Elders ! »

Fukasaku and Shima had clearly realized the seriousness of the matter.

The two Toad Sages glanced at each other, flickered onto Jiraiya’s shoulders, quickly clasped their hands to concentrate, and began to gather Natural Energy.

Deva Path Yahiko looked at this scene suspiciously.

Although he didn’t know Jiraiya’s specific intention, he knew well that « whatever the enemy desires, I must prevent. »

He immediately set aside his hesitation, his offensive becoming sharp and efficient, forcing Jiraiya to scramble and dodge awkwardly.

At this moment, Jiraiya had his hands clasped to maintain the seal and couldn’t spare a hand to counterattack. With the burden of Konan on his back, his movements were even more restricted.

He couldn’t help but complain, « Konan, you’re way too heavy ! It seems you really need to lose some weight ! »

Konan, on his back, was flushed with anger. Unfortunately, with her Chakra sealed, she couldn’t speak to retort and could only grit her teeth inwardly.

She wasn’t originally that heavy, but unfortunately, her body was stained with a large amount of Toad Oil, making her feel quite a bit heavier at this moment !

At this critical juncture, the reinforcements the Deva Path was waiting for finally arrived.

A giant summoned bird flew across the sky, and a young girl wearing a black cloak with red clouds jumped down lightly from it.

Jiraiya didn’t pay much attention at first, but with a casual glance, he was shocked to find that the girl’s eyes were also shimmering with the eerie Rinnegan.

« What ? Another pair of Rinnegan ! »

However, his shock was clearly premature.

The moment the girl landed, her hands formed seals like flying, and she slammed them onto the ground.

« Ninjutsu : Summoning Jutsu ! »

As the dust cleared, four more Ninjas who also possessed the Rinnegan appeared.

Jiraiya’s face turned ashen, and his mindset completely collapsed. « How are there Rinnegan everywhere ? Impossible ! Absolutely impossible ! They’re all fake ! Don’t you dare try to scare me ! »

Fukasaku Sage reminded him helplessly, « Little Jiraiya, it’s real. Those are truly the Rinnegan. You’re not mistaken ! »

Shima Sage also followed up to confirm, « That’s right. The Chakra of us three is now linked as one. All of us in a Genjutsu at the same time ? The probability of that is infinitesimal. »

« Six pairs of Rinnegan ? This is simply unheard of ! »

In a moment of hesitation, Jiraiya was already surrounded by the Six Paths of Pain.

Out of concern for the safety of Konan on his back, the Six Paths puppets did not use large-scale destructive Ninjutsu, but merely by relying on Taijutsu coordination and shared vision, they had already pushed Jiraiya into a desperate situation.

« Bang ! »

A heavy punch smashed hard into Jiraiya’s chest, accompanied by a tooth-aching sound of bone cracking as a rib snapped.

The pain was piercing. Jiraiya groaned, his body being knocked back like a kite with a broken string.

Seeing that his back was about to hit the wall behind him, the Deva Path extended his right hand, preparing to use « Banshō Ten’in » to pull Jiraiya in, so as not to affect Konan on his back.

However, Jiraiya suddenly flipped his body in mid-air, actually hitting the wall head-on, smashing a large crater, and then spitting out a mouthful of blood.

The violent shock almost made him release his hands, which had been maintained in a clasped posture.

But he soon grinned with his bloody mouth, revealing a playful smile. « Konan, was Teacher handsome just now ? You must be moved to tears, right ? »

Konan, on his back, originally felt a trace of warmth in her heart, but upon hearing this, her face instantly turned cold as frost, her heart filled with shame and annoyance.

'Jiraiya-sensei is too shameless. He should just go die !'

Deep within the high tower, Nagato’s face appeared exceptionally pale in the dim shadows, and a flash of reluctance and struggle seemed to pass by.

He closed his eyes, and when he opened them again, his gaze had returned to dead silence. The speed of his hand seals never faltered as he controlled the Six Paths of Pain to close in for the kill from all directions.
