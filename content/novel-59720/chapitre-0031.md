# Chapitre 31 — Great Sales

« Lord Daimyo, ten million ryo per bolt ? You want fifty bolts ? » Nara Shikaku was shocked to hear this ; this time he wasn’t faking it, he was genuinely stunned by the Daimyo.

Originally, he had planned to quote one million ryo per bolt and see how the nobles reacted, lowering the price if they found it too expensive.

He never expected that the Daimyo would increase his asking price tenfold, reaching ten million ryo per bolt. And the Daimyo even thought that was just slightly expensive, placing an order for fifty bolts right away—that meant five hundred million ryo ? It was simply inhumanly wealthy !

'Damn it, I quoted too low.' Shikaku felt incredibly annoyed. He had underestimated the spending power of these nobles. But since things had reached this point, he could only tearfully accept the profit.

The Daimyo frowned slightly and said, « That’s right, I want fifty bolts. What ? Are you afraid I can’t afford it ? »

« Forget it, you Ninjas are truly small-minded. I won’t make things difficult for you. Go with my attendant to the treasury later and collect the five hundred million ryo. Now you won’t have to worry about the woven goods not selling, will you ? »

Nara Shikaku quickly lowered his head, fearing that if he were too slow, the Daimyo would see the smile at the corner of his mouth. After pinching himself hard, he said, « Apologies, Lord Daimyo. That is not what I meant. I only meant that there is currently no stock of wood brocade, and we can only accept pre-orders. However, I believe that if it is for you, Hokage-sama will surely mobilize the entire strength of Konoha to weave it for you with all our might, so you will not have to wait too long. »

The Daimyo’s expression improved slightly, though he still seemed a bit displeased. He said impatiently, « So there is no stock of wood brocade and it needs to be pre-ordered. What about those ordinary silks ? Surely those don’t need to be pre-ordered too, right ? »

« Reporting to Lord Daimyo, while ordinary silk is also difficult to weave, it is far less challenging than wood brocade. If you do not require a large amount, I believe we can deliver it very soon. »

The Daimyo was overjoyed to hear this. Although he was a bit sad not to get the wood brocade immediately, it would be quite good if he could obtain a large amount of silk.

Ever since he had seen the magnificence of silk and felt its smooth, soft texture, he found the fabrics around him vulgar and unbearable. Even the clothes he was wearing felt extremely rough, making him feel uncomfortable all over.

« Hahaha, excellent ! Then give me one thousand bolts first—no ! Give me two thousand bolts of silk first. I want to replace all the fabrics in the Daimyo’s mansion with silk. The materials used for those curtains, table mats, bed sheets, and quilts are truly vulgar and unbearable. Only this elegant and luxurious silk is worthy of my status. »

« Right, quickly summon the tailor here. Use the silk to make clothes for me immediately ; I want to wear them tomorrow morning. Go ! »

After announcing his orders, the Daimyo slowly calmed down. He walked up to Shikaku, patted him lightly on the shoulder, and said, « You Konoha folks have done well this time. Speak, what reward do you want ? »

Shikaku replied respectfully, « Reporting to Lord Daimyo, Konoha presenting this treasure is merely to repay your kindness. We never thought of asking for any reward, so please withdraw your command ! »

The Daimyo was touched. « I appreciate your filial piety, but since I have already issued the order, how can I withdraw it ? Besides, if merit is not rewarded, how can I convince the masses ? Let’s do this : that Assistant to the Hokage came to request financial funding last time, and I didn’t approve it. But now I agree. This time, you can take the financial funding he applied for with you ! By the way, how much did he apply for ? »

A noble nearby whispered a reminder, « Lord Daimyo, that bandaged man applied for one hundred million ryo in financial funding. »

« Only one hundred million ryo ? » The Daimyo repeated, then continued, « One hundred million is too little. I’ll double it for you. Take two hundred million ryo back to Konoha. Do not decline any further ! »

Shikaku smiled helplessly and had no choice but to cup his fists and reply, « Then thank you very much, Lord Daimyo. Konoha will never be able to repay your kindness in a lifetime ! »

« Hmm, don’t mention kindness again. Delivering those two thousand bolts of silk to me as soon as possible is the best repayment for me. By the way, calculate the cost of the wood brocade and silk and go to the treasury to collect it yourself. I have other matters to attend to, so I will take my leave and won’t keep you. »

Seeing that Shikaku accepted, the Daimyo smiled with satisfaction and turned to leave. He wanted to go to the tailor to see how far along the clothes were.

After the Daimyo left, the numerous nobles immediately swarmed around. With the Daimyo here earlier, many things were hard to say, but now was the perfect opportunity.

« Ninja, I also want to custom order ten bolts of wood brocade, and two hundred bolts of silk. »

« I want some too. Do you have any stock ? I can pay extra. »

« Do you accept custom patterns for the wood brocade ? I want to incorporate my family crest into it... »

The surroundings were chaotic, and Shikaku couldn’t hear what these nobles were saying at all. He had to press his hands in the air a few times, signaling everyone to quiet down.

Once everyone calmed down, Shikaku replied, « My lords, as you all know, weaving silk is not easy, and we do not have that much stock. The quantity required by Lord Daimyo is also very large, so we cannot supply that much silk for the time being, let alone sell it to others. »

Upon hearing that there was no silk for sale, the many nobles immediately started to make a fuss. Frightened, Shikaku quickly said, « I have understood all of your requests. How about this : after I return, I will request that Hokage-sama increase production scale, striving to complete Lord Daimyo’s order as soon as possible. »

« At the same time, we will open a shop in the city center of the Capital of the Land of Fire, specifically for selling silk. You only need to pay a ten percent deposit to pre-order silk at the shop, and we will ship the goods in the order of the reservations. »

« However, considering that silk production is not high, each family is limited to ordering ten bolts of wood brocade and one hundred bolts of silk, striving to let everyone obtain their desired silk as soon as possible. »

Seeing that they really couldn’t buy silk right now, the nobles had no choice but to grit their teeth and accept Shikaku’s suggestion. Before leaving, they repeatedly urged them to increase production and notify them before the shop opened, only then leaving reluctantly. Each of them looked at Shikaku with eyes full of longing, like a lonely housewife.

After seeing off the nobles, Shikaku followed the Daimyo’s personal attendant to the treasury to collect the money.

Fifty bolts of wood brocade, ten million ryo per bolt, totaling five hundred million ryo.

Two thousand bolts of silk, two hundred thousand ryo per bolt, totaling four hundred million ryo.

The two hundred million ryo in financial funding promised by the Daimyo.

A grand total of one billion one hundred million ryo.

After receiving the money, Shikaku still couldn’t believe it. Just the silk order from the Daimyo of the Land of Fire actually brought in so much money, a full nine hundred million ryo, almost catching up to the financing amount of the Musi Group.

Doesn’t that mean they are almost breaking even ?

If they also completed the orders from those nobles, they would probably be able to repay the funds owed for purchasing a large number of mulberry trees, and there would still be a lot left over. Excluding the slight labor costs, the rest would be pure profit.

And this was only the first procurement order from the Land of Fire. The market in the Land of Fire was far from saturated, not to mention the other four great nations and all the small countries in the Ninja World.

'My Nara family is really going to be rich now !'
