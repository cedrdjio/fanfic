# Chapitre 9 — The Six Styles Training Class

After school the next day, at Training Ground Five.

All the students of Class 1-1 gathered here because Asakawa Shin had promised to personally teach them the Konoha Six Styles.

Before school today, their parents had repeatedly instructed them to study hard.

The commoner Ninjas, in particular, felt as if they had hit the jackpot, and everyone’s enthusiasm for learning was high—with the exception of a certain pineapple-head.

« Tch... what a drag. »

« Shikamaru, what are you muttering about ? Start running. Uncle Shikaku told me to keep a close eye on you yesterday.

Though, with how smart you are, I don’t think I really need to watch you, right ? »

Last night at school, Shikamaru’s father, Nara Shikaku, had approached him privately to request that he train Shikamaru thoroughly, giving him permission to scold or even hit him, and even gave him a large red envelope.

« I know, that’s why I’m saying, it’s a total drag, » Shikamaru said listlessly with his dead-fish eyes.

...

After the warm-up run, Asakawa Shin gathered everyone together and said after a moment’s thought :

« It’s still too early for you to practice the Six Styles. Most of you are still in the stage of physical conditioning. I will design a set of training methods, and you must follow them strictly.

Shikamaru, I officially appoint you as the Class Monitor of the Six Styles Special Training Class, responsible for coordinating all training matters.

The rest of the members will be divided into two groups : the Boys' Group and the Girls' Group. Naruto will be the group leader for the boys, and Ino will be the group leader for the girls.

The group leaders are responsible for managing their members, assisting the Class Monitor with training, as well as handling attendance, signing out, and leave requests.

Of course, this special training class is not mandatory. You can quit at any time. Does anyone have any questions ? »

« I do ! Why does an idiot like Naruto get to be a group leader ? I, Uchiha Sasuke, am the first to disagree ! »

Sasuke immediately raised his hand and shouted. Ever since Shin announced Naruto as the Boys' Group leader, that bastard Naruto had been winking and grinning maliciously at him.

If he actually became the group leader, wouldn’t that be the end of it ? He’d definitely find ways to make life difficult for him.

Seeing someone start the charge, Inuzuka Kiba immediately followed suit. No joke, the position of group leader sounded prestigious and low-effort ; it was only right that Lord Kiba should take it.

« I think Sasuke is right. The position of group leader is crucial. It should be held by someone more mature, stable, and better able to unite the classmates !

Don’t you agree, Akamaru ? »

The palm-sized, barely-furred puppy on his head echoed, « Woof ! »

Meaning Kiba was right, and he voted for Kiba !

While the Boys' Group was in an uproar, Sakura Haruno on the girls' side also looked conflicted.

She wanted her best friend to do well, but she didn’t want her to do *too* well.

Over the past two days, her best friend Ino Yamanaka had been riding high, her relationship with Asakawa Shin getting along smoothly and warming up by the day.

Now that she was also going to be the Girls' Group leader, wouldn’t she have a harvest in both her career and her love life ?

Looking at herself, Sasuke only thought about competing with Naruto every day and barely paid her any attention.

If I become the Girls' Group leader, wouldn’t I be the most outstanding girl in class ? Then Sasuke-kun would look at me in a new light !

Sakura Haruno was very anxious now, extremely anxious.

But Ino was, after all, her beloved close friend and sister-in-arms.

Regardless of Sakura’s inner conflict, Hyuga Hinata was also feeling restless.

The Boys' Group leader is Naruto-kun. If I become the Girls' Group leader, wouldn’t we be a perfect match ?

Rounding that up... wouldn’t that mean... getting married ?

Hiss... Just thinking about it made Hinata so excited she almost fainted.

'No... I can’t. I can’t fall. Naruto-kun is waiting for me !'

Hinata forced herself to stay upright with immense willpower. Her body swayed, but the fire in her eyes burned fiercely.

Hearing the rebellion and backstabbing from his subordinates, Naruto was instantly enraged :

« Bastard Sasuke, bastard Kiba, bastard puppy ! Shin is my best brother. If I don’t be the group leader, who can ? »

« The group’s affairs are mine to handle ! I shall do it today, who dares disobey ? »

Seeing that things were about to get chaotic, Shin said helplessly, « Alright, alright, that’s enough. Everyone, listen to me.

Ahem, everyone listen. Let’s do this : the group leader candidates are tentatively Naruto and Ino.

We’ll have an assessment in one month. Whoever has the best training results will be the group leader. After that, we’ll have a competition every month—the capable rise and the mediocre fall. How’s that ? »

The commotion was finally quelled. Shin hadn’t originally taken the selection of group leaders seriously ; after all, it was just an extracurricular study class.

He hadn’t expected these little kids to have such a strong desire for power that they almost started fighting.

But this was good too. Pressure creates motivation. Now he wouldn’t even have to watch them ; they’d practice extra on their own.

The self-cultivation of a combat Palu.

Wait, Shin noticed a blind spot. Why was everyone fighting for group leader, but no one was fighting for Shikamaru’s position as Class Monitor ?

« By the way, since the selection method for group leaders is settled, does anyone have any objections to the candidate for Class Monitor ? »

« No ! Shikamaru is both virtuous and talented, with outstanding ability. He’s the natural choice for Class Monitor. If anyone objects, I, Uchiha Sasuke, will be the first to disagree. »

What a joke. Sasuke only refused to submit to Naruto and wanted to suppress him. The duties of the Class Monitor sounded like a huge hassle ; he was more than happy to let Shikamaru be the scapegoat.

« Yeah, yeah, I’m also fine with Shikamaru being the Class Monitor. »

« Woof ! » Akamaru also indicated that Shikamaru was his good buddy and he would definitely support him.

Ino didn’t say anything to avoid suspicion. She didn’t want people to think she got her position through nepotism.

Choji, however, expressed that as the Ino–Shika–Chō trinity, Shikamaru being the Class Monitor was as good as him being the Class Monitor, and he would fully support Shikamaru.

Ino : « ... »

Asakawa Shin led them through physical training and found that everyone’s physical fitness was generally higher than that of students in the same grade. This class could be called an elite class.

Furthermore, this world did have Chakra, after all. Chakra could greatly enhance physical fitness in all aspects, and the enhancement of physical fitness could in turn stimulate Chakra growth.

Strictly speaking, practicing the Six Styles in this world was easier than in the world of One Piece.

However, even among classmates, the gap in physical fitness was enormous.

After finishing a set of physical training, some students looked as if nothing had happened, neither red-faced nor out of breath, while others were panting heavily and could barely walk.

« This beast. » Uchiha Sasuke gritted his teeth as he stared at Uzumaki Naruto.

On the surface, Sasuke looked calm and composed, but in reality, his heart was pounding wildly and his breathing rhythm was slightly erratic.

In contrast, Uzumaki Naruto was still jumping around. By the look of him, he could easily do another set.

At this moment, Sasuke somewhat understood the gap between him and Naruto. But so what ? I am Uchiha Sasuke. I’ll practice extra when I get home tonight !

'I will definitely outwork you, Naruto,' Sasuke secretly resolved.

Ino also felt a sense of crisis. She didn’t know what was up with that girl Hinata today, but she was training extremely hard and had a fierce look in her eyes ; her aura was very strong.

If that were all, it would be fine, but Hinata would glance at her from time to time and then train even harder.

Seeing Hinata like this, a bolt of lightning suddenly flashed through Ino’s mind, and she was startled. This girl wouldn’t be trying to usurp her power, would she ?

It was actually possible. Hinata practiced Gentle Fist Taijutsu, so her foundation was better than hers.

Not good, very not good.

Thinking of this, Ino suddenly felt someone staring at her from behind. Was there another expert ? She abruptly spun around...

'Phew... Oh, it’s just Sakura. I’m just too tense. Sakura is my best friend, after all.

It’s all because of Hinata, making me feel so much pressure.'

Sakura was also startled. She hadn’t expected Ino to be so sensitive ; she almost discovered her plan to revolt.

Fortunately, she was quick-witted and fooled Ino with a sweet smile. She breathed a sigh of relief and began training even harder.

Little did they know that all of this was being watched by a pair of Byakugan from behind.

Ino, Sakura, Hinata : 'This can’t go on. For Shin-kun/Sasuke-kun/Naruto-kun, I’ll practice extra tonight and outwork them all !'

...

« Shikamaru, you’ve noticed it too, right ? The training progress among the students is different. Go home tonight and draw up a detailed protocol and training plan ; I’ll check it tomorrow.

As the Class Monitor of the training class, you need to put in more effort. » Ignoring Shikamaru’s dead-fish eyes, Asakawa Shin gave him his instructions.

Even though he had accepted Uncle Shikaku’s red envelope and promised to look after Shikamaru, so what ? He was still going to use Shikamaru ruthlessly. Maybe Shikamaru would even thank him later.

Getting two uses out of one deer, jie jie jie~

At midnight, Shikaku looked at his son, who was writing furiously, and nodded with satisfaction.

Everything about his son was good—he was talented and intelligent—but he was too lazy, which worried him and his wife greatly. Now, seeing his son working so hard, he felt immensely relieved.

I’m so grateful to little Shin. It seems I’ll have to prepare another big red envelope to thank him later.

Shikamaru : « Speak up for me ! »
