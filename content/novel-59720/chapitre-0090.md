# Chapitre 90 — Killer Bee

Meanwhile, outside the Hidden Cloud Village.

Asakawa Shin’s three Shadow Clones were waiting in full battle array when one of them suddenly reported with excitement :

« I have sensed that hundreds of elite Ninja have left the Hidden Cloud Village, but the Two-Tails Jinchuriki is not in this group ; she is still stationed within the village and has not left.

It seems the time has come. I will now dispel my Shadow Clone to transmit the intelligence to my main body. You must keep a close watch on the movements of the Two-Tails Jinchuriki, and be careful not to alert the enemy. »

« Poof ! »

Valley of Clouds and Lightning.

Asakawa Shin sat leisurely on a mountain peak, admiring the charming scenery around him, when suddenly his body paused slightly, and a smile appeared at the corner of his mouth.

« Oh ? Has the plan begun ? Then it is time for me to take action as well. »

He slowly stood up and quickly formed hand seals with both hands : « Wood Release : Wood Transformation ! »

In an instant, countless branches grew rapidly from beneath his feet, intertwining and wrapping tightly around his entire body, as if he were donning a suit of wooden armor.

With this miraculous change, his body gradually transformed into an adult form, and on his head, he wore a tiger-striped mask—it was exactly the current appearance of Obito.

Wood Transformation is a physical Transformation Technique, extremely realistic, and ordinary people would be unable to see through it.

« Very good, the preparations are complete. Now, let’s go and greet Killer Bee first. »

Asakawa Shin cast his gaze around and quickly locked onto a small mountain. He nodded with satisfaction : « Not bad, the shape looks just like a javelin. You’ll do ! »

After saying this, he dashed over at high speed and struck the foot of the mountain with a fierce punch, creating a crack that rapidly spread around the base.

Immediately after, he let out a loud shout, and with an explosion of divine strength, he forcibly snapped this small mountain along the crack and lifted it high above his head.

He took a deep breath, took a few simple steps for a run-up, and then swung his arm violently, throwing this giant javelin toward the mountain peak where Killer Bee was training, crashing down with a boom.

At this moment, Killer Bee had just finished training and was walking out while stretching : « The light is so blinding. Is it noon already ? Ahem...

Fool, you fool ! All you fans, listen to my rap ! Training since morning, my stomach is empty. I love to rap, and my rhythm never falters. Right now I just want to eat until dawn ! Yeah ! »

« Hmm ? What is blocking the sun ? »

He looked up and was shocked to see a mountain peak, carrying astonishing momentum, tearing through layers of air barriers, and flying directly toward his head.

« Nani ? ! »

« Bee ! You idiot, hurry up and dodge ! »

The Eight-Tails inside him cursed anxiously, Tailed Beast Chakra frantically surging into Killer Bee’s body, instantly activating his flesh and enhancing his muscles and reflexes.

Only then did Killer Bee react, fleeing for his life to the side using both his hands and feet.

The moment he used the Body Flicker Technique to dodge, the mountain peak crashed down with a boom, and the violent impact kicked up a fierce shockwave that swept in all directions.

Killer Bee, still in the air, was directly struck by this storm and sent flying far away. Fortunately, he had Eight-Tails Chakra protecting his body, so he did not suffer any serious injuries.

« What a thing, gave me a fright. Scared out of my wits, truly a funny sight. Unknown enemy, makes me scream. The current situation, a little extreme. Yeah ! »

Killer Bee wiped the cold sweat from his forehead and climbed out of the rubble in a disheveled state. He warily scanned his surroundings, searching for the enemy hidden in the shadows.

However, he didn’t have to search for long before Asakawa Shin stepped out from the shadows while clapping, praising him incessantly :

« As expected of Master Killer Bee, improvising a rap on the spot—it is truly admirable. »

Killer Bee’s eyes lit up, and he couldn’t help but say happily : « Hmm ? You’ve heard my songs too ? »

Asakawa Shin was slightly taken aback but quickly reacted and replied casually : « Yes, yes, I am your fan. That was just an offline tip. »

Seeing that Killer Bee seemed to want to continue chatting, Gyuki was frantic in his mental world, roaring crazily :

« Bee, you idiot ! He is obviously mocking you ! Get your act together, this guy gives me a very bad feeling, very bad ! »

Even though Gyuki said this, Killer Bee was still a bit hesitant. After all, besides his own disciples, he didn’t have many fans ; what if this guy really was a fan ? It would be better to confirm it.

« Then name three of my songs to prove yourself. »

« ? »

Asakawa Shin was silent for a moment, then decided to explain : « Actually, I am also a rapper. »

« Rua-nima, you fake fan ! Give me your life ! Fool, you fool ! »

Killer Bee was instantly enraged, drew the seven swords from his back, and charged at Asakawa Shin with a bizarre posture.

« Acrobat. »

Asakawa Shin was taken aback. Can he really perform this sword technique while in the Tailed Beast Chakra cloak state ?

Before he could be too surprised, Killer Bee had already transformed into a blur and closed in, the seven sharp blades in his hands swirling toward him like a violent storm.

'So fast, such bizarre movements ! It’s no wonder Sasuke could hardly fight back even with the Sharingan and was turned into a sieve. Unfortunately, you are facing me, someone with Observation Haki who can foresee the future. In this wave, I am crushing you with mechanics.'

If Killer Bee could be compared to an aggressive Killer Bee, Asakawa Shin was the calm dragon in the storm, avoiding all attacks with leisurely steps.

The more Killer Bee fought, the more alarmed he became. No matter how his moves changed, he couldn’t even touch a corner of the opponent’s clothes, as if his every move had already been foreseen by the opponent.

Just in that fleeting moment of distraction, Asakawa Shin suddenly gave a light shout : « Hand it over. »

In a flash of lightning, he forcibly snatched away one of the sharp swords that was spinning at high speed in his hand.

« Nani ? ! »

Killer Bee was shocked and hurriedly retreated, attempting to create some distance.

But Asakawa Shin had no intention of letting him go ; instead, he charged forward rapidly, wielding the sword, his figure fluttering like a butterfly, his attacks dense and seamless, forcing him into a continuous retreat.

'This guy has such high speed and such sharp swordsmanship. I can’t parry at all. Gyuki, come out quickly and help me block.'

Unfortunately, he talked too much. Before he could effectively communicate with Gyuki, Asakawa Shin seized the opening and instantly knocked all six of his swords away.

At this moment, Killer Bee was wide open with no defense. Asakawa Shin sneered and swung the sword down without hesitation. Killer Bee’s chest immediately gushed blood, and he flew backward like a kite with a broken string, crashing heavily into the river and kicking up a sky full of water.

Watching Killer Bee sink into the water, quickly dyeing the clear river into a blinding crimson, Asakawa Shin couldn’t help but yawn in boredom.

This guy really overestimated himself. Of all things to compete with him in, he had to choose close-quarters swordsmanship—this was completely his comfort zone ; he handled him with ease.

It didn’t take long for Asakawa Shin to wait before the river water began to boil violently, and a giant ox-headed octopus slowly emerged from it. With round, wide-open eyes, it stared fixedly at Asakawa Shin and roared angrily : « Fool, you fool ! This time, for sure, I will make you pay ! »
