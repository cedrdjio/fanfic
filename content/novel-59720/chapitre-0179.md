# Chapitre 179 — Super Generation Bump

« Who’s there ? »

Black Zetsu was shocked and instinctively wanted to use the Mayfly Technique to escape.

However, a terrifying Psychokinesis firmly pinned him in place, making him unable to move at all.

A hand landed lightly on his shoulder. « Third Brother, why are you running ? »

Only then did Black Zetsu hear the address. His body trembled violently, and he weakly defended himself, « Have you mistaken me for someone else ? I am... »

« Of course I know ! »

Before he could finish, Asakawa Shin cut him off, his voice rising as he recited with resonance.

« You are the third son of the Ancestor of Chakra and the All-Powerful Goddess—Ōtsutsuki Kaguya, Black Zetsu, right ? »

« You... you... »

Having his identity exposed in one word, Black Zetsu was struck as if by lightning, too shocked to even speak.

« Are you wondering how I know ? Hehe, it’s actually quite simple, because I am Kaguya’s youngest son, your own younger brother ! »

Hearing this, Black Zetsu’s entire body trembled violently. Seeing this, Asakawa Shin released his restraint at the right moment.

Black Zetsu turned around tremblingly and finally saw the face of the person behind him.

« You... you are Asakawa Shin ? Who on earth are you ? And how do you know Mother’s name ? »

Even now, Black Zetsu couldn’t believe that after lurking low for a thousand years, his identity would be seen through in an instant.

Asakawa Shin smiled slightly. « Third Brother, I’ve already said it. I am Mother’s fourth son, your younger brother. »

He felt secretly smug. If he could successfully claim this kinship, wouldn’t that be a total reversal of the natural order—a super generation bump ?

At that time, he would leap from being Ashura’s descendant of unknown generations to becoming Ashura’s own uncle...

While Asakawa Shin was fantasizing, Black Zetsu had calmed down.

Seeing that the other party had completely exposed even his secret of being Kaguya’s son, Black Zetsu simply stopped pretending.

After all, things had come to this ; there was no point in hiding anymore.

He immediately darkened his face and retorted sharply :

« Absolutely impossible ! Mother only had time to create me as the final contingency before being sealed. How could she have had the chance to give birth to you ? Stop talking nonsense ! »

Asakawa Shin unhurriedly shook his head and pointed at the flower on top of his head.

« Third Brother, do you recognize this ? This is the best proof of my identity ! »

He sighed softly and slowly explained, « Actually, I am the conscious incarnation of the Ten-Tails God Tree.

After Mother was sealed, Hagoromo feared that no one could suppress my power after his death, so he heartlessly split me into ten.

Nine of those parts developed independent consciousness and became the current Nine Tailed Beasts, while the final main body—the Gedo Statue—was sealed on the moon by him.

After a thousand years of gestation, the Gedo Statue, due to its deep longing for Mother and intense resentment over having its Chakra drained, finally birthed a consciousness—which is the current me. »

Hearing this, Black Zetsu’s body jolted, and he looked at Asakawa Shin in disbelief.

« The Ten-Tails is the God Tree, and also a part of Mother. Could it be... you really are... ? »

Seeing Black Zetsu wavering, Asakawa Shin smiled and struck while the iron was hot :

« Correct. Decades ago, for some reason, my main body, the Gedo Statue, escaped the moon, and I was freed along with it.

But because my Chakra was completely drained, I couldn’t move at all. It wasn’t until twelve years ago, inspired by the reincarnations of Ashura and Indra, that I learned the Reincarnation Technique to take human form.

It wasn’t until this year, when the God Tree flower on my head bloomed, that I remembered everything. »

Black Zetsu slapped his thigh and realized, « So that’s it ! Decades ago, Uchiha Madara awakened the Rinnegan and summoned the Gedo Statue from the moon, and that’s how you escaped ! »

The more he thought, the more excited he became, finally laughing at the sky, « Hahaha ! It turns out I have a younger brother !

Yes, you are the incarnation birthed from the Ten-Tails' main body, and the Ten-Tails was originally a part of Mother, so you indeed count as my younger brother !

After all... I wasn’t born from Mother in the traditional way either ! Looking closely, you really do resemble Mother a bit. »

Before he finished, he suddenly burst into loud wailing.

Asakawa Shin was stunned and couldn’t help scratching his head. « Third Brother, why are you crying ? »

« Brother, you don’t know how hard my life has been these past thousand years, boohoo... »

Black Zetsu sobbed while venting his grievances.

Looking at the black shadow crying like a pile of mud, Asakawa Shin sighed and comforted him :

« Third Brother, stop crying. The priority right now is to find a way to rescue Mother ! »

Hearing this, Black Zetsu’s crying stopped abruptly. He flickered back into human form and nodded repeatedly.

« Brother is right ! I was originally troubled by your existence, but now it seems, this is wonderful, hahaha ! »

However, Black Zetsu still had doubts and asked tentatively, « Brother, since you’ve reincarnated as a human, how much strength do you have left ? »

Asakawa Shin smiled slightly and warmly beckoned Black Zetsu, signaling him to enter his sleeve.

Then, he used the Five Elements Escape Technique to travel swiftly underground, heading straight for a deserted wilderness.

'Is this the Mayfly Technique ?'

Black Zetsu was slightly surprised, but then he felt relieved. As an incarnation of the Ten-Tails, it wasn’t surprising to possess such means.

Asakawa Shin floated lightly into the air, clapped his hands together, and a low shout echoed through heaven and earth : « Wood Style : Deep Forest Emergence ! »

In an instant, the earth trembled, and countless towering ancient trees erupted from the ground, transforming the wasteland into a lush forest in the blink of an eye.

Seeing this, Black Zetsu nodded thoughtfully. « Wood Release ? The God Tree mastering Wood Release is only logical. »

« Third Brother, there’s much more. Look closely. » Asakawa Shin smiled calmly and pointed into the distance.

The sky suddenly changed ; the once clear sky was instantly swallowed by dark clouds. Amidst thunder and lightning, torrential rain poured down like a waterfall.

Black Zetsu’s pupils shrank. He said in shock, « This... you can actually control the weather ? »

Asakawa Shin shook his head slightly, his tone tinged with regret. « In my complete form, I could harness Natural Energy to create natural disasters that sweep the world. Now, I can only simply control the weather. It’s truly shameful. »

'The Ten-Tails' ability, Cataclysm ? I have indeed heard of it...'

Black Zetsu believed Asakawa Shin’s words a bit more. Could the person before him really be his younger brother ?

Otherwise, how could he know so many secrets, and why would he help me so wholeheartedly ?

Moreover, the flower on his head was definitely not a fake. At such a close distance, Black Zetsu could clearly feel the power of Kekkei Mora gestating within the bud.

While Black Zetsu was thinking, Asakawa Shin suddenly raised his hand and aimed at a nearby small hill.

« Third Brother, although I haven’t yet manifested the Rinnegan, I can still perform some of its powers. Watch closely ! »

He clenched his palm and shouted in a low voice, « Bansho Ten’in ! »

A strange gravitational force appeared out of air, and the small hill actually rose from the ground and flew toward them at high speed.

Just as the hill reached them, Asakawa Shin’s hand gesture changed sharply. He pushed his palm outward and shouted, « Shinra Tensei ! »

« Boom— ! »

The small hill seemed to hit an invisible solid wall, instantly crumbling and disintegrating. Countless stones shot out in all directions like a rainstorm, which was incredibly shocking.

« This... this is the ability of the Rinnegan’s Deva Path ? »

Asakawa Shin nodded convincingly to Black Zetsu, but he was laughing inwardly.

'Fooled you. It’s actually Psychokinesis, my dear brother.'

By this point, Black Zetsu had no doubt left about Asakawa Shin’s identity.

Just as he was getting excited about this, he noticed that Asakawa Shin’s expression seemed a bit uneasy, as if he were preoccupied, which made him feel somewhat confused.

« What’s wrong, Brother ? » Black Zetsu asked with concern.

Asakawa Shin sighed. « Third Brother, I was thinking... My consciousness was born after Mother was sealed.

When Mother is freed, will she... will she refuse to recognize me as her son ? »

Black Zetsu was startled ; that really was a possibility...

But he quickly calmed down and patted his chest, promising, « Don’t be afraid ! With your Third Brother here, I will definitely explain everything to Mother and ensure she accepts you as her son ! »

« Haha, Third Brother, you really are a filial son. »
