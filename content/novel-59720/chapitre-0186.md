# Chapitre 186 — Journey to the Land of Snow

Inside the Hokage Office, Tsunade was propping her chin up with one hand, her head nodding as she dozed off.

Suddenly, a flash of green light flickered, and a figure appeared silently behind her.

« Tsunade-shijie, did you miss me ? »

Tsunade jolted awake, instinctively guarding her vitals, and it took her a long moment to snap out of it.

« Eh ? My panties didn’t disappear ? That means... »

She quickly turned around, and sure enough, the familiar figure from her memory was standing right there.

« Junior Brother ! »

Tsunade excitedly hugged Asakawa Shin, who had a face full of black lines, and said enthusiastically, « Did you successfully develop your lewd Ninjutsu ? Congratulations, congratulations ! »

Asakawa Shin rolled his eyes and pushed her away.

« Hmph ! This Ninjutsu of mine points directly to the Source of the Great Dao ; you simply don’t understand it, Shijie. »

As they were talking, Kakashi knocked and walked in.

« Oh, Kakashi is here. I have a mission for Team 7. » Without even turning her head, Tsunade casually handed over a scroll.

Kakashi expertly took the scroll and opened it to check. « An A-rank mission ? Protecting the actress Yukie Fujikaze ? »

Asakawa Shin was slightly startled and snatched the scroll to look at it closely.

« Hokage-sama, I want to go on this mission too. »

Tsunade narrowed her eyes and looked at Asakawa Shin suspiciously. « No way, Junior Brother, have you taken a liking to this movie star now ? Can’t you show some restraint ? »

Asakawa Shin’s face immediately flushed red, and his fists clenched with a cracking sound. « It’s not like that ! This mission is very dangerous ; I want to go to help Kakashi ! »

« Fine, fine, stop right there. Since you want to go, then go. However, Shizune and I are busy with official business and can’t accompany you. Just take Haku and Karin with you. »

Tsunade waved her hand helplessly, feeling utterly exhausted.

Over the past few days, envoys from Sunagakure and Kumogakure had been frequently visiting Konoha to discuss the ransom of their Jinchuriki captives. It was currently at the most critical bargaining stage, so she couldn’t spare any time.

Asakawa Shin was overjoyed. He cupped Tsunade’s cheeks, gave her a « mwah » kiss, and said with a beaming smile :

« I knew Tsunade-shijie was the best. Don’t worry, I’ll definitely protect Naruto and the others. »

This scene happened to be caught by Kakashi. His one visible eye instantly went wide, and even the Sharingan under his eyepatch almost started spinning from the shock.

Tsunade’s ears burned as she felt Kakashi’s dumbfounded stare. She cursed the brat inwardly for becoming increasingly disrespectful.

She was the dignified Hokage-sama, after all ; how could she let someone « attack » her so casually ?

« Enough, enough, you two get going and discuss the mission details ! » she shooed them away irritably.

Only after the two walked out of the Hokage Office did Kakashi’s expression suddenly change.

He grabbed Asakawa Shin’s arm and lowered his voice, asking urgently, « Shin, how did you do that ? Teach me, quick ! »

« Huh ? Do what ? »

Asakawa Shin was stunned by the question, his face blank with confusion.

Thinking he was playing dumb, Kakashi quickly shook the copy of Make-Out Paradise in his hand and pressed further :

« The techniques written in the book ! Like the Harmonious Harem, Workplace Taboos, and things like that... »

After hearing this, Asakawa Shin gave a disdainful sneer, shook off his hand, and turned to leave.

« What’s the use of telling you such things ? You wouldn’t be able to use them anyway. »

Kakashi froze on the spot. It took him a while to recover, feeling quite disgruntled : « If you won’t teach, then don’t teach. Why the personal attack ? »

Amidst his annoyance, he rubbed the Make-Out Paradise in his hand thoughtfully. « Since Jiraiya-sama wrote this book, he must be very experienced in these matters. It might be good to ask him later. »

The more he thought about it, the more it made sense. Since the mission wouldn’t start for three days, he might as well go find Jiraiya now.

However, after hearing Kakashi’s question, Jiraiya looked as if he had been struck by lightning. No matter how Kakashi called out to him, he remained unresponsive.

Kakashi had no choice but to leave dejectedly, his heart full of frustration.

Only after watching Kakashi’s figure completely disappear did Jiraiya suddenly snap out of it.

A sharp light flared in his eyes, and he felt a surge of inspiration. He immediately pulled out pen and paper and began writing furiously, finishing an entire new work in one go.

After finishing, he sighed softly with a solemn expression. « This soul-searching interrogation and pain must be conveyed to every reader who supports me. »

Three days later, the group boarded a ship bound for the Land of Snow and set sail.

The sea breeze was biting, making the sails snap and flutter.

Asakawa Shin stood at the bow, eyes closed as he felt the moisture in the air, his brow slightly furrowed.

« What’s wrong, Shin ? Are you seasick already ? »

Kakashi leaned against the railing, holding the manuscript copy of the new Make-Out Paradise book he had just gotten from Jiraiya, his tone carrying a hint of subtle resentment.

Asakawa Shin gave him a faint glance and spoke calmly, « The director just said I have a good image and great temperament, and he insisted on having me debut as an actor. I’m just hesitating. »

Hearing this, Kakashi rolled his eyes mercilessly and took a half-step back to keep a safe distance, afraid of being injured by the aura of pretentiousness.

Bored, Asakawa Shin strolled over to the stern where Naruto and the others were.

This group had actually set up a makeshift studio on the deck and were filming a scene on the spot.

The Ninjas, having never seen such a spectacle, were immediately captivated and gathered around to watch with great interest.

« What amazing acting ! » Sakura’s eyes sparkled with admiration.

Naruto also scratched his head, looking impressed. « It really is great. She’s like a completely different person from the Yukie-jie in real life ! »

Seeing this, the manager Sandayu showed a proud smile and stood tall as he introduced :

« This is Miss Yukie. Once she’s in front of the camera, her acting skills are second to none in this world ! »

Just as he finished speaking, the sound of Yukie repeatedly calling « Cut » came from the filming set ahead.

It turned out that for a scene requiring her to be heartbroken, she couldn’t cry at all. In the end, she had no choice but to use some eye drops to squeeze out a few tears.

This scene instantly shattered the mysterious filter the Ninjas had for actors.

The long and boring voyage neared its end amidst such banter.

Early that morning, a panicked shout broke the silence of the sea.

« Director, this is bad ! »

Everyone rushed to the bow upon hearing the shout, only to see the director staring ahead with a pale face, sweating profusely.

« This... what on earth is happening ? »

The Stagehand explained with a mournful face, « When we woke up, we found the sea route ahead completely frozen. The ship can’t get through. What should we do ? »

The director was stunned for a moment, then seemed to remember something. He stared fixedly at the distant cliffs covered in ice and snow, and a fanatical light gradually ignited in his eyes.

« This is it ! It’s perfect ! »

He suddenly turned around and roared excitedly at the crew behind him, « Everyone, prepare to land ! Change the script immediately ; this scene must be filmed here ! »

At that moment, Asakawa Shin walked out of the cabin, yawning lazily.

« Haku, your Ice Release takeout order has arrived. Please get ready to receive it. »
