# Chapitre 86 — Xuanyuan Group

Asakawa Shin woke up groggily in the morning, feeling incredibly warm all over. When his consciousness returned, he realized he was curled up in a woman’s arms, his little hand resting on her chest.

He subconsciously rubbed it, and before he could gather his wits, a teasing voice reached his ears : « How does it feel ? Not bad, right ? »

Asakawa Shin nodded naturally, but immediately realized something was wrong.

He jerked his head up and, sure enough, saw Tsunade looking at him with a gaze full of mockery.

A tempest raged in his heart, but he immediately suppressed his panic.

He knew he absolutely could not show weakness right now, otherwise he feared he would be mocked by Tsunade as a laughingstock for countless years and never be able to hold his head up again.

'Self-rescue, I must save myself !'

In a flash, he quickly made up his mind.

Instead of showing panic, Asakawa Shin revealed an expression of pleasant surprise. He lunged forward to wrap his arms around Tsunade’s neck and acted affectionately :

« Senior Sister, why am I sleeping in your room ? I don’t have any memory of it at all ; I thought I was dreaming just now. »

Seeing Asakawa Shin so close to her, ripples couldn’t help but stir in Tsunade’s heart, and a moment of daze appeared on her face.

It wasn’t until Asakawa Shin’s voice sounded in her ear that she snapped out of it.

She couldn’t help but smile self-deprecatingly. Was she really getting old ? Why did she always love reminiscing about the past ?

Seeing her strange expression, Asakawa Shin asked concernedly : « Senior Sister, what’s wrong ? »

Tsunade gently rubbed his head and complained with feigned dissatisfaction : « It’s because you fell asleep in my arms while we were soaking in the hot spring last night.

Everyone was afraid of disturbing you, so they left early. I had no choice but to carry you back to the room, and you’ve been sleeping ever since. »

Asakawa Shin scratched his head in embarrassment : « Oh right, I remember now. You were holding me so comfortably that I couldn’t help but fall asleep, haha. »

Tsunade’s expression turned teasing again, and she mimicked his tone : « So, I hold you very comfortably, huh ? Do you like being held by Senior Sister that much ? »

Asakawa Shin’s little face turned red. How was her mouth so quick ? But he quickly said righteously : « Yeah, I just like being held by Senior Sister, is that not allowed ? Is Senior Sister unwilling ? »

Tsunade rolled her eyes in annoyance. Since returning from the hot spring, this brat’s skin seemed to have gotten much thicker ; he was almost becoming difficult to handle.

She snorted lightly, her tone slightly teasing : « Senior Sister would, of course, be more than happy to. How about Senior Sister carries you while we travel today~ »

Asakawa Shin was stunned and asked curiously : « Travel ? Where are we going ? Aren’t we staying in the Land of Hot Springs for a couple more days ? »

Tsunade covered her mouth and laughed, tapping Asakawa Shin on the head : « What ? Are you addicted to soaking in the hot spring with Senior Sister now ?

Don’t forget the important business ; we still have to treat Itachi.

One of the medicinal ingredients, called thunder-clap grass, is extremely rare and has excellent body-tempering effects, so we have to take a trip to the Land of Lightning. »

Asakawa Shin looked confused : « Can’t we just buy it ? As long as it’s a problem that can be solved with money, it’s not a problem. »

Tsunade rolled her eyes at him again. Is having money so great ? Can you do whatever you want just because you have money ?

She patiently explained : « The medicinal properties of thunder-clap grass are special. It must be harvested and made into a potion within twenty-four hours of being struck by lightning for the best effect ; it can’t be transported over long distances at all. »

Asakawa Shin nodded in understanding. Since it was to treat Itachi, there was no other way. The hot springs would have to wait until later.

After the group gathered and had breakfast, they embarked on a new journey, aiming straight for the Land of Lightning.

Fortunately, the Land of Lightning is very close to the Land of Hot Springs ; they only needed to head north across the Land of Frost to reach the territory of the Land of Lightning.

The group of five were all elite Ninjas, and it only took them one day to reach a bustling town in the Land of Frost, where they decided to rest for the night before continuing.

Just then, several Ninjas sped past in front of the group. Yūgao and Itachi immediately assumed a defensive stance.

Tsunade waved her hand and said : « They are just passing Ninjas, don’t mind them. There is a good tavern up ahead, I’ll take you all to try it. »

After speaking, she took the lead and walked toward the town. The others looked at each other and quickly followed.

Asakawa Shin, however, frowned deeply, feeling that those Ninjas from just now were somewhat familiar.

He was suddenly shocked ; those Ninjas all seemed to have tails wrapped around their waists. Could they be Saiyans ?

No... that’s not right... where would Saiyans come from...

He quickly realized that it seemed to be a mercenary organization called the Xuanyuan Group, which had once caused the Raikage great trouble, and this was the very town where the Raikage had sought Tsunade’s help to treat his subordinates.

Asakawa Shin pondered secretly : 'I didn’t expect the Ninja World to have been changed so much by me ; to think Tsunade could still run into the Raikage here.

No... that’s not the point.

It’s that the Raikage guy is also here. I remember he later dispatches elite troops to support, which finally scares off the Xuanyuan Group.

Doesn’t that mean...

The Hidden Cloud Village’s defenses will be very weak next ? This seems like an opportunity.'

Taking advantage of a bathroom break, Asakawa Shin quietly created a Shadow Clone.

He and the clone nodded at each other, immediately deciding to take advantage of this once-in-a-lifetime opportunity. The main body would launch a surprise attack on the Hidden Cloud Village to take the chance to collect the Chakra of the Two-Tails and Eight-Tails.

Just right, the Raikage himself could serve as the perfect alibi for him.

The Shadow Clone silently returned to the group and continued moving along with the crowd, while Asakawa Shin’s main body sped away at full speed, flying toward the Land of Lightning.

On the way, Asakawa Shin calculated secretly : « That guy Killer Bee has been confined to the Valley of Clouds and Lightning, so he shouldn’t be hard to find.

Nii Yugito, however, I don’t know where she is. It’s better to go to the Hidden Cloud Village first to find out her whereabouts before making further plans. »

Having determined his course of action, Asakawa Shin did not hesitate any longer and sped toward the Hidden Cloud Village like a shooting star.

Meanwhile, the Shadow Clone on the other side had already snuggled into Tsunade’s arms, secretly gloating in its heart :

'In the past, when the main body summoned me, it was either to move bricks or do manual labor. I didn’t expect to be sent to enjoy myself this time. A rare opportunity ; I must enjoy this properly.'

Yūgao, standing to the side, looked at Tsunade with envy, feeling anxious and sour in her heart :

'How did Lord Shin suddenly become so good at acting spoiled ? He even actively asked for hugs ?

Damn it... why doesn’t he treat me like that ? I want that too...'

« Boom ! »

A sudden explosion came from the forest, shocking everyone. Obviously, it was the work of the team of Ninjas that had passed by earlier.

The Xuanyuan Group possessed a unique Ninjutsu called « Explosive Bug, » capable of releasing parasites invisible to the naked eye that could sneak into an opponent’s body.

When the host used Chakra, it would absorb energy and grow, eventually exploding inside the body and causing fatal damage.

The Shadow Clone’s mouth curled into an imperceptible arc, and it sneered secretly in its heart :

'Well done, Xuanyuan Group. Keep causing trouble, the bigger the better. Drag the Raikage here and keep him stuck, hehehe.'

Tsunade turned a deaf ear to the explosion behind her and just walked toward the tavern on her own.

Holding Asakawa Shin’s Shadow Clone in her arms, she muttered to herself in her heart : 'This brat actually has the nerve to make me carry him the whole way. What a thick-skinned kid !

However, since I’m already carrying him, I might as well go to the most luxurious restaurant tonight, order the most expensive wine, and fleece him properly, hehehe.'
