# Chapitre 17 — Hiruzen Sarutobi blankly took the two walnuts.

« As expected of walnuts cultivated specifically with Wood Release ; the size, texture, and color of the two are almost identical.

Moreover, the density and shape are incomparable to ordinary walnuts ; they really are excellent for rubbing !

No... that’s not right... »

Hiruzen Sarutobi suddenly snapped back to reality ; now was not the time to be rubbing walnuts !

He asked in astonishment :

« Shin, that was Wood Release just now, right ? That makes sense. No wonder you have such an astonishing physique at such a young age ; only Wood Release could grant such terrifying talent.

That’s it, I should have thought of it sooner. Your mother was a member of the Senju Clan... »

He determined the answer himself without waiting for Asakawa Shin to reply, knowing that Wood Release held a supreme status in Konoha.

As the power that allowed the First Hokage, Hashirama, to end the Sengoku era and dominate the Ninja World, Wood Release had become Konoha’s faith ; even the emblem of Konoha was shaped like a leaf.

Hiruzen Sarutobi had previously wondered how Asakawa Shin had trained his physique to such a level at such a young age, knowing that physical training required steady, step-by-step progress, with absolutely no room for deception.

Being able to create Konoha Six Styles was a sign of high talent and good comprehension, which was different from the long-term, cumulative physical training, but now everything made sense.

Hiruzen Sarutobi had trained alongside the First Hokage and Tobirama since he was a child, so he naturally knew the terrifying nature of the First Hokage.

That man had defeated countless Ninjas, stood at the very pinnacle of the Ninja World, and was even revered by all Ninjas as the « God of Shinobi ».

Hiruzen Sarutobi’s disciple, Tsunade, one of the Sannin, had spent her whole life researching her pride and joy, the Super Strength and her trump card, Creation Rebirth, but they were merely passive abilities of Hashirama.

That man possessed immense power in every gesture, and ordinary means could not break through his defenses. Even if injured, he had seal-less healing that allowed him to recover without treatment, and his Wood Release Ninjutsu was so powerful it was inconceivable, almost like a demon god.

How glorious Konoha was when Hashirama was still alive ; the entire Ninja World was shrouded in his shadow, and a rare peace had settled over it.

Yet, in the few short decades after Hashirama died, three Ninja World Wars broke out, with scales of war far exceeding the Sengoku era, and even Hiruzen Sarutobi’s teacher, the Second Hokage, Tobirama, was killed in action.

Hiruzen Sarutobi was tasked with the position of Sandaime Hokage in a time of crisis, but he lacked the ability ; he made repeated mistakes while in the position of Hokage and struggled to hold things together, leading to Konoha’s gradual decline under his leadership.

Even his former companions and his own disciples disliked and looked down upon him. He didn’t know how he would face his teachers after death ; looking back, his life had truly been a failure.

But now it was not too late ; he saw hope again, the hope to make Konoha great again !

Yes, the God of Shinobi had passed away, but now, a child of god had been born in Konoha.

Thinking of this, Hiruzen Sarutobi couldn’t help but laugh wildly :

« Hehehehe, hahahahaha ! Heaven has not treated me poorly, not poorly at all ! »

Asakawa Shin looked at Hiruzen Sarutobi’s crazed appearance and couldn’t help but shake his head :

« Grandpa Sandaime, could you please hold off on the laughing for a moment ? You still haven’t said whether you can give me the Wood Release Ninjutsu scroll. »

Asakawa Shin looked at Sandaime, then at the walnuts, implying that he had already accepted the gift but hadn’t fulfilled his end of the bargain.

Hiruzen Sarutobi’s laughter stopped abruptly. He cleared his throat to hide his embarrassment and said gently to Asakawa Shin :

« Shin, you indeed have the talent to cultivate Wood Release, but Wood Release is no ordinary Ninjutsu. Its significance to Konoha is too great ; it cannot be passed on to anyone who is not virtuous, talented, and universally acclaimed. »

Asakawa Shin’s face darkened. Why was he starting to use bureaucratic jargon ? He asked, unwilling to give up :

« But Grandpa Sandaime, you promised me you would teach me Ninjutsu. You can’t go back on your word and lie to a child. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi laughed loudly : « Shin, look at you getting impatient again. Grandpa promised to teach you the Ninjutsu from the scroll of seals ; Wood Release is not included in that.

Wanting to learn Wood Release—how could it be that simple ? »

« But... but... oh, Grandpa Sandaime, I’m young and don’t understand all that talk. Just tell me, what exactly do I need to do for you to teach me Wood Release Ninjutsu ? »

Asakawa Shin was desperate for Wood Release ; he threw away his pride and fully embodied a cute six-year-old, shaking Hiruzen Sarutobi’s arm and acting spoiled.

Hiruzen Sarutobi fell for it, feeling his bones soften, and said to Asakawa Shin with an affectionate smile :

« The Wood Release Ninjutsu scroll was passed down by the First Hokage to my teacher, the Second Hokage, and then passed on to me.

Shin, do you understand the significance of this ? »

« Hmm... I get it. Do I have to become Hokage to learn it ? »

« No, I want you to take me as your master ! » Hiruzen Sarutobi finally stopped beating around the bush and stated his purpose.

« Eh ? Take you as my master ? » Asakawa Shin hadn’t expected this from Hiruzen Sarutobi. He remembered that Yamato wasn’t part of the Hokage lineage either, yet he still received the Wood Release inheritance ; why did it require taking a master in his case ?

This was actually only natural. After all, in Hiruzen Sarutobi’s view, Asakawa Shin had created a divine skill like Konoha Six Styles at such a young age ; he was not only exceptionally talented but also had astonishing combat power. He had clearly seen the power of that strike against Kato Dan ; even from far away, it had made the glass in his office rattle.

Even if Asakawa Shin hadn’t exposed his Wood Release today, he would have arranged for a white-haired teacher for him upon graduation to bring him into the Hokage lineage.

But now it was different. To Hiruzen, Asakawa Shin was someone who had naturally awakened Wood Release—definitely of the direct Senju lineage. With excellent talent and comprehension, his future was limitless. He had to personally guide such a monstrous genius.

Seeing Asakawa Shin seemingly still hesitant, Hiruzen Sarutobi felt a tightening in his heart and continued to sweeten the deal :

« If you take me as your master, not only will I teach you Wood Release, but I will also impart everything I have learned to you, and you will be free to utilize all of Konoha’s resources.

I will even fully support you for the position of Hokage in the future. How about it ? »

Asakawa Shin rolled his eyes ; he felt that becoming Hiruzen Sarutobi’s disciple had nothing but benefits for him, and as for the foolish things Hiruzen Sarutobi had done, they hadn’t harmed him, so it was none of his business.

As for the likes of Danzo and Orochimaru, he didn’t put them in his eyes at all.

There wasn’t much to pay attention to when taking a master in the Ninja World, but one had to be extremely cautious when accepting a disciple ; after all, killing one’s master was a tradition in the world of Hokage.

Since he was the one being the disciple this time, there was nothing to worry about.

Thinking of this, Asakawa Shin hesitated no longer. He adjusted his posture, knelt down, and respectfully kowtowed three times to Hiruzen Sarutobi :

« Disciple Asakawa Shin greets his teacher. »

« Hahaha, good child, good disciple ! » Hiruzen Sarutobi had gotten his wish and laughed until his eyes disappeared ; at his age, to be able to get such an excellent disciple, he was incredibly satisfied and found everything about Asakawa Shin pleasing to the eye.

He took a scroll from a hidden compartment in the bookshelf and handed it to Asakawa Shin ; this was the Wood Release Ninjutsu scroll that Asakawa Shin had been longing for.

« Seven days from now, I will hold a public apprenticeship ceremony and invite the various clans and renowned Ninjas of Konoha to attend. Such a joyous occasion should naturally be known to everyone, and let the villagers join in the excitement. »

« Also, regarding this Wood Release Ninjutsu, Shin, you must cultivate it diligently and not be negligent ! »

« I will specially open up a training ground for you underground. You must not let anyone know until you have achieved success in your cultivation. You must know that a tree taller than the forest will be destroyed by the wind. »

« Shin, your talent is too dazzling. If people find out, certain individuals might not be able to sit still. »

Speaking of this, a cold light flashed in Hiruzen Sarutobi’s eyes. Enemies outside the village certainly needed to be guarded against, but the 'insiders' within the village were sometimes even more dangerous.

Thinking of how Danzo had demanded Asakawa Shin from him a few days ago, Hiruzen Sarutobi felt his heart tremble with lingering fear.

Fortunately, he had sternly refused at the time ; otherwise, whether it was handing Asakawa Shin over or even forcing Asakawa Shin to the opposite side of Konoha, those were situations he absolutely did not want to see.

But would Danzo stop ? Absolutely not.

Hiruzen Sarutobi knew his old teammate Danzo too well—cruel, vicious, and never resting until he achieved his goal.

Previously, he still had uses for Danzo, so he had repeatedly tolerated Danzo’s extreme behavior, but now...

« I’m sorry, Danzo, your existence is too dangerous. I will personally... »
