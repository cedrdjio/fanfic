# Chapitre 203 — Tossing the Wok

« Hmm ? Wasn’t it you who caused Gaara’s injuries ? »

Sasuke leaned against the wall, his arms and waist wrapped in bandages—all injuries left by Naruto’s sudden sneak attack.

He looked at Gaara, who was currently deeply moved, and sneered mercilessly, « My Shadow Clone saw it with its own eyes ; there’s absolutely no mistake ! »

« Have you been beaten stupid by that puppet guy ? This is completely nonsensical ! » Naruto glared at Sasuke indignantly.

Gaara also furrowed his brows and retorted in a cold, hard tone, « Don’t you dare try to drive a wedge between us ! Naruto is my most cherished friend, a brother like hands and feet. How could he possibly harm me ? »

The corners of his mouth curled up slightly, and he added with confident certainty, « Besides, everyone saw it with their own eyes. It was Naruto who defeated Deidara. Could that possibly be fake ? »

« Hmph, he’s clearly just jealous of our deep bond ! »

« What a despicable person ! »

Sasuke’s face turned ashen with rage. He flicked his sleeve and slammed the door as he left.

« Hmph, Gaara, you’ll regret this ! »

« Shut up, Sasuke ! I am the Kazekage ! »

*Bam !*

Sasuke wandered the hospital alone, the frustration in his chest hard to quell. « This young master can’t be bothered to deal with those two idiots. As for friends, having Shin is enough. »

With that, he wanted to find Asakawa Shin to vent, but after circling the Sunagakure hospital several times, he still couldn’t find any sign of him.

Meanwhile, at Gaara’s home, Temari was busy scurrying around preparing lunch.

Nearby, Asakawa Shin was also wearing an apron, helping out as an assistant.

He nimbly finished washing a pile of ingredients. Then, with a light flick of his fingers, Chakra threads flashed, and the food was precisely sliced into uniform small pieces, neatly arranged by category.

« Shin-kun, you can leave this to me. You’re the great benefactor who saved my brother ; how could I let you do the cooking ? »

Temari’s eyes sparkled, and a bright smile rose on her lips as she thought to herself : 'Truly an impeccable man. I must win him over today.'

Asakawa Shin scratched his head and smiled modestly, « Actually, I didn’t really do much this time. It was all thanks to their own efforts. »

Temari, however, reached out her index finger and gently pressed it against his lips, her eyes full of an « I understand » rapport.

She smoothly untied Asakawa Shin’s apron and pushed him out of the kitchen without allowing any argument, pointing to a chair in the dining room.

« Shin-kun, sit here for a while. Lunch will be ready soon. I... am really looking forward to having lunch with you. »

Asakawa Shin hesitated slightly, « But your two brothers are still in the hospital. Shouldn’t we bring them food first ? »

The sound of sizzling stir-fry came from the kitchen.

Temari held the wok in one hand and brandished the spatula in the other, actually tossing the wok with quite some skill.

While stir-frying, she smiled distractedly, « It’s fine. Those two guys lie in hospital beds all day without exercising anyway ; it doesn’t matter if they eat a bit late or not at all. »

A hint of cunning flashed in Temari’s eyes. Taking the opportunity, she pulled a packet of powder from her bosom and, with a light shake of her wrist, sprinkled it evenly into the dish.

She sneered inwardly : 'Oh, my two foolish brothers, for your sister’s lifelong happiness, just make this small sacrifice. This is a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity ; after all, it’s just too hard to get time alone with Shin-kun.'

Immediately after, she quickly stirred it a few times with the spatula until the powder was completely integrated into the dish without a trace. Only then did she satisfactorily plate the food and carry it toward the dining room.

« Shin-kun, wash your hands and let’s eat. »

« Oh, okay. »

At the dining table, Temari kept serving food to Asakawa Shin. Her attentiveness was a bit overwhelming for him, and his cheeks flushed slightly as a result.

Seeing this, a sharp light flashed in Temari’s eyes, and she rejoiced inwardly : 'It looks like the medicine is taking effect. I need to strike while the iron is hot.'

With that thought, she suddenly pulled off her outer coat, revealing the skimpy clothing underneath. Then, she pretended to fan herself with her hand, saying coquettishly :

« Oh my, it’s suddenly so hot. Shin-kun, you won’t mind if I take off my coat, right ? »

« Ah ? I don’t mind. This is your home ; do as you please. »

« Shin-kun really is understanding. »

She chuckled, covering her mouth, and leaned over to fill Asakawa Shin’s wine cup. « Come, let me toast you again. »

Asakawa Shin looked up to thank her, but his gaze unexpectedly crashed into a patch of snowy white.

His breath hitched, and his heartbeat lost its rhythm.

« What’s wrong, Shin-kun ? Why are you dazed ? Hurry up and drink. »

« Ah... okay, okay. »

Just as he set down his wine cup, the plate in front of him was instantly piled high again with food served by Temari.

« Shin-kun, try this. This is a 'Special Love Dish' I made specifically for you. You must eat a lot of it. »

« Okay... okay, thank you, thank you. »

The more Asakawa Shin ate, the more he felt something was wrong. He cursed inwardly : 'Is the temperature in the Land of Wind really this high ? Just having a meal has made me break out in a profuse sweat.'

At this moment, he only felt his breathing becoming increasingly rapid, and the scene before his eyes began to grow hazy and blurred.

'Dammit, is this wine really that strong ?'

He had just glanced at the empty wine cup when Temari, with quick eyes and hands, picked up the wine pot, leaned over, and filled another cup for him.

« It seems Shin-kun really likes this wine. Then have some more. »

Asakawa Shin swallowed hard. An inexplicable heat and restlessness rampaged through his body, making it difficult for him to control himself.

« Yeah, I do like it. This wine... is really big. »

Seemingly realizing something was off, he shook his head vigorously, and his mind cleared slightly.

« I mean, this wine is really too strong. I think I’m a bit drunk. »

Temari covered her lips and chuckled, her eyes overflowing with tenderness : « Is that so ? As long as Shin-kun likes it. To receive such praise from Shin-kun is the honor of this 'wine'. »

As she spoke, she filled Asakawa Shin’s cup again and poured one for herself. Then, she leaned in close against him, lightly clinking cups to drink together.

However, just as the cup touched her lips, she seemed unable to handle the alcohol. Her body went soft, and she actually stumbled, spilling all the wine in her cup onto Asakawa Shin’s clothes.

« Ah, sorry, sorry. I seem to be... a bit drunk too. » Temari cried out in surprise, quickly pulling out an embroidered handkerchief and hurriedly wiping him down with a panicked expression.

« It’s... it’s fine. I can do it myself, » Asakawa Shin demurred, but his voice was weak and powerless.

At this moment, he only felt the sweet fragrance of the beauty before him, which sent his mind reeling. He couldn’t help but want to lean forward and sniff it closely.

Seeing this, Temari was overjoyed. 'Great, the medicine is finally working. I thought I’d bought fake medicine ; this is a potent drug enough to make twenty elephants go into heat.'

'Shin-kun ate so much, and it’s only just taking effect. Looks like I’ll have to double the dose next time.'

She slightly tilted her neck back, her arms naturally wrapping around Asakawa Shin’s neck, and teased :

« Is Shin-kun so impatient to taste the 'after-dinner dessert' ? So eager... why not have another cup ? »

With that, she filled the wine cup again, but didn’t hand it to Asakawa Shin. Instead, she tilted her head back and drank it all herself.

Then, she stood on her tiptoes, her red lips parting slightly, and slowly transferred the mellow wine from her mouth into his.

The last sliver of Asakawa Shin’s remaining sanity snapped completely at this moment.

He let out a low growl, suddenly lifted Temari, placed her on the dining table, and began to taste her wantonly.

Seeing that the fish had taken the bait, Temari simply cast off her disguise and met him with passion.

A long time later, the commotion gradually subsided, and the dining room was a mess.

Asakawa Shin leaned against the chair naked, still holding the disheveled Temari in his arms.

His face was dark as he gritted his teeth and questioned, « Are you crazy ? You actually drugged me ? »

Temari, however, put on a charming look. « How was that drugging ? That was just seasoning for the final dessert. I saw that Shin-kun was eating very happily just now. »

Asakawa Shin’s face darkened. He picked up Temari and scolded angrily, « You’re good at stir-frying ? Tossing the wok, is it ? I can do that too. »

Before Temari could understand what he meant, she was fiercely attacked in her vital spot, causing her to let out an involuntary moan.

« Hmph, this is just the beginning. Armament Haki : Hardening. »

« Su... Sugoi ! »

« Partial Multi-Size Technique. »

« Ah, this ? »

« Life Return ! Prepare yourself ! »

« Yada ! »

Late at night, the hospital in Sunagakure was silent. A full moon already hung high in the sky, casting down its cold radiance.

Gaara clutched his stomach and said weakly, « Sister... she didn’t really forget about me, did she ? I haven’t had a drop of water or a bite of food all day... »

He tossed and turned on the bed. « No, I’m so hungry I can’t sleep at all... Sister, what on earth are you doing ? »
