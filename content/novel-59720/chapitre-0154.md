# Chapitre 154 — Accessory Slots

« Hmm ? What on earth is going on ? »

« Making such a huge scene, and all to deal with just a little brat ? »

As soon as Aoda finished speaking, Manda interrupted impatiently, « Stop nagging. Hurry up and get rid of this annoying brat. I want to go back and finish my sleep. »

Facing the fierce glares of the two giant snakes, Asakawa Shin’s face tightened, not daring to be careless.

He slammed his hands together, his aura exploding, and he instantly entered Sage Mode.

Seeing this, Manda and Aoda couldn’t help but be slightly startled.

But it wasn’t over yet. Asakawa Shin bit his fingertip and performed a dizzying series of hand signs.

« Sage Art : Summoning Jutsu ! »

With a loud shout, dense smoke billowed up. Its momentum was unprecedentedly grand, instantly whipping up a gale that swept wildly in all directions.

When the smoke cleared, a colossal figure stood there, its body so majestic it was almost unimaginable.

Compared to it, Manda and Aoda were as tiny as toys.

Manda’s eyes widened in shock, and he cried out, « Is that Katsuyu ? There’s no mistake... but with this size, could it be that he summoned the main body of Lady Katsuyu from the Shikkotsu Forest ? »

The summoned Lady Katsuyu looked around curiously and was slightly surprised. « This is Ryūchi Cave, isn’t it ? Shin-kun, why did you summon me here ? »

Asakawa Shin laughed loudly. « Katsuyu-nee, take a look at who’s across from you. You’ll know why I summoned you with one look. »

Katsuyu then lowered her head and looked closely. « Hmm, that’s the White Snake Sage, and Manda ? I think I understand a bit now. »

As soon as she finished speaking, she raised her head, struck a pose she thought was majestic, and shouted at the dumbfounded Manda across from her :

« Hey ! You purple mangy snake, stop staring. I’m talking to you. Come over and fight this Sage one-on-one ! »

Manda looked left and right before finally confirming that Lady Katsuyu was indeed pointing at him.

He was shocked. When had he offended her ? Why did she insist on killing him ?

The White Snake Sage frowned and spoke with slight dissatisfaction, « Katsuyu, why are you following this kid in his nonsense ? »

Before he could finish, Katsuyu interrupted impatiently, « Nonsense ? What nonsense ? How could my boy be talking nonsense ? I think you’re the one who needs to reflect on yourself. Fine, I’m done talking to you ; I have a fight to get to. »

With that, she suddenly reared up her massive body and spat a huge glob of Corrosive Slime at Manda, shouting sharply, « Die, you little punk ! »

Seeing things were going south, Aoda had already slipped away, leaving Manda frozen alone on the spot.

Facing the Corrosive Slime pouring down like a flood, he panicked and had no choice but to dig a hole to escape.

Unfortunately, he was still a step too slow. His tail, left on the surface, was hit by the slime. Instantly, the flesh split open and turned to charcoal, making him let out a shrill roar of pain.

As he fled in disgrace, a light remark reached his ears with absolute clarity.

« What ? This is Orochimaru-senpai’s summon ? It’s actually this weak ! To think I specifically summoned Katsuyu-nee for this. How boring. »

Manda suddenly realized and cursed in his heart, 'Damn Orochimaru, so it was you who lured Lady Katsuyu here !'

'I’ve always lived in peace with the world. Why would I provoke such a great enemy for no reason ? Just you wait. If I don’t get revenge for this, I’m not a Great Snake !'

« All done. Shin-kun, did I do well ? »

Asakawa Shin nodded with satisfaction. « I really owe you this time, Katsuyu-nee. You go back first. After I finish dealing with the matters in Ryūchi Cave and return to Konoha, I’ll come play with you. »

After a puff of smoke, Lady Katsuyu disappeared.

Asakawa Shin turned around and cast a calm gaze at the White Snake Sage. « Well ? Do you want to continue fighting ? »

The White Snake Sage coughed lightly, a slightly stiff smile appearing on his face. « Not bad. You have successfully passed my trial. As a reward, I have decided to grant you three wishes. »

The Three Great Snake Princesses' eyes widened as they protested vehemently, « You can’t, White Snake Sage ! If you agree, you will definitely regret it in the end ! »

The White Snake Sage immediately snapped, « Shut up ! I am the White Snake Sage. This is all for the sake of Ryūchi Cave. It’s settled ! »

After speaking, he immediately changed his expression and said kindly to Asakawa Shin, « Alright, I’m a bit tired. I’ll let the three Snake Princesses accompany you from here on. »

« Then many thanks to the White Snake Sage ! »

Asakawa Shin nodded faintly and then turned to look at the Three Great Snake Princesses. « You lot, come with me. »

With that, he took the lead and walked out of the temple. The Three Great Snake Princesses looked at each other and had no choice but to grit their teeth and follow.

The White Snake Sage watched Asakawa Shin’s departing back with a thousand thoughts in his mind.

Tagitsuhime took out a scroll and handed it to Asakawa Shin. « This is the method for practicing the Ryūchi Cave Sage Mode. »

Asakawa Shin took the scroll, flipped through it briefly, and then tucked it into his robe with satisfaction.

His gaze shifted to the three Snake Princesses, and he gave a faint command, « From today onwards, you will stay by my side. However, your sizes are too conspicuous and inconvenient to carry. Can you become smaller ? »

Hearing this, Ichikishima-hime’s face was filled with shock and disdain. « Smaller ? What do you think we are ? Pet snakes ? »

Asakawa Shin shrugged as if it were a matter of course. « Otherwise ? Did you expect to go into battle and kill enemies ? Don’t tell me you don’t even know this bit of Transformation Jutsu. »

« My senior brother Orochimaru can turn into a small white snake. As Snake Princesses, are you actually inferior to him ? »

He thought for a moment and immediately assigned the accessory slots. « Tagitsuhime and Tajitsuhime, you two shrink down and become my earrings, hanging by my ears. »

« As for you, Ichikishima-hime, you will become my bracelet, wrapped around my wrist for me to play with at any time. »

Tagitsuhime and Tajitsuhime were initially a bit dissatisfied, but hearing Asakawa Shin say this, they quickly turned into two small snow-white snakes and obediently bit onto Asakawa Shin’s earlobes, acting as earrings.

Asakawa Shin examined himself in a water mirror but then frowned.

« You two, curl your tails up. What’s the point of hanging there so stiffly ? People who don’t know better would think I’m drying salted fish. Haven’t you ever seen earrings ? »

After speaking, he glanced at Ichikishima-hime, who still hadn’t moved, his eyes full of danger. « As for you, hurry up, or you know the consequences. »

Ichikishima-hime couldn’t help but shudder. Daring not to hesitate any longer, she quickly turned into a tiny white snake and wrapped herself tightly around Asakawa Shin’s wrist.

The snake’s head bit its tail, perfectly forming a closed loop.

Asakawa Shin nodded in satisfaction, took off this 'bracelet', and began to fiddle with it comfortably.

The result...

« Giggle... it tickles so much, I can’t take it... giggle. »

« You, shut up ! Whose bracelet laughs ? »

« But... but it’s really so ticklish, I can’t help it ! »

« Can’t help it ? If you dare to laugh again, I’ll make you into a Coiled Dragon. »

« Coiled Dragon ? What’s that ? »

Asakawa Shin looked down. « It’s a Pillar-Coiling Dragon, coiled around Little Shin. You choose for yourself. And you two earrings, if you don’t do a good job, you’ll swap positions with her. »

The Three Great Snake Princesses were instantly struck as if by lightning, their entire bodies trembling. They repeatedly promised they would do a good job.

After calming down, Ichikishima-hime was still unwilling and muttered softly, « Why do those two get to be earrings ? Can I switch positions with them ? »

Asakawa Shin rolled his eyes in annoyance. « Haven’t you noticed ? Those two are similar in size, making a perfect pair. Whereas you... are clearly a whole head shorter than them. »

No longer paying attention to this silly snake, Asakawa Shin looked around, feeling the abundant Natural Energy. With a smirk, he immediately began to cultivate.

« Hahaha, who can out-grind me ? »
