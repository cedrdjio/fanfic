# Chapitre 75 — "Listening to your analysis, it seems this masked man is indeed Uchiha Madara. As expected of you, Itachi. You tore through Madara's disguise so easily."

Asakawa Shin’s lips curled slightly, his tone playful, and he did not hesitate to offer his praise, causing Uchiha Itachi to straighten his back, a reserved smile appearing on his face.

« Now that you know his true identity, what do you plan to do next ? »

Asakawa Shin was also a bit curious ; since Itachi had sought him out to say so much, he must have a plan. Surely, he didn’t just want to play the role of a great detective.

Mentioning this, Itachi’s expression also turned serious, and he stated solemnly :

« Madara’s existence is far too dangerous for Konoha and the Uchiha, so I intend to go undercover by his side as a rogue ninja ! »

Asakawa Shin turned his head to look at him in astonishment : « Didn’t he just conspire with Danzo to kill your father ? Even if he didn’t do it himself, how could he possibly accept your defection ? Do you really take him for a senile old fool ? »

Itachi shook his head, noncommittal : « There are always more solutions than problems. As long as I show enough sincerity, I believe he will accept me. Although the elite hawks of the Uchiha Clan suffered heavy casualties, some remnants still remain ! As a former high-ranking official of Konoha, Danzo slaughtered their loved ones, which has only fueled their resentment, leading them to suspect that this was all a bitter-meat scheme by Konoha’s leadership. Because my father initiated the gathering, they have even directed this hatred toward my mother and Sasuke, which is truly unforgivable. »

« But this also gives me the opportunity and justification to act. If they hadn’t kept pestering my father, he wouldn’t have convened this damn gathering and lost his life in vain. They are the source of chaos in Konoha. As long as I follow Danzo’s example and purge them all, then pretend to fail in an assassination attempt on the Hokage, I will have a reason to defect from Konoha. Once that happens, if I take the initiative to hunt down Danzo, my father’s killer, I will have a high chance of going undercover by Madara’s side. »

« No, I don’t agree ! This is too dangerous. Madara is no idiot ; he won’t be fooled so easily. Moreover, how are the members of the Uchiha Clan innocent ? Their loved ones have died ; can’t you even let them complain a little ? Itachi, I am not like the Second or Third, and certainly not like Danzo ! As long as the Uchiha do not betray me, I will never betray the Uchiha ! As for Madara... »

Asakawa Shin sneered disdainfully, his expression arrogant and confident. A powerful burst of Haoshoku Haki erupted from him, sweeping outward like a hurricane, causing the rocks beneath his feet to crack : « Why should I fear a mere Uchiha Madara ! Never mind that he is old and frail, even if he were in his prime, I wouldn’t have thought much of him. Itachi, you and Shisui are the pillars of the Uchiha Clan ; you shouldn’t risk yourselves for such things. Leave this matter alone ; I will definitely make them pay for what they have done today. »

Uchiha Itachi was struck as if by lightning. Asakawa Shin’s words were like an arrow piercing deep into his heart, and the aura of his Haoshoku Haki shook his spirit violently, leaving him in turmoil and unable to steady himself. After a long while, he finally regained his senses, a surge of fervor rising within him. He quickly knelt on one knee, pledging his oath with an excited expression : « Lord Shin, from this day forth, whenever you have a command, I will do my utmost, through fire and water, without hesitation. I also represent the Uchiha Clan in expressing our gratitude for the trust you have bestowed upon us. Please rest assured, Shisui and I will certainly restrain our clansmen and maintain order ; we will never fail your expectations ! »

« Very good, that’s the spirit ! Now... take me to see your mother first ; I have something to discuss with her. »

« ? »

Uchiha Clan, former Clan Head’s residence. Uchiha Mikoto looked haggard and full of sorrow, her eyes still swollen and red, clearly having just been crying. Asakawa Shin’s sudden visit caught her off guard, and she was busy pouring tea for him. However, having had no appetite and being overcome with grief for the past two days, her hand holding the teapot trembled slightly.

Seeing this, Asakawa Shin quickly stopped her : « It’s fine, Aunt Mikoto, don’t trouble yourself. I’ve come for a serious matter, and I will leave once I’ve said it. Also, I am very sorry about Uncle Fugaku ; please accept my condolences. »

Mikoto nodded, flustered. She was in no mood for formalities now, so she went straight to the point : « I wonder what important matter Lord Shin has to discuss with a woman like me today ? »

Asakawa Shin stated his purpose directly : « I want to take Itachi out of the Village for a while ; the date of return is undecided. It might be a few days, or it could be a year or more, so I came to inform you today. »

Mikoto’s delicate body trembled. Her husband had just died, and now her son was to leave on a long journey ; she couldn’t accept it for the moment : « Um... Lord Shin, is it absolutely necessary for Itachi to go ? Could someone else accompany you instead ? »

Itachi gave his mother a dissatisfied look, but Mikoto pretended not to see it.

Asakawa Shin slowly shook his head and said helplessly to Mikoto : « This matter requires Itachi. »

Just as Mikoto frowned, a hint of irritation appearing in her eyes, Asakawa Shin’s follow-up words reached her ears. The information conveyed by those words caused her body to go limp, and she nearly collapsed to the floor.

« After all, for the treatment to work, he has to go in person. »

Mikoto was terrified, her face turning pale. Fugaku had died only a few days ago, and now her eldest son had come down with some unknown illness. Was it so serious that he had to leave the Village for treatment ? How was she supposed to survive this ? The previous dissatisfaction was gone ; she asked anxiously : « Illness ? Lord Shin, are you saying Itachi is sick ? Is it serious ? »

Asakawa Shin had seen Mikoto’s reaction clearly, and he felt a bit annoyed. So, he poured himself a cup of tea unhurriedly, took a sip, and said faintly : « It’s not serious, he just doesn’t have many years left to live. »

Upon hearing this, Mikoto felt her heart shake violently, and she was unable to make any expression. She sat there blankly like a marionette, as if she had lost her soul.

Seeing that he had perhaps gone too far, Asakawa Shin scratched his head and said apologetically to Mikoto : « Aunt Mikoto, don’t be too worried. Although Itachi’s condition definitely cannot be cured in the Village, my senior sister Tsunade will surely have a way ! She is the legendary master of medical ninjutsu. »

Itachi, sitting nearby, suddenly learned of his own death sentence. Even he couldn’t help but be shocked, a chill running down his spine. He suppressed his surging emotions, his throat moved slightly as he swallowed, and he asked in a low, heavy voice : « Lord Shin... what exactly is this illness I have ? Does it really require Lady Tsunade to cure it ? »

Asakawa Shin stared at him intently. Originally, he hadn’t intended to interfere with Itachi’s destiny. However, this guy had paved a path for himself today by taking the initiative to show goodwill and pledge his loyalty, which was why Asakawa Shin had taken this action.

« You awakened your eyes too early, which placed a huge burden on your body. Coupled with your constant overthinking, it has exacerbated the condition. Also, if I’m not mistaken, you have awakened those eyes, haven’t you ? The cursed Mangekyo Sharingan ! That is the final straw that broke the camel’s back. »

Itachi’s body trembled, and he murmured distractedly : « You mean... the bloodline disease ? »

Asakawa Shin nodded, confirming Itachi’s guess : « Correct, it is indeed that incurable bloodline disease. You should be able to feel it yourself, right ? The recent abnormalities in your body ; there isn’t much time left for you. »

« Bloodline disease ? » Mikoto had regained her senses by now. She suddenly knelt before Asakawa Shin, begging incessantly : « Lord Shin, I beg you, you must save Itachi. Fugaku has just passed ; I really cannot lose Itachi too. Please, save him. As long as you can save him, I am willing to be your servant and maid to repay your kindness ! »

Asakawa Shin rolled his eyes at her : « I already have two maids, so there are no vacancies for now. You can owe me the favor ; we’ll talk about it later. »
