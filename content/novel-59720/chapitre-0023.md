# Chapitre 23 — Fragrant Woman

Evening, Asakawa Shin’s private kitchen.

Asakawa Shin stared intently at Hinata, observing her every move, not missing a single glare or eye-roll.

« If odd changes, even stays the same... »

« Palace jade liquid wine... »

« How are you ? »

« Your salt, my vinegar... »

« West Sea... »

Hinata endured for a long time before finally snapping, mocking Asakawa Shin :

« Shin-kun, what are you mumbling about ? Are you not feeling well ? Do you need me to call a doctor for you ? »

« Haha, no need, Hinata. I was just practicing Fuinjutsu. You might not know, but there are many foreign languages in Fuinjutsu. »

« By the way, Hinata, why do you seem like a different person today ? It makes people worried. Do you need my help ? »

Facing Asakawa Shin’s concern, Hinata sighed and said : « It’s useless, Shin-kun, you can’t help me. There is great terror between life and death. Only after dying once did I realize what I truly wanted. »

« I used to be insecure and introverted, afraid to express myself. That’s why when I saw someone as dazzling as you—someone completely opposite to me—I couldn’t help but want to learn from you. »

« I wanted to become like you : confident, arrogant, looking down on everyone, and so good at 'flexing'... »

« Ah ? That... sorry to interrupt, Hinata, I’m not... »

« Shut up ! I’m not done yet ! Where was I ? Oh right, flexing. That’s why I wrote down your words and actions in a notebook, learning them frame by frame. I wanted to slowly change myself. »

« But the moment Naruto called me a 'stinky woman', I clearly understood I was wrong. There was no time for me to change slowly. The world wouldn’t stop turning just because of my will. »

« I was terrified at that moment. I was afraid Naruto would hate me, afraid of losing Naruto forever. If that happened, what would be the point of my life ? From that moment on, the old Hinata died. »

« That... Hinata, isn’t that a bit exaggerated ? Naruto was just... »

« Shut up ! How would you know what Naruto means to me ? He means everything ! That moment, I realized it. The way to change isn’t 'what do you want to become ?', but 'become it directly' ! »

« After all, I am a peerless genius too, Shin-kun. So now, I have become you. »

« Shin-kun, since Naruto likes you so much, he will definitely like me now, right ? Hehehehe~ »

« I want to hold Naruto tightly in my hands. No one can take him from me, not even you ! Naruto is my possession. Hehehehe~ Hahahaha ! »

« Hinata, please be normal. Don’t be like this, you’re scaring me. And you seem to have a huge misunderstanding about me. »

Asakawa Shin looked at Hinata, sweating profusely. What to do ? This Hinata seemed a bit broken.

Although this wasn’t necessarily a bad thing...

But it just felt weird. Even though ten Hinatas added together couldn’t beat him, she still made him feel scared. No... not scared, but creeped out.

'Damn it, it’s a yandere young lady !'

'Whatever, I’ll just have to make Naruto suffer a bit ! As long as Little Uzumaki Himawari...'

'Wait ? Little Uzumaki Himawari ? I remember she’s usually quiet and well-behaved, but when she gets emotional, her personality changes drastically, showing strong violent tendencies.'

'I previously thought she inherited the violent genes from her grandmother, Uzumaki Kushina, but now it seems she might have inherited them from her mother, or perhaps both ?'

'Inheriting from her mother is more likely. After all, Little Uzumaki Himawari’s appearance and usual personality are exactly like Hinata’s. If Little Uzumaki Himawari can change back, Hinata should be able to too, right ?'

« Hinata, look into my eyes ! » Asakawa Shin decided to give it a try and said to Hinata.

Hinata looked over in confusion. Suddenly, black and red lightning appeared on Asakawa Shin’s body. With a speed she couldn’t react to, it spread to her eyes in an instant.

« You f*cking... » Hinata only managed to say three words before rolling her eyes and passing out.

...

Asakawa Shin, looking nervous, gently shook Hinata : « Hinata, wake up, time to go pee. »

Hinata’s eyelashes fluttered, and her eyes gradually regained their luster. She shook her head slightly and slowly sat up.

« Ugh, Shin-kun ? I... what happened to me ? » Hinata asked in confusion, as if she had just woken up.

Asakawa Shin looked at the Hinata in front of him with doubt. It seemed she had changed back, but it could also be a disguise. Not sure, let’s observe more.

« That... » Asakawa Shin swallowed nervously and asked Hinata : « Hinata, do you remember what happened today ? »

« What happened today ? » Hinata repeated in confusion, falling into thought.

'I remember at school this morning, Naruto he... *whimper*.'

'I... what did I do ? How could I say such shameful things, and...'

As if remembering something, Hinata quickly searched her body. Sure enough, she found a folded piece of paper in her small pocket. She unfolded it with trembling hands...

'Hiss... it’s actually real. This is the

Best Friends Certificate

« , right ? That’s great...'

Asakawa Shin watched Hinata’s face turn red at a visible speed, looking like she was about to faint from happiness.

This gave him a fright, so he quickly reminded her :  »Hinata, calm down ! I have something to ask you !«

Hearing this, Hinata exhaled slowly and gradually calmed down.

»It seems you remembered what happened today. Can you tell me what’s going on ?«

Hinata didn’t know how to answer either. After deliberating, she said :  »I don’t know exactly what’s going on either. In short, there is another 'Hinata' in my mind. She shares my memories and brain. I can even communicate with her. The feeling she gives me is like another version of you.«

'What do you mean

another version of me

» ? When was I ever that arrogant ?'

Asakawa Shin thought with dissatisfaction, but business came first. He pressed Hinata : « Then can you control her ? Will she endanger you ? Do you two switch back and forth usually ? »

Hinata thought for a moment and said : « She is me, and I am her. We are both Hinata. However, since she was born from my imagination, my will is still the dominant one. »

« As for danger, she... she said that men are creatures who love the new and loathe the old, so she will get along with me peacefully. In the future, we can serve... serve... together. This way, we have a big advantage. *Whimper*. »

Asakawa Shin looked at Hinata, dumbfounded. Can a split personality work like that ? I haven’t read enough books, don’t lie to me.

« Hmph ! How useless, hiding away after only saying a few words. Hey, Shin-kun, do you think I was wrong ? »

« You don’t need to deny it. I am the personality born from simulating your character, so I know all the dirty thoughts in your heart. »

« Hey, tell me, does Naruto like her more, or does he like me more, or maybe both ? »

Asakawa Shin was startled. He exclaimed : « Since when did I have such dirty thoughts ? No... wait... are you the 'Stinky Woman' ? »

Hinata frowned, repeating with dissatisfaction : « Stinky Woman ? »

Asakawa Shin said matter-of-factly : « Yes, you were born from that sentence Naruto said, so your code name should be 'Stinky Woman' to commemorate your birth and distinguish between you two. »

Hinata noncommittally said : « That kind of thing doesn’t matter at all. But if I am 'Stinky Woman', then what about her ? »

« She is so well-behaved, so her code name should be... »

« Fragrant Woman ! »

« ... Fragrant your head. »
