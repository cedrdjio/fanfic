# Chapitre 163 — Susanoo

« Yo~ A loud boom in the sky, Lord Bee arrives, a flashy guy... »

Killer Bee had just struck his hip-hop starting pose and hadn’t even finished his first rhyme when he took a hard elbow to the ribs.

This strike was clearly super effective ; he instantly doubled over like a cooked shrimp, clutching his stomach tightly and gasping from the pain.

However, pain seemed to bring inspiration. A sharp light flashed in his eyes, and he endured the intense pain, struggling to stand up and continue swaying.

« My bones are... aching with pain, »

« The Cat Queen is wild and hard to tame, »

« She struck while I was at ease, »

« Caught by her sneak attack, if you please ! »

« Shut up ! Killer Bee, you’re giving me a headache. »

Yugito sighed helplessly, shaking her head to clear the discomfort.

« This Heavenly Transfer Technique is indeed convenient... but the side effects are a bit much, aren’t they ? The moment we landed, it felt like every bone in my body was falling apart. »

She looked around warily, her voice carrying a hint of exhaustion and pain.

« Is this Konoha ? The scenery is quite nice... unfortunately, it’s about to become scorched earth. »

« Hmph ! Such big talk. If you can do it, then try. »

Yugito looked toward the voice and saw a man in black stepping forward ; the « ping-pong paddle » emblem on his left chest was particularly eye-catching.

Yugito sneered. « So it’s you, Body Flicker Shisui. Do you really think you can stop us all by yourself ? »

« And what if you add me ? » Asakawa Shin walked out slowly.

Shisui was about to bow in greeting, but the moment his gaze fell on Asakawa Shin, his movements froze.

« Shin... Lord Shin ? Are you alright ? Your heartbeat... why is it so exaggerated ? »

« Huh ? »

Asakawa Shin blanked for a moment, subconsciously looking down, and immediately gasped.

On his chest and abdomen, a perfect heart-shaped pattern was beating violently through his clothes, making a clear « thump-thump » sound.

« Holy crap, what the hell is this ? ! »

His pupils shrank, and a prompt from the system panel instantly flashed through his mind.

'No wonder I felt thirsty and hot all over just now. It turns out Tom’s underlying code was triggered !'

Asakawa Shin glanced at Yugito beside him, filled with wariness, and couldn’t help but complain wildly in his head.

'Damn system, your requirements are quite specific ! Ordinary cats and Ninja Cats aren’t good enough ; it has to be the Two-Tails cat-girl to work ? But...'

He looked Yugito up and down. 'I have to admit, you have good taste.'

Asakawa Shin’s gaze involuntarily stuck to Yugito, his Adam’s apple bobbing nervously. That violently beating heart felt like it would burst his eardrums, and two pink hearts uncontrollably appeared in his eyes.

Yugito, who was originally full of killing intent, now felt her skin crawl from his bizarre appearance and couldn’t help but take a wary step back.

« Hey ! You... what are you staring at me for ? » Her voice carried a hint of unease.

Asakawa Shin took a deep breath, trying to force down the restlessness in his body.

« Shisui, leave the Eight-Tails to you. This little female cat... is mine. I’ll deal with her personally. »

Uchiha Shisui was slightly startled, then his expression turned solemn, and he agreed without hesitation.

Under his deliberate guidance, the battlefield was quickly divided.

Yugito was secretly on guard, her gaze scanning the youth in front of her.

'Is this guy the target of this mission ? The legendary Wood Release user ?'

While she was secretly pondering, Asakawa Shin across from her suddenly moved, giving her a start.

Asakawa Shin unhurriedly extended his right hand. At his fingertips, a sprout quickly germinated and grew into a branch.

In the blink of an eye, a delicate flower bloomed.

Then, his arm suddenly elongated, handing the flower to her.

« Is this for me ? No, wait, could there be some trick ? »

Yugito suddenly snapped out of it, her heart jumping, and she quickly leaped back several steps to distance herself.

However, Asakawa Shin didn’t care at all.

Just now, a system prompt had sounded in his mind, putting his worried heart at ease.

[Ding ! Detected host performing an action consistent with the template. Proficiency +1%. Current Proficiency : 91%]

'Damn system ! Talking about uncontrollably falling in love with a female cat... this side effect is way too overbearing.'

Asakawa Shin cursed inwardly. He had no fighting spirit at all right now ; his mind was filled with the desire to circle around this cat-girl. 'Tom, oh Tom, being a simp cat is a no-go.'

Thinking this, his figure blurred, and he pulled out a diamond ring from somewhere, handing it over cheekily again.

Yugito’s brow twitched. Although she had no idea what this guy was losing his mind over, stalling for time seemed beneficial to her side.

At this thought, a hint of a smile flashed in her narrow cat-like eyes, and she actually reached out her slender hand to take the diamond ring.

[Ding ! Perceived host performing an action consistent with the template. Proficiency +1%. Current Proficiency : 92%]

While this man and cat were playing house, the other battlefields nearby were already fighting intensely.

Uchiha Sasuke stared fixedly at Danzo Shimura, his teeth grinding.

« You bastard ! How dare you kill my father and desecrate his remains... Those Sharingan of yours belong to my father, don’t they ? »

A mocking sneer curled at the corner of Danzo’s mouth, and his light words pierced Sasuke like a blade.

« Correct. These eyes belong to that waste Fugaku. I must say, they are... quite handy to use. »

« You bastard ! Don’t you dare insult my father ! »

Sasuke instantly flew into a rage, his Chakra churning violently around him.

A sharp light flashed in Danzo’s eyes, and his tone became even more biting : « Am I wrong ? With the same eyes, Fugaku couldn’t bring out this kind of power at all. It’s a pity that the two ocular techniques he awakened weren’t combat-oriented. A waste is a waste. »

Sasuke could no longer suppress the rage in his chest. He drew a Kunai and lunged at Danzo.

Danzo snorted coldly. « Then let me show you... the true power of the Mangekyo Sharingan. »

Before his voice faded, Sasuke had already reached him, the Kunai in his hand thrusting forward with a chilling glint.

However, Danzo appeared calm and collected, suddenly activating the initial form of Susanoo.

A ring of illusory ribs appeared out of thin air, instantly constructing an indestructible barrier.

Sasuke, already blinded by rage, didn’t slow down but accelerated, his Kunai stabbing hard against the ribcage.

« Clang— ! »

The collision produced the sound of metal striking metal.

Despite the loud sound, the Kunai couldn’t even cut in half an inch.

« Nani ? What Ninjutsu is this ? »

Before he could react, Danzo activated the Mangekyo Sharingan again.

The ribcage suddenly expanded, transforming into a half-bodied skeletal giant in the blink of an eye.

Then, the giant raised its massive palm and slammed it down toward Sasuke with terrifying force.

« So fast ! »

Sasuke’s expression changed drastically. At such close range, there was no way to dodge !

At the critical moment, another giant hand condensed from Yin Release suddenly reached out from the flank, blocking the strike.

Sasuke, still shaken, hurriedly focused his gaze.

He saw that Uchiha Itachi had also activated the first stage of Susanoo at some point, the massive half-bodied giant emitting a suffocating pressure.

« What on earth... is this ? » Sasuke muttered to himself, his face full of disbelief.

Neither of the two men on the field answered his question.

'Stabbing Susanoo with a Kunai ?'

Uchiha Itachi glanced helplessly at his foolish younger brother through the phantom of Susanoo and said in a deep voice :

« Sasuke, right now... you cannot interfere in a battlefield of this level. Step back for now ; leave this to me. »

Hearing this, Sasuke grit his teeth, seemingly wanting to argue.

However, Itachi didn’t give him a chance to speak, continuing firmly : « Trust me, Sasuke. I will definitely avenge our father. »

Sasuke shuddered, the stubbornness in his eyes gradually replaced by trust.

He nodded solemnly, then glared fiercely at Danzo before reluctantly retreating to the side to provide support for Itachi.

Without Sasuke as a hindrance, the two Susanoo users finally unleashed their full power.

In an instant, the two massive skeletal giants crashed into each other, kicking up a cloud of dust that filled the sky.
