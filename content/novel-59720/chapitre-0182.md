# Chapitre 182 — Something to Look Forward To

« Enough ! What is this noisy behavior ? Do you still have any respect for me as your leader ? »

Pain shouted angrily, his voice echoing through the cave and making it buzz.

Deidara, intimidated by the aura, shrank his neck and quickly smiled apologetically. « Leader, of course I respect you... »

Before he could finish, Asakawa Shin interrupted sharply, « Hmph, I keep the leader in my heart. Your attitude is clearly a sign of great disrespect ! »

He turned abruptly and advised Pain with a righteous air, « Leader, such people who ignore organizational discipline must be severely punished. I think we should just blast him into dust ! »

« Enough ! »

Nagato pressed his brow with a headache and quickly controlled Pain to shout a stop.

The situation had completely deviated from his expectations. He thought today would be a fierce struggle between dragons and tigers, but it turned into a shrewish argument.

He took a deep breath, suppressing the irritation in his heart. « Both of you are new members and haven’t been assigned to a group yet... How about you two form a team ? »

« What ? ! » Deidara turned pale with shock and protested on the spot.

'A team with Tobi ? Are you kidding me ?'

Although he didn’t know what Ninjutsu that guy used just now, being able to restrain him instantly meant he was a top-tier powerhouse, and he had a very deep hostility towards him.

Wouldn’t teaming up with such a person be like a sheep entering a tiger’s mouth ? Absolutely not !

Asakawa Shin also looked disgusted and clamored, « Who wants to be in a group with this brat ? I want to be with Konan ! We can both fly ; it’s a match made in heaven ! »

Konan looked dazed. For some reason, her panty equipment slot had been blocked since a few days ago. These days, she always felt a chill in her lower body, making her restless and her energy greatly reduced.

Being named at this moment, she instinctively resisted, « No, I have already teamed up with Pain. Find someone else. »

Pain was even more on guard, knowing that he could never let Konan be alone with Madara, so he immediately echoed, « Right, don’t mention this matter again. »

Asakawa Shin frowned. He didn’t have any particular candidate he wanted to team up with besides Konan, so he looked around.

Kisame Hoshigaki, a blue-skinned shark, the subordinate of his subordinate. Teaming up was meaningless. Rejected for being ugly.

Sasori of the Red Sand, an ultimate shut-in who turned himself into a figure and stayed in a puppet all the time. His exposed image was even uglier than Kisame’s. Pass.

As for that nameless extra, Asakawa Shin took a look and couldn’t help but show a look of disgust. Next.

Other than that, only Kakuzu and Deidara were left.

Asakawa Shin’s gaze slowly swept across, finally settling on Kakuzu, his eyes revealing a hint of scrutiny and inquiry.

Kakuzu’s heart tightened suddenly, and an unprecedented chill rushed up his spine to his scalp. His sense of crisis was instantly maxed out.

Before he could speak, Asakawa Shin pointed at him with glowing eyes, « I’ve decided ! I want to be in a group with Senior Kakuzu ! »

Kakuzu’s heart shook violently, and he was about to refuse, but Pain’s voice fell indisputably, « Granted ! Tobi and Kakuzu will form a team. This matter is no longer up for discussion. »

High in the tower, Nagato quietly wiped the cold sweat from his forehead. 'Madara can team up with whoever he wants, as long as he doesn’t go after Konan.'

The nameless Ninja on the side secretly rejoiced upon hearing this and couldn’t help but cast a glance of pity at Asakawa Shin.

This kid probably doesn’t know Kakuzu’s notorious reputation as the « Teammate Killer. » Many former partners have become bargaining chips for him to exchange for bounties. There’s going to be a good show now.

In the end, Deidara helplessly chose Sasori of the Red Sand. Although this guy looked like a freak, at least he was more pleasing to the eye than that madman.

Kisame scratched his head, full of suspicion. 'Strange, why didn’t Mr. Madara choose me ? I’m clearly his direct subordinate...'

Seeing the dust settle, Pain immediately announced the meeting’s dismissal and left with the distracted Konan.

Only then did Kakuzu relax his tense expression and approach with a warm face.

« Tobi, from now on we are teammates in the same group. We should get to know each other, shouldn’t we ? »

A sharp light appeared in his eyes as he probed nonchalantly, « By the way, I don’t seem to have heard of your name before ? Do you have a bounty on the black market ? »

Asakawa Shin was shocked when he heard this and looked at Kakuzu in disbelief. Is this guy lusting after my body ?

But Kakuzu wasn’t wrong. Asakawa Shin’s true identity had a bounty of 500 million ryo on the black market, making him a prime target for Kakuzu.

Asakawa Shin narrowed his eyes and replied meaningfully, « Don’t worry, you’ll find out soon enough. »

A few days later, on the road leading to the Land of Hot Springs, this « Immortal Duo » was rushing at full speed.

Of course, the so-called flying was all handled by Asakawa Shin, while Kakuzu was responsible for running bitterly below.

Kakuzu looked up gloomily at the figure floating leisurely in mid-air and sighed inwardly.

After spending these few days together, he had gained some understanding of this kid’s strength.

It seemed that a large portion of his future income would be gone. Thinking of this, Kakuzu felt a bit down.

Without a teammate to kill, what else is there to look forward to in life ?

« Senior Kakuzu, cheer up. The target is just ahead. »

The voice from high above came leisurely, and Kakuzu immediately collected his thoughts, looking forward with piercing eyes.

« This guy is the legendary murderous demon of the Land of Hot Springs ? »

« Don’t underestimate him. »

Asakawa Shin looked down with interest at the figure below holding a Triple-Bladed Scythe, his eyes flashing with fanaticism.

« He also has an immortal body. He’s excellent experimental material. »

At this time, Hidan was kneeling to perform the Jashinism prayer ritual. Hearing this, several veins instantly popped on his forehead, and he roared through gritted teeth, « You bastards, don’t just interrupt someone’s prayer ! »

Asakawa Shin was quite curious about the so-called Jashin Ritual. Observing it up close now, he didn’t find anything strange, so he couldn’t help but urge, « Senior Kakuzu, can I trouble you to take him down ? It doesn’t matter if you’re a bit rough. »

Kakuzu frowned in dissatisfaction, cursing inwardly, 'Why does this kid sound like he’s ordering me around like a subordinate ?'

But thinking of the painful experiences of the past few days, he couldn’t help but shudder.

Forget it, why should he get angry with a junior ?

He locked his fierce gaze on Hidan and snorted coldly, « You think you’re the only fucking one with an immortal body ? »

Before his voice faded, he unscrupulously controlled the Earth Grudge Fear tentacles, turning them into a heavy punch that pierced through Hidan’s heart.

Hidan was incredibly weak and couldn’t react at all. He fell to the ground like a rag doll, twitching and wailing in Pain.

« Ow... it hurts, it hurts so much ! »

However, after a moment, Hidan still hadn’t died.

Asakawa Shin frowned slightly and looked closely, finally discovering a strange Yin Release energy slowly attaching to Hidan, maintaining his life.

« Hehe, caught you, little Evil God. »
