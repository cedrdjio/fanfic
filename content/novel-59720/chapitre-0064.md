# Chapitre 64 — Eight Gates

« These two Guys are really tough ! It’s surprising they aren’t seriously hurt ; they just fainted from exhaustion of physical strength and Chakra. A little treatment and a few days of rest, and they’ll be fully recovered. I just don’t know what kind of trouble they’ll stir up when they wake up~ »

Watching the figures of Naruto and Sasuke being carried away on stretchers, Asakawa Shin couldn’t help but feel a bit emotional : 'Compared to them, Neji and Sakura are completely useless, like fragile college students—they break at the slightest touch...'

« Let us give a round of warm applause to thank contestants Uzumaki Naruto and Uchiha Sasuke for the spectacular duel they brought us ! At the same time, we must congratulate contestant Uchiha Sasuke, who, relying on his tenacious fighting spirit and unyielding resolve, fought his way through and defeated many strong opponents to honorably become the new leader of the boys' group ! »

« With that, our first-ever World’s Greatest Martial Arts Tournament has come to a successful conclusion. Thank you all for your support ! Next, we will present a small bonus event for everyone : a friendly sparring match between this commentator and the referee. If you are interested, please stay and enjoy it. Thank you again for your support, and we’ll see you next time ! »

« Whoa ! » The scene erupted in thunderous applause, and the audience began to discuss excitedly : « I didn’t expect these little kids' matches to be so exciting, especially the last one—it really got my blood boiling ! »

« It really was. The two of them kept falling and getting back up, over and over again, just like they were doing sit-ups. »

« But what was that last move the blond kid used ? It seemed like he failed to execute it ; otherwise, he might have been able to turn the tables. »

« Very likely ! When that move came out, it almost scared the little Uchiha kid’s face green, haha. »

« Next is Lord Shin’s sparring match. I wonder if it will be even more exciting than the last one ? Lord Shin is Hokage-sama’s direct disciple, a genius who developed Rokushiki at such a young age. »

« The other one shouldn’t be underestimated either ; that’s one of the Village’s elite Jonin, possessing immense power ! It’s bound to be a fierce battle ! »

« Now, I will have a friendly sparring match with Guy-senpai. Please be sure to follow the Anbu’s instructions and immediately retreat five hundred meters. Thank you all for your cooperation ! »

Seeing the crowd retreat to the safe zone, Asakawa Shin nodded with satisfaction : « Then, let’s begin. Multi Shadow Clone Technique ! »

The moment the seals were formed, four Shadow Clones flashed and sped off, occupying the four corners of the training ground. Under the stunned gaze of the entire audience, the four Shadow Clones simultaneously formed seals : « Ninjutsu : Four Red Yang Formation ! »

Might Guy looked at the four fire-red barriers that had suddenly risen, his eyes nearly popping out of their sockets in shock. He stammered : « Shin, is this the Four Red Yang Formation ? The barrier Ninjutsu that requires four Kage-level Ninjas to cast together ? Your four Shadow Clones all have Kage-level Chakra reserves ? Isn’t... isn’t that a bit exaggerated ? »

What he didn’t dare to say out loud was : It’s just a sparring match, is there a need for such a grand display ? Anyone who didn’t know better would think I was a rogue Ninja and you were trying to kill me on the spot...

Might Guy swallowed hard. It seemed he would have to use his real skills for this sparring match, otherwise, it wouldn’t do justice to such a grand setup.

Asakawa Shin waved his hand, speaking with feigned humility : « Eh ! What Kage-level Chakra reserves ? That’s all useless stuff~ Besides, Guy, you’re not bad yourself. How many gates of the Eight Gates can you open now ? Can you open the Gate of Death ? »

Hearing this, Might Guy laughed heartily : « My Eight Gates can indeed open the Gate of Death. But that’s a move reserved for Kakashi ; I can’t use it in this sparring match, hahaha. »

« Ha, haha, Kakashi is really lucky to have you... Alright, it’s about time. Let’s begin ! »

« Shin, please go easy on me ! »

Might Guy surveyed the field enclosed by the Four Red Yang Formation and couldn’t help but adopt a serious posture : « Since you can do this much, it seems I have to take this seriously ! Then... Eight Gates, Fifth Gate, Gate of Limit, Open ! »

Asakawa Shin watched intently, but seeing it was only the Gate of Limit, he felt a bit disappointed : « Oh ? Just starting with the Fifth Gate ? It seems I’m being underestimated. »

Might Guy was puzzled upon hearing this : « Underestimated ? Shin, you don’t know, the Fifth Gate, the Gate of Limit, is already a very powerful force. Ordinary Jonin simply cannot withstand this blow. Shin, do you have some misunderstanding about the Eight Gates ? »

« Misunderstanding ? » Asakawa Shin smiled shyly and didn’t answer. Instead, under Might Guy’s astonished gaze, he struck his most familiar pose : « It’s hard to explain in words, Guy. You’ll understand once you see it for yourself. Eight Gates ! First Gate, Gate of Opening, Open ! Second Gate, Gate of Rest, Open ! Third Gate, Gate of Life, Open ! Fourth Gate, Gate of Pain, Open ! Fifth Gate, Gate of Limit, Open ! Sixth Gate, Gate of View, Open ! Seventh Gate, Gate of Wonder, Open ! »

« W-what ? How is this possible ? » Might Guy looked at Shin, who was burning with blue steam opposite him, his face filled with disbelief. He felt like his worldview was about to collapse.

« Guy, the reason I wanted to spar with you is that I wanted to exchange insights on using the Eight Gates. So you don’t have to hold back, come at me with everything you’ve got ! I can take it... »

Asakawa Shin was indeed telling the truth. Ever since he became Hiruzen Sarutobi’s disciple, he had obtained the training method for the Eight Gates. He was extremely interested in this Ninjutsu-taijutsu technique that nearly kicked its way to the series finale.

However, after training, he was surprised to find that the process seemed to have no bottlenecks at all ; he had very easily trained up to the Seventh Gate, the Gate of Wonder.

Moreover, logically speaking, after opening the Eight Gates, the turbulent flow of violent Chakra would damage various parts and organs of the body. But for him, it seemed to have no effect. At least when opening the Seventh Gate, the Gate of Wonder, he didn’t feel any discomfort. Apart from not being able to use Ninjutsu, it was as if that destructive power didn’t exist at all.

This made him both pleasantly surprised and somewhat worried : Is this Eight Gates I’m practicing authentic ? That was why he had arranged this sparring match with Might Guy today.

Might Guy stared fixedly at Asakawa Shin, his mouth opening as if to speak but stopping. After a long while, he took a deep breath and revealed a brilliant smile : « I understand, Shin. I will go all out in this battle ! Of course, except for the Gate of Death ; it’s not the time for me to burn my youth yet ! Hahaha ! Then... Eight Gates ! Sixth Gate, Gate of View, Open ! »
