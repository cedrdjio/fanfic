# Chapitre 71 — The Highly Disciplined Shadow Clone

« It seems that at this speed, it will be impossible to complete the evolution in a short time. »

Sensing the vigorous Natural Energy within Shikkotsu Forest, Asakawa Shin pondered to himself :

'If I want to evolve, I’m afraid it will require a massive amount of Natural Energy. If I return to Konoha, I’m afraid I won’t be able to meet this requirement.

Furthermore, I need to cultivate with a calm heart to strive for completing the evolution as soon as possible ; Konoha seems a bit too noisy.

It would be better to stay in Shikkotsu Forest for now ; this is a good place for secluded cultivation.

It’s just that the Shadow Clone will have to suffer a bit. But it’s just going to school, not going to prison. It should... be fine, right ?'

With this thought in mind, Asakawa Shin no longer hesitated. He quickly formed hand signs and summoned another Shadow Clone.

Asakawa Shin nodded at the Shadow Clone and handed him a sealing scroll :

« Then I’ll leave the research on the Contract Seal to you. As for me, I’ll first renovate the living environment in Shikkotsu Forest. »

The Shadow Clone rolled his eyes at him without a word, took the research materials, and walked aside.

Leaving Asakawa Shin’s main body standing there in the wind, though he quickly recovered.

After all, this wasn’t the first time he had been bullied by his own Shadow Clone ; he was somewhat used to it. At most, he would just curse Tobirama in his heart.

He smiled slightly, unbothered, and turned to look at the surrounding environment.

Asakawa Shin hadn’t originally intended to stay here for long. He didn’t care about the environment of Shikkotsu Forest ; even if it was harsh, it didn’t matter ; he could just tough it out.

But since he was now going to be in secluded cultivation here for a long time, such a terrible environment wouldn’t do ; he couldn’t tolerate it at all.

He turned his head and asked Sage Katsuyu : « Sister Katsuyu, can I build a house of my own here ? I want to be in secluded cultivation in Shikkotsu Forest for a while. »

« Build a house ? Of course you can, but you’re so small, do you even know how to build a house ? »

Sage Katsuyu had no objections. The environment of Shikkotsu Forest was quite comfortable for her, but it wasn’t as friendly to other living beings.

She was also a bit curious and wanted to see how Asakawa Shin would build a house.

« Build a house ? Doesn’t that just require hands ? I’m an expert at it ! »

Asakawa Shin smiled confidently ; years of bitter training were finally coming in handy.

« Sister Katsuyu, just open your eyes wide and watch ! »

Asakawa Shin confirmed the location and stepped into the air using Moon Walk.

He found a relatively dry piece of land, didn’t waste any words, clapped his hands together, and shouted : « Wood Release : Deep Forest Emergence ! »

'Wood Release again ? Could it be Ashura again ? Calculating the time, it seems about right...'

In the shocked gaze of Sage Katsuyu, countless trees began to grow competitively, and within a few dozen breaths, a sea of trees covering several hectares had formed.

These trees were dozens of meters tall, and at the top of their canopies, there was a wooden platform interwoven by thick trunks, completely insulating the humidity and acid mist of Shikkotsu Forest below.

Looking at this square platform, Asakawa Shin nodded with satisfaction :

« This way, the foundation is set. Although it might be damaged by the growth of the trees later, it will be no problem for short-term use for a few years.

Next is time to build the house. Heh heh ! Watch my specialty Ninjutsu ! Wood Release : Four-Pillar House Technique ! »

As Asakawa Shin formed the hand signs and performed the technique, the wooden platform quietly sprouted tender buds, which quickly branched and spread.

In an instant, a grand mansion constructed entirely of wood rose from the ground.

The structure inside the house was well-arranged, with wooden walls separating multiple rooms. Among them, furniture such as wooden beds and wooden tables were meticulously generated, all carefully constructed by Asakawa Shin.

Asakawa Shin examined the bedroom with satisfaction, though it was still missing some bedding and soft furnishings.

He took out a set of silk bedding from the sealing scroll and quickly tidied it up :

« Conditions are limited, so I’ll have to make do with this for now.

Sigh, it’s been more than a day since I left ; I wonder how Naruto and the others are doing today. I miss them. »

...

Meanwhile, at the Ninja Academy.

The Shadow Clone was reading frantically, feeling somewhat annoyed :

'That bastard of a main body, it’s been a whole day and he hasn’t come back yet, and he’s making me come to school again today !

Wait until I learn ten or eight books worth of high-level knowledge today ; once you dispel the Shadow Clone, I’ll stuff it directly into your brain. I’ll make you pass out ! ~'

Just as the Shadow Clone was thinking about his revenge plan, Sasuke walked over, brimming with satisfaction, and happily greeted Asakawa Shin :

« Good afternoon, Shin. You’re so disciplined. I saw you reading books early this morning.

No need to work so hard, right ? Give us ordinary students a way to survive. »

Asakawa Shin saw that it was Sasuke, so he closed the book. He had been reading all day and was a bit tired, so chatting for a while for a change of pace wouldn’t be bad.

« Oh, it’s Sasuke. How is your injury ? Is it all healed ? »

A flash of light passed through Sasuke’s eyes, a wicked smile appeared on the corner of his mouth, and he cleared his throat, speaking solemnly to Asakawa Shin :

« Shin, I was just about to report this matter to you as the leader of the Boys Group.

You see, our monthly competition happens once a month ; isn’t that a bit too frequent ?

After we finish competing, we need at least a week of recovery time. During this period, we can’t exercise at all, and it’s just wasted.

This actually lowers our training efficiency and is completely inconsistent with the original intention of establishing the Six Styles Training Class.

Therefore, I think, shouldn’t this time interval be changed ?

Of course, I’m just making a suggestion ; how to specifically proceed depends on your and Shikamaru’s decision ! »

Asakawa Shin heard Sasuke’s report and also showed a thoughtful expression ; it was indeed a bit too frequent.

By this calculation, a quarter of the month is spent recovering from injuries ; how can the students make progress ?

So Asakawa Shin nodded and praised Sasuke : « Not bad, Sasuke.

You were able to discover this problem ; it seems you really are thinking of the students in the training class.

You’re doing a good job as a leader ; keep it up, I believe in you ! »

Sasuke was full of excitement after hearing this. He didn’t know why, but he was really into being the leader now. Although it had been a short time, he had never felt so good.

Especially after seeing Naruto’s teeth-gritting expression, it was extremely satisfying.

'Being a leader is great ; being a leader is right. I, Uchiha Sasuke, was born to be this leader.'

Asakawa Shin asked casually : « Since it’s your suggestion, how long do you think the interval between competitions should be ? »

« I think six years... »

Sasuke answered cautiously. Seeing Asakawa Shin’s expression change, he quickly corrected himself :

« I mean, six selections in six years would be more appropriate ; it can fully mobilize everyone’s enthusiasm and won’t waste everyone’s time. »

« So, once a year ? That’s reasonable. How about this : you organize everyone to vote publicly, the minority obeys the majority, and let the vote count decide. »

...

« What ? Once a year ? How can that be ! Wouldn’t that mean Sasuke would be the leader for a whole year ?

No, absolutely not ! »

Uzumaki Naruto was shocked and quickly slammed the table to protest.

Sasuke retorted with dissatisfaction : « Why are you shouting so loudly ? If you don’t agree, just cast a dissenting vote. Don’t delay others from voting. Next ! »

In the end, except for Hinata in the Girls Group, and Naruto and Rock Lee in the Boys Group, the majority voted in favor, and the proposal was passed without a doubt.

Uzumaki Naruto : « It’s over, everything is over... »
