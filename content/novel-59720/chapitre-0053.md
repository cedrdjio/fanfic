# Chapitre 53 — An Opening

« A shocking blunder ! ! ! »

Contestant Inuzuka Kiba was actually disqualified by the referee for a serious violation, and contestant Uchiha Sasuke wins directly !

Let’s congratulate contestant Uchiha Sasuke on advancing to the next round of the finals !

« Next, let’s welcome contestant No. 3 of the boys' group, Hyuga Neji, and contestant No. 4, Rock Lee, to the stage ! »

...

Hyuga Neji and Lee looked at each other and moved in unison toward the center of the training ground.

Over the past few weeks, the two had come to Training Ground No. 5 to train hard every day after school ; spending time together day and night, they had become quite familiar with each other.

Neji was initially dismissive when Asakawa Shin called Lee a « genius of hard work, » but later he had to admit that while he couldn’t judge if Lee was a genius, that all-out, painstaking effort was real and impossible to ignore.

When Neji was training, he was training ; when Neji was eating, he was still training ; even when Neji was resting from exhaustion, this guy was still pushing himself to continue training without a moment’s rest.

This level of effort was too exaggerated ; if not for Asakawa Shin’s mastery of Medical Ninjutsu and his frequent treatment of the students' bodies, Lee would likely have collapsed long ago.

Lee knew well that with a constitution unable to use Ninjutsu or Genjutsu, the Rokushiki might be his only way to change his fate, which was why he fought so desperately to the end.

Neji also carried a deep past ; both were trapped by the shackles of fate, yet neither was willing to bow their head.

In this duel, two different types of geniuses were prepared to give their all, to prove themselves and practice the Ninja Way they walked upon.

'Sorry, Lee/Neji...'

'I have a reason why I must win...'

'The winner of this match... will be me !'

...

« Match start ! »

With Might Guy’s command, the situation on the field instantly became tense.

« Byakugan, Activate ! »

Neji knew how troublesome Lee was, so he intended to go all out from the start. He assumed the stance of the Gentle Fist Art, looking like a spider waiting for its prey to step into its web.

'Byakugan and Gentle Fist ?' Lee calmly thought about the information regarding the Gentle Fist.

As everyone knows, the Byakugan possesses a 360-degree field of vision and keen insight, as well as the Gentle Fist Art that seals Chakra pathways once a hit is landed.

Tricky, very tricky ! This combination is a perfect counter for a Taijutsu Ninja like myself !

However, there is a way. Since a single hit means certain defeat, then I just need to make sure he can’t hit me.

'Your Byakugan has a 360-degree field of vision, but your hands can’t possibly attack me from behind, can they ?'

The more Lee thought about it, the brighter his eyes became, but this tactic required his speed to far exceed Neji’s to be realized.

After all, to implement this strategy, he had to ensure that he remained behind Neji at all times while approaching him.

Having made up his mind, Lee finally moved. He slowly circled around Neji, as if looking for an opening.

Neji followed him at first, but after seven or eight circles, he finally couldn’t take it anymore : « Lee, what on earth are you doing ? Are we going to fight or not ? »

Lee ignored him and just kept circling until finally...

Neji seemed to lose patience and took the initiative to attack Lee. Lee didn’t engage him, instead leading Neji on a chase around the arena...

Neji was dumbfounded. What did this guy mean by not fighting and just running ? The most annoying thing was that he actually couldn’t catch up...

Suddenly, Neji was startled : 'Damn it ! Could Lee be trying to exhaust my stamina as much as possible because he has no chance of winning against me ?

What a malicious intention ! Knowing he can’t win, he doesn’t want me to win either ? If my stamina is depleted too much, won’t I definitely lose when I face Uchiha Sasuke ? !'

Thinking of this, cold sweat began to pour down Neji’s forehead.

'In that case, I’ll just wait for you to come to me ! I don’t believe you can keep circling forever !'

Having decided, Neji stopped chasing Lee and instead resumed his Gentle Fist Art stance, waiting for Lee to walk into the trap.

Seeing this, Lee finally breathed a sigh of relief : 'Why was this guy Neji running around everywhere ? Couldn’t he just stay still and let me give him a hard blow from behind ?'

Although things didn’t go exactly as he imagined, he had finally circled around to Neji’s back, hehehe !

The audience below also began to shout in dissatisfaction :

« What’s going on ? Are you two going to fight or not ? »

« Exactly ! Why are you playing hide-and-seek ? Can’t you play that at home when no one’s around ? »

Neji naturally heard them on the stage, and he gritted his teeth in anger : 'What do you mean...'

« An opening ! »

Noticing that Neji was actually distracted during the battle, Lee’s eyes lit up, and he used the Soru technique to launch a fierce attack toward Neji !

« Nani ? ! » Neji was shocked and suddenly snapped back to his senses, wanting to turn and counter Lee, but he didn’t expect Lee to adjust his position accordingly, staying firmly behind him.

'He can actually use the Soru technique consecutively ? This guy !'

The official explanation for Soru is to kick the ground dozens of times at high speed within 0.36 seconds, using the reaction force to achieve short-distance instantaneous movement.

Most students in the Six Styles Training Class can currently only use Chakra to strengthen their feet and barely kick a few times, which puts an extremely heavy burden on their feet.

And there are currently only three students who can perform parts of the Soru technique using pure physical strength...

Lee was precisely one of them !

Neji’s multiple turns at different angles and directions were quickly followed by Lee, and his distance from Neji was getting closer and closer...

As the distance closed, the distance Lee needed to move also shortened, making him appear even more composed.

'I see, is he trying to attack from behind me to avoid a direct confrontation with the Gentle Fist ?

Damn it, if only I knew Heavenly Spin, I could easily break this move.

But you think you can defeat me like this ? Too naive...

As for Soru... I can do it too !'

With a thought, Neji immediately performed the Soru technique and fled forward, trying to widen the distance from Lee and turn to face his enemy.

Lee’s expression darkened, and he gritted his teeth to follow. The two were back in a chase, only now the roles of the pursuer and the pursued were reversed.

Neji was now drenched in sweat. Even after using Soru multiple times consecutively, he still couldn’t widen the distance from Lee. He felt he was reaching his limit, and the pain in his feet could no longer support him to continue running...

'Damn it, I can’t just sit and wait for death, I’ll fight him !'

Seeing this, Lee breathed a sigh of relief ; his feet were also reaching their limit :

'Finally stopped hiding ? Good ! Let’s settle this, Neji !'

The distance between the two rapidly closed. The surrounding audience also grew tense, watching the arena without blinking.

At the moment they were about to collide, a hint of craftiness flashed in Neji’s eyes.

His feet gripped the ground firmly, his waist bent backward, and his upper body formed a bizarre 90-degree angle with his legs, actually performing an « Iron Bridge » move to counter Lee !

« Eight Trigrams Eight Palms ! »

« Nani ? ! ! »
