# Chapitre 89 — Transaction

« I have a general understanding of the situation. Your subordinate has been severely eroded by the Xuanyuan Group’s Explosive Bug, and his life is hanging by a thread.

And you, Lord Fourth Raikage~ there is also this kind of Explosive Bug hidden inside your body, and you could follow in your subordinate’s footsteps at any moment. That’s correct, isn’t it ? »

Tsunade stared at the Raikage, her eyes brightening the more she spoke, until finally, she became somewhat excited.

« Uhm, that’s correct. Please, Lady Tsunade, lend us a hand and help us ! » Amayi replied, trembling, his head covered in cold sweat. He kept feeling that the current Lady Tsunade had such a powerful and terrifying aura.

Tsunade’s gaze swept across the room, lingering briefly on Uchiha Itachi’s Mangekyo Sharingan before shifting to her precious junior brother, pausing for a moment on the seedling atop his head.

Then, a brilliant smile bloomed on her face. She reached out a jade-like hand toward the Raikage and playfully rubbed her fingers together :

« So, how much do you all think the Raikage’s life is worth ? Let me just throw out a number : three billion ryo, how about that ? »

« Three billion ryo ? You really are just throwing out numbers ! I think thirty ryo is more like it. Don’t even think about it ; even if I die out there today, I won’t pay you this amount of money. »

A was furious. Although their Hidden Cloud Village was one of the Five Great Ninja Villages, trying to come up with three billion ryo would undoubtedly hurt their foundation. He simply could not afford this black-hearted medical bill.

« Die out there ? You can do that as soon as you step outside. Those Ninjas outside followed you here, right ? They must be the so-called Xuanyuan Group. » Asakawa Shin said unhurriedly, secretly pressuring the Raikage.

Tsunade gave him an appreciative look, then continued to persuade : « Money is just an external possession ; you can always earn more if it’s gone. Besides, your Hidden Cloud Village has always been good at taking things by force ; this amount of money shouldn’t be hard for you to get, right ? »

A’s expression clearly faltered. After some internal struggle, he finally made up his mind and, gritting his teeth, stated his bottom line : « Three hundred million ryo ! This is the limit ; I can’t pay a single ryo more. »

Tsunade pondered for a moment, then slowly nodded : « Deal. But I have one more condition. I recently need a large amount of thunder-clap grass for medicine, and it must have been struck by lightning within the last twenty-four hours. You are the Raikage ; this shouldn’t be too difficult for you, right ? »

A silently breathed a sigh of relief. Fortunately, Tsunade didn’t make another exorbitant demand. If it was just thunder-clap grass, although troublesome, it could still be done if he mobilized the village’s manpower.

Therefore, he nodded solemnly and said in a deep voice : « Very well, then this transaction is considered concluded. Please hurry and treat Karai. »

« Hmm, lead the way ! »

...

« Karai, I’ve brought the world’s strongest medical Ninja—Lady Tsunade. Your life is saved ! »

Beside the sickbed, Karai lay there listlessly. Hearing his companion say this, he also breathed a sigh of relief. If he could live, he wanted to fight for it.

Tsunade walked forward in a great mood, not wasting any words, and began checking him directly :

« The situation is a bit tricky. The Explosive Bugs are tightly intertwined with the meridians in the body, and they have already grown to this size. Even the slightest stimulation could cause them to explode.

However, it can still be treated. Shizune, prepare for surgery. »

Upon hearing this, Amayi finally relaxed a little, but then, thinking of something, he hurriedly asked : « Then what about Lord Raikage ? What is his condition ? »

After all, Tsunade had taken their money, so she patiently explained : « Rest assured, his condition is much better.

Because of his Lightning Release secret technique, his Chakra is quickly consumed by the technique as soon as it is released, so the Explosive Bugs don’t have time to absorb it and grow.

However, Ninjas naturally use Chakra in their daily lives, so the Explosive Bugs inside him will soon grow to a visible size.

By then, a simple surgical procedure will easily solve it. Before that, just be careful not to use Ninjutsu. »

At this moment, Shizune had finished the anesthesia and reported to Tsunade : « Lady Tsunade, the surgical preparations are complete ; we can begin. »

Tsunade nodded : « Hmm, then I’ll leave this surgery to you, Shizune. »

A looked over in astonishment and asked, confused : « What do you mean ? Aren’t you going to perform the surgery yourself ? I paid so that... »

Before he could finish, he was rudely interrupted by Tsunade : « Shut up ! Let Shizune perform the surgery. If any accident happens, I will take full responsibility ! Since you chose me, then trust me !

Hoo... begin, Shizune. »

Shizune put on her gloves and held the scalpel, her face full of seriousness : « Now, we begin the chest opening surgery and the Explosive Bug removal surgery. I’ll leave the hemostasis to you, Amayi. »

« No problem ! »

However, just as the scalpel cut into the flesh, the Explosive Bugs seemed to sense danger and instantly absorbed another burst of Chakra, growing a size larger.

Shizune was shocked and quickly withdrew the blade, but the edge still sliced through Karai’s blood vessel. A spurt of blood shot out, spraying right onto Tsunade’s face.

Tsunade went completely stiff, and then her pupils contracted violently. Her body began to tremble uncontrollably.

Asakawa Shin asked with some concern : « Senior Sister, are you alright ? »

Upon hearing this, Tsunade’s body trembled again. She suppressed the panic in her heart and smiled somewhat forcedly : « Senior Sister is fine, just a little tired. I’m going outside to get some air. »

Watching her retreating figure, which seemed to cower, A finally reacted and realized with a start :

« I understand... it’s blood !

I never expected that the one hailed as the strongest medical Ninja in the Ninja World would suffer from hemophobia. No wonder she wouldn’t perform the surgery herself.

For a medical Ninja, this is simply a fatal curse. It’s such a pity. »

He turned his head, his gaze falling on Shizune, his tone filled with inquiry : « That person named Shizune, I want to know, why does Tsunade suffer from such a strange illness ? Can you tell me ? »

Shizune’s expression was grim, her eyes revealing deep pain : « It was the war... It took away the two most important people in Lady Tsunade’s life. Ever since then, she has... »

Touching upon this heavy past, everyone present fell into silence.

Asakawa Shin, concerned for Tsunade, spoke up : « I’ll go check on her. »

He walked slowly outside and, sure enough, found Tsunade’s figure in a dark corner.

She was curled up in the shadows, looking exceptionally helpless and fragile.

Asakawa Shin sighed softly, walked up slowly, opened his arms to embrace her, and comforted her softly :

« Senior Sister, I don’t know how to soothe your pain, but please remember, I will always stand by your side.

You must pull yourself together... I... I am very worried about you. »

Tsunade shuddered, slowly raised her head, and gazed tearfully at the small figure before her.

Her gaze was unconsciously drawn to the necklace on his chest. A moment later, as if having found her only support, she reached out and pulled him tightly into her arms, sobbing uncontrollably.

« I’m so scared... so sad... I just want to see them one more time... »

Letting Tsunade hold him and cry for a good while, Asakawa Shin finally patted her back and gently consoled her : « Senior Sister, the dead are gone. Those of us who are alive must always look forward. Only then can those who have passed away leave in peace.

Senior Sister, if you are truly sad, why not treat me as your younger brother, or your lover, and cry your heart out to me. »

After venting, Tsunade actually felt much better. Hearing him say this now, she couldn’t help but feel a touch of amusement in her heart.

A younger brother would be fine, but where did a lover come from ? She muttered to herself, it’s not like she hadn’t seen it before ; with you being such a little shrimp, you couldn’t even use it, right ?

Thinking of this, she actually broke into laughter through her tears and teased Asakawa Shin : « Shin, if you want to be a lover, you need to grow up quickly. You are still far from enough right now. »
