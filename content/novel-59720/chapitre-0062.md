# Chapitre 62 — Awakening

« Covered in sweat ? Me ? »

Uchiha Sasuke slowly raised his hand and touched his forehead, only to be surprised to find it already soaked. It turned out that, without realizing it, he had already broken into a cold sweat !

After his tense emotions relaxed, a heavy sound like a bellows pumping began to echo in his ears. This sudden sound startled Uchiha Sasuke again :

'Hmm ? What is that sound ? ...Heh, what the hell ! It’s just my own breathing...'

Uchiha Sasuke couldn’t help but feel a shock in his heart ; he hadn’t expected himself to be so exhausted without even noticing it.

It must be because he was too focused on dodging Uzumaki Naruto’s attacks, which led to a dulling of his senses.

No wonder Uzumaki Naruto’s attack frequency was so rapid ; it turned out he didn’t want him to realize his true intentions.

'Damn it ! What a miscalculation...'

« Uchiha Sasuke, you’d better just surrender ! The position of team leader must be held by the strongest, and you are far from qualified ! Let me, Uzumaki Naruto, continue as team leader and lead everyone ! Don’t worry, Uchiha Sasuke, as long as I have a mouthful of meat to eat, you’ll have a bone to gnaw on ! »

Uzumaki Naruto, seeing that Uchiha Sasuke had realized his situation, immediately struck while the iron was hot, attempting to persuade him to surrender.

To increase his chances of success, he simply brought out the same rhetoric he had overheard Shin using to persuade Shikamaru.

Thinking of how loyal Shikamaru had been to Shin ever since, he felt secretly pleased, thinking that Uchiha Sasuke would surely be deeply moved by this, right ?

« As long as you have a mouthful of meat to eat, I’ll have a bone to gnaw on ? » Uchiha Sasuke repeated in a low voice, his tone full of disbelief, and even his pupils were trembling slightly.

Uzumaki Naruto, seeing this, felt a surge of excitement, knowing that these words had worked. As expected of Shin’s rhetoric, the effect was immediate !

He nodded solemnly, his face blossoming with that signature brilliant smile, his gaze sincere and fiery, instantly pushing his charisma to its peak.

Uchiha Sasuke also returned a brilliant smile : 'Fine, fine, fine ! Uzumaki Naruto, you’re treating me like a dog ! I must defeat you today, otherwise, where will there be a place for me in this Six Styles Training Class ?'

Thinking of this, a new force seemed to surge through Uchiha Sasuke’s body, permeating his limbs. He knew deep down that this power was called dignity. Today, he must not lose !

« Enough talk, Uzumaki Naruto ! If you want the position of team leader, you’ll have to get past me first. Prepare to die ! »

Uchiha Sasuke finished speaking and lunged forward, no longer showing any signs of his previous exhaustion. Every punch and kick was full of power, and for a moment, he and Uzumaki Naruto were evenly matched.

« Hey ? Why did you suddenly start fighting ? Do you think one bone is too little ? If you support me as team leader, I can decide to give you all the bones ! »

Uzumaki Naruto continued to try and persuade him while trading blows with Uchiha Sasuke.

No reason, right ? This rhetoric works when Shin uses it, why doesn’t it work when I use it ?

Unexpectedly, it would have been better if Uzumaki Naruto hadn’t tried to persuade him ; under this persuasion, Uchiha Sasuke became even more ferocious : « Shut the hell up ! Today, only one of us will remain standing. Die ! »

In an instant, Uchiha Sasuke’s offensive became even more fierce, making Uzumaki Naruto somewhat overwhelmed and struggling to defend.

A flash of light appeared in Uzumaki Naruto’s eyes. He decisively adopted a defensive stance, using minimal movements to drain Uchiha Sasuke’s stamina.

In this way, the tactical situation between the two dramatically reversed !

It must be said that Uzumaki Naruto’s strategy was extremely effective.

After all, this was Uchiha Sasuke’s third match of the day ; even if he could burst out with power during the fight, it would be difficult to maintain for long.

As his stamina rapidly depleted, his offensive gradually weakened. Eventually, Uzumaki Naruto caught an opening, punched him in the cheek, sent him flying, and he crashed heavily to the ground, unable to get up.

Seeing that Uchiha Sasuke had finally fallen, Uzumaki Naruto couldn’t help but heave a sigh of relief : « Uchiha Sasuke, this guy is simply ungrateful and impossible to communicate with ! But fortunately, I am the more skilled one in this match. Let me continue as team leader, hahaha ! »

On the other side, Uchiha Sasuke lay on his back, his limbs spread out limply, as if all his strength had been drained away.

He stared at the clear sky above, his mind full of thoughts. Uzumaki Naruto’s wanton and arrogant laughter echoed in his ears, sounding so harsh and noisy.

He struggled to lift his body again to continue the fight, but his arms trembled uncontrollably, his strength long exhausted, and he eventually fell back to the ground powerlessly.

He recalled the events of the past month—Uzumaki Naruto’s day-after-day provocations and mockery, his special training with his brother under the quiet moonlight, and the thrilling, unexpected turns when facing Inuzuka Kiba and Rock Lee...

Finally, everything froze on Uzumaki Naruto’s incredibly brilliant smile—Uchiha Sasuke, as long as I have a mouthful of meat to eat, you’ll have a bone to gnaw on.

'I went through so much hardship just to stand here. Is all this effort destined to come to nothing ? Did I fight with all my might just to become Uzumaki Naruto’s lackey ? A bone ? Heh.'

'But... I am exhausted, I can’t fight anymore... Damn it ! Why am I so weak and powerless ? I crave power, I crave to smash that smiling face of Uzumaki Naruto ! It should be Uzumaki Naruto lying here ! It shouldn’t be like this !'

Intertwined with regret and unwillingness, Uchiha Sasuke’s eyes slowly began to turn red...

A strand of special Chakra was born in his mind, which then acted upon his optic nerves. In his eyes, a black Tomoe gradually emerged ; that Tomoe, like a gear of fate, began to slowly rotate.

Uchiha Sasuke, at this very moment, under the intense stimulation of Uzumaki Naruto, had awakened the legendary ocular technique—the Sharingan.

Shin’s eye twitched, and he looked at Uchiha Sasuke with an indescribable expression, inwardly complaining : 'You’ve got to be kidding me, big brother... Just because of a few taunts from Uzumaki Naruto, his eyes got hot and he awakened the Sharingan ? I remember in the original work it wasn’t...'

The audience began to discuss among themselves : « Look ! That Uchiha boy has stood up again, and his eyes... »

« There’s no mistake ! That is the Uchiha Clan’s Bloodline Limit—the Sharingan ! He has awakened the Sharingan ! »

« It’s truly unbelievable, he’s only six years old, right ? He’s a classmate of my son ! To awaken it at such a young age ! »

Only after hearing the audience’s words did Uzumaki Naruto realize something. He turned around slightly stiffly, and sure enough...

Uchiha Sasuke had already stood up, his pair of red eyes staring fixedly at him, with a ferocious smile on his face : « Yo ! Uzumaki Naruto, we meet again~ Now, it’s the second round ! »

Uzumaki Naruto covered his head with both hands, somewhat breaking down : « Uchiha Sasuke, you bastard ! How did you get up again ! Are you the one from the Uzumaki Clan or am I ? Are you that durable ? »
