# Chapitre 69 — Practicing Sage Art

« Manda ? Which Manda ? Is it the Manda I know ? That little punk from Ryūchi Cave ? »

Sage Katsuyu’s tone was odd ; she had never expected it to actually be this « formidable enemy. »

« Yes, that’s right, it’s exactly that Manda ! He is the summoning beast of my senior brother, the 'Cold-Blooded' Orochimaru.

His personality is cold-blooded and cruel, and his strength can be described as unfathomable.

When we meet him, Sister Katsuyu, you must perform well and help me boost my momentum ! »

Watching Asakawa Shin’s enthusiastic appearance, Sage Katsuyu shook her head helplessly ; it seemed that Manda would have to suffer a bit when the time came.

After all, it was a request from her own successor ; what else could she do ? She just had to spoil him.

« Alright, Sister agrees. When the time comes, Sister will definitely strike a cool pose and cooperate with you properly.

However, little brother Shin, you must also practice hard. Summoning Sister’s main body is not simple.

It’s not just a matter of size ; Sister’s main body is filled to the brim with Sage Chakra.

Compared to the little toads of Mount Myōboku, it’s not on the same order of magnitude. If you want to summon my entire main body, it’s estimated that you would need to use a Sage Art-level summoning jutsu to possibly pull it off ! »

After hearing this, a solemn expression appeared on Asakawa Shin’s face, and he firmly promised Sage Katsuyu :

« Sister Katsuyu, rest assured, I will never slack off in my training !

It is precisely because I have been training diligently day and night that I possess today’s strength.

The purpose of my trip here is specifically to learn Sage Art. Since time is tight, why don’t we start immediately ? »

Sage Katsuyu nodded in agreement. If Asakawa Shin could finish practicing Sage Art sooner, she could go out and play sooner. Thinking of this, a trace of impatience welled up in her heart.

Sage Katsuyu’s main body squirmed for a moment, and a small, delicate Katsuyu split off from it, slowly crawling toward Asakawa Shin.

The small Katsuyu stopped beside Asakawa Shin, then extracted a scroll and a bottle of solution that was constantly bubbling with green gas from her body.

Noticing Asakawa Shin’s curious gaze, Sage Katsuyu patiently explained :

« This scroll is the cultivation method for Shikkotsu Forest’s Sage Art—the final version improved by generations of successors.

So-called Sage Art is the step where you extract physical energy and spiritual energy to synthesize Chakra, then add an extra portion of Natural Energy according to a specific ratio to synthesize Sage Chakra.

However, this step is extremely dangerous. The ratio of Natural Energy has very strict requirements ; once a mistake is made, the Natural Energy will go berserk and erode the user’s body, eventually leading to the user’s body turning to stone and returning completely to nature.

But little brother Shin, you don’t need to worry. You will be practicing on my main body ; if anything goes wrong, I will promptly extract the Natural Energy from your body to keep you safe.

And to control Natural Energy, the first step is, of course, to perceive it. This bottle of strong acid was specially synthesized by my main body for this purpose. After you dilute it and apply it to your body, you will be able to sense and absorb Natural Energy. »

Asakawa Shin suddenly realized, but he only picked up the scroll and started reading it, completely ignoring the bottle of acid that looked very ominous. Facing Sage Katsuyu’s puzzled gaze, he smiled proudly and said with some relief :

« It seems I was born with the ability to perceive Natural Energy, so this bottle of acid shouldn’t be of any use to me. »

Upon hearing this, Sage Katsuyu was greatly surprised. She took a deep look at her successor ; it seemed this little brother Shin was also not simple. Could it be...

Not long after, Asakawa Shin had carefully studied the scroll, and the technique for refining Sage Chakra was completely clear to him.

He faced Sage Katsuyu with a solemn expression and bowed deeply : « In that case, I will begin practicing Sage Art. I’ll be troubling you, Sister Katsuyu. »

« Mm, then come on up. »

...

Asakawa Shin sat cross-legged on Sage Katsuyu’s back, eyes closed tightly, calming his mind and focusing, making adjustments before his first attempt.

Since evolving into the Wood Release Sage Body, he had been able to perceive the mysterious factors in the air—Natural Energy—but he had never tried to absorb it. Today was his first experiment.

With a thought from Asakawa Shin, a wisp of minute Natural Energy in the air slowly surged into his right index finger according to his will.

Unlike the safety and docility it displayed externally, as soon as this wisp of Natural Energy entered Asakawa Shin’s body, it began to riot violently, rapidly eroding his physical cells. Asakawa Shin’s right index finger actually began to swell at a speed visible to the naked eye.

Seeing this, Sage Katsuyu hurriedly controlled herself, split off a small Katsuyu, and with a light leap, it jumped onto Asakawa Shin’s right hand, quickly absorbing the out-of-control Natural Energy.

As the Natural Energy dissipated, Asakawa Shin’s right hand also recovered to its original state within just a few breaths.

Sage Katsuyu asked with concern : « Little brother Shin, are you okay ? The moment Natural Energy enters your body, you must start synthesizing Sage Chakra, otherwise, this situation will occur.

But it doesn’t matter, I’m here. Just feel free to try ; I said I would keep you safe, so rest assured. »

Hearing Sage Katsuyu’s concern, Asakawa Shin finally came back to his senses and said gratefully to Sage Katsuyu : « Thank you so much for just now, Sister. I will be more careful next time. »

After speaking, Asakawa Shin closed his eyes again, seemingly having gained some insight.

Sage Katsuyu did not know that Asakawa Shin’s inner state at this moment was far from as calm as he appeared on the surface. He was carefully sensing his right index finger that had just mutated, exclaiming continuously in his heart :

'What’s going on ? I didn’t feel wrong just now, did I ? My body seems to have eaten some Natural Energy ? How could this be ?

The Sage Art cultivation scroll didn’t mention this situation at all...

Is it because of the Wood Release Sage Body ? That shouldn’t be it either ; Shodai(1st)-sama’s scroll didn’t record this situation either.'

Asakawa Shin controlled Life Return to carefully examine the places in his body where the Natural Energy had just passed, discovering that these areas were significantly more active than other parts, and the vitality contained within the cells had also increased.

Or rather, the degree of evolution had advanced a step further than the rest of his body.

'Could it be that my unknown constitution, which is still in transformation, caused this situation ?

My cells are actually able to absorb Natural Energy to accelerate evolution, but why can’t my skin do it ?

Could it be that I need to actively bring Natural Energy into my body to absorb it ? This is actually good news...

It’s just that Natural Energy is too violent and cannot be utilized directly, but if I can fuse Natural Energy into Sage Chakra, perhaps it would be possible.

Whether it works or not, I’ll know after trying.'

Thinking of this, Asakawa Shin focused his mind, began to absorb a small wisp of Natural Energy, and attempted to fuse it into Sage Chakra.

Completely different from the difficulties he had anticipated, he actually effortlessly fused this wisp of Natural Energy with his own physical energy and spiritual energy according to the precise ratio, transforming it into Sage Chakra.

This process was unexpectedly smooth, leaving Asakawa Shin somewhat incredulous for a moment :

« Huh ? I seem to have... succeeded in my cultivation ? »
