# Chapitre 155 — Easy as Pie

Three days later.

As the Natural Energy in the air grew increasingly sparse, Asakawa Shin helplessly sighed.

Cultivating too quickly was also a kind of trouble. The Natural Energy accumulated over a thousand years by others was nearly exhausted by him in just three days.

The White Snake Sage had already come several times, each time looking more displeased than the last. The final time, she even spoke with a tearful voice.

'Alright, alright, this old ancestor is about to leave seclusion.'

If he didn’t leave seclusion soon, he was genuinely afraid the White Snake Sage would come here to throw a tantrum and roll on the ground.

'Still, it truly lives up to being one of the three great sacred lands. A mere three days here surpasses years of hard work outside.'

Seeing that Ryūchi Cave had no more 'oil' to squeeze, Asakawa Shin immediately decided to get up and leave.

With a flash of his body, he arrived at the entrance of Ryūchi Cave. The moment he stepped out of the secret realm, he vanished again, reappearing the next instant back in his home in Konoha.

Sensing this, the White Snake Sage was overjoyed to the point of tears. 'Children ! It’s New Year’s today !'

...

At home, the little slug, upon seeing Asakawa Shin return, let out a surprised cry and eagerly rushed up to greet him.

But upon getting closer and noticing three small snakes coiled around his body, she immediately puffed up in anger and let her head droop dejectedly.

'Lord Shin, what are they... ?'

Asakawa Shin smiled, picked her up, and gently placed her on his shoulder, saying proudly, 'From now on, they’ll be your underlings. Surprised ? Happy ?'

' ?'

The little slug blinked, a glint of wisdom flashing in her eyes.

'Hmph, these three little snakes must have taken human form and used their beauty to bewilder Lord Shin. How shameless.'

As she thought this, she suddenly had another startling realization.

'Maybe they even dream of replacing me ! This must be a plot by Ryūchi Cave, that old thing White Snake... No, I absolutely cannot let them succeed.'

Taking advantage of Asakawa Shin going to take a bath, she immediately put on a stern face and ordered the three little snakes, 'You lot, come here for a moment.'

Although the three little snakes didn’t understand why, they knew the true identity of this tiny figure before them and dared not disobey in the slightest. They obediently gathered close.

'Lady Katsuyu, what are your instructions ?'

'Hmm, I remember you all can transform into humans. What technique is this ? Tell me quickly !'

After finishing his bath, Asakawa Shin went straight to the Hokage Office, only to find that the person seated on the Hokage’s throne was not Hiruzen Sarutobi, but Senju Tobirama.

Seeing Asakawa Shin arrive, Hiruzen Sarutobi felt as if a great weight had been lifted off his shoulders.

These past few days, he had been constantly lectured and pressured by Senju Tobirama. One old incident after another was dredged up. Despite his age, he was almost being scolded like a grandson.

Tears welling in his eyes, he grabbed Asakawa Shin’s hand. 'Little Shin, have you come today because you need your teacher’s help with something important ?'

Asakawa Shin nodded. 'That’s right, teacher. Today, I actually wanted to ask you for advice about a Ninjutsu.'

Hearing this, Hiruzen Sarutobi immediately felt he had regained some face in front of Senju Tobirama.

He straightened his chest, patting it with a thump. 'No problem, Little Shin ! Whatever Ninjutsu you want to learn, just name it. Your teacher will teach you everything he knows.'

'Oh, it’s that Telescope Technique you’ve always kept to yourself. I...'

Before he could finish, Hiruzen Sarutobi hurriedly interrupted. 'What Telescope Technique ? What kind of technique is that ? Your teacher doesn’t know any such thing. Don’t talk nonsense.'

Asakawa Shin scratched his head, looking utterly confused. 'It’s that technique that uses the crystal ball, the one you often use to peek into the women’s bathhouse. I find its principles quite interesting and wanted to study them a bit.'

Before Hiruzen Sarutobi could reply, Senju Tobirama instantly appeared behind him and spoke in an eerie tone :

'Oh ? Monkey, you’ve been hiding such a powerful Ninjutsu, and your teacher was completely unaware. Why don’t you tell me about it ? Let your teacher 'learn' from it properly.'

After a commotion, Hiruzen Sarutobi, eyes full of resentment, handed a Ninjutsu scroll to Asakawa Shin.

Behind him, Senju Tobirama called out with concern, 'Some Ninjutsu can be studied, but remember not to misuse them. You’re still young...'

Before he finished speaking, Asakawa Shin had already slipped out of the Hokage Office, leaving this Forbidden Jutsu Master standing there in disarray.

Senju Tobirama stood awkwardly for a moment, then turned his dissatisfied gaze toward Hiruzen Sarutobi beside him :

'Monkey, it seems your life as Hokage these past few years has been quite 'fulfilling.' Come, come, let’s teacher and disciple have a good 'discussion.'

Returning home, Asakawa Shin eagerly began studying the 'Telescope Technique.'

His fingers flew, swiftly forming hand seals as he channeled Chakra into the crystal ball before him.

In an instant, light and shadow swirled on the ball’s surface, clearly reflecting scenes from within Konoha Village. Combined with his Observation Haki, even the slightest movement within dozens of kilometers was laid bare.

He switched scenes with great interest. The crystal ball was like a series of flowing paintings, presenting images of his classmates one by one before his eyes.

Finally, the image settled on the bright, smiling face of a little blond.

Asakawa Shin raised an eyebrow slightly. He hadn’t expected Naruto to still meet Jiraiya. This connection was truly wonderful, and they seemed to be getting along quite harmoniously.

Asakawa Shin gathered his focus and carefully stored the crystal ball away properly.

In just a short moment, he had grasped the wonderful utility of this divine tool.

Then he cleared his throat lightly, and with a flash of his body, headed in the direction where Naruto and Jiraiya were.

At that moment, Jiraiya was instructing Naruto in practicing the Rasengan. Suddenly, his back stiffened, and he looked toward a certain direction with a grave expression.

Not long after, Asakawa Shin arrived from that direction like a swift wind.

Jiraiya’s eyes flickered. 'Is this my junior brother ? Such immense Chakra... I’ve never heard of anything like it.'

Asakawa Shin was also sizing up Jiraiya, feeling quite curious.

This was his first time seeing this legendary Sannin. He wondered how the man’s journey across the Ninja World searching for the so-called 'Child of Prophecy' was going.

Just as the two were secretly assessing each other, a voice full of delight broke the silence.

'Shin ! Did you come to watch me train ?'

Naruto excitedly rushed over, his face beaming with pride. 'Let me tell you, I learned a super awesome Ninjutsu these past few days, and right now I’m training in another even more awesome one ! It’ll definitely surprise you all !'

As he spoke, his eyes shifted, glancing at Jiraiya beside him. Then he went over and started shaking the man’s arm, acting cute.

'Pervy Sage, hurry up and show Shin that move ! It’s a really, really awesome Ninjutsu !'

Faced with Uzumaki Naruto’s pleading gaze, Jiraiya helplessly scratched his head, a hint of exasperation in his eyes.

'Alright, I’ll make an exception and demonstrate it once for you. Watch closely.'
