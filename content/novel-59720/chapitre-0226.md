# Chapitre 226 — Hold On!

Nagato rose slowly, the corner of his mouth curling into a confident smirk that looked down upon the world.

As he clenched his fist slightly.

The Chakra surging like a tsunami within his body and the destructive ocular power flowing wantonly through his Rinnegan all declared his absolute status as a ruler.

He was invincible.

After all, he possessed the Rinnegan of the legendary Six Paths Sage ; these eyes were enough to make the world bow in worship.

Since childhood, he had known deep down that he was a special existence chosen by God, a deity descended into the world.

Yet now, a mere mortal dared to spout nonsense before him, which sent him into a rage.

« A mere ant, and you dare to look directly at a god ? »

« Asakawa Shin, let us settle this here and now ! »

Nagato approached slowly, his Chakra erupting like a volcano, causing his red hair to stand on end, while the rocks beneath his feet cracked inch by inch under the terrifying pressure.

Konan clutched her skirt tightly, the starlight of hope reigniting in her dead eyes as she wondered if this aura could truly defeat that man.

Asakawa Shin couldn’t help but look at him properly, silently evaluating : Uzumaki constitution, Hashirama cells, plus the Rinnegan.

This amount of Chakra was already equivalent to a quarter of what he had within himself.

Truly powerful !

Such a perfect, healthy Nagato had finally reached the standard for crafting the Six Paths of Pain.

Since that was the case, he might as well finish this quickly, kill him, and then use Edo Tensei to bring him back and claim a spot in the world of the living.

Thinking this, a pleasant smile hung on Asakawa Shin’s face, like one watching prey fall into a trap.

Nagato was stunned at the sight, his confident smile fading slightly as a flicker of annoyance at being looked down upon flashed in his eyes.

He had displayed such overwhelming power, yet the enemy could still smile so easily ; this was simply blasphemy against a god.

« Putting on an act ! »

Nagato let out a low shout and abruptly raised his right arm, his flesh instantly twisting and restructuring into a hideous mechanical cannon.

Dozens of micro-missiles, trailing white smoke, whistled as they bombarded Asakawa Shin.

Facing this rain of fire, Asakawa Shin remained indifferent, his right hand gently moving to the hilt of his sword at his waist.

« Clang— ! »

The blade drew from its sheath, a cold gleam cutting through the sky like lightning.

The next moment, the incoming missiles froze in mid-air, then were neatly sliced in two, exploding into clusters of brilliant fireworks.

Nagato’s pupils contracted violently ; with the Rinnegan’s astonishing insight, he had completely failed to see what had just happened.

He couldn’t even understand the principle behind it.

Why was the blade able to cut all the targets in mid-air when it clearly hadn’t touched the missiles ?

That level of precise control seemed as if it had been measured by precision calipers ; such swordsmanship was simply horrifying.

Asakawa Shin sheathed his sword and smiled.

« Stunned, aren’t you ? »

« Never seen a Great Swordsman’s slash before, have you ? »

« Country bumpkin. »

That smile reflected in Nagato’s eyes was nothing short of the most direct provocation, instantly igniting the fury in his heart.

He stomped his right foot on the ground, his body surged forward, and he instantly closed in on Asakawa Shin, carrying an astonishing momentum.

He naturally knew that Asakawa Shin’s swordsmanship might be more lethal at close range, but the comprehensive abilities granted by the Rinnegan gave him absolute confidence.

Whether it was the Deva Path’s Bansho Tenin and Shinra Tensei, or the Human Path’s soul-extracting instant death, they were all unsolvable kill moves for close-quarters combat.

Moreover, the Hashirama cells had granted him an extremely robust physique, and combined with the Rinnegan’s all-seeing ocular power, he feared no challenge.

Seeing Nagato take the initiative to start close-quarters combat, Asakawa Shin couldn’t help but be slightly stunned.

« Not only are you not begging for mercy, but you dare to face me head-on ? You must have a death wish. »

He tightened his grip on the kusanagi sword, and pitch-black Armament Haki instantly crawled over the blade, exuding a chilling aura.

His footsteps were just as fast ; he turned into an afterimage and charged straight at Nagato.

The two were already extremely close, and with their incredible speed, this distance was covered in the blink of an eye.

Asakawa Shin struck with his sword head-on, the blade enveloped in black and red arcs of electricity, crashing down like the sky collapsing.

Nagato had originally been full of momentum, but facing this earth-shattering sword strike, he felt every hair on his body stand on end, and a bone-chilling cold rushed straight to his heart.

If he couldn’t dodge, he would surely die !

In the blink of an eye, he instinctively dodged to the side, barely avoiding the edge of the blade.

Just as he breathed a sigh of relief, a terrifying gust of wind swept across his chest and abdomen ; he felt as if he had been struck by lightning, staggering back to create distance once more.

« What happened ? It clearly didn’t hit me. »

Before his confusion could fade, a ray of sunset light shone from behind him.

His body stiffened ; he looked to the side and saw that along the trajectory the blade had just traced, a rift valley stretched into the distance, its depth unknown.

The giant tree wall that Konan had condensed with her secret technique had also been sliced vertically, tearing open a huge gap.

Hearing is believing, but seeing is the truth.

Such astonishing destructive power made a wave of unease surge in Nagato’s heart.

The chill from just a moment ago was no illusion.

If there had been the slightest error in that strike, he would likely have truly died.

It was simply more dangerous than Dust Release.

Nagato cursed inwardly : « What a cunning brat ; who uses their ultimate move right from the start ? »

If he were to say this out loud, Asakawa Shin would surely scoff loudly :

« What ultimate move ? It was just a casual basic attack. »

Nagato, however, didn’t know this, and instead felt somewhat smug.

Everyone knew that ultimate moves not only consumed a lot of mana but also had long cooldowns.

With such terrifying power, it must be a secret technique with harsh conditions.

After all, even his Shinra Tensei had a five-second cooldown.

Since he had baited out Asakawa Shin’s ultimate move, hadn’t their chances of winning increased significantly ?

Just as a flash of realization crossed his eyes, Asakawa Shin was already upon him like a ghost.

An unprecedented sense of fatal crisis instantly shrouded his entire body like a haze.

Between illusion and reality, Nagato seemed to see a hideous crimson beast tearing through the void, roaring as it charged toward him.

The aura contained within it was terrifying.

It was no illusion.

A massive amount of Haoshoku Haki and Armament Haki were crazily intertwining and gathering, cutting through the sky along the blade.

A sound of scornful disdain also emanated from it.

« Anti-God Treasure ! »

« Divine Departure ! ! ! »

Nagato’s pupils shook violently, and he realized it instantly.

Was this the terrifying sword aura that had defeated the army in one blow over Konoha Village just now ?

At this life-or-death moment, he dared not show the slightest hesitation.

He thrust his right arm out, the vast, sea-like Chakra within him was instantly drained, and combined with the extremely bizarre ocular power of the Rinnegan, the two forces crazily twisted and intertwined.

Eventually, they transformed into a world-destroying repulsion wave that blasted outward.

« Super Shinra Tensei ! ! ! »

Although he hadn’t charged it, Nagato was using his own body to personally activate the Rinnegan ; not only was the burden reduced, but the power had multiplied exponentially.

Coupled with the massive vitality granted by the Hashirama cells, the Shinra Tensei blasted out at this moment was even more powerful than the previous strike.

In the blink of an eye, the pitch-black crescent sword energy collided fiercely with the invisible repulsion storm.

The two terrifying forces exploded instantly, kicking up a monstrous hurricane that ravaged the world.

Konan, watching from the side, was completely unable to resist this wild torrent ; her body instantly lost control, and she could only let herself be swept away by the air currents, like a helpless dandelion scattered in a storm.

« Nagato, hold on ! »

The concerned, mature woman’s voice gradually drifted away until it fell silent.
