# Chapitre 54 — Kidney Strike

« Oh no ! I can’t dodge this ! »

Facing the sudden change in technique executed by Hyuga Neji, Rock Lee’s pupils constricted, and a flash of panic crossed his eyes in an instant.

But that trace of panic vanished as quickly as it had appeared, replaced by a glimmer of determination silently rising in the depths of his eyes.

He had not expected Hyuga Neji to come up with this move ; caught off guard, he had indeed been taken by surprise.

However, so what ? As the saying goes, when two narrow paths meet, the brave one wins...

At this moment, Hyuga Neji’s posture for generating power was restricted ; how much of the Eight Trigrams Palms' power could he really unleash ? With his feet unable to move, he was like a living target—what was there to fear ?

'The advantage is mine ! ! !'

In a flash of thought, Rock Lee swiftly adjusted his posture in mid-air, turning his body slightly to nimbly evade the majority of the Gentle Fist attacks.

However, there were still some attacks that were truly impossible to fully dodge, so he decisively blocked in front of his face with his right arm, taking the force of the Eight Trigrams Palms head-on.

At the same time, he stretched his body to the limit in mid-air, gathering all his strength into his right foot, and kicked fiercely toward Hyuga Neji with the resolve of a suicide attack !

'Go to hell !'

« Konoha Whirlwind ! ! ! »

This kick was powerful and heavy, striking Hyuga Neji violently in the waist, causing him to immediately curl up like a shrimp and fly off into the distance like a soccer ball launched from a cannon.

Rock Lee was also hit directly by three strikes of the Eight Trigrams Palms, his entire body flying backward like a kite with a broken string.

The two figures flew more than ten meters through the air before finally landing and rolling continuously, kicking up a large cloud of dust that blocked everyone’s view, making it impossible to see the specific situation, but this was clearly a situation where both sides had suffered heavy injuries !

The crowd watching the battle was dumbfounded ; they hadn’t been fighting, but the moment they did, it was like the tip of a needle against the point of a wheat stalk—a clash of titans—and a winner and loser were decided in an instant ?

Uchiha Sasuke was even more excited as he watched : 'Good ! Fight like that, that’s exactly how you have to fight !

Rock Lee, keep kidney-striking Hyuga Neji hard like that, beat him until he can’t stand up again.

And Hyuga Neji, you too, try attacking his center ! What’s the use of hitting his arms ? Do you even know how to fight ?'

...

After a long while, the dust in the arena gradually dispersed, revealing the figures of the two.

Rock Lee was panting heavily, using his left hand to support himself on the ground as he struggled to stand up.

Just now, he had been hit directly by the Eight Trigrams Palms on his right arm and two acupuncture points under his armpit ; at this moment, his right hand was hanging limply in front of him, his fingers twitching and trembling uncontrollably, and his entire arm was unable to move.

Even worse, his right chest had also been struck by a palm from Hyuga Neji, causing damage to his right lung.

At this time, he was like a fish out of water, his breathing was extremely difficult, the air seemed to carry a metallic scent of blood, his body felt weak, his head was dizzy, and he felt like he couldn’t muster much strength.

Rock Lee had been beaten so miserably, but Hyuga Neji’s situation was even worse, his state even more tragic.

Turning the gaze to Hyuga Neji, he was seen with a face full of pain, curled up on the ground, one hand pressing against his left waist, his whole body trembling incessantly.

He had just been kicked fiercely by Rock Lee in his left kidney, sending him flying more than ten meters away.

At this moment, he felt like his whole body was falling apart, hurting everywhere, especially his left kidney, which felt as if a thousand needles were constantly stabbing it.

'Oh no, my kidney... my kidney is ruptured... Huh ?'

Seeming to sense that the dust had cleared and everyone was watching him, Hyuga Neji gritted his teeth and, with great willpower, crawled up. He glared fiercely at Rock Lee :

« Rock Lee, you damn... *bleh*... »

Before he could finish his sentence, he vomited a large amount of food residue, mixed with quite a bit of blood ; he had actually been beaten to the point of vomiting on the spot by Rock Lee !

Off the field, Hyuga Hinata looked at her cousin with a face full of disgust :

'Damn it ! What a loser, to actually get beaten into vomiting by someone ?

And he calls himself a genius ? He’s not even fit to carry my shoes ! Now he’s completely disgraced the face of our Hyuga Clan...

The only thing to do now is for me to wait until I go down there later and try hard to show off, and prop up the reputation of the Hyuga Clan...'

Asakawa Shin had not forgotten his own job at this moment and was still commentating with great passion :

« Oh my ! The dust has cleared, and we can see the figures of the two contestants ; it seems that both contestants are not in very good condition !

However, the offensive and defensive awareness displayed in that instant was truly shocking ; it is worthy of being a duel between two geniuses !

Speaking of which, I must solemnly introduce them to everyone ; one of them everyone already knows, it is the genius of the prestigious Hyuga Clan in Konoha — contestant Hyuga Neji !

He has shown his talent at a young age and is famous throughout Konoha ; his talent and aptitude are not inferior to mine~

The other one might be a bit unfamiliar to everyone, but being able to fight Hyuga Neji to this extent shows his extraordinary strength.

He is the one I have recognized as the 'Genius of Hard Work' — contestant Rock Lee !

He is the most hardworking Ninja I have ever seen, bar none !

I firmly believe that one day he will realize his dream — that one can become a great Ninja using only taijutsu, and gain recognition from everyone !

Huh ? We can see that both contestants have stood up ; it is truly exciting to look forward to the following duel !

Will the genius of the prestigious Hyuga Clan defend his glory, or will the grassroots commoner stage a shocking upset ?

Let us wait and see ! »

...

'Damn you, Shin !'

Just after finishing vomiting, Hyuga Neji heard Shin’s commentary and couldn’t help but curse silently in his heart.

He had originally planned to surrender and admit defeat, but now that Shin had pulled this stunt, how could he still be embarrassed to surrender ?

Yes ! He had thought about giving his all in this match to practice his Ninja way.

But then again, his kidney was almost beaten to a pulp ; what the hell was the point of continuing to fight ?

He practiced Gentle Fist and was so familiar with the human body’s meridians ; how could he not know the importance of the kidneys ?

If this was beaten to a pulp, wouldn’t his life be over ? Even if it just left some lingering injuries, he couldn’t bear it, could he ?

Taking a step back, even if he won this match, he would still have to face Uchiha Sasuke, who was waiting in the wings !

In his current state, he couldn’t even beat those civilian Ninjas who didn’t dare to participate in the selection tournament ; fighting Sasuke would be a dead end, with no chance of winning at all...

But the atmosphere at the scene had been built up to this point ; it seemed he couldn’t not fight !

'Huh ? What is that so dazzling ?'

Hyuga Neji couldn’t help but look forward in confusion, only to see that Rock Lee’s whole body was almost burning, filled with fighting spirit. When Neji looked at Rock Lee, Rock Lee also fixed his lightbulb-like gaze on him !

...

'Genius of hard work ? A great Ninja ? ! Recognition from everyone ? ! ! A grassroots upset ? ! ! ! Does Shin actually think so highly of me ? Then I absolutely must not let him down ! Waaaah ! I’m burning up ! ! !'

Rock Lee looked at Hyuga Neji with burning eyes ; this was a well-known genius of Konoha, and also the opponent he had to defeat in this battle. Since that was the case...

« Genius from a prestigious clan ! This is my final strike ! »

« Take this ! Grassroots Suicide Kick ! ! ! »

« Don’t come over here ! » Hyuga Neji’s face was full of horror ; this strike felt even more powerful to him than the last one !

On one side was a strike that burned everything and used all his strength ; on the other was a defense that had lost its fighting spirit and was responding in a rush. The result was already obvious...

Might Guy looked at the fallen Hyuga Neji, and after checking on him, officially announced :

« Contestant Hyuga Neji is unable to continue ; contestant Rock Lee wins ! »

« Wow ! » The scene erupted into enthusiastic applause like a tidal wave.

« Is that guy named Rock Lee ? He’s really a formidable character. »

« A grassroots upset ? Hahaha, that was truly wonderful ! »

« Well done, Rock Lee ! »

Rock Lee’s right foot had been hit by Hyuga Neji’s final Eight Trigrams Palms and was already unresponsive, but he still relied on his remaining left foot to support his body, standing proudly in the arena, his back straighter than anyone else’s.

Rock Lee listened to the cheers of the surrounding audience with tears in his eyes ; is this... have I been recognized by everyone ?

'This is just great !'
