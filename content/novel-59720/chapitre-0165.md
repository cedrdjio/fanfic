# Chapitre 165 — An Ordinary Jonin

« Alright Gamabunta, we’ll discuss those matters later. The priority now... is to move this behemoth away.

If we let him run amok here, I’m afraid it will lead to an irreparable disaster. »

Gamabunta curled his lip in dissatisfaction, his tone full of complaints.

« Easier said than done. This is a Tailed Beast ; it’s not that easy to deal with. »

Jiraiya scratched his messy hair, his face filled with confusion. « Then what exactly do you mean ? »

Gamabunta coughed twice, his tone suddenly becoming cheerful. « This is a big job ; you’ll have to pay extra ! »

« ... »

Shukaku narrowed his eyes at Jiraiya and the others, a cold sneer forming at the corners of his mouth.

« Good, good, good ! To dare calmly discuss tactics right in front of me, it seems you don’t take me seriously at all. »

A fierce glint flashed in his eyes. « However, I am a naturally rebellious Tailed Beast. The more you resist, the more I’ll do as I please ! »

« Wind Release : Air Bullets ! »

Shukaku suddenly opened his massive maw, and a highly compressed sphere of wind erupted, hurtling toward the spectator stands with a piercing shriek.

« Gamabunta, please ! »

Before Jiraiya could finish his sentence, Gamabunta had already slapped his belly, and a solid water cannon shot from his mouth, accurately meeting the Air Bullet.

« Water Release : Water Bullet ! »

Accompanied by a deafening roar, the violent shockwave instantly exploded, turning into water mist and gale-force winds that swept through the surroundings.

Shukaku’s brow furrowed slightly, clearly surprised that his attack was so easily neutralized, and he let out a dissatisfied grunt from his nose.

« To be able to take my move, it seems you do have some skill... »

Then his gaze suddenly turned cold, revealing a chilling glint. « But how long can your Chakra hold out ? Do you think you’re worthy of a battle of attrition with me ? »

With that, Shukaku opened his mouth again and spat out four formidable wind spheres in one breath, whistling through the air in a fan-shaped, indiscriminate bombardment.

Gamabunta was shocked and hurriedly countered, but his casting speed was ultimately slower than Shukaku’s. After barely blocking three, the fourth wind sphere was already approaching, accompanied by the sound of breaking air.

At the critical moment, Jiraiya suddenly acted and parried the blow.

Without giving Shukaku a chance to breathe, Jiraiya and Gamabunta, acting in perfect sync, decisively launched a counterattack.

Gamabunta kicked off the ground violently, his body leaping up like a cannonball, and his massive frog feet, carrying immense force, kicked Shukaku hard in the belly.

With a dull thud, Shukaku’s massive body flew backward, smashing through the stadium walls and disappearing outside the arena.

Gamabunta didn’t hesitate to give chase.

Shukaku had just struggled to get up and hadn’t even steadied himself when a solid Toad Oil Bullet fell from the sky, accurately splattering onto his face.

The viscous oil instantly blocked his vision, leaving him disoriented for a moment and nearly causing him to stumble again.

Gamabunta decisively seized the opportunity to rush forward, his arms tightly gripping the irritable Tailed Beast. Taking advantage of Shukaku’s instability, Gamabunta acted like a bulldozer, forcibly pushing Shukaku’s massive body toward the outskirts of Konoha Village.

Asakawa Shin witnessed this scene, and his tense nerves finally relaxed as he let out a long sigh of relief.

Although there were some twists and turns, the situation was still under control.

He pulled out a cigarette from somewhere, lit it, took a deep drag, and then turned his head, slowly blowing a perfect heart-shaped smoke ring toward Yugito, who was not far away.

Yugito’s expression was a bit strange. She was silent for a moment before suddenly speaking softly, « You... couldn’t possibly like me, could you ? But you’re really too young. However... it’s not entirely unacceptable. »

A hint of surprise had just flashed in Asakawa Shin’s eyes when her tone suddenly turned cold. « But, I have a condition. I don’t want to marry far away, so you must marry into our Hidden Cloud Village. Otherwise, there’s nothing to talk about. »

« ... »

On the high platform, Rasa was engaged in a fierce battle with Hiruzen Sarutobi.

Seeing the situation on the battlefield becoming increasingly deadlocked, he suddenly turned his head and roared toward the area below, « Ai ! You bastard, are you playing with me ? You can’t even take down an ordinary Jonin from Konoha ? »

The Fourth Raikage, Ai, had his own difficulties but could only grit his teeth and endure in silence.

Staring at the green-skinned frog in front of him, Ai fell into a brief moment of contemplation.

After a moment, he suddenly proposed, « Rasa, how about... we switch opponents ? I’ll leave this troublesome fellow to you, and I’ll deal with that old geezer Hiruzen Sarutobi ! »

Before he could finish his sentence, Ai had already turned and sped away, leaving behind a stunned Might Guy.

Might Guy’s expression tightened, and he didn’t hesitate to gather his breath and give chase.

At the moment they crossed paths, Ai’s lips curled slightly as he whispered, « Thanks, Rasa. Now... it’s up to you. »

In the blink of an eye, the Raikage’s figure had vanished from sight.

Rasa was slightly stunned, then he snorted coldly and said dismissively, « Nonsense. Fine, leave him to me. I’d like to see... just what he’s capable of ! »

With that thought, he suddenly controlled the Gold Dust to weave a barrier, successfully intercepting Might Guy and forcibly completing the switch of opponents.

Might Guy paused, a hint of surprise flashing in his eyes, and then he laughed heroically.

« 原來如此！是要与我一决高下吗？真是个令人热血沸腾的挑战，我接受了！ »

With that, he assumed the starting stance of the Eight Gates, his fighting spirit soaring to the heavens.

« Eight Gates Released Formation : Sixth Gate of View, Open ! »

« Boom— ! »

With a loud bang, an astonishing aura erupted from within Might Guy, and the surrounding air seemed to be instantly pushed aside, causing a sudden gale.

« Playing tricks ! »

Rasa watched coldly and, not wanting to be outdone, waved his hand to launch a killing move.

« Magnet Release : Gold Dust Imperial Funeral ! »

In an instant, countless grains of Gold Dust poured down like a rainstorm, converging into a golden sea of death that swept toward Might Guy, blotting out the sky.

The sheer scale of it was terrifying.

Facing this earth-shattering blow, Might Guy’s eyes were resolute, showing no fear.

His arms moved like lightning, instantly throwing hundreds of high-speed straight punches.

The wind from his punches whistled, tearing through the atmosphere and producing deafening sonic booms.

The intense friction caused the air to ignite, and his fists actually caught fire, resembling a magnificent peacock’s tail opening as they met the golden sea.

« Morning Peacock ! »

In that split second, the two immensely powerful forces collided with a bang.

Under Rasa’s horrified gaze, the seemingly indestructible Gold Dust was actually defeated.

The shockwaves of the Morning Peacock pushed forward in layers, wave after wave, nearly dispersing the Gold Dust completely.

Rasa was shocked and hurriedly increased his Chakra output, frantically releasing more Gold Dust in an attempt to stabilize his position.

Finally, accompanied by an earth-shaking roar and an extremely strong shockwave, the Gold Dust and flames exploded simultaneously, splashing in all directions.

This clash actually ended in a draw, with neither side gaining the upper hand.

Ai, who was currently beating up the old man, saw this and curled his lip slightly, shouting out, « Rasa ! You bastard, are you playing with me ? You can’t even take down an ordinary Jonin from Konoha ? »
