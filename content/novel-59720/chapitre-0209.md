# Chapitre 209 — Rap

« A new world ? »

Yugito’s gaze was dazed as she stared blankly at the helping hand extended before her.

Her gaze slowly shifted upward, and Asakawa Shin’s gentle smiling face came into view—an expression diametrically opposed to the hideous faces in the Hidden Cloud Village that wished for nothing more than her death.

'Perhaps this « engagement » isn’t so bad after all ?'

As this stray thought crossed her mind, she inexplicably gripped that large hand tightly and held it to her chest.

An indescribable sense of security slowly seeped into the depths of her heart along with the warmth emanating from his palm.

« Alright, I’ll go with you ! » she said with a firm tone.

Only then did the people in the Raikage’s office react, instantly erupting into an uproar.

« Asa... Asakawa Shin ? ! »

Exclamations rang out all around, and the crowd that had been closing in instantly retreated as if facing a formidable foe.

The air seemed to freeze, and the surroundings fell into an eerie silence.

Asakawa Shin pulled the still-dazed Yugito up from the ground and pulled her directly into his arms.

« What’s wrong ? Is the little kitty scared witless ? Didn’t I warn you long ago ? Now you should finally believe... »

Before he could finish, Yugito’s face had already turned beet red, unable to hold back her emotions.

She suddenly looked up, her eyes filled with tears at some point, and said aggrievedly, « They framed me and wanted to arrest me for trial, even though I clearly did nothing... »

Asakawa Shin couldn’t help but be taken aback, a sense of astonishment rising in his heart.

Was this still that haughty and arrogant « Cat Queen » ?

Could that cat, whose entire body burned with scorching flames, actually show such a vulnerable expression ?

His heart stirred as he felt the figure sobbing in his arms, and a strange sense of pity welled up from deep within.

He reached out with his other hand and somewhat clumsily patted Yugito’s back, saying softly, « Don’t be afraid. With me here, no one can touch a hair on your head. »

Killer Bee finally snapped out of his shock, his face so dark it looked like it could drip ink.

He took a step forward, making the ground tremble, and a low roar echoed through the office : « Asakawa Shin ! Are you declaring war on the Hidden Cloud Village ? ! »

Asakawa Shin didn’t even turn his head ; he just tightened his hold on the young girl in his arms, that cynical yet unquestionable smile returning to his lips.

His voice wasn’t loud, yet it clearly reached every corner.

« Declaring war ? No, I am taking my fiancée home. Whoever stands in my way is my enemy. »

« Heh, fiancée ? » Killer Bee said through gritted teeth, enunciating each word.

The enemy who killed his older brother, the Fourth Raikage Ai, was right before his eyes. Though his heart was filled with extreme hatred, he could only suppress that killing intent deep within.

The Hidden Cloud Village was now riddled with holes and could no longer withstand any more turmoil.

He exerted all his strength to barely suppress his trembling body from anger. Just as he was about to speak and negotiate, Asakawa Shin looked down, his gaze falling on Yugito in his arms.

Although hatred still lingered in the young girl’s eyes, that hysterical madness had already subsided.

'Still harboring illusions about the Hidden Cloud Village ? That won’t do.'

Asakawa Shin’s eyes flickered, his gaze sweeping over the crowd like a hawk, as he gave an imperceptible nod.

Almost in an instant, a figure suddenly lunged out from the crowd, his Chakra exploding as he went straight for Asakawa Shin.

It was precisely that « ordinary Jonin » who had been wantonly provoking Yugito just moments ago.

As he charged forward frantically, he roared, « Damn you, Asakawa Shin ! You killed the Fourth Raikage and so many of our comrades in the Village. Today, I will have my revenge ! »

Killer Bee was instantly aghast. How could this fool be so reckless ?

If he had known, he should have slapped him to death when he was provoking Yugito earlier !

Unfortunately, it was too late now, for Asakawa Shin had already made his move.

« Bastard ! The Hidden Cloud Village dares to ambush me ? If it’s war you want, then war it is. I’ll see it through to the end ! »

He held the girl in his arms tightly, spun in mid-air to draw a perfect semi-circle, and the kusanagi sword at his waist clanged as it was unsheathed, unleashing an earth-shattering slash.

« Divine Departure ! »

A hundred-meter-long pitch-black crescent moon appeared out of thin air, crashing toward that « ordinary Jonin » with unstoppable force.

The man didn’t even have time to scream before he was reduced to dust under the violent sword energy.

The sword energy’s momentum did not diminish, carrying a bone-chilling killing intent as it headed straight for the end of his line of sight.

Killer Bee’s pupils shrank ; this world-destroying sword stance instantly pulled him back to that blood-filled nightmare.

However, at this moment, the older brother who had once fought alongside him was no longer in this world.

« Asakawa Shin, stop ! We didn’t... »

Before the words of negotiation could leave his mouth, a more majestic voice drowned them out.

« Wood Release : Wood Human Technique ! »

The ground beneath their feet shook violently as countless sprouts burst through the soil like bamboo shoots after rain.

In an instant, these sprouts grew rapidly into thick, hard giant trees. They intertwined, coiled, and converged, eventually transforming into a towering Wood Human Arhat hundreds of meters tall.

This massive commotion startled the entire Village. Even from the most remote corners, villagers could see that colossal figure blotting out the sky from afar.

Immediately, everyone fell into chaos and uproar once again.

« Damn it ! What’s happened now ? Has another enemy come knocking ? »

« That’s... Wood Release ! It’s the legendary Wood Release ! Konoha is attacking ! »

« It’s fake, all fake... We are one of the grand Five Great Hidden Villages. How could enemies come knocking every single day ? »

However, this fragile self-consolation lasted only a moment before being shattered by the cruel reality.

Because that heaven-supporting Wood Human moved.

A wisp of black-red lightning appeared, like a drop of thick ink falling into clear water, staining and spreading across the Wood Human’s massive body.

In the blink of an eye, the entire Wood Human was covered by this eerie lightning, emitting a suffocating sense of divinity and majesty.

Immediately after, the Wood Human threw a punch.

It was just a basic Lunge Punch, yet it erupted with world-destroying power.

A terrifying shockwave burst from the fist, instantly turning into a roaring lightning storm that raged outward.

Wherever the storm passed, the earth was forcibly plowed into a deep ravine.

The temporary buildings that had just been erected were torn to shreds like paper, their debris swept up in the gale and flying everywhere.

« I told you to... stop ! »

Killer Bee was incensed, violent Tailed Beast Chakra gushing from his body as he expanded and transformed into a powerful Horned Octopus.

As soon as the Eight-Tails took form, it quickly launched a charge.

He frantically gathered Chakra onto his horns, which carried a chilling light as they pierced toward the Wood Human Arhat.

Asakawa Shin’s attention, however, was not on this.

He had felt something was off from the start, and seeing this octopus now, he finally understood why.

He frowned slightly and murmured under his breath, « Strange, why isn’t this old guy rapping anymore ? »

Yugito, beside him, was silent for a moment before answering, « He’s been like this ever since the Fourth Raikage died. »
