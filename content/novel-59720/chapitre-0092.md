# Chapitre 92 — Crushed

Asakawa Shin exerted force beneath his feet, stepping back to retreat and landing steadily atop a steep mountain peak.

Looking down from his high vantage point, his eyes narrowed slightly as he saw the terrifying storm in the distance, carrying an astonishing momentum as it advanced layer by layer, nearly sweeping right in front of him.

He did not appear panicked ; instead, he let out a low chuckle, his hands rapidly forming seals : « Ninja Art : Reverse Harem Technique : Modified ! »

« Bang ! »

Accompanied by several crisp pops, four Shadow Clones appeared out of thin air on the previously empty cliffside. Each one was identical to Asakawa Shin, all appearing in the form of Obito after using the Transformation Technique.

Asakawa Shin nodded slightly and said solemnly, « Well then, I’ll leave the rest to you guys. »

As soon as he finished speaking, two Shadow Clones stepped forward in sync and responded in deep voices, « Leave the defense to us. »

« Sage Art : Water Release : Water Formation Wall ! »

« Sage Art : Earth Release : Earth-Style Wall ! »

Thanks to the massive Chakra reserves of the Shadow Clones and the terrifying enhancement of Sage Mode, as soon as these two Ninjutsu were used, two giant barriers several hundred meters high instantly rose. They overlapped and supported each other, blocking the upwind side where the Uzumaki storm was approaching.

Asakawa Shin and all his Shadow Clones stood ready, preparing for the impending impact : « Everyone be careful. If we can’t hold it, reinforce the defense immediately ! »

In an instant, the storm arrived. Violent winds carrying rubble smashed fiercely against the barriers, and the massive force caused the thick walls to tremble violently.

At the critical moment, the Shadow Clones quickly increased their Chakra output, forcibly stabilizing their position and firmly resisting the violent impact.

One couldn’t help but shout, « Senjutsu Chakra is being consumed very quickly ! We can’t hold out for long, so let’s finish this quickly ! »

The other two Shadow Clones did not hesitate and began forming seals with their hands.

« Sage Art : Wind Release : Great Breakthrough ! »

« Sage Art : Fire Release : Great Fire Annihilation ! »

« Combination Ninjutsu : Flaming Wind Purgatory ! »

In an instant, a violent gale and a sea of fire radiating extreme heat rushed straight toward the Eight-Tails. The wind fueled the fire, and the fire empowered the wind ; their momentum surged instantly, colliding violently with the storm created by the Tailed Beast Eight-Wrap. For a moment, the two sides were locked in a stalemate, evenly matched.

Asakawa Shin’s original body slightly clenched his fist, feeling the incredible power within him, and couldn’t help but break into a smile : « It’s not over yet. Now, watch me ! »

Before his voice faded, he had already thrown a series of rapid punches forward. His fists rubbed violently against the air, and a succession of air cannons carrying scorching flames shot out, bombarding the front like a torrential downpour.

This fierce gale of punches merged into the Flaming Wind Purgatory, not only taking the power of the wind and fire to the next level but also granting the Ninjutsu incredible penetrating power.

For a moment, the Tailed Beast Eight-Wrap was actually overwhelmed. The firestorm advanced irresistibly, rapidly spreading toward the Eight-Tails' main body.

The Eight-Tails was pale with shock within the mental world, its voice full of disbelief : « Damn it ! Our Tailed Beast Eight-Wrap was actually crushed ? How is this possible ? Why is his Ninjutsu power so terrifying ? »

Killer Bee shouted anxiously : « Now is not the time to be conflicted ! Get moving, start spinning, baby ! »

The Eight-Tails' expression turned stern, and it gave a heavy nod : « You’re right. Then let him witness the grit of Lord Gyūki ! Let’s go ! »

In the outside world, the Eight-Tails' spinning suddenly stopped. Then, it turned its massive body sideways, transforming into a giant ball of flesh that rolled rapidly across the ground, narrowly avoiding the impact of the violent sea of fire.

After escaping the disaster, the Eight-Tails' expression relaxed slightly, but soon a ferocious look appeared on its face. With a slight adjustment, it charged violently toward Asakawa Shin’s direction.

Asakawa Shin watched dumbfounded and exclaimed in shock, « There’s actually a move like this ? Isn’t this just the Human Bullet Tank ? First it was Rotation, and now this. Did you pay the copyright fees to Konoha ? »

As the Eight-Tails changed its movements, the original violent hurricane also gradually dissipated. Asakawa Shin waved his hand at the Shadow Clones :

« Hard work, everyone. You can disperse now. Pass the Chakra back to me, and leave the rest to me ! »

« Bang ! »

Asakawa Shin felt the Chakra replenishing within his body and turned his gaze back to the Eight-Tails. Seeing the terrifying, earth-shaking offensive already closing in, he couldn’t help but grin :

« You guy, I hadn’t even gone to find you yet, and here you are coming to me first. »

Before he finished speaking, the Eight-Tails suddenly exerted force and jumped. Its massive frame actually soared into the air, carrying the weight of ten thousand tons as it bore down toward the mountain peak where Asakawa Shin was located.

Asakawa Shin’s figure blurred as he quickly retreated to avoid the strike. The mountain peak beneath his feet was not so lucky ; under the violent impact and rolling friction of the Eight-Tails' heavy body, it was actually smashed into countless fragments that scattered everywhere.

Having missed its strike, the Eight-Tails quickly adjusted its posture and continued its fierce assault toward Asakawa Shin.

Asakawa Shin looked around, and his eyes suddenly lit up. Timing it perfectly, his figure blurred, and while dodging the Eight-Tails' collision, he arrived in front of a small hill.

With a low shout, his brute strength exploded, and he actually uprooted that small hill. Then, clutching the peak of the mountain with both hands, he used it like a giant baseball bat and swung it fiercely at the Eight-Tails that was bearing down on him once again.

« BOOM— ! »

Amidst the violent sound of impact, that mountain peak instantly disintegrated, turning into a shower of rubble.

The Eight-Tails fared even worse ; the entire Tailed Beast was struck by this 'home run,' causing it to spray blood wildly. It couldn’t even maintain its spherical form as it tumbled and flew backward through the air, crashing heavily into the ground and kicking up a massive cloud of dust.

Asakawa Shin slowly let out a long breath, feeling completely refreshed. He strolled over to the Eight-Tails and said in a teasing tone, « You’re still conscious ? You really are tough, aren’t you ? »

The Eight-Tails turned its head with difficulty to glare at him. It wanted to shout an insult, but as soon as it opened its mouth, a mouthful of blood sprayed out directly. Its massive body then went limp on the ground, leaving only the sound of heavy panting echoing around.

Seeing that the opponent had no strength left to fight back, Asakawa Shin no longer hesitated and began to directly extract its Chakra.

However, after just a moment, his brow furrowed as he wondered inwardly : 'This Chakra amount is a bit off. Isn’t it a bit too little ? Even after that intense battle, it shouldn’t be down to just this much.'

Just then, Asakawa Shin’s body suddenly stiffened as if he had realized something, and a knowing smile curled at the corners of his mouth.

In the waters several hundred meters behind him, the surface suddenly rippled, and another Eight-Tails slowly emerged.

Although this Eight-Tails looked battered, its condition was clearly much better, though it was missing a section of a tentacle.

It turned out that the Eight-Tails that had fallen earlier was merely a Tentacle Clone it used to lure the enemy—a bait specifically meant to distract Asakawa Shin.

The Eight-Tails' eyes were fierce as it quickly mobilized the Chakra in its body to divide and aggregate, instantly forming several Tailed Beast Balls. It then spat them out in one breath, launching a saturation bombardment toward Asakawa Shin’s position.

« Die, you bastard ! Continuous Tailed Beast Balls ! ! ! »

Asakawa Shin seemed completely defenseless as he was hit head-on by this destructive attack. Without even letting out a scream, he instantly turned to ash and dissipated.

Witnessing this scene, the Eight-Tails couldn’t help but laugh out loud. The entire Tailed Beast was jumping for joy as it smugly declared its victory :

« Hahaha, the plan succeeded ! This damn bastard was indeed a bit troublesome, but in the end, he was no match for Lord Gyūki ! The winner of this battle is me, hahahaha ! »

« Is that so ? Does crushing my Water Clone make you that happy ? »

Just as the Eight-Tails was getting carried away, a mocking voice suddenly came from its shoulder, making its whole body tremble in fright.

It looked down in horror to find that Asakawa Shin, who should have been turned to ash, was currently standing there leisurely, looking at it with a playful expression.

The Eight-Tails instantly broke down, roaring hysterically, « Why won’t you just die, you bastard ? Why are you still alive ? »

Asakawa Shin pretended to pick his ear and complained with a face full of disgust, « Why are you yelling so loud ? You’re going to make me deaf. Do you have any civic-mindedness ? »

« Civic-minded my ass ! Just die ! » The Eight-Tails was fuming with rage, swinging its hand to strike its own shoulder violently, wanting to slap this annoying fellow to death once and for all.

Asakawa Shin’s lips curled slightly as he said softly, « You won’t kill anyone like that. Better watch me instead. »

'Ultimate Move : Serious Punch.'

He suddenly stepped forward, launching himself from the Eight-Tails' shoulder like an arrow from a bow. His body stretched to the limit in mid-air, followed by a lightning-fast twist and punch, delivering a terrifying Rising Dragon Punch that struck the Eight-Tails' chin first.

The behemoth didn’t even have time to scream before it was sent flying by this massive force. It traced a pathetic arc through the air and crashed heavily onto the water’s surface, its limbs twitching as its eyes rolled back, and it fell unconscious.

Its form also receded from the Tailed Beast transformation, reverting back to Killer Bee’s appearance, floating powerlessly on the water, motionless.

And so, the Eight-Tails Gyūki was utterly defeated !
