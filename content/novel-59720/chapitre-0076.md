# Chapitre 76 — Why Are You at My House?

« Itachi, make your preparations. We will leave the village on time tomorrow morning. I’ll take my leave now, as I need to go back and settle some things. Well then... Aunt Mikoto, goodbye. »

Just as Asakawa Shin was about to leave, Mikoto seemed to suddenly remember something and called out to him in a slight panic :

« Lord Shin, you just said that Itachi contracted the Bloodline Disease because he awakened his eyes too early ? What about Sasuke ? Sasuke awakened his Sharingan at six, and just a few days ago, because of Fugaku’s situation, he awakened his two-tomoe. Doesn’t that mean... »

Asakawa Shin was somewhat surprised to hear what Mikoto said. Sasuke really was showing promise ; he’d awakened his two-tomoe at seven !

However, he wasn’t worried in the slightest. After all, he was the reincarnated Chakra of Indra ; there was no way he would contract the Bloodline Disease just from awakening a two-tomoe at seven, right ?

Still, he would take this opportunity to give Sasuke a check-up, as there was something he wanted to verify with him.

That’s right, he needed to verify his own stagnant evolutionary state. He suspected that he might need powerful Yin Release Chakra to act as a catalyst.

Where else in the Ninja World could one find powerful Yin Release easier to obtain than Indra’s Chakra ?

As for whether it was as he imagined, he would know once he saw Sasuke.

So he agreed immediately : « Alright, Aunt Mikoto, don’t you worry. Sasuke is my good brother ; I will definitely give him a thorough check-up in a bit ! »

Mikoto finally revealed a smile and showered Asakawa Shin with praise : « Thank you, Shin. You really are a good child. Not only are you taking Itachi for treatment, but you’re also troubling yourself to check on Sasuke. It’s truly Sasuke’s good fortune to have a friend like you ! »

At this time, the Six Styles Training Class should have dismissed. Sure enough, not long after, Sasuke returned with a downcast expression.

He was taken aback to see Asakawa Shin actually in his home : « Shin, when did you get back ? And why are you at my house ? »

Asakawa Shin greeted him, and Mikoto quickly pulled him over and explained everything, leaving Sasuke exclaiming in surprise.

After a while, it was finally explained clearly, and Sasuke looked at Itachi with a worried expression.

Itachi, however, gave Sasuke a doting smile and gently flicked his forehead. He didn’t say anything, but that love had clearly been conveyed.

Sasuke’s expression calmed down as well. He looked at Asakawa with gratitude and then bowed deeply to him :

« Thank you, Shin. Otherwise, I might have lost my family again. I won’t say anything more ; if you need anything in the future, just let me know. »

Asakawa Shin looked at Sasuke with emotion. The plan he had formulated from the beginning was to latch onto Naruto and Sasuke, and he should have secured that now, right ?

Thinking of this, Asakawa Shin smiled and waved at Sasuke : « Alright, why are you being so sentimental ? Hurry up and come over so I can give you a check-up. »

Actually, there was no need for a check-up ; Asakawa Shin already knew the result.

After all, Sasuke held absolutely no attraction for him ; that instinctual craving for evolution in his body simply did not appear.

However, since the opportunity was rare, he might as well research Indra’s Chakra.

Thinking this, Asakawa Shin slowly placed his hand on Sasuke’s wrist and closed his eyes to sense it.

As expected, even though Sasuke had awakened the two-tomoe at seven, it hadn’t caused any burden on his body at all.

On the contrary, the Sharingan was slowly strengthening and nourishing his body. It was truly different once awakened.

'Found it. Indra’s Chakra.'

Asakawa Shin held his breath and focused for a long time before finding this special Yin Release Chakra in Sasuke’s brain region, but the result left him greatly disappointed.

'Is that it ? Why does it feel like it’s on the same level as my Haoshoku Haki ? I can’t feel that sense of high-level power at all. That makes sense ; Indra’s Chakra, when it comes down to it, is just one piece of the puzzle of the myriad things. Looks like it’s useless to me. Pass.'

Sasuke saw Asakawa Shin’s deeply furrowed brow and was scared to death. He stammered :

« Shin, am I going to be alright ? Should I leave the village with you tomorrow ? I feel like I can still be saved. »

Asakawa Shin heard him say this, a hint of a smile flashing in his eyes :

« Alright, there’s no need to save you anymore. Leave the chance to others. »

Seeing Sasuke’s dumbfounded expression, Asakawa Shin laughed out loud and continued :

« You couldn’t be any healthier. What are you talking about saving... »

After saying this, he slipped out of the house, with only one sentence drifting in :

« Itachi, tomorrow morning at eight, see you at the village gate. I’m off. »

Leaving only Sasuke, huffing and puffing as he watched Asakawa’s retreating figure, until he suddenly burst into laughter.

...

'Since Yin Release is useless, Yang Release goes without saying. So there isn’t much left. I’ll go find Naruto to verify next. His current location is... Hmm ? At my house ?'

The sensory result that appeared in his mind made Asakawa Shin shudder in shock. What was that bastard Naruto doing at his house ?

No, he had to rush back and take a look.

« Naruto, try this meatball. Auntie made it especially for you. See if it’s good. »

Hanako kept piling food onto Naruto’s plate, full of anticipation.

In this house, only Naruto loved the food she cooked the most, always showering her with praise ; it truly provided maximum emotional value.

Naruto scratched his head, feeling a bit embarrassed : « Aunt Hanako, you are pregnant now ; you absolutely must not make those strange delicacies anymore. Like that 'Pissing Beef Ball' last time, how could you keep chopping meat with a kitchen knife like that ? It’s not good for the baby. »

Kenji also nodded in agreement, cast an appreciative look at Naruto, and joined the army of people persuading Hanako : « That’s right, Hanako. And that 'Buddha Jumps Over the Wall' from last time ; it actually takes seven whole days to make. How could you handle that ? You shouldn’t look at those strange recipes of Shin’s lately. »

Hanako was all smiles, deeply touched by their concern, and started piling food onto their plates again.

When Asakawa Shin pushed the door open, he happened to see this scene of the family enjoying themselves.

It looked so wonderful, making one feel warm inside... As if !

At this moment, there was only one voice in Asakawa Shin’s heart : 'Oh no, my house has been stolen.'

The three of them were taken aback when they suddenly saw Asakawa Shin return, and it took a while for them to react.

Asakawa Shin only then noticed that Hanako was actually pregnant ; it looked like she was already five or six months along...

He hadn’t known at all. How long had it been since that previous Shadow Clone had returned ?

Seeing Hanako’s teary-eyed look, Asakawa Shin felt a bit guilty and quickly rushed over, wanting to snuggle into his mother’s arms.

Unexpectedly, he was intercepted by Hanako before he could reach his destination, one hand swiftly grabbing his ear with lightning speed.

Hanako’s angry voice followed : « Very well, Asakawa Shin, your wings have gotten stiff, haven’t you ? You dare to not come home for over half a year ! »

Naruto chimed in from the side : « That’s right, Shin is too much. Aunt Hanako, don’t be angry ; it’s not good for your health. »

Asakawa Shin finally turned his attention to Naruto, feeling a moment of suspicion and uncertainty : « Holy crap ! Naruto, you smell so good. »
