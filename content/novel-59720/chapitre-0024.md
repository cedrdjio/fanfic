# Chapitre 24 — A New Atmosphere

Time flowed slowly, and Saturday arrived once again.

Konoha was exceptionally lively today because it was the day the Hokage-sama would take on a disciple again after several decades.

A massive stage had been erected in the stadium, with a banner draped across it bearing the six large characters : « Hokage’s Disciple Initiation Ceremony. » The venue was decorated in a very eye-catching and festive manner.

The ceremony was set to begin at ten in the morning, and while it was still far from time, the area below the stage was already bustling with people. Major Ninja clans and well-known civilian Ninjas were taking their seats, and it looked as though almost everyone had arrived.

Those with keen sources of information knew that the Hokage’s new disciple was certainly no simple figure. He possessed exceptional talent, had already begun to distinguish himself at a young age, and had a deep understanding of politics, with excellent aptitude for strategy and character. It was rumored that the recent power shifts in Konoha were related to him.

This past week, the upper echelons of Konoha had been making frequent, major moves. Not only had there been drastic personnel changes, but the power structure of Konoha had also been adjusted, with several new departments established.

The former Assistant to the Hokage and leader of Root, Danzo, had been imprisoned for failing to manage his subordinates, corruption, and secretly slandering the Hokage. Root had also been completely disbanded.

The captured Root members had their curse seals removed one after another and were dispersed back to their respective clans. Those with nowhere to go were either incorporated into the Anbu or dispatched to guard the borders of the Land of Fire—truly a case of the tree falling and the monkeys scattering.

Only a few die-hard loyalists fled in different directions, their whereabouts unknown.

The former Konoha Advisors, Utatane Koharu and Homura Mitokado, had also retired to the background to enjoy their twilight years. The position of Advisor was directly abolished, though many members of their two families had entered the newly established departments of Konoha to take up posts.

The head of the Nara Clan, Shikaku, had become the new Assistant to the Hokage while concurrently serving as the Minister of the Konoha Ministry of Foreign Affairs. The intelligence agency that previously belonged to Root had also been placed under the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, making him the envy of all for a time.

The genius Ninja of the Uchiha Clan, Shisui, who had made a great name for himself earlier in the Third Ninja World War as « Body Flicker, » was also given a position of great responsibility, appointed by the Hokage-sama as the Minister of the Ministry of Public Safety.

The responsibilities of the Ministry of Public Safety included maintaining the safety of Konoha Village, internal law and order, population management, immigration control, and combating criminal activities ; its authority was by no means insignificant.

It is worth mentioning that the Konoha Military Police Force had also become a subordinate department of the Ministry of Public Safety, which seemed to have caused quite a bit of unhappiness within the Uchiha Clan.

The Minister of the Ministry of Operations was concurrently held by the Hokage himself, the Minister of the Ministry of Finance was served by Hiashi, the Minister of the Ministry of Organization was served by Kakashi, the Minister of the Ministry of Education was served by Akimichi Choza, and the Minister of the Ministry of Discipline Inspection was served by Inoichi Yamanaka.

The Minister of the Ministry of Medical Affairs was served by the former director of Konoha Hospital, Yuko Aoi, whose career path had widened, while the director of Konoha Hospital was now served by the former ace intelligence operative of Root, Yakushi Nonou.

Yakushi Nono had once been a member of Root and later served as the director of an orphanage. She was skilled in using Medical Ninjutsu and had excellent intelligence-gathering capabilities, earning her the title of « The Walking Miko. »

However, when Yakushi Nonou was serving as the director of the orphanage, she was threatened by Danzo : if she did not continue to serve Root and act as an undercover agent in other countries, he would no longer provide funding to the orphanage. Yakushi Nonou had no choice but to continue serving Root.

With Danzo’s downfall this time, Yakushi Nonou was placed under Shikaku’s command. Shikaku could not bear to see Yakushi Nonou’s talents buried, so he petitioned Hiruzen Sarutobi to transfer Yakushi Nonou to the position of director of Konoha Hospital, while the orphanage would be funded directly by the Ministry of Finance as a public welfare project of Konoha.

It is worth mentioning that Yakushi Kabuto had also been transferred to Konoha Hospital on the suggestion of Asakawa Shin to serve as the deputy director of the physical examination center.

People from all the major clans had been recruited into various departments, which could be said to be a cause for general rejoicing ; even their usual tone of voice had become a bit more amicable, with the Uchiha Clan being the most obvious example.

The Uchiha Clan had originally been split into two factions : the Doves and the Hawks. The Doves sought stability, while the Hawks advocated for a coup to reclaim the reasonable rights and interests of the Uchiha Clan.

But now, with Shisui serving as the Minister of the Ministry of Public Safety, a large number of Dove members had been recruited into various departments, entering the power structure of Konoha to hold positions.

Many of the Hawk elites had also come to their senses and were eager to take up posts, and Shisui had turned no one away, bringing them all under his command.

Now, apart from those die-hard radicals, the Uchiha Clan had no time for a coup ; they were almost overwhelmed by their official duties.

This series of sweeping reforms, although plagued by frequent problems in the initial stages, was rapidly undergoing a process of integration.

Most importantly, the division of responsibilities between departments was clear, the boundaries were distinct, and accountability was layered. The overall efficiency of Konoha had increased by several times over, and the effects of the reform were immediate.

Hiruzen Sarutobi had been in high spirits recently, as if he had become twenty years younger ; he walked with a spring in his step.

The first few days of the reform had indeed been arduous, but through the concerted efforts of all departments, the problems that arose were all overcome, which in turn further perfected the new system. It would be no exaggeration to say that Konoha Village was changing with each passing day.

Asakawa Shin was also very satisfied. His original intention in reforming Konoha was to ensure that when he eventually took office as Hokage, he wouldn’t have to spend all day hunched over a desk handling paperwork.

Based on his observations, the amount of paperwork that required the Hokage’s personal attention was now at most 10% of what it used to be. Often, it was enough to just hold a meeting to determine the general direction, and then the people below would naturally follow up and handle the details. Compared to before, it was more than ten times easier.

...

Hokage Office.

« Xiao Xin, you’re here. » Hiruzen Sarutobi looked at his young disciple, truly finding everything about him pleasing and satisfactory.

Asakawa Shin also nodded in satisfaction as he looked at Hiruzen Sarutobi. Before he had become his apprentice, he had found everything about this old man disagreeable...

But times had changed...

As the saying goes, where you stand depends on where you sit. This old man was not only teaching him everything he knew but also intended to pass the position of Hokage of Konoha to him in the future, which made him a beneficial figure !

Whoever speaks ill of my teacher, I’ll fight them !

Asakawa Shin scanned the office and, seeing that only Hiruzen Sarutobi was there, couldn’t help but sigh : « Teacher, are the two senior disciples still unwilling to return ? »

Hiruzen Sarutobi sighed slightly upon hearing this and said : « That bastard Jiraiya said those toads at Mount Myōboku have something to discuss with him, something related to a prophecy, which is very urgent. He has to go to Mount Myōboku first and will return after a while. »

« As for Tsunade, she hasn’t returned in person, but she wants me to give these three things to you. »

« The first is a Ninjutsu scroll of the Senju Clan, which contains the vast majority of the Senju Clan’s heritage : the First Hokage’s Wood Release, the Second Hokage’s Water Release and forbidden techniques, as well as Tsunade’s Medical Ninjutsu and Super Strength techniques. You must practice diligently. »

« This is your own Senju Clan’s Ninjutsu scroll. You can add to it to further enrich it, and you are free to choose whether or not to pass it on to others. »

« The second is the Shikkotsu Forest summoning scroll. This is Tsunade’s summoning creature and one of the top-tier summoning creatures in the world. It possesses strategic-level medical and support capabilities, and its combat ability is also excellent. »

« Most importantly, Shikkotsu Forest is one of the three great sage regions, and it holds the Sage Mode heritage you’ve been yearning for. Katsuyu is a very gentle summoning creature ; you might consider forming a contract with her. »

« The last one is the necklace of the First Hokage, which was formed from the crystallized Chakra of the First Hokage and is priceless. »

« It holds great significance for Tsunade ; by giving this to you, she has truly accepted you in her heart. »

Asakawa Shin solemnly accepted these three items. Although they were not very large, in his heart, they felt heavier than the entire Ninja World. He knew that this was the weight of the entire Senju Clan.

He secretly resolved in his heart : « Rest assured, I will make the name of the Senju shine across the world once again. »
