# Chapitre 205 — Wind Release: Hell Storm!

« An explosion occurred at the Raikage Building ! Is there a foreign enemy invasion ? Go check it out immediately ! »

In a matter of moments, the ruins of the Raikage Building were surrounded by layers upon layers of people, making it completely airtight.

« Raikage-sama, what exactly is going on ? Is that Masked Man the enemy ? »

« To dare attack the Hidden Cloud Village—kill him ! »

Asakawa Shin looked around to see a large crowd of dark-skinned Hidden Cloud Ninjas surrounding him, their foul language and insults incessant.

« It seems unruly people truly do come from such wretched lands. So this is what your so-called 'hospitality' means ? »

Yugito’s brows furrowed slightly as she made a prompt decision, ordering : « All Ninjas below the rank of Chunin, retreat ! Everyone else, follow me to slay the enemy ! »

Before her voice had even faded, her hands had already turned into a blur of afterimages, rapidly forming a series of complex hand seals.

« Fire Release : Fire Rat Ball ! »

With a low shout, a mouse-shaped blue fireball condensing high temperatures erupted from her mouth, trailing a long tail of flame as it lunged toward Asakawa Shin with incredible speed.

Seeing this, Asakawa Shin merely raised an eyebrow and lightly took to the air, calmly evading the strike.

However, a cunning smirk of triumph curled at the corner of Yugito’s mouth at that moment.

She secretly changed her finger signs, and the Fire Rat Ball that had seemingly missed exploded in mid-air, instantly transforming into dozens of blue flame bullets radiating intense heat. As if they had eyes of their own, they whistled through the air in hot pursuit of Asakawa Shin.

The other Ninjas understood the signal and began forming seals to cast their techniques.

A sky full of Lightning Release and Water Release Ninjutsu poured out like a torrential downpour, intending to completely submerge the enemy in the air.

« With such a large-scale battlefield Ninjutsu, there’s no way he can dodge ! To take to the air voluntarily is simply seeking death ! »

However, the expected violent explosion did not occur.

The torrent of Ninjutsu filling the sky strangely froze the moment it touched Asakawa Shin.

It then transformed into a massive energy vortex, eventually being entirely absorbed by his single hand until it vanished without a trace.

« What ? Our Ninjutsu was absorbed ? How is this possible ! »

The Ninjas were struck with horror, watching helplessly as the sky full of fire rats, lightning snakes, and water dragons were easily swallowed by the Masked Man like a whale gulping water, as if a clay ox had entered the sea without stirring even a ripple of resistance.

Asakawa Shin hovered in mid-air ; with a turn of his palm, that chaotic mixture of Chakra was easily assimilated, as if it had never existed.

He looked down at the terrified crowd below, his tone carrying a hint of nonchalant mockery and cynicism :

« This is the 'teamwork' the Hidden Cloud Village is so proud of ? It’s nothing more than a disorganized firework display. Truly disappointing. »

Before his words finished, his figure rose even higher until he reached the clouds.

With a casual wave of his right hand, Psychokinesis erupted violently, instantly tearing through the air to form a massive tornado.

« Wind Release : Hell Storm ! »

As he continuously injected Psychokinesis, Wind Release Chakra, and even the gravitational pull of the Rinnegan, the tornado expanded at an alarming rate, turning into a black vortex that devoured everything.

Wherever it passed, all things were swept into it.

The massive vortex unleashed a terrifying suction force, like an invisible giant hand, ruthlessly uprooting everything in the vicinity.

Towering buildings, deep-rooted ancient trees, and even those Ninjas trying to resist were all swept into this colossal storm without exception, tumbling and dancing helplessly in the violent currents.

Yugito bore the brunt of it, now firmly locked by the tornado’s gravity, her body struggling to maintain balance in the gale.

Relying on her instincts as a Ninja, she leaped between the ruins and debris, attempting to escape this deadly centrifuge.

However, human strength has its limits. As the tornado’s scale expanded exponentially, she not only failed to escape but was instead dragged closer and closer to the center of the vortex by that irresistible suction.

She watched as a nearby building was sucked into the hurricane and, in just an instant, was shattered into pieces under the violent wind pressure, turning into dust.

« Damn it, if this continues, the Village will be completely finished. There’s no time to hesitate ! »

In a flash of thought, Yugito sighed and called out Matatabi’s name in her heart.

Dark red Chakra erupted from her body like lava, her aura surging as her form rapidly expanded and transformed.

In an instant, a massive Two-Tailed Cat Demon shrouded in eerie blue flames appeared in the world, having entered a state of full Tailed Beast transformation.

A flicker of human-like compassion passed through those feline eyes, but it was quickly replaced by resolute determination.

« Sorry, everyone... this tornado must be destroyed, otherwise the Village will... »

With her mind made up, she didn’t hesitate for a second longer.

The Two-Tails' massive body gave her the weight to resist the storm ; the tornado that had been tearing everything apart now found it difficult to move her powerful frame.

Seizing this fleeting opportunity, she kicked off powerfully with her hind legs, her massive body lunging forward instead of retreating, charging straight into the eye of the violent storm.

Under the cutting of countless wind blades, she endured the intense pain, struggling to adjust her posture in the air until she was steadily aimed at the core of the storm.

She suddenly opened her massive maw as the Chakra within her surged wildly, rapidly compressing at a two-to-eight Yin-Yang ratio to form a high-density sphere of destruction.

« Tailed Beast Ball ! »

With a thunderous roar, the Tailed Beast Ball was released at an incredible speed.

Aided by the hurricane’s own suction, it spun directly toward the core of the storm as if it had eyes, detonating instantly.

Boom— ! !

An earth-shattering explosion tore through the sky, and a destructive shockwave erupted from the center of the storm, sweeping violently in all directions.

The nearby tornado bore the brunt of it, its structure pierced through by the zero-distance explosive force.

The previously rampaging airflow structure collapsed instantly, and amidst a series of violent distortions and wails, the massive storm slowly dissipated into the atmosphere.

Losing the storm’s restraint, the people and objects caught within were immediately flung in all directions by the powerful centrifugal force, like petals scattered by a celestial maiden.

With blood-red eyes, Yugito quickly controlled the Two-Tails to turn into a streak of blue lightning, weaving through the ruins in a desperate attempt to save her falling comrades.

Her heart was filled with sorrow.

Due to the previous explosion at the Raikage Building, a large number of Ninjas had gathered here in a short time.

Even though she had decisively ordered the evacuation of the Genin, a rough estimate now suggested that the casualties still numbered in the thousands.

This was undoubtedly a massive dereliction of duty as the Raikage. Even if she could win this battle, what awaited her would certainly not be a commendation ; being impeached and removed from office would likely be the best possible outcome.

« Just who is this person, to possess such god-like methods ? » Shock and hatred intertwined in Yugito’s eyes as she stared fixedly at the figure in the sky.

The storm had cleared, and the dust filling the sky slowly began to settle.

The clouds that had been spinning wildly due to the gravitational pull also finally came to a halt, leaving a shocking void in the center of the high altitude.

A beam of bright, piercing sunlight broke through the rift in the clouds, striking precisely upon the figure hovering in the air, framing him as if a god had descended, holy and impossible to look at directly.

This immensely oppressive scene caused Yugito to daze for a moment, but she quickly gritted her teeth and suppressed the shock in her heart, forcing herself to snap out of it.

Asakawa Shin in the sky seemed to notice her gaze. With a playful smile on his lips, he slowly raised his hands and began to clap in an unhurried manner.

« To actually be able to break my 'Hell Storm'—you truly are worthy of being the Raikage. Impressive, truly impressive ! »
