# Chapitre 191 — Loulan

« Is this Loulan ? »

Asakawa Shin stood atop a high platform, his gaze falling upon the semi-circular protrusion in the center.

A Flying Thunder God Kunai was stuck lonely at the top, with countless sealing formulas spreading like flowing blood vessels to the edge of the platform, forming a strict seal that firmly suppressed the restlessness of the underground Dragon Vein.

« There’s no mistake, this is the place. »

He muttered softly, reaching out to grasp the handle of the Kunai. « Seal, Release. »

As the Kunai was suddenly pulled out, the entire platform emitted a violent tremor as if it couldn’t bear the weight.

The terrifying Chakra accumulated for over ten years suddenly erupted, turning into a thick pillar of purple light that arrogantly pierced through the clouds, swallowing Asakawa Shin’s figure.

Asakawa Shin didn’t offer any resistance ; instead, he took the opportunity to merge into this violent torrent of energy.

Feeling his body being constantly washed over, his thoughts were unusually calm, his heart filled with doubt.

What exactly is the Dragon Vein ?

Rumor has it that it’s the meridians of the earth, but before the descent of the Divine Tree, Chakra shouldn’t have existed in the Ninja World...

Could it be that the source of this power really has some secret connection with the Divine Tree ?

The earth’s meridians... could it refer to the roots of the Divine Tree ?

The light gradually faded, and a strong sense of weightlessness suddenly hit him.

Asakawa Shin felt his body lighten, and his consciousness seemed to be pulled away, sinking straight into the depths of the earth’s veins following the fluctuations of that Chakra.

When he opened his eyes again, the surrounding scenery had changed drastically ; he was now inside a magnificent high tower.

« I really traveled through time... »

He shook his head, trying his best to dispel the dizziness, and looked around alertly.

From what he could see, all exits were sealed off by giant rocks, with only a broken window overhead letting in a faint sliver of light.

He took a deep breath, leaped up, and floated out through that narrow gap.

As soon as he appeared, the scene before him made his pupils shrink ; several puppets were frantically besieging a blonde youth.

« Nani ? Naruto ? »

Asakawa Shin’s heart jolted, and he blurted out, « How could he be here ? »

However, in a flash, he noticed the other’s slightly youthful face, about fifteen or sixteen years old.

'I see...'

He understood instantly, 'This isn’t the Naruto from my memory, but from a future time and space.'

A playful smile appeared on his lips as he whispered, « A teenage Naruto ? This is getting more and more interesting. »

The battle took a turn for the worse. Under the bombardment of the puppet swarm, Naruto struggled to cope and finally couldn’t hold on, being slammed hard into the ground by a heavy blow, kicking up a cloud of dust in a wretched state.

Asakawa Shin’s heart tightened. Although logic told him Naruto wouldn’t be in mortal danger, his heart still clenched uncontrollably.

Just as he was about to intervene, a dazzling yellow flash cut through the sky first, teleporting to Naruto’s side.

« Flying Thunder God Technique ? »

Asakawa Shin’s body stiffened, and a wave of shock hit his heart. « Could it be... Minato Namikaze ! »

Immediately after, two masked figures followed, quickly destroying the remaining puppets.

Amidst the dust, Naruto struggled to get up and said gratefully, « Thank you for saving me. »

Minato Namikaze’s eyes flickered slightly, and he said in a steady tone, « No need for thanks. We are also Konoha Ninjas on a secret mission. If you have no urgent business, you should leave as soon as possible. »

Before he finished speaking, he looked up at Asakawa Shin floating in the air and asked gently, « Are you also from Konoha ? Are you his companion ? Don’t be nervous, we mean no harm. »

As he spoke, he raised his hand to pull down half of his mask, revealing the forehead protector symbolizing Konoha.

Naruto was stunned, then pleasantly surprised : « Could it be Captain Yamato ? Or Sai or Sakura ? »

However, after seeing the face clearly, he froze, scratched his head, and looked a bit confused.

« Um... who are you ? I don’t know you. »

« Oh ? You don’t know me ? » Asakawa Shin frowned slightly and floated lightly to the ground.

« Look closely, Naruto, it’s me, Shin. »

Naruto thought hard for a long time, but finally shook his head helplessly : « Sorry, I really can’t remember. »

Seeing this, Asakawa Shin couldn’t help but let a faint smile play on his lips.

The fact that Naruto didn’t recognize him actually made him breathe a sigh of relief.

He turned around, facing Minato Namikaze and the other two, and explained in a calm tone :

« No need to be so guarded. I am indeed a Konoha Ninja, it’s just that my current situation is a bit special. »

Minato Namikaze nodded thoughtfully : « It seems so. »

Only Naruto remained completely baffled, not understanding what they were talking about at all.

Asakawa Shin couldn’t help but roll his eyes. Whose genes did he inherit with this IQ ? It’s so hard to guess.

A moment later, Minato Namikaze took off his mask, revealing a handsome yet solemn face.

« We came to Loulan to hunt down a Ninja named Mukade. If you have no urgent business, please leave quickly. »

« Mukade ? »

Hearing this, Naruto jumped up, « I’m also here to catch him ! This is my mission, I can’t give up halfway ! »

Asakawa Shin quickly assessed the situation in his mind.

According to the original plot, Mukade would soon be defeated, and the Dragon Vein would be sealed again.

The resulting space-time channel would also be completely closed.

And this... was definitely not what he wanted.

His original intention for traveling through time was to absorb the Natural Energy in this alternate space-time to complete his own evolution.

How could he let this plan fail so easily ?

« In that case... »

A smile appeared on his lips as he gently patted Naruto’s shoulder. « Naruto, you’re quite handsome when you grow up, but still, not as much as me. »

As he spoke, he pulled out a special Kunai engraved with the Flying Thunder God formula from his back and shoved it into Naruto’s arms without a word.

« Since it’s our first meeting and I didn’t prepare anything good, take this as a meeting gift. I have urgent business, so I’ll be going now. »

Before the words fell, Chakra flickered under his feet, and his figure soared into the air.

Just as he was about to leave, he suddenly turned around and gave Minato Namikaze a meaningful smile : « Senior Minato, I’m heading out. I won’t disturb your family reunion. »

As his laughter faded, his figure vanished into the horizon.

Minato Namikaze’s body jolted as he looked at Naruto in disbelief.

« I see... no wonder this youth feels so familiar. That face, those eyes, they’re exactly like Kushina’s. »

And that blonde hair... Your name is Naruto ? Naruto Namikaze ? It feels a bit short.«

Naruto tightly gripped the Flying Thunder God Kunai, his face full of bewilderment and confusion :  »Um... who exactly is he ?«

After Asakawa Shin left, he followed the Chakra sensation straight to where the Dragon Vein was located.

He quickly set up a Sealing Barrier and left a Shadow Clone to stand guard, then turned into a streak of light and sped away.
