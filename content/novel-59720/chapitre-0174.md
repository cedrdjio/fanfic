# Chapitre 174 — Nagato

In Amegakure, a towering spire pierced the sky, facing low-hanging dark clouds as if the leaden grey firmament could be touched with a simple reach of the hand.

The never-ending rain and mist were like a heavy gauze curtain, tightly wrapping the entire city in a suffocating oppression.

Nagato sat upright in a wheelchair, his frame as withered as dry wood, with dozens of black rods protruding from his back, emanating a chilling sense of pressure.

He was focused on adjusting a puppet when he suddenly paused, his gaze shooting to the side like a bolt of lightning.

A strange ripple fluctuated in the space there, and subsequently, two figures slowly emerged from within.

« Madara ? Zetsu ? What are you doing here ! »

Konan flashed forward, instantly shielding Nagato as sheets of paper swirled around her, warily eyeing the two newcomers.

« There is no need to be tense. »

Obito spoke softly, his tone flat : « I have come to report urgent intelligence. »

He glanced to the side, and Black Zetsu, understanding the cue, immediately stepped forward to recount the great war between Konoha, Sunagakure, and Kumogakure with added embellishments.

Konan’s heart shook as she listened, her brow furrowed with gravity. « You are saying Konoha is actually this powerful ? Even the Ichibi and Eight-Tails were captured by them ? »

« Indeed. »

Obito responded in a deep, solemn voice : « The Raikage and Kazekage have both fallen in battle. The strength Konoha has displayed far exceeds expectations and will likely pose a significant obstacle to our plans. »

Nagato let out a cold snort, a flash of contempt in his eyes : « No matter. When the time is right, I shall take action personally. Before these Eyes of a God, all resistance is futile !

I shall be the god who leads the new era. »

Black Zetsu and Obito exchanged a meaningful look, then Black Zetsu lowered his voice, reminding him gravely :

« The strength of that Wood Release User is no small matter ; the Kazekage and Raikage were utterly helpless before him. Nagato... you must not take him lightly. »

« Merely insignificant dust. »

Nagato interrupted coldly, his words dripping with bone-deep arrogance : « When I descend in person, he will learn the meaning of true pain. »

As soon as he finished speaking, his eyes shifted slightly, his sharp gaze instantly locking onto Obito. « You didn’t come today just to report intelligence, did you ? »

Obito hesitated for a long while before finally speaking in a low voice : « Nagato, I have found a way to heal your legs. »

A trace of nearly imperceptible surprise flickered through Nagato’s normally placid eyes, though he quickly suppressed it.

Beside him, Konan could not hide her excitement and asked urgently : « Is it true ? Can Nagato really stand again ? »

« Yes. »

Obito nodded solemnly. « The technology has matured now. I only refrained from mentioning it before because the risks were too high. »

« What technology is it exactly ? » Nagato narrowed his deep Rinnegan, questioning in a grim tone.

« Transplanting the cells of the First Hokage, Senju Hashirama.

His cells contain immense vitality, but if not controlled properly, the recipient could be eroded by the cells and eventually mutate into a tree.

However, we have now successfully overcome this drawback. »

Nagato remained silent for a moment before slowly asking : « What are the conditions ? »

« There is only one condition : complete our plan and bring true peace to this world. »

Hearing this, a hint of emotion stirred in the depths of Nagato’s eyes, and he finally responded deeply : « Interesting. Then let us begin. »

Preparations for the surgery were quickly completed.

Black Zetsu took out an anesthetic and was about to inject it, but Nagato waved him off : « No need. Begin directly. »

Konan swallowed nervously, her eyes full of worry.

Sensing her unease, Nagato turned his head slightly and gave her a reassuring look.

The moment the Hashirama cells were injected into his body, Nagato felt an unprecedented sense of relief wash over him like a tide.

The long-lost sensation of pain awoke in his legs, and the heavy burden of the Rinnegan was significantly lightened.

His body rapidly accepted the Hashirama cells without any signs of rejection.

Nagato thought to himself : ‘Madara didn’t lie to me. It seems even he can no longer bear the pressure from Konoha.

But none of that matters now, because now, I am invincible !’

Under Konan’s bated breath, his hands slowly gripped the armrests of the wheelchair. Gritting his teeth and exerting force, accompanied by the slight cracking of bones, he actually stood up unsteadily !

Obito gave him a deep look and said solemnly : « The surgery was a success. Once you have completely fused with the Hashirama cells, you will be restored to your original state. »

With that, he and Black Zetsu’s figures turned into swirling vortices, gradually dissipating into the air, leaving only a whisper echoing behind.

« Nagato, remember your promise. I will be watching you. »

As the two departed, Konan finally breathed a long sigh of relief, her eyes sparkling with joy : « Nagato, you really stood up ! It seems Madara isn’t that bad after all. »

Nagato, however, slowly shook his head, his gaze dark and uncertain. « He and we are not on the same path. I always feel he is hiding something...

But it doesn’t matter. Before absolute power, any schemes or intrigues will be powerless.

Thanks to him, not only have my legs gained new life, but my control over the Rinnegan has reached a higher level... What is this exactly ? »

« What should we do next ? » Konan asked softly.

« No rush. »

Nagato took a deep breath, a determined light shining in his eyes.

« We will wait for the Three-Tails to revive before we act. During this time, I will familiarize myself with this newfound power as quickly as possible. When the time comes, the beings of the Ninja World will welcome their god ! »

Meanwhile, in Konoha Village.

The Sandaime Hokage, Hiruzen Sarutobi, had actually fallen ill.

When Asakawa Shin visited, he was shocked to find that his teacher seemed to have aged over a decade in just a few days ; his body was stooped, and his aura was as weak as a candle flickering in the wind.

« Shin... you’re here. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi forced himself to rally his spirits and smiled bitterly : « I intended to hold on for a few more years before passing the title to you, but my old bones simply can’t take it anymore. Why don’t I hand this burden over to you now ? »

Asakawa Shin let out a soft sigh. « Teacher, it’s not time yet. I cannot take over the position of Hokage right now. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi coughed violently upon hearing this, and it took a long time for him to recover.

« But besides you, there is no other candidate I can trust. As you know, both Jiraiya and Tsunade avoid this position like the plague... »

Asakawa Shin’s eyes lit up, and he said in a low voice : « Don’t worry, Teacher. Let me go and convince Senior Sister. Let her serve as the temporary Hokage, and in a few years when I am ready, I will formally succeed her. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi nodded tiredly. « I suppose that is the only way... »

After bidding farewell to Hiruzen Sarutobi, Asakawa Shin immediately found Tsunade and got straight to the point : « Senior Sister, you’re going to be the Hokage. »

Tsunade’s pupils shrank at the words, and she instinctively took a step back. « Are you joking ? Only a fool would want to be the Hokage ! »

Asakawa Shin rolled his eyes and said irritably : « Then am I a fool too ? I want to be one. »

« Then you do it. Either way, I’m not doing it. »

« I won’t. I want to be the Hokage’s man. »
