# Chapitre 109 — That Man

« I don’t know why, but Lord Shukaku is free again ! What a familiar scent of wind and sand ! Ya-hoo ! Hahahaha !

Hmm ? Is this... the Hidden Sand Village ? »

Once Shukaku was free, he looked around unscrupulously. When he recognized that this was the Hidden Sand Village, he was immediately consumed by rage, his roars shaking the heavens and the earth :

« Hateful Sand bastards ! You’ve sealed Lord Shukaku for so many years. Today, I’ll wipe you all out ! »

« Wind Release : Drilling Air Bullet ! »

Shukaku suddenly opened his massive mouth, and a giant sphere of wind, condensed with immense Chakra, whistled out, its offensive swift and power staggering.

Rasa’s expression darkened as he immediately manipulated gold dust to construct a defense, barking orders at the arriving Anbu Ninja :

« Evacuate the crowd immediately ! Jonin, establish a defensive line ! You must block the shockwaves of Shukaku’s attacks ! Move ! »

While the Anbu Ninja were in a frantic scramble, Asakawa Shin watched with great interest from the side, a playful smile on his lips :

'That guy Shukaku really put in the hard work to counter the Nine-Tailed. He not only taught himself Ninjutsu but even dabbled in Fuinjutsu.

Unfortunately, the favored one always has nothing to fear. One tail is ultimately no match for nine tails after all...'

Seeing his attack easily blocked by Rasa, Shukaku’s teeth itched with rage, and the fire in his heart burned even more fiercely :

'It’s this guy again ! Last time I just came out for a breath of fresh air, he was the one who shoved me back in !'

But how could he let it go so easily ? Anyone who had been imprisoned without cause for decades would be furious upon seeing the light of day again, let alone a Tailed Beast like him.

At this moment, he harbored a deep-seated hatred for these Sunagakure Ninja. He flew into a frenzy, spitting Drilling Air Bullets indiscriminately in all directions.

Rasa’s face changed drastically at the sight. However, the attacks raining down were too scattered ; even with his strength, it was difficult to cover everything.

In the end, a few strays broke through his gold dust defense network and slammed into the village, instantly causing massive destruction.

« Damn it, this can’t go on. I must stop him immediately ! »

Rasa gritted his teeth inwardly. If he allowed Shukaku to destroy everything at will, even if he eventually resolved the crisis, his prestige in the village would take a huge hit. After all, the Ichibi Jinchuriki was his own biological son.

« Magnet Release : Gold Dust Waves ! »

Rasa’s hands formed seals at high speed, suddenly switching from defense to offense.

A massive amount of gold dust was instantly mobilized, converging in the air into a sea of sand waves that blotted out the sun. Carrying the weight of a thousand tons, it crashed down to suppress Shukaku.

« Damn it ! You meddling bastard ! »

Shukaku growled, quickly infusing his Chakra into the ground, instantly turning all the soil within several miles into sand.

Yellow sand soared into the sky, attempting to intercept the rapidly approaching wave of gold dust.

However, while he was fully focused on the frontal battlefield, he failed to notice several Ninja behind him working together to carry some heavy sacks.

With several sounds of breaking through the air, the sacks were hurled from a distance by catapults.

The sacks burst open, and a stream of pure gold dust flew out like a spirit snake, launching a sneak attack from behind Shukaku while his defenses were empty.

This gold dust was incredibly fast, instantly coiling around Shukaku’s massive body and rapidly penetrating his form, hindering the flow of his Chakra.

« Nani ? My... my body suddenly feels so heavy ! »

« Humph ! It seems the plan worked. »

Rasa snorted coldly, his expression indifferent.

The grand attack just now was merely to distract Shukaku. The true killing move came from the ordinary Ninja whom Shukaku regarded as ants and ignored.

They had personally carried Rasa’s specially made gold dust, and while Shukaku was focused on the front, they quietly circled behind him to launch a surprise attack.

Once successful, this special gold dust, with a density far exceeding ordinary sand, became a weapon to counter Shukaku, heavily binding his movements and completely securing victory.

« Then... now is the time ! »

Rasa exerted his strength, pouring out the massive Chakra within him, instantly crushing the sand waves Shukaku had raised. The golden torrent, carrying the momentum of a thousand thunderclaps, rolled forward.

In just one encounter, Shukaku was wretchedly knocked to the ground by this unrivaled power.

Immediately after, countless particles of gold dust swarmed like maggots on a bone, scrambling to penetrate Shukaku’s body, completely locking down his movements and rendering him unable to move.

« Sealing Team, move out immediately ! »

« Now, hurry ! Reseal this beast ! »

« As expected of the Kazekage, to be able to suppress Shukaku so quickly... »

« Of course ! Lord Kazekage has resealed a rampaging Shukaku many times ! Before that lord, any Tailed Beast is nothing more than a toy to be played with at will ! »

Rasa listened to the compliments of the surrounding Ninja, feeling quite satisfied. The hard work of suppressing the Ichibi had not been in vain.

Before long, the Sealing Team successfully resealed Shukaku. Along with Shukaku’s unwilling roar, the massive monster instantly dissipated, turning back into Gaara, who lay unconscious on the ground.

Rasa glanced down at Gaara, his gaze filled with disappointment and frustration at his perceived failure, before coldly turning away.

In Rasa’s heart, this child was nothing more than an unfinished product that still needed to go through trials and tribulations.

Asakawa Shin took in the blatant contempt in Rasa’s eyes, and a cold sneer rose in his heart :

'When you start obsessing over testing how durable a cup is, it’s destined to shatter.

Right now, Gaara is just like that cup, already covered in cracks and about to break~'

Just then, an Anbu Ninja quietly approached and whispered a report into Rasa’s ear :

« Lord Kazekage, something is wrong. The Anbu squad guarding Gaara has been completely wiped out ! »

« What ? What did you say ? »

Rasa was shocked upon hearing this, his pupils shrinking.

Could it be that Gaara’s rampage this time wasn’t accidental, but had some hidden agenda, or perhaps even an external force pulling the strings behind the scenes ?

In the moment Rasa was shaken and uncertain, Asakawa Shin had already silently circled behind him and launched a lethal surprise attack without hesitation.

« Boil Release : Unrivaled Strength ! »

In an instant, the armor on Asakawa Shin’s body spewed out several jets of scorching high-pressure steam. Accompanied by a piercing hiss, it caused his already amazing speed to soar once more.

He as if a bolt of lightning streaking across the sky, he surged toward Rasa at high speed with devastating power.

« Nani ? »

Such an efficient surprise attack left Rasa with no time to fully react.

Fortunately, he had kept a trick up his sleeve while listening to the Anbu’s report, having already quietly condensed a large mass of gold dust underground beneath his feet, which now came in handy.

The gold dust instantly burst from the ground, quickly forming a crescent-shaped shield that firmly blocked Rasa’s back.

Rasa had just breathed a sigh of relief when Asakawa Shin’s powerful kick arrived.

The gold dust shield was actually kicked to pieces in an instant, splattering in all directions.

Asakawa Shin’s figure only stalled for a moment before he accelerated again under the push of the massive steam behind him, kicking hard toward Rasa, whose pupils were shrinking in shock.

This kick, with the force of a thousand tons, sent Rasa flying far away, coughing up blood.

If the previous gold dust shield hadn’t absorbed most of the impact, he might have already gone to the afterlife to meet the previous Kazekages. Still, a Kazekage dying from a sneak attack is actually quite fitting...

Only then did the surrounding Ninja wake up as if from a dream, letting out cries of terror :

« Quick ! Enemy attack ! Protect Lord Kazekage ! »

« Damn it ! Was Shukaku’s rampage actually an enemy’s plot ? The goal was to exhaust Lord Kazekage’s Chakra ! How despicable ! »

« Quickly, go get Lady Chiyo and Lord Ebizo ! This time it might really be dangerous ! »

« Look at that outfit, and the Boil Release Bloodline Limit used just now ! There’s no mistake ! The attacker is that man, the Five-Tails Jinchuriki of Iwagakure,  »Steam Ninja«  Han ! »

« Spread the word ! Iwagakure... is attacking ! ! ! »
