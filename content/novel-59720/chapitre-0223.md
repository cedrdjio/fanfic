# Chapitre 223 — The Death of Jiraiya

« Big Brother, that white-haired guy ran away, aren’t we going to chase him ? »

Kurotsuchi raised her hand to her forehead, squinting as she gazed into the distance, where the white-haired figure had already shrunk to a mere speck.

Asakawa Shin looked indifferent and said slowly, « That is the karma between master and apprentice ; let them resolve it themselves. It is not our place to interfere. »

Kurotsuchi’s eyes rolled, her gaze darting with interest between Pain and Konan beside her, and her mouth couldn’t help but curl upwards, feeling extremely smug inside.

'I’ve dug up another earth-shattering secret ! The leader of the Akatsuki Organization is actually Nagato. My infiltration of Iwagakure is simply legendary !'

Who would have thought that in the next second, Pain’s indifferent Rinnegan would suddenly sweep over, and a bone-chilling killing intent instantly enveloped her, making her scalp tingle.

'Oh no, I know too much. Is Nagato going to kill me to silence me ?'

She reacted extremely quickly, lunging forward to shield Asakawa Shin, only poking her little head out to glare back vigilantly, which made her feel a bit safer.

Asakawa Shin’s temple throbbed, and unable to bear it any longer, he activated his Psychokinesis, directly hauling Kurotsuchi in front of him.

« What are you doing ? »

« Huh ? I just thought your back looked so majestic, Big Brother, I wanted to admire it a bit longer. » Kurotsuchi scratched her head, her eyes darting around.

« You better be ! »

Watching Asakawa Shin flick his sleeves and walk away, Kurotsuchi shivered and hurriedly scurried after him.

When the two arrived.

Jiraiya was already on his last breath, half-dead.

Watching Jiraiya, who had been pierced by the Six Paths of Pain like a hedgehog and was sinking into the deep water, still desperately trying to pass on information to the toads before dying, Asakawa Shin’s eye twitched, and he rushed over, wanting to attempt a rescue.

However, Konan stepped forward, blocking his path.

« What do you want to do ? »

Asakawa Shin rolled his eyes and pointed at the sinking Jiraiya.

« Recycling important organization assets, why are you stopping me ? »

« Important assets ? » Pain’s brows furrowed slightly.

« Nonsense, this guy is a treasure trove. »

Asakawa Shin suppressed his anxiety, stood with his hands behind his back, and spouted nonsense with a straight face :

« His heart can be dug out for Kakuzu ; the quality of a Sannin-level ninja will definitely make his combat power skyrocket. »

« The corpse can be given to Sasori to be turned into a Human Puppet, which would be another great weapon. »

« As for me, I only need a little flesh and blood to perform Edo Tensei. »

Asakawa Shin clapped his hands together, revealing a 'kind' smile :

« Isn’t this turning waste into treasure ? »

Konan’s face turned black as the bottom of a pot, and just as she wanted to retort, Asakawa Shin looked directly at Pain.

« What ? Leader ? You’ve already killed your teacher, and you still care about a corpse ? You’re truly a 'filial' disciple ! »

Pain’s body shook, and he took one last look at the dying Jiraiya.

His heart had already been pierced by himself ; even if Tsunade came, it would be hard to save him.

'I’m sorry, Teacher Jiraiya. For the sake of peace in the Ninja World, I must ask you to sacrifice yourself a little.'

« Konan, let’s go. » After saying that, he walked away without hesitation.

Although there was hesitation in Konan’s eyes, seeing that Nagato’s mind was made up, she ultimately said nothing more and turned to leave with Pain.

Watching them leave, the heavy stone in Asakawa Shin’s heart finally fell.

He suddenly raised his hand to point at the sky, shouting in horror : « Holy crap ! Kurotsuchi, look quickly, your grandfather is attacking ! »

« Nani ? ! »

Kurotsuchi’s heart jumped wildly, and she subconsciously turned her head to look back.

In the next second, she felt a Pain in the back of her neck, her vision went black, and she rolled her eyes and collapsed softly.

« Big Brother... you tricked me again... »

« This silly girl. »

Asakawa Shin shook his head and chuckled. Just as he was about to mock her a bit more, his Observation Haki suddenly sent a warning ; Jiraiya’s flame of life had only seconds left.

His expression changed, and he hurriedly used his Psychokinesis to fish the person out of the water, pouring a massive amount of Yang energy into his body continuously, which finally kept him hanging on by a thread.

Pulling out the black rods all over his body, the flesh and blood squirmed and regenerated crazily under the catalysis of the Yang energy. In the blink of an eye, Jiraiya’s originally weak breathing became steady and strong.

Asakawa Shin felt secretly pleased : 'The title of the number one healer in the Ninja World might change hands. This piercing wound to the heart would make even Senior Sister Tsunade shake her head if she were here.'

Glancing at the corpse-like Jiraiya, he curled his lips in disappointment : 'I told you not to be reckless, but you didn’t listen. Now I have to fish you out, truly looking for trouble.'

His eyes rolled, and a brilliant, devious plan popped into his mind.

A moment later, Asakawa Shin carried the unconscious Jiraiya and appeared in a cold, damp underground laboratory.

Not long after, a snake-like figure slithered in. After seeing the situation in the laboratory, the person was slightly startled : « Junior Brother ? Why did you bring this trash, Jiraiya, here ? What is this ? »

Asakawa Shin smiled and said : « Senior Brother Orochimaru, it’s a long story. Please help me keep an eye on him for a month ; just don’t let him run away. I have something urgent to do, so I’ll take my leave first. »

Before the words fell, his figure flickered, and he had disappeared into thin air.

Orochimaru’s eyes flickered, and he walked slowly to Jiraiya, examining him carefully.

« Chakra is sealed, and there are restrictions in his mind... What a brilliant Fuinjutsu, it even sealed part of his memories. »

Orochimaru gave a cold, sinister laugh and kicked the dog-like Jiraiya with his toe.

« You would have a day where you fall into my hands ? Perfect, accompany me for a month of live experiments first ; this is a rare material indeed. Seeing this situation, you clearly offended our good Junior Brother ! Then... as long as I don’t kill you, it should be fine, right ? »

...

In the Hokage Office.

« What ? Jiraiya... is dead ? »

Tsunade slammed the table and stood up, her face full of shock and disbelief.

Fukasaku and Elder Shima, the two sages, wore bitter melon-like toad faces and nodded heavily : « Yes, the enemy we encountered this time was truly too strong. They actually possessed the legendary pupil technique — the Rinnegan ! »

Tsunade was still immersed in the huge blow of Jiraiya’s death, unable to speak for a long time.

The two toads looked at each other, and just as they were about to report the key information they had gathered, a deafening explosion suddenly came from outside the window, abruptly interrupting their thoughts.

The two toads hurriedly jumped onto the windowsill and looked towards the direction the sound came from.

Fukasaku shuddered all over : « Old woman, am I seeing things ? Do you see that... does it look like the murderer who killed Jiraiya ? »

Elder Shima nodded repeatedly, her voice tense to the limit : « It’s him ! He’s attacking ! »

Tsunade finally came to her senses, her eyes filled with towering anger, and she ordered sharply.

In the next instant, several afterimages shot out like lightning, heading straight for the invaders.

Outside Konoha, inside a giant tree folded from countless pieces of paper.

Konan’s expression tightened : « Isn’t the plan to act in a month ? »

« It doesn’t matter. Since we have been exposed, let’s start early. »

Nagato’s Chakra surged all over his body, remotely injecting it into the Six Paths of Pain through the black rods.

In an instant, the Six Paths displayed their supernatural powers, and countless summonings and missiles roared out. Explosions occurred one after another in Konoha Village, and flames shot into the sky.

« Today, let’s take down the most troublesome Nine-Tailed first. »
