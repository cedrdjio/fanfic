# Chapitre 215 — Nail Polish

« Recommended by Deidara... so that means you two are in cahoots ? » Asakawa Shin muttered in a low voice.

« Exactly, exactly, Big Bro ! » Kurotsuchi was overjoyed upon hearing this, thinking she had found someone to rely on.

However, before that joyful expression could fully blossom on her face, Asakawa Shin slowly added, « Then I really can’t let you live. Deidara and I are mortal enemies. »

« No, no, no ! Big Bro, please show mercy ! »

Kurotsuchi was scared out of her wits and immediately changed her tune, spitting with a face full of righteous indignation, « Actually, I’ve hated that bastard for a long time ! Big Bro, as long as you spare my life, I’m willing to be your undercover agent and go back to backstab him ! »

« Oh ? Is that so ? » Asakawa Shin stopped in his tracks, looking her up and down with a faint, knowing smile.

« Absolutely true ! My loyalty to Big Bro is unwavering, as clear as the sun and the moon ! » Kurotsuchi raised a leg to swear, her expression incredibly sincere.

Asakawa Shin nodded in satisfaction and said no more.

He suddenly turned around and pressed his palm against Kurotsuchi’s lower abdomen, quickly tracing a complex and profound Flying Thunder God formula onto her skin.

« Big Bro, what are you doing ? »

Kurotsuchi was startled and looked down at the mark that seemed very ominous. « This... this definitely isn’t a normal tattoo, right ? »

« Don’t worry. »

Asakawa Shin withdrew his hand and spoke nonsense with a straight face, « It really is just an ordinary tattoo. It looks quite nice. »

« Big Bro ! I’m truly loyal to you ! » Kurotsuchi looked up, wanting to cry but having no tears, cursing Deidara’s ancestors back eighteen generations in her heart.

Because he was carrying the « burden » that was Kurotsuchi, Asakawa Shin couldn’t directly use the Flying Thunder God Technique. He had to be patient and experience the rare sensation of a long-distance trek.

It took an entire day before he finally met up with his previous Shadow Clone, bringing Kurotsuchi along.

Upon meeting, Kurotsuchi’s eyes widened, looking at the scene before her in disbelief.

That captive, a wounded man covered in bandages, was actually Han, the Five-Tails Jinchuriki ?

'Didn’t Grandpa go after him ? How could he not even stop a single clone, letting them bring Han back... Is Grandpa really getting old ?'

Asakawa Shin’s mouth also twitched slightly.

He looked down at Han’s injuries ; he had to admit, this guy was tough.

The power of the armor’s explosion had nearly torn him apart, and a large amount of metal fragments still remained in his body. Any ordinary Ninja would have seen the King of Hell long ago.

If Asakawa Shin hadn’t been forcibly sustaining his life with Chakra along the way, combined with a Jinchuriki’s abnormal vitality, Han would have had no chance of survival.

However, Asakawa Shin had no intention of healing him. As long as he didn’t stop breathing and could last until the Tailed Beast extraction ceremony was over, it was enough.

He extended a finger and injected a trace of Chakra into the « Suzaku » ring on his hand.

« Hum—— »

The space rippled, and Pain’s signature Phantom Projection appeared out of thin air within the dark cave.

« Well done, Tobi. » Pain’s voice was flat and authoritative.

Then, his gaze shifted, falling upon Kurotsuchi, who was huddled in a corner, and he asked with a hint of doubt, « Who is this ? »

Asakawa Shin waved his hand and explained casually, « Deidara’s sister. She was crying and shouting about wanting to join the Akatsuki Organization, saying Deidara introduced her, so I just brought her along. »

After a pause, he lowered his voice and added in a sinister tone, « Ask Deidara about it later. If he doesn’t claim her, then just dispose of her. »

Hearing this, Kurotsuchi’s body shook even more violently.

This grim and terrifying scene, like a cult gathering, was truly frightening. That bastard Deidara had actually joined a place like this ?

Before she could think further, light and shadow flickered in the cave as several figures appeared one after another.

The other members of the Akatsuki Organization arrived one by one through the Magic Lantern Body Technique.

At that moment, Kurotsuchi’s eyes suddenly lit up. Seeing a familiar figure, she felt as if she had found a savior and lunged forward with a wail :

« Big Brother Deidara ! It really is you ! Quick, save me ! »

Deidara was stunned for a moment and only came to his senses after a while.

Looking at the girl clinging to his leg and crying, he tried to pull his leg back with a look of disgust, his brow furrowed :

« Kurotsuchi ? What are you doing here ? Did you defect from Iwagakure too ? »

« I... I didn’t... »

« Hmm ? »

In an instant, several sharp gazes shot over like blades, focusing collectively on Kurotsuchi.

She suddenly felt as if she had fallen into an ice cellar, shaking like a sieve.

« I... I mean... »

Kurotsuchi stammered, cold sweat pouring down her forehead. She couldn’t squeeze out a complete sentence for a long time, and in her despair, she could only cast a pleading look at Deidara.

Deidara helplessly covered his forehead and let out a long sigh, turning to look at Pain beside him. « Leader, can Kurotsuchi join Akatsuki too ? Her Bloodline Limit talent is actually quite good. »

Pain’s brow furrowed slightly, and he remained silent.

In all honesty, he didn’t want any unnecessary complications.

Now that the plan to collect the Tailed Beasts was progressing smoothly, any variable could be a hidden danger.

But Deidara was an old member of the organization after all, and he had recently captured the Ichibi and the Three-Tails in succession, achieving great merit.

Fine, he would just plant a binding seal on her later ; he doubted she could cause any trouble.

After a brief weighing of options, Pain slowly nodded and uttered one word : « Approved. »

Seeing the leader speak, the other members naturally had no objections. A mere granddaughter of the Tsuchikage was of no consequence.

Pain turned his gaze to the only one physically present, Asakawa Shin : « You take her to the organization’s base to collect her uniform and... nail polish. »

« Nail polish ? » Kurotsuchi looked bewildered. Was this some kind of special sealing Ninja tool ?

She looked around, and upon careful observation, indeed all the members of the Akatsuki Organization wore nail polish.

'It doesn’t look like it has any special effect ? Is it organization culture ? Whatever, I don’t understand, but I’ll respect it...' Kurotsuchi thought to herself.

Pain gave a light cough, pulling everyone’s attention back. « Let the ceremony begin. Summoning Jutsu : Gedo Statue ! »

As the smoke cleared, a hideous behemoth nearly a thousand meters long suddenly appeared.

Witnessing this for the first time, Kurotsuchi screamed in terror and fell back onto her buttocks.

« Wha... what is this thing ? » Her teeth chattered, her voice as thin as a mosquito’s.

Seeing this, Deidara felt even more embarrassed. He rolled his eyes grandly and turned his head away in disgust, as if saying, « I don’t know this embarrassing person at all. »

He was a great artist, a genius Ninja destined to be famous in the Ninja World, and now his reputation was being tarnished.

This subtle expression of disgust did not escape Kurotsuchi’s eyes.

She immediately gritted her teeth in anger, thinking fiercely to herself : 'Fine, fine ! This bastard Deidara dares to look down on me. Just watch how I’ll act obediently in the future, and then backstab you hard !'
