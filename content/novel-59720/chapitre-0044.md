# Chapitre 44 — A Perfect Infiltration

« Eh ? Why did Mommy wet her pants too ? »

Karin seemed to have discovered a huge secret. Originally, she had been a bit embarrassed ; after all, she was already six years old and still wetting her pants...

But if her mother, who was in her twenties, still wet her pants, then her own situation was nothing at all.

« No wonder I always wet the bed ; it turns out I’m just like Mommy. I really am Mommy’s precious daughter ! »

Karin said happily, having completely forgotten her earlier embarrassment.

Of course, the one who was embarrassed now was someone else. Kana was blushing so hard she was practically glowing red, and Asakawa Shin could feel her whole body starting to tremble.

« Tsk... »

Asakawa Shin was also somewhat speechless. This mother and daughter were too timid ; they actually got scared into peeing themselves over such a small scene.

Being so timid, it was no wonder they had been bullied in Kusagakure all this time. Thinking of this, Shin felt a bit sorry for them.

However, since things had come to this, it would be better to find a hot spring inn to wash up first.

Asakawa Shin used Moon Walk to carry the two through the air towards the outskirts of Kusagakure. At this time, the Four Red Yang Formation was still being maintained.

The ruins of Kusagakure were pushed against the barrier of the Four Red Yang Formation by the shockwave, unable to move further. They piled up there and were then ignited by the formation.

Looking down from high above, the center of the original Kusagakure site had been razed to the ground. It had now become a square brazier burning fiercely.

« Haha, be grateful for my mercy ! This even saves the trouble of cremation. »

Asakawa Shin admired his masterpiece for a moment, and the displeasure in his heart dissipated quite a bit.

« Hmph ! Those who dare to bully my maid deserve such an end. »

While Asakawa Shin was admiring the fruits of his labor, he felt a familiar sensation slowly flowing over him again. He couldn’t help but turn his head in shock and say :

« Wait, Sister Kana, you... »

Kana let out a soft moan and panted, « I don’t know why... I just couldn’t hold it... I’m sorry, Master ! »

After saying that, her little mouth puckered as if she was about to burst into tears.

Asakawa Shin was startled and quickly comforted her in a gentle voice :

« Don’t cry, Sister Kana. I don’t mind this kind of thing. Besides, I just finished a battle and I’m covered in sweat ; I needed to change my clothes anyway.

But since you can’t hold it, why not just finish peeing all at once now ? Otherwise, it wouldn’t be good if it rained down on some kids while we’re traveling through the sky later. »

Under Asakawa Shin’s curious gaze, Kana indeed couldn’t hold back. Her whole body trembled as she peed. It took a full few dozen seconds before her body went limp, and she slumped into Asakawa Shin’s arms without moving.

« Mommy, if you have to pee, just pee. Why are you making those noises ? You gave me a fright. »

« Karin... »

« Hmm ? What is it, Mommy ? »

« Shut up ! »

« ...Oh ! »

...

As the Shadow Clone released the Four Red Yang Formation, Asakawa Shin flew away through the sky with the two women.

A long time later, a middle-aged Ninja slowly crawled up from the deep rift that had been sliced open by the « Slash Wave » sword qi.

He was an elite Jonin of Kusagakure. Seeing that things were going south earlier, he had decisively used the Transformation Technique to hide deep within the rift, thereby avoiding the Galaxy Impact.

He slumped on the ground, gasping for breath. As far as his eyes could see, there was a sea of fire. The Village he once knew and his former companions were all buried in this sea of flames.

His face was filled with lingering fear. If it hadn’t been for his quick thinking, he would have had a place in that sea of fire right now.

After catching his breath for a moment, he stood up, wanting to leave as quickly as possible.

« The only plan now is to go to Hozuki Castle and seek refuge with Lord Mui. He is the last hope of our Kusagakure. »

Just as he was about to put his plan into action, a playful voice came from behind him.

« Hehe, what a pity. You’re not going anywhere. »

« Wha... what ? » Hearing the voice behind him, his whole body trembled. It was him !

He would never forget that demon’s voice for the rest of his life. This pain was already etched into the depths of his soul ; he would not forget it for the rest of his days.

Of course, if there even were any days left.

He turned around in despair, and sure enough, the figure of that demon appeared in his sight—the monster who had completely destroyed Kusagakure single-handedly.

By now, there was nothing left to fear. He had done his best and could calmly accept his death.

Asakawa Shin’s Shadow Clone also looked at the man in front of him curiously. Was he actually this calm ?

« Oh ? You’re not afraid of death ? That’s a first for me. Do you have any last words to leave behind ? »

The man slowly shook his head. At this point, what was there to say ? The winner takes all.

« Then, farewell, little mouse.

Rankyaku : Willow Wind. »

Unlike the violent attack the man had imagined, this Willow Wind seemed exceptionally gentle. It merely lifted his body softly and slowly flew him into the sea of fire.

'What a gentle attack.'

That was his final thought. Subsequently, his brain was shattered by the Haki contained within the wind, and he completely lost consciousness.

Seeing his corpse fall perfectly into the sea of fire, Asakawa Shin’s Shadow Clone felt a sense of accomplishment.

« A family should be neat and tidy. No need to thank me, Uncle. »

« Do a good deed every day ; it starts with me. »

The other Shadow Clones also converged at this time.

« Everyone, is it over ? »

« We’ve scraped the ground three feet deep. Even the earthworms underground were split vertically in two, and the eggs had their yolks shaken loose. Even if a dog came by, it would have to take a slap before it could leave. There’s absolutely no way there are any survivors. »

« That’s good. The original body promised Uncle Shikaku not to cause any diplomatic disputes. This way, there won’t be any problems. »

« What about this rift ? I just investigated and it’s nearly ten kilometers long. The north-south traffic in this area of the Land of Grass has been completely cut off.

That’s not all ; this rift actually cut off the Green Grass River, and the downstream area is completely dried up.

The commoners still need to farm and have water to drink. The original body really has no sense of public morality ! »

« What the heck, the original body went too far. Why use that move for just a measly Kusagakure ? The damage to the terrain is too severe. »

« Don’t you know yourself ? It’s just so he could show off in front of the sisters. »

« We’re talking about the original body, why are you bringing me into it ? Are we going to discuss what to do with this rift now ? »

« It’s too much trouble. Forget it, let the original body handle it ! »

« True. Then, disperse ! »

« Poof ! »

...

Asakawa Shin’s figure paused slightly while traveling through the air, a smug smile curling at the corners of his mouth.

'Now that all the witnesses have been killed, this is a perfect infiltration operation.

As expected of you, Asakawa Shin !

This time, Uncle Shikaku will also look at me with new eyes. Hahaha.'

However, his smugness didn’t last long, and his brow furrowed deeply.

The Shadow Clones didn’t want to deal with that damn rift at all. He had self-awareness ; even if he made more Shadow Clones, the result would likely be the same.

'If left alone, the lives of the civilians in the Land of Grass will probably be very difficult. It seems I, the original body, must personally go and handle it.

Damn it, then wasn’t my learning the Shadow Clone Technique a waste ?

Couldn’t Tobirama have modified the Shadow Clone Technique ? This is clearly a bug !

My Shadow Clones are a bit off ; they’re really quite something.'
