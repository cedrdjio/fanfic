# Chapitre 83 — Lord God of Gamblers

Shizune doesn’t know how to play at all, yet she’s managed to win so much ; she’s doing great !

And Tsunade, hmm... she’s also doing great ; she truly lives up to her reputation as the legendary Big Fat Sheep.

Watching the dazed look in Tsunade’s eyes, Asakawa Shin suddenly felt a playful urge.

He held a silver chip with a denomination of 10,000 ryo and gently waved it in front of Tsunade. Tsunade was involuntarily drawn to it, her gaze following the chip back and forth.

'Oh ?' He raised an eyebrow, as if he had discovered a novel toy, and decided to use the chip as a cat teaser, amusingly teasing this big kitty.

Back and forth, swaying left and right, Tsunade seemed to be in a trance, following that glimmer of light as if hypnotized.

After teasing her a few times, she suddenly snapped out of it, her face darkened, and she snatched the chip away, shouting angrily : « You brat, are you itching for a beating ? »

A satisfied smile curled up on Asakawa Shin’s lips, and he said with a cheeky grin :

« Senior Sister, I just saw that you’d lost everything. Besides, we agreed to pay it back a hundredfold, remember ?

Here, this 10,000 ryo is for you. »

Only then did Tsunade glance at the chip box in front of Asakawa Shin, seeing it piled high, almost overflowing.

Her pupils constricted, and she gasped in shock : « This... you won all this from just 100 ryo ? »

Asakawa Shin shrugged, looking smug.

She turned her gaze to Shizune, and Shizune’s situation was even more exaggerated ; the chips in front of her were already piling up into a small mountain.

Tsunade did a rough calculation and realized it was no less than 500 million ryo, which made the corner of her mouth twitch, and she began to feel a bit dejected.

However, she quickly thought of something, her eyes lit up like a fox spotting prey, and she swiftly stood up and rushed to Shizune’s side.

Just as Shizune was about to share her joy, Tsunade sat down right where she was and shoved her aside...

Tsunade’s eyes lit up as she looked at the chips in front of her, and she shouted excitedly :

« Hehe, now I have money to win back my losses ! Well done, Shizune ! »

Shizune rubbed her sore bottom and scrambled up, pouting as she protested :

« What is this ! That was money I worked so hard to win ! Lady Tsunade is bullying me again ! »

Tsunade acted as if she hadn’t heard anything, completely turning a deaf ear to her protest.

On the other side, the casino owner felt a sharp pain in his heart. Why wasn’t this Big Fat Sheep playing by the rules ?

Didn’t she usually lose everything and leave quickly ?

This time, she was surprisingly cautious, betting only 10,000 ryo per round, while having the little girl next to her bet in reverse, quickly winning a large sum of funds.

But in the next moment, he felt relieved ; it was a good thing he had invited the Big Fat Sheep into the VIP room from the start.

If she had caused a crowd of gamblers to follow her bets in the main hall, he might have already declared bankruptcy by now.

Just then, a waiter from the outer hall hurried in and whispered into his ear, causing his expression to change drastically in shock.

He couldn’t help but turn his gaze toward Asakawa Shin, cursing inwardly : 'There’s actually another expert here ?

He’s challenged a dozen of my dealers, and in just a short while, he’s turned 100 ryo into 100 million ryo. Who is this guy ?

The Konoha God of Gamblers ? I haven’t heard of him. Could he be external help hired by the Big Fat Sheep ?

The situation seems a bit wrong. Should I just admit the loss, send them away, and cut my losses ?'

However, just as this thought arose, he caught sight of the Big Fat Sheep sitting in Shizune’s seat, waving her hand and losing another 10 million ryo.

His heart stirred ; fortune favors the bold, so he decided to win back the Big Fat Sheep’s 500 million ryo first.

Unfortunately, things didn’t go as planned, and Asakawa Shin soon joined the gambling game as well.

As soon as Tsunade called out « Big, » Asakawa Shin unhesitatingly bet all his chips on « Small. »

Tsunade raised an eyebrow and said with some concern : « Junior Brother, that’s not how you play !

If you lose like this, you’ll have no way to make a comeback. It’s better to keep some chips on you... »

Before Tsunade could finish, Asakawa Shin interrupted : « Senior Sister, you don’t need to say anything more. It’s precisely because I trust you with all my heart that I’m playing this way ! »

Veins popped on Tsunade’s forehead in anger, and she glared at him fiercely, shouting : « I’d like to see if you can keep winning ! »

As it turned out, he really could...

Whether Tsunade was convinced or not, no one knew, but the casino owner was thoroughly convinced by now.

He hurriedly stepped forward to interrupt the game, pleading with Asakawa Shin with a bitter face :

« This God of Gamblers, please show mercy and don’t continue.

Our casino cannot exchange any more cash. Even if you continue to win, we won’t be able to pay it out. »

Asakawa Shin looked over with some dissatisfaction. What is this ?

Tsunade had over 400 million ryo in front of her, and he had 400 million ryo in front of him ; that only adds up to 800 million ryo.

And they call themselves the largest casino in the Land of Hot Springs ? Isn’t the Land of Hot Springs a famous tourist country ? Is that all the strength they have ?

Tsunade was also annoyed. It was the first time she had controlled such a huge amount of chips at a gambling table ; it was more than all her mission rewards combined.

She hadn’t played enough yet, so how could she give up easily ?

Her eyes shifted, and she looked at Asakawa Shin with a flowery smile : « Junior Brother, how about... the two of us play a round by ourselves ? »

After saying that, she turned to the boss : « Borrowing your venue for a bit shouldn’t be a problem, right ? »

The boss agreed hurriedly : « Thank you both. Please, feel free to use it. »

Play a couple of rounds with Tsunade ?

Asakawa Shin also became interested : « Then who will be the dealer ? »

Tsunade thought about how she didn’t seem to be able to bet correctly no matter what, and she hesitated for a moment : « I’ll be the dealer. »

Asakawa Shin was noncommittal and instructed Yūgao, who was on the side :

« Later, help me collect and distribute the chips. I want to do something cool. »

After speaking, he actually turned his chair 180 degrees, keeping his back entirely to Tsunade :

« Senior Sister, to make you lose with conviction, I’ve decided to play without using my hands or eyes. Pretty loyal, right ? »

Tsunade was about to grind her teeth to dust, roaring in her heart : 'Damn it, he’s completely looking down on me ! This time, I’ll definitely make you pay !'

Tsunade slammed the table in anger, the force of which sent the dice flying into the air.

She quickly picked up the dice cup, caught all the dice still in the air, and shook them in a pleasing manner.

« Clap ! »

« Bets are closed ! »

A trace of a smile flashed in the depths of Asakawa Shin’s eyes. It was all flashy, but what was the point ?

He smiled slightly and said confidently : « Betting 10 million, 1-3-4, 8 points, Small. »

Yūgao immediately accepted the remote control excitedly. Soon, the result of the game was revealed, and it was exactly as Asakawa Shin had predicted, without the slightest error.

Yūgao was so excited that she jumped up, cheering : « We won, Lord Shin, we won ! »

Asakawa Shin waved his hand modestly : « Don’t call me Lord Shin, call me Lord God of Gamblers~ »

...

In contrast, Tsunade appeared somewhat dazed. He actually guessed the specific numbers accurately ? How on earth did he do that ?

In the following rounds, no matter how Tsunade changed the rhythm or shook the dice vigorously, Asakawa Shin was always able to state the precise numbers without hesitation.

Tsunade, with an expression of disbelief, picked up the dice and the dice cup to examine them carefully.

'The dice are fine ! Huh ? This dice cup is made of wood. Could it be because of this ? Let me try with a different material !'

A glimmer of hope flashed in her eyes. But she thought well, yet it was completely useless ; soon, the chips in front of her were reduced by half.

'O... Oyster, I have to change the way I play.

This brat is a bit evil ; no matter how I shake it, he can accurately report the numbers, and there hasn’t been a single trace of Chakra fluctuation the whole time. It really is baffling.

He’s cheating ; this jerk Shin is definitely cheating ! Damn it...'

Her brows furrowed slightly, and she calculated rapidly in her heart, then offered a charming smile to Asakawa Shin :

« Junior Brother, playing this all the time is so boring, and it’s making Senior Sister’s hands sore. Why don’t we change the way we play ? »

« ... »

Unfortunately, it was doomed to be futile. Tsunade soon lost all her chips, once again becoming a dazed, life-doubting salted fish.

Asakawa Shin looked at the mountain of chips in front of him and couldn’t help but scratch his head. This speed of making money seems much faster than selling silk, right ?

« Boss, exchange all these chips for cash. »

Just as Asakawa Shin was about to leave in satisfaction, Tsunade suddenly woke up as if from a dream, rushed forward excitedly, slid onto her knees, and hugged his thigh tightly :

« Junior Brother... No ! Lord God of Gamblers !

You have such amazing gambling skills, why didn’t you say so earlier !

Teach me quickly ; I don’t want to be the Big Fat Sheep anymore. I want to become the legendary Great Demon King ! »
