# Chapitre 162 — The Actors Appear

As a large number of spectators fell into a deep sleep following an unknown attack, the scene instantly devolved into chaos.

Seeing things take a turn for the worse, the Daimyo and the nobles hurriedly evacuated.

The Ninjas in the arena watched with flickering gazes, yet a strange tacit understanding formed between them ; all held their positions until the Daimyo and his party were far away, at which point battle suddenly erupted.

Hiruzen Sarutobi watched the Sunagakure Ninjas slaughtering Konoha Ninjas and turned to question with a dark expression, « Rasa, are you trying to provoke a war ? »

Rasa let out a long breath, his tense body finally relaxing.

His cold gaze slowly swept over Hiruzen Sarutobi, his heart filled with resentment.

Why was it that, as fellow Kage of their respective villages, he had to work himself to death panning for gold in the desert while Hiruzen Sarutobi could sit back and enjoy the fruits of others' labor, raking in money every day ?

Sunagakure was simply too poor, and it was hateful that the Daimyo even wanted to cut their funding, forcing him, the Kazekage, to search the desert for gold like a common gold miner.

And this was precisely the reason he started the war.

No other reason—just poverty.

A man with nothing to lose has nothing to fear ; just do it.

Raikage A, meanwhile, looked toward Asakawa Shin with a dark gaze ; the confrontation several years ago still left him with lingering fears.

Back then, that boy had made him feel a fatal threat with just his presence alone, even causing him to wake up from nightmares repeatedly over the years.

Just then, someone contacted him in secret.

He had to consider whether this was the only chance he would ever have in his life.

If they could gather the power of three sides, even against Konoha, even against Asakawa Shin, they might not be without a chance of victory.

Thus, the three parties made a secret agreement to join forces today to besiege Konoha Village and assassinate Asakawa Shin.

Unable to restrain himself, Rasa was the first to strike.

« Magnet Release : Gold Dust ! »

With a booming roar, Rasa unleashed his « money power » as a massive amount of gold dust poured out from the scroll he carried, crashing toward Hiruzen Sarutobi like a waterfall.

Hiruzen Sarutobi was startled and retreated repeatedly to dodge, while simultaneously forming hand seals and shouting :

« Earth Release : Earth-Style Wall ! »

An earthen wall rose in response, blocking his front and barely withstanding the impact of the gold dust.

However, as the gold dust accumulated, the earthen wall let out a series of strained groans, looking as if it were about to collapse at any moment.

The two traded blows, neither giving an inch, and for a time, they were locked in a stalemate.

A, however, remained unmoved, his gaze scanning the field as if waiting for something.

Hiding in the shadows, Orochimaru cursed under his breath and could only appear as agreed, launching a surprise attack on the Konoha Ninjas in the field.

« Oh no, it’s Orochimaru ! Everyone, be careful ! » The crowd cried out in alarm, everyone bracing themselves for battle.

Just then, Tsunade suddenly appeared, blocking Orochimaru’s path and engaging him in a fierce battle.

Seeing this, A’s brow furrowed slightly, but he still held his position, and the Kumogakure Ninjas beside him also watched coldly without moving.

In the shadows in the distance, Danzo and Obito exchanged a look.

« Madara, that old fox the Raikage is indeed cautious ; he’s waiting for us to strike first. Very well, let me go meet my old friend first. »

Before his voice had even faded, he stepped directly out of the shadows and sped toward the high platform.

This ostentatious move instantly drew the attention of the entire field.

« It’s him ! Danzo Shimura ! How could this damn rogue Ninja appear here ? »

Sasuke was shocked to hear this ; after dodging Gaara’s attack, he hurriedly turned his head to look.

« This bastard is Danzo Shimura ? The murderer who killed my father ? Unforgivable ! »

Sasuke gritted his teeth, killing intent boiling in his eyes.

Especially upon seeing that piercing Three-Tomoe Sharingan in Danzo’s eye socket, his fury became uncontrollable, and he wished he could charge up immediately to kill him and avenge his father.

But just as Sasuke was about to move, Gaara’s offensive struck again, leaving him no room to distract himself, so he could only temporarily give up.

Itachi, on the spectator stands, did not have such concerns ; after settling his mother, he immediately flashed out and sped toward the high platform to provide support.

Hiruzen Sarutobi had just traded a move with Rasa and was panting when he saw his old friend casually arriving. « Danzo ! Do you really intend to betray Konoha and collude with Sunagakure ? »

Danzo gave a cold laugh. « Monkey, from the moment you imprisoned me without distinguishing right from wrong, we were enemies. What is there to say now ? »

« Danzo, everything I did was for the sake of Konoha ! »

« Shut up ! You think I don’t know ? You did all that for yourself. Did you think that by controlling Asakawa Shin, you could control everything ? Today, I’m going to trample you completely into the mud ! »

By the time Danzo finished speaking, his face was contorted, and in the next second, he launched a fierce attack.

« Wind Release : Vacuum Sphere ! »

In an instant, a huge sphere of wind was spat out by him, blasting straight toward Hiruzen Sarutobi.

Hiruzen Sarutobi’s pupils shrank.

Aged and physically declining, he was already struggling to deal with Rasa alone ; now adding Danzo was like adding frost to snow.

What surprised and confused him even more was that Danzo’s strength had actually increased rather than decreased ; clearly similar in age to himself, his body was as robust as if he weren’t an old man at all.

What puzzled him most of all was that both Kakashi and Danzo had transplanted the Sharingan, and Danzo even had more than one...

Yet Kakashi was already exhausted and weak, while Danzo remained as vigorous as ever.

At this point, Hiruzen Sarutobi could only lament in his heart about that useless Kakashi.

Fortunately, at the critical moment, Uchiha Itachi arrived in time, and a Fire Release : Great Fireball Technique neutralized the crisis.

Danzo sneered and pointed at Itachi, questioning sternly : « Monkey, you’ve actually conspired with the Uchiha Clan. You’ve truly disappointed me. Have you forgotten the teachings of Tobirama-sensei ? »

Hiruzen Sarutobi had a strange expression and didn’t dare to respond at all.

Over this past month, he had been disciplined by Senju Tobirama until he was utterly convinced, and he kept this teacher’s « teachings » firmly in mind, not daring to forget for a single moment.

However, his silence was misinterpreted by Danzo as a guilty conscience, and his expression became increasingly smug : « What ? Nothing to say ? I said long ago that there’s no need to reason with these inherently evil Uchiha ; just kill them all and be done with it ! »

Hiruzen Sarutobi’s eyes darted around, and as if finally unable to restrain himself, he roared : « Shut up ! Tobirama-sensei meant no such thing ! Stop your nonsense and attempts to drive a wedge between us. If he were alive, he would certainly give you a good lesson ! »

Danzo snorted, his face full of disdain : « Lesson me ? Ridiculous ! I have long since surpassed Tobirama-sensei and grasped the true will of fire. Besides, he is now just a mindless puppet ; how could he lesson me ? Hahahaha ! »

In the shadows in the distance, Senju Hashirama firmly held back Senju Tobirama, advising in a low voice : « Don’t get angry, Tobirama. Children’s words carry no weight ; Danzo is still just a child, why bother taking him seriously... »

By the end, even Hashirama couldn’t help but laugh.

Tobirama gave him a fierce glare : « Just shut up, Big Brother ! Orochimaru has summoned us ; it’s time for us to make our appearance. »

With that, the two figures flickered and were transported via the Flying Thunder God Technique into the coffins Orochimaru had prepared beforehand.

A moment later, they feigned being under control and slowly walked out of the coffins.

The entire field witnessed this scene, and it instantly fell into a dead silence.

Seeing this, the Raikage finally made up his mind and turned to Tutai, whispering : « Act according to plan ! »

Inside Kumogakure.

« The Raikage-sama’s order has arrived. Move ! »

Mabui’s expression was grave as she nodded slowly : « I’m counting on you, Yugito-sama, Killer Bee-sama ! »

« Ninjutsu : Heavenly Transfer Technique ! »

Before the words had even faded, the two Tailed Beast Jinchuriki had turned into two streaks of light, instantly crossing space to arrive at the Konoha battlefield.

Accompanied by a flash of dazzling light, under the shocked gazes of everyone, two figures in semi-Tailed Beast transformation landed with a boom !

As they withdrew their Chakra and revealed their true forms, a burst of exclamations immediately erupted on the field.

« This... this is the Two-Tails Jinchuriki Nii Yugito and the Eight-Tails Jinchuriki Killer Bee ! Everyone, on high alert ! Kumogakure has also joined the war ! »
