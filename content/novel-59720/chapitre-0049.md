# Chapitre 49 — Karin Enrolls

The next day, in the hallway of the Ninja Academy.

Naruto rubbed his eyes in disbelief. « Shin, you guy, have you finally come to school ?

You suddenly disappeared for so many days. Do you have any idea how worried I was about you ? »

Seeing Naruto looking at him with teary eyes, Asakawa Shin also felt a bit guilty.

« Sorry, Naruto. That night, Hokage-sama suddenly summoned me, saying there was an emergency mission he needed my help with.

I was in such a rush that I forgot to leave a Shadow Clone behind. I’m sorry for making you worry. »

Asakawa Shin had indeed been in a hurry that night. Someone actually wanted to steal his money ?

It made him as anxious as a badger discovering stolen melons in a patch.

Seeing that Naruto’s mood had improved significantly, Asakawa Shin winked at him and said mysteriously :

« Naruto, I was definitely in the wrong this time. However, I brought back a surprise for you from my trip outside the Village. I guarantee you’ll be very happy.

For the sake of the surprise I brought you, will you forgive me this time ? »

« A surprise ? » Naruto looked at Asakawa Shin suspiciously, sizing him up. « Where is it ? How come I don’t see it ? »

« Alright, alright, that’s enough. If you keep circling me, I’m going to get dizzy. As for the surprise, you’ll find out soon enough.

Now, let’s head to class together. Speaking of which, you only just arrived at this hour. Were you almost late today ? As expected, you just can’t manage without me watching over you. »

After saying that, ignoring Naruto’s shouting, he took Naruto’s hand and led him toward the classroom.

By this time, almost everyone in the classroom had arrived. When they saw Asakawa Shin walking in holding Naruto’s hand, the room went silent for a moment before instantly erupting into a clamor.

« Shin, you’re finally here ! I heard you went on a secret mission ? Is that true ? »

« Isn’t that a bit of an exaggeration ? Aren’t we just first-year students at the Academy ? How can you already be going on missions ? You’re almost catching up to the legendary Kakashi ! »

« What is Kakashi compared to Shin-kun ? I heard from my dad that Shin-kun saved many people this time and prevented a huge loss for the Village ! »

Shikamaru stood up, his face full of gratitude. « Shin, thank you for saving my dad this time. He said if you hadn’t stepped in when you did, he would’ve met his end in some backwater. I... »

Asakawa Shin patted his shoulder steadily. « Shikamaru, you’re my right-hand man, my brother. Don’t mention things like great debts of gratitude or working like a horse. We have a long road ahead of us. »

'I didn’t even mention those things...' Shikamaru took a deep breath, adjusted his emotions, and asked Asakawa Shin for instructions :

« Shin, you’re back just in time. The monthly competition for our Six Styles Training Class is almost here. How about we hold it this weekend ? »

« You decide. » Asakawa Shin nodded noncommittally.

'Huh ? Why do I feel a chill down my spine ?' Asakawa Shin quickly turned around. Hmm ? Nothing unusual, just his classmates.

He didn’t notice that Hinata, in the corner, was glaring at him fiercely, her teeth clenched. However, because of the Byakugan, the focus of her gaze wasn’t clear.

'That hateful Shin, how can he hold Naruto’s hand in public ? Doesn’t he know about the impropriety of physical contact between men ?'

'Just as I thought, Shin is the biggest obstacle to me taming Naruto ! I have to find a way...'

Not long after chatting with everyone, the school bell rang, and everyone returned to their seats.

Soon, Iruka walked into the classroom. Unlike before, there was a little red-haired girl following behind him.

« Huh ? Who is that ? I’ve never seen her before. »

« Is she a new transfer student ? Red hair is quite rare. »

« She’s pretty cute. I wonder what her personality is like ? »

« Tsk, another troublesome woman. What a headache. »

Iruka tapped on the desk to get the students' attention. Once everyone was quiet, he gestured toward Karin and introduced her to the class :

« As you can see, this is a new girl in our class. Her name is Uzumaki Karin. From today on, you will all be classmates, so please get along well. »

The classroom immediately became lively again.

« What ? Uzumaki ? Isn’t that the same surname as Naruto ? »

« So, this new girl is from the same clan as Naruto ? She couldn’t be an idiot too, could she... »

« How could that be ? An idiot of that caliber isn’t that common~ »

« Uchiha Sasuke ! Who are you calling an idiot ? You’re the idiot ! You big dummy ! » Naruto shouted in anger, slamming the desk as he stood up.

Sasuke wasn’t happy either. He glanced at Inuzuka Kiba and said defiantly, « I wasn’t the first one to say it. Why are you only picking on me ? »

« I don’t care about the others, but it’s not okay for you, Uchiha Sasuke, to say it ! »

« What ? Don’t be so arrogant, you guy. Just wait until the competition this weekend ; I’ll give you a good lesson and show you how powerful Uchiha Sasuke is ! »

Asakawa Shin glanced at the two of them speechlessly. These two were as passionate as ever, their eyes practically shooting sparks at each other.

On the podium, Uzumaki Karin was also observing them : 'Uzumaki Naruto ? This guy is Lord Shin’s best friend ? And it seems he’s also my clansman...'

'Very well, it’s decided. Lord Shin’s friend is my friend. I’ll see if I can make friends with him later.'

While she was thinking this, she suddenly felt an unignorable gaze staring intently at her. Karin shifted her eyes and saw...

It was actually a little girl whose eyes looked like they had cataracts.

'Tsk, how pitiful. Blind at such a young age. Looks like I was mistaken.'

'But she is quite cute.'

She then stopped paying attention to the little girl and continued to observe Naruto.

Meanwhile, Hinata’s sense of crisis was at its peak, and sweat even began to bead on her forehead :

'That look... there’s no mistake, it’s disdain ! And she keeps staring at Naruto without letting go...'

'Oh... oh no, is this what they call the « transfer student type » in novels ? I remember that in these situations, the « childhood friend type » usually gets defeated and becomes a loser...'

'And me... Naruto saved me over a year ago, so we’ve known each other since then. Doesn’t that make me and Naruto childhood friends ?'

'Then wouldn’t I become... the loser ? Hiss... Absolutely not !'

By now, Iruka had finished scolding Naruto and Sasuke, and the class slowly quieted down.

He cleared his throat and looked at Uzumaki Karin. « Karin, you’re not familiar with everyone yet. Why don’t you introduce yourself ? »

« It’s a tradition, you know. We all did introductions on the first day of school, so you should do one too. »

« It’s very simple. Just say your name, what you like, what you hate, your interests, and your dreams out loud. »

Karin thought for a moment. An introduction ? I understand !

« My name is Uzumaki Karin. What I like most is Lord Shin. As for things I hate, I used to have them, but they no longer exist... »

« My interests and dreams are the same : to serve by Lord Shin’s side forever as his maid.

Just like a puppy—loyal, never leaving, never abandoning, and never betraying him for a lifetime !

In short, I want to be Lord Shin’s 'puppy' and offer him my loyalty~ »

« Eh ? »

« Holy crap ! »
