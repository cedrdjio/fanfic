# Chapitre 99 — It's a Promise

'My body... I can’t move. What is this feeling ?

A chill ?

I’m actually feeling a chill ?

It’s as if my body’s instincts are screaming ; if I move even a little, I’ll lose my life the next second. What on earth is this... ?'

The Raikage looked at Asakawa Shin with a face full of absolute horror.

Asakawa Shin merely glanced at him indifferently for a moment before withdrawing his gaze, seemingly not taking him seriously at all.

At the same time, that Haoshoku Haki, as violent as the breaking of a celestial river’s dam, gradually dissipated.

The Raikage felt the heavy pressure around him suddenly lift, and his tense body finally regained its mobility.

His body trembled, and he gripped his wine glass tightly, not daring to let go, forcing an explanation :

« Please don’t misunderstand, everyone. I just felt this banquet was a bit too dull and boring. »

« So I wanted to have our Kumogakure Ninjas come up and perform to liven things up. It was just a gesture of goodwill. »

« But since this young friend here doesn’t like it, I’ll just have them withdraw. »

As he finished speaking, the air at the table, which had been stagnant, seemed to flow once again.

Seeing that the Konoha group didn’t intend to pursue the matter, the Raikage breathed a sigh of relief, but the terror deep in his eyes had yet to subside.

'What kind of technique is this ? To be able to render me immobile in an instant, and it’s far from being targeted at just me...'

He panted slightly and looked around ; most of the Kumogakure Ninjas lying in ambush had already collapsed.

The remaining few dozen elite Ninjas, though barely conscious, were in a wretched state, hurriedly retreating with their fallen comrades.

This sent another wave of shock through his heart : 'In the face of this kid, numerical superiority is actually meaningless ?'

'Damn it, is he another Minato Namikaze ?'

'Why does Konoha always produce such geniuses one after another ? Why does the Six Paths Sage favor them so much !'

For a moment, he was so overwhelmed that he was at a loss, forgetting to put down the wine glass he was clutching until he heard Tsunade’s mockery :

« Raikage, are you nursing that drink ? Down it already ! »

Only then did he snap back to his senses. With a slightly stiff expression, he filled his glass and downed it.

Perhaps due to the strong liquor, the terror in the Raikage’s eyes gradually faded, and he regained his composure :

'Although most of the Ninjas were knocked down by that unknown technique, the true elites are still here and capable of fighting.'

'If we can regroup and give it our all, we might... not be without a chance.'

Just as he had this thought, Asakawa Shin’s gaze swept over him again, making his spine go cold and sending a shiver of dread through him.

'I’ll die...'

That familiar chill returned, and his instincts began to scream a sharp warning, leaving him paralyzed and afraid to make the slightest move.

Fortunately, Asakawa Shin only locked eyes with him for a moment, giving him a warning before looking away.

He took a deep breath, trying his best to suppress the emotions surging within him, and completely abandoned that bullshit plan in his heart.

A hint of bitterness appeared at the corners of his mouth as he tentatively addressed Tsunade, who was drinking incessantly :

« Tsunade, who exactly is this young friend beside you ? To be able to easily take down the Xuanyuan Group at such a young age, he’s truly a young hero ! »

Hearing this, a genuine smile appeared on Tsunade’s face as she introduced him with pride :

« This is my junior disciple, Asakawa Shin, and the future pillar of our Konoha ! »

« Pillar ? »

The Raikage’s heart skipped a beat again, and his gaze involuntarily fell upon the seedling atop Asakawa Shin’s head, countless conjectures flashing through his mind.

However, his hands were quick as he nimbly filled a glass and approached Asakawa Shin, politely offering it with both hands :

« Young friend, thanks to you for getting rid of the Xuanyuan Group. I haven’t had the chance to thank you for that favor yet, so let me toast you ! »

Asakawa Shin glanced at him in surprise, thinking to himself : 'This guy couldn’t have been dazed by the Haoshoku Haki, could he ? He’s actually toasting me.'

Seeing this, Yūgao hurried forward to stop him : « No ! Lord Shin is still a minor and cannot drink ! Lord Raikage, don’t you go corrupting Lord Shin ! »

The Raikage slapped his forehead in realization and quickly withdrew the wine glass, replacing it with a soft drink.

« Look at my memory. Then, young friend, have a drink to wet your whistle. »

Asakawa Shin didn’t take the drink. Instead, he picked up a cup of tea from the table and offered a distant toast : « Let’s toast together. It’s no fun drinking alone. »

The Raikage’s expression darkened, and a wave of displeasure rose in his heart. Was he guarding against being poisoned ?

As the grand Raikage, would he ever stoop to such a despicable act ? This was being far too cautious !

Little did he know that Tutai, standing nearby, had indeed considered poisoning, but abandoned the idea upon remembering that Tsunade was sitting right there.

He forced a stiff smile, downed the drink in his cup, and then cupped his hands toward the Konoha group :

« Please make yourselves at home. Kumogakure has urgent business and I need to head back early. I’ll take my leave now. Please continue, no need to see me out. »

Having said that, as if he had made a decision, he left without looking back.

Seeing him in such a hurry, Asakawa Shin couldn’t help but frown and kindly reminded him : « Lord Raikage, are you going the wrong way ? The checkout counter isn’t over there. »

The Raikage’s retreating figure suddenly stiffened. As if just coming to his senses, he smoothly turned around and headed for the checkout counter, muttering :

« Ah, right. This wine went to my head ; it’s made me a bit lightheaded. »

« Huh ? Strange, wasn’t he just drinking a soft drink ? »

« ... »

« Hello, guest. Your total consumption comes to three hundred million taels, » the waiter said with a professional smile and a respectful tone.

« How much ? Three hundred million taels ? ! »

The Raikage’s eyes widened, and his voice suddenly rose : « My subordinates just calculated it to be only two hundred million ! Is this a rip-off joint ? »

« Sir, three hundred million taels is the accurate figure. »

The waiter explained calmly : « The distinguished guests you hosted ordered an extra portion to go, so this amount is correct. »

« To go ? They actually ordered takeout too ? »

The Raikage’s vision went dark, and he nearly collapsed. He patted his pockets, feeling a bit guilty :

« Put it on my tab for now. You know, I am the Raikage. »

« I’m very sorry, guest. »

The waiter maintained a polite smile, but his tone was firm and unquestionable :

« This shop has no precedent for credit. Since you are the Raikage, you must know the Daimyo of the Land of Lightning, right ? »

« That gentleman is one of the shareholders of this establishment. »

« This 'no credit' rule was personally set by him. »

With a livid face, the Raikage turned around and whispered instructions to Tutai behind him :

« I have two hundred million taels here. Hurry and pool together the rest from the brothers ; we must make up the remaining one hundred million. »

« Once we’re back at the village, I’ll pay them back with interest. »

Asakawa Shin’s side was naturally unaware of these minor interludes. Once the Raikage left, the banquet hall immediately returned to its noisy state.

Tsunade was the first to lose her patience, rushing to Asakawa Shin in a few steps and shouting excitedly :

« Junior disciple, what you said just now was so cool ! You scared those Kumogakure Ninjas' faces green. »

Shin didn’t respond, merely giving a reserved smile.

Yūgao was even more excited, jumping up and down :

« Lord Shin, you intimidated that hulking Raikage with just a glance and even knocked out a whole bunch of lurkers. »

« It really gave me a fright. I had no idea so many people were hiding in this hall ! »

Itachi stepped forward, and after a careful inspection, replied coldly : « It was a pre-prepared secret passage. They certainly went to a lot of trouble. »

« Unfortunately, in the face of Lord Shin’s formidable strength, these methods were nothing but futile. »

The meal soon drew to a close, and the originally cheerful atmosphere gradually cooled.

Asakawa Shin finally couldn’t help but be the first to break the silence : « All good things must come to an end. Senior sister, we’re setting off to return tomorrow. »

« Are you... not coming back to Konoha with us ? »

Tsunade’s eyes flickered slightly as she turned her head away, pretending to be nonchalant :

« I still have some matters to attend to in the Ninja World, so I won’t be going back with you. »

Asakawa Shin nodded, glancing at Shizune out of the corner of his eye, and said solemnly to Tsunade :

« Then I’ll wait for you in Konoha. Once you’re done with your business, come back as soon as possible. »

Tsunade turned back to gaze at him, a hint of hesitation flashing in her eyes, before she finally nodded slowly.

« Yeah. It’s a promise. »
