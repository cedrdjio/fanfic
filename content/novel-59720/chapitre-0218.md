# Chapitre 218 — Convening the Five Kage Summit

« Lord Raikage, something terrible has happened ! The Seven-Tails Jinchuriki of Takigakure is confirmed missing ! »

« What ? ! »

Killer Bee paled in shock, suddenly springing up from his office chair as a chill ran down his spine to his scalp.

He quickly ran through the list in his mind ; at this rate of harvesting, the next targets would likely be himself and the Eight-Tails.

Recalling the eerie image of the spiral-Masked Man from the intelligence scroll, an unprecedented gravity appeared on Killer Bee’s face.

He paced back and forth restlessly in the cramped office, the Eight-Tails' Chakra churning uneasily within him as if sensing a crisis.

Beside him, Dodai pondered for a moment before looking up and suggesting solemnly, « Lord Raikage, the situation has exceeded the scope of what Kumogakure can handle alone. The Akatsuki Organization’s strength is far beyond expectations ; our only hope now lies in joining forces with outside powers. »

Killer Bee stopped in his tracks, his brow furrowed. « You mean... joining with Konoha ? »

Dodai nodded, his gaze sharp. « Exactly. The Nine-Tails is also on the Akatsuki’s hunting list ; Konoha has no reason to refuse. »

« Furthermore, not just Konoha—the Hidden Mist, Sunagakure, and Iwagakure, which have also lost their Tailed Beasts, are potential allies. »

« After all, reclaiming the Tailed Beasts is a shared objective. »

« You mean... convening a Five Kage Summit ? »

Killer Bee stroked the stubble on his chin, his gaze gradually turning resolute. « It seems we have no choice. »

He paused and added, « As far as I know, Konoha’s Nine-Tails Jinchuriki, Uzumaki Naruto, has an extraordinary bond with that Asakawa Shin ! »

« If we can draw them into the fray, we might stand a chance even in a head-on clash with the Akatsuki ! »

« Lord Raikage is wise ! »

Once the order was given, the Kumogakure Ninja moved swiftly.

Within just a few days, news that the five great hidden Villages would convene a Five Kage Summit in the Land of Iron spread through the Ninja World like wildfire, instantly ratcheting the already tense situation to its breaking point.

The other hidden Villages that received the invitation had varying reactions, but they all ultimately agreed to the request.

Anyone could feel that a massive storm was brewing, one that would sweep across the entire Ninja World.

Konoha Village, inside the Hokage Office.

Tsunade sat heroically on the edge of the wide windowsill with her arms crossed. « A Five Kage Summit ? Shin, what do you think ? »

« Huh ? Senior Sister, I’ll just sit and watch. »

Asakawa Shin was lazily slumped in the throne that symbolized the Hokage’s authority, enjoying a head-cushion massage provided by Yugito, looking somewhat dazed.

« Hey, hey, don’t joke around about something this serious. »

Jiraiya stood below them, filled with envy and jealousy, and spoke up indignantly with a suggestion :

« Actually, I’ve recently found clues regarding the Akatsuki’s suspected lair. Why don’t I personally infiltrate and investigate ? »

Asakawa Shin’s body jolted, causing Yugito, who was massaging him, to let out a muffled groan of discomfort.

'Is Jiraiya about to go and throw his life away ?'

He muttered to himself, « With Nagato’s current strength, he could probably finish off Jiraiya in just a few moves ; he wouldn’t even give him the chance to activate Sage Mode. »

« Moreover, Nagato has been completely brainwashed ; he won’t show any mercy to his former mentor. »

Thinking of this, he immediately refused categorically, « Forget it, Senior Brother. The Akatsuki’s waters are too deep ; you won’t be able to handle it. »

Jiraiya felt a bit embarrassed, but his innate confidence made him feel that merely gathering intelligence would be a simple matter.

He secretly decided that he must sneak into the Akatsuki base, uncover all the intel, and then amaze everyone.

Seeing that Jiraiya didn’t argue further, Asakawa Shin nodded, taking it as agreement with his suggestion not to act recklessly.

Asakawa Shin’s eyes darted around as he turned his gaze toward Tsunade on the windowsill. « Senior Sister, how about Naruto and I act as your bodyguards for the Five Kage Summit ? I guarantee we’ll take good care of you. »

Tsunade’s brow furrowed slightly, a hint of hesitation flashing in her eyes.

Could that airheaded Naruto handle the task ?

However, when her gaze inadvertently swept over Asakawa Shin, all her concerns vanished instantly, and she readily nodded in agreement.

It didn’t matter if Naruto was unreliable ; didn’t she still have her good Junior Brother ? It would definitely be fine !

After finalizing all travel arrangements, the high-level officials immediately adjourned.

Just after they parted, Asakawa Shin transformed into the likeness of Uchiha Madara and stood proudly before Nagato.

« I have received definite intelligence that both the Eight-Tails and Nine-Tails will appear at the Five Kage Summit. Nagato, the time for you to strike has come. You won’t get cold feet, will you ? »

Nagato’s figure paused slightly, his tone tinged with a hint of disbelief. « Is this true ? »

« As real as it gets. »

« Good. »

A flicker of determination flashed in Nagato’s eyes. « Then I shall go personally and capture them all in one fell swoop. »

« Oh ? »

Asakawa Shin feigned surprise. « The Five Kage will be gathered there ; you have such confidence ? Do you need to assemble the organization members to act together ? »

« No need. »

Nagato refused flatly. « My techniques have immense destructive power ; if they are present, they will only get in the way. »

Asakawa Shin was secretly surprised. 'Is this the real reason why the Akatsuki never fights as a team ?'

« Then I shall wait and see. »

With that, his figure flickered and vanished into a streak of light.

Konan looked at the empty spot, asking with a grave expression, « Nagato, is it really okay ? Facing the Five Kage alone... I’m worried about you. »

« Don’t worry. This was the original intention behind founding the Akatsuki, wasn’t it ? »

Nagato’s gaze was firm and deep, as if piercing through time and space. « Since we’ve reached the final moment, I will naturally give my all to fulfill our long-cherished wish. »

« Yahiko, your dream is finally within reach. Can you see it ? »

« One more month. Just one month left ! »
