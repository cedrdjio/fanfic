# Chapitre 94 — The Two-Tails is Still Attacking

Facing such a terrifying attack, many Hidden Cloud Ninjas couldn’t help but show despair. Their weapons scattered across the ground, having already lost their fighting spirit.

Seeing this, Yugito couldn’t help but curse inwardly. But as the commander, how could she give up ? She took a deep breath, resolutely stepped forward, and shouted words of encouragement to her companions :

« Everyone, don’t panic ! We have numbers on our side. As long as we stand united, we can surely... »

However, before she could finish, a shadow loomed over, completely blotting out the daylight. It was Asakawa Shin, who, ignoring any sense of martial ethics, launched an attack directly.

« What are you babbling about over there ? There’s no time to wait for you. Didn’t I say it just now ? I have urgent business with you. »

Yugito’s pupils shrank suddenly as she looked up. She saw the sky-blotting water dragon, carrying an unstoppable momentum, charging ruthlessly towards them. In the blink of an eye, it was already upon them.

« Quickly, set up a defense ! »

She only had time to issue this command before being hit head-on by the roaring water dragon, instantly swept into the surging torrent.

The other Ninjas fared even worse. Even those who managed to barely put up a defense were vulnerable before a Ninjutsu of this scale. They were instantly crushed and swallowed by the violent currents, swept away to distant lands by the vast flood.

In an instant, the dense crowd of the support force that had once pressured Asakawa Shin was completely swept away by the violent water dragon, their fates unknown.

Asakawa Shin then lightly patted his chest and let out a long sigh of relief : « Phew... That’s better. Just now, they were all glaring at me so fiercely, as if they wanted to skin me alive. It was really terrifying. Now it’s finally much quieter~ »

A full ten minutes passed before the surging waters of the Valley of Clouds and Lightning gradually calmed down.

Asakawa Shin gave a lazy yawn, tapped the water’s surface with his toes, and leisurely strolled towards the location of the Two-Tails.

At this moment, Yugito’s mood was the exact opposite, having already plummeted into the abyss of despair, with no room left to sink further—probably.

She looked around. Although the water had calmed down, only a few dozen Ninjas remained by her side. Most of them were those she had barely managed to save using the Two-Tails' Chakra when they were attacked.

The fate of those who hadn’t gathered yet could already be imagined.

Thinking of this, a trace of grief appeared in her eyes, but it was quickly replaced by a look of determination. She immediately ordered :

« Everyone, listen up ! As your commander, I announce that we are abandoning this mission ! »

« The enemy’s strength is no longer something that can be overcome by numerical advantage. I hereby order all of you to evacuate back to the Hidden Cloud Village immediately ! Do not delay ! »

Hearing this, the faces of the surviving Ninjas changed. One Ninja couldn’t help but blurt out, « Lady Yugito, what about you ? »

Yugito slowly shook her head, her voice low : « The enemy’s target seems to be me. I can’t leave, and I can’t escape. While he hasn’t arrived yet, evacuate immediately ! »

« But... »

« No buts ! Leave at once and take the intelligence back. » Yugito interrupted sharply, her gaze fixed on the distance, the hairs on her body standing on end.

The other Ninjas were then jolted awake and followed Yugito’s gaze.

Where the water met the sky, a figure was walking across the waves. His steps were composed, even somewhat elegant, yet he carried a sense of pressure that made them feel suffocated with fear.

« Damn it, that monster is coming ! I’ll hold him back. You guys, go ! »

Without another word, Yugito directly entered her Tailed Beast transformation. Amidst the violent surge of Chakra, a giant blue cat appeared on the vast waters.

« Lady Yugito, please take care ! We will definitely bring back the intelligence on this Masked Man ! » After saying that, the dozens of Ninjas looked at each other and fled in all directions without looking back.

Asakawa Shin sensed that there were still dozens of survivors but didn’t care. They were just insignificant small fry. As long as the target of this trip, the Two-Tails, was still here, it was enough.

As for the intelligence on the Masked Man ? It seemed fine to let them take it back. What did the intelligence on the Masked Man have to do with him, Asakawa Shin ?

The big cat wasn’t as relaxed as he was. She was still staring intently at the figure in the distance, her pupils fully dilated, her body lowered, and her rear wiggling—she had entered a state of full concentration for hunting.

« Oh ? It really is a cat, huh ? Haha, unfortunately, I’m not a little mouse. »

Asakawa Shin watched with interest. He had just raised his hand to say hello when the big cat opposite him instantly reacted.

With a roar, her figure darted out like lightning, rapidly closing in on Asakawa Shin. She leapt into the air and delivered a fierce « Cat Punch, » her claws gleaming coldly as they struck towards him.

Asakawa Shin nimbly leapt backward, just barely avoiding the strike. The Two-Tails' claws slammed into the water, kicking up a large splash that obscured Asakawa Shin’s vision.

Before the water curtain could fall, a massive ball of high-temperature fire burst out from the water, heading straight for Asakawa Shin.

« Water Release : Water Formation Wall ! »

Asakawa Shin calmly formed hand signs, and a water wall dozens of meters high rose abruptly from the ground, blocking the fireball.

The intense heat evaporated a large amount of water, exploding into a sky full of steam, instantly blocking all visibility.

At the same time, the Two-Tails had already silently circled behind Asakawa Shin, acting as a cold predator, launching a sharp and efficient pounce on its prey.

The power of this strike was so astonishing that under the burst of powerful biting force, it actually bit Asakawa Shin in half at the waist.

'Great, I got him !'

Just as this thought flashed through her mind, the corpse turned into two pieces of wood with a « poof. »

It turned out this was just the Body Replacement Technique. Was it a Cat Strip used as bait ?

'Oh... no !'

She instinctively tensed her muscles, standing ready to meet the opponent’s violent counterattack. However, as time passed second by second, the surroundings remained deathly silent.

« Huh ? Where is he ? »

She whispered softly while looking around warily, but she couldn’t even find a shadow of the enemy until she inadvertently lowered her gaze to look beneath the water’s surface...

There, she saw Asakawa Shin leisurely floating in the water, looking up at the giant cat, his eyes full of the satisfaction of having succeeded :

'Ohhh ! As expected, there really are Paw Pads ! It’s exactly like a cat. I want to raise it !'

Thus, the Two-Tails' gaze met Asakawa Shin’s from underwater. She was startled. Had this bastard been staying underwater all this time, peeking at her ? What on earth did he want to do ?

In her alarm, she quickly took several leaps backward to distance herself.

Seeing there were no more Paw Pads to look at, Asakawa Shin emerged from the water with a lack of interest. However, a sharp light soon flashed in his eyes, and he asked the Two-Tails in a very serious tone :

« Um... is the fur on your body real fire ? What’s the temperature ? Will it burn someone if they touch it ? »

The Two-Tails stared at him with full alertness, secretly speculating : 'What does this person want to do ? Is he trying to fish for information ? What a cunning guy ! I won’t tell him.'

Seeing that the Two-Tails ignored him, Asakawa Shin didn’t mind. After all, cats are proud by nature ; most of the time, the Poop Scooper needs to be more proactive.

So, he asked again, « Why aren’t you talking, big kitty ? Don’t be nervous. Actually, I have no ill intentions... »

« Hmm... although it’s a bit presumptuous, can you let me huff you ? I actually really like you. How about you become my cat ? »

A trace of shock appeared on the Two-Tails' cat face in a human-like way, which then turned into disgust, and finally, her entire face darkened.

She roared angrily at Asakawa Shin :

« Are you kidding me ? ! How dare you insult me like this. You really are a pervert, aren’t you ? »

« Perverts should just go die quietly for me. »

« Take this, Tailed Beast Ball ! »
