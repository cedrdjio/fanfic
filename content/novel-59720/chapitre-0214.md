# Chapitre 214 — Internal Recommendation

Ōnoki’s eyes flickered slightly, and he gave Han a subtle look.

It was a command for him to lead the charge.

Even though Han felt extremely disgruntled, he understood that this was the most logical tactic at the moment.

As the Five-Tails Jinchuriki, he was tough and resilient, and since he practiced fierce Taijutsu, he was the perfect choice to act as 'bait.'

He took a deep breath, and the Chakra inside his body surged like boiling water, instantly transforming into Boil Release Chakra.

« Boil Release : Unrivaled Strength ! »

« Woo— Woo— »

Accompanied by a low roar like a train whistle, the special red armor on Han’s body sprayed out a large amount of white steam. Carrying a momentum of ten thousand tons, he rushed straight toward Asakawa Shin with incredible speed.

However, a playful arc curled at the corner of Asakawa Shin’s mouth.

He whispered softly, « I’ve been waiting for this moment... for several years now. »

Before his voice had even faded, he suddenly reached out his palm.

An invisible and intangible Psychokinesis surged out like a tide, condensing into several 'plugs' in the air, tightly sealing all the steam vents on Han’s armor.

Even plugs crafted by the finest artisans might not have been able to withstand the impact of the internal high-pressure steam, but the Psychokinesis was like a sticky plaster, firmly adhering to the vents without budging an inch.

Han, who was originally charging at high speed, suddenly came to a halt. The interior of his armor instantly became like a pressure cooker and began to deform rapidly, emitting a strange, tooth-grating hum.

« Boom— ! »

With a loud bang, the armor exploded violently.

Han let out a scream, severely wounded by the power of the explosion from the heavy armor right against his body. He crashed to the ground like a broken kite, twitched twice, and then went still.

« Nani ? »

Ōnoki’s pupils shrank suddenly, his eyes nearly bulging out of his head.

The dignified Five-Tails Jinchuriki, the Steam Ninja famous throughout the Ninja World, was actually defeated in a single encounter ?

He fainted before he could even perform his Tailed Beast Transformation ; it was a complete disgrace to Iwagakure’s reputation !

His original plan had been calculated perfectly : let Han pin down the enemy while he took the opportunity to perform that invincible Kekkei Tota—Dust Release—to settle the battle in one stroke.

But who could have imagined that this Han was actually a useless show-off who didn’t even give him a chance to use his skill !

Before Ōnoki could recover from his shock, Asakawa Shin had already used his Psychokinesis to grab the unconscious Han and take to the air, clearly intending to retreat.

« Where do you think you’re going ! »

Ōnoki was furious and was about to take flight to pursue when a shrill cry for help suddenly came from behind him.

« Grandpa, save me ! »

Just now, taking advantage of the moment they were distracted, a figure also wearing a swirl mask suddenly appeared behind Kurotsuchi, grabbed her, and rapidly retreated in the exact opposite direction from Han.

« Kurotsuchi ! »

Ōnoki’s mind was in a mess, his gaze shifting frantically between the two directions.

After a moment of dead silence, a flash of determination crossed his eyes. He pivoted and lunged toward the direction where Han had disappeared. « Kitsuchi, you go after Kurotsuchi ! We’ll split up ! »

However, Kitsuchi was after all just flesh and blood. Even if he exerted his full strength to burst with speed, how could he catch up with an enemy flying at high speed in the sky ?

In just a moment, that figure completely disappeared into the horizon, leaving him alone on the ground to sigh in helplessness.

On the other hand, Ōnoki relied on his 'Earth Release : Ultralight-Weight Rock Technique' to stay right on Asakawa Shin’s tail in the air, actually managing not to lose him.

« Dammit... what kind of Ninjutsu is this kid using ? To be able to move so freely and flexibly in the air, it’s even more natural than my Iwagakure’s secret Light-Weight Rock Technique ! »

Seeing the distance between them grow further and further, a ruthless cold light suddenly flashed in Ōnoki’s clouded eyes.

His hands quickly formed that seal in front of his chest that made countless experts tremble with fear, locking onto the two people ahead from a distance, actually intending to take them down with him.

« Sorry, Han. If the Five-Tails dies, it can be captured again, but if it’s obtained by the enemy, that will be trouble. »

A sliver of shimmering white light gathered between his palms, then transformed into a white pillar of light that tore through the sky, shining into the distance.

« Dust Release : Detachment of the Primitive World Technique ! »

Asakawa Shin sensed the deadly threat coming from behind and sighed inwardly that this old fellow was indeed ruthless, showing no mercy even to his own capable subordinate.

Truly worthy of being an older generation Ninja.

« It’s just a pity... »

He blocked the empty space behind him with his right hand, and the unique Dojutsu of the Rinnegan instantly activated.

« Preta Path : Blocking Technique Absorption Seal ! »

An invisible shield expanded out of thin air, meeting that white pillar of light capable of decomposing all things into atoms.

The moment the Dust Release touched the shield, it seemed to fall into a bottomless vortex. The originally violent energy began to twist and rotate, and was finally completely swallowed up.

Taking advantage of the brief opening when the Dust Release was neutralized, Asakawa Shin’s mouth curled slightly, and he directly activated the Flying Thunder God Technique to take Han away from the area.

A ripple appeared in space, and the figures of the two instantly disappeared from the spot. Without the obstruction of the Absorption Seal, the Dust Release light pillar had no further hindrance and projected into the distant sky.

Ōnoki only felt a flash of white light before his eyes. When the light dissipated, the front was already empty, and not even a speck of dust had fallen.

He stared at that void and snorted coldly, « To be able to block Dust Release for a moment, you have enough to be proud of. No wonder you could beat those Kumogakure barbarians into a disorganized mess. »

As his voice fell, a trace of unavoidable loneliness crossed his brow.

For the future of Iwagakure, sacrificing a Han was a helpless but necessary choice.

He sighed slightly, and his figure seemed to age several years in this moment. With a hunched back, he slowly turned around and set off on his return journey.

On the other side, Asakawa Shin’s original body was currently speeding through the high altitude with Kurotsuchi.

An invisible Psychokinesis was suppressing Kurotsuchi like iron pincers, making her unable to move.

She could only stare with those lively eyes, using a fierce and defiant gaze to glare at the back in front of her, trying to express her protest this way.

« I’ll let you go, but you’re not allowed to scream, let alone move around. Blink if you understand, » Asakawa Shin said without turning his head.

Hearing this, Kurotsuchi hurriedly blinked her eyes desperately. Her well-behaved and submissive appearance was a complete contrast to her previous shrewishness.

Asakawa Shin nodded slightly and then withdrew the Psychokinesis that was binding her tongue and legs.

« You bastard, why did you grab me ? I’m telling you, my grandpa is the Tsuchikage... »

Regaining her freedom, Kurotsuchi flailed her legs in the air. Just as she was about to lash out, she immediately remembered her situation, and her voice involuntarily dropped an octave. « That... you’d better let me go immediately, or else... »

« Stop, stop, stop. »

Asakawa Shin waved his hand impatiently. « Didn’t you say you wanted to join the Akatsuki Organization ? Or were you playing me ? If that’s the case, then what’s the use of keeping you ? I might as well just kill you. »

As he spoke, a sliver of Chakra slightly condensed in his palm, as if he were about to ruthlessly destroy the flower in the next moment.

« Don’t, don’t, don’t ! Big brother, big brother ! »

Kurotsuchi was so scared her face paled, and tears were swirling in her eyes. She no longer looked like a strong woman of Iwagakure. « I wasn’t lying to you ! I really want to join the Akatsuki Organization ! And I have an internal recommendation ! »

« Oh ? An internal recommendation ? » Asakawa Shin raised an eyebrow.

« Yes, yes, yes ! »

Kurotsuchi nodded repeatedly as if she had grabbed onto a life-saving straw. « My big brother is Deidara. He’s the one who recommended me ! If you don’t believe me, ask him later. Our relationship is super solid ! »
