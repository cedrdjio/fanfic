# Chapitre 126 — Ice Release

« Haku... that is your name, right ? »

Asakawa Shin had subdued Haku and was in an excellent mood. He even showed mercy by personally healing Zabuza before telling him to get lost.

Of course, that is if we ignore the fact that he accidentally left a Flying Thunder God Mark on Zabuza’s heart during the healing process...

However, he did this to prevent any medical disputes, so it was quite reasonable...

Haku nodded respectfully in response : « Yes, Lord Shin, my name is Haku. »

Asakawa Shin waved his hand nonchalantly, signaling that Haku didn’t need to be so formal : « Introduce yourself first. Also, from now on, you don’t need to wear that mask anymore. »

« Yes, Lord Shin. »

Haku obediently took off the mask. To everyone’s surprise, beneath the mask was an exceptionally gentle and beautiful face.

Naruto widened his eyes and exclaimed : « So you’re actually a girl ? »

« So pretty ! I think I understand now. » Sasuke cast a thoughtful glance at Asakawa Shin. « I knew it. Shin has had ulterior motives from the start. »

Kakashi nodded in agreement : « I understand too. You said you wanted to take Zabuza as a dog, but you were actually after Haku, right ? I’m impressed, how shameless ! »

Asakawa Shin’s face darkened, and he quickly retorted : « What nonsense are you spouting ? Haku is a guy ! Besides, what kind of image do I have in your minds ? »

« What image ? Isn’t it the guy who picks up people everywhere... Wait, Haku is so pretty, and you’re saying he’s a boy ? »

Asakawa Shin straightened his back and asked righteously : « So what ? Isn’t that perfectly normal ? »

« Hiss... I guess you’re right. »

Seeing that everyone had finally quieted down, Asakawa Shin nodded slightly and signaled for Haku to start his self-introduction.

Haku coughed lightly and said solemnly : « My name is Haku, and I am from the Land of Water. I was picked up by Lord Zabuza while I was wandering the streets, and he taught me about being a Ninja. Since then, I have served him as Lord Zabuza’s tool, and I specialize in using Ice Release Ninjutsu... »

« Ice Release ? That’s a Bloodline Limit, right ? Are you a descendant of the Yuki clan ? »

Kakashi couldn’t help but exclaim, muttering to himself : 'Shin’s luck in picking up people is just too heaven-defying. He can just casually run into a Ninja with a Bloodline Limit, and it’s the extinct Yuki clan, just like Karin and her mother last time...'

« I don’t know anything about the Yuki clan. I don’t have a surname ; I’ve been called Haku since I was born. »

Asakawa Shin’s focus was elsewhere : « Zabuza’s tool ? Heh, he really struck gold. If it weren’t for you, he would have been dead long ago ! Listen well, Haku, you don’t owe him anything anymore. From now on, you only need to live for yourself, and for me. »

Haku was silent for a moment before nodding in agreement.

Asakawa Shin patted Haku on the shoulder and said casually : « Oh, and don’t take terms like 'tool' or 'dog' to heart. I was just saying that on a whim. As for the details, just learn from Karin. When we return to Konoha, I’ll buy you an apartment to settle in. From now on, you are my subordinate. »

Haku and Karin looked at each other and nodded thoughtfully.

...

Chatting along the way, the group finally arrived at Tazuna’s home before dark.

« Since the bridge isn’t finished yet, I’m afraid we’ll have to stay here for a while. » Asakawa Shin scanned the room and announced seriously : « Get some rest early tonight. Starting tomorrow, the hellish special training will officially begin. Frankly speaking, I am very dissatisfied with your performance today. »

Hearing this, the four of them looked at each other. Instead of fear, their eyes burned with fighting spirit. They nodded heavily and turned to head upstairs.

...

The next morning, everyone gathered in the depths of the forest, full of energy. The training was split into three groups : Kakashi took Naruto and Sasuke to train, Tsunade took Karin and Sakura to train, and Asakawa Shin took Haku to train separately.

« Haku, condense a wisp of Ice Release Chakra for me. I want to study it. » Asakawa Shin said straightforwardly.

Although Haku was a bit confused, he thought Lord Shin must have his reasons, so he obediently followed instructions.

Asakawa Shin closed his eyes and focused, carefully sensing the Ice Release Chakra within his body. Ice Release is a Bloodline Limit formed by fusing Wind and Water Chakra.

Asakawa Shin’s goal this time was to use Haku’s Ice Release Chakra as a guide to see if he could analyze and synthesize the Ice Release Bloodline Limit himself.

Theoretically, with his near-peak Chakra control and his special constitution that was evolving toward a Bloodline Net, it was entirely possible to achieve this ; it would just require some hard work.

And this was exactly what Asakawa Shin was best at.

He quickly formed hand signs, and in an instant, dozens of Shadow Clones appeared out of thin air, immediately diving into complex training.

Seeing the Shadow Clones all busy at work, his main body didn’t idle either ; he walked straight into a remote part of the forest.

He casually found a thick tree trunk, sat cross-legged, and began to absorb the surrounding Natural Energy.

About ten minutes later, he opened his eyes, a trace of helplessness flashing in them. The reason was simple : the Natural Energy within a radius of dozens of miles had been almost completely drained by him.

Not only that, he stared at the forest that had clearly become withered and listless, lost in thought.

'The progress is too slow. At this rate, who knows when I’ll be able to advance to a Bloodline Net and achieve a qualitative change. And if I keep absorbing it so recklessly, given the nature of the Divine Tree, I might just destroy the Ninja World. It seems I’ll have to check out those so-called sacred places ; after all, they have a lot of accumulated Natural Energy... It’s a pity, if I could go to other planets to plant trees, that would be even better.'

...

Time passed quietly, alternating between training and missions, and in the blink of an eye, a week had passed.

« Senior Sister, drinking heartily in the middle of the day ? Come here, I have something good to show you. » Asakawa Shin found Tsunade, who was drinking heavily, and said mysteriously.

Tsunade glanced at him, but she was polite enough to lean over : « Yo, what’s up ? Kid, do you want to have a couple of drinks with your Senior Sister ? »

Before she finished speaking, her expression turned serious. She leaned close to Asakawa Shin’s ear and whispered : « That kid named Haku, I’ve been observing him for a long time. Although I haven’t found any suspicious moves, I advise you to be careful... »

Upon hearing this, Asakawa Shin exaggeratedly looked her up and down. Only when she started to feel uncomfortable did he give her a thumbs up and offer some flattery : « As expected of Senior Sister Tsunade, your mind is so meticulous. So, your drunken appearance these past few days was all just an act to deceive people~ »

« Ha... haha, that’s natural. » Tsunade gave two dry laughs, a faint blush appearing on her cheeks, and quickly waved her hand to change the subject : « Alright, alright, enough of that. Didn’t you say you had something fun ? Hurry up and show it to me. »

« Then watch closely~ » Asakawa Shin was in high spirits. He extended his right index finger and slowly tapped it on Tsunade’s wine glass.

Just as Tsunade was about to ask, she saw the amber wine in the glass start to emit a wisp of cold mist. In the blink of an eye, a few pieces of crushed ice condensed and floated quietly in the wine.

Looking at this incredible sight, Tsunade couldn’t help but widen her eyes.

Asakawa Shin proudly puffed out his chest, wearing an expression that said, « Hurry up and praise me. »

« Senior Sister, drinking in this hot weather requires ice to be truly refreshing ! How about it ? Senior Sister, I’m good to you, right ? »

« This... is this Ice Release ? How did you do that, kid ? »

« Hmm... is it hard ? Isn’t it just a matter of having hands ? »
