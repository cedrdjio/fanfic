# Chapitre 112 — A Mistake

Asakawa Shin sped along and finally stopped on an open expanse of the sea.

He didn’t have to wait long. Accompanied by the sound of rushing wind, dozens of Hidden Mist Anbu surrounded him in an instant.

The crowd quickly parted to make a path, and Yagura walked out slowly with a powerful aura. Without saying much, he simply waved his hand coldly and ordered, « Kill him. »

Asakawa Shin looked at the man in front of him with interest, a wave of emotion rising in his heart :

'So this is Yagura ? He could be called the most miserable Kage in history. During his reign, he was controlled as a puppet for years, and even after death, he was pulled out to be used again. Truly an old unlucky fellow.'

The surrounding Hidden Mist Ninja didn’t bother with his inner thoughts. Upon hearing the Mizukage’s order, a bloodthirsty light flashed in their eyes, and they roared as they rushed toward him.

Asakawa Shin didn’t even blink, shouting in a low voice : « Boil Release : Unrivaled Strength ! »

In an instant, a large amount of scorching steam erupted from his body. His Chakra was instantly pushed to its boiling point, and his total physical strength surged. He turned into an afterimage and charged fiercely at the incoming Ninjas.

« So fast ! »

The Hidden Mist Anbu only felt a blur before their eyes, followed by the feeling of being hit head-on by a speeding train. Their bodies flew backward uncontrollably, and they were already dead by the time they hit the water.

In the blink of an eye, several Ninjas were taken out by Shin in a single charge.

At this moment, a Ninja let out a terrified scream :

« This Ninjutsu... I remember now ! This guy is the Five-Tails Jinchuriki from Iwagakure, the one known as the 'Steam Ninja,' Han !

Everyone be careful, his Boil Release is extremely powerful in both strength and speed.

But ! His Boil Release has a fatal weakness. Everyone, use Ninjutsu to block his steam vents ! »

« Nani ? »

Asakawa Shin was also startled when he heard this. He hadn’t expected this Hidden Mist Ninja to think of such a move.

He subconsciously looked back at his own venting steam ports, thinking to himself that it might actually be feasible.

However, he quickly relaxed, thinking : 'It’s only possible in theory. With my extreme speed, how could I be blocked so easily ?'

Thinking of this, he ignored the warning and charged at the Ninjas at high speed, knocking them over in an instant. It could only be said that while their idea was good, they were powerless to implement it against such an absolute gap in strength.

« Lord Mizukage, please take action ! We are no match for him ! »

« Yes, Lord Mizukage, ah— ! »

« Lord Mizukage, why are you... »

Yagura remained standing there with a calm expression, as if the massacre happening before him had nothing to do with him.

After a short while, no one else was left standing on the sea except Asakawa Shin and Yagura.

Asakawa Shin clapped his hands leisurely and asked Yagura, « Aren’t they your subordinates ? Why did you just watch me slaughter them without doing anything ? »

Hearing this, a flash of struggle that was hard to hide crossed Yagura’s eyes.

But soon, that light dimmed again. His gaze became hollow and lifeless once more. He stared intently at Asakawa Shin and barked, « Kill you ! »

As soon as he finished speaking, he rushed forward fiercely, holding that strange club shaped like a giant embroidery needle.

However, how could such an attack pose a threat to Asakawa Shin ?

Asakawa Shin moved nimbly, dodging easily.

At the same time, while parrying the moves, he activated his Observation Haki to perceive the surroundings with all his might, trying to find Obito’s trace.

But aside from Mei Terumī, he didn’t find any clues.

The sea was so open that any hiding place would have to be underwater, but Asakawa Shin searched carefully and still found nothing.

Was it that Observation Haki couldn’t perceive traces of another dimension, or was the opponent simply not here ?

Forget it, none of that was important right now. The purpose of this trip was the Three-Tails' Chakra ; everything else could wait.

Having reached this conclusion, Asakawa Shin stopped purely defending. He decisively caught a clear opening in Yagura’s attack and landed a heavy punch on his abdomen.

Accompanied by a burst of steam, the force of the Unrivaled Strength punch poured out instantly, sending Yagura flying into the air. When he fell heavily back down, he was already unconscious.

'Is he really just like an NPC ?'

Having defeated Yagura so easily, a sense of sorrow welled up in Asakawa Shin’s heart.

A Kage of a village and a perfect Three-Tails Jinchuriki had ended up in such a miserable state. This Ninja World was truly cruel.

He walked forward slowly and picked up the unconscious Yagura with one hand, preparing to extract the Three-Tails' Chakra.

Just then, a playful smile curled at the corner of Asakawa Shin’s mouth.

He glanced slightly to the side and saw that the sea behind him had become turbulent at some point. A huge water dragon was baring its fangs and claws as it tore through the air toward him.

Asakawa Shin retreated rapidly to avoid the sudden attack. He then held up Yagura and shook him slightly, a playful look in his eyes as he asked :

« Isn’t this man your Mizukage ? But the way you acted just now, it looked like you wanted to kill him along with me. »

Seeing her attack miss, Mei Terumī stopped hiding. She walked out gracefully with her hands behind her back, a beautiful and moving smile on her lips :

« Don’t talk nonsense, young man. How could I attack Lord Mizukage ? I just wanted to protect him. »

Before her voice finished, she switched to a serious face instantly and launched another surprise attack without warning, spitting out a huge water ball with the power of a meteorite : « Water Release : Water Formation Pillar ! »

Asakawa Shin’s eyes twitched imperceptibly. He turned into an afterimage and calmly dodged again, not forgetting to tease her :

« Your real target is clearly this Mizukage, isn’t it ? Yet you keep saying you want to protect him.

As expected, the more beautiful a woman is, the better she is at lying~ »

Mei Terumī looked frustrated. This move had failed too. How was this damned Yagura so lucky ?

However, after hearing « Han’s » words, she covered her mouth and chuckled, her eyes sparkling : « Then I’ll take that as a compliment.

But, could you please return our Lord Mizukage to me ? He is a very important person to me. »

After saying this, she added silently in her heart : 'Yagura, your absence is truly important to me, so could you please just die quietly ?'

With this thought, she quickly calculated a new tactic in her mind, determined to take down the troublesome enemy in front of her no matter what.

To her surprise, Asakawa Shin’s answer was completely unexpected. He actually agreed to her request quite readily :

« Sure, no problem. But you’ll have to wait for me to draw a few vials of his blood first. Once I’m done, I’ll return him to you exactly as he is. How about it ?

Since we’re both Boil Release Bloodline Limit users, trust me once, okay ? »

Mei Terumī clearly hadn’t expected him to answer like that. Her eyes flickered as she seemed to weigh the pros and cons.

A moment later, she made a « please » gesture to Asakawa Shin and actually walked to the side to wait patiently.

By now, Asakawa Shin had finished extracting the Three-Tails' Chakra. He took out a sealing scroll, retrieved several syringes, and directly drew Yagura’s blood to seal it away.

Then, he actually kept his promise and tossed Yagura toward Mei Terumī from a distance.

Mei Terumī quickly caught him. After confirming the person in her hands was indeed Yagura, a look of surprise and joy appeared on her face.

But she quickly calmed down, a flash of ruthlessness and determination in her eyes. She threw Yagura high into the air and quickly formed hand seals : « Boil Release : Skilled Mist Technique ! »

In an instant, a cloud of white acidic gas erupted from her mouth toward Yagura. Yagura met his end while still unconscious ; his body melted into a pool of bloody water in an instant, vanishing without a trace.

Asakawa Shin feigned a look of shock and dismay, exclaiming : « You... why did you kill him ? »

Mei Terumī snorted in disdain and sneered : « What use is there in keeping such a Mizukage ? His various absurd actions during his reign have already pushed the Hidden Mist Village to the brink of collapse. My killing him is ridding the people of a disaster. »

Asakawa Shin quickly waved his hands to explain : « Actually, you can’t entirely blame him. He was under a Genjutsu, after all. Everything he did was controlled by someone else.

Couldn’t you have just released the Genjutsu ? Look at you, you didn’t even want the Three-Tails ; you just had to kill him. »

« Gen... Genjutsu ? You’re saying he was under a Genjutsu ? How is that possible ? He was a perfect Jinchuriki... »

Before she could finish, Mei Terumī remembered Ao contacting her this morning, saying he had important information about the Mizukage to tell her in person.

Then she thought of Ao’s Byakugan, which could see through everything, as well as Yagura’s completely different behavior and all the anomalies of the past few years...

Thinking of this, she couldn’t help but tremble all over, murmuring under her breath : « Crap ! Could... could it be ? Did I really get it wrong ? »

« No... that’s not right. You did this on purpose, didn’t you ? »

Mei Terumī quickly reacted, glaring at Asakawa Shin with a murderous look in her eyes, her tone icy :

« Hmph ! Yagura died in battle on the front lines against an Iwagakure Ninja. What does that have to do with me, Mei Terumī ?

As long as I take you down today, then everything will be perfect... »

Asakawa Shin’s eyes twitched. He ignored her completely, activated his steam burst, and turned tail to run. Only one sentence echoed back : « You’re a moron~ »

With Mei Terumī's speed, she couldn’t catch up to him at all. She couldn’t help but roar in frustration behind him : « Han ! If you have the guts, don’t run ! Come and fight me for three hundred rounds ! Han ! Han ! ! ! »

« You bastard, I’ll remember you... »
