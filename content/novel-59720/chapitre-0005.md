# Chapitre 5 — School Life at the Ninja Academy

« What ? We have to bring our own bento ? I’m doomed, I didn’t prepare one. »

Naruto cried out in shock, his whole being turning a pale gray.

« Relax, relax. Here, this is yours. Hanako said it’s the 'Little Naruto Special Edition Heartfelt Meal,' with your favorite fried shrimp and Narutomaki. »

Asakawa Shin calmly pulled two bento boxes from his bag and handed the orange one to Naruto.

« Waaa, this is... specially prepared for me ? I can never repay Aunt Hanako’s kindness. »

Naruto took the bento with trembling hands, tears streaming down his face like thick noodles from emotion.

Indeed, when Hanako prepared the bento, she made two. If you’re herding one sheep, you might as well herd two.

She figured little Naruto, being a child living alone, wouldn’t know how to cook and would surely eat junk food like instant noodles or bread.

Anything not homemade is categorized as junk food—a prerogative of mothers across the multiverse.

She just didn’t expect that Naruto wouldn’t even bring instant noodles or bread ; he hadn’t brought anything at all. It could only be said that Hanako had underestimated Naruto’s carelessness.

However, with Naruto’s constitution, let alone missing one meal, he’d be fine even without eating for three days.

In the original story, the only consequence of Naruto constantly eating expired food was his short stature. Try that with anyone else, and the grass on their grave would be ten feet high by now.

In a corner where no one was looking, a young girl with the Byakugan rolled her eyes and silently pulled back her own bento.

« Hello, Shin-kun. I’m Ino Yamanaka. I heard just now—a mother’s heartfelt meal~ »

« I’m curious about how it tastes. Can I have a try ? »

« Oh, right, this is a bento I practiced making. Can we trade ? » The little blonde girl mustered her courage and made her move.

Shin looked at his classmate in surprise. This was Ino Yamanaka ? Wasn’t she a little fangirl of Sasuke ? Why was she coming to him ?

In fact, he had underestimated his own charm. It wasn’t just Ino ; nearly half the girls in the class were glancing his way, intentionally or not. But only the brave get to enjoy the world first.

« Haha, sure, let’s trade. You made this bento yourself ? You’re amazing~ »

Shin had a soft spot for Ino Yamanaka. She was a charming woman, appearing even more confident and independent when compared to her peers.

He had already planned to make friends with his classmates, and the six-year-old little cutie in front of him, blinking her big eyes, was just too adorable.

« Eh ? How can you do that ? Aunt Hanako specially prepared that for me ! » Naruto protested.

« Shut up, Naruto. »

« Got it. »

...

Sakura Haruno watched her best friend in shock. She successfully made friends just like that ? Does that mean I can too ?

Out of the corner of her eye, she saw Uchiha Sasuke finish packing his things and walk quietly toward the back row.

She hurried forward : « Hello, Sasuke, I’m... »

« Get lost, don’t get in my way ! »

« Eh ? »

...

After the lunch break, Iruka-sensei walked into the classroom to inform everyone to gather at the playground for the annual entrance ceremony.

On the high platform, an elderly man wore the Hokage hat and the ceremonial robe with the words « Sandaime Hokage » on the back. This man was the current Hokage, Hiruzen Sarutobi.

At this time, his back was slightly hunched, showing his age, no longer possessing the demeanor of the « God of Shinobi » from his prime.

'Is this Hiruzen Sarutobi ? Time is truly a terrifying monster ; the world-renowned God of Shinobi has aged so much.

Once a man gets old, his courage fades. In recent years, he has become increasingly obsessed with power and politics.

No wonder Orochimaru wanted to personally send him off ; it was to give his teacher a dignified end. After all, when does a Hokage ever die of old age ?'

Hiruzen Sarutobi had no idea someone was badmouthing him below. At this moment, he was giving a spirited speech on the platform :

« Where the leaves of Konoha dance, the fire shall burn. The shadow of the fire will shine upon the Village, allowing new leaves to sprout... »

Watching Hiruzen Sarutobi boast about the will of fire on stage, Asakawa Shin only found it exceptionally ridiculous.

The core value of the will of fire is to promote sacrifice and legacy. However, as Hokage, Hiruzen Sarutobi failed to use the right people and failed to distinguish between rewards and punishments, leading to Konoha’s gradual decline and the loss of its legacy.

Of his three disciples, one defected, and the other two were estranged, wandering outside of Konoha.

So, was there anyone on the stage that Shin hated more than Hiruzen Sarutobi ?

Yes, brother, there was. It was the Darkness of the Ninja World, Danzo Shimura, who stood beside the Hokage at this moment.

This guy was a real heavyweight. If you couldn’t find the culprit of an evil deed in the Ninja World, blaming him would be right eighty percent of the time.

Even if it wasn’t him, setting the facts aside, was Danzo completely blameless ?

Thus, he was jokingly called the « Scapegoat-kage » by many netizens.

On the high platform, Danzo listened to the annoying monkey calls in his ears while his gaze quietly scanned below.

The playground was full of heads ; the little ones looked at Hiruzen Sarutobi with glowing eyes, as if the monkey were speaking words of wisdom.

At this moment, Danzo just wanted to shout : 'Don’t be fooled by him ; those are all my lines !'

After a long time, before Danzo could even speak, Hiruzen Sarutobi announced with lingering enthusiasm that the entrance ceremony was over and each class should return in an orderly fashion.

Before leaving, he also assigned homework to the students : their reflections after listening to the Hokage’s speech, no less than 500 words.

Danzo : 'Damn you, Hiruzen Sarutobi, screwing me over again ! See if I don’t get back at you.

I’ll go back and double the falsified supplies ! And then secretly spread rumors about the Hokage peeping into the women’s bathhouse.'

Shin : 'Some civilian children can’t even read when they first enter school, and you’re already assigning homework ? Hiruzen Sarutobi, you’re courting death !

And that naturally evil old ghost Shimura, why was he staring at me just now ? One day, I will personally...'

...

Back in the classroom, the students received their textbooks for each subject in order, along with the wooden Ninja tools unique to the first grade—blunt and rounded, suitable for the constitution of most babies in the Ninja World.

These were free, but if damaged or lost, they would have to be purchased with their own money.

It was worth mentioning that the theoretical textbooks were quite numerous, including not only basic literacy and the history of the Ninja World but also a large amount of language arts, mathematics, physics, geography, politics, Chakra, and hand seals.

It was said that higher grades would also include Ninja tool throwing, trap setting and identification, the Three Basic Jutsu, fundamental principles of Ninjutsu, and practical combat courses.

With so many courses compressed into six years, it was no wonder so few civilian Ninjas became successful. If you weren’t a genius and had no one at home to teach you, you simply couldn’t understand it all.

Learning advanced Ninjutsu after graduation was even harder than climbing to heaven ; the highest achievement for most civilian Ninjas was becoming a Chunin.

...

One day later, the Hokage Office.

« 'Fallen blossoms are not heartless things ; they turn into spring soil to nourish the flowers' ? What a great poem, holy crap ! »

Hiruzen Sarutobi looked at the homework in his hand in shock. Is this a poem a first-grade freshman could write ?

Not just this line of poetry ; the entire essay praised his achievements over the years from five angles and seven aspects, making Hiruzen Sarutobi’s blood boil with excitement.

Between the lines, only three words were revealed : will of fire.

« What literary talent ! This magnificent essay is too good for this old man to keep to himself ! »

Hiruzen Sarutobi pressed a red button on his desk, and after a short while, a white-haired Anbu Ninja wearing a mask brought in a bottle of soda.

« Kakashi, wait a moment. Take this essay to the Konoha Propaganda Department.

Tell them this is an essay written by a freshman at the Ninja Academy this year and have them focus on promoting it. They’ll know what to do. »

After Kakashi left, Hiruzen Sarutobi sighed, « Asakawa Shin, huh ? What a good boy. Such talent should be vigorously cultivated. »

On the other side, Kakashi read the essay the Hokage had given him while on his way. The essay wasn’t long, only about five thousand words.

With Kakashi’s years of reading experience, he finished it in a moment. Between the lines, he only saw two words : utter nonsense.

The world is going to the dogs, and people’s hearts are no longer what they used to be !

« Asakawa Shin, huh ? Tsk~ »
