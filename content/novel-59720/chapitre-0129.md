# Chapitre 129 — The Chunin Exams Begin

Hokage Office.

« Theoretically, any Genin who has completed eight missions can participate in the Chunin Exams upon the recommendation of their Jonin sensei. So, first of all, for this year’s Ninja Academy graduates, do any of you Jonin sensei have candidates to recommend ? »

As soon as Hiruzen Sarutobi said this, everyone’s gaze turned forward in unison.

Tsunade also noticed the gazes of those present and stepped forward with a smile :

« In the name of Tsunade, I recommend my subordinates, Asakawa Shin, Haku, and Uzumaki Karin, to participate in this year’s Chunin Exams ! »

Hearing that thunderous name, the Hokage Office instantly became boisterous.

A flash of gratification and anticipation crossed Hiruzen Sarutobi’s eyes : 'Has the moment finally arrived ?'

He nodded : « Approved. What about the rest of you ? Do you have any candidates to recommend ? »

Kakashi Hatake stepped forward : « For Team 7, Uchiha Sasuke, Uzumaki Naruto, and Sakura Haruno. In the name of Kakashi Hatake, I recommend these three to participate in the Chunin Exams ! »

Kurenai Yuhi immediately followed : « For Team 8, Hyuga Hinata, Inuzuka Kiba, and Shino Aburame. In the name of Kurenai Yuhi, I recommend these three to participate in the Chunin Exams ! »

Asuma Sarutobi, with a cigarette still hanging from his mouth, spoke in a slightly lazy, raspy voice : « For Team 10, Ino Yamanaka, Nara Shikamaru, and Akimichi Choji. In the name of Asuma Sarutobi, I recommend them to participate in the Chunin Exams ! »

« What ? They actually recommended students to participate too... »

« To actually let rookie Genin participate in the Chunin Exams... this is a situation we haven’t seen in five whole years ! »

Iruka, who had been waiting silently in the corner, finally seized the opportunity and began to tout his achievements :

« Let me tell you, out of the 12 people just recommended, 11 of them were once my students ! They are truly outstanding and fully possess the strength to participate in the Chunin Exams ! Of course, them being this excellent, aside from their own hard work, is inseparable from the diligent teaching of me, their teacher... »

« Alright, alright, Iruka, that’s enough. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi glanced at him helplessly. Seeing that Iruka still looked like he had more to say, he quickly changed the subject and distributed the Chunin Exams registration forms.

« Those who want to participate, fill out the form. At 3 :00 PM in five days, have the candidates gather at Classroom 301 of the Ninja Academy to take the exam on time. That is all, dismissed ! »

« Yes ! »

« Eh ? Wait a moment, I haven’t finished speaking yet... »

« Lord Shin, is that Classroom 301 ahead ? Why are so many people blocking the doorway ? No, it seems a bit strange, that is... »

Karin turned her head to look at Asakawa Shin beside her and received his confirmation : « Correct, your observation is very keen. That is a genjutsu. It seems the first round of screening has already begun. Don’t pay any attention to them. If they can’t even see through a trick of this level, they have no qualifications to be promoted to Chunin. Let’s go. »

A group of people were still pushing and arguing at the door of 201, which had been affected by the genjutsu, completely unaware that Asakawa Shin and his group had already calmly passed them and gone upstairs.

Asakawa Shin pushed open the door to the real Classroom 301 and walked in first.

« You’re too slow, Shin ! We’ve been waiting for a long time. »

As soon as Naruto’s signature loud voice rang out, it instantly attracted the attention of the entire room.

Konoha Ninjas were one thing, but those Ninjas from other villages were also staring intently.

Especially the Ninjas from Otogakure, Kumogakure, and Sunagakure ; their gazes were particularly sharp, filled with scrutiny and hostility as they sized him up.

Asakawa Shin ignored them and familiarly bumped fists with Naruto.

Just as he was about to speak, a pair of hands affectionately wrapped around him from behind.

Ino Yamanaka hung onto Asakawa Shin, her face overflowing with undisguised excitement, chattering away about how much she had missed him :

« Shin-kun, you’re finally here ! The moment I thought about seeing you again after so long, I’ve been waiting here full of anticipation ! »

Karin’s eyes lit up instantly, and she immediately rushed forward to « protect » him, wrapping her arms around one side just like Ino, while loudly shouting :

« Ino, get down quickly ! You cannot be rude to Lord Shin. Lord Shin, don’t be afraid, I... I will protect you~ »

Not far away, Sakura clutched her clothes tightly, almost dying of jealousy, her inner personality screaming wildly :

« Damn it, that 'fat pig' Ino is getting too much ! When will I be able to be this close to Sasuke-kun ? How could this happen ? We were all on the same starting line... »

Witnessing Asakawa Shin with a girl on each arm, the crowd of examinees erupted.

« Nani ? How can he do that ? Who is that kid ? He’s so arrogant ! »

« You don’t know Lord Shin ? What village are you a hillbilly from ? Oh, so you’re a loser from Kusagakure ? I heard your village was destroyed a few years ago ? Then that’s no surprise... »

« Damn, I’m so envious of those two girls. If I could hug Lord Shin too, I’d be willing to give up my wealth and have many children and blessings. »

« You want to have your cake and eat it too ? You want to be an old cow eating tender grass ? You’re over twenty years old ; look in the mirror and check yourself. »

Tsunade outside the door : « ... »

Temari rested her chin in her hand, revealing a thoughtful expression :

'If there is a truce in the future, for the sake of peace between Sunagakure and Konoha, it seems it wouldn’t be impossible for me, the daughter of the Kazekage and princess of Sunagakure, to sacrifice myself and marry into Konoha, right ?'

Shikamaru stood low-key to the side, a gloating smile curling the corners of his mouth.

At this moment, the Team 8 trio also gathered around.

Hyuga Hinata walked in the center position, looking at Asakawa Shin’s current state with disdain :

« You have really fallen, Shin. Can you still be called a 'peerless genius' now ? Hurry up and wake up to reality ! »

Without waiting for Asakawa Shin to answer, she turned her gaze to Naruto, a hint of helplessness flashing in her eyes.

At this moment, for some reason, Naruto was arguing with Sasuke again, both of them pressing their foreheads against each other, neither willing to back down.

'Strange, what exactly is wrong... Why has my taming plan for Naruto made absolutely no progress ?'

It seemed to have touched Rock Lee’s underlying code ; before he arrived, his voice already came :

« Genius ? Hinata, there are too many geniuses in this world. Shin and you are even far above that level. But I won’t give up. Surpassing those so-called geniuses with my own effort—that’s what’s interesting, isn’t it ? »

Asakawa Shin finally managed to detach the two « koalas » from himself. He looked toward the direction the voice came from and clapped his hands with a laugh :

« As expected of you, Rock Lee, you’re very spirited. I wouldn’t dare make guarantees for others, but I know you will definitely be able to do it. Because you are also a genius—a genius of hard work. »

« Shin... »

« Rock Lee... »

Just as the two were feeling mutual admiration, a puff of smoke suddenly exploded on the podium, attracting everyone’s attention.

Immediately after, an extremely arrogant voice pierced through the smoke and came through clearly :

« Quiet, all of you ! You bunch of barking curs—except for you, Lord Shin. »

Hearing this, the examinees all fell to the floor.

The smoke slowly dissipated, revealing a group of Konoha Ninjas.

The examiner in the lead was burly, his face covered in ferocious scars, his gaze sweeping across the room as cold and sharp as a blade.

After the room was completely silent, he coughed twice and announced in a deep voice :

« Sorry to keep you waiting. I am the proctor for the first round of the Chunin Exams—Ibiki Morino ! »
