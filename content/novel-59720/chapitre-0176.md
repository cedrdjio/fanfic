# Chapitre 176 — The Third Template

« What ? Konoha wants our eastern territory ? Isn’t that area just a barren desert ? »

« Not just territory, but also massive war reparations, a reduction in our mission share, and they won’t even return the Jinchuriki captive ? This is simply absurd ! »

Hearing Chiyo’s furious roar, the messenger Ninja trembled and reported in a shaky voice :

« Reporting to Lady Chiyo, the messenger from Konoha’s attitude is extremely arrogant. He claims this is an ultimatum—not a negotiation, but a notification ! What should we do ? Should we... refuse ? »

Chiyo took a deep breath, suppressing the anger in her heart. « No... agree to them. As for the matter of ceding territory, we have no authority to decide ; we must consult the Daimyo. »

While Konoha was in the midst of intense negotiations with Sunagakure and Kumogakure, Asakawa Shin’s thoughts had already drifted elsewhere, because he had just... been promoted to the glorious rank of Chunin.

[Ding ! It has been detected that the host’s previous template fusion has reached 100%, and your Ninja rank promotion is complete. Congratulations, you have earned one chance to draw a new template.]

[Current Ninja Rank : Chunin.]

[Draw a world now ? Yes/No]

Asakawa Shin rubbed his chin, and after making a big show of bathing and changing his clothes, he finally made a solemn decision.

'System, let’s draw.'

[Ding ! Congratulations, host, you have successfully drawn the « One Punch Man » world. Please proceed to draw a hair color to obtain a template from that world.]

'One Punch Man ? Not bad. A world with both high floors and high ceilings. As expected, a sense of ritual is necessary.'

A sharp glint flashed in Asakawa Shin’s eyes. 'System, keep drawing for hair color !'

[Understood ! Drawing hair color for you, please wait...]

As Asakawa Shin swallowed nervously, the hair color draw was finally complete.

[Ding ! Congratulations, host, the hair color drawn is « green. » The system will now proceed to draw a template for you based on the world and hair color drawn. Please wait...]

'Green, could it be...'

[Ding ! Congratulations, host, you have successfully drawn the « Fubuki of Hell » template. Please note that once this template’s proficiency reaches 100% and your Ninja rank is promoted, you will receive another chance to draw a template.]

[Current Ninja Rank : Chunin. Next Rank : Jonin/Kage. Please check the manual for details.]

'Hahaha, this is amazing, it’s exactly... Huh ? Why does something feel off ?'

Asakawa Shin abruptly retracted his smile, his face suddenly turning pale with shock.

'Nani ? I pulled Fubuki instead of Tatsumaki ? I admit, I’ve made many mistakes regarding Fubuki, but you can’t play me like this~'

Before Asakawa Shin could finish his repentance, a strange power merged into his body.

At that moment, he felt as if an invisible « shackle » within his body had been shattered.

Asakawa Shin frowned, carefully sensing the changes in his body.

'Aside from an extra surge of Psychokinesis, there doesn’t seem to be anything else unusual.'

'Was it just an illusion caused by being too excited ? Well, it’s better than nothing. At the very least, I can fly now.'

Thinking this, he began testing his newly acquired Superpower. A wave of Psychokinesis gently lifted him up, carrying him straight into the sky.

« Holy crap, this feels great ! Soaring freely through the sky—it’s even more agile than what I dreamed of as a kid. »

And so, he excitedly flew in circles above Konoha Village.

Yet for some reason, he kept hearing the startled cries of girls coming from below.

Could it be that they were too excited to see him ?

Whatever, let’s go to the outskirts to test the strength of this Superpower.

He changed direction and flew rapidly toward the outskirts of the village, his speed so great that it directly triggered a sonic boom.

« Huh ? Is Fubuki’s Psychokinesis this strong ? Oh, I get it... it must have been enhanced after merging with my own spiritual energy, right ? »

Meanwhile, inside the Hokage Office, an Anbu Ninja suddenly burst in.

The Anbu was drenched in sweat, and upon seeing Tsunade, he hurriedly reported, « This is bad, Lady Tsunade ! A strange incident has occurred in Konoha Village. The panties of many girls have been destroyed by an unknown Ninjutsu... »

Before he could finish, he sensed that the atmosphere was off.

Looking up, he saw Tsunade’s face was flushed crimson, her teeth gritted in rage.

« I’m already aware of this. Don’t worry, I will definitely get to the bottom of this investigation. Now, leave. »

After the Anbu withdrew, Tsunade finally couldn’t hold it back anymore and smashed her desk to pieces with a fierce punch.

« Dammit, this is too much ! To even destroy my panties... they certainly have some skill. Just wait until I catch whoever did this ; I’ll skin them alive ! »

Asakawa Shin was currently oblivious to the chaos within Konoha Village. He was happily testing the strength of his Superpower.

With just a thought, his Psychokinesis surged out like a landslide or a tsunami, easily lifting a small mountain.

« Holy crap ! Fubuki’s actually got something ! No, it must be because it merged with my spiritual energy that it reached this level of strength. She just provided the key to the Superpower, but it’s still pretty good. As expected of my teacher. »

He cleared his throat and turned his gaze toward the sky once more.

« How far am I from Tatsumaki’s level now ? I remember she could use Psychokinesis to pull down meteors to destroy enemies at will. I wonder if I can do that now ? »

As Asakawa Shin focused intently, he was surprised to find that his sensory range had grown terrifyingly large.

With a shift of his thoughts, he actually managed to scan numerous meteors in outer space.

And then...

First, a small black dot appeared in the sky, gradually growing larger over time. A meteor was truly being pulled down from space by him, crashing toward the Ninja World with grand momentum.

As the meteor approached, the atmosphere began to tremble, and countless birds and animals fled in terror.

Some birds that were too close were even shaken to death by the atmospheric tremors, falling to the ground like raindrops.

« This is about enough for an experiment. I can’t actually let it crash down ; it would be bad if it destroyed Konoha. »

As he unleashed his Psychokinesis with full force, his body was gradually coated in a layer of green fluorescence.

Under the influence of this terrifying Superpower, the meteor seemed to have its deceleration button pressed, slowing down at a visible rate until it finally hung motionless in the air.

Asakawa Shin casually placed it on the ground and returned to Konoha with a face full of excitement.

However, when he arrived at the Hokage Office, he found that Tsunade’s expression was a bit off.

She looked dazed and was muttering to herself, « Who on earth did this ? How did the panties I just changed into shred again ? »

Asakawa Shin scratched his head as he listened. « Senior sister, what broke ? Your integrity ? Hahaha. »

Tsunade’s whole body shuddered, and she slowly turned a suspicious gaze toward Asakawa Shin.

« Junior brother, it couldn’t be... you who did it, right ? »
