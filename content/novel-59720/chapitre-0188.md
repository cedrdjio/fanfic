# Chapitre 188 — Immortal's Magic Treasure

« I’ve... really struck it rich this time ! »

Koyuki Kazahana was beaming with joy ; her previous fear had long since vanished, replaced by a face full of pride and determination.

She had heard so much about Asakawa Shin’s great reputation.

Things like « Konoha’s Number One Genius, » « defeating two great nations in one battle, » and there were even rumors that he could « kill a Kage with a single sword stroke. »

'He must be bragging, right ?' Koyuki Kazahana thought to herself.

Although there might be a lot of exaggeration in these rumors, even if those exaggerated parts were cut in half...

That still wasn’t something that bastard Doto Kazahana, who only acts tough at home, could ever hope to match.

With this big shot backing her, she was all set !

Unless forced by necessity, who would be willing to leave their home and flee for their life in constant fear ?

If she could take back everything honorably, who would be content being a fugitive star ?

She looked toward the north with high spirits, her eyes flashing with the light of ambition.

'Land of Snow, your exiled Daimyo has returned !'

Asakawa Shin looked at the woman before him with a hint of surprise ; wasn’t this person’s face-changing speed a bit too fast ?

He cleared his throat, hiding the smile at the corner of his mouth. « I actually preferred your previous rebellious look. Why don’t you switch back ? »

Koyuki Kazahana was stunned for a moment, then quickly reined in the pride on her face.

She was an actress, after all ; how could this be difficult for her ?

In an instant, that cold, serious Princess of the Land of Snow, who carried an air of a mature and authoritative woman, reappeared before everyone.

Asakawa Shin nodded slightly in satisfaction.

Not bad, this feels right. A Princess of the Land of Snow should be as cold and aloof as ice and snow.

« Lord Shin, I wonder how you plan to reclaim the Land of Snow ? »

Koyuki Kazahana bowed slightly, a hint of barely perceptible expectation in her tone.

« It’s simple, two steps. »

Koyuki Kazahana’s expression turned solemn, adopting a posture of listening respectfully.

« Step one : walk over there. »

Asakawa Shin held up his first finger, paused, and then held up a second finger.

« Step two : beat him to death. »

« Cough, cough ! »

Koyuki Kazahana was nearly choked by this blunt answer, but she was a professional actress after all. She quickly adjusted her breathing, gave a thumbs up, and showed a face full of admiration.

« As expected of Lord Shin, such Haki ! Then let’s set off quickly ? I’m already feeling a bit impatient. »

« Yeah, let’s go. »

Meanwhile, Nadare Roga fled for his life and finally reached a secret stronghold.

Only after closing the heavy iron door did he dare to stop and catch his breath.

« No, I must contact Lord Doto immediately and request reinforcements ! »

By the time Doto Kazahana received the news, Asakawa Shin’s group had already arrived at the Land of Snow’s port by ship and then transferred to snowmobiles to continue deeper.

Accompanied by the screeching of brakes, the snowmobile stopped steadily at the intended filming location.

However, not long after filming began, a grating sound of ice breaking suddenly rang out.

The ice surface the snowmobile had just passed over melted rapidly, revealing a train track hidden beneath the ice layer.

« Not good, someone injected Chakra to melt the ice. They’re coming. »

Sandayu’s expression changed drastically. After a hurried warning, he rushed to nearby villages to gather the samurai who supported Koyuki Kazahana.

Koyuki Kazahana looked worried, her small hands unconsciously clutching her collar.

But soon, she took a deep breath and forced herself to calm down ; instead, a hint of expectation flowed from her eyes.

They didn’t have to wait long. A few minutes later, a dull train whistle pierced through the wind and snow.

A fully armed armored train roared out of the tunnel and stopped steadily on the tracks not far from everyone.

Koyuki Kazahana’s gaze narrowed, staring fixedly at the two figures at the rear of the train.

Doto Kazahana squinted over expressionlessly, his tone indifferent. « So it really is Koyuki. It’s been over ten years since we last met, and you’ve grown so much. »

Before he finished speaking, Sandayu actually led fifty samurai in a sneak attack without any regard for martial honor.

Dozens of heavy logs were pushed down the slope by them, slamming hard into the flank of the train.

Unfortunately, the train had clearly been specially modified ; it remained completely unscathed after such heavy impacts.

Sandayu raised his arms and shouted, leading the samurai in a charge fueled by their loyalty to Princess Koyuki.

Doto Kazahana snorted in disdain. « So you still had a group like this hidden away ? Then I’ll let you taste the meaning of despair. »

At his command, the train actually began to transform violently.

The armor plates on the flanks flipped open, revealing Kunai launching mechanisms similar to machine guns.

In just an instant, countless Kunai poured out like a rainstorm, meeting the charging samurai.

Koyuki Kazahana’s face changed color, and she hurriedly pulled Asakawa Shin’s arm, pleading urgently, « Lord Shin, please save them ! »

Asakawa Shin sighed softly. Weren’t Koyuki’s subordinates his own subordinates ?

With a slight thought, his sea-like Psychokinesis surged out instantly, freezing the countless high-speed flying Kunai in mid-air and completely jamming the launching mechanisms along with them.

« Nani ? »

Doto Kazahana was horrified. This eerie scene made his skin crawl, and he immediately decided to play it safe and ordered a retreat.

But how could Asakawa Shin let him have his way ? With just a slight lift of his hand, he precisely severed the connection between the last carriage and the locomotive.

Doto Kazahana could only watch helplessly as the locomotive sped away, while he himself remained behind.

« Hey, I haven’t gotten on yet ! »

His face flushed red with anger, and then he turned his head, staring fixedly at Asakawa Shin with a murderous gaze.

« You bastard, are you the backer Koyuki found ? As long as I kill you, I’ll be able to get the Kazahana family treasure ! »

A cold light flashed in his eyes as he roared and charged toward Asakawa Shin.

Asakawa Shin didn’t even give him a direct look, simply giving a faint command : « Snake Hime, kill him. »

As soon as he spoke, the pendant hanging from his ear and the snake-shaped bracelet coiled around his wrist suddenly glowed, transforming into three large white-scaled snakes that roared out.

« What the heck... this broken place is so cold, I was about to hibernate, and you’re still making me work. »

Ichikishima-hime’s eyes gleamed with ferocity.

For the past few months, she had been played with as a bracelet by Asakawa Shin all day long ; her scales had been polished to a bright shine, and a stomach full of wicked fire had already built up in her heart.

Now that she finally caught an opportunity, she could vent it all out to her heart’s content.

She took a deep breath and launched an attack without hesitation.

« Sage Art : White Rage Technique ! »

In an instant, a dragon with a pearl in its mouth roared out, charging to Doto Kazahana’s side in a flash before exploding violently.

Intense white light and sound waves instantly swept the surroundings with terrifying momentum.

Doto Kazahana had never seen such a display ; he was hit head-on and collapsed stiffly into the snow while maintaining his charging posture, unable to move.

Tagitsuhime and Tajitsuhime on the side were not affected at all. They seized the opportunity to swarm forward, biting the prey directly and injecting a large amount of venom and Natural Energy.

How could Doto Kazahana withstand this combination of strikes ? In just one encounter, he completely bit the dust.

Koyuki Kazahana was dumbfounded, and only after a long while did she come to her senses, asking hesitantly, « Lord Shin, what... what is this ? »

« This... »

Asakawa Shin said calmly, « It’s called an Immortal’s Magic Treasure. »

« I see ! »

Koyuki Kazahana’s eyes lit up. « Ninjas... are so amazing ! »
