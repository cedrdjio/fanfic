# Chapitre 197 — Reliable

Asakawa Shin pondered for a moment, and realization dawned on him.

It must have been the pull of that world’s will when he traveled back to the original timeline, causing a slight deviation in the transit.

However, considering that Mukade and Naruto traveled at the same time yet ended up years apart, his own deviation was well within the normal range.

The only question was how to explain this to his friends and family in Konoha. They must have been worried sick...

In the dimly lit laboratory, Orochimaru narrowed his snake-like vertical pupils, carefully observing his junior brother whom he hadn’t seen in a long time.

Due to the combined influence of the matured God Tree and his physique from the Pirate World, he had grown to 1.8 meters tall, his frame upright and towering, actually a full head taller than Orochimaru.

However, what truly made his pupils shrink was that the flower of the God Tree on top of Asakawa Shin’s head, once considered his trademark, had already vanished.

In its place was an abnormal change on his forehead.

This left him a bit confused, but the desire for the unknown in his heart grew even stronger.

'I really want his cells.'

Orochimaru’s eyes flashed, he wiped the drool from the corner of his mouth, and then spoke : « Junior brother, you had better return quickly. You have no idea, over these past few years, those friends of yours have practically turned the Ninja World upside down looking for you. Even I have lost several bases and quite a few research projects because of it. »

Asakawa Shin rolled his eyes and retorted irritably : « Your research projects must have been illicit ; otherwise, Naruto surely wouldn’t have caused trouble. »

He was very certain of this.

« Alright, Senior brother, I have another surprise for you. »

Asakawa Shin suddenly snapped his fingers, turned around to show the group behind him to Orochimaru, and a wicked smirk curled on his lips.

« Look, this time I’ve brought you another big project, the descendants of the Six Paths Sage... I wonder if you’re interested ? »

Asakawa Shin casually grabbed a trembling figure from behind him and pushed them toward Orochimaru.

Orochimaru had originally only glanced over carelessly, thinking it was just a regular Hyuga, but upon learning that these people were actually the descendants of the legendary Six Paths Sage, he froze completely.

Those snake eyes, usually calm and unruffled, suddenly contracted, and then erupted with a nearly maniacal, scorching light.

He thrust out his tongue to lick his dry lips, his body trembling slightly from extreme excitement.

That gaze was enough to make the remnants of the Ōtsutsuki Clan on the moon shake like chaff in the wind.

« Alright, Senior brother, look into sustainable development, don’t scare them off... »

After settling the follow-up matters, Asakawa Shin flashed away from the spot, and when he reappeared, he had already returned to Konoha Village.

The Hokage Office was quiet and oppressive. Tsunade was dozing off over a mountain of documents, her brows locked with an inseparable gloom and self-reproach.

For these three years, she had been filled with regret every single moment.

'If I hadn’t given that necklace to Shin back then, perhaps he wouldn’t have mysteriously disappeared without a trace.'

Just then, the space suddenly rippled, and Asakawa Shin’s figure appeared out of thin air in the office.

This familiar Chakra fluctuation made Tsunade’s heart tremble.

She raised her head abruptly, her eyes filled with disbelief : « Shin ? Is it really you ? I’m not dreaming, am I ? »

Before the words had even faded, she stood up with a start.

The chair beneath her screeched harshly against the floor due to the immense force, and even the heavy desk was pushed several inches forward by her collision.

However, Tsunade was completely unaware of this ; at this moment, her heart and eyes were filled only with that figure she had been separated from for so long, and she rushed over, almost stumbling.

« You brat, where the hell have you been these three years ! »

Tsunade could no longer hold back, and with teary eyes, she threw herself into Asakawa Shin’s arms.

Her trembling fingers caressed his chiseled face, and then her fingertips suddenly tightened, pinching his cheek hard.

« Ouch— ! It hurts, it hurts ! »

Asakawa Shin’s cheek deformed instantly, and he let out an incoherent wail : « Senior sister, are you trying to murder your husband ? »

Tsunade snorted coldly, but couldn’t hide her reddened eyes : « You’ve been missing for three whole years, do you know how worried I was ? You... you just wait ! »

Before she finished speaking, she shoved Asakawa Shin away, turned around, and stormed out, slamming the door behind her.

Asakawa Shin rubbed his cheek, frowning slightly, a sense of foreboding welling up in his heart.

Facts proved that his intuition was correct.

Only a moment later, the door to the Hokage Office was slammed open, and a large crowd of people barged in.

Leading them was Asakawa Hanako, and close by her side was a little girl who only reached her waist, her delicate face looking as if it had been cast from the same mold as Hanako.

« Shin... »

Hanako’s voice carried a hint of imperceptible trembling, and her eyes quickly turned red.

She put her hands on her hips, wanting to maintain her authority as a mother, but her feet moved forward uncontrollably.

« Hanako, I’m sorry, I made you worry. »

Hanako rushed to him, and instead of the punches and kicks he imagined, she suddenly spread her arms and hugged him fiercely and tightly, as if trying to squeeze all the longing and fear of these three years into this embrace.

« Wuwuwu, how rude, you should call me Mom. »

« ... »

« And me, call me little sister ! »

« ... »

After much trouble, Asakawa Shin finally managed to calm down the questioning from everyone, feeling even more exhausted than dealing with extended relatives during the New Year.

However, when he let out a sigh of relief, an inexplicable sense of incongruity quietly crept into his heart.

He frowned slightly, scanned his surroundings, and a flash of inspiration suddenly struck his mind.

Something was wrong ; it was too quiet.

Where did that big mouth Naruto go ? And what about Sasuke, why is he nowhere to be seen ? Did they go out on a mission ?

« What ? They actually went on an S-rank mission ? »

Tsunade nodded with a solemn expression, her fingers tapping the desk unconsciously : « That’s right, the Fifth Kazekage of the Hidden Sand Village, Gaara, was attacked by unknown Ninjas and kidnapped. They went to provide support. »

« Oh... I see. » Asakawa Shin nodded in realization.

It seems the Shippuden arc has already begun.

In other words, the Three-Tails has resurrected, and the Akatsuki Organization has officially begun their Tailed Beast capture plan.

« It seems I need to head out too... »

Hearing Asakawa Shin’s whisper, Tsunade looked over, somewhat stunned : « Junior brother, are you interested in this mission too ? You just got back, why not rest for a few days ? »

Asakawa Shin shook his head gently.

He did indeed want to participate, but not as Konoha reinforcements ; rather, he wanted to infiltrate as a member of the Akatsuki Organization.

« No need, Senior sister, this is the time when the organization needs me ; I must go ! »

Tsunade couldn’t help but feel a surge of warmth upon hearing this ; as expected of a pillar of Konoha, he was truly reliable.

With him around, being the Hokage was just too easy.

« Then take care of yourself, and be sure to stay safe. »

« Understood ! Senior sister, then I’ll be off~ »

« Mm, go ahead ! »
