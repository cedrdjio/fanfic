# Chapitre 144 — The Third Exam Preliminaries

Once the five-day deadline arrived, Asakawa Shin used the Flying Thunder God Technique to teleport directly back to the central tower.

By this time, the tower was already buzzing with voices and had become quite lively.

The moment Naruto saw Asakawa Shin, his eyes widened, and he shouted in disbelief, « Shin ! Where on earth have you been hiding ? I’ve practically turned the entire tower upside down and couldn’t even catch a glimpse of your shadow ! »

Of course he couldn’t find him.

Five days ago, Asakawa Shin had already submitted the scroll and then leisurely teleported back to his villa in Konoha using the Flying Thunder God Technique.

As for the proctors, although they were well aware of this, they could only tacitly choose to look the other way ; no one dared to risk provoking trouble.

Estimating that the time was about right, he teleported back together with Karin and Haku.

It was a stark contrast to the disheveled and exhausted appearance of everyone else present.

The three of them were dressed as neatly as if they were brand new, their expressions relaxed, as if they had just returned from a pleasant vacation.

Using the Flying Thunder God to head home was truly well-deserved of its reputation.

Asakawa Shin casually made up an excuse and easily brushed Naruto off.

His gaze was sharp as lightning as he quickly scanned the entire venue.

Including the three of them, there were exactly twenty-one people left.

Apart from their team, the Konoha 12, Gaara’s team, and the Hidden Cloud Village’s Karui team, there wasn’t even a single team from a smaller nation left.

Soon, as a thick cloud of white smoke dissipated, Sandaime Hokage Hiruzen Sarutobi made a grand entrance, leading a group of Jonin.

After a long-winded opening speech, the proctor for the third exam finally appeared.

Just as the man was about to speak, he was suddenly seized by a fit of violent coughing, his body hunched over as if he were about to cough out his very lungs the next second.

The surrounding candidates saw this and cast looks of surprise and confusion.

However, Asakawa Shin keenly noticed that this proctor was staring right at him with a vicious and sinister gaze.

His eyes rolled slightly, and he immediately understood, 'Isn’t this Gekkō Hayate ? That simp for Yūgao.'

Yūgao had now been assigned by him to guard Hanako’s family, commanding several Anbu squads and busy with daily affairs ; by all accounts, they should have cut off contact long ago.

He hadn’t expected this guy to be so persistent...

Gekkō Hayate clutched his chest and coughed violently for a long while before finally catching his breath.

He suppressed the itch in his throat, swept a sharp gaze over the candidates, and announced loudly :

« The next event is the preliminaries for the third exam. This preliminary round will take the form of live combat, and the fight will continue until one side admits defeat or falls dead. In other words, this is a contest accompanied by the risk of death. If anyone wishes to withdraw, you may do so now. »

The candidates remained silent for a moment ; not a single person chose to withdraw.

Hiruzen Sarutobi stroked his beard and proposed : « Since that is the case, with twenty-one people participating, someone must have a bye. I propose that Asakawa Shin advances directly. Does anyone have any objections ? »

Before the words had even faded, Gekkō Hayate forced his sickly body to raise a hand in opposition : « Hokage-sama, this action might be unfair to the other candidates. I believe it would be more appropriate to decide by drawing lots. »

As soon as these words were spoken, the crowd was in an uproar.

Everyone looked in astonishment at this usually reticent proctor, and even Asakawa Shin couldn’t help but raise an eyebrow and look over.

He cursed inwardly : 'Gekkō Hayate, very well ! You simp for Yūgao, I didn’t expect that after all these years, you would still hold such hostility toward me. But counting the days...'

His eyes rolled, and he turned his head to the surrounding candidates and said : « Since the proctor has said so, let’s decide by drawing lots. However, if I don’t get a bye, I will break the leg of the next opponent I draw. »

Once this was said, the candidates looked terrified and waved their hands repeatedly.

Some even cast dissatisfied looks toward Gekkō Hayate and complained loudly : « I think a bye for Shin is very reasonable ; there’s no need to draw lots at all. »

The others' eyes lit up, and they chimed in : « Yeah, that’s right, Shin should get the bye ; that’s the only way it’s fair. »

Listening to the rising and falling discussions from the crowd, Gekkō Hayate’s face turned livid, his expression so gloomy it seemed like water could drip from it.

He took a deep breath, suppressed the resentment in his heart, and announced coldly :

« In that case, Asakawa Shin advances directly with a bye. The remaining twenty people will draw lots to form ten groups ; the winners advance to the official tournament, and the losers are directly eliminated. »

As the order was given, the list for the first match was quickly revealed.

When the names of the two opponents were called out, Uchiha Sasuke and Haku simultaneously tensed their bodies, their expressions solemn.

The two exchanged a glance across the crowd, alarm bells ringing in their hearts, 'This is definitely a troublesome opponent.'

In an instant, their fierce battle erupted.

Haku’s Ice Release, which he took pride in, was clearly insufficient in front of Sasuke’s pair of Three-Tomoe Sharingan.

His astonishing speed advantage was completely seen through by the Sharingan, his movements were constrained everywhere, and he instantly fell into the predicament of being passively beaten.

Even though he tried his best to use « Ice Release : Demonic Mirroring Ice Crystals » and « Hidden Mist Technique » to trap Sasuke in a narrow, closed space in an attempt to surround and attack him, his strikes could never hit his opponent.

In the end, Sasuke seized an opening, decisively used « Soru » to charge like lightning, and ended the battle in one fell swoop.

Asakawa Shin shook his head with regret on the sidelines : 'Haku is really unlucky ; his tactical system happens to be hard-countered by the Sharingan, so his loss is truly not unjust.'

The second match unfolded immediately, with the two opponents being Shino Aburame and Atsui from the Hidden Cloud Village.

It is worth mentioning that the Jonin leading the Hidden Cloud team this time was Atsui’s older sister, Samui.

The other two accompanying team members were Killer Bee’s disciples, Karui and Omoi ; all three were outstanding members of the younger generation in the Hidden Cloud.

Seeing that his opponent was Shino, Atsui not only showed no fear but could barely hide his excitement, grinning : « Not bad luck, to actually run into an Insect User from the Aburame clan. »

« Is that so ? » Shino’s expression was indifferent as he pushed up the glasses on the bridge of his nose. « I, on the other hand, think you have very bad luck. Secret Technique : Parasitic Insects. »

In an instant, a jet-black swarm of insects surged out from under Shino’s robe like a dark cloud, sweeping toward Atsui.

Atsui saw this and was not afraid ; on the contrary, a confident arc curled at the corner of his mouth.

He took the initiative to draw his sword and charge forward, shouting sharply : « Cloud Style : Flame Slash ! »

As the blade swung, scorching flames erupted like raging waves, instantly meeting the dense sea of insects.

Flying insects are naturally afraid of fire, and under the ruthless burning of the flames, the swarm fell to the ground like raindrops, turning into charred remains.

Shino frowned slightly and analyzed calmly : « So he is a taijutsu Ninja who can manipulate flames ; he is indeed a bit troublesome. »

Shino quickly adjusted his tactics, moving around to constantly keep his distance from his opponent, while simultaneously releasing small batches of parasitic insects, breaking the whole into parts to launch harassment from all directions.

Atsui found it difficult to parry for a moment, and what shocked him even more was that his own speed, which he was proud of, could not catch up with his opponent at all.

Having no other choice, he could only passively swing his sword to counterattack, and the battle instantly turned into a stalemate, beginning to test their endurance.

Regarding this, Atsui confidently raised the corner of his mouth ; as a Hidden Cloud Ninja who had practiced taijutsu since childhood, he had absolute confidence in his physical stamina.

However, it didn’t take long before he noticed something unusual.

The opponent’s stamina was not at all inferior to his own, whereas he, due to frequent sword swinging and dashing, was consuming much more energy than his opponent.

As time passed, Atsui gradually became out of breath, while Shino Aburame on the other side was only sweating slightly.

Atsui’s brows locked tight, and after gritting his teeth and struggling to hold on for a while longer, he finally felt powerless and took the initiative to raise his hand and admit defeat.

In the second match, Shino Aburame was the victor.
