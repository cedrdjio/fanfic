# Chapitre 79 — Sprouting

'What ? Sprouting ? I’m sprouting ? I didn’t mutate, did I ?'

Images of vegetable people and Groot flashed through Asakawa Shin’s mind instantly, and his consciousness, previously groggy from being suddenly woken up, became completely clear.

He did a kip-up and scrambled to his feet, quickly lowering his head to check his hands, feet, and body. Seeing no abnormalities, he finally let out a sigh of relief : « Hey ! Isn’t it fine ? Naruto, you’re scaring me again~ »

Naruto, however, pointed upwards in horror : « It’s not that, it’s that your head is sprouting ! You’ll know if you look in the mirror. »

As it turned out, Naruto had just woken up from his dream this morning, groggily noticing that his body felt very heavy and his arms were a bit sore and numb.

He couldn’t help but smile helplessly, moving Asakawa Shin’s leg away from his chest, and silently complained in his heart :

'Shin’s sleeping posture is really something else. My whole body is aching, like I’ve been beaten up...'

Rubbing his bleary eyes, he turned his head to look and froze instantly.

He saw a small, tender green sprout emerging from the center of Asakawa Shin’s head, trembling slightly with his breathing.

Naruto initially thought he was still dreaming, groggily reaching out to poke it twice, and the tactile sensation was so real.

Only then did he suddenly wake up, realizing that everything before him was real.

Immediately, not daring to be the least bit negligent, he turned and woke Asakawa Shin up.

Asakawa Shin walked to the mirror and examined himself carefully.

Sure enough, as Naruto said, a crystal-clear young sprout had really grown on top of his head. It was lush and verdant, quite good-looking, about three or four centimeters high, and looked exceptionally eye-catching against his dark hair.

The corners of his mouth twitched slightly, and he was certain in his heart that this must be a mutation caused by absorbing Nine-Tailed Chakra.

If his expectations were correct, he would need to collect the Chakra of the remaining eight Tailed Beasts to make this seedling continue to grow.

However, it seemed difficult to complete in the short term, as those eight Tailed Beasts were not easy existences to contact.

Each one was an important strategic resource for the major hidden villages, and if he wanted to collect them, he would inevitably have a direct conflict with these hidden villages.

Not only that, but there was also the Akatsuki Organization lurking in the dark ; they were also eyeing the Tailed Beasts and were his potential opponents as well.

'Hmph, things seem to have become a bit tricky.'

'But if someone wants to block my path, no matter who they are—the major hidden villages or the Akatsuki Organization—I will wipe them out without mercy.'

'No... not necessarily, perhaps it won’t need to be so troublesome...'

Withdrawing his thoughts, Asakawa Shin began to study the tender sprout on his head. It felt a bit peculiar to the touch ; the plant, which looked soft and delicate, was actually very resilient, almost comparable to his own stubborn little mouth.

This made him secretly sigh in relief ; at least he didn’t have an extra weak point for no reason.

After all, this tender sprout was so conspicuous that it might become a priority target for enemies to attack, just like the ship’s wheel of the Golden Lion.

Asakawa Shin collected his stray thoughts and carefully felt the changes inside his body. His cell vitality and life force had improved, and his Chakra quantity was also increasing rapidly. He was getting closer and closer to a higher-level life form.

Naruto stood to the side, his brows full of worry, and finally couldn’t help asking with concern : « Shin, are you really okay ? Why don’t we go to the Konoha Hospital and take a look ? »

« There must be something wrong, right ? After all... how can a person’s head sprout ? »

Asakawa Shin turned his head, smiled at him gently, his tone calm and mild :

« It’s fine, Naruto. This is proof of my successful cultivation. Not only is it harmless, but it’s actually a good thing. Look, aren’t I perfectly fine ? »

Naruto was a careless person by nature. After hearing Asakawa Shin say this, he truly felt relieved and even excitedly clamored to touch the sprout again.

Asakawa Shin couldn’t refuse him, so he had to agree helplessly.

« Wow, these leaves are soft, and they feel a bit warm when touched. The texture is also very special, elastic like rubber. »

« Look, it bounces back when pressed. Hahaha, how interesting ! Shin, such a cool thing, can you make one for me too ? »

Naruto’s eyes lit up, and his tone was full of envy and expectation.

A trace of a smile flashed in Asakawa Shin’s eyes, and he hurriedly interrupted : « Alright, Naruto, stop messing around. This is an exclusive accessory ; others can’t wear it. »

« I have to go on a long trip later and need to prepare in advance. Don’t you have to go to school too ? Let’s hurry up and wash up. »

After speaking, he fled as if escaping. The feeling of being played with by Naruto was just too strange. Only Naruto, who hadn’t had enough fun yet, was left behind, shouting in dissatisfaction.

...

After washing up, Asakawa Shin carefully sealed many items into a blank scroll, carried them with him, and then stood in the courtyard with a solemn expression.

He frowned slightly, hesitated for a moment, and then began to weave hand signs : « Ninjutsu · Shadow Clone Technique. »

With a « pop, » the white smoke dissipated, and a clone exactly like him appeared in front of him, arms crossed, staring at him with displeasure :

« Hey, you wouldn’t happen to want to go find Sister Tsunade to play by yourself, but leave me behind for class, right ? In your dreams ! Why don’t we switch ? »

'Is that so ?'

Asakawa Shin sighed and looked at the Shadow Clone with calm eyes, requesting in a solemn tone :

« You know that going this time isn’t for fun, but to handle important matters. Please endure it for two days, and I will definitely return as soon as possible. »

The Shadow Clone didn’t reply, just stared at him silently. Just when he swallowed and lost hope, he heard the Shadow Clone say somewhat impatiently :

« You’d better come back early, otherwise you know the consequences. »

After speaking, it rushed off to take Naruto to school. Only then did Naruto react and ask loudly :

« Eh ? You can do that ? Shin, this Ninjutsu of yours is too strong, teach me, quickly teach me, I don’t want to go to school either... »

...

'Phew... handled the most troublesome one.'

Asakawa Shin let out a slow sigh of relief. Now it was time to fulfill his promise. He bit his index finger and quickly wiped it on his hand.

This step was the key to performing the Summoning Technique. If he was too slow, the wound would heal and cause the technique to fail !

« Ninjutsu · Summoning Technique ! »

Another burst of smoke, and a small Katsuyu slowly crawled out, happily saying to Asakawa Shin : « We meet again, Lord Shin. Could it be that this time you want to... »

Asakawa Shin looked at this small Katsuyu helplessly. Having lived together for over a year, he already understood Sage Katsuyu’s special hobby—she was roleplaying again !

Fine, I’ll play along.

« That’s right, Katsuyu, I will perform a technique to block your summoning time limit in a moment. But can you not be so polite, don’t call me Lord Shin, just call me Shin. »

A trace of pleasure flashed in the small Katsuyu’s eyes, and she happily agreed : « No problem, Lord Shin ! »

« ... »
