# Chapitre 195 — Divine Departure

« A dog... barking ? »

Ōtsutsuki Toneri’s body trembled violently, as if he had heard the most absurd blasphemy in the world.

He was a god who controlled the Giant Tenseigan and could easily erase the Ninja World.

He was a supreme being carrying the purest bloodline of the Ōtsutsuki Clan ; how could he be humiliated to his face ?

« Brat, I will tear you into ten thousand pieces ! »

In the next moment, both he and the Giant Tenseigan turned into afterimages, vanishing instantly from the spot.

When they reappeared, they were already hundreds of meters away.

Only after putting distance between himself and Asakawa Shin did a sense of security well up in his heart ; otherwise, he felt as if the Tenseigan would be forcibly snatched away by the other party.

As the powerful Tenseigan ocular power continuously poured into his body, Toneri gradually regained his usual composure.

Under Asakawa Shin’s slightly surprised gaze, an emerald glow suddenly erupted around Toneri’s body as he entered the Tenseigan Chakra Mode.

« Oh ? The Giant Tenseigan can also activate such a form ? I’ve underestimated you. »

Sensing the surprise in Asakawa Shin’s eyes, a smug, cruel smile curled at the corners of Toneri’s mouth.

« Stupid fool, you shouldn’t have given me the chance to charge up. Use your life as a sacrifice for this arrogance ! »

Toneri slowly extended his right hand, and a thick, deep green Chakra instantly covered it.

In the center of his palm, a green orb emitting an eerie suction force gradually condensed, then shot out like a bolt of lightning.

This was the unique ocular technique of the Tenseigan, which could not only devour and neutralize enemy Ninjutsu but also pierce through an opponent’s body and remotely extract their Chakra.

Facing this highly threatening blow, Asakawa Shin remained calm and collected, merely tossing out a flying thunder god kunai as a casual response.

Seeing the opponent underestimate him so much, Toneri snorted coldly, a sneer appearing on his lips : « What a fool, actually thinking a Kunai can block a Tenseigan ocular technique... »

However, before he could finish his sentence, the moment the light orb collided with the Kunai, the terrifyingly powerful green orb simply vanished into thin air.

This sudden turn of events cut short Toneri’s unspoken mockery ; his upper body jerked forward, his mouth opening as if to curse but stopping short.

« How is this possible ? »

« Scared ? This is called Flying Thunder God : Guiding Thunder, brat. »

Asakawa Shin’s contemptuous attitude and the ease with which he neutralized the Tenseigan technique completely shattered Toneri’s pride.

He snapped his head up, his remaining Chakra erupting like boiling lava, sweeping across the surroundings.

« Bastard, how dare you look down on me ! »

Toneri let out a beast-like roar, raising his arms high above his head.

The Truth-Seeking Orbs floating behind him instantly disintegrated, turning into countless brilliant blades of light that gathered and compressed frantically in the air, finally forming a giant Chakra light sword that spanned the horizon.

« Die, you bastard ! Golden Wheel Reincarnation Explosion ! »

The blade stretched to the end of his vision, its entire length burning with a golden flame that could destroy everything.

Where the blade pointed, the void seemed to be torn apart, letting out a tooth-aching explosion.

The moon’s gravitational field became completely chaotic under this terrifying power ; surrounding rocks and dust rose into the air, swallowed and crushed by the violent gravity of the light sword.

« Oh ? Finally bringing out something decent ? »

Asakawa Shin’s originally lazy expression suddenly tightened, replaced by a look of gravity.

He keenly sensed the terrifying energy contained within that light sword—a destructive force capable of easily shattering continental plates that made one’s heart skip a beat.

Yet, instead of any fear, a long-lost, blazing battle intent ignited in his eyes.

« Since you want to play big, I’ll play along to your heart’s content. »

He suddenly drew the kusanagi sword from his waist, and a massive amount of Armament Haki and Haoshoku Haki instantly wrapped around the blade, turning into countless violent black-red lightning snakes slithering wildly.

A heart-palpitating pressure swept across the area ; the Ōtsutsuki clansmen watching from afar felt their breath hitch, and they immediately scattered in flight without looking back.

Toneri didn’t give him any more time to prepare ; he swung his arms forward, and the giant light sword spanning the horizon let out a deafening roar, carrying a momentum that could destroy everything as it slashed down at Asakawa Shin !

« This is the Tenseigan’s ultimate secret technique—Golden Wheel Reincarnation Explosion ! This is the true divine power of the Ōtsutsuki Clan ! Now die ! »

« Divine power ? Then you’ve found the right person ! This move of mine is called—Divine Departure ! »

Asakawa Shin gave a low shout, and the kusanagi sword in his hand slashed out violently.

A massive black-red crescent slash burst through the air, carrying a world-destroying momentum as it met the descending golden light sword head-on !

« Boom— ! ! ! »

Two world-destroying forces collided violently in mid-air.

There was no earth-shattering explosion ; instead, there was a tooth-aching tearing sound.

The golden light sword and the black-red slash were at a stalemate in the sky ; golden destructive power and black-red domineering power intertwined, collided, and annihilated each other frantically.

The sky was divided into black and gold, crushing against each other, finally turning into a terrifying shockwave that swept through the surroundings.

The surface of the moon began to collapse under this terrifying impact.

Giant cracks spread rapidly like spiderwebs, and the once-solid lunar palace instantly turned to dust.

Surrounding peaks were leveled in the blink of an eye, and giant rocks were swept into the energy storm, vaporizing instantly.

« How is this possible ? ! This was a strike capable of splitting the moon, yet it was blocked ? »

Toneri’s voice was hoarse, his face filled with disbelief.

He clearly sensed that his pride and joy, the Golden Wheel Reincarnation Explosion, was actually being eaten away and dismantled bit by bit by that black-red slash !

« This is divine power ? Nothing more than this ! »

Asakawa Shin snorted coldly, exerting force with his right hand once more.

In an instant, the black-red slash suddenly expanded, and an even more violent burst of Haki and Psychokinesis erupted, forcing the golden light sword to retreat steadily !

« Break for me ! »

With a thunderous shout, the black-red lightning exploded, instantly devouring the golden light sword completely.

Toneri let out a shrill scream as he was sent flying by the violent impact, crashing heavily into a distant mountain wall, coughing up blood.

The Golden Wheel Reincarnation Explosion was completely shattered at this moment.

« Heh, what a weak god ! »

Toneri had been struggling to stay upright.

Hearing this bone-chilling mockery, a sweetness hit his throat, his blood surged backward, and he finally couldn’t hold on, spitting out another mouthful of blood.

He collapsed to the ground and lost consciousness.

Meanwhile, in the Ninja World below, many Ninjas noticed something strange about the moon.

« Today’s moonlight seems a bit off. »

« Indeed, that light just now was a bit too blinding. »

Asakawa Shin slowly sheathed his sword, his gaze sweeping over the unconscious Toneri in the ruins with no emotion in his eyes, as if he had merely swatted away an eyesore of a fly.

The dust on the moon’s surface slowly settled, and a deathly silence filled the surroundings.

In the distance, those Ōtsutsuki clansmen who had luckily survived were already scared out of their wits, cowering among the ruins, not even daring to breathe loudly.

Asakawa Shin ignored them, walking straight toward the floating Giant Tenseigan, his fingers suddenly clenching in the air.

In an instant, the unconscious Toneri in the pool of blood in the distance rose into the air like a puppet with its strings cut, being pulled clumsily toward his palm.

« All elements are present. Begin the upgrade ! »
