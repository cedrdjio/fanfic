# Chapitre 104 — Clear Sky

« The Four-Tails' Chakra is actually... just like the Five-Tails', what on earth is going on... »

A full month had passed since that hearty battle with the Four-Tails, yet Asakawa Shin had only digested less than a tenth of the Five-Tails' Chakra within his body.

To his surprise, testing revealed that the Four-Tails' Chakra was in the same situation.

He carefully inspected his body internally but didn’t notice any discomfort ; instead, he felt an unprecedented sense of comfort and fullness.

« Since it’s not a problem with my body, does that mean the root cause lies within the Tailed Beast Chakra itself ? »

A sharp light flashed in Asakawa Shin’s eyes as he harbored a faint suspicion ; perhaps this wasn’t a bad thing.

Regardless, he wouldn’t be able to leave the Village for the next few months because Hanako’s due date was in a few days. He had to dedicate more time to accompanying her.

However, it didn’t seem like he was needed much at the moment. Hanako was never short of people caring for her. Kenji was one thing, but how could even...

« Naruto, don’t you have school today ? Why did you run all the way here ? »

Asakawa Shin stared at the blonde boy before him with a look of utter confusion, unable to fathom why he was there.

Naruto turned his head, saw it was Asakawa Shin, and immediately broke into a simple, honest smile. Scratching his head, he grinned widely and said :

« I have to thank you for the Ninjutsu you taught me ! That Shadow Clone Technique is just too useful !

When I told him I wanted him to help take care of Aunt Hanako, he actually shook his head repeatedly and insisted I come myself, then he ran off to school.

Shin, why didn’t you tell me sooner that you had such an amazing Ninjutsu~ »

« Huh ? »

Asakawa Shin was stunned. A Shadow Clone that fought to go to school ?

For real ? Why is its style completely different from my Shadow Clone...

« Wait, Naruto, that’s not the point. Hanako is about to give birth ; why are you more anxious than I am ? »

As soon as Asakawa Shin spoke, Naruto replied with a solemn expression, « Shin, that’s because I’m going to be an older brother !

Do you understand that feeling ? It’s like suddenly feeling a heavy weight of responsibility on your shoulders. »

While the two were chatting excitedly, Hanako suddenly clutched her abdomen and cried out, « Oh... oh no, this feeling... I think it’s time. Go get the doctor, quick ! »

« What ? »

The three men in the room were instantly struck with panic.

Asakawa Shin immediately broke into a jog, heading straight for the Konoha Hospital Director’s office. He grabbed Yakushi Nono, who was still processing official business, and pulled her along, urging her anxiously as they went :

« Quick, quick, quick ! Hanako is giving birth ! Hanako is giving birth ! »

Before long, the entire Konoha Hospital was alerted...

As Hanako was wheeled into the delivery room, dozens of female doctors and nurses followed close behind, entering the room in a staggering display of force.

At the door, Asakawa Shin tightly gripped Yakushi Nonou’s hand and requested solemnly, « I’m counting on you now, Yakushi Nonou ! »

Yakushi Nonou nodded solemnly in response, « Rest assured, Lord Shin. The best obstetricians in Konoha Hospital are all here ; it will definitely be fine. Now, I’ll go in to prepare. »

As the delivery room doors slammed shut, Asakawa Shin gradually calmed down.

With the best delivery room, the most advanced equipment, and the most elite medical team all here, nothing should go wrong.

Furthermore, with him personally overseeing things, his Medical Ninjutsu would be enough to handle any general emergencies.

At that thought, Asakawa Shin let out a long sigh of relief and slumped onto a bench in the hallway.

However, while he relaxed, the other two remained restless, pacing back and forth in the hallway and sighing constantly, leaving Asakawa Shin somewhat speechless.

'Wait, what’s with this guy Naruto ? I keep feeling like there’s something off about him... is it just my imagination ?'

...

Time ticked away in agony. Just as Asakawa Shin couldn’t help but hold his breath, the loud cry of a baby drifted out from the delivery room, causing him to spring to his feet.

The delivery room doors opened, and the three of them immediately crowded around, asking anxiously, « How is it ? What’s the situation ? »

Seeing them like this, Yakushi Nonou couldn’t help but smile slightly and said lightheartedly, « Don’t worry, both mother and daughter are safe. Congratulations ! The Asakawa family has been blessed with a daughter ! »

Kenji’s grin was so wide he couldn’t close his mouth. He had been hoping for a daughter, and now that his dream had come true, he had both a son and a daughter—a perfect family.

Asakawa Shin and Naruto were also overjoyed. Great, it was a cute little sister rather than a mischievous little brother ; a sense of happiness was about to overflow.

After all, if it had been a brother, considering their own personalities, just thinking about it made them feel exhausted.

In his ecstasy, Asakawa Shin waved his hand and produced a thick stack of red envelopes, thrusting them into the hands of every medical staff member with an attitude so firm they couldn’t refuse.

Soon, Hanako and the newborn were wheeled out. Though Hanako looked exhausted, she couldn’t hide her joy ; at this moment, she looked like a victorious female general, full of pride.

Asakawa Shin quickly took his little sister and examined her closely. Although the newborn’s skin was still slightly red and her features hadn’t fully developed...

...he was still delighted to find that her eyes and brows bore a striking resemblance to Hanako’s. This completely set his mind at ease ; great, his sister really took after their mother.

Naruto also hurried forward, looking at the little infant in her swaddling clothes with delight, chanting incessantly :

« Little Momo, hello ! I’m Big Brother Naruto. Remember, it’s Big Brother Naruto~ »

« Little Momo ? Naruto, is that her name ? » Asakawa Shin raised his head, full of suspicion and confusion.

Naruto asked back in confusion, « Huh ? Yeah. Uncle Kenji said if it was a girl, she’d be named Asakawa Momo. What, Shin, you didn’t know ? »

« Ha... haha, how could I not know ? I was just testing you, Naruto. »

Asakawa Shin gave a dry laugh and quickly changed the subject, cursing inwardly : 'That damned old man, not telling me something this important. He should have at least let me give some input.'

Not long after, Hanako fell into a deep sleep.

Asakawa Shin also gave Little Momo a full-body examination. Good, she was very healthy.

Or rather, she was overly healthy, which resulted in the baby being quite heavy. No wonder she took so long in the delivery room.

Asakawa Shin affectionately cradled the tiny life in his arms and whispered softly in her ear :

« Little Momo, remember, I’m Big Brother Shin, your only biological brother. Don’t forget~ »

As if she understood, Little Momo actually let out a burst of giggles.

Until Yakushi Nonou came over to urge him : « Quickly, hand the little one over to me. The routine check-up isn’t finished yet. »

Asakawa Shin only then reluctantly hand his sister over. His arms suddenly felt empty, and a sense of loss welled up in his heart.

Staring at the tender little face in the swaddling clothes, an unprecedented flutter of emotion surged in his heart :

'Naruto was right ; this feeling of shouldering responsibility really is heavy.

Don’t worry, Little Momo.

Even if the Ninja World becomes turbulent and full of disasters in the future, your brother will definitely give his all to hold up a clear sky for you !'
