# Chapitre 16 — Walnuts

In the Hokage Office, Hiruzen Sarutobi heard the Anbu report and couldn’t help but twitch the corner of his eye.

« This kid Asakawa Shin finally decided to come ; he really treats everything so casually. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi still remembered that night when this kid asked Iruka to hand over the Konoha Six Styles training scroll to him.

He was very happy and promised to allow Asakawa Shin to choose one Ninjutsu from the scroll of seals as a reward, letting Iruka tell him to come and get it when he was free.

This was, of course, just polite talk. Usually, when he said that, no matter what Ninja it was, they would immediately drop what they were doing and come.

But that day, he waited until late at night and no one came. It wasn’t until the Anbu informed him that Asakawa Shin had already gone to sleep that he left the Hokage Building. He still remembered that the wind was very strong that night.

Several days passed, and it wasn’t until today, Saturday, that he finally hoped for the person to arrive. It really was « come only when free. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi shook his head. Forget it, after all, he was just a child and was also his own fan, so of course, he chose to forgive him.

« Let him in. » The Anbu nodded and disappeared on the spot.

Before long, Asakawa Shin walked in leisurely, looking around curiously. « So this is the Hokage Office ? »

The office was very spacious, with only a few rows of bookshelves and a huge desk. On the desk were many files and a crystal ball.

Behind the desk was a row of windows with a very good view. Looking out, one could take in the entire Konoha Village.

'Not bad, but the decoration is too simple, and the layout is too monotonous. When I become Hokage, I’ll tidy it up properly.'

Hiruzen Sarutobi watched Asakawa Shin walk in, wandering around and looking about as if no one else was there, nodding from time to time, and shaking his head from time to time. He couldn’t help but ask curiously, « What are you doing ? »

« Oh, I was thinking about how to decorate this office when I become the Hokage in the future. »

Asakawa Shin answered without hesitation. After speaking, he realized something was wrong and covered his mouth half a beat late.

Hiruzen Sarutobi looked at the funny kid in front of him with black lines on his forehead. Although many children in the Village dreamed of becoming Hokage, he was the second one to say it so confidently in front of him.

The first was, of course, Naruto, but even Naruto wasn’t this off-the-wall ; he hadn’t even graduated from the Ninja academy and was already thinking about how to decorate the Hokage Office.

Thinking of these two « Sleeping Dragon and Young Phoenix, » Hiruzen Sarutobi couldn’t help but get a headache.

« Alright, alright, leave the decoration for when you become Hokage in the future. Don’t mess around now. Grandpa is old and has feelings for these things. By the way, what are you here for today ? »

Hiruzen Sarutobi was afraid this kid would start joking around again, so he quickly pulled the topic back.

« Oh, Third Grandpa, didn’t you say I could choose one Ninjutsu from the scroll of seals ? I came today to see if there is a Ninjutsu suitable for me. »

Asakawa Shin finally remembered why he came today and quickly replied : « My Taijutsu training is already on the right track, and I just need to practice step by step, but my foundation in Ninjutsu is too weak. I want to pick a powerful Ninjutsu. »

« Oh ? So that’s what it is. It’s been so long that Grandpa almost forgot. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi waved his hand to the side, and an Anbu Ninja immediately appeared beside Asakawa Shin, holding a scroll in his hand.

« This is the catalog of the scroll of seals. It records many powerful Ninjutsu and even forbidden techniques. The names, effects, training difficulty, and most importantly, the cost of performing these techniques are all clearly stated. Take a look and see which one you choose. »

Asakawa Shin took the catalog and opened it to check : [Multiple Shadow Clone Jutsu], [Spirit Transformation Technique], [Eight Gates], [Mutually Multiplying Explosive Tags], [Edo Tensei], [Flying Thunder God Technique], [Darkness Genjutsu], [Reaper Death Seal].

Hiss... so many forbidden techniques. They are all great ; I want them all.

Huh, no Four Symbols Seal ? Is this because he’s afraid I’ll accidentally unseal the Nine-Tailed ? There is also no Wood Release Ninjutsu I want. This catalog is probably incomplete ; it only contains a portion of the Ninjutsu that have been selected and allowed for me to practice.

Asakawa Shin suspected that even if he chose a Ninjutsu like the Reaper Death Seal, Hiruzen Sarutobi would find an excuse to push it off and wouldn’t let him learn it.

Asakawa Shin pondered internally. The Multiple Shadow Clone Jutsu is very good, but in the future, it will most likely be given to Naruto. When the time comes, I can just learn it from Naruto ; I don’t need it right now anyway.

After all, his own training is improved step-by-step from the system, with an incredibly solid foundation, so he doesn’t need these unorthodox methods.

The Eight Gates is the same. In a few days, I’ll pull Rock Lee into the Six Styles Training Class and learn it from Might Guy in the future, so I don’t need to waste this opportunity.

Besides, relying on his own talent and effort, he would still be invincible in the world without the Eight Gates.

The Edo Tensei technique recorded in the scroll of seals should be the version researched by the Second Hokage, not yet improved and updated by Orochimaru. It’s not worth much to redeem it, pass.

As for the rest of the techniques, except for the Flying Thunder God Technique, they are of no great use to him, pass.

However, although the Flying Thunder God is very strong and cool, it is not what he needs most at the moment. He is a Ninja who specializes in Taijutsu and swordsmanship, and with the Observation Haki that predicts the future, speed is not his weakness.

Not to mention a Flying Thunder God user, he even has the strength to fight against Admiral Kizaru, who has the ability of the Glint-Glint Fruit.

Learning the Flying Thunder God can only make up for his weakness in long-range mobility and increase some safety ; the cost-effectiveness is not very high.

Sure enough, what he wants most is still Wood Release.

Which man doesn’t want to pilot a giant robot ? Understanding the use of « Divine Departure » with the wood man ? But there is no Wood Release Ninjutsu on this scroll ?

Damn Hiruzen Sarutobi, what should I do ?

« That... Third Grandpa, is this scroll wrong ? Why is there no Wood Release ? » Asakawa Shin decided to ask directly.

With his current strength, he is enough to protect himself. Unless Hiruzen Sarutobi has gone crazy, there is no reason to push a potential talent like him to the opposite side.

Hiruzen Sarutobi was a bit strange. Why did he ask about Wood Release out of the blue ? He looked at Asakawa Shin’s innocent eyes and couldn’t help but soften his heart, patiently explaining :

« Little Shin, Wood Release is not an ordinary Ninjutsu, but a Bloodline Limit. Ordinary people can’t use it. And Wood Release is not a forbidden technique, so it is not in the scroll of seals. »

« But I am not an ordinary person. I want to learn Wood Release to protect Konoha and everyone, just like the First Hokage. » Asakawa Shin blinked his big eyes and said oath-boundly.

« Hahaha, what a good child. But Little Shin, you don’t know what a Bloodline Limit is, right ? The so-called Bloodline Limit is... holy crap ! »

Hiruzen Sarutobi hadn’t finished speaking when he stood up in shock, his eyes almost popping out.

He pointed a trembling finger at Asakawa Shin, « You... you have Wood Release ? »

Asakawa Shin ignored him, controlling the surging life force in his body and gathering it in his hands.

A tender sprout grew rapidly, bloomed, bore fruit, and produced two walnuts.

Asakawa Shin caught the walnuts in his hands, and the skin and pulp of the walnuts automatically detached, revealing the kernel inside.

« Third Grandpa, these two walnuts are for you. You can roll them around when you are free ; it can prevent dementia. Look, you even forgot what I came here for today. »
