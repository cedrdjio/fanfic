# Chapitre 169 — Double Lariat

« Stop ! We can talk... »

As soon as Danzo was resurrected, he roared out impatiently.

Asakawa Shin swung his blade backhandedly, and the surroundings instantly returned to a dead silence.

« I intended to be an undercover agent by the Masked Man’s side ; I didn’t truly betray Konoha... »

Another impatient strike fell.

« Wait... don’t kill me. I possess a lot of intelligence on the Masked Man ; I’m still useful to Konoha... »

Asakawa Shin sneered and interrupted, « Danzo, what are you babbling about ? I don’t eat beef ! »

Before his voice faded, a massive sword qi rose into the sky again.

« No ! Monkey, Teacher, spare my life... »

As Danzo was repeatedly executed at his respawn point by Asakawa Shin, the resentment and anger in his eyes gradually dissipated, replaced by a bone-deep despair and numbness.

Asakawa Shin silently calculated the time ; besides the Mangekyo Sharingan, Danzo now only had one Three-Tomoe Sharingan left.

He raised an eyebrow, his tone full of mockery, « It seems your Revive Coins are about to run out. »

Danzo was resurrected once more, his face as gloomy as water as he stared fixedly at Asakawa Shin, though his eyes were filled with unconcealable terror.

This time he learned his lesson ; the moment he appeared, he activated Izanagi and frantically pushed his Mangekyo Sharingan, nearly draining every bit of ocular power to manifest the third stage of Susanoo in a very short time.

Asakawa Shin still casually raised the sharp sword in his hand.

As the kusanagi sword swung down, a massive sword qi was generated out of thin air, slashing fiercely towards Danzo’s Susanoo.

That sword qi stood towering, several times taller than the entire Susanoo.

In the eyes of the onlookers, Danzo’s current resistance was simply like a mantis trying to stop a chariot, appearing comical and ridiculous.

The sword qi was as fast as a thunderclap, arriving before him in an instant.

Danzo endured the intense pain from his eyes and pushed his Susanoo with all his might.

The armored giant suddenly pulled out a string of Magatama and threw them forcefully at the sword qi, the collision between the two triggering a violent explosion.

However, the sword qi only faltered for a brief moment before breaking through the obstruction effortlessly, continuing its rapid charge toward Danzo.

Danzo’s pupils shook violently, and he hurriedly urged the Susanoo giant to block with its arm, attempting a final death struggle.

Unfortunately, it was all in vain.

The sword qi sliced through his Susanoo like a hot knife through butter, sweeping his body into the storm of sword qi and instantly shredding it into pieces.

Not far away, Raikage Ai and the Eight-Tails Jinchuriki Killer Bee exchanged a knowing glance.

On the field now, the Two-Tails Jinchuriki Yugito had long since disappeared, Kazekage Rasa’s fate was unknown, and Orochimaru’s trio didn’t even bother to act anymore, simply standing aside to watch the show, while One-Tail Shukaku had been moved to the outskirts by Jiraiya.

At this moment, only Danzo remained... still fighting alongside them.

Although the two were dissatisfied with Danzo’s earlier attempt to flee, they couldn’t just stand by and watch their last ally die a miserable death.

Raikage Ai suddenly burst forth, instantly entering Lightning Release Chakra Mode, his hair standing on end, clearly entering his full-power state.

The Eight-Tails Killer Bee didn’t hesitate either ; Gyuki’s Chakra erupted suddenly, coating him in a Tailed Beast Cloak armor.

The brothers looked at each other and charged towards Asakawa Shin without hesitation.

With the enhancement of the Lightning Release Chakra and the Tailed Beast Cloak, the two were as fast as lightning ; with a « zip » sound, they crossed hundreds of meters and arrived in front of Asakawa Shin in an instant.

They frantically gathered surging Chakra into their right arms and, with a thunderous roar, aimed straight for Asakawa Shin’s neck.

« Double Lariat ! »

The rest of the Konoha Ninjas finally snapped out of it, and Tsunade, unable to suppress the worry in her heart, shouted out, « Junior brother, watch out ! »

However, Asakawa Shin remained composed ; his Observation Haki, which could predict the future, had already seen this scene clearly.

This move, Double Lariat, might truly one-shot anyone else, but for him, it was nothing more than a futile effort.

« Iron Body ! »

Asakawa Shin’s loud shout echoed through the field as he intended to withstand that lethal Double Lariat with this move in front of everyone.

The Raikage brothers' eyes lit up, and they added a bit more force with perfect coordination.

With a loud « boom, » their hand-swords slashed fiercely onto Asakawa Shin’s neck.

To their shock, however, Asakawa Shin’s neck was as hard as steel ; their full-power strike hit it without leaving even a single scratch.

Their pupils constricted suddenly as they realized Asakawa Shin’s neck had already turned pitch black, with bolts of lightning swirling around it, remaining completely unharmed under their full-force assault.

Not only that, but the two of them instantly went from a super-high-speed state to a standstill ; the massive inertia and backlash sent their blood churning, causing them immense pain.

« Is that it ? It’s kind of itchy. »

Asakawa Shin suddenly grinned, and in the next instant, he vanished into thin air with the two of them, transferring to the outskirts of Konoha.

« Ir... Iron Body ? »

The crowd on the scene finally broke out into a clamor of discussion.

« Shin used Iron Body just now, didn’t he ? I knew it, this move is definitely a divine defensive skill. »

« I told you Guys before, but you wouldn’t believe me. »

« Look, it can even withstand that legendary Double Lariat, and he’s even unscathed. »

Hearing Might Guy say this, Kakashi also nodded in embarrassment, « You’re right, Guy. I was ignorant before. »

He scratched his head, his eyes darting around as he muttered, « It seems... I should also practice this Iron Body move well. If I master it, wouldn’t I be able to block any attack ? Wouldn’t that be invincible ? »

While everyone was marveling, Danzo was resurrected once more in a corner where no one was paying attention.

His eyes darted around, wanting to take the opportunity to escape.

But in the next moment, Uchiha Itachi and Uchiha Sasuke blocked his path.

Not only that, but Hiruzen Sarutobi, Might Guy, and the others also reacted and slowly surrounded him.

Hiruzen Sarutobi’s lips moved as if he still wanted to say something.

Uchiha Itachi narrowed his eyes and spoke first, « Everyone, my brother and I have a blood feud with this traitor for the death of our father. Could you leave his head to us ? »

Shisui also finished off the remaining enemies at this moment and walked over slowly.

« Indeed, this man murdered many members of my Uchiha Clan and used our clan’s Sharingan as consumables ; it’s simply unforgivable. Please give me some face and let us handle it. »

Upon hearing this, Hiruzen Sarutobi’s face turned pale and then flushed ; after hesitating for a long time, he finally let out a long sigh and retreated dejectedly to the side.

Senju Tobirama originally intended to clean house personally, but after a moment of silence, he eventually withdrew his Flying Thunder God Kunai.

Witnessing this, a hint of sorrow crossed Danzo’s heart.

He gave Hiruzen Sarutobi one last deep look, then unhesitatingly activated Susanoo again, launching a desperate charge toward the three Uchihas.

However, at this time, his Chakra and ocular power were nearly exhausted ; how could he be a match for Itachi, Shisui, and Sasuke ?

Danzo knew he was doomed today, so he steeled his heart and sacrificed Fugaku’s Mangekyo to forcibly cast Izanagi for a final struggle.

Unfortunately, it was all just a deathbed struggle.

After exhausting the ocular power of both Mangekyo, the Hashirama cells in his body finally went completely out of control ; his flesh writhed frantically, continuously sprouting branches and growing new leaves.

« No— ! »

Under this agony comparable to being sliced alive, he let out a roar of despair and stumbled toward the three Uchihas.

« At least... I’ll... take you all with me. »

« Reverse Four Symbols Sealing, open ! »

Blood and ink sprayed from all over his body, and finally, with a wild laugh, he successfully sealed Sasuke’s Shadow Clone.

« Hahaha, you naturally evil Uchiha brat, come to hell with me ! »
