# Chapitre 97 — The Hongmen Banquet

Asakawa Shin looked at the mess all over the battlefield and scratched his head : « Oops, it seems I went a bit overboard. Oh well, let’s just clean it up ! »

« Fire Release : Great Fireball Technique ! »

His hands quickly formed seals, and he spat out a huge fireball toward the corpses of the Xuanyuan Group on the ground, incinerating everything in an instant.

Now, let alone trying to search for any Secret Insect Manipulation Techniques, he couldn’t even find a single scrap of material if he wanted to use the Edo Tensei technique.

Asakawa Shin then clapped his hands and relaxed completely.

The Raikage and the others heard the commotion and rushed out of the house, assuming a combat stance. However, looking around, they only saw Asakawa Shin. They couldn’t help but ask in confusion, « What happened ? Was there an enemy attack just now ? »

Asakawa Shin nodded affirmatively, then jerked his chin toward the front : « There, look, those five piles of ash are the Xuanyuan Group. They still hadn’t given up and wanted to take your life. »

« But you’re our big client. How could I let you be killed right in front of me ? So I struck first and dealt with them. »

After hearing this, Karai’s face was full of disbelief. He blurted out in horror, « What ? You’re saying those attackers were the Xuanyuan Group ? The same Xuanyuan Group that pushed us to the brink ? They were actually... in such a short time... »

Asakawa Shin waved his hand casually and replied nonchalantly, « Yeah, it was that Xuanyuan Group. I thought they were really something, but I didn’t expect to finish them off with just two moves. »

« I think you guys just had bad luck. Once you know their intel, they’re simply incredibly weak. »

After saying that, he turned to the Raikage and said with a solemn expression, « Uncle Raikage, please rest assured. »

« Since you are our client, your safety is on me during this period. Just focus on receiving treatment. »

« However, please fulfill what you promised us as soon as possible. »

Karai still seemed not to have recovered from the shock. He opened his mouth to say something more, but was crudely interrupted by the grim-faced Raikage :

« Enough, Karai ! Your injuries haven’t healed yet, so speak less. I’ll take you back to your room to rest. »

After speaking, the Raikage gave Asakawa Shin a deep look, then forcibly dragged the unresponsive Karai back into the house.

It wasn’t until several hours later that the Hidden Cloud Village’s support troops finally arrived in a hurry.

The commander of this team was none other than Dodai. He bowed respectfully to the Raikage and reported :

« Lord Raikage, we have already obtained intelligence on the enemy. We will definitely be able to take them down this time. When we hand them over to the Torture and Interrogation Force, we can... »

Before he could finish, the Raikage’s face had already turned as black as the bottom of a pot. He said gloomily :

« There is no need for that. The Xuanyuan Group has long been burned to ashes. You arrived too late ! »

« But no matter. You stay stationed here for a few days. After these Konoha brats heal my injuries, I will host a banquet for them. »

« At that time, I will use a smashed cup as a signal. You will then immediately rush in and capture them all ! »

« Tsunade has developed hemophobia ; she is no longer a threat. »

« As for that Uchiha kid, he has actually awakened the Mangekyo Sharingan. His strength cannot be underestimated. I will handle him personally when the time comes. »

« And that kid with the grass growing on his head—being able to annihilate the Xuanyuan Group in a short time is no easy feat even with an intelligence advantage. He must have some skill. »

« He should be the new disciple the Hokage took in. Besides, that plant on his head concerns me. He and the other two women will be left to you. »

« Remember, you must capture them alive. I have a feeling this is our best opportunity. »

Dodai’s expression changed slightly. Was this a declaration of war against Konoha ? But after thinking it over, he felt there was indeed value in taking action, and their chances of success were high. He nodded solemnly to the Raikage and turned to leave to make the arrangements.

Soon, after a few days, the Hidden Cloud Village indeed sent the thunder-clap grass through the Heavenly Transfer Technique. Not only did they send a whole large crate, but the quality was also top-notch.

Tsunade immediately began preparing the medicine, making a whole large vat in one go—enough for several years of use !

At the same time, the Explosive Bug inside the Raikage had grown to a point where surgery could be performed. Shizune was still the primary surgeon for this operation.

Since the Explosive Bug was only the size of a soybean, the difficulty of the surgery was greatly reduced, and the damage to the patient’s body was minimal. Not long after the surgery, the Raikage was already able to walk as if flying.

He tentatively circulated his Chakra slightly. After feeling that everything was normal, he turned to Tsunade and said sincerely :

« I truly owe you this time. As expected of a world-renowned medical Ninja. Even the disciple you trained has medical skills far beyond the medical Ninjas of my Hidden Cloud Village. »

Pausing for a moment, a glint flashed in his eyes as his tone turned warm :

« By the way, I know you enjoy fine wine. To express my gratitude, how about I host a dinner tonight ? Let’s go to that tavern where we first met. I’ll set up a grand banquet for everyone. Please be sure to grace us with your presence ! »

Tsunade felt a bit of a craving upon hearing this. She had been to that tavern twice ; it was very high-end, and each visit cost over ten million Ryo.

Thinking of this, the corners of her mouth curled up, and she smiled with feigned modesty, « Oh my, how can I accept such a thing ? »

« However... »

« Since you’ve invited us so sincerely, if I were to refuse, wouldn’t it seem like I don’t know how to appreciate a gesture ? »

« Then it’s a date. We will definitely be there on time tonight. »

Beside her, a hint of suspicion flashed in Itachi’s eyes. He keenly noticed that the gazes of the surrounding Hidden Cloud Ninjas seemed a bit strange, carrying a hint of ill intent.

However, when he glanced at Asakawa Shin, who still looked relaxed and seemingly off-guard, he suppressed his doubts for the time being. He only secretly tensed his nerves to stay on guard.

After parting ways with the Hidden Cloud group, Tsunade’s expression suddenly turned grave. She said in a low voice, « Those Hidden Cloud barbarians are up to no good. Let’s run away now while they haven’t reacted yet. »

Asakawa Shin gave her a surprised look and said in shock, « Wait... Senior Sister, you actually knew ? Then why did you agree so readily ? I thought you couldn’t move your feet once you heard about fine wine. »

Tsunade rolled her eyes at him and feigned anger : « I was out making a name for myself when you didn’t even know where you were. How could I not see through the little schemes of those Hidden Cloud barbarians ? »

« I only agreed on purpose to mislead them. See, they’ve taken the bait now, which gives us the perfect chance to escape. »

Asakawa Shin scratched his head and smiled with a bit of embarrassment, « So Senior Sister had a plan all along. I thought you really didn’t notice. Impressive, impressive. »

Tsunade smiled confidently, patted her chest, and said proudly, « Of course. I usually just play dumb, but in fact, I’m sharp as a tack most of the time ! »

Asakawa Shin’s mouth twitched slightly. He thought to himself that this Senior Sister really knows how to take a compliment and run with it.

A sharp light flashed in his eyes as he suggested, « Senior Sister, actually, we don’t need to run at all. Let’s just go to the appointment tonight. We’ll eat and take whatever we can, giving him a huge bill and making him swallow his medicine in silence. »

Tsunade looked at him in surprise and said uncertainly, « So much confidence ? The old man said your Wood Release has reached mastery. Could it be true ? »

« Tell me the truth. If you’re not sure, we should make a strategic retreat now to avoid failing miserably later. That would be incredibly embarrassing. »

Hearing this, Asakawa Shin mimicked Tsunade by patting his chest and said heroically, « It’s fine. Senior Sister, just go wild. When the time comes, you just eat and drink as you please. Be as arrogant as you want. Don’t worry about anything else ; just leave the rest to me. »

After hearing this, Tsunade’s eyes flashed with excitement, and she laughed loudly, « Good, great ! My Junior Brother is invincible under heaven ! »
