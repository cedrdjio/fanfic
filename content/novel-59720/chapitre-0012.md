# Chapitre 12 — First, Bring a Cow, One Per Person

« A misunderstanding ? »

Hiashi did not know what was going on ; hearing this news, he actually felt a bit disappointed. He glared at his disappointing daughter and said to Asakawa Shin :

« Nephew, what exactly is going on ? Has there been a problem with Hinata’s training ? »

Asakawa Shin wiped the disheveled traces from his body. He did not rush to answer Hiashi’s question but instead asked in return :

« Uncle, is the Hyuga Clan experiencing major financial problems ? »

« Nephew, why do you say that ? I wouldn’t say the Hyuga Clan is overflowing with gold and jade, but we are certainly wealthy enough to be considered a major power. »

Hiashi looked at Asakawa Shin suspiciously.

Just now, in order to give this brat a show of authority, the table for receiving guests was bare—not to mention delicacies from land and sea, there weren’t even seasonal fruits, vegetables, or dried snacks.

Now, there was only a pot of dry tea in front of them. Could this brat be mocking me ?

« Oh ? Then I am a bit confused. The Hyuga Clan’s Gentle Fist Art is famous throughout the world ; surely you know about it ? »

« When training in taijutsu, one needs to consume sufficient nutrients to promote physical growth, which requires a large amount of high-protein food. But why is Hinata... ? »

Shin did not finish his sentence, but Hiashi already understood what he meant. He laughed loudly and said :

« Nephew, you don’t mean to say that Hinata is malnourished, do you ? Hahahaha, my daughter can eat two big bowls of rice every meal ; how could that be possible... »

Hiashi turned his head to look at Hinata, seemingly wanting to share something funny, but he saw Hinata looking at him pitifully. His laughter stalled :

« Hinata, why aren’t you laughing ? Wait, you... you don’t really mean you aren’t getting enough to eat, do you ? »

Hinata’s face flushed red, and she wanted to speak but hesitated.

Asakawa Shin sighed helplessly. This girl was good in every way, but her personality was too soft right now. In critical moments, it still had to be him.

« That’s right, Uncle. For us martial artists, the better our talent, the more we eat. And Hinata is a peerless genius, one in ten thousand ! »

« But being constantly underfed is undoubtedly a waste of her potential. I came here this time to talk to you about this matter. I truly cannot bear to see Hinata, this hidden gem, fall from grace. »

« Even if you say that... » Hiashi still had not recovered ; it turned out his own daughter had been starving all this time.

« Hinata, why didn’t you tell Father ? »

« I... I didn’t dare, and... it also doesn’t fit the etiquette of the Main House’s eldest daughter, » Hinata mumbled.

Hiashi felt a pang of heartache, and he couldn’t help but reflect on whether he had been too harsh on Hinata.

« How about this, Uncle ? Just with empty words, you might not understand what a peerless genius is. Why not have the kitchen prepare some food and let Hinata eat until she is full today ? »

« Hinata, eat as much as you want today. I want to see your limit. »

Asakawa Shin turned his head to comfort Hinata : « Don’t be shy. Being able to eat is a powerful talent. That is just how we peerless geniuses are. »

« By the way, Uncle, I didn’t have breakfast today either. I wonder... »

« Hahaha, Nephew, you are too polite. Why don’t I set up a banquet, and we can chat while we eat... »

Hiashi had not finished speaking when he was interrupted by Asakawa Shin : « Set up a table ? Who would that be enough for ? People might think we can’t afford to eat. First, bring a cow, one per person. »

Hiashi : « ? »

...

People came and went in the dining room. Dish after dish and snack after snack were brought up, and empty plates were taken away. There was also an attendant responsible for keeping records nearby.

Hiashi watched the two of them furiously devouring their food, dumbfounded. This brat was truly humble ; one cow was nowhere near enough for him.

And Hinata as well—it was as if he were meeting his daughter for the first time. Although she didn’t eat as much as Asakawa Shin, it was still extremely exaggerated.

Even an adult Ninja from the Akimichi Clan couldn’t compare to her. The key was that the Akimichi Clan had secret techniques to convert food into fat for storage, while Hinata was simply crushing it with sheer appetite.

How could such a tiny person eat food stacked up to be larger than her own body size ? Were their stomachs dimensional pouches ?

Hiashi had originally thought that the so-called not being full just meant missing a couple of bowls of rice. Now it seemed he had truly underestimated the heroes of the world.

A peerless genius, terrifying indeed.

« It’s bad, Clan Leader, the chef... the chef has fainted. »

The one who fainted was the Clan Leader’s private chef. He usually only had to be responsible for the meals needed by Hiashi’s family, and his culinary skills were superb.

He didn’t expect to take such a big order today ; even after giving it his all, he still couldn’t win.

Hearing this, Hiashi’s eye twitched : « First, gather a few chefs from the Branch House to fill in for me. »

...

« Clan Leader, it’s bad ! The food supplies stored in the clan have all been eaten ! »

« If it’s all eaten, then go buy more ! Why isn’t there more food stored in the clan ? »

« The food supplies in the clan are delivered by someone specially every day. Normally, it’s enough for a hundred people’s three meals ; it’s never finished in normal times. »

« Stop talking, go borrow some from the Branch House first ! »

« Uncle, no need. I’m almost full. » Asakawa Shin patted his stomach, indicating he was eighty percent full.

« Yes, Father, I... I am full too, » Hinata said, feeling a bit embarrassed. Being able to eat freely for the first time made her let go a bit, and she had shocked everyone.

However, the feeling of being full was really good ; Hinata was completely satisfied.

Hiashi finally breathed a sigh of relief. He beckoned to the side. The attendant responsible for recording and summarizing clicked away on an abacus, and it took a while before he figured it out and said :

« Reporting to the Clan Leader, the Eldest Miss ate about 50 servings of food, and Lord Shin ate 120 servings of food. »

Asakawa Shin was not surprised after hearing this. He knew that the future adult version of Hinata would win the Ichiraku Ramen competitive eating championship, and it would be a crushing victory.

She ate 46 bowls of ramen within a 30-minute time limit, a record that remained unbroken for many years.

« Only ate 50 servings of food ? Hinata, you need to eat more. You are at the age to grow. »

Shin expressed that he was not very satisfied with Hinata’s food intake ; she should keep up with him.

Hiashi felt ashamed. What had he starved his daughter into all these years ? While feeling a bit guilty, he was also somewhat excited. After all, according to Asakawa Shin, Hinata was a 'peerless genius'.

He had invested a lot of effort into Hinata over the years, but Hinata’s personality was weak, and her talent for the Gentle Fist was very average, making her unable to uphold the title of the Hyuga Clan’s young clan leader.

He had already prepared to train a 'new account'—Hanabi was born because of this.

« Um, Nephew, you said Hinata is a peerless genius, so does she have a lot of talent in taijutsu ? » Hiashi asked, a bit unsure. Hinata also became a bit nervous.

« Yes, Hinata is a one-in-ten-thousand peerless genius. Her talent in taijutsu is not below mine. »

Shin said without hesitation : « However... Hinata’s talent is in the Strong Fist Style. She shouldn’t practice the Gentle Fist anymore in the future. »

Hiashi : « ? »
