# Chapitre 13 — Sparring

« Stop practicing... Gentle Fist ? »

Asakawa Shin’s words echoed in Hiashi and Hinata’s ears, as if they had produced a reverberation, pounding against their minds over and over again.

« But, my dear nephew, the Gentle Fist is the foundation of our Hyuga Clan. » Hiashi said, unable to accept it.

Hinata nodded beside him. From the time she was old enough to understand, she had been instilled with the belief that she must inherit the Main House and carry forward the Gentle Fist.

Being told suddenly that she didn’t need to practice the Gentle Fist left her in a daze ; for a moment, she found it hard to believe.

« Then, may I ask, Uncle, what is Hinata’s aptitude for the Gentle Fist ? »

« Passable. » Hiashi recalled, not daring to boast.

Hinata lowered her head to the side. Hiashi was sparing her feelings ; that wasn’t what he said when he taught her in private.

« So, just average ? Although Hinata’s lack of proper nutrition plays a part, you can see it too, can’t you ? Her talent doesn’t lie there. »

Asakawa Shin said ruthlessly : « Besides, the ceiling for the Gentle Fist is fixed. Even if one practices it to the pinnacle, that’s all it amounts to. It’s hard to possess combat power that can rival a Kage-level Ninja. »

Hiashi’s expression turned ugly. Lies do not hurt people, but the truth is a sharp blade.

The principle of the Hyuga Clan’s Gentle Fist Art is to coordinate with the Byakugan’s perspective, injecting Chakra into the enemy’s body to directly damage the Chakra pathways, the « Tenketsu System, » and the enemy’s fragile internal organs, thereby sealing or obstructing the enemy’s Chakra, or even killing them.

Elite Ninjas of the Hyuga Clan are strong even among Jonin, and their utility is maxed out, but you can’t say there’s a Hyuga Ninja capable of threatening a Kage-level opponent.

Who among the Ninjas who have reached Kage-level combat power doesn’t have a few special tricks ? You can’t hit the ones who can fly with Gentle Fist, and you can’t penetrate the ones with 'Absolute Defense'.

As for the Jinchuriki of Tailed Beasts... just look at the Tailed Beast Chakra Mode and Tailed Beast Transformation. Gentle Fist can’t break their defense at all ; that little bit of injected Chakra is dispersed the moment it’s hit by the Tailed Beast’s Chakra.

As for the remaining Kage-level figures, some have mechanics so strong that Gentle Fist can’t compete, while others have stats so high that Gentle Fist can’t overcome them.

Looking across the Ninja World, the Gentle Fist can only be considered mid-tier. It is strong, but its strength is very limited.

Just like the chasm-like gap between the Byakugan and the Tenseigan, compared to the Eighty Gods Vacuum Attack, the Gentle Fist is as different as clouds and mud.

« Since Hinata’s prospects for learning the Gentle Fist are limited, why not let her practice the Strong Fist Style, which is more suitable for her ? That way, her talent won’t be wasted. »

Asakawa Shin struck while the iron was hot, continuing to persuade : « And I’m not asking her to completely abandon the family’s Gentle Fist.

It’s just that when she achieves more in the Strong Fist Style, she can combine it with the Gentle Fist Art from a higher vantage point, and perhaps forge her own path by balancing hardness and softness. »

Hiashi was finally moved, but he wasn’t so easily fooled. Although he had long heard of Asakawa Shin’s reputation as a genius, he had never seen it with his own eyes.

Since Asakawa Shin kept insisting that the Strong Fist Style was stronger than the Gentle Fist Style, it wouldn’t be too much to ask him to demonstrate, right ?

« My dear nephew, your reputation as a genius has spread throughout Konoha, and your self-created Konoha Six Styles are legendary. Who doesn’t know of them ? »

« However, it seems I’ve never seen you spar with anyone. It’s not that I don’t believe you, I just want to broaden my horizons. I wonder... »

« Uncle, that is exactly what I was thinking. Ever since my Taijutsu reached its peak, I haven’t had a chance to spar with anyone. The Hyuga Clan is filled with experts, and I’d like to see what level I’m at. »

A sharp light flashed in Asakawa Shin’s eyes. Since you want to weigh me, then I’ll let you see for yourself.

What a true, peerless genius is !

Shortly after, Neji and several Hyuga Ninjas of different levels were summoned before Hiashi ; the strongest among them was even a Jonin from the Main House.

« That is the situation. Neji, you are the recognized genius of our Hyuga Clan, and you are of a similar age, so you shall be the first to face Asakawa Shin.

It doesn’t matter if you lose ; just show the style of our Hyuga Clan’s Gentle Fist. »

Neji walked toward the training ground, his body somewhat stiff. Is this for real ? I’m fighting Shin ?

He had seen the performance Asakawa Shin put on at the Ninja Academy playground a few days ago. It wasn’t just him ; even those adult Ninjas might not be able to defeat Asakawa Shin.

'As expected of the hateful Main House...'

Asakawa Shin sized up the person who arrived. The first opponent is Neji ? A genius from the Hyuga Branch House, and he’s a year older than me.

If I were to instantly defeat Neji, it would be too disrespectful to Hiashi, especially since the other party just treated me to a grand feast.

I still understand the ways of the world. Oh well, I’ll play along with him for a bit.

On the training ground, the two performed the Seal of Confrontation.

The battle began. Neji did not dare to be arrogant and immediately created distance. He looked at Asakawa Shin with a solemn expression. This guy was striking such a relaxed pose—was it extreme self-confidence or a feint ? Even though his whole body was full of openings, it made Neji completely afraid to strike.

Asakawa Shin looked at Neji with some helplessness. This kid had been posturing for a long time and still wouldn’t start the fight.

If he were to make the first move, Neji might not be able to take even a casual strike. Forget it, I’ll try to provoke him.

« Why are you creating distance ? If I recall correctly, isn’t the Gentle Fist a close-quarters Ninjutsu/Taijutsu style ? You can’t attack me from that far away.

Bring it on. After all, if I make the first move, the battle will end in an instant. » Asakawa Shin said leisurely.

« You, don’t look down on people. » Neji took the bait of that taunt, and it was indeed highly effective ; he charged at Shin aggressively.

Strike after strike of the Eight Trigrams Palms were unleashed by Neji in anger, but they were easily seen through and dodged by Observation Haki. Shin moved through the gaps in Neji’s attacks as if he were a butterfly flitting through flowers.

'Can’t hit him, still can’t hit him. What’s going on with this guy ? He’s as slippery as a loach.'

'Damn it, always just a little bit off. Since you’re so good at dodging, I’ll increase the frequency of my attacks and block all your paths. Let’s see where you can run.'

Neji’s eyes narrowed, and he struck a pose, shouting : « Now ! »

Eight Trigrams Eight Palms.

Neji stepped forward, his hands moving so fast they created afterimages, transforming into eight palm strikes aimed at the vital points all over Asakawa Shin’s body.

Shin couldn’t help but twitch the corner of his mouth. Is this a super simplified version of the Eight Trigrams Sixty-Four Palms ? It really is kids fighting.

However, it is said that the Eight Trigrams Sixty-Four Palms is a secret of the Main House, and Neji, being from the Branch House, naturally had no qualifications to practice it. These eight palms must have been something Neji figured out on his own. Reaching this stage, he could be considered to have shown the style of the Gentle Fist, right ?

It’s time to end the show.

« Well done ! Paper Art. »

Shin’s body seemed to turn into paper in an instant, shedding all its strength and drifting away within the air currents of the incoming Eight Trigrams Palms, causing all of Neji’s attacks to miss by a hair’s breadth.

« Nani ? ! »

Neji was shocked. This was the first time he had seen the actual application of Paper Art. But before Neji could react, another voice came from behind him.

« Shave. »

'Oh no.' Neji had just had this thought when he felt a heavy blow to the back of his neck. In an instant, he was knocked to the ground by Shin. He tried to crawl but couldn’t get up.

Shin wiped the non-existent cold sweat from his forehead and exclaimed :

« That was close ! As expected of a genius of the Hyuga Clan. I was almost comboed to death by him. Good thing I was one step more skilled. »

Hearing this, Neji couldn’t hold on any longer. His vision went black, and he passed out.
