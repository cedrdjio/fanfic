# Chapitre 100 — Practicing the Flying Thunder God Technique

« Haha, Konoha, your emperor has returned. »

After two days of arduous travel, Asakawa Shin looked at the familiar gates of Konoha and couldn’t help but let out a cheer.

Yūgao blinked her large eyes and followed behind Asakawa Shin, asking curiously, « Lord Shin, what is an 'emperor' ? »

« Um... you don’t know ? Well, that makes sense... »

Asakawa Shin was momentarily stunned by the question, then it dawned on him.

On second thought, the Ninja World only had the division of power between Daimyō and Kage ; where would the concept of an emperor come from ?

He then explained patiently, « It’s something I read in an ancient book... »

« An emperor is the supreme ruler of a unified dynasty, possessing absolute power and status. »

« To put it simply, if a political power could sweep away all other forces in the Ninja World and incorporate the entire known world into its territory... »

« Then the supreme ruler of that power could be crowned as the Emperor. »

Yūgao nodded ignorantly, then said with a bit of admiration, « That sounds so amazing. Lord Shin, you know so much. »

'Emperor ! Unification ? ! Supreme power and status...'

Beside them, Uchiha Itachi’s pupils dilated in shock upon hearing this. His body trembled slightly from extreme excitement, and a massive wave of emotion surged within him :

'So, is this Lord Shin’s goal ? It is a grand undertaking beyond imagination !'

'While I am still obsessed with the gains and losses of a single clan or village, and fruitlessly searching for the so-called will of fire...'

'Lord Shin has already set his sights on the entire Ninja World. This level of vision is truly breathtaking.'

'But then again, only such a world-shaking hegemony that transcends past and present is worthy of Lord Shin’s pursuit, right ?'

'Compared to this, the title of Hokage seems far too insignificant ; it doesn’t match Lord Shin’s world-shattering talent at all.'

'No, my thoughts are too messy. I must discuss this thoroughly with Shisui when I get back. This is a monumental discovery !'

On the other side, Asakawa Shin had no idea that Itachi was having a wild internal monologue.

He was only thinking about the hardships of the journey and the long road ahead where he would inevitably have to collect Tailed Beast Chakra from all over the Ninja World.

Therefore, mastering the Flying Thunder God Technique had become his top priority.

After all, this technique was just too convenient...

Who wouldn’t want to instantly return home to take a shower and eat a meal after a frantic battle ?

Regarding the practice of the Flying Thunder God Technique, Asakawa Shin could currently successfully carve the Flying Thunder God Mark, but he still lacked proficiency.

One must know that Minato Namikaze could stealthily place a Flying Thunder God Mark on an enemy the moment they engaged ; he was still far from that level.

But it didn’t matter ; he could use Shadow Clones to practice this process repeatedly to increase his proficiency.

Another difficulty in mastering the Flying Thunder God Technique lay in whether one could instantly and accurately lock onto multiple high-speed moving Flying Thunder God Marks.

Fortunately, Asakawa Shin was a sensory-type Ninja.

Although spatial marks differed slightly from regular objects, his spatial situational awareness was unexpectedly excellent, allowing him to handle this situation with ease.

He wasn’t sure if it was a Senju Clan inheritance or a special gift brought by his transmigration...

The third difficulty in training was to complete the teleportation instantly after sensing the Flying Thunder God Mark and to react quickly upon arrival.

This required a high level of Chakra capacity, dynamic vision, and neural reaction speed.

However, Asakawa Shin’s constitution was different from ordinary people.

Not to mention his massive Chakra reserves, he also possessed dynamic vision and neural reflexes that were unattainable for normal people.

He even possessed Observation Haki, which allowed him to see into the future.

He perfectly met the training requirements, and Asakawa Shin was currently preparing to tackle this stage of practice.

...

After parting ways with Uchiha Itachi, Asakawa Shin took Yūgao back to his villa.

At this time, the house was quiet, with only Kana alone.

Hearing the door open, Kana turned her head in confusion.

After seeing that the person arriving was Asakawa Shin, a flicker of incomprehension flashed in her eyes :

« Lord Shin, why are you back at this time ? Don’t you have to go to school ? »

Asakawa Shin waved his hand and explained, « This is my original body ; I just returned from outside the village. »

« By the way, this is my guard, Yūgao Uzuki. She’ll be living with us from now on. You two should get to know each other. »

« Yūgao, this is Uzumaki Kana, my maid and Chief of Internal Affairs. »

Kana couldn’t help but laugh, covering her mouth as she giggled :

« Oh my, when did I get promoted to Chief of Internal Affairs ? How come I didn’t know ? »

« But, this position should allow me to manage other maids, right ? Just Karin alone, I suppose ? »

« Hahaha, actually, even without this title, I can still keep her in line. »

She finally managed to stop laughing. Life had been comfortable lately, and for some reason, her sense of humor had become more easily triggered.

Recalling the time in Kusagakure, she rarely showed a smile in front of Karin.

She cleared her throat and gave a formal greeting : « Hello, Yūgao, welcome to our family. »

« Follow me upstairs in a bit. You can pick any room you like, except for the master bedroom and the rooms already occupied. You can have your pick of the rest. »

Yūgao responded warmly, and before long, the two women were walking arm-in-arm, chatting away.

Seeing this, Asakawa Shin spoke calmly :

« Then Yūgao, you stay here. I’m going out to train in a bit. You don’t have to follow me all the time ; I’ll call you if I need anything. »

Yūgao suddenly panicked and said anxiously :

« But Lord Shin, I am your guard. I need to protect you closely. How can I leave your side... »

Her voice grew softer and softer until it was inaudible. She muttered to herself in her heart :

'Protect Lord Shin ? Saying that out loud feels so lacking in confidence.'

Asakawa Shin waved his hand casually and said in a relaxed tone :

« Alright, just consider it a vacation for you. Get settled in first. I’m leaving now, ja na~ »

...

He went straight to his private underground training ground to begin practicing the Flying Thunder God Technique.

To perform this slow, cumulative work that required time, he directly created dozens of Shadow Clones to put into practice.

At the same time, his original body took out a specially made Flying Thunder God Kunai ; rather than a Kunai, it looked more like a throwing knife.

The Kunai’s design was slender and elegant, with eight large characters carved onto the blade in a flowing style—'Above the Heavens and Below the Earth, I Alone am the Honored One'.

These eight characters were exactly the declaration made by Shakyamuni Buddha when he was born in Lumbini, pointing one finger to the sky and one to the earth.

However, since Asakawa Shin could summon a Great Buddha, it was quite reasonable for his Flying Thunder God Mark to be carved with these eight characters, right ?

He casually threw the Kunai into the distance and then closed his eyes to sense it.

The spatial mark of the Flying Thunder God was clearly discernible ; sensing it was effortless.

So, next...

Asakawa Shin focused his mind, following the pull of the spatial mark. He felt as if his entire body was passing through an invisible membrane, and in an instant, his form completed the spatial transfer.

« Hmm, this feeling is quite peculiar. It’s somewhat similar to the spatial transition of the Reverse Summoning Technique, yet not entirely the same. »

After speaking, he frowned slightly and reflected to himself :

« Moreover, when I teleported just now, I actually had a moment of distraction. »

« This won’t do ; I must adapt to this feeling as soon as possible. »

« Otherwise, if such a daze occurs in actual combat, the consequences could be disastrous. »

« Then, let’s continue... »

« Ninja Art : Multi-Shadow Clone Technique ! »

« Brothers, put some effort into it, let’s get to work ! »

« Oh— ! »
