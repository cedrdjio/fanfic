# Chapitre 157 — Two Phases of Heaven and Man

Jiraiya’s face turned pale with fright, and he hurriedly stepped forward to smooth things over.

« Junior brother, you must not be impolite to the Great Toad Sage ! This is a senior who saved the entire Ninja World a thousand years ago, a figure of great virtue and prestige ! »

Asakawa Shin ignored him, merely raising his hand impatiently to lightly flick the two small snake earrings, which were currently set to vibration mode and trembling incessantly.

« You two stop shaking. You’re making my head buzz. Can’t you show some backbone ? »

« ... »

« It’s fine, it’s fine. A child’s words carry no harm, a child’s words carry no harm. »

The Great Toad Sage didn’t mind at all, merely waving his hand magnanimously, a benevolent smile appearing on his face.

« Young friend, do not misunderstand. You are the Child of Prophecy long ago designated by my Mount Myōboku. Far from being your enemies, we are your fated assistants. »

« Child of Prophecy ? Me ? » Asakawa Shin repeated in a low voice, his expression full of oddity.

Then his eyes lit up, and he asked excitedly, « So that means, if I need any help from you, you’ll help me ? »

The Great Toad Sage nodded without hesitation and instructed Fukasaku and Shima at his side, « In the future, if this young friend has any requests, Mount Myōboku must provide full support without fail. »

As soon as he finished speaking, he let out a long yawn, his tone sounding slightly weary. « I am a bit tired. Let the two of them host you on my behalf. »

With that, he slowly closed his eyes, and after a short while, long snores began to sound.

Jiraiya finally snapped out of it, murmuring in shock, « Child of Prophecy ? But... shouldn’t the Child of Prophecy be my student ? How did he end up being my teacher’s student ? This... is this logical ? »

Though the two sages, Fukasaku and Shima Sage, also sensed something was off, since the Great Toad Sage had personally made the decree, there could be no mistake.

The two immediately adopted warm expressions and hospitably attended to Asakawa Shin, leading him on an enthusiastic tour of Mount Myōboku’s scenic spots.

Not until dusk fell did Shima Sage suggest, « It’s late now. Why don’t you two stay and try our Mount Myōboku specialty dinner ? Don’t worry, I’m quite confident in my culinary skills. »

Asakawa Shin flatly refused, instead taking the opportunity to request a look at Mount Myōboku’s Sage Mode cultivation method.

Fukasaku and Shima exchanged a glance, and after a brief hesitation, they agreed.

Asakawa Shin took the cultivation scroll with a face full of gratitude and followed up by saying, « In that case, I’m afraid I’ll have to trouble your esteemed place for a few days to focus on cultivation for a while. »

« Of course, of course. Stay as long as you like, young friend. »

Shima Sage replied with a smile, then added with some regret, « But, are you really not going to try our Mount Myōboku signature dishes ? »

The corner of Asakawa Shin’s eye twitched imperceptibly as he declined again, « Thank you for your kindness, Sage, but I no longer need to eat to maintain my vital signs. »

Seeing that Shima Sage’s expression clearly showed a bit of disappointment, he felt slightly bad and couldn’t help but scratch his head.

Scanning with the corner of his eye, he saw Jiraiya standing there like a wooden post. He had a sudden flash of inspiration and clapped his hands, proposing :

« However, since the two Sages are so hospitable, why not have my senior brother attend in my place and have a good taste ? »

« That would be perfect ! »

Shima Sage’s eyes lit up at the words, and she turned her gaze to Jiraiya. « Little Jiraiya hasn’t tasted my cooking in a long time, right ? You must not be polite this time ! »

Watching Jiraiya being dragged away by the two enthusiastic toad sages with a look of utter despair, a trace of a smile couldn’t help but appear in Asakawa Shin’s eyes.

Mount Myōboku’s delicacies ? They were all bizarre-looking bugs. Who would dare eat that ?

Such high-quality protein was better left for his senior brother to enjoy ; he himself was not so fortunate.

Asakawa Shin deftly weighed the scroll in his hand, his expression turning serious.

He found a hillside with beautiful scenery, sat down cross-legged at random, and also took out the Ryūchi Cave Sage Art cultivation scroll, spreading them out side by side.

Now that he had collected the Sage Art cultivation methods of the Three Great Sage Regions, he began to study them by cross-referencing and comparing.

Although the fundamental principles of cultivating Sage Art in the Three Great Sage Regions were the same, the specific energy circulation routes were vastly different, and the final effects of the Sage Arts performed each had their own merits.

Such complex differences really gave him a bit of a headache.

Using the old method, his hands quickly formed seals to perform the Multiple Shadow Clone Jutsu, creating dozens of clones.

Immediately after, the clones began to experiment individually, constantly learning through trial and error.

His main body personally tested the other two Sage Art cultivation methods, carefully sensing the subtle differences and commonalities between them.

Time slipped away during his research, and several days later, he finally slowly opened his eyes.

Just as it had been in Ryūchi Cave, the Natural Energy accumulated in Mount Myōboku for a thousand years had been almost entirely devoured by him.

However, the Great Toad Sage seemed completely unaware, still sleeping peacefully.

As for the other toads, they were strictly restrained by Fukasaku and Shima, and no one came to disturb him.

Asakawa Shin frowned slightly, his expression showing a bit of helplessness.

The vast Natural Energy deposited in the Three Great Sage Regions for a thousand years had now been completely scavenged by him.

But even so, the progress of the Kekkei Mora in his body had not even reached half.

According to the current progress, he would probably have to devour all the Natural Energy in the entire Ninja World to barely scrape together the required amount.

He certainly couldn’t do that.

How would such behavior be any different from the Ōtsutsuki Clan ?

If he really did that, wouldn’t he truly become the Child of Calamity in the prophecy, the culprit who destroyed the Ninja World ?

However, this trip was not entirely without gains.

He keenly sensed that an extremely pure Yin Release Chakra was quietly gestating within his body.

This powerful Yin power even stimulated his visual nerves through his brain, causing a tingling sensation in his eyes.

Furthermore, after these few days of study, that improved cultivation method was clearly more compatible with him, yet a certain feeling within it felt remarkably familiar.

After a long time of fruitless pondering, it wasn’t until he walked under an apple tree and was nearly hit by a falling apple that a flash of inspiration struck his mind.

« I remember now ! This feeling is exactly the same as that mysterious ancient tree in Takigakure ! I remember it was slowly absorbing Natural Energy. Could it be ? »

No sooner said than done.

With a few flickers of his body, he was already quietly standing atop that towering ancient tree in Takigakure.

The Flying Thunder God Mark he had left here years ago had come in handy now.

He sat cross-legged, his mind instantly sinking into the giant tree beneath his feet, sensing carefully.

Sure enough, the way this ancient tree absorbed Natural Energy was extremely similar to his improved energy circulation route, giving a feeling of clearly coming from the same origin.

However, he frowned, faced with a problem.

A large tree doesn’t have a meridian system, so how could he derive a cultivation method suitable for the human body ?

'Since the tree cannot become a person, then I shall become the tree.'

The corner of his mouth curled up slightly as his body suddenly fell backward.

A magical scene occurred...

The hard trunk actually rippled like a calm water surface, allowing him to slowly sink and finally merge perfectly with it.

In an instant, he felt as if he had truly become this ancient tree, his posture towering, deeply rooted in the earth, absorbing nutrients and moisture from underground.

Branches and leaves stretched out, basking in the sunlight for photosynthesis, while countless small lives thrived and multiplied among them.

At the same time, massive amounts of Natural Energy were breathed in, absorbed, and transformed by him, finally being released again to form a perfect cycle, becoming one with nature.

« So that’s how it is... »

Light flowed in Asakawa Shin’s eyes as he suddenly understood.

« This is the Sage Mode that truly suits me, completely different from the cultivation methods of those Three Great Sage Regions.

It is not blindly plundering and devouring, but interacting and resonating with heaven and earth, becoming one, turning into nature itself and benefiting all things.

But... this seems completely contrary to the characteristics of the God Tree. »

After all, the God Tree feeds itself by absorbing Natural Energy from heaven and earth, while this giant tree beneath his feet is the complete opposite.

Light flashed in Asakawa Shin’s eyes ; since the direction was clear, a single trial would tell.

To his surprise, both diametrically opposed paths were actually viable, and both could lead to a perfect Sage Mode.

After entering Sage Mode, his body did not undergo any mutation ; only the aura around him became increasingly ethereal and otherworldly.

He named the former 'Heavenly Way : Sage Mode,' taking the meaning 'The Way of Heaven is to take from the surplus to supplement the deficient,' primarily governing harmony and balance.

The latter was called 'Human Way : Sage Mode,' taking the meaning 'The Way of Man is to take from the deficient to serve the surplus,' primarily governing plunder and reinforcement.

Besides possessing the basic characteristics of Sage Mode, the special effects derived from these two were also completely different.
