# Chapitre 220 — The Language of Flowers

« Boom— ! »

A massive explosion shattered the tavern’s roof, and flames soared into the sky.

Terrified civilians screamed and scattered in all directions ; the scene was pure chaos.

A figure completely covered in white hair suddenly burst through the wall of fire, leapt onto the opposite roof, and scanned the surroundings warily.

Amidst the sky full of firelight, countless white papers fluttered upward like butterflies, quickly converging into a slender, graceful silhouette.

She wore a black robe with red clouds, her blue hair dancing wildly in the heat waves.

Unlike her usual cold demeanor, a faint, mocking smile played at the corners of her mouth as her gaze pierced straight through the disheveled man.

« Teacher, I didn’t expect to meet you in a place like this. My apologies, I seem to have interrupted your 'refined interests' just now. »

Jiraiya’s pupils constricted, and his mouth hung open wide enough to fit an egg. « Konan ? You’re alive ? And that outfit... are you also a member of the Akatsuki Organization ? Was the 'Big Sister Konan' that little girl mentioned you ? »

Before he could finish, he seemed to realize something, his whole body jolted, and his expression instantly collapsed as he waved his hands frantically. « Wait, Konan, let me explain ! It’s not what you think, I was just... »

Before he could finish his excuse, the flames inside the tavern suddenly turned violent, transforming into a massive fire tornado that shot straight into the clouds.

Then, the raging fire cyclone was instantly snuffed out as if by an invisible giant hand, revealing two figures at the center of the storm.

One of them wore a signature Swirl Mask and was gently stroking the hair of the young girl beside him.

« Little Kurotsuchi, are you okay ? Big Brother is here to save you. »

Though his tone was gentle to the point of being doting, it scared Kurotsuchi so much she nearly dropped the whip in her hand, her whole body shaking like a leaf.

« Bi... Bi... »

Asakawa Shin’s eyes were sharp ; he caught a glimpse of the whip and reached out to take it, spinning it around his fingertip with interest.

« Little Kurotsuchi, I didn’t know you had this skill. You were swinging it quite impressively just now, with quite the momentum. How about this, I’ll practice a bit next, and you can be my sparring partner. »

Kurotsuchi shuddered and instantly snapped out of it, defending herself incoherently, « Big Brother, you’ve misunderstood ! I saw him acting suspiciously and was just testing him. I definitely wasn’t playing some strange game ! »

Jiraiya’s eyes lit up upon hearing this, and he immediately clapped in agreement. « As expected of a member of the Akatsuki Organization ! I was gathering intelligence in disguise, yet you saw through me at a glance. You’re truly impressive, little girl ! »

Kurotsuchi was overwhelmed by the unexpected praise and quickly perked up, looking at the white-haired man with a sense of mutual respect. « Not at all, you are very strong too ! »

Jiraiya immediately turned to Konan in the air and shouted with an air of righteousness, « Konan ! Did you hear that ? I really didn’t lie to you ! »

Konan rolled her eyes in annoyance, her tone carrying a hint of disdain. « Enough, Teacher. Who in the Ninja World doesn’t know your character ? Besides, your 'icha icha paradise' has topped the charts for how many years now ? Does it even need proving ? Is there even any room left for your reputation to drop ? »

Kurotsuchi listened from the side, completely lost, and couldn’t help but mutter three questions in a row : « 'icha icha paradise' ? What kind of book is that ? Is it very famous ? »

Asakawa Shin heard her clearly and glanced at her with a strange look, thinking to himself : 'She doesn’t even know that ? Her knowledge base is way too meager ; looks like I’ll have to find some time to give her a crash course.'

Jiraiya waved his hands awkwardly and laughed it off, « Eh ? It’s all just empty fame, not worth mentioning, not worth mentioning at all. »

Sensing Konan’s expression suddenly turn cold, Jiraiya dropped his playful act and said solemnly :

« Konan, back then I heard all three of you had perished. What exactly has happened over these years ? Where are Nagato and Yahiko ? And why did you join the Akatsuki Organization ? »

Konan sighed softly but avoided the question, simply saying calmly, « Teacher, surrender without a fight. I don’t want to kill you. »

« Kill me ? »

Jiraiya’s heart sank. Countless thoughts flashed through his mind like lightning, finally turning into a helpless sigh. « It seems I’ll have to capture you first before we can talk slowly. »

Konan lifted her chin slightly, her expression indifferent. « Teacher, I am no longer that naive student from back then. Don’t worry, I won’t kill you. In just one more month, everything will be revealed. »

Her body rapidly split apart, and countless white papers flew through the air, carrying afterimages as they attacked with high speed.

« Ninja Art : Paper Shuriken ! »

Jiraiya’s pupils constricted. 'One month ? What does that mean ? Wait... the Five Kage Summit is scheduled to be held exactly one month from now. Does the Akatsuki Organization plan to take action then ?'

While his thoughts raced, his feet didn’t stop. He moved like a snake, narrowly avoiding all the incoming Paper Shuriken.

However, before he could breathe a sigh of relief, a series of earth-shattering explosions erupted behind him.

The Paper Shuriken that had landed on the ground detonated instantly, turning into a surging wave of air and pungent smoke that engulfed him.

« Are these... Explosive Tags ? »

Before his shock could settle, a massive shadow loomed over him.

Jiraiya looked up abruptly to see countless white papers falling like goose feathers, folding and transforming. In the blink of an eye, they turned into a torrential rain of shuriken covering the sky.

With a light wave of Konan’s delicate hand, this destructive torrent poured down.

« You girl... »

Before he could finish, the violent explosion completely submerged him.

The continuous fire raged for nearly a minute before finally subsiding.

Kurotsuchi watched dumbstruck, muttering to herself, « Is this Big Sister Konan’s fighting style ? It’s simply... simple, crude, and inhumanly wealthy ! »

A hint of admiration flashed in Asakawa Shin’s eyes as he gazed fervently at the graceful figure in the air. « This is the power of money ! She’s basically a walking money printer, undoubtedly the wealthiest woman in the Ninja World. »

With a thought, he decided to give Konan a unique gift tomorrow : a bouquet of flowers made from Steel Wool to express his feelings.

He would set the language of flowers as 'Endurance and Wealth.' He believed she would surely understand the deep meaning behind it once she received it.

In mid-air, Konan felt a bit creeped out by that burning gaze and finally couldn’t help but glare fiercely at Asakawa Shin.

« An opening ! »

Just then, a figure suddenly released the summoning of the Toad’s Stomach Wall, burst through the thick smoke, and lunged at Konan in the air.

Jiraiya’s hands moved in a blur of afterimages, the Chakra in his body rapidly completing a nature transformation. He then opened his mouth and spat out a large amount of thick, yellow oil toward Konan.

« Ninja Art : Toad Oil Bullet ! »

Konan quickly snapped back to her senses, her body instinctively decomposing into countless white papers in an attempt to escape.

Unexpectedly, the oil was extremely viscous. Once the papers were stained, they became incredibly heavy, and the speed of their flight suddenly slowed. The stickiness also caused Konan to lose her ability to split.

Jiraiya’s gaze sharpened. Seizing this fleeting opportunity, his hands quickly formed seals.

In an instant, his white hair grew wildly like a spirit snake, carrying a sharp whistling sound as it fiercely coiled and bound Konan until she could not move.

« Ninja Art : Wild Lion’s Mane Technique ! »

« This is the second lesson your teacher is giving you : The last leg of a journey is the hardest ; never let a moment of carelessness lead to total failure ! »

« Also, I’ve been wanting to say this since just now—Konan, you’re actually wearing a Lip Piercing ? You’ve gone bad, Konan ! »
