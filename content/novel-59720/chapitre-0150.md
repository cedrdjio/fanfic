# Chapitre 150 — Drawing Lots

As the remaining sand on the field was cleared away, the final match of the preliminaries was about to begin.

The two contestants were the female Ninja Karui from Kumogakure and Akimichi Choji from Konoha Village.

Asakawa Shin cast his gaze toward the arena with great interest, his eyes shifting back and forth between the two.

Karui had dark skin and wore her hair in a unique hedgehog style. As a prized student of Killer Bee, she was currently in a defensive stance, her eyes sharp as blades.

It is worth mentioning that this seemingly formidable young woman would eventually marry the gluttonous boy before her, Akimichi Choji.

However, the two did not know each other yet, and a fierce confrontation was inevitable.

Karui frowned, her gaze landing on Choji, who was stuffing potato chips into his mouth, and she couldn’t help but complain in a low voice :

« What the hell ? Is my opponent this time just a fat guy who only knows how to eat ? »

Choji’s fingers, which were grabbing potato chips, paused abruptly. The gentle and honest smile on his face vanished instantly, replaced by a look of seriousness and anger.

He raised his head, staring intently at her, and emphasized solemnly :

« I am not fat, I am just a little plump. »

Karui raised an eyebrow in surprise, her tone indifferent :

« Whether you’re fat or not, let’s just start the match. »

Seeing that both sides were ready, the referee immediately waved one arm and loudly announced the start of the match.

Choji let out a cold shout, his tone filled with determination that could not be underestimated :

« Let me show you the power of being 'plump' ! »

« Ninja Art : Multi-Size Technique ! »

Before he finished speaking, his abdomen, wrapped in thick clothing, suddenly expanded, instantly becoming round and heavy.

Following that, he retracted his hands, feet, and head into his sleeves, curling his entire body into a tightly sealed, giant meat ball.

« Human Bullet Tank ! »

Accompanied by a low roar, the high-speed spinning meat ball, carrying astonishing momentum, charged straight toward Karui.

Karui was startled and hurriedly leaped away.

The Human Bullet Tank whistled past, slamming into the wall with unstoppable force and instantly smashing a huge hole in it.

Before the dust settled, Choji had already changed direction and came rolling toward her again with aggressive momentum.

Karui frowned, no longer hesitating, and with a « swish, » she drew the long sword from her waist.

She flashed aside, once again avoiding the charge of the meat ball, and then swung her sword backhanded, slashing down fiercely with a sharp gust of wind.

However, the scene of blood spraying that she had expected did not occur.

As soon as the blade touched the high-speed spinning meat ball, it was violently deflected by a huge rotational force, numbing her hand and nearly causing her to drop the sword.

As a master of swordsmanship, Karui instantly understood the trick :

'A diagonal slash will only be deflected. Only by cutting in along the direction of the meat ball’s rotation is it possible to break through its defense. What a troublesome guy.'

Thus, Karui began to dodge and weave constantly, flexibly leading Choji and inducing the high-speed spinning meat ball to slam violently into the arena walls again and again.

For a time, the deafening roars were incessant.

The solid walls were riddled with holes, and the entire arena even began to totter.

Asakawa Shin sighed helplessly, formed hand seals to perform Wood Release, and quickly reinforced the surrounding walls.

He was afraid that the arena would collapse before a winner was decided.

As Choji’s offensive slowed down significantly due to exhaustion, a cold glint flashed in Karui’s eyes, and she thought to herself :

'The time is almost right. My stamina is also heavily depleted. I’ll decide the outcome with the next strike !'

When the Human Bullet Tank came charging aggressively again, Karui, contrary to her usual behavior, did not dodge or evade.

She gripped the sword hilt tightly with both hands, raised it high, and swung with all her might, following the momentum of Choji’s rotation.

Choji keenly sensed the danger, but instead of slowing down or evading, he suddenly tightened his body, driving the momentum of his rotation even more fiercely.

Just as the two were about to make contact, he let out an angry shout :

« Iron Body : Spin ! »

In an instant, the surface of the high-speed spinning meat ball seemed to congeal into an indestructible metal shell, slamming into her with the force of a thunderbolt.

Karui was horrified, but at this moment, there was no retreat. She gritted her teeth and swung her sword down with all her might, attempting to split this detestable meat ball in two.

However, the moment the blade touched it, a strange, slippery sensation traveled up the sword.

The meat ball was harder than she could have imagined, and the sword tip slipped and deflected sharply off the smooth surface.

Before she could react, the immense impact force pressed down along the sword, directly knocking her sword away.

Following that, the heavy meat ball slammed into her chest like a collapsing mountain.

« Bang— ! »

Karui was like a bowling pin struck by a ball, flying backward and falling heavily to the ground. After struggling a few times, she stopped moving.

Seeing this, the referee immediately stepped forward to check Karui’s condition, then raised his arm and loudly announced :

« Contestant Karui is unable to continue ! The winner of this match is Akimichi Choji ! »

As soon as he finished speaking, the medical team quickly entered the field, placed the injured Karui on a stretcher, and prepared to evacuate.

As they passed by Choji, she turned her head with great effort, her gaze locked deeply onto the figure still munching on potato chips, and she whispered through gritted teeth :

« You... damn fat guy, I’ll remember you. »

Choji, who was stuffing potato chips into his mouth, paused, a look of helplessness appearing on his face as he earnestly corrected her :

« I told you, I’m not a fat guy, I’m just a little plump. »

With the ten matches concluded, the winning contestants were once again gathered in one place.

Hiruzen Sarutobi scanned the group of young Ninjas with a satisfied expression and slowly began to announce :

« Now, you will all serve as official participants in the third stage of the Chunin Exams to be held in one month. »

« This exam will use a tournament format. Please draw your respective numbers first. »

As soon as he finished speaking, Anko stepped forward briskly, excitedly holding a lottery box.

Everyone stepped forward in turn to draw their own number tags. With the numbers settled, the match list for the Chunin Exams a month later was officially determined.

Uzumaki Naruto looked as if he had been struck by lightning, « What ? I have to face Lady Hinata in the first match ? »

Uchiha Sasuke’s expression was equally dark and ugly. He had drawn number 4, and number 3 was Gaara.

This meant that in the tournament a month later, the two of them would be opponents.

As the rest of them confirmed their opponents, Hiruzen Sarutobi gave a light cough and officially announced :

« Alright, for the next month, everyone must train hard. »

« At that time, not only will I be present, but the Kazekage, the Raikage, and invited daimyos and nobles from various countries will also attend to watch. You must all perform well. »

Uzumaki Naruto felt his heart in a turmoil as he listened, his pressure doubling.

He made up his mind that for the next month, he must beg Kakashi for hellish special training.

After all, the opponents ahead—whether it was Hinata, Sasuke, Gaara, or Asakawa Shin—were none of them easy to deal with. This battle was destined to be extremely difficult.

Sasuke also gritted his teeth secretly, preparing himself for a life-or-death struggle.

'Hmm, I’ll train desperately with Kakashi during the day, and go home to find my brother for special training at night. As long as it doesn’t kill me, I’ll train until I drop !'

The eyes of the other contestants also ignited with burning fighting spirit, swearing to show off their skills on the field a month later and prove that they were not cowards.
