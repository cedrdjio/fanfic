# Chapitre 137 — Shukaku Strikes

« Yo~ isn’t this Orochimaru ? Haven’t seen you in years, how did you get so pathetic~ »

Outside the battlefield, Anko was beaming with joy, clapping and laughing on the side, her eyes flashing with irrepressible excitement.

Originally, she had rushed here with a resolve to die.

Not only to settle past grudges once and for all, but also because, as the Chief Proctor of the Chunin Exams, she had to be responsible for all the candidates.

It was one thing for the candidates to fight to the death among themselves, but Orochimaru...

However, when she rushed to the battlefield, what met her eyes was the pathetic spectacle of Orochimaru being ruthlessly suppressed and beaten by Asakawa Shin.

This scene made her instantly forget her tension, and she couldn’t help but double over with laughter.

She was completely relaxed now, and in her high spirits, she even had the leisure to mock her former mentor.

Hearing Anko’s voice, Orochimaru was also slightly taken aback.

He glanced sideways, and what he saw was Anko laughing so hard she was trembling, nearly unable to stand upright.

Orochimaru’s face darkened, his lips twitched, and he was about to speak.

But in the next moment, a cold voice echoed faintly from behind him, making his whole body stiffen.

« Hey, you were distracted just now, weren’t you ? »

Orochimaru’s heart jolted, and he looked back in horror.

He saw Asakawa Shin’s right arm coated in Armament Haki, charging towards him while tanking several Water Severing Waves from Senju Tobirama.

At the moment Orochimaru looked back in shock, Asakawa Shin had already closed in, his left hand ready. A heavy uppercut, carrying the sound of wind and thunder, slammed hard into his jaw.

A massive impact exploded instantly, and Orochimaru was sent flying like a cannonball. His jawbone twisted and deformed in the violent collision, and he flew backward in a pathetic state.

Witnessing this, Anko couldn’t help but clap in delight, shouting repeatedly, « Well done ! Shin, keep hitting him like that ! »

Naruto, Sasuke, and the others also joined in upon seeing this, cheering for Asakawa Shin.

On the other side, Gaara and Shukaku were arguing fiercely within their inner world.

Gaara was stomping his feet in frustration, cursing at Shukaku : « Shukaku, you bastard, why haven’t you made a move yet ? Now is the perfect time for us to strike ! Don’t you want to prove yourself ? »

Faced with Gaara’s questioning, Shukaku seemed somewhat hesitant.

His eyes darted around, and he looked at Gaara timidly, his voice full of reluctance :

« B-but... the opponent is the First Hokage, Senju Hashirama ! You have no idea how terrifying that guy is.

Back then, many Tailed Beasts suffered greatly at his hands, and that kid who can fight the First Hokage to a standstill is even more of a monster.

Furthermore, he’s also a Wood Release User ; we hardly have any chance of winning. »

Hearing this, Gaara grew even more furious, his eyes filled with disdain :

« Didn’t you claim to be the strongest Tailed Beast before ? Didn’t you want to prove to the Nine-Tails that the number of tails doesn’t represent strength ? »

« Now is the time to prove yourself ! »

« Are you really the weakest just because you only have one tail, like the Nine-Tails said ? »

Shukaku was provoked into a rage, roaring furiously, « That’s absolute nonsense ! I’ll show you all just how powerful Lord Shukaku is ! »

The battlefield outside.

A smile played on Asakawa Shin’s lips as he spun around and delivered a kick, landing it precisely on the buttocks of Senju Tobirama, who had just teleported in, sending the Former Hokage of Konoha flying.

Senju Tobirama landed awkwardly, veins bulging on his forehead and his face darkened with lines of frustration.

For some reason, he felt that this junior from the Senju Clan was targeting him on purpose.

But that thought was simply too absurd.

After all, he had never even met this kid.

Just as Asakawa Shin was happily venting his years of resentment, a massive surge of Chakra suddenly appeared within his sensory range.

He turned to look and saw yellow sand filling the sky in the distance, where a giant sand monster was slowly rising from the ground.

The monster resembled a Tanuki, its body covered in mysterious and eerie black sealing patterns, radiating a suffocating pressure amidst the wind and sand.

Asakawa Shin couldn’t help but rub his temples, sighing inwardly : 'One-Tail Shukaku ? Why is he joining the fray too ?'

Orochimaru also sensed this violent Chakra. He focused his gaze, and upon seeing the silhouette of the giant beast, his heart filled with joy.

By his judgment, this Tailed Beast should be an ally rather than an enemy.

Sure enough, in the next moment, his speculation was confirmed.

As the Tailed Beast transformation was fully completed, the massive body slowly raised its head, its wheel-sized beastly pupils locking onto Asakawa Shin on the battlefield.

Immediately after, Gaara’s voice, filled with rage, roared from the giant beast’s mouth :

« You bastard ! You must have noticed me while you were on that Wood Dragon earlier, didn’t you ? »

« How dare you ignore me ? Are you looking down on me ? »

« This time, I’ll show you my true power. »

As soon as he finished speaking, Shukaku’s massive body took heavy strides, rushing toward the battlefield, each step making the earth tremble.

Asakawa Shin looked at the giant sand beast with slight surprise, feeling a bit shocked in his heart :

'That’s Gaara’s voice ? He actually reached a reconciliation with Shukaku and became a Perfect Jinchuriki ?

I underestimated him, but to become my enemy for such a ridiculous reason is simply outrageous !

First Orochimaru, and now Shukaku is joining in...'

A flash of annoyance crossed Asakawa Shin’s eyes, and he decided not to overthink it : 'Since they’re here, I’ll deal with them all together !'

Once the decision was made, he no longer hesitated. With a clap of his hands, the Wood Dragon, which had turned into a statue, woke up once again, its scales making a dull sound as they rubbed together.

« Roar— ! »

The two-hundred-meter-long Wood Dragon roared to the heavens, its massive body leaving an afterimage in the air as it charged head-on toward the rushing Shukaku.

« Rumble— ! »

The two colossal beasts charged simultaneously, each step making the ground shake, and the loud noise made people’s eardrums ache.

A sharp glint flashed in Shukaku’s massive beastly eyes. As he opened his huge mouth, giant wind spheres condensed with terrifying wind pressure whistled out, blasting straight toward the Wood Dragon.

« Wind Release : Air Bullets ! »

Several giant wind spheres struck the Wood Dragon’s body with thunderous force. However, the usually invincible « Air Bullets » only managed to shatter the surface scales of the Wood Dragon.

The Wood Dragon’s momentum remained unchecked as it tanked the bombardment of Air Bullets and attacked Shukaku.

Shukaku was greatly shocked : 'Huh ? Isn’t this hardness a bit off the charts ?'

Before he could regain his senses, the thick dragon body had already coiled around him like an iron hoop.

Despite his best efforts to struggle, he was still locked tight and couldn’t break free.

Worse still, a strange suction was frantically devouring his Chakra along the contact surface.

Shukaku felt his power draining rapidly, while the injuries the Wood Dragon had just sustained were healing at a rate visible to the naked eye.

With one side weakening and the other strengthening, Shukaku immediately fell into a huge disadvantage.

In the inner world, Gaara looked at the crisis outside, his face full of panic, and urgently questioned Shukaku : « Think of something ! Shukaku, if this continues, we’re bound to lose ! »

Shukaku was also fuming with rage at this moment.

His last few appearances had been all bark and no bite, ending in quick defeats, which caused his long-accumulated violent emotions to explode completely.

He slammed his hands onto the ground and growled through gritted teeth, « It seems I have no choice but to use that move. »

Gaara was startled to hear this, but on second thought, he could only nod helplessly, giving his tacit consent to this risky proposal.

Thus, the Shukaku outside suddenly opened his huge mouth and began to frantically mobilize his remaining Chakra, compressing it at a 2 :8 ratio of Yin and Yang into a high-density spherical Chakra polymer.

The sphere glowed with an eerie light, containing world-destroying energy—it was the ultimate move of the Tailed Beasts, the Tailed Beast Ball.

« You damn reptile, take this ! »
