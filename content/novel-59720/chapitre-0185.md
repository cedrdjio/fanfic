# Chapitre 185 — Limiter

Konan gritted her teeth and glared at the « Madara » before her. « You bastard, what exactly are you trying to do ? »

« What do I want ? Don’t you have a clue ? »

Asakawa Shin retorted, then reached into his robes and pulled out a slip of paper covered in writing.

It listed the required materials in detail, and he handed it directly to Konan.

« Don’t give me that nonsense. I’m just making a reasonable wage claim ! »

Asakawa Shin said righteously, « I’ve been working my life away for the Akatsuki Organization for so long. Don’t I even deserve a bit of salary ? »

« Salary ? »

Konan’s expression froze. With a mix of strangeness and suspicion, she cautiously took the paper and scanned it quickly.

'Huh ? Just these ?'

Surprised, she couldn’t believe it and checked it several times, fearing she might have missed some trap.

« Wait here for a moment. »

A moment later, she transformed into a flurry of paper and scattered away. Before long, she returned and handed over a Storage Scroll.

« Everything you wanted is inside. »

Konan said coldly, though an inscrutable look flickered in her eyes. « Also, consider this a favor from me. Not a single word of this secret must be leaked, especially to Kakuzu. Do you hear me ? »

« I understand. But I want half of the money Kakuzu earns from now on, otherwise... »

« Deal. »

The two shared a knowing look, completing their split of the loot. Only a world where Kakuzu was hurt was achieved.

Asakawa Shin took the scroll, checked it to confirm everything was correct, and then walked away without looking back.

Back at his secret base, Asakawa Shin placed the heavy, precious materials on the table, feeling quite good.

Without a moment’s rest, he immediately threw himself into the study of this profound Puppet Technique.

With a burst of thick white smoke, dozens of Shadow Clones appeared instantly.

They tacitly picked up the heavy scrolls and began to read and analyze the mysteries within, forgetting food and sleep.

Under such a massive amount of calculation, the progress was astonishing. In just one month, they were already able to proficiently create high-quality Human Puppets.

Asakawa Shin looked at the Human Puppet of the Fourth Kazekage Rasa in front of him and nodded with satisfaction. Then, he used Fuinjutsu to seal it into a scroll.

Then, his figure blurred, and he appeared instantly deep within the vast desert of the Land of Wind.

His eyes flashed with sharp light as he quickly formed hand seals, using Summoning : Edo Tensei to summon a heavy black coffin.

The lid slid off, and Rasa’s figure slowly stepped out.

The former Kazekage still retained his intelligence from life. As he looked around, he was shocked.

« This is the Land of Wind ? And... I’m in an Edo Tensei body ? Who are you ? How dare you turn me into this monstrous form ! »

Asakawa Shin remained indifferent, waved his hand casually, and tossed him a scroll.

Rasa reflexively caught it. Before he could react, he summoned the Human Puppet made from his own body.

« Nani ? Me again ? »

Seeing his own corpse desecrated like this with his own eyes, Rasa’s body trembled violently, his heart filled with unspeakable anger and sorrow.

However, he could not control his body at this moment. He was forced to follow Asakawa Shin’s will, controlling the Human Puppet and using Magnet Release to pan for Gold Dust in the desert.

When the two sources of Magnet Release merged, the power far exceeded what he had in life. Before long, they had gathered a large amount of Gold Dust.

Better yet, the Edo Tensei body didn’t need food, didn’t feel fatigue, and had infinite Chakra, which greatly increased work efficiency.

Watching the two identical figures walk further away, Asakawa Shin nodded coldly and whispered to himself :

« Since you dared to be my enemy, then use double the effort to mine and repay the sins you’ve committed. »

Two 007 gold miners, obtained.

Next, his figure flashed as he activated the Flying Thunder God Technique, returning instantly to his secret workshop.

After a series of tedious and precise operations, Hidan was also successfully made into a Human Puppet.

He immediately controlled Hidan, using him to summon the Shinigami, and commanded him to slice open the Shinigami’s stomach with a single stroke.

In an instant, dozens of imprisoned souls surged out, dissipating into the world like smoke.

Seeing this, Asakawa Shin acted immediately. Without hesitation, he sacrificed a White Zetsu and successfully performed the forbidden Edo Tensei, summoning the Fourth Hokage Minato Namikaze.

Not only Minato Namikaze, but just in case, he had also previously summoned the First Hokage Senju Hashirama and the Second Hokage Senju Tobirama.

Although he didn’t need to rely on their combat power given his own strength, he couldn’t let these legends of the Ninja World wander free.

If an enemy were to perform Edo Tensei on them first, the consequences would be endless.

He controlled Minato Namikaze to lie calmly in the coffin, which then slowly sank and finally disappeared.

As for the Nine-Tailed inside Minato Namikaze, he had no use for it for the time being ; it could wait.

With everything settled, Asakawa Shin finally had some free time to seriously examine his own condition.

Several months had passed, and he had already perfectly integrated the Fubuki template.

Yet, strangely, his mental energy was still increasing at a terrifying speed, as if there were no end to it.

Along with it, his Chakra was also exploding. After all, Chakra is formed by merging physical energy and mental energy in a one-to-one ratio.

Previously, his physical energy had been in excess, but now, with the rapid progress of his mental energy, this shortcoming was finally completely addressed.

He performed an internal inspection but found no root cause for the issue.

« There’s nothing abnormal... but why is it growing so fast ? Was Fubuki originally this powerful ? »

Asakawa Shin frowned, unable to figure it out.

But a moment later, his body shook violently, as if a bolt of lightning had cleared the fog in his mind.

« Powerful ? No, Fubuki herself was extremely weak with a very low upper limit. Compared to her sister Tatsumaki, she’s worlds apart.

But正因如此, her template merged with me, and instead it directly broke through the Limiter... »

Asakawa Shin stood up abruptly, pacing excitedly in the room, his heart racing.

« I remember Saitama-sensei became incredibly powerful precisely because he broke through the Limiter, even allowing his combat power to soar exponentially during battle... »

The so-called Limiter is the upper limit of power that exists for biological beings due to species and individual differences—a chasm that cannot be crossed no matter how much effort is put in.

And Asakawa Shin, after merging Fubuki’s template, had actually broken through that shackle so easily with external help while maintaining his human form.

Asakawa Shin unconsciously rubbed his chin, his gaze deep, and he had already decided on a set of rigorous training plans in his mind.

He decided to complete a hundred push-ups, a hundred sit-ups, a hundred squats, and a ten-kilometer run every day, rain or shine, for three whole years.

He grit his teeth. « Dammit... I didn’t expect the process to be so arduous. »
