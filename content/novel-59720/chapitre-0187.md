# Chapitre 187 — You Didn't Ask Either

« Ice Release ? »

Haku looked up in disbelief, a flicker of complexity crossing his eyes. « Could there be other survivors of my Yuki Clan here ? »

« Not exactly. »

Asakawa Shin shook his head, his tone flat.

« This kind of Ice Release can only manipulate natural ice and snow ; it cannot be used in snowless areas. It’s far inferior to your Bloodline Limit. »

Hearing Asakawa Shin’s explanation, Haku quietly breathed a sigh of relief. To him, family ties were an far too extravagant hope.

He nodded gently. « I understand, Lord Shin. »

Before his voice could fade, his figure blurred and he disappeared from the spot.

Meanwhile, the rest of the film crew remained completely unaware, still enthusiastically packing equipment and setting up the site, fully immersed in the movie’s filming.

Just as the camera was about to start rolling, a sudden, piercingly cold wind swept through without warning, and fine snowflakes began to drift down from the previously clear sky.

The director shouted excitedly, « Fantastic ! This atmosphere is just like special effects ! All departments ready, take this opportunity to start filming immediately ! »

On a distant cliff, three Ninjas silently watched everything.

« There’s no mistake, that’s Koyuki Kazahana ! »

« I didn’t expect her to still be alive, and even becoming a star with such fanfare. It really is a case of 'the darkest place is under the lantern' ! »

« Let’s move. »

The leader of the Ninjas lowered his voice, a cold glint flashing in his eyes. « I’ll hold back Kakashi. As for Princess Koyuki, I’ll leave her to you, Fubuki and Mizore. »

However, before they could leap out, Kakashi below seemed to have noticed something long ago.

With a flick of his wrist, he threw a Kunai with an explosive tag attached. It exploded violently as it neared the cliff, sending rock fragments flying.

The sudden explosion caused the film crew to scream in succession, many of them blaming Kakashi for ruining the shoot.

However, instead of being angry, the director’s eyes sparkled with intense light.

« Idiots ! This isn’t a disruption, it’s a godsend ! This is a real Ninja war ! »

He turned his head sharply and roared at the cameraman at the top of his lungs, « This is a gift from the God of Cinema ! Even if you’re blown to bits on the spot, you must capture this scene for me ! »

The cameraman’s eyes instantly became fanatical upon hearing this. He thumped his chest as a guarantee, then hoisted the heavy camera and shoved the lens right into Kakashi’s face, shouting, « Leave it to me ! »

« Crap ! What are you guys doing ? You’re blocking my view ! »

Kakashi rolled his dead-fish eyes, completely speechless.

« An opening ! »

Nadare Roga let out a low shout, his figure already closing in on Kakashi like a phantom, the cold glint of an ice blade stabbing directly toward Kakashi’s rear.

Kakashi felt his hair stand on end. At the critical moment of life and death, he formed hand signs at his fastest speed ever, instantly performing the Body Replacement Technique to narrowly avoid the strike.

It wasn’t until he escaped to a safe area that he breathed a long sigh of relief, then glared fiercely at the attacker.

« Kakashi, your reactions are quite fast... »

Kakashi stared at the newcomer, his gaze narrowing slightly. « It’s you ? »

« Naruto, Sasuke, and Sakura, protect Miss Yukie. »

In truth, there was no need for him to say anything ; the three youngsters had already formed a formation with perfect chemistry, shielding their client behind them.

Nadare Roga was about to spout some harsh words when he suddenly felt the atmosphere was a bit off.

'Strange, where are my teammates ?'

He turned his head sharply, his pupils suddenly contracting.

He saw that Fubuki and Mizore had somehow already been subdued by two Konoha Ninjas.

Mizore’s Chakra armor energy had already been drained by Haku. Currently, he was in a deep sleep after being hit in his pressure points by several precise Senbon.

Fubuki’s situation was even more miserable, tightly bound by Karin’s Adamantine Attacking Chains. She was respectfully « delivered » before Asakawa Shin like a human pretzel.

Asakawa Shin raised an eyebrow, looking at Fubuki with great displeasure.

« You have a lot of nerve, actually daring to be named Fubuki ? I feel offended ! »

Fubuki swallowed hard in fear and asked with a trembling voice, « My Lord... could it be that your name is also Fubuki ? »

« No, my name is Asakawa Shin... »

« Huh ? Then I don’t know how I have offended you, My Lord ? »

« That’s none of your business ! »

Asakawa Shin instantly became enraged and raised his hand to deliver a heavy rap to Fubuki’s head. « In short, you’re not allowed to be called Fubuki, do you understand ? »

« Ah... it hurts ! Lord Nadare, save me ! This person is purely looking for trouble ! »

Nadare Roga, who had been called out, finally snapped back to his senses, a drop of cold sweat seeping from his brow.

'This is bad. Fubuki and Mizore were defeated so easily. The situation has become quite tricky.'

« Who’s looking for trouble ? Clearly, you were in the wrong first, and yet you refuse to repent ! »

Asakawa Shin gave Fubuki another hard rap on the head, producing a crisp sound.

« Ah ! It hurts ! Why does it hurt so much ! »

Seeing this, Nadare Roga broke into a cold sweat.

He glanced at Fubuki, who was being lectured into submission by Asakawa Shin, then at the unconscious Mizore. Gritting his teeth, he suddenly turned and ran.

'At this point, I can only ask Lord Doto Kazahana to take action personally !'

Kakashi’s expression turned sharp, and he instinctively moved to give chase, but was unexpectedly blocked by the camera lens shoved right in his face.

This single moment of delay actually allowed Nadare Roga to escape successfully.

Only then did Koyuki Kazahana snap out of her daze. Her legs gave way, and she slumped onto the cold snow, gasping for breath.

Her manager, Sandayu, came stumbling over, patting his chest while incoherently checking on her well-being.

Kakashi frowned deeply, walked over to Asakawa Shin, and asked in a low voice, « Is it really okay to just let him go like that ? »

He naturally didn’t believe that Nadare Roga could slip away from under Asakawa Shin’s nose ; it was clearly a deliberate act of letting him go.

« What does it matter ? »

Asakawa Shin shrugged nonchalantly.

« We need to leave a messenger to report back to the big boss. Isn’t that how it always goes in movies ? Right, Director ? »

Not far away, the director was staring intently at the camera playback, his face overflowing with fanatical joy, not even looking up.

« Exactly ! This is how you handle it to create tension. That was filmed perfectly ! »

He waved his arms excitedly, though it was unclear who he was actually talking to.

Asakawa Shin didn’t bother with the old man immersed in his cinematic dreams and turned to walk straight toward Koyuki Kazahana, who was slumped on the snow.

« Now, should I call you Miss Yukie, or should I address you as Princess Koyuki ? »

Koyuki Kazahana held her head in her hands, clearly not having recovered from the fright. She remained immersed in her own world of fear, turning a deaf ear to Asakawa Shin’s question.

Asakawa Shin wasn’t impatient ; he simply added in a measured tone :

« Those who came just now were merely insignificant small fry. Next time, the Demon King himself might arrive. I wonder, Princess Koyuki, what are your plans ? »

These words pierced her eardrums like ice picks. Koyuki Kazahana’s whole body shuddered violently, and she finally raised her head slowly.

« You... what exactly are you trying to say ? »

The corners of Asakawa Shin’s lips curled into a calm and confident smile as he slowly extended his right hand.

« I want to make a deal with you. »

His voice was steady and powerful.

« I will help you reclaim the Land of Snow. In return, your entire country must submit to me ! »

« I am Asakawa Shin. »

Koyuki Kazahana’s brows furrowed slightly as she instinctively murmured the name : « Asakawa Shin ? »

In an instant, countless memory fragments flashed through her mind like a revolving lantern.

What she had seen and heard in Konoha, her father’s earnest entrustment before his death, and the past she had tried so hard to escape.

'I... I remember now. Isn’t he that Asakawa Shin who is called the Son of God ?'

After a moment of daze, the confusion in her eyes vanished, replaced by a wild smile that quietly crept onto her face.

She grabbed Asakawa Shin’s hand excitedly and shook it repeatedly.

« Big brother, if you’re this impressive, why didn’t you say so earlier ? »

« Hmm ? You didn’t ask either. »
