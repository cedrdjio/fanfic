# Chapitre 124 — Traits

« Hmph ! You want to use me as a whetstone ? What an arrogant approach. Aren’t you afraid that your blade will snap against my unyielding iron ? »

Zabuza Momochi wore a cold expression, casting his murderous gaze toward the four reckless brats, while his mind raced to formulate a countermeasure.

'In that case, I might as well turn the tables. I’ll seize the opportunity to severely injure these dead weights to pin down Tsunade, then find a chance to escape...'

Having made up his mind, Zabuza did not hesitate and acted immediately : « Water Release : Hidden Mist Jutsu ! »

Thick white mist rose instantly, obscuring everyone’s line of sight.

The four youngsters sensed something was wrong, immediately stopping in their tracks and forming a defensive formation, back to back.

They wanted to use this to guard against the enemy’s sneak attacks and avoid being picked off one by one.

Zabuza scoffed, completely unconcerned.

His figure flickered, constantly moving through the thick fog, his murderous voice coming from all directions, intending to shatter the four youngsters' will to fight.

« Eight spots ! »

« Throat, spine, lungs, liver, carotid artery, subclavian artery, kidneys, heart... So, which vital point should I choose ? »

« Wh... what ? »

Sakura was trembling with fear at these words.

She stared intensely at the fog before her, as if a vicious ghost were inside, ready to pounce at any moment and tear her to shreds.

Sasuke and Naruto were no better off.

After all, this was their first time fighting an enemy Ninja, and a heavyweight opponent like the « Seven Ninja Swordsmen » at that.

For a moment, they were at a loss, frozen in place.

Only Karin had a smirk tugging at the corner of her mouth. She whispered to reassure everyone :

« Everyone, don’t worry. I’ve already sensed his position. He’s at the eleven o’clock position ahead. Just listen to my callouts later ! »

A flash of surprise crossed Sasuke’s eyes : « Karin, you actually have this kind of sensory ability ? That’s great ! Everyone, make sure to protect Karin later ! »

Karin waved her hand, looking slightly shy, and said modestly :

« Oh, it’s nothing major. This is the 'Mind’s Eye of the Kagura' unique to our Uzumaki Clan. It’s said that even when pushed to the limit, the sensory radius is only a measly few dozen kilometers, completely not worth mentioning~ »

The others were choked up by this statement, but only Naruto’s eyes lit up, and he asked excitedly :

« Wait ! You just said that’s a secret technique of the Uzumaki Clan ? I’m also a descendant of the Uzumaki Clan. Can I learn this ability ? »

He roared frantically in his heart :

'Awesome ! Once I unlock this eye thingy, I’ll be able to beat Sasuke in every aspect. What is his Sharingan anyway ? It definitely can’t sense things from dozens of kilometers away, hahaha.'

Unfortunately, reality slapped him in the face quickly. Karin shook her head and patiently explained :

« Probably not ? This is a talent I awakened myself ; there’s no way to teach it to others. »

« Eh—— ? Why ! » Naruto instantly deflated, looking full of disappointment and unwillingness.

Just as they were chatting happily, Zabuza was starting to lose his cool. First his Kubikiribocho was sealed, and now his Silent Killing technique was negated. How was he supposed to play like this ?

He flickered, constantly changing positions, attempting to shake off that damned lock-on, but was easily pointed out by Karin every time.

« Damn it ! So what if you can sense my position ? Do you really think you wet-behind-the-ears brats can be my match in close-quarters combat ? »

Zabuza was annoyed and embarrassed. He formed hand seals rapidly, abandoning stealth, and charged straight toward the group.

« Careful ! The enemy is charging at high speed from the nine o’clock direction ! There’s a Water Clone feinting from the six o’clock direction ; leave that one to me ! »

Karin’s callout rang out crisply, and everyone instantly entered combat mode.

Before the words faded, several shuriken shot out from the churning mist ahead.

« Let me handle it ! »

Sasuke shouted, his Sharingan instantly activating.

In those crimson eyes, the trajectory of the shuriken seemed to slow down. He swung his Kunai with ease, blocking all the hidden weapons.

Zabuza’s pupils constricted sharply, and he was secretly shocked : « Another pair of Sharingan ? Is this kid actually from the Uchiha Clan ? »

However, the arrow was on the string and had to be fired.

He narrowed his eyes and simply accelerated his charge.

In the blink of an eye, the two were already in close combat.

« Clang—— ! »

The Kunai collided violently in the air, sparking.

Relying on the Sharingan’s prediction and years of hard training in taijutsu, Sasuke barely managed to keep up with Zabuza’s rhythm.

For a moment, blades clashed and shadows flew ; the two were locked in an evenly matched fight.

But looking at Sasuke, who was gritting his teeth with a flushed face, it was clear he was already at the end of his rope, and defeat was likely only a matter of time.

Fortunately, he was not fighting alone.

Naruto’s eyes lit up, quickly seizing the opportunity. With a « Shave, » he instantly closed in on Zabuza and slammed a punch toward Zabuza’s cheek.

Sakura was not to be outdone. Using a Body Flicker, she struck like lightning, delivering a kick straight to Zabuza’s crotch.

'So fast ! Is that Body Flicker ? And why didn’t I see any hand seals ? Besides, this move is too despicable... My Silent Killing technique isn’t this vicious...'

Zabuza’s pupils contracted. In the critical moment, there was nowhere to hide.

In a flash, he made a split-second decision, quickly blocking Sakura’s attack with his left arm, then choosing to take Naruto’s punch head-on, allowing himself to be sent flying backward.

Behind the team, Karin, who had just finished off the Water Clone, happened to witness this horrific scene.

She snuck a glance at Sakura Haruno, as if seeing this girl for the first time.

Sasuke and Naruto also went stiff for a moment, silently swearing in their hearts : 'Never provoke Sakura in this lifetime.'

...

In the distance, Zabuza stood up, shaken. The intense fear caused his adrenaline to spike, and for a moment, he couldn’t even feel the excruciating pain on his cheek.

He glared at Sakura with resentment, then his hands moved like illusions, forming hand seals at high speed.

Karin immediately sensed the violent fluctuations of Chakra and shouted out :

« Careful ! He’s condensing Chakra ; he must be preparing a high-power Ninjutsu. We can’t let him succeed, let’s go ! »

After saying that, she charged forward first, and the other three didn’t hesitate, immediately following suit.

However, just as they arrived, Zabuza had already completed the 44 hand seals.

« Die, you brats. »

« Water Release : Water Dragon Bullet Technique ! »

Accompanied by a roar, a ferocious water dragon slowly took shape, surging toward Karin and the others with destructive momentum.

« Oh no, does he really seal that fast ? What do we do ? »

« Don’t panic ! I still have one Great Fireball Technique ! »

« Great Fireball my ass, run ! »

At this critical moment, Asakawa Shin finally made his move.

His hands flew, forming seals rapidly : « Wind Release : Typhoon Passed ! »

In an instant, a gale howled, and the thick fog that had shrouded the world was swept away in the blink of an eye.

Immediately after, a playful glint flashed in Asakawa Shin’s eyes, and he actually stuffed his thumb into his mouth and blew hard.

His right hand inflated violently like a balloon...

In the blink of an eye, it transformed into a giant hand dozens of meters long, covered in pitch-black Armament Haki, and struck out to meet the water dragon.

In the astonished gaze of everyone, the giant hand grasped the dragon’s head with lightning speed, then seized it and refined it in an instant.

'Is it really feasible ? Tom’s trait, combined with the perfect control of Life Return... This way of fighting, doing whatever I want and being incomparably free, is it any worse than the abilities of a Mythical Zoan ?'
