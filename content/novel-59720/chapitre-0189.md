# Chapitre 189 — One Try and You'll Know

The three small snakes were in a world of ice and snow ; although they had long surpassed the scope of ordinary snakes, they still couldn’t suppress their instinctive shivering.

They hurriedly fled back onto Asakawa Shin, quickly turning into jewelry and clinging to him motionlessly.

« Ah, it’s still warmer here~ »

The people nearby couldn’t help but steal a few more glances.

They originally thought they were just ordinary accessories, but only today did they learn they were actually real snakes ?

Kakashi felt a bit embarrassed, thinking : « I wonder if I can beat these three snakes ? »

Naruto didn’t have so many scruples ; he directly reached out and poked the earring.

This provoked the snakes to hiss, which finally scared him back.

« Whoa, so fierce ! »

Naruto took two steps back and then approached again thick-facedly, « Shin, what are these ? Where did you buy them ? »

Asakawa Shin gave him a roll of his eyes.

« These are Snake Sages from Ryūchi Cave, you can’t buy them. »

His eyes darted around as he made a suggestion.

« But I remember you’re a Contractor of Mount Myōboku, right ? You could hang a couple of toads on yourself. »

Naruto imagined it and couldn’t help but shudder.

He gave a dry laugh, « Hahaha, let’s forget it. »

At this time, Haku and Sasuke walked forward, escorting Nadare Roga.

Seeing things were going south, this guy had tried to flee just now, but was quickly taken down by the two who had been keeping a close eye on him.

Seeing that he could no longer escape, Nadare Roga hurriedly crawled forward in a panic.

« Lord, and Princess Koyuki, I know I was wrong, please spare my life. »

Asakawa Shin waved his hand indifferently.

« This guy has mastered quite a few Ice Release Ninjutsu, and that Chakra Armor is also quite interesting ; take him down for strict interrogation. »

Seeing Nadare Roga being dragged away, Koyuki Kazahana finally relaxed completely.

She looked at Asakawa Shin, her heart full of emotion.

'Saying it’s for the Land of Snow, in fact, it’s all to protect my self-esteem... that was just an excuse, right ?'

The more she thought about it, the more she felt it was true.

What kind of place was the Land of Snow ?

The entire country was buried in ice and snow, the land was barren, the national strength was weak, and even the environment was extremely harsh.

Compared to the Land of Fire, it was simply a backwater ; what use would such a place be ?

'Lord Shin, he’s really too gentle, I’m crying my eyes out...'

As she thought, Koyuki Kazahana’s eyes gradually filled with tears, and her shoulders twitched as she began to cry.

Asakawa Shin found it inexplicable and had to reach out and pat her shoulder to comfort her.

Unexpectedly, Koyuki Kazahana grabbed his hand and fell into his arms.

« Lord Shin, thank you so much, not only for helping me avenge my father but also for helping me take the position of Daimyo of the Land of Snow. »

She quickly took out that Hexagonal Crystal and held it in her palms like a treasure.

« This is the key my father passed to me, and also the secret treasure of the Kazahana clan that Dotou Kazahana has always wanted to steal ; today I offer it to you ! »

Asakawa Shin frowned ; a broken Geothermal Generator, what would I want with it ?

'This woman doesn’t want to default on her debt, does she ? That won’t do.'

So he shook his head and refused without hesitation.

Koyuki Kazahana’s breath hitched, and her tears flowed even more.

She had never been good at acting out crying scenes and even needed eye drops for assistance.

But at this moment, she secretly decided that as long as she recalled today’s scene in the future, the tears would definitely come naturally.

'Uuuu, I must remember Lord Shin’s kindness.'

Koyuki Kazahana sobbed for a long time before finally calming her emotions.

« Lord Shin, could I trouble you to take me to 'The Other Side of the Rainbow' ? »

She looked toward the horizon, her hands tightly clutching the Hexagonal Crystal.

« I want to know what my father actually left for me ? Please. »

« Hmm... I see, then hold me tight. »

« Eh ? »

Before her voice fell, the two of them soared into the sky, heading straight for the place Koyuki was looking at.

Soaring together with them were the astonished director and the Cameraman who was death-gripping his camera.

« What’s going on ? Are we filming in the air ? » the director shouted loudly in the wind.

The Cameraman gritted his teeth, his hands protecting the equipment like iron pincers, his eyes incredibly determined : « Even if I die, I will film this scene ! »

In just a short while, everyone arrived at The Other Side of the Rainbow.

Six giant crystal mirrors stood towering, and a stone platform was clearly visible in the center, its slot obviously designed for the Hexagonal Crystal.

Koyuki Kazahana took a deep breath, solemnly took out the crystal, inserted it, and turned it.

A flash of light passed, and the huge device beneath their feet roared to life, making a dull clicking sound.

Along with the rising steam, the thick ice and snow covering the earth actually began to melt slowly, then shattered with a crash.

A touch of green rippled out, sweeping across the entire world in an instant.

The ice and snow between heaven and earth vanished without a trace, replaced by a vibrant spring scene.

This earth-shattering change left everyone in a long silence.

The director suddenly closed his wide-open mouth, his eyes flashing with fanatical light : « If this movie is made, it will definitely break box office records ! »

« So... this is the treasure my father left for me ? »

Koyuki Kazahana murmured to herself, staring at the reviving land beneath her feet.

Scalding tears burst from her eyes again, dripping onto the newborn green grass.

« It is indeed an incalculable fortune ! » Asakawa Shin looked around and nodded in sincere admiration.

The subsequent development of events was only natural.

Facing Konoha’s elite Ninjas and the Land of Snow’s long-dormant Restoration Samurai, Dotou Kazahana’s remnants were unable to resist at all and were soon completely wiped out.

Koyuki Kazahana successfully ascended to the position of Daimyo of the Land of Snow, while Asakawa Shin was titled « Guardian Wisdom King, » enjoying a prestigious status.

Konoha’s garrison quickly took over defense, and the core technologies of the Land of Snow were immediately sent back to Konoha for research.

Among them, the most eye-catching was the « Geothermal Generator, » which could be called black technology.

It could actually reverse a country’s natural environment ; although it was only a semi-finished product and couldn’t permanently maintain the spring scenery, its scientific research value was already incalculable.

In addition, the Chakra Armor once held by Dotou Kazahana also attracted much attention ; for Chunin and Genin, it was undoubtedly an excellent Ninja tool that could significantly enhance combat power.

However, what Asakawa Shin cared about most was the so-called Ice Release Ninjutsu.

His fingertips brushed over the organized Ice Release scroll in his hand, lost in thought.

« Not only can it manipulate ice and snow, but it can also shape them ; it’s quite interesting. If I can combine this technique with my Weather Control... »

Beside him, Koyuki Kazahana hesitated slightly and asked softly, « Lord Shin, are you leaving ? »

Asakawa Shin raised his eyes upon hearing this : « What, can’t bear to let go ? »

To his surprise, Koyuki Kazahana nodded heavily, her eyes instantly turning red, tears welling up.

« Stop, stop, stop, I give up... come here. » Asakawa Shin helplessly held his forehead.

Before his voice fell, Koyuki Kazahana immediately broke into a smile, showing no trace of her previous sadness.

Asakawa Shin shook his head, reached out to press against her lower abdomen, condensed Chakra at his fingertips, and quickly inscribed a strange Flying Thunder God formula.

« When you miss me, just inject a tiny amount of Chakra into it. »

Koyuki Kazahana nodded, seemingly understanding but not quite.

'What does he mean ? Whatever, I’ll find out tonight with one try...'

Thus, late at night.

« Koyuki Kazahana, you you you you... why did you summon me while you were doing something bad ? »

« Ah ? » Koyuki Kazahana’s cheeks were crimson, and after several hesitations, she still nodded.

« Do... »
