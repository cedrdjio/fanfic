# Chapitre 29 — Tribute

Musi Group raised 1.2 billion ryo in capital with 30% of its shares and integrated the resources of Konoha’s upper management, instantly growing into a behemoth.

Not only did they have money and power, but every major clan also sent their commercial talents to Musi Group to supervise and contribute, so the previously set development plan was carried out extremely rapidly.

Underground training ground.

Asakawa Shin was using Wood Release to ripen mulberries. He clapped his hands together, and the mulberries on the ground quickly sprouted, took root, and grew into adult mulberry trees.

Then, with visible speed, they blossomed and bore fruit, dropping seeds all over the ground, forming a cycle.

The Anbu Ninja nearby were unfazed, using Earth Release to uproot the patch of mulberry trees, load them onto ox carts, and have another group of Anbu Ninja take them away. Asakawa Shin walked forward ten meters to another plot of land and clapped his hands again.

Everyone had become accustomed to the whole process. Asakawa Shin could not even remember how many mulberry trees he had ripened ; he only felt that his palms were becoming numb from clapping.

He could not help but turn his head and ask the Anbu Ninja recording nearby : « Is it not enough yet ? How much of a gap is still left ? »

The Anbu Ninja looked at the notebook, calculated silently, and replied : « Lord Shin, the vast majority has been completed. Another 200,000 trees will be enough to fill it up. »

Asakawa Shin was shocked : « What ? Another 200,000 trees ? Just how much land did that bastard Hiashi approve ? »

« Not much. The first batch only allocated the wasteland south of Konoha Village to us, about 600 hectares. One hectare equals fifteen mu, and one mu of land can plant an average of 5,000 mulberry trees. If you walk another round and clap your hands a dozen more times, we can call it a day. »

« Nephew, the merchant you found for our channels is truly capable. 45 million mulberry trees were all delivered in just three days. Impressive, impressive. We must cooperate more in the future. » Hyuga Hiashi saw Asakawa Shin and could not help but say happily.

Asakawa Shin’s face darkened upon hearing this. He had been using Shadow Clone to attend school these past few days, and he was exhausted to the bone.

He asked unhappily : « No, do you eat mulberry leaves as meals ? Is it necessary to buy so many at this stage ? »

Hiashi was unhappy when he heard this : « Nephew, an adult mulberry tree is only 50 ryo, and a seedling is 20 ryo. Would you not buy them if it were you ? A bowl of ramen costs 60 ryo. Besides, he supports credit. We only paid a ten percent deposit, and we will give him the rest after the silk business expands. Such fools are rare these days, so I have to buy more. »

'You are the fool. 45 million mulberry trees are worth 2.25 billion ryo. The deposit paid was 225 million ryo, 25 million ryo was given to the Anbu Ninja as payment, 200 million ryo was given to the teacher as a tribute, and the rest is mine. Would I tell you that I earned 2 billion ryo in three days ?'

« Then why not buy some seedlings ? Buying only adult trees. » Asakawa Shin was also a little puzzled.

« Because it is cheap. Seedlings would take two or three years to mature. Buying adult trees directly does not cost much more. Now that we have planted a full 600 hectares of mulberry trees, it is enough for the short term. » Hyuga Hiashi smiled somewhat proudly.

« How are things going in other areas ? »

« The Aburame family’s silkworm room has been built, and large-scale breeding has begun. The silk reeling factory has also prioritized the completion of a part, and it has been put into production. A batch of raw silk has already been sent to the textile factory. We acquired a small textile factory with more than 200 skilled workers, which is enough for our initial experiments. »

« We also acquired a dye house at a high price. According to what you said, the dyes used are all extracted from pure plants, and there will absolutely be no mistakes. »

« We have purchased shops in the city center of the Capital of the Land of Fire, and they are currently being renovated. We have not bought any in other cities in the Land of Fire or the capitals of the other four countries yet. It will not be too late to buy them after silk spreads among the upper classes of the Land of Fire. Initial production capacity is definitely insufficient, so we will prioritize supplying the Daimyo and nobles of the Land of Fire. As for other countries, let them wait for now. »

Asakawa Shin nodded. After all, they were the elites of their respective families, and their work was quite reliable.

« How is the research on the pedal-operated loom that I mentioned to the R&D department ? »

« The inspiration and structure you provided were very clear, and the R&D department has already developed it. However, there are still some minor issues, and it is not very smooth to use. Now they are discussing adjustments with several veteran textile masters. It is estimated that it will be ready for mass production in a few days. »

« But do we not already have large water-powered spinning wheels ? Why do we need that ? » Hiashi asked, a little puzzled.

« Large water-powered spinning wheels are used to quickly produce ordinary silk, which means weaving raw silk into cloth first, and then degumming and dyeing it. This is called raw weaving. And the pedal-operated loom can produce colored embroidery. It involves degumming and dyeing the raw silk first, and then arranging it manually. The woven cloth naturally has patterns, and can even be customized. This is called finished-product weaving. However, the production difficulty of finished-product weaving is extremely high. Even skilled craftsmen can only weave four or five centimeters of cloth a day, and the output is limited. When the time comes, we will present it to the Daimyo as a tribute. If others want to buy it, it would not be excessive to sell it at a sky-high price, right ? »

« Hehehe, of course it is not excessive. This is the hard work of the craftsmen. Talking about money is too vulgar, hehehe. »

Ten days later, Capital of the Land of Fire, Daimyo’s Mansion.

The sunset glow reflected on the vermilion lacquered pillars of the main hall of the Capital of the Land of Fire. Guided by an attendant, Shikaku walked slowly up the jade steps.

In the main hall, the Daimyo sat high on the throne, his expression listless, looking like his soul was wandering outside.

Judging from the traces at the scene, it seemed that a banquet had just ended. The air was filled with a mixture of rouge, incense, food, and fine wine, making Shikaku frown imperceptibly.

Seeing him arrive, the Daimyo’s eyes regained some luster. He burped and said in a muffled voice :

« Hmm ? Ninja ? What is the matter ? Are you here to ask for financial allocation again ? »

Shikaku bowed slowly, his attitude neither humble nor overbearing, and said respectfully to the Daimyo :

« Reporting to the Daimyo, I am not here to ask for financial allocation this time. On the contrary, to thank you for your kindness to Konoha, we Ninja have specially prepared a treasure to present to you. »

As if he had heard something that interested him, the Daimyo’s drunkenness faded a lot. He shifted his body slightly, looked around at the nobles, and said loudly with three parts of ridicule in his expression :

« Hahaha, did you all hear that ? Ninja, wanting to present a treasure to me. »

The main hall suddenly became lively, and the surrounding nobles spoke one after another with sarcasm.

« It is really hilarious. Mere Ninja, what kind of treasure could they possibly know about. »

« Probably some broken stone picked up from some mountain ditch, right ? »

« Ignorant Ninja are like this. »

Shikaku waited for the surrounding noise to subside before taking a storage scroll from his sleeve and laying it flat on the ground. « Then, please forgive my rudeness. »

He quickly formed several hand seals, clapped one hand on the scroll, and a burst of smoke appeared. Several large boxes appeared on the spot.

« Reporting to the Daimyo, these are the treasures that Konoha has prepared for you, called silk. It is difficult to produce, looks incomparably luxurious, and is light, smooth, comfortable, and breathable when worn. Only clothes made of such precious fabrics can match your status. »

He slowly opened the box, revealing the fabric inside. Under the reflection of countless crystal lamps, the silk emitted a luster like flowing water, appearing colorful and incomparably beautiful.

The Daimyo stood up abruptly, staring fixedly at the silk, muttering :

« It is so elegant and luxurious, could it be woven from gold and silver ? Good, good, very good ! »
