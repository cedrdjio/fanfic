# Chapitre 216 — Accidental Death Liability Waiver

« Fuinjutsu : Phantom Dragons Nine Consuming Seals. » Pain’s voice sounded as if it came from the depths of the underworld.

Immediately after, a burst of azure light shot out from the Demonic Statue’s mouth, and nine dragon heads instantly bit into the unconscious Han.

« Ugh— ! »

Even in a coma, Han’s body convulsed violently from the intense Pain.

The Chakra within him, along with the boiling Five-Tails Chakra, was being frantically extracted by the dragon heads and continuously funneled back into the Demonic Statue.

« So... so brutal... » Kurotsuchi watched the bloody scene, subconsciously covering her mouth, her face as pale as paper.

Asakawa Shin stood to the side, one hand in his pocket, his eyes behind the mask calmly watching everything.

He glanced at the trembling Kurotsuchi and said indifferently, « Scared ? It’s not too late to back out now ; I can send you back to Iwagakure. »

« No... no need ! » Kurotsuchi shook her head hurriedly, a flash of quick-wittedness in her eyes.

She knew that if she backed down now, she might not even know how she died. Moreover, she still had to be an undercover agent and ruthlessly backstab Deidara in the future !

Just then, Pain turned his head, his gaze shooting toward Asakawa Shin like lightning. « Tobi, you are responsible for Kurotsuchi’s placement to the end. If she has any ulterior motives, kill her without mercy. »

« Understood, » Asakawa Shin nodded.

A few days later, the ritual ended.

Han lay slumped on the ground like a puddle of mud, his breath weak, the Five-Tails having been completely stripped away.

Pain’s figure gradually dissipated, and the other members left as well. Soon, only Asakawa Shin, Kurotsuchi, and the unconscious, dying Han remained in the cave.

« Let’s go. » Asakawa Shin picked Han up with one hand, skillfully sealing him into a scroll, then looked at Kurotsuchi. « I’ll take you to the base. »

« Oh... » Kurotsuchi nodded obediently and hurried to keep up.

...

« This is the Akatsuki Organization’s base ? It’s actually located in the Hidden Rain Village ? Could it be... the mastermind behind the scenes is actually that legendary Ninja Half-God—Hanzou ? »

Kurotsuchi couldn’t help but cry out in surprise, taking a sharp breath as she was startled by her own bold guess.

Thump !

Asakawa Shin unceremoniously gave her a rap on the head and urged her impatiently, « Shut up and keep up. »

« That hurts ! You jerk, Dago ! »

Kurotsuchi clutched her swollen forehead, her eyes teary, but she didn’t dare delay for a moment as she trotted after him.

At the logistics department, Konan expressionlessly handed over two uniforms and a bottle of nail polish, her tone serious as she cautioned :

« Use them sparingly ; the organization’s funds must be spent where they are needed most. »

Asakawa Shin glanced at her, secretly admiring her in his heart.

How could she say such a thing so righteously ?

If Kakuzu knew his hard-earned money was being 'rightfully' squandered like this, he would probably cry himself unconscious over the ledgers on the spot.

Kurotsuchi, on the other hand, took it seriously and nodded solemnly, completely deceived by this 'frugal' big sister.

« Don’t worry, Big Sister ! I’ll definitely use them sparingly and never waste a single needle or piece of thread from the organization ! »

'Hmph, bad woman, specifically deceiving naive little kids !' Asakawa Shin silently labeled Konan in his mind.

To his surprise, just as the thought crossed his mind, Konan suddenly turned her head to look over.

Those indifferent hydrangea-colored pupils seemed capable of seeing through a person’s heart as they stared straight at Asakawa Shin’s mask.

Asakawa Shin’s heart skipped a beat, and he subconsciously took half a step back, laughing dryly. « Wh-what ? Looking at me like that all of a sudden is pretty creepy. »

Konan reached out expressionlessly and pointed at his somewhat wrinkled Akatsuki Organization robe, her voice cold. « Your clothes are dirty ; remember to wash them. Do not ruin the organization’s external image. Also, don’t lead that new idiot astray ; the leader is still watching her. »

« I know, I know, » Asakawa Shin waved his hand, pulling Kurotsuchi—who was still immersed in her 'thriftiness' education—along as they fled. « Let’s go, let’s go. I’ll take you to get your room key. »

As Kurotsuchi walked, she looked back at Konan’s noble and aloof figure, whispering, « Dago, I think the big sister is a pretty good person. Not only does she care about subordinates, but she’s also so frugal. Why do you seem so afraid of her ? »

« Afraid of her ? What a joke, I’m her elder ! »

Asakawa Shin retorted righteously, then changed the subject, lowering his voice to warn, « But remember what Konan said ; never waste anything. Otherwise... just wait to be hunted to the ends of the earth by that miser Kakuzu. »

Kurotsuchi shrunk her neck and subconsciously clutched the bottle of nail polish to her chest as if it were some rare treasure.

Just then, a figure suddenly turned the corner at the end of the hallway.

Deidara, with a lollipop in his mouth, walked over with feigned nonchalance.

When he saw Kurotsuchi, he rolled his eyes exaggeratedly. « Tch, I didn’t expect you to actually crawl in here. How boring. »

« You... ! » Kurotsuchi was about to flare up when she remembered her 'undercover' status. She forced her anger down and instead squeezed out a bright smile. « Big Brother Deidara is right ! »

Deidara was stunned, and the lollipop in his mouth fell to the ground with a 'clack'.

He rubbed his eyes, looking incredulously at the bowing and scraping girl before him, feeling his worldview completely shattered.

« Hey, you... could you have been brainwashed by Tobi ? » Deidara took half a step back, looking at Asakawa Shin with a wary face. « Or did you use some kind of Genjutsu ? »

Asakawa Shin shrugged, looking innocent. « I didn’t do anything. Maybe Miss Kurotsuchi has finally seen the light ? »

'Seen the light my foot ! Sooner or later, I’ll open up your skull !' Kurotsuchi roared frantically in her mind, but on the surface, she maintained that 'positive and upward' fake smile.

Just then, a cold wind blew from the end of the corridor, making Kurotsuchi involuntarily shudder.

« Oh ? Is this the new brat in the organization ? »

A man covered in stitch marks walked over slowly, his lifeless eyes scanning Kurotsuchi as if appraising the value of a piece of cargo.

Kurotsuchi felt a chill rush from the soles of her feet to the top of her head, and she instinctively shrank behind Asakawa Shin. « Wh-who is this ? He feels so scary... »

« That’s Kakuzu. »

Asakawa Shin lowered his voice, his tone tinged with a bit of schadenfreude. « He’s the Akatsuki Organization’s 'Finance Minister.' The funds Konan mentioned earlier were all traded for with this guy’s life. »

« Finance Minister ? » Before Kurotsuchi could react, Kakuzu’s eerie voice rang out again.

« Sign this. »

Kakuzu’s eyes narrowed slightly as several black tentacles extended from his sleeve, wriggling and squirming in the air. « The organization’s 'Frugality Commitment Letter' and 'Accidental Death Liability Waiver.' Only after you sign these will you truly be a member of Akatsuki. »

He tossed out two pieces of paper covered in dense small print, with a blood-red skull drawn prominently at the bottom.

Kurotsuchi tremblingly took the pen. Looking at the unscrupulous terms stating that 'in the event of accidental death, ownership of the corpse belongs to the organization,' her tears almost fell.

This isn’t an organization ; this is clearly a sweatshop ! Just what kind of place has that bastard Deidara been wasting his life in ?

While signing with a mournful face, she cursed eighteen generations of Deidara’s ancestors again in her heart.

Watching Kurotsuchi’s utterly despondent look, Deidara suddenly felt much better and raised his chin triumphantly :

« How about it ? Scared now ? I told you before, the Akatsuki Organization is no place for a flower in a greenhouse like you. »

« I... » Kurotsuchi took a deep breath, clenching her fists tightly until her nails dug into her palms.

She looked up, eyes shimmering with tears, yet she stubbornly refused to let them fall, saying word by word, « I’ll sign ! For art... ah, no, for the organization ! I’m willing to do anything ! »
