# Chapitre 175 — Surrender

Inside the Hokage Office, bright sunlight streamed through the window, making people feel warm and cozy.

« Hahaha, I’ve really become the Hokage ! »

Tsunade sat upright on the Hokage’s throne, laughing heartily toward the sky, her golden hair showing off her bold personality in the shifting light and shadows.

Then, her gaze shifted, glancing down at Asakawa Shin, her lips curling into a charming arc.

« What are you standing there for ? Hokage-sama is thirsty now, so hurry up and pour some water. »

« Huh ? Me ? »

Asakawa Shin was stunned and pointed at himself in disbelief.

Tsunade raised an eyebrow and nodded as if it were a matter of course. « Nonsense, I’m the Hokage now, and you’re my Hokage secretary. What’s wrong with serving me ? Do you expect me to pour it myself ? »

« Nani ? When did I become your secretary ? How come I didn’t know about this ? »

Asakawa Shin’s face darkened, and he reluctantly moved to pour the water.

Tsunade took the cup and drained it in one gulp, letting out a satisfied sigh.

« Tsk, I have to say, being Hokage really has its perks. It’s great, hahahaha ! »

« Um... Senior Sister... »

« Hmm ? Call me Hokage-sama ! »

« Alright, Senior Sister. I wanted to say, shouldn’t your secretary be Shizune-san ? Why did you call me here ? I have things to do later. »

Tsunade frowned. « That won’t do. Shizune is swamped at the Konoha Hospital ; there are so many injured people that she can’t get away. You’re idle anyway, so I called you. »

She narrowed her eyes, her gaze sweeping over Asakawa Shin with mischievous intent.

« What, is it beneath you to keep a great beauty like me company ? »

Asakawa Shin hurriedly waved his hands to explain.

« No, no, how could it be ? But if an office romance gets out... wouldn’t the impact be bad ? »

Tsunade sneered, her face full of disdain.

« So what ? I said I didn’t want to be the Hokage, but you guys insisted. What choice did I have ? As they say, you get what you pay for. I must let you all know how foolish your initial choice was !

Don’t be afraid, I’ll take responsibility if anything happens. We’ll be as outrageous as we want ! »

She beckoned with her finger, her tone lazy : « My shoulders are killing me, come over and give me a massage. »

Asakawa Shin nodded, thinking to himself that they probably really were sore. After all, Senior Sister Tsunade was exceptionally gifted, and her daily load was too heavy. It would be strange if they weren’t sore.

« Senior Sister, massaging them won’t actually help much ; it only treats the symptoms, not the cause. Why don’t I teach you a way to lighten the load ? »

Tsunade raised an eyebrow, interested. « Oh ? You have such a skill, Junior Brother ? Hurry up and teach me ! »

Asakawa Shin smiled triumphantly and immediately got to work.

Under Tsunade’s dumbfounded gaze, he lifted her heavy load and gently rested it on the desk, then smiled proudly.

« How is it, Senior Sister ? Don’t your shoulders feel much lighter all of a sudden ? »

Once Tsunade realized what happened, her face turned bright red. She landed a punch on Asakawa Shin’s abdomen, sending him flying through the air, shattering the glass as he disappeared into the horizon.

« Eh ? I didn’t use Monstrous Strength, so how did he fly so far ? I know, that damn Jiraiya must have corrupted Shin. »

The more Tsunade thought about it, the angrier she got. She tapped the desk irritably. « Someone, get Jiraiya here for me, right now ! Immediately ! »

In the sky, Asakawa Shin glided comfortably, cold laughter in his heart.

« Humph ! Wanting me to be a secretary is just wishful thinking. With just a small trick, Hokage-sama lost her composure. At this moment, all I want to say is : if you’re bad, practice more. »

Meanwhile, the fall of the Raikage and Kazekage, combined with the capture of the Ichibi and Eight-Tails, caused the landscape of the entire Ninja World to collapse instantly, triggering an unprecedented upheaval.

While Kumogakure and Sunagakure were reshuffling, the Two-Tails Jinchuriki, Yugito, was somewhat confused.

During that great war, she had been inexplicably transported to the depths of the desert and narrowly escaped with her life.

By the time she rushed back to the Land of Fire at full speed, the war had already settled, and the intelligence she gathered was simply unbelievable.

After confirming it repeatedly, she had to accept this harsh reality and then hurriedly rushed to Kumogakure.

As soon as she stepped into the Raikage Office, all the high-ranking officials looked at her in unison.

Mabui frowned and took a step forward. « Yugito-sama, everyone has unanimously decided that you should take over the position of Raikage. What do you think ? »

« What ? Me ? »

Yugito was shocked and repeatedly waved her hands in refusal.

« I can’t, how could I be the Raikage... »

Before she could finish, Mabui interrupted her. « Yugito-sama, the situation is urgent now, so please do not decline.

The Fourth Raikage has died in battle, and Killer Bee-sama along with the elite forces have been captured by Konoha. Now, besides you, there are no other candidates left in the Village. »

Yugito remained silent for a long time before finally sighing helplessly and nodding in agreement. « Alright, Mabui, now is not the time for hesitation. I will do my best. »

Seeing her agree, Mabui was finally relieved. She turned to take her leave. « I’ll go inform the others. Yugito-sama, please make some preparations ; the inauguration ceremony for the Fifth Raikage will be held immediately. »

After she left, Yugito tiredly held her forehead, feeling the heavy burden on her shoulders. She couldn’t help but sigh softly, « Sigh, how did things turn out like this ? It’s really a headache. »

If Kumogakure could still pull out a Kage-level combatant to keep up appearances, then Sunagakure was truly facing a talent gap at this moment.

Inside the Kazekage Office, the atmosphere was depressingly suffocating.

Chiyo was the first to speak, breaking the silence. « Let’s all talk about what to do now. The Kazekage has died in battle, and the Ichibi Jinchuriki has been captured. Who else in our Sunagakure can step forward ? »

Everyone remained silent ; everyone knew the answer to that question.

Ebizo sighed. « Sister, you should take the helm of Sunagakure. We... can’t afford any more turmoil now. »

The others chimed in one after another.

« Yes, Chiyo-sama, please don’t decline. Sunagakure cannot do without you now. »

« That’s right. You are the pillar of our Sunagakure, and only you can lead us out of this predicament now. »

Chiyo nodded slightly and then gave a stern command : « Pass down my order. Surrender to Konoha immediately and end this war ! »

Before she finished speaking, the whole room was in an uproar.

« What ? Surrender ? Chiyo-sama, how can we do that ! »

« Indeed, how can we give up so easily ? Otherwise, wouldn’t the sacrifices of Rasa and many other Ninjas be in vain ? »

Chiyo snorted coldly, her sharp gaze scanning the room as she shouted sternly :

« Shut up, all of you ! Rasa’s death is already beyond recovery. What’s most important now is to avoid more senseless casualties ! »

The crowd still wanted to argue, but Chiyo waved them off. « My mind is made up, no more words ! While Konoha is in the middle of a power transition, we must surrender immediately !

Otherwise, when the Konoha army arrives at our borders, will you go first ? Or should this old woman go first ? »

As soon as these words were spoken, the entire room fell into a dead silence.

Was she joking ? Even an alliance as massive as theirs had been so thoroughly defeated. Wouldn’t sending them be the same as sending them to their deaths ?

Since Chiyo had made the final decision, they would just push the infamy of being cowards who surrendered without a fight onto her head. At least it would give them an explanation for the Ninjas below.

After the meeting adjourned, Ebizo looked at Chiyo with concern and asked softly, « Sister, is this really okay ? You’re at such an age, yet you have to bear such infamy. »

Chiyo waved her hand and said indifferently, « It’s fine. I’m already halfway into the grave, so what do I have to fear ? Besides, what else can we do besides surrendering ? Do we really want to fight Konoha to the bitter end ?

Surrendering is the best choice ; at least it can save the Village. It’s nothing more than paying Konoha a sum of money. Surely... they wouldn’t be interested in this barren land of ours ? »
