# Chapitre 151 — Departure, Ryūchi Cave

The drawing ceremony for the third round of the Chunin Exams finally came to an end.

Naruto glanced at Sasuke beside him, a flash of brilliance in his eyes, and probed, « Hey, Sasuke, now that we’re heading back to the Village, what are your plans for the next month ? You wouldn’t still be thinking of training desperately day and night, right ? »

Sasuke’s figure paused, he shook his head nonchalantly, « How could that be ? My opponent is that Gaara, what use is last-minute cramming ? Might as well have a good time ! What about you ? You wouldn’t... be wanting to secretly outwork me, would you ? »

Naruto scratched his head, decisively playing dumb, « No way, no way. My opponent is that young lady Hinata from the Hyuga Clan. No matter how much I train, I won’t be able to beat her. Might as well just lie flat to avoid embarrassing myself. »

« Haha, that’s true. »

Sasuke chuckled lightly, then turned to look at Asakawa Shin, who had been silent, « By the way, Shin, what about you ? You’re already ridiculously strong, you wouldn’t... still be planning to outwork us at this stage, right ? »

Asakawa Shin raised an eyebrow, looking at his two good brothers, « No, are you guys really planning to slack off ? Haha, count me in. By the way, why don’t we go fishing together tomorrow ? The kind without helmets. »

« No, no, I plan to sleep all day tomorrow. »

« Me too, those five days of the second exam were just too exhausting. I plan to rest well first. But, Shin, why would you need to wear a helmet to fish ? »

Asakawa Shin patted Naruto’s head with a smile, « Naruto, don’t worry about the details... Alright, since you guys are busy, let’s make it another time. I’m going first. »

...

The next day, 4 AM, before dawn.

Asakawa Shin had set an alarm and woke up early. Before he even opened his eyes, he released his sensory technique to probe outwards.

« Damn it, I knew it. Naruto and the others really don’t have good intentions. They’ve already crawled out of bed at this hour. It seems... I can’t slack off either. »

Asakawa Shin sensed that everyone was secretly working hard, and deep down, he felt inexplicably anxious, his sleepiness instantly dissipating.

And so...

« Senior Brother, is there something important that you specifically asked Nidaime(2nd)-sama to teleport me back for at this hour ? »

Orochimaru looked at Asakawa Shin in front of him, couldn’t help but lick his lips, a trace of obsession flashing deep in his eyes.

« Is it impossible to look for you if there isn’t anything important ? » Asakawa Shin frowned slightly, his tone carrying a hint of displeasure.

« How could that be ? You can look for me whenever you want, Junior Brother. Not to mention 4 AM, even in the middle of the night... I’ll be there whenever you call. »

Hearing Orochimaru say this, Asakawa Shin smiled with satisfaction, « That’s good. I do have something I need your help with this time. Senior Brother, you are a contractor of Ryūchi Cave, you should know its specific location, right ? Take me there. »

Orochimaru was stunned for a moment, then nodded in agreement, « No problem, but Ryūchi Cave is far away, we might need to spend some time. »

Asakawa Shin waved his hand impatiently, « Why go through so much trouble ? Senior Brother, just reverse-summon yourself there, and wait for me at the entrance of the Ryūchi Cave secret realm. Don’t you have my Flying Thunder God Mark on you ? I’ll just teleport over, won’t that be fine ? »

« ... »

After some operations, Orochimaru disappeared into a puff of smoke. About ten minutes later, Asakawa Shin sensed the Flying Thunder God Mark on him again.

With a thought, Asakawa Shin crossed a thousand miles to reach Orochimaru’s side.

« Is this Ryūchi Cave ? »

Asakawa Shin looked around, his gaze falling on the magnificent, palace-like hall in front of him, a hint of playfulness flashing in his eyes, « It’s completely different from what I imagined. It’s truly grand. »

Orochimaru opened his mouth, wanting to say something but stopping.

Seeing this, Asakawa Shin raised the corners of his mouth and smiled gently : « Senior Brother, thank you for this trip. But I know the rules. Let me continue the rest of the way alone. »

Orochimaru hesitated for a moment, but still urged : « Junior Brother, to enter Ryūchi Cave, you need to pass through layer upon layer of trials. Although your strength is very strong, you must be extra careful. Moreover, the cultivation of Sage Arts is extremely dangerous. If you really can’t master it, you must... not force it. »

« Sage Arts ? » Asakawa Shin was slightly stunned, then smiled, « Thank you for the reminder, Senior Brother. I will act according to my ability. In that case, I will send you back first. »

Before he finished speaking, his figure flashed, and he disappeared from the spot with Orochimaru. In the blink of an eye, he was left alone.

« I didn’t expect Senior Brother Orochimaru to worry about me. It’s really surprising. »

Asakawa Shin was quite moved and couldn’t help but reflect inwardly on whether he had been too harsh on Orochimaru in his daily life...

However, at this very moment, Orochimaru, thousands of miles away, was pacing anxiously, worried sick.

« Junior Brother, you must come back unscathed. Don’t ruin my perfect vessel. »

...

Asakawa Shin walked towards the palace complex. Before he could even get close, the palace doors opened on their own with a creak. A beauty in palace attire walked out slowly, her clothes fluttering, her temperament ethereal.

« Welcome to the Holy Land, Ryūchi Cave. I have been waiting here for a long time. I am Tagitsuhime, ordered by the White Snake Sage to specially welcome the distinguished guest here. »

She bowed slightly, her tone gentle and solemn, « The distinguished guest has come from afar. It’s been a hard journey. Please follow me inside. »

After speaking, Tagitsuhime turned and led the way. Seeing this, Asakawa Shin hurriedly followed and entered the palace together.

Passing through the long corridor with carved beams and painted pillars, the view in front of him suddenly opened up. He saw that the interior of the palace was simple and elegant, surrounded by spiritual mist.

« Please forgive the poor hospitality. »

Tagitsuhime stopped, turned to the side, and signaled with a smile, « Please make yourself at home, distinguished guest. All the delicacies here are specialties of our Ryūchi Cave. You can enjoy them to your heart’s content, no need to be restrained. »

Tagitsuhime smiled and extended her jade hand, making a gentle gesture of invitation.

He saw that the dining table in the distance was filled with all kinds of fine wines and delicacies, with an appetizing aroma.

Asakawa Shin swept his gaze over them and nodded slightly, « These foods are attractive in color and fragrant. I just don’t know how they taste. You... have performed this illusion quite well. »

Before he finished speaking, the smile on Tagitsuhime’s face suddenly froze, and her originally gentle expression instantly turned into a cold chill.

She snorted coldly, her figure twisting and changing violently. The originally beautiful beauty in palace attire turned into a hideous demon in an instant.

With the drastic change in her form, the magnificent palaces and pavilions around also made cracking sounds, collapsing with a roar and turning into dust in the sky.

The delicacies that were mouth-watering just a moment ago now all turned into a handful of yellow soil and withered bones under his feet.

« How did you find out ? Did Orochimaru tell you in advance ? »

Asakawa Shin shrugged, looking innocent, « Your illusion is indeed exquisite, but unfortunately, I am a sensory-type Ninja, so I saw through it at a glance. »

Tagitsuhime widened her eyes, « What ? How could this be ? »

She gritted her teeth, seemingly unwilling, but still waved her hand, « This level... I’ll count it as passed. The White Snake Sage is in the temple deep within Ryūchi Cave. Go find it yourself ! »

Before she finished speaking, her figure flickered and disappeared into the mist, leaving no trace in an instant.

Asakawa Shin didn’t care about this at all. He still walked forward calmly towards the vaguely visible lights in the distance, a playful smile curling at the corners of his mouth.

« A trial game ? How interesting... »
