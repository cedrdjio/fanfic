# Chapitre 2 — A Friend's Little Theater

« What’s so dazzling ? »

« Eh ? Did I say that out loud ? No... no, I meant the sunlight is so dazzling, I wasn’t talking about you. »

Naruto explained guiltily, « And my name isn’t 'Hey,' I’m Uzumaki Naruto, the man who will become a Hokage that surpasses all previous generations ! »

« Oh. »

« What do you mean 'Oh' ? Why aren’t you laughing at me ? Whenever I said that before, everyone would make fun of me. »

« Why should I laugh at you ? Being able to shout out your dream loudly is a great thing. »

Nothing can stop a man from rushing toward the sea of stars !«

'He understands me, he’s so pretty, and he speaks so nicely.' Naruto looked at the boy before him in shock, feeling his little heart thumping wildly. This was a feeling of excitement he had never felt before. 'Is this what the big sister next door called [Love] ? Is it ? It must be.'

»I only gave my name, I... I still don’t know yours.«

»Me ? I’m Asakawa Shin. A man destined for greatness in the future.«

Asakawa Shin spoke confidently. As long as he befriended Naruto and Sasuke and focused on steady development, the three of them would dominate the future. All according to plan.

»By the way, why did everyone on the street call you a monster just now ? Are you actually that... ? The legendary one.«

The voice wasn’t loud, but to Naruto, it sounded like thunder crashing through the nine heavens.

The distant laughter seemed to fade. The bright sunlight poured down, yet it offered not a shred of warmth.

The colors of the world were vanishing, with a streak of crimson bleeding through the gray and white.

'Is he going to leave me too ? Why does everyone call me a monster ? I haven’t done anything, I’m clearly not...'

»I’m not, I’m not a monster ! Why does everyone...«

»Oh, so you’re not. Then there’s no problem.«

»Wha... what ? You’re not afraid of me ? You don’t hate me ?«

»Why should I be afraid of you ? Why should I hate you ? Are you going to hurt me ?«

»I... of course I won’t !«

»So then there’s no problem. Let’s go play together.«

...Okay ! »

'Today is truly a beautiful day. The sunlight is just right, and the wind is gentle.'

...

After that day, Asakawa Shin and Uzumaki Naruto became friends. The two often played together, leaving behind many happy memories over the course of a year—mostly.

« Naruto, why do we only graffiti the Third Hokage’s face ? Why don’t we paint the other three ? »

« Because he doesn’t fit in. »

« What do you mean ? How does he not fit in ? »

« The other three Hokage are all dead, but he’s still alive. »

« ? »

...

« Naruto, you got your allowance today, right ? Let’s go eat some Three-Color Dango. »

« But they won’t sell them to me. »

« Easy. You have the money, I have the reputation. It’s a win-win cooperation. »

« Makes sense ! »

...

« Naruto, how did you catch so many fish ? How come I can’t catch any ? Teach me, will you ? »

« Huh ? Isn’t this something anyone with hands can do ? »

« Huh ? I’ll give you one more chance to rephrase that ! »

...

...Of course, sometimes it wasn’t so pleasant.

« Naruto, you bastard ! You actually gave me milk that was a year past its expiration date. I’ve had diarrhea for three days ! »

« Shin, you really don’t understand anything ! You have 'Sweet-Milk Intolerance.' »

« Some people and dogs just don’t have the fortune to absorb the nutrition in milk, so they get diarrhea. »

« Bullshit ! It’s called Lactose Intolerance. Wait, didn’t I throw away all your expired food ? How is there still more ? »

« Why throw it away ? It’s such a waste. I picked it back up after you left. »

« I was afraid you wouldn’t drink it, so I specifically threw away the packaging and poured it into a cup before handing it to you. I didn’t expect you to find out in the end. Hehe~ »

« ? »

« Also, Shin, how can someone have diarrhea for three days ? I’ve been eating this for years, don’t I know ? »

« It’s just an occasional bout ; you’ll be fine after a nap. Sometimes you need to look for the cause within yourself. Maybe you’re just weak ? »

« U-ZU-MA-KI NA-RU-TO ! »

In the end, Naruto was beaten black and blue, looking just like a pig’s head.

Under Shin’s « Serious Series » of strict education, he finally realized his mistake and sincerely promised never to touch expired food again.

Seeing this, Shin naturally forgave him generously. After all, he wasn’t a petty person~

...

« Auntie Hanako, the food you make is so delicious ! »

« Hahaha, if little Naruto likes it, eat more. Try this Fried Shrimp ; it’s my masterpiece. It’s a pity Kenji and Shin don’t like it ! »

« How can that be ? It’s clearly so good ! Shin is so inconsiderate. I’ll eat it all up, not leaving a single bite. »

« ? »

'When I send a question mark, it’s not that I have a problem, but that I think YOU have a problem, Naruto-kun. You’re dead. Even the Six Paths can’t save you, I said it !'

« Hiss... why does it suddenly feel a bit chilly ? »

« Oh ? The weather is turning cold, so you should wear more clothes. Little Naruto doesn’t take care of himself at all ! Come, have a bowl of hot soup to warm up. »

« Auntie Hanako, you’re so kind and so beautiful. If only you were my mother. I’m so jealous of Shin. »

« Teeheeheehee... » Hanako laughed until she sounded like a crowing rooster.

« ? »

...

« Naruto, you really do love Ichiraku Ramen, don’t you ? You come here as soon as you get your allowance. You’ve eaten it so many times and still can’t get enough. »

« Of course ! This is my second favorite food ! Especially this extra-large bowl of Chashu Pork Rib Ramen—it’s filling and delicious ! I love it so much. »

« Oh ? It’s only ranked second ? I think it could be number one. By the way, what’s your number one ? »

« Auntie Hanako’s cooking, of course. It tastes like a mother’s cooking ; it’s really delicious. If my mother were still around, she would probably cook this well too, right ? »

« ? »

« Asakawa Shin, I heard from little Naruto that your favorite food is Ichiraku Ramen. Is it even better than your mother’s cooking ? »

« Is that so ? Why don’t you tell your mother about it ? I’d love to learn a thing or two~ »

« Lest you keep being a picky eater and fail to grow tall, and it becomes your mother’s fault~ »

...

« Naruto, we’re starting at the Ninja Academy in a few days, right ? With so many people, do you think we’ll be in the same class ? »

« Six Paths Sage protect me, Third Grandpa protect me. Please let me be in the same class as Shin. He’s my only friend, I’m begging you. »

« It’s okay if we’re not in the same class. I’ll miss you, Naruto. And with so many classmates, we’ll make new friends too~ »

« No ! I don’t want that ! »

...

In short, the plan was going very smoothly. Not only had he successfully befriended Naruto, but barring any surprises, they would be in the same class.

As for Sasuke, he had run into him a few times while wandering around, but every time, that man was by Sasuke’s side—yes, the ruthless clan-slayer, Uchiha Itachi.

It was still too dangerous to make contact now. He had to realize he wasn’t a nobody anymore.

As the best friend of the Nine-Tails Jinchuriki, Uzumaki Naruto, he was already on the radar of the Hokage and the 'Shadow of the Hokage.' Approaching the Uchiha Clan rashly would likely cause trouble.

If it were just Sasuke alone, it would be fine, but currently, the Uchiha brothers were like conjoined twins, far too clingy.

No rush. I’ll play with him later.

Never mind their current brotherly love ; after the night of the massacre, the two brothers will have a falling out and part ways.

At that time, Sasuke will surely be in total disarray, his mind unguarded. I’ll take that opportunity to step in.

When the time comes, I’ll use a thousand methods and ten thousand schemes. Little Sasuke will be mine to manipulate as I please.

Jie jie jie jie jie~

...

Uchiha Itachi, carrying Sasuke on his back, stared blankly at the boy laughing maniacally on the street.

From time to time, the boy would mutter incomprehensible phrases like 'not dictated by heaven,' 'plucking the stars,' and 'like a long night.' Combined with that laughter, he looked somewhat deranged.

« To be so young and already crazy. What a waste of a good face. Sasuke, stay away from that guy in the future. I’m afraid he’ll rub off on you. »

...Okay, Brother.«
