# Chapitre 59 — Refund

« The match begins ! »

Looking at Hinata, whose oppressive aura was fully unleashed before her, Ino Yamanaka tensed her nerves, yet stood rooted to the spot, not daring to retreat even half a step.

The words Hinata used to mock Sakura just moments ago were still ringing in her ears ; if she were to create distance now, she would inevitably become the next clown !

And as everyone knows, there can only be two jokers in a deck of cards, and Ino Yamanaka absolutely did not want to break that rule.

She also dared not attack rashly, as her mind kept replaying that vicious headbutt from Hinata—a single strike that sent Sakura flying ten meters into the air, vomiting blood uncontrollably. It was truly terrifying, and just thinking about it now still left her with lingering fear.

'Damn it ! What on earth should I do !'

'I can’t beat her, and I don’t dare retreat ! Do I really have to just admit defeat ?'

'No, that is absolutely out of the question !'

'I am the team leader personally appointed by Shin, if I admit defeat without even fighting... wouldn’t that make Shin lose all face ?'

'Forget it, I’ll just... I’ll just fight her to the end ! ! !'

Just as she was preparing to make a move, she suddenly heard Hinata speak in a soft, sweet voice :

« Um... Ino, how about... how about we just make this match a light spar ? »

Ino was startled, and in the end, she couldn’t help herself. Like a frightened bird, she jumped backward to create distance :

'Oh no... oh no, I did it instinctively... I’m done for !'

'...Huh ? Why is it so quiet ? Why hasn’t Hinata started yet ? Is she not going to mock me ?'

Ino composed herself and looked toward Hinata, only to see her tilting her head, staring at her with a dazed, cute expression. Those clear eyes clearly revealed a hint of innocence and stupidity.

When she noticed Ino’s gaze, she even smiled at her in a friendly manner. This smile was so sweet, yet it made Ino’s heart inexplicably tremble, and a chill quietly rose within her :

'What’s going on ? How is it that Hinata seems like a completely different person... Something’s not right. Could there be some conspiracy ?'

Ino was not wrong ; Hinata had indeed changed into a different person, or to be precise, a different personality. As for why ?

That was, of course, specifically requested by Shin. Let’s rewind time to last night—in the exclusive kitchen for Shin and Hinata...

« Oh ? You want to let the sweet Hinata fight Ino ? Tch, you kid really know how to be chivalrous, don’t you ? » Hinata looked at Shin with a playful expression ; she hadn’t expected him to suddenly make this request.

« I have no choice ! You don’t know your own strength ; what if you break Ino ? » Shin didn’t even look at Hinata, speaking as if it were just a casual remark, not taking the request seriously at all.

Hinata stared fixedly at Shin, a flash of sharpness in her eyes as she decisively replied : « The proposal is good, but I refuse ! The sweet Hinata has a soft personality and too many variables ; I must ensure I win this match ! »

Shin didn’t even look up as he immediately replied : « Three private lessons, and I guarantee I will teach you the intricacies of Rokushiki without reservation. Besides, this is just a small effort for you, right ? Why not do it ? »

« Ten lessons, after all, I have to take on a certain amount of risk ! »

« What kind of risk could you possibly have ? Four lessons, no more ! »

« Seven lessons, or I won’t agree ! »

« Deal ! »

'...Damn it, I asked for too little !' Hinata felt a bit annoyed, but she quickly rolled her eyes and continued to tempt Shin : « What about Sakura ? What’s your offer for her ? »

Shin looked completely puzzled : « Sakura ? What does Sakura have to do with me ? »

Hinata had black lines on her forehead : « ...I can offer a discount ! »

« ... »

« Say something ! »

« ... »

« Hm ? Are you there ? »

On the battlefield.

Hinata still made no move, seemingly waiting for Ino’s reply.

Ino also slowly calmed down and carefully considered Hinata’s words. 'There doesn’t seem to be a problem, right ? Besides, at worst, it’s just a loss ; I wasn’t counting on winning anyway...'

So, she wiped the cold sweat from her forehead, cleared her throat, and replied : « Okay, Hinata, friendship first, competition second ; let’s just make this match a light spar. »

Hinata also nodded happily, seemingly very satisfied with this result : « Then Ino, I... I’m coming. »

Ino immediately tensed her body, concentrating her spirit, preparing to meet the storm-like attacks that would follow.

Unexpectedly, however, it was completely different from what she imagined. Hinata seemed to use no force at all, landing a light, fluttering punch that Ino easily dodged.

'Huh ? What’s going on ? Is Hinata going easy on me ?' Ino looked at Hinata in astonishment, and Hinata returned a sweet smile to her.

This made Ino even more convinced that Hinata was truly going easy on her. While feeling relieved, she couldn’t help but condemn herself in her heart :

'Hinata is truly my best friend. To keep me from losing too embarrassingly, she’s actually throwing the match in public !'

'She thinks of me in everything, and yet I often call Hinata a glutton in my heart. How could I do that ? I... I really deserve to die !'

After her self-reproach, Ino felt a little bit of joy. Sakura was beaten to the point of being unable to take care of herself by Hinata in just a few moves, yet she could fight Hinata to a standstill...

Rounding it up, doesn’t that make me the second-best person in the girls' group ?

Thinking of this, Ino’s mood brightened. Her coordination with Hinata was becoming increasingly tacit ; the two fought from the east side to the west side, trading blows and parrying moves, making for a very lively fight...

A few minutes later, seemingly feeling that the act had gone on long enough, the two exchanged a tacit look...

Ino seemed to make a mistake that Hinata seized upon, and with one punch, she was sent flying more than ten meters away, sliding another two or three meters upon landing, crying out : « I can’t take it anymore ! »

Then, her little face tilted to the side, and she fainted...

Might Guy watched from the side, his eyelids twitching as he stood frozen on the spot, only reacting when he saw Shin winking at him...

He hurriedly ran over, pretended to check a few things, and then announced with a heavy expression :

« Contestant Ino Yamanaka is unable to continue ; Contestant Hyuga Hinata wins ! »

The audience’s murmurs finally rang out a half-beat late :

« What’s going on ? It’s like I watched this match, but also like I didn’t ? I don’t feel anything at all ; it’s like I didn’t watch it ! »

« Yeah, I also feel like something is weird, I just can’t put my finger on it... »

« But isn’t the victory of Miss Hinata within expectations ? It’s just that it wasn’t as intense as imagined ; it was a bit too calm. »

« Are you idiots ? This is clearly throwing the match ! The acting is too fake ! Geneva, refund ! »

Shin cast his gaze over with dissatisfaction. The audience for this match included not only the parents of classmates but also some Konoha villagers who had heard the news and had some free time.

And the one shouting for a refund right now was a spirited villager wearing a red tracksuit...

'I didn’t even charge admission, what refund ? Are you delirious from watching the match ?' Shin waved his hand at the Anbu Ninja acting as security nearby :

« Someone, come and drag him out for me ! »
