# Chapitre 103 — Roshi

Asakawa Shin calmed his mind and focused, beginning to silently sense the Flying Thunder God Mark. In a flash of thought, he crossed more than half of the Land of Earth, instantly teleporting to his Shadow Clone’s side.

The Shadow Clone had been lying leisurely on the grass, napping, when it was caught off guard and literally jumped in fright. After seeing clearly that it was the original body, it appeared very angry :

« Holy crap ! Are you trying to kill me ? Don’t you know that people scaring people can scare them to death ! »

Asakawa Shin’s face was lined with frustration, but he didn’t say much. He just silently pulled out a small notebook and recorded :

« Konoha Year 59, Shadow Clone in the Land of Earth insulted me for no reason... »

Then he flipped back to the first page and, beneath Senju Tobirama’s name, solemnly added a stroke to a tally mark.

Seeing this, the Shadow Clone rolled its eyes in annoyance and simply ignored him.

Asakawa Shin flipped through the small notebook from beginning to end once more to reinforce his memory before solemnly tucking it into his robe.

Then he straightened his expression, squatted beside the Shadow Clone, and asked in a low voice, « What’s the situation ? Is there anything I need to watch out for with this guy ? »

The Shadow Clone shook its head, a hint of disdain flashing in its eyes. « This guy seems to be an ascetic monk, training from morning till night. Based on my observations, he should be an honest Ninja.

However, he possesses the Lava Release Bloodline Limit, giving him a natural advantage in Taijutsu. I suggest you follow Kisame Hoshigaki’s example and suppress him with pure Water Release Ninjutsu.

That’s all. If there’s nothing else, I’m going to travel the Ninja World. Oh, by the way, where’s my money ?

Didn’t we agree ? The first one to find Roshi not only doesn’t have to dispel the Shadow Clone but also gets a reward ? »

« Here is ten million taels, go spend it. Oh, and don’t forget to plant Flying Thunder God Trees along the way. »

Asakawa Shin reluctantly handed over a sealing scroll. This was precisely the key incentive that had made the Shadow Clone army work so hard this time.

However, Asakawa Shin wasn’t losing out either. Once the Shadow Clone dispelled, all those memories would return to him, so it was essentially like he went out for a trip himself.

Furthermore, being able to make the clone willingly plant trees across the Ninja World was simply a win-win—he won twice.

The Shadow Clone snatched the scroll and walked away without even looking back.

A mere ten million taels wasn’t exactly a huge fortune, but weren’t there plenty of casinos in the Ninja World ? As long as it was managed properly, this money would be excellent seed capital.

Asakawa Shin watched its departing back and called out worriedly, « Don’t forget to use the Transformation Technique ! If you cause trouble out there, don’t you dare give me up ! »

The Shadow Clone waved its hand impatiently, and its figure soon disappeared at the edge of his vision.

Asakawa Shin sighed, feeling a bit frustrated and stifled in his heart.

But soon his eyes lit up, and he sped toward Roshi’s sensed location.

Roshi was currently unaware that danger was approaching, still sitting cross-legged in an open space in the distance, clearly performing some special training.

A sharp light flashed in Asakawa Shin’s eyes as he quietly circled halfway around, hiding not far behind Roshi.

He gauged the distance between himself and Roshi and nodded with satisfaction.

Immediately, his hands slammed together, and he entered Sage Mode in an instant.

Then he crouched down, assuming a sprinting stance.

With a loud « Boom, » the thick rocky ground instantly cracked and shattered under his feet.

His figure turned into an afterimage, shooting toward Roshi like a bolt of electricity.

Roshi had sensed the movement by now, but Asakawa Shin’s attack was incredibly fast, and it was completely without honor or warning.

Before he could fully turn around, Asakawa Shin had already closed in like lightning, delivering a heavy, powerful flying kick squarely to Roshi’s lower back.

The massive impact forced Roshi’s entire body to bend backward, sending him flying in an extremely awkward posture.

His body bounced and rolled wildly across the ground, finally plowing a deep trench in a pile of rubble a hundred meters away, kicking up dust that obscured half the sky.

Asakawa Shin used his hand as a fan to disperse the dust in front of him, sneering :

« Hmph, to be so arrogant as to turn your back on me, your enemy—you clearly looked down on me. Now you know how powerful I am~ »

« ... »

Roshi was currently dazed and very angry ; some lunatic had appeared out of nowhere and launched a sneak attack from behind.

What was even more outrageous was that the opponent had the gall to blame the victim and strike first—it was simply preposterous, absolutely intolerable !

He endured the excruciating pain and turned his head with difficulty to look at Asakawa Shin.

He wanted to open his mouth to shout in anger, but as a result, a large mouthful of fresh blood gushed out the moment he opened his mouth.

The bloody froth was even mixed with small fragments of internal organs ; he was clearly severely injured.

If an ordinary Ninja suffered such a heavy blow, they would likely lose their life if not treated promptly.

But he was different ; as a Tailed Beast Jinchuriki, his vitality was far beyond that of an average person.

Feeling the severe injuries within his body, Roshi did not hesitate and immediately underwent Tailed Beast Transformation.

In an instant, a giant red monkey appeared before Shin—it was the Four-Tails, Son Goku.

Seeing this scene, Asakawa Shin nodded with satisfaction, thinking to himself :

'My experience in farming Tailed Beasts is really getting richer and richer. Now I can speed-run Phase One and jump straight to Phase Two.'

After Roshi completed the Tailed Beast Transformation, his aura stabilized significantly. Although he still clutched his lower back in pain, he was finally able to speak.

He endured the sharp pain and pointed at the opponent, shouting, « Who exactly are you ? How dare you launch such a cowardly sneak attack on me ! »

Facing the questioning, Asakawa Shin acted even more indignant than Roshi, immediately cursing :

« You ungrateful wretch, you have the face to ask me ?

As the Four-Tails Jinchuriki, you were an indispensable combat force for the village, yet you left the village without permission for your own selfish reasons !

Do you still have Iwagakure in your eyes ? Do you still respect Lord Ōnoki ?

I have come today to teach you a lesson on behalf of Lord Ōnoki, to let you know the consequences of provoking Lord Ōnoki ! »

« What ? You were sent by that old geezer Ōnoki ? Who are you ? How have I never seen you before ?

Besides, Ōnoki and I just have different political views... you... you must be lying to me ! »

The Four-Tails' monkey face was filled with shock and disbelief ; he clearly hadn’t expected Ōnoki to take such drastic measures.

« Lying to you ? What benefit would I get from lying to you ? »

Asakawa Shin snorted, his tone dismissive : « Don’t worry, I’m only ordered to give you a lesson today ; I won’t take your life.

You’d better stay still and let me give you a beating, then this matter will be settled... »

Before his voice had even faded, the Four-Tails' roar exploded like thunder :

« Bullshit ! You go back and tell that old geezer Ōnoki to wait for me, this matter is absolutely not over !

As for a lesson, let me teach you one first ! »

« Lava Release : Scorching Stream Large Eruption ! »

The Four-Tails' fists slammed into the ground. Amidst the roaring sound, the earth instantly melted, turning into a surging sea of lava.

That wasn’t all ; the lava erupted violently like a volcanic explosion, turning into a towering wave of fire that surged toward Asakawa Shin.

« Hmph ! Stubborn resistance ! »

Asakawa Shin snorted coldly and formed hand seals, the Senjutsu Chakra in his body immediately responding.

In an instant, an endless flow of water gushed from his mouth like a river breaking a dam, facing the sky-covering sea of fire head-on.

'Sage Art : Water Release : Great Exploding Water Colliding Wave !'

Asakawa Shin’s body rose rapidly as if on an elevator, lifted by the layers of surging water, easily avoiding the range of the lava attack.

Under Roshi’s shocked gaze, the seemingly endless water crashed down, instantly extinguishing the raging lava.

It then turned into a towering giant wave that washed over him mercilessly, sweeping the Four-Tails' massive body into it and washing him away.

Looking at the exaggerated effects of the technique, Asakawa Shin nodded with satisfaction, thinking to himself :

'Tobirama’s technique is quite useful ; against Lava Release, it’s practically a specialized counter.'

Under this scale of Water Release, the Four-Tails was like a rootless duckweed, being washed about by the torrent.

Asakawa Shin formed hand seals, spending a large amount of Chakra to once again manipulate the water, turning the interior of the Great Exploding Water Colliding Wave into a violent washing machine, tumbling incessantly.

Before long, the already severely injured Roshi ran out of stamina and fainted. The Four-Tails' massive body also slowly reverted to its original form.

This scene made Asakawa Shin even more convinced of the importance of seizing the initiative in battle ; it seemed he should use this move more often in the future.

With Roshi unconscious, the follow-up matters were much simpler. Asakawa Shin skillfully gave him the full service :

Absorbing Chakra, drawing blood, and carving the Flying Thunder God Mark. The entire set of operations was smooth and seamless.

And to ensure Roshi was thoroughly convinced, Asakawa Shin also specifically added a beating, striking until the other party was black and blue with a crooked mouth and eyes before finally stopping.

Patting off the non-existent dust on his body, Asakawa Shin nodded with satisfaction and chuckled softly :

« Alright ! The Four-Tails, I’ve got it ! »
