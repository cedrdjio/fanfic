# Chapitre 225 — Phase Two

Facing the terrifying slash that crushed down with the force of a collapsing sky, Deva Path Pain let out a hysterical roar.

The remaining Chakra in his body was squeezed out without reservation, instantly transforming into a thick barrier of repulsive force, attempting to protect himself.

However, all these struggles were in vain.

That pitch-black crescent slash was like a hot knife cutting through butter ; it tore through the repulsive barrier without the slightest resistance, then, carrying the momentum to destroy everything, slammed fiercely into the body of Deva Path Pain.

Like Mount Tai crushing down, endless immense force poured down upon him.

Deva Path Pain didn’t even have time to scream before he was pinned down by the black crescent, plummeting toward the distant ground like a meteorite.

« Boom— ! »

Accompanied by a deafening roar, the crescent slash smashed deep into the ancient forest.

In an instant, soil and rocks flew, giant trees snapped, and the earth was deeply plowed into a massive trench.

Dust and smoke blotted out the sky, as if the apocalypse had arrived.

When the dust settled slightly, the other five Pain had already arrived at the scene, leaping one after another into the bottomless crater.

In no time, they found the remains of Deva Path Pain in the center of the ruins.

To be precise, only half a broken head remained ; the rest had been obliterated in the previous slash.

The Hell Path Pain hesitated briefly, then formed hand seals, summoning the King of Hell.

Immediately after, the broken head of Deva Path Pain was shoved without hesitation into the open maw of the King of Hell.

The King of Hell began to chew slowly ; the time required for this resurrection seemed exceptionally long.

Several full minutes passed before the giant mouth slowly opened again, and a completely unscathed Deva Path Pain stepped out.

Nagato had just breathed a sigh of relief when a voice full of mockery drifted down from high above.

« What exactly is the principle behind this King of Hell ? It can even resurrect him like this ? »

The Pain were shocked and looked up sharply.

They saw Asakawa Shin squatting on a towering stone wall, looking down at the farce below with leisure.

Nagato’s expression was so gloomy it was almost dripping water ; he could no longer maintain his previous high-and-mighty god-like demeanor.

He formed hand seals at lightning speed, frantically controlling the Six Paths of Pain to engage the enemy.

Except for Deva Path Pain, the other five instantly displayed their various abilities, unleashing a barrage of diverse Ninjutsu that covered the sky, aiming to intercept Asakawa Shin.

Unfortunately, in the face of absolute power, all techniques are nothing but illusions.

In just a fleeting moment, the five Pain were like trash by the roadside, lying on the ground tattered and scattered, with no signs of life.

However, at this very moment, a strange sneer curled the corner of Deva Path Pain’s mouth.

A pitch-black sphere of light slowly drifted from his palm, swaying as it floated into the high sky.

As Deva Path Pain pressed his palms together, a terrifying gravitational force instantly erupted from that black sphere of light.

The ground beneath their feet cracked, the mountain walls on both sides collapsed, and everything was captured by this gravity, converging frantically toward that black singularity.

Asakawa Shin stumbled, and to his surprise, he was forcibly captured by this immense gravity and sealed inside.

A massive stone sphere several hundred meters in diameter floated in mid-air, completely burying Asakawa Shin.

The Ninja watching from afar were all dumbfounded, yet none dared to take a single step forward.

At this moment, inside a large tree camouflaged with paper.

Nagato bled from his nose and mouth, his body suddenly went limp, and he fell backward.

Konan was alarmed at the sight and rushed forward to support his body.

Since Nagato had transplanted Hashirama cells, he had never shown such a weak state, but now he was panting like an ox, looking like a terminally ill old man.

He spoke weakly : « Konan... the intelligence was wrong. While the enemy hasn’t discovered us yet, evacuate immediately ! »

Before he could finish, his pupils suddenly constricted, and he looked into the distance with an expression of disbelief.

In the direction he was looking, Asakawa Shin was completely unharmed, leisurely holding a black rod originally from the Six Paths of Pain, and then stabbed it fiercely into his own body.

A strange and clear sense of connection instantly flooded into both their minds.

Asakawa Shin sneered, his gaze piercing through layers of obstacles, and through that connection, he looked precisely at the location where Nagato was hiding.

The two gazes seemed to meet in the void, but this time, the « God » who had originally been high above had completely become the prey.

« Not good, he found us ! Konan, I’ll cover the retreat, you go first... »

Feeling that terrifying Chakra approaching at high speed, a trace of anxiety flashed in Nagato’s eyes as he spoke hurriedly.

Konan, however, smiled freely, shook her head slowly, her expression calm.

« There is no need, Nagato. If I retreat now, what difference is there between that and being dead ? »

Hearing this, Nagato sighed softly, and a smile of relief even appeared on his face, « Very well, success or failure, let us face it together today. »

« Clap, clap, clap. »

A sudden burst of applause rang out, and Asakawa Shin’s figure slowly stepped out of the shadows, completely lacking the tact to break this warm and tragic atmosphere.

« Sorry to interrupt, Mr. God, can you save the lovey-dovey stuff for later ? It seems our battle hasn’t ended yet. »

A cold sneer curled the corner of his mouth, his eyes mocking : « If you still have a later. »

Nagato was furious at these words, suddenly pulling a dark green vial from his chest and stabbing it fiercely into his heart.

Accompanied by an agonizing roar, Nagato’s originally withered and emaciated body became full and strong at a speed visible to the naked eye, and the aura around him climbed steadily, becoming terrifyingly immense.

« This is... high-concentration Hashirama cells ? You actually have this kind of thing ? »

Asakawa Shin’s thoughts raced, and he clicked his tongue in secret.

Could this guy have made some shady deal with Obito back then ?

Although Nagato felt the gaze from the other side was somewhat rude, he couldn’t be bothered with such trivial matters at this moment.

He only felt that every cell in his body was being torn apart and reorganized ; although his vitality was terrifyingly strong, it made him feel as if he were going to die completely in the next moment.

Countless tiny bumps squirmed frantically under his skin, as if they were about to burst out of his body and sprout branches at any moment.

'Is it still too much ? The concentration of Hashirama cells is too high ; the body is going to go out of control.'

Intense unwillingness flashed in Nagato’s eyes ; he was only one step away from success, Yahiko and Konan’s dream was only one step away !

Everyone in the Akatsuki Organization’s dream was only one step away !

« How can I... die here ! ! ! » He roared, his voice shrill.

It seemed that this intense unwillingness was perceived by the Rinnegan.

A faint light flashed deep within the Rinnegan, and a pure Yin Release energy dispersed, instantly flowing through his entire body, forcibly suppressing this violent vitality.

As Yin and Yang combined, that terrifying aura gradually calmed down.

He panted heavily, and then a flash of crazy confidence appeared on his face again.

He felt that he could do it again.

Nagato slowly stood up, a gloomy gaze fixed firmly on Asakawa Shin.

Even with the Rinnegan, forcibly merging with high-concentration Hashirama cells was still extremely dangerous ; it was no less than dancing with the Shinigami.

Fortunately, he won the bet.

He slowly raised his palm, and in the center of it, a tender green sprout broke through the skin, grew in the wind, and instantly transformed into a verdant young plant.

« This Chakra feels familiar, right ? That’s right, I have awakened Wood Release. »

« This must be destiny, right ? Konoha was founded by a Wood Release user, and now it is only fitting that it be ended by a Wood Release user. »

Fanatical light flashed in Nagato’s eyes, and his voice became increasingly high-pitched : « I admit you are strong, but no matter how strong a mortal is, they can never defeat a God ! »

Asakawa Shin picked his ear in boredom and interrupted impatiently : « Are you done yet ? Is the phase-two transition dialogue always this long ? »
