# Chapitre 143 — The Battle for the Scrolls

Watching the backs of Asakawa Shin and his group as they left riding the Wood Dragon, Naruto couldn’t help but let a bit of envious drool escape from the corner of his mouth.

« Seriously, Shin’s move is way too cool ! Damn it, why don’t I know Ninjutsu like that ! »

The other teams hadn’t gone far yet, and the massive commotion of the Wood Dragon bursting from the ground had long since alerted them.

The crowd leaped onto the tips of the towering ancient trees, gazing into the distance.

« Oh ? Has that monster already left ? Looking at the direction, he seems to be heading straight for the Central Tower... Heh, well then, the stage is set for our performance to begin... »

Within Team 7, Naruto was still shouting boisterously when Sasuke suddenly cut him off with a short cough.

« Enough, Naruto. Now isn’t the time for fooling around. It looks like we’re being treated as easy targets. Is it because Sakura and I are injured ?

These guys have a decent eye for timing. Since you’re already here, stop hiding like cowards and come out. »

Hearing Sasuke’s words, Naruto and Sakura instantly tensed up and entered a state of high alert.

In the next instant, three figures indeed stepped slowly out from the shadows, locking onto the three members of Team 7 with playful yet cold gazes, like hunters who had already cornered their prey ; it was the team from Otogakure.

Naruto strode forward with a flamboyant expression, waving his hand grandly at Sasuke.

« Sasuke, you just focus on protecting Sakura ! Both of you are injured, let me handle these guys ! »

Sasuke nodded slightly and silently drew a Kunai to stand guard on the sidelines.

He knew Naruto’s strength well, so he only offered a low, calm warning : « Naruto, don’t be careless. »

Dosu, seeing only Naruto stepping up to fight alone, couldn’t help but curl his lips into a mocking sneer.

« Heh, we really are being looked down upon. However, there’s only one of you and three of us. How do you plan to handle this ? »

Before he finished speaking, the three Sound Ninja instantly scattered, surrounding Naruto in a pincer formation.

Seeing this, Naruto didn’t panic at all ; instead, he put his hands on his hips and laughed toward the sky. « Only three of you ? It seems the advantage is mine ! »

In the next instant, his hands formed seals at lightning speed as he shouted, « Ninja Art : Multiple Shadow Clone Jutsu ! »

Accompanied by smoke rising everywhere, dozens of Shadow Clones appeared out of thin air, aggressively counter-surrounding Dosu’s trio.

The three were shocked by this display and were about to react when Naruto’s Shadow Clones shouted in unison :

« Shave ! »

In the next moment, the Shadow Clones turned into afterimages, lunging straight at the trio with a fierce offensive.

Though the Sound Ninja Trio fought back desperately, they were no match for such numbers. They were quickly beaten black and blue, collapsing to the ground, unable to move.

Several Shadow Clones fumbled through the trio’s belongings for a moment before pulling out a scroll and checking it, then shouting excitedly :

« It’s actually an Earth Scroll ! Great, now we’ve gathered our scrolls too. We’re really lucky.

But why didn’t these bastards come sooner ? Then we could have caught the express ride on the Wood Dragon. »

Watching from the side, Uchiha Sasuke’s eyelid twitched slightly.

Naruto, by perfectly combining the Multiple Shadow Clone Jutsu with Taijutsu, was incredibly difficult to deal with.

Not to mention the Uzumaki Clan’s staggering recovery and stamina ; even with his Three-Tomoe Sharingan active, facing such an overwhelming assault head-on would be no easy feat.

Deep in the dense forest, a pair of Byakugan was watching the battle with great interest.

Witnessing Naruto crush the powerful enemies so decisively, Hyuga Hinata couldn’t help but show a hint of relief.

She nodded gently, then turned to her teammates and whispered, « It seems Naruto’s side is fine now. We should move too. Let the hunt begin. »

As soon as she spoke, she took the lead, rushing toward the flank.

Kiba and Shino shared a look of understanding and immediately followed close behind.

However, not far away, Gaara’s team from Sunagakure had fallen into an unprecedented predicament.

Because Gaara had been unconscious since his previous defeat, Kankuro had to carry him while struggling forward.

Such an obvious weakness caused this team to be treated as prey, and they were instantly surrounded by three other teams, leaving them in a precarious situation.

Temari surveyed the surroundings with a grim expression ; Ninja from Kusagakure, Takigakure, and Amegakure were all present.

The situation was bad ; there were nine opponents in total, and only she and Kankuro were left with any fighting strength.

She gritted her teeth, suppressing her anxiety, and shouted loudly, « We are willing to hand over our scroll ! We don’t want to be your enemies ! »

However, the opposing Ninja burst into boisterous laughter upon hearing this.

« Don’t be naive ! If we kill you, the scroll will still be ours. Furthermore... »

One of them glanced coldly at the unconscious Gaara and sneered, « This kid is passed out now. If we don’t take this chance to eliminate this monster, he’ll become our most terrifying opponent once he wakes up, right ? »

Hearing this, Temari’s face instantly turned pale.

So that was it. These guys never intended to leave them alive from the start ; their true goal was actually to kill Gaara !

Kankuro carefully laid Gaara flat on the ground, then suddenly pulled the puppet from his back with a look of determination in his eyes.

« It looks like we really have to risk our lives this time. We can’t let them hurt Gaara ! »

The battle erupted instantly.

The three teams surrounding them seemed to have a prior understanding, quickly forming a perimeter and using hit-and-run tactics to constantly drain Temari and Kankuro’s Chakra, never engaging them head-on.

Soon, Temari and Kankuro fell into a disadvantage. Although the two fought back desperately and managed to knock down several enemies, their own Chakra was on the verge of exhaustion.

The two looked at each other in despair, their eyes filled with powerlessness.

A Ninja suddenly lunged forward, throwing a strange Ninja tool shaped like a giant umbrella.

The umbrella spun rapidly in mid-air, and countless poisoned black Senbon shot out like a rainstorm, heading straight for the two of them.

Temari snorted, a flash of disdain in her eyes. « You think a cheap trick like this will work ? Watch me ! »

As she spoke, she struggled to lift her iconic giant iron fan, attempting to use a Wind Release technique to blow the attack away as usual.

However, the depletion of her Chakra made her movements sluggish. Her body stiffened, and the Ninjutsu failed to activate.

« Oh no, I’m too late ! »

Just as the poisoned Senbon were about to pierce their bodies, at the critical moment, a large mass of sand emerged out of thin air like a shield, blocking them and intercepting all the hidden weapons.

Temari turned her head in surprise and, as expected, saw Gaara waking up.

At this moment, Gaara was watching her with a complex and deep gaze, whispering in a raspy voice, « You did well, Temari. Next... leave it to me. »

Hearing this familiar voice, Temari and Kankuro’s tensed nerves instantly relaxed, and both slumped to the ground.

They had never felt that this usually terrifying voice could sound so kind and warm.

The two looked at each other, both offering a bitter, self-deprecating smile : 'Kind ? I must be so tired I’m hearing things.'

The remaining Ninja turned pale upon seeing this, shouting in terror, « The monster is awake ? Retreat, fast ! »

Gaara snorted expressionlessly. « You want to leave now ? Too late ! »

« Sand Waterfall Funeral ! »

In an instant, a massive amount of sand poured out from the gourd on Gaara’s back, turning into a towering wave that swept across the field.

No matter how the enemies struggled or resisted, the sand wave remained unstoppable, completely swallowing those people.

Immediately after, Gaara’s eyes sharpened as he whispered three words : « Sand Binding Coffin. »

The people tightly wrapped in the shifting sand felt a terrifying crushing force from all sides ; their bones shattered inch by inch, and they finally turned into a crimson rain of blood that scattered in all directions amidst desperate screams.

Kankuro wiped away cold sweat and muttered under his breath with lingering fear, « It’s like this again... the scrolls are all crushed to pieces. Now we have to go through the trouble of stealing from others again. »

He hadn’t expected this complaint to be heard clearly by Gaara.

The boy slowly turned his head, his crimson pupils locking straight onto him, scaring Kankuro into pulling his neck back sharply, his very breath freezing.

« Oh, don’t mind him ! » Temari quickly tried to smooth things over, patting her brother’s shoulder with a dry laugh. « If the scrolls are broken, we can just steal more, right Kankuro ? »

« Right, right, right ! » Kankuro nodded like a woodpecker, the cold sweat on his forehead becoming even denser.

The air froze for several seconds before Gaara lowered his gaze and said softly, « ...It was my lack of consideration. It won’t happen again. »

Before he finished speaking, he had already turned and walked away, almost fleeing toward the front.

Only the siblings were left standing there, looking at each other, doubting they had hallucinated.

The Gaara who viewed human life like grass actually... apologized ?

Meanwhile, in the shadows across the Forest of Death, various teams from Konoha, Kumogakure, and others also bared their fangs.

The once silent dense forest instantly transformed into a Shura Field where hunters and prey were intertwined.
