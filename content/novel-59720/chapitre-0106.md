# Chapitre 106 — The Classmate Press Conference

Asakawa Shin had no idea what those two were whispering about.

At this moment, he was surrounded by a group of people, his ears filled with a barrage of questions as if he were holding a press conference, making him feel quite annoyed.

He raised both hands and pressed down in the air to signal for silence. Once the noise died down, he spoke helplessly.

« Alright, one question at a time. Besides, didn’t I leave a Shadow Clone at school ? Why didn’t you guys just ask him ? »

...

Shikamaru scratched his head and spoke with a look of helplessness, « So that was a Shadow Clone you sent out ? I always thought it was your real body. »

« You’re actually using him to attend school again ? Can you teach me ? I don’t want to go to this stupid school for even a single day ; it’s such a drag. »

As soon as he said this, a wave of envious murmurs broke out around them. Clearly, everyone was coveting this Ninjutsu.

Asakawa Shin waved his hand and flatly refused, « Forget it. I absolutely cannot teach you this one. »

« While this move isn’t as dangerous as the Multiple Shadow Clone Jutsu, the consequences of improper use are just as unimaginable. »

« Alright, there’s nothing more to discuss on this. Next question. »

...

Sasuke approached with a suspicious look, « Shin, let me ask you, where exactly did you and my brother go last time ? »

« I feel like ever since he came back, he’s been acting all secretive, spending all day plotting something with Brother Shisui. They stay together for the whole day. »

« When I ask him, he won’t say a word... »

Asakawa Shin glanced at him and shook his head with an innocent look.

« Fake news ! »

« I didn’t do anything ; I just took him to see a doctor. »

« Besides, his Sharingan’s Yin Release ocular power has already been sealed by me ; it won’t leak out. »

« As for what exactly they’re plotting, I really don’t know. Ask him yourself. Next question. »

...

Naruto’s eyes darted around, and he leaned in shamelessly, « Shin, how about I bring Little Momo with me to school from now on too ? »

« It’s called winning at the starting line. Education should start from when they’re babies ! »

Asakawa Shin squinted at him and snapped back irritably, « Give it a rest. Don’t force me to slap you while I’m at my happiest. »

« Tsk, Shin, you’re so bossy. You can bring her, so why can’t I... »

« What are you muttering ? Try saying that again. I’ll give you one chance to rephrase that. »

« ... »

Beside them, Hinata wore a thoughtful expression, thinking to herself :

'So Naruto likes children ? Should I... hide Hanabi in a bento box and bring her to school one of these days ?'

'It seems... like it might work...'

...

Sakura saw that everyone had gathered around, so she also seized the opportunity to step forward and ask, « Shin, did you see Lady Tsunade when you left the village last time ? Did you manage to get her autograph for me ? »

Speaking of this, Asakawa Shin wiped cold sweat from his forehead and stammered, « About the autograph, I actually did ask for one for you. »

« She... she said that proxy signing for scalpers is fundamentally not allowed. However, seeing my sincerity, she’d sign one for me for a mere 100 million Ryo, just as a small token. »

« But I refused. How about... you wait until she returns and ask her yourself ? I really don’t have the means ; I can’t afford that signature... »

Sakura was shocked to hear this. As expected of Lady Tsunade, she was so principled ! Exactly, scalpers should be dealt with harshly !

And Lady Tsunade was truly bold ; asking for 100 million Ryo for a single signature... So impressive...

But in the end, she still didn’t get Lady Tsunade’s autograph, and she couldn’t help but feel a bit disappointed.

But she quickly perked up again, catching the key point in Asakawa Shin’s words, and asked curiously :

« Hmm ? Shin, from what you’re saying, does that mean Lady Tsunade is about to return to the village ? »

Asakawa Shin thought back to the memories sent back by his Shadow Clone and nodded certainly, « That’s right ! »

« Two 'Gods of Gamblers' have appeared in the Ninja World over the past two years, respectfully called the 'Male and Female Duo of Terror' by everyone. Every casino they visit goes bankrupt and closes down ; it’s quite pathetic. »

« Now, there are hardly any casinos left in the Ninja World. However, the ones in Konoha haven’t been affected yet. I reckon Senior Tsunade won’t be able to wander around outside for much longer... »

Sakura was a bit hesitant, « But what does a casino closing have to do with whether Lady Tsunade returns or not ? I don’t understand... »

Asakawa Shin interrupted her directly, « Alright, Sakura, don’t ask any more. Otherwise... »

'Otherwise, I’m afraid you might stop being a fan because of it~'

Asakawa Shin didn’t say this out loud ; he just gave her a « figure it out yourself » look.

« Alright, next. »

...

Kiba quickly squeezed forward and happily took credit with Asakawa Shin, « Shin, about what you said last time regarding picking a pet dog for Little Momo, I’ve taken care of it. »

« I specifically picked the best of the best, choosing several puppies with excellent coats, personalities, and bloodlines from the pack. When you have time, take Little Momo to pick one and see which one she likes. »

Asakawa Shin nodded with satisfaction and said gratefully to Kiba, « Thanks, Kiba. By the way, what kind of dogs did you pick ? »

Kiba was stunned for a moment and quickly explained, « No, Shin, they aren’t stupid dogs. The puppies I chose are all very smart, just as smart as me... »

« Uh... thanks for that, Kiba. Alright, next. »

...

« Shin, Shin, I’m over here... »

Asakawa Shin looked around but couldn’t find anyone speaking...

It wasn’t until he used his Observation Haki that he realized there was actually a figure behind him. This gave him a fright, and he quickly turned around to look.

« Holy crap ! It’s just Shino. Your stealth is impressive ; if I hadn’t looked for you specifically, I would’ve ignored you... Anyway, what’s up ? »

« ... »

Shino was silent for a moment before continuing, « It’s like this : our Aburame family wants to give away a portion of our shares in the Musi Group, so I’ve come specifically to ask for your opinion. »

« After all, we never imagined back then that these shares would be worth so much, so... »

Hearing this, Asakawa Shin frowned and asked with some dissatisfaction, « What ? Did someone say something to you guys ? Why give them away all of a sudden ? »

« If you’re really doing it for charity, then take a portion of the dividends and donate them in the Musi Group’s name to Konoha’s orphanages, schools, and hospitals—public facilities like that. »

« If someone said something they shouldn’t have to you, tell them to come to me directly. I’ll talk to them. »

« No, we want to give half of the shares to the Senju Clan to thank them for the care they’ve always shown us... »

« Oh, oh, oh, so that’s how it is. I completely understand then. It’s rare for the Aburame clan to be so thoughtful. How about this, let’s discuss this in detail after school... »

« Next. »

...

Choji looked around, and seeing that everyone had taken their turn to speak, he finally stepped forward with satisfaction.

« Shin, the 'Food Street' project you mentioned last time is almost finished ! »

« All kinds of signature dishes have been developed according to your descriptions, and the shop renovations are complete. When the Ribbon-cutting ceremony comes, as a major shareholder, you must personally preside over it ! »

Asakawa Shin’s eyes lit up at the news, and he said excitedly, « Great ! I can’t wait. »

« Braised Chicken with Rice, Hot pot, Crawfish, Fried chicken, Roast duck, Barbecue, Braised food... »

« Just saying these names makes my mouth water. »

« Hmm ? Wait, what’s that sound ? »

« Holy crap ! Choji, you have a waterfall hanging from the corner of your mouth ! »
