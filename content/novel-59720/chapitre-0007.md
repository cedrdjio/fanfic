# Chapitre 7 — Wood Release

« No mistake, this is Wood Release. »

Sensing the weak but vibrant sprout in his hand, he knew this was the power originating from his own body.

« In other words, I actually possess the Senju bloodline ? But why ? »

According to his original assumptions, his parents were both ordinary Chunin, and their Chakra reserves were quite average.

He hadn’t expected such a big surprise ; it seemed he would have to ask Hanako about it later.

Moreover, if he remembered correctly, Wood Release was formed by merging Water and Earth natured Chakra.

He had originally inherited the Wind and Fire attributes from his father and the Water attribute from his mother, and now, with the awakening of Wood Release, he had gained the Earth attribute as well.

This meant that among the five basic Chakra natures, only Lightning was missing.

Next, Asakawa Shin repeated his old trick, moving a small amount of life force to test its effects.

The cells in the merged area seemed to be cheering with joy, which also stimulated the power of Wood Release.

Once he confirmed that this life force was harmless to his body, Asakawa Shin no longer hesitated and activated Life Return, merging the life force into his blood to circulate throughout his entire body.

This life force seemed inexhaustible, continuously strengthening Asakawa Shin’s body. The pleasure from the evolution of his life’s origin made Shin feel as if he were floating on clouds.

« Hiss, I’m really high right now ! »

Time passed in cultivation, and it wasn’t until the alarm clock rang that Asakawa Shin snapped back to his senses ; it was already the next morning.

No wonder they say there’s no sense of time in cultivation ; to him, it felt like he had only closed his eyes for a short while.

He touched his nose, and sure enough, those annoying nose hairs hadn’t reappeared, which made him breathe a sigh of relief.

After a night of cultivation, every cell in his body had been strengthened through blood circulation, yet his body still craved that life force.

« Does this mean I can still evolve ? And this life force doesn’t seem simple either. »

« Whatever ! »

After a quick wash, Asakawa Shin finished breakfast, greeted Hanako, and prepared to head to school.

When he reached the door, Naruto happened to be walking over ; the two of them had been going to school together lately.

« Shin, did you get taller ? » Naruto asked, somewhat puzzled.

« Maybe. My Taijutsu secrets have improved a bit. Is it that obvious ? » Shin replied noncommittally.

« Of course ! Yesterday we were about the same height, but after waking up from one night’s sleep, you’re a whole head taller than me ! »

Naruto exaggeratedly raised his hand above his head, indicating how much taller he had become.

« It’s not that exaggerated, only two or three centimeters. Want to learn ? I’ll teach you. »

Shin activated Life Return to sense his body. Because he had strengthened his body and awakened Wood Release, he had indeed grown a bit taller, and his body was stronger and more full of vitality.

Shin tried walking while using Life Return. It was a bit awkward, but he believed he would adapt quickly ; after all, many people in one piece used Life Return as a combat technique.

Once he got used to it, he could use that life force to strengthen his body at all times. « Hmm, let’s call it Life Return : Constant. »

« Is it those weird Taijutsu moves you’ve been practicing lately ? They can actually make you taller ?

Damn, why didn’t you say so earlier ? »

Naruto said excitedly :

« Teach me after school ! I must grow taller than that bastard Sasuke.

He actually mocked me for being a shorty last time. Unforgivable ! »

« Weird Taijutsu ? That’s called the Konoha Six Styles, Naruto you idiot.

This Taijutsu has very high physical requirements. Get ready to go through hell. »

Naruto’s foundation was good, but at his current age, it was unlikely he could successfully master the Rokushiki.

Shin planned to hone Naruto’s body while first teaching him the simplest and most practical technique : Soru.

As for the others, he would leave it to fate and see how the training went. Putting physical constitution aside, Naruto didn’t possess his world-shaking wisdom.

...

Before noon, the whole class knew that Asakawa Shin had a technique that could make people grow taller. This immediately stirred up a hornet’s nest.

These days, which kid doesn’t have height anxiety ? As soon as class ended, everyone swarmed around him.

« Shin-kun, I heard from them that you have a technique that can make people grow taller. Is it true ? »

This was actually true. Although Shin didn’t use Life Return specifically for height growth, he could indeed use this technique to manipulate bones and muscles to stimulate growth and achieve that goal.

Besides, if you can master this technique, how could your physical constitution be bad ? In the world of one piece, those who mastered the Rokushiki were called superhumans.

Asakawa Shin said confidently, « That’s the case. »

Sasuke said, a bit embarrassed :

« Um, I heard from Naruto that you’re going to teach him this Ninjutsu. Could you teach me too ?

I... I can trade with my family’s Ninjutsu. »

Normally, with his personality, he wouldn’t beg like this, but for some reason, when he heard Naruto say this morning that he would be a head taller than him in the future, he felt very irritated.

An image even appeared in his mind of a much taller Naruto pressing down on his head with one hand and asking if he yielded.

Hiss, such a thing was absolutely unacceptable.

Naruto immediately became unhappy : « This is a Taijutsu that Shin spent a month researching and developing. How could it be taught to others so casually ?

Especially Sasuke, absolutely not ! »

Hearing Naruto’s words, the others surrounding them also began to hesitate. Indeed, in the Ninja World, various Ninjutsu and secret techniques were very private ; many excellent techniques could even serve as the foundation of a clan.

Many present were heirs to clan secret techniques. They had just followed the crowd in a moment of excitement, but now they realized their request was indeed too presumptuous. Sasuke’s face was now as red as a monkey’s butt.

'Should I teach them ? Well... whatever. After all, they are all my classmates, and given the difficulty of this technique, even with Chakra’s help, I doubt many will be able to master it in the end.'

'However, if they practice my Taijutsu, they’ll have to join my crew in the future. Otherwise, they’ll have to see if my Divine Departure agrees.'

Thinking of this, Shin stood up, looked around, waved his hand, and said :

« It’s fine, I can teach this Taijutsu to you. However, it has very high physical requirements and is very difficult to practice. I’ll start by teaching you the simplest one : Soru. »

« Oh, right. Soru is one of the Six Styles. Besides that, there are Moon Walk, Rankyaku, Shigan, Tekkai, and Kamie. These six Taijutsu techniques are collectively known as the Konoha Six Styles. »

« And by mastering all six styles and integrating them, one can master the technique for increasing height—Life Return. »

The Rokushiki was indeed precious, but to Asakawa Shin, it was nothing special—just an extension of his first template, the Roger system.

Was Roger’s strength due to the Rokushiki ? Or is any Admiral or Emperor strong because of it ? Teacher Kaido was right : only Haki can transcend everything. After all, Teacher Kaido had truly witnessed it at God Valley.

By teaching them the Rokushiki today, I will gain more in the future. As they say, the most expensive things are those that are free.

Besides, the people here and even the entire Konoha will become my subordinates in the future.

Giving future Palus an advance on their wages while boosting my own reputation—why not ?

Hiruzen Sarutobi should already know about the existence of the Konoha Six Styles by now. Shin also planned to hand over the Rokushiki to gain some favor with the old man.

After all, he was the man who would become the Hokage in the future ; he had to establish his persona first.

He would use the Rokushiki to trade for the class’s affection, the goodwill of all the clans, and the support of the entire Konoha.

As for why he taught Soru first, it was because this move had many similarities with the Body Flicker Technique and was relatively simple to practice, yet it could also filter out true talent. Those who couldn’t even learn Soru weren’t qualified to practice the Rokushiki.

At first, no one cared much. After all, Naruto had said that Asakawa Shin had spent a month developing this Taijutsu, so how hard could it be ?

Only when they saw Asakawa Shin disappear with a « swish, » teleporting instantly to a distance, poking a hole in a rock with one finger, kicking out a slash wave that cut three large trees in half, remaining unharmed even after being hit by the falling trees, and then running through the air above the playground, did everyone erupt in an uproar.

They didn’t know that Asakawa Shin was cheating. Even though he had only been merging with the Roger template for a short month, he already possessed Kage-level combat power, which was completely illogical.

« Holy crap, if you get hit by that, you’ll definitely be black and blue all over ! »

« This is the Konoha Six Styles ? This is insane ! »

« Shin is flying ! He can fly ! »

As Shin drifted down like a piece of paper, everyone was still in disbelief.

« No way, Shin. This is a Taijutsu that can be developed in just one month ? »

« Of course not ! »

Just as everyone breathed a sigh of relief, they heard Asakawa Shin say nonchalantly :

« Developing the Six Styles took me one week. Developing Life Return only took me a month. »

« ... »
