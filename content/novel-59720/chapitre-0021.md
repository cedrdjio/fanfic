# Chapitre 21 — Stinky Woman

The next day, Ninja Academy.

Shikamaru : « Shin, I heard you became Hokage-sama’s disciple ? Is that for real ? Not that... I mean, that’s not bad at all. »

Ino : « Well done, Shin. I knew you could do it ! As expected of my... hehe. »

Choji : « Crunch... crunch... »

Inuzuka Kiba : « Damn, I’m so jealous of you, Shin. Being a disciple of the Hokage sounds so cool ! »

Hinata : « Shin, congratulations, and thank... thank you... »

Sakura Haruno : « Doesn’t that make Lady Tsunade your senior sister ? Shin, can you get an autograph from Lady Tsunade for me ? She’s the female Ninja I admire most. »

Sasuke : « You’re quite capable, aren’t you ? But just you wait, I’ll catch up to you immediately ! The Uchiha are the strongest ! »

Naruto : « Shin, I went to look for you this weekend, but you weren’t home. I asked Aunt Hanako and found out you went to Hinata’s house to play ! Does that mean our relationship isn’t as good as that stinky woman’s ? Don’t let that woman affect our bond ! Oh no, yeah low~ »

Hinata : « Stinky... stinky woman ? ! ? »

Asakawa Shin : « ? »

'Naruto, you idiot, is this going to make my cute little niece Uzumaki Himawari disappear ?'

« Damn ! Hinata fainted, someone come help ! »

« Uzumaki ! Naruto ! »

« Idiot Naruto, I went to Hinata’s house to talk business with Uncle Hiashi ! Apologize to Hinata quickly ! »

« Sor... sorry, Hina... Hinata. I didn’t... mean it... » Naruto, having provoked public outrage and been beaten into a pig’s head, apologized to Hinata with some difficulty.

Hinata didn’t say a word, just silently lowered her head and shed tears : « Wuwuwu... »

« Uzumaki ! Naruto ! »

« Eh ? Not... not me ! »

Holding the seventeen unequal treaties signed and thumb-printed by Naruto, Hinata was finally coaxed into a good mood.

Right now, she was beaming with joy, treating the treaty like a treasure as she folded it and tucked it into her pocket, no longer looking like the crying girl from moments ago.

Hinata happily said to Naruto : « Naruto, you said it, right ? Keeping your word is your Ninja way ! So, from now on, you are my servant. No objections, right ? »

« Eh ? Servant ? What kind of servant ? I clearly only agreed to be humble and polite to you at all times, address you as 'Hinata-sama', serve you tea and water, clean up the area around your seat, stand up for you when others bully you, not talk back, and obey your commands as long as it doesn’t violate morality... And the last point, to carry your bento from the school gate to the classroom at noon, hahahaha... Hinata, you’re so silly, who carries a bento, and I have to carry it out after eating too... hahahaha... »

It seemed to hit Naruto’s funny bone, and he laughed until he was almost out of breath.

Asakawa Shin watched Naruto, sweating profusely. This really couldn’t be considered a servant ; we usually call this kind of person a 'slave', or a 'boyfriend'.

Moreover, Asakawa Shin always felt something was a bit off. Is Hinata this kind of personality ? He kept feeling that Hinata hadn’t been right since she woke up. Could it be that Naruto’s « stinky woman » comment triggered Hinata’s phase two ?

Things are a bit messy, whatever...

Hinata smiled and said : « Naruto, you remember it very clearly. It doesn’t matter what it’s called, whatever makes you happy. But you have to do what you promised me, understand ? »

Naruto scratched his head and said : « I know, Hinata. »

« Hmm ? »

« Oh, no, Hinata-sama. »

« Hmm, I cried for so long just now, I’m a little thirsty. »

« ... »

Lunchtime.

Asakawa Shin patted Naruto on the shoulder and gestured with his head : « Let’s go, Naruto, let’s go to the school gate to get the bento together, shall we ? »

Hinata already had someone to fetch her bento, but Asakawa Shin didn’t have anyone to fetch his, so he had to take Naruto with him.

The Ninja Academy didn’t allow outsiders to enter during class hours, so deliveries could only be left at the gate for students to pick up themselves.

Now, the exclusive kitchen for Asakawa Shin and Hinata promised by Hiashi had officially started working, and Shin planned to have both lunch and dinner there from now on.

« Hmm ? Shin, are you eating takeout too ? Didn’t you bring a bento ? » Naruto asked with some confusion.

« Ugh ! I’ve been training in taijutsu recently, so I’m eating a bit more. Don’t ask, let’s go, I’ll take you with me. »

At the school gate, Naruto asked strangely : « Shin, didn’t you say we were coming to the school gate to get the bento ? Where is the bento ? Why are there only two big boxes ? »

To be precise, one large white box and one extra-large black box.

Asakawa Shin pursed his lips at the two big boxes : « Aren’t these two it ? The white one is Hinata’s, and this black one is mine. Alright, let’s go, I’m a bit hungry. »

After speaking, he ignored Naruto, lifted the black box over his head with one hand, and started walking back, leaving Naruto alone, dazed beside the white box that was taller than him.

« Eh ? This is it ? No... Shin, wait for me ! Damn ! It’s so heavy ! Wahhhhh ! »

Naruto used all his might, dragging and carrying, and finally brought the bento to the classroom door, already exhausted.

After waiting for a long time, Hinata, whose stomach was growling with hunger, impatiently took the bento... box.

« Wait a second, Hinata... sama, this is too heavy, I’ll carry it... »

Before he could finish speaking, he saw Hinata easily hoist the bento box onto her shoulder and walk into the classroom.

Inside the classroom.

Everyone stared dumbfounded as Asakawa Shin and Hinata ate.

Asakawa Shin was eating with both hands, swallowing a large meat leg in one gulp as if he didn’t need to chew, and from time to time he would chug a large bottle of drink. In no time, the mountain of food in front of him had diminished significantly.

Hinata’s way of eating was much more pleasing to the eye. If you ignored her chopsticks moving like afterimages and the stack of plates higher than the desk, she looked as elegant as a young lady of the main family.

Wait ? Isn’t she a young lady of the main family ?

« Hmm ? Naruto, sit down. »

Asakawa Shin looked strangely at the dumbfounded Naruto in front of him and handed him a bento box.

« Why did you take so long to come back ? This is your portion, eat up. »

Naruto seemed to have just come back to his senses, pointing at the mountain of plates in front of Shin and saying to him : « Shin, why are you eating so much ? Are you crazy ? »

Asakawa Shin rolled his eyes at him and said disdainfully : « Ignorant ! Isn’t it normal for us martial artists to eat more ? The more you eat, the better your talent. Both Hinata and I are one-in-a-million peerless geniuses, so it’s reasonable to eat a little more than others, right ? »

Hinata echoed : « That’s right, Naruto, you just eat too little. You couldn’t even carry a bento. »

Upon hearing this, Naruto was almost anxious. He said a lot of things about how the box surface was too smooth, there was no grip, he hadn’t eaten lunch yet, and he wasn’t in good condition today, which made everyone roar with laughter, and the classroom was immediately filled with a cheerful atmosphere.

Hinata shook her head and said dotingly to Naruto : « Alright, Naruto, a weak little Naruto is also very cute. »
