# Chapitre 122 — The Demon

Kakashi had already tied up the Demon Brothers by now. Everyone looked at each other and surrounded Tazuna, forming a temporary tribunal.

Kakashi turned his sharp gaze toward Tazuna.

« Now, don’t you think you owe us an explanation, Mr. Tazuna ?

Why are these Ninjas after your life ?

The request you filed was clearly for a C-rank mission ; by all rights, we should only have to guard against bandits and gangs.

But now, Ninjas are involved... what on earth is going on ? »

« How can you say these Ninjas are looking for me ? It’s also possible... »

« What ? Are you trying to say these Ninjas might not be coming for you, but for one of us instead ?

Quibbling.

The killing intent from those two just now was locked onto you ; that kind of thing can’t be hidden from our senses.

Besides, judging by their stance, they were just two pawns at most.

Mr. Tazuna, you’d better think carefully before you open your mouth.

This concerns whether your head can stay on your neck or not. »

Kakashi was like a master negotiator ; with just a few words, he broke down Tazuna’s mental defenses, forcing him to confess everything.

As it turned out, this was all due to a shipping magnate named Gato.

This man had monopolized all trade in the Land of Waves and controlled almost everything in the country.

Once the bridge Tazuna advocated building was completed, it would open a trade route between this isolated island nation and the Land of Fire, which would undoubtedly deal a heavy blow to Gato’s business empire.

To protect his interests, Gato did not hesitate to hire Ninjas to crush the bridge in its cradle.

« That’s how it happened... »

Tazuna said with a trembling voice, then fell to his knees with a thud, performing a formal dogeza bow.

« I’ve exhausted everything I have, but the reward I can pay is only at the C-rank standard. I am truly sorry for bringing you all so much trouble ! »

However, Kakashi did not soften because of his apology ; if apologies were enough, what would be the point of having Ninjas ?

He unceremoniously exposed Tazuna, tearing away his last shred of dignity.

« Trouble ? Do you realize that lying about a mission’s rank can get people killed ! This is no longer something that can be described as 'trouble'.

If we hadn’t taken this mission by some strange coincidence, you and the Ninjas who accepted it would likely be dead by now, wouldn’t you ?

The current situation is far beyond my students' ability to handle, so I have decided to terminate the mission immediately. »

Hearing this, Tazuna’s face turned ashen, and he could only kowtow in desperation : « I’m so sorry... but we really have no other way ! Please, save our Land of Waves ! »

Seeing the pity on the faces of Naruto and Karin, Asakawa Shin sighed and said smoothly :

« Mr. Tazuna, actually it’s not entirely without room for maneuver. I have an idea... »

« How about a collateralized loan ? »

...

« What ? You’re only protecting us for a while, and you want to set up a toll booth on the bridge once it’s built ?

No, I don’t agree. How is what you’re doing any different from Gato ? »

« Nonsense, there’s a huge difference. Gato uses forced buying and selling, while we’re setting rules and charging reasonable fees~

Humph ! Talk about ungrateful.

Kakashi, since he doesn’t agree, let’s pack up and head back to Konoha. »

« Wait, wait... don’t go ! »

In the end, under 'friendly' negotiations between both parties, Tazuna tearfully signed an agreement. Both sides would operate the bridge toll station together according to the contract.

« Congratulations, Tazuna. »

Asakawa Shin patted his shoulder, « You’re getting a great deal. With Konoha’s backing, the number of people in the Ninja World who would dare touch this bridge can be counted on one hand. »

Hearing Asakawa Shin’s words, Tazuna’s mood improved slightly. On second thought, it seemed right ; this deal wasn’t actually a loss.

If not for this, whether the bridge could even be completed would still be an unknown.

...

Meanwhile, in a dark underground base.

Gato’s face had turned the color of pig liver, spittle flying everywhere as he ranted at the Ninjas before him :

« You actually failed ?

I heard you were all very powerful Ninjas, which is why I spent a fortune to hire you...

And the result ? Is this your level ? »

The leading Ninja sat boldly on a sofa, a giant sword taller than himself slung over his shoulder, looking quite striking.

Gato’s unceremonious words made him frown slightly, feeling somewhat annoyed.

Then, he actually lifted the terrifying giant sword with one hand and pointed it straight at Gato, forcing the man to swallow his next words.

« You’re so noisy. I’ll take action personally next and use this Kubikiribocho to take Tazuna’s head. »

Being pointed at by the blade, Gato’s body went stiff, and even his voice trembled a bit :

« Is... is that really possible ? The other side must have hired some very powerful Ninjas, otherwise the Demon Brothers wouldn’t have... »

Before he could finish, he was rudely interrupted by the Ninja : « Hmph ! Who do you think I am ? I am Zabuza Momochi, known as the 'Demon of the Hidden Mist' !

No matter who the enemy is, I will slaughter them without mercy ! »

« Hmph ! You’d better ! »

...

After Gato left, Zabuza turned to the youth beside him with a serious expression.

« Haku, you will move with me shortly. A Ninja who can defeat the Demon Brothers won’t be easy to deal with. »

« Yes, Lord Zabuza ! » the youth replied respectfully.

The two then deployed their movement techniques and sped along, soon arriving at the mandatory path for Tazuna’s return, quickly vanishing into the surroundings to lie in ambush.

Only a few hours later, a group of people stepped into the area completely off-guard—exactly Zabuza’s targets.

Lurking behind the bushes, he carefully observed their every move.

Unexpectedly, after a series of feints, that blond kid actually captured his position and threw a Kunai toward him.

Unfortunately, the strength and speed were mediocre, and he easily used the Body Replacement Technique to dodge.

'I see !'

Through this observation, Zabuza also obtained the information he wanted : 'No wonder the Demon Brothers failed ; the opponent is actually Kakashi of the Sharingan ?

But the team composition is a bit strange ; it looks like the scale of two squads.

Could those two women without headbands also be Ninjas ? I feel like I’ve seen them before, or am I remembering wrong ?

It must be an illusion ; where would Tazuna get the money to hire so many powerful Ninjas...

Honestly, being able to hire the « Copy Ninja » Kakashi is surprising enough.

But so what if it’s Kakashi ? The advantage in this battle is mine !'

He looked sideways at Haku and lowered his voice to set the plan : « I’ll strike first to create panic ; you wait for an opportunity on the side. We must take them down in one fell swoop. »

Then, Zabuza looked at Asakawa Shin with a playful expression, locking onto his target.

« You’re the one, kid. You’re even wearing a flower on your head, how effeminate. If I don’t kill you, who else would I kill ? »

Haku : « ? »
