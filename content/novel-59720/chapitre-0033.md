# Chapitre 33 — Kakuzu Attacks

Shikaku’s predictions were correct ; for the remainder of their journey, they were constantly ambushed and intercepted by Ninjas. Fortunately, Shikaku and his group were an elite Anbu squad. Not only was their individual combat ability excellent, but their teamwork was also formidable, far surpassing that of these bounty hunters.

Along the way, they had engaged in over a dozen skirmishes. Although there were no casualties yet, the situation was already grim. Not only was their stamina and Chakra severely depleted, but more importantly, dawn was already breaking, yet they had covered less than one-fifth of the distance. They were completely bogged down.

Enemies were converging from all directions across the Ninja World, while they were nearly at a standstill. It looked like they were about to fall into a desperate situation, but fortunately, reinforcements were on the way. Thinking of the small figure among the reinforcements, Shikaku felt his heavy heart ease slightly.

However, he was clearly happy too soon.

« Lord Shikaku, a massive Chakra signature has appeared one thousand meters behind us, and it is rapidly closing in. Nine hundred meters, eight hundred meters... » The panicked voice of the sensory Ninja interrupted Shikaku’s thoughts.

« Stop advancing. Form a defensive formation and prepare to engage the enemy. » Shikaku understood that at their current speed, they would never be able to escape. Scattering would only lead to them being picked off one by one by the gathering enemies. There was only one path left : hold their ground and wait for reinforcements, pinning their hopes on them.

The group only had time to set up a few simple defensive positions before the enemy arrived at the battlefield like a gale.

« Hehehehe, I’ve finally caught up to you. »

Kakuzu was in an excellent mood. Ever since he finished reading the intelligence, he had been rushing non-stop toward the Land of Fire, Konoha Village.

He swore that he had never run this fast in his entire life ; his legs felt as if they were filled with wind.

This speed made him vaguely recall decades ago, when he received a mission from his Village and set out with the determination to die to assassinate the First Hokage, only to be easily defeated by him.

Fortunately, the First Hokage had spared his life, but he didn’t dare to linger for a moment, fleeing frantically almost purely on instinct.

Even though he had used every ounce of his strength back then, he hadn’t run as fast as he did this time. Indeed, human potential is limitless.

After all, he had spent a full one million ryo to purchase that intelligence. If he hadn’t caught up because he was too slow, he would surely have regretted it for the rest of his life.

Fortunately, hard work pays off. Since he had tried so hard, now was the time for the reward. As long as...

« Hey, Nara Shikaku, as long as you return the money that belongs to me, I’ll let you all go. How about that ? »

Kakuzu looked at the « turtle shell » formation the Ninjas opposite him had set up, and even he felt a bit of a headache. He, Kakuzu, was an honest Ninja ; to break through this turtle shell from the front, he would have to use a high-power combined Ninjutsu. He didn’t know who had the storage scroll right now. If he accidentally destroyed the storage scroll, where would he go to cry ?

If Nara Shikaku was sensible, he would make an exception and not take his bounty this time. Thinking of this, Kakuzu felt a pang of heartache.

« My friend, we are total strangers, so how could we have taken your money ? Is there some misunderstanding ? Why don’t we... »

« Are you stalling for time ? » A cold light flashed in Kakuzu’s eyes. With 1.1 billion ryo at stake, his brain had never run this fast. Seeing that things weren’t going his way, he stopped talking, launched an attack immediately, and charged forward at high speed.

« Don’t let him get close ! Use Earth Release to block him ! » Shikaku cursed inwardly and decisively engaged in battle.

« Earth Release : Earth Flow Wall. » Several Earth Release Ninjas performed the technique together, and a row of earth walls instantly rose from the ground, blocking Kakuzu’s path.

Kakuzu snorted coldly, formed hand seals, and without slowing down, charged straight at the earth walls.

« Earth Release : Earth Spear ! »

Kakuzu used the Earth Spear technique to harden his hands into a pitch-black color and smashed them fiercely into the earth walls. With a deafening roar, the walls shattered upon impact. Kakuzu’s body paused only slightly before he immediately accelerated again to close in on the group.

« Don’t be afraid ! Use Wind Release and Fire Release to attack him ! »

Shikaku ordered again, and several more Ninjas proficient in Wind and Fire Release stepped forward to launch a joint attack.

« Fire Release : Great Fireball Technique. » « Fire Release : Fire Dragon Bullet Technique ! » « Wind Release : Great Breakthrough. » « Wind Release : Vacuum Sphere. »

The wind fueled the fire, and the fire intensified the wind ; a massive sea of flames formed by several Ninjutsu surged toward Kakuzu.

Kakuzu’s expression tightened. His Earth Spear technique could harden his skin and make him immune to most physical attacks, but it was very difficult to defend against this kind of high-temperature Fire Release attack.

« Hmph, there’s no other way. I’ll let you see what a real combined Ninjutsu is. »

Kakuzu’s back writhed, and two monsters crawled out from it, perching on his shoulders.

The monsters were composed of long, thin black tentacles and a white human-faced mask. The mask seemed to be his face, appearing eerie and sinister.

As Kakuzu formed hand seals, the two masked faces opened their mouths, and powerful Ninjutsu fluctuations erupted from them.

« Wind Release : Pressure Damage. » « Fire Release : Searing Migraine. » « Combined Ninjutsu : Scorching Wind Turbulence ! »

« You’d better protect my sealing scroll, brats. »

The terrifying firestorm formed by the Scorching Wind Turbulence collided with the incoming sea of flames in an instant. After only a few seconds of stalemate, the Scorching Wind Turbulence shattered the sea of flames. It only shrank slightly, yet still surged toward the group with overwhelming momentum.

« Damn it ! Use Water Release Ninjutsu to intercept ! »

« Water Release : Water Formation Wall ! »

Under everyone’s tense gaze, water and fire collided fiercely, exploding into a sky full of steam. The Water Release was rapidly evaporated, and the Scorching Wind Turbulence was blocked and slowed down by the water vapor. Ultimately, due to elemental counter-effectiveness, it paused in place for a moment before being extinguished by the rising steam.

On the battlefield, everything was a vast expanse of white ; visibility was completely obscured, blocked by the steam.

The sensory Ninja shouted anxiously : « Lord Shikaku, there are Chakra reactions everywhere in the steam ! I can’t sense the enemy’s movements. The enemy has disappeared ! »

« Use Wind Release to blow away the steam ! » Shikaku’s expression darkened ; he felt a sense of foreboding.

« Everyone, stay alert ! Prepare to engage the enemy in squad units ! »

« Oh ? Using Wind Release ? Why don’t I help you with that ? » Kakuzu had crept to within ten meters of them without anyone noticing. His tone was mocking as his hands flew through hand seals.

« Die ! Wind Release : Pressure Damage. »

« Quick, form a defense ! »

« Water Release : Water Formation Wall. » « Earth Release : Earth Flow Wall. »

The gale was pervasive, penetrating through the gaps in their defensive Ninjutsu and blowing everyone off balance. Kakuzu seized the opportunity to charge, slaughtering his way into their formation.

'If it’s a turtle shell, it’s easy to destroy from the inside. I’ll get the scroll first and then kill them all.'

Using both fists and kicks, his figure darted about. There was no one who could withstand a single blow, and he forced the Konoha Ninja to retreat step by step.

« Don’t underestimate us ! Konoha Style Sword Technique : Dance of the Crescent Moon. » An Anbu Ninja drew their sword and charged. During the dash, their body split into three, attacking Kakuzu’s vitals from the left, top, and right simultaneously.

Kakuzu scoffed and easily dodged the three crescent-like slashes. However, he didn’t expect that none of the three figures were the real body. The Anbu Ninja suddenly burst out from his blind spot during the gap in Kakuzu’s dodge and severed Kakuzu’s right wrist with a single sword strike.

« Great ! I got him ! »

« Is that so ? Too bad it was intentional. Die ! Secret Technique : Tentacle Fist. »

« What ? » He only then noticed that there was no blood flowing from Kakuzu’s wound, but it was already too late.

Dense black threads suddenly erupted from Kakuzu’s severed wrist, stabbing violently toward the Anbu Ninja in front of him.

« Watch out ! » Nara Shikaku lunged forward, shoving the Anbu Ninja aside, but he himself was pierced through the shoulder blade by the attack.

« Oh my, what a good superior who cares for his subordinates. But if you lose your combat strength, your situation will be quite dire, won’t it ? » Kakuzu was overjoyed. Although he didn’t value Shikaku’s combat strength, crippling him so easily solved a major problem.

Who knew that Shikaku would smirk, saying to Kakuzu : « Perhaps it is your situation that is dire. Shadow Possession Jutsu, successful. »

« What ? ! »
