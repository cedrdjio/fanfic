# Chapitre 81 — The Legendary Big Fat Sheep

Little Tsunade walked into the dim alley alone and dispelled her transformation. She coldly snorted at the air with an expression of disgust :

« Who is it ? Show yourself ! You’ve been tailing me for so long, what are you after ? Are you some kind of perverted old man ? »

« It’s not an old man, it’s me, Senior Sister Tsunade ! I didn’t mean to stalk you ; the situation just wasn’t very convenient earlier. »

After confirming the area was safe, Asakawa Shin and the other two flashed out from their hiding spot.

Tsunade turned her head at the sound, her gaze sweeping over them before landing on the smallest figure, her pupils slightly constricting.

« Two Ninjas wearing Konoha forehead protectors... and there’s a child among them ? Huh ? That necklace ? And the slug on his shoulder ? You... could you be... »

Tsunade’s eyes grew brighter as she spoke, and her expression became excited.

Asakawa Shin also revealed a brilliant smile, nodding repeatedly, his eyes flashing with joy :

« Great ! Senior Sister Tsunade, you recognized me ? It’s me, it really is me ! »

Before he could finish, Tsunade suddenly tilted her head, frowning slightly, and muttered in an annoyed tone :

« What was your name again ? Hiss... why can’t I remember it all of a sudden ? Damn it, it must be that fake wine I drank too much of last night ; my brain isn’t working right. I really should smash that shady shop. »

Asakawa Shin’s face darkened, and he forced a smile :

« Ha... haha, Senior Sister Tsunade, it’s me, Shin. You should really drink less ; it’s not good for your health. »

Tsunade suddenly clapped her hands and exclaimed loudly :

« Ah ! I remember ! You’re Senju Shin, right ? Look at my memory, haha, Shin, please don’t take it to heart. It was purely because I drank too much fake wine last night and my brain short-circuited. I’m not usually this muddled. »

Just as Asakawa Shin was feeling slightly awkward and at a loss, a piglet darted out from the end of the alley, nose sniffing the ground—it was Tonton, who had tracked them down.

Following closely behind was a beautiful girl with short black hair—it was Shizune.

She spotted Tsunade at a glance, stepped forward, and said pleasantly :

« Lady Tsunade, I’ve finally found you ! Why did you run to such a remote place this time ? You made me search so hard. »

« Haha, Shizune, you arrived at just the right time ! »

Tsunade laughed heartily, boldly pulling Asakawa Shin into her arms to gesture to Shizune.

« Look, my Junior Brother Shin is here. Let’s go, hurry and find a tavern ; we must celebrate properly today ! »

Having said that, she strode away.

Shizune exchanged a look with Itachi and Yūgao, her eyes filled with helplessness, but she had no choice but to quickly follow.

In the tavern.

« Come, Shin, try this hot spring egg. It’s a signature delicacy of the Land of Hot Springs ; the texture is tender and smooth, and the flavor is delicious. There’s also this hot spring braised fish and hot spring barbecue ; they are all local specialties with unique flavors. It’s just a pity you can’t drink this premium sake brewed with hot spring water. What a shame ; it’s good stuff, so I’ll just have to drink it myself~ Gulp, gulp, gulp... »

Tsunade’s eyes twinkled with amusement as she enthusiastically piled food into Asakawa Shin’s bowl until it was almost overflowing.

The display of attentiveness made Shizune, who was watching from the side, twitch the corner of her mouth slightly.

Tsunade couldn’t help but be enthusiastic ; she knew that despite this kid’s young age, he was a super wealthy tycoon.

In just one year, the high-quality silk sent to her amounted to hundreds of bolts. She said it was for her to make clothes, but she turned around and sold them all at a high price, making a tidy profit.

The business of the Musi Group had expanded rapidly across the entire Ninja World in the past year, and it could be said that money was pouring in.

The reason it was able to open up the market so quickly actually had a lot to do with Asakawa Shin.

A year ago, Asakawa Shin single-handedly destroyed Kusagakure, causing the Daimyo of the Land of Grass to panic. He rushed to the Daimyo of the Land of Fire overnight to seek protection.

Who would have thought that upon meeting, he was immediately dazzled by the gorgeous, incomparably beautiful clothing the Daimyo of the Land of Fire was wearing ? He was so mesmerized and stunned that he completely forgot his fear and eagerly asked about the origin of the attire.

The Daimyo of the Land of Fire was secretly pleased to see this, and it also allowed him to see the immense value of silk.

Therefore, after carefully discussing it with Hiruzen Sarutobi, he decided to join forces with Konoha Village to host a grand event—a Silk Expo—and invited the Daimyos of various countries to attend.

To ensure that the guests would gladly attend, they even sent ten bolts of silk as gifts to each Daimyo in advance.

This move not only showcased the beauty and wonder of the silk, but also caused its fame to spread rapidly throughout the Ninja World, setting off a wave of craze.

With the unprecedented success of the event, the revenue of the Musi Group grew explosively. In just one year, the net profit exceeded ten billion Ryo, which was astonishing.

The Musi Group leapt to become the biggest winner, and the tax revenue of the Land of Fire increased dramatically as a result ; they were almost overwhelmed by the amount of money coming in.

Asakawa Shin’s personal share of the dividends actually amounted to billions of Ryo, and the tree-planting money the group had previously owed this farmer was also fully paid off.

However, Asakawa Shin was not the least bit carried away ; what was this little bit of money ?

Compared to the wealthy woman Konan, it was still far off.

Six hundred billion paper bombs—even if one was only worth one Ryo, six hundred billion Ryo was an astronomical figure.

Yet even so, in the end, it still failed to blow up Obito.

I really wonder what Kakuzu would think if he knew about this ? It’s a good thing he died early ; otherwise, he would definitely have had a heart attack from this, and it would be all five hearts acting up at once.

After three rounds of drinking and five courses of food, Tsunade saw that the atmosphere was just right, so she smiled gently at Asakawa Shin :

« Shin, you saw it earlier ; your Senior Sister is currently being chased by creditors, and life is really tough. I wonder if you have any way to help your Senior Sister ? »

Asakawa Shin was quite receptive to Tsunade’s gentleness, so he asked Tsunade :

« I don’t know how much debt you owe, Senior Sister. If it’s not much, I can pay it in advance. »

A flash of cunning light flickered in Tsunade’s eyes as she smiled sweetly :

« It’s not much, not much, only a few billion Ryo. For you, Junior Brother, it’s nothing. »

'My god, isn’t that my entire net worth ? Senior Sister Tsunade, did you check my account balance ?'

Asakawa Shin paused slightly, then coughed twice, suppressing the shock in his heart, and said calmly :

« But then again, it’s better to teach a man to fish than to give him a fish. Even if I help you pay it off this time, it will only quench your thirst for a moment. Why don’t I give you an idea that will solve it once and for all ! »

Tsunade listened, feeling a bit uninterested : « Then, Junior Brother, tell me ; let me hear what it is. »

« Senior Sister, you have always had the reputation of being the 'Big Fat Sheep,' losing every bet. Who in the Ninja World doesn’t know it ? Who isn’t aware of it ? Hey ? Senior Sister, don’t get impatient yet~ As the saying goes, bet against the bet, build a villa by the sea. As long as you let Sister Shizune join the bet and always buy the opposite result of yours, wouldn’t that be a sure win ? If you bet one Ryo, Sister Shizune bets five Ryo. Like this, if you gamble a round in the Ninja World, you’ll probably be able to pay off your debts soon. »

Shizune’s eyes grew brighter and brighter, but Tsunade’s face grew darker and darker. She shouted with dissatisfaction :

« How could this kind of method possibly work ? It’s not like I always lose, and besides, the reason I lose is because they are cheating ! You’re just giving them money this way ! Shin, you really are still a wet-behind-the-ears kid ; you don’t understand anything at all. This method of yours simply won’t work ! »

Shizune ignored her and calculated the possibility of this in her heart :

'Theoretically, it is completely possible, and the probability is infinitely close to 100% ! After all, I’ve never seen Lady Tsunade win once ! Hahaha, I, Shizune, am about to strike it rich !'

The meal was finished, but Tsunade was still rambling on. Asakawa Shin interrupted directly :

« There’s no use talking more ; why don’t we go to the casino and play ? »

Tsunade’s eyes lit up, but they quickly dimmed : « But I don’t have any money left ; they cheated and tricked me out of all my money this morning. »

Asakawa Shin was a bit speechless ; Senior Sister Tsunade really had a stiff mouth. He said decisively :

« Forget it, I’ll lend you... no, I’ll give you one hundred million Ryo as the appearance fee for the Big Fat Sheep. So now... where is the biggest casino in the Land of Hot Springs ? I want to verify the cards ! »
