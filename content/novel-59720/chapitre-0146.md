# Chapitre 146 — Acting Skills

The fifth match was still a battle between female Ninjas, with Temari from the Hidden Sand Village facing off against Tenten from Konoha.

The moment they exchanged blows, Tenten sensed that something was wrong.

Temari’s exquisite Wind Release was practically her natural enemy ; no matter what kind of Ninja tools she threw, they were easily blown away by the violent winds.

Realizing the situation was unfavorable, Tenten couldn’t help but take on a serious expression.

On the opposite side, a confident smile curled at the corner of Temari’s mouth ; the bad luck she had accumulated over the past two days had finally run out, and it was finally her turn to hold her head high.

A cold light flashed in Tenten’s eyes as she decisively changed tactics, drawing a Kunai and lunging straight at Temari, intending to force a close-quarters melee.

Temari’s gaze sharpened, instantly seeing through her opponent’s intention.

Without hesitation, she raised the giant fan in her hand and swung it fiercely at the charging Tenten.

« Second Star ! »

With a sharp cry, violent wind blades erupted from the fan’s surface, transforming into a visible, intense storm that swept forward.

Exerting force through her feet, Tenten used 'Shave' to forcefully change her position, narrowly avoiding the strike.

Temari was shocked to see this and couldn’t help but whisper, « What incredible speed ! »

Before she could recover, Tenten closed in rapidly by using 'Shave' again in one breath, her Kunai carving a sharp arc as it stabbed toward Temari.

Suddenly finding her opponent in close quarters, Temari was thrown into a panic ; caught off guard, she soon took several heavy blows, and several wounds were slashed across her body, leaving her in a rather pathetic state.

She let out a furious roar, abruptly unfolding her giant fan completely and swinging it violently at her surroundings.

« Third Star ! »

A violent gale suddenly exploded, turning into a raging wave that swept toward Tenten.

Caught unprepared, Tenten was hit head-on by this wide-range attack.

At this distance, she could no longer dodge, so she could only curl her body and use the momentum of the storm to distance herself from Temari, thus shedding most of the impact.

Despite this, several bloody gashes were still carved into her body by the invisible wind pressure ; for a moment, a situation where both sides were injured was formed.

Temari leaned on her fan, panting heavily ; her physical stamina and Chakra were both heavily depleted, and she even felt the injuries she had just recovered from over the past few days starting to ache slightly again.

In the distance, Tenten scrambled up from the ground in a mess, her body covered in small wounds from the wind pressure, her eyes warily locked onto Temari.

But when she saw Temari’s weakened, exhausted state, a relaxed smile appeared at the corner of her mouth.

In the blink of an eye, Tenten instantly changed her move, pulling two scrolls from her tool pouch and tossing them into the air, then leaping upward.

« Twin Rising Dragons ! »

Following her sharp shout, in an instant, countless Ninja tools poured out of the scrolls like a torrent, hurtling toward Temari.

Temari’s expression was grave as she gritted her teeth, condensing the last of the Chakra in her body and swinging her giant fan with all her might.

« Ninja Art : Sickle Weasel ! »

A violent gale swept out, colliding fiercely with the Ninja tools flying through the air, forcefully blocking all the attacks.

Just as a smile began to rise on Temari’s lips, she was stunned to find that no one was in front of her anymore.

Her heart sank as she realized something was wrong.

However, it was already too late. Tenten had taken advantage of the moment she performed the Ninjutsu to rush behind her with lightning speed, and was now lunging at her fiercely.

By the time Temari sensed her enemy’s presence and turned around in a panic, it was already too late.

Tenten’s prepared strike hit her stomach hard, sending her flying far away like a kite with a broken string.

When the dust settled, Temari remained face down on the ground, unable to get up for a long time.

Tenten tentatively threw the Kunai in her hand ; with a sharp whistling sound, the Kunai grazed Temari’s cheek and embedded itself deeply into the ground beside her.

Temari stared helplessly at the Kunai, unable to make any response.

Tenten then looked up at the referee and gave a slight nod.

Seeing this, the referee immediately announced : « Temari has lost the ability to fight, Tenten wins ! »

As soon as the words fell, Rock Lee jumped up excitedly, cheering loudly for Tenten.

Neji also showed a rare smile, clapping lightly, happy for Tenten.

Might Guy was instantly ignited with passion, hopping and jumping, finally hugging Rock Lee excitedly and weeping with joy.

On the other side, Gaara hesitated, unsure whether he should go forward to help Temari...

Then he was stunned to see Asakawa Shin walking over with large strides, performing the Mystical Palm Technique to heal Temari’s injuries.

No longer able to restrain the suspicion and shock in his heart, he stepped forward quickly and questioned sharply, « What are you doing ? »

Asakawa Shin glanced at him indifferently, his tone calm : « Can’t you tell ? I’m healing Temari. »

« I obviously know you’re healing her ! »

Gaara’s brow furrowed, a flash of confusion and wariness in his eyes, « But why are you doing this ? »

Before Gaara could finish, he was coldly interrupted by Asakawa Shin : « Why what ? Temari is my property now. You’d better understand the situation and don’t cross the line. »

« What ? You dare say that again ? »

Gaara was instantly enraged, killing intent surfacing in his eyes. He was about to lash out when he suddenly saw Temari leaning in Asakawa Shin’s arms, a suspicious blush appearing on her cheeks.

« Th... thank you, Shin-kun, » she stammered in a low voice, her eyes dodging his.

Seeing this, the corner of Gaara’s eye twitched violently, and his anger immediately died down.

Seeing that she was no longer in serious trouble, Asakawa Shin turned to leave amidst Temari’s reluctant gaze.

Since a moment ago, he had sensed a resentful gaze stuck to him that he couldn’t shake off. Following his intuition, he turned his head, and as expected, it was Tenten.

He hurried over, and before he could even stand still, he heard Tenten speak sarcastically : « Oh, what wind blew such a busy man over here ? »

Asakawa Shin scratched his head awkwardly and gave a dry laugh : « Sunagakure is an ally of Konoha after all ; it’s reasonable for me to go offer some condolences, right ? By the way, Tenten, how are your injuries ? Come here, let me check them for you. »

Tenten rolled her eyes in annoyance, « How could I be so bold ? After all, I’m not like Temari ; I don’t have the good fortune to be your 'property'~ »

Asakawa Shin laughed it off, trying to gloss over it : « Tenten, what are you saying ? I don’t understand... Alright, come over here, let Big Brother check your body. »

« Go away~ »

...

Immediately afterward, the draw results for the sixth match were out : Nara Shikamaru versus Omoi from the Hidden Cloud Village.

Nara Shikamaru habitually scratched his head and walked onto the stage with a face full of helplessness.

On the opposite side, Omoi had a lollipop in his mouth, his face also written with 'dejection,' as if he were about to stick his sword into the ground and sigh the next second.

When their eyes met, they found that they shared a certain similarity, and a feeling of mutual sympathy arose in their hearts.

As the referee gave the order, the two of them reluctantly pulled out their weapons and took their stances.

Facing Omoi’s sharp Lightning Release swordsmanship, Nara Shikamaru consistently adopted kiting tactics, dodging flexibly and resolutely refusing to face him head-on.

The scene instantly turned into a dull and boring chase.

Just as the audience was becoming impatient, a sharp light flashed in Nara Shikamaru’s eyes, and his hands instantly formed seals, « Shadow Possession Jutsu ! »

With a low shout, the shadow beneath his feet twisted like a living thing, merging with the shadows on the wall, and then launched a silent sneak attack on Omoi from behind.

However, Omoi was extremely cautious by nature and was already on guard against the Nara clan’s famous Shadow Possession Jutsu ; after all, this technique was too famous, it was hard not to know about it.

He quickly moved toward the center of the arena, not giving the shadow a single chance.

For a moment, the situation actually reversed.

« Hey, what on earth are these two doing ? Why haven’t they started fighting yet ? »

« Exactly, it’s just nonsensical... »

Shikamaru thought to himself : 'This guy is actually so cautious. Shin’s mission is such a drag ; should I just throw the match...'

The next moment, he decisively abandoned the use of his shadow secret techniques and charged straight forward with a Kunai in hand.

However, Omoi’s face changed drastically upon seeing this ; thinking Shikamaru had some trick up his sleeve, he turned and ran wildly without hesitation, a scene that left the surrounding audience with black lines on their foreheads.

Shikamaru was also completely defeated by this guy’s behavior. With his signature dead-fish eyes, he helplessly requested the referee : « I’m out of stamina, I want to forfeit. »

« Are you sure ? » The referee was dumbfounded.

Seeing Nara Shikamaru nod seriously in confirmation, the referee could only announce the result of the match with a stiff expression.

Asakawa Shin couldn’t help but put his hand to his forehead, his heart full of speechlessness : 'No way ? I told you to put on a show and let the Hidden Cloud Village’s lone survivor advance, and this is how you act ? Your acting is worse than a fresh-faced idol’s !'
