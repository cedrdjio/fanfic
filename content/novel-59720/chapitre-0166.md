# Chapitre 166 — Switching Opponents

Rasa gasped heavily ; that last clash had almost drained all the Chakra from his body.

Hearing the Fourth Raikage’s accusation now, he was momentarily speechless, his chest heaving with rage.

He then turned his head to look at Might Guy in disbelief, his lips parting slightly, but he ultimately couldn’t utter a single word.

In contrast, Might Guy just grinned, revealing a set of pearly white teeth that shone brilliantly in the sunlight, and then even gave Rasa a thumbs up.

« That attack just now was quite good, but the warm-up... should be over, right ? How about we get serious now ? »

Rasa’s pupils dilated upon hearing this, his voice carrying a hint of imperceptible trembling : « Warm... warm-up ? »

Before he could recover from his shock, Might Guy came charging at him, shouting something about « Youth » and « Hot-bloodedness ».

Rasa was horrified and hurriedly manipulated his Gold Dust to lift his body into the air ; only after hovering in mid-air did he let out a slight sigh of relief.

However, it didn’t last long...

« What ? You can actually fly ? That’s... great ! Aerial combat ? Interesting... »

The next moment, Might Guy’s eyes flashed with intensity as he suddenly exerted force under his feet to perform Moon Walk, actually treading on the air and dashing through the sky as if it were flat ground.

Rasa’s pupils constricted, his eyes nearly popping out of their sockets as he exclaimed in shock : « Is this the Konoha Six Styles mentioned in the intel ? To think it can actually walk on air—this is big trouble ! »

For a moment, Rasa was like a mouse seeing a cat, scurrying around in the air in a pathetic state while manipulating his Gold Dust, while Might Guy followed closely like a shadow, refusing to let up.

The two instantly staged a thrilling aerial chase high above.

Might Guy was fighting heartily and feeling fired up, but Hiruzen Sarutobi on this side was having a miserable time.

Originally, when facing Rasa, he could still manage to hold his own.

After all, both were Ninjutsu-type Ninja, and relying on his rich combat experience and seasoned tactics, he could completely hold the opponent off and solidify his reputation as the « Professor of Ninjutsu » and the « Strongest Hokage. »

However, when the opponent suddenly switched to the Fourth Raikage, the battlefield situation took a sharp turn for the worse.

Ai was the type of Taijutsu Ninja he was least adept at handling, and after activating his Lightning Release Chakra Mode, his speed was as fast as lightning ; with his current elderly and frail body, he couldn’t parry it at all.

After only a moment of exchanging blows, Hiruzen Sarutobi had already faced several life-threatening situations and had almost been captured by Ai on the spot.

At this moment, he was inwardly lamenting ; looking across the battlefield, everyone, whether friend or foe, was showing off their power, while only he was being beaten up pathetically.

What made him even angrier was that even that old geezer Danzo had shown off his strength in front of the juniors, which caused him to completely break down ; right now, he was gnashing his teeth in hatred.

Seeing Ai turning back and attacking with lightning, Hiruzen Sarutobi dared not be slow ; he hurriedly smeared the blood from the corner of his mouth with his fingertips and then rapidly formed hand seals.

« Ninja Art : Summoning Jutsu ! »

As a puff of white smoke cleared, a sturdy, muscular ape appeared—it was his Summoning beast, the Monkey King Enma.

« Hmph, a Summoning beast ? What can such a thing do to me ? »

Ai snorted coldly, his speed increasing instead of decreasing. Lightning Release Chakra suddenly erupted around his body, instantly concentrating on his right hand blade.

« Lariat ! »

Facing the menacing Raikage Ai, Hiruzen Sarutobi shouted confidently : « Enma, transform ! »

The old ape understood immediately. Accompanied by a cloud of smoke, his body instantly transformed into an Adamantine Staff, which then flew toward Hiruzen Sarutobi.

« Adamantine Prison Wall ! »

With a roar from the old ape, the Adamantine Staff suddenly split into countless clones, which rapidly arranged themselves around Hiruzen Sarutobi, instantly constructing an indestructible prison that protected him tightly.

Almost at the same instant, Ai’s attack arrived.

The hand blade, imbued with sharp Lightning Release Chakra, slammed fiercely against the Adamantine Prison Wall, only to be completely blocked by the Adamantine Staff without causing any damage.

A gleam flashed in Hiruzen Sarutobi’s eyes as he sneered : « It’s useless. Enma possesses an indestructible body ; ordinary attacks can’t hurt him at all. Now then... it’s my turn. »

Before Ai could react, a furry giant palm suddenly reached out from the Adamantine Staff, tightly gripping Ai’s arm and rendering him unable to move.

Meanwhile, inside the Adamantine Prison Wall, Hiruzen Sarutobi’s hands had already turned into a blur, rapidly forming hand seals.

The moment the Adamantine Prison Wall was released, the Ninjutsu, which had been primed and ready, was decisively launched.

« Water Release : Water Severing Wave ! »

Ai’s pupils constricted ; he instinctively wanted to use the Lightning Body Flicker to retreat, but the old ape’s strength was extraordinary, and he couldn’t break free for the moment.

At the critical moment, he violently concentrated Lightning Release Chakra into the soles of his feet and then slammed them heavily into the ground with thunderous force.

« Guillotine Drop ! »

The high platform roof beneath his feet was struck by this blow and instantly shattered like fragile porcelain.

Hiruzen Sarutobi lost his footing and stumbled, causing his Water Severing Wave to deviate by a fraction, allowing Ai to twist his body and dodge it at the limit.

« Oh... oh no... »

Compared to Hiruzen Sarutobi’s panic, Ai let out a cold laugh.

The moment he fell from the high platform and his body was descending, he unexpectedly reached back and grabbed Enma, lifting him high up.

At the same time, Lightning Release Chakra erupted around his body, even making his hair stand on end ; he had already entered the full-power state of the « Strongest Lightning Release Armor. »

Then, using the gravity of his descent, he exerted all his strength the moment he landed and slammed the Enma in his hands fiercely into the ground.

« Liger Bomb ! »

Amidst a thunderous boom, even though Enma possessed an indestructible body, he was dazed, vomiting blood from his mouth, and without saying a word... he was dismissed from the Summoning in a puff of white smoke.

« Enma ! »

Witnessing the old ape’s miserable state, Hiruzen Sarutobi’s eye twitched violently ; he made a prompt decision and ran away without looking back.

While scurrying away in a pathetic state, he shouted at the top of his lungs toward the sky : « Guy ! Stop playing, come down and switch with me ! »

Might Guy, who was chasing Rasa in the air, was stunned upon hearing this and hurriedly turned to look, only to see Hiruzen Sarutobi being chased and beaten by the Raikage in a pathetic state.

Before he could make a move, Rasa, who had just been sent flying while vomiting blood by his Flying Finger Pistol, had already taken a step ahead and was flying straight toward Hiruzen Sarutobi.

He manipulated his Gold Dust to suddenly cut off Ai’s path, then unhesitatingly stepped up to engage in a « fierce » battle with Hiruzen Sarutobi.

Ai was stunned for a moment, then flew into a rage.

However, before he could utter a word of reprimand, a familiar green figure descended from the sky like a meteorite, locking onto him with a burning gaze.

« That guy just now only knew how to run away, it was simply not Youthful at all ! Fighting you is much more interesting ; let’s continue, shall we ? »
