# Chapitre 156 — The Child of Calamity

« Nani ? You... you learned it just like that ? »

Naruto felt as if he had been struck by lightning. His face was filled with disbelief, and his entire spirit instantly deflated.

Asakawa Shin scratched his head, realizing he had overdone the showing off just now, and hurriedly laughed it off to cover it up.

« I’m just kidding, Naruto. Actually, I started learning this Ninjutsu a long time ago. After all, it was developed by the Fourth Hokage, a Ninjutsu meant to complement the Flying Thunder God ! »

This was a lie.

Although Asakawa Shin had long known the cultivation method and principles of the Rasengan, he had never actually practiced it.

To him, this Ninjutsu was a bit redundant.

After all, there was nothing a single punch couldn’t solve ; if there was, then another punch would do.

Only then did Naruto perk up again, scratching his head and laughing foolishly. « Geez, Shin, you almost fooled me just now. I really thought you learned it in just a few moments. »

« Haha, prank successful~ »

Asakawa Shin laughed with his hands on his hips. « By the way, this Ninjutsu is quite difficult. Which step have you reached ? »

Naruto sighed gloomily and held up the rubber ball in his hand, his tone full of resentment. « It’s this. No matter what I do, I can’t pop it. »

Asakawa Shin nodded understandingly. « Naruto, sometimes what one person can’t do alone might be possible if everyone works together. »

« Everyone together ? But this is Ninjutsu cultivation, how do we practice together ? »

Asakawa Shin winked mysteriously. « Figure it out yourself. »

Immediately, he turned his gaze to Jiraiya nearby. « I’m borrowing your Pervy Sage for a bit, you don’t mind, do you ? »

Naruto rolled his eyes in annoyance, turned around, and ran off to continue brooding over it on his own.

Asakawa Shin and Jiraiya walked some distance away with a mutual understanding.

Jiraiya spoke with a face full of emotion, « Junior Brother, your talent is simply too terrifying. To be able to use the Rasengan so skillfully after just one look. »

Asakawa Shin waved his hand casually and said nonchalantly, « Senior Brother overpraises me. After all, I am a Medical Ninja ; I still have quite a bit of confidence in the fine control of Chakra. »

Jiraiya nodded thoughtfully, though it was unclear if he truly believed this explanation.

« Junior Brother, did you seek me out today specifically because you have some important matter to attend to ? »

« Can’t I come to catch up with Senior Brother even if there’s nothing wrong ? »

Jiraiya had a 'keep making things up' expression on his face, clearly unmoved by this.

Seeing this, Asakawa Shin simply stopped pretending and laid his cards on the table. « Alright, I do have a request. I know Senior Brother is a contractor of Mount Myōboku. I want to visit Mount Myōboku, and I hope Senior Brother will grant this. »

Hearing this, Jiraiya frowned slightly. « Mount Myōboku is not a place one can enter and leave at will. There are many rules, and it’s quite troublesome. »

Seeing the disappointment on Asakawa Shin’s face, he immediately changed his tune : « However, I can first have Fukasaku-sama send word. If Mount Myōboku agrees, I will take you there immediately. »

Asakawa Shin nodded, secretly grumbling in his heart : 'As expected of a holy land connected to the Six Paths Sage, they certainly put on airs.'

« Then I’ll have to trouble Senior Brother. »

Jiraiya patted his chest and guaranteed, « Leave it to me. Junior Brother, wait patiently for a few days. As soon as there’s news, I’ll notify you immediately. »

A few days later, Asakawa Shin waited and waited until he finally received word from Jiraiya.

« Junior Brother, the Great Toad Sage has awakened, and he has agreed to your visit to Mount Myōboku. Get ready, we leave immediately. »

There was a hint of solemnity in Jiraiya’s voice.

Asakawa Shin didn’t say much and casually threw out a Flying Thunder God Kunai.

Jiraiya caught it in surprise, staring at the Kunai for a moment before exclaiming in amazement, « This is the Flying Thunder God Technique ? Junior Brother, your talent is truly stunning. It’s as if there is no Ninjutsu in this world you cannot learn. »

In an instant, the two had arrived at Mount Myōboku.

Asakawa Shin looked around and couldn’t help but admire it secretly.

This place truly lived up to its name as a legendary holy land. The scenery was like a painting, the air was clear, and it possessed a transcendent, immortal atmosphere.

Compared to this, Shikkotsu Forest and Ryūchi Cave were simply far behind.

Jiraiya caught the look of amazement in Asakawa Shin’s eyes, and the corners of his mouth curled up slightly, revealing a bit of pride.

He led the way while introducing the scenery along the route as if enumerating his family treasures, acting every bit like the master of Mount Myōboku.

It was as if he wasn’t a Ninja of Konoha Village, but rather a toad of Mount Myōboku.

Passing through a deep forest, a magnificent temple suddenly appeared before them ; that was the residence of the Great Toad Sage.

At the entrance, two small toads had been waiting for a long time—they were Fukasaku-sama and Shima-sama.

The two Sages looked up at Asakawa Shin, their eyes full of curiosity.

« Is this the human the Great Toad Sage specifically asked to see ? He is indeed extraordinary. It’s the first time I’ve seen a human with a flower growing on his head, » Shima-sama muttered in a low voice.

After everyone exchanged greetings, they moved into the Great Hall.

Reaching the center of the hall, they saw a giant toad sitting on a throne. His face was aged, and he was sleeping with his eyes closed.

Seeing this, Fukasaku-sama hurriedly stepped forward a few paces and reminded loudly, « Great Toad Sage, wake up ! The person you’ve been longing to see is here ! »

After calling several times, the giant toad slowly opened his bleary eyes and looked down in a daze.

« Oh... it’s Little Jiraiya. What brings you here ? »

Fukasaku-sama, standing to the side, facepalmed and helplessly reminded, « Great Toad Sage, you were the one who said you wanted to see them. You’ve forgotten again. »

The Great Toad Sage froze for a moment, then suddenly realized, « Oh, right, right, right ! It was I who wanted to see him. »

He turned his gaze back to the group, finally settling on Jiraiya with an approving look, and spoke slowly :

« Little Jiraiya, I didn’t expect you to change your usual ways this time and find the 'Child of Calamity' so quickly. »

The joy on Jiraiya’s face suddenly froze, replaced by a gaze full of disbelief.

He murmured to himself, « Child of Calamity ? I... I found him ? »

He stiffly turned his neck, his gaze falling on Asakawa Shin beside him, and he was instantly drenched in cold sweat.

« This is bad. Won’t the old man skin me alive now ? »

The Great Toad Sage chuckled and comforted, « Jiraiya, there’s no need to be nervous. We have no intention of harming him.

What I foresee is only a possibility, not a fixed future. As long as he is guided well, the Child of Calamity can also become the Child of Prophecy. »

Jiraiya opened his mouth to argue further, but the Great Toad Sage no longer paid him any mind. Instead, he focused all his attention on Asakawa Shin and said earnestly :

« Young friend, since a few years ago, I have been constantly dreaming of fragmented pieces. From that moment on, the future of the Ninja World became chaotic and filled with countless variables.

In several of those dreams, I saw you destroying the Ninja World, and through various means that were simply inconceivable.

I know that may not be your intention, but I implore you to think thrice before you act. The Ninja World is too fragile ; it really cannot withstand your meddling. »

« Child of Calamity ? Destroying the Ninja World ? Who ? Me ? »

Asakawa Shin looked up with a face full of confusion, then straightened his face and started shouting at the Great Toad Sage.

« Hey, I’m warning you, don’t think I won’t make a move just because you’re old.

If you don’t believe me, go ask the White Snake Sage. I don’t pull any punches when I hit old men and old ladies.

If you dare slander me again, watch out, I won’t be polite ! »
