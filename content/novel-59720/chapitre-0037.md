# Chapitre 37 — I'll Use This Move On You For A Lifetime

'Damn brat, you actually ambushed me, an old man, again !'

'And this brat actually has sensory abilities ! When was my Water Release heart discovered ? It was clearly hidden within the water condensed from Chakra.'

Kakuzu cursed under his breath. By the time he realized Asakawa Shin’s target, it was already too late. The massive attack range of the Yan Yue had already enveloped him ; the Water Release heart had nowhere to hide.

Before the Yan Yue even landed, the accumulated water where the Water Release heart was hiding began to churn and boil, instantly brought to a boil by the high heat.

Although the Yan Yue’s power was astonishing, the Water Release heart had no intention of awaiting execution. It rapidly condensed all its Chakra, to the point where cracks even appeared on its mask. It had clearly decided to give its all and fight a desperate battle.

'Brat who only knows how to ambush, let me show you the backbone of an old-school Ninja !'

'And those shameless Konoha Ninja, let me show you the true power of Water Release !'

'Water Release : Super Water Curtain Wall ! ! !'

With unprecedented resolve, Kakuzu condensed a massive amount of Chakra, even causing the Water Release heart to sustain heavy damage as it could not withstand the strain. At this cost, Kakuzu unleashed the strongest Water Release Ninjutsu of his life.

The massive amount of Chakra transformed into water flow, even drawing in the accumulated water around the valley to converge and form a terrifying water cannon.

This water cannon strike was like the waters of the Milky Way flowing in reverse. The surging torrent shot forth like an arrow released from a bow, rushing endlessly, with fierce waves galloping like wild horses, carrying astonishing momentum as it slammed violently into the Yan Yue.

Seeing the power of this water cannon from the air, Asakawa Shin couldn’t help but click his tongue. This was more than a hundred times more powerful than a coast guard ship’s water cannon ; if this were used to hit the Philippine monkeys, they’d be black and blue all over.

In this situation, Asakawa Shin couldn’t help but want to hum a few bars of 'Orchid Grass'.

Unfortunately, even though the Water Release technique that old man Kakuzu had risked everything to unleash held a slight elemental advantage, the collision was one-sided.

Although the Water Release Kakuzu unleashed was powerful and imposing, it was, at the end of the day, an AOE continuous damage attack.

The Yan Yue was the exact opposite ; its power was concentrated at the edge of the slash. Therefore, when they collided, the Yan Yue only paused slightly before piercing through the point of impact, cutting through the Water Curtain Wall like a hot knife through butter. Although the attached flames were diminished significantly by the constant washing of the torrent, that was all that happened ; the Yan Yue continued to descend firmly toward the Water Release heart.

'Nani ? Oh... oh no !'

Although the Water Release heart sensed that things were going poorly, its Chakra was almost completely exhausted, and it had no strength left to dodge this blow.

No sooner said than done, before the Yan Yue even arrived, the wind pressure pinned the Water Release heart in place, unable to move, forcing it to take the blow head-on. Then, under the tense gaze of everyone, the Yan Yue finally landed, suppressing the Water Release heart as it slammed fiercely into the ground, burying it deep underground.

« Art is Patrick Star, Hah ! »

With a deafening boom, the power of the Yan Yue fully erupted. A small mushroom cloud rose slowly, followed by a circular shockwave of heat that spread across the entire field, completely destroying the already dilapidated forest.

« Holy sh*t, I knew it, hurry... »

« Water Release : Water Formation Wall ! » *n

« Earth Release : Earth Flow Wall ! » *n

« ... »

Ten seconds passed, and the shockwave finally dissipated completely. The battlefield was filled with flying dust and smoke, and the temperature had soared to over a hundred degrees. Even with Chakra protecting their bodies, Shikaku and the others felt extremely uncomfortable. The hiding place of the Water Release heart had turned into a giant crater of magma, bottomless, and the areas slightly closer had already turned to glass. As for the Water Release heart, it was dead as dead could be.

Kakuzu ran for his life through the forest with a grave expression. That’s right, he had chosen to make a strategic retreat during the earlier chaos. He had already sensed the fate of the Water Release heart, and even an old veteran like him, who had seen many major scenes, was filled with terror at this moment.

A casual strike was a killer move on the level of a Tailed Beast Ball. How the hell is anyone supposed to fight this ? Is he cheating ? An honest Ninja like himself was completely countered by this, with zero chance of winning.

The only option now was to retreat first. Anyway, the goal of this trip had been achieved. He swore that in the future, if he encountered this brat, he would give him a wide berth. If I can’t beat you, can’t I just outlive you ?

The First Hokage was powerful, right ? Uchiha Madara was powerful, right ? But what was the result ?

Seeing this brat being so powerful at such a young age, he definitely won’t be an ordinary person in the future. By then, I’ll be able to brag to future Ninjas...

« Old man, where are you going ? »

Just as Kakuzu was imagining how he would show off in the future, a voice suddenly came from behind him, giving him a huge fright.

The person who arrived was none other than Asakawa Shin. Kakuzu had taken so much of his money ; how could he possibly let Kakuzu go ? When Kakuzu ran away, he had left behind a Shadow Clone to protect Shikaku and the others, while his real body had come to chase him down personally.

The reason he hadn’t shown himself until now was to secretly search for Kakuzu’s missing Lightning Release heart, but after following him the whole way, he hadn’t found a single trace.

'It seems old man Kakuzu is very cautious. Did he escape separately ? Forget it, never mind. Let’s get my money back first.'

« Nani ? When ? » Hearing the voice behind him made Kakuzu’s soul leave his body instantly. Because he was way too close, it was too late to turn around now ; the enemy definitely wouldn’t give him that chance. The only option now was...

Kakuzu quickly performed hand signs. The masks stitched onto his back suddenly came to life, opening their large mouths through his clothes to unleash Ninjutsu.

« Wind Release : Pressure Damage ! »

The violent storm tore Kakuzu’s coat to shreds and swept toward Asakawa Shin in an instant. Asakawa Shin had no time to react and was hit head-on by this Wind Release Ninjutsu, flying backward while screaming like a broken doll.

« Hahahaha hahahaha, you brat, didn’t expect that, right ? No matter how cunning you are, don’t you still have to drink your Grandpa Kakuzu’s foot-washing water ? »

Kakuzu turned around, feeling triumphant. At this moment, Asakawa Shin was covered in wounds, bleeding non-stop, looking like he was about to die, with none of his previous arrogance left.

Kakuzu delivered his victory declaration :

« I admit your talent is simply unparalleled, and you once brought me pressure like a mountain. But it’s such a pity ; our combat experience is just too different. After all, I am a Ninja who has fought against the First Hokage. »

« Brat, you destroyed two of my hearts, so use your heart to compensate ! »

Just as Kakuzu was about to use the Earth Grudge Fear secret technique to take Asakawa Shin’s heart, under his horrified gaze, Asakawa Shin’s body actually turned into mud and slowly collapsed.

« No... No way ! »

« Flying Finger Pistol : Yang Flower Thrust : Double Strike ! »

As the voice sounded, two red-gold spikes struck with lightning speed, instantly piercing through the tree behind Kakuzu and attacking behind him, targeting the two Wind and Fire mask hearts.

With no time to react at all, both of Kakuzu’s hearts were destroyed, and his entire body was sent flying like a rag.

As these two hearts were destroyed, Kakuzu’s consciousness within the Earth Grudge Fear quickly dissipated. In his hazy final moments, he heard...

« Let that teach you to ambush Uncle Shikaku ! See if I don’t ambush you hard ! »

« But honestly, this ambush move is really useful ! The way you were acting all arrogant just now was also pretty funny, hehehe. »

« One good trick is enough to eat for a lifetime ; the ancients did not deceive me. I’ll use this move on you for a lifetime, hehehe. »
