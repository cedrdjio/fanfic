# Chapitre 172 — I'm Ready

Deep in the forest outside Konoha Village, the area had turned into a chaotic battlefield. Two massive creatures were locked in a fierce life-and-death struggle, completely destroying a large swath of the surrounding forest.

Gamabunta panted heavily, his arm clutching the short sword trembling slightly. « Jiraiya ! And Pops, Ma ! Are you guys ready yet ? I can’t hold on much longer ! »

Jiraiya maintained his hands pressed together, with Fukasaku Sage and Shima Sage standing on his left and right shoulders respectively.

« Don’t rush me, Bunta, it’ll be ready soon ! »

« That’s right, Bunta, hold on a bit longer ! Don’t embarrass the toads ! »

Bunta made a clumsy leap, narrowly dodging the Air Bullets fired by Shukaku, and roared in dissatisfaction : « It’s easy for you to say ! I’m exhausted, hurry up ! »

Beside them, Naruto was full of terror, his hands tightly gripping Bunta’s clothes for fear of being thrown off.

« Pervy Sage ! Didn’t you say you had a super powerful Ninjutsu to show me ? Then use it already ! »

Jiraiya rolled his eyes. « I want to hurry too, but this is how the process for Sage Mode goes. I can’t help it ! »

« Sage Mode ? Is it the Sage Mode I know ? »

Naruto suddenly grew angry. « Pervy Sage, stop lying ! I’ve seen Shin use Sage Mode with my own eyes ; he just claps his hands and it’s done. It’s nothing like your troublesome way ! This move of yours definitely isn’t authentic ! Could it be... you spend all day focusing on writing those dirty books and didn’t learn it properly ? »

Jiraiya was so angry he nearly fainted, his face flushing red. « Shut up, you brat, Naruto ! You’ll see how powerful this move is in a moment. Besides, Sage Mode is extremely difficult to learn ; you might not even be able to learn it when the time comes ! »

« Hmph ! How is that possible ? I’m a genius ! » Naruto grunted defiantly.

...

« You bastards ! How dare you chat while facing this great lord ? So arrogant ! »

Before Shukaku could finish, Gaara’s voice, suppressed with anger, coldly cut in : « Shukaku, can you do this or not ? Every time you come out, it’s like this. Can you actually win for once ? »

Shukaku’s face turned red ; he also wanted to win a crushing victory, but the toad opposite him was too agile.

Gaara sighed helplessly. Although he had the heart to win, Shukaku was simply disappointing and got beaten every time. After losing to Rasa, he lost to Asakawa Shin, and now, sure enough, he was in a bitter struggle again. It was truly embarrassing.

He waved his hand wearily. « Forget it, just hand over control to me. »

With his intervention, Shukaku’s behavioral pattern suddenly changed.

He suddenly thrust his hands into the earth, pouring out a massive amount of Chakra, instantly turning the ground beneath him into sand, creating a giant wave of sand that swept toward Bunta.

« Sand Waterfall Flow. »

Bunta frowned and made a large leap, instantly leaving his original spot.

Seeing Gaara’s attack miss, Shukaku immediately shouted arrogantly : « See ! I told you this toad’s movement is too slippery. It’s not my fault at all ! »

Gaara’s brow furrowed deeply. The white-haired Ninja opposite him was clearly preparing a powerful technique. Now was not the time for internal strife with Shukaku ; they had to end this quickly.

Thinking this, he suddenly increased his Chakra output.

A massive amount of Chakra poured into the earth like a bursting flood, instantly turning the lush forest into a silent wasteland.

Bunta was shocked by the sight and immediately started running.

However, Gaara ignored him completely, turning around and speeding straight toward the Konoha Stadium.

Bunta cursed under his breath and turned to give chase.

He avoided the desert area, choosing to travel only on the solid ground that hadn’t been turned to sand.

To his surprise, the moment he passed, a massive amount of quicksand erupted from underground, wrapping around his limbs like venomous snakes, binding him tightly so he couldn’t move.

« Oh no, it’s a trap ! »

Before they could recover from their shock, Shukaku had already opened his giant maw. Blue and black Chakra fused frantically inside, condensing into a terrifying Tailed Beast Ball.

« Holy crap ! It’s a Tailed Beast Ball ! Jiraiya, do something ! » Bunta roared in panic.

« Sage Mode isn’t ready yet ! Bunta, show some guts and dodge it ! »

Bunta immediately cursed in a fit of rage : « Dodge your sister ! Are you blind ? Don’t you see my legs are buried ? »

While the man and the toad were arguing, Shukaku didn’t give them any time to dally and launched a Tailed Beast Ball right at them.

Naruto clutched his head and screamed in a breakdown : « Stop arguing ! The attack is coming ! »

At the critical moment, Asakawa Shin’s figure suddenly appeared, teleporting to everyone’s side.

As soon as he landed, he caught sight of a Tailed Beast Ball already in his face. He couldn’t help but roll his eyes, throwing a Flying Thunder God Kunai in response.

When Naruto saw Asakawa Shin appear, a look of « the savior is here » ecstasy appeared on his face. But the next second, seeing this light-handed action, he immediately cried out in shock :

« Shin, that’s a Tailed Beast Ball ! What’s the point of throwing a Kunai ? Use your invincible Wood Golem to take him down... »

Before he could finish, that seemingly tiny Kunai accurately collided with the Tailed Beast Ball.

An incredible scene unfolded : the attack, capable of destroying heaven and earth, instantly vanished without a trace.

Naruto’s eyes nearly popped out, his mouth hanging open wide enough to fit a fist. « Eh ? It’s gone ? »

Only then did Jiraiya breathe a sigh of relief, explaining to Naruto : « That’s the Flying Thunder God : Guiding Thunder. It can instantly teleport objects and attacks to a distant location. It’s an improved application of the Flying Thunder God Technique by the Fourth Hokage. »

« That’s so cool... » Naruto muttered to himself, dumbfounded.

But when his peripheral vision caught Jiraiya, he saw the guy was still striking a pose, and he immediately let out a merciless sneer :

« No way, Pervy Sage ? Your 'Sage Mode' still isn’t ready ? Shin, show him how it’s done—how you just clap your hands and enter Sage Mode. Let him see what’s what ! »

Hearing this, Asakawa Shin’s eyebrows twitched slightly, his expression calm. « What do you mean, clap your hands and enter Sage Mode ? I don’t know that kind of technique. »

Jiraiya felt a warmth in his heart, secretly breathing a sigh of relief. He thought Asakawa Shin was saving him some face and protecting his dignity.

However, in the next second, Asakawa Shin’s calm yet somewhat arrogant voice rang through the area :

« As for me, I’ve been maintaining Sage Mode at all times for a long while now. As for a Sage Mode that still requires clapping hands and weaving signs to enter... surely no one is still using that ? No way, right ? No way~ »

« ... »

In the distance, Gaara saw the attack miss and was instantly enraged. « Asakawa Shin ! You’ve come to ruin my plans again ! »

Asakawa Shin snorted coldly, his gaze sweeping over the opponent with contempt. « I almost forgot about you. A mere Ichibi dares to oppose me time and again. You don’t actually think you’re powerful, do you ? »

Shukaku hated hearing that the most. Just as he was about to retort, he saw Asakawa Shin’s hands already pressed together, and a low shout echoed through the sky.

« Sage Art : Gate of the Great God : Ten Seals ! »

Smoke billowed in the sky as countless giant Torii gates descended from above, accurately and dominantly pinning Shukaku’s limbs, head, and tail to the ground, rendering him completely unable to move.

Gaara was horrified. Feeling his body completely suppressed, he couldn’t help but curse at Shukaku inside him : « Shukaku ! You useless piece of trash, you haven’t lost again, have you ? I... I want to win. »

« You’re too noisy. » Asakawa Shin frowned slightly, seemingly annoyed by his clamor, and waved his hand again. « Sage Art : Gate of the Great God : Head Seal ! »

Several more thick Torii gates descended again, piling on top of Shukaku’s head, sealing him off completely.

The consciousness of both Gaara and Shukaku was forcibly sealed along with it, and they fell into a sleep as deep as an infant’s.

« Ah ? »

Naruto watched, dumbstruck. He scratched his head and asked in disbelief, « Is it... over just like that ? »

Only then did Jiraiya finally complete his final adjustments. He released his hands and muttered to himself quite awkwardly, « That... I... my Sage Mode is ready now. »

« So what ? Do you want us to give you an award ? »
