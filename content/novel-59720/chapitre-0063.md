# Chapitre 63 — Victory

« Sasuke, so what if you’re durable ? No matter how many times you get back up, I will knock you down again ! »

Uzumaki Naruto collected his thoughts, gazing at the high-spirited Uchiha Sasuke opposite him. He felt no fear and issued his declaration of victory to Uchiha Sasuke.

« Uzumaki Naruto, are you saying that as long as you want to, you can knock me down anytime, anywhere ? Interesting ! If you can do it, then come and try ! »

Uchiha Sasuke covered his right eye with one hand, his expression excited. His exposed left eye released a scarlet light, appearing exceptionally eerie : « Let me show you the power of our Uchiha Clan’s Bloodline Limit—the Sharingan ! »

Having said that, Uchiha Sasuke didn’t hesitate at all and charged toward Uzumaki Naruto like an arrow released from a bowstring.

Uzumaki Naruto was equally unafraid. The two rapidly closed the distance, and their right fists collided violently, launching a battle of strength between them.

Uchiha Sasuke’s pupils constricted slightly, and he took the initiative to retreat, widening the distance between himself and Uzumaki Naruto.

After this initial probing, he realized that although Uzumaki Naruto’s strength had weakened compared to before, it was still above his own.

However, the strength of the Sharingan lies in its exceptional dynamic vision and keen insight.

Although his physique was not as strong as Uzumaki Naruto’s, in a taijutsu duel, strength is not the only deciding factor ; the application of techniques is equally crucial !

Therefore, Uchiha Sasuke decisively adjusted his tactics and decided to engage in an intense head-on confrontation with Uzumaki Naruto.

His Sharingan, like a precision instrument, quickly analyzed the attack intentions and subtle openings in Uzumaki Naruto’s taijutsu.

Uzumaki Naruto’s attacks were either dodged or intercepted before they could fully exert force, making Uzumaki Naruto feel incredibly frustrated. As the battle progressed, the scales of advantage gradually tipped toward Uchiha Sasuke.

Soon, Uchiha Sasuke nimbly lowered his head, dodging Uzumaki Naruto’s straight punch.

A flash of brilliance erupted in his eyes. He decisively seized the opening and unleashed a ferociously powerful uppercut, striking Uzumaki Naruto’s chin hard and sending his entire body flying, causing him to crash heavily onto the ground.

« Hahaha, satisfying ! This is the feeling ; I wanted to do exactly this just a moment ago ! »

As expected, smiles do not disappear ; they only transfer. Uchiha Sasuke laughed wildly at the sky, and the hostility in his heart finally dissipated a little.

Uzumaki Naruto couldn’t laugh now. He used his arms to support his body and barely managed to crawl up, feeling only a dizzy head and nausea in his stomach.

He shook his head vigorously, trying to dispel this discomfort, his gaze fixed tightly on Uchiha Sasuke. He couldn’t help but shout in his heart : 'Something’s wrong ! There’s definitely a problem ! I feel like all my attacks are being seen through by Uchiha Sasuke. He’s like a loach, slippery and impossible to catch. Is this the power of the Sharingan he mentioned ? A capability similar to Hinata’s Byakugan ? Damn it, this is practically cheating ! How could a mere Uchiha Sasuke have this kind of ability...'

Uzumaki Naruto breathed with difficulty, struggling to calm the turbulent energy within him. He decided not to attack recklessly anymore, but instead adopted a defensive posture, gradually weakening Uchiha Sasuke’s stamina through a war of attrition.

Although he himself was nearing exhaustion, compared to Uchiha Sasuke, his endurance still held the upper hand. Uchiha Sasuke had clearly already fallen earlier, and for some unknown reason, he had gotten back up, but he was definitely at the end of his rope. As long as he held on, he could win !

Uzumaki Naruto’s plan was sound, but it is said that long-term defense inevitably leads to failure, especially when defending against the attacks of the Sharingan. Uzumaki Naruto was soon caught with another opening and was kicked flying by Uchiha Sasuke.

Uchiha Sasuke clearly noticed Uzumaki Naruto’s battle plan, and his face couldn’t help but darken a few shades. Although his Chakra had increased significantly due to awakening the Sharingan, and his stamina had recovered somewhat, he might not be able to outlast Uzumaki Naruto in a true war of attrition. He had to end it quickly !

Thus, the moment Uzumaki Naruto crawled up, Uchiha Sasuke had already closed the distance, and within a few moves, he knocked Uzumaki Naruto to the ground again.

Uchiha Sasuke was also nearing his limit at this moment, his body tottering, yet he still forced himself to stand, his eyes fixed intently on Uzumaki Naruto. He roared in the depths of his heart : 'Uzumaki Naruto, you bastard... hurry up and fall ! Stop struggling to get back up !'

Uzumaki Naruto breathed heavily, having been knocked to the ground repeatedly by heavy blows. Even with a physique far exceeding ordinary people, he had reached the brink of collapse. His vision was already a bit blurry, but he still didn’t give up. He supported himself on the ground with both hands, using all his strength to try and stand up, but ultimately his strength failed him, and his arms gave way, causing him to stumble and fall to the ground again.

'Is this the end ? Am I going to give up the position of squad leader to that bastard Uchiha Sasuke ? Damn it ! Doesn’t that mean he’ll be riding on top of me ? This kind of thing... this kind of thing is absolutely not allowed !'

This intense unwillingness seemed to be perceived by some existence, and a voice suddenly echoed in his heart : « Do you want power ? I can give it all to you, take it ! How much you can take depends on yourself, brat ! »

Thus, in Uchiha Sasuke’s shocked gaze, a surge of red Chakra suddenly appeared on Uzumaki Naruto’s body. This Chakra was exceptionally powerful ; its mere appearance stirred up gusts of wind, yet it gave people an unusually ominous feeling.

The Anbu Ninja beside the arena couldn’t help but exclaim : « Not good ! Is this the Nine-Tailed’s Chakra ? »

The Anbu Ninja all acted as if facing a great enemy. Just as they wanted to take action, they were stopped by Asakawa Shin with a wave of his hand : « Don’t be nervous, it’s just a little bit of leaked Chakra from the seal ! At the critical moment, I will intervene. Do not interfere with the progress of the match. »

The Anbu Ninja exchanged glances and all obeyed the order, retreating to the side.

It wasn’t that Asakawa Shin was arrogant ; it was that the Nine-Tailed in the seal posed no threat to him at all. Even a full-form Nine-Tailed would not make him afraid in the slightest.

He couldn’t feel afraid of the Nine-Tailed at all. Whenever he thought about how the Nine-Tailed was once grabbed like a figurine by Hashirama’s Great Buddha, pinched in the palm of one hand, he felt that this thing called the Nine-Tailed had no deterrent power whatsoever.

'Speaking of the Great Buddha, when exactly is Senior Brother Jiraiya coming back ? How long has it been... I haven’t even practiced my Sage Art yet. If he doesn’t come back in a few more days, I won’t wait. I’ll just contract with Sage Katsuyu and go to Shikkotsu Forest to train. When I learn Sage Art in the future, I’ll also summon a Great Buddha to grab a Nine-Tailed in one hand for fun, hahaha.'

« Eh ? Why did Uzumaki Naruto’s Chakra rampage suddenly end ? »

Asakawa Shin’s train of thought was suddenly interrupted. For some reason, the changes on Uzumaki Naruto’s body had suddenly ceased.

Just as Asakawa Shin was puzzled, inside the seal space within Uzumaki Naruto, Kurama was panting heavily, covered in sweat, its fur standing on end, the whole fox appearing exceptionally fluffy : « No... there’s no mistake ! The ominous feeling that malicious intent gave me just now was exactly like Senju Hashirama ! To think such a monster still exists outside, it’s too dangerous ! No, I can’t go out yet ; it’s safer here ! »

As Kurama’s Chakra was withdrawn, Uzumaki Naruto seemed to have been drained of his last bit of strength, his eyes rolled back, and he passed out cold.

Might Guy carefully checked, he officially announced : « Contestant Uzumaki Naruto is unable to continue, Contestant Uchiha Sasuke is the winner ! »

Uchiha Sasuke stood blankly in the arena at this moment, seemingly not having fully grasped the situation. When that red Chakra appeared just now, he was scared to death ; he thought he was going to be defeated by Uzumaki Naruto today... As it turned out, he didn’t expect that Chakra to be nothing but all show and no substance, it disappeared suddenly for some unknown reason, and that bastard Uzumaki Naruto lost consciousness and collapsed to the ground along with it.

« So, does this mean I really won ? Hehehe~ Hahahaha~ Gack... »

Uchiha Sasuke didn’t get to laugh for long before he also rolled his eyes and collapsed to the ground.

Looking at the chaotic situation in the arena, Asakawa Shin couldn’t help but scratch his head : « Is it time for the medical team to come out now... »
