# Chapitre 41 — Karin

Feeling that Kana in his arms was already trembling with rage, Asakawa Shin cursed under his breath and quickly stepped out from the shadows.

« Stop ! Let go of that girl ! ! ! »

Everyone was startled, and the guarding Anbu Ninja were also pale with shock ; they hadn’t even noticed the enemy’s presence until he was right in front of them. If he had attacked, they would have been finished...

Everyone quickly focused their attention and finally discovered Asakawa Shin’s presence.

'Hmm ? Just a brat ? Why is he holding a woman in his arms ?'

'Isn’t that the red-haired medical pack ? What is she doing here ?'

There were too many things to nitpick, leaving everyone unsure where to start for a moment. The scene suddenly fell into an eerie silence.

Kana, in Asakawa Shin’s arms, was the first to react. Under the strange gazes from all around, she quickly struggled free from his embrace.

'Oh no, it was so comfortable being held that I actually forgot for a moment.'

'No... that’s not right, Karin, my poor Karin...'

Uzumaki Kana rushed into the house, and the guarding Anbu didn’t stop her after confirming it was her.

Inside, Karin also saw the scene through a crack and was momentarily at a loss, even forgetting to cry.

When Kana entered the room, she saw her daughter with dull eyes, disheveled clothes, and a face covered in tear stains.

On her exposed arms were several bite marks as thick as her own, and she was huddled in a corner, not even reacting to the sight of her own mother.

'These beasts, Karin is still so small, how could they have the heart ?'

Grief surged in Kana’s heart. She stepped forward to pull Karin into her arms, gently patting her back, tears streaming down her face before she could even speak.

It seemed to work, as Karin’s dull eyes slowly regained their luster.

« Is it Mom ? *Sob sob*... Mom, where did you go ? I missed you so much, they all bullied me while you were gone... »

« *Sob sob*... Mom, I thought you didn’t want me anymore. »

Kana could no longer hold back and broke into tears, hugging Karin tightly.

« It’s Mom’s fault, I’m sorry. I’ll never leave you again... »

...

Until this moment, the Anbu Ninja outside finally reacted, drawing their swords and pointing them at Asakawa Shin, shouting :

« Brat, who are you ? Why are you with that woman ? Where is Lord Ryugen ? »

Asakawa Shin ignored him completely and simply walked toward the house.

« Who cares about some Ryugen or whatever ? How should I know ? »

Seeing Asakawa Shin acting so arrogantly, a trace of ruthlessness flashed in the Anbu Ninja’s eyes. Since you’re looking for death, then I’ll fulfill your wish !

« Die, brat ! »

He held his sword with both hands and slashed down fiercely at Asakawa Shin’s head. Seeing this, Asakawa Shin was not surprised but rather pleased :

'Oh ? This posture, this angle...'

Asakawa Shin’s eyes lit up. He quickly dropped into a horse stance and clapped his palms together upward, actually using a Blade Catch move to firmly trap the incoming blade between his palms, rendering it immobile.

« Nani ? »

Before the Anbu Ninja could react, Asakawa Shin tilted his palms to the side and easily snatched the sword into his hand.

The Anbu Ninja was pulled to the ground by this immense force.

Asakawa Shin didn’t even look at him ; he just casually delivered a kick with his toe, shattering the man’s internal organs and leaving him lying motionless on the ground like a dead dog.

The onlookers were dumbfounded and couldn’t help but shout at the Anbu :

« Hey ! What the hell are you doing ? Don’t be so fake ! And you, kid... »

Before he could finish, Asakawa Shin’s eyes sparkled as he replied :

« Oh, this move is called Mutodori ! Isn’t it cool ? I just borrowed the name. »

'Who asked you ? And what the hell is « borrowed » ? Can’t you come up with your own name ?'

Before he could speak, the other Ninjas felt a blur before their eyes and were directly cut down by Asakawa Shin’s swift blade. They were already gasping for their last breaths, on the verge of death.

« And there’s this move, called Thunderclap and Flash. Isn’t it also very cool ? »

Asakawa Shin continued his enthusiastic introduction, regardless of whether others wanted to hear it.

'I f*cking said, who asked you ?'

With a Chiburi, Asakawa Shin flicked the blood off and sheathed the sword into a scabbard he had picked up. It felt decent enough to use ; he could play with it more later.

Then, with the good mood of having found a favorite toy, he stepped into the house.

Inside the house.

« And so on and so forth... »

« ...That’s how it is. All these years, they’ve been using Mom as a medical pack. The Ninjas in this Village are all demons.

Karin, we can’t stay here anymore. We will definitely die if we stay. »

Karin hugged her mother’s arm, her heart aching so much she almost fainted. Her mother’s arm was covered in a dense array of old and new scars.

'I was only bitten a few times and it hurt like death. Mom, all these years...'

'I never even knew. No wonder Mom never took off her clothes in front of me.'

Just then, Asakawa Shin walked in. Kana quickly pulled Karin up and said to her solemnly :

« This older brother is Asakawa Shin. He is the young hero who came specifically to save us from this sea of misery.

We will be his handmaidens from now on. You must respect him just as you respect me.

Karin, quickly kneel and kowtow three times ! »

Karin knelt and kowtowed to Asakawa Shin without hesitation. She was a smart girl and knew what this boy, who was so beautiful he seemed to glow, meant for her and her mother.

He was redemption ; he was someone to rely on.

« Karin greets Lord Shin. »

After Karin acknowledged him as master, Asakawa Shin finally helped her up with a face full of smiles and patted her head, saying :

« Good sister, get up. We are family from now on.

Just remember one thing : only I can bully you, nobody else !

Alright, it’s time to go out and demand justice from those who bullied you. »

Karin nodded heavily. Her mother had been bullied so miserably ; she absolutely could not let them off.

Thinking of the scars on her mother and her own helplessness just now, the hatred in Karin’s heart burned like a fierce fire that could only be quenched by the blood of those villains.

The three walked out of the house, and there was surprisingly no one outside.

« Tsk, a small Village is just a small Village. Their defenses are so lax.

Fence-sitters are just fence-sitters, useless trash who only know how to act tough at home. »

Although Asakawa Shin’s movements were fast and quiet just now, the fact that so many people died in broad daylight without being noticed showed the poor combat quality of Kusagakure.

Asakawa Shin gave a disdainful sneer and, while stepping forward, slowly drew his sword.

Streaks of black lightning erupted from his body, wrapping around the sword like pythons and dyeing the blade completely pitch black.

He gave a cold laugh, his voice low and indifferent :

« Then let’s start by saying hello to them~

Asakawa Style Secret Technique : Slashing Wave ! »
