# Chapitre 201 — Have You Played Enough?

Sasori’s cold interrogation made Kakashi’s heart tighten, but before he could think further, the Fourth Raikage Human Puppet, whose Chakra Threads had been severed, suddenly self-destructed, and countless poisoned senbon poured down like a rainstorm.

He dodged in a hurry, but was still wounded by the unexpected hidden weapons. A strange sensation of numbness spread from the wound, and Kakashi felt his legs go weak as he collapsed to the ground.

« Oh no, Kakashi-sensei has been poisoned ! »

Sakura’s face was filled with anxiety as she gripped the only antidote and prepared to rush forward.

Unfortunately, Sasori had anticipated her move ; a surging wave of Iron Sand roared forth, cutting off her path.

Sasori laughed wildly towards the sky : « I noticed from the very beginning, you... are the son of the White Fang, aren’t you ? Hahahaha ! »

He suddenly turned his head and glared at Lady Chiyo : « It was his father who killed my parents, and now you’re actually helping him deal with me ? Grandma, have you truly gone senile ? »

Chiyo was momentarily speechless, and the movements of her hands couldn’t help but slow down for a moment.

« Now ! »

A murderous light gleamed in Sasori’s eyes. With a flick of his fingers, he controlled the Third Kazekage Human Puppet to activate Magnet Release.

The Iron Sand filling the sky instantly condensed into sharp spears, which pierced towards Sakura and Kakashi with a lethal whistle.

« Die, you eyesores. »

« Sakura ! » Chiyo reacted immediately, using Chakra Threads to pull Sakura backward. As for Kakashi, a flash of hesitation crossed Chiyo’s eyes, and in the end, she did not lend a hand.

Fortunately, Sasuke arrived just in time. He quickly grabbed Kakashi and leaped away, and the two of them rolled on the ground like a pair of gourds.

Although their movements were somewhat clumsy, they successfully avoided that fatal blow.

Sasori’s brow furrowed, and a surge of irritation rose in his heart.

These few individuals were simply too difficult to deal with. Clearly, none of them were a match for him alone, but together they were like an airtight net, forcing him to be repeatedly constrained.

His face was grim as his gaze swept over the four people opposite him one by one.

Lady Chiyo, who had an extremely deep understanding of puppetry, the son of the White Fang, and a Medical Ninja.

This Medical Ninja was even able to crack the deadly poison he took such pride in.

As for the remaining Uchiha, he subconsciously ignored him.

Just a small fry.

As he watched Kakashi stand up once more, a hint of a desire to retreat quietly sprouted in his heart.

Sasori cursed under his breath : « What a pain. That idiot Deidara, I wonder if he’s played enough yet ? »

On the other side of the battlefield, Deidara’s originally pleasant mood had long since vanished.

Initially, relying on his aerial advantage, he was playfully teasing the Konoha Ninja chasing him below, enjoying a game of cat and mouse.

Who would have thought that this Nine-Tails Jinchuriki would have no sense of sportsmanship at all ? After just a few taunts, he entered a berserk mode.

At first, when Naruto displayed his Four-Tails form, Deidara could still rely on his flight advantage to suppress him and output attacks calmly.

However, before long, two more tails suddenly sprouted behind Naruto.

This immediately caused Deidara to feel a mountain of pressure.

Six-Tailed Naruto’s speed soared abruptly, and those fox tails could even transform into flexible, giant palms.

Because Deidara’s initial flight altitude was limited, he was nearly struck by Naruto’s tails, which startled him into quickly climbing higher.

Failing to hit, those tails acted like giant hands, grabbing everything in the vicinity and throwing them at him like stones or spears in a frantic bombardment.

For a time, he was kept busy dodging, becoming nothing more than a mobile aerial target.

The worst was yet to come.

Naruto, unable to land a hit for a long time, was clearly enraged. He suddenly stopped, opened his huge mouth, and let out a low roar.

Countless blue-black Chakra light points were spat out by him, then frantically gathered and compressed back into his mouth, condensing into a high-density Tailed Beast Ball.

Deidara’s eyes twitched wildly as he cursed in his heart : 'Are you kidding me ? If I get hit by that thing, it’ll be game over for good.'

He hurriedly drove the Clay Bird beneath his feet, pulling up for altitude as if his life depended on it.

However, in the next instant, a bone-chilling cold suddenly surged into his heart.

He looked down sharply and saw that Tailed Beast Ball shooting towards his position with the force of a thunderbolt.

Deidara did a rough calculation of the trajectory and immediately felt that things were bad. He quickly controlled the giant bird to make a sharp turn to the side to evade.

Before he could fly very far, a world-shaking explosion suddenly erupted behind him.

The Tailed Beast Ball was actually detonated in mid-air by Naruto, exploding into a ball of flame that devoured heaven and earth. The violent shockwave swept out in all directions like a tsunami.

Deidara clung tightly to the Clay Bird. Man and bird were like a small boat in a raging tide, carried away by that world-destroying air current.

Under the tearing force of such terrifying high-pressure air currents, the body of the giant Clay Bird could not maintain balance at all. Its wing structure shattered, and finally, it plummeted towards the ground at an alarming speed with a shrill whistle.

On the ground, the Six-Tailed Naruto was extremely excited at the sight. He immediately set off on all fours, sprinting towards the spot where Deidara was falling.

With a thunderous boom, the Clay Bird smashed into the forest like a meteorite, crashing through several giant trees before hitting the ground and kicking up a cloud of dust.

After a moment, a blood-stained hand suddenly reached out from the shattered clay egg, and Deidara’s disheveled figure crawled out from the gap.

He gasped heavily, his chest heaving violently, but a fanatical smile curled at the corners of his mouth.

« Truly the ultimate art... that explosion just now was perfect ! »

Deidara muttered to himself. If he hadn’t ducked into the Clay Bird’s body in time to act as a buffer, he probably would have been smashed into meat paste by now.

However, before he could recover from the afterglow of the explosion, a roar suddenly came from the distant forest.

Towering ancient trees exploded and collapsed one after another, as if a prehistoric giant beast was charging through them.

Deidara’s pupils shrank suddenly : « Damn it, he’s here so fast ? »

He hurriedly chewed some clay and spat out a new bird, which took off into the air in a hurry.

Just after leaving the ground, Naruto’s figure had already arrived at the previous crash site.

Seeing his prey escape again, Naruto was furious. He swung his tail like a whip, and a thick tree was snapped in half.

Then, he glared fiercely at the deep pit on the ground and delivered another tail strike, sending the clay egg in the pit flying with a heavy blow.

Gaara’s figure was suddenly thrown out from the broken clay egg, rolling several times on the ground before collapsing limply by the tree roots, with no telling how many bones were broken.

Fortunately, he was a corpse now and couldn’t feel any pain at all.

Six-Tailed Naruto quickly climbed to the top of a towering ancient tree. His crimson vertical pupils locked onto the small black dot high in the sky, and blue-purple Chakra frantically gathered in his giant mouth once more.

Above the clouds, Deidara’s Adam’s apple bobbed. Looking at the Tailed Beast Ball that was condensing, he roared anxiously in his heart :

« Has that bastard Sasori-danna played enough yet ? If we don’t retreat soon, I’m going to be blown to pieces ! No, I have to go find him... »
