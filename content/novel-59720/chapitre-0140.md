# Chapitre 140 — Spoils of War

On the other side, Orochimaru and Senju Tobirama teleported to a secluded valley.

The surroundings were deathly silent, save for the soft rustle of the breeze through the treetops.

Orochimaru scanned the area, and her tense nerves finally relaxed slightly.

She turned her head, looking solemnly at Senju Tobirama beside her, and asked in a low voice, « Where is this place ? »

Senju Tobirama looked at him with interest, the corners of his lips curling slightly :

« This is the border region between the Land of Fire and the Land of Lightning.

In my earlier years, I left a Flying Thunder God Mark here ; I never expected it would come in handy today.

Speaking of which, what year is it now ? »

Senju Tobirama naturally sensed the dense Flying Thunder God Marks on Orochimaru’s body, but he had no intention of exposing it.

After all, this kid was a Konoha rogue ninja and a mortal enemy of his own descendants ; he couldn’t wait to see him suffer, so why would he help ?

Orochimaru shot a suspicious glance at Senju Tobirama, thinking warily : 'This old fellow is actually being so cooperative ?'

She lowered her voice and said solemnly, « It is now the 64th year of Konoha. By my count, you have been dead for over forty years, haven’t you ? »

Senju Tobirama was slightly stunned upon hearing this, a sense of daze washing over him : 'Over forty years... so much time has passed.'

At that moment, Orochimaru’s body suddenly stiffened, freezing for a moment as if struck by an electric shock.

She murmured to herself : « Was the First Hokage sealed ? »

This faint whisper naturally did not escape Senju Tobirama’s ears.

He felt a surge of joy in his heart, 'Is Big Brother finally free ? It seems I will be soon as well.'

As it turned out, his guess was spot on.

As Orochimaru was calculating his next move, a figure abruptly appeared behind him—it was Asakawa Shin, who had arrived using the Flying Thunder God Technique.

« Senior brother, where are you going ? You haven’t given me the meeting gift you promised ; you can’t go back on your word. »

Hearing that familiar voice, Orochimaru’s whole body stiffened as he slowly turned around.

As expected, Asakawa Shin was standing behind him, looking at him with a half-smile.

« Flying Thunder God Technique ? You actually left a mark on me ? » Orochimaru asked in a heavy voice.

Asakawa Shin shrugged and said lightheartedly, « Correct, but unfortunately there’s no prize. Senior brother, you’re trapped now ; it’s better to just surrender quietly. »

A bitter smile curved at the corner of Orochimaru’s mouth as he grumbled inwardly :

'Did this kid start cultivating in his mother’s womb ? Not only are his Taijutsu and Wood Release attainments astonishing, but he even handles the Flying Thunder God Technique with ease.

He’s simply a monster. Fine, losing to a fellow like this is something I can accept.'

Having reached this conclusion, he actually turned his head and gave Asakawa Shin a bright smile :

« Shin, what meeting gift did you say you wanted just now ? I will certainly grant it without exception.

After all, we are fellow disciples. »

Asakawa Shin couldn’t help but pat his shoulder, a look of approval in his eyes :

« As expected of you, Senior brother. They say a wise man submits to circumstances ; it seems you understand this well.

In that case, I’ll be blunt.

There are a few things I want : your other kusanagi sword, and the Summoning Scrolls for Ryūchi Cave and Rashomon.

Besides those, I want you yourself, Senior brother. »

Seeing Orochimaru instinctively take a step back, Asakawa Shin spat and then chuckled :

« Senior brother, you misunderstand. What I value is your talent for scientific research. As long as you are willing to work for me, I will let you go.

Remember the promise I made when we met ? It’s still valid. »

Orochimaru gritted his teeth, weighed his options repeatedly, and finally nodded.

At this point, he had no way out ; even if he could be resurrected via the Cursed Seal of Heaven after death, it would be meaningless.

'It seems I can only compromise for now. I’ll wait until I use Living Corpse Reincarnation to get rid of the Flying Thunder God Mark before making further plans...'

Senju Tobirama couldn’t help but roll his eyes from the side.

Has Konoha really fallen to this state ? What on earth has Monkey been up to these years, that his own personal disciple has become a rogue ninja ?

When he saw that fellow, he would definitely have to interrogate him thoroughly.

Giving a light cough, he turned to Asakawa Shin and asked, « Um... can I go with you to see Konoha ? »

« Of course, no problem, but you’ll need a bit of a disguise. »

'Back to Konoha ?' Orochimaru unconsciously swallowed. With his Soft Body Modification and Orochimaru-style Body Replacement Technique, he probably wouldn’t be beaten to death by Tsunade, right ?

Subsequently, Asakawa Shin raised his hand and released the Edo Tensei on Senju Tobirama.

Under Asakawa Shin’s burning gaze, Orochimaru reluctantly pulled out the items the other had requested.

Astoundingly, this fellow had hidden all these things inside his stomach.

He suddenly opened his huge mouth and first spat out two scrolls in one breath, followed by a live snake from his mouth ; hidden within that snake’s belly was a kusanagi sword.

Asakawa Shin stared at the three items before him and fell into deep thought.

These were indeed the treasures he dreamed of, but after Orochimaru’s antics, they were covered in a slimy liquid, which was truly unpleasant.

For a moment, he was stuck in an awkward position where he didn’t want to take them but didn’t want to leave them.

After hesitating for a moment, he eventually reached out and took them, then decisively used Water Release to give them a thorough cleaning.

A flash of doubt crossed Orochimaru’s eyes, and he couldn’t help but ask :

« Junior brother, with your strength, these items are likely just the icing on the cake and offer no substantial help to you.

What exactly is your intention in going to such lengths to collect them ? »

Asakawa Shin gave him a flat look and said indifferently, « As for their use, you’ll find out later. »

With that, he casually picked up the cleaned kusanagi sword and skillfully carved Flying Thunder God Marks onto the blade and scabbard respectively.

When Senju Tobirama and Orochimaru clearly saw the fleeting patterns on those marks, they couldn’t help but be greatly shocked.

« Above and below heaven, I alone am the honored one ? What an overbearing declaration ! »

A sharp light exploded in Senju Tobirama’s eyes.

So that was it ; did this kid actually possess such an overbearing spirit that looked down upon the world ? As expected of a descendant of my Senju Clan.

If Big Brother could have had such dominance back then, the Ninja World would likely have been a completely different scene by now, wouldn’t it ?

Asakawa Shin fondled the kusanagi sword lovingly.

The sword was two feet seven inches long with a silver-white blade ; it was a straight-edged single-handed sword without a crossguard, its lines sharp and clean, which deeply appealed to him.

He immediately decided to use this sword as his daily sidearm.

It was actually funny to think about ; he was originally a Great Swordsman, yet he had never carried a longsword.

Now, with the kusanagi sword tucked at his waist, he felt his entire temperament gain a touch of ethereal grace.

Asakawa Shin couldn’t help but cast an appreciative glance, « Not bad, Senior brother, you have good taste. This sword feels as if it were tailor-made for me. By the way, Senior brother, what exactly did you come to Konoha for this time ? »

Hearing this question, Orochimaru’s expression became slightly awkward.

After a moment of hesitation, he finally told the « truth » : « My goal for this trip was mainly for Uchiha Sasuke.

I am very interested in the Sharingan, so I wanted to take Sasuke as my disciple. »

Asakawa Shin’s brow furrowed as he asked in confusion, « Why Sasuke ? »

Orochimaru scratched his head awkwardly and said sheepishly, « Because he is the earliest genius in the history of the Uchiha Clan to awaken his eyes, and he is also Fugaku’s son.

And I... was once easily defeated by Fugaku’s Mangekyo. »

Asakawa Shin’s breath hitched.

« Fugaku’s Mangekyo ? Are you saying... Danzo ? Are you still in contact with him ? Where is he now ? I have urgent business with him. »
