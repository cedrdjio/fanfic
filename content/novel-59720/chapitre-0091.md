# Chapitre 91 — Eight-Tails

'As expected of the Eight-Tails, he really has some strength ! This terrifying amount of Chakra is almost half of mine !'

Asakawa Shin looked at the oppressive Eight-Tails before him and couldn’t help but feel emotional ; it was indeed the strongest Tailed Beast below the Nine-Tails.

Even with his strength, facing the Eight-Tails felt a bit tricky when he couldn’t use the abilities that would expose his identity.

...

« You fool, you idiot ! »

« This lord’s eighth blade, »

« Hasn’t even left its sheath, yet I’ve already lost the trade ! »

« The enemy’s power is a frightening sight ! »

« A close call, but I’m alright ! »

« Going all out now, look forward to the fight ! »

« Yo~ Yo~ »

Inside the mental world, Killer Bee was patting his chest, still shaken, drenched in cold sweat.

If he hadn’t desperately twisted his body to avoid that sword strike just now, he would have been sliced in two, with no time for Gyuki to save him.

Gyuki glared at him with dissatisfaction and said in a heavy voice, « I warned you long ago to stay on high alert. »

« This guy is definitely no ordinary person. If we aren’t careful, we might be the ones in danger this time ! »

« Stop playing around, Bee ! We have to go all out ! »

« I got it, brother ! »

On the battlefield outside, the Eight-Tails' expression suddenly turned fierce, killing intent exposed in his pupils as he locked onto Asakawa Shin’s figure.

He suddenly lowered his body, his limbs pushing off the ground, using his bull horns like a spear to launch a devastating charge.

« Watch me crush you, little bug ! »

« Haha ! Good timing ! » Seeing this, Asakawa Shin was delighted rather than surprised. His figure suddenly surged forward, choosing to meet the Eight-Tails' attack head-on with his own charge.

In an instant, the two collided violently on the water’s surface. Amidst a thunderous roar, Asakawa Shin actually used both hands to firmly block the Eight-Tails' horns, preventing him from advancing even an inch.

« How is this possible ? He actually blocked my charge ? »

The Eight-Tails was shocked. Looking at the tiny figure before him, his eyes were full of unbelievable horror.

However, a surge of ferocity instantly rushed to his heart. He exploded with all his brute strength to press down on Asakawa Shin, vowing to win this contest of power and completely impale his opponent with his horns.

« Oh ? You brute want to wrestle with me ? »

Feeling the overwhelming force, Asakawa Shin was not afraid but instead laughed out loud with excitement.

He also suddenly exerted force, pushing back, actually forcing the Eight-Tails' massive body to retreat continuously.

« How is this possible ? I’m actually at a disadvantage in terms of strength ? »

The Eight-Tails was terrified. In his panic, he failed to notice the reef on the water’s surface. He tripped while retreating, and his body lost its balance at this critical moment.

« Oh... Oh no ! »

Asakawa Shin’s eyes lit up, and he decisively seized the opportunity. The veins on his arms bulged as he firmly grabbed the giant horns on the Eight-Tails' head. He exploded with incredible divine strength, lifting the massive creature and swinging it around in the air.

The Eight-Tails' massive body spun five or six times before being thrown far away by Asakawa Shin.

This wasn’t over. His figure leaped, following the Eight-Tails' body into the air. He accurately grabbed the giant bull horns again mid-air, using the momentum of the fall to exert force, flipping the Eight-Tails over and slamming its head into a mountain peak ahead like a meteorite.

Amidst the loud crash, the mountain peak collapsed under the weight, kicking up dust that filled the sky and obscured the sun.

« Waaaaaah ! »

The Eight-Tails let out a roar of pain. With the help of his tentacles, he struggled to flip over and get back up to continue the fight. However, the intense dizziness made it impossible. With a stumble, his heavy body fell to the ground again.

Asakawa Shin leisurely clapped his hands. He didn’t take the opportunity to press the attack but instead seemed to have discovered something. Frowning, he stared fixedly at the Eight-Tails' horns, and for a moment, the scene became eerily quiet.

After a long while, Asakawa Shin finally couldn’t help but ask, « Hey ! Eight-Tails, is there no way for that broken horn of yours to grow back ? »

« Do you know that you look very asymmetrical right now ? It’s very uncomfortable to look at ! »

« What ? » The Eight-Tails thought he was hallucinating. He shook his head violently, trying to dissipate the dizziness.

This move seemed to work a bit. He finally stood up shakily, staring at Asakawa Shin with a suspicious and uncertain gaze.

« I said, can’t that broken horn of yours grow back ? I remember octopuses have regenerative abilities, don’t they ? » Asakawa Shin repeated, his tone carrying a hint of inquiry.

The Eight-Tails finally heard clearly this time. He retorted crossly, « You’re talking about an octopus’s tentacles, but what I broke was a bull horn. It can’t regenerate ! »

« I see. »

Asakawa Shin nodded in disappointment, then changed the subject, a spark of wisdom flashing in his eyes. « It’s fine. It doesn’t matter if it can’t grow back ; isn’t there another way ? »

Before the Eight-Tails could grasp Asakawa Shin’s words, he saw the other party charging at him like an arrow from a bow.

He cursed inwardly and decisively engaged. Eight massive octopus tentacles instantly turned into whips, lashing out at Asakawa Shin like lightning.

Unfortunately, completely predicted by Observation Haki, Asakawa Shin’s figure was as agile as the wind, moving through the gaps in the Eight-Tails' attacks.

The Eight-Tails' offensive didn’t slow down. A giant fist carrying the force of wind and thunder smashed down. However, Asakawa Shin easily avoided it with a slight sway of his body. Then, seizing the opportunity, he climbed onto the opponent’s thick arm and rushed toward the giant bull head.

« You bastard brat ! » The Eight-Tails was furious. His giant head smashed down, delivering a fierce headbutt toward Asakawa Shin.

Asakawa Shin, like a nimble swallow, leaped lightly off the Eight-Tails' arm. At the same time, his charging fist, accompanied by the sound of whistling wind, struck the Eight-Tails' cheek, sending the massive creature flying backward.

« Now is the time ! »

Asakawa Shin’s eyes lit up. He turned into an afterimage and advanced at extreme speed. While the Eight-Tails hadn’t yet gotten up, he delivered a swift hand-sword strike, hacking down fiercely at the intact bull horn.

Under the Eight-Tails' horrified gaze, his hard bull horn was cut as easily as tofu, spinning as it flew away.

Having successfully completed his objective, Asakawa Shin took a few steps back to create distance and began to inspect his masterpiece.

« Not bad, Eight-Tails. The surgery was a success. You look much better now—perfectly symmetrical. »

The Eight-Tails turned a deaf ear to Asakawa Shin’s words. He used a trembling tentacle to gently pick up the broken horn, looking as if he wanted to piece it back together.

However, things didn’t go as planned. With a « clatter, » the broken horn slipped heartlessly to the ground again.

At this moment, the Eight-Tails finally accepted reality. His massive body trembled violently with rage. He snapped his gaze toward Asakawa Shin and let out a heaven-shaking roar at him.

« You bastard ! You actually cut off my handsome bull horn ! »

« I will never forgive you ! Die ! »

« Tailed Beast Ball ! »

He suddenly opened his massive mouth. The Chakra inside his body surged rapidly, mixing and compressing Yin and Yang in a two-to-eight ratio. In an instant, it condensed into a pitch-black sphere, carrying a destructive aura as it shot toward Asakawa Shin. Before it even arrived, the intense gale had already swept over.

« Such strong power ! But no matter how powerful it is, it’s useless if it doesn’t hit ! »

Asakawa Shin’s eyes narrowed. He didn’t dare to be careless. He stomped his foot, his figure moving like lightning, avoiding the trajectory of the Tailed Beast Ball in an instant.

The next moment, the Tailed Beast Ball smashed heavily into the ground and exploded.

The violent shockwave instantly swept across the field, carrying dust into the sky and obscuring the vision.

« Did it hit ? »

« Of course not ! » As soon as the words fell, a figure tore through the atmosphere, approaching the Eight-Tails at high speed. The gale created by his movement was like a sharp blade, splitting the ground and water in two.

The Eight-Tails' pupils shrank, and he shouted in the mental world, « This bastard is so fast ! It’s no good, Bee ! We have to use that move ! »

Killer Bee responded with a serious expression, « Then let him see ! The power of our ultimate move ! »

« Tailed Beast Eight-Wrap ! ! ! »

The Eight-Tails suddenly retracted his tentacles, wrapping his massive body into a giant sphere, and then began to spin wildly.

In an instant, a world-destroying hurricane erupted from his rotation, sweeping in all directions.

Accompanied by a deafening roar, within a radius of several kilometers, whether it was mountain peaks or vegetation, everything was instantly crushed into powder by the violent wind pressure, involuntarily drawn into the vortex and dancing wildly with the storm.

Facing such an incredible attack from the Eight-Tails, even Asakawa Shin couldn’t help but be a bit stunned. « Although it looks a bit like a giant Rotation, the power of the technique is completely incomparable. This destructive power has definitely reached the Beyond-Kage level ! »

He said to the little Katsuyu inside the Wood Transformation armor, « Katsuyu, I’ll have to trouble you to help me gather Natural Energy next. Facing this kind of attack, I have to go all out too ! »

Katsuyu nodded obediently and replied, « No problem ! Lord Shin, leave the endurance to me ! »

Asakawa Shin neatly clapped his hands together and instantly entered Sage Mode !

« Katsuyu, let’s go too ! »
