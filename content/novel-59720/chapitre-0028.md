# Chapitre 28 — Musi Group

Hokage Building, conference room.

Hiruzen Sarutobi, Asakawa Shin, the heads of the major Ninja clans, and the department heads of Konoha gathered here to hold the financing conference for the Musi Group.

Asakawa Shin transformed into a PowerPoint presenter, introducing silk as an epoch-making product, outlining its bright future prospects, and once again bringing out that palm-sized piece of silk for everyone to inspect.

In truth, he had already reached out to his preferred partners beforehand, so this was merely a formality. As for those families or individuals he wasn’t familiar with, if you had the foresight, you could join in ; if you lacked the vision, then don’t blame him for leaving you out.

« That is all. » Asakawa Shin cleared his throat. Having spoken for so long, he felt a bit parched. Beside him, Shikaku quickly handed him a glass of water, causing the corner of Hiruzen Sarutobi’s eye to twitch.

The others also wore strange expressions, except for Hiashi, whose face was full of regret, as if to say, 'Damn it, he beat me to it.'

After drinking the water, Asakawa Shin felt much better and said to everyone, « I have finished my introduction. How is everyone’s consideration going ? If there are no issues, please make your offers. We need to raise funds and build the factory as soon as possible. The sooner we finish, the sooner we can start production. »

At this point, the group began to discuss again. The Ninja clans who were not very familiar with Asakawa Shin were especially busy making inquiries. Although they had been fired up by the presentation, they still needed to be cautious when it came to actually putting up real money.

Seeing that no one had made an offer for a while, Hiashi stood up to take the lead, saying, « The Hyuga Clan is very optimistic about the prospects of silk, so we have decided to invest two hundred million ryo. »

This statement shocked the small and medium-sized families. Two hundred million ryo was not a small sum. According to official records, Kakashi Hatake could be considered the number one among young Ninjas and a model of mission completion.

Up until the Chunin Exams in the original story, the total payment for all his completed missions, calculated at the minimum wage standard, was one hundred and twenty million ryo. It seemed like a lot, but this was before deducting the shares given to teammates. Furthermore, Ninjas themselves had high expenses ; basic living costs aside, the consumption of Ninja tools was an astronomical figure, making it very difficult to save money.

« As expected of the prestigious Hyuga Clan of Konoha, what great courage. » Asakawa Shin could not help but praise.

Hiashi responded modestly, « It is only because the prospects for this project are good that I am investing. In the future, I will have to trouble you to put in the effort, my nephew. »

With the Hyuga Clan taking the lead, other families also responded one after another.

« The Nara Clan will invest one hundred million ryo. »

« The Yamanaka Clan will also invest one hundred million ryo. »

« The Akimichi Clan will also invest one hundred million ryo. »

Although the Ino–Shika–Chō trio were not as wealthy as the Hyuga Clan, they still had considerable assets. The Nara Clan raised deer and cultivated medicinal herbs for sale ; the Yamanaka Clan grew flowers, and all the flower shops in Konoha belonged to them ; the Akimichi Clan ran barbecue restaurants that brought in money every day.

After hearing their offers, Hiruzen Sarutobi sighed inwardly. They were all old foxes. It seemed unrealistic to want to occupy the majority share. Oh well.

« I, on behalf of the Sarutobi Clan, will invest three hundred million ryo ! »

Hiruzen Sarutobi’s words caused another sensation. Asakawa Shin was blunt, saying, « Teacher, you’re quite wealthy. What ? Did you take out your coffin money ? »

Hiruzen Sarutobi glared at him crossly, « It’s more than just coffin money. Last night, I sold a few of the clan’s shops at a low price. They didn’t make much money a year anyway, so it’s better to invest in my good student. »

Asakawa Shin could not help but look at him with admiration. Going all-in was a form of wisdom ; it seemed his teacher was well-versed in it.

« The Hatake Clan will invest fifty million ryo. » Kakashi Hatake understood the inside story, so he was very optimistic about this project. This time, he really had taken out his marriage savings, which included money left behind by his father, Sakumo Hatake. But it didn’t matter ; he wasn’t going to take a wife anyway.

Oh no, Kakashi had really become a rich man. Asakawa Shin still remembered how Kakashi in the original story would feel heartbroken over paying for a barbecue meal. Now, he had turned his life around.

Also, what the heck is the Hatake Clan ? Isn’t the Hatake Clan just you alone ?

In the crowd, Fugaku was a bit hesitant. Originally, he had planned to invest a few tens of millions just to show support. After all, the Uchiha Clan cooperated with the Ninja Cats to sell Ninja tools, and their business spread throughout the entire Ninja World, so they were not short of money and didn’t care much about this new project.

But now it seemed this silk business was really not simple. Should he add more funds ?

While he was still considering, Shisui spoke up, « I, on behalf of our Security Department, will invest one hundred million ryo. »

'Can’t wait any longer.' Fugaku cursed inwardly and quickly spoke up, « I, on behalf of the Uchiha Clan, will invest one hundred million ryo. »

This Shisui actually spoke before him, the clan head. He was really showing less and less respect for him.

« The Kurama Clan will invest ten million ryo. »

« The Shimura Clan will invest thirty million ryo. »

« The Utatane Clan will invest thirty million ryo. »

« The Homura Mitokado Clan will invest thirty million ryo. »

« I, on behalf of our Medical Department, will invest fifty million ryo. » The newly appointed head of the Medical Department, Yuko Aoi, although not as powerful as Shisui and lacking his strong influence, had always had good foresight and a keen sense of smell. This time, she decisively seized the opportunity and invested all her assets—a total all-in.

Asakawa Shin looked at her deeply. This woman had good investment vision ; being a head of the Medical Department seemed a bit of a waste of her talent. According to his plan, the Konoha Development and Reform Commission was about to be established. Should he... observe her a bit more ?

...

« Thank you very much for your trust. This fundraising concludes here. We will distribute shares to everyone according to the ratio of the funds raised. After signing, everyone present will be a shareholder of the Musi Group. Congratulations. »

« This time, 30% of the Musi Group’s shares raised a total of one billion two hundred million ryo. The shares are distributed according to the ratio as follows :

Sarutobi Clan holds 7.5%, Hyuga Clan holds 5%

Nara Clan holds 2.5%, Yamanaka Clan holds 2.5%

Akimichi Clan holds 2.5%, Security Department holds 2.5%

Uchiha Clan holds 2.5%, Hatake Clan holds 1.25%

Medical Department holds 1.25%, Kurama Clan holds 0.25%

Shimura Clan holds 0.75%, Utatane Clan holds 0.75%

Homura Mitokado Clan holds 0.75%

That is all. Congratulations to you all. »

After clapping, Asakawa Shin took out a stack of documents and distributed them to everyone. This was the development plan for the next phase.

« Next is how to spend this money. First, we need to purchase land in the suburbs of the village to plant mulberry trees. The Musi Group is a major enterprise supported by Konoha, and the village will provide significant policy support, so the land price will not be too expensive. Secondly, we need to purchase a large number of mature mulberry trees, seedlings, and mulberry leaves. I have channels for this and can guarantee quality and low prices, so everyone need not worry. »

« Furthermore, we need to build silkworm rooms and silk reeling factories on the Aburame Clan’s land, purchase land by the river in the suburbs of Konoha to build textile factories, and construct large water-powered spinning wheels next to the river. This will take some time, so I suggest we first acquire a traditional textile factory to proceed simultaneously. We also need to acquire a dye house. »

« We will also recruit textile and dyeing talent from all over the Ninja World at high prices and encourage employee innovation. If anyone has good suggestions or improvement measures, high bonuses will be awarded once they are adopted. »

« At the same time, we will purchase shops in the capitals of the Five Great Nations and the bustling areas of major cities in the Land of Fire to sell our silk. If there are good shops, just take them ; don’t worry about them not being used. In the future, we will also acquire clothing factories to create Musi’s own high-end clothing brand, but there is no rush for that. Let’s focus on spreading the silk business first. »

« That is all. »
