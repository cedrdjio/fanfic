# Chapitre 77 — Nine-Tailed

« Hmm ? What are you saying ? Didn’t you hear me talking to you ? »

Hanako was furious. The jade hand that was tugging at Asakawa Shin’s ear began to stir again, as if about to launch a further attack.

« I’m wrong, I’m wrong, Mom, I’m wrong ! I was just saying that the food you cooked smells so good. I smelled it from far away, and it just so happens I haven’t eaten yet. »

Asakawa Shin knew well that a wise man submits to circumstances, so he immediately executed his smooth combo of begging for mercy and acting cute, which earned him an eye-roll from Hanako, who then messed up his hair a bit more before letting him go, seemingly still unsatisfied.

Naruto had already set an extra bowl and chopsticks and greeted Asakawa Shin enthusiastically : « Shin, sit down, don’t be polite. »

Asakawa Shin’s hand paused slightly as he pulled out the chair, and he gave Naruto a bright smile : « Thanks a lot, Naruto. Why would I be polite ? This is my house, haha. »

Hanako was very displeased when she heard this. She tapped Asakawa Shin on his little head again and began her 'spell' :

« You still know this is your house ? Then why haven’t you come back once in over half a year ? Do you know how much I missed you and worried about you... »

Asakawa Shin was numb from listening to her. He couldn’t even lift his head, and could only curse inwardly :

'It’s all the Shadow Clone’s fault. He got obsessed with reading and didn’t even come home. Wait... could he have done it on purpose ? It’s very possible.'

Asakawa Shin searched his memory ; the Shadow Clone had actually passed by their home three times without entering...

Each time, he just watched from afar and deliberately emptied his mind, leaving behind almost no memories, which meant he hadn’t noticed it at the time.

Damn it, to go to such lengths just to plot against me, he’s treating me like Danzo.

By the time the meal was finished, Asakawa Shin was already sweating profusely, but fortunately, Hanako’s 'tight-fillet spell' had finally ended.

Naruto skillfully finished washing the dishes and was about to leave after saying goodbye, but was hurriedly stopped by Asakawa Shin :

« Naruto, don’t be in such a rush to leave. We haven’t seen each other in so long. Why don’t you stay and sleep in my room tonight ? I’ve really missed you. »

Naruto scratched his head embarrassedly : « Wouldn’t that be too much of a bother for you ? »

« What bother ? Do we need to be so formal with each other ? »

After saying this, Asakawa Shin directly took Naruto’s small hand and walked towards his room, looking somewhat impatient.

Hanako’s eyes curved into crescents as she smiled, and she gestured towards Kenji with her chin : « Look how good their relationship is ; they’re just like brothers. »

Kenji also nodded with gratification. They are both good children.

Asakawa Shin led Naruto into the bedroom, decisively locked the door, pushed him without hesitation, pinned Naruto against the wall, and began to uncontrollably sniff his neck :

'Damn, it smells so good. This is too fragrant, it’s almost making me dizzy.'

« Hahaha, Shin, stop messing around. You’re making me so ticklish, hahaha. »

After a long while, Naruto couldn’t hold it in anymore. Unable to take it, he quickly pushed Asakawa Shin away, finally getting a chance to catch his breath and gulping down fresh air.

Away from Naruto’s heart-throbbing scent, Asakawa Shin finally regained some clarity. His reason slowly returned, and his sharp intelligence occupied the high ground once again.

« Sorry, Naruto, I lost my composure a bit. It’s just been too long, and I couldn’t hold back my emotions for a moment. But you were laughing way too exaggeratedly, right ? Isn’t it normal for brothers to hug ? »

He scratched his head and smiled awkwardly. But before the words had even faded, his smile suddenly froze, and he stared at Naruto in surprise : « Wait, why are you blushing like a teapot ? Surely it’s not that bad ? »

Naruto was a bit embarrassed and angry, accusing Asakawa Shin : « It’s all because of you, keeping your hair so long, it made me so ticklish. I... I couldn’t even breathe from laughing, I couldn’t help it. »

« Oh, right. After a year of seclusion, my hair has grown long. »

Asakawa Shin hurriedly ran to the bedroom mirror and examined himself carefully.

Compared to a year ago, he had grown significantly taller. After all, his physique had improved, which was within his expectations.

The baby fat on his face had quietly faded, his contours were more defined, and while he had lost a bit of childish cuteness, he had gained a touch of refinement.

With his long hair, his features grew increasingly similar to Hanako’s. Even someone who didn’t know them could tell at a glance that they were mother and son.

« What do you mean ? This looks pretty good. I thought I would look ugly after growing my hair out. Alright, enough of that. Naruto, let’s clean up and get to sleep ; I have urgent business. »

...

On the bed, Asakawa Shin hugged Naruto tightly like an octopus.

At this moment, for him, Naruto was like catnip to a cat, making him indulge in it, completely intoxicated.

After pretending to sleep for several hours, he finally confirmed that Naruto had entered the dreamland, and he couldn’t help but exclaim inwardly :

'As expected of the Uzumaki Clan, truly full of energy. It’s already the middle of the night and he’s only just fallen asleep. Well, now it’s my turn to take the stage. The plan begins.'

Asakawa Shin’s small hand slowly moved to Naruto’s lower abdomen, feeling the Eight Trigrams Seal existing there. He had long since understood the principles of this Fuinjutsu, and with a thought, he injected his Chakra into it.

Asakawa Shin closed his eyes, and when he opened them again, he had arrived in front of a massive water prison.

He smelled the heavy fragrance permeating the air, and a smile couldn’t help but curl the corners of his mouth. He looked at the bright, large fox in the water prison with great interest :

'It really didn’t take any effort at all. You really are the source of the fragrance, Nine-Tailed.'

In the depths of the dark water prison, the sleeping giant beast suddenly opened its eyes, a flash of terror passing through its scarlet slit pupils :

'This disgusting malice... There’s no mistake, it’s that monster brat ! How did he suddenly show up at my doorstep ?'

The Nine-Tailed’s fur stood on end, as if facing a great enemy. It instantly entered a combat stance, its eyes locked on the intruder, looking extremely wary.

'Hmm ? This is... a feeling of oppression I’ve never felt before, just like facing a natural predator. Who exactly is this brat ?'

Seeing this, Asakawa Shin was not nervous at all. Instead, the corners of his mouth lifted slightly, revealing a gentle smile. He pressed his hands down lightly in the air, a movement meant to be soothing and friendly :

« Nine-Tailed, don’t be so nervous. I don’t have any malice... »

Before he could finish, he was rudely interrupted by a roar from the Nine-Tailed : « Shut up ! You stinking brat, stop putting on an act ! I can sense malice, you can’t fool me ! Your malice is terrifyingly deep ; what exactly do you want ? »

'Uh, I just wanted to get a bite to eat, why the huge reaction ? Seriously...'

Asakawa Shin stood there somewhat stiffly, having forgotten his lines for a moment. Forget it, let’s start over...

He reorganized his thoughts, the corners of his mouth revealed a gentle smile, and he pressed his hands down lightly in the air, a movement meant to be soothing and friendly :

« Nine-Tailed, don’t be so nervous. I don’t have any malice... »

« ? »
