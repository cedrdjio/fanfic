# Chapitre 210 — Accusation

« I see... »

Asakawa Shin sighed, « Such deep brotherly love. »

Just as Yugito thought he was softening, he leisurely added, « It seems I should find a chance to reunite the brothers. Separated by life and death—how pitiful is that ? »

Yugito was left speechless by his words, turning her head away in anger and refusing to acknowledge him further.

The Eight-Tails' charge finally reached them.

However, from Yugito’s perspective, the massive Eight-Tails now seemed so small, only reaching the waist of the Wood Golem.

The charge that would seem unstoppable to an ordinary person was as powerless as a child’s play before the Wood Golem.

And that was indeed the case.

The Wood Golem slowly reached out a massive black palm, meeting the violent collision with a single arm.

The Eight-Tails' horns slammed heavily into the palm, causing the Golem’s movement to pause only slightly before the charge was forcibly halted by an unmatched, colossal force.

« How is this possible ? » the Eight-Tails exclaimed in a mix of shock and fury.

Asakawa Shin rolled his eyes and sneered, « You really are a stupid cow. Did you forget that your horns lost their tips long ago ? How are you supposed to gore anyone with those ? »

« Who says you can’t kill someone without the tips ? »

The Eight-Tails roared, exerting a sudden burst of strength to break free from the giant palm’s restraint. It then opened its massive maw, locking onto Asakawa Shin, who was right in front of it.

« Continuous Tailed Beast Balls ! »

With a low shout, several miniature Tailed Beast Balls that had already condensed within its body erupted like a barrage from a machine gun, striking directly at Asakawa Shin’s main body.

« Watch out ! » Yugito cried out in alarm.

Asakawa Shin simply shook his head slightly. « Wood Release : Hotei Technique ! »

With a mere thought, the Wood Golem Arhat sprouted branches and buds all over. Countless thick vines intertwined and coiled together, transforming into giant wooden hands that met the incoming Tailed Beast Balls.

Yugito instinctively closed her eyes, waiting for the impact.

However, while the expected explosions occurred as anticipated, they detonated far outside the Village.

She thought for a moment, and a look of realization flashed in her eyes. « Was that the Flying Thunder God Technique ? »

Asakawa Shin nodded slightly, his voice carrying a hint of smugness. « Correct, it was the Flying Thunder God : Guiding Thunder. My Flying Thunder God Marks are all over the Wood Golem. Things like Tailed Beast Balls pose no threat to me. »

Nearby, the Eight-Tails and Killer Bee looked grim, as if they had swallowed flies.

Their proudest killing move had been countered so easily—how were they supposed to fight this ?

Before they could devise a counter-strategy, Asakawa Shin grew impatient and controlled the massive Wood Golem to charge forward fiercely.

With the vast difference in their sizes and the Wood Golem’s body being covered in indestructible Armament Haki Armor, the outcome was already decided.

The battle was simply like an adult disciplining a child, or like Thanos pummeling the Hulk.

With just a few punches, kicks, and throws, the once-overbearing Eight-Tails was beaten until it had no strength left to fight back. It crashed heavily to the ground and reverted to Killer Bee’s form.

Asakawa Shin turned his head toward Yugito and asked casually, « It’s settled. How do you plan to deal with these people ? »

Yugito was slightly startled and lowered her head to look around.

Even though the Wood Golem’s movements had been quite restrained, every punch it threw had still brought devastating destruction to the Hidden Cloud Village.

Countless buildings had been reduced to rubble, and the earth was crisscrossed with ravines, violently splitting the once-prosperous Village apart.

As for the Ninjas ? Having learned their lesson from last time, they had scattered like startled birds the moment the Wood Golem appeared.

Aside from a few unlucky souls caught in the aftershocks, there weren’t any truly heavy casualties.

She remained silent for a moment.

Just a few days ago, this was the home she had sworn to protect with her life.

But now, looking at the miserable state of these people, she felt no pity in her heart. Instead, a strange sense of excitement welled up within her.

'I suppose I really am sick...' she mocked herself in her heart.

As a Jinchuriki, she had suffered the cold stares and bullying of the villagers since childhood.

It wasn’t until she mastered the power of the Tailed Beast that her situation gradually improved.

People began to nod to her in greeting, then respectfully addressed her as 'Lady Yugito,' and at one point even nominated her for the position of Raikage.

She had naively thought that she had finally won their recognition.

It wasn’t until today that the illusion was completely shattered.

She silently turned her head, her gaze falling upon Asakawa Shin beside her. She instinctively huddled closer into his arms, drawing from that rare warmth and an indescribable sense of security.

'Next, I’ll be going to Konoha to live with him, right ? I’m actually starting to look forward to it.'

She thought to herself : 'A Village that can nurture so many geniuses... surely it won’t have that damned bullying, right ?'

Asakawa Shin was still waiting for her answer. Seeing her lost in thought, he gently shook the person in his arms.

Yugito let out a dissatisfied whimper before speaking lazily, « Forget it, let’s just leave it at that. Shall we go home ? »

« ...Alright. »

In the next moment, the two of them vanished into afterimages, leaving only a colossal Wood Golem standing silently between heaven and earth.

...

The Hokage Office.

Asakawa Shin appeared there with a very smug smile, prepared to show off to his Senior Sister Tsunade.

He gently shook the figure in his arms, who had her head buried and refused to look up, and spoke in an excited tone : « Sister, I’ve returned with a full harvest this time. Look who I brought back ? »

*Bang !*

Tsunade slammed her palm onto the desk and stood up abruptly.

Her eyes were wide as she glared intensely at Asakawa Shin, the corner of her right eye twitching uncontrollably.

« You bastard ! »

Tsunade roared, her voice laced with unbelievable fury.

« You went out for one trip and kidnapped the Kazekage’s sister ? Do you know how many protest letters I’ve received from the Hidden Sand Village ? Dozens ! All of them accusing you of this atrocity ! And as if that weren’t enough, you actually dared to bring her straight back here ? ! »

Asakawa Shin was startled and quickly waved his hands to explain, « Sister, you’ve misunderstood ! This isn’t Temari ! Gaara is still lying in a hospital bed ; how could I bring Temari home so soon ? Maybe in a while. »

Tsunade’s movements stiffened slightly, but the fire in her eyes grew even more intense.

She took a few steps around the desk and walked straight up to Asakawa Shin, her gaze piercing like a blade toward the 'Vixen' hidden in his arms.

However, it was as if Yugito had eyes in the back of her head ; whichever way Tsunade moved, she turned her head the other way, persistently refusing to show her face.

This evasive posture only made the pressure around Tsunade drop further, making the air feel as if it were about to solidify.

Cold sweat broke out on Asakawa Shin’s forehead as he keenly sensed that his sister’s aura was becoming increasingly dangerous.

He quickly reached out and carefully gripped Yugito’s shoulders, then, like pulling a radish, he forcibly 'popped' her out of his embrace.

He then gave her a gentle push, positioning her directly in front of Tsunade.

« ... »

There was a brief, dead silence.

Tsunade’s pupils shrank, and she pointed at the Blonde cat-girl in front of her, her fingertips trembling. « You... you... you’re the Fifth Raikage ? ! »

Yugito forced an awkward smile and raised her right hand to give a stiff wave as a greeting.

« Hello, Sister Tsunade. Um... please look after me from now on. »

« Pfft ! »

Tsunade felt a surge of agitation in her chest and laughed out of sheer anger, her chest seemingly swelling even further from the rage.

« Are... are you kidding me ? ! »
