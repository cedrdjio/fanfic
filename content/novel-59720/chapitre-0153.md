# Chapitre 153 — White Snake Sage

« Hehe, we have a distinguished guest today. I apologize for not welcoming you properly. »

The White Snake Sage opened its eyes slightly, forcing a smile onto its wrinkled face.

Asakawa Shin was somewhat surprised, « Do you recognize me ? »

The White Snake Sage nodded as if it were a matter of course.

« I have lived for over a thousand years, after all, so I naturally possess some methods. Tell me, little one, what is your purpose in visiting my Ryūchi Cave today ? »

Asakawa Shin had specifically left Katsuyu behind today, but he had not expected the White Snake Sage to see through his identity so easily.

He scratched the back of his head and said, looking slightly shy, « I have come today for three reasons. First, I wish to learn the Sage Mode of Ryūchi Cave. »

« Sage Mode ? Very well. Since you were able to pass the trials of Ryūchi Cave and stand before me, you are naturally qualified to study it. »

The White Snake Sage agreed readily, though a hint of suspicion flashed in its eyes, « However, haven’t you already mastered the Sage Mode of Shikkotsu Forest ? Why go to such trouble... »

Asakawa Shin looked calm and spoke frankly, « The senjutsu of the Three Great Holy Lands is indeed powerful, but it is becoming increasingly incompatible with my current physique. Therefore, I wish to draw from the strengths of many schools to create a Sage Mode of my own ! »

The White Snake Sage gazed at him deeply, « Ambitious. And what of the other two matters ? »

Asakawa Shin looked at the void to one side, wearing a half-smile, « Second, I would like to borrow the Three Great Snake Princesses under your command. I hope they can accompany me and assist in perfecting this new Sage Mode. »

The Three Great Snake Princesses were startled.

They had just been shocked by the White Snake Sage’s good temper today, but the next second, the topic had shifted to them.

Unable to continue hiding and watching the drama, they quickly revealed their forms and eagerly voiced their objections.

« Absolutely not, White Snake Sage ! You must never agree to this human’s request ! »

« That’s right, if we all leave with him, who will serve by your side in the future ? »

« Exactly ! This human is definitely not a good person ! Look at how he turned me into a ball ; it was all his doing. You must not be deceived by him ! »

The White Snake Sage lowered its eyelids, ignoring the three noisy snake princesses.

It remained silent for a moment, then its gaze fell back upon Asakawa Shin, and it asked in a hoarse voice, « What is the third request ? You might as well state it all at once. »

Asakawa Shin smiled slightly, his true intentions revealed, « I have heard that Ryūchi Cave is filled with Natural Energy, so I would like to practice here for a period of time. »

« Swish— ! »

The White Snake Sage suddenly opened its eyes, its cold, slit pupils locking onto Asakawa Shin, its tone turning icy, « Boy, do not push your luck. To make such an exorbitant demand, do you think my Ryūchi Cave is weak and easily bullied ? Is that why you are deliberately looking for trouble ? »

« You misunderstand, White Snake Sage. »

Asakawa Shin quickly waved his hands, his expression remaining calm and smiling, « I have no intention of targeting Ryūchi Cave. I will be visiting Mount Myōboku later as well ; everything is about fairness, after all. »

« Oh ? You don’t refute the former ? It seems you truly do not hold Ryūchi Cave in any regard... »

The White Snake Sage laughed in extreme anger, its aura exploding, and it shouted sternly, « What an arrogant fellow ! Then let me measure your skills. My minions, prepare to engage the enemy ! »

At the White Snake Sage’s command, a teeth-grinding rustling sound immediately echoed all around.

Countless venomous snakes slithered out from the dark corners, and in an instant, they densely covered the ground.

« Tsk, after all that talk, we still have to fight. If I had known, I should have just started fighting when we met. »

Asakawa Shin shook his head helplessly and scanned the surrounding snakes, his eyes full of disdain, « But to rely on these things, aren’t you looking down on me too much ? »

Before the words had even faded, a terrifying aura exploded from his body, instantly sweeping across the entire area.

The snakes did not even have time to react before they fell to the ground and died, having been shaken to death by this pressure.

« What ? »

The White Snake Sage’s pupils constricted ; it clearly had not expected the opponent’s technique to be so terrifying that the snakes could not even get close.

It suppressed the shock in its heart and ordered with feigned composure, « You have some skills. Snake Princesses, attack together, and make sure to capture this boy ! »

« Ah ? We’re going up ? »

The three snake princesses looked at each other, their eyes full of hesitation, but under the White Snake Sage’s sharp gaze, they could only bite the bullet and rush forward.

As a result, they were taken out in a single exchange.

If Asakawa Shin did not still find them useful, he probably would have dealt with them right there.

Ichikishima-hime scrambled away in a sorry state, not forgetting to leave a harsh threat before leaving, « You brat, just you wait ! Sisters, hold him off, I’m going to get reinforcements and will be right back ! »

« Hmph ! A bunch of useless trash ! »

Witnessing the ugly display of the Three Great Snake Princesses, the White Snake Sage was even more furious. It sat up abruptly from its soft couch and decided to take action itself.

It gently waved its sleeve, and its entire body underwent a shocking transformation.

In an instant, it transformed into a huge white-scaled snake, and the friction of its scales emitted a sound like metal clashing, which was chilling.

The giant snake soared into the air, circling above, its cold snake eyes staring fixedly at Asakawa Shin.

Then, it suddenly opened its bloody maw and spat a mouthful of venom toward Asakawa Shin.

Asakawa Shin swayed slightly and easily dodged the attack.

The venom hit the ground, instantly corroding a bottomless pit, and the black poisonous mist spread rapidly like a living thing, attempting to swallow him.

« Child’s play. »

Asakawa Shin snorted coldly, his hands forming seals with lightning speed, and he instantly activated a technique.

« Wind Release : Typhoon Passage ! »

A fierce wind suddenly arose, instantly blowing away the sky-filling poisonous mist without a trace.

A cold light flared in the White Snake Sage’s eyes ; holding nothing back, it directly unleashed its ultimate senjutsu move.

« Sage Art : White Rage Technique ! »

With the violent surge of senjutsu Chakra, a divine dragon, entirely crimson, suddenly appeared.

The dragon bared its fangs and brandished its claws, its dragon claws tightly gripping a brilliant dragon orb, roaring as it charged with astonishing power.

The moment it approached Asakawa Shin, the dragon suddenly dived into the dragon orb, which then exploded violently.

The blinding white light instantly swallowed everything, making it impossible to see.

Immediately following this, the destructive sonic shockwave struck like a ten-thousand-ton hammer, causing his eardrums to ache as if they were splitting, and his internal organs and brain were violently shaken.

« Quite a flashbang. As expected of the White Snake Sage, you certainly have some tricks up your sleeve. »

Asakawa Shin frowned slightly, feeling a subtle prickling pain from within his body.

He hurriedly closed his eyes, simultaneously releasing Armament Haki to protect his entire body, only then blocking the attack without a scratch.

« It is impolite not to reciprocate. I happen to know a method to attack the inside of the body as well, so I’ll let you have a taste too ! »

Having said that, he leaped into the air, his body flashing with lightning, and swung a heavy punch toward the White Snake Sage.

« Fist Bone : Impact ! »

A violent shockwave suddenly erupted, sweeping toward the White Snake Sage.

The White Snake Sage’s pupils constricted, and it hurriedly constructed a defense, « Sage Art : Inorganic Reincarnation ! »

In an instant, the entire ground seemed to come alive, churning and writhing wildly. Thick rock and earth rose from the ground, condensing into a towering earthen wall, attempting to withstand this destructive impact.

However, the next instant, the shockwave arrived and slammed fiercely into the earthen wall.

After only a moment of stalemate, the solid defense collapsed with a roar, turning into sky-filling rubble and dissipating completely.

The shockwave, its power only slightly diminished, continued to blast forward.

The White Snake Sage’s expression changed drastically, but there was no time to think. Instinctively, it coiled its massive body into a ball, attempting to take the hit head-on.

But the power of the shockwave far exceeded its imagination.

With a deafening roar, the White Snake Sage’s huge body was sent flying like a kite with a broken string, crashing heavily to the ground as blood sprayed wildly.

It struggled to prop up its body, feeling as if its internal organs had shifted, its whole body in unbearable pain, its eyes filled with disbelief.

« To severely injure me in a single move, this level of strength... »

Just then, a round figure descended from the sky ; it was none other than Ichikishima-hime, who had fled earlier.

She looked at the White Snake Sage triumphantly, asking for credit, « White Snake Sage, I’ve brought reinforcements back ! This time you must reward me well ! Come out now, Manda, Shinya ! »

As soon as she finished speaking, dust flew on the ground, and two giant snakes burst through the soil, appearing before everyone.

Asakawa Shin watched this scene with great interest, the corners of his mouth curling up involuntarily.

« So this is Manda ? Truly a ruthless character. It seems this time... I’ll have to get serious ! »
