# Chapitre 128 — Migrant Workers

The blue sky was like a fresh wash, with white clouds like cotton wool. Suddenly, an eagle’s cry pierced the sky, breaking the peace of Konoha Village.

Kakashi Hatake, Kurenai Yuhi, Asuma Sarutobi, and the other Jonin leaders looked up almost simultaneously, their gazes shifting toward the horizon.

« Is this... a summons for the Jonin leaders ? Tsk, counting the time, is that about to begin ? »

One figure after another followed the order, speeding from all directions toward the Hokage Building. Soon, they were all gathered in Hiruzen Sarutobi’s office.

Hiruzen Sarutobi sat at the head of the room with a solemn expression. « I have summoned you all here for no other reason.

Seeing everyone gathered here, you should already have a conclusion in your hearts, shouldn’t you ? »

...

« It’s already that time of year, the biannual— »

« Naturally. After all, we can already see Ninjas from other Villages within our own Village. »

« So, when does it start ? The Chunin Exams. »

...

« In one week. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi took a deep puff from his pipe, his tone steady and unhurried.

« I hereby officially announce that seven days from today, on July 1st, the Chunin Selection Exams will officially begin ! »

...

Meanwhile.

Sakura was fuming, chasing a group of people—the Konohamaru Corps, led by Naruto, who were currently scurrying away in fear.

While running for his life, Konohamaru screamed piteously, « That ugly woman is terrifying ! She hits so hard, it hurts ! »

Because of his loose tongue, he had just been pummeled by Sakura. Now utterly terrified, he fled forward as fast as he could.

Glancing back, he saw Sakura still pursuing him like a vengeful spirit. He shuddered in fear, and his speed increased by another thirty percent.

As a result, because he was only looking behind him, he didn’t pay attention and crashed straight into the siblings Temari and Kankuro. The recoil sent him bouncing back onto the ground, landing hard on his rear.

« Ouch ! You brat, don’t you have eyes when you walk ? »

Before Konohamaru could recover, Kankuro grabbed him by the collar and lifted him off the ground.

Beside him, Temari was relatively more polite, speaking up to stop him : « Kankuro, forget it. Don’t cause trouble, or you’ll get scolded later. »

Seeing this, Sakura hurried forward to apologize : « I’m sorry, it’s my fault for messing around. If I hadn’t been chasing him, he wouldn’t have run into you. »

Naruto, however, couldn’t hold back. Seeing his subordinate being bullied, he barked, « Hey ! Let him go, or don’t blame me for being impolite ! »

« Oh ? So you’re a Genin from Konoha ? »

Kankuro looked disdainful. Instead of letting go, he held Konohamaru up high, openly provoking them : « I’d like to see just how you plan to be impolite ! »

Seeing Konohamaru’s painful expression, Naruto could no longer endure it and charged forward : « You bastard, see how I deal with you ! »

A flash of successful cunning crossed Kankuro’s eyes, and he thought to himself, 'Heh, provoked so easily ? Konoha’s Genin are nothing special.'

With a slight movement of his fingertips, a thin, silk-like Chakra Thread shot out silently, wrapping around Naruto’s ankle in an attempt to trip him.

Unexpectedly, Naruto noticed the moment his balance shifted. Using one hand to brace himself against the ground, he nimbly performed a backflip and regained his footing.

Before Kankuro could make another move, Naruto scratched his head and burst out laughing.

« Are those Chakra Threads ? Are you guys migrant workers who just arrived in the Village ?

You should have said so earlier. I thought you were some suspicious characters... »

« Workers... what workers ? »

« To work in a Textile factory, of course ! Aren’t Chakra Threads standard equipment for you guys now ?

Alright, alright. Seeing as it’s not easy for you to be carrying all that luggage, if you put Konohamaru down and apologize, I’ll act as if this never happened. How about it ? »

Hearing Naruto’s « magnanimous » words, Kankuro didn’t appreciate it at all. Instead, his face twisted in rage.

« Stop talking nonsense ! You bastard, you’re really making me mad ! I have to teach you a good lesson today.

As I thought, I hate brats like you the most. Clearly younger than me, yet you act so arrogant...

Die ! »

Beside him, Temari’s eye twitched. 'This idiot Kankuro, those words were clearly directed at someone else, right ? Whatever, I can’t be bothered with him.'

Just as Kankuro, completely enraged, swung his fist at Konohamaru, a pebble suddenly whistled through the air and struck his arm.

The sharp pain caused his fingers to spasm, forcing him to release Konohamaru.

Naruto was quick, catching the falling Konohamaru mid-air. He then completed a turn and delivered a sharp roundhouse kick, sending Kankuro flying.

Everyone looked in astonishment toward the direction the pebble had come from...

Sasuke was leaning against a large tree branch, playing with another pebble between his fingers. Looking cool, he said :

« What are you acting so arrogant for in someone else’s Village ? Get lost before I get angry. »

« Sasuke-kun is so cool~ »

Sakura and Moegi were instantly infatuated. Even Temari was secretly impressed, completely forgetting about Kankuro.

Naruto, however, gritted his teeth in frustration : « Sasuke, you bastard, stealing my thunder again ! I could have easily handled a guy like this alone ! »

Kankuro scrambled up from the ground in a pathetic state. Hearing this made him even more furious : « Infuriating brats, one after another... I’ll finish you all off right here ! »

Before he finished speaking, he suddenly tore off the bandages on his back, looking like he was about to get serious.

« Stop it, Kankuro ! »

At that moment, a light voice suddenly rang out, causing Kankuro to break into a cold sweat.

Sasuke was equally shocked, because the voice came from right next to him, and he hadn’t noticed a thing.

Sasuke snapped his head around to see a red-haired boy hanging upside down from the tree trunk next to him. The character for « Love » on his forehead was a piercing crimson, and the murderous aura radiating from him was bone-chilling.

However, when Sasuke’s gaze shifted slightly upward, the pressure brought by Gaara instantly vanished.

Above the tree canopy stood another boy with short blue-gray hair and a mysterious flower tucked into it. His handsome face was filled with calm confidence.

« Shin, when did you pop up ? You haven’t been watching the show this whole time, have you ? »

Indeed, the newcomer was Asakawa Shin. Hearing Naruto’s complaint, he waved his hand casually.

« I just got here too. I happened to see your heroic moment kicking that Sunagakure Ninja. Not bad, Naruto~ »

« Hahaha, you think so too ? I also thought that kick was super cool ! »

Gaara only then realized there was someone else standing above him. His heart tightened, and he quickly put some distance between them to regroup with Temari, warily eyeing the guy who had approached him so silently.

Asakawa Shin also looked over with interest, but upon seeing him, he couldn’t help but be greatly surprised.

'This guy’s dark circles have actually disappeared ? What’s going on ? Is his stress resistance that high ?'

Temari was almost dazed as she watched, unable to stop herself from complaining inwardly : 'What the hell ? Why are there so many handsome guys in Konoha ? Compared to them, everyone in Sunagakure is an eyesore. Dammit, I’m so jealous of the girls in Konoha.'

Asakawa Shin’s gaze was as cold as ice, and for a moment, Gaara actually felt a bit nervous. This was the first time he had felt such genuine pressure from someone his own age.

However, he soon thought of the Shukaku inside him and regained his composure.

'Is this guy also a Genin from Konoha ?

Hmph, what a pity.

No matter how strong you are, in the face of the absolute power of myself and Shukaku, you are ultimately nothing more than fragile porcelain.

But a prey of this caliber is worth me personally crushing.

The Chunin Exams... really haven’t disappointed me.'

« I am Gaara of the Desert. What is your name ? »

« Asakawa Shin. »

He stared deeply at Asakawa Shin, a flash of bloodthirsty excitement crossing his indifferent eyes. He then turned to command Temari and Kankuro :

« Let’s go. »

Kankuro wiped the cold sweat from his forehead and exchanged a glance with Temari. The two followed closely behind him and hurried away.
