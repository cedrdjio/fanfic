# Chapitre 39 — Come Over and Clean Up the Mess

« Odd changes, even stays the same... »

Uzumaki Kana asked with a face full of confusion, « Wha... what ? »

Asakawa Shin felt relieved seeing that her expression didn’t seem fake. « I was saying, Big Sister, I don’t even know your name yet ? »

« Oh right, my name is Uzumaki Kana, I’m a... Ninja from Kusagakure. » Uzumaki Kana said somewhat hesitantly.

« Uzumaki ? Your surname is Uzumaki too ? My best friend’s surname is Uzumaki ; his name is Uzumaki Naruto. Are you from the same clan ? »

« I suppose so ? There are still people from the Uzumaki Clan in Konoha ? And they’re the little hero’s friend ? »

Uzumaki Kana’s eyes lit up upon hearing this, then she seemed to fall into deep thought, her expression becoming very complex.

Her face changed several times before finally settling into determination. With a « thud, » she knelt before Asakawa Shin and began her plea :

« Little hero, actually, I lied just now. I... I’m not a Ninja of Kusagakure at all, but rather their medical pack.

My constitution is very special ; as long as someone bites me when they’re injured, they can recover their Chakra and wounds.

But this consumes my life force. I can feel it ; I won’t last more than a few years.

If it were just me, it wouldn’t matter. In these chaotic times, human life is as cheap as grass ; if I die, I die.

But I still have a daughter, Karin, who is currently being held as a hostage in Kusagakure to prevent me from escaping.

She inherited my constitution. If I die, she will likely fall into the same fate as me.

I beg of you, little hero, please save us. We mother and daughter are willing to serve as your slaves to repay your kindness. I beg of you... »

Uzumaki Kana kept kowtowing to Asakawa Shin ; she was truly out of options.

When the Land of Whirlpools was destroyed, she ended up in Kusagakure. Kusagakure was just a small Ninja village with no medical Ninjas and very poor medical conditions.

To repay them for taking her in, she had a moment of softheartedness and treated a Ninja from Kusagakure. She never expected that the Ninjas of Kusagakure were all demons.

After her special constitution was exposed, they kept her like livestock as a medical resource, sucking away her life force day and night.

Even the powerful constitution of the Uzumaki Clan was about to give out.

'It’s fine if I die, but Karin, my poor daughter, Mama will definitely save you. For you, Mama is willing to do anything !

If we go to Konoha, no matter how bad it is, it won’t be worse than now.'

Asakawa Shin’s heart stirred upon hearing this. He had money now and planned to acquire two large mansions in the core district of Konoha.

One for his parents to live in, and one for himself.

But while houses were easy to buy, it wouldn’t do if there was no one to clean them.

Of course, his desire to save the Karin mother-daughter pair was to uphold justice, definitely not because he wanted them to be his slaves.

However... the house would need to be a bit larger.

Thinking of this, he immediately put on an air of righteous indignation and said loudly :

« What ? There’s such a thing ? Kusagakure is actually such a den of filth. Don’t worry, I will definitely rescue Karin.

Also, what is a slave ? Is it like the maids at Uncle Hyuga’s house ? »

Seeing Uzumaki Kana nod, Asakawa Shin quickly helped her up and said happily, « Then from now on, you two will be my maids. »

Uzumaki Kana was also overjoyed. Although this little hero was young, he had a warm-hearted and chivalrous spirit. She and her daughter might have truly met a good person.

« Sister Kana, don’t worry yet. Once our Konoha main force arrives, I’ll take a trip to Kusagakure, blow them all away, and rescue Karin. »

Uzumaki Kana said with tears in her eyes, « Thank you, little hero... no... thank you, Master. »

Asakawa Shin felt a bit embarrassed. He scratched his head and said to Kana, « Um... my name is Asakawa Shin. In public, you can just call me Shin like everyone else.

As for the title of 'Master', just call me that in private. It’s a bit embarrassing with so many people around. »

« I understand, Master. In public, may I address you as Lord Shin ? »

« As you wish. Right, take this. Since you’re calling me Master, I’ll give you a meeting gift. »

Asakawa Shin took a scroll out of his Ninja tool pouch and handed it to Uzumaki Kana.

« This is your Uzumaki Clan’s Fuinjutsu scroll. It contains almost every type of seal. You mother and daughter must cultivate it well.

Especially that Strength of a Hundred Seal ; cultivate it seriously and never neglect it. »

« Thank you, Master~ Your kindness is something we mother and daughter can never repay in a lifetime. »

...

They waited in the valley for another half hour. Perhaps seeing the mess and corpses scattered everywhere, not a single person dared to jump out.

Just as Asakawa Shin was getting impatient, a large group of Ninjas finally arrived from the distant horizon. The main reinforcement force had finally arrived.

Fortunately, halfway there, Asakawa Shin felt they were too slow and rushed ahead to the rescue. Otherwise, at this current speed, Nara Shikaku’s head would have already been turned in for a bounty.

« Kakashi, you’re finally here. Come over and clean up the mess. »

« That group of Ninjas attacked me just now, so I dealt with them. See if there’s anything you need. »

The leading Ninja was indeed the dead-eyed Kakashi. He said somewhat helplessly, « Shin, I’ve told you so many times, when I’m operating as Anbu, you have to call me by my code name. »

Asakawa Shin rolled his eyes at him. « Stop it, Kakashi. Your shock of white hair is so conspicuous, and you have a Sharingan. Who wouldn’t recognize you ?

Just take off the mask already. Besides, the code name you picked is completely pointless. »

At that moment, a Ninja wearing a green jumpsuit with thick eyebrows and a bowl cut walked over. It was Guy, and he gave Asakawa Shin a thumbs-up.

« Those Ninjas all died from very powerful Taijutsu attacks ; their internal organs and brains were shattered. What a strike full of the fire of youth !

Shin, let’s spar together when we have time ? »

Asakawa Shin nodded. « Sure, Guy. I’m also very interested in your Eight Gates. We can exchange pointers when we’re free. »

« As for those Ninjas, as expected of you, Guy, to see the subtlety of my move at a glance.

My Taijutsu incorporates internal destruction techniques, allowing me to directly attack the enemy’s fragile organs and brain. Ordinary Ninjas can’t withstand it. »

Asakawa Shin looked at Guy’s thick eyebrows and, as if remembering something, said to him :

« Right, Guy, after we get back, the Six Styles Training Class is going to hold a monthly competition. You should come too. Maybe there will be children there who interest you. »

« Oh ? I’ll definitely attend. »

Guy was a bit surprised. Shin wouldn’t say that without a reason ; he would have to take a good look at those children when the time came.

'Internal destruction ? Is it an attack similar to Medical Ninjutsu or the Gentle Fist ? No, it’s even more overbearing than those.' Kakashi pondered in his heart, wondering if he could take that move.

After waiting a while longer, the medical team led by Yakushi Nono had finished treating the injuries of Shikaku and the others, and they met up with a group of wounded.

Kakashi laughed loudly, pointing at Nara Shikaku’s pathetic state on the stretcher. « You’re in quite a sorry state, actually getting beaten like this. »

Nara Shikaku’s face was full of bitterness as he shook his head and remained silent.

He had almost died this time. It really wasn’t that he didn’t try his best ; the enemies weren’t ordinary Ninjas.

Asakawa Shin also brought Uzumaki Kana over and introduced her to everyone :

« This big sister is named Uzumaki Kana. From now on, she and her daughter Uzumaki Karin will be joining our Konoha—as my maids.

I will report this matter to Hokage-sama personally. »

« Uzumaki ? »

Kakashi looked at that fiery red long hair in a bit of a daze. He looked around but didn’t see any red-haired little girl. « Then where is Karin ? »

« She’s still being bullied in Kusagakure. I’ll go and pick her up myself later. »

« Wait a minute, Shin. That will cause a diplomatic dispute. » Even while lying on a stretcher, the 'Diplomatic Minister' was still concerned about his official duties.

« Hmph ! As long as I kill everyone who knows about it, no one will know I took Karin, right ? Don’t worry, I know what I’m doing... »
