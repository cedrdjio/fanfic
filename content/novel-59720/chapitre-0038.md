# Chapitre 38 — Uzumaki Kana

A forest a hundred miles away from the battlefield.

« Damn it, damn it ! That brat has no sense of martial honor ! »

« My money... Waaaaah... »

As all the hearts in Kakuzu’s original body were destroyed, his main consciousness transferred into this Lightning Release heart, which was also his last remaining heart.

Although he hated Asakawa Shin to the core, his feet were very honest as he quickly fled.

'Hmm ? Someone is coming from ahead.' Kakuzu’s footsteps faltered, and he quickly hid himself.

His current state was very poor ; with only one heart left, his margin for error was too low. He had to be extremely careful.

Sure enough, before long, six Ninjas arrived together.

Kakuzu tried his best to minimize his presence, intending to wait for them to leave before continuing his journey. It wasn’t until they got closer and he heard their conversation that he suddenly changed his mind.

« We need to be careful ; Nara Shikaku should be up ahead if nothing goes wrong. »

« Then shouldn’t we pick up the pace ? What if someone else beats us to it ? »

« No need to worry. They’re just pawns leading the charge. The big shots always make their entrance last, haha. »

« True. Let those fools go and wear down Nara Shikaku and the others first, then we’ll reap the rewards. »

« Hehehe, when they’re fighting to the death, I’ll launch a vicious sneak attack ! Hehehe~ »

« What the hell are you laughing at ? »

« Lightning Release : False Darkness ! »

« Aaaagh... »

The battle started abruptly and ended just as quickly. These Ninjas weren’t strong to begin with, let alone against Kakuzu’s shameless sneak attack.

Kakuzu deliberately left them alive—not out of the goodness of his heart, of course, but for their hearts.

« Secret Technique : Heart Capture ! »

« Aaaagh... »

Kakuzu’s mood finally improved slightly. After all, this time he had attacked a complete squad, and he had managed to gather hearts with all five elemental Chakra natures. Afterward, he carefully tailored their corpses, eventually sewing them together into a brand-new Kakuzu.

« Too weak. The hearts are weak, the bodies are weak, and I’ve lost most of my Earth Grudge Fear. I don’t know how many years it will take to cultivate it back. »

« If only I could get my original body back. Otherwise, I can only upgrade my configuration bit by bit. This really is a huge loss. »

« However, quite a few Ninjas are gathered here. I wonder if there are any hearts or materials worth using... »

...

On the battlefield, Asakawa Shin tossed Kakuzu’s corpse to Nara Shikaku’s group with a look of disgust.

« The sealing scroll was sewn into his body. Find a way to get it out. Also, his corpse is of great research value ; take it back to the Torture and Interrogation Force and the Research Division for study. »

« The main reinforcement force should be arriving soon. Wait here for a moment. As for safety, don’t worry—my Shadow Clone is also here. I’ll go clear out the little rats in the area first. »

« Yes, Lord Shin. »

Asakawa Shin walked slowly out of the valley and said loudly, « Since you’re all here, why not show yourselves ? »

Seeing that there was still no movement around him, he took a blank scroll out of his Ninja tool pouch and toyed with it in his hand.

« Or is it that you’re all uninterested in low-brow pursuits ? For example... »

« This 1.1 billion Ryo ? »

As soon as he finished speaking, the sound of rushing footsteps filled the air. Within moments, the clearing in front of Asakawa Shin was filled with people—at least several dozen by a rough count.

Asakawa Shin looked at their positions. There were at least seven or eight squads in front of him, mostly extras joining in the fun. Asakawa Shin didn’t sense any experts worth killing among them, nor were there any familiar faces, but... there was a red-haired woman. That hair color wasn’t common. Could it be...

« Kid, is that the legendary 1.1 billion Ryo in your hand ? Hand it over to me, and I can spare your life. »

« Hey, shorty ! Hurry up and hand over that scroll, or I’ll twist your head off and kick it like a ball ! »

Asakawa Shin was stunned for a moment and couldn’t help but ask, « Who are you calling shorty ? »

The masked man was also taken aback by the question. « You, of course ! Who else could it be, me ? Is there anyone here shorter than you ? »

« Me... Me ? » Asakawa Shin pointed at himself in disbelief, and the onlookers nearby all nodded.

Asakawa Shin took a deep breath and scanned the scene defiantly. As his gaze moved, his expression grew darker and darker, until black lightning actually began to crackle behind him.

« I want to make a solemn declaration : I am just young, not short ! »

« Forget it, why am I arguing with dead people ? You all should be more careful in your next lives. »

« Shave ! »

Asakawa Shin couldn’t stand it for another second. It was one thing for these people to try and steal Konoha’s money, but why the personal attacks ? You all have already found your way to death.

In an instant, he appeared above the crowd, his right leg raised high. A massive amount of Chakra and black lightning covered it as he chopped down fiercely toward the masked man who had just spoken so arrogantly.

« Heavenly Foot : Tomahawk ! »

« Wha— ? » The masked man only managed to say one word before being smashed into a mist of blood. The tomahawk kick struck the ground without resistance.

The ground deformed violently the moment it was struck, rising like a surging wave before exploding at its highest point.

The shockwave from the explosion caused everyone to vomit blood incessantly, mixed with fragments of internal organs. Their bodies were also sent flying hundreds of meters by the inertia, making it clear they were unlikely to survive.

Only one red-haired older sister remained unharmed, protected in front of Asakawa Shin in a princess carry.

Up close, Asakawa Shin saw the bite marks on her shoulders and arms, confirming her identity.

'It really is her, Karin’s mother. Both mother and daughter are high-quality assets. I have to get them before Orochimaru does.'

'Coincidentally, my future squad after graduating from the Ninja Academy is also lacking qualified teammates. Karin would be a great choice...'

Asakawa Shin gently placed the red-haired sister on the ground, brushed the dust off her, and smiled at her with a face full of concern :

« Big sister, are you okay ? You were forced to be with them, weren’t you ? »

« I saw it all. They bit you when you didn’t listen. They’re so mean. But don’t worry, I’ve eliminated all those bad guys. »

« You’re safe now. »

Uzumaki Kana looked at Asakawa Shin in a daze. This innocent-looking child had gone 'whoosh' and then 'boom,' and that large group of people was sent flying. One of them even...

It was too fast ; she couldn’t see what had happened at all. She only knew that the child before her, who was about the same age as her daughter, possessed strength that was absolutely extraordinary.

Moreover, he had just saved her like a hero saving a beauty—it was just that this hero was a bit small.

While Uzumaki Kana was lost in thought, Asakawa Shin suddenly said, « By the way, big sister, you nodded just now too, didn’t you ? »

Upon hearing this, Uzumaki Kana instinctively nodded, then immediately shook her head with a look of terror. She was afraid that if she shook her head too slowly, she would explode into a mist of blood the next moment.

She hurriedly explained, « No, I didn’t mean that... »

« It’s okay, big sister. I just wanted to say that I’m only six years old ; I’ll grow very tall in the future. I’m not some shorty. »

« I won’t get angry about such things either. People only care about what they don’t have... »

« I’m not short, so I won’t care. Right ? »

Uzumaki Kana : « Ah, yes, yes, yes ! »

« ? »
