# Chapitre 152 — Breaking Through the Trials

Asakawa Shin continued walking deeper into Ryūchi Cave.

Before long, the figure of a woman dressed in splendid robes materialized not far ahead.

However, each time he tried to approach, the figure would dissipate like smoke, only to coalesce again at a distance once he stopped.

This repeated several times, always maintaining a tantalizing distance, ultimately leading him to the mouth of a deep, secluded cavern.

Asakawa Shin rolled his eyes impatiently at this and, without the slightest hesitation, stepped into the cave.

The moment his front foot crossed the threshold, the heavy stone door behind him slammed shut with a thunderous crash, instantly sealing off all retreat.

Only then did the woman slowly reveal herself, a mischievous, triumphant smile on her face, her eyes brimming with satisfaction :

'I am Ichikishimahime, the guardian of this second trial.'

She looked down at Asakawa Shin, her voice clear and melodious yet tinged with a dangerous edge : 'Next, you must pass my test. Otherwise... I will eat you.'

As she spoke, she extended her tongue to lightly lick her lips, a flash of greed flickering in her eyes. 'I have a particular fondness for 'ingredients' as young and pretty as you, especially with the abundant Chakra within you. It smells exceptionally delicious already.'

'Oh really ?'

Hearing this, Asakawa Shin showed no panic. Instead, he raised an eyebrow and chuckled lightly. 'That sounds like a rather nice end. Since you’re so eager, why not skip all those tedious middle steps and just start eating ?'

Ichikishimahime froze in place. Her eyes rolled once before she suddenly adopted an expression of utter disdain, covering her nose as she said :

'You’re not an idiot, are you ? Then I won’t eat you. It might be contagious and make me stupid.'

'*You’re* the idiot !'

Asakawa Shin’s face darkened upon hearing this. He immediately jumped up and unceremoniously flicked her forehead, knocking her straight down from mid-air.

'Ow ! What are you doing ? That hurt !' Ichikishimahime clutched her forehead, screeching in a fluster.

'Enough nonsense !' Asakawa Shin snorted coldly, striding forward to pinch her cheek.

'Today, you’ll eat whether you want to or not !'

Before Ichikishimahime could react, a torrent of pure, vast Chakra surged into her like a broken dam, instantly stifling her words, leaving only a few muffled, utterly aggrieved whimpers.

'Mmmph, this... this is...'

'This Chakra... it’s so delicious, absolutely exquisite... I want more, just a little more.'

'Thank you, human. You’re really not bad, a truly good person.'

'It’s the first time I’ve eaten so much delicious Chakra. Thanks for the treat.'

'Wait... I don’t want any more, I really don’t ! I’m already stuffed, I can’t eat another bite !'

'Mmmph, wu wu wu...'

By the time Asakawa Shin finally released his grip, Ichikishimahime had long since rolled her eyes back, lying plump and limp on the ground, completely unconscious.

Asakawa Shin looked at the round, prone form of Ichikishimahime on the ground, muttering with a face full of confusion :

'I thought snakes could swallow prey several times their own size ? And she just passed out from being full like this ?'

As he spoke, he reached out and unceremoniously patted her rosy cheeks. Seeing no reaction from her, he nodded to himself :

'Then this trial... should count as me passing, right ? Hmm... if you don’t say anything, I’ll take it as your silent agreement.'

With that, he didn’t linger any longer. He turned and strode purposefully towards the thick stone door.

However, the moment his body made contact with the stone door, a bizarre scene unfolded.

The solid stone door simply caved inward, imprinting a clear, human-shaped hole. Yet the stone walls around the hole remained completely intact, without even a single crack.

Only after Asakawa Shin’s figure had completely vanished into the darkness did Tagitsuhime reveal herself, looking thoroughly intimidated.

Gazing at the still-unconscious Ichikishimahime on the ground, she couldn’t help but shiver, murmuring with lingering fear :

'That was truly brutal... Good thing I had the sense not to provoke that scourge earlier. Otherwise, the one lying here now would probably be me.'

Asakawa Shin traveled alone through the deep cavern. Ahead, the outline of a brightly lit temple grew increasingly distinct.

Just then, a black shadow shot out from behind him, baring sharp fangs as it lunged viciously for his neck.

'Heh heh heh, I am Tsu*****ehime, the guardian of Ryūchi Cave’s third trial !'

The shadow laughed triumphantly, its voice sharp and piercing. 'Once I bite you, you will immediately fall into an endless illusion.

If you cannot break free, not only will you fail the trial, but you will also become my meal !'

However, the laughter ceased abruptly after only a moment.

'Huh ? Why can’t I bite through ?'

Tsu*****ehime looked up in confusion, meeting Asakawa Shin’s utterly contemptuous gaze.

'Why don’t you try the other side ?' Asakawa Shin raised an eyebrow, his tone full of mockery.

'That’s... that’s a great idea ! Why didn’t I think of that ?'

Tsu*****ehime actually nodded with apparent seriousness, then truly released her bite and turned to try and sink her teeth into the other side of his neck.

*Whap !*

A crisp sound rang out as Asakawa Shin backhanded her with a solid slap, sending her flying. 'Seriously ? You actually went for it ?'

'You... you cunning human.'

Tsu*****ehime scrambled up from the ground, clutching her swollen, reddened cheek, her eyes a mix of venom and resentment :

'You won’t let me bite you ; that doesn’t follow the procedure at all. Without a guardian’s approval, you absolutely cannot pass Ryūchi Cave’s trials !'

'Who cares about your procedure.'

Asakawa Shin shrugged, his expression one of complete indifference as he continued walking forward.

Tsu*****ehime, still unwilling to give up, tried to step forward and block him, but was promptly pulled back by Tagitsuhime and Ichikishimahime, who had just appeared.

'Let it go, just let him be.' Tagitsuhime waved a hand, looking utterly certain. 'He definitely won’t get past the White Snake Sage anyway.'

'Exactly, exactly.'

Ichikishimahime chimed in repeatedly, her eyes sparkling with the gleam of someone anticipating a spectacle. 'We might as well all go watch the show, see how this arrogant guy gets blasted to smithereens by the White Snake Sage.'

'Let’s go, let’s go...'

...

Entering the temple’s main hall, the first thing that met his eyes was a towering stone carving of a great dragon.

The dragon’s head was held high, its mouth firmly gripping an ancient plaque upon which the characters for 'Ryūchi Cave' were inscribed in a bold, flowing script.

Yet, at the foot of this imposing stone sculpture, there lay a soft divan.

Upon it reclined an old woman with a kindly face. Her eyes were slightly closed, seeming neither fully awake nor asleep, appearing fragile and weak...

But Asakawa Shin keenly sensed the incredibly dense Natural Energy surging within her.

Asakawa Shin’s gaze sharpened, sweeping over the old woman from head to toe before he spoke calmly, 'So, you’re the one they call the White Snake Sage ?'
