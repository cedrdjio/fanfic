# Chapitre 184 — A Talent

Just then, a sudden burst of applause rang out, interrupting Hidan’s manic laughter.

Hidan whipped his head around ; as expected, the figures of Orochimaru and Asakawa Shin stood not far away.

Orochimaru’s eyes were burning with intensity, sizing him up with an almost obsessive gaze as he murmured under his breath :

« A flawless immortal body, coupled with the Reaper Death Seal that can be used without paying the price—that is a terrifying technique capable of sealing even the Nine-Tailed. »

« It’s hard to imagine what kind of horrifying power this contains. I’m afraid even the current me is no match for him. »

Hearing this evaluation, a surge of wild joy instantly welled up in Hidan’s heart.

« That’s right, I am invincible now ! You’re so stupid ; instead of running away, you actually delivered yourselves to my doorstep. »

« This is wonderful. I will return the pain you inflicted on me a hundredfold, a thousandfold ! »

With killing intent surging, Hidan thrust his right hand forward, controlling the massive phantom of the Shinigami behind him to suddenly close in.

Orochimaru’s pupils constricted sharply, and he almost subconsciously moved to shield Asakawa Shin in front of him.

Asakawa Shin rolled his eyes speechlessly.

In the next instant, a violent burst of Conquerors Haki erupted from his body, striking Hidan like a bolt of lightning before he could react.

Hidan’s eyes rolled back, and he instantly went limp like a puppet with its strings cut, falling into a baby-like sleep.

The uncontrolled Shinigami phantom also dissipated, turning into a stream of light that merged back into Hidan’s body and disappeared.

Asakawa Shin turned to look at Orochimaru behind him and teased, « Senior Brother, there’s no need to be so nervous. His main body is incredibly fragile ; as long as he doesn’t get your blood or catch you off guard with the Shinigami, he’s no threat. »

The corner of Orochimaru’s eye twitched slightly. No threat ? That’s only true for you.

In the Ninja World, if anyone else faced the current Hidan and was even slightly careless, it would lead to eternal damnation.

If used properly, this guy could undoubtedly play an incredibly terrifying role.

Thinking this, Orochimaru’s gaze became fanatical again, staring fixedly at Hidan on the ground as if examining a rare treasure.

Fortunately, he still possessed a shred of reason and forcibly suppressed his inner agitation, asking curiously, « Junior Brother, why did you go to such lengths to create such a monster ? What is your intent ? »

« Senior Brother, don’t you think ? »

Asakawa Shin narrowed his eyes slightly, his gaze lingering on Hidan. « This guy has extraordinary abilities, but his own strength is weak. He’s the perfect material for making a Human Puppet. »

He silently added in his heart : 'Or, he would also be excellent as material for a Naraka Path puppet.'

Orochimaru was greatly shocked upon hearing this : « You mean... that fellow Sasori’s Human Puppet technique ? But isn’t Hidan immortal ? »

« It doesn’t matter. »

Asakawa Shin waved his hand casually. « We just need to seal his consciousness. »

With that, he gave a low shout : « Hokage-Style Sixty-Year-Old Technique : Kakuan Entering Society with Bliss-Bringing Hands ! »

The ground instantly split open, and a large Wood Release hand lunged out, slamming down onto the crown of Hidan’s head.

In just an instant, Hidan’s consciousness was completely sealed deep within his brain, and a complex, profound runic mark was engraved on his forehead.

After properly handling Hidan, Asakawa Shin bid farewell to the reluctant Orochimaru and returned to the Akatsuki Organization base with this « trophy. »

This trip was quite fruitful ; not only did he gain a talent like Hidan, but he also obtained Minato Namikaze’s soul from the Shinigami’s stomach along with half of the Nine-Tailed.

However, he did not yet possess the technology for making Human Puppets.

Thinking this, he walked toward Sasori’s laboratory.

He soon arrived at his destination and, after a brief hesitation, raised his hand and knocked on the door.

Before long, Sasori’s massive Hiruko puppet body appeared in his field of vision.

Accompanied by the sound of mechanical parts turning, Sasori’s impatient voice came from within : « It’s you, Tobi. Is there something ? If so, speak quickly. »

Asakawa Shin immediately got straight to the point : « I want the Human Puppet making technique in your hands. »

Sasori’s anger flared up upon hearing this.

This was his core secret, practically his lifeblood. How could he possibly hand it over to an outsider so easily ?

He immediately roared, « Are you joking ? ! »

However, in the next moment, his voice came to an abrupt halt.

He saw Asakawa Shin expressionlessly pull out a Ninjutsu scroll and hold it out to him : « Inside this is the body of the Fourth Raikage Ai. Exchanging it for your Human Puppet technique should be no problem, right ? »

Sasori’s expression changed instantly.

The Kage of Kumogakure—for his body not to have been ransomed back after dying in battle was simply unbelievable !

If he could obtain this body, it would undoubtedly be a massive boost to his combat power.

After weighing it in his mind for a moment, Sasori finally let out a long sigh and turned into the room : « Follow me. »

A flash of excitement crossed Asakawa Shin’s eyes.

The members of the Akatsuki Organization were all treasures, and they spoke so pleasantly ; he really liked it here.

Soon, the two arrived at a research room filled with various puppet materials.

Sasori took a scroll from a hidden compartment and tossed it to Asakawa Shin :

« This is the method for making Human Puppets. But I warn you, the process is tedious and requires extremely precise Chakra control. I can’t guarantee you’ll be able to learn it. »

Asakawa Shin nodded with a smile, took the scroll, and prepared to leave.

Sasori hesitated for a moment before calling out, « If you have any more bodies of this quality in the future, you can give them to me. I’m willing to exchange them for the Red Secret Technique. That is the top-tier puppet manipulation art in the Ninja World. »

Asakawa Shin gave a noncommittal nod and then drifted away.

Sasori closed the laboratory door and, unable to suppress his inner excitement, immediately began tinkering with the Raikage scroll in his hand.

Asakawa Shin originally wanted to go back to Orochimaru’s laboratory to continue his research, but on second thought, he had been in Akatsuki for so long and hadn’t collected a single paycheck yet.

He might as well go to Konan to get some puppet materials...

So, he righteously turned to find Konan.

When he found Konan, she was staring at him with a face full of resentment, gritting her teeth as she cursed, « Madara ! You are shameless and vile ! »

Asakawa Shin was momentarily stunned by the cursing, then realized what was going on and quickly denied it : « It wasn’t me ! It definitely wasn’t me ! I am the great Uchiha Madara, how could I do such a thing ? »

Konan’s gaze chilled and suddenly became sharp : « Madara, it really was you ! Every time I see you... and I haven’t even said what it was yet, and you’ve already confessed. Hmph, I’m going to gather everyone and let them see how shameless you are ! »

Asakawa Shin snorted coldly, unafraid : « Fine, then gather everyone ! I also want to report you to everyone for abusing your position to embezzle organization funds to buy Paper Bombs ! »

Konan’s expression changed drastically, and a hint of panic entered her voice : « How did you know ? I clearly did it flawlessly... »

Asakawa Shin chuckled, « Konan, you wouldn’t want others to find out about your business, would you ? »
