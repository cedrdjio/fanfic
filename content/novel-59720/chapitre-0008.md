# Chapitre 8 — You'll Regret This, I Am Hokage

Hokage Office.

« Hokage-sama, this is a Taijutsu secret technique scroll developed by Asakawa Shin, a student from Class 1-1, called the Konoha Six Styles.

Shin asked me to give it to you and requested that you choose a suitable candidate to teach it to, in order to strengthen Konoha’s foundation. »

Iruka Umino spoke respectfully to Hiruzen Sarutobi, his words filled with a sense of pride.

During the break this morning, Asakawa Shin demonstrated the Konoha Six Styles on the playground ; it was like a depth charge, instantly sending the Ninja Academy into a frenzy.

Even now, though school is over, the academy is still bustling. In particular, that large rock with a hole poked through it has become a popular check-in spot, with many students and parents gathering to see it.

If it were a Ninjutsu with such power, people wouldn’t be so shocked, but this is pure Taijutsu—and this technique is only one of the Six Styles.

The Rokushiki is a multi-functional ability encompassing attack, movement, defense, and evasion ; just the Moon Walk, which allows movement in the sky, was enough to blow everyone’s minds.

Currently, there are only a handful of Ninjas in the Ninja World who can fly, the most famous being the Sandaime Tsuchikage Ōnoki and the Fourth Kazekage Rasa.

Even among the Five Kage, only two can fly, which shows how rare flight ability is in the Ninja World.

Furthermore, it’s said that Life Return, the integration of all Six Styles, allows one to control every muscle, bone, blood vessel, and hair in the body ; such an effect is even more astonishing.

Just hearing the description brings to mind many Ninjutsu that could complement it, making it highly practical.

It’s no exaggeration to say that the existence of the Konoha Six Styles could support a medium-sized clan, yet now this technique is being taught to their children for free—and the one who created it is just a first-year academy student !

For various reasons, this news spread through Konoha like a supernova explosion, and even the villagers who aren’t Ninjas are talking about it with great interest.

Because of this, even the heads of the major Ninja clans have come. This is a technique that can increase a clan’s foundation, not to mention the commoner Ninjas ; if they can learn it, they might just change their fates.

Fortunately, Asakawa Shin had anticipated this ; he publicly stated that he would currently only personally teach his classmates.

As for others, he would compile the secrets of the Rokushiki into a scroll to be handed over to Hokage-sama ; whether others are qualified to learn it will be up to Hokage-sama.

After all, he’s just a child, which is why Iruka appeared in the Hokage Office.

« Asakawa Shin ? I remember that child. He wrote very well in his previous essays, but I didn’t expect him to have such talent. He has truly given me a grand surprise. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi took a puff from his pipe. The Anbu guarding Naruto had previously reported to him that Asakawa Shin was developing a type of Taijutsu.

He didn’t expect results so quickly, and of such immense value. He was also shocked when he received the news this morning.

« This child’s will of fire is truly pure. To hand over such a valuable secret technique unconditionally... however, we in Konoha cannot let those who contribute feel disheartened.

Iruka, go back and tell that child to come to the Hokage Building when he has time. I’ll take responsibility and let him choose any one Ninjutsu from the scroll of seals.

Iruka, as the homeroom teacher of Class 1, you must guide him and Naruto well. I have a feeling that the future of Konoha rests on their shoulders ! »

Iruka’s whole body jolted, and as if he’d been given a shot of adrenaline, he shouted :

« Yes, Hokage-sama ! I will do my absolute best to teach them ! If there is nothing else, I shall take my leave. Shin is still waiting for me. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi nodded slightly : « Go ahead. »

Shortly after Iruka left the Hokage Office, the door was suddenly slammed open.

Hiruzen Sarutobi knew who it was without looking up. He sighed softly in his heart, and sure enough, Danzo’s voice reached his ears a moment later.

« Hiruzen, that child Asakawa Shin must be handed over to me. Only by giving him to me can his talent be fully utilized ! Root needs such talent ! »

« Impossible. Fresh young sprouts need sunlight and dew, not to rot and stink in the dark Roots ! Danzo, you are not allowed to target him ! » Hiruzen Sarutobi said firmly.

Danzo’s face stiffened. He hadn’t expected the monkey’s attitude to be so uncompromising, so he slightly softened his tone :

« Hiruzen, that child’s talent is too outstanding. To create such Taijutsu at such a young age—and you should know the difficulty of training that technique, right ? »

« Outstanding talent, handsome features, commoner background, and arrogant—he’s simply another Orochimaru !

Hand him over to me, and I will forge him into the perfect weapon ! You wouldn’t want... »

« Shut up, Danzo ! I will personally foster that child ; you don’t need to worry about it. And don’t think I don’t know about the matter with Orochimaru.

Now, get out ! »

Hiruzen Sarutobi slammed the table and stood up. This bastard actually dared to mention Orochimaru ; back then, he already felt something was fishy about Orochimaru’s situation.

But in a short time, the matter was blown out of proportion and the whole Village knew ; the arrow was already on the string and had to be fired.

An investigation afterward revealed nothing, but no flaws is the biggest flaw of all.

In Konoha, the only person capable of infiltrating his Anbu and who coveted the position of Hokage was one person—Shimura Danzo.

No matter how wrong Orochimaru was, he was still his beloved pupil whom he once intended to pass the Hokage position to. Now, he was forced to defect from Konoha, which was an eternal pain in Hiruzen Sarutobi’s heart.

« Hiruzen, you will regret this ! Everything I do is for... »

« Danzo, I am the Hokage ! »

Hiruzen Sarutobi interrupted again. His eyes were bright and piercing, as if spitting fire, and for a moment, he actually regained a hint of his former aura as the Hero of Shinobi.

« I said ! Get. Out. »

« Hmph ! » Danzo felt a chill on his scalp. He didn’t dare to further enrage Hiruzen and slammed the door as he left in a somewhat disheveled manner, leaving only a loud crash.

« Bang ! »

Hiruzen Sarutobi watched Danzo’s retreating back with mockery ; he knew his old teammate all too well.

He hesitates in great matters out of self-preservation, yet forgets the greater good for petty gains. It was like that before, and it’s still like that now.

Unfortunately, he will never understand that the Hokage is meant to stand in the light for all to see, not hide in a dark corner with his backside exposed.

...

« Hanako, I have something to ask you. »

« How rude, call me Mom ! » Hanako placed one hand on her hip angrily and grabbed Asakawa Shin’s ear with the other.

« Ouch, ouch, ouch, ouch, ouch, my ear is going to fall off. Mom, I was wrong. »

Asakawa Shin pleaded for mercy. Only after Hanako let go did he breathe a sigh of relief ; in his distraction, he had accidentally called Hanako by her name.

« I wanted to ask if our family has any noble blood. After all, you’re so beautiful, and I have a bit of modest talent. »

« Eh ? Didn’t I tell you ? Let me tell you, Shin. »

Hanako said with a somewhat serious expression :

« Your grandmother was a Ninja from the prestigious Senju Clan. Following the command of the Shodai and Nidaime to integrate the Senju into the thousands of households of Konoha, she gave up the Senju surname and married outside the clan. »

« You were able to grow up so handsome because you inherited the world-shaking beauty of your grandmother and mother. You should be grateful to your mother, you know ? »

« In the future, when Mom says one, you can’t say two ; when Mom tells you to go east, you can’t go west ; when Mom argues with your father, you must help Mom ; when Mom argues with your wife, you must help Mom even more ; Mom... »

Shin : « ... »
