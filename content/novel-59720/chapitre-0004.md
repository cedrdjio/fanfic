# Chapitre 4 — Drawing a Template

[Ding ! System binding successful. Please host, draw a world and hair color. For the initial draw, you may select your hair color. The system will draw a qualifying template based on the world and hair color. The initial draw is guaranteed to be a 'Ceiling' tier template.]

[If the host’s hair color matches the drawn template, you will gain Acting Proficiency daily. Performing actions consistent with the template will also grant Acting Proficiency. Increasing proficiency will improve the template synchronization rate.]

'What is a Ceiling template ?'

[Characters who have reached the pinnacle of a certain field in their respective worlds can become Ceiling templates and be added to the prize pool. Please check the manual for details.]

[Draw world now ? Yes/No]

'Phew... Draw.'

[Ding ! Congratulations, the world drawn is the « One Piece » world. Please select a hair color to obtain your template.]

'Select black.'

Asakawa Shin chose black because, for one, there are countless black-haired powerhouses in the One Piece world, and for another, he naturally had black hair.

It would be inconvenient and unseemly for a six-year-old kid to dye his hair blonde. It definitely wasn’t because he was afraid of Hanako.

Speaking of which, can you dye your hair in the Ninja World ? Shin really hadn’t paid attention to that.

[Ding ! Congratulations, the Ceiling template you have drawn is « Gol D. Roger. » When this template’s proficiency reaches 100% and your Ninja rank is promoted, you will receive another template draw.]

[Current Ninja Rank : Genin Reserve (Newbie Benefit). Next Rank : Genin/Chunin/Jonin/Kage. Please see the manual for details.]

'It’s actually Roger ? I’ve really hit the jackpot.'

In an instant, Shin felt his physique and spirit strengthen several times over.

Various concepts regarding Taijutsu, Swordsmanship, and the three types of Haki flooded his mind, and even the Chakra within his body began to grow rapidly.

Before entering the academy, Shin had already learned Chakra Refinement from Hanako, but his Chakra capacity could only be described as mediocre.

But now, this Chakra was surging, intensifying, raging, and dominating. In a short span of time, it had multiplied dozens of times over, seemingly without end.

This violent growth in Chakra caused Shin slight discomfort, but he gained control over the energy in a flash. He could manipulate it even more freely than before, as easily as moving his own limbs.

It made sense ; Chakra is formed by mixing physical energy and spiritual energy in a specific ratio.

With Roger’s monster-like physical strength and unyielding powerful will, even a slight fusion made Asakawa Shin feel as if he had been reborn.

Roger’s own talent was nothing short of monstrous ; even with a diseased body, he could defeat countless heroes and reach the pinnacle of pirates.

Now, combined with Asakawa Shin’s disease-free body and the miraculous Chakra system, there was no doubt that another terrifying monster had been born into the world.

'It’s not surprising that the rapidly strengthening physique and spirit can be easily controlled with Roger’s power system.

But to be able to adapt to and master the control of the never-before-seen Chakra system so quickly... what a terrifying talent.

Should I say, as expected of you ? Pirate King !'

Converting Roger’s peak combat power to this world, he would likely be a standout among Super Kage-level fighters. He probably wouldn’t even lose to Reanimated Madara, and his stamina and recovery would be even more exaggerated.

With a single thought, the world suddenly felt wide open !

The Ōtsutsuki are fierce ? My sword is not without its edge !

...

It takes a long time to tell, but only a few minutes had actually passed. On the podium, Iruka had long since noticed Asakawa Shin.

This kid had been acting very strange for a while now. One moment he was breathing heavily, huffing and puffing.

The next, he was covered in sweat and wiping it off. It wasn’t even that hot today, was it ?

And now he was muttering something to himself while making weird « jie jie » cackling sounds.

He wasn’t the only strange one. Uchiha Sasuke, sitting in front of him, was trembling all over with a panicked expression, staring straight ahead as if he wasn’t quite normal either.

Looking to the left, that demon fox brat was sitting upright, grinning widely for some unknown reason.

Across the aisle, the little girl from the Hyuga Clan had her head tilted and her Byakugan rolled back as if she’d fainted ? He wasn’t sure ; he’d have to keep watching.

Iruka took a deep breath and scanned the room. Was that pineapple-headed kid sleeping ?

Groups of little girls were chirping excitedly about something, and there was a chubby kid eating in class, someone playing with bugs, and someone playing with a dog.

It was a chaotic mess, making Iruka’s vision go dark again and again.

« Are these really my students ? Can I really do this ? » Iruka fell into deep self-doubt.

A few days ago, Hokage-sama had summoned him, affirming his will of fire and saying there was a major and arduous task to give him—a task that could even determine the future of Konoha ?

He had accepted, of course. It was about teaching and educating, after all. Even if it included that demon fox problem child he cared so much about, he could suppress his anger and hatred.

After all, the child was innocent. As a teacher, he had the responsibility and duty to guide the children.

But now... Hokage-sama didn’t say the task would be this difficult ! The time to test my will of fire has arrived !

« Ahem... Everyone is doing great, very energetic ! »

Iruka coughed a few times to get everyone’s attention. Seeing that everyone was looking over, he continued :

« Alright, now everyone will introduce themselves. I’ll start with a demonstration. »

« My name is Iruka Umino. My favorite food is Ichiraku Ramen, I dislike Mixed Rice, and my hobby is soaking in hot springs.

My dream is for all my students to become excellent Ninjas and have a bright future. »

« Just like that, simple, right ? Say your name, what you like, what you hate, and your hobbies and dreams out loud.

Alright, let’s start from the first row and go in order. Let’s begin, you go first. »

« My name is Uchiha Sasuke. I like... I don’t want to say. I hate sitting at the very front ! ! !

My hobby... I don’t want to say.

My dream is to become a powerful and elegant Ninja like my brother, and let everyone witness the glory of the Uchiha Clan ! »

« Sasuke-kun is so cool !~ »

« Tch~ What’s he acting so smug for. »

« What, you got a problem ? Is being smug against the law ? Is there any law in Konoha that says a person can’t be smug ?

(Changes face) Go Sasuke-kun ! I support you~ »

I like Ichiraku Ramen, and I like Hanako-obasan’s cooking even more. I hate being alone. My hobbies are fishing and drawing with friends.

My dream for the future is to become a Hokage who surpasses all previous Hokages !

To do that, I’ll make everyone in the Village acknowledge my existence. I never go back on my word—that’s my Ninja Way ! »

« Haha, surpassing all Hokages ? What a boast ! »

My favorite food is Mother’s cooking made with love. I hate drinking milk. My hobby is lazily sunbathing in a breezy, warm place.

It’s Lord Asakawa Shin ! »
