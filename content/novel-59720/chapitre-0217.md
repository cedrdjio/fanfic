# Chapitre 217 — As Long as You're Happy

Kurotsuchi, this Akatsuki Organization 'intern', had been casually assigned by Pain to serve under Asakawa Shin for the time being.

The two of them walked one after the other along the mountain path leading to Takigakure ; the surroundings were eerily quiet.

Without any warning, Asakawa Shin slammed on the brakes, his body coming to a halt like a nail driven into the ground.

He slowly turned around, his eyes hidden behind the spiral mask glinting with a cold light. His piercing gaze locked onto Kurotsuchi behind him as he said gloomily :

« Alright, out with it. »

« Huh ? »

Caught off guard, Kurotsuchi froze on the spot, blinking in utter confusion. « Big bro, out with what ? I don’t understand... »

Asakawa Shin acted as if he hadn’t heard a word, remaining silent. However, his slightly narrowed eyes and the gradually dropping pressure around him emitted an increasingly dangerous sense of oppression.

This silent confrontation completely shattered Kurotsuchi’s psychological defenses.

Feeling her legs go weak, she suddenly burst into tears with a loud 'waa'. Mucus and tears smeared across her face as she wailed without a shred of dignity :

« Big bro, I didn’t want to ! But the Leader planted a cursed seal on me, forcing me to monitor you and report your movements to him regularly ! »

Even after she revealed this shocking secret, Asakawa Shin remained expressionless with his arms crossed, letting her continue her performance.

Seeing that she couldn’t hide it anymore, Kurotsuchi steeled herself and added with a trembling voice : « Also... also that bastard Deidara told me to keep an eye on you in secret ! He wanted me to be an undercover agent by your side to gather intel on your abilities... Big bro, I really didn’t mean to, I was forced into it ! »

Asakawa Shin sighed softly and said earnestly, « Kurotsuchi, don’t forget your identity. You are an undercover agent that I discovered. »

« Big bro, I remember, I remember ! » Kurotsuchi wanted to cry but had no tears left. She nodded her head like a woodpecker, solemnly swearing that she wouldn’t leak a single piece of useful intelligence.

It wasn’t until Asakawa Shin withdrew that suffocating gaze that she felt as if she had been granted a grand pardon and breathed a sigh of relief.

'How did things end up like this ? Adding in the Iwagakure side, wouldn’t that make me a quadruple agent ? The world outside the Village is way too complicated !'

After regaining her composure, Kurotsuchi immediately sensed something was off and asked suspiciously, « This mission is to capture the Seven-Tails, right ? Why bring me along for a battle of this level ? »

« To test you, of course. »

Asakawa Shin spoke as if it were a matter of course, then dropped a bombshell. « Once we encounter the Seven-Tails Jinchuriki later, I’ll leave her for you to handle. »

« Nani ? Me ? »

Kurotsuchi pointed at herself in disbelief. Upon seeing Asakawa Shin nod solemnly, her face instantly turned ashen. « I can’t do it ! How could I possibly defeat a Tailed Beast ! »

« No, you can. »

Asakawa Shin looked certain. « This Seven-Tails Jinchuriki is currently just a Genin. As long as you don’t let her enter a berserk state, you’re guaranteed to win. »

Kurotsuchi was stunned for a moment, then it was as if her meridians had been cleared. She instantly perked up : « Really ? Then I’ll take this job ! Let me teach her just how cruel the Ninja World can be ! »

She rubbed her hands together, sweeping away her previous dejection, her face filled with eagerness to try.

« Um... Big bro... I have something I’m not sure I should ask ? »

« Speak ! »

Kurotsuchi lowered her voice, her eyes full of curiosity. « Why is our Akatsuki Organization going to such great lengths to collect the Tailed Beasts ? What’s it all for ? »

Asakawa Shin said with great conviction, « For world peace ! »

Kurotsuchi curled her lip inwardly. If you don’t want to say it, then don’t. World peace... do you take me for a three-year-old ?

Asakawa Shin shook his head helplessly ; when he told the truth, she didn’t believe him.

Young girls these days were truly hard to please.

In Takigakure, atop the towering canopy of the Ancestral Tree.

Fu, the Jinchuriki, was irritably rubbing the shameful mark on her lower abdomen, muttering through gritted teeth : « Damn it... what’s going on ? Why won’t that Ancestral Tree Spirit come out no matter what ? To run away after teasing me, what a bastard ! »

In the shadows not far away, Kurotsuchi looked at Fu with a strange expression, then glanced down at the identical mark on her own abdomen. She then snapped her head toward Asakawa Shin, her eyes filled with a look of sudden realization.

« Oh ! » Asakawa Shin slapped his forehead, looking distressed. « How could I forget about that ? Since you’ve found out, it seems I’ll have to kill you to keep you quiet. »

« Don’t ! Big bro ! » Kurotsuchi trembled all over and quickly clung to his arm, pleading while shivering : « I’m stupid, I don’t know anything ! Please don’t kill me ! »

Looking at Kurotsuchi shivering while hanging onto his arm, Asakawa Shin sighed helplessly. « Let go. If you keep hanging on, my arm is going to break. »

« I won’t ! Even if you kill me, I won’t let go ! »

Kurotsuchi shook her head like a rattle, not forgetting to cast a wary glance at Fu on the canopy. « Big bro, although I don’t understand why that Seven-Tails Jinchuriki has your technique formula on her too, I swear I’ll take everything I saw today to my grave ! »

Just then, Fu on the canopy seemed to sense something was wrong.

She whipped around, her compound-like pupils staring intently at the two in the shadows. At some point, she had already gripped a Kunai in her hand.

« Who’s there ? ! »

Fu’s figure flashed as she instantly leaped down from the heights, landing steadily in front of the two.

When she saw Kurotsuchi’s Iwagakure forehead protector—which hadn’t even had a line scratched through it yet—she froze for a moment. Then, she warily took half a step back and shouted sharply : « Ninjas from Iwagakure ? And... a Masked Man ? What are you doing in my Takigakure ? »

Fu’s gaze fell on the mark on Kurotsuchi’s abdomen, and her body couldn’t help but stiffen. She blurted out : « Iwagakure... could it be they have an Ancestral Tree too ? »

« What kind of nonsense is that ? »

Kurotsuchi was completely baffled. Frowning, she decided not to think about it anymore and opted to strike first.

She took a large stride, her figure closing in like a lightning bolt. She raised her leg and sent out a powerful whip kick.

She had only intended to test her opponent, but to her surprise, Fu was completely defenseless and took the hit squarely, flying backward like a kite with its string cut.

« So strong ! »

Fu scrambled up from the ground in a sorry state, brushing off the dust. She looked at the girl of similar age in shock.

Kurotsuchi herself was stunned. 'This is only at the level of a Chunin, right ?'

She sized Fu up, her heart full of doubt. 'This is the strength of a Jinchuriki ? How can she be so weak ?'

Ever since joining the Akatsuki Organization, she had been surrounded by people at the Kage level or higher. As the pride of Iwagakure, she had always felt unable to hold her head high.

Now that she had met an opponent of equal standing, an excited light ignited in Kurotsuchi’s eyes. Finally, she had a chance to prove herself !

Fu’s expression turned solemn. No longer holding back, she instantly mobilized the Seven-Tails Chakra within her body.

In an instant, several pairs of brilliant insect wings unfurled from her waist. She took to the air, attempting to use her flight advantage to suppress her opponent.

However, the gap in combat experience soon became apparent. Lacking real combat refinement, Fu quickly fell into a disadvantage.

Fu cursed inwardly. Being on the canopy of the Ancestral Tree, she was wary of causing damage and didn’t dare to fully transform into her Tailed Beast form, fearing that even a slight lapse might crush the giant tree so vital to the Village. For a time, her hands and feet were tied, and she was riddled with openings.

After struggling to hold on for a moment, covered in wounds, she finally ran out of strength. Her eyes rolled back, and she fainted.

Kurotsuchi looked down at the fallen Fu, her self-confidence instantly exploding. She couldn’t help but remark :

« So the Jinchuriki are nothing special after all ! From what I know, that Nine-Tails Jinchuriki from Konoha is also a Genin. Since they’re so easy to catch, how about giving me the next mission too ? »

Hidden in the shadows, Asakawa Shin’s voice was indifferent : « As long as you’re happy. »

As soon as he finished speaking, his tone suddenly shifted, and he coldly ordered : « Come here. Stick out your tongue. »

« Eh ? For what, big bro ? » Kurotsuchi was confused.

« Fuinjutsu : Cursed Tongue Eradication Seal ! »

After a flash of white light, Asakawa Shin withdrew his hand and wiped it with satisfaction. « Alright, done. Now you won’t be able to speak any intelligence regarding me. Once you violate the restriction, I’ll be able to sense it immediately. »

« No ! Big bro ! »

Kurotsuchi covered her mouth and wailed in despair, « Now the Leader will definitely kill me when I go back ! »
