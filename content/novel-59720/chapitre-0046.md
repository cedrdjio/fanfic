# Chapitre 46 — Returning to Konoha

As Asakawa Shin got closer, he saw that it was indeed a White Zetsu, though its current state was quite pathetic.

This fellow seemed to have had bad luck and was caught in the shockwave of the sword qi.

Its entire lower body had vanished, and its belly was sliced open, revealing internal organs that looked like fungi.

A large hole was blasted in its head, and its brain, shriveled to the size of a walnut, was directly exposed ; it was as dead as could be.

After a careful examination, Asakawa Shin sighed. No wonder the White Zetsu’s corpse felt like a plant under the perception of Observation Haki ; it had been eroded by the God Tree and completely mutated.

However, White Zetsu is ultimately different from plants. Ordinary Observation Haki cannot sense it, but Listening to the Voice of All Things can detect the subtle differences.

After all, White Zetsu is a sentient creature, even if its brain has shriveled and it usually acts like an idiot.

But even so, it thinks far more than a plant. Listening to the Voice of All Things can perceive this difference in biological instinct ; even this White Zetsu corpse emits noises that a plant should not have.

'It seems I should periodically activate Listening to the Voice of All Things from now on. I wonder if this White Zetsu appearing here was just passing by, or if it was monitoring Kusagakure—or perhaps, monitoring me ?'

Normally, Asakawa Shin does not activate Listening to the Voice of All Things because the amount of information received is too vast and would place a burden on his brain.

But it is good to have a way to distinguish them ; otherwise, the thought of being watched by a White Zetsu without knowing is enough to make one’s skin crawl.

Asakawa Shin pulled out a blank scroll and sealed the White Zetsu’s corpse. This was also a good item, valuable for both research and cloning.

By now, the moon had begun to sink in the west, and time was running short. Asakawa Shin thought for a moment and created a Shadow Clone to try his luck.

The Shadow Clone also seemed to know it was not the time for fooling around. Under their joint efforts, the rift was finally repaired before dawn.

After dispelling the Shadow Clone, even with Asakawa Shin’s constitution, he felt a bit exhausted. He hadn’t closed his eyes for two days and nights, during which he had gone through several battles ; even the Wood Release Sage Body could hardly withstand this 24/7 grind.

By the time he returned to the hot spring inn, the sun had already risen high. When he saw Kana, he didn’t think twice before collapsing into her arms and falling into a deep sleep.

Suddenly having Asakawa Shin collapse into her arms, Kana was a bit startled but also somewhat happy. She looked at him and was surprised to find that he was already snoring lightly.

She frowned slightly with heartache ; he must be exhausted, right ? Civil engineering really isn’t a job for humans.

She gently carried Asakawa Shin back to the bedroom bed, took off his shoes, socks, and outer coat, and covered him with a quilt.

Asakawa Shin seemed immersed in a sweet dream, the smile at the corner of his mouth blooming like spring flowers, never fading.

This sleep lasted until the next morning. Asakawa Shin finally woke up and gave a great stretch.

« Yay, fully revived ! »

Kana walked in with a face full of smiles. Her master was saying things she didn’t understand again, but it was wonderful to see him so energetic.

« Sister Kana, I’ve rested well. Let’s go home, shall we ? »

« Go... home ? Hehe, alright, » Kana replied happily, her smile as brilliant as a painting.

...

After two days of long-distance travel, the three finally arrived at Konoha Village.

Actually, they could have been faster, but Karin couldn’t handle it ; this Airplane Technique was fast, but the constant jolting was very uncomfortable.

Asakawa Shin figured there was nothing urgent, so he slowed down to enjoy the sights along the way.

Hokage Office.

Hiruzen Sarutobi looked happily at his precious disciple ; after not seeing him for a few days, he seemed a bit thinner.

« Shin, you’re finally back. You must have suffered out there, right ? No one bullied you, I hope ? »

Nara Shikaku rolled his eyes nearby. Who could possibly bully him ? It was already good enough if he didn’t bully others.

« Teacher, it’s fine. As long as I successfully rescued Uncle Shikaku and ensured our Konoha’s property didn’t suffer any loss.

What does it matter if I personally suffer a little or get bullied ? » Asakawa Shin said righteously, patting his chest.

Hearing this, Nara Shikaku also braced himself, leaned on his cane, stood up, and said loudly :

« It’s all thanks to Shin this time. Otherwise, even if I died, the money we worked so hard to earn would have been snatched away by villains.

Hokage-sama, you have truly taken in a fine disciple ! Our Konoha is becoming more and more promising. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi also nodded with satisfaction. Shin had indeed handled this matter beautifully and brought him great pride, but another matter...

His eyes shifted to scrutinize the two women Shin had brought back. These must be the two women from Kusagakure mentioned in Shikaku’s previous report.

They are indeed from the Uzumaki Clan ; what nostalgic red hair.

Noticing Hiruzen Sarutobi’s gaze, Asakawa Shin hurried to introduce them :

« Teacher, I picked up this mother and daughter during the mission. Their Village was destroyed, and they were abandoned.

I saw they were homeless and pitiable, so I took them in as my maids to look after my daily life.

They also long for Konoha. Do you think they could join the Village and live here ? »

Both Hiruzen Sarutobi and Nara Shikaku’s eyelids twitched upon hearing this, and Shikaku couldn’t help but ask, « Are you sure no one else wanted them ? »

Asakawa Shin smiled triumphantly. « I’m certain, positive, and absolutely sure. »

« That’s good then. » Nara Shikaku breathed a sigh of relief.

« Shin, since they are so pitiful, you should take them in. By the way, do they have a place to stay ? »

« Teacher, I was just about to mention that. They can stay in an inn for now, and later I plan to buy two large houses in the Village center.

But I don’t have the money. When will you give me the money from selling the trees ? »

Asakawa Shin was quite excited. The reason he was so proactive when Shikaku called for help was because a large portion of that money belonged to him, and now it was time to harvest.

Hiruzen Sarutobi and the other group shareholders had already discussed this issue before Asakawa Shin returned, so he answered very straightforwardly :

« We plan to continue investing this money into the development of the Musi Group, investing 500 million Ryo to build factories, expand silk production, and purchase shops in major cities across the Ninja World.

Therefore, we can give you 400 million Ryo upfront. Of course, if you feel that’s not enough, some of the shops aren’t in a great hurry to be bought. »

Asakawa Shin was a bit surprised that the first batch of funds was so large and hurriedly said, « It’s enough, it’s enough. Buying two houses won’t cost that much money.

But since I have so much money, I might as well buy a large plot of land in the southern suburbs of the Village !

I want to build a luxurious grand manor there—the bigger, the better. I’m afraid there won’t be enough room once more people join later. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi couldn’t help but shake his head after hearing this ; this silly child had no idea what a massive figure 400 million Ryo was.
