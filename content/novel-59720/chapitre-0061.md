# Chapitre 61 — Hunter and Prey

The gazes of Naruto and Sasuke met in the air like lightning, instantly sparking invisible flames.

The two of them erupted into action almost simultaneously, rushing towards each other like arrows released from a bowstring...

At the moment they met, their right fists slammed into each other, the impact of their knuckles kicking up a faint ripple of air.

Sasuke’s expression shifted slightly, and his body involuntarily retreated two steps ; it was clear he had suffered a slight disadvantage in this exchange.

He was secretly shocked, never expecting Naruto’s strength to have grown to such a degree. It caught him off guard, especially since not long ago, he couldn’t even carry Hinata’s bento...

'No... that’s not right... Perhaps it’s because he was always carrying Hinata’s bento that he developed such immense strength ! I was too busy mocking him before and didn’t notice this. What a miscalculation !'

Sasuke realized this with a start and quickly began thinking of countermeasures in his mind : 'Naruto possesses the physique of the Uzumaki Clan ; his advantage in taijutsu is too great !'

'His strength, speed, and endurance all seem to be a cut above mine.'

'Furthermore, he is well-rested, while I have fought several matches in a row. Even though he took a hit from me just now, his condition is clearly better than mine.'

'How can I defeat Naruto in such a disadvantageous situation ? Hmm... what would my brother do ?'

« Here I come, Sasuke ! »

With a low shout, Naruto exerted force through his feet, his figure rushing forward as swiftly as a cannonball.

He clenched his right fist, bringing a sharp gust of wind as he aimed straight for Sasuke’s face. This strike was incredibly fierce, leaving seemingly no room for maneuver.

A sharp glint flashed in Sasuke’s eyes. He tilted his body slightly, swaying like a light leaf in the wind, skillfully dodging the fierce punch.

Naruto’s all-out strike missed, and the tremendous momentum caused his center of gravity to shift forward instantly, his body involuntarily rushing ahead.

Sasuke was waiting precisely for this fleeting opportunity. He quickly extended his right foot and decisively tripped him. Naruto instantly lost his balance, and under the force of his own forward momentum, he lunged forward awkwardly and landed flat on his face.

Seeing Naruto’s pathetic state as he fell to the ground, Sasuke felt reassured : 'No matter how strong your power is, if you can’t hit your target, it’s meaningless ! As long as I avoid a direct confrontation with Naruto, make him impatient, and keep draining his stamina, my chances of winning will only increase !'

Having formulated his battle plan, Sasuke didn’t hesitate and immediately began to execute it : « Naruto, why are you lying on the ground for no reason ? Get up quickly ; the ground is cold, and it’s not time for bed yet~ »

« Ugh... that hurts... No ! You bastard Sasuke ! What did you say ! Who’s lying here to sleep ! »

Naruto was predictably enraged by this taunt. He quickly scrambled to his feet and launched another storm-like barrage of attacks at Sasuke.

His straight punches, hooks, roundhouse kicks, and close-quarters charges came like a fierce squall, attempting to seal off all of Sasuke’s room to dodge.

Each strike carried overwhelming power ; it seemed that if just one landed, it could secure the victory in one fell swoop.

However, Sasuke never engaged Naruto directly. He moved flexibly within a small range, meticulously avoiding wasting even a fraction of his stamina, always making the most precise dodges just a moment before the attacks landed.

Although his speed was not equal to Naruto’s, he relied on his astonishing predictive ability to render all of Naruto’s fierce attacks futile.

Shin watched from the sidelines, shaking his head. Naruto was completely enraged ; his attacks had no method, relying solely on brute force. If this continued, he would suffer a great loss !

Sure enough... because every all-out strike hit only air, and every wild charge wasted stamina, Naruto’s movements gradually slowed, his breathing grew heavier, and impatience began to creep into his mind.

The more anxious he became, the messier his moves grew, and the messier they grew, the more openings he revealed. Sasuke’s eyes were cold and clear, observing calmly all the while, just like a hunter watching prey gradually walk into a trap.

He suddenly relaxed his left shoulder, intentionally leaving his ribs exposed and revealing an extremely obvious opening.

Naruto fell for the trick, dropping his shoulder and launching a savage charge, wanting to imitate Hinata and knock Sasuke away in one go.

But just before contact, Sasuke side-stepped, completely clearing the path. Naruto rushed into empty space, his center of gravity completely out of control, and he could no longer stop his forward lunge.

« Now ! »

Sasuke repeated his old trick, quietly extending his right foot, intending to trip Naruto again.

But he didn’t expect that Naruto seemed to have anticipated it ; he exerted force through his feet, and his whole body leaped into the air, narrowly avoiding the trip.

That wasn’t all ; Naruto stretched his body in mid-air, his legs curling up like a scorpion ready to strike, and swiftly unleashed a « Scorpion Tail Whip, » aiming straight for Sasuke’s face.

« Nani ? ! »

Sasuke was shocked. Faced with this sudden attack, he had no time to dodge and could only hurriedly block with his hands in front of him, taking the fierce blow head-on.

Like a leaf swept up by a gale, his body involuntarily flew backward under the immense force, finally crashing heavily to the ground, looking quite disheveled.

Having suddenly suffered a heavy blow, before he could recover from the dizziness, he heard Naruto’s triumphant laughter in his ears :

« Hahaha, Sasuke, you are simply too naive ! You actually thought you could use the same move on me twice ; do you take me for an idiot ? Feel good now, don’t you... How about that ! Is my 'Tail Wag' move impressive ? Hahaha... »

Sasuke turned a deaf ear to Naruto’s shouting ; those who truly want to win do not have smiles on their faces.

He shook his head vigorously, struggling to keep himself conscious, then climbed up from the ground with difficulty, carefully observing Naruto’s current state.

Naruto had also climbed up from the ground, covered in dirt, his breathing also becoming rapid ; he was no longer as full of vigor as he had been before.

Yet there was not a trace of panic on his face. Instead, he calmly shouted loudly at Sasuke : « Sasuke, I understand your intentions. Aren’t you just trying to drain my stamina ? »

Once these words were spoken, Sasuke was instantly shocked. Had this guy actually guessed his battle plan ? Then why did he...

« That look on your face~ it seems I guessed right~ Indeed, I didn’t notice it at first, but... Sasuke, your actions were too strange ! »

« You never fought me head-on, just kept dodging ; you were trying to use the smallest range of motion to trade for the exhaustion of my stamina, right ? »

« Your plan was good, but unfortunately, you didn’t realize that you yourself are already near your limit, right ? »

« When I discovered this, I just decided to play along with your act. Now, your stamina is almost completely depleted, right ? »

« If you don’t believe me, just feel it... Sasuke, you are already covered in sweat right now ! Hahaha~ »
