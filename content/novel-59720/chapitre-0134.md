# Chapitre 134 — The Kusanagi Sword

Orochimaru naturally sensed the astonishing movement in the distance. As he focused his gaze, a smile couldn’t help but surface on his face.

« I had my suspicions, but it really is Wood Release. And the scale of this Ninjutsu... isn’t it a bit too exaggerated ?

No wonder the old man has no shame, taking a disciple at his age. How pathetic. »

After speaking, his eyes were filled with heat. « Truly a perfect vessel. Compared to this, even the Sharingan is slightly inferior ! »

In a matter of moments, the Wood Dragon had arrived at the front lines.

Asakawa Shin’s gaze was like lightning, quickly scanning the field, and a hint of surprise couldn’t help but flash in the depths of his eyes.

Witnessing Sakura’s miserable state, his heart skipped a beat, and he secretly wondered in alarm : 'What’s going on ? Has Orochimaru actually become this cruel ?'

However, before this doubt could dissipate, the answer appeared before him.

Shifting his gaze to Sasuke, he saw that the boy’s eyes were blood-red, locked onto Orochimaru with undisguised hatred.

Not only that, Asakawa Shin was surprised to find that the other party had actually awakened the Three-Tomoe Sharingan.

'I see, so he used Sakura as a catalyst ? Strictly speaking, this isn’t a bad trade-off, though it’s quite hard on Sakura.'

Asakawa Shin nodded slightly, then instructed those around him : « Karin, Haku, take Sasuke and Sakura away from the battlefield. Leave this place to me.

Also, give Sakura some medical treatment while you’re at it ; her situation doesn’t look very optimistic. »

« Yes, Lord Shin ! »

With two crisp responses, Karin and Haku leapt down from the head of the Wood Dragon, quickly hoisting the two casualties to retreat into the distance, leaving only Naruto standing there in a daze.

It wasn’t until their figures were far away that Naruto finally snapped out of it and hurriedly ran after them, « Hey ! Wait for me ! »

Orochimaru watched silently, allowing Uchiha Sasuke to be taken away.

After all, everything is relative ; compared to Asakawa Shin’s Wood Release, Sasuke’s mere Three-Tomoe Sharingan was no longer as attractive.

He stared greedily at Asakawa Shin, his gaze becoming increasingly fanatical, his tongue uncontrollably licking around his lips.

However, before he could speak, Asakawa Shin on the opposite side also stuck out his tongue to lick his lips, looking over with a burning gaze.

« Senior Brother Orochimaru, you’ve finally arrived ? I, your Junior Brother, have been waiting for you for a long time. »

Orochimaru was slightly taken aback, a hint of unease clouding his heart. « Oh ? So you’ve recognized me, Junior Brother ? I recall we’ve never met before, have we ? »

Asakawa Shin waved his hand dismissively. « Don’t worry about the details. Let’s get straight to the point. »

Changing his tone, his expression turned solemn as he reached out a hand toward Orochimaru, issuing an invitation :

« Senior Brother Orochimaru, become my subordinate. If you do, I shall spare your life ! »

Even with Orochimaru’s level of composure, he couldn’t help but feel a bit incensed at this moment. He glared at Asakawa Shin :

« Junior Brother, do you think you’ve already won ? Young man, don’t be too arrogant ! »

As he spoke, he had already rapidly formed hand seals, launching a preemptive sneak attack.

« Wind Release : Great Breakthrough ! »

« What’s this ? In the end, it still comes down to a fight ! »

Asakawa Shin faced this fierce offensive without a change in expression. With a mere thought, he controlled the Wood Dragon to slightly tilt its head, effortlessly blocking the strike.

Orochimaru’s pupils contracted, and alarm bells rang in his heart : 'The hardness of this wood isn’t right. After taking my attack, it only lost a few wood chips ?'

After that initial probe, he didn’t dare linger and quickly made several leaps, trying to open up some distance.

A sharp light flashed in Asakawa Shin’s eyes as he controlled the massive Wood Dragon to press forward.

For a time, the Wood Dragon chased Orochimaru through the forest, rampaging and instantly turning the entire woods into ruins.

Smoke and dust billowed into the sky, and from a distance, it looked as if a catastrophic fire had broken out.

Asakawa Shin’s brow furrowed deeply ; Orochimaru’s movement was too agile, and the Wood Dragon couldn’t pin him down for the time being.

« Senior Brother, you should just surrender, lest things get too ugly later on. »

Orochimaru sneered upon hearing this : « Junior Brother, could it be that you’re running low on Chakra ?

It’s no wonder ; it’s already impressive for someone your age to have this kind of combat power, but unfortunately, you’re far too conceited.

Squandering Chakra like this is simply seeking your own death, hahaha ! »

Asakawa Shin’s expression turned peculiar. No wonder Orochimaru had been avoiding a direct confrontation ; so that was his plan.

He remained silent, deciding to respond with action. He clapped his hands together and shouted loudly :

« Wood Release : Deep Forest Emergence ! »

In an instant, the forest that had previously been destroyed by the Wood Dragon radiated with vigorous vitality once again.

Broken trunks sprouted branches and buds frantically, transforming into a sea of trees in the blink of an eye, surging like a giant wave toward Orochimaru.

« Senior Brother, faced with such a wide-range offensive, where are you going to hide now ? »

Orochimaru’s face was grim as he unhesitatingly lowered his body and opened his mouth wide.

« Ninja Art : Formation of Ten Thousand Snakes ! »

In a single moment, a massive number of venomous snakes gushed from his mouth, throwing themselves fearlessly at the surging sea of trees.

As they drew near, these snakes actually spat out sharp swords, colliding violently with the wooden tide.

However, the snakes were ultimately no match for the infinitely growing forest and were soon strangled to death by the trees.

The forest’s offensive was only slowed for a fraction of a second, and it continued to spread toward Orochimaru with unwavering momentum.

Facing this restless giant wave of green, Orochimaru’s pupils suddenly constricted. He immediately withdrew his stance and stabbed two Kunai into the ground.

Next, he performed a bizarre maneuver, spitting a snake out of his mouth, which then spat a sword out of its own mouth.

« Kusanagi Sword : Longsword of the Sky ! »

Under his precise control, the Kusanagi Sword’s spine accurately pressed against the two Kunai on the ground, and then the blade suddenly extended.

Using this reactionary force, Orochimaru’s body shot upward like a projectile.

Just seconds before the Formation of Ten Thousand Snakes completely collapsed, he narrowly escaped the range of the Deep Forest Emergence.

Standing atop the Wood Dragon’s head, Asakawa Shin felt a bit of a headache at the sight ; his Senior Brother’s methods were simply too bizarre.

A snake spitting a person, a person spitting a snake, a person spitting a person, a snake spitting a sword... this series of Ninjutsu developed on the 'Matryoshka' principle was truly inconceivable, making one’s sanity plummet.

Only when the fluctuations of the Deep Forest Emergence had completely subsided did Orochimaru retract the Kusanagi Sword and slowly land in the distance.

« As expected of my Senior Brother. These two moves of mine are enough to suppress ninety-nine percent of the powerhouses in the Ninja World, yet I didn’t expect you to still be standing. »

Asakawa Shin clapped and chuckled, slowly stepping down from the Wood Dragon.

« But you’d better be careful, Senior Brother. After all, what you’re about to face next is my second phase. »

With his departure, the massive Wood Dragon instantly lost its vitality, turning into a wooden statue and becoming completely still.

'Hmph, mere bluffing. Could it be that his original body is even stronger than his Wood Release ? Absurd.'

Thinking this, Orochimaru’s confidence redoubled.

Seeing Asakawa Shin still maintaining that calm and composed posture, he couldn’t help but tease in a flippant tone :

« Junior Brother, why have you come down ? Why not use that invincible Wood Dragon of yours to crush me directly ? »

Hearing this, Asakawa Shin gave him a deep look and then broke into a smile :

« Senior Brother, that sword you just used...

If I’m not mistaken, it should be the legendary Kusanagi Sword, right ? »
