# Chapitre 74 — Great Detective Itachi

« Teacher, since Danzo has defected, should we issue a wanted notice to the Ninja World ? »

Hiruzen Sarutobi thought of the tragic state of the Uchiha Clan, weighed the options repeatedly, and finally made a decision with a grave expression :

« Danzo has committed grave crimes and defected from the village. Effective immediately, he is classified as an S-rank rogue Ninja. Order the Anbu to begin pursuit immediately. However, given that he was once a high-ranking official of Konoha and possesses many secrets, he must be captured alive to prevent him from taking desperate measures when cornered, which would endanger the fundamental interests of Konoha ! »

« Teacher, you need not worry too much about the matter regarding Danzo, nor do you need to send personnel to hunt him down. I will bring him back in the future. »

Asakawa Shin hurriedly interrupted. Are you kidding me ? Only allowed to capture him alive ? Is he a Celestial Dragon ?

Wouldn’t that just let the pursuit team sacrifice themselves in vain...

It would be better to wait until I encounter him later and just casually grab him back. However, whether Danzo can withstand a casual grab from my giant Buddha statue will depend on his own luck.

After hearing Asakawa Shin’s words, the weight was finally lifted from Hiruzen Sarutobi’s heart.

If possible, he really did not want to kill his old teammate of many years.

Seeing Hiruzen Sarutobi’s weary expression and the undisguisable fatigue in his eyes, Asakawa Shin quietly gave Itachi a look, and the two bowed and took their leave with tacit understanding.

...

Uchiha Clan grounds, Naka Shrine.

Asakawa Shin looked at the corpses covered in white sheets all over the ground and shook his head helplessly.

Why did they have to organize a gathering, and why choose such a remote location ?

Now look, they were wiped out in one fell swoop, and it’s too late to save any of you.

Fugaku also failed to see the situation clearly ; since the momentum was lost, he should have just readily yielded his position to Shisui.

But he refused to do so and spent all day with these die-hards, closing the doors to praise themselves. Now they are truly dead and hard-headed.

Sigh, it’s just a pity for Aunt Mikoto, so young and already...

He casually selected a corpse and slowly pulled up the white shroud. As expected, the corpse’s eyes were gone.

It seems this was one of the goals of Obito and Danzo. Both of them had long coveted the Three-Tomoe Sharingan, and now they had hit it off perfectly.

Danzo must have defected to Obito by now, but joining Akatsuki seems unlikely, right ?

After all, Akatsuki’s first leader, Yahiko, died indirectly at Danzo’s hands. If he went to Akatsuki, wouldn’t he be minced into meat paste by Nagato ?

As expected, the gold content of the « Scapegoat Shadow » is still rising...

I wonder if Danzo and Orochimaru are still in contact. Now that they are both rogue Ninjas, perhaps they can rekindle their old relationship.

After all, they used to work together in Root ; they are old partners.

Just as Asakawa Shin was lost in thought, Itachi suddenly spoke :

« Lord Shin, could we step aside for a moment ? I have important intelligence that I would like to report to you privately ! »

A trace of confusion flashed in Asakawa Shin’s eyes, but he smiled and agreed :

« Oh ? I was just thinking the same thing. I also have something to discuss with you. »

The two walked side by side, moving away from everyone’s line of sight.

Until they strolled to the edge of a cliff, Itachi spoke after careful consideration :

« Lord Shin, I actually have a guess about the identity of that masked man. He should be that legendary Ninja—Uchiha Madara ! »

Seeing that Asakawa Shin indeed showed a shocked expression, a hint of pride flashed in Itachi’s eyes, and he continued :

« Actually, I met this masked man a long time ago. That was many years ago, when I was still a Genin. I unfortunately encountered him while executing a mission. One of my teammates died at his hands at that time. If Kakashi Hatake had not arrived in time, I fear I would have lost my life to him as well. It is thanks to him that I was able to awaken my Sharingan. »

Asakawa Shin nodded ; he had known this for a long time.

Uchiha Itachi smiled confidently like a great detective and continued to analyze for Asakawa Shin :

« According to my observations, every time he uses space-time Ninjutsu to teleport, it is accompanied by vortex-like distortions in space-time, and the center of that vortex is located in his right eye. After this brief engagement, I have finally confirmed that his right eye is indeed the legendary Mangekyo Sharingan ! And that elusive space-time Ninjutsu is very likely the ocular Jutsu awakened by this Mangekyo Sharingan ! Our Uchiha Clan’s Mangekyo Sharingan is very special ; if the ocular Jutsu is overused, it will lead to blindness. However, that masked man can use Mangekyo ocular Jutsu recklessly in battle, as if he doesn’t need to worry about the exhaustion of ocular power at all. This is very similar to Madara as recorded in the clan history ! »

« Furthermore, the masked man only used one eye from beginning to end. I have reasonable grounds to speculate that his other eye likely became permanently blind when he fought the First Hokage, due to the use of a forbidden Jutsu called Izanagi. Lord Shin may not know, but this Jutsu is a little-known forbidden ocular Jutsu within our Uchiha Clan. Its effect is quite peculiar ; it can reverse all events unfavorable to the user within a period of time, making them seem as if they never happened, while only allowing the results beneficial to the user to become reality. The conditions for use are also quite harsh ; one must have at least a Three-Tomoe Sharingan to activate this Jutsu, and after the Jutsu ends, this Sharingan will be permanently blind. It is very likely that Madara used this Jutsu to fake his death and escape ; this way, everything adds up ! »

« Why does that masked man have one, and only one, Mangekyo Sharingan, and why did he come to attack the Uchiha Clan ? Because when Madara left Konoha, not a single clansman chose to follow him, so he has always held a grudge against Konoha and the Uchiha Clan. This attack is Madara’s bloody revenge against the Uchiha Clan, and he also took the opportunity to seize some Three-Tomoe Sharingan to use as consumables for performing Izanagi ! »

« I even suspect that the Nine-Tailed incident was orchestrated by Madara. Because according to clan history, the Nine-Tailed was once Madara’s summoning beast, and the large Sharingan tomoe in the Nine-Tailed’s eyes cannot be faked ! At that time, all the Uchiha were under tight surveillance ; only he had the time and ability to commit the crime. This move could kill two birds with one stone, severely damaging both Konoha and the Uchiha Clan. Madara truly has such malicious intentions ! But now, all of this has been seen through by me ! »

Asakawa Shin listened, dumbfounded. As expected of you, Itachi !

You actually managed to make it all fit together. If I didn’t know the truth, I would definitely have been convinced by you.

Asakawa Shin said with some hesitation : « But if he is Uchiha Madara, wouldn’t he be an old man over 90 years old by now ? »

Itachi immediately retorted : « He is a legendary Ninja, after all ; it is very possible that he has some method to extend his lifespan ! »

Asakawa Shin questioned : « But what kind of hero was Madara ; how could he be such a person who hides his head and shows his tail ? »

Itachi answered in a second : « Madara is already an old man over 90, after all ; it is normal for his personality to have changed, right ? Just look at Hiruzen Sarutobi and you will know. »

« But Uchiha Madara possessed the power to pacify the chaotic Sengoku era ; that masked man simply couldn’t do that, right ? »

« He is over 90 years old ; how could he still be that strong ? Just look at Hiruzen Sarutobi and you will know. »

Asakawa Shin’s eye twitched, and he proactively cooperated : « Then there is only one truth ? »

Itachi looked at him with satisfaction, puffed out his chest, and proudly concluded : « Correct, all signs indicate that the masked man is undoubtedly Madara ! »

Asakawa Shin : 'Oh no, what’s going on ? I seem unable to argue with him. Now Obito has really become Madara...'
