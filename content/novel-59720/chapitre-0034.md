# Chapitre 34 — Reinforcements

« I can’t move my body. When did I get hit ? I was clearly being very careful... »

Kakuzu paused slightly as he said this ; he saw his own Earth Grudge Fear tentacles piercing Nara Shikaku...

Shikaku smiled confidently. « Noticed it, have you ? That’s right. The moment your spike pierced me, our bodies became closely linked. In other words, our shadows connected as well. »

« It was at that very instant that I performed a one-handed seal to activate the Shadow Possession Jutsu, elongating my shadow to disguise it as the shadow of your spike, thereby deceiving you. Surrender now ; your defeat is sealed. »

Kakuzu said with some admiration, « A one-handed seal ? No wonder I felt your posture was a bit off for a moment ; it was to hide the hand forming the seal, wasn’t it ? And then you intentionally took damage to lower my guard, seizing the opportunity to launch the Shadow Possession Jutsu. You’re truly impressive, Nara Shikaku. »

« But you don’t think you’ve won yet, do you ? »

« The control effect is mutual for both parties. »

« In other words, while it looks like you’ve controlled me, in reality, I’ve also controlled you. You can’t move either, can you ? Die, brat. »

The moment these words were spoken, the severed hand that Kakuzu had previously had cut off by an Anbu Ninja and dropped to the ground suddenly seemed to come alive, shooting out several black tentacles that viciously pierced toward Nara Shikaku.

Nara Shikaku did not appear panicked. The greatest purpose for which the Nara clan’s Shadow Possession Jutsu was created was to control opponents on the battlefield, allowing teammates to finish them off. As for his own safety ? At times like this, he just needed to trust his teammates.

Sure enough, although the Earth Grudge Fear attack was sudden, it posed no threat to Shikaku at all. Several surrounding Anbu Ninja immediately shielded him, using Kunai to block the piercing attacks.

« Do you understand now ? The biggest reason for your defeat in this battle is that you have no companions. Even the weakest of powers can create miracles when gathered together. »

Shikaku issued a victory declaration : « Kill him ! Use Ninjutsu ! »

« A wise judgment. Seeing that blade attacks are ineffective against me, you’ve decided to respond with Ninjutsu ? »

Kakuzu was growing more and more impressed with Nara Shikaku. It was a pity he was an enemy ; in that case, he would kill him here with his own hands.

At this moment, everyone’s attention was drawn to Kakuzu and his severed hand. A black shadow slowly emerged from the accumulated water ; it was Kakuzu’s Water Release heart.

Back when the battlefield was shrouded in steam, Kakuzu had separated his Lightning Release and Water Release hearts. The one that appeared now was the Water Release heart. It had been hiding upwind from the earlier Wind Release Ninjutsu. Because it hadn’t been affected by Wind Release : Pressure Damage, there was still a large amount of steam there, and even sensor-type Ninja could not detect this Earth Grudge Fear clone.

« Water Release : Water Curtain ! »

A powerful water cannon attack erupted from the mouth of the Water Release mask monster, striking everyone indiscriminately, including Kakuzu’s main body. Because their attention was drawn to the severed hand, by the time they noticed the attack, it was already too late to react, and it was right before their eyes.

« What ? ! »

The massive torrent swept everyone up, rushing for hundreds of meters and crashing through countless trees before finally coming to a stop in a valley.

As time passed, the torrent gradually slowed until it calmed, and a small lake had formed in the valley.

A black hand suddenly reached out from the water, breaking the surface’s calm. The owner of this hand gathered Chakra in their palm and pressed it slowly against the water surface, using the leverage to pull their body out of the water. It was none other than Kakuzu.

At the moment the water cannon struck, Nara Shikaku had been hit by the current, causing him to break the Shadow Possession Jutsu, and Kakuzu had thus freed himself.

The instant he broke free, he performed rapid hand seals, casting Earth Release : Earth Spear to harden his entire body’s skin. Not only did he withstand the Anbu Ninja’s Ninjutsu attack, but he also emerged almost completely unscathed from the impact of the torrent.

Kakuzu looked back. The area where the torrent had passed was in shambles ; countless trees had been snapped or even uprooted. Along the way, the corpses of several Konoha Ninja remained, their bones broken and limbs severed, unrecognizable.

He slowly stretched out his arm, and a severed hand, as if possessing its own consciousness, manipulated tentacles to rush toward him, automatically reattaching to the severed arm and quickly stitching the wound closed. In just a few moments, Kakuzu was back to his complete form.

« You... you guy, what... what kind of monster are you ! » Nara Shikaku was surprisingly still alive. He was currently leaning against a boulder, his left arm and left leg bent at unnatural angles ; evidently, he had been severely injured in the earlier Water Release attack.

A flash of unwillingness crossed Shikaku’s eyes. The guy before him not only possessed the immense power of a Kage-level fighter but was also extremely experienced in combat. Most importantly, this guy was a freak, definitely not an ordinary human. Even after fighting for a while, he still hadn’t gathered full intelligence on him, yet the opponent knew everything about him.

'Damn it... that was so underhanded.'

Unlike Shikaku’s resentment, when Kakuzu saw Shikaku, it was as if he were seeing a lover. He strode forward with eyes full of joy, ignoring Shikaku’s pain as he grabbed him and hauled him up from the ground, searching his body with his hands.

« Found it. Hahahaha. »

Kakuzu laughed loudly as he rummaged through Shikaku’s Ninja tool pouch and pulled out a storage scroll, tossing Shikaku aside to inspect the scroll.

After confirming that the sealing scroll indeed contained the 1.1 billion ryo fortune he had been longing for, Kakuzu could no longer hold back and laughed wildly toward the sky.

After laughing for several minutes, he was finally able to slightly control his emotions and look at Shikaku, his eyes filled with undisguised, burning murderous intent.

« Nara Shikaku, the new Assistant to the Hokage of Konoha, Minister of the Foreign Affairs Department, Head of the Nara clan, Captain of the Konoha Jonin Squad, Elite Jonin. Your black market bounty is a full 50 million ryo. »

« In consideration of you giving me so much money, I’ll give you a quick death. »

Nara Shikaku’s eyes flickered slightly ; at this moment, he was almost too weak to speak. His eyes were filled with bitterness, and several memories flashed through his mind, finally settling on the smiles of his wife and son.

'I’m sorry, Yoshino, Shikamaru. I won’t be able to join you for the picnic next weekend. I’m afraid this is the end for me...'

Kakuzu approached Shikaku, observing the opponent before him. A flash of admiration and regret passed through his eyes, finally settling on greed and satisfaction regarding the money.

'He was a decent opponent, letting me enjoy myself a little. I’ll just crush his neck with one stomp.'

« Farewell, brat ! »

Kakuzu performed hand seals to concentrate Earth Spear on his right foot. His right foot instantly turned pitch black, imbued with powerful Chakra and terrifying lethality, and he aimed it at Shikaku’s neck, stomping down hard.

At this critical moment, a small, also pitch-black foot stepped under Kakuzu’s foot, preventing him from continuing his downward stomp.

« Sorry, but the uncle in front of you is an important companion of mine. Could I ask you to lift your precious foot ? »
