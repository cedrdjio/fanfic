# Chapitre 149 — Rock Lee Can Use Genjutsu Now Too

Rock Lee felt the astonishing power radiating from Gaara and couldn’t help but look solemn.

He clenched his fists slightly, sensing the sharp pain coming from his body, and thought to himself : 'The burden of opening the Fifth Gate on my body is simply too great. It seems I must end this battle quickly.'

Thinking this, his gaze became firm once again, and he charged at Gaara without hesitation.

However, what he didn’t expect was that under the stimulation of the Tailed Beast Chakra, Gaara’s reaction nerves had been elevated to the extreme.

The opponent’s gaze was actually able to keep up with his speed, locking onto his position tightly.

Gaara let out a cold snort, lifted his arm slightly, and skillfully manipulated the sand, sweeping it toward Rock Lee.

However, the speed of the sand wave was still too slow, and it was unable to catch up to that figure moving like a gale. Rock Lee flickered repeatedly, easily dodging it.

For a moment, the battlefield fell into a subtle stalemate.

Gaara’s offensive was like a rainstorm, continuous and unending, but the surging sandstorms always missed by a hair, unable to touch a corner of his opponent’s clothes.

While Rock Lee relied on his astonishing speed to weave and dodge through the sky-filling gravel, remaining unscathed, he was also unable to break through the layers of sand barriers to get close to Gaara.

Gaara’s small face was tight, but after a moment, it suddenly relaxed, returning to his usual indifference.

He keenly captured his opponent’s flaw ; Rock Lee’s current state was clearly the result of using some kind of forbidden technique, overdrawing his body’s limits.

This approach was nothing more than a flash in the pan, destined not to last.

Thinking of this, a trace of coldness curled at the corner of his mouth, almost imperceptibly.

'The one who should really be feeling anxious is someone else, right ?'

On the other side of the arena, Rock Lee was trapped in a dilemma.

The Gaara before him was like a hedgehog ; the impenetrable defense surrounding him left him with no way to attack.

Worse still, his own stamina was nearing exhaustion. Every muscle contraction was accompanied by tearing pain, and even breathing had become increasingly heavy.

Past memories surged like a tide. The frustration of being perfectly countered and unable to fight back during his bitter battle with Neji seemed to strike him again.

But... times were different now. He had long since broken free from the shackles of only being able to fight at close range.

Even though he still couldn’t use Ninjutsu, he had already mastered methods for long-range attacks.

Taking advantage of dodging the sand wave attack again, Rock Lee decisively retreated, opened up a safe distance, and then lowered his waist and bent his knees, setting up an attack stance.

In Gaara’s stunned gaze, he suddenly spun and kicked, and the sharp wind actually turned into a crescent-shaped blue slash, whistling out.

« Storm Leg ! »

The next instant, the azure blade of light seemed to have eyes, precisely passing through the gaps in the sky-filling sand waves and striking directly at Gaara himself.

Gaara was shocked and hurriedly mobilized the sand to build a defense.

The power of the Storm Leg was astonishing, slamming heavily into the Shield of Sand, actually cutting a gash dozens of centimeters deep, nearly splitting the shield apart completely.

Seeing this, Rock Lee was reinvigorated and immediately changed tactics, kicking out several Storm Leg slash waves from different positions in succession, forcing Gaara into a state of panic and exhaustion.

Just in this flash of lightning, Rock Lee’s eyes flashed, and he decisively seized this fleeting opportunity to rush forward.

Gaara had just breathed a sigh of relief after blocking all the slash waves when Rock Lee arrived in a flash, kicking at the already scarred Shield of Sand.

This accumulated blow was swift and fierce, piercing through the Shield of Sand without any resistance, and slammed heavily into Gaara’s chest, shattering the Armor of Sand to pieces.

Just as a faint smile of victory curled at the corner of Rock Lee’s mouth, Gaara let out a low laugh first.

He actually withstood the blow, his body steady as a mountain, without moving an inch.

Immediately after, his hands reached out like iron pincers, tightly locking onto Rock Lee’s ankles, trapping him in mid-air.

Rock Lee’s pupils shrank, his face filled with disbelief.

'The same Taijutsu, yet this time it only caused him minor injuries ? No, it’s that my attacks have weakened, while the opponent has become stronger. With this trade-off, that’s why...'

He realized it now, but it was too late.

The surrounding sand waves surged instantly, winding around him like a giant python.

At this critical juncture, a trace of determination flashed in Rock Lee’s eyes, and disregarding his own safety, he curled his fingers into a sword shape and stabbed directly at Gaara’s right eye, intending to trade an injury for an injury.

« Finger Pistol ! »

Gaara was terrified and hurriedly let go of Rock Lee, his hands instinctively protecting his front.

The next instant, the sharp finger force pierced through the Armor of Sand instantly, gouging a bloody hole in his right arm, causing him to let out a scream of pain.

Rock Lee took this opportunity to stomp hard on his chest, borrowing the force to flip backward, escaping the blockade of the sand waves by a hair’s breadth.

This final kick was full of power, once again sending Gaara flying backward, tumbling miserably on the sand.

Rock Lee himself was panting, his stamina completely overdrawn. Upon landing, he stumbled and nearly fell, no longer able to maintain the Eight Gates state.

For a long time, Gaara struggled to get up. The penetrating wound on his arm was bleeding profusely, causing his face to twitch in pain.

He had always been arrogant, viewing his peers as nothing more than ants and dust, except for Asakawa Shin...

But now, he had been bitten until he was covered in blood by an ant. Shame and rage surged in his chest ; he had completely lost his composure.

His gaze swept coldly toward Rock Lee. Seeing that the opponent was unable to resist, a cruel smile couldn’t help but curl at the corner of his mouth.

He suddenly raised his hand, gripped the air in the direction of Rock Lee, and shouted sternly : « Sand Binding Coffin ! »

In an instant, a massive amount of quicksand swept over like a surging tide.

Facing this fatal blow, Rock Lee showed no panic. Instead, his gaze sharpened, his muscles tensed, and he exuded a sense of confidence :

« Iron Body ! »

Asakawa Shin patted his forehead and hurriedly winked at Might Guy.

Might Guy understood, flashed onto the arena, and with a fierce kick, instantly shattered the terrifying sand waves, successfully saving Rock Lee.

Immediately after, he turned to the referee and said rapidly : « The winner is decided. We forfeit this match ! »

Before the words fell, he had already hoisted Rock Lee up and escaped from the competition platform at the fastest speed.

Gaara stood stunned on the spot. After a moment, he suddenly came to his senses and roared in disbelief : « What kind of joke is this ? Who allowed you to leave ! »

However, Might Guy turned a deaf ear to this, only caring about carrying the unconscious Rock Lee and leaving the venue in a hurry.

Seeing this, Gaara was burning with anger. He turned and stared fiercely at the referee, his gaze so fierce it was as if he wanted to eat someone, and asked sternly :

« What kind of referee are you ? The match isn’t over yet, how could he interfere without authorization ! »

The referee nodded in agreement and declared righteously :

« That’s right, contestant Rock Lee seriously violated the competition rules, therefore I judge contestant Gaara as the winner ! »

Watching Gaara’s body trembling with extreme anger, Asakawa Shin hurriedly walked onto the arena and laughed awkwardly to smooth things over :

« Congratulations, Gaara, you won really cleanly and efficiently. »

« Clean... cleanly and efficiently ? »

Gaara shuddered. Turning back and seeing it was Asakawa Shin, the violence in his eyes finally receded slightly, and Shukaku’s Chakra dissipated quietly along with it.

He stared deeply at Asakawa Shin, and in the end, said nothing, only turning and leaving full of anger.

Asakawa Shin scratched his head awkwardly.

He was truly helpless. If he had really let Rock Lee take that hit just now, it wouldn’t have been as simple as a broken hand or foot ; it might have cost him his life.

As for the Iron Body that Rock Lee used...

What use could that thing possibly be ? To put it bluntly, it was just a Genjutsu.

'Huh ? Come to think of it, Rock Lee is also a Ninja who can use Genjutsu ? That’s truly joyous and congratulatory !'
