# Chapitre 111 — Village of the Bloody Mist

A year passed in a flash. Asakawa Shin had successfully fused the Chakra of the Ichibi and the Seven-Tails, undoubtedly taking his strength to the next level.

« Oh, oh, oh, is this Shukaku’s Magnet Release ? Being able to seal things is indeed interesting ; it’s a nice addition, I suppose. »

« But since I can freely control magnetic force, I wonder if it can be fused with Observation Haki ? »

A sharp glint flashed in his eyes as he muttered to himself.

« And with this, only the Three-Tails Isobu and Six-Tails Saiken are left among the nine Tailed Beasts. »

« Village of the Bloody Mist ? Such a hassle. It’s time to go meet them. »

For this trip to Hidden Mist, Asakawa Shin still decided to use the Transformation Technique to hide his true appearance.

Over the past year, friction between Sunagakure and Iwagakure had been constant, and Kumogakure was also showing signs of movement ; the situation was extremely delicate.

The Ninja World was now in a state of chaos, with major powers at each other’s throats, like a powder keg about to be ignited ; his identity must not be exposed at this time.

Having made up his mind, Asakawa Shin’s figure flickered, instantly crossing space to arrive at the Land of Water. As soon as he arrived, he felt the humidity in the air increase significantly ; it was truly a country located in the middle of the ocean.

After identifying his direction, he turned into a streak of light and sped straight toward the Hidden Mist Village.

The Hidden Mist Village was not by the sea but hidden among rolling mountains, shrouded in thick fog year-round, appearing mysterious and unpredictable.

Hiding outside the Hidden Mist Village, Asakawa Shin’s hands flew as he manipulated his Chakra, and in the blink of an eye, he took on the appearance of the 'Steam Ninja' Han.

He had grown increasingly fond of Boil Release ; not only did it fit perfectly with his own combat system, but even better, it could naturally shift suspicion toward Iwagakure.

With this appearance, such powerful Taijutsu, and an immense amount of Chakra, plus the signature Boil Release, even if he loudly argued he wasn’t Han himself, probably no one would believe him.

Asakawa Shin formed another seal, splitting off a Shadow Clone, and decided to move in two directions simultaneously.

His mind made up, Asakawa Shin immediately took action, silently infiltrating the Hidden Mist Village.

It had to be said that the Ninjas of the Hidden Mist Village trained exceptionally hard ; within his perception range, the Ninjas in the Village were either sweating in the training grounds or diligently studying at home.

This was normal, after all, the performance pressure in the Village of the Bloody Mist was quite high.

The figure in front of Asakawa Shin seemed to be the same ; his brow was furrowed as if thinking about something, and cold sweat even dripped from his forehead, so much so that he didn’t notice Asakawa Shin behind him for a long time.

This person was the Hidden Mist Jonin, Ao. This guy had taken a Byakugan from the Hyuga Main House during past wars and possessed the perceptive and X-ray vision abilities of the Byakugan.

This posed a huge threat to Asakawa Shin’s actions, so he was listed as the first target for elimination.

It wasn’t until Asakawa Shin suddenly struck, gouging out his Byakugan with lightning speed, that Ao woke up as if from a dream, hurriedly enduring the intense pain and using the Body Replacement Technique to pull away.

But this was only because Asakawa Shin intentionally let him go ; otherwise, with his strength, how could there be a chance to escape ?

At this moment, Ao suppressed the horror in his heart, quickly regrouped, and struck a defensive stance.

But when he finally saw the face of the attacker, he couldn’t help but cry out in shock : « You... you are the 'Steam Ninja' Han ? Why are you here, and why did you snatch my Byakugan ? »

Asakawa Shin glanced at him coldly, the corner of his mouth curling into a mocking arc, and said indifferently :

« Asking such nonsense. Everyone in the Ninja World knows the Byakugan has immense strategic value, but the difficulty of obtaining it is also nearly impossible. »

« However, since it’s hard to strike at Konoha’s side, wouldn’t it be fine to strike from your side ? After all, you don’t have the Caged Bird~ »

« See, didn’t I just get it easily ? »

Ao’s face turned pale. He had shouted so loudly just now, yet there was no reaction from outside ; clearly, those guards had already been dealt with silently.

Fortunately, his distress signal had finally been sent successfully, and reinforcements would soon...

But just then, a voice ruthlessly shattered Ao’s fantasy.

« Oh ? The distress signal was finally sent out ? You’re really slow ; I was getting impatient waiting. »

Asakawa Shin spread his hands like a little bear, shrugging helplessly as if complaining that Ao had wasted a lot of his time.

« Na... Nani ? »

Ao’s remaining eye almost popped out, but unfortunately, he would never have the chance to speak again.

Because in the next moment, an armored warrior spraying white steam had already lunged forward at high speed. A simple elbow strike to his chest sent him flying like a cannonball, instantly smashing through several houses and kicking up a cloud of dust.

Not until the dust cleared did the reinforcements Ao had been waiting for finally arrive late. The leader had green hair and purple eyes, a scar under his left eye, and a face and body like a child ; it was the Fourth Mizukage, Yagura Karatachi.

At this moment, the accompanying medical Ninja stood before Ao with a face full of rage, quickly assessing Ao’s injuries, and couldn’t help but shake his head and sigh :

« The chest has been completely crushed, even the internal organs are gone... he’s beyond saving. »

« More importantly, the Byakugan in his right eye has also disappeared without a trace ! »

Hearing his subordinate’s report, Yagura seemed to have no emotional reaction and gave orders very flatly : « Seal the Village and find the enemy ! »

The sensory Ninja was about to start investigating upon hearing this, when a low murmur suddenly came from a distance : « Water Release : Hiding in Mist Technique ! »

As soon as the voice fell, a large amount of mist appeared out of thin air, swallowing the entire Village in an instant.

The fog was so thick it wouldn’t disperse, like a heavy veil obscuring all vision. Not only that, the mist was full of Chakra reactions, hindering the sensory Ninja’s investigation.

Asakawa Shin took the opportunity to speed away into the distance ; to ensure they wouldn’t truly lose track of him, he even intentionally lowered his speed.

Yagura was completely unmoved, his hands flying through seals as he shouted lightly : « Wind Release : Passing Typhoon ! »

In an instant, a gale rose, tearing holes in the thick fog covering the Village, and the view ahead suddenly became clear.

The sensory Ninja immediately caught something unusual and pointed in a direction with a grave expression, reporting urgently :

« There ! Everyone, be careful, the enemy’s Chakra amount is exceptionally massive. A level like this... it’s very likely they are also a Tailed Beast Jinchuriki ! »

A nearby Ninja roared angrily : « So what if he’s a Jinchuriki ? The Byakugan is a vital strategic resource for the Village and must not be lost. »

For a moment, everyone’s eyes were fixed on Yagura, waiting for his decision.

Yagura, however, acted like an NPC, ordering expressionlessly : « All Anbu, follow me in pursuit. We must not let him leave with the Byakugan ! »

« Yes, Mizukage-sama ! »

After everyone left, a beautiful figure stepped slowly out from the shadows of a house.

She had dazzling red hair, a full and tall figure, and exuded the unique charm of a mature woman ; it was Mei Terumī.

She stood silently by Ao’s body, looking at that face that died without closing its eyes, full of resentment, and a trace of sorrow couldn’t help but appear in her eyes.

She slowly knelt down, reached out her slender fingers, and gently closed his eyes for him.

Then, she stood up, turned to the subordinates behind her, and sternly ordered them to ensure the body was properly handled.

After giving the instructions, she didn’t stay any longer, her figure flickering as she sped off in the direction Yagura had gone.

Meanwhile, on the outskirts of the Hidden Mist Village, at the secret base where the Six-Tails Jinchuriki Utakata was located.

Asakawa Shin’s Shadow Clone did not hide its tracks and stood boldly in front of Utakata.

The Anbu Ninjas who were originally responsible for guarding and monitoring were now all lying on the ground, blood staining the floor.

Utakata gripped his Ninja tool, staring at the uninvited guest with a face full of vigilance, questioning sharply : « Who are you ? How dare you intrude into the Hidden Mist Village. »

Asakawa Shin just glanced at him indifferently, then as if sensing something, muttered in a slightly lazy tone :

« I see, are you also a Tailed Beast Jinchuriki ? For the sake of our shared plight, I’ll spare your life for now, kid. »

Before his voice even faded, he stopped paying attention to the stunned Utakata, turned to select a direction, and turned into an afterimage, speeding toward the outskirts of the Village.

Leaving Utakata dazed on the spot, however, he quickly reacted, a flash of joy in his eyes as he whispered :

« Although I don’t understand the situation, isn’t now the best time for me to defect ? Heaven is truly helping me... »
