# Chapitre 55 — Blue Language

Since both contestants had suffered severe injuries—especially Hyuga Neji, whose kidneys on both sides were heavily damaged—the match was temporarily suspended. Shin, the commentator, stepped in to act as a temporary team medic.

While treating Neji, cold sweat broke out on Shin’s forehead, and he couldn’t help but curse inwardly :

'That kid Lee fights way too dirty. To specifically target the opponent’s kidneys... he really has no martial ethics. He even managed to crack them...'

No wonder he felt Neji’s final attack seemed hesitant and lacked decisiveness. Anyone in that situation would feel immense pressure, wouldn’t they ?

Fortunately, it was Shin who was treating Neji. After all, Shin’s Chakra was filled with vigorous life force, making his healing effects far superior to those of an ordinary Medical Ninja.

Thinking of this, Shin felt a bit relieved and proud. Otherwise, he really worried that Neji would be left with some undetectable hidden injury.

Then again, maybe it didn’t matter ? After all, Neji never got to use them before he died anyway, right ?

Lee’s injuries were much easier to handle. He just needed to disperse the foreign Chakra injected into Lee’s body and then use the Mystical Palm Technique to heal him.

In a short while, Lee was able to walk around, but he still needed a period of rest. It seemed it would be difficult for him to continue fighting for now.

Shin looked at Lee, who was doing rehabilitation exercises, filled with emotion. Who would have thought that the final winner would be him ?

Even if he couldn’t perform Ninjutsu or Genjutsu, Neji still had a higher chance of winning compared to Lee.

The Gentle Fist Art was practically flawless in its early stages, especially when facing Taijutsu-type Ninjas ; it held an overwhelming advantage.

He just hadn’t expected Lee to seize an opportunity to fiercely attack Neji’s kidneys, directly shattering Neji’s will to fight...

To be able to calculate to this extent, you’re truly terrifying, Lee !

Watching the figure of his 'little brother' Neji being carried away on a stretcher, Shin felt this scene was somewhat familiar...

He couldn’t remember... whatever...

He still had a match to commentate...

...

« Alright, the match continues. »

« First, let us thank Hyuga Neji and Rock Lee for bringing us such a wonderful match ! »

« Also, congratulations to Rock Lee for advancing to the next round of the finals ! »

« So, with that, the first round of the boys' elimination matches is completely over ! »

« Next is the girls' match, and these are the only two participants in the girls' division ! »

« Let us welcome contestant number 5, Hyuga Hinata, and contestant number 6, Sakura Haruno, to the stage ! »

...

« Hehe, is it finally my turn ? » Hyuga Hinata gave a confident smile, walking towards the center of the training field with light, graceful steps.

Sakura Haruno swallowed hard, feeling a bit nervous. To have to fight a monster like Hinata—it would be a lie to say she wasn’t anxious.

After all, Hinata was known as 'Little Shin.' Over the past month, she had witnessed Hinata’s growth with her own eyes.

'But so what ? For Sasuke-kun, I’ll dare to do anything !'

...

Seeing that both of them had completed the Seal of Confrontation, Might Guy didn’t waste words and announced the official start of the match with a wave of his arm !

As the referee made the announcement, Sakura Haruno’s feet lightly tapped the ground as she unhesitatingly pulled back to create distance from Hinata, observing Hinata’s every move with a solemn expression.

'Eh ? Why did Hinata’s eyes suddenly light up ? Is this some ability of the Byakugan ?'

While Sakura was wary and puzzled, Hinata’s melodious yet loud voice reached her ears :

« Why pull back ? If you truly want to hit me, you should get closer to launch an attack, shouldn’t you ? »

« Wha... what ? » Sakura was startled by the sudden question. Before she could react, she heard Hinata continue :

« Combat distance only has meaning when the opponents' strengths are equal. »

« Therefore, the combat distance between you and me carries no meaning at all. »

When the audience suddenly heard Hinata’s arrogant speech, the scene instantly erupted into a buzz of discussion :

« As expected of Lady Hinata ! That aura is simply invincible—confident and bold ! »

« For a Hyuga genius, you have to look at Hinata. What even was that Neji ? »

« Heh, hearing those words, I instantly feel like she’s on a completely different level from the other contestants ! »

« Forget the level, even the art style has changed ! »

...

Asakawa Shin’s eyelids twitched as he listened. He still remembered when Hinata used to pester him with a small notebook. Out of impatience, he had casually brushed her off with a few 'Aizen-style' quotes.

He hadn’t expected Hinata to record those words verbatim and now use them so flexibly in such a public setting.

This 'Hachi-Hinata' really has initiative ; she truly learns the good stuff...

...

On the field, Sakura had realized that Hinata was mocking her with that arrogant display, which made her fume with rage :

'Dammit, Sasuke-kun is still watching ! If this continues, won’t I become a clown like Neji ?

No, I can’t let this go on. Even if I lose, I have to show my style !'

Thinking of this, she glared at Hinata, her feet suddenly exerting force as she charged forward like an arrow released from a bow :

« You... there’s a limit to how much you can look down on people ! »

« Take this, Hinata ! »

As Hinata watched Sakura’s determined figure rushing toward her, instead of fear, a flicker of gratified amusement crossed her eyes :

'Shin’s Taunting Art really is useful ! I’ll have to review that « Shin’s Quotations » book again when I get home today...'

'The way of the poser is truly profound ; I gain new insights every time I watch !'

In the time it took Hinata to think, Sakura had already arrived like a gale, throwing a powerful straight punch aimed directly at Hinata’s face.

« Good timing, Sakura ! »

Hinata turned her body slightly, dodging with agility. The surging wind from the punch brushed past her hair as she easily evaded the strike.

Sakura was not discouraged, her fists and palms flying like dancing cherry blossoms as she repeatedly attacked Hinata.

Unexpectedly, Hinata was like a white butterfly fluttering among petals, her posture light and elegant, always causing Sakura’s attacks to miss.

In the spectator stands, Ino frowned. Although Sakura’s offensive was fierce, she failed to land a hit repeatedly. Most terrifying was that Hinata hadn’t even used her Byakugan yet, and she had already completely seen through Sakura’s offensive.

The gap between the two was simply too large, like an adult playing with a child. If Hinata wanted to, she could probably end this match in an instant.

Sure enough...

« Sakura, you’ve played enough, haven’t you ? I’m starting to feel a bit bored. »

« So, let’s end this. »

Hinata lowered her head and bent her waist, dodging Sakura’s swing at the very last second.

Simultaneously, she lunged forward like a leopard, using her shoulder as a weapon to execute a 'Mountain Lean,' slamming heavily into Sakura’s abdomen.

The power of this strike was immense, actually sending Sakura flying. She traced an arc in the air before falling heavily to the ground, rolling a few times before lying there motionless.

« Referee, announce the result. »

After speaking, Hinata ignored the crowd and turned to walk away.

Just then, a sudden uproar broke out among the spectators. Hinata’s departing steps paused, and she turned around with a look of surprise...

She saw that Sakura had already stood up, looking wretched and disheveled. She hadn’t even wiped the blood from the corner of her mouth. Her eyes were wide, her face full of anger, as she stared intently at Hinata.

Seeing this, Hinata couldn’t help but give a faint smile. « Oh ? This is... now this is getting interesting... »
