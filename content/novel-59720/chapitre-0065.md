# Chapitre 65 — Morning Peacock

« The sixth gate, the Gate of View, is it ? This is starting to get interesting. Bring it on, Guy ! »

Seeing Might Guy finally showing his true abilities, Asakawa Shin felt a surge of interest.

Might Guy, however, stared intently at Asakawa Shin, not daring to relax for even a second, his mind churning :

'Shin is so calm even after opening the Gate of Wonder ? It’s almost as if he’s in the same state I am when I open the third gate, the Gate of Life...'

'This physical strength is way too abnormal, right ? Is this even the Eight Gates I know ?'

'Is the Eight Gates I’ve been practicing not authentic ?'

'No... that’s impossible ! Anyway, let me test him first...'

« Take this, Shin ! »

Having made up his mind, Might Guy suddenly dashed forward like a cheetah, rushing towards Asakawa Shin.

He leaped up while running, then spun violently in mid-air, unleashing a powerful spinning kick from above with the force of a tornado.

« Konoha Strong Whirlwind ! »

This move was like Mount Tai pressing down, unstoppable !

Before the attack even landed, a powerful gale swept over. Facing this astonishing attack, Asakawa Shin remained calm and composed, standing firm like a rock, completely unmoved.

He calmly gathered Busoshoku Haki into his right arm and casually blocked it above his head, intending to take the blow head-on.

Soon, the attack arrived as expected. Two powerful forces collided, stirring up a violent storm that swept in all directions.

The rock ground beneath Asakawa Shin’s feet cracked like a spider web, as if it could hardly withstand the immense pressure.

However, Asakawa Shin’s expression remained calm, and his right arm was as steady as if it were cast from iron, not wavering in the slightest from this powerful kick.

On the contrary, Might Guy’s pupils contracted, his face full of disbelief. He felt as if he had kicked a piece of divine iron ; not only did he fail to cause any damage, but the recoil alone made his right foot ache.

He quickly pulled back, staring at Asakawa Shin’s pitch-black right arm, and said in disbelief :

« Shin, is this the Earth Spear technique ? Can you actually perform Ninjutsu while in the state of the seventh gate ? How did you do that ? »

Asakawa Shin smiled modestly, shook his head gently, and patiently explained to Might Guy : « Guy, this isn’t Ninjutsu, but pure Taijutsu ! »

« I call it 'Busoshoku Haki Infusion'. It’s a technique that only I can practice and cannot be taught to others. »

« As for Ninjutsu, the chaotic Chakra flow in the Gate of Wonder state is far too violent. Even with my control, I currently find it difficult to fully master it in the Gate of Wonder state, let alone use it to perform Ninjutsu. »

Might Guy suddenly realized ; no wonder he didn’t see Asakawa Shin forming hand seals just now. So it was actually a Taijutsu technique !

Just imagining it, one could tell how powerful this move could be in battle, especially when combined with the Eight Gates, it would be even more formidable. It was a pity this move couldn’t be taught to others, otherwise, he could have tried it out on Kakashi.

And the fact that Asakawa Shin could develop such an inconceivable Taijutsu meant he could no longer be viewed by the common sense of the Ninja World. Although he had repeatedly raised his evaluation of him in his heart, he didn’t expect that he had still underestimated him.

'To be able to take my Konoha Strong Whirlwind so lightly while in the state of the sixth gate, let alone testing Shin’s limits, I can’t even make him take me seriously...'

'And having opened the Gate of View for so long, I feel my body heating up. Since that’s the case... then the warm-up... should be over !'

Might Guy didn’t hesitate any longer. He gave Asakawa Shin a thumbs-up and flashed a brilliant smile :

« Shin, it seems that in my sixth gate state, wanting to fight you is a bit unrealistic. So, I’m going to go all out ! Eight Gates Formation ! Seventh Gate, Gate of Wonder, Open ! »

As Might Guy executed this move, the green steam on his body instantly transformed into blue steam.

The airflow formed by Chakra surged violently, ruthlessly blowing away the surrounding gravel.

His momentum soared at this moment, as if the entire training ground was trembling because of his power.

« Shin, the move I’m about to perform is called 'Morning Peacock' ! This was originally a powerful Taijutsu move after opening the sixth gate, but in my current sixth gate state, I’m afraid it won’t be able to pose a threat to you. Therefore, I’ve decided to open the Gate of Wonder to use this technique. Please advise ! »

After speaking, Might Guy’s figure erupted forward, his fists striking violently at Asakawa Shin like a storm. The speed of his punches was so fast that they frictioned intensely with the air, igniting raging flames, which gathered at the front of the shockwave erupted from his straight punches, turning into plumes of peacock feathers that burned the sky and scorched the earth, surging towards Asakawa Shin.

Its posture was like a peacock spreading its tail, dazzling and extremely beautiful. However, behind this ultimate beauty lay a deadly murderous intent.

An attack in the Gate of View state was already enough to threaten ordinary Kage-level powerhouses, and now, with Might Guy performing this technique while the Gate of Wonder was fully open, its power was undoubtedly even greater !

Seeing this powerful attack, Asakawa Shin was not afraid but happy. He also set up an attack stance and shouted to Might Guy : « Guy, I also have a move that is very similar to your Morning Peacock. It just so happens we can have a contest ! Flying Finger Pistol : Hundred Birds Facing the Phoenix ! »

Asakawa Shin put his fingers together like a sword, coated his hands with Busoshoku Haki, and violently stabbed into the void like lightning, actually triggering waves of air that looked like flaming birds, rushing head-on towards the Morning Peacock one after another.

The two collided violently in mid-air, and the resulting high-temperature airflow was like a flame storm, sweeping wildly in all directions.

Asakawa Shin and Might Guy ignored it completely. They only had eyes for each other, just blindly punching at each other, wanting to completely crush the other’s attack !

« Ora Ora Ora ! »

« Muda Muda Muda ! »

...

The two powerful attacks stood against each other in the air, and for a while, it was hard to tell who was better.

However, in the end, Asakawa Shin’s Hundred Birds Facing the Phoenix was superior, and the barrage of flames gradually approached Might Guy.

Although Might Guy gritted his teeth and swung his fists desperately, he still couldn’t resist this fierce offensive.

Finally, the attack of the flying birds completely disintegrated the defense of the Morning Peacock, and countless blazing firebirds bombarded him like a surging tide.

Asakawa Shin also stopped punching at this moment, a hint of amusement involuntarily appearing on his face. His Observation Haki had already seen the future, and the predicted result made him shake his head involuntarily.

On the other side, Might Guy’s expression was solemn. Everywhere his eyes could see were raging flames and bombarding flying birds ; it seemed he had nowhere to hide.

But he suddenly burst into a brilliant smile, his white teeth emitting a dazzling light in the reflection of the fire. Then, with all his might, he leaped up, actually using Moon Walk to stylishly escape in mid-air, his happy laughter still echoing in the air :

« Haha, Shin, how do you think I used this Moon Walk ? Did I get some of your true teachings ? »
