# Chapitre 82 — Bewilderment

« That golden twin-tail ! That 'Gambling' character ! And that incomparably magnificent bosom ! It’s absolutely her... »

« It’s her ! She’s here ! »

« Quick, go call the boss ! Tell him she’s here, the legendary ~ Big Fat Sheep is here ! ! ! »

As soon as these words were spoken, the casino instantly exploded into chaos, and the sound of colliding tables and chairs was incessant.

Gamblers dropped their chips and discarded their cards, rushing over from all directions. They pushed and shoved, standing on tiptoe to look, eyes filled with curiosity and shock, terrified of missing this chance to see the legendary figure in person.

« Lady Tsunade, please, this way to the VIP private room. »

« Many thanks for your care—oh no, I mean, welcome, welcome~ »

« Hurry, where are the servers ? Bring tea ! »

Asakawa Shin followed behind Tsunade, clicking his tongue in wonder.

Is this the prestige of the « legendary Big Fat Sheep » ? It’s actually this exaggerated~

Tsunade’s expression sank slightly, and a hint of displeasure rose in her heart. With these people welcoming her so enthusiastically, how could she not see through the trickery ?

They were clearly harboring ulterior motives, planning to set up a rigged game later to swindle her money !

It was hateful that she couldn’t see through their methods, which was why they were so arrogant.

However...

Feeling the heavy sealing scroll in her bosom, her mood brightened again.

'Shin is really rich. He actually gave me 100 million ryo straight up. He’s much better than that useless Jiraiya. He’s always slacking off, looking for some Child of Prophecy, and doesn’t even write his novels anymore. Now, there’s no oil left to squeeze out of him. Sigh !'

Tsunade slowly took out the sealing scroll from the cleavage between her breasts and slapped it onto the empty space.

With a « poof, » the smoke curled and dispersed, and a small mountain made of coins appeared before their eyes.

She waved her hand heroically :

« Go, exchange all these 100 million ryo for chips. Today, I’m going to slaughter everyone ! »

Seeing this, the casino boss smiled until his eyes narrowed into slits, waving his hands obsequiously.

Soon, Tsunade’s front was piled high with various chips of different denominations.

She smiled with satisfaction. However, when she turned around and saw that Asakawa Shin still hadn’t made a move, she couldn’t help but feel a hint of curiosity :

« Junior Brother, why haven’t you gone to exchange for chips yet ? Didn’t you say you wanted to play a few rounds together ? »

Asakawa Shin picked up a single 100-ryo chip, the smallest denomination, from the mountain of chips in front of Tsunade, flicking his fingertips to make it shimmer.

« Senior Sister, just lend me this 100 ryo. Once I win some money, I will definitely repay you a hundredfold. »

Tsunade rolled her eyes at him with a charming look : « Then Senior Sister will just wait and see. »

The dealer next to them bowed slightly and reminded them in a low voice : « Sir, the minimum bet in this private room is 10,000 ryo. I’m afraid 100 ryo won’t be enough to enter the game. However, out of respect for Lady Tsunade, I can take the liberty of gifting you ten 10,000-ryo chips as a welcoming gift. »

« No need. »

Asakawa Shin interrupted coldly, a trace of displeasure flashing across his brow.

What’s wrong with this guy ? Does he think I’m short of 100,000 ryo ? Isn’t this ruining my chance to show off ?

He turned back to Tsunade and winked playfully : « Senior Sister, you play here for a while, I’ll be right back. »

After speaking, he left the VIP room while playing with the 100-ryo chip.

After walking out the door, he clearly noticed that the surrounding environment had become smoky and noisy.

After wandering for a moment, he decided to play dice. This game had attractive odds, and each round was extremely fast, which suited him perfectly.

He sat down at the dice table. A gambler next to him saw that he was so young and couldn’t help but sneer :

« Little kid, are you even weaned yet ? Coming here to play this ? Do you want Uncle to help you find your mommy ? Hahahaha. »

Asakawa Shin glanced at him indifferently, a cold light flashing in his eyes, but he didn’t speak, just decisively placed his bet :

« One, two, three—six points, small. »

The moment the dice cup was opened, it was exactly as Asakawa Shin had predicted. The corners of his mouth turned up slightly, revealing a confident smile :

« Looks like my luck is good today. Guessed them all right, my capital has multiplied several times over. Next, all-in on two, four, six—twelve points, big. »

Following the dealer’s actions, the mystery was revealed, and Asakawa Shin had once again accurately predicted the result.

The dealer’s face turned a bit ugly ; it was clear he had run into a tough opponent.

He hurriedly gave a look to the waiter nearby. The waiter understood and quietly left.

Next, Asakawa Shin guessed correctly several more times in a row, and his chips quickly swelled to over a million ryo.

The dealer’s hands were now trembling uncontrollably.

Many people around noticed Asakawa Shin’s extraordinary skill and followed suit with their bets. Even the middle-aged gambler who had been rude to Asakawa Shin earlier joined in.

The chips in front of him surged from a few thousand ryo to 300,000 ryo, which made him overjoyed, and he felt a bit lightheaded for a moment.

Asakawa Shin’s eyes darted slightly. He casually picked up a 10,000-ryo chip and toyed with it, then pushed all the remaining chips forward and shouted in a deep voice :

« All-in, one, two, three—six points, small. »

The surrounding gamblers saw this and rubbed their hands, following suit without hesitation, going all-in again.

However, the points revealed were three, five, six—fourteen points, big, which was completely beyond everyone’s expectations.

After a brief moment of shock, the dealer wiped the cold sweat from his forehead and burst into wild laughter toward the sky :

« I won ! I won ! Hahaha... »

In stark contrast to his reaction were the gamblers around him, who completely couldn’t believe what was happening before their eyes.

Especially the gambler who had just spoken rudely to Asakawa Shin. He had been so confident just a moment ago, betting all his chips, but now he had lost everything.

He sat there blankly, dazed, suddenly clutching his chest. His body began to twitch uncontrollably, and it looked like he wasn’t going to make it.

A cold sneer couldn’t help but rise in Asakawa Shin’s heart. Seriously ? With such mental fortitude, he dared to be rude ? Truly pathetic.

Sigh, I thought I could play for a while longer, who knew this guy would be so useless.

Whatever, I’ll be merciful and forgive you this once.

He toyed with the only remaining 10,000-ryo chip, thinking to himself :

'So, what should I play next ?'

Meanwhile, Tsunade was sitting at the gambling table, sweating profusely.

In a short time, nearly half of the chips in front of her were already gone. She shouted as if she didn’t believe in bad luck :

« I bet big ! »

« One, four, four—nine points, small~ »

« Big ! »

« Small~ »

Shizune couldn’t bear to watch from the side.

Her eyes rolled, and she leaned into Tsunade’s ear and whispered :

« Lady Tsunade, why don’t you just bet 10,000 ryo each time, and give me some chips so I can try betting the opposite ? »

Tsunade glared at her with dissatisfaction, but still grabbed a handful of chips and handed them to Shizune.

Tsunade continued to bet : « I bet big ! »

Shizune hurriedly pushed all the chips out, betting them all on small.

« One, three, five—nine points, small. »

Shizune cheered : « Yay, I won. »

...

By the time Asakawa Shin came in with 100 million ryo in chips, he found the atmosphere at the table very strange.

Tsunade’s front was already empty, while the chips in front of Shizune were piled like a mountain.

But their expressions were surprisingly identical, both looking very bewildered.

It was normal for Tsunade to be in a period of confusion, doubting life, but how could Shizune also...

Asakawa Shin couldn’t help but ask curiously : « Sister Shizune, what happened to you ? »

Shizune looked bewildered, her lips slightly pursed, her voice carrying a bit of helplessness and grievance :

« Lady Tsunade has already lost all her chips, and she isn’t betting anymore. I don’t know which side I should bet on ! I... I actually don’t know how to play this at all~ »
