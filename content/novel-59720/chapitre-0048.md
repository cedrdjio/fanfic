# Chapitre 48 — Business Rivals

Asakawa Shin looked at Kenji with a somewhat indescribable expression. It was just spreading some medicine ; why was he so proud ? How could his own father be such a dunderhead ?

And shouldn’t they wait outside after spreading the medicine ? These two really have no common sense at all !

However, even though his urge to complain was nearly at its limit, they were his parents after all, so Shin forced himself to endure and didn’t let it out.

He waved toward the door, signaling for Kana and her daughter to come in, then turned back to introduce them to the bewildered Kenji and Hanako.

« Mom, Dad, I picked these two up outside. I saw they were homeless and quite pitiful, so I took them in as my maids. »

« I was thinking that if I buy a large mansion, it won’t do to have no one taking care of the daily chores. Plus, if I move there, I’ll need someone to look after my meals and daily life. »

Kenji and Hanako nodded understandingly. Their son was no longer an ordinary person, so having two maids was perfectly reasonable.

Hearing that Shin was moving out made Hanako feel a bit reluctant, but not overly so ; after all, it was so close that he could come back anytime.

Next, Shin introduced them to each other. Karin very lively and proactively greeted Uncle Kenji and Aunt Hanako, but Kana acted a bit strangely.

She had heard Shin and the others' conversation while outside the door, and after entering the room, she also smelled that thick scent.

This caused her pretty face to flush red, and she couldn’t help but steal a glance at Hanako. To her surprise, Hanako happened to be looking over as well. The two women’s eyes met, and both looked flustered, quickly lowering their heads.

Hanako cursed inwardly, all because of that big idiot Kenji, making her so indulged in carnal pleasures that she couldn’t pull herself away. This was truly embarrassing !

Kana was even more terrified. She knew too much ; would she be... ?

Fortunately, Asakawa Shin had things to do later. After the greetings, he quickly led them away, which allowed all the adults present to breathe a sigh of relief.

Given Hanako’s personality, she would have definitely kept Shin and the others there for dinner and to stay the night, but the current situation was clearly inappropriate.

After seeing them off, Hanako quickly opened the windows for ventilation and ordered Kenji to clean the room and destroy the evidence.

Asakawa Shin, of course, was unaware of everyone’s psychological activities. He enthusiastically took the two women to buy a large mansion.

Since the location in the village center was so good, there were naturally no new houses available. Asakawa Shin found a real estate agency, wanting to see if there were any suitable second-hand properties for sale.

When the real estate agency manager heard that Asakawa Shin wanted to buy a house, she immediately put down her work and came over to receive him personally amidst the resentful gazes of many sales associates.

With a kind smile on her face, she crouched down to eye level with Asakawa Shin and spoke in a gentle tone as if coaxing a small child.

« Lord Shin, I am the sales manager of the agency. You can just call me Airi. What kind of house are you looking for ? You can tell me all your requirements. »

Although Asakawa Shin felt it was a bit strange, he simply assumed the sales manager had a good service attitude.

« I want a large detached villa, preferably close to the Ninja Academy, the kind that can be sold immediately. »

Airi had a general idea after hearing this. « We do indeed have a property that meets your requirements right now, but the price is a bit on the expensive side. However... »

She looked around, then leaned in close to Shin’s ear and whispered.

« The owner of the house is the royal Cloth Merchant for the Daimyo of the Land of Fire, but I heard his company is having some minor issues and he urgently needs funds for cash flow. »

« If you can pay the full amount in cash immediately, you should be able to easily get it at a low price. »

Asakawa Shin looked at Airi with some surprise. Could there really be such a coincidence in the world ?

Originally, he didn’t care about the price ; he just wanted to buy it as quickly as possible. But since it was a business rival, that was another matter entirely. He had to show some 'respect' after all.

« Your name is Airi, right ? I’m not very familiar with property prices, so I’ll leave this matter to you. »

« Of course, I won’t let you work for nothing. No matter how much you can knock the price down, one-tenth of the savings will be your commission. »

Airi’s eyes lit up after hearing this. This large villa was the crown jewel listed with their company, with an asking price as high as eighty million Ryo. There was so much room for negotiation here.

« Lord Shin, please wait a moment in the lounge. I’ll go take care of this right away. Just wait for my good news ! »

She left with full fighting spirit after saying that.

Airi did not disappoint Asakawa Shin. In less than an hour, she returned with her head held high like a victorious hen.

« Lord Shin, I used all my persuasive powers to beat the price down. Originally, he listed it for eighty million Ryo, and according to our company’s appraisal, that price was reasonable and appropriate. »

« But now, it’s not seventy million, not sixty million, but only fifty million Ryo. There’s only one requirement : the seller wants the full amount in cash and a quick delivery. »

« Congratulations, Lord Shin. When he bought that house before, it cost more than fifty million, not to mention the huge amount he spent on renovations. »

« If you were to do it all yourself, the total would definitely exceed one hundred million Ryo. »

Asakawa Shin was also very happy. He didn’t care whether he spent more or less ; he just wanted his business rival to suffer.

« You did well, Airi. So, when is the transaction ? I have things to do later. »

Airi immediately stood up to lead the way for Asakawa Shin. « Lord Shin, the Cloth Merchant is already waiting for you in the villa. This way, please... »

Inside the villa’s reception room, the Cloth Merchant was somewhat surprised to see that the buyer was a child. However, he didn’t care ; now he only thought about getting the money quickly to solve his company’s financial crisis.

Asakawa Shin was also very satisfied. The villa was even better than he had imagined. It truly lived up to the high cost of its renovations ; besides being magnificent, it was also elegant and tasteful. After a simple cleaning, it would be ready for move-in.

Under the supervision of the agency, the two signed their respective contracts and exchanged them. The Cloth Merchant also breathed a sigh of relief after signing everything. With this money, his company should be saved now, right ?

Only then did he relax and check the buyer’s signature. Three words were written clearly upon it—Asakawa Shin.

Suddenly seeing the name that made him grit his teeth in hatred, he was completely stunned. The buyer was actually him ?

Just as he was about to show a furious expression, he saw Asakawa Shin take fifty million Ryo out of a sealing scroll. It took two large suitcases to hold that much money.

So he decisively changed his face, accepting the money with a smile. Exactly—how could a grown man like himself get angry at a child ?

Asakawa Shin didn’t care about him. After handing over the money, he ordered the Cloth Merchant, « The contract is finalized, so this villa is mine now. Now take your money and get the hell out of my villa. »

Watching the Cloth Merchant leave happily, Asakawa Shin gave a cold smile. 'Business rivals really are idiots. He actually dared to take this money. The next step is to expand production and crush you completely.'

Airi also received her reward after her colleagues left, a full three million Ryo. This made her so happy she could hardly contain herself.

« Lord Shin, thank you for your trust. If you have any other orders, just leave them to me. I have quite a bit of experience in real estate. »

Adhering to the principle of not letting a resource go to waste, Asakawa Shin pointed to Kana beside him.

« Then I’ll trouble you. I need you to hire a trustworthy cleaning company to clean the villa inside and out. Sheets, bedding, pots, pans, and other items also need to be entirely replaced. Discuss with Kana-san how to handle these things. »

« For now, clean up two clean rooms first. We’ll need them tonight. »

After saying that, Shin patted Karin’s little head. « Rest well tonight. We’ll go to the Ninja Academy to report together tomorrow. »
