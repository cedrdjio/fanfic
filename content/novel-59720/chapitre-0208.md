# Chapitre 208 — A New World

« Impossible ? »

A playful arc curled at the corners of Asakawa Shin’s lips.

Originally, this was indeed a fantasy, but he had secretly dispatched White Zetsu to disguise themselves as Kumogakure Ninjas, fanning the flames in the shadows and driving the narrative to a frenzy.

Moreover, Kumogakure had suffered heavy casualties in that battle, and the entire Village was almost reduced to ruins.

Without a doubt, a heavyweight high-ranking official was needed to take the fall for this.

Now, Kumogakure was already in a state of chaos, just waiting for Yugito, the former Raikage candidate, to return and ignite that final fuse.

Seeing that Yugito was still full of suspicion, Asakawa Shin spread his hands helplessly : « Fine, I’ll just send you back then. »

Before his voice had even faded, his figure moved like lightning, a sharp sword qi repelling the Wood Clone, and he immediately used Shunshin to transport himself and Yugito to a hidden forest on the border of the Land of Lightning.

Before Yugito could even steady herself, a flying thunder god kunai engraved with the Flying Thunder God formula was stuffed into her arms.

« Take this. If those old fogies in Kumogakure dare to make things difficult for you, just inject Chakra into it, and I will be there instantly. »

Yugito clutched the Kunai tightly. Although the clouds of doubt in her heart had not fully dissipated, the fact that she could still live after having her Tailed Beast extracted...

Such an incredible thing was clearly inseparable from Asakawa Shin’s full support.

Her eyes darted around, and in the end, she still tucked the Kunai close to her body. As her fingertips touched the cold metal, she felt a strange sense of confidence.

« Th... thank you... » she stammered, her voice trembling slightly.

Hearing this thank-you, which was much more sincere than before, a faint smile finally rose to Asakawa Shin’s lips.

« No need for words, as long as you are safe. Go back quickly. »

Yugito gave him a long, deep look, then turned and strode away without looking back.

« What a truly ill-fated and disaster-prone miserable person. » Asakawa Shin watched her receding back and let out a soft sigh.

As for where those disasters actually came from ?

Well, don’t you worry about that.

...

Meanwhile, the temporarily constructed Raikage’s office was already filled with talented people, and the atmosphere was heavy.

Killer Bee slammed his palm onto the desk, making the documents rustle loudly.

His voice was like a resonant bell, his eyes wide with anger : « Enough ! Yugito is definitely not the kind of person who fears death ! Stop mentioning this matter ; I will absolutely not take over the position of Raikage ! »

Advisor Dodai was sweating profusely and repeatedly tried to dissuade him : « Lord Killer Bee, now that Lady Yugito’s fate is unknown, even if she is innocent, it’s impossible for her to return safely under those circumstances. For the safety of the Village and the sake of the big picture, please temporarily take the position of Raikage ! »

The secretary Mabui also nodded in agreement from the side, her expression solemn : « Indeed, the Village’s old wounds have not yet healed, and it has suddenly encountered such a great disaster. People are panicked, and we urgently need a pillar of strength to step forward and take charge. Lord Killer Bee, this is a responsibility you cannot shirk ! »

The surrounding Ninjas also expressed their agreement one after another, as if as long as Killer Bee nodded, Kumogakure would have a savior.

Hearing this, a flash of struggle and hesitation crossed Killer Bee’s coarse face.

He had never expected to one day sit on the throne of the Raikage ; after all, his dream was to become a shining star rapper.

His big brother’s sudden passing had already caught him off guard, and now even Yugito...

He clenched his fists, the scales in his heart swaying violently.

Just then, a messenger Ninja stumbled into the room, his voice urgent : « This is bad ! Lady Yugito... she’s back ! »

As soon as these words were spoken, the entire room was shocked.

Everyone was stunned at first, but once they regained their composure, they felt that there was something fishy about this matter.

Before they could think further, heavy footsteps were already heard outside. Yugito, covered in the dust of travel, strode into the hall.

Everyone looked closely ; it was indeed Nii Yugito, without a doubt.

« Lady Yugito, you... you’re actually not dead ? » someone cried out in surprise.

« Hmph, you only show up after the enemy has retreated. Don’t you think it’s too late ? » A sharp voice rang out abruptly, filled with questioning and provocation.

Everyone was startled and looked toward the source of the voice, wondering whose subordinate was actually so bold.

It was one thing to spread rumors in private, but now it was being brought out into the open.

Where everyone’s gaze gathered, the speaker was just an ordinary Jonin, usually quite inconspicuous.

Yugito’s brow furrowed slightly. Were that kid Asakawa Shin’s words actually true ? Someone really was secretly framing her.

However, the innocent have nothing to fear ; she would just explain it clearly.

She took a deep breath and said in a low voice : « I did not flee from the battle. The enemy’s target was me from the start. I actively lured the enemy away just to protect the innocent villagers. »

Killer Bee nodded heavily upon hearing this ; he had long since determined that Yugito would never betray the Village.

The other high-ranking officials had varying expressions, their eyes flickering as they calculated that they would have to find someone else to take the fall.

It was just that this blame was too heavy ; an ordinary person simply couldn’t carry it.

While they were at a loss, a sensory Ninja suddenly issued a warning : « Watch out ! She’s not the Raikage ! Inside her body... there is no Matatabi’s Chakra ! »

Before the words had even finished, everyone was struck as if by lightning. They instantly scattered like startled birds and then surrounded Yugito in a heartbeat, brimming with killing intent.

Kumogakure had been plagued by misfortune these past few years, with Ninjas dying mysteriously one after another. A few days ago, the Akatsuki Organization had gone on a killing spree. Everyone was already jumpy, their nerves strained to the limit.

Being shouted at by the sensory Ninja like that triggered an immediate reaction.

Yugito’s expression changed slightly, and she quickly raised her hands to show she had no hostile intent.

She urgently defended herself : « The Two-Tails inside me was indeed extracted ! But I really am Yugito, I’m definitely not an impostor ! »

However, everyone turned a deaf ear to her words, instead sneering and closing in, ready to act.

Even Killer Bee showed hesitation, his heart wavering.

« Absurd ! »

An elder shouted sternly : « Who in the Ninja World doesn’t know that once a Jinchuriki has their Tailed Beast extracted, they must die ! You actually made up such a ridiculous lie ! »

« Exactly ! » another person chimed in. « Capture her first and interrogate her under torture ; the truth will naturally come to light ! »

In her desperation, Yugito blurted out : « Every word I say is true ! It was Asakawa Shin who saved me ! He used a forbidden technique to pull me back from the brink of death ! »

As soon as these words were spoken, everyone’s figures paused slightly. This name caused a moment of surprise.

« So that’s how it is ! »

The ordinary Jonin from before suddenly shouted, his voice laced with sarcasm.

« No wonder during the Konoha Crush, the Fourth Raikage died in battle and Lord Killer Bee was captured, yet you alone returned unscathed, without even having gone through a single fight ! It turns out you had already made a secret deal with Konoha ! »

« Whether it’s true or not, we’ll know once we take her down ! » everyone roared, their momentum growing even stronger.

Yugito’s face showed terror.

At this moment, her heavy injuries had not yet healed, and having lost the Two-Tails, she could only exert the strength of an elite Jonin.

Facing the enraged high-level members of Kumogakure, she had no chance of winning.

She looked at Killer Bee in despair, her eyes full of hope.

Killer Bee, however, let out a long sigh and slowly shook his head : « Yugito, just surrender. Don’t worry, I will do my best to save your life. »

These words were like a clap of thunder, and Yugito felt as if she had instantly fallen into an ice cellar.

She knew well that once she surrendered, what awaited her would surely be Kumogakure’s harshest judgment.

The accumulated resentment in the Village needed an outlet, and she, having lost her Tailed Beast and being isolated and helpless, was undoubtedly the best sacrifice.

She gave a miserable smile, her voice sorrowful : « Is this the Village I risked my life to protect ? How... how can you treat me like this ? I really... I really didn’t betray the Village... »

The surrounding Ninjas turned a deaf ear. Some high-ranking officials even showed expressions of having the victory in their grasp, as if they were certain that as long as they captured Yugito, they could perfectly quell this turmoil.

Yugito took in everyone’s expressions, and a bone-chilling realization suddenly surged in her heart.

'So that’s it... Whether I betrayed the Village or not doesn’t matter at all. What they really need is just a scapegoat who can take the anger of the entire Village.'

Her heart was like dead ashes. She slumped to the ground, her chest trembling slightly with her rapid breathing. A sudden chill hit her, making her snap back to her senses instantly.

She suddenly raised her head. Her originally gentle eyes were now filled with harsh hatred and malice.

« In this world, no one can judge me ! »

Without hesitation, a surge of Chakra was injected by her into the flying thunder god kunai in her arms.

In the next instant, space suddenly distorted, and a tall figure appeared out of thin air, blocking her path. He then reached out his right hand to her and issued an invitation.

« Come with me, to a new world ! »
