# Chapitre 193 — Returning to the Original Spacetime

At the summit of the Roran High Tower, Minato sat side-by-side with Naruto, chatting and laughing together.

The afterglow of the setting sun spilled over the father and son, casting long shadows ; the scene was so warm it felt as if even the wind held its breath.

Just then, space rippled slightly, and the smile on Minato’s face froze for an instant.

On that face, as warm as a small sun, a fleeting trace of hard-to-catch sadness and guilt passed by, so fast it seemed like an illusion.

Asakawa Shin stepped out from the shadows, scratching his head with some uncertainty.

« Did I come back too early ? How about... I go out for another stroll ? »

« No need, it’s already enough. »

Minato Namikaze shook his head and looked at the still somewhat dazed Naruto beside him.

Naruto finally snapped out of it and asked blankly, « Eh ? Are we leaving ? »

« Yes. »

Minato’s tone was gentle yet carried a hint of finality, « You ultimately do not belong to this spacetime ; it is time to return to your original place. »

The light in Naruto’s eyes dimmed slightly, but he quickly pulled himself together, forcing a smile onto his face : « That’s true, I should head back. Sakura and Sai must be worried about me too... »

Before Naruto could finish, his gaze shifted to Asakawa Shin, a hint of suspicion flashing in his eyes : « Um... Shin-kun ? Do you seem a bit different from when I first met you ? I just feel... »

He looked him up and down, then suddenly clapped his hands in realization : « Of course ! The flower on your head has bloomed ! I knew it... »

Minato Namikaze’s gaze narrowed slightly ; in fact, he had noticed this change long ago, but since he didn’t sense anything abnormal, he could only guess that the other’s purpose for coming to this spacetime was definitely related to this mysterious change.

Asakawa Shin nodded slightly, his tone indifferent : « It has indeed bloomed, but only halfway ; it’s still missing a bit of 'fertilizer.' Once I go back this time, I should be able to settle it completely. »

The group talked as they walked, and soon arrived at the Dragon Vein seal.

Minato Namikaze looked solemn as he spoke gravely : « To protect the stability of spacetime, I will seal your memories. Once everyone leaves Roran, this experience will be completely sealed away. »

Having said that, he looked at Asakawa Shin with some hesitation ; seeing the other offer no reaction, he breathed a slight sigh of relief.

If the other party had insisted on refusing, he would have been helpless, but now that the other had given silent consent, things were much easier to handle.

Asakawa Shin didn’t care about this at all.

This seal was practically non-existent to him ; he would just undo it himself after returning, so there was no need to cause further complications at the moment of parting.

Soon, the entire team had gathered.

Yamato hesitated for a moment, then raised his hand and gave the young Kakashi a sharp flick to the head ; watching the other grimace in pain, his mood instantly brightened significantly.

« You bastard, what are you trying to do ? » Kakashi shouted, clutching his head in exasperation.

« Who told Kakashi-senpai to always bully me ! This is just me getting even ! »

« Fine kid, your name is Yamato, right ? I’ll remember you—I’ll remember you in my subconscious—just wait and see how I deal with you in the future ! »

Minato’s gaze slowly swept over everyone, finally lingering on Naruto for a moment, before he said in a deep voice : « Everyone, I am about to begin the sealing ! »

Before his voice faded, he had already taken out a flying thunder god kunai and, with a flick of his wrist, accurately inserted it into the core of the Dragon Vein.

« Seal ! »

Countless complex sealing runes spread out like ripples, and the originally violent and restless fluctuations of the Dragon Vein were instantly suppressed until they were barely perceptible.

At the same time, a brilliant white light suddenly erupted from all the travelers from the different spacetime ; their figures gradually became transparent, finally dissipating slowly into the air.

Just as he was about to leave, Asakawa Shin suddenly felt a mysterious suction wrap around his body, tearing fiercely at his form and blocking his steps to depart.

« Is this... the Will of the World preventing me from leaving ? Why ? »

His thoughts raced, and he instantly understood the reason.

It must be that he had become tainted with the karma of this world ; could it be because he had drained the Natural Energy of the Three Great Sage Regions ?

Unfortunately, this tearing force was truly insignificant.

If it were in the past, it might have been a bit troublesome, but now...

A burst of Chakra containing spacetime attributes suddenly erupted, forcibly severing that bond like a sharp blade.

Whether it was an illusion or not, he actually perceived a wave of aggrieved emotion coming from this world, like the impotent rage of a mistreated young wife.

« Sorry, sorry, consider it that I owe you a favor. »

With this apology, Asakawa Shin’s figure slowly dissipated within the Dragon Vein.

The scene shifted, and Asakawa Shin found himself in the middle of desolate ruins.

« Eh ? Did the time travel fail ? »

Asakawa Shin felt a bit dazed, but he quickly reacted and hurriedly checked his own brain.

« I see, I have already returned ; were my memories sealed ? This technique... it should be the Fourth Hokage’s work. It seems this mission was a complete success. »

Asakawa Shin extended a finger and tapped his forehead lightly ; he actually poked a bloody hole directly into it, stirring inside at will, instantly destroying the core of the seal completely.

As he withdrew his finger, the terrifying wound closed rapidly in a single breath, the skin as smooth as before, as if he had never been injured.

« Alright, done, seal released. »

He shook his head slightly, a hint of playfulness flashing in his eyes. 'If the Hyuga Branch House saw this talent of mine, they would probably be envious to death, right ?'

With this ability, any 'Caged Bird' curse seal would be purely for show.

As his memories fully recovered, his expression gradually became solemn.

His fingertips lightly brushed the God Tree flower atop his head, feeling the layers of unfolding petals, which seemed carved from the finest gemstones, flowing with a luster where the eerie and the sacred intertwined.

At this moment, the flower had bloomed more than halfway, leaving only a few petals in the center still tightly closed, as if thirsting for some crucial catalyst.

Asakawa Shin’s brow furrowed slightly but then smoothed out in an instant, a flash of realization crossing his eyes.

He suddenly remembered that if the God Tree wanted to grow into its complete form and bear a Chakra Fruit,

In addition to massive amounts of Natural Energy, it also required the sacrifice of a clansman with Ōtsutsuki blood as 'fertilizer.'

He flipped his palm and took out the mysterious treasure known as 'Karasuki.'

To activate this divine tool, one likewise needed the Chakra of the Ōtsutsuki Clan as the key to open it.

These two matters happened to coincide, and could be resolved together.

But an Ōtsutsuki... that was a scarce resource.

In the current Ninja World, the legendary Progenitor of Chakra, the Goddess of the Rabbit — Ōtsutsuki Kaguya — had long been sealed away.

The even more powerful Isshiki was quite troublesome, and in his current state, Isshiki might not necessarily satisfy the God Tree’s requirements.

Thinking it over, there was only one Ōtsutsuki clansman left hanging high above the heavens.

That was Ōtsutsuki Toneri, who lived in seclusion on the far side of the moon and possessed pure blood.

Asakawa Shin looked up at the moon ; the cold moonlight was like frost and snow, blanketing the earth.

« Mine, it’s all mine. »
