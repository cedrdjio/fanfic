# Chapitre 133 — Orochimaru

« Something terrible has happened, Anko ! »

Anko was sitting on the roof of the proctoring area, happily enjoying Three-Color Dango and red bean soup, her little feet swinging back and forth.

Hearing the newcomer’s exclamation, she didn’t pay much mind, continuing to chew with her cheeks puffed out :

« What kind of behavior is this, being so reckless ? And you’re interrupting me while I’m eating my Dango and drinking red bean soup. If there isn’t something major going on, I won’t forgive you. »

The Ninja who had come to report knelt on one knee, a few black lines appearing on his forehead at her words.

He took a deep breath and continued to report :

« While on patrol, we discovered three examinee corpses with very strange causes of death. We would like you to confirm it yourself ! »

« Oh ? Corpses ? Have the examinees already started fighting ? »

Anko still had a sesame seed on her lip, and she nonchalantly licked the sauce from the corner of her mouth.

« It wasn’t inside the exam grounds ; they were killed before they even entered ! »

« What ? How can that be ? »

Anko’s pupils constricted sharply, and the bamboo skewer snapped with a « snap » between her fingers.

Realizing the severity of the situation...

She shoved the remaining Dango into her mouth, chewed haphazardly a few times, and swallowed.

Then, she leapt from the roof and landed lightly.

« What are you standing there for ? Lead the way, quickly ! »

« Ah ? Yes ! »

Soon, Anko followed the person to the scene.

The area had already been sealed off by Anbu Ninja, the grim atmosphere making it hard to breathe.

She dismissed everyone around her and knelt straight down to examine the corpses.

When she saw the tragic state of the corpses with her own eyes, her pupils couldn’t help but constrict, and her breathing hitched.

The skin on the faces of the three corpses had completely disappeared, as if it had been melted away, making it impossible to distinguish their original features.

« This... Is this the Removal of Face Technique ? »

A terrifying thought exploded in Anko’s mind like a thunderclap, chilling her spine and causing her to clench her fists involuntarily.

« This attire, is it that Kusagakure Ninja who released killing intent at me earlier ? No, I’m afraid that even then, he was already... The Removal of Face Technique, plus that bizarre long tongue... There’s no mistake, that monster has returned ! »

The figure from the depths of her memory suddenly emerged, igniting Anko’s fury.

Her nape felt as if it were being scorched by flames, sending waves of tearing phantom pain.

She raised her hand to lightly touch the Cursed Seal on her neck, her originally beautiful face now covered in frost.

« Something major has happened. It’s highly likely that that bastard Orochimaru has returned ! He peeled off the faces of these three and disguised himself as a Kusagakure Ninja to sneak into the Forest of Death ! »

She turned her head abruptly, her gaze piercing like a knife at her subordinates : « Report this to Hokage-sama immediately and request the highest level of reinforcements ! As for me, I am going to try to stop that guy. Do you all understand ? Move out immediately ! »

« Yes ! »

Once the figures of her subordinates had completely disappeared.

Anko tapped her toes, her figure shooting toward the Forest of Death like an arrow released from a bow.

The swift wind lifted her hair, and those eyes, originally slightly lazy, had now turned into pools of icy water, filled with an unyielding determination.

« Orochimaru, I’m going to kill you ! »

...Orochimaru, who was weighing heavily on Anko’s mind, was completely oblivious at this moment, currently filled with delight, enjoying the pleasure of beating up children.

Although the three members of Team 7 had undergone Taijutsu training, how could they possibly be a match for Orochimaru ?

Even their most practiced Taijutsu only managed to make Orochimaru take a second look.

The combat experience between the two sides was worlds apart. Just by being hit by the killing intent emitted from the Demonic Illusion : Hell Viewing Technique, the bodies of the few little ones began to tremble uncontrollably, and they completely lost their footing.

If it weren’t for Orochimaru intentionally holding back, wanting to test the limits of Sasuke’s abilities, the three of them would have likely already fallen.

Naruto had just defeated the giant summoning snake blocking their path, panting as he ran back to Sasuke and Sakura.

He wiped the sweat from his forehead, his eyes full of shock as he looked at Orochimaru : « I never would have thought that besides Shin, someone as troublesome as you was hidden in this exam ! »

Sasuke’s eyes were blood-red, his two-tomoe Sharingan spinning rapidly, locking tightly onto every move Orochimaru made. His muscles were tense, not daring to relax in the slightest :

« Something is wrong. Kusagakure is just a small Village ; it was even destroyed once a few years ago... Even if combat power of this level still survived, it’s absolutely impossible for them to just be a Genin. Who exactly are you ? »

Sakura swallowed nervously, her right hand gripping her Kunai, trembling slightly uncontrollably :

« Sasuke, her target must be you. Look at her eyes ; the possessiveness is practically overflowing. It’s truly disgusting and shameless ! »

She gritted her teeth, holding her Kunai horizontally in front of her chest, attempting to block in front of Sasuke with her body : « But don’t be afraid, Sasuke. I will definitely protect you ; I will never let this strange woman take you away ! »

Even though the atmosphere was tense and life-or-death... Sasuke still couldn’t help but turn his head, glancing at Sakura with a complex look, the corner of his mouth twitching uncontrollably twice.

« Um... You’re distracted, right ? Sasuke-kun ? »

« Nani ? Oh no... »

Sasuke’s pupils shrank, and he turned his head back in a panic.

He saw that Orochimaru’s figure had already crossed dozens of meters in that short instant, closing in on them like a ghost.

Before Sasuke could react, Orochimaru had already delivered a fierce kick, landing squarely in his lower abdomen.

Sasuke was instantly struck as if by lightning, flying backward and crashing through several thick tree trunks before falling heavily.

The moment he hit the ground, he could no longer hold it back, and a mouthful of blood sprayed out violently ; he had clearly suffered a serious internal injury.

« Sasuke ! » « Sasuke-kun ! »

Naruto and Sakura were horrified at the sight, rushing forward without hesitation, attempting to stop Orochimaru from continuing to approach Sasuke.

Three figures crossed instantly.

However, after taking the attacks from the two, Orochimaru’s body burst open without warning, turning into a puff of smoke and dissipating into the air.

Looking at the familiar Ninjutsu effect, Naruto was stunned for a moment, then snapped back to reality, hurriedly warning : « Careful ! It’s the Shadow Clone Technique ! »

However, Naruto’s warning was ultimately a beat too late.

Orochimaru’s real body had already silently snuck behind Sakura.

« Ninjutsu : Hidden Shadow Snake Hands ! »

That pale palm suddenly reached out, transforming into several venomous snakes that attacked Sakura.

Under the horrified gazes of Naruto and Sasuke, the venomous snakes tightly constricted Sakura’s body like iron pincers.

Immediately, the snakes all opened their mouths wide, delivering a snake bite in the desperate gaze of the three, injecting several types of snake venom into Sakura’s body in an instant.

Sakura’s face instantly turned purplish-blue, and after her body convulsed violently a few times, she went completely limp on the ground, her life or death unknown.

« Sa... Sakura ! ! ! »

Witnessing his companions falling one after another, Sasuke seriously injured, and Sakura’s life or death unknown.

This sudden change made Naruto’s whole body tremble violently.

A violent emotion surged into his mind, and his eyes also welled up with a terrifying scarlet.

Just at the moment he was about to lose control and go berserk, a series of deafening roars suddenly erupted from the distance, forcibly pulling his reason back a little.

He turned his head abruptly to look, and after seeing who it was, his tense nerves instantly collapsed, and the scarlet in his eyes receded like a tide.

« That... Is that Shin ? »

He shouted with a sob : « Holy crap ! Shin, help ! Someone is bullying us here ! »
