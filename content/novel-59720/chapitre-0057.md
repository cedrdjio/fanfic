# Chapitre 57 — Never Give Up

« Sakura is badly hurt ! Quickly ask Shin from the commentary booth to come over… »

Shin performed a detailed examination on Sakura. The results were indeed not optimistic ; she was injured even more severely than Neji.

Aside from the abdominal trauma caused by Hinata, Sakura’s body showed muscle strains, bone dislocations, and soft tissue contusions in many places. In particular, her right finger bones, wrist, and forearm showed signs of hairline fractures.

Clearly, Sakura had paid a heavy price for forcibly using power that exceeded her body’s limits.

Fortunately, Shin’s Medical Ninjutsu was extremely superb. After his meticulous treatment, Sakura was basically out of danger and only needed a week of rest to recover.

However, when Sakura woke up, she would likely feel quite sore.

After Sakura was carried off the field on a stretcher, the scene was tidied up slightly, and the competition resumed immediately. Shin skillfully switched back to his old job of commentating on the match.

« Let us thank Hinata and Sakura for the wonderful duel they brought us ! »

« The tenacious and unyielding spirit they displayed on the field truly touched every single member of the audience ! »

« Here, we also warmly congratulate Hinata for successfully advancing ! »

« And now, the final elimination match of our boys' group is about to begin. »

« Let us invite contestant No. 1, Uchiha Sasuke, and contestant No. 4, Rock Lee, to the stage ! »

…

« It’s my turn to take the stage again ! » Sasuke stepped onto the ring full of confidence. Regarding the upcoming match, his heart was completely calm ; he even felt like laughing.

After all, in the previous duel with Neji, Rock Lee had been injured quite badly. Even after receiving treatment from Shin, it was difficult to return to his best state in such a short time.

Trying to compete with him in such a state was simply a lost cause, and furthermore…

Sasuke adjusted his thoughts, looked sincerely at Rock Lee, who was walking towards him, his words seemingly filled with admiration :

« Rock Lee, your match with Neji was truly amazing. »

« You are worthy of the title 'Genius of Hard Work' bestowed upon you by Shin ! »

« Not only is your strength superior, but you also possess a spirit of never giving up, which is truly admirable. »

« It’s just… » Sasuke frowned slightly, analyzing carefully for Rock Lee :

« Your injuries look quite serious. Even after treatment, I imagine it’s difficult for you to support the upcoming fight, right ? »

« Forgive my bluntness, but if you force yourself to fight, I’m afraid your chances of winning are slim, and it will only worsen your injuries, affecting your future training… »

Sasuke’s eyes revealed a hint of regret, his tone filled with pity, and he finally got to the point :

« It really is a pity. How I long to compete against you at your peak. How about this : you forfeit this fight first, and once your injuries have healed, we can have a proper match. Wouldn’t that be wonderful ? »

Rock Lee was completely stunned at this moment. He stared blankly at Sasuke, tears gradually welling up in his eyes, and then he wailed loudly at Sasuke :

« Sasuke, do you really think that ? Do you recognize my Ninja Way too ? Wuu wuu wuu… that’s wonderful ! »

« You’re right, my current state is indeed not good. I’m no match for you at all. Fighting you rashly will only affect my future training plan… »

…

On the commentary stand, Shin looked at Sasuke in surprise, his face full of disbelief :

'Is this for real ? Is this still the Sasuke I know ?'

'Since when does he play mind games instead of acting weird ?'

'Could it be that his account was hacked ? I’ll go check the password with him later…'

…

Sasuke looked at the teary-eyed Rock Lee and couldn’t help but feel smug inside :

'As expected of my brother ! The intelligence he collected for me and the tactics he formulated are simply flawless and without a single opening !'

'Not only did I easily take down Kiba, but Rock Lee will soon be persuaded by me according to his personality flaws… In that case.'

'Naruto, you just wait and wash up ! I’m coming for you soon !'

'The person who wins this time will definitely be me ! After all, I’m not fighting alone this time !'

Just as Sasuke was daydreaming about how to beat up Naruto, he heard Rock Lee’s tone shift, and he said passionately :

« Facing this match with zero chance of winning, I… was planning to concede… »

« But, it was you—Uchiha Sasuke—who gave me strength ! »

« For your recognition, even if my condition is poor, I will bring out 120% of my strength to respond to your expectations ! »

« After all, I am the never-giving-up Rock Lee ! »

After finishing, Rock Lee gave a thumbs up to Sasuke, and a bright smile bloomed on his face. His white teeth shone in the sun, so bright it made Sasuke unable to open his eyes.

The audience also spontaneously applauded, moved by Rock Lee’s never-give-up spirit.

Might Guy was even more excited, his eyes shining. He looked at Rock Lee with gratification. This child is just like me ; his youth is truly passionate and brilliant !

Shin on the commentary stand held back his laughter until his face turned red. He pinched his thigh hard to stop himself from laughing out loud. After all, being a commentator was serious business, and basic professional ethics were still required !

Only Sasuke stood on the spot, at a loss, seemingly not having expected things to develop like this. He was stunned for a moment before coming back to his senses, and couldn’t help but laugh dryly :

« Ha, haha, then Rock Lee, let’s both do our best… »

…

« The match begins ! »

At Might Guy’s command, Rock Lee dashed forward and threw a straight punch at Sasuke’s face.

Sasuke had already collected his shattered composure and was trying his best to deal with Rock Lee.

He blocked the strike with his fist, but didn’t expect the power of Rock Lee’s punch to exceed his imagination, actually forcing him back two steps.

Sasuke couldn’t help but look at Rock Lee with shock. Rock Lee returned a bright smile :

« How about it ? Sasuke. »

« This is my response to your recognition ! »

« Also, thanks to your support, I feel like I’m about to burn up right now… »

Sasuke cursed inwardly : 'Didn’t you have a lung injury ? You were clearly having trouble breathing just a moment ago…'

'Since you’re so lacking in oxygen, there’s no need to be this fired up !'

Rock Lee didn’t care whether Sasuke could take it or not ; he just attacked fiercely without mercy, beating Sasuke until he was retreating steadily and in a sorry state…

Naruto clapped and laughed under the stage, constantly cheering for Rock Lee : « That’s it, Brother Rock Lee ! »

« Kick his butt hard. There’s no need to talk about honor when dealing with Sasuke… »

« Yes, yes, that’s it ! Use more force ! »

« Attack his lower base, attack his lower base ! »

« … »

« Go for it, the never-giving-up big brother ! »

Sasuke heard everything clearly on the stage ; after all, Naruto’s voice was too loud. This made him turn blue with rage, and he thought viciously in his heart :

« Uzumaki Naruto ! You brat, you’re really despicable ! How dare you mess with my mindset ! »

« Wait until I defeat this blockhead, then it’ll be your turn, you idiot ! »

« You better wash up and wait for me ! ! ! »
