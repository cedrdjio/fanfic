# Chapitre 171 — Sea Bottom Drop

Witnessing Killer Bee taking all the damage alone to protect him once more, a look of resolve flashed in Ai’s eyes.

He suddenly clenched his fists and transformed into a streak of lightning, charging straight at Asakawa Shin.

« Liger Bomb ! »

« Oh ? You still have the will to fight ? Impressive ! »

Facing Ai’s full-power punch that created a sonic boom, Asakawa Shin merely raised his left arm slightly and easily blocked the thunderous strike.

Looking at Asakawa Shin’s arm, which was as black as ink, Ai’s heart sank to the bottom.

‘It’s that seal-less Ninjutsu again, combining offense and defense with no openings. How on earth can it be broken ?’

Before he could think further, Asakawa Shin had already launched a counterattack. Lightning flashed violently from his right fist as he slammed it hard into Ai’s abdomen.

Ai’s pupils constricted, and he instantly used Body Flicker to retreat rapidly.

However, to his horror, Asakawa Shin’s speed was incredibly fast, sticking to him like a shadow.

« How is this possible ! You can actually keep up with my speed ? » Ai exclaimed in disbelief.

« Keep up with your speed ? Why, are you fast ? »

Asakawa Shin’s voice rang eerily in his ear, tinged with a hint of contempt.

« You didn’t really think I only rely on the Flying Thunder God to fight, did you ? »

Before his voice finished, a heavy punch wrapped in black-red lightning slammed hard into Ai’s abdomen.

« Pfft ! »

The domineering force pierced through the Lightning Release Armor. Ai screamed in pain as blood sprayed wildly, and he was sent flying like a broken kite, plowing a deep trench across the ground.

Only then did Asakawa Shin’s mocking voice spread across the field.

« There’s no harm in telling you—my short-range burst speed is actually... a notch faster than the Flying Thunder God. »

In the distant dust, Ai felt a surge of despair as he heard those piercing words.

His attacks couldn’t break the defense, and the speed he took pride in was completely crushed. This battle seemed to have no hope of victory.

But soon, the gloom in his eyes was replaced by a touch of ruthlessness.

He was the Raikage, the hope of the entire village ; how could he give up so easily ?

Ai took a deep breath, forcibly suppressed the surging blood within his body, and stood up again unsteadily.

He suddenly erupted with his full Chakra, frantically condensing it into his right hand’s palm-strike, as electricity crackled along the edge of his palm.

In the next instant, he lunged forward like an arrow from a bow, leaping into the air and using gravitational acceleration to deliver his strongest strike, betting on his dignity as the Raikage !

« Lightning Oppression Horizontal Chop ! »

This hand-blade was extremely sharp ; even before the blade arrived, the fierce wind pressure had already sliced through the air.

Back then, it was with this move that he cut off the Eight-Tails' horn in one strike, forcibly suppressing Gyuki’s rampage.

Now, this all-out strike, carrying a terrifying aura of destruction, hacked straight down with immense momentum.

Facing this formidable attack, Asakawa Shin remained calm, slightly tilting his head to easily avoid the lethal trajectory of the hand-blade.

« Clang— ! »

The hand-blade slammed into Asakawa Shin’s neck, producing the ear-piercing sound of clashing metal.

Looking closely, that area was already covered in a layer of ink-black, indestructible Armament Haki armor.

« Break for me ! »

Ai’s eyes were nearly bursting as he roared frantically.

The Lightning Release Chakra on his right hand-blade erupted with a blinding white light, pushing the nature transformation of Lightning Release to its absolute limit, even causing the air to distort from the heat.

However, all of this was ultimately in vain.

Under Ai’s horrified gaze, Asakawa Shin’s body, which had been forced into a bend by the explosive power, actually straightened up bit by bit against the immense pressure.

« Hmm ? You dare to get distracted while facing me ? »

Asakawa Shin decisively seized this fleeting opening, his right hand shooting out like lightning to grip Ai’s head like a steel pincer.

Immense power erupted, as if it were about to crush the other’s skull into pieces.

Ai was also a ruthless man ; even in a desperate situation, he continued to output Chakra frantically, completely abandoning defense and doing everything in his power to chop off Asakawa Shin’s head.

As the two’s Chakra collided violently, the ground beneath them could no longer bear the burden, instantly collapsing into a massive crater.

Unfortunately, Ai’s all-or-nothing attack ultimately failed to shake Asakawa Shin in the slightest. Instead, his own Chakra was exhausted, and his offensive weakened.

Finally, the lightning on his right hand dimmed ; he was clearly at the end of his rope.

Asakawa Shin narrowed his eyes, showing unmasked admiration : « As expected of the Raikage, I acknowledge your fighting spirit. Unfortunately, you are an enemy of Konoha. I shall send you on your way with my full-power strike. »

Haki erupted from all around Asakawa Shin, as black and red lightning rampaged wildly.

Ignoring Ai’s struggles, he used sheer brute force to drag him through the air, charging straight toward the clouds.

A massive amount of Haki was poured into his right arm while he simultaneously activated Sage Chakra to further strengthen his physique. Then, with all his might, he hurled Ai toward the ground like a human cannonball.

« Sea Bottom Drop ! ! ! »

At this moment, Ai felt his hair stand on end as the shadow of death instantly enveloped him.

He struggled desperately, but it was too late ; the immense inertia made his body unable to move.

In a brief instant, a flood of memories flashed through his mind like a revolving lantern, finally condensing into a helpless sigh : « Sorry, I lost this time. »

The next moment, he slammed into the earth like a meteor. The Haki entwined around him suddenly exploded, transforming into a terrifying shockwave that swept across the surroundings.

The ground shattered violently, and Ai’s body was driven deep into the earth like a nail being hammered in, with layers of rock crumbling in response.

Ultimately, that destructive power completely erupted deep underground, blowing the entire area to pieces. A strong tremor could be felt for dozens of kilometers around.

When the dust settled, a bottomless black hole was left in the ground, and Raikage Ai’s Chakra signature had completely vanished.

Not far away, the unconscious Killer Bee seemed to sense something, as a single crystal tear slowly rolled down from the corner of his eye.

Asakawa Shin clapped his hands and descended from the air with satisfaction : « Raikage, you should be satisfied with such a grand exit, right ? »

In the next instant, his figure blurred as he used the Flying Thunder God to teleport to Killer Bee’s side, stepped on his chest, and brought him back to the Konoha battlefield.

Seeing the sudden appearance of Asakawa Shin and the unconscious Killer Bee at his feet, everyone understood that the battle was decided. They looked at each other with eyes full of wild joy ; this war was a complete victory for Konoha.

Asakawa Shin ignored the crowd’s reaction and casually kicked Killer Bee in front of Kakashi.

« Kakashi, please seal him up. I’m going to find Shukaku to play for a bit. »

After saying that, his gaze swept across the field like lightning, and he asked with slight confusion : « Where’s Danzo ? Where did that guy go ? You didn’t let him escape, did you ? »

Tsunade’s expression became strange upon hearing this, and she pointed to a small patch of lush woods in the center of the area.

« Look, those are Danzo. His blood sprayed everywhere, and then these trees sprouted up. As for his remains, they’ve already been taken away by the old man. »

Asakawa Shin’s eye twitched slightly. « He actually sprouted ? I guess this counts as him practicing the will of fire. Where the leaves dance, the fire also lives on... »

After speaking, he glanced at Shisui and Itachi with satisfaction, and with a flash, he disappeared from the spot.

Uchiha Itachi’s eyes flickered, feeling secretly proud : ‘As expected, I guessed right. Shin-sama never intended to let Danzo leave alive. Is this what Shin-sama meant by being in the emperor’s good graces ?’
