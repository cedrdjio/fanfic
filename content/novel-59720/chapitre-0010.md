# Chapitre 10 — A New Brain Is Indeed Useful

« Shin, have you ever seen Konoha at four in the morning ? »

« No, why would I want to see that ? Don’t change the subject ; where is the training plan I asked you to make ? »

Asakawa Shin looked at Shikamaru in confusion, not understanding what Konoha at four in the morning had to do with the training plan he wanted, until he saw Shikamaru pull a plan as thick as a dictionary out of his schoolbag.

Asakawa Shin took the plan in a daze and flipped through it mechanically. Huh, does it actually have a table of contents ?

The table of contents listed every student’s name, and under each name, it was divided into several sub-items.

Training status, physical fitness, future phase goals, everyone’s strengths and weaknesses, and detailed training schedules tailored to each individual.

There were even health assessments, personalized dietary advice, and lifestyle guidance.

« Hiss... As expected of you, Shikamaru. I really didn’t misjudge you ; a new brain is indeed useful. »

Having flipped to this point, that was enough. Shin stood up with some pleasant surprise and patted Shikamaru on the shoulder.

« Shikamaru, I’ve decided. In the future, when I become Hokage, you will be my Assistant to the Hokage.

I’ll hand everything over to you ; you can make the final decision on everything in the Village.

Shikamaru, from now on, wherever I have a bite of meat, you’ll have a bone to gnaw on.

I have been sickly since childhood, so you must work hard for me. »

Shikamaru’s body jolted, and he hurriedly snatched back the training plan :

« Shin, I suddenly have some new ideas. The training plan can still be improved. If there’s nothing else, I’ll be going now. »

Asakawa Shin nodded with satisfaction.

Shikamaru turned his head with a smile on his face, but his expression suddenly became incomparably gloomy :

'Shin’s talent is so terrifying ; if things go on like this, he might actually become Hokage in the future. Doesn’t that mean I’ll have to work under the table for him for the rest of my life ?

Just thinking about that future makes my whole body cold. No, absolutely not.

It has come to this ; I have no choice but to make Naruto suffer. I’ll double Naruto’s training tasks when I get back.

Naruto, you must become Hokage !'

Watching Shikamaru’s retreating back, Shin reviewed his performance just now and felt it was excellent.

Knowing how to use people well, having a broad mind, and delegating power—where else could you find such an excellent leader as himself ?

As expected of me ; I subdued a subordinate in just three sentences.

Shikamaru’s body shook and shook just now ; he must have been moved by me, right ? Was he leaving in such a hurry because he was embarrassed ?

He is truly young, Shikamaru. Being seen through so easily, I’ll have to find more things for Shikamaru to do to train him in the future.

However... recalling the table of contents he had just seen, the personalized dietary advice reminded Shin of something.

That poor little thing, Hinata, hasn’t been getting enough to eat, right ? I almost forgot about that. It seems I’ll have to find time to visit the Hyuga Clan. You can’t train properly if you aren’t full.

Right, I also have to go to the Hokage Building to visit the old man left behind. I still haven’t collected the gold coins dropped by the old man. How annoying ; I’m so busy.

Shikamaru : « ? »

After school, at the Fifth Training Ground.

Naruto came to find Shikamaru with his tongue sticking out : « Shikamaru, is this right ? Why is my training volume so much higher than everyone else’s ? Do you know I almost died ? »

Shikamaru was even more shocked. He hadn’t expected Naruto to be able to complete this training volume at all ; he just wanted to test Naruto’s limits. He didn’t expect... As expected of the man I have my eye on.

Shikamaru kept a straight face on the surface and said :

« Naruto, you must use my title during training.

Also, everyone’s training tasks were formulated by me individually based on each person’s different physical fitness and training progress.

Don’t underestimate me, Naruto. »

« That means my physical fitness is very strong, right ? Hey, I’m saying, am I much stronger than Sasuke ?

Hahaha, that’s right, I’m the leader of the boys' group, what is this level of training ? How can mere Sasuke compare to me. »

Naruto left triumphantly ; there was no trace of fatigue to be seen.

Shikamaru narrowed his eyes and muttered to himself : « So he still had energy to spare ? It’s not just his endurance ; his recovery ability is even more astonishing.

If that’s the case, the training plan will have to be changed again... »

After Shikamaru also left, Sasuke slowly walked out from the shadows of a large tree.

His gaze was fixed on the direction Naruto had left. This beast—he was clearly about to pass out from training, yet his training progress was still far behind Naruto’s.

« Damn it, no, if this goes on, I’ll lose. I have to think of a way. Right, Brother ? He must have a way... »

At the girls' group training camp, Asakawa Shin found Hinata alone and proposed that he wanted to pay a visit on Saturday morning.

After all, the Hyuga Clan is a prominent family with many rules. Asakawa Shin didn’t want to be rude and offend the Hyuga Clan for no reason.

Hinata asked hesitantly : « I don’t know what important matter brings you to visit, Shin-kun, so I can report it to Father. »

« Tsk, Hinata, look at my brain ; I forgot to say it. My visit to see Hiashi this time is mainly for you. »

Asakawa Shin was prone to shocking statements. As soon as the words left his mouth, he saw the young girl opposite him blush instantly, and only then did he realize he had spoken improperly.

« Sorry, Hinata, I didn’t mean it like that. Sigh, I’ll just be direct. Hinata, you actually haven’t been getting enough to eat, have you ? »

The young girls in the Ninja World were more precocious than the last. Afraid of causing any more trouble, Shin got straight to the point :

« You know, I have some attainments in Taijutsu, so I can see a bit of your condition.

Your talent is clearly very good, but your performance is unsatisfactory. Hinata, are you often hungry ? »

Hearing this, Hinata felt as if she had been struck by lightning. She was blushing all over and stammered :

« H-how could that be ? I finished the bento I brought for lunch. »

« Hey ! Hinata, don’t lie to me anymore. That little bento is just a drop in the bucket ; who is that enough for ?

You don’t have to be shy. It’s very normal for those of us who practice Taijutsu to eat a lot. The more talented you are, the more you eat.

Actually, it’s the same for me. I’m never full every day, so I can understand your pain. »

Shin wasn’t lying to Hinata. Roger was a big eater. As his fusion proficiency increased, Shin felt his appetite becoming more and more exaggerated.

The bento that used to fill him up now only barely took the edge off his hunger, though he wouldn’t starve.

He also had this consideration in mind when visiting the Hyuga Clan this time.

« Shin-kun, is that true ? You’re also never full ? »

Hearing this, Hinata was suddenly very surprised. She had thought she was the only one in the world like this, and often felt inferior because of it.

Unexpectedly... I’m not a freak, but a genius ? That’s great !

« Of course, Hinata, I’ve said it before, you are very talented, and I really can’t bear to see a pearl covered in dust.

As the eldest daughter of the Hyuga Clan, you naturally don’t lack food. I can probably guess a little bit about your unspeakable difficulties.

Why don’t you let me go and speak to Hiashi about it ? »

Asakawa Shin’s words were naturally not without purpose. Hinata, being able to eat so much, was a good seedling for practicing Taijutsu.

Under his observation over the past few days, she also had great talent in the Strong Fist Style, yet she went off to practice that nonsense Gentle Fist.

If you could practice the ancestor’s Eighty Gods Vacuum Attack, that would be fine, but that is a Kekkei Mora, which is not very realistic.

Therefore, Asakawa Shin decided to train Hinata well in the Strong Fist Style.

It would be best to train her into a super-powered girl with arms strong enough to race horses. If she could combine it with the family’s Gentle Fist, she might even achieve a balance of hardness and softness.

Asakawa Shin had high hopes for Hinata ; after all, the last girl who could eat like that had already unified ancient Britain.

« I understand, Shin-kun. I will report it to Father.

Then on Saturday morning, I will be waiting for your arrival at home. Finally, thank you. » Hinata said, somewhat moved.
