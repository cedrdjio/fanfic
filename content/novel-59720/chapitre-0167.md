# Chapitre 167 — Legacy

Hiruzen Sarutobi and Kazekage Rasa looked at each other, their expressions tinged with a bit of awkwardness.

Especially when catching sight of the undried blood and disheveled clothes on the other, a sense of mutual sympathy welled up in their hearts.

« The Third Hokage is indeed the Professor of Ninjutsu ; your strength is truly unfathomable. »

« You’re too kind. Rasa, your Magnet Release Gold Dust is also incredibly powerful. Your Bloodline Limit truly lives up to its name. »

The two exchanged pleasantries, and the auras around them gradually calmed down.

Then they looked at each other and, with a tacit understanding, resumed their battle, with even greater momentum than before.

...

In the distant shadows, Zetsu, who looked remarkably like a pitcher plant, slowly emerged from the ground.

White Zetsu’s half-body glanced at Obito, his tone carrying a hint of doubt : « What’s wrong ? Obito, aren’t you going to make a move ? The situation looks a bit grim. »

Obito frowned slightly and shot him a dissatisfied sidelong glance. « I’ve said it before, address me as Lord Madara. »

White Zetsu rolled his eyes and playfully corrected himself, « Understood, Lord Madara. So, are you still not planning to step in ? »

Obito shook his head, his expression grave. « Something isn’t right. That guy Orochimaru is completely acting. This plan is a trap. Zetsu, let’s prepare to withdraw. »

« Withdraw ? » White Zetsu exclaimed. « Hasn’t Danzo already charged in ? Are we just going to ignore him ? »

Obito snorted coldly, saying dismissively, « Danzo no longer has any utility. I’ve already obtained the Edo Tensei from him. Whether he lives or dies is of no consequence. »

Before his voice had even faded, his body began to distort, eventually turning into a vortex and disappearing completely, leaving only an echoing voice behind.

« Zetsu, I’m heading back first. Just leave a clone here to continue monitoring. »

After Obito’s figure had completely dissipated into the air, White Zetsu immediately began to fuss, his tone full of complaints :

« That guy Obito... isn’t he being too cautious ? Haven’t you noticed ? Ever since he was taught a lesson by Minato Namikaze, his entire way of doing things has become excessively prudent. »

He then sighed again and frowned as he consulted Black Zetsu beside him. « So what do we do now ? Obito has already bailed, and the strength Konoha is showing is so terrifying. I’m afraid it will be a huge obstacle to our future plans for collecting the Tailed Beasts. »

However, Black Zetsu ignored his rambling.

His gaze was fixed intently on Asakawa Shin in the distance.

To be precise, it was fixed on that strange flower atop his head, his eyes filled with unbelievable horror.

« It can’t be... I could never mistake it. That’s the flower of the God Tree ! But how is this possible ? That child... what on earth is his background... »

Asakawa Shin seemed to sense something and suddenly turned his head to look in that direction. However, there was nothing there but mottled tree shadows.

He scratched the back of his head, his small face filled with confusion. « Huh ? Strange... was it just an illusion ? »

Little did he know, Black Zetsu, hidden deep underground, was now drenched in sweat.

« He could actually sense my presence ? I am a creation of Yin-Yang Release... »

Asakawa Shin shook his head and didn’t take the anomaly to heart. At this moment, his full attention was occupied by the little female cat in front of him, making it hard to focus on anything else.

But soon, he broke into a smile, a flash of anticipation in his eyes. « It’s almost time. »

« What’s almost time ? » Yugito frowned, a faint sense of unease in her heart.

As she spoke, she boldly stepped closer and gently reached out a finger to tentatively poke the miraculous flower on his forehead.

Asakawa Shin was slightly stunned, and then his expression became somewhat peculiar. Right on cue, a crisp system notification rang in his ear.

[Ding ! Detected that the host has performed an action consistent with the template. Proficiency +1%. Current Proficiency : 98%]

Meanwhile, on the battlefield where the Anti-Konoha Allied Forces had placed high hopes—where Orochimaru was—a hearty and heroic laugh suddenly rang out.

Everyone looked over and saw Tsunade taking on three opponents at once, forcing them back step by step, looking majestic.

The First and Second Hokage looked on with doting eyes. It was rare to see their granddaughter so happy, so they put even more effort into their cooperation.

In contrast, Orochimaru’s face was dark. While retreating, Tobirama intentionally or unintentionally blocked his path, causing him to be a beat slow and get sent flying by one of Tsunade’s punches.

Orochimaru scrambled up in a rage, just spitting out a mouthful of blood. Before he could react, Hashirama was also knocked back by Tsunade, landing right on top of him and pinning him to the ground again.

Tsunade chased after them, ruthlessly delivering another powerful kick.

Orochimaru was about to dodge when he realized his legs were pinned under Hashirama and he couldn’t break free.

His eyes twitched, and he simply gave up struggling, shielding his chest with both hands to take the hit.

Fortunately, he possessed the Orochimaru-style Body Replacement Technique ; this kind of attack couldn’t kill him, but his Chakra consumption was currently massive.

He gasped heavily, his chest heaving, his face full of disbelief. « Congratulations, Tsunade. It seems your Hemophobia has been cured. »

Tsunade was slightly taken aback. Only then did she belatedly notice that Orochimaru was covered in blood and gasping for breath, yet her heart didn’t feel the slightest ripple or discomfort.

« Eh ? When did my Hemophobia get better ? I didn’t notice at all. »

Hearing this, Senju Tobirama’s lips curled into a proud arc as he chuckled.

« The so-called Hemophobia is nothing more than a mental demon. Fear born of love will naturally be dispelled by love. Little Tsuna, don’t be afraid. We are always behind you. »

Tsunade was deeply shaken, a wave of emotion washing over her as her eyes instantly welled with tears.

She took a deep breath, trying to calm her surging emotions, and said solemnly :

« I understand, Grandpa, Granduncle. I won’t run away anymore. I will face my fears bravely. You... can leave in peace. »

« So Little Tsuna has already noticed ? » Hashirama and Tobirama looked at each other with a hint of surprise, then smiled in relief.

Their Edo Tensei bodies were already unable to be maintained for long due to the caster’s low strength, and the time limit was nearing its end after multiple battles.

However, seeing their granddaughter’s growth and knowing that Konoha had successors, they no longer had any regrets.

Tobirama hesitated for a moment, looking at Tsunade and speaking with a hint of elder concern :

« Um... Little Tsuna, you’re not young anymore. It’s time to consider starting a family. You’re the only one left in our lineage ; we can’t let the family line end. »

Hashirama glared at him and interrupted, « You have the nerve to talk about Little Tsuna ? What about yourself ? Where is your legacy ? »

Then, Hashirama couldn’t help but sigh and said in frustration :

« You two useless things, you’re far behind me in this regard. »

« But Little Tsuna, you need to hurry up too. Age isn’t an issue. Back in our day, there were plenty of people who married and became fathers at twelve. »

A blush crept onto Tsunade’s cheeks.

« Grandpa, I know. Rest assured, I... I’ll work hard ! »
