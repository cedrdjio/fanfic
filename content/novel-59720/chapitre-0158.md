# Chapitre 158 — Showcase

Deva Path : Sage Mode granted him perception beyond imagination. By a rough estimate, his sensory radius had easily surpassed a hundred kilometers.

Even more wondrously, in this state, he seemed to transform into a wisp of breeze or a blade of grass between heaven and earth.

With a slight thought, he could travel instantaneously through mountains and rivers by utilizing nature’s meridians ; the speed was staggering.

« This seems somewhat similar to the Mayfly Technique. I didn’t expect to stumble upon such a secret technique by accident. I’ll call it the Five Elements Evasion Technique. »

« And... it seems this mode can also control the power of nature. »

With a slight shift of his mind, he cast his gaze into the distance.

In the distant mountain range, the sky, which had been clear for ten thousand miles, was suddenly covered in dark clouds. Moments later, a torrential rain began to fall ; he was actually able to change the weather with a single thought.

On the other hand, the Human Path : Sage Mode was the polar opposite, returning directly to the basics and violently augmenting his physique, Chakra, and vitality.

The absorbed Natural Energy would be completely intercepted, continuously gathering into the flower atop his head ; its combat orientation was obvious.

Thanks to the perfect compatibility between these two modes and himself, Asakawa Shin could maintain Sage Transformation at any time and place without worrying about physical mutation.

Asakawa Shin nodded slightly in satisfaction. With a thought, his aura shifted, and he instantly switched to Deva Path : Sage Mode.

He decided to present himself in this form for his daily activities. If he encountered a powerful enemy, it wouldn’t be too late to switch depending on the situation.

« Who are you ? Why are you on top of the Ancestor Tree’s canopy ? »

A crisp voice rang out abruptly, interrupting Asakawa Shin’s contemplation.

He slowly turned his head to see a tan-skinned young girl with hands on her hips, questioning him in a low, wary voice.

Asakawa Shin’s lips curled slightly as he responded in a gentle tone, « Child, do not be afraid. I am the Spirit of the Ancestor Tree. The place where you are standing right now is precisely the top of my head. »

Hearing this, the girl focused her gaze, but the figure before her seemed shrouded in layers of mist. No matter how hard she tried, his face remained blurred.

Her eyebrows shot up in anger as she shouted sternly, « Nonsense ! Don’t try to deceive me ! Say, who exactly are you ? What is your purpose in sneaking into Takigakure while hiding your identity ? »

Asakawa Shin remained indifferent, saying nothing, but silently channeled his Wood Release Chakra.

The tree trunk beneath his feet instantly burst with life ; branches grew wildly and buds sprouted, lifting him slowly into the air before blooming into a sea of flowers.

With a thought, Asakawa Shin drew upon the surrounding Natural Energy.

A breeze rose from the ground, swirling the petals into the air in a dreamlike and beautiful scene.

« Well ? Do you believe me now ? Fu. »

« This... could you really be the Ancestor Tree ? »

Fu covered her lips in shock, but then looked confused. « But how do you know my name ? »

Asakawa Shin waved his hand dismissively. « Of course I know your name. You are the chosen child. The mark on your lower abdomen is the most direct proof. »

Hearing this, Fu gritted her teeth in anger. « What ? So you’re the one behind this mark ! Get rid of it now ! It won’t wash off no matter how hard I try ; it’s so annoying ! »

Asakawa Shin seemed slightly surprised and raised an eyebrow. « Oh ? You don’t like it ? Is the design not aesthetic enough ? I designed it meticulously... »

« Aesthetic my foot ! »

Fu flew into a rage, her cheeks flushing red. « Do you have any idea how embarrassing it is to have it printed there ? ! »

« What a hassle... »

Asakawa Shin sighed helplessly, as if admitting defeat to this troublesome young girl.

« Alright, alright, I give up on you girls. I’ll change the color for you, okay ? Let’s go with pink. »

Before he finished speaking, his figure flickered, closing the distance to Fu like lightning.

Before Fu could react, his palm gently pressed against her lower abdomen.

Chakra surged slightly, and the originally simple black mark was instantly refreshed into an elegant pink. The patterns were even more intricate than before, like a work of art.

By the time Asakawa Shin moved away, Fu finally snapped back to reality.

She looked down at the pink mark on her belly that now looked like a blooming cherry blossom, feeling a deep sense of despair.

« You bastard... »

Fu clutched her face in frustration. « With it looking like this, how am I supposed to wear my favorite crop tops anymore ? ! »

Seeing that Asakawa Shin was completely unmoved, her eyes darted around as she adopted a pitiful expression.

« I’m begging you, please remove this mark. It’s really embarrassing. »

Asakawa Shin shook his head firmly. « Absolutely not. This mark can save your life at a critical moment. You have a fatal calamity in your destiny, and only by relying on it can you survive. »

Then, as if remembering something, he leisurely added :

« Oh, right. This mark must be exposed to nature at all times to be effective. Don’t cover it up ; you should display it openly. »

« You... drop dead ! »

Shaking with rage, Fu pulled off her wooden clogs and threw them at Asakawa Shin with all her might.

But in the next moment, she was shocked to find that Asakawa Shin had vanished without a trace.

She looked around, but there wasn’t a soul in sight.

A chill instantly crawled up her spine. Without even picking up her shoes, Fu bolted toward the Village in a panic.

Asakawa Shin, however, had already quietly left Takigakure.

This time, he didn’t use his usual Flying Thunder God Technique to return to Konoha, but instead experimented with the newly comprehended Five Elements Evasion Technique with great interest.

The earth beneath his feet seemed to lose its resistance in his eyes. Soil, wood, water, and minerals were like illusions, allowing him to dart through the ground like a fish in water.

After a few attempts, he felt it was somewhat dull.

In terms of long-distance travel, this technique was indeed far inferior to the Flying Thunder God ; it was merely better than nothing.

However, it was an excellent choice for reconnaissance or infiltration. After all, who would pay attention to a rock or a plant ?

« It seems it’s time to head back. The one-month deadline is approaching ; I wonder how everyone’s preparations are going. »

No sooner had the thought occurred than his figure abruptly appeared inside the Hokage Office, startling the gathered high-ranking officials.

« Shin, you’re back from your training ? »

Asakawa Shin nodded slightly. Glancing around, he saw that the Konoha high-ranking officials were all present, seemingly discussing important matters ; even Orochimaru was there.

« What’s the situation ? Any movement from Sunagakure or Kumogakure ? »

« Don’t worry, Junior Brother. »

Orochimaru’s feminine voice rang out, his lips curling into a sinister arc.

« Everything is under control. This is their last chance. I have already coordinated everything in the shadows. When the time comes, they will surely strike with all their might, and the 'Konoha Crush Plan' will surely be a great success. Hehehehe~ »

As he spoke, he felt something was off. Seeing everyone staring at him fixedly, cold sweat began to bead on his forehead.

« Why... why aren’t you laughing ? Is it not funny ? »

Tsunade’s eyes lit up, and she immediately ordered, « He really does have ulterior motives. Pummel him ! »

Jiraiya lunged forward. « Orochimaru, I definitely won’t let you leave my side this time ! »
