# Chapitre 199 — Sasori vs Chiyo and Sakura

« Three ? Four ? »

Looking at the two pitch-black scrolls in Sasori’s hands, Chiyo’s body suddenly stiffened, as if she had caught a glimpse of some forbidden truth.

Seeing this, Sakura understood immediately. She retreated quickly, standing on either side of Chiyo in a pincer formation.

« Pointless struggling. » Sasori of the Red Sand’s lips curled into a mocking sneer, and he swung his hands abruptly.

« Bang ! Bang ! »

Thick smoke exploded, and two Human Puppets emitting a terrifying sense of oppression landed on the ground.

« This... this is... »

Chiyo gasped, her usually steady fingers now trembling slightly.

« The Third Kazekage... and even the Fourth Raikage ? You actually turned them into Human Puppets ! »

« Indeed. These are the perfect masterpieces I’ve spent my entire life’s work creating. What do you think ? »

Sasori’s fingers flicked lightly, and thin Chakra Threads danced between them.

As his fingertips moved, the two puppets made stiff and eerie movements, the sound of their joints grinding making one’s skin crawl.

Chiyo glared at this, her chest heaving with uncontrollable grief and indignation.

Although she had suspected it for a long time, it wasn’t until she saw it with her own eyes today that she truly confirmed the cruel reality.

The Third Kazekage, who had once dominated the world, had indeed died at the hands of her own grandson. Even the death of the Fifth Kazekage, Gaara, was inextricably linked to him !

« Sasori ! You bastard ! »

Chiyo’s eyes widened in anger. « Not only did you leave the Village, but you’ve repeatedly betrayed it ! I will never forgive such actions ! »

Sakura’s heart tightened as she realized these two puppets were actually refined from the remains of Kage-level powerhouses.

Just as a bad premonition arose in her heart, the crisis had already arrived.

« Die ! »

Sasori waved his right hand, and the Fourth Raikage’s Human Puppet activated instantly, turning into an afterimage wrapped in the power of thunder, fiercely attacking Sakura.

« So fast ! »

Sakura’s pupils shrunk, but her body was still frozen in place, unable to react at all.

At the critical moment, a great force suddenly pulled her back, narrowly avoiding that fatal blow.

« Th... thank you, Granny. » Sakura panted, still shaken. Just a little more, and she would have been finished.

« Pull yourself together ; this is no joke. »

Chiyo’s expression was grave. « These are forbidden Human Puppets ; they can use the Ninjutsu and Bloodline Limits they had when they were alive ! »

« Exactly ! »

Sasori gave a sinister laugh. The Third Kazekage puppet suddenly opened its mouth, and Iron Sand filled the sky like a black torrent, blotting out the sky.

'The Fourth Raikage for close combat and the Third Kazekage for long range. Sasori has actually grown to this extent.'

A flicker of imperceptible relief crossed Chiyo’s eyes, but in an instant, that relief was replaced by a deeper sense of regret and sorrow.

« Let’s end the sentimental drama here. Iron Sand Shigure ! »

With Sasori’s cold shout, the Iron Sand floating in the sky suddenly compressed, condensing into countless high-density Iron Sand bullets.

Then, they poured down like a rainstorm, covering the area where Sakura and Chiyo were.

Chiyo reacted extremely quickly. The Chakra Threads suddenly retracted, forcefully pulling herself and Sakura together.

Just as the two of them stood firm, the sky full of Iron Sand bullets had already poured down like a rainstorm.

« Boom— ! »

Amidst the loud explosion, dust rose everywhere.

When the dust settled, the scene inside became clear : two simple puppets were holding open Chakra shields, firmly protecting the two of them.

« What ? You still kept those things ? » Sasori’s face darkened.

« Of course. » Chiyo’s gaze was gentle and nostalgic. « These are the first pair of puppets my precious grandson made. They have great commemorative value, don’t they ? What were they called again... I’m getting old. »

Seeing Sasori’s face turn increasingly grim, Chiyo stopped smiling and said solemnly, « They are called 'Father' and 'Mother'. Sasori, do you still remember ? »

« Hmph, those are the only failures I’ve ever acknowledged. » Sasori’s voice was as cold as ice.

« What an untruthful child. »

« Shut up ! Go to hell ! »

Sasori was completely enraged, and his fingers suddenly pressed down.

The Fourth Raikage Human Puppet clicked and clattered, crashing forward again like a cannonball, this time aiming directly at Chiyo.

Chiyo reacted extremely quickly, controlling the two puppets, « Father » and « Mother, » to instantly block her. The Father puppet threw a heavy punch, while the Mother puppet deployed a Chakra shield.

Boom !

Lightning collided with the shield, and the massive impact sent Chiyo and Sakura stumbling back several meters.

Sakura gritted her teeth, green Chakra condensing in her palm. Taking advantage of the moment the Fourth Raikage Human Puppet was pinned down by Chiyo, she suddenly charged from the flank.

« Cherry Blossom Impact ! »

She punched toward the puppet’s joint connections.

However, at the moment her fist was about to make contact, the scattered Iron Sand seemed to come alive, instantly condensing into an indestructible shield in front of the puppet.

« 砰 ! »

The Iron Sand shield shattered, and Sakura’s fist also throbbed with intense pain from the massive counter-force.

She was horrified : 'Capable of both offense and defense, how troublesome.'

« Hmph, overestimating yourself. » Sasori glanced coldly at Sakura, his fingers moving slightly. « Since you want to die so badly, I’ll send you on your way first. »

« Sakura, get out of the way ! »

Chiyo’s exclamation suddenly reached her ears. Before Sakura could react, the thick curtain of Iron Sand in front of her suddenly split open to form a passage.

Immediately after, the Fourth Raikage Human Puppet shot out from within, wrapped in the power of thunder, its lightning-covered iron fist smashing toward Sakura.

« Don’t even think about it ! »

Chiyo gritted her teeth, her fingers dancing rapidly as she tried to control « Father » and « Mother » to rush forward and block.

However, the sensation from her fingertips made her heart sink.

The two puppets were now like they were stuck in a swamp. Not only were their joints stiff, but even moving a single step became exceptionally difficult, as if they were being dragged down by an invisible force.

« Oh... oh no... »

Chiyo’s face instantly turned pale.

« Too naive. » Sasori’s lips curled into a mocking sneer, his eyes full of contempt.

One should know that the Third Kazekage’s Magnet Release : Iron Sand was the natural enemy of puppet masters.

Not only could it suppress any steel mechanical parts inside a puppet, but those tiny Iron Sand particles, invisible to the naked eye, could also take advantage of any opening to creep into the puppet’s most precise joint gaps.

At this moment, the insides of Chiyo’s puppets were already filled with Iron Sand, making them as heavy as mountains and completely losing their former agility.

Chiyo made a prompt decision and quickly changed her strategy, actively disconnecting the Chakra Threads linked to « Father » and « Mother, » attempting to mimic her previous risky move and control Sakura’s body to escape the danger.

However, Sasori’s eyes flickered ; he had already anticipated her intentions.

A thick curtain of Iron Sand instantly rose from the ground, completely isolating the space between Chiyo and Sakura, sealing off Sakura’s path of survival.

« Boom— ! »

Accompanied by a deafening roar, that lightning punch had already smashed down heavily.

Chiyo immediately froze in place, as if she had fallen into an ice cellar.

« Sa... Sakura ! ! ! »
