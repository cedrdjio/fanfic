# Chapitre 118 — Medical Specialty Class

Before long, Tsunade walked back looking refreshed, followed by a bruised and swollen Kakashi.

Seeing this, Asakawa Shin couldn’t help but burst out laughing and teased, « Not bad, is Team 7 going for a unified look ?

But having said that, Sakura, you’re the only one without injuries now. It makes you look a bit like the odd one out. »

The four members of Team 7 turned their heads in unison, giving him a collective glare, and then subconsciously sized each other up.

« Pfft... Naruto, that pig-head look of yours is just too funny~ »

« You have the nerve to talk about me ? Look at those panda eyes of yours, Sasuke~ »

« And that teacher, what was his name again... Hahaha, I’m dying... »

« Um, my name is Kakashi Hatake. It seems we should start with some self-introductions. »

...

« Oh ? It seems Team 7’s first meeting went smoothly. Let’s not waste any more time, follow me ! »

Before Tsunade’s voice could even fade, she turned and strode away. The remaining three exchanged glances and quickly followed.

When the group arrived at an open training ground, Tsunade finally stopped.

She turned to face the three of them and said solemnly :

« First, I want to make one thing clear : I am your Jonin leader, not your instructor. Our relationship is strictly that of superior and subordinate.

You must understand this.

Of course, if Karin or Shizune have anything they don’t understand, you can ask me anytime. »

Hearing this, a mischievous glint flashed in Asakawa Shin’s eyes, and he couldn’t help but poke fun :

« Oh my, Senior Sister, do you really not want to be my teacher that much ?

Or is it... that you’re already impatient to fulfill that promise ? It’s okay, I’m ready whenever you are. »

Tsunade gave him a fierce glare and said overbearingly :

« Mind your status ! Now that you’ve joined my squad, you are my subordinates. This isn’t some house-playing squad assigned at graduation.

In this squad, my orders are absolute. Do I make myself clear ? »

Seeing Tsunade so serious, Asakawa Shin also reined in his joking mood and replied solemnly, « Yes, Captain ! »

« Good, you’ve got spirit ! »

Tsunade nodded in satisfaction and immediately issued her first captain’s order to Asakawa Shin :

« In that case, go get me a few bottles of good sake right now.

Actually... just book a private room !

Our squad is officially formed today, we have to celebrate properly. Let’s go, let’s go~ »

« Eh ? Wait, shouldn’t we have a bell-snatching test first ? »

« What would we snatch that thing for ? Are you crazy ? Stop talking nonsense and come drink with me... »

After saying that, Tsunade intimately threw her arm around Asakawa Shin’s shoulder and dragged him forward without allowing any argument.

« Wait, Senior Sister, you don’t intend to make me pay again, do you ? »

« Nonsense, do I look like someone who has money ? »

« But didn’t you just win a huge bet ? How could you be out of money ? »

« Most of the winnings went to paying off debts, and the rest was cheated away by Shizune. Where would I have any money ? Stop whining, are you paying for this meal or not ? »

« Senior Sister, I’ve realized your mouth is truly stubborn. »

Behind them, Karin was full of curiosity and leaned in to ask Shizune, « Senior Shizune, that promise Lord Shin mentioned just now... what did it mean ? »

Shizune’s eye twitched slightly as she gave a disdainful glance at Tsunade’s back and lowered her voice : « Actually, it’s... »

« What ? ! Lady Tsunade is actually robbing the cradle ? She made a move on Lord Shin this early ? »

Karin’s eyes widened in realization. « No wonder I felt the atmosphere between them was weird. In that case, wouldn’t the Senju Clan... »

« Shh— ! Keep your voice down ! »

Shizune hurriedly covered her mouth, her face turning pale. « If Lady Tsunade hears you, we’re both dead ! This is an absolute secret that cannot be told ! »

« Understood, understood. »

...

« Boys, bring out the best sake and the most expensive dishes in the shop ! My Junior Brother is treating today, be quick about it ! »

The moment Tsunade stepped into the tavern, she couldn’t wait to call the waiters. However, Asakawa Shin felt no pain in his wallet after hearing this ; the reason was simply that he owned this tavern.

To be precise, all the shops on this famous Konoha gourmet street were actually joint ventures between him and the Akimichi Clan.

Aside from the cost of ingredients and labor, there were almost no extra expenses for this meal.

Tsunade, however, knew nothing of this and looked smug at the moment, as if she had gotten a huge bargain.

Seeing this, Asakawa Shin laughed inwardly : 'Since you want to take advantage so much, I’ll let you take your fill. Sooner or later, you’ll end up paying with your whole self. No, I also need to have Sister Kana buy up all the casinos in Konoha. Any casino that doesn’t know its place, I’ll send Sister Shizune to crush them...'

After a few rounds of drinks, Asakawa Shin saw that Tsunade’s cup was empty and hurriedly filled it up, asking casually, « Senior Sister, what exactly does our squad do ? »

By now, Tsunade was somewhat tipsy, her eyes hazy and cheeks flushed, as she mumbled indistinctly :

« Hmm ? Didn’t the old man tell you ? We’re a Medical Specialty Class. Normally, we just take missions at the Konoha Hospital. You won’t have to do all the dirty and tiring work like your classmates.

But since you two just became Genin, I can’t let you have it too easy...

So I’ve decided to take some D-rank missions first, to let you experience the hardships of an ordinary Genin’s life.

Of course, if there are specific missions you really want to do, we can take those too. »

« Oh ? A Medical Specialty Class ? Come to think of it, all four of us happen to be Medical Ninjas.

Karin, your medical talent is quite outstanding. This is a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity to learn from Senior Sister Tsunade, so make sure to seize it ! »

Hearing Asakawa Shin say this, Karin also nodded solemnly.

She knew well that this was an opportunity many in the Ninja World could only dream of, and she secretly vowed to work twice as hard.

« Lady Tsunade, please guide me well ! I’ll toast to you ! »

« Hahaha, you got it ! »

Tsunade laughed heartily and then looked at Shizune beside her.

« But just drinking juice is no fun. You should learn more from your Sister Shizune. Look at her, her alcohol tolerance has increased greatly under my training ! »

Tsunade tilted her head back and drained the cup in one go, her movements bold and uninhibited.

However, drinking so fiercely meant the intoxication soon swept over her.

Sure enough, not long after, she became drowsy and slumped onto the table, unconscious.

Asakawa Shin shook his head helplessly and turned to Shizune, who was still somewhat sober, suggesting :

« Sister Shizune, stop drinking. While you can still recognize the way, point me in the right direction and I’ll take you back.

Karin, you support Sister Shizune ; I’ll handle Senior Sister Tsunade. »

As the night deepened, Asakawa Shin carried the deeply sleeping Tsunade on his back out of the tavern. Looking at Shizune beside him, whose steps were unsteady and swaying, he asked with concern :

« By the way, Sister Shizune, where are you staying ? If you don’t have a place yet, why not move into our villa for the time being ? There are plenty of empty rooms... »

« There’s no need for Lord Shin to worry, we... »

« Ouch ! »

Before Shizune could finish, a sharp pain came from her waist. It turned out Karin, who was supporting her, had secretly pinched her.

The pain cleared her head instantly, and the refusal at the tip of her tongue took a sharp turn. She changed her tune :

« ...We were just worrying about having no place to stay. Since you’ve extended such a kind invitation, we’ll humbly accept. Any room will do, thank you for the trouble. »

Asakawa Shin also nodded with a hint of helplessness, a trace of annoyance appearing on his face. « Sigh, since you have nowhere else to go, there’s no other way.

Sister Shizune, put your hand on my shoulder. We’re teleporting home. »
