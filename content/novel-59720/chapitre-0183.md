# Chapitre 183 — System Crash

With Hidan’s strength, he was no match for Kakuzu at all.

Even if Hidan managed to collect blood while Kakuzu was careless and activated Death Possession Blood...

Since Kakuzu also possessed an immortal body, the technique didn’t work, and the outcome was already obvious.

Asakawa Shin satisfiedly took over the tied-up Hidan, then pulled out a scroll from behind and tossed it to Kakuzu.

Kakuzu happily took the scroll, and upon opening it to see it was indeed 30 million ryo, he immediately beamed with joy and hurriedly thanked him.

« Thank you, Boss ! You’re so generous, Boss ! Feel free to find me for jobs like this in the future ! »

Asakawa Shin nodded slightly at Kakuzu, thinking to himself : 'This guy is practically a born workhorse ; as long as the pay is enough, he works himself to death without a single complaint.'

Honestly, he even felt a hint of admiration for Kakuzu ; the guy was truly a perfect tool.

Thinking this, he casually tossed another scroll and calmly instructed : « There’s a 10 million ryo bonus inside. I’ll be in seclusion busy with experiments lately and won’t have time for missions with you, so act on your own for now. »

Kakuzu caught the scroll cleanly and, without a single word of nonsense, turned and left.

As a smart man, he knew the boundaries of protecting his boss’s privacy.

Once Kakuzu’s figure had completely vanished from sight, Asakawa Shin slowly turned his head, casting a cold gaze at Hidan, who was still cursing incessantly.

He unceremoniously stepped on Hidan’s chest, the immense force instantly choking back the foul language from the other’s mouth.

Asakawa Shin flickered and disappeared with Hidan, appearing in Orochimaru’s laboratory in the next instant.

Orochimaru was focused on an experiment when the sudden appearance of a figure startled him ; his fingers trembled, and a test tube slipped, shattering into shards on the floor.

Orochimaru’s pupils constricted sharply, and the corners of his eyes twitched uncontrollably.

'It’s over ; several days of hard work have gone down the drain again.'

Suppressing his displeasure, Orochimaru turned to look ; sure enough, Asakawa Shin stood there with his hands behind his back, his disguise already shed.

Asakawa Shin approached with a smile and said, « Senior Brother, I’ve brought you another top-tier project—Immortality. What do you think, interested ? »

Before he finished, the gloom in Orochimaru’s eyes vanished instantly, replaced by a fervent smile.

He grabbed Asakawa Shin’s hand. « Oh, Junior Brother ! The project doesn’t matter at all ; the fact that you took the time to visit your Senior Brother is what makes me happiest ! »

As he spoke, his gaze drifted downward toward Hidan.

'This guy has two large holes in his chest and is still cursing ; it seems this is the so-called Immortality.'

Hidan was still swearing, but when he looked up and saw the feverish eyes of Orochimaru and Asakawa Shin, all his curses got stuck in his throat.

He swallowed hard, his arrogant aura instantly extinguished, and a hint of irrepressible trembling seeped into his voice :

« You... what do you two want ? I’m warning you, if you dare touch a hair on my head, Lord Jashin will never let you off ! »

Orochimaru raised an eyebrow and curiously repeated, « Jashin ? »

Asakawa Shin sneered disdainfully. « Just a remnant of the old era. I’ve already sensed his traces ; I’ll drag him out and study him thoroughly soon. »

Orochimaru nodded thoughtfully, a glint in his eyes. « I see. So, this guy’s so-called Immortality is entirely because of that Jashin ? »

He stared intently at Hidan, muttering to himself, « Truly bizarre. To survive even with a destroyed heart, and the wounds are slowly repairing... this 'Immortality' is worthy of its name. »

Over the next period, Orochimaru and Asakawa Shin researched Hidan’s Immortality in the lab.

From his initial arrogance to his current subservience and painful pleading, Hidan had been tortured to the brink of a mental breakdown.

However, Asakawa Shin felt no burden in his heart. This guy had preached wantonly in the Land of Hot Springs, ruthlessly killing anyone who refused to join or whose conversion failed ; no treatment was too harsh for him.

« What’s 1000 minus 7 ? »

As soon as Hidan opened his eyes, Asakawa Shin’s voice rang in his ears, frightening him into tears. « I’m sorry, I was wrong, I was really wrong. Please stop experimenting on me, I’ll do anything. »

« Oh ? Anything ? » Asakawa Shin rubbed his chin, seemingly considering it.

Seeing a chance, Hidan immediately pleaded, « Yes, I’ll do anything, please let me go. »

Asakawa Shin smiled slightly and handed Hidan a scroll. « In that case, show your worth.

The technique on this scroll is called 'Dead Demon Consuming Seal'. Once cast, it can seal an enemy’s soul ; even the Nine-Tailed was defeated and sealed by this move.

If you learn this technique, you’ll have utility value. »

Hidan immediately put on a show of extreme gratitude, took the scroll, and struggled to get up and kowtow to Asakawa Shin.

Prostrate on the ground, deep in his eyes was pure malice and hatred as he muttered, « Don’t worry, I will definitely learn this technique, definitely. »

Under Asakawa Shin’s daily enthusiastic medical assistance, Hidan’s desire to learn soared, and he actually mastered this Fuinjutsu in less than a month.

One day, Asakawa Shin and Orochimaru came to Hidan’s lab again, wanting to explore and research further.

To their surprise, Hidan had completely changed ; his previous obedience was gone, replaced by an arrogant and overbearing attitude.

He looked at the two with madness in his eyes. « Prepare yourselves. I’ve learned the 'Dead Demon Consuming Seal'. Today... I’ll make you pay. »

However, contrary to the panic he expected, they were watching him with great interest.

Asakawa Shin even impatiently reminded him, « Then hurry up and cast it. Let me see how your training went. »

Hidan was instantly enraged, his hands forming seals at a snail’s pace as if he had Parkinson’s.

After a full ten seconds, the technique finally activated, and a cold wind swept through the enclosed lab.

Under the shocked gazes of the three, a massive ghostly figure appeared behind Hidan ; it was unmistakably the Shinigami.

Hidan laughed maniacally. « It worked ! I really succeeded ! »

In his loss of emotional control, Hidan’s laughter turned into heart-wrenching weeping, his venomous gaze locking onto Asakawa Shin.

« You bastard ! I’m going to kill you today no matter what ! »

Before he finished, he violently manipulated the massive Shinigami phantom behind him, pouncing toward Asakawa Shin with claws and fangs bared.

Asakawa Shin just smiled slightly, placed a hand on Orochimaru, and vanished instantly, leaving Hidan standing alone in the sealed lab, looking dumbfounded.

« You bastard ! If you have the guts, don’t run ! Get out here ! »

No matter how hoarsely Hidan roared, Asakawa Shin was nowhere to be found.

With his meager skills, he was completely unable to break through the reinforced walls of this sealed lab.

Time passed quietly in anxious waiting. Just as Hidan was about to continue his shouting, his expression suddenly changed without warning.

The price of casting the 'Dead Demon Consuming Seal' is sacrificing one’s own soul to the Shinigami ; whether it succeeds or fails, the caster is doomed to die.

At this moment, the Shinigami began to harvest Hidan’s soul.

However, at this critical moment, a sudden change occurred.

A strange Yin Release energy intervened, firmly protecting Hidan and preventing the Shinigami from pulling out his soul.

Two eerie Yin Release Chakras instantly became fiercely entangled, neither giving way.

They seemed to have no intelligence, merely operating instinctively according to certain established rules.

After a stalemate, a loophole seemed to appear, and eventually, both fell into a strange silence, as if they had « crashed. »

Hidan gasped for air ; the experience just now made him shiver, feeling as though he could truly die at any moment.

He silently sensed the surging power within him and was shocked to find...

That the originally wild and untamable power of the Shinigami and Jashin could now be controlled at will, without paying any price.

Hidan was stunned for a moment, then burst into a fit of ecstatic laughter.

« Hahaha ! I... am invincible ! »
