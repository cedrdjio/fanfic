# Chapitre 15 — Long-term Meal Ticket

Everyone scrambled to stop the bleeding and bandage Hyuga Sosuke. After some treatment, they finally pulled him back from the brink of death.

Asakawa Shin couldn’t help but sigh in relief. This guy was just too fragile. If he had actually killed someone during a spar, it would be impossible to explain.

He was a guest of the Hyuga Clan, and Hiashi had even treated him to a grand meal.

« Nephew, don’t be too upset. This is Sosuke’s own fault ; you know he’s from the Main Branch and lacks combat experience... » Hiashi, fearing Asakawa Shin might feel guilty, prepared to console him.

« What ? That guy is a Jonin from the Main Branch ? Why didn’t he use Rotation to block the wind pressure ? To get himself into this state... he really is a flower in a greenhouse, fragile and weak. »

Asakawa Shin skillfully shifted the blame, expressing his deep dissatisfaction with Sosuke’s performance and claiming that he, the guest, had been frightened.

Hiashi rolled his eyes, the rest of his words stuck in his throat. This shameless brat had no heart ; he didn’t need consolation at all.

...

« Swish, swish, swish. »

'What’s that sound ?'

Hiashi turned his head, puzzled, and saw Hinata scribbling away with pen and paper, an excited smile on her face. He asked curiously, « Hinata, what are you doing ? »

« Ah... Father, I’m recording what happened today. »

Hinata replied while writing, « Look, Father, Shin-kun is a peerless genius, and I am a peerless genius too, but he is just so amazing and so confident ! »

« I... I’m not quite there yet, so I want to record his actions and words, study them frame by frame. One day, I’ll be just as amazing and confident as he is ! »

Hiashi felt a headache coming on. He just wanted to say that such effort wasn’t necessary, but looking at his daughter’s serious expression, he couldn’t bring himself to say it.

If his daughter wanted to change, he, as a father, should support her. It couldn’t be worse than her current timid state, probably.

...

With the wounded treated, the events at the training ground finally came to an end. The crowd began to disperse slowly, and the Anbu Ninjas also returned to report to the Hokage.

« Nephew, was that attack also a taijutsu technique from the Konoha Six Styles ? Why was the power so exaggerated ? »

Hiashi finally couldn’t help but ask. The dispersing crowd gathered back around, and the Anbu Ninjas also sneaked back, all silent and listening intently.

« That’s right. That was the Tempest Kick from the Rokushiki. I infused it with my understanding of swordsmanship and the nature transformation of Wind Release Chakra. I named it Tempest Kick : Cycle Sever. »

« This move creates a seal-less Wind Release : Vacuum Blade that can launch indiscriminate attacks on everything around it, severing everything, » Asakawa Shin said honestly.

« Actually, it’s not just the Tempest Kick. Other techniques of the Rokushiki can also be freely developed according to the user’s will. »

« The Rokushiki is just a basic template with infinite possibilities. How to apply it depends on the user themselves. »

« Hiss... So it really is the Rokushiki. » Hearing Asakawa Shin’s words, the surrounding Ninjas couldn’t help but gasp, contributing a little bit more to the global warming of the Ninja World.

Although everyone knew the Rokushiki had extraordinary potential, they hadn’t expected it to be this powerful. The lethality of this 'Cycle Sever' was already comparable to a Tailed Beast Ball.

« Shin-kun, can I learn this move ? » Hinata asked with shining eyes.

« Of course you can learn it. Didn’t I say we are both peerless geniuses ? I will teach you the Rokushiki without reservation, but how much you can learn depends on you. »

Hiashi said with some emotion, « Thank you, Nephew. If it weren’t for you, I wouldn’t have known Hinata hadn’t been eating enough. It was also you who discovered Hinata’s talent. »

« And now you’re even teaching Hinata to cultivate. Nephew, I, Hyuga Hiashi, will never be able to repay your kindness in this lifetime. If you ever have any requests in the future, the Hyuga Clan will definitely support you with all our might ! »

Hearing this, Asakawa Shin’s eyes lit up. He quickly grabbed Hiashi’s sleeve and began his act :

« Uncle, please don’t talk about repaying kindness. I help Hinata because I see myself in her ; it is definitely not to hold kindness over her head for a reward. »

« You know, Hinata and I are both peerless geniuses. »

« So I feel her pain as my own. »

« My parents were only Chunin, and my family situation wasn’t very good. »

« I’ve never had a full meal from childhood until now. The meal I had at your house today is the happiest meal I’ve ever eaten. »

« I was thinking at the time, how great it would be if I could eat a full meal every day ! »

Hyuga Hiashi interrupted with a headache : « Alright, Nephew, you don’t need to say anymore. From now on, I will set up a kitchen next to the Ninja Academy, responsible for your and Hinata’s meals. »

« I will have them work in three shifts, with unlimited ingredients. You can eat as much as you want ; no need to worry about going hungry. »

Although Hiashi felt a pinch in his wallet, the thought that a mere bit of money could win over a Kage-level powerhouse—and this Kage-level powerhouse was only 6 years old, his talent beyond common sense, not to mention he was teaching his own daughter—made it worth it.

The Hyuga Clan was wealthy ; could they be eaten into poverty ? Thinking of this, Hiashi felt he had won big.

Asakawa Shin also felt he had won big. Who was Hinata ? She was his future battle Pal. Cultivating Hinata was his own desire anyway.

This trip not only solved Hinata’s cultivation problem but also solved his own food problem.

How gratifying, truly gratifying.

« By the way, Uncle, if Neji wakes up, please tell him that if he is interested in taijutsu, he can come to my Six Styles Training Class. I consider myself to have a tiny bit of insight into taijutsu cultivation. »

Asakawa Shin suddenly remembered Neji, Rock Lee, and Tenten. Among the Konoha 12, those three were the only ones missing from the Six Styles Training Class. His collecting obsession and OCD were flaring up, so he decided to take this opportunity to bring them into the fold.

Hyuga Hiashi was overjoyed. Although he had exchanged for the Rokushiki training method from the Hokage based on his contributions, there was a big difference between that and being taught personally by Asakawa Shin.

From the strike that seemed to tear the sky apart, one could see his attainments in taijutsu. If Neji could join the special training class, it would be a great thing for him too.

« Then many thanks, Nephew. »

Asakawa Shin nodded and bowed to Hiashi : « Uncle, if there’s nothing else, I’ll take my leave. »

« Go ahead, Nephew. »

Hiashi felt a sense of relief. This kid was just too much trouble. If he stayed any longer, he feared this kid would cause more chaos, and his heart couldn’t take it.

Asakawa Shin bid farewell to the Hyuga and walked towards the Hokage Building.

The last time he submitted the Rokushiki training scroll to Hiruzen Sarutobi, he had promised that he could choose one Ninjutsu from the scroll of seals.

It was time to go see what that was all about.
