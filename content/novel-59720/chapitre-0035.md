# Chapitre 35 — Huh? You're faking death, too?

Kakuzu looked in astonishment at the teenager who had suddenly appeared before him ; his surefire strike had actually been blocked ?

Refusing to believe it, he secretly applied more pressure with his foot, but the teenager’s expression didn’t change in the slightest, and he even wore a calm, composed smile.

This teenager had immense strength ; he had put almost all his effort into that strike, yet the other party remained nonchalant.

In terms of raw strength, he had likely suffered a complete defeat, especially since the way the other party was exerting force at that moment was much more difficult than his own.

However, one might lose the fight, but not the spirit. It’s just smiling, right ? Who doesn’t know how to smile ? Kakuzu started to smile as well.

Among the three, only Shikaku was drenched in sweat. Hearing the creaking sound of their feet colliding, he felt like he was at risk of being crushed to death at any moment.

Shikaku couldn’t help but shout, « Shin, stop showing off, save me, save me quickly ! »

Asakawa Shin’s smile faltered upon hearing this. He had been enjoying the clash, feeling incredibly cool, but it was a pity Kakuzu didn’t possess Conqueror’s Haki. He hadn’t expected the atmosphere to be ruined by Luo... ah, no, by Shikaku.

Oh well, the Nara family members were all high-performance work pals ; he couldn’t let him die here.

Thinking of this, Asakawa Shin straightened his expression. His right index finger turned black in an instant, and he violently jabbed toward Kakuzu’s heart.

« Flying Finger Pistol : Yang-Hua Thrust ! »

Asakawa Shin’s finger rubbed violently against the air, creating a red-gold flame that attached to the air cannon generated by the Flying Finger Pistol, firing off in an instant.

« Nani ? Hand-seal-less... »

Kakuzu was greatly shocked, losing the calm smile he had just moments ago. He was too close to Asakawa Shin and only had time to slightly shift his body before being struck by the attack. Due to the height difference between the two, he was sent flying dozens of meters high like a tattered rag.

'Damn it, actually ambushing an old man like me ! How despicable !'

...

« Uncle Shikaku, are you alright ? » Asakawa Shin helped Shikaku up with a pained expression. With this work pal injured so badly, it would definitely affect his work performance, right ?

Nara Shikaku sighed upon hearing this, « I’m fine, just sustained some injuries, but the brothers are all... Shin, you must avenge the brothers ! »

Asakawa Shin’s expression turned somewhat strange. He shouted at the 'corpses' of the Konoha Ninja, « Brothers, stop pretending, reinforcements are here ! Is there any medical Ninja ? Quickly, treat Uncle Shikaku. »

Shikaku, still immersed in grief and not yet understanding Asakawa Shin’s meaning, saw a 'corpse' crawl up upon hearing him. With a 'poof,' it released the Transformation Technique. Although it looked weak, where was the eerie appearance of broken bones and snapped tendons from just moments ago ?

It wasn’t just him. With a series of 'poof' sounds, the guys who were corpses just moments ago all climbed up one by one, and several Ninjas even crawled out of the water.

« What the hell, Gekkō Hayate, you were faking death too ? »

« Huh ? Yūgao, you were faking death too ? As expected of you, even faking death looks so good. »

« Ouch, brothers, give me a hand, my leg is broken ! »

« Haha, I’m okay, just seven broken ribs. »

« What the hell, brothers, I thought I was the only one left who died, it scared the life out of me. »

« Stop messing around, how could Water Release kill anyone~ »

...

Nara Shikaku stared at all this, dumbfounded. He felt that the scene before him was extremely absurd, making it hard to distinguish reality from a dream. Oh no, could it be an illusion ?

He used his unbroken hand to form a one-handed seal, « Genjutsu, Release ! »

It didn’t work... That means...

« What the hell, you bunch of disloyal guys, you actually watched me about to be beaten to death ? »

« But, wasn’t that the order you gave at the last moment, Lord Shikaku ? No matter what, we must survive... »

« Yeah, yeah ! We were super hard at work. »

« That’s right, I’ve never used the Transformation Technique so well before. »

« ... »

Asakawa Shin ignored the commotion and focused his attention on Kakuzu. At this moment, Kakuzu had already fallen from the sky and crashed onto the rocks, creating a small crater.

Half of his body had been blasted apart, looking exceptionally miserable. But Asakawa Shin knew that he had only shattered one of his hearts ; there were still four such hearts. However, within his perception range, there were only three. One more, the Lightning Release one, was missing...

Was it destroyed and he hadn’t had time to replenish it, or had it escaped while he wasn’t paying attention at the beginning ?

Kakuzu’s expression was extremely ugly, not just because he had encountered a strong enemy and had one heart destroyed, but also because of that group of shameless Konoha Ninja.

He had originally felt smug about wiping out the other party with his excellent Water Release, but he hadn’t expected that he hadn’t killed a single one. Were they bullying him because he didn’t know sensory Ninjutsu ?

Also, is my Water Release really that bad ? Unforgivable !

Kakuzu controlled the Earth Grudge Fear to constantly repair his body. The current situation was extremely unfavorable for him. In his carelessness, his Earth Release heart had been directly destroyed, and the opponent was clearly a Ninja skilled in taijutsu, which meant that once approached, he would have no power to fight back.

'Damn it, originally taijutsu Ninjas were completely countered by my Earth Spear technique, but now...' Kakuzu thought unwillingly.

It was fortunate that Asakawa Shin didn’t know what Kakuzu was thinking ; otherwise, he would definitely have retorted loudly :

« Bro, what level are you at ? You want to counter me ? Ever heard of Armament Haki internal destruction ? »

Kakuzu slowly stood up. By now, his body had been repaired, and he looked as if he hadn’t been injured at all.

He smiled at Asakawa Shin and said, « That’s a truly impressive taijutsu. It reminds me of my first encounter with you Konoha Ninja. That man’s taijutsu was also this powerful, leaving a deep impression on me !

Oh, you don’t know who he is, right ? I’ll tell you... »

« He is none other than—the First Hokage, Senju Hashirama. »

« Nani ? ! »

Shikaku, as if triggering some underlying code, said in shock, « You’ve fought with Lord First Hokage ? Just how old are you, you guy ? »

The other Ninjas were also greatly shocked.

« No wonder he always calls us brats, so he’s actually that old ? »

« His body is different from ordinary people ; his longevity is likely related to this... »

« This is trouble. A Ninja who could fight Lord First Hokage and still escape unscathed—his strength is likely unimaginable ! »

« What are you afraid of ? We have the invincible Lord Shin here. Didn’t you see him send that tentacle man flying with a single blow just now ? Lord Shin is the best ! »

Asakawa Shin looked over in surprise. Who had such good taste ?

Hmm ? The purple-haired female Anbu Ninja holding a sword... Could it be Yūgao Uzuki ?

She’s actually a fan of mine, she has great taste ! Asakawa Shin returned a look of approval.

« An opening ! »

Seeing that Asakawa Shin dared to be distracted while facing him, Kakuzu immediately launched an all-out attack.

« Combined Ninjutsu : Searing Wind Wave ! »

This time, Kakuzu used his full power, and the destructive force far exceeded his previous strike against Shikaku, intending to severely injure Asakawa Shin in one fell swoop.

« Tsk, it’s so hot ! Senior, why do you resort to sneak attacks without a word ? »
