# Chapitre 30 — wood brocade

The Daimyo trotted down the jade steps with short, quick steps and walked to the side of the silk in pleasant surprise. The nobles on both sides also gathered around to admire it up close. Only when they were close could they see the details of the silk ; the surface was smooth and fine, the luster soft, and the colors vibrant and pure, unlike any ordinary fabric.

« Hisss… what kind of fabric is this ? It’s so beautiful ! »

« A treasure, it truly is a treasure ! A treasure I have never seen before ! »

« If one could wear this, wouldn’t it be… ? »

Upon hearing this, the Daimyo couldn’t help but reach out and touch it. Feeling the texture of the silk, he was even more astonished. « The texture is so smooth and soft ! A fine treasure, truly a fine treasure. »

Nara Shikaku sneered disdainfully in his heart, but on the surface, he respectfully said to the Daimyo, « Lord Daimyo, these are merely ordinary silks. Although they are valuable, they cannot represent Konoha’s gratitude toward you. The true treasure we wish to present is here. »

« What ? These aren’t it ? What on earth is the treasure ? Present it at once ! » The Daimyo was shocked upon hearing this. He was almost getting numb from Nara Shikaku’s theatrics, but after all, he was the Daimyo, so he quickly regained his composure and asked eagerly.

These didn’t even count as treasures ? What kind of thing could surpass them ? These Ninjas really do offer good things when they have them.

Nara Shikaku walked solemnly to a box. This box was a size smaller than the others, but its appearance was more unique, carved with dragons and phoenixes—truly luxurious.

He gently opened the box and slowly introduced it, « This bolt of silk is called wood brocade. It was woven by dozens of skilled craftsmen working in shifts day and night for several months. Currently, this is the only bolt in the world. We in Konoha wish to present the first bolt of wood brocade in the world to you. Only such a fabric is worthy of your status, Lord Daimyo ! »

« wood brocade ? The first in the world ? » The Daimyo was also stunned. His gaze was completely captivated by this wood brocade. The silk was golden overall, embroidered with landscapes, flowers, birds, fish, and insects. Every warp and weft contained different colors, with delicate patterns that were dignified yet elegant.

« wood brocade ? A truly magnificent wood brocade ! »

« This was actually woven directly into it ? It’s truly beautiful. »

« Hisss… that fish looks like it’s alive ! Its eyes are moving ! »

Originally, according to the original schedule, this wood brocade would not have been ready in time for this tribute. However, Asakawa Shin had a sudden inspiration : since I have already started planting mulberry trees, why can’t you all weave fabric ?

Thus, under his suggestion, every clan sent elite Ninjas to learn weaving from master craftsmen. In the end, four clans stood out. They were Konoha’s two great dojutsu clans : the Hyuga and the Uchiha Clan. The Ninjas they sent had excellent eyesight and learned quickly, soon surpassing their teachers.

The other two were the Aburame Clan and the Ninjas from the Medical Department. The reason they stood out was their strong micro-manipulation skills ; they could actually use Kikaichu and Chakra surgical threads to assist in the operation, making their weaving ability incredibly strong.

Kakashi Hatake was inspired by this and took out the Hidden Sand Village puppet secret techniques from his family’s collection for everyone to study. As everyone knows, controlling puppets requires the use of Chakra threads. After learning this, everyone’s weaving speed reached a new level. With three shifts working around the clock, this wood brocade was woven in just a few days.

Upon seeing the finished product, Hiashi made up his mind right then and there. When he returned, he would train the branch family Ninjas who were not suitable for combat in this direction. In the future, their Hyuga Clan would build a textile factory in their clan grounds and contract the high-end wood brocade business for the Musi Group.

So what if they have the Sharingan ? Awakening it is so difficult, and anything less than three tomoe simply can’t compare to our weaving speed. As expected, the number one dojutsu clan in the Ninja World has to be us, the Hyuga.

The Hyuga Clan is getting better and better under my leadership. Hehehehe.

The Daimyo truly couldn’t put this wood brocade down, constantly holding it up against his body. The nobles nearby were also extremely envious. Finally, someone couldn’t help but ask, « Ninjas, how much more of this silk do you have ? Are you selling it ? »

As soon as these words were spoken, the scene suddenly went quiet. Even the Daimyo turned his gaze over. After all, although wood brocade was good, there was only one bolt, enough to make only one set of clothes. How could that ever be enough ?

Nara Shikaku said somewhat embarrassedly, « We only used secret methods to weave these silks as a tribute to the Daimyo. The production process is extremely complex and difficult ; it is time-consuming and labor-intensive, so we hadn’t considered selling them. »

Upon hearing this, the noble almost got anxious and hurriedly retorted, « How can you not sell it ? How can you only weave this little bit of such good stuff ? Besides, this amount isn’t enough for Lord Daimyo. »

The Daimyo also nodded, « I have felt Konoha’s sincere heart. I like these silks and this wood brocade very much. You have put in a lot of effort, and I will reward you handsomely. However, what Hideyoshi said just now also makes some sense. This fabric will be gone after making a few sets of clothes, and it is indeed not enough. Why don’t you weave some more ? »

Perhaps seeing Nara Shikaku’s dilemma, the Daimyo added slowly, « How about this ? I also know your difficulties. These silks seem like things from heaven and must be difficult to weave. If you can weave more, I am willing to buy them at a high price. »

Nara Shikaku hesitated, « This… can this really be sold ? »

The nobles nearby, hearing that there was a chance, hurriedly persuaded him, « It will definitely sell, my friend ! We will buy as much as you can weave. »

« Yes, yes ! How could such good things fail to sell ? Just go ahead and weave. »

But Nara Shikaku still hadn’t made up his mind, looking hesitant. The Daimyo also asked patiently, « What is it ? Do you have any other concerns ? Why not tell me ? Let me see if I can help you solve them. »

Nara Shikaku hurriedly bowed and replied, « Reporting to Lord Daimyo, silk weaving is not easy. The materials and craftsmanship are all of the highest quality, and we dare not have the slightest mistake. If we sell them, the price will certainly be expensive, especially that wood brocade, whose value is simply immeasurable. I’m afraid… I’m afraid that if we weave them… and they don’t sell, I won’t be able to answer to the Hokage. »

« Hahahaha. » The nobles nearby all laughed out loud upon hearing this, as if they had heard some funny joke.

« How much do you intend to sell it for ? Why don’t you tell us ? I’d like to know just how high it is that you’re afraid we can’t afford it, hahahaha. »

Nara Shikaku smiled shyly. He pinched his fingers as if calculating, and after a long while, he answered cautiously, « A bolt of silk will cost at least 200,000 ryo, and the wood brocade is too precious ; it will cost at least… »

He slowly raised one finger, signaling one million ryo per bolt. This price was already equivalent to the reward for an S-rank mission. Selling fabric for a set of clothes at this price could be considered an astronomical price.

Seeing this finger, even the Daimyo frowned slightly. He sighed and said, « Ten million ryo per bolt is indeed a bit expensive. However, for such a treasure, there is a reason for it to be expensive. How about this ? First, give me fifty bolts. It would be best if the patterns and colors are all different. This won’t be too difficult for you, will it ? »
