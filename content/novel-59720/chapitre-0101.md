# Chapitre 101 — Business War

Underground training ground.

« Ninjutsu : Shuriken Shadow Clone Technique : Modified ! »

Asakawa Shin suddenly threw out a specialized Flying Thunder God Kunai, and the Ninjutsu activated instantly...

In a split second, it split into hundreds of identical Shadow Clones, sweeping toward the training wooden dummies like a tide.

In an instant, Asakawa Shin’s figure vanished like a ghost, reappearing next to a Flying Thunder God Kunai.

With a soft hum of the blade, he sliced off a wooden dummy’s head.

It didn’t end there ; as if his movements were frame-skipping, he flashed again and appeared beside another wooden dummy.

Sword shadows flickered, cutting it into two pieces.

In the blink of an eye, dozens of wooden dummies were all struck in their vital points, falling apart into pieces.

Asakawa Shin nodded with satisfaction. Utilizing Shadow Clones for frantic cultivation over the past month had finally paid off ; he had finally reached a level suitable for actual combat.

Perhaps he wasn’t much different from Minato Namikaze now.

After all, his physical strength far exceeded the other’s, making his error tolerance terrifyingly high.

Possessing both high stats and high skill, he was simply incredibly devious.

« Very good, I’ll just keep developing quietly like this, and then shock everyone ! »

Asakawa Shin waved his hand casually, and the Anbu Ninja beside him quickly stepped forward to clean the scene thoroughly.

The gaze they directed at Asakawa Shin clearly held a fanatical sense of admiration.

'Is this the future Hokage ? Where exactly will the future of Konoha lead ? Just thinking about it makes one incredibly expectant.'

A sharp light flashed in Asakawa Shin’s eyes. Since his « Homecoming Ninjutsu » was finally complete, it was time to set out on a new journey.

He had to gather the Tailed Beast Chakra as quickly as possible to avoid long nights leading to many dreams and unforeseen complications.

After a moment’s thought, he decided to temporarily set aside the One-Tail Shukaku of the Hidden Sand Village.

In any case, it would come to him sooner or later ; there was no need to rush.

The Seven-Tails Chomei of Takigakure could also be put off for now. After all, the opponent’s strength was weak and could be easily handled at any time ; there was no need to be too hurried.

It was better to save it and package it together with the One-Tail Shukaku.

Considering he could only absorb the Chakra of two Tailed Beasts at a time in the short term, it was perfect to group these two together.

As for the Three-Tails Isobu and Six-Tails Saiken of the Hidden Mist Village, he placed them at the very end.

After all, that place was now Obito’s base camp ; acting rashly might lead to unnecessary complications.

Although in the original plot, Yagura being released from the Genjutsu didn’t cause much of a stir, Asakawa Shin still intended to leave it for last.

The remaining targets were only the Four-Tails Son Goku and Five-Tails Kokuo of Iwagakure.

The Four-Tails Jinchuriki, Roshi, was stubborn and had political disagreements with the equally stubborn Tsuchikage Ōnoki.

He had long since left the Village to travel the world ; this person would be easy to deal with.

As for the Five-Tails Jinchuriki, known as the « Steam Ninja, » Han, as a top combatant of Iwagakure, he often left the Village to execute the highest-difficulty S-rank missions.

Asakawa Shin made a prompt decision and set him as the first target.

Having made up his mind, he immediately created a Shadow Clone.

After giving a few simple instructions, he set out on his journey.

Asakawa Shin had never been to the Land of Earth, which was located in the northwest of the Ninja World.

The territory was full of rocky hills, completely different from the fertile Land of Fire, which was perhaps the reason it repeatedly initiated Ninja World wars.

While traveling, Asakawa Shin did not remain idle.

He planted trees as he walked.

He even specifically left a hollow space in the middle of the trees, hiding a wooden Flying Thunder God Kunai inside.

This way, if he needed to in the future, he could teleport directly here.

He planted one every few dozen kilometers, which could be considered quite a contribution to the « energy conservation and emission reduction » of the Ninja World.

Since planting trees along the way took quite a lot of time, it took Asakawa Shin a full three days to reach the Land of Grass.

He specifically took a detour to check the ruins of Kusagakure, only to find that it hadn’t been rebuilt, and he didn’t know where the new Kusagakure had moved to.

He clicked his tongue ; as expected, the saying that criminals always love to return to the scene of the crime did not deceive him.

After a moment of mourning, Asakawa Shin continued his journey.

As long as he crossed the border to the north, he would officially enter the territory of the Land of Earth.

...

« Ninjutsu : Reverse Harem Technique : Modified ! »

Poof !

Dozens of identical figures appeared on the spot, all of them being Asakawa Shin transformed into Obito’s appearance.

That’s right, Obito’s skin was back for a limited time.

Asakawa Shin’s original body clapped his hands and gave a loud command to the army of Shadow Clones before him :

« Everyone, I’m counting on you for what comes next !

Each of you take a Flying Thunder God Kunai. Once you find Roshi, do not act rashly.

Just activate the Flying Thunder God Mark to notify me. Wait until I deal with the Five-Tails before we meet up.

Oh, right, while traveling, plant Flying Thunder God coordinates within the Land of Earth.

The tasks are assigned. The first one to find Roshi will be heavily rewarded. Let’s begin the operation ! »

Watching the Shadow Clones scatter, Asakawa Shin nodded with satisfaction.

This technique wasn’t entirely useless ; it was quite reliable for serious business. Tobirama’s hatred value -1.

Asakawa Shin’s original body took a short break and then continued to speed toward Iwagakure.

To reach Iwagakure, he had truly put in a lot of effort, crossing several large mountains and planting many trees along the way.

At this moment, Asakawa Shin looked at Iwagakure in the distance with surprise.

This was actually a Village surrounded by several high mountains, truly a natural fortress that was easy to defend and hard to attack.

Asakawa Shin closed his eyes and focused his mind, sensing carefully.

As expected, the Five-Tails' massive Chakra was as conspicuous as a light in the dark, and was accurately captured by him in an instant.

« It’s good that he’s in the Village ; it saves me from having to search all over the Ninja World.

But I don’t know when he’ll leave the Village. Since I’m idle during this time, why not find some fun ? »

Asakawa Shin narrowed his eyes slightly, a cunning and mischievous smile curling his lips. He slowly turned his gaze toward the vast mulberry forest at the foot of the mountain and clapped his hands in praise :

« As expected of Iwagakure, they really have strength ! In less than a year, they could create so many mulberry trees.

Looking at this scale, there must be at least dozens of hectares, right ? Could it be that they’ve scoured all the mulberry trees in the Ninja World ? »

Asakawa Shin’s guess was indeed correct.

Ever since that silk expo, the Tsuchikage Ōnoki had set his sights on silk.

He even began to reuse the long-neglected Insect Users—the Kamizuru Clan.

Decades ago, when Iwagakure and Konoha met in battle, the Kamizuru Clan, acting as the vanguard, actually lost to the Aburame clan of Konoha, who were also Insect Users.

After their defeat, they were neglected by Iwagakure due to the mission failure, and the clan members gradually withered away until very few remained today.

Who would have thought that this time, a pie would fall from the sky and hit them.

Now, the Kamizuru Clan had transformed into the favorites of Iwagakure, with the whole Village counting on them to raise silkworms and produce silk.

To cooperate with the Kamizuru Clan, Ōnoki did not hesitate to spend heavily to purchase mulberry trees in the Ninja World.

The price was so high that even Asakawa Shin had once been tempted, which led to the creation of this impressively scaled mulberry forest.

However, since Asakawa Shin was here today, it was their bad luck !

Without hesitation, he slammed his hands together, directly activating Wood Release Chakra.

A massive amount of Chakra spread silently through the earth, modifying these dozens of hectares of mulberry forest.

In the unknown depths of the ground, a bizarre change was quietly occurring.

The Root systems of the mulberry trees seemed to have an acceleration button pressed, starting to extend horizontally frantically.

An hour later, Asakawa Shin withdrew his Chakra with satisfaction.

By this time, the underground Root systems of the mulberry trees had expanded to more than ten times their original size, densely filling the limited soil space.

Mulberry trees were already deep-rooted species, and now under the catalysis of Wood Release, their Root systems were incredibly developed.

However, too much of a good thing is a bad thing.

Such a massive Root system would not only intercept a large amount of photosynthetic products and soil nutrients but also compete with each other for oxygen.

This would cause the above-ground parts to have a series of reactions due to « hunger, » eventually inevitably falling into a vicious cycle.

A living organism is like a precision instrument ; if any link has a problem, it will bring disastrous consequences.

Barring any accidents, this mulberry forest was essentially ruined.

Unless someone could immediately discover it and spend a lot of manpower and resources to clean up the redundant Roots, there was absolutely no possibility of saving it.

Asakawa Shin covered his mouth and laughed mischievously : « Don’t blame me, after all, high-end business wars often use the most primitive methods !

Watch me directly kill your money tree~ »
