# Chapitre 173 — Entrusting the Future?

« Tail-Locking Seal ! »

With a low shout from Jiraiya, he pressed one hand onto the now-incapacitated Shukaku.

The massive Tailed Beast body instantly disintegrated, re-condensing into Gaara’s small form, who immediately fell asleep.

Jiraiya let out a long sigh of relief, a hint of smugness flashing across his face. « At least this Sage Mode didn’t go to waste ; it finally came in handy. »

Naruto, standing to the side, cast a look of disdain and sighed inwardly : 'Pervy Sage really is thick-skinned. He truly is a famous author ; without this kind of mental fortitude, he couldn’t have written Icha Icha Paradise.'

With matters here concluded, the group placed their hands on Asakawa Shin’s shoulders, and in a flash, they instantly returned to the Konoha battlefield.

In the center of the stadium, the bodies of the two previous Hokage had already begun to crumble.

Surrounded by the crowd, Tsunade gazed at her relatives who were about to dissipate completely. The suppressed grief in her heart finally broke through, and tears rolled down her face like broken strings of pearls. She wept bitterly, her heart breaking, almost suffocating.

First Hokage Hashirama looked at his granddaughter, who was weeping uncontrollably, his eyes filled with helplessness and affection :

« Tsunade, there is no need to be so sad. We are already dead ; this is merely returning to the afterlife.

Being able to witness Konoha thriving decades later, and seeing that you have grown into a capable, independent Ninja, we are already satisfied. »

Compared to his brother’s sentimentality, Second Hokage Tobirama appeared more composed and restrained.

His gaze was like a torch, scanning the surroundings sharply.

He saw that although Konoha had endured the flames of war, it remained vibrant and full of life, with confidence for the future burning in everyone’s eyes.

Tobirama’s tense lips finally curled slightly upward, and he nodded. It seemed that this future generation could indeed be entrusted with peace of mind.

Asakawa Shin saw this and immediately understood.

He stepped forward quickly, gently patting Tsunade’s shoulder, soothing her in a soft voice.

Upon seeing him, Tsunade cried even louder. While sobbing, she secretly signaled to her two grandfathers.

Hashirama immediately understood and hurriedly began to speak in a stuttering manner, just as planned :

« Well... Shin, we are about to leave, but from now on, Tsunade will be all alone. My heart really cannot rest easy. »

Tobirama really couldn’t watch anymore and shoved his older brother aside. This acting was simply too clumsy.

Fortunately, Asakawa Shin was busy and flustered while soothing Tsunade, so he didn’t notice this awkward scene.

Tobirama coughed lightly, pulled Asakawa Shin’s hand over, and said solemnly : « Tsunade has been spoiled by us since she was young, so her temper is a bit big. Shin, you will have to bear with her in the future. »

Asakawa Shin finally realized what was going on. He hurriedly interlaced his fingers with Tsunade’s and promised solemnly : « Rest assured, Grandfathers, I will definitely take good care of Senior Sister Tsunade. »

Hashirama nodded in satisfaction and added : « It would be best to get married soon and have a few big, healthy boys to carry on the Senju Clan bloodline. »

'Have children ?' Tsunade’s cheeks instantly flushed crimson. She stole a glance at Asakawa Shin, and a hint of sweetness unexpectedly welled up in her heart ; it seemed... that wouldn’t be so bad.

With their wishes fulfilled, the Edo Tensei bodies of the two could no longer hold up and began to crumble inch by inch.

Two clear soul forms detached from within, nodded gently to the two of them in greeting, and then transformed into specks of white light, merging into the heavens and earth, vanishing without a trace.

After respectfully sending off the two previous Hokage, the group immediately moved to the Hokage Office to hold an emergency meeting.

When they saw Asakawa Shin return safely, with the now-restored Gaara lying at his feet, the Konoha high-ranking officials present finally felt relieved and let out a long sigh.

« That’s great ! With this, all the invading enemies have been captured by us ! »

« As expected of Lord Shin, a slight move from him has achieved what would be our absolute limit ! »

« What should we do next ? Should we directly declare war on the Land of Wind and the Land of Lightning ? They are currently severely weakened ; it’s the perfect time to strike ! »

Hiruzen Sarutobi listened to the surrounding noise and waved his hand wearily.

« First, have the prisoners strictly detained, especially the two Tailed Beast Jinchuriki ; they must be kept under tight guard. As for subsequent matters, we will decide after we have rested and regrouped. »

...

While Konoha was undergoing a brief post-war recuperation, in a secret base underground in the Land of Grass, the figure of Black Zetsu slowly emerged, breaking the dead silence.

« Obito, the situation is even worse than expected ! »

Black Zetsu’s voice carried a hint of gravity. « The Allied Forces suffered a crushing defeat. Only Yugito is missing, while the rest of the high-level combatants have been almost completely wiped out.

Kazekage Rasa, Raikage Ai, and Danzo are all confirmed dead, and the Ichibi and Eight-Tails have also been captured and sealed by Konoha. What should we do next... ? »

After listening, Obito felt a massive headache coming on.

He couldn’t understand at all how such a luxurious lineup, even if it couldn’t achieve a complete victory, wouldn’t end up in such a total annihilation.

The original plan was to sit on the mountain and watch the tigers fight, and after both sides were exhausted, they could reap the rewards.

Who would have expected the situation to take such a sharp turn for the worse, with Konoha emerging unscathed and victorious in every way.

After a moment of silence, his figure suddenly stiffened, and then he roared through gritted teeth :

« It’s Orochimaru ! It’s because of this bastard’s betrayal that we would never have suffered such a crushing defeat. Is he crazy ? A rogue Ninja actually still willing to risk his life for Konoha ? »

Black Zetsu sighed lightly, his tone carrying a hint of impatience :

« Now is not the time to dwell on this. The existence of Konoha has seriously hindered our 'Eye of the Moon' plan.

Now, we must bring out someone more powerful to lead us... »

Obito narrowed his eyes, seemingly guessing something : « What do you mean ? »

« Didn’t you say you have already mastered the Edo Tensei jutsu ? »

Black Zetsu’s voice was low and seductive. « Obito, it is time. Let us resurrect Lord Madara. »

As soon as Black Zetsu’s words fell, they were rejected by Obito without hesitation.

« No ! »

Perhaps realizing his attitude was too agitated, Obito took a deep breath and softened his tone :

« I mean, it hasn’t reached that point yet. Actually... I still have a plan. »

Black Zetsu’s expression darkened instantly, though fortunately, his skin was as dark as charcoal, so Obito didn’t notice anything unusual.

At this moment, he suppressed the anxiety in his heart and asked patiently : « Oh ? Obito, what other brilliant plan do you have ? Why don’t you tell me quickly. »

« The Three-Tails has not yet been resurrected ; even if we summon Lord Madara now, it’s meaningless. It’s better to endure for now. Besides... »

A gleam of light flashed in Obito’s eyes, appearing confident and in control. « There should be people more anxious than us right now, right ? »

Black Zetsu was stunned for a moment, then suddenly realized : « You mean Nagato ? But he is just a vessel we selected.

Although he possesses the Rinnegan, it is not his own power after all. He cannot bring out the true power of the Rinnegan, and I’m afraid he is no match for Asakawa Shin. »

« If he is no match now, can’t we just give him a boost ? »

Obito sneered, his tone filled with ruthlessness : « Also, White Zetsu, you immediately send clones to collect the corpses of powerful figures from previous generations. We must prepare early... »
