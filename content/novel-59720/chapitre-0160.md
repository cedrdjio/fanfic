# Chapitre 160 — You Get What You Pay For

The two little ones on the stands were frolicking together, but Naruto below was already drenched in sweat.

Facing Hinata, he was at a disadvantage in power, speed, reaction, and technique ; he only barely maintained the situation by relying on his massive Chakra reserves and stubborn endurance.

To neutralize Hinata’s lightning-fast offensive, Naruto was forced to use the Shadow Clone Technique, attempting to counter and exhaust her stamina with human wave tactics.

Ironically, this was his planned tactic, but now it made it difficult for him to keep going.

The Shadow Clone Technique is extremely taxing on Chakra ; if not for his extraordinary talent and the Eight Trigrams Seal forcibly drawing the Nine-Tailed’s Chakra to act as a power bank, he would have been overwhelmed long ago.

However, Hinata had already seen through his intentions. Facing the siege of Shadow Clones, her movements were light and precise, dispelling the attacks with a mere tap of her fingertips, never wasting a single bit of stamina.

Consequently, Naruto had instead walked into his own trap, falling into the exhaustion snare he had set.

« No, I’ll definitely lose if this keeps up. I have to get serious ! »

Deciding in his heart, Naruto quickly formed hand seals and summoned two more Shadow Clones.

Hinata’s Byakugan instantly captured this scene, and she couldn’t help but speak up to warn him :

« Naruto, these little tricks are useless against me. I’m not someone like Kiba ; no matter how many clones you make, it’s all in vain. »

However, the Shadow Clones before her didn’t swarm as usual ; instead, they stayed quietly by the original’s side, the three of them coordinating perfectly as if brewing something.

Under Hinata’s suspicious gaze, a deep blue Chakra sphere condensed in Naruto’s outstretched right palm, spinning at an incredible speed.

« This is ? To be able to compress such a massive amount of Chakra together... what incredible power ! »

Through her Byakugan, Hinata clearly saw the terrifying energy contained within the Rasengan, making her realize that the destructive power of this move was absolutely not to be underestimated.

Naruto scratched his head and chuckled, his tone carrying a hint of showing off :

« How about it, Hinata ? This move is called the Rasengan. It was originally my trump card for Sasuke ! You’d better be careful next. »

Halfway through, his voice involuntarily trailed off.

Because he discovered with horror that Hinata’s face had turned terrifyingly dark at some point, and her body was emitting a suffocating pressure.

« Who do you have to learn from... why learn from that girl Sakura, always talking about 'Sasuke, Sasuke' all day long ? It’s so annoying ! »

Hinata’s eyebrows arched, she gave a cold snort, and a cold light flashed in her eyes.

« And you just said it, right ? This move is a trump card for Sasuke. Do you really think you can defeat me so easily ?

It seems I need to teach you a lesson today, or you won’t know how formidable I am ! Bring it on ! »

Naruto swallowed hard, feeling bitter in his heart :

'Oh no, did Hinata take the wrong medicine ? Why did she suddenly become so violent ? Her emotions are way too unstable ! What should I do now ?'

He glanced at the high-speed spinning Rasengan in his hand, gritted his teeth, and thought : 'Whatever, I’m going all out today.'

« Hinata, take this ! Rasengan ! »

With a loud shout, Naruto gripped the Rasengan and charged toward Hinata like an arrow from a bow.

At the same time, the few remaining Shadow Clones also erupted, coordinating from all directions to launch a siege on Hinata.

« Hmph, mere child’s play. »

Hinata snorted coldly, lowered her stance slightly, and instantly assumed the starting posture of the Eight Trigrams Palms, then her whole body began to spin violently like a top.

« Kaiten : Amanodan ! »

In an instant, she skillfully used the centrifugal force of the high-speed spinning Kaiten to sweep out with a kick, launching a Rankyaku : Amanodan.

A crescent-moon-like blue slash whistled out with the sound of breaking air, instantly slicing all the incoming Shadow Clones in two.

With its momentum unabated, it headed straight for Naruto’s original body.

Naruto’s pupils shrunk, and he quickly pushed the Rasengan in his hand toward the slash, trying to meet it head-on.

« Boom— ! »

A deafening roar exploded as the two powerful forces collided in mid-air, the resulting intense shockwave sweeping outward like ripples and kicking up clouds of dust.

Naruto’s figure was immediately sent flying like a broken kite, while the shockwave heading toward Hinata was easily deflected by the still-active Kaiten barrier, leaving her unscathed.

As the rotation of the Kaiten gradually stopped, Hinata stood in place, her breathing slightly rapid.

In contrast, Naruto was struggling to climb up from the dust in the distance, looking disheveled, with a striking trail of blood hanging from the corner of his mouth.

He reached up to wipe away the blood and smiled broadly : « As expected of you, Hinata. This move combines offense and defense ; it’s simply flawless. »

Naruto looked around, seeing deep cracks left on the surrounding walls, and a nearby large tree had been cut in half ; the scene was a mess.

The audience broke into an uproar.

« Both of these kids are so strong, and you’re telling me they’re Genin ? »

« That must be the famous genius of the Hyuga Clan ; she really lives up to her reputation. »

« As expected of the eldest daughter of our Hyuga Clan ! I could never mistake that move ! It’s Lord Shin’s ultimate technique, 'Rankyaku : Amanodan' ! I didn’t expect her to actually reproduce it ! »

Listening to the discussions around her, Hinata felt dazed ; she hadn’t expected her childhood dream to actually come true today.

Meanwhile, Naruto was scratching his head, looking helpless : « I can’t do this, I can’t hold back anymore, or I won’t even get past Hinata. I have to use that move. Damn it, I wanted to keep it as a hidden card. »

He coughed lightly and said seriously : « Hinata, you’d better be careful with this next move ! If you feel like you can’t block it, admit defeat immediately ! »

Before his voice could fade, he suddenly bit his fingertip and quickly formed hand seals : « Ninja Art : Summoning Technique ! »

Along with a burst of thick smoke rising, a small toad appeared on the field.

Naruto was shocked at the sight : « Gamakichi ? Why is it you ? Where’s your old man ? »

Gamakichi curled his lip, looking dissatisfied : « Big brother Naruto, with your current Chakra level, it’s already quite good that you can summon me.

The amount of Chakra my old man needs is no joke. As they say, you get what you pay for, and you’re actually looking down on me ? »

Naruto was suddenly extremely embarrassed.

Gamakichi looked around and then grinned : « That girl opposite us is the enemy, right ? Don’t worry, leave it to me ! I’m very strong ! It’s a guaranteed win, okay ? »

After saying that, he leaped up and lunged aggressively toward Hinata.

« Wait... »

Before Naruto could stop him, Gamakichi was punched flying by Hinata, foaming at the mouth, and the summoning was released.

Facing Hinata’s gradually cooling gaze, Naruto broke into a cold sweat and scratched his head awkwardly :

« That... actually, this wasn’t my hidden card ; there seemed to be a bit of an accident just now. How about... you wait for me to recover some Chakra, and we’ll go another three hundred rounds ? »

Hinata said nothing, just clenched her pink fists, her eyes sharp as knives, as she approached Naruto step by step.

« Eh ? No, no, no... give me a chance, just once ! Lady Hinata~ »

« Ah— ! »

With a scream, the referee stepped onto the stage, looked at the already unconscious Naruto, and shook his head helplessly.

Contestant Uzumaki Naruto is unable to fight ; contestant Hyuga Hinata wins !

On the stands, Asakawa Shin slapped his forehead, looking speechless :

« Even a lion uses its full strength when hunting a rabbit. Naruto was too careless ; he deserved it. You truly are the 'Number One Unpredictable' Ninja. »
