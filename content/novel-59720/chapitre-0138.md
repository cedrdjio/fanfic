# Chapitre 138 — Wood Release: Wood Human Technique!

« Tailed Beast Ball ! »

Shukaku jerked his head, aiming the Tailed Beast Ball he had been brewing for so long directly at the Wood Dragon that was right in front of him, and fired it with ferocious force.

Asakawa Shin’s eye twitched imperceptibly. He swept his gaze over the group of examinees on the edge of the battlefield who were still watching the spectacle and sighed helplessly.

Immediately, he maneuvered the Wood Dragon. It opened its massive maw and swallowed the Tailed Beast Ball whole.

The moment the Wood Dragon consumed the Tailed Beast Ball, it underwent a violent distortion, its entire body swelling and bulging outward.

Then, under the terrified gazes of the surrounding examinees, it exploded with a thunderous roar !

The terrifying shockwave swept through the surroundings like a raging tide, destroying nearly a kilometer of forest and turning it into dust.

Fortunately, the examinees had already retreated to a distance, wary of the aftermath of the battle between Asakawa Shin and Orochimaru.

Otherwise, this single destructive blow would have been enough to cause heavy casualties.

As the smoke slowly cleared, the battlefield beneath Shukaku was left in ruins.

The majestic Wood Dragon had been blown into two pieces, scattered pathetically on the ground.

Shukaku was also panting heavily. Although the Wood Dragon had acted as a meat shield and absorbed most of the attack, the aftermath of the Tailed Beast Ball still left him in intense pain, and he was quite injured.

However, he was feeling extremely excited at the moment. He roared at the sky, « How about that ? Don’t you dare look down on me again ! The combination of me and Shukaku is invincible ! »

In the distance, Asakawa Shin was still locked in a fierce battle with Orochimaru and the other two. This senior’s endless survival techniques truly opened his eyes, and Tobirama’s elusive Flying Thunder God was particularly annoying.

However, Asakawa Shin keenly noticed that Orochimaru’s Chakra was running low and wouldn’t last much longer ; it seemed he would be able to capture her alive soon.

Hearing Gaara’s shout, he took a moment to glance at the distant battlefield and rolled his eyes speechlessly.

The next instant, he erupted with power, lightning-fast, beating back Orochimaru and the others, his hands weaving signs at high speed.

In an instant, the forest beneath his feet seemed to be infused with life. Countless ancient trees twisted and rose madly, intertwining to form a majestic Wood Dragon that roared toward Shukaku once again.

Asakawa Shin’s lips curled slightly, and he shouted loudly, « As expected of you, Shukaku, to be able to take my casual strike. So, how will you respond to this ? »

Watching the Wood Dragon take shape again, Shukaku could not help but be shocked.

« What ? A Ninjutsu of this scale can be condensed again in the blink of an eye ? How is this possible ! »

Without giving him any chance to react, the Wood Dragon, carrying a deafening roar, charged toward him.

Looking at the rapidly approaching Wood Dragon, a rare look of human-like panic appeared on Shukaku’s originally arrogant face.

In the mental space, the human and the Tailed Beast met urgently to discuss countermeasures.

Shukaku made a grave decision, « We absolutely cannot let him get close again ! Wood Release is too effective against me. Once he gets close, my Chakra will surely be absorbed in large quantities.

With the ebb and flow of power, I will definitely lose.

Listen well, Gaara, you and I must coordinate perfectly and use that technique. »

Gaara nodded solemnly, his eyes firm, « Don’t worry, this time, we will definitely take him down. »

Outside, Shukaku slammed his palms into the ground, and violent Chakra poured out, instantly turning the surrounding several miles of land into quicksand.

« Sand Waterfall Imperial Funeral ! »

Accompanied by Gaara’s roar, yellow sand filled the sky, rising up like a golden waterfall and pouring down, completely engulfing the roaring Wood Dragon.

It wasn’t over yet. The quicksand that made up Shukaku’s body spread, merging with the sand waterfall. In an instant, it condensed into a towering pyramid-shaped sand tomb, sealing the Wood Dragon tightly inside.

As the dust settled, Shukaku slowly withdrew his palms, forcing himself to stand tall and proud, and shouted triumphantly, « Hahaha, how is the power of our combo Ninjutsu, 'Sand Desert Layered Imperial Funeral Seal' ? »

Although he shouted loudly, he was at the end of his tether.

Despite being a Tailed Beast, which is said to have almost endless Chakra, he felt an unprecedented emptiness at this moment.

The entire Tailed Beast was panting heavily, looking like it was about to burn out.

When Asakawa Shin saw that his Wood Dragon had been sealed in a single encounter, he was instantly furious.

A mere Shukaku, daring to provoke him again and again, was truly unforgivable.

Fine, I will let you see my true power.

In a flash of thought, he pressed his hands together, his internal Chakra surging wildly, and a shout resounded across the battlefield, « Wood Release : Wood Human Technique ! »

In an instant, the forest beneath his feet seemed to wake up ; countless branches grew wildly, intertwining.

In just a moment, a ferocious yet holy-looking Arhat giant was born in this sea of trees.

It was majestic, standing three hundred meters tall, reaching the heavens and the earth, exuding a suffocating and terrifying sense of oppression.

Even Senju Hashirama was shocked beyond measure at this moment.

At his peak, his Wood Human Technique was only about two hundred meters, while the Wood Human summoned by Asakawa Shin was three hundred meters tall, exceeding it by a full half.

One must know that every ten meters of size increase meant a huge difference in mass, let alone this hundred-meter gap.

At this moment, this Arhat was truly like a god descending, emitting an indescribable and terrifying pressure.

Standing at its feet, everyone was as small as ants.

Orochimaru felt this particularly deeply. She stared at the Wood Human, her face full of unbelievable shock.

'No wonder this kid has been so relaxed ; so this is your trump card ?'

At this moment, she had no intention of fighting anymore ; only one thought remained in her mind—to escape as quickly as possible.

Catching a glimpse of Shukaku, who was also in shock, a wicked idea flashed through Orochimaru’s mind.

Immediately after, under her control, she and the two Hokages resurrected by Edo Tensei dashed toward Shukaku.

Her intention was obvious : to shift the battlefield to Shukaku’s side, thereby buying herself a chance to catch her breath and, incidentally, divert Asakawa Shin’s attention.

Even though Shukaku was a Tailed Beast that had lived for a thousand years and was knowledgeable, he couldn’t help but be dumbfounded.

He widened his eyes, his mouth open wide enough to fit a Nine-Tailed, as if he had seen the Six Paths Sage in person.

Deep inside, he roared wildly, « What kind of joke is this ? This guy is much taller than me ! How are we supposed to fight such a monster ? Not to mention my Chakra is almost exhausted... Gaara, let’s retreat ! »

Even the raging fire in Gaara’s eyes was extinguished by the cold reality.

His young face was filled with terror.

Unfortunately, it was already too late to leave.

Since Orochimaru and the others were fleeing in that direction, Asakawa Shin locked onto them all instantly and then maneuvered the Wood Human to pursue them.

Every step it took caused the ground to shake violently.

Shukaku glared at Orochimaru and the others, who were now right in front of him, grinding his teeth in hatred.

How could he not know what these guys were up to ?

But his body, made of sand, was slightly cumbersome, and he couldn’t shake off these three agile flies.

The Wood Human pursued relentlessly, approaching Shukaku in an instant. Before it even arrived, its massive shadow completely enveloped him.

He looked up abruptly, only to see the Wood Human blocking the scorching sun. Against the light, it appeared even more majestic and oppressive, like a god.

Orochimaru hurriedly shouted to Shukaku, « The enemy of an enemy is a friend. Why don’t we form an alliance for now and fight Asakawa Shin together ! »

Shukaku glared at them fiercely, and after a moment of hesitation, nodded.

Asakawa Shin did not interrupt them. After they finished discussing, he spoke loudly, « If alliances were useful, why would I bother training so hard ? I won’t bully you ; come at me together. »

The examinees gathered at the edge of the battlefield were already stunned.

Shikamaru habitually scratched the back of his head, looking at his two teammates beside him with a grave expression, « I think... we’d better retreat a bit further to be safe. I have a feeling that guy Shin might pull off even more shocking moves at any moment. »

Ino, however, waved her hand dismissively, her tone confident, « Relax, Shin definitely knows I’m here ; he wouldn’t hurt us. »

Hyuga Hinata stared at the towering, terrifying figure, biting her lower lip, deep feelings of powerlessness surging in her eyes, « Shin... so this is your true strength ? It’s terrifying. Is this what they call a peerless genius ? Then... what does all my effort count for ? »

Inuzuka Kiba, beside her, looked at her and comforted her in a low voice, « Don’t be silly, Hinata. That guy Shin has long since transcended the category of 'human.' Comparing yourself to him is just asking for trouble. »

On the other side, under Karin’s careful treatment, Sakura had awakened.

The snake venom Orochimaru injected looked scary, but it actually just caused her to faint. It seemed she never intended to take her life from the beginning, which was why Sakura was able to wake up so quickly.

After some treatment, Sasuke had also recovered some of his combat power.

Team 7, Karin, and Haku—the five of them—were standing on the edge of the battlefield, watching this earth-shattering battle.

Witnessing the heaven-shaking Wood Human, Naruto couldn’t help but scratch his head, clicking his tongue, « Just how strong has this guy Shin become ? No matter how I look at it, it feels like he’s no longer in the same dimension as us, right ? »

Sasuke gently touched his right eye, his eyes full of unwillingness, and whispered through gritted teeth, « Just you wait, Shin... I will definitely catch up to you. »

Karin was much simpler-minded. At this moment, she held her hands over her heart, her mind full of Asakawa Shin’s heroic figure, and jumped up and down, shouting, « Go for it, Lord Shin, blow all these bastards away ! »
