# Chapitre 120 — Setting Off, Land of Waves!

« Yeah, Naruto, we just finished a mission. We caught a pretty incredible wanted criminal~ »

Asakawa Shin had a mysterious smile on his lips, his tone carrying a hint of playfulness.

« A wanted criminal ? »

When Naruto heard this, he immediately exploded with excitement, his eyes bulging wide in disbelief.

« You guys are already doing missions of that level ? Dammit... why are we still doing these endless chores ! »

Naruto rushed in front of Hiruzen Sarutobi, shouting with a face full of defiance :

« Old man Third, I’ve had enough of those chores ! I want to take on difficult high-level missions too, like catching wanted criminals or something. I can definitely do it ! »

Hiruzen Sarutobi glanced at him with a half-smile, slowly raised his finger, and pointed at the Daimyo’s Wife in the distance, who was holding Tora and paying the reward with a beaming face.

« Are you sure ? Look over there, that’s the famous wanted criminal Shin was talking about. »

Naruto followed the finger and was instantly shocked. « What ? That fat woman is a famous wanted criminal ? Why is she taking out money ? Is she paying the bounty ? »

Kakashi couldn’t help but reach out and rap Naruto on the forehead, explaining patiently :

« Idot, it’s the cat ! That’s a habitual runaway, and its owner frequently posts missions to hunt it down.

Also, that is the Daimyo’s Wife. Be more polite when you speak. »

Naruto rolled his eyes in annoyance and glared at Asakawa Shin. « Che, so it was just that cat ! Shin, you tricked me again. I hate you so much. »

Before he finished speaking, he simply plopped down on the ground and started acting like a spoiled child. « I don’t care, I don’t care ! I’m not doing these unchallenging missions anymore. Switch it to something else ! »

Iruka was furious upon hearing this and slammed his hand on the table as he stood up.

He was truly afraid these students didn’t know the heights of the heavens and depths of the earth and would put themselves in danger. At this moment, he couldn’t help but scold sternly :

« Naruto, you idiot ! You’re just a rookie who’s just started. Everyone starts by accumulating experience from simple missions ! »

Naruto, however, interrupted him impatiently, craning his neck to argue back :

« That’s not true ! Shin went on an S-rank mission in his first year and even saved Shikamaru’s dad !

I don’t care ! All we’ve been doing lately are boring chores. I want to take on higher-level missions too ! »

Before he could finish, Naruto took another blow to the back of his head from Kakashi, making him roll on the ground in pain.

« That’s enough, Naruto. That guy Shin isn’t normal to begin with.

If someone said he wasn’t human, I’d believe them. Don’t compare yourself to him. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi also chimed in with pride, « That’s right. Shin is a genius that appears once in a millennium in the Ninja World. Anyone compared to him will pale in comparison.

Furthermore, Naruto, the mission system is far more complex than you imagine. Today, I’ll explain the inner workings to you... »

So, he began explaining as if no one else was there. After a while, he realized no one was listening at all. Veins popped on his forehead in anger, and just as he was about to lose his temper...

Asakawa Shin’s gentle voice rang out at the right time, interrupting his progress bar :

« Teacher, in my opinion, Naruto and the others have already grown into Genin who can hold their own. It wouldn’t hurt to give them some high-difficulty missions.

To be honest, the D-rank missions lately have been a bit dull. Why don’t our two classes team up and execute a more challenging mission together ? What do you think ? »

Hearing his beloved pupil speak, Hiruzen Sarutobi thought for a moment and nodded in agreement. « Fine, since you put it that way, I’ll give you a C-rank mission. You will be responsible for protecting someone. »

« Really ? Protecting who ? Are we protecting some feudal lord or Daimyo ? Or maybe a princess on the run ? »

Naruto finally got his wish. He stopped sitting on the ground and scrambled up excitedly.

« No need to rush. I’ll introduce him to you now. Please come in. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi turned his gaze toward the door, and everyone followed his lead.

They saw a middle-aged man reeking of alcohol stagger in. After scanning the room, he muttered :

« What’s this ? Why are there so many brats ?

Especially that yellow-haired one. He looks totally stupid. Are you really a Ninja ? »

« Yellow-haired ? Stupid ? »

Naruto looked around the room in confusion. Soon, his eyes lit up, and he shouted a reminder toward the back :

« Tsunade-shishou, this old guy said you’re super stupid. Can you stand for that ? If it were me, I definitely couldn’t... »

Kakashi instantly felt his scalp go numb. He instinctively swung a hand-chop to knock Naruto to the ground, shouting in a panic :

« Idiot ! Don’t talk nonsense ! He’s clearly talking about you ! Shut up already... »

Unfortunately, it was too late...

At some point, Tsunade had quietly appeared behind him. She lightly patted his shoulder, her tone gentle but radiating a hair-raising chill :

« Kakashi, come outside with me for a moment... »

« ... »

After a bit of a farce, Kakashi walked back with a bruised and swollen face, giving Naruto another fierce glare.

Seeing that the scene had finally calmed down, Tazuna quickly cleared his throat and began introducing the mission :

« ...In short, I am the legendary Master Bridge Builder—Tazuna.

During the time I return to my country to complete the bridge construction, you must give your all to ensure my safety. »

...

At the gates of Konoha Village.

Team Kakashi and Team Tsunade, totaling eight people, followed grandly behind Tazuna. The scale of the group was so large it looked exactly like a spring outing.

Tazuna was now dripping with cold sweat. He swallowed hard and cautiously confirmed once more :

« I... I only paid for a C-rank mission. Is it really okay with this many people ? »

Tsunade shot him a contemptuous look and waved her hand impatiently :

« You’ve asked that hundreds of times already. Aren’t you tired of it ? We are a Special Joint Task Force. The mission pay has nothing to do with you. You’re getting a bargain, so just be happy about it~ »

Naruto was completely immersed in his own excitement, turning a deaf ear to the others' discussion.

He was like a small beast that had broken free from its restraints, running and jumping wildly on the wide road, his hearty laughter echoing in the air :

« Hahaha ! This is my first time stepping out of the Village ! What kind of great adventure is waiting for me next ? Just thinking about it makes my blood boil ! »

Seeing this, Tazuna frowned deeply. He pointed at the figure jumping up and down, his tone full of doubt and unease :

« Hey, is it really going to be okay ? You’re not really going to let this brat protect me, are you ? »

Kakashi laughed loudly. « With a Jonin like me personally escorting you, you can rest easy. »

'Not to mention, there are two real monsters hidden in this team...

With this luxurious lineup, let alone a C-rank, even an S-rank mission would probably be more than manageable.' He thought proudly to himself.

« Jonin ? You... »

Tazuna’s gaze fell on Kakashi’s bruised and swollen face. His throat moved as if he wanted to say something...

But pressured by Kakashi’s increasingly dangerous gaze, he ultimately shut his mouth resentfully.

« Alright, stop dawdling ! »

Tsunade raised her arm and gave a crisp, decisive order : « Depart now ! Destination—Land of Waves ! »

« Oh ! »
