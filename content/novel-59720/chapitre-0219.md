# Chapitre 219 — Rough

Amegakure, the high tower.

Nagato’s brows were tightly furrowed, and while forming hand seals, he shouted in a low voice : « Ninja Art : Rain Tiger at Will Technique ! »

Ever since Asakawa Shin had tunneled through the mountain barrier in the Land of Wind that blocked moisture from moving west, the continuous rainfall in the Land of Rain had become increasingly sparse, and the Chakra consumption to maintain the « Rain Tiger at Will Technique » had risen accordingly.

Suddenly, his body stiffened, and his pupils shrank slightly. « This Chakra fluctuation... is it Jiraiya-sensei ? »

Upon hearing this long-lost name, a flicker of daze passed over Konan’s habitually cold and beautiful face.

« Nagato, let me go investigate. »

« Very well. If he’s just passing through, let it be. But if he has really discovered something... »

A faint glint flashed in Nagato’s Rinnegan. « Konan, do not show any mercy. »

« ...I understand. »

Before her voice had even faded, her entire body suddenly dispersed into a flurry of paper, vanishing with the wind.

Meanwhile, in the Akatsuki’s secret base.

Asakawa Shin was boredly teasing his little follower, Kurotsuchi, when his expression suddenly froze.

He tilted his head slightly, looking into the distance, a hint of annoyance surfacing between his brows.

« No way ? He really came... He really knows how to cause trouble. »

« What’s wrong, Tobi-senpai ? » Kurotsuchi blinked her large eyes, looking bewildered.

*Slap !*

Asakawa Shin backhanded her on the back of her head ; the force was light, but it caught her completely off guard.

It made her stumble, and she grimaced in pain.

« You’re questioning me now ? »

« Ouch ! Why is Big Brother’s slap so painful ! »

Kurotsuchi skillfully adopted a head-shielding crouch, muttering under her breath, « Damned guy, just you wait. One day... »

« What are you mumbling about ? Speak up ! »

Asakawa Shin’s angry shout made Kurotsuchi’s petite frame tremble.

« No... it’s nothing ! Big Brother, I have something to do, I’m leaving now. »

Full of frustration, Kurotsuchi walked along the wet streets of Amegakure ; she kicked aside a small dog that passed by.

« Hmm ? Another blind frog in the way ? Scram ! » She gave the frog a vicious kick, sending it flying, before turning away in satisfaction.

After a good while, the frog rolled its eyes and spat out a person.

Jiraiya scrambled to his feet, wiping cold sweat from his forehead. « That bratty girl has such a nasty personality, she wouldn’t even spare an innocent frog ! »

His gaze suddenly sharpened. That girl just now seemed to be wearing the black cloak with red clouds of the Akatsuki Organization ?

Could it be that he had really found the right place this time ?

Thinking this, he quickly suppressed his presence and quietly followed her.

Through the hazy curtain of rain, Kurotsuchi splashed through the puddles, her mood absolutely terrible.

« Bad Big Brother... Stinky Big Brother... I’ll stomp you to death ! You only know how to bully me ! If you’ve got the guts, go bully Sister Konan ! »

Jiraiya, following behind her, was startled. 'Konan ? Is she also a member of the Akatsuki ? A female, her status shouldn’t be low... but that name... and that Big Brother...'

He quickly pulled out a scroll and recorded all his findings, planning to throw it in the faces of Tsunade and Asakawa Shin later to thoroughly humiliate them.

Konan, following behind Jiraiya, was also slightly surprised.

Her eyes narrowed as she made a mental note against Kurotsuchi and Madara.

Asakawa Shin, following behind Konan, was greatly alarmed.

'You little brat, actually talking behind my back. I was almost fooled by your usual obedience. Just you wait.'

And Kurotsuchi was completely unaware of all this.

She walked into a tavern and, with a grand wave of her hand, ordered eight male models.

They stood in a row, waiting for her to address them.

Suddenly, her expression froze. Pointing at Jiraiya, who stood out like a crane among chickens in the lineup, she said tremulously :

« No, Uncle, you’re at such an age and you’re still out here taking jobs ? »

Jiraiya gave a confident smile. « Boss, you might not know, but my niche is different. »

« What niche ? » Kurotsuchi asked suspiciously.

Just as Jiraiya was about to introduce himself, Kurotsuchi’s eyes lit up, and she waved him off. « Forget it, it’s not important. Do you do roleplay ? »

« Of course ! I’m an expert at that ! »

Jiraiya felt a jolt of excitement. Was this girl actually into that ?

As expected of the inherently evil Akatsuki. I’ll personally...

While his imagination was running wild, Kurotsuchi had already pulled out a Swirl Mask and held it out to him, speaking in a low voice :

« This is a prop. I might be a bit rough later. If you can’t handle it, leave now ; I can find someone else... »

Jiraiya felt another jolt. He kicked and shoved the others away, shouting, « I can handle it ! Rough is exactly what I want ! Boss, I can do any job ! »

« Hmm, not bad. »

Kurotsuchi sized up his height and build, showing a look of satisfaction. He was about eighty percent similar to Big Brother ; he’d do.

« Come here, put the mask on ! »

« Hehehe, Boss, I’m coming. »

Jiraiya put on a lecherous face and hurriedly donned the Swirl Mask.

But the moment he put it on, he received a sudden, heavy slap to the back of his head.

It made him stumble, and he grimaced in pain.

« Boss, what... what are you doing ? » Jiraiya crouched on the ground, clutching the back of his head, completely baffled.

« Hmm ? You’re questioning me now ? »

Kurotsuchi crossed her arms, full of authority. « What ? Can’t handle it already ? This is just the beginning... »

'Damned bratty girl, she’s so hard to please. Making money is really like eating sh*t. No, I have to endure for now to get information from her. I’ll tolerate her for a bit.'

Jiraiya cursed inwardly. Seeing Kurotsuchi’s increasingly unfriendly gaze, he quickly replied with a smile, « No... no, I just wasn’t ready. Let’s continue. »

He looked at Kurotsuchi’s pretty face and long, straight legs, deciding to lie low. Once the warm-up phase was over, it should... be fine... right ?

Unexpectedly, Kurotsuchi suddenly barked an insult and slapped him. « Big Brother... you’re such a piece of trash. What’s with all that flying around and acting cool ? Once your Grandma Kurotsuchi learns the Super Light-Weight Rock Technique, I’ll definitely fly higher and faster than you ! »

The more Kurotsuchi spoke, the angrier she got. She couldn’t help but throw a heavy slap with every sentence, eventually unleashing a flurry of slaps that sent Jiraiya into a retreat.

While shielding his head, Jiraiya cursed in his heart : 'Damned bratty girl ! But that « Big Brother » can fly ? Combined with this Swirl Mask, he must be the guy who attacked the Hidden Cloud Village.'

His peripheral vision swept over Kurotsuchi again. 'Could this girl be that old geezer Ōnoki’s granddaughter, Kurotsuchi ? It has to be her...'

He felt a secret joy. At this rate, it wouldn’t take long to uncover all the Akatsuki’s secrets.

Before he could enjoy the thought, Kurotsuchi pulled out a long whip as if by magic, and the whip cracked in the air like a snake.

Sharp pain surged through him as Jiraiya was whipped repeatedly, scurrying around in a pathetic state.

In the shadows, Konan finally couldn’t hold it back anymore and let out a string of crisp laughter.

She laughed so hard her body trembled, and a tear of joy even welled up in the corner of her eye.

Kurotsuchi, who had been laughing triumphantly, instantly froze. Along with the wailing Jiraiya on the ground, she looked toward the shadow where the laughter came from with suspicion and uncertainty.

Jiraiya reacted quickly, his hands rapidly forming seals.

« Dammit, I was actually played ? ! Fire Release : Great Flame Bullet ! »
