# Chapitre 93 — The Two-Tails Comes to the Rescue

The Raikage’s Office.

« What did you say ? The Valley of Clouds and Lightning was attacked ? What is the outcome ? Did Lord Killer Bee crush the enemy ? »

Mabui slammed her hand on the desk and stood up abruptly, her pretty face cold as frost, her sharp gaze fixed on the reporting Ninja.

The Ninja responded frantically, « We don’t know yet. We’ve lost contact with the Valley of Clouds and Lightning. The messenger Ninja seems to have met with an accident.

But according to the last message sent back, Lord Killer Bee hadn’t defeated the enemy even after using the Tailed Beast Ball. He then used the Tailed Beast Eight-Wrap, and since then, no more information has come through.

I’m... I’m afraid...

In short, the enemy is definitely not someone to be trifled with. Please make a decision quickly. »

Mabui’s pupils constricted violently, her expression filled with disbelief as she murmured, « Tailed Beast Eight-Wrap ? What kind of enemy would require such a move...

No... that’s not right ! Now is not the time for that. Lord Killer Bee must not be lost !

Go and invite Lady Yugito here immediately ! Wait, call all the Anbu and available Jonin currently in the Village as well !

Damn it... Lord Dodai and many elite Ninja have gone to support the Raikage. Why does it have to be now...

Could this also be the enemy’s scheme ?

Darn it, the Village also needs forces for defense. There’s no way to fully support the Valley of Clouds and Lightning... »

A moment later, a female Ninja with a graceful figure walked in slowly. She had beige-colored, waist-length hair braided into cute pigtails. Her expression was cold and her features delicate ; she was the Two-Tails Jinchuriki, Yugito.

Seeing her arrival, Mabui’s tense expression softened slightly as she nodded in greeting.

After greeting her, Yugito leaned casually to the side with her arms crossed, waiting for the others to arrive.

Soon, all the Jonin in the Village had arrived. Mabui announced in a low, serious voice :

« We have just received an urgent report that the Valley of Clouds and Lightning is under attack.

To support Lord Killer Bee, we have decided to dispatch troops immediately. This operation will be led by Lady Nii Yugito herself.

Except for those staying behind for defense, all other Jonin will go to battle !

Those staying behind must not be careless. Strengthen your guard and beware of the enemy’s tactic of luring the tiger away from the mountain.

That is all. Depart immediately ! »

After speaking, she turned to Yugito, her gaze earnest : « I’ll leave the support mission to you. Please be very careful, and I wish everyone a successful campaign ! »

Outside the Village, Asakawa Shin’s two Shadow Clones slowly stood up. The clone on the left had a flash of excitement in its eyes :

« Finally some movement. It seems the main body has succeeded.

Good, the Two-Tails Jinchuriki is also 'delivering takeout' toward the Valley of Clouds and Lightning. There are nearly a thousand elite Ninja with her. Judging by the Chakra reactions, they’re at least Chunin. It seems they’re really desperate this time.

I’ll dispel my Shadow Clone now to pass the information to the main body. I’ll leave the remaining tracking and surveillance to you.

Ja na~ »

*Poof !*

As the smoke cleared, the remaining Shadow Clone was a beat slow to react. He cursed under his breath : « Damn it, he beat me to it !

If I’d known, I should have dispelled while he was blathering. Now look at me, the miserable tracking job falls on my head. Damn it... »

On the other side, Yugito led the support unit at full speed, finally arriving at their destination after half a day.

« Is... is this the Valley of Clouds and Lightning ? How did it turn into this ? What on earth happened ? » The accompanying Ninja were all horrified by the scene before them.

The originally towering and steep canyon had seen its central mountain completely collapse, turning into a rocky island in a vast lake.

The mountain faces on both sides were covered in blast marks, with ruins and debris everywhere.

« Lady Yugito, two massive Chakra reactions detected ahead ! »

The Sensory Ninja’s voice trembled, cold sweat pouring down his forehead like water. He reported in terror : « One of them is Lord Killer Bee, but the other...

The other Chakra is actually several times that of Lord Killer Bee. It’s simply like a monster ! »

At these words, all the surrounding Ninja paled.

Yugito’s expression hardened. Her gaze was like lightning as she looked at the Sensory Ninja and asked in a low voice : « You mean the enemy’s Chakra is several times stronger than the Eight-Tails ? Could it be the Nine-Tailed attacking ? But... »

She frowned slightly, but her eyes soon became firm : « Regardless, it’s a blessing that Bee is still alive.

Everyone, stay alert and follow me ! Sensory Ninja, watch out for enemy traps and movements. Move out ! »

Not long after, the Sensory Ninja shouted a warning : « The enemy and Lord Killer Bee are right ahead. »

In fact, he didn’t even need to remind them ; everyone could already see the enemy’s movements.

In the middle of the vast water surface, a mountain had been leveled to form a wide platform. Two figures stood still upon it, extremely conspicuous.

Everyone looked over and saw Killer Bee covered in blood, lying powerless to the side, while the other figure appeared exceptionally composed, sitting cross-legged lazily, clearly having waited for a long time.

Seeing this, Yugito’s eyebrows shot up as she demanded sharply : « Who are you ? What is your purpose ? Why did you attack Killer Bee ? »

Asakawa Shin, being an honest person, spoke the truth directly : « Oh, I came specifically for you. Tsk, as expected, you smell wonderful. I could smell it from this far away. »

At these words, Yugito instantly flew into a rage, her pretty face full of fury as the Chakra inside her body surged :

« Smell wonderful, do I ? Let’s see if I can roast you until you smell wonderful too !

Mouse Hairball ! »

In an instant, ten balls of ghostly blue fire were released, carrying a searing heat wave. Controlled by her fingers, they shot accurately toward Asakawa Shin.

Facing this attack, Asakawa Shin neither dodged nor defended ; he simply composedly pulled Killer Bee in front of him as a shield.

Yugito’s face changed drastically. She hurriedly manipulated the Mouse Hairballs to turn. The fireballs narrowly missed Killer Bee, and Asakawa Shin could even smell the scent of singed hair.

Yugito was furious and was about to curse : « How despicable... »

Before she could finish, Asakawa Shin drowned her out with a louder, more indignant voice : « How despicable ! To actually launch a sneak attack on me. If I weren’t so clever, I’m afraid I would have fallen victim to your foul play !

I answered everything you asked so cooperatively, yet you treat me like this. Kumogakure barbarians are truly unreasonable !

Fine then, let’s solve this in a way you can understand ! »

Asakawa Shin casually tossed Killer Bee at his feet and slammed his hands together. His aura changed abruptly as he entered Sage Mode once again.

He unceremoniously stepped on Killer Bee’s chest with one foot while rapidly forming hand seals, saying coldly : « There are too many of you, it’s making me feel pressured. I’d better clear some out first. »

Then, he turned his gaze to Yugito, his tone becoming somewhat gentler as he kindly reminded her : « Hey, little lady, you’d better be careful next. Don’t die ; I still have urgent business with you later. »

« Everyone, stay alert ! The enemy’s Chakra is being consumed rapidly. The next attack will likely be extraordinary ! »

Before the Sensory Ninja could finish, the water surface beneath them began to churn and boil.

Not just here, but the water currents of the entire Valley of Clouds and Lightning seemed to be pulled by an invisible giant hand, frantically converging and reshaping.

In an instant, a towering wave rose from the ground, condensing into a massive water dragon that blotted out the sun.

Dozens of Kumogakure Ninja didn’t even have time to scream before they were swept up by the violent currents, involuntarily becoming part of the water dragon.

« Take this ! Ninja of Kumogakure. »

'Sage Art : Water Release : Water Dragon Bullet Technique !'
