# Chapitre 114 — Graduation Exam

The Asakawa Residence.

« Naruto, stop spinning around, you’re making me dizzy. »

Just as Asakawa Shin grabbed Naruto, who was circling and inspecting him, he felt a weight on his head along with a warm sensation.

It turned out that Asakawa Momo was perched on his head, unable to resist her curiosity and taking a bite of the flower.

This gave him a start, and he quickly pulled Little Momo down.

Sure enough, the little girl was clutching her mouth with a pained expression, her eyes quickly filling with tears.

Seeing this, Asakawa Shin’s lips couldn’t help but twitch.

She must have chipped a tooth...

One must realize that this flower was now the hardest part of his body, bar none.

He even suspected that even against Garp’s Iron Fist, this flower would remain unscathed.

Thus, the consequences of Little Momo’s bite were quite predictable.

Seeing Little Momo rush into the kitchen looking aggrieved, Asakawa Shin’s heart tightened. Without a word, he grabbed Naruto and bolted, urgently prompting :

« Let’s go, Naruto ! We’re going to be late for school ! »

« Huh ? But we haven’t even had breakfast yet ? »

« Forget breakfast, just go ! » Before he even finished speaking, he had already pulled Naruto to the courtyard gate.

Sure enough, before long, Hanako’s angry roar echoed from behind.

Asakawa Shin let out a silent sigh of relief, thinking to himself that it was a good thing he ran fast ; otherwise, he would have been caught red-handed by Hanako and subjected to a severe scolding.

Naruto hadn’t noticed the danger at all and was still making a fuss.

But soon, his attention was drawn back to the flower on top of Asakawa Shin’s head, and he asked curiously :

« Shin, why has this turned into a flower again ? It smells quite fragrant too ; no wonder Little Momo couldn’t help but take a bite just now. »

« First it sprouted, then it bloomed—will it bear fruit later ? Hahaha, this is way too weird. »

Asakawa Shin just smiled enigmatically, « Who knows ? »

Naruto rolled his eyes, ignoring him, but then seemed to remember something and pleaded with a flattering expression :

« Then can you get one for me too ? Seriously, you look pretty good with that flower ; I want one too. »

« That’s easy. Can’t you just buy a hair clip ? This is a special outfit ; I can’t just conjure one up for you. »

« How can that be the same ! »

...

Amidst their banter, they arrived at school before they knew it.

By this time, many people had already gathered in the classroom, making it exceptionally lively.

Asakawa Shin smiled and waved, « Yo, everyone’s early today. »

« Isn’t it because the graduation exam is today ? Everyone is very excited. »

Halfway through her sentence, Ino’s gaze was drawn to the flower on his head, and she asked in a daze, « What... what is this ? »

Asakawa Shin raised an eyebrow slightly, showing off with a smug look, « How is it ? Looks good, right ? »

Ino’s cheeks turned slightly red as she whispered shyly, « Mm... it’s... it’s very pretty. It looks like a species I’ve never seen before, very noble and elegant. It suits you well. »

'Elegant ?'

Asakawa Shin chuckled inwardly ; upon closer thought, it did seem that way.

After all, this was the God Tree. Not only was it 'tall,' but when it bloomed, the entire Ninja World would fall silent. It truly embodied the saying, 'When my flower blooms, all others shall perish.' The word 'elegant' was indeed fitting.

As expected of the daughter of a florist ; her eye was sharp, seeing through to the essence at a glance.

Just then, Karin rushed into the classroom panting. As soon as she saw Asakawa Shin, she ran over and complained aggrievedly :

« Lord Shin, why did you leave so early today ? When I went to pick you up, Lady Hanako said you’d already left. I ran all the way but couldn’t catch up... »

Asakawa Shin scratched his head awkwardly and smiled apologetically, « Sorry, Karin. Things were a bit unusual this morning. I’ll definitely wait for you next time. »

« What ! Lord Shin, we graduate today ! There won’t be a next time ! »

« Ahaha, sorry, sorry, I forgot. »

Shikamaru leaned lazily in the back row. Witnessing this scene, he couldn’t help but let out a cold smirk, mocking in his heart :

'Heh heh heh, poor Shin. This is what you get for messing with girls all the time ; now you’re in trouble, aren’t you ?'

'As expected, I’m the smart one. The so-called « wise men don’t fall in love »—that’s exactly why.'

Time slipped away amidst everyone’s playfulness.

Soon, as the school bell rang, Iruka’s figure appeared at the classroom door.

He looked around at the group of students he had spent day and night with, his gaze complex. After a long moment, he cleared his throat and said loudly :

« As you all surely know, the graduation exam will take place today. »

« Students whose names are called, please come to the next classroom. The topic for this exam is the 'Clone Jutsu.' Please be prepared. »

Upon hearing this, Naruto immediately cheered excitedly, « All right ! What luck ! This is my best Ninjutsu ! »

Facing Naruto, who was shouting and disrupting discipline in class, Iruka acted out of character this time. Instead of scolding him, his eyes were full of gratification.

He thought to himself proudly : 'As expected of you, Iruka.'

'Even a mischievous child like Naruto has been trained so excellently.'

'Is there anything in this world you can’t do ?'

'If things keep going at this rate, the position of Ninja Academy Principal might just be mine to take, hahahaha~'

Soon, it was Naruto’s turn for the exam. Naruto walked into the next classroom with a cocky look on his face, curled his lip, and began to form hand signs : « Ninja Art : Multiple Shadow Clone Jutsu ! »

In an instant, white smoke filled the exam room, and countless Shadow Clones of Naruto appeared out of thin air.

Because the number was so staggering, the narrow space was instantly packed tight. Even Iruka and Mizuki were submerged in the sea of people, unable to move.

Iruka felt a tightness in his chest as if the air in his lungs was being squeezed out. He hurriedly shouted at the top of his lungs :

« Enough ! You pass, you pass ! Naruto, release the clones quickly ! »

When Naruto finally released the technique, Iruka scrambled up from the ground in a mess. Wiping the cold sweat from his forehead, he solemnly announced to the boy who was scratching his head and grinning :

« Congratulations, Naruto ! Your Clone Jutsu is actually the more advanced Physical Clone ; your performance was outstanding ! »

« In my capacity as the proctor, I officially announce—you pass ! »

« From this day forward, you are an official Genin of Konoha Village. »

Naruto’s eyes also grew moist. He took the headband with great excitement and then bowed deeply to Iruka :

« Iruka-sensei, thank you for your careful guidance all these years... »

While the two were immersed in the touching bond between teacher and student, Asakawa Shin, not far away, was busy with something else...
