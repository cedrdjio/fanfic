# Chapitre 26 — Social Butterfly

At the Nara Clan’s banquet.

« Uncle Shikaku, congratulations on your promotion to Assistant to the Hokage, though you won’t be holding that position for long. »

Asakawa Shin winked at Shikaku Nara, and before Shikaku could show a look of surprise, he changed the subject and continued :

« After all, I already promised Shikamaru that if I ever become Hokage, I’ll have him serve as my Assistant to the Hokage. »

« You should take him under your wing and train him well, hahaha. »

Upon hearing this, a sharp glint flashed in Shikaku’s eyes, and he almost laughed out loud. He patted Shikamaru on the head, ignored Shikamaru’s deadpan stare, and said to Asakawa Shin :

« This kid is still far from replacing me. Shin, if you have anything you need done, feel free to give it to him. Jade must be carved to become a gem ; this kid needs some serious training. »

Others from the Nara Clan also came up to offer toasts. The Nara Clan had really made something of themselves ; not only were they currently basking in glory, but they had even secured the future position of Assistant to the Hokage.

As for whether Asakawa Shin could become the Hokage in the future ? Wasn’t the current situation clear enough ? Hiruzen Sarutobi was building momentum for Asakawa Shin, grooming him for the position of the Fifth Generation Hokage.

Asakawa Shin did not refuse anyone, using tea to replace wine as he toasted each one of them before leaving.

At the Hyuga Clan’s banquet.

« Uncle, congratulations ! You’ve been promoted to Minister of Finance for Konoha. You’re holding the entire purse strings of Konoha now ; your financial future is limitless. » Asakawa Shin cupped his hands in salute to Hiashi Hyuga.

Hiashi Hyuga was also beaming, laughing as he said to Asakawa Shin, « Hahaha, many thanks to you, my nephew. I heard from the Hokage-sama that it was you who recommended me ? »

Asakawa Shin waved his hand dismissively. « Hey ! I was just afraid you’d go broke from feeding me ! What would I do if my meals were cut off ? I had to pay attention to that. »

Hiashi was also amused and shook his head. What was the little bit of food Asakawa Shin ate, anyway ?

Now that he held the power over Konoha’s finances, even just the scraps that slipped through his fingers, within legal and compliant bounds, would be more than enough for the Hyuga Clan to enjoy indefinitely.

However, he seemed to suddenly remember something, and with a troubled expression, he pointed towards the distance for Asakawa Shin to see :

« Nephew, I have something to ask you. Do you think Hinata is acting a bit strange ? I can’t quite put my finger on it, but she’s just… excessively excellent. »

Asakawa Shin looked in the direction he was pointing and saw Hinata flitting through the crowd like a butterfly, chatting animatedly with everyone. Especially the elderly men, who were being charmed to the point that their eyes were squinted into slits, completely hidden from view.

She was relaxed, poised, and gave off a perfectly balanced sense of approachability ; it was truly excessively elegant.

Whether from the main branch or the branch family, everyone in the crowd was being reeled in by her ; this six-year-old little dumpling was simply too good at this.

« Whose young lady is this amazing and cute ? Oh, she’s from our Hyuga Clan ! Our Hyuga Clan really has a promising successor ! »

Asakawa Shin’s mouth twitched. Is this that stinky woman Hinata ? This week he had witnessed how Hinata skillfully switched between two personalities, keeping Naruto wrapped around her finger.

Sometimes she was intimate, sometimes distant, sometimes a dainty little woman, and other times full of dominance. Naruto was attracted to her without realizing it, until he was completely under her control.

Now, Hinata was tied for Naruto’s best friend, and even when she teased him, he seemed to enjoy it.

'Tsk, poor Naruto.'

« Uncle, it’s nothing. Hinata has made progress in her taijutsu training and is gradually becoming more confident. After all, she is a peerless genius, so it’s normal for her to be a bit special. » Asakawa Shin consoled him, going against his conscience.

Hiashi felt a bit more at ease after hearing Asakawa Shin say this. After all, Hinata’s changes this week had been so drastic that he was feeling a bit rattled.

« If you have nothing else, Uncle, I’ll be taking my leave. »

Hiashi nodded and continued to observe Hinata.

After bidding farewell to the Hyuga, Asakawa Shin visited the Akimichi, Yamanaka, Inuzuka, and Aburame clans in succession, receiving a warm welcome from all—er, scratch the Aburame.

Asakawa Shin ate and drank to his heart’s content at the Akimichi banquet, defeating all the big eaters of the Akimichi clan and earning the respect of the entire clan.

When he left, a group of fat guys from the Akimichi clan bid Asakawa Shin a reluctant farewell, as if they were sending off a deity.

Leaving the Akimichi, he arrived at the Yamanaka banquet. The members of the Yamanaka clan looked at him as if he were a son-in-law, arranging for him to sit with Ino. Ino’s face flushed red with shyness.

Asakawa Shin, however, acted as if nothing was the matter, frequently serving Ino food and water, showing concern, which made the Yamanaka clan members unknowingly start wearing doting smiles, feeling even more satisfied as they looked at Asakawa Shin.

Leaving the Yamanaka, he came to the Inuzuka clan. Asakawa Shin directly demonstrated his ability to listen to the voice of all things, communicating with the dogs without any barriers.

This immediately made him the center of attention for the Inuzuka clan. It was the first time they had seen someone outside the Inuzuka clan who could communicate with dogs. But that didn’t matter ; as long as you liked dogs, you were a good friend of the Inuzuka clan.

Asakawa Shin was immediately given a warm welcome by the Inuzuka clan. It was just a bit too enthusiastic, and Asakawa Shin had to exert a lot of effort to escape the Inuzuka banquet.

Asakawa Shin searched the banquet hall several times before finding the location of the Aburame clan’s banquet. Unlike the other lively banquets, it was slightly quiet here.

The people of the Aburame clan were all very silent, eating on their own ; even when they communicated, it was in whispers, and their presence was very low.

It wasn’t until Asakawa Shin proposed collaborating with the Aburame clan to raise silkworms and produce a precious fabric called silk that they became interested and began to discuss it with him.

If silk was really as good as Asakawa Shin said, the prospects for this project would be very impressive. Both sides reached a rough consensus on the cooperation and agreed to discuss it in detail another day.

The Aburame clan needed to research how to raise silkworms, how to increase production, and the quality of the silk also needed improvement.

Asakawa Shin also needed to talk to Hiruzen Sarutobi about this. If the cooperation could be achieved, the profits involved would be terrifying.

After all, the Aburame clan was good at raising insects, and Asakawa Shin was good at planting trees, so the costs could be compressed very low.

As for the spinning wheel, Asakawa Shin planned to improve the spinning wheels currently used in the Ninja World into large water-powered spinning wheels. The Land of Fire had abundant water resources, and if they could be utilized, it would greatly increase production efficiency.

It was a pity that Senior Brother Orochimaru was not there ; he was a top-tier scientific research talent. Otherwise, if he were to design a pedal-powered spinning wheel, or even a mechanical one, it might not be impossible to create.

For now, he would talk to Hiruzen Sarutobi and see if they could establish a research department to study things needed for society and people’s livelihood.

He had many ideas in his head. If successful, it could open up a new source of revenue for Konoha. They couldn’t always rely on Ninja mission commissions and funding from the Daimyo ; Konoha needed its own economic sources.

'Sigh ! Senior Brother Orochimaru, where are you ? Your junior brother misses you. I wonder if you’ve established Otogakure yet.'

'Should I find some time to organize an Otogakure Collapse Plan ?'

'After all, Senior Brother, you really are so fragrant.'
