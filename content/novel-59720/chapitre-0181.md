# Chapitre 181 — What Level

Nagato’s heart jolted as he suddenly realized something was wrong.

Konan’s actions today were indeed unusual ; she had actually gone so far as to shield him in front of her.

Nagato turned his head, a trace of doubt in his eyes.

« What’s wrong ? Konan. »

« Ah ? I... I’m fine... »

Konan gave a slightly stiff, light cough, then abruptly turned her head to glare fiercely at Madara. She hadn’t expected this bastard’s observation skills to be so sharp.

She took a deep breath and quickly changed the subject : « Madara, what exactly is your business here this time ? »

Madara was baffled by the glare, and a sense of displeasure immediately arose in his heart. His tone unconsciously became more arrogant.

« I’m preparing to step onto the stage. You two make the arrangements ; I want to formally meet with the members. »

Nagato’s brow furrowed slightly, but now was not the time to have a falling out with Madara, so he could only endure for the time being.

It wouldn’t be too late to settle accounts once the Tailed Beast weapon was completed.

« Then pack your things and meet at the Akatsuki Organization base in three days. »

« Understood. My new name is Tobi, so don’t call me the wrong name when the time comes. »

Before his voice had even faded, his and Zetsu’s figures were enveloped by Psychokinesis, turning into a streak of light that vanished before their eyes.

Witnessing this strange flying Ninjutsu, Nagato’s pupils constricted slightly.

Konan spoke hesitantly : « That guy Madara seems to have become even stronger than before... »

Nagato’s expression was grave as he nodded in agreement.

« Indeed. Just looking at his figure makes one’s skin crawl, yet there is nothing at all in my Chakra perception. This sense of contradiction... is truly a bit eerie. »

However, in the next moment, he lightly stroked his right eye, a confident arc rising at the corner of his mouth.

« But it doesn’t matter. Before these Eyes of God, whether it’s Uchiha Madara or Asakawa Shin, they are nothing more than jumping clowns, not worth fearing. »

Hearing this, Konan breathed a slight sigh of relief : « I hope so. »

Three days later, at the Akatsuki Organization base.

Sasori complained with a face full of displeasure : « Damn it, what on earth is the leader up to ? Calling us all back ! I’m busy researching new puppets ! »

Kakuzu rarely chimed in with an agreement : « Indeed, this is delaying me from making money. It’s pure nonsense. »

Kisame, however, seemed quite indifferent : « Speaking of which, it really has been a long time since we’ve all gathered together. »

Just then, the figures of Pain and Konan finally appeared.

« Silence ! »

Just two words were enough to make this group of unruly members instantly fall silent, their gazes all shifting toward them.

Pain looked around at everyone and spoke slowly : « I have summoned everyone here today to welcome a new member and let you get to know each other. »

Along with his words, a figure wearing a black robe with red clouds and a swirl mask floated gracefully in through the entrance.

Everyone cast scrutinizing glances, but Asakawa Shin wasn’t flustered at all. He hovered in mid-air with great interest, sizing up each person.

'Is this the Akatsuki Organization, known as the strongest group in the Ninja World ?'

His gaze swept across, and he muttered secretly in his heart : 'Deidara is still just a kid, and Hidan is nowhere to be seen...'

However, when his gaze landed on one of the figures, he was suddenly startled, nearly losing his balance and falling from the sky.

The person before him was covered in stitch marks, with pale skin and eyes glowing with an unsettling green light—it was clearly Kakuzu. But...

Asakawa Shin tremblingly pointed a finger at him, blurting out in disbelief : « Handsome guy, who the hell are you ? »

Kakuzu’s brow furrowed, extremely dissatisfied with this impolite brat in front of him.

But before he could explode, a nameless Ninja nearby stepped forward first and introduced him loudly with a hint of awe :

« You don’t even know this lord ? He is the famous bounty hunter in the Ninja World, Lord Kakuzu ! He even fought head-on with the First Hokage, Senju Hashirama, back in the day ! »

'Is it really Kakuzu ?'

Asakawa Shin held his head in his hands, still finding it hard to believe.

'But why did this guy turn into a little Shota ? Has he lived so long that his mind has become twisted and he’s picked up some special hobby ?'

He snorted coldly, his heart filled with disdain : 'Clearly an old fossil, yet he insists on pretending to be young.'

Just then, Pain lightly clapped his hands, drawing everyone’s attention back.

« This is the newest member of the Akatsuki Organization, Tobi. His code name is Vermilion. »

Seeing Asakawa Shin’s figure consistently hovering in mid-air, the other members each had their own thoughts.

Just this mysterious and unpredictable method was enough to prove that this newcomer was definitely no ordinary character.

From the moment Asakawa Shin entered the room, Deidara had been frowning. Now, he finally couldn’t hold back and questioned :

« You can actually fly ? You... could you be a Ninja from Iwagakure as well ? Why have I never seen you before ? »

Asakawa Shin waved his hand dismissively and directly denied it : « I’m not a Ninja from Iwagakure. As for the ability to fly, that’s my own skill. »

Deidara was slightly stunned, then burst out laughing : « Who in the Ninja World doesn’t know that’s the Earth Release : Ultra-Light-Weight Rock Technique ? In Iwagakure, I’ve only ever seen the old man use that ability. Aren’t you a spy sent by Ōnoki ? »

The more he thought about it, the more suspicious it seemed. He pointed at Asakawa Shin and shouted excitedly, « Leader, he must be a spy ! Quickly use your invincible Rinnegan to kill him ! »

Being reported to his face, Asakawa Shin was momentarily dazed. Once he realized what was happening, he immediately became fuming with rage.

His eyes narrowed slightly, and his right hand suddenly reached out toward Deidara.

In the next moment, Deidara felt his body become weightless, his entire being imprisoned by an invisible force, unable to even blink.

« You bastard, how dare you slander me ? I’m definitely going to teach you a lesson today. »

Asakawa Shin snorted coldly, then pulled out a shiny razor from nowhere. With a wicked smile, he approached Deidara and began shaving his head clean with swift strokes, muttering all the while :

« Who gave you permission to have this hairstyle ? What nerve ! If I see you with it again, I’ll shave you every time I see you ! »

Deidara couldn’t move and could only watch as his proud, beautiful hair fell to the ground. His heart was filled with anger and hatred, and tears of humiliation welled up in his eyes.

Pain watched from the side, shaking his head and thinking to himself : 'Madara’s acting is a bit too over-the-top. If I didn’t know his background, I might really think he’s a lunatic.'

It took a long time before Asakawa Shin finally stopped, still seemingly unsatisfied.

By now, Deidara’s hair had been shaved clean, with some spots even showing the scalp. It was uneven, looking as if it had been gnawed on by a dog.

Asakawa Shin smiled with satisfaction. 'What level are you, thinking you’re worthy of having the same hairstyle as Ino ?'

Deidara finally regained his freedom. His hands trembled as he touched the top of his head, but all he felt was a barren waste.

His eyes were bloodshot, almost spitting fire, as he roared hysterically, « You... you bastard ! What have you done to my flowing long hair ? ! I’m definitely going to kill you today ! »

Asakawa Shin didn’t even blink. He calmly patted his sleeves, then turned to Pain with a look of extreme grief and indignation.

« Leader, you saw it too. As soon as this guy got his freedom back, he threatened to kill me. He has absolutely no regard for the organization’s discipline.

For the sake of Akatsuki’s unity, I propose that he be immediately expelled from the organization, and then you use your invincible Rinnegan to blast him into dust as a warning to others ! »
