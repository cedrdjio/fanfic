# Chapitre 190 — Moving Mountains and Filling Seas, Man Can Conquer Nature

Hokage Office.

Tsunade looks at Asakawa Shin suspiciously. « Junior brother, why do you look so pale ? Is the Land of Snow some kind of dragon’s den or tiger’s cave ? »

« How... how is that possible ? I’ve just been overworked lately and am a bit tired, » Asakawa Shin forced an explanation.

Tsunade was skeptical, but then she thought, what experts could the mere Land of Snow have ? She was indeed overthinking it.

« By the way, during your mission, the negotiations with Sunagakure concluded. A large area east of the Land of Wind has been assigned to Konoha.

That bastard Daimyo fell for it as expected. He refused to take it and let us manage it ourselves. »

Nara Shikaku, acting as the Hokage’s « external brain », chimed in at the right time : « He looked down on that uninhabited area because it’s all desert, so he abandoned it.

He doesn’t know that if Lord Shin’s plan succeeds, that desert can instantly turn into a treasure. »

Asakawa Shin blinked and smiled : « Uncle Shikaku is still the best, bribing the nobles to speak in front of the Daimyo. That’s how we got such a bargain. »

Saying that, he spread out a map, his fingertip pointing precisely at the mountain range on the border between the Land of Wind and the Land of Rivers.

« As long as this mountain range is leveled, the moisture from the Land of Rain and the Land of Rivers can drive straight in. Plus, with the help of my Wood Release... »

A glint flashed in Asakawa Shin’s eyes. « That desert can be transformed into an oasis in an instant. »

Tsunade’s eyes lit up as she followed up : « By then, this fertile land will be Konoha’s private property. But what do we need such a large territory for ? »

Asakawa Shin smiled slightly, his tone certain : « Senior sister Tsunade, a Ninja’s power has its limits. So I’ve decided... I’m not going to be a Ninja anymore. »

Seeing the worry in their eyes, Asakawa Shin waved his hand. « There’s no rush for this. It won’t be too late to start after I return from Roran. »

« Roran ? »

Tsunade was slightly stunned. « Isn’t that an ancient kingdom long buried by sand ? »

« Senior sister, I have my own plans. »

Asakawa Shin changed the subject. « By the way, give me the key to Konoha’s treasury first. I want to pick a treasure. »

Although slightly surprised, Tsunade readily handed over the key and even excitedly suggested going together.

She was really curious about what was hidden in Konoha’s treasury that could catch her junior brother’s eye.

The three of them soon arrived deep within the treasury.

Following a mysterious sensation, Asakawa Shin walked straight toward an inconspicuous display stand.

« This is the treasure you want ? »

Tsunade’s eyes widened. She picked up a turtle from the stand with a strange expression, looking it over.

« Isn’t this just a turtle ? »

She examined it for a long time but didn’t find anything special about it.

But on second thought, since her junior brother specifically asked for it, this item was definitely no ordinary object.

Asakawa Shin reached out and took the turtle, his fingertips lightly stroking the shell. A faint, imperceptible light flashed in his eyes.

This was the famous treasure—Karasuki.

Legend has it that it possesses the power to travel through time and space, but activating it requires a massive amount of the Ōtsutsuki Clan’s Chakra. With his current strength, he was still unable to use it.

Whether it was this trip to Roran or taking the treasure 'Karasuki', the ultimate goal was to cross time and space, travel to other Ninja Worlds, and try to obtain the Natural Energy there.

However, whether it would actually work remained to be seen.

After bidding farewell to Tsunade, Asakawa Shin rushed to the border of the Land of Wind without stopping.

A massive mountain range lay ahead, like a natural barrier, abruptly cutting off the moist airflow from the ocean.

The moisture stalled here, turning into continuous rain, making the neighboring Land of Rain rainy all year round, with the sun rarely seen.

While the Land of Rivers was slightly better, its territory was also filled with rivers and swamps.

In stark contrast, the Land of Wind on the other side of the mountain range, with moisture completely blocked, had not seen a drop of rain for years, eventually evolving into a vast, barren desert.

On this trip, Asakawa Shin had only one goal.

That was to move mountains and fill seas with human effort, breaking through this barrier and reshaping the continent’s moisture patterns.

For others, this might be a fantasy, but for Asakawa Shin, who possessed powerful Psychokinesis, it was not impossible.

He slowly closed his eyes, and the violent spiritual energy within him surged out like a tide, instantly condensing into a giant invisible hand that blotted out the sun, tightly gripping the towering mountain range before him.

« Rise ! »

A low shout rang out like a clap of thunder.

The ground beneath his feet suddenly trembled, countless rocks rolled down the mountain streams, and dust rose into the sky.

The massive mountain body that had stood for ten million years actually began to lift before everyone’s horrified eyes, making a tooth-aching « crackling » sound.

« It’s too big, a bit taxing... »

Asakawa Shin’s expression was grave, the veins on his forehead bulged, and bean-sized beads of sweat rolled down.

He whispered, then pulled the kusanagi sword from his waist. With a flick of his wrist, two cold lights cut through the sky like lightning.

Amidst the crisscrossing sword qi, the towering peak was precisely divided into four sections.

He gently set down three of the pieces and only lifted the remaining corner, feeling the pressure suddenly decrease and becoming much more relaxed.

In this way, it became a matter of slow, steady effort.

Over time, peak after peak was moved aside like a master butcher carving a bull. The massive mountain range stretching over a thousand kilometers was finally torn open with a huge gap.

And without the barrier, the moist airflow from the ocean found an outlet like a bursting flood, surging madly into the Land of Wind.

Above the originally dead desert, thick clouds gathered and surged at a speed visible to the naked eye.

A sweet rain, delayed for countless years, finally descended upon this long-parched land.

« It’s really raining ? Simply unbelievable ! »

« As expected of Lord Shin, Konoha’s Son of God ! »

« Even if someone said he was a god descended to earth, I wouldn’t doubt it for a moment ! How could a mortal achieve such great power ? »

Floating above the clouds, Asakawa Shin heard all these exclamations.

With his hands behind his back and a solemn expression, he looked down at the rain-soaked land with eyes like torches.

A moment later, he finally spoke slowly. His voice wasn’t loud, but it spread clearly in all directions :

« I don’t believe in gods. I only know that man can conquer nature ! The fate of this land will ultimately be rewritten by our own hands. »

« Ah, right, right, right ! »

« Long live Lord Shin ! »

Accompanying this deafening declaration, the countless people below instantly boiled over, and cheers swept out like waves.

Asakawa Shin smiled slightly, using the moisture accumulated over the years to begin his tree-planting plan.

He clapped his hands together and shouted.

« Wood Style : Deep Forest Emergence ! »

In an instant, countless thick tree roots burst from the ground, branches and leaves grew wildly, and in just a few breaths, the originally dead, boundless desert was transformed into a vast, vibrant sea of trees.

This shocking scene spread rapidly throughout the Ninja World as if it had grown wings.

Iwagakure.

« Cough, cough, cough ! » The Sandaime Tsuchikage Ōnoki, who was drinking tea, coughed violently upon hearing the news, almost straining his old back.

After confirming the intelligence was correct multiple times, the experienced Ninja tremblingly issued an order.

If anyone encounters Asakawa Shin, they can directly abandon their mission and flee without facing any punishment.

Hidden Mist Village.

The Fifth Mizukage Mei Terumī's beautiful face was filled with disbelief.

After a long while, she recovered from her shock, a strange light flashing in her eyes. « I heard this man is not only exceptionally powerful but also quite handsome ? Hmm... I’ve reached the age where I should consider my lifelong affairs. »

Sunagakure.

The Fifth Kazekage Gaara, who had recently taken office, had veins bulging on his forehead as he gritted his teeth and said, « Asakawa Shin again ! This guy is everywhere ! »

Temari, standing aside, looked infatuated, her eyes sparkling with little stars. « As expected of the man I’ve chosen ! Gaara, since the relationship between our villages is so tense, why not send me for a political marriage ? It’s a win-win ! »

Hidden Cloud Village.

The Fifth Raikage Nii Yugito had a strange expression, staring at the diamond ring on her finger with slightly flushed cheeks.

« That guy has actually become this strong ? But... didn’t he say he wanted to marry into our Hidden Cloud Village back then ? Should I send someone to check on the situation ? »
