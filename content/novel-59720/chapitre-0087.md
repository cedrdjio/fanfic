# Chapitre 87 — Wool Comes From the Sheep

« What is going on ? It’s already noon, why aren’t you up yet, Senior Sister Tsunade ! Seriously, why did you have to drink so much last night ? And that meal cost me ten million ryo ! That’s just too much. Get up, Senior Sister~ »

The more Asakawa Shin thought about it, the angrier he got, so he simply climbed onto Tsunade and shook her shoulders vigorously.

Unexpectedly, Tsunade didn’t react at all, muttering groggily in her sleep : « Such good wine... bring another jar~ »

Shizune shook her head helplessly from the side and advised : « Lord Shin, there’s no need to call her anymore. In my experience, Lady Tsunade will wake up on her own by evening. »

Asakawa Shin rolled his eyes, turned to Shizune, and said : « Then let’s just leave her be. We’ll go to the casino ourselves and play a few rounds without her~ »

Before the words left his mouth, Tsunade suddenly sat up, flipping Asakawa Shin, who was sitting on her, right off. She exclaimed excitedly : « Casino ? Are we going to play ? Let’s go, let’s go ! »

Fortunately, Asakawa Shin was prepared. He braced himself with one hand, performed a backflip, and landed steadily. He looked at Tsunade and teased with a smile : « Haha, this trick really works. Senior Sister, stop sleeping. It’s still early, let’s continue our journey. »

« What ! You brat, how dare you play tricks on me ? Didn’t we agree to go to the casino ? A real man keeps his word ! »

Seeing that Asakawa Shin was completely unmoved, Tsunade simply lay back on the bed and started acting like a rascal : « I don’t care. If we aren’t going today, I’m just going to stay here and not get up ! »

Asakawa Shin mocked her mercilessly : « What’s the point of going ? Senior Sister, you’re penniless right now. Are you just going to watch us play ? »

Hearing this, Tsunade gritted her teeth in anger and protested indignantly : « It’s all your fault, you brat ! You cheated and swindled all my money away. I don’t care, give my money back ! »

Asakawa Shin pointed at himself with an incredulous look : « Me ? I cheated ? I even let you use both hands and eyes... »

To her surprise, Tsunade nodded confidently : « Exactly ! Isn’t your ability to predict the future just cheating ? Gambling is supposed to be about luck. How can you be like this ? It’s completely unfair competition. »

« Tch, who cares. » Asakawa Shin scoffed and turned to walk out the door.

But before he got far, he stopped abruptly, looked down, and sighed helplessly : « Senior Sister, don’t do this. Let go of my leg. »

Tsunade hugged his thigh tightly, shaking it while laughing triumphantly : « I won’t ! Not unless you give me back my money. »

Shizune, standing to the side, had already covered her forehead, unable to bear watching.

Facing this shameless behavior, Asakawa Shin scratched his head helplessly. Finally, he took two sealing scrolls from his bag and handed them to Tsunade and Shizune respectively : « There are thirty million ryo in here. The usual rules—this is the Big Fat Sheep’s appearance fee. Shizune, this is your share. We’ll split the winnings fifty-fifty. »

Tsunade’s eyes lit up as she took the scroll, a cunning smile curling at the corner of her lips : 'So that’s how it is, this brat actually falls for this, hehehe~'

...

Sure enough, as soon as Tsunade appeared in the casino, it caused a huge sensation. The people in the casino swarmed to greet her with fiery enthusiasm. They were even more ecstatic when they saw Tsunade and Shizune casually take out tens of millions of ryo.

However, in just one afternoon, the once bustling and noisy casino declared bankruptcy. The owner even had to sell his property to tearfully pay out the massive amount of chips in Shizune’s hands.

Outside the casino, Asakawa Shin and Shizune began splitting the loot on the spot. After returning Asakawa Shin’s principal of sixty million, the two had made a net profit of one hundred million ryo. Watching them each receive fifty million, Tsunade immediately drooled with envy.

However, after getting the money, Shizune didn’t look very happy. Instead, a trace of reluctance flashed across her face, a subtle change in expression that Asakawa Shin keenly captured. He curiously leaned forward and asked : « What’s wrong, Sister Shizune ? You seem to have something on your mind ? »

Shizune nodded gently and sighed with a dim expression : « The casino owner deserved his fate, but seeing his wife and children crying so miserably just now... I can’t help but feel a bit of sympathy. »

Asakawa Shin waved his hand dismissively : « Sister Shizune, that way of thinking is wrong. Do you know how many people those casinos have ruined ? How many families have been destroyed and torn apart because of gambling ? If Senior Sister Tsunade is the Big Fat Sheep waiting to be slaughtered, then these gamblers are the lost lambs. By bankrupting the casino, you aren’t doing a bad thing ; you are performing a virtuous deed. If you and Senior Sister Tsunade traveled around the Ninja World and shut down every casino, think of how many families you would save ! »

Shizune’s eyes suddenly brightened, flickering with unprecedented excitement : « That’s right ! I’m not doing something bad ; I’m doing a great good deed ! I’ve decided, I will follow Lady Tsunade and win every last cent from them, and save all the lost lambs in the Ninja World ! »

Plan successful. A flicker of imperceptible triumph flashed in Asakawa Shin’s eyes, and he immediately gave a thumbs up, praising Shizune generously.

At this moment, Tsunade tiptoed behind Shizune, trying to secretly snatch the sealing scroll in her hand. Unexpectedly, Shizune reacted extremely quickly this time, shielding the scroll against her chest like it was a treasure, and warned vigilantly : « Lady Tsunade, don’t even think about it ! This money cannot be given to you. This is a charitable fund to save the lost lambs of the Ninja World. Next time we go to a casino, I’ll give you at most one-third as an appearance fee. »

Tsunade’s eye twitched, and she cursed inwardly. Why does it feel like Shizune has been brainwashed by this brat ? What happens to the gamblers in the Ninja World has nothing to do with you ! But on second thought, as long as she had money to spend later, she couldn’t be bothered to argue.

Believe it or not, after losing thirty million, Tsunade actually felt refreshed and in a great mood. She patted her stomach contentedly and laughed : « Come to think of it, it’s already evening. My throat is dry from shouting all afternoon. Shin, you brat, you’ve made a fortune today, so you have to pay for tonight’s meal ! Let’s drink until we drop. »

Asakawa Shin’s face immediately fell, and he protested dissatisfiedly : « No way, Senior Sister Tsunade, why is it me again ? »

« Who told you to be so good at making money ? Come here, good boy, Senior Sister will carry you there. »

After saying that, Tsunade smilingly pulled Asakawa Shin into her arms and strode towards the luxurious tavern from last night. Seeing that Asakawa Shin indeed stopped resisting, Tsunade laughed inwardly : 'It really worked. As long as I act spoiled, this kid does whatever I say. Hehehe, now I’ve really got you in the palm of my hand, you brat.'

At this moment, a Ninja wearing a Hidden Cloud Village forehead protector happened to pass by. Upon hearing this conversation, he couldn’t help but stop, his face full of surprise, muttering : « Tsunade ? » 'Could it be that legendary Lady Tsunade ? The role model and idol of all medical Ninjas !' A strong urge welled up in his heart to step forward and pay his respects, but he was still on a mission, so he had to forcibly suppress his inner excitement and continue on his way to report to the Raikage.

Meanwhile, Tsunade had already walked into the tavern, her bold voice resounding throughout the hall : « Hey, someone come here ! Bring us the best wine and the most expensive dishes you have here, and be quick about it ! »
