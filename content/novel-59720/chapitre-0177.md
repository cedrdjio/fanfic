# Chapitre 177 — Prejudice

« Nani ? Senior Sister, are you saying that many girls' panties in the Village are mysteriously shattering ? How could such a thing happen ? »

Asakawa Shin’s eyes widened, his face filled with disbelief.

« Wait, Senior Sister, why are you staring at me like that ? It definitely wasn’t me ! Why would I do something so boring... »

Before he could finish, his whole body jolted, and his expression became visibly panicked.

'System, come out ! What exactly are the side effects of this new template ?' he cried out anxiously in his mind.

[Ding ! The current template’s negative status is : As your sisters, they will be unable to wear panties ; a judgment will be triggered within a certain range of you. Once the fusion reaches 100%, the negative status will disappear. Please refer to the manual for details.]

'Holy crap ! Is this even Chinese ? I recognize every character, but combined... how can I not understand it ?'

'System, oh System, you’ve really screwed me over !' Asakawa Shin wailed internally.

At this moment, Tsunade had already approached him, grabbing his collar and pulling him close until their foreheads touched, staring him down with a fierce gaze.

« Answer me ! Look in my eyes ! »

« Tell me why ? »

Asakawa Shin couldn’t withstand the terrifying pressure and quickly pushed Tsunade away, wiping cold sweat from his forehead.

« Senior Sister, can you stop mimicking me ? Besides, you know me, I really didn’t do it on purpose ! »

« It really was you ? »

Tsunade narrowed her eyes, wearing a look of disgust as if she were meeting him for the first time. « Who corrupted you ? Was it the old man or Jiraiya ? »

Without waiting for his answer, Tsunade turned to storm out in a rage.

Asakawa Shin was scared out of his wits and hurriedly pulled her back.

« Senior Sister, please don’t ! »

« Hmm ? What ? You still want to protect them ? »

« No, Senior Sister, if you make a big scene out of this, how will I ever show my face in Konoha again ? »

As he spoke, tears streamed down his face as he hugged Tsunade’s leg.

« Senior Sister, you’re the Hokage now, you must have a way to save me, right ? I’m still young, I want to be the Hokage in the future, I can’t have this kind of dark history... »

Tsunade glared at him with disappointment, sighed, and agreed to discuss it further after work when they returned home.

Only then did Asakawa Shin fly out of the Hokage Office with a worried heart.

« Anyway, I’ll go dye my hair first to distract myself. »

At the barber shop.

« Big sister, you’re just dyeing my hair, why are you shaking so much ? You’re making me nervous too, » Asakawa Shin said helplessly.

The barber took a deep breath, trying to calm her emotions : « Sorry, Lord Shin, I... I’m not in a good state today, I’m very sorry. »

It took twice as long as last time, but Asakawa Shin finally finished dyeing his hair.

The color was the same dark green as Fubuki’s, complementing the pale purple exotic flower on his head, looking quite handsome.

He admired himself in the mirror for a while, then hid his form in the air and flew a lap around the Village.

Screams followed him all along the way.

Through his sensory testing, the range of the side effect was roughly a one-kilometer radius, and the targets were all young women older than him, fitting his perception of « sisters. »

They had to be older than him—even if by just a minute or a second, they were categorized as « sisters, » such as Sakura, Ino, and Karin.

Among the female classmates of the same year, only Hinata escaped because she was two months younger than him.

The system’s products were truly rigorous and terrifyingly precise.

After the test, he used the Flying Thunder God to teleport back to his villa, causing another round of gasps.

Asakawa Shin looked up to see everyone gathered in the living room, either sitting or standing, all staring at him with flushed faces.

« Eh ? Why are there so many people ? Senior Sister, you didn’t... »

Tsunade smiled calmly. « That’s right, I’ve told them. After all, you said it yourself—we are 'family,' and we should have no secrets, right ? »

Internally, however, she pumped her fist in triumph.

'Let’s see how you handle this, you brat who doesn’t know restraint.'

Before Asakawa Shin could react, he was pulled onto the sofa and surrounded by everyone.

Kana said worriedly : « Lord Shin, how could you do such a thing ? If word gets out, it will be a huge blow to your reputation. »

Yūgao immediately nodded : « Exactly, it’s fine to mess around like this at home, but how could you... »

Before she could finish, she noticed everyone around her looking at her strangely, causing a flash of panic in her eyes.

'Oops, I accidentally said my inner thoughts out loud.'

She coughed lightly and immediately changed her tune : « I mean, it’s not okay at home either. But if you wanted to develop a new Ninjutsu, you could have just told me. It’s not like I wouldn’t cooperate. »

Asakawa Shin scratched his head in confusion, « New Ninjutsu ? »

Yūgao nodded, « Yes, aren’t you developing a new Ninjutsu ? »

Karin had a sudden realization : « I see, as expected of Lord Shin ! Since this technique can silently decompose panties, it must be able to decompose clothing as well. If used suddenly, it would definitely catch the enemy off guard ! »

Shizune’s expression changed drastically : « What ? That’s too powerful ! This Ninjutsu should be classified as a forbidden technique and sealed within the scroll of seals. »

Tsunade, however, shook her head with a look of disdain : « What use is such a technique ? If I were to attack naked and then use Transformation Jutsu to turn into clothes, wouldn’t that solve it ? »

« As expected of Lady Tsunade, to crack this Ninjutsu so easily. »

« Indeed, how can something be disintegrated if I didn’t have it to begin with ? Such quick thinking—no wonder she’s the Hokage... »

While they were seriously discussing, Asakawa Shin was silently observing them.

The five women before him all fell into the category of his « sisters, » which meant...

He coughed lightly, interrupting them.

« Um, Senior Sister, do you have a way to save me ? This technique of mine will last for several months at the very least. If this continues, it will surely cause a riot, right ? »

The women were shocked, looking at him in disbelief.

« Sev... several months ? Lord Shin, this is too much ! »

Facing their fierce glares, Asakawa Shin swallowed hard.

« I can’t help it either. It’s normal for a powerful technique to have some side effects, right ? »

Tsunade snorted coldly : « I think your mind is just corrupt. As the saying goes, 'thoughts by day, dreams by night.' It must be your constant wandering thoughts that caused such a ridiculous effect. »

Then she changed the subject, asking curiously : « So what kind of technique is it exactly ? Tell us about it. »

Asakawa Shin waved his hand dismissively, « Oh, it’s just being able to move mountains and seas, fly through the air, and pull down stars from the sky to smash people. It’s not that useful. »

He waited for a while but heard no praise. Looking up, he saw everyone staring at him with disdain.

Their expressions seemed to say, 'Keep bragging, we’ll see if we believe you.'

Asakawa Shin couldn’t stand it for another second. He immediately used Psychokinesis to pull them to his side and used the Flying Thunder God to teleport to the far-off ocean.

Then, using Psychokinesis, he physically lifted a turtle the size of an island from beneath his feet, making it hover in the air and fly at high speed away from the mainland.

The women hovered beside him, watching in stunned silence.

« Holy crap ! Junior Brother, everything you said was true ? Can you teach me ? As for destroying a few pairs of panties ? That’s nothing. If it makes you happy, I’ll even give you a few pairs. »

« Yeah, yeah, we’ll give ours to you too. »

Asakawa Shin rolled his eyes helplessly : « Shut up, all of you ! I’ve realized you all have a prejudice against me. I... I’m really not a pervert ! »
