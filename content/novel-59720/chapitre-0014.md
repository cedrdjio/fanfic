# Chapitre 14 — Sorry, I'm in a Hurry

« Someone, carry Neji away and let him have a good rest. »

Hyuga Hiashi watched Neji’s retreating figure as he was carried away, feeling somewhat embarrassed.

He had specifically called Neji here this time to let him see a broader sky, hoping he wouldn’t keep getting lost in his own world and stagnating.

Yet, he hadn’t expected that instead of the sky, Neji would see hell. That kid, Asakawa Shin, really knew how to mess with people’s minds.

Hopefully, Neji will be alright.

« Genin and Chunin, stand down for now. Hyakka, you are a Special Jonin skilled in Taijutsu, so you shall take the field next. Do not be careless ; this kid is not simple. »

Hearing Hiashi say this, many in the crowd breathed a sigh of relief. It wasn’t that they were afraid of losing, but facing Asakawa Shin’s behavior—which went beyond just defeating an opponent to crushing their spirit—made them a bit timid.

After all, everyone present was a Byakugan user, and they could tell whether Neji had been knocked unconscious by force or by pure frustration.

However, they didn’t stay happy for long, because the next round of the match was halted by Asakawa Shin before it could even begin.

« Uncle, please wait. Isn’t this method of fighting a gauntlet one by one a bit inappropriate ? »

Hinata also chimed in : « Father, there are so many of us, and Shin-kun is all alone. This is too unfair to Shin-kun ; we should at least give him enough time to rest. »

Hyuga Hiashi realized it too and nodded : « Indeed, it is not very appropriate. Hyakka, you stand down for now. You all can begin again once my nephew has rested. »

« That’s not it, Uncle. I meant that the gauntlet is too slow. Why not just let them all come at me at once ? Sorry, I’m in a hurry. »

As soon as he finished speaking, the entire field fell silent.

Hyuga Sosuke, a Jonin from the Hyuga Main House, couldn’t help but speak up first : « Kid, what did you say ? I didn’t hear you clearly just now. I’ll give you another chance to rephrase that. »

Hyuga Hiashi also said in shock : « Nephew, there are two Jonin and one Special Jonin among them. Won’t you reconsider ? »

« There’s no need, Uncle. I really have something to do later, so please forgive me. »

As soon as Asakawa Shin said this, it instantly infuriated the group, and they were all eager to teach him a lesson.

However, this was exactly what Asakawa Shin wanted ; acting out that little play with Neji earlier had been truly boring.

Right now, he just wanted to show off and have some fun.

There was no better prey than a provoked beast.

...

On the training ground.

Over a dozen Byakugan users stared at the young man opposite them with expressions ranging from anger and mockery to disdain.

The young man, however, looked relaxed, as if he had returned home, exuding an aura of absolute ease.

This only made the Byakugan users more agitated, waiting for the referee’s signal to teach this arrogant brat a lesson.

Hyuga Hiashi looked at Asakawa Shin in the center of the field, who nodded at him with a smile.

'To be so composed… perhaps he really is confident. In that case, then…'

« Begin ! » Hyuga Hiashi announced as the referee.

As soon as he finished speaking, seven or eight Ninjas rushed toward Asakawa Shin like arrows released from a bow.

« Your courage is commendable, but you’re too slow ! Man ! »

Asakawa Shin didn’t retreat but advanced, using Shave to charge into the crowd. With just an elbow strike, he sent three Ninjas who couldn’t react in time flying, leaving them lying on the ground, unable to get back up.

The remaining Ninjas finally reacted and surrounded him tightly.

Sosuke and the other Jonin also closed in. Even they hadn’t been able to clearly see that extreme speed just now, and they were Byakugan users, at that.

'Even with the insight of the Byakugan, we couldn’t track it ?' Sosuke’s heart sank.

In other words, if Asakawa Shin’s target had been them, they might be the ones lying on the ground right now.

Sosuke decisively ordered : « This kid is not simple, everyone attack together ! »

The others responded, preparing to combine their strength to take down Asakawa Shin.

« Don’t be afraid, he’s only one person ! »

« Let’s use Gentle Fist together to seal his Chakra points. »

« Gentle Fist : Tenketsu ! »

« Gentle Fist Art : Eight Trigrams Sixty-Four Palms ! »

« All together now ! »

« Hmph… if attacking all at once worked, what would be the point of training ? How can a swarm of ants challenge a dragon ? »

Asakawa Shin sneered in disdain. He supported himself on the ground with one hand and spun his body rapidly, his feet slicing through the air like lightning, creating massive Wind Release blades that slashed in all directions.

« Tempest Kick : Perimeter Slash ! »

« Nani ? ! »

« Dodge, quickly ! »

...

The training ground was in shambles ; trees and buildings within hundreds of meters had been sliced in half at the waist, stirring up countless birds and filling the sky with dust.

Fortunately, the training ground was spacious and there were few people nearby, so there wasn’t too much damage or loss of life.

Hyuga Hyakka sat slumped on the ground. She had almost thought she was going to die just now, but unexpectedly, the invincible, massive wind blade suddenly rose into the air and swept over their heads by a few meters. Even so, the gale it created blew everyone dozens of meters away.

If they had faced that wind blade head-on, they would definitely have been dead. Her whole body ached now ; it felt no different from having taken a direct hit from a Wind Release Ninjutsu.

« Are you kidding me ? This kind of attack is too exaggerated ! »

« Hiss… is this kid trying to kill us ? I almost peed myself just now. »

« A forbidden jutsu ! The power of this attack is definitely at the level of a forbidden jutsu. Did you see that ? The wind blade flew thousands of meters into the sky ! »

« Holy crap, look at the sky ; the clouds have been sliced open. »

« I wonder if my Rotation could withstand that ? »

When this was said, everyone turned their gazes to see it was Hyuga Sosuke.

This guy had been the one shouting « Eight Trigrams Sixty-Four Palms » while charging at the very front, and he was also the person closest to the wind blade.

At this moment, his whole body was covered in tiny cuts, his clothes were tattered, and he had almost become a bloody mess.

That was caused by the cutting force of the wind pressure. He seemed a bit dazed, as if he had been broken.

« What, so he’s just delirious. I actually thought he could see Rotation blocking Perimeter Slash. »

« This guy Sosuke, he really is baffling. »

« Hahaha, sorry, sorry, I accidentally went a bit overboard. » Asakawa Shin walked over, scratching his head in embarrassment.

His Tempest Kick : Perimeter Slash from just now had fused Rokushiki, a Grandmaster Swordsman-level slash, and Wind Release Chakra nature transformation ; it was an experimental move born from a moment of inspiration.

Fortunately, he had a premonition that this strike would be terrifying, so he aimed it high into the sky. He hadn’t expected, however, that just the wind pressure generated by the passing wind blade would cause such massive destruction, especially to Hyuga Sosuke, who was closest and had basically been sliced to ribbons.

It took a while for Hyuga Hiashi to return with Hinata. There was no helping it ; although Hinata was far away, she was light, and she had been swept away by the gale for over a hundred meters. Fortunately, Hiashi had noticed something wrong in time and saved her.

Although Hinata looked disheveled at the moment, her eyes revealed an unprecedented, bright light. 'That move is so amazing. I’m a peerless genius too, can I learn it too ? !'

Hyuga Hiashi walked over slowly, looking at the scene around him with a face full of disbelief. Such massive destructive power and an attack range that covered the entire field—was this something Taijutsu could achieve ? If one took this strike head-on, there would definitely be nothing left of them.

Hiashi was not some small-time Ninja who hadn’t seen the world ; he deeply understood the terror of this move. Looking at Asakawa Shin’s calm and collected appearance, he figured that releasing this move was, if not as simple as lifting a hand or foot, not far off.

There was no doubt that this brat had already stepped into the Kage level at such a young age, and he definitely wasn’t weak among the Kage level either.

For a powerhouse at this level, the usual advantage of numbers was completely meaningless, unless one employed a human-wave tactic regardless of the cost.

The key point was that this brat was only six years old ! What was he doing when he was six ? Probably still playing Ninja games ?

« Clan Leader, are you alright ? »

« What happened here ? Did you encounter an attack ? Where is the enemy ? »

At this moment, a large number of Ninjas arrived, including even masked Anbu members. The wind blade from just now had raged across the sky with such massive momentum that it was clearly visible even from dozens of miles away.

Hiashi said with some helplessness : « No need to panic, there is no enemy. We were just sparring. »

« In any case, let’s deal with the injured first. Sosuke, I think he’s almost dead… »
