# Chapitre 80 — Yūgao Uzuki

« Katsuyu, do you know where Senior Sister Tsunade is right now ? »

On top of Asakawa Shin’s head, the little slug was curiously fiddling with the seedling. Hearing Asakawa Shin’s sudden question, she quickly got into character and respectfully replied :

« Reporting to Lord Shin, when Lady Tsunade summoned me a few days ago, she was in the Land of Hot Springs. If we pick up the pace, we might be able to meet her. »

Katsuyu’s answer brought a look of pleasant surprise to Asakawa Shin’s face. Could it really be the famous Land of Hot Springs ?

He had long wanted to visit, but unfortunately never had the chance. Now, he could finally get his wish.

Facts proved that the decision to summon Katsuyu after performing the Shadow Clone Technique was incredibly wise.

Otherwise, the Shadow Clone would have caused a huge scene upon hearing the news, creating unnecessary complications.

« Since time is tight, let’s set off now to rendezvous with Itachi. »

When he arrived at the Konoha gate, Itachi was indeed already waiting.

A flicker of satisfaction flashed in Asakawa Shin’s eyes, but...

Why was there a familiar-looking Anbu Ninja standing on the side ?

« Itachi, you’re here quite early. By the way, who is this ? »

Before Itachi could respond, the Anbu member snatched off her mask and squeezed forward, full of excitement :

« Lord Shin, it’s me ! Your bodyguard, Yūgao Uzuki, reporting for duty ! »

A year ago, Hiruzen Sarutobi had asked her if she was willing to serve as Asakawa Shin’s bodyguard. Naturally, she had happily agreed.

Asakawa Shin was not only her idol but also her savior.

Now, being able to follow her idol around while being paid was a dream come true for her.

Unexpectedly, Asakawa Shin left the Village to train, and this departure lasted for a long, arduous year, which drove Yūgao Uzuki crazy with anxiety.

She went to find Hiruzen Sarutobi every few days, insisting on serving as a bodyguard for Asakawa Shin’s Shadow Clone.

But that was just too absurd ; why would a Shadow Clone need a bodyguard ?

Furthermore, Yūgao Uzuki was an important combat asset for the Anbu, and the Anbu was currently short-staffed.

It turned out that ever since silk became popular in the Ninja World, many skilled Ninjas stopped performing missions and instead went to work full-time weaving at the textile factory established by the Hyuga Clan.

After all, the work there was both safe and the pay was more generous.

This caused missions in the Village to pile up. With manpower already insufficient, how could such an excellent talent as Yūgao be wasted ?

Hiruzen Sarutobi’s brain worked quickly, and he immediately promised Yūgao : as long as her Anbu performance review reached an excellent rating, he would make an exception and arrange her transfer as soon as Asakawa Shin returned.

Thus, throughout that year, Yūgao dedicated herself to completing every performance metric, often executing seven or eight missions a day. Such diligent effort finally earned her this morning’s encounter.

Otherwise, if Asakawa Shin had left this time, Yūgao would likely have missed her chance to meet him again.

Asakawa Shin naturally didn’t know about all this. Seeing Yūgao Uzuki looking teary-eyed, a touch of emotion welled up in his heart.

It seemed that this Yūgao Uzuki was not only his loyal fan but also deeply affectionate toward him. The loyalty of his fan was so strong that it truly made him sigh with emotion.

Thus, Asakawa Shin smiled and pulled out a pen, silently making a major decision to have a two-way connection with his fan :

« So it’s Yūgao. You’ve come at just the right time.

I was actually a bit worried about safety when leaving the Village this time. Since you’re here, why don’t you accompany me and protect me on this trip ?

By the way, do you need me to sign an autograph for you ? »

« An autograph ? » Yūgao Uzuki was slightly stunned, confusion rising in her eyes. Why was he suddenly offering to sign an autograph ?

But her eyes soon lit up, and she pointed to her left arm and asked :

« Lord Shin, can you sign here ? I plan to get it tattooed afterward as my exclusive mark. »

« Huh ? You’re going to get it tattooed ? Haha, Yūgao, you’re really interesting. »

Asakawa Shin was slightly stunned, then a flash of pleasant surprise crossed his eyes. This unique way of showing support made him feel very intrigued.

While Asakawa Shin was signing with great interest, Yūgao stared at the Anbu tattoo on her other arm, lost in thought :

'I’ll wash it off later. What kind of status do you think you have to be mentioned in the same breath as Lord Shin’s signature ?'

Uchiha Itachi, standing nearby, also wore a thoughtful expression. A tattoo, huh ?

...

The autograph was quickly finished. Just as Yūgao was overjoyed, a pale-faced male Ninja walked over in a hurry. His eyes revealed a hint of joy as he said to Yūgao :

« Yūgao, I finally found you.

I realized a brilliant sword technique last night, and this morning I went to the Anbu to look for you, wanting to share it with you.

But your teammates told me that you had already quit the Anbu, so I’ve been looking for you everywhere.

Are you... are you... going out of the Village to perform a mission ?

I wonder if your team is still short-handed ? I am also willing to do my humble part. »

Yūgao Uzuki, however, changed her previous fanatical expression and replied coldly :

« Oh, so it’s Gekkō Hayate. Sorry, our team is not short of people anymore.

Also, please don’t contact me in the future. I’m afraid Lord Shin might misunderstand. »

After saying this, she turned to Asakawa Shin, her face full of excited smiles, and asked softly :

« Lord Shin, it’s getting late. Should we set off now ? »

Asakawa Shin nodded noncommittally and led the team to start moving first. On the way, he asked with a curious tone :

« Yūgao, who was that man ? You two seemed quite familiar with each other. »

Yūgao’s heart tightened, and she hurriedly explained :

« Lord Shin, please don’t misunderstand.

We just met when we were learning sword techniques before, and afterward, we were just ordinary colleagues. I’m not close to him. »

Asakawa Shin slowly turned his head, ignoring her.

Just as Yūgao became increasingly panicked, Asakawa Shin finally spoke leisurely :

« Learning sword techniques ? I also know a thing or two about sword techniques. From now on, you can learn from me.

By the way, since you are now my bodyguard, you shouldn’t associate with those shady people anymore. »

Yūgao Uzuki was full of gratitude, tears welling up in her eyes. She choked out :

« Yes, Lord Shin. I understand. I will study diligently and will never let you down. »

As they went further and further away, disappearing at the end of the line of sight.

Outside the Konoha gate, only Gekkō Hayate was left standing alone, his gaze dull, as if his soul had left his body.

Kotetsu Hagane, who was guarding the gate nearby, really couldn’t watch anymore. He flicked Hayate on the forehead, half-jokingly and half-remindingly saying :

« Stop looking. She’s Lord Shin’s bodyguard now. »

Gekkō Hayate seemed not to feel it, still staring blankly ahead.

Seeing that he didn’t react after taking a hit, Kotetsu Hagane flicked him on the forehead again :

« Still looking ! »

Gekkō Hayate finally snapped out of it, hurriedly wanting to chase after them, shouting heart-rendingly while chasing :

« Yūgao, oh Yūgao~

How can I live without you~ »

But he was ill after all, and after running only a few steps, his asthma flared up, and he knelt on the ground powerlessly.

He could only let out a desperate cry to the sky :

« No— ! »

...

Asakawa Shin and the other two were too far away to hear anything. Traveling at full speed, they arrived at the territory of the Land of Hot Springs in just over a day.

Their luck was quite good ; as soon as they stepped into the town, they spotted Tsunade.

Because she was just too conspicuous, she was currently being chased by a group of people.

« Stop ! Don’t run ! Tsunade, pay up quickly ! »

« I beg you, it’s been six years ! Give us back our money. Just the principal is fine. If this continues, we really can’t hold on anymore ! »

Tsunade’s lips curled up, and she thought disdainfully :

'What a joke, pay back the money ? Why should I pay back money that I borrowed with my own ability ?'

So she ran even faster and quickly left the group far behind.

After turning a corner, she quickly formed hand seals, her body instantly transforming into a cute little girl, and she walked back swaggeringly, brushing past the debt collectors.

The debt collectors rushed past her, but they didn’t recognize her at all.

Tsunade watched their retreating figures, her lips curling up again, her heart full of smugness.

Asakawa Shin was dumbfounded, not knowing for a moment whether he should step forward to greet her.

'Hmm, maybe I should come back later...'
