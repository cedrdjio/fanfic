# Chapitre 73 — I Acknowledge You

Looking at the familiar Konoha gate before him, Asakawa Shin’s tense expression finally relaxed, and he let out a long breath.

He had been traveling at full speed for two whole days before finally making it back to this familiar land.

It wasn’t until Sage Katsuyu sent him out of Shikkotsu Forest that he realized that secret realm actually existed in a different space outside the Ninja World, and the entrance connected to the Ninja World was very far from Konoha.

'It seems the study of the Flying Thunder God Technique needs to be put on the agenda as soon as possible. Not to mention that it’s cool and powerful, it’s truly incredibly convenient for getting home. As it happens, I’ve already dabbled in the pile of pre-requisite techniques, and I have that bastard Shadow Clone to thank for that...'

The Ninjas guarding the Konoha gate spotted Asakawa Shin at this moment and hurried over to greet him : « Great ! It’s Lord Shin ! You’ve finally returned. Please, go to the Hokage Building right away. »

« That’s right, Hokage-sama has been waiting for you for a long time. »

Asakawa Shin cast his gaze over and saw it was none other than the Konoha gate guardians, the brothers Kotetsu Hagane and Izumo Kamizuki. Were these two already guarding the gate this early ?

How promising. It’s said that even Pain wouldn’t dare to force his way through the gate guarded by these two brothers...

But since they are so formidable, when he becomes Hokage in the future, he would just keep them guarding the gate.

Thinking of this, Asakawa Shin greeted them in a friendly manner and hurried towards the Hokage Building.

Hokage Office.

« Teacher, what happened to you ? How could you be so weak ? »

The moment Asakawa Shin saw Hiruzen Sarutobi, he couldn’t believe his eyes.

The old man before him seemed to have aged ten years all of a sudden ; he was dazed and listless, with an IV drip attached to his hand.

« Is... is that you, little Shin ? You’ve finally returned, wah ! »

Seeing that it was Asakawa Shin who had returned, Hiruzen Sarutobi’s normally serious face suddenly crumbled, and he burst into tears with a « wah. » After a while, he continued, emotionally agitated : « Danzo... Danzo killed Uchiha Fugaku, stole Uchiha Fugaku’s Sharingan, and has defected ! »

Hearing this, Asakawa Shin was also a bit dazed : « Who killed whom ? And who defected ? »

« It’s Danzo... it’s Danzo... wah~ » Hiruzen Sarutobi was sobbing uncontrollably by this point, unable to even sit steadily.

'He’s so old, why is he still acting like a child ?'

Asakawa Shin hurriedly stepped forward to support him and comforted him : « Teacher, please stop crying. Taking care of your health is what’s important ! »

Unexpectedly, Hiruzen Sarutobi suddenly grabbed his hand and looked at him with hope : « Little Shin, why don’t I pass the position of Hokage to you right now ? I... I have important things to do ; I need to go after Danzo... »

'Go after Danzo ? You’re so old, why are you copying Jiraiya ? Is this some kind of Konoha tradition ?'

Asakawa Shin was a bit numb at this moment. Although he was determined to obtain the position of Hokage, it wasn’t for now.

According to the order explained by the System, he should first become a Genin, then a Chunin, a Jonin, and finally the Hokage.

The System is so abstract ; what if he became Hokage now and it didn’t give him the template ?

Therefore, he decided to promote step-by-step according to the strictest rules. The position of Hokage must absolutely not be taken now ; Hiruzen Sarutobi still needs to shoulder this position for a few more years.

Thinking of this, Asakawa Shin was sincere and refused directly without hesitation : « No ! Teacher, I will absolutely not accept the position of Hokage. Right now, I am far from qualified to shoulder this heavy responsibility. This position still requires you, Teacher, to sit in it for a few more years for me ! »

Seeing Asakawa Shin refuse so decisively, a flash of surprise crossed Hiruzen Sarutobi’s eyes.

Having struggled in the arena of fame and fortune for so many years, how could he not see that this child was expressing his true feelings at this moment ?

'Sigh, this child Shin is truly incredibly filial !' Hiruzen Sarutobi was moved for a moment.

He had been too impulsive just now. Although this child was gifted, he was only seven years old after all ; suddenly ascending to the position of Hokage would likely bring him incomparable pressure.

Very well, he would just sit in this position for a few more years.

Hiruzen Sarutobi was already used to his beloved disciples declining the position of Hokage, as his previous two disciples had been the same.

So he no longer insisted, sighed lightly, and said : « Alright then, I will shoulder it for you for a few more years. »

A trace of gloom flashed in Asakawa Shin’s eyes : 'He changed his mind so quickly ? Shouldn’t there be three refusals and three acceptances ? Was that old man testing me just now ? Hmph ! It’s a good thing I’m sharp. However, whether it was a test or not, when the time comes, you will have to step down whether you want to or not.'

The master and disciple looked at each other and laughed out loud, and for a moment, the atmosphere in the room actually became cheerful.

Asakawa Shin laughed for a while before straightening his expression : « Teacher, are you saying that Danzo killed Uchiha Fugaku, stole his Sharingan, and then defected ? Wasn’t he being strictly detained before ? »

Hiruzen Sarutobi’s face also darkened, and he recounted in a low voice through gritted teeth : « That bastard used some unknown method to escape ! Not only that, but he also colluded with outsiders to go on a killing spree at the Naka Shrine, slaughtering all the Uchiha Ninjas who were holding a meeting at the time. Some former Root members were even involved. »

« Outsiders ? What outsiders ? » A flash of insight passed through Asakawa Shin’s eyes as he grasped the key point.

Hiruzen Sarutobi tapped on the table, and an Anbu Ninja pushed the door open and walked in : « Itachi, you explain it to little Shin. After all, you were an eyewitness to the incident. »

« Yes ! Hokage-sama ! » The Anbu Ninja was none other than Uchiha Itachi. He saluted Asakawa Shin and said : « Lord Shin, the person who colluded with Danzo was a man wearing a tiger-skin mask. He possesses an incredible space-time Ninjutsu ; no attack could hit him, which is why he was able to escape easily. »

Asakawa Shin nodded in understanding : « I have a general idea of the situation now. Itachi, I am very sorry about Uncle Fugaku ; please accept my condolences. By the way, where are Shisui and Sasuke ? They weren’t hurt, were they ? »

The Pain of losing his father made Uchiha Itachi look a bit dejected, but he managed a reluctant smile when he heard Asakawa Shin mention Sasuke : « They are both fine. Sasuke was training hard in the Six Styles Training Class at the time and wasn’t involved in this matter at all. Shisui and I were working overtime at the Security Department. When we received reports from the personnel monitoring the Naka Shrine and realized the situation had changed, we assembled our troops and rushed over. But it was too late by the time we arrived. We only had a brief encounter with Danzo and that masked man. We exchanged a couple of moves, and they used space-time Ninjutsu to teleport away. »

Asakawa Shin acutely discovered the point of interest and couldn’t help but ask : « Hmm ? You were working overtime, didn’t those Uchiha at the Naka Shrine have to work overtime ? And why were you monitoring them ? »

Itachi scratched his head, a bit embarrassed : « They were all hardliners of the Hawk faction and didn’t join any of Konoha’s departments, so... »

Asakawa Shin’s eyes narrowed slightly, and he gloated inwardly : 'Aren’t those just the unruly Uchiha ? I’m afraid that their meeting at the Naka Shrine was just to vent their dissatisfaction, right ? They really deserved to die... As expected of you, Danzo ! The top disciple of the Second Hokage, Senju Tobirama, and the master of the will of fire. You, who inherited Senju Tobirama’s will, truly didn’t hold back at all when dealing with these naturally evil Uchiha. I acknowledge you, Danzo.'
