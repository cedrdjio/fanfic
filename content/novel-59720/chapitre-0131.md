# Chapitre 131 — Forest of Death

The next day.

84 examinees, totaling 28 teams, gathered at the designated location according to the procedures of the Chunin Exams.

Anko Mitarashi’s gaze was sharp as lightning as she quickly scanned the crowd. Confirming that everyone had arrived, she walked up to them with her hands in her pockets :

« Welcome to Training Ground 44. This will be the venue for your second exam. It’s worth mentioning that this place has another name, called the 'Forest of Death.' As for why it’s called the Forest of Death, you’ll soon find out~ »

« De... Death Forest ? That name sounds really ominous ! »

Naruto widened his eyes, his face full of terror, as if he were truly frightened.

Anko was just about to laugh out loud when she saw Naruto suddenly wiggle his butt and start dancing. That wasn’t all ; he didn’t forget to arrogantly taunt her :

« Do you really think I’d believe such nonsense ? How naive ! No matter how you try to scare me, I won’t be afraid~ »

Asakawa Shin’s mouth twitched as he watched : 'Naruto, this guy is just too asking for it. Watching him makes me want to beat him up...'

Sure enough, with Anko’s fiery temper, she couldn’t tolerate it at all.

« Is that so ? What an ignorant brat. »

A vein popped on Anko’s forehead, and without another word, she abruptly threw a Kunai.

With a flash of cold light, the Kunai grazed Naruto’s cheek, leaving a bloody scratch, before embedding itself into the ground behind him with undiminished force. The Ninja behind Naruto was also caught in the crossfire, having a lock of hair sliced off.

« You... what are you doing ? »

Before Naruto could recover from his shock, Anko’s figure had already teleported behind him.

She stroked Naruto’s cheek with one hand, her fingertips stained with the fresh blood seeping from the wound, her tone and expression becoming incredibly cold :

« However, over-energetic brats like you often die the fastest. Just like this, gushing out my favorite fresh blood, and then... »

Before Anko could finish scaring the annoying brat, her pupils constricted, and a chill instinctively crawled up the back of her neck.

Her gaze hardened, and just as she was about to draw a Kunai to counterattack, her movements suddenly froze, and she stood rooted to the spot. This was because a long tongue had wrapped around the Kunai she had just thrown, hovering right in front of her.

'This... this tongue...'

« This Kunai... returning it to you. »

The voice from behind cut off her train of thought.

Anko quickly composed herself, snatched the Kunai, and turned around. She saw that the person was a plain-looking female Ninja, and judging by her forehead protector, she must be a candidate from Kusagakure.

'So it isn’t him ?'

Unpleasant memories flooded her mind. Anko wiped away her smile, and her eyes turned cold as she stared fixedly at the other person :

« Thank you for going through the trouble of picking it up for me. But next time, if you don’t want to die, don’t stand behind me with killing intent. »

The woman retracted her physically unsettling long tongue and replied in a similarly cold tone :

« Sorry, I’m the type who gets excited at the sight of blood. Besides, you were the one who cut my beloved hair first, so that’s why... »

The two locked gazes for a moment before the woman finally looked away first and turned back to her team. Only when the two had completely separated did the tense atmosphere at the scene finally ease.

Karin leaned in close to Asakawa Shin with a grave expression and whispered : « Lord Shin, that guy’s Chakra is a bit strange... »

Asakawa Shin waved his hand casually, his eyes filled with excitement : « Strange ? No, it’s just right ! »

Karin wanted to say something more, but seeing Asakawa Shin’s indifferent attitude, she ultimately swallowed her words. She simply exchanged a glance with Haku and secretly raised her guard.

While Asakawa Shin was observing Orochimaru, Orochimaru felt something and turned his gaze over :

'So this is my junior brother ? To be worth the old man taking on an apprentice at his age... I’m afraid he has some extraordinary talent. He can be added to the candidate list !'

The two looked at each other for a moment before breaking eye contact, both revealing confident smiles.

Anko cleared her throat and smiled again : « It seems this Chunin Exams has gathered quite a few hot-blooded people. It’s really something to look forward to. But that’s also the troublesome part. Listen up, everyone. Anyone who wants to participate in the second Chunin Exams must sign this agreement before continuing. »

She pulled a large stack of papers from her bosom, with the three large characters « Consent Form » clearly written on them. Facing the confused gazes of the crowd, she explained nonchalantly :

« After all, people can die in this exam, so I must have your handwritten signatures for confirmation... Otherwise, if something happens and it gets blamed on me, that would be bad, hehe. »

As she spoke, she distributed the consent forms, then unfolded a map of the Forest of Death and began to explain the exam rules in detail :

« Simply put, you need to carry out a wilderness survival challenge in the Forest of Death, which has a radius of ten kilometers. Each of your teams will be issued a scroll—either a Heaven Scroll or an Earth Scroll. There are a total of 28 teams that passed the first Chunin Exams, so 14 teams will carry the Heaven Scroll, and the other 14 teams will carry the Earth Scroll. To pass, you must collect both the Heaven and Earth scrolls and have all three members of your team reach the central tower of the Forest of Death within 120 hours. All these conditions are indispensable ; only then will you be considered to have passed the second Chunin Exams. »

As soon as these words were spoken, an uproar immediately broke out.

« Doesn’t that mean at most only 14 teams can pass ? »

« This is bad. If scrolls are damaged, lost, or obtained repeatedly by others, it could lead to a reduction in the number of teams that pass. »

« No wonder they dared to say at least half the people would be eliminated. It turns out that, by mechanism alone, it’s impossible for more than half to qualify ! »

« From now on, it will inevitably lead to teams fighting each other and snatching scrolls. Even Ninjas from the same village might not be trustworthy... »

« That’s right, everyone has become a hunter, and everyone is also prey. »

« 120 hours, that is, five days. Within this limited time, we have to collect scrolls and travel at least ten kilometers through a dangerous forest to reach the central tower... »

« How to survive and rest during this period is also a difficult problem. After all, enemies could attack at any time. In short, this is a troublesome exam. »

Anko clapped her hands, and seeing everyone look over, she continued :

« Very good, it seems everyone understands well. I would like to add one more elimination clause here ! That is, before reaching the tower, you are absolutely forbidden from opening the Heaven or Earth scrolls to check their contents ! »

Seeing Anko so serious, Naruto couldn’t help but break into a cold sweat : « Then what happens if we open them halfway ? »

« When you see the contents of the scroll, something very terrible will happen. As for exactly what... » Anko said, enunciating each word, and answered playfully : « Please imagine it for yourself~ »

« Tch~ »

« When you become Chunin, you will also have the opportunity to access confidential documents. This is to test whether you are trustworthy ! Alright, the explanation ends here. After signing the consent forms, go to the hut next to you to collect your scrolls. You will enter from 44 randomly drawn entrances. Lastly, one final piece of advice for everyone. Whatever you do, don’t die, hehehe~ »

« ... »

« Lord Shin, our entrance is number 42. It looks like quite a peripheral location. What should we do next ? »

Karin blinked her large eyes, her expression full of excitement.

Just thinking about the fact that she would have to survive in the wild for a full five days made her unable to calm down. All things considered, wasn’t this just like going on a honeymoon with Lord Shin ?

Asakawa Shin rubbed his chin and cast his gaze to the left : « I’m interested in a certain someone, but there’s no rush. The process of playing with prey is also worth enjoying. Hmm, anyway, let’s collect the scrolls first. »

« Yes ! »

Before the words had even faded, the three of them sprang into action and dashed into the Forest of Death.

At the same time, the remaining 27 teams were not to be outdone and stepped into this natural hunting ground.
