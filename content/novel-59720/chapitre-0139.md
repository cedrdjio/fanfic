# Chapitre 139 — Joining Forces

Orochimaru let out a low growl, his tone urgent : « Shukaku, it seems our only choice is to join forces and fight together. Only then is there a slim chance of survival. »

Shukaku also understood the gravity of the situation. He nodded his massive head heavily and replied in a deep voice, « Fine ! We will join forces for now ! »

Before his voice had even faded, he had already opened his mouth, condensing a massive ball of Wind Release Chakra, and shouted his move : « Wind Release : Air Bullets ! »

Immediately, the violent wind orb hurtled toward Asakawa Shin’s Wood Golem like a meteorite.

The controlled Senju Hashirama also pressed his palms together and gave a low shout : « Wood Style : Deep Forest Emergence ! »

In an instant, a small forest rose from the ground, as countless thick tree trunks coiled toward the Wood Golem’s feet like spirit snakes.

At the same time, Senju Tobirama had silently flickered in front of Orochimaru.

Sensing the other’s intent, Tobirama’s pupils shrank suddenly, and he gave a sharp warning filled with shock and anger : « Not good ! This bastard actually wants to use my Flying Thunder God Technique to escape ! Stop him quickly ! »

Upon hearing this, Asakawa Shin’s expression remained normal, but Shukaku, who was now in the same camp as him, had a massive change in expression, filled with shock and rage.

The entire Tailed Beast’s body stumbled violently, nearly falling to the ground.

« What ? You damned human ! You dare... »

He hurriedly turned his head toward Orochimaru’s location, but it was already too late.

Senju Tobirama’s palm had just touched Orochimaru’s shoulder when the two disappeared into thin air in a flash of light, escaping the Forest of Death via the Flying Thunder God Technique.

Asakawa Shin’s eyes flickered slightly, but he did not move to stop them.

The reason was simple : under his continuous offensive, Orochimaru’s entire body was already covered in Flying Thunder God Marks.

Unless he used some special means, it would be absolutely impossible to escape his tracking in the short term.

However, he soon frowned slightly, thinking of Senju Tobirama beside Orochimaru.

That damned Tobirama... being a fellow Flying Thunder God user, he might actually have the means to crack his marks.

Forget it, better to settle this quickly and deal with this stupid tanuki first.

He had to find Orochimaru and take back what belonged to him before the marks were cracked.

With his mind set, Asakawa Shin’s gaze swept coldly toward Shukaku.

From the perspective of that massive Wood Golem, Shukaku was practically like a child, barely reaching the Wood Golem’s waist.

Whether it was Shukaku’s counterattack or the Wood Release used by Senju Hashirama, before this gargantuan entity, they were like children waving toys, completely devoid of deterrence.

Aside from the Tailed Beast Ball, Shukaku seemed to have no moves left to show, and even the Tailed Beast Ball could be easily resisted using the Wood Human Technique.

Senju Hashirama was even worse off ; restricted by this shell, he couldn’t display his true strength at all.

The Wood Release Ninjutsu he performed, aside from that 'Deep Forest Emergence', was nothing else ; he could only barely manage to grow a forest to 'give the Wood Golem a pedicure' in an attempt to delay its movements.

But this was ultimately futile.

The Wood Golem lightly lifted its foot, instantly snapping and crushing all the coiling vines, pressing toward Shukaku without losing momentum.

At this moment, Shukaku’s heart was filled with panic. Looking at the approaching colossus, his massive body couldn’t help but tremble slightly.

But in the next moment, he forcibly suppressed his instinctive fear.

After all, his rival, the Nine-Tailed Jinchuriki, was present at the scene. He had clearly sensed the Chakra Naruto leaked earlier.

If he appeared too pathetic in front of the other, he would probably be mocked for centuries and never be able to live it down.

He gritted his teeth inwardly, mobilizing the remaining Chakra in his body to condense another Tailed Beast Ball.

He then controlled this sphere containing terrifying energy to strike the Wood Golem.

Facing the whistling Tailed Beast Ball, the Wood Golem simply reached out a giant palm and grabbed it directly.

Under Shukaku’s horrified gaze, the Wood Golem actually caught the Tailed Beast Ball with one hand, then charged toward him with giant strides.

Finally, it slammed this Tailed Beast Ball, which was powerful enough to destroy mountains, ruthlessly into his chest.

With a thunderous roar, the Tailed Beast Ball erupted in a powerful shockwave that swept through the surroundings. Shukaku and the Wood Golem bore the brunt of it, being hit directly by the attack.

Asakawa Shin clapped his hands, using 'Wood Release : Hotei Technique' to protect himself.

Immediately after, he controlled the Wood Golem to shift its body and press forward, while its other hand still gripped Shukaku tightly to prevent him from escaping.

For a time, the attack of the Tailed Beast Ball was entirely blocked by the massive bodies of Shukaku and the Wood Golem.

Even for the powerful Wood Golem, after taking this hit head-on, its body couldn’t help but vibrate violently.

On the other side, Shukaku’s situation was even more tragic. The Tailed Beast Ball had been slammed against his chest, forcing him to endure this terrifying blow at point-blank range.

As the dust settled, Shukaku’s massive body was already tattered and broken.

Accompanied by a mournful cry, it rapidly shrank and regressed, finally turning back into Gaara’s thin body, which slumped limply to the ground, having been defeated in one blow and completely losing the ability to fight.

Meanwhile, Senju Hashirama had still not given up, controlling Wood Release Ninjutsu to carry out continuous harassment and containment.

Asakawa Shin hesitated for a moment, then leaped down from the Wood Golem’s head. His figure turned into a bolt of black lightning, approaching Senju Hashirama directly at a startling speed indistinguishable to the naked eye.

In an instant, the two were face to face.

The Deep Forest Emergence blocking the path was violently shattered by Asakawa Shin, failing to slow him down even slightly.

The moment he closed in, he did not hesitate, driving a heavy punch ruthlessly into Senju Hashirama’s chest.

With just one hit, a huge hole was blasted through the Edo Tensei body, and the entire person was sent flying far away, unable to move for a time.

The drifting gray dust was slowly filling the gap in his chest as time passed.

Asakawa Shin flickered to Senju Hashirama’s side, quickly grabbed one of his hands, and overlapped it with his own to weave signs.

He gave a light shout : « Edo Tensei : Release ! »

Senju Hashirama felt his body lighten as the willpower controlling him vanished without a trace ; Asakawa Shin had actually forcibly released the control of the Edo Tensei.

« What ? That feeling of being controlled is gone ! I’m free ? »

Senju Hashirama was filled with a mix of surprise and joy, shouting in disbelief, « There’s actually such a maneuver ? »

Asakawa Shin was slightly taken aback and asked back, « Isn’t the method to release this technique recorded in the Senju Secret Scrolls ? Shodai-sama, did you not know ? »

Senju Hashirama was momentarily stunned upon hearing this, then he scratched his head and gave a simple, honest, and silly smile :

« Uh... now that you mention it... it seems Tobirama did mention this to me, but I didn’t take it to heart at all back then, hahaha ! »

Immediately, Senju Hashirama’s expression turned stern as he looked solemnly at Asakawa Shin :

« I’m sorry, I didn’t expect to cause you trouble even after death ! But now that you’ve defeated me, you should hurry and seal me away. »

Asakawa Shin shook his head and gave Senju Hashirama a slight smile :

« Doesn’t Shodai-sama want to see the current Konoha ? Why be in such a hurry to leave ? Why not stay and play for a few more days.

Besides, I think if Senior Sister Tsunade saw you, she would definitely be very happy. »

« Tsunade ? »

Senju Hashirama was stunned, a hint of nostalgia flashing in his eyes. « Little Tsuna ? She’s actually your senior sister ? In that case... then I’ll stay for a few more days to keep that child company. »

After dealing with Senju Hashirama, Asakawa Shin’s gaze then turned to the unconscious Gaara.

This kid has opposed me repeatedly, how should I deal with him ?

However, when he walked up to Gaara, he was surprised to find two people already blocking his path—it was Gaara’s older sister and brother, Temari and Kankuro.

Even though the two were trembling with fear, they still stood firm facing Asakawa Shin, protecting their younger brother behind them.

Asakawa Shin scolded coldly : « Move aside. »

Temari bit her lower lip, her eyes slightly red and tears welling up, but she still shook her head stubbornly : « I won’t. »

Asakawa Shin was somewhat confused : « Why ? He doesn’t even treat you as siblings, does he ? »

« That doesn’t matter. »

Temari answered firmly : « I only know that I am his sister. I beg of you... please let him go. »

Asakawa Shin was stunned for a moment, then gave a light laugh, a hint of playfulness flashing in his eyes : « Interesting... he’s truly lucky to have a sister like you.

But if you think you can get me to let him go with just one sentence, aren’t you overestimating yourself ?

Let’s make a deal : your life in exchange for his ! »

Kankuro interjected : « You... you take my life instead ! Don’t hurt Temari. »

« Get lost, I’m not interested in men ! »

« ? »
