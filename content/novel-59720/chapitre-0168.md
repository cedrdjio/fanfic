« Tsk, finally finished. What a hassle... » Asakawa Shin’s lips curled slightly as he let out a long sigh of relief.

Beside him, Yugito finally snapped out of her daze. Covering her cheek, she muttered with a dazed look in her eyes, « I... I was kissed ? »

Feeling a trace of moisture still lingering on her fingertips, her pretty face instantly turned crimson. She suddenly turned to glare at Asakawa Shin, a mix of shame and anger swirling within her.

« You... you lecher ! »

« Why are you glaring at me ? » Asakawa Shin asked back with an innocent look, sounding perfectly justified. « Didn’t you say you wanted me to marry into your family ? What’s wrong with a kiss ? »

Seeing Asakawa Shin’s 'dead pig not afraid of boiling water' attitude, Yugito stamped her foot in anger and cursed through gritted teeth, « Shameless ! »

Before her voice could fade, she raised her hand and swung a slap toward him.

However, her palm was effortlessly caught by Asakawa Shin. Then, with a forceful pull, he drew her into his embrace and planted another kiss on her other cheek.

By the time Yugito reacted, Asakawa Shin had already let go, his figure vanishing instantly.

Only then did she realize with a shock that the surrounding scenery had completely changed... what appeared before her eyes was actually a desolate desert.

« This is... the Land of Wind ? This is bad... »

Yugito looked around in panic. « How did that brat suddenly make a move ? And he actually used his looks to bewitch me, how despicable ! »

At this moment, Asakawa Shin had returned to the Konoha battlefield, and that 'Little Kitten' who had given him quite a headache was nowhere to be seen.

Everyone on the battlefield had been secretly paying attention to the movements over here. Seeing this scene now, their expressions changed drastically.

« Lady Yugito’s Chakra fluctuations have disappeared ? Could something have happened to her ? »

« Not good ! Asakawa Shin has been freed. This is going to be a big problem ! »

« How can this be ? We assembled such a powerful force, yet we still failed ? »

« Damn it, Orochimaru really screwed us over ! Are rogue Ninjas these days really this loyal ? Is he sick in the head or something ? »

Asakawa Shin’s gaze was like lightning as he quickly scanned the battlefield. The high-level combatants were still fine, but the ordinary Ninjas had already suffered heavy casualties.

Although deaths and injuries are inevitable in war, witnessing this scene still caused a surge of discomfort in Asakawa Shin’s heart.

After all, he was the one who proposed starting this war, yet he was the one who dropped the ball due to an unexpected situation.

« I’m sorry, I won’t let your sacrifices be in vain ! »

With his mind made up, Asakawa Shin no longer hesitated and struck directly.

The next moment, a terrifyingly powerful Haoshoku Haki suddenly erupted, sweeping across the entire field like a raging tide.

The ordinary Ninjas below Kage-level stiffened, their eyes rolling back as they lost consciousness.

Even among the Jonin, only a few with strong willpower could barely resist this terrifying pressure, yet they still trembled all over, temporarily losing their ability to fight.

The once noisy battlefield instantly fell into a deathly silence. Only a few Kage-level experts remained standing, their faces filled with horror, none of them daring to make a move.

Danzo gave Orochimaru a vicious glare, gritted his teeth, and turned to flee.

This action quickly triggered a chain reaction, as other Kage-level experts also began to contemplate retreat.

But in the next moment, their bodies stiffened as they stopped in their tracks, staring into the distance in dumbfounded shock.

They saw Asakawa Shin draw his kusanagi sword and deliver a slash in the direction Danzo was fleeing.

In the next instant, an earth-shattering sword qi soared into the sky, reaching Danzo at an incredible speed.

Danzo’s pupils constricted as he activated Susanoo without hesitation.

However, the first-stage Susanoo was like paper, sliced open directly by the sword qi. The sword qi’s momentum remained unchecked, shredding Danzo into pieces.

« Nani ? ! »

This scene caused everyone to gasp in cold air.

That was the legendary Susanoo, the divine power of the Uchiha Clan that had been boasted about so loudly !

Even if it was only the first stage, it shouldn’t have been so fragile.

The only explanation was that the slash was simply too strong, far exceeding the defensive limits of Susanoo !

Space rippled, and Danzo’s figure tumbled out like a ghost, panting heavily, his face written with utter terror.

If he hadn’t decisively used Izanagi just now, he would likely have been turned into a pile of mashed meat, losing his life right then and there.

Asakawa Shin glanced at him casually. 'Including the pair of Mangekyo, he still has seven Sharingan left ?'

'In other words, in this short amount of time, he has already been killed four times by Itachi. What a waste ; he’s squandering such a powerful ability.'

The next moment, Asakawa Shin raised the kusanagi sword again and swung it, sending another massive sword qi hurtling toward Danzo.

Under Danzo’s horrified gaze, the sword qi once again easily shattered his Susanoo and then tore him to shreds.

Such a brutal scene immediately scared everyone out of their wits.

Especially Rasa ; knowing he could never take that strike, his eyes darted around. Taking advantage of Asakawa Shin dealing with Danzo, he hurriedly controlled Gold Dust, intending to escape through the air.

« Where do you think you’re going ? »

A voice suddenly echoed behind him, scaring him half to death.

He turned his head in terror to see Asakawa Shin standing behind him at some unknown point, looking at him with a half-smile.

Then, under Rasa’s terrified and desperate gaze, Asakawa Shin raised the kusanagi sword.

Rasa didn’t dare hesitate, immediately manipulating Gold Dust to form a defensive wall in front of him.

In the next instant, the slash flashed past. The wall constructed of heavy Gold Dust was sliced open like thin paper, the cut as smooth as a mirror without any resistance.

Immediately following, a bone-deep, gruesome wound exploded across Rasa’s chest, running diagonally from his left shoulder to his right abdomen.

He spat out a mouthful of blood toward the sky in disbelief, his eyes rolling back as he could no longer maintain his form, falling alongside the scattered Gold Dust.

It was as if a golden rain was falling from the sky ; as a Kazekage’s final performance, it was quite beautiful.

After delivering that strike, Asakawa Shin turned back with confidence, no longer paying attention to Rasa. He turned and delivered another slash toward a patch of empty ground in the distance.

While everyone was confused, Danzo’s figure appeared out of thin air just a moment before the sword qi arrived.

Without making a sound, Danzo was once again shredded by the sword qi, his face filled with resentment.

'Damn it ! This naturally evil Senju brat...'
