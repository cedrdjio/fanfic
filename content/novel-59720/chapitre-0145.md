# Chapitre 145 — Best Friends

The drawing results for the third round were quickly revealed : Kankuro from the Hidden Sand against Uzumaki Karin.

Asakawa Shin glanced at the dark, chubby guy and cautioned, « The opponent is a puppeteer, watch out for his poison. »

Uzumaki Karin was full of confidence. « Don’t worry, Lord Shin, I won’t be careless. »

Kankuro frowned as he watched Karin step onto the stage. Was she really a teammate of that monster ? It seemed this battle would be tricky.

With the referee’s command, the battle was triggered instantly.

Kankuro didn’t dare to be careless, using his full strength from the very beginning.

With a flick of his wrist, he violently pulled the puppet off his back and controlled it to charge toward Karin.

Facing the whistling puppet, Karin didn’t retreat but instead advanced. With a tap of her toes, she charged forward like an arrow released from a bow.

« You’re looking for death ! »

A cold light flashed in Kankuro’s eyes as his fingers shifted rapidly.

The puppet instantly exploded and disassembled in mid-air, with every separated part carrying a sharp blade.

The blades quickly deflected in the air, flickering with an eerie purple glow, and stabbed toward Karin with lightning speed.

Karin gave a low shout : « Adamantine Sealing Chains ! »

In an instant, several chains burst out from behind her, dancing wildly in the air like spirit snakes. They either parried or entangled, completely dismantling Kankuro’s puppet offensive.

But that wasn’t all. Karin twisted her wrist, and the Adamantine Sealing Chains instantly wrapped around her right arm, transforming into a heavy iron hammer. Carrying a thousand pounds of force, she smashed it ruthlessly into the bound puppet remains.

With a loud « boom, » the hard puppet material was completely pulverized under this monstrous strength. Wood chips flew everywhere, scattering across the ground.

« Nani ? » Kankuro’s pupils shrank as he exclaimed in shock : « Is this monstrous strength ? That Monstrous Strength Punch of Tsunade’s ? »

How could Kankuro not be terrified ?

During the war between the Hidden Sand and Konoha years ago, Tsunade’s title as one of the Legendary Sannin was a nightmare for puppeteers.

Not only could she easily neutralize the potent poisons the Hidden Sand was proud of, but her mountain-shattering Monstrous Strength Punch was the natural nemesis of puppeteers. She had once been listed as the highest threat by the Hidden Sand Village.

And now, Kankuro had encountered a remarkably similar situation. It sent shivers down his spine, and cold sweat instantly soaked his forehead like rain.

The situation had clearly worsened to the extreme for him.

Just as he feared, the battle became a completely one-sided suppression.

No matter how he changed the puppet’s mechanisms and traps, or released poison mist and hidden weapons, Karin could always easily neutralize them with her amazing speed and chain defense.

On the contrary, those fists imbued with monstrous strength were heavy and powerful with every swing, easily smashing his puppets to pieces.

Kankuro knew very well that there was no chance of winning if the battle continued ; he could only watch helplessly as his puppets were destroyed.

After weighing the pros and cons, he decisively raised his hand to surrender, choosing to cut his losses in time.

As the referee announced the victory, Karin couldn’t hide her joy. She skipped back to Asakawa Shin’s side, seeking praise and credit : « Lord Shin, I won ! »

Asakawa Shin patted her head with satisfaction, thinking to himself that his efforts over the years hadn’t been in vain.

From the beginning, he had tailored this combat system for Karin, strictly training her in Medical Ninjutsu and fine Chakra control since childhood.

Because her foundation was so well-laid, she was able to master the Monstrous Strength Technique in such a short time.

Now, her attainments in Medical Ninjutsu had also progressed rapidly ; she was already a highly competent teammate.

After a short break, the fourth match officially began.

The combatants were Sakura Haruno from Team 7 and her best friend, Ino Yamanaka.

The two looked at each other from across the crowd, both their eyes burning with high fighting spirit. It was as if sparks flew where their gazes met.

Upon reaching the high platform, Sakura looked at the sword at Ino’s waist, a hint of surprise flashing in her eyes : « Ino, why are you carrying a sword ? »

Ino tilted her chin up and began to show off proudly, « Eh ? Sakura, how did you know Shin gave me a kusanagi sword ?

This is a very precious ninja tool, and it’s a matching pair with the one in Shin’s hand~

By the way, I wonder what special gift Sasuke gave you ? »

« Ku... kusanagi sword ? » Sakura felt like she had been struck by lightning. she looked at Ino in disbelief. Even though the match hadn’t started yet, she had already fallen into a complete disadvantage.

A moment later, she snapped back to her senses, forcing a mocking smile : « Hmph, so what if you have a kusanagi sword ? Do you even know how to use it, Ino ? »

To her surprise, Ino’s smile grew even wider, her eyes full of pride she couldn’t hide : « Sakura, you underestimate me too much. Over these years, training with Shin, I’ve practiced swordsmanship for a long time. Today is the perfect time to test my moves on you. »

« Swordsmanship ? You actually know swordsmanship ? » Sakura’s face instantly became extremely ugly.

'Fine, fine, so you were waiting for me here ! Secretly practicing the sword and picking me, your best friend, as your first opponent—that’s just too much !'

Thinking this, she also focused with all her might, responding cautiously.

After all, the name of the kusanagi sword was legendary, and as a top student, she knew the terror of this famous blade better than anyone.

The battle was triggered instantly.

Ino’s expression turned solemn as she flicked her wrist and drew the kusanagi sword. The sharp blade reflected a cold, grim light under the spotlights, and the sharp aura coming from it was chilling.

Sakura subconsciously swallowed her saliva and pulled a pathetic Kunai from her ninja tool bag, standing ready for the confrontation.

« Hmph ! » Ino gave a disdainful sneer and actually took the initiative, charging at Sakura with a tap of her toes.

Sakura gritted her teeth inwardly, unwilling to lose in terms of momentum, and charged forward while gripping her Kunai tightly.

The two soon met head-on. Ino raised her sharp sword and slashed down.

Sakura quickly swung her Kunai, attempting to parry.

However, a scene appeared that left Sakura terrified.

The kusanagi sword was like cutting through tofu, effortless. Under Ino’s strength, it instantly sliced more than halfway through the Kunai.

« This kusanagi sword is actually this sharp ! »

A flash of fear crossed Sakura’s eyes. Seeing Ino continue to apply downward pressure, her pupils shrank, and she decisively jumped back to create distance.

Fortunately, Sakura retreated in time. When she looked at the Kunai in her hand again, she saw it was nearly broken in two.

Seeing this, Ino said triumphantly, « Sakura, you’d better surrender quickly. I haven’t even used my strength yet, and you’re already about to fall~ »

Sakura gritted her teeth inwardly, 'What is that annoying Ino-pig so proud of ? She’s just relying on the advantage of a divine weapon ! I... I’m not jealous at all.'

However, facing Ino, whose momentum was at its peak, she seemed somewhat helpless.

As a taijutsu-type Medical Ninja, her fighting style was perfectly countered by Ino’s exquisite swordsmanship.

Although she had received guidance from Lady Tsunade for a period of time, she had not yet mastered high-level taijutsu like the Monstrous Strength Punch, and for a moment, she fell into a passive position.

« In that case, I can only use that move. »

As the thought flashed by, her pupils suddenly lost focus. She had skillfully switched to her Inner Persona.

In the next instant, she charged toward Ino like an arrow from a bow, her speed significantly faster than before.

Ino was startled and hurriedly raised her sword to parry.

However, facing the thrusting sword tip, Sakura’s body swayed lightly like a piece of paper, perfectly avoiding the attack. She followed up with a heavy whip kick directed straight at Ino’s abdomen.

Ino was caught off guard and could only raise her knee to block, but she was still sent flying by that powerful whip kick.

On the high platform, Asakawa Shin raised an eyebrow, his gaze turning toward Sakura in surprise :

'Was that Paper Art just now ? To perfectly fuse the beastly intuition of the Inner Persona with Paper Art, turning danger into safety while counterattacking ? Interesting.'

Ino, who had been pushed back, also stopped being frivolous, her expression becoming solemn and serious.

After Asakawa Shin’s careful training, her swordsmanship was no longer what it used to be ; her moves were sharp, efficient, and full of edge.

However, Sakura on the other side was by no means ordinary.

After fusing her Inner Persona with Paper Art, she not only greatly improved her evasion ability but also cleverly used the flexibility of Paper Art to effectively reduce the burden the Inner Persona placed on her body.

For a time, the two girls fought to a stalemate, the battle locked in a stalemate.

A sharp light flashed in Ino’s eyes. She decisively gave up on a head-on clash and instead adopted a hit-and-run strategy, constantly inducing Sakura to use movements that exceeded the limits of the human body.

This tactic indeed worked. Soon, Sakura’s movements lost their initial ferocity, and her joints showed a bit of stiffness as they moved.

After a few more moments, Ino saw the timing was right and decisively seized an opening, striking Sakura’s abdomen hard with the spine of her sword, sending her flying.

Sakura rolled messily several times before collapsing on the ground. Her chest heaved violently as she gasped for air, and her unfocused pupils slowly regained their focus.

« Was I still a little short in the end ? I’ll count this as your win, Ino-pig. »

She gave a helpless, bitter smile and then raised her hand to the referee : « I surrender. »

Ino hurried forward to help her up, offering comfort : « You’re so amazing, Sakura. To think you were hiding a trump card like that. If you could have stayed conscious, I’m afraid I would have been the one to lose. »

« Don’t get cocky. » Sakura brushed the dust off her clothes, curling her lip with feigned ease, « It was just your good luck. Once I’ve completely mastered this move, the winner next time will definitely be me. »

A hint of craftiness flashed in Ino’s eyes, « Ah, right, right, right. »
