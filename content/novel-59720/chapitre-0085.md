# Chapitre 85 — Hot Spring Night Talk

« Itachi, Itachi~ »

Even though Asakawa Shin shouted until he was hoarse, Uchiha Itachi remained completely indifferent, watching helplessly as Asakawa Shin was dragged away.

Before long, the group arrived at the hot spring. Before Asakawa Shin could even react, Tsunade stripped him bare and tossed him into the pool.

Tsunade didn’t waste any time either ; she quickly shed her clothes and jumped in with a « splash, » like a deep-sea bomb, sending water spraying everywhere.

She wiped the water from her face and let out a long, satisfied sigh.

Shizune shook her head helplessly and got up to prepare wine and snacks. Katsuyu seemed uninterested in the hot spring and crawled off to play on her own. Only Yūgao stood there blankly, feeling a bit at a loss.

Seeing this, Tsunade couldn’t help but feel curious : « Why aren’t you coming down ? We’re all women here, no need to be shy, right ? »

Yūgao said hesitatingly : « But I still have Lord Shin’s autograph on my arm. If I soak in the hot spring, it will be ruined... »

Asakawa Shin had completely lost his restraint by now. He waved his hand carelessly at Yūgao with a casual tone :

« It’s fine, if it washes off, I’ll just sign it for you again. Sign wherever you want. Hurry up and get down here, and help me scrub my back while you’re at it. »

Upon hearing this, Yūgao’s eyes lit up, and she asked with pleasant surprise : « Really ? Anywhere is fine ? Then I’m coming down right now ! »

As soon as she finished speaking, Yūgao couldn’t wait to shed her clothes and dove into the water with a standard diving posture.

Not long after, she popped her head up right beside Asakawa Shin, pulled him into her arms, and said coquettishly : « I knew Lord Shin was the best~ »

Yūgao was indeed a master of swordsmanship ; her body was perfectly proportioned, with every curve in the right place and not a single ounce of excess fat.

Asakawa Shin lay comfortably in her warm, soft embrace, enjoying this exclusive service, feeling secretly smug in his heart :

'As expected of Yūgao. My decision to choose her as my personal bodyguard was truly wise.'

Little did he know, Yūgao was already over the moon inside, her thoughts racing : 'Oh, oh, oh ! Is this the legendary top-tier fan service ?

Not only do I get to soak in the hot spring while on duty, but I also get to be this close to my idol. It’s simply heaven !

Mmm... Lord Shin’s skin is so smooth, it feels absolutely amazing.'

Perhaps because her « devouring » expression was too obvious, Tsunade couldn’t help but lean in, a mischievous smile on her face as she teased : « Little Shin, come here, let your senior sister check if you’ve grown up yet~ »

Before Tsunade even finished speaking, her hands were already like iron pincers, gripping Asakawa Shin’s waist. With a sudden burst of strength, she lifted him right out of the water and scrutinized him up and down without any restraint.

« Hahaha, you’re not capable at all, are you ? You really are just a kid, hahahaha. »

Asakawa Shin’s face turned beet red with embarrassment. He scrambled to break free and fled behind Yūgao, avoiding Tsunade’s teasing gaze.

The more he thought about it, the angrier he got. He couldn’t help but peek his head out and roar angrily at Tsunade :

« Don’t look down on me ! Thirty years east of the river, thirty years west of the river, don’t bully a young man for being poor ! »

Tsunade laughed until tears welled up in the corners of her eyes. Seeing that Asakawa Shin was genuinely angry, she forced back her laughter, toned it down a bit, and waved at him :

« Alright, alright, it’s senior sister’s fault. Stop hiding, come here, senior sister promises not to make fun of you anymore. »

Asakawa Shin hesitated for a moment but ran back, glaring at Tsunade with full vigilance.

Tsunade just smiled, made no further moves, and together with Yūgao, carefully began to scrub his body.

Only then did Asakawa Shin slowly relax and snuggle into her arms with peace of mind.

Looking at his well-behaved appearance, Tsunade’s gaze inadvertently fell on the necklace on his chest, and her thoughts were instantly pulled back to the past.

Back then, a six or seven-year-old Nawaki also loved to snuggle in her arms like this, wearing that same expression.

Recalling that small figure, the sharpness in Tsunade’s eyes gradually softened, and her expression became gentler.

She gently stroked Asakawa Shin’s little face, as if she could see the figures of Nawaki and Kato Dan in him.

This made her feel a bit dazed ; for a moment, she couldn’t distinguish between illusion and reality. « Shin, you must become the Hokage. »

Asakawa Shin seemed to sense something, a glimmer in his eyes. He immediately looked up and promised firmly :

« Don’t worry, senior sister. Not only will I become the Hokage, but I will also make the name of the Senju resound throughout the entire Ninja World once again ! »

« The Senju, you say ? »

Tsunade murmured softly. Her gaze intertwined with Asakawa Shin’s firm eyes, and the spirit contained within them made her break into a smile.

She gently cupped Asakawa Shin’s little face, leaned down, and planted a light kiss on his forehead :

« Then it’s a promise. A real man must keep his word. »

« Hmph, that goes without saying. »

...

Just then, Shizune pushed a small cart over, placed an array of delicious food and fine wine on the platform at the edge of the shallow water, and called out to them :

« Lady Tsunade, everyone, come over and have something to eat. »

Tsunade’s eyes lit up. She had long been parched, and now, smelling the rich aroma of the wine, her cravings were fully aroused.

She hurriedly hoisted Asakawa Shin onto her shoulder and rushed toward the shallow bank.

Asakawa Shin protested indignantly : « Hey, senior sister, what are you doing again ! Put me down, I can walk myself ! »

On the bank, Shizune could only see Tsunade’s smug smile and a round little butt flailing in the air, causing her to double over with laughter.

'Lady Tsunade really is something, how can she bully Lord Shin like that ? But this isn’t bad either ; it’s been a long time since I’ve seen Lady Tsunade look this happy.

Come to think of it, I’m a bit tired after working all day. I might as well go in and soak too, just the thing to relieve fatigue.'

Thinking this, Shizune shed her clothes and carefully stepped into the hot spring water.

A playful glint flashed in Tsunade’s eyes. She suddenly moved closer, grabbed Shizune’s calf, and pulled hard, flipping her right into the deep pool.

Only Shizune’s panicked scream and Tsunade’s triumphant laughter remained in the air.

« Lady Tsunade, really, you’re teasing me again. »

Shizune poked her head out of the water, her wet hair sticking to her cheeks. Although her tone carried a hint of complaint, her expression was more like she was acting spoiled.

Tsunade waved her hand dismissively : « Who told you to dawdle ? Isn’t that what I’m here for, to give you a hand ? »

She shook the wine flask in her hand and called out heartily, « Come over here, let’s have a drink together ! Glug, glug, glug... »

In the blink of an eye, the four of them gathered in the shallow area, sitting in a circle, clinking glasses, and having a lively time.

Asakawa Shin looked around ; everywhere he looked was just a sea of pale skin. He said somewhat unnaturally : « Aren’t... aren’t you all embarrassed ? »

Tsunade glanced at him disdainfully and scoffed : « Embarrassed in front of whom ? You ? Hahahaha~ »

Before she finished, the women were already laughing together.

Hearing the jarring laughter, Asakawa Shin couldn’t help but grit his teeth and secretly swear to himself : 'You three just wait. I will definitely repay today’s humiliation a hundredfold in the future !'

After the late-night banquet, the three women were flushed from drinking quite a bit of wine, and with the steam of the hot spring, they were now slightly tipsy.

They chatted lazily while soaking in the hot spring, passing Asakawa Shin around like a doll. Being tossed around by them, Asakawa Shin was now dizzy and a bit muddled himself.

He simply closed his eyes and let them do as they pleased, while secretly pondering in his heart :

'Mmm, this body is so resilient, no need to think, it must be Yūgao.

Hmm, this body is so soft, with long and nimble fingers, it must be Shizune without a doubt.

Ah this, this one is floating in the water, no need to ask, it must be Tsunade.

How long are they going to soak for ? I’m getting a bit sleepy.

Mmm... Senior sister is holding me quite comfortably, maybe I should just sleep here tonight.'
