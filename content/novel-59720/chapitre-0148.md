# Chapitre 148 — A Place of Great Talent and Remarkable Spirit

The drawing results for the ninth match were finally revealed : it was Rock Lee against Gaara.

As soon as the names were announced, everyone gasped, casting looks of awe and fear toward Gaara.

The memory of his previous fierce battle was still fresh in everyone’s minds ; no one dared to underestimate him in the slightest.

Sensing the heavy atmosphere, Tenten couldn’t help but look at Rock Lee with worry.

Even the usually hot-blooded Might Guy hesitated for a rare moment, advising in a low voice :

« Lee, regarding this match... should we just withdraw ? The danger of your opponent is beyond imagination ; he’s not someone you can contend with right now. »

Hearing this, Lee shook his head without hesitation, his eyes shining with an unprecedented light of determination :

« Guy-sensei, it’s precisely because the opponent is strong enough that I feel even more excited !

If I choose to back down whenever I face a strong enemy, then what was the point of all my years of hard training and persistence ?

No matter who the opponent is, I will never give up easily ! »

After hearing this, Guy was instantly moved to tears, which streamed down his face like thick noodles.

He shouted excitedly, « Lee, the fire of your youth is burning so brightly ! As your teacher, I am proud of you ! »

Lee was also deeply affected ; the master and disciple were on the same wavelength and immediately hugged each other, weeping loudly.

Everyone’s eyes focused on them, making Tenten feel incredibly embarrassed. That idiot Neji had been carried away on a stretcher, so there was no one to share the pressure with her, making her toes curl in embarrassment.

On the stage, Gaara frowned at the sight, his eyes showing clear disgust.

« Truly the type I hate the most. In a moment, I’ll simply crush him into mincemeat. »

Under the repeated urging of the referee, Lee finally stopped crying and leaped onto the martial arts stage.

He skillfully switched his expression, his face beaming with an eager smile as he introduced himself loudly to Gaara : « Hello ! My name is Rock Lee ! »

However, before he could finish, he was coldly interrupted by Gaara : « That sort of thing doesn’t matter. Let’s just get started. I can’t wait to crush you. »

Lee didn’t get angry upon hearing this ; instead, he clenched his fists excitedly. 'Is this guy also looking forward to fighting me ? That’s great !'

With the referee’s command, the match officially began.

Lee exerted force through his feet, his figure instantly turning into an afterimage as he dashed toward Gaara.

Gaara’s pupils shrank slightly, and he was greatly shocked : 'So fast ! Are all the guys from Konoha Village this fast ? However, he’s still within my reaction range !'

In a flash, he manipulated the sand by his side, quickly condensing it into a shield on his left.

At almost the same instant, Lee’s figure appeared like a ghost, and a side kick carrying a powerful wind slammed hard against the sand shield, producing a dull thud.

Having failed to land the hit, Lee didn’t linger ; his body flashed strangely in the air and vanished once again.

The next moment, the sound of breaking air came from above as another heavy flying kick descended from the sky.

However, a clump of sand had already risen there, constructing a solid line of defense that steadily blocked the strike.

Just as he showed a look of surprise, the clump of sand beside him suddenly surged like a living thing, winding toward him to entwine him.

Lee’s expression remained unchanged as he pushed off the ground, performing several consecutive backflips to quickly widen the distance.

After landing, he stared solemnly at Gaara and said deeply, « I see, so you’re a Ninja who manipulates sand ? Your reaction speed is actually this amazing. It seems I must show some of my true skill as well. »

After saying that, under Gaara’s bewildered gaze, he reached out and unfastened the weighted gear on his body.

He casually tossed the weights from his wrists and ankles to the side ; accompanied by several booming thuds, the hard ground was actually smashed into several large craters.

Gaara was greatly shocked. 'This guy... was actually wearing such heavy weights while fighting me just now ? And he could still maintain that speed... this is going to be a bit troublesome.'

Just as he thought, once Lee had shed his heavy burden, his speed instantly broke past its limits.

Before Gaara could react, a figure abruptly appeared in front of him, and a whip kick came swinging fiercely.

Fortunately, the Shield of Sand surged on its own, perilously constructing a defense to block the strike.

Seeing this, a flash of surprise crossed Lee’s eyes, followed by a determined curve at the corner of his mouth : « Oh ? You can still block it ? Then I’ll become so fast you can’t defend, and so strong you can’t block ! »

In the next instant, Rock Lee’s figure completely turned into a blurry afterimage, launching an offensive around Gaara at an incredible high speed.

Because the speed was so fast, the naked eye simply couldn’t track his trajectory. For a moment, Gaara completely lost his target, and all that remained in his ears were the dull 'bang bang' sounds of the Shield of Sand being repeatedly struck ; it was only a matter of time before the defense was breached.

After the initial shock, Gaara quickly calmed down.

He simply stopped paying attention to those elusive attacks and instead activated the gourd on his back.

« Sand Waterfall Flow ! »

Under his control, high-density quicksand poured out from the mouth of the gourd, and a nauseating smell of blood filled the air.

Those grains of sand spread frantically, eroding the ground beneath their feet, transforming the entire martial arts stage into a desolate desert in an instant.

Then, this desert seemed to come alive, kicking up waves of sand that swept toward Lee.

Quicksand also flew through the air, as if a sandstorm had started.

Lee retreated hurriedly, using the surrounding walls and ceiling to constantly move around, dodging this amazing attack.

Once the sand waves subsided, he landed back on the ground with a solemn expression.

By now, the martial arts stage was completely covered in sand, having turned entirely into Gaara’s home field.

The sand dust floating in the air also severely hindered Lee’s movements, causing his movement speed to suddenly slow down.

Using the perception provided by the sand grains, Gaara accurately captured the trajectory of every one of Lee’s flashes ; for a time, Lee’s sharp offensive was completely seen through, leaving him nowhere to hide.

Lee’s thick eyebrows furrowed as he quickly leaped back to create distance. « This guy is indeed a tough character who can go head-to-head with Shin ; he’s really troublesome ! In that case, I can’t hold back anymore either ! »

After speaking, he suddenly turned his head to look at Might Guy on the high platform, and their eyes met.

Might Guy had a solemn expression and nodded heavily, approving of his proposal.

Having received permission, Lee no longer hesitated and assumed the unique starting stance of the Eight Gates.

« Eight Gates, Fourth Gate of Injury, Open ! »

« Boom ! »

A violent surge of green Chakra air erupted from within his body, sweeping outward like a shockwave, instantly blowing the circling sand dust into a chaotic mess.

Lee’s eyes widened in anger as his speed once again broke past its limits, his entire being turning into a bolt of green lightning as he dashed toward Gaara.

Along with his charge, the large amount of sand accumulated on the path was pushed to the sides by the powerful airflow ; even the so-called 'Absolute Defense' of the Sand Shield couldn’t react in time.

In the next moment, a heavy punch wrapped in green Chakra airflow landed solidly on Gaara’s face.

Gaara clearly hadn’t expected his defense to be breached so easily ; he was sent flying back completely defenseless, crashing into the sandy ground and kicking up a large cloud of dust.

When the dust cleared, everyone was shocked to find that his body’s surface was covered in cracks like porcelain, which then turned into sand and fell off ; it turned out to be a layer of Sand Armor.

After the armor peeled off, Temari was shocked to see blood seeping from the corner of Gaara’s mouth and couldn’t help but cry out, « Gaara is actually injured ! »

Kankuro’s expression suddenly became solemn as he gave Lee a sympathetic glance : « This guy has really brought out the monster now. »

Before he could finish, Gaara had already begun to laugh neurotically : « Pain ? It’s been so long... for me to taste this feeling again. Konoha really is a place of great talent and remarkable spirit...

To thank you, I’m going to tear you to pieces and merge you with my sand forever ! »

In the next moment, a massive amount of sand swept toward Lee like crashing waves.

Lee’s brow furrowed as his figure flashed and turned rapidly, searching for the enemy’s weakness while dodging this wide-range offensive.

However, Gaara’s Chakra seemed infinite, as he constantly manipulated the sand waves to compress Lee’s dodging space, forcing him into a tight spot.

At the critical moment, Lee roared : « Eight Gates, Fifth Gate of Closing, Open ! »

The Chakra storm around him suddenly surged, his speed and power breaking past their limits once more. He actually wove through the gaps in the sand waves with ease, lunging straight at Gaara with the force of thunder.

Under Gaara’s horrified gaze, Lee’s foot pierced through the sand shield and kicked heavily into his chest, sending him flying like a cannonball.

Gaara scrambled up pathetically, the Sand Armor on his chest shattered again, and blood gushed from his mouth.

He gritted his teeth and growled in a low voice : « You... you green-skinned frog, I will never let you off ! »

Under the terrified gaze of the onlookers, crimson Chakra slowly covered his body like a cloak, and a tail gently swayed behind him.

His face was distorted, his eyes flashing with a bloodthirsty light. « Next, I’m going to tear you to pieces ! »
