# Chapitre 27 — Silk

Sunday, morning.

Asakawa Shin’s father returned.

A week ago, Asakawa Kenji received an emergency mission personally issued by Hokage-sama, which was delivered directly to his border camp via a summoning creature. Even the Anbu squad patrolling the border abandoned their mission just to escort him back to Konoha.

After a week of long-distance travel, Asakawa Kenji finally returned to the village, though he was still somewhat questioning reality at this moment.

« How could there be such a strange mission requirement ? Am I really under a genjutsu ? That makes no sense. »

Never mind, I’m already here. Even if it really is a genjutsu, if I can’t resist it, I might as well enjoy it.

Outside the door, Hanako and Shin had been waiting for a long time. It wasn’t until the Anbu came to inform them this morning that they knew Asakawa Kenji was coming home today.

Regular border rotation garrison missions usually lasted two to three years. Since Asakawa Kenji had only been gone for a little over a year, it was clear that Hokage-sama had pulled some strings.

« He’s here, he’s here ! Look, Mom, Dad is back ! »

Asakawa Shin excitedly shook Hanako’s arm, pointing towards the end of the street.

Having lived two lives, this was his first time seeing the creature known as 'father'. Kenji had left for his mission before his past-life memories awakened, so Shin had only seen Kenji in his memories, which was why he felt quite excited.

Clearly, the person at the end of the street had also spotted them. He shouted something at them at the top of his lungs, then spread his arms wide and sprinted toward them.

Shin also excitedly spread his arms and hurried to meet him. « Dad~ »

Who would have thought that only when they got closer did he clearly hear that Kenji was shouting Hanako’s name. He brushed right past him and lunged toward Hanako without looking back.

« My dear, I’ve missed you so much ! » Asakawa Kenji took a swift step forward and pulled Hanako into his embrace, a satisfied smile on his face, muttering something about 'Hanako energy recharging'.

Hanako rolled her eyes at him, then buried her head in Kenji’s chest, enjoying this long-lost warmth.

Only Shin was left standing on the side, dazed, looking quite redundant.

After a long while, perhaps because the 'Hanako energy' was fully recharged, Kenji finally noticed Asakawa Shin on the side.

He stepped forward and gently patted Shin’s head. « It feels really good—wait, no, Shin has grown so tall ! Dad missed you so much. »

'Missed me ? I couldn’t tell. I bet I was just a free gift that came with buying ninja tools, right ?'

Shin rolled his eyes at him without a word, grabbed Hanako’s hand, and walked into the house without looking back. The war known as the Battle for the Reclamation of Hanako had officially begun !

Only Kenji was left alone. He stood foolishly at the doorway, looking a bit out of place, but he didn’t care at all. After a brief moment of stunned silence, he beamed and hurriedly chased after them.

After a round of laughter and play, the whole family agreed to go out next week and take photos to commemorate it. Only then did Shin forgive Kenji.

Leaving the two of them at home, Shin went out to take care of business. He was very busy, unlike these two useless adults.

...

Hokage Office.

Like he was presenting a treasure, Shin handed over a piece of silk the size of a palm. Hiruzen Sarutobi reached out to take it, feeling its smooth and comfortable texture, and couldn’t help but marvel.

« Shin, is this the silk you mentioned ? It is indeed impressive. The texture is far superior to cotton, linen, or wool fabrics, and the color is surprisingly vibrant ! You want to do business with this, and you want me to have a share ? »

« That’s right, Teacher. Silk is lightweight, form-fitting, soft, smooth, breathable, brilliantly colored, lustrous, and elegantly noble. Its advantages are simply countless, and it will definitely be very popular. »

« The feudal lords and nobles will absolutely not be able to resist the charm of silk. Once they experience it, they will never be able to endure those cotton and linen fabrics again. »

« Of course, there are also disadvantages : it is expensive, prone to wrinkling, clings to the body easily, isn’t durable enough, and fades relatively quickly ! However, the Aburame clan is actively experimenting, and I believe the shortcomings of silk will be greatly improved in the future. »

« Although these silks have some minor flaws now, for the feudal lords and nobles, they might not necessarily be disadvantages. »

« What nobles pursue is having what others don’t ; only then can they prove their superiority. The more impractical something is, the more they chase after it. Moreover, silk itself is already so excellent ; I deduce that silk will definitely sweep across the entire Ninja World rapidly. »

Asakawa Shin was full of confidence. When he watched anime in his past life, he noticed what kind of things the feudal lords and ministers of the Five Great Nations wore. If they hadn’t been introduced as feudal lords, he would have thought they were just merchants from somewhere.

This will give those ignorant natives of the Ninja World a bit of dimensionality reduction strike, letting them know what is meant by 'Embroidered silk clothes shining in late spring, golden peacocks and silver unicorns knitted in.'

This is what you call a top-tier luxury item.

« Teacher, the Insect Controlling Secret Technique of the Aburame clan is just too perfect for raising silkworms. I just went to take a look ; in just one night, they actually made the number of silkworms multiply several times over. And they are still experimenting in batches, using Chakra to change the habits of the silkworms and even the quality of the silk. »

« It’s just that they are worried that with large-scale farming later on, there won’t be enough mulberry leaves to eat. But mulberry leaves, right ? You know what I mean, Teacher. »

Hearing this, Hiruzen Sarutobi was deeply moved. This was simply a business with a huge return on investment ; he could make money just by lying down.

He squinted his eyes and said, appearing indifferent : « Then how do you plan to split the profits ? »

« Teacher, I am the initiator of the project. All the processes, techniques, and planning involved were completed by me alone. Furthermore, I will need to provide the mulberry trees needed for raising the silkworms later on. I’ll take 30%, that’s not too much, right ? »

Hiruzen Sarutobi nodded : « 30% is not much ; it’s actually a bit low. »

Asakawa Shin shook his head : « Teacher, what I pursue is win-win cooperation, not temporary gains or losses. »

« The Aburame clan is responsible for using the Insect Controlling Secret Technique to raise silkworms and reel silk, as well as improving the habits of the silkworms so that they can produce more, tougher, more beautiful, and higher-quality silk. Moreover, they are my first partner, so I want to give them a 30% share. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi frowned : « Wouldn’t 30% be a bit high ? It’s just raising silkworms ; if an ordinary person got familiar with it, couldn’t they do it too ? »

Asakawa Shin shook his head again : « Ordinary people can indeed do it, but it’s too slow, and it would be affected by seasons and weather. »

« Silk will definitely be in short supply. The main thing restricting our production capacity will be the silk itself. With the Aburame clan and me joining forces, no one in the entire Ninja World will be able to compete with us on the supply chain. »

« Even if someone copies our silk in the future, we can crush them in a price war. Moreover, the silk improved by the Aburame clan’s secret technique will definitely be of the highest quality in the Ninja World, which will allow us to maintain an absolute advantage in competition. »

« As for what comes next, it’s weaving and dyeing. I plan to set aside a 30% share for various Konoha clans to raise funds and build factories, and subsequent profit sharing will be based on the contribution ratio. That way, no one can say I didn’t include them. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi calculated and said : « Then doesn’t that only leave 10% for me, your teacher ? »

Asakawa Shin rolled his eyes at him and said : « Teacher, isn’t it enough that you can get 10% in equity just by lending your name ? Besides, you can raise funds under the name of the Sarutobi clan ; how much you can get then depends on yourself. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi’s expression improved upon hearing this. That’s right, who said the Hokage couldn’t invest ? No, he had to secure a larger share this time. He would go back and count the clan’s assets after work.

At the same time, the news that Asakawa Shin, the Hokage, and the Aburame clan were establishing the Konoha Silk Group to jointly enter the textile industry spread throughout all the major ninja clans, and that they would offer 30% in equity to raise funds for building factories.

The attitudes of the various major ninja clans toward this were vastly different ; some were dismissive, some scoffed, but more were flocking to it.
