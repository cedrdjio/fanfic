# Chapitre 161 — Chidori

« That idiot Naruto really did lose after all. »

Sasuke watched the orange figure being carried away on a stretcher and couldn’t help but laugh wildly, his laughter filled with delight.

However, this smug laughter soon came to an abrupt halt.

Gaara was staring at him intensely with eyes full of cruelty and murderous intent.

And the opponent for the next battle was none other than the two of them.

As they say, smiles don’t disappear ; they only transfer.

A sick, twisted smile now spread across Gaara’s face.

Sasuke snorted coldly, suppressing the turbulent emotions in his heart and forcing himself to calm down.

At the referee’s command, the two slowly walked into the arena amidst the gaze of the crowd.

The audience stands immediately erupted into discussion.

« That must be the genius of the Uchiha Clan, Uchiha Sasuke, right ? He really is quite the talent. »

« The other one is just as impressive. That is the son of Kazekage Rasa, the genius of the Hidden Sand Village, known as 'Sand Waterfall' Gaara. »

« This battle is bound to be a fierce fight between dragons and tigers ; I’m really looking forward to it ! »

Gaara turned a deaf ear to the surrounding chatter, standing coldly in the arena, waiting only for the referee’s command to tear the kid before him into pieces.

Sasuke clearly felt some pressure, his expression becoming solemn and serious.

Once both were ready, the referee immediately announced the start of the match.

Gaara did not hesitate, abruptly activating the gourd on his back.

High-density sand immediately poured out like a torrent, rushing straight toward Uchiha Sasuke.

Sasuke’s gaze sharpened, and he leaped backward repeatedly, dodging it.

Facing the relentless pursuit of the sand, he moved with agility, constantly maneuvering and waiting for an opportunity to find a weakness in Gaara.

Seeing that the sand pouring from the gourd was running low, a flash of brilliance crossed his eyes, and his hands quickly formed seals.

In an instant, his right hand condensed a brilliant cluster of lightning, accompanied by the crisp chirping of birds.

It was none other than the A-rank Lightning Release Ninjutsu—Chidori.

Immediately, he decisively activated his Three-Tomoe Sharingan, combined with the physical activation from Lightning Release Chakra, plus Soru from Rokushiki.

Combining the three, his speed increased explosively.

With a flash, he was already closing in on Gaara.

Gaara was terrified and hurriedly manipulated the sand to condense into a Shield of Sand, blocking firmly in front of him.

Sasuke showed no intention of retreating ; the Chidori in his hand was like an unbreakable spear, thrusting fiercely at Gaara.

The two collided violently, as if a red-hot blade cutting into butter, and the Chidori pierced through the defense of the Shield of Sand with overwhelming force.

Fortunately, the Shield of Sand was not completely useless ; at the critical moment, it blocked Sasuke’s main body, allowing only the arm carrying the deadly lightning to pass through.

Gaara was hit squarely by the Chidori, the entire impact hitting him like a thunderbolt.

If not for his desperate dodge, narrowly missing his heart, he would likely have been done for by now.

Even so, the sharp lightning tore his flesh and made his whole body twitch violently.

The electric current roasted him to a crisp, and a smell of burnt hair immediately filled the air.

A fierce light flashed in Gaara’s eyes. Driven by a frantic surge of Chakra, the Shield of Sand that Sasuke had pierced suddenly contracted inward, attempting to trap Sasuke’s arm.

At the same time, the sand clinging to Sasuke’s body suddenly deformed, and countless sharp sand spikes extended out, aiming directly at Sasuke’s vitals, intending to pierce him on the spot.

Sasuke’s pupils constricted, and he hurriedly used Soru to leap backward, narrowly avoiding the attack.

After standing steady, he arrogantly tossed his hair, his tone full of self-satisfaction : « It’s useless. In front of this pair of Sharingan, all your attack intentions have nowhere to hide. »

Gaara turned a deaf ear to this, simply lowering his gaze to look at his left shoulder, which had been pierced by the Chidori, and a neurotic, cold smile curled at the corner of his mouth.

« Hahaha, once again... to think I’ve been injured in Konoha once again ? This is wonderful. »

He tried to move his left arm, but the entire limb seemed disconnected, unable to lift even an inch.

As he spoke, a thick black smoke rose from his mouth, causing his body to shudder, and his expression became even more manic.

« Damn it, I’m going to kill you all ! »

Following this hysterical roar, crimson Chakra slowly crawled over his entire body like magma, and Gaara did not hesitate to activate the Tailed Beast Cloak.

Even more shocking was that the gruesome wound on his left shoulder, pierced by the Chidori, was rapidly wriggling and healing at a speed visible to the naked eye.

If no one stopped him, he would likely regain full mobility in just a few moments.

Seeing this, Sasuke frowned deeply.

Under Uchiha Itachi’s explanation, he had long understood what kind of terrifying technique this was.

But knowing about it was one thing ; being able to deal with it was another.

At this moment, he only felt an unprecedented sense of trouble.

Ordinary Ninjas often feel this sense of despair when facing a Tailed Beast Jinchuriki.

The sheer difference in size alone was enough to render the vast majority of Ninjutsu ineffective.

Fortunately, Gaara had not yet fully transformed into the Tailed Beast, so there was still a chance to fight.

Sasuke’s eyes sharpened, and without further hesitation, he rushed toward Gaara again.

He had realized that Gaara’s Taijutsu was extremely weak and he lacked combat experience, and this... was his only chance of victory.

As long as he defeated Gaara at this stage and didn’t give him the chance to fully transform into the Tailed Beast, he could secure victory.

In the blink of an eye, the two were locked in fierce close-quarters combat.

However, now strengthened by the Tailed Beast Chakra Cloak, Gaara’s reaction speed had greatly increased, and he was barely able to keep up with Sasuke’s rhythm.

Worse still, Gaara’s adaptation speed was astonishing ; from being flustered at the beginning, he was later able to gradually parry Sasuke’s attacks.

Coupled with his thick skin, Sasuke’s attacks felt like nothing to him.

Sasuke’s heart sank, and he was secretly shocked : 'If this continues, I will undoubtedly lose ; I must take more powerful measures.'

The next moment, the familiar sound of the lightning bird chirping rang through the arena again.

Hearing this tremendous noise, Gaara became visibly alert.

The next moment, Sasuke’s speed surged, turning into an afterimage as he charged straight at Gaara.

Seeing this, Gaara sneered repeatedly ; to think he would try to use the same move against him was nothing but a pipe dream.

He violently mobilized the violent Chakra within him and launched a large-scale Ninjutsu.

« Quicksand Waterfall Flow ! »

As a massive amount of Chakra flooded into the ground, the hard ground beneath their feet turned to sand in an instant.

Countless quicksand surged up like a giant wave, sweeping toward Sasuke.

Sasuke was shocked, and in the critical moment, he forced his body to twist ; fortunately, he was currently in the state of Lightning Release Chakra physical activation, and relying on his agile movement, he barely managed to avoid this attack.

However, the situation on the battlefield was now extremely unfavorable for him.

Although this large-scale attack did not hit directly, it had completely changed the terrain.

Now, with yellow sand raging everywhere, it had completely fallen under Gaara’s control.

Just as Sasuke was locked in a bitter struggle, the atmosphere outside the arena gradually became eerie.

An Anbu Ninja wearing a mask walked silently to the edge of the stands.

Following this, he quickly formed seals with his hands and, without hesitation, activated Genjutsu : Temple of Nirvana.

Countless feathers drifted down from the sky like snowflakes, slowly covering the entire stands.

Every spectator who touched these feathers fell asleep without any defense.

Not far away, Asakawa Shin stood up lazily, a playful smile curling at the corner of his mouth.

« Geez, it’s finally started ? You’ve kept me waiting for quite a while. »
