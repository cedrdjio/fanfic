# Chapitre 222 — The Toad Sage

Facing the rapidly approaching Six Paths of Pain, Jiraiya grit his teeth and rose to meet them, but he was ultimately overwhelmed by their numbers. The battle situation deteriorated quickly.

Deva Path Pain finally found an opening, using a hand-blade to sever the bandages binding Konan and rescuing her in the process.

Having lost his leverage, Jiraiya was instantly struck hard and sent flying backward by a heavy kick.

The Asura Path did not hesitate ; the mechanical parts of its right arm suddenly transformed, and several missiles whistled out, accurately tracking Jiraiya’s retreating figure.

« Boom— ! »

The series of explosions was deafening, and rolling smoke instantly swallowed the battlefield.

Konan, who had just escaped the sealing, couldn’t help but cry out in alarm : « Master Jiraiya ! »

A hearty laugh pierced through the smoke : « Don’t be afraid, Konan, your teacher is doing just fine ! »

Six beams of golden light suddenly lit up within the thick smoke, and a violent aura swept across the area.

Following that, Jiraiya’s figure stepped through the smoke, walking out with large strides.

At this moment, he looked like a completely different person.

Several crimson markings climbed up his cheeks like flames, and his slightly toad-like face radiated a solemn pressure.

Standing firmly on his shoulders were the two Sages, Fukasaku and Shima Sage, their eyes shining like torches.

He had already entered a full-power Sage Mode.

The Toad Sage makes his grand appearance !

Seeing this, Konan’s tense nerves finally relaxed, and she let out a long sigh of relief.

Just a moment ago, she had actually felt an unprecedented sense of panic.

Memories of training with Jiraiya as three little toads flooded her mind ; that was likely the most carefree time of her life.

Jiraiya caught the concern in her eyes, and a gratified smile tugged at the corner of his mouth : « As expected, are women’s hearts still softer ? Our Konan has grown into a fine young woman after all ! »

His tone suddenly shifted, and his gaze sharpened like a torch, locking onto the Deva Path Pain ahead.

« Now, should I address you as Yahiko, or... Nagato ? »

The bodies of the Six Paths of Pain all jolted violently, as if the controller behind the scenes had wavered for a moment.

This subtle opening further solidified Jiraiya’s suspicion.

« As I thought... Is Yahiko no longer in this world ? Are you all now nothing more than specialized puppets ? »

« Because one of you, I saw in the Land of Grass. Although the hair color is different, I clearly remember that he has been dead for a long time. »

Jiraiya sighed, « Nagato, stop this ! »

However, the Deva Path opposite him only raised a hand expressionlessly, « Master Jiraiya, words are useless. Let’s begin ! »

« Shinra Tensei ! »

A repulsive force erupted, carrying flying sand and stones, reaching Jiraiya in the blink of an eye.

Jiraiya’s pupils shrank, and he quickly dodged out of the way.

If it were an ordinary person, they would likely have been crushed to pieces by this invisible attack.

Fortunately, Sage Mode not only strengthened his physique but also gave him the intuition to capture the flow of Natural Energy, allowing him to sense it in time and escape a catastrophe.

At this moment, he looked back at where he had just been standing, still shaken.

Dust was flying on the ground, and the sturdy building that had been behind him was now shattered into pieces, with debris flying everywhere.

« Has that child Nagato actually developed the Rinnegan to such a level ? To possess such inconceivable power... »

Jiraiya muttered to himself. Though there was a hint of a teacher’s pride in his tone, there was even more irrepressible regret and heartache.

If the Great Toad Sage hadn’t personally confirmed that Asakawa Shin was the Child of Prophecy, he might have been doubting his life right now.

« Sages, let’s not hold anything back. Let’s go together ! »

The two toad sages nodded in silent agreement. Jiraiya felt he no longer had any worries and suddenly burst forward in a charge.

Relying on his rich combat experience and the comprehensive boost of Sage Mode, he quickly captured a fleeting opportunity, aiming a heavy, powerful punch straight at the Animal Path Pain, whose taijutsu was weaker.

This was an assault from a visual blind spot and should have been foolproof.

Unexpectedly, the Animal Path seemed to have eyes in the back of its head, dodging the wind of the punch at a bizarre angle at the last possible second.

A smirk of success just crossed Nagato’s eyes, but in the next second, the Animal Path seemed to be struck by an invisible heavy blow. Its entire body was sent tumbling away, losing all signs of life.

« How is this possible ? I clearly evaded it... »

A trace of a smile flashed across Jiraiya’s face.

Kid, are you confused ?

This is Mount Myōboku’s secret taijutsu : Frog Kumite.

By manipulating the surrounding Natural Energy to increase the attack range, it is invisible and intangible yet possesses startling power.

It’s like wearing an invisible boxing glove.

Taking advantage of the enemy not yet understanding the trick, he quickly closed in, his offensive as fierce as a violent storm.

However, not long into the fierce battle, the Animal Path, whose head had previously been crushed, reappeared on the battlefield.

It was now completely unharmed.

« What’s going on ? How did she come back to life ? » Fukasaku Sage asked, uncertain.

Shima Sage’s face was filled with shock as she immediately analyzed : « It’s that bald one ! Just now he summoned a giant head of a summoned beast, and after chewing a few times, it repaired this puppet ! »

« Then we take him out first, otherwise this battle will never end ! » Jiraiya decided immediately.

« Little Jiraiya, listen to this old woman. »

Shima Sage, however, held a dissenting opinion and urged anxiously, « A war of attrition is extremely disadvantageous for us. Retreating now is the best course of action ! »

Jiraiya smiled bitterly in his heart. It would be incredibly difficult to disengage now ; the enemy would never leave him any opening to escape.

In an instant, Asakawa Shin’s warning that « the water in the Akatsuki Organization is very deep » echoed in his mind.

At the time, he had scoffed at it, but now it seemed this deep pool was far more dark and unpredictable than he had imagined.

Nagato also seemed to have seen through Jiraiya’s intentions, specifically deploying the Naraka Path Pain at the very rear of the group, only responsible for providing visual support and never taking risks.

Seeing his tactical intent had been seen through, a flash of determined ruthlessness crossed Jiraiya’s eyes.

His figure retreated rapidly while he lowered his voice and barked : « Sages, let’s use that move ! Burn everything before us... to ashes ! »

Before his voice had even faded, his hands were already flying through hand seals, and the boiling Senjutsu Chakra within his body erupted.

« Sage Art : Goemon ! »

Jiraiya spat out toad oil, Shima Sage followed immediately by releasing Fire Release, and Fukasaku Sage added Wind Release to increase the intensity.

The wind aided the fire, and the oil fueled the flames.

The three techniques merged perfectly, instantly turning into a sky-covering wave of scalding oil and fire.

These thousands of degrees of high-temperature flames were theoretically enough to swallow all physical matter.

Facing this amazing offensive, the Preta Path Pain suddenly stood at the very front of the formation, with the Deva Path right behind it, while the other Pains quickly retreated to the rear.

« Preta Path : Blocking Technique Absorption Seal ! »

The Preta Path Pain thrust out both hands, and a Chakra shield expanded, intercepting all of the sky-covering waves of boiling oil and fire, continuously absorbing them into its body until they finally vanished into nothingness.

However, the good times did not last.

As the absorbed Chakra continued to accumulate, the Preta Path Pain’s body began to swell rapidly.

In just a moment, it turned into a rigid toad stone statue standing on the spot, and the shield of the Absorption Seal also disappeared.

The moment the shield dissipated, the remaining oil and fire lost their obstruction and suddenly surged forward like a burst dam, threatening to burn everything to the ground.

At the critical moment, the Deva Path Pain finally acted.

He stepped forward alone to block the sea of fire and shouted deeply : « Shinra Tensei ! »

A terrifying repulsive force suddenly erupted, pushing back the sky-filling sea of fire.

Taking advantage of this gap, the other Pains scrambled to carry the Preta Path’s stone statue to the rear, starting preparations for the resurrection.

Jiraiya cursed under his breath upon seeing this, and taking advantage of the chaotic situation, he turned and ran.

« No, this is fundamentally unwinnable. I have to find a way to get out ! The water here is too deep ; I can’t handle it ! »
