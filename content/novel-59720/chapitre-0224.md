# Chapitre 224 — Super Shinra Tensei

Above Konoha Village, six figures descended like phantoms.

Deva Path Pain, leading the group, hovered in mid-air. His Rinnegan scanned the panicking Ninja below with cold indifference as he slowly raised his right hand.

« Shinra Tensei ! »

An invisible repulsive force field erupted from Deva Path Pain as the center. Buildings within a radius of dozens of meters were instantly razed to the ground. Dozens of Ninja who couldn’t retreat in time were blown away by the shockwave, crashing into ruins, their fates unknown.

« Enemy attack ! It’s the Akatsuki Organization ! »

Someone shouted, and the Konoha Ninja finally reacted, quickly forming defensive formations to meet the enemy.

« Earth Release : Earth-Style Wall ! »

« Fire Release : Great Fireball Technique ! »

Several Ninjutsu bombarded Deva Path Pain simultaneously, but they were easily blocked by the Asura Path behind him.

The Asura Path transformed his arms into mechanical cannons and fired a volley of missiles, blowing the Ninja who had cast the techniques to pieces.

On the other side, the Animal Path formed hand seals, summoning a giant multi-headed dog, which roared and lunged at the Konoha defensive line.

The multi-headed dog swiped its claws, and three Genin were torn to shreds before they could even scream.

« Don’t panic ! Form up ! »

A Jonin suppressed his fear and commanded the surrounding Ninja to form a perimeter.

However, the Preta Path suddenly rushed into the formation, opened his hands, and activated a Chakra-absorbing barrier. All Ninjutsu vanished the moment they touched him.

« Ninjutsu is ineffective ! Use Taijutsu ! »

« Too late. »

The Human Path instantly appeared behind the elite Jonin and placed his palm on his back.

« Ah— ! »

The Jonin screamed and fell to the ground as his soul was forcibly extracted. The Human Path read his memories, frowning slightly : « Still no sign of the Nine-Tailed. »

Akatsuki Organization base.

« What the hell ? ! »

Asakawa Shin bolted up from his seat, cold sweat soaking his back.

This exclamation was like a thunderclap, scaring Kurotsuchi, who was dozing off nearby, into a reflexively defensive crouch, covering her head and mumbling : « Don’t hit my face ! I was wrong ! »

However, the expected attack did not come.

There was dead silence in the room.

After a long while, Kurotsuchi cautiously raised her head, only to see the room was empty.

« Haha, you scared me to death ! I thought I was going to get beaten again ! So you were just trying to scare me. »

On the other side, Asakawa Shin had received the memories from his Shadow Clone and returned to Konoha.

What he saw was smoke rising everywhere and utter chaos.

He gritted his teeth instantly : « They actually raided my home ? Nagato, that brat is truly asking for death. »

« Shin, you’re finally here. Do you know this guy ? »

Turning his head, he saw Tsunade with a look of pleasant surprise, dashing over in a few breaths.

Behind her, Shizune and Karin quickly formed a combat formation, following closely.

Asakawa Shin nodded affirmatively, looking up at the sky : « Yeah, I know this guy very well. »

Deva Path Pain continued to ascend. He also sensed this suddenly appearing, massive Chakra, and his previously expressionless face couldn’t help but flicker with a hint of agitation.

Casting his gaze over, he saw that a dashing young man had appeared in front of Tsunade at some point, his eyes burning as he looked over.

Their gazes met, as if sparking brilliant electric arcs.

Nagato smiled confidently.

'Is this guy Asakawa Shin ? Indeed impressive, such massive Chakra...'

'But... in front of a god, all struggle and resistance are futile !'

Thinking of this, he increased the remote transfer of Chakra, continuously converging it toward Deva Path Pain.

The other Pains sensed the danger and quickly withdrew from Konoha.

For a moment, except for one summoned beast left behind to cover the retreat, the intensity of the battle bizarrely decreased.

Countless gazes were fixed on the ascending Deva Path Pain, some wary, some hateful, all varied.

Deva Path Pain didn’t care at all, looking directly at Asakawa Shin and speaking leisurely.

« Great nations are always so arrogant, and so are Ninja. »

« Always inflicting Pain on others without hesitation, while remaining detached themselves. »

« But it doesn’t matter. From now on, I will bestow this Pain equally upon everyone ! »

Facing countless increasingly gloomy gazes, he showed no fear and began to chant.

« Feel Pain, contemplate Pain, accept Pain, know Pain ! »

« Those who do not understand Pain cannot understand true peace. »

« From now on, let the world know Pain ! »

Karin heard this and suddenly turned her head to look at Asakawa Shin, complaining aggrievedly : « Master Shin, he’s plagiarizing you ! Teach him a lesson quickly. »

Asakawa Shin’s body trembled, then he nodded with a solemn expression.

The kusanagi sword was unsheathed. Countless amounts of Haki erupted and flowed, compressing onto the three-foot blade.

Black and red lightning twisted like long snakes, and a terrifying aura kicked up a storm that swept through the surroundings.

Deva Path Pain, high in the sky, also sensed this aura and paused slightly.

Nagato’s true body on the outskirts of Konoha suddenly stood up from his chair, hands clasped, veins bulging on his forehead.

An even more powerful Chakra was squeezed out of him and transmitted to Deva Path Pain.

Deva Path Pain’s preparation was finally complete.

He spread his arms, as if embracing a new world, or as if a god was passing judgment upon all living beings.

He spoke slowly, his tone cold and heartless.

« The Nine-Tailed not being in the Village is actually a good thing for me. Right here, I will wipe out you remnants of the old era. »

« Farewell, Konoha Village. »

« Super Shinra Tensei ! ! ! »

The moment the words were spoken, a powerful repulsive force erupted, crushing down from the sky with an overwhelming momentum.

Asakawa Shin sneered : « You guy ! Don’t go around causing trouble in a Village that others cherish ! »

« Do you know ? There are many of my businesses in there ! ! ! »

His Psychokinesis erupted around him, lifting him up to meet the visible shockwave head-on.

The moment that repulsive force was about to touch the buildings in the Village, his Psychokinesis turned into an invisible giant hand, tightly suppressing this repulsive bomb that was about to detonate.

Under a deafening roar, this repulsive force was actually held back, unable to advance even an inch.

« How is it possible ? »

Deva Path Pain’s pupils constricted. In his perception, he clearly detected an invisible force, extremely similar to his own repulsive force, acting in the opposite direction, canceling out the force he was controlling.

The two forces ground against each other, yet neither could do anything to the other.

'No ! Am I at a disadvantage ?'

His momentum was waning.

At this moment, after his Super Shinra Tensei had erupted, it was already losing its stamina and was being visibly pushed back.

Nagato roared, continuing to transmit Chakra endlessly, but it was useless.

Asakawa Shin laughed wildly, revealing a smile like a demon as he approached, the aura on his blade growing even stronger.

Forces are mutual.

Deva Path Pain’s repulsive force couldn’t repel the opponent, so the reaction force pressed down on himself.

His previously expressionless, god-like, indifferent face was now violently contorted, as if it didn’t belong in the same art style as the Ninja World.

Asakawa Shin pushed through the layers of repulsive force and reached in front of Pain.

The kusanagi sword, gathering immeasurable Haki, finally swung, transforming into a giant crescent moon that swept out.

« I heard you’re a god ? What a coincidence, that’s my specialty. »

« Die, Divine Departure ! ! ! »
