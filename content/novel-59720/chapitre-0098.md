# Chapitre 98 — Smash the Cup as a Signal

« Hahaha, you’ve arrived. Please, come in ! »

The Raikage saw Tsunade and her group truly keeping the appointment, and a grin spread across his face.

His expression looked like an old farmer finally looking forward to a bountiful harvest, but paired with his angular face, his smile seemed somewhat ferocious.

The Raikage and Tutai exchanged a look and quickly invited everyone to take their seats.

This time he had really spent a fortune, arranging the banquet in a magnificent hall where the carved beams and painted rafters exuded luxury.

Tsunade and the others took their seats with poise. As soon as she sat down, she impatiently beckoned a waiter and gave urgent instructions :

« Quick ! Bring out all the finest wines and most expensive dishes this place has to offer. »

« Follow the same standard as my last two visits, and be quick about it ! »

As soon as she finished speaking, she turned to the Raikage and laughed loudly :

« Haha, we’ve been to this tavern several times, and this is how we always order. »

Raikage, you won’t have any objections, will you ?«

Hearing this, the Raikage’s eye twitched imperceptibly, but he immediately composed himself, putting on a heroic smile and saying loudly :

»How could I mind ? Inviting everyone today is precisely to express my gratitude ! What is a little money worth mentioning ?«

Everyone, please drink to your heart’s content and feast heartily. Today we only talk about enjoying ourselves ; let’s not return until we’re drunk ! »

However, in his heart, he was gnashing his teeth and cursing : 'Eat, eat, eat, all you know is eating ! Later, I’ll make you spit it all out with interest !'

Tsunade was overjoyed to hear this. She gave the Raikage a thumbs-up and said with a face full of admiration :

« No wonder you’re the Raikage. Just looking at your vision and magnanimity, you truly deserve to be the Raikage ! »

« Hahaha, I’m happy today. Let’s not return until we’re drunk ! »

However, she secretly felt disdain in her heart : 'Today, I’ll fleece this big sucker for a meal to give you a lesson. You have no strength yet you still try to act tough !'

The two looked at each other and both burst into laughter. For a moment, the hall was filled with the joy of guests and host alike.

A moment later, the waiters began serving wine and dishes in a steady stream.

Just as a waiter was carrying a jar of ingredients and took out a scale to weigh it, Asakawa Shin frowned and shouted to stop him :

« Wait, what are you doing ? »

The waiter quickly bowed and explained, « Guest, this is top-tier caviar from the Land of Water. Because it is extremely rare and precious, our shop needs to charge by weight. »

Asakawa Shin sneered with disdain. He pointed towards the Raikage and questioned loudly :

« Do you know who this gentleman hosting today is ? This is the Raikage of the Land of Lightning ! He is a prominent figure even in the entire Ninja World. »

« With him as the host, you actually use such a small scale to weigh ingredients ? This is absolutely ridiculous ! »

« If the Four Kage next door saw this, wouldn’t they mock our Raikage for not even being able to afford this bit of display ? »

The waiter turned pale with fright and was terrified, his words becoming incoherent : « No, no, no, everyone is the same... »

Asakawa Shin roared with dissatisfaction again : « What do you mean everyone is the same ? With such a tiny portion, who is it enough for ? »

« Senior Sister, is it enough for you ? »

Hearing this, a trace of contempt appeared on Tsunade’s face, and she shook her head. « How could this be enough for me ? It’s not even enough to fill the gaps between my teeth. »

Asakawa Shin nodded in deep agreement, then turned to shout at the waiter :

« There, you heard her. This isn’t enough to share at all ! Go, bring another jar—one jar per person ! »

« And this scale, take it away, take it away quickly. Leaving it here, are you looking down on our Raikage ? »

The waiter hurriedly put away the small scale, but he was muttering to himself :

'Something’s not right. The last two times he came, he was watching me closely, afraid I would weigh too much.'

'How come this time...'

'So annoying, these rich people are harder to please one after another. They must all be crazy !'

Before long, the waiter returned pushing a cart and actually served a whole jar to each person.

The Raikage had been dazed since a moment ago and only now snapped out of it. He quickly grabbed the waiter and asked, pretending to be casual :

« How much does one jar cost ? To think it even needs to be weighed on a scale to be sold. »

The waiter did a quick calculation and bowed as he replied, « To inform the guest, our shop has never had a precedent for selling by the jar, so I really don’t know the specific amount. »

However, in my experience, one jar would probably cost over ten million Ryo !«

»Of course, please rest assured. When I took them just now, the kitchen had already weighed them. I’ll go ask for you in a moment.«

The Raikage felt his vision go dark after hearing this. He quickly coughed and lowered his voice :

»Actually, I’m allergic to seafood and can’t eat this caviar. Take my jar away.«

Seeing this, Tutai also quickly echoed,  »Me too ! Whenever I eat seafood, I itch like crazy all over. I can’t eat even a bit. Take mine away as well.«

Just as the waiter nodded and prepared to comply, Asakawa Shin suddenly raised his hand to stop him.  »Wait ! If they can’t eat it, they can give it to me.«

»I’m not allergic. How can such good stuff be wasted ? It just so happens that my jar wasn’t even enough to fill the gaps between my teeth.«

»By the way, move all the seafood from their table to mine. These are all good things ; we can’t let them go to waste.«

After speaking, he glanced at the two with some pity and shook his head slightly.

The Raikage’s face turned even darker instantly, but fortunately, his complexion was already dark, so it was hard to notice if one didn’t look closely.

He turned gloomily to look at Tutai. Tutai glanced sideways and shook his head slightly, indicating that the ambushing personnel were not yet in position and asking him to endure for the time being.

Tsunade and the others paid no mind to the small interlude in the corner and turned their attention to the delicacies before them.

However, only the carefree Tsunade was drinking heavily by herself ; the others only took light sips.

Asakawa Shin’s performance was particularly exaggerated. His hands were so fast they almost turned into afterimages as he inhaled the food in front of him like a storm.

It had to be said that expensive things indeed had their unique qualities ; every dish was extremely delicious.

The waiters responsible for serving even formed a long queue. Dishes wouldn’t be on the table for three seconds before becoming empty plates and being removed, creating a staggering perfect loop.

Seeing this situation, the Raikage felt as if his heart was being pierced by a knife. He had never felt in his heart that the Kumogakure Ninja’s efficiency was so slow.

After all, every second that passed meant burning hundreds of thousands of Ryo.

At this time, Tsunade was still shouting from the side,  »Quick ! The wine is gone. Hurry up and serve more, bring a few more jars !«

Just as the Raikage was on the verge of exploding, Tutai finally gave an imperceptible nod.

A sharp light flashed in the Raikage’s eyes, and he immediately raised his wine cup, calling out :

»Everyone, since we’re so happy today, why don’t we all drink this cup together ? Cheers !«

Hearing this, Tsunade quickly raised her cup and tilted her head back for a deep drink.

The Raikage snorted coldly in his heart and also drained the strong liquor in his cup.

A cruel smile appeared at the corners of his mouth. He was about to smash the wine cup in his hand to wipe out all the Konoha people in one fell swoop.

However, at that very moment, Asakawa Shin suddenly put down his chopsticks, his gaze piercing through like lightning.

Immediately after, a surge of Haoshoku Haki, condensed as if solid, transformed into invisible chains and heavily struck the Raikage, suppressing him so firmly that he couldn’t even move a finger.

This terrifying pressure did not stop but instead spread outward rapidly like a tide, precisely sweeping over the Kumogakure Ninja lying in ambush around them.

In an instant, screams and the sounds of heavy objects hitting the ground rose and fell one after another. The originally hidden ambushers fell unconscious in swaths, losing all awareness.

Only dozens of slightly stronger Ninja barely managed to remain standing, but they were also dripping with cold sweat, their faces full of horror and disbelief.

Only then did Asakawa Shin’s nonchalant voice leisurely ring out across the venue :

»Raikage, I wouldn’t go so far as to call you despicable.«

»But to strike so ruthlessly at seedlings that haven’t even grown up yet, you are being a bit too tactless.«

»Are you really that afraid ? Of the Ninja’s New Generation !«
