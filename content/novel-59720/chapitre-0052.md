# Chapitre 52 — Violation

Sunday, Training Ground No. 5.

The monthly leader selection match for the Six Styles Training Class was about to begin, and many had long been eagerly anticipating it.

For example, Uchiha Sasuke was impatient to teach Naruto a lesson. That bastard always used his status as leader to lord it over him, making his life miserable for the past month. Today, he would definitely wash away his shame !

Unfortunately, he couldn’t fight Naruto directly ; he had to win among the many challengers before he could challenge the leader, which annoyed him greatly.

Yes, the purpose of this match was to select the strongest in Taijutsu to serve as leader. Naturally, there were special rules :

1. In this competition, participants must register in advance. Only after the strongest is determined among the contestants can they challenge the leader. The winner of the two will serve as the new leader for the month.

2. This match is limited to Taijutsu ; Ninjutsu and Genjutsu are prohibited. However, the use of Nin-Taijutsu is permitted, which made Neji particularly happy because he could still use his Eight Trigrams Palms.

Just as everyone was looking forward to it, Asakawa Shin and Might Guy finally appeared side by side.

Asakawa Shin felt the gazes converging on them and noticed everyone’s curiosity about Might Guy’s identity. He clapped his hands to signal for silence. Once the field quieted down, he spoke with a solemn expression :

« Students, this is Konoha’s renowned [Blue Beast]—Might Guy, an elite Jonin who specializes in Taijutsu and possesses exceptional strength ! »

« Today, I have specially invited him to serve as the judge for this match, while I will be responsible for the live commentary. »

« By the way, after your match is over, Guy and I will also put on an exciting duel. Stay tuned ! »

Might Guy also faced the crowd, a brilliant smile spreading across his face, revealing his white teeth. He gave a thumbs up, beaming with high spirits :

« Hahaha, what spirited young people ! »

« Your vitality has ignited the fire of youth in my heart. Let us strive together and embark on a passionate journey ! »

« Let youth bloom ! »

The crowd was stunned by his passionate speech, and then they reacted, whispering among themselves...

« So it’s him ! I’ve heard of him ! He’s a powerful Ninja who is on par with the legendary genius—Kakashi Hatake ! »

« What ? Is he really that strong ? I often see him running around Konoha Village on his hands, I thought... »

« This weirdo... I mean, is this senior really going to duel with Shin ? How exciting ! »

« Yeah, I’ve never seen Shin fight seriously ! This is a great opportunity to see the gap between us. »

In the crowd, only Rock Lee said nothing, his eyes fixed intently on Might Guy. At this moment, his heart was pounding, and he kept repeating what Shin had just said :

'Specializing in Taijutsu ! An elite Jonin with exceptional strength ? ! So apart from Shin, there is someone else who can reach this level relying only on Taijutsu ? This is great, I am not alone on my path !'

Seeing that everyone recognized Might Guy, Shin didn’t waste any time and announced directly :

« Then I announce, the first World Martial Arts Tournament officially begins ! Let us welcome the boys' group contestant No. 1, Uchiha Sasuke, and contestant No. 2, Inuzuka Kiba, to the stage ! »

« What World Martial Arts Tournament ? Isn’t this our monthly match ? »

« Huh ? Don’t you know ? Shin insists on calling it that, saying that teaching us the Six Styles personally is because he wants the Ninjas from our class to become the best in the world and work as his Pals in the future... »

« What does Pal mean ? »

« I guess it means talent, after all, Shin refers to Lord Shikaku as a high-performance Pal... »

« Oh, oh, oh ! I will work hard to cultivate so I can be Shin’s Pal in the future ! ! ! »

Uchiha Sasuke looked arrogant and walked onto the stage with a vigorous stride : 'The best in the world ? That is a title that suits me !'

He looked at Kiba opposite him with disdain : « Kiba, you should just admit defeat. Wait until your puppy has grown all its fur before you come to challenge for the position of leader, hahahaha. »

Kiba and Akamaru on his head were furious upon hearing this. Their fighting spirit boiled, and they stared fixedly at Sasuke, waiting for the judge’s command to rush over, vowing to teach the other a lesson.

Might Guy saw that both were in position and shouted loudly : « Both sides, form the Seal of Confrontation ! »

Seeing that Sasuke and Kiba had formed the seal, he decisively waved his arm and announced that the match had officially begun.

Kiba let out a low shout and exerted force with his right foot. With the aid of Chakra, he stomped on the ground at high speed several times in an instant. His body immediately turned into an afterimage under the reaction force, rushing towards Sasuke !

Sasuke was not alarmed but happy. Kiba was an idiot with an impulsive and fiery temper, and sure enough, he had lost his composure after being suddenly provoked.

How could he be hit by such an attack ? And the beggar-version Soru that Kiba used put a heavy burden on his feet ; how many times could he use it ? The advantage is mine !

Sasuke rolled away from his original position with the greatest ease. Sure enough, after rushing over at high speed, Kiba could not control his body flexibly at all, brushing past Sasuke.

Sasuke stood up quickly, seized the moment of Kiba’s attack stiffness, and landed a fierce half-moon right hook on his side face.

'Oh... no !'

Kiba had realized that he had fallen for Sasuke’s provocation, but it was too late. He was hit solidly on the side of the face by the right hook and flew backward.

But at this critical moment, Akamaru seized the opportunity and jumped off Kiba’s head. The dog latched onto Sasuke’s face, blocking his vision and preventing him from continuing the pursuit.

The loss of vision caused Sasuke to panic. He quickly distanced himself, groped with his hand to pinch the back of Akamaru’s neck, and finally pulled it off his face with a « pop » sound.

« Well done, Akamaru ! »

Kiba had already crawled up from the ground. Although he looked disheveled, he clearly still had the strength to fight.

He forced himself to calm down, crouching on the ground like a beast, his eyes locked on Sasuke, as if he regarded Sasuke as prey to be hunted.

Just when this great battle was about to break out, they suddenly heard Might Guy announce loudly :

« Inuzuka Kiba violated the rules, Uchiha Sasuke wins ! »

The scene was in an uproar.

« What happened ? What happened ? Why did Sasuke suddenly win ? »

« I don’t know either, let’s see what happens !

»Nani ! ? I violated the rules ? Where did I violate the rules ?«  Inuzuka Kiba stood up, full of confusion, and questioned the judge.

»You released a dog to interfere with the match process, which is a serious violation ! So I judge Uchiha Sasuke the winner.«  Might Guy was also full of confusion. Wasn’t the violation obvious enough ?

»But Akamaru is my Ninja Dog. I’m from the Inuzuka clan, you know ?«

»I understand the logic, but does releasing a dog count as Taijutsu ? If it does, that’s the dog’s Taijutsu...«

»It doesn’t count ? Hiss... it seems... it really doesn’t count. Alright, I forgot... I lost this time...«

Inuzuka Kiba admitted defeat with a pained expression, formed the Seal of Reconciliation, and the boy and dog walked off the stage dejectedly.

Uchiha Sasuke couldn’t be happy either. Although his tactical goal for this match had been achieved—defeating Kiba while consuming a small amount of stamina—but... the scene was just too ugly.

Sure enough, from the stage, he heard Naruto’s mockery from afar :

»Hahahaha, that idiot Sasuke was actually... by Akamaru... that pose really cracked me up, hahahahaha !«
