# Chapitre 141 — "Senior Brother, are you saying that Danzo will also participate in this so-called 'Konoha Crush Plan'? Or even, that this was planned by the two of you together?"

Hearing Asakawa Shin’s question, Orochimaru gave an awkward smile. « According to the original plan, that should have been the case, but now that your strength has been exposed, Junior Brother, I’m afraid this plan will die prematurely because of it. »

Asakawa Shin frowned slightly, a cold glint flashing in his eyes.

Since their conspiracy was already known, there was no reason to spend a thousand days defending against thieves.

It would be better to take this opportunity to wipe out these scourges once and for all, eliminating future trouble.

Consequently, Asakawa Shin glanced at Orochimaru, a playful arc curling at the corner of his mouth : « The plan dies prematurely ? It’s just fear of my power. Since that’s the case, let’s up the ante. Your original plan only involved Sunagakure ; I want you to contact Kumogakure within the next month and invite them to join as well. I believe that fellow, the Raikage, wouldn’t want to see me grow to my full potential, would he ? »

Upon hearing this, a sharp glint flashed in Orochimaru’s eyes, and he immediately patted his chest in assurance : « No problem, leave this matter to me. By the way, there is also a mysterious person standing behind Danzo who also possesses a Mangekyo Sharingan and whose strength is unfathomable. Perhaps we could try to recruit him to join as well. »

Tobirama furrowed his brows, his tone carrying a trace of worry : « Shin, this is too risky. Sunagakure joining forces with Kumogakure, the power of two major hidden Villages... »

He shot a cold glance at Orochimaru, making no attempt to hide the wariness in his heart : « What’s more, this fellow is absolutely untrustworthy. If he were to turn against us at a critical moment, the consequences would be unimaginable. »

Asakawa Shin’s expression was indifferent, a confident smile playing on his lips : « It’s fine. In the face of absolute strength, all schemes and tricks are nothing but passing clouds. I intend to take this opportunity to completely purge these unstable factors. »

Tobirama opened his mouth, seemingly wanting to advise otherwise, but was interrupted by a wave of Asakawa Shin’s hand : « Alright, Nidaime(2nd)-sama. There is no need to discuss this further. Haven’t you always wanted to go back and see Konoha ? Let’s set off now. »

Hokage Office.

Hiruzen was leisurely drinking tea.

Listening to the report from the Anbu before him, he stroked his beard, beaming with joy : « As expected of Little Shin, to be able to simultaneously contend with Orochimaru, Shukaku, and even two former Hokage. »

The blonde woman on the side paced back and forth anxiously, her face full of indignation : « That bastard Orochimaru actually dared to desecrate the bodies of my two grandfathers ; he is truly a despicable scoundrel ! »

Hiruzen felt a bit guilty upon hearing this. He coughed lightly and consoled her softly : « Alright, Tsunade. Let’s leave this matter to Little Shin to handle ; we just need to trust him. After all, you should be aware of his strength, right ? »

As soon as he finished speaking, three figures arrived through the air ; they were none other than Asakawa Shin, Orochimaru, and Tobirama.

Immediately following, Asakawa Shin’s figure flickered slightly, and he vanished into thin air before everyone’s eyes, returning in an instant with Hashirama in tow.

Everyone in the office finally snapped out of their daze.

Tsunade’s eyes instantly turned red, and tears welled up.

She covered her lips tightly, looking at the two former Hokage with trembling eyes : « Is it really Grandfather and Second Grandfather ? It’s me, little Tsunade... »

Hiruzen also jumped up from the Hokage’s seat, as if he felt the chair was burning his backside.

He stepped forward with an excited face, his voice trembling slightly : « Shodai(1st)-sama, Teacher, is it really you ? »

Before he could finish, Tobirama furrowed his brows and backhanded him with a heavy slap, roaring in anger : « You bastard Monkey ! Look at the good job you’ve done ! Is this the good disciple you taught ? Not only did he defect from Konoha, but he even dared to desecrate our corpses. How exactly did you teach him ? »

« Ouch— ! » Hiruzen was caught off guard and received the blow, grimacing in pain, but he didn’t dare to look his mentor in the eye, given his sharp gaze.

At this moment, he hung his head, his expression awkward, as if he had turned back into that apprentice who had done something wrong and was timidly accepting a scolding.

When Tobirama’s anger subsided slightly, Hiruzen sheepishly glared at Orochimaru and explained in a low voice : « Teacher, this was just an accident... My other three disciples are all very promising. »

Upon hearing this, Tobirama thought of Asakawa Shin and Tsunade, and nodded slightly : « These two children are indeed not bad. »

Thinking this, his tense expression softened significantly, and his gaze subsequently turned to Tsunade, who was beside him.

At this time, Tsunade had already thrown herself into Hashirama’s arms, sobbing loudly, choking as she spoke of her longing : « Grandfather, I miss you so much ! Ever since you left, Konoha has... »

Looking at Tsunade, who had already become a tearful mess, Hashirama felt a complex mix of emotions in his heart.

When he passed away back then, she was still an ignorant child, following behind him all day learning gambling techniques. In the blink of an eye, she had grown up so much ; he figured the child must have suffered quite a bit over these years.

He gently patted Tsunade’s back, and after her emotions had calmed down slightly, he gently comforted her : « Alright, Grandfather is here now. Don’t rush, take your time. »

As Tsunade told her story, the expressions of Hashirama and Tobirama gradually became grave.

After a long while, Hashirama sighed softly : « I didn’t expect that after my death, so many changes would occur in the world. This so-called Ninja World War is truly too cruel. »

Tobirama also nodded in deep agreement, saying in a deep voice : « Indeed, Big Brother, perhaps your ideas from back then were truly wrong. »

Recalling the Sengoku era, wars were often limited to between families and clans, at most a melee of hundreds of Ninja.

However, since the establishment of the hidden Villages, the scale of war had increased day by day, often involving corps-level slaughter of tens of thousands of Ninja, with a level of brutality that was heinous.

Even I was unfortunately killed in that war.

Speaking to this point, he turned his head, looking at Hiruzen with a complex gaze : « Although you have made many mistakes over these years, in the Second and Third Ninja World Wars, you ultimately led Konoha to victory and preserved the Village. This point is done quite well. »

Hearing this, Hiruzen couldn’t help but shed old tears.

Tsunade caught a glimpse of this scene, a trace of disdain flashing in her eyes.

She was just about to open her mouth to report his misdeeds, but upon meeting his pleading gaze, the words that had reached her lips were swallowed back down.

« Tch, this old geezer... »

Tsunade was very unhappy, and just as she felt she had nowhere to vent, her peripheral vision caught sight of Orochimaru cowering in the corner.

She couldn’t help but raise an eyebrow, then turned to look at Asakawa Shin, saying coldly : « Shin, I have some private matters to deal with regarding your Senior Brother Orochimaru. Send us both to a quiet place ; I want to have a good talk with him. »

Asakawa Shin understood, grabbed both of them, and teleported them directly into a quiet, deep forest.

Inside the Hokage Office, only the « elderly trio » remained in an instant.

Tobirama was not polite at all, sitting down on the Hokage’s throne with a wide stance.

Hiruzen stood with his head bowed, not daring to let out a breath, let alone raise any objections.

Hashirama scratched his head, his expression slightly dazed and cute, seemingly not feeling anything wrong with this.

Tobirama nodded with satisfaction, then his expression suddenly turned grave, and he spoke in a deep voice : « Monkey, I will not comment on past matters, but right now, Konoha is about to face a major crisis. »

« Let’s put aside Orochimaru’s matter for now ; why did even Danzo defect ? What puzzles me even more is, how could he possess a Mangekyo Sharingan ? You must explain exactly what happened in full detail. »

« Mangekyo ? » Hiruzen was shocked upon hearing this, but in an instant, he seemed to have thought of something, a sharp glint flashing in his eyes. The master and apprentice immediately began a heated discussion in the office as if no one else was around.

Hashirama stood alone to the side, scratching his head, appearing somewhat at a loss and quite redundant.

About half an hour later, three figures appeared out of thin air in the office.

Tsunade stood with her hands on her hips, feeling refreshed, her expression full of satisfaction.

Asakawa Shin stood to the side with a smile, his expression composed.

Only Orochimaru was left, currently black and blue, lying limp on the ground with a fading breath, like a burnt-out candle.

However, this was only the beginning. As a snake, an instinctive warning signal suddenly struck, making him tremble all over.

He looked up in terror, only to see everyone in the room staring at him with burning eyes, all focused on him.

Tobirama sat leisurely on the Hokage’s throne, a playful arc curling at the corner of his lips, his tone slightly raised : « So, let us now discuss how to deal with this rogue Ninja of Konoha. »
