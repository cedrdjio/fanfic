# Chapitre 20 — Shisui

« Shin, do you have any trustworthy candidates within the Uchiha Clan ? » Hiruzen Sarutobi slowly stroked his beard, wearing a mysterious expression.

« Haha, Teacher, you’re trying to test me, aren’t you ? Does that mean you already have a candidate in mind ? Why don’t we each write a name on our palms and see if we chose the same person. »

« You little rascal, you always like to come up with these unusual tricks. Very well, Teacher will play along with you. »

Upon hearing Asakawa Shin say this, Hiruzen Sarutobi immediately became interested. The two of them each picked up a pen, wrote the name they had in mind on their palms, and then clenched their fists tightly.

The master and disciple looked at each other and slowly opened their palms. Both of their palms actually bore the name of the same person : Shisui !

They smiled at each other. No words were needed ; at that moment, the master and disciple seemed to share an indescribable tacit understanding.

« As your disciple, I would also like to recommend the head of the Hyuga Clan, Hiashi. »

Asakawa Shin thought for a moment and continued, « Both the Hyuga Clan and the Uchiha Clan are among the two great noble clans of Konoha, so they deserve a seat. Furthermore, giving him a seat would allow us to win over the Hyuga to keep the Uchiha in check. »

« Actually, I have two other candidates in mind : my two senior fellow disciples. One could represent the civilian Ninjas, and the other could represent the Senju Clan. Using them would leave no worries behind.

Unfortunately, those two senior disciples are like dragons that only show their heads but never their tails ; I fear it would be difficult for them to stay in one place for long. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi felt a bit irritated when he heard about those two rebellious disciples. He was already getting on in years, yet they were still out fooling around and didn’t know to come back and help their old master.

The position of Hokage, which others coveted, was treated by them like discarded shoes ; neither of them was willing to take over.

Thinking of this, his gaze towards Asakawa Shin softened a little more. It was Shin who knew how to care for his teacher ; seeing him still processing documents late at night, he even knew to offer advice and strategies.

What was more, despite Shin’s young age, his words were quite insightful. Hiruzen Sarutobi, after all, had been Hokage for decades ; after this bit of guidance, he already knew how to carry out reforms.

He had previously valued power too much, wishing to grasp all authority in his own hands and doing everything personally, but in reality, that was a lesser approach.

As long as he held the core power of the Hokage and the armed forces in his own hands and delegated the rest, he only needed to master the art of checks and balances. His power would not only not decrease, but would instead increase because the cake itself would grow larger.

Moreover, this move would give subordinates opportunities for promotion, and the conflicts and dissatisfaction within the Village would also decrease.

Not to mention that as the highest authority, he could easily win over allies and suppress political opponents.

He had actually given Danzo the power to form armed forces and special departments before ; no wonder he became too powerful to control and difficult to balance. Thinking about it now, it was truly foolish.

Fortunately, he had struck with thunderous speed before Danzo could react, clearing out Root.

Then there were his two old friends, Homura and Koharu ; they were quite old now but still held the positions of Konoha Advisors. Thinking about it, they had struggled for their whole lives, and now it was time for them to enjoy their twilight years. This time, he would use the momentum to solve this trouble as well.

Brilliant, truly brilliant. The general idea was there, but the specific systems and oversight mechanisms still needed to be discussed thoroughly with Shikaku.

...

« Well, Teacher, if there is nothing else, I will take my leave. I have to wake up early for school tomorrow. » Seeing Hiruzen Sarutobi lost in thought, Asakawa Shin spoke up to excuse himself.

Hiruzen Sarutobi nodded upon hearing this and smiled gently. « That’s true, it’s already late. Hurry home, or your mother will be worried. I will have the Anbu escort you back. »

After hearing this, Asakawa Shin chuckled and waved his hand. « Alright, Teacher, there’s no need. Escort ? It’s not even certain who would be protecting whom. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi realized this as well. The little tyke in front of him was no ordinary child ; he had subconsciously treated him as someone who needed protection. Recalling the Anbu’s report from the afternoon, he feared that even he might not be a match for this child anymore.

« Alright, alright, I know you’re capable. Go on now. »

« Mm, goodbye, Teacher. »

After Asakawa Shin left, the office fell into silence once again.

« Tsk, why am I not used to it being this quiet ? » Hiruzen Sarutobi remarked with a sigh.

He hadn’t experienced such a warm moment in many years.

« If that bastard Asuma could be half as good as Shin—no, even one-tenth—that would be enough. Sigh ! »

After sighing, he poured his energy back into processing official business, but the tediousness of what he used to find sweet—called « power »—was now something he could no longer endure.

« Damn it, it’s this cat named Tora again. Why does it run around all day ? Does a mission like this really need to be issued by me personally ? It seems reform is imperative...

Forget it, I’m not in the mood to process official business today. I’ll hurry and find Shikaku tomorrow to discuss a plan. »

« No... that won’t do. » Hiruzen Sarutobi tapped the desk lightly, and an Anbu instantly appeared in front of the desk, awaiting his orders.

« Go and bring Nara Shikaku here. Tell him I have something important to see him about right now. Go. »

The Anbu disappeared from the spot with a « swish. » One could imagine that the Hokage Office would be brightly lit for another night.

...

Asakawa Shin couldn’t sleep after returning home. His mental energy was now frighteningly abundant ; even if he didn’t sleep for several days and nights, it wouldn’t affect him in the slightest.

With nothing to do, he took out the Uzumaki Clan’s Fuinjutsu scroll. The scroll recorded almost all of the Uzumaki Clan’s Fuinjutsu, even including techniques like Adamantine Sealing Chains, though one couldn’t practice them at all without the physique of the Uzumaki Clan.

« Fire Sealing Method ? It can seal the flames of Amaterasu, a special attack against fire-style techniques. Must learn ! »

« Evil Sealing Method ? The Fuinjutsu that Kakashi used in the original work to suppress Sasuke’s Cursed Seal of Heaven. Might come in handy in the future. Must learn ! »

« Contract Seal ? A Fuinjutsu that can block summoning contracts. It could be used to stop Obito and Madara from summoning the Nine-Tailed. Better learn it just to be safe. »

« Tailed Beast Sealing ? Can suppress Tailed Beasts ; it’s a prerequisite skill for Kakuan Nitten Suishu and the Gates of the Great God. Must learn ! »

« Four-Symbols Seal ? Two Four-Symbols Seals nested together form the Eight-Trigrams Seal on Naruto. It’s not only more stable but can also convert the Nine-Tailed’s leaking Chakra to nourish Naruto himself. Worth learning. »

« Reverse Four-Symbols Sealing ? Consumes the caster’s entire life force to indiscriminately absorb all matter within range. It needs to be pre-set within the caster’s body.

It’s the one Danzo had engraved on his body as a trump card for a final mutual destruction. Although it only succeeded in sealing a bridge, its powerful lethality cannot be denied. Pfft. »

« Dead Demon Consuming Seal ? Hmm, I’ll pass on this one. Just looking at it is enough. Not learning it, definitely not. »

« Five Elements Seal and Five Elements Unseal ? Can disrupt the original sealing structure within a target’s body... »

Asakawa Shin devoured the various knowledge of Fuinjutsu with unquenchable thirst. However, Fuinjutsu wasn’t something that could be learned so easily. It was easy to foresee that he would have plenty to do in the coming days.

Asakawa Shin thought confidently, 'There shouldn’t be anyone in Konoha more diligent than me, right ?'

Nara Shikaku : « Speak up for me ! »
