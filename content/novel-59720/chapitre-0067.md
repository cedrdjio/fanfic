# Chapitre 67 — Contracting a Summoning Animal

Two full days have passed since the monthly grand competition that physically shook all of Konoha...

Might Guy ultimately ended up with a crushing defeat in his sparring match against Asakawa Shin.

However, he did not lose his fighting spirit. On the contrary, having witnessed a broader horizon during this match, he became determined to climb to even greater heights in taijutsu.

In a surge of excitement, he even proposed to become life-long rivals with Asakawa Shin, but unfortunately, this idea was ruthlessly rejected by Asakawa Shin.

The reason was quite sufficient : Guy had not yet completely defeated Kakashi, and as everyone knows, there can only be one fated rival.

Although Guy was unwilling to accept it, he soon came to terms with it and made up his mind to settle the score with Kakashi ! Yes, he would go tomorrow.

Besides that, he also promised Asakawa Shin that in his spare time during missions, he would go to the Six Styles Training Class to guide the students' training.

In fact, he was quite happy to do so ; there were many promising seedlings in the training class that he approved of.

Especially one thick-eyebrowed boy ; his experiences and ninja way were exactly the same as his own when he was young. Seeing him was like seeing a reflection of himself.

In Training Ground No. 5, the large crater in the center of the field had already been repaired by Asakawa Shin.

At this moment, watching Might Guy leading everyone in endurance training, he couldn’t help but nod with satisfaction. There probably wasn’t a better taijutsu teacher than Guy.

Since Pal’s tasks had already been assigned, it was now time to handle his own affairs.

...

Hokage Office.

« Teacher, when exactly is Senior Brother Jiraiya coming back ? Can I get a straight answer ? »

Asakawa Shin’s tone was very dissatisfied. Previously, Hiruzen Sarutobi had sent several letters in a row asking Jiraiya to return and guide him when he had time.

As a result, several weeks had passed, and there was still no sign of him. He clearly hadn’t taken it to heart at all !

Seeing his disciple getting anxious, Hiruzen Sarutobi hurriedly comforted him :

« Shin, don’t be anxious. Your senior brother has genuinely encountered a major issue, which is why he has been delayed !

You might not know, but those toads at Mount Myōboku gave him another mysterious prophecy, saying that in the near future, there will be a great crisis that will destroy the Ninja World !

He didn’t go into details about the prophecy, only telling me that the future of the Ninja World has been altered. If not handled well, the future of the Ninja World will be in trouble !

He is currently stressed out all day because of this, searching the entire Ninja World for clues.

I don’t think you need to wait ; I estimate he won’t be back anytime soon... »

Asakawa Shin’s eyelids twitched as he listened, and he pondered inwardly :

'A great crisis destroying the Ninja World is one thing ; there are no shortage of crises in the Ninja World, so one more doesn’t hurt.

But the future being changed ? Could it be... because of the butterfly effect I brought ?'

Thinking of this, Asakawa Shin felt a bit guilty for a moment, and his resentment toward Jiraiya inexplicably diminished quite a bit.

Let it be, let it be ; it’s not easy for anyone, so of course, I choose to forgive him !

However...

« Since Senior Brother Jiraiya won’t be back for a while, Teacher, I might as well just sign the summoning scroll for Shikkotsu Forest !

Practicing Sage Mode at Shikkotsu Forest is the same...

You don’t need to trouble Senior Brother Jiraiya with such small matters anymore ; let him go run around for the sake of all living things in the Ninja World. »

Asakawa Shin rolled his eyes and decided to execute Plan B.

Although he wasn’t very familiar with the Sage Mode training process at Shikkotsu Forest, not as well-versed as he was with the training method at Mount Myōboku.

But he couldn’t wait indefinitely for Jiraiya to return ; after all, time is precious and cannot be wasted.

Mastering Sage Mode sooner meant learning the complete Wood Release sooner, and he always had a feeling that this Sage Mode was very important to him.

Hiruzen Sarutobi nodded in approval, and he earnestly instructed Asakawa Shin :

« Sage Katsuyu is an elder of the Ninja World who has lived for a thousand years. You must be very polite when facing her true form, just like you respect me, understand ? »

'Just like I respect you ? I see. Now that the old Guy puts it like that, I understand everything !'

Asakawa Shin smiled slightly and nodded in understanding.

« Then let’s begin ! I wonder if Tsunade has mentioned this matter to Sage Katsuyu. If not, I can just introduce you.

By the way, for the first summoning, you only need to input a small amount of Chakra, understand ? A small amount ! »

Hiruzen Sarutobi remembered this child’s terrifying amount of Chakra and couldn’t help but quickly add a reminder.

Otherwise, he was really afraid that Asakawa Shin, lacking experience, would summon an extra-large slug, and what if it burst the Hokage Building ?

Asakawa Shin rolled his eyes at him without replying, and instead took out a summoning scroll from behind his back. He had specifically brought it today, thinking that if Jiraiya didn’t return, he would just contract with Shikkotsu Forest, and sure enough, it came in handy !

Asakawa Shin decisively opened the summoning scroll, pulled it to the end, found a blank spot, and signed the blood contract :

« So, this should be done, right ? »

Hiruzen Sarutobi nodded : « Correct, the contract is formed. Next, you just need to use the hand you signed the contract with, combined with your blood, and perform the Summoning Jutsu to summon Katsuyu. »

« No time to lose, then Teacher, I’ll begin ! »

Asakawa Shin looked expectant, his hands forming seals at lightning speed. In just a short instant, he completed the release of the five seals : Boar, Dog, Bird, Monkey, Ram :

« Ninja Art : Summoning Jutsu ! »

The technique was successful, and a cloud of smoke slowly dissipated. A palm-sized, cute creature appeared on the spot. She looked up in a daze and looked around :

« Hmm ? Is this Konoha ? Does Lady Tsunade need me for something ?

No... wait. You are Lady Tsunade’s teacher, the little monkey, right ?

What is this ? »

Hiruzen Sarutobi stepped forward with a solemn expression. He knew that even though this little thing in front of him looked as cute and harmless as a little white rabbit, she was actually the Sage Katsuyu who had lived for thousands of years.

Because there was only one slug in the entire Shikkotsu Forest, all the summoning entities contracted by summoners were merely clones she split off.

No one had ever been able to summon her entire true form, because the amount of Chakra required was immense. From this point, one could tell that the strength of this summoning animal was absolutely extraordinary.

...

« ...That is how it is, Sage Katsuyu. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi explained the current situation in detail to the little Katsuyu.

The little Katsuyu also turned her gaze toward Asakawa Shin and nodded with satisfaction : « Then please take care of me, Lord Shin !

By the way, Lord Shin, you can call me Katsuyu ; that is my name. »

Asakawa Shin could no longer hold back. Seeing that Katsuyu had finally accepted him, he hurriedly stepped forward, cupped Katsuyu in his palms, and said to her happily :

« Katsuyu, please take care of me from now on. Also, Katsuyu, you don’t need to call me Lord Shin ; just call me Shin like everyone else. »

« Alright, Lord Shin ! »
