# Chapitre 194 — Toneri

To reach the moon, there are usually two paths to choose from.

One is to pass through a secret realm in the Ninja World, which was established by Ōtsutsuki Hamura back then, allowing direct access to the moon’s interior.

But this method was far too tedious ; Asakawa Shin was too lazy to search for that so-called secret realm, so he simply chose the second path.

And that was—to fly up directly.

He took a deep breath and then transformed into a streak of light, soaring fiercely into the sky and racing toward that bright moonlight.

This was an attempt ; if this path didn’t work, he could still return to the Ninja World using the Flying Thunder God Technique.

It wouldn’t be too late to look for that secret realm then.

However, the result was much smoother than he had anticipated ; the so-called vacuum of space couldn’t break through his Psychokinesis barrier at all.

This allowed him to easily travel through the void, approaching the moon at an extremely high speed.

At this moment, on the moon, two factions were locked in a fierce battle, and no one noticed this uninvited guest from space.

« Patriarch, this is bad ! The Branch Family has gone mad ! They’ve gouged out all the clansmen’s Byakugan and condensed them into a giant Tenseigan... »

« Not only that, they even forcibly took the eyes of the Main Family members who were defeated earlier... »

The Patriarch of the Main Family was horrified and cried out in shock, « What ? They dare to openly violate the ancestral teachings ! »

« Patriarch, what should we do now ? The power of that Tenseigan is simply not something our people can withstand ; the front line has completely collapsed ! »

Hearing this, the Patriarch of the Main Family swayed and collapsed to his knees, his whole body trembling uncontrollably.

« Is our Ōtsutsuki Clan destined to perish here today ? The Branch Family’s actions... are simply suicidal ! »

Just then, a leisurely and playful voice suddenly reached his ears, making his whole body shudder.

« Uh... need some help ? Professional mercenary work, honest and fair, with reasonable prices ! »

« Who’s there ? »

The remaining members of the Main Family acted as if facing a great enemy, warily gathering around and surrounding Asakawa Shin.

However, Asakawa Shin didn’t care at all, and even took his time to look around.

'They are indeed members of the Ōtsutsuki Clan, but unfortunately, their quality is too poor. It seems it really has to be Toneri. After all, the younger and more gifted an Ōtsutsuki is, the more abundant and powerful the Chakra Fruit they produce will be.'

While he was thinking as if no one else was around, the Patriarch of the Main Family pushed through the crowd and walked straight up to him, looking this uninvited guest up and down.

« What exactly did you mean by 'mercenary work' just now ? Are you some kind of mercenary who works for money ? »

« More or less. As long as the payment is sufficient, I’ll even kill a god for you. »

« A god ? ! »

The Patriarch’s eyes flickered, and he actually began to ask seriously, « Then, what kind of reward do you want ? »

« Patriarch, how can you believe him ? »

A nearby member of the Main Family hurriedly tried to stop him, « This person’s origins are unknown ; he’s very likely a spy sent by the Branch Family ! »

Another person nodded repeatedly in agreement, « Exactly ! Besides, our opponent possesses the Tenseigan. How could this boastful kid possibly defeat the Tenseigan ? It’s absolutely impossible ! »

Asakawa Shin chuckled, his gaze passing over the noisy clansmen and locking onto the giant Tenseigan rising slowly on the distant horizon, emitting a devastating aura.

« As for the reward... »

He slowly raised his hand, a meaningful smirk playing on his lips. « I’m actually not interested in low-level tastes at all. »

The Patriarch’s pupils shrank suddenly. Before he could speak, a piercing hum suddenly erupted from the distant sky.

The giant Tenseigan suddenly lit up with a blinding light, and a green beam of light, powerful enough to evaporate mountains and rivers, streaked across the sky, blasting straight toward the position where Asakawa Shin and the others were.

« It’s over ! » the Patriarch cried out instinctively.

However, Asakawa Shin stood his ground without moving an inch, not even the hem of his clothes fluttering.

The moment the world-destroying light beam was about to engulf them, he simply raised a finger casually.

« I said, professional mercenary work. »

An invisible Psychokinesis barrier appeared out of thin air, like an indestructible shield, forcibly intercepting that devastating beam of light in mid-air.

The terrifying energy shockwave exploded beside him, instantly shattering the surrounding palaces and rocks, yet the ground beneath his feet remained perfectly intact.

« Now... » Asakawa Shin turned his head, a feverish glint in his eyes like a hunter seeing his prey, « can we talk about the contract details ? »

« It’s a deal ! I’ll give you whatever you want ! »

The Patriarch was scared out of his wits ; why would such a powerful person even negotiate terms with him ?

If he were to be angered... whether the Ōtsutsuki Clan could even continue to exist would be a question.

Asakawa Shin nodded in satisfaction.

Although the Ōtsutsuki Clan on the moon didn’t have such pure bloodlines, considering Toneri could directly awaken the Tenseigan by transplanting Hanabi’s Byakugan, they were highly valuable for research.

He could send a few live subjects to Orochimaru for research later ; after all, live ones were much more useful than corpses.

On the other side of the lunar surface, Ōtsutsuki Toneri was suddenly startled.

« How is this possible ? To be able to take a hit from the Tenseigan head-on ? Does the Main Family still have such a trump card hidden ? »

The long-standing internal strife had already severely weakened both the Main and Branch families.

This time, the Branch Family had spared no expense to fuse the Tenseigan, intending to use it to settle the matter once and for all. Who would have expected such a variable to appear halfway ?

« No matter, I, who possess the Tenseigan, am invincible ! »

Toneri was very certain of this.

He had already planned that after his victory and a brief rest, he would descend to the Ninja World to marry his destined wife.

Thinking of that girl, he couldn’t help but frown slightly ; Hinata’s overly violent personality was really a bit hard for him to accept.

But for the sake of the Ōtsutsuki Clan’s revival, he could grit his teeth and bear it.

« What a treasure, truly a peerless treasure ! »

A sudden voice exploded on the silent lunar surface, making Toneri jump.

It turned out that while he was contemplating his life’s major events, Asakawa Shin had arrived next to the Tenseigan at some unknown time.

At this moment, he was feeling the Tenseigan while sighing in admiration.

« Who are you ? Stop ! Don’t touch my Tenseigan ! »

The scene before him completely shattered Toneri’s sanity.

He instantly flew into a rage, pushing his right palm forward violently. A devastating repulsive force suddenly erupted from deep within the Tenseigan, turning into an invisible heavy hammer that smashed fiercely toward Asakawa Shin’s body.

However, facing this blow that was enough to crush mountains, Asakawa Shin didn’t even bat an eye.

Surging Psychokinesis instantly poured out, meeting the repulsive force like an indestructible barrier. The two collided and annihilated each other in mid-air.

Asakawa Shin’s figure remained motionless, standing right where he was.

« What great power. This Tenseigan is truly excellent, enough to become my sacrifice ! »

Letting out another satisfied sigh, Asakawa Shin finally withdrew his gaze and turned his head to glance contemptuously at Toneri.

« What were you barking about just now ? You’re so annoying ! »
