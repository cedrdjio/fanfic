# Chapitre 115 — The Second Template

Amidst Asakawa Shin’s nervous anticipation, his second template world was finally determined...

[Ding ! Congratulations to the host for successfully drawing the « Tom and Jerry » anime world. Please proceed to draw a hair color to obtain this world’s template.]

'Tom... Tom and Jerry ?'

'Isn’t that world full of gag characters ? I was hoping to become a god in one step, but this is what I get ?'

'System, I [bleep]— !'

'You [bleep]— !'

[Understood ! Proceeding to draw hair color for you, please wait...]

'Huh ? Are you deaf ? Wait a second...'

[Ding ! Congratulations to the host, the drawn hair color is « Blue-gray. » The system will continue to draw a template based on the world and hair color you have drawn. Please wait...]

'Blue-gray ? Masaka...'

Before Asakawa Shin could react, the system announcement rang in his mind once again.

[Ding ! Congratulations to the host for successfully drawing the « Tom Cat » template. Please note that when this template’s proficiency reaches 100% and your Ninja rank is promoted, you will receive another chance to draw a template.]

[Current Ninja Rank : Genin. Next Rank : Chunin/Jonin/Kage. Please check the manual for details.]

'It’s actually him ? The Tom Cat who’s practically a Phantom Beast type ? Holy crap ! Actually... it seems okay !'

'Setting aside that pile of flashy skills, I remember Tom has an immortal body, right ?'

'He can deform at will no matter how badly he’s hit, and even if he’s blown to ashes, he can recover instantly. This kind of recovery power...'

'Even that puny Evil God probably wouldn’t even be allowed at the same table as him.'

'Oh man ! This is actually great...'

'System, you’re awesome ! As expected of you !'

In an instant, a mysterious power surged into Asakawa Shin’s body, causing a miraculous transformation in his physique.

Not only that, but a massive influx of skill prototypes flooded into his mind.

From playing musical instruments and conducting symphonies to fencing, car drifting, precision shooting, and complex housework—it had everything.

Looking at these additional abilities in his mind, the corners of Asakawa Shin’s mouth curled up uncontrollably into a wicked smile.

'System, I admit I was talking too loud just now. Don’t take it to heart.'

'This blue-gray you drew is very much to my liking. Anyway, let’s change my look first.'

...

« So, what exactly are you doing suddenly running off to dye your hair ? Aunt Hanako definitely won’t agree if she finds out ! »

Naruto’s eyes widened as he stared at Asakawa Shin with a face full of confusion.

At this moment, Asakawa Shin was completely different from before.

Not only had he neatly cut his originally long hair short, but he had also dyed it a cool blue-gray.

His whole person looked refreshed, and his temperament had changed significantly.

Hearing Naruto’s words, Asakawa Shin wagged his finger dismissively.

« I can just use a Transformation Technique to fool Hanako. Besides, I don’t stay there often, so she won’t notice at all. »

As soon as he finished speaking, he seemed to think of something. His gaze suddenly became dangerous, locking onto Naruto as he added word by word :

« As long as... nobody snitches. »

Naruto clearly felt that invisible pressure. His eyes darted around frantically, not daring to meet Asakawa Shin’s gaze.

« How could I ? How could I possibly snitch ! Shin, we’re best brothers~ »

« You’d better be ! Alright, let’s part ways here. You should get some rest too. Tomorrow is the day for official team assignments, so look sharp and don’t embarrass yourself. »

Before his voice faded, Asakawa Shin’s figure flickered and vanished into thin air like a ghost, leaving Naruto alone to be confused in the wind.

Not long after, Naruto’s indignant complaints echoed in the silent street.

« Dammit ! Is being able to teleport that great ? This is too much, leaving me behind again... »

Asakawa Shin naturally didn’t hear the complaints behind him. At this moment, he had already flashed to the underground training ground.

Only because while he was getting his hair cut, he suddenly remembered an important matter.

According to the system, every template comes with corresponding side effects.

The Roger template’s side effect was crazy nose hair growth, so what kind of strange aftereffect would the Tom template bring ?

He was too excited when he drew Tom earlier and forgot to confirm the key information.

He hurriedly muttered in his mind :

'System, system, quick, tell me, what is the side effect of the Tom template ?'

[Ding ! The current template’s negative status is : You will uncontrollably fall in love with beautiful female cats. Once the fusion reaches 100%, the negative status will disappear. Please refer to the manual for details.]

After hearing this, the corner of Asakawa Shin’s eye couldn’t help but twitch violently, and he cursed inwardly :

'This is too abstract. What kind of female cat counts as « beautiful » ?'

'How far will this « uncontrollable attraction » go ? No, I have to test it myself.'

He turned to look at an Anbu nearby, beckoned him over, and then gave an unquestionable command :

« Go find me a few beautiful female cats. I mean that literally. »

« Remember, they must be female cats, and they must be beautiful enough ! Go quickly. »

The Anbu Ninja was indeed an elite of Konoha. Even though this order was a bit strange and nonsensical, his efficiency was still amazing.

Without making Shin wait long, he successfully completed the order and returned to the training ground.

Asakawa Shin’s gaze swept over the few female cats brought before him, and a hint of confusion couldn’t help but appear on his face.

From his perspective, these kittens were indeed beautiful and cute, but he himself didn’t feel that « uncontrollable » urge at all.

The slight ripple in his heart was merely normal affection for cute pets.

'Although I’m not sure of the specific reason, this side effect seems to have no impact on me ? Did the system bug out again ?'

A sharp light flashed in Asakawa Shin’s eyes as he ordered the Anbu Ninja again :

« Alright, return these cats. »

« I remember the Uchiha Clan has a Summoning Contract with the Ninja Cat Clan. Go find Shisui—never mind, he should be busy dealing with Mizuki’s matter right now. »

« Go find Itachi and ask him for a few beautiful Ninja Cats. Remember, they still have to be female. »

Soon, the results were verified again. Facing these female cats, Asakawa Shin’s heart remained calm.

'It seems I can rest easy now.'

He calculated inwardly : 'Based on my experience, as long as I deliberately grind it out, I can complete the fusion in a few months.'

'By then, the side effect will naturally be lifted. At that time, I can also take the opportunity to advance to Chunin. It’s simply perfect.'

« Alright, send these little cuties back. Thanks a lot this time. »

After Asakawa Shin finished his instructions, his figure flashed and he vanished without a trace once more.

Only the Anbu Ninja was left standing there blankly, listening to the noisy chatter of the female cats, not moving for a long time.
