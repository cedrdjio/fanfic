# Chapitre 51 — What's Wrong with Being a Dog?

After school, Karin and Shin returned to their new home together.

As soon as Karin entered, she saw that Kana was already waiting for them. Her eyes lit up as she rushed over and bragged to Kana with a look of pride :

« Mama, I made friends at school today~ »

« Her name is Ino Yamanaka. She’s a very pretty girl and super friendly. She was actually the one who approached me first... »

« And then there’s Hyuga Hinata. It turns out her eyes aren’t cataracts after all ; I thought they were at first... »

Kana looked dotingly at Karin, who was squirming around in her arms like a little piglet.

Listening to her chatter incessantly about what happened at school, jumping for joy whenever she got excited, Kana felt a bit dazed for a moment.

Seeing Karin so happy, Kana, as a mother, was also overjoyed.

She breathed a sigh of relief, as if a great weight had finally been lifted from her heart :

'School, huh ? It really is a wonderful place. Deciding to leave Kusagakure was definitely the right choice...'

'Even though Lord Shin isn’t very old, he’s already a man one can rely on...'

...

Asakawa Shin couldn’t quite understand these young girls today either.

In just a single morning, the three of them had actually become best friends—the kind who were practically inseparable.

Is making friends between girls really that simple ?

However, Naruto didn’t seem very interested in Karin, his fellow clansman.

Even though they both belong to the Uzumaki Clan, they hardly spoke a word to each other all day. Instead, he was particularly focused on Uchiha Sasuke, and the two were still bickering heatedly.

What a strange guy. Could he actually like Sasuke ?

Karin finally finished bragging to her mother. Her eyes darted around before she ran over to Asakawa Shin’s side and said ingratiatingly :

« Lord Shin, I saw you running around all day and even giving them pointers on Taijutsu training. You must be exhausted, right ? »

« Why don’t you let Karin serve you by washing your feet ? I can give you a foot massage to help you relax~ »

Asakawa Shin was startled. If he let Karin wash his feet, wouldn’t Sister Kana be angry ?

He stole a glance at Kana out of the corner of his eye and found that not only was she not angry, but she was looking at Karin with approval, nodding with a gratified expression...

'This mother and daughter duo...'

Asakawa Shin felt a conflict within him and said hesitantly, « That’s not necessary, is it ? I can wash them myself... »

« Hmph ! How could that be okay ? »

« Ino asked me today how I, as your maid, usually take care of you. I was stammering and couldn’t say a word ; it was so embarrassing... »

« Only then did I realize that Mama usually takes care of you, and I haven’t done anything for you at all... »

« It wasn’t until Hinata mentioned by chance that the Hyuga Clan’s newly opened Health and foot therapy club is doing great business that I thought of this idea. I want to serve you by washing your feet too... »

« I specifically asked Hinata for a Foot acupoint massage technique. I owe her a big favor for it. »

« Just let me try, please~ »

Seeing Karin’s expectant face, Asakawa Shin didn’t want to discourage the girl’s initiative, so he could only accept her service with « tears » in his eyes.

I have to say, it really was something.

This Hyuga Clan massage technique was actually quite impressive ; it was truly professional.

Asakawa Shin leaned back in the lounge chair, squinting his eyes comfortably as he enjoyed Karin’s meticulous foot massage. Even his voice became lazy :

« Karin, I’m glad you’re so thoughtful. Tell me, what kind of reward do you want ? »

Karin’s small hands paused slightly during the massage, and she said as if casually :

« Being able to serve Lord Shin is already a great reward for Karin. How could I dare to ask for anything else ? »

« However... »

« After school today, Karin practiced with everyone in the Six Styles Training Class, but I felt like I was much worse than the others. This made Karin a bit apprehensive. »

« It doesn’t matter if Karin makes a fool of herself, but Karin is Lord Shin’s maid. »

« If I’m too much worse than the others, I’m afraid it will make Lord Shin lose face, right ? »

Asakawa Shin nodded in agreement. After all, the students in the class had been training for a month, so it was normal for Karin to be behind.

But Karin was his maid, after all, and she represented his reputation in public. In that case...

« I think I understand what you mean. How about this, Karin ? »

« From now on, I will personally train you every day when we get home. This process will probably be very hard, so don’t cry out in pain or beg for mercy, okay ? »

« I... I understand. » Karin looked pitiful on the outside, but her heart was already blooming with joy :

'Who says being a dog is bad ? Being Lord Shin’s dog is wonderful !'

'Not only did he save my mother and me from danger, but there’s no shortage of money or guidance in cultivation.'

'Isn’t the Uzumaki Clan’s Fuinjutsu great ? Isn’t Lord Shin’s personal guidance great ?'

'Wuwuwu... My mother and I can never repay Lord Shin’s kindness in this lifetime ! I must serve Lord Shin with all my heart and soul !'

'Loyalty !'

Under Karin’s grateful service, the foot therapy was finally completed. Asakawa Shin slumped onto the lounge chair, not wanting to move a single finger.

« Karin, not bad. That was really comfortable. Do this for me every day from now on. »

« Yes, Lord Shin~ As long as you like it. Ino, Hinata, and I have also agreed to go to the Hyuga Clan on Saturday to learn more from an Old master... »

« Good, good. Karin, you really have a lot of initiative ! »

...

Karin poured out the footbath water and looked around. Seeing no one, she breathed a sigh of relief.

She sneakily brought her small hand to her nose and sniffed gently, an intoxicated smile spreading across her face.

After a long while, she snapped out of it and exclaimed in her heart :

'I thought I had smelled it wrong earlier because I was so excited while massaging Lord Shin’s feet, but it really is fragrant !'

'This scent is so strange, as if it’s a collection of the fresh fragrance of plants and trees. It smells so good. Hehe, Lord Shin actually has a natural body fragrance~'

'Mmm... decided. I’m not washing my hands tonight.'

Karin was intoxicated for a while before showing a look of regret. Pouring out that basin of footbath water was a crying shame. Since it was so fragrant, it could have been used to soak... soak her own feet...

After all, everyone is responsible for saving water~

...

« Karin, your Uzumaki Clan’s constitution is special. You’re naturally stronger than others, so you’re actually very suitable for practicing Taijutsu. »

« However, you can’t be lazy because of it. There are no shortcuts in Taijutsu training ; you can only stay grounded and take it one step at a time... »

'Except for me, of course, since I’m using cheats.' Asakawa Shin added silently in his heart.

He seriously guided Karin on some basic Taijutsu knowledge.

For example, he taught her how to do a Horse stance for a more stable lower body, how to adjust her breathing rhythm while running for higher efficiency, and even personally stepped in to correct some of her mistakes.

During the teaching, some physical contact was inevitable, which made Karin’s entire face flush with excitement. Her determination to practice Taijutsu soared.

Taijutsu is great ; Taijutsu must be learned...
