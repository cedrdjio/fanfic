# Chapitre 88 — Raikage

« Hahaha, this is truly great wine ! Hey, Shizune, your cup is empty, hurry up and fill it ! Shizune ? What the... to think you got drunk after drinking so little. Yūgao, Yūgao... tsk, none of you are any use. »

Tsunade looked around and discovered that, apart from Itachi who was serving as a guard, she was the only one still sober. Asakawa Shin had also long since fallen into a deep sleep on her lap.

This made her shout out quite unhappily : « Hey, all of you, stop sleeping, get up and keep drinking with me... »

Just as she was making a scene, a Ninja abruptly burst into the private room. Itachi was about to step forward to stop him, but the Ninja, without saying a word, performed a deep kowtow to Tsunade, which startled her.

« You must be the legendary Sannin, Lady Tsunade, right ? I apologize for the intrusion, but I have a request, and I hope you will grant it ! »

Tsunade frowned her beautiful brows, a trace of displeasure flashing in her eyes : « It certainly is quite intrusive, huh. Who are you ? Coming here to ruin my good mood. »

The Ninja remained prostrate, not even lifting his head as he replied : « I am a Ninja from the Hidden Cloud Village, named Amayi. I know that you are the strongest Medical Ninja in the Ninja World, and I want to beg you to take action and save my comrade. »

Upon hearing this, an expression of 'are you kidding me' immediately appeared on Tsunade’s face, and she said irritably : « You are a Medical Ninja from the Hidden Cloud Village, right ? If a comrade is injured, you should be responsible for treating them. Yet you come to beg me now ; isn’t that ridiculous ? »

Amayi finally lifted his head. His face was full of anxiety, and his tone revealed a hint of helplessness : « His injuries... my medical skills are truly powerless against them. In this world today, only you can save him ! Please, I beg you to save him. »

Tsunade did not rush to answer, but lazily poured herself a cup of wine and drank it in one gulp, then slowly said : « I don’t want to. Do you Ninjas of the Hidden Cloud Village really have the nerve to come begging to Konoha ? Didn’t you just try to steal the Hyuga Clan’s Byakugan not long ago ? You couldn’t have forgotten so soon, could you ? »

Amayi hurriedly explained : « Regarding that matter, both sides have actually reached a reconciliation... »

However, Tsunade scoffed at this, too lazy to even listen.

Amayi was so anxious that cold sweat broke out on his forehead, and he wanted to make one last effort : « Lady Tsunade, as the saying goes, a doctor’s heart is like a parent’s. Please set aside your prejudices and extend a helping hand to save him. I beg you. »

« Enough, Amayi ! » A sudden roar rang out. A dark-skinned, two-meter-tall man burst in aggressively. Itachi reacted extremely quickly, instantly blocking in front of the group, staring at the newcomer with full vigilance.

Tsunade, however, waved her hand carelessly at Itachi, her brows revealing a bit of impatience : « And who are you ? Why do you keep coming one after another to annoy me... »

Amayi quickly introduced : « This is the leader of our Hidden Cloud Village, the Fourth Raikage ! »

Tsunade was slightly surprised upon hearing this, then examined this uninvited guest with interest, her tone playful : « Oh ? Is that so ? I didn’t expect such a big shot to show up. »

The Raikage, A, had eyes like torches, locking onto Tsunade’s figure. Thinking of his subordinate still struggling in pain, an unquestionable determination erupted from his entire body, and he shouted in a deep voice : « I expected you wouldn’t agree easily, but that doesn’t matter. If you insist on refusing, even if I have to use force, I will take you away by force ! »

The intoxication in Tsunade’s eyes instantly dissipated, replaced by a gaze as sharp as a blade. She met the Raikage’s gaze without backing down, coldly retorting like a needle against a wheat awn : « Interesting, use force ? Who do you think you are talking to ? If you have the guts, bring it on. I will definitely make you regret it. »

A and Tsunade’s gazes collided fiercely in mid-air, as if invisible electric sparks were crackling and exploding.

After a moment of stalemate, A was the first to look away, a confident arc curling at the corner of his mouth. He turned and strode away, leaving behind only a heroic declaration : « I’ll be waiting for you in the tavern’s backyard ! »

Without waiting for Tsunade’s response, he left the private room with his head held high. Seeing this, Amayi hurriedly bowed and trotted to catch up.

Once the two had walked away, Itachi asked in a low, cold voice : « Lady Tsunade, do you need me to take care of them ? »

Tsunade looked at him with slight surprise upon hearing this, then smiled indifferently : « No need to bother. I didn’t agree to go, after all. Speaking of which, Itachi, they are all drunk, why don’t you come and have a couple of drinks with me ? »

Itachi : « ... »

« Tsk, are you a mute ? » Tsunade rolled her eyes irritably, ignored him further, and started drinking straight from the wine pot.

Not long after, she felt the drunkenness rising, slumped onto the table, and fell into a deep sleep.

In the large private room, only Itachi remained standing alone, like a silently guarding scarecrow.

In the hotel’s backyard, heavy rain poured down.

A and Amayi stood in the rain like two statues, their bodies already soaked through, but they remained motionless, stubbornly waiting for Tsunade to keep the appointment.

However, time ticked by second by second. By the time the rain cleared and the east began to brighten, there was still no sign of Tsunade.

« ? »

A had never realized so clearly as he did at this moment that he had been played. A nameless rage shot straight to his head, and he roared angrily : « That damn woman, how dare she stand me up ! »

After speaking, he took heavy steps and walked towards the hotel room again. However, this time he was stopped by Itachi, who was guarding the door : « Sorry, the two adults are still resting, please stop. »

A, however, seemed to turn a deaf ear to this and strode forward, intending to barge in, not putting this child in front of him in his eyes at all.

Itachi’s expression turned cold, and his eyes slowly flushed with blood ; he opened his Sharingan without the slightest hesitation.

Three tomoe spun rapidly within them, and in an instant, they merged into a windmill-shaped Mangekyo, locking firmly onto A’s figure, seemingly about to launch an attack at any moment.

A’s footsteps came to a sudden halt. He looked at Itachi’s eyes with a face full of astonishment, disbelieving : « No... impossible ! This is the Mangekyo Sharingan ? »

Itachi politely repeated himself, which also seemed to be a final warning : « Sorry, the two adults are still resting, please wait here. »

This sentence was like adding fuel to the fire ; the veins on A’s forehead instantly bulged, almost about to burst.

He was the dignified Fourth Raikage, first toyed with by Tsunade all night, and now he was being blocked outside the door by a half-grown child. This was simply a great humiliation !

At this moment, he was like a long-dormant volcano, his anger ready to explode at any touch.

Just as the Lightning Release Chakra around his body was about to erupt, and he was preparing to force his way in, a young but clear voice suddenly pierced the air and reached his ears.

« If you don’t want to die, don’t move. You have an Explosive Bug parasitic inside you. If you recklessly mobilize your Chakra, you will be blown to pieces in an instant. Your life is not worth regretting, but if you wake up my Senior Sister Tsunade from her sleep, that would be a grave sin. »

The one speaking was none other than Asakawa Shin. He slowly pushed open the door of the private room, walked straight up to the Raikage, and stared directly at this powerhouse who shook the Ninja World without a trace of fear.

Asakawa Shin was also feeling a bit helpless in his heart. If the Raikage were to fight Itachi in this state, one careless move would mean death without a burial ground.

He had no intention of completely tearing things apart with the Hidden Cloud Village right now, so he had to show himself and take action to prevent the situation from worsening further.

Amayi, standing beside him, immediately showed an expression of horror and hurriedly spoke to dissuade him : « Lord Raikage, please stay calm ! Allow me to check your physical condition first. »

Without waiting for the Raikage’s permission, he quickly stepped forward and activated Medical Ninjutsu to conduct a detailed investigation.

A moment later, his expression changed drastically, and he exclaimed : « You were right ! Your body really does harbor the same explosive Chakra block as Karai’s. Before it is completely removed, you must absolutely not use any Ninjutsu ! »

After saying this, he turned to face Asakawa Shin, bowed deeply in gratitude, his tone full of the relief of surviving a disaster : « Thank you for your timely reminder. We of the Hidden Cloud are extremely grateful for this kindness ! »

Asakawa Shin curled the corner of his mouth into a mocking smile, his tone indifferent : « Didn’t I just say ? I just didn’t want him to explode suddenly and disturb my Senior Sister’s sweet dream. Who cares if he lives or dies. »

« Seriously, you are all so noisy, how can I have any sweet dreams left ? What on earth is going on ? » Tsunade rubbed her sleepy eyes and walked out of the private room in a daze.

Her gaze swept across, landing on the burly figure of A, and she tilted her head with slight confusion : « Hmm ? Who are you ? »

Seeing this, Amayi, who was on the side, introduced in a low, helpless voice : « Lady Tsunade, this is the Raikage of the Hidden Cloud Village. »

Tsunade paused for a moment, then showed a look of slight surprise : « Oh ? So it’s a big shot. »

A was already shaking with anger, his fists clenched so tightly they were creaking.

But thinking of the critically ill Karai and the Explosive Bug lurking in his body that could detonate at any moment, he could only forcefully suppress his overflowing rage.

He took a deep breath, and under the astonished gazes of everyone, this arrogant Raikage actually bent down and bowed deeply to Tsunade : « I beg you, Tsunade. Please, save my comrade ! »
