# Chapitre 206 — Arrogance

« Who exactly are you ? What are you plotting by coming to our Hidden Cloud Village ? »

Yugito lowered her center of gravity, her back arching high, as her cat-like pupils suddenly dilated, locking onto the figure in the sky.

'Oh ? Is this the kitty’s spinosaurus form ? How cute. Is this posture meant to intimidate me ?'

Sensing the blatant amusement in Asakawa Shin’s eyes, a chill ran through Yugito, and a terrifying thought flashed through her mind.

'Could the enemy’s real target be me ? It’s highly likely...'

'First Gaara, and now me. What exactly is the Akatsuki’s goal ?'

'To attack the Five Kage to show off their military might and spark a war ?'

'Or is it for... the Tailed Beast Jinchuriki ?'

To verify her suspicion, Yugito suddenly turned and fled toward the outskirts of the village.

This action immediately caused an uproar.

« What ? Lord Raikage actually abandoned us and fled ? That’s impossible ! »

« Are... are you kidding me ! What kind of Raikage flees ? I will never acknowledge that woman Yugito’s status ! »

« Damn it ! At worst, we die. That bitch Yugito has completely disgraced our Hidden Cloud Village ! »

Yugito was oblivious to the insults behind her ; her entire focus was on the figure in the sky, leaving her no time for anything else.

Just as she expected, after Asakawa Shin saw her fleeing, he followed without hesitation.

« So the target really is me ? »

A shadow of gloom passed through Yugito’s eyes.

Being hunted ruthlessly like prey was undoubtedly a great humiliation, but she had no choice now.

However, the escape route she chose was by no means aimless ; it was carefully considered.

This route led straight to the Valley of Clouds and Lightning.

The urgent report of the village being attacked must have already reached there. Killer Bee would surely return to help upon receiving the news. As long as she could hold out until merging with that « Killer Bee, » the outcome of this battle would still be uncertain.

However, things did not go as smoothly as she had hoped.

A cold voice drifted down from high above, tinged with a hint of playfulness.

« Alright, this is far enough from the Hidden Cloud Village. Fighting here won’t harm those innocent ants, and you won’t have any worries. So, let’s end this game here. »

« Bansho Ten’in ! »

Before the words had even faded, a terrifying gravitational force descended out of thin air.

Yugito’s sprinting form suddenly stalled. Her massive Two-Tails body felt as if it were being pulled by an invisible giant hand, making every step forward incredibly heavy.

« D-Damn it... »

A flicker of despair in her eyes was quickly replaced by fierce determination.

She suddenly opened her mouth, and a high-heat Chakra fireball condensed, transforming into a beam of light that shot straight at Asakawa Shin, aided by the acceleration of the gravitational pull.

The fire fed on the wind.

The fireball expanded rapidly in flight, turning into a sky-burning blaze that rushed toward him with a terrifying momentum.

« Tsk, this technique really is quite inconvenient ; it actually enhanced the enemy’s attack in reverse. »

Asakawa Shin muttered dissatisfiedly, but his movements were lightning-fast.

The gravitational pull in his palm suddenly shifted, turning into an even more powerful repulsive force that he thrust out violently.

« Shinra Tensei ! »

« Boom— ! »

A dull explosion erupted as the repulsive force collided violently with the fireball.

The massive ball of flame was instantly torn apart and shattered by the violent power.

A giant flower of fire bloomed in the sky, and a brilliant rain of fire scattered like meteors, a scene of tragic beauty.

Yugito’s pupils contracted. At the very moment the fireball exploded, a dark shadow had already closed in like a ghost.

She even lacked the time to make any reaction before a palm was pressed heavily against her brow.

A strange suction force suddenly hit her. Yugito felt the Chakra in her body surging uncontrollably toward that palm like a collapsing celestial river.

As her power drained away rapidly, the massive Two-Tails body shriveled quickly, reverting to human form in the blink of an eye. She was held firmly by the throat by that large hand, suspended in mid-air.

« Sleep for a while... »

That was the last whisper she heard echoing in her ears before she sank into darkness with a heart full of resentment, completely losing consciousness.

« Whew... Done. »

Asakawa Shin let out a long breath, his gaze sweeping over the unconscious Yugito as he calculated silently.

« I tested several abilities of the Rinnegan this time. The power is indeed somewhat broken ; ordinary Ninjas probably wouldn’t even have a chance to fight back against this kind of power. Truly a God’s Eye. »

At this moment, he finally understood a bit why Nagato always spoke of being a 'God.'

With such ridiculous abilities, it seemed almost wrong not to be a little arrogant.

He threw Yugito over his shoulder, and with a slight flicker of his body, he vanished from the spot.

It was a long time later before Killer Bee rushed to the scene, but unfortunately, the place was already deserted ; it was too late.

In a deep and hidden cave in the Land of Frost.

Asakawa Shin’s figure appeared abruptly, stirring a weak current of air.

« This will do ; it’s safe enough, » he whispered to himself, looking around.

Then, he raised his hand, his finger lightly stroking the ring codenamed « Zhu, » and quietly injected a trace of Chakra into it.

« What is it, Madara ? »

Nagato’s cold, emotionless voice echoed in his mind.

« The Two-Tails Jinchuriki has been captured. You can arrange for personnel to prepare for the extraction of the Tailed Beast, » Asakawa Shin said calmly.

Nagato was slightly surprised.

He had urged him several times before, but this « Madara » had always made excuses. He hadn’t expected him to succeed so suddenly.

« Understood. Stand by. »

The ring flickered slightly before falling completely silent.

Asakawa Shin’s brow furrowed, his face full of displeasure.

This Nagato was truly arrogant to the extreme, ordering him around without the slightest bit of respect.

He indignantly pulled out a small notebook he carried with him and made a heavy note in it.

« It seems just being a so-called high-ranking member isn’t enough. This kind of life without real power is truly stifling. I might as well take the position of Akatsuki leader for myself ! »

Several hours later, the ring finally reacted again.

Nagato gave a low shout and activated the « Magic Lantern Body Technique, » using Chakra images to gather all the organization members across a great distance.

The illusory lights and shadows converged into form. Nagato’s emotionless voice echoed in the cave : « The Two-Tails has been captured by 'Tobi.' Begin the Tailed Beast extraction immediately. »

« Tobi ? »

In the crowd, Deidara flew into a rage upon hearing this, his long blonde hair bristling.

« You bastard, you’re actually still alive ? ! Just you wait ! I’ll definitely settle the score with you for shaving my hair ! »

Asakawa Shin, reclining in mid-air, swept his gaze over without any emotion.

This guy actually grew his hair out again ? Truly unrepentant and arrogant to the extreme.

Asakawa Shin snorted coldly in his heart, deciding to settle this debt later.

Seeing that Deidara was about to roar again, Nagato quickly interrupted : « Enough. Business first. Resolve your private grudges after the mission is over. »

With that, he gave no one else a chance to speak. He slammed his hands together and a low shout rang through the cave :

« Fuinjutsu : Phantom Dragons Nine Consuming Seals ! »
