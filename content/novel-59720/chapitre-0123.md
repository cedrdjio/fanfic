# Chapitre 123 — Wild Kubikiribocho

Zabuza’s eyes sharpened as he ripped the Kubikiribocho from his back.

Then, he unexpectedly threw it with great force as a projectile.

The Kubikiribocho spun violently with terrifying momentum, slicing toward Asakawa Shin’s position.

Looking at the trajectory, he actually intended to kill the surrounding team members and the mission target Tazuna, with Asakawa Shin at the center.

Kakashi immediately shouted, « Everyone, get down now ! »

He had actually sensed something was wrong earlier ; the rabbit Naruto had startled with a Kunai was pure white, without a single spot.

This fur color only briefly appears in the wild during the dead of winter for camouflage in the snow, and is more commonly seen in domestic rabbits raised by humans.

Obviously, that white rabbit was a tool specifically prepared for performing the Body Replacement Technique.

This small flaw he discovered immediately put him on high alert.

Such a reaction was truly worthy of an elite Jonin ; in contrast, Asakawa Shin was the polar opposite of Kakashi—he had noticed it but simply didn’t care.

For no other reason than that his Observation Haki, which could predict the future, was no joke ; a sneak attack was completely futile.

As long as the opponent’s gaze was slightly off, before they could even make a move, Asakawa Shin’s massive slap would have already landed squarely on their face.

And that was exactly the case now...

Facing the whistling, flying Kubikiribocho, he didn’t show the slightest hint of alarm ; instead, a simple smile of impending harvest appeared on his face.

Without dodging or weaving, he actually reached out directly toward that high-speed spinning blade of death.

Zabuza, while repositioning himself, caught a glimpse of this scene and couldn’t help but let out a cold, mocking sneer :

'What an arrogant brat, daring to catch my Kubikiribocho with his bare hands !

This is a killing move thrown with full force infused with Chakra. Kid, get ready to be sliced into... Nani ?'

What he saw was worlds apart from his expectations. His heart tightened suddenly, and his forward steps even faltered, nearly causing him to tumble down from the tree.

Asakawa Shin, as if he had anticipated it long ago, held his arm out calmly in mid-air, waiting for his prey like a hunter waiting for a rabbit.

At the very moment the blade whistled toward him, he suddenly applied force with his fingers and palm, firmly clamping the sharp blade in his hand and rendering it immobile.

In Zabuza’s horrified vision, the Kubikiribocho, which had been carrying thunderous momentum and leaving a trail of afterimages, seemed to have its pause button pressed, stopping instantly the moment it touched the young man’s palm.

The terrifying force condensed on the blade’s body vanished instantly, as if it had never existed.

Asakawa Shin didn’t care what he was thinking ; after seizing the Kubikiribocho, he began to operate with fluid motions.

First, he skillfully applied a Contract Seal, directly severing the Hozuki Clan’s summoning connection with the great sword.

Next, he branded a Flying Thunder God Mark on the hilt, then took out a blank scroll to seal the Kubikiribocho inside. Finally, with a casual Flying Thunder God : Guiding Thunder, he teleported the scroll back to his personal collection room.

This series of actions seemed complex, but in reality, it was all completed in just a few breaths. The entire process was smooth and seamless, without the slightest hesitation.

Only then did he clap his hands in satisfaction, a successful smirk playing on his lips :

« I didn’t expect to pick up a wild Kubikiribocho just by walking down the road.

This is one of the famous Seven Ninja Swords, highly collectible. My luck today is really good. »

The surrounding crowd was already stunned by his skillful series of operations. They were all looking at him with strange expressions, each with their own thoughts.

But Zabuza was a bit close to breaking down. He hadn’t even shown his face yet, and his weapon was already lost—how did that make any sense ?

He was so anxious that cold sweat broke out. Jumping up and down on a large tree ahead of the road, he cursed loudly :

« Hey ! You brat with the flower in your hair ! Where did you put my Kubikiribocho ? Give it back to me right now ! »

Asakawa Shin slowly turned his head and gave a simple shrug : « Hey, uncle, what are you talking about ? I don’t understand a word you’re saying. What 'your' Kubikiribocho ? If you call it, do you think it’ll answer ? »

Zabuza was so angry he laughed. He glared fiercely at Asakawa Shin and said through gritted teeth :

« Fine, fine, fine. It seems I misjudged you. I didn’t think your team would have someone like you in addition to Kakashi of the Sharingan... »

Before he could finish, Asakawa Shin glanced at him with a bit of schadenfreude and raised an eyebrow at the woman beside him :

« Tsk, Senior Tsunade, it seems you’ve really been wasting your time all these years. People even recognize Kakashi, but they don’t know you... »

Kakashi broke into a cold sweat, his survival instinct kicking in as he waved his hands to clarify :

« That’s not it at all ! The only reason I have this much recognition is because of this white hair and this Sharingan I can’t close.

In terms of reputation, how could I ever be compared with Lady Tsunade ! »

Zabuza on the treetop also broke into a cold sweat.

Hearing how Asakawa Shin addressed her, a flash of insight hit him, and he finally understood why this woman looked so familiar.

That’s right, isn’t the person before him one of the famous Sannin of Konoha ?

It was just that Tsunade hadn’t performed a mission as a Ninja for over ten years, which had caused him to subconsciously overlook her.

Tsunade, Kakashi, and a youth he couldn’t see through who called Tsunade 'Senior'...

Does this damn Tazuna actually have this much influence ? To be able to hire so many top-tier experts for an escort.

He cursed inwardly : 'If you were this powerful, you could have just taken out Gato directly. Why bother hiding your strength ? Now I look like a clown.'

However, he was now in a difficult position. Multiple presences had locked onto him, making him afraid to make a move.

Tsunade gave Zabuza a look of disgust, then turned and instructed Asakawa Shin : « This guy is a real eyesore, just kill him. »

Asakawa Shin nodded in agreement and gestured toward Naruto : « Naruto, you go take care of him. »

« Ah ? Me... Me ? »

Naruto froze instantly, stammering and unable to speak.

He had never even killed a chicken, and now he was expected to kill a person directly. This was truly beyond his psychological threshold.

Seeing his shrinking and hesitant appearance, Asakawa Shin sighed helplessly : « Fine, Sasuke, Sakura, Karin, you three go help him.

Work together and finish this guy off. »

Seeing this, Naruto wiped the cold sweat from his forehead and breathed a sigh of relief.

Although he still had to do it in the end, fighting alone versus four people working together were two completely different things.

The four of them shared a knowing look, then stepped forward together to face this unfamiliar, powerful enemy.

Kakashi instantly understood Asakawa Shin’s intention. Was he planning to use Zabuza as a whetstone to sharpen these kids ?

After a moment’s thought, he agreed with this approach, but still spoke up to warn them out of concern :

« Don’t any of you get careless. This man is a rogue Ninja from the Hidden Mist Village, known as Zabuza Momochi, the 'Demon of the Mist' !

He is one of the Seven Ninja Swordsmen of the Mist, a dangerous character extremely skilled in Water Release and kenjutsu... »

Halfway through, he stumbled over his own words.

After all, that iconic Kubikiribocho had already been snatched away.

Kakashi coughed lightly to hide his embarrassment and quickly changed his tune : « In short, watch out for his Water Release Ninjutsu and close-quarters assassination ! »

Naruto clapped his hands and summarized : « I see ! So, this eyebrowless uncle is just a toothless tiger now !

Don’t worry, with the four of us working together, we’re definitely going to win ! »

Before his voice had even faded, the four of them, full of confidence, quickly formed an encirclement and rapidly closed in on Zabuza.
