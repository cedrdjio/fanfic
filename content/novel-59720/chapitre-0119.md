# Chapitre 119 — D-Rank Missions

« Hmm ? Where am I ? My head hurts so much... »

Tsunade rubbed her temples, looking around in confusion.

« Lady Tsunade, you’re awake. »

Shizune stood by the bed, her hands twisting uneasily, her voice a bit shaky.

« You drank too much last night, and I had too much as well, so I couldn’t find the way back for a while.

Seeing the situation, Lord Shin had no choice but to settle us in his home first. »

« Oh ? That brat’s house ? »

Tsunade didn’t pursue the matter further ; instead, she looked around the interior decorations with great interest.

« As expected of a wealthy man, this decoration is truly grand. »

« Yes... it is. »

Shizune wiped the cold sweat from her forehead and hurriedly said, « Lord Shin specifically mentioned that this room will be your exclusive room from now on, and mine is next door. Oh, and... »

She pointed across the hall, « Lord Shin’s room is right across from yours. »

Tsunade’s cheeks warmed slightly, and she hastily looked away to hide her embarrassment. « Who wants to know that ? I’m hungry. Shizune, what time is it ? »

« It’s still early, just ten in the morning. I’ve already prepared hot water for you. Once you’re finished washing up, you can enjoy lunch. »

« As expected of you, Shizune ! »

Tsunade nodded with satisfaction, a gratified smile in her eyes. « You’ve been clever since you were a little girl... »

After Tsunade finished washing up and walked downstairs with her hair still damp, the dining table was already filled with all kinds of delicious dishes.

She scanned the table and frowned slightly. « Strange, with such good food, how can there be no fine wine to go with it ?

Shin, where’s the wine ? Don’t tell me your luxury villa doesn’t have any good wine. »

Asakawa Shin felt a bit helpless. « Senior sister, have you forgotten ? We’ve formed a squad now.

If you accidentally get drunk, how are we supposed to take on missions this afternoon ? Karin and I still want to make progress... »

Hearing this, Tsunade unceremoniously rolled her eyes and argued, « Will drinking affect my missions ? Who do you think I am ? »

Asakawa Shin recalled Tsunade’s various 'glorious deeds' from the past and nodded with certainty.

« It’s precisely because I know what you’re like when you drink that I’m saying this.

Senior sister, stop fooling around. I still want to do a few more missions and participate in the Chunin Exams in a few months.

As for the wine, let’s save it for tonight. »

Tsunade rolled her eyes again, a hint of oddness appearing in her expression.

« Chunin Exams ? In my opinion, you might as well just tell the old man you want to be Hokage. He’d probably be more than happy to give you the position.

I’ll support you then ; you can just sit directly in the Hokage’s seat.

In the entire Village, only Jiraiya is qualified to compete with you, but if he actually dares to fight you for it, I’ll break his legs. »

Although Asakawa Shin was quite touched, he still waved his hands repeatedly in refusal.

« No, no, I must first become a Chunin, then a Jonin, and finally reach for the position of Hokage.

Senior sister, don’t ruin my plan. I’m a bit obsessive-compulsive about this. »

« Tch, what a pain. »

Tsunade curled her lip, then changed the subject : « Fine, then you’d better have good wine and food ready for me tonight. »

The group quickly finished lunch, but Asakawa Shin only had a few bites.

His current constitution only required him to absorb Natural Energy to satisfy his physical consumption.

He didn’t need to eat at all ; eating was just to satisfy his appetite. He was becoming more and more like a plant.

The group soon arrived at the mission assignment office. Hiruzen Sarutobi clearly placed great importance on this batch of newly graduated Genin, as he was personally presiding there alongside Iruka.

Asakawa Shin raised a hand in greeting and called out lazily, « Yo, old man~ »

Hiruzen Sarutobi’s eye twitched slightly, and he suddenly turned to glare at Tsunade. « Tsunade ! You, don’t keep leading little Shin astray ! I haven’t even settled the score with you for taking him to the casino last time ! »

Hearing this, Tsunade actually showed a rare moment of guilt. Her eyes darted around, and then she nonchalantly started whistling as if it had nothing to do with her.

Hiruzen Sarutobi glanced suspiciously at Tsunade, thinking to himself :

'Something’s wrong... With this girl’s explosive temper, she didn’t talk back to me on the spot ?

Could it be that she’s caused an even bigger mess behind my back ?

Sigh, I really don’t know if making her the Guiding Jonin was right or wrong.'

Asakawa Shin on the side had clearly lost his patience and urged irritably, « Teacher, stop reminiscing and assign us a mission already.

Let’s see if there’s anything interesting...

Forget it, I’ll look for myself. »

With that, he snatched the mission list. After scanning it for a moment, his brows furrowed. « Huh ? Babysitting for an elderly person ? Weeding in the fields ? Running errands to the next Village ?

What is all this mess ? I expected it to be boring, but I didn’t think it would be this boring. Isn’t there anything more interesting ? »

Tsunade laughed loudly, pulled out a random mission request, and handed it to Hiruzen Sarutobi. « Let’s choose this one. I’ll take them to experience the life of an ordinary Genin. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi took the mission and, after a moment’s thought, nodded in approval.

Indeed, only by going through hardships personally can one truly understand the difficulties of a low-level Ninja.

Every Ninja starts from these seemingly insignificant low-level missions.

« In that case, I’ll leave this mission to you. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi handed the mission slip back to Asakawa Shin, his tone gentle but with a hint of teasing : « This is indeed a suitable mission for newcomers to get started. Go and thoroughly clean that river channel outside the Village ! »

On the outskirts of Konoha Village, by a winding little river.

« Karin, keep it up ! Maintaining balance on rapidly flowing water isn’t an easy task, » Asakawa Shin shouted from a distance in a relaxed tone.

However, at this moment, Karin was already dripping with sweat.

Logically, walking on water in a small river like this should have been easy for her...

After all, having followed Asakawa Shin’s training for years, she was no longer the person she used to be, and she could even skillfully perform some low-level Medical Ninjutsu.

However, the current situation was completely different.

« Seriously... Lady Tsunade, and Lord Shin ! »

Karin gritted her teeth, barely stabilizing herself, and shouted indignantly, « Stop using your Chakra to disrupt the current ! What kind of small river is packed with 'Miniature Water Dragon Bullets' like this ! »

Tsunade and Asakawa Shin exchanged a glance, their lips curling into a knowing, mischievous smile at the same time.

« Isn’t this for your own good ? » Tsunade said with a raised eyebrow. « You know, Medical Ninjutsu tests fine Chakra control the most.

Only when you can stand firmly on this water will you be considered to have truly entered the threshold. »

Asakawa Shin also nodded repeatedly, his tone carrying a bit of pride :

« Exactly ! If it weren’t for you, who else could get me and Senior Sister Tsunade to personally act as sparring partners ?

Just be happy about it. Keep going, I believe in you~ »

As soon as Asakawa Shin finished speaking, Karin became as excited as if she’d been injected with chicken blood, her eyes shining :

« Oh oh oh ! Lord Shin personally cheered for me ! I absolutely cannot fail these expectations, I’ll... »

However, before she could finish her bold words, her emotional excitement caused her Chakra output to go out of control. Her foot slipped, and with a 'splash', she fell into the river.

On the bank, Tsunade couldn’t help but mock, « Shin, your 'encouragement' seems to have had the complete opposite effect. »

Asakawa Shin scratched his head, looking confused. « Tch, that shouldn’t have happened. »

...

« Lord Shin, I’ve sensed the mission target. It’s just ahead. » Karin’s expression was solemn, her tone carrying a hint of tension.

Asakawa Shin chuckled and nodded with satisfaction. « Good job. Then I’ll leave it to you, Karin. »

« Yes ! » Karin nodded solemnly, then lowered her body and crept forward.

After confirming, her eyes flashed, and she suddenly lunged forward, instantly hugging the 'mission target' tightly in her arms.

« Hooray ! Success ! That’s right, it’s this cat. Look at the bow on its left ear ! »

« Great job, Karin. Your sensory abilities have developed quite well. »

Asakawa Shin nodded in satisfaction and reached out to stroke the still-struggling cat. « Well then, the mission to capture the lost pet Tora is perfectly complete ! Let’s go hand in the mission. »

After returning Konoha Village’s 'Most Wanted Fugitive' Tora to its owner, Asakawa Shin finally met the legendary wife of the Fire Daimyo.

It was this lady who, single-handedly, supported countless Genin in Konoha Village who relied on cat-finding missions to make a living.

At this moment, Tora was being held tightly in her arms, her cheek rubbing affectionately against the cat.

However, this star cat clearly didn’t enjoy this 'deep love.' Its four paws kicked wildly in the air, trying its best to escape this suffocating embrace.

Looking at Tora’s expression of having lost all hope in life, Asakawa Shin couldn’t help but feel a bit of empathy.

Just then, the figures of Team 7 happened to appear at the entrance of the mission assignment office.

Naruto spotted Asakawa Shin in the crowd at once. A bright smile instantly bloomed on his face, and he hopped and waved, shouting, « Shin ! What a coincidence, are you here to hand in a mission too ? »
