# Chapitre 170 — Galaxy Impact

On the outskirts of Konoha, the wind suddenly picked up.

Asakawa Shin and his two companions appeared abruptly like ghosts. Before the Raikage brothers could react, a sharp sword light slashed down diagonally.

« He’s coming for me ! »

Killer Bee’s pupils suddenly contracted as a bone-chilling coldness pierced through his spine, making his scalp tingle and hair stand on end.

Within his mental world, he urgently called for help.

Gyuki, sharing a mind with him, instantly initiated the Tailed Beast Transformation, but it was too late. The close-range slash had already descended with a roar.

A massive wave of energy slammed into Killer Bee’s Tailed Beast Cloak, causing him to spray blood and fly backward like a broken kite, smashing through countless giant trees and rock walls along the way.

« Bee ! »

A’s eyes threatened to split from rage. Chakra erupted around his body as he delivered a heavy elbow strike, carrying the force of wind and thunder, smashing toward Asakawa Shin.

« Lariat ! »

With a thunderous « boom, » A’s certain-hit strike suddenly missed.

Facing the sneak attack from behind, Asakawa Shin simply used the Flying Thunder God Technique to avoid it with ease.

Before A could recover from the lag of his attack, Asakawa Shin’s figure had already flickered behind him.

Lightning flickered on his left fist, and with a piercing sonic boom, he punched out violently.

« Fist Bone : Impact ! »

« Ugh— ! »

A spat out a mouthful of blood, his body twisting backward as he was propelled high into the air by the terrifying force of the punch.

« Brother ! »

Killer Bee cried out in alarm. At the critical moment, he had completed his Tailed Beast Transformation and used a « Tentacle Clone » to escape being cut in half by that slash, though he was still heavily injured.

He hurriedly extended a tentacle to catch A, but unexpectedly, he was dragged by that terrifying giant force instead, pulling even the massive body of the Eight-Tails along with it !

There was another earth-shaking roar as the Eight-Tails crashed heavily to the ground. Amidst the rising dust, he couldn’t help but vomit blood again.

Gyuki’s eyes widened in horror, and he growled in disbelief, « Bee, this guy is simply strong beyond human limits ! In terms of pure strength, I’m afraid even if the Six Paths Sage were reborn, he might not surpass him ! »

« I understand, but we have no choice but to succeed ! »

Killer Bee tremblingly raised a tentacle to check A’s injuries. « Brother, how are you ? »

A gritted his teeth, veins bulging on his forehead as he forced himself to stand. « I’m fine, Bee... thanks to you just now. »

He clutched his lower back and panted heavily, his chest heaving. Once his breathing calmed slightly, his gaze sharpened, and he leaped onto the Eight-Tails' shoulder in one stride.

« Bee, I didn’t think we’d be fighting side-by-side again. The opponent this time is still a Flying Thunder God user from Konoha. You remember our coordination, right ? »

The Eight-Tails nodded heavily and wiped the blood from the corner of its mouth. « Of course I remember, Brother. Just leave it to me ! »

The two held their breath, staring fixedly into the distance.

Once the dust settled, Asakawa Shin walked over leisurely with light, brisk steps.

« Tsk, as expected of athletes, you’re really thick-skinned. You didn’t die even after taking a hit like that. »

Hearing Asakawa Shin say this, the corners of the brothers' mouths twitched simultaneously.

Coming from your mouth, why does that sound so wrong ? Who’s thick-skinned ? It’s clearly you !

Before they could voice their complaints, they saw Asakawa Shin suddenly sink his knees, lowering his center of gravity. The Haki around him erupted like a volcano, sparking streaks of lightning.

« Soru ! »

With a powerful stomp, the ground beneath him instantly cracked and shattered.

He shot forward like an arrow from a bow, using the powerful reaction force to launch himself at a speed too fast for the naked eye to track.

Everywhere he passed, the ground was sliced open as if by an invisible blade, leaving behind a deep trench.

« So fast ! »

Killer Bee exclaimed, suddenly looking down at A.

« Brother, grab my horn quickly. I’m going to use that move. »

Understanding immediately, A leaped onto the Eight-Tails' head, his hands tightly gripping the broken bull horn.

Gyuki’s eight tentacles retracted and spiraled, instantly wrapping itself into a giant ball of flesh.

Following that, the heavy ball of flesh began to spin frantically, erupting with incredible centrifugal force.

« Tailed Beast... Eight-Wrap ! »

With this roar, the spinning ball of flesh kicked up a violent tornado hurricane, with layers of wind blades expanding outward like a vortex.

Along its path, entire forests and hillsides were destroyed and turned to dust, carrying a world-ending power as it charged unstoppably toward Asakawa Shin.

'This move again. It’s quite nostalgic.'

Seeing this, a glint flashed in Asakawa Shin’s eyes.

He had experienced this attack from the Eight-Tails back at the Valley of Clouds and Lightning, and its power was indeed impressive.

Unfortunately, my strength is no longer what it used to be.

In a flash of thought, lightning exploded around him, and powerful Haki instantly condensed into a suit of armor, protecting his entire body airtight.

Then, he exerted force with his feet, his speed increasing rather than decreasing as he charged head-on into the world-destroying vortex storm.

« You’re looking for death ! »

Sensing this, the Eight-Tails couldn’t help but feel a surge of wild joy.

« Heh, I didn’t expect this enemy to be stupid enough to take my 'Tailed Beast Eight-Wrap' head-on ! Then let you, ignorant fool, properly experience the true power of Lord Eight-Tails ! »

Thinking this, the Chakra within its body surged violently, and the eight tentacles spun at high speeds as if wound up.

In an instant, the already terrifying storm’s power increased sharply, as if it were going to swallow the world whole.

In the blink of an eye, Asakawa Shin had already broken into the core of the storm.

The terrifying hurricane, capable of moving mountains and filling seas, was like a gentle breeze before him, unable to hinder his progress in the slightest.

The Haki around him condensed into indestructible armor, deflecting all the wild sand and stones within the storm.

Under the horrified gaze of Killer Bee and the Eight-Tails Gyuki, he tore through layers of wind walls at super-high speed. His figure was like lightning, instantly closing in on the two, with only the final layer of the storm remaining.

However, the closer he got to the Eight-Tails' body, the more violent and terrifying the tearing force of the storm became.

Facing this final barrier, even Asakawa Shin felt a slight strain.

He snorted coldly. « This distance... is enough. »

Before his voice faded, he erupted with his full Haki, gathering it into his right fist like a wild tide. In an instant, thunder and lightning roared.

As the power continued to compress and condense, the edge of his fist burst with a blinding light, as if it contained the power of the stars.

Then, he swung his fist violently, and a destructive shockwave broke through the air.

« Fist Bone : Galaxy Impact ! »

With Asakawa Shin’s shout, the Haki, vast as the sea, turned into a surging torrent and poured out.

Carrying the impact of a galaxy pouring down, this punch force was unstoppable, slamming heavily into the final layer of the storm.

Immediately after, accompanied by a blinding flash, a massive explosion swept out.

The Eight-Tails felt a tyrannical force strike. Its frantic spinning momentum suddenly stalled, and its speed rapidly slowed down.

Killer Bee and A clearly noticed this fatal turn of events and shouted one after another, « Gyuki, hang in there ! »

« I’m trying ! » Gyuki gritted its teeth, putting everything into maintaining the rotation.

Unfortunately, the Galaxy Impact was like a merciless brake pad, pressed firmly against the high-speed « tire » of the Eight-Tails, making its rotation slower and slower until the storm it formed gradually disintegrated.

Finally, the vortex storm could no longer withstand the giant force, reached its breaking point, and completely collapsed.

The domineering shockwave, now unobstructed, slammed heavily onto the Eight-Tails' body.

« I can’t hold on... »

With a cry of pain, the Eight-Tails Gyuki spat blood and could no longer support itself, flying backward. Its massive body rapidly shrank, returning to the form of Killer Bee, who had completely lost consciousness.

« Bee ! ! ! »
