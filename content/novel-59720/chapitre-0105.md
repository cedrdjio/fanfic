# Chapitre 105 — Bloodline Limit

« Phew... after all this time, the Chakra from the Four-Tails and Five-Tails has finally been fully digested.

And... just as I expected... »

Asakawa Shin slowly raised his left hand. In his palm, a surge of crimson, lava-like Chakra churned. Under his control, it emitted waves of intense heat—it was the Lava Release of the Four-Tails, Son Goku !

Not only that, with a slight thought, the lava-like Chakra instantly dissipated. Next, a mass of boiling, steam-like Chakra condensed. Amidst the rising mist, it emitted an astonishing heat—it was clearly the Boil Release of the Five-Tails, Kokuo !

Even though he had anticipated it, Asakawa Shin still couldn’t hide the excitement in his heart when these two Bloodline Limits truly awakened within his body.

This was completely different from the Bloodline Limits Naruto would later rely on Tailed Beast Chakra to perform ; this was an original power awakened autonomously by his own body through devouring Tailed Beasts.

Not only was it as easy to control as his own limbs, but it also contained infinite potential, capable of evolving and growing stronger alongside him.

A Bloodline Limit is no ordinary thing. From the moment Asakawa Shin awakened them, this power had quietly reshaped his physical foundation, pushing him toward a higher realm.

The fusion of multiple Bloodline Limits further elevated his talent and potential across all aspects.

« It took two whole years to trade for this power, but it was worth it. »

The corner of Asakawa Shin’s mouth curled up as he thought to himself : 'Among the remaining four Tailed Beasts, there’s still the Magnet Release of the One-Tail Shukaku and the Saiken’s Boil Release of the Six-Tails.

If I want to completely digest these two Chakras, I’m afraid it will take another two years...

In that case, it will be finished exactly in the year of graduation from the Ninja Academy ? Interesting~

However, the sprout on my head has also grown a bit larger. I wonder what it will look like by then.'

As Asakawa Shin was immersed in his thoughts, a soft and sweet call suddenly came from behind : « Brother, what is this ? Are you boiling water ? It feels so hot... »

Hearing the voice, Asakawa Shin quickly withdrew the steaming Boil Release Chakra from his palm and turned around with a gentle and bright smile.

Standing behind him was a little girl as delicate as carved jade, with clear eyes and teeth like white shells. She was so cute that one couldn’t look away. At this moment, she was tilting her head, observing him with curiosity.

« Haha, it’s my fault for making our Little Momo feel hot. I apologize...

But you’re right ! That was called Boil Release. It’s excellent for boiling water. Little Momo is so smart~ »

Before Asakawa Shin could finish, Little Momo’s eyes darted around, and she nimbly pounced into his arms, waving her arms excitedly and shouting : « An apology, right ? Then I want to ride a horse ! No, no, I want to fly into the sky ! »

« Hahaha, alright, whatever you say. »

Asakawa Shin agreed with a smile and then stood up.

He had indeed grown quite a bit over the past two years. Even though he was only nine and a half, his height was already comparable to a normal twelve or thirteen-year-old, making him stand out among his peers.

His eye twitched slightly as he muttered to himself :

'Having inherited Roger’s physique, I won’t end up growing to an exaggerated two hundred and seventy centimeters, will I ?

Please, no. That’s way too tall ; it can be quite inconvenient sometimes...'

He easily secured Little Momo in his arms with one hand, tapped the ground with his toes, and then used Moon Walk to soar into the air, heading straight for the Hokage Rock.

In the sky, Little Momo’s cheers and silvery laughter intertwined and echoed for a long time.

Standing atop the Hokage Rock and looking down at the entire Konoha Village, the unparalleled magnificent scenery was soul-stirring, as if the whole world were within one’s grasp.

Just as Asakawa Shin was immersed in this sentiment, Little Momo in his arms suddenly raised her hand to point into the distance, asking curiously with wide eyes : « Brother, what’s going on over there ? Why are there so many children there ? »

Asakawa Shin looked in the direction she was pointing and saw that it was recess at the Ninja Academy. The playground was filled with students playing and frolicking, making it very lively. No wonder it caught Little Momo’s attention.

« That’s your brother’s school. Do you want to go see ? »

« So this is where brother and Brother Naruto study ? I want to go ! Little Momo wants to go play ! »

Upon hearing this, Little Momo immediately squirmed excitedly in his arms, her small hand pointing straight toward the school, her body leaning forward as if she couldn’t wait to fly there immediately.

This movement startled Asakawa Shin, who quickly tightened his arms to hold her securely, gently soothing her :

« Alright, alright, we’re going now. I’ll introduce you to my classmates ; I’m sure they’ll like you. »

As soon as he finished speaking, Asakawa Shin took off with Little Momo, flying toward the school.

When the two landed lightly in the middle of the playground, it immediately caused a stir of surprise.

Carrying his sister, Asakawa Shin walked straight toward his classroom. As soon as he entered, he became the focus of the entire class.

« Eh ? Is this... Shin’s sister ? She’s grown so much already. »

« So cute ! She’s practically a carbon copy of Shin. As expected of siblings, their looks are just too high. »

« Damn you, Shin ! I want a sister too ! I’m so jealous of you people with sisters. »

Ino, who had been standing next to Asakawa Shin’s Shadow Clone just a moment ago, had already dashed in front of Little Momo. With a fawning smile on her face, she asked softly :

« Does Little Momo still remember me ? I held you just a while ago. »

Asakawa Momo looked at the blonde older sister before her. First, she showed a thoughtful expression, then her eyes lit up, and she cried out in surprise :

« I remember ! It’s Big Sister Ino ! I heard Mommy praising you ! »

Before she even finished speaking, she impatiently pounced toward Ino’s arms.

Ino caught her with a look of pleasant surprise, holding her carefully in her arms, and pretended to be curious as she pressed further :

« Oh ? Your mother really praised me ? What did she say ? »

Little Momo blinked her large, watery eyes and scratched her head sheepishly : « I... I seem to have forgotten a little... »

« Is that so ? »

Ino understood perfectly, a sly smile playing at the corners of her mouth.

Holding the little glutton in her arms, she walked familiarly to the desk, rummaged through her small satchel, and finally produced a shiny candy as if by magic.

« Then take this and think about it carefully ? »

Little Momo’s eyes lit up instantly. She eagerly took the candy, her eyes curving into crescents as she smiled : « Mm... I think I remember a little bit now... »

« Is that so ? »

Ino made a grand gesture and generously handed over another one.

« How about now ? »

Little Momo nodded contentedly, skillfully stuffing the second candy into the small pouch on her chest. Then she leaned forward, whispered into Ino’s ear, and said mysteriously in a low voice :

« Actually, my mommy said... »
