# Chapitre 11 — Visiting the Hyuga

Hiashi Hyuga was in a very bad mood.

Yesterday, his daughter returned from school and said a male classmate wanted to visit him. That would have been fine, but there has to be a reason for a visit.

When asked, his daughter stammered for a long time without being able to say anything, and her face turned red.

Seeing this, how could Hiashi Hyuga not understand ? His blood pressure spiked on the spot, and he wanted to explode.

However, looking at his daughter’s pitiful expression, he suppressed his anger and waited for the brat to arrive today, fully prepared.

...

'Whoo... So this is the Hyuga Clan ? They truly deserve their reputation as a prestigious family in the Ninja World. Even the main gate is so imposing. It’s just...'

The person who arrived was none other than Asakawa Shin. He was dressed neatly and properly, standing straight, holding a packaged Ninja tool maintenance gift box and some fruit. However, the smile on his face was slightly stiff.

There was no help for it. His Observation Haki was automatically warning him, sensing more and more eyes—at least a hundred Byakugan gazes were fixed on him right now.

It made him feel a bit uneasy ; the scanning of the Byakugan made his body instinctively uncomfortable.

'What the hell ? Such tight security ? Just because you have the Byakugan, you can do whatever you want ?'

Forget it. After all, this is Hinata Hyuga’s family home, the headquarters of the Byakugan. Let’s endure it for now.

Little did he know, the inner compound of the Hyuga Clan was in chaos. This child, despite his young age, possessed such a massive amount of Chakra—it was practically like a Tailed Beast.

« What ? Comparable to a Tailed Beast ? Then I have to see this. Byakugan, activate. »

« What ? Let me see what’s going on. »

Word spread quickly. Initially, only a few guards had discovered this powerful Chakra, but amidst the continuous exclamations of surprise, more and more Byakugan were watching.

« Ahem, excuse me, gatekeeper, could you please announce me ? I am Hinata Hyuga’s friend, Asakawa Shin. I am here to visit Clan Leader Hiashi Hyuga as promised. Please accept this small gift. »

Asakawa Shin arrived at the gate and politely handed over his calling card. When the gatekeeper heard the name Asakawa Shin, he couldn’t help but look at him properly. The gifts were naturally taken by an attendant nearby.

« I will go report to the Clan Leader immediately. Please wait a moment, honored guest. » The gatekeeper apologized and hurried toward the inner gate.

In the main hall, Hiashi Hyuga was somewhat annoyed : « Didn’t I say to let him wait for a while to curb his spirit ? Why are you reporting him already ? »

The gatekeeper reported : « Reporting to the Clan Leader, the young lady’s friend claims to be Asakawa Shin. He is here specifically to visit you, sir. I did not dare to be negligent, so I came to report immediately. »

« Asakawa Shin ? It’s him ? I was too hot-headed last night and forgot to ask his name. If it’s him, then it makes sense. »

Hiashi Hyuga was enlightened and looked at the gatekeeper with approval : « You did well. Go collect your reward later. »

He had seen Asakawa Shin at the school that night. To develop such powerful Taijutsu at such a young age—his talent was outstanding, and he was good-looking too. No wonder his daughter had fallen for him.

He had heard the Asakawa family were just commoner Ninja. If they could... thinking about it carefully, it wasn’t impossible.

« Quick, invite him in ! No... I will go greet him personally. »

...

« Hahaha, nephew, you’ve finally arrived. You kept me waiting for a while. » Hiashi Hyuga’s voice arrived before he did.

« You blind things ! Didn’t I say that when my nephew arrives, there is no need to report, just invite him in directly ? »

Hiashi Hyuga glared at the gatekeeper, then stepped forward to take Shin’s little hand and apologized : « The servants are ignorant and have neglected you, nephew. I hope you don’t mind. Please, come in. »

The Hyuga Clan did have servants. After all, not all Hyuga clansmen could awaken their eyes. Those who couldn’t awaken them couldn’t even use the Gentle Fist. Their status was even lower than that of ordinary branch family members, so they could only have the Caged Bird seal carved and become servants responsible for non-combat related jobs.

Asakawa Shin was a bit confused by Hiashi Hyuga’s enthusiasm, but he didn’t take it to heart. He assumed Hinata Hyuga had already explained his intentions, so he followed Hiashi Hyuga into the inner courtyard with peace of mind.

The two strolled to the living room. Hinata Hyuga was already waiting at the door. Thinking about what Shin was going to say to her father later, Hinata Hyuga blushed, and she couldn’t help but feel fidgety :

« Shin, you... you’re here. Come on in. »

Hiashi Hyuga’s fist, hidden in his sleeve, tightened involuntarily. He felt his blood pressure rising again. He put on a forced smile and said to Asakawa Shin :

« Nephew, please take a seat. You don’t know this, but Hinata Hyuga has been waiting for you since early this morning. »

'Is it my imagination ? Why does it feel like Hiashi Hyuga’s aura has become stronger ? There’s even a sense of oppression.'

Asakawa Shin looked at Hiashi Hyuga suspiciously. Seeing nothing unusual, he put the thought aside and stopped paying attention.

Anyway, with his strength, he feared no schemes. He would just handle whatever came his way.

There were dedicated attendants nearby who prepared tea for the three of them before retreating to the side.

The three chatted for a while. Hiashi Hyuga sat at the head of the table, squinting slightly as he asked :

« What brings you here today, nephew ? If you have any requests, feel free to speak. Our Hyuga Clan will certainly do our best to help. »

Hiashi Hyuga had already guessed Asakawa Shin’s intentions and asked intentionally, wanting to see what this shameless kid had to say.

Asakawa Shin was also a bit surprised. Hadn’t Hinata Hyuga explained his intentions ?

Well, if Hinata Hyuga had dared to say it, she wouldn’t have been hungry for so many years...

Then why was Clan Leader Hiashi Hyuga so enthusiastic toward him ? After a moment of thought, Asakawa Shin realized it must be because he had taught the Rokushiki without reservation, and the Hyuga Clan was grateful for his kindness.

Since that was the case... let’s get straight to the point.

« Uncle Hiashi Hyuga, I am here today specifically for Hinata Hyuga. »

Even with Hiashi Hyuga’s cultivation, he couldn’t help but feel a bit annoyed at this moment. He said somewhat stiffly :

« Don’t you think it’s a bit too early, nephew ? »

« Early ? Why is it early ? Isn’t it better to do these things as early as possible ? »

Asakawa Shin didn’t understand Hiashi Hyuga’s meaning for a moment and retorted with some confusion.

'The earlier... the better ?'

Hearing this brat’s shocking words, Hiashi Hyuga turned his head to look at his daughter sitting below. Hinata Hyuga was blushing and looking down, not daring to look at him.

He turned his head again to look at Asakawa Shin. This kid was calm and composed, and even seemed righteous ?

Fine, fine. Looking at this shameless drive, with his talent and the support of the Hyuga Clan, what future achievements would he lack ? The most important thing was that his daughter liked him.

But he still said somewhat unwillingly : « But you are only six years old ! »

Asakawa Shin didn’t care : « I disagree with you, Uncle. What’s wrong with being six ? Six is the perfect age to build a foundation ! »

Seeing that Hinata Hyuga didn’t refute him, Hiashi Hyuga asked somewhat powerlessly : « Then when do you plan to marry my daughter ? »

Hearing this, Asakawa Shin was so shocked that he spat out a mouthful of tea. He didn’t even bother to wipe it off and asked in a daze :

« You... which daughter ? »

« ? »

'You animal ! Hyuga Hanabi... is only one year old !'

Watching Hiashi Hyuga’s face turn from white to red, and then from red to black, Hinata Hyuga also looked up with a stunned expression. How could Asakawa Shin not understand that there had been a misunderstanding ?

He quickly explained : « Uncle, you misunderstood. I am here today for Hinata Hyuga’s Gentle Fist training, nothing else. »

Hiashi Hyuga, who had been halfway through the motion of slamming the table and standing up, froze in place. He lost his coordination and fell onto his butt.

He didn’t care about his loss of composure and quickly climbed up, asking : « Mis... misunderstood ? »
