# Chapitre 180 — "Madara"

After the family reunion ended, Black Zetsu’s entire temperament changed. His usual gloominess vanished, replaced by an unprecedented sense of confidence and composure.

« Brother, I wonder what your next plan is ? Since the plan has already unfolded, why don’t we act immediately to collect the Tailed Beasts while the iron is hot ? »

Asakawa Shin didn’t even think before flatly refusing Black Zetsu’s slightly impatient suggestion.

« No. The Three-Tails has not yet revived. At this moment, collecting the One-Tail and Two-Tails is not only meaningless, but it will also alert our enemies. It’s better to wait for the right opportunity. »

Hearing this, Black Zetsu’s excitement gradually calmed down, and he nodded with some regret.

« You’re right... I just think about being able to save Mother soon and get a bit ahead of myself. Do you know ? Today is the happiest day for me since my birth... »

« Is that so ? Don’t worry, there will be many more 'good days' ahead. »

Asakawa Shin let out a light chuckle, then suddenly changed the subject. « By the way, how far has the Akatsuki organization’s plan progressed ? And where has that idiot Obito run off to ? »

Black Zetsu was pale with shock : « Brother, you even know about these things ? »

« Of course. Although my main body cannot move, my sensory range is extremely wide. I am actually well aware of your every move. »

Black Zetsu realized, and then his expression changed drastically : « Oh no ! That fool Obito actually transplanted Hashirama cells into Nagato not long ago, causing his strength to increase greatly and making him somewhat out of control... I’m afraid he might pose a threat to your plan ! »

« Oh ? A healthy Nagato ? Interesting... »

Asakawa Shin narrowed his eyes slightly and suddenly suggested : « Third Brother, can you arrange for me to enter the Akatsuki ? I want to go in as an undercover agent to personally take control of the situation. »

Black Zetsu’s expression was a bit odd upon hearing this, but considering it was his brother’s first request, he immediately decided : « No problem, I’ll arrange it. However, it’s best if you use a slight disguise to avoid exposure. »

« Don’t worry, I’ll just take the form of Uchiha Madara, and then wear a mask to ensure nothing goes wrong. »

With that said, Asakawa Shin immediately performed the Wood Style : Wood Transformation Technique.

He looked down at himself and nodded with satisfaction : « Very good. From now on, I am Uchiha Madara ! »

He grinned, his heart filled with confidence.

His Taijutsu, Ninjutsu, Wood Release, and immortal body were all superior to Madara’s. He could even summon meteors without weaving hand signs. Except for being unable to use Susanoo, who would dare say he wasn’t Madara ?

Even if Madara himself showed up, they’d have to fight to find out ; only the victor is the true Madara.

Thus, when Obito finally finished his work and returned to the base, he froze on the spot upon seeing 'Madara’s' figure.

« Madara... Lord Madara ? How could you... and this appearance, could it be an Edo Tensei body ? »

« Oh, Obito has arrived. »

Asakawa Shin’s tone was level, carrying a hint of indifference.

« Although the method of resurrection differs slightly from what I envisioned, there is no doubt... I have been reborn at this moment. You have done well. »

Obito’s heart tightened, and his whole body went numb. His plan hadn’t even started yet—was it already going to end ?

Facing Uchiha Madara, he had absolutely no chance of victory in his heart.

'Calm down... how much power can an Edo Tensei Madara really have ? Don’t scare yourself.'

Obito took a deep breath and looked directly into « Madara’s » eyes : « Lord Madara, how much of your strength can your current body exert ? Would you like me to test it with you ? »

« Madara » glanced at him expressionlessly : « Very well. I also want to see if you’ve made any progress after all these years. »

A cold light flashed in Obito’s eyes as his hands quickly formed signs : « Fire Release : Blast Wave Wild Dance ! »

With a low rumble, Obito took the lead by activating Kamui, twisting the surrounding space into a spiral and kicking up a violent airflow.

Immediately after, he spat out scorching Fire Release. The rotating flames and the spiral gale merged instantly, transforming into a massive fire tornado.

The violent flames surged out from his whirlpool mask, carrying the aura of destruction as they lunged straight for « Madara. »

Facing this fierce offensive, « Madara » only gave a cold snort and indifferently raised his hand.

« Shinra Tensei ! »

In an instant, a terrifying repulsive force erupted like a tsunami. The seemingly invincible fire tornado was easily torn apart like fragile paper the moment it touched this power, turning into a sky full of sparks.

The remaining repulsive force continued to push forward, slamming heavily toward Obito.

Obito’s pupils shrank suddenly. In the nick of time, he hurriedly activated Kamui to phase through, his body twisting and dissipating like water ripples as he perilously hid in the alternate dimension, avoiding the fatal blow.

He was horrified. He didn’t expect his carefully developed and proud combination Ninjutsu to be so vulnerable before the opponent.

As the terrifying repulsive force gradually dissipated, Obito had just emerged from the alternate dimension when he unhesitatingly formed signs again and shouted sharply :

« Wood Style : Cutting Sprigs ! »

This was a killing move he developed using the properties of Kamui. Countless sharp Wood Release thorns instantly spread from the twisted space, growing wildly like venomous snakes and stabbing toward « Madara’s » vitals.

However, « Madara » remained indifferent and unmoved.

He simply waved his hand, and a majestic burst of Psychokinesis erupted. The seemingly terrifying Wood Release thorns were as fragile as paper mache the moment they touched the force, instantly shattering and collapsing.

Seeing this, Obito’s Adam’s apple bobbed as he swallowed hard, cursing Black Zetsu in his heart : 'Damn thing ! He actually summoned the real Madara. Now I’m in big trouble !'

Just as his mind was unsettled and his thoughts were racing, « Madara » raised his hand again.

The next moment, a terrifying repulsive force, like an invisible heavy hammer, slammed hard against his chest.

Obito had no time to react at all. He felt as if his internal organs were displaced, a mouthful of blood sprayed out, and he was sent flying like a kite with a broken string.

After a long time, Obito struggled to crawl out of the ruins, looking wretched and panting heavily : « How is this possible ? Doesn’t Shinra Tensei have a cooldown time ? Why can you use it continuously ? »

« Madara’s » lips curled into a cruel smile : « What nonsense are you talking about ? My Shinra Tensei has no cooldown time at all. »

« Nani ? ! »

Obito’s pupils shook violently, his eyes wide, his face filled with disbelief : « Is this the true power of the Rinnegan ? Compared to this, that fellow Nagato is simply a waste ! »

At this moment, his wariness of « Madara » climbed to the peak.

With his current strength, let alone the fact that his heart still had the cursed seal Madara planted back then, even without that weakness, he was absolutely no match for the man in front of him.

A sense of bitterness welled up in Obito’s heart. He could only helplessly suppress all his ambitions and schemes, obediently bowing his head and completely abandoning the thought of resistance.

At that moment, in order to maintain his persona, « Madara » coldly added another sentence.

« Also, your Wood Release compared to Hashirama’s is like a firefly to the bright moon—insignificant and not worth mentioning. I advise you not to perform such clumsy Wood Release in the future, lest you become a laughingstock. »

« ... »

A short while later, « Madara » and Zetsu both arrived at the Hidden Rain Village. The familiar high tower still stood tall.

At this time, Nagato was undergoing recovery training. Seeing the two people suddenly appear, a flash of astonishment crossed his eyes : « Why have you come again ? »

Asakawa Shin looked indifferent and took a step forward without ceremony. « Nagato, I’ve come to see your recovery progress. From the looks of it, you’re in good shape. »

As he spoke, he reached out his hand, intending to pat Nagato’s shoulder to show « closeness. »

Nagato’s brow furrowed. Before the other party could touch him, the Rinnegan in his eyes instantly activated, and a burst of repulsive force erupted, forcibly snapping Asakawa Shin’s hand away.

« Madara, what exactly do you want ? » Nagato asked in a cold, deep voice.

« Don’t be so nervous. »

Asakawa Shin withdrew his hand, but the smile on his face didn’t fade in the slightest, his tone remaining composed.

« I just wanted to help you condition your body. There’s no need for you to be so excited.

Also, I’ve been meaning to ask since just now—Konan, why are you blushing ? »
