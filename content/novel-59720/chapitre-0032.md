# Chapitre 32 — The Return Journey

Shikaku Nara left the Capital of the Land of Fire in a hurry, accompanied by over a dozen Anbu Ninja.

Originally, Shikaku felt there were too many Anbu Ninja accompanying him. After all, he didn’t want to be as high-profile as Danzo, who always brought dozens of Root Ninja to make a show of force whenever he appeared. The Nara clan was currently in a high position of power, and he didn’t know how many people were watching them from the shadows ; it was the time when they most needed to keep a low profile, but now...

« I feel like the escort is a bit sparse. What if someone attacks to rob us of the money ? That was a miscalculation. I didn’t expect to earn so much money on this trip. Wealth truly moves people’s hearts. »

« It’s too late to say anything now. Even if I said the money wasn’t with me, I’m afraid no one would believe it. It’s no longer safe to stay in the Capital of the Land of Fire. »

Thinking of this, Shikaku Nara couldn’t help but feel a tightness in his chest. He ordered those around him, « Everyone, set off for Konoha immediately. Increase our marching speed and aim to arrive at Konoha before dawn. »

« Yes ! » The Anbu Ninja obeyed and took action immediately. Fearing that something might go wrong on the road, Shikaku even specifically summoned a message back to Konoha, asking Hiruzen Sarutobi to send people to meet them to prevent any unforeseen events.

His worries were not unfounded ; shortly after they left, some people in the shadows also scattered with flickering gazes.

Capital of the Land of Fire, underground Black Market Exchange.

« Boss, come out quickly, I have important intelligence to report ! » A person wearing a cloak and mask rushed into the Black Market Exchange and frantically found the manager. After a brief conversation, the two were led into an underground dark room.

« Boss, the news was passed to me by that adult above. It is absolutely true. This is a huge fortune, a full 1.1 billion ryo ! »

The Black Market Exchange was an underground organization that had been passed down for hundreds or thousands of years. They sold intelligence and issued bounties for killing, with intricate and complex forces behind them. Their hideouts were spread almost all over the Ninja World, and many famous Ninja had accepted missions at the Black Market Exchange.

« 1.1 billion ryo ? ! » The manager of the Capital of the Land of Fire’s Black Market Exchange was also shocked. Even with his extensive work experience and contact with the bounties of many big shots, he was taken aback by this astronomical figure.

In the original story, Asuma Sarutobi, with his many identities as a Konoha elite Jonin, one of the Twelve Guardian Ninja of the Daimyo, and the son of the Third Hokage, only had a bounty of 35 million ryo, and this sum of money was more than 31 Asumas combined.

« This won’t do, this is major intelligence. We must report the news immediately and pass it on to other Black Market Exchanges. » The manager made a prompt decision and began to act. The Black Market Exchange had been able to do intelligence business in the Ninja World for so many years without failing ; naturally, their intelligence transmission ability was top-notch. Just a few minutes later, the Black Market Exchanges across the entire Ninja World learned of this news through summonable animals, and the entire underground world instantly boiled over.

The way the Black Market Exchange operated was not to kill people themselves ; they only earned money from selling intelligence and collected commissions as intermediaries for issuing bounty orders. This time, the higher-ups of the Black Market Exchange decided to sell the information on this huge sum of money at a high price.

Land of Hot Water, underground Black Market Exchange.

A Ninja with green bean eyes, wearing a black robe with red clouds, slowly walked in, holding two small boxes that emitted an ominous, bloody smell.

The staff at the Black Market Exchange saw him and quickly stepped forward to greet him : « Master Kakuzu, have you successfully hunted again ? Are you exchanging everything for cash this time too ? »

« Hmm, exchange both of these for cash, and give me a copy of the latest bounty orders in the Ninja World. »

The staff took the boxes, opened them, and couldn’t help but twitch his eyes. He remembered that the owner of this head was Master Kakuzu’s companion who came last time. Sure enough...

The staff was already somewhat used to it ; after all, Master Kakuzu had already changed several companions, and every time it was... He skillfully verified the identity of the two heads and handed the bounty to Kakuzu.

« By the way, Master Kakuzu, there is a piece of intelligence you will definitely be interested in. It involves a super large sum of money. » The staff said while organizing the bounty orders.

Upon hearing this, Kakuzu was indeed very interested. His two green eyeballs were almost glowing, and he couldn’t help but ask, « A super large sum of money ? How much ? »

The staff said word by word, « 1.1 billion ryo ! »

« 1.1 billion ryo ? ! » Kakuzu repeated in disbelief. He only froze for a moment before coming back to his senses and shouted frantically, « Give me this intelligence quickly ! »

« Of course, but you know the rules of the Black Market Exchange. It’s 1 million ryo. »

« What ? 1 million ryo ? You might as well kill me ! » Kakuzu found it hard to accept this high price, feeling the five hearts in his body twitching with pain. But if he gave it up... No, his heart hurt even more.

The staff was also a little scared ; Kakuzu’s expression at this moment was too terrifying. He said tremblingly, « Master Kakuzu, the rules are like this. I guarantee the intelligence is absolutely true. This 1 million ryo is worth more than the price ; many people have already bought this intelligence. »

« What ? Many people bought it ? Where are they ? » Kakuzu scanned around, but didn’t see half a person.

« They left half an hour ago. If you don’t hurry, I’m afraid... »

« You’re too long-winded. You’d better ensure the intelligence is really worth this price, otherwise... » Kakuzu counted out 1 million ryo with a pained expression and handed it to the staff.

« Master Kakuzu, thank you for your patronage. Uh... please let go... Okay, here is the detailed information of the intelligence. I wish you good luck in battle. »

Kakuzu took the intelligence and quickly checked it : 'Shikaku Nara ? It’s actually this guy. He is already a bounty target himself, and he is carrying such a huge sum of money. He is truly courting death. Taking them down is not a problem, it’s just that the distance is a bit far. Damn it, I must act as quickly as possible !'

While Kakuzu was chasing his dreams, Shikaku Nara was in big trouble.

« Lord Shikaku, nine hundred meters ahead, there are seven Ninja in ambush. The Chakra reaction indicates two Jonin and five Chunin. » A sensory-type Anbu Ninja accompanying him reported.

Shikaku’s face sank upon hearing this. This was already the fourth wave of Ninja to attack them in the last two hours. Their forward speed was severely hindered. If this continued...

« Can we go around ? » Shikaku asked.

« No, this is the only way. If we take a detour, it will delay the journey by at least several hours. By then, I’m afraid... »

« Then prepare for battle. Let’s end this quickly. Reinforcements are already on the way ; we absolutely cannot be dragged down here. » Shikaku made a decision, and everyone immediately formed a combat formation and surrounded the ambush point.

The Ninja ambushing in the distance had not yet felt the approaching danger and was still happily thinking about the huge sum of 1.1 billion ryo. By the time he reacted, the Kunai was already right in front of his eyes.

'How is this possible ? Why didn’t the trap trigger ?' Along with the last trace of doubt in his mind, his life was ended by a small Kunai.

« Damn it ! Discovered ! Prepare to fight ! »

But only the whistling wind responded to him. Everyone except him had been killed by the first wave of attacks.

« Ho... how is this possible ? » The remaining Ninja was on the verge of a breakdown. They had clearly agreed to...

The Anbu Ninja ignored him and just continued to attack, swarming him and killing him in an instant.

Although they won easily, Shikaku’s face was extremely gloomy. Guys this weak dared to come and intercept them ; I’m afraid the intelligence spread faster than he imagined...

'Tsk, this is really troublesome now.'
