# Chapitre 127 — The Return

« Strange, truly strange... »

Kakashi watched Asakawa Shin mutter to himself incessantly and finally couldn’t help but ask, « What’s wrong, Shin ? What are you mumbling about over there ? »

Asakawa Shin shrugged helplessly. « Look, this bridge is about to be finished. Why hasn’t there been any movement from your distant cousin Gato ? Doesn’t he have a bunch of thugs ? »

Kakashi rolled his eyes hard at this and said crossly, « Gato is long dead. How could he come to cause trouble ? And what 'cousin' ? My surname is Hatake, not Gato, okay ? How can you even link me to him ? »

« Dead ? When did he die ? How did he die ? How come I didn’t know... »

Asakawa Shin was shocked to hear this. He had originally planned to find an opportunity to test the combat effectiveness of his Ice Release. How could the opponent have disappeared beforehand ?

« He was taken out by Zabuza. »

Kakashi explained calmly, « I was worried Gato might pull some tricks, so I went to investigate and found out. »

« Some conflict arose between the two sides, and in a fit of rage, Zabuza simply dealt with Gato and all of his men. »

After hearing Kakashi’s words, Asakawa Shin became even more confused. He scratched his head, looking completely puzzled.

« A mercenary Ninja killing his employer ? Is Zabuza crazy ? Does he not plan to take jobs in the underworld anymore ? »

With a thought, he secretly sensed Zabuza’s Flying Thunder God Mark. That guy had already left the Land of Waves long ago.

« Forget it. Although I don’t know the specific reason, it seems this mission will indeed end peacefully. »

...

Time flew by. Without Gato’s interference, the bridge’s construction progressed extremely quickly. The main structure was basically complete, and a delicate toll booth had even been built at the bridgehead.

Asakawa Shin watched the scene before him with satisfaction. After the Konoha personnel completed the handover with Tazuna, he turned to his companions and asked :

« The mission in the Land of Waves has concluded perfectly. It’s time for us to head back. »

« There are two options now. One is to walk back to the Village as per tradition, completing the standard mission procedure. »

« The second is for me to use the Flying Thunder God Technique directly to teleport everyone back. What’s your choice ? »

Tsunade’s eyes lit up, and she immediately raised her hand high. « Need you even ask ? Teleport back, of course ! I can’t wait to get back to Konoha~ »

Her gaze swept across the group, and everyone nodded in agreement under her pressure.

Only Naruto looked reluctant ; after all, this was his first time out of the Village, and the novelty hadn’t worn off yet.

Following the principle of the minority submitting to the majority, Asakawa Shin signaled everyone to place their hands on him. With just a thought, they traversed through space and returned to Konoha Village.

Everyone felt a blur before their eyes, and then they saw the familiar Konoha gates.

Aside from the few who had already seen the Flying Thunder God and could remain calm, Sasuke, Sakura, and Haku were shocked, hardly able to believe their eyes.

« Is this the Flying Thunder God ? We walked for several days before, yet we crossed back in the blink of an eye ? »

Asakawa Shin waved his hand casually, looking reserved and humble. « This is, after all, an ultra-high-difficulty space-time Ninjutsu created by the Second Hokage and popularized by the Fourth Hokage. It’s only natural for it to have such an effect, right ? »

After saying that, he couldn’t help but glance at Naruto. He really couldn’t understand how someone as smart as Minato Namikaze could produce such a blockhead.

With Naruto’s level of intelligence, which was often lacking, he probably wouldn’t be able to learn the Flying Thunder God Technique in this lifetime. He’d likely only be able to make some meatballs to get by.

He hadn’t inherited any of the Uzumaki Clan’s bloodline abilities either...

Not to mention Mind’s Eye of the Kagura and Physical Revitalization ; even the Adamantine Sealing Chains is a high-level sealing technique that even the current Karin hasn’t mastered yet, let alone Naruto.

« Let’s go. Time to turn in the mission. »

The group walked briskly toward the Hokage Building and soon arrived before Hiruzen Sarutobi.

« Oh ? You’re back so soon ? »

« The mission was completed excellently this time. Not only did it bring great benefits to the Village, but it even involved a Ninja of Zabuza’s caliber... »

« Therefore, I’ve decided to upgrade this mission’s rank to S-rank. The mission rewards will be covered by the Village. Congratulations. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi rambled on for a while before his gaze slowly shifted to Haku, the satisfaction in his eyes deepening.

'As expected of my most prized disciple. Not only did he over-deliver on the mission, but he also brought back the precious Ice Release Bloodline Limit for Konoha.'

'Most importantly, the one he brought back this time is a boy. Now I can truly be at ease...'

Upon hearing this, Naruto immediately started dancing with excitement. « An S-rank mission ? I actually completed an S-rank mission so soon ? As expected of me ! »

Sasuke looked disdainful and mocked him directly, « Idiot. What does this mission have to do with you ? You just rode their coattails. »

« What did you say ? You looking for a fight ? » Naruto instantly bristled.

« Bring it on if you have the guts ! » Sasuke’s eyes were contemptuous.

Veins throbbed on Sakura’s forehead. Looking at these two, she felt a sense of powerlessness and thought to herself :

'Sometimes I really want to call the police and have these two bastards locked up in the Security Department. And they must be kept separate—one at the east end and one at the west end.'

Seeing this, Kakashi sighed helplessly and gave each of them a sharp rap on the head, finally restoring peace to the scene.

Hiruzen Sarutobi gave Kakashi an appreciative look before continuing :

« Haku, welcome to Konoha. From today on, you are an official Ninja of Konoha, temporarily ranked as a Genin, assigned to Tsunade’s squad. »

« The reason for this arrangement, as you’ve likely heard, is that the semi-annual Joint Chunin Exams will begin in a month. Besides the Hidden Sand Village, the Hidden Cloud Village will also be sending participants this time. »

Kakashi was very surprised to hear this. « Wait, why would the Hidden Cloud Village join in on the fun ? They shouldn’t be on the list. »

Tsunade nodded thoughtfully and turned her gaze to Asakawa Shin. « It’s likely because of you. The Raikage has seen your strength and knows you’re graduating this year. He probably wants to take the opportunity to scout you out. »

A look of disdain appeared on Asakawa Shin’s face, his tone arrogant. « Scout me out ? What a joke. No matter who it is, as long as they dare to hinder me from becoming a Chunin, I will crush them all without mercy. »

« Why are you so obsessed with becoming a Chunin ? »

« Because I crave progress. »

« Then wouldn’t it be better to just become the Hokage ? »

« No, I have OCD. I must start from Chunin. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi coughed twice, helplessly interrupting the conversation between the master and disciple.

He, the Hokage, was still alive and well. Why were they already discussing successors ?

« Anyway, Shin, Karin, and Haku, the three of you will form a squad and participate in the Chunin Exams together. »

« Since the Hidden Cloud Village actively requested to participate, let’s show them what we’ve got. This time, you must demonstrate the prestige of our Konoha ! »

A sharp light flashed in Asakawa Shin’s eyes as he nodded thoughtfully. « Demonstrate our prestige, right ? I think I completely understand... »

« Huh ? The Chunin Exams ? What’s that ? »
