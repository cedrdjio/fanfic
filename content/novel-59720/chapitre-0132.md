# Chapitre 132 — Wood Release: Wood Dragon Technique

« Adamantine Sealing Chains ! »

« Water Release : Thousand Flying Water Needles of Death ! »

« Waaaah ! »

Haku skillfully rummaged through the fallen Ninja for a moment, then pulled out a sealing scroll.

A flash of pleasant surprise appeared in his eyes, and he handed it to Asakawa Shin like a treasure : « Lord Shin, this is great ! It’s the Heaven Scroll ! Our mission is complete ! »

Asakawa Shin took the scroll in surprise : « Not bad luck, we managed to collect the scrolls after encountering only the second team. »

He tucked the scroll into his robe and turned his gaze toward Haku : « However, Haku, you are a bit too gentle... Every time you attack, you only knock your opponents unconscious ; that won’t do. In this chaotic world, being soft-hearted can be fatal. »

Haku’s earlobes turned slightly red, and he scratched his head in embarrassment, at a loss for words.

Beside him, Karin rolled her eyes dramatically.

This guy’s « feminine charm » was just too strong, so strong that even she felt a strange sense of crisis.

What if Lord Shin gets corrupted by this ? Any kind of change like that was not what she wanted to see.

She narrowed her eyes, walked up, and slapped Haku on the back, muttering continuously : « Haku ! Straighten your back ! You’re a boy, can’t you be a bit more masculine ? Walking around shyly with your head down all day, who are you doing that for ? »

Asakawa Shin’s eye twitched slightly, and he muttered to himself : 'Sometimes, I wish you two could just balance each other out.'

Suddenly, his figure paused slightly, his gaze piercing as he looked into the distance, a playful smile curling on his lips : « Oh ? Senior brother can’t wait any longer and wants to make a move ? Very well, we might as well go join in the fun. Karin, Haku, stop fooling around, stand steady. »

Before he finished speaking, he slammed his hands together, and the Chakra within him surged out like a tide, transforming into massive life force that poured into the forest beneath his feet.

In an instant, the earth trembled, and countless thick branches burst from the soil, growing, weaving, and intertwining madly, condensing into a ferocious, two-hundred-meter-long Wood Dragon in a very short amount of time.

'Oh ? The length and size of the Wood Dragon have almost doubled. Could it be because my physique has evolved again ?'

Asakawa Shin speculated to himself : 'And it’s still far from reaching its limit. If I were to activate Sage Mode and use my full power, the might would likely be even more astonishing.'

Haku, who had never seen such a shocking scene before, had his mouth open wide enough to fit an egg : « Is... is this Lord Shin’s Bloodline Limit ? The same Wood Release as the legendary God of Shinobi ! »

« Hold on tight, everyone, we’re setting off~ »

Meanwhile, Team 7 had just defeated a Ninja who was impersonating Naruto.

The opponent had made a mistake while using the Transformation Technique and failed to replicate the scar on Naruto’s face. It was this tiny flaw that exposed him, leading to his easy defeat by Sasuke.

To prevent similar situations from happening again, the three of them were sitting cross-legged, discussing a communication code.

Unbeknownst to them, a terrifying enemy had already set his sights on them and heard their code clearly.

« So that’s how it is. Fugaku’s son is indeed exceptionally intelligent. Unfortunately, it is precisely because you are so outstanding that you are more suitable to be my vessel. »

Before the voice faded, he had finished forming the hand signs and shouted in a low voice : « Wind Release : Great Breakthrough ! »

Violent air currents surged from his mouth, tearing the earth and forest in front of him to shreds, and rushed toward Sasuke and the others with lightning speed.

The sudden attack caught the three of them off guard. At the critical moment, Sasuke grabbed Sakura and used the Body Flicker technique to narrowly escape the range of the Wind Release attack.

Although they were still cut by the powerful wind pressure, they were not severely injured.

However, Naruto did not manage to escape this disaster. He was hit head-on by the violent wind pressure, and his entire body, like a kite with a broken string, was swept away by the storm and thrown into the distance, disappearing from sight.

On the other side, in the quiet forest, Kankuro swallowed hard and followed closely behind Gaara.

Although Gaara was his own younger brother, this taciturn and cruel-natured boy in front of him truly made him, as an older brother, feel a chill down his spine.

The five days and five nights of travel were, for Kankuro, as agonizing as being in purgatory.

Just then, Gaara, who was at the very front of the team, stopped without warning. Kankuro was so startled that he shuddered, almost jumping on the spot.

« What’s wrong ? Gaara ? Did you sense an enemy again ? »

« No... » Gaara’s pupils trembled ; it seemed he had discovered something incomprehensible.

« It’s Shukaku ! Shukaku... he is trembling... »

His gaze suddenly turned to the right : « Quick, hide ! Something... is coming ! »

Kankuro and Temari were shaken to the core upon hearing this, their faces full of disbelief. But instinct drove them, and the three of them flashed into the shadows almost simultaneously.

A few minutes later, the earth began to rumble and shake. In the horrified eyes of the three, a massive, two-hundred-meter-long Wood Dragon was rushing toward the west from several kilometers away with overwhelming power, pushing aside layers of forest.

In just a moment, the behemoth approached, its momentum so vast that it was chilling.

On top of the Wood Dragon, three figures stood prominently. Asakawa Shin stood in the center with his arms crossed, his gaze disdainful as he looked down at the area ahead.

Karin and Haku stood on his left and right, respectively, standing with dignity.

The Wood Dragon roared past, kicking up dust everywhere.

At that moment, Gaara’s heart tightened. He clearly felt that Asakawa Shin’s cold gaze had swept precisely over their hiding spot, and then moved away indifferently.

As if he had only caught a glimpse of insignificant dust.

Soon, the behemoth sped away in an instant, and the deafening roar gradually dissipated into the horizon.

The three of them finally breathed a sigh of relief, collapsing to the ground as if exhausted, their backs soaked through with cold sweat.

Temari’s face was deathly pale, and her voice couldn’t stop trembling : « That was... Wood Release ! That student of the Hokage actually possesses the legendary Wood Release Bloodline Limit ! No wonder... »

Kankuro wiped the cold sweat from his forehead in a sorry state, his soaked clothes clinging to his body, bringing a bone-chilling cold. He muttered to himself, still terrified : « To actually use this kind of world-destroying technique just for traveling ? That guy... just how strong is he ? »

Compared to his two companions who were still shaken, Gaara’s expression was as dark as if water were about to drip from it, his teeth clenched so hard that blood began to seep out. He stared fixedly at the direction where the Wood Dragon had disappeared, his eyes filled with a mix of humiliation and anger : « That look just now... he clearly discovered us long ago ! Why didn’t he kill us ? Does he think I’m not worthy of being his opponent at all ? Damn it ! »

He turned abruptly and resolutely chased after them.

« Wait ! Gaara, what are you doing ? »

Temari and Kankuro were shocked to hear this ; the two looked at each other, both seeing helplessness and bitterness in the other’s eyes. However, looking at their brother’s stubborn figure moving away, Temari finally gritted her teeth hard and started to run after him : « There’s no choice, let’s follow ! We absolutely cannot leave Gaara alone in a place like this ! »

The area where Naruto was located was Entrance 6, while Asakawa Shin had swept all the way here from the distant Entrance 42, crossing almost the entire Forest of Death.

This earth-shaking commotion instantly alerted all the examinees. Everyone was stunned, staring at the giant dragon that had broken through the sea of trees.

« Is that Shin ? So he is a Bloodline Limit holder ? And... it’s the legendary Wood Release ! It’s simply unbelievable ! »

« So that’s it, no wonder his physique is ridiculously strong, and no wonder there is a flower growing on his head ; it turns out it’s an anomaly caused by the Wood Release Bloodline Limit ! »

« Are you kidding me ? A monster of this level is still just a Genin ? Are we supposed to compete on the same stage as this kind of monster ? »

The three Ninjas from the Hidden Cloud Village also scratched their heads : « Does this count as completing the Raikage’s mission ? Asakawa Shin... he really can use Wood Release ! »
