# Chapitre 56 — Inner Sakura

The spectators were also shocked to see Sakura stand up so quickly, and they couldn’t help but start discussing among themselves :

« This girl’s resilience is incredible ! She’s spitting blood but looks like nothing happened... »

« Is she really okay ? That hit looked like it really hurt ! »

« Look, what’s wrong with her eyes ? Is that the Byakugan ? »

« How is that possible ? It looks more like her eyes rolled back into her head... »

« How could she still be standing if her eyes rolled back ? It must be some secret technique ! Looks like she’s showing her true strength now... »

...

On the field.

Might Guy looked at Sakura with a look of doubt and uncertainty. With his vast experience, he couldn’t have misjudged—the girl had clearly lost consciousness earlier ; he was even about to announce the match result.

But now she had stubbornly stood up again. Could he really have been mistaken ?

No... that’s not right...

At this moment, she probably still lacked consciousness. Although her eyes were wide open and fixed on her opponent, her pupils were dilated and dull, lacking focus, like a shell controlled by her subconscious.

'Interesting, how very interesting !' Hinata thought with a smile, eyeing Sakura with great interest. 'Is she the same type as me ? But she seems...'

« Hey, Sakura, can you still hear me ? » Hinata noticed something was off about Sakura’s state, so she waved her arm, trying to get her attention.

However, Sakura seemed completely immersed in her own world, turning a deaf ear to Hinata’s call.

Instead, she glared at Hinata, her entire being ignited by rage. She charged forward like an arrow released from a bow, shouting loudly :

« Hinata, you jerk, I will never forgive you ! »

« To make me lose face like this in front of Sasuke-kun ! I’ll teach you a lesson ! »

« Die ! »

« Huh ? » Hinata’s eyes flickered, a hint of surprise passing through them in an instant.

Sakura’s speed seemed to have increased slightly, but...

This level was still far from enough, especially with such a straightforward attack...

Hinata simply tapped the ground and retreated, easily dodging the strike.

Sakura failed to pull back her strength in time after the punch missed. Her fist slammed into the ground with a loud bang, creating a small crater about half a meter in diameter, sending dust flying everywhere.

« How is this possible ? How can Sakura have such great power ! » Hinata was slightly surprised by the destructive force of the move.

As a potential rival for the position of team leader, Hinata appeared confident on the surface, seemingly looking down on everyone.

However, Hinata usually paid a bit more attention to these potential rivals than others, and she knew Sakura’s training progress like the back of her hand.

Sakura definitely didn’t have such immense power. Therefore, the issue could only lie in this special state of hers.

...

Missing her strike, Sakura quickly got up and rushed forward without hesitation to launch another fierce attack on Hinata. Hinata, naturally unafraid, met Sakura’s offensive head-on, and the two engaged in a fierce exchange of blows.

Although Sakura gave it her all, she was clearly no match for Hinata and gradually began to show signs of defeat.

Soon, during an exchange of offense and defense, she failed to block Hinata’s fierce assault and was struck squarely on the cheek by Hinata’s left hook.

To Hinata’s surprise, however, Sakura only retreated half a step. Even though her neck audibly creaked, she pushed against the fist and, with lightning speed, swung a backhanded slap toward Hinata’s face.

Hinata was shocked and hastily leaped back to create distance, barely managing to avoid this unexpected counterattack.

'What is wrong with this girl ? I felt something was off just now...'

'The way she exerts force has clearly exceeded her body’s capacity ; her muscles and joints are already swollen and red.'

'She doesn’t even flinch when I hit her. Doesn’t she feel pain ?'

'No pain ? !' Suddenly, a flash of insight struck Hinata’s mind, making her realize :

'I see, I get it now...'

'In this state, because she’s lost consciousness, she has no sense of pain at all !'

'And because she’s lost pain as a restriction and protective mechanism, every strike can exert the body’s maximum power without any regard for whether the body can handle it.'

'That’s why she can exhibit strength and speed far beyond her normal levels, but this will also cause immense damage to her body !'

'Oh Sakura, you’re going to be in for quite a surprise when you wake up from this~'

...

Sakura didn’t think that much. Seeing her strike miss, she adjusted her direction and pounced toward Hinata like a raging beast :

« Hinata, how dare you mock me in front of Sasuke-kun ! »

« I’ll tear your mouth apart ! »

Hinata’s eye twitched. This girl was impossible to communicate with ! She was completely dominated by anger and Sasuke...

'Sasuke this, Sasuke that... has she really lost consciousness or not ? It’s so confusing !'

'But this fight has gone on long enough. If it continues, Sakura’s body probably won’t hold up.'

'Since this tigress, who only knows « Sasuke, Sasuke, » is leaping through the air and can’t dodge, I’ll just end this fight right here...'

With this thought, Hinata’s eyes turned cold, and she decided to stop holding back.

She lowered her stance, using a smooth sliding tackle to dodge Sakura’s pounce while positioning herself directly beneath her. Her body tightened like a coiled spring before she launched herself upward in fifth gear.

Under the stunned gaze of the crowd, she suddenly jerked her head up, delivering a sharp headbutt precisely to Sakura’s abdomen. The impact instantly doubled Sakura over like a shrimp and sent her flying high into the air.

« Waaaaah ! » Sakura was knocked over ten meters high, spitting blood in the air before crashing onto the ground like a broken doll, motionless.

'Holy crap ! Toad Style !' A certain commentator was also startled by the brutal scene and quickly signaled the referee to check if Sakura was still alive...

Might Guy was also startled and rushed over to check carefully, confirming repeatedly that Sakura wasn’t dead and that she truly wouldn’t be getting up again.

Might Guy breathed a small sigh of relief, cleared his throat, and announced loudly : « Sakura Haruno is unable to continue ! The winner is Hyuga Hinata ! »

The audience forgot to applaud this time, completely stunned by the fight between the two :

« So brutal ! Girls fighting is just too brutal ! I feel like those guys in the boys' group are no match for them at all ! »

« True, even Neji wouldn’t last a single slap against them... »

« Lady Hinata’s strength is terrifying ; she didn’t get hit once from start to finish. A flawless victory. »

« Sakura isn’t bad either. Look how much punishment she took ! If it were you, you’d have been down long ago ! »

« No matter how tough you are, you can’t take a beating forever. Especially that last hit—it was just too savage. I hope she’s okay... »
