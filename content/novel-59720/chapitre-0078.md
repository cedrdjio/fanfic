# Chapitre 78 — Something Terrible Has Happened!

« You bastard brat ! How dare you play me ? I’m going to kill you ! »

After realizing what happened, the Nine-Tailed flew into a rage. The entire fox transformed into a streak of crimson light, ferociously pouncing toward the seal’s gate. A sharp claw pierced through the gap in the gate, striking toward Asakawa Shin like lightning.

« Hmm ? You dare bare your fangs ? »

Facing this swift and fierce attack, Asakawa Shin didn’t even blink. His expression was filled with disdain as he quickly formed hand seals and shouted :

« Wood Release : Deep Forest Emergence ! »

In an instant, countless trees burst from the ground, sprouting branches and leaves. Within a short time, they grew into a dense forest. Their branches, like giant pythons, tightly coiled around the Nine-Tailed’s body, rendering it unable to move.

That ferocious claw stopped abruptly just a few centimeters from Asakawa Shin’s face, firmly trapped by the interlocking trees, unable to advance even a fraction further.

« Nani ? Wood Release ? You actually possess the Wood Release Bloodline Limit ? Dammit, you stinking brat, let me go ! I’m going to kill you. »

The Nine-Tailed was somewhat incredulous. Seeing Asakawa Shin’s body so close to the gate, it had thought there was an opportunity to severely damage his consciousness, but it hadn’t expected to be firmly blocked by the suddenly appearing Wood Release.

Asakawa Shin glanced at it indifferently. The Nine-Tailed was now bound tightly, like a fox-stuffed zongzi. He shook his head helplessly :

« Look at you, getting impatient again. Why do you always interrupt me ? You’re so naughty ; can’t you just listen properly ? »

« Bastard, how dare you look down on me ! »

The violence in the Nine-Tailed’s eyes flashed and vanished. It used all its strength, struggling desperately to break free. The tree trunks wrapped around its body groaned under the strain, and with sheer brute force, it actually managed to snap them apart.

In its rage, the Nine-Tailed didn’t hesitate to pounce at Asakawa Shin again the moment it broke free.

Asakawa Shin looked at it with some admiration. To be able to snap the restraints of Wood Release—it was truly worthy of being the Nine-Tailed. He nimbly dodged backward, instantly moving out of the Nine-Tailed’s attack range.

The Nine-Tailed roared furiously : « You bastard brat, if you’ve got guts, don’t run ! Fight me, Lord Nine-Tailed, to the death ! »

A hint of amusement flashed in Asakawa Shin’s eyes, and he couldn’t help but chuckle :

« Run ? Do I need to run ? It seems a beast is indeed just a beast ; not only can it not see the situation clearly, but it’s also fundamentally incapable of communication. Very well, since words won’t work, I’ll just have to beat you into submission. Perhaps then you’ll be able to clear your head. »

Killing intent surged in the Nine-Tailed’s heart. Even though so many years had passed, hearing the word « beast » instantly brought Uchiha Madara to mind.

That bastard’s contemptuous tone was exactly the same as this brat’s before him ; both seemed to view it as a wild animal that could be crushed at will.

The Nine-Tailed was so angry now that it was on the verge of losing its sanity. Driven by rage, its Chakra erupted violently, causing the entire sealed space to tremble intensely.

A hint of impatience flashed in Asakawa Shin’s eyes : « If I let you keep making a scene, it’ll disturb Naruto’s sleep, right ? Better to settle this quickly ! »

Having made his decision, he didn’t hesitate. He clapped his hands together and instantly entered Sage Mode, shouting at the Nine-Tailed : « Sage Art : Myojinmon ! »

From the sky, ten torii-like structures were summoned, slamming down onto the Nine-Tailed.

The speed was so fast that there was no time to react. In a single moment, all nine of its tails and its neck were pinned to the ground, rendering it immobile.

That wasn’t all. Asakawa Shin didn’t give it a chance to catch its breath. He clapped his hands again and shouted : « Wood Release : Wood Dragon Technique ! »

Driven by a massive amount of Chakra, the forest that had been destroyed by the Nine-Tailed regained its vitality, began to grow, intertwine, and finally transformed into a giant dragon over a hundred meters long, charging toward the Nine-Tailed with majestic momentum to coil around it.

The moment the Wood Dragon made contact with the Nine-Tailed, it began to frantically drain its Chakra. In just a short while, the intensity of the Nine-Tailed’s struggles weakened significantly.

Seeing the Nine-Tailed defeated so quickly, a trace of a smile flashed in Asakawa Shin’s eyes. This was the charm of elemental suppression.

The Nine-Tailed still hadn’t given up and was struggling, but it was destined to be a futile effort.

Myojinmon was a sealing technique capable of briefly suppressing the Ten-Tails, let alone just half of the Nine-Tailed. Furthermore, Wood Release was the ultimate authority, keeping the Tailed Beast firmly in check.

Under this silky-smooth combo, the Nine-Tailed quickly lost the ability to resist.

It panted heavily, its gaze fixed firmly on Asakawa Shin :

« Damn brat, you’re actually a Wood Release user. No wonder, no wonder you give me the same disgusting feeling as Senju Hashirama. Dammit, if I were at my peak, how could I have lost to you ? »

Asakawa Shin rolled his eyes upon hearing this and scoffed disdainfully : « You didn’t use your full power, but did I get serious ? You’re really a sore loser. »

The Nine-Tailed’s cursing came to an abrupt halt. It finally realized that from beginning to end, Asakawa Shin had only moved once and clapped his hands a few times ; he hadn’t even broken a sweat.

Could this be another monster like Senju Hashirama ?

Thinking of that towering, heaven-shaking figure, its eyes cleared up slightly, and its tone lowered :

« *Ahem*, brat, what exactly did you come here for ? With your strength, you shouldn’t need to use my power, right ? »

A hint of playfulness flashed in Asakawa Shin’s eyes. Finally ready to talk properly ? Then let’s continue...

« Nine-Tailed, don’t be so nervous, I don’t mean any harm... I’m visiting uninvited today because I want to borrow a bit of your Chakra. You shouldn’t be that stingy, right ? Hmm ? Nine-Tailed, why aren’t you saying anything ? If you don’t speak, I’ll take that as consent. Then, I won’t be polite. »

Without waiting for the Nine-Tailed’s angry glare, he calmly formed a few hand seals, easily passed through the gate, and sat cross-legged on the Nine-Tailed’s back.

« Truly worthy of being a genuine leather cushion ; it feels quite nice. Uh, business first. Well then... I’m starting. »

Asakawa Shin held his breath and focused, concentrating fully on extracting the Nine-Tailed’s Chakra.

As this powerful energy surged into his body, it immediately triggered a strong resonance.

The cells in his body seemed to have been awakened, eagerly devouring this energy.

The long-dormant wheel of evolution slowly began to turn again. Like a chrysalis breaking out of its cocoon, he took a big step forward.

His heart stirred, and a trace of joy welled up. As long as it’s effective !

As long as there is a direction, even if the process is fraught with dangers and difficulties, he is confident in continuing to push forward.

Meanwhile, the Nine-Tailed was plunged into panic ; the Chakra inside its body was rapidly flowing away.

What terrified it even more was that the lost Chakra was not regenerating as it had in the past. It was as if its ownership had been completely stripped away, permanently erased from its existence.

Asakawa Shin, sitting on its back, felt like a towering mountain in its perception, so heavy that it couldn’t catch its breath.

Even more bizarrely, the sensation of having its Chakra extracted felt like being preyed upon by an invisible giant beast, and the food was none other than itself.

'Am I... being eaten ?'

The Nine-Tailed was horrified. Fortunately, this bizarre ritual ended quickly, and its Chakra had been permanently reduced by one-tenth.

Asakawa Shin came before the Nine-Tailed with a satisfied expression and released the restraints :

« Thanks for the treat, Nine-Tailed. This Chakra is very important to me. Consider it a favor I owe you ; I will definitely repay you in the future. »

Upon hearing Asakawa Shin say this, a trace of surprise flashed in the Nine-Tailed’s eyes, and its expression improved quite a bit.

It scrutinized Asakawa Shin carefully and suddenly felt that this brat was much better than that bastard Uchiha Madara, and not so annoying to a fox after all.

Asakawa Shin greeted the Nine-Tailed and left the sealed space.

He felt extremely exhausted at this moment ; his head was heavy and dizzy, and he could barely open his eyes.

Indeed, shortly after his consciousness returned, he fell into a deep sleep, and was only awakened by Naruto’s exclamation early in the morning.

« Shin, wake up, something terrible has happened ! You... you’ve sprouted ! »
