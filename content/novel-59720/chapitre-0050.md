# Chapitre 50 — Karin Enrolls, Part 2

« What ? What did she say her interests and dreams were ? Did I hear that right just now... »

« I don’t think you misheard, because I heard it too... »

« But how can someone’s interest and dream be to be a dog for someone else ? »

« But if it’s Shin-kun, I feel like it wouldn’t be impossible... »

« Hmm ? Who ? Who was speaking just now ? »

Uchiha Sasuke slammed the table and laughed so hard that tears came out : « As expected... the Uzumaki Clan never disappoints me, hahahaha. »

Iruka was already sweating profusely. Damn that Hiruzen Sarutobi, he sent him another problem child. Did he not think Iruka was tired enough already ?

He quickly wiped the cold sweat from his forehead and said loudly to everyone, « Ha, haha, Karin-san has quite the sense of humor. Everyone will definitely remember her now, right ?

You could all learn a thing or two from this little bit of cleverness... »

« Learn what ? Learn how to be a dog ? »

As soon as those words were spoken, the classroom was filled with a joyful atmosphere...

« Woof ! » Akamaru was a bit unhappy. What’s wrong with being a dog ? Did I eat your rice ? How rude...

Uzumaki Karin was also very unhappy. She grumbled at Iruka in dissatisfaction, « What humor ? I’m serious ! Don’t look down on my dream, you bastard ! »

Asakawa Shin was feeling a bit restless at the moment. He could clearly feel many people in the class stealing glances at him with strange eyes, as if he were some kind of pervert. Even with his thick skin, he was starting to feel embarrassed.

« Uh... Karin, that’s enough. Come over and sit down. I’ll introduce my friends to you later...

And you bastards, what are you looking at ! Karin was using a metaphor, do you understand ? »

Karin’s eyes lit up when she heard this, and she bowed. « Yes, Lord Shin ! I’m coming right now. »

After saying that, she jogged all the way and sat down in the row in front of Asakawa Shin. The only empty seat near Asakawa Shin was the one where Sasuke had sat before.

She raised her hand and asked Iruka, « Teacher, can I sit here ? »

Iruka’s eye twitched. How could he dare to disagree ? Otherwise, who knows what this bratty girl would pull next. He nodded in resignation.

« Yay, great ! It’s so close to Lord Shin, I can even smell the scent on Lord Shin’s body~ »

Karin showed an intoxicated smile and gave Iruka an appreciative look.

Hyuga Hinata watched this scene with shining eyes :

'That’s it, that’s exactly it... That’s how I want to train Naruto !

Shin is truly an unparalleled genius ; he’s quite capable. And speaking of which, the Uzumaki Clan really has the potential to be dogs.

Also, it turns out this woman isn’t a love rival, but an ally. That’s wonderful.'

Asakawa Shin adjusted his mindset, turned to the side, and began to take credit with Uzumaki Naruto :

« Look, Naruto, this is the surprise I brought back for you from outside the Village. This is a member of your Uzumaki Clan.

Now you won’t be alone in the Village. Are you happy ? »

Naruto looked at Asakawa Shin in disbelief, then glanced at Karin, who was still in a trance :

« Are you sure this is a surprise and not a shock ? If today’s events get out, how can I ever show my face again...

It’s over, our Uzumaki Clan’s reputation is finished... »

Asakawa Shin nodded with satisfaction. It wasn’t in vain that he spent so much time and effort bringing Karin and her mother to Konoha.

As long as Naruto is happy. Look, he’s so happy he’s lost his mind. Reputation ? Since when did your Uzumaki Clan have such a thing ?

The class ended in an absurd atmosphere. As soon as it was over, Ino Yamanaka aggressively went to find Karin.

« So you’re Uzumaki Karin ? Come with me to the restroom for a moment. »

After saying that, she turned around elegantly, leaving Karin with a cool view of her back.

Karin looked at Ino’s departing back in confusion. Is this how girls in Konoha ask their classmates to go to the restroom together ?

It’s actually quite cool, it really has the aura of a Great Village.

« Lord Shin, I think I’ve made a friend, it’s that... »

Karin turned around to brag to Asakawa Shin, only to see that Ino was almost out of sight. In a panic, she hurried to catch up.

« Lord Shin, we’ll talk later, I’m going to the restroom with my friend. »

...

The girls' restroom.

« Hello, I’m Karin. I was so happy just now that I forgot to ask your name. »

Ino looked at Karin with some surprise. Since when did people become so polite when picking a fight ?

'Oh, I see. This must be a mutual introduction between combatants, right ?

And she’s actually happy. Is she the type who gets excited at the thought of a fight ?

I’d better be careful.'

So Ino cleared her throat and introduced herself solemnly : « I am Ino Yamanaka. Based on your introduction earlier, you like Shin-kun too, don’t you ?

Then we are rivals. Today, I’m definitely going to... »

Her harsh words were interrupted by Karin’s exclamation : « Eh ? Ino likes Lord Shin too ? How far have you two progressed ? »

Karin had to be cautious. If Ino was Lord Shin’s girlfriend, wouldn’t she be the Mistress of the house ?

She had to handle this carefully...

« What do you mean, how far ? »

« You know, kissing, hugging, being swept off your feet ? How far have you gone ? »

« No... none of that... »

Karin looked at Ino with a look of disappointment : « So it hasn’t even started yet ? Ino, you’re too useless.

Since ancient times, blondes have often been the losing dogs. Ino, you have to work harder ! »

Ino was startled. There was actually such a saying ? She subconsciously touched her hair, a small gesture that Karin noticed.

Karin thought about it. Ino was, after all, the first friend she had made in Konoha, so she might as well help her. Karin said to Ino with a meaningful look :

« Ino, you haven’t even done the first step right. How can you win Lord Shin’s heart ?

Do you know what kind of girls Lord Shin likes ?

You don’t, right ? Let me tell you. Lord Shin likes girls with long hair. Your short hair is a major deduction... »

« Eh ? Long hair ? Why ? » Ino touched her smooth short hair, looking like she had been struck by lightning.

Karin shook her head with a self-satisfied look : « Stupid Ino, although Lord Shin has never said it.

But Lord Shin’s mother, Lady Hanako, had jet-black hair that reached her waist.

Boys naturally have a favorable impression of girls with the same hairstyle as their mother. »

« I see, so that’s how it is ! Karin, you’re too smart... No... wait.

Karin, why are you telling me all this ? Don’t you like Shin-kun too ? » Ino looked wary, as if she wasn’t easily fooled.

Karin patted her chest and said proudly :

« I am Lord Shin’s maidservant, so of course I have to consider Lord Shin’s interests in everything.

Whether Lord Shin has a girlfriend or how many he has doesn’t matter to me, as long as Lord Shin likes them.

It’s more important that we all live a good life together ! »
