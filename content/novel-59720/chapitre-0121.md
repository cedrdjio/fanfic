# Chapitre 121 — Demon Brothers

The journey was quite boring.

Kakashi cast his gaze upon the group of students before him, who were stepping out of the Village for the first time, their eyes filled with a mix of innocence and foolishness.

He cleared his throat, uncharacteristically setting aside his usual laziness to explain the layout and general knowledge of the various Great Ninja Villages.

The group listened with great interest, and Sakura couldn’t help but ask :

« Kakashi-sensei, are there Ninjas in the Land of Waves where we’re going ? »

Kakashi glanced at Sakura, seemingly sensing something. He suddenly stopped and ruffled the girl’s hair :

« Don’t worry, Sakura. The Land of Waves doesn’t have a Ninja Village ; it’s just a remote small island nation.

Moreover, C-rank missions are mostly just simple escort jobs and usually don’t involve life-and-death duels between Ninjas.

Rest assured, you won’t be in any danger on this trip. »

Sakura clearly relaxed a lot : « That’s great, then I don’t have to worry about running into foreign Ninjas... »

« Of course, » Kakashi replied smoothly.

Hearing this, Tazuna trembled. To avoid drawing attention, he hurriedly lowered his head, a few drops of cold sweat even beading on his brow.

He thought his disguise was flawless, but in reality, many in the group had already noticed his abnormality and exchanged meaningful looks with each other.

Even Sasuke narrowed his eyes : « Tsk, it seems this mission isn’t simple at all. This is getting more and more interesting. »

Seeing that Naruto was completely oblivious to it gave him a slight sense of superiority.

Sensing that this trip might have some twists and turns, Sasuke was extra vigilant during the journey.

Sure enough, only a few hours later, he noticed something strange.

Even though the weather was very hot and it hadn’t rained in the last few days, a small puddle abruptly appeared on the dry road ahead.

Kakashi had clearly noticed this detail as well, but he only gave it a faint glance, his peripheral vision seemingly sweeping over Sasuke.

That one look made Sasuke’s heart skip a beat : 'Kakashi-sensei has clearly noticed the anomaly. Is he signaling me not to act rashly ?'

He then proceeded as if he knew nothing, continuing forward with a composed expression.

Just after everyone passed the puddle, things indeed took a turn.

Ripples spread across the water’s surface as two figures rose from it like ghosts.

The newcomers were rogue Ninjas from the Hidden Mist Village—Meizu and Gozu, known as the « Demon Brothers. »

In a flash, Meizu suddenly threw Gozu forward, while he himself turned into an afterimage following close behind.

With the chemistry built over years of cooperation, the chains in the brothers' hands turned into man-eating vipers. They launched a surprise attack on Kakashi, who was bringing up the rear, instantly binding him tightly so he couldn’t move.

That wasn’t all ; the two brothers looked at each other, their lips curling into a cruel sneer.

As their muscles tightened and exerted force, Kakashi’s body was actually torn to pieces by their chains like an assembly toy.

In an instant, blood rained down and limbs scattered ; the scene was extremely bloody.

« The first one. »

The Demon Brothers declared with such confidence.

Witnessing their teacher, who had been gently instructing them just moments ago, die a tragic death on the spot.

Sakura’s fear and grief reached their peak in an instant, and she let out a heart-wrenching scream.

Naruto was also panicking, standing dazed on the spot, staring in shock at Kakashi’s « corpse. »

'Are they really just two greenhorn brats ?'

The Demon Brothers didn’t bother with them at all, instead dashing straight toward Asakawa Shin.

The reason was simple : in the group, Kakashi, the only adult male, had already fallen.

The rest were either women, the elderly, or the weak. Only Asakawa Shin, who was precociously developed, stood out in the crowd.

« You’re next, the second one ! »

The chains coiled around Asakawa Shin like venomous snakes. With a tooth-grinding grinding sound, his body was also torn into fragments in an instant.

« Nani ? Even Shin... »

Only then did Asakawa Shin’s head, which had rolled to the ground but remained intact, seem to come to its senses :

« Haha, so this is what it feels like to have your body torn apart ? This unique perspective is actually quite interesting. »

« Nani ? He can still talk even after being turned into this ? »

Seeing the looks of horror in everyone’s eyes, Asakawa Shin finally satisfied his mischievous streak and reined it in.

A violent burst of Haoshoku Haki suddenly erupted from his eyes, striking straight into the depths of the Demon Brothers' souls.

The two’s brains instantly shut down, and they fell flat on their faces while still in their lunging posture.

The others in the group felt only a light breeze as the battle came to an abrupt halt in the blink of an eye.

Asakawa Shin gave a soft snort, and his head rolled casually on the ground. His scattered body parts automatically returned to their positions and reassembled like precision machinery ; even his clothes were intact.

Sasuke put away his shuriken in a daze, his eye twitching uncontrollably.

He had already planned how to show off, but he didn’t expect Asakawa Shin to give him no chance at all.

At this moment, Kakashi also walked slowly out from the shadow of a large tree with a look of amazement on his face :

« As expected of you, Shin.

The gap between us is really getting wider and wider. I can’t even understand what kind of technique you’re using.

Is this even Ninjutsu anymore ? »

Sakura shouted in surprise : « Kakashi-sensei, so you’re okay too ! »

Naruto rolled his eyes : « Actually, the moment I saw Shin get shredded, I figured it out. It was a Body Replacement Technique, wasn’t it ? »

Asakawa Shin moved his body casually but felt his arm was in an awkward position. With a « pop, » he pulled off his arm, readjusted it to a more comfortable angle, and reattached it. Only then did he wave his hand dismissively :

« Oh, it’s just a small trick, nothing useful...

You know I’m a Medical Ninja, so it’s normal to have some special methods, right ? Isn’t that so, Senior Sister Tsunade ? »

Tsunade didn’t respond immediately, as if she had only just snapped out of her shock.

Trembling, she felt Asakawa Shin’s head with both hands. After confirming the bastard was indeed unharmed, she gave him a sharp rap on the head :

« You bastard, you scared the life out of me ! I really thought you were shredded into pieces...

Wait, you clearly were shredded just now, right ? What on earth happened ? »

Seeing the worry in Tsunade’s eyes, Asakawa Shin also felt a bit embarrassed.

He scratched his head and gave a silly smile : « This is actually a new technique I developed, something like an Immortal Body effect.

I just wanted to test its performance in actual combat, so I gave up on defending...

Well, okay, I didn’t think it through. I’m sorry, Senior Sister. »

« Immortal Body ? Is it that stuff preached by the Jashin Cult ? »

« Not at all, it’s the Holy Fire Meow Meow Cult... Pfft ! It has nothing to do with religion.

It’s literal. Your junior brother is awesome now ; it’s hard for me to even die. »
