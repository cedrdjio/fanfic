# Chapitre 19 — The Strategist

Hokage Office.

« Little Shin, coming to see your teacher so late ? Have you had dinner ? » Hiruzen Sarutobi asked kindly while sitting in his chair.

« Teacher, I have eaten. I came this time because I have some questions about my cultivation that I would like to ask you. »

« The Wood Release scroll has some techniques involving profound Fuinjutsu that I don’t understand. There are also a few techniques that require a power called Sage Arts, and I have no clue about these Sage Arts either, so I came to ask you. »

« However, the other techniques are easy enough, and I have mastered them, though some consume a lot of Chakra, especially the Wood Human Technique. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi was not surprised. The commotion from Asakawa Shin’s training today was huge ; Anbu Ninja from the underground training ground had already reported it. Originally, he had felt a bit dejected that his student was progressing too fast, making him feel a bit redundant, but now an opportunity had arrived.

« Hahaha, then you have asked the right person, my good student. The First Hokage’s Wood Release indeed utilizes a lot of Fuinjutsu. That is because his wife, Mito, was from the Uzumaki Clan and was very skilled in Fuinjutsu, so he borrowed much from it. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi took another scroll from a hidden compartment in the bookshelf and handed it to Asakawa Shin.

« This is the Uzumaki Clan’s Fuinjutsu scroll. It contains many powerful and practical Fuinjutsu. Start from the easiest and practice in order. If there is anything you don’t understand, you can come and ask me. »

« As for Sage Arts ? Little Shin, you should know that Chakra is formed by the fusion of physical energy and mental energy. »

« What is called Sage Arts is fusing Natural Energy with these two in a certain proportion, thereby producing Sage Chakra. »

« This Sage Chakra is more powerful than ordinary Chakra, but it is also very dangerous. Your senior, Jiraiya, has some research in this area. I will write to notify him to come back. You can ask him for advice, but before that, absolutely do not experiment recklessly. Remember that. » Hiruzen Sarutobi said solemnly to Asakawa Shin.

Asakawa Shin nodded. He knew the dangers of practicing Sage Arts and would not try it lightly. It would not be too late to practice after learning more from his cheap senior, Jiraiya. It would be best to visit Mount Myōboku.

« Teacher, I understand. You should rest early too. It is almost ten o’clock ; why are there still so many files ? » Asakawa Shin glanced at the desk and frowned.

Being Hokage was more tiring than being a beast of burden in his past life. Naruto would be the same ; after becoming the Seventh Hokage, he wouldn’t even have time to attend his own children’s birthdays.

This wouldn’t do. He knew he would be Hokage in the future, and he didn’t want to be a beast of burden. Although he only wanted to be Hokage for a while, just until he finished the mission rewards, then he would pass it to Naruto.

At the same time, he was curious. Although Konoha was known as one of the Five Great Hidden Villages, it was just a Village in the end. Where did so much business to handle come from ?

« Teacher, can I take a look ? » Asakawa Shin asked, pointing at the pile of files.

Hiruzen Sarutobi nodded. Of his four disciples, one had notoriously betrayed the Village, one suffered from hemophobia and was gambling and running from debts across the Ninja World, and one chased after the so-called Child of Prophecy and never returned to Konoha without a reason—that was fine, but the erotic books he wrote were selling well in the Ninja World. He could say his reputation in the education sector was ruined and he had become a laughingstock. Now, only one seedling remained.

Hiruzen Sarutobi already viewed Asakawa Shin as the hope for Konoha’s future and the only candidate for the Fifth Hokage. Letting Asakawa Shin learn about the Hokage’s work in advance was a good training opportunity.

Asakawa Shin picked up a stack of files and started reviewing them. After a short while, he frowned.

Good grief, handling everything from big to small, no wonder you are busy ! No wonder Naruto would be overwhelmed in the future even with Shadow Clone...

And holding power tightly without delegating to subordinates—no wonder a few years later, when Orochimaru came for him, not a single old subordinate came to help.

Shin flipped through the files. Only a small part involved important matters for various departments that required the Hokage’s personal review. The rest were trivial matters.

For example, the Daimyo’s wife lost her cat, requiring a mission to find it. Just post the mission ! Does the Hokage need to decide such a small thing ?

For example, a villager complained about the Konoha Military Police Force enforcing the law too strictly. He was just drunk and accidentally burned the boss’s shop. He had apologized, yet he was still detained for seven days and had to pay a large sum of money.

Asakawa Shin couldn’t help but twitch his mouth when he saw this. This kind of unreasonable person dared to complain ? Keeping him for three to five years would be fair. How could such an obviously unreasonable complaint be sent to the Hokage for personal handling ?

For example, the personnel change submitted by the Konoha Hospital director. The original head of the medical examination center was retiring soon, and the work would be taken over by Hanako Asakawa.

The position of head of the medical examination center was important, but not that important. The hospital should decide it internally. Why send it to the highest administration... Wait ? Hanako Asakawa ?

Hmm, this director is quite sensible. He knows how to play the game.

Ahem, in summary, at least 80% of the matters were trivial and didn’t need the Hokage, the highest authority, to handle personally.

« Teacher, many of these are small matters, right ? There’s no need for you to handle them personally, right ? »

« Little Shin, you don’t understand. If you let others handle them, the Hokage’s power will be dispersed. Over time, who could tell who the Hokage is ? »

Hiruzen Sarutobi’s concern had some merit. If he handed over the three things mentioned above to others, he would lose the power to issue low-level missions, the power to handle complaints and control departments, and the power to intervene in lower-level personnel adjustments.

« Then design a system. Find more people, distribute power to them, and then distribute the power in their hands to even more people, thereby supervising and restricting each other to form a balance of power. It won’t threaten the Hokage ; instead, it will strengthen the Hokage’s power. After all, each of them needs the Hokage’s support. »

« Taking a step back, as long as you hold the armed forces firmly in your hands, you will be invincible. As long as you unite the majority, even if a small minority is disobedient, it won’t hurt. Just divide, win over, and suppress them. »

« Besides, Teacher, I will stand behind you and support you forever. If our teacher and student are of one mind, what is there to fear ? »

Hiruzen Sarutobi was stunned, wishing he could pull out a pen and paper to write it down. He had wanted to educate Asakawa Shin, but he hadn’t expected to be educated in return.

« Then how do you say this system should be changed ? » Hiruzen Sarutobi asked humbly.

Asakawa Shin rolled his eyes and said : « Teacher, I’m only six years old ; how would I know so much ? But I recommend one person to you : Nara Shikaku. »

« He is a smart man. If you talk to him, he will know what to do. And the Ino–Shika–Chō trio is a single entity. These three clans have always been firm supporters of the Hokage lineage. Their loyalty is unquestionable. You can build a team with them as the core. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi nodded. He had always valued Nara Shikaku highly, even appointing him as the Jonin Commander. He was very satisfied with this candidate.

« In addition, the Uchiha Clan is the cornerstone of Konoha’s founding. Although their clan has always been controversial, there are many inheritors of the will of fire within the clan. »

« Didn’t the Second Hokage take Uchiha Kagami as a disciple back then ? It shows that the Second Hokage also recognized Uchiha Kagami’s will of fire. Why doesn’t Teacher follow suit ? »

Hiruzen Sarutobi nodded again upon hearing this. Uchiha Kagami was his companion and indeed an Uchiha full of the will of fire. Thinking of Kagami, he couldn’t help but think of a young man, Uchiha Shisui, Kagami’s descendant and also a trustworthy Uchiha.

« Teacher can choose a trustworthy candidate from the Uchiha to be given heavy responsibility. Not only can this appease the Uchiha’s dissatisfaction, but it can also set them up against the Uchiha Clan’s leader, Uchiha Fugaku. We don’t even need to make a move ; they will disintegrate internally. »

« Excellent ! »
