# Chapitre 110 — Tactics

« Quick ! Medical corps ! Lord Kazekage is severely injured and needs emergency treatment ! »

Following an urgent shout, the medical corps rushed to the scene and immediately began emergency treatment on Rasa.

The surrounding Ninjas were on high alert, forming a tight circle with tense expressions, vigilantly confronting this madman who dared to break into the Hidden Sand Village alone.

However, Asakawa Shin completely ignored these hostile gazes.

He knew that Rasa was severely injured and unable to fight back. With a quick glance, he locked onto Gaara, who was not far away, and launched a sudden assault.

The steam device on his back let out a deafening roar, like a locomotive accelerating at full speed. Amidst the surging white mist, he was instantly propelled to extreme speed.

With a flash of an afterimage, Asakawa Shin broke through layers of defenses. The Ninjas blocking him were sent flying by the massive impact before they could even react.

In an instant, he had closed in on Gaara.

Seeing this, several Ninjas near Gaara desperately tried to stop him, but they were swept off their feet by Asakawa Shin’s « Konoha Great Whirlwind, » left utterly powerless.

Asakawa Shin then walked up to Gaara with a leisurely pace, easily lifting the young boy with one hand like a sack of grain and slinging him over his shoulder.

Then, in this awkward posture, he actually began to secretly drain the Chakra of the One-Tail Shukaku.

The surrounding Sunagakure Ninjas were all horrified, their hearts filled with confusion and fear. What did this audacious intruder want by seizing the Jinchuriki ?

Under the emergency treatment of the medical corps, Rasa’s injuries had slightly improved, and he could barely stand steady.

He pushed aside the crowd blocking him, completely ignoring the dissuasion of the nearby Anbu Ninja, and walked straight to « Han, » questioning him sternly :

« Han ! What exactly is your purpose for breaking into the Hidden Sand Village ? Not only did you ambush and severely injure me, but now you want to seize the Tailed Beast Jinchuriki ? Is your Iwagakure village determined to start a war ? »

Asakawa Shin waved his hand and spoke the truth : « Actually, I’m not Han. Lord Kazekage, didn’t you see that 'Konoha Great Whirlwind' move just now ?

My true identity is actually a Konoha Ninja. I have nothing to do with Iwagakure... »

Before he could finish, he was crudely interrupted by Rasa. At this moment, Rasa was trembling with rage, his eyes fixed on the enemy in front of him as he roared angrily :

« Are you joking ! Konoha is our ally ; why would they attack us for no reason ? Don’t even think about using such clumsy lies to sow discord !

Furthermore, the Ninjutsu you just performed can clearly only be achieved with the help of the Five-Tails' Chakra.

Stop playing tricks ! Speak ! What are Ōnoki’s intentions in sending you to the Sand ? »

Asakawa Shin gave a helpless shrug. Seeing that Rasa was completely unmoved, his expression suddenly turned cold, and the « disguise » on his face faded, smoothly transitioning into a cold smirk.

He no longer hid it, saying in a low, raspy voice, « Heh, I didn’t expect to be seen through by you after all. Fine, I’ll stop pretending and show my hand.

I am indeed the elite Jonin of Iwagakure, the Five-Tails Jinchuriki, known as 'Han the Steam Ninja.'

I came here to give your Hidden Sand Village a profound lesson. As for the reason... as the Kazekage, you should know very well in your heart, right ? »

Rasa’s pupils contracted sharply upon hearing this, and he began to calculate rapidly in his mind :

'Which move revealed a flaw and led Han to come to my door personally ? Damn it, with so many secrets passing through my hands, how should I know which one you’re talking about ?'

For a moment, a trace of imperceptible guilt and panic welled up in Rasa’s heart, but in the next instant, a fierce light flashed in his eyes.

Regardless of which matter it was for, since this kid had inexplicably delivered himself to the door, there was no reason to let the tiger return to the mountain !

This was a great opportunity to weaken the strength of Iwagakure !

Just as tensions were at a breaking point, dust flew in the distance as a large number of Sunagakure Ninjas quickly closed in.

Standing at the very front of the group were two people : Chiyo and Ebizo.

Seeing the reinforcements arrive, Rasa’s tense nerves finally relaxed slightly.

In his current state of being severely injured and unrecovered, he had no chance of winning against « Han the Steam Ninja » alone, but now with these two seniors overseeing the situation, the circumstances were quite different.

Chiyo and Ebizo exchanged a look, then turned to Rasa and asked in a deep voice :

« We have already patrolled the Hidden Sand Village’s defensive lines, and there are no signs of a large-scale invasion nearby. It seems Han is alone in this raid.

Rasa, you are the Kazekage. What do you plan to do next ? »

Asakawa Shin’s eyebrows twitched slightly upon hearing this. He reached out and patted the small bottom of Gaara on his shoulder, then gave Rasa a playful smile and asked slowly :

« Rasa, you better think this through. This is your biological son, the One-Tail Jinchuriki of the Hidden Sand Village.

As the saying goes, even a vicious tiger does not eat its cubs. You wouldn’t be so heartless as to abandon even your own son, would you ? »

Rasa’s face changed several times, and finally, under the burning gaze of all the Ninjas in the village, it turned into a look of resolute determination.

His tone was filled with grief, yet his voice was shockingly high-pitched : « That’s right, Gaara is my biological son, and my love for him is beyond doubt !

But... I am not only his father, I am also the Kazekage of the Hidden Sand Village !

What I must protect is not just my own small family, but the peace of the thousands upon thousands of families in the Hidden Sand Village. This is my inescapable responsibility as the Kazekage !

Although this decision is incredibly difficult, I have no choice. Hear my command : kill without mercy !

If he dares to use Gaara as a shield, we will kill him even if it means killing my son along with him ! »

As soon as these words were spoken, the surrounding Sunagakure Ninjas showed expressions of extreme shock and couldn’t help but discuss loudly :

« This... is this our Kazekage ? He would actually sacrifice his own family for the greater good ? How noble is this ! »

« Long live Lord Kazekage ! For Lord Kazekage, for the Hidden Sand Village, we must kill this Steam Ninja today ! »

« That’s right, Lord Kazekage’s command is simply brilliant. Exchanging an immature One-Tail Jinchuriki who might go on a rampage at any time for a top-tier combatant of Iwagakure—this trade might not be a loss ! »

While everyone was still secretly rejoicing over that « brilliant » decision, a cold arc curled at the corner of Asakawa Shin’s mouth.

His two light pats on Gaara earlier were by no means for mindless amusement.

Instead, after having already finished extracting the One-Tail’s Chakra, he deliberately woke the sleeping Gaara just so he could hear this wonderful play with his own ears.

The cold and heartless decision just made by the Hidden Sand Village had already entered his ears without missing a single word.

At this moment, Gaara’s eyes were wide open, his face written with disbelief, and his eyes quickly filled with tears.

But this was not a cry of sadness ; it was a precursor to ultimate despair.

He let out a roar of rage in his heart : 'Why... why do you all want me to die ? Even Father is like this ?

No... isn’t this the ending I should have expected long ago ? Over the years, has he sent few people to assassinate me ?

Hateful ! Utterly hateful ! All of you people deserve to die !

Hey ! You monster inside me, haven’t you always wanted my body ? Then take it !

I have only one request : kill all these bastards in the Hidden Sand Village, leave none alive ! If you can do that, what does it matter if I give you this body ?'

Shukaku had a completely stunned expression inside him. After a few seconds of silence, he clutched his stomach and let out a burst of wild laughter, his gaze toward Gaara filled with unmasked appreciation :

« You’re actually quite interesting, kid. This great me is starting to admire you a bit.

Fine ! It’s a deal. Next, keep your eyes wide open and see what 'Lord Shukaku’s methods' are ! »

Unfortunately, although his words were loud, as soon as he came out, Asakawa Shin had already thrown him out as a shield.

Under the precise prediction of Observation Haki, his newly manifested Tailed Beast body happened to meet the Ninjutsu released by the Sand crowd. Under the wild bombardment, he wailed incessantly for a time.

Even though he resisted desperately, he didn’t last long. Under the siege of three Kage-level figures and a host of Sunagakure Ninjas, his strength failed and he retreated, once again falling into the state of being sealed.

Before finally losing consciousness, Gaara complained to Shukaku : « You noisy guy, you talk in my ear every day saying that as long as I fall asleep you’ll go out and destroy the Hidden Sand Village. And the result ? This is it ? Were you lying to me all along ? You f*cking... »

Shukaku was now completely shut down, the entire Tailed Beast curled up in a corner of the sealing space, completely ignoring Gaara’s complaints.

Asakawa Shin had taken advantage of the moment when the Hidden Sand Village crowd was distracted by Shukaku to choose a direction with weak defenses and quickly break through. As his fists and feet flew, not a single person could match him.

Rasa witnessed this scene from afar, his eyes nearly splitting with rage. However, their minds were entirely occupied by Shukaku, and they could only watch helplessly as that trail of white steam drifted further and further away.

By the time they had sealed Shukaku, there was no trace of Han left on the battlefield.

« Dammit ! 'Han the Steam Ninja,' is it ? I, Rasa, will remember you ! And the despicable Iwagakure, I will never forgive you either ! »
