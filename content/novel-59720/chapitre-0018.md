# Chapitre 18 — Urgent Mission

The news that the Third Hokage was about to take on a disciple spread through Konoha, as expected.

It wasn’t just the major Ninja clans ; everyone in the streets and alleys was discussing it. If you didn’t know, you were out of the loop.

« Have you heard ? The one about to become his disciple is that genius Asakawa Shin, the kid who developed the Konoha Six Styles. »

« I told you so. Hokage-sama hasn’t taken a disciple in decades. Turns out it’s him ; that explains it. »

« Yeah, Hokage-sama only had three disciples before, the legendary 'Sannin'. »

« Who is Asakawa Shin ? Is he that famous ? »

« The Security Department, that’s him. I suspect he’s a spy. »

...

Konoha Hospital.

« Congratulations, Hanako ! Shin is just too outstanding. I heard he’s going to be accepted as a disciple by Hokage-sama. »

« Eh ? That Asakawa Shin is actually Hanako’s son ? I just found out. »

« Nani ? Hanako has a son ? No way... »

« Incredible, simply incredible. I heard he developed the Konoha Six Styles technique at just six years old. That’s an achievement most people can’t reach in their entire lifetime ! »

Hanako’s surgical office was bustling with people. Everyone around her had turned into a sycophant today, showering her with compliments as if they were free, making her so dizzy she almost couldn’t find north.

Oh no, my mouth is starting to cramp from smiling.

Not only that, the director found her today, affirmed her work ability, and wanted to increase her responsibilities. It just so happened that the head of the medical examination center was about to retire, and he asked her to spend these next few days familiarizing herself with the work and preparing for the handover.

As it turned out, when she went to take a look, that person in charge didn’t do anything at all. Every day was just drinking tea and holding meetings, while the deputies handled all the specific affairs.

Hanako understood perfectly well ; this was just a place for her to retire comfortably. However, she accepted it with peace of mind. After struggling for so many years, couldn’t she enjoy herself a little ?

Feeling the enthusiasm of those around her, Hanako sighed inwardly : 'They say raising children is for old age insurance ; it’s really true.'

...

Land of Fire border, Konoha camp.

« Asakawa Kenji, this is an urgent mission personally issued by Hokage-sama. Burn after reading. »

« This mission is extremely important and only you can complete it. Even if you exhaust yourself completely, you must do it. It is time to test your will of fire. »

Asakawa Kenji swallowed hard, looking at the Anbu Ninja in front of him. If the camp commander wasn’t standing next to him with a serious face, he would have thought he hadn’t woken up yet.

'Am I not just an ordinary Chunin ? An urgent mission personally issued by Hokage-sama ? Exhaust myself completely ? Me ?'

Kenji took the mission scroll with trembling hands. The surrounding Ninja consciously turned away to avoid looking. He took a deep breath to build up his mental fortitude before slowly opening it.

[Order Konoha Chunin Asakawa Kenji to immediately abandon the border defense mission, follow the Anbu squad back to Konoha Village, and propagate and multiply with your family member Asakawa Hanako. No mistakes allowed !]

« Damn it, when did I fall under a Genjutsu ? Genjutsu, release ! »

Anbu squad leader : « Nani ? There’s an enemy ? Everyone, be on alert ! »

...

Ignoring the chaos at Hanako and Kenji’s location, Asakawa Shin stood slowly in the underground training ground.

As expected of the Hokage ; the underground training ground mentioned yesterday was already ready today.

It was said that Root had falsely reported supplies and spread rumors about the Hokage. They were caught red-handed. When their actions were exposed, instead of surrendering, they violently resisted law enforcement, which infuriated the Hokage.

Overnight, many Root Ninja were arrested, and their leader, Danzo, was stripped of his titles as Assistant to the Hokage and leader of Root, then thrown into prison under strict guard.

This training ground was the confiscated property of Root, slightly renovated on that foundation, and now it was his exclusive training ground.

Taking a deep breath, Asakawa Shin closed his eyes and blocked out external noise, looking inward at his body. Following the continuous practice of Life Return · Changzhong over the past few days, controlling that life force to circulate throughout his body with his blood flow, and continuously strengthening his physique, he finally felt a palpitation today.

'Not bad, this is the feeling. It’s evolving again !'

When Asakawa Shin first awakened Wood Release, the life energy in his body was like a newly sprouted seedling, and the power of the Wood Release he could use was extremely limited, about the level of Yamato.

Afterwards, as his body was continuously strengthened, the life energy within him grew day by day, growing from a seedling into a small tree, and the strength of his Wood Release underwent earth-shaking changes, reaching the level of Yamato possessed by Tobi.

And at this moment, this small tree was growing rapidly as if time had been accelerated. It continuously extended its Root system and grew branches. In the blink of an eye, it became a towering tree. This was a leap in the source of life.

« Hiss... This feeling is just too great ! »

« The magnitude of Chakra and the strength of my physique have both increased significantly. Moreover, I can clearly feel a mysterious factor in the air. This should be Natural Energy, right ? »

Asakawa Shin felt the changes happening inside and outside his body, and a natural realization appeared in his mind.

'Is this bloodline atavism ? I’ve become a Wood Release Sage Body. Can I do this without Ashura Chakra ? The level of that life force is probably beyond my imagination.'

'Not only that, my body is still instinctively absorbing that life force. Could it be... could it still evolve ?'

'What will it become if it evolves again ? This touches my blind spot of knowledge !'

The speed of evolution was too fast, so fast that Shin was a little scared. People instinctively feel fear towards unknown things beyond their control.

'Hmph ! Whatever ! A small cheat isn’t really cheating ; it’s a good thing anyway.'

Asakawa Shin felt the powerful life force contained in his physique. Even though his six-year-old body had not fully developed, he could further unlock Roger’s power.

Originally, his young body couldn’t keep up with the unlocking speed of the Roger template, thus restricting some of his power. Now, after awakening the Wood Release, it was the opposite ; the unlocking speed of the Roger template couldn’t keep up with the strength of the Sage Body.

However, there was no rush for now. He still needed that life force to strengthen his body and complete the evolution. If the template fusion degree reached 100%, the life force that acted as a negative effect would also disappear, which would be counterproductive.

Current fusion degree : 31.3%, and it can be fully utilized, already reaching the level of an Admiral candidate.

...

Asakawa Shin calmed down for a while and began to focus his mind, studying the Wood Release scroll.

'Eh ? So it requires hand seals ? It’s not just clapping your hands and shouting whatever comes to mind ? But the number of seals is indeed not large.'

'Hiss, this is it, the Four-Pillar House Technique I’ve always wanted, and the Continuous Pillar House Technique, known as the strongest infrastructure Ninjutsu, essential for missions in the wild.'

'Wood Locking Wall, Tree Boundary Wall, Wood Suppression Wall, Wood Transformation, Wood Clone, Multiple Wood Clone, Wood Substitution, Deep Forest Emergence, Deep Forest Bloom, Wood Dragon, Wood Human, and many other attack Ninjutsu, Fuinjutsu, Sage Arts...'

'This is too comprehensive. First Hokage, worthy of being the God of Shinobi, simply flawless.'

'For Wood Release, my evaluation is : Extra Large Cup. Strong, cool, and comprehensive.'

Just looking at the names and effects of these techniques made Asakawa Shin break into a sweat. One could imagine the pressure on the Ninja of Hashirama’s era.

'These techniques are all very good. They are mine now.'

Asakawa Shin concentrated his spirit, seriously testing and mastering the effects of each Ninjutsu. In just half a day, except for the Fuinjutsu and a few Sage Arts that required Sage Mode, all other techniques were mastered by Asakawa Shin.

'Wood Release is great in every way, powerful and very comprehensive, but the consumption of Chakra is just too terrifying. However, this is not a problem with Wood Release, but my problem.' Even with Asakawa Shin’s current Chakra volume, comparable to the Eight-Tails, he couldn’t quite handle this intense consumption.

Especially the Wood Human Technique ; the moment of casting it alone drained one-third of his Chakra. The Chakra consumption to maintain and propel the Wood Human Technique was also terrifying. With his current Chakra recovery rate, it was running a deficit.

'Damn it, if I fully propel the Wood Human Technique, I’ll run out of Chakra in three hours. How can this be ? This is too insecure.'

'What kind of real man can’t fight for ten days and ten nights ! But as I gradually grow up, my Chakra volume will also grow rapidly. No, it’s too slow. I have to continue to fleece the system, striving to evolve as soon as possible.'
