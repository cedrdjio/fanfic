# Chapitre 117 — The Lead Jonin

« Haha, Sasuke, you idiot ! How did you end up with a black eye just in the time it took to eat lunch ? »

Naruto was clutching his stomach in the classroom, laughing so hard he was rolling around. Even though his own injuries hadn’t healed yet, he was laughing so much that tears were almost coming out.

Asakawa Shin stood to the side, his brow slightly furrowed. His gaze swept over Sasuke’s bruised face, his voice filled with confusion :

« Who did you mess with ? How did you end up in such a mess ? Your clothes are all in tatters... »

Sasuke’s lip twitched slightly, a flash of shame and anger in his eyes. But he quickly suppressed his emotions, forced a smile, and waved his hand with feigned nonchalance :

« It was just a spar, nothing big. I just got a bit too invested in the training. Don’t make such a fuss. »

Sakura’s eyes sparkled with admiration as she gave Sasuke a thumbs-up. « As expected of Sasuke-kun ! With that kind of perseverance, you can accomplish anything, right ? »

Before she could finish, Naruto cut in with a sneer. « He can’t do anything. For example, he can’t even become a group leader~ »

As if a sore spot had been poked, Sasuke suddenly stood up, his face flushed as he retorted loudly, « What do you mean I can’t ? I’ve served for two consecutive terms ! »

« Who do you think you’re talking to ? I’ve served as group leader for five whole terms ! You’ve only done two, how can you compare to me ? »

Naruto finally caught an opportunity and began to brag triumphantly.

« Does the initial probationary period even count ? Besides, the rules of the competition were biased toward you ! »

Sasuke retorted indignantly, « If Ninjutsu were allowed... »

« Why wouldn’t it count ? And even with Ninjutsu, you’d still be no match for me ! My Shadow Clone Technique is ridiculously strong—when I get serious, I even scare myself ! »

Seeing the two of them starting to butt heads again, Sakura felt her temples throbbing and couldn’t help but interrupt, « Enough, enough ! You two stop fighting ! »

This shout was indeed effective, as both of their gazes instantly focused on her.

Sasuke even raised an eyebrow and asked out of the blue, « Sakura, has anyone ever told you that your voice sounds very pleasant ? »

Sakura froze, her cheeks instantly flushing crimson. Her fingers unconsciously twisted together as she stammered, « No... no one has. Why ? Sasuke-kun ? »

« Then why are you so talkative... » Sasuke glanced at her expressionlessly. « Can’t you just shut up ? »

« ? »

As time passed minute by minute, the noisy classroom gradually fell into silence. Looking around, only the five of them were left sitting there all alone.

Naruto finally couldn’t hold back anymore. He slammed the table and complained, « What’s going on ? The other classes left with their new teachers ages ago. Even Iruka-sensei has finished work. Why hasn’t our teacher shown up yet ? »

Karin was also full of doubt. She couldn’t help but lean closer to Asakawa Shin and ask in a low voice, « Lord Shin, our teacher... they haven’t forgotten about us, have they ? »

Asakawa Shin scratched his head helplessly, a hint of uncertainty in his tone. « Surely not ? Even though that person is usually a bit unreliable, they wouldn’t forget something this important. »

...

After waiting a while longer, Naruto really couldn’t stand the boredom and started pulling some stunts.

« Hey, Naruto, what are you up to ? Stop it ! » Sakura hurriedly tried to dissuade him.

However, Naruto only grew more enthusiastic.

He balanced a chalkboard eraser steadily on the doorframe, a smug smile on his face as he meticulously set up this prank trap.

« Humph, it’s their own fault for being late. Watch me give them a special welcome gift. »

Sasuke watched coldly from the side, his lips curling into a disdainful sneer. « You think a low-level, childish trick like that will succeed ? You have no idea how formidable a Jonin is ! A so-called Jonin... »

Before Sasuke could finish his sentence, the classroom door was pushed open with a « creak. »

In an instant, everyone’s eyes were fixed on the chalkboard eraser above the doorframe.

With a crisp « thud, » the eraser landed squarely and accurately on the newcomer’s forehead.

« Ouch ! Ow, ow, ow, ow ! ...Huh ? A chalkboard eraser ?

Damn it ! Which blind brat dared to set a trap to ambush me ? I’ll skin them alive ! »

« Lady Tsunade, are you all right ? »

A man and a woman behind her rushed forward. The woman, who was holding a small pig, looked especially anxious as she hurriedly asked. It was Shizune.

Asakawa Shin also pulled Karin forward, wrinkling his nose and sniffing in distaste :

« What a strong smell of alcohol... Tsunade-shijie, you didn’t get drunk and forget about today’s meeting, did you ? »

« Uh... Hahaha, how could that be ? Today is the big day we officially form our team. How could I forget ? »

Tsunade gave a dry laugh, trying to hide her embarrassment. Her eyes darted around before shifting the topic to Karin. « This young girl must be Karin, right ? Hmm, not bad. She looks quite spirited. »

Asakawa Shin nodded, then solemnly introduced her to his companion. « Karin, this is our team’s lead Jonin, one of the world-renowned 'Sannin'—Lady Tsunade.

And this beside her is Senior Shizune, who is also an incredibly powerful Medical Ninja. »

« I... It’s an honor to meet you, Lady Tsunade ! It’s an honor to meet you, Senior Shizune. » Karin was overwhelmed by the favor and hurriedly bowed in greeting.

Sakura, standing nearby, couldn’t contain her surprise and joy. She stepped forward quickly and exclaimed, « Your lead Jonin is actually Lady Tsunade ? This... this is too incredible ! »

Then, as if suddenly remembering something, she hurriedly fumbled through her bag for paper and a pen, excitedly handing them to Tsunade :

« Lady Tsunade, could you give me an autograph ? I’m a huge fan of yours !

I’ve finally met you in person today. My biggest dream is to become a top-tier Medical Ninja just like you.

I... my name is Sakura. »

« Oh ? So you’re Sakura. Not bad, not bad at all. You have good taste. An autograph ? Piece of cake~ »

Tsunade readily took the paper and pen, signed her name with a flourish, and didn’t forget to raise an eyebrow at Asakawa Shin with a very smug expression.

Naruto couldn’t contain his curiosity either. He stepped forward and circled Tsunade several times, sizing her up and down before asking Asakawa Shin with a face full of confusion :

« Hey, Shin, why is your lead Jonin a woman ? She doesn’t look like anything special. Is she really that strong ? »

Seeing this, Sakura grew frantic. She grabbed Naruto’s sleeve and scolded him in a low voice :

« Naruto, don’t be disrespectful to Lady Tsunade ! She is Shin’s senior sister, the direct disciple of Hokage-sama, and she’s recognized throughout the Ninja World as the strongest Medical Ninja ! »

« What ? This woman is really that strong ? I can’t tell at all. No matter how I look at her, she just seems like a big sister who likes to drink, » Naruto said, his eyes wide with disbelief.

Naruto’s loud voice naturally drew Tsunade’s attention.

However, after her gaze lingered on his hand for a moment, her face suddenly darkened, and her eyes became sharp :

« Hey ! Brat, there’s still chalk dust on your fingers... That trap on the door just now, it wouldn’t happen to be your doing, would it ? »

« Uh... It was me, but my target wasn’t you at all. I wanted to... »

Before Naruto could finish, the silver-haired man who had been standing there for a long time, seriously studying, finally closed the book in his hand and took over the conversation :

« Your target was probably me, right ? Unfortunately, as an Elite Jonin, it’s impossible for me to fall for such a low-level trick... »

« Oh ? According to you, since I fell for the trap, I’m not fit to be a Jonin ? »

Tsunade’s gaze instantly became dangerous, and a cold smile curled on her lips. « It’s been a few years, Kakashi, and it seems you’ve grown quite arrogant !

The rest of you stay here. As for you, Kakashi, come outside with me for a moment. »

Leaving those words behind, Tsunade turned and left the classroom aggressively.

Kakashi, who had been too absorbed in his book earlier, had now snapped out of it and hurriedly chased after her, explaining frantically, « Lady Tsunade, you’ve misunderstood ! I didn’t mean it like that... »

Seeing this, Naruto curled his lip and muttered in a low voice with a face full of disdain, « Tsk, so that’s our lead Jonin ? He seems pretty useless. »

Sasuke’s brow furrowed slightly as he scrutinized that flustered departing figure, his tone full of doubt. « He just claimed to be an Elite Jonin ? But it feels more like... »

Sakura, on the other hand, held her hands to her chest, her eyes shimmering with envy as she whispered regretfully, « Damn it, I really wish I could be Lady Tsunade’s subordinate. I’m so jealous of Shin and Karin. »
