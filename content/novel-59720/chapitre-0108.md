# Chapitre 108 — Gaara

« Is this Shadow Clone an idiot ? He’s acting like a bot... »

Asakawa Shin cursed angrily ; he sensed something was wrong as soon as he teleported to the Land of Wind.

Looking around, a large tree stood abruptly amidst the swirling yellow sands, clearly planted by the Shadow Clone using Wood Release Chakra.

The tree’s vitality was incredibly stubborn, refusing to die anytime soon. It looked completely out of place in the desert, as eerie as a mirage.

« Sigh ! »

Asakawa Shin let out a long sigh and slammed his hands together. A strange power erupted from his palms, beginning to modify the tree.

In an instant, the lush tree from a moment ago seemed to be accelerated by time, its life force drained in a flash.

It withered and wilted at a visible rate. The thick trunk shriveled rapidly, and the leaves turned to dust, scattering with the wind. In the blink of an eye, it became a piece of dead, silent wood.

Asakawa Shin raised his hand and gave a light push. The heavy, dead stump fell with a crash, sinking deep into the soft sea of sand. It kicked up a cloud of dust before being completely swallowed by the ruthless desert.

« I reinforced the wood density ; it should last here for quite a few years. »

Asakawa Shin brushed the dust off his hands, looked at the solitary stump, and muttered to himself, « This will have to do for now. »

He looked around at the boundless yellow sand. Sand and stones flew through the air in a chaotic mess, making it impossible to tell north from south.

Asakawa Shin scratched his head in frustration. He quickly closed his eyes to concentrate, sensing the positions of other Flying Thunder God Marks. Once he locked onto the coordinates, he turned into an afterimage and sped off in one direction.

It had to be said, the Land of Wind was truly a godforsaken place. The harshness of the environment was appalling, only slightly better than a gloomy place like Shikkotsu Forest.

The weather here was unbearably hot, with sandstorms raging and water resources being extremely scarce. The citizens of the entire nation could only survive by huddling in a few limited oasis water sources.

Traveling through this vast sea of sand was pure torture. After traveling through the wind and sand for most of the day, Asakawa Shin finally reached the outskirts of the Hidden Sand Village.

Night had already fallen. From a distance, the lights in the Hidden Sand Village flickered on one by one, adding a rare touch of human presence to the dull and silent desert.

Unfortunately, this peace would soon be shattered. Asakawa Shin’s expression turned serious as his hands quickly formed seals : « Wood Release : Wood Transformation ! »

In an instant, countless tough branches burst from the ground at his feet, winding and growing rapidly like spirit snakes, completely enveloping him.

This transformation lasted for a while. The branches didn’t grow blindly ; guided by Chakra, they constantly changed shape, arranging themselves in a precise combination.

As the dust settled, a complex wooden armor had formed, fully armoring Asakawa Shin.

When the last vine snapped into place, a familiar figure appeared in the wasteland—the imposing elite Jonin of Iwagakure clad in heavy armor, known as the « Steam Ninja, » Han.

Asakawa Shin carefully examined the hard armor on his body, the corners of his mouth curling up as he whispered with excitement, « I have to say, this looks pretty cool. »

« Rumor has it that the Fourth Kazekage Rasa’s Magnet Release : Gold Dust is incredibly overbearing. I wonder which is stronger compared to my Steam Ninja’s Boil Release ? Tonight is the perfect time to test his mettle... »

« And that bastard Han, not only did he make me wait for a month last time, but he even dared to ambush me. Today, I’ll turn up the difficulty for you ! »

« But before that, I should take care of business first. »

With that, he vanished into the darkness and quietly slipped toward the Hidden Sand Village.

It had to be said that as one of the Five Great Hidden Villages, the Hidden Sand Village’s detection barrier was far superior to the likes of Kusagakure or Takigakure.

Even Asakawa Shin had to go through some trouble to sneak in undetected.

However, once inside the village, the situation improved significantly ; the guards were noticeably more lax.

Asakawa Shin continued to sneak forward until he stopped five hundred meters away from Ichibi Shukaku.

Within his perception, there were more than a dozen Ninjas lurking openly and secretly within this five-hundred-meter radius, forming a defensive perimeter to strictly guard and monitor Gaara.

Seeing this, a flash of excitement crossed Asakawa Shin’s eyes, feeling a bit like he was playing Assassin’s Creed.

He quickly analyzed the guards' defensive zones and positions, then created two Shadow Clones. Together with his main body, they flanked from three sides and launched a simultaneous surprise attack.

Within a few breaths, all the guards were silently dealt with, without making a single sound.

Looking at the fallen men, Asakawa Shin smiled modestly, secretly impressed by his own skillful execution.

At this moment, Gaara was standing alone at the edge of a rooftop, gazing up at the cold moonlight with an unreadable expression, lost in thought.

Asakawa Shin stood silently behind him, watching for a moment while calculating in his head :

'This posture, this angle... it’s perfect. Should I just do it ?'

'But seeing him look so melancholic, I feel a bit bad for him.'

'Oh well, if I knock him out, he won’t feel sad anymore. I guess I’m technically doing a good deed, right ?'

Thinking this, Asakawa Shin no longer hesitated. He moved stealthily and pounced forward like a thief, delivering a heavy slap to the back of Gaara’s head.

Gaara didn’t react at all, but the sand protecting him instinctively sensed the danger and surged forward to block him.

Unfortunately, this was all within Asakawa Shin’s expectations. He applied a bit more force to his wrist, his palm strike as sharp as a blade, easily shattering the layer of sand defense.

A solid slap landed right on the back of his head.

Gaara’s vision went black. The blow sent him stumbling, and he immediately lost his balance, falling face-first onto the ground and passing out.

In the final moment before his consciousness faded into darkness, a thought flashed through his mind :

'It hurts, it hurts, it hurts ! So this is what pain feels like ? This is my first time...'

Asakawa Shin nodded in satisfaction. He was about to step forward to extract the Ichibi’s Chakra, but in the next second, he suddenly froze and quickly hid himself.

Almost at the same time, a massive amount of violent Tailed Beast Chakra erupted from Gaara’s body—a sign that he was about to undergo Tailed Beast Transformation.

Asakawa Shin cursed inwardly. This Fuinjutsu was simply too pathetic ; he was going to go berserk just like that ?

However, such a huge commotion would likely alert the Hidden Sand Village. Those Ninjas would surely react quickly.

Sure enough, before long, Rasa arrived at the scene with a group of elite Ninjas.

At this moment, his face was as dark as water, and his gaze toward Gaara was a mix of disappointment and disgust.

He was about to give orders to the surrounding Ninjas : « Sealing Team, quickly follow me and... »

Before he could finish his sentence, Gaara’s rampage entered a new stage.

A massive amount of sand-like Chakra surged out. His entire body began to swell violently, and in an instant, he transformed into the terrifying form of One-Tail Shukaku.

Rasa sighed softly, waved his hand to the surrounding Ninjas, and ordered in a deep voice :

« All of you, fall back. Once I’ve suppressed Shukaku, find the right moment to perform the seal immediately. »

« Yes, Lord Kazekage ! » the Ninjas replied in unison.

Asakawa Shin’s eyes flickered, and he cheered inwardly : 'Oh ? Looks like there’s a good show to watch. Truly a case of « filial piety » between father and son~'
