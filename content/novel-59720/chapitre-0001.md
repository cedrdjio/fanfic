# Chapitre 1 — First Encounter

It was a sunny autumn afternoon.

Shin was sprawled on the ground, looking at the sky with a panicked expression, as if he had seen something incomprehensible.

At this moment, the sky was cloudless and a clear blue as far as the eye could see. There was nothing special about it, except for a flying Dayun truck...

It turned out to be the Ultimate Killer King (crossed out), a « Hundred-ton King » with brake failure. It had crashed through the guardrail and flown off the mountain road. Quite reasonable !

'Has Earth Online finally encountered a bug after running for so many years ?'

'The mountain road is several kilometers away from my house. Buddy, how did you fly here ? Are you Optimus Prime ?'

Before he could say the words, Shin was struck squarely by the heaven-sent Dayun truck. As his soul ascended to heaven, he seemed to experience a strange hallucination.

« Oh no, oh no, a bug appeared ! It’s all your fault—I told you not to drink but you insisted, and I messed up in a moment of carelessness... »

« You’re an intern, what are you afraid of ? Just give him some Carnelian as compensation. »

...

Land of Fire, Konoha Village.

'Loading the Golden Finger failed again !'

Six-year-old Asakawa Shin sat at home with a gloomy expression, his eyes fixed on a virtual panel that only he could see. On it were four words...

[Loading...]

That’s right, he had transmigrated. He had transmigrated into a world called Naruto. Perhaps because an infant’s development is incomplete, he didn’t fully awaken his memories until last year.

From the moment he awakened, he knew his situation was likely dangerous. The world of Naruto was not a safe and peaceful paradise, and the Konoha Village he was in was even more disaster-prone, repeatedly devastated.

Fortunately, Shin had the talent to become a Ninja. He was born into a small Ninja family in Konoha. His father, Asakawa Kenji, and his mother, Asakawa Hanako, were both Chunin.

Because Hanako was about to give birth, they were at Konoha Hospital when the Nine-Tails' Attack occurred. Setting aside Hanako’s labor for a moment, Kenji’s temporary mission was to help transfer the wounded. He didn’t face the Masked Man or the Nine-Tailed directly, thus escaping the disaster.

One must know that the Ninjas who went to the battlefield at that time suffered heavy casualties. This made him one of the few among his peers whose parents were both alive.

'Asakawa ? I’ve never even heard of this surname. I’m not some background character or cannon fodder, am I ?'

'The most annoying thing is my Golden Finger. The progress bar has been stuck at 99.9% for a whole year. Could it have a bug too ?'

'No, I can’t just accept my fate. Haven’t I heard that heaven always leaves a path ? As long as I want to walk, the path is beneath my feet.'

'Even without a Golden Finger, how can I just wait for death ? With my advantage of foresight, it’s not impossible to become a powerhouse of a generation. I still need to plan carefully...

In short, I’ll develop low-key for now, cling to Naruto and Sasuke’s legs, and stay safe until the grand finale.'

...

« Shin, school starts tomorrow. Are your stationery and water bottle ready ? »

His mother Hanako’s voice came from the hallway, interrupting his thoughts. A feeling of happiness arose from within.

He was an orphan in his previous life, and now he suddenly had both parents. In this regard, he was actually coming out ahead.

His mother Hanako was extremely beautiful, with curved willow-like eyebrows and flowing black hair. Her inherent maternal radiance added even more to her charm.

He didn’t know how his idiot father managed to win such a beauty. Back when Lady Tsunade was still in the Village, she and Hanako were known as the Twin Flowers of Konoha Hospital.

« What are you giggling about ? Mommy is asking you something. » Hanako gave her silly son a helpless look and took down his backpack from the wall hook to check it.

« Not bad, everything is ready. As for the bento, what do you want to eat ? Mommy will make it for you tomorrow morning... »

Before she could finish, she felt her silly son hug her. Hanako looked down and met Shin’s bright eyes.

« Mommy, I will protect you ! »

« Pfft, you ? Wait until you’re taller than my leg first. »

...

Time passed in Shin’s excited anticipation, and in the blink of an eye, the next morning arrived.

Today was the day to report to the Ninja Academy. According to Shin’s thinking, as a third-generation Ninja of the Asakawa family—though the Asakawa family wasn’t some prestigious Ninja clan, they had shed blood and tears for Konoha—his background was pure and loyal.

Moreover, after a year of hard work, Shin had become friends with the Nine-Tails Jinchuriki Uzumaki Naruto. There was a high probability they would be assigned to the same class, but he was still a bit nervous before it was confirmed.

Ever since he awakened all his memories a year ago, he had been consciously wandering the streets. Sure enough, one day he encountered that child—the boy whom the villagers secretly called the « Demon Fox. »

At that time, Naruto was being chased out by a food shop owner holding a broom. « Get out ! We don’t sell things to you in our shop. Leave now ! Or don’t blame me for being rude ! »

The surrounding villagers were used to it. Not only did they not stop it, but they also pointed and whispered at Naruto.

« It’s him, it’s that monster... »

« See that ? You two stay away from that child. Don’t play with him. »

« Get lost, you stink. »

...

'But I... just took a bath this morning.' Naruto lowered his head.

But this defense was never spoken, because he knew it was useless to say it.

Naruto had just received his allowance today and was excitedly planning to buy some new food.

Although the instant noodles and milk at home came in many flavors and had always been his favorite foods, eating them for a long time made him inevitably a bit tired of them. He wanted to try other flavors.

Drinking too much milk would also cause diarrhea—something called lactose... whatever intolerance. He had heard this while passing by a neighbor’s house.

That family’s little puppy used to have diarrhea often. The big sister next door took it to see a doctor before she found out the reason.

'Now I know too. I don’t even need to see a doctor, hehehe.' Naruto became proud again at the thought.

Seeing Naruto dazed, the shop owner wanted to push him, which snapped Naruto out of it.

« If you won’t sell, then you won’t sell. I didn’t want to buy anyway. Your bread tastes terrible, blehh... »

Naruto made a face and quickly ran away, leaving the fuming owner behind.

« Stupid brat ! Don’t let me see you again... »

...

In the park, Naruto sat alone on a roadside stone, watching children his age playing on the swings. Beside them were their parents, a scene of laughter and joy, very lively.

He didn’t go over. He had once plucked up the courage to walk toward those children.

But as soon as he took a few steps closer, those parents would pull their children away in a panic as if avoiding a plague, leaving only the empty playground and a lonely Naruto.

He sat alone on the swing, his feet lazily brushing the ground. The arc of his swinging grew smaller and smaller until it finally stopped.

Looking at the once lively empty space, he suddenly felt that some distances could not be closed just by taking steps.

From then on, when he encountered such scenes again, he would just stand far away or turn and leave. He no longer approached, no longer expected, and no longer made a fool of himself.

...

'They’re laughing so loudly. Is playing with friends really that fun ?'

'Damn it, I want to know what that feels like too. Why don’t they take me with them ?'

'Sigh, if only I could have a friend, how great that would be.'

Just as Naruto was lost in thought with his head down, a pair of small feet walked up to him.

« Hey, blondie, what’s your name ? Why are you here by yourself ? I haven’t seen you before. »

Naruto looked up, dazed for a moment before clearly seeing the boy in front of him.

The boy stood with his back to the light, with black hair and black eyes. His face was handsome, a smile played on his lips, and Naruto’s reflection was mirrored in his eyes, which seemed to hold light.

'Wow, so... so dazzling.'
