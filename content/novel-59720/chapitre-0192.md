# Chapitre 192 — After bidding farewell, Asakawa Shin galloped towards the Three Great Sage Regions without a moment's delay.

During the flight, he asked the Katsuyu on his shoulder with great interest, « How do you feel now ? Have you established a connection with the main body of Shikkotsu Forest in this world ? We are going to be guests. »

Katsuyu shook her head, her voice crisp, « No, it seems something is blocking my perception. I cannot establish a connection. »

Asakawa Shin didn’t mind and smiled nonchalantly.

In just half a day, he arrived at the entrance of Shikkotsu Forest.

He was clearly familiar with this swamp, stepping into it without hesitation.

However, the moment he stepped in, the small Katsuyu that was eating in the swamp stiffened, looking over with an expression of disbelief.

Asakawa Shin also paused, feeling a chill wash over him.

Deep within Shikkotsu Forest, countless Katsuyu of all sizes were staring at him in unison, maintaining the same posture. It was a spectacular sight, yet upon closer inspection, a bit creepy.

His expression stiffened slightly, and he hurriedly waved to greet them, « Hello, Sister Katsuyu. It’s a long story, but I am a successor of Shikkotsu Forest from another spacetime. My name is Asakawa Shin, please treat me well. »

The Katsuyu on his shoulder spoke up, « No need for such trouble, Lord Shin, let me handle it. »

As she spoke, she leaped off Asakawa Shin’s shoulder and split into two smaller Katsuyu, one of which quickly merged into the body of the Katsuyu of this world.

In just a few moments, the frozen atmosphere was broken, and the many Katsuyu of Shikkotsu Forest became restless.

« To think such a thing could happen, how interesting. »

« What ? The me from that world can actually go out and play whenever she wants ? I’m so envious ! »

Subsequently, one Katsuyu bowed respectfully, « Lord Shin, the main body is already aware of everything. Please, come and see her. »

Asakawa Shin nodded slightly, picked up his Katsuyu with both hands to place her back on his shoulder, and then flashed away, speeding towards the depths of Shikkotsu Forest.

Not long after flying, he noticed something unusual.

Deep in Shikkotsu Forest, the massive, kilometer-long body of the main Katsuyu was slowly squirming and deforming, finally transforming into a stunningly beautiful woman standing a thousand meters tall.

She looked down at herself and nodded with satisfaction, « Who would have thought that the techniques of Ryūchi Cave would be somewhat interesting, allowing one to transform into a human form. »

However, she quickly sighed with some annoyance and muttered, « This is a bit too big, isn’t it ? I can’t even see my toes when I look down ; it’s a bit of a hindrance. »

Asakawa Shin watched, dumbfounded. Was this woman with the incredibly hot figure Lady Katsuyu ? But why... would she take on such an appearance ?

Lady Katsuyu clearly hadn’t had enough fun yet. Seeing Asakawa Shin approach, she greeted him enthusiastically, « How is it ? Is my transformation beautiful ? »

Asakawa Shin answered without hesitation, « Beautiful, but it seems a bit too big. »

Unexpectedly, Lady Katsuyu nodded solemnly, « Indeed, it is too big. It feels like a hindrance when moving vigorously. »

« I didn’t mean that part is too big ; I meant your size is too big. »

« Oh, you mean that ? »

Lady Katsuyu waved her hand, « That’s fine, I can shrink a bit. »

Before she finished speaking, her figure gradually shrank, eventually becoming a human form similar in size to Asakawa Shin.

« I already know the purpose of your visit. You may use the Natural Energy of Shikkotsu Forest as you please ; don’t be shy. After all, in a sense, you are also my successor. »

Asakawa Shin did not stand on ceremony either, casually finding a spot to sit cross-legged, « Then I shall accept with gratitude. »

In an instant, the Natural Energy of Shikkotsu Forest transformed into a massive vortex storm, which was rapidly absorbed by Asakawa Shin.

How thick was this Natural Energy accumulated over a thousand years ? Even with Asakawa Shin’s current absorption speed, it took several days to drain it completely.

He opened his eyes, unable to hide his excitement, « Not bad. At this rate, it is truly possible to complete the transformation into the Kekkei Mora. »

He took out a Flying Thunder God Kunai and handed it to Lady Katsuyu to keep safe, then turned into a streak of light, rushing straight towards the other two Great Sage Regions.

Meanwhile, Naruto and Minato joined forces to defeat Mukade, who had assumed the alias Anlushan.

Because the father and son had similar Chakra properties, the Rasengan they used together unexpectedly formed a combination Ninjutsu—the Taiji Rasengan.

How could a mere Mukade be a match for this move ? He was defeated in a single blow.

Just as Minato Namikaze stepped forward to seal the Dragon Vein, a barrier suddenly appeared, knocking him back violently.

Minato Namikaze was shocked and quickly turned his head, « Who is there ? »

Asakawa Shin’s Shadow Clone slowly walked out from the shadows, raising his hand to press down in the air, signaling that he had no malicious intent.

« Sorry, sorry. I still need to stay in this spacetime, so I can’t let you seal this Dragon Vein. Can you wait a few more days ? »

Minato Namikaze frowned, « You are called Shin, right ? Since you are also a Konoha Ninja, please leave immediately. Although I don’t know which spacetime you are from, I suspect that once the timeline is altered without authorization, it will likely cause terrible consequences. »

Asakawa Shin certainly knew this.

His purpose for this trip was only to collect Natural Energy from the Three Great Sage Regions, and the Sages of the Three Great Sage Regions had lived for thousands of years ; naturally, they were well-versed in the art of keeping secrets and would not change the future direction of the Ninja World.

« I naturally know this. But I really need a few more days to finish. »

Minato Namikaze smiled apologetically upon hearing this and took out a Flying Thunder God Kunai, « Sorry, I don’t know what you are doing. But whatever you are doing, please stop immediately. »

Asakawa Shin nodded understandingly and also took out a Flying Thunder God Kunai, « Then let’s settle this with our skills. »

Naruto held his head with both hands, his face full of confusion, « Eh ? What’s going on ? Why are you fighting ? Aren’t we all Konoha Ninja ? »

Neither of them paid him any attention, quickly throwing the Kunai in their hands, and then a dazzling Flying Thunder God battle unfolded.

Golden and green flashes of light quickly appeared and disappeared in the air, with Flying Thunder God Kunai flying all over the sky, yet neither side could claim victory.

Minato Namikaze took a large jump and retreated.

This was indeed the Flying Thunder God Technique without a doubt, and he always had a feeling that his opponent was not using their full strength.

No matter how much effort he added or how much faster he went, the other party could always respond calmly, as if he were just playing with him.

But how could that be ? He was the fastest in the Ninja World !

Just then, Mukade, who had originally been defeated by the two, miraculously revived under the stimulation of the Dragon Vein energy. He quickly absorbed the bodies of the surrounding puppets, turning into a gigantic centipede, and screamed frantically :

« I possess infinite Chakra ! You bastards, I will crush you all ! »

Naruto was shocked, « Oh no, why did this guy come back to life again ? »

Just as Mukade charged up with astonishing momentum, Asakawa Shin frowned, put his hands together, and shouted in a low voice :

« Infinite Chakra, huh ? I love that the most ! Wood Release : Wood Dragon Technique ! »

The next moment, countless trees burst out from the ground, intertwining and deforming, finally turning into a massive Wood Dragon hundreds of meters long.

Compared to it, the centipede that Mukade had turned into was as small as a real insect.

The Wood Dragon opened its massive mouth, grabbed the centipede in one bite, then coiled around it, rapidly absorbing his Chakra.

« What ? How is this possible ? I possess infinite Chakra ! »

Asakawa Shin nodded in agreement, the corners of his mouth curling up, « Indeed, it’s quite delicious. »

Minato Namikaze was stunned. This was actually Wood Release ?

Then he smiled in relief and put away the Flying Thunder God Kunai in his hand.

Asakawa Shin raised an eyebrow and looked over, « Why, are you not fighting anymore ? »

Minato Namikaze nodded with a smile, « At first, I thought you were pretending to be a Konoha Ninja. Looking at it this way, you must have your reasons for doing so. I will not interfere anymore. »

Naruto only then reacted and said to Yamato, who was on the side, « So, Captain Yamato, your Wood Release is so powerful ! Then why don’t you use it usually ? »

Yamato’s face turned red, and he stammered, « I... I don’t like using it. »
