# Chapitre 6 — Rokushiki, Life Return

The morning after Asakawa Shin fused with the Roger template, in the bathroom of the Asakawa household.

« Eh ? What’s this ? Is it a mustache ? »

Asakawa Shin looked into the mirror in disbelief, seeing his sleepy-eyed self with a shocked expression.

Most importantly, there was a tuft of black hair between his lips and nose, looking exactly like a stereotypical Japanese look.

« It’s not a mustache, it’s nose hair ! It’s freaking nose hair ! »

'System, get out here. Did you do this ? Don’t try to hide it. What exactly is going on ? Why is my nose hair so lush ?'

[Ding ! This is a normal phenomenon. Host, please do not worry. This is a negative status caused by fusing with the template. Once the fusion level reaches 100%, the negative status will disappear.]

[The negative status is closely related to the drawn template. The current negative status is vigorous nose hair vitality, with the growth rate accelerated until it approaches the template’s image. Please check the manual for details.]

'What ? How am I supposed to go out with this nose hair ! And don’t fool me, Roger has a mustache, right ? Hey, what are you doing ?'

'Ignoring me ? Acting all cold ?'

...

'Hmph ! How could a mere side effect stump me ? It’s just nose hair, right ? I’ll pluck them all out one by one !'

Hiss... Ow, that hurts like hell.

Fortunately, not all the nose hairs had mutated. Only the outermost ring was growing lushly, and Shin gritted his teeth and plucked them out one by one.

Compared to the power gained, let alone plucking a few nose hairs, even if all the hair on my body were plucked, so what ? Would I be afraid ?

Shin was originally quite happy this morning. Upon waking up, his role-play proficiency had increased by 0.1% simply because his hair color matched the template. Theoretically, as long as his hair stayed black, he could max out his proficiency in three years without doing anything, becoming invincible in the Ninja World and challenging the Five Kage to a dance.

But the system gave him a blow to the head, stating that a young, ordinary human cub could not carry all of Roger’s power, and forcing its use would cause bodily harm.

Simply put, the system had imposed an anti-addiction measure to protect the host. Although the template fusion was 100% complete, his power output was restricted, and it would gradually unlock as his body and brain matured.

The time for full unlocking was estimated by the system’s simulation to be around age 16. You have to know that even a cheater like Luffy only became a Yonko at age 19.

This didn’t stop Shin from getting a new template. If he reached 100% fusion and graduated early to become a Genin, he would meet all the conditions to draw a new template.

However, Asakawa Shin didn’t plan on graduating early. Yesterday after school, he spent his pocket money on a wooden sword and actually gained 1% proficiency. Naming the wooden sword [Ace] added another 1%, and buying a Straw Hat to keep at home added another 1%. However, it seemed the same action could only grant a bonus once.

Even so, gaining proficiency was as easy as playing a game. Is this an internal beta version ?

It feels like I can max it out effortlessly. In that case, there’s no need to rush ; I might as well let nature take its course.

I’m invincible ; do as you please.

...

After plucking his nose hair, Asakawa Shin almost arrived late for school. Not only was his nose red now, but his eyes were also bloodshot.

It couldn’t be helped ; while plucking the nose hair, physiological tears kept streaming from his eyes.

He sensed an unusual vitality in his nose. This vitality was repairing the hair follicles, and it was also what had caused the nose hair to sprout before.

Shin had a bitter smile on his face. Well, if a tooth breaks, drinking a glass of milk fixes it, let alone mere nose hair.

At this rate, the hair follicles would be repaired after one sleep. He had to find a way ; he couldn’t get up and pluck them every morning—he couldn’t take it.

The last class today was history. Listening to the history teacher’s hypnotic lecture, Asakawa Shin slowly immersed himself in his inner world.

Roger’s strength was unquestionable. In his power system, Taijutsu, Swordsmanship, and the Three Types of Haki didn’t need training ; they would naturally improve as the fusion level deepened. What Shin needed to do was attempt to merge the two different power systems.

But that was for later. Right now, there was another type of Taijutsu in his mind that he was very interested in, which even Roger hadn’t fully mastered.

That was the Marine Rokushiki.

As a Taijutsu that high-level Marine elites and CP Organizations in the One Piece world almost certainly had to learn, Rokushiki indeed had its unique merits.

Most importantly, if one mastered all of the Rokushiki and achieved total comprehension, they would learn a powerful ability called [Life Return].

Life Return allows control over every muscle, internal organ, the flow of blood, and even all the hair on the body.

Exactly. Shin wanted to learn Life Return to suppress the abnormal growth of his nose hair.

With Roger’s ability and talent, it wasn’t that he couldn’t learn Rokushiki, but rather that it was unnecessary.

For Roger, Rokushiki was completely useless « chicken ribs, » but for Shin, it was just right.

How sweet it is.

It just so happened that Shin had watched the anime and knew the training methods for Rokushiki. With his talent and effort, it wouldn’t take long to develop it, which would also serve to spread his reputation as a genius.

No sooner said than done. After school, Asakawa Shin dragged Naruto toward the training ground.

« Shin, where are we going ? »

« To the training ground to train, of course. Did you think I was taking you out to eat ? » Asakawa Shin gave him a look.

« I have an idea I want to test out. Don’t mind me ; you go run a hundred laps around the training ground first. »

« A hundred laps ? You might as well kill me. »

« How do you know you can’t if you don’t try ? Run, brother ! »

Having said that, he ignored Naruto and walked to the side to start practicing swordsmanship.

This was for show ; otherwise, if he suddenly pulled it out without ever having practiced, people would definitely be suspicious.

But if they knew you practiced swordsmanship regularly, the most they would do when you showed it off is consider you a genius.

After all, it’s reasonable for a genius to be just a « tiny bit » faster than others, right ?

Thus, under the existential-crisis-inducing gaze of the Anbu protecting Naruto, Shin went from a rookie who couldn’t even hold a sword correctly to an apprentice with decent form in just five minutes.

An hour later, he had become a swordsman with a clear style, each move quite sharp.

« Whew... That’s enough swordsmanship for today. Naruto, are you taking a stroll ? Speed up ! »

« Shin, I can’t... I can’t run anymore. »

« Five more laps and I’ll treat you to Ichiraku Ramen—the deluxe version. »

« Ohhh ! Power ! Power is surging within me ! »

...

In the blink of an eye, over a month had passed.

Evening, at the Asakawa household.

'Finally... I’ve finally learned Life Return. I didn’t expect to learn all of the Rokushiki in a week, but reaching the so-called level of total comprehension was so difficult.'

'A month ! Do you know how I’ve spent this past month ? System, speak !'

[...]

Next was the time to test the results. Asakawa Shin took a deep breath, activated Life Return, and tried to control all the hair on his body.

'It works ! I can suppress this abnormal vitality, and I can even...'

Sensing his body’s instinctive craving for this vitality, Asakawa Shin thought about it and decided to follow his body’s instinct.

He separated a tiny sliver of this vitality and fused it into his blood, letting the blood flow carry it to the tip of his right index finger.

'Now, let’s test if it can be fused elsewhere and see what the effect is.

Fuse !'

As that weak vitality fused into the skin of his right index finger tip, Asakawa Shin clearly felt that this grain-of-rice-sized patch of skin had changed, harboring a vibrant power.

No, instead of saying it was harboring power, it was more like an awakening.

He could feel that this power originated from his body itself, merely awakened by the fusion of that sliver of vitality that didn’t belong to this world.

As a thought in his mind activated this power, to Shin’s shocked eyes, his index finger actually sprouted.

'What the hell, is this... ? !'
