# Chapitre 3 — Ding!

Ninja Academy Registration Point.

'Nice, I really was assigned to the same class as Naruto.

Besides that, there are many familiar names ; it seems there’s no obvious deviation from the plot in my memory.'

Asakawa Shin looked at the notice on the bulletin board with a hint of excitement.

« Shin, you’re here so early ! »

That familiar voice, that loud volume—turning around, it was indeed that guy Naruto.

Asakawa Shin said helplessly, « It’s you who’s too slow, Naruto. »

« How is it ? Shin, are we in the same class ? Have you seen the class assignments ? »

« Relax, relax, I’ve already seen them. We’re in the same class. Please take care of me for the next few years, Naruto-kun. »

« Hahaha, that’s great ! Please take care of me too, Shin, » Naruto said earnestly, scratching his head.

« We’re in Class 1-1. Let’s go drop our things off first. I heard there’s an opening ceremony this afternoon, and Hokage-sama will be there too. »

The two chatted as they walked, and before long, they arrived at the classroom, which already had quite a few people in it.

The room suddenly went quiet as the two entered together, but it quickly became noisy again a moment later.

« So... so handsome ! Sakura, did you see him ? The boy who just came in—he is a boy, right ? »

The speaker was a blonde little girl with a very unique style. She gently nudged the pink-haired little girl in the seat next to her, her face filled with excitement and a trace of uncertainty. As if remembering something, her expression shifted, and two suspicious patches of crimson appeared on her small face.

« Based on the clothes, he should be. He is... quite good-looking, but I still think Sasuke is cooler ! Sasuke is the best ! »

The girl called Sakura’s eyes flickered for a moment before becoming determined, as if she had made some kind of decision.

« Sakura, you don’t understand ! The two of them are different.

Sasuke has an aggressive kind of handsome, while he... he has a more inclusive kind of beauty.

I think he’s more my type. I just don’t know what his name is... » The two began to discuss with giggles.

In the row behind them, a pineapple-headed boy couldn’t help but hold his forehead and sigh softly :

« Tsk... what a drag. What’s wrong with Ino and these other girls in the class ?

I have a feeling the next few years won’t be easy. Don’t you think so, Choji ? »

'Crunch, crunch.'

What responded to him was a rhythmic sound of potato chips being chewed.

« Tsk. »

...

As soon as Asakawa Shin entered the classroom, he quickly scanned the room to determine everyone’s seating. He turned to Naruto and said nonchalantly :

« Let’s sit over there. I like the window seat. »

After saying that, he pulled Naruto to sit in the row of seats directly behind Sasuke. This position was simply perfect.

Sasuke was in front, and across the aisle from Naruto sat Hyuga Hinata.

'Is this Hinata ? It’s my first time seeing her...'

While Asakawa Shin was lost in thought, the state of this little Byakugan girl was a bit off.

She tried her best to shrink her head into her collar, leaving only her large eyes visible. Her two index fingers were constantly poking each other, and her exposed face quickly turned bright red, with steam seemingly rising from her head.

'Too close, Naruto-kun is sitting too close to me... Ugh, what should I do ?'

Uchiha Sasuke, sitting in front, also seemed a bit strange. He looked restless, as if he were being watched by some primordial beast.

Asakawa Shin saw through him at a glance ; the kid was pretending to look out the window while actually using his peripheral vision to secretly observe him.

So, Shin smiled at him. As the saying goes, one doesn’t hit a smiling face ; it’s never wrong to smile more and show some goodwill.

Who would have thought that instead of smiling back, Uchiha Sasuke would flinch at the smile and quickly turn his head back, pretending to look straight ahead.

His little bottom was even wiggling around as if he were sitting on pins and needles, leaving Asakawa Shin dazed.

But who was he ? He was the Ninja World’s Know-It-All. With a roll of his eyes, he immediately showed an expression of realization.

'As expected of Indra’s reincarnation, to be so sensitive to a gaze from behind.

It’s said that back in the day, if someone stood behind Uchiha Madara, he couldn’t even pee.

I didn’t believe it at first, but now it seems these unofficial histories might not necessarily be fake.

Fine, I’ll just stop staring. How fussy.'

He turned to chat with Naruto, and sure enough, Uchiha Sasuke’s symptoms eased, which further confirmed his judgment.

...

About twenty minutes later, the school bell rang, and a young Ninja with a Gale Swordsman hairstyle stepped inside.

He scanned the room from the podium, his gaze seemingly pausing on Naruto for a split second before he spoke :

« Hello everyone, I am your homeroom teacher for Class 1-1, Iruka Umino. From today on, you are all students of the Ninja Academy.

There are no classes this morning. In a moment, everyone will take turns introducing themselves to get to know each other.

I’ll give a demonstration first, but let’s take attendance now. »

« Kiba Inuzuka. »

« Present ! (Woof !) »

« Asakawa Shin. »

« Present. »

« Shino Aburame. »

« Present. »

« Huh ? When did this person appear ? He gave me a fright ! »

« I don’t know, I didn’t see him either. »

« Me neither. »

« Hmm ? How rude. I’ve been here the whole time, it’s just... »

There seemed to be some commotion in the class, but Asakawa Shin acted as if he hadn’t heard it at all, because a voice suddenly appeared in his mind...

[Loading complete...]

[Ding ! Sensed that the host has entered the Ninja Academy and become a Genin Reserve. Awakening the Dyeing Hair Role-play System !]

[Genin Reserve status is a newbie benefit. Please work hard, host, to become an official Ninja as soon as possible.]

'Sys... Sys... Sys...'

[Please read this system manual and complete real-name authentication to bind this system as soon as possible !]

'...tem ! Bind ? Right, I need to bind it quickly. The manual ? Where is the manual ?

Huh ? What kind of decent person has a manual over five thousand pages long ?

Just scroll to the end, check « I accept, » confirm, real name Asakawa Shin, bind...'

« Whew... » Only after finishing the binding did Asakawa Shin let out a long sigh of relief. In just a short moment, he was already covered in sweat.

'That was close... Finally bound ! After all that trouble, I was about to give up.

If something had happened to the system after it finally finished loading, I’d be finished.'

...

In a certain high-dimensional space.

« Whew... that was close... he finally fell for it ! Luckily he didn’t find the compensation and complaint clauses in the manual, otherwise I’d be finished !

Hmph, even if he finds them later, it’ll be too late. All rights of interpretation belong to me.

But I’ve done right by you ; this is the Minister’s latest work. I only got it after drinking with the Minister many times.

The Minister did say there’s a minor, harmless bug, but that should be fine, right ? »

« It’s fine, that’s the Minister we’re talking about. Are you done ? If you’re done, let’s go for a drink. »

« Sigh ! Forget it, I’m not drinking anymore. From today on, I’m quitting alcohol ! »

« Eh ? Why ? »

« Drinking is bad for the health ; my butt always hurts after drinking ! »

« ? »
