# Chapitre 142 — Shut Up, Big Brother!

Orochimaru stood in the center of the Hokage Office, looking awkward and as humble as a minnow.

Senju Tobirama glared, staring fiercely at Hiruzen Sarutobi : « Look at the fine student you’ve taught ! Daring to use villagers for live human experiments—couldn’t you have just captured people from other villages instead ? »

Hashirama couldn’t help but whisper a reminder from the side : « Tobirama, even if they were from other villages, such behavior is... »

« Shut up, Big Brother ! »

Tobirama immediately retorted sharply : « I strongly opposed distributing the Tailed Beasts to various countries back then, but you insisted on not listening.

Look at the current situation ; three Ninja World Wars have broken out since your death, leaving the world in ruins and soaked in blood.

Big Brother, have you still not realized your mistake ? »

Upon hearing this, Senju Hashirama immediately shrank his neck, not daring to make a sound.

At this moment, Orochimaru coughed lightly, attempting to defend himself : « Actually, those experimental subjects were all provided by Root, and the leader of Root is none other than Danzo Shimura.

In the end, I was framed by that villain. »

Hiruzen Sarutobi nearly laughed out loud upon hearing this.

He silently gave Orochimaru a thumbs-up in his heart : 'Well done, Orochimaru ! Since that old fox Danzo isn’t here, feel free to pour all the dirt on him.'

Hashirama, standing to the side, exclaimed : « Danzo ? Tobirama, isn’t that your student ? »

« Big Brother ! I told you to shut up, are you deaf ? » Tobirama bristled instantly, shouting a stern rebuke.

« ...Oh. »

Tsunade covered her face with her hand, unable to bear watching the behavior of this group.

After a long while, everyone finally agreed on a compromise.

In name, Orochimaru remained a missing-nin of Konoha and would continue to be wanted.

In reality, he would move into the shadows, becoming the Root of Konoha in the dark, responsible for providing intelligence, executing secret missions, and conducting scientific research.

This was the best result everyone present could accept.

After all, regardless of whether he was framed or not, the fact that he used villagers for experiments was an indelible truth.

Being able to avoid the death penalty and regain his utility was already a lenient, extra-legal treatment for him.

After this matter was settled, everyone’s gaze turned simultaneously to Asakawa Shin.

Hiruzen Sarutobi looked hesitant, asking cautiously : « Are we really going to initiate the 'Konoha Crush Plan' ? »

Asakawa Shin nodded solemnly, his tone firm : « The rat hiding in the shadows is far too troublesome ; this time, we must force him out into the open to solve the problem once and for all.

I’m actually more worried that they won’t dare to show their faces at all. »

Having said that, he turned to look at Orochimaru, his gaze deep : « Senior Brother, this first step depends on your persuasion. »

Hearing this, Orochimaru narrowed his eyes, and a sinister arc curled at the corner of his mouth : « Rest assured, Junior Brother. I will certainly prepare a 'big gift' for you. »

Asakawa Shin also narrowed his eyes and returned the smile : « Then I’ll leave it to you, Senior Brother. By the way, I see you are all alone, which must be quite taxing. How about I recommend an assistant to you ? »

As soon as he finished speaking, Asakawa Shin turned his head, cast his gaze outside the door, and ordered lightly : « Come in, Kabuto. »

« Yes, Lord Shin. »

A response echoed from outside the door, and the next moment, a white-haired young Ninja stepped inside.

He first bowed respectfully to Asakawa Shin, then his gaze swept over everyone present, and he bowed deeply to Orochimaru, saying in a humble tone :

« Greetings to all the adults, and greetings to Lord Orochimaru. My name is Yakushi Kabuto. From now on, I will be your assistant. I look forward to your guidance. »

Orochimaru examined him thoughtfully for a moment, then finally nodded slightly in return.

Seeing this, Senju Tobirama made the final decision : « Since that’s the case, let’s not delay ; let’s act now ! »

Tsunade was a bit anxious : « Second Grandfather, are you leaving too ? »

Tobirama nodded : « Naturally. I brought Orochimaru away in broad daylight, so it is only right that I accompany him. This way, it will also increase our bargaining chips. »

Although Tsunade was reluctant, she could only nod helplessly.

The next moment, Tobirama took Orochimaru and Kabuto, flashed, and disappeared without a trace.

The office suddenly became empty.

Asakawa Shin turned to the remaining three, speaking calmly : « Shodai(1st)-sama, you only need a little disguise to tour Konoha freely.

During this time, let Senior Sister Tsunade accompany you. I need to rush back to participate in the Chunin Exams, so I won’t stay any longer. »

As soon as these words were spoken, the three people in the office looked at him with strange expressions.

Asakawa Shin nodded slightly, then flashed, activated the Flying Thunder God, and disappeared without a trace.

Senju Hashirama scratched his head, muttering in astonishment : « Chunin Exams ? Does that mean... this kid is actually still a Genin ? Young people these days are truly remarkable. »

Tsunade straightened her back, her brow filled with undisguised pride : « It’s more than that. Shin has also learned the Sage Mode of Shikkotsu Forest, and he has perfectly inherited all your Wood Release techniques, using them with ease. »

Hearing this, Senju Hashirama was shocked and added a bit of relief : « Is that so ? This kid has already surpassed his master ; even I am left in the dust.

But Little Tsunade, why are you so proud ? Why do I always feel like you’re acting strange ? »

Tsunade hurriedly laughed it off and changed the subject : « Grandfather, what are you talking about ? How am I acting strange ?

By the way, I’ll take you to see the current Konoha, especially the few newly opened casinos. The way they play is very different from our time.

Let’s go, I’m feeling itchy to play ; let me take you to try a few hands ! »

Hearing this, Hashirama’s eyes lit up instantly, and he urged impatiently : « Little Tsunade, why didn’t you say so earlier ? No time to lose, let’s go immediately ! »

Before the words were finished, the two tacitly performed the hand signs for the Transformation Technique, turned into someone else, and went off happily together.

In the office, only Hiruzen Sarutobi remained. He looked at the empty doorway and let out a helpless sigh.

Then he cheered up ; Orochimaru had returned, could Danzo be far behind ? He was really looking forward to seeing the wonderful expression on that guy’s face when he saw Teacher Tobirama.

Heh, just a mere missing-nin.

Meanwhile, Asakawa Shin flashed and appeared in the Forest of Death, having used the Flying Thunder God transfer via the mark on Karin.

His sudden appearance gave a huge fright to Naruto, who was arguing with Sasuke, and he jumped up in shock.

Upon seeing that the person who arrived was Asakawa Shin, Naruto’s shock instantly turned to joy, and he shouted excitedly :

« Shin, so it’s you ! You’re finally back ! What was that move you just used ? That huge wooden man was simply too amazing ! »

Asakawa Shin looked indifferent and smiled humbly : « Hey, that’s nothing amazing ; it was just a casual strike. »

Hearing this, the corner of Sasuke’s eye couldn’t help but twitch. This guy was too good at acting cool ; a casual strike, really ?

But looking at Asakawa Shin’s calm and composed demeanor, he couldn’t refute it.

Moreover, Sasuke also noticed that Asakawa Shin was carrying a sword at his waist, which looked quite cool...

Asakawa Shin turned his gaze to Sasuke and Sakura, asking with concern : « How are you ? How are your injuries ? »

Sasuke waved his hand : « I’m fine. It’s just that Sakura is still very weak and needs rest. »

Asakawa Shin nodded, then reached out his hands to check them.

After the check, Asakawa Shin clapped his hands and said to the three members of Team 7 : « Hmm, you are indeed out of danger. Sakura will fully recover after resting for two days.

Since that’s the case, let’s part ways here. We have already collected our scrolls, so you all need to work hard. »

The three members of Team 7 looked at each other, flames of confidence burning in their eyes, and nodded in unison : « Hmph ! We will also arrive at the central tower soon ; see you then ! »

« Karin, Haku, let’s go. »

« Wood Release : Wood Dragon Technique ! »
