# Chapitre 66 — Day Tiger

Asakawa Shin was not the least bit surprised that Might Guy could learn Moon Walk.

To be precise, he had long wondered if Might Guy would be the one most likely to learn it.

After all, if one followed the original fate, Might Guy could run on air as if it were flat ground during the fierce battle against Uchiha Madara, with speed and flexibility even surpassing Moon Walk.

However, that required him to be in the state of the Gate of Death, with his body amplified to its limit, allowing for passive use. Now, he could actively perform it.

After all, he was the man recognized by Uchiha Madara for his taijutsu ; besides Hashirama, he was the first.

Thinking of this, a trace of curiosity welled up in Asakawa Shin’s heart, so he asked directly : « Guy, how is your training with Rokushiki going ? Have you mastered the other techniques as well ? »

After dodging the sharp attack of the Hundred Birds Phoenix, Might Guy landed on a piece of clear ground nearby that had not been affected.

Hearing Asakawa Shin’s question, he scratched his head in embarrassment : « Among the Rokushiki, I have currently only mastered Soru and Moon Walk. »

« Although I have some insights into Finger Pistol and Tempest Kick, I still need to ponder them carefully. »

« But for Iron Body and Paper Art, I still have no clue. Therefore, I plan to practice the other four techniques first, leaving these two for last to focus on. »

« By the way, Shin, I must thank you for this. Your Rokushiki has been a great help to me ! »

« Rest assured, if you want to become Hokage in the future, I will definitely support you with all my might ! »

Asakawa Shin nodded with a satisfied expression, then his eyes rolled, and he asked with a mischievous smile : « Then, if both Kakashi and I want to become Hokage, and you had to choose one, who would you pick ? »

« W-what ? » Asakawa Shin’s sudden question nearly choked Might Guy. He stared at Asakawa Shin, wanting to speak but stopping, wanting to stop but speaking again, until his face turned red.

Just as he was at a loss, his body paused slightly as if he remembered something, and he said solemnly to Asakawa Shin :

« Shin, this is not the time for idle chat. Let’s finish this spar first, then we can talk about it in detail. »

« Opening the Gate of Wonder places a huge burden on my body, and I don’t have much time left to maintain it. We must end this quickly. »

« Next, I will use the taijutsu ultimate technique of the Gate of Wonder — Day Tiger. »

« This move is extremely dangerous and is my final trump card. Please be careful and make sure not to get hurt. »

« Oh, oh, Day Tiger ? I got it. Just do your best, don’t worry about me ! » Asakawa Shin also remembered this, feeling slightly awkward.

He found opening the Gate of Wonder as simple as eating and drinking, completely ignoring the pressure Might Guy was under at the moment.

He hadn’t expected Might Guy to be so polite, chatting with him for so long while keeping the Gate of Wonder open, only interrupting when his body truly couldn’t hold on anymore.

...

« Then, Shin, get ready, be careful. Here I come ! »

« Got it, Guy, you’re so long-winded ! Come at me head-on ! »

Might Guy looked solemn, placing his arms in front of his chest, forming a gesture like a fierce tiger pouncing on prey.

His eyes were firm, and with a low roar, a super-high-speed straight punch broke through the air like lightning.

The air was sharply compressed in an instant, turning into a fierce tiger that roared towards Asakawa Shin with the momentum to tear everything apart.

« Well done ! »

Facing this earth-shattering blow, Asakawa Shin was fearless. Busoshoku Haki quickly condensed on his right fist, sparking black lightning.

Then, his punch shot out like a dragon, attacking the distance with astonishing power, and he also unleashed a powerful shockwave to meet the Day Tiger head-on.

« Fist Bone · Impact ! »

Under the watchful eyes of everyone, the Day Tiger and the Fist Bone collided violently in the center between the two, twisting and deforming in an instant before exploding with a bang.

The Day Tiger was like an inflated balloon ; the massive amount of air compressed within was suddenly released, forming a world-destroying shockwave that spread in all directions.

The Fist Bone was not to be outdone ; the countless amounts of Busoshoku Haki contained within erupted like a raging tsunami, sweeping across the surroundings.

The two powerful explosions intertwined, and the entire training ground shook violently as if there were an earthquake.

If not for the Four Red Yang Formation blocking the spread of this shockwave in time, its power would likely have reached several kilometers away.

Even so, the onlookers were knocked off balance by the sudden tremor, struggling to stand firm.

For a moment, exclamations rose one after another.

« Holy crap ! I’m dead, I’m dead ! »

« Is this what a battle between Ninjas looks like ? It’s too terrifying ! The aftermath is so powerful ! »

« I was wondering what this barrier was for before ; so it was to protect us ! »

« It seems Lord Shin had foreseen this and was thinking of us all along ! So grateful ! »

Compared to these villagers, the focus of Asakawa Shin’s classmates was completely different :

Shikamaru was sweating profusely because, following this trend, the probability of Asakawa Shin becoming Hokage was already very high !

And the Naruto he was secretly supporting had just been defeated by Uchiha Sasuke and was still lying in the hospital...

Rock Lee looked at the competition field with longing ; both people inside were fighting with taijutsu, and the power was so immense !

This made him feel like he saw a bright future ! Extra training tonight, strive for an early recovery !

Inuzuka Kiba had stars in his eyes, seemingly fantasizing about something : « A real man should be like Shin ! »

Hinata sneered inwardly : 'Hmph ! What’s so great about that ? One day I will replace him and become a true peerless genius !'

Ino was a bit worried : 'The explosion just now was so powerful, I wonder how Shin is doing. But looking at the barrier still being maintained, there shouldn’t be any problems, right ?'

Karin looked nervous, glancing around : 'Bad, very bad ! The bathroom was blown down ! What to do ? Suddenly I really need to pee...'

...

As time passed, the dust in the field gradually dissipated, and the scene inside was revealed.

A huge pit had been blown into the center of the original training ground, appearing pitch black and bottomless from a distance.

Might Guy sat leaning against a piece of debris, panting heavily.

Maintaining the Gate of Wonder for a long time and releasing a move like Day Tiger had consumed him greatly.

Immediately after, he had to withstand the shockwaves of two explosions in a narrow space, which had almost drained all his physical strength.

At this moment, he was exhausted, as if all his strength had been drained.

In sharp contrast, Asakawa Shin had his Haki released to form an invisible armor.

Not only was he unscathed in this explosion, but he hadn’t even been stained by a speck of dust, appearing calm and composed, with plenty of energy to spare.

« That was truly a hearty battle ! »

« Don’t you think so ? Guy. »
