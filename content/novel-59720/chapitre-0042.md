# Chapitre 42 — Slashing Wave

Asakawa Shin held his sword overhead with one hand, as a massive amount of Haki merged into it, forming visible streaks of black lightning.

Time seemed to slow down ; a heart-palpitating pressure permeated the air. Finally, Asakawa Shin swung his sword downward.

« Slashing Wave ! »

A massive black sword wave was instantly generated, hurtling toward the central building of Kusagakure—the Kusagakure Building.

The sword wave was over a hundred meters high, slicing open the ground like ripples on water as it passed.

Trees, buildings, and Ninja who couldn’t dodge in time were all shredded into debris, swept away into the distance by the sword wave.

The target, the Kusagakure Building, was no exception, being sliced in half in an instant.

It was then shattered into fragments of varying sizes by the airflow driven by the sword wave, scattering all over the ground.

The sword wave met no resistance, its speed undiminished as it traversed the entire Village until it reached the horizon.

It left behind only a bottomless, deep rift, a testament to its existence.

The massive commotion instantly jolted Kusagakure awake. Countless Ninja leaped onto rooftops, staring at the scene in stunned silence.

« What kind of Ninjutsu is this ? Such power ! The Village has been... »

« There’s no Chakra fluctuation, yet the Kusagakure Building was destroyed without a reaction... This is a disaster. »

An Anbu intern on duty was in a panic ; he was the one responsible for sensory reconnaissance today.

« Enemy attack ! It’s an enemy attack ! Sound the alarm, quickly ! »

« What’s the point of an alarm after such a huge commotion ? Have you lost your mind ? »

« Where is the leader ? Where are the elders ? Come out quickly and take charge ! »

A Kusagakure Jonin looked at the wreckage of the Kusagakure Building and swallowed hard. « The leader might have already perished, and even the Anbu elite... »

His guess was correct. Just moments ago, the leader of Kusagakure and the high-ranking officials were in a meeting, debating whether to continue sending elite Ninja to assassinate the Nara Squad in secret.

They never expected an attack to fall from the sky, wiping them all out in an instant. The defensive Ninjutsu they deployed was as flimsy as paper, pierced through with a single strike.

The high-ranking officials of Kusagakure—completely annihilated.

...

Karin looked at the scene before her in disbelief. Even though she hadn’t left the Village since she could remember, she understood exactly how powerful Asakawa Shin was to be able to use such an attack.

Her eyes sparkling, she ran forward and looked at Asakawa Shin with adoration, then at the sword. She said nothing, yet her expression said everything.

Kana, being a mother, was much more composed than Karin, though her breathing was ragged, her legs were weak, and she suddenly felt a pressing need to relieve herself.

Asakawa Shin secretly enjoyed Karin’s worshipful gaze and smiled at her. « Karin, was that move cool ? Do you want to learn it ? »

« Lord Shin, that move was incredibly cool ! I was completely dazed.

But Lord Shin, why are you asking if I want to learn ? Can I actually learn it ? »

Karin asked with anticipation, her large eyes blinking brightly.

'Haha, she really is a child.'

Asakawa Shin felt a surge of satisfaction from Karin’s admiring gaze.

As for Karin’s doubt, Asakawa Shin did not disappoint her and nodded.

« Of course. Karin, you’re still young. Once we return to Konoha, I’ll arrange for you to enter the Ninja Academy.

Normally, you should enroll next year, but since you are my maid, you must naturally serve by my side.

At that time, you’ll join our class as a transfer student. As for the missed lessons, I will personally teach you during this period.

Once you have a good foundation, you must not neglect Fuinjutsu and Taijutsu. I will be very strict, so be prepared, Karin. »

Instead of being afraid, Karin was filled with motivation.

She secretly vowed to train hard so she could become Lord Shin’s right-hand person and share his burdens in the future.

Kana heard everything clearly as well. Seeing Asakawa Shin plan out Karin’s future so clearly, she felt incredibly happy for her daughter.

'Lord Shin is truly too kind to us. I must serve him well in the future to repay his kindness.'

...

« The enemy is here ! » A loud shout broke the warm atmosphere between the three.

Ninja in the distance heard the shout and converged on their location. Before long, they had the place surrounded so tightly that not even a drop of water could leak through.

« They are the enemies ? Are you sure ? »

« Yeah, I recognize those two women. Aren’t they just special tools from the Medical Corps ?

As for that little boy, could someone so young really release such a terrifying attack ? »

« No ! It’s them ! Look closely, the starting point of that rift is right here ! »

A sensory Ninja chimed in, drenched in sweat :

« The enemy is definitely him ! It’s that little boy. Do not underestimate him under any circumstances !

Because his Chakra capacity is greater than everyone here combined ! »

Hearing the report from the sensory Ninja, everyone present was shaken. While Kusagakure wasn’t one of the Five Great Ninja Villages, it was a first-rate power beneath them.

There were over a thousand Ninja gathered here now. The combined Chakra of these thousand Ninja actually couldn’t compare to this little boy ?

With such a staggering amount of Chakra and that terrifying attack from earlier, was he a monster ?

A white-bearded elder slowly stepped forward, pushed to the front by the crowd. He said with a solemn expression :

« Sir, we have no past grievances or recent enmity. I don’t believe we have offended you. Why have you come to our Kusagakure today to start a massacre ? »

Asakawa Shin laughed loudly. « No past grievances or recent enmity ? What nonsense !

The high-ranking officials of your Kusagakure were just in a meeting planning to attack our Konoha’s Assistant to the Hokage, and they had even taken action earlier.

I could have overlooked that and not settled accounts with you.

But you actually dared to bully my elder and younger sisters. Look at how much you’ve bitten them ! This is truly unforgivable ! »

As soon as he said this, the expressions of everyone around them became strange, as if they wanted to complain but didn’t dare to.

Kana turned completely red, her legs pressed together, her whole body trembling.

'Is he insane ? He doesn’t care about an attack on the Assistant to the Hokage, but bullying his sisters is unforgivable ?

What do you take Konoha’s Assistant to the Hokage for ? Wait, this kid is from Konoha ?

And they are mother and daughter, right ? No matter how you calculate the seniority, they can’t be your sisters !'

The white-bearded elder’s face darkened. « The mother and daughter, Kana and Karin, are Ninja of our Kusagakure. Since when did they become your sisters ?

If you wanted to find an excuse to cause trouble, you should have investigated beforehand. It seems today, you intend to oppose us to the bitter end ? »

Asakawa Shin stood with one hand on his hip, scanning the field.

« Correct. Today, I only wish to beat everyone here to death, or be beaten to death by everyone here ! »
