# Chapitre 84 — Medical Treatment

« Senior Sister, I have told you countless times, I really cannot teach you any gambling techniques. »

Asakawa Shin had been pestered by Tsunade the entire way and was incredibly annoyed ; he could no longer remember how many times he had rejected her.

Tsunade remained all smiles, asking curiously : « Junior Brother, just teach me~ How did you know the exact points ? Could it be that you listened for them ? »

She thought to herself, if she learned this trick, wouldn’t she be able to dominate the gambling world ?

« Sigh ! How exactly am I supposed to explain this to you ? Let me think... I have an idea. Watch closely, three leaves will fall from the tree in a moment. »

Asakawa Shin pointed at a large tree ahead, his tone carrying a hint of mystery.

A few seconds later, a gentle breeze blew past, causing the branches to sway slightly. Just as he had said, three leaves detached and drifted slowly to the ground.

A flash of surprise crossed Tsunade’s eyes as she spoke in disbelief : « Junior Brother, how did you know ? Could it be that you can sense the state of plants ? »

Asakawa Shin did not answer, instead raising his hand to point at the pond on the right, his tone firm : « Next, a fish will jump out of the water. »

Barely seconds after his voice fell, the calm water surface suddenly rippled, and a small fish leapt out, tracing a silver arc before falling back into the water with a « splash. »

This scene caused the pupils of those present to contract sharply in shock. Combining this with Asakawa Shin’s performance in the casino, Uchiha Itachi couldn’t help but speak first :

« Could it be that Lord Shin is able to see visions of the future for a short time ? »

Asakawa Shin gave Itachi an appreciative look, confirming his guess : « Correct, I possess the ability to foresee the future. »

Upon hearing this, everyone exclaimed in unison : « Foresee the future ? »

Tsunade sighed in sudden realization : « No wonder, no wonder I couldn’t win against you no matter how I changed my techniques. So you can actually foresee the future. This kind of method... truly worthy of the God of Gamblers, I didn’t lose unjustly ! »

Asakawa Shin rolled his eyes at her, unable to help but complain internally : 'Winning against you has nothing to do with this ; even if I replaced myself with Akamaru, it would be a guaranteed win~'

Asakawa Shin’s gaze returned to Itachi, who had spoken just moments ago : 'I knew I had forgotten something ; I was so busy eating, drinking, and having fun today that I completely forgot about Itachi’s condition.'

Thinking of this, a wave of guilt welled up in his heart. He turned and looked at Tsunade seriously : « Senior Sister, actually, I came to find you this time because there is something I need your help with. »

Tsunade was slightly stunned, a trace of joy flashing in her eyes as she murmured : « Oh~ so you have something to ask of me. »

She walked up to Asakawa Shin with a gentle expression, softly stroking his little head, and said very loyally : « What is this about helping or not ? You and I are as close as siblings ; your business is my business, isn’t it ? Even if you are heartless and won’t teach me gambling techniques, I will still help you with all my might. Tell me, what is it ? »

Asakawa Shin looked at her in surprise, not expecting his Senior Sister to be such a reasonable person.

He pointed at Itachi and said : « Actually, it is not me, but Itachi. He has contracted a Bloodline Limit disease, and my Medical Ninjutsu is somewhat powerless against it. Senior Sister, you know me. I can handle surgery, but for this kind of pathological treatment, I have to come to you. »

« Bloodline Limit disease ? The Sharingan ? » Tsunade’s brows furrowed. This kind of ailment could be said to be one of the most troublesome in the Ninja World, extremely difficult to deal with.

She didn’t care that they were on the street and grabbed Itachi’s wrist to examine him carefully. After a long while, she said in a low voice : « His body indeed shows signs of being eroded by Yin Release, but fortunately, he is still in the early stages of the disease. If treated in time, there is a great hope for recovery. »

Tsunade thought carefully for a moment before giving several suggestions : « First, try to use your Sharingan as little as possible in the future. It is best to completely seal it until your body is fully developed and not use its power. Second, you must persist in daily physical training to enhance cell activity and resist the erosion of Yin Release. I will formulate two prescriptions for you : one for internal use to repair damage, and one for external application to strengthen the body. »

Uchiha Itachi nodded seriously and bowed to thank Tsunade.

Tsunade turned to Asakawa Shin and said slowly : « Actually, there are two more thorough methods. One is to directly remove the Sharingan, replace them with ordinary eyeballs, and switch them back after the body recovers. Two is to design a Fuinjutsu to lock away the Yin Release energy leaking from the Sharingan. However, the workload is too great and requires a lot of trial and error, which is difficult to complete in the short term. I heard from our teacher that you are researching Fuinjutsu ? Why don’t you give it a try ? »

Asakawa Shin nodded slightly, understanding in his heart. The purpose of these measures was to restore the balance of Yin and Yang in the body.

After all, Itachi was only thirteen, and his body could not yet bear the huge burden brought by the Sharingan. He thought that transplanting First Hokage’s cells would definitely solve this problem, but he did not have the complete technology. However, the current plan was sufficient to cope with it.

« As expected of Senior Sister Tsunade, to provide several detailed plans so quickly ; the reputation of the Medical Sage is well-deserved. » Asakawa Shin exclaimed with admiration on his face.

Upon hearing this, Tsunade pouted, retorting with a displeased expression : « I don’t want to be any Medical Sage ; I want to be the Gambling Saint. Junior Brother, just teach me. »

Asakawa Shin said with a headache : « Senior Sister, it really is not that I won’t teach you, but that this secret technique must be used in conjunction with my special constitution. You can understand it as a special Bloodline Limit ; it is truly difficult to teach to you. »

« Bloodline Limit ? »

Upon hearing this, Tsunade’s originally sparkling eyes instantly dimmed, and she lowered her head in discouragement : « It’s actually that troublesome. »

Seeing this, Asakawa Shin couldn’t bear it. He had a flash of inspiration and suggested : « Senior Sister, you are actually quite amazing too. Not just anyone can lose every time they gamble and earn the title of 'Legendary Big Fat Sheep'. Do you remember our previous plan ? If it succeeds, not only will you be able to easily pay off your gambling debts, but you will also have a continuous stream of funds to enjoy yourself in the Ninja World to your heart’s content. »

Tsunade’s eyes lit up after hearing this. Thinking about it carefully, this actually didn’t seem so bad, right ?

« When I’m in a good mood, I can’t help but want to drink. »

She said to Asakawa Shin with a smile : « How can we come to the Land of Hot Springs without soaking in the hot springs ? Since it’s getting late, I know of an excellent hot spring inn. We can enjoy the hot springs while drinking fine wine ; wouldn’t that be wonderful ? »

...

Tsunade’s taste was indeed unique ; the inn she chose was extremely luxurious, and naturally, the price was not cheap. However, Asakawa Shin didn’t even look and waved his hand to book the top-tier suite.

The group headed to their suite full of anticipation. Indeed, the space inside was incredibly spacious and comfortable, and it was thoughtfully equipped with two huge hot springs, separated for men and women.

Just as Asakawa Shin was about to go soak in the hot spring with Itachi, he was grabbed by the three women.

« Hmm ? What are you doing ? Aren’t you going to soak in the hot spring ? » Asakawa Shin looked at them strangely.

They also looked at him with strange expressions : « Hmm ? Aren’t you coming with us ? »

Asakawa Shin said as a matter of course : « Of course not, that’s a female hot spring, and I am a boy, how can I go over there. »

The three women looked at each other and said with mischievous smiles : « Let’s go, little brat, let your big sisters check your body. »

« Hey ? No, don’t ! Itachi, save me... »

« ... »
