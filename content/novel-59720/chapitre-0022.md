# Chapitre 22 — The Three Little Genin Assemble

After school, Training Ground No. 5.

Stimulated by the « lunch box incident » at noon, Naruto was training desperately. He had found a square stone and was practicing gripping and lifting it.

Unfortunately, it was futile. After school, Asakawa Shin had heard Hinata instruct her private kitchen :

From now on, the large white box was to be split into two medium-sized boxes, and each box was to be fitted with straps and handles for Naruto to carry easily.

In this way, even though it was still heavy, Naruto could now carry it.

'Tsk, why do I suddenly feel full ? I don’t even want dinner. Should I remind Naruto ? Forget it, it’s rare for him to be so motivated.'

...

Ignoring Naruto, Asakawa Shin patrolled the field to check everyone’s training progress. Compared to last week, there was significant improvement ; it seemed no one had slacked off over the weekend. The one with the most improvement was undoubtedly Hinata.

Since Hinata’s diet became regular and her nutrition caught up, combined with a scientific training plan, her physique was transforming at an astonishing speed. The box that Naruto couldn’t even lift at noon, she could easily carry on her shoulder and walk with nimble strides.

At this moment, she stood on the training ground, her pink fists dancing with a fierce wind, her footwork like lightning, soft yet firm. Each strike carried a precise sense of rhythm, her soft pink hair flying in the wind, yet her eyes were sharp as swords.

Her confident and agile figure resembled a hunting leopard, putting immense pressure on Ino and Sakura nearby.

Only after hearing Asakawa Shin’s explanation at noon did they suddenly realize that Hinata was actually a one-in-a-million peerless genius. For all these years, her talent had been buried simply because she couldn’t eat enough.

Otherwise, why would the title of « genius » in the Hyuga Clan have fallen to Neji ?

For some reason, Asakawa Shin always saw his own shadow in Hinata. Well, it must be an illusion.

It was all Naruto’s fault ; Hinata seemed like a different person after being stimulated.

Just as he was thinking this, three people slowly walked over from afar : Neji, Rock Lee, and Tenten.

Today, Neji had approached him, wanting to join the Six Styles Training Class. Asakawa Shin naturally agreed, but he had one condition : he must bring Rock Lee and Tenten along. This concerned his « Konoha Twelve Genin » collection plan and could not fail.

Although Neji didn’t understand, he still found Rock Lee and Tenten after school. The two agreed without hesitation. This was, after all, Asakawa Shin’s Six Styles Training Class, a legendary existence in the Ninja Academy. Rock Lee, in particular, felt as if he had won the lottery ; he was so excited he looked like he was about to jump.

At this time, Rock Lee hadn’t met Guy yet and was at his most confused stage. As a Ninja, his Chakra reserves were pitifully low, and he couldn’t even perform the simplest Ninjutsu. The same applied to Genjutsu ; he could only rely on Taijutsu to fight. Could such a person really be called a Ninja ?

Therefore, the day he saw Asakawa Shin perform Rokushiki on the playground, it was like a believer seeing a deity.

'No... no Chakra reaction ? This is actually pure Taijutsu ? What exactly is this ? Can I... can I do it too ?'

A glimmer of hope arose in Rock Lee’s heart. If pure Taijutsu could reach this level, then he could do it too. Even if he wasn’t a genius, so what ? If he couldn’t do it once, he would put in ten times, a hundred times the effort. If that still wasn’t enough, then a thousand, ten thousand times the effort.

Asakawa Shin sized up the three approaching. Rock Lee didn’t have his iconic green jumpsuit and bowl cut yet ; instead, he sported a « pigeon » hairstyle, looking quite fashionable, which made Asakawa Shin’s mouth twitch as he barely kept a straight face.

Tenten hadn’t changed much from the future ; there were two cute blushes on her small face, and her iconic two buns on her head made her look like a little Nezha.

« You’re here. I’ve been waiting for a long time. First of all, welcome to the Six Styles Training Class. From today on, gather at Training Ground No. 5 after school, and we will train together. »

« Taijutsu training is a process of accumulation ; you cannot be absent without a valid reason. »

« Later, I will take you to see Shikamaru. He is the class monitor of the training class and is responsible for coordinating and managing all the affairs of the training class. He will also formulate detailed training plans for each of you, and you must strictly follow the requirements. »

« In addition, the training class is divided into a boys' group and a girls' group. Tenten, come over and meet your girls' group leader — Ino Yamanaka. If you have any problems, you can report them to Ino. Ino, this is Tenten. »

« Hello, Ino, you are so pretty. »

« Haha, no way, Tenten, you are so cute too. »

Asakawa Shin took them to familiarize themselves with the environment and introduced Ino, Naruto, and Shikamaru to them one by one.

« By the way, the leaders of the two groups are not set in stone. We will have a competition every month, and the one with the best training results will become the new leader. »

« You are one year older than the other members, and your physical development is more mature. This is a huge advantage, so you have a great chance ! »

This was a lie. There were monster-like existences in both groups that could no longer be bridged by a small age gap.

However, if it could stimulate their competitive spirit and make them work hard, then a white lie would be forgiven, right ?

« Alright, that’s about it. Do you have any other questions ? » Asakawa Shin asked, looking at the three who were eager to try.

« Um... I have a question. Why did you choose me ? » Rock Lee asked, somewhat confused.

« Neji is a famous Hyuga genius, so it’s natural for him to be chosen. Tenten is also the strongest girl in our grade, so I can understand why she was chosen. »

« But me, I’m just a dead last, I can’t even use Ninjutsu... »

« Because you are a genius. » Asakawa Shin interrupted without hesitation.

« Wh... What ? »

« I said ! Because you are a genius, that’s why I chose you. » Asakawa Shin explained again, enunciating every word.

Watching Rock Lee’s bewildered look, he continued softly :

« There are many kinds of geniuses, and you are exactly the genius of effort. Although you may not have such good talent, and things that Neji can learn in one class, you have to spend a day or even several days to learn. »

« But you have never given up, tireless, unafraid of hardship. I have seen all your efforts. »

« And Taijutsu training is a boring and painstaking process. People without great perseverance and great determination cannot persist, and you are exactly that kind of genius. »

« As long as you don’t forget your original intention and keep moving forward, you will definitely become a great Ninja in the future. »

« I believe in my judgment ; my eye for talent has never been wrong. »

« So, keep moving forward, Rock Lee ! I will always be watching you. »

Rock Lee was just born in the wrong world. If he were in the one piece world, would there be any room for that Guy Kobe who worked 200 times harder ?

Rock Lee looked at the young man in front of him with teary eyes. This young man was a genius known to everyone in the Village, his talent was shockingly high, and even the Hokage had taken him as an apprentice.

And now, this young man, as dazzling as the sun, called him a genius—a genius of effort ! Had he finally been recognized by someone ?

« Wuu wuu wuu, thank you, Shin, thank you for your recognition, I... I will work hard to move forward, wuu wuu wuu... »

Looking at Rock Lee who was crying like a baby, Asakawa Shin was also a little helpless. Did he overdo it ?

« Alright, Rock Lee, stop crying. Go find Shikamaru. Show him your strength, and he will formulate a training plan for you. Go on. »

After Rock Lee finally calmed down, Asakawa Shin suddenly seemed to remember something and said to Rock Lee : « By the way, Rock Lee, your hairstyle is very nice ; it reminds me of an old friend. »

« I was able to notice you in the first place because of your hairstyle. »

« Rock Lee, promise me, okay ? Don’t change your hairstyle in the future—as proof of our friendship. »

« ...Proof of friendship, huh ? I understand, Shin. I will definitely not let you down ! »
