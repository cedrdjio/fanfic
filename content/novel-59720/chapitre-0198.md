# Chapitre 198 — The Gaara Rescue Mission

Inside the deep underground base within the Land of Grass, the dim candlelight flickered unsteadily.

Black Zetsu was leaning over, studying a topographical map on the table, when suddenly, a strong spatial fluctuation tore through the air, and Asakawa Shin’s figure appeared out of thin air without warning.

« Little brother, you’re not dead ? » Black Zetsu’s form froze in shock before he finally spoke in surprise.

Asakawa Shin’s face darkened, and he rolled his eyes in annoyance.

Why did everyone ask him that the moment they saw him ? It was really quite rude.

« Third Brother, rest assured, I won’t die even if you do. »

« Then I’m relieved. » Black Zetsu gave a dry laugh and waved his hand dismissively, though his eyes couldn’t help but scan him up and down.

After listening to Asakawa Shin’s brief explanation about the Ancient Kingdom of Loulan, a rare look of wonder flashed in Black Zetsu’s eyes.

As an ancient existence who had lived since a thousand years ago, to be completely ignorant of such a miraculous place was truly shameful.

« I understand what you mean. You want to participate in the Tailed Beast capture plan and specifically take charge of the Two-Tails and Seven-Tails ? »

After a brief moment of thought, Black Zetsu agreed without hesitation.

Although he couldn’t figure out what his little brother was up to, this was clearly a beneficial boost for the Akatsuki Organization with no downsides.

« I’ll handle the communication with Nagato ; you’re free to act as you wish ! »

Meanwhile, in a dark cave on the border of the Hidden Sand Village, the Akatsuki Organization’s Chakra extraction ritual had just concluded.

To the side, Gaara’s lifeless body lay on the cold stone floor.

« Tch, this guy was clearly a perfect Jinchuriki, wasn’t he ? »

Deidara nonchalantly caressed the wound on his severed arm, an undisguised sneer on his face beneath his golden hair.

« Yet for the sake of his so-called 'Village' and 'comrades,' he actually didn’t undergo Tailed Beast transformation. In the end, he was easily defeated by my Explosive Clay... how incredibly boring. »

« Life is fragile and fleeting ; only eternity can be called true art. »

Sasori of the Red Sand stood nearby, coldly stating his own views.

« Hey, hey, Sasori-danna, although I respect you as a senior... »

Deidara curled his lip in dissatisfaction, and the mouth in his palm mimicked the gesture, « The essence of art is an explosion ! Only that moment of brilliance and destruction can be called true art ! »

BOOM— ! !

Before his voice could fade, a deafening explosion suddenly erupted like a thunderclap on a clear day.

The thick rock wall at the cave’s perimeter was violently blasted open into a jagged hole, with rubble flying like rain.

« Oh ? The barrier was flashily destroyed... It seems we can’t keep chatting here. The pursuit force is faster than expected. »

Deidara narrowed his eyes, looking toward the cloud of dust.

« I’ll leave things here to you two. Sasori, Deidara. »

Pain’s astral projection gave a brief command, and then he and the other Akatsuki Organization members vanished as afterimages.

Deidara wasn’t afraid at all ; instead, he was eager to try.

« I’m quite curious about the Nine-Tails Jinchuriki. I’ll take charge of meeting that fellow. »

As he spoke, the small mouth in his palm spat out a glob of clay. He clenched his hand and quickly molded the shape of a bird.

With a soft « poof, » the bird hit the ground and exploded into a large cloud of smoke, instantly expanding into a giant monster bird nearly four meters long.

As Naruto rushed into the cave, his pupils suddenly constricted.

In his field of vision, the giant bird dove down, its sharp beak like iron pincers picking up Gaara’s body and swallowing him whole in an instant, leaving only one leg dangling helplessly in the air.

« Hey, Nine-Tails ! Keep up ! »

Deidara gave a playful smile, tapped his toes on the bird’s back, and controlled the giant bird to break through the gap in the rock wall, speeding toward the sky.

« Put Gaara down ! »

Naruto let out a beast-like roar and chased after him without a second thought.

Kakashi and Sasuke exchanged a look, both seeing the worry in each other’s eyes, and followed closely behind without hesitation.

In the blink of an eye, only Sakura and Lady Chiyo remained in the cave, facing Sasori of the Red Sand’s cold gaze that looked as if he were staring at dead people.

Sakura looked at Sasuke’s determined back and couldn’t help but curse inwardly, then she quickly gathered her focus and clenched her fists, standing side-by-side with Chiyo.

« Brat, how long do you plan on standing there in a daze with your fists clenched ? »

Lady Chiyo’s aged but steady voice echoed in the empty cave like a morning bell, instantly shattering the confusion in the depths of Sakura’s eyes.

Sakura’s body shook slightly, and the residual trace of bitterness in her eyes vanished instantly, replaced by a resolve as cold as ice.

She took a deep breath, her gaze locking onto Sasori of the Red Sand opposite her.

« Sasori is an extremely dangerous Puppet Master. Every one of his attacks is coated in deadly poison ; even a scratch means certain death ! » Lady Chiyo explained at a rapid pace.

Sakura nodded understandingly ; she had some experience when it came to dodging attacks.

A flash of surprise crossed Lady Chiyo’s eyes, but she quickly regained her composure.

Even if this child made a slight mistake, she could use her Chakra Threads to provide timely rescue.

However, in the next second, she realized she had been worrying unnecessarily.

« I just need to smash this broken shell, right ? Leave it to me ! »

Sakura gave a confident smile, and before her voice finished, she had already lunged forward like an arrow from a bow.

« Such boastful words ! » Sasori snorted coldly, a flash of contempt in his eyes.

In an instant, Hiruko’s massive body suddenly crouched, its gaping mouth opened wide, and countless Senbon glowing with purple light poured out like a rainstorm, accompanied by the piercing sound of breaking wind, heading straight for the two of them.

With Lady Chiyo’s combat experience, she naturally handled such an attack with ease, dodging it effortlessly.

What was even more shocking was that the pink-haired girl was like a kite dancing in the wind, weaving lightly through the dense barrage of projectiles, her figure elusive as she closed in on Sasori’s main body completely unscathed.

« Well done, Sakura ! »

Lady Chiyo’s eyes showed undisguised admiration.

This girl’s performance far exceeded expectations, leaving her with absolutely no need to be distracted by looking after her.

« Coordinate with me ! » Lady Chiyo barked, her hands quickly forming seals, « I’ll pin down his movements, you take care of destroying Hiruko ! »

« Understood ! »

Sakura gritted her teeth, her toes kicked off the ground violently, and her figure surged upward once more.

Just then, Hiruko’s crimson scorpion tail suddenly transformed into a sharp spear, letting out a scream as it tore through the air, lunging fiercely toward Sakura’s heart.

However, just as the tip of the spear was about to touch Sakura, that unstoppable attack strangely froze in mid-air, as if held fast by an invisible giant hand.

« Now ! »

A sharp light exploded in Sakura’s eyes, and the green Chakra glow on her right fist surged, smashing down on Hiruko like a falling meteorite.

BOOM— !

A loud explosion rang out, and dust rose everywhere.

The hard puppet shell shattered like fragile porcelain before the absolute power, with wood chips and parts flying in all directions.

An afterimage wrapped in a black robe turned into a streak of light, narrowly avoiding the follow-up pursuit and landing leisurely on the ground not far away.

« It really isn’t easy to see one’s grandson, » Lady Chiyo mocked mercilessly.

Sasori’s main body shook slightly, and then he turned around expressionlessly.

Lady Chiyo’s pupils constricted sharply, and she cried out in shock : « How is this possible ? Your face... why hasn’t it changed at all in twenty years ? »

After only a moment of shock, a terrifying hypothesis surged into her heart.

She pointed a trembling finger at Sasori : « Could it be that you’ve turned yourself into a Human Puppet too ? Have you gone mad ? »

Faced with his grandmother’s questioning, a weird smile actually appeared on Sasori’s face.

He slowly raised his hand and took out two ancient scrolls from his robes, which were clearly marked with 'Three' and 'Four'.

« Grandmother, let me show you my art. »

Sasori’s voice was calm to the point of madness, « You’ll be proud of me, right ? »
