# Chapitre 107 — Takigakure

After days of busyness, Asakawa Shin finally finished all the matters in Konoha and made time to prepare for a new journey.

Currently, four Tailed Beasts were still at large and had yet to experience Asakawa Shin’s methods.

He planned to follow his original plan, starting with the Seven-Tails, Chomei. This fellow should be the easiest to obtain among all the Tailed Beasts.

Additionally, he planned to stop by Takigakure to loot a bit and teach them a lesson.

After all, they actually dared to send someone to assassinate the First Hokage-sama back then ; such conduct was truly unforgivable.

The sages said that a feud of ten generations can still be avenged, let alone only a few generations have passed ?

Thinking of this, Asakawa Shin felt even more confident and nodded with conviction. His action was not only justified but also a perfect practice of the will of fire !

With a thought, he had already crossed thousands of mountains and rivers to arrive at the borders of the Land of Waterfall.

Asakawa Shin looked around and felt very satisfied. The Shadow Clone did a good job this time. Now, most countries in the Ninja World had his Flying Thunder God Mark. In the future, he could just teleport to travel, which was truly convenient.

He briefly identified the direction and then, moving like lightning, sped straight towards Takigakure.

Takigakure is a mysterious village hidden behind a giant waterfall. This unique geographical location has prevented Takigakure from ever being invaded by other countries.

To truly enter this village, one must first pass through the waterfall’s curtain of water, then dive into a deep pool within a cave, swim through a winding labyrinth of underground rivers, and finally reach this village that resembles a hidden paradise.

Naturally, this was no challenge for Shin. The Seven-Tails' massive Chakra acted like a precise navigator, clearly pointing out the direction for him.

Before long, Asakawa Shin arrived at a vast body of water.

He looked up and saw bits of light filtering through the water’s surface, indicating he had already left the range of the underground river.

« Looks like this is the place. »

Asakawa Shin slowly surfaced and quietly observed the village before him.

In the center of the village stood an incredibly thick giant tree, its diameter likely exceeding a hundred meters.

Even more amazing was that this giant tree was still full of vitality. Its trunk pierced the clouds, and its lush branches and leaves were like a canopy, covering the entire mountain range.

It was almost like the World Tree from myths and legends, breathtaking and making one feel as if they were in a dream.

This tree was also one of Asakawa Shin’s goals. The Hero’s Water, which Takigakure regarded as a treasure, was produced by it. Just one drop could increase the Chakra in a person’s body several times over, but the price was a drastic consumption of lifespan.

Asakawa Shin naturally scoffed at this Hero’s Water that relied on overdrawing life, but the tree itself was extremely unusual.

He vaguely felt that this giant tree might have some connection to the legendary God Tree ; otherwise, it could never exhibit such miraculous properties.

Takigakure seemed to have been comfortable in this hidden paradise for too long, as they were completely defenseless.

A sharp glint flashed in Asakawa Shin’s eyes, and he immediately activated Earth Release : Hiding in Surface Technique, moving silently underground straight toward the Seven-Tails' location.

The Jinchuriki of the Seven-Tails was named Fu, who was currently a dark-skinned, petite, and cute little girl.

She was leaning on a table reading a book, one small hand propping up her cheek. Her upper and lower eyelids were constantly fighting, and her little head nodded rhythmically, as if she might fall into a dream at any moment.

Asakawa Shin quietly poked his head out behind her and couldn’t help but shake his head at the sight.

It was precisely this kind of laziness and neglect of training that led to her being easily defeated by an old senior rogue ninja from the village in the future, resulting in the Tailed Beast being extracted.

'Well then, today, I shall give you a lesson.'

Fu remained completely unaware, still struggling against the waves of drowsiness.

It wasn’t until she accidentally caught a glimpse of a slightly eerie shadow on the tabletop that her muddled brain barely began to function.

'Eh ? Why do I have two shadows ? Am I so sleepy that I’m seeing double ?'

She quickly rubbed her bleary eyes and looked again, only to be startled. There were indeed two shadows ! Could there be another person standing behind her right now ?

She turned her head abruptly but found nothing. This made her even more suspicious and uneasy. She slowly circled the room, carefully inspecting it. After confirming there was nothing unusual, she breathed a sigh of relief and sat down again :

'It seems I was hallucinating. It must be because I’ve been studying for too long. Sigh, maybe I shouldn’t read this book today ?'

'Eh ? Wait... where’s my book ?'

She quickly searched up and down, but soon her whole body stiffened. She discovered with horror that the eerie shadow had appeared again. Not only that, but the shadow now even had the outline of a book. Was the shadow taking the book to read ?

Fu’s mouth instantly fell open, and just as she was about to let out a terrified scream, she felt a pain in her neck and then passed out with her eyes rolling back.

Asakawa Shin also couldn’t help but roll his eyes, sighing inwardly :

'I’m willing to call you the weakest Jinchuriki. You’re just like a small mob encountered right after leaving the starter village—easily farmed, and there’s no sense of achievement in defeating you.'

But since it had come to this, it was time to get to work.

He skillfully pressed his hand onto Fu’s abdomen and began to absorb the Seven-Tails' Chakra.

The poor Seven-Tails didn’t even get a chance to show its face before a large chunk of its Chakra was forcibly snatched away.

After finishing with the Chakra, Asakawa Shin proceeded to give Fu the usual « full service » as per routine.

That wasn’t all ; he specifically carved a special Flying Thunder God Mark on Fu’s abdomen, shaped like the silver crest of some abyssal demon.

« Not bad, perfect. This counts as a lesson for you. If you don’t study hard, in the future you’ll only be... Hehehe~ »

Asakawa Shin examined his masterpiece with satisfaction, a playful smile curling at the corners of his mouth.

Having completed the primary goal of this trip, he was in high spirits and immediately decided to explore Takigakure’s Library.

To Asakawa Shin, Takigakure’s so-called defenses were practically non-existent. He easily broke the Fuinjutsu at the entrance and quietly slipped inside.

However, after browsing for a bit, Asakawa Shin was greatly disappointed.

Aside from an Earth Release : Earth Spear Technique, the other high-power Ninjutsu that the Takigakure rogue ninja Kakuzu had once used all required the Earth Grudge Fear secret technique as a foundation.

And the records regarding this forbidden technique had long been lost in the current Takigakure.

As for that so-called Earth Spear Technique, it couldn’t even compare to a single hair of Busoshoku Haki. It could only be said to be better than nothing.

Asakawa Shin picked and chose from the remaining Ninjutsu scrolls, preparing to take them back to Konoha to expand the Ninjutsu Library, considering it compensation for their past mistake of assassinating the First Hokage-sama.

« Is this all Takigakure has ? Truly disappointing. I might as well go see that miraculous ancient tree. »

Soon, Asakawa Shin climbed to the top of the giant tree. He closed his eyes and concentrated, extending his perception like roots deep into the massive object beneath his feet.

A moment later, he snapped his eyes open, a flash of surprise in them : « It’s actually slowly absorbing Natural Energy ? Does this tree really have some origin with the legendary God Tree ? »

« It seems that’s not impossible ; otherwise, it would be hard to explain why that Hero’s Water can defy common sense like that. »

« However, if it’s just this, the tree seems useless to me ? Whatever, I’ll leave a Flying Thunder God Mark ; maybe it will come in handy in the future... »

With matters here concluded, Asakawa Shin calculated in his heart : 'It’s time to head to the Hidden Sand Village to beat up Gaara. So busy, it’s really annoying...'
