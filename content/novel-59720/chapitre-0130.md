# Chapitre 130 — The First Exam

« Nani ? The first exam is actually a written test ? Doesn’t that mean I’m doomed ! »

Naruto looked at the test paper in the examiner’s hand, his eyes twitching slightly as he had a very ominous premonition.

After receiving the test paper, he hurriedly scanned it, only to find that he couldn’t understand the questions at all, and immediately clutched his head in despair.

'Oh… oh no, I don’t know a single question ! If this keeps up, won’t I be killed by Sasuke and Sakura ? After all, if I get a zero, the whole team will be disqualified !'

Sasuke also frowned : 'Aren’t these questions too hard ? It’s like they’re forcing us to cheat. But once caught cheating, two points are deducted, and the total score is only ten points. In other words, getting caught five times means immediate disqualification !'

Sakura rubbed her temples : 'With questions this hard, besides me, how many others can answer them ? Setting Sasuke aside, Naruto definitely won’t know !'

She scanned her surroundings, only to be surprised to find that several candidates were already writing furiously...

A bolt of lightning suddenly flashed through her mind.

'So that’s it. This exam isn’t testing our knowledge at all, but rather our intelligence-gathering ability ? By obtaining the correct answers to the test paper without alerting the examiners—only in this way can one pass this exam !'

Other candidates slowly realized this as well, and consequently, the entire exam hall became lively.

For a time, everyone was pulling out all the stops, and the sound of pencils « swish-swishing » on the paper was incessant.

Sand Eye, puppets, Byakugan, dogs, insects, sound waves, mirrors, Sharingan, Mind Transfer Jutsu...

Everyone was working hard on the test, except for Naruto, who was slumped over his desk clutching his head, his legs trembling.

'This is bad, I’m completely terrible at cheating ! It looks like I have no choice but to hold out until the 45th minute and wait for that tenth question. At the very least, I can’t get a zero and hold everyone back !'

« What an idiot~ » Asakawa Shin saw Naruto in this state and smiled until his eyes narrowed into slits.

'Oh well, oh well, at the critical moment, you still have to rely on your old father to bail you out~'

He activated Soru, and with a speed difficult for ordinary people to perceive, he swapped Naruto’s test paper with that of a lucky candidate who had just finished theirs.

A sudden gust of wind swept through the exam hall. The examiners knew someone was cheating, but after careful observation, they found absolutely nothing.

Even Naruto himself only felt a blur before his eyes, and the test paper was already filled out.

He immediately covered his mouth in shock, fearing he might cry out in excitement the next moment.

After a long while, Naruto finally suppressed his surging emotions.

He rubbed his eyes hard, confirmed multiple times that he hadn’t misread it, and then broke into a bright smile.

'But who is helping me ?'

He quietly looked around and saw Asakawa Shin turn back and wink at him, and he immediately realized what had happened.

'That guy Shin... *sniff*, he really is my good brother.'

After the excitement, he hurriedly crossed out the original owner’s name on the test paper and changed it to his own.

'Gaara ? What kind of bird-brained name is that ? It’s not even worth a single hair of mine, Uzumaki Naruto !'

On the other side.

'Nani ? How did my test paper...'

Gaara had just finished writing the answers and breathed a sigh of relief, but the next second, his test paper turned blank, which startled him greatly.

He looked closely and discovered that in the candidate’s name section, the four big characters « Uzumaki Naruto » were written, and he finally understood.

He gritted his teeth and glared fiercely at the back of a certain blonde : 'It’s him ! I remember the other guys calling him Naruto, Naruto. I didn’t expect him to hide such a trick ! Fine, fine, fine, playing dirty, are we ? I’ll remember you.'

For the next period of time, candidates were constantly being caught cheating five times and were expelled from the exam hall along with their other two teammates.

Gaara had no time to care about any of this and just kept writing furiously.

The chief examiner, Ibiki Morino, looked at his watch, finally snorted coldly, and said : « It seems the trash has been cleared out enough. Calculating the time, 45 minutes have already passed. Very well, next, I will announce the tenth question ! »

The candidates all put down their pens and turned their gazes toward the chief examiner.

A hint of amusement flashed in Ibiki’s eyes : « But before that, there is one more rule to add. »

After speaking, he paused again and added : « A rule that will bring despair. »

Hearing this, everyone became nervous.

Seeing the atmosphere in the room becoming increasingly heavy, Ibiki continued : « First, you must make a choice : whether or not you will answer this tenth question. »

« What do you mean ? »

« We have to choose ? Then what happens if we choose not to answer this tenth question ? »

Ibiki smiled grimly : « If you choose not to answer, that candidate will be given a score of zero on the spot, meaning they will be disqualified ! Of course, their two teammates will also be implicated and disqualified together ! »

« What ? If that’s the case, then surely we have to choose to answer. »

« Yeah, being disqualified along with others, this rule is just too reckless ! »

Ibiki ignored the complaints of the crowd and continued to apply pressure : « Next, I will announce the second rule. If a candidate chooses to answer but gets it wrong, the entire team of the person who answered will be disqualified, and they will be permanently stripped of the qualification to participate in the Chunin Exams ! In other words, you will remain Genin for life, unable to be promoted. »

The second rule undoubtedly broke through many people’s psychological defenses. After some hesitation and struggle, quite a few people raised their hands to withdraw, being disqualified along with their teammates.

The exam hall suddenly became much emptier.

Just as more candidates were hesitating about whether to raise their hands and withdraw, Uzumaki Naruto suddenly slammed his hands on the table and stood up : « Don’t underestimate me ! I will absolutely not run away from a fight. I want to participate. Even if I can only be a Genin for the rest of my life, I will definitely find a way to become Hokage. »

He gritted his teeth, his whole body filled with stubbornness : « I... I’m not afraid at all ! »

Cold sweat dripped from Sasuke’s forehead : « This bastard, when making this decision, he didn’t consider us at all, did he ? But... he’s got guts, Naruto. »

Sakura smiled with relief : « That’s right, I almost forgot. Naruto is just that kind of big idiot. The word 'give up' has never been in his dictionary. Whatever, let’s go for it. »

Naruto’s astonishing aura and passionate speech ignited the entire room, and many candidates seemed to be deeply encouraged.

For a long time after that, not a single person chose to withdraw.

'Still 84 people left ?'

Ibiki scanned the room, calculated the remaining number of people in his mind, and was somewhat dissatisfied with the result : « I will ask one last time, is there anyone else who wants to give up ? Think about it carefully. This is a choice where you are gambling your lives. If you want to give up, do it now ! »

Seeing that everyone remained unmoved, he sighed, and then, under the gaze of everyone in the room holding their breath, he softly announced : « In that case, I hereby officially announce that everyone still remaining in this exam hall, you have passed ! »

« ? »

« What ? How did we suddenly pass ? What exactly was the tenth question ? »

Ibiki smiled triumphantly : « That thing never existed from the start ! If you really must ask what the tenth question was, it was the choice you just made between 'quitting' and 'staying' ! »

He paused, scanned the room with eyes like torches, and his voice suddenly rose : « Those with wavering hearts and weak wills have no qualification to be promoted to Chunin ! And you who were able to stay have undoubtedly passed this trial of will ! So, I congratulate you all ; you have passed the first exam ! »

A chorus of cheers immediately erupted in the classroom.

But before they could be happy for too long, a dark shadow crashed through the glass from outside and flew onto the podium.

Everyone looked closely and saw that a curtain had been set up on the podium at some point, completely blocking Ibiki Morino from view.

A heroic-looking female Ninja stood in front of the curtain and struck a cool pose for them : « Hey, hey, hey, now is not the time to be happy. I am your chief examiner for the second exam, Anko Mitarashi ! From here on out, perk up and follow me ! »

Unfortunately, the crowd was indifferent to her meticulously designed entrance, and many people even looked embarrassed.

Only Asakawa Shin reacted, taking the lead in applauding, which made Anko’s expression look much better.

She hurriedly returned a look of gratitude, then scanned the room, frowning slightly : « Ibiki, you’ve got to be kidding me, right ? Why are there still so many people ? You didn’t go easy on them in the first exam, did you ? »

Ibiki shrugged helplessly : « Can’t be helped, encountered a small accident. »

« An accident ? »

Anko looked at the candidates with great interest, a mischievous smile curling at the corners of her mouth : « I really can’t do anything about you, but it doesn’t matter. Now, leave the rest to me ! I guarantee that after the second exam, at least half of them will be eliminated~ »

« H... half ? » The candidates exclaimed in shock.

« Tsk, that’s exactly the kind of look that’s truly exciting. »

Watching the candidates' panicked expressions, the smile on Anko’s face grew even more flamboyant : « Alright, I’ll explain the specific details tomorrow. As for the time and place to gather, go ask your respective Jonin teachers. That’s all for today’s briefing. Dismissed ! »
