# Chapitre 136 — Edo Tensei

« Summoning Jutsu : Edo Tensei ! »

Following Orochimaru’s loud shout, three coffins rose from the ground amidst a cloud of smoke.

However, just as the third coffin was about to fully rise, it seemed to encounter an invisible resistance, shuddered violently, sank back down, and finally vanished into the earth.

The two successfully manifested coffins stood silently, with the vigorous characters « First » and « Second » engraved on their respective lids.

« Oh ? It seems there was a slight accident with the third one... »

Orochimaru raised an eyebrow, swept his gaze over the two coffins, and then chuckled nonchalantly, « But it doesn’t matter ; these two alone are more than enough. »

With the appearance of these two coffins, Orochimaru’s tense nerves finally relaxed significantly.

His aura also relaxed, once again revealing his usual ease.

However, when his gaze fell upon Asakawa Shin’s still indifferent expression, a trace of displeasure quietly crossed his mind.

'I have already set up such a formation, yet you still dare to be so arrogant ? Or is it that you simply don’t know the power of this technique ?'

He narrowed his eyes and secretly formed hand seals.

In an instant, the two coffins began to tremble violently.

Immediately after, the heavy coffin lids were blown off from the inside by a great force, crashing heavily to the ground.

As the dust settled, two tall figures stepped out slowly.

They wore forehead protectors symbolizing Konoha Village and were clad in the heavy plate armor characteristic of the Sengoku era, exuding a dignified pressure.

They were none other than Konoha’s First Hokage, Senju Hashirama, and the Second Hokage, Senju Tobirama.

« Where is this place ? This body... is it Edo Tensei ? Tsk, what a pain. »

Senju Hashirama clicked his tongue, his brows filled with helplessness, « That guy Tobirama, I told him long ago not to research these troublesome forbidden techniques, and now it has come true. »

Before his complaining words had even landed, he turned his head and ran into Senju Tobirama, who stood to the side with a slightly stiff expression ; an awkward atmosphere permeated between the two.

Hashirama scratched his messy hair and squeezed out a silly smile on his face : « Haha, so you’re here too, Tobirama ! »

Senju Tobirama coldly glanced sideways at his older brother, his eyes seeming to say « I’ll settle the score with you later, » then he withdrew his gaze and scanned his surroundings with a sharp look.

Finally, with a gloomy face, he stared fixedly at the culprit behind them, Orochimaru.

« So that’s how it is ; is it this young man who used the forbidden technique to summon us here ? He certainly has some nerve. »

His gaze shifted slightly, landing on the figure directly in front, his brows furrowed, « And the enemy opposite... is actually a Ninja from Konoha ? And that statue, that is a Wood Dragon, right ? What on earth is going on... »

Feeling two scrutinizing gazes cast upon him, Asakawa Shin’s expression tightened, and he quickly put on a look of righteous indignation, shouting sternly :

« Senior Brother Orochimaru ! Not only did you defect from Konoha, but now you dare to desecrate the remains of the former Hokage. How can such actions be worthy of the careful cultivation of Teacher Hiruzen Sarutobi ?

It seems you have completely turned your back on the 'will of fire' !

Fine, today I will stop your madness ; the future of Konoha will be borne by me ! »

« Oh ? So you are the monkey’s disciple ? »

Senju Hashirama raised an eyebrow slightly, his gaze crossing over Asakawa Shin and landing on Orochimaru, his tone carrying a bit of helplessness and teasing, « Come to think of it, is the one behind also the monkey’s disciple ? Good grief, what on earth has the monkey been up to all these years ? »

« Older brother, now is not the time for idle chatter. »

Senju Tobirama’s expression was grave, his gaze piercing toward Asakawa Shin like a knife, and he reminded in a low voice : « Although this Edo Tensei body is far from able to unleash our full power from when we were alive, it is more than enough to deal with a boy of this age. »

Before Senju Tobirama could finish speaking, Orochimaru interrupted impatiently : « Enough, the old generation’s reminiscing ends here ; let’s get straight to the point. »

Senju Hashirama sighed upon hearing this, his tone carrying a bit of fated helplessness : « Is it impossible to avoid fighting in any era ? »

Orochimaru snorted coldly and did not answer.

He flipped his wrist, and two Kunai attached with paper charms slid into his palms.

Immediately, his figure moved, rushing directly toward the two Hokage, intending to forcibly seal their consciousness.

This was exactly the moment Asakawa Shin had been waiting for !

He slammed his palms together, his aura erupting suddenly, and he shouted angrily :

« Don’t even think about desecrating the ancestors of my Senju Clan ; the glory of the Forest Senju shall be protected by me today !

Wood Style : Deep Forest Emergence ! »

In an instant, the sea of trees reappeared.

Countless thick branches burst from the ground like furious giant pythons, weaving into a green cage that frantically strangled toward Orochimaru from all directions, stopping him from further action.

« Damn it ! »

Orochimaru cursed under his breath, forced to interrupt his intention, and hastily controlled the two Edo Tensei bodies to retreat together.

'Is this kid’s Chakra endless ? He actually still has the energy to cast a Ninjutsu of this level !'

Seeing this, the First and Second Hokage both had their pupils shrink.

« Older brother, it’s actually Wood Release ! »

Senju Tobirama’s voice could not hide his shock : « And listening to what he said just now, could it be... that this is a descendant of our Senju Clan ?

Sure enough... my Senju Clan truly produces heroes, far from those naturally... »

« Ahem, Tobirama ! »

Senju Hashirama hurriedly coughed twice to interrupt, glancing at his younger brother with slight embarrassment, « Don’t say these things in front of the younger generation !

Eh ? Oh no... my body is completely out of control !

Careful, the next technique I cast will also be Wood Release ! »

Before the words left his mouth, his hands were already uncontrollably slammed together.

« Boom— ! »

Another « Wood Style : Deep Forest Emergence » erupted, attempting to block Asakawa Shin’s offensive.

However, the scene that followed left everyone present, including the caster himself, in a stunned silence.

The forest world summoned by Hashirama was as fragile as a fleeting wave in the face of Asakawa Shin’s overwhelming sea of trees.

It only stalled for a moment before being completely swallowed and assimilated by that massive Wood Release power.

It then became a part of the opponent’s power, continuing to sweep toward them with destructive force.

« Older brother, you... »

« Sigh ! This body is just too weak ; it can’t unleash my true strength at all... that’s why...

No, isn’t this a good thing ?

Since that’s the case, monkey’s disciple, just go ahead and take us down in one go ! »

Senju Tobirama, who was on the side, also reminded loudly : « Remember, we are currently in Edo Tensei bodies and possess immortal bodies ; you cannot kill us, sealing is the only way ! »

Watching this scene, Orochimaru felt faint, and a sense of helplessness couldn’t help but well up in his heart :

'Why does it feel like these two legendary former Hokage are useless after being summoned ?

Not only did they fail to suppress the enemy, but they are just as flustered and fleeing as I am.

It seems these two can only be saved to deal with that old guy, Hiruzen Sarutobi.'

On the outskirts of the battlefield, a dozen squads had followed Asakawa Shin’s Wood Dragon to this place, and the scene before them would be unforgettable for the rest of their lives.

They saw Asakawa Shin fighting all three sides alone, his offensive like a raging storm.

Whether it was the First Hokage’s Wood Release secret technique or the Second Hokage’s Water Release Ninjutsu, all were easily neutralized by him.

And Orochimaru was even more miserable, almost turning into a ball, being knocked flying back and forth.

If it weren’t for his Orochimaru-style Body Replacement Technique, he would have been beaten to death on the spot long ago.

« Careful ! The next technique is a high-level Water Release I have meticulously researched ; it possesses an unstoppable piercing power, so you absolutely must not take it head-on ! »

« Water Release : Water Severing Wave ! »

Before the words finished, Asakawa Shin had already blocked his right arm across his chest.

In an instant, the dense Armament Haki surged and condensed like a tide, bringing up streaks of black and red lightning.

The high-pressure water gun formed by the Water Severing Wave slammed into it, merely kicking up a large amount of water mist before completely dissipating.

When the mist cleared, Asakawa Shin’s arm was still intact, and not even a scratch was left on his skin.

Senju Tobirama’s eye twitched upon seeing this, and he said somewhat sheepishly : « Well... then never mind. »

Senju Hashirama : « Oh oh oh ! As expected of my Senju brat ! »
