# Chapitre 178 — Lying Low

After an overnight family meeting, the group finally came up with a solution.

« Junior Brother, I’m sorry to put you through this. The power of your technique is simply too terrifying. For the peace of the Village, you should temporarily leave to lie low for a while. It won’t be too late to return once you can control it at will. »

At the Village entrance, Tsunade spoke with earnest concern.

« I will announce to the public that you are on a secret mission. If anything happens in the Village, I will certainly send word to you immediately. You don’t need to worry about things here, » she added.

Asakawa Shin sighed softly, a trace of loneliness flickering between his brows.

Seeing this, Tsunade felt a pang of pity. Her lips moved, about to offer a few words of comfort.

« I knew Senior Sister couldn’t bear to see me go ! »

Asakawa Shin instantly swapped to a beaming smile and leaned in, saying, « Don’t worry, Senior Sister, I’ll come back to visit you often. Besides, you have my Flying Thunder God Mark on you, so we can see each other whenever we want. »

Before he could finish, Tsunade coldly interrupted him, « That won’t be necessary. Junior Brother, you should focus on 'cultivating your character' while you’re away. Unless it’s something important, do not come back. »

Seeing Asakawa Shin’s look of disbelief, Tsunade quickly tried to salvage the situation : « I’ve already pinned this entire matter on Danzo. If you show up recklessly, wouldn’t that be a dead giveaway ? »

« Huh ? But isn’t Danzo already... »

« So what ? He’s done so many bad things anyway ; one more won’t make a difference. »

After a reluctant farewell to Tsunade, Asakawa Shin flew aimlessly through the air, momentarily unsure of where to go.

A moment later, his eyes twinkled as the figure of his 'good senior brother,' Orochimaru, surfaced in his mind.

With a single thought, he activated the Flying Thunder God Technique, and in the next instant, he arrived outside Otogakure in the Land of Rice Fields.

As he made a high-profile descent from the sky, the Ninjas of Otogakure quickly noticed the uninvited guest.

Before the guards could even shout a warning, a surge of Haoshoku Haki, far more powerful than before, erupted from Asakawa Shin’s body like a landslide or a tsunami, instantly sweeping through the entire Otogakure.

Deep within the underground laboratory, Orochimaru, who was immersed in his research, suddenly stiffened. The test tube in his hand, which represented a week’s worth of painstaking effort and was on the verge of completion, slipped from his grasp and shattered on the floor.

Orochimaru’s eye twitched, but he could only take a deep breath, force a stiff smile, and hurry to the surface to welcome his 'good junior brother.'

He had originally intended to bring a few subordinates to keep up appearances, but as it turned out, everyone except him had rolled their eyes back and fainted.

He looked up, and his heart filled with even more dread.

The heavens looked as if they had been cleaved by an invisible giant blade ; the clouds were neatly split into a massive, churning rift. From a distance, it looked as if the sky itself had cracked open.

« Junior Brother, we haven’t seen each other for only a few days, yet your strength has improved by such a large margin. Truly an astonishing talent. »

Seeing the poorly concealed greed in Orochimaru’s eyes, Asakawa Shin shook his head regretfully.

« Senior Brother, you’re the one who has changed the most. In just a few days, you’ve actually changed from a woman’s body back into a man’s. What a pity. »

« A pity ? »

Orochimaru looked suspicious. « A pity for what ? »

« Hahaha, don’t worry about such details. »

Asakawa Shin laughed awkwardly to brush it off. « I’ve come today because I want to make a deal with you, Senior Brother. I want the complete Edo Tensei from your hands. »

« In exchange, I will provide you with the most suitable vessel for Living Corpse Reincarnation, as well as the necessary sacrifices for Edo Tensei. »

Orochimaru’s eye twitched. He found it hard not to suspect that this kid was doing it on purpose.

He had just completed his Living Corpse Reincarnation and entered the cooling-off period when this so-called perfect vessel was delivered to his doorstep.

Orochimaru’s eyes flickered. « Fine, but I need to inspect the goods first. »

Without a word, Asakawa Shin tossed him a scroll containing the corpse of a White Zetsu.

Orochimaru pulled out the body and gasped in shock, « This... this is White Zetsu ? »

« What ? Are you excited to see an old colleague ? » Asakawa Shin teased with a mischievous grin.

Orochimaru was startled again. His gaze suddenly became profound as he stared intently at Asakawa Shin. « Junior Brother, you... you actually know ? »

« Heh, it’s just the Akatsuki Organization. »

Asakawa Shin smiled faintly. « I know far more than you imagine. »

« As expected of my junior brother. However, is White Zetsu’s physical constitution truly as miraculous as you say ? » Orochimaru expressed deep doubt.

Asakawa Shin made a 'please' gesture. « Senior Brother, you’ll know once you try it ! »

Orochimaru nodded and immediately set to work. As time passed, his eyes grew brighter and brighter, until finally, he suddenly slapped himself hard.

« Dammit, it’s actually true... Why didn’t I think of this sooner ? Back then, I was too busy thinking about how to ambush Pain... »

Asakawa Shin patted his shoulder and encouraged him, « Senior Brother, with your talent, it shouldn’t take long to produce results, right ? Congratulations in advance. »

Orochimaru was also very happy, his tone carrying a hint of sincerity. « I really must thank you this time, Junior Brother. Without your guidance, I don’t know how many detours I would have taken. »

Saying this, he opened his mouth wide and suddenly spat out a scroll, handing it warmly to Asakawa Shin.

« Junior Brother, this is the Ninjutsu you wanted. Please accept it ! »

The Ninjutsu scroll was covered in slime, making Asakawa Shin’s skin crawl.

He hesitated, not really wanting to touch it.

But soon his eyes lit up, and he used Psychokinesis to take it.

After rinsing it with Water Release, he carefully opened it to inspect it.

Seeing this operation, Orochimaru smiled faintly.

« Don’t worry, Junior Brother. I really didn’t trick you this time. »

« It’s fine, Senior Brother. Even if you did trick me, I could just use the Flying Thunder God Mark to teleport over and settle the score with you. »

Orochimaru felt disdainful in his heart. He had already changed bodies through Living Corpse Reincarnation ; a mere Flying Thunder God could no longer do anything to him.

But he soon stiffened and abruptly turned to look at Asakawa Shin.

« Hehe, that’s right, Senior Brother. It’s exactly what you’re thinking ! »

Asakawa Shin nodded at him affirmatively.

« Those two pats I gave you just now were to leave a new Flying Thunder God Mark on you. Otherwise, if something happened to you, I wouldn’t be able to save you in time. No need to thank me. »

Orochimaru’s mouth twitched as he said speechlessly, « Then I really must thank you, my 'good' junior brother. »

« Mm, just keep it in mind. Senior Brother, you get back to work. I’m leaving now. »

With that, he vanished in a flash.

Only then did the Ninjas of Otogakure begin to wake up one after another, reporting to Orochimaru while breaking out in cold sweats.

« This is bad, Lord Leader ! We suddenly passed out for some reason, and many female Ninjas are reporting... »

Orochimaru asked impatiently, « Reporting what ? Hurry up and say it, don’t stutter ! »

« Reporting... reporting that their panties are missing... »

Orochimaru was stunned for a moment, then he couldn’t help but chuckle. « As expected of you, Junior Brother. To think you have such wicked taste. But then again, a youthful heart is only natural... right ? »

He paused for a moment and then ordered, « Go and call Guren here. I have an important mission for her. »

After bidding farewell to Orochimaru, Asakawa Shin immediately rushed toward the Land of Grass without stopping.

The mention of the Akatsuki earlier had piqued his interest, and he decided to infiltrate the Akatsuki Organization himself to investigate.

He might as well, since he had nothing else to do.

With his current flying speed and sensory range, it didn’t take long for him to find Obito’s secret base.

After confirming several times that only Black Zetsu was home, he decisively activated the Five Elements Escape Technique to sneak up behind Black Zetsu.

He opened with a heart-to-heart, which gave Black Zetsu a fright.

« Third Brother, I’ve finally found you ! How have you been these past thousand years ? »
