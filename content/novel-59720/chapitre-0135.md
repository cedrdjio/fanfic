# Chapitre 135 — A Welcoming Gift

« The kusanagi sword ? »

Orochimaru raised an eyebrow, his expression darkening. « So what if it is ? »

Asakawa Shin didn’t answer his question. Instead, his eyes lit up, and he looked at Orochimaru with burning intensity :

« Senior Brother, I remember you should still have the summoning scrolls for Ryūchi Cave and the Rashōmon on you, right ? »

« Junior Brother, are you trying to say... »

« That’s right. I actually quite like these few items. Why don’t you part with them and give them to me ? Consider it a welcoming gift~ »

People really do laugh when they are extremely speechless, and Orochimaru chuckled at this moment. « Since Junior Brother likes them so much, why don’t you come and take them yourself ? »

« Oh ? Then I won’t be polite... »

Before the words faded, Asakawa Shin’s figure seemed to glitch, instantly closing in on Orochimaru.

A cruel smile flashed across the corner of Asakawa Shin’s mouth. He struck first, his fist wrapped in black and red lightning, carrying a sonic boom that tore through the air, suppressing Orochimaru fiercely.

The wind from his fist surged, creating visible shockwaves in the air.

'So fast ! When did he move ?'

Orochimaru only had time for this thought to flash through his mind before Asakawa Shin’s iron fist heavily penetrated his lower abdomen.

A domineering burst of energy instantly pierced through his entire body, even tearing the clothes on his back and ripping a large hole.

Immediately following, as Asakawa Shin continued to exert force with his punch, his entire body flew backward like a cannonball.

With a « boom, » he crashed through several large trees along the way. The immense impact caused him to bounce and roll on the ground, eventually plowing a long gully before he could barely stop.

Dust filled the air, leaving a mess.

When the smoke cleared, his entire upper body was buried in the dirt, and the limbs exposed above ground were twisted into weird knots.

With broken bones and severed tendons, it looked very bad.

« Tsk, I might have gone a bit too far. But if the opponent is Senior Brother Orochimaru, it should be fine, right ? »

As soon as Asakawa Shin’s words fell, Orochimaru’s counterattack arrived.

His head, like a spirit snake emerging from its den, burrowed through the ground and suddenly burst out from behind Asakawa Shin.

Without time to catch his breath, he opened his mouth slightly, and a long snake shot out. The snake’s mouth spat out a sharp blade, and the sword edge extended crazily in an instant, turning into a deadly cold light stabbing directly at Asakawa Shin’s heart.

« kusanagi sword · Sky Blade ! »

When Asakawa Shin heard the commotion and turned back, the chilling blade was already right before his eyes.

He seemed unable to dodge in time and could only hurriedly raise his hand to block in front of his chest, wanting to use this to stop the thrust of the kusanagi sword.

'Great, I got him ! To dare to use a hand to catch the indestructible kusanagi sword head-on, how foolish !'

He also saw the Armament Haki covering Asakawa Shin’s right hand, but he didn’t take it to heart.

This seemed to be the Earth Spear Technique, but so what ?

With the sharpness of the kusanagi sword, it was enough to easily pierce through all defenses in the world.

Thinking of this, he increased his Chakra output slightly, wanting to severely injure Asakawa Shin in one strike.

He even slightly deflected the sword blade to avoid Asakawa Shin’s vital heart.

Lest he really kill Asakawa Shin with one sword ; after all, this was the perfect vessel he had chosen.

But obviously, his worry was unnecessary.

The blade arrived, breaking through the air. Asakawa Shin didn’t dodge or evade, instead reaching out to grab the cold, sharp blade.

Under Orochimaru’s stunned gaze, that palm covered in Armament Haki steadily clamped onto the sword blade, with his thumb pressing against the tip, preventing it from advancing even a fraction.

Asakawa Shin’s feet were also as stable as a rock, nailed to the spot without moving a muscle.

On the other hand, Orochimaru was forced by the violent counter-force to jerk his neck backward, looking somewhat disheveled for a moment.

In horror, he had to hastily cut off the Chakra supply, forcefully stopping the extension of the kusanagi sword.

« Impossible ! This is the indestructible kusanagi sword ! You... »

Before he could finish, Asakawa Shin already revealed a look of contemptuous sneer.

He used the same technique with his left hand to firmly grasp the blade.

Then, he exerted force suddenly, waving the kusanagi sword to draw circles in the air, actually forcibly pulling Orochimaru’s head out of the ground.

The part of Orochimaru’s neck buried underground was also like a wire, pulled out of the ground by this huge force, then following the vine to find the melon, it connected all the way to Orochimaru’s body, circling and dancing in the air along with the extended kusanagi sword.

Looking from a distance, Asakawa Shin looked like he was waving a giant long whip.

And the end of that long whip, due to high-speed waving, even tore the air, emitting a sharp piercing sound.

Orochimaru was extremely miserable at this moment. The intense centrifugal force made his bones feel like they were falling apart, and he could only passively roll in the air, unable to find an opportunity to counterattack at all.

'No... no way, if this goes on, I’ll definitely die. I must escape !'

A ruthless glint flashed in his eyes, and he suddenly bit off the body of the poisonous snake in his mouth.

As the connection medium broke, he finally broke free from this death vortex and shot out like a cannonball.

Accompanied by a loud roar, he crashed through several more giant trees before he could barely stabilize his figure.

Although his body was severely damaged—which would have killed an ordinary person—this was just a small problem for Orochimaru, a mere fatal wound.

I saw the « remains » on the ground suddenly open a big mouth, and a pair of human hands reached out from it, pulling hard to both sides, forcefully stretching the mouth open to half a meter wide.

Immediately after, a brand-new figure slowly crawled out from it.

This scene was like a snake shedding its skin. The newborn body was unscathed ; where was there any trace of the previous disheveled appearance ?

Although Orochimaru escaped death, his current state could not be called good at all.

He gasped rapidly, staring closely at Asakawa Shin, as if looking at an indescribable monster.

« You... are you really a human ? »

Asakawa Shin had no time to pay attention to him at this moment. After getting the kusanagi sword as he wished, Asakawa Shin began a series of operations.

First, he used a Contract Seal to block Orochimaru’s summoning connection, then engraved a Flying Thunder God Mark on the blade, and only then began to clean it up with a face full of disgust.

After wasting a large amount of water, Asakawa Shin finally overcame his psychological barrier, reluctantly gripped the hilt, and began to test the sword.

After waving it a few times, he shook his head and finally turned his gaze to Orochimaru :

« Senior Brother, this kusanagi sword of yours is barely satisfactory, the design is really hard to look at. Where is that 'Sky Blade' of yours ? Give that one to me. »

Orochimaru had just taken the opportunity to recover some of his state, and his pupils shrank upon hearing this.

How does this guy know I have another kusanagi sword ? Did he plant a spy beside me ? Who exactly betrayed me ?

For a moment, countless suspicions surged in his heart.

Asakawa Shin saw him silent and couldn’t help but frown.

Why is this Senior Brother so stingy ? Compared to Senior Sister Tsunade, he’s just like a dog on the side of the road.

Thinking this way, his tone became impolite as well.

« Senior Brother, I’m talking to you, are you deaf ? »

This sharp shout interrupted Orochimaru’s thoughts, making him suddenly snap back to reality.

« Junior Brother, you do have the capital to be arrogant with the Wood Release Bloodline Limit, but Wood Release is not your exclusive property. Let you see it today ! What is true Wood Release ! Summoning Technique · Edo Tensei ! »
