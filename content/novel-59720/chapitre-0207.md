# Chapitre 207 — Absolutely Impossible

Deep in the dark cave, the massive Gedo Statue was summoned by Nagato’s Astral Projection.

Its ten fingers, like withered ancient trees, stood upright eerily.

Faint blue characters glowed at the fingertips, corresponding exactly with the rings on the hands of the eight Akatsuki Organization members, forming a mysterious and oppressive ritual scene.

« Fuinjutsu : Phantom Dragons Nine Seals ! »

With a low shout from Nagato, the other seven coordinated their hand signs, their Chakra intertwining and merging into the giant fingers beneath them.

The Gedo Statue’s tightly closed mouth slowly opened, and a sky-blue nine-headed dragon made of pure Chakra roared out, its cry shaking loose rocks from the cave walls.

The nine dragon heads latched onto the unconscious Yugito on the ground like maggots on a bone.

Her brows furrowed as a pained groan escaped her lips, and her body was forcibly dragged into mid-air.

The Two-Tails within her let out a shrill wail, but under the absolute suppression of the Fuinjutsu, it was powerless, forced to watch its Chakra being continuously drained like a river into the sea, pouring into the statue’s mouth.

« What on earth is this thing ? Why is it suppressing me so strongly ? This is bad, very bad ! »

Within the mental world, Matatabi repeatedly called out Yugito’s name, but unfortunately, its 'poop scooper' was completely oblivious to the outside world, giving no response no matter how it called.

Asakawa Shin stood speechlessly on one of the Gedo Statue’s fingers, complaining inwardly :

‘How can there be such a stupid Fuinjutsu ? It actually requires ten Kage-level fighters to spend three whole days just to seal one Tailed Beast !’

‘Now that we only have eight people, the progress bar will probably be even slower. This is a complete waste of my precious time.’

He clearly remembered Madara controlling the Gedo Statue and sealing a Tailed Beast in just a few breaths.

Is Nagato really that much of a waste ? I’ll mark another one down against him.

In his boredom, Asakawa Shin’s gaze swept over his companions beside him.

Unlike him, the others were all illusory projections created by the Astral Projection Jutsu.

In other words, at this solemn sealing site, he was the only 'honest man' standing there staring blankly...

‘Forget it, better to finish this quickly.’

Thinking this, he quietly mobilized the Chakra in his body and poured it frantically into the Gedo Statue beneath his feet.

The effect was immediate.

The previously sluggish nine-headed dragon seemed to have been injected with a stimulant ; its body suddenly swelled, and the speed of extracting the Tailed Beast instantly increased several times over.

Nagato’s real body was slightly startled and snapped his eyes open.

Konan noticed his abnormality and looked over, « What’s wrong ? »

« That fellow Madara’s Chakra capacity is unfathomable, even far exceeding my own. It seems I underestimated him a bit, » Nagato said in a low voice, his tone tinged with gravity.

Konan’s delicate brows furrowed slightly, a hint of worry showing in her eyes.

She had always felt that this Uchiha Madara had ulterior motives ; it seemed she would need to prepare more Explosive Tags.

She quickly composed herself and comforted him, « Madara is a legendary Ninja after all, so it’s normal for him to have more Chakra, isn’t it ? »

Nagato hesitated to speak. This wasn’t just « a bit more » ; the other’s Chakra capacity was clearly several times his own !

One must know that as a descendant of the Uzumaki Clan, he naturally possessed vast Chakra reserves ; otherwise, he wouldn’t have been chosen as the vessel for the Rinnegan.

Not to mention that he had later transplanted Hashirama cells, which not only shared the burden of the Rinnegan on his body but also boosted his Chakra capacity to even greater heights.

Even so, he was still far inferior to that Uchiha Madara. How could this not shock him ?

...

Time flowed by, and in the blink of an eye, a day had passed.

Under Asakawa Shin’s full acceleration, the sealing ritual finally neared its end.

When the last wisp of Chakra was stripped away and the Two-Tails turned into a stream of light, completely absorbed into the Gedo Statue, Pain finally slowly lowered his hands.

A hint of strangeness flashed in the eyes of the other members ; they were well aware of the abnormality of this sealing ritual.

Just because of the addition of « Tobi, » the efficiency had increased several times compared to when sealing the One-Tail. This was truly a bit...

Nagato also sensed the subtle atmosphere and immediately announced in a deep voice, « The Two-Tails sealing is complete. Disperse. »

As he finished speaking, he immediately released the Astral Projection Jutsu.

Before his silhouette vanished, he gave Asakawa Shin a deep look and then completely faded away.

Following that, the Gedo Statue also turned into a large cloud of smoke, ending the summoning contract.

Asakawa Shin didn’t care at all and leisurely walked up to Yugito.

At this moment, with the Tailed Beast completely extracted, her body was like a massive void had been opened, and her life energy was leaking out frantically like a bursting dam.

At this rate, she would perish in a matter of moments.

He reached out and pressed lightly on her abdomen, and the Six Paths Yang Power surged from his palm, instantly mending the large void.

But it wasn’t enough.

Asakawa Shin rubbed his chin with his fingertips, already choreographing a grand play titled 'The Poop Scooper Bravely Battles the Evil Doctor' in his mind, determined to save this unfortunate cat-girl from her desperate situation.

No sooner said than done.

With a flash, he teleported Yugito to the deep mountains and old forests of the Land of Earth.

He then created a Wood Clone and launched a world-shaking battle against his original body.

Just as the battle reached its peak, he quietly injected a trace of Yang Power, waking Yugito at the perfect moment.

Yugito opened her eyes dizzily. A wave of intense weakness surged over her, nearly drowning her, with only the sharp Pain in her abdomen from the Tailed Beast extraction remaining clear.

« Cough, cough... »

She gritted her teeth and propped up her upper body, only to see an earth-shattering battle unfolding before her.

At this moment, she was standing atop a massive Wood Golem, facing off against a storm that connected the heavens and the earth.

Violent air currents raged, turning the surrounding rocks into powder.

Yugito turned her head to look at the figure encircling her waist, her pupils shrinking. « You... you’re Asakawa Shin ? Why are you here ? »

Asakawa Shin didn’t look back.

« To save you, of course. I sensed your life force rapidly fading, so I teleported here immediately... »

Yugito clutched her throbbing forehead, half-believing and half-doubting.

« How did you sense it ? Did you tamper with my body ? »

Asakawa Shin froze for a moment, then gave a dry laugh to change the subject. « Oh, don’t worry about the details. By the way, who is that guy opposite us ? I went to a lot of trouble to snatch you back. »

Hearing this, Yugito looked around, and indeed, a scene of devastation met her eyes.

Several towering mountains had been cut in half, countless ancient trees uprooted, and several bottomless ravines split the landscape like scars.

This scene of devastation silently spoke of the ferocity of the battle that had just occurred.

After taking a few deep breaths, Yugito’s muddled mind finally cleared a bit.

Suddenly, her body stiffened, and she clutched her abdomen, exclaiming in shock, « Something’s wrong... where is the Two-Tails ? Why am I still alive without the Tailed Beast ? »

Asakawa Shin immediately took the opportunity to take credit, his tone somewhat smug. « You were originally certain to die, but I used a Forbidden Jutsu to drag you back from the Gates of Hell. No need to be too grateful ; after all, we still have an engagement. »

« Engagement ? »

Yugito gave him a strange look, but seeing Asakawa Shin’s pale face, it was clear the price of that Forbidden Jutsu was no small matter, so she had no way to argue for the moment.

She opened her mouth, her voice as faint as a mosquito’s buzz. « Then... thank you. But, could you be a good person to the end and send me back to the Hidden Cloud Village ? »

While controlling his Wood Clone to trade a blow, Asakawa Shin responded indifferently, « Go back for what ? The Hidden Cloud Village has long since struck your name from their records, saying you fled from battle and brought shame to the Village. »

« Nani ? ! Impossible, absolutely impossible ! I am the Raikage ! »
