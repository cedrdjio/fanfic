# Chapitre 70 — Sage Mode

« This is it ? Success ? Is that for real ? »

Upon hearing Asakawa Shin’s words, Sage Katsuyu hurriedly leaned her massive head in to observe closely :

« It’s actually real ! There is no doubt about it ; this is Sage Chakra ! It truly surprises me—to think you could do it so easily... »

Sage Katsuyu couldn’t help but be shocked ; the speed was simply too fast, unnaturally fast.

The previous inheritor of the Shikkotsu Forest Sage arts was Senju Hashirama, revered by all in the Ninja World as the « God of Shinobi. »

Even he had spent over half a month to master the Shikkotsu Forest Sage arts, a speed that was already considered astonishing.

Yet, for a human like Asakawa Shin to master Sage arts in such a short time, in the records of the three great holy lands, only one person in a thousand years had ever achieved it...

His name was Otsutsuki Hagoromo, also known as the legendary Six Paths Sage !

But the Six Paths Sage was, after all, the son of that Goddess, so having such talent was only natural. Yet, to think that Asakawa Shin could also...

Thinking of this, a sharp gleam flashed in Sage Katsuyu’s eyes.

Could it be that this time, it is finally Shikkotsu Forest’s turn to prosper ?

This thought stirred her emotions, and as she looked at Asakawa Shin again, her gaze held even more satisfaction and anticipation.

« Little brother Shin, to think you have succeeded in synthesizing Sage Chakra so quickly ! It shows that your Chakra control is outstanding, and your affinity for Natural Energy is truly extraordinary. You have truly amazed me. »

« Next, you can try merging multiple strands of Natural Energy to begin your training in Sage Mode. »

Asakawa Shin nodded in anticipation : « Then I will trouble you to protect me, big sister. »

With that, he closed his eyes again to sense his surroundings. With a thought, he controlled over a dozen strands of Natural Energy, letting them flow continuously into his body.

Not daring to be careless, Asakawa Shin quickly steadied his mind and began to synthesize Sage Chakra with the utmost seriousness.

The process went as smoothly as ever. As a large amount of Sage Chakra was synthesized, Asakawa Shin’s form underwent significant changes.

Patterns quietly appeared on his face, and his pupils were unconsciously dyed a golden-yellow. He had successfully entered the perfect Sage Mode.

Asakawa Shin suddenly opened his eyes. Feeling the state of his body, he couldn’t help but exclaim in surprise :

« Hiss... Is this Sage Mode ? »

« It has further strengthened my physique. Using Taijutsu in this state would likely be terrifyingly powerful, and my vitality has also been greatly enhanced. »

« Not only that, the quality of Sage Chakra is vastly superior to ordinary Chakra. Using Sage Chakra to cast Ninjutsu and Genjutsu will undoubtedly increase their power significantly. »

« Truly powerful ! »

« However... the duration seems a bit short... »

Feeling the Sage Chakra rapidly depleting within him, Asakawa Shin couldn’t help but knit his brows tightly.

At this rate of consumption, Sage Mode could only be maintained for about ten minutes at most. If he were to use Ninjutsu as well, the duration would likely be even shorter.

Such poor endurance made Asakawa Shin somewhat anxious. However, as his peripheral vision swept over Sage Katsuyu, a flash of lightning suddenly crossed his mind, causing him to show a thoughtful expression :

'The Nine-Tailed can enable and extend Sage Mode for Naruto and Minato, and Sage Fukasaku and Sage Shima can assist Jiraiya in activating Sage Mode. There is no reason Sage Katsuyu couldn’t do it. I wonder if a Katsuyu Mode is feasible...'

Sage Katsuyu felt a bit creeped out by Asakawa Shin’s stare, feeling that the brat was thinking about something impolite.

She gave a light cough to interrupt Asakawa Shin’s train of thought : « Little brother Shin, what are you looking at ? Is there a problem with your Sage Mode ? Do you need big sister’s help ? »

Asakawa Shin’s eyes lit up, and he hurriedly replied : « Yes, yes ! I have just encountered a tricky problem that I really need your help with, big sister ! I found that Sage Chakra is consumed too quickly ; I fear Sage Mode can only last a few short minutes. That amount of time is nowhere near enough. So I was wondering, could I ask you to assist me in gathering Natural Energy during combat to help extend the duration of my Sage Mode ? »

After hearing this, the stone in Sage Katsuyu’s heart finally dropped.

She carefully considered Asakawa Shin’s suggestion and pondered : « Theoretically, this plan is feasible. In critical moments, we could even skip this stage ; I could synthesize Sage Chakra within my own body using Natural Energy and then transfer it to you. That way, you can focus on the battle without distraction, and I will take care of the endurance issue. »

Asakawa Shin was overjoyed upon hearing this. Truly, having an elder in the house is like having a treasure.

However, synthesizing Sage Chakra requires consuming Sage Katsuyu’s own physical and spiritual energy, which would undoubtedly significantly reduce her summoning duration. There is no need to use such a trump card unless absolutely necessary ; having Sage Katsuyu assist in gathering Natural Energy to extend the duration of Sage Mode is enough.

Since the endurance issue was resolved, there was only one last thing left to verify. Asakawa Shin bowed to Sage Katsuyu and said apologetically :

« Big sister Katsuyu, I have had some realizations while practicing Sage Mode, and we might need to stay in Shikkotsu Forest for a while longer. But don’t worry, I can use this time to study the contract seal and see if I can break through the time limit of the Summoning Jutsu. I will be imposing on you for a bit longer, and I hope you will forgive me. »

Although Sage Katsuyu was a bit surprised, she didn’t say much else. Little brother Shin’s cultivation was important ; she had endured for over a thousand years, so a little more time wouldn’t hurt. On the contrary, having someone here to chat with made her quite happy ; she wouldn’t have to play single-player in Shikkotsu Forest all by herself anymore.

So she nodded to Asakawa Shin : « It’s fine, little brother Shin. Just treat Shikkotsu Forest as your own home ; stay as long as you like. »

Asakawa Shin looked at her gratefully. Without further ado, he sat cross-legged on the ground and began to test the effects of Sage Chakra.

He looked inside his body, controlling the Sage Chakra to merge into his limbs and bones. Once fused into Sage Chakra, the Natural Energy contained within was like a tamed beast, easily and rapidly absorbed by his body, and Asakawa Shin’s physique underwent subtle changes as a result.

'It really works ! Then, let’s continue...'

So for the next day, Asakawa Shin did nothing but synthesize Sage Chakra and merge it into every part of his body. However, he discovered that the speed of evolution had gradually slowed down ; it didn’t have the immediate effect it had at the beginning.

Through careful observation and experimentation, he found that the Natural Energy must be combined with that vitality from the System to strengthen him, only then could it be quickly and effectively absorbed by the body. But that vitality was, after all, meant for growing nose hair, and the output was like a trickling stream, which was extremely limited.

This made Asakawa Shin curse inwardly : 'What a useless System, ruining my grand plan. Can’t it produce more vitality ? If my nose hair grows a bit faster, would I actually get angry ?'
