# Chapitre 159 — The Grand Show Begins

The third round of the Chunin Exams' main tournament kicked off amidst great anticipation.

Inside the Konoha Stadium, the roar of the crowd was like a surging tide.

Diplomatic missions from various countries and noble clans gathered here ; even Kazekage Rasa and Raikage Ai were present in person.

The stands were packed to capacity, with countless eyes focused on the arena below, as a suffocating sense of tension filled the air.

This was not just a Chunin Exam, but a peak confrontation between the geniuses of the new generation.

At Hiruzen Sarutobi’s signal, the referee slowly stepped onto the stage, swept his gaze across the venue, and then announced loudly :

« The third round of the Chunin Exam finals officially begins ! Now, let the eleven contestants take the stage ! »

As soon as he finished speaking, thunderous applause instantly resounded throughout the venue.

Under the watchful eyes of the crowd, eleven boys and girls strode out in formation from the entrance.

« Wow, there are so many people ! »

Naruto looked around and couldn’t help but exclaim in surprise.

Sasuke rarely refrained from mocking him ; his expression was tense as he constantly searched through the bustling crowd.

A moment later, his eyes suddenly lit up as his mother and older brother were sitting in the stands, waving at him.

Sasuke immediately raised his hand and waved back vigorously in response.

Asakawa Shin also reined in his usual nonchalance, his expression solemn and focused, exuding an air of maturity and stability.

In the stands, Hanako was holding Asakawa Momo, shouting at the top of her lungs to cheer for him.

Even more eye-catching was that Kana, Yūgao, and Kenji had actually put up a striking banner, cheering for him, Naruto, and Karin.

Naruto was infected by this enthusiasm and waved excitedly, shouting : « Aunt Hanako, Little Momo, and everyone else, thank you ! Thank you for your support ! »

« Stop being so noisy~ »

Gaara’s voice was cold and abrupt, filled with undisguised impatience : « You’re just a supporting character, what are you shouting about ! »

Before Naruto could flare up, Asakawa Shin had already appeared beside Gaara at some point, « intimately » putting an arm around his shoulder.

« Naruto is just too excited to see his family~ »

Asakawa Shin had a warm smile on his lips as he tilted his head slightly toward the stands, « Gaara, why are you angry ? Look, isn’t your family also cheering for you ? »

« My... family ? »

Gaara’s gaze suddenly froze, as if a tight nerve had been touched, and he jerked his head to follow Asakawa Shin’s line of sight.

Sure enough, at the end of his vision, Temari and Kankuro were waving their arms vigorously.

The corners of Gaara’s mouth had just turned up by a single pixel when today’s mission flashed through his mind, and his face instantly darkened.

The hand resting on his shoulder felt as heavy as a thousand pounds, making his entire body feel cold.

'This guy... seems to have gotten stronger again. Although the naked eye can catch his figure, he feels like nothingness in Chakra perception. This feeling... is truly hair-raising...'

On the high platform, Hiruzen Sarutobi took in this scene and then turned his head, saying with a smile :

« Kazekage, that’s your son, isn’t it ? To be able to perfectly control a Tailed Beast at such a young age, he’s truly a young hero. »

Hearing this, a fleeting smile flashed in Rasa’s eyes : « Not at all, this child also gave me quite a headache when he was younger. Besides, speaking of young heroes, your disciple... is even more astonishing, isn’t he ? »

To the side, Raikage Ai’s gaze darkened as he muttered in a low voice : « Asakawa Shin... the legendary Wood Release ? I really want to see it for myself and see if it lives up to its name. »

« Oh ? The Raikage probably hasn’t seen the First Hokage’s Wood Release, right ? Then you can certainly look forward to it. »

A sharp light flashed in Hiruzen Sarutobi’s eyes, 'Just as long as you don’t get so scared you wet your pants when the time comes.'

...

The referee glanced at his watch, confirmed the time was up, and then turned his gaze to the highest stand.

Hiruzen Sarutobi nodded slightly, and the referee, understanding the signal, immediately announced the rules of the match in a loud voice :

« This match is an elimination format, with the same rules as the prelims, continuing until one side surrenders or dies.

However, if I judge that the outcome has been decided, I will terminate the match immediately. Is that clear ? »

Seeing no objections, he officially announced :

« Then the first duel begins now. The opponents are Uzumaki Naruto and Hyuga Hinata. The other contestants, please return to the waiting room first. »

Soon, the other contestants left the stage, leaving only two figures standing opposite each other.

Naruto and Hinata’s eyes met, and they each took their stances as the air instantly filled with a tense fighting spirit.

« Hinata, I’m going all out in this match ! »

Naruto took a deep breath, his eyes burning, « I hope you do the same. »

The two personalities in Hinata’s mind quickly reached an agreement, and her gaze suddenly became determined, wanting to fight to establish her status in the household.

« Naruto, I won’t show any mercy, so bring it on. »

Seeing that both were ready, the referee waved his hand and announced in a deep voice : « Match start ! »

As soon as the words fell, both had already made their moves.

Naruto instantly activated the Shadow Clone Technique, and the field was suddenly filled with dozens of clones.

Hinata activated her Byakugan, her figure closing in like a flash of lightning.

With a light tap of her finger, a clone dissipated with a bang, turning into a cloud of white smoke.

« So fast ! »

Naruto’s heart tightened, sensing trouble ; the Byakugan’s insight combined with this terrifying Taijutsu was indeed tricky.

Hinata moved through the siege of clones as if strolling through a garden, her clothes fluttering as she lightly dodged all attacks.

Even sneak attacks from her blind spots were instantly perceived by the Byakugan, rendering them useless.

Meanwhile, her attacks were like lightning ; every light tap of her fingertip was accompanied by a muffled « bang. »

Naruto’s Shadow Clones didn’t even have time to react before they all collapsed into white smoke.

The battle showed a one-sided trend, and Naruto was momentarily forced into an absolute disadvantage.

In the stands, Hanabi couldn’t contain her excitement, clapping and laughing loudly : « Hahaha, Big Sister is awesome ! »

« Hanabi ! »

Hyuga Hiashi’s face darkened as he scolded in a low voice : « You are not allowed to learn such nonsense from your sister. »

« ...Oh ! »

Hanabi stuck out her tongue and sullenly shut her mouth.

Coincidentally, not far away, Asakawa Momo was puffing out her cheeks in frustration and started shouting loudly : « What’s going on... what’s wrong with Big Brother Naruto ? He seems completely unable to beat this big sister ! »

« Hmph, isn’t it normal that he can’t beat my sister ? She’s the genius of our Hyuga Clan. »

Asakawa Momo was stunned by the words and turned her head to see a little girl about her own age standing beside her.

She blinked and quickly caught the key point : « Your sister ? And your eyes... could it be you are— »

« That’s right, we are biological sisters ! »

The girl raised her head and introduced herself : « My name is Hyuga Hanabi, nice to meet you ! By the way, what’s your name ? »

« My name is Asakawa Momo. »

« Asakawa ? That surname sounds so familiar... but why can’t I remember it ? »

Hanabi tapped her little head, making a « thump-thump » sound, « Forget it, if I can’t remember, I won’t think about it ! Momo, since we’ve already exchanged names, from now on, we’re best friends ! »

« Friends ? Isn’t that... a bit too fast ? »

« Not at all. » Hanabi shook her head seriously, « This is how making friends works. »
