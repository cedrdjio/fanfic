# Chapitre 125 — Isn't One of Me Enough?

« Is this the Multi-Size Technique ? No... that’s not right, it seems a bit different. »

Kakashi looked at Asakawa Shin with some confusion ; he always felt that this guy’s Ninjutsu and their style were completely different, creating an indescribable sense of dissonance.

Asakawa Shin ignored the surrounding gazes and looked straight at Karin : « Alright, I’ve already gauged your strength. Leave the rest to me. »

After some observation, although their practical combat experience was slightly raw, they had already reached the level of elite Chunin. Working together, even an ordinary Jonin might not be able to get the upper hand.

However, facing an opponent of Zabuza’s caliber, it was still a bit too early for them.

After all, their combat systems had not yet been fully established.

Zabuza’s body stiffened slightly. The Water Dragon Bullet Technique, in which he had placed high hopes, had been effortlessly crushed by the youth before him, giving him a very bad premonition.

Asakawa Shin looked at Zabuza expressionlessly, unable to muster the slightest interest in the opponent before him because he was simply too weak.

So, refusing to work overtime, he got straight to the point : « Hey, Zabuza, be my dog. If you do, I can spare your life. »

« What kind of joke is that ! Who would want to be your dog ! You brat ! »

Zabuza was clearly enraged ; there should be a limit to looking down on people. He opened his mouth to say something more, but was directly interrupted by Asakawa Shin.

« What a pity, wrong answer. »

Asakawa Shin was very dissatisfied with Zabuza’s attitude, so he couldn’t be bothered to listen to his nonsense. He lifted his finger slightly and pointed it into the air.

« Flying Finger Pistol. »

In an instant, an overwhelmingly powerful air cannon flew from his fingertip, blasting toward Zabuza in the distance.

Zabuza felt his hair stand on end ; he clearly felt the chill of death in this strike.

At this critical moment, he erupted with a speed of hand seals he had never achieved in his life, using a Water Body Flicker to narrowly escape the direct hit of the Flying Finger Pistol.

However, the air cannon slammed heavily onto the water surface, violently displacing the surrounding water as if a large-scale Water Release technique had been cast.

Although Zabuza avoided the Flying Finger Pistol, he could not escape its violent aftermath.

The tremendous force instantly sent him flying. While tumbling backward, he violently spat out a mouthful of blood ; clearly, he had suffered serious internal injuries.

Asakawa Shin frowned and complained with great dissatisfaction : « Zabuza, you guy won’t be my dog, and you won’t just die obediently, which is really annoying ! Forget it, I’ll finish you off with the next strike. »

Before his words faded, he suddenly unleashed his Chakra without reservation, forming bursts of violent air currents that swept in all directions.

His current Chakra level had long surpassed that of the full-form Nine-Tailed, and with his continuous absorption of Natural Energy recently, it was growing rapidly.

Bursting out at this moment, it was truly like a landslide or a tsunami, bringing an incomparable sense of oppression to everyone.

Especially for Karin, who was a sensory Ninja, and Zabuza, who was the enemy, the feeling was the most distinct.

« Is... is this Lord Shin’s Chakra ? It’s so vast and so warm, it’s simply like a god’s ; it’s truly incredible. »

« Is this a monster ? With this amount of Chakra, how am I supposed to fight him ? Maybe... »

Just as Zabuza was hesitating whether to just surrender, a few senbon suddenly flew from a distance, aiming for the acupuncture points on his neck.

Zabuza’s eyes flickered, but he ultimately did not move, allowing the senbon to pierce his neck, causing a spray of blood.

« Nani ? »

This sudden situation startled everyone. In their shock, their gazes all turned simultaneously toward the direction from which the senbon had been shot.

They saw a masked Ninja standing proudly on a large tree in the distance.

Facing everyone’s gaze, he bowed politely : « Thank you all. I have been waiting for a chance to kill Zabuza for a long time. This time, it’s all thanks to you. »

Kakashi checked Zabuza’s pulse and confirmed that his heart had stopped beating, then he looked up at the newcomer : « That mask... are you a hunter-nin from the Hidden Mist Village ? »

The masked Ninja nodded slightly : « Exactly. My mission is to hunt down the rogue Ninja Zabuza Momochi and bring his corpse back to the Village. After all, this body contains too many secrets. I wonder if you all could do me a favor... »

Before the other party could finish speaking, Asakawa Shin lazily interrupted : « Of course not. This guy was disrespectful to me just now, so I’ve decided to chop him up and feed him to the dogs. This way, I get to vent my anger, and you don’t have to worry about the Village’s intelligence leaking. Isn’t that the best of both worlds ? »

The masked Ninja said nothing upon hearing this, just silently walked to Zabuza’s corpse and met Asakawa Shin’s gaze head-on.

Asakawa Shin scoffed and waved his hand, as if making a concession : « Fine, since you’re so persistent, I’ll grant you permission to take his head back. That should be enough, right ? »

The masked Ninja remained silent for a long time before letting out a barely perceptible sigh : « When did you discover it ? »

Asakawa Shin finally couldn’t help but laugh out loud, pointing his fingertips at Tsunade, Shizune, and finally himself in turn : « Our team is a special medical unit, you understand what that means, right ? We aren’t laymen like Kakashi, who can be fooled by just having his pulse checked a couple of times. »

Kakashi rolled his eyes in annoyance but couldn’t refute it.

Sasuke was shocked and blurted out : « That means Zabuza is actually not dead yet ? He wanted to use feigned death to escape ? »

Naruto finally snapped out of it and realized : « So that’s it ! But Kakashi-sensei is too lame. If it weren’t for Shin being sharp, we would have been fooled by the enemy ! »

Before he could finish, Kakashi gave Naruto a flick on the forehead. Watching Naruto clutch his head and howl, he immediately felt much better : 'I can’t beat Shin, but I can’t deal with you ? Why are you shouting out the truth like that...'

Seeing that the disguise was exposed, the masked Ninja didn’t panic or flee ; instead, he quickly leaned down to perform emergency treatment on Zabuza.

Time waited for no one ; if they delayed any longer, this state of feigned death would likely become real death.

After some effort, Zabuza’s unfocused pupils finally refocused.

He coughed weakly a few times, and only when he saw the familiar mask before him did he breathe a sigh of relief : « Haku... mission cancelled. The waters in the Land of Waves are too deep ; we must leave immediately. As for that idiot named Gato... »

Before he could finish, he realized that the atmosphere around them was eerily heavy.

The corner of his eye twitched slightly, and he turned his neck with difficulty, only to see a large group of people looking at him playfully.

Zabuza’s face immediately turned bitter, and he gave a miserable laugh : « So that’s it... have I been discovered ? Oh well, victory goes to the king and defeat to the bandit. Do it, give me a quick end. »

Haku, however, shook his head and looked at Asakawa Shin with burning eyes : « My lord, you didn’t kill us immediately, which means we are still useful to you, right ? You also heard that Lord Zabuza has already abandoned this mission. May I ask if you could spare our lives ? »

Asakawa Shin nodded with satisfaction : « Not bad, you are quite sensible, much better than that idiot Zabuza. You heard what I said earlier, right ? I need a dog to serve me, but he is unwilling to be one. However, seeing how loyal you are to him, why don’t you take his place ? As long as you nod, I will spare his life. »

Before Haku could reply, Karin couldn’t hold back her grievances and muttered in a low voice : « Lord Shin, as for a dog, isn’t one of me enough ? Don’t take him, he... he isn’t loyal... »

As soon as these words were spoken, the surrounding air instantly froze.

Everyone looked at Asakawa Shin in unison with an extremely subtle gaze ; even Tsunade couldn’t help but look over, an indescribable expression appearing on her face.

Asakawa Shin, stung by these gazes, blushed and immediately flew into a rage, sternly reprimanding : « Karin ! I’ll repeat it one more time : you are my maid, not a dog ! How many times do I have to say this before you remember ? Shut up right now ! »

« ...Oh ! »
